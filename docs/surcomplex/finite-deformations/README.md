## What the report is

Let `f = (f_1,…,f_n)` be an ordinary holomorphic complete intersection on a fixed
polydisk `D ⊂ C^n` whose only common zero in `D` is the origin, with ordinary
colength `μ`. The report proves that every Hahn perturbation `F = f + E` whose
error support is well ordered and **strictly positive** carries the whole finite
geometry of `f` intact, on the unshrunk domain, with an explicit support
certificate:

* **Division.** `G = Σ_a c_a(G) e_a + Σ_j F_j Q_j` with
  `supp c_a, supp Q_j ⊆ supp(G) + M`, where `M = E*` is the monoid generated by
  the error support. The remainder is **unique**; the witnesses are explicitly
  **not** unique, and their ambiguity is a Koszul syzygy transported with the
  same certificate.
* **Integral structure.** `H⁺_Γ(D)/(F) ≅ (K°)^μ` and `H_Γ(D)/(F) ≅ K^μ` on the
  unchanged ordinary basis, over a valuation ring that may have high-rank value
  group and need not be Noetherian; nothing is completed.
* **Conservation of multiplicity.** The characters are exactly evaluation at the
  **actual** displaced surcomplex zeros; every zero in the full surcomplex monad
  already lies in the workspace; the local Artinian factors are the formal local
  algebras **at those zeros, not at their standard parts**; and `Σ_ζ μ_ζ = μ`,
  with the same count over every infinitesimal target.
* **Residue duality.** A coefficientwise residue `Res_F` — defined by a *series*,
  not by a surcomplex contour integral — gives a perfect pairing over `K°` whose
  Gram determinant is a unit even at a collision, and satisfies
  `Res_F(G·J_F) = Tr(M_G) = Σ_ζ μ_ζ G(ζ)`, so `Res_F(J_F) = μ` exactly, degenerating
  to `Σ_ζ G(ζ)/J_F(ζ)` at simple zeros. Individual summands are routinely infinite
  while the total is finite.

The engine throughout is the positive-support Neumann inversion of the
Hahn–Neumann support lemma. No argument anywhere uses convergence of partial sums
in any topology.

## Coefficient rings — the naming discipline

Four coefficient rings occur in this material under nearly identical notation and
carry nearly word-for-word identical theorem statements. §2.3 of the article names
all four once, and **every theorem states the ring it holds over**. No theorem is
moved between rings anywhere in the report.

| Ring | Used in this report |
|---|---|
| fixed-polydisk `H_Γ(U) = O(U)((t^Γ))` | §§3–9, 11–16 — the report's primary ring |
| formal-coefficient `F_{n,Γ} = C[[z]]((t^Γ))` | §10 only |
| radius-free `C{z}((t^Γ))` | named only; belongs to the analytic-geometry report |
| common-domain germ ring | named only; belongs to the analytic-geometry report |

The only bridge used between the first two is the explicit Taylor homomorphism
`H_Γ(U) → F_{n,Γ}` at an ordinary point, used **once**, in Theorem 10.7, and never
reversed. The two results relating the radius-free ring to the others — the
strictness example and the faithful-flatness theorem — are cited, not reproved,
and are not used to move any theorem.

## Which archives it came from, and what each contributed

All five archives are dated 21 September 2026 and extend one supplied manuscript,
*Surcomplex Analysis: Infinitesimal Calculus, Hahn-Coherent Holomorphy, and
Contour Theory*. Their `.tex` sources are in `sources/`. Roughly 60–70% of each
was the shared core above; it is printed here once, in the base's formulation
except where another source proved something strictly stronger.

**08 — `08-finite-maps-residue-duality` (the base).** Fixed-polydisk ring,
arbitrary isolated complete intersection. It is the only one to prove the
**integral** structure over `K°` with perfect pairing over `K°`, from which every
`K`-linear statement in the other four specializes, and it proves it over a
possibly non-Noetherian valuation ring without silently completing it. Also
contributed: the Hahn contraction formula as a standalone theorem with full
operator-algebra proof; parameter preparation and division with exactly one
ordinary shrink and none thereafter; the finite-witness transfer principle; the
parameter-linear tensor contraction for perturbed monomial systems; relative
residue duality over a parameter polydisk; change of generators; the coupled
rank-four two-scale example and the nonpolynomial multiplicity-five example.

**05 — `05-multivariable-division-deformations`.** Same ring, same generality.
Contributed the Koszul/homological-perturbation transport of *every* relation with
explicit preimage; the precision theorem (normal forms, residues and spectral
invariants lose no valuation under a change of perturbation); the
discriminant–Jacobian identity; the discriminant threshold for preserving simple
zeros; the finite-parameter comparison principle; the coefficient-extraction
residue formula; and the rank-three quartic whose exponents are compared
lexicographically in `1, ω, ω²` rather than by a single-real-parameter Puiseux
approximation.

**06 — `06-uniform-support-fibres`.** The formal-coefficient variant. Contributed
the warning example that fixes the correct algebra (`z²−t` is a **unit** in
`K[[z]]`, its inverse having the non-well-ordered support `−1,−2,−3,…`, so the
theorem is not a base-change assertion about `K[[z]]`); the fibre theorems, which
are statements about the **map** and not merely the zero set — every infinitesimal
target has exactly `μ` preimages with multiplicity, and at `μ = 1` the induced map
of monads is a fine-analytic bijection with fine-analytic inverse; the
monad-by-monad conservation theorem over an ordinary base; exact cluster moments
and separating linear forms; the valuation localization bound; and the
triple-collision example resolved at a scale smaller than every power of the first
infinitesimal.

**11 — `11-support-controlled-intersections`.** Parametrized coordinate-power
specialization. Contributed the sharp conditioned root-stability theorem with
**both** bounds proved attained — `v(h) ≥ σ − κ` and
`v(h + A⁻¹Δ(a)) ≥ 2σ − 3κ` — and the threshold `σ > 2κ` proved strict by an
explicit merging example; the matching corollary; the ordinary holomorphic
parameter domain `V`; the summable matrix functional calculus; the Bézoutian
reproducing kernel; localized residues at actual surcomplex points; and coordinate
-change invariance.

**15 — `15-hartogs-coherence-finite-maps`.** Same specialization. Contributed the
automatic Hahn–Hartogs coherence theorem — separate coherence in each variable,
the others fixed at arbitrary finite surcomplex values, implies joint coherence,
with the global well-ordered support a **consequence** rather than an added
hypothesis — which is foundational and is placed in the preliminaries (§5), not
beside the deformation theorems; support-controlled **matrix** division, which is
what controls the coupling in an induction over the equations; the
support-preserving lift of the ordinary root cover with unchanged monodromy; the
one-variable collision-stable trace and residue formulas; and the cubic example
separating residue duality from trace degeneration.

## Conflicts resolved, not averaged

* **Which ring "the ring of Hahn analytic germs" denotes.** Definitional
  divergence, not a logical contradiction: each theorem is true of its own ring.
  Handled by the naming discipline above.
* **How stability of a simple zero is certified.** Two complementary certificates,
  one of them provably lossy if merged away. §15.3 states both and says which is
  sharper for which purpose: 11's theorem when individual roots are to be matched
  and its two attained bounds are wanted; 05's discriminant threshold when only
  "all zeros remain simple" is needed and no root labelling is available — 05
  *declines* root matching outright, on the ground that roots of a multiple
  leading system generally involve fractional exponents and inverse Jacobians can
  have negative valuation, and that refusal is printed as the strictly more
  general certificate it is; plus the recorded remark that `0 ≤ κ_a ≤ v(det A)`,
  so the determinant-only bound is strictly weaker.
* **The trace element `e_F = Σ b_a b^a`.** 06 and 15 state it and explicitly
  decline to assert `e_F = [J_F]` in general, verifying it only in examples. 08
  and 11 prove it. Remark 13.7 prints both proofs and says which manuscripts
  declined: 08's finite-witness transfer settles it for an arbitrary isolated
  complete intersection, 11's Bézoutian settles it for coordinate-power systems
  and additionally yields the reproducing kernel.
* **Two symbols named `Δ_F`.** Fixed once: `Δ_F = det M_{J_F}` (basis independent)
  and `disc_F = det T_F` (basis relative), with the proved relation
  `disc_F = (det G_F)·Δ_F`.
* **Notation generally.** §2.1 fixes one symbol per concept. Where the sources
  disagree, the synonyms are noted there once and never again — in particular the
  standard-part topology, which three sources call the residue, reduction and
  shadow topology, is called the **standard-part topology** throughout.

## What is not proved

§17.3 preserves, per member, every statement of what is *not* claimed. The main
ones:

* "Finite flat" follows here from the stronger, explicitly proved "finite free".
  `Spec A⁺ → Spec K°` being finite flat of rank `μ` **is not a claim about proper
  maps in an undeclared topology on the class SC^n**, and "finite map" means only
  the stated finite-fibre and finite-algebra properties — it does not assert
  properness for a topology on a proper class.
* Strong summability is **not** a valuation limit: `E* ∩ (−∞,η)` need not be
  finite, `1+t+t²+⋯` is a valid Hahn sum whose partial sums do not approximate it
  to valuation `> ω`, and no construction is ever justified by "the valuation
  improves at each step, hence the iteration converges".
* The coefficient recursion is exact when ordinary operations and support
  membership are effective; it is **not** a claim that every valuation truncation
  can be listed in finite time, and an arbitrary well-ordered support is not
  automatically an effective input format.
* The contraction's existence proof uses ordinary choices of vector-space
  complements and **is not an effective algorithm** for an arbitrary black-box
  holomorphic system. The Hahn contraction formula is **not a new abstract
  perturbation lemma**.
* The residue is defined by a **series**. The superscript `H` **does not denote a
  fine-topological Riemann integral over a surcomplex parametrized torus**; all
  contours are ordinary compact complex cycles. No universal identification with
  every Grothendieck-residue construction over non-Noetherian Hahn analytic rings
  is assumed. The residue is generally *not* the top normal-form coefficient.
* The perfect pairing and the trace–Jacobian identity do **not** require simple
  zeros; the formula with individual terms `G(ζ)/J_F(ζ)` does, and at a multiple
  zero it must not be used.
* No labelling of individual roots is claimed Lipschitz. The support bounds are
  not asserted for individual roots — `z²−t` already has roots `±t^{1/2}`. The
  valuation localization is a bound, not a Puiseux claim.
* "Rouché" here means conservation under a positive Hahn perturbation of the
  leading system, not an unrestricted boundary-norm theorem on `SC^n`.
* "Global support control" means uniform support on one stated ordinary domain,
  **not** the all-scale coherence condition on every surreal affine chart.
  Müller–Strohmaier's "Hahn holomorphic/meromorphic" functions are a different
  notion and should not be confused with these coefficient families.
* Status (consolidated once, §17.5): targeted literature search through
  21 September 2026, not exhaustive; **no named published conjecture is claimed
  solved**; not independently refereed and not formally verified; the archives'
  symbolic checks validate the displayed examples and are **not** machine
  verification of the general proofs.
