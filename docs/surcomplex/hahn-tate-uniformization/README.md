## What the report claims

Let `K = C((t^Gamma))` for a set-sized ordered abelian exponent group `Gamma`,
with no restriction on its rank or divisibility, and let `q` have valuation
`alpha > 0`. Let `H_alpha` be the principal convex subgroup generated by
`alpha` — the values bounded in absolute value by some *ordinary* integer
multiple of `alpha` — and put `U_q = {u in K^* : v(u) in H_alpha}`.

1. **Exact domain, in both directions.** The bilateral theta family is
   strongly Hahn-summable exactly for `u in U_q`; and, **for `u` outside the
   coordinate poles `q^Z`**, each of the two bilateral Tate coordinate
   families is strongly Hahn-summable exactly for `u in U_q`. The failure
   direction is proved, not assumed: for `v(u)` above every `N alpha` the
   leading exponents of the summands themselves form a strictly decreasing
   sequence, and likewise below every `-N alpha`. Because strong summability
   is a property of the *family* of supports, no cancellation between summands
   can repair this, and the article says so.

2. **Surjectivity onto all rational points.** `Phi_q : U_q -> E_q(K)` is a
   group homomorphism with kernel exactly `q^Z`, and it is onto **all** of
   `E_q(K)`, giving `U_q / q^Z ≅ E_q(K)`. The domain may be a proper subgroup
   of `K^*` while every elliptic-curve point is still reached. The proof runs
   classical rank-one Tate uniformization in the residue workspace
   `F = K_{H_alpha}` and then lifts each coarse infinitesimal through the
   formal group, using a proved unrestricted formal-evaluation lemma.

3. Exact scalar-extension behaviour; the coarse reduction diagram; the
   valuation-circle quotient; and, for `j` with `v(j) < 0`, support-preserving
   recovery of `q` from `j` without enlarging its support subgroup. For an
   integer `n >= 2`, the torsion field formula
   `K(E_q[n]) = K(q^(1/n)) = K_(Gamma + Z alpha/n)`, of degree equal to the
   order of `alpha` in `Gamma / n Gamma`, cyclic and totally ramified,
   generated through its coordinates by the single point `Phi_q(q^(1/n))`.

4. **If `Gamma` is divisible**, every elliptic curve over `K_Gamma` with
   `v(j(E)) < 0` is `K_Gamma`-isomorphic to such an `E_q`. For nondivisible
   `Gamma` the recovery of `q` still holds, but a curve with the same `j` can
   be a nontrivial twist.

5. Transport to `No[i]` through set-sized normal-form workspaces, with the
   admissible domain always a proper subclass of `No[i]^*`, and with every
   `E/No[i]` having `v(j(E)) < 0` covered.

## What the report does not claim

These are the source manuscript's own disclaimers. They are kept verbatim in
substance; `PROOF_AUDIT.md` carries the full list under "Essential
qualifications", and Section "Limitations and further mathematical questions"
of the article carries it again.

- This is a **candidate-original** manuscript, not a claimed solution of a
  named published open problem. The exact-domain/arbitrary-rank package was not
  identified in the targeted literature search. **Priority is not certified.**
- Tate's rank-one theorem, the classical theta and modular identities, the
  algebraic closedness of a divisible Hahn field over `C`, and the Kummer
  origin of Tate torsion are explicitly **credited as inputs, not claimed as
  new**.
- The proofs have **not** been independently refereed and have **not** been
  formalized in Lean. The package does not modify or claim to join the
  repository's checked Lean build.
- Maximality concerns the **specified bilateral families under strong Hahn
  summability**, not every imaginable extension or resummation. The article
  proves that those families fail outside `U_q`; it does not prove that no
  other summation rule could assign something there.
- **Hahn partial sums are not claimed to converge in the full surreal
  topology.** A strong sum is not an order limit. The finite-tail bound is a
  statement in the ordered value group, not an all-scale convergence theorem.
- The negative-`j` regime `v(j) < 0` is covered. **No general theorem for the
  good-reduction regime `v(j) >= 0` is asserted.**
- Nondivisible exponent groups are permitted for `E_q`, but a curve with the
  same `j` over such a field can be a nontrivial twist; the report does not
  silently identify all such twists with the split Tate curve.
- The value-circle exact sequence is algebraic and circularly ordered. **No
  unrestricted real-metric Berkovich skeleton theorem is claimed at higher
  rank.**
- Differentiation is with respect to an external variable. It is **not** the
  Berarducci–Mantova derivation on surreal scalars.
- The included computations are finite consistency checks. They do **not**
  prove strong summability, arbitrary-rank surjectivity, an identity at all
  orders, or the class-transport theorem.
- The repository audit was targeted. No line-by-line audit of every manuscript
  in the repository is claimed, and a negative keyword search is treated as a
  navigation aid, not as proof of absence.

## Relation to the neighbouring reports

**`rank-one-berkovich`.** That report fixes the rank-one base `C((t^R))` and is
emphatic about it. Its `prop:rankobstruction` proves that `Q + Q*omega` admits
no order-preserving injective additive map to `R`, so a higher-rank valuation
cannot be silently scalarized: any coarsening landing in `R` must kill a whole
convex layer. The present report is the arbitrary-rank work that report
declines to attempt, and it is about a different object — *a Tate algebra is
not a Tate elliptic curve*. Accordingly the real-valued coarsening used here is
applied only inside `K_{H_alpha}`, where `alpha` is an order unit, and is never
presented as a faithful image of `Gamma`; the scales it discards are recovered
by formal evaluation, not by a norm. A reciprocal pointer was added to
`rank-one-berkovich/article.tex`, minimally and with no label renamed there.

**`entire-functions-at-arbitrary-rank`.** The passage from an order unit to a
complete real-valued non-Archimedean absolute value is already stated in that
report as `ent:lem:coarsening`. This report now **cites it rather than
reproving it**, instantiated at exponent group `H_alpha` with order unit
`alpha`, whose kernel is exactly the subgroup of values infinitesimal relative
to `alpha`.

One hypothesis travels with that citation and is flagged in the text: that
report's standing assumption is a **divisible** exponent group, and this report
assumes divisibility nowhere — its torsion section depends on keeping
nondivisible groups. The proof of `ent:lem:coarsening` there uses only the
order-unit property, so the statement does transfer; because that transfer is
this report's and not the cited one's, the short direct completeness argument
is retained in `tate:lem:rank-one`, explicitly marked as a second proof of a
statement already in the collection rather than as an additional result.

## What was run

- `python code/check_identities.py`, on Python 3.14.4 with SymPy 1.14.0
  (`data/requirements.txt` pins the tested SymPy). It reproduced every value in
  the source package's recorded `verification.json` except the Python version
  string itself; status `PASS`. Its stdout is `data/verification.log` and its
  record is `data/verification.json`. The program uses exact rational symbolic
  arithmetic, with no network access and no floating-point evaluation; it exits
  with an assertion failure if a checked identity is false.

  Checked: the Weierstrass equation, the differential identity `u X' = X + 2Y`,
  and the inversion identity through `q^5`; discriminant against the product
  through `q^9`; `j` through `q^5`; inverse modular composition through `J^6`;
  the local-parameter sign at `q = 0`; theta shift coefficients; explicit
  decreasing-exponent witnesses in a rank-two lexicographic group; and sample
  torsion-degree calculations.

- `latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error article.tex`, which
  completes with **no undefined or multiply-defined reference** and no LaTeX
  warning, after the relabelling. `article.pdf` here is that build, at 27
  pages. The source package's own build record in `data/build_validation.json`
  is preserved unchanged and reports 26 pages; the difference is the
  cross-reference material added when the report joined the collection.

- The same build, run on `rank-one-berkovich/article.tex` after its reciprocal
  pointer was added: no undefined or multiply-defined reference, 31 pages, and
  its label count is unchanged at 86.

Neither build is a mathematical check. A clean compile proves nothing about the
proofs.
