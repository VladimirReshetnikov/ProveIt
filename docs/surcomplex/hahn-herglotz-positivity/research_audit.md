   https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15568

   https://arxiv.org/abs/funct-an/9712004
3. J. Y. Halpern, *Lexicographic probability, conditional probability, and nonstandard probability*, arXiv:cs/0306106v2 (2009).    https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0306106
4. E. Bottazzi, *A real-valued measure on non-Archimedean field extensions of R*, arXiv:2009.14086 (2020). The abstract and scope were used to distinguish its real-valued non-Archimedean-domain measure theory from our ordinary-boundary coefficientwise Hahn category.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.14086
5. M. Restrepo Borrero, K. Shamseddine, *On a Lebesgue-like integral over the Levi-Civita field R*, arXiv:2506.13930v2 (2025). Abstract/scope comparison only; no claim to refute that alternative integration theory.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13930
6. D. Flynn, K. Shamseddine, *On the topological structure of the Hahn field and convergence of power series*, arXiv:1901.09137 (2019). Abstract/scope comparison for the distinction between valuation/order and weaker coefficient-related topologies.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.09137

## What is and is not established

The mathematical arguments in the manuscript establish the stated results subject to their explicit definitions and standard imported background. The computational checks support the finite identities but are not proofs of the all-size or transfinite assertions. No named published open conjecture is claimed solved. No Lean proof has been produced. The exact novelty of the combined package remains subject to further full-text searches and independent review.

The strongest category boundaries are stated repeatedly: ordinary boundary circle, finite ordinary matrix sizes, common well-ordered Hahn supports, coefficientwise signed regular Borel measures, and a distinction between ordinary-domain halos and the full internal disk. The obstruction is not to every possible non-Archimedean notion of measure or spectral representation.
