## Novelty search

Searches included combinations of “Hahn series,” “small divisors,”
“cohomological equation,” “surreal frequency,” and “torus linearization,”
as well as direct searches for the cited primary papers. No exact match to
the package of results developed in this article was located. That limited
negative search result is not proof of originality. In particular, the
ordinary Fourier criterion, positive-support inverses, Liouville construction
method, and formal perturbative philosophy are not claimed as new.

## Verification boundary

The source was compiled repeatedly to stabilize references and contents.
The final PDF has 26 physical pages, including its title page. All pages
were reviewed in rendered contact sheets, with selected mathematical pages
and the final assumption audit inspected at higher resolution. Finite
identities were checked by the supplied exact-arithmetic Python program.
No full theorem was checked in Lean or another proof assistant.
