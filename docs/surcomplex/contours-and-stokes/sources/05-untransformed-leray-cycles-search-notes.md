residues, Leray cycles, ramification, finite analytic maps, and support-controlled
contour representations. The search did not locate a published statement with
the exact arbitrary-support, radius-free, uniform-protecting-cycle hypotheses
proved here. It was targeted, not exhaustive. This negative search result is
not proof of historical priority.

The article's claims rest on its stated hypotheses and proofs. It does not
claim a peer-reviewed result, a formal verification, or resolution of a named
published conjecture. The open question being addressed is explicitly a
