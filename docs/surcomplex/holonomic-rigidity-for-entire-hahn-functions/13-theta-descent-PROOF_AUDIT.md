# Proof audit and mathematical scope

This is an internal proof-dependency audit, not an independent referee report or a formal proof certificate.

## Standing assumptions

The field is K = k((t^Γ)), k has characteristic zero, Γ is a nonzero set-sized ordered abelian group, and increasing Hahn supports are well ordered. The valuation on k is trivial. The parameter is the monomial q = t^h with h > 0. No result silently generalizes q to a parameter with an arbitrary higher-order tail.

Ordinary finite natural numbers index the series and define order units. Every derivative differentiates the external variable, fixing K. All fields of meromorphic functions in the proof are fields of formal functions, not fields of numerical values evaluated at one point.

## 1. Exact coefficients and domain

The Chebyshev identity gives Taylor coefficients with exact leading valuation m^2 h and leading coefficient one. Therefore no coefficient vanishes.

The inclusion part of the domain theorem uses the full positive-support Hahn–Neumann lemma. Writing x = t^(-Nh) a t^β (1 + ε) with β >= 0 puts all terms in one positive support monoid. The number of h-generators bounds the Chebyshev index at every fixed total exponent. This proves both well ordering and finite contributions, including when h is not cofinal.

Outside the asserted domain, the consecutive leading valuations have difference (2m + 1)h + v(x) < 0. A strictly descending sequence belongs to the union of supports, so the evaluation family is not strongly summable. Cancellation after an inadmissible infinite sum cannot repair this.

## 2. Product and zeros

The Jacobi product is classical. The descent x = u + u^(-1) pairs its reciprocal factors. The result is proved first coefficientwise in the formal nome and then specialized using strong support control.

At every allowed argument, the tail of the product is a nonzero unit. Thus a zero must be a zero of one of the explicit finite factors. Each factor is linear in x, its root is distinct from the others, and the complementary product is nonzero at that root. This proves completeness and simplicity of the zero set without requiring algebraic closure of K.

Local perturbation formulas come from the exact product of (1 + e/(r_m-r_k)), with strong support control of the tail. The displayed valuations retain the derivative scale, so a small input error does not imply a small output error uniformly across all roots.

## 3. Third-order identity

The manuscript derives the elliptic identity by a classical pole-cancellation argument, with every normalization constant given explicitly. The nome in the Lambert constants is Q = q^2; confusing Q and q would change the result.

After clearing denominators, the identity belongs to Q[u,u^(-1)][[q]]. Ordinary complex identities force each formal q-coefficient to vanish. Specialization to Hahn coefficients is made only for the cleared expression, avoiding an unjustified claim that the geometric expansion of a reciprocal converges at all arguments.

The substitutions delta x = u-u^(-1), delta(u-u^(-1)) = x, and (u-u^(-1))^2 = x^2-4 give the third-order equation. Its coefficient of the square of the third jet is (x^2-4)^3 times the fourth power of the zeroth jet, so the differential polynomial is nonzero. The cleared relation, unlike its rational presentation, is valid at zeros and at x = ±2.

## 4. Minimal order

This argument assumes h is an order unit and works in a common Laurent-entire function field. Its automorphism sigma scales u by q^2 and commutes with the Euler derivation delta.

For Theta, L = delta Theta / Theta, and A = delta L:

    sigma Theta = q^(-1) u^(-1) Theta,
    sigma L = L - 1,
    sigma A = A.

A simple zero of Theta proves delta A != 0. Three separate minimal-polynomial arguments then give:

1. A algebraic over K(u) would have a sigma-invariant monic minimal polynomial. Its coefficients would be in K, forcing delta A = 0.
2. L algebraic over K(u,A) would force a rational solution to sigma b - b = d for a positive integer d. The constant Laurent coefficient at u = 0 excludes it.
3. Theta algebraic over K(u,A,L) would force sigma b_0 / b_0 = (q^(-1)u^(-1))^d. The left side has u-adic order zero and the right side order -d.

Thus the three functions are algebraically independent. The triangular chain-rule transformation transfers this to F_q, F_q', F_q''. No numerical theta-value transcendence theorem is used.

## 5. Differential field and non-D-finiteness

The cubic R(A)/(x^2-4) has odd pole order at A = infinity over the rational field generated by x, f, and f'/f. It is not a square. This proves degree two in the third jet, while the explicit rational differential system proves closure under all derivatives.

Non-D-finiteness is proved separately: normalized successive Euler derivatives of Theta are monic polynomials of strictly increasing degree in L over the elliptic coefficient field K(A, delta A). Transcendence of L excludes a linear relation. An order-three nonlinear equation by itself would not exclude a higher-order linear equation.

## 6. The no-order-unit implication

This is prior repository material, re-proved independently in exposition rather than imported as an unchecked assertion.

A minimal-total-degree algebraic relation between Euler jets has a nonzero linearization. Its first nonzero coefficient gives a nonzero polynomial I(n) multiplying the nth Taylor coefficient. After finitely many exceptional integer roots of I, all coefficients are recursively in one finitely generated field over k.

Rationally independent values would give algebraically independent elements by the least-valuation argument. Therefore that finitely generated field has finite rational value rank, bounded by one principal convex subgroup. If Γ has no order unit, an exterior value dominates that entire subgroup. Evaluating there gives descending leading valuations unless the series is a polynomial.

This reasoning uses the Euler derivation precisely because it does not lower formal x-order. It does not replace nonlinear convolution by an invalid bounded-step linear recurrence.

## 7. Surreal and omnific specialization

The embedding sends t^γ to omega^(-γ). With Γ = R and q = omega^(-1), the construction is entire in that one Hahn field. At x = omega^omega the leading exponents of its evaluation terms increase strictly in Conway convention; strong summability fails in the full surreal class.

The zero r_m = -omega^(2m-1) - omega^(-(2m-1)) has a nonzero infinitesimal tail and is not an omnific integer. Its omnific floor and ceiling are explicit. “No omnific zero” means no zero in the actual evaluation domain; the function is not assigned values outside that domain. This is not a claim about polynomial omnific Diophantine equations.

## Finite checks

The delivered run passed 1,622 exact checks. The product and the third-order equation have zero residual in all formal q-coefficients below 128, as exact polynomials in x. This checks finite algebraic consistency only. It is not a proof of arbitrary-support summability, transcendence, minimal order, or the full zero set.

## Remaining uncertainty and non-claims

The arguments have not been independently refereed or Lean-verified. Classical theta differential relations are explicitly credited. Priority for the precise arbitrary-rank descent, existence classification, and minimal-order proof is provisional, not certified. The manuscript does not classify all entire differentially algebraic functions, does not rule out another example of minimal order two, and does not claim a nonpolynomial strongly entire function on all of No(i).
