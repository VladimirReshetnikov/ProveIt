# Proof audit and mathematical scope

Internal audit of the argument, not an independent referee report or a formal proof certificate.

## Standing hypotheses

K = C((t^R)); the real-coefficient version works as well. Increasing Hahn supports are
well ordered. Every nonzero coefficient in the ordinary constant field has valuation zero.
All derivatives differentiate the external variable X and fix K. The theta nome is the
monomial q = t^h with h a positive ordinary real number. No derivative acts intrinsically
on the surreal coefficients.

## Dependency and novelty separation

The repository's `13-theta-descent` companion already proves the theta seed's exact
coefficient valuations, entire property, a third-order equation, and minimal order three.
None of those conclusions is claimed newly discovered. The exact-order appendix is a
separate proof of that existing conclusion. The proposed contributions concern independent
scales, polynomial jet compression, unbounded order, the generated field's transcendence
degree, and sampling consequences.

## 1. Strong summability

Valuations tending to +infinity give well-ordered union support and finite contributions
at each exponent. For products, the two weighted coefficient sequences are bounded below
and tend to infinity; therefore only finitely many pairs lie below each fixed bound.
This justifies convolution and regrouping. Conversely, failure of the cofinal-valuation
criterion at r produces leading exponents unbounded below at the monomial t^(-(r+1)).
The strong-evaluation convention and the cofinal-valuation criterion are therefore equivalent
in the fixed real-exponent Hahn field.

## 2. Gauss multiplication and independent growth

At every radius t^(-r), normalization followed by residue gives a nonzero finite polynomial.
The product of the two initial polynomials is nonzero, proving exact multiplicativity.
A polynomial coefficient in X has eventually affine Gauss valuation even when its
coefficients are arbitrary elements of K. The seed has exact profile

    w_r(F_h) = -r^2/(4h) + h*dist(r/(2h), Z_>=0)^2  (r >= 0).

The error is between zero and h/4. Independent reciprocal scales make the quadratic
coefficients of distinct monomials distinct. A unique smallest valuation prevents
cancellation. Failure of reciprocal independence is not asserted to imply dependence.

The quantitative threshold compares a positive quadratic gap with a linear term and a
bounded error. The extra +1 makes the inequalities strict, including ties in coefficient
or nearest-integer comparisons.

## 3. Compression

The universal derivation d is relative to the differential base field F; it is not the
external derivative partial. In particular d(p_i(X)) = 0 for weights in F. The coefficient-
to-jet transformation for degree < N polynomials is triangular and invertible over F,
with factorial diagonal entries. Arbitrary formal jet specialization tests polynomial
nonvanishing; it is not asserted to be an actual choice of independently prescribed
polynomial derivatives as functions of X.

Under the triangular jet specialization, the coefficient of lambda_1*...*lambda_N in

dg wedge d(g') wedge ... wedge d(g^(N-1))

is exactly da_1 wedge ... wedge da_N. Its unique assignment is what prevents cancellation.
A nonzero scalar coordinate of this exterior-valued polynomial has total degree N.
The finite-grid root bound then supplies one actual integer coefficient array. At least
one array works; there is no claim that a preselected array works, and no dependence
recognition algorithm is supplied.

## 4. Minimal order and finite composita

In characteristic zero, a least-order relation has a nonzero separant at the algebraic
last jet when written as its minimal polynomial. Differentiating solves for the next jet
inside the field generated by the existing finite jets. This proves ordinary field
finite generation and identifies minimal differential order with transcendence degree.

The upper bound 3N uses only differential algebraicity of each theta seed at order at most
three. The lower bound N is the compression conclusion. Joint independence of all 3N seed
jets is not assumed. The actual compositum dimension d_N can be realized as an exact order
by compressing a transcendence basis drawn from the first three jets of each seed, but d_N
is not evaluated beyond N <= d_N <= 3N. The remaining extension is finite algebraic, not
asserted trivial.

## 5. Exact-order-three appendix

The Laurent-entire fraction field is defined before the automorphism is used. Its radius-one
Gauss valuation is multiplicative and has residue characteristic zero. The commuting shift
acts by sigma Theta = q^(-1)z^(-1)Theta, sigma G = G-1, sigma H = H. The nonzero derivative
of H proves that H is transcendental over the constant field. A nonzero polynomial evaluated
at ordinary integers has eventually constant valuation because its residue polynomial has
only finitely many roots.

After a large shift, the valuations of finitely many grouped terms are

    -i*h*m^2 + 2*k*h*m + constant.

Largest i and then smallest k selects a unique smallest term. The chain-rule transformation
back to f,f',f'' is invertible over K(z). The proof concerns functions, not numerical values.

## 6. Classical input

The theta product and Tate cubic identity are imported classical mathematics, not established
by the finite checks alone. The manuscript states the exact normalization. The nome of the
Lambert sums is Q=q^2, not q. It shows the denominator-clearing and chain-rule steps explicitly.
The final third-order polynomial has leading third-jet coefficient (X^2-4)^3*f^4, which is
nonzero for the seed. The Tate coefficient signs and differential normalization were checked
against the cited course notes and by exact truncated polynomial arithmetic.

## 7. Cardinality and sampling

A positive Hamel basis gives continuum many independent reciprocal scales; this uses choice,
not CH or a large-cardinal axiom. Every well-ordered real support is countable, giving the
matching cardinality upper bound for K and its power-series ring. Every element of the field
generated by all DA entire functions lies in a finite DA compositum, so differential
transcendence degree is zero despite ordinary transcendence degree continuum.

For sampling, reduction of a normalized nonzero entire function yields a nonzero finite
polynomial over C. Its ordinary constant zeros contain all the ordinary constant zeros of
the entire function. The fixed-polynomial quantifier is essential: point values belong to K
and cannot be algebraically independent over K. No accumulation argument in surreal topology
is used.

## 8. Full-surreal boundary

The map t^a -> omega^(-a) embeds the fixed field. At X=omega^omega, leading exponents
n*omega-h*n^2 strictly increase, violating reverse well ordering. Thus the entire-function
claims do not transfer to all of No or No(i). No invariant of the intrinsic Berarducci–Mantova
derivation is asserted.

## Finite evidence

The delivered standard-library verifier passed all checks in `verification.json`, including
128 exact q-coefficient comparisons for each of the product and the differential identity,
852 Gauss-profile cases, and 65 irrational-scale dominance cases. Infinite independence,
summability for arbitrary supports, cardinality, and the general compression lemma are proved
in the manuscript, not certified by the program.

## Remaining uncertainty

No independent referee or Lean verification was obtained. A bibliographic antecedent of one
of the proposed results may exist. Exact order two, the complete order spectrum, d_N=3N,
efficient selection of a good candidate, and smaller-exponent-group analogues remain open
within this article's research program.
