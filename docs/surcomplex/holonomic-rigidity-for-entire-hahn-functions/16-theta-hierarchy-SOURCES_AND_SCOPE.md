## A discovery correctly excluded from the new claims

An initial direction was theta descent as a counterexample to order-unit nonlinear rigidity.
The direct directory read exposed a newer companion that already contained that result,
including exact minimal order three. The project overview had not yet fully caught up.
Accordingly, the new article expressly credits the seed and develops a different theorem
package: independent scale families, bounded polynomial jet compression, and unbounded
minimal differential order. It does not claim to answer the already-answered existence
question a second time.

## What remains unestablished bibliographically

No exhaustive MathSciNet/zbMATH review or complete literature/citation-network search was
performed. Some keyword searches were irrelevant; those searches supply no evidence of
novelty. In particular, finite-jet compression has a natural relationship to classical
primitive-element methods, so related formulations may already occur in that literature.

The article's claim is a precise theorem package with full proofs of its proposed
contributions and explicitly identified classical/repository inputs. Priority is provisional.
No named historical conjecture is claimed solved. The entire-function order-two problem and
the joint 3N-jet independence question are not solved here.
