## Primary mathematical sources consulted

1. Alessandro Berarducci and Vincenzo Mantova,
   *Surreal numbers, derivations and transseries*, JEMS 20 (2018), 339–390.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.00315
   Full text: https://arxiv.org/html/1503.00315v3
   The paper's normalized derivation, strong additivity, exponential
   compatibility, constants, H-field rule, and surjectivity are the main
   established differential inputs. Its normal-form and log-atomic
   discussions also support the specified Hahn environments.

2. Marc Paul Noordman, Marius van der Put, and Jaap Top,
   *Autonomous first order differential equations*,
   Transactions of the AMS 375 (2022).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.08152
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.08152
   Publisher record:
   https://www.ams.org/tran/2022-375-03/S0002-9947-2022-08515-9/
   The 2019 arXiv v1 was read for the geometric equation framework,
   Section 6 local formal normal forms, and the Rosenlicht algebraic
   independence result. Numbered references in article.tex are explicitly
   pinned to that version, not assumed identical to the journal numbering.

3. J. S. Milne, *Abelian Varieties*, version 2.00, March 16, 2008.
   https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/AV.pdf
   Standard setting for the abelian-variety discussion. The particular
   formal-logarithm proof needed for this manuscript is supplied in the
   manuscript, rather than attributed to an unchecked numbered theorem.

4. The Stacks Project, Section 29.43, Tag 0BX4, valuative criteria.
   https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BX4
   Standard properness interpretation of projective reduction. The article
   supplies an elementary homogeneous-coordinate reduction proof.

## Novelty boundary

Searches combining surreal derivations, autonomous equations, projective
curves, and abelian varieties did not provide a usable primary source stating
the exact combined classification and derivation-independence results.
Some keyword results were irrelevant; these are not evidence of absence.
The reviewed sources establish important ingredients, so the paper does not
claim invention of the local normal forms or formal-group logarithms.

The newness assessment is limited to the reviewed material. The results are
candidate new ambient realization and image theorems, with proofs offered
for mathematical review. A specialist may identify earlier equivalents or
stronger results. No certified literature-wide priority claim is made.
