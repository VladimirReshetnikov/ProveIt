This is a scope-based topic selection, not a line-by-line proof audit of the
fifteen packages or all their archived source manuscripts. Their own draft status
and their explicit non-claims remain in force. The article does not rely on an
unverified theorem from a companion draft: the finite-phase decomposition needed
for the differential argument is proved again from the stated algebraic and
summation inputs.

## Primary mathematical inputs

The standalone bibliography identifies the precise versions used. In particular:

- Berarducci–Mantova, *Surreal numbers, derivations and transseries*,
  arXiv:1503.00315v3, Theorems A and B; published JEMS 20 (2018), 339–390.
- Aschenbrenner–van den Dries–van der Hoeven, *The surreal numbers as a universal
  H-field*, arXiv:1512.02267v3, Theorem 1.
- Berarducci–Mantova, *Transseries as germs of surreal functions*,
  arXiv:1703.01995v3, the formal-series material and Theorem 8.4.
- Ehrlich–Kaplan, *Surreal ordered exponential fields*, arXiv:2002.07739v3,
  for the existence of surcomplex exponential constructions with different
  structural contracts.
- Bournez–Guilmant, *Surreal fields stable under exponential, logarithmic,
  derivative and anti-derivative functions*, arXiv:2211.08396, as further
  literature on specialized closed subfields.

The article separates these published/preprint inputs from the proofs supplied
in the new exposition. Its statements are not presented as resolutions of named
open conjectures or as established priority claims.
