# Global Hahn Support and the Surcomplex Riemann–Hilbert Problem

**Noncommutative monodromy, countably many singularities, and an exact realization obstruction**

Research manuscript, September 21, 2026. The PDF is 25 pages.

## Main result

Let D be a closed discrete subset of the ordinary complex plane, let U = C \ D,
and let K = C((t^Gamma)) be a set-sized Hahn field. For a representation
rho: pi_1(U,p) -> I_r + M_r(m_Gamma), the following are equivalent:

1. The union of the Hahn supports of rho(ell_d) - I_r, over all based meridians,
   is well ordered.
2. The representation is the monodromy of a positive globally common-domain
   Hahn-supported holomorphic matrix connection on U.
3. Such a connection can be chosen with all coefficients meromorphic on C,
   having at most simple poles in D and no other finite poles.

A fixed complex-linear Mittag–Leffler period section selects one unique
normalized realizer, with support in the additive monoid generated by the
prescribed monodromy support. The article proves framed gauge classification,
entire extension of comparison gauges in the logarithmic subclass, a local
positive Frobenius factorization, and an SL_r refinement. It also proves that
an unrestricted infinite-puncture period right inverse cannot be both linear
and continuous in the natural product/compact-open topologies.

The examples distinguish individual Hahn validity, global well-ordering, and
point-finiteness across punctures. A noncommuting two-puncture example works
also in the higher-rank value group Q + Q*omega.

## Run the checks

Use a Python environment with NumPy, SciPy, and SymPy. The exact tested
versions are recorded in the JSON output and requirements file.

```sh
python -m pip install -r requirements-tested.txt
python verify_examples.py
```

The script prints its results and overwrites `verification_results.json` in
its own directory. Eight exact algebraic assertions and four numerical
coefficient-ODE error assertions passed. Numerical maximum entrywise errors
were below 1.5e-13 in the recorded run; the assertions use a looser 2e-9
threshold to avoid depending on platform-specific last digits.

The numerical calculation integrates the coefficient equations through total
bivariate degree two. It does not substitute floating-point surrogates for
infinitesimal or infinite Hahn monomials.

## Status and limitations

This is a proposed original, AI-assisted research contribution in one
specified Hahn-coherent category. The targeted literature search did not
identify the exact global noncommutative theorem, but does not certify
priority. No named published conjecture is claimed solved. The manuscript
has not been refereed or formally verified in a proof assistant.

The main theorems are proved in the article; they do not depend on the
numerical tests. The tests establish only the displayed finite-example
identities and floating-point agreement with their coefficient formulas.

The results concern positive Hahn perturbations of the trivial connection,
ordinary complex base domains and ordinary coefficientwise paths. They do
not assert an unrestricted classical Riemann–Hilbert theorem, a theory of
fine-topological surcomplex loops, or convergence of Hahn partial sums in the
fine topology. Infinite-puncture realizers have no imposed growth condition
at infinity. The algebraic period section for arbitrary infinite data is not
claimed computable or continuous.
