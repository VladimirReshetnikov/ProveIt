Research continuation prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov, 18 September 2026.

## Main results

Write D_lambda for the cofinal subsets of lambda of order type omega, and Q_lambda for their quotient by finite symmetric difference. All cardinalities are ambient cardinalities, and OD_{V_lambda} permits ordinal parameters and individual parameters from V_lambda.

The central result is Corollary 5.2: at an ultraexacting lambda, every OD_{V_lambda} complete section T of Q_lambda has a class q for which |T intersect q| = lambda. Thus ultraexactingness is inconsistent with an admissibly definable complete section whose every fiber is smaller than lambda. The endpoint is sharp because every equivalence class has exactly lambda members.

Theorem 5.1 also excludes a nonempty OD_{V_lambda} family of fewer than lambda such thin sections. Its members need not be individually definable, and the fiber bounds may vary.

Lemma 6.1 canonically extracts a cofinal sequence from a countably additive probability law on increasing cofinal sequences. Theorem 6.5 uses this to exclude small definable families of classwise probability kernels, even when the measures are defined only on the sigma-algebras generated by coordinate cylinders. A finitely additive counterexample explains exactly why countable additivity is needed.

Additional results are the bounded-rank separation characterization in Theorem 7.1, the fixed cofinal-output-code obstruction in Section 8, and the I0-equiconsistent boundary package in Theorem 9.1.

## Logical and research status

The paper gives conventional proofs of conditional inconsistency statements. It does not claim an unconditional contradiction from exactingness, ultraexactingness, or I0. Ordinary representatives and Dirac kernels still exist by Choice; their restricted definability is what is excluded.

The equiconsistency calibration imports the published ultraexacting/I0 equiconsistency and HOD-coding forcing of Aguilera, Bagaria, Goldberg, and Luecke. It is not presented as a new consistency-strength determination for ultraexactingness. The other imported interfaces and their uses are recorded in Sections 2 and 10.

The results are offered as extensions relative to the supplied research, not as a verified claim of literature priority. They have not been independently refereed or machine-formalized. The inherited binary-selector problem and the cover-exacting/strongly-compact questions are not claimed to be resolved.

## Rebuilding

With a TeX installation providing pdfLaTeX, New PX, AMS math, tcolorbox, hyperref, and the other standard packages named in the preamble, run:

```sh
sh build.sh
```

The script writes intermediate files to `_build/` and replaces the PDF beside the source after a successful build. Shell escape is disabled. Some TeX installations emit a harmless `epstopdf` warning about shell escape; this paper contains no EPS figures and needs no external conversion.

Alternatively, run pdfLaTeX on the source three times to resolve the table of contents, citations, and cross-references.
