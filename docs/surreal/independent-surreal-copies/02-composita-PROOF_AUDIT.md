# Proof audit and review priorities

This is an author-side audit, not an independent referee report.

## 1. Exact coefficient-rank criterion

The coefficient space is taken over the base field `k`, not over `F`. Each basis coordinate series has support contained in the original support. The tensor image is the **ring** of finite coefficient rank; the compositum is its **fraction field**. Infinite coefficient rank alone is not an obstruction to compositum membership, as the geometric-series example proves.

## 2. Two base extensions

Coefficient extension uses coefficientwise linear independence of constants. Intermediate base change requires clearing denominators in the ring generated by the intermediate field and the other factor. Exponent-group extension uses coset slices, not an unjustified claim that arbitrary base extension preserves algebraic independence. No convexity is required for the coset-slice lemma, but the surreal iterated-coefficient presentation does require the explicit scale separation.

## 3. Rapid coefficient-degree theorem — primary review target

Critical hypotheses: characteristic zero; a transcendental coefficient variable; unbounded ratios `d_n/d_(n-1)`; infinitely many private indices relative to each finite subfamily.

Critical steps:

- Choose a nonzero relation of least total jet degree.
- Clear all coefficient denominators in the variable `x`, then remove negative outer exponents by a common power of `t`.
- At least one formal partial derivative is nonzero; its evaluation is nonzero by minimality.
- Let `ell` be the least outer valuation of a nonzero evaluated partial.
- Choose a private index `n > ell` outside the finitely many roots of the resulting polynomial in the integer eigenvalue `n`.
- All quadratic tail terms have outer order at least `2n`, strictly larger than `n+ell`.
- All partial coefficients below `ell` vanish, excluding contributions from later tail coefficients.
- The surviving term has coefficient degree at least `d_n`, while the truncated term has degree at most `D+d*d_(n-1)`.

The argument does not require sparse outer support. It does require a uniform finite degree in `x` after denominators are cleared.

## 4. Private coefficient blocks — primary review target

A finitely generated coefficient field is algebraic over a field generated by finitely many members of a transcendence basis. Coordinate derivations outside that finite set extend over the ambient algebraic extension because characteristic zero implies separability. These auxiliary derivations need not preserve any **inner** surreal summation; they are extended coefficientwise only in the explicitly used **outer** Hahn representation. This distinction is important.

They kill every coefficient of the chosen relation, including all the coefficients of each Hahn-series parameter. Their response to a private coefficient at outer position `n` is `n^j t^(nh)` on the `j`th Euler jet. A finite Vandermonde argument then kills all evaluated partials, contradicting minimal total jet degree.

The class version never chooses a class transcendence basis. A proposed relation is restricted to the set field generated by its coefficients and the countably many coefficient labels in its finitely many witness series.

## 5. Exact cardinality

Upper bound: the set of Laurent series has cardinality `|F|^(aleph_0)`. If `|F| <= continuum`, the rapid-degree family attains this bound. If `|F| > continuum >= |k|`, cardinal counting forces a transcendence basis of `F/k` to have cardinality `|F|`, and the prefix tree attains the bound.

The statement requires positive coefficient transcendence degree. Finite algebraic coefficient extensions give equality of the fields instead. Infinite algebraic coefficient extensions and very large base fields with small positive transcendence degree are not covered. In characteristic `p`, the Euler identity `partial^p=partial` explicitly rules out this differential-independence conclusion.

## 6. Explicit omnific witnesses

There are two normal-form levels. The `g_(alpha,n)` are inner monomials in `(0,1)`; the `a_(alpha,n)=omega^(g_(alpha,n))` are independent outer coefficient monomials. Ordinal arithmetic is used only for the injective label `omega*alpha+n`.

The shift exponent `b=omega^(15/4)` dominates every finite multiple of `h=omega^(7/2)`. Both dominate every lower-group contribution. Thus every witness exponent `b-nh+g_(alpha,n)` is positive, and consecutive exponents strictly decrease. This proves the actual omnific support assertion, not merely an asymptotic analogy.

The Euler derivation has weight `-lambda(g_1)` in Conway notation, so the `n`th witness term has eigenvalue `n`. The shift multiplier has derivative zero. This derivation is not the Berarducci–Mantova derivation.

## 7. Set-parameter resilience — primary review target

The supports of a set of parameters are sets at both normal-form levels. Their inner support union `T` determines a set-sized coefficient field `C_T`. Injectivity of the compression map makes its forbidden ordinal labels a bounded set. The tail coefficient exponents are rationally independent **modulo** the group supported on `T`; polynomial monomials therefore have disjoint support cosets over `C_T`.

The larger coefficient envelope over `C_T` contains both copy fields, all parameters, and all their Euler derivatives. Applying the local block theorem proves independence over the desired differential parameter field. No proper-class parameter set is allowed.

## 8. Arithmetic and complex conclusions

Monomial denominator clearing gives the fraction fields of the two copy rings and of the full arithmetic join. A witness transcendental over `M(S)` cannot be integral over `B[S]`. This does not compute normalization inside `M`.

Complex coefficients split into real and imaginary parts. The Euler derivation kills `i`, and multiplication by `1+i` is an invertible constant substitution on jets. Set-parameter resilience in the complexification follows by collecting real and imaginary parts of a set of parameters.

## Verification boundary

The accompanying 2,530 checks concern finite identities and finite ordered-group models. They are not a proof certificate for any infinite theorem. The article's proofs remain subject to independent review and eventual formalization.
