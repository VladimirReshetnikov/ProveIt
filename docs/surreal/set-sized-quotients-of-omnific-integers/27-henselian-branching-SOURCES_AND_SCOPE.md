## Proposed original contribution

The proposed new part is the maximal-branching construction for actual induced
omnific integer parts, especially the proof that every finite branch survives
localization; its combination with forced complexification, exact cardinality,
completion invisibility, and explicit strict/Gaussian generic fibers.

Standard henselian algebra and general Boolean-power consequences are credited
as such. The result is not claimed to resolve a named published open conjecture.
The 12 research questions are proposed directions, not an assertion that all
are historically recognized and currently unsolved.
