## Literature

The article's embedded bibliography contains 25 entries, including four
repository references. Primary mathematical constructions consulted include
Berarducci–Mantova's differential field and surreal-function work,
Ehrlich–Kaplan's exponential and trigonometric constructions, and
Costin–Ehrlich's integration theory (arXiv:2208.14331v5).

The Ehrlich–Kaplan published erratum was checked: it concerns in-text reference
numbering. The article uses the usual cos(b) + i sin(b) convention in its global
exponential formula, not the reversed pair in one displayed preprint formula.

The direct surreal-singularity proposal examined is J. A. Nieto,
arXiv:1611.09699. The relevant PDF pages were inspected. The critique concerns
its inference from admitting infinite scalars to removal of a singularity, not
an asserted impossibility of all future surreal-valued physical models.

Relativity and field-theory sources include Penrose, Raychaudhuri, Witten,
Hayward, Carballo-Rubio et al., Andersson–Rendall, Dafermos, Dafermos–Luk,
Donoghue, Hollands–Wald, and Steinbauer–Vickers. Source hypotheses and regularity
qualifications are retained; no current universal result about all black-hole
interiors is claimed.

The search was targeted rather than exhaustive. Statements about the lack of
a demonstrated physical completion concern the sources examined, not every
possible publication or future theory.

## Verification and status

The article contains explicit proofs of its elementary algebraic,
formal-geometric, and compatibility results, with no claim of research priority.
The companion program checks finite identities only. It is not a proof assistant
formalization or a construction of a surreal-valued physical universe.
