## What follows and what does not

Combining the older common-information theorem with the column criterion
already gives a negative coarse answer to C1. The research note spells out
that implication. Accordingly, the result is not presented as a new discovery
of the negative answer to a genuinely unresolved conjecture.

The direct finite-extension proof, exact representative spectra, equal-jump
pair construction, countable extension, and order comparison are proved in
the note. Their precise historical priority has not been established.
There is no claim of exhaustive literature novelty review or specialist
acceptance.

The general effective-dense question is not answered. For the particular
column family used here, its effective-dense spectrum is an ordinary upper
Turing cone and therefore does have a least degree.
