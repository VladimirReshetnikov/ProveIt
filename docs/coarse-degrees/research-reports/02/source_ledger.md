Inspected Definitions 2.3–2.4 and Question 7. The latter explicitly asks for

## Primary source: Hirschfeldt–Jockusch–Kuyper–Schupp

Denis R. Hirschfeldt, Carl G. Jockusch Jr., Rutger Kuyper, and Paul E. Schupp,
*Coarse Reducibility and Algorithmic Randomness*, Journal of Symbolic Logic
81(3) (2016), 1028–1046, DOI 10.1017/jsl.2015.70.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.01707
- https://arxiv.org/html/1505.01707
- https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~drh/Papers/coarsereducibility.html
- https://doi.org/10.1017/jsl.2015.70

Inspected Theorem 4.2: for a 1-generic X, only computable sets are computed by
every coarse description of X. The author and publisher records corroborate
the 2016 journal publication. The original arXiv posting is 7 May 2015; a date
printed by an automatically generated HTML representation is not substituted
for publication metadata.

Deduction made in the new manuscript: a least coarse representative would be
computed by every coarse description, hence would be computable; this would
make X coarsely computable, contradicting genericity. Thus the basic negative
answer is already implied by this older result. The new construction does not
import Theorem 4.2 as a dependency.

## Search scope and what it does not establish

Targeted searches included combinations of “Turing minimal pair” with “coarse”,
“coarsely equal”, “symmetric difference”, “generic”, “density zero”, and
“logarithmic”, together with searches for coarse classes and least Turing
degrees. Results repeatedly located the HJKS paper and work on minimal pairs
in the *coarse or generic degrees*. Those are not the same assertion as a
*Turing* minimal pair whose members have the same coarse degree.

No inspected source was found stating the exact combination in the present
construction: arbitrary computable unbounded monotone disagreement budget,
individual relative 1-genericity, relative Turing minimal-pair property, and
witnesses below the double jump. This is a limited search result, not a proof
of novelty or of prior open status. The spectral refinements are also supplied
with proofs but without priority claims.
