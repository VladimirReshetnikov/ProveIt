# Literature positioning audit

Date of search: 20 September 2026.

## Primary sources inspected

Janusz Brzozowski, Galina Jirásková, Bo Liu, Aayush Rajasekaran, Marek Szykuła,
*On the State Complexity of the Shuffle of Regular Languages*, DCFS 2016,
LNCS 9777, pp. 73–86. Expanded preprint arXiv:1512.01187v3 (15 July 2016).

https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.01187v3
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-41114-9_6

Relevant locations in the 15-page preprint: the abstract; Table 1 on PDF page 6;
Lemmas 8 and 9 on PDF pages 7–8; Theorem 10 on PDF page 8; Lemma 12 and
Corollary 13 on PDF pages 9–10. The paper reports tightness for one dimension
at most five (with both at least two), and the 6-by-6 case. Table 1 marks 6-by-7
and larger six-row cases as unresolved there. Its containment and isolated-cell
reductions are prior ingredients, not inventions of this note.

Pascal Caron, Jean-Gabriel Luque, Bruno Patrou, *A combinatorial approach for the
state complexity of the Shuffle product*, arXiv:1905.08120 (20 May 2019).

https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08120

The paper reformulates the conjecture through intersections of partitions.
Its concluding reduction to “dense” states concerns incomparability of row and
column supports, not numerical graph density. The present note does not claim
to have introduced the general idea of reducing to incomparable supports.

## Search limitation

Searches used these titles and combinations of shuffle, state complexity,
reachability, conjecture, six rows, arbitrary columns, 2025, and 2026. Work on
commutative languages and literal shuffle was not treated as a solution to the
unrestricted ordinary-shuffle problem. No prior six-by-arbitrary resolution was
located in this search. Some broad/date-qualified query results were irrelevant
and were not used as evidence.

This audit establishes the source of the selected question and the earlier
reported cases. It does not prove present-day priority, exhaustiveness of the
search, or the absence of unpublished or unindexed solutions. The result should
be independently checked and its priority investigated before publication.
