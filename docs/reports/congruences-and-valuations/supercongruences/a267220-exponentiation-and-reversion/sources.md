# Sources and attribution

Access date: 20 September 2026.

## OEIS target

**The OEIS Foundation Inc., A267220.**
https://oeis.org/A267220

The formula section contains two conjectures contributed by Peter Bala on 17 October 2024, one concerning `[x^n]A(x)^(m*n)` and one concerning the analogous powers of the reverted series `F`. The required modulus is `p^(2*r)` for primes at least 5, every integer parameter m, and positive n and r. These entries were still labeled conjectures when checked. Our article uses G in place of the entry's A.

An earlier integrality comment in the same entry already cites a proof by Beukers. It is not one of our selected conjectures. The live entry can be edited after the access date; no update has been submitted as part of this work.

## Prior framing theory

**Albert Schwarz, Vadim Vologodsky, and Johannes Walcher. _Framing the Di-Logarithm (over Z)._** arXiv:1306.4298 (2013).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.4298

**Albert Schwarz, Vadim Vologodsky, and Johannes Walcher. _Integrality of Framing and Geometric Origin of 2-functions (with algebraic coefficients)._** arXiv:1702.07135v2, 6 March 2017.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.07135
https://arxiv.org/html/1702.07135v2

These are the prior sources for the framing principle. The article gives its needed rational-coefficient calculation in full, tracks its own sign convention, and handles the unsigned two-adic loss explicitly. It does not claim discovery of framing or of the general theory of 2-functions.

## Apéry arithmetic background

**Ji-Cai Liu. _An extension of Gauss congruences for Apéry numbers._** arXiv:2404.16636v1, 25 April 2024.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16636
https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16636v1

The introduction records the standard recurrence, binomial-sum definition, connection with Apéry's irrationality proof, and the historical order-three results of Gessel and Coster. Those stronger congruences provide context, not an unproved dependency of the present argument: the required order-two seed statement is proved directly in section 3.

## Status of the application

The theorem proved in the article implies the two formulas currently labeled conjectures in the selected entry. This is a proof of those claims, but the source review is not an exhaustive priority investigation. The article and its computational artifacts are unrefereed and not proof-assistant verified. No third-party source text or full paper is redistributed in this archive.
