# Source and priority audit

Access date: **19 September 2026**.

## The selected conjecture

**OEIS A000139**, https://oeis.org/A000139

The entry defines a(n)=2(3n)!/((2n+1)!(n+1)!) and records a parity conjecture
attributed to Peter Bala, dated 24 July 2025. The proposed odd indices are
exactly the members of A022341. That conjecture wording remained in the entry
inspected during this work.

**Internal-format entry**, https://oeis.org/A000139/internal

The entry-level version metadata was revision 297, dated 12 May 2026,
15:53:24. This is distinct from the site-wide footer's modification date.
The same entry already credits the representation A=T(3-T), T=1+xT^3 to
Peter Bala, 5 February 2022. That representation is background, not a novelty
claim of the present article.

**OEIS A022341**, https://oeis.org/A022341

This defines the odd fibbinary numbers: odd positive integers without adjacent
binary ones. The first indices in the article were checked against this
entry. Fibonacci enumeration of such digit languages is classical.

## Primary supporting literature

Linus Lindroos, Andrew Sills, and Hua Wang,
*Odd fibbinary numbers and the golden ratio*, Fibonacci Quarterly 52(1)
(2014), 61–65.
https://www.fq.math.ca/Papers1/52-1/LindroosSillsWang.pdf

The paper supports terminology and background on odd fibbinary numbers.
It is not cited as proving the A000139 assertion or the valuation results.

Wenjie Fang, *Fighting fish and two-stack sortable permutations*,
Séminaire Lotharingien de Combinatoire 80B (2018), Article 7, 12 pages.
https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/wpapers/FPSAC2018/07-Fang.pdf
Full preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05713

This supplies the combinatorial context and the fighting-fish bijection.
Two-stack sorting here means two successive applications of the deterministic
stack-sorting map, not unconstrained operation of two stacks.

Philippe Flajolet and Robert Sedgewick, *Analytic Combinatorics*,
Cambridge University Press, 2009.
Authors' book page: https://algo.inria.fr/flajolet/Publications/books.html

This is general background on rational generating functions and analytic
limit laws. The specific spectral and Fourier arguments needed in this
article are supplied explicitly.

The two research PDFs above were inspected, including their rendered first
pages. No third-party paper, book, or font file is redistributed here.

## What the search does and does not establish

Targeted searches used combinations of A000139, parity, valuation, odd
indices, fibbinary, two-stack-sortable permutations, and fighting fish.
No prior resolution of this exact assertion was located in the retrieved
material. This is not a comprehensive search of all publications, theses,
preprints, OEIS edit histories, or unpublished correspondence.

Accordingly the justified claim is: the article proves an assertion still
recorded as conjectural in the inspected entry. It is not justified to claim
that the assertion was definitely unknown to every researcher, or that this
is the first-ever proof. Priority of the stronger distribution identities
also remains unverified.

No change was submitted to OEIS, and no external publication or communication
was sent on the user's behalf.
