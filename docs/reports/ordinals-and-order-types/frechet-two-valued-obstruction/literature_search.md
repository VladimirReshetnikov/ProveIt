Research date: **20 September 2026**.

## Primary source establishing the problem

Guillaume Massas, *A Semi-Constructive Approach to the Hyperreal Line*,
Australasian Journal of Logic 20(3) (2023), 490–536, article 6.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.26686/ajl.v20i3.8254
- Journal record: https://ojs.victoria.ac.nz/ajl/article/view/8254
- Journal PDF: https://ojs.victoria.ac.nz/ajl/article/download/8254/7451/12179
- Earlier preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.10818

Definitions 5.4, 5.5, and 5.7 on printed p. 515 give the filter category,
covering sieves, and reduced-power presheaf. Fact 5.10 on pp. 516–517 proves
non-sheafness when the value set admits an injection from 2^ω. The paragraph
immediately after its proof says that choice permits an extension to all
uncountable structures and asks whether that condition is necessary.

The actual published PDF was read, including images of the relevant pages.
Printed p. 517 is PDF page index 27 (zero-based). The present manuscript uses
exactly this category and dense topology, not a different filter-base site.

## What this record does and does not establish

It establishes a genuine, specifically stated published open problem and a
2024 repetition by the same author. It records a targeted search for a later
answer as of the research date.

It does **not** establish priority, exclude unpublished solutions, certify
that all relevant literature was indexed, or replace an expert review of
the proof. The finite-pasting, Boolean-algebra, and sheafification extensions
may overlap standard general results; they are presented with complete proofs
rather than claims of priority for the underlying techniques.

The counterexample and classification in the manuscript are the mathematical
output of this research attempt. No author was contacted and no submission
or external publication was made. Source PDFs are not redistributed in the
archive.
