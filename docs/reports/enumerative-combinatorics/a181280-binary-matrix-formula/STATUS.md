# Source and research-status audit

Audit date: 19 September 2026.

## Selected statement

The target is the explicit formula in Conjecture 20, Section 6.5, of:

Manuel Kauers and Christoph Koutschan, *Some D-Finite and Some Possibly D-Finite
Sequences in the OEIS*, Journal of Integer Sequences 26 (2023), Article 23.4.5.
The author version is arXiv:2303.02793v2, dated 24 April 2023.

Primary sources consulted:

- https://oeis.org/A181280
- https://arxiv.org/html/2303.02793v2
- https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL26/Koutschan/kout4.html
- https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/expmath/kauers25.pdf
- https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/expmath/archive25.html

The OEIS entry attributes the sequence to R. H. Hardin (10 October 2010) and the
formula to Kauers and Koutschan (2 March 2023). The formula remained explicitly
labeled conjectural in the retrieved entry. The original paper restricts it to
n >= 4; the restriction is necessary.

## Important later work

Kauers's talk *Guessing with little data* is listed in the Rutgers archive for
11 December 2025. Slide 39 (PDF page 137, counting from one) proves general
C-finiteness for fixed row count and an arbitrary allowed Gram set. This is
substantial prior work and is explicitly credited in the report.

Slide 30 (PDF page 104) still displays the exact formula with a question mark.
The overview tables on slides 24 and 40 also give A181280 a green check mark.
These facts must be read together: the slides certainly establish the general
existence of a constant-coefficient recurrence, but do not give an explicit
calculation certifying the displayed formula. The check mark may reflect work
or interpretation beyond the detailed argument shown. It is not safe to infer
from the OEIS label alone that nobody has previously settled the exact formula.

## Precise scope of this package

The package supplies a conventional mathematical proof plus exact finite
arithmetic of the displayed formula. The central explicit refinement is a
universal spectral-and-multiplicity bound leading to a quadratic-size recurrence
order and, for the target, a twelve-value certificate. It also establishes a
sharp common recurrence bound for four rows, the exact generating function,
minimal recurrence orders, the exceptional prefix, and quantitative
ordered-Gram equidistribution.

The finite arithmetic was actually executed. Two separate state encodings,
Fourier inversion, all 1024 small matrix annihilator identities, and independent
row-set brute force were checked. The scripts use no input OEIS data.

No standalone complete proof of the exact formula was located in the sources
retrieved during this task. This is a report of the search outcome, not a claim
that no such proof exists. The result should not be advertised as a first proof
or a newly solved open problem without a fuller literature and author check.
The report has not been independently peer reviewed or formalized in Lean.
Nothing was submitted to the OEIS or to a journal, and no author was contacted.

Third-party papers, slides, and fonts are not redistributed in the archive.
The certificate and sequence tables are generated by the included programs.
