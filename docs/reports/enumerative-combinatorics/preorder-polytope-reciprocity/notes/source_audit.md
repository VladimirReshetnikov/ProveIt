# Source and open-status audit

Access date: September 19, 2026.

## Selected research source

Christos A. Athanasiadis and Frédéric Chapoton,
*Polytopes and posets associated to preorders*, arXiv:2605.26916v1.

- Abstract/version record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26916
- HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26916v1
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.26916

The version record inspected listed v1, submitted May 26, 2026. The rendered
article also displays an internal date of August 24, 2026; the manuscript
identifies the source by its arXiv version rather than silently conflating
these two dates.

Relevant locations:

- Theorem 2.1: Postnikov's lattice-point formula in the normalization used.
- Proposition 3.3: ideal inequalities for the two-parameter polytope.
- Lemma 4.1: preorder graph draconian vectors correspond to dual lattice points.
- Section 4.2 / Conjecture 4.2 (printed page 11): matrix transpose identity.
- Conjecture 4.4 (printed page 12): multiset-word descent formula for h-star.
- Conjecture 4.9, equation (22): ordinary zeta evaluation. Equation (21) is
  its q-refinement and is not established by this manuscript.
- Remark 4.10(a): the ordinary zeta statement follows from the word statement.
- Proposition 7.1: the double Ehrhart polynomial and its rising-binomial sum.
- Conjecture 7.2 (printed page 19): the double reciprocity identity.
- Remark 7.3: the word statement follows from the appropriate specialization.

Conjecture statements and the relevant polytope inequalities were checked
against the rendered PDF, not only parsed snippets. Focused searches for
resolutions of the three selected conjectures did not locate a resolution.
This is evidence for choosing research targets, not a proof that no other
proof exists. Unpublished, unindexed, or subsequently posted work may exist.
The source itself reports finite computational evidence, in some cases
through larger sizes than our independent regression test.

## Classical counting input

Alexander Postnikov, *Permutohedra, associahedra, and beyond*, International
Mathematics Research Notices 2009 (2009), 1026–1106.
Preprint arXiv:math/0507163.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0507163
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0507163

Theorem 11.3, printed page 28 in the preprint, was checked in the rendered
PDF. Its untrimmed form increments the coefficient of a full-simplex
neighborhood by one. That is the source of the exceptional binomial offset
at the universal root. Corollary 11.8 concerns the general graph-duality
phenomenon. The article does not claim that phenomenon as new.

## Classical reciprocity input

Richard P. Stanley, *Combinatorial reciprocity theorems*, Advances in
Mathematics 14 (1974), 194–253, Section 6.

- Author-hosted PDF: https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/pubs/pubfiles/22.pdf

The manuscript uses the ordinary Ehrhart–Macdonald law on positive integer
parameter rays. No unspecified multivariate reciprocity theorem is required.

## What the bibliography is and is not

The bibliography documents the precise selected statements and the two
classical substantive inputs. It is not intended as a survey of all
order-polytopes, generalized permutohedra, or reciprocity theory. No copied
third-party PDFs or source archives are included in this package.
