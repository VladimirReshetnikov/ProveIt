# Claim and validation status — 20 September 2026

## Proved in the article

1. Full classification: the possible complete successive-L1-minimum lists for
   coefficient lattices of (r+2)-element integer sets are exactly the sorted
   lists of r even integers at least four.
2. Explicit mixed-radix witness, with arbitrary radix order permitted.
3. Integral carry basis, the bound q_j*|z_j| <= ||c||_1/2 for every relation,
   and the full short-relation subgroup filtration.
4. Rigidity and exact count of all ordered simultaneous minimizing bases.
5. Unique sumset normal forms, bivariate and ordinary generating functions,
   all-index cardinality formulas, and sharp affine-growth onset.
6. Exact binomial ideal and a regular-sequence / complete-intersection presentation.

## Source qualification

The source is the unnumbered conjecture after Lemma 1 on page 2 of
arXiv:2506.20836v2, not the source's Theorem 2 or numbered Conjecture 10.
Its displayed lower bound h_1=1 is impossible for distinct elements. The
article does not merely exploit that endpoint: it realizes every admissible
list with h_1>=2. The known two-minimum case is credited, not claimed new.

The live arXiv record inspected on 20 September 2026 listed v2 (18 August 2025)
as the latest version. Targeted searches found the source and discussions,
but no later resolution of this particular full-list conjecture. This is a
search outcome, not proof that no earlier or contemporaneous solution exists.
No author correspondence, comprehensive citation-index audit, independent
referee review, or proof-assistant formalization has been performed.

## Computational evidence

All checks in data/verification_summary.json passed using exact integers.
Ranks are computed from exhaustively enumerated fixed-size multiset
collisions rather than inferred from the prescribed radices. Separate set
addition verifies sumset sizes and membership. Tests are deliberately finite;
the universal statements rest on the written proofs.
