# Sources and their roles

OEIS entries and the MathOverflow answer were consulted on 20 September 2026.
The article's mathematical proofs are self-contained; the links below document
the problem statement, the shortlist, and prior work.

## Selected problem

- OEIS A225114: https://oeis.org/A225114
  Defines the counted skew partitions, records the conjectured generating
  function attributed to Mikhail Kurkov (3 September 2024), gives initial terms,
  and includes Joerg Arndt's composition-based counting program.

## Prior proof sketch

- Dima Pasechnik, answer to *Generating function for A225114*, MathOverflow,
  10 September 2024:
  https://mathoverflow.net/questions/478171/generating-function-for-a225114
  Sketches the generating-function connection and ordered-sequence construction.
  This source rules out claiming that the present article is the first solution
  of a previously untreated open problem.

## Connected diagrams and q-series background

- OEIS A006958: https://oeis.org/A006958
  Parallelogram-polyomino counts; connected continued fractions, q-series formulas,
  published initial values, and references to earlier literature.

- Peter Bala, *Fountains of coins and skew Ferrers diagrams*, 26 July 2019:
  https://oeis.org/A161492/a161492_1.pdf
  A primary mathematical note on the connected diagrams, refined q-series, and
  continued fractions. The note was read, including relevant rendered equations.

- M.-P. Delest and J.-M. Fédou, *Enumeration of skew Ferrers diagrams*,
  Discrete Mathematics 112 (1993), 65–79:
  https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(93)90224-H
  Historical bibliographic background, identified through the above sources.
  The full article was not retrieved in this investigation; no unexamined
  theorem from it is used as a premise of the proof.

## The other frozen candidates

  Fifth-largest distinct values in rows of Stern's diatomic triangle;
  conjectured rational generating function attributed to Alois P. Heinz,
  20 June 2022.

- OEIS A381190: https://oeis.org/A381190
  Connected minimal dominating sets in trapezohedral graphs;
  conjectured rational generating function attributed to Joerg Arndt,
  7 January 2026.

- OEIS A391632: https://oeis.org/A391632
  Mutual-visibility sets in Andrásfai graphs; conjectured rational generating
  function attributed to Andrew Howroyd, 12 January 2026.

The three unselected formulas are preserved in candidates.json and the article's
appendix. No proof or refutation of those three formulas is claimed here.
