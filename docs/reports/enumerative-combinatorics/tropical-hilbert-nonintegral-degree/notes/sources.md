# Primary sources and version audit

Checked on 20 September 2026.

## Main source

Dima Grigoriev, *Degree of 1-dimensional tropical prevariety*,
arXiv:2404.06440v2, revised 11 August 2025.

- Abstract/history: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06440
- Versioned HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2404.06440v2
- Versioned record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06440v2
- PDF text retrieved through web tool: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.06440

The abstract/history page listed v2 as the latest version retrieved. The
versioned HTML states both the precise strict-witness definition (Definition
1.1) and total-degree monomial cutoff (Definition 1.2). The introduction states
an eventual-polynomiality conjecture and Question 2, “Is the degree always an
integer?” The paper separately introduces a box degree cutoff. Our total and
box results are separately justified.

The min-plus equation definition is in the introduction. Our article gives an
explicit three-equation presentation of V_d, rather than depending on the
paper's general statement about rational polyhedra.

The article does not use the source's proof of existence of the one-dimensional
limit, its individual-polyhedron formulas, or its star formula. Its main proof
requires only the stated definitions and elementary real/integer arithmetic.

The PDF text and versioned HTML were retrieved. Attempts to obtain source-PDF
screenshots returned cache/fetch errors; no successful visual inspection of the
source PDF is claimed. The output article's own PDF was separately rendered
and visually checked.
