# PDF and package validation

The final article was compiled with pdfLaTeX and its cross-references were
stabilized by repeated compilation. The result has 18 A4 pages. The final
compilation log had no unresolved references, no overfull/underfull boxes,
and no remaining LaTeX or hyperref warnings.

All 18 pages were rendered with PyMuPDF and inspected in two nine-page
contact sheets. The lower-bound proof and the complete seven-term certificate
and gap matrix were also inspected at full rendered size. The layout has no
observed clipping, overlaps, broken formulas, or truncated tables.

The retained PDF has a text layer and working internal links. No external
fonts or source-paper PDFs are redistributed in the package. The source is a
single LaTeX file with an inline bibliography and a TikZ geometry diagram.

The article distinguishes mathematical proof from exact finite checks and
records that it has not undergone independent refereeing or proof-assistant
verification. Visual production checks do not add such mathematical validation.
