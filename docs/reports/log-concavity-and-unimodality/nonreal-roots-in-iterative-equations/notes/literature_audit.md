# Literature audit

Research and retrieval date: 20 September 2026.

This records the sources used to identify the public question and to
constrain the claims. The proofs in the article do not depend on importing
an unproved order-reduction theorem from these papers.

## 1. Target: Draga and Morawiec

Szymon Draga and Janusz Morawiec, *Reducing the polynomial-like iterative
equations order and a generalized Zoltán Boros' problem*, Aequationes
Mathematicae 90 (2016), 935–950.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00010-016-0420-4
- Versioned preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.00570v2
- HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/1503.00570v2
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.00570v2
- Version date: 1 December 2015.

Checked: introduction, definition of the iterative equation, interpretation
of polynomial factor deletion, recurrence setup, relevant order-reduction
context, and Section 6. Problem 6.1 was also inspected on the rendered
preprint page 13 (the preprint has 14 pages).

The question concerns removing nonreal roots of maximal modulus when a
real root has the same modulus. The paper's setup assumes continuity and
nonzero constant and leading coefficients. Our examples are increasing
homeomorphisms of the entire real line, so they satisfy those hypotheses.
The cubic's minimality makes the objection independent of whether one
attempts to delete all nonreal roots or just a proper portion of the relation.

## 2. Draga's subsequent note

Szymon Draga, *A note on the order of polynomial-like iterative equations*,
Commentationes Mathematicae 56 (2016), 243–249.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.01287v2
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.01287v2
- The preprint title is *A note on the polynomial-like iterative equations order*.
- v1: 5 April 2016; v2: 14 November 2016.

Checked: preprint and final remark, including the last rendered page. The
paper gives another root-elimination case and comments on the broader
nonreal-root question. This does not establish the question's status in 2026.

## 3. Initial lead: Petrov

Fedor Petrov, *Continuous linear recurrent relations*, MathOverflow,
question 232493, 1 March 2016.

- https://mathoverflow.net/questions/232493/continuous-linear-recurrent-relations

Checked: question and displayed discussion. The retrieved page had no
posted answer. The question asks more generally when a polynomial relation
among iterates forces a continuous function to be linear. The report does
not claim to settle that arbitrary-polynomial problem. Its exact rigidity
results are restricted to increasing solutions and one spectral circle.

## 5. Search coverage and limitations

Searches combined the authors' names with polynomial-like iterative
equations, nonreal-root elimination, roots of equal modulus,
counterexamples, and Problem 6.1. The original question, related primary
papers, and the MathOverflow lead were retrieved. Irrelevant search hits
were not used.

No earlier explicit answer was identified in the material retrieved.
This is a bounded search result, not proof of global absence. We did not
conduct an exhaustive subscription-database citation survey or contact
any author. The general conjugacy method is not claimed to be novel.
The independently derived classification may overlap prior results not
located in this search; no historical priority claim is attached to it.
