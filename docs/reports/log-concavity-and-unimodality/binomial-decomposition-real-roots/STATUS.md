## Exact primary source

Lili Mu and Volkmar Welker, *On a question about real rooted polynomials
and f-polynomials of simplicial complexes*, arXiv:2503.24076v1,
31 March 2025.

- Abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.24076v1
- Full text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.24076v1
- The recursive decomposition is on printed page 5 (Section 3.2).
- Question 3.10 is on printed page 6.
- The separate coefficientwise Conjecture 3.8 is also on page 6.

The definition and question were checked against rendered PDF pages, not
solely against search-result snippets or an experimental HTML conversion.
The paper uses f_{i-1} for the coefficient of t^i. This note uses a_i for
that coefficient and explicitly keeps the corresponding output indexing.

## Authorship and mathematical trust

The research attempt, proof development, exact computations, and article
were prepared with ChatGPT in response to the user's request. No outside
referee report or proof-assistant formalization was obtained.

The main counterexample and all-degree theorem have direct mathematical
proofs in the article. The coefficient-minimality statements are bounded,
exhaustive computational certificates specifically for degree three.

The source's Bell--Skandera question and Conjecture 3.8 are not settled.
The all-degree theorem proves failure of H, not simultaneous failure of
both G and H in every degree. The explicit cubic examples do prove
simultaneous failure of both outputs.

No third-party paper is redistributed in this archive.
