# Source and priority audit

Research date: 18 September 2026.

## Exact target

Brendan B. Connelly, Ezekiel Ito, Thomas C. Martinez, Olha Shevchenko, and
Kacey Yang, *Unimodality of q-Fibonomial coefficients for small cases*,
arXiv:2605.12822v1, Conjecture 5.4, printed page 14.

Version-pinned record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12822v1
HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.12822v1
PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.12822v1
Current record checked: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12822
Author project page: https://brendanconnelly.com/projects
Another coauthor's research page surfaced: https://sites.google.com/g.ucla.edu/shevchenko/research

The arXiv record checked lists v1 submitted 12 May 2026.
The relevant PDF page was inspected as an image, in addition to reading
the HTML and parsed PDF text. The statement uses `k<=3 OR r<=3`, not AND.
The source uses weak unimodality, so the central plateau of the counterexample
is allowed. Its reported tests stop at k<=5, r<=6, max(a_i,b)<=15.

The source's existing example [3]_q^4 [2]_(q^4) only disproves unrestricted
necessity outside the stated necessity range; it is not the example used here.
Our r=3, k=6 example lies inside that range.

## Mathematical scope

The counterexample refutes necessity in the stated auxiliary conjecture.
The sufficient half is proved in the report. Complete criteria are given
for r=2 and r=3. The original main q-Fibonomial conjecture is not settled.
For r>=4, no complete arbitrary-product classification is claimed, and
necessity for two or three ordinary factors is not settled by this report.

The target paper and author pages were found. No earlier correction was
located in the material returned. Several broad or formula-heavy searches
returned mostly irrelevant material, so this is a limited search, not a
systematic bibliography or proof of historical priority.

No claim is made that all related work has been ruled out, or that the
supplementary two-shift theorem has not appeared elsewhere. The mathematical
proofs are independent of that priority question. The source authors were
not contacted, and no external peer review took place.

## Computational provenance

`verification_results.json` was generated by actually running
`python3 verify.py --full --out verification_results.json`.
The exhaustive and random ranges and deterministic seed are recorded there.
The verifier uses exact integer coefficients, not numerical root calculations.

`verify.wl` was evaluated separately through the Wolfram connector. Its
returned kernel version and exact output are retained in `wolfram_output.txt`.
Both CAS calculations and finite tests are supplementary: neither constitutes
proof-assistant verification of the infinite statements.

The PDF was compiled from the included TeX source and visually inspected
through page renders. Source font files are not included.
