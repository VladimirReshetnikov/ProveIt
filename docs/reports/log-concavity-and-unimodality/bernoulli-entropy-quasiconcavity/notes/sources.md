# Primary-source and novelty-search audit

Consultation date: 20 September 2026. Only the primary mathematical sources below
are used for literature claims. The mathematical proofs in this archive are
independent derivations, not summaries of these sources.

## 1. Exact target

Erwan Hillion and Oliver Johnson, *A proof of the Shepp–Olkin entropy concavity
conjecture*, Bernoulli 23(4B) (2017), 3638–3649.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3150/16-BEJ860
Archival version: https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.01570
PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.01570

Checked the abstract/version metadata, the general affine-path setup on pages
1–2, entropy definitions in Section 4, and Conjecture 4.2 on PDF page 7.
The PDF page containing the conjecture was visually inspected using a screenshot.
The arXiv submission history lists v1, 5 March 2015. The proposed numbers are
2 and 3.65986..., the latter being the root above one of 2-4q+2^q=0.
The full setup allows mixed signs among the affine parameter slopes.

The publisher PDF endpoint returned no usable body through the browsing tool.
Accordingly, the precise conjecture transcription was checked against the
actual arXiv PDF, not claimed to be checked line-by-line against the publisher
PDF. The journal citation was independently corroborated by the author's
publication page and later bibliography:
https://people.maths.bris.ac.uk/~maotj/publications.html

## 2. Independent repetition of the proposal by a conjecture author

Oliver Johnson, *Entropy and thinning of discrete random variables*, The IMA
Volumes in Mathematics and its Applications 161 (2017), 33–53.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.05390
https://arxiv.org/html/1510.05390v3

Version v3 is dated 7 June 2016 in the archival metadata. Section 8, item 5,
explicitly repeats the generalized conjecture and both numerical thresholds.
The HTML rendering displayed a much later generated date in its body; that
rendering date was NOT treated as a 2026 revision or as evidence of current
open status. The archival version date controls the bibliography.

## 3. Distinct later monotonicity problem

Erwan Hillion and Oliver Johnson, *A proof of the Shepp–Olkin entropy monotonicity
conjecture*, Electronic Journal of Probability 24 (2019), paper 126, 1–14.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.09791
https://arxiv.org/html/1810.09791

Section 4 was read. Its Conjecture 4.4 concerns coordinatewise nondecrease for
parameters at most one half and orders up to two, not joint concavity along
mixed-sign paths. Its q=2 discussion and q>2 monotonicity examples do not
resolve the counterexample investigated here. The present article makes no
claim to refute the separate monotonicity conjecture.

## 4. Later Bernoulli-sum Renyi inequalities

Mokshay Madiman, James Melbourne and Cyril Roberto, *Bernoulli sums and Renyi
entropy inequalities*, Bernoulli 29(2) (2023), 1578–1599.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.3150/22-BEJ1511
https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.00896
https://arxiv.org/html/2103.00896v2

The consulted v2 is dated 15 March 2024. The abstract, introduction, relevant
concavity/majorization mentions and bibliography were inspected. The work
concerns variance comparisons, convolution entropy powers and related
concentration inequalities. No resolution of the numerical generalized
Shepp–Olkin joint-concavity threshold claim was found in these passages.
