# Sources and status check

Checked September 19, 2026.

## Exact problem source

Richard Stanley, “A conjectured rational generating function,”
MathOverflow question 431075, posted September 23, 2022.
https://mathoverflow.net/questions/431075/a-conjectured-rational-generating-function

The retrieved page stated the positivity, divergence and rational
exponent-generating-function assumptions, and asked about the number of
coefficients equal to a fixed positive k. It displayed no posted answers.
No monotonicity hypothesis was stated. The construction in the article
uses r=k=1 in the general formulation.

These sources provide the question, context, and standard terminology.
The counting formulas, counterexample proof, and family classification in
this package are derived in the article itself.

## Search limitations and claims deliberately not made

Targeted searches for the question title, its identifier with
“counterexample,” and binary/ternary coefficient-count variants did not
locate a prior answer matching the construction. The searches were not
exhaustive. A page with no posted answer does not prove that a question
has no solution elsewhere. Consequently historical priority is unverified.

The package does not claim to refute a Fibonacci-specific theorem, a
moment-of-coefficients theorem, or a monotone-exponent variant. It does
not claim an OEIS submission or identifier. No third-party source text,
font binaries, or third-party PDF papers are redistributed.
