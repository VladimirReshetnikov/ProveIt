# Claim and evidence ledger

## Proposed contributions in this draft

1. The finite rational–Conway-omega expression language, with a recursive
   sparse-fraction equality algorithm and an effective separated-grid
   compilation sufficient for omnific membership, integer parts, internal
   truncations, and coefficient extraction (Theorems 3.2, 4.2, 5.1–5.3).
2. The combined exact ordinal trace and sharp support analysis of that
   language, including depth-two cofinal support complexity below ω^ω,
   an explicit computable strong sum of length ω^ω outside the language,
   and the separate finite-lattice exclusion with support type ω
   (Theorem 6.2 and Section 7).
3. The presentation separation showing that always-valid names can have
   Π⁰₁-complete equality even when every individual value has at most one
   supported term and belongs to the decidable depth-two field. No uniform
   value-preserving compiler into a decidable-equality notation system is
   possible (Theorems 8.2–8.3).

These formulations were not found in the targeted sources inspected for
this article. This is a bounded-search statement, not a certified claim
of absence from published literature or from every file of the repository.

## Established ingredients, not claimed as new

- Conway normal forms and the omnific support/constant-term characterization.
- Ordered Hahn arithmetic and the Hahn–Neumann geometric support lemma.
- Abstract truncation closure of a field generated by a truncation-closed
  set (explicitly acknowledged via Camacho, Proposition 4.1(i)).
- Cantor normal forms, computable well-orders, the nonhalting reduction,
  and the tree well-foundedness completeness theorem.
- The constant-term retraction and resulting Diophantine transfer.
- Cayley–Hamilton recurrence zero tests and clearing-denominator jet tests.
- Familiar infinite geometric examples of omnific integers.

## Proof / program / formalization boundary

The article supplies mathematical proofs and identifies imported classical
facts. The prototype executes finite exact arithmetic with recursive
representations; SymPy supplies rational polynomial algebra only.
`data/verification.json` records 197 passing finite regression assertions.
These tests do not prove termination at arbitrary depth, ordinal support
bounds, infinite summability, or undecidability. No Lean proof was created
or checked, and no repository build was run.

## Important qualifications

- Ω is Conway's omega map, not the full surreal exponential.
- Constants are exact rationals, not arbitrary computable real names.
- The real trace of the field is Q; the field is not real closed.
- A support length below ω^ω is necessary, not sufficient, for membership.
- No polynomial-time or other uniform complexity bound is claimed.
- Ground equality is decidable; existential Diophantine solvability is not.
- The Church–Kleene cofinality statement additionally requires a uniform
  interpretation into computable well-order presentations. Decidable
  equality/order by themselves only bound the order type of the range,
  not the supremum of arbitrarily assigned ordinal values.
