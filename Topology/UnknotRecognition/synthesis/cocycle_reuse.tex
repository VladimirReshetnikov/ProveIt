\section{Shared discovery geometry and independent positive checks}
\label{sec:cocycle-reuse}

The preceding planar experiment removed duplicate geometry preparation
within its new checker, but the ordinary native search still prepared the
same triangulation separately for seed construction, raw-surface replay,
tree construction and optimized-surface replay. Profiling a continuing
miss identified this repeated validation as a larger cost than boundary
weight transport. The new search retains one prepared geometry for
candidate construction and defers full source-certificate replay until a
candidate could yield a positive answer. Independent verification of
positive certificates is unchanged.

\subsection{A height-based Euler prefilter}

For an internally constructed coherent integral cocycle, let $h_{t,i}$ be
its four local heights in tetrahedron $t$. At an optimized candidate add
the returned vertex potential to the corresponding corners. For each
global edge $e$, put $w_e=|h_{t,j}-h_{t,i}|$ using a local occurrence of
that edge. Coherence makes this independent of the occurrence. Let
$s_f$ be the height span on a global face, counted once, and let $s_t$ be
the span of the four tetrahedral heights. Then
\[
  V(S)=\sum_e w_e,\qquad E(S)=\sum_f s_f,\qquad
  F(S)=\sum_t s_t,\qquad
  \chi(S)=\sum_e w_e-\sum_f s_f+\sum_t s_t.
\]
Here $F(S)$ counts normal pieces, rather than faces of the ambient
triangulation. Every half-integral level between the lowest and highest
height cuts one normal polygon from a tetrahedron. On a triangular face
it cuts one normal arc; on an edge it cuts one point. Integral endpoint
heights therefore give precisely the three counts above. Interior faces
are counted once by their gluing pairs, and boundary faces once directly.
Tetrahedral constant shifts preserve these counts. Adding a vertex gauge
to the corner heights while subtracting that same gauge from the vertex
potential leaves the adjusted heights, and hence the surface, unchanged.

The helper scans $O(N)$ local cells and performs $O(N)$ dictionary
operations for $N$ tetrahedra. If heights and potentials have at most $B$
bits, its arithmetic and accumulated totals use $O(B+\log(N+1))$ bits.
In an indexed or constant-table-cost model its arithmetic work is
$O(N(B+\log(N+1)))$, with $O(N)$ stored integer entries. As with the earlier
Python container accounting, a conservative collision-sensitive bound
allows $O(N^2(B+\log(N+1)))$ work; no constant-time hashing assumption is
needed for the polynomial claim. No normal piece or binary height interval
is expanded.

This helper is a discovery prefilter. It does not validate matching,
primitivity, connectedness or source provenance. It reads the heights
produced by the maintained exact constructor, or those heights plus the
potential from the checked span optimizer. Tree and face candidates retain
their existing normal-coordinate analysis. If Euler characteristic can
support a disc or enabled annulus witness, the full independent cocycle
inspector reconstructs the source and checks the entire coordinate vector
before any positive verdict. A malformed vector with apparently promising
heights therefore cannot bypass verification.

\subsection{One prepared geometry per candidate search}

The rank-one constructor has a private detailed return containing the
prepared geometry it already built. Its public function still returns
the same seed dictionary, with the same cocycle statistics and allowance
semantics. The public tree generator still validates its supplied
triangulation; an internal generator accepts the prepared geometry from
the current native search. Its integral coherence checks, deterministic
root order, gauges, duplicate suppression and candidate vectors are
unchanged. Optional face analysis uses this same per-search geometry.

Raw and optimized misses use the height cell count instead of full
source-bound certificate replay. The constructor's primitive class and
zero-tree construction, gauge invariance and checked minimum-span
optimization establish the candidate family's mathematical invariants.
These discovery summaries are not negative knot certificates. Candidate
failure continues to mean only that the bounded search found no witness.

The existing positive inspectors receive ordinary certificate data and
construct their own authenticated source geometry. They accept no geometry
cache from discovery. Disc and annulus candidates receive the full cocycle
check; optional planar candidates receive the full cocycle and boundary
check. The public verifier and its independent arithmetic modules are
byte-identical to the preceding revision. Reusing producer geometry and
skipping negative replays does not remove any positive-certificate gate.

The candidate schedule, default trial counts and optional planar/face
settings do not change. The shared guard allowance covers the new prefilter
and retained checks. Guard totals can fall on continuing searches and rise
slightly on an immediate positive that now receives the additional cheap
prefilter. Exact allowance boundaries are therefore revalidated rather
than inferred from old work counts. Global cancellation continues through
construction, prefilter scans, discovery and independent positive replay.
