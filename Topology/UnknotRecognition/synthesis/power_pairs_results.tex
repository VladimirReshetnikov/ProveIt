\paragraph{The initial overhead and its exact remedy.}
The initial integration invoked discovery after every unsuccessful primitive
round, even when no mixed donor could qualify. A four-arm whole-recognizer
comparison found paired old/new ratios 0.935--0.968 on the fourteen survivor
and mirror cases. Gordian's ratio was 0.965: charged group work rose from
842,290 to 894,778, with 138 unnecessary detector passes and the same accepted
certificate. These consistent small regressions motivated the eligibility
guard proved above. The original raw records are preserved as
\path{data/power-pair-initial-benchmark.json}; its noisy tiny negative-control
ratios are not treated as gains. The initial audit and test logs are also
retained under \path{data/power-pair-initial-*}.
The final audit removes all 758 unsuccessful detector passes in each of the
projection and forest modes. Their charged work now matches the predecessor
on every one of the 88 diagrams, while the binary capacity fixtures still
activate the new rule.
