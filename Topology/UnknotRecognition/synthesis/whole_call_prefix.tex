\section{Fresh whole-call evidence for the lexicographic prefix}
\label{sec:whole-call-prefix}

The fixed-query comparison of Section~\ref{sec:capped-pivot-pilot} motivates
a complete-call experiment.  A changed positive point or dual can change
later objectives, cached answers, conflicts, candidate subsets and even
crushing successors.  Therefore an earlier stream of LP inputs cannot
predict the result of a fresh recognition call.  The present experiment
starts each arm from the original diagram, uses the same shared allowances,
and retains all fourteen outcomes.  It changes no maintained native code.

\subsection{Frozen implementation, inputs and allowances}

Both arms import the same isolated native package at revision
\texttt{4d1f76f06}, containing the prepared-matrix implementation of
Section~\ref{sec:prepared-euler-matrix}.  The baseline leaves its Bland
prefix unchanged.  The candidate changes exactly the entering and leaving
assignments in the current tableau helper: largest positive real reduced
cost, with least-index ties, and the full original-slack lexicographic ratio
of Proposition~\ref{prop:capped-pivot-lex}.  A checked syntax-tree replacement
recovers the original helper when these two expressions are restored.  In
particular the current tuple-safe matrix ownership, fresh tableau, original
rows, terminal-before-cap order, pivot updates and statistics remain.

A scoped wrapper selects this helper only for the polynomial policy.
Explicit \path{policy='bland'} still delegates to the original rule, even
inside a callback made by the candidate.  Nested public and prepared calls
restore their caller's helper after ordinary or exceptional exits.  This
is a single-threaded scratch implementation; it supplies no concurrency
guarantee for monkey-patching.  The alternative selectors add cancellation
polls inside their scans.  Callback position and count equality between
arms is therefore neither expected nor claimed.

The fixed inputs are the empty diagram, the trefoil, figure eight,
$TT=\widehat{\sigma_1^3\sigma_2^3}$, $T(2,5)$, $T(2,7)$, and
$W(3,4)=\widehat{(\sigma_1\sigma_2^{-1})^4}$, in that order.  Each receives
the baseline and then candidate arm.  Literal PDs and their typed digests
are retained.  There are no warmups, repeats, eligibility filters, elapsed
time measurements, or wall cutoff.

Both arms use source-model transversal search, conflict-directed candidate
selection, the envelope strategy, polynomial LP policy, diversified sector
order and quantum $64$.  Vertex reduction, boundary unlayering, strict
$3\to2$ reduction and geometric $D/UDD$ descent are enabled.  The common
allowances are $256$ sectors, $256$ tree nodes, and $10000$ each for bases,
simplex pivots, anchors, hitting states, ellipsoid iterations and feasibility
calls.  There is no separate reduction-count cap.  Every accepted reduction
strictly decreases the tetrahedron count, so this still gives a finite run.
The complete option dictionary is frozen in the protocol.  A default internal
simplex prefix means $(n+r+1)^2$ pivots, where $n$ is the retained Q width
and $r$ the number of independent equality rows, subject to the smaller remaining
user allowance; it does not mean an unlimited prefix.

Each actual \path{recognize_by_crushing} call performs its preprocessing,
source construction and search afresh.  No supplied source, old query list,
previous arm's chain, or resumed proof is injected.  Every full arm shares
its own $10000$-pivot allowance across all LP queries.  This differs from
the sum of independent captured residual allowances in the older pilot.

\subsection{Controls and independent replay}

Before the fourteen calls, the frozen controls retain $22$ polynomial-policy
records, each comparing fresh and prepared entry points; $10$ explicit
legacy Bland records; $96$ nesting records; two complete callback-position
exception-identity sweeps on the small fixture; and eight invalid-input
checks.  They include unreduced dependent and zero rows, rational and large
integer coefficients, forced positive and negative fallback, zero and last
allowed pivots, denied fallback, and both ordinary and base exceptions.
All these controls pass.  They are correctness controls, not performance
observations.

Instrumentation retains every actual query, objective, selected original
row, remaining allowance, result, mask and anchor context.  Identical matrix
and labelled source bodies are stored once, with equality checked on typed
bytes before reuse.  The complete chronological query records remain.
Remaining allowances are recomputed from prior actual answers within each
source search; the query sums bind that search and then the complete driver
totals.  Every attempt and matrix preparation is recorded independently of
the native answer.

Arithmetic replay checks original-row primal equations and dual inequalities,
normalization, primitive scaling, support nullity one, and the feasible
original slack basis.  Source reconstruction binds matrices and objectives
to their actual masks and anchors.  The formal STANDARD matching equations,
canonical Euler minimum choices and conflict sets are checked independently
for positive oracle points.  The final LP's primitive Q vector, after zero
extension, equals the oracle Q block; canonical oracle, search and next
normal-crushing coordinates agree literally where returned.
A zero extraction-descent counter alone would not prove that
the extraction routine was never invoked.

Each conclusive result is replayed against its original diagram by the
independent recognition verifier; a capped result has its full partial
crushing chain checked.  The retained source ledger includes the initial
triangulation, shelling, every accepted successor and terminal state.  Exact
typed source tables, strict decreases, elementary $D/UDD$ counts and operation
totals are checked.  Pair comparisons report the common labelled prefix,
first divergence and endpoint equality; equality is observed rather than
assumed from a verdict or tetrahedron count.  The final replay disables
recognition, LP, oracle, ray and topology discovery, including loaded aliases,
while allowing source algebra and independent certificate verification.

The candidate uses fewer pivots and tableau updates in each of the six

nodes increase from $16$ to $18$ and learned conflicts from two to three.

root with no branching and certifies the original diagram as knotted in
$6606$ pivots.  This is a fresh complete recognition result under the stated
allowances.  It must not be substituted into the old eight-query experiment.

\subsection{What these counts justify}

The native tableau-update counter measures its existing arithmetic updates.
It excludes the entering scan, full-slack ratio comparisons, source-rank
work and independent replay.  The reported maximum-tableau bit field is an
end-tableau statistic, not a peak over every visited tableau.  No elapsed
runtime or universal speedup follows from fewer pivots or updates, especially
when one arm completes and the other remains capped.  All extra LP calls,
search nodes and conflicts remain in the paired evidence.

The complete frozen package, protocol, controls, fourteen results, source
ledgers and logs are retained in
\path{data/whole-call-prefix-4d1f76f06.tar.gz}.
The maintained helper \path{data/whole_call_prefix_replay.py} checks its
inventory and reruns discovery-disabled verification in an isolated temporary
directory.  Its saved outputs are separate from the historical scratch
records.  Neither replay replaces the old fixed-query archive or reinterprets
its source pins.

This evidence informs a possible native integration of the new polynomial
prefix, with explicit legacy Bland behavior and a fresh regression run.
The maintained native default at this experiment's checkpoint was Bland;
Section~\ref{sec:native-lex-prefix} records the later implementation change.
Proposition~\ref{prop:capped-pivot-lex} supplies correctness and polynomial
bit cost for the capped prefix, not a bound on complete admissibility search.
Controlling the number of masks across all crushing stages remains a separate
requirement for general quasi-polynomial recognition.
