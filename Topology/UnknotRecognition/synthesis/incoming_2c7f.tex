\subsection{A fixed source bounds an entire monomial epoch}

Report 49 supplies a stronger invariant than the successive length estimates
in Sections~\ref{sec:primitive-projections} and~\ref{sec:primitive-forests}.
Freeze a presentation with $r$ generators, $m$ original relator slots, a shared
source grammar of $S_0$ reachable nodes, and maximum raw relator length
$L\ge1$. Write $B=\lceil\log_2(L+1)\rceil$. During the epoch permit only
verified disjoint primitive-pair projections and acyclic unit-coordinate
forests. Retain every unselected original slot, and perform no normalization,
Whitehead move, overlap move or change of source coordinates inside the epoch.
Proper-power donors retain the existing source-established torsion-free
hypothesis; the lattice calculation does not justify their nonabelian deletion.

Every cumulative substitution has the form
\[
 x_i\longmapsto z_{\pi(i)}^{k_i},\qquad k_i\ne0.
\]
Its matrix $K\in\mathbb Z^{r\times s}$ has one nonzero entry per row and
nonempty disjoint column supports $I_z$. Each column is primitive: initially
it is a coordinate vector, and merging two primitive disjoint columns
multiplies them by coprime coefficients $|v|$ and $-u\operatorname{sign}(v)$.
The gcd of the merged column is therefore one.

Let $A$ be the original exponent-sum matrix and $A_J$ its rows selected as
donors so far. After $q$ individual contractions, $s=r-q$, $A_JK=0$, and
$\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}A_J=q$. Indeed, the next selected row $a$
has nonzero current image $aK_{\rm old}$, whereas all previously selected
rows annihilate $K_{\rm old}$. The new row is independent of the previous
ones. Disjoint batches linearize immediately. Unit-coordinate forests
linearize from leaves toward their surviving roots, so an earlier eliminated
leaf cannot alter a still-needed donor.

Dimension and disjoint supports now give
\[
 \ker_{\mathbb Q}A_J=\operatorname{im}_{\mathbb Q}K,
 \qquad
 \ker_{\mathbb Z}A_J=\operatorname{im}_{\mathbb Z}K.
\]
For the second equality, write an integral vector as $Kc$ with rational
$c$. A Bezout combination within each primitive column expresses its
coefficient $c_z$ as an integer combination of the vector's integral entries.
Thus all coefficients are integral. Restricting to $I_z$ leaves a
one-dimensional kernel spanned by that primitive column.

If $t=|I_z|\ge2$, choose $t-1$ independent rows of the restricted matrix.
The signed maximal minors form a nonzero integral kernel vector. Dividing
its common content gives the primitive vector $(k_i)_{i\in I_z}$, up to sign.
Since every source matrix entry has magnitude at most $L$, Hadamard's bound
proves
\[
 |k_i|\le(t-1)^{(t-1)/2}L^{t-1};
 \qquad t=1\ \Longrightarrow\ |k_i|=1.
\]
All cumulative exponents consequently use
$O(r(B+\log(r+1)))$ bits throughout the entire epoch. This bound is
independent of the number of raw phases.

\paragraph{Reachable size and historical allocation.}
Set $\Lambda=8(r+1)(B+\lceil\log_2(r+1)\rceil+2)$, a convenient common
bit bound. A raw projection maps each old reachable concatenation to at most
one new concatenation. If $P_j$ counts the powering gates introduced in phase
$j$, the reachable and historical counts obey different recurrences:
\[
 S_{j+1}\le S_j+O(P_j),\qquad
 N_{j+1}-N_j\le S_j+O(P_j).
\]
There are at most $r-1$ contractions. Each current donor is an image of an
original slot, of length at most $L\max_i|k_i|$, so its local exponents also
have $O(\Lambda)$ bits. The existing forest updater shares parent power
circuits and charges each edge's exponent rather than re-encoding a full
path product. Thus $\sum_jP_j=O(r\Lambda)$, and for $d$ phases,
\[
 S_j=O(S_0+r\Lambda),\qquad
 N_d=N_0+O(dS_0+(d+1)r\Lambda)
     =N_0+O(rS_0+r^2\Lambda).
\]
Every surviving root remains an image of its fixed source word; length
metadata is bounded by $B+O(\Lambda)$. The existing planners, exact primitive
checks and raw updates therefore have polynomial total encoded cost on this
restricted epoch. The previous exponential recurrences were valid but loose
upper bounds. A short-depth assumption is unnecessary here.

The supplied source checker matches the current \path{primitive_projection.py}
Git blob and the complete \texttt{apply\_projection} syntax tree. Forest
applicability follows from the leaf-first linearization and the maintained
shared-power update above; the report's standalone lazy implementation only
implements disjoint pairs. Its maintenance-only timing claims do not measure
native discovery or mandatory source replay. Eagerly exporting its lazy state
after every round was slower in the delivered experiment, so adopting a lazy
state requires preserving that interface through planning and verification.

\paragraph{The meridional special case.}
If an integral character $h$ of the frozen source satisfies $Ah=0$, then
$h=Kc$. On a block where $h$ is nonzero, primitive columns imply
\[
 k_i=\varepsilon_z h_i/\gcd_{j\in I_z}|h_j|,
 \qquad \varepsilon_z\in\{1,-1\}.
\]
Every entry of $h$ on that block is nonzero. In particular, with consistently
oriented Wirtinger meridians $h_i=1$, all cumulative exponents have magnitude
one. Huge-power abstract presentations test a broader supplied-presentation
interface; they do not show such growth in this initial meridional epoch.
Changes of coordinates can create zero character values or large values,
and require their own complexity accounting.

\subsection{Acyclic singleton batches have linear grammar growth}

Report 51 improves the general, nonmonomial batch bound. Let $M$ count the
nonempty source nodes reachable from all current roots. A selected donor
$U_c c^{\epsilon_c}V_c$ contains its chosen child once in the raw spelling.
Follow the unique path $H_c$ to that occurrence in the shared binary grammar.
If $H_c$ and $H_d$ shared a node, its word would contain both $c$ and $d$.
Deleting either selected occurrence would leave the other, giving dependencies
$c\to d$ and $d\to c$. Acyclicity excludes this. Hence
\[
 H_c\cap H_d=\varnothing\ (c\ne d),\qquad \sum_c|H_c|\le M.
\]
Whole donor grammars can still overlap substantially; only the hole paths
are disjoint.

Both complementary substring cuts follow that path. At each internal path
node at most one cut needs a new concatenation; completely included siblings
are reused. Including the final rotation $V_cU_c$, all donor contexts require
at most $M$ new nodes. The source and contexts together have at most $2M$
nodes. In the report's compiler, each has two virtual orientations, and
selected terminals redirect to the corresponding signed context. A cycle in
this graph would imply a cycle among selected definitions. Topological
compilation of at most $4M$ states gives at most $4M$ output nodes, at most
$5M$ new persistent nodes including contexts, and at most $6M$ total nodes
when the initial arena contains only the reachable source.

Our current producer and independent checker materialize inverses before
compilation. Their globally memoized inverse operation introduces at most
$2M$ inverse nodes for the source-context union. The resulting union has at
most $4M$ nodes; compiling each once gives the conservative native bounds
\[
 M_{\rm out}\le4M,\qquad N_{\rm after}-N_{\rm before}\le7M.
\]
Historical unreachable nodes are included in $N_{\rm before}$, not silently
charged to $M$. These constants do not assert that the delivered virtual-state
compiler is already installed. Occurrence/support discovery may still scan a
whole donor grammar per selected child, so linear output size does not imply
linear running time for the current batch. It does give polynomial bit cost
in the actual reachable source: a binary terminal grammar with at most $4M$
nodes has $O(M)$-bit lengths and $O(M^2)$ total length metadata.

For an epoch consisting solely of these singleton batches, $d$ phases give
$M_d\le4^dM_0$ and historical growth $O(M_0 4^d)$. Thus their earlier
$2^{O(d\log(r_0+1))}$ composition estimate can be sharpened to
$\operatorname{poly}(M_0,r_0,m)2^{O(d)}$. A logarithmic number of phases gives
polynomial cost; $O(\log^2 n)$ phases with polynomial initial encoded size give
quasipolynomial cost. No such general diagram-to-endpoint phase bound is
proved. The polynomial monomial theorem above is stronger on its narrower
class and does not extend to arbitrary word-valued singleton definitions.

The report also supplies an ordered witness whose per-node summary is only
the maximum selected rank and its multiplicity capped at two. Donor $i$ has
summary $(i,1)$ exactly when its child is unique and all selected dependencies
are earlier. This can reduce checker support storage, but the current checker
accepts arbitrary entry order and derives its own dependency order. Changing
that contract requires compatibility work. The report's rank-one-completion
guard avoids eager partial-batch regressions on its older baseline; our
recovery-aware bounded adaptive trial is a different policy and is retained.

\subsection{Sparse incidence removes an exponential output interface}

Reports 50 and 52 are separate implementations of the same positive sparse
reconstruction idea. With $r$ interval-union ports, let $h(T)$ count components
meeting exactly the port set $T$. Marked-union orbit queries give the zeta
oracle
\[
 G(U)=\sum_{T\subseteq U}h(T).
\]
Subtract already recovered atoms. The residual is nonnegative and monotone.
Starting at the full port set, delete a port whenever the residual stays
positive. At termination every proper subset has zero residual, so the
remaining residual is exactly one new atom's positive weight. Repeating until
all total mass is recovered uses $O(1+sr)$ queries for $s$ nonempty atoms.
Balanced block deletion improves this to
\[
 O\!\left(1+s+\sum_{T\ne\varnothing:h(T)>0}
 |T|\log\left(1+\frac r{|T|}\right)\right).
\]
Every failed block intersects the ultimately recovered support; counting
failed blocks at each tree depth proves the bound.

Polynomial support for interval-union ports follows from the classical
weighted AHT theorem, rather than being a new sparsity hypothesis. Assign
one indicator coordinate per port; signs of the resulting nonnegative orbit
weights give the incidence signature. AHT returns polynomially many constant
weight blocks in the encoded interval input, hence polynomially many distinct
signatures~\cite[Theorem 16]{aht-orbits}. The sparse unweighted-query route
inherits this bound. It replaces a mandatory $2^r$ output array with positive
\texttt{[mask,count]} entries; preserving the old dense return type would lose
the advantage.

Independent certificates check atom weights, immediate-subset zero witnesses
and total mass. On a binary orientation cover, each base component lifts to
one or two components with the same port signature. Support discovery can
therefore be reused, with signed consistency counts derived from the base
and cover multiplicities. This local observation does not retain attachment
maps, cyclic orders or slopes needed by arbitrary future hierarchy moves.
The reports include a small counterexample to treating incidence equality as
a complete equivalence for future attachments.

\subsection{Weighted components and a smaller normal-disc query}

Report 53 develops weighted local-rule replay and a componentwise normal
surface census. On a validated connected orientable compact triangulation
with exactly one torus boundary, assign integral cell weights: $+1$ per
surface vertex, $-1$ per arc and $+1$ per polygon, each charged to a point
in its own component. Their orbit sum is its Euler characteristic. Two
additional intersection weights evaluate the component's boundary class
against an independently checked basis of the torus's mod-two homology.
A connected component is a compressing disc exactly when
\[
 \chi=1\quad\hbox{and}\quad (h_1,h_2)\ne(0,0).
\]
Nonzero boundary class excludes closed components, and surface classification
then makes Euler characteristic one equivalent to being a disc. An embedded
essential circle on a torus has primitive integral slope and nonzero mod-two
class. The predicate must be applied componentwise: totals can cancel across
two genuine discs, or combine nondisc components into misleading statistics.
Higher-genus boundary requires a different essentiality test.

For an admissible standard vector $x$, subtract at each global vertex the
minimum incident triangle coordinate times its vertex-link vector. Call the
remainder $Y$. These removed links can be realized disjointly and contribute
no compressing discs. If $g$ is the gcd of the quadrilateral coordinates,
face matching propagates divisibility by $g$ from a zero triangle around
each connected vertex-corner graph. Thus $P=Y/g$ is integral and admissible.
If all quadrilaterals vanish, $Y=0$. Otherwise, with $t$ tetrahedra,
\[
 \max_iP_i\le(4t-1)\frac{Q_{\max}}g,
 \qquad D(x)=gD(P),
\]
where $D$ counts compressing-disc components. Two-sided discs scale as
parallel copies. One-sided components can produce orientation doubles,
but in an orientable ambient manifold these cannot create disc components.
Full component counts consequently need not scale by $g$, while $D$ does.

The expensive three-weight orbit query sees only the primitive quadrilateral
scale, even if the original vector has enormous additive vertex-link
multiplicity and full coordinate gcd one. Reading, validating and binding the
original input still costs its original bit size. A positive count becomes an
unknot witness only with checked provenance tying this triangulation to the
input knot exterior; zero rejects only this supplied vector. The delivered
two-weight ambient-homology refinement is a proposal, not executed code.

\subsection{Reproduced evidence and integration boundary}

Before execution, all five original archive integrity checks passed. Their
selected suites pass 38, 21, 16, 33 and 103 tests respectively: 211 in total.
Report 49's audit exercises 600 signed trees, source-row/cofactor invariants,
exact raw-update comparisons, 32,664 signed primitive profiles and the
64,513-bit cumulative-exponent fixture. The copied updater is source-identical
to our native operation. Report 51's 54 saved positive certificates pass
108 literal/compressed source replays using its pinned final snapshot.
A separate maintained-engine audit checks 400 random singleton DAGs through
both our producer and checker, including exact literal results, disjoint hole
paths and the $4M/7M$ size bounds.

Report 50 reproduces 512 exhaustive native dense/sparse comparisons and
independent replays, huge integer transport and producer-disabled signed
checking. Its deliberately gapped larger queries remain censored under the
five-second driver allowance, and receive no speedup ratios. Report 52's
1,000-case audit checks both ordinary graphs and binary covers against
literal components. Report 53 reproduces the abstract weighted and mutation
audits and the frozen Regina corpus: 1,275 normal vectors on 45 triangulations,
5,100 component comparisons and 5,100 certificate replays. These local tests
provide evidence for the proposed interfaces. At this review checkpoint the
sparse and weighted additions had not run in the maintained suite;
Sections~\ref{sec:sparse-incidence} and~\ref{sec:weighted-components} supply
the subsequent maintained integrations.

The logs and JSON records are \path{data/incoming-2c7f-*}; the native grammar
check is \path{../fast/compressed_word_research/incoming_bounds.py}.
The delivered files remain unchanged after placement. Checksum-only files
omitted by intake are recoverable from the original commit's ZIPs; integrity
was checked on fresh original extractions before rerunning any generator.

costs; they leave the general quasipolynomial recognition goal open.
