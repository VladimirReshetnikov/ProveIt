\subsection{Source equivalence and independent cell-count controls}

The source audit compares 84 diagrams under four policies: four or 24
tree trials, each with planar discovery disabled or enabled. The old
search body is loaded from Git revision \code{a6947e39a}; its source hash
is recorded. It uses the preserved public constructor APIs. Every old
record exactly reproduces the preceding planar audit, including its work
count. For every completed old case, the new record is identical after
removing only the total \code{work} field. This includes verdicts,
certificate hashes, all stages, boundary census records and optimizer
statistics. All positive certificates in both arms are independently
replayed.

Coverage remains 24 and 27 native positives for the four-tree and extended
policies without planar discovery, and 25 and 30 with it. Each policy has
the same single capped input, Gordian, at 2,000,001 guards. The comparison
does not turn that cap into a completed miss. The nineteen ordinary
portfolio sources retain their expected verdicts and do not reach the
native stage under either implementation.

Representative work changes illustrate why guard totals should not be
confused with timing. The optimized disc fixture falls from 54,318 to
52,090 guards. Its earlier optional planar witness falls from 29,111 to
27,303. The seven-crossing optimized annulus falls from 93,012 to 89,862.
The extended-tree \code{random-81} annulus falls from 52,641 to 47,761,
and the trefoil four-tree miss falls from 22,041 to 19,099. In contrast,
the immediate \code{circle-9} disc rises from 13,305 to 13,817 guards
because of its new prefilter. That control tests the cost paid when there
is no repeated negative replay to remove.

The separate 74-source survey regenerates all 1,798 raw, tree and original
minimum-span surfaces from the preceding experiment. For every candidate,
the height-based Euler count equals both the independently reconstructed
normal-coordinate Euler value and Regina's Euler characteristic. The
normal-piece total also agrees with coordinate analysis. Regina reconfirms
connectedness, orientability and the preceding boundary counts. Candidate
ordering and the number of distinct tree gauges match the earlier survey.

New tests compare the formula under 20,000-bit tetrahedral shifts and
19,000-bit compensated vertex gauges. They check that a continuing trefoil
miss performs no positive replay, that every possible disc or planar
success requires its independent gate, and that a corrupted producer
vector cannot turn a promising height count into a positive answer.
Another test corrupts a returned source triangulation and verifies that
fresh replay rejects it while the original proof and later independent
searches remain valid. Existing exact allowance, cancellation, source,
primitivity, schema and binary-coordinate tests remain active.
