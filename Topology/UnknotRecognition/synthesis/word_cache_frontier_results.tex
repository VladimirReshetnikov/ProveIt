\paragraph{Word kernels and supplied-proof replay.}
The controlled kernel run has fifteen cases, four shuffled arms (old/current
and their A/A copies), five measured rounds and one warm-up. All 300 measured
calls and 60 warm-ups complete in 58.503 seconds. Timings for word queries
include construction of a fresh arena and its initial word. Bounded literal
assertions are performed after the timed call. Source-proof timings instead
include fresh diagram validation and full independent reconstruction/replay
of a fixed supplied proof; proof construction is outside that timing.
All ratios below are medians of paired old/new times.

A chain begins with $ab$, then appends alternating $a,b$ letters and queries
the extended root after each append. The table's size is its number of
extensions. At 2,048 extensions inverse evaluation improves from
1,438.629 to 12.118 ms, a paired ratio of 118.719; reduction improves from
1,444.585 to 10.489 ms, a ratio of 137.726. Inverse work drops from
4,212,748 to 16,396, with 4,102 nodes in both arms; reduction work drops
from 4,212,746 to 16,394, with 2,051 nodes in both. The new inverse traversal
collects 2,051 nodes across 2,049 queries, and reduction collects 2,049 across
2,049 queries. These are intentionally simple exact-word kernels that expose
the repeated traversal, not difficult knot inputs.

A power case starts at $(ab)^{2^s}$, performs its first query, then makes
128 alternating single-letter extensions and queries. The table's size is
$s$, not the expanded word length. At $s=4096$ the output length has
4,098 bits. Inversion improves 20.775-fold with work 1,094,798 to 29,710;
reduction improves 24.883-fold with work 1,094,796 to 29,708. Respective
node counts remain 8,454 and 4,227. No expanded power word is allocated.
Across all kernel and supplied-proof cases, old A/A ratios range from
0.973 to 1.016 and current A/A ratios from 0.986 to 1.041.

The supplied source proof has a powered Whitehead prefix with exponent
$2^s$, followed by a checked projection suffix. Full replay ratios are only
1.016, 1.014 and 1.005 for $s=64,256,1024$, comparable with control variation.
At $s=1024$, work falls from 91,623 to 89,567, while both runs still use
5,151 nodes and three equality calls. The kernel gains therefore do not
translate into a comparable gain for these complete supplied proofs.


\paragraph{Complete recognition.}
A second run measures nineteen fresh source inputs with old/current defaults
and old/current adaptive batch hosts, each with an A/A control. It includes
PD construction, the complete recognition pipeline and mandatory source
replay. All 760 measurements and 152 warm-ups complete in 81.088 seconds.

Default old/current paired ratios range from 0.970 to 1.067; portfolio ratios
range from 0.987 to 1.095. Several group-search inputs show modest gains.
For example survivor-02 improves 1.067-fold in default mode and 1.095-fold
with the portfolio; its corresponding current A/A ratios are 1.000 and
0.995. Survivor-03's portfolio ratio is 1.093, but its 38.139 ms current
portfolio median remains much slower than its 10.680 ms default median.
The word-engine optimization does not remove schedule-dependent regressions.

Gordian default medians are 1,462.026 ms old and 1,431.768 ms current, with
paired ratio 1.050. Portfolio medians are 1,498.568 and 1,448.005 ms, with
paired ratio 1.037. All four arms return the same 9,663-byte certificate.
The paired ratios need not equal quotients of the marginal medians.
The default old A/A ratio on Gordian is 0.973, so timing precision remains
limited even though its 60,548 saved producer work units are deterministic.

Across the full corpus, the four A/A ranges are 0.936--1.014 for old default,
0.973--1.039 for current default, 0.939--1.015 for old portfolio and
0.969--1.019 for current portfolio. Small regressions and noisy controls are
retained. These measurements support modest practical savings on several
search inputs, alongside the much larger deliberately isolated traversal
gains. They do not establish a uniform end-to-end factor or change the
unproved global discovery and grammar-growth requirements.
