The refreshed audit certifies 83 of the 106 supplied cuts. All 83 source
certificates replay independently. Regina constructs the actual cut and
recognizes all components in 99 cases, agreeing exactly with each of the

Data and runtime pins are retained in \path{data/core-annuli-audit.json}.

All 78 previously accepted inputs remain accepted, and their canonical
certificate hashes are unchanged. The five new v4 certificates are exactly
the private-column candidates identified by the earlier passive probe.
Each of those source cuts consists of one solid torus and one genus-two

The 23 remaining refusals comprise two annular cases without a complete

Thirty focused tests pass. They cover the five source families, hostile

schema downgrades, disabled producers, hexadecimal transport, budget caps,

conditional-chart tests compare the passive update $(2,1,1)\mapsto(0,3,1)$

column. Those algebraic test charts are not asserted to be source cuts.

\paragraph{A broader boundary-template diagnostic.}
A separate experiment appends candidate standard bundle-boundary rows to
all the real core rows, rather than first insisting that the core alone
cancel completely. For a base with $a<b$ attached boundary circles, it assigns
one dedicated boundary-generator column per circle. When all $b=a$ circles
are attached, it uses $a-1$ such columns, and the last boundary row crosses
each of them once. Other standard genus columns and bridge columns are
omitted from the finite elimination experiment because their proposed
entries are zero or at least two and cannot become singleton pivots under
positive updates.

The experiment tests the 13 unresolved cuts without three-handles. Both
remaining annular cases have complete finite elimination schedules; the
other eleven still stall. This is only an algebraic feasibility diagnostic,
not two additional accepted cuts. A source-to-template geometric construction,
correct treatment of the connecting handles and omitted columns, and an
independent whole-cut replay are still required. The prototype and records
are retained in \path{data/bundle-boundary-kernel-probe.py} and its JSON.
