\paragraph{Projectors and seams.}
The reviewed polynomial-projector construction is integrated and extended in
Section~\ref{sec:primary-continuation}. Its separate geometry kernel assembles
complete boundary covers with dihedral sheet maps, retaining original ports
and marks. It does not extract such pieces from an arbitrary knot exterior
or verify their embedding in that exterior. Geometry and the independently
proposed worker-communication repair remain separate integration items.

\paragraph{Presentations and early certificates.}
The report claims exact real-feasibility bounds controlled by generator count,
explicit relator length and retained word-circuit cuts. Its quaternion compiler
uses a noncommuting-generator test, rather than assuming arbitrary generator
images must differ for a nonabelian representation. It distinguishes an
arbitrary presentation from certified meridional generators, charges dense
polynomial construction, and identifies the published traceless-meridian
specialization as not yet implemented in its benchmarked solver. The required
small presentation or small circuit cut is not proved for every residual knot.
The delivered Z3 path is an optional exact external solver, not an independent
UNSAT proof checker. Those provenance and solver boundaries require review
before a production verdict path is added.

The same report supplies a first-jet observer and reverse-suffix modular
responses. The response proof retains a singular interior radical instead of
silently applying an invertible Schur-complement formula. Its claimed
$O(n(W+1)^2)$ field-operation bound concerns shared preparation of all suffixes
under a valid reverse filtration. It does not bound the number of live
Khovanov components or automatically establish a full-recognition speedup.
The next useful check is a maintained consumer and complete accounting for
response preparation, query reuse and exact fallback. The first-jet report
retains its observed regressions, so automatic dispatch is not justified by
the delivered timing evidence alone.

\paragraph{Compressed certificates.}
The endpoint matcher certifies an arithmetic progression using short endpoint
anchors, proves a period on the largest candidate window, and gallops and
bisects in candidate rank to locate the true overlap cutoff. This is stronger
than merely accepting an entire candidate progression when its two largest
members match. It should be compared directly against the current endpoint,
window and sparse-marker shortcuts, especially on broken progressions, before
changing their dispatch. The delivered whole-knot runs have zero endpoint
activations and establish no end-to-end speedup.

The regular-cover kernel represents compatible isomorphisms by at most two
congruence families per connected base graph, but fast equivalence checking
does not bound the number of distinct states. The disk checker validates a
finite triangulation, normal coordinates, primitivity, extreme-ray rank,
Euler characteristic and essential boundary parity. Even a correct accepted
disk still needs a verified link from that triangulation to the input knot
diagram. Neither geometry module is promoted to a knot-verdict path at this
checkpoint.

\paragraph{Separator transfer.}
The first-hit identity factors a typed DAG transfer through a vertex cut.
Prefixes must stop at their first cut vertex; unrestricted prefixes would
count a path multiple times when the cut is not an antichain. The numerical
rank budget then maximizes $\sum_h r_h$ subject to
$r_{h-1}+r_h\le c_h$ and supplied upper bounds on $r_h$. Saturating the
leftmost available path edge is optimal: any deficit can be moved from the
next edge to it without decreasing the objective, then the argument repeats.
Thus $\sum_h c_h-2\max\sum_h r_h$ is the sharp lower bound supplied by those
numbers. For a verified reduced knot complex, a bound greater than one is a
one-sided obstruction; a smaller bound does not certify an unknot.

All 36 delivered separator tests pass in the current environment. This is a
prototype gate, not a production Khovanov integration gate. The report supplies
neither a maintained cobordism-closure evaluator nor a cheap general exporter
of a suitable state; its braid utility first constructs an exponential cube.
Its actual-knot timings favor ordinary propagation. A future transfer must
validate the closure and grading conventions, compare expanded maps with the
current corridor implementation, and charge construction and cut discovery.
These obligations remain outside the current integration.

\subsection{The subsequent degree-budgeted SU(2) delivery}
Commit \code{ea9cf443c} adds the degree-budgeted SU(2) archive:
\begin{center}\small
\path{unknot_su2_degree_budget_20261008.zip}
\end{center}
All 50 checksum entries verify,
and all 30 delivered test methods pass in 1.049 seconds. The report has a
particularly concrete restricted recognition route: two \emph{certified}
meridian generators permit a complete compressed integer calculation.
The underlying topological input is established: Kronheimer--Mrowka,
Corollary 7.17 of \emph{Knots, sutures and excision}, gives an irreducible
$\mathrm{SU}(2)$ representation sending a chosen meridian to $i$ for every
nontrivial knot~\cite{km-traceless-primary}. The primary source was checked
directly during this review.

For noncommuting pure imaginary generator images $A,B$, set $T=AB$.
Since $A^2=B^2=-1$ and $ATA^{-1}=T^{-1}$, every word has the form
$(-1)^e T^k A^s$ with $e,s\in\{0,1\}$. Multiplication changes only one integer
and two parity bits:
\[
 (e,k,s)(f,\ell,t)=(e+f+st\bmod2,\ k+(-1)^s\ell,\ s+t\bmod2).
\]
Thus a binary straight-line word circuit can be evaluated without expansion.
For comparison relators with triples $(e_j,k_j,s_j)$, odd $s_j$ is impossible.
Otherwise the equations are $k_j\phi\equiv e_j\pi\pmod{2\pi}$ with
$0<\phi<\pi$. After ruling out $k_j=0,e_j=1$, let $d=\gcd_j|k_j|$.
If $d=0$, every interior phase works. If $d>0$, an odd $k_j/d$ fixes a parity
$e$; all equations are compatible exactly when
$e_j\equiv(k_j/d)e\pmod2$. An interior phase then exists exactly when
$\lfloor(d-1+e)/2\rfloor>0$. A compact witness is $\phi=\pi/d$ for odd parity
or $2\pi/d$ for even parity, subject to that strict interior condition.
These normal-form and phase arguments check out at the algebraic interface.

The report's bit bound is polynomial in compressed input size: intermediate
exponent bit lengths are linear in the number of word-circuit nodes, and the
remaining operations are gcd, quotient and parity calculations. It also gives
an exhaustive two-seed Wirtinger propagation class, with a claimed polynomial
complete recognition bound on diagrams admitting such a derivation. The
unrestricted multi-generator path is a formula compiler; the invoked general
existential-real algorithm is not implemented in this package.

The highest-priority next check is a maintained adapter that constructs and
replays the seed derivation from the validated PD, keeps every original crossing
relation and carries meridian provenance to the two surviving generators.
An arbitrary two-generator endpoint of a Whitehead search is insufficient:
exponent sum one does not prove that a generator is a meridian. The delivered
Boolean declaration is explicitly a precondition, not a topology certificate.
This checkpoint records the verified theorem and algebraic interface; it does
not yet expose those presentation-level answers as production knot verdicts.
