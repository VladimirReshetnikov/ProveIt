\paragraph{Final complete-call control.}
The final timing runs after the guarded full suite and audit, with no concurrent
tests, builds or commits. Both packages use the same optional primitive-forest
portfolio on the nineteen native diagrams. Each call constructs a fresh PD,
runs complete recognition and includes mandatory independent replay of group
certificates. Four shuffled arms contain old/new and an identical A/A control
of each. Five measured rounds and one excluded warmup per case give 380
measured calls and 76 warmups, all complete. The initial timing used the same
protocol and also completed every call; its results are not silently replaced.

new A/A ratios span 0.985--1.028. The consistent initial regression is absent,
but no broad speedup is established. Gordian has identical charged group work
842,290 and zero detector passes in both engines; its paired ratio is 0.995.
