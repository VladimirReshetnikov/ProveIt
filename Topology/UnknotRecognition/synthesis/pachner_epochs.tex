\section{Strict descent epochs reach an actual transported disc}
\label{sec:pachner-epochs}

The on-demand cover engine reaches useful moves, but its small single-family
search still returns no new nonempty-diagram witness in the preceding audit.
This continuation tests two ways to turn that exploration into reach. Exact
Euler-based event ordering alone does not succeed on the selected controls.
Restarting bounded \emph{strict descents} on successively smaller sources
produces an actual new transported disc for the genus-one-miss diagram.
The new policy is explicit, with a per-epoch upward allowance and a full
original-source proof; it does not change the existing total-upward family.

\subsection{Failed ordering and monotone pilots}

The existing bipyramid compiler scores Euler jumps and normal-disc changes
with a fixed number of exact integer operations on five local heights.
An ordering-only scratch experiment prefers downward Euler gains and upward
moves with smaller Euler loss. All eight calls on four selected actual
diagrams remain inconclusive at the two-million-unit allowance. This finite
negative observation neither proves such orderings useless in general nor
justifies adding a runtime policy with no demonstrated witness.

The maintained monotone \code{descend\_cocycle} pilot instead tries only
$3\to2$ moves, with exact score selection and independently replayed transport.
It is inexpensive on the selected diagrams, but most have no initial
eligible downward move; the optimized-positive control has three without
obtaining a disc. These observations motivate using the complete bounded
first-descent primitive to unlock a source, then restarting from the smaller
endpoint rather than remaining inside one original cover.

\subsection{One actual source chain through strictly smaller triangulations}

An epoch starts at source $(T_i,h_i)$ and calls
\code{find\_pachner\_descent} with at most $U$ total upward events \emph{in that
call}. A successful result is independently replayed and has
\[
                     |T_{i+1}|<|T_i|.
\]
The returned integral cochain becomes the next source cochain. All actual
move/transport records are concatenated; no new diagram exterior is silently
substituted. The adapter checks the coherent surface at the initial source
and after each successful epoch. Only an independently verified essential
disc component supplies the positive result.

The scope is deliberately different from one bounded family. A chain of
$e$ epochs can have up to $eU$ upward events, although each epoch has at
most $U$. A stationary result of the bounded descent primitive or a resource
cap remains inconclusive for the knot. It is not a terminal recognition
theorem for that manifold. The new mode is never described as a witness
with a whole-chain upward bound $U$.

\begin{proposition}[Strict restart and geometric bit bounds]
From a supplied $t$-tetrahedron marked source, the epoch policy has at most
$t$ successful restarts. Its completed first-descent move chain has at most
$t(2U+1)$ events. Geometry, cochain transport and bounded descent search have
$2^{O(U)}\operatorname{poly}(t+B)$ bit cost, with source/proof encoding growth
charged; component queries and essentiality replay remain separate costs.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Tetrahedron count is a nonnegative integer decreasing at every successful
epoch. Its first descent has $u\le U$ and $d=u+1$, hence at most $2U+1$
events. Summing gives the restart and event bounds.
For the height accounting, let $H$ bound the span of each current integral
height row. A formal bipyramid has five vertices. The old edge graph is
connected, so differences between its aligned vertex heights are sums of
at most four old edge differences, each bounded by $H$ in absolute value.
The new tetrahedron heights restrict those aligned values; untouched rows
retain their spans. Thus one move has new span at most $4H$.
After $m$ moves, bit lengths are at most $B+O(m)$, including the zero-span
case. With $m\le t(2U+1)$ this is polynomial in $t,U,B$. The live cell count
within an epoch is at most $t+U$. Applying the preceding complete
$2^{O(U)}$ bounded-descent geometry bound at most $t+1$ times and charging
transport and retained full snapshots preserves the displayed form.
No component-query or essentiality cost is removed by this argument.
\end{proof}

This is a concrete polynomial restart measure. It does not prove that a
small per-epoch $U$ reaches an essential disc for every unknot, that a
stationary source can be recognized cheaply, or that the hierarchy's other
pattern/cutting operations have controlled representations. The general
obligations of Section~\ref{sec:missing} therefore remain.

\subsection{Shared allowances and original-source proof authority}

\code{pachner\_epoch\_seed\_decide} prepares and binds the original exterior,
optional boundary shellings and one initial cocycle/span choice. It shares
one cooperative work allowance and one geometric-node allowance across
initial observation, every epoch and full positive replay. A separate epoch
limit can stop the chain, without invalidating already accepted moves or
providing a negative knot verdict. A failed component query also remains
inconclusive. Caller exceptions propagate through the same guarded replay
interface as ordinary transported witnesses.

A positive result uses the unchanged \code{diagram-transport-disc-v1}
schema: original PD input and source exterior, optional shellings, original
height rows, every actual transport step, final coordinates and the terminal
disc proof. The independent checker replays the whole concatenated chain
from that original input. It uses neither the epoch search nor an asserted
intermediate outcome; the final source cannot be replaced by another knot's
exterior. Missing or reordered moves, changed cochain values and a false
positive count are rejected.

The recognizer and CLI expose this as an explicit optional mode.
\code{pachner\_seed\_epochs=0} keeps the single-family policy unchanged.
A positive value bounds the number of strict descent epochs, with the
upward allowance now explicitly \emph{per epoch}. The CLI option is
\code{--pachner-seed-epochs}, used with \code{--pachner-seed}. Neither the
optional stage nor epoch mode becomes a default. A miss continues the
maintained complete recognizer, and its global deadline stays global.
