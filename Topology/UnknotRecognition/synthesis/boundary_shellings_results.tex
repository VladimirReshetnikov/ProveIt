\subsection{Source coverage and independent topology controls}

The retained audit covers the same 84 source diagrams as the geometry-reuse
experiment. Every default-off result exactly reproduces its preceding record,
including work count, stage summaries and certificate hash. With shellings
enabled and the ordinary four-tree candidate budget, the number of native
positives increases from 24 to 28. All 24 previous positives survive in this
corpus. The four added source labels are random cases 1, 25, 81 and 176; their
new witnesses are primitive connected annuli that can be capped. Cases 25 and
176 share their diagram, so source-label counts do not count distinct knots
or distinct certificates. Earlier optional extended-tree or planar searches
already recognized these examples; the gain here is within the four-tree,
planar-off schedule.

Case 1 shrinks from 80 to 40 tetrahedra using 40 shellings. Cases 25 and 176
shrink from 60 to 54, and case 81 from 100 to 94. Their total native work
counts change from 32,902 to 8,030; 26,620 to 7,043; 44,320 to 12,059; and
26,620 to 7,043 respectively. The old counts describe completed candidate
misses; the new counts include the successful source-bound proof. On the
original optimized-disc fixture, only six tetrahedra are removed, from 100
to 94, but a tree witness now preempts optimization and lowers native work
from 52,090 to 13,239. Small geometric changes can therefore matter more to
the candidate family than their tetrahedron reduction suggests.

Gordian loses just six of 2,820 tetrahedra. Nevertheless the new candidate
schedule completes its restricted miss at 1,859,545 guards, with optimized
Euler characteristic $-64$, instead of hitting the two-million-guard cap in
the old schedule. This is not a recognition of Gordian. Enabling planar
queries on the reduced triangulation still exhausts that allowance. Across
the corpus, enabling planar queries gives the same 28 positive source labels,
although some have earlier or different proofs. There is no claim that these
settings dominate one another on arbitrary inputs.

Regina accepts all 1,061 individual removals and agrees with every final
triangulation signature. All 84 reduced inputs remain connected orientable
valid manifolds with one torus boundary component. On the 74 sources with at
most 12 crossings, the audit constructs all 353 scheduled raw, distinct tree
and optimized candidates, even when the native recognizer would stop early.
Every candidate is connected and orientable in Regina, and its Euler value
agrees with the native coordinate calculation. Another 80 positive-result
surface checks across the three audit arms give 433 Regina surface controls
in total. Every tested Euler-one surface is independently a compressing disc.
The saved corpus contains 47 distinct positive proofs, of which 28 use the
shelling envelope; all are independently replayed. The shelling-on,
planar-off arm uses 22 distinct proofs for its 28 positive source labels.

The six new test methods exercise all three shelling types and every prefix
of the one-crossing-circle reduction; source immutability; repeated-vertex
exclusion; the stray-boundary obstruction; malformed traces and face records;
all three inner surface schemas; producer-free replay; wrong-source rejection;
20,000-bit harmless height offsets; exact work limits; cancellation; and the
recognizer's opt-in path. All 29 focused tests pass in 3.863 seconds, and all
1,229 maintained tests pass in 229.572 seconds. The source audit takes 77.295
seconds and partly overlaps the full suite; these times are validation costs,
not isolated performance measurements. Audit and timing datasets each pin
508 source files. On the 19 ordinary pipeline controls, both settings finish
through earlier recognizers, with zero native-stage attempts in all 38 runs.
