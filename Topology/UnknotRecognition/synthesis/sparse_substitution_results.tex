\paragraph{Repeated word queries.}
Twelve kernel cases use four shuffled old/current and A/A arms, five measured
rounds and one warm-up. All 240 measurements and 48 warm-ups complete in
118.781 seconds. Timings include fresh arena construction, initial reduction
and every singleton query. Ratios are medians of paired old/new times.

The singleton chain appends alternating $a,b$ letters and queries each prefix.
At length 2,048 the old/new medians are 2,497.807/11.843 ms, with paired ratio
212.355. Work falls from 6,302,722 to 18,434; both arenas contain 2,049 nodes.
Only 2,049 node summaries are built across all the new queries. This isolates
the removed quadratic metadata traversal on a growing immutable prefix.

The tail cases start with $(ab)^{2^s}$ followed by a letter $c$, and alternate
$c\mapsto d$ and $d\mapsto c$ for 128 substitutions while also retaining the
unchanged prefix as a root. Warm cases query singletons before each update;
cold cases never populate support metadata. At $s=4096$, the warm old/new
medians are 1,608.502/20.817 ms, with paired ratio 79.392. Work falls from
3,702,287 to 42,910, with 4,103 nodes in both arms. Only two affected nodes
are compiled per substitution, and represented length has 4,098 bits.
The power word is never expanded.

At the same size the cold arms each perform exactly 2,127,631 work units and
have 4,103 nodes. Their paired ratio is 1.010, compared with current A/A
1.138, so no cold-path speedup is supported. Across all kernel cases, old
A/A ratios span 0.886--1.072 and current A/A 0.970--1.138. The large warm
and growing-prefix gains are structurally explained by the counters;
small timing differences lie within substantial control variability.

\paragraph{Complete source-bound recognition.}
Nineteen fresh diagrams use old/current defaults and old/current adaptive
batch hosts, each with an A/A copy, five measured rounds and one warm-up.
The timed calls include diagram construction, discovery and mandatory source
replay. All 760 measurements and 152 warm-ups complete in 114.683 seconds.

Default paired ratios span 0.731--2.305 and adaptive-host ratios
0.647--1.379. The corresponding A/A ranges are 0.799--1.341 for old default,
0.616--1.046 for current default, 0.736--1.321 for old host and
0.739--1.560 for current host. These controls show a noisy shared-machine
run; neither its largest apparent gains nor its regressions should be read
as stable implementation factors.

On Gordian the default medians are 1,652.607/1,495.833 ms and the paired ratio
is 1.172. The host medians are 2,533.473/1,289.029 ms with paired ratio 1.330.
Paired medians need not equal ratios of marginal medians; the disparate A/A
marginal timings are another reason not to infer precision from these figures.
The unchanged audited certificate and deterministic default-work reduction
of 364,792 units (31.6 percent) provide firmer evidence of saved computation.
We retain the complete sample order, controls and outcomes, and make no uniform
end-to-end timing claim from this run.
