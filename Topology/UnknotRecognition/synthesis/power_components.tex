\section{Source-checked annihilation of plain-power components}
\label{sec:power-components}

The power-conjugacy delivery in commit \code{b365341b1} contains a graph
extension of the pair-minor idea integrated in Section~\ref{sec:power-pairs}.
We now implement its plain-power component inference. The maintained producer
can detect a contradiction around a cycle involving three or more generators,
even when every adjacent pair is individually consistent. A new independent
checker authenticates the words, removes tree leaves and multiplies around
the remaining cycle. The stronger conjugated-power proposal remains outside
this implementation.

\subsection{What the graph proves in a torsion-free group}

An authenticated mixed donor has cyclic spelling $x_u^a x_v^b$, with
$u\ne v$ and $ab\ne0$. It supplies the group equality $x_u^a=x_v^{-b}$.
A pure donor $x_u^a$ with $a\ne0$ supplies $x_u^a=1$. These are actual word
shapes, not merely exponent rows obtained by abelianizing arbitrary relators.
Conjugating a whole cyclic spelling does not change its normal closure.

Consider an oriented cycle written as
\[
 x_0^{a_0}=x_1^{b_0},\quad x_1^{a_1}=x_2^{b_1},\quad\ldots,\quad
 x_{k-1}^{a_{k-1}}=x_0^{b_{k-1}}.
\]
Taking integer powers successively gives
\[
 x_0^{A}=x_0^{B},\qquad
 A=\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}a_i,\quad B=\prod_{i=0}^{k-1}b_i.
\]
Every substitution uses powers of one group element at a time; no
commutativity or unique-root property between different elements is assumed.
If $A\ne B$, then $x_0^{A-B}=1$. In the source-established torsion-free knot
group, $x_0=1$, and each adjacent power equality makes the next generator
trivial. Triviality propagates through every attached tree in the same way.
A pure-power donor supplies an alternative starting vertex. Thus a pure seed
or an inconsistent signed cycle kills its entire connected component.

Signs matter. Plain equalities can give $A=-B\ne0$, which forces torsion and
therefore triviality. A cycle of \emph{conjugated} power equalities need only
make $x^A$ conjugate to $x^B$; the same signed conclusion would be invalid.
The native rule does not discard conjugator labels or invoke knot-group
balancedness. Its only group-specific premise is the already reconstructed
classical knot group's torsion-freeness, preserved by the checked move prefix.

There is also an exact completeness statement for this restricted graph
abstraction. In a component without pure seeds, assign a nonzero rational
potential $h_u$ to one vertex and propagate $a h_u+b h_v=0$ along a spanning
tree. If every remaining edge agrees, multiplying by a common denominator
produces nonzero integers $H_u$. The assignment $x_u=t^{H_u}$ in the infinite
cyclic group realizes every graph row with every generator nontrivial. Hence
no such generator is forced trivial by those rows alone in the class of
torsion-free groups. An inconsistent edge closes an annihilating cycle as
above. Other relators of the full presentation may impose further constraints;
this graph completeness statement does not make the recognizer complete.

\subsection{Producer discovery and a different independent proof}

The existing power-pair producer's immutable capped run summaries are factored
into a shared producer-only row extractor. It retains the same raw cyclic
one- or two-run language, including a run split across the cyclic cut. Current
slot numbers and live labels are reconstructed on every call. Pair selection
keeps its existing order and priority; its certificates are unchanged.

After the primitive and pair rules fail, the component detector is eligible
only with at least four live generators, at least three extracted rows and
at least two mixed rows. These are necessary conditions for the supported
component of at least three labels while leaving one survivor, including a
component killed by a pure seed. The checks avoid graph setup when the source
cannot provide a supported witness; they do not suspend a possible cycle
based on elapsed time or a heuristic score.

The producer traverses each connected component, assigning reduced rational
potentials along its spanning tree. A non-tree row that disagrees supplies a
cycle witness; a pure row supplies a seed witness. The emitted component
contains sorted live labels and sorted source-slot indices for the spanning
tree plus one witness. There are exactly as many selected rows as labels.
The potential values themselves are neither used as group substitutions nor
serialized as trusted certificate data. Distinct selected components have
disjoint labels, and at least one live label is retained.

Version ten adds \code{power\_component\_delete}. The independent compressed
checker reconstructs first and last signed letters, signed counts and capped
transition counts directly from the ordinary word grammar. At most two cyclic
signed-letter transitions and distinct absolute labels authenticate the
permitted power-word shape. The literal checker counts those transitions
separately in explicit words. Neither imports the producer's run summaries,
graph planner, rational potentials or substitution routine.

For each proposed component, the checker validates strict field types,
positive distinct live labels, distinct in-range source slots, and equal
numbers of rows and labels. It constructs the authenticated multigraph,
treating a pure row as a loop, and verifies connectivity. Repeatedly deleting
degree-one vertices leaves its unique cycle. A remaining pure loop is a valid
seed. Otherwise the checker walks the remaining cycle and multiplies the
signed coefficients on its two sides; the products must differ. Parallel
edges are allowed and form a two-edge cycle inside a larger claimed component.
This leaf-and-product computation is independent of the producer's rational
consistency computation.

Only after every component passes does each replayer substitute the empty
word for all its labels in every relator, retaining every slot. Roots or words
and the live-label set publish atomically after substitution succeeds. A node
or work limit, cancellation, malformed later witness or balanced cycle cannot
publish a partial accepted move. Historical speculative arena nodes may remain
charged on an interrupted export. Version-nine certificates reject the new
inference, and older moves keep their existing requirements.

\subsection{Binary complexity of discovery, proof and deletion}

Let $S$ be the reachable source grammar size, $m$ its relator slots, $r$ its
live labels and $B$ its largest raw-length bit size. Every extracted exponent
has at most $B$ bits. A reduced rational tree potential is a product of at most
$r-1$ edge ratios, so its numerator and denominator have $O(rB)$ bits. Fractions
are a producer data structure, not unit-cost real numbers or group roots.
There are $O(m+r)$ graph visits and rational comparisons, plus sorting and
exact gcd/arithmetic costs. The independent cycle products also have $O(rB)$
bits and use at most $r$ factors. No factorization or prime sieve is needed.

A component proof stores $r'$ labels and $r'$ source-slot indices for a
component of size $r'$. Across one disjoint batch, its structural size is
$O(r(\log(G+1)+\log(m+1)))$ bits, where $G$ is the largest live label.
It contains no expanded donor word or claimed large determinant. The checker
visits source descendants with shared memoization, authenticates their word
shape, and checks a linear-sized graph. Its encoded bit cost is polynomial
in the actual source and proof sizes.

Deletion only maps an old terminal to itself or zero and an old concatenation
to at most one new concatenation. For a deletion-only epoch starting at
$S_0,N_0,B_0$, reachable size and raw-length bit size cannot increase:
\[
 S_i\le S_0,\qquad B_i\le B_0,\qquad N_d\le N_0+dS_0.
\]
Component-only rounds satisfy $d\le\lfloor(r-1)/3\rfloor$; when interleaved
with the older pair deletions, $d\le\lfloor(r-1)/2\rfloor$ remains valid.
This accounts for historical allocation without assuming garbage collection.
It proves polynomial complete discovery, certificate production and checking
within this restricted epoch. Normalization, exposure and other changes of
generators end the bound; their number and encoded growth remain uncontrolled
for general unknot recognition.

\subsection{A cycle family beyond all adjacent-pair minors}

Choose $n\ge3$, $b\ge n-1$, and
\[
 P=1+2^b,\qquad Q=\frac{P^n-1}{2^{n-1}}.
\]
The quotient is integral because $2^{n-1}$ divides $P-1$. Consider
\[
 \left\langle t,x_1,\ldots,x_n\ \middle|\
 x_i^P=x_{i+1}^2\ (1\le i<n),\quad x_n^P=x_1^Q
 \right\rangle.
\]
The two cycle products differ by exactly one:
$P^n-2^{n-1}Q=1$. Rotating the cycle gives $x_i=1$ for every $i$ even
without torsion-freeness, so the whole presentation is unconditionally
infinite cyclic on $t$. This is a valid algebraic capacity family, not an
arbitrary torsion presentation treated as a knot group.

Every edge has coprime exponents greater than one: $P$ is odd and
$\gcd(P,Q)=1$. A positive two-run word with coprime counts $a,b>1$ has
prefix-height width $ab>a+b-1$, so it fails the maintained primitive criterion.
There are no singleton donors or unit primitive coordinates. Each adjacent
pair has only one relation and there is no pure seed, so the older pair-minor
rule has no witness. The graph rule closes the entire cycle in one move.
This strictly enlarges the supported local inference class. It is not a lower
bound against every possible search strategy or a hard family of diagrams.

All exponents have $O(nb)$ bits and the ordinary binary word grammar has
$O(nb)$ nodes. The new complete operation is polynomial in this encoding
although literal expansion has exponential length. The predecessor's configured
algebraic search stalls on the measured fixtures; its failure time is not a
valid baseline for a speedup ratio.
