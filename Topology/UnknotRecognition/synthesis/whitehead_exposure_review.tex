\section{Whitehead exposure: rank drops beyond strict length descent}
\label{sec:whitehead-exposure-review}

Commit \texttt{613bec60c3} supplies
\path{docs/incoming/unknot_whitehead_exposure_20261008.zip}; all 44 manifest
entries verify. Its main idea addresses a different objective from integral
shear length minimization: deliberately expose a defining occurrence, then
eliminate that generator, even if the intervening automorphism increases
length. The delivered adapter now runs against our maintained code in an
offline audit. No runtime policy is changed by this review.

\subsection{An exact one-step exposure oracle}

For a cyclically reduced relator $w$, put one unit on the undirected edge
$\{x,-y\}$ for every cyclic adjacent pair $xy$. Parallel edges aggregate
as positive capacities. For an admissible Whitehead pair $(a,S)$ with
$a\in S$ and $-a\notin S$, the transformed cyclic word has exactly
$c_w(S)$ occurrences of $a^{\pm1}$, where $c_w$ is the graph cut capacity.
Every other unsigned generator count is unchanged. The same noncancelling
skeleton argument used for integral shears proves this stronger occurrence
identity, and subtracting the original multiplier count gives the classical
length-change formula.

Consequently a one-step multiplier singleton exists exactly when the graph
has a capacity-one support bridge. A unit cut clearly isolates such a bridge.
Conversely, one component after deleting the bridge has odd weighted degree
sum. Signed degrees occur in equal inverse pairs, so that shore cannot be
closed under inversion: it separates some $a,-a$ and defines an admissible
Whitehead move. A bridge of capacity greater than one does not suffice.

To optimize a target relation's exposure, choose its unit bridge and contract
all its other support edges. A cut with that bridge as its only target edge
is constant on every contracted component. Pin the two bridge sides and the
multiplier pair in opposite shores, then solve a minimum cut in the quotient
of the \emph{total} presentation graph. Enumerating target bridges and signed
multipliers covers all one-step exposures. Absent signed letters must remain
as isolated vertices: assigning them can change the costs of other relators.
Equal pin patterns can share a flow computation. A feasible flow whose value
equals the cut certifies the selected pinned optimum; the checker does not
certify that every other bridge was searched. Topological validity only
requires replaying the chosen automorphism and elimination.

There is also an exact allocation objective. Let $L$ be total initial length,
$d(a)$ the total multiplier count, $c$ the total selected cut capacity, and
$t=|w_j|-d_j(a)$ for the target. Its transformed length is $t+1$ and its
solved replacement has length $t$. Removing it and substituting into the
other $c-1$ multiplier occurrences allocates exactly
\[
 U=L-d(a)+t(c-2)
\]
letters before the final reductions. Since $t\ge0$, the same minimum-cut
query optimizes $U$ for fixed target and multiplier. This is a bound on raw
substitution, not an estimate of final reduced length or future difficulty.

\subsection{A separation from strict automorphism descent}

Let $u=a^2bab$, $t=(ab^{-1})^3$, and let $v$ be the cyclically reduced
commutator $[u,t]$, of length 20. The presentation
\[
 P_m=\langle a,b\mid u,v^m\rangle
\]
presents $\mathbb Z$: the Whitehead map fixing $a$ and sending
$b\mapsto a^{-1}b$ takes $u$ to $ab^2$, so $u$ is primitive, and the
commutator relator already lies in its normal closure. After this exposure,
substituting $a=b^{-2}$ kills every relation.

The complete rank-two cut table gives the non-inner elementary length
changes on $(u,v^m)$ as $2m-2$, $2m-1$, $2$, and $3$, with complement
symmetries. Thus for $m\ge2$ every non-inner elementary Whitehead move
increases length. Signed permutations and inner moves preserve this
obstruction and the absence of a singleton. The least one-step exposing
increase is $2m-2$, yet the following elimination leaves length zero.
For the selected exposure, $L=20m+5$, the intermediate length is $22m+3$,
and the raw bound is $U=34m$. This explains why optimizing only immediate
length can miss an affordable algebraic simplification.

It is a separation for the restricted automorphism policy. It does not
establish failure of our overlap-enabled group search, and these abstract
presentations are not a family of difficult knot diagrams. Nor is the
one-step increase a lower bound on every longer increasing path.

The fused verifier composes the automorphism with its solved quotient map
and evaluates that map directly on all original grammar roots. On
$P_{2^k}$, the base commutator maps to the empty word before repeated copies
are evaluated. A grammar with $k+51$ nodes therefore supports exact replay
with a literal image cap of 100. The cap is enforced at every needed grammar
node; this is a bounded small-image evaluator, not a general compressed
free-reduction algorithm. The supplied $k=1000$ certificate was successfully
replayed without expanding its enormous relator.

\subsection{Rank bounds do not establish completeness}

The explicit rank-first stage performs at most $r-1$ rank drops and
$2(r-1)$ elementary moves. With stored-letter cap $M$, Whitehead workspace
is at most $3M$. Choosing a quasi-polynomial cap bounds this particular
possibly inconclusive positive search, including replay, by a quasi-polynomial
function on native Wirtinger inputs. It does not prove that every unknot
passes the stage, that the greedy choice finds every affordable path, or
that an exponential complete fallback inherits this bound.

The report gives two distinct obstructions. A primitive word can have no
unit bridge until a preparatory move is made, so absence of a bridge is only
a negative answer to the depth-one query. More permanently,
$\langle a,b\mid a^2,a^3\rangle$ is infinite cyclic, while an automorphism
keeps both relators proper powers; neither can acquire a singleton.
Relator interaction is indispensable. Exposure should therefore cooperate
with certified relator replacement rather than replace it.

\subsection{Reproduction and actual maintained inputs}

All 49 delivered unit tests passed. The deterministic audit reproduced
17,664 occurrence and length checks on 1,104 cyclic words, 1,104 bridge
existence comparisons, 640 selector comparisons against literal enumeration,
468 checked witnesses, 500 independent flow comparisons, 500 rejected
capacity mutations, and 32 barrier presentations. All three supplied
positive certificates passed their respective replay checks. The five-case
maintained adapter smoke test also passed; this closes the delivery's
previously unexecuted compatibility check, without implying a new production
integration.

Our reproducible \path{data/audit_whitehead_exposure.py} recovers the original
archive from its arrival commit and reconstructs actual maintained PD
presentations. On the 30 small diagrams from the independent port-register
Khovanov corpus, it returns 23 positives and seven inconclusive results.
Every positive has independent explicit and compressed full-source replay
and agrees with the stored Khovanov oracle. None uses an exposure: existing
singletons suffice. External cancellation propagates and a zero time budget
returns inconclusive.

We additionally ran the native adapter on the maintained 141-crossing
Gordian fixture. It uses one exposure, then stops at rank 11 with 97,684
letters. Reconstructing every recorded move from the native full presentation
and scanning the resulting relator graphs finds zero unit bridges. Thus the
final failure is not just an allocation cap. This reproduces the delivery's
negative residual through the actual maintained input boundary. Across all
31 diagrams there are 23 positives and eight inconclusive results, with one
exposure and no demonstrated new recognition coverage.

The delivery's reported selector speedups compare against exhaustive shore
enumeration, not our existing minimum-cut producer. We did not rerun those
timings or treat them as pipeline gains. The next useful experiment is a
bounded handoff from an intact maintained residual after ordinary group
search stalls, preserving relation slots, names, prior moves and input
binding. The supplied adapter restarts from the original PD and does not
implement that handoff. Its search and verification have separate work
allowances but a shared wall-clock callback; a production integration must
account for both. In particular, any overlap that creates a unit bridge in
the verified Gordian residual would be a concrete next algebraic target,
with affordable rank decrease as the criterion.
