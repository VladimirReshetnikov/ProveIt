# Timing provenance

Before the final protocol, an exploratory call tested seven width-five layers.
A subsequent five-repetition three-arm benchmark with that case exceeded the
execution allowance before producing a complete result file. Its partial log
was not pooled with the reported results and supplies no completed comparison
for that protocol.

The retained final benchmark was rerun from scratch with three repetitions,
six width-five layers, and the final hexadecimal-cost source encoding. Its
entire sample, order and A/A controls are in benchmark.json. The standalone
BFS search checker was added afterward; the timed kernel, assembly solver,
workload constructor and benchmark algorithm were not changed by that addition.
