A bad block may be any non-disc component with boundary under the gluing contract. The mesh audit chooses annuli as representatives; it does not certify arbitrary 3D embeddings. Closed components without seam contact may be excluded from the interface, but creation of closed surfaces by whole-boundary identifications is outside the separated-interval model.
