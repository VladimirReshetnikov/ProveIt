# Research questions following the proved results

## Geometric completeness

Prove a logarithmic excess bound for coherent discs in a source-checked triangulation family, including the cost and bit growth of any allowed retriangulations. Without this, the quasi-polynomial band optimizer is not a complete general recognizer.

Determine whether the one-extra-piece threshold can be strengthened. A particularly concrete target is a genuine knot-exterior example with a coherent disc at excess two but F* > 2D*, or a theorem excluding that phenomenon under useful triangulation hypotheses.

## Exact core topology

Use the at-most-k residual piece mass to optimize the primitive core rather than total Euler characteristic. Preserve actual component placement: a small admissible normal-coordinate summand is not automatically a disjoint component of a larger vector. Investigate whether arithmetic flow improvements have a directly recoverable geometric null-component deletion.

## Parameter dependence

Improve the T^{O(k)} support-enumeration dependence or prove an appropriate obstruction. Explore optimal-dual selection, repeated corner functions, and incremental reuse of feasibility and flow certificates. The complete band must remain unchanged when tied extrema or released equalities occur.

## Boundary patterns and hierarchy use

Characterize boundary constraints preserved under deletion of null unions. This is needed for extending the connected-output theorem to pattern-constrained hierarchy surfaces. Separate Euler optimization, boundary essentiality, annulus capping, and source validation rather than hiding them in a single objective.

## Native evaluation and verification

Run the new objectives against the source-bound native corpus, particularly the documented failure of the minimum-span Euler face on an unknot and the disconnected primitive trefoil regression. Measure total portfolio cost and new verified positives, not only inner network time. Formalize the finite certificate algebra and develop a new native source-bound verifier before enabling a production stage.

The article's Section 12 gives these questions more precise hypotheses and explains why the existing results do not already answer them.
