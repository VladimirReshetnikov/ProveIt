# Claim ledger

## Proved in the article

- A weighted stopping set persists under every positive singleton pivot, including when the pivot row belongs to that set.
- Full positive elimination, private-column peeling, absence of stopping obstructions and existence of a permanent-one row-spanning minor are equivalent.
- The maximal stopping residual is independent of peeling order.
- The inspected minimum-fill scoring rule is complete for the full-success matrix decision, and every successful run has zero fill.
- Sparse private-column production and timestamp verification require linear incidence work with compact array-indexed labels. Python map costs and the retained native heap path are separately qualified.
- Uniformly nonunit omitted columns do not change full eliminability or stopping cores.
- Minimum deferred rows equal the minimum transversal of nonempty stopping sets; maximum positive pivots equal rows minus that number.
- Small-deferral enumeration is exact and is quasi-polynomial when the deferral budget is logarithmic in encoded input length. This is a local matrix statement.
- Exact passive counts equal a finite positive path sum and have polynomial bit length.
- Geometric three-ball handle presentations can have linearly growing deferral number. This is not claimed to be a family exported by the current normal-cut implementation.

The classical stopping-set/peeling, triangular-matching, and inactivation ingredients are attributed. No exhaustive historical priority claim is made.

## Executed evidence

34 unit tests pass. The full-decision audit covers 31,907 matrices, plus 1,020 explicit obstruction-invariant cases and 1,241 optimal-deferral cases. Exact transfer has 400 randomized oracle comparisons and binary stress tests. All sample timings and regressions are retained. The benchmark includes the exact old/proposed *compositions of transcribed/reference matrix functions*, not the complete native source geometry pipeline.

## Proposed, not production-validated

The source-pinned integration adds completion-oriented producer fallbacks and a core-verifier timestamp fast path. The applicator and helper are tested on local fixtures; actual full-checkout application and production regression testing were not run. The whole-cut boundary verifier is not modified.

## Not established or measured

There is no general quasi-polynomial unknot-recognition theorem here, no general logarithmic stopping/deferral bound, no source-geometric exporter for the counterfamily, no ordered boundary-pattern transport from counts alone, no full difficult-knot benchmark, and no proof-assistant formalization. The two relevant incoming ZIP bodies were not readable, so nonduplication against them remains to be checked.
