## Contribution and what is classical

The new development here is the **two-sided restricted-envelope theorem**:
for candidate refinements of `sigma` and cap refinements of `rho`, the exact
binary completion rank is `2^beta1(G(sigma,rho))`, with binomial graded ranks
and a matching sharp family for every connected envelope. It is accompanied by
a cycle-coordinate construction, polynomial safe-envelope compiler,
whole-context composition proof, independent certificates, and executable
finite-language comparisons.

The greedy cost-ordered basis principle, Gaussian elimination, exterior
coordinates, linear-matroid representative families, and the use of connectivity
overlap are classical techniques. No claim of worldwide priority for every
algebraically equivalent formulation is made. The article proves the stated
result rather than relying on novelty as evidence of correctness.
