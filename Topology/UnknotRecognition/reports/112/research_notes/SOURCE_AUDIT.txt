PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL SOURCES

The computational complexity of knot genus and spanning area.
Transactions of the AMS 358 (2006), 3821-3850.
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0205057
The article relies on the established polynomial orbit-counting and weighted
extension. It does not claim to originate that theorem.

Marc Lackenby announces a new unknot recognition algorithm that runs in
quasi-polynomial time.
https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/node/38304
This is the source for the announced complexity target.

Marc Lackenby, 2021 lecture slides:
https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/lackenby/quasipolynomial-talk.pdf

not included as redistributed external PDFs.

Incompressible surfaces, hierarchies and unknot recognition.
arXiv:2607.23350v1, 25 July 2026.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23350v1

from the unresolved implementation-wide running-time claim.
