PROOF AND IMPLEMENTATION REVIEW
Lazy Weight Replay and Native Chamber Graphs for Unknot Recognition
Source pin: e0b2f802f336fcb51e9ec5f29174cfe28dfb9d39

This note records the conclusions of independent static reviews. The article
contains the complete proofs; executable observations are in results/.

1. Run-tree representation

Leaves store positive integer lengths and fixed-dimensional integer vectors.
Internal nodes store total length, leaf count, AVL height, endpoint run lengths
and effective values, and an optional uniform vector tag. A parent's endpoint
caches include its own tag; child caches do not include their parent's tag.
Operations create new nodes and preserve all existing shared nodes.

All rotations, splits, and structural joins push tags before changing their
scope. Uniform addition is injective on vector values, so internal canonical
run inequalities are preserved. Coalescing equal seam leaves cannot cascade:
their former neighbors were unequal to that same effective value.

The logarithmic split proof charges AVL join-height increases separately to
the accumulating left and right fragments. Both charges telescope. Charging
every join by a fresh logarithm would give an unnecessarily weaker bound.

2. Exact arithmetic and numerical size

Translation uses one arithmetic-progression anchor and actual suffix
derivative jumps before modular reduction. The event at residue zero is
omitted during integration because the independent anchor fixes that value.
Reflection maps [l,h) to [K-h+1,K-l+1) and reverses run order.

The coefficient proof uses a mathematical pointwise partition of original
weighted atoms. Stored bases and tags represent disjoint parts of an active
point's allocation. Zero-sum parts can be rebased without changing stored
numbers. This establishes the A bound for tags, endpoint caches and inherited
tag sums, and the constant-times-A derivative bounds. The atoms are not
materialized by the algorithm.

The fixed vector dimension is authenticated by the public source parser.
Private transport helpers rely on this contract and are not standalone
certificate parsers. Source bit-reading costs include coefficients attached
to empty intervals, even though such coefficients do not contribute to A.

3. Cost claims

The tree and residue-sort bounds are deterministic. Initial endpoint
dictionaries, histogram dictionaries, and unweighted pairing-row maintenance
are additional costs. The complete-star record-work theorem explicitly
charges map operations; it does not assume worst-case constant-time Python
hashing. Event dispatch contributes an E term even when an event changes no
weights. A polynomial source-only claim refers to produced polynomial
traces; an arbitrary supplied certificate has its own encoded input length.

Metrics distinguish logical receiver-run counts from visited source leaves.
The maximum-runs counter observes completed events; the theorem separately
bounds transient seam growth. Cooperative callbacks are polled at the
documented work boundaries. No constant real-time cancellation latency is
claimed inside sorting or an individual large-integer operation.

4. Native chamber geometry

Component labels refer to one actual embedded normal surface. The singleton
power-of-two census detects both omitted components and multiple proposed
labels on one component. The orientation-double count detects one-sided
components without assuming that coordinate gcd determines connectedness.

Triangle-arm and quadrilateral-chain marker formulas were checked against
the independently reconstructed horizontal-face charts. Global edge ranks
include both source orientation and directed-local-edge reversal.

For a nonempty accepted two-sided surface, every cut chamber touches a
marked side because the original manifold is connected. Its mask is
therefore nonzero and unique. The empty surface is a separate one-vertex
case with mask zero. The theorem states the nonempty hypothesis explicitly.

The full-cut regluing proof gives every labelled subcut partition. The
spanning-tree maximum-reward conclusion assumes nonnegative rewards. The
cycle-rank obstruction uses compact properly embedded surfaces, so its
relative homology intersection pairing is well defined.

5. Scope and attribution

Classical polynomial weighted orbit counting, the native R0+2T run bound,
the abstract cographic optimizer, and the dependency-reset theory are earlier
results. The new contributions are the receiver-independent native replay
construction and separation family, and the source-authenticated native
chamber interface.

Independent certificate checks share the stated finite source validators,
integer codec and existing normal-arc/orbit primitives. No end-to-end
quasipolynomial recognition theorem, geometric edit-persistence certificate,
or proof-assistant formalization is asserted.
