# Further research questions

Full formulations and motivation are in Section 11 of the article.

1. Prove a source-complete, polynomial-size exporter from native marked parallelity/core boundary data to edge-owned graph grammars, with source charts and embedded corner identities.
2. Implement and independently certify precompiled XOR cycle/transport maps; measure whether they eliminate the observed small-width regression.
3. Implement a separately named minimum-only gauge certificate using the proved descriptor-dominance proposition. Compare it fairly with the existing minimum-state backend.
4. Construct authenticated spherical nooses and violating discs from succinct graph coloring, cut-edge, and mark witnesses; bound backward disc transport through compressed hierarchy charts.
5. Identify which native geometric changes preserve unaffected graph sub-DAGs and their source interpretations. Combine those witnesses with genuine causal-restart accounting, rather than graph ancestry alone.
6. Sharpen the fixed-threshold constants by characterizing attainable local tags, while respecting the star's degree-k lower bound.
7. Extend the construction to finite complement-equivariant observation algebras with controlled zero-cost normalized states.
8. Establish a restricted but nontrivial native recognition theorem whose source production, local graph queries, geometric transports, and hierarchy repairs satisfy one common encoded-size recurrence.
