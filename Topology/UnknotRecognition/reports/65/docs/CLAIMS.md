## Explicit nonclaims

No general quasi-polynomial unknot algorithm; no default recognizer speedup; no native runtime patch; no proof-assistant verification; no exhaustive worldwide novelty claim; no preservation of arbitrary coning guards, full physical states, solution counts or Pareto sets; no compression of exponentially many genuine arcs merely by giving their bundle one label.

The rank-based and linear-matroid framework is prior work. The new report's contribution is the exact disk-specific derivation and sharpness, surface contract, context theorem, independent checks, and repository-directed continuation.
