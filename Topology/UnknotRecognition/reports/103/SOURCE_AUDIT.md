## Primary mathematical references

1. Marc Lackenby, *Incompressible surfaces, hierarchies and unknot recognition*, [arXiv:2607.23350v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23350v1), 25 July 2026. Section 9 explicitly leaves running time unestimated. Proposition 9.1, Proposition 10.2, and Theorem 14.1 are used with their separate step-count, hierarchy-length, and certificate-verification scopes. They are not cited as a completed quasipolynomial discovery algorithm.
2. Marc Lackenby, *Unknot recognition in quasi-polynomial time*, [February 2021 lecture slides](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/lackenby/quasipolynomial-talk.pdf), 109 pages. The announcement `n^{O(log n)}` appears on PDF pages 13–14. The later Oxford slide file is a distinct version; this article identifies the version it uses.
3. Adele Jackson, *Minimal triangulation size of Seifert fibered spaces with boundary*, [arXiv:2301.02085v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.02085v1), 5 January 2023. Lemma 3.2 gives classical unmarked elimination of three-handles; the following discussion distinguishes parallelity-bundle topology from its inclusion in the ambient manifold.
4. Jack Edmonds and D. R. Fulkerson, *Transversals and matroid partition*, Journal of Research of the National Bureau of Standards 69B(3), 147–153 (1965), [DOI:10.6028/jres.069B.016](https://doi.org/10.6028/jres.069B.016). [Original publication](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/69B/jresv69Bn3p147_A1b.pdf). The article specializes the classical partition/rank theorem and gives a constructive graphic exchange argument; it does not claim a new general matroid-union theorem.
5. Ilya Kapovich, *Compressed primitivity problem in free groups*, [arXiv:2607.21499v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21499v1), July 2026. Theorems 1.1–1.2 and Example 1.3 motivate the distinction between compressed batch operations and exponentially many elementary descent steps. No geometric curve-slide theorem is inferred from the algebraic result.

These primary sources were inspected for the specific statements used. Full papers and slides are not included in the archive.
