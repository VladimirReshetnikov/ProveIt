8188525b70033dcfe7c51ea5ae2c8723ad0c0198.

The upstream MIT No Attribution License is preserved in reference/ProveIt_LICENSE.

The frozen discovery corpus and reference/sector_envelope.py are copied unchanged
from docs/incoming/unknot_minimum_envelopes_20261009.zip at the same commit.

prior generators and selection rule. The second boundary cap, planar algorithm,
coverage verifier, sparse constructor, new tests, and present research analysis
are this continuation's additions.

established minimum-diagram geometry to the primary literature. No third-party
research paper is redistributed in this package. Citations point to the sources.

The package records the exact experiments performed. It does not assert peer
review or a formal proof-assistant verification of the mathematical text or code.
