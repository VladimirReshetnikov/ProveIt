# Integration into ProveIt

## Intake without changing recognition semantics

Assign the next available report number during intake rather than overwriting
an existing numbered report. Preserve this directory as a self-contained
research package, and add a synthesis reference after mathematical review.
No ordinary recognition dispatcher or exact fallback needs to change.

The default independent checker is `replay.verify(source, certificate)`.
It shares the exact atlas data model and source validator, but imports neither
the flag producer nor its local flag enumeration. It counts possible flags
by an independent dynamic program and checks supplied chart maps directly.
Do not replace this by rerunning the producer and comparing bytes.

The adapter `morse_adapter.packet_from_flags` exports codimension-one covers
only after successful regularization replay. The predecessor uses inclusive
rank intervals, whereas the new atlas uses half-open intervals. The conversion
is explicit (`hi` becomes `hi-1`). Imported arithmetic validity alone does
not establish native manifoldhood or patterned source provenance.

## Required native normal-cut exporter

The article proves a polynomial construction from canonical raw normal cuts;
this complete exporter is **not** present in the executable delivery. Its
implementation must bind a source triangulation, unscaled normal coordinates,
and complete native raw-cut certificate to the atlas. It must:

1. Independently authenticate `normal-cut-cells-v1` geometry against its source.
2. Split original arm polygons at the tip/rectangle event, and chamber families
   at the chain endpoint and triangle-tip events. Preserve all source ranks.
3. Supply exact convex local prototypes, including exceptional chambers; retain
   local face occurrences even when their global cell names coincide.
4. Recover every proper-face chart, not just matrix coefficients or signed
   codimension-one sums. Verify all nested-face squares.
5. Retain complete old/new boundary labels as downward-closed marked intervals.
   Arbitrary noncellular boundary curves are outside this input contract.
6. Check the linear family/record bounds and coordinate-bit accounting.
7. Cross-check nonzero, mixed, one-sided, self-glued and large binary normal
   vectors against independent literal local geometry at small sizes.

The delivered `tetrahedral_atlas` is a finite source importer, not this exporter.
Its native fixture is the repository's exact *empty* normal cut. Its source
acceptance checks reciprocal face gluings and absence of nontrivial local-face
holonomy; it does not classify all vertex links or establish manifoldhood.

## Geometry and downstream conclusions

A regular polytopal quotient is not automatically a 3-manifold. A marked
subcomplex is not automatically an authenticated Lackenby boundary pattern.
A collapse or homotopy equivalence does not automatically supply a homeomorphic
replacement with the correct pattern. Keep these gates separate.

Acyclic one-critical-vertex Morse evidence on an authenticated compact PL
3-manifold gives a ball witness, using the classical theorem stated in the
article. Matching discovery is not implemented for general cut components.
The convex-cone matching bridge accepts only its exact source constructor;
one million disjoint balls yield one million critical vertices, not one.

One flag subdivision yields a regular simplicial CW complex. Distinct edges or
simplices may still have the same quotient vertex set. Never deduplicate them
by their vertex sets. A second subdivision yields an abstract simplicial
complex when that stronger representation is required. Prefer one pass for
regular-CW Morse consumers; a second pass adds substantial constants.

Do not globally propagate rank breakpoints to force all maps to permute
interval atoms. The article gives an exponential obstruction on a periodic
circle. Source occurrence maps are checked compositionally, without that
normalization.

## Production hardening before exposing untrusted input

Set explicit byte, integer-bit, family, chart, local-prototype and execution
budgets before decoding external files. The research codec avoids Python's
large-decimal-integer limit, but is not a hostile-input service protocol.
Cancellation callbacks propagate through compiler and replay; literal reference
routines have separate numerical-size caps. A cancellation or budget exhaustion
must remain inconclusive, never an unknot or knotted conclusion.

After the native exporter and geometry gates are implemented, run the complete
maintained suite and paired *full* source-to-output measurements. Existing local
benchmarks start with the supplied atlas and therefore exclude native exporter
cost. A global quasi-polynomial claim additionally needs surface discovery,
admissibility repair, pattern transport, reset/depth bounds and total size
accounting. None follows merely from the `4607 T` local bound.
