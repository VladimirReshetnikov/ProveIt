## Executed counts

The 8,061 matrix/graph audit inputs contain 3,117 positives and 4,944 negatives according to exhaustive tree enumeration. All positives were certified; 3,391 negative inputs received exact local certificates and 1,553 returned UNKNOWN. Another 300 planted positive trials were all certified in 318 sampled rounds. These totals do not claim that every negative was decided.

The 71 unit tests supplement the conventional proofs; finite tests do not prove asymptotic complexity. Abstract benchmark families are not described as knot diagrams or as geometrically realized normal cuts. Exact parity compatibility is explicitly weaker than geometric realizability.

## Corrections and interpretation safeguards

The prior deterministic algorithm is not being replaced by a deterministic general single-exponential result: the new general theorem uses one-sided randomness. The exact deterministic negative protocol is restricted to `q=0, r<=b`. Sampled negative frontiers in other regimes are not accepted.

The prior loop example was already coverable by its selector. This contribution improves a comparison path and supplies another valid local witness; it does not newly establish that source's topology. Literal testing remains faster on that small example.

The largest historical/new ratio comes from an old branch explosion on a graph with only six trees; it is not an end-to-end knot speedup. Regressions and literal controls remain in the tables.
