## Actually executed

50 unit-test methods; 31,581 exhaustive matrix-case roles, including overlapping families; 63,162 optimized plans; 1,241 additional all-positive-pivot comparisons; 1,000 weighted, 500 restricted, 500 relabeling, and 1,000 transport comparisons; 300 parity graph contexts with 4,800 spanning-tree comparisons; 240 literal typed-path tables. Further large analytic examples and all saved binary-table products replay. Zero discrepancies were found.

Seven-round paired synthetic matrix and equal-query transport benchmarks retain small-input regressions and A/A controls. Complete symbolic query timings include construction, all-table replay and serialization. No inferred timings from timeouts.
