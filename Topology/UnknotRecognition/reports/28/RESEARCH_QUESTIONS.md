# Further research

1. Prove efficiently constructible polylogarithmic reset gaps for substantial topological families, including all preprocessing and representation costs.
2. Find actual classical diagrams exposing nonsingleton mixed-coefficient first-jet blocks, with or without verified scalar Fitting splitting. Preserve unsuccessful searches and compare with existing Euler-assisted sharing.
3. Extend the closure observation to several link components without falsely identifying all marked variables. Study how much of their module structure is necessary.
4. Combine first-jet compression with survivor-first radical transfer for blocks involving several matchings, rather than treating every mixed-matching component as opaque.
5. Develop incremental matching completion and reset policies that retain the theorem's gap guarantees while avoiding repeated validation overhead on already-simple states.
6. Formalize the polynomial derivative, matrix conjugation, first-jet multiplier and sound whole-summand inference; reduce the verifier's dependence on the exact scanner.
7. Characterize what graded information, or which coefficient rings beyond F2, admit analogues of these rank-only reductions.

The article expands each question and separates a possible sufficient theorem from an established result.
