# Further research targets

The article's Section 14 develops each target with a concrete theorem or
implementation milestone.

1. Characterize depth-two and bounded-depth Whitehead exposure without
   enumerating every intermediate automorphism; include intermediate-size
   and search-width bounds.
2. Couple one replayable relator-overlap replacement with exposure. Use the
   exact rank-11 Gordian residual to search for the first bridge-creating
   replacement, not merely an arbitrary length decrease.
3. Optimize or approximate grammar-relative fused quotient image width,
   rather than the conservative raw substitution size U.
4. Prove amortized compressed representation and reduction bounds after
   successive rank drops, with exact provenance preserved.
5. Identify topological properties of unknot-derived presentations that
   force discoverable small-cost progress. Purely algebraic Z-presentations
   are insufficient for a general recognition theorem.
6. Share pinned-cut computations across inverse-pair patterns and certify
   global selector optimality compactly, not just one chosen pinned optimum.
7. Schedule strict descent, relator interactions, exposure, and fused replay
   fairly under a common verified end-to-end budget. Preserve negative data.
8. Formalize the occurrence identity, inverse-pair parity argument, and
   elementary replay kernel, then separately tackle completeness and cost.
