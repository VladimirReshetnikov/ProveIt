# Further research targets

The article gives the detailed rationale and success criteria. Priority is by dependency, not a prediction of difficulty.

1. **Complete upstream prefix regression.** Run both maintained scanners on pinned inputs and orders, compare all proper-prefix bounds with completed reduced ranks, and check every recovered grading.
2. **Certified partition production.** Produce a fixed signed suffix graph, terminal partition, and exact phase from suitable frontier geometry in polynomial boundary cost; prove when the construction applies.
3. **Interruptible inference budgets.** Bound observer work without modifying the scanner on failure or turning resource exhaustion into a verdict.
4. **Real determinant-one performance.** Measure first witness stages and avoided work on Conway, Kinoshita–Terasaka, and the repository's hard determinant-one cases; include paired full-pipeline controls.
5. **Joint root and order choice.** Construct orders that preserve a marked root while controlling witness cost, not just maximum frontier.
6. **Genuine tangle coherence.** Identify topological classes for which even/odd homology overlap in each quantum residue vanishes after local simplification.
7. **Dynamic singular kernels.** Update exact response and nullspace data under changes in the suffix, including rank-drop events and coefficient bit bounds.
8. **Topological critical square classes.** Realize the fixed-graph quotient contract in useful tangle families and translate the signed cofactor law into correctly normalized link determinant constraints.
9. **Stronger tractable cyclotomic observers.** Improve the residue shadow while proving the matching-completion oracle remains efficient.
10. **Physical-size and exposure theorems.** Prove a useful input family has an early decisive prefix with polylogarithmic width and logarithmic physical-size exponent, or construct an obstruction family for this observer class.
11. **Certified composition with existing accelerators.** Combine homological windows, twist compression, and marked component observation without losing guards, grading, or relative homotopy provenance.
12. **Formal certificates.** Formalize potential uniqueness, norm inequalities, the singular kernel, and square law first; then connect them to the maintained scanner's marked tangle semantics.
