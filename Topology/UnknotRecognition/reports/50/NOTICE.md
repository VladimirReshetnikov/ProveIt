# Attribution

The four files in `snapshots/fastunknot/` are exact maintained ProveIt source
blobs, distributed under the repository's MIT-0 terms. Their authorship is not
claimed by this research package. Git blob identities, SHA-256 hashes, and source
paths are recorded in `PROVENANCE.json`.

The existing AHT implementation and its local verifier, coning identity, and
canonical-union sharing are inherited. The weighted orbit theorem belongs to
Agol, Hass, and Thurston. Generic O(r*s) coverage reconstruction belongs to the
prior literature, including Chakrabarty and Huang. The article explains the new
integration and verification layer separately from these inputs.

No third-party research paper, lecture slide deck, or font file is redistributed.
The PDF article was generated from the included new LaTeX source.
