# Claim ledger

“Proved” means a complete mathematical argument in the article under its stated hypotheses. It does not mean formal proof-assistant verification, refereeing, or an exhaustive priority claim.

## Mathematical results

| Result | Precise scope | Attribution/boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Positive/private equivalence | The same original row subsets can be removed by arbitrary nonnegative fill-producing unit pivots and by private-column schedules, for unrestricted unit pivots. | Existing ProveIt stopping-set mathematics, reproved here. Not a statement about general signed/geometric slides. |
| Weighted matching formulation | Private schedules correspond to allowed-unit uniquely restricted matchings in the bipartite support graph, weighted by matched rows. | Classical matching/triangularity connection; unit restrictions and weights stated explicitly. |
| Ordered-interface DP | One total order and one matched bit per bag vertex suffice, with exact joins and `poly(N,B) 2^O(w log(w+2))` time for a supplied width-w decomposition. | Main algorithmic contribution. Incidence width, not row-interaction width; no guarantee for the width heuristic. |
| Forced prepeeling | The full private-column queue preserves optimal nonnegative deferred weight. Restricting the decomposition does not increase width. | An exact preprocessing result; wrapper currently requires unrestricted unit pivots. |
| Strict partial improvement | An explicit 4-by-4 matrix has inspected minimum-fill result two but optimal result three, with independent stopping-set upper bound and exact passive count ten. | Reference transcription of the pinned decision rule, not execution on authenticated native geometry. The old routine does not promise partial optimality. |
| Contextual transfer | Full order/flag tables compose by max-plus products. Equal tables preserve matched-weight queries in every compatible typed context. | Freshness, edge ownership, and type/weight-preserving boundary identification are required. For deferred weight also carry total fresh-row weight. |
| Binary powers | A repeat-safe template can be raised to a binary exponent in polynomial encoded time at fixed width, with all score bit lengths accounted for. | No expansion of the graph or flat schedule. General native compressed geometries are not automatically such templates. |
| Complete power-table proof | Independent leaf replay and all-candidate product inequalities certify the actual optimum. | A feasible matching alone does not certify optimality. |
| Connected rank barrier | The family H_q has incidence treewidth exactly two, full row rank, a unit maximal minor, and deferred weight equal to the sum of pairwise minimum costs. | Abstract incidence family; no native knot-exterior realization is asserted. |

The bipartite width exponent is smaller than the general-graph quadratic-width exponent in the inspected 2025 Chaudhary–Sau–Zehavi paper. It does not resolve the general-graph problem. The order method and its components may have antecedents beyond the targeted search; no absolute first-in-literature claim is made.

## Executed

52 unit-test methods pass, including 100 seeded preprocessing comparisons. The completed standalone audit has 36,901 exhaustive DP/subset instances; 19,683 of those also undergo independent arbitrary-positive-pivot and matching-uniqueness comparisons. Additional audit counts: 300 weighted/transport cases, 150 restricted-private cases, 80 different-decomposition checks, and 336 power/expansion comparisons. These counts are distinct test roles, not all disjoint input sets.

The paired benchmark measures the supplied-decomposition DP, parsing, validation and independent feasible replay, versus independent increasing-size deferred-set enumeration. The complete binary-power benchmark includes compilation, construction, independent optimality replay and serialization. No output is inferred from a capped run. Raw samples and the frozen `2^1024` certificate are included.

## Not established or run

No unrestricted quasi-polynomial unknot recognizer, no uniform native incidence-width theorem, no source exporter for all useful repeat-safe templates, no geometric residual-repair procedure, no protected-boundary transport theorem, and no global hierarchy state/rebuild bound are proved.

No native patch application, full production test suite, source-geometric corpus rerun, hard-knot benchmark, or end-to-end knot timing was performed. Source access was targeted through GitHub. The incoming private-cocore archive body was not inspected.

A nonempty stopping set or positive optimal deferral is not a knottedness, non-handlebody, or nonball certificate. Omitted isolated optimization columns remain actual geometric handles unless separately cancelled. Zero numerical deferred rows remain actual two-handles.
