## Proved in the article

1. A connected positive-integral-capacity graph with r terminals has at most 2 sum_{j<=k} binom(2r-3,j) low-cut terminal colorings when r>=2; exact two-edge flags add at most a factor of four.
2. For general pieces the independent root-color factor is 2^c, where c counts terminal-bearing components only. Hidden components have no additional terminal-color factor.
3. Fresh edge-owned terminal gluing and forgetting preserve complete truncated marked responses.
4. Cost-feasible pairs obey a degree-k bound in the combined terminal skeleton size. The joined-star family proves this degree sharp for explicit state enumeration.
5. Supplied connected graph grammars have a polynomial-time evaluator for fixed k, including k=3, with arbitrary nesting and interface width.
6. The displayed component/threshold parameter condition gives a local n^{O(log n)} bound.
7. Connectivity counts and cubic metadata compose exactly. Witness DAGs and at most k original cut-edge occurrence addresses can be checked without expansion.
