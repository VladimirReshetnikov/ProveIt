# Literature and attribution notes

Date of research: 19 September 2026.

## Primary target

Vladimir Reshetnikov, *An explicit series representation for the analytic
tetration with complex height*, MathOverflow question 259278, posted
10 January 2017, with subsequent answers and comments:

https://mathoverflow.net/questions/259278/an-explicit-series-representation-for-the-analytic-tetration-with-complex-height

The question's Conjecture 1 concerns the q-series on Re(z)>-2. Conjectures 3
and 4 concern complete monotonicity and uniqueness. The manuscript supplies
proofs of these assertions in the stated subcritical real-base setting, as
well as a sharp boundary theorem. It proves the period and first-boundary
parts of Conjecture 2, not its entire global continuation assertion.

The first answer's identification of the series is conditional on its
convergence. The second proposes a uniqueness argument through periodic
perturbations; a comment by its author on 15 January 2017 acknowledges a
flaw. A third answer links a broader manuscript about iterated functions.
That advertisement is not treated as either a verified proof of the present
sharp theorem or evidence that the linked work contains no relevant result.

## Earlier positivity work

Vladimir Reshetnikov, *Complete monotonicity of a sequence related to
tetration*, Mathematics Stack Exchange question 1987944, 27 October 2016,
with answers by Sheldon L:

https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/1987944/complete-monotonicity-of-a-sequence-related-to-tetration/1996129

This discussion already connects complete monotonicity with signs of
inverse-Schroeder coefficients. That conceptual approach is prior work and
is credited as such. The present article gives a positive quadratic
recurrence, an exact complex radius argument, and a dominant-singularity
analysis needed for its sharp interpolation theorem.

## Classical tools

G. Koenigs, *Recherches sur les integrales de certaines equations
fonctionnelles*, Annales scientifiques de l'Ecole Normale Superieure,
series 3, volume 1 (1884), supplement 3-41; DOI 10.24033/asens.247:

https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1884_3_1__S3_0/

The required local linearization and its positive-real extension are proved
in the manuscript. This classical construction is not presented as new.

R. L. Schilling, R. Song, Z. Vondracek, *Bernstein Functions: Theory and
Applications*, second edition, De Gruyter, 2012. Authors' book page:

https://www.motapa.de/bernstein_functions/

The alternative moment-based uniqueness argument invokes the classical
Bernstein representation theorem. The stronger eventual-log-convexity proof
does not depend on that theorem. The author page was used for bibliographic
information; no claim is made to have retrieved the entire book.

## Related asymptotic question

Vladimir Reshetnikov, *Convergence of power towers*, Mathematics Stack
Exchange question 800862, 18 May 2014:

https://math.stackexchange.com/questions/800862/convergence-of-power-towers

The article derives a convergent large-height expansion and identifies its
normalization constant. This is a consequence of the constructed positive
spectral series, not the main problem selected for the research attempt.

## Limit of the status claim

No complete proof of the sharp boundary theorem was located in the examined
sources. This does not establish that it is unpublished or unknown. The
manuscript's mathematical arguments and its literature-priority status are
separate matters. Independent expert checking and a broader bibliographic
search remain appropriate before presenting this as a new published result.
No external papers or font files are redistributed in this package.
