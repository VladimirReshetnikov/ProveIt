## Relationship to source 11's research questions

- **Question 1: crossing the real axis.** Addressed by a confluent Hermite
  contour formula, smooth parity conditions, a complete inverse criterion,
  and the invariant homology-character classification in every h.
- **Question 4: stable inverse operators.** Addressed in compact-set topology
  and by the finite/infinite criterion for continuous linear period realization.
  Sharp boundary Sobolev and Hölder estimates are not claimed.
- **Question 5: infinite connectivity with growth control.** Unrestricted
  realization is proved. Positive continuous nonlinear and fixed-envelope
  linear sections are also proved. Optimal growth conditions near accumulating
  boundary components and fixed analytic norm spaces are not characterized.

The original quaternionic material already considered real-axis domains.
The present claim is not that this issue was entirely absent in dimension four:
it is the all-h, arbitrary-domain extension and its combined topology/stability
classification. The source report's current and Hermite identity are explicitly
credited and reproved, not relabeled as new.

records a relevant recent literature check rather than an imported theorem.
The focused comparison does not establish historical priority or absence of
analogous results elsewhere.
