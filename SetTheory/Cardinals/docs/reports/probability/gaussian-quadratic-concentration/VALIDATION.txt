Sharp Gaussian Quadratic Concentration from Signed Spectral Moments
8 October 2026

MATHEMATICAL REVIEW

The manuscript contains proofs of the signed spectral envelope, its optimized
rate, the complete cubic-balance coefficient frontier, and finite-matrix
sharpness for every exact real cubic ratio. Separate internal review passes
checked these arguments, the Radau moment extremality and optimizer, the
nonnegative remainder, the exact rational example and its first asymptotic
correction, and the stated applications.

Review led to explicit scope clarifications: the four-moment extremum is over
a probability-measure moment relaxation; realizability by finite matrices
requires additional multiplicity conditions. The diagonal-free subgaussian
extension transfers Chernoff bounds derived from Gaussian MGF envelopes,
not Gaussian-specific exact probability identities or finite CDF sandwiches.

This is an internally reviewed research draft. There has been no external
mathematical peer review or proof-assistant formalization. The source audit
does not guarantee priority. PROVENANCE.txt explains these distinctions.
