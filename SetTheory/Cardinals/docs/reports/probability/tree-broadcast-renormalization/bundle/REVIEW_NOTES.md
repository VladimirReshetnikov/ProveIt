## Nature of review

- First block crossings, strict annular inequalities, signed separation, and distinct original indices.
- Two-sided stability, half-open grid boundaries, and the two different window-span bounds.
- Conditional first-default routing and distinct terminal addresses after all selectors are fixed.
- Full polynomial sign vectors, denominator-square clearing, and choosing representatives before exposing remaining randomness.

- Closed residual projection using the original continuous functions, not discontinuous selected samples.

- Finite rational certificates from compact outer approximants without a stability or emptiness oracle.
- The computable measure modulus and the distinction from a computable distance function.

One definition was tightened: the degree bound now uses
Delta_N=max_(n<=N)(1+max(deg P_n,deg D_n)).
This handles zero numerator polynomials using the standard degree convention. The published citation to Gao's paper was corrected to include Mooroogen and Yip. No unresolved mathematical gap was found in these passes.
