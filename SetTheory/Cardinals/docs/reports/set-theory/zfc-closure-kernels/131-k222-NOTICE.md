# Attribution and scope

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository. New prose, scripts and
certificates use the MIT No Attribution terms in LICENSE. Existing third-party
files retain their own terms, which this notice does not replace or expand.

The new contribution is the composition of the merged equality-row and
witness-request 223-state constructions, its compatible closed grammar and
helper allocation, the exact 222-state machine, and fresh source, logic and
runtime certificates. Neither the K theory nor its seed reformulation is
claimed as new: these are inherited from Elliot Glazer's K230 development,
pinned at 3142e4dd980f0be12f4abb636c4ae2528afc17eb.

The two immediate 223-state contribution notices are retained in portable/vendor.
The unchanged K224 predecessor archive retains the K225/K226, Source116 and
K230 code and their provenance, including work credited to Mira, ProveIt,
Jeff's busy-beaver development, Andrew James Wade, BusyCoq and the Coq Library
of Undecidability. MIT, MIT X11, MIT No Attribution and MPL2.0 notices remain
with the corresponding materials. No new repository-wide license is asserted
for upstream files whose licensing is unspecified.

The exact 222-state endpoint is an ordinary mathematical proof formalizable
in ZFC, with executable exact and unbounded induction certificates. It is not
a newly compiled Rocq/Lean kernel theorem or a new internal PA proof-code
endpoint. No consistency assumption is used to state the equivalence; one
would be needed to conclude actual nonhalting.
