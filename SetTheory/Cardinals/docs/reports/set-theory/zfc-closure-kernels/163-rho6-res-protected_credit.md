# Finite protected-prefix credit at any candidate cutoff

This strengthens the ordinary scheduler lemma in ProveIt
`k217-protected-tail-seed/proof.md`. It applies to the current dominated AX
wire as well, because it retains the same number of field reads and ten
stack pushes. It also applies after omission of the successful-MP zero
push, since its conservative MP stack change is already zero.

## Exact statement

Let `W(n)` be the complete finite command word generated by candidate `n`
with the retained successor convention. Write `b(n)` for its conservative
stack balance and `m(n)` for the minimum of its prefix balances, including
the empty prefix. The assigned changes are MP=0, DROP=1, NIP=-1, AX=10.
Let `s` satisfy `s(s+1)/2 <= N < (s+1)(s+2)/2` and let

`E(N)=max(6,(s+1)(s+2)/2)`.

Let `d(N)` be the nonnegative credit needed by the **finite** concatenation
`W(N)...W(E(N)-1)`: minus its minimum prefix balance.

At a complete command root with candidate cutoff `N`, empty proof pointer,
theorem top and cache, and stack `pushL(L,S)`, the arbitrary tail `S` is never
read if every member of `L` is a theorem and `length(L)>=d(N)`. No theoremhood
hypothesis is imposed on `S`. The additive counter is arbitrary.

For a nonzero initial proof pointer `p`, first compute `(b,m)` for `W(p-1)`.
The sufficient (and conservatively exact) credit is

`max(0,-m,d(N)-b)`.

This makes the tail theorem usable after the inherited arbitrary selector
and scratch absorption: compute the resulting virtual clean root, and
check the finite credit of its resulting cutoff and proof pointer.

## Why the finite horizon is sufficient

The earlier diagonal proof establishes a fact stronger than its stated
np=0/1 application: **every whole Cantor diagonal with index s>=3 has
nonnegative relative balance at all command prefixes**, starting from
balance zero. Its proof uses the arithmetic nonnegativity of the global
prefix sums of `b`, but no machine-stack premise. At the end of the
finite tail diagonal used to define `d(N)`, all later complete diagonals
therefore require no additional credit. The special horizon at least6
includes the only exceptional diagonal s=2, whose initial NIP requires
one unit. Thus the finite minimum equals the infimum over the entire
infinite conservative schedule beginning at N.

For completeness of this dependence, the inherited diagonal proof proceeds
as follows. Put `f(y)=second(unpair(y))`, `g(0)=0`, and `g(r)=b(r-1)` for
r>0. Nonnegative prefix sums are preserved by pullback along f, because
f enumerates `[0],[0,1],[0,1,2],...`. On diagonal s>=3, the first s-2
words begin with AX. Their suffix balances are `g(f^3(y))`, with y ranging
from0 through s-3. By the prefix-pullback lemma, the first k complete AX
words have balance at least10k. Within the AX at position y, the suffix
loses at most `f^3(y)<=y`, so the relative balance is at least9y+10 after
its first command. The final NIP, DROP, MP words leave the respective
conservative lower bounds9s-19,8s-17,7s-17, all positive for s>=3.
The global prefix premises used here involve only candidate indices below
the current diagonal, so induction is well-founded.

## Soundness and completeness

Track a finite list of theorem codes above the fixed arbitrary tail.
Its length is at least the initial credit plus the conservative balance;
successful MP can only add further safe zeros if retained. Immediately
before every NIP this quantity is at least one, since the post-NIP
conservative balance stays nonnegative. Hence NIP always pops a theorem
pushed above the arbitrary tail. AX, MP, DROP and NIP otherwise preserve
the existing theoremhood invariant. Halting can occur only at a theorem
cache equal to falsity. This is a soundness argument, not an assumption
that the native theory is consistent.

The inherited strong command compiler and cofinal refuting candidate
family prove the converse for arbitrary retained tuples and every finite
cutoff. Together these give the same native-refutation equivalence from
the new protected-prefix seeds. All claims concern the exact abstract
source scheduler; physical initialization and full binary runtime remain
separate obligations.

In particular, an arbitrary initial stack is allowed at every triangular

its first command is NIP. One theorem prefix entry suffices there.
