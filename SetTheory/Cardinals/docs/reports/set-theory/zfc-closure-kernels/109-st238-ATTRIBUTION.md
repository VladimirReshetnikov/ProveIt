# Attribution and dependency boundaries

This package is intended as a research contribution to ProveIt. Its new material is the verified composition of two incoming state reductions, the exact 238-state tables and deterministic builder, the compatibility proof, independent source and open-tape checks, the canonical initializer audit, the integrated replay, and the supplementary three-way structural witness-sharing interpretation.
