4. PRESENT CONTRIBUTIONS AND TRUST BOUNDARY

The relative-consistency initializer theorem, concrete Cantor-negation
substitutions, changed decoder artifacts, new synthesized-action search
drivers, relational generator, independent zone and ledger checkers,
composable register-box oracle, mathematical report, and portable integration
wrappers are the present research contribution.

The underlying finite-atom model method and Cantor algebra are standard
mathematics; no claim of historical priority is made for them.

Finite reports are executable research evidence. They are not new
proof-assistant theorems, and the packet supplies no verified construction
with fewer than 252 working states. The imported register-box contracts and
the exact simulation/initialization obligations are documented separately.
