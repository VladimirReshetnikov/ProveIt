# Attribution and scope

Prepared on 21 October 2026 for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository,
through ChatGPT-assisted mathematical research, implementation, and independent
parallel review.

The principal new machine work is the exact 215-state composition of the
credited Partial Selector and Permuted Commands constructions, its constrained
fixed decoder allocation, deterministic reconstruction, and fresh universal
runtime and complete blank-tape checks.

Additional new work consists of the finite total-projection axiom basis and its exact
support analysis; the numerical-gap corollary; the scoped matrix analysis;
the residual-parameter command compiler; independent verification scripts;
the additional physical-search families; source transformations and measured
allocation results; and the concrete shortened-scanner counterexample.

The accepted K217 machine is **inherited**, not a new construction in this
packet. Its source, numerical calculus, ZFC consistency calibration, original
proof certificate, source language, compiler, arithmetic runtime, and earlier
reductions belong to their upstream authors. The original candidate notices
are retained under `baseline/`; the source experiments retain their own vendor
pins and notices. `theory/UPSTREAM_NOTICE.md` is the unchanged Source149 notice
and describes that predecessor's contributions, not the new work listed above.

Those inherited notices credit Elliot Glazer, Mira, Jeff's busy-beaver
development, Andrew James Wade, BusyCoq, the Coq Library of Undecidability, and
ProveIt's accumulated reductions. The original contributions are not claimed
here. Third-party files keep their supplied license terms. New files are
provided under the same MIT No Attribution terms as the ProveIt predecessor.

During this run, the public repository added two 216-state submissions. The
permuted-command submission is retained unchanged under `upstream216/`, with
its original attribution and licenses. Its construction is credited to that
submission; this packet contributes a fresh selected reconstruction, complete
blank replay, and universal-runtime replay. The public Partial Selector submission is preserved unchanged under
`candidate215/parents/`. The separate reductions remain their authors’ work;
the additional state reduction is the new compatible 215-state composition.

The ordinary mathematical arguments are intended to be formalizable in ZFC. Finite Python
checks and independent review are identified precisely; neither is presented
as a newly compiled proof-assistant theorem for a changed binary table.
