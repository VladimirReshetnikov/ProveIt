# Attribution and construction history

This construction combines two predecessor ideas. The 216-state permuted-command packet supplies local additive-field readers, command permutations 210 and 120, and the arithmetic helper replacement 81/0. The separate 216-state partial-selector construction supplies partial-row provider synthesis and a marker fold of 164/183. Preserving only a provider's trigger row, rather than both source branches, makes that selector affordable. An interval-inherited allocation composes these ideas without an extra alias-splitting state.

The exact direct predecessor is bundled unchanged as parent/K216_Permuted_Commands_2026-10-21.zip. It contains its own predecessor archive, source-calibration proofs, numerical-calculus calibration, provenance and licenses. The current source proof directly relates each new source to its corresponding public 216 source; it does not require importing the other 216 table's correctness as an additional axiom. The partial-selector source layout was developed in K216_Partial_Selector_2026-10-20.zip, SHA256 05c4e63b64e64426fe1c909c0cd2490146fe13805b51f5016b6ff9dd8748ebcc, credited here for the construction idea.

No assertion of minimality or universal discovery priority is made. These artifacts extend the ProveIt project's continuing joint search and retain the predecessor licenses and attributions.
