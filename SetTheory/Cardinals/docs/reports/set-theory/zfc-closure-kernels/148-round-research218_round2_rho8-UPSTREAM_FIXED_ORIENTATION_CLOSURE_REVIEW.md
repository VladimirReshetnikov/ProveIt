## The invariant, restated independently

Fix one builder `W(b,c)=R(b,c)` or `R(c,b)`. The only extra builder properties needed are:

1. W is membership-headed, residue 1 modulo 4.
2. `W(b,c)>c`.
3. `W(b,c)=1` implies `b=c=0`.

Use literal numeric constructors, their injectivity, their distinct residues, `I(x,y)>=x,y`, and `I(0,0)=0`. Define

`p(b,c)=I(c,W(b,c))`,

`H(a,b,c)=I(I(p(b,c),a),I(I(a,c),I(b,c)))`,

`J(b,c)=I(I(p(b,c),c),I(b,c))`.

For `a=W(d,p(b,c))` and `h=I(I(a,c),I(b,c))`, define

`D(b,c,d)=I(I(h,p(b,c)),I(d,p(b,c)))`.

Let S have finite witnesses generated by all retained literal rows, all strengthened bundles just described, all H/J/D instances, all identities `I(x,x)`, GEN, and quantified monotonicity

`S(I(x,y)) => S(I(Q_i x,Q_i y))`.

MP is deliberately absent from this definition. Identities, monotonicity, J, and D are permitted overapproximations, not assumed theorems of the altered calculus.

Every S code has residue 0 or 2, so no membership or equality code is in S. A Q-headed witness must end in GEN and therefore can be stripped. The three old forbidden shapes remain excluded:

- `I(E(i,j),1)`;
- `I(Q_i I(Q_i x,1),1)`;
- `I(V(a,b,c),Q_k d)`.

The new H/J/D families all have I-headed immediate fields. This excludes them from the first two shapes by the antecedent tag and from the third by the consequent tag. The old retained-row, identity, monotonicity, and GEN exclusions remain unchanged. In particular, the arbitrary full-bundle cores create no new exceptional case.

## Independent check of the new inversions

Write `w=W(b,c)` and `p=p(b,c)`, noting `p>=w>c` and `p>0`.

### H major

An H major requires an S minor `I(p,a)`. Last-generator inspection gives exactly two possible outputs:

- Identity: `a=p`, so the H conclusion is J(b,c).
- H(x,y,z): injectivity gives `c=p(y,z)`, `x=W(b,c)`, and `a=I(I(x,z),I(y,z))`. The H conclusion is precisely D(y,z,b).

All other generators fail. Q/E antecedent tags exclude monotonicity and the corresponding retained rows. GEN has the wrong outer tag. Rho6 would give `W(b,c)=1`, hence `c=0`, but also `c=Q_i I(Q_i a,1)>0`. A bundle equates its Q-headed core with W. A J minor gives `c=p(y,z)>=z=W(b,c)>c`. A D minor equates its I-headed p(y,z) with W.

This retains the genuine H/H MP step found in our earlier work: H(a,b,c) is the antecedent of H(h,d,p) when `a=W(d,p)`, and the result is D(b,c,d). There is no incorrect no-detachment premise.

### J major

Its required minor is `I(p,c)`. Identity would force `p=c`. An H minor forces `x=w` and `c=I(I(x,z),I(y,z))>=x=w>c`. A J minor forces `z=w` and `c=I(y,z)>=z=w>c`. A D minor forces an I/R collision. The rho6 and bundle cases fail exactly as above; all remaining generators fail by tags. Thus no J major has an available minor.

### D major

Its required minor is `I(h,p)`. Identity gives `h=p`, whose right fields force `I(b,c)=w`. Rho6 gives `I(Q_i I(Q_i p,1),1)=h`, forcing `1=I(b,c)`. An H minor's right field equals p only if an I-headed formula equals w. A J minor first gives `y=c,z=w` from the right field, and then `z=I(b,c)` from the left field. A D minor gives `p(y,z)=w`. Bundle has right field 1 whereas p is I-headed. Every remaining generator is excluded by its antecedent or outer tag. Thus no D major has an available minor.

The zero alias creates no extra trigger: p is strictly positive. Constructor injectivity is numerical and remains valid when a subsidiary `I(0,0)` is zero. It is never legitimate to equate that zero with membership, equality, or quantifier tags, and no step does so.

## Why this proves full consistency

Induct on the finite S witness for a major `I(u,v)`, with an arbitrary S(u) witness as parameter. Identity returns the minor. GEN cannot produce the major. In the monotonicity case, strip the minor's Q, apply the induction hypothesis to the strictly smaller major witness, and rebuild Q. This is a well-founded induction on witnesses, not a circular appeal to MP closure.

For retained literal axioms, rho1/rho6/bundle cannot fire by the old forbidden shapes; rho3/rho5/G need an absent equality-headed minor; rho2 uses Q stripping and the monotonicity generator; rho4 strips and rebuilds two Qs in reverse order. H produces the admitted J or D, while J/D cannot fire. Hence S is MP-closed.

Actual derivations embed into S by finite induction: truth is identity at zero; every actual axiom is admitted; GEN and MP preserve S. F=1 is membership-headed and therefore absent. This is a finite syntactic argument formalizable in ZFC and does not need a model of ZFC or a search bound.

A further immediate consequence is `not Prov(I(1,0))`, where `I(1,0)=4`. No nonidentity generator has an R-headed antecedent; identity cannot equal `I(1,0)`. Thus even the Heyting-valid law F→truth is absent. The K instance `I(0,I(1,0))` is likewise not derivable, since truth and MP would yield code 4. This is an explicit surviving propositional-bootstrap obstruction.

## Reconciliation with the fresh-diagonal theorem

Our earlier theorem assumes a Heyting implication, bottom-valued irreflexive membership, truth as top, assignments closed under `s[c:=s(0)]`, and freshness/locality of the decoded positive code c. Under those assumptions the changed row at `(a,b)=(1,0)` forces every realized C to be double-negation stable and hence validates unrestricted rho8.

The new consistency proof assumes none of those semantic representation properties. S can be viewed as a designated subset of the raw natural-code term algebra: the operations are the numerical constructors themselves, not Heyting implication or an ordinary quantifier over assignments. There is no lattice, single top truth value, extensional equality, or relation-valued assignment model supplied. All equality-headed codes are undesignated, and even F→truth is absent. This is allowed by the user's acceptance of non-FOL and junk-code formalisms, but it is much too weak for the desired calibration.

Therefore there is no conflict. The semantic collapse theorem remains a conditional obstruction to a separating model in that particular model class. The syntactic proof shows why one cannot infer that model class from consistency of the changed calculus. Any ZFC-provable representation implication strong enough to recover Con(ZFC) from this now ZFC-provably consistent calculus would contradict the second incompleteness theorem, assuming ZFC is consistent.
