## Source 145 — ZFC_218_State_Search

**Retain.** The packet adds two 219-state layouts, valid source transformations, stronger countermodels, positive logical specializations, and bounded failed searches. It gives no smaller calibrated machine.

* **Exact rho6 reparameterization:** replacing the row family `D(a,c)` by `D(a,b)` preserves its entire instance range, with explicit parameter maps in both directions. This is an axiom-enumeration argument, not pointwise equality of whole AX batches. Two repeated push blocks become existing b-push blocks. The reported 219-state result has 4,744 leaves, DAG206, 6,167 guarded cases, 1,516,088 runtime steps and the original 312,617-step blank startup.
* **Contextual NOP:** the six-leaf insertion uses the proved zero values of registers 7 and 4 at its entry. Four inner leaves are unreachable; its two zero branches contract to the old control edge. The reported 219-state layout has 4,750 leaves, DAG206, 6,175 guarded cases and unchanged startup. The invariant is part of the proof; the insertion is not arbitrary-context identity.
* **Halt specialization:** at the unique entry the comparator's second input is zero. The replacement separates original input 0, 1 and at least 2 and terminates in every case; it preserves the outer source behavior. Its naïve physical allocator overwrites protected CI5 row38/0. Thus the raw 219 count is not a valid machine. The separate longer six-leaf alternatives are also source proofs, not accepted allocations.
* **T-only countermodel:** replacing rho6 `Ex_i Q_i A → A` by `Q_i A → A`, with no additional explosion rule, has a full two-world HC countermodel constructed in ZFC. Root membership is ordinary membership; future membership adds reflexivity and antichain-coded edges. The uncountable fiber construction makes the relevant subset test exactly object equality. Countable witness closure supplies the literal K bundle, including its shadowed repeated binder and coincident public labels. This strengthens earlier relative T countermodels to a ZFC-provable consistency obstruction. The fact that the future world makes falsity true is essential: adding EXP invalidates this model.
* **Outer-head obstruction:** the supplied four-value implication matrix has bad unrestricted-rho8 assignments only when its fourth input takes value 2. Implication, quantifier and equality roots avoid that value. Combined with the HC construction, this rules out the stated infinite family of fourth-input formulas, not merely the enumerated examples. It does not establish an obstruction for arbitrary code-dependent membership terms or combinations of several distinct fixed orientations.
* **Fixed public variables:** retaining the full V component and classical basis permits a genuine K specialization with public labels 0 and 1 and private label c+2. Universal closure, capture-avoiding renaming and the compiler give both consistency translations. The proof does not assume alpha-renaming before classical logic is available. One-fixed-label and predecessor-private variants account separately for exceptional small code collisions. These are useful calibrated logical alternatives, but the measured source/allocator costs are worse than the present machine.

## Source 146 — ZFC_218_Research

**Retain.** This packet distinguishes a valid initialized 218-state core from its invalid blank startup, adds a separate 219-state layout, and develops useful new scheduler, logical and comparator contracts.

* **Initialized core:** deletion of301 and the 88/51 changes exploit the preceding decoder bit, with literal paths restoring the whole tape. The unchanged source has 4,744 leaves and DAG206; the reported initialized runtime covers 6,167 cases. The blank run reaches the invalid scan-exit context at step79 and a right-moving blank-ray loop at step85. Therefore this literal core is unconditionally nonhalting from blank and is not a ZFC-consistency machine. Correct decoder/runtime contracts do not repair initialization. Its table is distinct from accepted Source144's marker-based fold.
* **Alternative219:** a separately retained 35-row layout has its own source and full reported replay (6,167 cases, 1,512,606 steps, original startup). Disjoint-looking edits alone do not prove compatibility: shared helper row103 and contextual entry contracts must be checked after stacking.
* **General clean seeds:** at a complete represented source-root frame, every logical seed `[n,0,0,0,d,0,0,0,p]`, for arbitrary natural n,d,p, preserves halting/refutation equivalence. More generally the formula/cache/decoded stack must satisfy theoremhood. Soundness ignores n,d,p; completeness uses a reset prefix `START_U`, a uniform proof compiler, and a cofinal family `N_U = P(3,P(1,P(1,P(U,R))))`. An initial positive proof list drains without advancing the candidate counter. This extends the inherited K224/K230 contracts; it is ordinary mathematics, not a new Rocq build. It does not admit dirty theorem registers or partial physical frames, nor transfer old seed-dependent abstract domains automatically.
* **Every fixed power-of-two stride:** the four nested pairings satisfy `v2(N_(U+δ)-N_U)=v2(δ)-4` for positive δ divisible by16. Consequently `t ↦ N_(U0+16t)` is an isometry modulo every power of two and permutes residues. Successful refutation codes are cofinal in every fixed `2^k` residue class. A scheduler that drains each proof list and visits that fixed progression is complete. This is a potentially useful bootstrap/source redesign theorem, not a proof for variable strides or for the broken 218 initializer.
* **Fused D:** over retained unrestricted propositional rho8, `NN(Q_i A)→A` is theorem-equivalent to **T plus EXP**. All three displayed finite deduction directions are valid. This does not calibrate the replacement with the original theory: old rho6 is not derived. In particular it differs from the later K/S + `NN(Q_i A)→Q_i A` proposal that retains rho6. Its saved leaves and measured 221-or-larger allocations remain exploratory.
* **Terminal-cluster theorem:** for the stated constant-domain, reflexive-frame semantics with material implication, globally valid T+EXP and a nontrivial world, trivial worlds can be removed. On a terminal strongly connected component, the V and G rows force membership and equality constant; structural induction then makes every formula constant there. Quantifiers reduce to ordinary universal quantification and old rho6 holds. Thus a finite nontrivial model in this semantic class yields a model of the original calibrated theory. No representation/completeness theorem for consistent T+EXP is supplied, and infinite frames without terminal components are not ruled out.
* **Relative T+EXP countermodel:** the separate two-world construction starts with an ordinary model of the original theory. It satisfies T+EXP and the other rows while failing old rho6. This shows non-derivability relative to that model assumption; it is not the unconditional HC consistency result above. Equiconsistency could still hold between theories with different theorem sets.
* **Additional useful source contracts:** any proved membership atom is a valid contradiction target by universal instantiation and irreflexivity, but truth/equality/universal-truth codes are not. A new all-left comparator has a full natural-number proof: its reset loop decreases the sum until both registers are zero and its outer test distinguishes equality. It has 12 leaves and whole-source DAG206, loses existing Z sharing and introduces a right-CI6 entry obligation. Pair reversal and fresh-binder codecs are also logically sound only with their full update/renaming contracts. None has an accepted ≤217 allocation.

## Current comparison and useful next experiments

Sources140–144 already retain the ordinary equality/zero-terminal contracts, several decoder layouts, exact failed return edits and the accepted complete218 machine. These arrivals add materially stronger **T-only HC**, **fixed-public-label**, **arbitrary-clean-seed**, **2-adic progression**, **terminal-component**, and **all-left reset comparator** results. Their unique proofs and negative receipts should remain accessible as ancillary research, not be discarded because the main searches failed.

The archived claim that lower consistency for the three membership-fourth-input proposals remains open is now stale. The maintained `k219-fourth-parameter/six-orientations/README.md` records an obstruction for **each of all six fixed ordered distinct input orientations**. R(A,B) has a Lean-checked invariant; the other five extensions are ordinary proofs. This is not a theorem about a union of different rows. The archive's conditional 205-DAG sources therefore cannot be promoted as calibrated competitors.

Best justified ≤217 leads are: co-design startup with a general clean seed or fixed `2^k` candidate stride; exploit the invariant-backed halt target in a fresh allocator; measure the new all-left comparator against the actual218 helper contracts; and test calibrated row reparameterizations jointly with context changes. Every such move still needs a concrete source/codec bridge, complete represented-tape runtime proof and exact blank startup. No source DAG count, bounded search, finite algebra check or initialized-core proof supplies those missing obligations.

The row38 count discrepancy is the only concrete report correction found. The theory scopes above have no identified mathematical blocker. This review is independent of the packet authors and of the separate fresh machine-verification lane.
