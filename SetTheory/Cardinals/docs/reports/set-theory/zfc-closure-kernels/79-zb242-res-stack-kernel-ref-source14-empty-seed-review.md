## Mathematical audit

**EPSC removes the seed parameter.** Its active initial class is the class
of every raw empty object, expressed by `epsilon(x) := forall z, not(z in x)`.
There is no distinguished empty constant, Extensionality, logical equality,
profile-congruence axiom or separate IR. Full matrix/parameter generality,
classical equality-free first-order logic and nonempty domains are explicit.

The dependency order avoids circular bootstrap assumptions:

1. A false-matrix EPSC instance produces an empty object by support alone.
   That active empty input then gives exact globally subset-bounded constant
   classes, raw power objects and full-profile singletons.
2. The inherited gated full-profile singleton decoder is locally bounded in
   pure logic. Grafting `epsilon(x) and y subset a` at empty inputs and using
   the decoder at nonempty inputs produces a transitive hull containing `a`
   and every raw subset of `a`. A singleton intermediate remains nonempty,
   so its second decoding edge is not disabled by the graft.
3. This supplies raw Separation and Union before either is used to combine
   bounds. A common container for `a,b` uses the raw power of `b` when `a`
   is empty, or a disjoint empty/nonempty two-stage relation otherwise.
   Taking its raw union combines just two subset bounds. No family of local
   choices, Pairing or actual raw equality is assumed.
4. For a requested PSC matrix `rho` and seed `a`, use
   `R_a(x,y) := rho(x,y) or (epsilon(x) and y subset a)`.
   The two-bound lemma proves its local boundedness pointwise. Its EPSC hull
   contains every subset of `a`; separating its globally supported rho-outputs
   removes the extra seed outputs and gives the full PSC instance.
5. Conversely, the false PSC instance yields an empty `e`, and
   `x subset e iff epsilon(x)`. PSC at `e` gives EPSC directly.
