FC13B DISPATCH PACKING EXPERIMENT
Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt research, 18 October 2026.

The new material is formalism_dispatch_packing.py, README.md, and the generated
formalism_dispatch_results data. It proves and measures a selector-coordinate
packing construction; it does not provide a new Turing-machine implementation.

The five reference files are unchanged copies of the incoming source70
equational contribution incorporated in VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt at pin
e58338db7545909907e07a7603e8eb88625c527a. Exact repository paths and file hashes
are in reference/provenance.json. Inherited attribution and licensing remain
unchanged. No new authorship or license is assigned to those files.

The Boolean single-axiom theorem used by that backend is attributed to
William McCune, Robert Veroff, Branden Fitelson, Kenneth Harris, Andrew Feist,
and Larry Wos, Short Single Axioms for Boolean Algebra (2002).
https://www.cs.unm.edu/~mccune/papers/basax/v12.pdf

The original logical-consistency machine work remains attributed to
Mira-Cult, Jeff Ketchersid, Andrew James Wade, and the preceding ProveIt
contributions. This packet does not claim historical priority for generic
dispatch encoding, currying, equality completion, or term-algebra quotients.
