retain their original terms. No repository-wide relicensing is asserted.

The new contribution is three literal continuation-row variants, their exact
canonical tables and initialization receipts, and two independent ordinary
executable proofs of universal halting equivalence to that predecessor.
The source calculus is unchanged. The first checker is adapted only for
portable CLI input/output from `verify_alternate_rows.py`; the second
`check_joint_fold.py` is retained byte for byte.

The retained predecessor credits Elliot Glazer's K230 development, pinned at
commit `3142e4dd980f0be12f4abb636c4ae2528afc17eb`, for the K theory, seed
reformulation, K0 interpretation and original consistency calibration. Earlier
credits include Mira, ProveIt, Jeff's busy-beaver development, Andrew James Wade,
BusyCoq, and the Coq Library of Undecidability. MIT, MIT X11, MIT No Attribution,
and MPL 2.0 notices remain inside the unchanged inherited packets. Materials
with unspecified upstream licensing are not relicensed here.

The proof endpoint is ordinary mathematics formalizable in ZFC, not a newly
compiled exact-byte Rocq or Lean theorem or internal PA proof-code endpoint.
This packet claims neither unconditional nonhalting nor absolute consistency,
global priority, or minimality. Exploratory seeds are not promoted to certified
machines merely because their source or decoder checks pass.
