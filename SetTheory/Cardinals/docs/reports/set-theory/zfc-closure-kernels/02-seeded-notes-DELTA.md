# Delta against the supplied dossier

The three supplied reports were read from the mounted archive. Their hashes
and exact paths are recorded in `provenance/sources.json`. Prior work is
credited in the article, not rebranded as a new result.

## Additions

- A detailed analysis of the corrected MathOverflow set-seeded closure
  proposal, including its distinction from root seeding before bootstrapping.
- T_pt and T_sub: Extensionality plus one seeded bounded-image closure schema,
  deductively equivalent to the specified foundation-free ZF_0.
- Explicit noncircular bootstrap: exact constant relations, two-cycle Pairing,
  membership-closure Union, Russell-rooted Replacement, successor Infinity.
- Choice-free reverse construction using unique exact fibers and positive
  omega-stages, rather than selecting arbitrary local bounds.
- A generic reflexive-seed criterion that also permits kernel-sharing variants.
- Q_sub: direct equality-free calibration through member-profile congruence.
- Extensionality + Separation + subset-bounded root closure, including a
  singleton/subset switching relation that recovers transitivity before Union.
- Extensionality + NE + iterated subset images, replacing separate Infinity by
  a weaker nonempty-membership seed and iterative closure.
- New, same-basis formula measurements and executed finite regressions.

These are additions relative to this dossier. No historical priority or
absolute axiom/Turing-machine minimality claim is made.

## Retained controls and cautions

RCC (the supplied raw container/global-image-collection theory), the
Extensionality + Infinity + subset-image theory, and finite predicative class
theories remain controls. RCC's full nonextensional proof is inherited, not
independently reproved here. The designated-positive-target distinction and
diagonal-falsity/irreflexivity dependency are retained and applied to the new
candidates. The external 254-state benchmark is reported, not newly verified.

## Open, not claimed as calibrated

Deleting the membership arm of the mixed bound in the point-seeded theory
leaves a nontrivial Separation/Union bootstrap problem. Deleting EC from
Q_sub needs a different nonextensional interpretation or a strength analysis.
Neither deletion is asserted to preserve ZFC strength.

## Numerical scope

The 69, 72, 80, and 83 totals count formula-tree nodes, including matrix holes
but excluding variable payloads and proof-engine/backend costs. They are not
comparable without adjustment to a prior metric using another syntax basis;
in particular they are not Turing-machine state counts.
