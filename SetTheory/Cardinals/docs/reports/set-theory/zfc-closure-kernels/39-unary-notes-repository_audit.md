## New contribution identified during this session

The new direction is a direct permutation-structure obstruction for EPSC1.
The final version uses normalized two-cycles (or bi-infinite chains),
not ordinary urelements. It gives an extensional hereditary one-support
domain satisfying:

- full EPSC1, in fact one-parameter globally least exact closure;
- irreflexivity, and optionally every finite acyclicity axiom;
- arbitrary finite common containment and subset bounds;
- full arbitrary-parameter support-free closure.

It nevertheless fails exact bounded Pairing; incoming-profile equality is
literal equality, and the inherited hereditary-saturation domain equals the
whole structure. Thus the particular existing interpretation cannot simply
be extended from EPSC2 to EPSC1. This is a new obstruction relative to the
inspected project state, not a global historical priority claim and not a
consistency-strength separation.

The construction uses tagged nodes in ZFC and a set-sized permutation group,
so it needs neither a stronger model assumption nor a Jech--Sochor transfer.
It is not a standard Fraenkel--Mostowski model of ZFA: supports of size at
most one are not an ideal, and Pairing deliberately fails. The EPSC schemas
are proved directly by ambient unique-fiber Replacement and supported
omega closure.

The final main proof is included in sections/unary_theory.tex.
sections/support_deletion.tex proves the full support-free extension.
The earlier research_repo/epsc1_permutation_obstruction.md is a preliminary
ordinary-atom version, superseded by the stronger normalized-cycle theorem;
it should not be shipped as the main result.

## Primary literature check

Schindler and Schlicht, ZFC without parameters:
https://www.math.uni-bonn.de/people/schlicht/ZFC_without_parameters.pdf

The four-page primary note explicitly retains Pairing in its parameter-free
ZFC and ZC theories. Printed p2 uses tagged pairs to pack parameters and
explicitly cites Pairing for amalgamation. Their proofs do not establish
the same result in the absence of Pairing. They credit results known since
the1970s, specifically Azriel Levy, Parameters in comprehension axiom
schemas of set theory, Proceedings of the Tarski Symposium (1974),
pp309--324.

The direct proofs can be inspected for the weaker axiom assumptions they
actually use; omission of Foundation/Choice from a proposed application
must be justified by that inspection, not inferred from the paper's ZFC
notation alone.
