## Relation to the maintained goal

These reports keep the older target of equivalence provable in ZFC with Con(ZFC); they do not supply a new stronger theory or a concise replacement axiomatization. Their methods may help the newly broadened stronger-theory search, but no solved instance follows from that change of criterion. The current project criterion (`32414c3fc0`) keeps stronger assumptions within well-understood benchmarks widely believed consistent.
