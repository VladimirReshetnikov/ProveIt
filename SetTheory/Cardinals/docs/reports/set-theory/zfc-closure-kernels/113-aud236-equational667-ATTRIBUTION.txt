The contribution here is the direct compatibility proof for E692's changed
closure polynomial before d=top is known, followed by the exact E667
construction, codec replay, Boolean cofactor certificate, ground-instance
recipe and ordinary same-model equivalence theorem. It does not claim the
truth-removal idea, witness sharing, selector sharing, Boolean single axiom
or codec as new, and makes no priority or minimality claim.

The Sh1 Boolean single axiom is due to William McCune, Robert Veroff,
Branden Fitelson, Kenneth Harris, Andrew Feist and Larry Wos, Short Single
Axioms for Boolean Algebra, Journal of Automated Reasoning 29 (2002), 1-16.
Primary proof artifacts: https://www.cs.unm.edu/~veroff/BA/

The native theory/model calibration, shared constructors and scalar
predicate presentation are inherited through the original EPSC and ProveIt
equational packets, including Sources 79, 40, 14 and 08. The original small
binary consistency-machine source is attributed to Mira-Cult, its upstream
Rocq verification to Jeff Ketchersid and contributors, and its compiler
framework to Andrew James Wade. E667 supplies no binary-state count.
