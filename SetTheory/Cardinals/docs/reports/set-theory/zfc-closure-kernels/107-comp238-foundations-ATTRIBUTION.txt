This contribution builds on ProveIt's native-stack EPSC presentation
(Source79), reviewed at commit ce192939372ac7944fe2a0208c68a96d49fb5e3b,
and the complete946-bit packet in BusyBeaver239_Research_2026-10-19.
The raw EPSC schema and ordinary ZFC comparison are inherited from
Sources14/08, with Source40's finite-kernel and free-witness-slice methods.
The shared P/J constructor, truth fusion and Pick=W(x,d) specialization
are inherited from the946-bit packet and its cited predecessors.

The Boolean single axiom Sh1 is due to McCune, Veroff, Fitelson, Harris,
Feist and Wos, Short Single Axioms for Boolean Algebra (2002), Theorem2.
The exact original946 packet is regenerated here with the same equations,
symbol order, endpoints and SHA256, and is preserved as a comparison.

This contribution gives a complemented-witness binder and its exact
Source79 translation, combined with fully charged Boolean factoring and
auxiliary-flag elimination. The latter are standard logical operations;
no global historical priority or global shortest-packet claim is made.
The earlier conversation mention of E766 was unavailable for byte comparison.

The original small-machine proof calculus, set theory, source and254-state
machine remain credited to Mira-Cult; the upstream Rocq verification to
Jeff Ketchersid and contributors; the compiler framework to Andrew James Wade;
and the existing ProveIt reductions/formalizations to their original authors.
No theorem or state-count credit is transferred by inclusion here.
