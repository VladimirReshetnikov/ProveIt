# Attribution and proof scope

This packet accompanies the new 222-state construction for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository. New scripts, source adaptations, certificates and prose use the included MIT No Attribution license. Inherited components retain their original terms.

The immediate inputs are ProveIt commit `6ab3e41e4dcddfbd7f843bdf4be48ae5c7ba2430`, including the incoming seed-K and merged-G 223-state packets, and the unchanged `K224_Construction_2026-10-20.zip` at commit `49ee7cfdc8e0364c6db3614675d141d66f9350ed`. The K theory, seed reformulation, K0 quotient interpretation, and K/ZFC consistency calibration are due to Elliot Glazer and the attributed predecessor developments. The retained K230 source is pinned to `3142e4dd980f0be12f4abb636c4ae2528afc17eb`. The proof-search/compiler architecture builds on the work credited in the predecessor packets, including Mira, ProveIt, Jeff's busy-beaver development, Andrew James Wade, BusyCoq, and the Coq Library of Undecidability.

The new contribution combines the already calibrated seed-request K presentation with the already calibrated merged equality row G, binds that combination to an exact 208-subtree source, preserves the inherited decoder helpers while recompiling, and assigns two new source roles to formerly separate helpers through checked folds. The resulting 222-state bytes receive fresh direct runtime and startup checks. The conditional-K alternative composes a different inherited K presentation with the same equality frontend. No global priority or minimality claim is made.

`portable/runtime/symbolic_runtime.py`, `portable/runtime/independent_runtime.py`, the legacy label map, the pinned formal sources, and the original predecessor notices are retained unchanged. `portable/runtime/check_literal_runtime.py` is the repository's existing parameterized runtime adapter. `portable/verification/check_seed_folds.py` is a count/filename adaptation of the incoming G223 checker. `portable/verification/independent_seed_logic.py` extends the incoming independent G proof-DAG parser to natural-valued schematic label terms. `portable/source/` records the exact source/proof adaptations and pins its unchanged inherited utilities. The original seed and G notices are in `portable/vendor/NOTICE.seed223.md` and `portable/vendor/NOTICE.G223.md`; further notices and licenses remain inside the unchanged K224 archive.

The new machine has an ordinary mathematical correctness argument with executable finite and inductive certificates. This packet does not claim a newly compiled end-to-end Rocq or Lean theorem for the 222-state table. A successful replay does not prove Con(ZFC) or unconditional non-halting. The main theorem is the formalizable equivalence with Con(ZFC), using the inherited logical calibration explicitly documented in the report and `portable/source/seed_g_theory.md`.

The root report, documentation, exact provenance, exploratory measurement script and packaging records are new contributions under the same MIT-0 terms. Exploratory search receipts identify their exact input bytes and do not establish any lower bound.
