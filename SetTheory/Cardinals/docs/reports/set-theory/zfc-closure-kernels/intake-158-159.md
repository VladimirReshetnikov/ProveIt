## Source52: semantic seeds and exact role-allocation invariants

Finite acyclic membership patterns, after equal-value identification, are
exactly the quantifier-free patterns realized by hereditarily finite sets.
Distinct tags prevent accidental vertex equality or membership; the shared
construction needs at most `3*m+1` nodes. This gives an exact finite-fragment
criterion and a stronger startup screening method. Its executable recognizer
still returns unknown outside its supported/capped fragment. It is an extension
of the old incomplete recognizer, not a proof that it exceeds C254 theoremhood.

For semantic startup soundness, every formula must hold under every assignment
in one common set-sized model. Separate satisfiability is insufficient, and
removing initial universal quantifiers requires universally closing residual
free variables. Completeness can use the unchanged all-state theorem. A
semantic positive needs model-relative soundness and a PA-compatible bridge
before it can meet our target.

A transfer to a zero destination can be erased by exchanging physical roles,
provided the full physical vector, all macro boundaries, external entry,
unused zero slots and halt sites are respected. Exact singleton masks can
justify equal-value identifications; an unbounded value bucket cannot. A
strict epsilon rank proves progress after control/transfer erasure. These
hypotheses are essential, especially when mixing an all-zero-root allocation
with the broader semantic/dirty startup domain.
