Research experiment prepared on 18 October 2026 for the ProveIt investigation.

The exact ZFC source program, its native calculus, and the original machine
are by Mira-Cult. Jeff Ketchersid supplied the upstream verification,
including Prog254.v and Dag254.v. Andrew James Wade supplied the compiler
framework. The table used here is the already submitted ProveIt 246-state
contextual-fold candidate, whose attribution is unchanged.

safe_lifetime_witness.py and make_resource_merge_cases.py are new experiment
drivers. The literal source programs retain their existing upstream license
and attribution. resource_merge_search.cpp is adapted from context/merge_search.cpp
in the prior zfc-beyond246 incoming archive. The new screen changes the finite
fixture resources to 167 value-one spare blocks and header distance 181, uses
the retained 8,385 interval fixtures, and increases the represented tape window.
It does not claim a new mutation family, a new candidate machine, a proof of
minimality, or a new consistency equivalence.

python3 -B safe_lifetime_witness.py --dag PATH/TO/Dag254.v

Reproduction of the new finite merge screen from its included fixture file:
g++ -O3 -std=c++17 resource_merge_search.cpp -o resource_merge_search
./resource_merge_search resource-merge-cases.txt

The executable writes merge-search-summary.json and merge-survivors.txt in
the current directory. The expected full survivor count is zero. The fixture
generator and source hashes are retained in resource-merge-cases.json; it uses
instruction-metadata.json from the prior below246_v2 interval experiment and
check_literal_instructions.py from the inherited 246-state reference. No new
unbounded or proof-assistant check of modified machines is asserted.

Routing search additions:
anneal_routes_v1.cpp and its v2, scan, scan_gradient and zero6 variants adapt
search/grammar/code/anneal5.cpp from the prior below246_v2 incoming archive.
They add independently movable folded-entry routes, synthesized router-only
words, bootstrap locks, direct existing-child cuts, and zero-run objectives.
A local input adds the original state177 lookup word as an initial pure
router. The goal remains heuristic search; unsuccessful runs do not prove a
lower bound. check_route_grammar.py is a separate Python grammar audit.

check_short_scan_boot.py, short_scan_boot_lemma.tex,
map_reassociated_leaf.py, prepare_route_inputs.py, and
repair_scanner_grammar.py are new experiment artifacts. The successful
407915-move bootstrap of the shortened scanner is not claimed to validate
its ordinary instruction behavior. STATE_REDUCTION_REPRODUCE.md gives the
curated archive-relative reproduction commands.
