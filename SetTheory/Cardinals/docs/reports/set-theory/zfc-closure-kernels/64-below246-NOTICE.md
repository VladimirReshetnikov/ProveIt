# Attribution and status

The original 254-state construction is attributed upstream to Mira-Cult.
Jeff Ketchersid supplies the upstream formalization; the compilation lineage
includes Wade. ProveIt's permutation252 work, the attribution for the public
253-state reduction, and subsequent prefix/context research retain their
respective notices in the included baseline package.

`baseline/context246/` is an unchanged extraction of the incoming archive
at the ProveIt commit pinned in `audit/provenance.json`. Its original
`NOTICE.txt` file, provenance, and all source license files remain
authoritative for that material. Additional original DAG source is included
only with its exact upstream identity; no blanket license is asserted over
third-party files. The optional Z3 solver is not bundled.

The new contribution is the research report, new local proof instantiations,
counterexamples, alternate detector, scoped searches, and delivery replay.
The incomplete 244- and 245-state tables are diagnostic artifacts, not claimed
solutions of the user's state-count target. There is no fresh proof-assistant
kernel endpoint in this package.
