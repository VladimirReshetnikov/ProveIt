New here: applying all three identifications to E713; an explicit
combined upper-model proof with Q interpreted as existential negation;
the relative E704/E713 nonderivability theorem; the exact 704-bit
checkpoint; and a Boolean multiplexer identity saving two further
term nodes to give the complete E696 checkpoint; and sharing the unary Q
with selector successor to give E692, with a separate model proof and
exact builder/replay. Logical 692-bit length is distinguished from its

application to this kernel. No global priority or minimality claim.
