\section{Engine and encoding experiments: scope and rejected shortcuts}
\label{sec:engine-encoding-audit}

The successful construction changes the source encoding before sharing an
additional decoder state.  Several complementary experiments explain why a
direct deletion from the original K221 table was less effective.  This section
records reproducible finite results, not lower bounds for arbitrary Turing
machines.  All state numbers below are K221's native labels.  The baseline has
221 working states and 442 transition rows: 172 states implement source
decoding, while 49 are engine or auxiliary states.  Its 4,744-leaf source tree
has 208 distinct subtrees when identical subtrees are shared.  The supplement
pins the baseline table and source by SHA-256.

\subsection{Bounded local state-sharing searches}

For each radius $r$, a finite abstract interpreter computes admitted
$(2r+1)$-cell windows around the head, starting with the all-zero window in
state 0.  When the head moves, the newly exposed boundary bit may independently
be either 0 or 1.  Closure under this transition relation therefore
overapproximates every actual blank-tape execution.  A proposed replacement
block must reproduce the old transition's state, head position, and tape
contents for every admitted window, without reading or writing outside that
window.  Positive block lengths prevent a zero-step simulation argument.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
Search family on the unchanged K221 table & Radius & Row choices tested\\
\hline
One altered row, pair involving an engine state & 3, 5, 7 & 110,500 each\\
One altered row, any state pair & 5 & 236,028\\
Two altered rows, pair involving an engine state & 5 & 1,627,444\\
Two altered rows, pair involving a nonpure state & 5 & 3,707,496\\
Engine pair with head displacement $\pm2$ & 5 & 8,946,080
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

No fold passed these checks.  The one-row family retains a common direct
branch after identifying the two states.  The two-row families instead require
disjoint admitted windows and exclude such common branches; their replacement
blocks cannot revisit the merged state except at the required endpoint.  A
nonpure state has a row that changes its scanned bit or moves left.  The
head-displacement family deletes a state whose incoming moves all agree,
reverses those incoming moves, and compares configurations with the resulting
two-cell displacement.  Ordinary blocks have at most 21 steps; displaced
blocks have at most 23.  Counts are individual row-action choices considered
by these implementations, not counts of distinct complete machines.
They do not exhaust coupled rewrites, larger windows, longer blocks, or
different source layouts.  In particular, they impose no obstruction on the
successful re-encoding followed by a context-sensitive fold.

\subsection{Two tempting 220-state tables that fail}

\paragraph{Deleting state 50 from the reset scanner.}
The zero branch of the reset chain passes through
$2,3,5,9,15,23,34,50,75$.  Deleting state 50 and redirecting its incoming edges
to 75 shortens the recognized zero run from eight to seven.  A scan of every
source address finds that its longest zero run is six, and every six-zero run
is terminal.  This fact initially suggests that seven zeros might suffice.
It does not: the runtime introduces another zero immediately after the
program-counter word before filling the remaining gap with ones.  A terminal
six-zero source suffix can consequently become an internal transient run of
seven zeros.

The executable counterexample uses zero-based source ordinal 40, with address
\texttt{11101001001000000} and instruction $\mathrm{CD}_1$ on its zero branch.
All nine logical registers are zero, the spare list is $[1]$, and the extra
gap is zero.  Starting at dispatcher 84 with head position 0, K221 returns the
correct frame in 586 transitions.  The shortened scanner returns to the same
dispatcher and head position after 662 transitions, but its program counter
is wrong: the expected one at cell 15 has been replaced by a one at cell 13.
Every other one-cell agrees.  This is an exact valid-interface counterexample;
it does not assert that this particular interface frame is reached from the
blank initial tape.

\paragraph{Deleting state 198 by a spare-list shortcut.}
The exact initialized frame contains 167 spares, all equal to 1.  Every source
increment appends one more spare with value 1, and every other nonhalting
primitive preserves the spare list.  Thus the all-one spare invariant is
closed, and its length is always at least 167 at source boundaries.  However,
state 198 also participates in decrement lookahead and restoration; its role
cannot be inferred solely from its use while appending a spare.

The rejected patch deletes state 198 and changes the zero row of state 66 to
$(1,L,113)$.  A $\mathrm{CD}_8$ zero-branch frame at address \texttt{000},
with zero registers, spare list $[1]$, and zero extra gap, gives an exact
primitive-contract failure: the baseline completes in 602 transitions, while
the patch returns a different tape after 2,010 transitions.  More decisively,
the patch fails directly from the required blank initialization.  After
exactly 76 transitions it is in state 35, with head at cell 4 and rightmost
one at cell 2.  Its literal row is
\[
  \delta(35,0)=(1,R,35).
\]
Inductively, the head thereafter encounters a fresh zero at every step,
writes a one behind itself, and moves right in state 35 forever.  The
76-transition trace and this one row form a finite certificate of that
divergence; no long simulation or conjecture about eventual behavior is used.
Both rejected patches and their baseline comparisons are reconstructed by
\texttt{scripts/counterexamples.py}, with exact tape sets and trace hashes in
\texttt{receipts/counterexamples.json}.

The specialized all-one-spare symbolic interface audit still uses every one
of K221's 442 rows.  The same is true after omitting source branches ruled out
by the packaged zero facts.  These are usage results for overapproximated
interface families, not claims that every row occurs on the unique initialized
execution.

\subsection{Exact finite searches over axiom-emitter encodings}

The eight universally enumerated logical rows can each be reparameterized
bijectively without changing their axiom images.  Macro matching identifies
the complete emitter intervals, including the fourth-argument reader attached
to $\rho_8$.  Permuting the register slots for $a,b,c$ within an emitter leaves
its binary syntax-tree shape and guarded control-flow structure intact.
The fixed-shape optimization objective here is the number of distinct source
subtrees, not a claim about the optimum achievable compiled state count.

All $8\cdot6=48$ individual permutations were tested.  Exhausting their
$6^8=1,679,616$ joint combinations gives minimum 208, attained exactly twice:
the original source and the source that exchanges $b,c$ only in $\rho_6$.
The second minimizer also compiles to 221 states.  The exhaustive counter
precomputes unions of row-fragment subtrees and explicitly accounts for
cross-boundary subtrees; its reduction is compared with ordinary exact
interning on all 48 individual changes and 1,000 seeded joint changes.
Swapping the equal-length $\rho_3,\rho_5$ emitters and repeating all
1,679,616 parameter combinations gives minimum 217.  Twelve additional
fourth-argument splice variants for $\rho_8$ give minimum 209 and do not
produce a validated reduction.

A separate exhaustive search tries all $8!=40,320$ row orders.  It factors
the original program into irreducible closed atoms, moves each row's complete
contiguous atom list, and retains the original binary concatenation shape
over those atoms.  The original order is the unique 208-node minimum; the
next smallest count is 225.  This exhausts that specified shape family,
not all parenthesizations for every order.

These row changes preserve the semantic enumeration for a concrete reason.
Each moved emitter is closed; the fourth-argument reader moves with
$\rho_8$ and consumes the same input field once.  The other row emitters leave
that proof-list input unchanged.  Reordering the eight formulas changes only
their stack depths, so the leaf compiler chooses the corresponding depth in
its sequence of stack removals.  The generalization entry remains at depth
9, and the discard count remains 10.  Every logical-row code differs from
the halting sentinel 1.  Bijective parameter substitutions similarly
reparameterize the universally supplied axiom arguments.  The binding
receipts make these source intervals and macro matches explicit.

The portable supplement contains these scripts, their finite receipts, and
the small counterexample traces.  Register coalescing, unrestricted joint
row reordering and reparenthesization, and global engine minimality were
not established by this investigation.  These negative results guide
subsequent work without strengthening the separate correctness claim for
the accepted 220-state construction.
