\paragraph{Exact object and proof status.}
Let $M_{245}$ be the binary, one-tape transition table in
\texttt{gradient245-zero6/machine.tm}. Its 245 working states are numbered
$0,\ldots,244$, its initial state is~0, and its sole target-only halt state
is~245. The tape is two-way infinite and initially zero everywhere.
Its SHA-256 is
\begin{center}\small
\texttt{17f4d430747c4a0cd580dc2cc13ea06a74ce7f51f12be4c2d12f6a646691dd13}.
\end{center}
For readability, the proof uses the bijectively equivalent old-label table,
with initial state~95, dispatcher~96, and halt~254; its SHA-256 is
\begin{center}\small
\texttt{f8dbeacdad5fd89c57e3f240781324b09110be8e0ccdb838a9c710fb28d2a267}.
\end{center}
The result below is an ordinary mathematical proof using explicit finite
certificates and induction rules. No new Lean or Rocq kernel endpoint for
this exact table is asserted. In particular, the search heuristic, a small
zero-run statistic, and a successful blank prefix are not used as substitutes
for the full runtime obligations.

\section{A parameterized complete tape frame}

For $v\in\mathbb N$, write $B(v)=0\,1^{v+1}$, and let $B$ act on finite
lists by concatenation. Let $R=(r_0,\ldots,r_8)\in\mathbb N^9$ be the
physical registers and let $S\in\mathbb N^{<\omega}$ be a nonempty list of
spare values. The frame $F(h,p,D,R,S)$ has head~$h$, dispatcher state~96,
and complete tape
\[
 {}^\omega0\;1\;p\;0^{D-|p|}\;1\;B(R)\;B(S)\;0^\omega.
\]
The first displayed marker~1 is at $h-1$; the second is at $h+D$. Thus
$D$ denotes the header distance, not merely the zero-run length after the
written pointer. This definition includes every nonzero tape cell.

Every terminal source path $p$ in the reconstructed program has
$|p|\leq51$. The certificate uniformly uses $D=64+n$ with a universally
quantified $n\in\mathbb N$. It therefore proves a statement about all
header distances $D\geq64$, independently of all nine register values and
all nonempty spare lists.

Let $\iota(p)=(q,o)$ be binary successor at the full input width: $q$ keeps
all trailing zero cells, and $o\in\{0,1\}$ records overflow, including
successor of the empty word. Let $\sigma_{b,k}(p)$ remove the shortest suffix
containing $k$ occurrences of bit~$b$, removing the whole word if necessary.
The physical index of logical register $i$ is given by
\[
 \pi=(6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Complete-frame instruction contracts]
For each of the 6460 terminal source paths, the old-label $M_{245}$ table
simulates the corresponding instruction from every frame
$F(h,p,64+n,R,S)$ satisfying its named decrement guard. Every simulated
instruction has a positive finite number of literal Turing-machine steps.
For a nonhalting instruction its endpoint is
\[
 F(h-o,q,64+n+\delta+o,R',S'),
\]
with the following parameters:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lllll@{}}
\toprule
Instruction & $(q,o)$ or $q$ & $\delta$ & $R'$ & $S'$\\
\midrule
$\operatorname{inc}(i)$ & $\iota(p)$ & 0 & $R+e_{\pi(i)}$ & $S\mathbin{+\!+}[1]$\\
$\operatorname{dec}(i)$, positive & $\iota(p)$ & 1 & $R-e_{\pi(i)}$ & $S$\\
$\operatorname{dec}(i)$, zero & $\iota(\sigma_{0,1}(p))$ & 0 & $R$ & $S$\\
$\operatorname{break}(k)$ & $\iota(\sigma_{0,k}(p))$ & 0 & $R$ & $S$\\
$\operatorname{continue}(k)$ & $q=\sigma_{1,k}(p),\ o=0$ & 0 & $R$ & $S$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The unique halt instruction reaches state~254.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Certificate and justification of its rules]
The actual table is replayed symbolically for all 8391 named obligations:
1931 zero decrements, 1931 positive decrements, 2601 increments,
128 breaks, 1799 continues, and one halt. The checker preserves the complete
successor word and tests the complete canonical tape frame, including the
single register effect and the spare suffix. It accepts a completed case only
after positive literal progress. For this table the minimum recorded literal
progress is~13, and the largest case uses 628 symbolic rules.

Literal rules read the actual transition table. A self-loop scan over
$a n+c$ cells is justified by induction on that nonnegative length; it
preserves exactly the displayed untouched context. The bounded prefix part
can be exposed only when $c>0$, so an unknown zero-versus-positive branch is
never assumed. A decrement oracle is permitted only for the register named
by its guard and for the appropriate marked representation. The checker
also rejects a second effect applied to an already modified register box.

Before admitting these register oracles, an independent induction checker
replays their proofs on this exact 245-state table. Four one-block traversal
lemmas are verified with arbitrary natural block value and arbitrary left
and right contexts. Structural induction lifts them to arbitrary finite
lists. The free-word identity
$C(A)1=1B(A)$, where $C(v)=1\,0\,1^v$, and its reversal justify the carry
normalization. The three complete increment/decrement contracts and all nine
register selectors are then proved by composing those derived lemmas with
literal transitions. No bounded sample of register values is a premise.
All 342 named-guard and single-effect oracle checks pass.

The parameterization from the inherited width~40 to width~64 replaces the
same constant in both the initial run $n+64-|p|$ and the target run
$n+64+\delta+o-|q|$. The driver verifies nonnegativity explicitly. It does
not alter a transition, symbolic inference rule, named guard, or operation
effect. These facts prove exactly the displayed universal contract.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The header bound and spare condition are invariant]
If $D\geq64$ and $S$ is nonempty at a source instruction, the same facts
hold at its next nonhalting instruction.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The table above gives $D'=D+\delta+o$ with $\delta,o\geq0$. Equivalently,
the new affine parameter is $n'=n+\delta+o\in\mathbb N$. The only change
to $S$ appends a value~1, so no operation removes the final spare block.
A residual pointer is identified with the next source leaf by adding zero
bits or by dropping an ignored all-zero suffix. In either case the following
zero gap changes by the opposite number of cells, so the complete tape is
unchanged. The separately checked residual-pointer normalization verifies
this word identity for all 8390 nonhalting successors.
\end{proof}

\section{Source correspondence and the exact initial frame}

The original 226-node shared syntax DAG is independently expanded and
compared, leaf for leaf, with a fresh parse of the pinned
\texttt{Prog254.v}. The grammar transformation retains the same closed
atomic programs and changes their association and sharing. The resulting
6460-leaf program has a finite register-program bisimulation with the
original. Its certificate has 3013 related control pairs, 6024 checked
decrement-successor edges, and one halt pair. At each related decrement the
register and both guards agree; the intervening finite sequences have equal
increment counts for each register. Consequently the relation preserves
halting from any common initial register vector, not just a selected test.

Two exact blank-tape interpreters, one sparse and one dense, agree on all
one-cells after precisely 407915 steps. In old labels the endpoint is
\[
 F\bigl(-5,\epsilon,181,
 (0,0,0,3,0,0,3,0,0),[1]^{167}\bigr).
\]
There are 351 one-cells and all other cells are zero. The logical register
vector is
\[
 (N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)=(3,0,0,0,3,0,0,0,0).
\]
Thus the invariant begins at $n=181-64=117$, and the formula-bearing
registers $T,W,H$ are all zero. The 167 spare blocks are runtime resources;
they are not additional logical registers.

\begin{theorem}[Exact-table reduction to the original enumerator]
The blank computation of $M_{245}$ halts if and only if the original
register program $p_{254}$ halts from the displayed logical seed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the exact finite initialization, the complete-frame contracts and the
frame invariant to inductively simulate the reconstructed source program.
Every source instruction has a finite, positive clock. Determinism and the
complete covered frames therefore give both directions of halting
equivalence; an infinite source run consumes unbounded literal time, while
a finite source halt reaches the sole halt state. Compose with the finite
register-program bisimulation.
\end{proof}

\section{Composition inside ZFC}

The ProveIt dirty-root completeness argument applies to every natural-valued
register root: a refutation yields arbitrarily large padded encodings of a
contradiction program, so a counter that initially skipped finitely many
candidates still finds one. A dirty proof-list pointer is drained before
enumeration resumes. Soundness requires safe formula data; the displayed
seed satisfies that requirement because $T=W=H=0$. The pinned
\texttt{LogicalSeed246} source proof consequently identifies halting from
this seed with a numerical refutation of the finite-machine theory SIR.

The upstream theory comparison is with \emph{syntactic consistency}.
SIR has the transitive power-container axiom, the parameter-closed global
image-collection schema, and membership irreflexivity. Its set-coded
bisimulation quotient interprets ZF without Regularity. The source theorem
\texttt{Z\_main.ZFCReg\_equicons} supplies Regularity and Choice, giving
equiconsistency with conventional ZFC. Conversely, the corresponding
axiom and schema translations are derivable in ZFC.

These interpretation and relative-consistency arguments, the proof search,
the finite syntax computations, and the natural-number/list inductions above
can all be carried out in ZFC. A semantic presentation may use first-order
completeness for set-sized structures inside ZFC; neither external
$\omega$-standardness nor external well-foundedness of those structures is
required. No consistency premise is used to prove the equivalence.
Writing $\operatorname{Prf}_{\mathrm{ZFC}}(u,\bot)$ for the ordinary
arithmetized proof predicate, the composition proves
\[
 \mathrm{ZFC}\ \vdash\
 \halt(M_{245},0^\mathbb Z)
 \ \longleftrightarrow\
 \exists u\,\operatorname{Prf}_{\mathrm{ZFC}}(u,\bot).
\]
Classical negation gives
\[
 \boxed{\mathrm{ZFC}\ \vdash\
 \neg\halt(M_{245},0^\mathbb Z)
 \ \longleftrightarrow\ \Con(\mathrm{ZFC}).}
\]
This is not an assertion that ZFC proves its own consistency or the actual
nonhalting of the machine. An internally coded PA proof or a new
proof-assistant kernel endpoint is a further formalization task; it is not
silently imported from the proof of a different transition table.
