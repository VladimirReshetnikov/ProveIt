their combinations. The inherited engine itself traces to the ProveIt

and resume-file support from anneal_routes_zero6.cpp. Its new experiment uses
the five-zero gradient, weight 0.25, no bootstrap locks, and a verified
128-rule starting point. All inherited source notices remain applicable.

current contribution. They import the unchanged attributed symbolic rules
from the reference package rather than replacing their proof semantics.
