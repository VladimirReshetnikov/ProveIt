\section{Logical compression and alternative presentations}
\label{sec:logical-compression}

\noindent\textbf{Status.} This section establishes a mathematically
supported changed axiom source and finite proof-certificate evidence.
These results alone do not construct or verify a machine with fewer than
224 states.  A successful decoder and transition-table construction must
be supplied separately.

\subsection{The exact logical change supported here}

The most direct supported change to the pinned K224 source replaces its
Euclidean equality row
\[
 \rho_7(a,b,c):\quad E(c,a)\to\bigl(E(c,b)\to E(a,b)\bigr)
\]
by the symmetry row
\[
 \operatorname{Sym}(a,b):\quad E(a,b)\to E(b,a).
\]
The original Lukasiewicz propositional row, $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_6$, MP,
theorem generalization, membership vacuity, the fused K bundle, and both
additional AX parameter reads are retained.  In particular, this change
retains K224's restricted equality helper
\[
 H_{\kappa}(a,b):\quad E(a,b)\to\forall_{\kappa(a,b)} E(a,b),
 \qquad \kappa(a,b)=4P(a,b)+1,
\]
where $P(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b$.

This reduction is already permitted by Source 116's
\path{portable/theory/source116/proofs/general_kappa_equality_helper.md}.
That theorem explicitly includes primitive symmetry as an alternative to
$\rho_7$.  Its original set-theoretic application used CP/GIC.  The
application to the present K source additionally uses K224's
\path{portable/theory/k_equality_helper_extension.md}.  The contribution
here is the explicit composition of those interfaces, its independent
proof-certificate check, and its use as a current source-layout candidate.
The symmetry theorem itself is prior work.

The original postfix arithmetic word has eleven macro calls:
\[
 c\ a\ {\tt eq}\ c\ b\ {\tt eq}\ a\ b\ {\tt eq}\ {\tt im}\ {\tt im}.
\]
Symmetry needs seven:
\[
 a\ b\ {\tt eq}\ b\ a\ {\tt eq}\ {\tt im}.
\]
Both have the same stack effect and occupy one logical-row position.
Saving four calls is a concrete source reduction; it is not a four-state
saving.  Decoder layout and shared subexpressions determine the resulting
machine size.

\subsection{Why the restricted helper still suffices}

Here $E$ is the primitive equality predicate.  Define a different formula,
membership indiscernibility,
\[
 D(a,b)=
 \bigl[\forall z\,(R(z,a)\leftrightarrow R(z,b))\bigr]
 \mathbin{\wedge}
 \bigl[\forall z\,(R(a,z)\leftrightarrow R(b,z))\bigr],
 \qquad z=4P(a,b)+3.
\]
Conjunction, biconditional, negation and existence are expanded using the
retained implication and relative-falsity syntax.  The explicit choice of
$z$ matters: its numerical value changes with the endpoints.

For distinct endpoints, $\kappa(a,b)$ is different from both.  The helper,
Sym, and the two membership congruence rows yield all four membership
implications under universal quantification at that safe auxiliary.
The Source 116 membership-only transport proof then moves the auxiliary
to $4P(a,b)+3$, giving $E(a,b)\to D(a,b)$.  For coincident endpoints,
$D(a,a)$ is built from generalized propositional identities, so it needs
neither primitive reflexivity nor an equality helper.  Thus the bridge
$E(a,b)\to D(a,b)$ holds in every case.

Closed existential monotonicity and the retained row
$\rho_1(a,b)=\exists_a E(a,b)$ give $\exists_a D(a,b)$.  The remaining
translated equality laws---including reflexivity, symmetry, Euclidean
equality, both membership congruences, and full equality vacuity---are
laws of $D$ proved in the membership fragment.  This is an interpretation
of the full logical calculus; it does not assert that primitive $E$
itself regains reflexivity or full equality vacuity.

The dependencies are the original propositional basis, normal quantifier
distribution $\rho_2$, quantifier commutation $\rho_4$, $\rho_6$, the
witness row $\rho_1$, both membership congruences $\rho_3,\rho_5$,
membership vacuity, the actual $H_\kappa$ family, Sym, MP and theorem GEN.
The original $\rho_7$ is absent.  The retained $\rho_6$ is used both in
quantifier metatheory and to derive explosion for the relative falsity
$F=R(0,0)$.  Generalization under open assumptions and an unproved general
substitution rule are not used as primitive rules.

\subsection{The K matrix must be encoded after interpretation}

Let $\theta$ replace every primitive equality atom by $D$, preserve
membership, implication and each named universal binder, and send genuine
bottom to $F$.  For an ordinary K matrix $f$, set
\[
 \psi=\theta(f),\qquad c=\operatorname{enc}(\neg\neg\psi).
\]
The native code-label bound makes $c$ larger than every label occurring
in its matrix.  Double-negation padding gives $c>3$.  Choose the ordinary
comparison axiom with input label $1$, output label $3$, private label
$c$, and matrix $\neg\neg f$.  Ordinary K permits this fresh private
label; it need not be the original matrix's old code.

The outer K template contains no equality.  Consequently
\[
 \theta\bigl(K\operatorname{Core}(1,3,c,\neg\neg f)\bigr)
 =K\operatorname{Core}(1,3,c,\neg\neg\psi),
\]
whose numerical encoding is exactly the emitted
$\operatorname{kcore}(1,3,c,c)$.  Choosing $c$ after translating the matrix
is the step that prevents an incorrect matrix-code/private-binder
identification.  The propositional fusion already used in K224 does not
depend on $\rho_7$, so it remains applicable.

The full logical interpretation translates finite K+IR refutations to
numerical derivations of $F$.  The interpreted IR is the generalized
identity $\forall_0(F\to F)$.  Combining this effective translation with
the retained K+IR/ZFC comparison proves the lower consistency direction.
For the reverse direction, interpret primitive $E$ as genuine equality.
Sym is valid, and $H_\kappa$ is ordinary atomic vacuity off the diagonal;
on the diagonal, universal equality reflexivity validates it regardless
of the chosen binder.  The original K and relative-falsity soundness
arguments then apply.

Let $T_{\rm Sym,REL}$ denote this exact numerical calculus.  These
effective finite-proof comparisons establish, by an ordinary argument
formalizable in ZFC,
\[
 \mathrm{ZFC}\vdash
 \bigl(\neg\operatorname{Pr}_{T_{\rm Sym,REL}}(F)
       \leftrightarrow \operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})\bigr).
\]
The proof-search enumerator still needs its own source and machine
simulation theorems.  Keeping the row count and AX reads unchanged permits
the K224 block compiler to select the changed row in the same position;
Sym's implication-root code cannot equal the target code $1$.  The
reset-prefix and cofinal-cutoff arguments in
\path{portable/theory/enumerator_adaptation.md} then have the same form.
A measured transition table and the source-to-table verification remain
separate obligations.

\subsection{What the independent certificate establishes}

The new generator and independent checker are
\path{generate_symmetry_rel_helper.py} and
\path{check_symmetry_rel_helper.py}.  The generator reuses the unchanged
Source 116 proof constructors.  The checker accepts the exact new Sym
leaf and the exact numerical REL helper, and rejects primitive $\rho_7$
and full primitive equality-vacuity leaves.  The retained propositional
interfaces K, S and truth-table tautologies are justified by the original

upstream propositional compilation.

The delivered receipt checks 64 closed roots, 55,327 proof nodes and

with zero $\rho_7$ or full primitive equality-vacuity uses.  All four
negative controls---wrong helper binder, old helper, injected $\rho_7$,

\subsection{A tempting quantifier reduction fails}

Replacing
\[
 \rho_6(a,A):\quad \exists_a\forall_a A\to A
\]
by the shorter $\forall_a A\to A$ does not preserve the pure logical
interface.  A two-world counterinterpretation proves this even if full
primitive equality vacuity is granted, which is stronger than K224's
actual REL helper.

Take worlds $0,1$ with accessibility
$\{(0,0),(0,1),(1,1)\}$.  Interpret every named universal quantifier as
the same forward modal box, material implication classically, every
membership atom as false at both worlds, and every primitive equality
atom as true exactly at world $1$.  Relative falsity is genuinely false,
so the defined existential is the usual diamond.  The frame validates
normal distribution and $\Box A\to A$; all named boxes commute.
Every equality witness exists through world $1$, including coincident
labels.  Euclidean equality and membership congruences hold pointwise.
All membership and equality vacuity instances hold because their truth
sets are upward persistent.  The Lukasiewicz row is a classical tautology,
and MP and theorem generalization preserve validity at both worlds.

For the actual formula $A=E(0,0)$, however,
\[
 \llbracket A\rrbracket=\{1\},\qquad
 \llbracket\Diamond\Box A\rrbracket=\{0,1\}.
\]
Thus the original $\rho_6$ fails at world $0$ and cannot be derived from
the proposed retained logical rows.  The exhaustive finite check is
\path{check_t_replacement_obstruction.py}, with receipt
\path{evidence/t_replacement_obstruction.json}.  This is a countermodel
for the pure logical calculus, not for K's set-theoretic axioms.  It rules
out automatic reuse of the completeness proof; it does not by itself
settle the consistency strength of a K-plus-T theory.

Primitive equality reflexivity alone likewise does not recover primitive
Sym.  In ordinary first-order semantics, let $E$ be a nonsymmetric
reflexive preorder and let membership be empty.  The witness row, both
congruences, all atomic vacuity, the quantifier rows and reflexivity hold,
while Sym fails.  This observation blocks a direct symmetry derivation;
it does not rule out every possible different interpretation.

\subsection{Identifying parameters in the propositional single axiom}

The retained Lukasiewicz row is
\[
 L(a,b,c,u)=((c\to u)\to a)\to((a\to c)\to(b\to c)).
\]
Its fourth parameter is a tempting source of parser savings.  Exhaustive
small-matrix search finds that every one of the six pairwise parameter
identifications produces an incomplete single-axiom system for classical
implication.  Every obstruction already has three truth values and a
single designated value.  The search and its explicit matrices are in
\path{find_three_variable_l_matrices.py} and
\path{evidence/three_variable_l_matrices.json}.

For example, for $u=b$ use designated value $2$ and implication
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 \to&0&1&2\\\hline
 0&1&2&2\\
 1&1&2&2\\
 2&1&1&2
\end{array}
\]
Every instance of $L(a,b,c,b)$ has value $2$, and MP preserves designation
because $2\to x$ is designated only when $x=2$.  Nevertheless $0\to0$
has value $1$, Peirce's law fails at $a=b=0$, and the S axiom fails at
$(a,b,c)=(0,1,0)$.  The other five identifications have similarly small
matrices falsifying the K axiom.  Thus no pairwise identification can
replace the existing propositional completeness proof.  This result is
about the single implicational axiom with substitution and MP; it does
not claim that the other retained quantifier or set-theory rows could
never recover the missing strength by a different argument.

\subsection{Safe additional source-layout choices}

The file \path{safe_row_variants.json} gives explicit postfix words for
several interderivable row families.  With $p=E(a,b)$ and $q=E(b,a)$,
the symmetry family can be emitted as $p\to q$ (seven calls),
$p\to(p\to q)$ (eleven), $\neg q\to\neg p$ (nine),
$p\to\neg\neg q$ (nine), or $\neg(p\wedge\neg q)$ (nine).
The longer variants may share more decoder structure, especially the
contracted form ending in two implication constructors.

With Sym retained, the witness row may use $\exists_a E(b,a)$ instead of
$\exists_a E(a,b)$; existential monotonicity connects them.  Either
membership-congruence row may independently reverse only its equality
atom.  In $\rho_2$, $\rho_3$ and $\rho_5$, the two curried antecedents
may be exchanged by the tautology
$(A\to(B\to C))\leftrightarrow(B\to(A\to C))$.
These choices preserve the number of calls in their respective rows.
Independent AX parameter permutations, logical-row permutations, and all
eight native helper binders are also justified by the retained proofs.
Their state costs should be measured jointly with Sym, since a locally
longer word can yield a smaller shared decoder.

ZF/ZFC equiconsistency comes from G\"odel's constructibility proof;
NBG's finite axiomatization and conservativity concern a richer class
language.  Neither result implies that the corresponding binary TM is
small.  The relevant quantity is the complete blank-tape transition
table after axiom generation, syntax handling, inference search, decoder,
and initialization are included.  A universal interpreter or a
Diophantine proof encoding similarly moves description size into its
program or coefficients; without a short blank-tape printer and a
two-direction finite-proof comparison, it gives no numerical improvement.
An independent combinatorial statement also needs an explicit ZFC proof
of the requested consistency equivalence; unprovability alone does not
supply it.
