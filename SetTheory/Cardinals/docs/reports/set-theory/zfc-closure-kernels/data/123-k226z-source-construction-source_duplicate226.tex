\section{A further exact quotient: 227 to 226 states}
\label{sec:k226-duplicate}

The $227$-state construction admits one further reduction.  Unlike the
preceding context-dependent fold, the final state identification here is
an ordinary total quotient of the transition table.  Source
reassociation is needed first to expose the duplicate.

In the $227$-state table, state $85$ has rows $0R19$ and $1R65$,
and state $65$ has rows $0R114$ and $1R12$.  These encode
\[
 (X,(Y,Z)),
\]
where $X,Y,Z$ are closed source chunks of $19$, $7$, and $26$ leaves.
The left rotation at outer state $85$ and inner state $65$ changes this
to $((X,Y),Z)$.  It is applied simultaneously at all ten occurrences
of the shared source subtree.  The complete source graph, including
all $6656$ guarded successors, is unchanged.

After the rotation, state $65$ has the rows
\[
 65/0=0R19,\qquad65/1=1R114.
\]
These are exactly the two rows of the already present state $98$.
Define $\phi(65)=98$ and $\phi(q)=q$ for every other state, including
the start and halt states.  Delete $65$ and redirect every transition
target through $\phi$.  For every old working state $q$ and read symbol
$b$, the checker verifies
\[
 \delta_{\rm new}(\phi(q),b)
   =(w,d,\phi(r))
 \quad\text{whenever}\quad
 \delta_{\rm old}(q,b)=(w,d,r).
\]
All $454$ old transition rows are checked, including the two rows of
the deleted state.  This is a one-step bisimulation on arbitrary tapes;
no predecessor-bit, source-invariant, or register-value hypothesis is
needed for the quotient itself.

\subsection{Repairing the new source addresses}

The first rotation creates four source paths with a seven-zero run.
The unrepaired $226$-state table fails at leaf $650$, instruction
$\mathrm{CD}_1$, at path
\texttt{1110100100111000010000000}.
The counterexample uses nine zero registers, one zero spare block, and
zero extra gap.  It reaches the clean dispatcher with the expected head
position but with two incorrect tape cells.  Thus the exact quotient
alone is insufficient to justify the complete machine construction:
the decoder address contract also has to be restored.

A bounded search found the following five closed rotations.  Directions
use the explicit parenthesizations in Section~\ref{sec:k227-source-roles}.
All three chunks in every row have no escaping control and no halt.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{r r c r r r}
Outer & Inner & Direction & $|A|$ & $|B|$ & $|C|$\\
\hline
101 & 209 & left  & 595 & 242 & 231\\
101 & 208 & left  & 837 & 82 & 149\\
226 & 224 & right & 80 & 244 & 440\\
205 & 134 & left  & 2 & 82 & 80\\
207 & 205 & right & 84 & 80 & 230
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The search held fixed the helper rows required by the $227$-state role
proof.  The deterministic builder contains only the selected rotation
sequence and independently checks every guarded successor after each
rotation.  Neither the search score nor the finite discovery tests
serve as the runtime proof.

The final source has $5121$ leaves, $6656$ guarded cases, $211$ distinct
subtrees, maximum depth $41$, and maximum zero run $6$.
The resulting binary table has $226$ working states and $452$ rows.
Relative to K230 it deletes states $65$, $79$, $140$, and $175$ and
changes $52$ surviving rows.  Across the entire construction there are
four $H$ replacements, two boundary rotations, and eleven rotations
whose three chunks are closed.

The final source is also related directly to the original K230 source
by an offset-aware certificate with $2376$ paired checkpoints and
$4750$ checked branches.  Exactly eight entries use offset $+1$ in
$R_5$, and all eight test $R_6$.  The other $2368$ entries have equal
register files, and the longest straight-line segment has five
instructions.  This removes any need to infer source equivalence from
the discovery history.

The canonical table has SHA-256
\begin{quote}\ttfamily\small
b58279b8b2e2cf029dd94e084d47cd6097772d7bce2a62653767895123d29599.
\end{quote}
The source and table are reproduced by \texttt{build226.py}, extending
the separately retained $227$-state recipe.  Full native runtime and
startup certificates, and the kernel-checked consistency endpoint,
are supplied as separate proof layers.
