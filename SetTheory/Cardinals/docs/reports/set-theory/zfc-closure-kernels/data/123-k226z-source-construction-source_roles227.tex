\section{The source transformation and the final decoder fold}
\label{sec:k227-source-roles}

This section uses the original state numbers of the released K230 table.
The final dense numbering is obtained only after deleting states
$79$, $140$, and $175$.  Its start state is $0$, and its separate halt
outcome is $227$.  The source and literal table are reconstructed by
\texttt{build227.py}; the final table is byte-identical to the table checked
by the complete native interpreter audit.

\subsection{A closed transfer loop}

Write $(A,B)$ for a binary source node with children $A$ and $B$.
The K230 source contains exactly four copies of
\[
 H_{\rm old}
 =\bigl((\mathrm{CD}_6,(\mathrm{CI}_5,\mathrm{CC}_2)),
          \mathrm{CI}_5\bigr).
\]
They are replaced by
\[
 H_{\rm new}=(\mathrm{CI}_5,(\mathrm{CD}_6,\mathrm{CC}_2)).
\]
Both programs are closed and terminate for every register file.  On input
$(R_5,R_6)=(a,b)$, both return $(a+b+1,0)$ and preserve the other seven
registers.  In the old program, every positive decrement of $R_6$ adds one
to $R_5$, and the zero exit adds one more.  In the new program, the first
increment supplies the extra one, and each positive decrement returns
through that increment.  Induction on $b$ proves the stated action and
termination.  Closure permits replacement in the surrounding program.

Each replacement removes one leaf, so the complete source changes from
$5125$ to $5121$ leaves.  The resulting source has $6656$ guarded
instruction cases.  This is a semantic block identity, rather than
equality of the two instruction graphs.

There is also a finite certificate directly relating the final source to
the original K230 source.  It has $2373$ pairs of test checkpoints and
$4744$ checked branches.  At $2365$ entries the register files agree.
At the other eight, all registers except $R_5$ agree and
\[
 R^{\rm candidate}_5=R^{\rm reference}_5+1.
\]
All eight exceptional entries test $R_6$, so the test results agree.
There are two program-counter spellings for each of the four loop tests.
No segment between tests contains more than five instructions.  The
untrusted list is generated by \texttt{build\_offset\_certificate.py};
its generic checker is a separate proof artifact.

\subsection{Two boundary rotations and five closed rotations}

Throughout this section, ``right rotation'' means
\[
 ((A,B),C)\longmapsto(A,(B,C)),
\]
and ``left rotation'' means its inverse.  This convention fixes the
parenthesizations independently of terminology in earlier research notes.

The first two right rotations occur at source prefixes
\texttt{11101001001} and \texttt{111010010011}.
After the $H$ replacement, their three chunk sizes are respectively
$(81,4853,1)$ and $(527,4326,1)$.  The first two chunks are closed;
the last chunk is $\mathrm{CB}_1$ and is not closed.  These are boundary
rotations.  For each, the checker reconstructs every guarded successor
and verifies equality of the entire source graph.  Their justification
does not invoke the lemma for three closed chunks.

The five subsequent rotations are listed below.  All three chunks in
each row have zero forward and backward escape and contain no halt.
The outer and inner state numbers identify the two affected decoder
nodes at that stage.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{r r c r r r}
Outer & Inner & Direction & $|A|$ & $|B|$ & $|C|$\\
\hline
202 & 201 & right & 52 & 54 & 189\\
174 & 119 & left  & 189 & 212 & 651\\
148 & 154 & left  & 245 & 160 & 132\\
210 & 174 & right & 401 & 651 & 473\\
174 & 101 & right & 56 & 595 & 473
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The first rotation makes all source entries to the two-copy implication
decoder $150$ have predecessor bit $0$.  The second makes the composite
decoder $152$ have predecessor bit $1$.  The third exposes the existing
helper route used by the right branch of that composite.  The last two
repair the address lengths introduced by these changes.

The full graph is checked after every rotation, including all $6656$
guards.  The final source has $212$ distinct subtrees, maximum path
length $40$, and maximum consecutive-zero run $6$.

The address repairs are essential.  Before them, the proposed
$227$-state table fails an exact finite check at source leaf $1042$,
whose instruction is $\mathrm{CD}_2$ and whose path is
\texttt{11101001001110010000000}.  The terminal seven-zero run collides
with the inherited separator scanner.  The machine returns to the
dispatcher with the expected head position but with an incorrect tape.
The first repair shifts the obstruction to the next closed chunk;
the second removes it.  The rejected table and counterexample are
retained as discovery evidence.

\subsection{Reassigning decoder roles}

Let $p$ denote the closed source chunk entered at native state $90$,
$z$ the chunk entered at state $19$, and $i=\mathrm{CI}_1$, entered at
state $61$.  The four relevant decoder roles are
\[
 pp=(p,p),\qquad pi=(p,i),\qquad zi=(z,i),\qquad W=(A,B),
\]
where the two children of $W$ are entered at states $155$ and $171$.
Before the last fold, $pp$ uses state $150$, $W$ uses state $152$, and
both $pi$ and $zi$ use state $175$.  The bit immediately left of the
head distinguishes the latter two roles.

After the source rotations, the exact incoming rows are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{old decoder} & \text{incoming rows} & \text{predecessor bit}\\
\hline
150 & 183/0,\ 201/0 & 0\\
152 & 119/1 & 1\\
175\ (pi) & 155/1,\ 176/1 & 1\\
175\ (zi) & 184/0 & 0 .
\end{array}
\]
Every listed row preserves its read symbol and moves right.  Thus these
tag facts follow inductively from the literal table, starting in state
$0$, on arbitrary initial tapes.

The new decoder assigns $pp$ and $pi$ to state $150$, and assigns $W$
and $zi$ to state $152$.  It deletes state $175$.  Relative to the
rotated $228$-state basis, the six changed surviving rows are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c@{\qquad}c|c}
150/1 & 1L173 & 152/0 & 0L159\\
152/1 & 1L169 & 155/1 & 1R150\\
176/1 & 1R150 & 184/0 & 0R152 .
\end{array}
\]
The helpers are existing states.  No new working state is introduced.

\subsection{An all-tape local proof}

The relation on configurations preserves the entire tape and the head
position.  It maps old state $175$ to new state $150$ when the
predecessor bit is $1$, and to new state $152$ when it is $0$.
Every other old state maps to itself.  The old tag invariant for states
$150$ and $152$ is the incoming-row invariant displayed above.

For each role and read symbol, both machines reach exactly the same
child state with head one cell to the right of its entry position.
The exact positive block lengths are:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c c r r r}
Role & Read & Old steps & New steps & Common child\\
\hline
$pp$ & 0 & 1 & 1 & 90\\
$pp$ & 1 & 1 & 5 & 90\\
$pi$ & 0 & 3 & 1 & 90\\
$pi$ & 1 & 1 & 3 & 61\\
$zi$ & 0 & 3 & 3 & 19\\
$zi$ & 1 & 1 & 3 & 61\\
$W$  & 0 & 1 & 3 & 155\\
$W$  & 1 & 1 & 3 & 171
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

For example, the new $pp$/1 block is
\[
150\longrightarrow173\longrightarrow153\longrightarrow172
\longrightarrow184\longrightarrow90,
\]
and the new $W$/1 block is
\[
152\longrightarrow169\longrightarrow148\longrightarrow171.
\]
The checker \texttt{verify\_roles.py} fixes only cells $-1$ and $0$,
executes these paths, and rejects any attempt to inspect another cell.
All paths restore the two fixed cells exactly.  Every outside tape cell
is therefore arbitrary and unchanged.  The proof is an exact finite
block proof with an open outside tape, not a sample of complete machine
executions.

All remaining rows agree literally except the three incoming redirects,
which write precisely the predecessor bit used by the relation.
Every matched block has positive length on both sides and avoids halt
internally.  Concatenating the blocks in either direction proves
halting equivalence of the rotated $228$-state basis and the final
$227$-state table from state $0$ on arbitrary tapes.

Combined with the $H$ identity, source-graph equalities, the two earlier
native-fold certificates, and the complete runtime and initialization
proofs, this establishes the transfer from the released K230 program to
the final machine.  The literal $227$-state table has $454$ rows; compared
with K230 it deletes six rows and changes $36$ surviving rows.
