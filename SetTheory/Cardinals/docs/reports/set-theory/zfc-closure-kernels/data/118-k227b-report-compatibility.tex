\section{Which of the eight historical savings survive?}
The historical count $243-235=8$ cannot simply be subtracted from 230.
The following ledger accounts for all eight steps. Old labels refer to the
historical inherited-label machines; K labels refer to the released K230 table.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{243 to 242, old $27\to15$: already present.}
K11 already contains the synthesized $0L26$ row and redirected predecessors.
\item \textbf{242 to 241, old $39\to17$: already present.}
K13 already has the $0L91$ and $1L31$ rows and corresponding redirections.
Counting either of these as a new K230 saving would double-count it.
\item \textbf{241 to 240, source-loop rewrite and old $90\to72$: transfers.}
This is the four-occurrence K121 source rewrite and K79-to-K63 fold used in
our completed 228-state composition.
\item \textbf{240 to 239, old $218\to179$: transfers jointly after repair.}
The corresponding K150 and K175 states both have mixed predecessor tags.
The historical separation of the left-zero K175 role needs a decoder with
children 19 and 61; that literal two-row decoder is not already available.
A paid clone would erase the intended saving. The coordinated source repair
in the 227-state construction instead reuses K150 without paying for a clone.
\item \textbf{239 to 238, old $169\to80$: transfers.}
This is the K140-to-K71 delayed-erasure fold, with K80 repaired, proving the
229-state intermediate and composing into the 228-state result.
\item \textbf{238 to 237, old $235\to98$: its partner disappeared.}
The old235 role's 149-operation ordered stream occurs nowhere in the new K
source, even when jump-operand retuning is allowed. Its old98 partner survives
at K85. Reassociation alone cannot restore a missing ordered stream.
\item \textbf{237 to 236, rotation and fold $223\to182$: transfers jointly.}
The rotation ports at K148/K154 and exposes the desired helper edge, but the
following fold does not: K152 has a left-zero guard where left-one is required,
and K175 has mixed tags and two logical source roles. The four-rotation
repair resolves these obstructions together with the preceding role-sharing
mechanism, saving one additional physical state rather than two.
\item \textbf{236 to 235, seven rotations and old $193\to74$: not unchanged.}
Five large rotation contexts are absent in K. The smaller old74 and old193
streams (84 and 177 leaves) survive as ordered intervals but are not exposed
subtrees. Recovering their contexts would require a new source-aware search,
not literal reuse of the seven edits.
\end{enumerate}
The exact machine-readable ledger includes incoming-row inventories, literal
role matches, and the missing-context witnesses. These are scoped compatibility
observations. They are not proofs that 226 states is impossible or that no
semantic rewrite can overcome the obstruction.

\section{Verification boundaries and reproducibility}
There are three distinct forms of evidence:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The \emph{released Rocq theorem} calibrates the exact input K230 source,
seed, and machine against standard ZFC inconsistency. We pinned and inspected
its inputs; its clean kernel builds are reported by the release.
\item The \emph{new ordinary proof chain} validates each literal output table,
state renumbering, source equivalence, whole-tape startup, and quantified
instruction simulation. Every required candidate runtime case is reconstructed
from the actual table, rather than accepted because an old certificate exists.
\item \emph{Discovery searches} suggest possible further reductions. Their
finite samples, negative enumerations, and resource bounds are not substituted
for the preceding proofs, nor interpreted as a global state lower bound.
\end{enumerate}
The portable archive includes the literal tables, LaTeX source of this report,
checker sources and their required runtime dependencies, exact manifests,
proof receipts, and the source-loop identity certificate. Its README supplies
quick and full replay commands. The full runtime engine must run with Python
assertions enabled. Independent construction and startup checks additionally
use explicit exceptions and can be checked under Python's optimized mode.
A final clean-extraction replay and hash check accompany the delivery.

\subsection{Further logical reduction: separate status}
The review also identifies a possible transfer of our restricted-equality-helper
argument to K. The published K proof itself uses full equality vacuity, so its
completeness lemma cannot simply be reused after deletion. The proposed repair
first obtains a homogeneous model of equality-free K instances, then quotients
by two-sided membership indiscernibility. Equality-containing matrices are
translated before choosing their native codes. Even the standard translation
of logical bottom must be changed to remain equality-free.

That separate ordinary argument depends on the previously audited logical
interpretation and needs its own source/enumerator realization. It supplies
neither an additional state saving nor a new Rocq endpoint in this report.
The verified 227-state machine retains exactly K230's axiom families.
