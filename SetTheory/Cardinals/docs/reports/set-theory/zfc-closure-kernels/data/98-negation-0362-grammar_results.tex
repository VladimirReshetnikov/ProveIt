\section{A directly retained negation constructor and its literal realization}

The concrete result of this grammar lane is a 247-state control, comprising
130 grammar roles and 117 retained framework roles. The requested strict
improvement on 245 states remains open. Within the retained framework a
successful candidate must have at most 127 grammar roles.

Let $C(x,y)=\binom{x+y+1}{2}+y$ denote the source Cantor pairing convention.
At the clean constructor interface, with logical registers 6 and 7 zero,
the old fine word and the new fine word have the same effect:
\[
  \mathtt{LNPQMVQPW}\ \equiv\ \mathtt{LMPW},\qquad
  (t,s,0,0)\longmapsto (4C(t,1),s,0,0).
\]
The old word temporarily pushes the formula onto the stack and immediately
pops it. The new word retains it in scratch storage while constructing the
constant right argument. This identity has an unbounded arithmetic proof and
a separate whole-source interface audit. It is not asserted from arbitrary
scratch contents. The independent proof permits arbitrary register 6 provided
register 7 is zero, in which case the paired left argument is their sum.

All 30 negations in the original AX source can be contracted, reducing the
fine word from 1,270 to 1,120 tokens. To make this useful at machine level,
the backend changes AST112 from $(57,42)$ to $(40,42)$, AST113 from
$(112,78)$ to $(112,77)$, and the source-state 237 pair from $(218,105)$ to
$(218,124)$. The physical 218/read 0 projection 00 becomes $L$, while its
projection 10 remains $VQPW$. This makes its variant0 implement $LM$ and
retains its other variant. Related projection targets containing negation
are changed coherently and checked individually.

Transporting the earlier 128-rule grammar through these changes requires
two routing carriers in the retained witness, yielding 130 roles. One serves
the new 218 projection, and the other repairs an old use of the negation tree
as a routing carrier: its 01 projection changes from $VQPW$ to $PW$.
These costs describe this particular realization and are not lower bounds.

The final eight-DNE Church-layout combination revealed an inherited search
bug: a reported 134-role witness tried to route through a physical terminal
temporarily assigned a dictionary rule. Garbage collection removed that rule.
The independent audit rejected the witness; the exact failed candidate and a
regression reconstruction are retained. The repaired engine forbids descent
through physical terminals and rechecks all projections after collection.
Its final 90-second run yields a different, independently valid 134-role
grammar in 1,611,191 proposals, still above the required 127. This case
illustrates why reported search counts require independent witness checking.
