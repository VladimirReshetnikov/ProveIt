\begin{abstract}
The full fresh equality-vacuity family in the original substitution-free Tarski frontend can be restricted to one literal native-code binder per equality atom. Keep the original eight logical rows, membership vacuity, CP and native GIC, and replace
\[
 \Eq(a,b)\To\All_c\Eq(a,b)\quad(c\ne a,b)
 \qquad\text{by}\qquad
 \Eq(a,b)\To\All_{\weq(a,b)}\Eq(a,b).
\]
The resulting theory $\Hh$ is equiconsistent with ordinary ZFC, in the external classical metatheory and relative to the existing completeness and model-construction endpoints identified here. The proof interprets comparison equality as two-sided membership indiscernibility. A restricted quantifier-free specialization argument and explicit auxiliary-binder transport avoid circular uses of general substitution or alpha conversion. Equality-free comparison matrices preserve the actual native GIC output codes. Independent finite certificates support the construction, including label zero and binder collisions. This is a mathematical consistency result; the modified native Lean proof relation, proof-assistant verification of selected-source enumeration, and a new binary-machine halting endpoint remain to be supplied. A separate ordinary source/enumerator binding is included in Appendix~\ref{sec:binding}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result and research status}
The positive result is a reduction of logical requirements. It is not yet a reduction of the best specialized binary machine's state count. At this checkpoint, no validated machine with at most 235 working states has been found. The supplied 236-state result is inherited from commit \file{0b5fec6980}; it is not a new construction of this investigation.

\textbf{Relation to prior work.} Source113 already describes eliminating identity through full incoming/outgoing observational equivalence in a complete first-order calculus, and explicitly warns that this model theorem does not justify deleting a numerical rule~\cite{source113}. The contribution recorded here is the noncircular bootstrap inside the weakened numerical-style calculus, together with the precise code-specific equality-vacuity restriction and the code-preserving theory comparison. Observational equality itself and the general quotient idea are not claimed as new.

Three levels of evidence must remain distinct:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A uniform ordinary mathematical argument establishes the consistency comparison for arbitrary natural-number labels.
\item Executable proof certificates check explicit finite Hilbert derivations and an independent collection of edge cases. Their generality is bounded by their stated schemas or numerical instantiations.
\item The changed native proof relation and enumeration coverage still need proof-assistant formalization and an exact binary runtime/blank-start calibration. Appendix~\ref{sec:binding} supplies an ordinary source-level enumeration theorem, with finite source identifications; it does not supply those stronger endpoints.
\end{enumerate}
The article states the clean surgical result first. Broader $K/S$ frontends with canonical or projection vacuity also admit the interpretation; Section~\ref{sec:variants} describes the additional classicality and code obligations. A pinned generic compilation of the exact surgical theory has 263 working states, including its startup and totalization construction. It is a cost observation for that compiler, not a saving transferable to the specialized 236-state architecture.

\section{Exact syntax and source conventions}\label{sec:syntax}
Labels are natural numbers. The relative-falsity source language has membership atoms $\Rel(a,b)$, primitive equality atoms $\Eq(a,b)$, implication, and named universal quantification $\All_i A$. Its abbreviations are literally
\begin{equation}\label{eq:abbr}
 F=\Rel(0,0),\qquad \neg A=(A\To F),\qquad
 A\wedge B=\neg(A\To\neg B),\qquad
 \Ex_i A=\neg\All_i\neg A.
\end{equation}
There is no primitive bottom constructor in this source. In particular, the label 0 inside $F$ can be captured. A genuine-bottom language will be introduced explicitly for the comparison calculus and for a limited auxiliary syntax; its bottom will be translated to $F$.

\subsection{The original logical rows}
Writing $A,B,C,U$ for arbitrary formulas, and allowing all label coincidences unless a gate is stated, the original rows are
\begin{align*}
\rho_1&:&&\Ex_a\Eq(a,b),\\
\rho_2&:&&\All_i(A\To B)\To(\All_i A\To\All_i B),\\
\rho_3&:&&\Eq(a,b)\To(\Rel(c,a)\To\Rel(c,b)),\\
\rho_4&:&&\All_i\All_j A\To\All_j\All_i A,\\
\rho_5&:&&\Eq(a,b)\To(\Rel(a,c)\To\Rel(b,c)),\\
\rho_6&:&&\Ex_i\All_i A\To A,\\
\rho_7&:&&\Eq(c,a)\To(\Eq(c,b)\To\Eq(a,b)),\\
\rho_8&:&&((C\To U)\To A)\To((A\To C)\To(B\To C)).
\end{align*}
Our parameter order for $\rho_5$ is a renaming of the repository's row. The calculus additionally has atomic vacuity for $\Rel$ and $\Eq$ at $i\ne a,b$, modus ponens, and generalization of closed theorems. A theory axiom is available under every assignment; ``closed theorem'' here means a proof with no temporary undischarged hypotheses, not a formula without free variables. This distinction matters throughout.

\subsection{The surgical theory}
\begin{definition}
Let $\Hh$ keep $\rho_1$--$\rho_8$, the exact CP/native-GIC source families, full gated membership vacuity, MP and closed-theorem GEN. Replace only full equality vacuity by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:helper}
 V_{\mathrm{eq}}(a,b):\quad
 \Eq(a,b)\To\All_{k_{ab}}\Eq(a,b),\qquad
 k_{ab}=\weq(a,b)=4P(a,b)+3,
\end{equation}
where $P(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b$. Define $\Con(\Hh)$ to mean $\Hh\nvdash F$.
\end{definition}
Since $P(a,b)\ge a,b$, the chosen $k_{ab}$ is strictly larger than both endpoints, including when $a=b=0$. If the helper is emitted inside the original bundle gated by a third parameter $c\ne a,b$, every $(a,b)$ instance remains available: choose $c=k_{ab}$ and project the helper after establishing classicality. Its internal binder is $k_{ab}$, independently of the bundle's output parameter. There is no gate requiring $a\ne b$.

\subsection{The native code zero alias}
The native numeric constructors are
\begin{align*}
\operatorname{Imp}(m,n)&=4P(m,n), &\operatorname{Rel}(a,b)&=4P(a,b)+1,\\
\operatorname{All}(i,n)&=4P(i,n)+2, &\operatorname{Eq}(a,b)&=4P(a,b)+3.
\end{align*}
Thus $F$ has code 1, and $\Eq(0,0)$ has code 3. Numeric code 0 decodes to $F\To F$; encoding that displayed finite tree gives 16. Consequently $\enc(\dec(0))=16\ne0$. No part of the new proof assumes that encoding inverts decoding on arbitrary numbers.

For every positive code $n$, every label in $\dec(n)$ is less than $n$. Strong induction proves this: unpaired fields are at most $\lfloor n/4\rfloor<n$; positive recursive children use the induction hypothesis; a zero child decodes to a tree containing only label 0. The separate $n=0$ case is a truth alias. These facts will justify canonical vacuity in Section~\ref{sec:variants}. The main surgical reverse comparison needs only $k_{ab}>a,b$.

\section{Propositional and quantifier tools}\label{sec:tools}
The retained $\rho_8$ yields $K$, $S$, and the classical implicational basis through the existing finite derivations in \file{Lukasiewicz.lean}. Relative explosion also follows from $\rho_6$: for any $A$, $K$ gives
\[
 F\To\bigl(\All_i(\All_i A\To F)\To F\bigr)
   =F\To\Ex_i\All_i A,
\]
and composition with $\rho_6$ gives $F\To A$. Thus classical tautologies relative to the exact $F$ are derivable in $\Hh$. This includes $F\To\All_i F$ for every $i$, including 0; it does not require $F$ to be syntactically closed.

Every use of a propositional tautology below means substitution into this derived classical basis. In the main finite E certificates, \file{TAUT} leaves are checked by Boolean enumeration with $F$ false and every other non-implication expression an independent atom. These are explicit abbreviations for propositional derivations, not new first-order axioms. The finite replay does not expand every such abbreviation into $\rho_8$ proofs.

From a closed theorem $A\To B$, GEN and $\rho_2$ derive quantified monotonicity
\[
 \All_i A\To\All_i B.
\]
Contraposition on both sides derives existential monotonicity $\Ex_i A\To\Ex_i B$. Another consequence of $\rho_2$ and classical contraposition is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:allimpex}
 \All_i(A\To B)\To(\Ex_i A\To\Ex_i B).
\end{equation}
None of these steps generalizes an open assumption.

\begin{lemma}[Primitive symmetry without full equality vacuity]\label{lem:sym}
The theory $\Hh$ proves $\Eq(x,x)$ and $\Eq(x,y)\To\Eq(y,x)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $z=k_{xx}$. By $\rho_7$ and propositional contraction,
$\Eq(z,x)\To\Eq(x,x)$. Existential monotonicity and $\rho_1(z,x)$ give $\Ex_z\Eq(x,x)$. Apply existential monotonicity to the exact helper on $\Eq(x,x)$, obtaining $\Ex_z\All_z\Eq(x,x)$. Row $\rho_6$ now yields $\Eq(x,x)$. Finally $\rho_7$ with common first endpoint $x$, together with reflexivity, gives symmetry. Only the exact helper at a reflexive atom was used. This argument precedes every claim about defined equality.
\end{proof}

\section{Membership defined equality}\label{sec:definition}
For $z\ne a,b$, set
\begin{align}
 L_{ab}(z)&=(\Rel(z,a)\To\Rel(z,b))\wedge(\Rel(z,b)\To\Rel(z,a)),\label{eq:L}\\
 U_{ab}(z)&=(\Rel(a,z)\To\Rel(b,z))\wedge(\Rel(b,z)\To\Rel(a,z)),\label{eq:U}\\
 E_{ab}^{z}&=(\All_z L_{ab}(z))\wedge(\All_z U_{ab}(z)),\label{eq:Ez}\\
 E(a,b)&=E_{ab}^{k_{ab}}.\label{eq:E}
\end{align}
All conjunctions have the negative grouping in~\eqref{eq:abbr}. Equality is two-sided: both incoming and outgoing membership profiles are compared. Primitive $\Eq$ need not itself become logical identity in every weakened model; it will provide the witnesses needed to interpret identity by $E$.

\begin{lemma}[The exact helper supplies equality witnesses]\label{lem:eqE}
For all $a,b$, $\Hh$ proves
\[
 \Eq(a,b)\To E(a,b),\qquad \Ex_a E(a,b).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $z=k_{ab}$. Rows $\rho_3$ and $\rho_5$ give the forward membership implications in~\eqref{eq:L} and~\eqref{eq:U}. Primitive symmetry followed by the same rows with reversed endpoints gives their converses. Conjunction introduction yields the closed implications
\[
 \Eq(a,b)\To L_{ab}(z),\qquad \Eq(a,b)\To U_{ab}(z).
\]
Generalize each closed implication at $z$, and apply $\rho_2$. Compose with~\eqref{eq:helper} to obtain the two universally quantified conjuncts under the premise $\Eq(a,b)$, then conjoin. Existential monotonicity at $a$ and $\rho_1(a,b)$ yield the second theorem. The literal internal binder $z$ is unchanged throughout.
\end{proof}
The separate certificate for this bridge uses $K/S$, primitive symmetry, $\rho_1,\rho_2,\rho_3,\rho_5$, MP, closed GEN, and the exact equality helper. It does not use classical \file{TAUT}, primitive reflexivity, primitive transitivity, CP/GIC, alpha conversion, or full equality vacuity. Its primitive-symmetry premise is discharged by Lemma~\ref{lem:sym} in $\Hh$.

\section{Restricted specialization without a circular bootstrap}\label{sec:bootstrap}
The main obstacle is that changing endpoint labels changes the code-dependent auxiliary binder in $E(a,b)$. Assuming general alpha conversion, general specialization, or quantified Leibniz substitution at this stage would import precisely the logical structure being reconstructed. The following restricted construction avoids that dependence.

\subsection{A designated bottom in the auxiliary syntax}
For this section, use finite membership expressions with a \emph{designated} constant $\bot_*$, translated to $F$. Replacing a variable fixes $\bot_*$. It acts on the arguments of ordinary membership atoms as usual. Freshness ignores only the designated constant; it counts all other labels, including bound labels. This is an auxiliary proof notation, not a claim that raw renaming of the atom $\Rel(0,0)$ fixes its arguments.

The certificate generator represents $\bot_*$ and the raw atom $\Rel(0,0)$ by the same tuple. Its renaming function therefore fixes that tuple. Its API must not be described as an implementation of arbitrary raw-syntax substitution. For the needed E-body inputs this ambiguity causes no error: every non-bottom membership atom contains its auxiliary $z$, and $z\ne a,b$, so no original such atom is $\Rel(0,0)$. A substituted atom may become $F$; the atomic congruence proof still justifies that result. General raw substitution is used only after the full proof interpretation has been established.

\begin{lemma}[Membership-only fresh vacuity]\label{lem:vac}
If $A$ is an auxiliary membership-only expression and $i$ is absent from all its non-bottom labels, including bound labels, then its source translation satisfies $A\To\All_i A$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the expression. A membership atom uses retained membership vacuity; $\bot_*$ uses explosion. For implication, first note that an already derived $P\To\All_iP$ gives
\[
 \Ex_i P\To\Ex_i\All_iP\To P
\]
by existential monotonicity and $\rho_6$. Contraposition yields $\neg P\To\All_i\neg P$. From the induction hypotheses for $P,Q$, quantified monotonicity applied to the closed tautologies
$\neg P\To(P\To Q)$ and $Q\To(P\To Q)$ gives both branches
\[
 \neg P\To\All_i(P\To Q),\qquad
 Q\To\All_i(P\To Q).
\]
The classical equivalence $P\To Q\ \leftrightarrow\ \neg P\lor Q$ combines them. For $A=\All_jB$, generalize the closed induction theorem at $j$, distribute with $\rho_2$, and commute the two quantifiers with $\rho_4$. This proof renames no internal binder.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Quantifier-free specialization]\label{lem:qf}
Let $A$ be quantifier-free in membership and designated bottom, and let $x\ne y$. If $A'$ replaces all displayed occurrences of $x$ by $y$, fixing designated bottom, then the source calculus proves $\All_x A\To A'$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Atomic $\rho_3/\rho_5$, primitive symmetry, and induction on propositional constructors prove both directions of
\begin{equation}\label{eq:qfleib}
 \Eq(x,y)\To(A\leftrightarrow A').
\end{equation}
If both atomic arguments are $x$, rewrite one position at a time. The designated-bottom case is identity. There is no quantified-constructor case, hence no need to move an equality premise through a binder.

Shuffle the forward implication of~\eqref{eq:qfleib} to $A\To(\Eq(x,y)\To A')$, then generalize this closed theorem at $x$ and distribute. This gives
\[
 \All_x A\To\All_x(\Eq(x,y)\To A').
\]
Equation~\eqref{eq:allimpex} and $\rho_1(x,y)$ now give $\All_x A\To\Ex_x A'$. No non-bottom $x$ remains in $A'$, so Lemma~\ref{lem:vac} supplies $A'\To\All_x A'$. Existential monotonicity and $\rho_6$ give $\Ex_x A'\To A'$, completing the proof. If $x$ or $y$ is 0, the designated-bottom case is still justified by explosion rather than syntactic freshness.
\end{proof}
Only this quantifier-free lemma is needed to transport E's binders. A self-specialization example is also checked in the certificate package by a fresh detour; it is not assumed as an extra rule. We do not infer general substitution or alpha conversion from these finite examples.

\section{Auxiliary transport and the reconstructed equality rows}\label{sec:rows}
\begin{lemma}[Auxiliary-binder transport]\label{lem:transport}
For $z,w\ne a,b$, both $E_{ab}^{z}\To E_{ab}^{w}$ and $E_{ab}^{w}\To E_{ab}^{z}$ are derivable.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $z=w$, use identity. Otherwise project $\All_z L_{ab}(z)$ from $E_{ab}^{z}$ and apply Lemma~\ref{lem:qf} to get the closed implication $E_{ab}^{z}\To L_{ab}(w)$. Do the same for $U$. Since $w$ is different from $a,b,z$, it is absent from every non-bottom label of the entire premise $E_{ab}^{z}$. Lemma~\ref{lem:vac} therefore gives
\[
 E_{ab}^{z}\To\All_w E_{ab}^{z}.
\]
Generalize each previously derived closed implication at $w$, distribute, and compose with this vacuity theorem. Conjoin to get $E_{ab}^{z}\To E_{ab}^{w}$. Reverse the roles of $z,w$. At no point is the premise generalized at its own internal binder, and no general alpha theorem has been used.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Full fresh vacuity for E]\label{prop:Evac}
For $i\ne a,b$, $\Hh$ proves $E(a,b)\To\All_i E(a,b)$, including $i=k_{ab}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Choose $w=\max(a,b,i,k_{ab})+1$. Transport $E(a,b)$ to $E_{ab}^{w}$. The label $i$ is absent from the non-bottom labels of this new formula, so Lemma~\ref{lem:vac} yields $E_{ab}^{w}\To\All_i E_{ab}^{w}$. Quantified monotonicity applied to the reverse transport gives $\All_i E_{ab}^{w}\To\All_iE(a,b)$. Compose. The collision $i=k_{ab}$ is exactly why vacuity is not applied directly to the original presentation of $E$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Both membership congruences]\label{prop:cong}
For all $a,b,c$, including repeated labels,
\[
 E(a,b)\To(\Rel(c,a)\To\Rel(c,b)),\qquad
 E(a,b)\To(\Rel(a,c)\To\Rel(b,c)).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Choose $w=\max(a,b,c,k_{ab})+1$ and transport to $E_{ab}^{w}$. Project each quantified matrix and specialize it from $w$ to $c$ using Lemma~\ref{lem:qf}, then project its forward implication. The choice of $w$ verifies the required $w\ne c$, even if $c=k_{ab}$ or $c=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The exact translated rho7]\label{prop:E7}
For arbitrary $a,b,c$,
\[
 E(c,a)\To(E(c,b)\To E(a,b)).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Choose $w$ larger than $a,b,c,k_{ca},k_{cb},k_{ab}$. Transport both premises to this common auxiliary. Pointwise Boolean transitivity supplies closed tautologies
\[
 L_{ca}(w)\To(L_{cb}(w)\To L_{ab}(w)),\qquad
 U_{ca}(w)\To(U_{cb}(w)\To U_{ab}(w)).
\]
Generalize these closed tautologies at $w$ and distribute twice with $\rho_2$. The two universally quantified premises now yield the two conjuncts of $E_{ab}^{w}$. Conjoin and transport to $k_{ab}$. The two E premises themselves are never generalized as open hypotheses.
\end{proof}
Reflexivity $E(a,a)$ follows by generalizing membership identity tautologies and conjoining. Symmetry follows by pair reversal and transport, or by reflexivity and Proposition~\ref{prop:E7}. Lemma~\ref{lem:eqE}, Propositions~\ref{prop:Evac}--\ref{prop:E7}, and these checks cover all original equality requirements. Every fresh-label choice is an explicit maximum plus one over a finite set; the argument is uniform in arbitrary natural-number labels, not an extrapolation from the sample roots.

\section{Interpreting the complete substitution-free calculus}\label{sec:interpretation}
Use the genuine-bottom named syntax \file{Tarski.NF} and the original full calculus \file{Tarski.Thm}. Define a structural translation $\tau$ by
\begin{align*}
\tau(\Rel(a,b))&=\Rel(a,b),& \tau(\Eq(a,b))&=E(a,b),&\tau(\bot)&=F,\\
\tau(A\To B)&=\tau(A)\To\tau(B),&
\tau(\All_i A)&=\All_i\tau(A).
\end{align*}
It preserves every quantifier label. All logical axiom constructors have derivable translations:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
Original constructor & Derivation of its translation\\\midrule
$\rho_1$ & $\Ex_aE(a,b)$ from Lemma~\ref{lem:eqE}\\
$\rho_2,\rho_4,\rho_6$ & Retained rows at translated formula parameters\\
$\rho_3,\rho_5$ & Proposition~\ref{prop:cong}\\
$\rho_7$ & Proposition~\ref{prop:E7}\\
$\rho_8$ & Retained classical row\\
Membership vacuity & Retained gated row\\
Equality vacuity & Proposition~\ref{prop:Evac}\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
MP and closed GEN commute with $\tau$. Induction on a \file{Thm} proof therefore translates every derivation whose extra axiom translations are theorems of $\Hh$.

The proof relation has no substitution constructor. We do not assert that raw substitution commutes with $\tau$: changing $a,b$ changes $k_{ab}$, and substituting a label does not automatically recompute that code. All necessary changes of auxiliary binder have already been supplied by Lemma~\ref{lem:transport}. General specialization and alpha conversion can subsequently be imported as theorems of the complete comparison calculus; they were not assumptions of its interpretation.

This also explains why using the existing full-calculus completeness theorem is legitimate. Its proof internally uses the original equality-vacuity and derived substitution laws, but the proof interpretation supplies translations of every original constructor. It does not assume completeness for the reduced source itself.

\section{An equality-free comparison theory with exact output codes}\label{sec:theory}
The code-coupled GIC family creates an important constraint: expanding equality in a matrix generally changes its code, and the output binder is that code. The argument avoids this problem by choosing an equality-free comparison theory from the outset.

\subsection{Literal CP and GIC formulas}
To fix parentheses and internal shadowing, write the named formulas from \file{Code254Containers.lean} using the relative abbreviations~\eqref{eq:abbr}:
\begin{align*}
 C(a,b,c)=\Ex_b\bigl[&\All_a\bigl(\Rel(a,b)\To
   (D(a,b,c)\wedge\All_c(\Rel(c,a)\To\Rel(c,b)))\bigr)\\[-1pt]
 &\wedge\Rel(c,b)\bigr],\\
 D(a,b,c)=\Ex_c\bigl[&\All_b\bigl(\All_c(\Rel(c,b)\To\Rel(c,a))\To\Rel(b,c)\bigr)
 \wedge\Rel(c,b)\bigr],\\
 G(a,b,c,p)=\Ex_c\bigl[&\All_a\bigl(\Rel(a,b)\To
     \All_b(p\To\Ex_b(p\wedge\Rel(b,c)))\bigr)\\[-1pt]
 &\wedge\All_b(\Rel(b,c)\To\Ex_a p)\bigr].
\end{align*}
The repeated binders are intentional; no informal renaming is implicit. The exact source bundle couples the matrix to its output label. The comparison uses the following theory $T_0$ in the genuine-bottom named language:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The bot-free formula $C(2,3,1)$, supplied by the gated bundle whose matrix is $F$, of native code 1. Both gates $1\ne2,3$ hold.
\item For each equality-free ordinary de Bruijn formula $\varphi$, let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:matrix}
 f=\operatorname{toNamed}(0,\varphi),\qquad
 p=\pad(f)=((f\To f)\To f),\qquad c=\enc(p).
\end{equation}
Include exactly $G(1,3,c,p)$, with this actual $c$.
\item Genuine-bottom irreflexivity
\[
 \mathrm{IR}=\All_0(\Rel(0,0)\To\bot).
\]
\end{enumerate}
The name translation assigns free slot $j$ the odd name $2j+1$ and uses even, depth-indexed names for binders. More precisely,
\[
 \operatorname{names}(0,j)=2j+1,\quad
 \operatorname{names}(d+1,0)=2d,\quad
 \operatorname{names}(d+1,j+1)=\operatorname{names}(d,j).
\]
A new quantified variable at depth $d$ receives $2d$. Genuine bottom in $\varphi$ is translated to the membership atom $F$. These definitions select literal source matrices; they do not use an unproved renaming rule in $\Hh$.

\subsection{Why the native code coupling is preserved}
Finite-tree native coding gives $c>3$ for the padding in~\eqref{eq:matrix}. Every label occurring in $p$ is below $\enc(p)$, so $c$ is absent from $p$; in particular both source gates $c\ne1,3$ hold. The padding is equivalent to $f$ under every assignment. The existing named-matrix adapter handles the forward and reverse update orders inside $G$ without treating the repeated binders as distinct fresh variables.

Items 1 and 2 of $T_0$ contain no primitive equality and no genuine bottom. Their relative falsehood is already the raw membership atom $F$. Consequently
\[
 \tau(C(2,3,1))=C(2,3,1),\qquad
 \tau(G(1,3,c,p))=G(1,3,c,p)
\]
\emph{literally}, including the matrix, all binder labels, and its native output code. Classical projection retrieves the needed components from the emitted negative-conjunction bundle. If the measured frontend reorders classically equivalent conjunction trees, propositional equivalence and quantified monotonicity recover these exact forms.

Finally $\tau(\mathrm{IR})=\All_0(F\To F)$, a generalized identity theorem. The proof never translates an equality-containing GIC matrix while keeping its former output code. Nor does it pick a convenient fresh output label independently of the matrix. It starts with an encodable finite named matrix and its actual encoding, and never needs $\enc(\dec(n))=n$.

\section{The consistency comparison and exact existing endpoints}\label{sec:consistency}
\begin{theorem}[Surgical equality-vacuity reduction]\label{thm:main}
In the ordinary external classical metatheory, using the existing full-calculus completeness and CP/GIC model-construction results specified below,
\[
 \Con(\Hh)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\]
Here consistency on the right is absence of an ordinary finite ZFC refutation. This statement does not assert a new internal PA proof-code equivalence or a new machine-halting theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assume $\Hh\nvdash F$. A refutation of $T_0$ in the complete genuine-bottom Tarski calculus would translate, by Section~\ref{sec:interpretation} and the literal theory translations of Section~\ref{sec:theory}, to a proof of $F$ in $\Hh$. \section{Other helper binders and the diagonal zero case}\label{sec:kappa}
The main theorem does not require the equality helper to use an equality code. This extension gives the exact flexibility available to later source implementations; it leaves the pinned cost in Section~\ref{sec:cost} unchanged.

\begin{theorem}[Safe off-diagonal helper]\label{thm:kappa}
Let $\kappa:\mathbb N^2\To\mathbb N$ satisfy only
\[
 a\ne b\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\kappa(a,b)\notin\{a,b\}.
\]
Keep original $\rho_8$, $\rho_1$--$\rho_6$, membership vacuity and exact CP/GIC, and keep either original $\rho_7$ or primitive equality symmetry. Replace full equality vacuity by
\[
 \Eq(a,b)\To\All_{\kappa(a,b)}\Eq(a,b).
\]
The resulting ordinary theory is equiconsistent with ZFC at the scope of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}. There is no restriction on $\kappa(a,a)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In the $\rho_7$ variant, fix $x$ and put $z=\kappa(x,x)$, $d=\Eq(z,x)$ and $e=\Eq(x,x)$. Row $\rho_7$ gives $d\To(d\To e)$ and hence $d\To e$. Row $\rho_1(z,x)$ and existential monotonicity yield $\Ex_z e$. The diagonal helper and another application of existential monotonicity yield $\Ex_z\All_z e$, and $\rho_6$ yields $e$. Thus primitive reflexivity and symmetry are recovered even when $z=x=0$. No freshness premise appears in this derivation. The primitive-symmetry variant can skip it.

Keep interpreted equality fixed as $E(a,b)=E_{ab}^{\weq(a,b)}$. If $a\ne b$, the assumed condition makes $z=\kappa(a,b)$ safe. The same source-helper bridge proves $\Eq(a,b)\To E_{ab}^{z}$, and Lemma~\ref{lem:transport} takes this to $E(a,b)$. If $a=b$, both membership matrices defining the fixed-safe-auxiliary $E(a,a)$ consist of identity implications. Their negative conjunctions, closed GEN, and conjunction introduction prove $E(a,a)$ directly. Hence $\Eq(a,a)\To E(a,a)$ follows by $K$, without using the potentially colliding helper or any primitive equality fact. In both cases existential monotonicity and $\rho_1(a,b)$ supply $\Ex_a E(a,b)$. The remaining E laws and the theory comparison are unchanged.

For the reverse direction, an off-diagonal helper is an instance of old equality vacuity. A diagonal helper is derivable in the old calculus from equality reflexivity, closed GEN at the chosen binder, and $K$, regardless of collision. Primitive symmetry is also an old theorem.
\end{proof}
If the helper remains inside the old gate $c\ne a,b$, choose any such $c$ to project it. At a diagonal collision one must not choose $c=\kappa(a,a)$; the bundle parameter and the helper's internal binder have independent roles.

All native constructors $4P(a,b)+t$ for $t=0,1,2,3$, with either argument order, satisfy the condition. Positive tags always exceed both arguments. For tag 0, $P(a,b)>0$ unless $(a,b)=(0,0)$, and then $4P(a,b)>a,b$. The sole collision for either implication builder is $\kappa(0,0)=0$, explicitly covered above. Thus the canonical equality-code control and all seven other constructor/order choices are admitted; symmetry of $\kappa$ is unnecessary.

The included \file{proofs/check_general_kappa.py} checks 169 closed roots and 27,486 proof nodes under Python \file{-O}. It covers all eight builders, off-diagonal source-to-E bridges, primitive equality facts with diagonal labels 0, 1, 2 and 999, and fixed-safe-auxiliary diagonal E/existence proofs. The collision is tested literally. These finite checks supplement the uniform argument; they do not themselves certify an effective builder, its schema coverage, an altered enumerator, or a runtime.
