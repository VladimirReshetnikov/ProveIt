\section{Two repairs of the three-parameter logical frontend}
\label{sec:ksrepairs}

The useful outcome of this investigation is a pair of complete logical
replacement arguments, together with finite proof certificates for their
critical steps. These are frontend results. They do not supply a smaller
binary Turing machine. Their cost must be measured after compiling the
entire altered proof enumerator with the selected runtime and initializer.

The K/S/Peirce replacement itself is prior ProveIt work. Source~91 already
measured a three-parameter K/S/Peirce control, and Source~95 supplies both
an explicitly checked K/S/Peirce-to-Lukasiewicz certificate and a routed
250-state control. We reuse that basis comparison and attribute it to
those sources. The new repairs examined here replace Peirce by either
global double-negation elimination or a restricted quantified schema.

\subsection{The exact numerical algebra and retained rules}

Let
\[
 \pi(x,y)=\frac{(x+y)(x+y+1)}2+y,
 \qquad
 I(x,y)=4\pi(x,y).
\]
The numerical constructors are
\[
 R(i,j)=4\pi(i,j)+1,\quad
 Q_iA=4\pi(i,A)+2,\quad
 E(i,j)=4\pi(i,j)+3.
\]
We write $A\to B$ for $I(A,B)$ and keep the exact old constants
\[
 F=1=R(0,0),\qquad 0=I(0,0).
\]
The latter is a real alias: code~0 is a primitive theorem, and is not the
code of a finite constructor tree in a free syntax. Define
\[
 \neg A=(A\to F),\qquad
 A\ksAnd B=\neg(A\to\neg B),\qquad
 \exists_i A=\neg Q_i\neg A.
\]
Every displayed propositional derivation below uses only equations of
these operations and the explicitly named inference rules. It therefore
admits arbitrary numerical substitution, including values~0 and~1;
constructor injectivity is not an assumption of the finite proofs.

The old propositional row is
\[
 L(a,b,c,u)=((c\to u)\to a)\to((a\to c)\to(b\to c)).
\]
Let $\mathcal C_L$ be the old numerical theorem calculus. In addition to
$L$, it has truth~0, modus ponens, theorem-only generalization
$A\mapsto Q_iA$, the seven other old logical rows, and the old gated
set-theory bundle. Only three of the retained rows are needed in the
bootstrap:
\begin{align*}
 \rho_1(i,j)&=\neg Q_i\neg E(i,j),\\
 \rho_2(i,A,B)&=Q_i(A\to B)\to(Q_iA\to Q_iB),\\
 \rho_6(i,A)&=\exists_i Q_iA\to A.
\end{align*}
The other four logical rows, the generalization mechanism and every
component of the set-theory bundle are retained literally.

The relevant outer shape of the gated bundle is
\[
 \mathcal B(a,b,c)=
 (C(a,b,c)\ksAnd G(a,b,c))\ksAnd
 (V_R(a,b,c)\ksAnd V_E(a,b,c)),
\]
where
\[
 V_R=R(a,b)\to Q_cR(a,b),\qquad
 V_E=E(a,b)\to Q_cE(a,b).
\]
It is emitted as a theorem only when $c\ne a$ and $c\ne b$.
The symbols $C,G$ denote the unchanged full native CP/GIC matrices,
including their original numerical parameter convention. In particular
$c$ is both an index and a matrix code in the GIC emitter. We never
delete this gate, assume an unguarded bundle, or require $a\ne b$.

The two standard implicational schemas are
\[
 K(A,B)=A\to(B\to A),
 \quad
 S(A,B,C)=(A\to(B\to C))\to((A\to B)\to(A\to C)).
\]
Write
\[
 \ksD A=\neg\neg A\to A,
 \qquad \ksV iA=A\to Q_iA.
\]
In the next lemmas, a temporary assumption is discharged by the ordinary
K/S deduction construction. Generalization is applied only to previously
proved theorems, never to a formula depending on a temporary assumption.
The accompanying finite certificates eliminate all temporary assumptions
and explicitly check this distinction.

\subsection{A bottom supplied by the retained quantified rule}

\begin{lemma}[Explosion and seriality]
\label{lem:ksefq}
K, S, and the retained quantified rows prove, for every numerical $A,i$,
\[
 F\to A,\qquad Q_iF\to F.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For explosion, K gives
$F\to(Q_i\neg Q_iA\to F)=F\to\exists_iQ_iA$.
Compose with $\rho_6(i,A)$ by K/S and modus ponens.

For seriality choose any index $j$. K gives
$F\to(E(i,j)\to F)$. Generalize this theorem with index~$i$ and apply
$\rho_2$, obtaining $Q_iF\to Q_i\neg E(i,j)$. The exact old row
$\rho_1(i,j)$ is $Q_i\neg E(i,j)\to F$, so composition gives the result.
There is no freshness assumption on $i,j$, nor any appeal to Peirce,
classical semantics, or generalization under assumptions.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Positive vacuity implies stability]
\label{lem:ksvstable}
If $A\to Q_iA$ is a theorem, then $\ksD A$ is a theorem in the K/S system
with $\rho_1,\rho_2,\rho_6$ and theorem-only generalization.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Generalize the theorem $A\to Q_iA$ and use $\rho_2$ to obtain
\[
 Q_iA\to Q_iQ_iA. \tag{*}
\]
Assume $\neg\neg A$ and $H=Q_i\neg Q_iA$. Under the additional assumption
$A$, positive vacuity and $(*)$ give $Q_iQ_iA$. Distribution applied to
$H$ gives $Q_iQ_iA\to Q_iF$, so Lemma~\ref{lem:ksefq} gives $F$.
Discharge $A$ to obtain $\neg A$; the assumed $\neg\neg A$ gives $F$.
Discharge $H$ to obtain $\neg Q_i\neg Q_iA=\exists_iQ_iA$. The retained
row $\rho_6(i,A)$ yields $A$. Discharging $\neg\neg A$ proves stability.
The only generalization in this argument was applied before any local
assumption was introduced, to the given theorem $A\to Q_iA$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Implication stability]
\label{lem:ksimpstable}
If $\ksD B$ is a theorem then $\ksD{(A\to B)}$ is a theorem using K/S.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Assume $H=\neg\neg(A\to B)$ and $A$. Under $\neg B$, any assumed
$A\to B$ yields $F$, so $\neg(A\to B)$ follows by discharge. Apply
$H$ to obtain $F$ and discharge $\neg B$, obtaining $\neg\neg B$.
The given stability of $B$ yields $B$. Discharge $A$, then $H$.
This proof needs stability only of the consequent, and does not need
explosion or quantified rules.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Negative-product extraction without classical circularity]
\label{lem:ksbundle}
K/S prove
\[
 (A\ksAnd B)\to\neg\neg A,
 \qquad
 (A\ksAnd B)\to\neg\neg B,
 \qquad
 \neg\neg\neg A\to\neg A.
\]
Consequently, from a theorem
$(C\ksAnd G)\ksAnd(V_R\ksAnd V_E)$ and theorems
$\ksD{V_R},\ksD{V_E}$ one obtains $V_R,V_E$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For the first projection assume $A\ksAnd B$ and $\neg A$. Under $A$
one gets $F$, and K gives $B\to F$. Discharge $A$ to obtain
$A\to\neg B$ and apply the negative conjunction to obtain $F$.
Discharge $\neg A$ and then the conjunction. For the second projection,
$\neg B$ gives $A\to\neg B$ directly by K, and the same argument applies.

For triple-negation elimination, assume $\neg\neg\neg A$ and $A$.
The latter gives $\neg\neg A$ by discharging an assumed $\neg A$;
the former then gives $F$. Discharge $A$ and the first assumption.

Now let $W=V_R\ksAnd V_E$. The right projection from the outer bundle
gives $\neg\neg W$. Since $W$ itself is a negation, triple-negation
elimination gives $W$. Project to $\neg\neg V_R,\neg\neg V_E$, and
apply their assumed stability. This avoids assuming unrestricted
classical conjunction elimination in the system being bootstrapped.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Quantified idempotence suffices}

Let $\mathcal C_{KS4}$ replace the single old $L$ row by K, S, and
\[
 \mathsf Q4(i,A)=Q_iA\to Q_iQ_iA,
\]
with every other rule and gated bundle unchanged. Thus it uses three
logical replacement rows but only the existing three independently
enumerated source parameters. Let $\mathcal C_{KSDQ}$ make the same
replacement using
\[
 \mathsf{QDN}(i,A)=\neg\neg Q_iA\to Q_iA
\]
instead of $\mathsf Q4$.

\begin{theorem}[Full numerical stability from either quantified repair]
\label{thm:ksquant}
Both $\mathcal C_{KS4}$ and $\mathcal C_{KSDQ}$ prove $\ksD n$ for every
natural-number formula code $n$. Consequently they prove every instance
of the original $L$ row, and contain the old theorem calculus
$\mathcal C_L$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
In $\mathcal C_{KS4}$ apply Lemma~\ref{lem:ksvstable} to
$A=Q_iB$ and to the axiom $Q_iB\to Q_iQ_iB$. Every quantified code is
therefore stable. In $\mathcal C_{KSDQ}$ this is an axiom directly.

Fix any atom $R(a,b)$ or $E(a,b)$ and choose an index $c$ different
from both $a,b$. Such an index exists in the unbounded natural-number
index set. Its gated bundle is a theorem. The two vacuity leaves
$V_R,V_E$ have quantified consequents, already proved stable;
Lemma~\ref{lem:ksimpstable} makes both leaves stable. Apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:ksbundle} to obtain the leaves as theorems. Then
Lemma~\ref{lem:ksvstable} gives stability of both atoms. The CP/GIC
leaves are not used in this proof except as unchanged components of
the actual bundle. Their presence causes no conjunction-extraction gap.

Complete the proof by strong induction on $n$. Code~0 is handled
separately: it is a theorem, and K with antecedent $\neg\neg0$ gives
$\neg\neg0\to0$. Do not recursively unfold $0=I(0,0)$.
For $n>0$, examine $n\bmod4$ using the inverse pairing operation.
Membership and equality tags have just been handled, and the universal
tag has unconditional quantified stability. If $n=I(x,y)>0$, then
$y<n$, so the induction hypothesis and Lemma~\ref{lem:ksimpstable}
give $\ksD n$. To see the strict inequality explicitly, if
$\pi(x,y)>0$ then $y\le\pi(x,y)<4\pi(x,y)=n$.
No appeal to a fictitious finite parse tree for code~0 is made.

Explosion and unrestricted stability imply Peirce. Under
$H=(A\to B)\to A$ and $\neg A$, an additional assumption $A$ yields
$F$, hence $B$ by explosion. Discharge $A$ to obtain $A\to B$;
$H$ then gives $A$, hence $F$. Discharge $\neg A$, use stability of
$A$, and discharge $H$.

Finally derive $L(a,b,c,u)$ from K/S/Peirce: assume
$H=(c\to u)\to a$ and $J=a\to c$. Under $c\to u$, these yield $c$.
Discharge to obtain $(c\to u)\to c$, use Peirce at $(c,u)$ to obtain
$c$, and use K to obtain $b\to c$. Discharge $J,H$.
Every old axiom leaf thus has a finite replacement derivation, while
the remaining inference rules and axioms are unchanged.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The reverse refutation comparison.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:ksquant} proves old-to-new theorem inclusion. We do not
claim an explicit old numerical derivation of every $\mathsf Q4$ instance
from the finite certificates in this artifact. Instead, the required
reverse \emph{refutation} comparison follows from the same native SIR
interpretation used by the old calibration.

Here are its added soundness obligations explicitly. Decode code~0 as a
true formula. Decode positive implication codes recursively, universal
codes as ordinary quantification in their numerical binder, and atomic
codes as membership and native equality. For $I(0,0)=0$, both the
chosen truth interpretation and its implication with itself are valid;
the interpretation is compatible up to logical equivalence, which is
all soundness needs. Native irreflexivity makes $F=R(0,0)$ false under
every assignment, including assignments changed by a binder~0.

The K and S rows are valid Boolean implication identities. For Q4,
fix a model, an assignment $s$, and an index $i$. If $Q_i A$ holds at
$s$, it holds at every assignment $s[i\mapsto d]$: the truth condition
is still ``for every $e$, $A$ holds at $s[i\mapsto e]$'' because the
inner occurrence of the same binder overwrites the outer assignment.
Thus $Q_iQ_iA$ holds. This verifies same-binder Q4 even with numerical
index collisions. For QDN, ordinary Boolean double-negation elimination
applies because the interpretation of $F$ is false at every assignment.
The unchanged old rows, unchanged gated CP/GIC bundle and inference
rules use the existing native soundness proof.

Therefore any new derivation of numerical~1 yields an SIR refutation.
Conversely an SIR refutation yields an old numerical refutation by the
existing old completeness/calibration theorem, and hence a new one by
Theorem~\ref{thm:ksquant}. This is an ordinary proof of
\[
 \mathcal C_{KS4}\vdash1\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \mathrm{SIR}\vdash\bot
 \ \Longleftrightarrow\ \mathcal C_{KSDQ}\vdash1.
\]
The native comparison with ZFC then transfers. This is a refutation
bridge, not a claim that the finite Python checker has reverified the
entire native set-theoretic interpretation or compiled a new machine.

\subsection{Global DNE gives exact equality of numerical calculi}

Let $\mathcal C_{KSD}$ replace $L$ by K, S, and the unrestricted schema
\[
 \mathsf D(A)=((A\to F)\to F)\to A.
\]
All three schemas can use the existing $a,b,c$ source fields. The final
independent read of $u$ is unnecessary, but the extra axiom rows and
changed command layout still have to be compiled and calibrated.

\begin{theorem}[Exact relative-basis equivalence]
\label{thm:ksdne}
For every numerical code $n$,
\[
 \mathcal C_L\vdash n\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \mathcal C_{KSD}\vdash n.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The new calculus proves explosion by Lemma~\ref{lem:ksefq}. The Peirce
argument at the end of Theorem~\ref{thm:ksquant}, now using the DNE
axiom directly, proves every Peirce instance. The ensuing K/S/Peirce
derivation proves every old $L$ instance. This gives old-to-new inclusion.

Conversely, the old $L$ schema derives K, S and Peirce by the existing
uniform implicational derivations. These are verified as explicit
substitution/MP proofs in the upstream file \texttt{CD254.v}; Source~95
already provides a small independent checker. No freeness of the
numerical implication operation is required. The old retained $\rho_6$
then gives explosion by the same finite argument. To derive DNE from
Peirce and explosion, assume $\neg\neg A$. Under $\neg A$ this gives
$F$, then $A$ by explosion. Discharge to obtain $(A\to F)\to A$.
Peirce at $(A,F)$ gives $A$; discharge the first assumption. Thus every
new axiom is an old theorem, giving the reverse inclusion. Replacing
axiom leaves in finite proof trees proves the assertion for every code.
\end{proof}

The fixed-bottom qualification matters. We have not shown that DNE
alone replaces Peirce in an arbitrary weaker propositional system.
The inherited quantified row is what supplies explosion. Source~71's
finite obstruction to a K/B/DNE shortcut does not conflict with
Theorem~\ref{thm:ksdne}: that shortcut has composition~B instead of~S.
The theorem here explicitly retains S.

\subsection{Relation to Source 110's six-valued obstruction}

Source~110 already proves that K/S alone do not recover the original
\emph{logical} calculus. Its finite matrix uses the Heyting algebra
$H=C_3\times C_2$, with bottom $(0,0)$, top $(2,1)$, componentwise
implication, and the modal operation
\[
 q(x,y)=
 \begin{cases}(1+y,y),&x=2,\\(0,y),&x<2.\end{cases}
\]
Quantification applies $q$ to the meet over a two-element individual
domain. Atomic values may be the fixed points $(0,0),(0,1),(2,1)$.
The exact retained seven logical rows, fresh atomic vacuity, MP, GEN,
and irreflexivity are valid. The checker deliberately excludes CP and
GIC, so it is not a countermodel to the complete altered set theory.

Take an off-diagonal membership atom with value $A=(0,1)$ and a fresh
quantifier. Then $\neg A=(2,0)$ and $P=Q\neg A=(1,0)$.
This is the nonstable quantified value that blocks the proposed
classical induction. The present repairs eliminate it directly:
\[
 Q\neg A=(1,0),\quad QQ\neg A=(0,0),
\]
so the Q4 instance at $\neg A$ is not designated. Likewise the QDN
instance at $P$ is not designated. In particular, the theorem above
identifies and repairs the obstruction's exact missing quantified
stability property. It does not characterize KS-only equiconsistency
of the full CP/GIC theory.

\subsection{What the finite certificates establish}

The accompanying \texttt{formalism\_proofs.py} generates and independently
replays twelve finite Hilbert DAGs. Their final forms use only explicit
instances of K, S, the listed old quantified rows, the selected repair
schema, MP, and theorem-only GEN. Conditional lemmas list their exact
global theorem premises. There is no primitive deduction-theorem rule
and no residual local assumption in the exported certificates.

The checked targets include explosion, seriality, positive vacuity
implying stability, implication stability, negative-product projections,
quantified stability from Q4, the full negative-bundle extraction and
atomic stability argument for both quantified repairs, DNE-to-Peirce,
Peirce-to-DNE, and DNE-to-original-L. The replay checks 2,015 proof nodes
across the twelve DAGs. This is a finite mathematical proof-certificate
check; it is not a Lean or Rocq kernel result. The unbounded numerical
induction, native SIR interpretation and proof-enumerator calibration
are the separately stated ordinary arguments.

For an executable frontend, axiom enumeration must cover arbitrary
numeric substitutions for K/S and the repair. The existing three-field
enumeration and Source~95's generic enlarged-stack selector argument
can be reused with its hypotheses checked: the new logical row count is
ten; the removed $u$ read changes the AX command codec; proof selection
uses the new stack width; and cofinal padding must still produce
arbitrarily large successful proof candidates. Pure axiom equivalence
alone does not establish these operational facts. Nor does it license
retaining decoder folds whose caller conditions a changed source may
violate.

\section{An equality-free model route with full observational equivalence}
\label{sec:ksequalityfree}

There is a concise model-theoretic simplification of the equality-free
lane. It is useful for separating the strength of the axiom system from
the costs of the chosen proof calculus. It does not justify deleting
native equality or a congruence row from the current numerical engine.

Let $T^-$ be ordinary classical first-order logic without an equality
symbol, with one binary relation $R$, the equality-free native CP axiom,
and the full GIC schema for all equality-free finite formulas with
arbitrary finite tuples of parameters. Retain irreflexivity if the
literal bottom $R(0,0)$ is to be used. Write
\[
 x\subseteq_R y\quad:\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \forall z(R(z,x)\to R(z,y)),
\]
\[
 \mathsf{Pow}_R(x,W)\quad:\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \forall z(z\subseteq_R x\to R(z,W)).
\]
Then the exact equality-free CP axiom is
\[
 \forall p\,\exists V\,\bigl[
 R(p,V)\land\forall x\bigl(R(x,V)\to
 [x\subseteq_R V\land\exists W(R(W,V)\land\mathsf{Pow}_R(x,W))]\bigr)\bigr].
\]
Only its consequence that some represented set has a member is needed
for the singleton lemma below. The full strength of CP is retained for
the later native-theory comparison.

The GIC schema for a matrix $\varphi(x,y,\vec p)$ is
\begin{align*}
 \forall B\,\exists C\,\bigl[&
 \forall x\bigl(R(x,B)\to
   \forall y(\varphi(x,y,\vec p)\to
    \exists y'(\varphi(x,y',\vec p)\land R(y',C)))\bigr)\\
 &\land\forall y(R(y,C)\to\exists x\,\varphi(x,y,\vec p))\bigr],
\end{align*}
with universal closure in the parameters. This is the full schema,
not a restriction to fixed parameter slots or a single displayed matrix.

Define the equality-free formula
\[
 a\approx b\quad:\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \forall z\bigl[(R(a,z)\leftrightarrow R(b,z))\land
                (R(z,a)\leftrightarrow R(z,b))\bigr].
\]
It compares both outgoing and incoming membership observations.

\begin{theorem}[Full observational quotient]
\label{thm:kstwin}
Every nonempty set model $M$ of $T^-$ has a quotient set model of the
native equality-bearing CP/GIC theory, and of SIR when irreflexivity
is retained. Conversely, the relational reduct of a model of SIR
satisfies $T^-$ with irreflexivity. Thus these presentations have the
same consistency strength under ordinary first-order soundness and
completeness.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Pure classical predicate logic proves that $\approx$ is an equivalence
relation: each conjunct is a pointwise biconditional, so reflexivity,
symmetry and transitivity hold. It also proves congruence in both
coordinates. If $a\approx a'$ and $b\approx b'$, the outgoing
observation at $b$ gives $R(a,b)\leftrightarrow R(a',b)$, and the
incoming observation at $a'$ gives
$R(a',b)\leftrightarrow R(a',b')$.

In the ambient ZFC universe form the set quotient $M/{\approx}$ and
define $[a]\,\bar R\,[b]$ iff $R(a,b)$ in $M$. The congruence just
proved makes the relation well-defined. Interpret native equality by
identity of quotient classes.

For every formula $\psi$ of the equality-bearing relational language,
form $\psi^-$ by replacing each equality atom $x=y$ by $x\approx y$,
leaving membership unchanged, and commuting with Boolean connectives
and quantifiers. Rename the bound variable $z$ in the definition of
$\approx$ when necessary. Ordinary structural induction gives
\[
 M/{\approx}\models\psi([\vec a])
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad M\models\psi^-(\vec a).
\]
The atomic equality case is the definition of the quotient, membership
is its relation definition, and the quantifier cases use the surjective
projection from $M$ to its quotient. Formula induction also gives
invariance under changing representatives.

CP contains no equality atom, so the truth lemma transfers it directly.
For every equality-bearing GIC matrix $\psi$, the translated matrix
$\psi^-$ is one of the equality-free GIC instances assumed in $T^-$.
Applying the truth lemma transfers that whole GIC instance. The native
equality axioms hold because equality is actual identity; irreflexivity,
when assumed, transfers directly as well. The converse follows by
forgetting equality, since every equality-free GIC instance was already
an instance of the original full schema.

These are set-sized structures, so the quotient, satisfaction relation
and formula translation are ordinary ZFC objects. Soundness and Henkin
completeness convert model existence into the consistency comparison.
No transitive model, external well-foundedness assumption or global
truth predicate for the universe is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[One GIC instance yields full-twin singletons]
For every $a$, $T^-$ proves that some $B$ has exactly the members
observationally equivalent to $a$:
\[
 \forall y\,[R(y,B)\leftrightarrow y\approx a].
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use CP to obtain a represented set $C$ with at least one member $x$.
Apply GIC with seed $C$ and matrix $\varphi(x,y;a)=(y\approx a)$,
which is independent of the first argument. Since $a\approx a$, the
coverage clause gives $w$ with $w\approx a$ and $R(w,B)$. Outgoing
congruence gives $R(a,B)$. The purity clause gives
$R(y,B)\to\exists x(y\approx a)$, hence $R(y,B)\to y\approx a$.
Conversely $y\approx a$ and $R(a,B)$ give $R(y,B)$ by outgoing
congruence. Every use of the matrix is an allowed equality-free GIC
instance with the parameter $a$.
\end{proof}

Define the one-way outgoing profile
\[
 a\preceq b\quad:\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \forall z(R(a,z)\to R(b,z)).
\]
The twin-singleton lemma gives $a\preceq b\Longleftrightarrow a\approx b$:
the reverse direction is immediate, while for the forward direction
test $\preceq$ at the full-twin singleton of $a$. Its membership of $a$
forces membership of $b$, hence $b\approx a$ and symmetry finishes.

This resembles the raw-PSC outgoing-profile/twin-singleton principle
already documented in ProveIt's \texttt{outgoing\_profile.md}. No novelty
claim is made for identity elimination or the profile argument as a
general principle. The useful specific point is that the full
equality-free CP/GIC lane has a direct full-observation quotient proof:
it need not first reconstruct a bespoke extensional bisimulation
equality merely to regain native equality.

The scope restriction is substantial. Ordinary first-order logic here
already supplies complete substitution and specialization. Removing
the numerical native-equality constructor, removing $\rho_3$, or using
a fixed-slot GIC fragment changes that proof calculus and requires a
new soundness/completeness bridge. In particular Source~108's ordinary
GIC derivation of the second membership congruence cannot be spliced
into the current numerical engine by silently using the very
specialization theorem whose existing proof depends on that congruence.
The model theorem proves consistency strength of the full alternative
presentation, not an uncounted state reduction in the present compiler.
