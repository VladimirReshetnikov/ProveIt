4. Exact source theory of the 254/253 machines

The definition is in ZF/m254/coq/settheory/S_def.v.  The relational signature
has membership and equality, no function symbols.  S0 has the four equality
axioms, the following container sentence CP, and all universally closed
instances of the global-image collection scheme GIC.  SIR adds irreflexivity.

Let x subseteq y abbreviate forall z (z in x -> z in y).  Then:

  CP:
    forall p exists V [p in V and
      forall x in V (x subseteq V and
        exists W in V forall z (z subseteq x -> z in W))].

  GIC_phi:
    forall B exists C [
      forall a in B forall y
        (phi(a,y) -> exists y' (phi(a,y') and y' in C))
      and forall y in C exists x phi(x,y)].

  IR:
    forall x not (x in x).

The universal closures include arbitrary extra parameters of phi.  SIR does
not assume extensionality, Foundation, or Choice.  It is a scheme theory, not
a claim of finitely many ordinary first-order axioms.  The four equality
axioms are object-theory axioms for a relational equality predicate.

S_main.v contains both S_equicons and S0_equicons, relating refutations of SIR
and S0 to refutations of closed ZF.  Thus IR is dispensable for the abstract
equiconsistency theorem, while it remains relevant to the particular compact
formula coding used by the machine.

5. Coding cautions and optimization interface

The formula encoding is stated in Codes254.v and Wff254.v.  It uses the dual
Cantor pairing P(x,y)=(x+y)(x+y+1)/2+y and 4*P(x,y)+tag, with tags for
implication, membership, universal quantification, and equality.  The special
target code 1 is raw self-membership Rel(0,0), interpreted as falsity using IR.
Changing that convention without modifying the soundness argument is invalid.

The bundle construction is given in Bundle254.v.  Its freshness gate requires
c different from a and b.  Dropping that gate or treating raw self-membership
as pure logical falsity would alter the proof obligations, even if a finite
test suite continued to pass.

The runtime proof checks a complete prefix-code instruction tree.  The program
interface uses nine source-order registers, with nextproof initially 2.  The
public253 boot theorem reaches register program step 330 after 312,975 literal
TM moves, with header distance 40.  Thus a future runtime reduction may retain
the entire program/theory proof and replace only the table-specific simulation
and finite-prefix bridge, provided their hypotheses still hold.  This is a
concrete proof-preserving engineering interface, not a state-count estimate.

6. Foundation-inclusive ZFC endpoint

ZF.Std_iface.ZFc is closed ZF without Foundation.  The public endpoint named
zf253_halts_iff_ZFC is literally the theory ZFc plus Choice.  However,
ZFC.Z_main.ZFCReg_equicons already proves finite-refutation equivalence between
((ZFc plus Foundation) plus Choice) and ZFc.  The Foundation sentence is
RegCon.MinZF.ax_reg', not a symbol defined in the vendored minZF.v file.

FullZFC253.v composes those two existing equivalences.  It states:

  Public253_halts <-> Refutable_full_ZFC
  (~Public253_halts) <-> Consistent_full_ZFC

Here Consistent_full_ZFC is by definition the absence of a finite classical
natural-deduction refutation of the exact object theory including Foundation
and Choice.  Negating a biconditional in this manner is constructive; the
wrapper adds no classical meta-axiom.  The existing derivation transports do
the mathematical work.

Wrapper status: source-reviewed, not separately kernel rebuilt.  The companion
check_wrapper.py verifies its bytes, two assumption probes, 158 imported Coq
sources, and 8 build/provenance artifacts.  It also prints build commands.
It explicitly prints KERNEL NOT RUN.  upstream_source_manifest.json records
the necessary source hashes and CI pins.

7. External equivalence and the requested internal ZFC statement

The Coq propositions relate finite object-theory refutations to the actual TM
semantics.  A closed Coq proof term is not, by itself, an emitted first-order
ZFC derivation of the arithmetized assertion.  The manuscript should separately
describe why the finite syntax, machine simulation, and interpretation
arguments can be formalized in ZFC, or provide a proof-code translation.
The target assertion is ZFC proves (Nonhalts(M) iff Con(ZFC)), with a fixed
recursive proof predicate.  Neither the upstream proof nor this wrapper is a
claim to prove Con(ZFC).

For incompleteness consequences, mere consistency suffices to exclude a ZFC
proof of nonhalting once the required internal implication to Con(ZFC) is
established.  Both-sided independence additionally needs an appropriate
soundness hypothesis to exclude a false Sigma-1 halting assertion; in general,
consistency alone does not supply Sigma-1 soundness.  Avoid silently treating
these two hypotheses as interchangeable.

8. Historical comparators and exclusions

The 7910-state Yedidia-Aaronson construction is historically foundational but
its search uses a Friedman statement of stronger consistency strength.  It
must not appear in a table headed solely by exact Con(ZFC)-equivalent machines
without this qualification.  The 2016 preprint at arXiv:1605.04343 has 7910;
the older author-hosted PDF busybeaver.pdf still has 7918, so do not conflate
versions.

O'Rear's zf2.nql is an explicit ZF proof enumerator, and is the relevant
lineage for the 1919/748 and later Wade-style machines.  Its source comments
document replacing expensive syntactic side conditions by logical schemes.
Upstream's merged 335-state machine has the literal end-to-end ZF/regularity/
Choice interfaces, and the 254 machine is the smaller accepted baseline.

The repository also contains a 238-state PA-consistency result.  It does not
meet the ZFC-consistency target.  Public PR19's 296-state Grothendieck-universe
consistency machine concerns a stronger theory and likewise is not a smaller
same-target comparator.
