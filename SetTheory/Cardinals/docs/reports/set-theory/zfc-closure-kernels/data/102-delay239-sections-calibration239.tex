\section{Consistency calibration and the precise theorem}
\label{sec:calibration239}

Let $M_{239}$ be the literal 239-working-state machine specified in this
report, started in state $0$ at position $0$ on the all-zero, two-way infinite
tape. Let $H_{239}$ be its arithmetical halting sentence. Fix the recursively
presented first-order ZFC proof calculus used by the inherited calibration,
and write
\[
 \operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})\;:=\;
 \neg\exists p\,\operatorname{Proof}_{\mathrm{ZFC}}(p,\bot).
\]
Proofs here are finite syntactic objects; ordinary effective changes of
proof calculus give the usual equivalent consistency statement.

\paragraph{Theorem.}
The concrete construction and the inherited logical calibration give an
ordinary mathematical proof, formalizable in ZFC, of
\[
 \mathrm{ZFC}\ \vdash\
 \bigl(\neg H_{239}\ \longleftrightarrow\
             \operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})\bigr).
\]
The proof does not assume $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$.
This report does not claim a newly checked Lean theorem for this exact
binary machine or an internal PA proof of the displayed equivalence.

\paragraph{The inherited logical component.}
The register enumerator is calibrated to the non-extensional set theory
SIR: the container principle CP, schema GIC with its full
support clause, and irreflexivity IR. The published proof relates refutations
of this theory to ZF and ZFC refutations. Its coded-calculus calibration
handles native equality, variable freshness, and the use of the atom
$v_0\in v_0$ as relative falsity; irreflexivity is essential to that last
identification. The source rotations preserve the complete register-program
halting behavior. The archived theorem
\texttt{CalibratedRotation240.halts\_iff\_zfc\_refutation} consequently
identifies halting of the exact rotated source at the logical seed with
existence of a ZFC refutation.\cite{m254,source240,cal240}

\paragraph{The concrete machine component.}
The source list used here has exactly 6,455 leaves and is pinned by SHA-256
\texttt{74b1e7f7}\ldots\texttt{41d5a51d}. This packet freshly reconstructs
all 203 source-DAG rows and all 6,455 ordered leaves from the pinned Lean
definitions used by the calibration. The source hashes and exact DAG/leaf
identity are recorded in \texttt{evidence/source\_joins.json}. Thus the
logical identity of the simulated program is fixed independently of the
number of successful runtime cases.\cite{source240}

For the new literal table, all 8,386 guarded instruction cases establish
whole-tape simulation for arbitrary natural primary-register values,
arbitrary admissible gap, and an arbitrary nonempty finite spare-block list. Zero
and positive decrement cases exhaust the natural numbers. The symbolic
rules are literal transitions, induction on unary run length, and induction
on finite lists using the independently derived one-block traversal rules.
Every certified instruction block takes a positive finite number of actual
steps, with minimum constant term 13. Induction on source execution therefore
preserves finite halting. If the source continues indefinitely, concatenating
these positive blocks supplies an unbounded concrete execution and excludes
premature concrete halting. This gives both directions of the halting
comparison.

The exact blank bootstrap reaches the required dispatcher frame after
409,835 transitions. Its logical registers are
\[
 (3,0,0,0,3,0,0,0,0),
\]
its program counter is empty, and its auxiliary storage consists of 167
spare blocks of value $1$. The empty counter uses the established zero-padding
decoder convention. Completeness of the finite decoder tree covers this
initial case and every successor instruction. The spare blocks are auxiliary
frame data; all source register operands lie in $\{0,\ldots,8\}$. Their
presence therefore does not replace the logical seed by a different
proof-search input. The logical-to-physical register permutation, head,
marker, header, and zero exterior are included in the frame identification.
The inherited frame and runtime conventions are documented separately.
\cite{tree240,runtime240}

\paragraph{Internalization and verification status.}
The composition is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 H_{239}&\ \longleftrightarrow\
 \operatorname{Halts}(\text{rotated source},\text{logical seed})\\
 &\ \longleftrightarrow\
 \exists p\,\operatorname{Proof}_{\mathrm{ZFC}}(p,\bot).
 \end{aligned}
\]
Each finite table calculation can be represented by a finite computation
sequence in ZFC. The uniform simulation arguments use natural-number and
finite-list induction, and the fixed syntactic source calibration supplies
the second equivalence. Negating the two equivalent existential statements
gives the theorem. This internalizes the particular proof constructions;
it does not invoke a global soundness or reflection principle for Lean or
for the Python checker. The open exact-table Lean coverage/composition
endpoint is a separate mechanization task. The stronger claim that PA itself
proves this complete equivalence remains a separate obligation as well.

\paragraph{Independence assumptions.}
In the intended standard interpretation, the construction halts exactly
when a finite ZFC refutation exists. Under consistency of ZFC, it therefore
does not halt. G\"odel's second incompleteness theorem then rules out a ZFC
proof of its nonhalting, using the displayed internal equivalence. Bare
consistency alone does not justify the further claim that ZFC cannot prove
its halting: that assertion is equivalent here to excluding a ZFC proof of
its own inconsistency. Two-sided independence follows, for example, from
arithmetical $\Sigma_1$-soundness of ZFC. The more targeted sufficient
assumption $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}+\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}))$
also suffices. No unconditional nonhalting or absolute consistency is claimed.
