\section{Merging equality propagation and the canonical helper}
\label{sec:merged-equality}

The successful source reduction combines two obligations that the
224-state construction emitted separately.  The original equality row was
\[
 \rho_7(a,b,c):\qquad
 d_{ca}\longrightarrow(d_{cb}\longrightarrow d_{ab}),
 \qquad d_{xy}:=\operatorname{Eq}(x,y),
\]
and the gated set-theory bundle contained the additional helper
\[
 H(a,b):\qquad d_{ab}\longrightarrow Q_{\kappa(a,b)}d_{ab}.
\]
Here $Q_iA$ abbreviates $\operatorname{All}(i,A)$ and
\[
 \kappa(a,b)=\operatorname{RelCode}(a,b)
 =4\left(\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b\right)+1.
\]
This natural number exceeds both $a$ and $b$, so it is a safe binder for
the equality atom.  The replacement is the single logical row
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 G(a,b,c):\qquad
 d_{ca}\longrightarrow
       \bigl(d_{cb}\longrightarrow Q_{\kappa(a,b)}d_{ab}\bigr).
 \quad}                                                        \tag{G}
\]
The old helper is then removed from the set-theory bundle.  All remaining
logical rows, including $\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_6$ and $\rho_8$, are retained.

This change has a stronger justification than a new consistency
interpretation: the old and new theories prove exactly the same native
formula codes.  The proof below reconstructs the literal old $\rho_7$
row and the literal old helper from $G$, and derives $G$ in the reverse
direction.  No change to the previous equality interpretation or to the
native K-matrix/code adapter is required.

\subsection{Propositional and quantifier prerequisites}

Use the exact designated falsehood $F=1=\operatorname{Rel}(0,0)$, with
$\neg A=(A\to F)$ and $\exists_iA=\neg Q_i\neg A$.
The classical propositional bridge is unchanged.  Its precise dependency
is $\rho_8$ together with $\rho_6$: the condensed-detachment proofs in
\texttt{CD230.v} derive K, S and Peirce from $\rho_8$; the theorem
\texttt{thm\_F} in \texttt{Comp\_Prop230.v} derives $F\to A$ from K and
$\rho_6(0,A)$; double-negation elimination follows.  Thus the
propositional tautologies used below do not depend on $G$, $\rho_7$,
equality vacuity, or a set-theory axiom.  In particular, no assumption that
the raw label 0 in $F$ is syntactically closed is made.

From a closed theorem $A\to B$, closed generalization and $\rho_2$ give
quantified monotonicity
\[
 Q_iA\longrightarrow Q_iB.
\]
Classical contraposition gives its existential counterpart
$\exists_iA\to\exists_iB$.  Every use of generalization in this section
is on a closed theorem; there is no generalization of an open hypothesis.
Here closed means that the derivation has no open assumptions; the formula
may still contain free variable labels.

Seriality is also available without equality reflexivity:
\[
 Q_iA\longrightarrow\exists_iA.                                \tag{S}
\]
Indeed $\rho_1(i,i)$ gives $\exists_i d_{ii}$.  Generalize the tautology
$A\to(d_{ii}\to A)$, apply $\rho_2$, and use the existential consequence
of $\rho_2$ to obtain
$Q_i(d_{ii}\to A)\to(\exists_i d_{ii}\to\exists_iA)$.
Composing these closed theorems proves (S).
Applying it to $Q_iA$ and then applying $\rho_6(i,A)$ gives
\[
 Q_iQ_iA\longrightarrow\exists_iQ_iA\longrightarrow A.          \tag{D}
\]
The distinction between one and two quantifiers matters: (D) is uniform
in every native formula code, while a one-quantifier elimination principle
is not assumed.

\subsection{Recovering reflexivity and the deleted helper}

Fix a label $x$, and put $z=\kappa(x,x)$ and $e=d_{xx}$.  The instance
$G(x,x,z)$ says
\[
 d_{zx}\longrightarrow(d_{zx}\longrightarrow Q_ze),
\]
and propositional contraction therefore gives
$d_{zx}\to Q_ze$.  Existential monotonicity and $\rho_1(z,x)$ yield
$\exists_zQ_ze$.  Finally $\rho_6(z,e)$ yields
\[
 d_{xx}.                                                       \tag{R}
\]
No equality reflexivity or helper axiom was used to establish (R).

Set the third parameter of $G$ to $a$.  Its first antecedent is now the
theorem $d_{aa}$, so (R) and $G(a,b,a)$ give
\[
 d_{ab}\longrightarrow Q_{\kappa(a,b)}d_{ab}.                   \tag{H}
\]
This is exactly the deleted helper, including its original native binder
and all diagonal instances.

\subsection{Recovering the literal original equality row}

Let $e=d_{ab}$ and $i=\kappa(a,b)$.  Generalize the closed theorem (H)
at $i$, and distribute the quantifier with $\rho_2$:
\[
 Q_ie\longrightarrow Q_iQ_ie.
\]
Compose this implication with (D).  This proves the particular
one-quantifier elimination theorem
\[
 Q_{\kappa(a,b)}d_{ab}\longrightarrow d_{ab}.                   \tag{E}
\]
Composing the final consequent of $G(a,b,c)$ with (E) gives
\[
 d_{ca}\longrightarrow(d_{cb}\longrightarrow d_{ab}),
\]
which is the literal original $\rho_7(a,b,c)$ formula, with no equality
translation.  Primitive symmetry is an immediate supplementary
consequence: apply this recovered row with parameters $(b,a,a)$ and use
(R) to conclude $d_{ab}\to d_{ba}$.

Conversely, the old $\rho_7(a,b,c)$ followed by the old helper $H(a,b)$
gives $G(a,b,c)$ by propositional composition.  Thus the replacement
compresses the generator without weakening the native deductive theory.

\subsection{Exact bundle transformation and axiom coverage}

Let $K_{abc}$ denote the unchanged fused K core, and let
\[
 V_R(a,b,c)=
   \operatorname{Rel}(a,b)\longrightarrow
       Q_c\operatorname{Rel}(a,b).
\]
The original gate remains $c\notin\{a,b\}$.
The exact old negative bundle was
\[
 B_{\rm old}(a,b,c)=
  \neg\bigl(K_{abc}\to(V_R(a,b,c)\to\neg H(a,b))\bigr).
\]
The new bundle is the two-component negative conjunction
\[
 B_{\rm new}(a,b,c)=
  \neg\bigl(K_{abc}\to\neg V_R(a,b,c)\bigr).
\]
The retained classical bridge proves all three exact transformations
\[
\begin{aligned}
 B_{\rm old}&\longrightarrow B_{\rm new},\\
 B_{\rm old}&\longrightarrow H,\\
 B_{\rm new}&\longrightarrow(H\longrightarrow B_{\rm old}).
\end{aligned}
\]
For every ordered pair $(a,b)$, a fresh gate parameter exists, for example
$c=\max(a,b)+1$.  Consequently the old bundled theory supplies every
helper needed to derive the independent new $G$ row.  Conversely the new
$G$ row supplies (H) for every pair, so every old bundle is recovered from
the corresponding new bundle.  These extractions use only the unchanged
propositional bridge and the stated gate; they do not presuppose a K
theorem or an equality interpretation.

The independent $G$ row must cover all numerical triples, as the replaced
$\rho_7$ row did.  The gated bundle must cover the same successful triples
as before.  These are explicit source-generator obligations and should be
checked together with the emitted formula AST; formula algebra alone is
not a proof of runtime row coverage.

\subsection{Deductive equivalence and inherited ZFC calibration}

Write $\operatorname{Thm}_{224}(n)$ for derivability of native formula
code $n$ from the old source theory, and
$\operatorname{Thm}_{G}(n)$ for derivability from the modified source.
Replace each changed axiom leaf by the closed finite derivation above and
leave MP and closed GEN unchanged.  Induction on a derivation gives
\[
 \boxed{\qquad
 \operatorname{Thm}_{224}(n)
   \ \longleftrightarrow\ \operatorname{Thm}_{G}(n)
 \quad\text{for every native code }n.
 \qquad}
\]
The transformation is on literal codes.  It therefore preserves the
distinguished refutation target $n=1$ and inherits the old ordinary
consistency equivalence with ZFC.  In particular, the existing Source116
membership-defined equality interpretation and the K private-label/code
coupling proof are reused without modification: this construction first
returns to their exact calibrated native source theory.

The proof substitutions, fresh gate selection, and induction on finite
derivations are elementary arithmetical constructions formalizable in
ZFC.  This establishes the new source-theory obligation.  A theorem about
the changed binary Turing machine still also requires its own exact
source-compiler and transition-table simulation obligations; it does not
follow merely from a bounded run or from the state count.

\subsection{Finite proof certificates}

The accompanying
\texttt{merged\_euclidean\_helper\_certificate.py} exports closed proof
DAGs and checks every axiom leaf, every MP edge, and closedness at each GEN
node.  It then reloads the exported JSON and checks it again.  It uses
only Python's standard library and the bundled proof representation in
\texttt{combined\_helper\_certificate.py}.

The roots include reflexivity, the deleted helper, equality-box
elimination, literal $\rho_7$, symmetry, the reverse derivation of $G$,
and all three exact negative-bundle transformations.  The numerical cases
include label 0, repeated endpoint labels, and large labels.  The checker
rejects old-helper or old-$\rho_7$ leaves in all forward-direction roots,
and rejects $G$ leaves in the reverse-direction roots, so the claimed
interderivation cannot be certified by a circular premise.

The combined artifact has 315 proof nodes and 290 distinct formulas.  Its
propositional \texttt{TAUT} leaves are independently truth-table checked
and abbreviate the unchanged $\rho_8/\rho_6$ classical bridge described
above.  These finite instances accompany the uniform mathematical proof;
they are not represented as a new Rocq or Lean endpoint for the changed
machine table.
