\section{A complete compilation of the shorter-zero-run source}
The reassociated source graph \texttt{rotation\_zero\_candidate\_24.json}
contains 202 distinct pair subtrees, 6,460 instruction occurrences, and 8,391
guarded instruction cases. Its maximum instruction-word length is 36 and
its maximum zero run is six. The separate source certificate checks that
every instruction and register argument is preserved, and that the successor

We compiled this graph into a \emph{250-state runtime}. This is a verified
mechanism result; it does not meet the requested fewer-than-246 threshold,
and its exact blank-start prefix has now also been verified separately.

191 physical decoder states and 55 support states. Its twelve savings depend
on tape context and borrowed transition rows. For the new graph, matching
unchanged subtrees and then matching the intervals of instruction occurrences
assigns 182 pair classes to 173 old states. Twenty new inner pairs require
states, while only eighteen old decoder states become available. Assigning
these twenty pairs therefore gives 193 decoder states, nine shared-pair
savings, and a preliminary total of 248 states.
