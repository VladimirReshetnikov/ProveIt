\section{A separate 662-bit equational packet}
\label{sec:e662}

This observation concerns the E663 companion of the 235-state delivery,
not the K230 source or any of the new K-based transition tables.  Let
$E$ denote its unchanged seven equations over its nine-operation
signature.  Its complete ground query is $Q(d)=d$.
Append a fresh nullary operation $e$, unused by every equation, and
replace the query by $e=d$.  Then
\[
          E\vdash Q(d)=d
          \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
          E\vdash_{\Sigma\cup\{e\}} e=d.
\]
Here the superscripted signature indicates that derivations may use
terms containing the additional constant; no equation about it has
been added.

For the forward implication, two of the inherited equations are
\[
 V(P(x,s),d)=x,
 \qquad V(P(x,s),Q(j))=V(s,j).
\]
Assuming $Q(d)=d$, substitute $s=j=d$ to obtain
\[
 x=V(P(x,d),d)=V(P(x,d),Q(d))=V(d,d).
\]
This holds for both $x=e$ and $x=d$, proving $e=d$ by symmetry and
transitivity.  Conversely, uniformly replace every occurrence of
the fresh constant $e$ in a finite derivation of $e=d$ by the
old-signature ground term $Q(d)$.  Because $e$ occurs in no axiom,
this remains a valid equational derivation and proves $Q(d)=d$.
Thus the argument is an explicit finite proof transformation; it
does not depend on a bounded algebra search.

The inherited codec reserves four variable codes and uses four-bit
operation/variable tags.  Increasing the signature from nine to ten
operations keeps the total of fourteen tags within that width.
The header gains three arity bits, changing from 43 to 46 bits.
The seven equations retain exactly the same 150 term nodes and
608 serialized bits, including their framing.  The two endpoints
shrink from three nodes to two, changing from 12 to 8 bits.  The
total is consequently
\[
                    46+608+8=662\ \text{bits}.
\]
The generator reconstructs the inherited 663-bit packet and checks
its recorded digest before modifying it.  It independently parses
the new packet, verifies the exact equation-bit identity, and rejects
all 662 strict prefixes and three malformed controls.  The packed
file uses two trailing transport-padding bits, which are excluded
by its accompanying bit-length record.  The SHA-256 of the exact
662 ASCII bit characters, excluding a newline, is
\begin{center}\scriptsize\ttfamily
d51acfb76b7805a994fd022ccb02ac7f58f6b9c57fc977b353c077edde8dd26f.
\end{center}

The ordinary raw-EPSC/ZFC calibration of E663 remains inherited;
this work proves only the displayed conservative query change and
its exact codec cost.  It supplies no binary equational interpreter,
blank-tape writer, smaller Turing-machine state count, or new proof
assistant endpoint.  Appending an operation can change decoder cost
even when opcode width is unchanged.  The older fixed-signature
query optimum is not contradicted: the signature here is different.
Attribution and the complete baseline AST are retained beside the
standalone Python builder.
