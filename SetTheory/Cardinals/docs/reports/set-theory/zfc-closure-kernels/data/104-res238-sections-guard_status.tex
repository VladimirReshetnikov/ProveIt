\subsection{Fresh selector guard audit}
The separate full guard replay checks the exact 409,691-step boundary startup and every visit to state 169 in all 8,386 unbounded instruction cases. At a positive read, the right-facing 1-run is required to have a nonnegative affine length with constant term at least two; this implies the next physical cell is also 1 for every represented valuation. The checker also verifies that no accelerated rule can hide a visit: state 169 has no applicable unary self-loop, none of the four list schemas enters or traverses 169, and their empty-list bases take no steps. The source-successor normalization and positive complete instruction blocks then extend the property to the entire blank run. The final receipt and exact event counts are recorded in \file{erasure_audit/boundary_guard.json}.

The fresh guard replay passed all 8,386 cases with 2,247,513 symbolic rules. There were 96 symbolic state-169 visits, including 32 positive reads, all at increment-register-4 sites. Their right-hand 1-run lengths are $G+K$, where $G\geq0$ and $23\leq K\leq59$. The boot has 18 state-169 visits, six reading 1, each checked literally. The independent block-aligned boot checker also compares the entire old and new tapes at all 409,685 matching checkpoints. These facts close the guard and compatibility premises for the actual changed source.
