Framework research for Vladimir Reshetnikov / ProveIt, 18 October 2026.

The reference machine, register-program semantics, tape-frame convention,
literal fixtures, and symbolic induction checker are inherited from the
zfc245-proof-20261018 contribution and its attributed Jeff Ketchersid,
Andrew James Wade, and ProveIt sources. The external reference package is a
required, explicitly identified dependency for reproduction.

pair_merge_search.cpp and one_action_search.cpp are inherited from the
ProveIt incoming zfc-246-frontier runtime experiments. The shared bit-tape
interpreter is also present in the baseline's
code/state_reduction/resource_merge_search.cpp. The flexible resource and
contextual/joint searches here extend that interpreter and search family.
No claim is made that the one-action search method itself is new.

New work in this component includes the scanner contraction audit on the
exact 245-state baseline, independently replayed 818324-step experimental
bootstrap, parametric wrong-next-frame family, grammar-ancestry mapping,
contextual replacement and bounded joint search, and portable verification.
