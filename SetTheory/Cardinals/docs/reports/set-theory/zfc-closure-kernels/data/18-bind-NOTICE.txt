ATTRIBUTION AND SCOPE

This report and its new proof/checker artifacts were prepared for Vladimir
Reshetnikov's research request. The new contributions and limits are stated
in report.tex and report.pdf.

Prior work included for comparison:

- Mira-Cult designed the original 254-state machine and economical C/G
  calculus; Jeff supplied the published verification.
- Mira-emmy's upstream 253-state reduction is the source of
  machines/benchmark-zfc-253.tm. Its exact source pin and published build
  evidence are recorded in provenance/source-audit.json.
- Andrew James Wade supplied the public compiler framework used by the
  compiler controls. His original MIT No Attribution notice is preserved.
- Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt contributors supplied the repository,
  maintained source, formal developments, and incoming research reports.
- The original- and dual-rho6 248-state tables are prior incoming work from
  docs/incoming/zfc_small_machines_20261016.zip, not new results of this report.
- The 269-state exact-word grammar result and the independent certificate
  engines are attributed prior artifacts. The compiler directory includes
  their additional source and license-boundary notices.

The new text does not claim historical priority for counterinterpretations,
locally nameless syntax, proof certificates, or other established methods.
It claims the specific obstruction, checked binding development, concrete
replacement proposal, and source/compiler audit described in the report.

Preserved third-party notices are in licenses/ and
compiler/independent_verifier/. Third-party licenses are not altered.
No complete third-party paper, toolchain, or external repository is bundled.
