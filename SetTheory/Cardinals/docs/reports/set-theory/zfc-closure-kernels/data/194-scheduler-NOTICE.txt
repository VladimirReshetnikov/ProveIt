PROVENANCE AND SCOPE
====================

The original logic, numerical coding, enumerators, compiler contracts and
production interfaces are taken from the pinned ProveIt sources named in the
report and source_provenance.json files. Original source headers are retained.
LICENSE.ProveIt reproduces the repository's MIT No Attribution notice; it does
not override any separate upstream source qualification.

THIRD_PARTY.upstream.md and LICENSES/ reproduce the repository's upstream notices
and license-scope qualifications. Their description of the larger verification
development does not imply this packet redistributes every named upstream
library. Rocq/Coq, Lean and their standard libraries are external dependencies
and are not distributed here.

The six-goal implication graph is credited to the existing ProveIt commits
3a7a61ee973a32777f2c5e07b8a304e6f4418b37 and
49e99c5b7cc6c2e164331961de28bad3904361fd. The generic Rocq implication interface
builds on the shared-proof-core proposal. Scheduler extraction, rank arguments
and arbitrary-state completeness are credited to the earlier further-interface
and checked-transport work. This packet's contribution is the checked minimal
scheduler contract, current-source adapters, shared graph integration across
languages, preserved public interfaces, producer synchronization, counterexample,
and source-bound validation artifacts.

New text, scripts and proof-engineering additions in this packet are intended
for incorporation under ProveIt's MIT No Attribution license, subject to the
retained qualifications for inherited material. No additional restriction is
asserted over the new material.
