\section{Independent audit of the K223 logical change}

\noindent\textbf{Verdict.} The merged equality/helper argument is sound.
It proves equality of the old and new sets of derivable native formula
codes, not merely an external equiconsistency.  I found no circular use
of deleted equality axioms, illicit generalization, missing zero-label
case, or mismatch between the necessary G family and the stated emitter.
This audit concerns the logical change and its source interface; the
binary runtime and state folds have separate certificates.

Write $e_{ab}=\operatorname{Eq}(a,b)$,
$\kappa(a,b)=4P(a,b)+1$, and $Q_iA=\operatorname{All}(i,A)$.
The unconditional replacement row is
\[
 G(a,b,c)=e_{ca}\to(e_{cb}\to Q_{\kappa(a,b)}e_{ab}).
\]
The following dependency order is valid for all natural parameters.

First, the unchanged Lukasiewicz row gives K, S and Peirce.  The actual
pinned \texttt{CD230.v} derivations use only that row and MP.  The proof
of \texttt{Comp\_Prop230.thm\_F} uses K and retained $\rho_6(0,A)$ to
derive $F\to A$, for $F=\operatorname{Rel}(0,0)$.  Classical tautologies
with that relative falsity are therefore available before any equality
bootstrap.  Neither equality vacuity nor G is needed for this bridge.

Second, closed GEN and $\rho_2$ give universal and existential
monotonicity.  From $\rho_1(i,i)=\exists_i e_{ii}$ and the tautology
$A\to(e_{ii}\to A)$ one obtains
\[
 Q_iA\to\exists_iA,
 \qquad Q_iQ_iA\to\exists_iQ_iA\to A.
\]
The second implication ends with $\rho_6(i,A)$.  This argument does not
assume reflexivity and does not assume one-quantifier elimination.

Third, set $z=\kappa(x,x)$, $d=e_{zx}$ and $e=e_{xx}$.
Propositional contraction of $G(x,x,z)$ gives $d\to Q_ze$.
Existential monotonicity and $\rho_1(z,x)$ give $\exists_zQ_ze$;
$\rho_6(z,e)$ gives $e_{xx}$.  Reflexivity is thus recovered without
either deleted axiom.

Fourth, $G(a,b,a)$ and reflexivity at $a$ yield the literal deleted helper
\[
 H(a,b)=e_{ab}\to Q_{\kappa(a,b)}e_{ab}.
\]
Generalizing this closed theorem at $i=\kappa(a,b)$ and applying
$\rho_2$ gives $Q_i e_{ab}\to Q_iQ_i e_{ab}$.  The second implication
above now gives $Q_i e_{ab}\to e_{ab}$.  Composing with G recovers
the literal old Euclidean row $e_{ca}\to(e_{cb}\to e_{ab})$.

All displayed generalizations apply to theorems with no open assumptions.
Their formulas may have free variable labels.  The derivations impose
no freshness condition, so coincident labels and zero are included.
In the actual REL case $\kappa(a,b)>\max(a,b)$ anyway.  The same
syntactic interderivation even tolerates a diagonal binder collision;
freshness matters separately when validating a chosen helper in ordinary
equality semantics.

For the reverse direction, old Euclidean equality and old H give G by
propositional composition.  The gate causes no loss of H: for each pair
$(a,b)$, the old bundle at $d=\max(a,b)+1$ supplies H, independently of
G's third parameter.  Conversely, the G row is unconditional, so
$G(a,b,a)$ is available even though the bundle with $c=a$ is disabled.
This distinction is essential and is respected by
\texttt{portable/verification/check\_g\_emitter.py}.

The exact bundle change is from
\[
 \neg\bigl(K\to(RV\to\neg H)\bigr)
 \quad\hbox{to}\quad
 \neg\bigl(K\to\neg RV\bigr).
\]
Projection and recombination with H are classical tautologies.  The K
core, full gated membership vacuity, truth seed, remaining rows, and
inference rules retain their previous identity.  Replacing each changed
axiom leaf by the derivations above therefore gives, for every native
code $n$,
\[
 \operatorname{Thm}_{G}(n)\leftrightarrow
 \operatorname{Thm}_{224}(n).
\]
This is a uniform finite-proof transformation formalizable in ZFC.  It
returns to the exact K224 numerical theory, so the previous equality
interpretation and K code/private-binder calibration need no change.

\subsection*{Independent finite replay}

The new \texttt{audit\_k223\_logic.py} imports no generator or bundled
checker code.  It verifies well-formed natural-labelled formulas, exact
axiom schemas, topological MP/GEN edges, Boolean tautologies, all 33
claimed root formulas, and separation of old-theory and new-theory
premises.  Replay passed all 315 nodes and 290 formulas, including 144 MP
nodes, 22 GEN nodes, 75 TAUT leaves, and 16 G leaves.  The ten old-theory
leaves occur only in the reverse-direction proofs.

These finite checks supplement the uniform proof and the inherited
propositional compilation.  They do not claim a new Rocq or Lean kernel
endpoint for the 223-state machine.
