\section{Which historical savings transfer to the K230 packet?}
\label{sec:transfer-accounting}

State savings cannot be subtracted mechanically from a new compiled axiom
packet.  The historical $243$-to-$235$ sequence used a different source
dictionary and different sharing.  Inherited ProveIt labels in the table
below are distinct from the original K230 labels used in the source
construction sections.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{p{0.22\linewidth}p{0.22\linewidth}p{0.47\linewidth}}
Historical reduction & Status & K230 result\\
\hline
$27\to15$ and $39\to17$ & Already present &
Both savings are already in the released K230 reference.\\[3pt]
$H$ identity and $90\to72$ & Transferred &
Four closed source substitutions permit K230 $79\to63$, saving one state.\\[3pt]
Boundary split of $218$ & Combined transfer &
Its two K230 roles are reassigned from $175$ to $150$ and $152$,
together with the predecessor-fold idea below.\\[3pt]
Delayed erasure $169\to80$ & Transferred &
K230 $140\to71$ and its coordinated helper change save one state.\\[3pt]
$235\to98$ & Changed premise &
The new axiom packet lacks the corresponding standalone shared decoder
role.  No separate saving from this fold is claimed.\\[3pt]
Predecessor fold $223\to182$ & Combined transfer &
The four-role assignment deletes K230 $175$.  This is the same single
deletion as the boundary row, not an additional one.\\[3pt]
Final $193\to74$ & Direct transfer open &
The needed historical source roles are not standalone shared nodes in
the new dictionary.  The old literal fold was not transplanted.\\[3pt]
New exact duplicate & Additional reduction &
A closed rotation exposes K230 $65=98$; its total quotient saves one
more state after address repair.
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The net new savings relative to the released K230 table are therefore
four: the $H$ fold, delayed erasure, the combined boundary/predecessor
role assignment, and the new exact duplicate quotient.  The result is
$226$ working states.  This accounting identifies the actual successful
constructions; it does not claim impossibility for the open transfers
or minimality of the final table.
