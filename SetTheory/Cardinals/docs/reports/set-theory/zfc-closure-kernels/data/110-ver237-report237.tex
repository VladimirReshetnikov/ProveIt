\subsection{An invariant established by all incoming edges}

let $I(q,h,T)$ impose the following conditions and no others:
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
q&\text{required bits}\\\hline
98&T(h-1)=0\\
139,156&T(h-1)=1\\
235&T(h-2)=T(h-1)=1\\
240&T(h-1)=0.
\end{array}
\]
The complete incoming-edge inventory of these five states is
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{rl}
\toprule
Target & Incoming rows\\\midrule
98 & $86/0$, $187/0$\\
139 & $117/1$\\
156 & $195/1$\\
235 & $139/1$, $156/1$\\
240 & $135/0$, $242/0$\\
\bottomrule

Each listed row moves right and writes the required new immediate-left

Induction proves $I$ throughout each such old run.  The verifier checks
every incoming implication on the exact literal table; the inventory is
not limited to the blocks being modified.

unspecified bit; consequently every exterior cell is independent and
arbitrary, rather than implicitly filled with zero.  None of these paths
contains a halt.

Every one of the other 473 old rows obeys
\[
 \delta_N(\phi(q),b)=(w,d,\phi(q'))
 \quad\text{when}\quad \delta_M(q,b)=(w,d,q').
\]
This includes $235/1$ and both redirected callers.  Thus all 476 old
rows are covered.  The guards of the three exceptional identities are
exactly consequences of $I$ together with the scanned bit.

\subsection{Positive blocks prove both directions}

\begin{theorem}[All-initial-tape transfer]
For every binary tape $T$ and every head position $h$,
\[
 \Halt(M,95,h,T)\longleftrightarrow\Halt(N,95,h,T).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $C_t$ be the old run and let $k(C_t)$ be 3, 5, or 3 on the three
exceptional rows, and 1 otherwise.  Define $\tau(0)=0$ and
$\tau(t+1)=\tau(t)+k(C_t)$.  Induction using $I$ and the complete block
identities gives the matching checkpoint $N^{\tau(t)}F(C_0)=F(C_t)$.
An old halt therefore yields a new halt.  If the old run is infinite,
then $\tau(t)\ge t$ and every intermediate block has no halt, giving an
infinite new run.  Completeness and determinism of both transition tables
give the converse.  These are finite-table calculations and ordinary
natural-number induction, and can be formalized in ZFC.
\end{proof}

More precisely, if $a_t,b_t,c_t$ count the three exceptional old rows in
the first $t$ transitions, then
\[
 \tau(t)=t+2a_t+4b_t+2c_t,\qquad t\le\tau(t)\le5t.
\]
The invariant belongs to the old run.  It is not asserted at arbitrary
intermediate states of the modified helper in $N$.
