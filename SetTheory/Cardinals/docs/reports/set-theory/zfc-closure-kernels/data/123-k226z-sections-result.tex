\begin{abstract}
We construct a deterministic, binary, one-tape Turing machine with
\textbf{226 working states}, including blank initialization and excluding
one separate halt outcome. It improves Elliot Glazer's pinned 230-state
construction by four states. The smaller table combines three ProveIt
ideas with his more compact set-theoretic axiom system: moving an increment
across a terminating drain loop, delayed erasure, and sharing decoder
states using a preceding tape bit. The third saving requires an additional
change: first move one role out of a shared state into a different existing
helper, and then fold its remaining role. Two address repairs restore the
six-zero limit of the tape encoding. A further closed reassociation
exposes an exact duplicate state; five more rotations restore valid
addresses after that fourth saving.

The final literal table has 452 rows. Its full source protocol is proved
for every natural register file, arbitrary additional gap and finite
spare-register tail, with positive progress. An independent source audit
checks all four loop replacements and every entry and exit. These proofs
compose with the pinned numerical and set-theoretic calibration to give
\[
 \mathrm{ZFC}\vdash
 \bigl(\NH(M_{226},\mathrm{blank})\leftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})\bigr).
\]
The report distinguishes the ordinary internalization argument, the
external Rocq endpoint, and finite diagnostic runs. It also gives a
fully declared 574-bit prefix encoding of the inherited E663 equational
packet. That encoding is a separate research result and is not used to
count the 226-state machine.
\end{abstract}

\subsection*{What is established}
The new result is a concrete composition of inherited optimization ideas
with Glazer's axiom system, including a new reassignment of decoder roles and a duplicate exposed by
source reassociation.
It is not a global minimality result. The consistency equivalence itself
does not assume \(\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})\). Under that consistency assumption,
the machine really does not halt, and ZFC cannot prove that nonhalting
statement. Mere consistency is not being used to assert that ZFC also
cannot prove halting.
