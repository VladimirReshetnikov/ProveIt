\section{Three certified folds from 226 to 223 working states}
\label{sec:threefolds}

This section concerns the literal binary transition tables, independently of
any interpretation of their source program.  Let $M_{226}$ be the corrected
zero-run-six G-source table, and let $M_{223}$ be the result of the three
transformations below.  Both machines start in state $K_0$ and use $K_{254}$
as the distinguished halt label.  These labels are historical labels; the
final table has exactly 223 working states and 446 transition rows.

The parent table has SHA-256
\begin{center}\small\ttfamily
 e7069fe4dc12fe62ba66cd88c27a51bfd83d548daad07591d3e0b0afaf6afe3d
\end{center}
and the 223-state table has SHA-256
\begin{center}\small\ttfamily
 5bcee335bc5cf45b6ebc6d1f97a10e3cc8aae2af303b82c5cc9ce506a4d22ed7.
\end{center}

\paragraph{Simulation principle.}
A fold identifying an old state $v$ with a retained state $u$ maps control
states by $\phi(v)=u$, fixing every other state, and leaves the head position
and entire tape unchanged at simulation boundaries.  Every ordinary old row
has its target renamed by $\phi$.  At the two exceptional old states, each
possible old one-step transition must be realized by a nonempty finite block
of new transitions with exactly the mapped endpoint.  The proof may assume
an inductive invariant of the \emph{old} machine at that boundary.  No
interior transition may halt.  This gives halting equivalence from every
initial configuration satisfying the invariant: an infinite old computation
produces infinitely many positive-length new blocks, while an old halt
produces a finite new computation ending in the same halt state.

For the present folds, the initial invariant imposes no restriction on the
tape in start state $K_0$.  Thus each fold, and their composition, is valid
for every initial binary tape and every initial head position.  The invariant
is checked for every literal old row; an old branch can be excluded only
when its scanned bit contradicts the invariant.

\subsection{Fold 1: a bit immediately to the left selects the continuation}

The incoming rows of $K_{157}$ all write zero and move right.  They are
precisely the zero rows of $K_{192},K_{200},K_{209},K_{216},K_{221}$.
The sole incoming row of $K_{191}$ is
\[
 \delta(K_{300},1)=(1,R,K_{191}).
\]
Consequently the cell immediately left of the head is zero in $K_{157}$
and one in $K_{191}$.  Neither state is the initial state.

Remove $K_{191}$, redirect every incoming target $K_{191}$ to $K_{157}$,
and give $K_{157}$ the rows
\[
 \delta'(K_{157},0)=(0,L,K_{67}),\qquad
 \delta'(K_{157},1)=(1,L,K_{187}).
\]
The four old one-step endpoints are implemented by the following blocks.
Each displayed list gives the consecutive control states, including both
endpoints.  In every case the old scanned cell is preserved, the final head
is one cell to its right, and every other tape cell is unchanged.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cclc}
Old state & Read & New state sequence & Steps\\
\hline
$157$ & $0$ & $157,67,151,90$ & $3$\\
$157$ & $1$ & $157,187,186,86$ & $3$\\
$191$ & $0$ & $157,67,152,159,118,155$ & $5$\\
$191$ & $1$ & $157,187,76,148,173,182$ & $5$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Only the old scanned cell and its immediate predecessor are visited.  This
proves the reduction $226\longrightarrow225$ without any assumption about
the rest of the tape.

\subsection{Fold 2: use a branch that the old machine cannot take}

In the intermediate 225-state table, the only incoming row of $K_{159}$ is
\[
 \delta(K_{152},0)=(0,L,K_{159}),
\]
so the cell one step right of the head contains zero in state $K_{159}$.
The only incoming row of $K_{118}$ is
\[
 \delta(K_{159},1)=(1,R,K_{118}).
\]
Therefore $K_{118}$ always scans zero in this intermediate machine.  The
following pair of assertions is a directly checked inductive invariant:
\[
 K_{159}\Longrightarrow\tau(h+1)=0,\qquad
 K_{118}\Longrightarrow\tau(h)=0.
\]
Here $\tau$ is the tape and $h$ is the current head position.  The first
assertion is established by the write of $K_{152}$; the second follows by
the subsequent right move of $K_{159}$.

The two states to be identified have rows
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ccl}
State & Read & Old action\\
\hline
$K_{118}$ & $0$ & $(0,R,K_{155})$\\
$K_{118}$ & $1$ & $(1,R,K_{173})$, impossible under the invariant\\
$K_{303}$ & $0$ & $(0,R,K_{155})$\\
$K_{303}$ & $1$ & $(1,R,K_{153})$.
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Remove $K_{303}$ and redirect its incoming targets to $K_{118}$.  Keep the
zero row of $K_{118}$ and change its one row to $(1,R,K_{153})$.
The three possible old branches now agree with their mapped endpoints in
exactly one new step.  This proves $225\longrightarrow224$.

The invariant that $K_{118}$ scans zero belongs to the machine \emph{before}
this fold.  After the fold, the one row is used for computations formerly
in $K_{303}$.  It is neither necessary nor correct to assert that the one
row remains unreachable in the new machine.  The proof uses the old
invariant at each old simulation boundary and then composes the certified
simulations.

\subsection{Fold 3: identical predecessors}

After the second fold, $K_{159}$ and $K_{302}$ have identical rows:
\[
 \delta(q,0)=(0,R,K_{166}),\qquad
 \delta(q,1)=(1,R,K_{118}),\qquad q\in\{K_{159},K_{302}\}.
\]
Redirect $K_{302}$ to $K_{159}$ and delete its two rows.  This is an ordinary
one-step quotient, valid for every tape and head position without any
invariant.  The resulting table has 223 working states.  Every declared
working state is reachable from $K_0$ in the directed transition graph.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
The composition of the three positive-block simulations gives
\[
 M_{223}\text{ halts on }(K_0,\tau,h)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 M_{226}\text{ halts on }(K_0,\tau,h)
\]
for every binary tape $\tau:\mathbb Z\to\{0,1\}$ and every $h\in\mathbb Z$.
In particular it applies to the all-zero tape.  The separate source and
runtime argument identifying $M_{226}$ as the desired proof search may
therefore be transported to the 223-state table.

\paragraph{Independent check.}
The self-contained Python program \texttt{check\_folds.py} uses only the
standard library.  It reads the two literal tables and \texttt{folds.json}
as data, checks the pinned hashes and total binary table format, verifies
all invariant implications row by row, and executes the eleven exceptional
blocks on their finite known patches.  Any read outside a known patch is
rejected.  Hence acceptance covers every assignment to the infinite tape
exterior.  The checker also checks every ordinary renamed row, exact equality
with the delivered final table, and the final state count.  Its finite
sixteen-step block bound limits the certificates it can accept; it never
accepts an incomplete block.  The longest accepted block has five steps.

The receipt is \texttt{fold\_certificate.json}.  Three negative controls
are rejected: an incorrect predecessor-bit assertion, omission of the
inductive support for the $K_{118}$ assertion, and a wrong value for the
reclaimed one row.  Their receipt is \texttt{negative\_controls.json}.
