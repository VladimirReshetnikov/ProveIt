\section{Equality orientations and converse membership}
\label{sec:converse-encodings}

There is a useful distinction between replacing a logical axiom by a
plausible shorter formula and changing every occurrence of a primitive atom
consistently.  The second operation can preserve the entire proof calculus
by an explicit involution.  We tested two such changes against the frozen
source: converse membership in the nonlogical templates, and converse
equality throughout the logical templates.  The latter has a favorable
initial source metric, although a smaller transition table requires a
separate compiler experiment.

\subsection{Equality converse}

Work first with named formulas whose atomic constructors are
$R(i,j)$ and $E(i,j)$, with implication, falsity, and universal
quantification.  Here $E$ is syntactically a binary atomic constructor;
the original equality axioms give it the usual equality behavior.
Define an involution $\epsilon$ by
\[
 \epsilon E(i,j)=E(j,i),\qquad
 \epsilon R(i,j)=R(i,j),\qquad
 \epsilon\bot=\bot,
\]
and let it commute with implication and quantification.
It follows by structural induction that $\epsilon^2 A=A$,
that $\epsilon$ preserves the set of occurring names, and that it
commutes with every capture-avoiding renaming or substitution of names.

Let $\mathcal L$ be the complete named-variable calculus behind the
original eight logical rows, with modus ponens, generalization, and
both gated atomic-vacuity families.  Let $\mathcal L^\epsilon$ contain
the $\epsilon$-images of the eight rows and retain both full
atomic-vacuity families.  Reversing the equality-vacuity family does
not change that family, since both endpoint names range freely.

\begin{theorem}[Equality-converse transport]
For every sentence theory $T$ and formula $A$,
\[
 T\vdash_{\mathcal L} A
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \epsilon T\vdash_{\mathcal L^\epsilon}\epsilon A.
\]
The two translations of finite proofs are primitive recursive and
inverse to each other.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply $\epsilon$ to every line of a finite proof.  An axiom instance
becomes an instance of its image schema.  Modus ponens is preserved
because $\epsilon(A\to B)=\epsilon A\to\epsilon B$;
generalization is preserved because
$\epsilon(\forall_i A)=\forall_i\epsilon A$.
The side condition $k\ne i,j$ on atomic vacuity is unchanged.
No recursive variable substitution is added to the running enumerator:
this is a metatheoretic proof translation.  Applying the same operation
again gives the inverse proof translation.  The operation acts
recursively on finite syntax and proof sequences, so primitive
recursiveness and the proof-checker assertions are provable in PA
for the chosen elementary codings.
\end{proof}

For clarity, the four rows that actually change are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \rho_1^\epsilon&=\exists a\,E(b,a),\\
 \rho_3^\epsilon&=E(b,a)\to(R(c,a)\to R(c,b)),\\
 \rho_5^\epsilon&=E(a,c)\to(R(c,b)\to R(a,b)),\\
 \rho_7^\epsilon&=E(a,c)\to(E(b,c)\to E(b,a)).
\end{aligned}
\]
The equality-vacuity factor becomes
$E(b,a)\to\forall_cE(b,a)$.
The other four rows, GEN, the membership-vacuity factor, the
freshness tests, the command parser, and the scheduler stay as in the
original source.  Reversing an arbitrary selection of equality atoms
is not justified by this involution theorem alone.  The anchored family
proved next allows specified nonuniform reversals.

The nonlogical theory has CP, the full GIC schema, and IR.  Its CP
and IR formulas contain no equality atoms.  Its GIC wrapper contains
equality only inside the arbitrary matrix.  Consequently
\[
 \epsilon(\operatorname{GIC}[\varphi])
       =\operatorname{GIC}[\epsilon\varphi],
 \qquad \epsilon T_{\mathrm{SIR}}=T_{\mathrm{SIR}}.
\]
In particular, the modified named calculus has exactly the same
refutation strength as the original calculus over SIR.  The
diagonal formula used by the numerical source as falsity,
$F=R(0,0)$, is fixed by $\epsilon$; the truth alias is fixed as well.
This avoids a change in the meaning of the halt target.

The numerical source uses one scalar in two roles, as the GIC matrix
code and as its auxiliary name.  The preceding argument is not a
claim that $\epsilon$ fixes that scalar: transforming its decoded
matrix generally changes its code while leaving the displayed binder
name fixed.  A direct numerical conjugacy would therefore need an
additional fresh-name and encodability argument.  For equality
orientations this issue can be avoided completely by the local
axiom-replacement proof below: all GIC matrix codes stay literally
unchanged.  The involution theorem is a useful semantic explanation,
but the local proof is the stronger implementation interface.

\subsection{A directly calibrated 32-member equality family}

Write $\exists^F_i A=((\forall_i(A\to F))\to F)$ for the
original relative existential, where $F=R(0,0)$ is the literal
halt target.  The following argument works directly with that
formula; it does not treat $F$ as a closed primitive constant or
assume that numerical formula constructors are injective.

An \emph{anchor direction} $d\in\{0,1\}$ chooses
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
d&\text{existence anchor}&\text{Euclidean anchor}\\\hline
0&\exists^F_a E(a,b)&E(c,a)\to(E(c,b)\to E(a,b))\\
1&\exists^F_a E(b,a)&E(a,c)\to(E(b,c)\to E(b,a)).
\end{array}
\]
These are the original $\rho_1,\rho_7$ and their uniform
equality-converse images.  The four equality-free logical rows,
GEN, and both unrestricted gated atomic-vacuity families are
retained.  In particular, the gates remain exactly $c\ne a,b$.

\begin{lemma}[Anchors suffice for equality symmetry]
In either anchor direction, the basis derives
$E(x,x)$ and $E(x,y)\to E(y,x)$, independently of $\rho_3$
and $\rho_5$.  The proofs are uniform finite proof patterns in
the names $x,y$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The known finite $\rho_8$ certificates supply K, S and Peirce.
Only K, S and the unchanged $\rho_6$ are needed for relative
explosion: if $D=\forall_i((\forall_i A)\to F)$, then K gives
$F\to(D\to F)$, while $\rho_6$ gives $(D\to F)\to A$.
Composition gives $F\to A$.

Quantifier distribution and GEN give universal monotonicity.
Propositional contraposition on each side then gives
\[
 (A\to B)\text{ derivable}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 (\exists^F_i A\to\exists^F_i B)\text{ derivable}.
\]
If $A\to\forall_i A$ is derivable, monotonicity followed by
$\rho_6$ gives $\exists^F_i A\to A$.

For direction zero, choose $k=x+1$.  The Euclidean anchor with
both endpoint names equal to $x$ gives $E(k,x)\to E(x,x)$.
The existence anchor and existential monotonicity give
$\exists^F_k E(x,x)$.  Equality vacuity at $k\ne x$ and the
preceding existential elimination give $E(x,x)$.
The Euclidean instance
$E(x,y)\to(E(x,x)\to E(y,x))$ now gives symmetry.
For direction one, use $E(x,k)\to E(x,x)$ in the first argument,
and use
$E(x,y)\to(E(y,y)\to E(y,x))$ in the last step.
Every invocation has the same finite proof pattern and a
computable fresh name.
\end{proof}

The corresponding named-syntax proof is already visible in
\path{Tarski.eq_refl} and \path{Tarski.eq_symm}
in the pinned repository's \path{TarskiDerived.lean}.
The preceding argument lists its dependencies to rule out
circular use of either congruence row.

\begin{theorem}[Local equality-orientation replacement]
Starting from either anchor direction, independently perform
any of the following four changes:
\begin{enumerate}
\item reverse the sole equality premise in $\rho_3$;
\item reverse the sole equality premise in $\rho_5$;
\item reverse the concluding equality in $\rho_7$;
\item reverse both endpoint occurrences in equality vacuity.
\end{enumerate}
All $2\cdot2^4=32$ resulting numerical calculi have exactly
the same theorems, and hence the same refutations, as the
original numerical calculus.  This holds with the original
gated C/G bundle, matrix decoder and relative falsity code.
The proof translations replace axiom leaves by uniform finite
derivations and keep each arbitrary matrix code unchanged.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
First normalize the fourth and third changes.  Reversed equality
vacuity is the identical full family under the interchange
$a\leftrightarrow b$; its two freshness conditions are symmetric.
For direction zero, the changed Euclidean row is
$E(c,a)\to(E(c,b)\to E(b,a))$.  Its instance with $a,b$
interchanged, followed by the propositional antecedent-exchange
theorem, yields the original Euclidean row.  The reverse derivation
is identical.  The direction-one calculation is its literal
equality converse.  This step uses no equality symmetry.

The lemma now supplies symmetry in every variant.  For arbitrary
formula $P$, the two implications $E(x,y)\to P$ and
$E(y,x)\to P$ interderive using symmetry and propositional
composition.  This deals independently with both congruence
premises.  It also changes every premise or conclusion in either
Euclidean anchor when converting between anchor directions.
Existential monotonicity applied to equality symmetry converts
the two existence anchors.  Thus every logical row of each
calculus is derivable in the other.

The only changed bundle factor is equality vacuity.  Conjunction
projection extracts the C, G and membership-vacuity factors at
the original $(a,b,c)$.  A bundle at $(b,a,c)$ supplies the desired
orientation of equality vacuity.  Conjunction introduction
reassembles the desired bundle.  Both calls have the same valid
gate, and the C/G factors used in the reconstructed bundle retain
the original matrix code $c$ and the original names.  Relative
conjunction projections and introduction are derivable using the
propositional layer and the relative explosion established above.

Replace axiom leaves in a finite derivation by these blocks; copy
MP and GEN nodes.  Each code constructor used is the original
one, so the proof works by its arithmetic defining equalities
even at aliased codes.  Structural induction on the derivation
gives both theoremhood implications.  There is no transformation
of an arbitrary encoded formula in this proof.
\end{proof}

This direct transfer reduces the new endpoint obligation to
verifying the actual changed AX emitter and its compiled
implementation.  It still does not identify any unchecked
transition table with that emitter, nor prove a new blank-start
runtime theorem.  The existing output-selector construction can
extract the extra AX instances used by the replacement blocks,
with the protected theorem-stack suffix preserved.

\subsection{Converse membership}

Define $\mu R(i,j)=R(j,i)$, fix equality and falsity, and again
commute with the logical constructors.  The same proof-translation
argument applies to complete first-order logic.  At the theory level,
write $C^\mu$ for CP with every explicit relation atom reversed, and
$G^\mu[\varphi]$ for the GIC wrapper with explicit relation atoms
reversed but its matrix placeholder left opaque.  Since $\mu$ is an
involution on all matrices,
\[
 \{\mu(G[\psi]):\psi\text{ a formula}\}
       =\{G^\mu[\varphi]:\varphi\text{ a formula}\}.
\]
Also $\mu$ fixes the diagonal irreflexivity axiom.  Thus
$C^\mu+\{G^\mu[\varphi]\}_\varphi+\mathrm{IR}$ is
definitionally isomorphic to SIR by taking the converse of the
primitive binary relation.  The interpreted relation is converse
membership; ordinary membership itself is not asserted to satisfy
the reversed axioms.

All eight logical rows can remain unchanged, because they form a
complete calculus for an arbitrary binary relation.  Both ordinary
atomic-vacuity factors remain unchanged.  The source therefore
needs no recursive transformation of an input matrix: it constructs
the reversed wrapper around every decoded matrix, and the family of
matrices supplies the inverse transformation in the proof argument.

Combining membership converse with the 32 equality orientations
gives 64 fully specified source variants.  Only the equality
factor has the direct same-code replacement theorem above.
Membership converse changes the nonlogical wrapper and therefore
uses the ordinary native full-schema theory, together with the
usual padding, freshness and decoded-bundle bridges.  Its
numerical calibration has not been re-exported as a Lean theorem
in this experiment.

\subsection{Exact source measurements}

The independent source generator loads the pinned pure formula
definitions, re-emits the original logical rows and bundle, and
checks equality of their entire finest source-operation sequence
with the retained original AX word.  It then applies the specified
atom reversals.  All 64 words have 1,270 finest operations.  The
scalar-copy macros and three tag increments in the equality
constructor are retained unchanged.

The 44 existing semantic atoms provide a finite dictionary of
operation words.  Dynamic programming over every operation cut
computes the minimum number of dictionary calls spelling each
new word.  It is an exact optimization for that dictionary, with
no claim of optimality over Turing machines.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\hline
Source variant & Finest operations & Minimum dictionary calls\\
\hline
Original & 1,270 & 533\\
Membership converse in C/G & 1,270 & 546\\
Equality converse throughout logic & 1,270 & 499\\
Both converses & 1,270 & 512\\
Best anchored equality orientation & 1,270 & 493\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The original published spelling has 702 calls; its already known
best dictionary spelling has 533.  Thus the favorable comparison
for equality converse is $533\to499$, rather than comparing a newly
optimized spelling only with the older unoptimized 702-call word.
The script also checks the membership-converse semantic identity
in 1,028 small wrapper cases, with the matrix interpreted as an
arbitrary binary predicate.  Those finite tests validate the
implementation of the transformation; the all-formula theorem is
the structural proof above.

The best anchored word uses direction-zero existence and
Euclidean premises, reverses both congruence premises and the
Euclidean conclusion, reverses equality vacuity, and retains
ordinary membership in C/G.  Its manifest key is
\path{anchor0_r31_r51_r7c1_ev1_mu0}.  The four
nontrivial logical rows are explicitly
\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_1'&=\exists^F_aE(a,b),\\
\rho_3'&=E(b,a)\to(R(c,a)\to R(c,b)),\\
\rho_5'&=E(a,c)\to(R(c,b)\to R(a,b)),\\
\rho_7'&=E(c,a)\to(E(c,b)\to E(b,a)).
\end{aligned}
\]
The equality-vacuity factor is $E(b,a)\to\forall_cE(b,a)$.
Its exact improvement over the prior best dictionary spelling
is $533\to493$, or 40 calls.

The 40-call improvement is not a 40-state improvement.  Shared
grammar nodes, allowed incoming directions, scanner restrictions,
required roots, and the entire blank prefix must all be charged in
the complete compiler.  At this stage the result is an explicit
new source family with a rigorous logical justification and a
promising measured dictionary cost, not a new small-machine bound.
