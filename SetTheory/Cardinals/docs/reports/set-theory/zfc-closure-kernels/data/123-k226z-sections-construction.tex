\section{Why the reductions compose, and where they do not}
\label{sec:construction-overview}

The most useful change of viewpoint is to treat the set-theoretic frontend
and the native tape interpreter as separate sources of savings. Glazer's
K230 construction changes the axiom packet and its emitter, while retaining
the proof enumerator, the eight logical schemas, the unary register
conventions and much of the native interpreter~\cite{glazer}. ProveIt's
243-to-235 chain mainly changes how that interpreter and its source tree
use the available states~\cite{proveit235,proveit236}. Some of those savings
therefore survive the new axiom system, but others depend on source chunks
that it replaces.

The pinned K230 table already incorporates the two older 243-to-241
folds. They cannot be counted a second time. The new construction saves
four more states. The following table uses original K230 labels, not
the dense numbering of the final file.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lclX@{}}\toprule
Operation & Saving & Deleted role & Reason\\\midrule
Increment/drain transformation & 1 & $79\to63$ &
Move the final increment to the loop's entrance; the new decoder contexts
allow two native states to share a preceding-bit test.\\
Delayed erasure & 1 & $140\to71$ &
Reuse the original helper paths, preserving a bit until a later step.
The required right-hand guard holds throughout the runtime.\\
Decoder role reassignment & 1 & $175$ removed &
Move its implication/increment role to $150$, then fold its zero/increment
role into $152$ using the existing preceding bit.\\
Exact duplicate after reassociation & 1 & $65\to98$ &
Rebracket a closed source subtree until both decoder rows match an
existing state, then apply five address-repair rotations.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Thus the literal count is
\[
                 230-1-1-1-1=226.
\]
The table makes clear why simply subtracting eight from 230 would be wrong:
two savings are already present, and the remaining source-specific savings
have to be reconstructed and proved under the new contexts.

\subsection{The first transferred state: delayed erasure}

This especially small intermediate is useful for review. Delete K230 state
$140$, redirect $126/0$ to $71$, and change two retained rows:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}rlll@{}}\toprule
State / read & Old row & New row & Meaning\\\midrule
$71/0$ & $0R21$ & $0L91$ & inspect the preceding tag\\
$80/1$ & $0L42$ & $1L138$ & defer the erasure\\
$126/0$ & $0R140$ & $0R71$ & enter the shared state\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Every other surviving row is unchanged. The canonical 229-state table is
retained as \path{machines/k229.tm}; its SHA--256 is
\begin{quote}\small
\path{08dff69bd527a65967608fafc9d1205a667a6f6087289c4b55452faccb042273}.
\end{quote}

The syntactically inductive invariant is
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
q=58&\Rightarrow&t_{-1}=0,\\
q=71&\Rightarrow&(t_{-2},t_{-1})=(0,1),\\
q=80&\Rightarrow&(t_{-3},t_{-2},t_{-1})=(0,1,1),\\
q=140&\Rightarrow&t_{-1}=0,
\end{array}
\]
where $t_j$ is the bit $j$ cells from the head. The additional runtime
condition is
\[
                 q=140\ \land\ t_0=1\quad\Rightarrow\quad t_1=1.
\]
It is a fact about valid instruction frames and blank initialization;
the local argument does not assume it for every arbitrary initial tape.

Map $140$ to $71$ and every other state to itself. The four exceptional
old rows are simulated in respectively $5,3,5,9$ new transitions:
\[
          (71,0),\quad(140,0),\quad(140,1),\quad(80,1).
\]
The remaining 456 of the 460 old rows commute in one transition. The
blocks preserve the complete tape and end at the mapped old successor;
none contains an internal halt. Consequently the correspondence has
a strictly increasing time map, bounded between $n$ and $9n$ after
$n$ old steps. This proves both directions of halting equivalence.

The independent audit \path{verification/independent_fold229.py}
checks every incoming invariant implication symbolically, every admitted
Boolean assignment on the finite block footprints, every ordinary row,
the dense renumbering, and all $408\,420$ initialization checkpoints.
The latter compare the entire finite support of the tape, with a zero
exterior. The new initialization takes $408\,563$ transitions, exactly
144 more than K230. The separate symbolic guard proof covers all
$6\,660$ original instruction cases, using $1\,605\,903$ proof rules;
all 28 exposed read-one visits have a one-run constant at least 32.
The direct Rocq runtime replay is discussed separately below.

\subsection{The second transferred state: moving an increment through a loop}

The closed source block
\[
 ((\mathrm{CD}_6,(\mathrm{CI}_5,\mathrm{CC}_2)),\mathrm{CI}_5)
 \quad\longmapsto\quad
 (\mathrm{CI}_5,(\mathrm{CD}_6,\mathrm{CC}_2))
\]
has four occurrences in the K230 source. Both sides terminate with
\((R_5,R_6)=(a+b+1,0)\) from \((a,b)\), and change no other register.
Induction on $b$ proves the contract: the old form adds one for each
positive decrement and one at the end; the new form adds one at the
beginning and one after each successful return through the loop.

This replacement removes four primitive occurrences while preserving the
program's semantics: $5\,125$ leaves become $5\,121$, and $6\,660$ guarded
instruction cases become $6\,656$. The number of distinct source subtrees
stays 212. With the corresponding native decoder change, state $79$
can be folded into $63$. Combining this with delayed erasure gives a
verified 228-state intermediate.

The independent source checker does more than identify four matching
substrings. It builds both source trees directly from the pinned K230
site list and the proposed replacement list, checks the exact internal
graphs of both loop forms, checks every external entry and exit, and
compares all $5\,113$ control points after contracting the four loops
to their common operation. Thus an unintended jump into the middle of
the changed block, or any change elsewhere in the source, would fail.

\subsection{Why the third saving needed a new arrangement}

K230 state $175$ already serves two source roles. A direct transplant of
the older predecessor-bit fold would conflict with one of them. The
successful construction first makes an existing state $150$ handle that
role using an existing helper at $173$. State $175$ then has only its
other role, which can share $152$ through the helpers at $159$ and $169$.
The exact local whole-tape proof and source rotations are given in the
next section. No additional state, symbol or initialized tape cell is
introduced for this rearrangement.

The first 227-state trial had a single instruction path ending in seven
zeros. Its finite check failed at a zero branch of a decrement of register
2. That failure is preserved as a rejected control. Two further closed
source rotations repair the offending address without disturbing the
new shared-state contexts. The repaired 227-state checkpoint has maximum depth 40 and
maximum zero run six. Section~\ref{sec:k226-duplicate} gives the further
reduction and repairs leading to the selected 226-state machine.
