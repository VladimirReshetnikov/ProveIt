\section{Framework reductions: a relaxed bootstrap target and an exact obstruction}
\label{sec:framework-findings}

No table below 245 working states is certified by this section.  Its smaller
tables are explicitly named \texttt{unverified\_scanner244} and
\texttt{unverified\_scanner243}; they are retained to make the unsuccessful
reductions concrete and reproducible.  The reference is the old-label routed
245-state table with SHA-256
\begin{center}\small
\texttt{f8dbeacdad5fd89c57e3f240781324b09110be8e0ccdb838a9c710fb28d2a267}.
\end{center}
Its start, dispatcher and halt labels are respectively 95, 96 and 254.

\subsection{The original bootstrap equations are not necessary}

Delete state 58 and replace transition $(41,0)$ by $(0,L,84)$.  This yields
244 working states.  Deleting state 41 as well, and replacing $(29,0)$ by
$(0,L,84)$, yields 243.  Both retain a concrete blank prefix reaching a
complete frame with formula-bearing logical registers $T=W=H=0$.
Independent sparse-set and dense-array interpreters agree on every one-cell
and hence on the complete tapes:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
Table & Steps & Head & Header distance & Spare blocks\\\hline
Reference 245 & 407915 & $-5$ & 181 & 167\\
Unverified 244 & 818324 & $-8$ & 245 & 233\\
Unverified 243 & 1389062 & $-8$ & 331 & 321
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
All listed spares have value one.  In the order $(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)$, the
logical seeds are respectively
\[
(3,0,0,0,3,0,0,0,0),\qquad
(6,0,0,0,4,0,0,0,1),\qquad
(6,0,0,0,6,0,0,0,1).
\]
The complete one-cell counts are 351, 488 and 666; all their coordinates are
retained in \path{framework_work/exact-blank-prefixes.json}.
These facts justify relaxing the earlier fixed seed and 54 grammar
bootstrap equations during search.  A replacement search may instead check
its newly produced complete formula-clean frame.  A successful finite
bootstrap does not establish recurrent correctness.

\subsection{Why the direct 244-state contraction is not accepted}

For header distance $64+n$, the unchanged guarded symbolic checker examines
all 8391 instruction cases.  It accepts 8292 and fails 99: both guards of 49
decrements and one increment.  Exactly 50 source leaves are affected, and
these are exactly the leaves whose paths end in six zeros.  By physical
register, the failed decrement counts are 70 for register 2, 26 for register
0 and 2 for register 1; the failed increment uses register 2.  The checker
permits 1000 symbolic rules per obligation.  Its failures remain failures of
a sufficient procedure, not 99 separately asserted inequivalence proofs.

There is also an independently replayed literal defect.  Start at state 96,
head zero, pointer $p=11101001001000000$, header distance 64, nine zero
physical registers, and one spare of value one.  Put one precisely at
\[
\begin{split}
C=\{&-1,0,1,2,4,7,10,64,66,68,70,72,74,\\
    &76,78,80,82,84,85\};
\end{split}
\]
every other tape cell is zero.  This is a zero decrement of physical
register 2.  The required successor pointer is
$1110100100100001$.  The reference first returns to a complete frame at
step 574, state 96 and head zero, with one-cells $C\cup\{15\}$.  The mutant
first returns at step 650 with the same state, head and register file but
one-cells $C\cup\{13\}$, representing $11101001001001$.

Here a complete return frame has the sole negative one-cell at $-1$, the
unchanged entire register suffix at and beyond 64, and a zero interval
$[51,64)$ after the possible pointer; 51 is the reference maximum source
depth.  All earlier dispatcher encounters are checked against this explicit
criterion.  The mutant's second dispatcher encounter, at step 279, has head
11; the corresponding reference encounter, at step 310, has head zero.
This shows the false recognition of an internal zero suffix.  A second
dense-tape execution checks both final configurations.

The assertion is exact disagreement at the first complete instruction
boundary.  No reachability of this particular frame from blank is claimed;
nor is it claimed that the mutant can never revisit the expected frame later
or that its eventual halting differs.  The data are sufficient to reject the
proposed unchanged-source simulation argument, without overstating them as
a refutation of every possible correctness argument for this table.

\subsection{Expanded local search families}

The finite fixture family contains 8385 cases, has header distance 181 and
167 value-one spares, and exercises all 490 reference rows.  Its longest
reference case takes 2686 steps.  Each mutation is allowed 20000 steps and
tape coordinates $-64$ through 1983.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
Family & Recipes & Full survivors\\\hline
Pair quotient with four row selections & 119560 & 0\\
Individual edge retargeting & 119316 & 109\\
Combined surviving retargets deleting state 4 & 1536 & 0\\
Combined surviving retargets deleting state 42 & 30 & 0
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Only states 4 and 42 have surviving alternatives for every external incoming
edge in the individual-edge screen.  Their combinations do not survive.
This also demonstrates why individually successful fixture-preserving edits
must not be assumed to compose: each may depend on an original route removed
by the other.  These bounded sweeps establish no minimality theorem.

Pure router 166 was separately checked for context-dependent replacement.
Its only external callers write a known bit $c$ and move left; let the now
scanned predecessor be $b$.  After two transparent right steps, its endpoint
states for $b=0,1$ are $(53,49)$ when $c=0$, and $(54,90)$ when $c=1$.
Every other working state was tried as a replacement under this local
contract.  All newly encountered unknown tape bits are explicitly branched,
the whole tape must be preserved, state 166 may not be entered, and the
bound is twelve literal steps.  No replacement exists in this bounded
family.  Longer paths or newly synthesized routers are not excluded.

\subsection{An unlocked five-zero grammar search}

A modified inherited annealer removes the bootstrap locks and minimizes
$K+0.25b$, where $K$ is the grammar-rule count and $b$ counts expanded syntax
nodes with leading zero run greater than five.  Maximum zero run six and
depth 64 remain hard bounds.  Seed 710418 made 5615239 proposals in
600.006 seconds.  The retained weighted-best checkpoint has 132 rules,
maximum zero run six, depth 45 and eleven bad nodes; it is not a target
solution and is not a lower-bound certificate.

The concrete remaining objective is a 128-rule grammar with a five-zero
bound, separately passing full source bisimulation, all runtime obligations,
and its new blank-prefix check.  Together with the 116-state shortened
framework it would yield 244 states.  The present experiments show that
retaining the previous bootstrap equations is unnecessary for this search;
they do not claim to have solved its remaining grammar problem.

\subsection{The defect also appears on a literal blank trajectory}

An independent source-context map relates the routed program directly to the
annotated 219-node \path{Dag254.v} using a closed segment bisimulation:
3021 pointer pairs and 6041 checked straight-line segments. It places 49
affected leaves in \texttt{axioms()} and one in \texttt{mp()}. The argument
compares register tests and every register's increment count between tests;
it does not assume the two source compilations have identical leaf order.
The complete annotation paths are in \path{scanner-semantic-contexts.json}.

The MP leaf, source index 6367 and path $1111000000$, is reached by the
contracted 244-state machine itself from blank at literal step 835524.
The complete frame has state 96, head $-8$, header 238, physical registers
$(0,0,0,4,0,0,6,0,0)$ and 233 spare blocks of value one. This is the zero
branch of a decrement of $T$. Starting with that same complete frame,
the reference first returns cleanly after 2044 steps with the required
residual pointer $111100001$; the contraction first returns after 2102
steps with pointer $1111001$. Relative to the common initial one-cell set,
the reference adds coordinate $0$ and the contraction adds coordinate $-2$.
The latter configuration therefore occurs at actual blank step 837626.
Every cell in these finite prefixes and both fork endpoints is independently
replayed with sparse and dense tapes by
\path{reachable_scanner_discrepancy.py}.

The two residual pointers also give different register files on their next
return to the root under the ordinary source semantics. The expected fork
reaches the root after 652 source instructions with $T=0$; the changed fork
does so after 43 with $T=4$. The other logical registers in both files are
$(N,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)=(6,0,0,4,0,0,0,0)$. This is a finite source comparison;
it does not identify all subsequent steps of the altered binary machine.
In particular, neither the blank-reachable discrepancy nor the different
root files proves different eventual halting. A revised source contract or
a stronger reachable-state invariant has not been excluded.
