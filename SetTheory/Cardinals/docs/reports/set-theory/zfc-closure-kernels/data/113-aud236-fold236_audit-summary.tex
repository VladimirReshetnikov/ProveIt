\section{Independent audit of the newly arrived 236-state construction}

The incoming archive \texttt{ZFC236\_Construction\_2026-10-19.zip}, added in
ProveIt commit \texttt{0b5fec6980cc3a5ba7dd47b404f0cbbe9a451c02}, contains a
236-state construction that was not present in the earlier pinned checkout.
The independent audit here confirms its local reduction. The construction is
credited to that incoming submission; the following is an independent review
and a limited further-search result.

Relative to the earlier composed237 table, the reassociated237 table changes
exactly four rows:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
\text{row}&\text{earlier237}&\text{reassociated237}\\\hline
177/0&0R165&0R185\\
177/1&1R185&1R212\\
185/0&0R192&0R165\\
185/1&1R212&1R192
\end{array}
\]
These rows perform the literal binary-tree rotation
$(165,(192,212))\mapsto((165,192),212)$ without changing the number of states.
Its source-level semantic justification is a separate obligation from the
local binary fold.

The fold deletes state223 and maps it to182. The new rows are
$182/0=0L198$, $182/1=1L210$, and the old incoming row $237/0=0R223$ becomes
$0R182$. The complete old incoming lists are just $76/1=1R182$ and
$237/0=0R223$. Since the start is95, every visit to old182 has a1 immediately
left of the head, while every visit to old223 has a0 there. These guards hold
on every initial tape and at every initial integer head position.

The four exceptional simulations are:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc|c|c}
\text{old state}&\text{read}&\text{left bit}&
\text{three new targets}&\text{old target}\\\hline

Every block reads only the entry cell and its left neighbor, preserves every
bit it reads, and moves $L,R,R$. Thus each block exactly simulates one old
transition, with identical head and entire tape. All470 other old rows commute
with the state map in one step. The independent checker verifies the complete
row sets and these finite premises against pinned table hashes, importing no
upstream proof or discovery module.

Consequently, after $j$ old transitions the new clock is between $j$ and $3j$;
no block has an interior halt. The two machines therefore have the same halting
behavior on all common initial tapes, including the blank tape. Their head and
tape even agree at a matched halt. This ordinary proof concerns the
reassociated237-to236 fold; consistency calibration and the changed source's
runtime interpretation are checked separately.

The enabling change can be isolated exactly. Without the source rotation,
the proposed block for old182 reading1 ends at185 after three steps, whereas
the required target is212. The new row $177/1=1R212$ supplies that target.
The earlier fixed-source local searches did not allow this semantic source
rotation. The audit additionally verifies that the old $235\to98$ fold commutes
with the four-row rotation, and that after rotation the two binary folds
