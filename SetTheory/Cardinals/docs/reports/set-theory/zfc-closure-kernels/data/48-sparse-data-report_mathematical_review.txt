INTERNALIZATION

1. Both consistency directions are correct. The literal execution bridge
identifies halting with a native refutation, so nonhalting gives Con(N).
Countable completeness, the native-to-ZF interpretation, and the
constructible-model interpretation then give a ZFC model and Con(ZFC).
Conversely, Con(ZFC) gives a coded ZFC model; theory inclusion and the
ZF-to-native obligation give a native model, Con(N), and nonhalting.
No unconditional model-existence or self-consistency assertion is made.

2. The four project-specific internal ZFC hypotheses remain explicitly
conditional. The arithmetic graph theorem is not presented as proving
the register/table simulation, native/ZF comparison, or Choice transport.
The existing external Lean theorem is treated as a blueprint rather than
as an automatic reflection theorem into ZFC.

3. Axiom certificates correctly avoid assuming that the unannotated
range of an arbitrary recursively enumerable axiom presentation is
decidable. Closed certified axioms and finite proof conditions give the
intended fixed consistency predicates. Countable Henkin completeness
uses ordinary sets, without transitive-model existence or external
well-foundedness assumptions.

4. Model satisfaction and the all-axiom clauses quantify over ambient
formula codes. The text correctly distinguishes these from a model's
potentially nonstandard internal syntax. Constructibility is selected by
a fixed formula inside a set-coded model; no global truth predicate for
the ambient universe or proper class L is used. The uniform inner-model
axiom discharge remains a separate obligation.

5. RESOLVED CLARIFICATIONS: the least-representative minimum now explicitly
ranges over b in D; the displayed E* relation includes a,b in D*; and
the L(A) satisfaction identity explicitly restricts to well-formed formula
codes and assignments into D_L. These guards make the displayed formulas
literal relations on the stated domains.

6. Guarded witness search correctly gives ZFC-provably-total general
recursive translators only after the internal witness implications have
been established. It makes no primitive-recursive extraction claim.
The final distinction between consistency and the stronger soundness
needed to exclude false existential halting proofs is appropriate.

ARITHMETIC GRAPHS

7. Pairing, bounded beta lookup, initial invalid row, strict sortedness,
all twelve cases, and the separate acceptance/rejection endpoints agree
with the exact numerical evaluator. Missing dependencies cannot silently
become valid. Redundant correct interior rows are soundly permitted.

8. All quantifiers after the leading existential blocks are bounded,
including beta-division witnesses bounded by their own B parameters.
The existential W is chosen above every actual and prospective circuit
value. In particular, a rejected MP comparison with valid premises is
not incorrectly bounded by its stored zero result. Choosing W before the
two CRT encodings introduces no circular bound on their B parameters.

9. Functionality and finite-history induction establish soundness; total
evaluation plus finite CRT encoding establish existence. Acceptance and
rejection are therefore complementary Sigma_1 presentations of the exact
relation. The statement that these finite arithmetic proofs can be
carried out in PA is justified by primitive-recursive course-of-values
evaluation and finite sequence coding. The report correctly distinguishes
this ordinary representability proof from delivery of a formal PA proof
or an arbitrary-model proof-assistant package.

SPARSE BOUNDS

10. The bit-growth proof includes zero premises and invalid input codes.
The differences n^2 and (u-v)^2 establish N(N+2)<=3 bits(c). The cache
bound is correctly limited to canonical certificates. Height, total bit
mass, squared bit mass, and the unfolded-family sharpness claim follow
with the stated qualifications.

11. The stronger exponent D=2^23 is justified by the bundle tag and its
nonzero valid payload, the confirmed depth 23, and the GEN induction.
The fixed-circuit constants are explicitly acknowledged as enormous.
The prospective rejected-MP bound is sufficient for the quadratic
bit-model algorithm. Direct linear storage is not confused with CRT
witness length or with a smaller binary machine state count.
