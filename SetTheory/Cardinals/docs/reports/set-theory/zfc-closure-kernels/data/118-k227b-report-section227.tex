\]

operation names the physical decoder of the outer node and its inner child.
The certificate checks that all three chunks are closed: no break, continue,
or halt escapes the chunk. It also compares the complete ordered operations

\subsection{A rejected intermediate and the repair}
Without the fourth rotation, the proposed 227-state table has a seven-zero
instruction ending at a decrement of register~2. Its full runtime proof fails
at source site 1042, on the zero guard, after 1,358 proved cases. A complete
finite-frame replay confirms the scanner-contract violation. Passing source
prefix macros and blank startup did not suffice. This raw table is not part
of the verified candidate set.

The fourth rotation removes the seven-zero defect while preserving the
repaired context tags. All finite whole-frame cases then pass, followed by a
fresh unbounded proof of the repaired table. The changed K176/1 row is also
used as a runtime helper; it is explicitly covered by this proof rather than
assumed harmless because its decoder use works.

head, state relation, and nonhalting of intermediate steps. The independent
