\section{Alternative formalisms and where their costs occur}
\label{sec:formalism-routes}

The search considered both mathematical axiom systems and their concrete
machine encodings. The successful state reduction preserves the calibrated
theory. The following comparison explains why a shorter-looking axiom
list cannot be converted directly into a subtraction from the machine's
state count, and identifies a precise obstruction for one especially
attractive simplification.

\subsection{The compact theory already used by the machine}

The inherited native theory has equality congruence, the power-container
axiom CP, the Global-Image Collection schema GIC, and irreflexivity IR.
In readable notation, with raw membership and
$x\subseteq y$ abbreviating $\forall z(z\in x\to z\in y)$, these are
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{CP}:\quad
 &\forall p\,\exists V\,
 \bigl(p\in V\ \land\
   \forall x\in V\,[x\subseteq V\ \land\
      \exists W\in V\,\forall z(z\subseteq x\to z\in W)]\bigr),\\
\mathrm{GIC}_{\varphi}:\quad
 &\forall B\,\exists C\,
 \bigl(\forall a\in B\,\forall y\,
   [\varphi(a,y)\to\exists y'(\varphi(a,y')\land y'\in C)]\\[-2pt]
 &\hspace{43mm}\land\ \forall y\in C\,\exists x\,\varphi(x,y)\bigr),\\
\mathrm{IR}:\quad &\forall x\,\neg(x\in x).
\end{align*}
The GIC schema permits arbitrary formula parameters; its support clause
has an unrestricted input $x$. This precise placement matters to the
published compact syntax. Extensionality and the usual ZFC axiom list
are not supplied literally to the enumerator. The inherited interpretation
recovers ZF on a bisimulation quotient of raw sets, and the opposite
direction is obtained inside a constructible model. These are the
published calibration arguments, not new axiomatization claims in this
report.\cite{upstream-settheory,calibration}

The upstream set-theory development also proves the equiconsistency of
$S_0=\mathrm{CP}+\mathrm{GIC}$, without IR, with ZF. Nevertheless, simply
deleting IR is not an executable optimization for the present proof
enumerator. Its falsity code is the atom $v_0\in v_0$, and the soundness
of that alias depends on irreflexivity under every assignment, including
assignments changed by a binder of $v_0$. The exact gated axiom bundle
also permits the input-label collision $a=b$, whose soundness uses the
same diagonal property. A different theory can therefore have the right
consistency strength while requiring a different formula coding and
proof-calculus bridge.\cite{upstream-logic,upstream-settheory}

The latter two are investigated design routes rather than new measured
constructions. This report assigns them no machine count. A universal
interpreter advertised with a small control count is insufficient here
unless the particular theory and program are also generated from the
blank tape within the counted table. Likewise, an equiconsistency proof
alone gives no bound on the compiled cost of its proof checker.

The already calibrated CP/GIC engine offers a particularly useful
separation of concerns: source transformations can preserve every proof
operation while the binary decoder is compressed independently. The
successful 238 composition takes advantage of that separation, with a
fresh interface check where the two optimizations interact.

\subsection{The fourth parameter is a precise research target}

The axiom constructor emits
\[
 L(a,b,c,u)=((c\to u)\to a)\to((a\to c)\to(b\to c)),
\]
the four-parameter implicational schema from the published logical
basis. Its fourth argument is read separately from the proof stream.
Replacing it by a term in the other three parameters could remove
concrete source work. The earlier reports explored several such
specializations and diagnostic compiler outputs.\cite{companion239,upstream-logic}

The new theorem below covers every finite implication term
$U(a,b,c,F,\top)$, including terms containing falsity and of arbitrarily
large size. It shows that none recovers the full classical logical
subsystem under the specified retained rules. Its proof partitions all
terms into five possible leaves and the implication-root case; a
four-valued matrix handles the latter uniformly because implication
never returns the one value needed to falsify the specialized axiom.
A second matrix handles the remaining leaf.

This result gives useful information about a concrete compression
proposal. It does not bound the possible consistency strength of every
modified calculus, and does not prevent a different translation of
classical formulas. The conditional model construction in
Section~\ref{sec:colored-model-addendum} strengthens the obstruction for most replacements by
adding the exact CP/GIC bundle.
