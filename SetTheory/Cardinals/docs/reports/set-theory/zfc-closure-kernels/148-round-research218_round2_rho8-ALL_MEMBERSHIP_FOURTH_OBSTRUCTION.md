# Stronger obstruction: every membership-headed fourth parameter, including F

Date: 2026-10-20. Status: new ordinary mathematical proof, independent review requested. No received code, proof checker, or formula enumeration was executed. No proof-assistant or machine-runtime endpoint is claimed.

## Theorem and immediate relevance

Retain every native K219 row except unrestricted rho8, the full gated seed/support bundle, truth 0, MP, and GEN. Admit instead every rho8 instance

`H(a,b,c;v)=I(I(I(c,v),a),I(I(a,c),I(b,c)))`

whose fourth code v is membership-headed, meaning `v=R(l,m)` for arbitrary independent natural labels l,m.

**ZFC proves consistency of this entire enlarged calculus.** It also proves that no implication with a membership-headed immediate antecedent is derivable. In particular neither native `F→F` nor `F→truth` is derivable.

This covers the union `U=F` plus `U=R(b,c)`, all six membership orientations and their union, every code-dependent builder whose output always has residue 1 modulo 4, and unions of any such builders. It closes the proposed relative-truth route at `tau=I(F,F)`: tau is not an available theorem, and it has not been identified with raw truth 0.

The key improvement over the previous invariant is **not to admit arbitrary identities**. They were harmless for the narrower proof but obscure the stronger restriction here. Truth 0 remains explicitly admitted and retains its genuine alias `I(0,0)=0`.

## 1. Literal retained syntax and elementary arithmetic

Write `I(x,y)=4P(x,y)`, `R(x,y)=4P(x,y)+1`, `Q_i x=4P(i,x)+2`, and `E(i,j)=4P(i,j)+3`. Let `F=1=R(0,0)` and `N(x)=I(x,F)`.

All four constructors are numerically injective in their two fields and have disjoint residues. Also `I(x,y)>=x,y`, `Q_i x>x`, `I(x,y)=0` iff x=y=0, and every membership code is positive. For the retained rho6 antecedent put

`C_i(a)=Q_i N(Q_i a)`,

`X_i(a)=N(C_i(a))=I(Q_i I(Q_i a,F),F)`.

Then `C_i(a)>a` and `X_i(a)>a` for all natural a.

The retained rows are exactly

`r1(i,j)=I(Q_i N(E(i,j)),F)`,

`r2(i,x,y)=I(Q_i I(x,y),I(Q_i x,Q_i y))`,

`r3(a,b,c)=I(E(a,b),I(R(c,a),R(c,b)))`,

`r4(i,j,x)=I(Q_i Q_j x,Q_j Q_i x)`,

`r5(a,b,c)=I(E(c,a),I(R(c,b),R(a,b)))`,

`r6(i,a)=I(X_i(a),a)`,

`G(a,b,u)=I(E(u,a),I(E(u,b),Q_(R(a,b)) E(a,b)))`.

As before, safely strengthen the exact full seed bundle to every

`Bundle(a,b,c;k,z)=I(I(V(a,b,c),Q_k z),F)`,

where `V(a,b,c)=I(R(a,b),Q_c R(a,b))`. The literal nks222 core is Q_c-headed, so its exact entire body is an instance of z. Omitting the gate inequalities and allowing every Q-headed core is an overapproximation, not a weakening or projection of the seed.

## 2. The finite-witness class S

For membership codes v,w, define

`p(c;v)=I(c,v)`,

`h(b,c;w)=I(I(w,c),I(b,c))`,

`D(b,c,d;v,w)=I(I(h(b,c;w),p(c;v)),I(d,p(c;v)))`.

Also define the rho6-trigger output family

`M(i,a,b)=I(I(a,C_i(a)),I(b,C_i(a)))`.

Let S have finite witnesses generated by:

1. Truth 0.
2. Every literal retained row and every strengthened Bundle.
3. Every H(a,b,c;v), for arbitrary a,b,c and membership code v.
4. Every D(b,c,d;v,w), for arbitrary parameters and membership v,w.
5. Every M(i,a,b).
6. GEN: S(x) implies S(Q_i x).
7. Quantified monotonicity: S(I(x,y)) implies S(I(Q_i x,Q_i y)).

There is **no MP constructor and no unrestricted identity constructor**. Monotonicity, D, and M are auxiliary overapproximations, not new rules silently added to the target calculus.

Every S output has residue 0 or 2. Every Q-headed witness must end in GEN, hence can be stripped. Every I-headed S generator other than truth has immediate antecedent with residue 0, 2, or 3, never 1. The truth alias has immediate antecedent 0. These are last-generator facts, before any MP closure is proved.

## 3. Three inherited forbidden shapes

No S witness has any of these forms:

`I(E(i,j),F)`,

`X_i(a)=I(Q_i N(Q_i a),F)`,

`I(V(a,b,c),Q_k z)`.

The previous retained-row exclusions apply directly. For completeness, truth cannot equal any target because each is positive; GEN has the wrong outer tag. H, D, and M all have I-headed immediate antecedents and I-headed immediate consequents, so they fail the first two shapes by the antecedent tag and the third by the consequent tag. Monotonicity has Q antecedent and Q consequent. For the first target, r3/r5/G have I consequents rather than F; the other retained antecedent tags mismatch. For the second, the only possible r1 match would force E(i,j)=Q_i a; the other rows mismatch a tag. For the third, the only free-consequent case r6 would match its Q-headed first inner field to R(a,b), impossible. Bundle itself has consequent F. Thus the exclusions are independent of MP.

## 4. A new forbidden shape for the M family

For every i,r define

`Z_i(r)=I(r,N(Q_i Q_i r))`.

There is no S witness of Z_i(r). Inspect its last constructor:

- Truth 0 cannot match because N(Q_i Q_i r) is positive.
- GEN has outer Q tag.
- Monotonicity and r4 have Q-headed consequents, whereas Z's consequent is I-headed.
- r1 and Bundle have consequent F, whereas Z's consequent is I-headed.
- r2 has consequent I(Q_j x,Q_j y); matching its right field with F is impossible.
- r3/r5 have consequent I(R(...),R(...)); matching its first field with Q_i Q_i r is impossible.
- G has consequent I(E(...),Q_(...)(...)); matching its right field with F is impossible.
- H has consequent I(I(a,c),I(b,c)); matching its right field with F is impossible.
- D has consequent I(d,p(c;v)); matching its right field with F is impossible, because p is I-headed.
- M has consequent I(b,C_j(a)); matching its right field with F is impossible, because C is Q-headed.
- The sole remaining case r6(j,x) forces `x=N(Q_i Q_i r)` and `r=X_j(x)`. But `x>=Q_i Q_i r>r`, and `X_j(x)>x`, so this forces r>r.

This exhausts S. No monotonicity, substitution, or MP rule is used to establish the exclusion.

## 5. Every possible H detachment

An H(a,b,c;v) major requires the minor `I(I(c,v),a)`, with v membership-headed and positive.

Truth cannot be that minor: its left field I(c,v) is positive. GEN has the wrong outer tag. Monotonicity and r1/r2/r4 have Q antecedents; r3/r5/G have E antecedents. The remaining cases are:

- Bundle: matching its inner Q core to v is impossible.
- D: matching its I-headed p to v is impossible.
- M: matching its Q-headed C_i(x) to v is impossible.
- r6(i,x): matching gives a=x, c=C_i(a), and v=F. The H conclusion is exactly M(i,a,b).
- H(x,y,z;w): matching gives `c=I(z,w)`, `x=v`, and `a=I(I(v,z),I(y,z))`. The H conclusion is exactly D(y,z,b;w,v).

These are the only possible outputs. Unlike the earlier identity-enlarged invariant, no J family is needed or generated.

## 6. Every D major is inert

For a D(b,c,d;v,w) major write p=I(c,v), h=I(I(w,c),I(b,c)). Its required minor is I(h,p).

Truth cannot match because p is positive. GEN and all Q/E-antecedent generators mismatch tags. The remaining cases are:

- Bundle has consequent F rather than I-headed p.
- r6(j,x) would force x=p and X_j(p)=h; matching their right fields gives F=I(b,c), impossible.
- Any H minor has consequent I(I(x,z),I(y,z)); equality with p forces I(y,z)=v, impossible.
- Any D minor has consequent I(e,p(z;u)); equality with p forces its I-headed p(z;u)=v, impossible.
- Any M minor has consequent I(e,C_j(x)); equality with p forces Q-headed C_j(x)=v, impossible.

There is no identity case because identities are not generators. The cases above exhaust S.

## 7. Every M major is inert

An M(i,a,b) major requires an S minor `I(a,C_i(a))`.

Truth and GEN fail by positivity or outer tag. Rows r1 and Bundle have consequent F rather than Q. Rows r2/r3/r5/G and all H/D/M families have I-headed consequents rather than Q. Two retained cases remain:

- r4(j,k,x) would give `a=Q_j Q_k x` and `C_i(a)=Q_k Q_j x`. Injectivity forces k=i and `N(Q_i a)=Q_j x`, an I/Q collision.
- r6(j,x) forces `x=C_i(a)` and `a=X_j(x)`. Since C_i(a)>a and X_j(x)>x, this is impossible.

Finally a monotonicity witness can match only with label i and `a=Q_i r`. Its premise would then be exactly

`S(I(r,N(Q_i Q_i r))) = S(Z_i(r))`,

excluded in Section 4. Thus no M major has an available minor. This last monotonicity branch is the essential new check; simply arguing from root tags would be incomplete.

## 8. MP closure and actual consistency

Induct on an S witness for a major I(u,v), keeping an arbitrary S(u) witness as parameter.

- If the witness is truth 0, numerical injectivity of I(0,0) gives u=v=0, and S(v) is truth.
- A GEN witness cannot conclude an I-headed major.
- For a monotonicity witness, strip Q from the minor, apply the induction hypothesis to its strictly smaller premise witness, and rebuild Q by GEN.
- r1 needs a Q-prefixed first forbidden shape; strip Q and use Section 3.
- r2 strips Q and uses the admitted monotonicity constructor.
- r3/r5/G need equality-headed minors, absent by the output-tag invariant.
- r4 strips and rebuilds two Q prefixes in exchanged order.
- r6 and Bundle need the second and third forbidden shapes.
- H yields an admitted D or M by Section 5.
- D and M cannot fire by Sections 6 and 7.

All branches are covered, so S is MP-closed. Finite induction on actual native derivations embeds each theorem into S: truth, retained axioms, the literal complete bundles, GEN, and the restricted H rows are all admitted, and MP is now proved closed. Since F has residue 1, F is absent. The proof is finite constructor arithmetic and witness induction formalizable in ZFC.

Moreover, the immediate-antecedent invariant from Section 2 implies that no actual theorem can equal I(v,x) for membership-headed v, by injectivity and tag disjointness. In particular tau=I(F,F) and code4=I(F,0) are not theorems. The absence of unrestricted identities was essential to seeing this stronger obstruction, not an omission of a native axiom: Thm222 has no identity rule.

## 9. Scope

Assuming ZFC is consistent, no subcalculus covered by this theorem can have consistency provably equivalent to Con(ZFC) in ZFC. This includes F+Rbc and arbitrary unions of membership-headed fourth builders, not merely the six fixed orientations. All other native rows and the entire support-bearing K bundle are covered.

The theorem does not cover a fourth builder that sometimes produces a nonmembership code, a genuinely unrestricted extra propositional basis, or an additional identity axiom schema. It does not claim that every such extension is calibrated. It also does not supply a machine runtime or a physical-state lower bound.

A translation using another truth code would have to be defined and proved separately; the particular tau=I(F,F) is unavailable as a theorem, and the entire calculus already has a ZFC proof of consistency. Thus no alternative truth convention can rescue a ZFC-provable reverse calibration for this exact calculus.
