## Additional results in this follow-up

1. A complete reconstruction of the MathOverflow closure theory without
   Foundation or Choice, including the singleton bridge and an Infinity
   proof using the total successor operation rather than a prior notion of
   the set of natural numbers.
2. Extensionality + Separation + rooted subset-bounded closure is
   deductively equivalent to foundation-free ZF. A gated singleton decoder
   derives transitive containment before Union is available.
3. Extensionality + one exact-fiber hull schema absorbs all separate
   set-existence axioms, Separation, and Replacement. A converse proof
   collects unique exact fibers rather than choosing local bounds.
4. One profile-congruence sentence gives a directly calibrated equality-free
   version. Its syntax control is smaller than the comparable extensional
   container–collection control, but larger than the aggressive raw
   nonextensional prior theory in the declared template metric.
5. Independent regression experiments test formula expansion and finite
   semantics. These are not proofs of the equivalence theorems.

No historical-priority claim is made. These are the additional developments
relative to the supplied package, not a claim of an exhaustive literature
novelty search.

## Simplifications that are not justified

**Change element-seeded closure to rooted closure without a bridge:** the
one-object empty-membership structure satisfies Extensionality, Separation,
and all element-seeded closure instances, but not rooted closure. It fails
Power Set, so it is not a countermodel to the actual MathOverflow theory.

**Make exact fibers merely containing fibers:** the one-object full-relation
structure satisfies every weakened instance and Extensionality. Exactness
is supplying the Separation strength and cannot simply be removed.

**Use member-bounds instead of subset-bounds in exact-fiber hulls:** ZFC
proves that H_(omega_1) is a set model of the resulting theory. Assuming
ZFC is consistent, that theory cannot be PA-equiconsistent with ZFC, by the
second incompleteness theorem. This argument adapts the attachment's
member-bounded-image obstruction to the new hull schema.

**Delete EC, transitivity, or a machine's irreflexivity gate:** not proved
safe in this package. They have different logical roles and cannot be
removed merely because one of the other presentations does not state them.
