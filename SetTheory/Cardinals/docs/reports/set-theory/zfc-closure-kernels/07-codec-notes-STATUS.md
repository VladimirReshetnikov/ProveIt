## Why finite graph tests do not prove consistency

The graph tests check specified combinatorial constructions on finite
relations. They do not assert that the tested structures satisfy the entire
closure schema. The calibrated theory derives Infinity. An exhaustive scan
of tiny graphs is not a consistency proof and cannot verify all matrix
instances of an infinite schema.
