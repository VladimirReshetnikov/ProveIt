# Literature and status record

Research/status check: 19 September 2026.

## Selected published question

Sergio Abriola, Simon Halfon, Aliaume Lopez, Sylvain Schmitz, Philippe
Schnoebelen and Isa Vialard, *Measuring well-quasi-ordered finitary powersets*,
arXiv:2312.14587v2 (17 July 2024).

- Record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14587
- Versioned text: https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14587v2
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.14587v2

The extension question appears in Section 6, printed page 23, under
“Elementary wqos.” That PDF page was visually inspected. The current arXiv
record exposed v2 as its latest listed version at the time of the check.
The question concerns extending the family while retaining computable ordinal
invariants. The present article chooses finite-poset lexicographic gluing as a
specific instance and gives a conditional maximal-type rule plus an exact
uniform height result. It does not purport to answer every part of that broad
research direction.

## Background

D. H. J. de Jongh and Rohit Parikh, *Well-partial orderings and hierarchies*,
Indagationes Mathematicae 39(3), 195–207 (1977).

This is the classical attribution for attained maximal linearizations and the
natural ordinal operations associated with disjoint unions and products.
The bibliographic details and relevant attributions were cross-checked in the
primary papers cited in the article. The 1977 paper is also indexed under the
parallel proceedings numbering, Series A 80(3).

Mirna Džamonja, Sylvain Schmitz and Philippe Schnoebelen, *On Ordinal Invariants
in Well Quasi Orders and Finite Antichain Orders*, Trends in Logic 53, 29–54
(2020); updated preprint arXiv:1711.00428v5 (28 January 2025).

- https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00428

Used for the standard invariant framework. The article does not rely on the
older, subsequently corrected general lexicographic-product assertion.

## A nearby problem already treated in the literature

Isa Vialard, *On maximal order type of the lexicographic product*, Logic Journal
of the IGPL 33(4), jzaf051 (2025), published 17 July 2025.

- https://doi.org/10.1093/jigpal/jzaf051
- https://academic.oup.com/jigpal/article/33/4/jzaf051/8205767

The final publisher text states and proves a formula for uniform lexicographic
products. It supersedes a stale status statement found in an earlier arXiv
version. The current project does not claim that the uniform lexicographic
product problem is still open. Its appendix recovers the finite uniform case
as a consequence of a finite nonuniform block calculation.

## Novelty boundary

Targeted searches combined the terms “finitary powersets,” “lexicographic,”
“maximal antichains,” “maximal order type,” and related formulations. No source
containing the specific frontier formulas was located. This is not an exhaustive
literature review or a guarantee that the formulas are unpublished. The article
therefore gives complete arguments and explicitly leaves priority unverified.

The powerset nonfunctionality phenomenon is already established in the selected
paper. The small examples in this package illustrate it; they are not described
as its first discovery.
