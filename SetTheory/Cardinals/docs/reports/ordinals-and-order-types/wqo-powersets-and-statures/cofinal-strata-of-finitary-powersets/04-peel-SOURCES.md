## Novelty limits

Searches concerned finitary powersets, lexicographic gluing, nonuniform height,
and persistent coordinates. Search quality was uneven. No verified antecedent
for the exact persistent-coordinate theorem was identified, but this is not
proof of novelty. The defensible claim is an explicit, proved extension beyond
the inspected repository report. No priority or independently established
literature-wide breakthrough is asserted.
