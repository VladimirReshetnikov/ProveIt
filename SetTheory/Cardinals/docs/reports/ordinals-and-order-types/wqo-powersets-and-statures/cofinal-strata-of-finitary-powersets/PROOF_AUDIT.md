# Proof dependency and scope audit

## Standard inputs

The argument uses maximal-linearization attainment for WPOs, the natural-sum
formula for disjoint unions, the natural-product formula for Cartesian
products, induced-subposet monotonicity, and elementary Cantor-normal-form
arithmetic. These are attributed to the classical literature in the article.
Finitary Hoare powersets of WPOs are WPOs. No claimed new result depends on an
unproved general powerset formula.

## Assumptions that must not be suppressed

**Finite index.** Finitely many smaller monomials have natural sum below a
larger pure power. Infinitely many can reach it. The grouping proof is finite.

**Pure stratum types.** Cofinality does not preserve arbitrary maximal types.
A cofinal omega+1 can gain an additional finite term from another point below
its top. The absorption lemma requires a pure power.

**No internally completed fibers.** Canonical frontiers require that a whole
fiber cannot be generated by finitely many points in that fiber. In the main
maximal-type theorem this follows automatically from o(K(P_q)) being a positive
pure power: a finite cofinal set would give K(P_q) a greatest element and hence
a successor maximal type.

**Cofinality is not universal comparability.** Two ordered frontier strata can
share active coordinates. A large shared coordinate in the lower stratum need
not be below a small shared coordinate in the higher stratum. The proof uses
constructed cofinal witnesses rather than silently treating strata as a
lexicographic sum.

**Uniformity for height.** The lower-bound construction varies a coordinate
which is completed at the next frontier stage. Equal fibers guarantee that
this coordinate has the full required type. The explicit nonuniform
counterexample shows what fails when the largest coordinate persists.

**Inclusion-maximal versus maximum-cardinality antichains.** All
inclusion-maximal antichains index strata. Only the leading coefficient counts
maximum-cardinality antichains. Confusing these notions deletes necessary
strata in fork examples.

**Empty downset.** It belongs to the least-frontier stratum; it is not an extra
stratum. The empty skeleton has singleton K(empty) and is handled separately.

**Ordinal operation discipline.** The maximal-type exponent uses natural
multiplication rho natural-product k. The height uses ordinary multiplication
alpha*k. The implementation names natural operations explicitly.

## What the computations establish

The delivered run passed 216,386 assertions. It checks the finite frontier
construction, weighted survivor identities against a separate subset-DP block
ordering calculation, profile inclusion against Hoare domination, explicit
cofinal witnesses on sampled profiles, recursive series-parallel identities,
and the counting bijection. It also checks CNF arithmetic and the appendix
formula against expanded pure blocks.

These checks do not establish the validity of a proof for all infinite WPOs,
maximal-linearization attainment, or novelty. Infinite upper bounds are justified
by the written proofs. The source was not formalized in Lean or another proof
assistant, and the transfinite arguments were not independently peer reviewed.

## Claims intentionally not made

There is no general ordinal-width formula here, no nonuniform height formula,
no unrestricted iteration theorem for K, and no replacement of the published
full elementary-WQO calculus. The reusable pure-output criterion concerns
using a constructed lexicographic sum as a subsequent fiber, not automatically
using its finitary powerset as a fiber. The literature search is documented but
cannot certify priority.
