# Source and literature notes

Checked during preparation on 19 September 2026.

## 1. The chosen open question

Jean Goubault-Larrecq and Bastien Laboureix,
*Statures and sobrification ranks of Noetherian spaces*.
Houston Journal of Mathematics 49(1) (2023), 1–76.

Public version consulted:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06828v5
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.06828

The arXiv v5 submission is dated 24 February 2023; the title page of its
PDF bears 27 February 2023. The public preprint has 68 pages, different
from the 76-page journal pagination.

Publisher table of contents confirming journal metadata:
https://www.math.uh.edu/~hjm/Vol49-1.html

Locations in the public preprint:

* Section 15, Question 1, printed page 63 (zero-based PDF page 62): full
  spectrum question and whether omega^||X|| is attainable at every infinite
  input stature. The page was inspected as an image as well as parsed text.
* Proposition 11.2: old lower and upper bounds.
* Proposition 11.4: equality of statures for finitary and full constructions.
* Theorem 10.9: product formula ||X x Y|| = ||X|| natural-product ||Y||.
  This is the one substantial imported theorem used for T1 sharpness.
* Example 11.9: related finite-support successor construction.

## 2. Decisive earlier wqo work

Sergio Abriola, Simon Halfon, Aliaume Lopez, Sylvain Schmitz,
Philippe Schnoebelen, Isa Vialard,
*Measuring well-quasi-ordered finitary powersets*.

Version consulted: arXiv:2312.14587v2, 17 July 2024.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14587v2
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.14587

* Theorem 3.3, PDF page index 8 / printed page 9, gives
  1+o(A) <= o(P_f(A)) <= 2^o(A).
* Theorem 4.3 gives sharp upper-bound witnesses at every ordinal.
* The proof of Theorem 3.3 uses the same finite / infinite-successor /
  limit organization as the proof in the new manuscript. This parallel
  is credited explicitly; it is not presented as a wholly original
  proof strategy or as discovery of the binary bound in wqo theory.

The arXiv version, rather than unverified final journal bibliographic
metadata, is used in the bibliography.

## 3. What the searches did and did not establish

Targeted searches included combinations of “Noetherian”, “Hoare”,
“stature”, “binary”, “spectrum”, and the exact paper titles. Relevant
primary sources were read, including images of the original open question
and the wqo upper-bound theorem. Many broad search results were irrelevant.

The search did not locate an explicit published general-Noetherian proof
of the binary bound or the finite-tail classification in this package.
This is a limited negative search result, not a priority certificate or
proof that no such result exists. Unpublished work and incompletely
indexed literature were not exhaustively ruled out.

No third-party source papers are copied into the archive; the URLs above
identify the sources and versions.
