# Sources and status check

Consultation date: **19 September 2026**. These are provenance notes, not proof certificates. Complete bibliographic entries are also embedded in the article.

## Selected open question

Sergio Abriola, Simon Halfon, Aliaume Lopez, Sylvain Schmitz, Philippe Schnoebelen, and Isa Vialard, *Measuring well-quasi-ordered finitary powersets*.

- Version used: arXiv:2312.14587v2, 17 July 2024.
- Record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14587
- HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14587v2
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.14587v2
- Section 6, subsection “Intrinsic Weakened Invariants”: source of the selected research question.
- Definition 1.1: elementary constructor class.
- Definitions 5.6 and 5.8: syntactic approximations and weakened invariants.
- Theorem 5.10: height of the finitary powerset equals weakened maximal order type on elementary orders.
- Section 5.2: existing ideal-completion argument that motivates this attack.

The question is broader than the note's result. The cap example refutes the note's own universal ideal-embedding identity, not a conjecture stated by these authors. Be careful with notation: the source sometimes uses an arrow for a reflection, while the new relation in the note requires preservation and reflection.

## Ideal-completion background

Jean Goubault-Larrecq, Simon Halfon, Prateek Karandikar, K. Narayan Kumar, and Philippe Schnoebelen, *The Ideal Approach to Computing Closed Subsets in Well-Quasi-orderings*, Trends in Logic 53 (2020), 55–105.

- Record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.10703
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.10703
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-30229-0_3

The article proves the elementary ideal identities it needs; this reference supplies context and avoids claiming established ideal theory as new.

## Milner–Rado arithmetic and topological comparison

Jacob Hilton, *The topological pigeonhole principle for ordinals*.

- Version used: arXiv:1410.2520v4, 12 July 2016.
- Record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.2520
- HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/1410.2520v4
- Definition 2.19 and Theorem 2.20: Milner–Rado sum and its finite CNF calculation.
- Theorems 1.1, 4.5, 4.6, and 4.7: the closed, open, and mixed monomial topological pigeonhole formulas.

The powerset-height formulas are derived independently in the note. The numerical pigeonhole comparison uses Hilton's results and is restricted to countable positive exponents. The height formulas themselves do not have this countability restriction.

## Publication-status and novelty limitations
