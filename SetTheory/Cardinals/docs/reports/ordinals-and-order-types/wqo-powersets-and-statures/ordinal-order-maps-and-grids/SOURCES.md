# Sources and locators

Consulted on 19 September 2026. These are literature locators, not a claim
that all subsequent or related literature has been exhausted.

## Published motivating question

Sergio Abriola, Simon Halfon, Aliaume Lopez, Sylvain Schmitz, Philippe
Schnoebelen, Isa Vialard, *Measuring well-quasi-ordered finitary powersets*.

- Version used: arXiv:2312.14587v2 (2024).
- Abstract and version metadata: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.14587v2
- Full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14587v2
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.14587v2
- Open direction: Section 6, final paragraph headed “Elementary wqos.”
- Context: Section 5, exact calculations for the selected elementary family.
- Existing nonfunctionality results: introduction and Section 3; the present
  article does not claim to discover nonfunctionality in general.

The PDF conclusion was visually checked in addition to reading the HTML.
No copy of the paper is included in this archive.

## Standard maximal-order-type results

D. H. J. de Jongh and R. Parikh, *Well-partial orderings and hierarchies*,
Indagationes Mathematicae 39(3), 195–207 (1977).

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/1385-7258(77)90067-1
- Theorem 2.13: maximal linearization is attained.
- Theorem 3.5: maximal type of a Cartesian product is natural product.
- The accessible survey below was used for theorem statements and locators;
  the original article is credited for the results.

## Accessible survey

Mirna Džamonja, Sylvain Schmitz, Philippe Schnoebelen,
*On Ordinal Invariants in Well Quasi Orders and Finite Antichain Orders*.
Published in Trends in Logic 53 (2020), pp. 29–54; consulted preprint v3 (2024).

- https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00428v3
- https://arxiv.org/html/1711.00428v3
- Theorem 3.4: maximal linearization.
- Lemma 4.2: disjoint sums.
- Lemma 4.5: Cartesian products.
- Lemma 4.6: rank of a point in a Cartesian product.

Only these standard ingredients are imported. In particular, no
lexicographic-product maximal-order-type identity from the survey is used.

## Search scope and limitation

Searches included variants of “maximal order type isotone maps,” “maximal
order type finite poset ordinal maps,” and related finitary-powerset terms.
They did not establish priority for the formulas of this manuscript.
Priority is explicitly left unconfirmed rather than inferred from a failed
keyword search. The selected grid problem is our concrete restriction of a
published open direction, not a verbatim conjecture attributed to the authors.
