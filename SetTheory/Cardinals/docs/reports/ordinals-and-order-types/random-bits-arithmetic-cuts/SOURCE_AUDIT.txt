SOURCE AUDIT
============
Report: Random Bits, Finite Catalogs, and Arbitrary Cuts of Arithmetic
Research/source inspection date: 5 October 2026

1. DIRECT CONNECTION TO ELLIOT GLAZER
-----------------------------------
Elliot Glazer, Coin flipping on models of arithmetic to define the standard
cut. MOPA seminar, 17 October 2023. Official abstract:
https://nylogic.github.io/mopa/2023/10/17/coin-flipping-on-models-of-arithmetic-to-define-the-standard-cut.html

The abstract announces a uniform-formula fair-coin standard-cut result for
countable models of PA, even Q, and raises further questions for uncountable
countably saturated models. The present report reconstructs the PA case
using its own binary-coding formula. The public abstract does not identify
Glazer's particular formula. The report does not claim the Q generalization
or a solution to the uncountable/measurability problems. No personal
endorsement, current professional role, or private preference is inferred.

3. FINITE SOURCE CODING
----------------------
Ioannis Kontoyiannis and Sergio Verdu, Optimal lossless data compression:
Non-asymptotics and asymptotics. IEEE Transactions on Information Theory
60(2) (2014), 777-795.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2013.2291007
Preprint title: Lossless Data Compression at Finite Blocklengths.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.2668
https://arxiv.org/html/1212.2668v1

Shuqing Chen, Michelle Effros, and Victoria Kostina, Lossless source coding
in the point-to-point, multiple access, and random access scenarios.
IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 66(11) (2020), 6688-6722.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2020.3005155
https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.03366
Official repository record checked for publication details:
https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/5wn11-wae69

Optimal ranking by atom probability and the fixed-bias normal expansion
are classical. The sparse Poisson staircase is derived here directly from
Hamming-layer ranking and binomial-to-Poisson convergence; it is not
presented as a newly discovered general source-coding theorem.

4. LIMITED INDEPENDENCE
----------------------
Itai Benjamini, Ori Gurel-Gurevich, and Ron Peled, On K-wise Independent
Distributions and Boolean Functions.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.3261
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1201.3261
Proposition 25, equation (12), gives the exact pairwise atom formula.
Substitution of p=1/2 yields 1/(N+1) for odd N and 1/(N+2) for even N.
The arXiv upload is 2012; the abstract identifies an earlier manuscript
available on authors' websites since 2007. The report uses conservative
dating and explicitly treats this extremum as established work.

Their cited source is Endre Boros and Andras Prekopa, Closed form two-sided
bounds for probabilities that at least r and exactly r out of n events
occur. Mathematics of Operations Research 14(2) (1989), 317-342.
That bibliography and its relevance were checked through Benjamini et al.;
the original Boros-Prekopa paper was not independently inspected.

6. SEARCH AND NOVELTY LIMITS
---------------------------
Focused repository searches for random predicates and coin flipping did
not return an existing treatment of the selected problem. This was not
an exhaustive review of the full repository or all of its archives.
Focused public literature checks support the attributions above. Failure
to find an exact formulation is not proof of historical novelty.

The article's stronger arbitrary-bias, prescribed-cut, countable Boolean,
finite-marginal, and induction statements stand or fall by their proofs.
They are proposed research contributions, with no established-priority
claim and no claim that a named published open problem has been settled.
