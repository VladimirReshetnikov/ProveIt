## Novelty search and limits

Targeted searches covered combinations of countably generated filters, filter character, regular-open completion, pseudointersections, sheafification, and distributivity. They did not identify this exact bounded-character package in the inspected sources. That search was not exhaustive and does not establish priority.

The p, h, and u invariants, Boolean completion, closure-to-distributivity, mixing, and the classical countable diagonal are not presented as new inventions. The proposed contribution is their precise bounded-character interaction here, especially the two-sided comparison obstruction and its connection to completed Fréchet models.

Only the new manuscript and its support files are packaged. No third-party PDFs, repository source files, or font files are redistributed.
