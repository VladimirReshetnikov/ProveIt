### Already in that report

- Two values suffice for failure of the reduced-power sheaf condition.
- The failure persists on the countably generated free-filter subsite.
- Separatedness, finite pasting, and a finite-Boolean-quotient local criterion.
- On the full site in ZFC, sheafification is the product of ultrapowers.
- The full binary map is the inclusion of Stone clopens into the power set of the ultrafilter set, not an order-dense Boolean completion.
- The row–tail closed set is not clopen and is not necessarily nowhere dense.

### What the present article adds relative to that inspected text

- The explicit Booleanization of the countably generated site.
- The hierarchy indexed by modulo-finite generating character.
- An exact p threshold for marked Boolean completion and for complete event-preserving comparison.
- The ultrafilter-character atom/point spectrum and the separate atomicity criterion.
- An intermediate-atomicity application of a published forcing model.
- The specialized mixing sheafification at every stage, with finitary transfer and a sharp infinitary gap.
- The regular-global-ring and primitive-idempotent consequences.

The repository-specific extension should not be mistaken for certified novelty in the broader literature. Other repository projects were not audited theorem by theorem.

## Novelty limits

The article does not announce a new value or independence theorem for p or u. It does not prove or need p = t. The principal density threshold is a classical pseudointersection characterization applied to a specified site; the atom threshold is a character calculation. The original contribution being proposed is the assembled site hierarchy, its complete-comparison obstruction, the explicit smaller-site/sheaf-model calculation, and the associated applications.

Targeted searches did not establish a publication containing this exact combined treatment. That does not prove that it is new: equivalent statements may occur under other terminology in work on Boolean completions, modified Stone spaces, or ultrafilter character. No source author was contacted and no article was submitted on the user's behalf.

The further research list is a program of precise proposed questions, not a claim that every entry is a documented unsolved problem in the literature.
