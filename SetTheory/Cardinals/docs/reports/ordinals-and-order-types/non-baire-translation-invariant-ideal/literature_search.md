Date of investigation: September 20, 2026.

This file records a targeted source check, not an exhaustive bibliographic

prove that it remains unresolved today. No definitive priority claim is made.

## Selected primary source

Rafał Filipów and Jacek Tryba, *Densities for sets of natural numbers vanishing on
a given family*, Journal of Number Theory 211 (2020), 371–382.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2019.10.015
- arXiv record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.00982
- Fixed-version full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2309.00982v1
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.00982
- Author preprint: https://mat.ug.edu.pl/~rfilipow/papers/2020-JNT-preprint.pdf

The arXiv submission history identifies v1, posted September 2, 2023; the journal
reference is 2020. An automatically rendered date in an HTML version should not
be interpreted as a new author revision without checking the submission history.

Question 1 was checked in the full text and in a screenshot of PDF page 7
(zero-based page index 6). It asks for a translation-invariant ideal lacking the
Baire property with a continuum-sized I-TAD family. The surrounding definitions
require the sets to be I-positive and require the translated intersections of
distinct members to belong to I.

The relevant established tools in the source are the ideal category criterion
(Theorem 1.7), Plewik's countable-intersection result (Theorem 1.9), the rich
abstract density construction from a continuum TAD family (Theorem 3.1), and
translation cores (Proposition 3.8). The note supplies its own direct category
proof for the constructed ideals and explicitly credits the density mechanism.
