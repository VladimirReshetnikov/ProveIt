2. IMPORTANT PRIOR ART

Todd Ebert, Wolfgang Merkle, Heribert Vollmer,
On the Autoreducibility of Random Sequences,
SIAM Journal on Computing 32(6) (2003), 1542-1569.
https://doi.org/10.1137/S0097539702415317
https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2002/056/

Theorem 6.6 and its proof already contain Hamming-code prediction,
coordinatewise XOR of repeated rounds, and arbitrary slowly vanishing
densities of correct partial predictions. These ingredients are explicitly
credited in the article.

Readable final text was also inspected in the author upload:
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220975426_On_the_Autoreducibility_of_Random_Sequences

The hosting page describes an earlier conference version, but its uploaded
text is visibly the 2003 SIAM final paper with the three authors and DOI.

The original theorem permits abstention and uses sequence-dependent finite
hardwiring to remove errors. The present article provides one fixed
compulsory strategy, accepts finitely many bad finite blocks, and proves
its exact scores and prefix behavior directly.
