FINITE VERIFICATION NOTES
=========================

Reproduce the checks
--------------------
Python 3.10 or later is sufficient. No third-party packages are required.

    python3 verify_finite.py

The program writes results.json beside itself. To choose another location:

    python3 verify_finite.py --output /path/to/results.json

The JSON has no timestamps or random sampling output. Its rational numbers
are exact strings, such as "3/4"; integer quantities remain JSON integers.

What is checked
---------------
1. Activated early-stopping Eldredge blocks:
   Every hat assignment and every effective activation mask is checked for
   m=1,...,5 and alpha=1/2,1/4. Biased masks carry exact integer weights.
   The operational query transcript is checked for owner blindness and
   fresh inspections, and against the independent Boolean block formula.
   Checks include the all-black surplus law -Binomial(m,alpha), the
   singleton gain probability alpha*m*4^(-m), neutral configurations,
   nonbad completed-pair monotonicity, and bounds at every logical prefix.
   Exact query means, partner-conditioned means, and geometric tails are
   checked. The m=1 case is an intentional edge case; charged query counts
   follow the displayed algorithm, even when a Boolean rule simplifies.

2. Public gating:
   A single public gate, independent of the hats and private activations,
   is off with probability epsilon=alpha. Off-gate players guess zero
   without querying; on-gate players use the activated block.
   Exact per-owner means are checked against

       S: (1-alpha)*(1+alpha*(1-2^(-(2*m-2))))
       T: 1-alpha.

   Both are at most one. This is one shared gate for the whole strategy;
   the program makes no assertion of almost-sure success for these gates.

3. Finite sign and variance inequalities:
   Constant guesses, neutral pairs, privately thinned pairs, activated
   blocks, and public-gated blocks are enumerated. The centered score,
   variance bounds, general zero-query sign inequality, and cost-only
   inequality are checked with exact fractions. The stronger private
   inequality is checked only when independent private seeds apply.

4. Owner-indexed Fourier accounting:
   The integer Walsh transform is used, without floating-point arithmetic.
   Every deterministic activation profile for blocks m=1,...,4 is checked.
   There are also 32 fixed synthetic profiles for each ambient size n=1,...,5
   and parity teams. Synthetic profiles are selected by the specified
   xorshift64 stream with seed 0x20261004C0FFEE. Their full hat truth tables
   are then checked exhaustively; no statistical inference is drawn.

   Every scored prefix is checked, so later hats can lie outside the scored
   set. The checks compare direct scores with aggregated owner coefficients,
   compare Fourier influence with direct coordinate derivatives, and verify
   Parseval, total owner energy, support multiplicity, and the critical
   low-degree estimate Var(F) <= 4*Var(P_{<=2}F)+3*K whenever the displayed
   exact slack K=max(0,total_influence-number_of_scored_owners) applies.

Executed result
---------------
All 499,552 logical checks passed:

   10 activated-block strategies: 74,896 weighted hat/seed state cases.
   10 public-gate strategies:     77,624 weighted hat/seed state cases.
   15 additional strategies:       8,946 weighted hat/seed state cases.
   195 deterministic Fourier profiles; 691 scored prefixes, 445 with K=0.

"State cases" counts effective hat/mask combinations before probability
weights are applied; it is not the number of underlying fair-bit strings.
The detailed exact laws, costs, and prefix energy data are in results.json.

Limits of the certificate
------------------------
These are finite algebraic and algorithmic checks. They do not prove the
infinite schedule, Borel--Cantelli conclusions, supermartingale convergence,
almost-sure divergence, or nonattainment theorem. Those assertions require
the proofs in the article. The verification is supplementary evidence of
the finite identities and implementations used in those proofs.
