ESTABLISHED INGREDIENTS

Eldredge's Proposition 6.6 supplies the rare-loss paired block rule.
The present article restates it and gives a partner-first early-stopping
implementation. The block's existence is not claimed as a new invention.
Eldredge's Remark 6.8 discusses rates; the earlier repository report also
contains stronger unrestricted growth constructions.

Boolean hypercontractivity and the standard one-sided sign bound are
classical. The article provides an elementary proof of a weaker version
and explicitly attributes the sharper bound to:

RESULTS DEVELOPED AND PROVED IN THE ARTICLE

* A finite-color obstruction that gives nullity for measurable rules and
  meagerness for Baire-measurable rules, including infinite neighborhoods.
* The uniform finite adaptive-query impossibility and conditional
  finite-sign estimates.
* Sparse Fourier-support label bounds and the exact one-query optimum.
* A robustness bound in terms of aggregate high-degree Fourier mass.
* The exact deterministic expected-query threshold 2.
* The optimized block's exact cost law, geometric tails, and moments.
* Exact asymptotic equivalents for the displayed block schedules.
* The count-budget classification under eventual positivity, with sparse
  placement and computability details.
* Simultaneous count/radius sufficiency under h(i) -> infinity, together
  with an explicit separation for a merely unbounded radius profile.
* Correctness independence under acyclic directed visibility.
* Private-randomness sufficiency for every expected cap C > 1.

The statement "proved in the article" describes the proofs supplied in
this report. It does not establish first publication, worldwide novelty,
or recognition as a breakthrough. Literature comparison covered the
directly relevant primary sources and repository manuscripts, not an
exhaustive priority review.
