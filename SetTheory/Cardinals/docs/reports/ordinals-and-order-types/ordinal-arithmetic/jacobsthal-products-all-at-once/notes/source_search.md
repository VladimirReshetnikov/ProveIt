# Source and literature-search record

Search date: 20 September 2026.

## Primary problem source

Harry Altman, *Intermediate arithmetic operations on ordinal numbers*,
Mathematical Logic Quarterly 63 (2017), 228–242.
DOI: 10.1002/malq.201600006.

```text
https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.05747
https://arxiv.org/html/1501.05747v9
https://doi.org/10.1002/malq.201600006
```

The inspected author version is v9, dated 18 September 2017. Question 2.16
asks for an all-at-once interpretation of finite and infinitary Jacobsthal
products. Theorem 2.10 contains the classical binary CNF formula. Theorem 2.13
contains classical finite associativity. Theorem 2.14 states infinitary laws.
Question 5.2 asks for order-theoretic proofs of further comparisons. The article
addresses only its binary multiplication inequality portion.

## Directly relevant related source

Paolo Lipparini, *An infinite natural product*, Annales Mathematicae Silesianae
32(1) (2018), 247–262.

```text
https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.06908
https://arxiv.org/html/1703.06908v2
https://journals.us.edu.pl/index.php/AMSIL/article/view/13924
```

Version 2 was inspected. Section 3.2 recalls the ordinary finite-support model;
Section 3.3 states Carruth's finite natural-product maximum theorem; Section 3.4
explains why unrestricted infinite finite-support linear extensions are not
the appropriate maximum. Lemmas 2.4–2.5 use leading-monomial estimates for
countable natural products. The operation studied there is natural multiplication,
not the Jacobsthal construction proposed in the manuscript.

## Classical references

Ernst Jacobsthal, *Zur Arithmetik der transfiniten Zahlen*, Mathematische Annalen
67 (1909), 130–143. Bibliographic details and the relevant classical statements
were verified through Altman's primary exposition. The manuscript supplies its
own proof of the binary formula, rather than claiming to have independently
transcribed the 1909 article.

Philip W. Carruth, *Arithmetic of ordinals with applications to the theory of
ordered Abelian groups*, Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society
48 (1942), 262–271. Its publication record was located; the exact finite-product
maximum theorem used here is explicitly restated in Lipparini's Section 3.3.

## Search scope and limit

Targeted searches included the phrase “Jacobsthal multiplication,” Altman's
paper title, infinitary Jacobsthal products, order-theoretic interpretation,
and all-at-once associativity. Related natural-product and recent infinitary
semigroup work was also inspected. Some multi-phrase searches produced noisy
results; conclusions were based on the identified primary sources, not on
unrelated search snippets.

No later resolution of the selected question was located. This is not a claim
that every relevant paper, language, repository, thesis, citation database,
or unpublished manuscript has been searched. Priority and the current status
of the informal word “natural” remain matters for external expert assessment.
No third-party source PDFs are redistributed in this archive.
