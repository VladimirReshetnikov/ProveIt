# Source and problem-status audit

Consultation date: September 19, 2026.

## Primary source used for the problem and external theorem

Paolo Lipparini, *Monotone infinitary operations on ordinals (extended version)*,
arXiv:2505.00424v2, April 30, 2026.

- Versioned abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00424v2
- Versioned HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2505.00424v2
- Versioned PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.00424v2
- Latest-version history: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00424

The arXiv history consulted lists v2 as the latest revision. The manuscript
identifies itself as an extended version of *A Monotone Infinitary Operation
on Ordinals*, Mathematical Logic Quarterly 72, no. 2 (2026),
DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/malq.70019 .

Relevant locations in v2:

- Definition 2.4, equations (2.1) and (2.2e): weak monotonicity and e-special
  strict monotonicity.
- Theorem 2.6.a(A): existence of the least operation N.
- Definition 3.1: the evaluated operation S.
- Theorem 3.4: monotonicity and minimality of S.
- Remark 6.1: S fails e-special strictness; small checks for N, including the
  constant-omega value, are already known.
- Problem 6.2: asks for an explicit definition and study of N. The problem was
  confirmed in the HTML and visually on printed page 29 of the PDF.
- Appendix II: a direct proof of the minimality part of Theorem 3.4.

## What this audit establishes

The consulted latest arXiv manuscript explicitly poses the problem. Targeted
searches conducted during preparation did not identify a separate solution.
This makes it a documented open-problem target for the present attempt.
