# Source review and problem-selection notes

Review date: 20 September 2026.

## Why this problem was selected

Isa Vialard's thesis *Measuring well quasi-orders and complexity of verification*
(Universite Paris-Saclay, 2024), printed page 110 (PDF page index 109), identifies
the ordinal measurement of the finite undirected graph-minor ordering as a
promising unresolved direction. That page was checked in both parsed text and
its rendered page image.

Source: https://isavialard.github.io/home/mwqo.pdf

The broad research program concerns graph-minor orders and their labeled
embedding variants. The present manuscript specializes to **unlabeled finite
simple degree-two graphs** and related restrictions. It does not assert that
this exact specialization was itself posed as an open conjecture in the thesis.
The source establishes the broader motivation, not priority of our special-case
formulas.

Targeted searches considered combinations of graph minors, maximal order types,
ordinal width, paths, cycles, linear forests, and component bounds. They did not
establish historical novelty or exhaust the graph-minor literature. No claim
that a formula is previously unpublished should be inferred from this archive.

## An apparent open problem rejected after publication checking

The lexicographic-product maximal-order-type question initially looked suitable
because an older proof had been marked incorrect. Later publication checking
found:

Isa Vialard, *On maximal order type of the lexicographic product*, Logic Journal
of the IGPL 33(4), jzaf051. Published online 17 July 2025; revision and acceptance
24 June 2025.

https://doi.org/10.1093/jigpal/jzaf051
https://academic.oup.com/jigpal/article/33/4/jzaf051/8205767

The publisher's page gives a formula and proof. Consequently this was not
retained as the open target. An older withdrawn version is insufficient evidence
that the question remains open.

## Interpretation of the final manuscript

The graph-specific formulas are derived and proved in the manuscript, with the
external dependencies explicitly separated. Whether these are new, rediscovered,
or immediate but unrecorded consequences of existing work has not been settled.
The paper is a concrete partial attack with exact results, not a claim to solve
the ordinal measurement of the full graph-minor order.

No full third-party paper or font file is included in the archive. Bibliographic
links are supplied for verification of the source material.
