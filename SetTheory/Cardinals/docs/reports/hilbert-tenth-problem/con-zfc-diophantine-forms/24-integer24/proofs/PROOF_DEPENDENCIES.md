# Proof dependencies and the verification boundary

The mathematical target is an internal ZFC equivalence:

`not exists solution <-> Con(ZFC)`.

The construction proves `exists solution <-> blank K215 halts <-> a finite
ZFC refutation exists`, then negates. It does not assume consistency in
proving that equivalence and does not prove consistency as a conclusion.

   endpoint `blank_halts_iff_zfc_refutation` and its negation. The complete
   Lean dependency graph is not rebuilt or bundled here.

   `upstream/wang943_language.md`, `wang943_compact_layout.md`,
   `fixed_blank_67_proof.md`, `blank_helical_proof.md`, and
   `ordinary_blank_proof.md` supply the unchanged tile language and the

3. **Signed and odd canonical witnesses.**
   `upstream/signed_domain29.md` and `odd_scale27.md` provide the signed
   Pell-rank argument, negative-scale exclusion, and canonical odd/square
   witness choices. The new signed root norm must recover their hypotheses
   before using their conclusions.
4. **Existing comparison polynomials.** `norm12_proof.md`,
   `monic_merger_proof.md`, and `retained59_proof.md` establish the source
   forms. Exact algebraic refactorings preserve them. Their new coefficient
   binding uses the full adapter proved in this report.

All inherited source bytes are pinned by `evidence/SOURCE_PINS.json`.
The archive's `NOTICE.md` and `licenses/` retain their attribution.

## New mathematical steps

- **Rational Pell saturation and separating weights.** Section 2 proves
  the integrality lemma, the pre-root bounds, conjugate separation,
  integral main quotient recovery, and the uniform degree cancellations
  for the degree-1069 and degree-1073 forms.
- **Signed root recovery.** Section 3 permits signed scales and auxiliary
  coordinates, excludes `w=0` and `T=0` without dividing by a potentially
  zero expression, recovers all integer roots, and then invokes the signed
  source theorem. Six positive guards plus six unrestricted coordinates
  produce 24 integer unknowns.
- **Exact arithmetic refactoring.** Section 4 factors a common odd
  component in a quadratic algebra. The identity is polynomial and
  introduces no divisions, even when that component vanishes. The two
  constant-shift cancellations in the integer lift save two further gates.
- **Complete coefficient replacement.** Section 5 proves Boolean coverage
  of the actual modulus `d*N` using the explicit prime 452453, tunes the
  actual index `R`, proves the selected cells do not wrap, and preserves
  the square and odd canonical restrictions. A check only modulo `N`, or
  a partial replacement of the four ports, would not suffice.

Separate AI-assisted reviews checked the degree-1069 estimates, the signed
boundary argument and leading certificate, and the coefficient controller
against its inherited padding source. They are mathematical review
activities, not independent human peer review or proof-assistant checks.

## Finite checks and what they establish

- The literal 215-state, 430-action table is re-enumerated into 943 tiles.
- The 989 native positions, carry threshold, 69,230 basis convolution
  cases, rotation isolation, unique anchor alignment, population and
  residue identities are checked.
- The four actual gzip payloads are independently decoded and audited.
- The prime, multiplicative orders, lifting valuation and unit residues
  are verified by exact integer arithmetic.
- The free-polynomial norm identities and weight/domain factorizations
  are checked coefficient by coefficient over the integers.
- All delivered DAGs are checked for prior-defined inputs, valid gates,
  liveness, operation counts, coherent coefficient binding and provenance.
- Independent conjugate-product evaluations and full univariate
  finite-field evaluations corroborate the emitted graphs. Finite-field
  calculations are diagnostics, while the displayed uniform leading
  forms prove exact degree for the authentic fixed tuple.

No finite check searches for or establishes an accepting K215 run. The
universal compiler and arithmetic equivalence remains a mathematical
proof obligation, addressed by the stated new and inherited proofs.
