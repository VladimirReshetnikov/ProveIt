# Explicit Diophantine formulations of Con(ZFC)

Research contribution prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov / ProveIt, 22 October 2026.

**Start with `report.pdf`.** Its editable source is `report.tex`. The exact
coefficient recipe, complete polynomial circuits, handwritten proofs,
independent review, reproducible scripts, and pinned source dependencies
are included in this directory.

## Main result

Each delivered construction has a solution exactly when the fixed first-order
proof calculus for ZFC has a finite refutation. The equivalence is proved by
ordinary mathematics formalizable in ZFC. Therefore ZFC proves that absence
of solutions is equivalent to `Con(ZFC)`.

| Construction | Strictly positive unknowns | Equations | Degree | Arithmetic operations |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Recommended ordinary system | 16 | 7 | 28 | 64 |
| Its sum of squared residuals | 16 | 1 | 56 | 84 |
| Normalized product | 16 | 1 | 157 | 74 |
| Reduced auxiliary product | 16 | 1 | 125 | 75 |
| Ordinary auxiliary product | 16 | 1 | 101 | 76 |
| Eliminated-root polynomial `P15` | 15 | 1 | 295 | 82 |
| Eliminated-root comparison system | 15 | 6 | 244 | 71 |

For a system, the degree is the maximum degree of the expanded residuals.
Operation counts include every supplied binary `+`, `-`, or `*` gate,
including multiplication by fixed constants; for systems they compute the
comparison sides. Fixed-coefficient construction is not included in this
evaluation cost. Registers in these circuits are polynomial abbreviations,
not additional unknowns.

The new reduction fixes the input marker `W = 2^(2d+b)`, removes the universal
input Pell block, and proves positive restoration of its deleted witnesses.
It saves two unknowns and ten arithmetic operations relative to the chosen
universal parent circuits. This is a fixed-input specialization, not a new
universal-input complexity bound or a claim of global minimality.

## Concrete coefficients

The source is pinned to ProveIt commit
`cdd582bec13f908df3711fa9e05d1ddac368a5db`.

The exact 215-state table is `source/k215.tm`, with SHA-256
`fdbcef0825886035c3091c8537a69535bc96f9e265f0ae45863543d40ae70090`.
The normalized folded machine has 436 states and six symbols; its full
2,616-transition table is included. The window alphabet has 2,628 tiles.

The coefficients are **specific finite integers**, defined by finite sums
over the exact local predicate and fixed numerical ranges. No unknown
recursively enumerable index, unbounded coefficient search, hypothetical
halting time, or refutation witness is left to choose. However, their digit
expansions are not materialized: the fixed base is already `B = 2^(5^85)`.
The six circuit ports have the exact meanings in `coefficient_recipe.json`;
in particular `MF = MF_native + B - 1`.

## Domain conventions

Main witnesses range over strictly positive integers. Replace each by `n+1`
for a formulation over nonnegative integers with the same number of unknowns.

The exact polynomial `Pos(a,b,c)=1+a²+b²+c(c+1)` maps `Z³` onto the positive
integers. The archive also includes:

- a 45-integer-variable polynomial of degree 590;
- a 48-integer-variable seven-equation system of maximum degree 56;
- its single sum-of-squares polynomial of degree 112;
- 87 explicit quadratic equations in 128 unrestricted integer variables;
- their single sum-of-squares polynomial of exact degree four.

## Reproduce the checks and PDF

Python 3.9 or newer, standard library only:

```sh
python3 source/verify.py
```

The driver is read-only unless `--receipt PATH` is supplied. It verifies the
pinned inputs, regenerates the finite machine and coefficient recipe, regenerates
all circuits and domain variants, compares the generated bytes with the
delivered artifacts, reruns the independent source audit, and compares the
displayed formulas against the circuits on diagnostic integer assignments.
It uses temporary storage and requires no network or ProveIt checkout.

To rebuild the PDF with a standard TeX Live installation:

```sh
python3 source/render_circuits.py
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error report.tex
```

To regenerate individual artifacts:

```sh
python3 source/compiler_k215.py --table source/k215.tm --out artifacts
python3 source/fixed_marker.py --root vendor --out artifacts
python3 source/review_fixed_marker.py --upstream vendor --artifacts artifacts --output checks/independent_review.json
python3 source/domain_variants.py
```

## Proof and verification status

The report supplies a mathematical proof of the new reduction and identifies
its exact inherited compiler/Pell lemmas. `proofs/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md`
reviews the rank interface, the positive input-witness restoration, and the
signed-root issue. `proofs/FORMAL_STATUS.md` explains the actual ZFC proof:
the K215 calibration includes first-order completeness, quotient and
submodel arguments, and the constructible-model transfer from ZF to ZFC,
as well as finite simulation. No global soundness or conservativity theorem
for Lean or Rocq is assumed.

The inherited K215 endpoint has reported Lean and Rocq verification.
This submission does **not** include a new end-to-end kernel-checked
Diophantine proof, an encoded derivation in ZFC's own proof syntax, or a
rerun of the inherited full assistant builds. Its executable checks verify
finite source, arithmetic, and layout claims. The historical compiler
correctness remains an ordinary mathematical proof dependency.

No consistency assumption is needed for the equivalence. If ZFC is consistent,
the equations have no standard solutions, and ZFC cannot prove their lack of
solutions by the second incompleteness theorem. Consistency alone does not
justify a two-sided independence claim.
