\begin{abstract}
We compare the seven audited wave-4 packages in ProveIt's collection and
combine their complementary constructions. The largest improvement is a new
complete fixed-coefficient encoding of the same 943-tile blank computation
language, with $B=2^{1,012,115}$ instead of $2^{1,953,125}$. Its four materialized
integer ports total 506,664 bytes instead of 977,220, a 48.15\% reduction.
Port-specific label widths and a shared synchronization source marker are
combined with mixed-radix clauses and an explicit prime-padding controller.
Independently, exact algebra lowers the 12-positive-variable degree-1081
polynomial from 111 to 109 binary operations, and its three-equation system
from 107 to 103. The degree-1069 alternative drops from 123 to 120 operations.
The 31-variable quartic is combined with constant folding and a factored
coefficient evaluator to reduce its nine-variable presentation from 1840 to
1587 operations over $\N$, or 1582 over $\Pos$, preserving its degree.
All recommendations are concrete integer polynomials or systems with finite,
halting-independent coefficient recipes. Composing the supplied reductions
with the inherited compiler and calibrated K215 theorem proves in ZFC that
their lack of solutions is equivalent to $\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$.
No global optimality or new end-to-end proof-assistant certificate is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{What was improved, and what ``simplest'' means}

The requested property is an internal provable equivalence, not a consistency
proof. With $\operatorname{Ref}_{\mathrm{ZFC}}$ denoting the usual arithmetized
assertion that a finite ZFC refutation exists, our target is
\[
 \mathrm{ZFC}\vdash
 \bigl(\exists\mathbf x\in D^n\;P(\mathbf x)=0\bigr)
 \longleftrightarrow \operatorname{Ref}_{\mathrm{ZFC}},
 \qquad D=\N\text{ or }\Pos.
\]
Negation gives exactly the user's requested statement. The domains are part of
each construction: $\N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and $\Pos=\{1,2,\ldots\}$ throughout.
None of our positive-domain equations is silently interpreted over all of $\Z$.

There is no established single minimum simultaneously in variable count,
degree, arithmetic evaluation size, and coefficient height. A nine-variable
equation can have astronomical degree; a 67-operation expression has more
variables but a far shorter evaluator. Accordingly, the report gives an
explicit small collection of choices, with precise improvements on the current
repository rather than an unsupported assertion of an absolute optimum.

\section{The logical chain and its verification boundary}

The common semantic chain is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \text{polynomial/system has a solution}
 &\longleftrightarrow \text{marked periodic Wang943 tableau exists}\\
 &\longleftrightarrow \text{literal K215 halts on blank tape}\\
 &\longleftrightarrow \text{a finite ZFC refutation exists}.
 \end{split}
\]
The first arrow includes the Pell and population-count compiler, not only a
finite tile lookup. The final arrow has an inspected formal endpoint in
\path{Computability/BusyBeaver/Lean/BusyBeaver/ZFC/KEndpoint215.lean}, namely
\texttt{blank\_halts\_iff\_zfc\_refutation}. Its stated theorem relates
\texttt{BlankHalts machine215} to the syntactic bounded-proof predicate for
ZFC refutation. A provenance copy is included in \path{proofs/}.

The present work did not rebuild the complete Lean or Rocq developments.
Nor does it use the unqualified soundness of Lean as a theorem inside ZFC.
The internal equivalence is obtained by the ordinary arithmetical proofs,
which are formalizable in ZFC; the existing formal endpoint is corroborating
and reusable mathematical infrastructure. The new norm identities and
coefficient adapter are ordinary proofs plus finite certificates, not a new
kernel-checked end-to-end development.

\begin{theorem}[Combined consistency presentation]\label{thm:main}
Bind each recommended arithmetic circuit to the complete coefficient tuple of
Section~\ref{sec:coeff}. For the nine-variable circuits, also use their
reconstructed quartic index and all fixed coefficient recipes, rather than
those of the previous tuple. Then ZFC proves that the circuit's output has no
zero in its stated domain, or its displayed system has no simultaneous
solution, if and only if $\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The new coefficient adapter proves the same soundness and canonical
completeness interface as the inherited blank compiler. The identities of
Section~\ref{sec:norm} prove exact equality of the positive polynomial templates,
and the shifted transport equations preserve existence of positive solutions.
The inherited separating-root arguments therefore apply to those circuits.
The small-operation baseline circuits use the unchanged inherited interface
directly. For the nine-variable circuit, the retained quartic has the same
zero-existence predicate, the coefficient bounds define a valid Jones index,
and the new evaluator computes the same parametric Jones polynomial under
those fixed leaves. Jones's reduction supplies the remaining equivalence.
Composing these arithmetic equivalences with the calibrated machine theorem
and negating gives the assertion. No choice in the coefficient construction
depends on whether the machine halts.
\end{proof}

If ZFC is consistent, its second incompleteness theorem prevents ZFC from
proving the displayed no-solution assertion. We make no unconditional
two-sided independence claim: ruling out an erroneous existential proof
requires an appropriate soundness assumption, not consistency alone. This
logical distinction does not weaken the requested provable equivalence.

\section{A twelve-variable polynomial with a shorter evaluator}\label{sec:norm}

\subsection{Complete definition}

All twelve supplied variables are strictly positive integers:
\[
 J,F,\alpha,z,h,i,T,s,w,\eta,\zeta,t.
\]
All symbols introduced next are polynomial abbreviations, not extra variables.
Let $\beta,K,M_C,M_F$ be the four fixed integers of
Section~\ref{sec:coeff}, with $M_F$ already shifted. Define
\begin{align*}
 q&=\beta J+1,& X&=wq,&Y&=sq^3,&E&=XY,\\
 k&=\eta+\zeta,&c&=kY+\eta,&a&=E+Y,&H&=4a+3,\\
 \Delta&=a^2+H,&\ell&=EkY,&A&=1+\ell(\ell+k),&G&=1+\Delta c^2,\\
 b_0&=X+ac,&C&=q-F-\alpha.
\end{align*}
Next put
\begin{align*}
 R&=((q-1)(q-F)+\alpha)(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\\
 N_k&=k-hE-R,\\
 N_t&=(K+w)C+q-F-z(q-1),\\
 I&=ic^2,\qquad D=1+\Delta I^2,\qquad Q=\Delta^2I^2,
 \qquad d_0=Q-1,\\
 b&=c+RD,\qquad P=QD c^2T^2,\qquad U=Qb^2.
\end{align*}
Here $d_0$ is unrelated to the fixed coefficient exponent $d$ below. For the
short evaluator choose
\[
 M=k(I^2+T^2),\qquad L=1+HM,\qquad W=b_0+Ht.
\]
The following is an entirely division-free evaluator:
\begin{align*}
 n&=(HM)^2A,&D_n&=d_0 n,\\
 H_P&=H^2P,&H_U&=H^2U,\\
 H_e&=H_P+H_U-H^2,&j_0&=4H_PH_U,\\
 \mathcal T&=2d_0(W^2+L^2G),&
 K_r&=d_0(W^2-L^2G),\qquad \mathcal K=K_r^2,\\
 A_A&=2(D_n+H_e),&c_0&=(D_n-H_e)^2-j_0,\\
 a_t&=\mathcal T-A_A,&b_t&=c_0-\mathcal K,\\
 c_1&=16D_nj_0,&
 v&=c_1-a_t(a_t\mathcal T+2b_t),\\
 u&=b_t^2-a_t^2\mathcal K,&
 N&=\mathcal K v^2-u(\mathcal T v-u).
\end{align*}
The recommended one-equation form is
\begin{equation}\boxed{\quad P_{109}=N_kN_t(1+N)-1=0.\quad}\label{eq:p109}
\end{equation}
The literal binary schedule, including shared intermediates and operations by
2, 4, and 16, is \path{positive/circuits/short109_product.json}.
Its exact total degree is 1081 and its evaluator has 109 operations.

For the system, use a shifted positive transport coordinate $z$, corresponding
to $z_{\rm old}-1$ in \eqref{eq:p109}. The recommended three equations are
\begin{equation}\boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 k-hE-R&=1,\\
 (K+w)C&=F+z(q-1),\\
 \mathcal K v^2&=u(\mathcal T v-u).
 \end{aligned}}\label{eq:s103}
\end{equation}
The supplied variables are still exactly the twelve listed above. This system
has 103 operations and largest residual degree 1072. Replacing a zero-output
test by a comparison is accounted for explicitly; it is not an uncharged
subtraction concealed in the ledger.

\subsection{Why the shorter norm is exactly the previous polynomial}

For a formal argument $z_0$ define
\[
 \mathcal F(z_0)=
 \bigl[(d_0z_0^2)^2-A_A(d_0z_0^2)+c_0\bigr]^2-c_1(d_0z_0^2).
\]
If $g^2=G$, the two expressions $x_\pm=d_0(W\pm Lg)^2$ satisfy
\[
 x_++x_-=\mathcal T,\qquad x_+x_-=\mathcal K.
\]
Reduce modulo the monic quadratic $x^2-\mathcal T x+\mathcal K$:
\begin{align*}
 x^2-A_Ax+c_0&\equiv a_tx+b_t,\\
 (a_tx+b_t)^2-c_1x&\equiv u-vx.
\end{align*}
Consequently
\begin{align*}
 \mathcal F(W+Lg)\mathcal F(W-Lg)
 &=(u-vx_+)(u-vx_-)\\
 &=u^2-\mathcal Tuv+\mathcal Kv^2\\
 &=\mathcal Kv^2-u(\mathcal Tv-u)=N.
\end{align*}
This is an identity in integer polynomial rings. In particular, it remains
valid when radicands vanish, become squares, or coincide. No division or
generic nonsingularity assumption is involved.

To identify the inner expression with the inherited separating norm, put
$e=P+U-1$, $B_a=2Q(cT)b$, and $m=HM$. The identity
$j_0=H^4DB_a^2$ follows directly. Taking the product for
$\tau=\pm\sqrt A$ in
\[
 [d_0(z_0-m\tau)^2-H^2e]^2-H^4DB_a^2
\]
produces $\mathcal F(z_0)$. Taking the remaining product for
$g=\pm\sqrt G$ is exactly the preceding calculation. Thus the shorter
evaluator computes the original complete norm, rather than an approximation
or a polynomial merely agreeing at tested points.

There are two elementary savings. Computing
\[
 \mathcal K=[d_0(W^2-L^2G)]^2
\]
uses one fewer multiplication than separately computing $d_0^2$ and the
other square. Reversing the sign of the linear remainder coefficient costs
nothing, and the final form $\mathcal Kv^2-u(\mathcal Tv-u)$ uses six gates
rather than the seven gates of the expanded three-term norm. In a system its
final subtraction is unnecessary, saving one more gate. Finally,
\[
 \bigl[(K+w)C+q-F-(z+1)(q-1)\bigr]-1
 =(K+w)C-F-z(q-1)
\]
gives the transport saving. The inherited compiler bounds imply $C>0$,
$w>q>F$ at a solution, and hence $z_{\rm old}\ge2$. The shifted coordinate
therefore stays strictly positive in the reverse direction.

\subsection{The semantic root argument is preserved}

The arithmetic refactoring does not alter the delicate inverse argument.
For orientation, the inherited short weight has the two strict separations
\[
 2(cT\sqrt D+b)<MkY^2
 <\min\{M\sqrt A,M(\sqrt G-\sqrt A)\}.
\]
The outer transport and index equations supply the size hypotheses before
any missing roots are recovered. Every complex root of the inner polynomial
lies in the disk $|x|<M\sqrt G$. If $G$ were nonsquare and the outer norm
vanished, its two quadratic conjugates would give roots farther apart than
that disk allows, because $L=HM+1$. Thus $G$ is an integer square. The same
separation selects the positive main root, and rational recovery plus Pell
saturation recovers the remaining integer witnesses.

For the product equation, unit signs also matter. The norm satisfies
$N\equiv\square\pmod4$, so $1+N$ cannot be $-1$. A zero of
\eqref{eq:p109} therefore forces $N=0$ and gives the two outer factors a
common sign. The inherited index argument removes the remaining negative
case. This proof retains the initially possible $C=0$ boundary until the
signs have been settled. The archive includes the full predecessor proof,
not just this outline, in
\path{positive/upstream/POSITIVE12_PROOF.original30.md}.

\subsection{The degree-1069 alternative}

Keep every definition above, but replace the separating weight and carrier by
\[
 C_\sharp=E+2(X+Y)+sq^2,\qquad
 M=I(2T+RC_\sharp ic),\qquad L=1+2HM,\qquad W=Lb_0+Ht.
\]
The same trace/product evaluator now computes package 24's deliberately
cancelling norm. It has 120 operations for the degree-1069 product and 114
for the shifted three-equation system, whose maximum degree is 1060.
Its existence theorem is inherited by exact polynomial identity and the
same transport substitution.

The exact nonzero leading homogeneous form of this norm is
\[
 9\,2^{48}i^{48}(\eta+\zeta)^{96}w^{76}s^{176}
 (\beta J)^{648}(\beta J-F)^{16},
\]
of degree 1060. The outer leading degrees are 7 and 2, giving 1069.
For the short norm the leading term is $H_{e,\mathrm{top}}^8$, of degree
1072; the other expanded terms have smaller degrees. Its product therefore
has exact degree 1081. These arguments hold uniformly for every authentic
$\beta>0$, rather than relying on a generic syntactic degree bound.

\section{A smaller complete coefficient encoding}\label{sec:coeff}

\subsection{Finite language and fixed numerical parameters}

The tile language is the same ordered list of 943 Wang tiles as in the source
packages. It represents the literal blank-tape computation of K215, performs
its actual first transition during initialization, and has distinguished
Start and impossible Forbidden tiles at selector positions 0 and 1. The
list is reconstructed from the 430 literal transitions and compared with its
fixed SHA-256. Horizontal labels range up to 249; vertical labels range up
to 438. Hence horizontal ports need eight identifier bits plus occupancy,
whereas vertical ports need nine identifier bits plus occupancy.

Choose
\[
 b_c=115,\quad L_c=8801,\quad d=b_cL_c=1,012,115,
 \quad \rho=2^{115},\quad B=\rho^{8801}=2^d.
\]
The four ports, always bound together, are
\begin{equation}\boxed{
 \beta=B-1,\qquad K=D_C+BD_R,\qquad M_C,\qquad
 M_F=M_{F,\mathrm{native}}+B-1.}\label{eq:ports}
\end{equation}
These are specific integers. The following bounded sums give their full
definition; the archive also contains their actual bytes.

Let the four ports have widths $r=(9,10,9,10)$, starting field indices
$f=(0,9,19,28)$, and native copy offsets $o=(1023,1013,1003,983)$.
For port $s$ and bit $0\le j<r_s$, let $e_s(j)=o_s+j$.
Use the occupied code $x+2^{r_s-1}$ for an edge label $x$ on that port.
Set
\[
 E_c=\{0,\ldots,942\}
 \cup\{e_s(j):s<4,\ j<r_s\}
 \cup\{1063,1064,1095,2195,3296\}.
\]
Thus $|E_c|=986$. Unlike the earlier layout, the source marker 1095 supplies
both tested synchronization anchors, and marker 1096 is absent.

Put $w_j=2^{10+2j}$ for $0\le j<41$. For tile $i$ with edge labels
$x_{i,s}$, define
\[
 c_i=1+\sum_{s<4}\sum_{j<r_s}
   \operatorname{bit}_j(x_{i,s}+2^{r_s-1})w_{f_s+j}
   +w_{38}+w_{39}+w_{40}.
\]
At each copy position set $c_{e_s(j)}=w_{f_s+j}$. The three anchors
1095, 2195, 3296 receive $w_{38},w_{39},w_{40}$ respectively; the two dummy
positions 1063, 1064 receive coefficient zero. Define
\[
 \mu=1022+\sum_{j=0}^{40}w_j,
 \qquad
 Z_0=(2^{113}-1)\sum_{e=8792}^{8798}\rho^e
                +(2^{73}-1)\rho^{8799}.
\]
The literal coefficient recipe is
\begin{align*}
 D_C={}&\rho^{30}+\rho^{62}+\rho^{1100}+\rho^{2201}
       +\sum_{e\in E_c}c_e(\rho^{4393-e}+\rho^{5495-e}),\\
 D_R={}&\rho^{42},\\
 M_C={}&B-1-\sum_{e\in E_c\setminus\{1\}}\rho^e-2\rho^{1063},\\
 M_{F,\mathrm{native}}={}&4+\mu(\rho^{4393}+\rho^{5495})
       +\sum_{j=0}^{9}\rho^{1013+j}
       +\sum_{j=0}^{8}\rho^{1065+j}
       +\rho^{2195}+\rho^{3296}+Z_0.
\end{align*}
All exponents here are fixed nonnegative integers. The bit-extraction symbols
occur only in the finite construction of integer coefficients, not in the
Diophantine equation evaluated on unknowns.

\subsection{Decoding before assuming payload carries are absent}

Suppose $n$ selector bits are set in a native cell. Before decoding,
$0\le n\le943$. Every other clause contribution is a multiple of 1024.
The low ten binary bits of the clause sum therefore equal $n$. The low
mask 1022 forbids all but bit 0, forcing $n=0$ or $n=1$.

Only after this conclusion do we examine the radix-four payload fields.
Each is a sum of two Boolean bits, hence is in $\{0,1,2\}$ and cannot
carry into its next field. Testing the low bit permits exactly 0 or 2.
Consequently an occupied cell has the selected tile's edge bits and all
three anchor bits; an empty cell has none. Like edge ports use the same
width, so horizontal and vertical matching remain injective. The unequal
widths introduce no comparison of incompatible codes.

The native overlap identities are

\midrule
$1013+j$, $0\le j<10$ & $983+j$ & none & $1013+j$\\
$1065+j$, $0\le j<9$ & $1003+j$ & $1023+j$ & none\\
2195 & 1095 & none & 2195\\

stated finite identities for arbitrary native assignments.

\subsection{Rotation synchronization, zero fields, and population}

The exact circular support checks are
\begin{align*}
 ((4393-E_c)\bmod8801)\cap((5495-E_c)\bmod8801)&=\varnothing,\\
 ((2195-E_c)\bmod8801)\cap((3296-E_c)\bmod8801)&=\{0\}.
\end{align*}
For an explanatory integer calculation, 1102 is absent from $E_c-E_c$,
and 1101 occurs only as $3296-2195$; moreover $8801>2\max E_c$ excludes
an additional circular case. These are the two clean-band and unique-anchor
conditions used by the native compiler. Synchronization starts at the
marked Start cell: selector zero is already one there, and a clean clause
forces its shared source anchor to one. The two tested anchors remain
distinct even though their source is shared. The binary parity argument
therefore still rules out a nonzero bit alignment, including the single
upper-dummy spill exception. Whole-cell shifts then recover the same Wang
matching semantics; only then does spatial matching propagate occupation
globally. No coprimality of a decoded temporal shift is assumed.

The conservative carry mass is
\[
 \mathcal M=(986+2)\left(2\sum_{e\in E_c}c_e+6\right).
\]
Its bit length is 112, and $4\mathcal M+8$ has bit length 114.
The maximum zero-field mask digit is $2^{113}-1$, with
$2(2^{113}-1)+4<2^{115}$. The same bounds control carries before decoding
and preserve the strict canonical slack.

The largest unshifted computation exponent is $5495+3296=8791$.
All 864 padding bits in $Z_0$ are above this exponent, in fields 8792--8799,
and are identically zero on aligned computations, including safe dummy
changes. They strengthen soundness without excluding a canonical accepting
tableau. Their number is $7\cdot113+73=864$. Since $\pc(\mu)=50$, the
high-mask population is
\[
 1+2\cdot50+10+9+2+864=986.
\]
The exact materialized integers satisfy
\[
 \pc(M_C)=d-986,\quad \pc(M_{F,\mathrm{native}})=986,
 \quad M_C\equiv2\pmod4,\quad M_{F,\mathrm{native}}\equiv4\pmod8,
\]
as well as $0<M_C,M_{F,\mathrm{native}}<B-1$. The population identity
required by the Pell compiler is therefore unchanged.

\begin{lemma}[Boolean coverage of the actual modulus]
Let $T_c=N_c/\lambda$. Every residue modulo $dN_c$ is a Boolean subset sum
of
\[
 \{B^{2+k_cj}:0\le j<T_c\}\cup\{B^3\}.
\]
The associated dummy cells and all required spatial and temporal translates
can be chosen without wrapping.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Set $C_0=d\lambda$, so $dN_c=C_0T_c$ and $T_c>C_0$.
The valuation certificate and elementary binomial lifting show that $B^{k_c}$
has order $T_c$ modulo $\lambda^n$ and is 1 modulo $d$. Its first $T_c$
powers are exactly the residues $1+C_0z$ modulo $C_0T_c$.

Normalize a desired residue by $B^{-2}$. Choose $\varepsilon\in\{0,1\}$
so that subtracting $\varepsilon B$ leaves a nonzero residue modulo
$\lambda$. Let $a$ be the least residue of this difference modulo $C_0$.
Then $1\le a<C_0<T_c$ and $\gcd(a,T_c)=1$. Write the remaining target as
$a+C_0u$, and solve
\[
 az_0+\frac{a(a-1)}2\equiv u\pmod{T_c}.
\]
The $a$ distinct orbit entries with additive coordinates
$z_0,z_0+1,\ldots,z_0+a-1$ modulo $T_c$ have the desired sum; include
$B^3$ exactly when $\varepsilon=1$.

The largest selected position is at most
$N_c-N_c/\lambda-\lambda+3$. Since $h_c>\lambda$, one has
$w_c<N_c/\lambda$, so translations by 1 and $w_c$ stay below $N_c$.
Position 3 is distinct and satisfies the same bounds. No cell is reused.
\end{proof}
