## 1. Twelve positive variables: conclusion

Reviewed `direct12/PROOF.md`, `direct12/algebra12_build.py`, and the resulting
`radical12_product.json`. I found no mathematical gap in the new reduction.
The three points specifically requested by the root agent all check:

- completeness after imposing that the normalized multiplier is divisible by H;
- separation of all algebraic conjugate branches and subsequent integrality;
- extraction of the correct resultant zero from the product guard modulo four.

The exact degree argument also checks. The arithmetic generator is consistent
with the displayed successive norm formula on inspection. This review does not
replace its author's finite arithmetic diagnostics.

### 1.1 The finite-group completeness argument really forces the divisor

Let A0=a+2 and N=Hc^2. The Pell matrix at index R,

    U_R = [[chi_A0(R), Delta psi_A0(R)],
           [psi_A0(R), chi_A0(R)]],

has determinant one. Its reduction modulo N is in a finite group of at
most N^4 elements. Its order therefore is at most N^4 and divides (N^4)!.
Consequently U_R^((N^4)!) is the identity modulo N. The Pell multiplication
law identifies its lower-left entry as psi_A0(m), m=R(N^4)!, so
Hc^2 divides psi_A0(m). Thus the new positive i is genuinely an integer.

The new strong index need not retain the old index's parity; that causes no
problem here. The auxiliary quotient congruences use R=3 modulo four and

    rho^2=-Delta (mod f),       rho^2=0 (mod c),

which still hold for the reselected index. The odd Chebyshev quotient gives
V=-c modulo f and V=-R modulo c. Then o=(V+c)/f is a positive integer.
From of=-R modulo c and f^2=1 modulo c, multiplication by f gives
o=-Rf modulo c. Hence T=(o+Rf)/c is an integer, and direct substitution
recovers the exact polynomial V=c(Tf-1)-Rf^2. No unproved divisibility of
the old witness is assumed.

The main root and gamma are unchanged during this reselection. Recomputing
the weights and setting t=ell gamma+M tau+y gives a positive witness.

### 1.2 Branch separation and rationality

The doubled weight M=2(D+v^2) is necessary for the stated simple bound:
d M^2 A>4(e+1), whereas B sqrt(D)<=e+1. It implies

    |y|<M sqrt(A).

The explicit bound Ubound satisfies |M tau+y|<Ubound for every real branch,
not just the canonical accepting one. With ell=1+H Ubound and sqrt(G)>1,
opposite main-root signs have a difference greater than the largest possible
difference of the remaining summands. Once r is fixed, opposite tau signs
are similarly separated by |y|<M sqrt(A). Then y is fixed by the linear
relation and f by d y^2=e-Bf, since B>0.

The Galois step does not assume independent square classes. Under every
embedding of the field generated by r,f,tau,y, the three roots r,f,tau remain
real because their fixed radicands are strictly positive. The linear
relation forces the image of y to be real too. Each conjugate tuple is
therefore among the branches just shown unique. Every generator is fixed
under every embedding; in characteristic zero it is rational. One can
equivalently pass to a normal closure and its Galois fixed field.

This handles possible repeated square classes as well as ordinary
nondegenerate cases. A putative imaginary y conjugate cannot satisfy the
linear relation whose other terms are all real.

### 1.3 Integrality, and the two uses of coprimality

Rational r,f,tau are integral because their squares are integers. The linear
relation gives Hy integral. If y=u/v in lowest terms, v divides H while
v^2 divides d. Since Q has a factor H^2, gcd(H,d)=gcd(H,Q-1)=1, forcing
v=1. This part would fail without the restricted strong multiplier.

The restored gamma=(r-ac-X)/H has denominator dividing H. The same linear
relation gives ell gamma integral. Since ell=1+H Ubound is coprime to H,
gamma is integral. These are separate denominator arguments and both are
needed; the draft gives both correctly.

The signed source theorem must be used for restored gamma,f,tau,y. It is
not enough to call the original positive-gamma compiler. The draft identifies
this issue explicitly. The actual package-14 soundness proof needs a nonzero
normalized multiplier and positive X,Y; oddness of i and a square w were ways
of ensuring those conditions in its 29-integer parameterization. Here H*i is
positive and w is already positive, so the extracted proof applies. This
should be described as reusing that signed-core argument, not simply citing
the narrowly parameterized 29-integer theorem verbatim.

### 1.4 Product guard

Delta=a^2+4a+3 is 0 or 3 modulo four, so G=1+Delta c^2 is 0 or 1 modulo
four. A norm U^2-GV^2 then has residue in {0,1,3}, never 2. Therefore
1+R12 cannot be -1 when it is an integer unit. From
Nk Nt (1+R12)=1 we obtain R12=0 and equal unit signs for Nk,Nt.
The inherited outer estimates were explicitly stated for both signs,
so there is no circular sign assumption at this step.

### 1.5 Degree

The root expansion r_+=ac+2c+terms of scaling degree below five follows
from sqrt(1+Delta c^2)=c sqrt((a+2)^2-1+c^-2). The correction after
ac+2c has leading degree c/a=-1, so the stated bound is safe.
Thus S-ell r_+ has degree68 and S-ell r_- degree74. The W tau term has
degree63 and cannot cancel either leader. The two signs of f do not affect
the leading degree of the factor. Four factors have degree194, four206,
and the resultant has exact degree1600. The nonzero leading form
2^16 d_top^8 ell_top^16 a_top^8 c_top^16 is consistent with their product.
Multiplication by Nk and Nt adds7+2, yielding exact degree1609.

## 2. Nine-variable majorant weights and explicit finalizer

Reviewed the weight change and final norm in
`nine/nine_compressor.py`, together with the exact Lean statements in
`Common/RefinedRelationCombining.lean`,
`Common/RefinedRelationCombiningPolynomial.lean`, and
`Paper1982/NinePoly.lean`. This section does not independently rederive
the full Jones nine-variable compiler or its coefficient index conditions.

### 2.1 The elementary majorant is global

For arbitrary integers a,b let M(a,b)=a^2+b^2+1. Then

    |(a^2-1)b^2+1| <= (a^2+1)b^2+1 <= M(a,b)^2,

because the difference in the second inequality is exactly

    a^4+b^4+a^2b^2+2a^2+b^2 >= 0.

Thus every complex square root of that radicand has modulus at most M(a,b).
No positivity assumption on a, b, or the radicand is needed.

For a0=(P^2-1)K^2+1, V0=M(P,K)+1 bounds every root by V0-1.
For a1=D F I, each factor has the same Pell form. Multiplication of the
three nonnegative bounds gives

    sqrt(|D F I|) <= M(A,C) M(A,E) M(Gp,H)=V1-1.

Both weights are integers >=1. These are exactly the hypotheses of the
refined relation-combining theorem, including for negative radicands and
coinciding square classes. Therefore the new weights preserve the semantic
equivalence globally, not only on intended accepting witnesses.

### 2.2 The divisor is strictly positive without hidden assumptions

The source theorem `Jones1982.Nine.JF_ne_zero (V : Vars)` is unconditional
in the integer fields of V. Directly, J=4A-5 cannot vanish. If
F=(A^2-1)E^2+1 vanished, then A=0 and E=+1 or -1; but at A=0 the
definition E=i J D C^2 makes E divisible by5. This is impossible.
Hence d=(JF)^2>0 at every integer point, and the dividend c is a square,
so c>=0. No margin assumption is used to establish the divisor hypothesis.

### 2.3 The explicit norm has the correct signs

Writing W=d(2m-1) and T=dn+c-W(c+V0V1), the four radical factors of
the refined combiner are

    T-W(sigma sqrt(a0)+tau V0 sqrt(a1)), sigma,tau in {+1,-1}.

Their exact product is

    (T^2-W^2(a0+V0^2 a1))^2-4W^4 V0^2 a0 a1,

the literal formula emitted by the script. The identities hold as polynomial
identities, not only when the radicands are positive squares. The imported
refined theorem then enforces the square conditions, divisibility, and positive
margin with the single additional natural witness n.

The requirement n in N is essential for extracting the margin sign. The
nine-variable construction must therefore retain its stated nonnegative
integer domain. Unrestricted-integer evaluation of the same nine-variable
polynomial is a separate, unproved assertion; the three-square substitutions
give a valid integer conversion when required.

## 3. Recommendation

Include both reductions with the trust boundary stated above. The12-variable
form is a direct low-degree alternative to the concrete nine-variable
specialization, while the new27-integer construction gives a moderate-degree
signed alternative. Do not conflate the old formal endpoint or generic
relation-combining theorem with a kernel-checked build of the newly emitted
specialized circuits.

## 4. K215 calibration: ordinary proof versus intended semantics

I inspected these files at the same pinned revision:

- `Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/candidate-k215/proof.md`;
- `Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/candidate-k218/alternatives/dominated/proof.md`;

- `Computability/BusyBeaver/Lean/BusyBeaver/ZFC/KEndpoint.lean`;
- `Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/k225-formal/verification/k215/README.md`.

The calibration is not merely an informal intended machine-semantics claim.
The ordinary K215 exposition explicitly composes source equivalence, quantified
primitive runtime simulation, the exact blank initialization, cofinal proof
enumeration from counter4, and the dominated numerical calculus calibration.
It states the ordinary conclusion formalizable in ZFC: blank halting is
equivalent to a finite ZFC refutation. It explicitly warns that finite source
identity or bounded blank execution alone would not prove this conclusion.

The endpoint source supplies further precise evidence. Its theorem has the
unconditional type

    BlankHalts machine215 <-> BProv ZFCax_s [] Form.fBot.

The shared endpoint first proves halting equivalent to derivability of code1
in C222. `c222_iff_zf_refutation` has both directions: the upper direction is
`KUpper222.zf_refutation_of_c222`, while the lower direction uses semantic
completeness of C222 and the seed-to-ZF-consistency theorem. Finally it invokes
`ConstructibleChoice.zf_refutation_iff_zfc_refutation`. Thus the statement is
about a syntactic finite proof predicate, not merely model existence or an
assumed sound intended interpretation.

The ordinary dominated-calculus document also explicitly describes the proof
translations: restricted axiom leaves are replaced by a fixed finite derivation
of unrestricted rho8, and conversely each restricted leaf already is an
unrestricted axiom. It then describes soundness invariants and a cofinal
enumeration compiler. These preserve the literal theorem codes and handle the
finite initial enumeration cutoff.

The precise trust boundary remains significant. The K215 proof imports the
predecessor numerical-calculus/ZFC calibration and quantified runtime theorems.
The files inspected here are a compositional ordinary proof and the final
formal source statements, not a fresh re-verification of every predecessor
proof. The repository records successful exact-table Lean and Rocq builds,
but those builds were not rerun in this task. Its verification README explicitly
distinguishes the external proof-assistant theorems from a derivation encoded
in ZFC's own proof syntax and says the internal PA endpoint remains open.

Accordingly, a defensible report wording is: "We use the repository's ordinary
K215 calibration theorem, whose proof is stated formalizable in ZFC and whose
exact-table external endpoint is separately formalized in Lean and Rocq. The
new Diophantine reductions are ordinary mathematical proofs; this archive does
not supply a newly compiled endpoint or an internally encoded ZFC proof."
One should not infer ZFC provability merely from Lean's global soundness; the
ordinary mathematical calibration is the relevant imported theorem.
