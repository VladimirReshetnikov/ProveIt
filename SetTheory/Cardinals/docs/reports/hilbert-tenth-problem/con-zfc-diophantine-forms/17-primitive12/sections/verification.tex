\section{Logical calibration, reproducibility, and remaining work}
\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{The exact logical statement}

Let $\operatorname{Ref}_{\rm ZFC}$ mean that a finite derivation of
falsity exists in the repository's calibrated effective proof calculus
for ZFC.  Its equivalence to the usual finite-proof definition of
syntactic inconsistency is part of the inherited calibration.
Define $\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$ as $\neg\operatorname{Ref}_{\rm ZFC}$.
The proof is the composition, formalizable in ZFC, of
\begin{align*}
\exists\mathbf x\in\Z_{>0}^{12}\;\mathcal P_{12}(\mathbf x)=0
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\text{normalized/signed Pell core},\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\text{marked native tableau},\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\operatorname{Halt}(K215,\mathrm{blank}),\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\operatorname{Ref}_{\rm ZFC}.
\end{align*}
Negating this equivalence gives the requested consistency statement.
The integer variants have the same last three links.

The literal Lean endpoint included for identification states
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
BlankHalts machine215 $\leftrightarrow$ BProv ZFCax\_s [] Form.fBot.
\end{quote}
The pinned verification directory reports completed Lean and Rocq
checks of this exact machine, with their own source, dependency, and
toolchain records \cite{RepoK215}.  We did not rerun those builds.
The ordinary calibration note explicitly proves the corresponding
equivalence by reasoning formalizable in ZFC.  We use that mathematical
calibration, not an inference from the external soundness of a proof
assistant to an internal ZFC theorem.

The new Diophantine part is an ordinary mathematical proof.  The
archive's numerical and algebraic checks are finite diagnostics and
artifact checks; they are not kernel proofs of the new theorem.
No accepting K215 run, ZFC refutation, or proof of $\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$
is produced.  In particular, the equivalence should not be described
without qualification as a proof that both directions of the equation's
solvability question are undecidable in ZFC.

\subsection{Explicit proof dependencies}

The following list separates the new arguments from the inherited
mathematics that they use.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Machine calibration.}  The exact-table K215 endpoint and
      the ordinary calibration identify blank halting with a finite
      ZFC refutation \cite{RepoK215,RepoCalibration}.
\item \textbf{Native coefficient compiler.}  The fixed raw universal
      78-operation proof supplies the carry-free local tests, clean
      bands, and rotation framework.  Its 76-operation successor supplies
      actual-rank Boolean dummy control.  The new proof replaces the
      alphabet and the anchor-specific synchronization argument, then
      checks all finite numerical hypotheses \cite{RepoNative}.
\item \textbf{Blank input and Pell bridge.}  The blank-helical and
      67-operation proofs remove the input, preserve a unique Start,
      and connect the packed word to the normalized arithmetic.  The
      half-binomial kernel and signed Pell results remain substantive
      mathematical dependencies \cite{RepoBlank,RepoPell}.
\item \textbf{Signed domains.}  The twelve-positive-unknown proof uses
      package 12's ordinary signed theorem with
      $i_{\rm ord}=\Delta i$.  The unrestricted-integer proofs use
      package 14's narrower, seven-positive-guard theorem with odd $i$
      and square $w$ \cite{RepoSigned}.
\item \textbf{New compression.}  The sixteen-branch separation,
      rational-ordinate integrality, recovery of the main quotient,
      exact norm, and leading-term analysis are proved in this article.
      The 27-integer construction adapts package 09's three-root
      amalgamation with an even weight and a new signed denominator
      analysis \cite{RepoAmalgamation}.
\end{enumerate}

An especially delicate inherited point is the ordinary strong equation
\[
(i_{\rm ord}c^2)^2=\Delta(f^2-1).
\]
One cannot simply cancel $\Delta$ and assert the normalized Pell
divisibility.  The relaxed Pell lemma uses a large-rank bound, strong
divisibility in the integer-coefficient quadratic ring, and a
proper-divisor estimate before recovering the required index relation.
The inherited arithmetic proof establishes its large-rank hypothesis.
Both that proof and the relaxed lemma are bundled for inspection.

\subsection{What the executable checks establish}

All new programs use Python's standard library.  Their diagnostics
cover four distinct tasks:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Literal data and finite compiler geometry.}  Authenticate
      all 430 transitions, enumerate the pruned tile list, verify its
      census and hash, exhaust the copy/anchor target conditions, and
      verify exact carry, population, and five-adic inequalities.
      Eighteen halting control tableaux exercise 1,143 local windows.
      These use control transitions, not an alleged halting run of K215.
\item \textbf{Circuit integrity.}  Check every gate dependency, live
      input and operation count.  The delivered JSON uses only binary
      addition, subtraction, and multiplication; the final comparison
      is to zero, or the specified constants for a system.
\item \textbf{Independent algebra.}  Check the norm against the product
      of all sixteen branches over finite fields, including encoded-root
      fixtures, and verify the finalizer identities and modulo-four
      property.  The 26-integer checks include the signed and zero-scale
      cases relevant to its altered proof.  The 27-integer circuit is
      also compared with a separately written direct formula.
\item \textbf{Degree corroboration.}  Exact univariate specializations
      over finite fields recover the claimed degrees.  They complement
      the uniform leading-form proofs; alone they would not prove
      noncancellation for every valid coefficient tuple.
\end{itemize}
Independent mathematical reviews are included for the 12-, 26-, and
27-variable constructions.  These are ordinary reviews by separate
automated research workers, not independent theorem-prover certificates.

\begin{samepage}
From the package root, run
\begin{verbatim}
python3 verification/check_package.py
make verify

\end{verbatim}

The first command checks the delivered data and circuit structure.
The second regenerates circuits and reruns the finite semantic/algebraic
diagnostics; a separate \texttt{make degrees} target reruns the slower
degree corroboration.  PDF compilation requires a standard LaTeX
installation with the packages listed in \texttt{report.tex}.
No Lean or Rocq build is included in these targets.
The huge four coefficients are never expanded.  The auxiliary residue
evaluator computes their values modulo any requested positive integer
from the same exact finite recipe.

The formulas remain valid with one of the original complete coefficient
tuples when its compiler hypotheses apply.  That fallback can isolate
the new algebraic argument from the new coefficient adapter during
review.  Partial replacement of ports has no such interpretation.

\subsection{Promising next reductions}

The present package supports several concrete next steps.
First, sharpen the separating weights.  The factor of sixteen in the
primitive norm is structural for these four binary root choices, but
the very high \emph{total} degree mostly comes from conservative weight
growth.  A smaller dominating weight could improve degree and operation
count without changing the recovered variables.

Second, examine whether one of the seven positive guards in the signed
theorem can be removed.  Each safely removed positivity guard saves
two unrestricted integers after the three-variable substitution.
This needs a new sign-domain proof, not just a circuit change.

Third, optimize the local alphabet and anchor positions together.
The compact pattern proves that the old geometrically growing anchor
set was unnecessary.  More aggressive pruning must retain a completeness
padding argument and preserve the actual-rank tuning congruences.

Fourth, formalize the new bridge in manageable layers: the rational
Pell-ordinate lemma; finite rank-four algebra and its factorization;
separation and integral recovery; then the coefficient adapter and its
connection to the existing K215 endpoint.  The exact domain distinctions
and the zero-scale order in the 26-integer proof are natural first
formalization targets.

For incorporation, the defensible claims are therefore a reduction
from 13 to 12 positive unknowns in this explicit family, from 29 to 26
unrestricted integers with a useful 27-integer tradeoff, and a 25-fold
reduction in the bit length of the base-minus-one coefficient.
The old 67-operation expression and existing low-degree systems remain
valuable members of the comparison.
