\section{A smaller complete coefficient tuple}
\label{sec:compiler}

The arithmetic constructions above do not require a new machine.
They use the supplied literal K215 table, with 215 working states,
two tape symbols, and 430 transition rows.  The initial state is 0,
the blank symbol is 0, and the halting state is 215.  The table's
SHA-256 is
\begin{center}\scriptsize
\texttt{fdbcef0825886035c3091c8537a69535bc96f9e265f0ae45863543d40ae70090}.
\end{center}
The archive includes the literal table and its authenticated transition
projection.  Every transition entering halt retains its original write
and movement; stationary absorbing padding rules are added only after
halt.

The new binding is named
\texttt{pruned-T4-5252-compact-5pow16}.
It modifies the four-cell local compiler in package 11 \cite{RepoT4} in two
independent ways: prune the tile alphabet, then shorten the synchronization
anchor layout.  We give the modified proofs here and retain the generic
carry, overlap, and Pell compiler proofs as named dependencies.

\subsection{Pruning while retaining every finite blank halt}

Retain the 439 cell codes of T4:
\begin{itemize}
\item 0 and 1 are headless tape symbols;
\item $2(q_0+1)+b_0$ is a tape symbol $b_0$ carrying state
      $0\le q_0\le215$;
\item $L=434,Q_0=435,R_0=436$ are the initialized blank phases,
      with $Q_0$ carrying the initial head;
\item $V_0=437$ and $H_0=438$ are vertical and horizontal boundaries.
\end{itemize}
Here $Q_0,R_0,V_0,H_0$ are cell names, not arithmetic registers.
A tile $(l,c,r,n)$ describes the left, central, right, and next-time
central cells.  Begin with the exact old T4 predicate, retained as
\texttt{old\_valid} in the delivered checker.

Define a horizontal edge predicate as follows.  If an endpoint is
$V_0$, the other is not $V_0$; a phase on its right must be $L$, and
a phase on its left must be $R_0$.  Otherwise $x=H_0$ if and only if
$y=H_0$, and if either endpoint is a phase, the pair must be one of
\[
(L,L),\ (L,Q_0),\ (Q_0,R_0),\ (R_0,R_0).
\]
Apply this predicate to $(l,c)$ and $(c,r)$ and impose three more
conditions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item If the triple meets $V_0$, it contains neither a head nor the
      headless tape symbol 1.
\item If $n=H_0$, the triple contains no initialized phase and no
      nonhalting head.
\item If $c=H_0,l=V_0$, require $n=L$; if $c=H_0,r=V_0$,
      require $n=R_0$.
\end{enumerate}
Order the tiles with the Start tile $(434,435,436,1)$ first and the
distinguished forbidden tile $(438,0,0,438)$ second, followed by all
other retained tiles lexicographically.  Keep the forbidden tile as
an exception to the new filter.  It satisfied the old predicate but
was globally impossible: its left $H_0$ has an ordinary right neighbor,
contradicting horizontal boundary propagation there.

\begin{proposition}[Local-language preservation]
The new marked periodic-tableau language is nonempty exactly when
the literal K215 machine halts from its blank tape.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Every retained tile is an old T4 tile.  Old soundness therefore gives
a blank halt; the exceptional forbidden tile cannot occur globally.

Conversely a finite blank halt visits a finite tape interval.  Put the
walls beyond that interval with at least three permanently unused blank
cells on each side.  Choose the padded width and height independently
as sufficiently large powers of five.  Genuine rows satisfy the edge
predicate: initialized rows are $L^uQ_0R_0^v$, horizontal boundaries
propagate within their strip, and all other interior rows are ordinary
tape configurations.  The unused margins satisfy the new wall
restriction.  A row immediately preceding a horizontal boundary is a
halted row, so its only head is a halt head and it contains no initialized
phase.  K215's first transition goes to working state 93, which also
excludes an initial row immediately followed by a stop boundary.
The first row after a horizontal boundary has $L,R_0$ next to the walls.

The helical extension shifts the adjacent strip by one time step across
a wall.  Permanently blank margins remain headless at both times, and
$V_0$ blocks horizontal phase/boundary propagation.  Thus all new
conditions hold across seams as well.  In particular, a tile centered
at $V_0$ may see different row types on its two sides; the predicate
explicitly permits this.  The old construction's canonical Start and
absence of the forbidden tile are preserved.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Why the shorter anchors preserve synchronization}

Let $M_0=11839$.  The old anchor set was
$\{M_0,3M_0,9M_0,27M_0\}$ after appropriate layout parameters.
Use instead
\[
\{M_0,2M_0,3M_0,7M_0\},
\]
with tested targets $3M_0,7M_0$ and bare shifts $2M_0,5M_0$.
All nonanchor support is below $M_0$.
At the first target only the first shift hits the anchor $M_0$;
at the second only the second shift hits $2M_0$.
The bare shifts cannot reach any copy target.  The low copy shift 1317
is smaller than every anchor gap and cannot create an extra anchor hit.

The target difference $4M_0$ occurs in the support difference set
in exactly one ordered way, $7M_0-3M_0$.
Differences among the lower three anchors or nonanchor support are less
than $3M_0$.  Differences from $7M_0$ to the other lower anchors are
$5M_0$ and $6M_0$, and differences to nonanchor support exceed
$6M_0$.  There is no circular alternative, since
$L_{\rm cell}>2(7M_0)$.  Thus an aligned radix rotation satisfying
both target tests must have zero within-cell shift.

For arbitrary binary rotations, the inherited clean-band argument
still applies.  Here $E_{\max}=82873$, $T_1=771293$, $T_2=937043$, and
\[
T_2-T_1=2E_{\max}+4,\qquad
T_2+2E_{\max}+2<L_{\rm cell}.
\]
At least one clause band is clean and makes its four anchors equal
one.  A within-radix residue $1,\ldots,b-2$ makes all candidate radix
digits even.  Residue $b-1$ can produce only one odd digit, from the
single possible upper-dummy spill.  The two anchor targets require two
odd digits, so both cases are impossible.  The residue is zero, and
the preceding difference argument proves whole-cell alignment.

The copy geometry is unchanged in relative positions: a shift by
$3\cdot439$ tests next-to-central agreement, and shifts by $605107+439$
and $605107$ test the horizontal overlaps.  The high copy band is below
$T_1-E_{\max}$; the two clause bands cannot contaminate each other.
The exact checker tests all 1,319 copy/anchor targets against the full
support.  Once alignment and overlaps hold, the same native compiler
recovers the four-neighbor computation plane.  The local-language
proposition supplies its machine semantics.

\subsection{Carries, populations, and canonical signed witnesses}

The mask has $\pc(m_0)=12+2835=2847$, so
\[
\pc(M_F^{\rm nat})=2\cdot2847+3\cdot439+2+1=7014,
\qquad\pc(M_C)=5^{16}-7014.
\]
The extra one in the first identity is the low marker 4.  In the second,
7013 support bits other than selector 1, and the upper-dummy bit, are
removed from $B-1$.  Consequently the mask population identity and
the required low congruences hold:
\[
\pc(M_C)+\pc(M_F^{\rm nat})=5^{16},\quad
M_C\equiv2\pmod4,\quad M_F^{\rm nat}\equiv4\pmod8.
\]
Both masks are strictly between 0 and $B-1$.

For the inherited uniform carry bound define
\[
\mathrm{mass}=(|\mathcal E|+2)\left(2\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}v_e+6\right).
\]
Exact manageable-integer arithmetic gives bit lengths 22893 for
$\sum v_e$, 22909 for $4\mathrm{mass}+8$, and 36856 for $m_0$.
The sufficient radix exponent bound is $b_{\min}=36871<78125$.
In particular $4\mathrm{mass}+8<\rho$ and $2m_0+4<\rho$.
Thus the original carry-free tests remain valid for arbitrary native
cells, including the permitted upper-dummy bit.

The two clause contributions cancel modulo five because their gap
is 2 modulo four and $b=78125$ is 1 modulo four.  The remaining terms
give
\[
D_C\equiv3\pmod5,\qquad D_R\equiv3\pmod5,\qquad
2D_C-D_R\equiv3\pmod5.
\]
The inherited Boolean dummy-bit construction therefore tunes the
\emph{actual packed rank} $R$ modulo $dN$, using the dummy at 10520.
It does not merely tune an auxiliary formal index.  The required
independent power-of-five width and height padding was preserved above.
The canonical origin mask test and positive raw slack are unchanged;
fresh Pell witnesses are chosen after the final rank is fixed.

This also supplies the signed-domain compatibility needed in
Section~\ref{sec:integers}.  The exponent $d=5^{16}$ is odd, the canonical
word length $N$ is a power of five, and the canonical tuning still has
$R\equiv3\pmod4$.  Therefore
\[
q=2^{dN},\qquad w=2^{R-dN}
\]
has square $w$.  Choosing the canonical auxiliary index $m=cR$ supplies
an odd strong multiplier $i$.  These are completeness choices, not
claims about every solution of the arithmetic core.
