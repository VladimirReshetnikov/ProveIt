# Provenance and scope

Prepared on 22 October 2026 for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository.
The new synthesis, arithmetic generators, coefficient adapter, article,
and checks were prepared with AI assistance and separately assigned
mathematical reviews. No human authorship or proof-assistant certification
of the new work is implied.

The substantive existing results and source files belong to the ProveIt
development identified in `SOURCE_PINS.json`. Credit for the K215 machine,
underlying proof calculus, native computation compiler, Pell constructions,
and packages 09--15 remains with their respective contributors. The
machine-specific inherited notice is retained in `machine/NOTICE.md`.

The existing licenses are retained in `licenses/`. `LICENSE` reproduces
the ProveIt MIT No Attribution license for incorporation of this package;
inherited third-party notices, including the Wade attribution, remain
applicable to their respective material. Literature is cited in the
article rather than redistributed as full papers.

The new mathematical claims are ordinary proofs formalizable in ZFC,
composed with explicit inherited mathematical dependencies. Finite
programmatic checks corroborate algebra and finite compiler data. They
do not constitute a fresh end-to-end Lean, Rocq, or Isabelle proof.
