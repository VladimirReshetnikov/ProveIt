\subsection{A smaller twelve-positive-variable norm}
The following synthesis has twelve strictly positive unknowns
\[
 J,F,\alpha,z,h,i,T,s,w,\eta,\zeta,r.
\]
Its product has exact degree $1081$ and $120$ binary arithmetic operations.
The three-equation system has maximum residual degree $1072$ and $115$
operations when its last equality is compared directly. The sum of squares
has degree $2144$ and $123$ operations. Abbreviations are polynomial
expressions, not additional unknowns.

Use the authentic four fixed ports $(\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F)$, with the shifted
$M_F$ convention, and set
\begin{align*}
 q&=\beta J+1,&C&=q-F-\alpha,&X&=wq,&Y&=sq^3,&E&=XY,\\
 k&=\eta+\zeta,&c&=kY+\eta,&a&=E+Y,&H&=4a+3,&\Delta&=a^2+H,\\
 \ell&=EkY,&A&=1+\ell(\ell+k),&G&=1+\Delta c^2,\\
 R&=((q-1)(q-F)+\alpha)(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\\
 N_k&=k-hE-R,&N_t&=(K_0+w)C+q-F-z(q-1).
\end{align*}
Define the normalized auxiliary expressions
\begin{align*}
 I&=ic^2,&D&=1+\Delta I^2,&Q&=(\Delta I)^2,&d&=Q-1,\\
 v&=cT,&b&=c+RD,&e&=Q(Dv^2+b^2)-1,&B&=2Qvb.
\end{align*}
The new separate weights are
\[
 W=2I(T+Raic),\qquad L=1+HW,\qquad M=2W,\qquad S=X+ac+Hr.
\]
The integer polynomial norm is
\[
 N=\prod_{u^2=G,\ \tau^2=A,\ f^2=D}
 \bigl[d(S-Lu-HM\tau)^2-H^2e+H^2Bf\bigr],
\]
where the product is over all eight sign choices. This is only a compact
notation for a polynomial; the delivered circuit uses $+,-,\times$ alone.
The final presentations are
\[
 N_k=N_t=1,\quad N=0;
 \qquad P=N_kN_t(1+N)-1=0.
\]

For a literal evaluator put
\begin{align*}
 a_0&=H^2M^2A,&V_0&=S^2+L^2G,&V_1&=2SL,\\
 C_0&=d(V_0+a_0)-H^2e,&C_1&=dV_1,&K_f&=4d^2a_0,\\
 K_0'&=K_fV_0,&K_1'&=K_fV_1,&Z&=4H^4(QDv^2)(Qb^2),\\
 U_0&=C_0^2+GC_1^2-K_0'-Z,&U_1&=2C_0C_1-K_1',\\
 N_0&=U_0^2+GU_1^2-4ZK_0',&N_1&=2U_0U_1-4ZK_1',\\
 N&=N_0^2-GN_1^2.
\end{align*}

\paragraph{Why the norm introduces no extra witnesses.}
At a zero of the product, $N\equiv N_0^2\pmod4$ forces $N=0$ and
$N_k=N_t\in\{1,-1\}$. The inherited weak transport bounds imply
$X\ge16$, $Y\ge4096$, $a\ge64$, $R<c$, and $Q\ge3$. A coherent
vanishing norm factor gives
\[
 S=Lu+HM\tau+Hy,\qquad d y^2=Q(vf-b)^2-1.
\]
Every complex conjugate satisfies
\[
 |y'|<\tfrac43(v\sqrt D+b)<\tfrac34W\sqrt G,
 \qquad \sqrt G<\tfrac98\sqrt A.
\]
The second inequality follows from
$(1+66/1024)(65/64)=70850/65536<9/8$; the first follows by dividing
by $ac<\sqrt G$, using $\sqrt D<(a+2)I$, and $a\ge64$, $R<c$.
Since $HW>36$,
\[
 HM\sqrt A>\tfrac{16}{9}HW\sqrt G
  >(1+\tfrac74HW)\sqrt G>L\sqrt G+H|y'|.
\]
Galois conjugation therefore first forces $\tau$ rational, then forces $u$
rational. Both are integral because their squares are integers. Next $y$ is
rational and $f=(e-dy^2)/B$ is an integer. The special Pell saturation lemma
\[
 m^2V^2-(m^2-1)y^2=1,\quad m\ge2,\quad m,V\in\mathbb Z,
 \quad y\in\mathbb Q\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y\in\mathbb Z
\]
applies with $m=\Delta I$. Reducing modulo $H$ now recovers integral
$\gamma=(u-X-ac)/H$. The inherited signed compiler theorem resolves the
remaining signs and gives blank K215 halting. Conversely, a canonical
compiler witness gives the positive coordinate
$r=\gamma+Wu+M\tau+y$. Thus ZFC proves that absence of positive solutions
is equivalent to $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$.

\paragraph{Exact degree, including the tied leading terms.}
Write $q_0=\beta J$, $k_0=\eta+\zeta$, and use $*$ for the highest
homogeneous form. Put $Z_*=d_*H_*^2R_*^2D_*^2$, of degree $134$.
The top forms are
\[
 (dV_0)_*=4Z_*,\quad(da_0)_*=16Z_*,\quad(H^2e)_*=Z_*,
 \quad C_{0*}=19Z_*,\quad K_{0*}'=256Z_*^2.
\]
Hence $U_{0*}=105Z_*^2$ and $N_*=105^4Z_*^8$. Explicitly,
\[
 N_*=2^{32}105^4i^{48}k_0^{96}w^{80}s^{176}
       q_0^{656}(q_0-F)^{16},
\]
nonzero for every authentic $\beta>0$. Thus $\deg N=1072$,
$\deg P=1081$, and the sum-of-squares degree is $2144$.

This is an ordinary mathematical reduction, not a new end-to-end
proof-assistant certificate or a proof of ZFC consistency. Full integrality,
sign, source, coefficient-binding, and verification details are in the
companion proof and executable packet.
