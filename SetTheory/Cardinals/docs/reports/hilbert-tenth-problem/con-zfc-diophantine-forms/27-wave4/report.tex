\begin{abstract}
We compare the eight wave-3 packages at ProveIt commit
\texttt{ae3007ca4467} and combine their methods. A new equation in twelve
positive unknowns has degree $1081$ and a $120$-operation circuit, improving
our preceding degree-$1145$, $123$-operation equation. A signed-scale
extension reduces the moderate-degree unrestricted-integer presentations
from $26$ to $24$ unknowns. Shared evaluation reduces the existing
nine-variable polynomial from $20\,531$ to $1485$ operations after fixed
coefficient specialization; a sharper majorant gives a different
nine-variable equation of lower degree. A mixed-radix compiler reduces the
fixed coefficient base to $2^{5^9}$ and supplies all four actual binary
integers. We also give smaller integer low-degree lifts and positive
systems of lower degree at unchanged arithmetic cost. These are explicit
mathematical constructions with reproducible finite audits, not new
end-to-end Lean or Rocq proofs, consistency proofs, or optimality claims.
\end{abstract}

\section{What is minimized, and what the theorem says}
There is no single total ordering of ``simplest'': fewer unknowns, fewer
equations, smaller degree, shorter arithmetic circuits, smaller fixed
coefficients, and shorter proofs can conflict. We retain a small collection
of useful tradeoffs rather than declare one universally best equation.
Every operation count charges each binary $+,-,\times$, including multiplication
by a fixed numeral. Reused registers and comparisons between already
computed expressions are free. Registers are not existential unknowns.
All exponentiation in the delivered circuits is unrolled into multiplication;
there is no variable exponentiation or hidden divisibility predicate.

\begin{theorem}[Meaning of each delivered construction]
For each designated circuit or system $P$ below, with its stated domain
$\mathcal D$ and its complete fixed coefficient binding, the ordinary
mathematical reductions give
\[
 \mathrm{ZFC}\vdash
 \bigl(\neg\exists \mathbf x\in\mathcal D\;P(\mathbf x)=0\bigr)
 \ \longleftrightarrow\ \Con(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\]
For a system, $P=0$ means all its displayed comparisons hold. The assertion
is an equivalence, not a proof that either side holds.
\end{theorem}
The dependency chain is finite syntactic ZFC refutation $\leftrightarrow$
blank halting of the literal K215 machine $\leftrightarrow$ marked periodic
tiling $\leftrightarrow$ the inputless native-window/Pell interface
$\leftrightarrow$ the equations in their proper domains. The new steps are
proved below and in the accompanying detailed notes. The native compiler,
Pell-rank lemmas and finitary machine calibration remain cited mathematical
dependencies. The source endpoint
\file{KEndpoint215.blank_halts_iff_zfc_refutation} mirrors the last calibration;
we do not derive ZFC-provability from Lean's soundness or claim a fresh build.
No unconditional two-sided independence statement is made.

\section{A literal common norm evaluator}
Fix the four constants $(\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F)$ from Section~\ref{sec:coeff};
$M_F$ is the \emph{shifted} high-mask port. For twelve supplied ports
$J,F,\alpha,z,h,i,T,s,w,\eta,\zeta,r$, define
\begin{gather*}
 q=\beta J+1,\quad C=q-F-\alpha,\quad X=wq,\quad Y=sq^3,\quad E=XY,\\
 k=\eta+\zeta,\quad c=kY+\eta,\quad a=E+Y,\quad H=4a+3,\quad \Delta=a^2+H,\\
 \ell=EkY,\quad A=1+\ell(\ell+k),\quad G=1+\Delta c^2,\\
 R=((q-1)(q-F)+\alpha)(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\\
 N_k=k-hE-R,\qquad N_t=(K_0+w)C+q-F-z(q-1),\\
 I=ic^2,\quad D=1+\Delta I^2,\quad Q=(\Delta I)^2,\quad d=Q-1,\\
 v=cT,\quad b=c+RD,\quad e=Q(Dv^2+b^2)-1,\quad B_1=2Qvb,\\
 S=X+ac+Hr.
\end{gather*}
Choose weights $L,M$ as specified in the following sections. The norm shorthand
\[
 N=\prod_{u^2=G,\;\tau^2=A,\;f^2=D}
 \left[d(S-Lu-HM\tau)^2-H^2e+H^2B_1f\right]
\tag{1}\label{eq:norm}
\]
means the product of all eight sign choices, with multiplicity if a radicand
vanishes. It is an integer polynomial, not an equation with additional roots.
Here is a literal root-free evaluation schedule:
\begin{align*}
 a_0&=H^2M^2A,&V_0&=S^2+L^2G,&V_1&=2SL,\\
 C_0&=d(V_0+a_0)-H^2e,&C_1&=dV_1,&K_f&=4d^2a_0,\\
 K_0'&=K_fV_0,&K_1'&=K_fV_1,&Z&=4H^4(QDv^2)(Qb^2),\\
 U_0&=C_0^2+GC_1^2-K_0'-Z,&U_1&=2C_0C_1-K_1',\\
 N_0&=U_0^2+GU_1^2-4ZK_0',&N_1&=2U_0U_1-4ZK_1',\\
 N&=N_0^2-GN_1^2.&&&
\end{align*}
The three finalizations are
\[
 N_k=N_t=1,\quad N_0^2=GN_1^2;\qquad
 P=N_kN_t(1+N)-1;\qquad
 P_{\rm sos}=(N_k-1)^2+(N_t-1)^2+N^2.
\tag{2}\label{eq:finals}
\]
Counting a system's last equality directly avoids an unnecessary subtraction;
this explains the one-gate distinction from residual-zero schedules.

\section{Twelve positive unknowns: separate the two weights}
All twelve ports are strictly positive. Set
\[
 W=2I(T+Raic),\qquad L=1+HW,\qquad M=2W.
\tag{3}\label{eq:positiveweight}
\]
Using the same large weight for both roots is unnecessary. The main-root
weight must be $1$ modulo $H$; the first-root weight only needs sufficient
separation. Formula~\eqref{eq:positiveweight} distinguishes these requirements.

At a zero of $P$, the integer factors in~\eqref{eq:finals} are units.
Since $N_1$ is divisible by $8$, $N\equiv N_0^2\pmod4$; hence $N=-2$ is
impossible, and $N=0$, $N_k=N_t\in\{-1,1\}$. Weak transport and the source
index bounds give $X\ge16$, $Y\ge4096$, $a\ge64$, $R<c$ and $Q\ge3$.
A vanishing factor in~\eqref{eq:norm} supplies coherent algebraic roots with
\[
 S=Lu+HM\tau+Hy,\qquad
 (Q-1)y^2=Q(vf-b)^2-1.
\]
Every conjugate, including complex ones, satisfies
\[
 |y'|<\tfrac43(v\sqrt D+b)<\tfrac34W\sqrt G,
 \qquad \sqrt G<\tfrac98\sqrt A.
\tag{4}
\]
The first bound follows from $ac<\sqrt G$, $\sqrt D<(a+2)I$, $a\ge64$
and $R<c$; the second follows from the same elementary size estimates on
$X,Y,c$. Full rational inequalities are in \file{norms/TWO_WEIGHT_PROOF.md}.
As $HW>36$,
\[
 HM\sqrt A>\tfrac{16}{9}HW\sqrt G
   >(1+\tfrac74HW)\sqrt G>L\sqrt G+H|y'|.
\]
An automorphism flipping an irrational $\tau$ would contradict this strict
separation. Thus $\tau$ is rational and therefore integral. An automorphism
flipping irrational $u$ then contradicts $L\sqrt G>H|y'|$, so $u$ is integral.
Now $y$ is rational and $f=(e-dy^2)/B_1$ is rational and integral; $B_1>0$.

\begin{lemma}[Special auxiliary-Pell saturation]
If $m\ge2$, $m,V\in\ZZ$, and $y\in\mathbb Q$, then
\[
 m^2V^2-(m^2-1)y^2=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y\in\ZZ.
\]
\end{lemma}
This is not a claim about arbitrary rational Pell points. Write
$m^2-1=D_0s_0^2$ with $D_0$ squarefree. Writing $s_0y=p/q$ in lowest terms, $q^2$ divides squarefree $D_0$, so
$q=1$ and $s_0y\in\ZZ$. Classify the two integral Pell solutions
$(m,s_0)$ and $(|mV|,|s_0y|)$ by powers of the fundamental unit. The standard
divisibility rule for their first coordinates forces the second index to
be an odd multiple of the first; the second-coordinate divisibility then
gives $s_0\mid s_0y$. The supplied source proof also treats the zero and
boundary cases. Apply the lemma with $m=|\Delta I|$ to recover integral $y$.

Reduction modulo $H$, using $L\equiv1\pmod H$, recovers
$\gamma=(u-X-ac)/H\in\ZZ$. The restored first, main, strong and auxiliary
norms are exactly the source signed-root interface. Its rank/sign argument
recovers a genuine blank computation. Conversely choose canonical positive
source witnesses and put
\[
 r=\gamma+Wu+M\tau+y>0.
\]
This annihilates one norm factor and establishes completeness.

For exact degree, put $q_0=\beta J$, $k_0=\eta+\zeta$. The leading form is
\[
 N_{\rm top}=2^{32}105^4 i^{48}k_0^{96}w^{80}s^{176}
 q_0^{656}(q_0-F)^{16}.
\]
It is nonzero for every authentic fixed $\beta>0$, so $\deg N=1072$ and
$\deg P=1081$. The tied leading terms must not be silently discarded:
the reduced pair calculation gives $C_{0,*}=19Z_*$ and
$K_{0,*}'=256Z_*^2$, hence the factor $105^4$. The JSON schedules have
$115$, $120$, and $123$ operations for the system, product, and SOS.

\section{Twenty-four unrestricted integer unknowns}
Only $J,F,\alpha,z,\eta,\zeta$ retain positivity guards. Replace each by its
own copy of
\[
 \operatorname{Pos}(x,y,t)=1+x^2+y^2+t(t+1).
\]
This is positive and onto $\PP$, by the three-square theorem applied to
$4n-3$. Set $i=2i_0+1$, $s=2s_0+1$, $w=w_0^2$, and let $h,T,r$ be arbitrary
integers. There are exactly $6\cdot3+6=24$ unknowns. Positivity of $s$ is
\emph{not} assumed during root recovery.

\subsection{The least-operation variant and its boundary cases}
Set $W=v^2+D+2b$, $L=1+HW$, $M=L$ in~\eqref{eq:norm}.
The baseline costs $163$ operations at degree $2432$; its comparison system
costs $158$ at maximum degree $2416$. Before restoring any roots, weak
transport gives $C\ge0$ and
\[
 q(q^2-1)<R<q^4-2,\quad |Y|\ge q^3,\quad
 ac>0,\quad |H|>6,\quad \Delta>q^4,
 \quad 1<b<Q,\quad G>4.
\]
These hold also for negative $s$ and for $w=0$. Every possible conjugate
satisfies $|y'|<W$. If $H<0$, $L=1-|H|W$ is negative, but
$|L|>|H|W/2$; thus $|L|\sqrt G>|H|W$ still separates the main root.

The $X=0$ boundary must be excluded \emph{before} using the usual first-root
dominance. There $A=1$, so $\tau=\pm1$ is already integral; the main-root
separation gives integral $u$ and rational $y$. If $B_1\ne0$, recover $f$
and use saturation. If $B_1=0$, then $T=v=0$ and saturation applies directly
to $Qb^2-(Q-1)y^2=1$. In either case $y$ and $\gamma$ are integral. But the
main norm at $X=0$ would give
\[
 1=H(2ac\gamma+H\gamma^2-c^2),
\]
impossible for $|H|>1$. Hence $X\ge q\ge16$.
Now $\sqrt G<4\sqrt A$ and $|H|>6$ imply
$|HL|\sqrt A>|L|\sqrt G+|H|W$. Both roots restore as before.
Finally $T=0$ would imply $(Q-1)(y^2-b^2)=b^2-1$; with $1<b<Q$, integral
$y$ makes this impossible. Thus division by $B_1$ is justified, not assumed.

\subsection{A lower-degree ratio weight}
A useful additional tradeoff is
\[
 W=2\bigl[(iI)(1+5Rac)+v^2\bigr],\qquad L=1+HW,\qquad M=2W.
\tag{5}\label{eq:integerweight}
\]
Although $i,I,a,c$ may be negative, $iI>0$ and $ac>0$, so $W>0$.
The exact cancellation $Dc^2<GI^2$ gives
$\sqrt D/\sqrt G<|I|/|c|$. Consequently
\[
 \frac{|y'|}{\sqrt G}
 <2|iT|c^2+2+4Rac\,iI
 <iI+v^2+5Rac\,iI=W/2.
\]
Here $2|iT|\le i^2+T^2$ and $Rac\,iI>2$ are used; no positive-scale
conclusion is used. This restores the main root on the $X=0$ boundary
exactly as above. Once $X>0$, the uniform estimate
\[
 \frac{\sqrt G}{\sqrt A}
 <\left(1+\frac1{16}+\frac2{16\cdot32}\right)
   \left(1+\frac1{32}\right)=\frac{9009}{8192}<\frac98
\]
and $B_*=|H|W>8$ imply
\[
 |L|\sqrt G+|H||y'|
 <\frac{13}{8}\frac98 B_*\sqrt A<2B_*\sqrt A.
\]
Thus the first root separates with the smaller multiplier $M=2W$.
The product costs $168$ operations and has degree $1936$; the system costs
$163$ with maximum degree $1920$. Its leading norm is
$89001^4Z_*^8$, where $Z_*=d_*H_*^2R_*^2D_*^2$ has degree $240$.
The number $89001=99\cdot899$ comes from tied leading terms, not a generic
syntactic-degree estimate. An intermediate split-weight option has degree
$2256$ and $164$ operations.

\subsection{Why signed odd scale is enough}
After reconstruction, invoke the actual signed-scale theorem from source 23,
not a positive-only parent theorem. Its negative-scale argument puts
$Y=-Z$, classifies the first and main Pell indices, recovers $p=R$ from the
signed auxiliary congruences, and derives $Z>2^R$. The two possible main-root
signs then give respectively
$X\equiv-2^R\pmod{|H|}$ or $X2^R\equiv1\pmod{|H|}$, contradicting the
strict size bounds. Hence $s>0$, and source compiler soundness applies.

Completeness needs odd $s$, not just nonzero $s$. For canonical witnesses
the disjoint packed data and mask give
\[
 \pc(R)=3t+2,\qquad q=2^t,\qquad v_2(Y)=\pc(R)-2=3t.
\]
Thus $s=Y/q^3$ is odd. The same canonical witness simultaneously has odd
$i$ and square $w$; these are not three incompatible witness choices.
The new coefficient adapter proves all these promises. For the baseline
take $r=\gamma+Wu+L\tau+y$; for~\eqref{eq:integerweight} take
$r=\gamma+Wu+M\tau+y$. No sign restriction on $r$ is needed.

\subsection{Low-degree integer lifts}
The old low-degree presentations retained a separate $s$ register and
three positivity parameters with an equation $s=\operatorname{Pos}$.
Replacing that retained register by $2s_0+1$ deletes three parameters and
one guard equation, without raising any residual degree. This gives
$51$-integer quartic, $42$-integer sextic, and $36$-integer octic equations.
The saving is three in this retained-register representation, but two in the
fully substituted $26$-to-$24$ presentation. The quartic exposes two auxiliary
quotients instead of the merged $T$; the supplied proof restores the signed
rank congruences directly, rather than assume their positive quotient proof
still applies. Exact coefficient-level substitutions and degree certificates
are supplied in \file{integers/LOW_DEGREE_PROOF.md} and its JSON systems.

\section{Nine variables: shorten evaluation, then sharpen the majorant}
Source 18 gives a concrete instance of the parameterized
\file{Jones1982.Nine.wset_iff_nine}, with $\nu=32$ and dummy input $x=1$,
using the normalized positive quartic32. It does not instantiate the
separate public theorem that fixes $\nu=58$. Neither specialization is a
new universal small-variable bound. The fixed index constants and polynomial
coefficient leaves are finite recipes, not additional unknowns.

\subsection{Shared digit-polynomial evaluation}
Let $B=b^5$, $T_0=5^{32}$, $L_0=5T_0$, and
$\operatorname{expo}(\alpha)=\sum_{j=0}^{32}\alpha_j5^j$.
The expensive source term is
\[
 e(B)=\sum_{|\alpha|\le4}(z_*+c_\alpha)B^{L_0-\operatorname{expo}(\alpha)}.
\]
Define circuit registers $t_j=B^{5^j}$ and $v_j=B^{T_0-5^j}$ by
$t_{j+1}=t_j^4t_j$, $v_{32}=1$, $v_j=t_j^4v_{j+1}$. Then
\[
 B^{L_0-\operatorname{expo}(\alpha)}
 =t_{32}\,t_{32}^{4-|\alpha|}\prod_jv_j^{\alpha_j}.
\]
This identity is polynomial even at $B=0$, with no division. The dense
constant part is a degree-four complete homogeneous polynomial in
$(t_{32},v_0,\ldots,v_{32})$; its $346$ sparse corrections can use shared
multivariate Horner evaluation. Also
\[
 \sum_{j=0}^{4L_0-1}B^j
 =\prod_{j=0}^{32}(1+t_j+t_j^2+t_j^3+t_j^4)(1+q)(1+q^2),
 \quad q=t_{33},
\]
using $1+t+\cdots+t^4=1+t(1+t)(1+t^2)$. The rest of the source polynomial
is unchanged. With independent formal coefficient leaves this reduces
$20\,531$ operations to $1616$. Applying the \emph{actual fixed coefficient
recipes}, folding their literal integers and merging equal recipes, reduces
the count further to $1485$. Both variants are supplied, so the extra
specialization assumption is explicit and independently checked.

\subsection{Sharper natural-domain bounds}
In the nine-natural-variable presentation the basic inputs, shifted auxiliary
inputs and full digit polynomial are nonnegative. Sign-erasing each subtraction
in the packed expression gives $\overline R\ge|R|$. Subsequent Jones expressions
have nonnegative bounds $\overline P,\overline K,\overline A,\overline C,
\overline E,\overline G_p,\overline H$, obtained by triangle inequalities.
The full formulas appear in \file{nine/IMPROVEMENT_PROOF.md} and the DAG;
nonnegativity of $e$ follows from $z_*+c_\alpha>0$, not from positivity of
its sparse signed corrections. For nonnegative majorants,
\[
 |(a^2-1)b^2+1|\le(\bar a^2+1)\bar b^2+1
 \le((\bar a+1)\bar b+1)^2.
\]
Use
\begin{align*}
 V_0&=(\overline P+1)\overline K+2,\\
 V_1&=2\bigl[((\overline A+1)\overline C+1)
 ((\overline A+1)\overline E+1)
 ((\overline G_p+1)\overline H+1)\bigr]+1.
\end{align*}
These dominate the absolute square roots of the two radicands $a_0,a_1$
in the source ratio conditions. With its expressions $d>0,c\ge0,m$, set
\[
 \Omega=d(2m-1),\quad
 \Theta=dn+c-\Omega(c+V_0V_1),
\]
\[
 F_9=[\Theta^2-\Omega^2(a_0+V_0^2a_1)]^2
       -4\Omega^4V_0^2a_0a_1.
\tag{6}
\]
The DAG specifies every abbreviation by integer arithmetic. If $F_9=0$,
the radical combination $r_0+V_0r_1=\Theta/\Omega$ is rational. If nonzero,
squaring isolates rational, hence integral, roots. If zero, the strict
bound $|r_0|<V_0$ forces $|r_1|<1$, and both integer radicands vanish.
This covers negative radicands, zero roots and dependent square classes.
The integer $Z=c+V_0V_1+r_0+V_0r_1$ satisfies $Z\ge c+1$, so
$dn+c=d(2m-1)Z$ implies $d\mid c$ and $m>0$ because $n\ge0$.
Conversely, if both radicands are squares, $d\mid c$ and $m>0$, they give $n=(2m-1)Z-c/d\ge1$.
Thus the same ratio equivalence applies.

Writing $L_0=5^{33}$, the exact new degree is
\[
 120764L_0+368
 =14\,058\,779\,925\,107\,955\,932\,617\,187\,868,
\]
versus $161844L_0+484$ previously. The diagonal leading coefficient is
$9\cdot2^{252}$, independent of the fixed compiler tuple; tied terms have
leaders $2^{128}$ and $2^{126}$ before the final square. The independent-leaf
circuit uses $1673$ operations; exact fixed-recipe specialization uses $1542$.
The equivalent positive-domain forms use $1668$ and $1537$, respectively,
after replacing each natural variable by one less than a positive variable
and eliminating redundant shifts. Degree is unchanged. These are not
unrestricted-integer equations.

\section{A fully materialized smaller coefficient tuple}\label{sec:coeff}
Retain source 19's ordered $943$ Wang tiles and occupied ten-bit edge labels.
They are reconstructed from all $430$ actions of the literal K215 table,
including its first action and its original write/move upon entering halt.
Only after halt are stationary loops added. The language's marked periodic
tiling equivalence and independent powers-of-five padding remain unchanged.

Set $\rho=2^{125}$, $L_c=15625$, $d_c=125L_c=5^9$, $B_c=\rho^{L_c}$.
Let $E$ consist of selector positions $0,\ldots,942$, forty ten-bit copy
positions with offsets $(1023,1013,1003,983)$, dummies $1063,1064$, and
anchors $1095,1096,2195,3296$. Thus $|E|=989$. Define $w_j=1024\cdot4^j$
for $0\le j<44$. A tile's coefficient is $1$ plus the $w_j$ corresponding
to its four occupied binary labels, plus $w_{40}+\cdots+w_{43}$.
Copy and anchor coefficients are their respective $w_j$; dummy coefficients
are zero. Put $\mu=1022+\sum_{j=0}^{43}w_j$, and define
\begin{align*}
 D_C&=\rho^{30}+\rho^{62}+\rho^{1100}+\rho^{2200}
       +\sum_{e\in E}c_e(\rho^{7631-e}+\rho^{8733-e}),\\
 D_R&=\rho^{42},\\
 M_F^{\rm nat}&=4+\mu(\rho^{7631}+\rho^{8733})
  +\sum_{j=0}^{9}(\rho^{1013+j}+\rho^{1065+j})
  +\rho^{2195}+\rho^{3296}+\sum_{j=12030}^{12889}\rho^j,\\
 M_C&=B_c-1-\sum_{e\in E\setminus\{1\}}\rho^e-2\rho^{1063}.
\end{align*}
The four arithmetic constants are
\[
 \beta=B_c-1,\quad K_0=D_C+B_cD_R,\quad M_C,
 \quad M_F=M_F^{\rm nat}+B_c-1.
\tag{7}\label{eq:coeff}
\]
They are fixed by bounded finite recipes and do not depend on waiting for
the machine to halt. No coefficient from another tuple is spliced in.

\paragraph{Two distinct carry arguments.}
The selector count is below $1024$, and higher clause digits are multiples
of $1024$. The low mask $1022$ therefore forces count $0$ or $1$ first.
Only then do the $44$ base-four payload digits have sums at most $2$;
their low-bit masks force matching. It would be wrong to assume base-four
carry-freedom before selector extraction. Independently, the uniform
whole-clause mass satisfies $4\,\mathrm{mass}+8<2^{125}$ before any decoding,
so there are no radix-$\rho$ carries even for many-selector assignments.

\paragraph{Exact geometry and harmless new tests.}
The spatial targets are now $1065,\ldots,1074$ with displacement $42$;
