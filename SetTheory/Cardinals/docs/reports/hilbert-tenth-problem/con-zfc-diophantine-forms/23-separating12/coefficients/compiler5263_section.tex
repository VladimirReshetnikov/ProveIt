\section{Smaller fixed coefficients: 5,263 tiles and $B=2^{5^{16}}$}
\label{sec:coefficient5263}

The arithmetic construction can use a new coefficient tuple with the same
inputless compiler interface.  The previous smallest coefficient backend,
package~11's T4 construction, had 37,075 tiles and $B=2^{5^{18}}$.
The following refinement has 5,263 tiles and $B=2^{5^{16}}$.
In particular, the bit length of $B-1$ decreases by a factor of 25.
All four coefficient ports change together.

\subsection{A smaller local language}
Keep the original 439 cell symbols and exact completed K215 transition
table.  The symbols $0,1$ are headless tape bits; $2(q+1)+b$ represents a
head in state $q\in\{0,\ldots,215\}$ reading bit $b$; and
\[
L_0=434,\quad Q_0=435,\quad R_0=436,\quad V_0=437,\quad H_0=438
\]
are the initialization and boundary symbols.  Keep Start
$S=(434,435,436,1)$ first and the forbidden tile
$E=(438,0,0,438)$ second.  Otherwise retain precisely the old T4 tiles
$(l,c,r,n)$ satisfying three additional conditions:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Both horizontal pairs $(l,c)$ and $(c,r)$ satisfy the phase and
boundary adjacency rules.  Apart from $V_0$, adjacent cells agree on
being $H_0$ and on being initialization phases; phase edges are
$L_0L_0,L_0Q_0,Q_0R_0,R_0R_0$.  Immediately after $V_0$ a phase must be
$L_0$, immediately before it $R_0$; two adjacent $V_0$'s are forbidden.
\item If $V_0$ occurs in the horizontal triple, every ordinary cell in
that triple is blank and headless, and $Q_0$ is absent.
\item If $n=H_0$, every head in the horizontal triple is halted.
\end{enumerate}
Order all remaining tiles lexicographically.  There are exactly
\[
1751+1744+1720+16+5+5+1+9+12=5263
\]
tiles, classified respectively by center $0$, center $1$, working-head
centers, halted-head centers, $L_0,R_0,Q_0,V_0,H_0$.
For example, each ordinary headless center has
$4+2\cdot432\cdot2=1732$ ordinary successors and
$4+2\cdot2\cdot2=12$ terminal successors without a vertical boundary.
Center~0 has six additional boundary cases and the single forbidden tile.

Every retained tile is an old T4 tile, so soundness is inherited.  The
extra tile $E$ remains globally impossible: a left neighbor centered at
$H_0$ cannot have ordinary right neighbor 0.  Conversely, any finite
halting run admits two untouched blank headless cells between all visited
head positions and each boundary.  Its canonical tableau satisfies the
new rules, including at helical seams.  Width and height remain arbitrarily
large independent powers of five.  Thus the new language has a marked
periodic tableau exactly when blank K215 halts.

\subsection{The complete finite coefficient recipe}
Let $w_i=(l_i,c_i,r_i,n_i)$ be the ordered tiles and set
\[
A_c=8192,\qquad \rho=2^{78125},\qquad
B=\rho^{1953125}=2^{152587890625}=2^{5^{16}}.
\]
For $0\le u\le438$, define
\[
e_l(u)=6580+u,\quad e_c(u)=6141+u,\quad
e_r(u)=5702+u,\quad e_n(u)=5263+u.
\]
The support $\mathcal E$ consists of $0,\ldots,5262$, these four intervals,
the dummy position 7019, and the anchors
\[
7021,\quad21063,\quad63189,\quad189567.
\]
It has 7024 elements.  Put
\[
\mu=A_c-2+\sum_{j=1}^{2840}A_c^j.
\]
Define the selector, copy, anchor, and dummy coefficients by
\begin{align*}
v_i&=1+A_c^{1+l_i}+A_c^{440+c_i}+A_c^{879+r_i}
       +A_c^{1318+n_i}+\sum_{t=1}^{4}A_c^{1756+t},\\
v_{e_l(u)}&=A_c^{1+u},&v_{e_c(u)}&=A_c^{440+u},\\
v_{e_r(u)}&=A_c^{879+u},&v_{e_n(u)}&=A_c^{1318+u},\\
v_{3^{t-1}7021}&=A_c^{1756+t}\quad(1\le t\le4),&v_{7019}&=0.
\end{align*}
With $T_1=738963$, $T_2=1118101$, and $H_c=359389$, set
\begin{align*}
D_C={}&\rho^{878}+\rho^{359828}+\rho^{56168}+\rho^{168504}
 +\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}v_e
       \bigl(\rho^{T_1-e}+\rho^{T_2-e}\bigr),\\
D_R={}&\rho^{H_c},\\
M_F^0={}&\mu(\rho^{T_1}+\rho^{T_2})+
       \sum_{u=0}^{438}\rho^{6141+u}+\rho^{63189}+\rho^{189567}\\
 &+\sum_{u=0}^{438}
       \bigl(\rho^{H_c+6580+u}+\rho^{H_c+6141+u}\bigr),\\
M_C={}&B-1-\sum_{\substack{e\in\mathcal E\\e\ne1}}\rho^e
              -2\rho^{7019},\qquad
M_F^{\rm nat}=M_F^0+4.
\end{align*}
The inseparable four-tuple is
\[
\boxed{\beta=B-1,\quad K=D_C+BD_R,\quad M_C,\quad
M_F^{\rm src}=M_F^{\rm nat}+B-1.}
\]
Every exponent and finite summation limit here is a fixed integer.  These
definitions therefore specify ordinary integer coefficients, without
introducing exponential unknowns.

\subsection{Why the compressed layout preserves the compiler}
Writing $M_0=7021$ and $E_{\max}=27M_0$, the two clause bands satisfy
\[
T_2-T_1=2E_{\max}+4\equiv2\pmod4,\qquad
T_2+2E_{\max}+2=1497237<1953125.
\]
The support and upper dummy spill lie below the same radius $E_{\max}$;
all nonanchor positions, including that spill, lie below $M_0$.
Thus the original two-band synchronization and anchor-pair argument apply
to every rotation.  In particular, $(9M_0,27M_0)$ remains the unique
support pair separated by $18M_0$.

The only changed temporal-copy shift is $878=2\cdot439$: it sends the
next-copy position to the center-copy position.  The spatial shift remains
$H_c+439$.  Direct finite set checks verify all 1319 copy/anchor targets,
including absence of unintended convolution hits.  The mask population
and low-bit invariants are
\[
\operatorname{pc}(M_F^{\rm nat})=7024,\quad
\operatorname{pc}(M_C)=5^{16}-7024,\quad
M_C\equiv2\pmod4,\quad M_F^{\rm nat}\equiv4\pmod8.
\]
The sufficient carry threshold requires radix exponent only 36936,
whereas the chosen exponent is 78125.

The five-adic unit changes, but remains valid:
\[
D_C\equiv D_R\equiv2\pmod5,\qquad 2D_C-D_R\equiv2\pmod5.
\]
For power-of-five width $w$, height $H$, and $N=wH$, one has
$B\equiv q=B^N\equiv B^w\equiv2\pmod5$.  Consequently the exact
dummy-bit coefficient
\[
\Gamma=\rho^{7019}(q^2-1)
       \bigl[1+q(D_C+BD_R+B^w)\bigr]
\]
satisfies $\Gamma\equiv3\pmod5$ and is a unit modulo $5^{16}N$.
The inherited subset adjustment therefore tunes the actual packed rank
to the same temporal displacement.  It does not introduce a separately
chosen exponent.  Start remains selector~0, the forbidden tile selector~1,
and the shifted high mask remains essential.

The accompanying standard-library program independently enumerates
2,289,385 successor candidates, checks the exact scalar geometry and
carry bounds, and verifies 18 finite control tableaux with 927 windows.
These finite checks support the adapter above; the universal native
compiler and Pell theorems remain mathematical dependencies.  No new
end-to-end kernel proof is claimed.  The largest coefficient $K$ has
180,665,156,251 bits; its exact finite recipe, rather than its expansion,
is supplied.
