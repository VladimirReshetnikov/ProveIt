weights and their proof; the structured nine-variable majorants and
signed-divisor finalizer; the resulting circuits, exact-degree certificates,
independent finite checks, synthesis article and reproducible packaging.
Independent reviews here mean separate review work within this collaboration,
not an external human endorsement.

The K215 transition table and its calibration, the native compiler, all
Wang943 tile and coefficient machinery, the parent quartic, earlier norm
circuits, signed-domain interfaces, and the underlying Jones reduction are
inherited. Their sources are identified in SOURCE_MANIFEST.json, the report's
bibliography, and the preserved notices. The actual compact coefficient
tuple is reused, not claimed as a new coefficient-size record.

The supplied Jones builder is derived from package 18's nine_compressor.py;
the twelve-variable builder imports the retained package-21 circuit emitter.

numerical-calculus, machine-compiler and formal-verification contributors.

ancestry. Historical links and snapshot statements in those notices describe
their original packages.
