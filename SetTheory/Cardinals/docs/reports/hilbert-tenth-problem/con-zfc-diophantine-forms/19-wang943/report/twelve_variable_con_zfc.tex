\begin{abstract}
We review the new repository packages 09--15 and combine their primitive-root, signed-domain, ordinary-Pell and compiler ideas. The principal new polynomial has twelve strictly positive unknowns, exact degree 1257 and a fully charged 139-operation straight-line program. It eliminates four supplied witnesses at the price of one weighted primitive element. Applying rational Pell saturation before recovering the main quotient removes an unnecessary index restriction. The same idea yields a polynomial in 26 unrestricted integers of degree 2465 with 192 operations. Two earlier twelve-variable alternatives are retained with their complete proofs. Separate grouped equations improve the 15-variable degree-44 polynomial from 78 operations to 76.

A directed-signal Wang compiler with 943 tiles reduces the radix to $B=2^{5^{10}}$. Its four actual integer coefficients are supplied as compressed binary payloads: 5,312,269 bytes uncompressed and 20,260 bytes in deterministic gzip form. This is not merely a recipe for inaccessible astronomical numerals. The mathematical reduction still depends on the imported native-window/Pell and exact K215 calibration theorems. We distinguish those dependencies, new mathematical proofs, finite circuit and coefficient checks, and the absent end-to-end proof-assistant certificate. No proof of consistency, accepting K215 execution, or global minimum is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{The common normalized core}\label{sec:core}

Every symbol below other than the twelve explicitly supplied coordinates and the four fixed ports denotes a computed polynomial expression. Write
\begin{align}
 q&=\beta J+1,& C&=q-F-\alpha,& X&=wq,&Y&=sq^3,&E&=XY,\notag\\
 k&=\eta+\zeta,&c&=kY+\eta,&a&=E+Y,&H&=4a+3,&\Delta&=a^2+H=(a+2)^2-1.\label{eq:geometry}
\end{align}
Set
\begin{align}
 L&=E(kY),& A_1&=1+L(L+k),&A_2&=1+\Delta c^2,\notag\\
 R&=\bigl((q-1)(q-F)+\alpha\bigr)(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\label{eq:rank}\\
 N_k&=k-hE-R,\qquad B_0=X+ac,\notag\\
N_t&=(K_0+w)C+q-F-z(q-1).\notag
\end{align}
The symbol $z$ here is the old positive transport quotient, not package 12's shifted variable. The coefficient port $M_F$ is the \emph{shifted source port}; in Section~\ref{sec:coeff} it is $M_{F,\mathrm{source}}$, not the native field mask.

The primary construction supplies
\[
(J,F,\alpha,z,h,i,T,s,w,\eta,\zeta,t)\in\Pos^{12}.
\]
The older restricted-$i$ alternative instead supplies $j>0$ and computes $i=Hj$. The scaled-$y$ alternative keeps the same unrestricted positive $i$ as the primary construction. In either case put
\begin{align}
 I&=ic^2,& S&=\Delta I,&Q&=S^2,&D&=1+IS,&d&=Q-1,\notag\\
 v&=cT,& b&=c+RD,& e&=Q(Dv^2+b^2)-1,&B_a&=2Qvb.\label{eq:aux}
\end{align}
We eliminate four old roots:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fourroots}
 \tau^2=A_1,\qquad g^2=A_2,\qquad f^2=D,\qquad
 d y^2=e-B_af=Q(vf-b)^2-1.
\end{equation}
The eliminated main quotient is $\gamma=(g-B_0)/H$. Eliminating $g$ is therefore simultaneously a quotient-integrality problem; a purely formal resultant does not solve it.

Before recovering any roots, the inherited weak transport bootstrap applies even if $N_k=N_t=-1$. It gives
\[
 q\ge16,\quad C\ge0,\quad 0<R<E-2,\quad k>R.
\]
In particular $v,b,B_a,d$ are positive. This ordering avoids dividing by an expression whose nonvanishing has not yet been established. Strict $C>0$ need not hold in the provisional negative-unit branch.

\section{Explicit twelve-variable norm polynomials}\label{sec:norm}

\subsection{Small separation weights}
Since $\Delta>1$ and $I>1$, $D=1+\Delta I^2<\Delta^2I^2=S^2$. Every conjugate of $f$ has absolute value $\sqrt D<S$. From \eqref{eq:fourroots}, every real conjugate of $y$ satisfies
\[
 |y'|^2=\frac{Q(vf'-b)^2-1}{Q-1}<2(Sv+b)^2.
\]
Thus $Y_b=2(Sv+b)$ is an integer strict bound. Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:weights}\begin{gathered}
 W_b=\begin{cases}Y_b&\text{unscaled versions},\\dY_b&\text{scaled-$y$ version},\end{cases}
 \quad P=HW_b,\quad K=P+1,\\ M=6W_b,\quad u=6P=HM,
 \quad Z=Ht+KB_0.
\end{gathered}\end{equation}
Here $K$ is a separation weight, not the fixed port $K_0$. In particular $K\equiv1\pmod H$. The intended primitive coordinate is
\[
 t=K\gamma+M\tau+y\quad\hbox{or}\quad t=K\gamma+M\tau+dy.
\]
Because $a+2<2E$ and $c<2kY$, one has $A_2<16A_1$. Also $P\ge14$, and therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:dominance}
 u\sqrt{A_1}>K\sqrt{A_2}+HW_b,\qquad K\sqrt{A_2}>HW_b.
\end{equation}
Indeed $u\sqrt{A_1}-K\sqrt{A_2}>(2P-4)\sqrt{A_1}>P$. These inexpensive weights are the ones used in the final JSON circuits. Earlier audit notes used larger sufficient weights; they are not the final circuit definition.

\subsection{Definition without radicals or hidden quantified registers}
Work in the free rank-four algebra with formal generators $r,s$ and relations $r^2=A_1$, $s^2=A_2$. Let $W=Z-ur-Ks$. Define
\begin{align}
 F_{\mathrm{p}}(r,s)&=(dW^2-H^2e)^2-H^4B_a^2D,\label{eq:Fprimary}\\
 F_{\mathrm{s}}(r,s)&=(W^2-H^2de)^2-H^4d^2B_a^2D.\label{eq:Fscaled}
\end{align}
For the chosen version set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:resultant}
 \mathcal R=\prod_{\epsilon,\delta\in\{-1,1\}}F(\epsilon r,\delta s),
 \qquad \mathcal P_{12}=N_kN_t(1+\mathcal R)-1.
\end{equation}
The product is invariant under both formal sign changes and is an integer polynomial in the supplied unknowns. This definition does not require the specialized algebra to be a field, and works when the radicands or their product are squares. There is no new unknown $r$ or $s$ in \eqref{eq:resultant}.

For an explicit radical-free evaluator, let $(\lambda,\widetilde e,\widetilde B)=(d,e,B_a)$ for the unscaled versions and $(1,de,dB_a)$ for the scaled version. Define
\begin{align*}
 p_0&=\lambda(Z^2+u^2A_1+K^2A_2)-H^2\widetilde e,\\
 p_1&=2\lambda Zu,\quad p_2=2\lambda ZK,\quad p_3=2\lambda uK,
 \quad\ell=H^4\widetilde B^2D,\\
 f_0&=p_0^2+A_1p_1^2+A_2(p_2^2+A_1p_3^2)-\ell,\\
 f_1&=2(p_0p_1+A_2p_2p_3),\quad
 f_2=2(p_0p_2+A_1p_1p_3),\quad
 f_3=2(p_0p_3+p_1p_2),\\
 n_0&=f_0^2+A_1f_1^2-A_2(f_2^2+A_1f_3^2),\\
 n_1&=2(A_2f_2f_3-f_0f_1),\qquad
 \mathcal R=n_0^2-A_1n_1^2.
\end{align*}
These formulas are two successive quadratic norms. The archive contains the fully shared binary-gate schedules; Appendix~\ref{app:slp} prints the complete primary schedule. The three-equation alternatives are simply $N_k=N_t=1$, $\mathcal R=0$.

\subsection{The product guard}
Since $B_a$ is even, $F$ is a square modulo four in the free algebra. Consequently $\mathcal R$ is a square modulo four, as also follows from $n_1$ being even in the explicit evaluator. If $\mathcal P_{12}=0$, the three integer factors $N_k,N_t,1+\mathcal R$ are units. The last cannot be $-1$, since that would require $\mathcal R=-2$, which is not a square modulo four. Therefore
\[
 \mathcal R=0,\qquad N_k=N_t=\epsilon,\qquad\epsilon\in\{-1,1\}.
\]
No unproved assertion that every unit factor is positive is being used.

\section{Why the norm does not introduce spurious roots}\label{sec:proof12}

\subsection{Rationality by separation of actual field conjugates}
At a zero of $\mathcal R$, choose real square roots $\tau^2=A_1$, $g^2=A_2$ for a vanishing factor. In the primary case set
\[
 y=(Z-u\tau-Kg)/H,\qquad f=(H^2e-d(Z-u\tau-Kg)^2)/(H^2B_a).
\]
In the scaled version put instead
\[
 \rho=(Z-u\tau-Kg)/H,\qquad
 f=(H^2de-(Z-u\tau-Kg)^2)/(H^2dB_a),\qquad y=\rho/d.
\]
The factor identity implies $f^2=D$ and respectively $dy^2=e-B_af$ or $\rho^2=d(e-B_af)$. Every displayed denominator is nonzero by the earlier bootstrap.

The field $\mathbb Q(\tau,g)$ is a totally real Galois extension, possibly of degree one, two or four. The derived $y$ or $\rho$ and $f$ belong to that field. Every automorphism preserves the rational integer $Z$ in
\[
 Z=u\tau+Kg+H y\quad\hbox{or}\quad Z=u\tau+Kg+H\rho.
\]
All actual conjugates of the final term have absolute value less than $W_b$. If an automorphism flipped $\tau$, subtracting the two identities would contradict the first strict inequality in \eqref{eq:dominance}. Thus every automorphism fixes $\tau$. The second strict inequality then prevents a flip of $g$. Both are rational and hence integral, since their squares are integers. The last term and $f$ are then rational, and $f$ is integral for the same reason.

This proof only quantifies over actual automorphisms. It does not falsely identify all independent formal sign choices with field automorphisms when the radicals are dependent. This is the point that makes the argument valid at nongeneric specializations.

\subsection{Primary denominator recovery: saturate first}
In the primary unscaled construction, the previous Galois argument has already made $\tau,g,f$ integral and $y$ rational. Put $V=vf-b\in\Z$. The auxiliary identity is
\[
 S^2V^2-(S^2-1)y^2=1.
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:saturation} below therefore gives $y\in\Z$ without any condition on $i$ modulo $H$. Only now recover $\gamma=(g-B_0)/H$. The identity $t-M\tau=K\gamma+y$ gives $K\gamma\in\Z$, and $K\equiv1\pmod H$ forces $\gamma\in\Z$.

This order is the final simplification: rational Pell saturation must precede quotient recovery. It removes both the computed factor $i=Hj$ and the need to merge the scaled root $dy$. Completeness keeps every old positive normalized witness and sets $t=K\gamma+M\tau+y>0$. There is no new period, congruence or canonical divisibility choice.

\subsection{Earlier restricted-$i$ alternative and its period repair}

In the earlier restricted-$i$ alternative $i=Hj$, so $Q$ is divisible by $H$ and $\gcd(H,d)=1$. The denominator of rational $y$ divides $H$ from its linear formula, while its square divides $d$ from $dy^2\in\Z$. Hence $y\in\Z$. The identity
\[
 t-M\tau=K\gamma+y,\qquad \gamma=(g-B_0)/H
\]
then gives $K\gamma\in\Z$. Because $\gcd(K,H)=1$, $\gamma\in\Z$.

Completeness requires regenerating a normalized auxiliary witness with $H\mid i$. Merely taking the old index $m=2cRH$ is \emph{not} a valid argument. For instance $a=2$, $a+2=4$, $\Delta=15$, $H=11$, $R=3$ and $c=\psi_4(3)=63$ give $\psi_4(4158)=3\pmod{11}$, not zero.

Here is the repair. For $N=Hc^2$, the element $(a+2)+\xi$ is a unit in
\[
 (\Z/N\Z)[\xi]/(\xi^2-\Delta),
\]
with inverse $(a+2)-\xi$. Its finite order $e_0$ is at most $N^2$. Choose $m=2cR e_0$, or the completely bounded proof-only choice $m=2cR(N^2)!$. Then $N\mid\psi_{a+2}(m)$. Set
\[
 f=\chi_{a+2}(m),\quad i=\psi_{a+2}(m)/c^2,\quad j=i/H,
 \quad S=\Delta\psi_{a+2}(m),
 \quad V=\chi_S(R)/S,\quad y=\psi_S(R).
\]
The canonical native mask supplies $R\equiv3\pmod4$. The usual odd-index quotient identities give $V\equiv-c\pmod f$ and $V\equiv-R\pmod c$. Together with $f^2\equiv1\pmod c$ these prove that
\[
 T=(V+c+Rf^2)/(cf)
\]
is a positive integer. The normalized strong and auxiliary equations are restored with $j>0$, and $t=K\gamma+M\tau+y>0$ completes the restricted-$i$ construction. The enormous period or factorial occurs only in the proof of existence, not in the polynomial or its operation ledger.

\subsection{Rational Pell saturation and the scaled alternative}
In the scaled version, rational $\rho$ has integral square $d(e-B_af)$, so $\rho\in\Z$. The same congruence argument makes $\gamma$ integral. It remains to prove that $y=\rho/d$ is integral, although its denominator need not be coprime to $H$.

\begin{lemma}[Rational auxiliary Pell saturation]\label{lem:saturation}
If $S>1,V\in\Z$, $y\in\mathbb Q$, and
$S^2V^2-(S^2-1)y^2=1$, then $y\in\Z$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $S^2-1=D_0b_0^2$ with squarefree $D_0>1$. The number $b_0y$ is integral: its reduced denominator squared would otherwise divide the squarefree integer $D_0$. Both $(S,b_0)$ and $(|SV|,|b_0y|)$ are positive integral solutions of $x^2-D_0z^2=1$. Write them as powers $m,n$ of the least positive integral Pell unit, with coordinates $x_j,z_j$.

Since $x_m=S$ divides $x_n$, reduce $n$ modulo $2m$. In the quadratic ring modulo $x_m$, the unit to power $2m$ equals $-1$. Reflecting the remainder gives a first coordinate congruent up to sign to $x_r$, $0\le r\le m$. If $r<m$, then $1\le x_r<x_m$, impossible. Hence $n\equiv m\pmod{2m}$. Expanding a positive power of $x_m+z_m\sqrt{D_0}$ shows $z_m\mid z_n$. Therefore $b_0\mid b_0y$, and $y$ is an integer. The fundamental unit here belongs to the integer-coordinate Pell equation, not a potentially half-integral larger ring of integers.
\end{proof}
Apply the lemma with $V=vf-b$ and $Q=S^2$. This proves integrality without a restriction on $i$. Completeness simply keeps all old positive normalized witnesses and sets $t=K\gamma+M\tau+dy>0$; no modular period is needed.

\subsection{Signed recovery is an inherited theorem, not a sign convention}
The recovered $f,\tau,y,g,\gamma$ may initially be signed. Replace $\tau,y$ by their absolute values. If necessary replace $f,T$ simultaneously by their negatives, preserving $f^2$ and $Tf$. The exact signed-domain extension from the pinned sources permits the resulting signs. Its strong-rank congruences give $R=\pm p\pmod c$, where the main lower Pell coordinate is $c=\psi_{a+2}(p)$. Bounds $0<R,p<c/2$ force $p=R$ independently of the signs of $T,V,g$.

The first-index bound and Pell ratio then force $N_k=N_t=1$. Before presuming $g>0$, that ratio gives $a>X2^R$. A negative $g$ would imply $H\mid X2^R+1$, whereas $0<X2^R+1<H$. Thus $g>0$, the projection yields $X=2^R$, and $\gamma>0$ follows from the Pell coordinate identities. The remaining half-binomial, population and native-window decoding does not inspect the auxiliary signs. This is an equivalence in existence, not a coordinate-preserving bijection between every pair of witness tuples.

\section{A polynomial in 26 unrestricted integers}\label{sec:integer26}

The smaller integer lift uses the primary unscaled norm and saturation before quotient recovery. Retain positivity only for
\[
 J,F,\alpha,z,s,\eta,\zeta,
\]
and take $h,T,t$ unrestricted, $i=2r_i+1$ odd, and $w=r_w^2$. Thus $i\ne0$, while $w$ is provisionally allowed to vanish. The polynomial bound valid for signed $i,T$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:signedbound}
 W_b=2\bigl((Si)v^2+b\bigr),\quad P=HW_b,\quad K=P+1,
 \quad M=6W_b,\quad u=6P.
\end{equation}
Here $Si=\Delta i^2c^2\ge|S|$ and $v^2\ge|v|$ for integral $v$, so $W_b\ge2(|Sv|+b)$. No absolute-value gate occurs in the circuit. Use $F_{\mathrm p}$ from \eqref{eq:Fprimary}, not the scaled norm.

If $B_a\ne0$, root recovery gives $f^2=D$ and $dy^2=e-B_af$. If $B_a=0$, then $T=0$ and the factor equation directly gives $dy^2=e=Qb^2-1$. In either case all actual conjugates satisfy $|y'|<W_b$. No division by $B_a$ is performed on its zero branch.

If $w=0$, then $X=E=L=0$ and $A_1=1$, so the first recovered root is integral. The second separation inequality makes $g$ integral and $y$ rational. When $B_a\ne0$, the recovered $f$ is a rational integer and Lemma~\ref{lem:saturation}, applied to $V=vf-b$, makes $y$ integral. When $B_a=0$, apply the same lemma directly to the integral $V=-b$, without needing $f$. Now $K\gamma=t-M\tau-y\in\Z$ and $\gcd(K,H)=1$ give $\gamma\in\Z$. The main norm with $g=ac+\gamma H$ reduces to $1=0\pmod H$, impossible. This excludes $w=0$ before using the positive-scale first-root estimate.

For $w\ge1$, $X\ge q\ge16$ and full Galois separation makes $\tau,g$ integral and $y$ rational. The same two saturation branches make $y$ integral and then $\gamma$ integral. The weak transport bounds imply $h\ge0$, $0<R<a<\Delta$ and $1<b<Q$. If $T=0$, the already established equation $Qb^2-(Q-1)y^2=1$ gives
\[

\subsection{Global marked-tiling equivalence, including seams}
V occurs on one vertical side exactly when it occurs on both, so V columns persist in time. From a Start, the horizontal phase chain must meet V columns on both sides by periodicity; there is no phase cycle containing Q. Between the nearest V columns the initialization row is $L^uQR^v$ with $u\ge0,v\ge1$. Its predecessor is an H-reset row. The initialization implements the literal first action, and directed signals thereafter transfer the unique head exactly one cell in its genuine direction. A head cannot cross V, since no V seam accepts a directed state signal.

Temporal periodicity supplies a subsequent reset row. Immediately above its first occurrence every cell must be a finish tile, and the unique head is halted. A finish cannot mix with an ordinary successor across that strip: in the next row HB edges propagate across all H cells, while ordinary tiles have no HB edge. This proves soundness without assuming a globally unique Start.

Conversely, pad a finite blank halting run with at least two never-visited blank cells next to each V boundary, and with absorbing halt rows. Width $w_c$ and height $h_c$ may be any sufficiently large integers, independently, hence powers of five. At the V seam, choose its west color from time $t-1$ on the last cell and its east color from time $t$ on the first cell. The nine independent seam choices are exactly what is needed for
\[
 \operatorname{tile}(x,t)=G(x\bmod w_c,(t+\lfloor x/w_c\rfloor)\bmod h_c).
\]
This has periods $(w_c,-1)$ and $(0,h_c)$. The cyclic coordinate
$i=-(x-x_{\rm Start})-w_c(t-1)\pmod{w_ch_c}$ puts Start at zero, the right neighbor at shift $-1$ and the temporal neighbor at shift $-w_c$. Conversely any cyclic word with these two matching shifts pulls back to a periodic plane, without requiring a divisibility or coprimality condition between the shifts and word length. Start's distinct north and south colors exclude temporal shift zero. This preserves all independent padding required by the native dummy-control theorem.

\subsection{Why the compact masks preserve the compiler}
A clause sum $\sum c_ed_e$ has no carries in base $A$: its units digit is at most 943 and every other digit at most 944, below 1024. The low mask $A-2$ allows selector count zero or one. For each bit the tested digit is selected-bit plus copy-bit; $A-3$ allows precisely zero or two. Thus empty cells have zero copies and anchors, while an occupied cell's copies equal all forty tile bits and all anchors are one. The 78 padding bits start at bit 450, above every possible clause sum, so they test identically zero data. Saturating the masks therefore adds no constraint on valid cells.

The occupancy bit propagates from Start through the whole cyclic word along the spatial shift one. The remaining bits enforce the exact directed edge equalities. This replaces the previous one-hot occupancy argument rather than simply deleting it.

The population identities are
\[

The compact layout uses $H_c=5497$, $T_1=7631$, $T_2=8733$ and the exact support
\[

\]
The possible upper-dummy spill position 1064 already belongs to this set. The earlier sufficient interval conditions, such as a band gap exceeding twice the support span, are no longer assumed. What is required and checked is isolation at the positions actually tested.

At $1013+j$, bare shift 30 sees only the current south copy $983+j$, paired with the temporal north copy. At $6520+j$, bare shift 5517 sees east copy $1003+j$, paired with the spatial west copy $1023+j$ shifted by $D_R$. The small support component has span 1096, and subtracting those intervals from the two high anchors lands in gaps of the support.

At the clause targets themselves, the bare and spatial shifts see no support. The band gap $1102$ is absent from $\mathcal E-\mathcal E$: $2195-1102=1093$ and $3296-1102=2194$ are absent, and the small component is too short. Thus the two possible rotation-hit sets $(T_1-\mathcal E)\bmod L_c$ and $(T_2-\mathcal E)\bmod L_c$ are disjoint. Every binary rotation leaves a clean clause band. Supports may overlap away from tested positions; the uniform carry bound already includes both bands and those unused positions are not decoded.

For a nonaligned binary rotation $b_ct+\varepsilon$, $1\le\varepsilon\le b_c-2$ makes all rotated digits even, and $\varepsilon=b_c-1$ permits at most one odd upper-dummy spill. Start needs odd hits at two distinct anchors, excluding both cases. For an aligned rotation the unique support pair at difference $3296-2195=1101$ is $(2195,3296)$. There is no circular wrap because $L_c=15625>2\cdot3296$. Thus the simultaneous anchor intersection is exactly $\{0\}$ and whole-cell alignment follows.

The no-carry threshold still has 529 bits, below $\rho_c=2^{625}$. The largest unshifted exponent is $8733+3296=12029<15625$, and all reflected exponents are positive. These exact inequalities replace the old conservative spacing. Finally $\rho_c\equiv2\pmod5$, the band gap is $2\pmod4$, and paired clause terms cancel, leaving

This is a unit, sufficient for the retained dummy-control theorem to tune the \emph{actual packed rank}. The temporal stride is not treated as an independent exponent. Arbitrarily large power-of-five padding supplies the theorem's no-wrap hypotheses. These are the explicit interface reasons that the Wang tuple can replace the old authentic tuple.

\section{Further research questions and unsuccessful directions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Reduce norm cost, not just its bound.} The twelve-variable improvement raises the cost from the previous 13-variable 84/85-gate frontier to 139. A more economical norm evaluator, or a different denominator-clearing primitive element, could improve this tradeoff. Any proposed cancellation must be checked at dependent-radical and zero-cross-term specializations.
\item \textbf{Remove another supplied parameter safely.} The remaining positive geometry, transport and strong-index parameters cannot be dropped merely because they have canonical values in completeness. Soundness must rule out every new zero branch. No eleven-positive-variable construction is established here.
\item \textbf{Shrink the integer lift.} The 26-integer count uses seven independent three-integer positivity maps. A reduction would need a genuinely weaker signed domain or a jointly justified positivity representation, not an uncharged change of domain. The $w=0$ and $T=0$ branches show why the issue is substantive.
\item \textbf{Go below 67 operations.} Grouping improves low-degree schedules but does not beat the existing minimum-operation polynomial. The 60-operation comparison system does not immediately imply a 66-operation product because its shifted residual is not already the necessary unit.
\item \textbf{Improve the quartic independently.} Bounded direct-elimination searches from the pinned 32-variable quartic, including positive root shears, did not establish fewer than 32 positive variables at degree four. The bounded beam search is not a lower bound. The 25-variable sextic and 20-variable degree-eight forms remain different tradeoffs.
\item \textbf{Reduce coefficient height further.} Wang943 makes materialization practical. Tighter signal alphabets, binary encodings or anchor geometry may reduce it again, but local tile counts alone do not prove a global compiler. Preserve the marked periodic-plane argument, helical padding, both population identities and actual-rank dummy tuning.
\item \textbf{Formalize the bridge.} The largest remaining verification gap is a kernel-checked composition of machine calibration, compiler, signed Pell restoration and the new radical elimination. Finite byte checks and independently reviewed prose do not close it by themselves.
\end{enumerate}
