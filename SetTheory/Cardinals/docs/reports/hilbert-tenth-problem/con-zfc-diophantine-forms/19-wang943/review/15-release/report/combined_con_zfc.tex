\begin{abstract}
The eight repository packages do not have a single winner under every notion of simplicity. Combining their inputless compiler, ordinary strong Pell equation, strong-root elimination, and careful low-degree lifting gives several concrete improvements over the previous 15-variable, 67-operation, degree-155 polynomial and 34-variable quartic. We obtain a 14-positive-variable polynomial with 75 operations and degree 293; a 33-positive-variable quartic; a 25-variable sextic; and a 20-variable degree-eight equation. A 15-variable six-equation system retains the previous 61-operation cost while reducing maximum degree from 60 to 28. Positive-gap substitutions reduce that maximum further to 22 at a cost of two operations. A separate signed-domain argument yields smaller unrestricted-integer presentations.

The coefficient instance is also improved: direct blank-tape K215, restricted local windows, and tighter clause spacing reduce the radix from $2^{5^{67}}$ in package 06 to $2^{5^{43}}$, without changing the arithmetic skeleton. All four coefficients have bounded exact definitions; they are not expanded into decimal numerals. We distinguish mathematical proofs, imported Pell/compiler and machine theorems, finite independent checks, and the absent end-to-end Lean/Rocq certificate. No claim of global minimality or proof of $\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$ is made.
\end{abstract}

\section{Conclusions and the precise claim}

Let $\N=\{0,1,\ldots\}$ and let $\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$ mean that no finite syntactic ZFC proof of a fixed contradiction exists in an adequate effective first-order calculus. The repository review is pinned to
\[
\texttt{3b201cdea32d2c816f5f3d20734051f25fd3ef14}.
\]
The eight packages were originally based on revision
\texttt{cdd582bec13f908df3711fa9e05d1ddac368a5db}. Their provenance is preserved rather than treating the previous report as the source of every idea.

\begin{theorem}[Mathematical construction]\label{thm:main}
Fix the four integer coefficients $\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F^*$ by Section~\ref{sec:coefficients}. For each of the positive-domain polynomials or systems explicitly specified below, ZFC proves that its nonexistence of positive integer solutions is equivalent to $\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$. Replacing every positive variable $v$ by $n_v+1$ gives the same statement over $\N$, without changing the number of variables or the total degree.

The proof composes the pinned K215 blank-halting calibration, the native-window/Pell compiler, and the reductions established here. It is an ordinary mathematical proof formalizable in ZFC, not a newly checked proof-assistant theorem or a supplied derivation encoded in ZFC syntax.
\end{theorem}

Several apparent near-ties conceal real distinctions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Package 02's normalized system has degree 60, while its ordinary-strong system has degree 28 at the same 63-operation cost. Package 03 has the analogous distinction at 64 operations. Package 05's degree-34 system instead unscales a normalized factor; that is not the ordinary replacement.
\item Packages 06 and 08 have identical 67-operation and 61-operation arithmetic skeletons up to one register rename. Their coefficient tuples are different and must not be mixed. Thus the independent inputless discoveries are credited to both packages, while package 06 supplies the smaller raw-machine coefficient baseline.
\item Root elimination in packages 03 and 05 reduces unknown count but greatly increases degree. This is the idea combined with the inputless skeleton below; the new contribution is the 14-variable specialization and its correctly ordered sign argument, not the general elimination trick.
\item Package 07's initially signed repunit, and package 02's squared marker, require existential reconstruction proofs. They are not free algebraic substitutions. The present report does not claim to have safely removed a further repunit variable using those ideas.
\item Variable counts over $\Pos$, $\N$, and $\Z$ are not interchangeable. In particular the previous 34-positive-variable quartic cannot be compared directly with a larger unrestricted-integer lift without charging the positivity conversion.
\end{enumerate}

The machine alphabets also differ. Packages 01--05 use respectively 3510, 3502, 2628, 669, and 4376 cell symbols; package 06 uses 439; packages 07 and 08 use 4366. Package 04's small alphabet comes from a different two-sided compiler. Package 06's raw, padded-halting K215 machine avoids the normalization and input-erasing overhead. The improvement in Section~\ref{sec:coefficients} starts there rather than splicing its coefficients into an unrelated fixed-marker formula.

\section{Shared inputless arithmetic}\label{sec:shared}

Use the fifteen positive unknowns
\[
(J,F,\alpha,z,f,h,i,T,s,w,\tau,\eta,\zeta,y,\gamma).
\]
All symbols defined in the following display are polynomial abbreviations, not extra unknowns:
\begin{align}
q&=\beta J+1,&X&=wq,\label{eq:core1}\\
Y&=sq^3,&E&=XY,\\
k&=\eta+\zeta,&c&=kY+\eta,\\
a&=E+Y,&H&=4a+3,\\
\Delta&=a^2+H,&D&=X+ac+\gamma H,\\
C&=q-F-\alpha,&L_0&=E(kY),\\
G&=(q-1)(q-F)+\alpha=q(q-F)-C,\\
R&=G(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F^*)J,\\
N_1&=\tau^2-L_0(L_0+k),&N_2&=D^2-\Delta c^2,\\
N_I&=k-hE-R,\\
N_T&=(K_0+w)C+q-F-z(q-1),\\
V&=c(Tf-1)-Rf^2.\label{eq:V}
\end{align}
In these expressions $K_0$ is the fixed transport coefficient, not the temporary main-norm register denoted $K$ in an older lift. Also $M_F^*$ is the shifted source coefficient, not the native one-cell field mask.

For reference, the previous compact polynomial has
\[
Q_n=(\Delta i c^2)^2,\quad
N_A=Q_n(V^2-y^2)+y^2,\quad N_S=\Delta f^2-Q_n,
\]
\begin{equation}\label{eq:P67}
P_{67}=N_1N_2N_AN_IN_TN_S-\Delta.
\end{equation}
Its six-equation presentation is
\[
N_1=N_2=N_A=N_I=N_T=1,\qquad N_S=\Delta.
\]
A proof-only cancellation of $\Delta>0$ precedes the integer-unit argument. One must not simply assert that all six factors of \eqref{eq:P67} are units: $N_S$ equals $\Delta$, not 1.

\subsection{The ordinary strong replacement}

Retain \eqref{eq:core1}--\eqref{eq:V} and define
\[
I=ic^2,\quad Q_o=\Delta(f^2-1),\quad
N_A^o=Q_o(V^2-y^2)+y^2,\quad N_S^o=1+I^2-Q_o.
\]
The recommended short system is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ordinarysystem}
\boxed{N_1=N_2=N_A^o=N_I=N_T=1,\qquad I^2=Q_o.}
\end{equation}
It uses 61 operations and has exact maximum degree 28. The polynomial
\[
P_o=N_1N_2N_A^oN_IN_TN_S^o-1
\]
uses 69 operations and has exact degree 99. Its factor degrees are
$22,18,28,7,2,22$. The sum of the six squared residuals in
\eqref{eq:ordinarysystem} costs 78 operations and has exact degree 56.

This is not a same-$i$ identity with the normalized system. Completeness sends
$i_n$ to $i_o=\Delta i_n$ and keeps the other coordinates. Soundness uses the ordinary strong-rank theorem; it does not assume $\Delta\mid i_o$.

\subsection{Positive gaps lower degree}

Set $\tau=L_0+t$ and $y=V+e$, where $t,e$ are new positive coordinates replacing $\tau,y$, not additional variables. Then
\[
N_1=t^2+L_0(2t-k),\qquad
N_A^o=Q_o(-2Ve-e^2)+(V+e)^2.
\]
The product has 71 operations and degree 83, while the six-equation system has 63 operations and maximum degree 22; its sum of squares has 80 operations and degree 44. The literal shared-square schedule, rather than a naively expanded display, defines the operation count.

On an old positive zero, $\tau^2-L_0^2=L_0k+1>0$, and the auxiliary norm gives $y^2-V^2=(V^2-1)/(Q_o-1)>0$. Thus $t,e>0$. Conversely, on a positive sheared zero, the unit argument gives $Q_o=I^2>1$ and auxiliary norm 1. The restored $V+e$ is nonzero, so initially use $|V+e|$ to obtain an old positive zero. Its theorem gives $V>1$, and hence $V+e>0$. This establishes an actual positive-zero bijection, without assuming the needed auxiliary sign beforehand.

For exact degree, put $Q_0=\beta J$, $k_0=\eta+\zeta$ and
$T_2=w(Q_0-F-\alpha)-zQ_0$. The sheared factor degrees are
$12,18,22,7,2,22$, and their product has nonzero leading form
\[
16h\gamma i^2w^6s^{13}k_0^7Q_0^{45}Tf^3e(2t-k_0)T_2.
\]
This proves degree 83 uniformly for $\beta>0$, rather than only by sampled evaluations.

\section{Eliminating the strong root gives fourteen variables}\label{sec:fourteen}

Delete $f$ from the supplied unknowns, retaining the other fourteen variables in Section~\ref{sec:shared}. Keep all abbreviations not involving $f$ and put
\begin{align}
Q&=(\Delta i c^2)^2,&d&=1+\Delta i^2c^4,\\
b&=c+Rd,&u&=cT,\\
A_*&=Q(du^2+b^2-y^2)+y^2-1,&B_*&=2Qub,\\
H_*&=A_*^2-dB_*^2.
\end{align}
The new single equation is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:P14}
\boxed{P_{14}=N_1N_2N_IN_T(H_*+1)-1=0.}
\end{equation}
It has 75 operations and degree 293. The equivalent positive-domain system is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:system14}
\boxed{N_1=N_2=N_I=N_T=1,\qquad H_*=0.}
\end{equation}
It has 69 operations and maximum degree 244. Its sum of squares has 82 operations and degree 488. The circuit evaluates $dB_*^2$ as
$4(Qdu^2)(Qb^2)$, so the displayed auxiliary $B_*$ is not an uncharged extra gate.

\subsection{The order of the soundness proof}

The delicate point is the sign of the recovered $f$. A rational square root is integral, but is not automatically positive. Nor may one invoke the original positive-auxiliary theorem while $T$ or $V$ is still signed. The following order avoids both gaps.

\paragraph{Unit signs and the boundary $C=0$.}
At a positive zero of \eqref{eq:P14}, all five factors are units. Since $B_*$ is even,
$H_*+1\equiv A_*^2+1\pmod4$, so it cannot be $-1$. Hence $H_*=0$.
The consecutive-product negative-Pell descent gives $N_1=1$;
$\Delta\equiv0$ or $3\pmod4$ excludes $N_2=-1$. Thus
$N_I=N_T=\epsilon\in\{1,-1\}$.

Transport gives $C\geq0$, because
\[
q-F-z(q-1)=1-F-(z-1)(q-1)\leq0
\]
and $K_0+w\geq2$ excludes $C<0$. Do not yet assume $C>0$.
The native mask ranges and $M_F^*=M_F+\beta$ give
\begin{gather}
q\leq G\leq q^2-q,\qquad
0<(M_C+qM_F^*)J<(q-1)(1+2q),\\
q(q^2-1)<R<q^4-q^3+q^2-1<q^4-2,\label{eq:weakR}\\
R>3q+1,\qquad q\geq16,\qquad E\geq q^4>R+2,\quad a>R+2.
\end{gather}
These are pre-decoding bounds, valid even at $C=0$.
In particular $b,u,B_*>0$ in the all-positive construction.

\paragraph{An integral root with no sign assumption.}
Set $f=A_*/B_*$. From $H_*=0$, $f^2=d\in\Z$. Reducing a rational fraction to coprime terms proves $f\in\Z\setminus\{0\}$. Put $V=uf-b$. The exact polynomial identity
\[
QV^2-(Q-1)y^2-1
=A_*-B_*f+Qu^2(f^2-d)
\]
yields the auxiliary norm, and $f^2-\Delta i^2c^4=1$ is automatic.
For the next argument one may replace $f$ by $|f|$ and $T$ by
$\operatorname{sign}(f)T$; $Tf$ and all equations are unchanged. Now $f>0$, but $T$ may have either sign.

\paragraph{Signed auxiliary rank.}
Define Pell coordinates by
\[
\chi_A(n)+\psi_A(n)\sqrt{A^2-1}
=(A+\sqrt{A^2-1})^n.
\]
Put $A_0=a+2$ and $P_0=2XY^2+1$. First and main norm classification gives
\[
k=2\psi_{P_0}(n),\quad \tau=\chi_{P_0}(n),\quad
c=\psi_{A_0}(p),\quad D=\chi_{A_0}(p).
\]
Here $p>n$, and the index congruence gives
$2n\equiv R+\epsilon\pmod E$, so $n\geq(R-1)/2\geq24$.
Pell growth gives $c>2p$, while positive $h$ gives $c>2R$.
The normalized strong norm gives
\[
f=\chi_{A_0}(m),\qquad \psi_{A_0}(m)=ic^2.
\]
Strong divisibility gives $p\mid m$. Expanding the unit to exponent $m/p$ modulo $c^2$ then gives $c\mid(m/p)$; hence $c\mid m$ and $m>2p$.

Let $\rho=\Delta ic^2$, so $Q=\rho^2$. Auxiliary norm classification gives
\[
|\rho V|=\chi_\rho(\ell),\qquad y=\psi_\rho(\ell).
\]
Divisibility of the first coordinate by $\rho$ forces $\ell=2v+1$ odd. The integer polynomials $Q_v$ defined by
\[
\chi_Z(2v+1)=ZQ_v(Z^2)
\]
satisfy
\[
Q_v(-\Delta)=(-1)^v\psi_{A_0}(\ell),\qquad
Q_v(0)=(-1)^v\ell.
\]
Since $V\equiv-c\pmod f$, $V\equiv-R\pmod c$, and
$\rho^2\equiv-\Delta\pmod f$, squaring the first quotient congruence and using Pell doubling gives
\[
\chi_{A_0}(2\ell)\equiv\chi_{A_0}(2p)\pmod{\chi_{A_0}(m)}.
\]
The inherited plus step-down lemma with $0<2p<m$ gives
$\ell\equiv\pm p\pmod m$, hence modulo $c$. The second congruence gives
$R\equiv\pm p\pmod c$. Because $0<R,p<c/2$, necessarily $p=R$.
The possible sign of $V$ is absorbed into these two plus/minus alternatives.

Writing $2n=R+\epsilon+vE$ with $v\geq0$, the bounds $E>R+2$ and $n<p=R$ exclude $v\geq1$. If $\epsilon=-1$, then $p=2n+1$, and Pell duplication yields $c>k(Y+1)$, contradicting $kY<c<k(Y+1)$. Thus $\epsilon=1$, $2n=R+1$, and only now $N_T=1$ excludes $C=0$.

\paragraph{Power extraction and decoding before auxiliary positivity.}
Put $r=(R-1)/2$. The first/main Pell ratio gives
\[
Y>X^r/3,\qquad a>X^{r+1}/3>2^R.
\]
The main projection gives $X\equiv2^R\pmod H$. Both positive representatives are smaller than $a$, so $X=2^R$.
Thus $q=2^t$ for some integer $t$, and $R>3q>3t$ restores the symmetric $q^3$ scales. The ratio-only half-binomial calculation, which uses no auxiliary sign, yields
\[
0<\frac ck-\frac\xi2<\frac{16r}{X+1}<\frac12,
\quad \xi=\frac{(X+1)^{2r}}{X^r}=M+\theta,
\]
\[
M=\binom{2r}{r}+\sum_{j=1}^r\binom{2r}{r+j}X^j,
\qquad 0<\theta<\frac14.
\]
The integer $M$ is even; the interval $Y<c/k<Y+1$ forces $Y=M/2$.
Central-binomial valuation gives $v_2(Y)=\pc(r)-1$, and therefore
$\pc(R)\geq3t+2$.

The exact repunit gives $q=B^N$, and inverse-population packing yields the two authentic shifted masks
\[
(C-1)\mathbin{\&}(M_CJ+1)=0,\qquad
F\mathbin{\&}(M_FJ-1)=0,
\]
where $M_F$ here is the native mask, not $M_F^*$. They type the Start-only word, put Start at its origin, and forbid selector 1. In particular they give $C\equiv1\pmod4$, and only now $R\equiv3\pmod4$.
The physical transport quotient $z_{\rm old}=z-1+wC$ is positive. The unchanged compiler therefore decodes a genuine initialized halting K215 run. This proves soundness without asserting that the recovered root or auxiliary coordinate was positive.

\paragraph{Converse and optional positive restoration.}
An old positive $P_{67}$ zero satisfies $f^2=d$ and $A_*=B_*f$. Deleting $f$ gives a $P_{14}$ zero. Conversely, if a positive old-style tuple rather than just acceptance is desired, after decoding choose fresh
\begin{gather*}
m=2cR,\quad f'=\chi_{A_0}(m),\quad i'=\psi_{A_0}(m)/c^2,\\
\rho'=\Delta\psi_{A_0}(m),\quad
V'=\chi_{\rho'}(R)/\rho',\quad y'=\psi_{\rho'}(R),\\
o'=(V'+c)/f',\qquad T'=(o'+Rf')/c.
\end{gather*}
Binomial expansion proves the $c^2$ divisibility. The established $R\equiv3\pmod4$ gives the two minus congruences and integrality of $o'$; then $\gcd(f',c)=1$ gives integrality of $T'$. All are positive. This is an existence map and may change $i,T,y$; it is not a same-tuple inverse.

\subsection{Exact degree}

With $Q_0,k_0,T_2$ as above, the four retained factors have exact degrees
$22,18,7,2$ and product leader
\[
8h\gamma k_0^3w^5s^8Q_0^{29}T_2.
\]
The main factor's degree 18 includes the cancellation of its $a^2c^2$ terms. The degrees of $Q,d,b,u$ are $46,34,38,6$. The unique degree-122 term of $A_*$ is $Qb^2$, so $H_*$ has degree 244: the competing term $dB_*^2$ has degree 214. The degree-293 leader of $P_{14}$ is
\[
8h\gamma i^{12}k_0^{27}w^{21}s^{48}Q_0^{177}(Q_0-F)^4T_2.
\]
For example the coefficient of
$J^{182}h\gamma i^{12}\eta^{27}w^{21}s^{48}z$ is $-8\beta^{182}\neq0$.

\section{A quartic in thirty three positive variables}\label{sec:quartic}

Use the fifteen original witnesses of Section~\ref{sec:shared}, together with
\[
N,E,P,c,a,X,D,C_2,G,L,R,F_2,V_0,V,g,I,B_a,\Delta.
\]
These are eighteen additional unknowns, for a total of 33. In this section only
$q=\beta J+1$, $k=\eta+\zeta$, $H=4a+3$, $C=q-F-\alpha$ are abbreviations.
Here is the entire system of 24 equations:
\begin{align}
L&=q^2-1,& N&=q(L+1),\label{eq:q33start}\\
X&=wq,&a&=E+sN,\\
E&=X(a-E),&c&=k(a-E)+\eta,\\
P&=E(c-\eta)+k,&\tau^2&=P(P-k)+1,\\
D&=X+ac+\gamma H,&\Delta&=a^2+H,\\
C_2&=c^2,&D^2-\Delta C_2&=1,\\
k&=R+1+hE,&G&=(q-1)(q-F)+\alpha,\\
R&=GL+(M_C+qM_F^*)J,\\
(K_0+w)C+q-F-z(q-1)&=1,\\
F_2&=f^2,&V_0&=Tf-1,\\
V&=cV_0-RF_2,&g&=y^2-V^2,\\
I&=iC_2,&B_a&=I^2,\\
B_a&=\Delta(F_2-1),&y^2-B_ag&=1.\label{eq:q33end}
\end{align}
Each literal residual has degree at most two. Defining $Q_4$ as the sum of the squares of these 24 residuals gives one polynomial of exact degree four. This costs one fewer positive variable and one fewer quadratic equation than the previous 34-variable lift.

The changes are precise. Delete the old register $K=D^2-1=\Delta C_2$, replace its two equations by $D^2-\Delta C_2=1$, and replace $I=iK$ by $I=iC_2$. The latter changes normalized strong to ordinary strong, so this is not just algebraic register elimination. From a normalized solution use $i_o=\Delta i_n$; the values of $I,B_a,f$ and every retained register stay unchanged.

\subsection{Ordinary strong soundness and the missing divisibility}

In the system, transport immediately gives $C>0$. The pre-decoding estimates strengthen to
\[
(2q-1)(q^2-1)<R<q^4-q^3,\qquad E>R+2,\qquad a>R+2.
\]
First and main Pell classification gives $p>n\geq(R+1)/2$, and elementary Pell growth then gives
\[
c>(a+2)\Delta^2,\qquad c>2p,\qquad c>2R.
\]
The imported generic ordinary strong-rank theorem applies to
\[
c=\psi_{a+2}(p),\qquad I=ic^2>0,\qquad I^2=\Delta(f^2-1).
\]
It yields
\[
f=\chi_{a+2}(m),\quad I=\Delta\psi_{a+2}(m),\quad
p\mid m,\quad c\mid m,\quad m\geq c>2p.
\]
In particular
\[
f^2-1=I\psi_{a+2}(m)=ic^2\psi_{a+2}(m),
\]
so $c^2\mid(f^2-1)$. This divisibility must not be obtained by informally dividing the original ordinary equation before the theorem is applied.

The supplied positive $V$ now permits the integer restoration
\[
o=cT-Rf,\qquad j=Tf-1-R(f^2-1)/c,
\]
with $of=V+c>0$ and $jc=V+R>0$. Hence $o,j>0$, and the auxiliary step-down applies. It gives $p=R$, and $E>R+2$ excludes an index wrap. The remaining power, half-binomial, mask, and Start-only decoding arguments are unchanged. For the compact ordinary product, the unit-sign argument and the auxiliary gap estimate first recover these same positive coordinates; package 02 supplies precisely this ordinary-strong bootstrap.

The imported ordinary-rank statement is a mathematical theorem, not a new result of the finite JSON audit. This dependency is explicit in the artifact rather than silently replacing it by a claimed normalized identity.

\section{A sextic in twenty five positive variables}\label{sec:sextic}

\[

\]
Only $q=\beta J+1$, $k=\eta+\zeta$, $H=4a+3$, $C=q-F-\alpha$ are abbreviations. The complete 16-equation system is
\begin{align}
L&=q^2-1,\\
a&=E+sq(L+1),\\
E&=wq(a-E),\\
c&=k(a-E)+\eta,\\
t^2+E(c-\eta)(2t-k)&=1,\\
D&=wq+ac+\gamma H,\\
\Delta&=a^2+H,\\
D^2-1&=\Delta c^2,\\
k&=R+1+hE,\\
R&=L\big((q-1)(q-F)+\alpha\big)+(M_C+qM_F^*)J,\\
(K_0+w)C+q-F-z(q-1)&=1,\\
V&=c(Tf-1)-Rf^2,\\
I&=i(D^2-1),\\
B_a&=I^2,\\
B_a&=\Delta(f^2-1),\\

\end{align}
All residuals have degree at most three. Their sum of squares $Q_6$ has exact degree six. This system retains the normalized strong equation, unlike the quartic in Section~\ref{sec:quartic}.

There is an exact positive-zero bijection with the previous 34-variable system. Eliminate
\[
N=q(L+1),\ X=wq,\ C_2=c^2,\ K=D^2-1,
\]
\[
G=(q-1)(q-F)+\alpha,\ F_2=f^2,\ V_0=Tf-1,\ g=y^2-V^2.
\]
Also eliminate $P$ and shear the first root by
$\ell_0=E(c-\eta)$, $t=\tau-\ell_0$, so $P=\ell_0+k$.
Because $a-E=sq^3>0$ and $c-\eta=k(a-E)>0$, one has $\ell_0>0$;
$\tau^2=\ell_0(\ell_0+k)+1>\ell_0^2$ gives $t>0$.
Conversely $\tau=t+\ell_0>0$ and $P=\ell_0+k>0$ restore the original equations. The old positivity lemma, applied to the restored supplied witnesses, gives the positivity of every deleted register. In particular $G>0$ follows from transport, $V_0>0$ from the strong bound $f>2c$, and $g>0$ from the auxiliary gap.

The shear is necessary for the cubic count: eliminating $P$ from the unshifted first norm would give a quartic. Eliminating both $\Delta$ and $F_2$ would also make the strong equation quartic, so $\Delta$ is retained. The first-norm residual's nonzero cubic leading form proves degree six for the sum of squares; leading forms of real squares cannot cancel identically.

Retain the original fifteen witnesses and only five registers $E,c,a,V,I$.

\section{Domains and unrestricted integer equations}\label{sec:integers}

A sum of squared integer residuals vanishes exactly when every residual vanishes. This justifies all the single-polynomial conversions above over $\Pos$ or $\N$; the shift $v=n_v+1$ preserves degree. It does not justify forgetting positivity and allowing arbitrary signed variables in the compact products.

A separate signed-domain argument shows that only these nine original ports must remain positive:
\[
J,F,\alpha,z,i,s,w,\eta,\zeta.
\]
The six remaining supplied coordinates $f,h,T,\tau,y,\gamma$ may be signed in the normalized 15-coordinate construction. All derived registers may be signed as well when their defining equations are retained. The statement is equality of existential truth values, not a pointwise absolute-value bijection.

The proof first derives $C\geq0$ and \eqref{eq:weakR}. From the index equation, $h\geq0$; $h=0$ would put the first Pell coefficient $k$ strictly between 2 and $XY^2$, where a consecutive-square argument excludes it. Thus $h>0$. First, main, strong, and auxiliary Pell classification then works with absolute roots. The signed quotient argument in Section~\ref{sec:fourteen} gives $p=R$ without any assumption on the sign of $T$ or $V$.

The sign of the main root is not skipped. Write $A=a+2$ and
$e_R=\chi_A(R)-a\psi_A(R)$. Projection gives $e_R\equiv2^R\pmod H$.
If $D=-\chi_A(R)$, the defining equation for $D$, multiplied by the identity
\[
(\chi_A(R)-ac)(\chi_A(R)+ac)=1+Hc^2,
\]
forces $X2^R+1\equiv0\pmod H$. The ratio bound is strong enough to give
$0<X2^R+1<a+1<H$, a contradiction. Hence $D>0$, then $X=2^R$, and
$e_R=2c-\psi_A(R-1)>c>X$ gives $\gamma>0$. Decoding now proceeds with no sign assumptions on the auxiliaries. The ordinary construction uses its generic strong-rank theorem to supply the same divisibility and bounds.

Define
\[
\PP(u,v,r)=1+u^2+v^2+r(r+1).
\]
It is always positive and is surjective onto $\Pos$. Indeed $4n-3\equiv1\pmod4$ is never of the excluded form $4^a(8b+7)$, so Legendre's three-square theorem expresses it as one odd square and two even squares; division by four gives the displayed formula. Replace each of the nine positive ports by its own copy of $\PP$. This adds eighteen variables, not three variables for every coordinate.

For the root-eliminated construction, only five of the remaining coordinates are signed. Its signed-domain extension also requires ruling out $T=0$, so that division by $B_*$ remains legal. At $T=0$, $u=0$, $H_*=0$ forces $A_*=0$ and thus the auxiliary norm with $|V|=b$. The pre-decoding bounds give $1<b<Q$, while the nontrivial auxiliary Pell gap gives $b\geq2Q-1$, a contradiction. This closes the denominator case rather than assuming it away.

\subsection{The 439 cell codes and the local language}

Use codes 0 and 1 for headless tape symbols, $2(q+1)+s$ for a head in state $0\leq q\leq215$ on symbol $s\in\{0,1\}$, and codes 434,435,436 for phases $L,Q,R$ with respective payloads blank/headless, blank/state 0, blank/headless. Codes 437 and 438 are vertical and horizontal boundaries, denoted $V_b,H_b$; they have no payload or phase.

For a $3\times3$ row-major window, write $P,L_c,C_c,R_c,N$ for its prior, left, center, right, and next cross entries. Time increases downward. A window is \emph{Good} precisely when these finite tests hold:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Vertical-boundary membership agrees at $C_c,N$; $C_c,R_c$ are not both $V_b$; $C_c,N$ are not both $H_b$. If neither $C_c$ nor $R_c$ is $V_b$, their horizontal-boundary memberships agree.
\item A boundary center has no further original center conditions. At an interior center, a phase is present exactly when $P=H_b$. When a phase is present, a $V_b$ on the left forces $L$, and one on the right forces $R$; unless $R_c=V_b$, the adjacent phase pair is one of $LL,LQ,QR,RR$.
\item An interior head with $N=H_b$ is in state 215. A head may not move through an adjacent $V_b$.
\item At an interior center, if $N\neq H_b$, its payload is the exact deterministic update of the current three payloads. A center head writes; otherwise the symbol stays. Incoming heads come from a right-moving left head, stationary center head, or left-moving right head. More than one incoming head is forbidden. A vertical boundary is blank and headless for this test; a horizontal neighbor cannot supply an interior update.
\item In each of the window's three rows, at most one entry is headed. There are 433 headed codes and six headless codes. This is the added restriction relative to package 06.
\end{enumerate}
These are asymmetric center tests, not extra unmentioned tests centered at all nine cells. The first four clauses are package 06's compiler; the last clause is the new language restriction.

\[

\subsection{Why the extra head restriction preserves halting}

Soundness follows from the original local conditions. More explicitly, overlapping windows with cyclic offsets $-1$ and $-h$ reconstruct a plane by taking the center at cyclic index $i-d_x-hd_y$ for offset $(d_x,d_y)$. This remains valid if offsets coincide. Horizontal periodicity and the phase graph $LL,LQ,QR,RR$ force vertical boundaries on both sides of any occurrence of $S$, because that graph has no cycle through $Q$. Vertical propagation gives a finite strip. The horizontal row above $Q$ propagates across it, and temporal periodicity gives a next such row. Between them lies one blank initialized computation with a unique state-0 head; the update and no-escape rules preserve it, and the last interior head must be in state 215.

Completeness needs a seam argument, not just ``a machine has one head.'' Given a finite halting run, choose a strip with its leftmost and rightmost two interior cells outside every visited head position. Pad with absorbing halt rows, and let the finite rectangle be $G$ of width $w_b$ and height $H_t$. Extend it helically by
\[
\mathcal T(x,t)=G\bigl(x\bmod w_b,\ (t+\lfloor x/w_b\rfloor)\bmod H_t\bigr).
\]
Within a strip every three-cell row has at most one head. A three-cell row crossing a helical seam meets the boundary or only its permanently headless two-cell margins. It cannot collect heads at two different time offsets. Thus the added restriction holds everywhere. The periods are $(w_b,-1)$ and $(0,H_t)$; cyclic coordinate
\[
i=-(x-x_S)-w_b(t-1)\pmod{w_bH_t}
\]
places $S$ at zero and gives right/next-time offsets $-1,-w_b$.
Width and height can independently be padded to arbitrarily large powers of five, as required by the compiler's Boolean dummy controls.

The exact window count is independently reproduced by two methods. For a headed row center the corners have $6^2=36$ assignments; for a headless center they have $6^2+2\cdot6\cdot433=5232$. The original predicate depends only on the cross, so these weights give an exact finite count. The C++ exhaustive center/pair calculation and Python category calculation agree for each of the 439 centers, not merely in their grand total.

\section{Further directions that are worth testing}\label{sec:future}

The following are research questions, not advertised improvements.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can the signed repunit idea of package 07 eliminate another inputless coordinate while retaining the asymmetric $X=wq$ bootstrap? Its $X=wq^2$ proof cannot simply be transplanted. A successful proof would need pre-decoding packing bounds and a delayed sign recovery.
\item Can strong-root elimination be combined with an ordinary strong certificate to beat the present variable/degree tradeoff? Squaring a rational reconstruction without controlling its denominator and signed auxiliary rank is not enough.
\item Can the nine genuinely positive ports be reduced further? Each reduction saves two unrestricted-integer variables under the three-variable positivity parametrization. The signs of the data/field budget and scale ports are structural, so every proposed relaxation needs a soundness proof.
\item Can one remove another register from the 33-variable quadratic system without creating a cubic residual or sacrificing a necessary positivity argument? The 25-variable and 20-variable systems show the cost of moving along this tradeoff.
\item Can the local language be pruned by additional globally necessary restrictions while preserving all helical seams and independently paddable widths and heights? A concrete next test is consistency of the relative positions and states of observed heads in consecutive window rows with the actual transition, excluding initialization boundaries. The two-cell seam margins may support such a restriction, but it has not been implemented or claimed here. Exact counting alone does not establish the completeness of a pruning rule.
\item The most valuable confidence improvement is an end-to-end formalization of the specific compiler interface and new reductions. Existing K215 endpoint certification does not automatically certify this Diophantine bridge.
\item Classical universal-polynomial results give fewer unknowns in existential representations, including nine-variable bounds. Extracting a competitive, completely instantiated coefficient recipe and arithmetic circuit is a different task. The present fourteen-variable result is not a global record.
\end{enumerate}
