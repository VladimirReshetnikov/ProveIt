# Independent review of the restricted raw-machine coefficient instance

The new relation is a subset of the package-06 relation, so its soundness implication is inherited once the literal K215 table and overlap directions are kept fixed. Completeness is not inferred merely from a single-head machine: a helical seam compares distinct times in adjacent strips. Two permanently headless interior columns on each side of each vertical boundary ensure every three-cell row crossing such a seam has no head. Every other three-cell row belongs to one true computation row and has at most one head. Width and time padding remain independently arbitrary, so the required powers-of-five choices remain available.

The forbidden window E has no heads and survives the restriction. Its previous center H followed by center V violates vertical-bit propagation when the previous position is itself tested; hence E remains impossible globally. The marked window S uses the actual first transition (0,0) to (1,R,93), and has one head in each of its headed rows. No auxiliary machine or zero-length initialization is introduced.

There are 433 headed codes (432 ordinary heads and phase Q) and six headless codes. A row with headed center has 36 allowed corner pairs; a headless center has 36+2*6*433=5232. These weights must be applied separately to the top and bottom rows, while restricting the center row before summation. The independently authored C++ and Python counts implement these distinct steps, rather than multiplying an unconditional corner count.

The shorter band gap g=4*ceil(Emax/2)+2 is the least integer greater than 2Emax congruent to 2 modulo 4. L>=T2+2Emax+2 implies T2+Emax<L and L-g>Emax. These are the clean-band and wraparound inequalities; no full 4Emax gap is required. With rho=2 modulo 5, the paired clause terms cancel since rho^g=-1 modulo 5. The remaining four shifts give 2DC-DR=3 modulo 5. Thus the optional correction is genuinely unnecessary.

Padding count, native-position count and coefficient-mass bounds agree with the original modified75 compiler contract. The new numerical constants must be recomputed for the restricted and reordered alphabet: they are not interchangeable with package06 or package08 values. In particular the final MF port remains MF_native+B-1.

This review found no mathematical defect in the restriction, padding, or shorter-band change. Its conclusion is ordinary mathematics relative to the cited native compiler, not a new proof-assistant certificate. The full alphabet and giant coefficients were not materialized.
