# Attribution and status

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project with an OpenAI assistant
and parallel assistant-agent research and review, 22 October 2026.
The independent reviews in this archive are separate work within that
collaboration; they are not external human peer review.

The new contribution consists of:

- Relative root separation yielding the degree-1081 equation in twelve
  positive variables and its 110-operation shifted system.
- Signed separation and rational Pell saturation yielding the equation
  in twenty-four unrestricted integers and its 157-operation shifted system.
- A parity-separated placement of copy tests for the inherited 943-tile
  language, reducing the largest fixed coefficient to 9,766,251 bits.
- The accompanying proofs, exact-degree certificates, generators,
  independent finite checks, manuscript, and reproduction packaging.

These contributions reuse substantial mathematics and code from ProveIt
snapshot a477d0fc8b6feda53796835564a988015e744d4d. The main dependencies are
the fixed inputless arithmetic compiler and its Pell lemmas, the K215
machine and exact ZFC-refutation calibration, packages 19--23, and their
credited ancestors. The manuscript, SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json, and the
coefficient package's provenance identify the relevant sources.
