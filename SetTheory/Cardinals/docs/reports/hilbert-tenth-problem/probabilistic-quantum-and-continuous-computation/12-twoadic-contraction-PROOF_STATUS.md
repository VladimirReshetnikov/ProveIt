# Proof status and audit boundaries

## Mathematical dependencies

1. The binary two-tape-number encoding is proved directly, including the
   left-move formula `R' = a + 2*w + 4*B`.
2. The unit computation map loses at most `r+2` bits of precision.
3. The radial map `F_h(2**t*u) = 2**h(t)*G(u)` is a strict contraction whenever
   `h(t) >= t+r+3`. The proof treats equal and unequal input valuations
   separately. The clock need not be monotone.
4. Finite-precision computability is proved by a residue algorithm, without
   an equality test for an arbitrary infinite 2-adic stream.
5. Exact simulation is a stepwise induction. A machine halting at time H
   reaches zero at dynamical time H+1, including H=0.
6. Finite-state realization is proved using a bounded bootstrap and a FIFO
   buffer. It is not inferred just from the Lipschitz property.
7. Necessity in the clock classification follows from eventual periodicity
   of the internal state after a sequence of zero input bits. A fixed
   nonhalting unit suffix detects the exact added delay. Sufficiency uses
   a finite leading-zero controller and finitely many fixed delays.
8. Differentiability at zero forces derivative zero along halting rays.
   A fixed nonhalting ray then forces the added delay to tend to infinity;
   the same valuation estimate proves sufficiency uniformly over units.
9. Scalar analytic rigidity uses the first nonzero Taylor coefficient to
   isolate the zero in a small ball. Global contraction gives a uniform
   entry time into that ball. An identically zero germ is treated separately.
10. The bounded quartic is a sum of squares over natural witnesses. Every
    residual is quadratic or linear. Unique base-four input digits, natural
    quotient/remainder, and deterministic padded transitions establish
    uniqueness of all witnesses.
11. The unbounded polynomial representation is inherited from MRDP after
    recursive enumerability is proved. Ordinary circuit quadratization
    yields degree at most four with more witnesses, but does not establish
    uniqueness of the original MRDP witnesses.

## Assertions that are NOT made

- No finite-resolution/clopen computational universality is claimed.
- No one-pass finite automaton is claimed to recognize all r.e. languages.
- No global C-infinity regularity is deduced from flatness at zero.
- No multivariate analytic rigidity theorem is claimed.
- No fixed-arity single-fold MRDP theorem follows from the bounded compiler.
- No minimal state count or minimal witness count is asserted.
- The demonstration machine is not universal.
- The unbounded universal polynomial has not been expanded into coefficients.
- No new Lean proof, repository rebuild, or independent priority verification
  is claimed.

## Exact computational checks

`verification_report.json` is generated by the included code, not hand-entered
as a simulated log. Its 121,822 checks were completed using exact Python
integers and a deterministic seed. Tests include finite residue comparisons,
full transducer evaluation, certificate counts, and perturbations of valid
witnesses and public inputs. The expanded quartic is checked against its
residual form at the accepting example.

These tests are evidence about the implementation. The article's proofs,
not finite sampling, justify statements about all inputs and all precisions.
The differentiability, analytic-rigidity, and general clock-classification
proofs are mathematical arguments; they are not claimed to be exhaustively
verified by these finite tests.
