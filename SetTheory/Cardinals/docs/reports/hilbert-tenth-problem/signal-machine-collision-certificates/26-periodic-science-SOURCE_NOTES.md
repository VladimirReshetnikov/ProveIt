## Selection

Three candidate directions were considered: exact accumulation/deadline arithmetic hierarchy; periodic collision-word acceleration; perturbation-stable finite histories. The first risks reproducing known non-c.e. forecast/liveness observations, and the third overlaps the repository's finite collision chambers and perturbation-radius theorem. The selected problem is a substantive missing semantic step: certify *every repetition of one supplied complete collision macro*, including infinite strict collision guards and the physical elapsed-time clock.

The proof's restriction to at most four live signals is used only to derive a collision-anchored return section of dimension at most two and hence explicit quadratic sign tests. It is not claimed to be a maximum decidable population. Signal count and speed count are different quantities.

## Primary literature read

- Florent Becker, Mathieu Chapelle, Jerome Durand-Lose, Vincent Levorato, Maxime Senot, [*Abstract Geometrical Computation 8: Small Machines, Accumulations & Rationality*, arXiv:1307.6468v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1307.6468v1). Definition 1 explicitly requires distinct incoming and outgoing speeds; the outgoing co-location convention appears in Section 2.1. Section 3.1, Figure 7 and Lemma 6 supply the classical shrinking-wall shuttle. The three-signal/four-speed accumulator is prior work. Adding a spectator here does not make the basic Zeno mechanism new
- Joel Ouaknine and James Worrell, [*Positivity Problems for Low-Order Linear Recurrence Sequences*](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/james.worrell/pos12.pdf), [arXiv:1307.2779](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.2779). The introduction and Section 6 were checked, including the explicit difference between strict and non-strict positivity and the qualification on their order-five result. The current proof is only order two, supplies every case directly, and does not import a general Positivity solution

## Proposed contribution, with no priority claim

The proposed contribution is the explicit composition of collision-anchored dimension reduction, complete chronological guards, all-cases order-two positivity, and a deterministic natural sign compiler, together with exact observable-clock summability. The finite-schema chamber, elementary order-two recurrence facts, Boolean arithmetic, rational generating functions, and contracting shuttle are not individually claimed new. The four-signal spectator example isolates the weak limiting boundary and the failure of a whole-matrix spectral-radius test. The rational-limit theorem gives a separate obstruction to periodic-macro descriptions of irrational accumulations.

No public repository was modified and nothing was published.
