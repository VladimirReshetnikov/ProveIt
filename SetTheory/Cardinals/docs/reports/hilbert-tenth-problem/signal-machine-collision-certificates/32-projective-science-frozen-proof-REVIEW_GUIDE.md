## Evidence boundary

The complete conventional proof depends on the two preserved prior compiler proofs. The new static checker supports its algebra but is not a proof assistant. No physical trajectories were simulated and no upstream executable or saved schedule was run. Rule-table generation declares a word; it does not execute it.

The primary-source audit establishes close precedents and the boundary of the stated result. In particular, a coordinate conjugacy is not a physical implementation of that conjugacy in the original marker coordinates.
