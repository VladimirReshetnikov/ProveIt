## Alhazov and Imai 2016

A. Alhazov and K. Imai, Particle Complexity of Universal Finite Number-Conserving Cellular Automata, CANDAR 2016, pages 209–214.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/CANDAR.2016.0045

Publisher page: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7818615

The previously verified publisher abstract describes finite-support nonnegative-integer-state NCCAs, radius-one simulation via two-register machines, and a five-particle universal construction when a sufficiently large finite radius is allowed. The full paper was unavailable in this check, and a fresh direct publisher fetch failed. The abstract gives an upper bound, not a claim that five is minimal, and does not establish a binary five-particle construction.
