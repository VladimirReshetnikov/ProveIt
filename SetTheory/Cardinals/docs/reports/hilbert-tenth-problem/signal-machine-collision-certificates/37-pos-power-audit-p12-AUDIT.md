## 3. Independent reconstruction and soundness

All twelve leaves positive imply w≥b≥2, y≥C≥1, beta≥5, v=y²q_v≥y≥1, t≥C≥1, M>bo>0, d>0, A>0, and q_alpha≥1.

### 3.1 Recover alpha

Set alpha=A/d as a rational number. H5=0 gives

    alpha²=1+((w+1)²−1)(wg)².

The right side is an integer and exceeds w². A rational p/q in lowest terms with q>0 can have integer square only if q² divides p², forcing q=1. Therefore alpha is an integer. Since A/d>0, alpha>w≥b. Its old adapter alpha−1 is positive. Rearranging A=d alpha gives M=2b alpha−b²−1.

This integrality recovery is independent of u. The predecessor's shortcut alpha=beta−u q_alpha is unavailable before u has been restored, so invoking H5 first avoids circularity.

### 3.2 Recover u and its sign

Set u=U/(d q_alpha). Its denominator is nonzero. From H2=0,

    u²=1+(alpha²−1)v².

The right side is integral, so the same rational-square lemma forces u to be integral. Because v≥1 and alpha≥2, |u|≥alpha>0. The definition of U yields beta=alpha+u q_alpha. If u<0, then u≤−alpha and the positive-integer bound q_alpha≥1 gives beta≤alpha−alpha q_alpha≤0, contradicting beta≥5. Thus u is a positive integer.

Both the integrality of u and the integer domain q_alpha≥1 matter. Assuming only q_alpha>0 over the rationals would not establish that inequality.

### 3.3 Restore every old domain and equation

Set

    x=y(alpha−b)+bo+M q_r,
    s=x+u q_sigma.

These are positive integers, since alpha>b, bo>0, M>0, and the quotients are natural. Direct identities give X=dx and S=d q_alpha s. Dividing H1 by d² and H3 by d²q_alpha² yields the x/y and s/t Pell norms. H2 supplies the u/v norm, H4 the second w slack, and H5 the auxiliary norm. The other ten old equations are identities from the definitions. In particular all fifteen residuals of the 22-leaf module vanish.

The restored w,M,x,y,u,v,s,t are positive. beta−1=4yq_b>0 and alpha−1>0 restore their old adapters. The old natural q_alpha has adapter q_alpha+1; all retained natural aliases retain their own positive adapters. No eliminated positive-domain obligation is omitted.
