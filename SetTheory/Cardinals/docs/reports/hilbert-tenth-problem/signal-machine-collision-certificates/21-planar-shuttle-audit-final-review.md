## Optional exact-radius strengthening

The suggested strengthening is valid for this particular rule, without implying optimality among other possible constructions.

An exact-component indicator is the square-free polynomial

`M_(P,a)(c)= ∏_(p∈P+a)c_p · ∏_(q∈N₂(P+a)\(P+a))(1−c_q)`.

The local output equals the central input plus sixteen signed translated indicators: 2 E, 4 W, 4 R, and 6 L. This equality is valid on all binary inputs because only genuine isolated components are recognized and different component outputs cannot overlap. The resulting expression is already multilinear in the 78 possible input variables.

The halo cardinalities, and hence indicator degrees, are 30, 35, 40, and 45 respectively. The six L terms have distinct degree-45 top monomials. For each one the coefficient is nonzero: its halo has 45 sites, its required input has 3 sites, and thus the top coefficient is `(-1)^42=1` before the rewrite's plus/minus sign. Lower-degree E/W/R terms cannot cancel these monomials, and different L halos cannot cancel one another.

Their six halo supports cover exactly the rectangle `[-6,6]×[-3,2]`. Therefore every one of its 78 variables appears in a nonzero monomial of the unique real multilinear polynomial representing the Boolean local function. A variable appearing in this polynomial is essential: if the function were independent of it, uniqueness of multilinear interpolation on the binary cube would force all coefficients containing that variable to vanish. Hence all 78 input cells are essential, and G has exact centered Chebyshev radius 6. Translation gives the corresponding rectangle `[-6,6]×[-4,1]` and exact radius 6 for F.
