\begin{theorem}[Even-rank nonextension to free83]
\label{thm:free}
Let $A\ge4$ be even, $\D=A^2-1$, $p\ge2$ even,
$c=\ps A p$, and $R$ odd. Retain five outer factors with product
$P_5=1$. There are no positive integers $f,S,T,y_*$ satisfying
\begin{align}
 V&=c(Tf-1)-Rf^2,\label{eq:freeV}\\
 \Na&=S^2V^2-(S^2-1)y_*^2,\qquad
 \Ns=\D f^2-S^2,\label{eq:freenorms}\\
 \Na\Ns&=\D.\label{eq:freeproduct}
\end{align}
There is no sign restriction on the intermediate integer $V$ and no
squarefreeness assumption on $\D$. Thus no Report45 outer tuple can
be transferred to free83 merely by changing these four auxiliaries.
\end{theorem}

The first two results are exact within each fixed outer tuple. They
give no lower bound for certificates from a different outer construction.
The third excludes one broad invariant sector of free83.
During final preparation, a separate commit-pinned construction was
independently audited: the unchanged inherited free83 compiler recipe
has infinitely many full positive zeros at every positive ordinary
input on every valid fixed-program slice, using prime main indices
$p\equiv1\pmod4$ \cite{primecollapse}. That all-input collapse is
compatible with our nonextension theorem for fixed even main indices.
It excludes neither other 83-operation circuits nor different
coefficient recipes. No new universal operation bound, generic
soundness theorem, or priority claim is made here.
