The third and fourth README sources distinguish fixed universal polynomial
bounds from bounded substrate interfaces. The fourth explicitly reports an
existing order-free self-assembly quadratic with first-arrival ranks and a
whole-grid terminality extension. The present paper credits that work and
does not claim to originate quadratic self-assembly certificates.

The full merged `article.tex` was too large for the GitHub fetch interface.
No full proof audit or exhaustive nonduplication claim is made. Indexed GitHub
search could reflect a later main commit, so comparisons are tied to the
explicitly pinned README content rather than inferred from a moving branch.

## Primary literature consulted

- Winfree, *Algorithmic Self-Assembly of DNA*, Caltech thesis (1998),
  doi:10.7907/HBBV-PF79. Primary thesis abstract and metadata: computational
  universality of algorithmic self-assembly; not a new numerical alphabet here.
- van Emden and Kowalski, JACM 23(4) (1976), 733–742,
  doi:10.1145/321978.321991. Author's bibliography and author-posted text:
  classical Horn program semantics. Also van Emden's arXiv:cs/0602098 abstract.
- Cochet-Terrasson, Gaubert, Gunawardena (1999),
  doi:10.1080/026811199281967. Publisher abstract and metadata: min/max
  fixed-point methods for discrete-event systems predate this work.
- Hendricks, Patitz, Rogers, arXiv:1608.03036: the directed intrinsic
  universality obstruction is not the same as ordinary computational universality.
- Caballero, Gomez, Schweller, Wylie, JCSS 136 (2023), 1–16,
  doi:10.1016/j.jcss.2023.03.002. Publisher preview and authors' university
  record: prebuilt multi-tile attachments change verification complexity.
- Matiyasevich (2010), doi:10.1007/s10958-010-0179-4. Publisher abstract:
  the single-fold/finite-fold question is a different uniform representation problem.
- Ginsburg and Spanier (1966), doi:10.2140/pjm.1966.16.285. Publisher author
  index and bibliographic metadata: the classical Presburger/semilinear equivalence.

These are not claimed to be a comprehensive survey. In particular, no assertion
of first historical discovery is based on a search failing to find a match.

## Results proved in this article

- Unique total capped causal semantics for the positive-delay exclusive-site model.
- Degree-at-most-two, orthant-nonnegative compiler with a unique full natural zero.
- Exact witness, affine-square, product and gate allocations.
- Symbolic-cap selector that does not increase the degree with variable delays.
- Witness-height and sparse-description bounds.
- Optimized total Horn closure compiler and the stated fixed-candidate tile allocation.
- Explicit occupancy-free-relaxation counterexample and compatible-box union argument.
- Finite timing-phase decomposition, explicit-outcome exponential lower bound,
  and a nonmonotone timing family.
- Direct Horn simulation of deterministic Turing machines and the permanent-absence
  obstruction for a fixed universal program.

## Not claimed

- A new universal 87-operation record or any improvement over it.
- A fixed-arity universal quadratic polynomial obtained by quantifying a box size.
- A resolution of general single-fold/finite-fold MRDP.
- Correctness for zero/negative delays, negative glues, inhibition, stochastic
  kinetics, detachment, consumption, or arbitrary multi-site attachments.
- Enumeration of all nondeterministic aTAM outcomes by the deterministic eager model.
- A newly instantiated numerical universal tile set.
- Kernel verification, independent peer review, or exhaustive integer testing.
