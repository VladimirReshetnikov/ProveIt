# License and provenance

This package is prepared for private research review. It contains a new research exposition and independently written verification/compiler programs, together with the minimum numeric machine data needed to reproduce the results. It does not claim to relicense any third-party work.

## Included upstream mathematical data

The two files under `source/` are the original fixed Waterfall matrix and source Turing-machine transition table from Iijil's MTGPrograms repository. Their exact origin and hashes are recorded in `SOURCE-PROVENANCE.md`. The source TM file retains its attribution to Neary and Woods.

No repository-wide license was observed in the inspected upstream repository. The files are included as factual mathematical machine specifications needed for reproducibility, not as a grant to redistribute the upstream repository's software or prose. The copied Go compiler and repository README are deliberately absent. A reader wishing to redistribute upstream software should check the then-current repository terms or obtain the relevant permission.

## Primary paper and language specification

Neary and Woods retain credit for the published universal Turing machine and its universality proof. Their paper is cited and linked, but neither the article PDF nor page images are bundled. The author-deposited archive identifies its copy as CC BY-NC-SA; this package does not purport to broaden those terms.

The Esolang specification is cited via a fixed revision, whose page states a CC0 public-domain dedication. No substantial copied article text is bundled.

## New material

The LaTeX research note and Python checks were prepared with AI assistance for this research task. No blanket open-source or publication license is assigned in this private review package. This statement is provenance information, not legal advice or a claim of rights over mathematical facts.
