## Primary literature consulted

1. James P. Jones, *Universal Diophantine equation*, Journal of Symbolic Logic
   47(3) (1982), 549–571, DOI 10.2307/2273588.
   https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-symbolic-logic/article/abs/universal-diophantine-equation/113EBF3D928DFC6011C52BECAE81A087
   Used for the classical universal Diophantine representability interface.

2. Tien D. Kieu, *A reformulation of Hilbert's tenth problem through Quantum
   Mechanics*, arXiv:quant-ph/0111063v2, October 8, 2003 (initial submission 2001).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0111063
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0111063

   equivalence on printed page 3 were inspected. No proposed physical
   hypercomputation claim is used. The arXiv version label, rather than the
   PDF's automatically generated title-page date, identifies this source.

   https://doi.org/10.1007/s10958-010-0179-4
   Used for the distinction between general, finite-fold, and single-fold
   representations and the need not to conflate polynomial and exponential
   representation results.

   *The quest for Diophantine finite-fold-ness*, Le Matematiche 76(1) (2021).
