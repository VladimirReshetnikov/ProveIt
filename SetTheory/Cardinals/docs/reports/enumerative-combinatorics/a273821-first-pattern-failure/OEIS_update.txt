FORMULA
For n > k >= 1,
T(n,k) = 2^(k-1)*binomial(2*n-2*k,n-k-1)
         - binomial(2*n-k-1,n-k-1).
T(n,n) = 2^(n-1), n >= 1.

G.f.:
F(x,y) = x*y/(1-2*x*y)
       + x^3*y^2*C(x)^3/((1-2*x*y)*(1-x*y*C(x))),
where C(x) is the Catalan generating function.
This is algebraically the same as the expression presently marked
conjectural. The accompanying report supplies a combinatorial proof.

For fixed k >= 2,
T(n,k) ~ ((k-1)*(k+4)/2^(k+3))*4^n/(sqrt(Pi)*n^(3/2)).
Both T(n,k)^(1/n) and T(n+1,k)/T(n,k) tend to 4.

For 1 <= k <= n,
sum(T(n,h), h=k..n)
 = 2^(k-1)*binomial(2*n-2*k+1,n-k)
   - binomial(2*n-k,n-k-1),
with a negative lower binomial argument interpreted as zero.

sum(2^k*T(n,k), k=1..n) = (n+2)*binomial(2*n,n)-4^n.

COMMENTS
Under the uniform measure on 123-avoiding permutations, the statistic
has limiting probabilities (k-1)*(k+4)/2^(k+3), k>=2, with mean 9/2
and variance 21/4.

Under the measure proportional to 2^k, k/n converges to Beta(1,1/2),
with cumulative distribution 1-sqrt(1-t), 0<=t<=1.

PROOF SYNOPSIS
Insert successive minima. If d is the length of the terminal decreasing
suffix, the 123-preserving child states are 1,...,d+1. Their descendant
generating function is C(x)^(d+1). Until a 132 occurs, only appending or
inserting before the last entry is safe. The number of safe size-k
prefixes permitting a first failing insertion with j>=2 entries to its
right is 2^(k-j). Hence, for n>k,
T(n,k) = sum(2^(k-j)*[x^(n-k-1)]C(x)^(j+1), j=2..k).
Summing yields the g.f.; Catalan coefficient extraction and a binomial
telescoper yield the explicit entry formula.
