# Sources and literature-status audit

All listed sources were checked on 20 September 2026. Only primary mathematical
sources are used for the article's substantive dependencies.

## Selected open problem and exact counting machinery

Charlie Hill, Ambrose Luo, Vu Trinh, Andrés R. Vindas-Meléndez,
*Lattice slices, Ehrhart polynomials, and magic positivity of generalized
parking-function polytopes*, arXiv:2607.15503v1, submitted 16 July 2026.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15503v1
- https://arxiv.org/html/2607.15503v1
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.15503

Conjecture 8.1 is on printed page 27; the PDF page was inspected visually.
It explicitly leaves arbitrary positive vectors open. Theorem 7.2 treats
(a,b,...,b). Section 8 describes finite experiments in dimension three and
some dimension-four examples. Theorem 3.5 gives lattice layers, Theorem 4.1
lattice recursion, and Section 5 polynomiality and dilation. These inputs are
credited and rederived, not claimed as discoveries of this package.

The arXiv version history showed v1 only. Searches for the arXiv identifier,
conjecture number, title, magic positivity with generalized parking-function
polytopes, and coefficientwise positivity did not locate a later resolution.
The author's institutional publication page also lists the preprint:
https://math.hmc.edu/arvm/math-articles/
This does not establish priority against inaccessible or unpublished work.

## Reciprocity

I. G. Macdonald, *Polynomials associated with finite cell-complexes*,
J. London Math. Soc. (2) 4 (1971), 181–192.
https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s2-4.1.181

The publisher DOI could not be opened by the web tool. The metadata was
cross-checked against bibliographies in primary published papers. An accessible
independent proof source for the reciprocity theorem is:

Matthias Beck and Mike Develin, *On Stanley's reciprocity theorem for rational
cones*, 2004. https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0409562

Its theorem implies the Ehrhart–Macdonald identity used in the article.

## Related context, not proof inputs

Luis Ferroni and Akihiro Higashitani, *Examples and counterexamples in Ehrhart
theory*, arXiv:2307.10852v4, https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.10852.
This earlier work motivates the magic-positivity question; the exact target
for this investigation is the later explicit Conjecture 8.1 above.

Pitman–Stanley polytopes and their magic positivity are a different family;
results about them were not substituted for a proof about X_n(b). Similarly,
the exclusion manifest's lucky-parking-spots statistic is not the lattice
point enumerator of this convex hull.
