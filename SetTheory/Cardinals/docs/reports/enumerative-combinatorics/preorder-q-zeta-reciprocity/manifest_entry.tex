\entry{Support Reciprocity and the Full $q$-Zeta Conjecture for Preorders}
{enumerative-combinatorics/preorder-q-zeta-support-reciprocity}
{article.pdf}{preorder\_q\_zeta\_support\_reciprocity.zip}
{A complete proposed proof of the full $q$-refinement of Conjecture~4.9,
 equation~(21), of Athanasiadis and Chapoton (arXiv:2605.26916v1), beyond the
 ordinary $q=1$ case already listed. Proves the stronger identity counting
 maximal lattice points with each exact support by a signed Boolean sum, and
 proves it twice by independent arguments. The first proof reduces the negative
 evaluation by weighted incidence inversion and then evaluates the signed sums
 geometrically, in either of two interchangeable ways: by identifying maximal
 points supported in $T$ with interior lattice points of
 $Q_{\tau^*}(\mathbf1+\mathbf1_T)$ through Postnikov's bipartite draconian
 duality, or, importing only Postnikov's lattice-count theorem, by residual
 Hall matchings and a standalone Euler identity for every bipartite graph. The
 second proof uses no incidence matrix, matching theorem or second polytope:
 independent-capacity reciprocity, a single specialization annihilating every
 non-maximal layer, and the involution $\mathcal T f(z)=(1-z)f(-z/(1-z))$.
 Only the second reaches every $[-r]_q$, where it gives nonnegative integral
 coefficients with a colored-composition meaning, monic degree $rn$ and lowest
 exponent the number of maximal equivalence classes; only the first reaches
 arbitrary normalized height functions. Includes transversal-matroid and Tutte
 interpretations, a characterization of feasible supports, explicit coefficient
 formulas, a product rule, three obstructions bounding purity, transitivity and
 unit capacities, and three independent exact verifiers covering all 7,332
 labelled preorders of sizes zero through five plus further seeded cases at two
 different seeds. Merges three earlier drafts of the same result; cites the
 earlier $q=1$ package as prior work. AI-assisted, unrefereed research draft;
 not formally verified.}
