## Primary external research

1. Felix Roehrle and Martin Ulirsch, *Logarithmic Concavity of Bimatroids*.
   Annals of Combinatorics 30 (2026), 501-523; online 14 August 2025.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s00026-025-00780-z
   https://arxiv.org/html/2402.15317v3
   Theorem A uses the smaller shore, not actual rank. Example 2.4 gives the
   relation bimatroid; Example 2.3 gives matrix representations.

2. Christos A. Athanasiadis and Frederic Chapoton, *Polytopes and posets
   associated to preorders*, arXiv:2605.26916, v1 submitted 26 May 2026.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26916v1
   Used for the original polytope and support-polynomial definitions.

3. T. S. Motzkin and E. G. Straus, *Maxima for Graphs and a New Proof of a
   Theorem of Turan*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 17 (1965), 533-540.
   https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1965-053-6
   The classical weighted clique inequality; an elementary proof is supplied.

4. Robert Davis and Florian Kohl, *Perfectly Matchable Set Polynomials and
   h*-polynomials for Stable Set Polytopes of Complements of Graphs*.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.14759
   Terminology and prior perfectly matchable set theory.

5. Hidefumi Ohsugi and Akiyoshi Tsuchiya, *PQ-type adjacency polytopes of join
   graphs*.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2103.15045v1
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-022-00447-z
   Terminology and matching-versus-support distinction.

6. Thomas M. Liggett, *Ultra Logconcave Sequences and Negative Dependence*,
   Journal of Combinatorial Theory A 79 (1997), 315-325.
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0097316597927904
   Classical convolution theorem.

7. Leonid Gurvits, *A short, based on the mixed volume, proof of Liggett's
   theorem on the convolution of ultra-logconcave sequences*.
   https://arxiv.org/html/0804.1181v1
   Theorem 1.1 explicitly states the finite-order ULC(l)+ULC(d) -> ULC(l+d)
   normalization used in the disjoint-union corollary.

No third-party papers have been copied into this package.
