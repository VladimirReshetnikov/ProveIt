Consulted for this research on 28 September 2026. URLs identify primary papers
or the original posted mathematical argument. No primary-source PDFs are
redistributed in the package.

Repository: https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt

Report:
`SetTheory/Cardinals/docs/reports/enumerative-combinatorics/preorder-root-polytopes/article.tex`

Title: *A Reflexive Root-Polytope Model for Preorder h-Polynomials*, dated
20 September 2026. Consulted blob SHA:

Read the construction, weighted Hall and fiber argument, examples, limitations,
and hypertree appendix. The report leaves flag realizability, gamma-positivity,
and real-rootedness unproved. The new article gives an independent height-two
root identity and explicitly distinguishes a nonflag canonical triangulation
from failure of an existential flag realization.

## Numbered conjectures

C. A. Athanasiadis and F. Chapoton,
*Polytopes and posets associated to preorders*:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26916
https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26916v1

Conjecture 5.2 is general gamma-positivity; 5.3 is real-rootedness of the support
polynomial; 5.1(d) is existential flag simplicial-polytopal realization. The
support polynomial is not the Ehrhart numerator of the original preorder
polytope. Version-specific statements are used throughout.

## Classical graph and polytope inputs

T. Kálmán and A. Postnikov,
*Root polytopes, Tutte polynomials, and a duality theorem for bipartite graphs*:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.04449
DOI: 10.1112/plms.12015.

H. Ohsugi and A. Tsuchiya,
*Reflexive polytopes arising from bipartite graphs with gamma-positivity associated
to interior polynomials*:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12258
Example 3.7 supplies the classical nonreal-rooted degree-eight graph polynomial.
The paper's fully signed type-B polytope has a factor 4 in its gamma substitution;
it is not the one-sided root model used in this article.

R. Davis and F. Kohl,
*Perfectly Matchable Set Polynomials and h*-polynomials for Stable Set Polytopes
of Complements of Graphs*:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.14759
Theorem 3.10 identifies the augmented edge-polytope numerator. The cycle formulas
are also credited. Complement bars can be lost in parsed PDF text, so the
relevant displayed source formulas were inspected visually during research.

H. Ohsugi and A. Tsuchiya,
*Symmetric edge polytopes and matching generating polynomials*:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.08621
https://arxiv.org/html/2008.08621v2
Theorem 7.2 already gives the univariate cactus result. The article does not claim
that result anew; it develops a concrete complex-matrix proof and refinements.

J. R. Stembridge,
*Counterexamples to the poset conjectures of Neggers, Stanley, and Stembridge*:
DOI: 10.1090/S0002-9947-06-04271-1.
Author data: https://websites.umich.edu/~jrs/data/pocon/

The old width-two poset and the new height-two orientation are explicitly
distinguished in the article.

## Earlier eight-element posting discovered during the final audit

Shivam Patel, *An eight-element counterexample*, original posted solution:
https://mathdb.com/p/374962/real-rootedness-conjecture-for-preorder-polytope-h-polynomia

The mathematical posting specifies the height-two graph Theta_3 and the
polynomials reproduced and independently checked in the article. Its exact
calendar posting date was not established; the page displayed a relative date
of one month earlier and labeled it unverified. The site's broad historical
metadata about the age of the conjecture is not relied on.

This finding changes priority, not the correctness of the independently derived
17-element example. The delivered article expressly does NOT claim first
discovery of a counterexample to AC Conjecture 5.3. The all-m theta formula and
complete root counts are presented as structural extensions.

A search also surfaced a withdrawn SSRN listing for an eight-element
counterexample (abstract 7385158). Its full text was unavailable, and neither its
contents nor the reasons for withdrawal are used as evidence for any mathematical
or priority claim in the article.
