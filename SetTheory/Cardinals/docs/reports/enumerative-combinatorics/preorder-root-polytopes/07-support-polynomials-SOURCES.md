# Primary sources and inspected versions

Accessed for this manuscript on 29 September 2026 (America/Los_Angeles). References are also embedded in the LaTeX article.

1. **Christos A. Athanasiadis and Frédéric Chapoton.** *Polytopes and posets associated to preorders.* arXiv:2605.26916v1 (2026). Preorder context and conjectures. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26916

2. **Ziyi Dai, Qilin Hou, Zhiyuan Liu, Warut Thawinrak, Hongyu Wang.** *Counting Lattice Points in Minkowski Sums of Cross Polytopes.* arXiv:2608.16037v2, 27 August 2026. Definition 1.1 and Theorem 1.2 give the demand/root-polytope bridge; Section 4.3 discusses preorders; Problem 5.3 asks about unimodality, palindromicity, and real-rootedness. https://arxiv.org/html/2608.16037v2

3. **Robert Davis and Florian Kohl.** *Perfectly Matchable Set Polynomials and h*-polynomials for Stable Set Polytopes of Complements of Graphs.* arXiv:2207.14759v1 (2022). Theorem 3.10 states the Ohsugi–Tsuchiya augmented-root identity. The PDF and its opening page were inspected. https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.14759

4. **Felix Röhrle and Martin Ulirsch.** *Logarithmic Concavity of Bimatroids.* Annals of Combinatorics 30 (2026), 501–523; published online 14 August 2025. Theorem A proves regular-minor ULC; Theorem E gives the realizable bimatroid volume-polynomial construction. This is the crucial prior result, not a theorem first proved in the present manuscript. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00026-025-00780-z ; preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15317

5. **Petter Brändén and June Huh.** *Lorentzian polynomials.* arXiv:1902.03719v8, 2024 revision. Theorems 2.10, 2.30, and 3.10; Example 2.26. https://arxiv.org/html/1902.03719v8

6. **Nima Anari, Kuikui Liu, Shayan Oveis Gharan, Cynthia Vinzant.** *Log-Concave Polynomials II: High-Dimensional Walks and an FPRAS for Counting Bases of a Matroid.* arXiv:1811.01816v3 (2019). Theorem 1.1 and the counting discussion. https://arxiv.org/html/1811.01816v3

7. **Charles J. Colbourn, J. Scott Provan, Dirk Vertigan.** *The complexity of computing the Tutte polynomial on transversal matroids.* Combinatorica 15 (1995), 1–10. DOI: 10.1007/BF01294456. The author-institution publication record was inspected for the #P-completeness statement; the article's subsequent graph/preorder reductions are proved explicitly, rather than attributed to the abstract. https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/the-complexity-of-computing-the-tutte-polynomial-on-transversal-m/

8. **ProveIt repository, relevant four-part report.** Commit `de2f8fa8c6f0107b1a0f1f77e2e25c343e562180`; report path `SetTheory/Cardinals/docs/reports/enumerative-combinatorics/preorder-root-polytopes/`.
