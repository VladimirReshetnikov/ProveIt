# Sources and proof scope

Report 274, *A Finite Positive Amplitude for Stressed Kunz Words*, 6 October 2026.

The article proves the exact boundary renewal, its length refinement and activity transformation, the loop-aware container lemma, all standardization and weighted preimage bounds, the positive-activity convergence criterion, the rational certificate beyond the critical point, the early-one suppression estimate, and the analytic simple-pole conclusion. Its corollaries use the Fibonacci filter, which is also proved in the article. Earlier root or consecutive-ratio theorems are not proof dependencies. The computation is bounded diagnostic evidence, not the asymptotic proof.

## Primary literature

- Daniel G. Zhu, *Sub-Fibonacci behavior in numerical semigroup enumeration*, Combinatorial Theory 3(2), article 10 (2023), https://doi.org/10.5070/C63261988. Version 3, https://arxiv.org/html/2202.05755v3, checked 6 October 2026. Relevant parts are Sections 3, 4.1, 5.4–5.5, 7.3(c), and 7.4. The dense-window standardization framework is due to Zhu. The formal polynomial-prefactor statement allows exponent zero; the interval 1.6–1.7 is a numerical suggestion, not a proved theorem or a positivity requirement in the formal statement.

The lower family and stressed-composition perspective are antecedents; Section 15.4, https://arxiv.org/html/2105.04200v1#S15.SS4, discusses central and terminal transition regions and leaves their typical sizes unresolved. Checked 6 October 2026.
- Wojciech Samotij, *Counting independent sets in graphs*, European Journal of Combinatorics 48 (2015), 5–18, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2015.02.005 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.0940. Reference for the classical fingerprint/container framework; the precise loop-aware lemma needed here is proved in full.
