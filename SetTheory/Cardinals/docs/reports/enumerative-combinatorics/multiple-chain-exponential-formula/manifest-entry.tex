\entry{Multiple-chain preorder polytopes: two proofs of the exponential formula}
{enumerative-combinatorics/multiple-chain-exponential-formula}
{article.pdf}{multiple\_chain\_exponential\_formula\_research.zip}
{Proves the unnumbered conjectural identity in Section~9.5 of
Athanasiadis--Chapoton (arXiv:2605.26916v1): for a chain preorder with $n$
blocks of size $d$, its support polynomials satisfy
$\sum_{n\ge0}h(\tau_{n,d},t)z^n=
\exp(\sum_{m\ge1}B_{dm}(t)z^m/m)$, where
$B_N(t)=\sum_j\binom Nj^2t^j$ is the type-$\mathbb B$ Narayana polynomial.
Two structurally different formal walk proofs are given: a two-subset
encoding whose excursions have the bounded symbol $(1+u)^c(1+t/u)^d$ and a
constant-term logarithm, and the original-coordinate slack meanders whose
positive-part factorization is carried out in
$\mathbb{Q}[t]((u^{-1}))[[z]]$ and
evaluated by stars and bars.
A rectangular budget-$c$ extension replaces the square by
$\binom{dm}{j}\binom{cm}{j}$, and a commutative mixed-block-size formula
covers unequal blocks once one sums over orders, which an explicit
counterexample shows cannot be dropped.
Also: support duality, an endpoint refinement, Ehrhart evaluations, an
algebraic root-of-unity product with two proofs of the degree bound $2^d$,
a double-chain quartic with two elimination certificates, gamma integrality
and strict positivity, fixed-support polynomiality in $n$ of exact degree
$2j$ with leading coefficient $d^{2j}/(j!(j+1)!)$, Stieltjes moments,
explicit $n^{-3/2}$ asymptotics with two expressions for the boundary
constant, and a support central limit theorem with exact variance.
Gamma positivity for composition polytopes and the general walk factorization
are credited to prior work; priority is provisional. Two independent exact
verifier suites over disjoint parameter ranges accompany the proofs.
The target differs from the reciprocity and root-polytope conjectures of the
same paper already catalogued here.}
