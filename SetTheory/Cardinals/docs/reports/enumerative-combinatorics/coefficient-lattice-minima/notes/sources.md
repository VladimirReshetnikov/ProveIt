## Own derivations versus source claims

The explicit mixed-radix witness, cut-coordinate inequality, integral
filtration, rigidity, normal forms, all-index formulas, and complete proof
presented here are derived in this response. No claim is made that every
underlying idea (positional notation, carry relations, monic division) is new.

theorem are identified as source material. The endpoint obstruction h1>=2
is proved directly rather than attributed to the source.
