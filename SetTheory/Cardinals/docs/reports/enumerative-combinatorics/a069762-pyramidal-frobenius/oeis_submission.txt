FORMULA
For n >= 2, let h(n) = 3*[n is odd] + 2*[n == 2 (mod 3)]. Then

a(n) = (4*n^5 + 32*n^4 + (101-10*h(n))*n^3
        + (175-45*h(n))*n^2 + (102-65*h(n))*n
        - 36 - 30*h(n))/36 + e(n),

where e(2)=10, e(3)=40, e(5)=315, e(11)=1612,
e(17)=3135, e(23)=2400, and e(n)=0 otherwise.

In particular,
a(n) = n^5/9 + 8*n^4/9 + (101-10*h(n))*n^3/36 + O(n^2),
and the full expansion is the exact quasipolynomial displayed above.
The least eventual quasiperiod is 6, with sharp uniform cutoff n=24.

RECURRENCE

sum(d(j)*a(n-j), j=0..18)=0, where d(0)..d(18) are
1,2,-1,-8,-9,6,23,16,-14,-32,-14,16,23,6,-9,-8,-1,2,1.
This recurrence has minimal eventual order 18.
