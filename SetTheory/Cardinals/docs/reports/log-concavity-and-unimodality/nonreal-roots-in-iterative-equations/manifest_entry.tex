\entry{Nonreal Roots Survive in Polynomial Iterative Equations}
{functional-equations/nonreal-circular-spectrum}
{article.pdf}{nonreal\_roots\_research.zip}
{A negative answer to Problem~6.1 of Draga and Morawiec,
\emph{Reducing the polynomial-like iterative equations order and a generalized
Zolt\'an Boros' problem} (Aequationes Math.\ 90 (2016), 935--950;
arXiv:1503.00570v2, p.~13): an explicit odd increasing bi-Lipschitz
homeomorphism satisfies $F^{\circ3}(x)=8x$ but $F(1)=3$, with minimal
iterative polynomial $z^3-8$. A real-analytic counterexample has minimal
polynomial $(z-1)^2(z^2+1)$. Also classifies exactly the minimal polynomials
of increasing real-line homeomorphisms with all roots on one circle:
for radius $r\ne1$, the positive root has odd multiplicity at least as
large as every other; at radius~1, either the polynomial is $z-1$, or
$1$ has even multiplicity strictly exceeding every other. Includes
constructive realizations, rigidity and regularity criteria, and exact
rational certificates. The draft is unrefereed and makes no independently
verified historical-priority claim.}
