# Reproducing the degree four preorder results

This package proves rank-ultra-log-concavity for the gamma coefficients of every
finite preorder of actual gamma degree at most four, using the stated ordinary
proof dependencies and independently checked exact certificates. All 76
four-attachment templates are approved and every one of the 39,367 outstanding
face tasks is covered. The approved template and certificate lists are in
`sources/preorder-gamma-degree4-independent-audit/four-attachment/approved_replay_inputs.json`.

It contains support-count data, independently written checkers, certificate
identities, coverage generators, ordinary proof dependencies, and immutable
audit receipts. The accompanying article explains the structural reduction and
mathematical hypotheses.

The support convention is important: gamma_k counts ordered pairs of DISJOINT
k-element subsets of the same ground set admitting a directed perfect matching.
It counts each endpoint pair once, not once per matching and not in a two-copy
bipartite enlargement. Populations are nonnegative integers. Binomial-multiplier
certificates need not be nonnegative at fractional populations.

## Run the checks

Python 3.11 or later is sufficient for exact certificate replay. Full regeneration
also needs a C++17 compiler named `g++`. No numerical optimization package is
needed for either verification mode.

- `python verify.py --mode hashes` checks only file integrity and pinned inputs.
  It does not recheck algebra or enumeration.
- `python verify.py --mode fast` replays all stored population-certificate rational identities, gamma-to-gap
  reconstructions, population-face aliases and complete template coverage. It also
  checks the ordinary proof source hashes. It does not enumerate all bounded cores
  afresh. This may take several minutes or longer.
- `python verify.py --mode full` performs the same checks and additionally
  regenerates the a=0/1, a=2, a=3 and a=4 kernels with independent Hall-based
  support counting and exhaustive coverage. The ten-element enumeration is the
  expensive step and may take substantially longer.

The final package needs no `--allow-incomplete` option. Its final coverage assertion
requires all 76 templates and zero remaining tasks. The option is retained only
for explicitly labeled historical partial checkpoints, which otherwise fail.

Logs and a machine-readable result are written to `verification_results/`, or to
`--output PATH`. A successful process exit and the final coverage result are
required. Merely starting a command or seeing an intermediate PASS is insufficient.

## What is trusted and what is replayed

The mathematical dependencies are the ordinary Gallai-Edmonds reduction, the
exterior Lorentzian theorem, the balanced and outward-star proofs, the
actual-degree-at-most-three theorem, the bipartite rank-four theorem, and ULC
product closure. Their proofs, independent audits and cited primary literature
are supplied. Hash checking a proof is not a new proof of its mathematics.

The previous degree-three and bipartite rank-four theorem packages are included
under `dependencies/`. Their ZIP hashes are checked here; use their own documented
reproduction commands to replay those earlier computational components. They are
explicit dependencies, not silently treated as regenerated by this wrapper.

The independent checkers use exact arithmetic and do not import the producer's
optimization modules. Floating-point LPs, eigenvectors, warm starts and Gram
facial reductions were discovery tools only. Every final square identity is
expanded over every symmetry action and compared coefficient by coefficient.

To make the original checkers portable, the wrapper copies `sources/` into a
fresh temporary directory and replaces only the literal `/workspace/shared`
prefix in executable Python/C++ source. The original bytes are unchanged. The
old pendant auditor's obsolete flat-ZIP transport assertion is replaced by the
package-wide manifest check; its finite-difference quadratic algebra is unchanged.
Historical a4 positivity receipts are removed from this temporary execution tree
before replay. Thus an old receipt cannot substitute for an omitted current
certificate. Fresh receipts alone are used for the final coverage union, together
with the explicitly identified ordinary theorem approvals.

`examples/` contains useful failed sufficient bounds and a non-real-rooted
specialization. These examples are not used to certify positivity. No proof
assistant formalization or novelty priority is asserted by this package.
