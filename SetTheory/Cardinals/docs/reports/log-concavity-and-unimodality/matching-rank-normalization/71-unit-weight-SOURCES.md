# Sources and provenance

1. Robert Davis and Florian Kohl, *Perfectly Matchable Set Polynomials and
   h*-Polynomials for Stable Set Polytopes of Complements of Graphs* (2022),
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.14759
   Background for the standard perfectly matchable set polynomial.
   Theorem 3.10 and Lemma 3.11 identify it with the h*-polynomials of the
   augmented graph’s edge polytope and the complement’s stable set polytope,
   respectively. The exact augmentation is stated in the article.

2. Felix Röhrle and Martin Ulirsch, *Logarithmic Concavity of Bimatroids*,
   Annals of Combinatorics 30 (2026), 501–523, published online 14 August 2025,
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s00026-025-00780-z
   Theorem A supplies the known smaller-shore ULC normalization. It is used
   for context and the interpretation of the deficiency-one result, not to
   prove the negative integer certificate.

3. *Hall Bottlenecks and the Limits of Rank Normalization*, research
   manuscript prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov with ChatGPT, 30 September
   2026. Pinned incoming archive:
   https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/a866ff9a2/docs/incoming/ProveIt_Hall_Bottlenecks.zip
   Its “Unit weights” question explicitly asks whether rank normalization
   holds without external fields. The present note answers that version
   negatively. The source's weighted counterexamples alone do not imply
   the all-unit claim.

The pendant identity was derived and checked during the present research.
The first exact all-unit witness was then reduced by a bounded numerical
parameter search. The delivered examples use exact integer arithmetic only;
all printed claims have a written proof or an explicit finite certificate.
Global priority has not been established.
