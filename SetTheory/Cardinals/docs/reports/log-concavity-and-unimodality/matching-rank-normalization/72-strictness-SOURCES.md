# Source ledger and research boundary

Inspected 1 October 2026.

1. Weighted Rank Five and Covers with a Two Vertex Shore, AI-assisted ProveIt research report, 1 October 2026. The existing rank-(q+2) transversal lift supplies the exact weighted support-polynomial identity. This companion recalls its columns, activities, two full-color bases and six counting cases. It adds strictness and equality; it does not replace or silently edit that report.

2. Swee Hong Chan and Igor Pak, Equality cases of the Stanley-Yan log-concave matroid inequality, arXiv:2407.19608v2. Theorem1.3 characterizes unweighted equality by parallel classes in rank-two contractions. Theorem1.11 explicitly prevents treating single-index equality as total equality for arbitrary matroids. The primary HTML, theorem statements and equality proof were inspected. The weighted criterion used here is proved directly with Lorentzian Hessians.
https://arxiv.org/html/2407.19608v2

3. Petter Brändén and June Huh, Lorentzian polynomials, Annals of Mathematics192 (2020),821-891. Theorem3.10 gives Lorentzian matroid basis polynomials, Corollary2.11 gives closure under nonnegative directional derivatives, and Theorem2.10 gives nonnegative linear substitutions. These exact interfaces were checked in the primary text.
https://arxiv.org/html/1902.03719v3

4. T. S. Motzkin and E. G. Straus, Maxima for Graphs and a New Proof of a Theorem of Turán, Canadian Journal of Mathematics17 (1965),533-540, Theorem1. The first-gap argument is explicitly an application of this classical result, not a new Motzkin-Straus theorem. Publisher record and primary PDF inspected.
https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1965-053-6
https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/S0008414X00039493
