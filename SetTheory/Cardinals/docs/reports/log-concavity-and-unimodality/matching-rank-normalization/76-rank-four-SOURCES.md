# Sources and provenance

Inspected on 1 October 2026.

- Julius Borcea and Petter Brändén, The Lee–Yang and Pólya–Schur Programs II: https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.3087
  Published stability framework and polarization input. The package includes direct proofs of the differential operation and expected determinant lemma it uses.
- Petter Brändén and June Huh, Lorentzian polynomials: https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.03719
  Published matroid Lorentzian input for the smaller-shore inequality.
- Felix Röhrle and Martin Ulirsch, Logarithmic concavity of bimatroids, version 3: https://arxiv.org/html/2402.15317v3
  Theorem A and Example 2.4 provide the relation/bimatroid smaller-shore context.
- ProveIt Matching Rank incoming archive, pinned to commit 1b3960d8acdabd983147b7c609bd9dfb2d67a0a0: https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1b3960d8acdabd983147b7c609bd9dfb2d67a0a0/docs/incoming/ProveIt_Matching_Rank_Research.zip
  “Finish rank four” is explicitly open there. The prior leaf-compression note settled its weighted rank-three question.
- Leaf Compression and Weighted Rank Three for Matching Support Polynomials, separately delivered companion note, 1 October 2026, package ProveIt_Weighted_Rank_Three_Leaf_Compression.zip
- ProveIt root-polytope report: https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/tree/main/SetTheory/Cardinals/docs/reports/enumerative-combinatorics/preorder-root-polytopes
  Part VI already uses independent-column expectations and proves stable complete-bipartite blocks and block locality. Those methods are credited rather than claimed as new.
- Hall Bottlenecks archive: https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/a866ff9a2/docs/incoming/ProveIt_Hall_Bottlenecks.zip
  Exact two-shore weighted rank-six counterexamples.

A targeted primary-source literature check supports the established tools. It does not certify worldwide priority for the new structural applications, and the article makes no such claim.
