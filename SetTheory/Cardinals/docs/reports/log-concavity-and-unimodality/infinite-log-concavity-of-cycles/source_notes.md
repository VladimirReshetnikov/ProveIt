# Primary-source provenance and scope of the literature check

Access date for the source inspection: September 19, 2026.

## 1. The target conjecture

Tewodros Amdeberhan and Victor H. Moll, *Infinite log-convexity*.
Publisher PDF:
https://combinatorialpress.com/article/ojac/vol17/305.pdf

- Printed page 9, Conjecture 21: the absolute chromatic coefficients are
  proposed to be infinitely log-concave.
- Printed page 8 includes the standard chromatic polynomial for C_n.
- The page footer identifies Online Journal of Analytic Combinatorics,
  issue 17 (2022), article 6. The first page is dated December 30, 2023.
  Both facts are retained rather than silently choosing one year.
- The first page and the conjecture page were inspected as rendered PDF
  pages, as well as through the parsed text.

Only this conjecture is being refuted. No assertion is made here about
unrelated statements in the paper or about its discussion of log-convexity.

## 2. Definitions and the invariant cone

Peter R. W. McNamara and Bruce E. Sagan,
*Infinite log-concavity: developments and conjectures*,
arXiv:0808.1065v2 (2009).
https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1065
https://arxiv.org/html/0808.1065v2

Section 1 explicitly uses zero extension for finite sequences. Lemma 2.1
provides the invariant factor-log-concavity constant (3+sqrt(5))/2.
The report uses the convenient larger constant 3 and includes the proof.
The arXiv version metadata, rather than a dynamically displayed HTML
render date, is used for the bibliographic date.

## 3. Related sufficient condition

Petter Branden, *Iterated sequences and the geometry of zeros*,
arXiv:0909.1927; J. reine angew. Math. 658 (2011), 115-131.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.1927

The primary abstract states that the relevant nonlinear transformation
preserves real nonpositive zeros and hence gives infinite log-concavity
of the coefficient sequence in that setting. This is background only:
the cycle classification in the report is proved without that theorem.

## 4. Ordinary chromatic log-concavity

June Huh, *Milnor numbers of projective hypersurfaces and the chromatic
polynomial of graphs*, arXiv:1008.4749;
J. Amer. Math. Soc. 25 (2012), 907-927.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.4749

The abstract states the log-concavity theorem for absolute chromatic
coefficients. The counterexamples in this report concern higher
iterations and do not contradict ordinary log-concavity.

## What the search does and does not establish

Targeted searches combined the authors' names, the paper title,
"Conjecture 21", chromatic coefficients, cycles, infinite log-concavity,
and counterexamples. The author's sample-publications page was also
inspected. No relevant prior cycle disproof was located in those results.
Several searches returned largely irrelevant matches; therefore their
failure to find a result is not evidence of an exhaustive literature gap.

The reliable conclusion is that the primary source inspected contains the
conjecture, and the report gives an exact counterexample to its standard
zero-padded formulation. Priority and the absence of an earlier correction
have NOT been established. Independent bibliographic review is appropriate
before making a first-disproof or first-publication claim.
