Source title: Binary Carry Structure in Iterated Lacunary Series:
A complete modulo-four theorem for OEIS A168362, an exact exception
criterion, and a logarithmic counting law.

DIRECT TARGETS

Question 13.2 asks if binary digit weight greater than q forces every
iterate coefficient to vanish modulo 2^q. The article proves this for
all integer iterates, all q >= 1, and all positive degrees, as a special
case of a theorem for every prime.

Question 13.3 suggests an odd-prime modulo-p^2 cutoff at digit sum two.
This is a suggestion in a research question, not a theorem of the source.
The exact identity [x^(2p-1)]F_p(F_p(x)) = p disproves the suggestion for
odd primes. The correct necessary cutoff is digit sum at most p.

PRE-EXISTING RESULTS THAT ARE NOT CLAIMED AS NEW

The source proves the full binary modulo-four iteration array and a finer
diagonal exception classification and counting law. Those results are
credited, not re-proved or claimed as new here. The real asymptotic ratio
conjecture a(n+1)/a(n) ~ e*n is not resolved by the present work.

Legendre's factorial valuation, integral exponential-series composition,
Lagrange inversion, Fuss-Catalan coefficients, the finite-Cartier-kernel
criterion, and Frobenius lifting are classical tools. The article supplies
the proofs needed for its application and cites primary background.

The new contribution relative to the inspected report is the all-precision
digit-sum theorem and its composition/inverse extension, the explicit
normalized second-iterate residue and highest-digit stability, the three
sharp families for the full seed, quantitative support consequences,
the explicit fixed-forward-iterate Cartier construction, and the
digit-sensitive sufficient period of coefficientwise p-adic iteration.
Historical priority beyond the inspected sources is not certified.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS

Two independent mathematical reviews checked the main algebraic and
combinatorial arguments. Exact-arithmetic programs use independently
constructed ordinary, Bell, digit-polynomial, and Cartier computations;
their finite ranges and results are recorded in data/*.json.

The specific full second iterate is proved sharp in infinite families at
weights one, two, and three. Sharpness at every supported degree is proved
only for the broader composition-and-inversion class via the classical
inverse of x+x^p. Fixed-iterate automaticity covers nonnegative integer
iterates. No diagonal or inverse-iterate automaticity claim is made.
The p-adic period is sufficient, not asserted to be minimal.

No source repository, OEIS entry, or external publication was modified.
