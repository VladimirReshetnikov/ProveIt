# Source provenance

Consulted 19 September 2026. The article contains conventional numbered
references and self-contained proofs. This note records the role of each
source, not a claim that the literature search was exhaustive.

## OEIS A000364

https://oeis.org/A000364

The target is Peter Bala's comment dated 8 May 2023, asserting pure
periodicity from index one modulo every positive integer, with period
dividing the totient. The entry still displayed it as a conjecture on the
consultation date. The sequence is the POSITIVE SECANT sequence, with
`a(n) = (-1)^n E_(2n)` in the usual signed-Euler notation.

## Tauno Metsänkylä (2014)

“Note on Kummer's congruences for Euler numbers,” The Fibonacci Quarterly
52(2), 160–162.

https://www.fq.math.ca/Papers1/52-2/Metsankyla.pdf

The short congruence on page 161 contains the Euler factor responsible
for the initial transient. The counterexample can therefore also be
obtained directly from classical results. The report's finite-moment
proof makes the mechanism explicit rather than presenting the underlying
congruence as a new discovery.

## Zhi-Wei Sun (2005)

“On Euler numbers modulo powers of two,” Journal of Number Theory
115(2), 371–380. Preprint version: math/0410085v3, 7 December 2004.

https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0410085
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2005.01.001

Equation (1.1) records the odd-modulus finite-moment identity; Theorem 1.2
states the exact dyadic congruence for signed Euler numbers at even
indices. The report supplies a falling-factorial proof of the latter.

## Donald E. Knuth and Thomas J. Buckholtz (1967)

“Computation of tangent, Euler, and Bernoulli numbers,” Mathematics of
Computation 21(100), 663–688.

https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1967-0221735-9

Foundational reference. The bibliographic information and historical
period attribution were cross-checked in OEIS and the later papers.
The attempted direct AMS PDF fetch was unavailable in this session;
no part of our mathematical proof depends on an unseen passage in it.

## Sanjay Ramassamy (2017)

“Modular periodicity of the Euler numbers and a sequence by Arnold,”
Arnold Mathematical Journal 3(4), 519–524.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.08666

Studies the FULL up/down sequence, whose exponential generating function
is `sec(x)+tan(x)`, rather than just its even subsequence.

## Berke Güleç (2026)

“Modular periodicity of the Euler up/down numbers at odd prime powers,”
arXiv:2608.27058v2, 3 September 2026.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27058
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27058

Recent related work on the full up/down sequence: sharp odd-prime-power
periods, finite-frequency arguments, and a preperiod analysis. The report
acknowledges this overlap and writes out an argument specialized to
negative-square modes for the secant subsequence. No claim is made to
solve the remaining preperiod conjectures of this paper.

## Provenance of the new computations

All counterexample values, modular tables, witnesses, density cutoffs and
numerical enclosures in `data/` were generated by the Python programs in
this archive. No numerical data were copied from the cited papers.
The distribution formulas and mean estimates are deductions proved in
the report; global priority for those deductions has not been established.
