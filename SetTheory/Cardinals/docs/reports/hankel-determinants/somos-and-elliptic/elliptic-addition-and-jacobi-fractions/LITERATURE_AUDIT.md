# Literature audit and claim boundary

Research date: 19 September 2026. These are source links, not locally bundled
copies of external papers. Failure to locate an exact proof is not evidence
that none exists. Mathematical validity and bibliographic priority are separate.

## Published target

Paul Barry, *Integer sequences from elliptic curves*, arXiv:2306.05025v2,
3 November 2023 (first submitted June 2023).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05025v2
https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05025v2

Section 3, Conjecture 4 gives the conjectured elliptic Jacobi fraction.
Conjecture 3 gives both coordinates via ordinary and modified Hankel
determinants. Conjecture 2 gives the normalized Hankel/division sequence.
The coordinate initialization and the text before Conjecture 4 have an
indexing inconsistency: consistent use requires the original x_n,y_n to
represent (n+1)P, not nP. This report writes X_m,Y_m for mP throughout.
The ordinary regular fraction also requires avoiding torsion degenerations.
The report proves the substantive intended formula, not merely the existence
of a typographical inconsistency.

## A result already proved in 2026

Feihu Liu, Ying Wang, Zihao Zhang, *Hankel Transform and (alpha,beta) Somos-4
Sequences*, arXiv:2608.08703v1, submitted 9 August 2026.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08703v1
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.08703

The introductory table explicitly attributes the resolution of Barry 2023
Conjecture 2 to Theorems 5.4 and 5.5. Section 5.2 proves the normalized
Hankel/division-polynomial statement. The report credits this result and
does not claim the Somos-4 identity or division-polynomial identification
as a new solution. The present proof instead centers on an explicit closed
family of complete quotients yielding the consistently indexed Conjectures
3 and 4.

## Earlier and related general theory

Andrew N. W. Hone, *Continued fractions and Hankel determinants from
hyperelliptic curves*, Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics 74 (2021),
2310-2347; DOI 10.1002/cpa.21923; arXiv:1907.05204v3.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.05204v3

This establishes broad links between continued fractions, birational dynamics,
and Hankel determinants. The overall connection is not new here. The research
report supplies an explicit four-parameter algebraic calculation for Barry's
normalization rather than claiming a new general theory.

Paul Barry, *Elliptic Curves, Riordan arrays and Lattice Paths*,
arXiv:2507.16765v1, 22 July 2025.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16765v1
https://arxiv.org/html/2507.16765v1

This contains further elliptic/continued-fraction and lattice-path
correspondences. It is relevant context, not omitted because it is newer
than the target conjecture.

Xiang-Ke Chang, Jiyuan Liu, *Hankel determinants from quadratic orthogonal
pairs for hyperelliptic functions and their applications*,
arXiv:2603.11670v1, 2026.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11670v1

This is additional recent context on quadratic/hyperelliptic moment and
Somos constructions. The report does not assert that its own proof supersedes
or is independent of the full general frameworks in the literature.

## Standard theorem used for the non-torsion example

Andrew V. Sutherland, *18.782 Introduction to Arithmetic Geometry, Lecture 24*,
MIT, 3 December 2013.
https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.782/2013fa/LectureNotes24.pdf

Corollary 24.19 and Theorem 24.21 support the good-reduction and Nagell-Lutz
certificates used in the worked examples. The point arithmetic and finite-field
counts themselves are regenerated by the included standard-library script.

## Scope of the contribution

The provided argument proves a precise, corrected version of a published
conjectural statement, constructs every complete quotient explicitly, derives
both coordinate formulas, and analyzes torsion/nonrationality with exact
examples. Neither finite testing nor the label "conjecture" in a 2023 paper
certifies that this is the first resolution as of September 2026. Priority
would require further specialist assessment. The report makes no stronger
historical claim.
