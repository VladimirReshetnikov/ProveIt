Notation: A_k denotes OEIS A005259. D_n=det(A_{i+j})_{0<=i,j<=n}
denotes A228143, with D_{-1}=1. Put C=17+12*sqrt(2), Lambda=C/4.
Let phi be the positive density on [0,C] representing the A_k moments,
as established by G. A. Edgar (arXiv:2005.10733).

Proposed additional formula:

D_n/D_{n-1} ~ K*Lambda^(2*n), where
K=(Pi*C/2)*exp((1/Pi)*integral_0^C log(phi(x))/sqrt(x*(C-x)) dx).
Numerically K is approximately 0.633427435172.
Consequently
log(D_n)=n*(n+1)*log(Lambda)+(n+1)*log(K)+o(n).

In the normalization of the ratio plot linked from this entry:
D_{n+1}/((1+1/sqrt(2))^(8*n+4)*D_n) tends to Lambda*K,
approximately 5.379471608281.
