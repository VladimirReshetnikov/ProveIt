## Limits of the literature check

The original paper retains the conjecture labels, and focused searches
for the exact paper, its Hankel statements, and the associated sequences
did not identify a later resolution of the selected assertions. This does
not exclude equivalent formulations, unindexed literature, private proofs,
or unpublished communications. No exhaustive bibliographic search or
publication-priority certification is claimed.

The present manuscript is an independently developed proof attempt with
complete arguments and reproducible audits. It is unrefereed and has not
been checked in a proof assistant. Its proofs should be evaluated on their
mathematical content. Finite computation is not a substitute for them.
