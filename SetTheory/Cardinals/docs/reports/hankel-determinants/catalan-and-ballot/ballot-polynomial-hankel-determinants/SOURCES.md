# Sources and research scope

Checked during preparation, 19 September 2026.

## Primary problem source

Johann Cigler, *Hankel determinants of middle binomial coefficients and
conjectures for some polynomial extensions and modifications*.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.14492v3
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.14492
- Version 3 revised 30 December 2021.
- Section 5 begins on printed page 19. Conjectures 13 and 14 are on
  printed page 20; the rest of 14 and Conjecture 15 are on page 21.
- The page images, not only extracted PDF text, were inspected to verify
  all signs, floors, denominator powers, and reciprocal exponents.

The mathematical target consists exactly of the three conjectures in
that section. The strict log-concavity, explicit product evaluation, and
asymptotic profile are additional results derived in the report.

## Background on the reduction method

Johann Cigler and Christian Krattenthaler, *Hankel determinants of linear
combinations of moments of orthogonal polynomials*, arXiv:2003.01676.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.01676

Christian Krattenthaler, *Hankel determinants of linear combinations of
moments of orthogonal polynomials, II*, arXiv:2101.04225.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.04225

These are context for the general determinant/orthogonal-polynomial
method. The particular reduction needed here is proved directly in the
article by a block determinant; no unquoted theorem from these papers is
needed to fill a proof gap.

## Integer-sequence connection

OEIS A001405: `a(n) = binomial(n, floor(n/2))`.

https://oeis.org/A001405

Specializing the ballot-polynomial moments at t=1 yields this sequence
by a telescoping sum. The report does not claim that the newly tabulated
coefficient arrays are absent from OEIS or assign them unverified IDs.

## Status-search limits

Targeted searches used the arXiv identifier, the exact paper title,
Cigler's name, and descriptions of the Section 5 conjectures. No later
resolution of these exact statements was located. Some searches containing
conjecture numbers returned irrelevant results, which were not treated as
evidence. Other papers proving differently formulated conjectures of
Cigler were not substituted for this target.

This records what was established, not a proof that the problem remained
unresolved everywhere on the search date. The article distinguishes its
mathematical proof from an unverified historical priority claim.

No third-party paper, page screenshot, or font file is redistributed in
the archive.
