## OEIS context

The OEIS Foundation Inc., *The On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences*, A398540, inspected October 3, 2026.

- https://oeis.org/A398540

A398540 records decimal digits of a conjectural consecutive-ratio constant, rather than integer counts or the exact rational probabilities. Its formula explicitly labels the limit conjectural. The report first defines r_* by the proved nth-root limit and then proves that the consecutive ratios converge to it. Its numerical certificate is the rational bracket stated in the article; proving existence of the limit does not verify the posted digits.

## Classical analytic input

Herbert Robbins, “A remark on Stirling's formula,” *The American Mathematical Monthly* 62 (1955), no. 1, 26–29.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.2307/2308012
- Publisher/JSTOR record: https://www.jstor.org/stable/2308012

The article uses the explicit factorial remainder bounds 1/(12n+1) < r_n < 1/(12n). It proves the needed positive-coefficient Tauberian step directly using moments and polynomial approximation. The article proves its own complex modulus bound and Cauchy coefficient argument to obtain the leading equivalent; no external coefficient transfer theorem or prior coefficient equivalent is assumed.

The mathematical results are presented relative to the inspected model and sources, without a claim of exhaustive literature coverage or universal priority.
