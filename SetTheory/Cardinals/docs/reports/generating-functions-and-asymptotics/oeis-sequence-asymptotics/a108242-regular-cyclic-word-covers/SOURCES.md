The model and indexing corrections are explained in article Section 4. Third-party papers are linked in the article bibliography and are not included in this archive.

Generated exact counts, rational coefficients and symbolic outputs are separate research outputs. Floating-point diagnostics use 80 decimal digits of working precision and are not interval-certified bounds.
