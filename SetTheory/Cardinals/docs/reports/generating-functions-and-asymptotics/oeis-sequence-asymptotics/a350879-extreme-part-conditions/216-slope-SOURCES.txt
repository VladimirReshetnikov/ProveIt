OEIS A350879: https://oeis.org/A350879
Conjugate definition kM=L, exact equality generating function, and the
17 October 2024 leading equivalent attributed to Václav Kotěšovec. The
p(n)-normalized law k! (pi/sqrt(6n))^k p(n) is already posted. Its alternative
exponential formula has a literal pi discrepancy, described precisely in
Section 1 of the report; this does not make the whole leading law new.

OEIS A237753: https://oeis.org/A237753
Equality specialization E(2,0). Source-listed definition, generating function,
terms, and prior leading equivalent were read in indexed OEIS content; direct
access was intermittent. Failed fetches were not treated as full latest-page
reads.

OEIS A237751: https://oeis.org/A237751
The strict condition 2M<L conjugates to M>2L, hence T(2,1), not T(2,0).
The source-listed prefix includes 4760 at n=39. No asymptotic was displayed in
the inspected record; absence from a page is not proof of historical novelty.

G. Szekeres, Asymptotic distribution of partitions by number and size of parts,
Number Theory, Colloquia Mathematica Societatis Janos Bolyai 51, 1990, 527-538.
https://www.mek.oszk.hu/24100/24107/pdf/24107_1.pdf
Original paper fully read, including visually checked printed pp.529-530.
p(n;r,k) is an EXACT joint count. Theorem1 permits lambda,mu between
(log n)/4 and n^(1/4)/log n and retains correlation terms in its leading
exponent. A narrower corollary gives an independent-extreme equivalent.
Its proof retains o(1) exponential accuracy, not the present arbitrary-order
summed diagonal calculus. The original exact-count definition controls.

T. Jiang and K. Wang, A generalized Hardy-Ramanujan formula for the number
of restricted integer partitions, J. Number Theory 201 (2019), 322-353.
https://arxiv.org/html/1805.06108
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2019.02.006
Theorem1/Remarks1.2-1.3 concern cumulative rectangles with both cutoffs at
least 4sqrt(n), including unbounded normalized cutoffs, and relative error
O(n^(-1/4+epsilon)). This range overlaps relevant sqrt(n)log(n) cutoffs.
That error does not supply arbitrary-order rare diagonal differences.

S. Melczer, G. Panova, R. Pemantle, Counting partitions inside a rectangle,
SIAM J. Discrete Math. 34 (2020), 2388-2410.
https://arxiv.org/html/1805.08375
https://doi.org/10.1137/20M1315828
Theorems1-2 keep (ell/m,n/m^2) in a compact subset of x>=2y>0.
At m~sqrt(n)log(n), the second coordinate tends to zero. Consecutive-n
refinements hold the rectangle fixed. This particular compact regime does
not include the whole dominant fixed-slope extreme sum.

L. B. Richmond, A George Szekeres Formula for Restricted Partitions (2018).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.08548
Theorems1-2 give central and extended cumulative ranges; equation(10) also
treats exact joint counts. The central displayed error is O(n^(-1/10)log n).
The paper is not cumulative-only, but does not state the all-fixed-order
slope-coupled expansion/inverse proved here.

H.-K. Hwang, Distribution of integer partitions with large number of summands,
Acta Arith. 78 (1997), 351-365.
https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa78/aa7844.pdf
The analytic sieve and controlled-remainder Proposition1 are methodological
antecedents. Theorem1 compares p(n,m) to p(n-m) in a large-length range;
later results treat restricted part sets and length laws. The particular
coupled M=kL+b / M>=kL+b sums and inverses are not stated there.

Hieu T. Ngo and Robert C. Rhoades, Integer partitions, probabilities and
quantum modular forms, Research in the Mathematical Sciences 4, article17,
2017. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40687-017-0102-4
Arbitrary-order partition-statistic expansions and general amplitude transfer
are prior methodology. The report does not claim generic transfer novelty.

F. Johansson, Efficient implementation of the Hardy-Ramanujan-Rademacher
formula, LMS J. Comput. Math. 15 (2012), 341-359.
https://arxiv.org/html/1205.5991
https://doi.org/10.1112/S1461157012001088
Equations(1.4)-(1.8) supply the directly used exact U-series, exponential
sum bound by unit-modulus terms, and convergent truncation statement.
The report derives its own needed one-term exponential gap from that formula.

H. Rademacher, A convergent series for the partition function p(n), PNAS23
(1937), 78-84. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.23.2.78
Original bibliographic credit. Direct scan retrieval was blocked; the exact
normalization used in the proof was read in Johansson, not inferred from an
unread original scan.

NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, 23.18.5-23.18.7.
https://dlmf.nist.gov/23.18#E5
Eta inversion in the exact normalization used for the Cauchy t^(-3/2) cost.
The exponentially small remaining partition product is bounded directly.

F. J. Dyson, A new symmetry of partitions, J. Combin. Theory7 (1969), 56-61.
https://www.mi.uni-koeln.de/NumberTheory/teaching/Seminare/Partitionen/pages/vortragS12.pdf
Ordinary rank M-L and alternating identities. k=1 is excluded from the
present finite-depth hierarchy, whose valuation gain is k-1.

A. Berkovich and F. G. Garvan, Some observations on Dyson's new symmetries
of partitions, author's version. https://qseries.org/fgarvan/papers/dyson.pdf
Equations (6.1) and (6.2) define the named k-rank using successive
Durfee squares. This differs from M-kL. A bounded terminology search is not
proof that no unindexed treatment of fixed-slope diagonal extremes exists.
