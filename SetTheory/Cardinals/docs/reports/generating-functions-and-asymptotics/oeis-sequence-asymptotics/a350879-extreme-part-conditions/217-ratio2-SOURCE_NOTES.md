# Source notes and limits of comparison

Comparison date: 4 October 2026. This note records source scope, not priority
or novelty clearance. No absence claim follows from a failed full-text read.
No third-party paper or downloaded OEIS data file is redistributed here.

## Direct sequence and leading equivalent

OEIS A118096: https://oeis.org/A118096

The record defines partitions with largest part exactly twice smallest, gives
the finite-interval generating function, and lists the leading equivalent
attributed to V. Kotěšovec, 13 June 2025. The leading equivalent is already
known in the sense of being publicly recorded. Absence of an adjacent proof
in an OEIS entry is not an open-problem declaration.

## Prior radial all-order framework

R. J. McIntosh, Asymptotic transformations of q-series, Canadian Journal of
Mathematics 50 (1998), 412–425, Theorem 2 and general-product discussion:
https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1998-022-x

N. H. Zhou, Asymptotic formulae for Eulerian series, arXiv:1709.08550v1 (2017),
Theorems 1–2 and surrounding definitions:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.08550

Full primary texts were available for the comparison. Their real-axis
all-order machinery includes this type of Eulerian series. The radial
expansion and its saddle are not claimed as new. The self-contained complete
circle and discrete saddle proof in this report establishes the needed
coefficient conclusion directly, without asserting that such a conclusion
is absent from other literature.

## Closest unresolved historical comparison

B. Pittel, Limit shape of a random integer partition with a bounded max-to-min
ratio of parts sizes, Journal of Combinatorial Theory A 114 (2007), 1238–1253:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2007.01.006
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0097316507000179

The primary abstract concerns max/min ≤ k, k>1, and a parameter w satisfying
w^k+w=1. Full theorem text was not accessible in this comparison. In
particular, the abstract does not settle the precision of its enumeration
results or their implications for this exact-boundary model. Historical
comparison remains incomplete. No originality or solved-open-problem claim
is made, and this paper must be read in full before any such assessment.

## Distinct neighboring mock-theta models

G. E. Andrews, A. Dixit, A. J. Yee, Partitions associated with the
Ramanujan/Watson mock theta functions ω(q), ν(q) and φ(q), Research in Number
Theory 1 (2015), article 19:
https://doi.org/10.1007/s40993-015-0020-8
https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.06860

The introduction in the available primary text identifies q chi_1(q) with
max<2min, and chi_0(q) with max≤2min plus a unique minimum. The exact equality
boundary is a different class. The elementary difference with the unrestricted
weak-ratio class is proved in the report; it does not supply a product or
mock-theta identity for that unrestricted weak-ratio class.

product used to encode the already justified finite Gaussian calculations:

## Claim boundary

The report is an independently derived, explicit fixed-order refinement with
proof and exact computation. Its mathematical proof does not rely on an
unread theorem from Pittel. Its historical comparison nevertheless remains
incomplete. Those two statements are compatible and must be kept separate.
