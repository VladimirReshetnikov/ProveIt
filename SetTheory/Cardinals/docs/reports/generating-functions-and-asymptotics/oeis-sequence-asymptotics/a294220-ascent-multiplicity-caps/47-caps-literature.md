# Primary sources and scope

Checked 1 October 2026. This is a scoped source check, not an exhaustive priority certification.

## Published paper

A. R. Conway, M. Conway, A. Elvey Price and A. J. Guttmann, Pattern-Avoiding Ascent Sequences of Length 3, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 29(4) (2022), P4.25, DOI 10.37236/11266.

Published PDF: https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v29i4p25/pdf/
Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.01279

The published PDF was retrieved and its definitions and cap-two discussion checked. The cap-two compacted recurrence and the numerical conjecture 8/(3*pi^2) are prior work. This family report does not claim them as new. The companion cap-two deliverable has its own fuller scoped literature check; its identity is recorded in provenance.json.

## Pattern-avoidance background

Paul Duncan and Einar Steingrimsson, Pattern avoidance in ascent sequences, arXiv:1109.3641 (2011), https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.3641 . The primary preprint page was retrieved and confirms that it concerns ascent-sequence pattern avoidance and growth rates. No general bounded-cap constant theorem is attributed to it here.

Exact-ID and multiplicity/growth searches did not locate a directly matching later family theorem in the retrieved results. Search coverage was limited, so this is not proof of novelty. External results for modified ascent sequences, inversion sequences, or bounded matrix statistics cannot be substituted without a statistic-preserving proof.

## Contribution and limitations

The report provides a self-contained general-cap proof, a uniform-in-state barrier, a full normalized-root limit, a coarse fixed-cap logarithmic error, and separately a controlled large-cap expansion. It does not claim a full coefficient equivalent, a ratio limit, an all-orders expansion in length, or a uniform joint limit. Mathematical audits in this archive are independent checks of the presented reasoning, not conventional journal peer review.
