# Source audit — October 1, 2026

## OEIS and literature status

The definition is OEIS A033552, partitions into Catalan numbers, with allowed
parts C_k for k >= 1. The duplicate value C_0 = C_1 = 1 is included only once.
The inspected entry provides the product generating function and a recursive
computation; no asymptotic formula was present.

https://oeis.org/A033552

The entry links Igor Pak, *Complexity problems in enumerative combinatorics*,
arXiv:1803.06636v2 (2018), section 2.5. That discussion is about exact counting
complexity. The present asymptotic result does not resolve that question.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.06636

Targeted searches included `A033552 asymptotic`, `partitions into Catalan
numbers asymptotic`, and `Catalan partitions asymptotics`. The last phrase
is ambiguous and was not treated as identifying A033552 without checking the
definition. No claim of an exhaustive publication search is made.

## Classical analytic context

of partition functions,” J. Austral. Math. Soc. Series A 21 (1976), 447–456.
This establishes the classical context of periodicity in restricted partitions;
it is not represented as a source for the specific Catalan constants derived
in this article.

https://specfun.inria.fr/dumas/Publications/FlGoDu95.pdf

https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.17

classical nonvanishing of zeta on Re(s)=1 and the absence of gamma zeros.
