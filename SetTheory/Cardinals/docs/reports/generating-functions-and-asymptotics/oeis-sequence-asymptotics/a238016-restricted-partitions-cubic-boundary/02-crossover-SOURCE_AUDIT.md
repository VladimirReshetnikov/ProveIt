## Status of the contributions

Proved in the accompanying manuscript:

- An all-fixed-orders logarithmic expansion uniform for N >= a*m^2,
  including arbitrarily small saddles as N grows without upper bound.
- The centered m^(5/2) threshold and the transition law associated with each
  nonzero entropy coefficient.
- An exact rational coefficient extraction formula, the first six coefficients,
  and infinitely many nonzero coefficients (not nonvanishing at every order).
- A uniform analytic inverse chart and a separate integer-threshold error bound.

Classical or already in the inspected repository:

- Leading Szekeres formulas, the uncentered cubic law, the leading A238608 law,
  its -61/288 correction, and the general methods of Gaussian saddle analysis,
  Euler–Maclaurin summation and series inversion.

Not established:

- Exhaustive novelty, independent peer review or machine-checked proofs.
- All-coefficient negativity, optimal truncation, individual exponential waves,
  Borel summation or a complete resurgent transseries.
- Uniformity as N/m^2 -> 0, certified numerical remainder constants, or an
  exact rounding rule for arbitrary thresholds.
