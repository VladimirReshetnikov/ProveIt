2. OEIS A109253. Type B full-support counts, generating function, and previously posted factorial asymptotics: https://oeis.org/A109253
3. OEIS A112225. Type D full-support counts with offset 2: https://oeis.org/A112225
4. OEIS A003319. Indecomposable permutations: https://oeis.org/A003319

6. Richard J. Martin and Michael J. Kearney. Integral representation of certain combinatorial recurrences. Combinatorica 35 (2015), 309-315. Supplies the general shifted-Stirling/compound-Poisson construction, connected-permutation moment representation, and B/D integrals: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-014-3183-3
7. Louis Comtet. Sur les coefficients de l'inverse de la serie formelle sum n!t^n. C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris A 275 (1972), 569-572. Classical connected-permutation asymptotic background; the required lemma is also proved directly in the report.

The first source states the conjecture resolved here. The underlying models, generating functions, leading factorial counts, and general transform machinery are credited prior results. The specialized late corrections and uniform sparse-sign crossover are proved in the report. The source audit was bounded and does not claim exhaustive worldwide priority.
