# Independent audit of the fixed-alphabet balanced-excursion theorem

Reviewed1October2026: `leading_comparison.md` and `all_orders_and_inverse.md`. The leading theorem, all-orders extension and inverse pass mathematically.

1. With the two extreme step types unmarked, length rn and the r−2 extracted contents n determine both extreme counts uniquely as n. The marked bridge coefficient is exactly the multinomial coefficient; no zero-step insertion factor is missing when the zero step is retained.
2. The conditional mark covariance is positive definite by the two omitted extremes. Its determinant is4h²/(r^r sigma²), including the zero-dimensional r=2 case. The mark-height vector is orthogonal to the all-ones vector, so the determinant lemma gives the stated factor without a hidden rank-one term.
3. The equality phases on the height circle give omega=pi k/h and alpha=pi k, with k modulo2h. The interior mark map has kernel of size g, the gcd of h+s_i: g=1 for odd r and2 for even r. Thus there are exactly r−1 mark points and g time copies at each. For balanced extraction the residual phase is exp(−2in alpha)=1. Every local correction, not only the leading amplitude, is transported by the same gauge identity. Total multiplicity is2h.
4. The bridge saddle consequently reproduces the exact multinomial leading constant sqrt(r)(2pi n)^(-(r−1)/2)r^(rn). Dividing the corresponding excursion saddle gives e_r/(rn), with the stated K_r. The direct all-orders excursion recipe therefore has only the rescaling r^(−j), not an additional Stirling or zero-insertion correction.
5. Away from those finitely many marked phases, compactness supplies a larger t disk with no kernel root on the height circle. The bridge contour and inside-root product are analytic there. Inside-root collisions do not invalidate the product argument. Near the principal point only the positive critical pair crosses the contour; its critical point is nondegenerate. Periodic copies are handled explicitly.
6. At positive critical time, the only unit-circle root is1, of multiplicity2. After removing it, the stable inside and outside groups are separated. Their factors are coprime to one another and to the critical quadratic even if a stable factor has repeated roots internally. Finite Hensel lifting of the factors is therefore valid; individual repeated roots are never differentiated. The chosen critical square-root branch and the stable product give the finite algebraic Puiseux jets. Normalization cancels the common Gaussian constants, giving real algebraic corrections at every order.
7. Uniform marked transfer, positive covariance and the outer-torus estimate give the claimed fixed-order polynomial-Gaussian remainder. Odd half-orders vanish. The Gaussian differential-operator recipe and the inverse expansion have the correct normalization and error scope.
8. The algebraic constant e_r is selected by the inside roots of Q_r. I also derived the r=4 relation p+p^(-1)=1+sqrt(5), hence p=phi−sqrt(phi), and verified the r=2,3,5 constants. The identity B=1+tE'/E follows directly by differentiating the root product and summing bridge residues; its convergent logarithmic series is supplementary to the algebraic specification.
9. Algebraicity followed by diagonal closure proves D-finiteness. To make the generalized diagonal explicit, take the r-section in the length variable first and replace t^r by a new variable, then take the ordinary diagonal with the r−2 content marks. A finite multisection of an algebraic series is algebraic. This does not identify a guessed or minimal recurrence.
