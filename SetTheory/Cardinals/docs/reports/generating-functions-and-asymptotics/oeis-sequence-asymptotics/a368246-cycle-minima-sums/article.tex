{\large Complete cancellation of the nonoscillatory correction sector}\par\vspace{7pt}
5 October 2026

\begin{abstract}
Let $a_n$ count permutations of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ whose cycle minima sum to $n$. The same numbers count permutations whose record positions sum to $n$ and form OEIS A368246. For $b_n=a_n/(n-1)!$, we prove a finite expansion to every fixed inverse-power order. The constant is $\ee^{-\gamma}$, the term of order $n^{-1}$ is absent, and every subsequent coefficient is a polynomial in $\log n$ with periodic coefficients of mean zero. The reason is the exact rooted-tree identity $T(s\ee^{-s})=s$, which cancels all higher logarithms at the positive singularity of a shifted infinite product. We give the second- and third-order terms, Gamma-product root amplitudes, the exceptional delay caused by zeros at $\pm\ii$, a direct global boundary remainder, and a real finite Newton construction with a rigorous two-ceiling inverse envelope. The method and the leading constant are classical; the focus is the sequence-specific cancellation and its refinements. Numerical checks are separated from the analytic proof, and no effective remainder constant, convergence of an infinite transseries, or publication-priority claim is made.
\end{abstract}
