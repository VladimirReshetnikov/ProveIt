## Mathematical inputs

The companion core-report.pdf and editable source establish the positive Bessel spectrum, large-node and weight estimates, and all-orders real-saddle coefficient algorithm. Its mathematical-verification.md counterpart is included as core-mathematical-verification.md.

The new addendum supplies the complex zero-free wedge, global exponential spectral replacement, exact finite-window Poisson decomposition, infinite omitted-mode bound, and fixed-complex-sector expansion.

## Standard formulas and methods

- NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, 10.9.7: Schlaefli's exact Y-Bessel integral, https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.9.E7
- NIST DLMF, 5.11: sectorial Stirling expansion and remainder estimates, https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11
- NIST DLMF, 4.13: Lambert-W branches, https://dlmf.nist.gov/4.13
- R. B. Paris, The asymptotics of the Touchard polynomials, 2016, https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.07883; primary full text at https://arxiv.org/html/1606.07883

Paris treats complex Touchard saddle expansions and explicitly describes the infinite Lambert-W saddle family. The addendum credits this established saddle framework. Its Poisson and Gaussian techniques are also standard; their applicability to the exact powered-Catalan Bessel weight is proved directly rather than inferred from the Bell analogy.

This is a scoped attribution check, not a comprehensive literature search establishing worldwide novelty. No external submission or upload has been performed.
