Primary exact-target predecessor
--------------------------------
P. Erdos and J. H. Loxton, Some problems in partitio numerorum,
J. Austral. Math. Soc. Series A 27 (1979), 319-331.
https://doi.org/10.1017/S144678870001243X
https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/0EF53ED5F6F9CCA0FD425E8D82B89C35/S144678870001243Xa.pdf/some-problems-in-partitio-numerorum.pdf
Section 3 was read. Their q(n)=a_(n+1), Q(x)=S_(floor(x)+1).
Lemma 1 gives positive binary comparison. Lemma 2 gives a uniform
multiplicative approximation by positive binary-recurrence sequences.
Theorem 3, printed page 324, concerns log Q(x), not log q(x), and already
establishes the periodic summatory leading law. Lemma 4 and (14) supply
sectorial periodic equivalents for fixed approximants. Printed page 328
transfers to sums and explicitly leaves convergence of the cumulative
binary ratio unresolved. These are direct result and method predecessors.

Tree interpretation and earlier growth
--------------------------------------
G. Gati, F. Harary, R. W. Robinson, Line colored trees with extendable
automorphisms, Acta Math. Sci. 2 (1982), 105-110.
https://oeis.org/A003238/a003238.pdf
Section 3, pp.109-110, gives the chain-partition citation bridge for the
one-color tree case and repeats the 1979 periodic summatory formula. The
other colored-tree square-root asymptotics concern different sequences.

M. K. Gol'dberg, E. M. Livshits, On minimal universal trees, Math. Notes 4
(1968), 713-717; Russian original Mat. Zametki 4.
https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/mzm9458
https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Emmanuel-Livshits-2/publication/226029337_On_minimal_universal_trees/links/54fe22500cf2672e223e9fe3/On-minimal-universal-trees.pdf
The full Russian text and appendix were read. Theorem 2 identifies the
uniform-tree partial sum and floor recurrence. The appendix establishes
logarithmic leading growth; it does not state a periodic-amplitude,
individual-coefficient, or all-fixed-order theorem.

F. Harary, R. W. Robinson, The number of achiral trees, J. Reine Angew.
Math. 278/279 (1975), 322-335.
https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1975.278-279.322
https://oeis.org/A002995/a002995_1.pdf
Full scan read with OCR and relevant pages visually inspected. Section 6,
equation (36), is the exact planted generating equation with P(z)=z F(z).
Other achiral-tree classes should not be identified with A003238. Page 334
poses an asymptotic question; no present all-order theorem is supplied.

Other inspected exact/nearby sources
-----------------------------------
F. Chung, D. Coppersmith, R. Graham, On trees containing all small trees
(1981), pp.265-272.
https://fanchung.ucsd.edu/ron/papers/81_04_small_trees.pdf
All eight pages read by OCR. Gives logarithmic size through a log n log log n
term with O(log n) error; uniform binary trees and binary-partition estimates
are important neighbors, not the present full-order exact-target theorem.

Kochkarev (2012), arXiv:1205.0344.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.0344
Full eight pages read: recurrence, tree interpretation, prime-increment
characterization, and crude bounds; no inspected periodic all-order result.

R. C. Read, 1974 letter and notes.
https://oeis.org/A003318/a003318.pdf
Four pages inspected: earlier reference, first terms, and calculation notes.

Lebowitz-Lockard, arXiv:2504.03446v1 (2025).
https://arxiv.org/html/2504.03446v1
Full six-page preprint read. Principally concerns distinct chain partitions.
Its background Theorem 5 writes the 1979 summatory formula for an individual
count. The primary 1979 p.324 says log Q(x); the report follows the primary
source. A final 2026 journal version was not inspected.

Classical and general methods
-----------------------------
K. Mahler, On a special functional equation, J. London Math. Soc. 15
(1940), 115-123.
https://carmamaths.org/resources/mahler/docs/068.pdf
General one-scale functional equation on pp.121-122 and partition
specialization in Section 5 inspected. Important method prior; its exact
hypotheses do not give the infinite all-integer dilation equation here.

N. G. de Bruijn, On Mahler's partition problem, Proceedings of the Section
of Sciences of the Koninklijke Nederlandse Akademie van Wetenschappen
te Amsterdam 51(6) (1948), 659-669 (publisher metadata on the linked scan).
https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/4342300/597482.pdf
Introductions, formulas, hypotheses, and periodic correction/whole-circle
issue on pp.659-660 inspected. Essential power-product prior. This ledger
does not claim that every technical lemma was independently audited.

P. Dumas, P. Flajolet, Asymptotique des recurrences mahleriennes: le cas
cyclotomique, J. Theor. Nombres Bordeaux 8 (1996), 1-30.
https://doi.org/10.5802/jtnb.154
https://jtnb.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/jtnb.154.pdf
The theorem, synopsis, Sections 3-4, and conclusion were read; theorem
visually checked. Complete analytic-periodic coefficient expansions are
already given for its cyclotomic product family. Multiple saddles and
negligibility are prior. Its finite Mahler/product hypotheses do not match
the present all-integer dilation forcing.

C. Banderier, H.-K. Hwang, V. Ravelomanana, V. Zacharovas, Analysis of an
exhaustive search algorithm in random graphs and the n^(c log n)-asymptotics,
SIAM J. Discrete Math. 28(1) (2014), 342-371.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.6549
https://doi.org/10.1137/130916357
Theorem 2.1, Proposition 2.4, (2.18), Theorem 3.1, and Section 6 were checked.
Their binomially weighted recurrence and inhomogeneous pantograph problem
supply substantial inverse-log and periodic saddle method prior. A survey
mention of universal trees is not an application of their theorem to A003238.
Journal metadata independently checked at the arXiv record on 4 October 2026.
Full original Pennington and Kato-McLeod scopes were not independently read.

Bounded conclusion
------------------
No inspected source supplied the exact two ratio nonconvergence statements,
or the complete fixed-order individual and summatory expansion with inverse
uncertainty for this recurrence. This absence finding is bounded by the
listed reading. The periodic leading shape, binary-prefix idea, generic
Mahler/saddle methods, and generic inversion method are not claimed as new.
