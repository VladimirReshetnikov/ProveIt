A293239 — correction and proved reductions
Prepared 2026-09-20. This file is not an OEIS submission.

1. The recurrence as printed for n>6 is false:
   at n=8 its right side is 2*28-21+11-2*7+4=36, but a(8)=35.

2. The proposed rational generating function has denominator
   1-2z+z^2-z^4+2z^5-z^6 and numerator of degree 13.
   Its coefficient sequence obeys the homogeneous recurrence for n>=14.
   It fails at n=13. Do not remove the conjecture label for the actual
   derivative count merely by correcting the recurrence's range.

3. Proved exact reduction:
   b(m,r)=[u^r] product_{h=0}^{m-1}(u+r-h).
   Z(n)=#{(m,r):1<=r<=m<=n and b(m,r)=0}.
   a(n)=1+n(n+1)/2-Z(n).

4. Proved zeros:
   (m,r)=(4h+1,2h), h>=1; and (m,r)=(8,5).
   The first family is known in the Lehmer--Comtet literature.
   Completeness of this zero list is not proved in this report.

5. Unconditional growth bounds:
   n+1+floor(n^2/4)<=a(n)<=q(n),
   where q(0)=1 and
   q(n)=1+n(n+1)/2-floor((n-1)/4)-[n>=8] for n>=1.
   Hence a(n)=Theta(n^2), but the equivalent n^2/2 is not proved here.

6. Exact computations included with the report establish a(n)=q(n)
   for every 0<=n<=3000. They do not prove the formula for all n.
