# Primary attribution check

4 October 2026 UTC. The public pages below were read through the web tool as
inert text. No code listed by any source was run or imported. This is a bounded
attribution check, not an exhaustive literature or priority search, a raw-byte
archive of the remote pages, or authentication of their revision histories.

- https://oeis.org/A196460 states the exact single-sum formula used in the
  manuscript, attributes the entry to Paul D. Hanna (2 October 2011), and
  credits the leading asymptotic a_n ~ 2^(n^2) to Vaclav Kotesovec (25 June 2013).
- https://oeis.org/A047863 gives sum binom(k,r) 2^(r(k-r)), hence the already
  known sequence k! alpha_k. It describes graphs with two named proper colors
  and explicitly records the parity-dependent discrete-Gaussian/theta leading
  asymptotic, credited to Kotesovec (24 June 2013). It links Read's 1960 paper
  and Finch's exposition. The manuscript does not portray these constants or
  the colored-graph interpretation as new.
- https://oeis.org/A002027 describes the connected version of that graph
  count and gives its exponential generating function log(A(x))+1. The
  manuscript restricts the connected interpretation to k>=1 and leaves the
  entry's a(0)=1 as a separate convention. The entry links Read and Wright's
  1970 correction/extension.

The relational-model attribution to Svatos, Jung, Toth, Wang and Kuzelka,
arXiv:2302.04606v1, Appendix A.1 Table 3, printed page 32, is inherited from the
unchanged predecessor article and its retained qa/PRIMARY_SOURCE_CHECK.md.
