## Definitions and conjecture

- OEIS A290354: https://oeis.org/A290354. The entry defines the diagonal of the iterated Euler transform, supplies the rooted-tree interpretation, and attributes the leading equivalent with c=4.4923... to Vaclav Kotesovec, 14 August 2017. A290354_first22.json contains only the 22 displayed numerical values, n=0..21. The full linked b-file could not be retrieved in the initial review (HTTP 403). No claim of a full external comparison is made.
- OEIS A290353: https://oeis.org/A290353. The square array has degree n and height m, with A(0,m)=1 and column zero generating function 1+x. The full ordinary generating function of column m is 1+F_m. The asymptotic theorems only use n,m positive.

## Mathematical antecedents

- Ryuji Kaneiwa, On r-fold Partitions and a Certain Form of Infinite Products, Humanities Research 61 (1980), 11-26. Authoritative repository: https://barrel.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/2331. Persistent identifier: https://hdl.handle.net/10252/2056. PDF: https://barrel.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2331/files/RLA_61_11-26.pdf. Printed pages 12, 18, and 19 were visually inspected, not solely read by OCR. The distinction between p and the tilde-marked labelled function matters: Kaneiwa's Propositions 1 and 2 prove the unlabelled polynomiality and zigzag leading coefficient; his Bell attribution on page 18 concerns the labelled function. Here p(r;n)=[z^n]F_(r+1).
- Milo Bechtloff Weising, Higher Order Bell Symmetric Functions, arXiv:2505.18504v2: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18504v2 and https://arxiv.org/html/2505.18504v2. Definition 1.6, Definition 2.1, and Proposition 2.12 give the plethystic construction and coefficient recurrence. Our direct one-variable specialization identifies [m_(n)]B_n^(m)=[z^n]F_(m+1); this diagonal asymptotic is not a theorem attributed to that paper. Version metadata says 21 September 2025 while the HTML body bears 24 August 2026. We cite the version identifier without silently resolving those dates.
- Related ProveIt report: https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/main/SetTheory/Cardinals/docs/reports/generating-functions-and-asymptotics/oeis-sequence-asymptotics/a139383-iterated-bell-diagonals/README.md. It studies the labelled iterates n![z^n](exp(z)-1) composed m times, OEIS A139383/A261280, and credits Prellberg's parabolic-coordinate method. Indexed source commit checked: 65969409cfc007047182e9303e1d2d0106324dea; article.tex blob ab5014ecd6366ef621d4d45393609bf177c88339. The present unlabelled Euler recurrence has essential subsidiary forcing, so the labelled theorem cannot simply be transferred.
