## Classical parabolic and unforced antecedents

A. Dudko and D. Sauzin, *The resurgent character of the Fatou coordinates of
a simple parabolic germ*, C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris I 352 (2014), 255–261.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crma.2013.12.005
Primary PDF:
https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/mathematique/item/10.1016/j.crma.2013.12.005.pdf

Theorems 1 and 2 give general formal/sectorial Fatou structure and Gevrey
asymptotics. This supports the classical context, not a theorem about the
forced Euler array. The direct finite proof in Report 228 uses the exact
normalization of Report 225. The nonzero resiter is 1/3; results restricted
to vanishing resiter cannot be cited as an unchanged direct application.

T. Prellberg, *On the Asymptotic Analysis of a Class of Linear Recurrences*,
September 2002 seminar, summary by M. Mishna, Algorithms Seminar 2002–2004
(2005), 47–50:
https://algo.inria.fr/seminars/summary/Prellberg2002a.pdf

Sections 2.2 (p. 49) and 3.1 (p. 50) give inverse-coordinate expansions, the
parabolic logarithmic factor, and the specialization 1/3 for exp(z)-1.
They do not state the exact endpoint density coefficients or a beta-to-zero
uniform transfer theorem. A fresh retrieval of the related FPSAC slides timed
out; no new full-slide inspection is claimed.

S. V. Nagaev and V. I. Vakhtel, *Limit theorems for probabilities of large
deviations of a Galton-Watson process*, Discrete Math. Appl. 13(1) (2003), 1–26.
https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/dm183
https://doi.org/10.1515/156939203321669537
Primary full-text repository:
https://opus.bibliothek.uni-augsburg.de/opus4/files/55382/Limit%20theorems%20for%20probabilities%20of%20large%20deviations%20of%20a%20GaltonWatson%20process.pdf

Search-indexed primary text was inspected; direct PDF retrieval timed out,
so numerical typography was not newly verified from screenshots. Theorems
1–2, printed p. 3, equations (10)–(12), treat k/m tending to infinity with
k=o(m^2). The Cramer correction has gamma=1-2C/(3B^2), hence 1/3 for Poisson.
Equation (12) and the discussion on p. 4 give a correction of the size
exp[-gamma*u*log(u)/m] in their stated range u=o(m).

For an unforced high-moment identity, the heuristic saddle k approximately
nm/2 lies in the stated range when m/n tends to infinity, including the
logarithmic window. This is an important older antecedent for the mechanism
and exponent. A complete moment-transfer and its tail domination have not
been established in this comparison. The paper is not claimed to state the
Bell moment ratio verbatim, and it does not supply the forced Euler-array
shrinking-frequency proof of Report 228.

## Scope of the contribution and finite evidence

The universal endpoint proof is supporting parabolic calculus. The Euler-
specific extension is the quantitative coefficient transfer for
(log m)^(-D)<=n/m<=B0, every fixed D>0, followed by the logarithmic-height
crossover, exact Kaneiwa normalization, and smooth ratio inverses.
The bounded source review is not a worldwide priority proof.

The public code is a fresh finite adaptation of the exact Euler recurrence
used in Report 226 and the displayed rational coefficient identities. The
nine table counts are generated integers, with an independent finite-product
check of the three degree-20 rows and smaller tables. Floating constants,
ratios, floors, and asymptotic approximations are unvalidated diagnostics.
Neither a decimal certificate nor an effective finite-n onset is claimed.
