\begin{abstract}
Restricted two-covers admit a positive Poisson sum whose unique real saddle gives an expansion to every fixed order. We prove a relative $O_K(n^{-K-1})$ remainder at the exact saddle, then transfer the result through arbitrary fixed polynomial exponential multipliers, including the signed correction for labelled line graphs. A separate finite rational construction gives explicit Lambert-$W$ coefficients with the logarithmic remainder that their growth requires. We compute the first corrections, explain the distinction between the corrected line-graph sequence and the legacy formula recorded as A132219, and derive inverse expansions with a precise separation between model roots and certified integer thresholds. Exact arithmetic, independent endpoint enumeration, and reproducible TeX and code accompany the report. The previously known leading equivalents and exact identities are credited to Cameron, Prellberg and Stark; no claim of worldwide priority is made for the higher-order refinements.
\end{abstract}
