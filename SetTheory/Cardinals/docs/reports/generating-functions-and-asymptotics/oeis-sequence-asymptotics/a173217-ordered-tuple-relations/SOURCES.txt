The first three supply the d=2,3,4 geometric-binomial family, signed-Stirling
code and known leading equivalents. A301468 includes the general d>2 leading
equivalent. A000670 records the positive-degree Fubini pole identity, credited
to Dean Hickerson. Some cubic/quartic source checks used indexed primary-site
text where direct export was unavailable. This is not a certified live export.

SCOPE
Known foundations include enumeration, leading equivalents, signed-Stirling
and Fubini identities, raw pole bounds, and general collision/kernel methods.
The article develops the H_d-specific post-transform remainder, specific
kernel, and absolute recovery estimates with proofs. A bounded source
comparison is not an exhaustive novelty or priority certification. No claim
is made to solve the neighboring double-coset or matrix-composition questions.
No third-party full texts or source-paper PDFs are included in this package.
