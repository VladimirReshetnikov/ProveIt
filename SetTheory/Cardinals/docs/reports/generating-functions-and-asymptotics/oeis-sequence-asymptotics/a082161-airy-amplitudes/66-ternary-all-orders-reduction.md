# Constructive all-orders extension: formal recursion and transfer reduction

Status: independently approved relaxed expansion to every fixed finite order; see all-orders-audit/audit.md. This includes formal recursion, high-order remainder bounds, common scalar normalization, and error transfer. No all-orders DFA theorem is implied.

## Exact formal recurrence

Let ε=i^−1/3, x=ε(j+1), τ=(1−ε³)^−1/3, and

 U(ε,x)=2−6(xε²−3ε³)/(2+xε²−ε³).

For Φ(ε,x)=Σ_(m≥0)ε^m φ_m(x) and s(ε)=3+Σ_(m≥2)s_mε^m, the exact formal evolving equation is

 U(ε,x)Φ(ετ,(x−ε)τ)+Φ(ετ,(x+2ε)τ)=s(ε)Φ(ε,x).           (A)

Set φ_0=f=Ai(a+x), a=a_1, and s_2=3a. Require φ_m(0)=0 for every m. This is the exact discrete bottom boundary, not a continuum substitute.

At order ε^(m+2), m≥1, the only new unknown profile is φ_m and the only new scalar is s_(m+2). The equation is

 3(∂²−x−a)φ_m = s_(m+2) f + P_m f + Q_m f′,             (B)

where P_m,Q_m are known polynomials from earlier stages, after reducing every derivative with f″=(x+a)f. The cancellation of order ε^(m+1) is the critical zero drift; profiles φ_(m+1),φ_(m+2) do not appear in (B).

## Polynomial solvability, including the boundary

Write φ_m=A_m f+B_m f′. For polynomial A,B,

 (∂²−x−a)(Af+Bf′)
 =[A″+2(x+a)B′+B]f+[2A′+B″]f′.

For a prescribed polynomial forcing Pf+Qf′, eliminate A to obtain

 K B := −B‴/2+2(x+a)B′+B = P−Q′/2.                      (C)

K is invertible on polynomials: in degree d its diagonal entry is 2d+1; the remaining terms decrease degree. A constant added to the right side changes only B's constant term, since K(1)=1. Therefore the scalar s_(m+2) in (B) is uniquely chosen to make B_m(0)=0. Then φ_m(0)=0, since f(0)=0 and f′(0)≠0. Recover A_m by integrating A_m′=(Q−B_m″)/2. Its integration constant fixes a scalar-amplitude gauge; for example A_m(0)=0 works consistently.

For m=1 this gives s_3=15/2, B_1=0 and A_1=−5x²/12−ax/6, exactly the leading proof's first correction.

Thus no Fredholm obstruction or logarithm appears in this polynomial recursion. All coefficients are polynomials over the field generated by a and rational numbers. This is a formal statement, not an error estimate.

## High-order remainder and common normalization

Fix a sufficiently large starting time L, the leading factors t_i=3[1+a i^−2/3+(5/2)i^−1], and

 G_i=3^i exp(3a i^(1/3))i^(5/2),
 Π_(m=L)^i t_m ∼ κ G_i,    κ>0.

The leading normalized transfer factors are s_i=t_i N_i/N_(i−1), hence

 S_i:=Π_(m=L)^i s_m = [Π_(m=L)^i t_m]N_i/N_(L−1).          (D)

For each finite formal truncation choose a scalar

 H_i=G_i exp(Σ_(r=1)^J h_r i^(-r/3))

with leading constant exactly one. Its logarithmic step ratio can be matched to the formal s(ε) from (A) to any fixed order. The leading ε² and ε³ coefficients already agree. The new h_r first enters at order ε^(r+3), with coefficient −r/3, so every later equation is triangular and solvable. This requires no extra logarithm.

Cut off Φ_i=Σ_(m=0)^M i^(-m/3)φ_m(i^(-1/3)(j+1)) as in the leading proof. For any prescribed p>1, take K≥max(4,ceil(3p)), solve profiles through K−2 and scalars through K, and match the logarithmic H_i ratio through ε^K. Taking one further full stage is harmless and supplies order slack. Then

 ||T_i[H_(i−1)Φ_(i−1)]−H_iΦ_i||_ω ≤ C_p H_i N_i i^−p.    (E)

Here are the analytic estimates behind (E), rather than a bare formal matching assertion. Every fixed derivative of every polynomial–Airy φ_m is bounded by a polynomial in x times exp(−c x^(3/2)) for x≥1 and is bounded near zero. On x≤2log i, both τ=(1−ε³)^−1/3 and U(ε,x) admit Taylor expansions with denominators uniformly bounded away from zero; the remainders are powers of ε times a polynomial in x. The arguments differ from x by O(ε+ε³x), so Taylor remainders of φ_m have the same uniform polynomial Airy envelope (a fixed slight reduction of c absorbs the shifts). Squaring and summing on a mesh of width 3ε contributes a factor O(ε^−1), exactly canceled by N_i²≍ε^−1. At the bottom, Φ_(i−1)(−1)=0 identically because every φ_m(0)=0. The moving upper boundary is far outside the cutoff. Differences of the cutoff are supported where x≥log i−O(ε), where every polynomial factor is dominated by exp(−c(log i)^(3/2)); these errors are smaller than any fixed power of i. Increasing M and J therefore proves (E) for every prescribed p.

Define the normalized approximate solution

 Z_i=(κ/N_(L−1)) H_i Φ_i/S_i.                            (F)

Since H_i/G_i→1, (D) gives H_i/S_i∼N_(L−1)/(κN_i). Also Φ_i−F_i=o(N_i) in norm, where F_i is the leading proof's first corrected profile. Thus Z_i−ψ_i→0 and its projection onto ψ_i tends to one, independently of the truncation order. Dividing (E) by S_i and using H_iN_i/S_i=O(1) proves

 ||Z_i−(T_i/s_i)Z_(i−1)||_ω=O(i^−p).                     (G)

This fixes the common scalar normalization needed below. In particular, a truncation-dependent multiplicative constant has not been silently absorbed into the final amplitude.

## Abstract high-order transfer, once that remainder is proved

Let A_i=T_i/s_i be the leading normalized evolution in the leading proof, and let u_i be its exact solution with limiting projection C. Suppose a high-order approximate solution Z_i has projection tending to 1 and defect

 Z_i−A_i Z_(i−1)=O(i^−p)

in the fixed weighted norm. Set E_i=u_i−C Z_i and decompose E_i=α_iψ_i+w_i, with α_i→0. The already proved leading-profile block estimates give

 |α_i−α_(i−1)|≤C i^−4/3|α_(i−1)|+C i^−1||w_(i−1)||+C i^−p,
 ||w_i||≤(1−c i^−2/3)||w_(i−1)||+C i^−4/3|α_(i−1)|+C i^−p.

If α_i=O(i^−q), the contraction convolution gives

 ||w_i||=O(i^(−q−2/3)+i^(2/3−p)).

Backward summation of the first inequality, using α_∞=0, then gives

 α_i=O(i^(−q−1/3)+i^(1−p)).

Starting with bounded α, iterate this improvement finitely many times to obtain

 ||E_i||=O(i^(1−p)).

Because ψ_i(0)≍i^−1/2, pointwise endpoint extraction loses at most i^1/2, leaving a relative error O(i^(3/2−p)). Arbitrary p would transfer every finite asymptotic order with the SAME positive amplitude C. No higher-order singular-vector expansion is needed; only the original compression gap and two-sided leading-profile estimates are used.

## Endpoint expansion and scope

Since every φ_m(0)=0, Taylor expansion at x=ε gives Φ_i(0)=ε[Ai′(a)+Σ_(r≥1)c_r ε^r] to any requested finite order. Combining this with H_i, the transfer estimate, and Stirling yields a full expansion for relaxed ternary trees in powers of n^−1/3, with the same positive leading amplitude at every order. For any desired final remainder, choose p large enough that i^(3/2−p) is smaller than that remainder and retain sufficiently many scalar/profile coefficients.

The independent all-orders audit approves the formal recursion, analytic remainder, scalar conventions, and bootstrap. The leading amplitude theorem was independently reviewed separately. The DFA amplitude reduction in dfa-ratio-transfer.md is qualitative and does not transfer all these orders: growing-level defect asymptotics need separate control.
