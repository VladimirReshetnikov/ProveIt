GENERAL FIXED-MULTIPLICITY RESULT
Let B(n,k) be the number of permutations of k*n labeled cards, with k cards
in each of n ranks, such that adjacent cards have different ranks.  For every
fixed integer k >= 2,

  B(n,k)/(k*n)! = exp(-(k-1)) * (
      1 - (k-1)^2/(2*k*n)
      + (k-1)^2*(3*k^2-14*k+7)/(24*k^2*n^2)
      - (k-1)^4*(k^2-10*k+17)/(48*k^3*n^3)
      + O_k(n^-4)).

More generally, the number X(n,k) of equal-rank adjacent pairs in a uniformly
random shuffle converges in distribution to Poisson(k-1).  Its exact mean and
variance are

  E[X(n,k)] = k-1,
  Var[X(n,k)] = (k-1) - (k-1)^2/(k*n-1).

A330266
The displayed integral is the k=4 case of

  B(n,k) = Integral_0^infinity ((-1)^k*k!*LaguerreL[k,-1,t])^n*exp(-t) dt.

In particular,

  A330266(n)/(4*n)! = exp(-3) *
    (1 - 9/(8*n) - 3/(128*n^2) + 189/(1024*n^3) + O(n^-4)).
