# Sources

Checked 2 October 2026. Exact definitions, bibliographic details and the stated prior-art boundary are documented here. This file is a reader-facing source guide, not a claim that every related publication has been exhausted.

1. Ken Kamano, Lonesum decomposable matrices, Discrete Mathematics 341 (2018), 341–349. DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2017.09.002 ; preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.07157 ; full text https://arxiv.org/pdf/1701.07157 . Proposition 1.2 proves canonical nonzero block decomposition. Theorem 2.1 gives the forbidden 2-by-3 pattern and its transpose. Theorem 3.1, equation (9), printed page 7, supplies the EGF. Proposition 4.1 supplies an exact Stirling formula. The identification with an induced P5 follows directly by reading the four nonzero entries of the forbidden pattern as edges.

2. OEIS A299907 https://oeis.org/A299907 and A299906 https://oeis.org/A299906 . The supplied fixtures are the initial n=0..15 values of A299907, independently reproduced by the finite exact sum. The current direct OEIS requests made during final preparation failed; identification and the fixture transcription were inherited from the preceding successful source review, then verified by independent arithmetic. A000262 https://oeis.org/A000262 counts sets of nonempty lists; its EGF and recurrence are derived in the article, rather than assumed from the database.

3. Jessica Khera, Erik Lundberg and Stephen Melczer, Asymptotic enumeration of lonesum matrices, Advances in Applied Mathematics 123 (2021), 102118. DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2020.102118 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.08850 ; full text https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.08850 . This concerns ordinary lonesum matrices and variants. Remark 2 explains extension to higher orders. Its rational EGF differs from the exponential-of-a-pole EGF used here.

4. Beáta Bényi and José L. Ramírez, Some Applications of S-restricted Set Partitions, https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03949 ; full text https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.03949 . Section 4.3, Theorem 11, treats restricted decomposable matrices and their exact bivariate generating functions. The arXiv source has these two authors.

5. Jessica Khera, Lonesum Matrices and Acyclic Orientations: Enumeration and Asymptotics, Ph.D. dissertation, Florida Atlantic University, 2021. Official listing https://www.math.fau.edu/mathdepartment/dissertations/dissertations.php and defense abstract https://www.math.fau.edu/mathdepartment/dissertaion-defense-anouncement-jessica-khera031121.pdf . The full dissertation was not available to the scoped search. No assertion of worldwide priority is made.
