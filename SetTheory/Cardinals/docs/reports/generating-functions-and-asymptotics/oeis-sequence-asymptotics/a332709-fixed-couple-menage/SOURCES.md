## Primary literature

1. Vladimir Shevelev and Peter J. C. Moses, *Alice and Bob Go to Dinner: A Variation on Ménage*, Integers 16 (2016), A72. [Published full text](https://emis.de/ft/19616). Full 12-page paper read; Lemma 4 and equations (30)-(31) supply the staircase rook polynomials and fixed-couple factorization. The relevant column listings and page-10 unimodality observation were checked. The 2015 arXiv revision is earlier than this published paper. These exact counting methods are credited as prior work.
2. M. Wyman and L. Moser, *On the problème des ménages*, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 10 (1958), 468-480. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1958-045-6). Original 13-page paper downloaded; Section 3, especially printed pages 473-475, inspected. Equation (3.1) attributes the asymptotic expansion to Kaplansky-Riordan; equation (3.23) is the nearest-integer formula used for m >= 2. Signed U(1)=-1 is separate. Equation (5.7) also contains an exact finite identity. The expansion and refinement are classical, not new results here.
3. Irving Kaplansky and John Riordan, *The problème des ménages*, Scripta Mathematica 12 (1946), 113-124. Credited through Wyman-Moser and the OEIS references; the full 1946 paper was not independently audited in this project.
4. Peter Kagey, *Ranking and Unranking Restricted Permutations*, [arXiv:2210.17021v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17021v2), 9 November 2023. Full 26-page preprint read, especially Section 5, Lemma 30, Corollary 31 and Theorem 35. Its general prefix factorization and counting framework subsume the basic computational tools here. Text searches and reading found no theorem of the present row-difference/concavity form in that preprint. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2024.05.010): Discrete Applied Mathematics 355 (2024), 247-261. The final publisher full text returned 403 and was not independently inspected; no full-version non-overlap claim is made.
5. Yu-Xin Gu and Feng-Zhen Zhao, *The Log-Behavior of Ménage Numbers*, Results in Mathematics 76, article 160 (2021). [Publisher page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00025-021-01468-5). Abstract, reference list and publisher first-page preview available; the full paper was paywalled and not inspected. The abstract addresses log-balancedness in the size variable for ordinary and straight ménage numbers. This appears distinct from fixed-size position-row concavity, but complete non-overlap remains unverified.
6. [Donald Knuth's ménage note](https://oeis.org/A000179/a000179.txt) was read as a historical caution: the note recognizes that a rediscovered finite formula already occurs in Wyman-Moser equation (5.7). No argument in this report depends on novelty of that formula.
