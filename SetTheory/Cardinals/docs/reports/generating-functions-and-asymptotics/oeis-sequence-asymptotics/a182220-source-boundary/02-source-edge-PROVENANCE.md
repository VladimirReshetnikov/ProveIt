## Scope of the research claim

The present article supplies proofs answering the specific repository
natural-boundary question and develops the radial, arc-integral, and
subsequence results stated in its theorems. The limited source review does
not certify historical priority or rule out overlapping results elsewhere.
The report is research prepared with ChatGPT assistance, with no claim of
peer review or proof-assistant certification. It does not designate the
repository owner as the author of the new article.

The computational distinction is explicit: Python-integer assertions are
exact finite checks; mpmath tables and Matplotlib figures are numerical
diagnostics. Analytic estimates control truncation in the radial sums, but
floating-point roundoff is not enclosed by interval arithmetic. The
complete run specification and recorded results are in `README.md` and
`data/validation.json`.
