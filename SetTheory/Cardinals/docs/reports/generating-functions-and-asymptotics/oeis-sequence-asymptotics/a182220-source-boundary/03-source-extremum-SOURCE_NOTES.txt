Title: The source boundary of extensional acyclic digraphs:
An OEIS conjecture, finite-defect enumeration, and dyadic non-holonomicity.
Date on the starting manuscript: 3 October 2026.

The precise target is Section 12.2, Question 1: natural boundaries of the
fixed-deficit generating functions B_d. The starting report proves their
finite radius and non-D-finiteness, and explicitly leaves natural
boundaries open. It also already contains the source maximum,
Boolean-core classification, fixed-deficit formula, and covering estimate.

The present manuscript re-proves every counting and analytic estimate it
needs, so none of the new conclusions depends on treating an unrefereed
repository assertion as an axiom. The new results are the natural boundary,
quantitative dyadic radial phase, radial growth, local L^p comparison,
the no-bidisk obstruction, and explicit first block/amplitude corrections.

OEIS PRIMARY ENTRIES

https://oeis.org/A182220
Largest possible number of sources, n-ceil(log_2 n).
The retrieved entry still marks displayed logarithmic formulas conjectural.
This formula is NOT claimed as new in the present manuscript.

https://oeis.org/A182162
Labeled extensional acyclic digraphs by number of sources.
The n! conversion is justified by rigidity, not assumed.
Johnston's marked-source program is dated 18 April 2012.

https://oeis.org/A001192
Full/transitive finite sets by cardinality; equivalently unlabeled
extensional acyclic digraphs. U_0=1 is the empty graph/set.

All three entries were opened on 5 October 2026 (UTC). The exact
sequences and code formulas in our numerical files are computed by
the supplied script rather than copied wholesale from OEIS tables.
