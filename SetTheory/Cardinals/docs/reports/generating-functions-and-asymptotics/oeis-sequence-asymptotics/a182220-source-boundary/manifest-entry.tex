\paragraph{The source boundary of extensional acyclic digraphs.}
A self-contained proof of the formula in OEIS A182220, with explicit credit
for Tomescu's 2011 upper bound. The main development gives exact extremizer
counts in terms of A001192, a finite-defect specialization of the known
A182162 source sieve, uniform Boolean-core covering estimates, dyadic entropy
profiles, and a proof that every fixed-defect boundary sequence is
non-P-recursive. Includes an all-orders Boolean-factor expansion and a
binomial arc-deficit law near complete layers. Conventional proofs and
independent finite checks; no Lean verification or priority claim.
