# Sources and attribution

Checked 5 October 2026. The article is an explicit specialization of established assembly and saddle methods and makes no exhaustive priority claim.

- OEIS A323297, https://oeis.org/A323297, exact EGF credited to Andrew Howroyd, 18 August 2019. Defines the labelled 3-uniform count allowing isolates
- OEIS A323296, https://oeis.org/A323296, Howroyd's same-date component interpretation and EGF. Defines the no-isolate count, including the empty object at zero

- R. Arratia and S. Tavaré, Independent process approximations for random combinatorial structures, Advances in Mathematics 104 (1994), 90–154, https://doi.org/10.1006/aima.1994.1022. Full text https://arxiv.org/html/1308.3279, §§2–3, §4.2 and §6, provides the refined Poisson conditioning and exact TV identities used here. The HTML metadata line has a misleading placeholder volume/page entry. Correct journal data also appear in the author's journal copy https://www.columbia.edu/cu/simontavare/STpapers-pdf/AT94.pdf
- P. Keevash, D. Mubayi, R. M. Wilson, Set systems with no singleton intersection, SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics 20 (2006), 1031–1041, https://doi.org/10.1137/050647372. Inspected full text https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/keevash/papers/no-singleton-journal.pdf concerns extremal size and stability, not total labelled enumeration

The report's substantive claims are the explicit coefficient rule and scale of its remainder, the model-specific reversed logarithmic coefficients with fine exact-saddle inverse interpretation, the globally summable conditional likelihood and sharp TV constant, saddle-shift deletion corrections, and the projected/hybrid marked laws. The article proves these statements directly. Source review was bounded and is not an exhaustive literature certificate.

Only original report source, original check programs, their receipts, and the report PDF are included in the archive. No third-party paper PDFs or private research/review notes are distributed.
