# Source status and bounded search

Checked 1 October 2026.

- [OEIS A113226](https://oeis.org/A113226/internal), entry revision #49 dated 5 November 2025, defines avoidance of the vincular pattern 12-34, records equivalence with {3,2+2}-free naturally labelled posets, and displays 21 terms. Its formula section gives a recursive enumeration, without the closed EGF derived here.
- David Bevan, Gi-Sang Cheon, Sergey Kitaev, *On naturally labelled posets and permutations avoiding 12–34*, European Journal of Combinatorics 126 (2025),104117, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2024.104117), [primary accepted PDF](https://pure.strath.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/256336418/Bevan-etal-EJC-2025-naturally-labelled-posets-and-permutations-avoiding-12-34.pdf). Proposition11 gives the lowest-ascent-top/last-entry insertion tree; Conjectures13–14 give the growth constant 1/log4 and the stretched-exponential form. Those are explicitly conjectural in the inspected 2025 publication. The present continuation equations reformulate the same state in continuous ranks and retain its diagonal separately.
- Sergi Elizalde, *Asymptotic enumeration of permutations avoiding generalized patterns*, Advances in Applied Mathematics36 (2006),138–155, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2005.05.006), [author PDF](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~sergi/papers/pp05_aam_elizalde.pdf). Proposition5.1 gives the b_k,c_k block recurrences and the coefficientwise envelopes. The present exact EGF is not inferred from the envelope inequalities.
- The initial-excedance-set connection uses a known bivariate formula of E. Clark and R. Ehrenborg, *Explicit expressions for the extremal excedance set statistics*, European Journal of Combinatorics31 (2010),270–279. It is reproduced as Eq(3.1) in R. Ferraz de Andrade, E. Lundberg and B. Nagle, [*Asymptotics of the extremal excedance set statistic*, arXiv1403.0691v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.0691). That source's Corollary1.4 proves the growth constant for a two-pattern subclass and explicitly leaves the single-pattern limit open. The A113226 exponential-transform relation is derived in the current note, while the excedance formula and related class counts are credited to their authors.
- [OEIS A136127](https://oeis.org/A136127/internal), revision #55 dated17February2026, gives the initial-excedance-set sequence. The latest cited paper, Benjamin Testart, [*On minimal pattern-containing inversion sequences*, arXiv2602.12130v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12130v1), concerns a different interpretation. Its inspected text did not supply the A113226 identity or asymptotic.
- ProveIt source baseline is origin/main commit1512ef8356d10fef99e7caaa2753620f0c900c83. The enumerated file inventory and the current Combinatorial Transseries Inverses exemplar contain no named A113226/A136127 target. The gamma-core inversion framework is existing background, not claimed as a new general inversion theorem.

Live searches for A113226, 12-34 exact/exponential generating functions, arctangent formulas, and the 2025 conjecture numbers did not locate a later proof. Searches are bounded evidence only. The report does not claim a worldwide priority determination or external peer review.

The general saddle method is classical. The new candidate results are the exact single-pattern EGF and recurrence, its shifted-exponential-transform relation, explicit resolution of the cited asymptotic conjectures, and the complete coefficient/inverse algorithms with remainders.

The exact formula also gives a differential-algebraic equation and infinitely many accessible finite singularities, hence non-D-finiteness and failure of P-recursiveness. Additional bounded searches for A113226/12-34 with D-finite and P-recursive did not locate this conclusion. The non-D-finiteness mentioned elsewhere in the 2025 paper concerns a different interval-order class and is not a proof for this sequence.
