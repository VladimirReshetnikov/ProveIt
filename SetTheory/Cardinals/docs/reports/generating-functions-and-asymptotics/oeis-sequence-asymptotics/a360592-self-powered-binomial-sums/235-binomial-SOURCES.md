# Sources and claim scope

Report235, 5 October 2026, is self-contained. Report230, *Corrected Poisson Endpoint Expansions* (5 October 2026), is credited as the scalar predecessor; none of its files is needed by this package. The present article proves the marked critical law, full weighted binomial comparison and global optimization. No worldwide priority, external peer review, effective asymptotic constants, or exact finite-n optimizer is claimed.

## Primary sources

- OEIS A360592: https://oeis.org/A360592 . Indexed primary page inspected 5 October 2026. Direct fetch was unavailable; latest revision history was not verified. It gives the generating function, finite sum and initial terms. The partial-function inactive-vertex interpretation in the article is derived from those weights. Report230's scalar refinement correction is credited and reproduced.
- Barbour, Kowalski and Nikeghbali, *Mod-discrete expansions*: https://people.math.ethz.ch/~kowalski/mod-discrete.pdf ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s00440-013-0498-8 . Author PDF numbering: Proposition 3.3, Corollary 4.2, Theorem 5.1. Signed Charlier and positive fitting mechanisms are classical.
- Barbour and Xia, *Poisson perturbations* (1999): https://numdam.org/item/PS_1999__3__131_0.pdf . Theorem 4.1 equation (4.2) gives a standard small-success-probability rate mechanism, not the present conditioned-law theorem.
- Soon, *Binomial approximation for dependent indicators* (1996): https://www3.stat.sinica.edu.tw/statistica/oldpdf/A6n314.pdf . Two-parameter fitting and integer rounding are precedents. Its structural hypotheses are not assumed for this law.
- Cekanavicius, Pekoz, Rollin and Shwartz, *A three-parameter binomial approximation* (2009): https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.2855 . Inspected v1. Shifted-binomial fitting includes support/rounding issues and is a different approximating family.
- Chhaibi, Delbaen, Meliot and Nikeghbali, *Mod-phi convergence: Approximation of discrete measures and harmonic analysis on the torus* (2020): https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/aif.3332.pdf . Theorem 3.11 is an antecedent for sharp Gaussian derivative/Hermite TV constants, under its global residue hypotheses. Parity and the vanishing leading coefficient require additional control here.
- Meliot, Nikeghbali and Visentin, *Mod-Poisson approximation schemes and higher-order Chen-Stein inequalities*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.13818 . Inspected v1 (2022). Higher-order signed approximation and positive conversion are classical mechanisms, not a direct proof for this model.
- Ehm, *Binomial approximation to the Poisson binomial distribution* (1991): https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-7152(91)90170-V . Publisher abstract only; no original theorem-level claim used.

## Limits

The primary literature comparison is bounded and cannot prove historical absence of a related exact constant or optimization theorem. General moment matching, Charlier expansions and Hermite limits are not presented as new. None of the cited probabilistic approximation theorems is used as an unverified black-box proof input. Public checks are finite exact algebra or high-precision numerical corroboration. Numerical roundoff is not interval-certified. Every asymptotic bound keeps the requested order and the model parameter fixed.

## Supplementary coupling background

The appendix reproves a bounded residue coupling for positive finite log-concave weights. Its monotone-likelihood-ratio ingredients are classical. Background sources: Saumard and Wellner, *Log-concavity and strong log-concavity: a review*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.5886 ; Duembgen and Moesching, *On Stochastic Orders and Total Positivity*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.07868 . The arXiv primary records were verified 5 October 2026. The exact modular coupling is proved in full, not attributed to an uninspected theorem or claimed new. No complete higher-modulus endpoint approximation theorem is asserted in this article.
