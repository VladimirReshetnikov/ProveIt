# Scoped primary-source overlap check

Checked 2 October 2026, in addition to the scoped checks documented in the frozen report.

- OEIS A202061, https://oeis.org/A202061, was reopened. It identifies the same classical 120-avoiding ascent sequence problem.
- Conway, Conway, Elvey Price and Guttmann, EJC 29(4) (2022), P4.25, https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v29i4p25/pdf/ remains the relevant primary asymptotic study. Its pure 3/8 stretch and numerical subexponential constants are estimates; the exponential rate was proved. The published authors explicitly warn that the fitted subexponential constants depend sensitively on the exponent estimate.
- Public searches for A202061 with asymptotic/logarithmic terms and for recent classical 120-avoiding ascent sequence work did not locate a primary source proving the leading constant derived here. The 2026 work on simultaneous length-4 avoidance is a different problem.
- https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt was reopened. Its top-level current project description does not document A202061. The frozen report records earlier scoped connector code searches and partial tree evidence, with their exact limitations. No new complete recursive repository search was performed in this refinement; top-level absence is not a repository-wide absence proof.
- The unpublished polynomial-time enumeration credited in the 2022 paper remains a possible overlap for the gap/state reduction. No claim of exhaustive publication priority, repository completeness, or formal Lean/Rocq certification is made.
