# Report126: independent finite exact companion

This self-contained, standard-library Python companion checks finite instances
and exact algebra for A279556 (Class 759, growth 9) and A279551 (Class 247,
growth 8). It does not prove the all-size analytic estimates in the report.

## Reproduce

Python 3.9 or later; no network, compiler, symbolic-algebra package, or other
third-party dependency is used. From any directory:

```sh
python3 /path/to/checks/verify.py --output /tmp/report126-verification.json
python3 -O /path/to/checks/verify.py --output /tmp/report126-verification-O.json
python3 /path/to/checks/negative_tests.py --output /tmp/report126-negative.json
```

The delivered reference outputs are `../data/verification_results.json` and
`../data/negative_results.json`. Both programs refuse an output path inside
this sealed `checks/` directory. Their output is deterministic and independent
of the current working directory. Normal and optimized (`-O`) verification
produce byte-identical result JSON. All guards use explicit exceptions; no
Python `assert` guards are used. Bytecode generation is suppressed.

The six-member closed inventory is README.md, verify.py, negative_tests.py,
evidence.json, provenance.json, and manifest.sha256. No directories, symlinks,
unlisted files, duplicate/missing manifest entries, or unresealed source
changes are accepted. The manifest seals every member except itself; the
outer report inventory seals the manifest. These hashes protect against
accidental or uncoordinated changes, not replacement of all code and anchors.
Run trusted code. Each checker leaves source bytes and inventories unchanged.

## Independent implementations and exact ranges

The checker neither imports nor executes any producer or previous audit
program. Prior programs and outputs were reference comparisons during its
development; they are not its implementation, dependencies, or input answers.
All arithmetic is integer, rational, or sparse formal-polynomial arithmetic.
There are no floating-point floors, regressions, fitted amplitudes, numerical
analytic constants, or numerical effective onsets.

1. **Direct avoidance and completed extraction, n=0..8.** Every inversion
   sequence is generated from the independent Cartesian coordinate product.
   Forbidden relation triples are tested directly. A separate raw label-tree
   implementation has the same terminal counts. Terminal extraction is c=0
   for 759 and c=0,p<=2 for 247. Class 247 auxiliary all-zero states with p>2
   are retained during growth. The direct counts are
   `1,1,2,5,15,51,190,759,3206` and
   `1,1,2,4,10,27,79,247,816`, respectively.

2. **Physical decorated histories, n=0..8.** States are physical inversion
   sequences together with ordered nonempty blocks of pending positions.
   Onto words are independently generated and their prohibited standardized
   triples tested. Selected-zero/pivot/suffix operations grow these physical
   states. Every decorated pair has path multiplicity one, every physical
   growth step passes its unique reverse rule, the full label distributions
   agree with the raw tree, and the completed endpoint sets equal the direct
   avoidance sets. The n=8 auxiliary counts are 13,631 and 10,746, distinctly
   larger than the target counts 3,206 and 816. Onto-word multiplicities are
   separately checked for widths 1..6 (21 width/letter cases per class).
   This finite experiment corroborates, but does not prove, all-size bijection
   or no-overcount claims.

3. **Duration-resolved renewal, n=0..24.** A separate renewal convolution uses
   a finite first-return duration law generated by a Pascal-type commitment
   countdown. It agrees with the full raw boundary distribution at each n,
   both unrestricted and with boundary-height cutoffs H=2,4,7. There are 100
   distribution comparisons per class. The actual original duration w+1 is
   retained; no mean-duration substitution is used. The b=0 transition takes
   exactly one step and has no bulk segment. Digest summaries of the exact
   boundary distributions are recorded in the result JSON.

4. **Normalization.** Independent propagation of tilted edge weights agrees
   with the full endpoint-by-endpoint formula
   `count*x^p*y^c/mu^n` through n=12. Full-layer substochastic totals and target
   upper bounds are checked through n=24. Fixed access/termination paths are
   checked at p0=2..12, including the Class 247 descent to p=2. Converting a
   boundary path mass back to normalized counts multiplies by x^(-p), with
   the inverse exponent. This checks the fixed terminal factor, without
   treating a growing top-height factor as a fixed constant.

5. **Completed cycles.** For b=1..10, ell=b..3b+2, w=b..2b+3, all 1,495 exact
   rational cycle factorizations per class are checked, including 700
   zero-mass out-of-support choices for 247. Literal binary-word enumeration
   independently checks 57 first-return cases with b=1..6 and w<=12. Class
   247's factor is 3/16; its ordinary-binomial center has the bounded +1/3
   shift recorded in the proof, not a different exact mean.

6. **Both joint transforms.** With u=exp(lambda), v=exp(eta), sparse rational
   functions independently assemble the commitment-removal generating
   function and the first-return duration generating function. Clearing
   denominators verifies `A*d_b*R^b` for every b=0..8 in both classes, including
   the b=0 interpretation. Euler differentiation u*d/du and v*d/dv verifies
   A(0), R(0), both zero first displacement derivatives, second displacement
   derivatives 3/2 and 2/3, first duration derivatives 3/2 and 4/3, and zero-
   tilt boundary masses 2/3 and 5/8. These are formal rational-function
   identities; they do not certify convergence of an infinite b-sum at
   positive tilt. The report uses only legal truncated positive-tilt b-sums.

7. **Barrier algebra.** Exact differentiation in monomials
   t^a(log t)^b with half-integer a,b gives the stated first and second
   derivatives of sqrt(t log t). The second derivative has only strictly
   negative nonzero coefficients; for t>=2 its monomials are positive.
   This confirms the algebra and concavity sign, not global superharmonicity,
   a uniform contraction constant, or an optional-stopping theorem.

8. **Rational-certified logarithmic floors and filling.** The selected
   k-values are 256..320 and 512,1024,4096,10000,10^6,10^8: 71 values.
   An 80-term positive atanh series with a geometric remainder and binary
   range reduction encloses each logarithm by rational endpoints. A floor
   is accepted only if both endpoints have the same floor; the scaled
   enclosure width is less than 10^(-30). No floating-point log is used.
   The selected floors include
   `p_256=363664` and `p_100000000=184206807539523654`.
   There are 710 stage/plateau support, moderate-window and endpoint checks,
   with the full plateau interval certified by its endpoints; 22,964 exact
   filling cases using constructive run-length-encoded compositions; and 32
   complete ascent/fill/descent length assemblies. Large target lengths can
   be checked without allocating every plateau cycle. The finite k sample
   does not assert a valid numerical onset for the all-k analytic lemma.

9. **Inverse directions and strictness.** Direct append-largest injections
   and strictness witnesses are checked in the finite avoidance range.
   Rational threshold examples verify first-hit bracketing and the direction
   of the elementary growth-based inverse bound. Algebraic positive/negative
   margin checks and exact rational ceiling equivalences verify the two
   inverse directions and positive correction sign. The scale exponents
   1/3,2/3 and the conjectured exponent 3/8 have gap 1/24. These checks do not
   infer an asymptotic inverse statement from a finite sample.

The rational values and internal count prefixes in evidence.json are frozen
claims that the independent calculations test, not a source of derived answers.
