\section{Explicit Kostka sum corrections through cubic order}\label{tks:sec:expansions}
Combining Theorem~\ref{tks:thm:main} at $S=6$ with \eqref{tks:eq:shiftratio} yields, for $t=n^{-1/2}$,
\begin{align}
\frac{a_n}{CI_n}=1
 &+H_3t^3+(H_4-\tfrac32H_3)t^4\notag\\
 &+(H_5-2H_4+\tfrac{27}{8}H_3)t^5\notag\\
 &+(H_6-\tfrac52H_5+6H_4-\tfrac{37}{8}H_3)t^6
 +O(t^7).\label{tks:eq:normalizeda}
\end{align}
Relative to the elementary leading function $CB(n)$, this becomes
\begin{align}
\frac{a_n}{CB(n)}=1
 &+\frac7{24}t-\frac{119}{1152}t^2
 +(H_3-\tfrac{7933}{414720})t^3\notag\\
 &+(H_4-\tfrac{29}{24}H_3+\tfrac{1967381}{39813120})t^4\notag\\
 &+(H_5-\tfrac{41}{24}H_4+\tfrac{3265}{1152}H_3
          -\tfrac{57200419}{1337720832})t^5\notag\\
 &+(H_6-\tfrac{53}{24}H_5+\tfrac{6121}{1152}H_4
          -\tfrac{1453513}{414720}H_3
          +\tfrac{6340449533}{687970713600})t^6
 +O(t^7).\label{tks:eq:amultiplier}
\end{align}
No coefficients are fitted to sequence data. These expressions are finite compositions of the support coefficients and the rational involution expansion.

For inversion define
\begin{equation}\label{tks:eq:kappa}
\kappa_0=\log C-\frac14-\frac12\log2.
\end{equation}
At every fixed order $K\geq0$ there are effectively computable real coefficients $\beta_j$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{tks:eq:loga}
\log a_n=\frac n2(\log n-1)+\sqrt n+\kappa_0
 +\sum_{j=1}^{K}\beta_j n^{-j/2}
 +O_K(n^{-(K+1)/2}).
\end{equation}
Through $K=6$ they are
\begin{align}
\beta_1&=\frac7{24},&
\beta_2&=-\frac7{48},\notag\\
\beta_3&=H_3+\frac{37}{1920},&
\beta_4&=H_4-\frac32H_3+\frac5{128},\notag\\
\beta_5&=H_5-2H_4+\frac{27}{8}H_3-\frac{5879}{107520},\notag\\
\beta_6&=H_6-\frac52H_5+6H_4-\frac{37}{8}H_3
             -\frac12H_3^2+\frac{43}{1440}.\label{tks:eq:betas}
\end{align}
The quadratic term $-H_3^2/2$ first appears at order $n^{-3}$ and is required by the logarithm. At any higher fixed order, compute enough $H_s$, form the ratio series using \eqref{tks:eq:shiftrule}, and take a finite logarithmic series. Theorem~\ref{tks:thm:main} supplies the remainder needed to justify this algebra.

The resulting hierarchy has three useful normalizations. The shifted-involution form preserves the support interpretation and positivity of $H_s$. The $CI_n$ form displays how the aggregate differs from an exact involution count. The logarithmic form isolates a monotone elementary function suitable for controlled inversion. They are equivalent fixed-order descriptions, rather than independent asymptotic assumptions.
