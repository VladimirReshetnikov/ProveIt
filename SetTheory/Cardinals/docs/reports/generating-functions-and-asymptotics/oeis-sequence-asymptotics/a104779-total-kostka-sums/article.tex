{\LARGE\bfseries All Fixed Order Asymptotics\\[5pt] for Total Kostka Sums}\par\vspace{9pt}
{\large Shifted involutions, margin laws, and controlled inverses}\par\vspace{7pt}
5 October 2026
\end{center}
\begin{abstract}
Let $a_n$ be the sum of all entries of the Kostka matrix indexed by partitions of $n$, the sequence OEIS A104779. We prove, for every fixed $S\geq0$,
\[
a_n=C\sum_{s=0}^{S}H_s I_{n-s}
       +O_S\bigl(I_n n^{-(S+1)/2}\bigr),
\]
where $I_n$ is the involution number, $C=\prod_{j\geq2}(1-1/j!)^{-1}$, and the nonnegative constants $H_s$ are effectively computable, with $H_0=1$ and $H_1=H_2=0$. A positive global support bound controls the growing family of cycle types, while analytic coefficient stabilization identifies every fixed support. We give half-integer corrections through $n^{-3}$, rationally certified forty-decimal intervals for five constants, and a Lambert-$W$/Newton inverse with a precise two-ceiling qualification. The entire nonunit margin profile converges to independent geometric multiplicities, with sharp total-variation distance $(2H_3/C)n^{-3/2}+O(n^{-2})$. The same method gives factorial-shift expansions for A321652 and A068313. An exact Gaussian half-axis split further supplies separately controlled dominant and alternating recessive sectors, the latter smaller by $e^{-2\sqrt n}$. The cycle-index identities are due to Schwob; the involution and special-function asymptotic methods are classical. No complete transseries theory, explicit asymptotic onset, or certified finite-input inverse is asserted.
\end{abstract}
