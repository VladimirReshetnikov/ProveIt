\section{The matrix model and its graph encoding}\label{ssm:sec:model}
A symmetric sign matrix is a symmetric matrix $X=(x_{ij})_{1\le i,j\le n}$ with every $x_{ij}\in\{-1,+1\}$. Its diagonal is variable. Let $A_n$ be the number for which every row sum is nonnegative. Symmetry makes the column conditions identical. Positions are labelled: simultaneous permutations of rows and columns are not identified. We set $A_0=1$ by the empty-matrix convention; the published sequence begins with $A_1=1$ and $A_2=5$ \cite{oeis}.

For $M\ge1$, $0\le k\le M-1$, and $0\le f\le M$, let $U_f(M,k)$ count simple graphs on the fixed vertex set $[M]$ such that the first $M-f$ degrees are at most $k$. The last $f$ vertices are specified in advance and unrestricted. Write $B(M,k)=U_0(M,k)$. There is no factor $\binom Mf$ in this definition.

\begin{proposition}[Exact graph bridge]\label{ssm:prop:bridge}
For every $n\ge0$,
\begin{equation}\label{ssm:eq:bridge}
 A_n=U_1(n+1,\lfloor n/2\rfloor).
\end{equation}
For $n\ge1$, one also has the exact soft-boundary formula
\begin{equation}\label{ssm:eq:soft}
 A_n=2^n\sum_{\substack{G\text{ on }[n]\\\Delta(G)\le k}}2^{-N_k(G)},
 \qquad k=\lfloor n/2\rfloor,
\end{equation}
where $N_k(G)$ is the number of vertices of degree $k$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Add a distinguished vertex $*$ to $[n]$. Put an edge $ij$, $i\ne j$, precisely when $x_{ij}=-1$, and an edge $i*$ precisely when $x_{ii}=-1$. Each of the $n(n+1)/2=\binom{n+1}{2}$ independent signs specifies one possible edge, so the correspondence is bijective. The number of negative entries in row $i$ is exactly $d_i$, giving row sum $n-2d_i$. Hence the row condition is $d_i\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor$, with no condition at $*$.

Alternatively, fix only the negative off-diagonal entries, giving a graph $G$ on $[n]$. If $d_i>k$, no diagonal sign is allowed. If $d_i=k$, the diagonal must be positive. If $d_i<k$, either diagonal sign is allowed. Multiplication over vertices gives $2^{n-N_k(G)}$, proving \eqref{ssm:eq:soft}.
\end{proof}

The diagonal-to-extra-vertex correspondence is a prior structural fact: Greenhill and McKay state it explicitly for symmetric $0$--$1$ matrices with diagonal weight one \cite[p.~902; preprint p.~3]{gm}. Proposition~\ref{ssm:prop:bridge} includes the short proof to fix the sign convention and the inequality cap. Their dense Theorem 1.4 treats prescribed exact row sums with a variable diagonal, a direct pointwise predecessor; summing to the capped event still requires the relative estimates developed here.

The extra vertex is useful because it replaces the boundary weight in \eqref{ssm:eq:soft} by a single free degree coordinate. Treating the row sums as independent would lose the relevant rare-event correlation. A multivariate central limit theorem with an absolute error is likewise insufficient: the desired probability decays exponentially in $n$.

\subsection{What is imported and what is proved here}
McKay and Wormald's uniform dense degree-sequence enumeration \cite[Theorem 3(ii)]{mw} is the pointwise counting input. McKay, Wanless, and Wormald \cite{mww} already determined the all-capped graph equivalent, including its exponential rate, constant, and square-root boundary effect. We use their Corollary 2 for the final matrix constants, and their Lemma 2 and equation (29) for the first correction to an auxiliary saddle mass.

The proof below supplies the passage from all-capped to mixed capped and free coordinates, including the window $f=O(\sqrt M)$. It does not apply the comparison theorem \cite[Theorem 3]{mww} directly to $2^{-N_k}$. That theorem requires a polynomial bound on the ratio of an observable's maximum to its comparison-model expectation; this condition has not been established for the weight in \eqref{ssm:eq:soft}. Instead, the proof sums the pointwise enumeration formula with an exact tilt and controls its positive exponential weight. The switching argument is an unequal-cap adaptation of the method in \cite[Lemma 4]{mww}, and its counts are derived explicitly here.

Flammenkamp's university page identifies a joint counting project with Sillke in 1981 and links an exact table agreeing with $A_n$ through order $16$ \cite{sf}. This dates the project, not necessarily every value in the current table. The underlying unpublished manuscript or proof notes cited by OEIS were not recovered. Anderson's cited Section 3.1 \cite{anderson} was also not inspected. Greene and Kleitman's complete displayed article \cite{gk} was read; it concerns symmetric chains, strengthened Sperner bounds, lattice paths, and two-row plane partitions, and does not state the sign-matrix asymptotic used here. These are precise limits on the inspected historical record, not evidence that no earlier unpublished result exists.

Later neighboring work addresses loopy exact margins, partial stability at exponential-rate scale, and friendly bisections at constant-factor precision \cite{gm,dgz,mss}. The inspected results do not directly supply the mixed-cap relative equivalent below. In particular, broad partial-constraint ideas and the graph bridge are not presented as new. The present article makes no priority claim.
