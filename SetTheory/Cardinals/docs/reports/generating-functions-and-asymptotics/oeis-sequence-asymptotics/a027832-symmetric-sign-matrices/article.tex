{\LARGE\bfseries Symmetric Sign Matrices\\[5pt] and Free Degree Constraints}\par\vspace{9pt}
{\large Leading asymptotics, a critical window, and controlled inverses}\par\vspace{7pt}
5 October 2026
\end{center}
\begin{abstract}
Let $A_n$ count symmetric $n\times n$ matrices with entries in $\{-1,+1\}$ and nonnegative row sums, with diagonal entries included. This is OEIS A027832. An exact graph encoding adds one distinguished vertex whose degree is unrestricted. We prove that removing a fixed number $f$ of degree caps near half the graph order multiplies the all-capped graph count by $\Phi(\ell)^{-f}(1+o(1))$, where $\phi(\ell)=\ell\Phi(\ell)$. More generally, uniformly for $f=O(\sqrt M)$ free vertices, a finite-saddle formula and a fully explicit equivalent retain a collective quadratic correction and a bounded-offset linear correction. The argument combines uniform dense degree-sequence enumeration with relative graph concentration, an exact exponential tilt, exponential-square uniform integrability, and explicit balancing of the even degree-sum lattice. Applying the established all-capped asymptotic gives a leading relative equivalent for $A_n$ with an essential factor $\exp(\ell\sqrt n)$ on even orders. We derive the precision of a continuous inverse and rigorous eventual two-ceiling threshold bounds. Exact finite checks and reproducible TeX and Python accompany the proof. The result is a leading $1+o(1)$ theorem; no all-orders count expansion, effective onset, or historical priority is asserted.
\end{abstract}
