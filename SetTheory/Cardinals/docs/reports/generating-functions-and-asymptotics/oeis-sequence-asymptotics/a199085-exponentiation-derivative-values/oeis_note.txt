A199085 — proof note (not submitted)

FORMULA

a(1)=a(2)=1; a(n)=floor((n^2-2)/3) for n>=3.

An overlap-counting formula valid for all n>=1 is
  a(n) = 1 + binomial(n-1,2) - Sum_{j>=2} max(n-3j,0).

Therefore
  A(z) = z/(1-z) + z^3/(1-z)^3 - z^7/((1-z)^2*(1-z^3)),
which simplifies to the generating function conjectured in the entry.

PROOF

All parenthesized expressions F have F(1)=F'(1)=1. Define
k(F)=F''(1)/2 and b(F)=F'''(1)/3. Direct differentiation yields
  k(G^H)=k(G)+1,
  b(G^H)=b(G)+2k(G)+2k(H)+1.
Both statistics vanish on the single leaf x.

Consequently k(F) is the number of internal nodes on the left spine.
Writing F=(((x^H_1)^H_2)...^H_k), one obtains
  b(F)=k^2+2*Sum_i k(H_i).
If F has n leaves, the right subtrees have n-1 leaves in total.
For k=n-1 they are all single leaves, giving b=(n-1)^2.
For 1<=k<=n-2, their total depth s runs through every integer
1<=s<=n-k-1. Necessity follows from k(H_i)<=size(H_i)-1 and
positivity for non-leaves. To realize a prescribed s, make H_1
have n-k leaves and left depth s, and make the other right
subtrees leaves. A tree of size m and any depth h in 1,...,m-1
is obtained from x^(right_comb(m-h)) by adding h-1 outer powers x.

Thus, apart from the disjoint largest singleton (n-1)^2, the possible
b-values are P_k={k^2+2,k^2+4,...,k^2+2(n-k-1)}.
Different parities are disjoint. Within a parity both endpoints
increase, so P_k intersects the earlier union in exactly
P_k intersect P_(k-2), of size max(n-3k+3,0). Adding the intervals
successively therefore gives
  a(n)=1+binomial(n-1,2)-Sum_{j>=2} max(n-3j,0).
The n=1,2 cases also satisfy this identity.

Finally, for j>=2,
  Sum_{n>=1} max(n-3j,0) z^n = z^(3j+1)/(1-z)^2.
Summing on j gives z^7/((1-z)^2*(1-z^3)), proving the generating
function. Evaluating the finite correction sum by n modulo 3 gives
the floor formula above.

Full details and exact-arithmetic verification are in article.pdf,
article.tex, and verify.py in this archive.
