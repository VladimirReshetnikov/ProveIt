For every fixed number m of rows, the generating function in the number
n of columns is a diagonal of a rational function, hence D-finite. A proof
cuts the prefix-state path at the m row births and m row completions. The
intervening strict-chamber walks have reflection-determinant counts, and
the finite event decomposition is a multiple binomial sum. This gives
existence of a P-recursive relation without assuming the conjectured
recurrence displayed in the entry.

**Do not remove the conjectural status of the displayed specific
recurrences or finite-sum formulas on the basis of this result alone.**
The manuscript does not certify those particular operators or identities.

The manuscript also studies the refined count R_m(n) of arrays with
first-row completion before last-row birth. No OEIS identifier for this
refinement is assigned or proposed without checking for duplication.
