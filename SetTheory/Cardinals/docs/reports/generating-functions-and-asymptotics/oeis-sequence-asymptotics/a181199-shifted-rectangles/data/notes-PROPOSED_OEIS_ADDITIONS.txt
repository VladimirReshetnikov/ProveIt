General COMMENT for the fixed-height family A181196:

Let T_m(n) count m-by-n arrays and fix m. Put d=m*(m-1)/2,
J_m=Product(j!,j=0..m-1), M_m(n)=(m*n)!/(n!)^m. Then

T_m(n) = M_m(n)*J_m/(4*n)^d *
 (1 + m*(m^2-1)/(12*n)
    + m^2*(m^2-1)*(m^2+2)/(288*n^2) + O_m(n^(-3))).

There is a full inverse-power expansion with rational coefficients given
by a finite standardized-binomial moment formula in the article.

The ordinary generating function Sum(T_m(n)*z^n,n>=0), T_m(0)=1,
is algebraic over Q(z) exactly when m=1 or m=2.
