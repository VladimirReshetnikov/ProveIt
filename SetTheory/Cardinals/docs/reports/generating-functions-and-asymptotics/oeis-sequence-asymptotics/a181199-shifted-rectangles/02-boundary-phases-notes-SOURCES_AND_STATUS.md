## Attribution boundary

### Imported or recovered, not new

- The shifted hook product for strict partitions.
- The classical chamber reflection principle (a proof is included).
- The multiple-binomial-sum/rational-diagonal theorem.
- Gaussian Vandermonde normalization and polynomial orthogonality tools
  (short derivations of the needed identities are included).
- The prior ProveIt expansion and algebraicity classification for total counts.
- Lambert-W leading inversion and ordinary formal series reversion.
- Catalan and fixed-width formulas used as controls.

### Developed and proved in this manuscript

- Finite birth/completion skeletons as bounded-dimensional binomial sums
  for these arrays, and the resulting all-height rational-diagonal theorem.
- Exact event-word realizability and marked rank-total extensions.
- The rare first-completion hook core, explicit separation bounds, and
  beta–binomial representation.
- The sharp rare-sector rate and constant, all-order coefficient rule,
  universal first correction, and evaluated higher corrections.
- The corresponding rare conditional shape law and rare-profile inversion
  application; the probability ratio additionally imports the predecessor's
  total asymptotic.

These distinctions describe the manuscript's contribution relative to its
inspected sources. They are not a historical-priority certification.

## Verification and limitations

The exact verification suite passed 1,954 finite tests. It compares three
finite counting methods, checks the reflection determinant independently,
checks several exact inequalities, and recomputes rational coefficients.
The symbolic coefficient engine is an implementation of a proved finite
formula. It is not a proof-assistant certificate.

The numerical tables use 70-digit arithmetic against exact integer counts,
but are not interval enclosures. The displayed profile inversion is not a
certified integer-threshold computation. The existence of asymptotic error
constants is proved; optimized explicit constants and onset indices are
not delivered.

No candidate recurrence is used as a premise or to generate verification
values. No specific conjectured OEIS recurrence is declared proved.
No complete resurgent transseries, Borel summation, or Stokes multiplier
is established. No growing-height theorem is asserted.

No results were submitted to OEIS, no persistent Library content was
modified, and no GitHub commits were made.
