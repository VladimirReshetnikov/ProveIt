ESSENTIAL EXTERNAL THEOREM
Michel Bauer and Olivier Golinelli
Random incidence matrices: moments of the spectral density
Journal of Statistical Physics 103 (2001), 301-337
https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0007127v2
https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0007127
https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1004879905284
Primary text inspected: Sections 5.3-5.6, especially Section 5.5 equation (9)
and its proof. In the report's normalization, the theorem is
S(t,l) <= I(t,l) <= Catalan_l S(t,l). This coefficientwise comparison is an
essential credited input, not a result attributed to this report. Section 5.3
provides the fixed-graph-moment limit and Section 5.4 the departure-refined
first-edge recurrence. Section 5.6 fixed-tree-size star asymptotics have a
different regime from the full growing-index sum.
The Touchard(4) envelope used here is proved in the report using positivity,
Cauchy's bound and a single Dobinski summand; no earlier report is needed.

LATER TREE-WALK CROSS-CHECK
Eva-Maria Hainzl and Elie de Panafieu
Tree walks and the spectrum of random graphs
https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08347v1
https://arxiv.org/html/2405.08347v1
https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.AofA.2024.11
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2026.104386
Primary preprint definitions and main statements inspected and directly
rechecked during Report213 preparation. Theorem 3 is a fixed-excess
rational-generating-function description; Theorem 4 expands fixed moments
as mean degree tends to infinity. Neither inspected statement supplies this
report's fixed-mean-degree, high-moment relative equivalent. The 2026 journal
record was located; only abstract/introduction were retrieved in the source
check. No full-version identity or exhaustive nonoverlap is presumed.

SCOPE
The proof's external mathematical dependence is the BG coefficientwise theorem.
All Bell estimates and dominant-window steps needed for the theorem are supplied
in the article. Bounded searches and inspected statements did not locate the
same equivalent, but absence from those materials does not establish novelty.
