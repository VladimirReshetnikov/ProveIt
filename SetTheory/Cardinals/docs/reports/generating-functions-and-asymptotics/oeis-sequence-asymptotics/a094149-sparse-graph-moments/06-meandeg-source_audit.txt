SOURCE AND SCOPE AUDIT
Checked 8 October 2026

Purpose
-------
This is a bounded literature and provenance audit, not a certification of
worldwide priority. The mathematical proofs in the accompanying article are
intended to stand independently of the novelty assessment. The two inspected
repositories contain research manuscripts; their claims are not treated as
refereed theorems merely because they are present in a repository.

1. Direct ProveIt precursor and overlap
--------------------------------------
Repository snapshot: 9570aaede2458830be9b3bbb072481c88f4f5a4c
Source:
https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/9570aaede2458830be9b3bbb072481c88f4f5a4c/SetTheory/Cardinals/docs/reports/generating-functions-and-asymptotics/oeis-sequence-asymptotics/a094149-sparse-graph-moments/article.tex

The inspected merged source develops the c=1 expansion of the sparse-graph
even moments around 2 B_(k+1), including the dominant-departure reduction,
weighted Polya-urn bounds, finite Newton collision transforms, and every
fixed collision order. Its question labelled sgm:q:meandegree explicitly
asks for general mean degree. It does not supply the sharp total variation
laws for the maximum-departure deficit proved here.

Crucial overlap exclusion: after an all-fixed-order expansion is known,
the leading remainder at order p follows by invoking order p+1 and using
the equivalent of A_(p+1). Improving a displayed log-power remainder in
this manner is a corollary, not an independent breakthrough or a new
method. The article distinguishes this fact from its parameter extension
and its distributional results.

2. Bauer--Golinelli: verified external enumerative input
-------------------------------------------------------
M. Bauer and O. Golinelli, Random incidence matrices: moments of the
spectral density, Journal of Statistical Physics 103 (2001), 301--337.
https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0007127
https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0007127

Full primary preprint inspected. Section 5.5, equation (9), proves the
coefficientwise Stirling--Catalan bounds
    S(k,l) <= I(k,l) <= Cat_l S(k,l).
These imply T_k(c) <= m_k(c) <= T_k(4c) for the present unscaled moments.
Section 5.6, equation (13), also gives I(k,l) ~ 2 S(k,l) for each FIXED
l >= 2 as k tends to infinity, via star configurations. That fixed-l
statement cannot by itself be summed through the moving Bell saddle
l of order k/log k. The article's complement and collision analysis
addresses the necessary global walk enumeration.

----------------------------------------------------------------
O. Khorunzhy, M. Shcherbina, V. Vengerovsky, Eigenvalue distribution of
large weighted random graphs, Journal of Mathematical Physics 45 (2004),
1648--1672; DOI 10.1063/1.1667610.
Publisher:
https://pubs.aip.org/aip/jmp/article/45/4/1648/383196/Eigenvalue-distribution-of-large-weighted-random
Author's institute publication list:
https://ilt.kharkiv.ua/bvi/structure/depart_e/d24/mariya_shcherbina.htm
Full author PDF linked by that list:
https://ilt.kharkiv.ua/bvi/structure/depart_e/d24/rm_10.pdf

The PDF's embedded text encoding is damaged, so its main-result pages
were rendered and read visually. Theorem 1, page 4, equations (2.7)--(2.8),
gives the limiting moment recurrence for fixed mean parameter. Theorem 3,
pages 5--6, establishes the limiting resolvent equation under finite fourth
moment. Pages 6--7 discuss finite-order inverse-spectral-parameter

closes the access gap noted in the ProveIt precursor, without claiming
that every result in the surrounding literature has been excluded.

4. Hainzl--de Panafieu: distinct large-mean regime
-------------------------------------------------
E.-M. Hainzl and E. de Panafieu, Tree walks and the spectrum of random
graphs, European Journal of Combinatorics 136 (July 2026), 104386;
DOI 10.1016/j.ejc.2026.104386.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08347
https://arxiv.org/html/2405.08347v1
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0195669826000545
Conference version:
https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.AofA.2024.11

The full 2024 primary preprint and the indexed 2026 journal introduction
and main-result summary were inspected. Theorem 3 describes generating
functions for walks of fixed excess as rational functions of the Catalan
generating function. Theorem 4 expands each fixed normalized spectral
moment through order c^(-5) as c tends to infinity; Conjecture 5 concerns
continuation to arbitrary order. These results concern a different limit
from a fixed compact set of c with moment order tending to infinity,
and from the simultaneous critical scale c W(k/c) of order sqrt(k).
Direct retrieval of the complete journal text returned an access error;
the journal main-result summary agrees with this regime distinction.

5. Spectral-tail motivation, with the approximation status retained
-------------------------------------------------------------------
G. Semerjian and L. F. Cugliandolo, Sparse random matrices: the eigenvalue
spectrum revisited, Journal of Physics A 35 (2002), 4837--4851;
DOI 10.1088/0305-4470/35/23/303.
https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0202406
https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0202406

Section 4.4, equations (30)--(33), derives a single-defect approximation
with Poisson-weighted spectral peaks near plus or minus sqrt(k), then a
smoothed tail proportional to
    exp[-mu^2 log(mu^2/(p e))].
The authors attribute the tail form to Rodgers--Bray (1988). Section 4.5
discusses corrections from the first-neighbor environment; the conclusion
states the difficulty of controlling the validity range and the errors of
successive approximations. The present rigorous moment results must not
be advertised as a pointwise spectral-density equivalent. The limiting
spectral measure has atoms, and moment asymptotics alone do not justify
such a claim.

Rodgers--Bray primary abstract:
G. J. Rodgers and A. J. Bray, Density of states of a sparse random matrix,
Physical Review B 37 (1988), 3557--3562.
https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.37.3557
Only the publisher abstract was directly accessible in this audit.

6. Classical Poisson component-count context
--------------------------------------------
B. Pittel, Random Set Partitions: Asymptotics of Subset Counts,
Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A 79(2) (1997), 326--359;
DOI 10.1006/jcta.1997.2791.
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0097316597927916

The primary indexed abstract establishes total variation approximation
of suitable block-count segments by independent Poisson variables with
means r^j/j!, where r exp(r)=n. Thus Poisson approximation for singleton
counts in assemblies is classical background. The fresh law in the
article is a marked-singleton law for a weighted partition assembly;
the claimed new distributional content is the collision correction for
the walk's maximum-departure deficit, its two sharp total variation
constants, and optimality among all Poisson means. The full Pittel paper
was not retrieved in this audit.

For standard Stein--Chen context, see R. Arratia, L. Goldstein and
L. Gordon, Poisson Approximation and the Chen--Stein Method, Statistical
Science 5(4) (1990), 403--434:
https://dornsife.usc.edu/larry-goldstein/wp-content/uploads/sites/221/2023/06/pacs-1.pdf
The accompanying deficit proof includes its own required Stein-solution
bound, using the immigration and death semigroup; no unproved specialized
Poisson approximation theorem is needed there.

8. Earlier varying-parameter Bell and sparse-matrix results
-----------------------------------------------------------
O. Khorunzhiy, On Asymptotic Properties of Bell Polynomials and
Concentration of Vertex Degree of Large Random Graphs, Journal of
Theoretical Probability 35 (2022), 20--51; published online 4 October 2020;
DOI 10.1007/s10959-020-01025-w.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10959-020-01025-w
https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.01339
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.01339

The full primary preprint v4, 22 July 2020, was inspected. Theorem 2.1
gives asymptotic regimes for ordinary Bell/Touchard polynomials as both
order and parameter vary. Section 4, equation (4.5), and Lemma 4.1
provide the local-limit saddle equivalent
    T_k(c) ~ k! exp(c(e^w-1)) / [w^k sqrt(2 pi k(1+w))],
with w exp(w)=k/c, for nonvanishing c in its stated limiting setting.
Theorem 2.2 treats restricted Bell polynomials/centered Poisson moments.
Applications concern vertex degrees and their maxima. This establishes
substantial prior varying-c Touchard analysis; that analysis by itself
does not enumerate the full spectral tree-walk moment or prove the
Catalan multiplier of the article's critical theorem. The article gives
the quantitative estimates it needs directly rather than importing a
remainder that is absent from this cited equivalent.

O. Khorunzhiy, On High Moments of Strongly Diluted Large Wigner Random
Matrices, in Seminaire de Probabilites XLVIII, Lecture Notes in
Mathematics 2168 (2016), 347--399.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.7021
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1311.7021

The primary preprint v4 abstract and Theorem 1.1, pages 3--4, were
inspected. Its regime is N,rho -> infinity, rho=o(N^(1/5)), with the
half-moment order s=floor(chi rho) for sufficiently small fixed positive
chi and bounded symmetric edge weights. The theorem approximates the
moment by a Catalan-type recurrence involving the second and fourth
edge-weight moments, and supplies a corresponding bound. Thus
simultaneous large-order, large-mean sparse-matrix analysis is established
prior work. The critical regime proved in this article instead has
k/c -> infinity and c W(k/c) of order sqrt(k), after taking the graph-size
limit. No deduction of its finite exponential multiplier from the
inspected fixed-ratio theorem was found.

Priority assessment
-------------------
The bounded searches and source comparisons did not locate the specific
collision-corrected maximum-departure law, its sharp/optimal Poisson
approximation constants, or the stated full-moment critical Catalan
multiplier at c W(k/c) of order sqrt(k). This supports presenting these
as candidate new results with complete proofs, while reserving a
definitive priority claim for broader expert review. The use of saddle
points, Bell/Touchard
polynomials, Poisson approximation, Charlier polynomials, and dominant
star geometry is established methodology and is credited as such.
