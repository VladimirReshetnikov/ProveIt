Report209 public source ledger — checked 4 October 2026

1. M. Bauer and O. Golinelli, Random incidence matrices: moments of the spectral
   density, Journal of Statistical Physics 103 (2001), 301–337.
   https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1004879905284
   Full primary preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0007127v2
   Full relevant sections read: 5.3–5.6. Eq. (8) is the recurrence, Eq. (9)
   gives the Stirling/Catalan comparison, Eq. (12) contains the bi-star
   decomposition whose single-use-pendant-edge specialization is our overlap,
   and Eq. (13) is the fixed-edge asymptotic. The two star rootings already
   imply M_(2k) >= 2B_k-1. No priority for those results is claimed.

2. OEIS A094149
   https://oeis.org/A094149
   Sequence identification and displayed 13-term positive-k prefix checked.
   The displayed comparison is not evidence of an open-problem status.

3. A. Spiridonov, author-hosted senior thesis on sparse random graphs.
   https://alexey.im/papers/sparse-random-graphs-senior-thesis.pdf
   Full thesis available; Section 3.3 checked. Its older Bell/Catalan
   comparison conjecture is already covered by Bauer–Golinelli.

4. E.-M. Hainzl and E. de Panafieu, Tree walks and the spectrum of random graphs.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.08347
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08347
   https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.AofA.2024.11
   Journal record: European Journal of Combinatorics 136 (2026), 104386.
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2026.104386
   Full 2024 preprint read; fixed-excess and large-c scope checked, along with
   its discussion of bivariate asymptotics on printed p. 15. It uses
   adjacency/sqrt(c) normalization. Publisher full journal text was blocked
   and not independently read. Journal metadata is not a full-text audit.

5. E.-M. Hainzl, Formulas and asymptotics of hypergraph Catalan numbers.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.08865
   https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.AofA.2026.20
   Theorem 1 and Sections 1–2 checked: equal per-edge traversal multiplicity,
   with edge count tending to infinity at fixed multiplicity. This scope is
   different from the unrestricted moment sum in Report209.

6. P. Valigi, J. W. Baron, I. Neri, G. Biroli, C. Cammarota, Eigenvalue spectral
   tails and localization properties of asymmetric networks.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.20225
   https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/ae16ec
   Abstract, Sections 2–3, Eq. (24), discussion and S7 checked. Spectral-tail
   approximations do not supply an exact A094149 asymptotic or prove this bound.

7. O. Khorunzhiy, On high moments of strongly diluted large Wigner random matrices.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.7021
   Abstract-level check only: dilution tends to infinity with moment index
   proportional to dilution, rather than fixed mean degree one.

8. O. Khorunzhiy, On asymptotic properties of high moments of compound Poisson
   distribution. https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00379
   Abstract-level scope check only: compound-Poisson moments/weighted degrees.

No exact shifted-Bell/Fibonacci inequality or matching rigorous full-moment
asymptotic was located in these bounded checks. This is not a novelty proof,
an exhaustive survey, or a global statement of unresolved status. No external
publication or OEIS submission accompanies this package. No third-party full
text or source-repository code is redistributed in the archive.
