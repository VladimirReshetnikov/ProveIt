Report212 - Public sources and bounded inspection scope
Checked 4 October 2026. Source absence is not a priority or global-open-status claim.
No third-party full text is redistributed.

Michel Bauer and Olivier Golinelli
Random incidence matrices: moments of the spectral density
Journal of Statistical Physics 103 (2001), 301-337
https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0007127v2
https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1004879905284
Primary full text inspected in the preceding moment reports: tree-walk moment
identification, first-edge recurrence and its distinct-edge refinement, Section 5
bounds and fixed-size star/bi-star asymptotics. The present recurrence is credited
to this infrastructure, not claimed as new. The arXiv record was rechecked.

Eva-Maria Hainzl and Elie de Panafieu
Tree walks and the spectrum of random graphs
https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.08347v1
https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.AofA.2024.11
Primary full preprint inspected in the preceding reports, including its stated
asymptotic regimes and kernel/decorating framework. Record rechecked. The 2026
journal full text was not independently inspected, so no exhaustive statement
about its contents is made.

Ella Hiesmayr and Theo McKenzie
The Spectral Edge of Constant Degree Erdos-Renyi Graphs
https://arxiv.org/html/2309.11007
https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.70011
Primary preprint inspected: Theorems 1.2 and 1.4; Section 1.4 negative edge;
Definitions 2.1-2.2; Lemmas 2.10-2.13, 4.2, 4.9-4.11; Section 6 framework.
Theorem 1.2 and Lemma 4.10 were directly rechecked while preparing this report.
Journal metadata: Random Structures & Algorithms 66 (2025), e70011.
The journal full text was not independently inspected. These high-probability
finite-graph extreme-eigenvalue results do not themselves establish the annealed
relative moment equivalent in the report's order of limits.

Bhaswar B. Bhattacharya, Sohom Bhattacharya, Shirshendu Ganguly
Spectral Edge in Sparse Random Graphs: Upper and Lower Tail Large Deviations
https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.00611
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.00611
Primary abstract and Sections 1.1-1.2, including Theorems 1.1-1.2, inspected.
The fixed-multiplicative logarithmic deviation scale is not a relative
Bell-moment theorem; no claim to exhaust all adaptable technical estimates.

Louigi Addario-Berry, Gabor Lugosi, Roberto Imbuzeiro Oliveira
The top eigenvalue of uniformly random trees
https://arxiv.org/html/2403.08443v2
https://problab.ca/louigi/papers/topeigenvalue.pdf
Theorem 1.1, proof overview, Proposition 1.3 examples, rewiring Lemma 1.5 and Section 2
organization inspected. Different model and precision; deterministic coding
methods are identified as possible tools rather than an existing proof here.

Markus Heydenreich, Peter Muller, Sara Terveer
Return probability on Bienayme-Galton-Watson trees and spectral asymptotics
of sparse Erdos-Renyi random graphs
https://arxiv.org/html/2603.00344v2
Checked July 21, 2026 version: operator definitions/equations 1.1-1.3, 1.13-1.20;
Theorems 1.1 and 1.4; Remarks 1.2 and 1.5. Supercritical survival-conditioned walk
returns and lower-edge graph-Laplacian statements are distinct from this report's
critical adjacency high-moment problem. The thesis cited for a Laplacian
high-energy remark was not independently located or inspected.

Charles Bordenave
Sparse graphs and their Benjamini-Schramm limits: a spectral tour
https://arxiv.org/html/2510.10299
Theorem 4.5 discussion and citations inspected as a scope/reference cross-check,
not used as a primary proof of the desired equivalent.

OEIS Foundation Inc.; A094149, contributed by Alexey Spiridonov
https://oeis.org/A094149
Positive-order sequence identity and initial terms checked. M0=1 is added
separately in the mathematical convention. An OEIS comparison is neither a
novelty search nor an asymptotic proof.
