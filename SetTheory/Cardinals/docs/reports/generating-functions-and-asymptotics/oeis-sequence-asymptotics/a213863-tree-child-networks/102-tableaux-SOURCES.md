# Primary source provenance

Source inspection and bounded literature check: 2 October 2026.

- Fuchs, Guan-Ru Yu, and Zhang, *On the asymptotic growth of the number of tree-child networks*, European Journal of Combinatorics 93 (2021), 103278; arXiv:2003.08049v2. Definition 1 fixes the root/degree/leaf-label convention. Proposition 2 gives TC_(n,n−1)=n!a_(n−1). Sections 3–4 establish the Airy two-sided scale; the final discussion identifies the first-order equivalent problem. https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.08049
- Banderier and Wallner, *Young tableaux with periodic walls: counting with the density method*, SLC 85B (2021), Article 47. Theorem 4.1, p.8, states the A213863 Theta estimate with base 12, exponent alpha(3n)^(1/3), and n^(−2/3). https://www.emis.de/journals/SLC/wpapers/FPSAC2021/47Banderier.pdf
- Chang, Fuchs, Liu, Wallner, and Guan-Ru Yu, *Enumerative and distributional results for d-combining tree-child networks*, Advances in Applied Mathematics 157 (2024), 102704. Definitions 1.1–1.3 agree at d=2 with the earlier binary convention. Corollary 1.11(i) gives the ratio sqrt(e); Remark 1.12 identifies the remaining amplitude obstacle. Lemmas 3.17, 3.19, and 3.20 with Remark 3.18 also imply the O(n^(−2/3)) ratio and total-variation bounds proved explicitly in the article; this is presented as a direct corollary of published estimates, without a novelty claim. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2024.102704
- Lin, Feihu Liu, Jiahang Liu, Jing Liu, and Xin, *Proof of a conjecture on Young tableaux with walls*, arXiv:2601.09551v3 (6 September 2026). The identity is enumerative; Section 2.6, Corollary 2.15 (p.22), has higher corrections for fixed k, not k=n−1. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09551
- Hexuan Liu, Wallner, and Guan-Ru Yu, *A combinatorial framework for the Pons–Batle identity: Young tableaux, lattice paths, and limit laws*, LIPIcs 381 (AofA 2026), Article 13, pp.13:1–13:20 (13 July 2026); arXiv:2605.07587v1 remains the latest arXiv version inspected. Proposition 2 is on p.13:2. Theorems 16, 18, and 21 on pp.13:12, 13:14, and 13:15 give bounded-reticulation parameter limits; Section 6, p.13:16, discusses extension to fixed k>1. https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.AofA.2026.13
- Hao Yu and Zhang, *Asymptotic counting of binary phylogenetic networks*, arXiv:2605.23126v2 (13 June 2026). Its k=o(sqrt(n)) regime excludes maximal reticulation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23126
- Fuchs and Hao Yu, *A cube-root phase transition in tree-child networks and the enumeration threshold for galled networks*, arXiv:2608.20860 (2026). Its sparse-reticulation regime is distinct from k=n−1. Hao Yu is not Guan-Ru Yu. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20860
- Hao Yu and Zhang, *A short combinatorial proof of the Pons–Batle identity for counting tree-child networks*, arXiv:2609.04979v1 (4 September 2026). Theorem 6.4, p.12, proves the exact recurrence; no diagonal asymptotic theorem is stated. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04979
- Elvey Price, Fang, Louf, and Wallner, *Bivariate asymptotics via random walks: application to large genus maps*, arXiv:2506.06924v2 (13 April 2026). Section 10.1 discusses tableau/network and tree applications. https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06924
- OEIS A213863, accessed 2 October 2026: exact initial terms, offset zero, recurrence, and literature links. https://oeis.org/A213863

No positive diagonal amplitude equivalent or all-order diagonal theorem was found in this bounded inspected set. This statement does not certify exhaustive worldwide priority. Fixed-k higher-order formulas in the literature are explicitly distinguished from the maximal-reticulation diagonal treated in the article.
