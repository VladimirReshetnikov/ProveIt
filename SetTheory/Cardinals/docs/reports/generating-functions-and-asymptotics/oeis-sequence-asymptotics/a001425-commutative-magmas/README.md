## What the report proves

A commutative magma is any map `Sym²([n]) → [n]` (diagonal entries free; no
associativity, identity or idempotence); `N = n(n+1)/2`,
`E_n = n! U_n / n^N` is the mean automorphism-group order of a uniform labeled
law, and the identity permutation contributes exactly 1. A moved cycle type
`μ` (parts ≥ 2) has support `s`, `c` cycles and defect `d = s − c`.

- **Proposition 2.1:** the exact sector `T_μ(n)` from input orbits (with the
  `0^0 = 1` convention for fixed-point-free types), checked by a
  trace/Möbius route.
- **Theorem 1.1:** for every fixed `D ≥ 0` and every `n ≥ max(8, 4(D+1))`,
  `0 ≤ E_n − 1 − Σ_{1≤d(μ)≤D} T_μ(n) ≤ B_D(n)` with the explicit bound
  (1.7) over **every** omitted permutation (Lemmas 3.1–3.2, Proposition 3.3);
  so the remainder is `O(n^{−(D+1)n+(D+1)(D+3)})`.
- **Theorem 4.1:** `T_μ(n) = (e^{C_μ}/z_μ) n^{−dn+β_μ} e^{−sn/2} A_μ(1/n)`
  with `A_μ` holomorphic in `|t| < 1/s` and rational Taylor coefficients by an
  exact recurrence; sectors ordered by defect, support, then `β_μ`.
- Section 5: the transposition, 3-cycle and double-transposition sectors
  (5.1)–(5.6) and the three-sector identity (5.7) for every `n ≥ 12`.
- **Theorem 6.1:** `P_n ~ T_2(n)` (labeled) and `Q_n ~ 2T_2(n)` (unlabeled);
  conditional on nonrigidity the automorphism group is generated by one
  transposition with probability tending to 1.
- **Theorem 7.2:** with the exact-Gamma comparison function `G_D`, the
  threshold satisfies `⌈θ_D − η_D⌉ ≤ N_*(y) ≤ ⌈θ_D⌉`, `η_D` superexponentially
  small in `θ`; the ceilings can still differ arbitrarily far out.
  Proposition 7.3: a Lambert-W initializer
  `θ_D = u + 1/2 − 5/(4 log u + 2) + O(1/u)`, `u = (4L/W(4L))^{1/2}`.

(Section, statement and equation numbers are those of the committed PDF.)

## What the report does not claim

The exact Burnside count and the leading equivalent are in OEIS A001425 and
are background; Freese's labeled/unlabeled framework is classical context;
the unrestricted results of Freese, Tureček and Burris are not applied to the
commutative space. Harrison (1966), Tamura (1970) and Murskiĭ were not read,
so no worldwide originality or nonoverlap is claimed. Everything is
fixed-defect and eventual: no growing-defect uniformity, no optimal radius, no
effective inverse onset, no single-ceiling rule, no subgroup-resolved higher
terms; finite Taylor truncations do not resolve later sectors; numerical
values are diagnostics.
