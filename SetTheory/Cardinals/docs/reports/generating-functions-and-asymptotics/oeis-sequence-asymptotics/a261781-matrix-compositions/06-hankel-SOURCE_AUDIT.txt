2. CURRENT OEIS RECORDS

A261780: https://oeis.org/A261780
Counts nonnegative integer matrices of mass n with k ordered rows,
positive column sums, and any number of columns. The entry gives
(1-x)^k / (2(1-x)^k-1) as the fixed-k generating function and cites
Munarini--Poneti--Rinaldi and Louchard.

A261781: https://oeis.org/A261781
Counts the same matrices with every row used. The entry contains two
comments labeled conjectures, dated October 14, 2017, by Vaclav Kotesovec:
an order k(k+1)/2 column recurrence, and
d(k+1)-d(k) -> 1/log(2) for the exponential growth constants.
The recurrence's existence and the underlying fixed-k growth formula
already appear in older literature. The article adds explicit minimality
and the initial-index qualification; it does not claim those older
formulas as newly discovered.

A261784: https://oeis.org/A261784
The diagonal T(2n,n). It already records
a(n) ~ c d^n (n!)^2/n,
an exact Lambert-W expression for d, and a numerical c.
The formula is attributed to Kotesovec, February 18, 2017, updated
April 20, 2024. The article proves an exact expression for c, gives
correction coefficients, and embeds the diagonal in a uniform ray
expansion. Discovery of the leading law is not claimed.

3. PRIMARY ENUMERATIVE WORK

E. Munarini, M. Poneti, S. Rinaldi, Matrix Compositions,
Journal of Integer Sequences 12 (2009), Article 09.4.8, 28 pages.
https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL12/Rinaldi/rinaldi.html
https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL12/Rinaldi/rinaldi.pdf

Relevant results in that paper:
- Proposition 1: unrestricted generating functions.
- Propositions 12-13: root-of-unity formula and fixed-row asymptotics.
- Propositions 22-24: no-zero-row inclusion-exclusion and rational GF.
- Proposition 25: a recurrence of order binom(k+1,2).
- Proposition 29: positive Stirling/Fubini transform.
- Proposition 21: a size-k Cassini/Hankel identity for the unrestricted
  fixed-height sequence, distinct from the size-binom(k+1,2) weighted
  no-zero-row determinant proved in this article.

Earlier version:
E. Munarini, M. Poneti, S. Rinaldi, Matrix compositions,
Proceedings of FPSAC 2006, San Diego, pp. 445-456.
https://garsia.math.yorku.ca/fpsac06/papers/49.pdf
Its Section 3 already includes the spectral formula and leading
unrestricted fixed-row equivalent.

4. CLOSEST PROBABILISTIC WORK INSPECTED

G. Louchard, Matrix compositions: a probabilistic analysis,
Pure Mathematics and Applications 19 (2008), nos. 2-3, 127-146.
Author's long version dated July 10, 2008:
https://guy.louchard.web.ulb.be/louchard.papers/compmat.ps
Author's publication list, item 99:
https://guy.louchard.web.ulb.be/index_old.htm

This work fixes the row count and studies column count, column sizes,
missing column sizes, and related Gaussian/Brownian asymptotics.
An empty column size is different from a zero matrix row.
Its text was inspected for the growing-row, empty-row results pursued
here; no matching coverage-window statement was found.
