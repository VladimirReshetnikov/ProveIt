A261781

For k > 0, the reduced denominator of the ordinary generating function of
column k is Product_{j=1..k}(2*(1-x)^j-1). Therefore its minimal eventual
homogeneous recurrence with constant coefficients has order k*(k+1)/2.

Proof: inclusion-exclusion expresses the column generating function as the
alternating binomial sum of (1-x)^j/(2*(1-x)^j-1). For each j > 0 all j poles
are simple and genuine. Poles from different j cannot coincide: at such a pole
|1-x| = 2^(-1/j), which determines j. Thus no pole cancels. The generating
functions and inclusion-exclusion are earlier results; this is their explicit
minimality consequence.

The fixed-column asymptotic constants are
c(k)=1/(2*k*(2^(1/k)-1)), d(k)=1/(1-2^(-1/k)).
The differences d(k+1)-d(k) strictly increase to 1/log(2).
For d extended to x>0, d'(x)=(1/log(2))*(y/sinh(y))^2, y=log(2)/(2*x),
which is strictly increasing and tends to 1/log(2).

These statements prove the two claims labeled conjectures in the inspected
October 3, 2026 entry, attributed there to Vaclav Kotesovec, October 14, 2017.
Cite Munarini--Poneti--Rinaldi (2009) for the underlying generating functions.

A261784

Let L=log(2), t=2+LambertW_0(-2*exp(-2)). The decimal prefactor c in the
already posted asymptotic has the exact expression
c=2^(t/2)/(4*Pi*L*sqrt(t-1)). The growth rate is
 d=4/(L^2*t*(2-t)).

There is an all-orders fixed-truncation expansion
 a(n) ~ c*d^n*(n!)^2/n * (1 + delta1/n + delta2/n^2 + delta3/n^3 + ...),
where
 delta1=-0.52180670155648266388914410306946945484974842970591...
 delta2= 0.12828882808870577845979670423435993165024344140948...
 delta3=-0.00244353842284859500374038095590013033570627782694...

These decimals are evaluations of finite exact prescriptions, not fitted data.
An explicit exact expression for delta1 is given in the article; all orders
follow from its finite Stirling-kernel and Gaussian-moment algorithms.

The leading rate and decimal prefactor were already posted by Kotesovec
(February 18, 2017; updated April 20, 2024). Do not present those existing
predictions as new, or describe the line as explicitly marked a conjecture.
The additional report establishes proofs and higher-order refinements.
