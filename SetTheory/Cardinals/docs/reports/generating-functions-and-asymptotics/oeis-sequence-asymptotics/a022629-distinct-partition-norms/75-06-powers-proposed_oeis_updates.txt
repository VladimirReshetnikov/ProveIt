1. Entries A022629, A092484, A265840, A265841, A265842

Put r=1,2,3,4,5 respectively, m=sqrt(2*n), L=log(m), u=Pi^2/r^2.
The elementary theorem gives

    log(a(n)) = r*m*(L-1) + O(m/L).

This proves and sharpens each posted leading asymptotic conjecture. The refined
formula is

    log(a(n)) = r*m*(L-1 + u/(6*L) + u/(6*L^2)
                     + u*(12-u)/(72*L^3)
                     + u*(3*u+20)/(120*L^4) + O(L^(-5))).

An all-order coefficient generator and eight explicit correction coefficients
are given in Section 6. The leading conjectures are credited to Vaclav Kotesovec:
May 8, 2018 for A022629, and December 27, 2020 for the other four entries.

2. Positive fixed columns of A292189

The same formula applies to every fixed positive integer column index r.
It is not uniform when r grows with n, and does not apply to column r=0.

3. Positive fixed columns of A297321

For fixed positive integer column index s, put m=sqrt(2*n/s), L=log(m), u=Pi^2.
Then replace r*m in the displayed formula by s*m. The first 32 positive columns
are cross-referenced by A297321 to A022629 through A022660.

IMPORTANT: These are logarithmic asymptotic expansions. Exponentiating any
fixed truncation does not generally yield a relative asymptotic equivalent.
For a relative coefficient expansion use Section 5 of the report, with the exact
saddle, logarithmic product, variance, and cumulants.
