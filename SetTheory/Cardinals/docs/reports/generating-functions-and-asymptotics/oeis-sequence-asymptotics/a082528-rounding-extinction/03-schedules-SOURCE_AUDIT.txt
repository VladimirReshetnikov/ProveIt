3. Contribution and priority limits

The manuscript supplies self-contained proofs for a bounded augmented
empirical-law theorem, the general commensurable Gamma product, the
activation discontinuity and density family, a nonlimit construction,
the joint loss measure, continuity and bounded sparse stability, the
sharp real-weight spectrum, normalized-shape equivalence, and exact
bounded-law recovery by finite Mobius inversion.

These results answer the cited repository questions under the article's
explicit hypotheses. A targeted primary-literature search did not find
the same schedule theorem, but it is not an exhaustive priority audit.
No claim is made that the article settles a famous classical conjecture
or that every result is globally unpublished. The manuscript has not
been peer reviewed or formally verified.

The exact computations verify finite instances and the implementation;
they are not a substitute for the asymptotic proofs. See
VERIFICATION_NOTES.txt for their scope and reproducibility.
