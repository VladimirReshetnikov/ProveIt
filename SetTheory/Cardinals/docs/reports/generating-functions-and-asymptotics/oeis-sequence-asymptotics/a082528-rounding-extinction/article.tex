\begin{abstract}
For $m>0$, start from an integer $n\geq0$ and repeatedly replace the current
value by the largest multiple of $k^m$ below it, at stages $k=2,3,\ldots$.
We prove that the first zero occurs at
\[
 \tau_m(n)\asym
 \left[m\,\Gamma\!\left(\frac{m}{m+1}\right)^{m+1}n\right]^{1/(m+1)}.
\]
This establishes the real-parameter asymptotic conjecture recorded by
Benoit Cloitre in OEIS A082528 and identifies the constants conjectured
numerically for A082527 and A082528. The case $m=1$ recovers the classical
Tchoukaillon asymptotic and is not a new claim.
The proof reverses the rounding process, establishes a deterministic
scaling limit despite a discontinuous ceiling function, and solves the
limit through an explicit Gamma product. We also prove the same limit
for positive weights satisfying $k(w_k/w_{k-1}-1)\to m$, give elementary
finite-product certificates for the constant, and show by an exact block
construction that ordinary regular variation alone does not determine it.
Exact integer computations, including rational exponents, accompany the
proof. Quantitative error terms, the bounded-error conjecture in the
linear case, and more general microscopic rounding schedules remain open
within this report.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Editorial note (7 October 2026, batch 110).}
Since batch~110 the report has three Parts. Part~I is the report described
above. Part~II (\cref{rex:qt:sec:front} onwards) is built from Report~188
of a session bundle, written without sight of this report, on the same pure
power process: it proves the leading law of \cref{rex:thm:main} again by a
second route and adds the remainder
$T_m(K)=L_mK^{m+1}+O_m(K^{(m+1)^2/(m+2)})$, uniform on compact $m$-sets,
with an explicit all-index enclosure, a global minimal-mass profile with a
rate and a law for positive terminal quotients; this answers Research
question~\ref{rex:q:rates}. Part~III (\cref{rex:sch:sec:front} onwards) is
built from the manuscript \emph{Microscopic Schedules in Rounding
Extinction}, which starts from Research question~\ref{rex:q:schedules}: for
nondecreasing weights with bounded scaled increments whose joint empirical
law of increment and activity converges, it proves the extinction law with
an averaged ceiling drift, finite Gamma products, a jump of the constant
from $1/(2\pi)$ to $\pi/8$ under perturbations of the weights smaller
than every inverse power of the index,
a schedule with no limiting constant, the exact range $(0,1/(m+1))$ of the
constants, and recovery of the law from the drift. It answers that question
for this class and the sparse-exception question in its bounded form. The
two Parts continue the two directions named at the end of
\cref{rex:sec:questions}. Their labels carry the prefixes \texttt{rex:qt:}
and \texttt{rex:sch:}; each keeps its source's letters, with a table of
readings. Dated notes in \cref{rex:sec:results,rex:sec:history,rex:sec:constant,rex:sec:certificates,rex:sec:clock,rex:sec:questions}
record what has changed. Parts~II and~III are AI-assisted, unrefereed and
not formalized.
