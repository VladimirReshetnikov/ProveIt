Suggested comment:
The sequence is strictly log-convex. More strongly, its Hankel matrix
(a(i+j))_{i,j>=0} is strictly totally positive of order 5, but not totally
nonnegative of order 6. In fact,
det(a(1+i+j))_{0<=i,j<6} = -26875777009408537346562624.
It is therefore not a Stieltjes moment sequence. The positive result has
an exact rational-polynomial certificate valid for all indices, not only
a finite numerical check.

A sharper truncated statement: a(0),...,a(10) are moments of a positive
measure on [0,infinity), but a(11) cannot extend them. The minimum admissible
replacement for a(11) is
87170364947144797711187234 / 6107572673541.
