A284366 (positions of 1):
With s=(sqrt(21)-1)/2 and C=(6+sqrt(21))/5, the sharp bounds are
(sqrt(21)-9)/5 < n*s-a(n) < C for all n>=1.
The set of subsequential limits of n*s-a(n) is the entire closed interval
[(sqrt(21)-9)/5, C]. Both endpoints are unattained at finite indices.
The conjectured upper bound 2 is false; its first counterexample is
n=2977771, a(n)=5334043. Its error is
(2977771*sqrt(21)-13645857)/2 = 2.00487217332612884794...
The conjectured lower bound -1 is true.
The asymptotic density is (1+sqrt(21))/10, with bounded counting error.

A284365 (positions of 0):
With r=(9+sqrt(21))/6 and C=(6+sqrt(21))/5, the sharp bounds are
-(12+2*sqrt(21))/15 < n*r-a(n) < C for all n>=1.
The set of subsequential limits is the entire corresponding closed interval.
The conjectured upper bound 2 is false; its first counterexample is
n=8933313, a(n)=20222898, with the same error as above.
The conjectured lower bound -2 is true.
The asymptotic density is (9-sqrt(21))/10, with bounded counting error.
For all n>=1: A284365(3*n)=A284366(n)+5*n.
More generally, for h in {1,2,3}:
A284365(3*(n-1)+h)=A284366(n)+5*n-6+2*h.
The zero-rank violations occur exactly at three times the one-rank violations.

A284364 (binary fixed word):
Let O(N)=Sum_{k=1..N} a(k). Then
-(33+3*sqrt(21))/50 < O(N)-(1+sqrt(21))*N/10
  < (27+7*sqrt(21))/50 for every N>=0, with both constants sharp.
In particular the conjectured asymptotic mean is (1+sqrt(21))/10.
The comment defining u(n),v(n) by counting terminology while multiplying
n by the position slopes should instead distinguish counts from positions.

An infinite extremal family for A284366:
n(0)=1, n(1)=13, n(j+2)=15*n(j+1)-9*n(j)-2.
p(0)=1, p(1)=22, p(j+2)=15*p(j+1)-9*p(j)+7.
Then A284366(n(j))=p(j), and, with q=(sqrt(21)-3)/2,
s*n(j)-p(j)=C*(1-q^(2*j+2)). This exceeds 2 for every j>=6.
The generating functions are
Sum_{j>=0} n(j)*z^j = (1-3*z)/((1-z)*(1-15*z+9*z^2)),
Sum_{j>=0} p(j)*z^j = (1+6*z)/((1-z)*(1-15*z+9*z^2)).
