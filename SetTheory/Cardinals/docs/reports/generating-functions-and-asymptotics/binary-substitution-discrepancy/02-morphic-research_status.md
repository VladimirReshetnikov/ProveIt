# Research status and claim boundary

Date: October 1, 2026.

The article is a self-contained AI-assisted research draft. It has not been
peer reviewed or formalized in a proof assistant, and no OEIS edit or GitHub
repository change was submitted.

The main mathematical claims are:

1. the exact four-state extrema and bounded-discrepancy phase transition for
   `0 -> 1`, `1 -> 1 0^a 1^b`;
2. the exact interval attractors and sharp position-error envelopes for
   A284369, A284370, and A284371;
3. constructive extremal subsequences and rational generating functions for
   their indices.

A284368 is already cross-referenced by OEIS to the generalized s-Wythoff pair
A184484--A184485. Its sharp floor formulas therefore have an independent
route and are included as a calibration and a verification of the general
state method, not as the principal novelty.

A limited source search found no proof of the A284369 bounds. This is not an
exhaustive priority claim. Classical spectral connections between primitive
substitutions and discrepancy are credited to the existing literature; the
claimed contribution is the exact legal-prefix state solution for the stated
family and specializations.
