A284368 / A184484 / A184485
For the fixed point of 0->1, 1->1011, let u(n) and v(n) be the positions
of the n-th 0 and n-th 1. Then

  (1+sqrt(13))/3 < n*(5+sqrt(13))/2 - u(n) < (4+sqrt(13))/3,
  (sqrt(13)-5)/3 < n*(sqrt(13)-1)/2 - v(n) < (sqrt(13)-2)/3.

All four constants are sharp but unattained. The densities of 0 and 1 are
(5-sqrt(13))/6 and (1+sqrt(13))/6.

A284369 / A284370 / A284371
For the fixed point of 0->1, 1->1001, let u(n) and v(n) be the positions
of the n-th 0 and n-th 1. Then

  -(3+sqrt(3))/2 < n*(3+sqrt(3))/2 - u(n) < 2+sqrt(3),
  -2 < n*sqrt(3) - v(n) < 1+sqrt(3).

All four constants are sharp but unattained. The densities of 0 and 1 are
1-1/sqrt(3) and 1/sqrt(3).

The standalone A284371 page currently appears to omit "- a(n)" in its
conjectured inequality; the parent A284369 statement gives the intended form.

Proof and exact verification are supplied in the accompanying article.
