# Primary sources and dated status audit

Consulted and status-checked on 20 September 2026.

1. Daniel Tarnu, *On maximal autocorrelations of Rudin–Shapiro sequences*,
   Journal of Approximation Theory 287 (2023), article 105866.
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jat.2023.105866
   Consulted arXiv v2 (20 October 2022):
   https://arxiv.org/html/2202.05897v2
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05897v2
   Section 4 states the uniqueness and normalized-location conjecture and asks
   whether the nearest integer to 2^(m+1)/3 maximizes infinitely often.
   Its numerical small-index table was not treated as an oracle; all values
   reported here were recomputed. The matrix and periodic-correlation
   recurrences are prior work, not claimed as new.

2. Stephen Choi and Daniel Tarnu, *Limiting behavior of Rudin–Shapiro sequence
   autocorrelations*, Transactions of the AMS, Series B 12 (2025), 1101–1129.
   Published 29 August 2025. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/btran/237
   Author-hosted PDF: https://www.sfu.ca/~schoia/paper/rudin-dist.pdf
   Conjecture 1.5 on printed page 1103 reiterates the discrete conjecture.
   That page was inspected as a PDF image as well as parsed text. The paper
   studies a related limit model on a real domain; results for that model
   must not be silently substituted for finite-level discrete maximizers.

3. Daniel J. Katz and Courtney M. van der Linden, *Peak sidelobe level and
   peak crosscorrelation of Golay–Rudin–Shapiro sequences*, IEEE Transactions
   on Information Theory 68(5) (2022), 3455–3473.
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2021.3135564
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.07318
   Establishes the growth scale and the sharp uniform 5*lambda^(m-4) bound.
   The cubic growth rate is not a new result of this report.

These searches located no prior resolution of the discrete maximizer
conjecture. This is a report of a bounded literature search, not proof of
novelty. No claim is made about unpublished or unindexed work. A 2026 preprint
at https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202605.0907 was screened as adjacent
work on heuristic modifications of binary sequences, not a solution of the
fixed Rudin–Shapiro sequence's maximizing-shift problem.

## Scope of quotations and source use

The article supplies its own derivations and results, with citations for the
problem statement and established facts. It does not reproduce external
papers. Bibliographic metadata are included in references.bib. External PDFs,
third-party images and font files are not redistributed.
