# Sources and attribution

## Counting model and result attribution

The objects are rooted accessible complete deterministic transition structures with k individually distinguished input letters and no accepting-state subset, up to state isomorphism. Their binary and ternary counts are OEIS A006689 and A006690. Adding accepting-state choices contributes a factor 2^n; state labeling contributes the factorial factors explained in the article.

The leading equivalent is classical Korshunov, with the Stirling-number reformulation and simplified constant developed by Bassino–Nicaud and Lebensztayn. The coupon interpretation and conditioned-profile arguments are informed by Amri–Chassaing; Chassaing–Flin–Zevio provide a newer leading-order treatment. The article develops a self-contained two-boundary derivation of fixed-order corrections and inversion. It does not claim exhaustive publication priority.

The article and computational supplement were prepared with OpenAI model assistance. The written proof and executable checks, rather than model output alone, provide the basis for evaluating the claims.

## 1978 access and priority limitation

The 1978 survey's bibliographic identity is verified, but its full text was not obtained for direct inspection. HathiTrust lists volume 34 as limited/search-only: https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/000597515

No conclusion that the survey omits higher corrections follows from bibliographic records or later citations. Publication priority remains unresolved pending direct inspection of an authorized readable copy. Korshunov's directly inspected 1976 Theorem 8 already includes every fixed alphabet size at least two, as well as growing-alphabet regimes; its displayed result is a leading equivalent.

Third-party source papers are not redistributed in this package.
