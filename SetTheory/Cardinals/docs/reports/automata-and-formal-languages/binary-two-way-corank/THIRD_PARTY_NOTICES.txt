CHANGES MADE IN THIS PACKAGE

The article replaces the support-based rank accounting by a sharp
repetition-independent corank budget; proves the full p,q amplification with
that budget; changes the numerical recurrence and optimizes its parameters;
uses a sparse transition-table candidate set in the local matching
construction; provides a different two-register compiler; proves the encoded
context quotient; combines these into explicit binary bounds; and adds
support stability, finite audits, provenance, and a research agenda.

The separate complementation section cites and states an external theorem
from the companion OpenAI manuscript. It does not reproduce the companion
paper's full proof or claim the new package formally verifies that theorem.
