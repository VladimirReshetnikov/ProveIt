Scope
-----

most relevant to an algebraic fixed-alphabet research extension. It was not
a proof audit of every result in either repository. Repository reports are
treated as research manuscripts, with their own stated formalization and
review boundaries.

Primary literature and prior construction
-----------------------------------------
Dolinka et al., Enumeration of idempotents in diagram semigroups and algebras,
JCTA 131 (2015), 119-152, arXiv:1408.2021v2. Theorem 5 and Proposition 17
provide the classical component structure; the article supplies its own
connector proof. The exact corank-budget application is not attributed
as a theorem of that paper.

East and Gray, Diagram monoids and Graham-Houghton graphs: Idempotents and
generating sets of ideals, JCTA 146 (2017), 63-128, arXiv:1404.2359.
A targeted inspection did not locate the explicit repeated-word
sum-of-generator-coranks statement. This negative search is not a proof
of novelty.

Kapoutsis, Nondeterminism Is Essential in Small Two-Way Finite Automata with
Few Reversals, Information and Computation 222 (2013), 208-227.
The author's primary PDF was inspected, especially Section 8 on liveness
encoding. The restricted-target results are not conflated with unrestricted
determinization.

Yan, Lower Bounds for Complementation of omega-Automata via the Full Automata
Technique, arXiv:0802.1226. Cited for established alphabet-substitution
methodology, not as a source for the new compiler's exact counts.

TheoremDB R816:
  https://www.theoremdb.org/records/R816/
  "The one-way-liveness bound transfers to a fixed binary alphabet"
  Inspected authored record says the reduction was written and audited
  28 July 2026. It gives 3h four-letter states, a 9h binary decoder, and a
  2s+2-state macro transfer independent of block length.
  It identifies its evidence as self-reported and content as CC0-1.0.
  Its stated quadratic lower bound imports Adeogun and Kapoutsis.
  Alphabet transfer itself is therefore credited as prior work.

Adeogun and Kapoutsis, A Quadratic Lower Bound for 2DFAs against One-Way
Liveness, arXiv:2602.24279v2. Bibliographic details and the quoted theorem
are also recorded by the inspected OpenAI source and TheoremDB record.
The new proofs do not depend on this quadratic lower bound.

Pighizzini, Two-Way Finite Automata: Old and Recent Results, EPTCS90 (2012),
3-20, arXiv:1208.2755. Used as historical context, not as a current status
determination in October 2026.

Limits of the review
--------------------
No claim is made of exhaustive literature coverage, independent human peer
review, priority certification, or new machine-checked formalization.
The new article's theorem ledger distinguishes classical material,
adapted arguments with new estimates, independently proved extensions,
and the one imported algebraic premise.

The source_manifest.json file records SHA256 hashes of the inspected local
source snapshots, so the bibliography and path audit can be compared with
