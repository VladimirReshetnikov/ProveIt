# Source and status record

Checked on 20 September 2026.

## Primary problem source

Charles E. Hughes, *Undecidability of Adjacent Equality for Insertion, Shuffle,
and Crossover Language Operations*, arXiv:2608.27755v1, submitted 27 August 2026.

- Abstract/version: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27755v1
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.27755
- Section 2, page 2: k-insertion and its orientation; empty factors allowed.
- Section 10, pages 7-8: iteration-depth definition and no-gap conjecture.
- Section 11, page 8: general insertion-degree No-Gap Conjecture.
- Section 11, page 9: singleton conjecture and finite representation-erasure problem.

The PDF text was read and pages 8-10 were also inspected as rendered page images.
The available arXiv submission history listed v1. Searches by exact title,
identifier, and the phrases "insertion-degree spectrum" and "No-Gap Conjecture"
did not locate a subsequent resolution. This is not an exhaustive priority check.

The present article does not depend on the source paper's undecidability proofs.
It proves its own mathematical assertions from the stated insertion definition.
No claims are made about the validity of unrelated results in that source.
