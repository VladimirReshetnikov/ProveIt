# Proof dependency ledger

## Main consistency construction

1. Andretta–Dimonte, *The iterability hierarchy above I3*, arXiv:1712.03877v2,
   Section 3: the finite iteration system and amenable-predicate elementarity.
2. Aguilera–Bagaria–Goldberg–Lücke, *Large cardinals beyond HOD*,
   arXiv:2509.10254v1, Proposition 5.1, Definition 5.2, and the proof of
   Theorem 5.4: the fixed-code, well-founded-part, and Prikry localization
   interface. The normal-measure genericity criterion is used in its
   model-relative form, as in that proof.
3. Report Lemma 5.1: the coded localization is unpacked, with an explicit
   argument that every transported parameter from the original small hull
   is fixed by the shift. The ordinal coding uses omega*(beta+1) to avoid
   a collision with the distinguished slot at zero.
4. Report Lemma 4.1: a forcing-name value belongs to a family of at most
   lambda evaluations over finite changes of the *range* of the generic
   sequence. All those generics give the same extension. The family is
   invariant under shifting the sequence to its tail.
5. Report Lemma 3.1: bounded ordinal maps code partial maps on high-rank
   structures. A finite-list tree belongs to the inner model. An external
   branch contradicts any genuine ordinal ranking; hence an internal
   branch exists. Its union is the required local elementary embedding.
6. Report Theorem 5.2: a hypothetical bad name is localized, put into the
   invariant family, and contradicted by the internalized witness.
7. Report Corollary 6.1: rank-bounded witnesses descend to the rank model;
   a countable elementary collapse and a generic produce a transitive set
   model of the final theory.

The Blue–Goldberg July 2026 cover-exacting notes supply the definition,
comparison with the earlier explicit I2 upper bound, and the anticipation
of related cover constructions. They are not cited as an independently
refereed proof of the report's exact I3_wf(0) formulation.

## Definability obstruction

1. Aguilera–Bagaria–Lücke, arXiv:2411.11568v4, Theorem 2.10:
   an exacting lambda is regular in HOD_(V_lambda).
2. Report Theorem 7.1: ordinal coordinate projections of a uniformly
   definable family belong to that hereditary definability model.
   If all projections were bounded, their uniformly defined bounds would
   form a short cofinality contradiction. An unbounded projection has
   order type lambda, yielding the ambient cardinality lower bound.
3. Report Corollaries 7.2 and 7.4: small candidate families and the stated
   small-OD-covering principle are incompatible with exactingness.
4. Report Proposition 8.1: an external increasing countable cofinal set
   has finite intersections with all internally well-orderably small
   test sets, but is not itself in the definability model.

## Explicitly not established

- Equiconsistency of cover exactingness with I3_wf(0) or with ordinary
  exactingness.
- Cover exactingness above a strongly compact cardinal.
- Ordinary exactingness above an extendible cardinal.
- An ultraexacting upgrade of the internally selected embedding.
- Unconditional inconsistency of a standard open large-cardinal axiom.
- Historical priority, specialist review, or formal proof verification.
