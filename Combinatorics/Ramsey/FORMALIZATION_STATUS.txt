New Section 18 infrastructure
  Proofs18IntervalTransfer:
    A modular progression with natural representatives at most n lifts to an
    ordinary progression when 2*n < N. Descending representatives are reversed.
    The interval-image wrapper gives a progression in the original natural set.
  Proofs18AffineTransfer:
    Proper progression indices are injective. Pullback and cyclic embedding
    cardinalities are exact. An index progression transports to a nonconstant
    progression in the old modulus, without a primality assumption there.
    Density in a new modulus M is (length/M) times density on the progression.
  Proofs18UniformInterval:
    Corollary 3.6 now supplies both an integer-interval uniform stopping case
    and a uniform stopping case in a fresh cyclic model of a proper progression.
