    Inline 29 short single-use lemmas as local have blocks
    
    A single-use lemma with a proof of up to six lines is inlined
    (inline_have.py) when its only use is a whole line of the form
    `have h := L args`, `have h : T := L args` or `exact L args`. The use
    becomes the idiomatic
    
        have h : T := by        -- T: the caller's own annotation if it had
          <lemma tactics>       --    one, else the lemma's statement
    
    or, for `exact`, the lemma's tactics spliced in place. Parameters are
    replaced by the arguments, and the lemma is deleted. Each rewrite was
    elaborated and kept only if the module compiled.
    
    Examples: residueChunkFamily_isProper and the residue-chunk length
    bounds (Proofs02Partition), the BSG constant bounds bsg_c_le_one and
    bsg_delta_le_one, fourthMoment_eq_correlationEnergy, the Fejer
    selection helpers, and sq_le_two_tDegT3 in Proofs18FinalStep.
    
    Not inlined:
    - nine whose uses do not elaborate after substitution, for example where
      a hypothesis name is used as a `rw ... at` location;
    - four whose substituted statements would contain applied lambdas or
      run past 100 columns: extraction_product_filter_card_first/second,
      iteratedRealDifference_scaled_full, fejerArrangementVertex_decode;
    - every lemma used inside an expression or a tactic list, and every
      proof longer than six lines.
    
    Long `have` headers were wrapped after the colon; no file gains a line
    over 100 columns.
    
    19 files, +133 / -268 lines.

    older than its source; 0 duplicated names; final-route stub check
    passes with only approved axioms.

    Inline 14 single-use lemmas whose proof is one or two lines
    
    Among the 1,306 single-use declarations on the final route, a lemma
    whose proof has at most two lines costs more in docstring, signature
    and spacing than in proof. inline_short.py replaces its single
    application by the proof itself, with the arguments substituted. Where
    the proof needs its type to elaborate, it uses `show STATEMENT by
    TACTICS`. `by exact t` becomes `t`, and a surrounding pair of
    parentheses is reused instead of nested.

    audit_profile_shift_four, audit_card_cube_le, audit_anchor,

    Delete 89 dead declarations from final-route modules
    
    A declaration counts as dead when (dead_decls.py):
    - no declaration of the Proofs18Final environment uses it, with
      auxiliary constants folded into their owners;
    - its name occurs nowhere else in any .lean file, including modules
      outside the final route, modules without a local olean, and audits;
    - it is not mentioned in any non-Lean file (notes, status files,
      verification records);
    - it is not paper-named, and carries no attribute.
    
    The first scan found 77 such declarations. Git history separated them:
    - 28 orphans: the name once appeared in another module, so a later
      refactor removed its last user (or it is private);
    - 49 standalone declarations, never used outside their own module.
    
    Deleted: the orphans and private declarations, plus the 31 standalone
    helpers of at most 16 lines (monotonicity, positivity and symmetry
    facts, small cardinality and threshold identities). Then repeated
    rescans deleted what those deletions orphaned in turn, six rounds to a
    fixpoint (e.g. the stage-13.5 encodings, the large-class pruning
    helpers, proper_short_product_recurrence and
    proper_product_multilinear_recurrence).
    
    Kept on purpose: 18 standalone results of 18 lines or more that were
    written as results in their own right. Examples: the interval and
    explicit cyclic quadratic dichotomies, the Lemma 13.5 mod-four
    counterexample, the Section 18 cyclic consequence of Theorem 18.1, and
    several Section 16 cover and slice-provider theorems. They are unused
    but are not leftovers.
    
    delete_decls.py removes a declaration with its docstring and any
    `set_option ... in` / `open ... in` prefix, and keeps a module's
    deletions only if the module still compiles. No modifier was left
    attached to a different declaration.
    
    53 files, -1201 lines. Public-theorem counts in inventories built from
    the source go down accordingly.

    olean older than its source; 0 duplicated names; final-route stub check
    passes with only approved axioms.
