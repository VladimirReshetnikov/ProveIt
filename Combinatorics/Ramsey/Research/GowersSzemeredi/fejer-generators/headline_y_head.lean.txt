/-! **Every threshold one lower: the polynomial partition constant charged at
`2^(k+9)`** (Notes L.16p).

The step lemma charges `32·polynomialPartitionConstant (k+2) ≤ 2^(4(k+3)²+5)`
at `X^(2^(4(k+3)²+4))`, through `4(k+3)²+5 ≤ 2^(4(k+3)²+4)`. That costs
`4(k+3)²` per step in the doubly logarithmic scale. Since
`4(k+3)² + 5 ≤ 2^(k+9)` (`four_sq_add_five_le`), `X^(2^(k+9))` suffices, and
a step then costs `k + 19` there instead of `4(k+3)² + 14`. This module
contains the envelope of `Proofs18CoverFejerHeadlineZ` with that charge
(generated by `assemble_y.py`), with closed forms from `k = 15` and
kernel-evaluated envelopes from `k = 5`. It gives Theorem 18.1 at `d + 2` from `d = 15`, `d + 3` from `d = 10` and
`d + 4` from `d = 7`. The Theorem 18.2 envelope does not gain: its inverse-step
threshold raises the refinement constant to the power `polynomialPartitionConstant`
(`inv_thr_le_ex`), so there `4(k+3)²` is a genuine cost. -/
