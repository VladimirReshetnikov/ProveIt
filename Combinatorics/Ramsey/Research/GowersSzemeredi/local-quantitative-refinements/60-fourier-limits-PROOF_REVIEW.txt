FINAL DISPOSITION

No unresolved proof gap was identified in the stated theorems. The results
are asserted within their written hypotheses and scopes. The article leaves
the exact unrestricted odd-order limit, the common-prime optimum, effective
norm convergence, sparse repair, and global Szemeredi improvements open.

CRITICAL STEPS REVIEWED

1. Norm compactness and asymptotic reduction

   - The autocorrelation moment bound follows with exponent 2^(D-1).
   - Distinct conjugate-pair monomials have multiplicity (2m)!; their
     coefficients are nonnegative. Further group relations cannot reduce
     the lower bound.
   - The Fourier fourth-power tails are uniform when D tends to infinity.
     This justifies recovering all mass after constructing the limit.
   - Stabilized marked relations define a countable discrete dual Gamma.
     Oddness excludes order-two elements in Gamma, not in K=dual(Gamma).
   - Positive finite Fourier kernels transfer every fixed-degree norm bound.
     Their limit is bounded by one by the real trigonometric-polynomial
     high-degree lemma. That lemma controls signs before restricting a
     nonnegative square average.
   - Weak-star reconstruction, centering, and finite odd-grid approximation
     preserve the objective. The sup/limit interchange is therefore proved,
     rather than inferred from a fixed-group pointwise limit.
   - The sharp finite-character cotangent bound and Fourier mass cap were
     checked independently.
   - In the joint large-prime-divisor limit, Gamma is torsion-free and K is
     connected. The imported Christ-Iliopoulou inequality has exactly the
     required hypotheses. Layer-cake integration and the subgraph lift give
     the bound 1/3, with all normalizing factors checked.

2. Exact arrangement profile and equality

   - The test includes repeated coordinates, independent labels, and the
     zero increment. Its denominator is |G|^(s-1)|H|^(s+1).
   - The constant Fourier coefficient, the nonconstant coefficient cap,
     Parseval, and the convex chord have compatible nonnegative slack.
   - Interior equality forces a two-valued map, an order-two target value,
     independence from labels, and a subgroup coset support.
   - Modal row integration and the increment cap produce B_s. Equality
     forces exactly an index-two kernel of vertical increments.
   - The affine-spike distance lemma works for all subgroup indices n>=3,
     including even n; the exact nearest-model distance is 1/(2n).
   - The local polynomial is not globally increasing. The coarse decoder
     is explicitly used to activate a specified increasing branch.
   - All-order inverse optimality is sequential Taylor-coefficient
     optimality along a sequence of equality distances. It is not a claim
     of an exact continuous pointwise envelope at every real distance.

3. Odd-target and common-prime refinement

   - Every proper subfamily of the balanced coordinates is independent,
     so the three-query remainder estimate also applies when s=4.
   - In an odd target, opposite values form distinct pairs, giving c+d<=1
     for the conditional nonzero distribution and the improved quadratic
     upper coefficient.
   - Positivity of the profile multiplier, derivative of the cubic branch,
     activation epsilon<=1/(240s^2), and inverse-range coverage were checked.
   - The growing block of half-interval rows has the exact nearest-model
     distance and a remainder uniform in the prime. The odd-prime imbalance
     enters at cubic order when the number of active rows is floor(sqrt p).
   - The displayed bracket concerns the infimum of admissible quadratic
     coefficients. It does not place an upper bound on every valid, possibly
     weaker, coefficient.

4. Two-direction repair

   - Different bilinear parts have distance at least 1/4.
   - After their synchronization, the independent row/column value
     distributions yield the exact robust-intersection modulus J.
   - The orthogonal conditional-variance decomposition has the required
     direction and the global collision factor 2 delta(1-delta).
   - The sum-normalized profile is P_s=2(1-delta)B_s. Its activated inverse
     has the stated coefficients.
   - Sparse rows have exact defect C_s, and C_s-P_s starts at degree s-1.
     This proves optimality only through inverse degree s-2.
   - Mixture testing requires 0<p<1 and the factor min(p,1-p), including
     in the activation threshold.

5. Translated restriction

   - Q=m(L-1)+1 finite shifts force an integer zero-sum condition on
     coefficient vectors; no unjustified modular independence is used.
   - The integer principal line contributes the exact order-sensitive
     leakage sum. Other vectors have a nonzero minor of size <=2L-2.
   - Domain torsion is bounded by the size of the corresponding kernel,
     and nonprincipal vectors are charged their total weight J-aS.
   - Actual tuple survival uses distinct points. The multiplicity-product
     correction is charged only to repeated bad tuples, at cost L^-1-a.
   - The signed objective gives retained count and high respected fraction
     simultaneously. The clean prime threshold makes the necessary torsion
     assumptions automatic.
   - The graph-energy interpolation and the sixteen-term exponents agree
     with the b=|B| normalization.
