RESULT MAPPING

fourier:*
  Gowers Lemma 5.2 and the missing-interval discussion in source 37.
  Solves the unequal-budget problem near every interior common-budget
  point; gives an exact certificate valid wherever its conditions hold.
  The old certificate's entire optimality region is a finite polytope.
  Do not label the local branch as a global solution for every budget.

cthree:*
  Graph additive energy and affine rigidity, source 11 and the recent
  gowers-character-affine-refinements manuscript.
  Keep the elementary abelian 3-target hypothesis explicit. The domain
  may be any finite abelian group unless exponent three is stated.
  The new global thresholds are for uniform four-query graph energy,
  not every homomorphism-testing distribution. The sharp cutoff is 13.

interp:*
  Shared-anchor interpolation, source 39, especially Section 10 and
  the lower-bound/compression questions; Gowers Lemma 16.10.
  Replace sum L_i + E_t by E_t when at least t lists are nonempty.
  Use the separate formula for fewer active lists. The generic lower
  construction is sharp for the stated local interface; it does not
  certify every geometric product-property premise of the full proof.

norm:*
  Symmetric-colouring and polynomial-degree chapters, especially
  sources 10, 14, 22, and 38; construction credited to Shi-Dong.
  No degree cap is needed in the independent norm-block classification.
  Preserve the characteristic p > 2m and field-size/length hypotheses.
  Do not replace "independent norm blocks" by "all polynomials".
  The unrestricted scalar quintic problem remains open.

NUMBERING AND BUILD

All theorem and equation labels are namespaced as above. Bibliography
keys are local to the complete article; rename them on merging if they
conflict with existing keys. The section sources use standard AMS theorem
environments, enumitem, booktabs, graphicx, and the packages in frontmatter.
The figure paths assume this directory is the compilation root.

The full assembled TeX is reproducible from the section files using
code/assemble.py. Its two PDF figures are required by the default build.
Internal draft cutoff13_notes.tex and transient compiler files are not
part of the distribution.

FORMALIZATION

No Lean proof is included. Suggested order:
  (1) interpolation upper bound and generic collision construction;
  (2) exact signed graph expansion and two-color inequality;
  (3) the seven-case finite-field projection table and synchronization;
  (4) norm-ratio reduction and exact irreducibility certificates;
  (5) character estimates conditional on the classical Weil theorem;
  (6) supported moments, exact matrix optimum, and Hessian calculation.

The distinction between the proof, finite certificate, and diagnostic
script should be retained in any merged presentation. Mathematical
novelty claims are relative to the recorded baseline; the research audit
does not establish exhaustive literature priority.
