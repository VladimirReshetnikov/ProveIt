WHAT THE NEW CLAIMS DO AND DO NOT SAY
-----------------------------------

Selection:
  The exact score theorem includes both degree-one diagonal events and the
  survival correction for repeated physical vertices. The fair comparison
  has three levels:
      earlier unweighted one-monomial Fejer exponent: 2s;
      weighted unstratified full-feature comparison: (s-1)/2;
      degree-weighted exponent: E_(k,m) ~ 2s/k.
  The middle bound is proved in this article. For k>=2 the final bound is
  strictly stronger. These are sufficient exponents, not intrinsic lower
  bounds for every conceivable selection method.

Norms:
  The new energy inequality retains additional repeated-entry additive
  quadruples. The cosine identities are credited and reproved. The scalar
  certificate is analytic and uses exact rational comparisons; no numerical
  optimization is needed to prove coefficient 5. Strict inequality for each
  nonzero f does not by itself determine the infimum or classify extremizers.

Partitions:
  The exact threshold D*C(S) belongs to the constructed phase family.
  It yields a lower bound on a universal threshold for arbitrary phases.
  The exponent corollary assumes a guarantee for every sufficiently large R
  and every target 2<=L<=cR^a, with nonnegative tolerance exponents. It does
  not assert the same conclusion for a guarantee restricted to only the
  single target floor(cR^a). The exact obstruction at D*C(S)-1 does not imply
  that every smaller length is bad; the packing formula describes that issue.

  The exact periodic realization may use composite modulus N=R+1. Transfer
  to a prime modulus uses an explicitly larger N>2R with a coefficient
  approximation bound. It does not resolve the prime regime N asymp R.

Kovac-Rogers Theorem 1.1 concerns nonnegative functions and groups without
compact open subgroups, so that strict log-convexity statement excludes
finite groups. It is distinguished from the present signed centered odd
finite-group U4/U2 comparison. This scope check is not a complete priority
search. No contribution in the package is labeled a new global Szemeredi
bound or a resolution of a famous open problem.
