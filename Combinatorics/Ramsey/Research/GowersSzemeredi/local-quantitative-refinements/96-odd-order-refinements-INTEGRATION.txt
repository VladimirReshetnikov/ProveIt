Conditions that must remain explicit
------------------------------------
All affine and arrangement probabilities include repeated inputs.
The affine theorem refers to a specified affine reference.
Affine oddness is a target condition; norm oddness is a domain condition.
Energy sums are unnormalized in the affine proof.
Fourier and Gowers averages are normalized in the norm proof.
Arrangement s is the number of vertical edges; s=16 is eight-arrangements.
The exact arrangement model is a(x)+B(x,y)+ell(y), including arbitrary a.
The erasure theorem assumes a near-full domain and uses ambient distance.
Conditional failure eta, validity lambda, and theta=lambda*eta are distinct.
The weighted affine theorem draws labels independently at occurrences.
The thin-row sharpness assertions concern the explicitly stated limits.
The finite row-profile envelope is optimal for its relaxation, not
claimed attainable by every arrangement-labeling problem.
