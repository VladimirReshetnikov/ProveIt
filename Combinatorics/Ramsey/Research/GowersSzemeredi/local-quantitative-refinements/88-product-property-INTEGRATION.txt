IMPORTANT MATHEMATICAL DISTINCTIONS

- The exponent 8(4^k - 3^k) is for full configurations.
- The stronger exponent 4(4^k - 2^k) is proved for cube pairs.
- Cube-pair weights use the fractional moment E w^(2^(1-k)).
- Repeated physical vertices retain their occurrence multiplicities.
- The two orientation maps are injective separately. Their union is
  not asserted to be injective; AM-GM is what gives the weighted bound.
- The collision denominator is coupled before its lower bound is used.
- The counting theorems have no characteristic restriction.
- The imported finite-field selector has p > 2(L-1), separately from
  its field-cardinality threshold. Large extension fields do not
  automatically have large characteristic.
- Exact local test-count optimality is not optimality of the final
  frequency exponents. The configuration density exponent and leading
  coefficient are separately proved optimal.

FORMAL STATUS
