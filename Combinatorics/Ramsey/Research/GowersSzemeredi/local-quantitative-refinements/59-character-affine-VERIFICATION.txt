SCOPE AND REPORTING LIMITS

1. The main scripts do not numerically test infinite groups, arbitrary real
weights, translated-arc expectation, the lonely-runner transfer, or the full
finite-image ceil(N theta/k)/N rounding theorem. Those assertions rest on their
analytic proofs. The extraction script does test finite-exponent occupied-cell
bounds and exact fibers, which are different stated refinements. The graph
supplement checks finitely many relations in a free abelian group and one
specified integer weight vector per construction; it does not exhaust an
infinite group or all possible weight vectors.
2. The affine script directly checks the final support gains of 3 and 4,
the no-order-three min{2-3t,4-8t} color profile, both local equality
classifications in the open ranges, the original averaged-kernel equality
classification, and both endpoint conclusions in the eligible existing maps.
Coset status is determined by translation and closure under addition and
negation, rather than inferred from the set-energy identity. The new checks
reuse the original suites and do not enlarge the stated map inventory.
3. The internal, separately executed 12,816-map audit has no supplied script in
this package. It is deliberately EXCLUDED from all counts and publication
verification claims in this inventory.
4. The one-point identity tests verify the displayed values, not maximality over
all maps on domains of orders 16-40. Maximality follows from the proved global
recovery theorem.
5. All three programs must be run without Python -O/-OO, since their checks
use assertions. All three explicitly reject optimized execution.

RIESZ EXTENSION: INDEPENDENT ANALYTIC AUDIT

The optional statement is correct for integer ell>=1 and q=|D|>=1. Its constant
is c_ell=2^(-ell)binom(2ell,ell), because

 (1-cos theta)^ell = 2^(-ell)(1-z)^ell(1-z^(-1))^ell, z=exp(i theta),

and the constant coefficient is 2^(-ell)sum_j binom(ell,j)^2. Every product
frequency has the form sum_f a_f f with |a_f|<=ell. Therefore ell-dissociativity
ensures that only the all-zero frequency contributes to Haar integration;
torsion introduces no extra term under this hypothesis. The same holds after
deleting any factor. All products are nonnegative.

Let delta=max_chi min_f ||chi(f)||, attained by compactness. The closed bad
arcs at radius delta cover the dual. On each such arc the associated normalized
factor is at most (1-cos(2pi delta))^ell/c_ell. Integrating the covering
inequality and the remaining normalized products yields
1<=q(1-cos(2pi delta))^ell/c_ell, exactly the claimed lower bound for delta.
The inverse-cosine argument is valid because c_ell<=2^ell and q>=1. No
torsion-free hypothesis or stronger 2ell-dissociativity is needed. Attribution
to Bedert's Riesz-product argument should remain explicit.
