# Report291 exact companion

This standard-library Python companion reproduces bounded, exact arithmetic behind the report. It contains no floating-point calculations, numerical optimization, network calls, installation steps, or runtime reads of research files. It writes no files. Its mathematical checks use explicit exceptions and remain active under `python -O`.

## Run

From the report directory:

```sh
python -I -B -X int_max_str_digits=640 companion/exact_checks.py
python -I -O -B -X int_max_str_digits=640 companion/exact_checks.py
python -I -B -X int_max_str_digits=640 tests/test_companion.py
python -I -O -B -X int_max_str_digits=640 tests/test_companion.py
```

The first two commands print identical deterministic JSON. The CLI accepts no configuration arguments; unknown arguments are rejected before checks run. `--help` prints usage. The tests also execute the companion in normal and optimized isolated subprocesses, compare their output, and check invalid inputs under optimization.

For programmatic use, `run_checks()` returns exact Python objects; `jsonable(run_checks())` converts them to the JSON schema. Integers remain integers, `Fraction` values become rational strings, and dictionaries with non-string keys become arrays of `{key, value}` records. An `Energy` object records ordinary energy, respected energy, and their exact ratio. Schema version: 1; report number: 291.

## What is checked

### Three- and four-point polynomials

Literal cyclic triples, with the fourth vertex forced by addition, reconstruct every coefficient of the multivariate C3 polynomial, its failed part `4abc(a+c)`, the C3 substitution `(1,t,1)`, and the C4 substitution `(1,t,t,1)`. The C4 rational weights give ordinary/respected energies `16416/625` and `10716/625`, hence `47/72`.

Polynomial arithmetic modulo `t^4+4t^2-2` checks the minimizer's stationarity, `(t^2+2)^2=6`, and `t^4+12t^2+6=8(t^2+1)`. A rational bracket locates the positive root between `2/3` and `7/10`. Integer-square comparisons reproduce the strict rational margins used in the paper. The test suite includes zero-weight boundary cases.

### All eight C9 derivative rows

The interval counter treats exceptional edges at `0,k`, for `k=1,2,3,4`, and the two equality classes `ord(w)=2` and `ord(w)>2`. Every full nine-entry histogram row is compared with its stated value. Each row is independently realized by an actual map to C18 or Z and checked using both pair histograms and literal source-additive triples.

The full respected energies are `505,433,449,489` in order two and `393,393,441,489` in higher/infinite order. The order-two spike on `{0,1,3,4,6,7}` gives ordinary energy 162 and respected energy 106.

### Finite quotient lifting

The CLI checks 28 finite cyclic lifts: C3 with kernel orders 1 through 16, and C4 with kernel orders 1 through 12. It enumerates every source-additive triple, partitions the corresponding quadruples into quotient patterns, and checks that each pattern has exactly `|K|^3` lifts. Ordinary and respected weighted energies are then independently checked against the same scaling. The API additionally supports quotient orders 1 through 8, subject to the limits below.

### Staircase and completed index-three identities

A fixed seed, 683744248, generates 256 exact rational staircase weights, including negative indices. Direct weighted graph-pair counting is compared with the three residue-fiber convolution formula. Another 24 eight-point cases use a literal four-loop oracle. Sixteen finite index-three cuts check the carry term on C_(3m).

For every M from 1 through 128, triangular ordered-pair counts give

- `H_M=(2M^3+M)/3`
- `J_M=(2M^3-2M)/3`
- `H_M-J_M=M`

Exact polynomial cross-multiplication verifies both the finite ratio and finite error formulas. It requires no floating-point evaluation of rho. Forty-eight rational finite-staircase examples independently check the energy formula itself.

Finally, 32 finite convolution examples check all three completed attainment identities and their cubic consequence: constant fibers with carry one on C_(3m), and proportional nonconstant fibers with carry zero on C3 x C_m. A separate negative test checks that the third identity excludes `A=C=0,B=1`, which the first two alone permit. These are identity checks only; they do not stand in for the general attainment proof.

## Explicit input and enumeration bounds

Public counting functions validate before enumerating. Booleans, floats, wrong container types, wrong lengths, negative weights, zero total weight, out-of-range coordinates, and oversized exact numbers are rejected with `ValueError`.

- Weight containers are exact-length tuples, or finite coordinate dictionaries where specified
- Individual weights are nonnegative `int` or `Fraction`, with numerator bit length at most 64 and denominator bit length at most 32
- General cyclic energy: source order 1 through 48; integer target values from -4096 through 4096; optional target modulus 1 through 4096; at most 48 squared pairs or 48 cubed forced-fourth triples
- Branch arrays: 1 through 10 entries, equal coefficient/weight lengths, coefficients 0 through 3; source modulus 10 through 18 or `None` for no wrap; at most 10 to the fourth literal quadruples
- Branch order parameter: 2 through 4096 or `None` for infinite order; symbolic slopes must lie in `[-M,M]`; the realization helper permits slopes from -6 through 6
- Histogram input: at most 39 positive entries, wrap index -1 through 1, coefficient -6 through 6, diagonal present, reflection symmetry required; each rational histogram count has numerator and denominator bit lengths at most 4096
- Finite quotient lifts: quotient order 1 through 8, kernel order 1 through 16, total source order at most 48
- Staircase weights: at most 48 supplied dictionary entries, coordinates from -96 through 96; literal quadruple mode permits at most eight positive support points
- Finite index-three cuts: kernel order 1 through 16 and exactly three times that many weights
- Triangle length: 1 through 128, with at most 128 squared ordered pairs
- Polynomial inputs: nonempty ascending coefficient tuples of degree at most 24; signed exact coefficients obey the same bit limits as weights; products exceeding degree 24 are rejected
- Energy objects also validate sign, positivity of the denominator, ordering, and a 16,384-bit bound on each exact numerator and denominator

The raw histogram scorer validates its finite algebraic input format and necessary symmetry. It does not assert that every accepted synthetic histogram is the histogram of a branch or a group map. Internal helpers are not general-purpose public input interfaces.

## What these checks do not prove

This is a reproducible arithmetic companion, not a proof-assistant certificate. Finite sampling does not prove an arbitrary-group theorem, a global real minimization, the sharp complex pointwise inequality, propagation on an infinite cyclic group, Fourier injectivity, or finite nonattainment. Those arguments are in the written report. The quotient-ring equalities alone do not choose the positive real minimizer or establish that it is a global minimum.

Likewise, the finite convolution examples do not classify all endpoint maps, all minimizing weights, or all possible ambient extensions. The companion does not search for a lower universal constant, claim novelty or priority, or claim that every third-class map has a finite witness at or below rho. No numerical search or floating-point tolerance is used as evidence for any of those conclusions.
