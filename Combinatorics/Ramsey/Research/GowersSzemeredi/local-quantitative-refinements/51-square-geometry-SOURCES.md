## Fixed-density complete-square comparison

At [the fixed-density source pin](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/77f7d8c1f7506254c0b2a49ce9f80ffca8d3675c/Combinatorics/Ramsey/Lean/GowersSzemeredi/Proofs13CompleteSquareExtraction.lean#L39-L48), the source already proves complete context-to-square extraction for every fixed exact density 0<alpha<=1 at sufficiently large prime moduli. It retains a subset of original A, density 2^-137 alpha^704, and side N^gamma with gamma=2^-386 alpha^1856 / 2^(13 section13Q(alpha)). The article credits this existing full construction and compares its clean asymptotic power to the local finite-budget bound c^(fg/2) N^(2 gamma)-1. It makes no new formal-check claim for that source.

The stage definitions, explicit budget theorem and grid file were fetched again at that 77f7 pin; decoded bytes exactly matched the frozen primary snapshots. Curated unchanged-pin rows preserve their full commits and hashes without distributing raw retrieval responses. The fixed-density theorem's threshold depends on exact alpha; uniformity over varying actual alpha above a lower bound does not follow solely from its quantifier order. For irrational fixed alpha, exact density is impossible. For rational fixed alpha=a/b in (0,1) in lowest terms, exact density forces b|N^2, permitting no sufficiently large prime N. Hence the pointwise theorem is eventually vacuous for each fixed alpha<1; this is a limitation of that theorem type as a density-uniform extraction statement, not a claim that its proof is false. Neither that source theorem nor this report's re-selection argument supplies a grid for every arbitrary previously retained Stage 13.9 witness.
