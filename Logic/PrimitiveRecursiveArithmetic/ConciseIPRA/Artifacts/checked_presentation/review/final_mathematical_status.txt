MATHEMATICAL STATUS AND THE SUCCESSOR-UNIQUENESS GAP
12 November 2026

The verified result is a smaller implementation of an existing axiomatization.
No genuinely simpler underlying mathematical axiomatization has been proved
here. The existing native calculus is not known to attain an absolute
mathematical lower bound. The observed coordinate minima are properties of
the supplied comparison corpus, not universal lower-bound theorems.

A narrower candidate discussed in the incoming research is successor-only
uniqueness. In ordinary intuitionistic logic with actual equality, scalar
iteration, numerical coverage and separation, retain the rule

    Gamma |- f(S z) = S(f(z))
    ---------------------------
    Gamma |- f(t) = I(t, f(0); u.Su)

with z fresh for Gamma and the fixed parameters, and f a fixed term template.
The completed argument for this candidate recovers contextual induction for
zero equations q(n)=0. It does not establish induction for arbitrary literal
equations s(n)=t(n), and therefore does not yet establish the iPRA bridge.

One central missing obligation is an internally constructed equality detector
Delta with

    Delta(a,b)=0  <->  a=b

for actual equality throughout the numerical domain. Together with the
zero-equation induction result, this would transfer induction to arbitrary
equations. The inherited scalar predecessor and reflection arguments cannot
simply be reused here: their uniqueness applications include updates other
than successor. Doing so would assume the disputed strength.

Additive cancellation and identity-update uniqueness are intermediate issues;
neither has been shown here to close the gap. The available two-fiber
zero-induction countermodel does not settle the candidate because it fails
successor uniqueness itself.

Changing domains may help a future construction, as the user's request allows,
but mutual interpretations alone would not suffice. Both composite
interpretations must have internally definable isomorphisms preserving the
full interpreted structure. A quotient that forgets labels may lose exactly
the data those isomorphisms need. Likewise, finite primitive-recursive
descriptions do not provide a total primitive-recursive universal evaluator.

Successor-only uniqueness is therefore a precisely stated research candidate,
not a completed smaller theory or a claimed solution of the original target.
