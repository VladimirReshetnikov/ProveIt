## Prior art screened

* The original Choice-DI already removes Q from the public formula grammar
  in favor of reusable context clauses. Q-to-clause elimination is not new.
* Restricting crossed transport to one term context, from `s=t` and
  `h(s)=u` to `u=h(t)`, is already in the incoming `one_context` archive.
  Two applications recover two-sided atomic transport.
* Root-determined progress at every atomic matrix requires care: replacing
  conditional D by progressive D on the entire unguarded language changes
  formulas. With an unused witness and `E(y) := (y=1)`, the conditional
  reading is true (`0=1` implies everything), whereas progress is false.
  Existing guarded-core papers already avoid claiming full round trips for
  their entire administrative language.

## Exact context-length invariant

Consider the current incoming `kernel_refinement/code/checker_bytes.py`.
Every call reachable from `c(q())` satisfies

    k = 1 - len(g)

at function entry. Initially `g=[0]` and `k=0`. A recursive major-premise
call receives the unchanged `g,k`. A continuation receives `g+[h']` and
`k-1`. These two cases prove the invariant by induction on the call tree.

Consequently the explicit `k` argument is eliminable by setting
`k = 1 - len(g)` on entry and omitting it from both recursive calls. The
local `k -= 1` before the final two traversals must remain. The program
`scope_candidate/checker_valid.py` does exactly this.

### Universal trace correspondence

At related calls, the goal, context, unconsumed certificate stack and local
integer k are identical. Thus their traversal functions are extensionally
identical. The hypothesis test takes the same branch. Otherwise both consume
the same packet, perform the same elimination test, and recursively verify
the same major premise with related entry states. The recursive result and
remaining stack therefore agree. Both calculate the same h, decrement k,
normalize the same hypothesis and goal under the decremented lower bound,
and make related continuation calls. This is a bisimulation of terminating
executions, including exceptions and rejection, with the standard caveat
that different Python source may have different implementation resource
limits. No mathematical axiom changes.

## Why the tempting 514-byte variant is invalid

Setting `k=-len(g)`, changing `k <= a < d` to `k < a < d`, and deleting
the decrement would match the original pre-step scope. But the old traversal
closes over mutable k. After the decrement it admits the newly generated
eigenname while normalizing an inserted term via `i(t,d=3)`. The naive
variant does not admit its own new eigenname and rejects valid proofs.
Nine of the 21 supplied local fixtures distinguish it from the baseline.
It must not be delivered as an improvement.

The valid two-argument variant agrees with the baseline on all 21 local
fixtures. The universal trace proof, rather than those finite checks, is
the reason it is correct. `scope_candidate/validation.json` records all
results, including the invalid candidate's failures.

## Novelty search limits

Targeted `rg` over the three incoming archive contents, including their
frozen references, found no occurrence of `1 - len(g)`, `-len(g)`, or the
phrases `context length`, `length of the context`, or a scope-length/fresh-
length combination. This is a narrow search, not an exhaustive novelty
claim over the whole historical corpus.

## Result

No new mathematical simplification meeting the lower-envelope size target
was established in this sub-investigation. The state invariant and its
counterexample are useful audit results; presenting the naive candidate as
a smaller axiomatization would be incorrect.
