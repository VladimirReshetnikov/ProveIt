Independent audit of the counter-aware equality-reflection proof

Result: no use of quantifier-free, disjunctive, or quantified induction is
needed before the promised reduction from equation induction. The only
induction instances are literal equations. Coverage provides the case splits.

1. With x fixed, equation induction on y proves
   p(d(Sx,y)) = d(x,y).
   Base: p(d(Sx,0)) = p(Sx) = x = d(x,0).
   Step: p(d(Sx,Sy)) = p(p(d(Sx,y)))
                         = p(d(x,y)) = d(x,Sy).
   The middle equality is congruence applied to the equation hypothesis.
   Hence d(Sx,Sy) = d(x,y) by the defining equation of d.

2. Equation induction on a in d(Sa,a)=1 is valid; it is still one literal
   equation even though a occurs twice. Base is d(1,0)=1. At the step,
   d(S(Sa),Sa)=d(Sa,a)=1 by the cancellation equation from item 1.

3. The remainder invariant is the one equation
   C(sg(d(x,y)), x, y+d(x,y)) = x,
   with x held fixed and y the induction variable.
   Base uses d(x,0)=x, 0+x=x (another equation induction), and
   C(v,x,x)=x. The latter is numerical cases on v, not induction.
   At the step abbreviate r=d(x,y), r'=p(r)=d(x,Sy).
   Use coverage on r': either r'=0 or r'=Sz.
   - If r'=0, sg(r')=0 and the new left side selects x.
   - If r'=Sz, coverage on r gives either r=0 (impossible because
     p(0)=0 and no successor equals zero) or r=Sw. Then w=p(r)=r',
     so r=Sr'. Both sg(r) and sg(r') equal 1. The single old equation
     becomes y+r=x. The new left side is
       Sy+r'=S(y+r')=y+Sr'=y+r=x.
   The two addition identities used here are direct computation in the
   right argument and an earlier equation induction on that argument.
   Every existential witness introduced by coverage is discharged normally.

4. If d(Sa,b)=1, the remainder invariant at (Sa,b) gives b+1=Sa.
   Since b+1=Sb, successor injectivity gives a=b. Conversely a=b uses
   d(Sa,a)=1 from item 2. No order comparison theorem is assumed.

5. K(n)=C(n,1,p(n)) satisfies K(0)=1 and K(Sz)=z. Coverage and separation
   therefore prove K(n)=0 iff n=1. Similarly sg(n)=0 iff n=0, and sg is
   Boolean by coverage. Thus epsilon(a,b)=sg(K(d(Sa,b))) has
   epsilon(a,b)=0 iff a=b and epsilon(a,b)=0 or epsilon(a,b)=1.

6. Structural induction on each finite quantifier-free formula A constructs
   b_A and proves b_A=0 iff A. This is metatheoretic syntax induction,
   not arithmetic induction. The implication case uses only the already
   proved two Boolean alternatives for b_A. Equation induction on
   b_A(n)=0 then yields induction for A(n) under unchanged side assumptions.

Scope limitation: this audits the written inherited arithmetic argument.
It is not a Lean/Rocq verification or an exhaustive checker correctness proof.
