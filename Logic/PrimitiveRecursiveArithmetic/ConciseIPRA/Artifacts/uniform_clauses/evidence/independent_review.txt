Independent review: selected clause checker
===========================================
Source: logic_work/clausal_horn_shared.py
Verdict: PASS (written mathematical and source audit).

The selected list-premise format repairs the nullable prototype's ignored
payload. A zero-premise clause requires []; a one-premise clause requires
[p]. Every supplied proof in that list is recursively checked, and the
resulting formula list must equal the retained antecedent list exactly.
There is no ignored proof slot.

The shared syntax validator correctly distinguishes terms from formulas:
Eq has two term children; D has one formula child and binds two variables;
an iterator has three term children and binds only its third child;
successor has two children with literal-zero head. Exact integer typing
excludes booleans. Outer goals and witnesses are validated before branch
scopes grow. Thus fresh parameters cannot escape DE or DI.

Opening is capture avoiding: every inserted term is checked at binder
depth zero, so it has no positive free indices; negative parameter levels
remain rigid under binders. The inferred-formula/context invariant ensures
that Eval's length-two nonzero counter is a genuine successor, while an
iterator counter (length three) is rejected.

A clause ([]; y.B) reads forall y B. A clause ([A]; y.B) reads
A -> forall y B, with y absent from A. The initial clause is ([]; y=y).
DI's local assumption is a zero-premise clause whose binder is unused.
DE's added clause is ([B(b,0)]; y.B(b,y)), with b fresh.

The theorem relation is unchanged from raw CE5. Translate Refl(t) into
Use of the initial clause at t; translate neutral local assumptions into
zero-premise clauses and omit their former 0=0 proofs. In reverse, initial
clause uses become Refl, and local zero-premise uses receive Refl(0) as
their CE5 neutral premise. DE, DI witnesses, terms and goals are preserved.
The removed identity-Eval case follows from two zero-Eval steps through
J=I(0,s;u.u): s=r and J=J give J=r; s=t and J=r give r=t.

No substitution into whole derivations is required. In particular, the
witness-sensitive DI test must not be called directly substitution-stable:
a parameter witness can become literal zero and change its mode.
Local proof translations retain each witness literally.

Counting: FOUR instruction families (Use, Eval, DE, DI), plus ONE fixed
initial reflexivity axiom. Splitting Eval into its two cases gives FIVE
inference schemata, plus that axiom. Reflexivity has not been removed.

Terms and Eq/D formulas are unchanged, so the established CE5/native/iPRA
bridge transfers with the whole numerical domain, actual equality and
identity numerical composites. This audit is not a Lean/Rocq verification,
an external human review, or evidence of an absolute minimum.
