VALIDATION CONTRACTS MUST REMAIN SEPARATE
All sub-500-byte checkers assume canonical closed guarded Eq/D public goals
and exact integer/list certificate data. They do NOT independently validate
the complete public grammar or the whole input alphabet. Finite scoped
substitution is checked; malformed administrative raw aliases can be
accepted. They implement the inherited scalar iterator calculus, not the
constant-free or guarded-register theories.

PRIOR CONTRIBUTIONS TO AVOID REPEATING
- Three-field packets already infer and reuse elimination source/major.
- Context-first variadic arguments and neutral truth resources already exist.
- Successful recursive return encoded by a nonempty comprehension already
  produces the 487-byte module.
- Deterministic newest-parameter introduction and deterministic selected
  clause use/opening have existing proofs/packages.
- Explicit simultaneous ordinary bi-interpretations and identity numerical
  graphs are inherited requirements, not new results.
- Constant-free J syntax has no constant, successor, or initializer but its
  strict reference checker is much larger (3072/3349/13723).
- Guarded register syntax has one nonnullary constructor with a formation
  restriction but its strict checker is 1041/1181/3467.
- Single-binder universal macro D_{u,z} G_z A(P(z)) is already in incoming;
  it reduces translated quantifier rank and expansion but not trusted rules.
- Deleting the initial scope scan has a concrete false-goal certificate
  (exists x, x=S(x)); this is not a legitimate 250-token improvement.
- The 18-token/19-node/459-byte zlib/base64 executable illustration is already
  explicitly discounted: identically packed incumbent ties it and expanded
  payload/runtimes must be accounted for.

CAUTION ON THE WORD LOWER BOUND
The recorded minima are empirical source-size upper bounds on the unknown
optimum. They are not proved mathematical lower bounds for all possible
presentations. Existing claim files explicitly say so. A construction can
beat the observed envelope; it cannot simultaneously be below a genuine
lower-bound theorem within its hypotheses.

AUDIT LIMIT
No independent full rerun of all historical Lean metatheorems was attempted.
Archive written proofs and advertised control equivalences are not silently
upgraded to machine-verified results. The measured best sources were
inspected, but this audit itself does not prove their entire soundness.
