Closest existing constructions
------------------------------

The general normalizer framework in ch04_comp.tex assumes substitution
coherence as condition (nu4). Its rule templates and bodies are normal
syntax. Atomic-initializer normalization violates that condition; retaining
raw metadata and normalizing after each raw instantiation is therefore an
actual difference, not an instance justified by that framework alone.

The zero_normal and initializer_equality constructions contract every
zero-counter iterator, with arbitrary initializer. The latter's new equality
rule replaces an initializer and reconstructs arbitrary congruence through
one iteration. Neither examined description uses atomic-only contraction
or the raw-QE derivation of general zero computation.

canonical_recursors builds full zero computation into canonical syntax for
a successor-seeded R constructor. bounded_initializer studies tied-seed
recursors, bounded initialization, and an internal compiler; it is a different
arithmetic construction.
