The three following newly filed packages were inspected through their
README and full standalone LaTeX report, including the exact primitive
rule specification. Local authenticated copies are in baseline/late_audit/.

1. parameter_substitution
   Source: ipra_parameter_substitution.tex, sections 2 and 3.
   Terms are raw scalar iterator terms; no computation normalization.
   Compute contains both zero and successor computation axiom schemas.
   Param infers r = psi(s,t,u) from s=t and u=r. If s is a free numerical
   variable, psi substitutes t for that variable in u. Otherwise psi
   either performs common-root joining (u literally s) or reverses the
   minor equation. Fresh aliases derive arbitrary template transport.
   No SeedEval instance is a primitive rule here. No initializer-evaluation
   fusion matching the proposed rule was found in the inspected report.

2. variable_substitution
   Source: report.tex, sections 2 and 3.
   Both scalar computation equations are fully normalized in the syntax.
   The changed arithmetic rule is VSub:
       x=t, u=v  ==>  v=N(u[t/x]),
   with x a free numerical variable, further restricted to the newest
   parameter in the selected presentation. The package proves recovery
   of general transport by a two-row existential naming construction.
   It does not use zero-only SeedEval or a computation/initializer fusion.

3. deterministic_specialization
   Source: report.tex, section 2.
   It retains exactly the current zero-normal Refl plus Step arithmetic:
       K(c,a,h)=t, I(S c,a,h)=r  ==>  r=N(h[t/u]).
   The changed rule is clause Use: the newest clause specializes at 1,
   older clauses at the newest eigenparameter. Its proof handles the
   associated failure of naive weakening with persistent procedures.
   No SeedEval replacement is present.

Novelty boundary

These three packages do not duplicate the proposed new arithmetic rule
       a=b, I(S c,a,h)=r  ==>  r=N(h[K(c,b,h)/u]).
This is a scoped repository comparison, not a literature-wide priority
claim. initializer_equality remains the nearest earlier arithmetic source:
its old Seed is replacement in the initializer, with successor computation
retained separately. The new rule should be called SeedEval or initializer
evaluation to avoid confusing those two rules.

deterministic_specialization already states the two-Step congruence proof
that reuses the first computational equality as the next minor premise.
Consequently the four-zero-step crossed transport macro must not itself
be claimed as a new discovery relative to this updated snapshot. It is
useful in the new SeedEval proof, and shorter than the particular expanded
NativeZeroStep.trOneNf macro, but the idea has a newly filed predecessor.

Compatibility observation

The SeedEval/Step translations use only Refl and arithmetic nodes within
the existing context and numerical scope. They neither add clauses nor
open eigenparameters. Thus the same equivalence works after deterministic
Use is adopted; it does not assume weakening of its restricted proofs.
This is a direct consequence of the stated arithmetic-fragment theorem.

The other late packages are being inspected separately by the logical
simplification agent. No claims about those packages are made in this
three-package audit.
