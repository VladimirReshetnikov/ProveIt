2. EXACT GUARDED CALCULUS

Primary concise source: docs/Article/chapters/ch11_cert.tex,
cert:def:U, cert:def:Aminus, cert:rem:imported.
Primary proof source: docs/Article/chapters/ch03_guard.tex,
guard:thm:aux, guard:thm:public, guard:cor:biinterp.

Terms: x | 0 | S(t) | I(n,a;u.h), binding u in h only.
Native formulas: s=t | Q_y B | D_xy B.
Ordinary reading: Q_y B = B(0) -> forall y B(y),
D_xy B = exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y)).

Public formulas contain Eq and D only. An atomic matrix immediately under D
must have the literal shape I(y,0;u.h)=I(y,0;u.k). Compound public matrices
are otherwise unrestricted recursively. Internal derivations may use ALL
native formulas, including Q and unguarded atomic D matrices.

Six primitive rule families:
Hyp: A in Gamma => Gamma |- A.
I0: Gamma |- I(0,a;u.h)=a.
IS: Gamma |- I(Sn,a;u.h)=h[I(n,a;u.h)/u].
SE: Gamma |- M(s,t;F), Gamma |- F(s) => Gamma |- F(t),
where M=Q_v F(v) when s is literally 0 and M=(s=t) otherwise.
DE: Gamma |- D_xy B, Gamma,Q_y B(b,y) |- A => Gamma |- A,
where b is fresh for Gamma, A and D_xy B.
DI: Gamma,B(t,eta_B(z)) |- B(t,Sz) => Gamma |- D_xy B,
where z is fresh for Gamma,t,D_xy B and eta_B(z)=z at literal equations,
eta_B(z)=0 at compound matrices.

CRITICAL CAVEATS:
- The root-selected introduction is weaker than unrestricted native case
  introduction on all auxiliary formulas. The theorem is for public sequents.
- The stronger internal soundness invariant uses mixed/auxiliary reading
  exists x forall y (E(x,y)->E(x,Sy)); Q retains conditional-closure reading.
  Its ordinary reading agrees on public guarded formulas because their zero
  instances are theorems. Ordinary reading is ALSO sound for all auxiliaries,
  since this calculus is a subcalculus of the native one. What must not be
  claimed is ordinary-reading completeness or both interpretation round trips
  for every unguarded auxiliary D.
- Restricting all internal formulas to the guarded grammar while retaining
  source-selected transport loses equality completeness (0=t need not give
  t=0). Auxiliary unguarded progress matrices repair this.
- Numerical substitution may turn a nonzero literal source into literal 0;
  rule-by-rule substitution of derivations cannot be assumed. Checker soundness
  uses the ordinary theory T_eq, where contextual substitution is admissible.
- Mode selection must not enable progress for compound formulas: that would
  yield unrestricted induction, beyond iPRA's quantifier-free induction.

3. PUBLIC TRANSLATION AND BI-INTERPRETATION

Let C(v,a,b)=I(v,a;u.b) with u not in FV(b).
Let gamma_v(s=t) be C(v,0,s)=C(v,0,t).
Let L(E)=D_av gamma_v E with a,v fresh.
Let P(E)=L(E) for an equation; P(B)=B for compound B.
Define T(E)=E, T(Q_y B)=D_ay P(TB) with a unused, and
T(D_xy B)=D_xy P(TB). Every T(A) is public.
The native calculus derives A <-> T(A); the guarded-source calculus derives
B <-> T(B) for public B. Public consequence relations agree. The maps on
terms are identity; both formula composites are internally derivable.

Compose this with the native scalar bridge in ch01_base.tex,
base:thm:bridge, to obtain full first-order iPRA bi-interpretability.
The numerical domains are whole, equality is actual numerical equality,
and both numerical comparison graphs are x=y. This is stronger than merely
mutual consistency or mutual derivability of one contradiction.

4. ORDINARY REALIZATION T_eq AND LOGICAL MACROS

T_eq is intuitionistic first-order equality logic over the scalar terms with:
both computation equations; coverage x=0 or exists y x=Sy;
separation 0=S0 -> false; induction for literal equations, allowing arbitrary
side assumptions. Native derivability and T_eq ordinary readings correspond.
Predecessor, equality reflection, quantifier-free induction, pairing and the
compiler for all primitive recursive functions are DERIVED, not extra axioms.
In the opposite direction each fixed iterator body is primitive recursive;
there is no universal evaluation function for arbitrary PR codes in iPRA.
See ch01_base.tex base:def:teq, base:sec:arith, base:def:tau,
base:prop:sigma, base:prop:termrt, base:thm:bridge.

Set Z(c)=C(c,1,0), N(n)=I(n,0;u.Z(u)).
Native connectives (ch01_base.tex base:def:macros):
bottom_* := 0=S0;
A =>_* B := Q_y Sel(y,A,B);
A and_* B := Q_y gate(y,Sel(N(y),A,B));
exists_* x A := D_xy gate(y,A);
A or_* B := exists_* x Sel(x,A,B);
forall_* x A := A(0) and_* Q_x A.

Formula selector Sel is not simply a pointwise term operation at mismatched
binders. The full recursion is base:def:gate:
Sel(v,s=t,r=w):=C(v,s,r)=C(v,t,w).
Sel(v,s=t,Q_y B):=Q_y Sel(v,C(y,0,s)=C(y,0,t),B).
Sel(v,s=t,D_xy B):=D_xy Sel(v,C(y,0,s)=C(y,0,t),B).
gate(v,B):=Sel(v,0=0,B).
Sel(v,Q_y A,B):=Q_y Sel(v,A,gate(y,B)).
Sel(v,D_xy A,B):=D_xy Sel(v,A,gate(y,B)).
Bound variables are renamed fresh for v and the other operand.
The resulting native macros must then be translated by T for guarded public
goals. Formula composites follow from the displayed selector/gate laws and
the term round trips, not merely from external equality of theorem sets.
