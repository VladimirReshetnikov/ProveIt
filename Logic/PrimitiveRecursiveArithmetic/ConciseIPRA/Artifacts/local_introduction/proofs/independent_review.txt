VERDICT
The relative compilation argument is mathematically defensible as a written
proof. I found no fatal circularity, forbidden weakening, or eigenparameter
escape. It establishes removal of Close's witness argument from the raw
Alias/Back calculus AFTER changing its mode selector to the inherited diagonal
matrix test. It does not establish a reduction below the current compact
248/270/479 frontier, and is not a new Lean/Rocq formalization.

PRECISE ASSUMPTIONS NEEDED
1. Back is allowed on arbitrary Eq/D formulas, including compound formulas.
2. Alias exports F[t/b], not merely a b-free F. Its b is genuinely fresh and
   defining term t belongs to the outer scope.
3. The Close mode is tested on the RAW MATRIX before witness substitution:
   progress exactly at I(y,y;u.h)=I(y,y;v.k), cases otherwise.
4. Old parameters remain available when scopes extend. Newest is a choice
   of available term, not an erasure of older parameters.
5. Realizers are effective finite-proof construction procedures and are
   quantified over all relevant future context/parameter extensions.
6. All renamings lift under term/formula binders, and all substitutions are
   capture-avoiding simultaneous single substitutions.

PERSISTENT FAMILY FORMULATION
A world w=(Pi,Gamma) consists of an ordered scope and clause list. An
extension w' >= w retains all old data and appends fresh parameters and
well-scoped clauses. Fam_w(A) is an effective procedure which, for each
w' >= w, constructs a finite target derivation of A at w'. Source clauses
have persistent realizers: for each w' >= w and target term r well-scoped
there, a conditional clause takes Fam_w'(antecedent) to Fam_w'(body[r/y]);
an antecedent-free clause takes no family argument. This is metatheory only.
No proof family, oracle, or evaluator is an additional target inference.

ACTUAL CLAUSE REALIZERS
An actual retained clause is persistently realizable. At any extension,
open a fresh alias c=r, obtain its antecedent from the supplied family in
that enlarged context, Use the retained clause at newest c, and export via
Alias. Ordinary assumptions have a body independent of the clause variable,
so their persistent use is immediate and ignores the supplied numerical r.

EQUALITY FRAGMENT
The displayed Alias/Back constructions for reflexivity, symmetry, and full
transport check exactly. Every Back source is its fresh local alias c and
therefore newest at that point; any nested proof of a premise returns to
that same scope before Back occurs. The distinguished variable in H is a
fresh template variable, so repeated holes and binder occurrences are safe.
These operations do not require computation, induction, normalization,
coverage, weakening of a completed restricted proof, or a target substitution
metatheorem. Terms s,t may mention older parameters; Back itself also allows
self-containing t because the substitution is simultaneous once.

CRITICAL CLOSE STEP
For source witness t, introduce target alias b=theta(t), then invoke target
Close with newest witness b. The branch introduces z', which is newer than b.
The actual hypothesis is theta(B)(b,eta(z')). Equality operations on families
give theta(B)(theta(t),eta(z')); use this as the realizer of the source's new
ordinary assumption. Compile the proper source premise p to obtain
theta(B)(theta(t),S(z')), transport back to theta(B)(b,S(z')), close, and
discharge alias b. The exported D(theta(B)) contains no b because b was
fresh and not in the image of theta. The fact z' is newer than b is harmless:
each transport introduces its own fresh newest auxiliary alias.

TERMINATION / NONCIRCULARITY
The new assumption realizer in Close uses only the actual added hypothesis
and actual alias equality, never the compiled result of p. Thus it does not
call itself through p. Source Use may request a recursively compiled
antecedent family, but that is a proper source subderivation. Alias/Open
likewise recurse only on proper source branches. Equality wrappers invoke
premise families finitely often. Structural induction on the finite source
proof therefore gives total effective proof construction; no separate
well-founded recursion on target proof size is needed. Arbitrary persistent
realizers in the generalized theorem must themselves satisfy this effective
totality condition, as the actual constructed ones do.

RENAMING / SCOPE
Injective renaming fixes the two designated matrix binders, so it preserves
and reflects the raw diagonal shape. Open conclusions remain free of the
newly opened parameter by the source side condition. At Alias, translating
the exported substitution commutes with the injective parameter map.
The proof never claims source-sensitive instructions can simply be copied
under added numerical parameters.

BRIDGE TO KPrf NEEDS EXPLICIT ADAPTERS
NativeClausalDiagonal.lean does not use literally the same rule list:
KPrf has Refl, two Eval cases, Use, Elim, Intro, and every clause has one
antecedent. Explain these adapters rather than saying all other rules match.
* Antecedent-free [;y.B] corresponds to [0=0;y.B]. Truth 0=0 follows from
  Alias-derived reflexivity; arbitrary assumptions are interchanged similarly.
* The raw alias calculus derives Refl and full transport. Its computation
  equations plus equality derive the two Eval cases by equation composition.
* KPrf derives the raw computation equations and ordinary equality; its
  existing soundness/completeness bridge validates Alias and Back.
* Cite kprf_iff_nprf with NFm.unQ for arbitrary native formulas, and
  kprf_iff_nprf_of_qfree for already-Q-free Eq/D sequents. The latter alone
  should not be advertised as an assertion about all unmodified Eq/Q/D syntax.
These adapters are routine, but should be stated. Full formula round trips
are then inherited under the identity on raw terms/formulas plus the existing
macro translations. Whole-domain actual equality is preserved.

FAILED SHORTCUTS / LIMITATIONS
Keeping witness-tested eta (progress iff equation and literal-zero witness)
while replacing witness by fresh alias b changes the mode and invalidates
this proof. The diagonal guard is essential to the stated construction.
Restricting Back to equations is not established here: transport of the
compound Close assumption and conclusion currently uses arbitrary formulas.
The mathematics simplifies a rule interface; the complete checker can grow
because the diagonal test has a cost. Neither raw candidate equivalence nor
byte savings may be presented as already formally verified.

NOVELTY SCOPE
The current incoming AliasBack package explicitly leaves its arbitrary Close
witness in place and lists its removal as an open question. The diagonal
calculus, newest-witness proposals, and AliasBack equality are inherited.
The relative alias-based compiler is a new combination/proof relative to this
inspected corpus, not a literature-wide originality or absolute-minimality claim.
