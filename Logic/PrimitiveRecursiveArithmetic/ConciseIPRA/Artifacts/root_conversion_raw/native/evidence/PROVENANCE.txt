controls/scalar_bh3_checker.py is a byte-for-byte copy of code/bounded_checker.py
from that package. The new root_checker.py retains its tagged-tree grammar
architecture, variable substitution, logical rules, validation and CLI.
Changes: the recursor body binds counter and state, root contraction opens
both, and reversible conversion has no address parameter or traversal.

The named producer follows bounded_head/code/build_certificates.py, with
counter-aware named terms and a new three-row packet. Its symmetry lemma
uses a fresh reflexive parameter and does not need the earlier zero-alias
construction. The original parity-based packet is absent.

All source hashes and exact measured sizes are in verification.json.
The checker has no runtime dependency on the upstream code or repository.
