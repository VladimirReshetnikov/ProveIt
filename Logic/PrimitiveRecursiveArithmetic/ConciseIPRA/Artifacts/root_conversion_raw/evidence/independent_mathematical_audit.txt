Independent audit of the root-only conversion construction
=========================================================

Status: the construction below has a complete written open-sequent equivalence
argument. It is a proposed new mathematical reduction relative to the inspected
ConciseIPRA snapshot, not a claimed checker-size record. No machine-checked Lean
proof has been supplied by this audit.

TERMS

Raw terms: variables, 0, S(t), R(n,a;i,u.h), with i and u bound only in h.
The two one-step root reductions are

  R(0,a;i,u.h) -> a
  R(S n,a;i,u.h) -> h[n/i, R(n,a;i,u.h)/u].

The substitutions on the right are simultaneous and capture-avoiding. Neither
the counter nor a proper subterm is normalized. Reversible conversion is a
proof rule for the left endpoint of a literal equation, in either direction,
using exactly one displayed root step.

FORMULAS AND RULES

Formulas are s=t, Q_y B, D_{x,y} B, with their usual conditional-closure and
existential-closure readings. Retain Hyp, Refl, QE and DE. Add VSub:

  Gamma |- b=t      Gamma |- u=v
  --------------------------------  b a free numerical variable
  Gamma |- v = u[t/b].

WI is the existing single introduction: from Gamma,B(w,eta) |- B(w,S z)
infer Gamma |- D_{x,y} B, with z fresh and eta=z exactly when B is literally
an equation and w is literally 0; otherwise eta=0.

Together with root conversion, these are seven rule families. Root conversion
has zero/successor and forward/backward cases. Those cases must be charged in
any implementation comparison; counting seven families is not a size proof.

DERIVED SYMMETRY (NO TRANSPORT USED)

1. D_{x,y}(0=0) is a theorem by WI at witness 0: its progressive branch proves
   0=0 under the identical hypothesis (or by Refl).
2. To reverse u=v, eliminate this dummy theorem at a fresh b.
3. Refl yields b=b in the extended context. VSub on this major and u=v yields
   v=u[b/b]=u.
4. DE exports v=u since b was fresh for u,v and Gamma.

Consequently reversible root conversion is available at either endpoint:
reverse, convert the left endpoint, reverse back. No conversion below a term
constructor or under a logical binder has been used.

DERIVED EQUATION CASES (NO TRANSPORT USED)

If Gamma proves E(0) and Gamma proves E(S z), with z fresh, then Gamma proves
E(t). Apply WI to D_{x,y} E(y), x unused, at witness 1=S0. This selects cases,
even for an atomic E. Its extra assumption E(0) can be ignored. Eliminate D
and use its Q-closure with the known E(0), then specialize at t by QE.

THREE ROW PACKET

Define

  C(v,a,b) = R(v,a;i,u.b),
  T(y;a,b,c) = R(y,a;i,u.C(i,b,c)).

The parameters a,b,c do not contain the bound i,u. At root only:

  T(0;a,b,c) -> a,
  T(1;a,b,c) -> C(0,b,c) -> b,
  T(2;a,b,c) -> C(1,b,c) -> c,
  T(S z;a,b,c) -> C(z,b,c).

Given Gamma |- h(s)=r and Gamma |- s=t, form

  P(x,y) := T(y;0,h(x),x) = T(y;0,r,t).

To introduce D P with witness s, its successor instance is root-convertible
to C(z,h(s),s)=C(z,r,t). Its zero instance follows from h(s)=r, and its
successor instance from s=t, by root conversion at the two endpoints. Derived
equation cases proves it for arbitrary z. This proof uses only Gamma, so it
can ignore whichever assumption WI supplies. In particular the case s=0,
which selects the progressive mode, needs no special alias or congruence.

Open D P at a fresh b. The base P(b,0) follows by Refl 0=0 and reverse root
conversion. QE at 1 and 2 and root conversion give respectively h(b)=r and
b=t. VSub yields r=h(t), independent of b, and DE exports it. This is exactly
the unrestricted one-template crossed transport rule.

RECOVERY OF THE FULL RAW R KERNEL

Computation equations follow by Refl and root conversion. The preceding
packet derives unrestricted transport. WI at witness 0 with an unused
existential variable, followed by DE, derives QI by equation progress.

WI already gives DI by cases except when its matrix is a literal equation
and its selected witness is literally 0. For that case use the nonliteral
alias w=R(0,0;i,u.0), which has w=0 by root computation. The recovered
transport moves both endpoints of each equation between witness w and
witness 0, so the assumed case derivation at 0 gives the required case
derivation at w. Apply WI at w. The resulting D formula is the desired
original D formula; w is only the chosen witness, not part of its matrix.

Weakening, injective renaming and cut are straightforward inductions on proof
trees. The above argument needs these, but does not assume a direct arbitrary
term-substitution theorem for WI, whose literal witness test is not stable
under substitution.

Conversely, root conversion and VSub are sound in the full R kernel by its
computation equations and equality transport, and WI is one of its case or
equation-induction instances. Thus the calculi agree on all open sequents.

IPRA BRIDGE

The full counter-aware R kernel contains the scalar kernel by translating
I(n,a;u.h) to R(n,a;i,u.h) with i unused. Alternatively the usual native
logical macros can be derived directly. Each fixed R term denotes a fixed
primitive recursive function in iPRA; conversely every PR definition compiles
compositionally to R. Equation induction proves uniqueness of each fixed
recursion in both theories and hence both term round trips. Formula induction
and the existing native logic/readback equivalence give formula round trips.
The numerical domain is whole and the comparison graph is x=y.

NOVELTY BOUNDARY

The snapshot already has raw scalar variable substitution with weak-head
conversion, including a color obstruction without conversion; full-normal
counter-aware presentations; and naming packets with three rows. What is new
here is the combination of counter-aware recursion with the explicit T packet
whose rows are exposed by *root-only* conversion. No search for a redex inside
a counter and no general term normalizer are needed. This is a precise
mathematical claim; it does not imply the new standalone checker will beat
the empirical compact envelope.

ANCILLARY CU RESULT

Over scalar computation, cases and the constancy rule CU, suppose Gamma proves
q(0)=0 and Gamma,q(x)=0 proves q(Sx)=0. Let

  h(u)=C(q(u),S u,u),   r(n)=I(n,0;u.h(u)).

Cases on q(u) give q(h(u))=q(u): the zero branch uses the given step and the
successor branch has h(u)=u. Therefore q(r(Sn))=q(r(n)), so CU yields q(r(n))=0.
Consequently r(0)=0 and r(Sn)=S r(n). This gives a successor-preserving copy
whose image lies in the desired set, but it does NOT prove that the copy is
the identity or surjective. Claiming full induction from this would leave a
real gap unless successor rigidity is established independently.

GENERAL FINITE TABLE LEMMA

For a fixed finite list of terms a_0,...,a_k, define a term template in y by

  T_0(y;a_0) = a_0,
  T_{k+1}(y;a_0,...,a_{k+1})
    = R(y,a_0;i,u.T_k(i;a_1,...,a_{k+1})).

At each constructor choose i,u fresh for all entries a_j and other enclosing
binders. The register u is unused. For 0 <= j <= k, T_k(S^j 0;entries)
reduces to a_j by root steps only. More strongly,

  T_k(S^k z;a_0,...,a_k) ->* a_k

for an arbitrary raw term z, also by root steps only. Proof is metatheoretic
induction on k: the zero rule exposes a_0, and a successor root step exposes
T_{k-1}(z;a_1,...,a_k). It uses no object-level induction, equality transport,
recursor-body extensionality, or normalization theorem. For j<k the reduction
has j successor root steps and one zero root step; at j=k it has k successor
root steps. The three-row packet is T_2.

The T notation in the packet above is exactly T_2; C is T_1. The finite-table
lemma gives arbitrary fixed finite observations, not a universal evaluator
for a coded table and not an assertion that a variable-length table can be
processed with a fixed normalization bound.

INDEPENDENT CHECKER REVIEW

Reviewed research/root_conversion/code/root_checker.py and the named producer
build_certificates.py. No concrete mathematical mismatch was found. In step,
the state occupies de Bruijn index 0 and the prior counter occupies index 1.
Replacing index 1 by shift(predecessor), dropping that binder, then replacing
index 0 by the prior recursive value is a correct simultaneous substitution:
the first lift protects the counter replacement while the state binder still
exists, and the second substitution removes that lift without recursively
substituting into an inserted replacement.

One targeted independent calculation used a body containing a nested recursor,
both outer bound variables, and older free parameters; the computed root step
matched the manually specified simultaneous substitution and both sides passed
raw scope validation. This supplements, rather than repeats, the existing
29-fixture / 58-process validation run.

The validator checks every active raw hint before conversion/substitution can
erase it. DE handles freshness by adding a new outer de Bruijn variable and
shifting the target and old context; no fresh variable can leak into the
export. WI at literal-zero witness uses its unsubstituted matrix as the
progress hypothesis. RC invokes step solely at the whole left endpoint, so
there is no implicit recursion into a counter, seed, state body, or successor
child. These are code-review conclusions, not a proof of implementation
correctness in a proof assistant.
