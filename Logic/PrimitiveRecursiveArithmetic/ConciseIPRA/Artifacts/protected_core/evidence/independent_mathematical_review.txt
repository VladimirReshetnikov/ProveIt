INDEPENDENT REVIEW: PROTECTED ATOMS, GUARDED INTRODUCTION, AND SOURCE DISPATCH
Reviewer: arithmetic_reduction subagent
Comparison snapshot: 1517d9d79ce33f631e0f82960c849427cbba23bf

STATUS
The composition is mathematically viable with a protected PUBLIC formula
language. It should not be claimed to preserve the consequence relation of
all unprotected administrative formulas. In particular, do not try to apply
the old zero-source admissibility theorem after changing the introduction
rules: its equation-induction premise would create a circular bootstrap.

1. USE A STABLE-SOURCE SUBCALCULUS FIRST

Let H have the scalar numerical terms 0,S,I, both computation equations,
the retained-clause / D elimination rules, and deterministic introduction:
progress for literal equation matrices, cases for compound matrices. In H,
allow primitive transport only when the major equation's LEFT term has an
outer I constructor. Its right term is unrestricted. The formula template
may be an administrative equation or a canonical formula.

The larger raw checker may allow every source other than literal zero.
For completeness, work only inside H. Numerical substitution is admissible
in H by ordinary induction on derivations: an I-headed source remains
I-headed after substitution. This fact is essential when the guarded EI
construction specializes a supplied step derivation at 0 or at S(w).

It is NOT sufficient merely to say that final equations are protected:
an arbitrary raw proof could still contain a variable transport source,
and substitution could turn that internal source into literal zero.
H avoids this problem rather than assuming it away.

2. PUBLIC GRAMMAR

A protected equation has an I constructor on EACH side. This restriction
is stable under numerical substitution. A public formula is either:
  (a) a protected equation;
  (b) D_{x,y} B, where B is a compound public formula;
  (c) D_{x,y} B, where B is the protected equation
      I(y,0;u.h) = I(y,0;u.k).
The updates h,k may contain x,y and passive parameters. They need not be
state-independent. Canonical clauses have public antecedents/consequents.

Define W(t)=I(0,t;u.0). Every W(t) is I-headed. The public contradiction
is W(0)=W(S0). It has the intended ordinary reading of 0=S0.

Unprotected equations may occur as arithmetic bookkeeping judgments,
notably the primitive computations I(0,a;h)=a. Their presence is not a
new interpreted numerical sort. All such equations have their ordinary
equality meaning; the public language is a restriction on statements.

3. SAFE EQUALITY LEMMAS

Reflexivity r=r is available even for unprotected r. Let b=W(r). The
computation b=r used as both transport premises in the motive v=r gives
r=r. The transport source is b, hence I-headed.

For p=q with p I-headed, symmetry follows by transporting p=p in v=p;
the result is q=p. Congruence K(p)=K(q) follows by transporting the
reflexivity K(p)=K(p) in K(p)=K(v). These uses only require p I-headed.
Transitivity of a=b and b=c is available whenever b is I-headed.
Consequently full equality algebra is available between protected terms.

ZERO JOIN is also available without a zero-source transport. If p,q are
I-headed and p=0,q=0, put z=W(0), b_p=W(p). Computation gives b_p=p.
Start with b_p=b_p and transport p=0 in W(v)=b_p, obtaining z=b_p.
Reverse at z, then transport b_p=p to obtain p=z. Similarly q=z.
Reverse the latter at q and compose through z to get p=q. Every source
is one of p,q,z,b_p,b_q and hence I-headed.

4. GUARDED ENDPOINTS AND PADDING

For a protected equation E=(s=t), define
  G_v(E) := C(v,0,s)=C(v,0,t), C(v,a,b)=I(v,a;u.b).
The zero instance G_0(E) follows from two zero computations by zero join.
At a successor, both endpoint computations have I-headed sources AND
I-headed targets s,t. Thus both directions of the computation may safely
be used as transport majors. This gives G_{Sz}(E) interderivable with E.

Define L(E)=D_{u,v}G_v(E), with fresh u,v, and P(A)=L(A) for an equation,
P(A)=A for a compound formula. From E, atomic progress introduces L(E)
because G_{Sz}(E) follows independently of the local G_z(E). Conversely,
DE opens G_0(E) => all v G_v(E), the zero theorem supplies its antecedent,
and its instance at 1 gives E. No equation induction and no zero-source
symmetry is used.

For protected U=(s=t),V=(r=q), let
  M_w(U,V) := C(w,s,r)=C(w,t,q),
  U =>g V := D_{a,w} L(M_w(U,V)).
All M_w endpoints are I-headed; at w=0 and w=S(z) they are safely
interderivable with U and V respectively. Compound case introduction and
DE therefore give both implication rules for =>g. Again no EI is used.

5. NUMERICAL CASES AND PROTECTED EQUATION INDUCTION

Numerical cases for compound A follows from case DI with an unused witness,
DE, and clause use with the supplied base. Cases for protected E follows
by applying compound cases to L(E) and padding/unpadding its endpoints.

For protected E(y), suppose Gamma proves E(0), and a supplied H derivation
proves E(Sz) from Gamma,E(z), with z fresh for Gamma. Put J(y)=G_y(E(y)).
Prove the compound formula Phi(y) := J(y) =>g E(Sy) by numerical cases.
At y=0, ignore J(0), apply the supplied step specialized at 0 to E(0).
At y=Sw, use J(Sw)->E(Sw) and the supplied step specialized at Sw.
These specializations are legal because H has stable I-headed sources.
Cases gives Phi(z) under Gamma. From local J(z), implication elimination
therefore gives E(Sz), then J(Sz). Atomic progress introduces D_{u,y}J(y).
Its true base yields E(Sw) for every fresh w. Numerical cases, together
with the originally supplied E(0), then yields E(t) for arbitrary t.

The dependency order is acyclic:
  computation + I-source transport
  -> safe equality and zero join
  -> guard endpoints, padding, equation implication
  -> numerical cases
  -> protected EI.
Nothing in this list uses unrestricted zero-source symmetry.

6. TRANSLATION OF UNRESTRICTED EQUALITY

Translate an old atom s=t to W(s)=W(t). The translation of an old computation
U=V is derivable: U is I-headed, so its primitive equation can be used for
congruence with K=W, yielding W(U)=W(V).

Given W(s)=W(t), one can derive W(K(s))=W(K(t)) for any numerical template K:
let Khat(v)=W(K(v)). The computations W(s)=s and W(t)=t give
  Khat(W(s))=Khat(s), Khat(W(t))=Khat(t)
by safe congruence. The given equation gives
  Khat(W(s))=Khat(W(t)).
All displayed endpoints are I-headed, so reverse/compose to obtain the
claim. Apply this to both sides of an old equational transport motive and
join with the translated minor premise. This translates unrestricted
equational transport without deriving or using transport from literal 0.

For full formula transport, derive expansion/contraction
  F(W(s)) <-> F(s)
for every protected public template F. At atoms use the same safe links
between I-headed endpoints. At D use the binder-congruence construction
below. Now use the I-headed major W(s)=W(t) between F(W(s)),F(W(t)).

7. BINDER CONGRUENCE AND ROUND TRIPS

Suppose public matrices B,C are uniformly interderivable, with all
substitutions and side contexts permitted. To convert D B to D C, open
the source at fresh witness a.
  * If target C is compound, introduce it by cases: convert local C(a,0)
    back to B(a,0), use the source clause at Sz, then convert the result.
  * If target C is a canonical guarded atom, its base C(a,0) is a theorem.
    Convert that theorem to B(a,0), use the source clause at Sz, convert
    to C(a,Sz), and introduce by progress, ignoring local C(a,z).
This works in either direction and even when one matrix is compound and
the other is a guarded atom. It requires no EI.

The old D-only/clause calculus translates by
  T(s=t)=W(s)=W(t),
  T(D B)=D P(TB).
Clauses translate their antecedent and consequent. Case DI translates
using padding. Equation-progress DI translates using protected EI under
the padded base, followed by compound case DI. DE translates by replacing
each use of its old clause with pad/use/unpad. A first-class old Q B can
instead translate to D_{dummy,y}P(TB), using a dummy witness.

For the reverse inclusion, use the existing ordinary meanings and original
native rules; protected public formulas are legitimate old formulas.
The old composite is equivalent to identity by ordinary equality and
padding. The new composite at a protected atom s=t is W(s)=W(t): forwards
use congruence W with source s (I-headed); backwards transport W(s)=s and
W(t)=t in the corresponding two sides. At D use the binder congruence
above, together with the recursive equivalence and padding.

The numerical sort and term translations are literal identities between
the two native presentations. Equality W(x)=W(y) has ordinary meaning
equivalent to x=y because computation proves W(x)=x. Composing with the
repository's established scalar/iPRA bridge yields the requested whole-
domain bi-interpretation and identity numerical composites.

8. RAW ADMINISTRATIVE PROOFS: ORDINARY SEMANTIC SANDWICH

Use the SAME ordinary reading on every administrative formula:
  eq: ordinary equality;
  Q_y B: B(0) -> forall y B(y);
  D_xy B: exists x(B(x,0)->forall y B(x,y)).
Clauses have their ordinary implication/universal reading.

Every raw combined inference is already a permitted inference of the old
Choice-DI calculus. Atomic deterministic DI is its equation-progress
option, compound deterministic DI is its case option, and source dispatch
only restricts transport. Thus raw derivations map literally into the old
calculus. Under the Lean kernel inventory, full template transport and
witnessed equation-progress DI are the existing derived rules NPrf.tr
and NPrf.dIProg. Equivalently, ordinary soundness of atomic DI uses equation
induction to turn its step proof into the conditional closure it asserts;
DE is then ordinary existential/closure elimination.

Consequently ALL administrative formulas have ordinary soundness. No mixed
semantics is needed for this positive argument. The optional mixed reading
in the old guarded_core paper is useful only to exhibit incompleteness at
certain unguarded formulas; it is not required for the new soundness proof.

For canonical contexts/conclusions, H provides the converse direction via
translation and its canonical identity composites. Since H is included in
the raw combined checker, this gives equality of canonical consequence
relations by a sandwich. The public grammar restriction is needed for
COMPLETENESS and the composites, not for raw ordinary soundness. Nothing
here claims an identity round trip for all administrative formulas or
unrestricted direct substitution into raw nonzero-source derivation trees.

9. CHECKER ACCOUNTING QUALIFICATION

The compact kernel admits administrative statements outside the public
protected/guarded grammar. Thus its source size is a canonical-statement
kernel measurement, NOT the size of an all-input validator that rejects
every noncanonical final proposition. The existing guarded_core package
has the same qualification. State this next to any record claim and measure
a strict public-goal validator separately. Compare complete programs under
the same input contract. Do not silently transfer a <526-byte measurement
to a stronger input-validation contract.

10. SEPARATE CORRECTION TO UPSTREAM README

The claim that substitution is not admissible in ZPrf is too strong.
The proved theorem zprf_iff_nprf for EVERY context immediately transports
NPrf.subst to ZPrf. Only naive rule-preserving substitution into the same
derivation tree fails. An optional Lean corollary can be obtained by first
mapping to NPrf, applying NPrf.subst, and mapping back. This observation is
independent of the new guarded/protected proof and is not its bootstrap.
