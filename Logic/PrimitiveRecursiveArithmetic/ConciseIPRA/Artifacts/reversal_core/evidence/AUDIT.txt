1. Exact validator equivalence
-----------------------------

Let F_old(a,s) and T_old(a,s) be formula and term from the frozen
guarded_core/reference/baseline_strict_goal.py. Let V(a,d) be the
shared validator in code/checker_rev_strict_eqd.py. For every JSON value a and
every nonnegative integer s:

    F_old(a,s) iff V(a,2s)
    T_old(a,s) iff V(a,3+2s).

Proof is simultaneous structural induction. Both programs require exact
Python int/list types; booleans, floats, strings, dictionaries and null
are rejected. At a numerical leaf, d=3+2s and both predicates admit
exactly 0 and the odd indices 3 <= a < 3+2s. At a formula leaf, the
even-depth test rejects all integers, as the old formula predicate does.

At formula depth 2s, unary D increases depth by 4, corresponding to two
bound slots; binary equality increases it by 3 and enters the numerical
sort. At numerical depth 3+2s, unary successor increases depth by 4,
corresponding to two inherited dummy slots; ternary iterator increases
it by 2, corresponding to one slot. No other list arity is accepted.

For full Eq/Q/D goals, extend the old formula function by admitting
exact diagonal triples [B,B,B], checking B at s+1. Extend V by admitting
ternary formula roots and requiring their three entries to be equal.
The same induction applies: a ternary formula root increases depth by 2.

Finite corroboration in parser_audit.json ran 260500 comparisons across
malformed trees, canonical generated trees, scopes 0 through 4, and both
term/formula modes. No mismatches occurred. This is evidence of faithful
implementation, not a replacement for the structural-induction proof.

3. Root reversal and noncanonical ternary formulas
------------------------------------------------

Under the old raw-alias convention, a ternary formula reads only its
last child as a Q matrix. Unconditional array reversal is NOT sound
under that convention: it exchanges the first and last children.
Canonical public-goal validation alone does not fix this, since internal
certificate expressions can be noncanonical.

A sound symmetric convention is available. Interpret any ternary formula
[B0,B1,B2] as the ordinary intuitionistic formula

    Q_y B0 AND Q_y B1 AND Q_y B2,

where Q_y B means B(0) -> forall y B(y). This is a conjunction OF closures;
it is not the closure of a conjunction. Keep the inherited interpretation
at all other arities. A canonical triple [B,B,B] remains equivalent to
Q_y B by conjunction idempotence.

The opening traversal visits the three children at the same depth, so
capture-free substitution acts componentwise in this reading. Root
reversal exchanges the first and third conjuncts and is intuitionistically
an equivalence. At a binary formula it swaps the sides of equality; at a
unary D formula it is the identity. Other admitted formula arities have
the inherited constant-truth reading, also invariant under reversal.

Audit of the kernel supply/use cases:

* QE's major matcher uses the exact diagonal [template]*3. Thus its major
  still supplies Q(template), and the reversed target is equivalent to
  the ordinary target under the symmetric reading.
* A proved unary D major exports major*3, an exact diagonal Q resource.
* A proved nonempty major of arity other than one exports an array of
  arity at least six, hence only the inherited administrative truth.
* Equality transport is sound under ordinary equality in every decoded
  formula; applying root reversal then preserves equivalence.
* DI by cases is sound for arbitrary decoded matrices by numerical
  coverage. This includes conjunctions of closures.
* The progress mode introduces genuine induction assumptions only at
  binary equations; admitted even padding roots decode to truth.
* Computation equations and numerical expressions are unchanged.

This is a changed administrative interpretation, and should be stated
explicitly. The inherited numerical-codec proof still supplies the
term-opening lemma. The only new formula-opening case is the componentwise
ternary case just described.

An alternative implementation preserving the old raw-alias convention
uses a[::len(a)%2 or -1] rather than a[::-1]. It reverses binary equations
and even truth-padding roots while leaving Q and D roots unchanged. This
adds 15 normalized bytes. It avoids the symmetric-triple proof obligation.

4. Scalar-neutral administrative truth
-------------------------------------

The local source-dispatch profiles seed their retained context with the
empty-array formula []. Replace that administrative truth by scalar 0,
so the default context becomes [0] or the one-element tuple (0,). The
opening operation returns 0 at a scalar formula position of even depth.
Both spellings have the inherited ordinary reading 0=0. Neither is a
canonical public Eq/Q/D formula, so the native formula language is unchanged.

A canonical proof producer replaces only neutral packet majors [] by 0.
The introduction mode remains in its original field: initializer_or_mode=[]
selects cases, and initializer_or_mode=0 selects equation progress. Formula
or term bodies, numeral zero and all non-neutral majors are unchanged.
The major matcher cannot mistake 0 for a nonempty equality or Q list.
All subsequent branch tests give the same effect because both administrative
markers are falsy. The recursive proof of the neutral major closes at the
new initial context. Thus the canonical compiler still realizes every
native rule, and soundness retains exactly the same ordinary truth axiom.

This is not an identity of arbitrary serialized-certificate languages.
It is a change of one administrative truth representative. The global
source_dispatch token profile has no initial neutral formula and does not
benefit from this change. Its 259-token frontier is not improved here.

Frozen four accepted derived examples and one deliberately rejected old
primitive control were run on original and neutral-zero AST/byte profiles,
in normal and optimized Python. All 40 expected-verdict checks agreed.
The transformation and source hashes are recorded separately. The new
byte figure is 525 versus 526; the AST figure is 282 versus 283, attained
by different programs. Novelty was checked against the inspected three
source_dispatch profiles only. No exhaustive historical novelty claim
or absolute minimality claim follows from these measurements.
