COMPOUND-ONLY D INTRODUCTION CANNOT PRESERVE THE NATIVE LANGUAGE

Let N^- be the pinned native scalar core, retaining QI-progress on literal
equations but restricting DI-case to matrices B that are not literal equations.
Then N^- does not prove D_{x,y}(0=0), although the original core does.

Proof. Interpret numerical terms in standard N with the intended iterator.
Interpret equations as actual equality and Q_y B as B(0) -> forall y B(y).
Interpret D_{x,y} B as false when B is literally an equation, and as
exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y)) otherwise. This is compositional and
respects numerical substitution, since substitution cannot alter whether
a formula has an equation, Q, or D at its root.

Hyp, computation, and equational transport are sound. QE is immediate.
DE at an atomic matrix has an impossible major premise, so is sound;
DE at a compound matrix is ordinary existential elimination. DI-case is
sound at every permitted compound matrix by zero/successor coverage of N.
QI-progress is sound by ordinary induction on the equation's numerical
truth value, with context and passive parameters fixed. All eigenvariable
conditions have their usual interpretation.

The proposed target D_{x,y}(0=0) is false in this model. In the original
calculus, equality reflexivity gives the DI-case premise, so the target
is provable. Thus the restriction fails to preserve the original sequents.

The model also validates full formula transport and all ordinary admissible
structural operations of the native system. Adding these does not repair
the missing atomic D introductions. A nonempty atomic introduction family,
a new bridge rule, or a changed formula language/reading is necessary.

UPSTREAM WORDING CORRECTION

The README claims substitution is not admissible for ZPrf because a
substitution can change a nonzero source into literal zero. This explains
why direct constructor-preserving substitution fails, but not admissibility.
The proved equivalence zprf_iff_nprf for every context, together with
NPrf.subst, implies admissibility by ZPrf -> NPrf -> subst -> ZPrf.
No inference about formalization correctness is drawn from this wording.
