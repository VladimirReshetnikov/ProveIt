# A shared iPRA recurrence lemma

Status: proved on paper as a derived rule of ordinary iPRA. This is a useful
refactoring of the bridge, not a new axiomatization, a new checker record, or a
claim of novelty for fold fusion. The Lean interface below is a proposal and
has not been compiled.

## The most economical shared lemma

Fix primitive recursive terms F(n,p), G(n,p), and H(n,r,p), with fixed
parameter tuple p. Suppose iPRA proves the following equations, uniformly in
their displayed free variables:

    F(0,p) = G(0,p)
    F(S n,p) = H(n,F(n,p),p)
    G(S n,p) = H(n,G(n,p),p).

Then iPRA proves `forall n, F(n,p) = G(n,p)`.

Proof: apply quantifier-free induction to the single literal equation
`F(n,p)=G(n,p)`. Its base is the first hypothesis. In the successor step,

    F(S n,p)
      = H(n,F(n,p),p)
      = H(n,G(n,p),p)
      = G(S n,p).

The middle equality is congruence in the previous-value argument, using the
induction equation. No induction on a quantified formula is used. The
parameters remain fixed throughout. Universal quantification of the final
equation is the conclusion of the usual induction schema.

It is enough to have the recurrence hypotheses under a fixed context Gamma
that does not contain n free. In the formal scope discipline, Gamma is shifted
under the fresh counter. The empty-context version suffices for both existing
bridge proofs.

## Proposed internal Lean interface

Here `s,t : PTm (n+1)` have variable 0 as counter, and
`h : PTm (n+2)` has variables 0 and 1 as counter and previous value. Define

    step h r := Syn.subst (Fin.cases (Syn.var 0) (Syn.repl r)) h

for `r : PTm (n+1)`. This is exactly the substitution already appearing in
`PRAAx.precSucc` and `RoundTrip.code_prec_succ`.

The intended statement is:

    theorem recurrence_unique (s t : PTm (n+1)) (h : PTm (n+2))
        (h0 : [] |-PRA (s = t)[inst zero])
        (hs : [] |-PRA s[repl (succ (var 0))] = step h s)
        (ht : [] |-PRA t[repl (succ (var 0))] = step h t) :
        [] |-PRA forall (s = t)

where the displayed proof notation is explanatory rather than literal Lean.
Use `PRA.qfInd (s ≐ t) trivial h0` and, in its step context `[s ≐ t]`,
compose `hs.ofNil`, `Prf.eqCongr h`, and `(ht.ofNil).eqSymm`. The arguments
of the congruence proof are reflexivity for variable 0 (the counter),
`Prf.hyp0` for variable 1 (the previous value), and reflexivity for all
remaining variables. This puts the only induction and its counter/previous
slot bookkeeping in one place.

## Exact specialization: iterator extensionality

`ToPRA.iterSym_ext` assumes

    ha : a'(z) = a(sigma(z))
    hh : h'(u,z) = h(u,sigma(z)).

Take

    F(n,z) = iterSym(a',h')(n,z),
    G(n,z) = iterSym(a,h)(n,sigma(z)),
    H(n,r,z) = h(r,sigma(z)).

The common body ignores the counter. The base follows by
`iterSym_zero`, `ha`, and the reverse of `iterSym_zero` on the right.
The left recurrence is `iterSym_succ` followed by `hh` instantiated at
`u=F(n,z)`; the right recurrence is `iterSym_succ` directly. The existing
`subst_inst_up` and `subst_repl_up` lemmas normalize these substitutions.
After recurrence uniqueness, `Prf.allE t` supplies the requested count t.

In source syntax the common body H is the term

    PTm.app h (Fin.cases (Syn.var 1)
      (fun j => Syn.shift (Syn.shift (sigma j))))

over the variables `(counter, previous, z...)`. This avoids treating the
parameters as if they were free of all binder shifts.

## Exact specialization: primitive recursion round trip

For fixed codes `f : PR k`, `g : PR (k+2)`, take

    F(n,x) = ToPRA.tau (FromPRA.code (.prec f g)),
    G(n,x) = PTm.app (.prec f g) Syn.var,
    H(n,r,x) = PTm.app g Syn.var,

where H is in the enlarged `(n,r,x...)` scope. The base is already the
statement of `RoundTrip.tau_code_prec_zero f g ihf`.

The left recurrence needs no induction hypothesis. It is the first three
links in the existing `tau_code_prec_succ` proof:

1. `tau_subst_repl (Tm.succ (Syn.var 0)) _`;
2. `ToPRA.interp.sound (code_prec_succ f g)`;
3. `tau_subst_code ihg _`.

These conclude `F(S n,x) = g(n,F(n,x),x)`. The right recurrence is
`PRAAx.precSucc f g`. Recurrence uniqueness owns the remaining congruence
under g and the induction. `Prf.allOpen` yields the unquantified equality
used in the `prec` branch of `tau_code`.

This is a simplification of the *interface of the proof*. It should not be
claimed to reduce Lean line count until the refactoring is implemented and
compiled. It does not remove any induction principle from iPRA.

## More general indexed fusion

If desired, one can package the common generalization. Given recurrences

    F(0)=a, F(Sn)=f(n,F(n)),
    G(0)=b, G(Sn)=g(n,G(n)),

and a term K(n,r), suppose

    K(0,a)=b,
    K(Sn,f(n,r))=g(n,K(n,r)).

One literal-equation induction gives `K(n,F(n))=G(n)`. This is a schema over
fixed primitive recursive terms, not quantification over functions. Identity
K gives recurrence uniqueness. A projection K gives correctness of a paired
state implementation of primitive recursion. The stronger generality is not
needed for the two current Lean proofs and may add implementation overhead.

## What this does not establish

- No checker size reduction or new minimum.
- No deletion of a logical or arithmetic rule.
- No claim that fusion is mathematically novel.
- No Lean compilation or formal verification of the proposed interface.
- No generic statement for higher-type functions or non-primitive-recursive K.
