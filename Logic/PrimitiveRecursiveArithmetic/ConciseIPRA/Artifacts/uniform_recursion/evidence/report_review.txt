============================================

------------
A separate AI review agent read the complete main LaTeX source,

Conclusion
----------
No unresolved mathematical error was identified in the stated construction.
This is a written mathematical review, not proof-assistant verification or
an independently implemented complete formal proof of every metatheorem.
Finite implementation tests and their results are recorded separately.

Mathematical points checked
--------------------------
1. Successor-seeded counter-aware recursion has the stated endpoint rules.
   W(0)=1, W(Sn)=n, the selector C, true predecessor P, and K(1)=1,K(2)=0
   follow by literal normalization. The proof does not require K to be a
   parity function or to satisfy an additional range theorem.

2. The direct primitive-recursion compiler J has the literal zero and
   successor identities stated in the paper. It uses the counter argument
   explicitly and does not require pairing or an encoded counter state.

3. Ordinary D elimination and numerical cases are recovered before general
   equality transport. The two-row Name packet therefore does not assume
   symmetry, transitivity, or the transport rule it is proving.

4. Formula transport uses external induction on formula structure and a
   fresh syntactically nonzero witness. No induction over arbitrary
   quantified object-language formulas is smuggled into the construction.

5. Atomic case introduction at witness zero uses the compound-envelope
   repair. It does not incorrectly assume a nonliteral closed zero survives
   full normalization.

6. The gate, padding, and aligned-selector macros support the stated
   implication, conjunction, disjunction, and quantifier interfaces. Padding
   changes depth through the gated closure L(A), not an unused constant D,
   which would generally be true rather than equivalent to A.

7. The residual-reflection argument uses induction only on literal
   equations. In its successor case, coverage and predecessor equations
   justify r=S(Pr) when Pr is nonzero. The derived two-valued equality test
   then supports the quantifier-free characteristic formulas, including
   implication without excluded middle for arbitrary formulas.

8. Both arithmetic translations use the whole numerical domain and actual
   equality. Internal recurrence uniqueness establishes preservation of
   operations in the double translations; agreement merely on standard
   numerals would not have sufficed. Native/ordinary formula round trips
   and the explicit separation convention address the logical boundary.

9. The source distinguishes the external total normalizer from a uniform
   primitive-recursive evaluator. The fuel-bounded interface is the stated
   way to obtain a primitive-recursive certificate predicate without adding
   an internal normalization-totality axiom.

10. The paper specifies the one fixed reflexivity clause, all three
    inference families, their scope/discharge requirements, and computation
    as part of canonical syntax. No additional primitive equality,
    hypothesis, ordinary logical, case, or induction rule was detected.

Corrections prompted by review and incorporated
-----------------------------------------------
- The auxiliary normalization translation explicitly reads the canonical
  R(0,a;0) node as the independent successor S(a), with additional zero
  reductions when a reduction creates that canonical shape.
- The logical section explicitly proves that D preserves pointwise
  interderivability of matrices before invoking binder congruence.
- Gate endpoint statements are explicitly pointwise when the gated formula
  already depends on the control variable, as happens in conjunction and
  universal-quantifier macros.

Implementation issue found during the earlier independent audit
---------------------------------------------------------------
Two-binder recursor substitution must remove the counter slot first under
the retained state slot. The initial accumulator-first prototype could
capture ambient positive de Bruijn indices. The delivered implementation
uses counter-first substitution, and the implementation suite includes the
nested-binder regression identified by this review. For an ambient v,
R(1,v;j,w.w) must normalize to S(v), not S(0).
