Conclusion
==========
I found no material gap in the ordinary arithmetic/logical bi-interpretation
as stated, provided the explicit separation/falsity bridge is retained and
the guarded compact checker is claimed complete only for its translated
public language. The scalar predecessor, equality reflection, and PR
compiler are substantial inherited dependencies, not consequences of the
short executable's size. The checker refinements do not delete any
arithmetic or logical axiom.

1. A complete ordinary first-order theory
=========================================
Define T_I in ordinary intuitionistic first-order logic with actual equality
and genuine falsity. Its staged numerical signature begins with 0 and S.
For each fixed earlier-stage term h(u,p_1,...,p_r), add a function symbol
 i_h(n,a,p_1,...,p_r)
with universally closed defining equations
 i_h(0,a,p)=a,
 i_h(Sn,a,p)=h(i_h(n,a,p),p).
Descriptions are finite external syntax. No object variable ranges over
programs, and no universal PR evaluator is asserted.

Its other nonlogical principles are exactly:
 (a) coverage: forall x [x=0 or exists y (x=Sy)];
 (b) separation: (0=S0) -> bottom;
 (c) induction for each literal equation E(n,p), equivalently the contextual
     rule from Gamma |- E(0,p) and Gamma,E(z,p) |- E(Sz,p) infer Gamma |- E(t,p),
     with z fresh for Gamma, t, and p.

All ordinary intuitionistic logical/equality rules are present in this
ordinary realization. Native certificates reconstruct them by macros;
this should not be confused with suppressing the logic from T_I.

2. Complete native inventory and its ordinary reading
====================================================
Numerical terms: x, 0, S(t), I(n,a;u.h), where only h binds u.
Native formula grammar: s=t, Q_y B, D_{x,y} B, with x and y distinct.
Designated native contradiction: 0=S0.
Context: finite reusable formula list.

Primitive axioms are I0 and IS, the two iterator equations above.
Primitive inferences are Hyp; transport from s=t and F(s) to F(t);
QE from Q_y B and B(0) to B(t); DE from D_{x,y}B and a proof of A under
Q_y B(b,y), with fresh b, to A; DI_case from a proof of B(t,Sz) under
B(t,0), with fresh z, to D_{x,y}B; and DI_progress from a proof of
E(t,Sz) under E(t,z), with fresh z and literal equation E, to D_{x,y}E.
Assumptions displayed under DI/DE are discharged. Full-formula induction
is not primitive. The clause-context presentation in inherited_choice_basis
is equivalent: a clause [A;y.B] means A -> forall y B.

Ordinary readings:
 (s=t)^o = (s=t),
 (Q_y B)^o = B^o(0) -> forall y B^o(y),
 (D_{x,y}B)^o = exists x [B^o(x,0) -> forall y B^o(x,y)].
Domain formula is x=x; equality is unchanged.
DI_case is sound using coverage. DI_progress is sound using equation
induction under an assumed base. QE/DE/transport are standard inferences.

3. Falsity is the most important explicit assumption
=====================================================
Native explosion from 0=S0 is valid: with C(v,a,b)=I(v,a;u.b), the assumed
0=S0 gives a=C(0,a,b)=C(S0,a,b)=b. All equations follow. Case introduction
and external induction on formula syntax then yield every native formula.

This DOES NOT prove that the ordinary theory consisting only of positive
iterator equations, coverage, and equation induction has genuine falsity.
A singleton ordinary structure satisfies all those principles and 0=S0,
while interpreting genuine falsity as false. Therefore the explicit
ordinary axiom (0=S0)->bottom is necessary for the stated translation.

With this axiom, native bottom* reads as 0=S0, which is equivalent to
ordinary bottom by separation in one direction and ordinary explosion in
the other. Conversely, the ordinary separation axiom translates into the
native identity implication bottom* ->_* bottom*, hence is derivable.
There is no circularity here: native explosion and ordinary separation
perform different roles in the two directions of the interpretation.

4. Logical macros and both formula composites
=============================================
Set C(v,a,b)=I(v,a;u.b). Define G_v(s=t) as
 C(v,0,s)=C(v,0,t), and push G through D. By computation, equality, and
binder congruence, G_0 A is a theorem and G_{Sz} A is interderivable with A.
The unused-witness padding D_{r,z}G_z A is interderivable with A.

A selector M_v(A,B) is defined recursively by aligning binder depths using
that padding, then applying C to the two sides of the terminal equations.
The mixed-depth clauses in inherited_choice_basis strictly decrease the
maximum binder depth and give M_0(A,B) <-> A and M_{Sz}(A,B) <-> B.

Let p(z)=I(z,0;u.C(u,1,0)); fixed computations give p(1)=1, p(2)=0.
The six macros are:
 A ->_* B = D_{r,z} M_z(A,B),
 A and_* B = D_{r,z} G_z M_{p(z)}(A,B),
 exists_*x A = D_{x,y}G_y A,
 A or_*B = exists_*x M_x(A,B),
 forall_*x A = A(0) and_* D_{r,z}G_z A(z),
 bottom* = 0=S0.
Newly bound r,z,y are fresh; r is unused.

The universal macro needs its explicit A(0) conjunct: the gated paired
formula alone supplies positive instances. Coverage supplies an arbitrary
instance from zero and successor instances. Conjunction needs both fixed
instances 1 and 2. No excluded middle for arbitrary quantified formulas is
used. Decidability is later derived only for quantifier-free formulas.

The decisive native composite is
 D_{x,y}B <-> exists_*x [B(x,0) ->_* forall_*y B(x,y)].
Forward: open D at a fresh witness, use its closure under the base, form
universal, implication, existential, and discharge the witness.
Backward: open the existential and use case DI at its witness; its base,
implication, and universal elimination give the successor premise.

External structural induction gives both formula composites. The displayed
ordinary readings alone would establish soundness, not both composites;
the macro derivations and paired composite are the needed extra argument.

5. Arithmetic dependency audit
===============================
The supplied dependency order is legitimate:
 1. Equation induction + coverage/cases + scalar computation construct P.
 2. P(0)=0 and P(Sn)=n imply successor injectivity and strengthen coverage
    to x=0 or x=S(Px).
 3. Monus d(a,b)=I(b,a;u.Pu), selector C, and separation construct a
    Boolean characteristic of actual equality throughout the domain.
 4. External recursion on each finite quantifier-free formula constructs
    its characteristic equation. Equation induction therefore gives QFI.
 5. Only then use QFI to construct pairing/projections and compile general PR.

The scalar predecessor proof is nontrivial, but its displayed inductions
all have literal equations as invariants. Its case splits on Boolean values
come from coverage, not an illicit induction on a disjunction. Diagonal
substitutions are applied to already proved universally parameterized
identities, not to an active induction hypothesis with altered parameters.
No pairing or equality characteristic is presupposed in the predecessor.

The equality-reflection appendix initially presents a general PRE language,
but its opening paragraph explicitly restricts the scalar application to
terms already available: P,C,addition,monus and their compositions. That
qualification is essential; otherwise the proof order would appear circular.
Its residual identity proves d(Sa,b)=1 iff a=b internally. This gives an
equality characteristic without appealing to equality on standard numerals.

The square-root pairing proof uses only QFI after QFI has been recovered.
The root invariant is quantifier-free. Surjectivity of pairing is unnecessary
and is not claimed. Pairing is an internal finite-state device; the original
numerical domain is not restricted to its range.

6. Both numerical interpretation composites
============================================
Interpret every fixed scalar iterator in iPRA by ordinary primitive recursion
whose update ignores its explicit counter argument. In the reverse direction,
for f(0,p)=g(p), f(Sn,p)=h(n,f(n,p),p), use
 V(n,p)=I(n,pi(0,g*(p));u.pi(S L(u),h*(L(u),H(u),p))),
 f*(n,p)=H(V(n,p)).
Equation induction proves L(V(n,p))=n; the projection equations establish
both defining equations of f*.

For each fixed description, two realizations satisfying the same base and
step equations are equal at all internal counters by equation induction.
External induction on the finite description therefore proves that its double
translation equals the original operation. This includes coherence when
substitution changes the staged syntactic description of an update.
Consequently x=y is an internally total bijection preserving all operations
in both composites. This is stronger than standard-model numerical agreement.

7. Guarded compact public language
===================================
The compact kernel keeps progress at equation matrices and cases at compound
matrices. It is not the full native calculus on every raw formula. To make
its public language faithful, atom matrices under binders have the gate form
 I(y,0;u.s)=I(y,0;u.t), with u absent from s,t.
Their zero row is a theorem, so a case premise can be converted to a progress
premise by supplying that theorem and ignoring the induction hypothesis.

After replacing Q by D with an unused witness, replace each atomic matrix
immediately beneath an original D by the padding D_{r,z}G_z E. Recursion
through compound matrices yields guarded innermost equations. The outer
matrix becomes compound, permitting the original case rule. Original
progress steps may use unguarded auxiliary atomic progress formulas before
transferring to the padded public formula. Thus it is permissible for internal
proof formulas to be broader than public formulas.

Formula round trips use padding equivalence and binder congruence. Missing
literal-zero-source transport instances require the inherited source-restriction
recovery theorem; they should not be silently assumed in a direct rule audit.
The relevant results are referenced in the source and the README, but the
short main paper is not a new proof of every restricted-source lemma.

In particular, the correct theorem has the shape
 native Gamma |- A iff compact pub(Gamma) |- pub(A),
with numerical realization unchanged and formula round trips. A claim
native Gamma |- A iff compact Gamma |- A for arbitrary raw formulas would
be false for the root-determined introduction restriction.

8. Complexity and verification limitations to state
====================================================
The compact programs do not validate the canonical public grammar. Scalar
padding and surplus arrays have stipulated semantic meanings (truth or zero).
Their acceptance is not itself arithmetic unsoundness, but these are aliases,
not canonical object formulas. Do not advertise a compact profile as rejecting
all malformed public goals. The fully validated native interface has a separate
and larger trusted contract.

The inherited arity-checker appendix describes a different earlier protocol
with both choices at equations. Its broad theorem about canonical native
sequents cannot simply be quoted as the direct completeness theorem of the
new guarded-only protocol. Use the explicit guarded translation in report.tex.

Finite-runtime stack/memory rejection may reject valid very large proofs.
The mathematical checker theorem is for finite certificates with sufficient
resources. Regression tests establish executable behavior on tested inputs;
they do not prove interpretation or soundness for all proofs. The source-size
improvement preserves inherited mathematical dependencies rather than replacing
them with a new axiomatization.
