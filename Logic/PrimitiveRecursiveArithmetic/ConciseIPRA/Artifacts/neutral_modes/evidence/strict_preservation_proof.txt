EAGER OPENING IN THE THREE-FIELD NATIVE PRESENTATION
==================================================

Scope and claim
---------------
This is a composition of two existing constructions, not a new arithmetic
axiom deletion. The three complete modules used as controls are in the pinned
ConciseIPRA incoming archives at commit
7a7633980dbca7f599322a5e7f1e65fb1cddb7c8. The three-field certificate protocol
and the fully validated public syntax come from
ipra_validated_three_field_2026-11-13. Eager opening and the empty-array
administrative premise come from ipra_checked_reduction_2026-11-13.

The new measured modules are:
  checker_tokens304.py   304 tokens, 359 AST nodes, 652 normalized bytes;
  checker_ast345.py      328 tokens, 345 AST nodes, 665 normalized bytes;
  checker_joint311.py    311 tokens, 350 AST nodes, 685 normalized bytes.
The first and third use three-field records. The second uses four-field
records. They validate the full input alphabet and the initial closed native
Eq/D goal. Only the first two improve an individual current strict record.
No one program attains all coordinate minima, and none beats the compact
promised-grammar family in all coordinates.

The theorem is soundness for every accepted finite parsed input and
completeness for canonical encodings of the closed native Eq/D theorems.
The existing full first-order iPRA interpretation is inherited unchanged.
Exact equality of accepted arbitrary raw certificate languages with the
controls is NOT asserted. Eager opening can reject aliases on which a lazy
opener never examines its image. The administrative premise is recoded.
There is no new Lean or Rocq verification of Python semantics.

1. The two changes
------------------
In each matched control, replace the opener's final expression

       (d % 2 or not g) and i(t, d=3)

by

       d % 2 * i(t, d=3).

After the initial goal validation, replace g += b'\x00' by g += [[]].
All other program syntax is unchanged. In particular the scope inequalities,
public grammar, substitution order, arithmetic rules, native case/progress
distinction, and whole-input alphabet check are retained.

The first replacement eagerly checks an image even at an even depth; at an
even depth the result is numeric 0 or the empty list, according to the
image's type. Both have the true-formula auxiliary reading. The second
replacement gives the empty list its established role as an administrative
truth premise. It is needed because the eager no-image scan rejects the old
formula-position scalar 0 at depth zero.

2. The sorted reading and the opening lemma
------------------------------------------
This is the existing interpretation of raw intermediate expressions, not a
new unrestricted public grammar. At a formula position depth is 2p; at a
term position it is 3+2p, where p counts protected slots. Names in [k,0)
are rigid parameters. At term positions the scalar codes 0,1,2 denote zero,
and 3+2j and 4+2j denote protected slot j when present. All other scalars
are the substitution hole. At formula positions every scalar denotes the
true equation 0=0. Arrays are read by their arity:

  arity     term position                 formula position
  0         zero                          0=0
  1         successor                     paired binder D
  2         zero                          equality
  3         iterator                      closure Q (last child)
  >=4       zero                          0=0.

The arity-dependent increment d+5-len(a) supplies the existing genuine and
dummy slots. Children with no reading are annotations; their traversal can
reject but does not change the semantic reading. The complete environmental
definition is ch11_cert.tex, cert:def:reading and cert:def:opener.

Let E(a;t,d) be the new proof-phase opener at a fixed floor k. Whenever E
returns, its output is closed at the same sorted position and its reading
is the reading of a with the term read from t substituted for its free hole.
With the missing image 0j, successful opening preserves the reading and
establishes closedness of a.

Proof. Induct structurally on the traversed tree, with the usual simultaneous
argument for validation of the inserted image. At a retained numeric leaf
the returned value is a or 1; the only changed code is 0 to 1, and both read
as numerical zero at a term position. At an exposed odd-depth leaf the image
is validated at root term depth 3 and inserted without capture. At an exposed
even-depth leaf the eager product is 0 or []; either reads as 0=0. The missing
image cannot pass validation: 0j reaches an invalid ordered comparison. Array
cases are the unchanged structural recursion. Consequently extra eager image
checks may fail, but every successful opening has the same semantic property
as the old opener. This is exactly the composition of cert:lem:opening,
cert:lem:plus-opening and cert:lem:eager. The exposure and freshness lemmas
continue to hold, since their interval and protected-slot conditions are
unchanged. On canonical uniform-zero expression trees with an admissible
image, the opener agrees literally with the old canonical substitution.

3. Initial validation and phase
------------------------------
The complete input first passes the unchanged test that its Python printed
form contains only digits, '-', '[', ']', ',', and space. Among parsed JSON
values this admits exactly finite integer/list trees; strings, objects,
Booleans, floats and null are rejected, including in unused input prefixes.
A non-list top-level value cannot provide the required pop operation.

Initially k=0 and g=[]; the first traversal is therefore the strict public
validator. At an even formula depth it admits only unary D and binary Eq;
at an odd term depth only unary successor and ternary iterator. A scalar
must be a positive odd code at an odd depth, within the protected scope.
These are precisely the control's P_ed_1 goal conditions. Public zero is 1.
All failed shapes and scope checks reach the eager missing-image failure,
including an invalid scalar at an even depth. Thus replacing the old explicit
validation flag inside the fallback has not silently admitted truth atoms.

After the first scan, each expanded context contains []. Subsequent calls
have a nonempty context and use the auxiliary syntax. Both g += [[]] and
major calls can append extra copies of []; these are copies of a closed
theorem. Their number and order are not used to determine scope. Every
substantive branch assumption is still added by g+[h], which creates a fresh
list. A proof's local assumptions do not leak to its caller. In the AST
profile the empty-premise append remains inside the existing matched-record
case; no syntax of that case has changed.

4. Soundness of the three-field profiles
---------------------------------------
Induct on the finite tree of returning calls. Maintain that the current goal
and every context entry have closed auxiliary readings at the entry floor;
the readings of context entries are the available assumptions. The opener
lemma establishes the invariant for all successful scans and substitutions.
Initial validation separately establishes that the root denotes the actual
declared closed native formula.

Membership is the hypothesis rule. Matching [] is use of the already
derivable truth 0=0.

For a nonterminal packet (s,u,t), first compare the target opening i(t,u)
with the current goal. On equality, replace the goal by i(t,s), reconstruct
the major [s,u] when s is true-valued and [t,t,t] otherwise, and clear u.
The reconstructed major is recursively proved. The opening lemma gives the
two instances of one equation template, or a closure and its zero instance;
this is the inherited transport/closure-specialization rule. The comparison
and the witness opening occur at the OLD floor, as in the matched control.
In particular a later recursive proof cannot make an earlier witness name
its own future eigenparameter.

If u is false-valued and s is true-valued, the major is recursively proved
and s*3 is the retained resource. If s is unary, triplication creates its
Q body; opening at the newly allocated name is paired-binder elimination.
Every other array arity gives the inherited neutral-reading case. The
semantic opening and freshness proofs are unchanged.

If u and s are false-valued, the new administrative source [] is closed by
membership. The goal's matrix is opened at witness t before decreasing k.
The mode test s or len(A)%2==u is UNCHANGED. For a genuine equation matrix
(arity 2), u=0 gives equation progress. The literal empty-list marker u=[]
gives cases. For compound D/Q matrices (arities 1/3), both false-valued
markers give cases. Arrays of arity at least 4 have the neutral reading;
an attempted length test on a scalar fails. Thus this composition never
introduces induction for arbitrary compound formulas.

If u is true-valued, the hypothesis is the inherited computation frame.
The three-field source reuse can make its image fresh rather than zero.
The existing zero-instance computation lemma cert:lem:zeroinstance says
that after setting the newly admitted private names to zero the supplied
hypothesis is an instance of I0 or IS. The old context and goal contain
none of those names. Apply the continuation induction hypothesis in the
ordinary theory T_eq and specialize those names to zero; discharge the
resulting computation axiom by cert:lem:supply. This argument requires
only the reading and freshness properties of opening, already proved above.
It does NOT claim that every raw supplied H(z) is itself an arithmetic axiom.

These cases exhaust every successful call. Hence every accepted root is
a theorem of the native calculus. The same proof for the four-field AST
profile is the control's proof with the two opening/neutral-resource changes;
its major is explicit, and its original computation and mode guards remain.

5. Canonical completeness and certificate translation
----------------------------------------------------
Begin with the inherited canonical compiler for the chosen control. Its
expression trees use zero code 1, have binary equations at every formula
leaf, and use admissible term images. Therefore every eager image preview
needed in the new module succeeds and every substantive canonical opening
has its old literal value. Each administrative [] is already in the context
and closes immediately. No additional numerical name is allocated there:
the local floor is advanced at the same inference point as in the control.

For canonical three-field packets, replace a control source s=0 by [] only
in introductions, where u is false-valued. Preserve the difference between
u=0 (equation progress) and u=[] (cases). Leave computation rows (u true)
and closure-specialization rows unchanged. Canonical specialization targets
are numerical terms and encode zero by 1, so a canonical specialization
does not accidentally meet this introduction-row recoding. Its source 0 is
reconstructed into a nonempty Q major before a major proof is called.

For canonical four-field packets simply replace the explicit administrative
major m=0 by []; do not change sources, images, or mode markers.

All primitive native inferences therefore retain finite certificates in
the new modules. The word 'canonical' is essential: the simple recoding
function is not a theorem about all malformed auxiliary inputs. Soundness
for arbitrary accepted integer/list inputs comes from section 4, separately.

6. Interpretation, evidence and limitations
-------------------------------------------
The public native theory, its ordinary realization, and both interpretation
maps have not changed. Consequently the existing whole-domain actual-equality
bi-interpretation with full first-order iPRA transfers; both numerical
comparison graphs remain x=y and the existing formula round trips remain.
This is a transfer through preservation of the public theorem language, not
a new independent proof of all arithmetic definitions.

The delivered fixture run consists of 334 isolated executions under CPython
3.12.14, ordinary and -O: 65 three-field cases on each of two modules, and
37 four-field cases on the AST module. All expected results were obtained.
The cases include computations, closure and paired-binder reasoning, used
induction hypotheses, native atomic cases, false public goals, malformed
public syntax, future-name capture, and unused invalid JSON data. These
finite checks support the implementation; sections 2-5 are the universal
written correctness argument. Resource bounds are not identified between
implementations; completeness is for ideal finite executions.

The exact new normalized ASTs are separately checked against the frozen
upstream index and all currently downloaded incoming Python sources. Lack
of an identical program is not a claim of mathematical priority. In
particular both semantic devices above are explicitly inherited.
