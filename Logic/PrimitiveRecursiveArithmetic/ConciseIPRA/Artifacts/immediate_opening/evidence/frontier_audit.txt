EXCLUSIONS THAT MATTER

1. Packed fixed-payload wrappers: 18 tokens / 19 AST nodes / 459 bytes occur in
   incoming 502_scope_audit/illustrations. Both an old and a proposed checker
   pack to the same size. They are deliberately excluded from direct-source
   comparisons in that package and here. An exec/gzip wrapper is not a new
   inference-system reduction.
2. no_initial_open.py is a deliberately UNSOUND 250-token negative control.
   It removes `i(a)` from the entry scope scan. Its 17-packet certificate proves
   the closed canonical guarded formula D_{x,y}(I(y,0;u.x)=I(y,0;u.S(x))),
   which ordinarily entails that some x equals S(x). The original checker
   rejects. Preserve the entry scan in any refinement.
3. Other smaller source files are builders, measurement tools, converters,
   wrappers invoking larger modules, or reproduction/manifest scripts; they
   are not complete standalone acceptance modules.
