INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF DELAYED TARGET OPENING
=========================================

Status: the typed, scoped export lemma below is valid in intuitionistic
first-order logic, and the retained-equality opening identity follows from
the repository's sorted decoder. This does NOT by itself prove soundness,
completeness, or bi-interpretability of an entire new raw checker.

1. General derived rule
-----------------------

Let J=(j_1,...,j_r) be pairwise distinct numerical parameters absent from
Gamma and A. Let H be a formula with these parameters, and let sigma be a
simultaneous capture-avoiding substitution j_i -> u_i, where no u_i contains
a parameter of J. If

    Gamma |- H sigma          Gamma,H |- A,

then Gamma |- A. Introduce the r existential quantifiers from H sigma,
using u_1,...,u_r as witnesses, to obtain Gamma |- exists J H. Eliminate them
into the second derivation; its eigenvariable conditions hold because J is
absent from Gamma,A. This proof is intuitionistic and works for arbitrary H.
For r=0 it is just cut. The native scalar calculus may use its established
existential macros and admissible substitution/cut to implement this rule.

The right premise does not require H to be a theorem for arbitrary J.
For example, H(j) may be j=u; it has an old-language instance and is
existentially inhabited without being universally true. This is precisely
why treating delayed targets as ordinary unconditional theorems is wrong.

2. Retained equality branch
---------------------------

Suppose the old global floor is K and the minor check finishes at L<=K.
Let J be the finite new negative names in [L,K). The old caller context and
goal have already passed their old-scope checks and contain no meaningful
occurrence of J. Ignore raw occurrences inside semantically ignored
annotations; the sorted decoder maps them to constants or omits them.

Suppose the retained raw major [s,u] decodes to S=U, and its meaningful
numerical endpoints are closed in the old scope. Consequently S and U
contain no free name of J. Decode the raw template T at floor K as the
one-hole formula F(v). Successful old-source opening decodes to F(S), so
the recursively checked minor gives Gamma |- F(S). The retained equality
gives Gamma |- F(U) by ordinary equality transport.

Let H be the sorted decoding of the successful target opening O_L(T,u).
Then the identity needed by the export argument is

    H[J := U] = F(U),

where equality is literal equality of decoded formulas up to harmless
renaming of bound variables. It is NOT equality of raw arrays. It need not
be formulated by actually executing O_K(T,u): success of that extra raw
call is unnecessary.

Proof: structural induction through the sorted decoder. At a meaningful
numerical leaf, an old protected coordinate or old rigid parameter stays
the same. A coordinate newly admitted in J was an old hole and is now a
free parameter j; substitution restores U. Any remaining old hole remains
a hole and the successful new opening inserts U. Inserted U has no new
free name and is checked at its own numerical root, so insertion is
capture-free. The unary successor and ternary iterator cases follow with
their protected zero/state slots. Equation, D and Q follow with the
displayed slot environments. Scalar formula padding is Top at either
floor; ignored numerical arities denote zero; surplus formula arities and
the first two Q fields have no mathematical content. Those cases are
immediate even when their raw arrays differ substantially.

Thus Gamma |- H[J:=U]. The general derived rule discharges a continuation
Gamma,H |- A and yields Gamma |- A. The new J may be explicitly treated as
the existential aliases of the old term U.

3. Retained closure branch
--------------------------

If [T,T,T] is retained, its meaningful third child decodes to B(y), with no
new free parameters J. At old floor K, O_K(T,0) is B(0). A successful minor
therefore supplies B(0), and the retained closure Q_y B is B(0)->forall y B.
Hence Gamma proves forall y B.

A successful delayed target opening decodes to B(U_L), where U_L is a
well-formed numerical term in the enlarged scope. U_L may contain J. Give
each new parameter the old-language witness 0. Then H[J:=0] is
B(U_L[J:=0]), an instance of the established universal formula. Thus Gamma
proves exists J H, and the same continuation discharge is valid. In this
case H is in fact provable uniformly in J from the universal formula, but
the existential version provides one common scoped-export formulation.

4. Indispensable hypotheses
---------------------------

* The recursively checked minor must be a derivation from the original
  caller context. Its locally added assumptions must not leak back. The
  immutable tuple-context protocol satisfies this structural requirement.
* Every caller context formula and caller goal must be closed at its old
  floor at meaningful sorted positions. Freshness concerns decoded free
  variables, not arbitrary integers in ignored raw annotations.
* In the equality case, the retained endpoints must be closed at the old
  floor, so U is independent of J. Membership in a properly maintained
  checked context supplies this; it is not true for an arbitrary raw pair.
* Opening is interpreted through the complete sorted decoder. Its parity
  rules and ignored annotation cases cannot be replaced by an unsorted
  literal-substitution claim.
* Eigenvariables from the completed child proof may have the same printed
  integers as new parameters now used in the continuation. They belong to
  disjoint proof scopes. Alpha-renaming the child's bound eigenvariables
  prevents capture when assembling the full derivation.
* The new raw checker still needs a full global invariant and an induction
  covering every instruction branch, including automatic branch collisions,
  D opening, introduction mode selection, computation padding, continuation
  scope checks, and a separate completeness compiler. This lemma establishes
  one branch-preservation mechanism, not that complete theorem.

5. Relevance and novelty limit
-----------------------------

The logical derived rule is standard existential introduction/elimination.
The useful observation here is that a changed global floor can split an
old syntactic hole into several new rigid names, and all of them may be
existentially named by the old target term in the retained-equality branch.
This removes the particular semantic objection to delayed target opening
without pretending that two raw openings at different floors agree.
It is a scoped export lemma for this protocol, not a new arithmetic axiom
or a proof of a smaller complete iPRA presentation.
