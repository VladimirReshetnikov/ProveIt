INDEPENDENT MATHEMATICAL AUDIT OF POSITIVE ITERATION NOTE
======================================================
Source reviewed: theory_positive_iteration.tex (10 November 2026).
Review status: written mathematical audit by another assistant instance;
not external human peer review or proof-assistant verification.

Verdict: the displayed theorems and constructions are valid with the stated
standard-natural-number scope. No counterexample or substantive proof gap
was found. It is safe to include as a new standard-algebra research section,
while keeping it separate from the smaller full iPRA checker and from claims
about arbitrary models of the eager equations.

1. Classification
The constant/projection/growth alternatives are disjoint and exhaustive by
the given structural induction. A crucial fact is that in the nonprojection
state-dependent case, the update is strictly increasing in its state and
uniformly at least state+1. Thus positive iteration cannot make a seed or
counter dependency disappear. If a passive coordinate is essential in the
update, the first update already makes its effect strict; at least one update
is performed even for counter zero. Extra changes through seed/counter/other
passive roles only add nondecreasing effects. The set union U_n ∪ U_a ∪ V is
therefore exact, not merely an upper bound on dependence. The output lower
bound A+N+1 treats seed/counter coordinates; the final update treats passive
update coordinates. Constants need not be evaluated for the classification
algorithm because no branch depends on their exact value. Fixed numerical
parameters may safely be replaced by their finite numeral terms.

2. Tuple-domain actual-equality obstruction
For a coordinate i varying on D, a unit law rules out an output coordinate
function depending on only one whole input tuple. The coordinate function
is consequently in the growth class. At least one essential left coordinate
j_i must vary on D; otherwise f_i(z,e) would be constant there. The right
unit law then gives z_i≥z_j_i+1 for every z∈D. A directed cycle in the finite
nonempty set of varying coordinates is impossible. This argument does not
need D to be definable, and the d=0 case is correctly separated. The use of
actual coordinatewise equality is essential and is not accidentally extended
to quotient equality.

3. Pairing
q(a,b)=a+b+1 and d(b)=2b+1. The seed S(d(b)) is 2b+2. Iterating v↦2v+1 exactly
a+1 times gives π(a,b)+1=2^(a+1)(2b+3). The odd factor recovers b after the
2-adic valuation recovers a. The numerical examples π(1,0)=11<π(0,2)=13 and
π(0,1)=9<π(1,0)=11 are correct and separately disprove monotone unary inverse
terms for the first and second coordinates. No hidden decoder is supplied.

4. Quotient ring
m(a,b)=F(a,0;u.q(u,b))=(a+1)(b+1). The atomic equivalence q(a,d)=q(c,b) is exactly
a-b=c-d in standard N. The two coordinate differences in the displayed sum
and product are respectively (a-b)+(c-d) and (a-b)(c-d); the extra constants
cancel. The quotient is Z, zero is [(0,0)], and negation swaps coordinates.
Minor presentational completion suggested: explicitly identify the
multiplicative unit as [(1,0)]. The nonnegative-difference formula a=b or
exists r(q(b,r)=a) is exact and invariant under the equivalence.

5. Scope and finite model
The standard identities do not prove these quotient facts internally in the
bare eager theory. In the cited two-element monotone-update model, S is
constant1, q is constant1, the displayed E is universal, and the quotient
collapses to one element. This correctly explains why the construction does
not repair the full-theory interpretation obstruction. Adding successor
injectivity excludes this particular model but is not claimed to prove a
new iPRA interpretation. No extension to arbitrary relational operations or
quotient equality is smuggled into the actual-equality obstruction.

Research significance and qualification
The exact dependence classification is a genuine additional structural result
relative to the stated weaker standard zero-profile invariant, and the pairing
and quotient examples sharply delimit the negative result. This is not a
smaller full iPRA axiomatization and should not be described as resolving the
requested foundational breakthrough. Literature-wide novelty is not established
by the local corpus search. All these limitations are already expressed in
the reviewed note.
