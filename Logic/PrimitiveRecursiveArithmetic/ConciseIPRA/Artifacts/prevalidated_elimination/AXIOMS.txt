COMPLETE NATIVE PRESENTATION
===========================
Numerical expressions:
  t ::= variable | 0 | S(t) | I(n,a;v.h).
Only v in h is bound. Every update is fixed finite earlier-stage syntax.
Passive parameters remain fixed. There is no numerical universal evaluator.

Native formulas:
  A ::= (s=t) | Q_y A | D_{x,y} A.
Designated absurdity: 0=S0.
Ordinary meanings:
  Q_y B = B(0) -> forall y B(y).
  D_{x,y} B = exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y)).
These meanings describe an interpretation, not primitive ordinary connectives.

NONLOGICAL COMPUTATIONS
  I(0,a;v.h)=a, if a is not syntactically literal zero.
  I(Sn,a;v.h)=h[I(n,a;v.h)/v], if neither n nor a is literal zero.
The restrictions are admissible: C=I(2,1;v.0)=0 is a direct allowed
computation. Replace any forbidden seed or predecessor by C and transport
C=0 once. This recovers every unrestricted computation instance.

ALL PRIMITIVE LOGICAL RULES
  Hyp: Gamma |- A when A occurs in Gamma.
  Tr: Gamma |- s=t and Gamma |- F(s) imply Gamma |- F(t).
      F is an arbitrary native formula template.
  QE: Gamma |- Q_y B and Gamma |- B(0) imply Gamma |- B(t).
  DE: Gamma |- D_{x,y} B and Gamma,Q_y B(a,y) |- A imply Gamma |- A.
      The witness a is fresh for retained context, conclusion, and parameters.
  Case DI: Gamma,B(t,0) |- B(t,Sz) implies Gamma |- D_{x,y} B.
  Progress DI: Gamma,E(t,z) |- E(t,Sz) implies Gamma |- D_{x,y} E,
      only when E is a literal equation. Compound progress is forbidden.
      In both introductions z is fresh for Gamma, t, and passive parameters.
      Local hypotheses are discharged; substitution avoids capture.

IMPLEMENTED COMPUTATION SUPPLY (DERIVED RULE)
  If H(0,...,0) is one of the generated computation theorems and b are fresh
  for Gamma,A, a proof Gamma,H(b) |- A yields Gamma |- A by simultaneous
  zero substitution and cut. The checker implements only its explicit
  computation generator, not a general theorem recognizer.

DERIVED RESULTS
  Equality reflexivity/symmetry/transitivity/congruence; numerical cases;
  contextual equation induction; all ordinary intuitionistic logical rules;
  predecessor; reflection of numerical equality; quantifier-free induction;
  pairing and every fixed primitive recursion.

EQUIVALENCE AND LIMIT
  Both interpretations use the whole numerical domain and actual equality.
  Numerical return isomorphisms have graph x=y. Q/D formula returns are
  internally proved. Hence this presentation is equiconsistent and
  bi-interpretable with full first-order iPRA.
  The axiomatization is inherited. The present new contribution is a smaller
  checker via prevalidated elimination and guarded internal sentinels.
  No absolute minimum or significant new arithmetic foundation is claimed.
  See the report for complete written proofs and their inherited attribution.
