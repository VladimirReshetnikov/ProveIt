CURRENT INCOMING NOVELTIES THAT MUST NOT BE REPEATED AS NEW

- single_binder: U_x A(x) := D_{u,z} G_z A(P(z)); one paired binder and one
  copy of A. Predecessor plus gate provides introduction and elimination.
  Exact expanded formula counts show exponential-to-linear growth for the
  stated iterated-universal example. This improves macros, not axiom count.
- constantfree: numerical terms x | J(n;i,r.h), no numerical constants,
  successor, initializer, pairing or equality test primitive. An anchored
  graph theory derives unique zero and gives whole-domain bi-interpretations.
  Its transparent checker is larger than the scalar comparator.
- guarded_register: scalar J(n;u.h) with no independent successor or
  initializer, a restricted body grammar and two computation equations.
- three_field: infer elimination major from endpoint, reuse a packet field
  and clear the initializer to dispatch major checking. Different global
  and local fresh-name protocols are measured separately.
- retained_premises: retain checked major premises, repair dispatcher
  collisions with fresh initializer aliases; raw variable-only substitution
  without reflexivity cannot derive 0=0.
- verified_refinements: making Q-rooted matrices eligible for progressive
  introduction yields induction for arbitrary formulas, an unsafe extension.
- progress_independence: written nonstandard-model independence of progress,
  using staged ultrapowers; this is already a submitted claim and not a
  result to advertise anew. It has not been independently proof-assistant
  verified by this audit.
- 502_scope_audit: deleting the entry goal scan lets a 17-packet certificate
  prove a closed guarded encoding of some number being its own successor.
  The negative control is deliberately unsound.

OPEN DIRECTIONS IN THE INDEX (SOME WRITTEN PACKAGES GO BEYOND LEAN)

- A shorter internal predecessor construction is a major unsolved source
  of mathematical proof complexity; present scalar proofs use parity, a
  mod-three ramp, factor map, comparator and saturating counter.
- A shorter formula-level conjunction/selector construction is also open.
- On raw terms, variable-only substitution without root joining is weaker
  in the bare form; full normal forms recover equality through naming packets.
- Deterministic newest-parameter introduction exists in written packages,
  but the corresponding exact deterministic formalization remains incomplete.
- Many recursor alternatives have written bridges but need their own Lean
  syntax/interpreters. The existing Lean proof does not automatically verify
  their Python checkers or their newly chosen recursors.
