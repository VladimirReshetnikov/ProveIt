1. EXACT TARGET THEORY

The target is full FIRST-ORDER INTUITIONISTIC primitive recursive arithmetic,
not merely its equational theorem fragment, not classical PRA, and not HA.
PR.lean defines one symbol for every finite primitive-recursive DESCRIPTION:
zero (arity 0), successor (arity 1), projections, composition, and primitive
recursion. Distinct descriptions remain distinct symbols even when their
standard functions agree. PTm consists of variables and applications.
PRAAx consists of projection, composition and the two recursion equations,
S(x)=0 -> bottom, S(x)=S(y) -> x=y, and induction for quantifier-free formulas.
Induction is the axiom schema
  A(0) -> ((forall n (A(n) -> A(S n))) -> forall n A(n)).
QF formulas contain equality, bottom, conjunction, disjunction and implication;
negation is implication to bottom. The logic has unrestricted quantifiers.
Deduction.lean deeply embeds ordinary intuitionistic natural deduction with
equality reflexivity and simultaneous Leibniz substitution. In particular
there is no object-language excluded-middle rule. The derived contextual
induction rule allows arbitrary, even quantified, side assumptions Gamma,
provided the fresh induction variable is absent from Gamma.

2. ORDINARY SCALAR THEORY T_eq

Terms: variables, 0, S(t), I(c,a;u.h), with u bound only in h. Ordinary
intuitionistic first-order logic with actual equality. Primitive axioms:
  I(0,a;u.h)=a;
  I(S c,a;u.h)=h[I(c,a;u.h)/u];
  x=0 or exists y (x=S y);
  0=S 0 -> bottom;
  the preceding induction schema restricted to literal equations A.
These are exactly TeqAx in Scalar.lean. Successor injectivity and general
S(x) != 0 are DERIVED. Separation is essential: a one-element algebra
validates the remaining axioms. The public native falsity 0=S0 must therefore
be explicitly connected to ordinary bottom in this realization.

3. EFFICIENT ARITHMETIC BRIDGE OUTLINE

All auxiliary symbols below are finite terms, not additional primitives.
Write C(c,a,b)=I(c,a;u.b), 1=S0, Z(c)=C(c,1,0),
  N(c)=I(c,0;u.Z(u)),       a+b=I(b,a;u.Su),
  pow2(c)=I(c,1;u.u+u).
C selects a at zero and b at every successor; N is parity. Equation induction
derives the addition laws and N(2a)=0, N(S(2a))=1, N(3a)=N(a).

A completely explicit inherited predecessor (75 nodes in the article's
expansion convention) avoids assuming pairing or primitive recursion:
  j(u)=Z(u)+2N(u);
  M(n)=I(n,0;u.j(u));
  G(n)=I(n,0;u.S(u+M(u)));
  H(u)=C(N(u),G(u),u);
  L(x,y)=N(I(y,pow2(x);u.H(u)));
  beta(k,u)=C(L(S(Su),k),u,Su);
  W(t,k)=I(t,0;u.beta(k,u));       P(m)=W(m,m).
Here 2a=a+a and 3a=(a+a)+a; neither multiplication nor predecessor occurs in
these definitions. Key equation-induction lemmas, in dependency order:
  j(0)=1, j(1)=2, j(2)=0;
  M(G(n))=N(n), G(Sn)=S(G(n)+N(n)), G(S(Sn))=S(S(S(G(n))));
  G(2n)=3n, G(3n)=3G(n)+N(n);
  H(2n)=3n, H(1)=1, H(3n)=3H(n);
  I(y,3a;u.Hu)=3I(y,a;u.Hu), I(y,1;u.Hu)=1;
  L(0,y)=1, L(Sx,0)=0, L(Sx,Sy)=L(x,y), L(Sn,n)=0.
The comparator step uses iterator rotation
I(Sy,a;u.Hu)=I(y,H(a);u.Hu), derived by equation induction.
Now beta(Sk,Su)=S(beta(k,u)) and beta(S(Sk),0)=1. Induct on t to get
W(St,S(Sk))=S(W(t,Sk)); then induct on n to get W(n,Sn)=n. Consequently
P(0)=0 and P(Sx)=beta(Sx,x)=x. Congruence gives successor injectivity.
These derivations use only computation, equality and equation induction.
The potentially tempting Boolean case splits can be replaced by the proved
interpolation F(Z(c))=C(c,F(1),F(0)) and the two-point rule for b satisfying
Z(Z(b))=b; see EqParity, EqCompare and EqPred. In ordinary T_eq, numerical
coverage also permits those case splits, as in TeqParity/TeqPred. This order
prevents circular use of names, pairing, or equality reflection.

Define a dotminus b = I(b,a;u.P(u)). Congruence through Z and separation
give Sx != 0. Coverage plus P(Sx)=x gives x=0 or x=S(P(x)). Equation
induction and numerical cases prove
  (a+b) dotminus a=b,
  a dotminus(a+b)=0,
  a dotminus b=0 -> a+(b dotminus a)=b.
Use the symmetric equality characteristic employed in Lean:
  e(a,b)=C(a dotminus b, b dotminus a, 1).
If e(a,b)=0, cases on a dotminus b force it to vanish, then b dotminus a
also vanishes, and the displayed order identity yields a=b. Conversely
e(a,a)=0. Thus e(a,b)=0 iff a=b over the WHOLE domain. Coverage on e(a,b)
also gives decidability of atomic equality without classical logic.

For each QF formula define its zero-true characteristic term recursively:
  chi(s=t)=e(s,t);                chi(bottom)=1;
  chi(A and B)=C(chi(A),chi(B),1);
  chi(A or B)=C(chi(A),0,chi(B));
  chi(A -> B)=C(chi(A),chi(B),0).
Induction on syntax, using term cases, proves chi(A)=0 iff A in every context.
Literal-equation induction on chi(A(n))=0 therefore gives all QF induction.
This includes implication: its converse uses cases on chi(A), not excluded
middle for an arbitrary quantified formula.

The Lean pairing avoids a vague appeal to generic bounded search:
  step(k,v)=C(pow2(Sv) dotminus k,Sv,v);
  log(w)=I(w,0;v.step(w,v));
  pair(a,b)=pow2(a+b)+a;
  fst(w)=w dotminus pow2(log(w));
  snd(w)=log(w) dotminus fst(w).
The counter reaches a+b and stays there on pair(a,b), so log(pair(a,b))=a+b,
fst(pair(a,b))=a, snd(pair(a,b))=b. The exact invariants and equation-induction
proofs are TeqOrder/TeqPair and article section 'Order, powers of two and
pairing'. Pairing need not be surjective and DOES NOT restrict the domain.

Compile genuine counter-aware primitive recursion by carrying the counter:
  A(c)=I(c,pair(0,a);w.pair(S(fst(w)),g(fst(w),snd(w))));
  R(c,a;y,v.g)=snd(A(c)).
One equation induction gives fst(A(c))=c. Computation and projections then
give R(0,a;g)=a and R(Sc,a;g)=g(c,R(c,a;g)). The syntax-recursive compiler
sigma maps PR zero/successor/projection literally, composition by substitution,
and prec to R. It respects substitution literally. All defining equations,
successor axioms and QF induction translate to theorems of T_eq.

In the other direction tau codes each iterator term (and its finite free
variable context) as a PR definition. The simulated recursion step ignores
its counter and applies the code of the update body to the preceding value.
Tau respects substitution UP TO PROVABLE EQUALITY, not necessarily literally,
because the body code is part of a function-symbol description. A single
parameter-relative recursion-uniqueness lemma, proved by QF induction,
establishes this property. ScalarToPRA's ToPRA.prec_uniq is the exact lemma.
Coverage in iPRA is derived via its ordinary PR predecessor and QF induction;
the remaining scalar axioms translate immediately.

For round trips: induction on scalar term syntax plus uniqueness of iteration
proves T_eq |- sigma(tau(t))=t. Induction on PR descriptions proves
iPRA |- tau(sigma(f(x)))=f(x); the prec step is equation/QF induction using
the translated recursion equations. Extend to arbitrary terms by congruence.
Term round trips imply formula round trips by structural induction, including
implication and BOTH quantifiers. This is stronger than merely mutual
interpretability or equiconsistency.

4. NATIVE LOGICAL BRIDGE

The native scalar calculus has equations, Q_y B and D_(x,y) B, with readings
  Q_y B := B(0) -> forall y B(y),
  D_(x,y) B := exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y)).
The eight-rule Lean NPrf has Hyp, two computations, crossed one-term transport,
QE, DE, DI by cases, and QI for equation progress. It is equivalent to the
reports' IPrf with arbitrary-formula transport and witnessed progress.

Formula selection C(v,A,B) pushes numeric selection to the terminal equation,
aligning unmatched binders with gate(y,X)=C(y,0=0,X). Its proved laws are
C(0,A,B) equivalent to A, C(Sv,A,B) equivalent to B, gate(0,B) provable,
and Q_y gate(y,B) equivalent to B (with y fresh). Define
  bottom*=0=S0;
  A ->* B = Q_y C(y,A,B);
  A and* B = Q_y gate(y,C(N(y),A,B));
  exists* x A = D_(x,y) gate(y,A);
  A or* B = exists* x C(x,A,B);
  forall* x A = A(0) and* Q_x A.
The inherited proof derives all ordinary intuitionistic natural-deduction
rules for these macros. Native DI needs ordinary coverage in its soundness
proof; equation QI needs only equation induction. No full formula induction
schema is introduced. Conversely the macros translate every T_eq axiom and
logical rule to a native derivation. Structural induction proves both native
and ordinary formula round trips. In particular native falsity corresponds
to ordinary bottom using separation; omission of this step would be a gap.

Compose the native logical translations with tau and sigma. Variables are
fixed, both numerical domains are the whole domain, quantifiers are never
relativized, equality is actual equality, and BOTH numerical composite
isomorphisms have graph x=y. Consistency equivalence follows by translating
bottom (native bottom being 0=S0); no self-consistency claim is involved.
