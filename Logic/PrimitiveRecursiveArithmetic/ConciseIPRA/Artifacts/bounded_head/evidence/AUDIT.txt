CONCEPTUAL AUDIT
The weak-head proof retains raw inference hints deliberately. The stronger law
W(t[s/x])=W(t[W(s)/x]) is false, e.g. t=Sx. Its proof instead uses only
W(W(t)[sigma])=W(t[sigma]), so this apparent issue is correctly handled.
The fused predecessor's Boolean interpolation avoids implicit disjunctive cases
or compound induction. The finite cycle update J(v)=I(Sv,v;w.C(w,Sv,0)) indeed
has expanded size 9 and values 0->1->2->0. No gap identified in the written proof.
The raw-variable-substitution countermodel distinguishes proof variables from
neutral numerical binder values and handles the needed recolouring facts explicitly.
No defect identified that would justify discarding its open-sequent obstruction.
