INDEPENDENT AUDIT: RAW VARIABLE SUBSTITUTION WITH BOUNDED HEAD CONVERSION

Scope: raw terms x,0,S,I(n,a;u.h); raw formulas Eq,Q,D. No normalizer.
Primitive families H,R,V,QE,DE,Intro,HC, seven families total.
HC changes only the left equation endpoint by one forward or reverse I0/IS
computation in a counter-spine context with at most three enclosing iterator
counter edges. Depth zero means the root. Every supplied term is well scoped,
and the IS contraction uses simultaneous capture-avoiding substitution.

VERDICT
I independently reconstructed the equivalence proof. No gap found. The proof
does not require arbitrary-term substitution into derivations, which V and the
witness-sensitive Intro do not support by a direct structural induction.
It does require weakening, fresh injective renaming and cut, all of which admit
the usual direct induction on derivations before equality is recovered.

1. EQUALITY BOOTSTRAP WITHOUT TRANSPORT
Let A(x,y) be C(y,0,x)=0, with C(y,a,b)=I(y,a;u.b).
Introduce D_xy A at raw witness 0. Intro selects progress; its target
C(Sz,0,0)=0 follows from R:0=0 by one reverse HC step at depth zero.
The local progress assumption is unused. Thus D_xy A is a theorem.
Open it at fresh b. Its zero row C(0,0,b)=0 follows from R and one reverse HC.
QE at 1 gives C(1,0,b)=0, then one HC gives b=0.
Given s=t, weaken its proof into this branch and apply V with source b,
replacement 0, raw minor s=t. Because b was chosen fresh for s,t, the output
is exactly t=s. DE exports it. This derives symmetry at arbitrary contexts.
Consequently a right-endpoint HC is symmetry, left HC, symmetry. There is no
appeal to the transport rule being recovered.

2. EQUATIONAL FREE-BRANCH CASES BEFORE TRANSPORT
From Gamma,E(z) |- E(Sz), introduce D_xy E(y) at the literal witness zero,
with x unused, then DE using its closure hypothesis to derive Q_y E.
If Gamma proves E(0) and Gamma proves E(Sz) for fresh z, weaken the latter by
the unused hypothesis E(z), derive Q_y E, and use QE at any raw term r.
No substitution of r into an existing derivation is involved.

3. PACKET INTRODUCTION
Let Z(v)=C(v,1,0), P(v)=I(v,0;u.Z(u)), and
Pi(x,y) = C(y,0,C(P(y),x,h(x))) = C(y,0,C(P(y),t,r)).
Assume Gamma |- s=t and Gamma |- h(s)=r, with x reserved as a template hole.
For E(v)=C(v,s,h(s))=C(v,t,r), E(0) follows by two endpoint HC expansions
from s=t; E(Sw) follows similarly from h(s)=r. The free-branch case rule at
P(Sz) gives E(P(Sz)). Two root endpoint HC expansions give Pi(s,Sz).
Thus Intro at s derives D Pi, ignoring its local assumption in either mode.

4. EXACT DEPTH BOUND FOR THE THREE ROWS
Pi(x,0) reduces to 0=0 using root HC only.
For Pi(x,1), after the outer C contraction, each endpoint has shape
  C(P(1),a,b)
  -> C(Z(P(0)),a,b)       [depth 1]
  -> C(Z(0),a,b)          [depth 2]
  -> C(1,a,b)             [depth 1]
  -> b                    [depth 0].
For Pi(x,2), after the outer C contraction, each endpoint has shape
  C(P(2),a,b)
  -> C(Z(P(1)),a,b)       [depth 1]
  -> C(Z(Z(P(0))),a,b)    [depth 2]
  -> C(Z(Z(0)),a,b)       [depth 3]
  -> C(Z(1),a,b)          [depth 2]
  -> C(0,a,b)             [depth 1]
  -> a                    [depth 0].
Every arrow is exactly one allowed computation contraction; the final
contraction on the previous-to-last line is the outer Z with successor
counter. Abbreviations stand for expanded raw syntax, not derived equations.
Thus rows 0,1,2 are respectively 0=0, h(x)=r, x=t. Neither h, s, t nor r
is reduced internally. A raw hint may have arbitrarily deep computation
redexes, without affecting the uniform bound for these packet computations.

5. TRANSPORT
Open D Pi at fresh b, derive its zero row from R and inverse HC, and apply
QE at 1 and 2. The bounded conversions above give h(b)=r and b=t.
V gives r=h(t), and DE discharges b. This proves the crossed one-term
transport rule for arbitrary raw templates and terms.
Reflexivity plus this rule gives all ordinary equational reasoning; two
one-term transports give transport in a literal-equation template.

6. RECOVERING NATIVE RULES
Both raw computation equations follow from R by reverse root HC.
Direct QI-progress was recovered in step 2.
Only native DI-by-cases on an equation at raw witness 0 is missing. Replace
the witness by the closed raw alias a=I(0,0;u.0), which is not literally 0.
Its equality a=0 follows from R and root HC. Under B(a,0), transport to
B(0,0), cut the given case derivation, then transport its conclusion back
to B(a,Sz). Intro at a selects cases and gives the required D formula.
Compound matrices already select cases at all witnesses.
Thus all native rules are recovered on the same raw sequents. Formula-wide
transport can then be inherited from native equivalence; it need not be
proved prematurely or used in this bootstrap.

7. REVERSE DIRECTION
Native equality and computation justify each HC step (at any allowed
counter-spine depth) and V. Its case and equation-progress rules justify
the two Intro modes. All other primitive rules are native or derived there.
Therefore both translations preserve exactly every open native sequent.
Bi-interpretability follows by composition with the existing full native-iPRA
bridge, not merely from equal consistency strength. The native syntax and
ordinary interpretation remain unchanged, so the first equivalence is identity.

IMPLEMENTATION RISKS TO CHECK
- Count the root as depth zero and allow depth three, inclusive.
- HC is one syntactic step, not a comparison of fully normalized terms.
- HC may expand as well as contract, but every expansion must validate one
  corresponding legal contraction, including capture avoidance.
- Right-endpoint conversion is derived, not a hidden primitive.
- V's source is a free literal numerical variable, never an arbitrary term
  or a bound index captured by substitution.
- Intro tests the RAW witness against literal 0; no conversion occurs there.
- Do not assert arbitrary-term substitution admissibility by direct induction.
- Proof of equivalence is mathematical; finite program tests do not formally
  verify the checker or prove global source minimality.
