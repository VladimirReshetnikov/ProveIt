INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE CONSTANT-FREE PRESENTATION

Scope: the written constant-free mathematics, the adapted standalone checker,
and the four explicit certificates for U(t)=1_a, Z(t)=Z(a), existence of a
fixed point of Z, and uniqueness of that fixed point. This is a same-session
independent assistant review, not external peer review or proof-assistant
formalization of the complete bi-interpretation.

MATHEMATICAL CHECKS

1. The fixed-anchor proof of constants is noncircular. Write 0=Z(a),
   S(t)=J(t;i,r.Z(r)), and 1=S(0). This successor abbreviation contains no
   anchor parameter. CF0 gives K(0)=1. The increment branch
   gives K(1)=S(1). CF0 gives U(0)=1, and the load branch under U(n)=1
   gives U(S(n))=1 because the instruction is K(1)=S(1). The only induction
   invariant is the literal equation U(x)=1. The load branch for
   B(n)=J(n;i,r.r), at n=0, gives B(1)=0. Thus Z(t)=B(U(t))=0=Z(a).
   The proof does not use successor injectivity, separation, or coverage.

2. Parameter-free zero is represented by a definable element, not a closed
   term. Z(t)=Z(a) implies Z(Z(a))=Z(a). In a nonempty numerical domain,
   an arbitrary parameter a therefore supplies the existential witness
   Z(a), and the parameter can be discharged because the existential
   conclusion has no free occurrence of a. Two fixed points are equal by
   the anchor-independence theorem. This justifies the temporary unique
   definable-constant extension in the interpretation proof.

3. The register bootstrap remains valid with this zero. For constant
   instruction f(n,v)=J(n;i,r.v), CF0 and the load direction give
   f(0,v)=1 and f(S(n),S(v))=v. The equality f(n,0)=S(n) is now a
   theorem rather than a definition: the proved equation Z(r)=Z(a)
   identifies the two instruction bodies, and body extensionality gives
   equality of the computations. Body extensionality requires only
   equation induction and the graph schema, so this change is noncircular.
   The forward graph direction, instantiated at the actual output
   W(v)=f(1,v), gives v=0 or v=S(W(v)), establishing coverage.
   The stated q, D, p, G and C terms have the advertised equations.
   In particular p(S(v))=v supplies injectivity, and q separates
   successor values from zero using CFsep.

4. The primitive-recursion compiler is internally justified by two load
   instances. At old counter 0 it loads b; at old counter S(n) it loads
   h(n,r), because the selector and predecessor equations already hold.
   No induction on a non-equation is needed to establish its defining
   equations. Equality reflection then uses literal-equation inductions;
   coverage supplies constructive case splits inside those derivations.

5. Both interpretation composites have a sound route. In iPRA, the fixed J
   descriptor is a primitive-recursive function with its increment/load
   transition. In the constant-free theory, the compiler recovers each
   primitive-recursive descriptor. Pointwise body equality implies equality
   of J outputs by equation induction: forward graph reasoning for one
   output transports its disjunct to the other graph, whose backward
   direction identifies that output. This also justifies binding syntax as
   a notation for ordinary fixed first-order descriptors.

6. The translation using exists z (z=Z(z) and A_z) is justified by provable
   existence and uniqueness, including intuitionistic implication and
   quantifiers. It is equivalent to a compositional function-graph
   interpretation; it is not an appeal to unrestricted choice. Both
   numerical domains can remain whole and both equality relations actual.

CHECKER REVIEW

7. Macro construction avoids capture. J binds only in its body, not its
   counter. Simultaneous substitution renames conflicting body binders
   before inserting free variables. Alpha comparison keeps free names
   distinct and respects shadowing. Dedicated finite tests cover capture,
   the external counter scope, and anchors named like generated binders.

8. Every anchor is validated in the current numerical scope. The J0 and
   graph rules compare their complete claimed instances with the expanded
   macro formulas. The induction anchor, target term, and retained context
   are fixed before introducing the induction eigenvariable. The invariant
   is explicitly required to be a literal equation.

9. Param is sound in ordinary nonempty-domain intuitionistic first-order
   logic. Its conclusion is validated in the outer scope, so the fresh
   numerical parameter cannot escape. Universal introduction and existential
   elimination use the same scope discipline. All contexts arise from
   previously validated formulas; freshness relative to scope therefore
   implies freshness relative to retained free assumptions.

10. The JSON front end rejects duplicate object keys and nonstandard numeric
    constants. Proof nodes have exact field sets, and hypothesis indices
    require actual integers rather than Python booleans. An uncaught malformed
    input exception, if any, would be a rejected CLI run because acceptance
    requires status zero and exactly ACCEPT on stdout.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS

The reproducible validation script checks 19 certificate cases under ordinary
Python and -O, for 38 CLI executions, plus 6 capture/substitution checks.
The four delivered mathematical proofs and one pure logical nonempty-domain
proof are accepted. Fourteen altered or malformed certificates are rejected,
including compound induction with otherwise matching premises, a future
eigenvariable used as an anchor, and existence without a parameter scope.
See validation.json for exact case descriptions, payload hashes, and results.

The complete normalized new checker is larger than the matched register-graph
control. The mathematical contribution is deletion of a primitive numerical
constant from the term grammar; it is not a smaller complete checker, a
proof of absolute minimality, or a proof-assistant verification of the entire
bi-interpretation. The compact-profile improvements elsewhere in the archive
are independent implementation compositions on the old scalar calculus.
