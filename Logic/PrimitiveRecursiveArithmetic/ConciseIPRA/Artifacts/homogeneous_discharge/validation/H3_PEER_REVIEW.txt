Independent review of the homogeneous partial-discharge construction
==================================================================

Status: the written mathematical reduction is sound on the stated raw
term/formula syntax. This is a review, not a proof-assistant verification.
It does not establish a checker-size record against the full repository.

1. The three rows of partial evaluation have the advertised ordinary
   arithmetic/logical readings. The first two are exactly the raw Eval
   rows. The compound-first-premise row exports an already proved F.
   Requiring the exported result to omit the opened parameter is essential.

2. H3 to H4: an arithmetic discharge expands to Eval within the opened
   clause followed by Open; a compound discharge expands to Open and drops
   the unused compound auxiliary proof. Scope side conditions coincide.

3. H4 to H3: Theta = D_xy(0=0) has an Intro+Use proof in every context
   containing the reflexivity seed. Eval is a discharge of Theta with its
   two premises weakened into the fresh scope. Open is a compound discharge
   with a weakened proof of its compound major (or of Theta) as the unused
   auxiliary. No cut admissibility theorem is presupposed.

4. The diagonal introduction requires a nontrivial adaptation of the
   homogeneous gate-access proof. To recover A=D B from kappa_1 A, an
   introduction at a fresh witness can be progressive if B is diagonal.
   Then B(b,0) follows from zero computations, so it can replace the zero
   assumption used by the original case-introduction proof. The progressive
   assumption is ignored. Conversely kappa_1 B itself is not diagonal.

5. The proposed seed-only Use refinement is valid. The fixed first context
   entry rho(v)=(v=v) may be used without a premise. Every other entry must
   have an explicit proof of its zero instance. Every D^j(r=r) is derivable
   by induction on j: use rho at j=0; otherwise Intro at witness 0, ignoring
   the gate assumption, and recurse on the successor instance, whose leaf
   is still literally reflexive. This works in either introduction mode.
   Thus every former omitted-premise Use can be expanded. The reflexive-leaf
   predicate can be removed from the trusted checker without circularity.

6. The positive de Bruijn implementation was checked independently against
   its declared binder order. In a D body the indices 1 and 2 are y and x.
   branch_context lifts ambient parameters while retaining each clause's
   distinguished index 1. Gate inserts its distinguished parameter outside
   all nested D binders and shifts the fresh iterator variable correctly.
   export rejects exactly the newly opened ambient variable, also under
   binders. Source guards reject noninteger variable aliases.

7. Assertion-based checkers must refuse Python -O. The current modules do
   so explicitly. Otherwise an Intro whose proof exists only in an assert
   expression could accept the false ordinary formula D_xy(y=0).

8. h3_independent_audit.py carries out 7,200 deterministic generated checks
   of export/lift, gate naturality, specialization naturality, gate-zero
   reflexivity, gate-one computations, and diagonal renaming. It also checks
   rejection of the false Intro and refusal of optimized execution. These
   are finite regression checks and do not prove universal correctness.

Remaining limits
----------------
The full iPRA interpretation and numerical round trips are inherited from
the repository's native bridge. The new comparison is a proof translation
to that native system, not a new mechanization of its arithmetic. A final
report must distinguish a matched source reduction from the much smaller
compact/raw/stream validation-contract frontiers in the current repository,
including the incoming 239-token and 256-node candidates.
