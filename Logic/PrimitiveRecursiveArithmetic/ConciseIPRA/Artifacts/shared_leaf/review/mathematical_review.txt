INDEPENDENT MATHEMATICAL REVIEW: RESOURCE-LEAF FUSION

Baseline: computation_leaves, pinned repository 12197d86f63e9ec73b2d91399c4de2a8cb0077ee.
Candidate inspected: audit_work/membership_bytes.py (470-byte draft).

1. Honest mathematical claim
Replace the three leaf schemata Hyp, I0, IS by the single interface Init:
    A belongs to Gamma union {C}  ==>  Gamma proves A,
where C is one supplied instance of I0 or IS. The other rule families are
unchanged. This is a proof-presentation reduction: both computation equations
remain axioms. It does not establish independence, deletion, or a smaller
mathematical axiom basis, and does not establish a global complexity minimum.

2. All-open-sequent equivalence
Old to new: an old computation leaf supplies its own equation C; an old
hypothesis leaf supplies a fixed harmless theorem C0 = I(0,0;u.0)=0.
New to old: if the leaf equation is in Gamma, use Hyp; otherwise use I0 or IS
according to its supplied computation instance. Non-leaf rules and all
eigenvariable conditions are unchanged. The translations preserve the
conclusion and the entire open context. They preserve renamed and
admissibly substituted open sequents; literal commutation of proof trees with
substitution is not required or claimed. Hence the old ordinary
realization and its bi-interpretation with iPRA transfer without changing
domain formulas, equality, or comparison graphs.

3. Deterministic resource-packet version
A certificate packet denotes H(s,u,t). It may be sound to recognize only
a computation fragment in which the successor predecessor is not the literal
zero, provided the inherited known-zero supply and canonical completeness
argument are retained. Thus do not advertise packet H as literally containing
all I0/IS instances; say its canonical proofs realize the full axiom family.

4. Eager byte opener
In the inspected lambda profile the no-image sentinel is the checker function
c. Every scalar outside k <= a < d enters d%2 * i(c,d=3). Multiplication is
eager, and i(c,d=3) fails. Consequently, whenever i(a) with no image succeeds
on a finite exact-integer/list tree, it equals a literally. Therefore changing
the direct-computation equality a == H into membership i(a) in g+[H]
preserves its literal meaning. This addresses a subtle difference from the
lazy token/AST profiles.

5. Lazy opener
The lazy no-image opener can replace an out-of-scope scalar at even/formula
depth by zero. It is nevertheless a partial retraction: if O(a) succeeds,
O(O(a)) succeeds and equals O(a). Its successful output preserves the sorted
reading of a. If O(a)=H, then H is no-image validated, and the inherited
direct-leaf lemma makes its sorted reading a computation theorem. On canonical
inputs O(a)=a. Accordingly, for lazy profiles state theorem-set equivalence
and canonical certificate translation, not necessarily identical acceptance
on every raw alias certificate.

6. Operational translation for the eager profile
The old checker consumes no instruction at a hypothesis match. The new checker
consumes a packet at every visit. Insert one dummy packet [[],0,0,0] at every
old hypothesis return. H(0,0,0) is valid in every floor. Retain every nonleaf
and computation-leaf packet. Conversely, erase a new packet exactly when the
validated current goal already belongs to the context; retain it otherwise.
Each old active node corresponds to the same new node, no additional scope
decrement occurs, and every recursive return is truthy in both programs.
Induction on the finite successful call/continuation trace proves equivalence.
Unused source prefixes may remain unused as permitted by the baseline contract.

7. Broader safe theorem
The same resource-leaf construction works for any calculus with hypotheses and
a decidably supplied axiom family, if at least one universally available axiom
instance is available as the dummy leaf resource. It changes no derivability
judgment. For the concrete old/new compact profiles the witness map is primitive
recursive on finite successful proof trees and adds one packet per hypothesis
occurrence. It adds no numerical eigenparameter and no semantic evaluation.

8. Required boundaries in final reporting
- Coordinates are empirical minima of inspected implementations, not lower bounds
  on all implementations or all equivalent theories.
- Preserve separate compact/native/strict input contracts in comparisons.
- Gzip is an informational measurement, not a replacement for direct source size.
- A proved calculus equivalence and tests of its Python implementation do not
  constitute a new Lean/Rocq proof of the full checker correspondence.
