EQUATION-ONLY ALIASES WITH NEWEST-PARAMETER SPECIALIZATION
Research note, 2026-11-12

STATUS
This is a proposed mathematical strengthening of the incoming alias_back
construction, with a written proof below. It is not a claim of a smaller
complete checker and is not yet Lean/Rocq formalized. The existing alias_back
note explicitly leaves its arbitrary-formula Alias export in place when
specialization is restricted. The theorem here removes that generality:
BOTH Alias and Back may be restricted to literal equations while Use still
specializes at the newest parameter only. The remaining raw term syntax,
computation equations, D-elimination, and witness-selected D-introduction
are unchanged.

1. SYNTAX AND COMPLETE RULES

Terms are raw (not normalized):
  t ::= x | 0 | S(t) | I(n,a;u.h).
Only u is bound in h. Write C(v,a,b)=I(v,a;u.b), with u fresh in b.
Formulas:
  A ::= s=t | D_{x,y} B.
Ordinary reading: D_{x,y}B = exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y)).
A clause is [A;y.B] or [;y.B]. It means A -> forall y B, or forall y B.
An ordinary assumption A is [;v.A], with v unused. Contexts are finite
lists of clauses. A numerical scope is an ordered list of free parameters.
last(Pi) is its newest member, or 0 if Pi is empty. The root context and
scope are empty. Contradiction is 0=S0. All substitutions are capture free
ordinary syntactic substitutions, with no hidden normalization.

The inference schemes are:

I0.       Gamma |- I(0,a;u.h)=a.
IS.       Gamma |- I(Sn,a;u.h)=h[I(n,a;u.h)/u].

UseLast.  If [A;y.B] is a retained clause, from Gamma|-A infer
          Gamma|-B(last(Pi)). For an antecedent-free clause omit the premise.

EqBack.   From Gamma|-b=t and Gamma|-E[t/b] infer Gamma|-E,
          where b is newest and E is a literal equation. The term t may
          contain b; simultaneous one-pass substitution has its usual meaning.

EqAlias.  From Gamma,b=t |- E infer Gamma|-E[t/b], where E is a literal
          equation, b is a fresh new parameter, and t is an outer-scope term.
          Here b=t is an ordinary assumption. Thus b is absent from Gamma,t.

Open.     From Gamma|-D_{x,y}B and
          Gamma,[B(b,0); y.B(b,y)]|-A infer Gamma|-A,
          with b fresh and absent from A (and the old Gamma).

Close.    From Gamma,B(t,eta)|-B(t,Sz) infer Gamma|-D_{x,y}B,
          with z fresh, eta=z if B is a literal equation and t is literally0,
          and eta=0 otherwise. The witness t is an outer-scope term.

The arithmetic equations remain TWO schemas even if grouped in a program.
There is no reflexivity clause, no formula-valued Alias or Back, and no
arbitrary numerical argument to UseLast. The unrestricted target of Open
is essential to the proof below and has not been removed.

2. A TECHNICAL DISCIPLINE: UNIFORM PROOF PROCEDURES

UseLast and EqBack are not stable under adding a newer free parameter.
Consequently the following proof never weakens a completed target proof.

A uniform proof procedure for Gamma|-A takes:
  (a) any injective embedding theta of the source parameters in any target
      ordered scope (which may have extra parameters in any positions), and
  (b) an occurrence map placing the exact theta-images of Gamma's clauses
      in any larger target context,
and returns an actual target derivation of theta(A).

An occurrence map matters when clauses repeat. All new local assumptions
are appended explicitly, and all old premise procedures are reinvoked in
that extended context. An operation on uniform procedures is itself uniform
if its construction works at every target scope and occurrence map.
Uniformity here is a specification of proof-generating functions. It does
not assume admissibility of weakening/cut for the target calculus.

An ordinary retained assumption A is uniformly available: use its constant,
antecedent-free clause [;v.A], whose instance does not depend on last(Pi).
The computations I0 and IS are uniformly available under renaming.

3. THE EQUATIONAL BASE

All the equational constructions from alias_back use EqBack and EqAlias
only on equations. They are uniform procedures: at every outer target
scope introduce genuinely fresh aliases, then reinvoke earlier uniform
premise procedures inside those scopes.

Reflexivity for t: open EqAlias b=t, use its ordinary assumption b=t, and
close the alias. The result is t=t.

Symmetry from s=t:
  open alias b=s;
  EqBack on b=s and s=t gives b=t;
  reflexivity gives t=t;
  EqBack on b=t and t=t, with conclusion t=b, gives t=b;
  close b=s to obtain t=s.

Transport of equations from s=t and E(s) to E(t): first produce t=s by
symmetry. Open alias b=t. EqBack on b=t and t=s gives b=s. EqBack on b=s
and E(s), with conclusion E(b), gives E(b). Close b=t to obtain E(t).
The distinguished template variable in E is fresh for the aliases. Its
occurrences may be multiple or under term binders. The one-term crossed
transport of the native core follows by symmetry of the transported
literal equation.

Atomic arbitrary specialization: if [A;y.E(y)] is a retained clause, E is
an equation, and an antecedent proof procedure is given (when needed),
then obtain E(t) as follows. Open EqAlias b=t; reinvoke the antecedent
procedure in that extension; UseLast at the now newest b yields E(b);
close EqAlias to export E(t). This construction is uniform even if the
antecedent A has arbitrary formula depth.

4. A VERY SMALL NAME

For any outer term t define
  N_t(x,y) := C(y,t,x)=t,
  Name(t) := D_{x,y} N_t(x,y).

Name(t) is a theorem: Close with witness t and prove its successor branch
  C(Sz,t,t)=t
by the IS equation (whose step body is the constant t). The local hypothesis
is unused. This also works if t is literally0, when Close chooses progress.
No reflexivity proof, equality transport, predecessor, or induction lemma
is needed for this name theorem.

On opening Name(t) at a fresh b we retain the equational clause
  [ C(0,t,b)=t ; y. C(y,t,b)=t ].
Its antecedent is exactly I0. Atomic arbitrary specialization at1=S0 gives
C(1,t,b)=t. The IS equation gives C(1,t,b)=b. Equational transport/symmetry
therefore yield b=t. This equality has a UNIFORM proof procedure in every
further extension of the opened context, because the naming clause is a
retained clause and the atomic specialization procedure works at all
scopes. This is the critical property used below.

5. SIMULTANEOUS INDUCTION ON FORMULA DEPTH

Let depth(s=t)=0 and depth(D B)=1+depth(B). There are no binary formula
connectives in this native language, so depth is just the number of D's.
For each k prove these operations:

S_k: arbitrary clause specialization with body of depth at most k,
     from a UNIFORM antecedent procedure, yields a UNIFORM procedure.

T_k: transport F(s) -> F(t), for depth(F)<=k, from UNIFORM procedures
     for s=t and F(s), yields a UNIFORM procedure.

For positive depth also prove a stronger local variant:

L_k (k>0): at a fixed target context and scope, given an ACTUAL LOCAL proof
     of F(s), where depth(F)=k, and a UNIFORM procedure for s=t in all
     extensions of that context, construct an ACTUAL LOCAL proof of F(t).

The qualification about L_k is not cosmetic. The proof of F(b) obtained
by UseLast while b is newest need not be weakenable to a scope containing
later parameters. L_k avoids such an invalid weakening.

Base: S_0 and T_0 are the constructions of section3. No L_0 is asserted.

Inductive transport (construct L_k first, k>0): write
  F(v) = D_{x,y} B(v,x,y), depth(B)=k-1.
At the present context use the supplied LOCAL proof of F(s) as the major
of Open, introducing a genuinely fresh c and retaining
  C_s := [B(s,c,0); y.B(s,c,y)].
The proof of F(s) is used once, before any newer parameter is introduced.
It is never moved to a deeper context.

In the opened context introduce the desired F(t) by Close with witness c.
Because c is a variable, this is always CASE mode. The branch introduces
fresh z and the ordinary assumption B(t,c,0). Construct that branch:
  (i) T_{k-1}, with the symmetric uniform equality t=s, transports the
      ordinary assumption to B(s,c,0).
  (ii) S_{k-1} specializes the retained clause C_s at Sz, obtaining
       B(s,c,Sz).
  (iii) T_{k-1} transports this to B(t,c,Sz).
Each of these three branch derivations is UNIFORM over arbitrary further
extensions: it starts from retained clauses, ordinary assumptions, and
the given uniform equality. Thus its use in the next operation is valid.
Close gives F(t); Open discharges c because F(t) omits c. This proves L_k.

Now T_k follows: at each target scope and context supplied to the uniform
procedure, invoke the given uniform minor procedure once to obtain its
local proof, and apply L_k with the given uniform equality procedure.
This yields a uniform result by construction.

Inductive arbitrary specialization S_k (k>0): at an arbitrary target
scope/context, build Name(t), Open it at a new b, and obtain the uniform
name equality b=t by section4. Invoke the uniform antecedent procedure in
this opened context. Primitive UseLast on the original clause now gives a
LOCAL proof of F(b), since b is the newest parameter. Apply L_k to that
local proof and the uniform b=t procedure to obtain F(t). Finally Open
of Name(t) discharges b, because F(t) is in the original outer scope.
All these constructions can be repeated for every outer embedding, so
S_k is uniform. There is no invocation of S_k in its own construction:
L_k uses only S_{k-1} and T_{k-1}; S_k uses L_k and S_0 for the name.

Thus all S_k and T_k exist. This is a finite induction on formula depth,
not an assumed structural rule for the newest-only target calculus.

6. COMPLETENESS COMPILER

Take as source the repository's raw Eq/D clause calculus with arbitrary
specialization, unrestricted equation transport, reflexivity, the two
computations, Open, and the witness-selected Close. (Equivalently the
corresponding raw alias_back temporary calculus can be used after the
standard equivalence.) Compile a source derivation into a uniform proof
procedure, by induction on that SOURCE derivation.

- Reflexivity: section3.
- Computations: same computation schema under the embedding.
- Equation transport: T_0 (and symmetry for the crossed orientation).
- Use at t with body B: S_depth(B), supplied with the already compiled
  proper-source-premise procedure for its antecedent, if any.
- Open: invoke the compiled source major in the current target context,
  choose a new target parameter, extend the parameter embedding and clause
  occurrence map, and invoke the compiled source branch there. Primitive
  Open then discharges the parameter.
- Close: translate its witness, choose a new target parameter for z, append
  the translated ordinary assumption, and invoke the compiled source branch.
  Injective renaming preserves literal-equation and literal-zero tests,
  so the mode is unchanged. Primitive Close gives the translated conclusion.

All recursive compiler calls on source derivations are on proper premises.
S_k and T_k are already defined by the independent finite depth induction.
There is therefore no recursion cycle between proof compilation and the
formula-depth macros. No target proof is weakened, renamed, or cut.
At the identity embedding with no extra context, the compiler gives the
required target proof.

7. SOUNDNESS AND BI-INTERPRETABILITY

I0/IS, UseLast, Open, Close are instances of the known sound raw system.
EqBack is equality transport in the reverse direction. EqAlias is sound
by substituting its outer term t for the fresh b in the ordinary derivation:
the extra assumption becomes t=t and is discharged by equality reflexivity.
This is an ordinary-logic argument and does not assume substitution is
already admissible in the newest-only primitive calculus.

The old whole-domain bridge identifies those native consequences with full
first-order iPRA. The new compiler yields the reverse native consequence
inclusion; soundness yields the forward inclusion. Consequently this new
presentation has EXACTLY the same native Eq/D consequences, over arbitrary
contexts, as the old one. The standard elimination of Q via an unused D
witness restores the full native Eq/Q/D language. Compose with the existing
ordinary iPRA interpretations: numerical domains are whole, equality is
actual equality, both numerical composites are the identity graph x=y,
and the old formula round trips are unchanged. Equiconsistency follows.

This claim depends on the cited established iPRA/native bridge, not on
finite tests. The new metatheorem is the calculus-to-calculus compiler above.
No smaller measured checker follows just from restricting Alias/Back;
checking those restrictions may add code unless a new representation shares
the equation checks already present elsewhere.

8. FURTHER QUESTIONS

(a) Can EqAlias and EqBack share a raw arity-code representation with UseLast
    so that this stricter interface actually lowers complete checker size?
(b) Can one replace EqBack by crossed replacement of one term, preserving
    the template-free goal-directed character and making a smaller kernel?
(c) Can Open export only equation conclusions as well? The L_k proof uses
    compound Open essentially, so the theorem above gives no such reduction.
(d) Formalize uniform proof procedures and the L_k/S_k/T_k induction in
    Lean, then extract a certified compiler into the target proof relation.
