INDEPENDENT REVIEW: EQUATION-ONLY ALIAS / NEWEST SPECIALIZATION
Reviewed source: review/math_innovation.txt, 12 November 2026.
Reviewer: separate same-run mathematical agent; not external peer review.

VERDICT

The proposed calculus-to-calculus equivalence has a complete written proof
at the level stated, conditional only on the explicitly inherited equivalence
of the source raw template calculus with native iPRA. I found no remaining
use of an unproved target weakening, cut, or arbitrary-substitution rule.
The crucial distinction between uniformly reconstructible derivations and a
single local proof is sufficient to close the apparent scope circularity.
This is not Lean/Rocq verification, a proof of a new checker-size record, or
an independent reproof of the repository's complete arithmetic bridge.

One definitional clarification is recommended before publication: in L_k,
'uniform equality in all extensions' must mean the FULL notion of section 2
(arbitrary injective parameter embeddings and clause-occurrence maps), not
just appending unused parameters. The later construction of T_k invokes that
full uniform notion. Read that phrase with the section-2 definition and the
proof is sound; make it explicit to prevent a weaker, insufficient reading.

1. EQUATIONAL BASE AUDIT

EqAlias may export only equations; all uses in the reflexivity, symmetry,
and equation-transport constructions meet this restriction. Every use of
EqBack has an equation conclusion and uses the newly appended alias as its
newest parameter. Aliases are fresh for the terms they name.

For symmetry, under b=s, EqBack with conclusion b=t has minor s=t. The
second EqBack has conclusion t=b and minor t=t. Discharge exports t=s.
For transport, under b=t, the first EqBack yields b=s from t=s, and the
second yields E(b) from E(s); EqAlias exports E(t). Capture avoidance works
with repeated template occurrences and iterator binders because b is fresh
and the distinguished template variable is chosen outside the aliases.

These constructions use their premises inside larger alias contexts. That
would be invalid for an arbitrary completed newest-only proof. The note
does not do that: it requires uniform premise procedures and reinvokes them
in the requested larger context. Thus the base T_0 claim is correctly limited
to uniform premises. There is no illicit L_0 claim.

Atomic arbitrary specialization S_0 uses EqAlias directly: name the desired
term t by fresh b, recompile the antecedent in that scope, use the clause at
newest b, and export its equation. The antecedent may have any formula depth;
the S_0 construction needs only its already-supplied uniform proof procedure,
not an induction hypothesis on that antecedent's depth. This distinction
prevents a hidden complexity recursion.

2. NAMING AUDIT

Name(t)=D_{x,y}(C(y,t,x)=t), C(y,a,b)=I(y,a;u.b).
Close uses witness t. Its successor goal C(Sz,t,t)=t is exactly an instance
of the successor computation schema, since the iterator body is constant t.
No reduction of a term beyond the explicit schema is hidden here. If t is
literally zero the mode changes to progress, but the hypothesis remains
unused and the same computation proof works.

Opening the name at b retains
[C(0,t,b)=t ; y.C(y,t,b)=t]. Its antecedent is exactly zero computation.
S_0 at 1 gives C(1,t,b)=t; successor computation gives C(1,t,b)=b.
T_0 (or its symmetry/composition special cases) gives b=t. Every ingredient
is uniform over the retained naming clause. Naming therefore supplies more
than a local equality proof: it supplies the uniform equality needed after
further Open/Close scopes are introduced.

3. POSITIVE-DEPTH LOCAL TRANSPORT AUDIT

At depth k>0, F(v)=D_{x,y}B(v,x,y). A merely local proof of F(s) is used
as the major of Open at its original scope. The proof is not transplanted
under the fresh witness c. This is the exact point that fixes the earlier
tempting but invalid argument.

Inside the Open branch, the target F(t) is introduced at witness c. Because
c is a free parameter, it is not the literal numerical zero, so the rule is
case introduction, including when B is an equation. The fresh z branch has
ordinary assumption B(t,c,0), which is uniformly available as an
antecedent-free constant clause. The source Open clause is retained as a
clause occurrence and is uniformly accessible by S_(k-1).

The branch is built in three steps, all at lower formula depth:
  T_(k-1) with t=s: B(t,c,0) -> B(s,c,0);
  S_(k-1) at Sz on the retained clause: B(s,c,0) -> B(s,c,Sz);
  T_(k-1) with s=t: B(s,c,Sz) -> B(t,c,Sz).
The first minor is a uniform ordinary assumption; the second has a uniform
antecedent from the first step; the third has a uniform minor from the
second. Therefore even when k=1, no local atomic transport theorem is being
smuggled in. Each atomic use has the uniform premises required by T_0.

Close and Open then export F(t), which does not contain c. The condition
that s,t and the template's external data precede c is ensured by genuine
freshness and capture-free renaming of the bound variables x,y.

4. INDUCTION AND SOURCE COMPILER AUDIT

The dependency graph is well founded:
  S_0 and T_0 are direct equational constructions;
  L_k uses only S_(k-1), T_(k-1), and primitive Open/Close;
  T_k uses L_k;
  S_k uses L_k and the atomic naming construction.

In S_k, the arbitrary original clause is used at a fresh name b. That gives
only a local proof of F(b), but L_k was deliberately strengthened to accept
exactly such a minor. Its equality b=t is uniform by the name lemma. The
outer Open discharges b after F(t) has been constructed. Thus S_k does not
depend on itself, or on target weakening, even indirectly.

The separate compiler is a structural induction on SOURCE proofs. It emits
a uniform procedure for each conclusion. At a source Use, its antecedent is
a proper source premise and has already been compiled; the appropriate S_k
has already been defined by the independent depth induction. The source
antecedent may be deeper than the specialized body without causing a cycle:
the source-proof induction, not the formula-depth induction, produces it.
At source Open and Close, extend the parameter embedding and clause
occurrence map and invoke the already-compiled proper branch. Injective
renaming preserves the literal-zero and equation-root tests, so Close modes
are unchanged. Equation transport uses T_0, and computations/reflexivity
use the base constructions. No completed target proof is renamed or weakened.

The source should be identified as the actual raw template calculus already
used as alias_back's matched control (arbitrary clause use, equation
transport, reflexivity, both computations, Open, Close). This avoids a
presentation mismatch with a differently named CPrf variant whose primitive
arithmetic rule is Eval. The corpus establishes their equivalence, but the
report should name the chosen source directly.

5. SOUNDNESS AND WHAT TRANSFERS

EqBack is valid equality substitution in the ordinary realization. EqAlias
is valid by substituting the outer term for its fresh parameter; its extra
assumption becomes t=t. This argument takes place in ordinary logic, not
inside the restricted target calculus, so it does not assume the very target
substitution theorem being investigated. Other rules are retained from the
known sound source.

Thus target-to-source soundness plus the explicit source-to-target compiler
give equality of native consequences. Keeping identical terms and formula
readings permits the existing whole-domain iPRA interpretations and formula
round trips to transfer. Merely equiconsistency would not have sufficed, but
the note actually proves the stronger exact consequence equivalence needed.

6. LIMITS AND IMPLEMENTATION CHECKS TO KEEP DISTINCT

The theorem settles the specifically identified incoming Alias/Back question:
both equality rules can be equation-only while specialization stays fixed to
the newest parameter. It does not remove compound targets from Open; those
targets are essential in L_k and S_k. It does not remove the arbitrary Close
witness, the computation equations, or equation progress.

The proof is a mathematical restriction of an existing rule interface. It
does not automatically save code, since validating the equation restriction
can add a check. Any below-frontier claim needs separately measured code.
If an implementation is supplied, useful structural test cases are:
  - Use of a compound clause at a non-newest arbitrary term;
  - the same operation with an antecedent deeper than the clause body;
  - source proof compilation into an interleaved/non-order-preserving scope;
  - duplicate clauses and noncontiguous occurrence maps;
  - literal-zero names, names involving iterator binders, and nested D depth;
  - rejection of every compound EqAlias/EqBack conclusion and escaping names.
Passing such tests supports the implementation but does not prove this
metatheorem; the proof above does not rely on finite tests.
