3. 'LOWER BOUND' AND MINIMALITY

The numeric envelopes are minima among surveyed implementations and hence
upper bounds on the best achievable complexity. None of the inspected
packages proves a universal lower bound for complete proof-checker size.
Rule-independence proofs for one syntax/corpus do not become source-size
lower bounds for every allowed changed-language/domain theory. The finite
specialization-menu obstructions and fixed binder-depth lower bounds have
narrower hypotheses and cannot establish global minimality either.

The question's word 'simpler' must therefore be operationalized by the fixed
corpus metric. A decrease in one coordinate with increases in others is a
Pareto tradeoff, not a checker below the entire coordinatewise envelope.
The user has not explicitly demanded simultaneous dominance of all three
coordinates in this turn, so do not falsely attribute that stronger demand
as a quotation. But a response must say exactly what metric improved and
must not attach the 248/270/479 envelope vector to one program.

4. MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATIONS AND PROOF STATUS

The native scalar core's iPRA equivalence is inherited: variables, 0, S,
scalar iteration; Eq/Q/D formulas; computation equations; hypothesis;
transport; closure elimination; paired elimination; case introduction; and
literal-equation progress. The whole-domain ordinary interpretation uses
actual equality and internal numerical comparison graphs x=y in both
directions. Formula round trips are an additional obligation, not implied
by sharing the standard natural-number model.

The pinned README identifies Lean theorems for the native interpretation and
many rule rearrangements. It explicitly marks numerous newest combinations
as not formalized. The incoming reports rely on written checker simulations
plus that inherited bridge and explicitly state they did not rebuild the
Lean project. A new Python regression suite is not Lean/Rocq verification.

Guarded-root introduction and source-restricted transport lose some native
consequences on arbitrary raw formulas; the public gate translation is an
essential part of the compact theory. Do not silently drop it. Likewise,
ordinary falsity must retain its bridge from 0=S0; equiconsistency alone is
weaker than the user-requested bi-interpretation.

5. INCOMING MATHEMATICAL CONTRIBUTIONS NOT TO REDISCOVER

alias_back: raw Eq/D with clauses, Fresh Alias plus Back at the newest
parameter, and fixed clause specialization at the newest parameter (0 in
empty scope). Back: b=t and F[t/b] imply F. Alias: from Gamma,b=t |- F,
export Gamma |- F[t/b], with b fresh and t outer-scope. Alias derives
reflexivity by exporting its own defining hypothesis. Back+Alias derive
symmetry/full transport without arithmetic/normalization/induction. A
generalized compiler handles scope extension instead of assuming restricted
proofs weaken unchanged. Back can be restricted to equations; restricting
Alias to equations while retaining fixed specialization is not proved.
The new checker is larger: (773,916,2903) versus (716,838,2683).

backward_replacement: backward crossed all-occurrence replacement gives
native consequence equivalence but adds a trusted traversal and increases
all matched size coordinates.

refinement: two-field packets with computation recognized from the current
goal, no computation packet. This simplifies certificate data but the
checker (295,352,742) does not beat the frontier.

scope_and_frontier: k=1-|g| holds at recursive-call boundaries, but NOT at
every program point. The floor changes before context extension. Replacing
all scope checks by a single inclusive context-length rule is unsound; the
archive supplies a false four-packet public-goal certificate. The exact
scope-elimination implementation grows, (267,303,500) vs (267,298,492).

reductions: right inverse/rigidity lemmas for successor uniqueness; standard
classical exact Eq/D binder-depth hierarchy. They do not yet bootstrap the
full equation-induction bridge from successor-only uniqueness.

immediate_application: treating reserved coordinates 1 and 2 as zero aliases
in the native goal validator loses public-language completeness for the
unchanged raw protocol. The off-contract equation [1,0] exposes it.

6. RESEARCH DIRECTIONS STILL POTENTIALLY DISTINCT

(a) The most concrete new mathematical opening is equation-only Alias with
fixed specialization, or another scope/equality fusion that retains compound
formula transport through a proved compiler. It must beat a matched FULL
checker, since deleting fields alone has repeatedly increased source size.
(b) A finite, simple reencoding of clause scopes that makes the fresh parameter
phase intrinsic could remove actual scope machinery, but the known inclusive
floor attack is a mandatory test and boundary invariants alone are inadequate.
(c) Successor-only iteration uniqueness remains a mathematical bootstrap
question after the new rigidity lemma; obtain equality reflection/quantifier-
free induction, or a genuine countermodel, before claiming iPRA equivalence.
(d) The user explicitly permits changed domains/languages; the repository's
whole-domain convention is a benchmark choice, not an additional user
requirement. A changed-domain construction is admissible but must supply
definable internal isomorphisms, not just mutual interpretation or arithmetic
conservativity. No such smaller completed construction was found by this audit.

Audit limits: source review and exact measurements of named incumbents,
authenticated incoming artifacts, and consistency checks across their claims.
No assertion that every inherited arithmetic proof has independently been
reproved here; no source-code lower bound, new theorem, or new record claimed.
