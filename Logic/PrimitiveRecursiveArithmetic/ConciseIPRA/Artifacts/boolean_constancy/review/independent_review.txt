1. The recorded minima are upper bounds on optimum program size, not proved lower bounds. The article states this explicitly in ch11_cert (section Further questions, Lower bounds; and envelope/metric questions). A coordinatewise envelope need not be attained by one program. Initial pinned snapshot b9aa3610 gave 248 tokens / 270 nodes / 479 normalized bytes. Final freshness inspection of incoming ipra_absorbing_predecessor_2026-11-12.zip at 7703601c48baf2fb4a051dc74559b0b766ea5a4b corrects the current direct envelope to 244 tokens / 267 nodes / 479 normalized bytes. Its token and AST leaders already use sequential JSON records. Our versions of those two leaders change only from json import loads to from json import *, saving four normalized bytes each without changing tokens or AST nodes. No streaming novelty or token/AST record remains. The independently proved eager-any byte refinements still lower 479 to 478 and 637 to 636.

2. Comparison contracts must remain fixed. Compact R/U programs decode raw JSON into sorted terms/formulas, allowing administrative aliases and exceptions as rejection. Their completeness is a theorem about canonical public goals; they do not validate the public grammar. T/ed and T/g include exact public/input validation and have separate larger envelopes. Imports, JSON reading, kernel, and success output must all be charged. Normalizing source by ast.unparse does not neutralize a change in framing or importer behavior.

3. Guarded U public grammar: equations; D over public compound matrices; D over E only when E is I(y,0;u.h)=I(y,0;u.k). The auxiliary reading of an unguarded atomic D is existential progress, while its opened Q is conditional closure. Public restriction cannot be silently removed. All calculations about bi-interpretability should be presented for public formulas and their ordinary realization (or use a fully native calculus and pay its validator).

4. New small source need not mean new mathematical axiomatization. The repository already has a native six-rule calculus, five-rule and three-family clause calculi on full normal forms, equality at discharge, inferred witnesses, deterministic introduction, variable-only transport, successor-seeded and constant-free recursion syntaxes. Simple recombinations must be checked against the README and unified chapters before novelty is claimed.

5. Induction caveat: replacing case introduction B(0) |- B(Sz) by progress B(z) |- B(Sz) for arbitrary compound B strengthens to full formula induction unless a restricted matrix class/semantics is justified. Quantifier-free equation induction alone suffices in iPRA because actual equality is internally reflected by a term; one cannot assume that reflection to prove it.

6. Stronger obstacle than standard examples: ch09_ctr, canonical_recursors, constructs a duplicate-fiber model of full iterator computation, coverage, separation, successor injectivity and induction for zero equations, which refutes induction for arbitrary equations. Any reduction to zero equations must establish reflection of actual equality, not merely compute a standard equality-test algorithm.

7. Successor-only uniqueness SU is genuinely unresolved in the corpus. It gives zero-equation induction and associative commutative addition; scalar predecessor construction uses arbitrary update uniqueness, so it cannot be imported. The duplicate-fiber model is not a SU model (its label-erasing stationary iterator defines a nonidentity successor-preserving term), so it does not resolve SU negatively either.

8. Stationarity f(Sz)=f(z) => f(t)=f(0) recovers quantifier-free induction over an explicit full PR interface with truth flags and counter-aware recursion, as already proved in ch10_obs. It is not a new smaller scalar basis until that interface is derived without the removed induction rule.

9. Candidate directions still open in the corpus: focused clause use with immediate paired-formula opening; shared normalization and opening traversal that rebases dummy binder slots correctly; intrinsic validated goal grammar; identity/successor-only uniqueness bootstrap; verified raw-decoder semantics. Each has material outstanding proof obligations, especially arbitrary-counter induction cuts.

No independent novel mathematical reduction was proved in this review.
