4. SAFETY OF TRANSFERRING NEW SIMPLIFICATIONS

Root-determined progress at every atomic matrix is not equivalent on all raw
native formulas. It is only complete on the gated public language. The README
even gives countermodels: restricted iterator-source transport loses raw
variable transitivity, and root-determined atomic progress loses raw case
introduction D[x,y](y=1). Any direct theorem claiming identity on all raw
native sequents needs the witness/diagonal mode rule, not that shortcut.

Progress must remain restricted to equations. Compound-formula progress
would supply stronger full induction and change the theory. Deterministic
newest-parameter DI is not directly substitution-stable; the new reports need
the purely equational predecessor/naming construction and a structural proof.
Combining it with a compact implementation cannot simply assert ordinary
substitution admissibility in the restricted calculus.
