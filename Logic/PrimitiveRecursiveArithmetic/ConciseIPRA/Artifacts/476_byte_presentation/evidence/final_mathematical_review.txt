Supplementary mathematical review
Date: 2026-11-12

Scope
-----
This is a human-readable mathematical and source-code audit of report.tex,
the readable PD checker, and the cited portions of the archived bridge and
diagonal-clause sources. It is not proof-assistant verification, a new Lean
or Rocq development, or a proof of the Python interpreter's operational
semantics. It does not establish the requested checker-size improvement.

Verdict
-------
No fatal gap was found in the stated PD primitive rules, tree-preserving
substitution proof, optional/mandatory-antecedent context conversion,
equivalence with the diagonal clause calculus, or transfer of the inherited
bi-interpretation. The report correctly treats the repository's scalar
arithmetic bridge and both term/formula round trips as cited dependencies.

The iterator, formula and clause binder conventions are explicit. Intro
tests the matrix before witness instantiation; substitution lifted under
the two protected binders preserves and reflects the diagonal shape.
All three defined partial-evaluation rows preserve their row under
substitution. The eigenvariable conditions permit freshening in both
Intro and Discharge. The closed compound theorem Theta recovers ordinary
elimination and standalone evaluation without relying circularly on the
arithmetical bridge.

The readable checker's fixed reflexivity clause, test on B0, positive
evaluation cases, compound identity case and output scope validation agree
with this presentation. Finite execution tests remain supplementary
implementation evidence, not universal mathematical proofs.

Clarification of the earlier independent review
----------------------------------------------
The earlier review described an auxiliary K^u with separate primitive
reflexivity (five families when the two evaluation rows are grouped).
The final report instead presents K^u with the fixed reflexivity clause
and derived reflexivity, yielding four primitive families. In contexts
containing the fixed clause these versions are equivalent: replace every
reflexivity inference by Use of that clause. This final presentational
change affects neither the equivalence nor the substitution arguments.
The report's matched control is correctly identified as four-family.

Limitations to preserve
----------------------
The new PD presentation has not beaten the compact 244-token, 265-AST-node,
477-byte coordinate envelope. Those coordinates are attained by different
programs and are achieved upper bounds on an unknown optimum. Three rule
families alone do not establish a new minimum; their selectors, subcases,
syntax validation, input handling and fixed initial clause remain charged.
