Existing positive ideas: do not present as new
----------------------------------------------
- certified_zero: use the initializer's nonempty syntax as computation dispatch,
  replacing the distinct arithmetic opcode. The missing zero initializer is
  repaired via C=I(2,1;v.0)=0; the same C also repairs a zero predecessor.
  Every affected computation leaf becomes three native inference nodes. Source
  encodings and contexts/end-sequents unchanged. This is the current frontier.
- arity_calculus: a D formula is unary; its Q closure is three copies of its body;
  every array uses opening-depth increment 5-len(node). Surplus arities have a
  conservative constant interpretation. This is a representation change.
- affine_arity: depth+len(node)+1 with extra coordinate below equations and a
  bottom interpretation for surplus arities; exact introduction-shape guard.
- uniform_predecessor: construct successor from supplied predecessor. A boxed
  three-command step becomes one command; reduces contextual proof 48 to 40
  commands. Driver-only controls can erase reported whole-program size gain.
- flat_computation: boxed predecessor B(p)=I(0,p;v.0)=p supplies arbitrary p via
  transport. Arithmetic packets [null,n,a,h] construct sources and open with 0.
- constructed_computation: construct successor sources from predecessors; only
  validate a recursive image when state is actually used. Small certificate
  format improvement, inherited arithmetic/logical presentation.
- affine_supply: compose affine scopes, uniform predecessors, arity coding, and
  immutable-context loops. Direct zero witnesses. No single profile dominates
  all its comparison coordinates. Mutable shared contexts are unsound.
- compact_kernel and checker_and_barriers: compose previous encodings and
  shared continuation construction. Gains are small checker reductions;
  same-driver controls are essential.
- scalar_successor, successor_seed_completion, transient_seed: all complete
  predecessor/bootstrap proofs for successor-seeded scalar R with R(0,a;h)=Sa,
  R(Sn,a;h)=h(R(n,a;h)), and S(a)=R(0,a;0). Recover I by
  P(R(n,a;v.S(h(P(v))))). Distinct finite-swap, shifted-affine, or transient/reset
  proofs; the candidate syntax/equations already appeared in an earlier draft.
- counter_congruence: root-left equality replacement + congruence only in the
  recursor counter derive full numerical and formula transport using cases.
  Numerical congruence adds 56 inference nodes independent of template depth.
  Its new-calculus checker is much larger (1617 tokens/1822 AST/6834 norm bytes).
- bounded_initializer: new tied-seed B + Boolean actual-equality test epsilon +
  constructor separation + contextual literal-equation induction, in ordinary
  intuitionistic first-order logic. Bounded initializer, majorant compiler,
  cap coherence, and finite quantifier-free function graphs recover full iPRA.
  The small measured checkers in that package implement OLD initialized-I
  Choice-DI, NOT this new arithmetic theory. Do not conflate the results.
- research: progressive H_xy B := exists x forall y(B(x,y)->B(x,Sy)), with
  unrestricted-progress introduction and equation-only iteration elimination;
  parity/half construction encodes universals, while Boolean Cases stays.
  The H presentation does not receive the small D-checkers' measurements.
- local_scopes: lexical proof scopes improve one byte coordinate; arithmetic
  and full bi-interpretation inherited. Unbounded selector completes tied seed
  numerically, without a smaller full logical checker claim.

Existing obstruction results: do not present as new
--------------------------------------------------
- adaptive_seed: almost-unary coordinate-bound invariant for finitely adaptive
  queries to the body's own update, and finite-dimensional term-defined unital
  magma quotient obstruction. ceil(log2(d+1)) compositions of unit maps suffice
  for finite quotient collapse. Arbitrary relational operations excluded.
- uniform_predecessor: fixed finite register vectors, body probes, counter-
  dependent probe maps, PR combiners, unary PR functions and bounded PR
  observations still cannot recover full independent initialization when all
  passive/state data reach combiners solely through the one body.
- local_scopes: full ordinary first-order finite-dimensional bi-interpretation
  obstruction for tied-seed B with computation, coverage, separation and
  equation induction. Allows arbitrary formula-defined domains/graphs/quotients
  and finite parameters. Uses finite term profiles and existential-free witness
  extraction plus the source-side return isomorphism. Does not exclude every
  one-way interpretation or addition of arbitrary positive existence axioms.
- research: eventually constant powers satisfy equations, equation induction,
  universal Horn laws and finite-conjunction induction; have involutions after
  arbitrary finite parameter expansions, obstructing ordinary bi-interpretation
  with models of iPRA including quotient/different-domain constructions.
- compact_kernel: strengthens coordinatewise transfer in eventually constant
  powers to atoms, truth, conjunction, existential and universal quantification
  with arbitrary alternation, plus all standard-true implications between such
  formulas. Thus stronger positive theories retain the automorphism obstruction.
- checker_and_barriers: Boolean cases remain necessary even for the progressive
  H binder; explicit N^2 mixed-parameter counterexample. Positive-iteration
  R+(n,a;h)=h^(n+1)(a) zero sets are only empty/universal/one zero hyperplane,
  excluding predecessor, zero test, addition and initialized iteration as terms.
- certified_zero: eager F(0,a;h)=h(a), F(Sn,a;h)=h(F(n,a;h)) has a two-element
  model even with coverage, separation and all-formula induction. It cannot
  ordinarily interpret iPRA with any fixed finite-dimensional domain/quotient.
  Projection-box substitution restriction also loses basic reflexivity.
- counter_congruence: dropping root replacement loses reflexivity; term-based
  seed-deletion and naive paired-binder reversal obstructions.
- constructed_computation: simultaneous restriction of DI witnesses and QE
  targets gives a finite-range obstruction.
- adaptive_seed: restricting paired-elimination major premises to hypotheses
  loses an explicit proof of Q_y(0=0); this normalization is not admissible.
