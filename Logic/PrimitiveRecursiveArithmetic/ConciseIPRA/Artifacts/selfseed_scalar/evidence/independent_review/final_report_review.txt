FOCUSED CHECKS
1. Finite first-order staging. Each F body is a fixed finite source body over
   earlier-stage symbols. The theory contains neither function quantifiers
   nor a total evaluator for program codes.
2. Optional successor elimination. The constant-body symbol F_a is available
   at the first description stage without S. Defining Sa=F_a(0) makes its
   base equation tautological, but its step equation and separation and
   injectivity axioms remain substantive. Thus the stated mutual definitional
   translations do not require a circular source-language generation rule.
3. Predecessor and coverage. The W tail identity is proved using a fixed
   passive parameter before specialization. The finite-case predecessor
   graph is total without coverage. The joint QF invariant derives coverage
   and the predecessor successor law together; no constructor-coverage
   axiom is being silently used in that derivation.
4. Finite-case induction. The proof explicitly expands ordinary terms and
   QF matrices into finite source cases. It does not infer restricted
   induction merely from conservative definability.
5. Source-body compiler. Graph-defined operations are expanded before any
   new F body is used. Every candidate value, including inactive branches,
   is clipped and encoded. The two sentinels therefore fall outside the
   maintained real-state range while remaining valid bounded-selector
   candidates. Initial cap zero is allowed by the encoded range [2,2].
6. Arithmetic bootstrap. The saturated diagonal argument bounds addition
   without presupposing addition. Later multiplication and pairing use
   only already defined finite-case graphs. Bounded root search has a
   clock-or-fixed invariant and a provably fixed terminal state.
7. General PR compilation. Source unary majorants are explicit, monotone
   and inflationary. The state bound is guarded by the allowed clock,
   and the next-state equations are used only when the next clock remains
   in the bound. Cap coherence handles changes in the chosen input maximum.
8. Whole-domain round trips. Fixed-description/stage induction and internal
   QF recurrence uniqueness identify both function composites at arbitrary
   domain elements. The graph-elimination lemma supplies source induction
   for composite graph functions. Actual equality and the whole domain are
   retained, so identity equality gives both domain isomorphisms. This is
   stronger than mere agreement on standard natural numbers.
9. Complexity statements. 554 bytes and 291 AST nodes belong to different
   complete programs for the inherited theory. No new program improves all
   three measured coordinates. The observed sizes are upper bounds on the
   unknown optimum, not a proved lower-bound theorem. The tiny gains and
   the lack of a measured new-theory checker are correctly disclosed.
10. Parser qualification. Generic iterable traversals rely on real Python
    stack exhaustion to reject some malformed strings; the report expressly
    does not claim total abstract parsing for those loop profiles.
