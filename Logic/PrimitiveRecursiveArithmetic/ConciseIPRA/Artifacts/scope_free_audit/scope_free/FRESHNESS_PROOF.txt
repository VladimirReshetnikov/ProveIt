LOCAL DECIMAL DOMINATION AND CERTIFICATE COMPILATION
==================================================

1. Finite-tree bound

Let g be a finite context of finite integer/list trees (the root context is
[0]), a a finite integer/list goal, and r=[s,u,t] the current packet. Write

    L = len(str([g,a,r])),       nu = -10^L.

Python's ordinary decimal rendering of every integer leaf n of those trees
contains all decimal digits of |n|. The rendering of [g,a,r] contains that
rendering as a substring and also punctuation. If d is the number of decimal
digits of |n|, then d <= L and |n| < 10^d <= 10^L = |nu|. Hence nu is distinct
from every integer leaf in all three old objects. This uses finite acyclic
trees only, as supplied by JSON. It does not use a probability estimate,
hash collision assumption, memory address, or global uniqueness promise.

The rule dispatcher computes nu before changing a or checking a major
subproof. All current input fields s,u,t occur inside r, so they need not
appear separately in the bound. Temporary variables from a completed major
subproof do not occur in the caller's state. Their names may be reused in
another branch without violating the local eigenvariable conditions.

2. Why absence as a leaf gives semantic freshness

In the inherited sorted arity reading, every negative integer leaf denotes
the free numerical parameter with that name, whenever the leaf is in a
meaningful term position. No constructor invents a free parameter not
already among the negative leaves of its raw tree. Arrays or coordinates
ignored by the sorted reading can only reduce that support. Thus absence
of nu from every leaf of g,a,r entails absence from the free-variable
supports of every corresponding formula and term.

The scalar opener retains all negative leaves, since a negative integer is
less than every meaningful nonnegative binder depth. It fills only the
exposed positive coordinate. Therefore the usual capture-free opening lemma
is the baseline opening lemma with negative numerical names treated as
unrestricted free parameters rather than checked against a lower floor.
Malformed surplus arities require the inherited auxiliary sorted-reading
lemma, not a claim that every accepted internal tree is canonical syntax.

3. Rule-by-rule use of the bound

Hypothesis and computation leaves allocate nothing. Their meaningful
arithmetic and logical rules are unchanged.

In an equality or Q elimination, both source/target and template already
occur in r. The checked major has exactly its required meaning; a subsequent
administrative continuation may use nu, but all required transported terms
are free of it before opening.

For D elimination, the major D formula occurs in r and the target is a.
The context, major, and exported conclusion are all free of nu. The branch
may therefore open the existential witness at nu under the usual local
eigenvariable condition. Names used strictly inside the proof of the major
are not exported and are irrelevant to this condition.

For D introduction, the body and any remaining free variables occur in a,
and the chosen witness occurs in r. Thus nu is fresh for the context,
matrix's parameters, and witness. In the equation branch the retained
premise is E(nu) and the target is E(S nu); literal-equation induction gives
the required closure. In the compound branch the premise is B(0) and the
target B(S nu); the inherited numerical-case rule gives the closure.

The rest of the proof is the existing scalar calculus's soundness argument,
including its interpretation of raw aliases. This note proves the new local
freshness obligation; it does not replace or silently assume the nontrivial
iPRA bi-interpretation theorem.

4. Completeness is alpha-renaming, not identical raw traces

Start with a finite derivation in the typed scalar calculus. At a rule that
requires a fresh numerical parameter, first encode its current context,
conclusion, and packet under the names already chosen in outer scopes.
Compute nu from these finite values. By (1)--(3), nu meets that rule's
freshness condition. Alpha-rename its bound branch parameter to nu and
continue compiling the premises. The current packet never contains its
own not-yet-introduced eigenparameter, so this construction is well-founded.

Hypothesis and computation packets compile directly. Equality and closure
elimination compile their typed major and minor premises; D introduction
adds a neutral major packet and compiles its branch under the new assumption;
D elimination compiles the major and then the witness branch. Packet order
is the checker's actual recursive order. Every object is finite and the
integer 10^L exists for every finite L, so this is a total mathematical
construction on finite typed proofs in an unbounded-resource model.

A compiler that tracks a larger outer numerical scope may first retain a
harmless proven major p=p for each active parameter whose name would otherwise
be absent from the raw context. The nonmatching neutral packet [Eq(p,p),0,0]
proves that reflexive equation and continues toward the same typed goal with
Eq(p,p)*3 added. This array has the administrative truth reading but retains
p literally. Generated names satisfy p < -1, so the raw opening of that
six-entry array preserves its p leaves. Reflexivity is derived from the
zero computation and transport. Such neutral padding can remember every
active outer label before local allocation; it is untrusted proof production
and adds no kernel rule. The new bound then avoids the entire active scope.

Alternatively, additional parameters absent from a local sequent may be
renamed or instantiated within that subproof. Accordingly, one must NOT claim literal
acceptance equivalence on every arbitrary raw input stream under a naive
global replacement map. Untyped inference-selection coincidences can change
under such a map. The translator in this directory is an explicitly bounded,
execution-guided fixture tool; it refuses unsupported encounters. Its seven
successful cases are evidence for implementation, not the universal compiler
theorem or a test of every alias case.

5. Fixed-name falsification

The public formula

    D_{x,y}(I(y,0;u.x) = I(y,0;u.y))

has base 0=0. At y=1 it forces x=1, and at y=2 it forces x=2, so its ordinary
arithmetical reading is false. A checker that always chooses eigenname -1
can be attacked by supplying the witness S(-1): its alleged successor case
then captures that fixed parameter and collapses to reflexivity. The supplied
six-line attack makes that deliberately unsound control accept. The genuine
checker chooses a negative number larger in magnitude than the occurrence
of -1 in the witness packet, so that collapse cannot occur; it rejects the
same stream. Both ordinary and optimized Python executions are recorded.

6. What is and is not established

The implementation deletes a mutable scope floor and replaces its role by
an explicit locally dominating name. The negative integers now represent
free parameters without a numeric lower-bound promise. This changes proof
representation, not the inherited numerical domain, actual equality, or
the defining arithmetic and logical clauses. Bi-interpretability and
equiconsistency transfer only after the rule-level soundness and typed
completeness argument above is combined with the repository's full bridge.

This construction is attributed to the repository's earlier long_names
experiment. Its measured complete source ties the matched control's bytes
and uses more tokens and AST nodes. It is not a new complexity record,
a proof of optimality, or a claim to have met the requested global target.
