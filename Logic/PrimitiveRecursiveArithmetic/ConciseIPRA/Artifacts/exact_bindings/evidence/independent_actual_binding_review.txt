INDEPENDENT REVIEW: OPENER WITH ONLY GENUINE BINDERS

Reviewed candidate:
  research_math/EXPERIMENTAL_proper_binders.py
  candidates/proper_agent/proper_bytes.py

The proposed change replaces the old uniform child depth d+5-r by
  d+5-r                       at formula positions (d even),
  d+2                         at an iterator body (d odd,r=3,j=2),
  d                           at every other term child.
This removes the dummy slots of successor and of iterator counter/seed.
The change needs a new codec and a new raw-reading argument; it is not
certificate-by-certificate operational equality with the old checker.

RAW READING
A formula below p genuine numerical binders has depth 2p; a term has depth
3+2p. At numerical positions: integers in [k,0) denote rigid parameters;
0,1,2 denote zero; codes 3+2j and 4+2j denote slot j when j<p; all remaining
integers denote the substitution hole. Slots are numbered from innermost.
Scalar formula nodes denote 0=0. An array at a term position denotes:
  length 1: S(child), with the child below the same p slots;
  length 3: I(counter,seed;u.body), with counter/seed below p slots and
            the body below p+1 slots;
  other length: numerical zero, all children being ignored annotations.
An array at a formula position denotes:
  length 1: D_xy(body), with y innermost and x next, below p+2 slots;
  length 2: equality of its numerical children, below p slots;
  length 3: Q_y(last child), below p+1 slots; first two children ignored;
  other length: 0=0, all children ignored.

The raw decoder is total on the integer/list alphabet. The opener may
reject because it traverses ignored annotations too. Such rejection is
permitted: no completeness claim for arbitrary raw aliases is needed.
All semantically meaningful children are traversed at exactly the depth
specified by this reading. At ignored nodes no semantic claim about the
child's depth is required; in particular arbitrary arities may produce
negative depths only in annotations.

SEMANTIC OPENING
The old structural proof transfers with these explicitly changed binder
counts. A successful no-image scan closes every active numerical position
and preserves its reading. A successful image scan replaces the exposed
numerical hole by the reading of the image validated at root term depth3.
Copying such an image beneath additional binders causes no capture: it has
only rigid parameters and variables bound within its own subtrees. At a
formula-position hole the old parity mechanism still produces neutral
truth; it never inserts a numerical tree into a formula position.

The outermost-slot exposure lemma also transfers. Lowering an input depth
by two preserves its parity, and every new child increment depends only
on parity, arity, and child position, not the absolute depth. Therefore all
active descendant depths decrease by precisely two, exposing exactly the
previous outermost numerical slot. The usual freshness lemma is unchanged:
a no-image scan at floor k has no active parameter name below k; every
subsequently allocated smaller name is fresh.

COMPUTATION AND OTHER RULES
A matched computation leaf I(S(s),u;h)=O(h;I(s,u;h),3) validates s and u at
root term depth3 and h at depth5. Thus the right side opens precisely the
one state binder. The zero-counter computation has its usual reading.
Transport and closure elimination use semantic opening; paired elimination
uses the exposure and freshness lemmas; the two introductions use the same
lemmas and retain their equation-only progress test. Tripling a unary major
still produces Q of its body, and tripling every other nonempty array gives
an ignored neutral formula of arity>=6. Scalar and empty neutral cases keep
their previous readings. Consequently the raw successful-trace soundness
proof uses the same inference rules, with only the opener lemma changed.

PUBLIC SYNTAX IS NOW EXACT
During the initial strict scan, only Eq/D formula arrays and S/I term arrays
are admitted. At depth3+2p the positive odd scalar range is exactly
1,3,...,2p+1: zero and the p genuine numerical variables. There are no dummy
slots to reference. Thus the accepted closed public grammar is precisely
the encoder image. Decoding and re-encoding a validated public goal returns
that same tree. This is stronger than the earlier sorted-arity contract,
which admitted inaccessible dummy-variable aliases.

COMPLETENESS
Encode a native derivation using only these genuine binder positions.
Canonical opening is then literal capture-avoiding substitution on its
encodings. The existing rule-by-rule certificate construction and dynamic
negative-eigenparameter allocation apply with the new encoder. This proves
completeness for every formula in the now-exact public grammar. The
arithmetic and logical calculus, and its inherited iPRA bi-interpretation,
are unchanged. No new assumption about the standard model is required.

API AND SCHEDULING
The opener retains exactly three positional arguments. This matters because
i(*a,t,2) uses Python's argument-count check to exclude opening a binary goal
as a unary D formula. No extra parameter is added here. The order of goal
validation, floor reservation, witness preparation, major checking, and
continuation checking is unchanged from the selected reference profile.

CONCRETE FRESHNESS TEST
The closed formula D_xy(I(y,0;u.x)=I(y,0;u.y)) has the new code
  [[[3,1,7],[3,1,5]]].
Its zero row is true and its rows at1 and2 force x=1 and x=2, so it is false
in the natural numbers. A malicious packet selects witness S(beta), where
beta is the future introduction eigenparameter. The correct programs reject.
Controls which prepare the witness only after checking the neutral major
accept this false formula. For the return-reservation three-field profile
beta=-1; for the entry-reservation four-field profile beta=-2.
