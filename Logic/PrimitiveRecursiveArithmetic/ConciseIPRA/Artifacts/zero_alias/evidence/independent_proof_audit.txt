STATUS AND SCOPE

The mathematical result below is a conservative definitional change of the
public alphabet, together with an unchanged proof protocol and an explicitly
charged initial equality. It is not deletion of an arithmetic axiom.

The precise complete-checker statement is:
(S) every accepted raw goal has an ordinary reading provable in iPRA;
(C) every abstract native theorem has an accepted distinguished canonical
encoding in the new alphabet.

We do NOT claim that every redundant spelling admitted by the sorted public
grammar has a certificate whenever its ordinary reading is true/provable.
The grammar admits references to administrative dummy slots, whereas the
distinguished canonical encoder never uses them. Upstream's base completeness
proof is stated for the encoder's image. The decoded theorem set is exactly
the intended native theory, which is sufficient for its stated bi-interpretation.

1. ABSTRACT THEORIES

Write N_0 for the inherited full native calculus over 0,S,I and Eq,Q,D, or
its Q-free public form; use the ordinary realization T_eq when discussing
models. Write N_0[c] for its expansion by an extra fixed numerical constant c,
and E for c=0. Let N_c be the literal renaming of the public constant 0 to c.
Its terms are x,c,S(t),I(n,a;u.h); its public formulas are equations and D.
The ordinary meaning of D in N_c is
  D_{x,y}B := exists x (B(x,c) -> forall y B(x,y)).
Its two iteration equations use c as the initial count, its contradiction is
c=S(c), and its logic macros are the inherited macros with 0 replaced by c.

The auxiliary language of the certificate checker still has the old zero 0
and the original computations and rules, together with the initial equality
E. Therefore auxiliary D uses the old 0. Under E this agrees with the public
D reading: equality substitution gives B(x,c) <-> B(x,0), so their antecedents
and their whole D formulas are equivalent.

The public theory N_c is a renaming of N_0, and hence inherits its whole-domain,
actual-equality bi-interpretation with iPRA. The checker establishes the same
public consequences by proving them in N_0[c]+E. It does not ask the public
user to trust an unimplemented translation: E occurs literally in its initial
context, and the existing proof instructions can use it.

2. CONSERVATIVE DEFINING-EQUALITY LEMMA

For a formula A over 0,c,S,I write bar(A) for A[c:=0], and likewise for contexts.
Then, for every finite context Gamma and formula A,

  N_0[c], E, Gamma |- A  iff  N_0, bar(Gamma) |- bar(A).

Left to right: translate the native derivation, not its encoded certificate,
by the uniform replacement c:=0. Computation, equality, logical and equation-
induction rules remain valid; c is a fixed parameter and no binder captures
it. E becomes 0=0, which N_0 proves, and cut eliminates that assumption.
If the source uses the restricted-source auxiliary calculus A-minus, first
embed it into unrestricted N_0; literal-zero source restrictions need not be
preserved by this substitution.

Right to left: regard the given derivation as a derivation in N_0[c]. From E,
full equality substitution gives each A <-> bar(A), also under every Q or D
binder. Thus convert the hypotheses Gamma to bar(Gamma), apply the given
derivation, then convert bar(A) to A.

The required equality substitution is the already established full Leibniz
rule of the native calculus. If desired its extension to c can be checked
directly: term congruence is an induction on terms; at an iterator the bound
state variable is fresh for c and the replacing term. At a Q or D formula,
open the binder, apply the induction hypothesis to its matrix, and close it
again using the inherited logical rules and binder congruence. Constants
remain fixed throughout. The source restriction in A-minus has its inherited
admissibility proof, which is schematic in all fixed parameters and therefore
still applies with c in the language.

Consequences: this is equivalence of open consequence relations, not merely
equiconsistency or a theorem-level/Pi_2 conservation statement. Both numerical
domains are the full number domain, equality is actual equality, and the
graph of each domain round trip is x=y. Formula round trips are the inherited
round trips for N_0 plus this explicit defining-equality lemma.

3. RAW ALPHABET AND DECODER

The inherited scalar codec uses negative integers for rigid eigenparameters,
positive odd integers 3+2j for protected slot j, unary arrays for S or D by
sort, binary formula arrays for equations, and ternary term arrays for I.
Every term position has odd depth at least 3. The opening operation retains
an integer a when k <= a < d, with k the current nonpositive floor.

Use the parametric decoder of ch11_cert.tex, cert:prop:reserved: the reserved
raw leaf 1 at a term position denotes the new global constant c. Raw leaf 0
continues to denote auxiliary zero. Raw leaf 2 may denote a second arbitrary
constant, or may be specialized to zero; it is excluded from public goals
and is irrelevant to completeness. Positive odd protected coordinates >=3
have precisely their previous binding meanings. Dummy slots retain their
old zero reading. A scalar at a formula position has the old neutral reading.

The new distinguished encoder sends the public zero constant c to raw 1.
It is otherwise the inherited canonical encoder and never refers to a dummy
slot. Thus it is exactly replacement of term leaves 0 by 1 in the old
distinguished encoding. This statement is about encoded EXPRESSIONS only,
not complete certificates: their dispatch markers and their auxiliary old-zero
terms retain their original roles.

At every term position, raw 1 lies in [k,d) and is retained by every opening.
It is never the hole. An inserted term is checked at depth 3, where raw 1 is
again retained, so copying it below further binders does not capture it. The
semantic opening, exposure, fresh-parameter and supply lemmas therefore extend
with c fixed. This is already proved in the source's cert:prop:reserved, even
for TWO new constants. No new raw-decoder theorem is being silently assumed.

4. INITIAL CONTEXT

The candidate starts at floor 0 with g=[0,[1,0]]. The first member is the
existing neutral formula (ordinary reading 0=0). The second is precisely E,
whose parametric reading is c=0. Both trees are closed at floor 0. No
eigenparameter has been allocated by adding E, so the correct initial floor
remains 0. The program keeps k explicitly; it does not reconstruct k from
the old formula 1-len(g). With two initial entries the corresponding length
invariant would instead be k=2-len(g).

The soundness invariant of the protocol is stated for an arbitrary list g of
closed formulas, not for a singleton only (cert:thm:base-sound and subsequent
native/parametric refinements). Thus an accepting run derives its goal's
parametric reading from E. By the defining-equality lemma this yields an old
native theorem when c is read as zero. The added equality is NOT a numerical
contradiction: raw 1 is a syntactic constant code, not the numeral S(0).

The inherited failure result for a relaxed validator says exactly that, in
the unchanged old kernel and initial context, [1,0] has no certificate. The
parametric decoder can give c a nonzero value. Our checker explicitly supplies
the missing defining equality, and its source size is counted. Hence that
countermodel is excluded for a stated, charged reason.

5. EXACT TYPING AND PUBLIC GOAL CHECK

The candidate retains the whole-input printed-form test. For standard JSON
values, str(p).strip('0123456789-[], ') is empty exactly when all nodes of p
are arrays or exact integers, apart from top-level exact integers which are
then rejected because they have no pop method. Every string occurring inside
an array contributes quotation marks; booleans/null/floats/objects contribute
forbidden characters. An empty top-level array is rejected by pop. Thus the
driver accepts only a nonempty array of integer/array trees, including all
unused packets.

If the stripped string is nonempty, q is a string and q() fails; if it is
empty, q is p.pop. This is equivalent to the old inverted Boolean gating,
with the same full-input validation and no eval or hidden dependency.

During the first call v=1. The array condition is unchanged: at depth d,
arity must be 1 or 2+(d mod 2). The scalar test is
  k <= a < d and v <= (d % 2 & a).
At initial k=0,v=1 this holds exactly when d and a are odd and 0<a<d.
Therefore public leaves are c=raw1 or in-scope positive odd slots >=3.
In particular 0 and2, negative/unbound leaves, every scalar at a formula
position, and forbidden array arities are rejected. The missing-image path
forces rejection rather than silently replacing an invalid goal leaf.

After the first scan v becomes 0 before ANY major proof or subsequent
opening. For every integer a and integer depth d, (d%2)&a is 0 or1, so
0 <= ((d%2)&a) is always true. Thus the changed parity test imposes no new
restriction in raw proof mode. All proof instructions use the inherited
untyped raw protocol.

The first scan completely traverses the goal before membership is checked.
Consequently adding E to g cannot let a malformed goal evade validation.

6. IMPLEMENTATION CONTROL FLOW

The audited source uses a named def c which returns None on every successful
call. The previous lambda version returned either True (membership success)
or a nonempty list (recursive success). The old expression
  c(g,m,k,...) and m*3 or H
is therefore m*3 or H after a successful major check. The new expression
  c(g,m,k,...) or m*3 or H
has precisely the same value because the new call returns None. Both evaluate
the major before the export, and both propagate a failed major as failure.
If the checked goal is already in g, the named function falls through and
returns None without reading an instruction. Otherwise it reads exactly one
instruction and makes the same continuation call. Induction on successful
finite calls establishes equivalent control flow after matching contexts.

The assignment (v:=0) is evaluated as an argument of the major call, after
the goal scan and before the later openings. The opener i closes over v and
k in its own call of c, so it sees v=0 thereafter, and it sees the decreased
k before the newly opened hypothesis and the continuation goal are formed.
The decrement k:=~-k is k-1 for integer k. This is the crucial scope order;
it was not deleted or moved.

The main candidate never mutates g. Every continuation receives g+[entry],
a new list; every major receives the current list without mutation. The
input stack p alone is mutated by pop, as in the inherited protocol. The
alternative alias_context.py intentionally mutates g and would need a
different invariant; this audit and theorem concern alias_inverted.py.

7. COMPLETENESS AND CERTIFICATE COMPILATION

Let A be any theorem of the abstract public theory N_c, and let A_0 be its
literal renaming c:=0. By the inherited equivalence, A_0 has a derivation in
the old native calculus and in its source-restricted auxiliary presentation.
Work now with the extra fixed constant c and hypothesis E. The defining-
equality lemma gives A from A_0 by equality transport through the whole
formula, including nested binders. Equivalently compile a derivation of
E |- A in the expanded native calculus.

The inherited canonical compiler extends verbatim to this fixed constant:
encode c by raw1, which is in scope at every term depth and is true-valued.
Canonical templates and openings commute because c is never exposed as a
hole. Computation instructions still use raw0 at their actual zero counter;
an initializer c is a true-valued term and uses the ordinary computation
branch. The certified-zero repair applies to auxiliary literal0 exactly as
before. In introduction instructions the case/progress control markers are
unchanged; the witness is an expression field and may contain c. No extra
induction rule or broader induction class is introduced.

The compiler's stronger induction hypothesis permits arbitrary retained
contexts containing the encodings of the assumptions (see
cert:thm:base-complete). Instantiate it with g=[0,[1,0]] at floor0. The initial
axiom is already retained, so the compiler uses membership when E is needed.
If the extra retained axiom causes a goal to be solved earlier, the compiler
prunes that subderivation; it does not leave an obsolete block of instructions
to be consumed by the following subproof. Thus completeness is existential
certificate generation, not reuse of the same old JSON stack.

The goal enc(A) passes the new public validator by induction on A. This
establishes (C). Together with (S), the images under ordinary decoding of all
accepted goals are exactly the abstract native theorems. Open consequence
equivalence follows from Section2 (or by the inherited deduction/quantifier
macros for a closed-theorem interface).

8. RELATION TO PREVIOUS WORK AND LIMITATIONS

The source's uniform_zero package already encodes zero by1, but changes the
opener so that a retained false scalar is normalized to1. Our candidate keeps
the opener and all arithmetic proof instructions unchanged, and instead
charges the explicit initial equality [1,0]. The source's reserved-coordinate
obstruction explicitly allows this sort of repair if its cost and proof are
counted. The new saving comes from exchanging a disjunctive public-leaf test
for a parity test plus that defining equality.

This is a definitional-alphabet reduction, with a small host-language control
flow improvement. It establishes neither an absolute minimum nor a smaller
raw guarded checker. Comparisons must identify the changed canonical public
zero spelling and the same strict whole-input typing obligations. The native
abstract theory and its iPRA strength are unchanged. No Lean formalization of
this candidate, its Python semantics, or its compiler is claimed here.
