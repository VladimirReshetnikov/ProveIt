The review checked the eight-rule native inventory, binding and freshness
conditions, the formula-selector recursion and logical macros against the
source article, the distinction between native and guarded auxiliary D

The new default-expression transformation was independently checked by an
exact AST comparison and by inspecting its initialization semantics under
CPython 3.12.14. The candidate differs only by moving k=0 into the default

and does not reset k on subsequent calls. The complete-module coordinates
are 247 tokens / 261 AST nodes / 557 normalized bytes, compared with the
matched control's 246 / 262 / 556. This is a one-node improvement with
token and byte tradeoffs.

Three wording corrections were requested and are incorporated:
1. The uniform conditional reading of auxiliary D remains sound; the
   root-dependent reading is needed for the faithful inherited auxiliary
   correspondence. Soundness and faithfulness are now distinguished.
2. Exact execution equivalence is stated in the usual unbounded execution
   model. Source metadata and arbitrary physical resource failures are not
   claimed identical.
3. The relation-rigidity consequence is restricted to the algebraic
   countermodels actually considered: exact iterator identities and a
   unary-compatible relation interpreting equations.

The relation-rigidity proof was checked independently. An exhibited
distinct related pair is sent to 0,1 by separation, then to every requested
pair by amplification. The unrestricted identity-or-universal dichotomy
is explicitly an external classical statement; its constructive core and
the role of stable equality are stated separately. Exact iteration gives
amplification, while separation is not established for the weakened
calculus. The report does not claim to settle its open transitivity problem.

Limits of this review:
- The inherited Python-to-calculus correspondence and arithmetic bridge
  were inspected as stated dependencies, not independently mechanized.
- The inherited Lean sources were not recompiled or independently proved
  in this environment.
- Regression examples are supporting evidence, not a universal soundness
  proof or a replacement for the inherited correspondence.
- No new arithmetic or logical axiom deletion, simultaneous improvement
  in all size coordinates, global minimum, or complexity lower bound is
  established. The stronger requested mathematical simplification remains
  unestablished.

After the stated corrections, no further mathematical misstatement or
overclaim was identified within the scope of this review.
