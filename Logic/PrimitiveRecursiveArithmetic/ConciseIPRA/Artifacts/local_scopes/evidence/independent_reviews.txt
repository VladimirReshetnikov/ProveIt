Finite-profile invariant

Witness extraction and non-bi-interpretability
--------------------------------------------
The audit reviewed the complete relational_obstruction.tex argument. On a
tracked leading-existential branch, antecedents stay existential-free;
there is no existential-left rule. Implication-left preserves its first
premise and tracks the second; disjunction-left tracks both. Extraction
stops when the designated output tuple has been supplied, leaving the
arbitrary matrix proof untouched. Falsity leaves use zero witnesses.
Extraneous parameters are removed after eigenvariable freshening. Equality
is handled by universal atomic congruence and equivalence axioms.

The O(N^d) range bound for finite d-tuples of B witnesses was checked against
the N^k disjoint fibers required for compression. The source-side return
isomorphism removes all interpreted-domain conditions from source inputs.
Interpreted arithmetic tupling, equality congruence, and inverse-functionality
of that isomorphism provide disjoint fibers; no choice function, unique raw
representative, or surjectivity of the return relation is required. The
parameter extension includes the finite parameters in the output tuple and
requires their condition to be provably inhabited.

Limits retained after review
----------------------------
The main non-bi-interpretability theorem requires ordinary intuitionistic
logic, finite-dimensional first-order interpretations, an existential-free
axiomatization in the B term language, and its standard numerical model.
It does not exclude equiconsistency alone, all one-way interpretations,
arbitrary positive-existence extensions, or every possible new numerical
basis. The independently checked 668-byte I checker remains a modest
implementation change to an inherited complete theory.
