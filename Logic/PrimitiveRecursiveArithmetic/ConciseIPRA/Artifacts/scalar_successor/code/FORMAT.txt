SCALAR-R GENERATED-SOURCE CERTIFICATE FORMAT
============================================

Input is one JSON array. The final item is a closed formula. Earlier items
are four-field instructions [m,s,u,t], consumed by pop() from the end.
Prove the major before its continuation. A goal in context consumes no
instruction. An unused prefix is inert. ACCEPT and exit 0 means success;
REJECT and nonzero exit means failure, possibly with a traceback.

RAW TREES
---------
Arity   Numerical meaning                  Formula meaning
0       zero                               truth (0=0)
1       successor (a defined abbreviation) Q conditional closure
2       zero with checked annotations      equality
3       scalar successor-seeded R          paired D closure

Every traversed array has arity at most three. The opening depth increases
by 2, 1, 4 at unary, binary, ternary nodes respectively. Formula depth 2q
and numerical depth 2q+1 protect q slots. Positive coordinates 2j-1 and 2j
alias the j-th protected numerical slot. Zero is zero. Negative labels are
allocated rigid parameters; the lower floor begins at 0 and decreases by
one for every non-hypothesis continuation, including arithmetic requests.

A unary numerical node has one dummy-zero slot. Counter and initializer
children of R have two dummy zeros. Its update has two aliases of ONE
state variable. They are not two independently bound update arguments.
For D the protected slots are instead two distinct variables, nearest y,
then x. Its first two fields are checked padding. Formula Q protects y.

Q_y B means B(0) -> forall y B(y).
D_xy B means exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y)).

OPENING
-------
The routine i(a,t,d) retains numerical leaves in [k,d), substitutes t at
an exposed numerical coordinate, and replaces exposed formula leaves by
truth. A used image is itself validated at numerical root. Missing used
images reject. The strict AST profile's exception-based list traversal
retains the a+[] guard. Strings and mappings used as syntax reject.
Canonical proofs use integers and arrays; inherited Boolean and finite
fractional aliases have the documented integer-floor interpretation.

INSTRUCTIONS
------------
1. Truthy major m: first prove m in the retained context.

   If m=[s,u] or (s is false and m=[t]), and i(t,u) is the current goal,
   change the goal to i(t,s). These are equality transport and Q use.
   The new assumption m[2:] is empty/truth at these arities. If endpoints
   or the target fail to match, keep the original goal and add only truth.
   A three-field D major contributes the fresh opened Q clause instead.
   The major proof is checked even for a neutral endpoint request.

2. Falsy m and truthy original t: introduce D using numerical witness t.

   Open the third formula field with t, leaving the induction coordinate.
   Marker m=[] chooses the base hypothesis. Marker m=0 chooses the current
   hypothesis only for a literal equation; at a compound matrix it still
   chooses the base. The new goal is the fresh successor instance.
   A truthy zero encoding such as numerical [0,0] can represent witness 0.

3. Falsy m and falsy original t: generate a computation assumption.

   s=[n,a,h] supplies an old scalar recursor; original u is a clause flag.
   For canonical u=0, generate R(0,a;h)=S(a), ignoring the supplied n.
   For canonical u=1, generate R(Sn,a;h)=h(R(n,a;h)). The successor source
   is constructed by the kernel. The old counter n can be ANY numerical
   term. It need not itself have a successor shape.

   The exact source is:
       n,t,v=s
       h=[[u and [n],t,v],i(v,s,1) if u else [t]]

   If the update uses its recursive image, insertion validates the entire
   old recursor before fresh allocation. If the update ignores it, the
   generated successor equation does not depend on its value. The common
   context opening still checks all meaningful generated syntax.

SHARED CONTINUATION AND THE ZERO WITNESS
---------------------------------------
Allocate k-1, open the requested induction coordinate to successor [k],
append one opened local formula, and recurse. The new assumption's image
is k when m is truthy or len(a)%2==m; otherwise it is zero.

A generated equation H(w) may not hold for every w, but its zero instance
is a theorem. The old goal and retained context exclude that fresh w.
Substituting zero and cutting the computation theorem discharges H(w).
This known-instance rule is proved in the report; there is no theorem
oracle or numeric evaluator in any checker.

CANONICAL SERIALIZATION
-----------------------
R0 leaves use [m=0,s=[0,a,h],u=0,t=0]. RS leaves supply the predecessor
recursion description [n,a,h] and u=1. All other packets follow the rules
above. Labels must be regenerated after every shared continuation; copying
packets from the older supplied-source protocol does not preserve meaning.
The untrusted named producer reserializes these choices and is tested by
the complete checkers. It is not a verified universal arithmetic compiler.
