INDEPENDENT REVIEW: THREE-FAMILY EVALUATION AT WITNESS DISCHARGE

Reviewed implementation
-----------------------
agent_logic/checker.py, SHA-256
d4aed2e227fd0f018e40215c4cdeda1e2ec62cb6700286fbdd905794807fc842

The earlier goal-directed prototype agent_logic/eval_discharge.py was also
inspected. This review's concrete checks and hash refer to checker.py, the
inference-synthesizing version with clause-count scopes.

Conclusion
----------
No scope, capture, normalization, or inference-soundness defect was found in
this review. The proposed fusion has an elementary exact correspondence
with separate SeedEval and witness elimination. The essential restriction
is that only the normalized exported formula, after the evaluation map, is
required to be free of the fresh witness. The checker enforces that order.
This is a written code/mathematical review and a small adversarial test set,
not a proof-assistant verification of the implementation.

Mathematical correspondence
--------------------------
Let ev_E(F) return F unless E is s=t and F is I(S c,s;u.h)=r. In that
matching case it returns r=nf(h[I(c,t;u.h)/u]). A fused discharge proves
D B, opens its witness clause at fresh b, proves E and F in that same
extended context, and exports ev_E(F) if that formula is b-free.

In the separate calculus the matching branch is one SeedEval application
inside the opened scope, followed by ordinary witness elimination. In
the nonmatching branch ordinary elimination applies directly to F. This
proves soundness in the old calculus and also explains why E may be ANY
formula, rather than an equation with a mandatory successful match.

Conversely, ordinary witness elimination is recovered by selecting the
already proved compound major D B as the auxiliary E in its opened scope.
Weakening is admissible: injective renaming and insertion of an unused
clause preserve all literal shape tests. Since E has D root, ev_E(F)=F.
This uses no numerical 0/1 case and no special choice of a mismatching seed.

SeedEval is recovered by opening the closed dummy theorem
theta = D_{x,y}(0=0). Introduce theta at witness 0: its progressive branch
has the local ordinary assumption 0=0 and uses that assumption. Thus theta
does not require the fixed reflexivity axiom clause. Under the dummy opened
clause, weaken the two supplied SeedEval premises and apply fused discharge.
Its result is independent of the dummy witness, so discharge is permitted.

Together with unchanged Use and Intro, this gives exact translations of
finite derivations in both directions. The axiom clause of universal
reflexivity remains a separately counted assumption of both presentations.
No rule-count claim should hide it.

Zero-only normalization and substitution
---------------------------------------
The representation has integer 0, negative free-variable levels, positive
bound indices, successor [0,t], iterator [n,a,h], equation [s,t], and D [B].
Only the iterator's third child adds one binder; the sole D child adds two.
The recursive walk rebuilds the tree and contracts [0,a,h] to a. It does
not unfold an iterator whose counter is a successor. In particular this
is not the full computation normalizer used by earlier Name presentations.

The omission of a shift of an inserted term is correct under the actual
invariant: every substituted t is an externally well-scoped numerical term
with no loose positive bound indices. Its own internal bound indices remain
under their own binders. Inserting it below another binder therefore needs
no change to its representation. Every substitution site in the checker
meets this condition:

* Use supplies an externally validated numerical term.
* Intro and witness opening insert such a term, 0, a fresh negative level,
  or its successor.
* SeedEval extracts h from the root iterator of an equation. Thus h has
  just its own one open iterator variable, and I(c,t;u.h) closes that variable.
  This inserted iterator has no loose positive index.

The SeedEval optimization uses t directly when c is literally 0, otherwise
it constructs [c,t,h]. The latter is already zero-normal because c,t,h were
parts of fully validated normal formulas and c is not 0. Subsequent opening
of h performs every new surrounding zero contraction.

Scope discipline
----------------
The proof starts with exactly one clause (universal reflexivity), so n=1
means zero free numerical parameters. A retained context of length n has
the free levels 1-n,...,-1. Each Intro or fused discharge adds exactly one
clause and creates the fresh parameter b=-n. No other instruction allocates
a parameter or changes context length. This justifies deriving the scope
bound from clause count for the closed-theorem certificate interface.

Every recursive prove result is validated by syntax(A,n,True). Consequently
an exported formula containing b is rejected, whether the evaluation map
matches or falls back. The public goal is independently validated with n=1.
The validator checks exact integer types (not bool), formula/term sorts,
successor's zero tag, constructor arities, bound-index depth, free levels,
and zero-normality. Returning the normalized tree is not sufficient: it must
equal the original syntax, so nonnormal raw inputs are rejected.

Intro validates its matrix through the final synthesized result [B0], after
checking the branch. Its earlier traversal may process malformed material,
but successful acceptance necessarily reaches and passes that final complete
matrix check. This delayed validation does not allow an ill-scoped or
nonnormal matrix to be accepted. For example, a zero-headed iterator erased
in the branch is still rejected if present in the original matrix B0.

Primitive-recursive certificate relation
---------------------------------------
All computational recursion is structural on a finite syntax or proof tree.
The normalizer only deletes nodes; it never recursively calls itself on a
newly unfolded successor computation. Substitution traverses the ORIGINAL
input tree once and inserts the supplied term as a value, without traversing
that inserted copy again. With tree-node size |.|, a valid substitution has
the conservative bound

  |sub(a,t)| <= |a| * max(1,|t|).

Zero contraction can only decrease this bound. A SeedEval result constructs
at most one iterator before one such substitution. Other rule operations
are validation, comparison, clause lookup, list extension, and finitely many
substitutions. Each recursive proof call consumes a proper supplied
subcertificate. A primitive-recursive majorant follows by iterating, for
example, a sufficiently generous polynomial size/time bound once per proof
node. This bounds intermediate formula sizes and the cost of their traversals.
Thus the ideal finite-tree certificate predicate is primitive recursive.
No uniform numerical evaluator, full-normalizer totality axiom, or supplied
normalization fuel is needed. Actual Python processes retain their ordinary
parser, recursion, and memory resource limits.

Novelty and comparison boundaries
--------------------------------
At pinned repository commit 11814771b7490dfbea0ed5fcf0f5b23586334764, the
filed seed_evaluation and clause_contexts packages already supply the main
arithmetic interface and four-family clause calculus. The incoming
substitutive_discharge package combines general equality replacement with
witness discharge but keeps a separate calculation rule. The incoming
clause_discharge package has three families but uses full computation normal
forms (and either explicit Name or inspection of its own witness clause).
The incoming prefix_clauses package simplifies formula storage, not this
fusion. The new distinction is three families with deterministic root
SeedEval at witness discharge and zero-only normalization.

This reduction does not establish a global minimum and cannot be advertised
as beating every compact checker in the repository. Any source-size claim
must use the actual measured matched control and state the other distinct
profiles, including the known compact 516-byte profile. The exact mathematical
equivalence, rather than the bare count of rule names, is the reliable result.

ADDENDUM: RAW THREE-FAMILY PRESENTATION (NO NORMALIZATION)
========================================================

Reviewed implementation
-----------------------
agent_math/checker_raw.py, SHA-256
53fea58739a0972806bfeeaeca347c5842e5db91c79810ef5a7974d588f4254f

No defect was found in the raw sibling. It is a particularly clean main
mathematical presentation, because its separate-rule comparator is exactly
the raw VPrf calculus already present in NativeUniformClauses.lean. It does
not require the zero-normalization layer, a full normalizer, or a fuel field.
The presence of two computation patterns inside one total evaluation map
should be stated explicitly rather than hidden by the three-family count.

Exact arithmetic patterns
------------------------
The raw evaluation map has these disjoint successful cases:

  E = s=t,                 F = I(0,s;u.h)=r :  ev_E(F) = r=t.
  E = I(c,a;u.h)=t,        F = I(S c,a;u.h)=r : ev_E(F) = r=h[t/u].

Otherwise ev_E(F)=F. These are precisely VPrf.evalZero and VPrf.evalSucc
in NativeUniformClauses.lean, also EPrf.evalZero and EPrf.evalSucc in
NativeEval.lean. The guards compare the entire appropriate seed or
predecessor term, including c,a,h; matching merely an iterator head would
be unsound, but the implementation performs the required full comparison.
The two successful patterns cannot overlap because raw 0 is distinct from
the successor constructor [0,c].

The same generic fusion proof applies without any normalization qualifications:
in the old VPrf calculus, perform the matched Eval inside the witness scope
and then eliminate D; in the fallback case directly eliminate D. Conversely,
recover ordinary elimination with a compound auxiliary and recover each raw
Eval case by opening theta=D(0=0). Every term and end formula is literally
unchanged by the translations. Thus the equivalence is on the same raw
syntax and open consequence relation. Composing with vprf_iff_cprf and the
repository's native/arithmetic bridge needs no new normalization theorem.

Code and capture checks
-----------------------
walk now only rebuilds its input; no constructor is contracted. The
previous valid substitution invariant is unchanged: inputs inserted by sub
are externally scoped numerical terms without loose positive indices.
In the successor case h is the root iterator's body, hence has precisely
one open external binder to instantiate. The Eq root of F ensures that
there is no unaccounted surrounding formula binder. Nested iterator binders
are handled by walk's depth increments. In the zero case h is deliberately
discarded by the arithmetic inference, not by a syntactic normalizer.

This distinction matters when h mentions the witness: the rule can export
a witness-free result even when its premise mentions b in the discarded
body. All premise formulas are still validated before dispatch. The final
syntax(A,n,True) check prevents witness escape in both successful and
fallback cases.

The public wrapper no longer insists that type(data) is dict before
checking its keys. For finite JSON inputs this does not create an acceptance
gap: non-object inputs either fail set(data)=={'goal','proof'} or fail the
subsequent string-key access. Acceptance still requires the exact two keys,
a valid closed goal, and a fully checked derivation. This is an input-wrapper
observation only, not part of the mathematical reduction.

Raw certificate verification is primitive recursive for the same structural
reason, now with the simpler substitution bound and no normalization step
at all. Each successful evaluation performs either no substitution (zero)
or one ordinary binder-opening substitution (successor). It does not run a
numerical evaluator for primitive recursive programs.

Independent raw fixtures
------------------------
raw_independent_fixtures/ contains 12 independently constructed examples
and results.json. Each ran under ordinary Python and python -O: all 24
expected outcomes were observed.

Five accepted examples:
* Raw zero computation produces I(0,a;u.h)=a while retaining the raw iterator
  in its conclusion, demonstrating absence of hidden normalization.
* Successor computation with a nested iterator body checks capture avoidance:
  h=I(1,u;v.u), with its inner occurrence of outer u at bound index 2.
* The zero evaluation case removes a witness occurring in its discarded body.
* The successor evaluation case removes a witness from both its predecessor
  and successor-iterator premises; the constant body yields 0=0.
* A compound auxiliary forces the ordinary-elimination fallback.

Seven rejected examples:
* A mismatching seed attempts to derive the false equation I(0,0;u.0)=1.
* A mismatching predecessor attempts to derive the false equation
  I(1,0;u.u)=1.
* Fallback tries to export the fresh witness.
* Matching zero evaluation leaves the fresh witness in its result.
* A term has a loose positive bound index.
* A Boolean is supplied as a numerical term.
* A malformed successor has a nonzero tag.

The raw fusion is not the already delivered substitutive_discharge result:
that presentation retained calculation as an independent family and used
canonical successor-seeded terms. Here both existing raw computation cases
are absorbed into witness discharge, with raw ordinary substitution alone.
The incoming full-normalization Name variants likewise do not implement
this raw three-family inference basis. This establishes scoped novelty
against the inspected repository material, not historical priority over
all possible proof presentations.
