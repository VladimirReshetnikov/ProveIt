Independent mathematical review: fused finite logical blocks
============================================================

Reviewed source: math_result/fused_finite_blocks_note.md.
Frozen comparison: ProveIt commit 1156099ffe30f7497d2c11ab20271f46cffcd115.

Conclusion
----------
The construction is valid in the inherited full native Eq/D calculus,
under the displayed ordinary reading and with the stated freshness
conditions. It is a new formula-size refinement of the documented finite
block compiler. It is not a new axiom deletion or a smaller proof checker.

1. Gate and padding
-------------------
Replacing the terminal equation s=t by C(c,0,s)=C(c,0,t) leaves the D
prefix unchanged. At c=0 the terminal equality is reflexive and every
outer D is provable by case introduction with witness zero. At c=S(v)
the terminal equation converts to s=t, so binder congruence gives the
original formula. If x,y are fresh for A, D_{x,y}G_y(A) is equivalent to A:
open D and specialize its closure at1 in one direction; introduce by cases
from A in the other. No induction on a quantified formula is required.

2. Aligned selection
--------------------
At common D prefixes, alpha-rename both bound pairs to the same fresh pair
and recursively select their bodies. Pad a shorter operand by the gate
construction at every unmatched level. External induction on maximum
rank proves M_0(A,B) interderivable with A and M_{S(v)}(A,B) with B.
Numerical coverage therefore gives both common-conclusion elimination
and introduction from both operands, for every control term.

The exact terminal payload is T(A)+T(B)+2|c|+2+6|r(A)-r(B)|. There are
two occurrences of the control, two selectors, and precisely one 6-node
gate per unmatched D level. Fresh renaming prevents a control's free
variables from being captured by a merged prefix.

3. Let and arithmetic map
-------------------------
Putting I(1,a;w.s) and I(1,a;w.t) at the two terminal positions gives
the capture-avoiding substitution A[a/w] by the two computation laws.
It contributes exactly 2|a|+6 raw term nodes. The source's fused absorbing
map H has26 nodes at a variable input, H(2m)=3m, H(2m+1)=0.
These arithmetic identities are inherited proved dependencies; standard
numerical behavior alone would not suffice as their justification.

4. New selector and reachability
-------------------------------
Sigma_(n+1)(v) = Let_(w=H(v)) M_w(A0,Sigma_n(w;A1,...,An)).
One level contributes 58 nodes for Let_H and4 for M_w, hence62.
The recursive syntax is always built at a fresh variable w, so nested
occurrences do not duplicate an expanded H inside subsequent H bodies.
This is actual raw-tree syntax with explicit one-step iterator lets,
not uncharged sharing.

The positive numeral2^j selects Aj. Its route is
2^j, 3*2^(j-1), ..., 3^j,0.
Every intermediate even input yields a positive output and selects the
tail; the final odd input yields zero and selects the current first
operand. A fully general induction statement permits every positive odd
multiple of2^j. Restricting those multipliers to closed standard odd
numerals already suffices for the displayed witness2^j; symbolic odd
multipliers also follow from the inherited arithmetic identities.

Uniform introduction from all operands and common-conclusion elimination
are external inductions on the finite list of operands, using numerical
cases at each node. They assert nothing about termination of the orbit
of an arbitrary numerical control. At the last leaf the control is ignored.

5. Padding and outer logical block
---------------------------------
For descending operand ranks in a right comb, unmatched-depth charges
telescope to rmax-rmin. Among all binary trees of aligned selectors, a
path from the root to a minimum-rank leaf has total rank drop rmax-rmin;
each node's padding charge is at least its contribution to that drop.
Thus this padding is optimal within that expressly limited construction.

For conjunction use D_(u,z) G_z(Sigma_n(z)), with u unused.
For disjunction use D_(x,z) G_z(Sigma_n(x)).
The conjunction closure specializes at the positive code2^j, so excluding
the zero control by the outer gate loses no operand. The generic
universal-quantifier macro with a predecessor substitution is NOT needed.
For disjunction, choose witness2^j for introduction; for elimination open
at fresh b, specialize the gate closure at1, and use uniform elimination
on Sigma_n(b). This is intuitionistic and valid over arbitrary contexts.

The rank is rmax+1. Full expanded size is exactly

  sum_j T(Aj) +62(n-1)+6(rmax-rmin)+rmax+8.

The final8 consists of six gate-term nodes, one new D, and the terminal
equality node. The old D prefix contributes rmax.

6. Comparison nuance
--------------------
The source's documented 66-node branch uses the 21-node ceiling ramp.
Its tail is selected only at even controls. Replacing that ramp by the
existing20-node floor ramp therefore already gives a valid64-node
intermediate, since the ramps agree on even inputs. The new absorbing-map
fusion gives62, hence saves2 beyond that intermediate and4 beyond the
documented66. No optimality of62 or of overall formula size is proved.

The original66 construction and counts appear as outlined claims in
ch01_base.tex, question base:q:blocks. Its proof completeness should not
be overstated; this note supplies full derivations for the new construction.
