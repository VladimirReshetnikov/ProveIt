INDEPENDENT REVIEW: PRIMITIVE-POSITIVE EVENTUALLY-CONSTANT MODEL OBSTRUCTION

Reviewed file: logic_audit_work/pp_obstruction.tex
Reviewer: axiom_design agent, 2026-11-10
Status: The mathematical argument is sound on its stated ordinary first-order
interpretation interface. I recommend the small semantic clarification below.
This is a written review, not a proof-assistant verification or external review.

1. Primitive-positive transfer

The induction on formulas is valid. Every finite tuple of eventually constant
parameters is equal to one fixed tuple outside a finite set. If an existential
formula has a witness at each coordinate, choose witnesses separately on that
finite exceptional set and one witness for the common tail. The assembled
witness is eventually constant. For nested existential quantifiers, the same
argument applies to the inner primitive-positive matrix by structural induction.
No uniform definable choice function or infinitary choice principle is needed.
Finitarity of every signature symbol is essential for closure and common tails.

2. Regular axioms and primitive-positive induction

True regular implications transfer because a true antecedent supplies its
coordinatewise antecedents and the reverse transfer reconstructs witnesses in
the consequent. True negative constraints transfer at any one coordinate.

For induction, the crucial localizing sequence really does work. Given the
global base A(0,p) and the global step, fix coordinate i and suppose A(k,p(i)).
Take n(i)=k and n(j)=0 for j != i. The base supplies A(n(j),p(j)) at every other
coordinate. Primitive-positive transfer gives the global premise A(n,p), so
the global step yields A(k+1,p(i)). External ordinary induction in standard N
then proves each coordinate instance; transfer finishes arbitrary n in E.
The parameters never change. Retained contextual assumptions need not be pp:
the model satisfies the full displayed induction sentence, so the usual rule
under arbitrary retained assumptions is sound there.

3. Intuitionistic semantics: the precise claim needed

A classical structure is a one-world intuitionistic Kripke model. Interpret an
ordinary intuitionistic first-order bi-interpretation in this one-world model;
the result is a classical, one-world model M of iPRA. The proof needs only the
fact that this classical domain structure has no nonidentity finite-order
automorphism. It does NOT need to assert that arbitrary multiworld Kripke
models, including their frames, have no finite-order automorphisms. Frame
permutations can introduce irrelevant automorphisms even when each numerical
domain is fixed.

Suggested clarifying sentence after the first automorphism paragraph:

  "Only automorphisms of the classical numerical domain of the one-node
  interpreted model are used here; no rigidity claim about general Kripke
  frames is needed."

The definable linear order in every classical iPRA model is standard arithmetic
available with quantifier-free induction. One may use x <= y iff x dotminus y
= 0 (the primitive-recursive truncated subtraction formulation), or the
displayed existential addition definition. In either formulation, iPRA proves
the order laws and totality. An automorphism preserves this parameter-free
definable order. If its order divides r, a strict increase or decrease would
give a strict cycle after r iterations. Hence it is the identity.

4. Quotients, dimensions, and parameters

A definable interpretation with domain D subset E^d and definable quotient
equality is functorial under any automorphism fixing its finitely many defining
parameters: apply the automorphism coordinatewise to representatives. The
well-definedness and inverse follow from invariance of D and the equivalence
relation. The induced automorphism's order divides the original order; it need
not have exactly that order, and the proof correctly says 'dividing two'.

Parameters of the second interpretation and of a return isomorphism can be
represented by finitely many finite tuples in E, even when those parameters
are quotient classes. Choosing a tail swap fixing all those representatives
is stronger than fixing their classes and is sufficient. If parameters are
chosen from a definable parameter family, choose any permitted finite tuple
first; the same tail argument applies. The source-side definable isomorphism
then is equivariant, and its injectivity forces the original tail swap to be
the identity. This handles arbitrary finite dimension and arbitrary formulas
in domains, graphs, quotient equality, and return maps.

5. Concrete limits of the model: coverage and Boolean range

Let e_i be 1 at coordinate i and 0 elsewhere. It is eventually constant.
It is not 0, and it cannot equal S(b): any successor sequence is positive at
every coordinate, whereas e_i has zero coordinates. Thus the true standard
sentence

  forall x (x = 0 or exists y (x = S(y)))

fails in E. This is a concrete disjunctive axiom outside the regular fragment.

If the signature includes the PR sign function sg(0)=0, sg(Sn)=1, then
sg(e_i)=e_i. Therefore both sg(e_i)=0 and sg(e_i)=1 are false in E. The standard
Boolean-range principle sg(x)=0 or sg(x)=1 also fails. Its usual pointwise
equational identities remain true, demonstrating why those equations alone
do not supply the missing cases principle. These facts are compatible with
classical excluded middle, which the one-node structure satisfies: the
missing disjunction is a substantive arithmetic assertion, not an absence of
classical logical reasoning.

If addition and truncated subtraction are present, e_i and e_j for i != j
are incomparable under the pointwise arithmetic order. Thus totality of that
order is another true standard disjunction not preserved by this construction.

These examples give a concrete boundary for the obstruction. They do not
claim an optimal logical fragment, rule out every axiom outside regular
logic, or prove any source-size lower bound.

6. Scope and novelty wording

The 'same conclusion for every theory having E as a model' statement is
correct for theories in the displayed signature (or expansions retaining the
same tail-swap automorphism property). It should not be read as covering
arbitrary non-pointwise expansions of the underlying set E, which may destroy
the symmetry. The final scope paragraph already makes this distinction.

Attribution to the earlier equation/conjunctive-induction EC construction is
appropriate. The existential-transfer/pp-induction extension is the actual
new result relative to the inspected corpus. The manuscript correctly avoids
a literature-wide priority claim or a positive smallest-axiomatization claim.

ADDENDUM: UNIVERSAL QUANTIFIERS AND ARBITRARY FINITE ALTERNATION

The stronger transfer claim proposed after the initial review is valid.
Replace 'primitive-positive' by the precisely displayed conjunctive grammar

  C ::= atom | top | C and C | exists x C | forall x C.

Carry out one simultaneous structural induction on all formulas of this
grammar. The atomic, conjunction, and existential clauses in the original
proof remain correct. In the existential reverse clause, the induction
hypothesis is for the entire smaller matrix, which may now itself contain
universal quantifiers. At each exceptional coordinate choose a scalar witness
satisfying that whole matrix; choose one such scalar witness at the common
tail tuple. No future universally quantified variable has been fixed in making
that choice: if the smaller matrix begins with a universal, the selected
scalar witness satisfies it for all scalar values already. The induction
hypothesis then turns this into satisfaction for all permitted sequences.

Universal forward clause: Suppose E satisfies forall x C(x,p). Fix coordinate
i and scalar u. The constant sequence with value u is in E. Instantiate the
universal with that sequence, apply the induction hypothesis, and read off
N satisfies C(u,p(i)). Since u was arbitrary, N satisfies forall x C(x,p(i)).

Universal reverse clause: Suppose every coordinate satisfies forall x C(x,p(i)).
Take an arbitrary sequence a in E. At each coordinate C(a(i),p(i)) holds.
The induction hypothesis gives E satisfies C(a,p). Since a was arbitrary,
E satisfies forall x C(x,p).

These clauses do not impose any alternation bound. For example, under
forall x exists y forall z exists w R(x,y,z,w,p), after x is assigned an
eventually constant sequence, the scalar y witnesses can be chosen to satisfy
the complete remaining forall z exists w formula. This gives one eventually
constant y before z is chosen. For each later z sequence, the finite tuple
(x,y,z,p) again has a common tail, allowing an eventually constant w. Thus
there is no hidden reversal of quantifiers or illegal anticipation of a future
universal choice.

The localized induction proof then applies verbatim to every formula C in
this enlarged class. Each standard-true implication C -> D between two such
formulas, as well as C -> falsity, also transfers by the same argument as
before. These implications are no longer all 'regular' in the usual logical
terminology, because C and D may contain universals. Prefer the explicit
grammar and 'conjunctive sequents' or 'positive Horn formulas' over extending
the term 'regular' without explanation.

The model and automorphism argument are unchanged. Arbitrary interpretation
formulas remain allowed in the no-bi-interpretation theorem; the conjunctive
restriction applies to the proposed source axiom/induction architecture, not
to the candidate interpretations.

This does not imply that the model satisfies induction for all ordinary
formulas of arithmetic. Although standard arithmetic can Booleanize many
matrices using PR characteristic functions, the equations of those functions
do not force their outputs to be globally either 0 or 1 in E. The sign(e_i)
counterexample above explicitly witnesses that failure. A claimed replacement
of ordinary disjunction or negation by such equations would need its own
internal equivalence theorem, which is precisely unavailable in this model.

Final verdict on the strengthened theorem: accepted as a written mathematical
argument, subject to the same one-node semantics, parameter, and signature
scope qualifications documented above.
