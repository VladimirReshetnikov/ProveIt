Scope
-----
This is an independent written audit of the proposed composition of the
source-dispatch transport restriction with root-determined introduction.
It is not a proof-assistant verification and does not certify Python code.
The source proofs inspected were source_dispatch/proofs/
zero_source_admissibility.tex and guarded_core/sections/guarded_core.tex.

The correct system has TWO syntax levels.

1. Public formulas are the canonical formulas G of guarded_core: numerical
   equalities, and D_xy B where B is compound or, when atomic, has the
   zero-initialized form I(y,0;u.h)=I(y,0;u.k).
2. Auxiliary derivations permit unrestricted atomic matrices beneath D.
   At atomic matrices the sole D-introduction is progress; at compound
   matrices it is cases. D-elimination exports the ordinary Q clause.
   Transport is permitted only when its source term is not literally 0.

The intended claim is equality of consequence relations ON PUBLIC CANONICAL
SEQUENTS. It must not be strengthened to an equivalence of all auxiliary
formulas with their original conditional-universal reading.

A. THE BOOTSTRAP IS ACYCLIC
--------------------------
In the auxiliary calculus, contextual equation induction is immediate.
Given Gamma |- E(0) and Gamma,E(z) |- E(Sz), use atomic-progress D with
an unused witness, eliminate its D to obtain Q_y E(y), and specialize Q
using E(0). This uses no transport. The auxiliary D is essential here:
E need not have the public zero-initialized guard.

Numerical cases for equations follow by the same derived induction rule,
ignoring the local induction hypothesis. Numerical cases for compound
formulas follow by compound-case D, D-elimination and Q-specialization.
Consequently every numerical-cases instance used by the source-dispatch
zero-source proof is available, without padding and without any preceding
unrestricted substitution theorem.

The source-dispatch proof of numerical equality then applies literally:
(i) reflexivity from I(0,r;u.0)=r using its I-headed source;
(ii) safe symmetry/congruence at nonzero syntactic sources;
(iii) joining two nonzero syntactic expressions known equal to zero;
(iv) the unconditional singleton test L(Sv)=Z(v) and zero filter
      v=C(Z(v),v,0), proved by numerical cases;
(v) in the FIXED context {0=t}, equation induction on
      n=I(n,t;u.Su), yielding 1=St;
(vi) the singleton test yields Z(t)=1 and the filter yields t=0.

Every use of equation induction here fixes its passive parameters. The
argument never substitutes zero into a derivation whose transport source
is a variable. Its arbitrary helper targets come from the derived cases
rule, while substitutions into explicit helper derivations preserve the
S/I root of every source, as documented in the source proof.

After (v)-(vi), full numerical symmetry, congruence and transitivity follow
by the existing zero-join argument. Thus transport is recovered for EVERY
equation template, including a literal-zero source.

B. CANONICAL FORMULA TRANSPORT IS RECOVERED
------------------------------------------
Use external structural induction on the public canonical template F(v).
The equation case is full numerical equality algebra from A.

For F(v)=D_xy B(v,x,y) with COMPOUND B, eliminate F(s), obtaining
Q_y B(s,a,y). Introduce F(t) by compound-case D at the fresh witness a.
Use the induction hypothesis and reversed source equality to convert the
new local B(t,a,0) into B(s,a,0); use Q at Sz; use the induction hypothesis
for the forward conversion to B(t,a,Sz). Close both inferences.

For F(v)=D_xy B(v,x,y) with GUARDED ATOMIC B, eliminate F(s) at a.
The equation B(s,a,0) is an unconditional theorem: both sides compute to 0.
Q therefore gives B(s,a,Sz), and full equation transport gives B(t,a,Sz).
Introduce F(t) by atomic-progress D, IGNORING its local B(t,a,z).
This proof uses the guard's true base; it would be invalid for an arbitrary
auxiliary atomic matrix.

Thus every primitive full-transport instance of G is derivable. The other
G rules already are auxiliary rules. This provides an effective translation
of all G derivations, including derivations from retained assumptions.

C. SOUNDNESS OF ALL AUXILIARY PROOFS
-----------------------------------
Interpret equations as actual equality. Interpret D_xy B as
  exists x forall y (B -> B[Sy/y])          if B is literally an equation;
  exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y))      if B is compound.
Interpret the exported Q resource as B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y).

Atomic-progress introduction is sound directly. Atomic D-elimination is
sound because contextual equation induction converts progress into the
exported ordinary Q. Compound introduction/elimination uses ordinary
coverage. Restricted transport is sound because numerical substitution
does not change whether a matrix is an equation. These checks also cover
auxiliary intermediate formulas not belonging to the public grammar.

On every public guarded atomic matrix, its base is provable, so progress
and conditional-universal closure are equivalent in iPRA. Structural
induction extends this agreement to all public formulas and their retained
contexts. Soundness followed by the inherited G/iPRA completeness theorem
therefore gives the converse inclusion for canonical sequents.

The public calculus consequently inherits the established whole-domain
bi-interpretation with iPRA. Numerical terms and actual equality are
unchanged, so its numerical composites retain the identity graph x=y.

D. A CLAIM THAT WOULD BE FALSE
-----------------------------
If the same syntactic restrictions are imposed but intermediate formulas
are ALSO required to be canonical, the result is weaker. There is a
syntactic counterinterpretation in the standard natural numbers:
  (0=t)* = true,                           if the left side is literally 0;
  (s=t)* = ordinary equality,              otherwise;
  (D_xy B)* = exists x (B*(x,0) -> forall y B*(x,y)).
Every atomic formula beneath a public D is guarded and has an I-headed
left side. Its translation therefore commutes with numerical substitution.
Translate a retained clause <A;y.B> as A* -> forall y B*.

Computations have I-headed sources and are true. Guarded progress is sound
by induction; compound cases and D-elimination are sound. Nonzero-source
transport is sound: changing its source s to t can change a bare equation
from ordinary equality to true when its left side becomes literal 0, but
can never change true to ordinary equality. Instance formation for clauses
has the same one-way property. Hence every rule remains sound.

At x=1, the assumption 0=x is interpreted as true, while x=0 is false.
Therefore 0=x |- x=0 is NOT derivable in that canonical-only restricted
calculus. The unrestricted auxiliary equation induction in A is a real
logical resource, not dispensable notation.

E. DELIVERY QUALIFICATIONS
--------------------------
The written argument above has no unresolved mathematical gap identified
in this review, provided the two syntax levels and inference rules are
stated exactly as above. It does not establish:
* completeness on every unrestricted auxiliary formula;
* full zero-source transport through every auxiliary atomic D;
* a smaller checker under an input contract that requires rejection of
  every noncanonical goal unless that validator is included and measured;
* global minimality or proof-assistant verification.

In particular, do not import the final unrestricted-formula-transport
lemma of source_dispatch wholesale: its D proof invokes case introduction
at arbitrary atomic matrices. Replace it by section B of this audit.
The numerical part of that proof is the reusable part.
