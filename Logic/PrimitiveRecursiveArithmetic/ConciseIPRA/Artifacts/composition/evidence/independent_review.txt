Verdict
-------
No mathematical counterexample or remaining circularity was found in the
corrected calculus. The root reindexing theorem has a direct complete proof.
The soundness interpretation and the two-direction comparison now have a
coherent written, scheme-level argument through the existing native kernel.
This is not a machine-checked proof of the new metatheorem or of the Python
implementation. The finite example/tests do not establish bi-interpretability.

The full requested optimization goal remains unmet. The compact modification
improves one source-size coordinate, 238 to 236 significant tokens, under the

coordinates, does not simplify the mathematical axiom basis, and does not

1. Root reindexing and freshness
------------------------------
With P;Q interpreted as Q after P, the three root cases give

  read(A down W)(e) = read(A)(W(e)),
  A down Id = A,
  (A down W) down V = A down (V;W).

The second and third identities are literal word/list identities. They do not
use a Cartesian normal form or an evaluator. Bound bodies remain unchanged.

The y-first D interpretation is essential to the simple written DE rule. At
branch environment pair(b,e), Q[<L,R;p>,B] means exactly the closure in D[p,B]
at witness b. Its exported goal A down R and all old assumptions ignore b.
DI's witness and prefix likewise depend only on the old tail. Thus the
freshness conditions are enforced by constructed projections, rather than
being omitted.

2. The explicit structural rule repairs an earlier proof issue
-------------------------------------------------------------
An exact-context Reindex rule alone does not make weakening/cut admissible by
an automatic induction: an arbitrary additional hypothesis need not have a
preimage under a noninvertible environment map. The final Sub rule explicitly
supplies every substituted source hypothesis. Reindex, weakening, exchange,
contraction and cut are then honest instances of Sub and Hyp. This removes the
earlier circular structural argument, at a real and disclosed checker cost.

3. Equality and the additional programs
-------------------------------------
Post is sound pointwise. J0 supplies C=P, and Post applied to two copies with
the empty postfix derives P=P. Symmetry, transitivity and postcomposition
congruence follow. Pair congruence then preserves the old environment when a
replacement depends on external parameters. Predicate Transport is ordinary
substitution of equal input values in a predicate.

There is correctly no arbitrary congruence rule inside J_H from a conditional
pointwise equation H=K. An iterator invokes H at inputs other than the current
one. The uniform-body lemma instead assumes an empty-context identity, uses
Sub to instantiate it at each state/environment input, and proves equality of
iterates by equation induction. CE then covers every triple-coded input.

C0 and generalized CE must be retained as written. Cartesian distribution
F;<P,Q>=<F;P,F;Q> follows from reindexed CL/CR, Pair, and CE; it is not literal
syntax. The report charges these arithmetic schemes instead of hiding them
inside normalization.

4. Canonical pairing and nonstandard models
-----------------------------------------
Projection and reconstruction alone do not pin a computable pairing. The
three extra recurrence schemes do. First P00/P0S give

  pair(0,b)=2b

by equation induction on b. Then PS gives

  pair(a+1,b)=2 pair(a,b)+1,

so a second equation induction identifies pair with 2^a(2b+1)-1. One can avoid
relying on exponentiation notation by taking the canonical native definition
to be iteration of u |-> 2u+1 from seed 2b. This uniqueness argument is inside
the arithmetic and therefore applies to nonstandard models as well.

The elementary PR arithmetic of bounded division and 2-adic valuation gives
the canonical inverses. After pairing is identified, CL/CR/CE identify L and R
with those inverses. Programs remain finite metasyntactic descriptions of
individual PR functions; there is no quantified function sort and no asserted
uniform total PR evaluator. These distinctions are required for the claimed
bi-interpretation.

5. Iterator naturality and the numerical compiler
------------------------------------------------
The displayed iterator naturality equation is correct with
H_F=<L,R;F>;H. Its base is J0. At a successor, JS reduces the two sides to H
applied to the respective previous state and F(e); Pair and postcomposition
carry the induction hypothesis. This proof precedes and does not use native
compiler completeness.

The finite substitution tuple in the compiler lemma can be made precise as
follows. Choose m greater than every source-variable index occurring in t.
For substituted terms theta(0),...,theta(m-1), form

  F=<theta(0)^b,<theta(1)^b,...<theta(m-1)^b,Z>...>>.

Under a new iterator variable, use lift(F)=<L,R;F>. Variable cases follow from
the projection equations, zero from C0, and successor from postcomposition.
The iterator case uses the counter/seed induction hypotheses, the lifted body
hypothesis, uniform body congruence and iterator naturality. No substitution
inside an iterator body is being justified by mere syntactic concatenation.

6. Logical naturality: the retained QI rule closes the gap
---------------------------------------------------------
Explicit QI-by-cases is a sound addition: its hypothesis is the zero row,
while its conclusion covers successor rows; it does not permit induction on
an arbitrary formula. Retaining it permits a direct proof of

  Q[p,B down lift(F)]  <-->  Q[p;F,B].

To introduce either target, QI supplies its zero-row assumption. Cartesian
conversion and Transport turn that assumption into the source zero row; QE
gives the source successor row; the reverse conversion supplies the target
successor row. This does not use a formula round-trip theorem.

For D, opening the left side supplies

  Q[q,B down lift(lift(F))],  q=<L,R;p>.

The Q result with F replaced by lift(F) converts this to Q[q;lift(F),B].
Cartesian equations identify q;lift(F) with <L,R;p;F>. Direct witnessed D
introduction at L then gives the right target under the branch, and DE exports
it. Reversing these steps proves the converse. The general body-congruence
argument uses the supplied B-to-C and C-to-B derivations through Sub at the
zero and successor inputs. Thus logical naturality is now supported without
silently identifying different raw bodies.

7. Sealing and the two formula round trips
-----------------------------------------
The raw compiler maps source x_0 to L, not to Id. Consequently the correct
unary-program comparison is

  P = <Id,Z> ; compile(P_native(x_0)).

Without this sealing map the identity program would already give a false
round-trip claim. The final seal has seal;L=Id by CL. The direct native decoder
mentioned above makes the formula expression (A^natural)^b well typed: the
compiler is defined on native syntax, not directly on arbitrary ordinary
first-order syntax.

For n source free variables, the explicit tuple readout recovers all n values
by projection. For a sentence, the compiled predicate is independent of the
ambient tail; nonemptiness handles closure. There is no use of an impossible
bijection N^0=N. On the ordinary realization, the added functions have their
fixed native/iPRA definitions and the individual numerical domain stays whole.
The identity numerical comparison graph is therefore consistent with the
context-level use of tuples.

Atoms use the sealed program identities. For Q/D, prefix changes use
Transport, while body changes and lifted parameter maps use the already
proved logical congruence/naturality lemmas. The order of the corrected
arguments is noncircular: arithmetic and logical naturality first, compiler
substitution second, native embedding third, fixed-program and formula round
trips last.

----------------------
