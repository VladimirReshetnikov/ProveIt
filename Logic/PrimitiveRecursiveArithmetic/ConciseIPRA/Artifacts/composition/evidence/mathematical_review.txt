INDEPENDENT MATHEMATICAL AND KERNEL REVIEW
13 November 2026

Reviewed material
  report.tex, mathematical sections through the comparison theorem, in the
  revision containing the explicit native decoder A^natural and sealing map.
  code/pointfree_checker.py, all validators and all inference branches.

Verdict
  I found no remaining mathematical contradiction, missing primitive rule,
  unsound inference, or mismatch between the displayed PF calculus and its
  reference checker. The comparison theorem is supported by a coherent
  written scheme-level proof, conditional on the explicitly cited inherited
  native-kernel/iPRA bridge and native representation of primitive recursive
  functions. It is not established by the finite semantic tests, and it is
  not a machine-checked bi-interpretation. No claim of absolute novelty,
  minimality, or a checker smaller than the current global frontier follows.

  The initial, simpler drafts would NOT have justified the theorem. The
  repairs listed below are substantive. They must remain in the delivered
  specification and in any future optimization unless separately derived.

1. Root reindexing
  Concatenation order is consistent: P;Q means Q(P(e)). At an Eq root,
  reindexing prepends the same word to both sides. At Q/D it prepends only
  to the stored prefix and leaves the body unchanged. Therefore the semantic
  substitution identity and the literal unit/associativity laws follow by
  the three stated root cases. This claim needs no arithmetic normalization.
  The report correctly does not equate this lemma with the full compiler
  substitution theorem.

2. Arithmetic schemes and canonical pairing
  C0 supplies real constancy of the zero instruction at the word level.
  CE is the general schema <P;L,P;R>=P, not merely <L,R>=Id. Together with
  reindexing, CL/CR and Pair this proves fork distribution; no uncharged
  cartesian normalizer is assumed.

  The pairing equations are correct for beta(a,b)=2^a(2b+1)-1:
    beta(0,0)=0;
    beta(0,b+1)=beta(0,b)+2;
    beta(a+1,b)=2 beta(a,b)+1.
  Equation induction first determines beta(0,b) as doubling and then
  determines beta(a,b) by induction on a with b as parameter. These
  equations are essential: inverse/eta equations alone admit arbitrary
  pairing bijections, potentially adding an undefinable oracle.

  After canonical pairing is pinned, CL/CR/CE determine L and R as its
  unique inverses. The cited native arithmetic supplies the fixed PR
  inverse definitions and their equations. This use is noncircular:
  the forward native compiler needs a cartesian environment representation,
  not the prior proof that its chosen pairing is the canonical one.

3. Structural substitution and contexts
  The implemented Sub rule exactly matches SubstCut in the report. Its
  first premise is Delta |- A; there is a further premise Gamma |- B down W
  for every entry B of Delta; the conclusion is Gamma |- A down W.
  Its ordinary interpretation substitutes the ambient numerical variable
  and then supplies all substituted open assumptions. This is sound.

  Weakening, cut, contraction, exchange and reindexing are actual instances
  of this rule with Hyp. No illicit inverse of W is required. In contrast,
  a bare exact-context Reindex rule would not justify the draft's earlier
  casual weakening/cut induction. That earlier argument has been removed.

4. Equality and iteration
  CrossedPost is ordinary pointwise numerical equality reasoning. PairCong
  preserves the old environment when necessary. Predicate transport uses
  equality of the entire numerical input to a predicate; it is sound for
  quantified bodies as well as atoms.

  Reflexivity is derived exactly as stated: duplicate a J0 equation C=P
  and apply CrossedPost with the empty suffix. Symmetry, transitivity and
  postcomposition congruence then follow. No reflexivity oracle is hidden.

  There is correctly no unrestricted congruence rule on the body label of
  J_H. From a conditional equation H(e)=K(e), one cannot replace H by K at
  the other numerical inputs an iterator visits. The uniform body lemma
  uses an empty-context identity and reindexes it at the needed inputs.

  Iterator naturality has a direct atomic-induction proof: both zero rows
  are the same seed; JS turns the successor rows into H of the previous
  values paired with the same transformed environment; Pair and Post carry
  the induction hypothesis. CL/CR and fork distribution justify expansion
  of H_F. This proof does not assume the native compiler or its completeness.

5. Logical rules and quantified-body naturality
  The y-first D convention is used consistently. At DE's branch input
  beta(b,e), the added Q prefix <L,R;p> is beta(b,p(e)). The branch target
  A down R and old hypotheses down R ignore the witness by construction.
  This replaces a freshness side test soundly.

  DI's witness T(e) depends only on the old environment. QI assumes the zero
  row, not the predecessor row, so it is numerical case distinction, not
  induction for arbitrary formulas. Ind checks a literal Eq body. Thus the
  calculus does not inadvertently add full quantified-formula induction.

  Retaining QI explicitly closes the logical naturality proof. QE and QI,
  with input-map equalities and predicate transport, prove the Q lift law;
  DE and derived witnessed D introduction prove the D lift law. Body
  congruence is then obtained by reindexing the two body derivations via
  Sub. None of this treats distinct bound-body spellings as literally equal.

6. Forward compiler and both round trips
  The native term substitution proof has the necessary iterator case:
  counter and seed identities are handled by equality congruence; the
  body identity is unconditional and hence can use uniform body congruence;
  iterator naturality moves the changed captured environment; fork
  distribution aligns the input words. This covers nested J instructions.

  The report now supplies a concrete native decoder. In particular,
    J_H_PR(x) = I(ell(x),ell(r(x)); u.H_PR(beta(u,r(r(x))))).
  Thus each finite program has an explicitly selected native term, with
  no varying-code total evaluator. The symbol ^flat is applied to native
  syntax, not silently to an arbitrary first-order formula.

  The sealing map <Id,Z> is indispensable. A native term with free x0 is
  compiled for a tuple with x0 in its first coordinate. Hence the correct
  program comparison is P = seal;compile(P_PR(x0)), as stated. CL proves
  seal;L=Id. Raw L would not be the identity. The same sealing convention
  is present in the formula round trip.

  Canonical pairing uniqueness and induction uniqueness for J identify
  every added program with its native definition by structural induction.
  Atom round trips then use program identities; prefix and body round trips
  use Transport and the independently proved logical naturality/congruence.
  For source formulas, a fixed tuple represents their finitely many free
  variables; a source sentence is environment-independent. The report
  correctly avoids identifying N^0 with N.

  These constructions support a bi-interpretation of the ordinary numerical
  realization, with the numerical domain unchanged and equality actual.
  The theorem is not merely an equiconsistency argument based on the standard
  natural-number model. The two decoder/compiler composites, including the
  sealing convention, are explicitly addressed.

7. Reference checker audit
  The program implements all nine arithmetic schemas and ten other rule
  families. Every step validates a context and conclusion; program and
  formula constructors have exact arities; booleans/floats are excluded as
  integer tags. Premise indices are exact nonnegative integers strictly
  earlier than the current node. The final context must be empty.

  Arithmetic constructors reproduce the stated C0, CL, CR, CE, P00, P0S,
  PS, J0 and JS equations literally. Sub checks every substituted premise.
  Post, Pair and Transport check matching contexts and their exact endpoints.
  QE, DE, DI, QI and Ind use the specified prefix maps, including DE's exact
  lifted branch context and Ind's atomic-matrix restriction.

  No certificate-supplied code is executed. No program evaluator or
  arithmetic normalization procedure is consulted by the proof checker.
  Exceptions reject malformed certificates; checks do not depend on Python
  assert statements. Physical recursion/memory limits remain the usual
  implementation limitation and are distinct from ideal finite-proof
  completeness.

Limits of this review
  This is an independent assistant review of a written proof and implementation,
  not an external human referee report. I have not converted the metatheorems
  into Lean/Rocq proofs or generated checker certificates for every schematic
  translation lemma. The reported finite tests support implementation examples;
  they do not establish the universal compiler or bi-interpretation theorems.
  The proof is appropriately characterized as a written scheme-level result,
  with those verification limits stated explicitly. The size target remains
  unmet by this environment calculus, as the report candidly records.
