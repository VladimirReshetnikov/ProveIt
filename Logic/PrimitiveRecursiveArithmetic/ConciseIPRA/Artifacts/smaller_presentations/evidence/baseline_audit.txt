## Census method and limits

`census.py` reads every `Artifacts/**/*.py` and `*.py.gz`, and every such
member of the five current `docs/incoming/*.zip` archives. It never executes
or imports audited sources. Gzipped Python is measured after decompression.
It deduplicates decoded raw bytes by SHA-256, parses the whole module, normalizes
with `ast.unparse` plus one LF, and does **not** remove docstrings. Significant
tokens exclude ENCODING, ENDMARKER, NEWLINE, NL, INDENT, DEDENT, and COMMENT.
AST nodes include everything returned by `ast.walk` on the normalized module.
Gzip uses level 9 and `mtime=0`.

The completed census finds 4,942 source occurrences, 1,788 distinct decoded
raw sources, 1,664 distinct normalized sources, and zero parse failures.
`census.json` retains every source identity, all paths, measurements, imports,
and simple classifier flags. There are 130 nonpacked modules below at least
one compact record threshold; none contains the `ACCEPT` literal. These are
build scripts, translators, metric tools, audit drivers, and related helpers,
not new candidate complete checkers under the documented acceptance contract.
A bare count or `ACCEPT` search does not prove source eligibility; the record
claims above were also checked against the five incoming READMEs and CLAIMS
files and the known compact sources.

## Correctness pitfalls to retain in any reduction

1. The initial scope traversal cannot be deleted merely because the root goal
   is closed. `Artifacts/502_scope_audit/tests/counterexample` gives a canonical
   false guarded goal, saying some number equals its successor, accepted after
   this deletion. Major checking can substitute an out-of-scope coordinate
   and return to a caller that reinterprets the original raw major differently.
2. A local floor cannot use an inclusive upper endpoint or otherwise admit an
   eigenparameter into an exported formula; there are named unsound experiments
   in `scope_and_frontier` that happen to have attractive metric vectors.
3. Source-selected elimination is not stable under substitution as a literal
   transformation of inference trees: a source can become zero. Prove the
   intended reading in the ordinary theory, or establish a separate corrected
   translation. The same caveat applies to syntax-selected Intro variants.
4. For a compact atomic matrix, progressive D has the auxiliary reading
   `exists x forall y (E(x,y) -> E(x,S y))`. The ordinary reading
   `exists x (E(x,0) -> forall y E(x,y))` agrees on the guarded public syntax;
   the distinction cannot be dropped on unrestricted auxiliary equations.
5. Finite fixture tests do not prove soundness, completeness, or
   bi-interpretability. A relative checker transformation needs a universal
   trace/preservation argument and a completeness construction.
6. The existing eight-rule native kernel has an irredundance result, including
   progress independence proved through a tower of ultrapowers. This gives no
   lower bound for other calculi, rule fusions, or checker source size.
