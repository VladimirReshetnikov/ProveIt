Independent review of the 477-byte opener refinement
12 November 2026

Scope
  Reviewed the complete compact477.py module, the exact compact478.py
  control, the proposed compact477_proof.tex section, and the inherited
  sorted-reading, opener, canonical-opening, and compilation proofs in
  upstream_certificate_proofs.tex. This is a written proof/code review,
  not a new proof-assistant verification.

Conclusion
  No flaw was found in soundness or canonical-certificate completeness
  under the stated exact-integer/list alphabet and canonical closed guarded
  Eq/D public-goal contract. The two programs need not accept the same raw
  auxiliary certificates. The proof correctly does not assert that.

Recommended wording correction
  Replace the assertion that source and seed are "closed numerical terms"
  by "numerical terms well scoped at the current floor: their free
  parameters have names in [k,0), and they contain no free template slot."
  The inherited definition of "closed at floor k" means absence of the
  distinguished hole, and permits those rigid parameters. Ordinary readers
  may otherwise understand closed to mean having no free parameter at all.

Successful-opening lemma
  Structural evaluation induction is valid even with the missing-image
  sentinel. At an unprotected scalar of even depth, the new opener checks
  its image and returns either 0 or []; the old opener returns 0 without
  checking. At odd depth both check the image at numerical root depth 3.
  Consequently new success implies old success, and the outputs agree
  after recursively replacing every empty array by scalar zero.

  This replacement preserves the sorted reading: both spellings denote
  zero in term position and truth in formula position. Replacing an empty
  leaf does not change a containing nonempty array's arity. An annotation
  has no reading. Thus the inherited semantic-opening lemma transfers.

Soundness
  The new initial resource [] reads as truth. Every retained-resource or
  elimination-pattern equality is still a literal equality, which implies
  equality of readings. Computational construction, the source-major flag,
  the introduction selector, and scope-floor ordering are unchanged.
  Applying the inherited soundness proof with the new semantic-opening
  lemma is legitimate; it does not require a raw accepted-trace simulation.
  New rejection caused by eagerly checking an otherwise unused image cannot
  invalidate soundness.

Canonical completeness: branch coverage
  1. Canonical formulas have even formula depths. Equality enters term
     depth by adding 3. Successor adds 4 and iteration adds 2, so meaningful
     scalar occurrences in terms have odd depth. D and internal Q preserve
     the even formula parity. All actual substitution openings therefore
     agree literally, since their images are well-scoped numerical terms.
     Canonical goals without a supplied image also validate identically.

  2. A computation packet may eagerly view its numerical update template
     at formula depth in the automatic-elimination comparison. These are
     otherwise unused calls. Their additional numerical checks are on the
     supplied s/u, which are valid at root depth 3 and the active floor.
     The result cannot enable the comparison: the administrative major []
     is different from both nonempty reconstructed majors [s,u] and [t]*3.
     The numerical computation opening itself remains in odd depth.

  3. Real transport and closure elimination use canonical formula templates;
     their source and target openings remain literally equal to the old
     openings. Paired elimination keeps the same canonical Q resource.

  4. Introduction opens its canonical matrix at even formula depth 2, then
     its remaining coordinate at depth 0. Its meaningful term leaves are
     still odd-depth leaves. The raw major remains false-valued when 0 is
     changed to [], so the equation/compound mode selector is unchanged.

  5. Tripling a nonunary major generates an ignored administrative truth
     resource. It is opened at the newly allocated integer parameter. Any
     extra even-depth check sees this in-scope integer, and multiplication
     by zero returns exactly the scalar zero returned by the old opener.
     These resources are not needed as proof goals by the canonical compiler.

  6. The administrative premise is preserved: opening [] returns [], and
     [] occurs in the new initial context. The canonical compiler otherwise
     retains the same meaningful formulas and consumes the same packets,
     changing only administrative major 0 to []. The final [goal,0]
     framing is shared by the 478- and 477-byte modules.

  The arithmetic seeds/source terms can contain existing eigenparameters.
  The change in the floor only enlarges the permitted interval for later
  calls, so previously valid images remain valid. No global closedness
  assumption about these images is needed.

Limits
  The result inherits the established guarded scalar calculus and its iPRA
  interpretation. It is a one-byte implementation improvement, not a new
  mathematical axiom basis or a proof of global minimality. It does not
  improve the independent 244-token and 267-AST records.
