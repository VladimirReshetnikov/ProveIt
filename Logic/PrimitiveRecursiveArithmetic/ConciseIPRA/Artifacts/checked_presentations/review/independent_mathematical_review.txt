The audit below is a written proof review, not a proof-assistant verification.
It relies on the pinned article's native/iPRA bridge and semantic-opening
lemmas, and on the incoming computation-leaf and uniform-zero proofs.
Finite tests may supplement this argument but do not establish its universal
claims. All execution statements concern ideal finite successful runs;
resource-exhaustion equivalence is not asserted.

1. WHAT THE COMPLEXITY COMPARISON MEANS

The pinned article, ch01_base.tex, section "The measurement protocol", says
explicitly that its checker sizes are achieved upper bounds on an unknown
optimum, not lower bounds for all calculi or implementations. A coordinatewise
minimum across a fixed corpus is an observed lower envelope of that corpus.
It need not be achieved by one program.

A comparison is meaningful only within the same input contract. The strict
profile validates the entire parsed JSON integer/list alphabet and the full
native Eq/D public goal. The compact profile assumes a canonical guarded
public goal and an integer/list promise. Beating the strict envelope does
not mean beating the smaller compact programs. If three different programs
improve three coordinates, report them separately, and do not describe their
coordinatewise minimum as the size of a single program.

These candidates are implementations/presentations of the inherited native
theory. Their proof establishes no new deletion of an arithmetic or logical
axiom, no global minimum, and no breakthrough resolving the scalar successor-
uniqueness bootstrap. The meaningful new claim is the measured improvement
of a precisely stated profile, if the independent source census confirms it.

2. CANONICAL UNIFORM-ZERO CODING

Let R replace a numerical zero leaf 0 by 1 in a canonical expression. Bound
slots 3,5,7,..., negative rigid parameters, constructor arities, and meanings
are unchanged. In particular, numerical one is [1], not the scalar 1.
Administrative record fields are not uniformly rewritten: source marker 0
still selects zero computation or closure elimination, and neutral majors
remain neutral markers. Recoding must be field-aware.

The proof-phase opener is the pinned O+ operation:

  O+_k(a;t,d) = [O+_k(b;t,d+5-r) : b in a] if a is an array of arity r;
               a or 1                         if k <= a < d;
               O+_k(t;missing,3)               otherwise, d odd;
               0                              otherwise, d even.

The article proves semantic opening, outer-slot exposure, and freshness for
O+. The only altered retained scalar is zero, and both 0 and 1 have the same
sorted reading: numerical zero at term positions and true (0=0) at formula
positions. Inserted terms are still checked at root term depth 3. This scope
check is essential and is present in all reviewed candidates.

On canonical expressions, opening commutes with R. This is not a literal
commutation theorem on arbitrary malformed raw aliases. In particular O+
need not be an idempotent raw-tree retraction at an ignored formula annotation:
an even-depth fallback may return 0, and a subsequent retained-zero pass may
return 1. Both have the same reading, which is all soundness requires.

3. WHOLE-INPUT AND PUBLIC-GOAL VALIDATION

For a value returned by json.load, str(p).strip('0123456789-[], ') is empty
exactly when every recursive component is an exact integer or a list.
Strings retain quotation marks, objects braces, and floats, booleans and null
have excluded characters. This rejects malformed unused prefixes too. A
top-level integer subsequently fails pop. A nonempty lexical residue is a
string, and attempting to call it rejects; no acceptance follows that branch.

Initially k=0. At even (formula) depths the arity gate admits 1 or 2; at odd
(term) depths it admits 1 or 3. The scalar gate requires k <= a < d, d odd,
and a odd. Thus it admits the canonical zero 1 and legal odd bound slots.
It rejects scalar formula roots, old zero spelling 0, escaping parameters,
and wrong arities. Inherited dummy-slot aliases retain their harmless zero
reading. Completeness is stated for the fixed canonical public encoding.

4. THE CONTEXT REPRESENTS THE PHASE IN STRICT_SEARCH_0/1

The initial context is empty. During the first no-image traversal, the empty
context activates the public arity and scalar gates. If this traversal
rejects, no subsequent instruction can undo the rejection.

After successful initial validation, before any auxiliary opening, g += [0]
makes the context nonempty. Every recursive major receives that nonempty
context, and every continuation receives it with another resource appended.
Hence every auxiliary traversal is in proof phase. No context can become
empty later. Mutations performed by a major can only append neutral 0 entries
to a shared list; duplicate neutral truths change neither membership of a
meaningful formula nor derivability. The phase therefore cannot revert.

Consequently the g-dependent predicates are exactly the initial validator
followed by O+ in all proof-phase calls. They compose the established
context-phase invariant with uniform-zero coding. The update of k still
occurs after the major proof and locally in the continuation. It creates
the fresh parameter k-1; inserted terms retain the root-depth scope check.

5. CONDITIONAL SELECTION IN STRICT_SEARCH_0/1

Write M for the elimination-pattern comparison

  (m,a) == (s and [s,u] or [t]*3, i(t,u)).

The old expression eagerly computed i(t,s) in the tuple (a,i(t,s)) and
selected a or that computed value by M. The new expression computes

  i(t,s) if M else a.

The comparison M still checks i(t,u). If M is true, i(t,s) is still computed
and validated, so every equality-transport or closure-elimination premise
needed by the transition remains checked. If M is false, the goal is a;
the unselected source instance is not a premise of any inference made by
that step. The helper i is pure: it consumes no records, does not mutate
contexts, and does not allocate or change a scope floor. Omitting the unused
opening can only avoid an irrelevant rejection; it cannot change the selected
goal or establish an otherwise unsupported inference.

This is a theorem-set preservation argument, not equality of raw acceptance
languages. Canonical certificates have all required openings defined, so
their transitions and successful proof compilation are preserved.

The multiplier (1 if m else len(a)%2==u) in strict_search_1 is arithmetically
identical to bool(m or len(a)%2==u) in strict_search_0 when multiplied by the
integer floor. A truthy m gives 1 in either case; otherwise both use the same
Boolean comparison. Both forms short-circuit the comparison for truthy m.
Imports, input walruses, the two equivalent conditional-block forms, and the
placement of the binding of i do not alter this invariant.

6. THE SHARED TERMINAL IN STRICT_SHARED_BYTES

Each call first reads [m,s,u,t]. It then validates/opens the goal and tests
membership in the retained context augmented, for this terminal test only,
by neutral 0 and the projected computation resource O+_k(H(s,u,t)), where

  H(s,u,t) = [[[s],u,t], O+_k(t;[s,u,t],3)]   if s is truthy;
             [[s,u,t],u]                     if s is falsy.

Python evaluates the left membership operand first. Thus at the root the
public-goal traversal is completed with v=1 before (v:=0) is evaluated in
the right operand. The explicit assignment then changes the very closure
used to construct H to proof phase. Major and continuation calls use v=0.
Malformed public syntax cannot exploit the phase change to bypass validation.

The terminal succeeds only through a retained resource, neutral truth, or
the validated computation resource. A projected H is a computation theorem,
not merely an equation with a true zero specialization:

  - Falsy s is 0 or [], both numerical zero. The right occurrence of u is
    checked at term-root depth. This excludes meaningful dependence on the
    extra dummy slot available under the left iterator, so the two seeds
    have the same reading and I0 proves the equation.

  - Truthy s gives a successor counter. If the update uses its state in a
    meaningful position, the construction of the right side validates the
    recursive source [s,u,t] at term-root depth before substitution. This
    excludes a meaningful dependence on dummy slots that are available only
    under the additional successor node. Semantic opening then gives IS.
    If the update is state-independent, IS gives the same constant-update
    result on both sides. Ignored annotations may cause rejection but do not
    change the equation's reading.

The outer no-image traversal O+ of H is explicitly present. The O+ semantic
opening lemma transfers the direct-leaf proof from the zero-coded controls.
Canonical zero1 also makes IS at predecessor zero available directly: the
source code 1 is truthy, so the produced counter is [1]. No certified
nonliteral-zero workaround is needed for that canonical computation.

The computation resource is transient. If the terminal test fails, that
resource is not retained as an assumption. The continuation adds only the
resource justified by its checked major or by its introduction premise.

7. THE CRITICAL MODE REPAIR

The reviewed terminal checker uses

  any((m, len(a) == u == 2)).

For a neutral major m, an introduction has first exposed the D matrix into
a. Progress is then selected exactly when this matrix is a literal equation
(arity two) and u is the scalar marker 2. Every other meaningful matrix uses
cases. In particular compound D and Q matrices can never select progress.
For a nonneutral major, the fresh-witness mode remains selected as before.

This exact-arity test is necessary. The unrepaired candidate used
len(a)%2==u after deleting the old truthy-u computation branch. That deletion
made truthy u newly available to introductions; u=1 at a compound matrix
would select progress, allowing induction for compound formulas and exceeding
iPRA. The repair is therefore a substantive soundness correction, not merely
a coding style choice.

The marker 2 is proof-only; the public zero code remains 1. Scalar 2 is a
numerical-zero alias in raw proof expressions, so its incidental computation
preview is defined. A canonical progressive introduction must use marker 2
rather than the supply protocol's old marker 0. Case introduction can use [].
Neutral raw matrices have the truth reading and cannot add a nontrivial
induction principle, whatever their irrelevant annotations.

8. CANONICAL COMPLETENESS OF THE TERMINAL PROTOCOL

Use the inherited native proof calculus, with contextual hypotheses and the
two computation axioms. Replace Hyp, I0 and IS leaves by one rule:

     C is a computation instance, A belongs to Gamma union {C}
     -------------------------------------------------------
                         Gamma |- A.

This preserves every open sequent. A computation leaf chooses its own C;
a hypothesis leaf chooses the fixed I(0,0;u.0)=0. Conversely choose Hyp if
A belongs to Gamma and otherwise the supplied computation axiom. No scope
or assumption is discharged by a terminal.

At each such leaf emit one harmless/correct four-field record. For a
hypothesis or neutral major, [0,0,0,0] gives a total true computation preview.
For I0 emit a zero source marker and the recoded seed/body; for IS emit the
recoded predecessor, which is truthy even when numerically zero. At logical
nonleaves use the inherited major-first continuation compilation, replacing
the progressive-introduction marker by 2. The shift from root formula depth
0 to computation-preview depth 3 protects the canonical free-hole spelling;
allocated negative parameters remain protected. Opening and introduction can
choose harmless zero preview fields. Thus canonical previews are defined.

An old certificate that retained a computation resource need not be translated
record-for-record. Its mathematical derivation can inline that computation at
each use (equivalently use admissible cut), and then be compiled by the direct
leaf protocol. Dropping administrative supply steps may change fresh integer
names; the ordinary eigenvariable-renaming theorem handles this. Literal raw
certificate equivalence is not asserted.

9. PRECISE THEOREM AND TRANSFER OF THE BI-INTERPRETATION

The logical reading here is the FULL native one: Q_y B means
B(0) -> forall y B(y), and D_{x,y} B means
exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y)), INCLUDING when B is an equation.
The two permitted introductions are cases at arbitrary matrices and progress
only at literal equations. The explicit marker keeps both introductions.
The final public grammar is arbitrary Eq/D, not just guarded atomic matrices.

Do not substitute the compact guarded calculus's auxiliary reading: in that
calculus a D over an equation can instead mean existential progressivity,
and the equivalence with the ordinary conditional reading holds only after
the public guarding translation. That distinction does not arise for the
reviewed strict native candidates. The direct computation-leaf lemma imported
from the compact paper concerns arithmetic computations and raw opening only;
it does not import its different auxiliary D reading or its public restriction.

For each reviewed checker P:

  (Soundness) Every accepted arbitrary JSON certificate has a decoded closed
  public Eq/D goal derivable in the full native theory.

  (Canonical completeness) Every closed public Eq/D theorem of that native
  theory, in uniform-zero canonical coding, has an accepted certificate of P.

The proof is induction over a finite successful run using the invariants
above and the inherited raw-case native soundness proof; completeness is the
canonical compiler or the common-leaf translation just described.

Therefore the induced mathematical theory is unchanged. If J interprets iPRA
in its ordinary realization and I interprets that realization in iPRA, the
same proof translations establish both formula round trips

  iPRA |- A <-> (A^J)^I,
  T    |- B <-> (B^I)^J.

R preserves term meanings, has the canonical inverse 1-to-0 on numerical
leaves, and neither changes the numerical domain nor replaces equality.
Both numerical comparison graphs remain x=y. Thus full bi-interpretability
and equiconsistency transfer from the pinned bridge. This is stronger than
equiconsistency alone, but remains an inherited mathematical bridge rather
than a new proof of a smaller axiomatic basis.

10. INHERITED BRIDGE OBLIGATIONS THAT MUST REMAIN EXPLICIT

The bridge has four logically distinct dependencies:

  (a) The native Eq/Q/D calculus and its Eq/D public fragment reproduce the
      ordinary intuitionistic connectives and quantifiers through the pinned
      selector/gate translations. Eliminating Q by a D with unused witness
      preserves both directions of derivability.

  (b) The ordinary realization is intuitionistic first-order logic with
      equality on scalar iterator terms, the two iterator computations,
      numerical coverage x=0 or exists y x=S(y), separation 0=S(0)->false,
      and induction for literal equations under fixed side assumptions.

  (c) Internally this realization derives predecessor, reflected/decidable
      equality, quantifier-free induction, pairing/unpairing, and a compiler
      for each primitive-recursive description. The compiler is external
      recursion on finite descriptions, not a total internal universal
      primitive-recursive evaluator.

  (d) The interpretations prove both term round trips and, by structural
      induction and the logical translation, both formula round trips.
      Neither arithmetic domain is relativized; equality is actual equality;
      the two numerical comparison relations are the graph x=y.

Equiconsistency alone, a standard-model evaluation, a one-way interpretation,
or matching finite fixture results would not establish (a)-(d). These are
proved dependencies of the pinned article, not consequences of the code-size
measurements. Our new work preserves the theorem set and hence reuses these
proofs unchanged. No new Lean/Rocq verification is claimed.
