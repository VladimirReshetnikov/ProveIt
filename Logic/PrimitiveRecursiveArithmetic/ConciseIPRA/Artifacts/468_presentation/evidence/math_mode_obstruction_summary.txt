Conditional calculus theorem:
  Assume the existing native QE, DE, and gated-closure interface remain
  admissible. If D-progress is also available for Q-headed matrices, define
  K(y)=Q_v gate_v A(y). Gates give K(y) interderivable with A(y).
  An arbitrary step A(z)->A(Sz) therefore gives K(z)->K(Sz). Q-progress
  gives D_{x,y}K(y), with x unused; DE gives Q_yK(y). From A(0), get K(0),
  specialize to K(t), and recover A(t). This is contextual induction for
  arbitrary formulas A. A can be a guarded public Eq/D formula throughout;
  only the intermediates use auxiliary Q.
