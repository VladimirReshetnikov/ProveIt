New positive result
-------------------
The ordinary theory with 0 and R(n,a;u.h), S a := R(0,a;u.0),
R(0,a;u.h)=Sa, R(Sn,a;u.h)=h(R(n,a;u.h)), full intuitionistic first-order
equality logic, coverage, separation, and literal-equation induction
does admit a finite term P with whole-domain proofs P0=0 and P(Sa)=a.

The comparator is developed independently in sr_comparator.tex.
Transient seeds outside a reachable finite-residue class provide the
zero-start doubling, tripling, and ramp trajectories needed for the
factor-map comparator. No predecessor or arbitrary initialization enters
this bootstrap.

The predecessor construction is in sr_predecessor_and_equivalence.tex.
For the comparator L, let F_m(u)=S u if L(Su,m)=1 and 0 otherwise,
implemented with the available shifted selector T. F_m(Sm)=0 makes Sm
an accessible transient seed. Applying F_m outside the recursor yields
zero-start trajectories J_m under F_m and K_m under F_m composed with itself.
One literal gated equation induction proves the bounded-run property.
Two further literal equation inductions prove the period and doubled
trajectory identities. Their diagonal specialization gives
P(n)=K_n(n), P(Sa)=a without cancelling S.

The initializer compiler is then P(R(n,a;v.S h(Pv))). It has the ordinary
base and step equations by direct computation. The inherited internal
scalar-iPRA reconstruction supplies the remaining arithmetic and
whole-domain bi-interpretability argument; it must be included and
attributed rather than replaced by a standard-model observation.

New negative result
-------------------
binary_recursor_obstruction.tex proves a bounded-coordinate theorem for
the distinct tied-seed candidate B(n;u,v.h), whose base is S h(0,0).
Every term is globally bounded or bounded by a function of one input
coordinate alone. The four structural cases are global bound, passive
coordinate, old-counter coordinate, and previous-state coordinate.
Consequently no term represents ordinary addition, the unrestricted
two-branch selector, or an injection from N^2 to N. This does not exclude
arbitrary relational or domain-changing interpretations of a theory.
binary_recursor_bounds.py illustrates the constructive bound compiler;
294 finite transcriptions passed.
