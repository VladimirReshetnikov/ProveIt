ARRAY-TRUTH PROTOCOL REVIEW

Established change
------------------
The checker starts with the single list-valued hypothesis []. Under the
inherited sorted interpretation this is the fixed theorem 0=0. A proof of
0=0 already exists: use H := I(0,0;v.0)=0 twice in transport with motive
F(w): w=0. The certified-zero normalization supplies the excluded zero
initializer in an actual certificate. Adding [] is therefore conservative
by ordinary assumption cut.

Every packet verifies its major. A canonical absent major is the literal
empty list, so its proof closes immediately without consuming a packet or
allocating a parameter. The branch around major-premise verification is
removed. The original major is still used to select the continuation.

The introduction mode is moved to u, NOT to s. The preceding computation
branch ensures that u is false-valued at an introduction. Thus
len(matrix)%2 == u can hold for a literal equation but never for an odd
compound-matrix arity. Canonical u=0 selects equation progress; u=[] selects
cases. An attempted u=1 selects computation and cannot enable compound
induction. The field s remains unused in introduction.

Why the guardless matcher is safe
--------------------------------
The inherited matcher contains the value False when s is truthy. A numeric
major 0 would compare equal to that sentinel. The checker does not grant
numeric 0 the privilege of the empty-list truth.

For the explicit-list profiles, the following simultaneous finite-trace
invariant applies: every actual continuation context is list-valued, and
no numeric goal has a successful proof from such a context. Prove this by
induction on the size of a successful finite checking trace.

A numeric goal cannot close by membership: its opening is numeric, while
all context entries are lists. A successful major subproof in a smaller
trace cannot have a numeric major. Nonempty list majors export lists;
computation constructs a list. Transport cannot change a numeric goal into
a list: equality of i(t,u) with a numeric goal forces the successful root
opening of t to be numeric, and scalar opening at depth zero is independent
of its image. The continuation goal remains numeric, contradicting the
smaller-trace induction hypothesis. Introduction at a numeric incoming goal
fails unpacking. If introduction from an array produces a numeric matrix,
len(matrix) fails before any continuation context is constructed. Therefore
it cannot introduce a scalar hypothesis. This also proves context closure.

Consequently a falsy successful major in the strict profiles is []; it is
unequal to False and to either nonempty matching candidate. Nonempty majors
use the inherited major rules. No extra major Boolean guard is needed.

For the generic-traversal profile, empty strings and empty mappings can
open to [], giving additional harmless truth aliases. They are unequal to
numeric zero and False. A truthy mapping cannot export m*3. Numeric-major
exclusion is unchanged. The inherited malformed-nonempty-string/runtime
qualification remains; no ideal all-JSON termination claim is made.

Arithmetic and full logical strength
------------------------------------
The continuation image for a computation may now be zero or the fresh
parameter, depending on u and the goal arity. The inherited known-zero-
instance supply lemma covers both. It is not claimed that every generated
hypothesis is uniformly true in its fresh parameter.

All arithmetic equations, paired introduction/elimination, equality
transport, closure specialization, and the literal-equation induction
restriction otherwise have their inherited meanings. Canonical old
certificates are translated by replacing absent majors by [] and, at an
introduction, moving the old mode from m into u. No new allocation or
instruction is introduced. The inherited full-domain bi-interpretation is
therefore retained. This is a written mathematical review, not a
proof-assistant formalization.

Novelty and size scope
----------------------
The exact initial-array-truth/unconditional-major/u-mode protocol was not
found by searches of the inspected current corpus. Its arithmetic basis,
arity encoding, certified-zero normalization, and logical reconstruction
are inherited and attributed. No literature-wide novelty or absolute
minimality claim follows.

The complete explicit/byte/joint programs measure respectively
550/263/307, 542/263/309, 578/261/295 in normalized bytes/tokens/AST nodes.
The attained coordinate envelope is 542/261/295, compared with the current
corpus envelope 558/262/296, from different programs. No single new program
is below all three old coordinate minima.

Concrete validation
-------------------
The standalone tests cover 46 examples across four independently executable
files in ordinary and optimized Python: 368 complete-process expectations.
Two additional mutation runs change only the progress selector from u to s;
they then accept the otherwise rejected compound-progress attack. This
shows that the negative certificate exercises the specific restriction.
All 370 expectations passed. Finite tests support the implementation review;
they do not replace the universal trace and interpretation arguments.
