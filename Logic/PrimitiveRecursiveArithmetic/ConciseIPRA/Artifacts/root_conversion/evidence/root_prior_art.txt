EXACT NEW CLAIM SUPPORTED BY THIS AUDIT

For raw terms with the ordinary counter-aware recursor
  R(0,a;i,u.h) -> a,
  R(Sn,a;i,u.h) -> h[n/i,R(n,a;i,u.h)/u],
the seven-rule schema Hyp, Refl, variable-only crossed substitution,
QE, DE, witness-selected DI, and bidirectional ROOT conversion of the
LEFT endpoint of a literal equation recovers full one-term transport.
The three-row naming packet is
  P(x,y) := R(y,0;i,u.C(i,x,h(x))) = R(y,0;i,u.C(i,t,r)),
where C(z,a,b)=R(z,a;i,u.b). Its rows at 0, 1, and S(Sz) expose respectively
0=0, x=t, and h(x)=r using root steps only. The old counter i is supplied
directly by the outer successor contraction. No computation beneath a
counter, successor, seed, body, or formula binder is primitive.

No matching theorem was found in the inspected proposals. The specific
advance is the completeness of this restricted computation discipline
after changing from the scalar iterator to counter-aware recursion.

WHAT IS ALREADY PRESENT AND MUST BE ATTRIBUTED

1. Head conversion: article chapter 4, section "Weak-head conversion with
   raw hints", definition comp:def:vh and theorem comp:thm:vh. This has
   Hyp, Refl, VSub, QE, DE, and the SAME witness-selected DI. It normalizes
   equation endpoints, including through counter spines, and uses the
   same general naming-packet strategy to recover full transport.

2. Bounded conversion: chapter 4, definition comp:def:bh and theorem
   comp:thm:bh, originating in Artifacts/bounded_head. This ALREADY has
   seven rule families Hyp, Refl, VSub, QE, DE, WI, HC_3, raw terms,
   bidirectional conversion on the LEFT endpoint only, and no normalizer.
   Its scalar parity packet requires counter-spine depths up to three.
   The seven-rule inventory, variable-only substitution, single DI,
   left-endpoint restriction, and absence of a normalization algorithm
   therefore are NOT new individually or as that scalar combination.

3. The incoming ipra_bounded_conversion_2026-11-12.zip repeats the scalar
   depth-three, seven-rule result with its own detailed proofs/checker.
   Its Lemma "Symmetry without computation" proves symmetry by opening
   D_(x,y)(0=0), taking Refl b=b, and applying VSub to a passive equation.
   That is exactly the optional symmetry argument in the new audit and
   should be credited as inherited, not a new observation.

4. Counter-aware normalized packets: chapter 9, equation ctr:eq:packet
   and lemma ctr:lem:openrules, use
     C(y,0,C(P(y),x,h(x))) = C(y,0,C(P(y),t,r))
   with normalized rows truth, x=t, and h(x)=r. The numerical-case
   construction and extraction of the two payload rows are inherited.
   These calculi use full canonical normal forms and a successor-seeded
   recursor (R(0,a;...)=S(a), with successor defined). The present proof
   uses the ordinary R(0,a;...)=a with an independent S, and reprograms
   the packet so that the successor step exposes the case variable at
   the root, without an internal predecessor calculation.

5. Counter evaluation: chapter 9, ctr:eq:CE and ctr:thm:arith, already
   exploit the old counter supplied to a recursor body to recover
   congruence. Its major equation has ARBITRARY source s=t, and its terms
   are zero-body canonical. This is distinct from raw variable-only
   substitution plus root conversion, but should be acknowledged as an
   earlier use of the counter-aware interface for equality recovery.

CAREFUL COMPARISON WORDING

It is accurate to say: "For counter-aware recursion, the bounded-conversion
method can be reduced to depth zero by a packet whose branch is selected
directly by the supplied old counter."

It is inaccurate to say: "The old scalar depth-three bound is reduced to
zero in the same language", "seven rules are new", "the naming-packet
method is new", "normalization is removed for the first time", or "this
establishes a checker below the current source-size frontier".

The new proof uses expansions as well as contractions. A contraction-only
claim needs another argument. Formula transport and arbitrary numerical
substitution in derivations must be recovered after equality transport;
neither may be assumed while constructing the packet. The witness-selected
DI combination is valid: numerical cases can be derived with unused witness
1, and the packet introduction ignores its local assumption in either mode.
