# Independent audit: counter-aware root conversion

## Result

The proposed naming packet recovers unrestricted one-term crossed transport
from variable-only substitution, reflexivity, the native logical rules, and
reversible computation at the root of an equation endpoint. No conversion
inside a successor, recursor counter, seed, body, or compound formula is
needed. In particular there is no normalization algorithm in this argument.

This audit is a written derivation, not a mechanized proof. It assumes the
standard capture-avoiding substitution and eigenvariable checks stated below.

## Exact arithmetic assumptions

Terms contain 0, S, and the ordinary counter-aware primitive recursor

    R(0,a;i,u.h) -> a,
    R(S n,a;i,u.h) -> h[n/i,R(n,a;i,u.h)/u].

The arrow is a one-step root contraction. Only i,u are bound in h.
Define C(z,a,b)=R(z,a;i,u.b), with unused fresh i,u. Therefore

    C(0,a,b) -> a,
    C(S z,a,b) -> b.

The proposed restricted equality rule is

    x=t, u=v  /  v=u[t/x],

where x is a literal variable and replacement is capture-avoiding, affecting
free occurrences only. It is sound even if t contains x: equality gives
u=u[t/x], and together with u=v gives the displayed conclusion. No occurs
check is needed for soundness; replacement is performed once.

## Numerical cases precede transport

Given proofs of A(0) and A(S z) with z fresh, a closure of A is introduced
with an unused witness coordinate at the witness 1=S0. This is case mode
even in the witness-tested single-introduction system. Open the resulting
D formula, retain its Q closure, and use QE with A(0) to obtain A(t).
The witness coordinate is unused, so no equality transport is involved.

The same derivation works when A is an equation. It therefore applies to
the successor-row formula in the packet below before transport is known.

## Packet and its rows

Fix premises s=t and h(s)=r, with h a one-variable term template. Choose
x,y fresh for all passive data and all bound variables fresh for those data.
Let

    P(x,y) :=
      R(y,0;i,u.C(i,x,h(x)))
      = R(y,0;i,u.C(i,t,r)).

Both recursor bodies ignore their recursive-result variable u. At the
three required kinds of rows:

1. P(x,0) converts to 0=0 by one R0 step on each endpoint.
2. P(x,1) converts to x=t by an RS step followed by C0 on each endpoint.
3. P(x,S(S z)) converts to h(x)=r by an RS step followed by CS on each
   endpoint.

All of these conversions are at the current root. The second contraction
is at the root exposed by the first one; it is not a below-root contraction.

By numerical cases on z, the two supplied premises prove P(s,S z): the
zero case is s=t and the successor case is h(s)=r, with the displayed
root conversions in reverse. Thus DI at s proves D_{x,y}P(x,y), ignoring
its local hypothesis. This works in either case or progress mode, so a
literal-zero witness is not an exception.

Open this D formula at a fresh b. Refl 0=0 and the reverse R0 steps prove
P(b,0). QE at 1 and 2, followed by the row conversions, give

    b=t,  h(b)=r.

Variable substitution with source b then gives r=h(t). The variables b,x,y
were chosen absent from h's passive parameters, s,t,r, so this is exactly
the required capture-free instance and b does not escape the discharge.

## Completeness consequence

The result is the native one-term crossed transport rule

    s=t, h(s)=r  /  r=h(t).

Reflexivity and root conversion yield both defining computation equations
as theorems. Therefore all rules of the unrestricted counter-aware native
calculus are recovered. Conversely, every new primitive rule is valid in
that calculus: variable substitution is an equality consequence and each
root conversion is an instance of a computation equation used in an
equation context. The logical rules are unchanged.

After that equality recovery, computation in arbitrary term contexts and
formula transport are derived. They are not used to establish the packet.
The usual definitional equivalence of counter-aware primitive recursion
with iPRA gives the arithmetic bridge; that bridge must still be stated and
attributed in a complete paper.

## Optional left-endpoint restriction

Even conversion on both endpoints need not be primitive. General symmetry
can be proved before transport. Introduce the true formula
D_{a,y}(0=0), open it at a fresh b, and use Refl b=b as the major premise
of variable substitution on any given u=v, with b absent from u,v. The
result is v=u. Discharge b. Thus right-endpoint conversion is simulated by
symmetry, a left-endpoint conversion, and symmetry. Reversible root
conversion on the left endpoint alone is sufficient.

The present proof does not show that root contractions alone suffice:
its construction of the packet rows uses expansions. Do not omit the
reverse direction without a new proof.

## Prior-art boundary within the inspected repository

`docs/Article/chapters/ch09_ctr.tex`, equation `ctr:eq:packet` near line 3120,
already uses the normalized packet

    C(y,0,C(Pred(y),x,h(x))) = C(y,0,C(Pred(y),t,r)),

with rows truth, x=t, h(x)=r, to obtain transport. Its calculus uses fully
normalized terms. The current packet moves the numerical case variable
directly into the recursor body's counter variable, so the row exposing
Pred(S z) no longer needs internal computation.

`Artifacts/head_conversion` / chapter 4 provides variable-only substitution
with weak-head normalization. `Artifacts/bounded_head` proves completeness
for the scalar iterator with conversions at root or at up to three counter
edges. Its selected checker is 1056 tokens, 1127 AST nodes, 3089 normalized
bytes; it explicitly does not meet the global frontier.

No raw counter-aware, root-only conversion theorem was found in the inspected
chapter or package descriptions. The warranted novelty claim is therefore
the reduction in the required computation discipline for counter-aware
recursion. The naming-packet mechanism and its three-row proof are inherited
and should be attributed, not claimed as independently new.
