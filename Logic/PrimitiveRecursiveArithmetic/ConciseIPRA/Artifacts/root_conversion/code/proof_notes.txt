ROOT CONVERSION FOR A COUNTER-AWARE RECURSOR
==========================================

Status
------
This is a candidate mathematical simplification of the amount of conversion
trusted by a variable-substitution calculus. It is NOT a smaller complete
checker than the current compact frontier. The delivered checker is a readable,
fully scope- and sort-validating reference implementation. The mathematical
metatheorem below is a written proof, not a Lean/Rocq formalization.

The current integrated article already has the iterator calculus BH_3, whose
primitive conversion reaches three counter levels, and several counter-aware
calculi on normalized terms. Its counter-aware transport packet uses
C(y,0,C(P(y),x,h(x))) with normalized predecessor rows. The contribution here
is the directly staged packet F(y;a,b) below: it performs all its administrative
computations at the root, on raw syntax. A corpus check of the INTEGRATED
article alone found no explicit theorem for this exact combination. That is
not a claim of global novelty, nor a substitute for inspecting incoming ZIPs.

1. Syntax, substitution and ordinary reading
--------------------------------------------
One numerical sort. Raw terms:

  t ::= variable | 0 | S(t) | R(n,a; i,u.h).

Only i and u are bound in h. The intended computation equations are

  R(0,a;i,u.h) = a,
  R(S n,a;i,u.h) = h[n/i, R(n,a;i,u.h)/u].

The body h is syntax, not a quantified function object or a program code
passed to a universal total evaluator. Equivalently, each fixed body gives
its own ordinary first-order function symbol with its free variables as
parameters, in a staged definitional language. There is no universal
primitive-recursive evaluation function hidden in the signature.

Formulas:

  A ::= s=t | Q_y A | D_{x,y} A.

The ordinary first-order intuitionistic readings are

  Q_y B := B(0) -> forall y B(y),
  D_{x,y} B := exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y)).

Distinguished contradiction: 0=S0. Terms and formulas are raw. Substitution
is capture-avoiding syntactic substitution; it never performs computation.
No congruence, semantic evaluation or normalization is implicit in identity.

2. The eight primitive rule families
-----------------------------------
All contexts are finite lists of assumptions. Every rule retains the same
outer context Gamma. Repetition, weakening and fresh renaming are admissible
by induction on derivations; they are not additional proof-node tags.

Hyp:       A in Gamma                         / Gamma |- A.
Refl:                                         / Gamma |- t=t.
HC:        Gamma |- s=t                       / Gamma |- s'=t,
             where either s contracts to s', or s' contracts to s,
             by ONE of the two displayed R equations at THE ROOT.
VSub:      Gamma |- x=t; Gamma |- u=v         / Gamma |- v=u[t/x],
             where x is a variable. The replacement t may contain x.
QE:        Gamma |- Q_y B; Gamma |- B(0)      / Gamma |- B(t).
DE:        Gamma |- D_xy B;
           Gamma,Q_y B(b,y) |- A             / Gamma |- A,
             where b is fresh for Gamma,A,D_xy B.
DI:        Gamma,B(t,0) |- B(t,S z)           / Gamma |- D_xy B,
             where z is fresh for Gamma,t,D_xy B.
QI:        Gamma,E(z) |- E(S z)               / Gamma |- Q_y E(y),
             where E is a literal equation and z is fresh for Gamma,Q_y E.

There are no primitive computation axioms: they follow by expanding the left
endpoint of a reflexivity. HC cannot rewrite a right endpoint, go below a
successor, inspect an iterator counter, or normalize a term in one step.

3. Preliminary derived rules without transport
----------------------------------------------
Symmetry uses no HC. Prove T := D_xy(0=0) by DI, using reflexivity for its
premise. Open T at b. Reflexivity gives b=b, so VSub on b=b and the weakened
premise u=v gives v=u. The conclusion is b-free, so DE closes the branch.
Consequently right-endpoint HC is derivable by symmetry, left HC, symmetry.

Case closure: if Gamma,B(0) |- B(S z), introduce D_xy B with unused x and
witness 0, then eliminate that D. The local Q_y B is independent of its
fresh witness, so it may be returned. Thus Gamma |- Q_y B.

Numerical cases: from Gamma |- B(0) and Gamma |- B(S z), with z fresh,
weakening gives the premise of case closure; QE at an arbitrary term t gives
Gamma |- B(t). This uses no transport or equation induction.

4. The directly staged packet
------------------------------
Define two raw term abbreviations; bound variables are renamed freshly:

  C(v,a,b) := R(v,a;i,u.b),
  F(v;a,b) := R(v,0;i,u.C(i,a,b)).

The unused state coordinates are deliberate. Root conversion alone yields

  C(0,a,b) -> a,
  C(S n,a,b) -> b,
  F(0;a,b) -> 0,
  F(S n;a,b) -> C(n,a,b).

In particular F(1;a,b) -> C(0,a,b) -> a and
F(2;a,b) -> C(1,a,b) -> b. Each arrow is a single ROOT contraction.
There is no reduction inside a counter, no predecessor, and no parity term.

Given Gamma |- s=t and Gamma |- h(s)=r, choose fresh x,y and put

  P(x,y) := F(y;x,h(x)) = F(y;t,r).

Introduce D_xy P at witness s. Its desired successor premise is P(s,S z),
which root-converts at both endpoints to

  C(z,s,h(s)) = C(z,t,r).

Numerical cases on z prove this last equation: at z=0 it root-converts to
the supplied s=t; at z=S w it root-converts to the supplied h(s)=r. The
local assumption P(s,0) is unused. Hence Gamma |- D_xy P.

Open this formula at fresh b. Its Q assumption has the base P(b,0), obtained
from 0=0 by root expansions. QE at 1 and 2, followed by root contractions at
the endpoints, gives respectively

  b=t,                  h(b)=r.

VSub with source b gives r=h(t). DE discharges b. This proves unrestricted
one-template crossed transport:

  s=t, h(s)=r  /  r=h(t).

No use of equality transport occurs before the final VSub. The numerical
cases and the packet introduction are valid even if s is literally 0.
This is the noncircular bootstrap that makes root conversion sufficient.

5. Equivalence and bi-interpretability
--------------------------------------
Let N_R be the native Q/D scalar calculus on these R terms with the two
computation equations and unrestricted one-template crossed transport,
together with Hyp, QE, DE, DI and QI. Refl is derivable there, or may harmlessly
be retained. The eight-rule root calculus derives every rule of N_R:
computation follows by HC from reflexivity; crossed transport is section 4;
the other rules are primitive. Conversely N_R derives HC by a computation
equation, symmetry, congruence and transitivity; VSub is transport with the
term template u. Every inference is therefore intertranslatable, with the
same raw context and conclusion. This is an equivalence of OPEN consequence
relations, not just a coincidence of closed theorem sets.

The standard native scalar adequacy theorem applies with R terms. One route
is completely explicit: the original iterator is the abbreviation
I(n,a;u.h)=R(n,a;i,u.h), with unused i, so the native scalar theory and all
its definitions of intuitionistic connectives occur in N_R. After transport
is recovered, equation induction gives uniqueness of every fixed primitive
recursion. All numerical terms of N_R are primitive recursive, by structural
induction: the R constructor is ordinary primitive recursion, with its other
free variables and the initializer carried as parameters. Conversely every
primitive recursive description translates directly to an R term, by
induction on the description (zero, successor, projections, composition and
primitive recursion). Thus R is a staged definitional extension of the
primitive-recursive language already interpreted by the scalar native core.

For clarity, one may compare first with ordinary intuitionistic first-order
PRA in the full language of primitive-recursive descriptions. Interpret each
fixed R term by its primitive-recursive description, and interpret each such
description by the R term just constructed. The domains in both directions
are all numbers, equality is actual equality, and the maps on objects are
the identity. Induction on descriptions, using equation induction and
uniqueness of primitive recursion for the recursion case, proves both term
round trips. The native Q/D adequacy theorem supplies the formula round trips
for all intuitionistic connectives and unbounded quantifiers. It follows by
induction on arbitrary formulas that each formula is provably equivalent to
its double translation. Both domain-comparison graphs are x=y. Contradiction
translates to 0=S0 in both directions, so equiconsistency follows as well.

This bridge invokes the pinned repository's established native scalar/iPRA
adequacy theorem, rather than reproving its entire connective macro library.
It adds no appeal to normalization: every new term equation is a fixed
primitive-recursive definition, and every administrative packet reduction
is explicitly a root step. The fact that R has counter access is essential
to this particular proof; it does NOT settle whether BH_0 or BH_1 on the
original counter-blind iterator language is complete.

6. Seven-rule presentation (implemented in checker_seven.py)
-----------------------------------------------------------
DI and QI can be replaced by the established witnessed introduction:

  Gamma,B(t,eta) |- B(t,S z) / Gamma |- D_xy B,
  eta = z if B is an equation AND t is literally 0; eta=0 otherwise.

Case closure before transport uses the unused dummy witness 1, which selects
case mode. The packet proof ignores its local assumption and therefore works
in either mode. Symmetry uses the trivial D(0=0), whose premise is reflexive
in either mode. Once transport is available, the missing case introduction
at witness 0 is recovered at the raw alias j=R(0,0;i,u.0), which is not
literally 0 but equals 0 by HC. Transport moves the relevant instances
between witnesses 0 and j. QI is recovered by witnessed introduction at 0
with unused witness coordinate, followed by DE. This is the same witness-rule
repair already present in ConciseIPRA, now applied to the root calculus.
It is a theorem about a seven-rule presentation. checker_seven.py implements
exactly this version; checker.py retains the eight-rule version as a readable
control. This is not a compact-frontier size win.

7. Checker and verification scope
---------------------------------
checker.py checks exactly the eight rules in section 2; checker_seven.py
checks the seven-rule variant in section 6. Numerical terms are
tagged lists: ["z"], ["s",t], ["r",n,a,h]; variables are exact nonnegative
Python integers, with index 0 newest. In an R body, u has index 0 and i index
1. Formula tags are ["=",s,t], ["q",B], ["d",B]; a D body has y at index 0
and x at index 1. Initial public goals are closed and are fully validated.

HC proof node: ["root",new_left,proof_of_old_equation]. The checker compares
one root contraction in either direction, and does not normalize. A local
parameter may not escape DE: its branch result must literally equal the
lifted, validated outer target. QI in the eight-rule version rejects compound
induction matrices. In the seven-rule version there is no QI tag: the single
DI rule selects progress exactly for a literal equality matrix and a literal
zero witness. It does not normalize the witness before this test.
Assertions are NOT used for certificate acceptance; optimized Python has
the same intended checks. checker's primitive syntax walker is used only on
validated input or formulas already derived by the checker.

verify.py is an untrusted certificate generator and regression script. It
constructs a genuine open transport derivation, not a hand-added transport
axiom. It also derives the CLOSED theorem

  S(R(0,0;i,u.u)) = S0

by that transport construction. Direct HC on its nested redex is rejected.
Further tests cover a nonvacuous equation-induction step, illegal source
shapes, an escaping witness, ill-scoped and Boolean variables, a false
computation, and the restriction of induction to equations. The closed
certificates are replayed as separate CLI processes under normal Python
and Python -O. These finite tests check the implementation's important
boundaries; they do not replace the metatheoretic proof above.

Measurements are reproduced by verify.py and by verify.py --seven, and
recorded in verification.json and verification_seven.json. The latter also
checks transport at the literal zero witness, whose progressive assumption
differs from the case assumption but is unused by the packet proof.
The source is far larger than the compact frontier. The comparisons must not
equate its fully validated tagged-input contract with a frontier checker
whose public grammar is an external precondition. No absolute-minimality
claim follows from any of these measurements.
