New beyond that current baseline
-------------------------------

1. Put the payload directly in the inherited toggle, with shifted
   count:
      H(v)=U(Sv,R(v))=C(N(v),R(v),0).
   H has 28 raw nodes instead of 30. This is outside the architecture
   of the current 30-node lower bound; the payload is integrated in
   the toggle rather than selected by a separate control tree.
2. Reverse the saturating comparison. The current predecessor uses
      I(SSu,E(m);v.Hhat(v))
   as its update signal. The reversed version uses
      I(m,E(Su);v.Hhat(v))
   and swaps the outer selector branches. It saves one successor
   occurrence and has 49 nodes.
3. Combine them:
      P(m)=I(m,0;u.C(I(m,E(Su);v.H(v)),Su,u)).
   The final expanded term has 47 nodes and obeys P(0)=0 and P(Sm)=m
   in the equational fragment.

The general toggle lemma is not claimed as new. Neither the absorbing
idea nor selector cancellation is claimed as new. The manuscript
includes their proofs to make the dependency chain auditable.

Claim boundary
--------------

This does not establish a lower bound on arbitrary predecessors and does
not beat the compact standalone proof-checker envelope. It is a concrete
arithmetic-term reduction inside the unchanged, inherited iPRA-equivalent
calculus. Both predecessor equations and the identity interpretation of
the numerical domain are preserved. The new theorem has a written
equational proof and independently checked finite artifacts, not a new
Lean/Rocq proof object.
