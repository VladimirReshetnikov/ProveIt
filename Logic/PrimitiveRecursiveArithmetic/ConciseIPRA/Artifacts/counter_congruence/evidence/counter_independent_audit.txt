CHECKED DEPENDENCY SEQUENCE

1. Reflexivity needs only RS and RL. For a term s, the computation
   rho_s = R(Ss,0;u,v.u) = s is an RS axiom. Using this equation as
   both RL premises gives s=s. No equality congruence is used.

2. RL and reflexivity give symmetry, transitivity and recovery of an
   available equation as an object proof. Explicitly, an available s=t
   and the object proof s=s give t=s. A second symmetry gives s=t.
   If p=q and q=r are proved, symmetry gives q=p; RL with minor q=r
   gives p=r. This works under arbitrary retained contexts.

3. General object-assumption recovery is external induction on Eq/D
   formula depth. The equation case is step 2. For D, eliminate the
   assumption, case-introduce the same D with the exposed witness, and
   use its Q clause at the new successor after recursively recovering
   the smaller base hypothesis. No formula transport is needed.

4. Numerical cases need only DI-case, DE and QE. Given derivations of
   E(0) and E(Sz), weaken the successor proof by an unused E(0) base,
   case-introduce D with unused witness coordinate, eliminate it to
   obtain the Q clause, and apply QE to the base at the target term.
   This argument applies to arbitrary object E; the proposed theorem
   uses it only on a literal equation. It invokes neither equation
   induction, arithmetic coverage nor numerical congruence.

5. For a fixed finite numerical template F, choose all bound variables
   and the case parameter fresh for its passive parameters. Put
     H_F(n) = R(n,F(0);u,v.S(F(Su))),
     W(n)   = R(n,0;u,v.u),
     J_F(n) = W(H_F(n)).
   At 0, R0 gives H_F(0)=S(F(0)). At Sz, RS gives
   H_F(Sz)=S(F(Sz)); the update ignores v. Apply CC to W, apply W's
   RS equation W(Sa)=a, and compose using step 2. Step 4 therefore
   gives J_F(n)=F(n) at every target term n. No induction or arbitrary
   contextual rewrite has entered the proof.

6. Given an available s=t, CC applied to H_F yields H_F(s)=H_F(t).
   CC applied to W yields J_F(s)=J_F(t). The identities from step 5
   and root equality algebra give F(s)=F(t). The seed and update of
   H_F contain only fixed passive parameters, not the generic counter
   variable n. This is the crucial syntactic condition for CC.

7. Apply step 6 to both numerical sides of any atomic formula. Together
   with root equality algebra this recovers arbitrary atomic transport.

8. The already inherited backward-base / closure-use / forward-successor
   argument lifts atomic transport through D by external formula-depth
   induction. Identity expansion from step 3 supplies its needed object
   base proofs. All local contexts remain allowed. No quantified or
   compound arithmetic induction instance is introduced.

9. For the reverse inclusion, original atomic transport immediately
   derives RL (motive v=r) and derives CC from reflexivity with the
   literal-equation motive R(s,a;h)=R(v,a;h). Hence exact contextual
   consequence equivalence, rather than only one-way soundness, holds.
