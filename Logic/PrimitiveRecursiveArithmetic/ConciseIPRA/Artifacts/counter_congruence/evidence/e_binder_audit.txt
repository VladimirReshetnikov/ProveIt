AUDIT OF THE PROPOSED E BINDER
=============================

Proposed ordinary meaning:
  E_{x,y} B(x,y) = forall y exists x (B(x,0) -> B(x,y)).

Naive introduction rules being audited:
  Case:     Gamma, B(t(z),0) |- B(t(z),S z)
            --------------------------------
            Gamma |- E_{x,y} B(x,y)

  Progress: Gamma, B(t,z) |- B(t,S z)
            --------------------------------
            Gamma |- E_{x,y} B(x,y)
            with B a literal equation and t independent of fresh z.

Here z is fresh for Gamma and fixed parameters; t is supplied as finite
numerical term syntax at the inference node. The proposed elimination
instantiates y, then exposes an existential eigenwitness x and its local
conditional B(x,0)->B(x,y). Ordinary direct Hyp and numerical equality
transport may remain present.

FINDING: the intended full formula roundtrip is not derivable from these
rules. The obstacle is the order of witness choice, not merely a missing
identity lemma.

Counterinterpretation
---------------------
Use standard N, actual equality and the usual primitive-recursive meanings
of numerical terms. Recursively interpret E by

  E_{x,y} B  iff there is a primitive-recursive unary function f such that
                for every y, B(f(y),0) -> B(f(y),y).

This is an external semantics for the native proof calculus, not an
additional axiom or an internal uniform evaluator. Passive parameters are
fixed natural numbers in each valuation. Nested formulas have their
recursively defined meanings.

All naive primitive rules are sound in this semantics:

1. Case introduction supplies the primitive-recursive selector
   f(0)=0, f(Sz)=t(z). The implication at 0 is tautological; the premise
   supplies every successor instance.
2. Atomic progress uses the constant selector f(y)=t. If B(t,0) holds,
   ordinary induction on N and the premise give every B(t,y); if the base
   is false, every required implication is true. Freshness fixes Gamma.
3. Elimination at a numerical term u chooses the available value f(u), so
   its ordinary existential continuation is valid.
4. Hypothesis, equality transport and numerical computation are sound.

The proposed macro families still have their ordinary meanings in this
counterinterpretation. When x is unused, the PR-selector requirement is
vacuous (choose constant zero), giving conditional universal closure.
The existential macro E_{x,y} G_y A(x) holds iff there is an x with A(x):
one direction uses the selector at y=1; the other uses a constant function.
Thus implication, conjunction and universal closure encoded with unused
witness variables retain their ordinary truth values. This is a one-world
intuitionistic semantics, so classical truth at this metalevel suffices to
refute derivability of the claimed reverse implication.

Separation of E from its intended ordinary meaning
------------------------------------------------
Choose any total recursive function F that is not primitive recursive.
For complete self-containment, enumerate all unary primitive-recursive
descriptions with junk interpreted as zero, use the terminating recursive
description evaluator, and diagonalize F(n)=f_n(n)+1. This is total
recursive; F cannot equal any enumerated f_e at its own index e.

Fix a Turing program computing F. Let R(n,c) be its primitive-recursive
finite halting-trace predicate and out(c) its primitive-recursive output
decoder. R is total in c for each n because F is total. Any primitive-
recursive choice c(n) satisfying R(n,c(n)) would make out(c(n)) a
primitive-recursive implementation of F, a contradiction.

Use the native numerical gate to form a literal equation B(x,y) which is
automatically true at y=0 and, at y=S n, means R(n,x). This is expressible
by a fixed primitive-recursive characteristic term, predecessor, and the
zero/successor selector.

Then the intended ordinary reading forall y exists x
(B(x,0)->B(x,y)) is true in standard N: choose any x at y=0 and a valid
halting trace at each successor. But E B is false in the counterinterpretation:
a PR selector f for E would give c(n)=f(Sn), hence a PR implementation
out(f(Sn)) of F.

Because the ordinary logical macros retain their ordinary meanings, the
native encoding of the intended ordinary reading is true while the raw
E formula is false. Therefore the reverse formula composite cannot be a
theorem of the naive calculus.

Scope and possible repairs
--------------------------
This disproves the proposed identity-domain, intended-meaning roundtrip.
It does not disprove every conceivable bi-interpretation of every theory
using an E-shaped binder. No such alternative interpretation is supplied.

An explicit repair must permit the witness to be obtained within the
scope of the newly introduced arbitrary y, after local eliminations.
A two-stage introduction judgment or continuation can do that, but its
syntax/checker cost must be charged. Another route restricts native E
matrices to separated modes: x unused for closure, or y appearing solely
as a gate around an x-formula for existential introduction. That changes
the formula grammar and needs a closure and interpretation proof.

The original D = exists x forall y conditional avoids this obstruction:
its existential witness can be exposed once, before the universal
introduction. Moving forall before exists creates the uniform-choice
problem for a single rule with a preselected term witness.
