2. NOVELTY EXCLUSIONS WITHIN THE PRESENT CORPUS

Already done: explicit elimination endpoint certificates; elimination-only
assumption availability with derived identity; arbitrary equality transport
derived from atomic transport; named specialization via variables plus 1;
shared continuations; benign failed endpoint matches leaving the goal
unchanged; computation equations generated as local assumptions; discharge
of a fresh generated equation by its known zero instance; successor-seeded
counter-aware recursion with immediate predecessor and a direct PR compiler.

The generated-assumptions draft already combines the last two continuation
ideas in a complete 685-byte program. Its generator permits H(w) even when
H(w) is not universally true, provided H(0) is a computation theorem and
w is absent from the retained context and goal. Soundness is substitution
of zero followed by cut. Merely putting axiom checking into context
extension, or calling this existential elimination, is not a new theorem
relative to this corpus.

The atomic-transport draft itself admits the compound transport lifting
already existed historically. Its new restriction costs source bytes and
is not the cause of its total byte improvement.

Other completed structural mathematics: the scalar countdown report proves
a fixed reflection sentence equivalent to quantifier-free induction over
its uniqueness base; the successor-recursion report adds a commutation
completion. These have not beaten the small complete checkers.

Already stated obstruction: the fused-seed recursor R(0;h)=h(0),
R(Sn;h)=h(S R(n;h)) with only variables, zero and successor is monotone
in each numerical input on N. It cannot term-define zero test. This rules
out one same-domain term compiler, not different-domain or graph-defined
bi-interpretations.

3. MATHEMATICAL SCOPE AND AUDIT FINDINGS

The scalar Choice-DI bridge has no fatal gap identified in this review.
Its important safeguards should be retained in any successor construction:

- Progress introduction is restricted to a literal equation; case
  introduction permits compound formulas. A binder around an equation
  does not count as literal. General formula induction would strengthen
  the theory, and must not enter through a certificate alias.
- Arithmetic signature stages are external finite descriptions. There
  is no internal universal total evaluator for all PR programs.
- The ordinary first-order realization explicitly includes numerical
  coverage and the falsity bridge (0=1)->bottom. Positive equations alone
  have singleton models. Native designation of 0=1 as contradiction must
  not be confused with having derived ordinary falsity from equations.
- Whole-domain equality reflection is proved internally before use of
  pairing and quantifier-free characteristic functions. Standard-N tests
  alone do not establish the claimed bi-interpretation.
- Numerical round trips require internal uniqueness for each fixed
  recursion description, preserving all passive parameters. Formula
  round trips require Q and D to recover their ordinary conditional
  universal and existential meanings; theorem equivalence alone is weaker
  than bi-interpretability.
- Mathematical proofs remain written proofs. None of these current tiny
  checkers comes with a full Lean/Rocq verification of raw JSON soundness.

For generated assumptions, the eager validation of the predecessor
iterator is essential. Without i([*n,t,u],d=1), a two-child counter can
be ignored by a constant update and generate a false equation. The
existing draft includes a concrete mutation counterexample. Do not delete
that check merely because the update does not use its state.

The raw codec accepts inert aliases and ignores unused packet prefixes.
Soundness is only about the decoded requested formula of successful
traces; canonical completeness promises one valid encoding per theorem,
not validity of all syntactic aliases. Any claimed simplified codec should
be judged at this same complete-program boundary, or state a changed one.

4. USEFUL DIRECTION WITHOUT A NOVELTY CLAIM

A genuinely new structural improvement would remove one generator,
opener operation, or fundamental rule family while keeping the complete
proof representation and interpretation bridge explicit. Combining
already published variants or Python golfing can still improve the
measurement but is not a significant new arithmetic foundation. The
parent's candidate should be checked against the exact present envelope
and adversarial raw-scope cases before any dominance claim is made.
