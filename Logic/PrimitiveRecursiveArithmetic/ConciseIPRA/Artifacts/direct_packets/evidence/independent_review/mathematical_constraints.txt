Independent mathematical audit, 2026-11-12

Scope. The active request asks for a complete presentation of full first-order intuitionistic PRA, with equiconsistency and a bi-interpretation. Equivalence of closed arithmetical consequences, relative consistency, or matching standard-model denotations is insufficient. The user's domain freedom is broader than the existing README's whole-domain/identity-graph qualification; a genuinely different domain is allowed if both interpretation composites are internally definably isomorphic to the identities.

The current corpus is extensive. The simple ideas examined independently are already present: progressive H binder with alternating true/A clock for universal quantification (ch10_obs, section The progressive binder presentation); full and zero normalization; witness-free/newest-variable introduction; one-template equality; three-family Use/Intro/Name; discharge/evaluation fusion; equality from iterator computation; clauses and one-body contexts.

Reflexivity obstruction. Theorem Closed seeds do not replace reflexivity in ch05_claus.tex (label claus:thm:reflobs) supplies a concrete barrier. Delete universal reflexivity and add finitely many true closed equations S. Let M be the finite set of their standard numerical endpoint values. Interpret an atom s=t as: s and t have the same standard value, and that value lies in M. Read D ordinarily as exists x (B(x,0) implies forall y B(x,y)); clauses likewise ordinarily. All the remaining Eval/Use/Open/Intro rules preserve truth. Hence the system cannot prove k=k when k is outside M. In particular a seed 0=0 alone cannot replace universal reflexivity. This is a counterinterpretation of the inference system, not a candidate arithmetic interpretation; that distinction makes the argument legitimate even though the intended equality is identity.

Consequently the three-rule full-normal-form calculus cannot simply lose its universal reflexivity clause by using Intro self-application. A substantive replacement must break the colored-equality invariant, e.g. parameterized computation axioms (which themselves incur specification cost), or a universally ranging diagonal seed.

Complexity caveat. The repository's coordinatewise observed envelope is not a theorem establishing a lower bound on all presentations. Different programs attain its coordinates. It is legitimate to claim a specific improvement in one coordinate over the pinned observed corpus, but not to claim that a 240-token program simultaneously beats a 469-byte record if its own byte size exceeds that. The README's earlier 249/271/479 figures are stale against the incoming packages. At least ipra_fixed_points_and_checker reports 241/262/469, and the final census may find still smaller incoming sources.

Interface caveat. The compact profile assumes canonical guarded public goals and exact integer/list proof data; it does not supply all-JSON public grammar and alphabet validation. A complete logical theorem checker on that defined syntax can still be mathematically sound, but its byte comparison is not interchangeable with the stricter arbitrary-JSON/public-goal profiles. Report the contract expressly. Properly sorted public goals and the semantic meaning of malformed proof-data failures must not be hidden behind a small entrypoint.

Normalization caveat. Normalization of primitive recursive terms may consume enormous resources, but under ideal finite resources it can terminate. The trusted normalizer belongs in the checker-size measurement. Nontermination on a finite malformed certificate or a valid term is a different issue from physical resource exhaustion.

No new mathematical rule deletion established by this audit. The inspected reductions are useful but already covered. A claim of a new simplest axiomatization requires an actual new calculus or an explicitly qualified implementation-level improvement; novelty cannot be inferred from a new name or a repackaged combination.
