# Independent audit of the inherited full-iPRA bridge

Sources inspected in the current certified-zero archive:

- `reference/inherited_choice_basis.tex`
- `reference/scalar_predecessor.tex`
- `reference/equality_reflection.tex`
- `reference/primitive_recursion.tex`

This audit verifies the mathematical dependency order on paper. It is not a
Lean/Rocq formalization, and it does not turn finite implementation tests into a
proof of the universal interpretation theorem. No circular dependency or false
calculation was found in the inspected bridge.

## 1. The logical reconstruction precedes the arithmetic bootstrap

The case version of paired introduction, paired elimination, and closure use
give numerical cases for every fixed native formula. Progress is not required.
The computation equation r=t, where r=I(0,t;u.0), used as both transport premises
with the motive v=t, gives t=t. The usual equality laws follow.

The gate and selector constructions recurse externally on finite formula
syntax. In mixed-depth selector cases, padding an equation leaves an equation
as the recursive matrix, so the maximum remaining binder depth really drops.
No universal formula evaluator is introduced. The derived implication,
conjunction, existential, disjunction, universal, and falsity rules are
intuitionistic. In particular the universal macro retains a separate zero
conjunct, and conjunction projection uses only the two fixed computations at
1 and 2. The disjunction argument uses the already available numerical-cases
rule, not quantifier-free induction or an assumed equality characteristic.

Under 0=1, the selector gives s=C(0,s,t)=C(1,s,t)=t for all terms s,t.
External induction on native formula depth then gives falsity elimination.
This proves the explosion needed by the optional bottom-completion argument.

Progress at a literal equation, with an unused existential coordinate, gives
contextual equation induction. It does not give induction at arbitrary native
formulas. Thus the arithmetic argument starts with exactly the resources it
claims: computation, numerical cases, equality, and literal-equation induction.

## 2. Scalar predecessor does not assume predecessor, pairing, or QFI

The addition identities used in the scalar bootstrap are obtained by literal
equation induction alone. They require no cancellation or successor
injectivity. Boolean ranges of Z and N come from numerical cases on their
arguments or iteration counters, rather than induction on disjunctions.

The crucial factor-map calculation was checked explicitly:

- N has period two, N(2n)=0, N(2n+1)=1, and N(3n)=N(n).
- The three finite values j(0)=1, j(1)=2, j(2)=0 suffice; no modular-division
  theorem about M is assumed.
- M(G(n))=N(n) is one literal equation. Its step uses the two already proved
  Boolean cases of N(n), applying one or two successor computations of M.
- G(n+2)=G(n)+3 gives G(2n)=3n.
- For G(3n)=3G(n)+N(n), the successor left side becomes
  3G(n)+2N(n)+4, and the successor right side becomes
  3G(n)+3N(n)+3+Z(N(n)). These agree separately at N(n)=0 and N(n)=1.
- H(u)=C(N(u),G(u),u) therefore has H(2u)=3u, H(1)=1, and H(3u)=3H(u).
  This does not assume that arbitrary elements already have even/odd normal
  forms; it splits the finite characteristic N(u).

The iterator rotation and scaling identities are equation inductions with all
passive parameters fixed. Their use in the comparator establishes, internally
at all arguments,

    L(0,y)=1,  L(Sx,0)=0,  L(Sx,Sy)=L(x,y).

The saturated update B_m has B_(Sm)(Su)=S B_m(u) and B_(Sm)(0)=1.
Induction on t with m fixed yields W(St,Sm)=S W(t,m). Only after proving that
parameterized theorem is the substitution t=m=n made to obtain W(n,n)=n.
The extra-step identity W(Sn,n)=n follows from the comparator diagonal.

The final predecessor compares two fixed scalar iterators V(t,m),W(t,m) by
equation induction at fixed m. It does not assume an independent extensionality
rule for updates. Substituting t=Sm after that theorem gives P(Sm)=m.
Successor injectivity is then available by applying P, rather than being used
to construct P. This dependency order is sound.

## 3. Equality reflection is internal and uses already constructed terms

The equality-reflection appendix is written in a general PRE signature but
explicitly specializes to scalar terms in its introduction. Every operation
actually used before QFI is recovered is one of P, C, addition, truncated
subtraction I(y,x;u.Pu), sign, countdown, or a finite composition of them.
Consequently its general-PRE wording does not smuggle in a primitive-recursive
compiler needed later.

The central residual invariant

    C(sg(d(x,y)), x, y+d(x,y))=x

is a single equation. Its induction holds x fixed. In the successor step,
the zero residual case computes immediately; in the positive case the coverage
of the previous residual gives r=S(Pr), so the old induction equation and the
addition identity (Sy)+r'=y+Sr' establish the step. No induction on an
implication or disjunction is used.

The reflection d(Sa,b)=1 iff a=b is then valid on arbitrary domain elements:
the residual invariant gives b+1=Sa; addition makes this Sb=Sa; P yields b=a.
The converse is the earlier diagonal equation. The characteristic
e(a,b)=sg(K(d(Sa,b))) has range {0,1} and e(a,b)=0 iff actual equality a=b.
Decidability of actual equality follows from these theorems and separation; it
is not assumed from standard numerical behavior.

The Boolean compiler for quantifier-free implication is intuitionistically
valid because each antecedent characteristic has an internally proved
two-value range. No law of excluded middle for quantified formulas follows.
Literal-equation induction on b_A(n)=0 recovers each contextual QFI instance,
including with arbitrary quantified retained assumptions.

## 4. Pairing comes after QFI and does not change the domain

The pairing appendix abbreviates elementary order/semiring inductions as
standard arguments instead of spelling out every proof tree. Its construction
is nevertheless within the now-established scalar QFI language; it does not
invoke an unbounded minimization operator.

For the square-root search, n is fixed throughout induction on the separate
counter i. The three displayed invariants can be combined into one
quantifier-free formula. If the update increments, its test contradicts the
strict-upper-bound alternative, forcing the old state to equal i. If it does
not increment, the new strict upper bound follows from the failed bounded
test. Substitution i=n happens only after proving the fixed-n invariant.

The resulting square pairing pi(a,b)=(a+b)^2+a has the two left inverse
identities L(pi(a,b))=a and H(pi(a,b))=b. Surjectivity is neither claimed nor
needed. Pairing only stores the fixed finite recursion state; the interpreted
number domain remains the whole domain.

The standard scalar compiler keeps all passive parameters fixed while
equation induction proves L(V(n,p))=n. The projection identities then give
both primitive-recursion equations for H(V(n,p)). This is an external
construction for each fixed finite description, not a single internal total
evaluator for all program codes.

## 5. Both composites are stronger than equiconsistency alone

Uniqueness of each fixed recurrence is proved internally by comparing its
values with a single equation induction. External induction on the finite
description then proves both numerical double translations equal the original
operation at all arguments. The domain isomorphism has graph x=y.

For formulas, the paired composite is explicitly reconstructed as
exists x (B(x,0) implies forall y B(x,y)), using the derived ordinary
connectives. The reverse implication uses case introduction at the actual
existential witness, with the base retained. This is a full native-formula
round trip, not just agreement on equational consequences. Falsity is preserved
using the explicit 0=1 bridge. Hence the written bridge supports full-domain
bi-interpretability and equiconsistency, subject to its stated ordinary
first-order interpretation convention.

## Publication caveats

- Attribute this bridge to the earlier drafts; the mandatory-major result does
  not invent its arithmetic bootstrap.
- Describe the new result as equality of canonical native consequence
  relations plus all-input soundness. Do not infer that arbitrary raw JSON
  aliases form a separate bi-interpretable object theory.
- Distinguish the complete checker implementation and its tests from the
  paper proof of the checker metatheory and the full-iPRA interpretation.
- The pairing appendix contains routine compressed arithmetic arguments, not
  a fully expanded mechanically checked derivation. No contrary claim should
  be made about it.
