INDEPENDENT MATHEMATICAL AUDIT OF exact_fused_json_if.py

Scope: inspected exact_fused_json_if.py, the original exact-binding
proper_tokens.py, the incoming intrinsic-validation checker and readable
expansion, and syntax_work/intrinsic_soundness_review.md. This is a written
audit, not a proof-assistant verification or a claim based on random tests.

1. Public input and sort

At floor k=0 the list guard enforces arity 1 or 2 at formula depths, and
arity 1 or 3 at numerical depths. At a formula node, 5-len(a) gives the
existing D/Eq/Q binder increments; Q is excluded at the public root and
public formula positions by the public arity check. At numerical nodes
the increments are 0,0,2 for an iterator and 0 for successor. Every
surviving public scalar is an odd nonnegative integer below its binding
depth. Boolean true behaves as 1; public false is rejected. There is no
separate literal guarded-matrix-shape validator; describe the public
grammar as the inherited sorted native Eq/D grammar, using the appropriate
existing calculus with its certificate-selected case/progress mode.

2. Active binding and raw annotations

The expression j & 2 equals 2*(j==2) at every active numerical child
index (0,1,2). It differs at later indexes in overwidth raw nodes.
Consequently there is NO literal trace equivalence on arbitrary raw
integer/list inputs with the original proper_tokens.py, whose increment
is 2*(j==2). For example, at floor -1 and numerical depth 3, opening
[1,1,1,3] at image 1 gives [1,1,1,3] with j&2, versus [1,1,1,1] with
2*(j==2).

The change can nevertheless be sound under the inherited total raw
reading: a numerical list of any nonoperative arity denotes numerical
zero, with all children annotations; a formula list of nonoperative
arity denotes administrative truth. At an operative node the two binding
rules agree, and at a nonoperative node no child's interpretation affects
that of the parent. The proof therefore needs this erasure argument, or
must start from the incoming intrinsic-validation checker that already
uses j&2. Do not silently replace it by a universal trace-equivalence
claim against proper_tokens.py.

3. No-image scans prohibit forged names

For JSON-decoded data, a*0==[] recognizes lists. Null and objects raise
on multiplication; strings give an empty string; numerical multiplication
does not give a list. A scalar float whose value is in the retained
interval reaches d & (a % 2), which raises TypeError. An out-of-interval
float reaches the default 0j image, whose comparison raises. Thus a
successful no-image scan contains no floats, strings, nulls, or objects.
Booleans are harmless integer aliases. The same statement applies to
every actually demanded replacement image, which is scanned without an
image of its own.

4. Template floats are holes, not numerical parameters

In a successful opening with an explicit image, a float can survive only
as an out-of-interval hole. Replace such an occurrence by the integer
equal to its current local depth. That integer is also outside the strict
retained interval, and produces the same opening result. The two openings
of an equality-elimination template are performed at the same floor and
depth, before the recursive major proof; hence one local integer/list
reification suffices for both. This is local to the rule occurrence; it
does not require a global conversion of all unused JSON data.

5. Computation leaves

Every terminal goal first passes a no-image scan. If it matches the
constructed computation equation, any scalar aliases in the compared
candidate must be integral floats or Booleans equal to the corresponding
integer; they can be replaced by that integer. A falsy source is absent
from the zero-computation candidate and its exact JSON spelling is
irrelevant. A truthy source is present in the successor counter and must
match a pure term. Any demanded iterator image is scanned. If membership
succeeds instead at an already retained context formula, the unused
candidate data require no additional interpretation.

6. Freshness and entry reservation

The proof-call goal is scanned BEFORE the current floor is decremented.
Thus the newly reserved floor cannot appear as a previously validated
free name. Equality target/source instances and an introduction witness
are prepared before the recursive major proof. Every major proof,
including an administrative neutral proof, consumes a packet and strictly
lowers the floor before returning. The subsequently selected fresh name
therefore lies strictly below the names that could survive the earlier
scans. Replacing an out-of-scope template scalar by a hole is not retaining
that scalar as a free name. These observations are the essential reasons
that references to future eigenparameters do not become captured.

7. Control-flow fusion

The main expression is an expression statement, not a returned tuple.
The proof function returns None on every normal completion. On a terminal
match the right operand of 'or' is skipped. Otherwise the tuple first
performs the optional target/source assignments and then calls the
continuation. Both the target opening and the source opening happen
before the raw major is reconstructed and proved. This preserves the
intended premise order. Full computation-candidate construction occurs
before membership even when an earlier context entry will match; its
eagerness is part of the implemented protocol.

8. Outcome and limits

I found no mechanism in these changes for proving a false closed native
goal. The local all-JSON reification and freshness arguments are coherent,
provided the report explicitly retains the inherited total reading of raw
administrative nodes and starts from the correct intrinsic control or
supplies the erasure lemma in item 2. The claim is accepted-input
soundness and canonical completeness for public goals. It is not exact
typing of the entire document, equality of all raw certificate languages,
a new mathematical axiom deletion, or a corpus-wide minimum.
