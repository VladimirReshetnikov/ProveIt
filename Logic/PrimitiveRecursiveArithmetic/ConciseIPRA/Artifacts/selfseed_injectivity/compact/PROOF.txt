SHIFTED SHARED LEAVES: COMPOSITION AUDIT
======================================

Claim and boundary
------------------
checker468.py independently reconstructs an existing implementation
composition of the shared-leaf 469-byte checker and the even-depth offset
used by offset472.py. The mathematical axioms, rules, and guarded public
theory are unchanged. It saves one normalized source byte, one token and
one AST node against its exact historical shared-leaf control. This is
not a new current record and supplies no arithmetic axiom deletion.

The domain of the compact interface is finite exact-integer/list data,
with a canonical closed guarded Eq/D public goal. An input is a JSON
array ending in [A,0]; earlier consumed instructions have four fields
[m,s,u,t], and are popped from right to left. The terminal numerical

not independently validated by the compact checker. Exceptions reject;
normal termination with exactly ACCEPT and LF accepts. Unused prefixes
are permitted. Actual Python resource limits are separate from finite

2. Shifted sorted syntax and the opening lemma
---------------------------------------------
At p protected slots, the formula depth is 2+2p and the term depth is
5+2p. At a term position, scalars 0,1,2,3,4 denote zero. Scalars 5+2j and
6+2j name protected slot j, provided j<p. A negative integer in [k,0)
is the rigid parameter with that name. Other scalars denote the one
substitution hole. Formula-position scalars denote the true equation.

The array decoding is inherited unchanged: at term positions arities
1 and 3 denote successor and scalar iteration; at formula positions
arities 1,2,3 denote D, equality and Q. Other arities have constant
zero/true readings. Unary term nodes protect two dummy zero slots;
iterator counter/seed children protect one dummy slot and the body
protects its state slot. D protects two slots; Q protects one. Children
are traversed at depth d+5-arity. The arity-2 formula transition has
increment 3 and switches to the term sort. All other meaningful
increments are even and preserve the sort.

Canonical zero is 0. Canonical bound-slot codes are 5+2j, and negative
parameters are unchanged. Thus canonical old encodings shift every
positive slot/hole code upward by two. The added positive aliases 3,4
are zero aliases, not new variables.

The actual opener is eager. An out-of-scope scalar computes and validates
the replacement image at depth 5 before multiplication by depth parity.
The missing replacement is the function c, which fails numeric/tree
operations. Consequently a successful no-image opening fixes its input
literally. At an even-depth hole a supplied image yields scalar zero or
an empty list; either reads as true. The actual partial operation may
reject extra annotations, and cannot be replaced by a lazy function in
an acceptance-equivalence claim.

Tree induction proves: successful opening gives a closed reading;
no-image opening preserves the reading; an actual hole is replaced
capture-avoidably by the reading of an old-scope root term. A validated
replacement's indices refer only to its own internal binders, zero and
old negative parameters, so copying it below additional outer slots does
not capture it. Lowering depth by two exposes precisely the outermost
slot. A D matrix has depth 6; opening at depth 4 substitutes its witness
while retaining its numerical index, and opening at depth 2 substitutes
that index. Repeating a unary D array three times changes it to a Q
array and exposes its witness. These are exactly the opener and exposure
lemmas of OFFSET_PROOF, with their full raw-annotation qualifications.

The floor change k to k-1 is untouched. All previously closed meaningful
readings use negative names in [k,0), so the new name k-1 is fresh. The
positive depth shift does not weaken this old-scope condition.

3. Shared-leaf soundness
-----------------------
Every call still consumes exactly one packet. It validates its goal and
generates the same computation expression H(s,u,t), now at shifted
depths. It closes if the validated goal belongs to g+[H]. Because the
no-image opener is the identity on its success domain, a successful
comparison to H is literal equality, exactly as in the eager shared-leaf
control. A comparison to an old context entry is a hypothesis step.

If s is false-valued, it is 0 or [] and H reads as the zero iteration
equation. If s is true-valued, H reads as the successor iteration equation.
In the latter case the right-hand opening substitutes the reading of
[s,u,t] for the state. If the body does not use its state, the same
computation holds with a constant update; if it does use its state,
validation of the image ensures the closed predecessor and seed readings
agree on both sides. Extra dummy references remain zero. Thus every
successful computation leaf is sound, including noncanonical internal
integer/list trees. This is Section 4 of OFFSET_PROOF with an additional
hypothesis membership alternative, precisely the shared-leaf theorem.

The fresh initial entry 0 has the reading 0=0 and now validates at formula
root depth 2. It can therefore replace the old [] truth entry. Dummy
hypothesis packets [0,0,0,0] generate I(0,0;u.0)=0 in every scope.

4. Non-leaf soundness and the important mode boundary
----------------------------------------------------
A successful recursive call always returns a nonempty singleton list.
Therefore the expression c(g,m,k) and m*3 verifies its major before using
it. As before, a unary major opens D at a fresh negative parameter, a
binary equality major or ternary closure major supports the source-selected
backward use, and other accepted major shapes contribute only a true
administrative context entry. The optional replacement test is evaluated
in the new scope; the old goal is fresh-parameter free and is recovered
by equality transport or closure elimination before that parameter is
discharged. The neutral-major branch opens the witness in the OLD scope,
then allocates its fresh induction/case parameter. None of this sequencing
has been changed.

At that introduction the selector 3 % len(a) is true at a literal equation
(arity 2), false at meaningful compound roots D and Q (arities 1 and 3).
It therefore supplies E(w,b) for atomic progress, and B(w,0) for cases.
Replacing the selector by len(a)-1 would incorrectly apply progress to
auxiliary Q matrices; canonical Eq/D public syntax does not rule out such
raw auxiliary intermediates. That apparent shortcut is not justified.

For faithful auxiliary interpretation, atomic D is read progressively:
  D_xy E := exists x forall y (E(x,y) -> E(x,S y));
compound D is existential conditional closure, and Q is conditional
closure. The public guarded atomic matrices have provable zero instances,
so their progressive and ordinary conditional-closure readings coincide.
The same public arithmetic/logical bridge is inherited unchanged. Merely
calling every auxiliary D an ordinary closure would require care: this
reading is sound but not complete for all unguarded auxiliary formulas.

An induction on the finite successful call tree, using these leaves and
the unchanged non-leaf argument of OFFSET_PROOF, proves soundness for
every accepted raw certificate in the promised alphabet with a canonical
public goal. The initial truth entry is discharged as a theorem.

5. Completeness and the two theory interpretations
--------------------------------------------------
Compile an arbitrary derivation of the inherited guarded/source-selected
calculus in canonical shifted syntax. The opening lemma gives the same
witness substitutions and rule premises. Compile a computation leaf by
its computation packet, using the inherited nonliteral-zero supply where
successor computation's predecessor is semantically zero. Compile each
hypothesis leaf with one dummy packet [0,0,0,0]. This extra packet introduces
no parameter. Ignore semantically true administrative context entries.
The neutral major is scalar 0 and always closes against the initial truth
entry with a dummy packet. Thus every public theorem has a finite accepted
certificate. This is a canonical completeness argument; it does NOT say
that arbitrary raw accepted control certificates survive a naive numeric
shift. The supplied translator is intentionally limited to canonical
integer/list certificates.

The public source theory and the ordinary realization are unchanged.
Use the inherited interpretations to and from full first-order intuitionistic
PRA. All translated axioms and all formula round-trip proofs transfer
through the unchanged theorem set. Both numerical domains are whole,
equality is actual equality, and both numerical comparison graphs are
x=y. Hence bi-interpretability and equiconsistency transfer. Their underlying
arithmetic/logical bridge is an attributed dependency, not a new proof-assistant
formalization by this package.
