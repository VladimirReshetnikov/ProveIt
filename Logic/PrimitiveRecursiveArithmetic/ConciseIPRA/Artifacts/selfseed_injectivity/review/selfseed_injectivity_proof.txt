SUCCESSOR INJECTIVITY FROM TWO STOPPING ORBITS
Research proof, 12 November 2026 (Pacific time)

STATUS AND SCOPE

This is a written proof of the axiom deletion T_F^- = T_F in the current
ConciseIPRA self-seeded recursor presentation. The old T_F adds successor
injectivity to T_F^-. The proof below derives that axiom in T_F^- itself.
It uses ordinary intuitionistic first-order logic, actual equality, the
faithful two-valued equality test, self-seeded computation, separation,
and literal-equation induction with parameters and side assumptions.
No general excluded middle, successor cancellation, numerical order,
least-number principle, initializer, or counter argument is used.

The definitions and all F bodies below are terms of the ORIGINAL language;
none contains a function introduced by a finite-case graph.

1. THE THEORY

Terms: x, 0, S(t), epsilon(s,t), F(n;v.h). In F, v is bound only in h.
Write 1=S(0), 2=S(1). The nonlogical axioms/schemes are:

  F(0;v.h) = S(h[0/v]);
  F(S(n);v.h) = h[F(n;v.h)/v];
  epsilon(s,t)=0 OR epsilon(s,t)=1;
  epsilon(s,t)=1 IFF s=t;
  0 != 1;
  literal-equation induction, with arbitrary parameters and side assumptions.

The induction rule for a literal equation E is:

  Gamma |- E(0),  Gamma,E(z) |- E(S(z))
  -------------------------------------  z fresh for Gamma and parameters
                  Gamma |- E(t).

All standard intuitionistic first-order logical and equality rules are
included. This proof does not claim to eliminate those logical rules.

2. ELEMENTARY PREPARATIONS

Put C(n,a)=F(n;v.a), Z(n)=C(n,0). Direct computation gives

  C(0,a)=S(a),   C(S(n),a)=a,
  Z(0)=1,       Z(S(n))=0.

Consequently S(n)!=0: otherwise Z(S(n))=Z(0) would give 0=1.

For Boolean b,c define

  not_B(b)=epsilon(b,0),
  and_B(b,c)=epsilon(C(b,epsilon(c,0)),0),
  or_B(b,c)=not_B(and_B(not_B(b),not_B(c))).

Their ordinary Boolean truth tables follow by cases b=0/1,c=0/1. In
particular, at b=0 the argument C(0,epsilon(c,0)) is a successor and hence
nonzero; at b=1 that argument is epsilon(c,0). No successor is cancelled.
Every quantifier-free formula A has a Boolean characteristic term chi_A
with chi_A=1 iff A (use epsilon at equations, 0 at falsity, and the Boolean
operations, with implication not_B(chi_A) or_B chi_B). Thus induction on
chi_A(n)=1 gives contextual quantifier-free induction. Every case split
below is a Boolean/equality case split justified by this construction.

There are no successor fixed points. The term e(v)=epsilon(v,0) has no
fixed point: e(w)=w would make w either 0 or 1, and both cases contradict
e(0)=1 and e(1)=0. If S(a)=a, computation gives

  F(a;v.e(v))=F(S(a);v.e(v))=e(F(a;v.e(v))),

contrary to this fact. In particular 0,1,2 are pairwise distinct.

There are no successor two-cycles. Put

  q(v)=Z(epsilon(v,1)),
  c(v)=C(q(v),C(q(v),epsilon(v,0))).

Since 0!=1, at most one of epsilon(v,0) and epsilon(v,1) is 1. If v=0,
then q(v)=1 and epsilon(v,0)=1, giving c(v)=1. If v=1, then q(v)=0 and
epsilon(v,0)=0, giving c(v)=2. At every other v, q(v)=1 and the inner
epsilon is 0, giving c(v)=0. Therefore c has range {0,1,2} and

  c(0)=1, c(1)=2, c(2)=0.

It follows by three Boolean cases that c(c(w))!=w for every w: if equality
held, the range of the outer c would put w in {0,1,2}, on each of which
two applications move it. If S(S(a))=a, the F successor equation twice
would make F(a;v.c(v)) a fixed point of c composed with itself. Hence

  S(a)!=a,    S(S(a))!=a                                  (A)

uniformly in a. Neither fact uses induction.

3. COVERAGE WITHOUT INJECTIVITY (INHERITED, REPRODUCED)

Define

  g_a(v)=or_B(epsilon(v,0),epsilon(S(v),a)),
  theta_a(v)=C(g_a(v),v),
  U(j,a)=F(j;v.theta_a(v)),
  pstar(a)=U(a,a).

The update theta_a holds v when v=0 or S(v)=a and increments otherwise.
Since theta_a(0)=0,

  U(0,a)=1,   U(S(j),a)=theta_a(U(j,a)).

Quantifier-free induction on j proves

  U(j,a)=S(j) OR S(U(j,a))=a.                             (B)

At zero the first disjunct holds. If the second holds, the update holds
the state. Otherwise the first disjunct gives U(j,a)=S(j)!=0, the guard
is false, and the next state is S(S(j)).

Apply (B) with j=m and a=S(S(m)). Both disjuncts imply
S(U(m,a))=a. The state is consequently fixed by theta_a. Two additional
computation steps yield U(S(S(m)),a)=U(m,a), so

  S(pstar(S(S(m))))=S(S(m)).                             (C)

Now use quantifier-free induction on

  n=0 OR n=1 OR S(pstar(n))=n.                           (D)

The base is immediate. For the successor step: n=0 gives S(n)=1; n=1
uses (C) at m=0; the last disjunct rewrites S(n) as S(S(pstar(n))),
to which (C) applies. Thus (D) holds for all n. Consequently

  n=0 OR there exists p with n=S(p).                     (E)

If n=1, take p=0; in the third case take p=pstar(n). This uses no
successor cancellation and no new F body.

4. STOPPING AT A STATE, NOT AT ITS SUCCESSOR

For each parameter a define the ORIGINAL-LANGUAGE terms

  b_a(v)=or_B(epsilon(v,0),epsilon(v,a)),
  h_a(v)=C(b_a(v),v),
  A_a(n)=F(n;v.h_a(v)).

The update h_a has the provable behavior

  h_a(v)=v    if v=0 or v=a,
  h_a(v)=S(v) if v!=0 and v!=a.

In particular h_a(0)=0, so

  A_a(0)=1,   A_a(S(n))=h_a(A_a(n)).                     (F)

LEMMA 1 (range/orbit invariant).
  A_a(n)=S(n) OR A_a(n)=a.                               (G)

Proof. Quantifier-free induction on n. The base is A_a(0)=1. If the
state is a, the update fixes it. Otherwise (G) gives state S(n), which
is nonzero; the update increments to S(S(n)). End.

LEMMA 2 (diagonal absorption).
  If a!=0 then A_a(a)=a.                                (H)

Proof. By coverage (E), choose p with a=S(p). At n=p, both alternatives
of (G) give A_a(p)=a. Therefore A_a(a)=h_a(a)=a by (F). End.

LEMMA 3 (cross absorption under a successor collision).
  If a!=0 and S(a)=S(b), then A_a(b)=a.                  (I)

Proof. Write c=S(a)=S(b). By (G), A_a(b) is either c or a. Suppose it is
c. We have c!=0 and c!=a, the latter by no fixed points (A), so h_a(c)=S(c).
On the other hand (H) gives

  A_a(S(a))=h_a(A_a(a))=a.

Equality S(a)=S(b), followed by (F), therefore gives

  a=A_a(S(a))=A_a(S(b))=h_a(c)=S(c)=S(S(a)),

contradicting no two-cycles (A). The alternative A_a(b)=a is forced. End.

COROLLARY (unique predecessor of 1).
  S(b)=1 implies b=0.                                  (J)

Proof. Decide b=0. In the unequal case apply (I) with target b and source
0, using S(b)=S(0). This gives b=A_b(0)=1. But then S(b)=1=b, contrary
to no fixed points. End.

This short argument replaces the inherited metatheoretic finite-numeral
rigidity construction at k=1; the general numeral-rigidity theorem is not
a dependency of the injectivity proof.

5. THE NEW INJECTIVITY ARGUMENT

THEOREM. T_F^- proves S(a)=S(b) -> a=b.

Proof. Fix S(a)=S(b), and decide a=b. Suppose a!=b. By (J), neither a
nor b can be zero: otherwise the other has successor 1 and is also zero.
Lemmas 2 and 3 give

  A_a(a)=a, A_a(b)=a, A_b(a)=b, A_b(b)=b.                (K)

First establish the forbidden-hit statements

  A_a(n)!=b and A_b(n)!=a, for every n.                  (L)

For example, suppose A_a(n)=b. Its range invariant (G), and a!=b,
force S(n)=b. Since b!=0,a, computation yields

  A_a(b)=A_a(S(n))=h_a(b)=S(b).

But (K) gives A_a(b)=a. Thus a=S(b)=S(a), contradicting no fixed points.
The other statement follows by exchanging a and b.

Now apply literal-EQUATION induction to

  A_a(n)=A_b(n).                                        (M)

The base is 1=1 by (F). For the step, assume both sides at n have value v.
By (L), v!=a and v!=b. If v=0, both updates are 0. If v!=0, both updates
are S(v). Thus h_a(v)=h_b(v), and (F) gives (M) at S(n). All case splits
are decisions of equality, so this is valid intuitionistically.

Equation induction yields (M) at n=a. By (K) its left side is a and
its right side b. This contradicts a!=b, completing the decidable equality
case split and proving a=b. End.

The final induction is one literal equation on TWO separately defined
stopping orbits. This avoids the predecessor cancellation needed by the
earlier attempted intertwining of one orbit with its successor shift.

6. AXIOM DELETION AND INTERPRETATIONS

T_F is, by definition, T_F^- plus S(a)=S(b)->a=b. The theorem proves that
the added axiom is redundant. The two theories have the same terms and
formulas and prove exactly the same sequents. Their mutual interpretations
are the literal identity translations.

The inherited article proves that T_F is bi-interpretable with full
first-order intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic, using whole
numerical domains, actual equality, and numerical comparison graphs x=y.
Composing those existing interpretations with the identity equivalence
above transfers BOTH internal formula round trips and hence equiconsistency
to T_F^-. This uses the actual inherited bi-interpretation, not merely two
conservativity directions or a standard-model interpretation.

Optionally remove primitive successor by the existing definitional
abbreviation S(a)=F(0;v.a). This is independent of the new axiom deletion.

The result is a mathematical simplification of the SELF-SEEDED presentation.
It does not by itself establish that its complete software proof checker
is smaller than the compact native-scalar record. Any size claim for a
separate implementation of the native scalar calculus must remain separate.
