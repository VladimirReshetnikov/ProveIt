1. STRICT 632-BYTE CHECKER

The only source change from the incoming strict637.py is

  q = not residue and p.pop
to
  q = residue or p.pop,

where residue = str(p).strip('0123456789-[], '). The assignment is followed
immediately by c([0], q(), v=1), and ACCEPT is printed only after that call.

When residue is empty, both assignments evaluate and retain exactly p.pop.
If accessing p.pop fails, both programs reject at that access. Otherwise
they execute identical checking code with identical data. When residue is
nonempty, the old q is False and the new q is a nonempty string. Neither is
callable, so the next q() rejects in both programs before any checking or
ACCEPT output. Any failure while evaluating str or strip is shared.

This dichotomy covers all JSON-decoded values, not merely well-typed proof
certificates. Exception type/messages can differ; accepted finite inputs
and successful proof traces do not. The standard definition of acceptance
is normal exit with exactly ACCEPT followed by one newline.

2. ABSORBING-ORBIT PREDECESSOR

The reviewed term is

  P(m) = I(m,0;u.C(I(SSu,E(m);v.H(v)),u,Su)),
  H(v) = C(N(v),R(v),0).

The proof that P(0)=0 and P(Sm)=m is valid in the stated equational scalar
calculus. In particular:

* The ramp invariant M(R(n))=N(n) follows by equation induction and the
  two-point principle for parity values. Endpoint calculations require no
  Boolean disjunction or even-number decomposition.
* The triple-ramp identity R(3n)+N(n)=3R(n) uses addition rearrangement and
  N(n)+Z(N(n))=1. It uses neither subtraction nor cancellation.
* H(3u)=3H(u) follows by selector distribution and absorption:
  C(b,a+b,0)=C(b,a,0) for b=N(u). Both endpoint calculations are valid.
* Orbit rotation and tripling are ordinary literal-equation inductions.
  The comparator Lambda(x,y)=Z(O(Sy,E(x))) satisfies all three displayed
  recurrence equations. The extra successor in P's SSu counter is required
  and correctly accounts for Lambda(Sm,Su)=Lambda(m,u).
* The saturation proof first proves W(St,Sm)=S W(t,m) with m fixed, and only
  then applies a separate diagonal induction. It never changes a passive
  parameter in an active induction hypothesis.
* Pointwise equality of the two update terms is lifted by the explicitly
  supplied equation induction V(t,m)=W(t,m). Iterator-body extensionality
  is not silently assumed.

No use of order, excluded middle, successor injectivity, pairing, equality
reflection, or primitive recursion beyond the scalar iterator was found.
The elementary addition and parity identities are predecessor-free. They
can be supplied directly by equation induction: first prove Z^2(N(n))=N(n),
then the two-point principle gives N(n)+Z(N(n))=1; double and triple parity
follow by unfolding two and three successor steps respectively. Expanding
this short dependency paragraph would make the report more self-contained.
