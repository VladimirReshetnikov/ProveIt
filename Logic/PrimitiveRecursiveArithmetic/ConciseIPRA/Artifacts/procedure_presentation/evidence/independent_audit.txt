Independent proof audit notes
============================

Baseline reviewed: ConciseIPRA at dd308d5d938b20dce41a1150fa5db49fc53c5d4e.

1. The empirical coordinate envelope is not a proved universal lower bound.
   At the pinned incoming corpus the compact coordinates include 241 tokens,
   262 AST nodes and 469 normalized bytes, attained by different modules.

2. Guarded public formulas matter. Root-selected Close uses progress on a
   literal equation and cases on a compound matrix. Its unrestricted auxiliary
   D-over-equation reading is progressive, not the ordinary conditional
   D-reading. On guarded matrices the zero equation is provable and equation
   induction identifies the two readings. The public translation and both
   formula round trips must accompany any inherited bi-interpretation claim.

3. Distinguish source restrictions. The integrated PGPrf iterator-source
   calculus fails raw variable transitivity; the current shared-leaf Use
   permits every nonliteral-zero source and is not subject to that particular
   obstruction. Do not transfer the PGPrf counterexample indiscriminately.

4. In the eager compact decoder, successful no-image traversal is literal.
   Even a zero multiplier first validates its image. This permits the direct
   computation-leaf proof to use the validated goal as its scope witness.
   The lazy decoder has only a semantic-retraction statement.

5. Direct computation previews need the full resource lemma. In the successor
   case, if the update uses its state, evaluating the RHS independently checks
   the recursive source at term-root depth; this prevents capture by the extra
   dummy slots under the left successor. If the update does not use its state,
   both sides have the same constant-update value. Merely showing that a
   preview becomes true after replacing a new parameter by zero is inadequate.

6. Offset composition: moving formula depth 0 to 2 and term depth 3 to 5
   keeps sort parity and all child increments. Canonical protected/hole
   spellings >=3 move up by 2; numerical zero remains 0; negative rigid names
   remain unchanged. Neutral formula zero becomes admissible at the root.
   This does not give a universal raw certificate-language bijection: extra
   scalar aliases and inert annotations can behave differently. Correctness
   is semantic soundness plus canonical proof compilation, as offset472 states.

7. Exact return-value composition theorem. Suppose L is the one-record
   list-comprehension lambda checker and F is its def/statement version.
   Every successful L-call returns a singleton list containing a truthy value;
   every successful F-call returns None. Preserve all expression evaluation
   order, and replace each recursive-major occurrence `L(...) and m*3` by
   `F(...) or m*3`. Then successful major calls produce the same subsequent
   value m*3. Leaf branches and all subsequent openings agree literally.
   Under ideal finite execution, induction on successful call trees proves the same record
   consumption, same scopes, same intermediate formulas and same acceptance.
   Logical exceptions reject in both. Concrete CPython resource limits are
   excluded: removing comprehension frames changes recursion-depth overhead.
   This is an operational implementation reduction,
   not an axiom deletion or a new interpretation theorem.

8. Any global mutable-context variant must restore the exact caller context
   after each major proof. A leaf call cannot pop a caller-owned live
   assumption unless its own frame has first been pushed. Monotone freshness
   alone does not imply scope/context restoration. For synthesized conclusions,
   validate the exported formula at the scope saved on entry, not merely at
   the decreased floor after a descendant allocated a fresh witness.

9. Recent mathematical proposals already present in incoming:
   fixed_points_and_checker: even fixed-point preservation plus commuting
   iteration and canonical successor iteration need not give equation
   induction; an ultrapower countermodel is supplied. orbit_equality: a
   44-node Boolean equality indicator (45 with zero true) and a shorter proof
   of the existing 45-node predecessor. root_conversion: a seven-rule
   counter-aware recursor calculus with variable-source substitution and
   reversible conversion at the root of an equation endpoint.

10. Scalar root-only conversion was investigated but not proved complete.
    A Q-selection with two equation rows encodes E->F, not E and F. The
    usual conjunction macro uses parity; N(1) becomes a selector with an
    unreduced iterator in its counter, so root-only contraction does not
    evaluate it. The counter-aware proposal's three-row packet avoids
    this difficulty by exposing the previous counter. This is a failure of
    a proof route, not a nonexistence theorem.
