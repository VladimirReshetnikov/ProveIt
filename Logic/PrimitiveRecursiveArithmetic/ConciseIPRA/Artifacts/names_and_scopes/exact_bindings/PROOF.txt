A bit selector for actual numerical binding
==========================================

Claim boundary
--------------
This is a small implementation refinement of the exact-binding presentation
already delivered in ipra_exact_bindings_2026-11-13.zip. It does not delete an
axiom or change the native mathematical theory. Its bi-interpretation with
full first-order iPRA, and its proof-checker soundness and canonical
completeness, are inherited subject to the local preservation argument below.
It does not beat the existing compact checker frontier. A measured corpus
minimum is not a mathematical lower bound.

Definitions and the inherited invariant
---------------------------------------
The native terms are x,0,S(t),I(n,a;u.h); its public formulas are equations
and D_{x,y} B, with D read as exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y)). The
auxiliary Q binder is read as B(0) -> forall y B(y).

In the exact-binding codec zero is 1, S(t) is [t], I(n,a;u.h) is [n,a,h],
an equation is [s,t], and D B is [B]. Q B is internally [B,B,B]. A bound
slot j, counted from the innermost genuine numerical binder, has code 3+2j;
negative integers are rigid parameters.

At a formula position below p genuine numerical binders, d=2p; at a term
position, d=3+2p. Let r=len(a) and let j be a child's index starting at 0.
The inherited child depth is

  delta(d,r,j) = d + (2*[j=2] if d is odd, else 5-r).

Thus S has child depth d; I has child depths d,d,d+2. At a formula D the
child depth is d+4, at equality d+3, and at Q d+2. All genuine binders are
therefore counted, and no administrative dummy slot occurs.

The auxiliary raw reading uses only arity 1 and 3 as numerical constructors:
any numerical array of any other arity reads as zero, and its children are
annotations. At a formula position arities 1,2,3 read as D, equality, Q;
Q uses its last child, and every other array arity reads as truth. Formula
scalars read as truth. The checker may nevertheless traverse annotations
and reject them. That operational fact does not give annotations any
mathematical meaning.

The local change
----------------
Replace 2*[j=2] by j & 2, the bitwise AND with 2. Thus

  delta_bit(d,r,j) = d + (j & 2 if d is odd, else 5-r).

For j=0,1,2 one has j & 2 = 0,0,2 = 2*[j=2]. These are exactly all
children of a meaningful numerical node: S has only j=0, and I has j=0,1,2.
At formula nodes the child-depth rule is unchanged for every arity and index.
For an array of another numerical arity the whole reading is zero, so no
child is meaningful. Although its annotation traversals can now behave
differently, a successful traversal preserves the zero reading.

Theorem 1 (public grammar and canonical opening)
------------------------------------------------
The initial traversal accepts exactly the same canonical closed Eq/D goals
as the inherited exact-binding traversal, and returns the identical tree.
On canonical term and formula data, opening has the identical result,
including its success/failure and its treatment of every genuine binder.

Proof. The initial grammar permits formula arities 1 or 2, numerical arities
1 or 3, and properly scoped positive odd numerical leaves. At every permitted
constructor all child-depth increments coincide, by the three-index identity.
Scalar validation and substitution are unchanged. Simultaneous structural
induction on terms and formulas proves both assertions. No property of
annotations is needed because canonical data have no irrelevant arities.

Theorem 2 (soundness on all accepted auxiliary data)
----------------------------------------------------
Every successful opening with delta_bit has the inherited semantic opening,
exposure and freshness properties. Consequently each delivered checker is
sound on every integer/list input it accepts, including noncanonical proof
annotations; completeness on canonical native goals is inherited.

Proof. Repeat the inherited simultaneous structural induction on source
trees and missing-image validation. Scalar cases are unchanged. At a
meaningful constructor every child receiving a meaningful reading has the
same sort and depth as before. At any other numerical arity the output keeps
that arity and hence still reads as zero, regardless of the changed child
traversals. A successful annotation traversal can impose extra checks but
cannot change the node's zero reading. Formula arities and their depths
were not changed at all. This proves semantic substitution at all meaningful
positions and preservation of the reading by a successful no-image scan.

Exposure continues to hold because delta_bit(d+2,r,j)=delta_bit(d,r,j)+2:
adding two preserves parity. Closedness uses the same bound cutoffs at
meaningful leaves, so the floor-decrease freshness argument is unchanged.
Computation leaves contain only meaningful S/I/equality constructors and
therefore retain exactly their inherited arithmetic readings.

The proof control is textually unchanged in the three basic profiles. Its
successful-call induction uses precisely semantic opening, exposure,
freshness, and the computation leaves; all survive. Canonical compilation
uses only the meaningful constructor arities, so Theorem 1 preserves every
compiled certificate and thus proves completeness. This argument establishes
the same public theorem set, not identical behavior on every malformed raw
certificate. No monotonicity of raw acceptance is claimed.

Tuple selector
---------------
The byte-oriented companion uses (5-r,j&2)[d%2] in place of the conditional.
Depth d and index j are integers, and d%2 is always 0 or 1, including at
negative depths in annotations. Both branch expressions are defined integer
operations once the array traversal is active. Eagerly computing both cannot
raise a new exception or change state. Thus this replacement is an exact
operational identity, on arbitrary raw arrays, not just canonical ones.

Expression-input composition
-----------------------------
proper_expression.py further composes the already delivered restricted
expression reader from ipra_nontraditional_inputs_2026-11-13.zip. The allowed
characters are digits, minus, brackets, comma, and ordinary ASCII whitespace.
Any other character makes q a nonempty string and the first call fails.
For allowed characters, Python expressions can only construct integers and
lists, negate/subtract integers, index the resulting lists, or form comma
tuples at the expression root or as a multidimensional subscript. A root
tuple has no pop, and a tuple subscript is rejected by a list. Parentheses
are unavailable, so a tuple cannot be nested as an element of a list. Any
ill-typed operation rejects. There are no identifier, string, attribute, call,
comprehension, multiplication or assignment characters. Thus this reader
cannot execute supplied executable code beyond those data expressions. A
successful root must have pop and therefore be a list, whose elements are
exact integers or recursively such lists. This is the inherited reader
argument, not a new evaluation device.

Every ordinary canonical JSON certificate uses this alphabet and evaluates
to the same list under the restricted expression reader. Hence canonical
completeness is preserved. Soundness follows from the all-integer/list
soundness established above. The expression profile is a different input
contract and must be labeled separately from the whole-JSON profiles.
