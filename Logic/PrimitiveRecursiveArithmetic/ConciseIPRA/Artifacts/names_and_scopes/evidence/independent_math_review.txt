## Audit of the proposed joint alias boundary

Reviewed `review/math_notes.md` against the existing incoming
`equational_transport/proofs/theorem_fragment.tex` and article Chapter 6,
`disch:thm:alias-rank` and `disch:thm:alias-atomic`.

No fatal mathematical issue was found in the stated joint-restriction result.
The finite growing-domain evaluation is deliberately not an ordinary FO Kripke
model: numerical terms can denote values outside the active quantifier domain.
That is legitimate for a proof-calculus invariant because every restricted rule
was separately checked. Equation Alias moves to a larger world containing its
defining value, then pulls back an equation whose truth is world independent.
This argument fails for compound naming conclusions exactly as required.

The positive Name-Alias repair is noncircular. Its premise Name(b) follows
directly from Close at the newest b and constant-body successor computation.
Atomic specialization is available before the rank induction via Equation Alias;
that gives persistent extraction b=t from an opened naming clause. In the
compound transport construction, the local source derivation is consumed as
the major of Open at its original scope before any new scope is introduced.
Only persistent families are used in later extensions. This avoids illicit
weakening of deterministic newest-parameter derivations.

The rank construction should explicitly invoke symmetry when transporting
B(t,c,0) back to B(s,c,0), and explicitly define a persistent clause realizer:
at every extension and every term r over that extension, it maps a persistent
proof of the antecedent (if any) to a persistent proof of the body at r.

Novelty must be bounded. The T_r/S_r/L_r rank method and naming-clause extraction
already appear in Chapter 6 for arbitrary Close witnesses. The new contribution
is the joint restriction, the obstruction, and the fact that one fixed rank-one
export shape restores completeness with deterministic Close. The positive proof
adapts the old rank method and the newer diagonal-zero-row argument.

## Further optional strengthening: finite naming-template repairs

This is an independently developed proof outline, not a tested checker result.

Fix finitely many raw numerical term templates t_1,...,t_m. Add to K0 the schemes
Name(t_j(a_1,...,a_r)), where the a_i are scope parameters (allowing repetitions),
not arbitrary compound terms. Even including successor among these templates,
the resulting calculus fails some open naming theorem. Arbitrary term
instantiation is an essential exclusion: allowing it for the identity template
would already give the full naming scheme.

Choose a monotone primitive-recursive unary f with f(x)>x and f(x)>t_j(vec a)
whenever every argument in vec a is at most x. This can be built using bounded
maxima of the finite collection, with an x+1 summand. Work in a nonstandard model
M of PA expanded by its primitive-recursive functions, choose a nonstandard
c, and define the external initial cut

    I = { a in M : a < f^k(c) for some standard natural k }.

This cut contains c, 0, and every standard numeral; it is successor closed and
closed under every t_j. Let H(n,x)=f iterated n times from x, a primitive-recursive
function represented by the raw iterator term I(n,x;u.f(u)). Since c exceeds
every standard k and f is strictly increasing along its iterates,

    d = H(c,c)

lies outside I.

Use the two forcing worlds with active domains I and M, ordered by inclusion,
while all arithmetic terms are evaluated in M. Formulas and clauses have the
same active-domain forcing as in the finite obstruction. They are monotone.
Computation, equality, Back, Use, Open and cases Close are sound as before.
Equation Alias can always move to the M world and pull back the exported equation.

For progress Close, only literal-equation progress is asserted. At a fixed
world with active target q, every M-element k<q is active by initiality. The
sound step premise therefore gives, in M, the bounded statement
forall k<q (E(k)->E(k+1)). PA's induction in M yields E(q) from E(0). This is
valid even when q is nonstandard. Unlike the finite model, no claim is made
for progress on arbitrary compound formulas, whose forcing may not be definable
inside M.

Each added Name(t_j(params)) is forced because its numerical value lies in the
current active domain. Its selector row is constantly true at that witness;
PA proves C(y,a,a)=a for every y, including nonstandard y. But with the scope
parameter taking value c, Name(H(c,c)) fails at I: any available witness a in I
is different from d, and the y=1 row forces a=d. The ordinary iPRA realization
of this open naming theorem is provable. Thus finitely many fixed numerical
name operations do not supply the full name scheme under these restrictions.

This is not a lower bound for arbitrary theories or encodings. It is a
calculus-specific obstruction to replacing full term naming by finitely many
parameter-instantiated term templates.


Root review: both arguments were checked against their exact rule restrictions; the finite-template statement additionally received a separate mathematical review.
