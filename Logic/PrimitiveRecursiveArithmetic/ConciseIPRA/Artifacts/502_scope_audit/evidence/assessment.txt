Independent mathematical assessment
===================================

Scope: ProveIt commit b1b3ae103314a9668b9f87c76594ecfcc5c177e6,
including the supplied smaller_envelope and context_phase packages.

1. No mathematical lower bound is supplied by the coordinate minima.
   They are minima over a finite collection of concrete Python programs.
   A new program below them is a measured implementation improvement,
   not a proof of a globally simplest axiomatization.

2. The compact public contract is a canonical guarded Eq/D goal.
   It does not validate that complete grammar. Full-input-checking profiles
   must be compared separately. Ordinary D semantics remains sound for
   auxiliary unguarded formulas; completeness and spelling interchangeability
   are only claimed for the designated public grammar and proof compilation.

3. The source-zero switch performs genuine work. Nonzero source selects
   the equality premise [s,u]; zero selects [t,t,t], a conditional closure.
   Admissibility of transport at zero does not identify those two majors.
   Removing the switch while retaining only binary equality would remove QE.
   Encoding a closure as [B,B] would conflate it with reflexive equality under
   the existing sorted decoder. A new interpretation and completeness proof
   would be needed.

4. Replacing the exported closure m*3 by one instance loses reusable
   specialization under a single witness. In D elimination the assumption
   is B(b,0) -> forall y B(b,y), not B(b,t) unconditionally. A direct supply
   of B(b,t) is unsound unless its zero premise is established; permitting
   only one instance does not straightforwardly simulate arbitrary uses of
   the same opened witness. The existing clause-context calculi solve this
   dependency correctly but have larger implementations.

5. The local scope floor equals 1-len(g) at every entry of the 506-byte
   compact checker. It nevertheless changes within an activation: goals
   and major premises are checked at the old floor, then continuation
   goal and hypothesis are opened at the new floor. The old/new distinction
   enforces eigenvariable freshness. A fixed floor computed from len(g)
   cannot replace the decrement. The safe substitution of the parameter
   by k=1-len(g), retaining the decrement, measures 514 normalized bytes,
   267 significant tokens, 308 AST nodes in this environment: no improvement.
   The superficially smaller contextfloor_open.py and exact.py prototypes
   are INVALID and are research scratch only. Do not distribute them.

6. The inferred-major transformation in smaller_envelope has a clean
   proof: repair each consumed packet's major to the value actually used
   after the endpoint test. The repaired old run checks that same major,
   then makes the same continuation. This is successful-trace simulation,
   not equality of all malformed-input acceptance languages. Canonical
   completeness uses that numerical templates have no binary equality
   node, whereas every canonical formula has one, so nonelimination
   packets cannot accidentally trigger endpoint inference.

7. Future candidate review should inspect: argument evaluation order;
   walrus updates visible inside the opener closure; goals and majors
   checked before eigenparameter admission; context mutation through major
   recursion; whether an invalid raw input newly succeeds; and whether a
   certificate transformation preserves the public goal and interpretation.

No new 20-token structural reduction was established by this independent
investigation. The candidate has to earn its improvement by a measured,
specified transformation; neither classicalizing Q nor dropping the
zero premise is a permissible shortcut.
