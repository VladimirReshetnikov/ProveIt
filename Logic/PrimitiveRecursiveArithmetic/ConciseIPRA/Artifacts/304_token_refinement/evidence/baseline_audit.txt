Remove genuine docstrings/comments, reconstruct by `ast.unparse`, append a final LF, then count significant tokens, AST nodes, UTF-8 bytes. Gzip-9 is informational. ## Established mathematical baseline

The native scalar kernel has terms `x | 0 | S(t) | I(n,a;u.h)` and formulas equality, `Q_y B := B(0) -> forall y B(y)`, and `D_xy B := exists x Q_y B`. The Lean kernel uses hypothesis, two iterator equations, one crossed term-transport rule, Q-elimination, D-elimination, case D-introduction, and literal-equation progress Q-introduction. It is fully bi-interpretable with first-order iPRA, with whole domains, actual equality, identity numerical comparison graphs, both formula round trips.

Many apparent simplifications already exist: root-selected or diagonal-selected introduction; transport with one template or only nonzero/iterator sources; completely template-free replacement; equality fused with computation; source dispatch; direct closure; normalized terms eliminating computation rules; uniqueness/constancy variants; naming packets; inferred/deterministic witnesses; deterministic newest-parameter introduction; clauses replacing Q; one formula body per context entry; equality fused with existential discharge.

There are already **three-family** clausal systems, Use/Intro/Name or Use/Intro/Open or Use/Intro/Discharge. Their small rule count does not beat the compact checker envelope because normalization, context management, parsing and scope validation have costs. Raw homogeneous three-family H3-rho is already integrated (`homogeneous_discharge`) and explicitly does not beat the global frontier. It has 1097/1223/3427 versus its matched four-family control 1138/1262/3547.

All current compact record programs implement variants of the inherited native scalar core; their gains are implementation refinements, not new mathematics.

## Pitfalls and rejected routes

- A progress introduction at arbitrary compound formulas permits full formula induction; restriction to literal equations or a provably equivalent guard is essential.
- Replacing global entry validation by hypothesis membership admits a documented false-goal attack. Freshness must be checked before a witness or major is exported; a floor derived from remaining certificate length can allow capture.
- Dropping opener parity accepts false public goals. The apparent 460-byte record is explicitly `REJECTED_UNSOUND_shared_no_parity.py`.
- In the raw native calculus, variable-only substitution without a reflexivity source fails even to derive `0=0`. Full normalization changes that situation; raw and normalized cases cannot be interchanged without proof.
- Numerical coverage plus scalar constancy does not recover equation induction, even with several fusion/fixed-point/extensionality laws (`coverage_constancy`, `coverage_obstruction`).
- Restricting both DI witnesses and QE targets to one finite menu is incomplete. Literal zero alone or variables alone are insufficient for DI; zero plus variables is formalized sufficient. Deterministic newest parameter is already in packages, but weakening/substitution become delicate.
- A finite compositional primitive-recursive signature cannot term-represent every unary PR function; this does not obstruct relational languages or binder-based encodings.
- Compression wrappers around existing checkers already produce artificial 18-token/19-AST/459-byte figures and are explicitly rejected as changing accounting rather than reducing mathematics.
- Scope decorations or extra reserved scalar aliases may be sound but incomplete for claimed canonical public spellings; do not confuse soundness on raw codes with completeness on an enlarged public grammar.
