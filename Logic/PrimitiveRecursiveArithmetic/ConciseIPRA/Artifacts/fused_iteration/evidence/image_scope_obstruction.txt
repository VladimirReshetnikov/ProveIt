IMAGE VALIDATION CANNOT BE DROPPED, EVEN AFTER FIXING INTRODUCTION WITNESSES

Experiment: replace the opener's O_k(t; missing, 3) with the raw image t.
This reduces the 479-byte checker to 471 bytes. Restricting its introduction
witness to the current newest parameter blocks the already-known future-
witness-capture fixture, but does not repair the following independent flaw.

Define C(z,a,b)=I(z,a;u.b), and
  B(y)=D_{x,z}(C(z,0,x)=C(z,0,S y)).
Under the established ordinary reading, B(y) is equivalent to
  exists x (x=S y),
because the zero instance of the matrix is 0=0 and every successor instance
is x=S y. Consequently both B(0) and Q_y B(y) are iPRA theorems.

The canonical raw template for B(y), with y exposed as the hole, is
  t = [[[5,0,7],[5,0,[13]]]].
The unvalidated image u=11 refers, after insertion at the occurrence of y,
to the inner existential witness x. The modified opener therefore computes
  O'_0(t;11,0) = [[[5,0,7],[5,0,[11]]]],
which reads as
  D_{x,z}(C(z,0,x)=C(z,0,S x)),
and is equivalent to exists x (x=S x). This is false in the standard natural
numbers. Meanwhile O'_0(t;0,0) is B(0).

The unchecked QE step thus maps the two true premises Q_y B(y), B(0)
to the false conclusion exists x (x=S x). The original opener rejects u=11,
since an image must be closed at root term depth 3, so 11 is out of scope.
The numbers 11 and 13 account for the two slots under D, the iterator state
slot, and the two dummy slots under S in the repository's raw-tree codec.

This is a local rule counterexample. It does not require compiling the true
premises into the speculative newest-witness checker: if that checker is
complete, it can derive the premises and is unsound; if it cannot derive
them, it is already incomplete. Therefore the 471-byte experiment cannot
meet both requested conditions, irrespective of its witness restriction.

This argument concerns deletion of image validation only. It establishes
no universal lower bound on all proof checkers and leaves open replacing the
raw representation by one in which every inserted image is closed by
construction. The speculative files newest.py and newest_tokens.py are
experiments, not certified iPRA checkers.
