4. Exclusions and distinct contracts

Compact assumes finite exact-integer/list certificate data and a canonical
closed guarded Eq/D public goal. It checks proof steps and scope, but does
not validate arbitrary JSON or the complete public grammar. Native-goal
validation allows all native Eq/D public matrices and validates that goal,
but promises the proof alphabet and may ignore unused malformed prefixes.
Strict validates whole parsed input and public grammar. Do not mix profiles.

A 465-byte incoming source is named REJECTED_UNSOUND_no_parity.py and is
accompanied by a canonical false-goal acceptance; it is not an incumbent.
Integrated packed execution wrappers measure 18 tokens / 19 AST / 459 bytes
but hide compressed Python and are explicitly a different source-accounting
class. Measuring helper/translator modules, restricted proof-of-one-goal
drivers, or compressed payload strings as complete direct checkers is invalid.

All physical Python processes have resource ceilings. Completeness means
finite ideal execution with no fixed bound; it does not mean CPython's
fixed recursion limit accepts every finite mathematical certificate.

5. Already-existing mathematical ideas; do not resubmit as new

- Shared computation/hypothesis terminal lookup, every call consuming a
  harmless packet; all-open-sequent height-preserving equivalence.
- Direct computation leaves replacing computation-supply continuation.
- Tail recursion replacing compact continuation loops.
- Eager any-based Intro mode selection and its canonical completeness proof.
- Constant-one expression leaf / O+ recoding with 581-byte native and
  608-byte strict companions.
- Goal abstraction AQE reconstructing a closure matrix, with crossed
  replacement; no smaller checker.
- Conversion limited to a literal equation's left endpoint along a counter
  spine of depth at most three; primitive-recursive certificates, but larger
  checker than weak-head conversion. Minimal depth not proved.
- Vacuous closure Export with zero arbitrary numerical rule arguments,
  retaining Use, Close, Export plus reflexivity clause; normalized terms
  and formulas still carry numerical data. Checker1830 bytes, no compact win.
- Newest-variable-only Use through deterministic Export; no compact win.
- Diagonal partial evaluation-discharge preserving substitution modes;
  three-family checker2087 bytes, larger than E3's1892 bytes.
- Successor rigidity SR0 together with Boolean equality split; existing
  synthesis, no smaller checker.
- A45-node expanded predecessor compiled from scalar iteration; prior work
  with equation induction, no claim of unrestricted predecessor minimality.
- Canonical recursors, full/zero/weak-head normalization, iterator successor
  seeds, counter-aware registers, no constant, fixed witnesses, crossed
  equality and three-family clausal variants are integrated prior work.

6. Important correctness hazards

- Initial old-scope validation cannot simply be dropped, even for a closed
  goal. A caller can reinterpret an improperly substituted raw major.
- The source-selected elimination and literal shape-selected Introduction
  are not automatically stable under arbitrary substitution. State the
  ordinary semantics or prove a repaired translation.
- Progressive reading of D on auxiliary unguarded equations differs from
  ordinary conditional closure. It agrees only on guarded public syntax.
- The compact proof certificate protocol alone does not prove strict
  first-order bi-interpretability. Use the complete ordinary realization
  and both formula round trips through the inherited bridge.
- Finite regression checks support, but cannot prove, universal soundness,
  completeness, consistency, or interpretation equivalence.

7. Remaining mathematically meaningful targets

The most promising genuine simplification would implement a smaller trusted
logical/arithmetic basis (e.g. a new composition of the existing three-family
calculus with a cheap normalizer) below the direct-source envelope. The
article highlights unknown closed-theorem completeness of raw terms with
variable-only equality substitution, and simplification of the predecessor,
formula pairing and formula selection bridge proofs. None is solved by a
one-byte Python expression rewrite. The strong task remains open unless a
new theory/checker and both interpretations are actually established.
