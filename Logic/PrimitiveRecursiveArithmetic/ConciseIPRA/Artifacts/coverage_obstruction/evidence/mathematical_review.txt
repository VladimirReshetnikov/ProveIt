# Independent audit of the finite-word-fiber countermodel

Audited draft: the countermodel proof now in sections/coverage.tex.
The three presentation repairs below were implemented in the delivered text.

## Verdict

I found no fatal mathematical defect. The construction appears to give a
classical model of SC-minus plus coverage, contextual constancy, fixed
points, seed shift, term extensionality, and contextual fusion, with
noninjective successor and enough involutions to obstruct ordinary
finite-dimensional bi-interpretations with iPRA. This is a handwritten
mathematical assessment, not a proof-assistant verification.

## Checks of the main risks

1. For each fixed finite suffix-closed support D, the coordinate maps of
   every fixed term are primitive recursive. Iterator bodies never create
   colors outside D: zero and delta return the empty color, successor
   takes a suffix, and iteration stays in the support. Nested iteration
   therefore creates no uniform-evaluator issue.

2. Coherence under inclusion D -> E follows first on standard inputs by
   structural induction and finite iteration, and then transfers as a
   separate first-order identity for each fixed term and fixed pair of
   supports. A convenient presentation uses one fixed global coding of
   finite words, so the inclusions do not require relabeling color codes.
   Arbitrary finite codings also work, with explicit finite relabeling.

3. The level-bound lemma is sufficient for closure of the EXTERNAL domain.
   If there is any colored input, its minimum colored level is nonstandard;
   any colored output has at least this level. If all input colors are
   empty, the uniform bound holds for every internal b, so a colored
   output is impossible by taking b above its level. Thus internally
   nonstandard iterations cannot leave M through a low-level colored point.

4. Constancy does not assume external induction along a nonstandard chain.
   It uses host induction only for the fixed primitive-recursive coordinate
   maps of f on the canonical copy of N*. Every remaining colored point
   reaches that copy in |w| externally finite successor steps. Arbitrary
   formula contexts are handled by fixing a satisfying assignment before
   applying this semantic implication; they are never transferred into N*.

5. Extensionality/fusion are more delicate than unconditional identities,
   because their premises may hold on M but not on the larger A_D*. The
   proposed orbit argument correctly handles this: every internally
   indexed orbit state h^j(a) is in M by the closure lemma. Consequently the
   premise gives a first-order statement for ALL internal j, and host
   induction proves the orbit comparison. This avoids assuming global
   extensionality on the illegal low-level colored points of A_D*.

6. The color permutation pi_v commutes with deletion of the LEFTMOST bit.
   Its bit flip is the bit immediately preceding the fixed suffix v; the
   boundary case bv -> v is important and is correct. Finite-support
   equivariance then transfers to all internal levels.

7. The automorphism obstruction includes quotient interpretations. Fix
   source parameters, return-map parameters, and finite representatives of
   interpreted-side parameters. An automorphism acting trivially on the
   first interpreted quotient acts trivially on the double interpretation.
   Definability of the return isomorphism forces it to fix the source
   pointwise. Finite-dimensionality makes the collected parameter tuple
   finite. The nonidentity involution fixing this tuple contradicts the
   absence of finite-order nonidentity automorphisms of a linearly ordered
   classical iPRA model.

## Small presentation repairs recommended

- In the automorphism proof choose D explicitly to be the suffix closure of
  C union pi_v(C), where C is the finite set of input and parameter colors.
  This D is pi_v-invariant because pi_v commutes with taking a suffix.
  Merely taking an arbitrary support that contains the listed colors and
  their images would not guarantee invariance of any extra members.

- State once that the ordinary substitution/composition identities for
  each fixed term also hold in the finite-support interpretations by
  standard structural induction and elementarity. This makes the
  equivalence between the binding syntax and the staged first-order
  symbols completely explicit.

- Display the additional laws with their side conditions, or cite their
  exact definitions, so that 'fusion' cannot be read as a stronger rule
  quantifying over arbitrary external functions. The proof establishes
  the stated schemes for fixed term maps with finite passive parameters.

## Limits that should remain explicit

The result does not show that T is inconsistent, does not exclude
equiconsistency with iPRA, does not prove a universal checker-size lower
bound, and does not address nonstandard interpretation notions involving
infinite parameter sets or non-first-order domains. It rules out the
specified SC-minus-plus-coverage repair. It can be presented as a negative
answer to the repository's question disch:q:sc(1), not as the smaller
axiomatization originally requested.
