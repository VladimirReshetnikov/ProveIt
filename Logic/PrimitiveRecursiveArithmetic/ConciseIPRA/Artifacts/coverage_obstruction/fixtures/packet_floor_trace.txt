A SEVEN-PACKET COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THE UNVALIDATED PACKET-COUNT FLOOR

q := I(0,0;u.0), encoded [0,0,0]. Thus q=0.
E := q=b, encoded [[0,0,0],-3], with b=-3.
D E := [E]; Q E := [E,E,E].
The public root goal is q=S0, encoded [[0,0,0],[0]], and is false.

N counts the whole initial stack, including the root goal. The goal is popped
first, so the floor is -1 immediately before the root proof call. Each call
pops a packet before its scope checks.

Proof packets in CONSUMPTION order:
  1. [0,[0],E]
  2. [[E],0,0]
  3. [0,0,0]
  4. [0,0,0]
  5. [0,0,0]
  6. [0,0,0]
  7. [0,0,0]
The delivered JSON file reverses these packets and appends the public goal,
as required by the whole-JSON stack interface.

CALL TRACE
1. Floor -2, goal q=S0, context {truth}. The first packet selects
   backward Q-specialization: b=-3 is out of scope and therefore a hole in
   E. Opening E at S0 gives the public goal; opening E at0 gives q=0.
   The inferred major is Q E. The pending minor is q=0.
2. Floor -3, goal Q E, context {truth}. The same b=-3 is now retained as a
   rigid numerical parameter. The second packet selects D-elimination with
   major D E. Its promised continuation is Q E.
3. Floor -4, goal D E, context {truth}. The third packet selects D-introduction
   at witness0. E has no meaningful witness/index occurrence, so its progress
   premise and conclusion are both the unchanged equation q=b.
4. Floor -5, goal the neutral truth0. It closes by context membership.
5. Floor -6, goal E under the newly added E. It closes by context membership.
   D E has now been proved.
6. Floor -7, goal Q E under the clause Q E created by opening D E. It closes
   by context membership. The root's inferred major Q E is now proved.
7. Floor -8, goal q=0. It is exactly the zero-computation leaf from[0,0,0].
   The root returns normally and prints ACCEPT.

ROOT CAUSE
The template's scope changes between its use to infer a specialization and
validation of its inferred major. A negative out-of-scope scalar serves as
a substitution hole at the root, but becomes a fixed parameter in the major.
The proved major is a closure of a constant equation, not the closure of the
variable equation whose instances the root uses.

The fact that the later D-elimination eigenparameter is fresh does not repair
this earlier mismatch. The provisional freshness audit missed this semantic
drift. Thus its soundness conclusion must be withdrawn.

REPRODUCED RESULTS
REJECTED_UNSOUND_packet_floor.py accepts the attack both under ordinary Python3.12.14 and under -O.

rejects both ordinarily and under -O.

MINIMAL REPAIR OBLIGATION
The inferred major must be validated at the OLD floor, before its proof call
consumes a packet. Replacing the major call c(g,s) by c(g,i(s)) evaluates i(s)
in the old scope, then checks the resulting major after the packet is consumed.
Keep the original s in the mode selector and the prepared hypothesis; do not
silently substitute a normalized s everywhere, since that can change whether
a scalar source is false-valued.

The old-scope semantic-opening lemma then shows that i(s) has the old reading
of s, closed at the old floor. Future scope extensions preserve that reading.
The selected eigenparameter at the major return is strictly below that old
floor, because every major consumes a packet. This restores both the ordinary
freshness argument and stability of the inferred major across the proof call.

LOCAL_REPAIR_NOT_CERTIFIED.py implements this repair. It rejects the counterexample
and accepts zero computation, successor computation, and a constant-equation
D-introduction, each ordinarily and under -O. These focused checks establish
the local repair behavior, not a complete proof of checker soundness.
The source has262 tokens,291 AST nodes,563 normalized bytes; no size record.
