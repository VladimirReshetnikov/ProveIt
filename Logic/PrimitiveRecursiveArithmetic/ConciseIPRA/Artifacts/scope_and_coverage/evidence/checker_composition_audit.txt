Compared against

  and Boolean-dispatch checker variants in ipra_uniform_zero.

Conclusion
  I found no new soundness defect in the composition. The source changes
  preserve the inherited initialized-iterator / Choice-DI calculus,
  subject to the attributed sorted-decoder and semantic-opening lemmas.
  This is an independent review of the composition, not a formal proof
  of the complete Python program or of those inherited metatheorems.

1. Lexical scope restoration
  Each c invocation owns its integer k and its closure i. The major call
  c(m,g,k) receives the old floor, but assignments to k in that callee
  cannot modify its caller's floor or opener closure. Every context
  extension uses g + [entry], not mutation of g. Thus neither newly
  allocated names nor local hypotheses of a major proof can leak into
  its caller. The parent next decrements its own floor by one. Reusing
  the corresponding names in disjoint completed scopes is legitimate.
  The mutable-looking default g=[[]] is not mutated anywhere.

2. The unguarded major matcher
  The inherited minimal-successful-trace lemma remains valid for every
  starting floor: no numeric scalar can be successfully proved from
  list-valued assumptions. Local versus global floors do not enter that
  induction argument. Both selected profiles recognize only lists as
  containers; failed JSON strings, dictionaries and null cannot create
  alternative successfully checked scalar majors. Consequently every
  successfully checked major at a reached continuation is a list.
  The only false-valued one is []. It cannot equal the False alternative
  produced by `not s and [t]*3` when s is true. The formerly dangerous
  accidental numerical-zero match therefore remains unreachable.

3. Chained Boolean computation dispatch
  On a reached successful continuation, a nonempty list major has a
  nonempty m*3. Otherwise the major is []. A true-valued u constructs a
  two-element list equation, which is nonempty regardless of its contents.
  Only when m=[] and u is false-valued is the final assignment/opening
  branch evaluated. Thus the `or` expression has exactly the conditional
  dispatch's result and side effects on all potentially accepting traces.
  The early result does not conceal a validation obligation: construction
  of the selected arithmetic equation retains the same evaluations as
  the conditional predecessor; the final opener still traverses its
  complete selected result.

4. Goal substitution and a false-valued replacement
  The endpoint condition, the check i(t,u)==a, and the source opening
  are inherited unchanged. A numeric replacement cannot match a list
  goal through i(t,u). A replacement [] can occur only from an empty
  template, which would also require target goal []; such a goal already
  closes against the initial [] assumption before a command is read.
  More generally, falling back to the old goal when a replacement is
  false-valued does not license any inference; it retains the obligation
  to prove the old goal. No new acceptance is produced by this `or`.

5. The introduction mode is u
  Introduction is reached only with m=[] and false-valued u. No such
  JSON value equals numeric 1, so an odd-arity compound matrix cannot
  receive a progress hypothesis. For a literal equation (arity 2), a
  zero-like numeric u selects progress; u=[] selects its zero instance.
  A scalar opened matrix cannot survive the subsequent len(a). The
  unrestricted s field is not consulted as the progress mode. This
  distinction is necessary and is implemented correctly.

6. Generated arithmetic and zero-instance freshness
  The original goal is opened before fresh allocation. A matched major
  and matched target are likewise checked at the old floor; all supplied
  assumptions already belong to that retained scope. Moving from k to
  k-1 can admit only finitely many new raw names present in the finite
  packet, including fractional numeric names in that interval. These
  names are fresh for the decoded retained goal and context under the
  inherited opening lemma.
  The generated arithmetic assumption need not be uniformly true at
  its fresh names. Its simultaneous zero completion is the inherited
  I0/IS computation instance. Substitute zero for the new finite family
  and cut that computation proof into the continuation. This is the
  correct discharge argument even when a noncanonical initializer makes
  the mode expression select a fresh opening. Lexical floor reuse does
  not alter the zero-instance proof or freshness premise.

7. Scope of the conclusion
  The audit uses the original zero codec, not the `a or 1` normalization
  in the primary uniform-zero variant. It does not claim that arbitrary
  raw global-scope certificates have identical operational outcomes
  after lexicalization: canonical certificates are alpha-renamed by
  their lexical producer. The explicit-list companion has the same
  decoded mathematical rules. The 529-byte result is a complete smaller
  implementation of an inherited theory, not a new arithmetic basis.

No additional tests were introduced by this review. The existing fixture
and mutation receipts were inspected as corroborating finite evidence;
the universal composition argument above does not infer soundness from
their pass count.
