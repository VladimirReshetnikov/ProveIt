THE PRECISE STRENGTH OF ITERATOR FUSION

Fusion rule (parameters and a fixed context Gamma permitted; x fresh):
  from  f(h(x))=k(f(x))
  infer f(I(n,a;u.h(u)))=I(n,f(a);u.k(u)).

1. Equation induction proves Fusion.
Under the commuting premise, induct on n in the displayed conclusion. The
base follows from zero computation. At a successor apply successor computation,
the commuting equation at I(n,a,h), the induction hypothesis, and congruence.

2. Fusion plus eta implies the existing scalar-uniqueness rule.
Assume f(S x)=k(f(x)). Apply Fusion with h=S and a=0:
  f(I(n,0;u.Su))=I(n,f(0);u.k(u)).
Using eta, I(n,0;u.Su)=n, gives
  f(n)=I(n,f(0);u.k(u)).
The repository's scalar_uniqueness bootstrap then supplies its written equivalence
with equation induction (chapter 2, theorem kern:thm:ur; the arbitrary-update rule
is UR, not the successor-only SU). The converse UR-to-induction is explicitly not
Lean-formalized (kern:rem:ur-sources). Its proof derives zero-equation induction,
then a predecessor, then subtraction and an accumulator, then equality reflection,
and finally full equation induction. Pairing is not used in the bootstrap.
This route is not a new smaller rule. Fusion has three
templates f,h,k and an arbitrary seed, whereas scalar uniqueness has two templates
f,k and the implicit seed f(0).

Thus, over ordinary intuitionistic first-order equality logic, both computation
schemas, coverage, and separation, adding Fusion plus eta yields exactly the same
derivable sequents as adding full literal-equation induction. The forward
translation follows from equation induction as in part 1 and the analogous
induction proving eta; the reverse translation factors through UR above. This is
a positive equivalence theorem using the repository's written UR theorem as a
dependency, not a newly formalized Lean result.

3. Fusion alone does NOT imply eta, even with numerical coverage, separation,
successor injectivity, and all standard-numeral-equation induction. Here is an
explicit countermodel (independently audited by /root/independent_logic).

Take a nonstandard model M of PA with definitions for all primitive recursive
functions. Externally let
  D = {(m,m): m is standard} union
      {(m,n): m and n are both nonstandard}.
Zero is (0,0); successor acts coordinatewise; actual equality is pair equality.
Projection p:D->M is the first coordinate and is surjective. The fiber over each
standard numeral k is the singleton (k,k). Coverage holds: every nonzero standard
diagonal pair has its diagonal predecessor, and every pair of nonstandard numbers
has its coordinatewise predecessor still in D. Injectivity and separation hold.

First define scalar terms as primitive recursive pair-valued functions on ALL of
M^2, under valuations with pairs. Variables, zero and successor have the expected
meanings. Interpret an iterator with counter n=(n1,n2), initializer a and body h
by internally iterating the pair-valued function h in M exactly n1 times. The
second coordinate of the counter is unused. Each fixed term defines such an
internal primitive recursive pair function, by external induction on syntax.

PROVENANCE LEMMA.
For each term t(x1,...,xr), there are primitive recursive functions sigma and d
of the FIRST coordinates alone, with sigma in {0,...,r} and d>=0, such that
its two output coordinates are
  (x_sigma,1 + d, x_sigma,2 + d),
where x_0=(0,0). These identities hold in M for arbitrary pair valuations.

Proof of lemma. A variable chooses its own index and offset zero; zero chooses
index 0 and offset zero; successor increases the offset by one. For an iterator,
use the lemma for its initializer and for its body, where the bound state is one
extra input pair. Track in an ordinary primitive recursion the first state value,
its provenance index, and its offset. If the body's selected source is its state,
keep the current index and add the body's offset. If it selects a passive input
or zero, reset the index and offset accordingly. All decisions and added offsets
depend on first coordinates alone. PA induction on this internal recursion proves
the invariant. Evaluate it at the first coordinate of the counter term. QED.

The provenance lemma proves closure of D under every term and under every
internally M-indexed iteration of a term body from a seed in D. If the source pair
is standard diagonal, adding the same d keeps it diagonal. If both source
coordinates are nonstandard, adding nonnegative d keeps both nonstandard. The
diagonal source 0 is covered by the first case. Thus the above operations restrict
to a genuine scalar-term structure on D.

Both iterator computation laws hold by the internal primitive recursion and the
fact that the first coordinate of S(n) is n1+1. The projection of a term is its
ordinary scalar interpretation on M. Therefore all standard-numeral equations
t=kbar reflect exactly along p, and their induction rules (with parameters and
arbitrary fixed contexts) hold by the same singleton-fiber argument as in
identity_barrier.txt. Identity iteration also holds, by PA induction for a pair
identity map.

FUSION IS VALID.
Fix parameters in D and a valuation satisfying Gamma and the commuting premise
for every x in D. Write a_j=h^j(a) for the internally M-indexed h-orbit. The
provenance lemma shows a_j in D for EVERY j in M. Hence f(h(a_j))=k(f(a_j)) for
every j in M. Although D is externally specified, this last assertion, after
substituting the internal orbit and the internal pair functions, is an ordinary
internal first-order statement with the fixed parameters, true in M. Internal
PA induction now proves f(a_j)=k^j(f(a)) for all j in M. Specialize to j=n1.
This is exactly Fusion on D. No induction over an externally defined predicate
D is used.

EXTENSIONALITY CHECK.
If two body terms give the same actual function on D, their internally M-indexed
orbits from any a in D agree: the first orbit stays in D, so the two updates agree
at every point of that orbit; the resulting internal universally quantified
statement and PA induction give equality of their iterates. Thus the iterator
value depends only on the actual update function restricted to D, despite being
initially described using a primitive recursive extension to M^2.

FAILURE OF ETA AND EQUATION INDUCTION.
Take a non-diagonal n=(c,d) in D. Then
  I(n,0;u.Su)=(c,c) != (c,d)=n.
Nevertheless the equation E(n): I(n,0;u.Su)=n has a base and a successor step
provable from the two computation equations and congruence. It is therefore an
explicit failed instance of full literal-equation induction.

Consequences. Fusion is sound under equation induction but, by itself, is a
strictly weaker replacement even when supplemented by all numeral-equation
induction. Adding eta restores the known scalar-uniqueness route. This rules out
claiming a simpler complete iPRA presentation from bare Fusion. The separate note
automorphism_barriers.txt strengthens this example to exclude bi-interpretability
with iPRA even with finite parameters and tuple or quotient domains. Neither
argument excludes every possible one-way interpretation of the weaker theory.
