AUTOMORPHISM OBSTRUCTIONS TO BI-INTERPRETABILITY

These strengthen the two preceding countermodel results. Both arguments were
independently checked in parallel mathematical reviews within this work.
This is not a claim of external peer review or formal mechanization.

A. GENERAL LEMMA

Let N be a classical structure with a parameter-free definable linear order
on its whole domain.
Every finite-order automorphism of N is the identity. Indeed, if sigma(a)>a then
iteration gives a strictly increasing finite cycle, impossible; the case
sigma(a)<a is analogous. This remains true when the order is defined using
parameters fixed by the automorphism.

If structures D and N are bi-interpretable, a definable interpretation induces
an action of Aut(D) on N, and the action is faithful. To see faithfulness, an
automorphism acting trivially on N acts trivially on the interpretation back in
N; the definable isomorphism from D to that composite interpretation commutes
with automorphisms, so the original automorphism must be trivial. For a
bi-interpretation with parameters A, use Aut(D/A). This reasoning works for
finite-dimensional interpretations and definable quotient domains as well:
an automorphism acts on tuples and then on equivalence classes, and definability
of the composite isomorphism still gives faithfulness.

Every classical model of iPRA has the usual definable linear order, for example
x<=y iff there exists z with x+z=y. Its linear-order laws are provable in iPRA.
Consequently a structure whose automorphism group has a nontrivial finite-order
element cannot be parameter-free bi-interpretable with a model of iPRA. If such
an element exists in Aut(D/A) for every finite A, no finite-parameter
bi-interpretation is possible either. Applying a hypothetical intuitionistic
bi-interpretation to classical models is legitimate: its intuitionistically
proved translation and round-trip identities are also classically valid.

B. THE FUSION MODEL EXCLUDES EVEN FINITE-PARAMETER BI-INTERPRETABILITY

Use the model D from fusion_barrier.txt: standard diagonal pairs together with
all pairs of nonstandard elements of a nonstandard PA model M, with iteration
using the first coordinate of its counter. Fix any finite parameter tuple A in D.

Choose distinct positive standard integers r,s such that no parameter (a,b) in A
satisfies b=a+r or b=a+s. Only finitely many positive standard differences are
excluded, so this is possible. On all of M^2 define the primitive recursive map
  tau(m,n) = (m,m+s), if n=m+r;
           = (m,m+r), if n=m+s;
           = (m,n), otherwise.

The two tests are disjoint. The map is an involution, fixes the first coordinate,
fixes every diagonal pair, fixes A, and commutes with common nonnegative addition:
  tau(m+d,n+d)=tau(m,n)+(d,d).
Cancellation in M justifies preservation of the two tests under adding d.
It preserves D: a standard diagonal pair is fixed, and a swapped pair in D has
nonstandard first coordinate, so both its old and new second coordinates are
nonstandard. It is nontrivial on D, since it swaps (m,m+r) and (m,m+s) for any
nonstandard m.

For every scalar term t, its provenance representation is either a diagonal
(d,d), or an input pair x_i plus a common offset (d,d), with i and d depending
only on first coordinates. Applying tau to all inputs leaves i and d unchanged.
Since tau fixes diagonals and commutes with common addition, it follows that
  t(tau(x_1),...,tau(x_k))=tau(t(x_1,...,x_k)).
Thus tau is an automorphism of the full scalar term structure, including every
staged iterator operation, not merely of its zero/successor reduct. It is a
nontrivial involution in Aut(D/A).

By the general lemma, D is not bi-interpretable with any model of iPRA, even with
any finite parameters and even allowing tuple or quotient interpretation domains.
Therefore the theory consisting of the weak scalar arithmetic axioms, Fusion,
and all standard-numeral-equation induction is NOT bi-interpretable with iPRA
in that sense. Adding identity iteration does not change this conclusion since
the same model already satisfies it. This is stronger than merely observing
that eta or equation induction is not a theorem. It does not say that this
weaker theory has no one-way interpretation of arithmetic.

C. AN EQUIVARIANT IDENTITY+ETA COVER EXCLUDES PARAMETER-FREE BI-INTERPRETABILITY

Use the two-fiber set D and projection pi from identity_barrier.txt. Define tau
to fix every standard numeral and to swap (m,0) with (m,1) at every nonstandard
m. Then tau commutes with successor, fixes zero, and satisfies pi o tau=pi.
The class H of actual projection-preserving update functions is stable under
conjugation h -> tau h tau, with the same projected function f on M.

The iterator paths can be chosen tau-equivariantly. Write a path on a
nonstandard successor chain C as a function b:C->D satisfying
  b(S n)=h(b(n))
and the required projection identity. The involution acts on path problems by
  (h,a,C) -> (tau h tau, tau(a), tau(C)).
Its action on these triples is FREE: every nonstandard chain has a fixed label,
and tau(C) has the opposite label, hence is a different chain. If b solves the
problem (h,a,C), define its partner on tau(C) by
  b'(tau(n))=tau(b(n)).
This solves the conjugate problem. The projection is unchanged and conjugation
gives the recurrence. Thus one may choose paths for one representative of each
two-element orbit and define the other by this equation.

The special prescriptions in identity_barrier.txt can be made equivariant first.
The identity paths b(n)=a are equivariant. The paths at successor body and
standard numeral seed k, b(n)=S^k(n), are equivariant. For a fixed nonstandard
b in M put a0=(b,0), a1=(b,1). At successor body, prescribe, for all nonstandard
counter chains, the following output labels:
  seed a0:   label 0;
  seed S a0: label 1;
  seed a1:   label 1;
  seed S a1: label 0.
The first coordinates remain the ordinary successor-iteration values in M.
The first two prescriptions are exactly those needed to refute the equation
E(n) in identity_barrier.txt. The last two are their tau-images. These four
seeds are distinct and nonstandard; they cannot conflict with standard seeds,
and successor is not the identity update. All four path prescriptions satisfy
the recurrence since successor preserves labels. Hence the prescribed subset
of problems is closed under the involution and its prescriptions agree with it.
Choose all remaining paths orbit by orbit as above.

Standard-counter iterations h^k(a) are automatically equivariant. Therefore
the entire chosen operation satisfies
  I(tau(n),tau(a),tau h tau)=tau(I(n,a,h)).
Structural induction on term syntax proves that tau is an automorphism of the
full staged scalar structure. It remains a nontrivial involution, fixes all
standard numerals, and preserves every axiom established in identity_barrier.txt:
both computations, coverage, separation, successor injectivity, all standard-
numeral-equation induction, identity iteration, all standard-seed numbering laws,
and extensionality in the actual update function.

The general lemma now excludes parameter-free bi-interpretability of that theory
with iPRA, including interpretations with tuple or quotient domains. The same
argument permits parameters that are standard numerals (or other elements fixed
by this automorphism). It does NOT prove the corresponding statement for arbitrary
finite nonstandard parameters: a nonstandard parameter is moved by the global
flip. No claim about those parameterized interpretations is made here.
