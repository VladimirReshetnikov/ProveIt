INDEPENDENT MATHEMATICAL REVIEW
===============================

Scope
-----
This review concerns two negative constructions for proposed weakenings of
the scalar-iterator presentation. It establishes no smaller complete proof
checker and no positive axiomatization satisfying the requested complexity
target. The first construction gives same-language nonderivability, and
its equivariant refinement in section 4 also excludes parameter-free
bi-interpretability. The second excludes ordinary first-order
bi-interpretability even with definable quotient domains and finitely many
arbitrary parameters.

Notation
--------
T0 has full intuitionistic first-order logic with actual equality, zero,
successor, scalar iterator terms I(n,a;u.h), both computation equations,
coverage, separation, and induction restricted to t(n,p)=0, with side
assumptions independent of the induction variable. Both models below also
satisfy successor injectivity and induction for t(n,p)=kbar for every fixed
standard numeral kbar. M is a nonstandard model of PA with its definitional
expansion by all primitive recursive descriptions. A classical model is a
sound model of the intuitionistic axioms and rules in question.

1. IDENTITY ITERATION AND ETA DO NOT RECOVER EQUATION INDUCTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

Use the two-fiber cover from the repository's ch09_ctr.tex, theorem
ctr:thm:zeroind. Its domain is

  D = {(m,0): m in M} union {(m,1): m nonstandard},

with coordinate projection pi and successor preserving the second label.
For every actual map h:D->D whose projection is a primitive recursive
map f:M->M with parameters, define I on standard counters by finite
iteration. For a fixed seed a and each nonstandard successor chain
n_j=(m_j,i), choose a compatible path in the finite nonempty fibers above
It_f(m_j,pi(a)). Compactness gives such a path. Make the following specific
choices before choosing all remaining paths:

  (a) For the actual identity map, use the constant path a, for every seed
      and every chain. Thus I(n,a;u.u)=a universally.

  (b) For the actual successor map and every standard seed kbar, use
      I(n,kbar;u.Su)=S^k n. In particular I(n,0;u.Su)=n universally.
      Standard finite iteration gives the same values at standard counters.

  (c) Fix a nonstandard b and a=(b,0). For the actual successor map with
      seed a, use label 0 at all nonstandard counters; for seed S(a), use
      label 1. The projected values are b+m_j and b+1+m_j. They are all
      nonstandard, so both labels are available. Successor preserves each
      chosen label, making both prescriptions compatible paths.

These prescriptions do not conflict. Identity and successor are distinct
actual functions; a and S(a) are distinct nonstandard seeds; neither is a
standard seed. Choosing by actual h, rather than by the syntax of a body,
ensures extensionality even when two different body descriptions denote the
same function. The unprescribed triples can be filled arbitrarily using the
finite-fiber path lemma. The choices for all actual functions are made
before interpreting nested terms, so this is not a circular recursion.

The repository's term-projection proof applies unchanged. Consequently a
term equals zero in D iff its projected primitive recursive value equals
zero in M. Surjectivity of pi transfers the base and universal step of
each zero-equation induction instance to M; PA induction and reflection
at the singleton zero fiber transfer its conclusion back. No projection
of an arbitrary side context is needed: fix a valuation satisfying that
context first. The same proof works for each standard numeral fiber.

Let

  E(n) := I(n,S(a);u.Su) = S(I(n,a;u.Su)).

Its base and its implication E(n)->E(Sn) follow just from computation and
equality congruence. It fails at every nonstandard counter: the left side
has label 1 and the right side label 0. Therefore T0 plus the identity
iterator law and eta still does not derive arbitrary equation induction.
The construction even validates all the displayed standard-seed equations.

Limits of the construction as so far specified: this does NOT by itself
show non-bi-interpretability of this first weakened theory, failure of
equiconsistency, or impossibility of interpretations on changed domains.
Section 4 supplies an additional equivariance condition that strengthens
the first point. The identity iterator law says that the designated
iteration of the identity preserves its seed; it is NOT the assertion that
every solution to an identity-step recurrence is globally constant.

2. THE FUSION COVER
-------------------

Let

  D = {(k,k): k is a standard numeral in M}
      union {(x,y): x and y are both nonstandard in M}.

Zero and successor are coordinatewise. Interpret a scalar iterator by the
internally defined PA iteration of its pair-valued body for n_1 steps,
where its counter is n=(n_1,n_2); the second counter coordinate is ignored.
Each fixed term has a primitive recursive interpretation on ALL of M^2,
before restricting its arguments to D.

The essential closure lemma should be proved internally on M^2. For each
term t(x_1,...,x_l), its value is either

  (d,d),  or  x_i+(d,d),

where d>=0 and the origin index i (including the diagonal-origin case)
are primitive recursive functions of the first input coordinates alone.
For an iterator body, track the origin and the accumulated common offset
as part of an internal finite state. A step either preserves the state
origin and adds an offset, resets to an external parameter origin and an
offset, or resets to a diagonal origin. Internal PA induction proves this
description for every internal iteration count, not only standard counts.
This makes every term operation, and every intermediate body iterate from
D, remain in D. Projection to the first coordinate is the ordinary scalar
iterator interpretation in M. Each standard numeral has a singleton fiber.

Coverage, separation, successor injectivity, and zero/numeral-equation
induction follow as in the first cover. The identity iterator law holds,
because an internal iterate of the identity is the identity for all counts
in M.

Full term fusion is valid. If f(h(x))=k(f(x)) holds for every x in D,
then, for a fixed a in D, EVERY internal h-iterate of a is in D. Therefore
the PA-definable equation

  f(h^j(a)) = k(f(h^j(a)))

holds for every j in M. This is a true internal universal statement in M,
even though membership in D is external. PA induction now proves

  f(I(n,a;u.h(u))) = I(n,f(a);u.k(u)).

The argument also validates the fusion rule under arbitrary side contexts:
fix the context valuation and use the universally fresh variable in the
premise. It is essential that the proof first establishes closure for all
internal iteration counts. An appeal only to ordinary external induction
would be insufficient here.

Eta fails: for n=(n_1,n_2) with distinct nonstandard coordinates,

  I(n,0;u.Su)=(n_1,n_1) != n.

Thus fusion and identity iteration do not restore equation induction in
this cover. More strongly, this cover obstructs bi-interpretability as
follows.

3. A FINITE-ORDER AUTOMORPHISM OBSTRUCTION TO BI-INTERPRETABILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------

Fix distinct positive standard naturals r,s. On all of M^2 define tau by

  tau(m,m+r)=(m,m+s),
  tau(m,m+s)=(m,m+r),
  tau(m,n)=(m,n) in all other cases.

The cases are disjoint in PA. This is an internally primitive recursive
involution. It preserves the first coordinate, fixes all diagonal pairs,
fixes zero, and commutes with coordinatewise successor and with every
common nonnegative translation. For the last assertion, cancellation in
PA gives n+d=m+d+r iff n=m+r, and similarly for s. It preserves D: standard
diagonal points are fixed, and on nonstandard pairs a switched coordinate
m+r or m+s stays nonstandard. It is nontrivial on D, for example on
(b,b+r) for any nonstandard b.

It commutes with every fixed scalar term operation, including parameters.
Prove this by structural induction on terms interpreted on all of M^2.
The only substantive case is the iterator. The inductive hypothesis gives

  h_{tau(p)}(tau(u)) = tau(h_p(u)).

Since tau is PA-definable, PA induction proves conjugacy of the internal
iterates for all internal counts. The number of steps agrees on both sides
because tau preserves the first coordinate of the counter. Thus

  t(tau(x_1),...,tau(x_l)) = tau(t(x_1,...,x_l)).

This proves tau is an automorphism of the full staged first-order scalar
language on D. It does not rely on assuming the fusion conclusion as a
meta-level property of arbitrary external functions.

It can fix any specified finite parameter tuple. Each tuple entry excludes
at most one positive standard value r for which its second coordinate is
its first coordinate plus r. Choose two distinct positive standard r,s
outside the finite set of excluded values. Then tau fixes every named
parameter, including any parameters used by both interpretations and both
comparison maps, while remaining a nontrivial involution.

Every classical model of iPRA carries its definable strict linear order.
A linear-order automorphism has no nontrivial finite order: if sigma(a)>a,
then successive applications give a strict increasing chain back to a,
and similarly when sigma(a)<a. In particular every involution is identity.

Suppose an ordinary first-order bi-interpretation between the fusion
theory and iPRA existed, possibly using definable subsets of finite powers,
definable equivalence relations, and finitely many parameters. Use the
fusion model D and choose tau fixing ALL those parameters. It induces an
automorphism on the interpreted arithmetic model, of order dividing two.
That automorphism must be identity by the definable linear order.

Now interpret the fusion theory back. An identity automorphism on the
arithmetic structure induces identity on every definable set and quotient
in that structure. The comparison isomorphism j from D to this double
interpretation is definable with fixed parameters, so it is equivariant:

  j(tau(a)) = j(a).

Injectivity of j forces tau(a)=a for all a, contradiction. This is the
faithfulness of the action supplied by a bi-interpretation; it remains
valid for quotient domains. For intuitionistic syntactic interpretations,
pass to classical models: every intuitionistically provable interpretation
and comparison law is still classically sound, so the same contradiction
applies.

Conclusion: the fusion weakening (T0 plus the full term-fusion rule, and
even additionally identity iteration and successor injectivity) is not
bi-interpretable with iPRA in this ordinary finitary first-order sense.
It may still be interpretable in useful directions and equiconsistent;
neither possibility is contradicted. The arbitrary-finite-parameter
version belongs to the fusion cover; section 4 gives a more restricted
automorphism conclusion for the identity-plus-eta cover.

4. EQUIVARIANT REFINEMENT OF THE FIRST COVER
--------------------------------------------

Define tau on the two-fiber cover to fix standard numeral points and flip
the label of every nonstandard point. It commutes with successor, preserves
pi, is an involution, and is nontrivial. The set H of actual projected
primitive recursive maps is closed under h -> tau o h o tau. This map has
the same projected function f as h.

For a nonstandard counter chain C, tau(C) is the distinct chain over the
same base chain with the opposite label. Thus the action

  (h,a,C) -> (tau o h o tau, tau(a), tau(C))

on path-choice triples is free. Choose one representative from each
two-element orbit, choose its compatible path, and define the other path
by applying tau. Applying tau to the recurrence for h produces precisely
the recurrence for tau o h o tau; projection is unchanged. This defines
the entire unprescribed iterator equivariantly.

The prescribed paths can also be made equivariant. Identity paths
constant at a already transform to paths constant at tau(a). The successor
paths from each standard seed are S^k n, which transform correctly since
tau commutes with successor. For the four distinct nonstandard seeds

  a0=(b,0), a1=(b,1), S(a0), S(a1),

assign successor-body path labels 0,1,1,0, respectively, on every
nonstandard counter chain. The first and third recover section 1's
counterexample, and the second and fourth are their tau-translates.
These families are pairwise disjoint and do not overlap the standard-seed
families or the identity-body families. No choice conflict remains.

The resulting semantic operation satisfies

  I(tau(n),tau(a),tau o h o tau) = tau(I(n,a,h)).

Standard counters satisfy the same equation by finite iteration. A
structural induction on term syntax therefore proves that tau is an
automorphism of the full staged scalar language. Since it is a nontrivial
involution, the linear-order and faithfulness argument of section 3 rules
out parameter-free bi-interpretability of the identity-plus-eta weakening
with iPRA, even on definable quotient domains. The same holds when all
parameters of the interpretations and comparison maps are standard
numeral values, which tau fixes.

Do NOT claim that this tau fixes arbitrary finite nonstandard parameter
tuples: it moves every nonstandard element. No arbitrary-parameter
extension of this first-cover obstruction has been established here.

Recommendations for the final report
------------------------------------
1. State the positive user target as unmet unless a smaller complete
   checker has independently been constructed and measured.
2. Keep the two countermodels separate; they impose different extra laws.
3. State the projection and provenance lemmas in full, and distinguish
   external compactness/path choices from internal PA iteration/induction.
4. Include the involution proof and explicitly collect all comparison-map
   parameters when applying its finite-parameter version.
5. Explain that no new Lean/Rocq formalization of these constructions has
   been compiled. Finite experiments are not proofs of these universal
   model-theoretic claims.

FINAL REPORT REVIEW
-------------------
Reviewed output/ipra_iteration_barriers_2026-11-12/report.tex against these
arguments and the pinned article's definitions and arbitrary-update
uniqueness theorem (kern:thm:ur). The two model constructions, body
extensionality argument, full fusion proof, automorphism constructions,
non-bi-interpretability applications, and identity-plus-eta parameter
qualifications are mathematically sound as written. The positive
fusion-plus-numbering equivalence is correctly identified as relying on
the inherited UR theorem, not as a new independently formalized bootstrap.
The report explicitly acknowledges that the requested smaller checker
has not been obtained.

Two precise wording corrections were requested and sent to the parent:

  (1) In the abstract faithfulness lemma, require the linear order on B
      to be definable WITHOUT PARAMETERS, or additionally require the
      automorphism to fix all order-defining parameters. Merely saying
      "definable" could include parameters, making that general lemma
      false for a finite pure set ordered with parameters. The application
      to iPRA is unaffected: its arithmetic order is parameter-free.

  (2) In the finite-parameter bi-interpretability contradiction, explicitly
      include finite tuples of representatives for parameters lying in
      interpreted quotient domains. Fixing those representatives fixes
      their equivalence classes. Together with interpretation parameters
      and comparison-map parameters, this gives the required finite tuple
      in the original fusion model.

The parent reports that these wording corrections are being applied.
No further mathematical changes were identified as necessary.
