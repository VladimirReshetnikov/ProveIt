ATTRIBUTION AND RESEARCH STATUS
==============================

The baseline corpus is VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt, initially frozen at
b9c4d74a0018bdafc5bc06e755a0145f4ed2b8d6 and rechecked after migration at
89f5bdfef815cd2b2c5422978896edc9c819af3f. Exact locations and identities are
recorded in evidence/snapshot.json, evidence/corpus_metrics.json and
evidence/latest_snapshot_recheck.json.

Inherited work, not claimed as new here:

- The complete Choice-DI native calculus and its ordinary realization.
- The reconstruction of ordinary intuitionistic logic and both formula
  translation round trips.
- The initialized-iterator predecessor, equality reflection, pairing,
  primitive-recursion compiler and full-domain bi-interpretation with iPRA.
- The neutral-major protocol, its raw-input proof, lexical fresh-name
  techniques, certified zero and tail-loop techniques in the earlier drafts.
- Frozen comparison checker files and the inherited test producer/support
  modules. Original comments and provenance notices are retained.

The unmodified mathematical sources are in reference/. Adapted, integrated
versions are in theory/; the report states their provenance. In particular,
the equality-reflection appendix explicitly restricts its prerequisite
function inventory to the already available scalar constructions, before
the general primitive-recursion compiler is established.

evidence/mathematical_provenance.json records original proof-source hashes.
evidence/baseline_provenance.json records selected comparison program hashes.
evidence/support_provenance.json identifies distributed support programs
whose bytes match source strings in the frozen corpus. The corpus retains
all original archive locations for each distinct source. The repository's
MIT-0 license is preserved in reference/LICENSE-ProveIt.txt.

New work in this deliverable:

- The protected self-seeded orbit, coverage without successor cancellation,
  no-fixed-point argument, and transient-marker proof family for unique
  predecessors of fixed standard numerals.
- The resulting two-exception predecessor simplification with injectivity
  retained.
- The explicit one-binder prenex-class equivalence and constructive
  higher-depth representability calculation, with their stated limits.
- The measured compositions of inherited certificate techniques, their
  written invariant analysis, and the fresh-variable allocation attack.
- The static comparison audit, final migration recheck, integrated report,
  independently reviewed proof details and expanded executable diagnostics.

Novelty is relative to the inspected supplied corpus. No exhaustive
literature-priority claim, proof of an absolute size lower bound, Lean/Rocq
verification, external peer review or injectivity-free complete foundation
is asserted. The complete theory A and the weaker research theory B in
AXIOMS.txt are deliberately distinguished throughout the report.
