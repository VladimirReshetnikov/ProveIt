2. Review of checker468.py

The candidate's 271 / 305 / 468 vector was independently remeasured.
It improves the matched control in all three coordinates, but improves only
the byte coordinate of the corpus envelope. Its mathematics is the existing
guarded scalar theory. The new source composes the existing shared-leaf
protocol with the existing offset codec.

The decoder proof requires all five source changes: formula depth 0 to 2,
root term depth 3 to 5 at both validation and computation opening, witness
opening depth 2 to 4, and initial neutral resource [[]] to [0]. The child
increment stays 5 minus arity. Thus zero has aliases 0 through 4, the slot
intervals move up by two, and the negative eigenparameters remain unchanged.
Depth parity is unchanged. The independent review found no gap in applying
the inherited sorted-opening and successful-call arguments to this source.

The claim must remain equality of represented canonical public theorem sets.
It is not equality of arbitrary raw certificate languages. The new zero
aliases and ignored annotations prevent an unconditional raw conjugacy.
Likewise, the small field-aware translate() routine is an untrusted producer
for its canonical fixture subset, not an arbitrary-certificate compiler.

3. Two targeted adversarial checks

boundary_audit.py reconstructs two exact false PUBLIC goals. Each is rejected
by checker468.py, but accepted after one designated safety-critical deletion.
Both results are reproduced in ordinary and optimized Python, for eight
isolated executions with zero failed expectations. The temporary unsound
controls are generated by the audit and are never used as delivered checkers.

PARITY (seven packets).
  A = D_(x,y) (I(y,0;u.0) = I(y,0;u.S(0))).
The x variable is unused. At y=0 the equation is true; at y=1 it is false.
Deleting d % 2 lets the scalar template 2 act as a formula identity while
still being a numerical zero alias. The proof follows the already-known
sort-parity attack, adapted to offset shared leaves. This is not a new
mathematical obstruction. Its prior mechanism and pre-offset certificate
are in incoming/ipra_whole_json_refinement_2026-11-12/negative/README.txt.

EARLY FLOOR (five packets).
  A = D_(x,y) (I(y,0;u.x) = I(y,0;u.y)).
The zero instance is true, but the universal instance would require x=1
and x=2. Thus A is false. Replacing k <= a < d by k-1 <= a < d admits the
not-yet-fresh parameter z before introduction. The illegal witness S(z)
makes the progressive step literally R=R for R=I(S(z),0;u.S(z)). That
reflexivity has a finite derivation from two zero-computation leaves and
one equality-transport instruction; no fake computational equality is used.
The resulting complete five-packet certificate is new to this audit, while
the future-eigenparameter-capture obstruction is inherited prior art.

In raw shifted coordinates, R=[[-1],0,[-1]], s=[0,R,0], major=[s,R], and
template=[5,R]. The stack is:
  [0,0,R,0],
  [0,0,R,0],
  [major,s,R,template],
  [0,0,0,0],
  [0,0,0,[-1]],
  [ [[[7,0,9],[7,0,7]]], 0 ].
The last row is the goal/floor header. The proof checker consumes backwards.
The candidate rejects at the intended boundary: S(z) is not a closed term
at the parent floor zero.
