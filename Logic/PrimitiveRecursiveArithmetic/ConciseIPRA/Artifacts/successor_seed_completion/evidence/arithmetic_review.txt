INDEPENDENT ASSISTANT REVIEW OF J_bootstrap.tex
=============================================

Reviewer role: separate reasoning agent in the same research session.
This is a written mathematical audit, not a proof-assistant formalization
or an external peer review.

Scope reviewed
--------------
The complete fragment J_bootstrap.tex, from the staged numerical grammar
through the whole-domain predecessor and initializer-recovery theorem.
The inherited scalar PR compiler and native logical return-translation
are separate obligations and are explicitly identified in that fragment.

Result
------
No mathematical gap was found in the reviewed arithmetic bootstrap.
The argument uses contextual induction only on single literal equations
and the numerical-cases rule. It does not assume successor injectivity,
a predecessor, ordinary unshifted addition, pairing, an equality
characteristic, or induction on quantified formulas before deriving them.

Specific checks
---------------
1. Shifted addition a oplus b has endpoints S(a) and S(a oplus b).
   Its displayed commutativity, associativity and successor shifts follow
   by separate equation inductions without cancellation of S.

2. The two finite cycles are well founded. N has range {0,1} by cases on
   Z's argument. The range of j is {0,1,2}; its cycle 0 -> 1 -> 2 -> 0
   gives M(Sn)=j(Mn) and M(S^3 n)=Mn. These range statements can be read
   as admissible finite case splits, without using the later ordinary
   logical connective macros.

3. The ramp invariant M(Gn)=S(Nn) uses one equation induction.
   At Nn=0, the update adds one successor and changes the residue 1 to 2.
   At Nn=1, it adds two successors and changes the residue 2 to 1.
   Thus G(S^2 n)=S^3 G(n). No claim that every arbitrary element has a
   pre-existing definable half is needed.

4. The affine terms are genuinely available before predecessor:
     D(a)=S^2(a oplus a),
     T(a)=S^4((a oplus a) oplus a),
     R(a)=S^3(a oplus a).
   Their standard meanings 2a+3, 3a+6 and 2a+4 are explanatory only.
   The equation G(Ta)=T(Ga) oplus Na has the correct base 10=10.
   Its induction step changes both sides by four successors when Na=0
   and five successors when Na=1.

5. The factor identities H(2)=2, H(Da)=Ta and H(Ta)=T(Ha) follow
   from the finite parity cases and the ramp scaling equation.

6. E(Sx)=D(E x), with E=S A, follows from shifted addition. The
   crucial identity F(Sx,Sy)=T(Fxy) is proved with x fixed by induction
   on y. Its base is H(D(E x))=T(E x); its step is H o T = T o H.
   This proves L(0,y)=1, L(Sx,0)=0 and L(Sx,Sy)=L(x,y) on the whole
   source domain, rather than only for standard numerals.

7. Conditional increment commutes with successor:
     K(c,Su)=S K(c,u).
   Hence B_(Sm)(Su)=S B_m(u). This establishes
     V(St,S^2 k)=S V(t,Sk)
   by induction on t with k fixed. The diagonal V(n,Sn)=Sn then uses
   substitution into that completed lemma. It does not change a passive
   parameter inside an active induction hypothesis.

8. The capped predecessor Q satisfies Q0=Q1=1 and Q(S^2 k)=Sk.
   The update comparison with V is first proved at an independent
   counter t with k fixed; t=S^2 k is substituted only afterward.

9. The finite correction
     P(m)=B(Z(L(3,m)),Q(Q(m)),L(2,m))
   gives P0=P1=0, P2=1 and P(S^3 k)=S^2 k. These equations yield
   P(Sn)=n by numerical cases. If desired, use two literal-equation
   inductions: first P(S^2 n)=Sn, then P(Sn)=n.

10. The initializer compiler
      I(n,a;v.h)=P(J(n,a;u.S(h[Pu/v])))
    has the correct base P(Sa)=a. At a successor the outside P strips
    a displayed S, leaving h applied to the previous compiled value.
    No positivity invariant about an arbitrary internal state is used.

Limits
------
This audit establishes no absolute lower bound on proof-checker size and
makes no claim of literature-wide novelty. It also does not certify an
implementation's raw JSON acceptance semantics. Those questions need
separate provenance, whole-program measurements, and implementation
validation. The native logical interpretation and the inherited general
primitive-recursion compiler must accompany the fragment to support the
full iPRA bi-interpretability claim.

ADDENDUM: PRE-SELECTOR CONTEXTUAL FINITE CASES
-------------------------------------------
A follow-up audit identified a dependency that the arithmetic fragment
must not leave implicit. The theorem form of numerical cases does not
immediately permit case assumptions b=0 or b=1 inside a retained
induction context. The completed proof supplies the required native
lemma in native_bridge_notes.tex:

* Functional finite-range elimination, with closed endpoint premises,
  is derived directly from numerical cases and computations.
* The three-cycle M/j proves successor cancellation on the finite
  values 0,1,2 without global successor injectivity.
* Each branch Gamma,b=r |- u=v is packaged by case introduction as
  D_(a,y)(B(y,r,u)=B(y,b,v)), with a unused. Functional range elimination
  yields the self-guard r=b. Its zero instance is reflexive, and Q
  elimination yields u=v.

This explicitly validates the finite splits in the ramp and affine
induction steps before predecessor and the unrestricted selector exist.
The full construction passes the dependency audit with this lemma
included. The arithmetic fragment should not be treated as self-contained
without that finite-case justification or an equivalent proof.
