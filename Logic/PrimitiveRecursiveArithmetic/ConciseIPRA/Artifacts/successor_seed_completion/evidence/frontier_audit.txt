Scoping investigation
---------------------
The arity increments (2,1,4) deliberately unify three sorted pairs:
  unary: Q / successor;
  binary: equation / checked numerical zero annotation;
  ternary: D with two binders / scalar iterator whose state has two aliases.
Giving the numerical iterator only one slot while retaining two for D breaks
the shared traversal and adds sort-specific code.
The eager opening i([*n,t,u],d=1) is essential twice: it forces a nonzero raw
counter to be unary and validates update/seed/predecessor scope even when the
update ignores its recursive image. Its deletion admits false equations.
The recursive image check inside the substitution helper prevents capture.
For example, a purely syntactic capture-permitting specialization could change
forall y exists x (x != y) into exists x (x != x). Goal matching alone cannot
replace numerical-root validation of arbitrary QE targets or DI witnesses.

A branch-local fresh floor removes mutable global state. The opener is nested
inside c(a,g,k=0), major recursive calls retain k, and continuation calls pass
k-1. It is sound after certificate reserialization because no eigenparameter
internal to a major subproof can occur in its checked outer end-sequent.

syntax, so it does not beat the complete current envelope. No universal
proof-certificate translator or full test suite is supplied for this exploratory
variant; it should not be presented as a validated deliverable.
