explicitly unresolved candidate in ipra_shared_continuation_2026-11-10.

the two-constructor candidate, or the conditional initializer compiler.

predecessor obligation. The new proof constructs the missing predecessor
from the candidate's own terms and proves its equations throughout the
whole source domain using only literal-equation induction and numerical
cases. Its key adaptations are the shifted factor-map/initializer alignment
and the capped-predecessor finite correction. No earlier completion of that
obligation was found in the inspected current/embedded text corpus.

This is an audit judgment supported by the recorded search and relevant
source inspection. Text matching cannot rule out every differently
presented equivalent construction, and this report makes no global
literature-priority assertion.
