All 13 direct ZIP files were traversed recursively, including ZIPs nested
inside prior reports. This yields 2,701 Python source occurrences and 370
distinct raw SHA-256 hashes. The audit only parses source and never executes
downloaded checker, build, or test code.

The measurement boundary is the entire source module. Imports, the JSON
parser call, scope traversal, arithmetic/logical branches, and verdict
driver are included. Genuine module/function/class docstrings are removed;
comments disappear through parsing. Normalization is ast.unparse followed
by a newline. AST nodes include the Module node and every descendant.
Significant tokens exclude encoding, comments, layout, newline, endmarker.
The source-token count additionally excludes genuine docstring tokens.

Environment: CPython 3.12.14, zlib 1.3.2.
Gzip is informational only: gzip.compress(bytes, compresslevel=9, mtime=0),
including the gzip wrapper, measured on both raw and normalized sources.

BOUNDARY CLASSIFICATION

All 31 distinct source files at or below at least one of the three current
normalized coordinate minima were read. Three are the complete checkers
above; 28 are build scripts, artifact hash verifiers, reproduction drivers,
untrusted certificate translators, or external-checker test harnesses.
Their exact hashes, source occurrences, and individual reasons are in
boundary_classification.json. This exhaustive boundary argument is enough
to certify the displayed observed minima without classifying the much
larger modules as complete/sound/theoretically equivalent. In particular,
one small test harness contains ACCEPT and JSON code but imports/runs an
external checker; string searches alone are not a correct classification.

The new sources under source_corpus and hash-named archive inventories are
counted by their actual bytes. Embedded historical files are not dismissed
merely because they are under reference/, ancillary/, or experiments/.
Experimental larger programs are measured but this audit does not certify
their soundness or full iPRA equivalence.

LIMITATIONS AND OPPORTUNITIES

The current mathematical basis and logic in all frontier programs remain
the inherited scalar-iteration Eq/Q/D Choice-DI presentation. Recent new
work often changes certificate syntax or Python encoding rather than the
arithmetic foundation. Claims of significant foundational novelty should
not be inferred from small source reductions.

The affine-arity report's 648-byte program still has the explicit
catch/print-REJECT/rethrow wrapper. Dropping it under the current exit-status
convention yields a smaller matched control; likewise for arity-calculus
profiles. These combinations are useful controls, but should be credited
as inherited representation/driver combinations rather than a fresh theory.

The corpus already contains a counterexample to restricting all major
premises to hypotheses. It also contains strong negative results for
tied-seed single-body recursors, pure equational algebraic presentations,
and term-based finite-dimensional quotient interpretations. Repeating those
restrictions without a different argument is not a new positive result.

This is an implementation-size/source-boundary audit. It is not a formal
soundness proof, a Lean/Rocq verification, or an independent verification
of every inherited bi-interpretation argument.
