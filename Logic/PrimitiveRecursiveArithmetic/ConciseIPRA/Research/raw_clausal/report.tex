\begin{abstract}
We give a complete proof presentation of full first-order intuitionistic
primitive recursive arithmetic using raw scalar-iterator terms, two formula
constructors, reusable conditional-universal clauses, and five primitive
inference patterns. The numerical domain is unchanged. Both ordinary
interpretation composites have internally definable isomorphism graph
$x=y$.

The simplification combines the repository's computational equality with
its recently submitted clause presentation. It removes canonical term
normalization and supplied equality templates from the five-rule interface.
The existing successor-seeded proposal also has five rules; being the first
five-rule presentation is not a claim of this report. Complete matched
Python checkers, proof translations, validation cases, and reproducible
source metrics accompany the construction.

The improvement is measured within the transparent, fully validated checker
family. The much smaller historical compact encodings are not surpassed.
No absolute minimality theorem or new proof-assistant verification is claimed.
A separate proved observation shows that uniqueness of zero-starting
successor trajectories already implies every zero-equation induction
instance; its missing full scalar bootstrap is identified explicitly.
\end{abstract}
