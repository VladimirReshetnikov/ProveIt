\section{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Can successor rigidity close the arithmetic bootstrap?}
The proved reset construction reduces every zero-equation induction
instance to SR$_0$. A faithful scalar equality test would complete the
reduction to full equation induction. Constructing it without importing
the general inductions being replaced is the substantive missing step.

\item \textbf{Can the raw clause interface improve the compact envelope?}
The measured benefit here concerns transparent complete checkers. A
comparison against the 516-byte profile must charge its public grammar,
certificate protocol, all typing checks, and any compilation layer under
one explicitly fixed contract. The present measurements do not resolve
that question.

\item \textbf{Can introduction be made stable under all substitutions
at equal or smaller total cost?}
The witness-directed rule tests whether the witnessing term is literally
zero. Ordinary mathematical consequences are substitution closed through
the equivalence proof, but a translated primitive instruction need not
remain the identical instruction. The repository's existing guards and
changed closure readings address related tradeoffs; a smaller complete
stable interface remains a useful target.

\item \textbf{Can the exact five-rule equivalence be formalized economically?}
The earlier repository contains Lean results for several component
reductions. A focused formalization should prove the clause-to-formula
translations, the Eval reconstruction of transport, the DI zero-witness
case, and both formula composites. A formal checker-refinement proof can
then follow the negative-level scope invariant used here.
\end{enumerate}

The immediate completed result is the raw five-pattern presentation with
its matched smaller implementation. The questions above identify further
mathematical or implementation work and are not premises of its
bi-interpretability theorem.
