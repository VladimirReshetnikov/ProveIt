\begin{abstract}
We specify an axiomatization of full first-order intuitionistic primitive
recursive arithmetic with two numerical computation schemas and a paired
logical binder. General assumption use and transport through compound
formulas are both derived. Primitive equality transport has only a literal
equation as its motive. The removal of compound transport is proved by
an explicit structural construction using case introduction, without
induction on compound formulas. Together with the inherited successor
recursor, this gives a theory with whole-domain, actual-equality
bi-interpretations with iPRA and effective consistency reductions.

The complete standalone implementation has 304 normalized Python tokens,
345 abstract-syntax-tree nodes, and 714 normalized bytes. An audit of all
ten current draft archives at the pinned ProveIt revision finds the
coordinate envelope 287/323/738, attained by different complete programs.
The new checker improves the byte coordinate by 24 bytes (3.25\%); it
does not improve the other two minima. The byte saving combines inherited
certificate and arithmetic techniques and must not be attributed to the
transport restriction alone.

This is a modest, reproducible advance. It does not establish an absolute
simplest axiomatization, a program below every coordinate of the current
frontier, or the significant mathematical breakthrough requested. All
axioms, rules, side conditions, interpretation composites, implementation
boundaries, and the distinction between written proofs and finite tests
are stated explicitly.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Attribution and the precise contribution}

The successor-seeded recursor, its direct primitive-recursion compiler,
the paired binder, choice between case and progress introduction,
elimination-only use of assumptions, and explicit endpoint packets are
already present in the supplied drafts\cite{recursor,elimination,endpoints}.
They are inherited here. The smaller byte implementation combines these
ingredients. No new numerical basis or priority claim is made for them.

The mathematical consolidation re-establishes that \emph{literal-equation
transport suffices even in the elimination-only Eq/\D\ object calculus}.
Both general assumption use and full compound transport can be restored
without a primitive compound induction instance. The proof separates
identity expansion from transport expansion, avoiding a circular use of
the rules being removed.

A historical cross-check is decisive for novelty. The older
\file{ipra_fixed_position_2026-11-09.zip}, retrieved at repository commit
\file{7e694b036f89a0f16313089329555da531d6a656}, already proves the same

atomic transport from still more restricted equality rules, at the cost
of changed first-step arithmetic computation. The no-hypothesis adaptation
here uses the already known identity expansion with the inherited R
computation substituted at its leaves. Accordingly this report claims
\emph{a consolidation and an implemented smaller byte checker, not a new
structural transport theorem}. That historical archive is not one of the

\section{Complete primitive theory}
\label{sec:theory}

\subsection{Numerical syntax and its two computation schemas}

Numerical terms are variables, zero, and
\[
                    R(n,a;u,v.h).
\]
Only the update $h$ binds $u,v$, which are distinct. Its remaining
parameters are fixed throughout that recursion. Define
\[
             S(a):=R(0,a;u,v.0),\qquad 1:=S0.
\]
Thus $S$ is a fixed abbreviation, not a further primitive numerical
operation. The only numerical axiom schemas, in any retained context, are
\begin{align}
 R(0,a;u,v.h)&=S(a), &&\tag{R0}\label{eq:R0}\\
 R(Sn,a;u,v.h)&=h[n/u,R(n,a;u,v.h)/v].
 &&\tag{RS}\label{eq:RS}
\end{align}
Substitution is simultaneous and capture-free; the recursive value in
RS retains the original update $h$.

Every body is a fixed finite expression. Equivalently, form a staged
first-order signature: for each earlier-stage term $h(u,v,\vec p)$,
declare a function symbol $r_h(n,a,\vec p)$ with these equations.
There is no quantified function variable, universal evaluation operation,
or claim of uniform primitive-recursive evaluation of all descriptions.
The stages are an external syntactic construction. Coherence under
substitution of descriptions will follow from equation-induction
uniqueness in Section~\ref{sec:interpretations}.

\subsection{Object formulas and available elimination majors}

The object language is
\[
                     A::=(s=t)\mid\D_{x,y}A.
\]
Both distinct variables $x,y$ are bound by $\D$. A retained context
$\Gamma$ is a finite reusable list of object assumptions and internal
clauses $\Q_yB(y)$, with $B$ an object formula. A clause supplies $B(t)$
when supplied a proof of $B(0)$. It is not itself an object-formula
constructor. Its ordinary meaning is $B(0)\to\forall yB(y)$.

The availability judgment has exactly these clauses:
\[
 \frac{}{\Gamma\av M}\quad(M\in\Gamma),\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A}{\Gamma\av A}\quad(A\text{ an object formula}).
 \tag{Avail}
\]
Availability may be used only as an elimination major below. In
particular, there is no primitive rule from $A\in\Gamma$ to
$\Gamma\vdash A$. Internal $\Q$ clauses are available by membership.
Their object representative is $\D_{w,y}B(y)$, for unused $w$.

\subsection{All primitive proof-building rules}

Besides R0 and RS, the entire inventory is
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\av s=t\qquad\Gamma\vdash U(s)=V(s)}
      {\Gamma\vdash U(t)=V(t)}\quad\mathsf{AT},
 \tag{Atomic transport}\label{rule:AT}
\]
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\av\Q_yB(y)\qquad\Gamma\vdash B(0)}
      {\Gamma\vdash B(t)}\quad\mathsf{QE},
 \tag{Closure use}\label{rule:QE}
\]
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\av\D_{x,y}B(x,y)\qquad
       \Gamma,\Q_yB(a,y)\vdash F}
      {\Gamma\vdash F}\quad\mathsf{DE},
 \tag{Paired elimination}\label{rule:DE}
\]
\[
 \frac{\Gamma,B(t,\eta_\varepsilon(z))\vdash B(t,Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash\D_{x,y}B(x,y)}\quad\mathsf{DI}_\varepsilon,
 \qquad \eta_0(z)=0,\quad\eta_1(z)=z.
 \tag{Paired introduction}\label{rule:DI}
\]
Case introduction ($\varepsilon=0$) is allowed for every object matrix.
Progress introduction ($\varepsilon=1$) is allowed only when the matrix
is a literal equation. An equation under a binder is not literal.
In DI, $z$ is fresh for $\Gamma$, $t$, and all fixed matrix parameters.
In DE, $a$ is fresh for $\Gamma$, $F$, and all fixed matrix parameters.
Local assumptions and clauses are discharged. Bound variables are
renamed before substitutions. AT's $U,V$ are fixed numerical templates;
its motive is never a compound formula.

Call this calculus $\Acal$. Native absurdity is designated as
$\bot_*=(0=1)$. There are no other logical or nonlogical axioms,
reflexivity rule, primitive hypothesis-conclusion rule, coverage axiom,
separation axiom, or extra induction schema. We derive the ordinary
intuitionistic logical interface below; it is not silently imported into
the checker.

\section{The transport reduction without circular reasoning}
\label{sec:atomic}

\begin{lemma}[Equation reflexivity and symmetry]
For each numerical term $s$, $\Gamma\vdash s=s$. An available $s=t$
gives a proof of $t=s$ using only atomic transport.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $r_s=R(Ss,0;u,v.u)$. RS gives $r_s=s$. Use two copies of this
computation, one as the available equality and one as the minor, with
atomic motive $w=s$. The result is $s=s$. This is a three-node proof.
For symmetry use available $s=t$, minor $s=s$, and motive $w=s$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Object identity expansion]
If the object formula $A$ belongs to $\Gamma$, then $\Gamma\vdash A$.
If $d(A)$ is its number of paired binders, an expansion uses
$4+3d(A)$ proof-building nodes, counting availability as an edge annotation.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For $A=(s=t)$, transport the three-node reflexivity proof of $s=s$
along the available $s=t$, with motive $s=w$. This uses four nodes.
For $A=\D_{x,y}B$, eliminate its available assumption at fresh $a$.
The new clause is $\Q_yB(a,y)$. Reintroduce $A$ by case DI at witness
$a$. Its retained base is $B(a,0)$, whose proof is supplied recursively
by the smaller identity expansion. QE at $Sz$ gives the required step
$B(a,Sz)$. Close DI and DE. This adds three nodes. No compound
transport is used at any stage.
\end{proof}

Weakening, exchange, contraction, renaming, and numerical substitution
are admissible by induction on derivations. For object cut, replace each
availability use of the cut assumption by its supplied proof, freshening
local parameters. A numerical substitution preserves a matrix's root
constructor, so the progress restriction survives these operations.
These structural arguments do not require arbitrary-formula transport.

\begin{theorem}[Compound transport from atomic transport]
\label{thm:atomic-transport}
For every fixed Eq/\D\ object template $F(w)$, the rule
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\av s=t\qquad\Gamma\vdash F(s)}
      {\Gamma\vdash F(t)}
\]
is admissible in $\Acal$. The construction uses no progress DI except
what may already occur in the supplied premise proofs.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct externally on the number of paired binders in $F$. The
equation case is AT. Suppose $F(w)=\D_{x,y}B(w,x,y)$, with binders
fresh for $s,t$ and $\Gamma$. Eliminate the given $F(s)$ at a fresh
parameter $a$ and retain
\[
                       \Q_yB(s,a,y).
\]
To introduce $F(t)$ choose the same witness $a$ and use case DI.
Its local assumption is $B(t,a,0)$; obtain a proof of it by the already
proved identity expansion. Symmetry supplies $t=s$, and the structural
induction hypothesis for the smaller object template gives $B(s,a,0)$.
QE on the retained clause at $Sz$ then gives $B(s,a,Sz)$. Apply the
structural induction hypothesis forwards along $s=t$ to obtain
$B(t,a,Sz)$, exactly the case-DI premise. Close DI and DE.

The parameter $z$ is fresh for the opened clause and $a$, and $a$ is
fresh for the conclusion. All recursion here is on fixed formula syntax,
not an object-theory induction on formulas. The only new DI instances
are case instances. The two recursive transports preserve all retained
assumptions. This completes the construction.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact object-sequent equivalence]
Replace AT by unrestricted object-formula transport and add general
object hypothesis use. The resulting reference calculus and $\Acal$
prove exactly the same Eq/\D\ object sequents in the same clause contexts.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Every $\Acal$ inference is valid in the reference calculus. In the other
direction replace hypothesis leaves by identity expansion and each
compound transport by Theorem~\ref{thm:atomic-transport}. Translate
finite derivations recursively. Proved majors remain available, and
context majors remain available by membership.
\end{proof}

The boundary is deliberate: it is not a theorem that every raw unary
$\Q$ object goal is preserved. A $\Q$ is an internal reusable clause;
its Eq/\D\ representative uses an unused existential coordinate.
Deleting general hypothesis use while continuing to assert raw-$\Q$
identity would be incorrect.

\section{The short numerical construction}
\label{sec:numerical}

The following direct compiler is inherited from the successor-recursion
draft\cite{recursor}; it is included to make the present construction
self-contained. Atomic equality reasoning already gives transitivity
and numerical congruence. Define
\[
 W(n):=R(n,0;u,v.u),\qquad
 C(n,a,b):=W(R(n,a;u,v.Sb)),\qquad
 P(n):=C(n,0,W(n)).
\]
Computation alone proves
\begin{align*}
 W0&=1,&W(Sn)&=n,\\
 C(0,a,b)&=a,&C(Sn,a,b)&=b,\\
 P0&=0,&P(Sn)&=n.
\end{align*}
The counter input $u$ of R is essential: it is not an alias for $v$.
Applying W proves that successor is injective.

Suppose fixed terms $g(\vec p)$ and $h(n,z,\vec p)$ are already
represented. Put
\[
 J_{g,h}(n,\vec p)=
 W\bigl(R(n,g(\vec p);u,v.S h(u,W(v),\vec p))\bigr).
 \tag{Compiler}\label{eq:compiler}
\]
Then
\[
 J_{g,h}(0,\vec p)=g(\vec p),\qquad
 J_{g,h}(Sn,\vec p)=h(n,J_{g,h}(n,\vec p),\vec p).
\]
At zero, R returns $Sg$ and W removes the successor. At a successor,
R returns $S h(n,J_{g,h}(n,\vec p),\vec p)$ and W again removes
the displayed successor. Both identities hold at arbitrary source
elements; there is no standard-numeral restriction or positivity premise.
Zero, successor, and projections are terms; composition is substitution.
External recursion on any fixed PR description therefore compiles it.
No scalar predecessor search, pairing construction, or equation induction
is needed for this compiler. Scalar iteration is its special case with
$h$ independent of its counter.

\section{Deriving the full intuitionistic logical interface}
\label{sec:logic}

This section adapts the inherited Choice-DI interface\cite{elimination,recursor}.
Every use of a retained object formula means identity expansion, and
every compound equality transport means Theorem~\ref{thm:atomic-transport}.
Thus none of the following reasoning adds a primitive checker rule.

\subsection{Cases, equation induction, and congruence of the binder}

With an unused coordinate $x$, progress DI introduces $\D_{x,y}E(y)$
from $\Gamma,E(z)\vdash E(Sz)$ for a literal equation E. DE opens
its clause $\Q_yE(y)$; a supplied base proof $E(0)$ and QE at t give
$E(t)$. Hence contextual equation induction is admissible:
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash E(0)\qquad\Gamma,E(z)\vdash E(Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash E(t)}. \tag{EI}\label{rule:EI}
\]
Case DI in the same construction, ignoring its base assumption in a
given successor proof, yields numerical cases for any object template:
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A(0)\qquad\Gamma\vdash A(Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash A(t)}. \tag{NC}\label{rule:NC}
\]
The usual freshness conditions are retained. Witness 1 may be used
for the unused coordinate. These derivations never need a proof whose
conclusion is an internal Q clause.

Uniform pointwise interderivability of matrices is preserved under D.
Eliminate the source D at a fresh witness, introduce the target D at
that witness by case DI, convert its base backwards, use the opened
source clause at $Sz$, and convert the result forwards. This is the
same constructive pattern as the transport theorem, now with arbitrary
uniform conversion derivations. Reverse the conversions for the converse.

\subsection{Numerical gates and formula selectors}

For a numerical control z define, externally on fixed formula syntax,
\[
 G_z(s=t):=(C(z,0,s)=C(z,0,t)),\qquad
 G_z(\D_{x,y}B):=\D_{x,y}G_zB.
\]
Freshen binders away from the control. Computation, reflexivity, and
binder congruence prove $\vdash G_0A$ and $G_{Sz}A\dashv\vdash A$.
The padding operation $\operatorname{Pad}(A)=\D_{u,y}G_yA$, with
unused u,y, is equivalent to A: case DI proves the forward direction;
DE, the true zero gate, and QE at 1 prove the reverse direction.

Define the selector on two equation leaves by
\[
 M_z(s=t,r=w):=(C(z,s,r)=C(z,t,w)).
\]
At aligned fresh binders extend it by
\begin{align*}
 M_z(\D_{x,y}A,\D_{x,y}B)&=\D_{x,y}M_z(A,B),\\
 M_z(E,\D_{x,y}B)&=\D_{x,y}M_z(G_yE,B),\\
 M_z(\D_{x,y}A,E)&=\D_{x,y}M_z(A,G_yE),
\end{align*}
where E is an equation. The maximum input binder depth decreases in
each recursive call. Padding and binder congruence give
\[
             M_0(A,B)\dashv\vdash A,\qquad
             M_{Sz}(A,B)\dashv\vdash B.
\]
These are finite syntactic operations, not object-language function symbols.

\subsection{Connectives and quantifiers}

Use the scalar iteration I compiled by \eqref{eq:compiler}, put
$Z(n)=C(n,1,0)$ and $p(n)=I(n,0;v.Z(v))$. The only parity facts
needed are the finite computations $p(1)=1$ and $p(2)=0$.
With fresh bound variables and unused u define
\begin{align*}
 A\Rightarrow_*B&=\D_{u,z}M_z(A,B),\\
 A\wedge_*B&=\D_{u,z}G_zM_{p(z)}(A,B),\\
 \exists_*x A(x)&=\D_{x,y}G_yA(x),\\
 A\vee_*B&=\exists_*x M_x(A,B),\\
 \forall_*x A(x)&=A(0)\wedge_*\D_{u,z}G_zA(z),\\
 \bot_*&=(0=1).
\end{align*}

Implication introduction converts its retained zero selector into A,
applies the supplied derivation of B, and converts B to the successor
selector; case DI closes. Elimination is DE followed by QE at 1.
Existential introduction uses its supplied witness and the successor
gate. Elimination opens D, supplies the theorem given by its zero gate,
and uses QE at 1. These are the usual eigenparameter conditions.

Disjunction introductions use control witnesses 0 and 1. For elimination,
turn the two branch derivations into $A\Rightarrow_*F$ and
$B\Rightarrow_*F$. NC gives $M_z(A,B)\Rightarrow_*F$ at any control;
eliminate the existential selector and apply the implication.
For conjunction introduction NC gives $M_v(A,B)$ at every v from the
two supplied proofs. Specialize to $p(Sz)$, add the successor gate,
and use case DI. Projections are QE at 1 and 2 after DE, using the two
displayed parity values.

NC now gives the coverage formula
\[
             (x=0)\vee_*\exists_*y\,(x=Sy).
\]
At zero use the left injection of reflexivity; at a successor use the
right injection with its evident witness. Coverage permits a case split
even if x occurs in retained assumptions: it adds an equality naming
the case, rather than illicitly substituting into the whole context.
Universal introduction obtains the zero and successor cases of a
fresh-parameter proof and forms the two conjuncts. Universal elimination
uses coverage, the first conjunct at zero, and the gated closure at a
successor. This avoids an eigenvariable assumption about the retained context.

Finally, under $0=1$, computation and atomic transport prove
\[
                   s=C(0,s,t)=C(1,s,t)=t
\]
for arbitrary s,t. Every equation follows; external recursion on object
syntax, using case DI at each D, proves every object formula. This
derives explosion for the designated absurdity. We have thus derived
the full intuitionistic natural-deduction rules, with actual equality,
without postulating excluded middle for quantified formulas.

\section{Full arithmetic and both interpretation composites}
\label{sec:interpretations}

\subsection{An explicit ordinary realization}

Let $T_R$ be ordinary intuitionistic first-order logic with equality
in the staged R signature, the equations R0 and RS, contextual
literal-equation induction EI, and
\[
 \forall x\,(x=0\vee\exists y\,x=Sy),\qquad (0=1)\to\bot.
 \tag{Boundary}
\]
For definiteness, ordinary logic here has hypothesis, implication
introduction and modus ponens, conjunction introduction and its two
projections, the two disjunction introductions and case elimination,
universal introduction and specialization, existential introduction and
fresh-witness elimination, and explosion. Equality has reflexivity and
substitution. The usual eigenvariable restrictions are imposed. These
ordinary rules specify the comparison theory, not additional primitives
of $\Acal$.

Realize its object and clause syntax by
\[
 (s=t)^\circ=(s=t),\quad
 (\D_{x,y}B)^\circ=\exists x(B^\circ(x,0)\to\forall yB^\circ(x,y)),
\]
\[
                 (\Q_yB)^\circ=B^\circ(0)\to\forall yB^\circ(y).
\]
The interpretation domain is $x=x$ and equality is actual equality.
Case DI is sound by coverage. Progress DI is sound by equation
induction under its temporarily assumed base. All other native rules
have their direct ordinary meanings. Conversely Section~\ref{sec:logic}
derives the ordinary rules, coverage, and EI. Separation translates to
the identity implication $(0=1)\Rightarrow_*(0=1)$.

The explicit falsity bridge matters. The positive numerical equations
alone admit a singleton structure, so declaring them an ordinary
first-order theory while silently omitting separation would be a mistake.

\subsection{Actual equality reflection}

For clarity, the remaining arithmetic bridge is given internally,
not merely by agreement on standard numerals. Every fixed PR description
is already compiled by Section~\ref{sec:numerical}. In particular use
\[
 x+0=x,\quad x+Sy=S(x+y),\qquad
 d(x,0)=x,\quad d(x,Sy)=P(d(x,y)).
\]
Let $\operatorname{sg}(x)=C(x,0,1)$. Coverage and the computation
equations show that sg takes only 0,1, that
$\operatorname{sg}(x)=0\leftrightarrow x=0$, and $C(x,a,a)=a$.
Applying sg and separation gives $Sx\ne0$. Coverage and W give
$W(x)=0\leftrightarrow x=1$.

Literal-equation induction proves, in order,
\[
 0+y=y,\qquad (Sx)+y=S(x+y),\qquad
 d(Sx,Sy)=d(x,y),\qquad d(Sa,a)=1.
\]
For the first two induct on y with x fixed. For the third induct on y:
the base is $P(Sx)=x$, and its step applies P to the induction equation.
For the fourth induct on a and use the third identity in the step.

One more equation induction, on y with x fixed, gives
\[
        C(\operatorname{sg}(d(x,y)),x,y+d(x,y))=x. \tag{Residual}
\]
The base is $C(\operatorname{sg}(x),x,x)=x$. In the step set
$r=d(x,y)$ and $r'=Pr=d(x,Sy)$. Coverage splits $r'$ into zero
or successor. At zero the selector already returns x. In the successor
case, $r'\ne0$ forces r to be a successor, and $P(Sq)=q$ gives
$r=Sr'$. The induction equation says $y+r=x$. The new second branch
is $(Sy)+r'=y+Sr'=y+r=x$. This completes the induction without
using a decidable actual-equality predicate in its premises.

Consequently $d(Sa,b)=1\leftrightarrow a=b$: forwards, Residual yields
$b+1=Sa$, hence $Sb=Sa$, and W cancels the successors; backwards
use $d(Sa,a)=1$. Thus
\[
                 e(a,b)=\operatorname{sg}(W(d(Sa,b)))
\]
is Boolean and satisfies $e(a,b)=0\leftrightarrow a=b$ for arbitrary
elements of every source model.

For a fixed quantifier-free formula A, define its zero-true
characteristic by
\begin{align*}
 b_{s=t}&=e(s,t),&b_\bot&=1,\\
 b_{A\wedge B}&=C(b_A,b_B,1),&
 b_{A\vee B}&=C(b_A,0,b_B),&
 b_{A\to B}&=C(b_A,b_B,0).
\end{align*}
An external induction on A, using the proved Boolean ranges, gives
$b_A\in\{0,1\}$ and $b_A=0\leftrightarrow A$ intuitionistically.
The implication case uses that $b_A=1$ proves $\neg A$; it does not
assert excluded middle for arbitrary quantified formulas. Applying EI
to $b_A(n)=0$ recovers every contextual quantifier-free induction instance.

\subsection{The two numerical interpretations}

Take iPRA in its full first-order language of fixed PR descriptions,
with defining equations, constructor separation, and quantifier-free
induction. Its atomic-induction formulation is equivalent by the
characteristic argument above; this is the usual first-order iPRA
convention\cite{salehi}. Interpret its descriptions in $T_R$ by
\eqref{eq:compiler}, leaving domain, equality, and ordinary logic unchanged.
All defining equations, successor axioms, and induction instances have
just been verified internally.

Conversely, each fixed R body is interpreted in iPRA by ordinary
primitive recursion with base $Sa$ and step $h(u,v,\vec p)$. The
abbreviation $S(a)=R(0,a;u,v.0)$ then has ordinary successor value.
R0, RS, the boundary sentences, and EI are iPRA theorems.

For the stronger bi-interpretability claim, first anchor the successor
abbreviation. Its iPRA interpretation is the zero instance of the
recursor with zero update, so its defining base clause gives ordinary
$Sa$. Compiling that interpreted successor back gives the original
$S(a)$ by the compiler's base computation. This includes the expansion
$r_0(0,a)$ used to name successor, and does not presuppose uniqueness
of all R symbols. Thus the same successor occurs in both copies of each
recursion before the following staged uniqueness argument begins.

Now fix independent initializer and parameter variables. Two solutions
of the same fixed R recursion
are internally equal by induction on their value equation: bases agree,
and their steps agree by equality congruence and the induction equation.
Induct externally on the finite staged description. Its double
translation satisfies the same equations once earlier subdescriptions
have been identified, so uniqueness identifies it with the original.
The identical argument works for ordinary PR descriptions. Only after
these identities have been proved for independent parameters do we
substitute counter-dependent actual arguments. This also establishes
coherence when substitution changes the name of a staged body.

Both numerical interpretation domains are the entire original domain;
both internally definable isomorphisms have graph $x=y$. Nothing is
restricted to the standard cut or quotiented by a numerical observer.

\subsection{The logical round trips}

The ordinary meanings of gates and selectors give the advertised
meanings of all six macros in Section~\ref{sec:logic}, with coverage
in the universal case and separation for falsity. For the only primitive
object binder the reverse composite is explicitly
\[
 \D_{x,y}B\dashv\vdash
 \exists_*x\bigl(B(x,0)\Rightarrow_*\forall_*yB(x,y)\bigr).
\]
Forwards, open D at fresh a, use its available clause under the assumed
base to prove each $B(a,y)$, form the conditional universal, and introduce
the existential at a. Backwards, eliminate the existential at fresh a,
introduce the original D by case DI, and use its retained base, the
conditional, and universal specialization to obtain the successor case.
Neither direction requires Q to be an object goal. External induction
on formulas establishes both logical composites.

\begin{theorem}[Whole-domain bi-interpretation]
The ordinary realization of $\Acal$ is bi-interpretable with full
first-order iPRA. The two numerical isomorphisms are internally given
by $x=y$, and both logical formula composites are provably equivalent
to their originals. Effective finite proof translations preserve the
designated contradiction, hence the two theories are equiconsistent.
\end{theorem}
This is an external metatheorem. No claim is made that either theory
proves its own consistency or the uniform correctness of an arbitrary
primitive-recursive evaluator.

\subsection{Raw syntax and scope}

The canonical representation uses JSON lists of arity at most three
and integer leaves. Its two sorted meanings are
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{cll}
\toprule
List arity & Numerical position & Formula position\\
\midrule
0 & zero & truth ($0=0$)\\
1 & successor abbreviation & internal closure Q\\
2 & annotated zero & equation\\
3 & R recursor & paired binder D\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The additional scalar zero denotes zero or truth at the corresponding
sort. The first two children of D are checked padding; binary numerical
nodes denote zero with checked formula annotations. These aliases are
inherited representation conventions, not extra mathematical operations.

For a nearest-first environment $\rho$, coordinates $2j-1,2j$
both select its j-th entry. Negative coordinates name allocated rigid
parameters. Canonical numerical decoding is
\begin{align*}
 T_\rho([p])&=S(T_{(0,\rho)}p),\\
 T_\rho([n,a,h])&=
 R(T_{(0,0,\rho)}n,T_{(0,0,\rho)}a;
          u,v.T_{(v,u,\rho)}h).
\end{align*}
Equation children use the same environment, while Q prepends y and D
prepends $y,x$. The traversal's increments $2,1,4$ preserve the sort
parity: formula positions have even depth and numerical positions odd
depth. Opening at depth 3 protects the state coordinate while substituting
the counter; opening at depth 1 then supplies the recursive state.

The freshness floor k begins at zero and decreases at each DI or DE.
A scalar is retained exactly when $k\le a<d$. An exposed numerical
coordinate requests the supplied image, which is checked at numerical
root before insertion. An exposed formula leaf is an inert truth alias.
The initial \file{i(q())} is indispensable: it validates the public goal
before any eigenparameter has been allocated. Removing it accepts goals
with undeclared numerical parameters. The validation suite includes
that specific regression.

Canonical producers use only integer leaves and strict lists. On the
larger JSON input language, unsupported syntax raises, used numerical
images are validated, and unused annotation fields can be inert.
Finite real coordinates in an accepted numerical position have the same
slot meaning as their floor; booleans have Python's integer alias
meaning. Nonfinite leaves cannot serve as root numerical terms or used
numerical images, but they can be exposed template holes and disappear
under opening. This is an accepted alias, not an additional numerical
value. Nonnumeric used numerical leaves fail validation.
No soundness claim rests on trusting a producer or on executing submitted
code. The readable checker and \file{code/FORMAT.txt} explain the same
format without relying on terse variable names.

\subsection{Four-field instructions and all acceptance branches}

Input is an array with a closed goal as its last element. Earlier cells
are consumed from the end, each with four fields $[m,s,u,t]$. An unread
prefix is inert. A successful run prints \file{ACCEPT} and exits zero;
a failed run prints \file{REJECT} and exits nonzero, possibly with a
traceback. The checks also run under {\small\texttt{python -O}}; no logical check
uses an assertion removed by optimization.

For truthy m the checker first validates m and either finds it in the
context or proves it recursively. A failed major raises and cannot
fall through into introduction. The shared endpoint condition is
\[
 \bigl(m=[s,u]\ \&\ \operatorname{len}(t)=2\bigr)
 \quad\hbox{or}\quad
 \bigl(s=0\ \&\ m=[t]\bigr).
\]
The first alternative is exactly atomic transport. The second is Q
specialization and is allowed at compound object matrices. The target
opening must be the requested goal, and the source opening must have a
proof. A failed endpoint check on a unary or binary major cannot become
DE: accessing its third child fails. A ternary major yields precisely
the fresh-witness Q clause for DE. Canonical DE packets use $t=[]$,
because the new root guard evaluates \file{len(t)} even there.

For falsy m and truthy t the node is DI. The witness is opened, a fresh
parameter allocated, and exactly one local assumption is retained.
The integer tag $m=0$ permits progress at a literal equation;
$m=[]$ selects the case choice. Compound matrices receive the case
choice. A zero numerical witness is represented by a nonempty term
computing to zero, for example $R(S0,0;u,v.0)$, with equality transport
providing its usual representative change when necessary.

When both m and t are falsy the goal must be a computation equation.
At a zero counter its result is the singleton successor of the seed.
At a successor counter, the call \file{i(r,*n,3)} enforces exactly one
counter child by fixed function arity, even if the update ignores that
coordinate. The recursive state keeps the original r. The division by
the Boolean endpoint equality explicitly rejects a wrong result.

\subsection{Why the byte claim is limited but complete}

The atomic restriction itself adds a guard. Its mathematical value
is the smaller primitive logical interface. The byte saving comes from
combining elimination-only assumptions, explicit endpoint packets, and
the existing short R computation branch. The package also measures a
matched version with the atomic guard removed, so these sources of the
change can be distinguished. We do not assign an implementation saving
to a theorem merely because they occur in the same report.

For raw internal Q expressions the absence of general hypothesis
conclusion is essential. A Q-root goal has no successful finite proof:
computation and atomic transport end at equation roots; DI requires a
D root; DE keeps the goal; and Q specialization at a Q-root matrix
requires another Q-root minor. Following that minor gives an impossible
infinite descent in a finite accepted trace. The same argument excludes
Q on the object-formula spine. From an empty initial context, successful
object proofs therefore have the Eq/D purity required by the theory.
Q clauses remain usable as elimination majors. Inert numerical annotations
are not claimed to be additional object proof goals.

Soundness follows by induction on a successful finite checking trace,
using the sorted-opening and freshness invariant at each step and the
rule correspondence above. Canonical completeness follows by serializing
finite $\Acal$ derivations, expanding derived identities and transports,
and renaming eigenparameters in the checker's actual allocation order.
Very large valid proofs may exceed a real Python process's resources;
finite-resource rejection is not an additional inference principle.

\section{Validation and reproducibility}
\label{sec:validation}

Run {\small\texttt{python3 reproduce.py}} from the extracted directory. It checks
the archive manifest, remeasures complete programs with the frozen
normalizer, rebuilds certificates with the untrusted producer, and runs
positive and targeted negative cases against the public checker. The
tests include symbolic recursion equations, compound identity and
transport expansions, witness reuse, lexical binding, incorrect endpoints,
malformed counters, direct compound transport, and undeclared parameters.
The producer is not imported by the measured kernel. Its output is data
and is checked afresh by the actual standalone program.

\section{What this investigation leaves open}

The construction meets a strict complete-checker byte comparison and
re-establishes an exact logical restriction theorem in the combined interface.
It does not dominate all
three coordinates, establish global simplestness, or supply the requested
significant breakthrough. Its numerical basis and the structural transport
idea are inherited. The exact combined implementation and executable proof
expansion are the contributions of this package. This is the strongest
checked result obtained here.

Two more substantial implementation changes were examined. A bottom-up
proof synthesizer produced conclusions instead of repeatedly checking
targets; its prototype measured 356/422/934. A certificate-controlled
conversion traversal replaced computation leaves by reflexivity; its
prototype measured 324/373/883. Both were larger. They are not delivered
as certified alternatives. The separate note
\file{notes/certified_conversion.txt} gives the mathematical conservative
conversion construction without claiming a smaller complete program.

Unrestricted normalization is a particularly misleading shortcut: a
uniform primitive-recursive evaluator for all PR descriptions cannot
exist by diagonalization. A finite conversion certificate avoids that
claim, but its checks and scope machinery still have to be counted.
Likewise, allowing progress DI at arbitrary compound matrices recovers
unrestricted formula induction and changes the target theory. Deleting
the reusable Q clause loses the ability to specialize repeatedly at the
same existential witness. None of those costs has been hidden in this
package to manufacture a record.

The next substantive target is a numerical/logical representation that
removes one of these checked obligations while retaining both internal
interpretation composites.
