BiInterpretation.lean and NativeBiInterpretation.lean are theorem statements
and implementations copied from Lean/ConciseIPRA. Their repository imports
are not bundled as a standalone Lean build here; this package does not claim
that its new deterministic-discharge theorem was checked in Lean.

archive docs/incoming/ipra_witness_discharge_2026-11-11.zip, respectively

earlier snapshot 0ebd0c6b358f38bb0ad2883faf775421599c8aae.

The following validation helpers originate in the witness-discharge archive:
  validation/reference_kernel.py       unchanged independent reference kernel
  validation/convert.py                unchanged prior-certificate conversions
  validation/control_translations.py   unchanged separate-rule conversions
  validation/macros.py                 adapted packet proof constructors

The change to macros.py swaps the parity-selected packet rows: the first
positive row is now the naming equation x=t, and the second is h(x)=r.
The corresponding case proofs and row-use certificates were swapped too.
The arithmetic selector, numerical-case construction, equality bootstrap,
and recursive formula-transport constructions are inherited and credited.

The new validation/translations.py and verify_translation.py implement and
check the deterministic-discharge translations and the new input contract.
All validation helpers are untrusted certificate producers or independent
replayers. Neither selected standalone checker imports a validation module.

SHA-256 hashes and reproducible source measurements are in evidence/metrics.json.
They distinguish the new mathematical rule's measured cost from the shared
scope and certificate-interface simplifications, and preserve the smaller
516-byte and 788-byte prior encodings as comparison limits.
