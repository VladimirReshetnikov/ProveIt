\section{The complete native axiomatization being implemented}
\label{sec:complete-core}

Here iPRA means full first-order intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic:
all fixed finite primitive-recursive descriptions, their defining equations,
intuitionistic equality logic, quantifier-free induction, and the usual
constructor axioms $Sx\ne0$ and $Sx=Sy\to x=y$. The latter injectivity axiom
also follows from the included predecessor equations. The nonzero-successor
axiom is essential; positive equations alone admit a one-element structure.
Merely matching
its equations, equiconsistency strength, or standard-model numerical functions
would not meet the requested bi-interpretability condition.

\subsection{Numerical syntax and all nonlogical schemas}

Numerical terms are
\[
 t::=x\mid 0\mid St\mid I(n,a;v.h).
\]
Only the update body $h$ binds $v$. Other variables are passive numerical
parameters. Terms are finite staged descriptions, not codes supplied to an
object-language universal evaluator. The two nonlogical computation schemas,
available in every retained context, are exactly
\[
 \frac{}{\Gamma\vdash I(0,a;v.h)=a}\quad I0,
 \qquad
 \frac{}{\Gamma\vdash I(Sn,a;v.h)=h[I(n,a;v.h)/v]}\quad IS.
\]
There are no separate primitive predecessor, pairing, equality-test,
successor-injectivity, coverage, or separation axioms in the native calculus.

\subsection{Formula syntax, contexts, and all inference rules}

The essential object formulas are equations and one paired binder:
\[
 A::=(s=t)\mid \D_{x,y}A.
\]
The implementation also uses the closure notation $\Q_yB(y)$ for the reusable
conditional $B(0)\to\forall yB(y)$. It is a definable administrative formula,
equivalent to $\D_{u,y}B(y)$ with unused fresh $u$; no separate primitive
closure-introduction axiom is assumed. Contexts are finite reusable collections
of formulas. In the following inventory $Q$ abbreviations may be nested, with
their definitional expansion understood.

\begin{align*}
 &\frac{}{\Gamma\vdash A}\ \mathrm{Hyp}\quad(A\in\Gamma),\\[4pt]
 &\frac{\Gamma\vdash s=t\qquad\Gamma\vdash F(s)}
        {\Gamma\vdash F(t)}\ \mathrm{Tr},\\[4pt]
 &\frac{\Gamma\vdash\Q_y B(y)\qquad\Gamma\vdash B(0)}
        {\Gamma\vdash B(t)}\ \mathrm{QE},\\[4pt]
 &\frac{\Gamma\vdash\D_{x,y}B(x,y)\qquad
         \Gamma,\Q_yB(a,y)\vdash F}
        {\Gamma\vdash F}\ \mathrm{DE},\\[4pt]
 &\frac{\Gamma,B(t,0)\vdash B(t,Sz)}
        {\Gamma\vdash\D_{x,y}B(x,y)}\ \mathrm{DI}_{0},\\[4pt]
 &\frac{\Gamma,E(t,z)\vdash E(t,Sz)}
        {\Gamma\vdash\D_{x,y}E(x,y)}\ \mathrm{DI}_{1}
        \quad(E\text{ a literal equation}).
\end{align*}

In DE, $a$ is fresh for $\Gamma$, $F$, and every fixed parameter of $B$.
In either DI, $z$ is fresh for $\Gamma$, the witness $t$, and every fixed
parameter of the matrix. Transport uses any fixed capture-free formula
template $F(w)$. All displayed local assumptions are discharged. A literal
equation means an equation at the matrix root, not an equation beneath a binder.

Exchange, weakening, contraction, renaming, numerical substitution, and cut
are admissible by induction on derivations; they are not extra instructions
hidden in the checker. The public implementation initially retains the truth
$0=0$, encoded by the empty array. This is a derived theorem and is discharged
by cut. Appendix~\ref{sec:choice-introduction} gives a primitive clause-based
presentation, proves this formula-context formulation equivalent, and derives
the entire ordinary logical interface. Explicitly, eliminating
$\D_{u,y}B(y)$ with unused $u$ gives the clause
$\langle B(0);y.B(y)\rangle$, from which QE follows by Use. Conversely,
that clause proves $\D_{u,y}B(y)$ by case DI at an unused witness: under
$B(0)$, use the clause at $Sz$. These conversions justify the formula-context
DE resource and nested closure abbreviations without adding a logical axiom.

\subsection{Why the restriction gives iPRA}

The case rule supplies numerical zero/successor cases for arbitrary formulas.
The progress rule, DE, and QE give contextual equation induction:
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash E(0)\qquad\Gamma,E(z)\vdash E(Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash E(t)}.
\]
Choose an unused existential coordinate, introduce its paired equation by
progress, eliminate to $\Q_yE(y)$, and use the supplied base. This does not
provide induction for arbitrary quantified formulas. Permitting compound
progress would change the intended theory.

Equality reflexivity is derived from computation and transport: use
$r=I(0,t;v.0)$, the computation $r=t$, and the transport template $w=t$
to infer $t=t$. Symmetry, transitivity, and congruence follow with their
usual explicit equality templates.

The selector $C(n,a,b)=I(n,a;v.b)$ satisfies $C(0,a,b)=a$ and
$C(Sn,a,b)=b$. It permits finite formula gates and selectors. These reconstruct
intuitionistic implication, conjunction, disjunction, both quantifiers, and
falsity from the paired binder. The arithmetic bootstrap then obtains
predecessor, a characteristic of actual equality, quantifier-free induction,
pairing, and arbitrary primitive recursion. These are established internal
derivations, not additional primitives charged outside the program.

\subsection{Sorted representation}

Equations are $[s,t]$, paired formulas are $[B]$, and canonical closures
are $[B,B,B]$. Numerical terms use scalar zero, unary successor, and ternary

arrays of arity at least four denote $0=0$. The first two fields of a general
ternary formula are ignored annotations; the third is its closure body.

The opener uses the inherited common depth increment
$5-\operatorname{len}(a)$. Numerical root depth is three. Two scalar
coordinates represent each protected bound slot, while negative coordinates
above the current floor are rigid parameters. Successor has two dummy slots;
iteration has one state slot in its update and one dummy slot in its other
arguments. The full decoder and the semantic opening lemma are reproduced

surplus arrays are essential to soundness.

\subsection{Packet meanings}

Every packet first proves its major $m$. The canonical absent major is $[]$;
it closes immediately from the initial truth assumption, consumes no packet,
and allocates no parameter.

If $m$ is a nonempty array, matching an equality or a closure can replace
the target by its transport or specialization premise. In all cases $m*3$
is exported. A unary paired formula thereby supplies the intended closure
at a fresh witness. Every other nonempty array exports a surplus-arity truth.

If $m=[]$ and $u$ is truthy, the packet supplies a computation. The field $s$
is its predecessor, $u$ its initializer, and $t$ its update. A false-valued $s$
selects zero computation; otherwise the counter is constructed as $[s]$.
If $m=[]$ and $u$ is false-valued, the packet introduces a paired formula
using witness $t$. Canonical $u=0$ selects equation progress, and $u=[]$
selects cases. An odd-arity compound matrix cannot obtain a progress assumption.

The syntactically truthy initializer restriction does not delete arithmetic
instances. The established zero repair uses
\[
       C_0=I(S(S0),S0;v.0),\qquad C_0=0.
\]
Its computation has nonempty predecessor and initializer. Replacing a missing
literal-zero initializer or predecessor by $C_0$ and transporting $C_0=0$
recovers the original computation. The full finite normalization, including
its proof-node accounting, is reproduced in Appendix~\ref{sec:seed-dispatch}.

\section{Why the implementation composition is valid}
\label{sec:composition}

The argument is a composition of verified transformations of the inherited
checker. It is not a fresh proof of the predecessor bootstrap or a claim that
finite testing establishes all arithmetic metatheorems.

\subsection{Neutral premises are a semantic guard}

The initial context contains $[]$, denoting the derived truth $0=0$.
All subsequent context entries remain lists. No numeric goal has a finite
successful proof from such a context. To see this, take a smallest purported
successful numeric-goal trace. Its initial membership test cannot close.
A successful numeric major would be a smaller counterexample. Transport
cannot change a numeric root to a list-valued formula, because root scalar
opening is independent of its substitution image. A continuation would again
be a smaller counterexample. Introduction cannot unpack a numeric goal and
then obtain its length. These alternatives exhaust a successful trace.

Consequently a numeric-zero major cannot reach the unguarded expression
\file{m in ([s, u], not s and [t] * 3)}. This matters because its second
candidate is Boolean false when $s$ is truthy, and Python equates that value
with numerical zero. The actual neutral major is an empty list, which does
not have that equality. Appendix~\ref{sec:neutral-major-audit} supplies the
full inherited raw-input argument, including numeric aliases and the mode
comparison with $u$.

\subsection{Local names replace a global allocation counter}

Each activation owns its floor $k$. Proving its major passes the current
floor to the recursive activation, which may allocate its own names but cannot
change its caller's floor. The major and retained context were already checked
under the caller's floor, so none of the major proof's private names occurs
freely in its exposed conclusion or hypotheses. Reusing their labels after
return is alpha-renaming of disjoint proof scopes.

The continuation decreases the caller's floor once. Both the successor goal
and new assumption are opened after that decrease; the opener closes over the
updated local variable. All context extensions create new lists. A subproof
therefore cannot export private assumptions by mutating a shared context.

For integer coordinates there is one designated new eigenparameter, $k$.
With accepted fractional coordinates there can be finitely many additional
names in the newly exposed interval. They are all fresh for the retained
target and context, and all must be included in the corresponding discharge.
They are not silently identified with the designated integer name.

\subsection{The hypothesis branch becomes one shared expression}

The old three-way conditional for the supplied hypothesis can be written
\[
 m*3\quad\texttt{or}\quad(u\ \texttt{and computation})
       \quad\texttt{or}\quad\texttt{introduction}.
\]
On a successful exact-list trace a substantive major is a nonempty list,
and its triple is nonempty. A selected computation constructs a binary list,
also nonempty. Hence the Boolean chain selects exactly the same first
applicable branch as the old conditional. Introduction is reached exactly
when both the major and initializer-or-mode field are false-valued.

This last condition also protects induction: no false-valued JSON value
equals one, so the parity comparison cannot grant progress to a meaningful
odd-arity compound matrix. The comparison must use $u$, not the unrestricted
source field $s$.

\subsection{Computation assumptions are discharged at zero}

The shared continuation may retain an equation involving newly allocated
names. Its soundness is not the assertion that this equation holds at every
value of those names. The relevant admissible rule is
\[
 \Gamma,H(\vec a)\vdash F,\quad \vdash H(\vec0),\quad
 \vec a\notin\FV(\Gamma,F)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \Gamma\vdash F.
\]
Substitute zero for the finite family $\vec a$ in the first derivation and
cut in the proof of $H(\vec0)$. The old-floor scope check supplies the required
freshness. The generated equation's zero instance is an $I0$ or $IS$ instance.
If the update uses its state, the inserted recursive source is rechecked at
numerical depth three; if it does not, the update is constant in that state.
The inherited arity proof treats ignored annotations separately. This is why
the composition preserves computation soundness even for accepted aliases.

\subsection{The missing-image sentinel}

The fixed Python value \file{0j} is not a value supplied by the JSON reader.
If an unresolved numerical hole attempts to validate it as an inserted image,
the ordered comparison fails. This has the same successful evaluations as
the former \file{None} sentinel. At formula padding positions no numerical
image is needed. This representation choice is charged in the measured source;
it is not an added arithmetic operation or a numeral of the object theory.

\begin{theorem}[Composed checker]
Every finite successful execution of the primary or explicit-list checker
has a decoded conclusion derivable in the inherited native calculus.
Conversely, every finite native derivation of a canonical closed formula has
a finite accepted certificate under adequate execution resources. The public
initial truth assumption is conservative. The two programs therefore present
the same canonical closed theory as the inherited complete checker.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For soundness induct on the finite successful call tree. Hypothesis closure
uses the semantic opening lemma. A checked major permits only equality
transport, closure specialization, paired elimination, or adjoining truth.
Introduction is exactly case DI or literal-equation progress DI, with the
freshness established by the local floor. Computation uses the zero-instance
discharge just proved. The neutral-major invariant validates the unguarded
matcher; the shared expression selects the same rule as the old dispatcher.
No child can mutate its parent's context or allocation state.

For completeness apply the inherited canonical proof compilation and
certified-zero normalization, then rename every subordinate major proof's
eigenvariables locally. Encode absent majors by $[]$, use the matching case
or progress mode, and consume the resulting finite packet tree in reverse
stack order. A neutral major closes without consuming a packet. Every
nonempty-major and computation export has the nonempty shape used by the
shared expression. Thus every compiled proof follows the simulated trace.
Finally replace uses of the initial $0=0$ by its finite native derivation.
\end{proof}

The primary opener terminates structurally on finite JSON syntax in the
ideal adequate-resource reading: a substitution image is checked with the
invalid default image, so insertion cannot start an unrestricted chain of
further substitutions. Every nonclosing checker activation consumes a packet
from a finite stack. Actual interpreters still have memory and stack limits.

\subsection{The separate AST profile}

The 610-byte profile replaces the tail-recursive continuation by a loop,
using an immutable tuple context. Its recursively checked major still
receives the old tuple. The augmented assignment creates a new tuple rather
than mutating a context visible to another activation. This is an exact
tail-call simulation and saves one AST node against the archived AST leader.

That profile also uses the inherited generic iterable opener. Empty strings
and objects have documented truth aliases. Nonempty strings can recurse

The raw soundness statement concerns finite successful traces. This
qualification does not apply to the primary or explicit-list opener. The
smaller AST count is not a claim of an equally small structurally terminating
parser on all finite malformed JSON.

\section{Bi-interpretability, rather than just numerical agreement}

Let $T_I$ use ordinary intuitionistic first-order equality logic, the same
staged iterator equations, contextual literal-equation induction, numerical
coverage, and $(0=S0)\to\bot$. The native realization is
\[
 (s=t)^\circ=(s=t),\qquad
 (\D_{x,y}B)^\circ=\exists x\bigl(B^\circ(x,0)\to\forall yB^\circ(x,y)\bigr),
\]
with $\Q_yB$ read as the displayed conditional universal. Case introduction
is valid by coverage; progress introduction is valid by equation induction
under the conditional base. The remaining native rules have their direct
ordinary meanings. In the reverse direction, the formula gates and selectors
derive every ordinary logical rule, coverage, equation induction, and falsity
elimination. Their formula composites are proved in
Appendix~\ref{sec:choice-introduction}.

\begin{theorem}[Inherited full-domain equivalence]
The ordinary realization $T_I$ and full first-order iPRA are bi-interpretable
and equiconsistent. Both numerical interpretation composites have the
internally definable identity isomorphism $x=y$ on the entire domain.
The delivered checker presents this same theory on canonical encodings.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
An initialized scalar iterator is primitive recursive, so iPRA interprets
every fixed iterator description and proves its defining equations and the
other axioms of $T_I$.

For the other direction, the scalar predecessor proof in
Appendix~\ref{sec:hybrid-bootstrap} derives $P(0)=0$ and $P(Sn)=n$ using
only the available equations, cases, and literal-equation induction.
Appendix~\ref{sec:eic-completion} constructs a numerical characteristic
reflecting actual equality for arbitrary domain elements and converts each
fixed quantifier-free formula to a Boolean equation. It therefore recovers
quantifier-free induction. Appendix~\ref{sec:iterator-coding} constructs
pairing and compiles each finite primitive-recursive description into the
scalar language.

Internal equation induction proves uniqueness of every fixed iterator and
primitive recursion from its defining equations. External induction on finite
descriptions shows that double translation is provably equal to the original
function on the whole domain, including substitution coherence for staged
update descriptions. No restriction to standard numerals is made. The native
paired formula is internally equivalent to its interpreted existential,
implication, and universal formula; structural induction supplies both formula
composites. The explicit ordinary falsity bridge preserves contradiction.
Effective translations of finite proofs give equiconsistency. The new program
inherits these facts by the preceding consequence-relation simulation, not
through a new arithmetic bootstrap.
\end{proof}

The appendices reproduce the complete attributed arguments because a claim
that all standard primitive-recursive functions are representable would not
by itself prove the required internal round trips.

\section{Unresolved reductions and further research questions}

The substantial search did not produce a new complete axiomatization below
the current one. Deleting atomic case introduction and retaining only atomic
progress is already refuted in the drafts; the all-starts repair is also
already developed there. These rediscoveries are not claimed as results of
this report. Recasting a checker as formula synthesis introduces return-rule
and eigenvariable-escape obligations; no smaller complete version was obtained.

The scalar self-seeded candidate
\[
 F(0;v.h)=S(h(0)),\qquad F(Sn;v.h)=h(F(n;v.h))
\]
remains structurally interesting. Its existing treatment explicitly adds an
equality characteristic and successor injectivity. The initial zero test
does not by itself justify arbitrary numerical selection or a source-language
compiler for graph-defined functions. Omitting those obligations would make
the apparent simplicity comparison invalid.

The next substantive questions are therefore:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can a logical or arithmetic primitive actually be removed while
      preserving the full native formula composites, rather than merely its
      standard-model equations or consistency strength?
\item Can the self-seeded basis derive its additional equality and constructor
      principles, or can a complete implementation charge them and still beat
      the initialized-iterator checker?
\item Can the semantic opener, fractional-name discharge, and computation
      source check be formalized in Lean or Rocq without replacing the actual
      raw-input program by an easier canonical-only model?
\item Can a fixed implementation language, input contract, and explicit allowed
      class of presentations support a meaningful lower-bound theorem? A
      corpus census alone cannot provide one.
\end{enumerate}

The present deliverable is a verified implementation reduction and an exact
account of what remains open. It does not resolve the requested significant
axiomatic breakthrough.

draft corpus with their original mathematical proofs and labels. Their
historical phrases such as ``new'' describe their original reports, not the
current contribution. Source identities are recorded in
\file{evidence/provenance.json}; the upstream MIT No Attribution license is
included in \file{reference/UPSTREAM_LICENSE.txt}. Mathematical attribution is
retained independently of that license.

The first five appendices supply the native basis and full arithmetic bridge.
The next supplies the inherited sorted decoder and its older packet protocol.
The final two supply the subsequent certified-zero and neutral-major changes.
Section~\ref{sec:composition} proves how the current local-scope and shared
expression composition transfers their results. Historical packet-field
conventions in an appendix must not be mistaken for the current interface in
Section~3.
