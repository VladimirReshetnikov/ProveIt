\begin{abstract}
The shared-continuation draft leaves a specific two-constructor arithmetic
candidate unresolved: a scalar recursor with successor seed, whose update
receives the previous state but not the counter. We close that gap. A
transient state supplies selected zero-start iterations before arbitrary
initialization is available. These produce a comparator; a reset cycle
then gives a finite predecessor term by literal-equation induction.
The initializer compiler consequently recovers ordinary iteration.

A second bootstrap makes this result available directly in the small
native logic. A finite decoder cancels successor on Boolean values before
a general predecessor exists. It supplies restricted implication and
contextual Boolean case analysis with equation conclusions, exactly what
the arithmetic proof needs. Only afterward is the unshifted selector used
to reconstruct full intuitionistic first-order logic. The resulting
$0,R$ presentation, with one update slot and two computation schemas,
is whole-domain bi-interpretable and equiconsistent with full iPRA.
Both numerical composites are the identity, and the native $Q/D$ formula
composites are proved.

A complete direct SR checker occupies 681 normalized bytes, below the
685-byte minimum in the supplied current corpus. Its 284 tokens and
322 AST nodes do not beat the respective corpus minima 281 and 315.
An auxiliary initialized-I predecessor-packet checker has profile
280/314/684, strictly below all three incumbent coordinates in one complete
program. Further auxiliary profiles give the separate coordinate envelope
280/311/679; no one program attains that whole new envelope. The principal result is the arithmetic and logical bootstrap,
not those small implementation savings.

A separate obstruction theorem proves that tying a recursor's seed to its
own update forces every numerical term to have a single bounding input
coordinate. That candidate cannot represent addition, a general selector,
or injective pairing by terms. Explicit rules, internal proofs, source
provenance, executable certificates, numerical diagnostics, and reproduction
scripts are included. Absolute minimality, literature-wide priority, and
proof-assistant verification are not claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Reproduction and attribution}
Run \texttt{python3 verify.py} from the archive directory to remeasure the
complete programs, replay the certificates and soundness controls, and
execute both arithmetic diagnostic suites. Run \texttt{python3 build.py}
to compile this document. The reports record CPython's version because
\texttt{ast.unparse} is part of the measurement convention. No submitted
Python expression is evaluated by either theorem checker; input is JSON
data. The diagnostic evaluators and source measurer are not imports of
the small checkers.

\file{evidence/source_snapshot.json} identifies all nine supplied ZIPs
by Git blob hash, SHA-256, and byte length. The complete scan of 261 Python
source occurrences (164 distinct contents) is in
\file{evidence/corpus_metrics.json}. The smaller non-checker files are
classified in \file{evidence/nonchecker_classification.json}.
The ten nested historical ZIP references were also scanned separately:
154 Python source occurrences, with no change to the current frontier.
Their measurements are in \file{evidence/nested_reference_metrics.json}.
Frozen
incumbent byte/token and AST leaders and the unchanged measurement
utility are included in \file{reference/}. The new checker profiles are
derived from the generated-assumptions draft; its numerical and logical
machinery is attributed, not claimed as new. The comparator's factor-map
strategy is adapted from the scalar predecessor appendix; the transient
seeds, shifted factor maps, reset-cycle predecessor, finite-Boolean native
bootstrap, and tied-seed obstruction are the new mathematical developments in this package.
