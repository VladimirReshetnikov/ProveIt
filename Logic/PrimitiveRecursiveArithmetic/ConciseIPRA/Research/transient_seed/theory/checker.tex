\section{The native checker comparison, with its exact boundary}
\label{sec:checker}

The principal program in Appendix~\ref{app:programs} directly checks
native successor-seeded scalar recursion, SR0/SRS. Its numerical grammar
is $x,0,R(n,a;u.h)$, with a serialized unary node for the fixed abbreviation
$Sa=R(0,a;u.0)$. The auxiliary I profiles instead supply initialized
iteration, and their independent source changes are described below.
Its formulas are equations, $Q_yB$, and $D_{x,y}B$, with
ordinary meanings
\[
 Q_yB=B(0)\to\forall y B(y),\qquad
 D_{x,y}B=\exists x\bigl(B(x,0)\to\forall y B(x,y)\bigr).
\]
The designated native absurdity is $0=S0$.

\subsection{Its entire primitive inventory}

Hypothesis use and transport are
\[
 \frac{}{\Gamma\vdash A}\ (A\in\Gamma),\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash s=t\quad\Gamma\vdash F(s)}{\Gamma\vdash F(t)}.
\]
Here $F$ may be any native formula. The closure and paired eliminations are
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash Q_yB\quad\Gamma\vdash B(0)}{\Gamma\vdash B(t)},\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}B\quad\Gamma,Q_yB(a,y)\vdash A}{\Gamma\vdash A}.
\]
The eigenparameter $a$ is fresh for the conclusion, context, and passive
matrix parameters. The two permitted paired introductions are
\[
 \frac{\Gamma,B(t,0)\vdash B(t,Sz)}{\Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}B},\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma,E(t,z)\vdash E(t,Sz)}{\Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}E}.
\]
The second is allowed only for a literal equation $E$; $z$ is fresh for
all retained data. The local hypotheses are discharged. Compound matrices
have only the first, case-style introduction.

Finally the computation-supply rule is
\[
 \frac{\Gamma,H(w)\vdash A}{\Gamma\vdash A},\qquad
 w\notin\operatorname{FV}(\Gamma,A),\qquad H(0)\text{ is a computation theorem}.
\]
The implementation generates only the specified SR0/SRS instances (I0/IS in the auxiliary profiles) and their
zero-extension aliases; it has no general theorem recognizer. This rule
is justified by substituting $w:=0$ into the premise and cutting its
known instance. Conversely an ordinary computation leaf is supplied as
a constant-in-$w$ hypothesis and closed by Hyp. Neutral unmatched Eq/Q
requests add only the theorem $0=0$ after their major has been proved.
There is no additional logical axiom or unrestricted induction rule.

\subsection{Why this is a full intuitionistic presentation}
\label{sec:logical-reconstruction}

Section~\ref{sec:native-sr-bootstrap} derives predecessor in native SR
before using the present subsection. Its initialized-iterator macro then
gives the unshifted selector $C(n,a,b)=I(n,a;u.b)$ by computation. On equations define the gate and selector
\[
 G_v(s=t):\ C(v,0,s)=C(v,0,t),\qquad
 M_v(s=t,r=q):\ C(v,s,r)=C(v,t,q).
\]
Extend gates through $D$ binders. A $Q$ formula is interderivable with
a $D$ formula with unused existential variable, by case introduction,
paired elimination, and closure specialization. The padding equivalence
$A\dashv\vdash D_{u,y}G_yA$ follows from the true zero gate and its
successor instance. Pad the shallower operand of a selector until its
binders align, then extend the selector through the aligned binders.
Induction on this finite syntax gives
\[
 \vdash G_0A,\quad G_{Sv}A\dashv\vdash A,\qquad
 M_0(A,B)\dashv\vdash A,\quad M_{Sv}(A,B)\dashv\vdash B.
\]
For $p(z)=I(z,0;u.C(u,1,0))$, finite computations give $p(1)=1,p(2)=0$.
The ordinary connectives and quantifiers have the macros
\[
\begin{aligned}
 A\Rightarrow_*B&=D_{u,z}M_z(A,B),\\
 A\land_*B&=D_{u,z}G_zM_{p(z)}(A,B),\\
 \exists_*xA(x)&=D_{x,y}G_yA(x),\\
 A\lor_*B&=\exists_*xM_x(A,B),\\
 \forall_*xA(x)&=A(0)\land_*D_{u,z}G_zA(z),\\
 \bot_*&=(0=1).
\end{aligned}
\]
All unused binders and bound-name changes are fresh. The selector endpoints
prove the implication rules; the gates prove the existential rules;
numerical cases prove disjunction elimination. The instances 1 and 2
give the conjunction projections. Numerical coverage, obtained by cases,
gives universal elimination; the zero instance and successor cases give
universal introduction. Under $0=1$, transport through $C$ proves any
equation, and case introduction by formula induction proves explosion.
Progress introduction gives contextual equation induction. No induction
on a quantified formula is introduced.

The nontrivial native return formulas are
\[
 Q_yB\dashv\vdash B(0)\Rightarrow_*\forall_*yB(y),\qquad
 D_{x,y}B\dashv\vdash
 \exists_*x\bigl(B(x,0)\Rightarrow_*\forall_*yB(x,y)\bigr).
\]
Forward, open the paired formula and use its closure under an assumed
base. Backward, eliminate the displayed existential and use case
introduction, with implication and universal elimination providing its
successor premise. These arguments cover arbitrary raw native matrices.

For arithmetic, interpret SR in ordinary iPRA by
$R(n,a;h)=I(n,Sa;h)$. Conversely the native bootstrap below derives the
predecessor and initialized iterator; the present logical reconstruction
then applies, followed by Section~\ref{sec:completion}. Recursion uniqueness
supplies the numerical identity composites, with successor anchored by
SR0 first. The displayed $Q/D$ return equivalences supply the native-formula
composites. Thus both measured native presentations have full equivalence;
the direct SR checker needs no uncharged translation to an I checker.

\subsection{Codec and scope}

Input is one JSON array. Its last entry is the requested closed native
formula; earlier four-field commands $[m,s,u,t]$ are popped backward.
An unused prefix is inert. Success prints ACCEPT and exits zero; failure
prints REJECT and exits nonzero, possibly with a traceback.

The sorted tree decoder uses arity 0 for numerical zero/formula truth,
arity 1 for successor/$Q$, arity 2 for annotated numerical zero/equality,
and arity 3 for the scalar recursor/$D$. Its depth increments are
respectively immaterial, 2, 1, and 4. At numerical depth $2q+1$ there
are $q$ protected numerical slots; the positive coordinates $2j-1,2j$
alias the same slot. Successor adds a dummy zero; a recursor's counter
and seed add two dummy zeros, whereas its update gets two aliases of
one state. The two unused fields of a $D$ node are checked annotations.
The global negative floor decreases at each continuation, introducing
a rigid fresh parameter. The opener checks images at numerical root;
its structural induction gives capture-free substitution and scope
safety. Arrays of arity greater than three reject.

The old goal is checked before fresh allocation. A matched transport or
closure minor is likewise checked before allocation. Arithmetic eagerly
checks the recursive predecessor term, even if its update ignores the state.
The generated equation's zero instance is an SR0 or SRS equation (I0 or IS for the auxiliary profiles) after
dummy zero slots are identified. Freshness plus known-instance cut
therefore discharges its continuation. These are mathematical guards,
not optional input hygiene. The supplied mutation controls demonstrate
false acceptance if any of major checking, predecessor validation, or
the equation-only induction restriction is removed.

\subsection{What changed in the measured programs}

The direct SR byte profile additionally replaces the zero-counter
result \texttt{t} by \texttt{[t]}, the serialized successor. This changes
the checked arithmetic theory exactly from initialized I to SR; the
nonzero computation, validation, freshness, and logical dispatch are
unchanged. A unary successor remains a fixed syntactic abbreviation and
is fully charged as syntax in the implementation.

The auxiliary I byte profile replaces one conditional assignment statement by its
equivalent conditional expression. Evaluation order, lazy selection, and
all accepted inputs remain exactly the same. This saves six normalized
bytes and increases the other two measures. It is a small source refactoring.

The structural profile removes the redundant list concatenation
\texttt{a + []} from the existing exception-based traversal. Canonical
array/integer certificates are unchanged. The additional successful
traversed JSON aliases have the following explicit erasure:
\[
 \texttt{""}\mapsto[],\qquad \texttt{\{\}}\mapsto[],\qquad
 \texttt{\{"": value\}}\mapsto[[]].
\]
The ignored object value has no numerical or logical meaning. Nonempty
strings recurse into nonempty characters and fail; objects with any
nonempty key fail for the same reason. A dictionary cannot survive the
major-premise slice or the DI goal's integer lookup. Empty aliases are
zero counters; the one-key alias is successor zero, whose starred
predecessor is the empty-string zero and is eagerly normalized.
Every successfully appended context entry remains a list.

Soundness is proved by decoding each successful inference step with
these aliases. Do not erase an entire certificate and claim operational
equivalence: erasure can change a neutral mismatch into a matched
elimination. The direct decoded proof still uses exactly the sound
logical and arithmetic rules above. Canonical completeness is unchanged
because canonical certificates have the original array/integer form.
This is a serialization simplification, not a new logical principle.

The executable examples cover actual use of induction hypotheses,
nonvacuous witnesses, transport below a binder, generated zero witnesses,
scope failures, malformed counters, failed majors, and forbidden compound
induction. Finite evaluations of the SR macros check the numerical
