\section{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Can the comparator be shortened or bypassed?} The factor-map
method is explicit and uses only equation induction, but its finite term
expands substantially. A direct predecessor cycle that requires no Boolean
comparison could reduce both the proof and the generated certificates.

\item \textbf{How general is finite cancellation as a logical bootstrap?}
Here successor can be cancelled on Boolean terms before it can be cancelled
on arbitrary numbers. That recovers restricted implication, then precisely
the contextual cases needed for arithmetic. Determine which other weak
numerical signatures admit this route to their full logical interface.

\item \textbf{How general is transient initialization?} If a required orbit
admits a known successor-state preimage, one extra application of its
update supplies that initial state. Identify an abstract class of finite
reset mechanisms sufficient to recover arbitrary initialization in other
restricted recursion bases.

\item \textbf{Can the direct SR checker beat all three coordinates at once?}
The direct program beats the incumbent normalized-byte minimum. An
auxiliary I program now beats all three old minima by one unit each, while
other I profiles improve individual coordinates further. Achieving the
same joint improvement for the direct SR syntax would connect that
stronger implementation comparison to the new arithmetic grammar.
All parsing, scope, image, freshness, and induction restrictions must remain
charged and justified.

\item \textbf{Can the tied-seed obstruction extend beyond term translations?}
The bounded-coordinate theorem excludes ordinary addition, selection,
and pairing terms. It does not settle definable relational or multi-coordinate
interpretations. A model-theoretic invariant stable under such interpretations
would be a substantially stronger negative result.

\item \textbf{Can the whole proof be machine checked?} The highest-value
targets are the factor-map identities, the guarded literal induction,
fixed-parameter cycle identities, the native Boolean case compiler,
and the internal interpretation composites. The supplied primitive
certificates and numerical evaluations are useful checks but cannot replace
that formalization.

\item \textbf{What should the simplicity benchmark fix?} Source language,
runtime primitives, input grammar, malformed-input behavior, and the treatment
of staged descriptions all affect an implementation comparison. The current
metric is reproducible once those choices are fixed. It does not supply a
universal lower bound on mathematical axiomatizations.
\end{enumerate}

The closed result is a new native arithmetic and logical presentation,
a complete written bi-interpretation, and a direct checker below the
supplied byte frontier. Absolute simplestness across arbitrary languages,
domains, and implementations remains an open optimization problem.
