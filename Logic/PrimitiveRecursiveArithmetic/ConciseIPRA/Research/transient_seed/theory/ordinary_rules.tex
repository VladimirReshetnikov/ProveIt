\section{The complete ordinary axiomatization}
\label{sec:ordinary-rules}

Use one numerical sort and finite terms
\[
 t::=x\mid0\mid R(n,a;u.h),\qquad Sa:=R(0,a;u.0).
\]
Only $h$ binds $u$. Bound names are renamed before capture-free
substitution. Passive parameters may be arbitrary numerical terms; the
description of each update is fixed finite earlier syntax. Formulas are
equations, genuine falsity $\bot$, and their closure under
$\land,\lor,\to,\forall,\exists$. Negation abbreviates implication to
$\bot$. We use the usual full first-order iPRA, for which atomic induction
is a standard presentation~\cite{salehi}; the equivalence to
quantifier-free induction is proved in Section~\ref{sec:completion}.

\subsection{Every nonlogical axiom and induction rule}

The computation schemas are exactly
\begin{align*}
 R(0,a;u.h)&=Sa, &&\text{(SR0)}\\
 R(Sn,a;u.h)&=h[R(n,a;u.h)/u]. &&\text{(SRS)}
\end{align*}
There are two additional boundary sentences:
\[
 \forall x\,(x=0\lor\exists y\,x=Sy),\qquad
                 (0=S0)\to\bot. \tag{Cov,Sep}
\]
The only primitive induction is the contextual rule for a literal equation
$E(n,\vec p)$:
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash E(0,\vec p)\qquad
       \Gamma,E(z,\vec p)\vdash E(Sz,\vec p)}
      {\Gamma\vdash E(t,\vec p)}. \tag{EI}
\]
Here $z$ is absent from $\Gamma,t,\vec p$. The context may contain
quantified formulas. Induction on an implication, a disjunction, or an
arbitrary quantified formula is not primitive. There is no additional
successor-injectivity axiom, predecessor axiom, selector axiom, or
initializer axiom.

\subsection{Every logical axiom and rule}

Contexts are finite reusable lists. The assumption, equality, and falsity
rules are
\[
 \frac{}{\Gamma\vdash A}\ (A\in\Gamma),\qquad
 \frac{}{\Gamma\vdash t=t},\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash s=t\quad\Gamma\vdash F(s)}{\Gamma\vdash F(t)},\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash\bot}{\Gamma\vdash A}.
\]
The equality motive $F$ is any fixed finite formula. The propositional
rules are precisely
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \frac{\Gamma,A\vdash B}{\Gamma\vdash A\to B},\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A\to B\quad\Gamma\vdash A}{\Gamma\vdash B},\\[5pt]
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A\quad\Gamma\vdash B}{\Gamma\vdash A\land B},\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A_0\land A_1}{\Gamma\vdash A_i}\ (i=0,1),\\[5pt]
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A_i}{\Gamma\vdash A_0\lor A_1}\ (i=0,1),\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A\lor B\quad\Gamma,A\vdash C\quad\Gamma,B\vdash C}
      {\Gamma\vdash C}.
\end{gathered}
\]
The quantifier rules are
\[
\begin{gathered}
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A(z)}{\Gamma\vdash\forall x\,A(x)},\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash\forall x\,A(x)}{\Gamma\vdash A(t)},\\[5pt]
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A(t)}{\Gamma\vdash\exists x\,A(x)},\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash\exists x\,A(x)\quad\Gamma,A(z)\vdash C}
      {\Gamma\vdash C}.
\end{gathered}
\]
In universal introduction $z$ is fresh for $\Gamma$ and the passive
parameters; in existential elimination it is additionally absent from
$C$. Displayed local hypotheses are discharged. Numerical substitution,
renaming, weakening, exchange, contraction, and ordinary assumption cut
are admissible by induction on finite derivations. No excluded middle,
double-negation elimination, or other classical rule is included.

This explicitly defined theory is denoted $T_{\rm SR}$. The comparator
and predecessor constructions below use this ordinary logical interface.
That choice is part of the cost and dependency statement, not an
unlisted logical oracle.
