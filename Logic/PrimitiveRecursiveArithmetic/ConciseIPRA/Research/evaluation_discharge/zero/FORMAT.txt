ZERO-NORMAL SCALAR COMPANION

Run: python3 verify.py
Check one certificate: python3 code/checker.py < examples/positive/fixed_reflexivity.json
A certificate is accepted only if the process exits zero and prints ACCEPT.
Errors, including resource exhaustion, reject. No acceptance gate uses assert.

The document is exactly a JSON object with goal and proof members.
Terms: 0; negative free levels; positive bound indices; [0,t] for successor;
[n,a,h] for I(n,a;u.h). Only h binds one index. Stored terms are zero-normal:
no iterator has literal counter0. Substitution contracts newly exposed zero
iterators but never unfolds successor computation.
Formulas: [s,t] for equality; [B] for D binding x then y (y=index1,x=index2).
Every proof starts with the unconditional clause ([],[1,1]), universal reflexivity.
A context of n clauses permits free levels 1-n through -1; new level is -n.
Each i or e adds exactly one clause and one numerical scope.

Proofs:
 ["u", clause_index, term, premise]
Use the selected clause at term; prove its antecedent when nonempty.
An empty-antecedent clause deliberately ignores premise; null is conventional.
 ["i", matrix, witness, branch]
Introduce D(matrix). The local unconditional assumption is B(witness,eta),
with eta=fresh if the matrix is an equation and witness literally0, otherwise0.
The branch must infer B(witness,S fresh).
 ["e", major, auxiliary, branch]
Major must infer D B. Open its clause [B(b,0); y.B(b,y)] at fresh b.
In the extended context infer E from auxiliary and F from branch.
If E=s=t and F=I(S c,s;u.h)=r, output r=nf0(h[I(c,t;u.h)/u]);
otherwise output F. The inferred result must be free of b.
The auxiliary E may be any formula. A compound E forces identity fallback.
All inferred formulas, including exported results, undergo full syntax/scope validation.

The matched control has separate e=["e",major,branch] and x=["x",auxiliary,branch]
instructions; x uses the identical total evaluation map. Both kernels have
identical terms, formulas, initial axiom, Use, Introduce and public contract.

The finite tests do not substitute for the general mathematical proof.
