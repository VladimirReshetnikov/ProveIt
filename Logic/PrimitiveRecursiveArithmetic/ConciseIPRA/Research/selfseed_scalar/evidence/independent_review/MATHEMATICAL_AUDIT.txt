CHECKED MATHEMATICAL POINTS
--------------------------
1. Boolean induction bootstrap.
   D(0,a)=Sa and D(Sn,a)=a give the displayed Boolean truth tables, using
   epsilon's explicit {0,1} range. Actual equality reflection makes every
   source quantifier-free formula decidable. Its Boolean characteristic
   transfers contextual induction to quantifier-free matrices, including
   implication without using excluded middle for arbitrary formulas.

2. Predecessor orbit.
   For U_a(v)=D(epsilon(Sv,a),v), V(j,a)=F_Ua(j), the invariant
   a!=1 -> [V(j,a)=SSj OR S(V(j,a))=a] is a valid fixed-a induction.
   At a=SSSm,j=m, either disjunct gives V=SSm by S injectivity.
   The update then fixes it; three more steps prove W(SSSm)=SSm.
   The exceptional values W0=2,W1=2,W2=4 are correct.

3. Removal of the coverage axiom (independent audit contribution).
   The finite-case P graph is total and unique from equality cases alone;
   neither its definition nor W(SSSm)=SSm requires numerical coverage.
   Finite-case expansion transfers QF induction to ordinary terms using P.
   Induct jointly on
      (n=0 OR n=S(Pn)) AND P(Sn)=n.
   The base uses P1=0. At the step, coverage for Sn follows from P(Sn)=n.
   For P(SSn)=Sn, split the first old conjunct: n0 uses P2=1; otherwise
   n=S(Pn), so SSn=SSS(Pn) and W gives P(SSn)=SS(Pn)=Sn.
   Thus predecessor and functional coverage are derived together.
   This is a legitimate quantifier-free induction after finite-case
   elimination; it is not a standard-model argument.

4. Capped counting and discrete order.
   C(n,a)=SSn OR SSa and the cap-fixed property give
      L(n,Sb) iff [L(n,b) OR n=Sb].
   This unilateral law repairs the naive invalid parameter-changing
   induction for linearity. With a fixed, induct on a<=n OR n<=a;
   the second branch at a=Sb uses the unilateral law, not an IH for b.
   Antisymmetry uses Sn not<=n and the same target split. Transitivity
   is induction on c with a,b fixed in (a<=b AND b<=c)->a<=c.
   Consequently C(n,a)=SS min(n,a): if a<=n the cap definition gives
   SSa; if n<=a but the cap is reached, antisymmetry yields n=a;
   otherwise the orbit invariant gives SSn.

5. Source-body staging and bounded graph iteration.
   The finite-case extension theorem concerns ordinary finite terms only.
   It does NOT justify adding F bodies over opaque defined functions.
   The actual compiler correctly expands all graph cases first. Every
   candidate t_i is encoded and clipped by C(t_i,b), in [2,SSb], even
   when its guard is false. This matters: inactive candidates may be
   unbounded. Nested source Sel therefore has valid bounds uniformly.
   Encoded state u=SSv is decoded by finite source cases u2->0,u3->1,
   otherwise W(Wu). The two-sentinel source update has
      H0=B, H(SB)=SSB, H(SSB)=seed, H(valid)=compiled_update.
   Therefore F0=SB,F1=SSB,F2=seed, and F(SSn) is exactly n initialized
   steps. All candidates lie in [2,SSB]. This works also at raw cap0.
   No defined graph symbol remains unexplained in an F body.

6. Saturated addition and changing caps.
   A_c uses clipped successor and clipped initialization. Seed commutation
   is ordinary equation-induction uniqueness. It gives bounded argument
   commutativity. The diagonal identity A_Q(b)(n,n)=db(n), guarded by
   n<=b, uses two successors per step and db(Sn)<=db(b)<Q(b).
   Monotonicity then bounds A_Q(b)(n,a)<=db(b) for n,a<=b.
   Coherence between input bounds b,b' is guarded induction on
   n<=b AND n<=b', with a<=both fixed. Both bases are a and both next
   steps are genuine successors. Thus choosing the first maximum by
   finite cases remains correct when a successor changes the maximum.

7. Multiplication and pairing.
   The invariant v_j<=E(b+j) under j<=b is valid: v_j+a<=2E(b+j),
   whereas the next E value is 2E(b+j)+3. The cap E(Qb) dominates
   all stages since b+j<=db(b)<Qb. The square-root update preserves
   u^2<=z and u<=z. Its clock-or-fixed invariant guarantees a fixed
   state after z steps: if u_z=z, (Sz)^2>z already fixes it.
   Thus floor-root projections of kappa(x,y)=(x+y)^2+x are correct.
   All operations are total finite-case graphs; no minimization over
   an unbounded search domain has been used.

8. General PR majorants.
   D=K o H o G o Q is a literal monotone inflationary source unary
   term, with D(v)>=Sv. U=F_D is monotone and U(t)>=St.
   Seed <=K(Gb)<=D(b)<=D(Ub)=U(Sb).
   If z_j<=s=U(Sb+j) and j<=b, both projections and parameters are
   <=s. The next counter <=Ss<=Q(s); the new value <=H(G(Qs)).
   Hence the next pair <=D(s)=U(S(Sb+j)). The stage index is <=SQb,
   so the cap B(b)=U(SQb) prevents clipping.
   Counter/value laws are explicitly guarded by j<=b, with next-step
   equations only when Sj<=b. Two-cap coherence then proves the full
   graph recursion equations. At an arbitrary larger input bound,
   monotonicity of B or cap coherence gives the stated bound.

9. Both full-domain interpretation composites.
   Source F_h translates to one ordinary PR description, at each fixed
   finite syntax stage. Conversely, all PR descriptions receive total
   unique finite-case graphs. QF induction transfers by finite guard
   refinements, rather than merely by conservative definability.
   Uniqueness of each fixed recurrence proves the source-side and

   counters, including nonstandard ones. Equality characteristics agree

   Identity x=y is therefore the domain isomorphism. Ordinary intuitionistic

correctly use call-local k. A major proof sees the caller's g,k and cannot
