\begin{abstract}
We give a new proof presentation of the inherited scalar-iteration
axiomatization of full first-order intuitionistic primitive recursive
arithmetic. Every instruction now verifies a major premise. Instructions
that previously had no major use a persistent, proved truth. A simultaneous
induction on successful checking traces proves that every goal and every
context entry is array rooted. Consequently the checker itself excludes
scalar major expressions, including a false sentinel that would otherwise
permit an unsound equality rewrite. No extra input validator or trusted
well-formedness promise is required.

The clearer complete Python checker has 548 normalized bytes, 262
significant tokens and 303 syntax-tree nodes. A compact profile with the
same canonical consequence relation
has 537 bytes, 263 tokens and 308 nodes. Their maximum-compression gzip
sizes are respectively 347 and 342 bytes. The current supplied collection
has coordinatewise minima of 558 bytes, 262 tokens and 296 nodes, attained
by different programs. Thus both delivered checkers beat its source-byte
minimum, but neither is strictly smaller in every reported metric.

Canonical numerical and formula syntax, logical strength, and the
whole-domain bi-interpretation with iPRA are preserved. The report states
the entire primitive theory, proves the new trace and translation
theorems, and reproduces the attributed arithmetic and logical bridge.
The archive supplies executable certificates, adversarial controls,
source measurements and all 443 distinct Python sources in the pinned
comparison corpus. This establishes a structural reduction of the proof
presentation. It does not establish an absolute simplest axiomatization
or the requested major new arithmetic foundation.
\end{abstract}

\section{The result and its precise scope}
\label{sec:result}

The useful simplification is to make a premise compulsory. The old
checker first asks whether an instruction has a major premise. The new
checker always verifies its major; an instruction without substantive
premises names the empty-array truth already present in the context.
This removes a proof-control branch, rather than omitting the obligation
to validate a submitted major.

There is a real correctness issue here. The implementation uses Python's
false value as one nonmatching sentinel in its combined equality and
closure test. Numeric zero compares equal to that sentinel. Simply
deleting the old guard, or silently trusting a list-typed input interface,
would therefore leave a soundness obligation. The new successful-trace
invariant discharges it: no scalar goal can have a successful proof from
the array-rooted contexts generated by this checker. The recursive proof
check is itself enough to validate the relevant outer constructor.

\subsection{What is new and what is inherited}

The new contribution is the persistent neutral major, the relocation of
the case/progress marker, and the mutual trace invariant that validates
the resulting unguarded control flow. The complete native consequence
relation is unchanged. Scalar iteration, complementary paired
introductions, the Eq/Q/D logical encoding, the arity opener, constructed
computation assumptions, and the nonempty certified zero are inherited
from the supplied reports \cite{certified,arity,uniform}.

\section{The entire native theory}
\label{sec:native}

Here \(\ipra\) means full first-order intuitionistic primitive recursive
arithmetic, with its primitive-recursive function symbols, defining
equations, successor boundary laws, and quantifier-free induction.
Atomic induction is an equivalent convention in the expanded PR language;
Salehi uses the latter convention \cite{salehi}. Our scalar language
requires an internal equality-reflection argument before making that
conversion; the argument is included, not assumed.

\subsection{Numerical syntax and all nonlogical axioms}

There is one numerical sort. Terms are
\[
 t ::= x\mid 0\mid St\mid I(n,a;v.h).
\]
Only the state variable \(v\) in \(h\) is bound. All other parameters
are fixed during iteration. There are exactly two computation schemas:
\begin{align}
 I(0,a;v.h)&=a,\tag{I0}\label{eq:i0}\\
 I(Sn,a;v.h)&=h[I(n,a;v.h)/v].\tag{IS}\label{eq:is}
\end{align}
Substitution is capture free. These are staged finite descriptions:
for each fixed earlier term \(h(v,\vec p)\), an ordinary first-order
elaboration introduces \(i_h(n,a,\vec p)\) with those equations.
No numerical variable quantifies over functions, and no total universal
evaluator for primitive-recursive codes is postulated.

\subsection{Formula syntax and every primitive logical rule}

The native formulas are equations and a paired binder,
\[
 A ::= (s=t)\mid \D_{x,y}A.
\]
For convenient certificate contexts, also permit a closure \(\Q_yB\).
It is eliminable in favor of \(\D_{u,y}B\), with \(u\) unused.
Its intended ordinary meanings are
\[
 (\Q_yB)^\circ=B^\circ(0)\to\forall y\,B^\circ(y),
 \qquad
 (\D_{x,y}B)^\circ=
 \exists x\bigl(B^\circ(x,0)\to\forall y\,B^\circ(x,y)\bigr).
\]
Contexts \(\Gamma\) are finite reusable lists of formulas. There are
the two computation axiom schemas above and precisely the following
logical inference patterns:
\[
 \frac{}{\Gamma\vdash A}\ (A\in\Gamma),\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash s=t\quad\Gamma\vdash F(s)}
      {\Gamma\vdash F(t)},\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash\Q_yB(y)\quad\Gamma\vdash B(0)}
      {\Gamma\vdash B(t)}.
 \tag{Hyp, Tr, QE}
\]
Here \(F\) is any fixed native formula template, including quantified
ones. Equality transport is not restricted to atomic formulas. Paired
elimination is
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash\D_{x,y}B(x,y)\quad
       \Gamma,\Q_yB(a,y)\vdash A}{\Gamma\vdash A}.
 \tag{DE}
\]
The eigenparameter \(a\) is fresh for \(\Gamma,A\) and the fixed
parameters of \(B\). Paired introduction has two alternatives:
\[
 \frac{\Gamma,B(t,0)\vdash B(t,Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash\D_{x,y}B(x,y)},\qquad
 \frac{\Gamma,E(t,z)\vdash E(t,Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash\D_{x,y}E(x,y)}.
 \tag{DI-case, DI-progress}
\]
The progress alternative requires a literal numerical equation \(E\).
An equation beneath \(D\) or \(Q\) does not qualify. In each
alternative, \(z\) is fresh for the retained context, witness and
fixed parameters. The displayed local hypothesis is discharged.

This is the entire primitive theory \(\mathcal A\). No separate
intuitionistic connective axiom, reflexivity axiom, successor-injectivity
axiom, coverage axiom, falsity rule, or further induction schema is
primitive. The native contradiction is \(0=S0\). Weakening, exchange,
contraction, renaming, numerical substitution and ordinary-assumption
cut are admissible by induction on finite derivations, with the usual
renaming of eigenparameters before insertion.

\subsection{Derived administrative rules used by the checker}

A computation axiom can equivalently be supplied as a local hypothesis:
\[
 \frac{\Gamma,E\vdash A}{\Gamma\vdash A}
 \quad(E\hbox{ an I0 or IS instance}).\tag{Supply}
\]
The equivalence is Hyp in one direction and cut with the computation
theorem in the other. Since \(0=0\) is derived, it may be retained in
every context. A second admissible rule handles raw computation data:
\[
 \vdash H(\vec0),\qquad \Gamma,H(\vec b)\vdash A
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Gamma\vdash A,
 \qquad\vec b\notin\FV(\Gamma,A).
 \tag{Zero instance}
\]
Substitute zero for the fresh names in the second proof and cut in the
first. This rule is an ordinary finite proof transformation. It is not
a consistency principle, an oracle, or an assumption that \(H(\vec b)\)
is universally true.

\section{Why the theory is full intuitionistic PRA}
\label{sec:bridge-summary}

The full inherited derivations appear in the attributed appendices.
The essential constructions and the interpretation composites are
recorded here so the exact claim is visible in the main report.

\subsection{Recovering all intuitionistic connectives}

Put \(1=S0\), and define
\[
 C(v,a,b)=I(v,a;u.b),\qquad
 \delta(v)=C(v,1,0),\qquad
 p(z)=I(z,0;u.\delta(u)).
\]
Then \(C(0,a,b)=a\), \(C(Sv,a,b)=b\), \(p(1)=1\), and
\(p(2)=0\). On equations define
\[
 G_v(s=t)=(C(v,0,s)=C(v,0,t)),
 \quad
 M_v(s=t,r=q)=(C(v,s,r)=C(v,t,q)).
\]
Extend these operations through paired binders. Different binder depths
are aligned using the derived padding equivalence
\(A\dashv\vdash\D_{u,y}G_yA\), with \(u\) unused. Fixed formula
syntax recursion gives
\[
 \vdash G_0A,\quad G_{Sv}A\dashv\vdash A,\qquad
 M_0(A,B)\dashv\vdash A,\quad M_{Sv}(A,B)\dashv\vdash B.
\]
The full logical interface is therefore expressible by native formulas:
\begin{align*}
 A\Rightarrow_*B&=\D_{u,z}M_z(A,B),\\
 A\wedge_*B&=\D_{u,z}G_zM_{p(z)}(A,B),\\
 \exists_*x\,A(x)&=\D_{x,y}G_yA(x),\\
 A\vee_*B&=\exists_*x\,M_x(A,B),\\
 \forall_*x\,A(x)&=A(0)\wedge_*\D_{u,z}G_zA(z),\\
 \bot_*&=(0=1).
\end{align*}
All newly introduced variables are fresh. The zero conjunct in the
universal macro is essential. Conjunction projections use instances
one and two. Case introduction, DE and QE prove the ordinary
introduction and elimination rules for these macros. Explosion from
\(0=1\) gives every equation by transport through \(C\), then every
native formula by external induction on its finite syntax.

The unused-witness progress rule gives contextual equation induction:
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash E(0)\qquad\Gamma,E(z)\vdash E(Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash E(t)}.
\]
Case introduction gives numerical cases for arbitrary native formulas.
Thus this is full intuitionistic logic, including disjunction and
existential quantification, not merely its equational or negative part.
No excluded-middle rule for arbitrary quantified formulas has been added.

\subsection{Arithmetic reconstruction without circularity}

In ordinary intuitionistic first-order logic, let \(T_I\) have the
staged iterator equations, contextual equation induction, and
\[
 \forall x\,(x=0\vee\exists y\,x=Sy),\qquad
 (0=S0)\to\bot.
\]
This explicit falsity bridge is necessary in the ordinary realization:
positive equations alone admit a singleton model. The native logical
reconstruction interprets these principles in \(\mathcal A\).

The arithmetic dependency order is strict. First, scalar iteration and
equation induction construct predecessor \(P\), with
\(P0=0\) and \(P(Sx)=x\), using a factor map, an internally proved
comparison recurrence, and saturated iteration. Pairing and a faithful
equality detector are not assumed at this stage. Next, predecessor
gives monus and a characteristic of actual equality on the whole domain.
This converts each fixed quantifier-free formula into a characteristic
equation and recovers quantifier-free induction. Only then are pairing
\(\pi\) and projections \(L,H\) constructed.

For a fixed primitive recursion
\[
 f(0,\vec p)=g(\vec p),\qquad
 f(Sn,\vec p)=h(n,f(n,\vec p),\vec p),
\]
the scalar compiler is
\begin{align*}
 V(n,\vec p)&=I\bigl(n,\pi(0,g^*(\vec p));
 u.\pi(SL(u),h^*(L(u),H(u),\vec p))\bigr),\\
 f^*(n,\vec p)&=H(V(n,\vec p)).
\end{align*}
Internal equation induction proves \(L(V(n,\vec p))=n\) and the
desired defining equations. Passive parameters remain fixed during
each induction; diagonal substitution occurs only in a previously
proved universally parameterized identity.

\begin{theorem}[Whole-domain interpretation and both round trips]
The ordinary realization of \(\mathcal A\) and full first-order
\(\ipra\) are bi-interpretable and equiconsistent. Both domains are
the whole numerical domain, equality is actual equality, and both
definable composite isomorphisms have graph \(x=y\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Interpret each fixed scalar iterator in iPRA by primitive recursion
whose update ignores the counter. Interpret every fixed PR description
in the opposite direction with the scalar compiler above. The internal
predecessor, equality-reflection and characteristic proofs preserve the
successor axioms and every quantifier-free induction instance.

Equation induction proves uniqueness of each fixed recurrence with
parameters held fixed. By external induction on a finite description,
its double translation satisfies its original defining equations and
is therefore internally equal to the original operation at every
argument. The identity relation is internally total, bijective and
preserves all interpreted operations. This is a statement about all
internal elements, not just standard numerals.

The logical macros have their ordinary meanings. In the native theory,
\begin{align*}
 \Q_yB&\dashv\vdash B(0)\Rightarrow_*\forall_*yB(y),\\
 \D_{x,y}B&\dashv\vdash
 \exists_*x\bigl(B(x,0)\Rightarrow_*\forall_*yB(x,y)\bigr).
\end{align*}
In the forward paired direction, DE exposes the conditional closure;
under its base, QE gives every instance, and the derived logical
introductions apply. In the reverse direction, eliminate the
existential and use case introduction under its base. Structural
induction on formula syntax gives both formula composites. Falsity is
preserved in both directions by the displayed bridge. Effective finite
proof translations give equiconsistency; no self-consistency theorem
is asserted.
\end{proof}

\section{The complete implementation and its input language}
\label{sec:implementation}

The following is the entire recommended checker. The explanatory
\file{code/readable_checker.py} is an expansion of it, not a dependency.
\lstinputlisting[language=Python]{code/checker.py}

Input is one JSON array. Its final element is the requested closed
formula; earlier four-field packets are consumed from the end. An unused
prefix has no effect. Acceptance means both exit status zero and exactly
\texttt{ACCEPT} followed by a newline. Exceptions reject. No trusted
\texttt{assert} is disabled by Python's optimization mode.

\subsection{Sorted syntax and opening}

The canonical encodings are
\[
 \D_{x,y}B\mapsto[B],\qquad s=t\mapsto[s,t],
 \qquad\Q_yB\mapsto[B,B,B].
\]
Numerical and formula interpretations are sorted. Their array meanings
are as follows; ternary formulas use their last field as the matrix.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cll}\toprule
Arity & Numerical meaning & Formula meaning\\\midrule
0 & zero & \(0=0\)\\
1 & successor & paired binder \(D\)\\
2 & zero annotation & equation\\
3 & initialized scalar iterator & conditional closure \(Q\)\\
at least 4 & zero & \(0=0\)\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Scalar padding at formula positions may retain the inherited truth
reading. An optional falsity reading is also sound, but unnecessary
for the reduction. Scalar expressions are never successful goals in
the recommended checker; they are not added to its canonical object
language.

At numerical root depth three, coordinates \(0,1,2\) denote zero.
Under protected slots, \(3+2j\) and \(4+2j\) name slot \(j\),
counted from the innermost slot. Admitted negative labels name rigid
parameters. Formula root depth is \(2p\), and numerical root depth
is \(3+2p\), under \(p\) protected slots. The child-depth increment
is uniformly
\[
                  d\longmapsto d+5-\operatorname{arity}.
\]
A unary numerical successor carries two dummy zero slots. The counter
and initializer of an iterator carry one dummy zero slot; its update
uses that slot for the genuine previous state. A paired formula has
slots \(y,x\), and a closure has slot \(y\). The named producer
performs the corresponding shifts in canonical encodings.

The opener preserves a scalar in \(k\le a<d\). At a numerical
position, an escaping scalar is a substitution hole whose image is
validated at numerical root depth three. At a formula position,
escaping scalar padding becomes zero. Array annotations are traversed
even when they are semantically ignored; a successful traversal preserves
their constant meaning. Failed validation rejects rather than silently
assuming that raw data have the canonical shape.

\subsection{Instruction meaning}

Every packet is \([m,s,u,t]\), and every packet first proves \(m\).
The canonical instructions are:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lX}\toprule
Kind & Fields and effect\\\midrule
Elimination & Nonempty \(m\) is the actual equality, closure or paired
major. A matching endpoint rewrites the goal; its export is \(m*3\).\\
Computation & \(m=[]\), \(u\) a nonempty initializer. Falsy \(s\)
selects zero computation; otherwise \(s\) is a successor predecessor.
The update is \(t\).\\
Case introduction & \(m=[]\), \(u=[]\); \(t\) is the witness.
Retain the zero instance of the opened matrix.\\
Progress introduction & \(m=[]\), \(u=0\); \(t\) is the witness.
Retain the arbitrary-index instance only for a literal equation.\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
In introductions, \(s\) is unused. Ordinary literal-zero witnesses
are permitted. The persistent empty-array premise closes by Hyp and
consumes no instruction. The proof-format change need not shorten
serialized certificates or improve checking speed; it reduces the
implementation of the checker.

\subsection{Why excluded computation inputs lose no theorems}

The current certified-zero draft already proves
\[
                         \zeta=I(2,1;v.0)=0.
\]
Its direct successor computation has nonempty predecessor and initializer.
For a computation leaf with a forbidden literal-zero initializer, or a
successor predecessor literally zero, replace exactly those positions
simultaneously by a fresh variable \(w\), obtaining a motive \(E(w)\).
Then \(E(\zeta)\) is an allowed direct computation. Transport through
\(\zeta=0\) derives \(E(0)\), the desired original leaf. Affected
leaves use three native inference nodes: two computations and one
transport. Passive zeros inside the fixed update are left unchanged.
This inherited normalization is independent of the new uniform-premise
argument, and is included to make the arithmetic dispatch complete.

\section{Validation, counterchecks and reproducibility}
\label{sec:validation}

The exact delivered primary and compact checkers passed 184 full-process
expectations: 46 cases, two programs, and ordinary plus optimized Python.
Each mode/profile combination includes 20 accepted and 26 rejected
certificates. The named producer separately regenerates and replays the
31 inherited core cases. The cases include genuine atomic induction,
conditional-closure specialization, nonvacuous paired elimination,
nested binders, contextual induction, escaping variables, malformed
data, nonempty-zero repair, and the fresh-computation zero-instance
boundary. Finite tests supplement the trace and interpretation proofs;
they do not establish universal soundness on their own.

An independent review tested a closely related explicit-list candidate
before selection of the ordinary conditional: 22,248 negative checks
and nine complete CLI controls had no mismatches. Its exact source hash
and code are retained as \file{reference/reviewed_boolean_checker.py}.
This evidence is not mislabeled as a separate 22,248-case run of the
final conditional file. The inherited arithmetic bridge was also
reviewed for fixed-parameter induction and circular dependencies.
The pairing appendix abbreviates routine order/semiring derivations;
none of these written proofs is a Lean or Rocq formalization.

\subsection{A mutation that really proves a false equation}

The following raw certificate requests \(1=0\):
\begin{lstlisting}
[
  [[], [0], [0], 0],
  [false, [[[0]], [0], 0], [0], [3, 0]],
  [[0], 0]
]
\end{lstlisting}
Both delivered checkers reject it. Delete only the unconditional
\texttt{c(m, g)} line: both mutants accept the false conclusion, in
ordinary and optimized Python. The false major now collides with the
false sentinel in the combined matcher and rewrites the goal without
an equality proof. Thus the omitted guard has been replaced by a real
proof obligation, not by trusting the input. These four mutation checks
are part of the default verification command.

\subsection{Canonical completeness has an explicit boundary}

Sound decoding of an accepted raw expression does not imply completeness
for every arbitrary raw spelling of a true formula. The raw expression
\texttt{[0]} has scalar padding as its paired body. Its short introduction
attempt rejects at the matrix length check. Under the optional bottom
decoder its meaning is \(D(\bot)\), which is true, but this is not its
canonical spelling. The canonical \(D(0=S0)\), encoded by
\texttt{[[0,[0]]]}, has the expected accepted case proof. The general
completeness transformation starts with a native derivation, regenerates
its certificate in the new context, and may prune truth subproofs. It
does not promise to preserve every historical raw packet stream.

The selected programs use explicit finite-tree list recognition and do
not use the generic iterable trick in some comparison profiles. As with
ordinary recursive proof checkers, mathematical completeness assumes
adequate resources; finite CPython recursion and memory limits can reject
a sufficiently large valid certificate.

\section{Assessment of the requested breakthrough}

The new mechanism is a proved reduction in the complete proof
presentation: every instruction has a major, the trivial one is already
proved, and successful proof checking enforces the required constructor
class. This is an advance over the pinned implementations and gives a
small source-byte record within that collection.

It leaves the arithmetic and logical basis intact. It does not show
that the native theory is absolutely simplest, that the kernel is
optimal under a fixed universal description language, or that one
implementation beats all current source-byte, token and AST minima.
No major new foundation meeting the stronger part of the request was
established in this investigation. Calling the measured reduction such
a breakthrough would overstate the result.

\bibitem{certified}
\emph{A certified zero removes a computation opcode}.
\file{ipra_certified_zero_2026-11-10.zip}, in \cite{corpus}.

certified-zero normalization, the direct predecessor computation
generator, the attributed bridge appendices, and the baseline fixtures.

\bibitem{arity}
\emph{An Arity Calculus for iPRA}.

Source of the unary paired binder, triplicate closure, numerical root
depth three and uniform arity opener.
