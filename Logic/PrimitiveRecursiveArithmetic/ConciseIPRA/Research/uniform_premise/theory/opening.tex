\section{Opening, arithmetic generation and the exact scope boundary}
\label{sec:opening-details}

This section restates the inherited opening and generated-computation
arguments \cite{arity,certified} for the selected instruction format.
It explains the semantic facts used in the new trace proof; it does not
charge an external validator to the untrusted producer.

\subsection{An explicit decoder for admitted numerical coordinates}

At a meaningful numerical position with \(p\) protected slots, the
depth is \(3+2p\). Nonnegative coordinates in \([0,3)\) denote zero.
For \(0\le j<p\), coordinates in \([3+2j,5+2j)\) denote slot \(j\),
starting with the innermost slot. Thus a canonical integer slot has
the two spellings \(3+2j,4+2j\). This interval convention also covers
noninteger JSON coordinates. Negative admitted numbers are distinct
rigid parameters. Python's booleans act as coordinate values zero and
one; both coordinates decode to numerical zero. Values that fail the comparison,
including nonfinite coordinates, can only survive at meaningful
numerical positions by being replaced by a validated image; they are
not extra numerical constants in the object language.

At a formula position scalar padding is the chosen constant formula
reading. The numerical reading of a unary successor's child uses two
additional zero slots. An iterator's counter and initializer use one
additional zero slot; its update uses one state slot. A paired matrix
has the two slots \(y,x\), and a closure matrix has slot \(y\).
These are exactly the environments described by the array table.
Surplus numerical arrays denote zero and surplus formula arrays denote
truth, independently of their annotations.

\begin{lemma}[Semantic opening]
For every successful opening at a meaningful numerical root
\(3+2p\) or formula root \(2p\), its decoded result is capture-free
filling of the distinguished numerical hole in the decoded template.
Protected slots and admitted rigid parameters retain their meaning.
A successful no-image check preserves the meaning of a closed expression.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the finite traversed tree. The scalar interval comparison
protects exactly the displayed slots and admitted names. Outside that
interval, a relevant numerical occurrence is filled by the image. The
image is checked at numerical root depth three: it therefore has no
free positive slot, and its free numerical parameters are rigid negative
names. Copying it under additional slots captures none of these names.
Its own internal binders retain their relative indices.

At a meaningful unary, binary or ternary array, the depth increments
\(4,3,2\) create exactly the environments in the decoding table.
An ignored child has no effect on the mathematical meaning. Traversal
of an ignored annotation can fail and reject a certificate; if it
succeeds, the relevant constant meaning remains unchanged. In
particular, negative child depths inside a surplus array do not require
a fictitious numerical interpretation of those ignored children.
These cases prove both filling and validation. Structural equality of
successfully checked raw results consequently implies equality of
their decoded meanings.
\end{proof}

No claim is needed that every raw tree is idempotent under every later
revalidation. Surplus arrays can have odd validation behavior in ignored
annotations while still having a constant meaning. The lemma is stated
only at meaningful sorted positions and for successful calls.

\subsection{Why fresh names cannot escape into a retained conclusion}

The global floor only decreases. When its old value is \(K\), its
next allocated integer label is \(K-1\). Earlier relevant goals and
context entries have passed no-image validation, so they contain no
relevant numerical occurrence below their then-current floor. They
therefore contain none of the names newly admitted in \([K-1,K)\).
An occurrence inside an ignored annotation is not a free variable of
the decoded formula.

For integral canonical data there is one new label. For arbitrary
finite raw numerical data, finitely many additional fractional names
may lie in the interval. They are distinct fresh parameters, not
aliases of its integer endpoint. Whenever a generated assumption
uses them, the zero-instance discharge replaces every one of them
simultaneously by zero. The retained context and conclusion are fixed.

\subsection{The generated computation equation}

With empty major and nonempty initializer \(u\), the raw assumption is
\[
 H=\begin{cases}
 [\,[s,u,t],u\,],&s\text{ falsy},\\
 [\,[[s],u,t],\ i(t,[s,u,t],3)\,],&s\text{ truthy}.
 \end{cases}
\]
It is always a binary formula array. Its final image may be zero or
the fresh parameter, as selected by the common tail of the code.

\begin{lemma}[Zero-instance computation]
If the generated expression and the subsequent checks succeed, the
decoded assumption after simultaneous zero substitution for the newly
admitted names is an instance of I0 or IS.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In the falsy predecessor case, every successfully checked meaningful
numerical predecessor is a zero representation. Empty arrays, zero
numbers and false denote zero; a null, string or mapping in a meaningful
numerical position fails. Both endpoint readings of the initializer
agree after the new names and unprotected holes become zero. This is I0.

In the truthy case the left counter is explicitly a successor.
If the update depends on its state in a relevant position, opening
\(t\) at root three inserts the source \([s,u,t]\). The insertion
first validates that entire source at numerical root three. In
particular it revisits its update under precisely one state slot;
an extra escaping update coordinate fails. It also checks counter
and initializer with their prescribed dummy zero slot. The source
therefore has exactly the state interpretation needed by the
recursive value on the right.

Additional dummy slots inside the explicitly constructed successor
do not change the decoded predecessor: closed relevant positive
coordinates denote those zero slots, and rigid parameters are
unchanged. Semantic opening now identifies the right endpoint with
the update applied to that recursive predecessor value. This is IS.
If the update does not use its state, its zero-normalized decoded
value is independent of the unused image on both sides, which again
gives IS. Checks performed only inside ignored annotations can
reject more data but cannot change this successful decoded equation.
\end{proof}

The zero-instance conclusion is all that the continuation requires.
The admissible zero-instance rule simultaneously replaces every newly
admitted name by zero and then discharges the generated assumption.
No assertion that every raw open assumption is uniformly true is
needed. In particular, if the raw initializer is coordinate \(3\),
the selected format opens its separate endpoint at zero: the tail test
\texttt{len(a)\%2 == u} is then false.

\subsection{The paired slots and the induction restriction}

For a unary \(D\) goal, \texttt{i(*a,t,2)} opens its witness slot
at the numerical witness \(t\) and retains the index slot. The final
goal opening with \texttt{[k]} supplies the successor of the fresh
index. The local hypothesis is the arbitrary-index instance when
\(u=0\) and the opened matrix has even arity; otherwise it is the
zero instance. The only nontrivial even-arity formula constructor is
the binary equation. Other even arities denote truth. Thus no
induction on a nontrivial compound formula is silently admitted.

For a unary major \([B]\), triplication gives \([B,B,B]\). Opening
this at the fresh name leaves its index bound and replaces the
existential witness slot. The continuation is exactly DE. For a
nonunary nonempty major, the export has at least six fields and
denotes a harmless truth. These inherited slot calculations continue
to hold when the verified empty major replaces the optional-premise
opcode.
