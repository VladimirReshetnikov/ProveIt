% Independent proof audit of the selected recursive-formula packet checker.
\section{Proof audit of the selected packet certificate format}

\subsection{Syntax and context invariant}
Numerical terms use \(0\), negative free levels, positive bound indices,
a singleton successor, and a triple iterator with a nonzero literal
counter. Formula codes are endpoint pairs \([s,t]\) and
\([\mathtt d,B]\). The string \(\mathtt d\) is not a numerical term.
Every opening accounts for the one iterator binder or two \(D\)
binders and contracts newly exposed zero-counter iterators.

A context entry is a conditional clause \((A,B)\), read
\(A\to\forall yB(y)\), where \(y\) is bound only in \(B\).
An unconditional Horn clause is represented using the neutral antecedent
\(H=(0=0)\). In particular a local ordinary assumption \(A\) becomes
\((H,A)\), with its clause variable unused. The fixed ambient clause
\(\forall y(y=y)\) is implemented automatically and is not included in
the user-selectable context index list.

The scope of a reachable checker state is exactly the context length.
The root has neither an explicit clause nor a free parameter.
Only Open and Close extend a context, and each adds exactly one clause
and one fresh numerical level. Thus the equation
\[
 \mathrm{scope}=|\Gamma|
\]
is preserved by every recursive call. This invariant permits omission
of a separate scope argument; it does not identify arbitrary contexts
and scopes in the abstract calculus.

At scope \(m\), every context antecedent is well scoped at \(m\), and
every body is well scoped with its one additional clause binder.
The checker validates each requested goal at that scope before doing
anything else.

\subsection{Automatic universal reflexivity}
After validation, the test that the two top-level fields of the goal
are equal is exactly a reflexive-equality test. For a \(D\) formula the
first field is the string \(\mathtt d\), whereas its second field is a
formula list, so they cannot be equal. For an equality, equality of the
two fields is literal identity of its numerical endpoints.
Hence automatic acceptance uses only the fixed universal reflexivity
clause. It consumes no packet.

In particular \(H=(0=0)\) is always discharged automatically.
The implementation can therefore check every clause antecedent
uniformly, including those representing unconditional Horn clauses,
without a dummy certificate or a branch for missing antecedents.
The axiom of universal reflexivity remains counted in the complete
specification; it has not been derived from Step alone.

\subsection{Four two-field packet forms}
A packet is \([b,d]\), consumed from the right:
\begin{enumerate}
\item If \(b\geq0\) is an integer, it selects context clause \(\Gamma_b\);
\(d\) must be a numerical target. The requested goal must equal that
clause body's normalized instance at \(d\). Its antecedent is then
checked recursively. If that antecedent is \(H\), this consumes no
additional packet.
\item If \(b=-1\), the requested goal must be a \(D\) formula and \(d\)
must be a numerical witness. Close checks its successor instance at
one new level under its local ordinary assumption.
\item If \(b\) is a \(D\) formula, first check \(b\). Require the second
field to be exactly the integer zero, then Open its fresh witness and
check the original goal under the canonical exported clause.
\item If \(b\) is an equality, first check \(b\), then check the formula
\(d\). The two formulas must be the Step premises
\[
 I(c,a;u.h)=t,\qquad I(Sc,a;u.h)=r,
\]
using the zero-normal convention for the first source. The requested
goal must be exactly \(r=h\langle t/u\rangle\).
\end{enumerate}
The cases are determined by validated arithmetic types and the
distinguished \(D\) tag. The Step minor pattern is an equation whose
left side is an iterator with successor counter; it cannot also be a
\(D\) formula. All equality checks are literal checks of finite JSON
data after the specified opening, not appeals to an external solver.

\subsection{Soundness: the suffix-consumption invariant}
Suppose \(\operatorname{check}(G,\Gamma)\) returns successfully from a
well-scoped reachable context, starting with packet list \(P\) and
leaving \(P'\). Then \(P'\) is a prefix of \(P\), and the consumed suffix
determines a finite derivation of \(G\) from the readings of \(\Gamma\)
and the fixed universal reflexivity clause. The unconsumed prefix
provides no assumptions.

Prove this by the finite call tree. Automatic reflexivity is the
zero-packet case. Every other call removes the last packet first.
Its recursive premise calls consume consecutive preceding suffixes
and, by induction, supply the premise derivations of Use, Close,
Open or Step. The goal comparisons ensure that these inference
conclusions are exactly the goals requested by their callers.

For Open, both the requested goal and the proved major have been
validated at the old scope, before introduction of level \(-m-1\).
That level therefore cannot occur in the outer goal or major.
For Close, its \(D\) goal and witness are validated at the old scope;
the local assumption and successor target are then constructed with
the new level. Retained negative free levels are absolute, so enlarging
scope does not rename or reinterpret any old variable.
These checks enforce both eigenvariable conditions.

Every nonautomatic call consumes a packet before recursing. Therefore
a finite certificate yields a finite attempted call tree; requesting
an absent packet or violating a condition rejects the input.
Host-language resource limits can prevent checking an excessively
large derivation, but cannot turn a failed inference into acceptance.

At the top level the last array entry is the declared goal, checked
with an empty explicit context and hence scope zero. Successful
completion also requires the packet array to be empty. Thus the
checker proves a closed declared formula and rejects all unused input.

\subsection{Completeness: an explicit serializer}
Every finite derivation in the four-rule Horn presentation can be
serialized. First prune every subderivation with a literal reflexive
equality as its conclusion, replacing it by the fixed ambient-clause
proof. All such subderivations then require no packets.

Let \(E(p)\) be the packet list of a pruned derivation, in storage order.
Concatenation is denoted by juxtaposition. Write
\(\langle b,d\rangle\) for a singleton list containing one packet
\([b,d]\). Then
\begin{align*}
 E(\mathrm{Refl})&=[],\\
 E(\mathrm{Use}_i(d,p))&=E(p)\,\langle i,d\rangle
       &&\text{for a conditional clause},\\
 E(\mathrm{Use}_i(d))&=\langle i,d\rangle
       &&\text{for an unconditional clause},\\
 E(\mathrm{Close}_d(p))&=E(p)\,\langle-1,d\rangle,\\
 E(\mathrm{Open}_{B}(p_{\rm major},p_{\rm branch}))
   &=E(p_{\rm branch})\,E(p_{\rm major})\,\langle B,0\rangle,\\
 E(\mathrm{Step}_{B,C}(p_{\rm major},p_{\rm minor}))
   &=E(p_{\rm minor})\,E(p_{\rm major})\,\langle B,C\rangle.
\end{align*}
Here \(B,C\) are the actual premise formulas, not proof terms.
For unconditional Use, lowering the clause antecedent to \(H\) adds
an automatic reflexivity premise and no packet.
The checker processes a major before its minor or branch; this is why
the major's serialized proof lies immediately to the left of the outer
packet.

Induction on \(p\) proves the stronger statement that checking its
conclusion consumes exactly \(E(p)\) and leaves any preceding packet
prefix unchanged. Append the closed conclusion as the final JSON item
to obtain the complete input. The ambient reflexivity clause is not
indexed, so a translation from a representation that stores it at
index zero subtracts one from all remaining context indices.
Fresh negative levels are renamed according to the current depth of
nested Open/Close branches; this is admissible injective fresh renaming.

Conversely, a successful checker call reconstructs its derivation from
the actual call tree by the soundness argument. Thus packet and tree
formats prove exactly the same closed formulas.
The initial-clause interpretation and the exact Horn equivalence extend
this to the native scalar calculus and its iPRA bi-interpretation.

\subsection{What the protocol simplifies}
The conclusion already specifies the term needed for reflexivity,
the \(D\) matrix needed for introduction, and the desired target of an
elimination. Only information not determined by the goal remains in a
two-field packet. A single stack order encodes proof-tree boundaries.
There is no proof search, code evaluation, or arithmetic theorem oracle.
The improvement is in the complete certificate checker and its data
representation; its mathematical axioms are still stated explicitly.
