% Optional mathematical quotient. Not a measured-checker claim.
\section{Optional quotient: recover public depth from variable support}

\subsection{Support and the public normal form}
For a numerical term \(t\), let \(\mu(t)\) be the greatest positive
de Bruijn index free relative to its own iterator binders, with value
zero when there is none. Thus
\[
 \mu(j)=\max(j,0),\quad
 \mu(St)=\mu(t),\quad
 \mu(I(n,a;u.h))=\max(\mu(n),\mu(a),\max(\mu(h)-1,0)).
\]
Zero here also denotes the arithmetic constant. In a formula at depth
\(2k\), these free positive indices refer to its surrounding \(D\)
binders; negative indices still refer to external numerical parameters.

For a well-scoped ranked formula \(F=(k,s,t)\), put
\[
 r(F)=\left\lceil\frac{\max(\mu(s),\mu(t))}{2}\right\rceil.
\]
Then \(r(F)\leq k\). Define its public normalization by
\[
 P(k,s,t)=
 \begin{cases}
 (s,t),&r(F)=k,\\
 (0,0),&r(F)<k.
 \end{cases}
\]
An unranked public pair \(a=(s,t)\) is decoded as
\[
 U(a)=(r,s,t),\qquad
 r=\left\lceil\frac{\max(\mu(s),\mu(t))}{2}\right\rceil.
\]
All numerical terms are zero-normal before \(P\) is applied. The pair
\((0,0)\) decodes to the ordinary equality \(0=0\).

\begin{lemma}[The only lost public formulas are theorems]
For every well-scoped ranked formula \(F\), the old calculus proves
\(F\) and \(U(P(F))\) interderivable, with arbitrary retained context.
Also \(P(U(a))=a\) for every well-formed public pair \(a\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
When \(r(F)=k\), the first assertion is literal identity.
When \(r(F)<k\), both variables of the outermost \(D\), whose indices
are \(2k\) and \(2k-1\), are absent from both endpoint terms.
Hence \(F=D_{x,y}B\) where \(x,y\) are absent from \(B\).
Its ordinary reading is \(\exists x(B\to B)\), so it is a theorem.

There is also a native proof without appealing to ordinary logic:
Close at witness \(0\) has the same formula \(B\) as local assumption
and target, whatever mode its test selects. Ordinary assumption use
closes this premise. Thus \(F\) is a theorem. Its public normalization
\(0=0\) is also a theorem, so each follows under the other's assumption
by weakening. The last assertion follows because \(U\) uses exactly
the support-derived rank that \(P\) tests.
\end{proof}

This quotient deliberately contracts only the case where both variables
of the outer \(D\) are absent. Some other theorems, such as
\(D_{x,y}(x=x)\), are retained; no completeness of the tautology detector
is claimed.

\subsection{Internal clauses must retain rank}
The normal form is suitable for public formulas. It does not determine
the rank of a clause body with one extra external bound variable.
For example the same positive index \(1\) can be the distinguished
clause variable of a rank-zero body or the innermost variable of a
rank-one body with an unused clause variable. Therefore internal clause
bodies retain their explicit rank (or would need a different,
equally explicit binder representation).

A precise quotient calculus can retain fully ranked internal clauses
\([A_1,\ldots,A_m;y.B]\), with \(m\in\{0,1\}\), while judgments have
unranked public pairs. Use asks for proofs of \(P(A_i)\) and returns
\(P(B\langle t/y\rangle)\). Step acts on public pairs of inferred rank
zero. Open decodes a nonzero-rank public major, exports its ranked
canonical clause, and checks a branch for the original public goal.
Close decodes a nonzero-rank public goal, installs its ranked ordinary
assumption, and asks for the packed successor instance. The initial
clause remains ranked \([\,\,;(0,1,1)]\).
A variant may pack antecedents as well, provided conversions of their
equivalent readings are included in the same argument.

Only genuine formulas, with all extra clause binders instantiated,
are passed to \(P\). Calling \(P\) prematurely on an open clause body
would confuse its external clause variable with a logical binder.

\subsection{Soundness in the ranked calculus}
Interpret a public pair by \(U\), and retain the ordinary readings of
internal ranked clauses. The preceding lemma converts any proof of
\(U(P(A))\) into one of \(A\), and conversely.

For Use, convert each packed antecedent proof to its ranked antecedent,
apply old clause use, and convert the result to its packed version.
For Step, all formulas have rank zero and no packing change is made.
For Close, convert the local unnormalized base to the packed assumption
used by its branch, and convert the packed target back to the true
successor instance. Apply old Close. The decoded public goal has its
support-derived rank, so it packs back to the same public goal.
For Open, the decoded public major is an actual ranked \(D\); old Open
exports exactly the internal ranked clause. Its branch conclusion is
the decoded public goal and has the required fresh-variable condition.

These conversions are formal cut/weakening operations in the old
calculus. If internal antecedents have also been packed, replace a use
of the exported antecedent by the corresponding conversion; the
pointwise body instances are unchanged. Hence each quotient rule is
sound under arbitrary side assumptions.

\subsection{Completeness and a collapsed elimination major}
Transform an old derivation recursively, packing its public conclusion.
Use and Step transform directly. For a Close whose goal survives
packing, the same Close applies, with packed local hypothesis and
target. If its goal collapses, Use of the initial reflexivity clause
at \(0\) proves its normalized goal \(0=0\), and the premise may be
discarded. Open transforms directly whenever its major survives.

It remains to handle an old Open whose major \(D_{x,y}B\) collapses.
Then \(x,y\) are absent from \(B\), so the exported clause is exactly
\([B;y.B]\). Every use of this identity clause returns the same packed
formula as its own antecedent proof. Delete that Use and retain its
antecedent derivation. Context weakening and index adjustment remove
the obsolete clause occurrence from the remainder of the branch.
The branch may still use the formerly fresh eigenvariable \(b\) in
intermediate numerical terms, even though its outer conclusion does
not contain \(b\). It must not simply be treated as a closed-scope proof.

To discharge that parameter, use the fixed surviving theorem
\[
 T_D=D_{x,y}(x=x).
\]
It has rank \(1\) and support index \(2\), so this quotient does not
collapse it. Close at witness zero proves \(T_D\): both progress
instances are \(0=0\), supplied by reflexivity or the local assumption.
Open \(T_D\) with a fresh \(b\). Its extra clause
\([b=b;y.(b=b)]\) is harmless. Weaken the previously transformed branch
into that clause and conclude its old-scope goal. This replaces the
collapsed-major Open with a valid Open of \(T_D\), preserving the
eigenvariable scope without a substitution-stability assumption.

This proves completeness by induction on the old proof tree. The
local transformation deleting an identity clause is itself a structural
induction on the transformed branch: its selected Uses are replaced by
their antecedent proofs, and all other nodes are translated recursively.
The fresh parameter is therefore discharged by an actual inference,
not erased by convention.

\subsection{Substitution, ordinary interpretations, and limitations}
Numerical hereditary opening is performed first. It may erase the
highest variable occurrences by contracting a zero iterator; \(P\)
must then be recomputed on the resulting endpoints. The lost formula
is again an outer-vacuous \(D\) and the displayed theorem lemma applies.
Keeping a stale support-derived depth after substitution would be
incorrect.

The old-to-new and new-to-old theorem equivalences above are effective.
Their formula composite on the old side is \(F\mapsto U(P(F))\), proved
equivalent to \(F\) by the theorem lemma; the other composite is literal
identity. The ordinary numerical domain and actual equality are
unchanged. Composing these translations with the baseline
bi-interpretation yields bi-interpretability with iPRA and
equiconsistency. No induction is needed for the new vacuity lemma.

This is a genuine logical quotient, unlike mere spine flattening, but
it does not automatically make the complete checker smaller. Computing
relative positive-variable support and keeping internal clause ranks
can cost more than the public rank field it removes. A measured
implementation is required before claiming a complexity reduction.
