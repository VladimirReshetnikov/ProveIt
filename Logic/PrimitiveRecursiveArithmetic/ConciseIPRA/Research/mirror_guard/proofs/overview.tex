\section{Result and comparison target}
\label{sec:overview}

The comparison is with the supplied repository at the exact commit in
\cite{snapshot}, including the newly formalized no-literal-zero-source
theorem.  The baseline is therefore not an earlier version that still
requires formula transport or witnessed equation progress as primitive.
Its current native presentation and its smallest Python profiles are
distinguished: the Lean kernel favors an uncomplicated structural
metatheory, whereas the compact programs exploit a common arity codec.

The report uses the established clause presentation of the native core:
$Q_yB$ is a reusable resource, and its formula representation is
$D_{u,y}B$ with unused $u$.  This is connected to the current Lean
Eq/$Q$/$D$ kernel by the inherited equivalence, not a claim that the
current kernel lacks $Q$.

The present change concerns the logical interface, not the numerical
domain or the expressive power of the scalar iterator.  Keep
\[
 t ::= x\mid 0\mid S(t)\mid I(n,a;u.h),
 \qquad C(v,a,b):=I(v,a;u.b)
\]
with $u$ fresh in the selector abbreviation.  The previous guarded
language allowed an atomic matrix immediately beneath $\D_{x,y}$ only
in the form
\[
                 I(y,0;u.h)=I(y,0;u.k).
\]
Our atomic matrices instead have the form
\[
                 \boxed{\ a=I(y,a;u.h)\ }.
\tag{M}\label{eq:overview-mirror}
\]
The initializer $a$ may depend on both $x,y$ and passive parameters;
$h$ may also depend on its state variable $u$.  The zero instance is
the reverse of one computation axiom.  One iterator is displayed rather
than two, but no uniform claim is made about expanded term length:
the initializer is repeated.

For an arbitrary equation $E\equiv s=t$, the useful particular guard is
\[
                H_y E\ :=\ s=C(y,s,t).
\]
Its zero instance is provable; its successor instances are equivalent
to $E$.  The essential detail is that $s$ remains on the left of the
equation.  In the restricted calculus an assumption $0=t$ can therefore
be padded without first using the forbidden transport from $0$.
This permits both reductions at once:
\begin{enumerate}
\item There is one introduction schema: it retains the progress
instance at an equation and the zero instance at a compound matrix.
No case/progress choice remains in the certificate.
\item A transport major $s=t$ must have $s\not\equiv0$ as finite syntax.
It is the source field that distinguishes equation transport from
closure specialization in the compact certificate protocol.
\end{enumerate}
Both points have predecessors in the supplied repository.  The new
mathematics is their compatibility with the simpler, oppositely oriented
guard and the proof that establishes it without a substitution
circularity.  A plain combination of the earlier guards and the source
restriction would not establish the same theorem.

\paragraph{Meaning of the strength claim.}
We prove effective translations of all sequents, with formula round
trips, between the new presentation $\Mcal$ and the inherited native
calculus $\Ccal$.  The ordinary reading of every canonical paired
formula remains uniform:
\[
  (\D_{x,y}B)^\circ
       =\exists x\bigl(B^\circ(x,0)\to\forall y\,B^\circ(x,y)\bigr).
\]
After composing with the existing $\Ccal\leftrightarrow\Teq
\leftrightarrow\ipra$ bridge, both interpretation domains are defined
by $x=x$, equality is actual equality, and the numerical isomorphisms
are defined by $x=y$.  Thus the result is stronger than agreement on
standard numerical computations or an isolated consistency reduction.

\paragraph{Meaning of the size claim.}
We follow the repository's complete-module convention: remove genuine
docstrings, normalize by \texttt{ast.unparse}, append one newline, then
count significant tokens, AST nodes, and UTF-8 bytes.  JSON parsing is
the only imported service.  Gzip at compression level 9 is reported
separately for information.  Different complete programs may attain
different coordinates; their minima must not be attributed to a
single nonexistent program.  The strict-goal profile, which also
rejects all noncanonical public statements, is measured in full.

\paragraph{Verification boundary.}
The new metatheorems below are written proofs.  The Python checks test
the concrete implementations and certificates.  The inherited bridge
is present in the repository's Lean development~\cite{lean}; it was
inspected but not rebuilt in this environment.  No claim of a new Lean
or Rocq verification, a universal lower bound, or literature-wide
priority is made.
