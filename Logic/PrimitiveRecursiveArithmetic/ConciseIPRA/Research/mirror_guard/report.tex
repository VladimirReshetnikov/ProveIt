\begin{abstract}
We give a new presentation of intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic
by changing the guard beneath the paired logical binder.  Its atomic
matrices have the form $a=I(y,a;u.h)$: the same initializer occurs on the
left of equality and inside one iterator.  This orientation permits a
single introduction schema selected by the matrix root and transport
restricted to sources other than the literal term $0$.  Their compatibility
is substantive: the corresponding combination with the previous
left-iterator guards loses an equality consequence.

The proof first recovers numerical cases and equation induction, without
substituting into a restricted derivation, then restores unrestricted
transport and gives both formula composites with the existing native
calculus.  Composing with the pinned ConciseIPRA bridge gives
bi-interpretability with full first-order iPRA, with whole numerical
domains, actual equality, and identity numerical composites.

The complete executable checkers and their separately reported size
profiles improve the pinned repository frontier under its canonical
encoding convention.  The reduction is modest; no absolute minimality or
new proof-assistant verification is claimed.  A strict public-syntax
validator, readable checker, reproducible measurements, and positive and
negative certificate checks are supplied, with their costs made explicit.
\end{abstract}
