\section{What fixed countdown uniqueness actually determines}
\label{sec:countdown-action}

This independent observation isolates a remaining gap in the supplied
scalar-countdown program. The source is
\texttt{ipra\_scalar\_countdown\_2026-11-09.zip},
member \texttt{theory/completion.tex}, subsections
``The only induction-type rule and the reflection sentence'' and
``Uniqueness of a zero-start successor trajectory,'' in the same pinned
ProveIt snapshot. That report proves its completion using an additional
level-two faithfulness sentence. The following lemma needs no such
sentence, but its conclusion is weaker than equality of counters.

Use the source's bouncing predecessor $W$ and scalar countdown $L$,
which satisfy
\[
 L(0,a)=a,\qquad L(Sn,a)=W(L(n,a)).
 \tag{Countdown}\label{act:countdown}
\]
The contextual rule $U_W$ is
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash g(Sz,\vec p)=W(g(z,\vec p))}
      {\Gamma\vdash g(t,\vec p)=L(t,g(0,\vec p))},
 \tag{$U_W$}\label{act:UW}
\]
where $g$ is one fixed finite numerical template and $z$ is fresh for the
retained context, target term, and passive data. No function variable is
quantified in this rule.

\begin{lemma}[Every zero-start successor clock has the same countdown action]
\label{act:clock-observation}
Suppose, in a retained context $\Gamma$, that a fixed term template $f$
satisfies
\[
        \Gamma\vdash f(0)=0,
        \qquad \Gamma\vdash f(Sz)=S(f(z)),
\]
with the ordinary eigenvariable condition. Then for every target term
$t$ and every parameter $a$,
\[
                  \Gamma\vdash L(f(t),a)=L(t,a).
                  \tag{Action}\label{act:clock-action}
\]
Thus the full ordinary logical presentation proves the universal closure
over $a$ of this equality.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Choose the eigenvariable $z$ fresh also for $a$ and set
$g(n)=L(f(n),a)$. The assumed successor equation and
\eqref{act:countdown} give
\[
 g(Sz)=L(S(f(z)),a)=W(L(f(z),a))=W(g(z)).
\]
The assumed initial equation gives $g(0)=L(0,a)=a$. Apply
$U_W$ to this fixed template and substitute the latter equation in its
conclusion. This is exactly~\eqref{act:clock-action}. All passive
parameters remain fixed throughout the rule instance.
\end{proof}

For example, if $A(n,a)$ is the source's initialized successor iterator,
then $f(n)=A(n,0)$ satisfies the hypotheses by its computation equations.
Consequently
\[
                       L(A(t,0),a)=L(t,a)
\]
already follows from $U_W$.

\begin{corollary}[The missing faithfulness implication]
\label{act:faithfulness}
If the countdown source additionally proves
\[
 \forall x\,\forall y\,
 \bigl((\forall a\,L(x,a)=L(y,a))\longrightarrow x=y\bigr),
 \tag{Faithful action}\label{act:faithful-action}
\]
then it derives $f(t)=t$ for every zero-start successor clock as above.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Instantiate~\eqref{act:faithful-action} with $x=f(t)$ and $y=t$ and
apply the universal closure of~\eqref{act:clock-action}.
\end{proof}

This corollary is conditional. No proof of
\eqref{act:faithful-action} from $U_W$ alone is supplied, and no
equivalence with the source's stronger single-observation sentence
\[
        \forall a\,\forall b\,
        \bigl(L(b,S^2a)=2\longrightarrow a=b\bigr)
\]
is claimed here. Equality of all countdown observations is a statement
about an action; it must not be silently replaced by actual equality of
its counter indices. In particular, the displayed lemma does not remove
the faithfulness axiom from the current countdown axiomatization and
does not establish a new complete axiomatization of $\mathsf{iPRA}$.
