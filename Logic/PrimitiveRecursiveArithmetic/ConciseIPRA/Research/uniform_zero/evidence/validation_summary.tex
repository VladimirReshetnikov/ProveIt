programs in ordinary and optimized Python. The suite contains 36 cases per
program per mode. It includes direct zero computation, zero witnesses,
zero-source equality transport, nested closures, an actual existential
witness, equation induction under a retained hypothesis, and transport under
a quantified formula. Negative controls cover false conclusions, wrong
computation endpoints, future-parameter capture, invalid witnesses and
specialization targets, forbidden compound progress, missing fields, and
malformed input.

Four deliberate guard-mutation checks also behaved as expected. Removing
major verification admits a forged proof of $0=1$; removing the
literal-equation restriction admits the forged compound-progress
certificate. Each mutant was checked in both interpreter modes. These
checks show that the suite exercises the guards; they are not a replacement
for their mathematical soundness proof.
