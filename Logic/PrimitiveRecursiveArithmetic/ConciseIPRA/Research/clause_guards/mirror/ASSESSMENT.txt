Full-language mirror dispatch: scoped result
===========================================

Pinned baseline: ProveIt 443cff49563457a48ee0f663012d56c4bdb58fa0.

The checker is an exact copy of frontier_audit/diagonal_clausal/checker.py,
apart from the module description and the predicate called diagonal. The
function name is retained solely to preserve the common proof-producer API.
It recognizes the mirror matrix

    a(x,y) = I(y,a(x,y);u.h(x,y,u)).

On all Eq/D formulas, introduction takes its progressive premise exactly
for this matrix and its case premise otherwise. The predicate is not a
restriction of the public formula grammar. Refl, two-case Eval, clause Use,
and D elimination are unchanged. No normalization is added.

The mirror shape and the lift

    H_E(y) := s(y) = I(y,s(y);u.t(y))

are inherited from Research/mirror_guard/proofs/mirror_basis.tex. That
package restricts all atomic D matrices to the mirror grammar, uses two
computation equations and restricted transport, and postpones arbitrary
substitution until equivalence. The present complete-formula dispatch is
a different combination. The broader principle of repairing a newly
recognized guard by cutting in its zero-base theorem is already present
in Research/reflexive_base and must not be claimed as an entirely new
mathematical idea. A detailed independent written proof for the present
combination is in ../logical_mirror_notes.tex.

Guard substitution boundary
---------------------------

Capture-free substitution preserves an existing mirror guard. It need not
reflect it: p = I(y,q;u.q) is not mirrored when p and q are distinct free
parameters, but identifying the two parameters creates the guard.

When the guard is created, the translated old case subderivation has local
assumption B(t,0), whereas the new primitive inference expects B(t,z).
The zero base B(t,0) is a theorem, by symmetry of the zero computation.
Cut that theorem into the translated case subderivation, and then weaken
by the desired progress assumption. This yields a valid proof image.
The original proof tree need not itself remain valid after substitution.

The verification script exhibits this exact distinction. For the open
matrix p = I(y,q;u.q), its case proof first uses the local zero base to
obtain p=q, then expands q to the successor iterator. Identifying p and q
at five term images causes naive proof substitution to be rejected; the
local cut repair is accepted in every case. Three of these are closed
JSON certificates and two use open parameter contexts. Separately, mapping
the parameters to distinct terms preserves case mode and is accepted
without repair. Provably equal but syntactically different images also
remain in case mode, as the specification requires.

Measurements
------------

All figures are for the entire checker after removing the module docstring
and applying ast.unparse plus a final newline, under CPython 3.12.14.

                       bytes   tokens   AST nodes   gzip 9
mirror clausal          3059      874       1012      1000
diagonal clausal        3056      872       1013       997
diagonal native         3395      975       1138      1048
witness clausal         2956      837        977       962

The mirror alternative saves one AST node relative to diagonal but costs
three bytes and two tokens, and requires substitution repair. It therefore
does not strengthen the primary size claim. The diagonal presentation is
the recommended main result when literal proof-tree substitution is the
intended advantage. Renaming the predicate to mirror would cosmetically
save bytes but is not counted as a mathematical improvement.

Verification
------------

Run: python3 mirror_clausal/verify.py

The focused suite passes 13 accepted and 13 rejected closed certificates,
52 actual process checks with and without Python -O, five guard-creation
substitution repairs, and 300 random guard-preservation checks. Tests are
finite implementation evidence; they do not replace the general proof.

Reflexivity deletion is not an unused opportunity. The pinned source
Research/raw_clausal/theory/reflexivity_obstruction.tex already proves that
Eval/Use/DE/DI plus any finite set of true closed equation seeds does not
derive all numeral reflexivities. Its rule-preserving countervaluation
makes s=t true exactly when their equal numerical value lies in a fixed
finite set. The same obstruction applies to the unchanged local equality
interface; moving universal reflexivity into a permanent clause relocates
its cost instead of removing the axiom.
