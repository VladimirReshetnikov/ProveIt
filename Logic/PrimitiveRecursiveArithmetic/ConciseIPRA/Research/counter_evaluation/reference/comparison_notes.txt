# Counter evaluation prototype

## Files and contract

`counter_eval.py` is a complete standalone verifier. It accepts exactly a JSON object with `goal` and `proof`, checks a closed Eq/D goal, and prints `ACCEPT` on success. Exceptions reject in ordinary and optimized Python. No nonstandard imports or trusted helper files occur.

Terms: integer `0`; negative free-parameter levels; positive de Bruijn bound indices; and `[n,a,h]` for the canonical counter-aware recursor. Its body binds the state at index 1 and the counter at index 2. A literal-zero counter requires literal-zero body. Successor is `[0,a,0]`. Equations are `[s,t]`; paired formulas are `[B]`.

The five proof patterns are `r` (reflexivity), `v` (counter evaluation), `u` (clause use), `e` (paired elimination), and `i` (witness-directed paired introduction). U/E/I have the same clause-context convention as the incoming raw CE5 checker. Ordinary assumptions use the neutral antecedent `0=0`.

Counter evaluation has only one successful shape:

    s = t       R(Ss,a;i,u.h) = r
    ---------------------------------
    r = h(t,R(t,a;i,u.h))

The matching counter is checked literally. Both substitutions are capture avoiding, and recursor normalization is fully included in the source.

## Prior work and exact new interface

The counter-aware successor-seeded recursor is NOT new. It appears in `Research/counter_congruence/theory/counter_theorem.tex`, and `Research/scalar_successor/report.tex` attributes its immediate predecessor and primitive-recursion compiler to `ipra_successor_recursion_2026-11-09.zip`.

The old counter-congruence interface has separate R0/RS computations, root replacement, and congruence in a counter. CounterEval instead combines computation and shifted-counter replacement into one partial evaluation schema. With reflexivity, the counter projection body supplies an identity frame; the root rule is then derived by two CounterEval applications. State-independent bodies give arbitrary term congruence. These reductions do not need numerical induction or logical rules.

Canonical zero normalization and the clausal Eq/D interface are also inherited ingredients. The complete five-pattern combination is the new proposal; no historical priority claim is made for any of its components.

## Validation

The independent generator and checks are under `../ce_validation`. They compare sequential binder opening with an independent simultaneous-substitution implementation, test exact proof-node bounds, construct predecessor/selector/recursion certificates, use an induction hypothesis nontrivially, reject malformed and escaping-scope examples, and invoke the public CLI in ordinary and optimized modes. The counts and receipts are in that directory. Finite tests support the written proof; they do not prove universal correctness.
