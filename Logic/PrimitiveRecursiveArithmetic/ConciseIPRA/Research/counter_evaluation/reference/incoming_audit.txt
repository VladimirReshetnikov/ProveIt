## Two proved local obstructions / remaining gaps

`ipra_raw_clausal/theory/reflexivity_obstruction.tex` proves that deleting Refl from CE5, even after adding finitely many closed true equation seeds, fails. Interpret an equation as equality of numerical values lying in the finite set of seed values. Eval and the three logical schemes preserve this interpretation; a numeral reflexivity outside the set is false. This is an inference-system counterinterpretation, not a structure with actual equality. Adding a permanent universal reflexivity clause merely relocates the axiom.

`ipra_raw_clausal/theory/rigidity.tex` proves SR0 (zero-starting successor rigidity) implies contextual induction on every zero equation q(n)=0. The reset is e(x)=C(q(x),x,0), and r(n)=I(n,0;u.e(Su)). Coverage proves E(r(n)) directly, and the induction step makes r a successor trajectory. SR0 yields r(n)=n. Full equality reflection and predecessor under SR0 remain explicitly unresolved; the general UR bootstrap cannot be imported because it uses unbounded updates other than successor.

## Strongest proven new direction already exhausted by incoming UR

The modified ramp G uses update g(u)=u+N(M(u))+1, with a direct fusion law M(g(u))=Z(M(u)). It yields predecessor without numerical induction. Equality reflection then uses Boolean diagonal descent and an explicit scalar accumulator phi(n,a,b)=mu(n,a)+mu(d(n,a),d(b,a)), mu(n,a)=d(n,d(n,a)); constant-recurrence uniqueness proves the commutativity of a+d(b,a), hence faithful equality reflection. Merely suggesting scalar iteration uniqueness, a retraction proof of zero induction, a modified ramp, or the Boolean accumulator would repeat this archive.

## Honest possible next steps

1. Combining raw CE5 with the already proved two-case Eval may improve its checker, but is a composition of known results and must be described that way.
2. A successful restriction of scalar UR to SR0 would be substantive, but the arithmetic bootstrap is a real missing theorem.
3. A new restriction of Eval or a new primitive presentation must be checked against both raw and zero-normal companion calculi, not only the older eight-rule native kernel.

All five archives state written mathematical proofs plus finite executable validation, not a new Lean/Rocq verification. The repository's main Lean bridge applies only after the new calculus-equivalence proof is justified.
