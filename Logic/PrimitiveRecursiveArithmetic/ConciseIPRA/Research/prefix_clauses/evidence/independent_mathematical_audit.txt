1. Equality bootstrap and the independent reflexive-prefix theorem
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The permanent entry y=y has the literal-reflexive base 0=0, so Use with
omitted minor derives arbitrary reflexivity. The two Eval cases on these
reflexivities give both computation equations. For premises s=t and s=r,
let Z=I(0,s;u.0). Eval(s=r, Refl(Z)) yields Z=r; Eval(s=t, Z=r) yields
r=t. Thus symmetry and transitivity are derivable, without induction.

For a term template h, let Z=I(0,s;u.h(u)) and P=I(S0,s;u.h(u)). From
s=t derive Z=s and Z=t by zero Eval. Successor Eval gives P=h(s), which
combines with h(s)=r to give P=r. Successor Eval on Z=t and P=r then
gives r=h(t). This recovers the exact crossed one-template transport
needed by the existing native witness calculus.

Before using the gate-retrieval theorem, prove that every D-prefix with
a literally reflexive equation leaf is derivable. Induct on the prefix
length k. At k=0, use the reflexivity just derived. At k>0, apply Intro
at witness 1; this selects case mode. Opening the witness and successor
slots preserves literal equality of the two leaf terms. The resulting
goal therefore has a reflexive leaf and k-1 binders, so the induction
hypothesis proves it in the enlarged context. Ignore the temporary
context entry and close Intro. This argument uses no gate retrieval,
gate conversion, or compound omitted minor.

Consequently the final Use shortcut for any D^k(r=r), not merely an
equation at the root, is a conservative abbreviation independently of
the gate proof. Its skipped premise can always be replaced by the
preceding finite derivation. The shortcut checks literal identity of
the leaf terms; it does not recognize arbitrary provable compounds.

2. The gate and the depth induction
----------------------------------
The reviewed gate is I(0,s;u.t)=I(v,s;u.t), with u unused in t and
capture-free insertion. At v=0 its two sides are syntactically identical;
at any successor they compute to s and t respectively. Formula gating
recurses through D and does not change the number of D binders.

For every A, kappa_0 A has a literally reflexive leaf. Its theoremhood
therefore follows from the independent reflexive-prefix theorem above,
before any appeal to gate retrieval or conversion.

At depth d, prove conversion kappa_1 A <-> A using retrieval and
conversion only at depth d-1. In the D case the elimination witness b is
a fresh variable, hence not literal zero. Introduction at b therefore
uses case mode in either direction. The retained source closure is used
at S z with exactly its zero instance as minor. Once conversion at
depth d has been proved, retrieve A from retained kappa_v A by Use at 1,
then apply conversion. The minor can be omitted for every formula depth,
because the computed zero gate has a literally reflexive leaf. Replacing
that omission by the independent zero-gate proof gives the same argument
in the narrower system. The simultaneous induction is well founded.

3. Soundness
------------
Every context entry B(y) has the same ordinary reading B(0)->forall y B(y).
The fixed reflexivity entry is true. Every omitted reflexive-prefix minor
is valid by the independent prefix theorem (and its ordinary reading is
an intuitionistic equality theorem). Ordinary formula induction
gives kappa_0 A and kappa_(S z) A <-> A, so numerical coverage proves
A -> Q_v kappa_v A. This validates Intro's encoded local assumption.
The existing case/progress soundness argument then applies verbatim.
No extra equality test, induction scheme, or semantic change of D is used.

4. Completeness and round trips
------------------------------
Replace native Q_y B by D_(r,y) B with unused r. This preserves the
atomic/compound root test and the witness term. Prove derivation
translation with the stronger context invariant that every translated
source hypothesis already has an H derivation. Intro gets its new
hypothesis from gate retrieval. Q-elimination opens the dummy D and uses
the retained template. Native D-elimination's local Q hypothesis is
reconstructed by dummy-witness Intro at witness 1, retrieval of the local
zero fact, and Use of the retained template at S z. Consequently no
unproved clause cut, arbitrary context builder, or substitution into an
arbitrary derivation is assumed.

Weakening and fresh-parameter renaming used in this translation are direct
inductions on the four primitive families; these operations do not change
the witness's literal-zero status. General substitution can instead be
transported through the existing native equivalence, so no false claim of
rule-by-rule substitution stability is needed.

The ordinary formula readings are preserved exactly. Q and unused-witness
D are equivalent in the native theory. Gated ordinary assumptions and
their source formulas are equivalent because Q_v kappa_v A <-> A.
Both term maps are identities and the numerical domains stay whole.
Composing with the pinned native bi-interpretation therefore supplies the
required identity-graph numerical round trips and equiconsistency.

5. Scope and implementation obligations
---------------------------------------
The positive mathematical reduction deletes the separately stored
antecedent in every retained clause. Four rule families already occurred
upstream; this is not a new absolute rule-count minimum. The gate,
literal leaf-reflexivity test, term validation, scoping, substitutions, JSON
interface, and final-conclusion check all belong in a measured complete
checker. A flattened Eq/D formula representation is legitimate because
every formula has one equation leaf below a unique D-chain, but the same
representation must be allowed in a fair matched control.

The new gate puts t under one iterator binder: a relative-index
implementation must lift it. At a D-leaf with k surrounding binders,
the gate's exterior control variable lies outside all 2k formula-bound
variables. These are capture conditions, not optional simplifications.
