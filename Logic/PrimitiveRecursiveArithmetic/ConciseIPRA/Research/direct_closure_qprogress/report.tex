\begin{abstract}
The current ConciseIPRA calculus introduces equation induction inside an
existentially witnessed paired binder. This note removes that witness from
the primitive induction rule: from $\Gamma,E(z)\vdash E(Sz)$, infer
$\Gamma\vdash Q_yE(y)$, for a literal equation $E$. The paired binder keeps
only its case introduction. Explicit finite proof transformations show that
the new and old calculi prove exactly the same sequents, with arbitrary native
assumptions. Hence the existing whole-domain bi-interpretation with full
first-order intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic transfers unchanged.

The result is a modest refactoring of the primitive interface. The admissible
rule already occurs as \texttt{NPrf.qIProg} in the pinned Lean development.
It is not a new induction principle or a new arithmetic completeness theorem.
Most importantly, it does not improve the repository's complete-checker size
record: the two implementations here measure 568 and 552 normalized Python
bytes, against 529 for the current byte profile. Thus the stricter request for
a smaller complete proof checker remains unresolved. This distinction is
part of the result, not a qualification deferred to the end.
\end{abstract}

\section{Comparison point and the meaning of simplicity}

The comparison is with Vladimir Reshetnikov's ConciseIPRA project at the
fixed commit
\begin{center}\texttt{8662bc0a00f4e77e482f7d141d7a4f919f70f9aa}.\end{center}
Its path is
\texttt{Logic/PrimitiveRecursiveArithmetic/ConciseIPRA}. The project README
identifies the native scalar core and records the complete-checker metrics
of its research packages~\cite{repo}. The present snapshot was retrieved on
November 10 in the user's Pacific time zone (November 11 UTC).

Two notions of simplicity must be distinguished here. A primitive inference
can require less mathematical data even when a particular encoding of a
complete checker becomes longer. Our replacement removes an existential
witness, the substitution of that witness into a two-variable matrix, and
its associated freshness condition from the induction rule. It leaves the
overall number of primitive inference/axiom patterns at eight. It does not
delete a numerical constructor or weaken the equality motive language.

The repository's implementation comparison is stricter. It measures the
whole standalone Python module, including certificate input and the public
verdict. Its coordinates are significant tokens, Python AST nodes, and UTF-8
bytes after \texttt{ast.unparse} normalization; maximum-compression gzip is
reported only for information. Under that comparison the present candidates
are larger. No source-selection trick or changed acceptance interface is used
to manufacture an improvement.

The 529-byte source is the \texttt{neutral\_scope/code/checker.py} profile.
The README also records better token and AST coordinates in other profiles;
529/266/310 is not a claim that one source simultaneously minimizes all three
coordinates. Because our candidates already lose against this one concrete
complete source, no stronger audit of a coordinatewise frontier is needed to
reject a new-record claim.

\section{A complete primitive presentation}
\label{sec:calculus}

\subsection{Terms, formulas, and contexts}

There is one numerical sort. Terms are finite expressions
\[
 t ::= x\mid 0\mid St\mid I(n,a;u.h).
\]
Only $u$ in the update body $h$ is bound. The counter $n$ and initial value
$a$ do not lie within that binding. The body is fixed finite syntax and may
contain passive numerical parameters. There is no numerical quantification
over function descriptions and no uniformly total evaluator for all
primitive-recursive programs.

Native formulas are
\[
 A ::= (s=t)\mid Q_y A\mid D_{x,y}A.
\]
The two bound variables of $D$ are distinct. All substitution is capture
avoiding, with bound-variable renaming when necessary. A context $\Gamma$ is
a finite reusable list of arbitrary native formulas, including formulas with
binders. We write $E$ for a \emph{literal equation}: its root is equality
between two numerical terms. A $Q$ or $D$ formula whose innermost expression
is an equation is not a literal equation.

The ordinary readings, used to state the interpretation theorem rather than
as additional primitive rules, are
\begin{align}
 (s=t)^\circ&=(s=t),\label{eq:read-eq}\\
 (Q_yB(y))^\circ&=B^\circ(0)\to\forall y\,B^\circ(y),\label{eq:read-q}\\
 (D_{x,y}B(x,y))^\circ
   &=\exists x\bigl(B^\circ(x,0)\to\forall y\,B^\circ(x,y)\bigr).
   \label{eq:read-d}
\end{align}
Thus $Q$ is a conditional universal closure, while $D$ existentially
quantifies a witness for that closure.

\subsection{All axioms and inference rules}

The only computation axioms, available in every retained context, are
\begin{align}
 I(0,a;u.h)&=a, &&(\mathrm{I0})\label{eq:i0}\\
 I(Sn,a;u.h)&=h[I(n,a;u.h)/u]. &&(\mathrm{IS})\label{eq:is}
\end{align}
The new calculus $\CQ$ has exactly the following six inference patterns:
\begin{gather}
 \frac{}{\Gamma\vdash A}\quad(A\in\Gamma)
 \qquad (\mathrm{Hyp}),\label{eq:hyp}\\[4pt]
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash s=t\qquad\Gamma\vdash F(s)}
      {\Gamma\vdash F(t)}
 \qquad (\mathrm{Tr}),\label{eq:tr}\\[4pt]
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash Q_yB(y)\qquad\Gamma\vdash B(0)}
      {\Gamma\vdash B(r)}
 \qquad (\mathrm{QE}),\label{eq:qe}\\[4pt]
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}B(x,y)\qquad
       \Gamma,Q_yB(b,y)\vdash A}
      {\Gamma\vdash A}
 \qquad (\mathrm{DE}),\label{eq:de}\\[4pt]
 \frac{\Gamma,B(t,0)\vdash B(t,Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}B(x,y)}
 \qquad (\mathrm{DC}),\label{eq:dc}\\[4pt]
 \boxed{\frac{\Gamma,E(z)\vdash E(Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash Q_yE(y)}}
 \qquad (\mathrm{QP}).\label{eq:qp}
\end{gather}

The side conditions are part of these rules:
\begin{itemize}
\item In Tr, $F(v)$ is any fixed native formula template. Its numerical
placeholder may occur more than once and beneath binders, subject to capture
avoidance.
\item In DE, $b$ is fresh for $\Gamma$, $A$, and the passive parameters of $B$.
\item In DC, $z$ is fresh for $\Gamma$, $t$, and the passive parameters of $B$.
\item In QP, $E$ is a literal equation, and $z$ is fresh for $\Gamma$ and the
passive parameters of $E$. There is no existential witness in this rule.
\end{itemize}
The displayed local assumptions are discharged. These are all the primitive
axioms and rules. In particular, there is no primitive reflexivity,
successor-injectivity, separation, ordinary logical connective rule, coverage,
or further induction schema. The designated native contradiction is $0=S0$.

\subsection{The single change from the current calculus}

Call the current calculus $\CD$. It has the same syntax, computation axioms,
Hyp, Tr, QE, DE, and DC, but replaces QP by
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\Gamma,E(t,z)\vdash E(t,Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}E(x,y)}
 \qquad (\mathrm{DP}).\label{eq:dp}
\end{equation}
Here $E(x,y)$ is a literal equation and $z$ is fresh for the context, witness
$t$, and all passive matrix parameters. The change is therefore exactly
\[
     \text{DP with a witness}\quad\longmapsto\quad
     \text{QP without a witness}.
\]
The existential constructor remains useful for ordinary existential logic;
it is removed only from the primitive presentation of equation induction.

\section{Exact preservation of every native sequent}
\label{sec:equivalence}

\begin{lemma}[Structural admissibility]
Weakening, exchange, contraction, injective numerical renaming,
capture-avoiding numerical substitution, and ordinary-assumption cut are
admissible in both $\CD$ and $\CQ$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct externally on a finite derivation. Before adding a context, term, or
proof, rename local eigenparameters away from its free variables. Computation
schemas and all the displayed rules are stable under the resulting
substitutions. Substitution preserves a literal-equation root, so the
induction restriction is preserved. For cut, replace uses of the cut
hypothesis with its supplied derivation, weakened to the current local
context. Freshening eigenparameters makes each replacement valid. No
arithmetic induction is invoked by this induction on proof syntax.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Identity-language equivalence]
For every finite native context $\Gamma$ and every native formula $A$,
\[
       \Gamma\vdash_{\CD} A
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
       \Gamma\vdash_{\CQ} A.
\]
Both directions are effective finite proof transformations. Terms, contexts,
and conclusions are preserved, up to harmless renaming of bound variables.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Only the two different primitive induction rules require a construction.

\emph{Simulate QP in the old calculus.}
Suppose the already translated premise is
\[
                  \pi:\quad\Gamma,E(z)\vdash E(Sz).
\]
Choose a fresh variable $x$ absent from $E$ and $\Gamma$. Apply old DP to
the matrix $E(y)$, treating $x$ as an unused existential coordinate and using
witness $0$. This gives
\[
                  \Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}E(y).
\]
DE opens this formula at a fresh $b$. Since $x$ was unused, the opened
resource is exactly $Q_yE(y)$, with no occurrence of $b$. Its continuation
is the one-node hypothesis proof
\[
            \Gamma,Q_yE(y)\vdash Q_yE(y).
\]
DE therefore concludes the desired $\Gamma\vdash Q_yE(y)$.

\emph{Simulate DP in the new calculus.}
Suppose the translated old premise is
\[
                  \pi:\quad\Gamma,E(t,z)\vdash E(t,Sz).
\]
Form the fixed one-variable equation template $F(y):=E(t,y)$, renaming the
bound index first if it occurs freely in $t$. The original freshness
conditions ensure that $\pi$ is a valid QP premise for $F$. Thus
\[
                  \Gamma\vdash Q_yE(t,y).
\]
To introduce the original paired conclusion, use DC with witness $t$. Under
its local base assumption $E(t,0)$, weaken the closure proof into that
context and specialize it by QE at $Sw$, where $w$ is fresh. The result is
\[
                \Gamma,E(t,0)\vdash E(t,Sw).
\]
DC gives exactly $\Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}E(x,y)$.

Fresh $w$ can also be chosen away from every internal eigenparameter of
$\pi$. Both constructions use the premise proof exactly once. Replacing
these nodes recursively throughout an arbitrary derivation proves the
theorem. The simulations use only weakening and fresh renaming: they do not
require equality transport or any computation equation.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Specified proof-tree costs]
Count each primitive inference, hypothesis, and computation axiom as one
node. If a new proof has $N$ nodes and $r$ QP occurrences, its displayed old
translation has $N+2r\leq3N$ nodes. If an old proof has $M$ nodes and $s$ DP
occurrences, its displayed new translation has $M+3s\leq4M$ nodes.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A QP node becomes DP, DE, and Hyp: three nodes replace one. A DP node becomes
QP, QE, Hyp, and DC: four replace one. Every premise subtree is copied once;
weakening and renaming do not add primitive nodes.
\end{proof}

These are bounds on the specified inference trees. They are neither minimum
proof lengths nor bounds on JSON bytes or checking time. The two proof
translations are not inverse functions on proof objects; their composites
preserve the end sequent and may lengthen a proof.

\subsection{An optional deterministic closure rule}

For implementation comparison it is useful to permit the already admissible
Q-case rule on compound matrices. Define $\CQp$ by replacing QP with
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\Gamma,B(\eta_B(z))\vdash B(Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash Q_yB(y)},\qquad
 \eta_B(z)=
 \begin{cases}
    z,&B\text{ is a literal equation},\\
    0,&B\text{ has a }Q\text{ or }D\text{ root}.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:extended}
\end{equation}
DC is unchanged and always uses its zero case. The mathematical rule is
determined by the goal constructor and matrix root; it has no freely supplied
case/progress choice for that same goal.

\begin{corollary}
$\CD$, $\CQ$, and $\CQp$ prove exactly the same native sequents.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Any Q-case instance is derived by DC with an unused existential variable and
witness $0$, followed by DE returning the opened Q hypothesis. Thus every
new compound case of \eqref{eq:extended} is already admissible in $\CQ$.
The atomic case of that rule is QP. Conversely every QP instance remains
available in $\CQp$. The old/new simulations therefore apply unchanged.
\end{proof}

Although QP alone introduces an atomic Q formula by progress, an atomic
Q-case proof is still available through DC and DE. This matters: for example,
$Q_y(y=1)$ is recoverable from its zero-case premise, whose assumption is
$0=1$. The refactoring does not make that native formula disappear.

\section{Full iPRA, both interpretations, and both composites}
\label{sec:biinterp}

\subsection{The ordinary arithmetic realization}

Take ordinary intuitionistic first-order logic with actual equality and
genuine falsity. Over the iterator language, let $\Teq$ have I0 and IS,
coverage, separation, and contextual induction for literal equations:
\begin{align}
 &\forall x\,(x=0\vee\exists y\,x=Sy),\label{eq:cover}\\
 &(0=S0)\to\bot,\label{eq:sep}\\
 &\frac{\Gamma\vdash E(0)\qquad\Gamma,E(z)\vdash E(Sz)}
        {\Gamma\vdash E(t)},\qquad E\text{ a literal equation}.
        \label{eq:eqind}
\end{align}
These describe the \emph{ordinary realization}, not additional native
axioms silently added to Section~\ref{sec:calculus}. Separation must be
stated in this realization: positive numerical equations alone admit a
singleton structure, whereas ordinary falsity must be preserved.

Interpret native formulas by \eqref{eq:read-eq}--\eqref{eq:read-d}, leaving
the entire numerical domain and equality unchanged. QP is sound directly:
under a temporary assumption $E(0)$, contextual equation induction applied
to its premise proves every instance $E(y)$; discharge the base assumption
to obtain $E(0)\to\forall y E(y)$. DC is sound by coverage: its retained
base handles zero and its premise handles all successors. Hyp, Tr, QE, DE,
and both computations have their displayed ordinary interpretations.

The target is full first-order intuitionistic PRA, not just the
quantifier-free equational fragment. The usual first-order iPRA literature
uses symbols for primitive-recursive functions and atomic induction;
quantifier-free induction is recoverable by arithmetic characteristic
functions~\cite{salehi}. The pinned project explicitly implements the
quantifier-free-induction version and proves the scalar bridge~\cite{biinterp}.

\subsection{Why ordinary intuitionistic logic is retained}

The equivalence theorem preserves every native formula and every native
context. Consequently all logical macros and all their introduction and
elimination rules from the current calculus remain available. This is
stronger than an assertion that the two calculi have the same closed
equations.

For orientation, the numerical selector is
\[
     C(v,a,b):=I(v,a;u.b),\qquad
     C(0,a,b)=a,\quad C(Sn,a,b)=b.
\]
The inherited construction lifts selection to native formulas, with
$M_0(A,B)\dashv\vdash A$ and $M_{Sn}(A,B)\dashv\vdash B$. Its gate
$G_v(A)$ is true at $v=0$ and equivalent to $A$ at successors.
These are finite syntactic constructions; their complete recursive
definitions are frozen in \path{reference/lean/ConciseIPRA/NativeGate.lean}.
They are not extra trusted primitives. With
\[
 p(y):=I(y,0;u.C(u,1,0)),\qquad 1:=S0,
\]
the upstream native abbreviations can be written as follows~\cite{nativelogic}:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
Ordinary operation & Native abbreviation \\
\midrule
$\bot$ & $0=1$ \\
$A\to B$ & $Q_y M_y(A,B)$ \\
$A\wedge B$ & $Q_yG_y\bigl(M_{p(y)}(A,B)\bigr)$ \\
$\exists x\,A(x)$ & $D_{x,y}G_y(A(x))$ \\
$A\vee B$ & $\exists_*x\,M_x(A,B)$ \\
$\forall y\,A(y)$ & $A(0)\wedge_* Q_y A(y)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Here the stars mean the already displayed native abbreviations, and all
new binders are freshened. The baseline derives all intuitionistic natural
deduction rules for these operations. Applying the exact proof translation
to those derivations recovers the same rules in $\CQ$.

In particular, the paired-formula round trip is the inherited equivalence
\[
 D_{x,y}B\ \dashv\vdash\
 \exists_*x\bigl(B(x,0)\to_*\forall_*y B(x,y)\bigr).
\]
Forward, DE exposes a conditional closure, which supplies its instances
under the base assumption. Reverse, existential elimination supplies a
witness; DC reconstructs the paired formula using the implication and
universal at the successor instance. The Q round trip is the corresponding
conditional-universal equivalence. External induction on formulas then
gives both logical composites. None of these constructions is changed by
moving the primitive progress rule.

\subsection{The inherited scalar arithmetic bridge}

The numerical bridge must be internal, rather than mere standard-model
agreement. The project establishes, in order, a scalar predecessor, reflection
of actual equality into a numerical characteristic, quantifier-free induction,
pairing with its projections, and a compiler for each fixed primitive-recursive
definition. The unchanged written details are supplied in
\texttt{reference/mathematics/}; the frozen Lean development states the final
bi-interpretation in \texttt{BiInterpretation.lean}~\cite{biinterp}.

For example, once pairing $\pi$ and its projections $L,H$ have been proved,
the general recursion
\[
 f(0,\vec p)=g(\vec p),\qquad
 f(Sn,\vec p)=h(n,f(n,\vec p),\vec p)
\]
is compiled by storing the counter with the current value:
\begin{align*}
 V(n,\vec p)&=I\bigl(n,\pi(0,g^*(\vec p));
       u.\pi(SL(u),h^*(L(u),H(u),\vec p))\bigr),\\
 f^*(n,\vec p)&=H(V(n,\vec p)).
\end{align*}
Equation induction proves $L(V(n,\vec p))=n$, and the projection identities
then verify the two defining equations. This is an external compilation of
each finite description, not a universal evaluator within arithmetic.

In the other direction, iPRA interprets each scalar iterator by a primitive
recursion whose update ignores its counter argument. Internal equation
induction proves uniqueness of such iterations. External induction on a
finite function description therefore identifies each function with its
double translation. This is the essential numerical composite argument.

\begin{theorem}[Bi-interpretability and equiconsistency]
The ordinary realization presented by $\CQ$ and by $\CQp$ is bi-interpretable
and equiconsistent with full first-order iPRA. The inherited interpretations
use the entire numerical domain, actual equality, and identity return graph
$x=y$ in both theories.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The exact-sequent equivalence identifies the new native presentation with
the old one on the same language, not merely up to an arithmetic fragment.
Compose this identity equivalence with the old native/ordinary translations
and with the pinned $\Teq$--iPRA bi-interpretation just described. Every
derivation of an interpretation axiom and every derivation of a formula
round trip transfers to the new basis. Both numerical domains and operations
are unchanged; hence the existing internally provable return isomorphisms
still have graph $x=y$. Falsity is preserved through $0=S0$ and the explicit
ordinary separation sentence. Finite proofs of contradiction translate in
both directions, giving equiconsistency.
\end{proof}

This proof deliberately reuses the established scalar bridge. It does not
claim that the new rule refactoring independently rediscovers the predecessor
construction or supplies a fresh proof-assistant verification of that bridge.

\section{A concrete proof that becomes shorter}

Put $F(n):=I(n,0;u.Su)$ and $E(y):=(F(y)=y)$. The supplied new proof of
$F(n)=n$ has the following structure.
\begin{enumerate}
\item IS proves $F(Sz)=S(F(z))$.
\item Under $E(z)$, transport along $F(z)=z$ in the template
$F(Sz)=S(w)$ gives $F(Sz)=Sz$.
\item QP discharges that equation hypothesis and proves $Q_y(F(y)=y)$.
\item I0 gives $F(0)=0$, and QE at $n$ yields $F(n)=n$.
\end{enumerate}
Counting the hypothesis, both computations, transport, QP, and QE gives six
primitive nodes. The exact old translation of the QP node inserts the unused
witness DP and DE construction, giving eight nodes.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
\toprule
Supplied named-syntax proof & Primitive nodes \\
\midrule
Direct new proof of $I(n,0;u.Su)=n$ & 6 \\
Its translation into the old calculus & 8 \\
That old proof translated back to the new calculus & 11 \\
Separate old example with a nonvacuous existential witness & 4 \\
Its translation into the new calculus & 7 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The latter example deliberately gives the witness a free variable whose name
matches the index binder in the matrix. Capture-avoiding substitution renames
that binder and preserves the free parameter. The proof trees, their checked
conclusions, and the translator are included in \texttt{audit/}.

This shows a useful reduction for a particular common induction pattern.
It does not show that every new proof is shorter, that either supplied proof
is shortest, or that the complete proof checker is smaller.

\section{Implementation comparison and validation}
\label{sec:implementation}

\subsection{The complete-source measurement}

All three modules below are standalone. They use only Python's standard
JSON reader and contain their own normalization, scope handling, inference
dispatch, input entry point, and public verdict. They do not import a theorem
table, a producer, the independent audit, or another checker.

Measurements were reproduced on CPython 3.12.14. The normalized text is obtained with
\texttt{ast.unparse}, applied to the parsed source, followed by one newline. Significant token counts omit whitespace/layout,
comments, and the terminal token; AST counts use \texttt{ast.walk}.
Gzip uses compression level 9 and timestamp zero on the normalized bytes.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}
\toprule
Complete module & Bytes & Tokens & AST & Gzip 9 \\
\midrule
Current \texttt{neutral\_scope} & 529 & 266 & 310 & 333 \\
Literal-equation QP only & 568 & 285 & 331 & 348 \\

The literal-only candidate costs 39 additional normalized bytes (about
7.4\%); the extended candidate costs 23 (about 4.3\%). Neither improves any

validation offset the simpler induction interface in this arity encoding.
This is an observed implementation result, not a lower bound proving that
no encoding of the new basis can ever win.

\subsection{Certificate interface}

The compact interface follows the inherited scalar arity representation:
equations are binary arrays, paired formulas unary arrays, and canonical
closures triple repetitions of their matrix. Numerical zero, successor, and
iteration share the corresponding scalar, unary, and ternary shapes in
numerical positions. A depth-sensitive opener distinguishes numerical and
formula positions, protects bound slots, and replaces the selected hole.
The frozen arity-checker appendix specifies the original decoder, including
its deliberately permissive aliases.

Input is a JSON array. Its final item is the closed goal; preceding
four-field instruction packets are consumed backwards. An unused prefix is
inert, as in the baseline. Acceptance requires exit status zero and standard
output exactly \texttt{ACCEPT} followed by a newline; an exception rejects.
Finite successful runs are the relevant certificates. Practical recursion
and memory limits can reject otherwise valid, excessively large proofs.

The new canonical introduction packets are
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
Packet & Intended inference \\
\midrule
\texttt{[[], 0, [], witness]} & D-case introduction \\
\texttt{[[], 0, 0, 0]} & Direct Q introduction \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
In the literal-only module the second packet requires an equational matrix.
In the extended module it uses equation progress or compound Q-case according
to the matrix root. The last packet field in a Q introduction is unused;
it is retained to preserve the common four-field packet interface. This
implementation detail must not be confused with an existential witness in
the mathematical QP rule.

Nonempty major premises keep the inherited transport, QE, and DE protocol.
Truthy initializer fields keep the inherited computation protocol. Its
restricted direct computation supply is repaired using the baseline's proved
zero representative; this package does not silently count a richer trusted
arithmetic engine. The supplied process fixtures are already serialized, so
their verification needs neither the upstream repository nor an external
producer.

\subsection{What was checked}

The independent readable audit implements named terms, alpha equivalence,
capture-avoiding substitution, all eight old/new rules, and both translations.
It checks the displayed six-node proof, its translations, a nonvacuous
witness example, and preservation under retained contexts. Negative cases
exercise compound-progress rejection and eigenvariable capture or escape.
These are checks of particular derivations, not proofs of a universal
metatheorem.

The compact modules are also run as actual processes, in ordinary Python and
with \texttt{-O}. Fixtures cover direct closure induction, contextual
induction, paired introduction/elimination, nested closure formulas, equality
transport under binders, inherited positive examples, and malformed or
scope-leaking certificates. The extended candidate deliberately accepts
admissible compound Q-case; that acceptance is not misreported as induction
on a compound formula. All 290 process runs passed: 158 accepts, 132 rejects, and zero
timeouts. Exact case inventories and per-process outcomes are recorded in \texttt{compact/evidence/validation.json} after reproduction.

\paragraph{A guard failure that matters.}
An unsuccessful Python Boolean guard is not automatically a rejecting
certificate result. In this compact representation, returning
\texttt{False} from a failed matrix test can put scalar zero into the context
and close an altered scalar goal. The literal-only source therefore uses an
invalid non-JSON complex sentinel for that failure. The extended source
instead gives compound Q matrices the sound Q-case rule. This is why the
shortest superficially plausible rewrite was not accepted as a size result.

\paragraph{Scope of assurance.}
The mathematical sequent-equivalence proof is explicit and independent of
the programs. The Python audit provides supporting evidence and concrete
proof objects. The raw compact interfaces reuse the inherited decoder and
its scope arguments; neither the old nor the modified implementation was
verified here in a proof assistant. The frozen upstream Lean files were
inspected but not rebuilt in this environment. These distinctions prevent
finite tests or existing Lean files from being presented as a new formal
verification.

\section{Why several more tempting deletions were not adopted}

The repository already contains the important obstructions and reductions
behind the following cautions. They are used to avoid repeating a failed
proposal, not claimed as new results here.

\paragraph{Eliminating Q from the printed formula grammar.}
An unused existential coordinate makes $Q_yB(y)$ interderivable with
$D_{x,y}B(y)$. The earlier mathematical presentation already uses only Eq/D
objects together with reusable closure clauses. Removing the Q notation
alone does not eliminate the conditional-universal resource or its
implementation cost.

\paragraph{Making every atomic D introduction progress-only.}
That deletion loses a native theorem. The old calculus proves
$D_{x,y}(y=1)$ by its zero case: the base assumption is $0=1$, from which
the required successor equation follows. A sound alternative interpretation
of the reduced rules reads atomic D as
$\exists x\forall y(E(x,y)\to E(x,Sy))$; it falsifies this example at $y=1$.
The current forced-progress obstruction gives the full argument~\cite{obstruction}.
Our refactoring keeps atomic D-case, so it avoids this loss.

\paragraph{Allowing Q-progress on arbitrary matrices.}
If the literal-equation restriction in QP is simply deleted, QP followed by
QE gives induction for every native formula. Through the inherited logical
macros this supplies unrestricted first-order induction. Such a change
cannot be justified as the same iPRA presentation by the present argument.
The extended rule uses the zero case for compound matrices instead.

\paragraph{Dropping numerical coverage in the ordinary realization.}
The coordinatewise structure $\mathbb N^2$ satisfies the iterator equations,
separation, and contextual equation induction, but $(0,1)$ is neither zero
nor a componentwise successor. For equation induction, its solution set is
$U_0\times U_1$; the base supplies zero in both factors, and testing the step
at $(r,0)$ and $(0,r)$ makes each factor successor-closed. Hence both factors
are all of $\mathbb N$. This illustrates why equation induction alone does
not yield the arbitrary-formula case interface needed for full logic. The
product obstruction is already developed in the supplied research family.

\paragraph{Hiding computation inside conversion.}
Replacing I0/IS by a contextual conversion rule still requires both rewrite
patterns and an account of reflexivity. The baseline derives reflexivity
from computation plus transport. An uncharged conversion engine would not
be a smaller complete implementation.

\section{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Can direct closure induction win under a different honest
encoding?} The measured loss comes from distinguishing the two root openings.
An alternative representation might share them without admitting compound
induction or losing the exact Q/D round trips. A credible improvement must
count the complete recognizer and exercise malformed, aliasing, and scope
cases as well as canonical proofs.
\item \textbf{Can a simpler logical basis reduce an actual branch?}
The present change removes a witness field, but keeps eight primitive
patterns. A stronger result would delete a trusted inference case while
preserving arbitrary native contexts and all logical formula composites.
Restricting attention to closed equations would not settle that question.
\item \textbf{Can focused proof forms justify a simpler major-premise
protocol?} Permuting logical introductions and eliminations might reduce
general recursive major checking. Equation induction makes such a
normalization theorem nontrivial. The theorem must precede any removal of
proof checking, rather than be assumed by a producer.
\item \textbf{Can the current native bridge and the new refactoring be
formalized together?} A direct Lean definition of the replacement derivation
type, both derivation translations, and the native formula round trips would
turn the present written equivalence into a mechanically checked result.
The supplied frozen project is the natural comparison point.
\end{enumerate}

The answer established here is therefore precise but limited: direct closure
progress is an exact, simpler induction interface for the existing theory.
It is not a new minimum-size complete axiomatization. The requested stronger
improvement remains open in this package.
