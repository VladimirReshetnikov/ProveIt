1. Mixed-binder presentation: correctness
---------------------------------------
Reviewed review/agent_logic/mixed_binder.tex before its provenance revision.
The displayed calculus is mathematically coherent and the full-iPRA bridge
appears valid, with the following essential details checked independently.

- Literal equations and compound matrices have DIFFERENT binder meanings.
  Homogeneous binder congruence is valid: in the atomic-to-atomic case the
  converted local premise uses Step; in the compound-to-compound case it
  uses Base. Unrestricted congruence across the two kinds is not asserted.
- Contextual equation induction is genuinely charged in atomic Base.
  Atomic introduction merely gives progress. Omitting Base loses this
  induction derivation; omitting Step loses the arbitrary native atomic
  binder's progress-composite derivation. Both must remain in the kernel.
- Numerical cases are derivable for atoms by ignoring the induction
  hypothesis, and for compounds by ignoring the case-base assumption.
  This proves the weak fresh-variable case rule before ordinary logic.
- The gate preserves matrix kind at every depth. Its zero theorem follows
  by structural induction; its positive equivalence follows by the proved
  homogeneous congruence. No cross-kind exchange is used.
- P(A)=D(unused,y).G_y A is equivalent to A even when A is an equation:
  from the atomic progress clause, its theorem G_0 A and Base at 1 yield A.
  The forward direction ignores the progress premise and uses A directly.
- Padding each WHOLE shorter operand to equal binder depth before aligned
  selector recursion is valid for arbitrary native formulas. Every pair
  of matrices encountered at corresponding levels then has the same kind.
  This avoids the unsound general technique of padding a submatrix inside
  an already present binder and silently changing that binder's meaning.
- Implication uses M-plus with positive binder depth. Its outer binder is
  therefore the compound closure form and retains the antecedent at zero.
  This avoids requiring contextual numerical cases in the implication
  bootstrap. The other displayed connective macros work at either kind.
- Universal elimination is not arbitrary formula induction: the gated
  clause gives all successor instances by its allowed Base rule, then the
  already derived numerical-cases rule adds the separately supplied zero.
- Both native formula composites hold. The atomic composite uses Step to
  derive the progress implication and uses deterministic atomic DI in the
  reverse direction. The compound composite uses Base and deterministic
  case DI. Their ordinary translations preserve equation versus compound
  root class, so the necessary congruence in external formula induction is
  homogeneous. Full domain identity isomorphisms transfer from the scalar
  arithmetic bridge. Separation from genuine ordinary falsity is charged.
- If the wire implementation also exposes Q clauses as formulas, their
  representative D(unused,y).A preserves their compound outer class. The
  text should be read as equivalence of reusable resources, with the
  corresponding object-formula definitional extension made explicit when
  describing the extra wire constructor.

That source explicitly calls the predecessor calculus P the system with

The WHOLE-OPERAND padding proof in the current fragment can be presented
as a simplification of that earlier logical-interface proof: it avoids
the earlier safe-sublanguage validator in the metatheory and its separate

its deterministic introduction, its semantics, or its two eliminations.

The old source also resolves the proposed all-starts circularity. It sets
tau(r,s)=I(r,s;u.Su) and permits atomic E(r) -> E(tau(r,s)). Choosing r=0
and s=t gives tau(0,t)=t by the DIRECT I0 computation; equation induction

proves tau(r,0)=r, tau(r,1)=Sr and tau(r,Ss)=S tau(r,s), and recovers Step.
Thus the current fragment's final unresolved-circularity paragraph must
be replaced or sharply restricted to its particular opposite orientation.
The general all-starts fusion is established prior work in this corpus.

3. Right predecessor without successor injectivity: correctness
--------------------------------------------------------------
Reviewed review/agent_arithmetic/right_predecessor.tex.
No hidden use of successor cancellation or of constructor coverage was
found. The result is a right inverse on successors, not a left inverse.

- In the no-injectivity F+epsilon theory, D(0,a)=Sa and D(Sn,a)=a prove
  successor/zero separation using 0 != 1. The Boolean conjunction table
  only needs S(a) != 0, not injectivity or distinction of S1 from 1.
- Equality reflection and Boolean ranges give decidable quantifier-free
  formulas. Equation induction on chi_A=1 yields the needed QF induction
  without any coverage or cancellation premise.
- The clock body includes the guard v=0 OR Sv=a, so h_a(0)=0 and its
  initial F value is exactly 1. This removes the earlier shifted initial
  value and exceptional-target assumptions that needed cancellation.
- For fixed passive a, the invariant V(j,a)=Sj OR S(V(j,a))=a is valid.
  If the second alternative holds the update fixes its value. Otherwise
  the first alternative makes the value nonzero and the guard false, so
  the update advances it by one. This is a legitimate fixed-parameter
  QF induction, not induction with a changing target.
- Only after this universal induction, specialize a=S^2m,j=m. In either
  disjunct S(V(m,a))=a follows directly, WITHOUT cancellation. Two actual
  F computation steps preserve the state, proving S(W(S^2m))=S^2m.
- The finite P graph has exhaustive, disjoint guards n=0,n=1,otherwise.
  Its definitional extension is conservative. More importantly, the text
  explicitly expands finite P cases to transfer QF induction. This avoids
  the invalid blanket assertion that any conservative definition preserves
  a restricted induction schema. No primitive F body containing P is added.
- The induction on n=0 OR n=S(Pn) is valid. In the nonzero old branch,
  Sn=S^2(Pn), so the clock lemma supplies S(W(Sn))=Sn; cases Sn=1 versus
  Sn!=1 then select the correct graph value of P. This establishes coverage.
  Applying coverage at Sn gives S(P(Sn))=Sn by zero/successor separation.

The theorem heading 'right cancellation' would be clearer as 'a right
predecessor': the statement does not cancel S. Its concluding limitation
is essential. Applying P to Sa=Sb gives only P(Sa)=P(Sb); the missing
identities P(Sa)=a and P(Sb)=b are not supplied by this argument. Therefore
the precursor theory is not shown bi-interpretable with iPRA without its
successor-injectivity axiom. Do not assign the inherited tiny iPRA checker
to this no-injectivity precursor theory.
