\section{Induction from arbitrary starting points}\label{sec:choice-introduction}
\label{sec:all-starts}

This section changes the meaning of an atomic closure, rather than
deleting a hypothesis while retaining its old meaning. Atomic closures
express progressiveness. Their elimination rule permits a jump from any
starting point through any finite number of successor steps. The same
rule supplies ordinary equation induction by choosing starting point
zero. Consequently no separate progression-elimination rule is needed.
Compound closures retain the old conditional-universal meaning.

The resulting calculus is equiconsistent and bi-interpretable with iPRA,
subject only to the explicitly identified inherited scalar numerical
development below. Deterministic introduction, the progression reading
of atomic closures, and the safe logical interface are inherited ideas.
The contribution here is an exact rule fusion: one all-starts
elimination replaces the separate progression and closure eliminations,
without changing their consequence relation. It is not an
identity-language equivalence with the earlier calculus that gave
every atomic closure only its conditional-universal reading. The complete-checker measurements below determine whether this
mathematical change also saves implementation complexity.

\subsection{The complete syntax and primitive inventory}

Terms are the finite expressions
\[
 t::=x\mid0\mid St\mid I(n,a;u.h).
\]
Only the update expression $h$ binds $u$. Other free variables of $h$
are passive parameters, held fixed throughout its iteration. Every
update is fixed finite syntax, not an object-level function code. The
entire nonlogical axiom inventory consists of
\[
 I(0,a;u.h)=a,\qquad
 I(Sn,a;u.h)=h[I(n,a;u.h)/u].
 \tag{AS-I}
\]
All substitutions avoid capture. Equivalently, introduce a staged
first-order function symbol for each such update and these two defining
equations. There is no uniform total evaluator.

Native formulas and contexts are
\[
 A::=(s=t)\mid Q_yA\mid D_{x,y}A,
 \qquad\Gamma\text{ a finite reusable list of formulas}.
\]
Write $\operatorname{at}(B)$ when $B$ is literally an equation, and set
\[
 \kappa_B(r)=
 \begin{cases}r,&\operatorname{at}(B),\\0,&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}
 \qquad
 \tau(r,s):=I(r,s;u.Su).
 \tag{AS-kappa}
\]
The case distinction is on fixed syntax. It is not an object-level
predicate, and numerical substitution preserves it. Notice the order
of the iterator arguments: $\tau(0,s)=s$ is a computation equation.
This orientation is essential to the noncircular induction bootstrap.

Besides AS-I, the complete primitive inference inventory is
\[
 \frac{}{\Gamma\vdash A}\ (A\in\Gamma)
 \quad\mathsf{Hyp},
\]
\[
 \frac{\Gamma,B(t,\kappa_B(z))\vdash B(t,Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}B(x,y)}
 \quad\mathsf{DI},
 \tag{AS-DI}
\]
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash Q_yB(y)\qquad
       \Gamma\vdash B(\kappa_B(r))}
      {\Gamma\vdash B(\tau(\kappa_B(r),s))}
 \quad\mathsf{QE},
 \tag{AS-QE}
\]
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}B(x,y)\qquad
       \Gamma,Q_yB(a,y)\vdash F}
      {\Gamma\vdash F}
 \quad\mathsf{DE},
\]
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash s=t\qquad\Gamma\vdash F(s)}
      {\Gamma\vdash F(t)}
 \quad\mathsf{Tr}.
\]
In DI, $z$ is absent from $\Gamma$, $t$, and all passive parameters.
In DE, $a$ is absent from $\Gamma$, $F$, and all passive parameters.
The displayed local hypotheses are discharged. In QE, $r,s$ are
arbitrary numerical terms in the retained scope. At a compound matrix,
QE has the derived, computation-simplified form
$Q_yB,B(0)\vdash B(s)$; $r$ is irrelevant in that case.
In Tr, $F(v)$ is an arbitrary fixed finite native formula template.

There are no other primitive rules, logical axioms, coverage axioms,
separation axioms, or induction schemes. Native absurdity is designated
as $0=S0$. An ordinary realization must identify it with genuine
falsity; merely viewing the positive equations in a one-element
ordinary structure would be a different theory.

Weakening, exchange, contraction, alpha-renaming, numerical
substitution, and assumption cut are admissible by induction on finite
derivations. For cut, replace the selected hypothesis leaves by its
proof after freshening local eigenvariables. Numerical substitution
does not change the literal-equation test. Reflexivity follows from
$I(0,t;u.0)=t$: use that equation as both premises of Tr with template
$v=t$. Symmetry, transitivity and term congruence then follow by Tr.

\subsection{A noncircular arithmetic bootstrap}

Applying DI with an unused witness coordinate and then DE gives
\[
 \frac{\Gamma,B(\kappa_B(z))\vdash B(Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash Q_yB(y)}.
 \tag{AS-QI}
\]
For a literal equation $E$, the standard equation-induction rule is
now immediate:
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash E(0)\qquad
       \Gamma,E(z)\vdash E(Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash E(t)}.
 \tag{AS-EI}
\]
Indeed AS-QI supplies $Q_yE$. Apply QE with $r=0,s=t$ and the
given base, obtaining $E(\tau(0,t))$. Its counter is literally zero,
so AS-I and Tr give $E(t)$. This does not use predecessor, coverage,
addition identities, or a standard-model assumption.

Equation induction proves
\[
 \tau(r,0)=r,\qquad
 \tau(r,1)=Sr,\qquad
 \tau(r,Ss)=S\tau(r,s).
 \tag{AS-tau}
\]
For each equation induct on $r$, holding $s$ fixed where relevant.
The base is AS-I. Its step is obtained by applying $S$ to the
induction equation, because $\tau(Sr,s)=S\tau(r,s)$ is AS-I.
Thus a raw atomic closure has the derived progression elimination
\[
 Q_yE(y),E(r)\vdash E(Sr),
 \tag{AS-progress}
\]
by QE with $s=1$ followed by AS-tau. Both equation induction and
one-step progression have therefore been obtained from the same
primitive QE pattern.

\subsection{Exact fusion of the two inherited eliminations}

Let $\mathcal P$ be the progression-and-closure calculus with the same
terms, formulas, computations, deterministic DI, Hyp, DE, and Tr, but
with the following two specialization patterns in place of AS-QE:
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash Q_yB\qquad\Gamma\vdash B(0)}
      {\Gamma\vdash B(t)}
 \quad\mathsf{QE}_0,
 \tag{AS-old-zero}
\]
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash Q_yE\qquad\Gamma\vdash E(r)}
      {\Gamma\vdash E(Sr)}
 \quad\mathsf{QE}_+\qquad(E\text{ a literal equation}).
 \tag{AS-old-plus}
\]
These are the separate closure and progression interfaces whose fusion
is at issue. Let $\mathcal A$ denote the new all-starts calculus.

\paragraph{Rule-fusion theorem.}
For every finite raw native context $\Gamma$ and raw formula $F$,
\[
       \Gamma\vdash_{\mathcal A}F
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
       \Gamma\vdash_{\mathcal P}F.
 \tag{AS-fusion}
\]
The translations leave the end sequent unchanged and are effective
finite proof transformations.

\paragraph{Proof.}
In $\mathcal A$, QE$_0$ at an atomic matrix is AS-QE with $r=0$;
at a compound matrix it is the same rule's computation-simplified
form. QE$_+$ is AS-progress, using the internally proved AS-tau
identity. Thus every $\mathcal P$ inference is derivable in
$\mathcal A$.

Conversely, $\mathcal P$ derives equation induction from atomic
AS-QI and QE$_0$. It therefore proves the three AS-tau identities
by the same equational inductions as above. To simulate atomic AS-QE,
retain $Q_yE$ and the supplied $E(r)$, and induct on $s$ in the
literal equation
\[
                       E(\tau(r,s)).
\]
At zero, $\tau(r,0)=r$ gives the premise. In the induction step,
QE$_+$ gives $E(S\tau(r,s))$ from $E(\tau(r,s))$; the equation
$\tau(r,Ss)=S\tau(r,s)$ converts this to the required next instance.
All retained assumptions are permitted by contextual equation
induction. For a compound matrix, QE$_0$ gives $B(s)$, and
$\tau(0,s)=s$ converts it to the exact AS-QE conclusion. This
simulates every $\mathcal A$ inference in $\mathcal P$ and proves
AS-fusion. No appeal to semantic completeness is needed.

The theorem isolates the mathematical change precisely. It compresses
two primitive elimination patterns into one parameterized pattern;
it does not invent deterministic introduction or the interpretation
of atomic closure as progression, nor does it reduce the old
conditional-closure calculus merely by deleting its atomic base.

\subsection{Numerical cases and elementary control terms}

For an arbitrary native formula $A$, numerical cases are admissible:
\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A(0)\qquad\Gamma\vdash A(Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash A(t)}.
 \tag{AS-NC}
\]
If $A$ is atomic, use AS-EI with an unused induction hypothesis.
If $A$ is compound, use AS-QI with an unused base hypothesis and
then compound QE. This establishes cases before constructing any
ordinary logical connectives.

The passive-parameter grammar immediately supplies
\[
 C(n,a,b):=I(n,a;u.b),\qquad
 C(0,a,b)=a,\quad C(Sn,a,b)=b.
 \tag{AS-C}
\]
Put $Z(u)=C(u,1,0)$ and $p(n)=I(n,0;u.Z(u))$. Finitely many
computations give $p(1)=1$ and $p(2)=0$. No predecessor is needed
for this selector or for the logical bootstrap below.

\subsection{The safe sublanguage and its relative logical interface}

Use the Eq/$D$ sublanguage with this restriction: every immediate
atomic $D_{x,y}$ matrix is literally
\[
 L_y(s,t):=(C(y,0,s)=C(y,0,t)).
 \tag{AS-safe}
\]
Top-level equations are unrestricted. Matrices with a $D$ root need
only satisfy the condition recursively. The payloads may contain
$x,y$ and passive parameters. This is an auxiliary sublanguage for
the proof of equivalence; the full calculus accepts unrestricted
native $Q,D$ formulas. Consequently no safe-grammar validator is
being proposed as part of the primitive checker.

For a gated equation its zero instance is a theorem. Hence QE with
$r=0$ supplies exactly the old conditional specialization
\[
 Q_yL_y(s,t),L_0(s,t)\vdash L_v(s,t).
\]
At compound matrices the same specialization follows directly.
DI already has the gated calculus's atomic-progress/compound-base
form. Thus the safe sublanguage has the complete gated logical
interface, under arbitrary retained native contexts.

Here are the requisite operations and proofs, to specify that interface
without assuming arbitrary cross-kind binder congruence. Pointwise
interderivable safe matrices can be exchanged beneath a common safe
$D$. If the target matrix is compound, its retained zero instance
transfers to the source zero instance. Either atomic specialization
from zero or compound QE then supplies source successors, which
transfer back to target successors. If the target is gated atomic,
its zero instance is a theorem; transfer it to the source, specialize
the source closure from zero, and transfer back. Atomic QE from zero
is available for the source as well. DI may ignore its progression
hypothesis. Reverse the roles for the converse. This proof uses
the zero theorem when it crosses the atomic/compound boundary;
the corresponding statement is not asserted for arbitrary raw matrices.

Rename binders away from a free control $v$. Define
\begin{align*}
 G_v(s=t)&:=L_v(s,t),\\
 G_v(D_{x,y}L_y(s,t))
   &:=D_{x,y}L_y(C(v,0,s),C(v,0,t)),\\
 G_v(D_{x,y}B)&:=D_{x,y}G_vB
       \quad(B\text{ compound}).
\end{align*}
These operations preserve AS-safe. Structural induction, computation,
and safe binder congruence prove
\[
 \vdash G_0A,\qquad G_{Su}A\dashv\vdash A.
 \tag{AS-gate}
\]
For fresh unused $x,y$, put
$\operatorname{Pad}(A)=D_{x,y}G_yA$. Then
$\operatorname{Pad}(A)\dashv\vdash A$: forward introduction uses
$A$ at every positive index; backward elimination uses the zero-gate
theorem and specialization from zero to $1$.

For atomic inputs put
\[
 M_v(s=t,r=q):=(C(v,s,r)=C(v,t,q)).
\]
At matching binders use the auxiliary operation
\[
 N_{v;y}(L_y(s,t),L_y(r,q))
       :=L_y(C(v,s,r),C(v,t,q));
\]
if at least one input is compound, set $N_{v;y}(A,B)=M_v(A,B)$.
Align bound names, fresh for $v$ and the other operand, and define
\begin{align*}
 M_v(D_{x,y}A,D_{x,y}B)&:=D_{x,y}N_{v;y}(A,B),\\
 M_v(E,D_{x,y}B)&:=D_{x,y}N_{v;y}(G_yE,B),\\
 M_v(D_{x,y}A,E)&:=D_{x,y}N_{v;y}(A,G_yE).
\end{align*}
Every recursive call decreases maximum input binder depth. Safe
congruence and padding prove the endpoint equations
\[
 M_0(A,B)\dashv\vdash A,\qquad M_{Su}(A,B)\dashv\vdash B.
 \tag{AS-selector}
\]

Define all ordinary connectives on safe formulas as follows, with all
new binders fresh and $u$ unused:
\begin{align*}
 A\Rightarrow_*B
   &:=D_{u,z}M_z(\operatorname{Pad}(A),\operatorname{Pad}(B)),\\
 A\wedge_*B&:=D_{u,z}G_zM_{p(z)}(A,B),\\
 \exists_*x A(x)&:=D_{x,y}G_yA(x),\\
 A\vee_*B&:=\exists_*x M_x(A,B),\\
 \forall_*x A(x)&:=A(0)\wedge_*D_{u,z}G_zA(z),\\
 \bot_*&:=(0=1).
 \tag{AS-logic}
\end{align*}
All results are safe. Implication uses padded operands, ensuring a
compound immediate matrix and retention of its antecedent. Conjunction
uses only the displayed finite toggle computations, so its projection
at $1$ yields $B$ and its projection at $2$ yields $A$. Its introduction
uses numerical cases to obtain the selected operand at any index.
Existential introduction uses its given witness; elimination uses
DE and the true zero gate, then specialization at $1$. Disjunction
is the existential selector, with witnesses $0,1$; numerical cases
and the derived implication rules supply its elimination.

Universal introduction supplies $A(0)$ and the positive instances in
its second conjunct. For elimination, the two projections give $A(0)$
and the gated closure. Inside DE, specialize that closure from zero
to $Sz$ and remove the gate to obtain $A(Sz)$, with $z$ fresh. Numerical
cases then give $A(t)$. This is why the displayed universal macro
does not depend on a predecessor term.

Under $0=1$, AS-C and transport give
$s=C(0,s,t)=C(1,s,t)=t$ for arbitrary terms. Every equation follows,
and external structural induction using AS-QI and DI gives every raw
native formula. Thus the designated absurdity has explosion.

These derivations prove the entire ordinary intuitionistic
natural-deduction interface for safe formulas. They work under arbitrary
raw retained contexts. In addition, the conclusion of derived
existential elimination may be an arbitrary raw native formula:
its continuation is fed directly to primitive DE, whose conclusion
$F$ has no safe-syntax restriction. This relative existential rule is
needed for the normalization argument below. At this stage only safe
conclusions are claimed for the derived disjunction-elimination rule;
that restriction avoids assuming a selector for arbitrary raw formulas.

\subsection{Completion of the numerical interpretation}

At this point the new system has computation, equality replacement,
literal-equation induction, numerical cases, and full intuitionistic
logic in its safe sublanguage. It also proves numerical coverage in
the predecessor-free form
\[
 \forall_*x\bigl(x=0\vee_*\exists_*y\,x=Sy\bigr):
\]
apply AS-NC, using the left disjunct at zero and existential witness
$z$ in the successor case. This is a derived theorem, not an axiom.

The remaining numerical input is the inherited scalar arithmetic
development. Its dependency order is predecessor, actual equality
reflection and quantifier-free induction in the scalar language,
then pairing, projections, and the general PR compiler.
Appendix~\ref{sec:hybrid-bootstrap} reproduces the predecessor proof
that uses numerical cases; Appendices~\ref{sec:eic-completion} and
\ref{sec:iterator-coding} give the subsequent completion and compiler.
Both equation induction and the required logical case interface have
already been established before these appendices are applied. Using
predecessor and scalar-defined addition and subtraction, the inherited
completion next constructs a scalar characteristic for actual numerical
equality, and then for each fixed quantifier-free formula in that
language. AS-EI on the characteristic equation proves contextual
quantifier-free induction. The pairing/projection development and the
general PR compiler follow, with the defining equations of each fixed
description proved throughout the domain. That compiler is an inherited
mathematical dependency, not a new theorem of this section and not
uncharged code executed by the checker.

This ordering does not presuppose a general PR equality test before
primitive recursion has been compiled. Equality reflection uses actual
whole-domain scalar equations, coverage, and separation; it does not
follow merely from standard-numeral tests. Applying the resulting
compiler and characteristic translation gives every contextual
quantifier-free induction instance in the full PR signature. Thus the
safe logical interface contains a faithful copy of full first-order
iPRA.

For an entirely independent numerical variant, one may replace the
scalar grammar by the usual fixed-description PR grammar with its
projection, composition, and primitive-recursion equations. Then $C$,
$\tau$, predecessor, and all PR symbols are direct fixed descriptions,
and the same new logical proof applies. That variant does not inherit
the small scalar checker's source-size claims.

\subsection{Ordinary realization and soundness}

Interpret numerical terms by their ordinary primitive-recursive
functions and actual equality. Define the ordinary realization
recursively by
\[
 (s=t)^\circ=(s=t),
\]
\[
 (Q_yB)^\circ=
 \begin{cases}
  \forall y\bigl(B^\circ(y)\to B^\circ(Sy)\bigr),
       &\operatorname{at}(B),\\
  B^\circ(0)\to\forall y B^\circ(y),&\text{otherwise},
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 (D_{x,y}B)^\circ=\exists x\,(Q_yB)^\circ.
 \tag{AS-realization}
\]
The apparent $Q$ on the right denotes the preceding translation
clause, not an extra predicate in iPRA.

Computations, Hyp, Tr, and DE translate directly. Atomic DI gives its
progression statement by ordinary implication and universal introduction.
Compound DI gives a conditional universal statement by zero/successor
coverage, retaining its base; no induction on a compound formula is
used. For atomic QE, assume the translated progression statement and
$E(r)$. In iPRA, quantifier-free induction on $s$, using AS-tau, proves
$E(\tau(r,s))$. The quantified progression assumption remains in the
context, which is allowed by contextual quantifier-free induction.
Compound QE is ordinary specialization and $\tau(0,s)=s$.
Therefore every primitive rule is sound in iPRA.

Equivalently, the atomic clause in AS-realization can be written
$\forall r\forall s(E(r)\to E(\tau(r,s)))$. The forward equivalence
is the same equation-induction argument; the reverse uses $s=1$ and
$\tau(r,1)=Sr$. Thus ``all starts'' describes exactly what atomic QE
exposes, while the shorter progression formula is convenient for the
round-trip proof.

\subsection{Normalization of every raw native formula}

Safe formulas alone do not prove bi-interpretability of the full new
language. In particular, arbitrary raw atomic $D$ matrices need not
have true zero instances. We now give an internal normalization of
every raw formula into the safe sublanguage.

Define $\mathcal N$ and an auxiliary closure expression $\mathcal C$
by finite structural recursion:
\[
 \mathcal N(s=t)=(s=t),
\]
\[
 \mathcal C_y(B)=
 \begin{cases}
  \forall_*y\bigl(E(y)\Rightarrow_*E(Sy)\bigr),&B=E
       \text{ a literal equation},\\
  \mathcal N(B(0))\Rightarrow_*
       \forall_*y\mathcal N(B(y)),&B\text{ compound},
 \end{cases}
\]
\[
 \mathcal N(Q_yB)=\mathcal C_y(B),\qquad
 \mathcal N(D_{x,y}B)=\exists_*x\,\mathcal C_y(B).
 \tag{AS-normalization}
\]
In the compound clause, recursive calls are on the proper subformula
$B$ and its numerical instances. The recursion therefore terminates;
all outputs are safe. It commutes with capture-free numerical
substitution up to bound-variable renaming.

We prove, simultaneously and uniformly under arbitrary retained
contexts,
\[
 Q_yB\dashv\vdash\mathcal C_y(B),\qquad
 A\dashv\vdash\mathcal N(A).
 \tag{AS-roundtrip}
\]
For atomic $B=E$, a raw $Q_yE$ gives $E(z)\Rightarrow_*E(Sz)$
by AS-progress and implication introduction, then its universal
closure. Conversely, its safe universal progression statement gives
$E(Sz)$ under $E(z)$, so AS-QI gives the raw $Q_yE$.

For compound $B$, assume the induction hypothesis identifies every
$B(t)$ with $\mathcal N(B(t))$. From raw $Q_yB$, under
$\mathcal N(B(0))$ recover $B(0)$; compound QE at a fresh $z$
gives $B(z)$ and hence $\mathcal N(B(z))$. Introduce the safe
universal and implication. Conversely, from that safe conditional
universal statement, assume raw $B(0)$, convert it, specialize at
$Sz$, and convert back to $B(Sz)$. Compound AS-QI gives raw $Q_yB$.

From raw $D_{x,y}B$, DE supplies a fresh $a$ and raw $Q_yB(a,y)$.
Normalize that closure and introduce the safe existential with witness
$a$, then close DE. Conversely, safe existential elimination supplies
fresh $a$ and $\mathcal C_y(B(a,y))$; the already proved closure
equivalence yields raw $Q_yB(a,y)$. Apply DI with witness $a$.
For an atomic matrix its local $E(a,z)$ gives $E(a,Sz)$ by
AS-progress; for a compound matrix its local $B(a,0)$ gives
$B(a,Sz)$ by compound QE. The relative safe existential elimination
then returns the raw target $D_{x,y}B$.

This last step is permitted even though the target is not necessarily
safe, as established above. No raw atomic/compound binder congruence
has been invoked. This explicitly repairs the missing native formula
composite in the earlier deterministic-progress-only attempt.

\subsection{The two interpretations and the status of the result}

The forward interpretation is AS-realization. The reverse interpretation
uses the scalar compiler for fixed PR symbols and AS-logic for all
ordinary logical symbols. The safe interface proves every ordinary
rule and every iPRA arithmetic axiom, including quantifier-free
induction. AS-realization proves soundness in the other direction.

Under the ordinary interpretation, a safe atomic binder has a true
zero instance. Progressiveness, all-starts closure, and conditional
universal closure are then equivalent by equation induction. Thus
each safe logical macro has exactly its intended ordinary meaning.
External induction on ordinary formula syntax gives its internal
round trip. For arbitrary raw native formulas, AS-normalization and
AS-roundtrip give the other internal formula round trip.

Both numerical interpretations use the full domain and actual
equality. Their composite isomorphisms are identities. For each
staged iterator, its double translation satisfies the same base and
step equations after its earlier update subterms have been identified;
literal-equation induction proves its whole-domain equality with the
original iterator. The same fixed-description compiler identifies the
ordinary PR symbols on their composite. These are not assertions only
about standard natural numbers or about a numerical retract.

The translations therefore establish equiconsistency and genuine
bi-interpretability with full first-order iPRA, including arbitrary
retained contexts and both internally provable formula composites.
Native absurdity translates to $0=1$, which iPRA equates with genuine
falsity; ordinary falsity translates back to the native designated
absurdity. This is an effective external proof translation, not an
internal assertion of self-consistency.

The mathematical gain is a deterministic one-assumption introduction
paired with a single arithmetic closure-elimination mechanism that
supplies both induction and progression. Its cost is a changed
elimination payload and an atomic/compound distinction at closure
specialization. All-starts elimination must be implemented and measured
as part of a complete checker before any reduction below the supplied
checker frontier is claimed. The theorem alone establishes a different
exact axiomatization, not the user's requested global size record.
