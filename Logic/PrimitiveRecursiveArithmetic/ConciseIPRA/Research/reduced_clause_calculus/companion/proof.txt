1. THE CANONICAL EQUATIONAL INTERFACE

Let smartI(0,a;h)=a, otherwise smartI(c,a;h)=I(c,a;h). Normalize all
arguments before testing c. Substitution recursively rebuilds terms with
this constructor, including inside update bodies and D matrices. A
supplied input term must already be canonical; the public verifier rejects
raw zero-counter iterators. Therefore the public end formula is nf(A),
not the literal raw A. The raw/canonical comparison proves A <-> nf(A).

Refl and UE imply root composition. Put K(s)=I(S0,s;u.u). Given p:s=t
and q:s=r, the proof UE(p,UE(q,Refl(K(s)))) concludes r=t. The inner
UE has predecessor source smartI(0,s;u.u)=s and concludes K(s)=r;
the outer UE has the same predecessor source and concludes r=t.

Successor computation is UE on the reflexivities of its predecessor
iterate and successor iterate. For congruence in a template h, set
H=I(S0,s;u.h). The two UE inferences
  UE(Refl(s),Refl(H)) : H=h<s/u>
  UE(p,Refl(H))       : H=h<t/u>
and root composition give h<s/u>=h<t/u>. Two such congruences and
root composition transport both endpoints of an equation. This is before
any logical inference, induction, numerical cases, or proof substitution.

2. CLAUSES AND THE FIVE PRIMITIVE PATTERNS

The three logical patterns are U, E, and I of the raw-clausal source.
U specializes a retained [A;y.B] clause using a proof of A. E opens
D_{x,y}B at a fresh x and retains [B(x,0);y.B(x,y)]. I retains the
ordinary assumption clause [0=0;v.B(t,eta)] and proves B(t,Sz), where
eta=z for an equation matrix at literal witness 0 and eta=0 otherwise.
Refl proves the neutral equation 0=0 used to access an ordinary clause.
Every instance is now a canonical substitution instance.

The clause and formula translations from raw CE5 are independent of the
arithmetic equality implementation. Thus the canonical six-pattern
native equality-from-iteration calculus converts to the present five
patterns by the same clause compilation used by raw CE5. Conversely,
ordinary clauses and their U rule are represented by the inherited
conditional closure macros. This comparison preserves full contexts.

3. THE WITNESS-MODE ISSUE AND ITS REPAIR

Raw witness t may be nonliteral but normalize to 0; raw case mode then
would turn into canonical progress mode. Let E(x,y) be the canonical
equation matrix. Choose Z=I(S0,0;u.0), which is canonical and nonliteral,
and has a UE proof Z=0. Under the new local assumption E(Z,0), transport
along Z=0 to obtain E(0,0). Cut it into the normalized old case premise,
obtaining E(0,Sz), and transport along 0=Z to obtain E(Z,Sz). I at Z
is a case inference and produces the unchanged D formula.

No general substitution theorem for syntax-directed I is presupposed.
The proof compiler realizes this argument explicitly. It repairs uses
of the changed ordinary clause; negative free levels of retained
parameters remain stable beneath nested local scopes. The inserted
transport uses only Refl, UE and U, so it adds no colliding eigenparameter.

4. EFFECTIVE RAW-TO-CANONICAL TRANSLATION

Normalize Refl annotations and U targets. Normalize E and I formulas and
their numerical instances recursively. Raw successor Eval translates to
UE by coherence of normalization and substitution. Raw root Eval and
raw zero Eval both normalize to root composition and are replaced by the
K(s) frame. Perform the case repair of section 3 when necessary.

Induction on the proof tree shows that if raw CE5 derives A from clauses
Gamma, the compiler derives nf(A) from nf(Gamma). Its public wrapper
validates the source with the raw checker and the result with the
independently implemented canonical checker. Executed examples include
one where merely replacing the canonical witness by literal 0 makes the
proof fail, so the mode repair is exercised substantively.

5. REVERSE DIRECTION AND BOTH INTERPRETATIONS

The converse follows by interpreting canonical terms as raw terms and
using the inherited proof that t=nf(t), including the equation-induction
proof of iterator-body extensionality. This is not a purely syntactic
identity: normalized instances can differ from raw substituted instances.
The equality equations and formula-congruence lemmas convert the premises,
local assumptions and conclusions at each raw rule. The inherited
normalization development proves both formula composites: normalizing an
already canonical formula is literal identity, and raw A is interderivable
with its normalized inclusion.

Compose with the raw-clausal/native bridge and the existing native/iPRA
bi-interpretation. Domains are whole, equality is actual equality, and
both numerical isomorphism graphs remain x=y. Hence the five-pattern
canonical calculus is bi-interpretable with iPRA and equiconsistent with
it. This written transfer is not a newly compiled Lean theorem. The
Python artifact implements the forward certificate compiler only.

6. COMPARISON

The single-arithmetic-schema result is a valid simplification of the
inference interface. It is not the best measured checker in this delivery:
canonical.py is 3050 normalized bytes, versus 2999 for the root-deleted
raw companion. Its value is a cleaner arithmetic specification and an
explicitly verified normalization compiler, not a checker-size record.
