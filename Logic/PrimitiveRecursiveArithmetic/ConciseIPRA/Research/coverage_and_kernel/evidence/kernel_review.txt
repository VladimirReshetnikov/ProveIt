LEXICAL NEUTRAL-MAJOR CHECKERS: RESULTS, PROOF OBLIGATIONS, AND LIMITS
================================================================

Snapshot and contribution
-------------------------

The comparison sources were read from ProveIt at commit
b9c4d74a0018bdafc5bc06e755a0145f4ed2b8d6.  The new files compose existing
ideas from three current drafts:

1. ipra_monus_and_neutral_major_2026-11-10: the initial derived truth [],
   an unconditional major-premise check, and the induction selector u.
2. ipra_selfseed_scalar_2026-11-10: lexical fresh-name floors, immutable
   premise contexts, and the loop implementation.
3. ipra_uniform_zero_2026-11-10, code/control_compact.py: short-circuit
   folding of branches whose constructed hypotheses are nonempty lists.

The composition does not introduce a new arithmetic basis or a new logical
inference rule.  In particular, none of these checkers implements the new
self-seeded, tied-seed, or monus arithmetic proposals.  They all implement
the inherited initialized scalar iterator / Choice-DI calculus.

-----------------------

The readable expansion has 2307 raw bytes including explanatory comments
and docstrings. Its gzip9-normalized count is 546 bytes. The other files
are already ast.unparse-normalized. The runtime is Python 3.12.14 and
zlib 1.3.2. Gzip uses compresslevel=9, mtime=0, includes its wrapper, and
is informational only. Exact hashes and sizes are in evidence/metrics.json.

The explicit-list profile is the recommended compact implementation.  It
does not depend on exception-driven iterable traversal. The byte profile
uses the previously employed exact-JSON predicate a*0 == []; only arrays
satisfy it. On finite JSON, unsupported scalar/dictionary inputs either
fail a numerical comparison or fail multiplication and cannot turn an
arbitrary value into a list.

The generic AST profile retains the inherited runtime qualification:
nonempty malformed strings may recurse until the concrete Python stack
limit rejects them. Its canonical finite array/number traversal is
structural, but it is not a total abstract parser for unlimited-stack
Python. This qualification must accompany its smaller AST count.

Comparison
----------

The frozen neutral-major byte profile is 542 bytes / 263 tokens / 309 AST.
Its explicit-list sibling is 550 / 263 / 307. The new byte and explicit
compositions each save 11 normalized bytes, adding 3 tokens and 1 AST node.
The byte reduction 542 -> 531 is about 2.03 percent.

The previous whole-corpus AST minimum reported by the root audit is 291,
from a different program. The new AST composition reaches 289, about a
0.69 percent reduction. It has 613 bytes and 263 tokens, so it does not
improve the previous byte or token minima. The token minimum remains 261.

Thus the family improves the observed envelope from (542 bytes, 261 tokens,
291 AST) to (531 bytes, 261 tokens, 289 AST). These coordinates are achieved
by different programs. This is not a single program below every coordinate,
not a theorem about an optimum, and not the requested significant new
foundation. The programs are useful reproducible implementation progress.

Logical theory implemented
-------------------------

Numerical terms are variables, 0, successor S, and I(n,a;v.h), binding the
previous state v in h. The two computation schemas are

  I(0,a;v.h) = a;
  I(Sn,a;v.h) = h[I(n,a;v.h)/v].

The native formula constructor is

  D_(x,y) A(x,y) := exists x (A(x,0) -> forall y A(x,y)),

with equations as atomic formulas. Internal proof contexts additionally
use the resource Q_y A := A(0) -> forall y A(y). The complete inherited
rules are hypothesis, numerical computation, arbitrary-formula equality
transport, Q specialization, D elimination with a fresh witness, and two
D introductions. The case introduction derives the successor instance
under the zero instance. The progress introduction derives the successor
instance under the current-index instance and is restricted to a literal
equation matrix. The witness is chosen before the induction parameter.

The inherited arithmetic and logical reconstruction proves full first-
order iPRA bi-interpretability, not merely agreement of arithmetic
consequences. That bridge is unchanged by these implementations. A complete
final report should reproduce or include its precise native rules and
interpretation proofs with attribution, rather than assign these tiny
checker sizes to the independent new arithmetic proposal.

Lexical floor and context invariant
----------------------------------

At a call c(a,g,k), k is the fresh-name floor on that proof branch. The
inner opener closes over this call's k. The recursive major call receives
the same g and the same numerical k value. Its local reassignment of k
cannot change the caller's k. Context extension is g + [H] in the recursive
profiles. In the loop profile it is tuple rebinding g += (H,), not in-place
list mutation. The default list in the recursive profiles is never mutated.

A completed major proof therefore cannot export any of its private
assumptions or change the caller's supply of names. Different major branches
may reuse names after return. All old negative labels and protected slots
are checked by the opener at the old floor before the continuation's new
parameter is admitted. The interpretation of a rigid old label is unchanged
by a smaller floor. Fresh fractional labels in a newly admitted interval,
if a noncanonical JSON certificate uses them, are handled as a finite family
of fresh parameters, just as in the inherited neutral-major audit.

The initial list g=[[]] contains the already derived theorem 0=0. Adding it
is conservative by hypothesis cut. The crucial inherited invariant is that
every reached context is list-valued and no numeric formula goal can be
proved from such a context. Consequently, a scalar zero cannot act as an
absent major. The empty-array major closes immediately, consumes no packet,
and allocates no name. This makes the unguarded equality/Q matcher safe:
its potential Python False alternative cannot equal any successfully
proved scalar major, because there is none.

Short-circuit folding
---------------------

The byte/explicit profiles construct a hypothesis with

  m*3 or (u and COMPUTATION) or INTRODUCTION.

Here COMPUTATION is a two-element list. If m is a surviving nonempty list,
m*3 is nonempty, exactly the original major-export branch. If m=[] and u
is true, the computation list is nonempty, exactly the original numerical
branch. If m=[] and u is false, the introduction expression is selected.
No branch condition is removed. The AST profile folds only the outer
major branch and retains the conditional arithmetic/introduction choice.

For the generic AST parser an empty dictionary can be an iterable truth
alias in the old implementation; evaluating m*3 rejects that alias before
continuation. This can only restrict its accepted redundant raw encodings.
All canonical encodings use m=[], and they are unaffected. Do not claim
byte-for-byte equality of accepted raw JSON languages between every profile.

The equality-goal Boolean expression in the recursive profiles retains
the inherited fallback behavior if a replacement is false-valued. Canonical
formula templates have a nonempty fixed outer constructor; their root
opening is therefore nonempty. The only empty-list target already closes
against the initial truth before any command is read. The explicit
conditional update in the loop profile thus has the same canonical
inference behavior. The inherited list-valued-context invariant excludes
the scalar-goal loophole on successful noncanonical runs.

Soundness
---------

Induct on a finite successful checking trace. Hypothesis closure uses a
retained formula. A substantive major is established by the recursive
induction hypothesis in precisely the caller's old context. Endpoint
matching implements equality transport or Q specialization. A unary D
major exports the corresponding Q resource at a fresh witness; other
nonempty arities export only constant truth aliases under the sorted
decoder. Introduction retains its one permitted zero/progress instance;
the u field is false-valued in this branch, so it cannot compare equal to
the odd arity of a meaningful compound matrix. Equation-only progress is
therefore preserved.

The generated computation branch is justified by the inherited known-zero-
instance rule. A generated open H(b) need not be true for all b. Its zero
completion H(0) is a computation theorem, while b is absent from the old
context and conclusion. Substitute zero for b in the continuation and cut
in H(0). For a finite family of fresh fractional names, substitute zero for
each name and rename later eigenparameters first. Local proof scoping
preserves exactly the freshness needed for this discharge.

Finally remove the initial derived truth by cut. This gives a derivation
in the inherited native calculus, whose whole-domain interpretation in
iPRA proves the decoded formula. This is a written metatheoretic argument
using the inherited sorted-opening and arithmetic lemmas, not a Lean/Rocq
formalization.

Canonical completeness
----------------------

Start with a finite derivation in the native calculus. Apply the inherited
certified-zero computation normalization: Z*=I(2,1;v.0) satisfies Z*=0
by a directly permitted successor equation. A computation with a literal
zero initializer or predecessor is recovered by replacing all offending
positions simultaneously by Z*, then transporting along Z*=0. This uses
one transport and two direct computation premises per repaired leaf.

Next serialize the proof tree with a branch-local name supply. On entry
to a named producer derivation with n genuinely extended local hypotheses,
start its floor at -n. The initial truth is a fixed theorem and does not
count toward that naming depth. Major subproofs receive the same floor;
each continuation gets its own next negative label. Alpha-renaming makes
all rule eigenparameters meet this discipline. No mathematical formula
or inference is changed.

Canonical majorless commands use m=[]. For D introduction, u=0 selects
literal-equation progress and u=[] selects cases. The empty major proof
closes immediately. Boolean folding chooses the same hypothesis. A loop
iteration is the original tail recursive call with an immutable context.
The resulting finite certificate is accepted. Hence the set of decoded
canonical theorems is exactly the inherited calculus, and its iPRA
interpretations and both identity composites transfer unchanged.

Finite evidence and focused controls
------------------------------------

Run `python3 measure.py` and `python3 tests/verify_compositions.py` from this
directory. The producer is copied with attribution from the neutral-major
draft and is not imported by a checker. Its LocalEmitter wrapper changes
only branchwise fresh-name allocation. Saved certificate trees can be
regenerated independently of the checker.

The suite covers arithmetic base/step instances, uses of the state, zero
normalization, nonvacuous existential witnesses, both D introduction
modes, nested closures, quantified equality transport, contextual equation
induction, incorrect endpoints, future labels, malformed JSON, and known
false-proof attacks. Both ordinary and optimized Python are tested. Three
mutations deliberately accept the particular bad proof they are intended
to guard against: using unrestricted source s to select compound progress,
mutating a caller's context in place, and allocating a parameter before
opening the witness. The correct profiles reject those certificates.

The compound-progress mutation tests a forbidden inference/certificate;
its target is not asserted here to be false or underivable in iPRA, and
this finite test proves no strict arithmetic-strength separation.

New ordering counterexample
---------------------------

The small added certificate tests/future_witness_capture.json targets

  D_(x,y) [ I(y,0;v.x) = I(y,0;v.y) ].

At y=0 its equation is 0=0; at y=S(n) it is x=S(n). Thus its standard
meaning is `exists x, forall y>0, x=y`, which is false in N.

An unsafe mutation moves `k -= 1` before the construction/opening of h.
It can now choose the witness x=S(k), using the induction eigenparameter
that was supposed to be fresh after the witness was fixed. The successor
goal becomes reflexivity of I(S(k),0;v.S(k)); ordinary computation and
transport prove that reflexivity. Four instruction packets (one D
introduction, one equality transport, and two base computations) plus
the final goal suffice. All final profiles reject at old-floor validation;
the mutation accepts in ordinary and optimized Python.

This is an order-sensitive soundness obligation. It explains why h and the
witness must be constructed before fresh-name allocation. It is not a new
axiomatization or a checker-size reduction.
