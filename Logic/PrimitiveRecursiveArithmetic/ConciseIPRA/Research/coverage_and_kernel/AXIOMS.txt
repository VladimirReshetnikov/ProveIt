EXACT THEORIES AND THEIR DISTINCT CLAIMS
======================================

A. COMPLETE INITIALIZED-I CALCULUS IMPLEMENTED BY code/*.py

One numerical sort; terms x, 0, S(t), I(n,a;u.h). Only u in h is bound.
Each h is a fixed finite earlier-stage source term, with passive numerical
parameters. This is a schema over source syntax, not a numerical universal
evaluator or a function-code quantifier.

Primitive computation schemas:
    I(0,a;u.h)    = a
    I(S(n),a;u.h) = h[I(n,a;u.h)/u]

Native formulas:
    A ::= (s=t) | Q_y A | D_{x,y} A.
Designated absurdity: 0=S(0).

Ordinary interpretations:
    Q_y B(y)       = B(0) -> forall y B(y)
    D_{x,y} B(x,y) = exists x (B(x,0) -> forall y B(x,y)).

Primitive inference patterns, in reusable formula contexts Gamma:

Hyp:  A in Gamma entails Gamma |- A.

Tr:   Gamma |- s=t and Gamma |- H(s) entail Gamma |- H(t),
      for every fixed native formula template H.

QE:   Gamma |- Q_y B(y) and Gamma |- B(0) entail Gamma |- B(t).

DE:   Gamma |- D_{x,y} B and Gamma,Q_y B(b,y) |- A entail Gamma |- A,
      with b fresh for Gamma, A and the passive matrix data; discharge
      the displayed Q assumption.

DI case:
      Gamma,B(t,0) |- B(t,S(z)) entails Gamma |- D_{x,y} B(x,y).

DI progress:
      Gamma,E(t,z) |- E(t,S(z)) entails Gamma |- D_{x,y} E(x,y),
      only when E is a literal numerical equation.

In both DI rules, z is fresh for the retained context, witness t and fixed
matrix parameters, and the displayed assumption is discharged. All
substitutions avoid capture. Structural rules are admissible. There are
no further primitive equality, successor or logical axioms. The full
ordinary realization, derivations and interpretation composites appear
in the report and attributed appendices. This calculus is bi-interpretable
and equiconsistent with full first-order iPRA on the whole numerical domain.

The code additionally uses sorted array encodings, truth padding, generated
zero-instance hypotheses and an explicit acceptance contract, specified in
the report. These are certificate conventions for this calculus.

B. WEAK SELF-SEEDED-F THEORY FOR THE NEW MATHEMATICAL LEMMAS

Ordinary intuitionistic first-order logic with actual equality; terms
0, S, epsilon and staged scalar F(n;v.h), with the same fixed finite
earlier-stage source convention.

Nonlogical schemas, universally closed in their free numerical variables:
    F(0;v.h)     = S(h[0/v])
    F(S(n);v.h)  = h[F(n;v.h)/v]
    0=S(0) -> false
    epsilon(a,b)=0 or epsilon(a,b)=1
    epsilon(a,b)=1 iff a=b
where 1 abbreviates S(0).

Contextual literal-equation induction:
    Gamma |- E(0) and Gamma,E(z) |- E(S(z)) entail Gamma |- E(t),
    where E is a literal equation and z is fresh for Gamma, t and passive
    parameters. Discharge E(z). Ordinary logic and equality have their
    usual full intuitionistic rules, written explicitly in the report.

Successor injectivity and numerical coverage are NOT assumed in B.
The new proof derives coverage, no successor fixed points, and uniqueness
of predecessors of each fixed standard numeral. It does not derive
universal successor injectivity or prove B bi-interpretable with iPRA.
The measured code/*.py programs implement A, not B.
