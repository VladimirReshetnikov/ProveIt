\begin{abstract}
The current ConciseIPRA presentation permits both case and progress
introduction at literal equations. This report removes that choice from
the canonical calculus. An atomic matrix immediately below the paired
binder must have the form
\(I(y,0;u.h)=I(y,0;u.k)\), whose zero instance is a computation theorem.
The one introduction schema then uses progress at an equation and cases
at a compound matrix. Padding reconstructs the missing logical uses;
a short argument with implication between equations recovers unrestricted
contextual equation induction without assuming predecessor or pairing.
Explicit proof translations and both native composites transfer the
existing whole-domain bi-interpretation with full first-order iPRA.

Under the repository's canonical-statement checking convention, the
direct checker decreases from 529 to 528 normalized Python bytes. An
additional implementation composition gives 527 bytes. Separate profiles
give 260 significant tokens and 286 AST nodes, compared with the current
reported minima 261 and 289. These minima belong to different programs.
Gzip-9 sizes are recorded as supplementary measurements.

The gain is small and no global minimality theorem is claimed. The
canonical grammar and the broader administrative certificate language
must be distinguished: an unrestricted atomic binder has a different
sound interpretation, and the entire unrestricted syntax is not asserted
to be bi-interpretable with iPRA. A separately measured standalone checker
with explicit canonical-goal validation is also included. The new
metatheorems are written proofs, supported by executable certificates;
they have not been formalized in a proof assistant.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result and comparison contract}
\label{sec:result}
The comparison is frozen at ProveIt commit
\begin{center}\small\texttt{b17dd5487f34adea473a0204c5320fb3eeb8910e}.
\end{center}
The current proposal is the directory
\file{Logic/PrimitiveRecursiveArithmetic/ConciseIPRA}. Its README describes
the native scalar core and its smallest complete checker profiles. We
inspected that README, the current Lean source, the principal reports and
claim files, and the exact comparison programs. The three comparison
programs are supplied unchanged in \file{reference/}. This is a
comparison with the pinned proposal and its documented frontier; it is
not a new exhaustive search through every conceivable checker.\cite{snapshot,bytes,tokens,ast}

The mathematical result concerns the \emph{canonical guarded calculus}
\(\mathcal G\). Its terms, number domain and equality remain those of
the scalar core. The new ingredients are a restriction on atomic
paired matrices, a deterministic introduction rule, and explicit
translations showing that the restriction loses no expressive or
proof-theoretic strength. The arithmetic compilation into iPRA is
inherited, not newly attributed to this report.

The repository's arity-encoded checkers accept a larger class of raw
arrays than the canonical object language. Their completeness theorem
is stated for canonical statements; surplus shapes have specified
administrative readings. The present compact comparison uses the same
kind of contract. In particular, the compact program does not reject
every noncanonical public goal. This qualification is needed for the
mathematical statement, not merely for malformed-input handling.
Section~\ref{sec:implementation} specifies it precisely and supplies a
stricter alternative with its full additional cost.

not used as logical gates.

\section{The ordinary theory and the iPRA bridge}
\label{sec:bridge-summary}
Here iPRA means full first-order intuitionistic arithmetic with a symbol
for each fixed finite primitive-recursive description, the defining
equations, the usual successor separation and injectivity axioms, and
contextual quantifier-free induction. Function descriptions are external
finite syntax; there is no object-language universal evaluator.

The ordinary scalar realization is the theory \(T_{\rm eq}\) with
intuitionistic first-order equality logic, the two iterator equations,
\[
 \forall x\,(x=0\vee\exists y\,x=Sy),\qquad
 (0=S0)\to\bot,
\]
and contextual induction for literal equations. Its numerical domain is
the whole original domain, and its interpreted equality is actual
equality. Every canonical guarded binder has the uniform ordinary
reading
\[
 \bigl(\D_{x,y}B\bigr)^\circ
      =\exists x\bigl(B^\circ(x,0)\to\forall y\,B^\circ(x,y)\bigr).
\]
The mixed reading used for administrative checker syntax is not the
definition of this ordinary realization.

The inherited arithmetic bridge has four logically ordered stages:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Equation induction and numerical cases construct a scalar
predecessor \(P\), with internal proofs of \(P0=0\) and \(P(Sn)=n\).
Successor injectivity then follows by applying \(P\).
\item Truncated subtraction and selectors yield a characteristic term
for actual equality, with a proof of reflection on the entire domain.
\item Every fixed quantifier-free formula is represented by a Boolean
equation, recovering contextual quantifier-free induction.
\item Pairing and projections compile every fixed primitive recursion
into an initialized scalar iterator. Equation induction proves the
defining equations and the uniqueness needed for both round trips.
\end{enumerate}
The full written constructions are reproduced in the attributed
appendices. The current repository also contains Lean theorems
\file{biInterp}, \file{teq_iff_pra}, \file{pra_iff_teq} and
\file{teq_consistent_iff}, and the newer native theorem
\file{NPrf.iff_teq}. We inspected those sources; the present package does
not claim a fresh Lean build or a Lean proof of the new guarded
translation.\cite{lean-bi,lean-native}

The translation \(T\) of Section~\ref{sec:guarded-core} changes formulas
by computable padding. Inclusion \(J\) changes nothing numerical.
Their native composites, composed with the inherited ordinary
interpretations, give both formula composites. Both numerical composites
are internally represented by the identity graph \(x=y\). This is
stronger than equiconsistency or agreement of standard numerical
functions, and it does not use a restriction to standard numerals.

Contradiction in the native calculus is \(0=S0\). The ordinary
separation bridge is stated explicitly because positive numerical
equations alone admit a singleton structure. Translating finite proofs
in both directions preserves contradiction, so the two theories are
equiconsistent; no assertion of either theory's self-consistency is
involved.

\section{Novelty, limits, and reproduction}
\label{sec:limits}
The current papers already contain the one-binder essential language,
padding gates, the Choice-DI equivalence, witness-dependent introduction,
progressive-binder alternatives, zero-supply normalization, and the arity
codec. None of those is claimed as new here. The new combination is the
guarded canonical language with root-determined introduction, the
predecessor-free recovery of full equation induction, the two explicit
native composites, and the use of the mixed administrative reading to
justify a smaller checker without an uncharged guard test. The source
inspection did not find this guarded restriction-and-translation theorem
in the pinned principal reports; that is a comparison with this
repository, not a literature-wide priority claim.

The result has three material limitations. First, the measured gain is
small. Second, under a different input contract requiring an explicit
validator for canonical goals, that validator must be charged, and the
compact frontier figures cannot be quoted for it. Third, the new
universal metatheorems are written mathematics; process tests exercise
concrete certificates and rejected attacks but do not formally verify
the proofs for all inputs.

The archive contains the complete report source and PDF, the four compact
profiles, a readable profile, a standalone strict-goal profile, frozen
baseline programs, the untrusted named proof producer, positive and
negative certificates, source hashes, measurements and the independent
mathematical review. The proof generators and measurement scripts are
not imported by any checker.

From the archive directory, run:
\begin{lstlisting}[language={}]
python3 scripts/reproduce.py
python3 code/checker.py < tests/root_cases/guarded_literal_equation_introduction_local_neutral.json
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error report.tex
\end{lstlisting}
The reproduction script reruns the certificate checks in ordinary Python
and with \file{-O}, remeasures each whole module, and writes fresh
machine-readable receipts. Python and zlib versions can change byte
normalization or compression figures; the supplied receipt records the
environment used for this delivery.
