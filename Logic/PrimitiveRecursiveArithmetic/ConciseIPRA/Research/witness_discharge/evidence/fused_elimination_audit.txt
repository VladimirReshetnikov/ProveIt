INDEPENDENT MATHEMATICAL AUDIT: EQUALITY-AWARE PAIRED ELIMINATION

Candidate base: canonical full-computation-normal iterator terms; Eq/D formulas;
reusable clauses [A;y.B(y)]. Refl, Use, and witness-directed paired I unchanged.
Let N denote normalization and [t/b] ordinary simultaneous capture-free substitution.

Replacement for ordinary E and standalone equality transport:
  Gamma |- D_(x,y) B
  Gamma, [B(b,0);y.B(b,y)] |- b=t
  Gamma, [B(b,0);y.B(b,y)] |- F
  ----------------------------------------------------- E=
                    Gamma |- N(F[t/b])
Require b fresh for Gamma and passive matrix parameters, and N(F[t/b]) free of b.
F and t are branch-scoped; t MAY contain b. t=b must be permitted.

1. Soundness.
In ordinary realization use D elimination to choose b, then equality substitution
on the two branch conclusions, obtaining N(F[t/b]). Normalization is valid by
iterator computation. Its b-freedom permits existential elimination. No restriction
b notin FV(t) is needed: simultaneous substitution of t(b) into F is a valid equality
inference from b=t(b); substituted copies of t are not traversed again.

2. Ordinary E is immediate.
Use t=b, Refl(b), and the original branch F. Since F is b-free, the conclusion is F.
Thus every proof construction using only Refl, Use, I, ordinary E is already
available before any equality transport has been recovered.

3. Numerical cases before equality.
q_y A := D_(w,y) A for w unused. Its case introduction uses witness 1, hence
is in case mode even for an equation matrix. Ordinary E and Use give arbitrary
specialization. Thus from A(0) and A(S z) with z fresh obtain A(v), without equality.

4. Recover uncrossed term transport without circularity.
Given s=t and N(h(s))=r, choose new bound x,y. Let C(v,a,b)=N(I(v,a;u.b));
its endpoint equations are literal normal-form identities. Let
P(y)=N(I(y,0;u.C(u,1,0))); then P(1)=1 and P(2)=0 literally.
Use the inherited two-row naming matrix
  B(x,y) :=
    C(y,0,C(P(y),x,N(h(x)))) = C(y,0,C(P(y),t,r)).
I at witness s proves D B: the successor equation is supplied by numerical cases
on C(v,s,N(h(s)))=C(v,t,r), using the two given equations as endpoint proofs.
The branch assumption of I is unused, so this works for s=0 and for other s.
Open via E=. Since B(b,0) is literally H=(0=0), Use at 1 and 2, with Refl(0), gives
  N(h(b))=r, and b=t.
Choose F=N(h(b))=r in E=. Normalization/substitution coherence and freshness of b
make the conclusion N(h(t))=r. This recovers uncrossed replacement of an arbitrary
term inside an arbitrary fixed numerical template. No symmetry or congruence
was assumed in forming the package.

5. Recover symmetry, crossed transport, and the baseline.
In step 4 take h(w)=w and r=s, with premises s=t and s=s. The conclusion is t=s.
Then step 4 followed by symmetry yields crossed transport r=N(h(t)). The full
canonical baseline is recovered: Refl, Use, ordinary E, I, crossed transport.
The reverse simulation of E= uses baseline whole-formula equality transport
(which follows from its ordinary case introductions and induction on formula
rank) followed by ordinary E. Hence exact identity-language sequent equivalence.

6. Structural facts.
Weakening, injective renaming, and ordinary-assumption cut are direct inductions
with alpha-freshening of each E= eigenparameter. The equality source is the parameter
lexically bound by the E= node, not a dynamically chosen newest parameter throughout
an arbitrary derivation. Thus the newest-source weakening problem from the prior
variable_substitution proposal is absent. Arbitrary term substitution into I may
change its witness-selected mode; do not claim primitive-tag-preserving substitution.
It is transferred only after equivalence to the canonical baseline is established.

7. Scope/implementation risks to test.
- Allow t=b for recovering ordinary elimination.
- Enforce that the NORMALIZED exported formula is outer-scoped; merely checking F
  in inner scope is insufficient. A forged final goal containing the fresh b must fail.
- Check both branch premises under exactly the same newly appended clause.
- Do not let branch context mutations leak into the major premise or sibling proofs.
- Substitute free b only, avoiding D and iterator capture. If numeric free levels are
  negative, preserve all bound positive indices and deeper fresh negative levels.
- If t contains b, substitution is simultaneous/one pass; do not recurse into inserted t.
- Treat base/witness=0 tests on canonical syntax exactly as in the chosen baseline.
- Full computation normalization must remain counted in the trusted checker.
