CORRECTED NEUTRAL-MAJOR INTERFACE: RAW-INPUT AND NOVELTY AUDIT

The reviewed final selector is (m or len(a) % 2 == u) and k.
Comparing with s instead is a rejected construction. A concrete mutation

Core proof is in neutral_major_audit.tex. It proves, for arbitrary starting
integer floors and arbitrary finite stacks, that no finite successful c(a,g)
can have numeric a and a context containing only lists. The minimal completed
trace argument handles the recursive major and continuation as proper traces.
A successfully matched transport cannot turn such a scalar goal into a list:
root scalar opening does not depend on the substitution image. Introduction
cannot unpack a scalar raw goal. All other continuations preserve list-valued
context and numeric goal, contradicting minimality.

For general goals, every reached command retains a list-valued context. A
scalar introduction matrix does NOT produce a scalar hypothesis: len(a)
raises before the continuation context can be constructed. Context extension
uses a fresh list, and recursive major proofs cannot mutate the caller's list.
Thus numeric m=0 (or False, 0.0, -0.0), including truthy numeric alternatives,
cannot reach the matcher after a successful major check. Its potential
comparison to the False sentinel is unreachable. Negative and fractional
rigid names, booleans, and default-json-loader NaN/infinities do not alter
this type-based invariant. No full opener-idempotence assumption is used.

RAW INPUT CLASSIFICATION (positions actually sent to the opener)
- Arrays: opening returns an array, preserving the top-level length. Surplus
  arities cannot produce scalar context entries. Their meanings remain those
  of the inherited sorted decoder.
- Integers/floats/booleans: formula-root opening returns the same negative
  admitted value, or numeric zero. Never equals any list-valued hypothesis.
  No numeric root goal can succeed from the initial [[]] context.
- Null: fails when sent to the opener. It can remain in inert packet fields;
  no blanket claim is made that a JSON document containing null is rejected.
- Strings/dictionaries in strict-list and zero-product profiles: fail when
  actually sent to the opener; unused packet data need not be inspected.
- Generic profile empty string / empty dictionary: iterable aliases for [].
  They may act as absent majors, but neither equals False or numeric zero.
- Generic dictionary with exactly the empty key: opens to [[]], ignoring
  its value. If used as a truthy major, raw m*3 raises before continuation.
- Generic nonempty strings and nonempty dictionary keys: no finite successful
  opening; CPython's finite recursion limit leads to rejection. Unlimited-
  stack execution may diverge. The audit does not claim ideal totality.
- Non-array top-level proof stacks fail the input-pop/packet protocol unless
  the ordinary program actually supplies the required no-argument pop; raw
  JSON object/string/number/null roots do not provide a valid such protocol.

WHY u IS THE RIGHT MODE FIELD
Introduction requires u false, so len(a)%2 == u cannot hold for odd arity1/3.
The original equation-only induction restriction is preserved. u=0 selects
progress for literal equations; u=[] selects cases. In contrast s is unused
and unrestricted in introduction, so s=1 would enable compound progress.
For noncanonical arithmetic packets where the new selector chooses a fresh
opening, inherited known-zero-instance discharge (including all newly admitted
fractional names) is required; uniform truth of the open hypothesis must not
be assumed.
