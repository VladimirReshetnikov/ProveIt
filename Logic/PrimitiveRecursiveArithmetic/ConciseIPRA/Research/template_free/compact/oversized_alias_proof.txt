OVERSIZED COMPUTATION ALIASES RECOVER SELECTIVE CONGRUENCE

Primitive replacement R is top-down, all-occurrence, and stops at a match:
from s=t and u=v infer v=R_s^t(u). Its source and target are closed relative
to the currently retained numerical parameters. Inserted targets are not
visited again. Matching under an ordinary de Bruijn binder uses the lifted
source; in the inherited arity codec the closed source can be matched raw.

ROOT ALGEBRA
Reflexivity: let J(a)=I(0,a;w.0). The computation J(a)=a, used as both major
and minor, gives a=a by R. To handle the compact kernel's omitted literal-
zero initializer uniformly, instead let C_a=I(2,1;w.a). One allowed successor
computation gives C_a=a, and using it twice also gives a=a.

Symmetry: from a=b, use major a=a and minor a=b. The result is b=a.
Common-left joining: from a=b and a=c, the first as major and second as
minor give c=b because replacement matches the entire source a.
Transitivity: from a=b and b=c, reverse the first and join at b.

SIZE LEMMA
Let H[-] be a finite numerical term context, possibly with several holes,
local iterator binders, and arbitrary passive parameter occurrences.
Choose a closed numeral N whose syntax tree has more nodes than H[-],
counting each hole as one node. Let K=I(0,s;w.N).
Then K=s by computation and node_count(K)>node_count(H[-]).

In H[K], every occurrence of a subtree with exactly the syntax of K is a
designated inserted copy. A passive subtree inherited from H[-] is smaller
than K. A proper subtree within an inserted K is smaller than K. A strict
ancestor containing an inserted K is larger than K. These four categories,
together with the designated copies themselves, exhaust every subtree.
Therefore top-down replacement gives exactly
    R_K^s(H[K])=H[s],    R_K^t(H[K])=H[t].
The same proof works under binders: capture-free lifting changes variable
indices but not node counts. Passive subterms still cannot have the needed
size, and ancestors containing an inserted lifted K are still too large.

CONGRUENCE
Given s=t, transitivity gives K=t from K=s. Reflexivity gives H[K]=H[K].
Using K=s as the replacement major gives H[K]=H[s]. Using K=t gives
H[K]=H[t]. Common-left joining yields H[s]=H[t]. No existential naming,
fresh eigenparameter, logical introduction, induction, path, mask, or
selective replacement primitive is used.

ACTUAL COMPACT COMPUTATION INTERFACE
For all canonical source terms, including literal zero, use
    C_s=I(2,1;w.s),    K=I(0,C_s;w.N).
Both C_s=s and K=C_s are allowed computation packets: their initializer is
nonempty, and the successor packet's predecessor is 1. Transitivity yields
K=s. This avoids any circular appeal to omitted zero computation instances.
It also gives uniform reflexivity at zero via C_0=0 as above.

TWO-SIDED EQUATIONAL TRANSPORT
Given a(s)=b(s), derive a(s)=a(t) and b(s)=b(t) by the congruence construction.
Join the first with the retained minor to obtain b(s)=a(t); join that with
the second to obtain a(t)=b(t). Thus the old equational transport is a
derived rule. All other native rules can be left literally unchanged.

FINITE EVIDENCE
verify.py independently produces primitive proof trees. Its cases include
H(z)=I(z,s;w.s), where primitive R_s^t would wrongly replace both passive
occurrences of s, but the oversized-alias proof leaves them unchanged;
two-sided transport for a(z)=I(0,z;w.s), b(z)=z; literal-zero sources; and
replacement beneath two iterator binders. The final suite currently has
50 successful process expectations (25 cases in normal and optimized modes).
This evidence does not constitute a proof-assistant verification.

SHARED OPENER CAUTIONS
The replacement-source sentinel is the unforgeable function c; the default
missing image is None. They must differ. Using c for both lets i(c,d=3)
match the replacement sentinel and return instead of rejecting a scope
violation. The source argument is keyword-only, preserving the old
i(*goal,witness,2) guard that forces a nontrivial DI goal to be unary.
Complex zero is not an equality sentinel because 0j==0 in Python.

DE FLOOR ORDER
Construct the fresh witness export after decreasing the local floor. Check
the continuation at that floor, restore the old floor, then rescope its
result. The positive regression uses the witness in QE and computation
before discharging it; an unused-witness test alone would not check this.
