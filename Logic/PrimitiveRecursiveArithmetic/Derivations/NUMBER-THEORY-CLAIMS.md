# Number theory survey: source-claim audit

This audit covers every theorem, lemma, proposition and corollary environment
in [`diophantine-number-theory/number_theory_ipra_2026-11-22`](../docs/proofs/diophantine-number-theory/number_theory_ipra_2026-11-22/).
There are 106 such environments in seven chapters: 52 are derived, 27 are derived
in part and 27 are open. The full repository scope is recorded in
[PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md).

**Derived** means that a statement of the same mathematical content is proved
as a derivation in `Prf PRAAx` by the named declarations; differences of
formulation (natural numbers in place of integers, signed quantities as pairs
of natural parts, denominators cleared) are noted. **Partial** names what is
derived and what is missing. **Open** means that no derivation of the claim
was found. Most of the derivations were written for the dedicated packets of
the same topics; this packet is a survey, and the audit matches its claims
against those modules statement by statement.
