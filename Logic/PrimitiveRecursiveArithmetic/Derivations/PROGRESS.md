The active scope is every claimed theorem in the delivered packet index.
A packet is complete when its source claims have explicit object-language
statements, checked derivations in `Prf PRAAx`, and documented assumptions
and encoding conventions. The table below indexes the whole packet scope
and the current derivation families. Each module records the mathematical
statements it proves. Source-claim reconciliation remains open across the
packet inventory.

## Source statements needing correction

- In finite-use-v2 `thm:nested-name`, nesting and shrinking diameters give
  a regular center name. Equality with a particular admissible solution also
  needs its membership in every isolating region, or a theorem locating it
  there. For example, the target set `{0, 1/8}` and balls centered at zero
  of radius `2^(-k-2)` meet the displayed nesting, nonemptiness and local
  diameter conditions. Both solutions belong to the initial ball. The
  solution `1/8` lies outside later balls and differs from the center name.
  The source's global assertion needs that extra compatibility hypothesis.
  [Source statement](../docs/proofs/foundations/ipra_finite_use_framework_2026-11-15_v2/sections/09_compactness.tex)
- In Barnes `eq:product-cutoff`, the displayed cutoff using
  `ceil(2^(p+5) R³ / 3)` yields `B_N ≤ 2^(-p-4)` for
  `B_N = 2 R³ / (3N)`. The following sentence claims `2^(-p-5)`.
  At `R = 1`, `p = 0`, the displayed cutoff is `N = 11`, and
  `B_N = 2/33 > 1/32`. Using `2^(p+6)` in the cutoff supplies the claimed
  bound. The underlying `eq:qtail` bound has its stated constant.
  [Source cutoff](../docs/proofs/analysis/barnes_g_ipra_2026-11-16/paper/sections/03_canonical_product.tex)

- In FIBER Section 8, “Gap-guarded labeling,” a positive distance gap
  between complex-root clusters does not make lexicographic box order
  independent of the certificate. The roots `i` and `−i` have gap `2`;
  arbitrarily small rational boxes containing them can have their real
  coordinates perturbed in either order. Real-coordinate ties require an
  additional order-separation or locator convention. Metric separation alone
  does give unique geometric matching at sufficiently fine precision.
  [Source proposition](../docs/proofs/foundations/fiber_ipra_2026-11-16/paper/sections/08_algebra.tex)
