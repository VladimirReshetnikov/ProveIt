## Literature

- Arno Pauly, *On the topological aspects of the theory of represented spaces*. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.3763)
- Akitoshi Kawamura and Stephen Cook, *Complexity theory for operators in analysis*. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0453)
- Mathieu Hoyrup and Cristóbal Rojas, *Computability of probability measures and Martin-Löf randomness over metric spaces*. [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ic.2008.12.009)
- S. S. Vallander, *Calculation of the Wasserstein distance between probability distributions on the line*. [English translation](https://doi.org/10.1137/1118101)
- The requested background pages on [constructive analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructive_analysis) and [computable analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computable_analysis) provide the broad subject context.
