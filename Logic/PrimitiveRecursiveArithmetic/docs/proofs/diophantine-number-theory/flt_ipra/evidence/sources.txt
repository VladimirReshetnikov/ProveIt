MATHEMATICAL SOURCES AND THEIR ROLES
==================================

1. ProveIt / ConciseIPRA
   https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/tree/bb4427b10219ad61e0d2875fdde04bcd23b47ec6/Logic/PrimitiveRecursiveArithmetic/ConciseIPRA
   Role: actual iPRA syntax, deduction rules, PR function codes, qf induction,
   and translation to the native scalar core. Blob hashes are in
   repository_snapshot.json. No FLT theorem imported from this repository.

2. Mathlib NumberTheory.FLT.Three
   https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/NumberTheory/FLT/Three.html
   Role: primary documentation for an independently formalized semantic
   cubic specialization; not an internal iPRA certificate used by the paper.

3. Mathlib NumberTheory.FLT.Four
   https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/NumberTheory/FLT/Four.html
   Role: primary documentation for the stronger quartic theorem and ordinary
   Lean formalization. The paper independently gives the bounded descent.

4. isomorphic-ai/fermat, Fermat/Five
   https://github.com/isomorphic-ai/fermat/tree/07b35d10984632caa14b1af26ec5dec2363ee920/Fermat/Five
   Role: inspected comparison source for the Dirichlet quintic route.
   The present paper gives an explicit bounded unit normalization and fixes
   the even-state coefficient at 2 throughout its descent.

5. V.-A. Lebesgue (1840), original exponent-seven proof, pp. 276-279
   https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1840_1_5__276_0.pdf
   Role: source of the quartic descent, reconstructed in full in the paper.

6. V.-A. Lebesgue (1840), correction, pp. 348-349
   https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1840_1_5__348_0.pdf
   Role: essential repair of factor-allocation III. Included in full
   mathematical detail; not omitted or replaced by a citation.

All sources inspected on 15 November 2026, America/Los_Angeles.
The article contains an original exposition of the mathematical arguments;
external source files are not bundled as substitute proofs.
