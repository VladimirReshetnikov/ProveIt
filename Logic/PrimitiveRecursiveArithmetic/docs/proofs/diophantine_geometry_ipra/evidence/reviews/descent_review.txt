Overall conclusion

I found no material mathematical error or missing unbounded principle in the
descent, rational correspondence, or complete E1 classification. The argument
is suitable for the stated iPRA base after the indicated primitive-recursive
arithmetic infrastructure is formalized. No edits were made to the reviewed
chapter or program.

Arithmetic and descent checks
-----------------------------
1. Finite descent is genuinely a bounded primitive-recursive iteration. Its
   induction invariant G(s_j) AND rho(s_j)+j <= rho(s) is quantifier-free after
   Boolean compilation. Evaluating at j=rho(s) gives zero measure while the
   preserved validity predicate gives positive measure. The conclusion is
   obtained by implication to False, with no double-negation elimination,
   least-number principle, or induction on an unbounded formula.

2. The gcd square-splitting proof is correct. With h=gcd(a,c), a=hu, c=hv,
   the equation ub=hv^2 and gcd(u,v)=1 imply u|h. Writing h=ue gives
   a=eu^2, b=ev^2; gcd(a,b)=1 forces e=1. Thus the code's returned roots
   (a//h,c//h) are exactly (u,v). No prime factorization is needed.

3. Normalizing a square-area triangle preserves the square-area root:
   after a=gA,b=gB and parity yields AB=2K, s^2=g^2K forces g|s.
   The normalization therefore does not silently assume that a rational
   square root of an integer is integral.

4. The primitive Pythagorean parameter extraction is complete. U=(C+B)/2
   and V=(C-B)/2 are positive coprime integers, and UV=(A/2)^2. The parity,
   uniqueness, and bounds for the extracted m,n are all justified.

5. In the main descent, m,n,m+n,m-n really are pairwise coprime: the only
   potentially exceptional pair is m+n,m-n, whose common divisor divides 2
   and is odd. Splitting their product produces the four squares claimed.
   If m were even, m=r^2 and n=s^2 would give m-n=3 mod 4, impossible;
   hence s is a positive even integer. Thus every division in (6) is exact
   and S'=s/2 is positive.

6. The new triangle identities are correct:
      A'^2+B'^2=(t^2+u^2)/2=r^2,
      A'B'=(t^2-u^2)/4=s^2/2=2(s/2)^2.
   Coprimality follows from gcd(t,u)=1. Its hypotenuse r is strictly smaller
   than C=m^2+n^2. The contradiction is obtained from a total specified D,
   rather than from a nonconstructive existence statement about a smaller
   state.

7. Clearing the product of denominators of a,b,c,q gives the integer
   square-area state (La,Lb,Lc,L|q|). Both the Pythagorean identity and
   ab=2q^2 scale by L^2, so the rational corollary has no denominator gap.

8. The quartic and three-squares corollaries have the correct side lengths
   and area factors, including the case r=0 in the latter. T>R ensures
   both constructed legs remain strictly positive.

Rational maps, signs, and projective classification
--------------------------------------------------
9. The signed triangle correspondence is an actual bijection on its stated
   domains. Writing delta=c-a, the identities
      x^2-n^2=ay, x^2+n^2=cy, 2nx=by, ax=y
   hold for x=nb/delta, y=2n^2/delta. Delta cannot vanish because ab=2n!=0.
   Conversely y!=0 on E_n forces x and x^2-n^2 nonzero, gives ab=2n, and
   gives c-a=2n^2/y!=0. The inverse identities are correct.

10. The treatment of signs is adequate. It does not assume x>0 on E_n:
    points with -n<x<0 are handled by signed legs, and taking absolute
    values subsequently preserves ab=2n>0 and the right-triangle equation.
    A nonzero y is allowed to have either sign, and c may have either sign
    in the actual bijection.

11. The projective E1 proof accounts for the unique point at infinity and
    all affine cases. The y!=0 branch contradicts the area-one theorem;
    y=0 gives x=0,1,-1 by decidable field equality and cancellation.
    The four listed projective points are distinct and satisfy the equation.
    The smoothness check is also valid over an arbitrary characteristic-zero
    field; no elliptic group-law theorem is being used to prove the list.

Minor clarifications worth considering, not blockers
----------------------------------------------------
A. Lemma descent-gcd(3) permits c=0, while its proof immediately uses
   gcd(c,d) and the earlier item is stated for positive inputs. Section 1
   already says the zero-input gcd cases are handled separately, so this is
   covered by the shared infrastructure. Adding the sentence "If c=0 the
   assertion is immediate; otherwise ..." would make this local proof
   entirely self-contained.

B. The point [-1:0:1] is a projective representative rather than the
   canonical primitive tuple whose first nonzero coordinate is positive.
   The corresponding canonical tuple is (1,0,-1). This is normal projective
   notation and is not an error, but any explicit internal four-code list
   should normalize this representative (as the package's convention says).

Executable check
----------------
I independently ran python3 code/descent.py. It exited successfully with:
  3,931 coprime square-splitting cases;
  1,474 Pythagorean parameter cases;
  5,896 signed rational correspondence cases;
  four additional named examples, including negative x on E_5;
  33,489 rational pairs in the bounded E1 sanity check.

The program correctly labels these checks as finite arithmetic validation.
It also correctly states that no valid input to the square-area descent can
exist, so the main valid branch cannot be empirically exercised and its
preservation must be established symbolically. This distinction is important
and is maintained consistently in the chapter and code.
