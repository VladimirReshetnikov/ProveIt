\section{Purpose, results, and the scope of the source review}
\label{sec:scope}

The objective is a reusable way of doing mathematics in first-order
intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic, abbreviated \ipra.  The
objects of this theory are natural numbers.  Its language has the
primitive recursive function symbols, its logic is intuitionistic
first-order logic with equality, and induction is restricted to
quantifier-free formulas.  A mathematical construction therefore has two
obligations which ordinary mathematical notation can conceal: its objects
must have appropriate numerical presentations, and its proof must respect
the restricted induction scheme.

The framework developed here uses \emph{finite contracts}: a specified
primitive recursive construction, a finite description of the observations
it needs, and an arithmetic proof that those observations imply its output
guarantee.  Universal hypotheses about an infinite name are retained as
hypotheses when needed; each use is reduced to a particular finite
observation.  In addition to this general organization, we give detailed
proofs of three substantive applications:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A quantitative finite-window theorem for bounded monotone sequences,
and its bounded-cardinality counterpart.  These give primitive recursive
metastability bounds without assuming a convergence modulus.  We also
prove why a general final cutoff cannot be recovered from that information.
\item A local power-series branch for polynomials with arbitrary fixed
primitive recursive Cauchy coefficient names, from a finite contraction
certificate.  The input centre need only be an approximate root.  This
extends the representation permitted on a regular chart beyond the exact
algebraic coefficients used in the existing Puiseux package.  From a
supplied root name and derivative gap, we also construct the certificate
with a prescribed primitive recursive precision bound.
\item A uniform \(m\)-th-power gap certificate for integral points.  It
extends the square-completion Runge argument in the Siegel package to all
\(m\ge2\), includes an explicit certificate constructor, and gives a
complete elementary classification for
\(y^m=x^{mr}+x+1\), \(m\ge3\), \(r\ge1\).
\end{enumerate}
The underlying tools---bounded search, contraction, gap estimates, and
metastability---are established mathematical ideas.  The contribution is
their precise common arithmetic interface, the explicit extensions of the
identified packets, and the accompanying implementation and formalization
work.  No claim of priority over the mathematical literature is intended.

\subsection{What an embedding means in this report}

There are several legitimate interpretations of a mathematical theorem,
and they must not be silently interchanged.
\begin{description}
\item[Exact finite interpretation.]
An integer matrix, polynomial, finite group table, rational polytope, or
finite proof certificate is a natural-number code.  Its equality and
finite checks are decidable primitive recursive predicates.
\item[Fixed represented-family interpretation.]
A real, function, or series is described by a fixed PR family of finite
approximations.  Parameters and requested precisions are number variables.
Validity, equality, and continuity may be first-order formulas with
unbounded universal number quantifiers.  These formulas are not thereby
eligible as induction invariants.
\item[Finite-table interpretation.]
The inputs are finite tables, with a declared bound on their domain and
finite compatibility checks.  The theorem applies to every passing table.
A particular represented family supplies such tables by a separate PR
construction.  Arbitrary infinite objects can also be discussed externally
through these finite theorems, without adding a function sort to \ipra.
\item[Certificate-relative interpretation.]
A finite datum verifies a sufficient condition, such as a contraction
inequality or a polynomial identity.  Soundness of the certificate is one
theorem.  Its existence or construction for a proposed input class is a
different theorem.  A certificate containing the desired universal
conclusion as an unchecked field has not solved the mathematical problem.
\end{description}

These interpretations support broad mathematics while preserving the
actual strength of the theory.  They do not represent every real by a
natural number, supply arbitrary countable choice, or turn every total
computable algorithm into a primitive recursive one.  The numerical sort
of the native concise calculus remains the numerical sort of \ipra.

\subsection{Proof status}

The mathematical arguments in this report are fully developed written
proofs with explicit algorithms and induction audits.  The archive also
contains compiled Lean 4.32.0 files for the finite contract lemmas, finite
change budgets, the all-integer power-gap lemma, and a small genuine
object-\ipra induction example.  The power-series module defines precise
finite targets and checks concrete rational examples; it does not supply
proofs of all its general targets.

The large new mathematics has not been converted into complete
\texttt{Prf PRAAx} terms.  The formalization section gives the remaining
definitions, main lemma statements, and a dependency order that leads to
those terms.  An elementary compiled example demonstrates the intended
object-theory boundary; it is not a completed compiler for arbitrary
mathematical proofs.  General effective Siegel, Faltings, Roth,
Mordell--Weil generation, Chebotarev, Siegel--Walfisz, and
Bombieri--Vinogradov are not asserted as consequences of this framework.

\subsection{Relation to proof mining}

The emphasis on explicit moduli and finite observations is closely related
to proof mining.  General metatheorems in that subject have carefully
specified theories, logical forms, and extensionality restrictions
\cite{Kohlenbach}.  We use direct finite proofs here, and do not import a
general extraction theorem with stronger hypotheses than \ipra supports.
In particular, numerical realizability obtained merely by replacing all
recursive procedures by primitive recursive procedures is not a sound
interpretation of full intuitionistic logic \cite{Plisko}.  Our witnesses
are specified PR terms and our correctness proofs are assembled directly.
