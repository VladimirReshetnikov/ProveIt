\section{Resolved difficulties and remaining obligations}
\label{sec:final-status}

The framework resolves several recurring \emph{implementation and
representation} difficulties, and two substantial additional mathematical
classes.  Its status is best recorded by the information that each result
actually provides.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{longtable}{@{}P{.25\linewidth}P{.34\linewidth}P{.32\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Problem & Result provided & Remaining boundary\\
\midrule\endhead
Bounded invariants hidden in analysis prose & Explicit Booleanization,
guarded bounded-loop rule, finite observation composition. & A complete
general Lean proof compiler is still to be implemented.\\
Arbitrary PR program evaluation & Static symbols, finite tables, or
clocked traces. & No total universal PR evaluator exists.\\
Unknown precision & Rational gate bounds, explicit operation schedules,
and finite-grid integration. & Domain, magnitude, and modulus hypotheses
must be proved for each application.\\
Incoherent approximate witnesses & Finite residual minimization plus a
supplied isolation modulus gives a fast name. & Qualitative uniqueness
does not provide a PR isolation modulus.\\
Local branches for Cauchy coefficients & Contraction theorem; a certificate
producer from simple-root data; coefficient names, radii, tails, and residuals. & Singular chart
discovery and general exact Puiseux selection remain obstructed.\\
Monotone convergence without a modulus & Primitive recursive stable
windows with a supplied control function. & A final Cauchy modulus does
not follow; an explicit PR counterexample is given.\\
Integral points near an \(m\)-th power & Explicit height bounds,
certificate construction, complete lists, and a uniform family
classification. & No general height bound for all curves is obtained.\\
Rational change of coordinates & Numerator bounds with a supplied
denominator bound. & Unbounded rational denominators still prevent
general rational-point finiteness.\\
Mordell--Weil; prime distribution & A common interface for existing
conditional proofs and their unresolved inputs. & Quotient coverage,
field-algebra certificates, and the major analytic estimates are not
supplied by abstraction alone.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{center}

The most useful immediate implementation work is the common rational and
Booleanized finite-loop library.  It is independent of the deepest open
applications and directly reusable.  The next mathematical tasks should
be selected by their missing information: a genuine construction of
covering representatives, a uniform analytic parameter bound, a height
estimate for a new class, or a verified regularizing chart.  Merely
renaming one of those as a certificate does not resolve it.

\subsection{Reproducibility and validation}

The archive contains the full LaTeX sources and PDF, Lean modules,
exact-arithmetic Python programs, source hashes, and build records.
Finite checks cover invalid inputs, sign and parity, chart production,
power completion, denominator transport, contraction, and finite windows.
They verify the recorded implementations on the tested inputs; the
universal arguments and compiled proof terms have their separate status.
