CONTEXT-FREE CHAPTER: MATHEMATICAL AUDIT AND STATUS
==================================================

Final scope
-----------
26 grouped numbered results: 20 theorems, 1 lemma, 3 propositions, 2 corollarys.
Two additional numbered definitions specify CFG certificates and the exact
nondeterministic, empty-stack PDA convention.

Results proved in the human-readable chapter
--------------------------------------------
1. Derivation/parse-tree equivalence with explicit size and step bounds.
2. Nullable and productive saturation; emptiness and a bounded witness.
3. Empty-rule and unit-rule removal, followed by full Chomsky normal form.
4. Explicit grammar-size and forward/backward tree-size bounds.
5. CYK correctness, reconstruction, exact positive-tree node counts and
   finite table-operation bounds.
6. Independent direct fixed-point parsing for arbitrary CFGs, with empty
   intervals, ranked witnesses, and a Q-round stabilization bound.
7. Counting parses of the normalized grammar for a fixed word.
8. Union, concatenation, star, reversal, homomorphic images, context-free
   substitution and intersection with regular languages.
9. Disjointness against a regular language, CFG-to-regular inclusion, and
   equality with a finite language.
10. Finite EBNF compilation, including optionality and star/plus repetition,
    with explicit finite-certificate semantics for recursive references.
11. Both directions of CFG/nondeterministic-PDA equivalence for arbitrary
    finite push lengths, with first-pop decomposition and trace bounds.
12. PR PDA membership even in the presence of epsilon cycles.
13. Inverse homomorphism closure, including erasing maps, with a protected
    bottom marker and a finite buffer of image suffixes.
14. A pumping decomposition and uniform PR pumping-tree constructor.
15. Finiteness decision by the finite length window [2^M,2^(M+1)).
16. Equal-block CFL/nonregular example; an explicit PR separating word
    against every CFG for {a^n b^n c^n:n>=0}.
17. Explicit failures of intersection and complement closure.
18. A bounded quadratic-time tape recognizer for the three-block language,
    with the noncontracting grammar and hierarchy result cross-referenced
    to the effective-language chapter.

The balanced-parentheses unique-parse proof is cross-referenced to
count:dyck rather than duplicated.  The noncontracting grammar for the
three-block language is cross-referenced to eff:abc rather than duplicated.

Review findings and resolutions
-------------------------------
A separate internal mathematical review checked the normalization bounds,
CYK and direct-chart invariants, the PDA first-pop decomposition, the finite
buffer for erasing inverse homomorphisms, the pumping bound, the finite
length-window test, and the explicit separating-word algorithm.

The following conventions were made explicit during review:
* {a^n b^n c^n} includes n=0.  The effective-language chapter's grammar
  therefore includes a fresh isolated start with an empty alternative.
* Star and substitution do not invoke unrestricted finite collection of
  existential witnesses: the already proved PR parser constructs every
  factor tree, with a uniform finite output bound.
* A PDA segment must leave its protected suffix untouched until its final
  configuration.  This condition is needed by the triple-variable grammar.
* The inverse-homomorphism PDA can load erasing input letters even after
  its simulated stack has emptied, because a protected bottom marker stays
  in place until acceptance.
* Normalization preserves the language, not the number of old derivations
  or the original grammar's ambiguity.
* The inclusion decision is L(CFG) subset L(DFA).  The reverse direction
  and arbitrary CFG equality are not asserted decidable.

Formalization status
--------------------
These are complete non-formalized mathematical arguments with explicit
primitive recursive algorithms, certificate transformations and bounded
Boolean invariants.  The Lean file is an outline, not a compiled proof.
It defines all finite records and checker conditions and lists exact
algorithm and theorem interfaces in dependency order.

No machine-checked object-level iPRA derivation for this whole chapter is
claimed.  The required completion path is to implement the Nat-coded PR
functions, prove correspondence with convenient Lean finite-data models,
prove the stated Boolean invariants using quantifier-free induction, and
then assemble the intuitionistic first-order conclusions.  A proof solely
in Lean's unrestricted ambient logic would not replace these object-level
translation obligations.
