/- EXACT REPRESENTATIVE CLOSED TARGETS (informal notation for Fm builders)

All symbols below are their PR term constructors; validity predicates are
equalities of bits to 1. Universal closures bind only number variables.

T01: forall k u v w,
  Valid(k,u) and Valid(k,v) and Valid(k,w) ->
  append(k,append(k,u,v),w) = append(k,u,append(k,v,w)).
  Invariant: equality of lengths and all digits below that common length.

T02: forall M w,
  ENFAValid(M) and WordValid(alphabet(M),w) ->
  dfaAccept(determinize(M),w) = enfaAccept(M,w).
  Invariant: after prefix i, every one of n state bits agrees with the
  bounded reachability definition. Witness length <= (|w|+1)n+|w|.

T03: forall M N,
  DFAValid(M) and DFAValid(N) and sameAlphabet(M,N) ->
  (separatorFound(M,N) = 0 <-> forall w,
    WordValid(alphabet(M),w) -> dfaAccept(M,w)=dfaAccept(N,w)).
  Positive direction: fixed-point product table + word scan.
  Negative direction: separator witness length < states(M)*states(N).
  The unbounded forall over w is introduced after the arbitrary-word proof.

T04: forall G w,
  CFGValid(G) and WordValid(alphabet(G),w) ->
  (cyk(G,w)=1 <-> exists t, TreeCheck(G,start(G),w,t)=1).
  The forward witness is the explicit reconstructed tree. The backward
  proof fixes a supplied t, normalizes it, then scans its nodes.
  A stronger bounded existential uses the encoding bound for the reverse
  image of a CNF tree with 3*|w|-1 nodes (empty input separate).

T05: forall G w,
  CFGValid(G) and Generated(G,w) and |w|>=2^vars(CNF(G)) ->
  PumpCheck(G,w,pumpCuts(G,w))=1.
  PumpCheck includes w=u v x y z, |vxy|<=p, |vy|>0.
  For every supplied i prove a checked tree for u v^i x y^i z.
  No formula with an unbounded forall over trees is used as an induction
  motive. Use the parser's selected tree and fixed finite bounds.

T06: forall G w,
  NCValid(G) and WordValid(alphabet(G),w) ->
  (ncAccept(G,w)=1 <-> exists t, DerivationCheck(G,w,t)=1).
  Bound: V=sum_{i<=|w|} totalAlphabet(G)^i vertices, fewer than V steps.
  A finite simple-path encoding yields a primitive-recursive number bound.

T07: forall d,
  not (BooleanTotal(d) and forall e,
    (exists t,haltAt(e,e,t)=1) <-> exists t,outAt(d,e,t,1)=1).
  Use the explicit program constructor P_d and totality at its code.
  This is an intuitionistic refutation from a totality premise, not a
  universal termination theorem for all recursive programs.

T08: forall M n,
  DFAValid(M) and n>=states(M) ->
  sum_{i<=states(M)} denominatorCoeff(M,i)*countAccepted(M,n-i)=0.
  Integer expressions are encoded pairs. Prove finite polynomial
  adjugate identity by permutation involutions, then coefficient extraction.

T09: forall n, (n+1)*dyckCount(n)=binomial(2*n,n).
  Count finite enumerations; prove first-crossing reflection and its inverse,
