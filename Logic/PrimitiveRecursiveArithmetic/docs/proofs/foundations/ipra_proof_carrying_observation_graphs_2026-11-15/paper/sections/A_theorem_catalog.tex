\subsection*{Inherited versus new}

The trace theorem and stable-observation proof combinators are inherited from
existing packets and should be integrated into the main library.  The PCOG
graph fold, its staged-internalization formulation, the unified status
discipline, and the synthesis of demand, relational, resource, and observation
layers are the new organizational contribution of this package.  The finite
algorithms themselves draw on ideas already present throughout the repository.

\subsection*{No formal overclaim}

The three Lean files in this archive are not listed as compiled.  Two are
explicit blueprints.  The core graph file is designed to be close to a
compilable integration module, but the proposed import
\texttt{ConciseIPRA.FiniteTrace} does not yet exist at the inspected repository
path.  Its creation is milestone 1, not an assumption silently satisfied here.
