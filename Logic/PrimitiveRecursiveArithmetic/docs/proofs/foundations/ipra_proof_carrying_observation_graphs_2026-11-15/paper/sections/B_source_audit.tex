\subsection{Comparison with prior framework packets}

The report intentionally avoids rebranding prior contributions.

\begin{itemize}
\item Backward demand propagation appears in several earlier packets.  Here it
is connected explicitly to a proof-producing graph fold and stated with a
leastness theorem.
\item Finite relational viability and shrinking clouds appear in the unified
and finite-use packets.  Here the finite-horizon diagonal selection is proved
in a form directly usable for regular names and connected to gap collapse.
\item Proof-producing trace combinators exist in the local-global packet.  Here
they become the recursive node interface of a general graph compiler.
\item Stable observations from independent certificates exist in the spectral
packet.  Here they become a generic node class and exactness tier.
\item Height/list conversion and finite auxiliary exhaustion are developed in
prior packets.  Here they are made standard resource interfaces and contrasted
systematically with bare cardinality.
\end{itemize}

The synthesis is motivated by a concrete formal gap recorded repeatedly in the
repository: many sophisticated packets stop before a completed embedded proof
of their headline theorem.  A reusable compiler should reduce that gap to a
finite list of local \texttt{Prf} obligations.

\subsection{Audit limitations}

This was a targeted architectural audit, not a line-by-line verification of
all seventy-two packets.  Reported build statuses of inherited packet modules
were read from their source documentation and inspected declarations; they
were not rerun in this artifact environment.  The current package's Python and
PDF builds were rerun locally.  The Lean source was not, because the executable
was absent.
