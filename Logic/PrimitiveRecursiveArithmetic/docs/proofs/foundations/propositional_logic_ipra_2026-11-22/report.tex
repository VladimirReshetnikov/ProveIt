\vspace{18mm}
{\Huge\bfseries Propositional Logic}\par
\vspace{4mm}
{\LARGE in Intuitionistic Primitive\par Recursive Arithmetic}\par
\vspace{12mm}
{\Large\sffamily Finite proofs, Boolean certificates,\par and Kripke countermodels}\par
\vspace{13mm}
\color{black}
\begin{minipage}{.91\textwidth}
This report develops a broad, self-contained body of propositional
metamathematics within intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic.
Its central results are constructive classical completeness and a direct
finite saturation proof of intuitionistic Kripke completeness. It also
treats proof-system translations, analytic tableaux and classical cut
elimination, Boolean normal forms, SAT algorithms and NP-completeness,
finite interpolation and definability, negative translation, finite
Heyting semantics, and disjunction extraction.

Every positive theorem is accompanied by a finite construction or a proof
transformation. Explicit bounds and bounded invariants explain why the
arguments fit quantifier-free induction. Small Kripke models distinguish
classical tautologies from intuitionistic theorems.
\end{minipage}
\vfill
\color{navy}
{\large Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov}\par
{\large 22 November 2026}\par
\vspace{6mm}

of its main iPRA theorems.
\end{titlepage}
