\section{Reusable proof templates}

\subsection{Template 1: deterministic finite algorithm}
Input: fixed PR state, bound, QF invariant, base/step/finish contracts.  Normalize theorem-specific rewrites, invoke the trace kernel, specialize at the bound, and export an open contract.

\subsection{Template 2: bounded alternating statement}
Input: reflected atoms and explicit bounds.  Generate truth/falsity certificate layouts, checkers, canonical constructors, and reflection contracts.  Export a local decision theorem or retain the checked certificate as an input.

\subsection{Template 3: residual producer}
Input: a difficult quantitative theorem.  State the exact formula it must deliver, its coding conventions, and its parameters.  Keep the consumer graph checked independently and close by contract cut when the producer arrives.

\subsection{Template 4: finite relation}
Input: bounded state sets and compatibility.  Compute backward viability, search the least viable successor, and either return a finite path or erase choice through a stable observer.

\subsection{Template 5: coherent path}
Input: viable initial state and PR extension with preservation and compatibility contracts.  Iterate by primitive recursion and prove the open path invariant by QF induction.

\subsection{Template 6: regular name}
Input: finite observations, adjacent motion bounds, and a tail modulus.  Prove finite triangle estimates and sample at the modulus.  Do not infer the tail modulus from metastability.

\subsection{Template 7: guarded operation}
Input: a total implementation, positive margin, demand transformer, and local estimate.  Reflect the guard, compute demand before the trace, prove the intended branch under the guard, and leave behavior outside the public domain unspecified.

\subsection{Template 8: finite net}
Input: net generator, coverage certificate, modulus, and local verifier.  Verify all net points, search a covering cell for an arbitrary coded query, and transport the estimate with an explicit error budget.

\subsection{Template 9: height-bounded list}
Input: code box, verifier, canonicalization, height, and height-bound formula.  Enumerate, filter, deduplicate, prove soundness unconditionally and completeness under the height residual.
