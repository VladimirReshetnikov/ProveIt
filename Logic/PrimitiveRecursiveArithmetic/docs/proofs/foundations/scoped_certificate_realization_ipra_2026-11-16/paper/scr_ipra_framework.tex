{\Huge\bfseries\color{deepblue} Scoped Certificate Realization\par}
\vspace{0.35cm}
{\LARGE A General Proof-Producing Framework for Mathematics in\par}
\vspace{0.15cm}
{\LARGE Intuitionistic Primitive Recursive Arithmetic\par}
\vspace{1.25cm}
{\large A repository-grounded research report for the \repo\ project\par}
\vspace{0.5cm}
{\normalsize Baseline: \code{f444b76e8480ebea128c15b4a59f158e14f478cc}\par}
\vfill
\begin{minipage}{0.87\textwidth}
\small
This report develops a reusable method for turning finite, quantitative
mathematical arguments into literal derivations of the checked judgment
\(\Gamma\turnstile A\).  Its central device is \emph{certificate escape
analysis}: auxiliary witnesses remain under the existential scope in which
they are needed, and a bounded least selector is introduced only when a
certificate itself must escape into the externally visible theorem.
\end{minipage}
\vfill
{16 November 2026}
\end{titlepage}
