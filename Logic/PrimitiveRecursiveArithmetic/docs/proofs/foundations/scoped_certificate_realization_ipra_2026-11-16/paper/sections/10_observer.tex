\section{Stable observers, quotients, and exact-label gates}
\label{sec:observer}

Many mathematical objects have valid finite presentations but no natural
canonical presentation: a polynomial's roots may be permuted, an eigenspace
may have many bases, a manifold may have many atlases, and a homotopy class may
have many simplicial representatives.  SCR treats presentation choice as a
local certificate and exports only proved-invariant observations.

\subsection{Exact observer erasure}

Let \(V(q,c)\) validate certificates and let \(O(q,c)\) be a term-valued
observer.  Suppose iPRA proves
\[
 V(q,c)\wedge V(q,d)\;\longrightarrow\;O(q,c)=O(q,d).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact observer erasure]
\label{thm:observer-exact}
The relation
\[
  \mathsf{Out}(q,o)\;:\!\Longleftrightarrow\;
  \exists c\,[V(q,c)\wedge O(q,c)=o]
\]
is single-valued.  Every consumer invariant under equality of \(o\) can be
composed with a sealed producer for \(V\) without selecting a certificate.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Open witnesses \(c,d\) for two outputs.  Observer independence gives
\(O(q,c)=O(q,d)\); the defining equalities then give \(o=o'\).  For a consumer,
open one valid certificate, introduce its output relation, apply the consumer,
and close the scope.
\end{proof}

For root sets, elementary symmetric polynomials are exact observers.  For a
finite matrix, the characteristic polynomial and traces of powers are exact
observers under change of basis.  For a finite chain map induced by a carrier,
the homology map is an exact observer once any two selections are proved
contiguous and the prism homotopy is verified.

\subsection{Quantitative clouds}

Exact equality is often too strong.  Let \(d\) be an encoded rational metric
or seminorm and suppose
\[
 V(q,c)\wedge V(q,d)\;\longrightarrow\;
 d(O(q,c),O(q,d))\le\delta(q).
\]
Then valid certificates form an output cloud of diameter at most \(\delta\).
Downstream operations carry moduli which transform input diameters into output
diameters.  Composition is by arithmetic on explicit error budgets.

\begin{theorem}[Diameter composition]
Suppose \(F\) has modulus \(m\):
\[
  d_X(x,x')\le m(q,\eps)\to d_Y(Fx,Fx')\le\eps.
\]
If a certificate cloud for \(x\) has diameter at most \(m(q,\eps)\), then the
image observer cloud has diameter at most \(\eps\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Open two valid certificates, apply their diameter premise, then the supplied
modulus theorem.  Since the conclusion contains neither certificate, close
both existential scopes.
\end{proof}

This is the correct unconditional output at collisions.  It supports finite
precision consumers without pretending to have a globally coherent branch.

\subsection{The exact-label gate}

Suppose candidate labels \(\ell_0,\ldots,\ell_{r-1}\) are separated by
\(g>0\), and every valid observation lies within \(e\) of one label.  If
\(2e<g\), two different labels cannot both explain valid observations.

\begin{lemma}[Gap stabilization]
In a metric encoded by rational inequalities, if
\[
 d(\ell_i,\ell_j)\ge g\quad(i\ne j),
 \qquad d(o,\ell_i)\le e,
 \qquad d(o',\ell_j)\le e,
 \qquad d(o,o')\le\delta,
\]
and \(2e+\delta<g\), then \(i=j\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
If \(i\ne j\), the triangle inequality gives
\(g\le d(\ell_i,\ell_j)\le e+\delta+e<g\), a contradiction.  The formal
version clears rational denominators and uses the checked order library.
\end{proof}

The gate yields an exact branch label only on a region with a proved positive
gap.  At the discriminant locus the API falls back to a cluster or invariant.
This resolves, in maximal constructive form, the recurring exact-root,
eigenvector, rotation-axis, and chart-selection difficulties.

\subsection{Witnessed setoids}

A general quotient interface consists of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item a certificate type represented by natural codes;
\item a reflected validity predicate \(V(c)\);
\item a reflected equivalence witness \(E(c,d,w)\);
\item proofs of reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity at the level needed by
      consumers;
\item observer invariance under \(E\).
\end{enumerate}
No quotient representative is normalized unless a separate normalization
contract is proved.  This is often smaller than coding a full equivalence
relation as a decidable Boolean: equality may be witnessed by an ideal
combination, a chain homotopy, a coordinate transition, or a finite zigzag.
The witness itself is sealed after observer invariance has been used.

\subsection{Coherent names across precision}

To obtain a primitive-recursive Cauchy name rather than unrelated finite
clouds, an application must supply a coherence contract.  A common sufficient
form is
\[
  d(o_{k+1},o_k)\le 2^{-k-c}
\]
for a fixed \(c\), verified by a finite transition certificate.  A summable
motion budget gives a Cauchy modulus by a finite geometric-tail estimate.  If
only finite-horizon compatibility is known, SCR exports finite coherent paths
or metastable windows, not an infinite name.  Again, the missing tail modulus
is a producer obligation, not a logical inference.
