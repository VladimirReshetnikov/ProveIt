\section{Audited setting: what is already checked}
\label{sec:audit}

\subsection{The canonical endpoint}

The correct endpoint for new work in this part of \repo\ is not a semantic
claim that a host program terminates, nor a theorem in Mathlib about ordinary
natural numbers.  It is an inhabitant of the deeply embedded judgment
\[
   \Prf\;\PRAAx\;\Gamma\;\varphi,
   \qquad\text{written}\qquad
   \Gamma\turnstile\varphi.
\]
The current \code{ConciseIPRA/Lean/ConciseIPRA/PR.lean} defines primitive
recursive symbols \(\mathsf{PR}(k)\), terms \(\PTm(n)\), and the axiom family
\(\PRAAx\).  The latter contains the defining equations for projection,
composition, and primitive recursion, the two successor axioms, and induction
for quantifier-free formulas.  The logic in \code{Deduction.lean} is
intuitionistic natural deduction with equality, scoped simultaneous
substitution, and the structural theorems
\[
 \Prf.\mathsf{subst},\qquad
 \Prf.\mathsf{weaken},\qquad
 \Prf.\mathsf{trans},\qquad
 \Prf.\mathsf{shift}.
\]
These are not mere conveniences.  They are the generic proof compiler already
present in the repository: substitution specializes a mathematical contract,
weakening aligns local contexts, and \code{Prf.trans} performs simultaneous
cut.

The checked native scalar core remains valuable as a compact backend.
The checked native bi-interpretation module proves both
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \NPrf\;\Delta\;A
  &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \Delta.\mathsf{map}(\mathsf{toPRA})\turnstile A.\mathsf{toPRA},\\
  \Gamma\turnstile\varphi
  &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \NPrf\bigl(\Gamma.\mathsf{map}(\mathsf{ofPRA})\bigr)
       (\mathsf{ofPRA}(\varphi)).
\end{aligned}
\]
The five-rule clause calculus \(\KPrf\) additionally has checked substitution,
cut, and export to \(\NPrf\).  SCR therefore treats \(\KPrf\) as an optional
compressed intermediate representation, not as a competing foundation.  The
portable public interface remains \(\Prf\;\PRAAx\).

\subsection{The checked arithmetic substrate}

The \code{IPRADerivations} library has advanced beyond a toy arithmetic core.
At the audited baseline it contains object-theory derivations for semiring
algebra, cancellation, integers as differences, order and truncated
subtraction, pairing and projections, powers, parity, bounded sums and
products, descent, pigeonhole, permutations, quotient and remainder, gcd and
B\'ezout, bounded search, finite Fubini, and finite linear algebra.  The
relevant engineering lesson is that a packet should import these results at
arbitrary terms and under local hypotheses, not reproduce them in a private
host model.

Two checked tools are especially central here.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \code{Core/Basic.lean} supplies open quantifier-free induction
\[
  \frac{\;\turnstile \phi(0,\vec x)\qquad
        \phi(z,\vec x)\turnstile\phi(Sz,\vec x)\;}
       {\;\turnstile\phi(n,\vec x)\;}
\]
with all parameters explicit, together with instantiation of closed open-formula
derivations at arbitrary terms.
\item \code{Core/Search.lean} defines the least bounded zero
\(\mu_f(N,x)\), returning the sentinel \(N\) when no zero occurs, and proves
inside iPRA its bound, correctness, and minimality.  Thus a certificate can be
canonically named once---and only once---an actual numerical cutoff and a
reflected verifier are available.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The checked bridge and its remaining asymmetry}

The Foundation--Concise bridge translates closed theorems in both directions.
Its implementation is already more general internally than the public API
suggests.  In the Concise-to-Foundation direction,
\code{toFoundation\_provable\_of\_axioms} transports an arbitrary
\(\Prf\) derivation with a finite local context under any assignment of free
variables to Foundation terms.  The public wrappers then close the free
variables and specialize to an empty context.  In the reverse direction,
\code{hilbertToConcise} is uniform in the images of free variables, but
\code{theoryToConcise} is exposed only for closed theory consequences.
Section~\ref{sec:bridge} explains how to finish the open-sequent interface
without changing either object theory.

\subsection{The packet layer and the actual gap}

The proof-packet catalogue explicitly warns that the filed archives were not
rerun, compiled, or checked during placement.  Many packets have strong finite
mathematics and exact reference programs, yet their Lean files are described
as scaffolds, blueprints, or targets.  The recurring gap has two different
causes and they must not be conflated.

\begin{description}
\item[Proof-production gap.]  The finite objects, bounds, and local lemmas are
available, but no term of type \(\Prf\;\PRAAx\;\Gamma\;A\) has been assembled.
This is the gap addressed by SCR.
\item[Producer gap.]  The theorem requires data not supplied by the argument:
a uniform height bound, a complete list of covering representatives, an
explicit convergence modulus, a separation gap, a finite subcover, or an
effective exceptional constant.  No generic compiler can manufacture such
mathematics.  SCR isolates it as a producer boundary.
\end{description}

For example, the filed Faltings and Siegel packets explicitly stop at missing
height information; the Mordell--Weil packet does not give a uniform generator
algorithm; exact axis selection is restricted by a constructive obstruction;
and several analytic packets state results only for fixed names equipped with
moduli.  A trustworthy framework must make those distinctions visible in its
final theorem type.

\subsection{Prior framework work}

The repository already contains several ambitious interfaces: finite
observations, certified finitary presentations, proof-carrying observation
graphs, finite-query normal form (FQNF), certified bounded interaction programs
(CBIP), and native clause programs.  Their shared achievements are substantial:
fixed finite queries, reflected checkers, whole-path certificates, relational
witness clouds, stable observations, and explicit producer boundaries.  Their
shared unresolved point is also clear from their status files: the generic
object-theory compiler remains largely a human proof or Lean blueprint.

SCR does not discard those ideas.  It changes the logical normal form beneath
them.  A global least certificate is no longer the default.  The default is a
locally scoped existential, eliminated by the checked natural-deduction rule
that was designed for exactly this purpose.  Packing and bounded selection are
recoverable optimizations for the cases in which static escape analysis proves
they are needed.
