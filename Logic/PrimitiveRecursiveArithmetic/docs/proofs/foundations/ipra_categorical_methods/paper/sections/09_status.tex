\section{Results, remaining obligations, and validation}

The main outcome is a reusable way to organize finite mathematical data and
their arithmetic proofs, together with concrete producers for categorical
constructions and polynomial certificates. Its strength comes from exposing
the hypotheses that make a construction work. The following comparison records
what this changes in the inspected repository difficulties; it does not repeat
the proofs or claim that a general change of language resolves their missing
mathematical inputs.

\begin{longtable}{@{}P{.23\linewidth}P{.70\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Question & Result and remaining boundary\\
\midrule\endhead
Reusable mathematical interfaces & Finite-demand contracts compose with
explicit backward queries. Category laws, universal properties and finite
gluing receive numerical implementations. Each application still needs its
local arithmetic contracts proved in the target theory.\\
Finite algebra certificates & The Macaulay closure test constructs action
identities and a complete rational solution list at supplied successful degree
caps. This extends the constructors in \cite{repo-witnesses}. A failed cap is
not a proof of inconsistency; no general terminating cap search is supplied.\\
Mordell--Weil & The existing rational-halving and supplied-cover descent
remain valid conditional interfaces \cite{repo-mw}. The skeleton analysis
explains why a component bound cannot generally replace representative data.
No covering-list constructor for arbitrary elliptic curves is proved.\\
Siegel & Finite algebra sections can be synthesized by the new closure test.
This improves one concrete certificate-production step. The general
approximation cover, its uniform existence, and the remaining geometric
reductions in \cite{repo-siegel} are not established here.\\
Faltings & Successful finite algebra certificates give explicit heights and
lists for their systems. Positive-dimensional curves with finite rational
point sets need not admit those certificates. The general height/list target
of \cite{repo-faltings} remains open in this report.\\
Puiseux with arbitrary names & The obstruction in \cite{repo-puiseux} remains:
arbitrary computable complex Cauchy coefficient names do not support a
uniform computable producer of a finite ramification integer and individually
accessible exact Puiseux coefficient names. Approximate aggregates and
conditional regularity statements have different specifications.\\
Coherent infinite objects & Finite gluing and explicitly liftable finite
towers have constructive producers. A node at every finite horizon alone
does not supply a computable coherent branch. The counterexample proves a
limit of that proposed inference, not the failure of finite limits.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

In particular, this report does not prove general Mordell--Weil generation,
Siegel, or Faltings in iPRA, and makes no independence claim for those
theorems. The algebraic and categorical ingredients are established methods;
the additions here concern their explicit arithmetic interfaces, the bounded
certificate producer, and the precise scope of uniform constructions.

\subsection{What was executed}

The four Python suites were run with Python \texttt{3.12.14}; every program
returned exit code zero. The records are
\path{validation/reproduction.json}, \path{category_checks.json},
\path{contract_checks.json}, \path{descent_checks.json}, and
\path{macaulay_checks.json}, with the latter four also under
\path{validation/}. All arithmetic in these diagnostics is exact. The counts
below describe finite checks, not independent universal theorems.

\begin{longtable}{@{}P{.23\linewidth}P{.70\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule

\midrule\endhead
Finite categories & 9 category/opposite checks; 4 malformed-table
rejections; 7 functor-category constructions; 75 Yoneda presentations and

These checks exercise conventions, guards and examples that could invalidate
an implementation: arrow composition order, empty cases, failed coverage,
incompatible residues, insufficient degree caps and redundant generators.
Their success supports the supplied reference programs. It does not establish
their universal correctness or constitute an arithmetic proof checker.

\subsection{Formalization status and concrete next steps}

\textbf{No new Lean source in this archive has been kernel checked.}
No Lean executable was available in the inspected environment. The delivered
object-calculus expressions are uncompiled source-level constructions using
the actual \texttt{Prf PRAAx} interface; the larger specifications define
propositions still to be proved. No compilation result or theorem-dependency
audit is claimed for these files. Section~\ref{sec:lean} gives the exact
definitions and the ordered completion route.

The first milestone is to compile the small object-calculus constructions
against the pinned repository and discharge their source-level issues before
claiming any checked derivation. The next is to implement the common numerical
list and bounded-predicate library, then complete one uniform constructor,
such as finite compatible-tuple limits, from its PR code through its Boolean
invariant to its \texttt{Prf PRAAx} theorem. That would validate the shared
method on a substantial example.

For the new algebraic contribution, the decisive milestone is the bounded
row solver with its solution-or-separator specification, followed by checked
action-identity synthesis and the rational-grid proof. Those steps can be
completed without resolving the separate existence of successful degree caps.
Only after the intended arithmetic derivations exist should the repository's
native bi-interpretation be applied. The missing approximation covers,
curve bounds and exact-name selection principles remain mathematical research
obligations rather than work delegated to a translation layer.
