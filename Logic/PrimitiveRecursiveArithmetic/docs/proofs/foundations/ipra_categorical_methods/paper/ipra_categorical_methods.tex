\begin{abstract}
We develop a reusable arithmetic interface for mathematical constructions and
their proofs, with a substantial category-theoretic layer. The interface combines
explicit numerical presentations, finite observations, forward witness maps,
backward finite demands, and derivations of the local laws in intuitionistic
primitive recursive arithmetic (iPRA). We distinguish uniformly coded finite
structures from fixed primitive recursive presentations and external schemes
over programs. Within these boundaries we give complete human proofs of finite
limits and colimits, finite exponentials, Yoneda, finite Kan extensions,
adjunction identities, constructive equivalences, and finite gluing. A bounded
linear algebra construction produces polynomial multiplication certificates
from supplied degree caps and then a complete rational solution list, even for
redundant, noncommuting action matrices. This extends the certificate-production
interfaces in the inspected ProveIt packets. Two explicit computability
obstructions explain why finite cardinality or compatible observations at every
finite horizon need not provide the missing global witnesses. Lean definitions,
object-calculus proof expressions, and a detailed completion route are included;
the new Lean sources are uncompiled and no completed formalization of the main
theorems is claimed. Exact executable diagnostics accompany the human proofs.
\end{abstract}
