\subsection{Residual-work taxonomy}

After translation into this framework, every remaining gap should be classified
as one of:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{R\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Finite-kernel engineering:} the theorem and invariant are known,
      but a shared code, arithmetic, fold, or path library is not yet embedded.
\item \textbf{Quantitative mathematics:} a separation bound, modulus,
      contraction estimate, zero-free constant, or combinatorial invariant is
      genuinely missing.
\item \textbf{Producer problem:} a verifier is complete, but no uniform
      primitive-recursive method is known for producing accepted certificates.
\item \textbf{Impossible selector:} the requested exact output is discontinuous
      or would decide a known noncomputable problem; the statement itself must
      be changed or guarded.
\end{enumerate}

This taxonomy prevents library work from being misdiagnosed as a foundational
obstruction and prevents ineffective mathematics from being hidden as routine
encoding.
