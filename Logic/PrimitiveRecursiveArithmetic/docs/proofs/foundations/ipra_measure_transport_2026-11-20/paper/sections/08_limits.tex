The limitations are mathematical rather than merely a shortage of
formalization. The halting reduction in Proposition~\ref{m:no_rate}
rules out a uniform convergence-rate extractor on the stated class.
The diagonal example rules out a continuous operation on the proposed
\(L^1\) quotient itself. The kernel example rules out a norm estimate
that would incorrectly replace a column bound by an average bound.
These counterexamples help choose the correct statements before
writing proof code.

Related constructive and reverse-mathematical work confirms that
the formulation and data attached to convergence matter
\cite{avigad-dean-rute,metastable-dct}. We use those sources for context,
not as a theorem asserting that every classical convergence principle
is unprovable in our exact presentation of \ipra. Our own obstruction
is the explicit bounded-simulation example above. Similarly, a
general proof-mining result may produce a functional of higher
recursion strength; it must not be called primitive recursive without
an independent bound. Here the finite energy argument supplies that
bound directly for each fixed primitive-recursive challenge.

The framework therefore separates three productive outcomes:
construct a named object when a rate is available; construct a finite
stable observation when only a finite stability theorem is justified;
or exhibit the exact missing datum when neither construction follows.
For the concrete classes developed here, that distinction supplies
working constructions in integration, categorical analysis,
continuous semantics, complex analysis, and algebraic topology, with
their finite arithmetic proof obligations visible.
