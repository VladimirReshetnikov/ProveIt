\begin{abstract}
We develop a finite arithmetic interface for continuous and abstract
mathematics in intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic. The main
constructions use rational dyadic data, regular names, explicitly bounded
operators, and verified error propagation. A stable clipping inequality
repairs a source-level Vitali argument and constructs an integrable limit
from a bounded-metric Cauchy rate and uniform tail data. Coherent dyadic
masses yield a density from energy certificates, and a local sibling bound
produces those certificates. An energy cap alone yields a finite
metastability theorem, with an explicit obstruction to extracting a
uniform convergence rate. Bounded operators, integral-quantifier semantics,
Rips interleavings, contractive integral equations, and holomorphic
transforms are developed with their exact hypotheses. Counterexamples
identify invalid diagonal substitution and unsuitable operator norms.
The package contains complete human proofs, a standalone compiled Lean
kernel of finite rational lemmas, and detailed definitions and proof plans
for the remaining object-\ipra\ derivations. The new analytic endpoints
are not claimed to be fully reified as \(\Prf\,\mathrm{PRAAx}\) proofs.
\end{abstract}
