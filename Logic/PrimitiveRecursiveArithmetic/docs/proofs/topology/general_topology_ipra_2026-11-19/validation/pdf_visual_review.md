through five contact sheets. Separate full-page inspection included pages
17, 18, 43 and 46. The final source-status tables and bibliography were
also inspected in the complete contact-sheet review.

are legible. The three-pass final LaTeX build reports no undefined references,
undefined citations, overfull boxes, underfull boxes, or compilation errors.

verification scope is recorded separately in `source_audit.json`,
