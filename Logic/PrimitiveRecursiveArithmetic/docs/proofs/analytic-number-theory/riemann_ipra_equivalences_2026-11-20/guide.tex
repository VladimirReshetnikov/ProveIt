\section*{Guide to the formalizations}\pdfbookmark[1]{Guide to the formalizations}{guide}
All formulas called \emph{closed} below are terms in the first-order ProveIt syntax. The family column uses R for ordinary RH, D for Dirichlet GRH and K for Dedekind ERH.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{.32\linewidth}p{.12\linewidth}p{.48\linewidth}@{}}\toprule
Arithmetic form & Family & Where its equivalence is proved\\\midrule
Dyadic prime-power discrepancy & R, D, K & Closed syntax and Lean interpretation. Classical zero correspondence in Sections~\ref{sec:local}--\ref{sec:families}.\\[3pt]
No counterexample, first failure, cumulative counts, sparse clean prefixes, additive slack & R, D, K & Internal iPRA derivations for all forms and a first-failure extractor and a computed unique slack, Sections~\ref{sec:audits}--\ref{sec:slack}.\\[3pt]
Integer probes, dyadic endpoints, weighted windows, cumulative slack and mixed-radix prefixes & R, D, K & Constructive finite proofs and checked equivalence of the denotations of the closed formulas, Section~\ref{sec:discrete}.\\[3pt]
Prime powers at breakpoints & R, D, K & Lean equivalence of the closed formulas in the standard interpretation, Section~\ref{sec:discrete}.\\[3pt]
Prime-only or full products, arbitrary dilation and summation order & R, D, K & Closed syntax, finite perturbation bound for witness forms, and a Mellin-transform converse for each fixed order, Section~\ref{sec:smoothing}.\\[3pt]
Mertens sums and masked determinants & R, D, K & Checked integer matrix identity and finite proof of the coefficient identification, Section~\ref{sec:matrices}. The analytic bridges are in Appendices~\ref{sec:mertens-start}--\ref{sec:dedekind}.\\[3pt]
Gcd quadratic form and sawtooth square mean & R, D, K & Arithmetic conversion of growth witnesses with explicit constants, Section~\ref{sec:energy}.\\[3pt]
Dyadic series, bounded or with a Cauchy modulus & R, D, K & Arithmetic conversion in both directions, with a prescribed primitive-recursive rate, Section~\ref{sec:series}.\\[3pt]
Farey rank discrepancies, absolute and squared & R, D & Closed formulas; proof via discrepancy, primitive roots and progression Mertens sums, Section~\ref{sec:fareyrank}.\\[3pt]
Lagarias divisor-sum bounds & R & Uniform rational upper tests and strict lower tests with a precision witness, Section~\ref{sec:lagarias}.\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
There are infinitely many equivalent sentences even within a single row. For example, one may fix any primitive-recursive offset on cumulative failure counts, or fix any dilation $a\ge2$ and summation order $j\ge0$ in the analytic prime test. Individual data, quantifier order and proof status give more information than a count of formulas.

\medskip\noindent
The universal prime test, the hierarchy and the supplementary Mertens sums come from the supplied archive. The cumulative and additive-witness derivations, numerical alternatives, smoothing theory, divisor matrices, energies, convergence rates, Farey tests and rational divisor-sum tests are developed in this edition.
