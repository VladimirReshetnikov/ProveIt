\section{Checking the formalization and open proof obligations}\label{sec:buildnew}
The default Lake target checks the new and inherited modules. The file \code{verification/Audit.lean} prints representative types and the Lean kernel axioms on which those declarations depend. A proof of a statement about \code{Prf PRAAx} may use extensionality of functions when identifying finite variable assignments in Lean; the rules admitted inside \code{Prf} remain the rules of iPRA. Mathlib proofs about ordinary matrices or rational inequalities are separate Lean theorems.

The energy example $E_6=17/5$ for the ordinary Mertens function is reproduced three ways: by the sum over $d$, the double gcd sum, and rational integration over the affine pieces of the sawtooth. The scripts also exercise empty Farey progressions, the endpoint $1/1$ and Lagarias's equality at one.

\paragraph{Further research questions.}
Can the finite Euler-factor and permutation-enumeration bridges be derived inside \code{Prf PRAAx}, connecting the compiler's particular integer codes directly to the polynomial and determinant identities? Can one convert a supplied universal prime bound into Mertens witnesses using iPRA, with an explicit dependence on the requested exponent and field? Finally, for rational rectangles away from the critical line, can a single PR construction provide certified boundary clearances and reciprocals for all the coded fields? These are concrete additions to the current proof scope. The external analytic arguments establish standard-model correspondences without assuming answers to these questions.
