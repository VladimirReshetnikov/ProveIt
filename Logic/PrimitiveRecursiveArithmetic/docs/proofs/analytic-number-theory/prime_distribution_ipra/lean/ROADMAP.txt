LEAN DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

The full mathematical obligations and code interfaces are in the PDF’s
Lean chapter and the preceding proof chapters. This file is a navigation aid.

1. FiniteArithmetic: encode signed integers, rationals with positive
   denominators, lists, bounded Boolean quantifiers, division, gcd, prime
   tests, factorization, phi, mu, factorial and finite sums as fixed PR codes.
   Verify their defining recurrences by quantifier-free induction.

2. RationalAnalysis: finite logarithm/exponential polynomials, their tails,
   finite telescoping calculus, bounded bisection, positive margins for
   inversion, finite product/convolution rules and rational-gap elimination.
   Fix precision before induction; never induct on an unbounded precision
   quantifier or import arbitrary real order as decidable.

3. FiniteCharacters: unit groups, exponent tables, subgroup extension,
   orthogonality and compatible separated numerical root names.

4. LFunctionJets: bounded partial summation, moment recurrences, the finite
   N-by-N convolution rectangle, coefficient-tail allowances and the endpoint
   identity. Then DirichletMargin and DirichletWitness, ending in the actual
   factorial-scaled arithmetic matrix at Y(q,B).

5. SelbergFinite: CRT, Mobius inversion, quadratic-form diagonalization,
   exact weights and the harmonic lower denominator. The asymptotic BT
   consequence uses an integer root and explicit threshold.

6. SharpBrunTitchmarsh: three separated-kernel estimates, explicit rational
   grid maximization and residual cancellation, weighted cosecant/large
   sieve, Ramanujan sums, decreasing weights, exact finite denominator
   certificate, and the two overlapping ranges. The integer endpoint is
   the four-parameter lower-log matrix in Targets.lean; rational endpoints
   use a numerator and positive denominator.

7. VaughanFinite and GramFinite: exact finite algebra valid for rational
   weights. Prove aggregate approximation slack before using finite maxima
   or sums over moduli. SW and BV outer existential constants must remain
   before ALL later endpoint and precision variables.

8. NumberFields/Chebotarev: implement and certify the proposed field/ideal/
   Frobenius interface. These are unfinished mathematical-library obligations.
   The modulus reduction is conditional on estimates not proved here.

9. ObjectAssembly: construct genuine PR programs, verify their semantics,
   build Prf PRAAx [] of the actual formulas, and only then apply
   pra_iff_native to obtain NPrf. Semantic Lean theorems about Nat/Rat/Real
   or Mathlib’s ordinary Dirichlet theorem do not supply this derivation.

There are no analytic theorem axioms or sorry-based proof claims in the
supplied files. That is a status fact about a specification, not evidence
that the specified theorems have been formally proved.

Research gaps for full SW, BV and Chebotarev remain as stated in the report.
Completing their Lean development requires solving those gaps first; it
cannot be described as transcription of a complete proof in this package.
