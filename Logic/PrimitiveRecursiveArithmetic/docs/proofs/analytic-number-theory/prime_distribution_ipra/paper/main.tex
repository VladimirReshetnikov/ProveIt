\vspace{8mm}
{\Large Explicit Dirichlet and sharp Brun--Titchmarsh proofs;\par constructive obligations for three density theorems\par}
\vspace{14mm}
{\large Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov\par}
{\large 15 November 2026\par}
\vspace{12mm}
\noindent\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
\textbf{Scope of this delivery.} The package proves Dirichlet's theorem with an explicit primitive recursive bound, a rational Selberg sieve inequality, and the sharp Brun--Titchmarsh bound with coefficient $2$ for every rational $x>q$. It gives a detailed finite arithmetic realization of these human proofs. It develops exact arithmetic targets and finite reductions for Chebotarev, Siegel--Walfisz, and Bombieri--Vinogradov, whose full $\ipra$ proofs remain unfinished here. This is a partial resolution of the five-theorem request.

\medskip
\textbf{Formalization status.} Human proofs and an explicit arithmetic realization are supplied. The Lean sources are an uncompiled specification and development outline. No completed object-language derivation is claimed.
\end{minipage}
