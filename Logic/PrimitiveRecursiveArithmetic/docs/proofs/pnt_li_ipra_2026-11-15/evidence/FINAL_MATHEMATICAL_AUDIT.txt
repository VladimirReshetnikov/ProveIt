Independent mathematical and arithmetic audit
============================================

Reviewed analytic_notes/analytic_core.tex after its expansion to E1--E8
and A1--A8, and transfer_notes.tex. This is human mathematical review,
not kernel verification. No repository build or Lean compilation was run.

Review-time file names map to the delivered package as follows:
  analytic_notes/analytic_core.tex -> tex/analytic.tex

Final coefficient check
-----------------------
The later simplification to the coefficient 2^-26 in the stretched
exponential was independently checked. With D=2^241, the dyadic root
choice gives q > (log n)^(1/10)/D^(1/10) - 1. Consequently
  2^(-(q-5)) <= 64 exp(-(log 2)(log n)^(1/10)/D^(1/10)).
Since D^(1/10)<2^25 and log 2>=1/2, the claimed coefficient follows.
The threshold n>=2^(D*6^10) ensures q>=6. No further scope reduction
was identified in the final review.

Analytic route
--------------
The proposed log^-9 strip is coherent and constructive in its quantitative
form. The cosine identity supplies a lower bound for |zeta| at sigma>1;
a finite horizontal derivative estimate transfers a strictly positive
lower bound to the strip. Thus this avoids the nonconstructive inference
from not-zero to apartness. The chosen constants 2^-88, 2^-71 and 2^86
check out. The pole neighborhood uses the explicit regular function
w=(s-1)zeta and gives positive denominator separation.

The smoothed Perron argument, tail bounds, rational contour movement,
and desmoothing estimates were checked. The final A8 bound is
  |psi(x)-x| <= x * 2^(-k/8),
not x * 2^(-k) / 8. The initially irrational contour sides were repaired
in the current analytic notes: Q=k+2, delta=2^-88 Q^-9,
alpha=1-delta, c=1+1/b, b=ceil(log_2 y). These are rational and retain
the numerical margins. E1's finite Euler identity has the correct
endpoint convention. E5's finite product error and its positivity slack
are sufficient. E7's displayed Dirichlet-tail cutoff is generous enough.
E8's 1/M vertical closing estimate works uniformly even when v=1;
there is no need for a lower bound on |v-1|.

The analytic route proves a nonoptimal exponent 1/10. It does not prove
the de la Vallee Poussin square-root exponent, Korobov--Vinogradov exponent,
or a Riemann-hypothesis estimate. Every inverse logarithmic order and
every fixed factorial asymptotic truncation do follow from exponent 1/10.

Finite arithmetization
---------------------
The proposed explicit derivative bound for the pole-subtracted integrand
is safe. I independently derived a smaller coarse bound. The missing
point in a bare Taylor-cell sketch is the amplification of rational
sample error along the growing total internal perimeter. The exact
finite bound and precision schedule are now in finite_cauchy_audit.tex:
  norm(C_N,eta) <= 2 M R^3/N + 4 R N eta + 2 R^2 eta.
It is proved by rational affine cancellation on every cell followed by
exact cancellation of internal edges. The supplied N and eta schedules
cover all three terms. This file also contains explicit log and complex
exponential series tails, Euler-integral truncation and quadrature bounds,
and a finite pole-kernel integral computation on a rational square.

Finite Euler-product input evaluation must include the number of factors:
per-factor tolerance epsilon/[8 P 4^P (2+2/d)^16] suffices. A precision
depending only on the final eight-factor product bound would omit the
error accumulated when computing each finite Euler product. This detail
is covered in finite_cauchy_audit.tex.

Uniform expansion order
-----------------------
All algebraic constants in transfer_notes.tex were checked, including
the endpoint constant for Li_2, the square-root split, arbitrary inverse
logarithmic order, the factorial boundary terms, and the eventual
first-omitted-term inequalities. For uniform r, a cleaner finite proof
uses the exact telescoping derivative
  S_r'(t)=1/log t - r!/(log t)^(r+1),
then a single rational mesh with r-dependent derivative bound. The
appendix gives B*=2^(r+4)(r+2)^4 r! and a displayed mesh precision.
Thus no induction on an analytic equality for all precisions is needed.

Required logical status
-----------------------
Explicit primitive recursive witnesses do not alone establish iPRA
provability. The article must retain the local rational identities,
positive margins, finite error schedules, and QF loop invariants as
part of the proof. At the fully formal level the necessary endpoint is
still an actual Prf PRAAx [] phi term. Ambient Lean theorems about Real,
Complex or Rat, even without sorry, would not replace that endpoint.
The current material is a detailed human proof and a formalization path;
its not-yet-produced embedded proof objects must be described as such.
