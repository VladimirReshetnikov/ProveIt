LEAN STATUS
===========

All three .lean files in this directory are UNCOMPILED OUTLINES.
No Lean executable is available in the construction environment.
They must not be described as kernel-verified or as a completed formal proof.

ArithmeticTarget.lean
--------------------
An exact Nat/Rat specification of the prime count, binary length, lower
logarithm approximation, logarithmic-integral approximation, giant threshold,
and final denominator-cleared arithmetic checker. There is no proof of
ArithmeticTarget. The hard theta estimate appears only as an explicitly
parameterized contract; this file supplies no inhabitant of that contract.

The target threshold is
  2 ^ ((2 * 2^240) * (m+7)^10).
The checker compares its exponent to binary length and never attempts to
construct that entire giant integer just to reject a small numerical input.

FiniteAbel.lean
--------------
A small complete proof candidate for a genuine finite summation-by-parts
identity, plus its one-step recurrence. No analytic theorem is assumed.
It is an ambient Mathlib rational-arithmetic proof, not an iPRA proof object.

NativeBridge.lean
-----------------
The closed object-language target and generic transport of a SUPPLIED iPRA
derivation through ConciseIPRA.pra_iff_native. Neither the compiled PR 3
checker nor its required object derivation has been constructed here.

First compilation commands
--------------------------
Run these in a Mathlib/ConciseIPRA Lake workspace, after placing the files
at a visible path:
  lake env lean PATH/ArithmeticTarget.lean
  lake env lean PATH/FiniteAbel.lean
  lake env lean PATH/NativeBridge.lean

The plain Mathlib files may be compiled independently of ConciseIPRA.
NativeBridge.lean requires the ConciseIPRA module search path.
