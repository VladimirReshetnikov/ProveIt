Repeated formulas on the page are grouped, but different hypotheses and convergence interpretations are distinguished. An entry marked ``corrected'' gives the valid statement and explains why the unqualified original cannot be asserted. Theorems appearing only as names in links are not statements made by that page; the Trotter and higher-order splitting results are nevertheless included as useful additional consequences.

\noindent\textbf{Beyond the source's finite displays.}
The paper supplies an exact formula for every associative-word coefficient, Dynkin's complete commutator sum, two total-order constructions, every homogeneous order in the number of $Y$'s, an explicit all-$X$ quadratic kernel, every Zassenhaus exponent, full effective exponents for splitting products, all-order coframe and metric expansions, and exact displacement matrix elements. The accompanying tables are finite illustrations of these unlimited formulas, not their claimed endpoint.
