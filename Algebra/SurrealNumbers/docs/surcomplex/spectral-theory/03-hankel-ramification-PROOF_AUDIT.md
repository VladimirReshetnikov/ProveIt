# Proof dependency map and verification boundary

## Logical structure

1. Standard Hahn foundations: the Hahn-Neumann support lemma, field
   operations on well-ordered supports, and real closedness of
   R((t^(Gamma tensor Q))). No cofinality or ordinary convergence assertion
   is substituted for strong summability.
2. Positive square classes (Section 2): a positive element is a square
   exactly when its valuation belongs to 2*Gamma. Finite sums of squares
   are squares. Finite half-scale Hahn extensions and their intermediate
   fields are described by sign characters on finite quotient groups.
3. Spectral prerequisite (Section 3): symmetric matrices over an arbitrary
   real Hahn field are orthogonally diagonalizable over that same field.
   The proof uses finite dimension induction, residue spectral splitting,
   Hensel factors, orthogonal polynomial projectors, and Gram-Schmidt.
   This theorem is established background and is not offered as new.
4. Hankel trace forms (Section 4): Newton sums give the trace matrix without
   solving for roots; its principal minors are the Vandermonde-square
   subdiscriminants. Positivity characterizes squarefree real-rooted input.
5. Exact field (Theorem 5.1): adjoining square roots of the Hankel pivots
   turns the multiplication operator into a symmetric matrix. Spectral
   descent gives an upper field. Every intermediate field is Hahn and
   Pythagorean, so Vandermonde-square identities give the reverse inclusion.
6. Profile (Theorem 6.2): positive diagonal forms have intrinsic
   multiplicities of valuation classes modulo 2*Gamma. The Hankel pivot
   diagonalization is compared with the monomial diagonalization of the
   trace forms of the irreducible root fields.
7. Realizability and applications: finite sums of subspace indicators
   classify possible profiles; base change pushes them forward. Definite
   pencils reduce to squarefree real-rooted polynomials. Independent
   quadratic blocks and a trace-form construction attain the dimension
   bounds in their stated senses.

## Important distinctions retained in the manuscript

- The whole ambient Hahn field on the divisible hull need not be algebraic
  over the original Hahn field. Only the explicitly described finite
  extensions are asserted to have the computed algebraic degree.
- All matrix dimensions are ordinary finite integers. All exponent groups
  and support unions used as workspaces are sets.
- The base field is the full Hahn field on Gamma, not the smaller ordinary
  field generated by the displayed coefficients.
- Hyperbolicity (all roots real) and squarefreeness are essential. Repeated
  roots must be removed before using the determinant and factor-count tests.
- The zero-class multiplicity counts irreducible factors, NOT base-field
  roots. Equal profiles need not imply equal factor-degree lists.
- The field of generalized eigenvalues need not contain a normalized
  eigenbasis for the original positive metric.
- Exact valuation and modulo-2*Gamma operations are separate algorithmic
  requirements. Finite arithmetic-operation counts are not general
  computability or bit-complexity results for arbitrary Hahn names.
- The matrix examples do not supply a new algebraic extension of the full
  surreal field: all the workspace fields already embed inside No.

## Checks actually executed

The included Python program was run with SymPy 1.14.0. Every assertion passed.
It verifies finite determinant identities and displayed example profiles.
The program does not verify any infinite support assertion or prove the
main theorem. The proof dependencies above were reviewed in ordinary
mathematical language, not checked by a proof assistant.

The LaTeX document compiled successfully, references resolved, and its
rendered layout was inspected. Typesetting success is not proof validation.
The novelty search and repository comparison are explicitly limited; no
