The GitHub connector was used to read the repository reader map and relevant
report guides. This was a targeted scope review, not a full clone, exhaustive
source grep, or line-by-line audit of every LaTeX and Lean file. Long connector
responses were not always returned in full. Conclusions are restricted to the
relevant returned content. Negative searches for “algebraic probes” and
“finite-lattice” are weak evidence because equivalent mathematics can use other
terminology and indexing may be incomplete.

The Herglotz guide explicitly excludes a matrix-valued null-ideal criterion. The
present article addresses its two-scale case. It does not call that scope sentence
a named published conjecture or claim to solve the full arbitrary-support problem.

## Existing material explicitly not reclaimed

- The spectral guide already treats finite Hermitian diagonalization, congruence,
  Schur corrections, finite-index monomial extensions, and character calculations.
- The Herglotz guide already treats scalar Hahn null ideals and the two-scale
  scalar absolute-continuity criterion, as well as matrix halo normalization.
- The collection already has tail-span/differential-transcendence constructions.
  The present construction uses prime denominators in exponents and proves
  ordinary algebraic independence over a different, large support-defined base.
- The dynamics guide already covers extensive scalar multiplier and surreal
  frequency small-divisor theory; those initially considered directions were
  not pursued here.

- Krapp–Kuhlmann–Serra, *On Rayner structures*, Communications in Algebra 50(3)

The automorphism PDF and available arXiv text for the support-family and
henselian-density papers were inspected. Some supplementary keyword searches
returned low-relevance results; their failure is not treated as proof of novelty.

## Candidate contribution, with boundaries

1. Exact primorial degree required to cross successive prime-tail precision levels.
2. Uniform separation of almost-disjoint tails over the finite-lattice-supported
   field and its valuation closure, and explicit algebraic independence over that
   large base.
3. Maximal hidden negative inertia for vector probes from those fields, with
   workspace-independent transport to No[i]. This is not hidden from minors.
4. A two-scale matrix-valued measure criterion combining scalar negative-part
   null ideals with a vector-total-variation absolute-continuity condition.

These are proposed research contributions with complete written arguments.
Priority is not certified; independent proof review remains appropriate.
No Lean verification or resolution of a named published conjecture is claimed.
