## Finite supports, universal embeddings, and two generic regimes

## Main construction

Let k be the real algebraic numbers. Let B consist of 1 and the positive square
roots of all rational primes. In the surreal numbers, put

    x(q,b) = omega^(b * omega^q),   q rational, b in B.
    K_A = relative real closure of k(x(q,b) : (q,b) in A).
    K*  = K_(Q x B).

The powers of omega here are Conway's omega-map, not Gonshor's exponential.
All fields are countable and belong to one set-sized ambient real closed
field K*. The family {K_A} is a closed Cantor subspace of the power set of K*.

## Results

* Every ambient element has a unique finite generator support; membership in
  K_A is a clopen condition on A.
* Embeddability on this closed family is a complete analytic quasiorder.
  Quadratic additive groups Q(sqrt(p)) encode colors without naming color
  predicates in the field language.
* In the rank-one subfamily generated by omega^(omega^q), embeddings and
  isomorphisms correspond exactly to embeddings and isomorphisms of the
  underlying rational suborders.
* One fixed reverse-omega source already has an analytic-complete existence
  locus. A second tree transform handles the rational-order source.
* Fixed sources of finite transcendence degree have open loci. Infinite
  transcendence degree gives empty interior; a nonempty locus is open
  exactly in the finite-transcendence-degree case.
* The rank-one parameter space has a comeager conull isomorphism type.
* In the larger family there are two analytic-complete fixed-source loci
  with opposite generic behavior: one is comeager/conull and the other
  meager/null. Both are dense. General category and measure zero-one laws
  are proved.

## Attribution and limits

The unrestricted complete-analytic embeddability theorem for countable real
closed fields is already known (Calderoni, Marker, Motto Ros, Shani,
arXiv:2010.08049, Section 7). It is not claimed as new here. Colored-order
completeness, surreal normal forms, real-closure theory, and the classical
completeness of ill-founded trees are explicit external inputs.

The proposed contribution is the common explicit countable surreal ambient
field, compact finite-support localization, fixed-source threshold, and
comparison of generic regimes. Worldwide priority has not been established.
No published open question of Glazer is claimed solved. The connection to
his work is descriptive/topological logic and the proved sampling warning
for evaluation, rather than a claim that these particular field questions
were posed by him.

No Lean proof is included. No repository build or kernel verification of the
new results is claimed. Finite checks are not proofs of infinite theorems.
