2. SOURCE SYNCHRONIZATION FINDINGS

The research README describes an earlier 116-page four-source merge.
The actual pinned article contains substantial later additions from six
further manuscripts, called sources 13-18 in its editorial apparatus,
including Parts VI-VIII. Its older question index is superseded in
several places by later theorem statements and status notes.

An introductory description mentions a planned Part IX, but that part
is absent from the pinned body. A roadmap was therefore not treated as
proof of its proposed results. The new article uses actual inspected
statements and proofs, and explicitly identifies separately inspected
earlier project manuscripts.

3. INHERITED MATERIAL

The inherited baseline includes fixed-set cardinality and ordinal
spectra; birthday cardinal-block and finite-tail embedding calculations;
fixed-stratum density, cellularity, and gap spectra; uniform normalization
of free, injective, and increasing set-length surreal words; elementary
comparison of raw class well-orders by their common labelled prefix;
the bounded-support class core of global enumerations; represented and
exhaustive core-cut criteria; and earlier class-condensation constructions.

The present article credits these results and reproduces the portions
needed for its extensions. In particular, the singular scheduling and
variable-residual machinery are not presented as newly invented here.
The double-split Borel phenomenon has a classical antecedent in Fremlin,
Measure Theory, Volume 4, section 419L(b); the manuscript credits it and
proves the version needed for the countable enumeration strata.

5. INSPECTED LEAN FOUNDATION

All five modules are under Algebra/SurrealNumbers/Surreal/Foundations/:

SignSequence.lean
    SignSequence lives in Type (u+1), with birthdays in Ordinal.{u}.
    The source provides the numerical order, bounded birthdays,
    prefix/truncation primitives, an ordinal embedding, and non-smallness.

SignSequenceCut.lean
    Small-cut fillers and separators at a prescribed birthday above
    the option birthdays.

SignSequenceCutOperation.lean
    Canonical small-cut operations, birthday bounds, dense order,
    and absence of endpoints.

SignSequenceSimplicity.lean
    existsUnique_simplest_separator requires an explicit separator-
    existence hypothesis. That premise cannot be dropped or read as
    arbitrary cut existence.

SizeObstructions.lean
    Lower-universe option indexing and the impossibility of unrestricted
    cut filling, including the universal-lower-cut obstruction.

These declarations were inspected. No new Lean code was written or
compiled, and the article's suggested formal milestones are proposals.

6. PRIMARY-SOURCE CHECKS

The bibliography cites Gonshor and Bagayoko-van der Hoeven for surreal
sign-sequence foundations; Kanovei-Shelah for the lexicographic ordering
of redundant ultrafilter presentations; Gitman-Hamkins and Hamkins-Woodin
for class-recursion and comparability principles; and Kechris and Fremlin
for descriptive set theory and split-order Borel structure.

Kanovei-Shelah's actual index consists of maps from the continuum cardinal
to P(N) whose ranges are ultrafilters, with repetitions allowed. The
relevant definition appears in section 1 of the preprint, on page 2.
It is not literally a set of all exhaustive well-orderings of the reals.

Hamkins-Woodin Theorem 6 supplies ETR => strong class-well-order
comparability. Their Question 7 discusses related converses. This
manuscript proves its stated condensation/comparability equivalence;
it does not assert a converse from comparability to ETR or claim a
new survey of the current status of every related open problem.

Complete bibliographic links appear in the LaTeX source and the manifest.
