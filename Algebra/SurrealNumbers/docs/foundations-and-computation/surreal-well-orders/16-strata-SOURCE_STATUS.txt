STATUS OF THE WORK

The prior merged surreal well-order report identifies its own mathematical
results as unrefereed, and records no Lean mapping for its swo: labels.
This article reuses several of those results and develops additional
arguments. Its status ledger distinguishes reused results from extensions.

New arguments include all-stratum density/cellularity, countable witnesses
to incompleteness, regular-stage gap spectra, word-tree and termination
classification, direct simultaneous block extraction, prefix-cylinder
recognition and first-failure classification, and the stated refinement
of the sufficient comprehension bound for elementary nonembedding.

The proofs underwent independent mathematical checks during preparation,
with particular attention to smallness, uniformity, endpoints, limit
prefixes, and the distinction between elementary and class-quantifier
comprehension. This is not external peer review, a proof of historical
novelty, or verification by a proof assistant.

No repository files were modified. No Lean project build was run and no
new Lean declarations are supplied. The proposed formalization is a
design tied to inspected existing primitives, with explicit separation
of universe-polymorphic type theory and internal class-model semantics.

ARTIFACT VERIFICATION

The standalone source compiles to the included PDF with resolved
references and citations. The final compilation log contains no LaTeX
warnings, overfull boxes, or underfull boxes. All 33 physical pages were

The included Python diagnostic script uses exact dyadic arithmetic for
