PRIMARY LITERATURE CHECKS

The complete bibliographic entries and their uses appear in the article.
Particular checks were made against these primary sources:

- Kanovei and Shelah, A definable nonstandard model of the reals.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0311165
  The actual map index set and repetitions were checked in Section 1.

- Gitman, Hamkins, Holy, Schlicht, and Williams, The exact strength of
  the class forcing theorem.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.03700
  Used for the distinction between set-valued recursion and ETR.

- Williams, The Structure of Models of Second-order Set Theories.
  https://juliakw.net/research/pubs/diss/kwilliams-diss.pdf
  Used for model/class realizations and preservation of well-foundedness.

- Hamkins and Woodin, Open class determinacy is preserved by forcing.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.11180
  Used to locate the separate preservation questions concerning stronger
  class principles under forcing.

- Gitman, Hamkins, and Johnstone, Class choice and the surprising
  weakness of Kelley-Morse set theory, research preprint (2026).
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.23165
  Used for the distinction between KM and additional class-choice
  principles, with the source's metatheoretic qualifications retained.

- The Univalent Foundations Program, Homotopy Type Theory, Section 11.6.
  https://homotopytypetheory.org/book/
  Used for the explicit universe shift and small option families.

- Ehrlich's treatments of the surreal sign tree and of the absolute
  arithmetic continuum supply the stated classical order background;
  the exact bibliographic references are in the article.

LIMITS

No exhaustive novelty search or historical priority claim is made.
No repository build, proof-assistant elaboration, or kernel check was
performed. The new results have written proofs with independent internal
checks; they remain a research manuscript. The new article and this audit
do not promote the repository's existing research manuscripts to formal
verification status.
