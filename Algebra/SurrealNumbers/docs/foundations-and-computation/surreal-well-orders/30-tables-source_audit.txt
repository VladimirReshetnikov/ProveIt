inspection of every file in the large repository or every related publication.

Primary external sources:
- Kanovei and Shelah, A definable nonstandard model of the reals (2004).
  The index objects are maps from c to P(N) whose ranges are ultrafilters;
  they are not literally all bijective well-orderings of R.
- Ehrlich, The Absolute Arithmetic Continuum and the Unification of All
  Numbers Great and Small (2012).
- Miller, On the Borel Classification of the Isomorphism Class of a
  Countable Model (1983), especially Section 2, Example 5, and Section 3.
- Hamkins's original surreal/global-choice answer and global-choice exposition.
- Gitman and Hamkins, Open determinacy for class games.
- Gitman, Hamkins, Holy, Schlicht, and Williams, The exact strength of the
  class forcing theorem.
- Williams, Minimum models of second-order set theories (2019), especially
  the minimum GBC-realization criterion for countable models.

Precise bibliographic entries and source links are in the article.
