trace-collision results, and external model classification were reviewed
independently during preparation. This is not external peer review.

isomorphism; embedded cube spectrum and coding length; internal and
external well-foundedness; admitted and merely amenable branches; and
ordinary set recursion and class-valued ETR.

FOUNDATIONAL SCOPE
- Bounded carriers, fixed-type spectra, restrictions, and bounded
  completion results use ordinary ZFC.
- Supplied class relations and elementary prefix constructions are stated
  in GB/GBC with their stated choice and admission hypotheses.
- Domains of all admitted class orders, their automorphism groups, and
  global completions are actual sets in an external universe around a
  specified set-sized GBC model.
- An inaccessible-cardinal full-class model is an optional semantic
  example, not an assumption of the set-sized theorems.
- The exact binary-cube spectrum is proved for every admissible fixed
  type. Least coding length is exact when the two explicit ordinal
  bounds agree; the remaining singular non-strong-limit cases are
  identified as further research.
