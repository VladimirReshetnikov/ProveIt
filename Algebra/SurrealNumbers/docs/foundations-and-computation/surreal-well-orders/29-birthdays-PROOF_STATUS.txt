2. MAIN RESULTS ESTABLISHED IN THE MANUSCRIPT

Section 4 (ZF): The exact numerical birthday cube spectrum
B_beta embeds No_<theta iff beta < theta, and consequently
No_<theta embeds No_<eta iff theta <= eta. The section also gives
least binary coding lengths and finite-tail equimorphism types.

Section 5 (ZFC): Residual-cardinal bounds and exact embedded-cube and
native-birthday capacities for every admissible enumeration length at
a noninitial birthday cutoff. The singular non-strong-limit upper-
coding gap is kept explicit. For every omega < theta < omega_1 and
alpha = Lambda + n of continuum cardinality, with Lambda limit,
W_alpha(No_<theta) is equimorphic to B_Lambda times the finite chain n!.
This uses no continuum hypothesis and makes no isomorphism assertion.

Section 6 (GBC for full surreal class isomorphisms): The canonical
termination-cut factorization and centered increasing-word type
T^op times_lex T. Three zero conventions have that type; the fourth
is an adjacent-pair expansion. Actual surreal thresholds translate
to zero. Nonprincipal class cuts retain separate parameters.

Section 7 (GBC, with supplied classes or higher-order semantics):
Shorter-first adjacency for numerical class carriers is exactly
A < A union {b} when b is the least strict upper bound of every
member of A. Necessarily A is a proper class with unattained supremum.
The longer-first order is dense. All numerical final class segments
occur as available-label orders, and a branching obstruction prevents
extending the set-prefix tree isomorphism to all class carriers.

Section 8 (ZFC): At P_(omega+n)=Bij(omega+n,D), with D countable dense
and discrete coordinate topology, the native and coordinate Borel
structures agree for n=0,1, have strict native inclusion for n=2,
and are incomparable for finite n>=3. The topology/category and
finite-split normal forms are explicit. The native strict-order
relation is not measurable for the rectangle-generated product
sigma-algebra when n>=2. At all countable lengths the coordinate
comparison relation is Delta^0_2. Infinite-tail native Borel
classification remains a research question, subject to the already
proved nonstandardness of those full native Borel structures.

Section 9 (GBC): The ordered injection-prefix tree is transitive on
supplied Ord-length injection branches through uniform class
automorphisms. The tree therefore does not recognize exhaustiveness.
An analogous increasing-tree construction does not preserve numerical
cofinality. Literal cross-node labels are not preserved. Every fixed
set-height restriction can be matched by an exhaustive-branch-
preserving automorphism, so that restriction alone is insufficient.

Section 11 (GBC): A supplied terminating canonical set-interval history
produces a finite ordinal-coefficient normal form, with initial image
in the polynomial order on its stage well-order. A terminating history
on schedule Gamma+1 exists exactly when the input initially embeds in
that polynomial order. The global canonical-history principle is
equivalent to strong class-well-order comparability.

3. PRINCIPAL DEPENDENCIES AND LIMITS

The set-sized results state ZF or ZFC explicitly. Uniform full-class
normalizations and the class-order results use GBC. Class-valued
recursion existence is not silently inferred from set recursion.

The article does NOT establish CWO => ETR. It distinguishes the
condensation-specific existence principle from general elementary
transfinite recursion. Nor does it assert that all proper-class
well-orders form an ordinary class, even in KM. Full collections of
class relations are treated with an additional sort or externally
over a specified two-sorted model.

The exact embedded-cube spectrum does not by itself identify the least
upper binary code at the remaining singular residual cardinals.
Equimorphism is not upgraded to isomorphism. Formula-coded classes are
not combined with an internal universal satisfaction predicate.
