# Source audit

Audit date: 28 September 2026.

## Repository

Repository: https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt

Pinned snapshot: `e21766d04c2b8a9b2cdba0cd43e563024b3bd1b9`.

The GitHub connector was used to inspect the recursive tree, the surreal
subproject guide, the report collection guide, and the guide for:

`Algebra/SurrealNumbers/docs/foundations-and-computation/surreal-fields-across-universes/`

The opening of that report's LaTeX source was also inspected. This was a
targeted source inspection, not a claim to have read every line of the
large merged manuscript or audited every report in ProveIt. The guide
explicitly marks its claims as unrefereed and unformalized, and identifies
higher gap characters and infinite old-field families among its research
directions. The necessary foundational arguments are reproved in the new
article rather than silently imported as verified statements.

The neighboring critical-point-defects guide was consulted during topic
selection, but no mathematical claim from it is used in the article.

## Primary literature used

### Fischer–Koelbing–Wohofsky

Vera Fischer, Marlene Koelbing, Wolfgang Wohofsky, *Fresh function spectra*,
Annals of Pure and Applied Logic 174(9), article 103300 (2023).

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apal.2023.103300

Primary author-hosted manuscript:
https://www.logic.univie.ac.at/~vfischer/fresh_function_spectra.pdf

Journal metadata was checked against the University of Vienna publication
record and publisher record. Definitions and theorem statements concerning
actual spectra, Easton closure, preservation, and Prikry forcing were
inspected. The published Easton-closure definition has a Mahlo exception;
the article does not use the more restrictive wording found in earlier
talk notes. The PDF's relevant pages were also inspected visually.

### Lévy–Solovay

A. Lévy and R. M. Solovay, *Measurable cardinals and the continuum
hypothesis*, Israel Journal of Mathematics 5 (1967), 234–248.

Publisher record: https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02771612

Used for the classical small-forcing preservation of measurability in the
Cohen–Prikry tower. This is an imported theorem, not part of the new proof.

### Classical background

Conway and Gonshor supply surreal foundations; Ehrlich is cited for the
class-field viewpoint; Marker supplies the model-theoretic background;
Jech supplies standard forcing conventions. Exact editions are listed in
the article bibliography. No claim is made that these books were read in
full during this task. None of their copyrighted full texts is included
in the delivery.

## What the search does not establish

The search distinguishes the chosen repository questions, established
forcing inputs, and the proposed new consequences. It does not certify
that every equivalent formulation of the relative or nonclassification
results is absent from all published and unpublished literature. Priority
remains unverified, and the manuscript should be treated as a research
draft with supplied proofs, not as an independently refereed breakthrough.
