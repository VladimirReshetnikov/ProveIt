## Primary forcing literature

Vera Fischer, Marlene Koelbing, Wolfgang Wohofsky,
**Fresh function spectra**, *Annals of Pure and Applied Logic* 174(9) (2023),
article 103300.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apal.2023.103300
- Author-hosted text: https://www.logic.univie.ac.at/~vfischer/fresh_function_spectra.pdf
- University publication metadata:
  https://ucrisportal.univie.ac.at/en/publications/fresh-function-spectra/

The precise advanced inputs used in the main proof are:
- Proposition 4.10: cardinal and cofinality preservation for the relevant
  GCH Easton products.
- Corollary 4.21: a full product over regular cardinals unbounded in a singular
  lambda adds a lambda^+-Cohen subset under GCH. The source credits the
  underlying singular-product theorem to Shelah.

Supplementary comparison table:
- Proposition 6.1: Cohen fresh-function spectrum in ground regular cardinals.
- Theorem 7.21: Prikry fresh-function spectrum.
- Theorem 7.23: Namba fresh-function spectrum under CH.

The paper's definitions and selected formula pages were inspected directly.
No external paper PDF is redistributed in this ZIP.
