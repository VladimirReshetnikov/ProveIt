PROOF STATUS AND CONTRIBUTION SUMMARY
Date: 4 October 2026

Principal extensions, with full written proofs in the article:
- Theorem 4.2: global real-parameter consolidation, including
  every surreal ROD iff V=HOD(r) for one real r.
- Theorems 4.4 and 4.6: a definable section of the canonical real/cardinal
  sign decoder exists exactly when the relevant HOD(p) successor cardinal
  agrees with the ambient one. Corollary 4.7 treats all cardinals uniformly.
- Theorem 5.1: canonical operations and complete Conway normal forms are
  absolute between nested transitive ZFC models without a same-reals
  assumption. Theorem 5.7 supplies Delta_1 operation graphs.
- Theorem 8.1: a parameter-free quotient interpretation of (V,in) in the
  ordered surreal field with Oz and the Conway omega map named.
  Representatives lie among monomials omega^a with 0<a<1.
- Theorem 9.3: intrinsic first-order definitions of Hahn summability and
  the Hahn sum for explicitly coded set-indexed families.
- Theorem 10.1 and Proposition 10.2: a uniformly definable coefficient-indexed
  family of commuting strong real-linear derivations, with canonical partial
  integration. General diagonal derivations are prior background.
- Corollaries 11.2 and 11.3: inner-model algebraic independence in a bounded
  omnific interval; preservation of fixed Levy definability levels by a
  reversible monomial code.
- Theorem 11.4: canonical omega–omnific structures have the same elementary
  theory exactly when their set models do; for nested transitive models,
  elementary inclusions agree in the two languages.

Additional explicit background/consequences:
- Ambient definability hulls are real closed and have omnific integer parts
  with the same fraction fields.
- Proposition 6.1 treats the pure omnific ring: order is definable,
  the fraction interpretation is bi-interpretable with (No,field,Oz), and
  its parameter-free definable elements are exactly Z in the canonical
  or transitive setting.
- Coefficientwise lifting, Hadamard products and Boolean support algebras.
- First omitted ordinals, first new birthdays, and the difference between
  individual definitions and uniform collection.

Scope limitations:
- No bi-interpretation with set theory is proved for the omega–omnific
  structure. The necessary comparison with original sign representations
  remains open.
- Exact intrinsic definable closure of that richer structure is not
  determined. Interpretation of the universe does not alone settle it.
- The theorem about all sets uses ZFC. In the ZF reading, the graph quotient
  presents the hereditarily well-orderable sets.
- In nonstandard models, the interpretation uses internal subsets,
  internal well-foundedness, and internal collapse.
- The Delta_2 encoder bound is conditional on cardinal agreement.
- The literal description-length bound uses the explicitly stated fixed
  finite parameter and finite-alphabet convention.
- The derivations D_b are diagonal Euler derivations, not the standard
  Berarducci–Mantova derivation.
- Sums require whole set-indexed family codes. No proper-class sum or
  internal universal truth evaluator is asserted.
- The novelty comparison is against the audited repository and a targeted
  literature search. No claim of literature-wide priority is made.
