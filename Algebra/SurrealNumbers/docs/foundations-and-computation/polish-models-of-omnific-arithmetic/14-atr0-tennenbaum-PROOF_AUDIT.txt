C. EXACT ARITHMETIC SCALES

Checked:
- c = max(c0,(b+2)^4), t0=c, t[n+1]=floor(sqrt(t[n])),
  and q[n]=floor(c/t[n]).
- For each fixed external n, a quotient/remainder tree injects [0,c]
  into [0,t[n]]^(2^n). A hypothetical enumeration of the latter
  interval would enumerate [0,c] by reconstruction.
- This replaces induction on the projective predicate of
  uncountability with arithmetic induction on finite evaluation traces.
- The scale inequality q[n+1] >= q[n]t[n+1]
  >= q[n]t[n+2]^2 > 8q[n]t[n+2] is exact.
- The shifted scales make every h[k,n] exceed the arbitrary b.
- Every continuity parameter used for transport is m-x[n]>c>b.

D. FUSION AND CATEGORY CONTRADICTIONS

Checked:
- Edge bounds 2^(-n-4) Delta[n] imply the stated uniform tail bound
  2^(-N-3) Delta[N].
- Finite-level separation implies continuous injectivity of the
  limiting Cantor map.
- Additive growth makes all subset sums distinct.
- Multiplicative growth gives Q[n]^4<y[n] and distinct products.
- One-bit identities use only the specific unary continuities assumed.
- Positivity of multiplicative limits follows from injectivity and
  zero-divisor cancellation, not a limit of arithmetic inequalities.
- Each bounded-comparison event is disjoint from its sufficiently
  distant one-bit-flip image and is therefore meager.
- Both directions of finite-change invariance use the explicit growth
  estimates.
- The tail set and its complement image are disjoint, have comeager
  union, and contradict the category zero-one law.
- A single tagged Baire construction supplies a uniform meager cover.
- The optional measure proof uses the correct non-strict finite bound
  mu(E_n) <= 2^(-k) and avoids floor-ratio endpoint ambiguities.

The restricted multiplicative assumption was audited separately:
only maps x -> e(a)x with a>=b are used. The proof does not silently
assume continuity of arbitrary multipliers or joint multiplication.

E. NONSPLIT PRESBURGER CONSTRUCTION

Checked:
- The factorial generator identities and common-denominator normal
  form for finite combinations.
- The kernel of projection D->Q is precisely Z e0.
- e0 is the least positive element.
- Any proposed retraction gives n! b[n] = b[1]+s[n], eventually
  impossible because 0<b[1]+s[n]<n!.
- The positive cone of R lex D has countably many clopen half-line
  slices and is locally compact, perfect, Polish, and uncountable.
- Addition is jointly continuous.
- The quotient by the standard-integer subgroup is divisible,
  giving Presburger arithmetic by the established Z-group criterion.
- Nonsplitting survives the added real coordinate.
- Definable relations are Delta^0_2 by fiberwise linear conditions
  and clopen congruences, followed by Presburger quantifier elimination.
- The displayed finite surreal normal forms preserve addition,
  order, injectivity, and the unit.
- The omnific constant coefficient forces the impossible retraction.
- Preservation of the unit is essential: multiplication of the
  displayed surreal realization by omega gives a nonunital embedding
  into the omnific integers.
- No compatibility with the surreal order topology is claimed.

F. RESEARCH AGENDA

The agenda was narrowed where an elementary argument already supplies
an answer:
- All countable Z-groups yield the same type of countable-slice
  Polish construction; general existence is not left as an open question.
- The continuous-section parameter set of a Borel family is coanalytic;
  the remaining questions concern sharpness, Borelness, and category
  specifically for arithmetic families.
- Omnific embedding questions specify extra definability, topology,
  or initiality requirements, rather than leaving bare ordered
  existence vaguely unresolved.
