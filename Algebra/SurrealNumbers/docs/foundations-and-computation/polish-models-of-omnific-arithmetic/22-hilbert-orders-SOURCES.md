The full paper was read, including the final questions; the last page was also

here. No claim about employment, present appointments, or personal profile
attributes is needed. The LinkedIn page supplied by the user was inaccessible;

## Oscillation ranks and topological inputs

A. S. Kechris and A. Louveau, *A classification of Baire class 1 functions*,
Transactions of the American Mathematical Society 318 (1990), 209–236.
DOI: 10.1090/S0002-9947-1990-0946424-3.

https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/xtm8s-6zw43

The authors' institutional repository supplied the bibliographic record and
abstract. Full-PDF retrieval attempts did not yield a usable parsed copy in
this session. The present article defines the derivative it uses explicitly
and proves the required exact rank calculation directly. It does not invoke
an uninspected rank comparison or boundedness theorem from this paper.

B. J. Pettis, *On continuity and openness of homomorphisms in topological groups*,
Annals of Mathematics (2) 52 (1950), 293–308.
DOI: 10.2307/1969471.

https://www.jstor.org/stable/1969471

Bibliographic attribution was checked. The automatic-continuity argument needed
by this report is supplied directly. Standard descriptive-set-theoretic inputs
are additionally credited to A. S. Kechris, *Classical Descriptive Set Theory*,
Springer, 1995: the G_delta subspace criterion and the analytic/coanalytic
criterion. The book is a standard reference, not a claim of a full in-session
reading. Baire category, the algebraic Hahn–Banach theorem, and elementary
Hilbert-space completeness are also classical dependencies.

## Arithmetic and surreal inputs

Presburger quantifier elimination and the Z-group characterization are credited
to Presburger's original paper and Cooper's elimination method in the article's
bibliography. They are standard theorem inputs rather than new claims or
computer-verified results. No independent full-text rederivation of quantifier
elimination is supplied.

J. H. Conway, *On Numbers and Games*, 2nd edition, A K Peters, 2001, is cited for
surreal normal forms. An accessible corroborating primary source was consulted:

S. L'Innocente and V. Mantova, *A factorisation theory for generalised power
series and omnific integers*, arXiv:1710.07304v5 (22 January 2024).

https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.07304
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.07304

Its opening normal-form description was read. This report uses reverse-well-
ordered support, integer constant term, absence of negative exponents for
omnific integers, and coefficientwise addition. It does not use or claim to
review the source's factorization theory.

## Claim boundary

The article supplies conventional proofs for its category-to-flag theorem,
Hilbert classification, exact oscillation rank, ordinal embedding criterion,
operator descriptions, and arithmetic/surreal deductions. These are not formal
certificates. The precise novelty of the converse and its combination with the
rank calculation remains subject to full literature comparison and independent
expert review. The original Presburger existence question is not presented as
newly answered here; the repository's prior answer is acknowledged.
