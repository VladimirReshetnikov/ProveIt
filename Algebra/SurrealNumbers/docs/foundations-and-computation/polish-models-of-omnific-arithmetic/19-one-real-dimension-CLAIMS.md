# Claim and verification boundaries

## Mathematical assertions proved in the article

1. The complete normal form assumes a countable torsion-free pointed group (E,u)
   with E/Z u torsion-free divisible, product topology R usual x E discrete,
   and an invariant total order having (0,u) as its least positive element.
   The Baire property of the positive cone suffices and forces Delta^0_2.
2. The general invariant retains the countable extension A <= E. A divisible
   quotient is NOT assumed to split. Only the rational-vector-space special
   case uses a justified direct sum.
3. Positive-cone group-completion continuity is proved for the constructed
   presentation class. Existence of a good completion for an arbitrary locally
   compact Polish monoid remains outside the theorem.
4. Borel completeness is for countable structures and is stated in an explicit
   standard Borel space of normal-form data. It is not completeness for all
   analytic equivalence relations or a claim about unspecified raw order codes.
5. The abstract automorphism formula applies to R lex D lex Z for countable
   divisible ordered D. The 2^continuum count assumes D is nonzero. The Borel,
   continuous, and analytic automorphisms coincide in this family.
6. The omnific map has a set-sized domain and finite supports. Its topology is
   transported externally. Its image is additive, not a subring.

## Independent external inputs

Cooper elimination is classical and is proved in the required group-uniform
form in Appendix A. The Friedman–Stanley universality theorem is an explicitly
identified external theorem; it is not proved or claimed as new. The finite
Conway-normal-form arithmetic used for the omnific realization is also standard.

## Priority and open scope

The earlier positive construction for Glazer's Question 2 is acknowledged as
prior companion work. The article addresses the earlier report's one-dimensional
order-classification question. No global priority claim is made for the new
formulations. Glazer's ATR_0 question, the higher-dimensional classification,
and arbitrary Polish-monoid completion are not asserted to be solved.
