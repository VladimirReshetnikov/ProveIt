SOURCE AND NOVELTY AUDIT
Local Geometric Codes and Topological Arithmetic

PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This note records the source boundary of the manuscript. The search was
targeted at Glazer's topological arithmetic work, relevant published
background, and the current and recently archived ProveIt manuscripts.
It was not an exhaustive search of the mathematical literature and does
not establish bibliographic priority. The article supplies its proofs
and distinguishes proposed contributions from imported results and open
questions. The connection to Elliot Glazer is grounded in his authored
mathematical work, especially the paper below.

THE PRECISE PRIOR PROJECT QUESTION

Earlier manuscript:
  Diophantine Definability and Topological Arithmetic:
  An ATR_0 proof of Glazer's theorem, cofinal unary discontinuities,
  and nonsplit Presburger models

Permanent source archive:
  https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/fa0a0576e5cd7ff8a2978d6ad0aefd4d24511808/docs/incoming/glazer_atr0_research.zip

Member inside that archive:
  glazer_atr0_research/glazer_atr0_topological_arithmetic.tex

Question 12.2, in subsection 12.2, "Weaker object arithmetic," asks for
a power-of-two multiplier obstruction with arithmetic weaker than
I Delta_0 + Exp, without supplying a total Borel exponential as extra
data. Its following discussion proposes partial exponentiation cuts.
The earlier Theorem 8.1 uses a total Borel exponential, and an earlier
step obtains such a map through an internal Diophantine argument over
I Delta_0 + Exp. These are prior, unrefereed project results.

The present article resolves the explicitly beta-coded local direction:
one existing geometric code suffices to obstruct continuity on every
externally uncountable subinterval of its index domain. It then proves
that, under separate continuity of multiplication, all such lengths
lie in one canonical countable arithmetic core. The broader bare
I Delta_0 question and comparison with ordinary partial exponentiation
remain open in this manuscript.

OTHER REPOSITORY OVERLAP CHECKED

The inspected material included the consolidated Polish-arithmetic work,
the Baire_Category_Arithmetic_Rigidity and Glazer_ProveIt_Baire_Rigidity
manuscripts, polish_arithmetic_package, polish_presburger_frontiers,
and the latest embeddings/thresholds arrival. Their relevant results
concern Presburger models, isolated zero, polynomial semirings, signed
additive-group rigidity, inherited cone topologies, and embeddings.
The latest surreal/choice/Replacement archives concern different topics.
A matching fixed-code local theorem and the resulting all-base countable
length-domain theorem were not located in the inspected sources.

The signed-group results have different topological hypotheses from the
positive-carrier theorem proved here. The existing polynomial semiring
example fails open induction; it therefore does not settle sharpness at
I Delta_0. The repository's coding modules motivate a formalization route
but do not verify this manuscript.

PUBLISHED INPUTS AND WHAT IS IMPORTED

1. Elliot Glazer, A Topological Tennenbaum Theorem,
   arXiv:2311.13699v1, 22 November 2023.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2311.13699v1

   The principal starting point is the multiplication obstruction in
   Theorem 2 and its Cantor/Baire-category method. That theorem assumes
   total exponentiation. Glazer also introduces the cut of elements
   with countable initial intervals. The present work does not claim
   that cut's definition or the overall obstruction strategy as new.

2. Leo Harrington, David Marker, Saharon Shelah, Borel Orderings,
   Transactions of the AMS 310 (1988), 293-302.
   https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1988-0965754-3
   https://shelah.logic.at/files/95596/215.pdf

   The classical structure theorem for Borel linear orders supplies the
   countable cofinality and coinitiality input. The arithmetic properties
   and applications of the resulting countable core are proved in the
   present article. These global consequences are stated in ZFC.

3. Ali Enayat, Joel David Hamkins, Bartosz Wcislo,
   Topological models of arithmetic, Fundamenta Mathematicae 256
   (2022), 171-193.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.01270

   Remark 17(a), together with Glazer's introduction, is the inspected
   primary account used for the existence of uncountable Borel PA models.
   The underlying theorem is attributed to Malitz, Mycielski, and
   Reinhardt, Axiom of Choice, Lowenheim-Skolem Theorem and Borel models:
   https://doi.org/10.4064/fm-137-1-53-58
   Its erratum is listed for bibliographic completeness:
   https://doi.org/10.4064/fm-140-2-197
   The erratum's text was not directly inspected. The article uses this
   established existence result to construct a polynomial cut retaining
   a long local code while losing total exponentiation.

4. Ali Enayat, Automorphisms of models of bounded arithmetic,
   Fundamenta Mathematicae 192 (2006), 37-65, especially Lemma 2.1.
   https://doi.org/10.4064/fm192-1-3

   This is background for bounded arithmetic and ordinary partial
   exponentiation. It does not by itself identify an arbitrary ordinary
   exponential endpoint with the explicit code required by our theorem.

The manuscript bibliography also identifies Blass's perfect-set partition
theorem, Buss's exposition of arithmetic proof theory, and Simpson's
reverse-mathematical framework. The proof states where those background
inputs enter. No repository prose or published theorem is silently
relabelled as an original result.

NOVELTY CLAIM LIMIT

The proposed contribution is a local, explicit-code refinement with
interval discontinuities, all-base countability consequences, a Borel
certificate relation, and a concrete non-total-exponentiation scope
example. It is not a claim to a certified breakthrough, a complete
answer to all weaker-arithmetic questions, established publication
priority, external peer review, or a proof-assistant-checked theorem.
