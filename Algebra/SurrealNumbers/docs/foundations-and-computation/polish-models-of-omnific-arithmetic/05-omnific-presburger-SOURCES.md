submitted November 22, 2023.

- Record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13699
- Versioned source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.13699v1

  including the rendered page, rather than inferred from a search snippet.

## Priority boundary

Searches checked the exact question, the arXiv record, and combinations of
Glazer, Presburger arithmetic, uncountable Polish models, lexicographic
groups, and continuous addition. They did not identify a prior affirmative
answer, but this is not a proof that none exists. The article therefore claims
an affirmative construction for the question **as written**, not independently
established publication priority. Further scholarly review is needed before
making a first-solution claim.
