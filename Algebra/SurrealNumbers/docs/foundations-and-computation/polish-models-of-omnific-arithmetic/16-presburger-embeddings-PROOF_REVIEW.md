# Mathematical review record

The assembled article was read by two independent mathematical review agents,
in addition to the principal derivation. A separate source audit checked the
closest ProveIt reports and a separate literature audit checked the Hahn-group
and topological-vector-space antecedents.

Both readers reported no remaining substantive mathematical defect in the full
assembled text, including the quantifier-elimination appendix. This record
describes internal AI-assisted review, not external refereeing or formal proof.

## Issues specifically checked

1. **Arithmetic maps.** The extension by differences is well-defined; the
   maximal divisible subgroup removes any unwanted integer-coordinate term;
   positivity of `(h,-1)` forces strict positivity and injectivity of the
   coefficient map. Congruences are preserved and reflected.
2. **Polishness.** The positive cone is G_delta, not generally closed. Its
   ambient metric need not remain complete. The Baire-space characterization
   uses nowhere local compactness, not just uncountability.
3. **Finite ancestry.** The inverse dependency tree is finitely branching and
   well-founded, so each inverse row has finite support even at a limit index.
4. **Closed-subspace duality.** Arbitrary pivot values are realized using
   finite-support annihilator equations and closedness, rather than a potentially
   invalid transfinite sequence of row subtractions.
5. **Orbits versus types.** Ambient automorphism orbits are determined by the
   actual pivot subset S. Abstract isomorphism is determined only by otp(S).
6. **Quotient dimensions.** Infinitely many omitted pivots produce a full
   rational product; its algebraic dimension is continuum, not countable.
7. **Topology at a limit.** Wild shears fail product-topology continuity while
   preserving the Hahn valuation. These topologies must not be conflated.
8. **Distinct thresholds.** At omega+n every map is onto on the terminal finite
   block, forcing its image to be the inverse image of a closed omega-stage
   image. At omega*2 the inserted functional coordinate plus shift is an
   actual self-embedding with a dense graph image.
9. **Self-copy core.** All final finite ranks are fixed by every increasing
   self-map of the ordinal. A nonterminal coordinate can be deleted while
   preserving order type. Finite-coordinate approximations give the exact
   clopen-intersection criterion.
10. **Quantifier elimination.** The Cooper argument uses invariance under
    entire standard congruence classes. It does not confuse this with
    invariance under finitely many successor shifts.
