INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
1. Every tested value is a standard integer or exact rational number.
2. Strict scale separation is checked at levels satisfying t[n+2] > 8;
   standard chains eventually leave this regime and terminate at 1.
3. Subset and far-block checks exhaust the reported finite mask ranges;
   they do not exhaust all possible growth sequences or all block sizes.
4. Product logarithms mean exact integer exponents ell_i with y_i=2^ell_i.
   No floating-point logarithms or approximate exponentials are used.
5. The retraction check covers b1=-1000..1000. The argument for every
   integer b1 is the symbolic proof in the manuscript, not this search.
6. No computation here proves internal MRDP, verifies a weak-arithmetic
   derivation, constructs a nonstandard model, or formalizes ATR0.
7. No computation here proves the infinite fusion, Borel, category,
   measure, Polish-model, splitting, or surreal-embedding theorems.
8. These are reproducible finite checks of arithmetic details, not a
   proof-assistant certificate or a substitute for the mathematical proofs.
