PROOF AND VERIFICATION STATUS
Local Geometric Codes and Topological Arithmetic

MATHEMATICAL STATUS

The manuscript gives conventional mathematical proofs. Independent
in-session reviews checked the main arithmetic and topological argument
and its stated corollaries. These reviews are not external peer review.
No new Lean, Rocq, or other proof-assistant certification is supplied.
No change to the ProveIt repository is part of this deliverable.

The principal local theorem is conditional on an explicitly existing
internal beta code. Its assumptions are a Polish carrier, Borel
arithmetic, bounded induction I Delta_0, and a fixed code for a geometric
progression through C. Its conclusion applies to every externally
uncountable arithmetic interval inside [0,C]. Discontinuity is that of
a unary multiplier on the full carrier. The proof does not assume
continuous addition, a topology generated by the arithmetic order, a
global Borel exponential, or total exponentiation.

MAIN PROOF CHECKPOINTS

* Fixed-code remainder evaluation is Borel, using uniqueness in the
  analytic definitions of arithmetic order, quotient, and remainder.

* Bounded induction proves positivity, strict growth, and the local
  product identity within the code's index domain. Code parameters are
  fixed throughout this part of the argument.

* Exact integer square roots and quotients produce the needed scales
  and buffers. The fusion keeps every evaluated exponent within [0,C].

* A shifted finite-product identity uses continuous multipliers at
  indices in the chosen interval, even when auxiliary increments lie
  below its left endpoint. Products are not rewritten as an out-of-domain
  value of E. In particular, no value at a combined index such as
  2b plus a tail sum is assumed to exist inside the code.

* Completeness supplies limits in the fusion. Positivity and cancellation
  are established without assuming that arithmetic inequalities survive
  topological limits. The final contradiction uses Baire category.

COROLLARIES WITH THEIR EXACT SCOPE

In ordinary ZFC, classical Borel-order theory shows that the set N_M of
elements with externally countable predecessor intervals is countable.
It is the largest countable initial segment, is closed under successor,
addition, and multiplication, and is a Delta_0-elementary initial model
of I Delta_0. This conclusion needs Borel arithmetic, not continuity.

Under separate continuity of multiplication, every geometric-code
length for every base q >= 2 belongs to N_M. Consequently their union
is countable and F_sigma. For a fixed base, endpoint uniqueness yields
a countable endpoint graph, hence an F_sigma graph. The full relation
Geo(q,C,u,v) of valid certificates is Borel by a countable union over
possible lengths and countable intersections over their initial
intervals. No uniform effective Borel code or uniform rank is asserted.

For a fixed code, each convex subset of the set of continuous multiplier
indices is countable. The entire set of such indices is not proved
countable.

The polynomial-cut construction gives uncountable Borel I Delta_0
models with an externally long valid local code and without total
exponentiation. They are not examples with continuous multiplication:
the local theorem rules out separate continuity for every Polish
realization of their Borel structure.

The article explains an ATR_0 upper bound for the local proof with
stated coding interfaces. The global Borel-order countability results
are kept in ZFC. No reversal, exact reverse-mathematical strength,
global satisfaction predicate, or machine-checked formalization is
claimed.

FINITE DIAGNOSTICS

The supplied Python script and JSON record concern finite explicit
identities and integer bounds. The recorded deterministic run, with
seed 20261004, passed 14,953 assertions:

  1,692 beta-code assertions;
    900 scale assertions;
 12,180 buffer assertions;
    181 anchored-identity assertions.

The checks include 24 small CRT coding cases in bases 2, 3, 5, and 7
through length 9; 24 scale cases with c near 2^512, 2^1024, 2^2048, and
2^4096; 2,856 finite vertex checks; and 36 anchored-identity cases.
Sixteen scale cases have an anchor larger than the local width.
Eighteen anchored cases deliberately have a combined product index
outside the code, while using only separately valid local values.

These tests detect rounding, domain, and identity errors in finite
instances. They do not verify nonstandard models, external
uncountability, infinite fusion, Borel regularization, ATR_0 provability,
or the mathematical theorem. Standard finite CRT constructions in the
script do not establish arbitrary internal code existence in I Delta_0.

UNRESOLVED LIMITS

1. The coded domain has not been proved equivalent to every ordinary
   bounded-formula definition of partial exponentiation. A recoding
   principle is a further research question, not an implicit premise.

2. Restriction makes each fixed-base coded length domain downward
   closed. Closure under successor, addition, multiplication, or code
   concatenation is not established. Those properties need code-existence
   arguments beyond restriction of an existing code.

3. The existence of an uncountable Borel Polish I Delta_0 model with
   separately or jointly continuous multiplication remains unresolved.
   Thus the broader bare-bounded-induction question is not settled.

4. Bases, code parameters, and endpoint values are not all proved to lie
   in the countable core. Full elementarity and exponential closure of
   the core are also not claimed.

5. The variable-base endpoint relation is analytic by projection.
   Its Borelness is not proved; endpoint uniqueness alone does not
   supply a Borel domain. The valid-certificate relation itself is
   Borel under the stated continuity hypothesis.

6. The source audit found no matching local theorem in the inspected
   material, but it does not establish global bibliographic priority.
   All novelty claims remain subject to further literature review and
   independent mathematical scrutiny.
