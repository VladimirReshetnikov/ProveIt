# Status and claim boundary

## Proven in the manuscript by conventional arguments

1. Finite-moment fibres through the dyadic spectrum with fixed exact support.
2. Chebyshev root power-sum matching and the exact first unmatched cumulant.
3. A middle ordered-coordinate gap of at least 1/(48 n^2).
4. A Fourier H^1 estimate implying TV <= 40 n (6/7)^(2n-1), for every n >= 2.
5. Variance normalization, uniform bounds on all density derivatives, and
   convergence in total variation to the dyadic law.
6. Failure of pairwise local Hölder inversion; a logarithmic-squared inverse
   modulus lower bound; and a logarithmic-squared minimax risk lower bound.
7. Elementary positive recovery certificates under additional finite-factor,
   known-tail, and separation hypotheses.

## Established background, not claimed as new

Generalized uniform-sum cumulants and full-law identifiability are in
Billey–Swanson (arXiv:2010.12701v2, Lemma 3.11 and Theorem 3.13).
The article gives elementary proofs of the summable cases it needs.
Their topological parametrization theory and general moment-inversion
ill-conditioning are also acknowledged explicitly.

## Not established

- Priority over all literature or all ProveIt reports.
- A solution of a named, published open conjecture.
- Optimality of the logarithmic exponent or a matching estimation upper bound.
- Failure of pointwise Hölder stability relative to the exact dyadic law.
- Exponentially close pairs with exact support [-1,1] as well as all the
  quantitative properties of the main construction.
- An exhaustive audit of ProveIt or formal status inherited from that project.

The eight research questions in the article are proposed continuations;
they are not claims that all those questions were previously published as open.
