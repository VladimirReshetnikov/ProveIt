# Comb interpolation

This directory is an index. Its only live mathematical publication is
[`comb_interpolation_synthesis/`](comb_interpolation_synthesis/), the canonical
additive- and geometric-comb synthesis. No parallel manuscript remains at this
level.

The canonical package keeps each kind of record in one place:

- its [README](comb_interpolation_synthesis/README.md) is the reader and
  reproduction guide;
- its [provenance record](comb_interpolation_synthesis/PROVENANCE.md) gives the
  complete editorial lineage, source pin, file disposition, deduplication
  policy, and recovery path;
- its [validation record](comb_interpolation_synthesis/assets/VALIDATION.md)
  reports the completed and outstanding checks; and
- its [companion-evidence index](comb_interpolation_synthesis/assets/README.md)
  routes the unique retained scripts, data, outputs, and figures.

The geometric q-series layer also cross-links to the forward-theory part of
the canonical
[`q_pochhammer_q_binomial_monograph`](../../exponents-and-q-series/q_pochhammer_q_binomial_monograph/)
synthesis and its 334-page publication PDF. Its retained figure PDFs are
research assets only.
