# Comb interpolation

This subgroup contains documents about additive or geometric sampling combs
and the interpolation, quadrature, extrapolation, and stability problems they
induce:

- [`Dyadic_Comb_Frontiers/`](Dyadic_Comb_Frontiers/)
- [`geometric_comb_interpolation_report/`](geometric_comb_interpolation_report/)
- [`geometric_comb_interpolation_report-3/`](geometric_comb_interpolation_report-3/)
- [`geometric_comb_q_fabius_report/`](geometric_comb_q_fabius_report/)

The geometric reports are distinct arrivals rather than byte duplicates.
Their q-series machinery cross-links to `exponents-and-q-series`, but their
primary subject is interpolation on a sampling comb.
