[`comb_interpolation_synthesis/`](comb_interpolation_synthesis/), the canonical
additive- and geometric-comb synthesis. No parallel manuscript remains at this
level.

The canonical package keeps each kind of record in one place:

- its [README](comb_interpolation_synthesis/README.md) is the reader and
  reproduction guide;

  complete editorial lineage, source pin, file disposition, deduplication

  reports the completed and outstanding checks; and
- its [companion-evidence index](comb_interpolation_synthesis/assets/README.md)
  routes the unique retained scripts, data, outputs, and figures.
