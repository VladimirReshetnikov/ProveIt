# Comb interpolation

This subgroup now has one canonical publication:

- [`comb_interpolation_synthesis/`](comb_interpolation_synthesis/) —
  *Comb Interpolation and Sampling Frontiers: Additive and Geometric Combs in
  the Fabius--Rvachev System*.

The synthesis replaces four overlapping report trees: the former consolidated
additive-dyadic volume and three standalone geometric-comb reports. It develops
additive dyadic quadrature and interpolation together with geometric
Lagrange/Jackson--Newton filters, Gaussian-Pascal transforms, stability,
regular variation, Mellin methods, Fabius boundary recovery, and Rvachev
sinc-product reconstruction. Shared results are stated once; source-specific
extensions and alternative proof ideas are kept only when they add
mathematical value.

The additive-dyadic source had already absorbed nine earlier manuscript
packages. Their duplicate documentation, along with the three geometric
manuscript wrappers, has been retired after source-level reconciliation.
Unique scripts, exact data, text outputs, and PNG figures remain under the
canonical package's
[`assets/companion-evidence/`](comb_interpolation_synthesis/assets/companion-evidence/),
grouped by historical source slug. Git revision
`73f0b373126ef22a3b5dccadfa7b99d61d445345` is the immutable pre-retirement
baseline.

See the canonical package's [README](comb_interpolation_synthesis/README.md),
[provenance record](comb_interpolation_synthesis/PROVENANCE.md), and
[validation record](comb_interpolation_synthesis/assets/VALIDATION.md). The
source disposition is established; a final PDF and complete publication
validator are separate gates and are not inferred from this navigation entry.
