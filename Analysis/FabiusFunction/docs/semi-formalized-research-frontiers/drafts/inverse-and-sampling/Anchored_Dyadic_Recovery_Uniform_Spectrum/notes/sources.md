## Novelty boundary

Qualitative identifiability, Newton identities, sine products, Rouché, the
argument principle, Plancherel, and DKW–Massart are not claimed as new.
The proposed contribution is the anchored sharp logarithmic exponent, the
separated smooth counterexample family, the unknown-infinite-tail prefix
Lipschitz theorem, and the derived testing/confidence consequences.
An exhaustive literature-priority search and independent mathematical review
remain outstanding.
