Audit method
------------
The recursive GitHub directory structure was traversed by TeX-bearing document family.
Mathematical content was read through the current canonical documents and the large
consolidated compilations, with archived originals spot-checked to detect prior
appearances and normalization changes. Frozen historical revisions and reports
absorbed verbatim into later compilations were treated as one mathematical lineage;
this is a mathematical-content audit rather than a claim that byte-identical copies
were separately line-read.
