# q-Series and inverse q-analogs

This subgroup now has one canonical package:

- [`q_series_and_inverse_analogs/`](q_series_and_inverse_analogs/) contains
  *q-Series and Inverse q-Analogs: A Proof-Oriented Synthesis*. Its single
  master combines forward q-Pochhammer, Gaussian, hypergeometric, theta,
  partition, Bailey, cyclotomic, interpolation, Thue--Morse, and
  Fabius--Rvachev theory with the branch-aware inverse calculus, asymptotics,
  certification, and research frontiers.

The former forward monograph and inverse-q synthesis are implementation and
provenance sources of this neutral successor, not sibling publications. The
three general guides were likewise retired after their independent material
was integrated. The completed
[`source_concordance.csv`](q_series_and_inverse_analogs/source_concordance.csv)
records a reviewed disposition for all 547 source result environments in the
five-publication merge. Repository history and the two pinned audit revisions
preserve every source layout. All five superseded manuscript PDFs were
removed, and no replacement PDF was generated by user direction; retained
PDF figures under the canonical `assets/` tree are reproducibility evidence
rather than documents.
