\begin{abstract}
This volume replaces six overlapping reports on inverse \(q\)-analogs and
their series expansions with one branch-aware account.  Its organizing
principle is that inversion is meaningful only after the forward observable,
the parameter being recovered, a base point or asymptotic regime, and a branch
have all been specified.  Universal reversion is developed once and then
specialized to finite and infinite \(q\)-Pochhammer products, Gaussian and
multinomial coefficients, \(q\)-gamma and related special functions,
cyclotomic limits, and the Fabius--Rvachev geometric-product setting.

Every nonconjectural result is accompanied by a complete human-readable proof.
Claims backed by Lean are named explicitly; correct classical arguments that
remain outside Lean are labelled as human-proved frontier results; numerical
observations are not promoted to theorems.  Statements that remain genuinely
open appear only in theorem-style environments named \emph{Conjecture}, with
their outstanding obligations stated.
\end{abstract}
