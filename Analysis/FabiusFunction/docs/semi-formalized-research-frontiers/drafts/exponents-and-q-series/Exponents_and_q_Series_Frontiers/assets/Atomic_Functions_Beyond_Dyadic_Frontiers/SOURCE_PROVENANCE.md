# Source provenance and reconstruction notes

The report was reconstructed from the two files in `source_notes/`:

- `rvachev_source_ocr.tex` — OCR transcription of the Russian chapter.
- `rvachev_source_pages.pdf` — the surviving printed pages used to resolve formulas and typography.

The printed pages cover the latter part of the first-order atomic-function analysis and the beginning of the chapter's treatment of the Rvachev `up` function. They include the family `h_a`, its random-series interpretation, support, Taylor-coefficient recurrence, superfactorial estimates, the `a>2` full-measure polynomial-spline structure, the repeated derivative formula with binary signs, the dyadic specialization `h_2=up`, moment formulas, dyadic values, and a fast binary evaluation recursion.

OCR defects were not silently converted into invented formulas. When a printed formula was unambiguous, it was typeset in standard notation. When indices were structurally recoverable but individual glyphs were damaged, an invariant equivalent statement was independently verified. The most damaged indexed formulas are recorded in the report's OCR reconstruction ledger rather than presented as character-for-character transcriptions.
