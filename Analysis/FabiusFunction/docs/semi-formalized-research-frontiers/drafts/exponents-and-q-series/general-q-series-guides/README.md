# General q-series guides

These three independent proof-oriented guides have the strongest mutual
overlap in this topic: q-Pochhammer products, Gaussian coefficients, basic
hypergeometric summation, theta products, partitions, Bailey machinery, and
Rogers--Ramanujan theory. They remain distinct documents pending a deliberate
claim-level comparison; this subgroup simply places the natural comparison
set together.

| Package | Primary emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| [`q-series-proof-oriented-article/`](q-series-proof-oriented-article/) | The most extensive proof-oriented article and the broadest secondary-identity coverage of the three. |
| [`q_series_from_first_principles/`](q_series_from_first_principles/) | A shorter first-principles route through products, sums, theta functions, partitions, and Bailey pairs. |
| [`q_series_monograph/`](q_series_monograph/) | A compact proof-driven monograph emphasizing hypergeometric identities, Bailey chains, and Rogers--Ramanujan theory. |

See the parent [topic index](../README.md) for status and provenance, and the
repository-wide [draft manifest](../../MANIFEST.md) for arrival history.
