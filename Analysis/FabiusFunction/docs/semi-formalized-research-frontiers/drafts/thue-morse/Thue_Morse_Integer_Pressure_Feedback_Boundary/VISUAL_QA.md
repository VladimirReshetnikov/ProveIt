# Visual verification

The final eight-page PDF was rendered with Poppler at 100 dpi. Every page was
visually inspected. The final correction changed only pages 7 and 8; these were
re-rendered and inspected, while pages 1–6 were byte-identical to their already
inspected page images. No clipping, overlap, missing glyph, or isolated
reference page remains. The final TeX log has no warnings or overfull/underfull
boxes. Mathematical transcription and trace verification were reviewed
independently from layout.
