# Proof status

## Universal conclusions

For every integer m>=2, H_(m,m)>0 and the degree 4m pressure coefficient is
strictly positive. The proof combines:

1. An absolutely convergent mixed-orbit regularization at the first feedback boundary
2. A strictly positive explicit pure-insertion resolvent
3. A uniform strict tangent-product comparison
4. A local-limit diagonal asymptotic with explicitly defined constants
5. An exact analytic cutoff m>=70, using the countable Bernoulli integer-mean mode theorem
6. Exact integer enclosure certificates for every m=2,...,69

The finite part consists of 68 moments and 4828 response orders. The primary
generator uses verified fraction-free inverses, a fixed rational grid,
weighted-l 1 norm error propagation, and checked integer rounding. Compressed
traces preserve every vector and error bound. A separate checker validates
coverage, baselines, error recurrences, final intervals, and cross-comparisons.
Full matrix-level replay is supplied separately from the quick audit.

## Sharpness and finite orientation

H_(2,3)=-488/27 proves that the universal response region 1<=r<=m cannot be
extended to r=m+1 for all m. Pressure at the corresponding degree remains
positive. Exact first-negative pressure degrees above 2m are recorded only
for m=2,...,14; no general rounding formula or limiting ratio is claimed.

## Review and limits

The analytic decomposition, positive pure term, tangent refinement, tail
normalization, asymptotic constant, cutoff, pressure absorption, finite
recurrences, exact enclosure method, per-moment traces, and final transcription
received independent mathematical review. All final PDF pages were rendered
and visually inspected.

The result is unrefereed ordinary mathematics, with a finite computer-assisted
component. It is not a Lean formalization or a claim of worldwide priority.
Individual atomic-basis coefficient positivity and the pressure-only range
1<=r<2m remain open in this report.
