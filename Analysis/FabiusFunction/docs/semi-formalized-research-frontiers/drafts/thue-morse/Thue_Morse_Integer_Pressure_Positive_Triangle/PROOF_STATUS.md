# Proof status

## Proved

1. For every positive odd n, the decreasing rearrangement of
   |tan(n*pi/2^(j+1))|/n is weakly log-majorized by tan(pi/2^(j+1)).
2. The corresponding infinite tangent products satisfy coefficientwise domination.
3. The full pre-feedback eigenfunction response triangle is strictly positive:
   H_(m,r)>0 for every integer m>=2 and 1<=r<m.
4. The same full pressure triangle is strictly positive: E_(m,r)>0.
5. The explicit lower bound in Theorem 1.1 is valid uniformly in the indicated integer range.

The tangent proof uses finite prefix products and a single crossing, avoiding
an infinite product of ratios. Only summable tangent products and absolutely
convergent orbit sums require limits. A self-contained finite-dimensional
majorization lemma is included.

- 91 response and pressure pairs, m=2,...,14 and 1<=r<m
- 28 independent direct phase-pressure comparisons, m=2,...,8
- 36 exploratory even eigenbasis responses, m=2,...,9
- The negative m=2 coefficient at degree twelve, via a simple-root cubic residual

## Not claimed

- Universal nonnegativity of individual eigenbasis response coefficients
- Positivity of all post-feedback pressure coefficients
- A uniform phase-analyticity radius as m varies
- External peer review, formal theorem-prover verification, or worldwide priority
