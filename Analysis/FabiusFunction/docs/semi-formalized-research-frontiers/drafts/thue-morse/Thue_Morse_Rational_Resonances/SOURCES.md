## Closest external comparisons

**Bartosz Sobolewski, Cyclotomic properties of polynomials associated with automatic sequences.**
https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.12052

This establishes an existing setting for recurrence relations at roots of unity. The present article does not claim that the existence of a root-of-unity recurrence is new; it derives explicit local profiles and all weighted coefficients.

**William Duke and Ha Nam Nguyen, Infinite products of cyclotomic polynomials.**
https://www.math.ucla.edu/~wdduke/preprints/cyclotomic.pdf

This supplies a close comparison for infinite products and their behavior near roots of unity. The present work instead emphasizes finite-depth moving-window normalization and coalescing dyadic roots. The author-hosted PDF's title page and relevant text were inspected.

## Background and recent neighboring work

**Jean-Paul Allouche and Jeffrey Shallit, The Ubiquitous Prouhet–Thue–Morse Sequence.**
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4471-0551-0_1
https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~shallit/Papers/ubiq15.pdf

Classical survey and historical context. The author-hosted text was consulted.

**J. Fabius, A probabilistic example of a nowhere analytic C-infinity-function.**
https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00536652

Historical attribution for the distribution construction. The product and the elementary smoothness facts needed here are proved in the article.

**Michael Baake and Michael Coons, Correlations of the Thue–Morse sequence.**
https://arxiv.org/html/2209.07102v2

**Philipp Gohlke, Marc Kesseböhmer, and Tanja I. Schindler, Generalized Thue–Morse measures: spectral and fractal analysis.**
https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22109v1

**Benoit Cloitre, The Thue–Morse Transform.**
https://arxiv.org/html/2604.06243v1

These papers were consulted to distinguish the proposed extensions from current work on correlations, diffraction, and generalized Prouhet transforms. The article's digit-series laws are not identified with the classical Thue–Morse diffraction measure.
