## Primary mathematical literature

1. Philipp Gohlke, Marc Kesseböhmer, Tanja I. Schindler.
   *Generalized Thue-Morse measures: spectral and fractal analysis*.
   arXiv:2509.22109v1, submitted 26 September 2025.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22109
   https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22109v1
   Used for the published framework, the explicit atomic pressure formula,
   and the order-two analytic case. Not used as an unexamined spectral-gap
   assumption in the new proofs.

2. Philipp Gohlke, Georgios Lamprinakis, Jörg Schmeling.
   *On a family of singular potentials: Parameter dependence of thermodynamic
   characteristics*. arXiv:2603.19001v1, submitted 19 March 2026.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19001
   https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19001v1
   Theorem 2.2 establishes positive-order phase continuity; Theorem 2.3 and
   the following discussion identify the stronger regularity question.
   The relevant PDF page was also visually inspected during research.
   Their sine convention has atomic phase 1/2, corresponding to phase zero
   in the cosine convention used here.

3. Tosio Kato. *Perturbation Theory for Linear Operators*.
   Springer, 1995 reprint of the second edition.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-66282-9
   Standard reference for the Riesz spectral reduction framework. The
   particular norm-square compression lemma is proved in the article.

4. NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions.
   https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.5
   https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.11
   Gamma/digamma reflection identities and the Hurwitz zeta function.
