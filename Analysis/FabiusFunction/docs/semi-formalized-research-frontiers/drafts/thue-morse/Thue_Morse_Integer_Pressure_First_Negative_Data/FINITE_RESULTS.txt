Preserved limitations:
- Cases m=16,...,20 have some inconclusive coefficients strictly after the first-negative endpoint.
- Initial auxiliary precision p=256 attempts at m=64 and128 were inconclusive and are recorded separately.
- Repeated high-precision runs at m=64 (p=1536) and128 (p=2560) certified the entire required prefix and endpoint.
- No finite calculation supplies an effective cutoff for an existential asymptotic theorem.
