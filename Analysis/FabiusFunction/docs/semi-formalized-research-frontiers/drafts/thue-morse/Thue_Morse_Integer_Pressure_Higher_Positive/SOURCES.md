# Sources and provenance

The precise starting source is the ProveIt manuscript *Integer Pressure and a
Missing Taylor Coefficient*, inspected at commit
6a98f89bac85cc6b4ba5d5c3b63996037d9824e1, source blob
1973fd17245864bb8a83e1b1d90416ac032ba7c1.

https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/6a98f89bac85cc6b4ba5d5c3b63996037d9824e1/Analysis/FabiusFunction/docs/semi-formalized-research-frontiers/drafts/thue-morse/Thue_Morse_Integer_Pressure/article.tex

That source supplies the finite transfer convention, local simple eigenvalue,
atomic spectrum, feedback identity, and missing degree-2m coefficient. The
separate research companion *The First Positive Coefficient in Integer Thue
Morse Pressure*, dated 1 October 2026, supplies the previously proved
quadratic-response positivity. Both sources are cited in the article.

The present proofs concern higher eigenfunction responses, the second
post-cancellation pressure coefficient, and a uniform region of positive
coefficients. The negative later coefficient is independently checked from
the source's explicit cubic. No exhaustive literature or priority claim is
made. This package leaves the source and first companion unchanged.
