# Proof status

Established in this report:

- An absolutely convergent half-integer formula for the eigenfunction
  coefficient H_(m,r) whenever m>=r+1
- A uniform explicit lower bound from the two nearest half-integer branches
- Strict positivity of H_(m,2) and the pressure coefficient at degree 2m+4
  for every integer m>=2, with exact exceptional cases at small m
- Strict positivity at pressure degree 2m+2r throughout m>=3r
- The exact negative coefficient -35360872/93555 at m=2, degree 12

Inputs explicitly credited:

- The source manuscript's local finite transfer model, simple atomic spectrum,
  and pressure identification
- The separate first-coefficient companion's positivity of H_(m,1)

The full derivation was independently reviewed. The exact small-case response
values were compared with a distinct pressure recursion. The negative term
was independently verified using the cubic characteristic equation. All final
PDF pages were rendered and visually inspected.

Not claimed:

- Positivity at every later degree
- An optimal uniform positive region
- A uniform radius or remainder in a joint limit of m and phase
- Worldwide priority or formal proof-assistant verification
