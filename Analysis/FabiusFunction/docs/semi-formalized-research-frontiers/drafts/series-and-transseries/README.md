# Series and transseries

Draft packages whose subject is the formal-series calculus itself — the
algebra, division, composition, and reversion of asymptotic expansions —
rather than any one special function.

- [`polynomial-logarithmic-transseries/`](polynomial-logarithmic-transseries/)
  holds the six independently written articles on polynomial-logarithmic
  transseries.

The group was created on 2026-09-02 by splitting these packages out of
[`../lambert-w/`](../lambert-w/), where they had been filed on 2026-09-01
because Lambert W is their guiding example.  The move was verbatim: no source,
checksum ledger, or PDF changed, and no PDF was rebuilt for it.

See [`../MANIFEST.md`](../MANIFEST.md) for the group record.
