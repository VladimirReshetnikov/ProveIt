is the canonical volume *Polynomial--Logarithmic Transseries: Algebra,
Composition, Series Reversal, and the Lambert W Archetype*: a
self-contained operational treatment of the scale generated by `t = 1/X`
and `L = log X` as `X` tends to infinity, covering the formal rings and
their valuation calculus, arithmetic and the differential calculus of the
distinguished derivation, composition and the exact formal Taylor formula,
series reversal by four independent routes, Wright omega and the Lambert
polynomials, the Lambert W branch expansions, the passage from formal
transseries to Poincare asymptotics, algorithms with residual
certificates, and the wider transseries landscape.

## Provenance

Six independently written packages arrived as bare directories in
direct-arrival commit `730e1763291099cd50ca1e20ed2c62c38d95ab4f`, were
filed on 2026-09-01, regrouped here on 2026-09-02, and were merged
editorially into this volume the same day.  No source was a superset of
the others and each contributed at least one layer no other supplied.
The absorbed directories were deleted after a residue audit; git history
is the archive, and the volume's provenance appendix carries each
source's intake and absorbed SHA-256 receipts.

Every result in the volume carries a proof.  Where a source asserted a
result without one, the proof was supplied or the assertion was demoted to
an explicitly labelled open problem; where a source claimed analytic
validity on the strength of formal algebra, the claim was weakened to what
the algebra establishes.  Corrections are marked `% ed.:` at the point of
repair and collected in the volume's ledger of editorial repairs.

None of the proofs is machine-checked.  The volume's formalization
register records what the Lean corpus does and does not cover, and
distinguishes a formalized neighbour from actual coverage.
