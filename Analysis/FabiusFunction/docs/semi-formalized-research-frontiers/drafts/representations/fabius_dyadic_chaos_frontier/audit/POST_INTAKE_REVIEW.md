# Post-intake mathematical and reproducibility review

Review date: 2026-08-30.

This note records the repository-side review performed after the archive had
passed the quick intake gate and had already been published. It deliberately
separates four kinds of evidence:

1. a proof written in the manuscript;
2. a successful deterministic numerical replay;
3. a matching or supporting Lean declaration;
4. a Lean theorem with exactly the manuscript's hypotheses and conclusion.

These are not interchangeable. In particular, the manuscript's `P` and `I`
markers concern the first category, while `LEAN_CROSSWALK.md` audits the third
and fourth categories statement by statement.

## Mathematical re-assessment

The principal product, ANOVA, q-binomial, phase, Laplace, Thue--Morse, and
Lambert-W normalizations survived an independent formula audit. The review did
find several local statement or proof defects. The TeX source was repaired as
follows.

### Zero-field conventions

- The local odds are now defined everywhere by
  `r(x) = m(2x) / m(x)^2 - 1`, so `r(0) = 0`. The identity
  `r(x) = x coth(x) - 1` is used only for `x != 0`.
- The activation probability is totalized by `p(0) = 0`; any bound containing
  `1/x` is stated only for `x > 0`.
- The conditional local Legendre degree law has its natural continuous
  extension at zero: degree one has mass one and every higher degree has mass
  zero. Equivalently, its generating function satisfies `D(0,z) = z`.

### Infinite product and tensor arguments

- The notation `E_(S^c)` now denotes conditional expectation onto the
  sigma-algebra generated by the coordinates in `S`, rather than an
  unanalyzed infinite product of coordinate projections.
- The finite-prefix Hoeffding proof includes the missing tail mean. Its prefix
  sum is the exact conditional expectation
  `M_(>n)(t) exp(t sum_(j<=n) a_j V_j)`, and the countable result follows by an
  `L^2` martingale limit.
- Local Legendre Parseval is proved before it is used. The tensor expansion is
  then obtained from finite tensor products and the same conditional-
  expectation limit.

### Analytic domains and continuation

- The Maclaurin identity for `log R` is stated on `|t| < pi`. The identities
  for `R` and `R - 1` are stated on their sharper disk `|t| < 4*pi`.
- The `sech^2` exponential series is integrated term by term only in
  `Re(s) > 2`, where absolute Fubini is valid. The resulting identity is
  continued holomorphically to `Re(s) > 0`, after which integration by parts
  gives the activation Mellin formula in `0 < Re(s) < 1`.
- Boundary Fourier values explicitly use the named continuation
  `M_p^cont`; the equivalent derivative-kernel formula remains an actually
  convergent integral.
- The bilateral phase probability-generating function is stated on
  `C^* = C \ {0}`, with locally uniform convergence there.

### Phase limit and mode set

- The no-central-limit-width statement is now an assertion as `t -> infinity`,
  uniform in dyadic phase. It is not asserted uniformly as `t -> 0`.
- Variance convergence uses the direct omitted-tail estimate

  ```text
  0 <= Var(K_theta) - Var(K_t)
     = sum_(k<=-n) p_(theta,k) (1 - p_(theta,k))
    <= 2/t.
  ```

  This replaces the invalid inference that bounded second moments alone imply
  uniform integrability of their squares.
- Log-concavity and full support prove that the mode set is a nonempty finite
  interval of consecutive integers. The paper no longer claims, without a
  strictness argument, that the interval has at most two points.

### Endpoint statements

- The continuous Thue--Morse corner theorem now assumes `g in C^N(I)` on an
  open interval containing the entire corner and integration interval.
- The Legendre cutoff `n_epsilon(x)` is defined as a minimum on the monotone
  tail of the explicit coefficient envelope. The ratio
  `b_(n+1)(x) / b_n(x) = |x| / (2n+3)` proves that every later coefficient,
  not merely the first crossing, satisfies the tolerance. The Lambert-W
  expression is the asymptotic inversion of this certified cutoff.

## Lean boundary

`../LEAN_CROSSWALK.md` contains every one of the 41 nonconjectural report
labels exactly once: 14 theorems, 8 propositions, 3 lemmas, and 16
corollaries. It also inventories the 3 conjectures and 4 open problems.

At the start of this review, no report result was a Lean theorem exactly as
stated. Many have strong formalized cores - especially the finite q-binomial
identities, centered cumulants, negative-Laplace decomposition, mixed-
difference/Thue--Morse algebra, Legendre infrastructure, and principal
Lambert-W inversion - but a supporting core is not a proof of the enclosing
paper result.

This review added and focused-built
`FabiusFunction.ThueMorseSymmetricDifference`. Its two definitions and eleven
public theorem declarations provide a centered additive-action API, the exact
Boolean-cube expansion, sharp arbitrary-step polynomial extraction and
cancellation, the centered dyadic Thue--Morse block, and both
common-denominator and report-facing positive-order reindexings. The focused
target completed with `LAKE_JOBS=1`, exit code zero, and no warnings. The
crosswalk still classifies `thm:TM-corner` as near-complete rather than
complete because the manuscript theorem also states the repeated-integral
identity under `C^N` hypotheses. That analytic clause and a final
report-shaped wrapper remain; the displayed decreasing-weight reindexing is
no longer a gap.

A subsequent integration completed and source-verified the seven-module
activation tranche. `FabiusFunction.HyperbolicActivation` exposes four
definitions and 58 public theorem declarations for the real totalized
hyperbolic sinc, totalized `tanh(x)/x`, activation odds and probability,
their continuity/parity/sign algebra, the global activation bounds, and
`Fabius.tanhDiv_eq_dslope`, which identifies the removable quotient with the
divided slope of `Real.tanh`. `FabiusFunction.ActivationTaylor` adds three
theorems: `Fabius.tanh_sub_taylor_nine_isBigO`,
`Fabius.activationProbability_sub_taylor_eight_isBigO`, and
`Fabius.activationProbability_sub_taylor_eight_isBigO_pow`.  They prove the
degree-nine `Real.tanh` jet and both the canonical norm-power and literal
`O(x^10)` forms of the report's activation jet.
`FabiusFunction.ActivationSeries` adds three theorems for the rescaled pointwise
bound, genuine summability, and the global budget of an arbitrary-index
square-summable real family. `ActivationAsymptotics` adds two
punctured limits, including arbitrary real dilation, and
`ActivationSeriesAsymptotics` adds the exact Tannery limit. Finally,
`GeometricActivationDimension` exposes two definitions and 30 theorems,
including the exact normalized geometric-weight square sum, while
`GeometricActivationAsymptotics` adds the sharp generic and dyadic
coefficients. The combined public surface is 6 definitions and 99 theorems,
or 105 declarations.

  tensor-Legendre limit, phase coupling, and Lambert cutoff are still
  human-readable arguments until their exact crosswalk rows are closed in
  Lean.
- The numerical program is a deterministic consistency check, not an interval
  certificate and not a substitute for a proof.
- The three conjectures and four problems remain open. They are not represented
  by premise-free placeholder theorems.
- Stronger reusable targets suggested by the review include a general
  product-observable active-set theorem, an arbitrary-independent-coordinate
  interaction bound, a basis-independent marked-chaos theorem, and the fixed-q
