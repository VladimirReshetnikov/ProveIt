# Corpus audit and nonduplication boundary

## Scope

The target was the recursive LaTeX documentation tree under
`Analysis/FabiusFunction/docs` in Vladimir Reshetnikov's `ProveIt` repository.
The current top level contains the principal Fabius--Rvachev exposition, the
mathematical glossary, the non-elementarity study, the Lean walkthrough,
transcribed papers, archives, semi-formalized material, and the canonical
non-formalized research-frontier system.

The repository's current draft manifest records a reorganization dated
28 August 2026. Earlier living drafts were absorbed into thematic
consolidations while provenance was retained. The audit therefore used the
current consolidated source files as the primary source-level interface and
used the recursive path inventories/provenance ledgers to screen absorbed and
historical documents rather than counting duplicated revisions as independent
prior art.

## Source families inspected

The comparison included the current consolidated material on:

- foundational Fabius, inverse Fabius, signed extension, and Rvachev theory;
- Thue--Morse formulas and finite Prouhet cancellations;
- exponent sequences, Gaussian q-binomial coefficients, q-Pochhammer products,
  reciprocal-base identities, and generalized geometric laws;
- Fourier decay, infinite sinc products, zero multiplicities, spectral
  determinants, heat traces, and arithmetic rays;
- Lambert-W endpoint analysis and all-orders inverse-Fabius asymptotics;
- integrations, transforms, resolvents, fractional calculus, and quantile
  transport;
- inverse and sampling frontiers, Appell deconvolution, Barnes--Bernoulli
  prefixes, and q-Richardson recovery;
- representation frontiers, Legendre/Lagrange synthesis, multiresolution,
  finite shifted-up systems, and operator models;
- archived source-paper transcriptions and frozen historical versions as
  identified by the repository manifests.

The audit was claim-oriented. In addition to reading theorem statements,
proof interfaces, conclusions, and cross-references, exact and close-variant
searches were made for the principal structures proposed here: Koopman
operators/eigenfunctions, affine-chain polynomial Poisson equations,
fixed-point Stein discrepancies, differential Stein operators, reversibility,
Appell martingales, transfer determinants, and scalar Stein kernels in Fabius
coordinates.

## Principal corpus-relative additions in this report

The contribution boundary is the dynamical/operator composition of those
interfaces. The report proves:

1. exact Appell--Koopman diagonalization for the stationary affine-uniform
   chain, including the general parameter `-1 < r < 1`;
2. a q-Weyl/oscillator calculus, exact spectral coordinate projectors, dual
   eigendistributions, and a Legendre--Appell conversion formula;
3. finite and Appell-Fock transfer determinants with q-Pochhammer form;
4. closed polynomial resolvent and Poisson equations;
5. a fixed-point Stein discrepancy quantitatively equivalent to Wasserstein-1;
6. a differential-dilation Stein operator whose adjoint is Rvachev's equation;
7. exact polynomial and exponential martingales and all-lag Appell correlation
   formulas;
8. a factored exact irreversibility witness proving nonreversibility for every
   nontrivial affine-uniform chain with `0 < |r| < 1`;
9. an exact scalar Stein-kernel formula in Fabius coordinates, its dyadic
   arithmetic, inverse-Fabius representation, and Appell moment transform;
10. a Lambert-periodic two-term endpoint asymptotic for the Stein kernel.

## External-priority caution

Targeted literature screening was used to place the Appell-martingale and Stein
method terminology in context. It did not reveal these exact Fabius--Rvachev
formulas under the present normalization. Nevertheless, the report makes only a
repository-relative novelty claim, not a complete global-priority claim.
