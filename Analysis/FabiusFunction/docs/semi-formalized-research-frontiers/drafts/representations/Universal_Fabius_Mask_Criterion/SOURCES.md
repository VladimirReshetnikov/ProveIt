## Classical context

D. Blackwell, Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments, Annals of Mathematical Statistics 24 (1953), 265–272. https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032

E. N. Torgersen, Comparison of Translation Experiments, Annals of Mathematical Statistics 43 (1972), 1383–1399. https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177692372
Its abstract credits earlier convolution-divisibility criteria to Boll and Strassen. The present report supplies direct proofs for its own sum-reweighted model and makes no general priority claim.

F. W. Steutel and J. G. F. Thiemann, On the independence of integer and fractional parts, Statistica Neerlandica 43 (1989), 53–59. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9574.1989.tb01246.x

https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08375v3
The abstract and introduction identify q-binomial coefficients with counts of partitions in a rectangle. That identity is classical and is not attributed as a new result of that paper or of the present note.
