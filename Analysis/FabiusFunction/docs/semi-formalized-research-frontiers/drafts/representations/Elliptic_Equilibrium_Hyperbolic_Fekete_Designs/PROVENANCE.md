## Claim status

The elliptic equilibrium solves a repository-local question by an explicit
self-contained calculation within classical Green-capacity theory. It is
not represented as a newly discovered elliptic capacity formula.

The bounded-defect and dimension-uniform boundary analysis, including its
three-order critical-window consequence, is developed and proved in this
manuscript. A targeted literature search did not establish an exact prior
match, but that does not establish worldwide priority.

The supplied code performs checks, not proof verification. No Lean build or
independent peer review was performed. Fixed-interval microscopic endpoint
spacings and the complete joint MacMahon crossover remain research targets.
