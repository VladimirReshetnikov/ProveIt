# Revision 2 dated 1 October 2026

The article and package keep their original filenames. The first edition is preserved locally under `versions/v1/` and is not duplicated inside the current ZIP.

Changes in this edition:

1. Proves every fixed dyadic contact order j >= 1 for the two factors U_(1/2) and U_(2^(-j)), with a positive explicit order-j Fourier lower coefficient
2. Extends the theorem to every fixed integer base B >= 2, with resonance q = 1/B and factors U_(1/B), U_(B^(-j))
3. Gives the complete finite signed-kernel, tail-interpolation, weighted-integrability, Taylor, and positivity-repair proof for all fixed depths
4. Retains the non-dyadic linear one-sided coefficient theorem, full tangent-cone proof, and explicit quadratic density/energy construction
5. Adds a worked cubic example, three reproducible checkers and receipts, and more precise source attribution
6. Replaces the former open question about a higher-depth law with the remaining questions about exact coefficients and uniform estimates

The general theorem gives matching upper and lower orders. It does not claim an exact normalized higher-order limit, evaluated optimal coefficients, or estimates uniform as B and j vary. No proof-assistant formalization or global novelty certification is included.
