PROVEIT FABIUS-FUNCTION CORPUS AUDIT
====================================

Repository
----------
https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/tree/main/Analysis/FabiusFunction/docs
Access date: 30 August 2026

Scope requested
---------------
The user requested screening of LaTeX documents under Analysis/FabiusFunction/docs,
including subdirectories, for a frontier report that avoids exact duplication.

Method used
-----------
1. The live documentation tree was inventoried at the top level.  It contains the
   main Fabius/Rvachev exposition, glossary, non-elementarity development, Lean
   walkthrough, papers, semi-formalized research frontiers, and a historical
   archive.

2. The two large canonical LaTeX volumes were treated as theorem/formula atlases:

   Analysis/FabiusFunction/docs/Fabius_Function_and_Rvachev_Up/
     Fabius_Function_and_Rvachev_Up.tex

   Analysis/FabiusFunction/docs/semi-formalized-research-frontiers/
     semi-formalized-research-frontiers.tex

3. The draft MANIFEST was used as the global thematic and provenance inventory for
   active, absorbed, relocated, and historical research drafts.  It was searched
   across the requested themes: Thue--Morse, Fabius and inverse Fabius, Rvachev up,
   endpoint and iterate asymptotics, q-series and inverse q-analogs, infinite sinc
   products, Lambert W, Legendre/Lagrange representations, Bell/Bernoulli
   polynomials, Stein/Koopman structures, transport, total positivity, and related
   families.

4. The closest-overlap raw LaTeX reports were read directly, especially:

   .../drafts/representations/fabius_iterates_nowhere_analytic/
     fabius_iterates_nowhere_analytic.tex

   together with the canonical derivative-cell and Fourier-decay sections.

5. The historical archive's own provenance notice states that byte-identical copies
   were pruned when the live tree already preserved them.  Frozen or absorbed
   duplicates were therefore represented through the manifest/provenance map rather
   than treated as independent mathematical sources.

Novelty boundary used in the report
-----------------------------------
The closest existing iterate report contains pointwise Denjoy--Carleman lower-growth
remarks and explicitly says that it does not provide an interval-uniform
classification.  The canonical exposition already contains exact derivative cells,
sharp global derivative bounds, the sinc product, prefix Fourier bounds, the sharp
logarithmic-Gaussian Fourier exponent, and the peak ray.  Those are imported as
baselines, not relabelled as new.

The report's principal new layer is the exact interval-uniform ultradifferentiable
geometry generated by those identities: a quantitative local derivative law, exact
local Denjoy--Carleman comparison, exact factorial-normalized Faà di Bruno transform,
iterate-class synthesis, and the discrete Legendre comparison with sharp Fourier
decay.  The Bell-edge expansion is explicitly conjectural and numerically tested.

Limitation and priority disclaimer
----------------------------------
This was a corpus-wide semantic audit designed to avoid duplicating results in the
specified repository.  It is not an assertion of absolute priority in the wider
mathematical literature, and none of the newly written proofs is claimed to be
Lean-verified.  The report labels each result as proved here, proved synthesis,
numerical evidence, conjecture, or open problem.
