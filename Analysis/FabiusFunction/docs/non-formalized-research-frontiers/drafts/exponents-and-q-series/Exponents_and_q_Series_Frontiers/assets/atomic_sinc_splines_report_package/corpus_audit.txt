surviving Section 5 ends midstream.  The PDF has eleven pages, printed pages

contains recurrent OCR defects: missing exponents and indices, Cyrillic/Latin
confusions, damaged binomial coefficients, broken integral limits, and isolated
wrong equation numbers.

Editorial rule used:

  * prefer the PDF page image when it resolves the OCR;
  * otherwise correct only when normalization, neighboring identities, and the
    source's own argument determine the formula;
  * explicitly label reconstructed passages;
  * do not manufacture the missing continuation after equation (3.100).

6. NON-DUPLICATION FINDINGS
---------------------------
The living repository already contains, in binary form or in its q-probability
layer, many ingredients that the Russian chapter presents in older notation:

  * Fabius/Rvachev definitions and the relation between F and up;
  * the binary infinite sinc product;
  * Thue-Morse derivative identities;
  * exact dyadic values and moment recurrences;
  * Bell-polynomial and Bernoulli-number organization;
  * q-binomial products and Lambert-series identities;
  * Lambert-W endpoint and inverse-Fabius asymptotics;
  * formalization crosswalks and a non-formalized frontier quarantine.

Those ingredients are marked [R] in the report, even when the attached source
uses a different normalization.

The following parameterized theorem packages did not appear in the audited
living sources in the same explicit form and are therefore marked [N], with
proofs included:

  * the affine identification of the full h_a family with the geometric-q
    density at q=1/a;
  * complete Bernoulli cumulants and Bell moments for every a>1;
  * the weighted Prouhet identity at geometric base a and the resulting full
    derivative hierarchy;
  * exact L-infinity and L-one derivative norms throughout a>=2;
  * the finite truncated-power spline formula and exact polynomial-gap atlas;
  * one-branch symbolic derivative localization on the Cantor set K_a;
  * strict interior positivity and the resulting complete Taylor-germ
    classification for a>2;
  * a rational-power Strang-Fix reproduction theorem on one scalar lattice;
  * an elementary log-Gaussian Fourier envelope for general a;
  * an all-orders C^r expansion for ordinary finite sinc prefixes, including
    the leading correction -h_a''/[6(a^2-1)];
  * a deterministic coupling estimate and C^r convergence as a decreases to 2.

Conjectures concerning scalar-lattice necessity, strict log-concavity in the
overlap regime, periodic Lambert endpoint terms, and critical double scaling are
marked [C], not [N].
