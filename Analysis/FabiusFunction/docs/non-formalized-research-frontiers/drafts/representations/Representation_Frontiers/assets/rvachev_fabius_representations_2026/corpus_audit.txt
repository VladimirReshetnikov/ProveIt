METHOD
------
The audit used the current repository MANIFEST, the source-level corpus
inventory, and the current consolidated frontier volume to enumerate the
mathematical TeX sources. Overlap-sensitive living reports were then read
directly in raw form. The repository's multiresolution report records an
89-TeX-path audit at commit 924652f4a9a656fd957d2936b2f053747c6f6c47;
the separate pinned corpus inventory contains 67 sources plus subsequently
added living reports and auxiliary source papers. Historical snapshots were
used as a duplication ledger rather than counted as independent novelty.

EXISTING FAMILIES EXCLUDED FROM NOVELTY CLAIMS
----------------------------------------------
The report does not claim the following as new, because they already occur in
the audited tree: infinite sinc products; the canonical dyadic zero product;
finite box-convolution splines; finite Thue-Morse truncated-power formulas;
Cauchy/Stieltjes transforms; logarithmic finite resolvents; the differential
resolvent equation; moment and cumulant formulas; Bell and Bernoulli
polynomials; one-sided Fourier-Laplace transforms; transform formulas for F
and F^{-1}; cotangent/digamma/Hurwitz-zeta logarithmic derivatives; Fredholm
determinants; Jacobi/Stieltjes continued fractions; Haar/Schauder/Walsh and
beta-mixture expansions; fractional Volterra integrals; Mellin/Newton complex
moments; inverse endpoint asymptotics; half-integer sign sampling; dyadic
cardinal reproduction; and the non-holonomicity obstruction from unbounded
zero multiplicities.

REPOSITORY-RELATIVE GAP SEARCH
------------------------------
Exact-term and structural searches in the consolidated volume and major
living reports found no mathematical occurrence of the following proposed
bridges:

  Caratheodory function; OPUC; Verblunsky coefficient; CMV matrix;
  Joukowski transform; probability-Chebyshev moment; Jacobi-Anger/Bessel
  product-series identities; argument-principle Thue-Morse formula;
  spectral zero-count encoding; logarithmic-monodromy encoding.

The multiresolution report contains an unweighted density-Chebyshev
expansion. The present report carefully distinguishes it from the new
probability-Chebyshev moments

  c_n = integral_{-1}^1 T_n(x) up(x) dx.

This distinction is structural: c_n are the Fourier moments of a unit-circle
probability lift and become coefficients in new Caratheodory, Joukowski,
Bessel, Schur, and CMV representations.

NOVELTY LANGUAGE
----------------
"New" in the report means "not located in the audited ProveIt subtree." It is
not a claim of priority over the entire mathematical literature. Standard
background results (Jacobi-Anger expansions, Schur algorithm, Geronimus
relations, Szego theorem, CMV representation, Shannon sampling, and the
argument principle) are cited and used as tools to derive repo-relative new
identities for the Fabius/Rvachev objects.
