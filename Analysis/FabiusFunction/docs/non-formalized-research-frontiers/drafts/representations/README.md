# Representations

Series and orthogonal-expansion representations of the up-function:
Jacobi-coefficient expansions with exact even moments, resolvent and
logarithmic-derivative identities, and related representation-theoretic
frontiers. (The random-variable representation itself is formalized in
`ProbabilityRepresentation.lean`; the drafts here explore analytic
expansions beyond it.)

Members: `Fabius_Rvachev_Representation_Frontiers`,
`Fabius_Rvachev_Multiresolution_Report` (dyadic multiresolution and
product–series representations: Haar–Schauder and Walsh–Thue–Morse
expansions, beta-mixture limits, Bell–Bernoulli scale energies,
inverse-quantile duality; arrived 2026-08-28).
