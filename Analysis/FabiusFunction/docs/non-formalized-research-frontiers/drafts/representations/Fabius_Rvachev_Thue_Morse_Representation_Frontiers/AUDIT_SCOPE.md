# Audit scope and novelty convention

The report is anchored to the 67-path recursive LaTeX ledger for
`Analysis/FabiusFunction/docs` at commit
`32d6d36c51d803289e6d6a0dc0c37753766eba47`, together with the live additions
listed at the end of `corpus_inventory.tex` and the principal living sources
preserved in `source_audit/`.

Because the repository's `main` branch is an active research tree, the phrase
"new" in the report means **not found in the audited snapshot after targeted
formula- and theorem-level comparison**. It is not an unconditional claim of
priority over all mathematical literature. Theorem/proposition/lemma labels in
the report carry proofs; conjectures and numerical observations are explicitly
identified as such.

The source audit distinguishes byte-identical or superseded historical copies
from mathematically distinct content. The full path ledger, including archived
lineages and paper-source transcriptions, appears in `corpus_inventory.tex` and
as an appendix of the report.
