consolidated (2026-08-28) into the single volume
[`Frontier_Compilations/`](Frontier_Compilations/) (259 pp; ten absorbed
reports; ten rendered parts):

  (formerly `Fabius_Rvachev_New_Frontiers/`);
- **Source report VI (rendered Part VI)** — *Confluent Digital Extrapolation and …*
  (formerly `fabius_frontier_report_bundle/`);
- **Source report VII (rendered Part VII)** — *Dyadic Spectral Determinants for the Fabius–Rvachev

- **Source report X (rendered Part X)** — *Beyond the Dyadic Fabius Web*
  (formerly `beyond_dyadic_fabius_web_report/`).

The mathematical bodies of source reports I–IX were preserved modulo
standalone wrappers and mechanical prefixing of labels, citation keys,
macro names, and asset paths. Source report X received the same mechanical
treatment plus post-snapshot Lean-status updates documenting exact-support
results. The former report directories were deleted; provenance with
SHA-256 hashes is recorded in the volume itself, and git history is the
archive.
