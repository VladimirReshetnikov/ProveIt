# Frontier compilations

The broad multi-topic "collected new results" report series,
consolidated (2026-08-28) into the single volume
[`Frontier_Compilations/`](Frontier_Compilations/) (262 pp, ten parts):

- **Part I** — *Tail Quadrature, Exact Dyadic Error Laws, …*
  (formerly `Fabius_Rvachev_Frontier_Report/`);
- **Part II** — *Arithmetic Spectra of the Rvachev Sinc Product*
  (formerly `Fabius_Rvachev_Frontier_Report-2/`);
- **Part III** — *Zero-Divisor-Preserving q-Richardson Extrapolation
  for the Fabius–Rvachev Sinc Product*
  (formerly `Fabius_Rvachev_Frontier_Report-3/`);
- **Part IV** — *Midpoint Transmutation, Dyadic Cardinal
  Reproduction, …* (formerly `Fabius_Rvachev_Frontier_Report_2026-08-27/`);
- **Part V** — *Gamma Duality, Total Positivity, and …*
  (formerly `Fabius_Rvachev_New_Frontiers/`);
- **Part VI** — *Confluent Digital Extrapolation and …*
  (formerly `fabius_frontier_report_bundle/`);
- **Part VII** — *Dyadic Spectral Determinants for the Fabius–Rvachev
  System* (formerly `fabius_frontier_results_bundle/`);
- **Part VIII** — *Logarithmic q-Richardson Acceleration and Lambert
  Phase Locking in Fabius–Rvachev Analysis*
  (formerly `fabius_frontier_new_results/`);
- **Part IX** — *Binary Spectral–Endpoint Bridges for the Fabius and
  Rvachev Functions*
  (formerly `fabius_frontier_spectral_endpoint_report_bundle/`);
- **Part X** — *Beyond the Dyadic Fabius Web*
  (formerly `beyond_dyadic_fabius_web_report/`).

The member drafts were absorbed verbatim (labels, citation keys, and
asset paths mechanically prefixed per part; no mathematical content
altered) and their directories deleted; provenance with SHA-256 hashes
is recorded in the volume itself, and git history is the archive.
