# Frontier compilations

Broad multi-topic "collected new results" reports on the Fabius/Rvachev
system: each gathers a batch of new identities, asymptotics, and programs
across several themes rather than developing one. The numbered
`Fabius_Rvachev_Frontier_Report*` directories form a series and are kept
together even where an individual title leans toward another group.

`beyond_dyadic_fabius_web_report`.

see the placement rule in [`../../README.md`](../../README.md). Drafts are
temporary inboxes: absorbed material is deleted, and
[`../MANIFEST.md`](../MANIFEST.md) records the mapping from the pre-2026-08-28
flat layout.
