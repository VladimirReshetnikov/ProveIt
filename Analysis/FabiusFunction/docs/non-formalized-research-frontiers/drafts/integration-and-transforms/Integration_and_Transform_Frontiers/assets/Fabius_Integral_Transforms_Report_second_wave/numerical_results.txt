7. Integrability contrast (interpretive numerical note)
Negative moments E[X^{-s}] are finite for every fixed s>0, whereas
the Lebesgue integral of F(x)^{-s} over x in (0,1) diverges.  The report
proves this contrast from the quadratic endpoint logarithm.
