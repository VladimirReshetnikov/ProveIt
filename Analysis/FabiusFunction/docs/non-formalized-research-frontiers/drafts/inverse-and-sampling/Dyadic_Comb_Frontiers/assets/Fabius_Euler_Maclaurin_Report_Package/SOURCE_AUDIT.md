# Source audit and nonduplication notes

The report was prepared against the current public documentation tree at:

- `Analysis/FabiusFunction/docs/`
- the canonical `Fabius_Function_and_Rvachev_Up` exposition;
- the consolidated `non-formalized-research-frontiers` volume and its draft
  manifest/provenance structure;
- the integration-and-transform and inverse-and-sampling syntheses used to
  identify existing weighted-integral, moment, alias, and shifted-sampling
  results;
- the archive inventory, which marks frozen historical versions and explains
  when older `.tex`/`.pdf` copies are redundant or superseded.

The external summation sources were:

1. V. Reshetnikov, “An integration-via-summation formula,” Mathematics Stack
   Exchange, question 3265474.
2. A. A. Ruffa, *The Generalized Method of Exhaustion*, arXiv:math/0011078.
3. NIST DLMF §2.10, the Euler--Maclaurin material.
4. Wikipedia, “Euler--Maclaurin formula.”
5. E. W. Weisstein, “Euler--Maclaurin Integration Formulas,” MathWorld.

The report's nonduplication boundary is stated in Section 1. In particular,
existing exact moment formulas and dyadic Rvachev self-sampling are cited as
prior art. The additive results are the Bernoulli-periodized `P(x)F(x)` rules,
2-adic finite termination, reflection-reduced sharp thresholds, phase-selected
extra degree, exact first defects, closed Ruffa tails, rational annihilator
extrapolation, and the arbitrary-integer-mesh extension of the Rvachev rule.

Archived predecessors were used as provenance and coverage checks rather than
counted as independent current results when the repository's own manifest
identifies them as absorbed, byte-identical, or superseded.
