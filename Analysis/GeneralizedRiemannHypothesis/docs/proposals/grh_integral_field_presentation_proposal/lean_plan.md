# Lean migration plan (not a compiled patch)

Use the pinned readable project and its own `lean-toolchain`/manifest. Do not change the final theorem or the parameters. All lemma names proposed below are schematic names, not claims about existing Lean identifiers. Preserve the actual signatures from the pinned files.

## Stage 0: establish baseline

Record `git rev-parse HEAD`, the Lean and Mathlib revisions, and the results of the existing documented build/check commands. Inventory the original declarations and their callers in `PrimeDecomposition`, `ChosenPlaces`, `TightFrames`, `ThetaProducts`, `MarkOperator`, and `MarkedPrimes`. Measure both line count and the number of reachable declarations before refactoring. A simple `wc -l` of the changed files is not enough if helper modules grow.

## Stage 1: integral order and trace dual

1. Use the existing `K`, `E`, `ζ`, `β_K` and cyclotomic irreducibility facts. Construct the product basis from the two monogenic integral bases, and identify its Z-span with the subalgebra generated by the two elements. Prove the two scaled-integrality assertions from trace-dual bases and the adjugate of the trace matrix. Apply Bezout to the discriminants. Preserve `relDifferent_spec` and the original integral-generator conclusions. Do not introduce a second incompatible model of `E`.
2. For a monogenic basis with monic separable minimal polynomial `f`, prove the trace identity `Tr(α^k / f'(α)) = if k = h-1 then 1 else 0` for `k < h`, using interpolation in a splitting field. For larger `k`, use monic polynomial reduction. Check whether the pinned Mathlib already exposes the needed monogenic different theorem; using it instead of formalizing interpolation again may be shorter. Obtain unimodularity by reversing one matrix's rows or columns, then use inverse matrix integrality.
3. Deduce the same relative different and absolute different ideals, and keep the existing divisibility of `relDifferentGen` by the conductor. Use the relative trace-dual corollary in the tight-frame trace-integrality wrapper. If the existing wrapper is shorter as code, keep it; mathematical simplification does not require every local rewrite.

## Stage 2: phase

Show `Φ_n` is reciprocal, and prove the derivative identity at a primitive root via the differentiated reciprocal identity (or direct coefficient reversal, if the Laurent-polynomial route is cumbersome). Use the witness `ζ ^ ((n/4 + 1 - φ(n)/2) mod n)` with integer arithmetic for the exponent before taking a natural representative. The witness has norm one at every embedding and makes the derivative real under conjugation. Prove the existing phase existential lemma using this witness, rather than changing the downstream choice immediately. Check that `differentGenPhase` users need only the specification, not the actual chosen value. Rebuild `ChosenPlaces` and the vacuum files before any definitional change.

## Stage 3: geometric orbits (small version first)

Prove a reusable finite-field evaluation lemma: for two F_p-algebra maps from a ring to an algebraic closure, with finite images, equal kernels iff one is a Frobenius iterate of the other. Apply to `O_E` and its maps on `ζ`, `β`. Recover `smul_eq_iff_exists_frobElem_pow`, its self/star variants, and `q_eq_pow_orderOf_frobElem`. Keep the source pullback action convention. Avoid constructing a new full tensor-product library if this direct route is smaller. For the original unramified valuation lemma, the existing different/conductor proof remains a valid inexpensive choice.

Next derive `not_dvd_q₁_of_star_eq`, `natCast_eq_neg_one_of_star_eq`, and the inert-prime residue-degree wrappers from the general orbit statement. Treat `p=d`, `p|q₁`, and `p=2` explicitly. If useful, formalize the full residue-algebra presentation and local-length theorem separately; it is not necessary for first-stage deletion. Preserve transitivity and any other original facts with independent consumers. Do not delete general decomposition theory in `Lib` without a dependency audit.

## Stage 4: full-ring congruences and consumers

Retain `forall_map_galOfPlaceInt_eq_pow_iff` as a separate arbitrary-ring interface. In odd characteristic, use `(2β-1)^(p^j) = χ^j(2β-1)` with the appropriate embedding and characteristic. At `p=d`, the nilpotent is not zero modulo `p`, so never cancel it. Modulo the uniformizer it vanishes and the required comparison holds. Preserve `natCast_dvd_pow_sub_galOfPlaceInt_star` with its inert and odd hypotheses. Rebuild `ThetaProducts`, `MarkOperator`, `MarkedPrimes` and all their consumers. A proof of an equality of maximal ideals alone is not an acceptable replacement for the ring congruence used by `norm_pow_sub_conj_partner_le`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Original theorem statements and final RH/Dirichlet-GRH statement unchanged; no new hypotheses, axioms, `sorry`, or `admit`.
- Source's documented full check passes on the original and refactored versions, including the axiom audit of the top-level theorem. A finite Python test is not a Lean check.
- All candidate declarations that remain used are proved, or their callers are updated with a formally proved equivalent interface.
- Record net lines and reachable declarations, including helper lemmas, and build time if measurable. In the final comparison, distinguish comments, proof terms, generated code and dependencies. Keep only stages which improve the proof's overall clarity or size.

If the orbit stage introduces more supporting machinery than it removes, retain stages 1–2 alone. Those stages are logically independent of the finite-field orbit implementation.
