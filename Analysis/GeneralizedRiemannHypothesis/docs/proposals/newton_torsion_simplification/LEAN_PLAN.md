## 2. Suggested new lemmas (names are proposed)

* `primitiveExponents_prefix_diameter`: for the first `k+1` integers in `[1, ℓ^j-1]` coprime to ℓ, the difference of maximum and minimum is ≤ `2*k`. Use the number of multiples in the initial interval. For `k=0` return zero.
* `primitiveExponents_prefix_residue_count`: for a selected index `i` and `r ≥ 1`, the number of other prefix entries congruent modulo `ℓ^r` is at most `2*k / ℓ^r` as a rational inequality. Count points to the left and right separately. No probability or topology required.
* `prefix_dividedDifference_denom_le`: using `ellVal_sub_one_eq_rootRadius` and the exact order of the quotient of two primitive roots, expand `ℓ^v` as `1 + Σ_{r=1}^v (ℓ^r-ℓ^(r-1))`. The result is `v(∏(a_i-a_h)) ≤ 2*j*k*t_j`. Exponents are finite naturals; take `j≥1` and handle the singleton shell explicitly. Avoid subtracting infinities.
* `newtonMonomial_expand`: for a sequence `a : ℕ → intQpBar ℓ` with all `v(a k)=t`, set `N k=∏_{h<k}(X-C(a h))`. Construct coefficients for `X^d` recursively using `XN_k=N_{k+1}+a_k N_k`. Prove they vanish for `k>d` and have valuation ≥ `(d-k)*t`. This works even if nodes repeat beyond the first `K_j` entries.
* `mvNewton_expand_weighted`: use finite support of `MvPolynomial` and products of these one-variable expansions. Define a finite family indexed by exponent vectors `σ →₀ ℕ`; all coefficients outside the componentwise downward closure of support are zero. For every coefficient `C_α` establish the weighted lower bound by the minimum over monomials `ν≥α`. Conversely every Newton product has Gauss valuation `t*|α|`. Prove equality of the two minima and existence of a nonzero minimizing Newton coefficient. A minimizing monomial `ν≥α` exists by finiteness and the first bound. Use the existing univariate Gauss infrastructure where possible; a direct finite support definition suffices for the multivariate version.
* `newton_prefix_functional`: prove the one-variable Lagrange leading-coefficient identity over the fraction field on distinct nodes. For `N_k` with `k` less/equal/greater than `α` its value is `0/1/0`; the last case is vanishing at all used nodes. This avoids a bound on the degree of the original polynomial. Tensor by finite products and sums. If using an existing Mathlib interpolation lemma, check its exact interface in the pinned version; no name is asserted here.
* `minimal_newton_degree_le_drop`: a monomial minimizing at `t_j` bounds its own weight at `t_{j+1}` from above. Therefore `c_ℓ*t_j*|α| ≤ Δ_j`; if `Δ_j<c_ℓ` all components of `α` are below `K_j`. First prove the finite-valued inequality, then transfer to the valuation type.
* `exists_primitive_shell_eval_le`: choose that minimizing Newton coefficient, use the divided difference and the ultrametric bound on a finite sum; there is an evaluation with `v(P(z)) ≤ w_t(P)+2*j*Δ_j/c_ℓ`. For a constant or zero variables the argument is the empty product. Nonzero polynomial is required; in the application this follows from the finite reference evaluations.
* `exists_common_primitive_order_eval_le`: set `η=min ε 1`, form the finite product of all tests. The consecutive nonnegative Gauss drops sum to at most `B`. Choose `H` with `Σ_{j=d₀}^H 1/j > 2*B/(c_ℓ*η)`. This is an ordinary harmonic divergence statement; the explicit real exponential ceiling in the paper can be proved afterward, and is not necessary to reproduce the old existential statement. The source's existing harmonic lemma uses `(h+1)⁻¹`, which is smaller than `h⁻¹` for `h≥1`; use it directly with a suitable constant and sufficient bound. At the selected shell all evaluations are above their individual Gauss weights. Product valuation gives the sum of their nonnegative excesses; hence each reference bound follows. Handle an empty family by the constant product `1`.
