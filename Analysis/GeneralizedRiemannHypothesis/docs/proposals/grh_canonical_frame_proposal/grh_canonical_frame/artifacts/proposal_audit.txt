# GRH proposal exclusion audit

## Snapshot and scope

Read-only audit of `VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt` at commit
`f2d6ab6c4b70ea73b2deac9e33ad966bc92ea36f`, specifically
`Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis/docs/proposals`.

The pinned corpus has **28 directories, 43 TeX files, 11,944 TeX source lines,
and 136 `.tex`/`.md`/`.txt` files**. A byte-hash inventory is in
`proposal_text_manifest.json`. This is a mechanism/target audit based on the
README and mathematical structure of every package, its relevant integration
plan, and targeted mathematical/future-direction passages. It is not a
line-by-line verification of all 11,944 TeX lines, nor a global priority search.

All target source paths below are relative to
`Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis/Lean/grh/src/`. File lists identify the
principal targets rather than every retained dependency.

## Strong exclusions relevant to a new endpoint proposal

The following are already covered and should not be presented as new:

1. Using only existence rather than a full density theorem for good quadratic twists.
2. Selecting a twist by averaging Gaussian tails or by two short Dirichlet polynomials.
3. Replacing complex transform computation by real Laplace samples and continuation.
4. Replacing Landau with Pringsheim/nearest-pole reasoning and finite differences.
5. Weakening a zero-free rectangle to noncancellation at the hypothesized zero plus an appropriate positive real sample. `grh_real_samples_and_nearest_poles` has a specific targeted-twist criterion; it does **not** prove a cheaper arithmetic existence theorem for that criterion.
6. Sparse fourth-power/dyadic cutoffs or reciprocal-window averaged estimates.
7. Direct Perron at the far cutoff; fixed-modulus PNT absorption of far errors.
8. A different rank recurrence, centered covariance, ramified-source selection, or adaptive completion alone.

## Parameter-construction overlap and openings

The current corpus has no fully worked replacement of the complete admissible-data
hierarchy or the general eventual inequality construction. However:

* `theta-lift-simplifications/paraboloid.tex` already replaces the sparse-place
  progression-free construction in `Data/Existence.lean` with an explicit
  digit/paraboloid code. It carries `n >= 3` and `n - 2 <= 2 ell_0` through the
  rank-dependent and eventual construction. Do not propose that same code.
* `rh_sparse_theta_proof`, section “What could be simplified further?”, proposes
  **fixed-length block construction**: hold `m` and the cyclotomic exponent fixed
  on a dyadic block and seek uniform parameters for its `O(sqrt m)` sampled
  cutoffs. This is future work, not a proved uniform-family replacement.
* `grh_diagonal_sweep_proposal`, further-research item 4, suggests combining
  exact class/endpoint counts to reduce auxiliary-form exponents and simplify
  the admissible-data tower. It does not give an audited new parameter hierarchy.
* The centered proposal suggests a single optimized rank–height budget and
  weaker orbital estimates at the actual tolerated error. These are unresolved
  directions, not substitutes already proved in the corpus.

A new result in this area should specify a genuinely new uniform mechanism,
prove every affected source constraint, and explain why it is more than the
already suggested “use the better constants to optimize the parameters.”

## Other uncovered areas that warrant investigation

These are candidate territories, not established simplifications:

* The higher hyperbolic-plane descent step has no worked replacement here.
  The false-lift proposal explicitly lists extending support annihilation to
  that step as future work, so a new report should do substantive new work.
* `New/Spanning/Frames/RealPlaceGauge.lean` (1,337 current lines), the Bessel
  matrix-coefficient layer, and real-place remainder estimates are not main
  targets of these packages. Gram-adjoint/branching proposals touch adjacent
  finite-dimensional representation theory, so overlap must be checked at
  the actual proposed identity.
* `New/Congruences/Lifting/` (cup products, cut exponents, low jumps) is largely
  untouched. The three Ribet/Fitting packages simplify first-order relations,
  not the higher lifting/exponent analysis.
* The large cohomology/period/comparison libraries have no dedicated replacement
  in this corpus; whether a smaller theorem suffices is a dependency question,
  not evidence that those theories can simply be deleted.
