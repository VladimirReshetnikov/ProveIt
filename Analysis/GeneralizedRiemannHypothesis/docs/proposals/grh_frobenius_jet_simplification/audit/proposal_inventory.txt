## Explicitly proposed future directions

These are *not* established replacement proofs in the existing reports. The user asks not to repeat proposals; merely restating one of these directions would still overlap. A genuinely proved solution may be a substantive extension, but should say which prior question it answers.

### Spanning, completion and global determinant

The centered-moment note already proposes:

1. A single continuously varying rank–height budget, balancing the marginal cost of one extra translate against one missing dimension.
2. Weaker orbital estimates made possible by the exact tolerance formula for moment error.
3. Charging local index by newly acquired rank rather than full translate dimension; it warns the current uniform colength estimate alone does not imply this.
4. An exterior-power chain determinant estimate that could eliminate completion; it warns that an unsigned height inequality does not recover the required main term.

The signed-gain note already proposes:

1. Replace the (N)-level cost (mfb) by the actual rank-weighted cost of nontrivial elementary divisors.
2. Jointly optimize gauges and colength through exterior powers.
3. Establish arithmetic estimates only for the twist pair used after choosing the regular family, rather than universal integrality.
4. Track individual lower-height losses more sharply.

### Local upper bounds

The discrete-Hecke note asks whether one can:

1. Work at finite packing scale (M=1) instead of asymptotic Frobenius power packing.
2. Use an Ehrhart quasipolynomial identity to remove even its elementary limit.
3. Propagate the full Smith-factor profile instead of only the exceptional count.
4. Use root-by-root shifts for a multi-block variant.

The edge-Hecke note asks for a direct perfect pairing on the integral small-defect space, bypassing invariant averaging; the residue dimension estimate alone is explicitly insufficient. It also mentions tighter multinomial carry constants and avoiding the power-packing/volume argument (the latter is now partly addressed by the discrete-Hecke note).

### Local labels and places

The robust-label note explicitly asks for a direct scalar-unit conclusion instead of an intermediate divisibility statement; general ranks beyond powers of three; simultaneous reduction of field towers and sparse-place costs; and exploitation of exact Green weights to relax the all-allowed condition.

The theta-lift local note asks for summing individual local determinant losses before their worst case, and for counting additional robust labels whose exponent is divisible by 3.

### Integral adjoints

The Gram-adjoint note already suggests exact paired bounds at individual embeddings, extension of rational duality to auxiliary-form completion with nontrivial stabilizers, and reuse for other Weyl/Schur modules.

## Important audit cautions

- Several existing notes overlap each other. Their claims of “distinct from all existing proposals” concern older snapshots with only 5–14 folders. The current exclusion task must use the union above.
- Count gains, smaller constants or numerical parameter relaxations do not by themselves demonstrate a shorter full Lean proof.
- Source files mentioned as candidates for deletion are affected regions, not verified net savings. Their helper declarations can have unrelated callers.
- The user's “likely implemented” instruction means a new proposal should survive composition with these changes. Re-deriving the same public interface by another elementary calculation is not automatically a significant new simplification.
- A new proof should identify an untouched mechanism, give its replacement theorem, prove the missing bridge and inspect all downstream consumers. In particular, preserving the common main term in the determinant comparison is essential.
