\section{What is being simplified}\label{sec:scope}

The central recommendation is to remove two intermediate constructions while retaining
the contracts they discharge. The first replaces recovery through generalized eigenspaces
of an off-place operator by one finite polynomial trace identity. The second retains the
Gram matrix of a negative frame instead of first normalizing that frame and tracking the
normalizing triangular matrix.

\subsection{Location in the supplied argument}

The source endpoints are explicit. \src{Statements}{Statements} defines the zero and
nonvanishing statements using Mathlib's Dirichlet \(L\)-functions.
\src{Main}{Main} obtains its five public results from \code{New.grh} and the
functional-equation reductions in \src{New/Main}{New.Main}.
Here the GRH endpoint concerns Dirichlet characters.

The source reading guide organizes the arithmetic proof into three estimates:
short forms, enough forms, and a lower bound saying that integral forms are long.
Their comparison is \src{New/Reduction/MainInequality}{star\_inequality}.
At the level relevant here it has the shape
\[
 H-E_{\mathrm{low}}
 \ \leq\ \text{corrected logarithmic covolume}
 \ \leq\ H-\frac{n}{d}\,mR+E_{\mathrm{up}}.
\]
The leading term \(H\) cancels. Since \(n/d\) is bounded below and
\(m\) is of square-root size in the observation scale, this yields the one-sided
prime-remainder bound used in the analytic reduction. This report does not change
that cancellation, the arithmetic divisibility estimates, or the analytic endpoint.

\subsection{Significance and the limits of the size claim}

At this pin the source tree has 803 Lean files and 476,849 physical lines,
including comments and blank lines. All 803 files lie in the static import closure
of \code{Main}; this is not a declaration-level dependency analysis.
An unused-declaration sweep and a mathematical replacement are different kinds of saving.

The new trace theorem strengthens an infinite, two-index premise to a finite,
one-index premise with the off-place operator occurring only to the first power.
It therefore simplifies both the local linear-algebra contract and the test functions
sent to the geometric trace comparison.

The new integral argument targets a substantial local construction in a 1,030-line
module: negative-basis reconstruction, triangular normalization, determinant bookkeeping,
and the normalized last-column estimates. Its surrounding output inequality is preserved.
The audit identifies the affected declarations precisely; it does not count the entire
spanning argument as removable.

\keyresult{\textbf{No measured net line saving is claimed.}
These are proved mathematical replacements with concrete migration plans.
An integrated Lean implementation is needed before reporting a net line count or build-time
reduction. In particular, the 7,438-line trace-formula subtree and the 1,003-line
\code{SchurOrbit} module are not deletion claims.}

\subsection{Verification scope}

This is a targeted source review and a proof of the replacement mathematics.
The supplied guide was used to locate the proof's contracts, and the Lean statements
and their callers were inspected directly. A standalone Lean support development
was checked with the source's pinned Lean and Mathlib. The full GRH project was not
rebuilt in this session, and the report does not present a new independent certification
of its public theorems. Section~\ref{sec:validation} distinguishes the checked support,
the human proofs, and the remaining source integration.
