\vspace{0.25in}
{\Large\color{muted}Finite trace recovery and a direct real-place determinant integral\par}
\vspace{0.3in}
\textcolor{accent}{\rule{\linewidth}{1pt}}\par
\vspace{0.15in}
\textbf{Recommendation.}
First replace the normalized negative-basis calculation in the real-place majorant by an
unnormalized Gram matrix and a bordered determinant integral. A second, independent
replacement recovers the auxiliary-place trace from finitely many one-index moments.
Both preserve the mathematical output needed by the surrounding proof.

\textbf{Distinctness.}
The mechanisms were compared with all 28 proposal bundles at the source pin and three
later additions: 31 distinct bundles across the two snapshots. The closest existing
proposals act on different source contracts. No historical priority claim is made.

\textbf{Evidence.}
The report supplies full mathematical proofs, exact source interfaces, an exclusion audit,
reproducible exact-arithmetic checks, and separately identified Lean support files.
The proposed replacements have not been integrated into a rebuilt GRH package.
