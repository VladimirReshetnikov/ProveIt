# Formalization of the GRH article

Build these Lean results from the repository root with `lake build GRHArticle`.
The priorities come from [remarkable-results.tex](../Article/remarkable-results.tex).
Each entry below identifies the part of the article covered by the Lean statements.

## Singular moduli

| Article statement | Lean development | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Support upper bound and independence of powers in `smd:eq:trivial` and `smd:thm:five` | [Support.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/Support.lean) | Defines the minimum over all nonzero annihilators with no bound on their degrees. Proves the upper bound, the exact values for zero and nonzero degree-one elements, and the equivalence between support bounds and independence of finite sets of powers. |
| Extension of the coefficient field in `smd:prop:cmfield` | [BaseChange.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/BaseChange.lean) | Proves invariance when the new coefficient field is linearly disjoint from the field generated by the element. Also states the upper bound using the degree of that generated field. The CM application still needs linear disjointness from the ring class field theorem. |
| Separated conjugate sizes in `smd:lem:separated` | [Separated.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/Separated.lean) | Proves the determinant and polynomial root assertions. Gives the maximum-support criterion when all conjugate images can be chosen with this separation. |
| Factorial depth thresholds in `smd:cor:threshold` and the trinomial disk cutoff | [FactorialThreshold.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/FactorialThreshold.lean), [SevenAdicCutoff.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/SevenAdicCutoff.lean) | Proves the interval bounds for `p ≥ t` and for `p = 2, 3` with at most five terms. Proves the exact cutoff `B₇(3, 1/2) = 2`. |
| Coefficients and forward substitution in `smd:lem:disk` | [Moments.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/Moments.lean), [PadicConvergence.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/PadicConvergence.lean), [PadicExpSubstitution.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/PadicExpSubstitution.lean) | Proves initial moment nonvanishing, the monic integer recurrence, coefficient convergence on the closed unit ball, and the identity after forward p-adic exponential substitution. |
| Strassmann root bound in `smd:lem:disk` | [Strassmann.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/Strassmann.lean) | Proves the distinct-root bound for a convergent coefficient series over a complete ultrametric normed field, including the form with an explicit cutoff. |
| Exact coefficient cutoff in `smd:lem:disk` | [PadicCutoff.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/PadicCutoff.lean) | Translates strict factorial weight comparisons into coefficient norm comparisons. Applies Strassmann to count roots of the resulting exponential series on the unit ball. |
| Local root count `smd:lem:disk` | [PadicDiskRoots.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/PadicDiskRoots.lean) | Proves the bound over the p-adic complex numbers for any prime and rational depth above the exponential threshold. Identifies the bound with the greatest admissible factorial weight index. Includes the polynomial-support formulation and the two-root bound for trinomials at depth one-half and prime seven. |
| Field extensions in `smd:lem:disk` | [DiskRootTransfer.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/DiskRootTransfer.lean) | Proves the same root count in any algebraic normed extension of the p-adic rationals, including finite extensions and the algebraic closure. The proof uses the spectral norm to show that embeddings preserve the disk. |
| Depth thresholds and determinants in `smd:cor:threshold` | [ThresholdDiskRoots.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/ThresholdDiskRoots.lean) | Proves the open-disk root count, including all three numerical threshold families. At the stated depth, any generalized Vandermonde matrix on the prescribed distinct points and exponents is nonsingular. |
| Conjugate obstruction used in `smd:rem:riffaut` | [ConjugateDisks.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/ConjugateDisks.lean) | More than the factorial cutoff number of distinct conjugate images in a disk excludes all annihilators with the given support bound. In particular, three conjugates in a depth-half disk at seven exclude every rational trinomial. |
| Hasse coefficient calculations in `smd:lem:reduction(iii–iv)` | [HasseCoefficients.lean](GRHArticle/SingularModuli/HasseCoefficients.lean) | For nonsingular short Weierstrass models, proves the Hasse coefficient vanishes exactly at j = 6 in characteristic 7, j = 5 in characteristic 13, and j = 18 or 7 in characteristic 19. At j = 0 it is nonzero for every prime congruent to one modulo three. The identification with geometric supersingularity still requires the Hasse criterion. |

The five-term classification still needs the CM reduction, bounds on the conjugates
to place them in the disks, and finite certificates from the
[singular-moduli section](../Article/sections/smd.tex).

## Integer geometric progressions

[Rankin.lean](GRHArticle/ProgressionFree/Rankin.lean) proves that Rankin's
valuation rule excludes all nonconstant three-term geometric progressions
with rational ratios. It uses the equivalence between `a * c = b * b` and the
arithmetic midpoint relation at every prime exponent. It also proves
progression-freeness for the positive-integer instance of the one-digit
exchange in `gpi:thm:onedigit`. The same file proves the exact finite
reciprocal-mass difference and uses divergence of prime reciprocals to find
a finite set of odd-prime satellites giving a strict gain with marker two.
[Density.lean](GRHArticle/ProgressionFree/Density.lean) proves the exact natural density for fixed prime valuations, finite correlated families of valuation tuples, and finite independent exponent rules. [RankinTruncation.lean](GRHArticle/ProgressionFree/RankinTruncation.lean) specializes this formula to finitely many Rankin primes and exponents, and bounds which integers the truncation can misclassify.
The limiting density comparison and the specific 225-prime exchange remain open in Lean. [Article section](../Article/sections/gpi.tex)

## RH and Dirichlet GRH input

The article uses the five theorems in
[the vendored Main.lean](../vendor/grh-lean/grh/src/Main.lean).
The vendor pins Lean 4.35.0 and Mathlib revision `56a005855e`.
Andrew Sutherland reports kernel replay, axiom checks, and statement comparisons for that
exact vendored revision. [Independent verification](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/shortgrh/)

The `GRHArticle` target uses the repository's Lean 4.32.0 and Mathlib pin.
The singular-moduli results above are unconditional. The build connection for
future Dirichlet GRH applications remains open.
