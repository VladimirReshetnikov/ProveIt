\section{Which open directions are now most credible?}

The following ordering reflects the exact interfaces inspected and the
work already present in the incoming reports. It is a research judgment,
not a prediction that any target will be solved by simply importing a
Lean declaration.

\subsection{A nonabelian test case for the arithmetic mechanism}

The splitting field of $X^3-4X+1$, whose discriminant is $229$, is a
useful controlled case already developed in the prior nonabelian report
\cite{priornonabelian}. The extension over its quadratic subfield is
cyclic and unramified. The earlier work supplies concrete integral frame
information and explains why adjoining this field to an appropriate
cyclotomic field need not create a new root-discriminant cost.

The next step should be an actual nonabelian local-divisibility identity
for the intended prime weight. It must come with uniform estimates as the
cyclotomic modulus and dimensions grow. The fact that a suitable frame
exists for every fixed lattice does not give a threshold small enough for
the source's hierarchy of scales. Likewise, replacing the source's
cyclotomic odd-cocycle estimate by an asserted analogue would assume an
essential part of the new theorem.

The improved spanning interface contributes a quantitative allowance:
if the analogue of the raw near-isometry and second-moment estimates has
errors $u$ and $v$, then its unavoidable abstract deficit is
$(2u+v-u^2)/(1+v)$, with an explicit finite cost. One can now compare a
proposed arithmetic construction against that formula without enlarging
both errors to one convenient parameter. The arithmetic construction
still has to be supplied.

\subsection{The uniform symmetric-group comparison}

The previous reflection report asks for a positive rational monomial
decomposition of
\[
 U_n=(n-1)\operatorname{Ind}_{C_2}^{S_n}\sigma
       -\operatorname{Reg}_{S_n}+1
\]
in every degree. It supplies certificates only for $3\le n\le12$
\cite{priorreflection}. Such a decomposition would prove its
optimal multiplicity comparison uniformly, without assuming that all
individual Artin factors are holomorphic.

The identity in the present report verifies a particularly simple
degree-five instance of the stronger auxiliary problem
\[
 V_n=\operatorname{Ind}_{C_2}^{S_n}\sigma-\mathrm{std}_n
 \quad\text{belongs to the positive monomial cone}.
\]
The recurrence $U_n=\operatorname{Ind}_{S_{n-1}}^{S_n}U_{n-1}+V_n$
would turn a uniform solution for $V_n$ into a uniform solution for $U_n$.
We did not obtain that uniform decomposition. The $S_5$ certificate is
an exact base case and a useful factorization, not evidence that an
induction step can be omitted.

The obstacle is meaningful. A genuine representation need not be a
positive rational sum of representations induced from linear subgroup
characters. The earlier report already supplies a counterexample to a
general graph-homology argument. The special structure of the symmetric
group, its subgroup characters, or its adjacent-transposition graph must
do additional work.

\subsection{Non-CM Selmer data and automorphic extensions}

The source's congruence estimates involve more than a familiar Selmer
group written abstractly. They impose local conditions, labels, weight
requirements, and a precise raw-cocycle construction. The recent
arithmetic report derives exact global Hom terms in specified non-CM
examples but correctly leaves the full datum unfinished
\cite{priorarithmetic}.

A valuable next result would construct one complete such datum and
check every clause, including the primes at which the residual or
crystalline hypotheses change. A global trace-discrepancy calculation
alone does not provide this. The current spanning improvement is useful
only after the new local construction reaches that interface.

The same caution applies to Selberg's eigenvalue problem, the generalized
Ramanujan conjecture, and zero statistics. The existence of spectral,
theta, and unitary-group material inside a Dirichlet proof does not
identify the operator or positive kernel required for those questions.
No direct theorem proving one of those targets was found in the inspected
source. They remain less concrete transfers than a specified degree-two
nonabelian $L$-function with an identified prime statistic.

\subsection{Ingredient-level problems with a shorter path}

The inspected collection still leaves focused questions about cycle
signings outside the $8m$ family, quantitative projective frames,
Lubin--Tate coefficient-depth asymptotics, and rational-ratio
progression-free sets. These have exact finite interfaces and can be
attacked without assuming another Riemann hypothesis
\cite{prioradvance,priorideal,priormcnew}.

The strongest connection to this report is quantitative spanning:
it gives an explicit tradeoff among moment error, lost source dimensions,
number of translates, and retained target rank. That is the reusable
object. A future paper should identify which one of those quantities is
the actual obstruction in its target problem, rather than describe a
broad similarity to the GRH proof.

\subsection{What has and has not been resolved}

The report resolves the quantitative question of what the specified
source moment estimates guarantee: the exact limiting deficit and the
stronger finite-budget source theorem are proved. It also proves the
explicit $S_5$ zeta-comparison statements via a three-term monomial
decomposition. These results go beyond applying the RH/GRH endpoints as
black boxes.

It does not resolve the general $S_n$ positive-cone problem, Artin
holomorphy, nonabelian GRH, Selberg's conjecture, Ramanujan's conjecture,
or zero simplicity and pair correlation. For those targets, the report
identifies the missing mathematical input rather than treating a
conditional reduction as its proof.
