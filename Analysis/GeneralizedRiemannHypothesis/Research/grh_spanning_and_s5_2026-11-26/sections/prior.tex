\subsection{Two corrections that matter for choosing the next task}

First, the claim that the source only supplies a finite-level Selmer
estimate is obsolete for the readable development. The latest report
identifies its saturation argument and strict scalar descent. A useful
extension must address the actual raw-cocycle conditions and quantitative
bound, not repeat that earlier objection.

Second, many natural-looking finite detectors are already in the corpus,
including reflection, dihedral, Sylow, and projective-linear examples.
The uniform symmetric-group problem remains a well-defined algebraic
frontier, but merely observing that its candidate is a genuine character
does not put it in the positive cone of monomial characters. We retain
that distinction in the $S_5$ section.

The searches found no earlier statement of the separate-error square-root
spanning profile or the $b^{-4}$ source upgrade established below.
This is evidence of an addition to the inspected collection, not an
exhaustive literature novelty theorem. The underlying rank principle is
closely related to the classical Welch bound \cite{welch,mixon}.
