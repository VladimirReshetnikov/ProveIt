OVERALL CONCLUSION
The corpus already contains a very large number of correct-looking conditional analytic detectors and ingredient-level arithmetic/combinatorial theorems. A new one-sided Landau criterion, positive class-function detector, torsion-point specialization threshold, finite Smith-factor formula, or saturation lemma would usually repeat a previous report. The substantive unresolved route toward new nonabelian RH is the arithmetic construction that realizes nonabelian Frobenius information while retaining integral divisibility, near-full spanning, compatible twists, and determinant lower bounds. A focused new step should attack a quantitative arithmetic input or an explicitly open finite-group problem. A report can also honestly consolidate a completed prior resolution of a published problem, with attribution and fresh checking, rather than invent a new conjecture resolution.
