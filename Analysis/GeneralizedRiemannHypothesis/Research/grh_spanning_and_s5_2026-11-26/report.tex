\textbf{Question.} Which ingredients of \texttt{lsernau/grh-lean} can advance
other mathematical problems, beyond familiar consequences of RH and Dirichlet GRH?

\textbf{Main result.} Keeping the two errors already proved in the source,
rather than enlarging them, improves its spanning theorem. The weighted
translate budget decreases from $5d\log(1+2b^2)$ to $4d\log(1+2b^2)$,
while the guaranteed missing fraction decreases from $4/b^2$ to $20/b^4$.
This is a mathematical strengthening under the same admissible-data hypotheses;
it has not been compiled as a new Lean theorem.

\textbf{Second result.} An exact identity involving three subgroups of $S_5$
gives explicit Hecke factorizations and holomorphic comparison quotients,
including an entire quotient of four Dedekind zeta functions.
It is independent of RH and requires no individual Artin holomorphy conjecture.
