------------------
ana.tex already defines the regularly varying gamma-sum model, the exact
exponential saddle, the tilted local Gaussian limit, finite-dimensional
conditional Brownian-bridge laws, independent exponential slack, and
total-variation equivalence on every lower block of height h=o(u).
It also computes a macroscopic independence obstruction and the height
carrying half the mass. Those results were not presented as new here.

New contribution relative to the inspected text
-----------------------------------------------
The new observable is the largest INDIVIDUAL spectral contribution and
its moving spectral location. The proof derives an exact exceedance
center, a marked Poisson process, the Gamma(alpha/p,1) location mark,
the smaller height scale u/(log N(u))^(1/p), top-k limits, and localization
in a vanishing-mass band. Uniform singleton domination is supplied to
control the infinite tail after rare conditioning.

The source explicitly does not assert a functional bulk limit. The
present proof supplies C-tightness, including repeated-ordinate groups
and the endpoint at infinity, and proves full Brownian-bridge convergence
jointly independent of the extreme process and slack.

Additional deterministic refinement
-----------------------------------
For exponential weights, integration of a two-term counting law against
a Gaussian heat kernel gives a sharper implicit center, including
conductor dependence. The exact intensity's logarithmic slope is at most
minus one, converting the heat-trace error into a finite root enclosure.
The synthetic inverse-count diagnostics validate this formula using the
proved global counting-error bound of one.
