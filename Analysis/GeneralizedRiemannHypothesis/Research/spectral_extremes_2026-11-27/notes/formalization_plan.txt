FORMALIZATION DEPENDENCY PLAN

Status: no Lean source or kernel certificate for the new results is included.
This is an implementation plan, not a claim of completed formalization.

1. Spectral model and summability
   Define a locally finite positive multiset, its counting function, and
   regular variation with index alpha. Prove p>alpha implies summability
   of gamma_j^(-p). Construct the independent gamma product measure and X.

2. Exact exponential tilt
   Prove the Laplace product and normalized measure change. Identify all
   finite marginals under the tilt, then the infinite product law. Prove
   the saddle exists uniquely for 0<x<E[X].

3. Spectral summation
   Establish weak convergence of counting measures and dyadic tail
   bounds. Derive C_r and uniform convergence of the variance clock.

4. Fourier local limits
   Prove the characteristic-function expansion, the retained-band
   integrable envelope, Fourier inversion, and uniform singleton deletion.
   This is the main analytic infrastructure requirement.

5. Exact conditional density ratios
   Derive the block/slack likelihood; prove submacroscopic-block TV
   equivalence and uniform single-coordinate domination. Reuse these
   lemmas for both the extreme and bulk results.

6. Marked extreme process
   Define exact b by exceedance mass one; prove existence and growth;
   establish marked intensity convergence with both mark tails controlled.
   Prove independent rare-event convergence via Laplace functionals and
   transfer the result through the block/slack lemma. Formalize top-k
   extraction only after its upper/lower height and mark tightness bounds.

7. Conditional functional bridge
   Group equal ordinates. Use gamma fourth moments and the deterministic
   variance clock to prove tilted C-tightness. Transfer prefix bounds
   through bounded conditional likelihoods; use the reverse finite-tail
   maximal inequality for the endpoint. Identify the joint limit from
   the finite-dimensional normal density factorization.

8. Dirichlet interface
   Supply a fixed even completed product with central zeros removed, its
   paired Hadamard product, and zero-counting asymptotic. Import the
   appropriate Dirichlet zero-location theorem only at this final stage.

The existing article target and vendored GRH project pin different Lean
versions (4.32.0 and 4.35.0 in the inspected README). A real formalization
must choose and build an explicit common dependency graph. No import
connection or transitive kernel check is inferred from the present notes.
