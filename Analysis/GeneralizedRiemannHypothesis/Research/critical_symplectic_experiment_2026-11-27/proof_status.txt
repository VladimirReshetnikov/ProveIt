PROOF AND VALIDATION STATUS

Complete written proofs
-----------------------
The paper proves the following chain without using RH or GRH:

  Exact two-plane incidence / likelihood
        -> aggregate subspace survival comparison
        -> truncated subspace-lattice inversion
        -> total variation for the whole candidate subspace

  Tensor-rank rigidity for distinct projective coefficients
        -> factorization of mixed kernel-subspace moments
        -> even-nullity L2 polynomial approximation
        -> independent projective nonsingularity patterns

  These two chains + exact likelihood
        -> null binomial mixture and planted size bias
        -> optimal testing and recovery curves
        -> strict information gains and non-Poisson third moment
        -> expected polynomial cost of exact optimal procedures

Independent reviews within this task checked both main proof chains,
the transition from bounded statistics to size-biased distributions,
the strictness statements, the third factorial moment, and the algorithm.
Small presentation issues found in review were corrected, including
the finite-nullity support and the equality case q=2,a=1,k=1 for the
dimension likelihood. No external peer review is claimed.

The proof does NOT assume that arbitrary finite-field moments determine
a distribution. The subspace step uses an explicit inversion remainder;
the alternating-matrix step uses even-nullity L2 interpolation. These
are essential to the proof.

Exact and numerical checks
--------------------------
1. code/exact_binary_experiment.py uses exact integers and Fractions,
   verifies likelihood normalization, candidate-count means, and finite
   testing/recovery identities for all sample lengths 0 through 10.
2. code/pencil_checks.py enumerates four finite ensembles exactly; seven
   further cases are simulations and are marked as such in its JSON.
3. code/critical_profiles.py evaluates 39 critical profiles, checks
   normalization and moments, and repeats at higher precision. Its stored
   discrepancy is below 5e-88. This is not formal interval arithmetic.
4. LaTeX is compiled repeatedly; all pages are rendered and inspected.
   PDF layout and cross-references are checked before delivery.

Scope
-----
q, a, and the number of matrices in the standalone pencil theorem are
fixed as even n tends to infinity. No growing-q or growing-pencil claim
is made. The kernel step includes a computable, nonoptimized finite TV
bound; the complete experiment is stated as an asymptotic law.

The existing ProveIt source is pinned and its immediate predecessor
archive bytes are verified. This task does not independently verify all
194 merged reports or all 17 incoming archive bodies. It does not rerun
the vendored GRH Lean development. No new Lean theorem is claimed.
