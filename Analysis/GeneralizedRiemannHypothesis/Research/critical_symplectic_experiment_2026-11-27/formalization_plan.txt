FORMALIZATION PLAN (NOT A COMPILED OR COMPLETED LEAN FORMALIZATION)

The deliverable's proofs are ordinary mathematical proofs. This file gives
a possible dependency order for future Lean/Mathlib work and deliberately
contains no theorem stubs presented as verified results.

Finite algebra
--------------
1. Represent alternating forms as bilinear maps with forall v, H(v,v)=0.
   Avoid defining them by skew symmetry alone in characteristic two.
2. Prove form-space dimension binom(n,2), rank parity, radical quotient,
   the GL/Sp count of nondegenerate forms, and projective form counts.
3. Prove Gaussian-binomial subspace counts, the subspace-lattice Mobius
   formula, its partial-sum identity, and the explicit l1 remainder bound.
4. Prove the tensor-rank rigidity lemma by finite scalar extension large
   enough to avoid the finitely many hyperplanes, or via an infinite
   algebraic extension. State extension invariance of dimensions first.

Finite probability and exact identities
--------------------------------------
5. Define uniform two-planes and the mixture over nondegenerate forms.
   Define N, its dimension K, and projective nondegenerate count Z.
6. Prove the exact likelihood Z/(M p^t), uniform posterior, and optimal
   Bayes identity. Handle the event Z=0 explicitly.
7. Prove additive-character orthogonality and the alternating rank Fourier
   identity E psi(G(u,v))=q^(-rank G).
8. Establish the low-rank-span exceptional count and the aggregate
   survival discrepancy for every fixed subspace dimension.

Limits and uniform integrability
--------------------------------
9. Use truncated inversion to prove full kernel TV. Keep the order of
   limits explicit: n first at fixed cutoff, then cutoff to infinity.
10. Establish each fixed Gaussian moment and sufficient higher moments
    for weighted TV and size bias. Avoid a generic moment-determinacy
    theorem, which would not justify this argument.
11. Count direct-sum kernel configurations for mixed alternating moments;
    bound all nongeneric configurations using the tensor-rank gap.
12. Prove the even-nullity interpolation estimate uniformly in n, then
    apply Cauchy-Schwarz and mixed polynomial factorization to indicator
    products. Recover every pattern by finite inclusion-exclusion.
13. Prove the binomial mixture, planted size bias, and risk formulas using
    truncation and uniform integrability. The reference law pi_b can be
    derived from the finite rectangular matrix count included in the paper.

Algorithm
---------
14. Formalize the projective enumeration and Gaussian elimination only
    after the probabilistic statement is complete. The expected-cost
    bound requires moments up to order three; a worst-case polynomial
    bound is not asserted.

All upstream analytic GRH declarations can remain outside this dependency
graph, since the new theorems are finite-field/probability statements.
