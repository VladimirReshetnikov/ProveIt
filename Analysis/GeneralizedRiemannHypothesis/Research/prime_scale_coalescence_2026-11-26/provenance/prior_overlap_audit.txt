Title:
  Strict multiscale covariance of smoothed prime errors

The main theorem states positive definiteness of the joint long-log-time
covariance for all residue classes at arbitrary finitely many distinct
scales, even after deleting finitely many zero frequencies. Its proof
isolates translated prime atoms in a distributional explicit formula.
Its test-function proposition gives a positive covariance lower bound,
without a sharp scale-separation asymptotic. The final research questions
ask: "What is the sharp decay as two scales merge?"

Read more closely: the all-lag theorem, spectral covariance formula,
translated-prime-atom proof, prediction corollary, finite-atom certificate
and final research questions.

PROPOSED NEW CONTRIBUTION
For the zeta/native-ramp specialization, a fixed r-point cluster
h_j = epsilon u_j has one order-one covariance eigenvalue, one
order-epsilon^2 eigenvalue, and r-2 eigenvalues of order
epsilon^3 log(1/epsilon). This gives determinant and prediction-error
laws, a lower cubic-spline quadratic form and refinements under a
squared-multiplicity-density assumption. The main two-sided order law
does not require simple zeros or linearly independent ordinates.
This directly addresses the predecessor's scale-merging question for
q=1. It does not resolve the entire multicharacter matrix problem.

EXTERNAL PRIOR ART REQUIRING EXPLICIT CREDIT
- H. L. Montgomery, The pair correlation of zeros of the zeta function,
  Proc. Sympos. Pure Math. 24 (1973), 181-193.
  Equation (6) and printed pp. 191-192 yield the squared-multiplicity
  estimate under RH. This is an established theorem, not PCC.
  https://public.websites.umich.edu/hlm/paircor1.pdf
- S. Barthelme and K. Usevich, Spectral properties of kernel matrices in
  the flat limit, SIAM J. Matrix Anal. Appl. 42 (2021), 17-57.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.14067
  https://doi.org/10.1137/19M129677X
  Finite-smoothness eigenvalue groups and spline distance matrices are
  established general ideas. The paper's precise leading-term theorems
  assume an expansion/analytic dependence in the scale parameter.
- K. Usevich and S. Barthelme, Computing asymptotic eigenvectors and
  eigenvalues of perturbed symmetric matrices.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.17047
  https://doi.org/10.1137/24M1677460
  Successive Schur-complement asymptotics are established machinery.

The present proposed contribution is the arithmetic logarithmic law,
its handling of unknown multiplicities, and its application to the
specific smoothed prime signal. Neither generic kernel-matrix flat
limits nor the cubic-spline identity is claimed as newly discovered.
Targeted searches found no identical arithmetic coalescence statement.
This is not an exhaustive priority certification or peer review.
