# Comparison with ProveIt incoming research

Commit 274841c1cb137aafbb60ab5f519c51fd42eee98e contains 31 report archives under docs/incoming. We retrieved 28, verified their Git object hashes and sizes, and searched their TeX sources. The three larger archives dirichlet-grh-research.zip, grh_beyond_dirichlet_grh_2026-11-25.zip and grh_transfer_analysis.zip were inventoried from metadata. The inventory gives manuscript paths for each retrieved ZIP. Searching this group does not establish historical priority.

The closest predecessor is grh_exact_torsion_transfer_2026-11-25.zip, Theorem “Uniform exact specialization.” It proves the zero-tolerance, fixed-radius scalar estimate over unrestricted integral coefficients, with a quadratic sufficient exponent. Our unrestricted optimum answers the order-bound question and adds a whole-good-coset assertion.

grh-torsion-and-zero-data.zip and grh-torsion-and-prime-sampling.zip solve different extremal problems for positive parts of individual losses or the moving shell baseline. Their bounded-ramification shell count includes the sharp unramified specialization. The second gives the cost 1/gcd(e,phi(p^h)) directly. These fixed-field comparisons are antecedents. Quantitative twists, CM relations, prime-race methods and nonabelian zero detectors are also represented in the inventory.
