  pi*d/Q*, with a precise endpoint test and sharp zeta-beta products. The source
  theta kernels and existence mechanism are not presented as new.

  Detectors: grp:q:odd asks about odd-characteristic family detection and removing
  Artin holomorphy. The new exact optimum treats q == 1 (mod 4), including q=9;
