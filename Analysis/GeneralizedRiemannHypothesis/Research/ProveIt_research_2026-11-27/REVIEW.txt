MATHEMATICAL REVIEW AND VERIFICATION RECORD

Review was performed within this AI-assisted research session. It was not
external peer review or an independent proof-assistant kernel certification.
All four arguments were read beyond their initial author. The record below
identifies the mathematically delicate steps and the limits of computation.

1. Endpoint filters
  Reviewed: normalization of completed L-functions and central-zero removal;
  preservation of divisibility under symmetrization; Nevanlinna quotient bound;
  uniform right-half-plane Stirling lower estimates; extension of exponential
  type across a fixed horizontal strip; symmetric indicator diagram; second-
  order vanishing of the critical envelope; zero-type bounded-line principle;
  coefficient A; both theta normalizations and convolution AM-GM constants.
  Applied the clarification a > max(3/2, 2b/d) when choosing the strip.
  Exact verifier: 48 family cases and the indicator Taylor expansion.
  Five theta evaluations are floating-point illustrations only.
  No gap was found in the reviewed argument. The arbitrary-family attainability
  question and endpoint cases with A < 0 are explicitly left unresolved.

2. PSL2 detectors
  Reviewed: both character identities, fixed-space dimensions, positivity of
  induced linear-character factors, Heilbronn restriction to cyclic subgroups,
  Borel affine argument, subfield torus sums, exceptional subgroup list,
  q=9 unipotent fusion and A4 invariants, optimality obstruction, and q=3 edge
  case in the all-odd identity. Primary Dickson statement was inspected.
  Exact verifier uses integers and fractions: 94 q == 1 mod 4 field sizes,
  41636 class-value cases, 21006 irreducible cases, 183 all-odd field sizes,
  eight finite exceptional lower-bound cases, and the q=9 identity.
  No gap was found. Spectral optimality is carefully separated from analytic
  detection; the latter is established by an additional positive identity.

3. Sparse p-adic roots
  Reviewed: moment normalization over arbitrary ramified coefficient fields;
  digit-sum weight and last-minimum root count; reduction modulo p*O_K;
  all thick-residue multiplicity patterns within 2p+4 terms; quadratic/cubic
  relations and their index reduction; values of the attaining functional;
  unique minimum at p^2+p; exponential/logarithmic disk correspondence;
  rational perturbations, full support, integrality and simple roots;
  two preceding endpoints and their lower-bound functionals.
  Exact verification output retains squarefree integer witnesses for
  p=5,7,11,13,17, with root counts 30,56,132,182,306. It also retains 419+541+691
  translated residue profiles at p=5 and 21 local-algebra identity cases.
  No mathematical gap was found. The finite scans are checks, not a proof by
  extrapolation to every prime; the uniform argument is in the manuscript.

4. Mirror Hessian
  Reviewed: support-width definition and target multiplicities; strip identity;
  inherited positive residue certificate; invertible local moment Jacobian;
  continuation of the global optimizer; second variation as an integrated
  variance; Hermite congruences for the atom-velocity polynomial; the exact
  reflection kernel; local squared-distance bounds and varying-mean formula.
  The three-mode formula is checked symbolically. A 65-digit numerical solve
  checks mixed directions, repeated multiplicities, and unequal real parts.
  The numerical solves are explicitly not proof certificates. Locality, strict
  positivity, distinct labelled targets and paired multiplicities are required.
