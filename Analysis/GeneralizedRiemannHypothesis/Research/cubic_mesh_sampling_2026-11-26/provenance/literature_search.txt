Result of search
----------------
No identical total weighted cubic-gap criterion, explicit deleted-quartic
mesh with infinitely many X^(5/6) gaps, or combined optimal reconstruction
statement was located. This supports describing the result as a proved
refinement that appears new relative to the checked sources. It does not
certify publication priority.

The claim of sharpness is confined to inference from convexity/positivity
and sample values. The extremizing weights are artificial nonnegative
arithmetic weights, not von Mangoldt weights. The report makes no claim
that actual primes cannot support a sparser criterion by other methods.
