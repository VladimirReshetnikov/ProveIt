Purpose and limits
This is a focused source review supporting the mathematical report. The
repository's full proof was not rebuilt or replayed in this session. The
new sampling theorems were checked through their written arguments by
assistant agents; they have not been independently certified or formally
verified in Lean. The formalization file is explicitly an uncompiled
architecture outline.

What was directly inspected
Read-only GitHub access was used to inspect the root README, readable-edition
README and GUIDE, public Statements/Main files, the main inequality and
one-sided reduction, prime-error transform, Landau lemmas, quadratic-twist
zero-density argument, toolchain, Lake configuration, manifest and
PrintAxioms script. Exact source paths, URLs and Git blob SHAs for the fetched
code/configuration files appear in upstream_source_manifest.json. That
manifest identifies inspected upstream material; it is not a record of
local compilation or a proof certificate.

Public theorem scope
The five public theorems state:
1. Mathlib's RiemannHypothesis;
2. nontrivial zeta zeros have real part 1/2;
3. Dirichlet L-functions do not vanish to the right of 1/2, with the
   principal-character pole at s=1 excepted;
4. zeros of Dirichlet L-functions in 0<Re(s)<1 have real part 1/2;
5. the preceding assertion for primitive characters.
All positive moduli and primitive or imprimitive Dirichlet characters are
included. The repository does not claim general automorphic or Artin GRH.

Mathematical interface checked
The upstream arithmetic argument compares upper and lower bounds for a
corrected lattice covolume and obtains a one-sided smoothed prime error.
The prime-error coefficients are
  1_(n=1 mod q) Lambda(n) (1-(n/d)),
which are nonnegative.

The report's normalization E(x)=xR(x) turns this sum into nonnegative
convex ramps minus (a*x*log(x)+c*x). Here a=1/phi(q), and the entire centered
term is subtracted. If
  W(s)=a/(s-1)+b+O(s-1),
the centering constant is c=b-a. This agrees with the upstream identity
  W(s)=a*s/(s-1)+logDerivMean(s).
Direct Mellin integration gives
  M[E](s)=W(s)/(s(s-1))-a/(s-1)^2-c/(s-1).
The Laurent coefficients at s=1 cancel.

Away from s=0,1 the residue is
  (sum untwisted zero orders - sum twisted zero orders)
  /(phi(q)*s*(1-s)).
The upstream twist-avoidance theorem supplies an auxiliary prime for which
the twisted orders vanish in a prescribed right-half-plane rectangle.
That rectangle includes every real point above its threshold. These are
the exact sign and zero-multiplicity inputs used by the report.

The existing IsPolar.landau is a classical pointwise one-sided Landau
criterion. The report proves its required positive-part-integrability
version in ordinary mathematical language. The sampling refinement and
sharp convex interpolation costs are proved separately from the arithmetic
GRH construction.

The source's bad-twist count has exponent
  1-(beta-1/2)/18.
The optional sparse-auxiliary-prime corollary uses a strict growth
advantage over that bound. It does not assert an unsupported equality case.

No full upstream source is redistributed in this package. Pinned source
references and the manifest permit retrieval of the exact inspected files.
