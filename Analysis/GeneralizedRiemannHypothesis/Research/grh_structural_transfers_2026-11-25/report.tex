\begin{center}
{\color{MidnightBlue}\LARGE\bfseries Beyond Dirichlet GRH}\par
\vspace{5pt}
{\Large Self-duality, congruence costs, and the next arithmetic step}\par
\vspace{10pt}
Research memorandum prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov\par
25 November 2026 (Pacific date)
\end{center}
\vspace{5pt}

\begin{abstract}
We analyze the internal arithmetic of \texttt{lsernau/grh-lean}, beyond the standard
consequences of its RH and Dirichlet-GRH theorems, and compare all twenty-six
archives currently in \texttt{ProveIt/docs/incoming}. The main result of this
analysis is a precise transfer boundary: the Selmer engine requires a uniformly
finite conjugate-dual congruence term, which is infinite whenever a nonzero
rational conjugate Tate-dual map exists. Anticyclotomic twisting preserves this
obstruction. A Smith-normal-form theorem gives the exact divergent part and the
bounded residual part. Inspecting the mirror argument reveals a sharper,
sign-sensitive bound: its image consists of symmetric pairings, permitting a
bounded replacement for opposite-sign self-dual representations. The Weil
pairing relevant to BSD has the surviving sign, so a substantive arithmetic
kernel remains uncontrolled. We also extend the source's scalar-inertia
obstruction to every positive symmetric power of a rank-two representation,
and compute an exact Hecke-projector denominator in weight 24. Working with a
whole Galois orbit avoids that particular isolation cost. These are proved
interface and obstruction results, with exact finite certificates; they do not
prove a new nonabelian or modular RH, BSD, or a new Selmer vanishing theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Findings and status}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Findings and status}

\textbf{The source contains credible methods for further progress, but the new
arithmetic inputs cannot be obtained by changing the name of the target
$L$-function.} Its chain of theta lifts, common lattice comparison, local
twist selection, and one-sided analytic endpoint are reusable ideas. The
present analysis identifies exactly where several attractive transfers fail
and a narrower place where the source can plausibly be strengthened.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A complete criterion for the global congruence term.}
For a lattice $T$ and its conjugate Tate dual $D(T)$, write
$h=\dim_F\Hom_{F[G]}(T_F,D(T)_F)$. There are fixed nonnegative integers
$a_j$ such that the finite-level term is
\[
 C_n(T)=hn+\sum_j\min(n,a_j).
\]
Thus the source's finiteness condition is equivalent to $h=0$.
The obstruction is invariant under every anticyclotomic twist.

\item \textbf{A sharper positive result inside the mirror argument.}
Its map from the dual Selmer group lands in symmetric conjugate-dual maps.
When the only rational invariant pairing is alternating, this smaller target
has bounded length. Under a perfect pairing and the source's integral Burnside
condition, its exact length is $\min(n,v_\pi(2))$.
The proposed extension of the complete twisting theorem still requires a Lean
refactor and verification of all its other hypotheses.

\item \textbf{A precise reason this does not settle BSD.}
For the Tate module of an abelian variety descending to a totally real field,
the conjugate Weil pairing is symmetric. It survives the sharper mirror
condition. Quotienting it out may destroy injectivity; the missing bound is
on its inverse image in the dual Selmer group.

\item \textbf{A certified modular-form case study.}
On the integral weight-24 cusp lattice, the matrix of $T_2$ is
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}0&1\\20468736&1080\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The projector onto one eigenform has exact denominator ideal
$(24\sqrt{144169})$. Its smallest positive rational integer clearing all
entries is $3460056$. Treating both conjugate forms together uses the identity
projector and avoids this cost; a new arithmetic estimate is still required.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{What is new relative to the supplied corpus.}
We found no prior treatment of the global self-duality obstruction, its exact
Smith formula, the symmetric-image refinement, or the explicit projector
analysis. The rank-two scalar-inertia obstruction and modular symmetric-power
analytic targets were already present; their roles are refined here.
These are comparisons with the supplied reports, not claims of worldwide
mathematical priority. Several predecessors claim completed arithmetic
classifications or finiteness results; we inventory those claims rather than
silently dismissing or recertifying them.
