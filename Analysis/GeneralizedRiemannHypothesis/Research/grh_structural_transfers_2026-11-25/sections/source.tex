\subsection{Pinned sources and verification scope}

The primary source is \texttt{lsernau/grh-lean} at commit
\nolinkurl{72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207}~\cite{repo}.
The comparison corpus is the incoming directory of
\texttt{VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt} at
\nolinkurl{6fe2c1bd450aa7f19eab17fb9a6e76170b4fce00}~\cite{prior}.
All twenty-six ZIPs were downloaded, and their Git blob hashes were matched.
The exact source manifest, prior-report inventory, and overlap notes are
included in the archive.

The five public Lean statements concern the Riemann zeta function and
Dirichlet $L$-functions, for every positive modulus and every character,
including imprimitive ones. They do not quantify over arbitrary number
fields, Artin representations, or cuspidal automorphic representations.
The proof's use of Hecke values over CM fields does not change the scope of
these statements. We use \emph{Dirichlet GRH} throughout to avoid ambiguity.

Andrew Sutherland's independent verification page reports successful builds,
axiom audits, statement comparison, and independent kernel checks of this
exact shorter commit~\cite{verification}. Our local clone matches all
1,625 files in the published SHA-256 manifest. We also performed the
included reproducible lexical scan: 803 Lean files in the readable
\texttt{src} tree and 794 in the compact tree contain no active
\texttt{sorry}, \texttt{admit}, \texttt{axiom}, \texttt{unsafe},
\texttt{implemented\_by}, or \texttt{native\_decide} tokens after comments
and strings are stripped. The separate challenge deliberately contains
placeholder proofs; it is outside these source trees.

\emph{No local Lean rebuild or kernel replay was performed in this work.}
The published checks and our file-identity verification are distinct from
our new mathematical arguments, which are paper proofs with finite exact
computations. This report contains no claim that the new results have
already been formalized.

\subsection{The arithmetic comparison}

The source compares one common covolume, corrected by an index, from two
directions. In schematic notation let $D$ be the target dimension, $n$ the
number of independent short vectors, $A$ the main amount, $R$ the arithmetic
remainder, $m$ the number of theta lifts, $V$ the logarithm of a squared
covolume, and $\delta$ the index cost. Its architecture is
\[
 D(A-\text{lower error})\ \le\ V+2\delta,
 \qquad
 V\ \le\ n(A-mR+\text{upper error}).
\]
The main amounts cancel. Almost-full spanning controls $D/n$, and a chain
length $m\asymp\sqrt{x}$ produces the square-root scale. In the source's
actual notation, \texttt{star\_inequality} proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:star}
 m\,\mathrm{totalRemainder}\le f a(\log b)^5.
\end{equation}
The final reduction gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:remainder}
 R_q(x)\le \frac{\varphi(q)(\log b)^6}{m}
       +|R_q(x_1)|+\log b\,|R_1(x_1)|.
\end{equation}
Here $m\ge\sqrt{x}$, $\log b$ can grow as an arbitrarily small power of $x$,
and $x_1$ is much larger. These are fixed-target eventual estimates, not
uniform conductor-aspect constants supplied for every prospective target.

Crucially, the coefficients in $R_q$ are
\[
 \Lambda(n)(1-\chi_K(n))\,\mathbf 1_{n\equiv1\pmod q},
\]
where $\chi_K$ is a quadratic character. Their occurrence is proved through
the local divisibility of theta derivatives and matching global periods.
A useful nonabelian Frobenius class function or Hecke trace is not a
substitutable parameter of this theorem.

\subsection{Reusable interfaces and their costs}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.25\textwidth}p{0.69\textwidth}}
\toprule
Interface & What a transfer must preserve\\\midrule
\endhead
Common twisted lattice & A single family of vectors, compatible integrality
matrices at each finite place, and a global comparison of determinant heights.
Choosing favorable local twists independently is allowed only inside this
common comparison.\\
Short forms and coverage & Integral short vectors span, while controlled
translates fill at least $(1-4/b^2)$ of the target dimension. Restricting to a
small eigenspace does not inherit the required lower determinant estimate.\\
Long forms & The lower bound uses determinant ratios and congruence-denominator
control for theta lifts. It is not a lower bound for every individual
eigenvalue of a positive operator.\\
Selmer twisting & Finite families of admissible representations and a finite
global/local length budget admit favorable anticyclotomic twists. Admissibility,
the original untwisted Selmer group, and vanishing are separate issues.\\
One-sided zero detector & An arithmetic remainder bound is turned into
nonvanishing by an analytic argument. Earlier reports substantially generalize
this endpoint; those generalizations do not supply the new arithmetic bound.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
