compiling this report. It includes nineteen unmodified upstream files
most relevant to the analysis, with the upstream Apache license and
attribution. These excerpts are not the complete Lean dependency tree.
The full repository can be cloned at the pinned commit to reproduce the
upstream build or the full lexical scan.

From the top directory, run:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 computation/verify_exact.py
sh build_report.sh
\end{verbatim}
The build script uses three \texttt{pdflatex} passes to settle the table of
contents and cross-references. Standard \TeX{} Live packages suffice; the
exact environment used here is recorded in
\sourcepath{evidence/tex_build_environment.json}.
The certificate uses no floating point. It passed 26 exhaustive commutant
counts, 27 symmetric-pairing counts, eight Smith-factor calculations, and
eleven prime Hecke-operator checks. The largest finite enumeration is a
small auxiliary check of the general proofs; no enumeration over arbitrary
torsion levels is claimed. The new arguments themselves are proved in the
body of the report.

For the unipotent matrix model in Section~\ref{sec:polarization-obstruction},
the symmetric invariant forms have exact length
\[
 2\min(n,r)+\min(n,r+v_\ell(2)).
\]
Indeed a symmetric matrix
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}u&v\\v&w\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ is fixed by
$A^{\mathsf t}(-)A$ and $B^{\mathsf t}(-)B$ precisely when
$\ell^r u=\ell^r w=2\ell^r v=0$ modulo $\ell^n$.
The script verifies the resulting formula independently by enumerating the
three matrix entries. At $r=0$ it gives exactly the $2$-torsion term of
the alternating-sign theorem.

\paragraph{Suggested Lean work, in dependency order.}
Define the symmetric submodule of \texttt{conjDualHom}; prove that the
constructed map \texttt{Phi} factors through it; derive the improved length
bound. Next prove the matrix-algebra commutant and primitive-intertwiner
lemmas, and the exact two-sign length theorem. Only then remove the old
finite-full-Hom field from a generalized datum and replay
\texttt{Descent}, \texttt{Line}, and \texttt{GeneralTheorem}. Existing
\texttt{MainSummands} constructions remain eligible because the symmetric
submodule is contained in the original Hom module. A successful extension
must finally instantiate every other arithmetic hypothesis in a concrete
new setting. This plan is a specification, not an already compiled patch.

\paragraph{Validation limits.}
The new paper proofs were cross-reviewed; exact finite calculations passed;
the PDF was rendered and inspected. We did not re-prove every predecessor
claim, independently rebuild the large Lean project, or establish a new
generalized RH or Selmer vanishing theorem. Keeping these distinctions is
essential to using the archive as a reliable starting point for further
research.
