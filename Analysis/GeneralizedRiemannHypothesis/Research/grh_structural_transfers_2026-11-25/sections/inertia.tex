\section{Scalar inertia is a substantive transfer hypothesis}

The source's \texttt{SelmerDatum} is not an arbitrary Selmer representation.
Besides a finite global congruence term, it requires an element $\gamma$ in
inertia at a place away from $\ell$ such that both $\rho(\gamma)$ and
$\rho(\gamma^{-c})$ are the scalar $\zeta I$, with $\zeta^2-1$ a unit.
See \texttt{New/Congruences/Selmer/SelmerData.lean}, the fields
\texttt{gamma\_mem\_inertiaAbove}, \texttt{rho\_eq\_smul\_one}, and
\texttt{isUnit\_sq\_sub\_one} (the first two identifiers are written with
Greek letters in the Lean source). The following tests are elementary
consequences of that requirement, not new conjectures about Selmer groups.

\begin{proposition}[Rank and determinant obstruction]
Let $\mathcal O$ be a discrete valuation ring of residue characteristic
$\ell$, with fraction field of characteristic zero, and let
$\rho:G\longrightarrow\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal O)$, $n>0$.
Let $I\subseteq G$ be a subgroup on which $\det\rho$ is trivial. Write
$n=\ell^a n_0$ with $\gcd(n_0,\ell)=1$.
If $n_0\in\{1,2\}$, there is no $\gamma\in I$ for which
$\rho(\gamma)=\zeta I_n$ and $\zeta^2-1\in\mathcal O^\times$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Taking determinants gives $\zeta^n=1$, so $\zeta$ is a unit. In the residue
field $k$,
\[
  (\bar\zeta^{n_0}-1)^{\ell^a}
       =\bar\zeta^{\ell^a n_0}-1=0.
\]
As $k$ is a field, $\bar\zeta^{n_0}=1$. If $n_0$ is $1$ or $2$, this
implies $\bar\zeta^2=1$. Thus $\zeta^2-1$ lies in the maximal ideal and is
not a unit.
\end{proof}

This applies whenever the determinant of the representation is unramified
at every finite place away from $\ell$, taking $I$ to be any of those
inertia groups. It is a necessary test, not a sufficient criterion for a
\texttt{SelmerDatum} to exist. For example, it rules out all ranks $1$ and
$2$, and ranks $\ell^a$ and $2\ell^a$, with such determinant. The hypothesis
on the determinant matters: adjoining a ramified scalar character changes
it and can remove this particular obstruction.
Such a preliminary character must have suitable nontrivial residual
order. The source's own $\ell$-power-order twists reduce to $1$ in
residue characteristic $\ell$, so those twists alone cannot turn
$\bar\zeta^2=1$ into $\bar\zeta^2\ne1$. Tensoring by a scalar also
cannot turn a nonscalar inertia matrix into a scalar one.

\begin{lemma}[Scalars on symmetric powers]
Let $F$ be a field of characteristic zero, let $A\in\mathrm{GL}_2(F)$,
and let $r\geq1$. If $\mathrm{Sym}^r(A)$ is a scalar matrix, then $A$
is a scalar matrix. Consequently, if also $\det A=1$, then
$\mathrm{Sym}^r(A)$ is $I$ or $-I$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Extend scalars to an algebraic closure. If $\lambda,\mu$ are the eigenvalues
of $A$, then $\lambda^r$ and $\lambda^{r-1}\mu$ occur among the eigenvalues
of $\mathrm{Sym}^r(A)$. They are equal, so $\lambda=\mu\neq0$.
If $A$ were not scalar, a Jordan basis would give
$Ae_1=\lambda e_1$ and $Ae_2=\lambda(e_2+u e_1)$, with $u\neq0$.
On the symmetric monomial $e_1^{r-1}e_2$, its action would then be
\[
 \lambda^r(e_1^{r-1}e_2+u e_1^r),
\]
which is not scalar. Hence $A=\lambda I$. The determinant condition gives
$\lambda^2=1$, and the final assertion follows.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[All positive symmetric powers]
Let $V$ be a two-dimensional $\ell$-adic representation whose determinant
is trivial on inertia away from $\ell$. For every $r\geq1$ and every
integer $t$, no stable lattice in
$\mathrm{Sym}^r(V)\otimes\chi_\ell^t$ can directly be a
\texttt{SelmerDatum} with those inertia groups.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The cyclotomic character is trivial on inertia away from $\ell$. If an
inertia element acts as $\zeta I$ on the indicated symmetric power, the
lemma gives $\zeta=\pm1$. Thus $\zeta^2-1=0$, contradicting the unit
condition. The argument takes place over the fraction field and so is
independent of the stable lattice.
\end{proof}

No hypothesis of semistability, tame ramification, diagonalizability, or
finite inertia image was used. In particular, wild inertia and nontrivial
unipotent matrices are covered by the Jordan argument. For an elliptic
curve, the Weil pairing~\cite{milne} gives $\det V_\ell=\chi_\ell$, so the corollary
applies to its positive symmetric powers and all Tate twists. This
includes cases in which the preceding rank test alone does not apply.

\paragraph{Interaction with the global obstruction.}
Suppose in addition that $V$ is a two-dimensional representation of
$G_{\mathbb Q}$ with determinant $\chi_\ell$, restricted to a CM field.
Conjugation by complex conjugation is an inner operation on the
representation, and $V^\vee\simeq V\otimes\chi_\ell^{-1}$. Therefore, for
$W=\mathrm{Sym}^r(V)\otimes\chi_\ell^t$, its conjugate Tate dual is
\[
 D(W):=(W^c)^\vee(1)
       \simeq\mathrm{Sym}^r(V)\otimes\chi_\ell^{1-r-t}.
\]
For odd $r$ and $t=(1-r)/2$, one has $D(W)\simeq W$. Thus these central
normalizations also fail the finite-global-congruence-term hypothesis.
A preliminary ramified anticyclotomic character with suitable prime-to-$\ell$
residual order can sometimes remove the scalar-inertia obstruction,
subject to its existence and local conditions; the source's
$\ell$-power-order twists cannot. Every anticyclotomic twist nevertheless
preserves $D(W)\simeq W$, since
$D(W\otimes\psi)\simeq D(W)\otimes\psi$ when $\psi^c=\psi^{-1}$.
Accordingly, anticyclotomic pre-twisting does not repair both problems.
For even symmetric powers the displayed integral central normalization is
unavailable; one must not claim this particular self-duality obstruction
solely from the rank-two origin.

\paragraph{Why the source avoids the global obstruction.}
The source's main summands obey, on traces,
\[
 \rho^c\simeq\rho^\vee\otimes
       \chi_\ell^{-(t-1)_{\mathbb N}},\qquad
 D(\rho)\simeq\rho\otimes
       \chi_\ell^{(t-1)_{\mathbb N}+1}.
\]
The subscript denotes truncated natural-number subtraction, as used in
Lean. The shift is positive even when $t=0$. At the scalar Frobenius
selected by the source, the annihilator of the conjugate-dual Hom module
is
\[
 \alpha\bigl(1-p^{n((t-1)_{\mathbb N}+1)}\bigr).
\]
This is nonzero and has a valuation bound supplied by the chosen finite
linear groups. The exact implementation is
\sourcepath{IsMainSummandLike.normLength_congruenceTerm_le} in
\sourcepath{New/Congruences/Selmer/MainSummands.lean}; the elementary
annihilator estimate is \sourcepath{congruenceTerm_le} in
\sourcepath{SelmerData.lean}. Replacing the source by a central self-dual
representation sets the analogous scalar difference to zero, so this
bound becomes infinite instead of small.
