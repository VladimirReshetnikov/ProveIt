FORMALIZATION PLAN — NOT COMPLETED LEAN CODE

The new theorems in this report have not been compiled in Lean. Selected
upstream .lean files are exact source snapshots, not formalizations of
the new results.

Suggested dependency order:

1. Abstract exponent monoid.
   Prove that the base-three {0,1} set contains no nonconstant 3-term AP.
   Formalize the finite cube exchange and its nonnegative-step restriction.
   Prove n=2b(n)-a(n) and the greedy-prefix obstruction.

2. Weighted monoids.
   Define the summable geometric product measure for finitely many primes.
   Prove the exact exchange-gain identity and rational upper bound for T.
   Formalize the pure-prime exponent exchange {0,1,3,4}->{0,1,3,7,8}.

3. Ideals ordered by norm.
   Reuse unique factorization of nonzero ideals, multiplicativity of norm,
   and ideal counting. Prove finite valuation independence and uniform
   square-divisibility tail control. Deduce existence of the relevant
   natural densities and the all-fields gain theorem.

4. Smooth-fiber extrema.
   Define B and U. Formalize the harmonic upper bound for natural density,
   the rough-fiber upper bound for upper density, and the squarefree-rough
   lower constructions. The upper-density construction must define one
   infinite set with permanently assigned fibers at a lacunary sequence.
   Deduce U-B >= c/[M(M+1)], then the global gap and computability.

5. Character arithmetic.
   Formalize the S-ray quotient R_Q/<r_3,r_beta>, including the precursor
   decomposition-group condition and injectivity of xi->xi/xi^c in the
   permitted support class. Connect integer certificates to cyclic group
   quotients and local conductor formulas. The Python output alone is not
   a class-field proof.

6. Strict cocycles.
   Prove coefficient descent for the exact source functor, without
   quotienting by coboundaries. Include the prime 2 and the special j=0
   local rule. Combine finite-image continuity with torsion saturation
   to prove the fixed-field single-precision equivalence.

7. Later arithmetic transfer.
   The uniform bounds on X, compatible divisibility and determinant
   construction, and all simultaneously required twisting conditions
   are open tasks within this report, not formalization-only tasks.
