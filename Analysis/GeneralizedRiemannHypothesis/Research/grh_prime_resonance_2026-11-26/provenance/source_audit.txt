2. ACTUAL UPSTREAM SCOPE

Statements.lean imports Mathlib to state the five public claims independently
of internal proof definitions. Main.lean derives those claims. The endpoints
include RH and nonvanishing of all Dirichlet L-functions in Re(s)>1/2, except
the principal pole, and critical-line location of their open-strip zeros.
This is not a blanket theorem for every Artin or automorphic L-function.

The upstream README reports Lean 4.35.0, Mathlib revision
56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679, and the standard axiom set
propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound. It also reports independent checker
acceptance. These are repository reports. We did not rebuild upstream.

The relevant method was checked in GUIDE.md, PrimeError.lean, Criterion.lean,
Landau.lean, MainInequality.lean, and OneSidedBound.lean. A positive smoothed
prime remainder has a meromorphic transform whose residues see zero orders.
The arithmetic comparison of covolume plus twice an index yields a one-sided
bound; the analytic Landau criterion excludes right-half-plane zeros.

This work reuses the ramp weight and residue denominator after the zero-location
theorem, giving positive spectral weights 1/(1/4+gamma^2). It does not extend
the high-rank theta-lift construction.

5. DISTINCT CONTRIBUTION AND LIMITS

The proved refinement is the uniform covariance expansion whose off-diagonal
coefficient is an exact signed prime-power matrix, the determinant expansion
to every inverse-logarithmic order with O(h/log(1/h)) error, and diagonal
conjugacy showing that all such coefficients depend only on ramified primes
p<=N after the conductor shift. Equal coefficients are not asserted to recover
the conductor. The full kernels remain character-dependent.

The Brownian process is constructed from the zero spectrum. This is not a
Gaussian-limit theorem for ordinary prime errors or values of zeta under
random shifts. There is no zero-simplicity or zero-independence assumption.
All asymptotics keep the character, dimension, basepoints and time interval fixed.
