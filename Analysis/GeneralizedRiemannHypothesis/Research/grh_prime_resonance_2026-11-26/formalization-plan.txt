FORMALIZATION PLAN -- NOT A COMPLETED LEAN PROOF

The mathematical proofs are in main.tex / grh_prime_resonance.pdf. No .lean
file in this delivery is advertised as checked. The upstream RH development
was not rebuilt. This plan isolates the new finite and local arguments from
the much larger analytic prerequisites.

1. ABSTRACT LOCAL KERNEL

Work over a real even continuous function K. Assume, with explicit constants:
  K(t) = kappa + (t/2)(log t - d) + O(t^2) for t > 0 near 0;
  at each of finitely many nonzero lags a, K(t)=G_a(t)+J_a max(t-a,0),
  where G_a is twice continuously differentiable on a fixed neighborhood.
Define mixed increments by the four-term difference of K.

Prove the uniform diagonal expansion using x log x -> 0 at zero. Prove the
off-diagonal formula by the rectangle integral of G_a'' and the elementary
identity (u-v)_+ - u = -min(u,v) for u,v >= 0. Package the finite collection
of neighborhoods using a positive minimum radius. Prove the triangular
profile by 2v_+-(v+1)_+-(v-1)_+=-(1-|v|)_+.

2. FINITE MATRICES

For Fin N, define the symmetric prime-power quotient matrix using exact
integer divisibility. Distinguish the case i=j before examining j/i.
Prove the covariance matrix approximation and divide by the true variance.
Use operator norm perturbation and the spectral theorem for real symmetric
matrices to obtain positivity and the log determinant trace series. The
power-series tail is bounded by N r^(M+1)/((M+1)(1-r)) when ||A/ell||<=r<1.

Prove the quadratic coefficient by enumerating undirected edges twice.
Prove the cubic coefficient by classifying triangles i,ip^a,ip^(a+b).
The finite exact computations through trace degree 8 are supporting checks;
the proof for every degree follows from diagonal conjugacy.

3. REAL-CHARACTER GAUGE

Factor each integer n into the part supported on primes dividing q and its
coprime part n_unr. Let epsilon(n)=chi(n_unr) in {1,-1}. Prove epsilon(i)
epsilon(ip^k)=chi(p)^k for p not dividing q. At p dividing q both compared
matrix entries are zero. This proves A_chi=D A_deleted D with D^2=I.
Deduce equality of all traces, characteristic polynomials, and convergent
log determinant series. Align conductors by the elementary identity
ell_q(q epsilon)=log(1/epsilon)+1-gamma_E-log(2pi).

4. ANALYTIC INPUT

Import the zero-location theorem through the stable Statements/Main boundary
of the pinned upstream project, not through an invented replacement axiom.
Formalize or reuse the classical zero count and the paired Hadamard product.
For zeta, obtain the absolutely convergent smoothed explicit formula with the
residues at 1, the nontrivial zeros, 0, and the negative even integers. Endpoint
weights vanish at n=x. Prove the closed formula for the trivial-zero tail.

For real primitive nonprincipal chi, prove the Laplace-transform comparison
using its completed function, the parity digamma integral, and the absolutely
convergent Dirichlet series. Retain possible central zeros. The exact transfer
formula supplies the local hypotheses of step 1. Primivity and reality are
explicit requirements; do not silently extend this proof to complex or
imprimitive characters.

5. POSITIVITY AND OPTIONAL PROBABILITY LAYER

Construct the deterministic real Hilbert-space vector v_chi(t), with one
cos/sin coordinate pair for every distinct positive ordinate and a constant
coordinate for central multiplicity. Summability proves its Gram identity.
This suffices for all matrix and determinant theorems.

The Gaussian corollary may then be formalized using an isonormal Gaussian
process or the independent normal-coordinate construction. Prove continuous
modification and tightness from fourth moments bounded by C |u-v|^(3/2).
State functional convergence in distribution in C([0,U])^N, not almost surely.

6. SCOPE GUARDS

Keep N, the character, the distinct basepoints, and U fixed as h tends to 0.
Do not infer a uniform conductor bound, growing-N theorem, simple zeros,
linear independence of ordinates, or a Gaussian law for prime errors.
