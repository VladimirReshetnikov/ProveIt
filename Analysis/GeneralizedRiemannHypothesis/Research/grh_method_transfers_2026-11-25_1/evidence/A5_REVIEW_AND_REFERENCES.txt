A5 TRANSFER: HYPOTHESES, CHECKS, AND REFERENCES
============================================

Purpose and status
------------------
The A5 section proves a conditional transfer theorem and exact finite algebraic
certificates. It does not prove the required nonabelian prime-sum estimate or
the Riemann hypothesis for an A5 extension. The finite checker does not certify
analytic continuation, class field theory, or the Lean build.

Files
-----
code/a5_detector.py
data/a5_detector_results.json

The checker uses only the Python standard library. Its output was regenerated
from the copy included in this deliverable and compared byte-for-byte with the
original successful run; the outputs match.

Theorem hypotheses
------------------
1. L/Q is finite Galois with group A5.
2. K5, K6, K12 are fixed fields of subgroups A4, D5, C5 of orders 12, 10, 5.
   Thus their degrees over Q are 5, 6, 12; D5 means order 10 here.
3. RH for the Riemann zeta function is used to control the denominator of
   F(s)=zeta_K5(s)*zeta_K6(s)*zeta_K12(s)/zeta(s)^2 in Re(s)>1/2.
4. For every epsilon>0 there must be constants C_epsilon,X_epsilon such that
   the explicitly normalized remainder R_W(x) is at most
   C_epsilon*x^(-1/2+epsilon) for all x>=X_epsilon.

The fourth hypothesis is the new arithmetic estimate still missing. No
holomorphy assumption is imposed on individual irreducible Artin L-functions.

Finite algebra independently checked
-----------------------------------
The checker enumerates all 60 even permutations on five letters. It constructs
A4 as a point stabilizer, C5 from a five-cycle, and D5 as its normalizer in A5.
The permutation-character values are computed from fixed-coset counts, rather
than taken from a character-table library.

Classes:       1A   2A   3A   5A   5B
Class sizes:    1   15   20   12   12
pi5:            5    1    2    0    0
pi6:            6    2    0    1    1
pi12:          12    0    0    2    2
W:             21   1    0    1    1

The exact identities checked on every group element are
  W = pi5 + pi6 + pi12 - 2*1 >= 0,
  reg_A5 = 4*pi5 + 2*pi6 + 3*pi12 - 8*1.
The mean of W is 1, its variance is 7, and its peak is 21. Positivity was checked
also on every power of every group element. Artin formalism yields
  zeta_L*zeta^8 = zeta_K5^4*zeta_K6^2*zeta_K12^3.

Prime powers and ramification
----------------------------
For inertia I_p and a Frobenius lift g_p, the coefficient at p^k of -F'/F is
  a(p^k) = log(p)/|I_p| * sum_{h in I_p} W(g_p^k*h).
This follows by taking the trace of Frobenius times the projection to inertia
invariants. It is valid for the virtual character by linearity. Consequently
0<=a(n)<=21*Lambda(n), including at ramified primes. At an unramified prime
the coefficient is W(g_p^k)*log(p), not W(g_p)*log(p) for every k.

Normalization and analytic certificate
--------------------------------------
If -F'/F = 1/(s-1) + b_F + O(s-1), define c_F=b_F-1 and
  R_W(x) = sum_{n<=x} a(n)*(1/n-1/x) - log(x) - c_F.
The Mellin transform is exactly
  (-F'/F)/(s*(s-1)) - 1/(s-1)^2 - c_F/(s-1).
Both Laurent poles at 1 cancel. At a different point rho its residue is
  ord_rho(F)/(rho*(1-rho)).
The sign agrees with the residue -ord_rho(F) of -F'/F.

For real 1/2<u<1, the residue is nonnegative: zeta has no zero there under RH,
while the three numerator zeta functions have nonnegative orders. For u>1
there is no zero and the residue vanishes. The coefficient bound supplies
measurability and E(v)=R_W(exp(v))=O(1+v^2), which is sufficient growth for the
repository's polar-transform Landau lemma. The missing one-sided estimate
then rules out every zero of F in Re(s)>1/2. In that half-plane the order of F
is a sum of three nonnegative Dedekind zeta orders, so each numerator is
zero-free. The regular-character identity gives the same conclusion for
zeta_L; functional equations yield the claimed Riemann hypotheses.

Optimality and abelian obstruction: exact scope
---------------------------------------------
Among a*pi5+b*pi6+c*pi12+(1-a-b-c)*1 with real a,b,c>=1, the identity value is
1+4a+5b+11c>=21. The displayed nonnegative detector attains 21. This is a
scoped optimum in this family, not global optimality over all possible fields
or kernels.

A5 has no nontrivial abelian quotient. For any finite abelian B/Q, L and B
are disjoint, so Chebotarev gives for every complex function f on Gal(B/Q):
  limiting prime mean |W(Frob_L)-f(Frob_B)|^2
    = 7 + group mean |f-1|^2.
This excludes representation of W by a fixed finite set of Dirichlet
characters. It is not a logical independence theorem and does not cover
moduli changing with the prime-sum scale.

Explicit A5 field certificate
----------------------------
The polynomial is X^5+20X+16. The script computes its exact resultant with its
derivative and obtains discriminant 1024000000=32000^2. Modulo 3, it has no
linear factor and remainders 1, X+1, X+1 modulo the three monic irreducible
quadratics. It is therefore irreducible. Modulo 7, its factorization is
(X+2)*(X+3)*(X^3+2X^2-2X-2), with the cubic having no root. These provide
elements of orders 5 and 3 in a subgroup of A5. Order 15 is excluded by Sylow's
theorems (the group would be cyclic, impossible inside S5); order 30 would be
an index-two subgroup of A5, impossible since A5 is generated by 3-cycles.
Thus the Galois group is A5.
