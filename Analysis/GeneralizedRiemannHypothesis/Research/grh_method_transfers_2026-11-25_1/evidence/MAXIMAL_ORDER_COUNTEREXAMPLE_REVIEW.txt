INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE MAXIMAL-ORDER CHARACTER COUNTEREXAMPLE
=============================================================

Let p be prime, m>=2, N=p^m, and let zeta be a primitive N-th root of unity in
C_p. Normalize v(p)=1. Let G=C_N with generator g and identity 0, and work in
the integral group algebra O_{C_p}[G]. Define

  nu = product_{a=1}^{p-1} ([g] - zeta^a[0]).

The augmentation is product_{a=1}^{p-1}(1-zeta^a). Since every a in this range
is prime to p, each factor has valuation

  r_m=1/phi(p^m)=1/(p^{m-1}(p-1)).

Therefore the augmentation has finite valuation

  t=(p-1)r_m=p^{1-m}<1.

Every character of exact order N is determined by chi(g)=zeta^u with p not
dividing u. Its evaluation on nu is

  product_{a=1}^{p-1}(zeta^u-zeta^a).

There is exactly one a_0 in {1,...,p-1} with u=a_0 modulo p. For each of the
other p-2 factors, u-a is prime to p and its valuation is r_m. If
u=a_0 modulo N, the evaluation is zero and its valuation is infinity.
Otherwise j=v_p(u-a_0) satisfies 1<=j<=m-1. Since zeta^(u-a_0) has exact order
p^(m-j), the remaining factor has valuation p^j*r_m. Thus every finite
primitive-character evaluation has the exact valuation

  (p-2+p^j)r_m >= (2p-2)r_m = 2t.

This includes p=2: then there are no other factors, and the same formula and
inequality hold. Equality is attained for u=a_0+p (mod N), so the lower bound
2t is sharp among finite values.

Conclusion: for any 0<epsilon<t, no character of maximal/exact order p^m has
evaluation valuation at most t+epsilon, although augmentation valuation t is
strictly below 1. A statement selecting a character of order dividing p^m is
different: the trivial character has valuation exactly t.

Quantifier caveat: t=p^{1-m} shrinks as m increases. This family refutes an
unqualified maximal-order replacement at each finite level and arbitrarily
small allowed losses. It does not refute a theorem with a fixed positive
epsilon for all sufficiently large m, nor does it itself give a counterexample
with one fixed measure and fixed positive augmentation valuation as m varies.

This review is an exact symbolic valuation argument, not a floating-point
calculation. It presupposes only the standard cyclotomic valuation formula
v(1-zeta_{p^h})=1/phi(p^h).
