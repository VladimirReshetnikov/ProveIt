\section{Which research directions now deserve work?}
\label{sec:program}

The most useful next step is to integrate a theorem with an already
identified arithmetic role. Further general zero criteria have lower
value until the corresponding local divisibility construction exists.
The present results lead to the following priorities.

\paragraph{1. Formalize the rank-free source estimate.}
The smallest improvement needs only finite-level cyclicity and the
source's existing scalar-annihilation lemma. It does not require the
whole trace-rigidity formalization. One can then replace the coarse
global bound in \path{SelmerData.lean}, propagate it into
\path{MainSummands.lean}, and retain the existing local terms.
This is a concrete strengthening with unchanged arithmetic hypotheses.
Its effect on downstream numerical constants should be assessed after
the formal corollary is built; an exponent improvement is not implied.

\paragraph{2. Complete one genuine non-CM Selmer datum.}
The Ramanujan family is a useful test because the global congruence
term is now known exactly. At a noncentral shift, the missing issues
are exposed rather than hidden in a generic boundedness assumption:
an appropriate ramified character to create the required scalar
inertia, the full local crystalline conditions, and the label/level
compatibilities needed by the actual theorem.
The calculation is not evidence that all those conditions can be
simultaneously fulfilled. It supplies a precise target for deciding
that question.

At the central shift \(j=-5\), the obstruction is already decisive:
\(C=C^+=\infty\). A direct use of the source's finite-global-term
theorem cannot work there. Anticyclotomic twisting preserves the
obstruction. A valid central Bloch--Kato application would need a
different arithmetic theorem that removes or accounts for the fixed
self-dual line and re-proves the global duality step. Merely quotienting
that line in an abstract module does not do this.\cite{priorstruct}

\paragraph{3. Pursue one nonabelian divisibility identity.}
The \(S_3\) cubic target remains preferable to another large-group
detector: it is a two-dimensional non-Dirichlet factor, its Frobenius
traces have only three values, and its determinant is explicit.
The appropriate immediate target is a local identity translating
those values into divisibility of an actual family of sections,
forms, or periods. The determinant upgrade of
Lemma~\ref{lem:ranktwo} can be used once that family exists.
It cannot substitute for it.

\paragraph{4. Preserve the distinction between odd and even cyclotomic data.}
The source's odd cyclotomic cocycle estimates are not even class-group
vanishing statements. Vandiver's conjecture and the missing
\(K\)-theory comparisons cannot be obtained by changing notation.
The prior reports already identify strict-Selmer, ramification,
witness-quantifier, and determinant-certification gaps.
The improved congruence term does not establish those comparisons.

\paragraph{5. Consolidate completed elementary transfers.}
The density-gap result in Appendix~\ref{app:density-audit} is a completed
theorem about a specific pair of open questions in the cited paper.
Its proof and finite certificates can be consolidated into a standalone
manuscript without pretending it is a new result of the present session.
Other reported graph, finite-ring, and sparse-polynomial theorems should
receive comparably focused checks before being used as established
inputs. The ledger makes that distinction reviewable.

\paragraph{What would count as a resolved conjecture?}
For a proposed new \(L\)-function, a complete arithmetic estimate plus
its analytic reduction is required. For a Selmer or \(K\)-group
statement, the module must be identified with the standard arithmetic
object, every local condition matched, and the complete bound shown to
force the desired conclusion. For an inherited elementary statement,
the proof must address precisely the quantifiers in the original
question. These are mathematical completion criteria; this report
claims them only where the full proof has actually been supplied.
