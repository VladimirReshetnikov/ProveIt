The raw source names \texttt{New}, \texttt{Lib}, and \texttt{shaOdd}
do not establish novelty, a full standard-library implementation, or an
identification with a customary global Selmer group. Their definitions
and hypotheses must be read. Similarly, a proof of a stronger local
arithmetic fact cannot be inferred just because a familiar theorem
name appears in a source comment.

For prior work, the authoritative per-archive provenance is
\path{evidence/prior_audit_ledger.csv}. Its status column distinguishes
claims reported in a manuscript from the targeted proofs independently
reviewed in this investigation. The abstract classifications in
Section~\ref{sec:prior-landscape} are a map of that corpus, not blanket
certification of its conclusions.
