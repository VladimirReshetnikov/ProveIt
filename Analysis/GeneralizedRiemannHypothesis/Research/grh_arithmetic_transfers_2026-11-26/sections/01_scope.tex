\section{Answer, scope, and evidentiary status}
\label{sec:scope}

\textbf{Yes: the internal arithmetic machinery has useful transfers beyond
standard consequences of RH and Dirichlet GRH.} The concrete gain in this
report is an exact, computable version of a global congruence term used
to control denominators. It gives both a sharper source estimate and a
worked family of modular Galois representations. The existing corpus also
contains completed results outside the usual RH consequences; one such
result is revalidated in Appendix~\ref{app:density-audit}.
The analysis does not turn the available nonabelian zero criteria into
proofs of their missing arithmetic inequalities.

The exported upstream statements are Mathlib's Riemann hypothesis and
four assertions about zeros of zeta and Dirichlet \(L\)-functions.
In particular, the strongest nonvanishing endpoint ranges over
\(\chi:\) Dirichlet characters of every positive integer modulus:
\[
 \Re s>\tfrac12,\qquad (\chi\ne1\ \text{or}\ s\ne1)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad L(s,\chi)\ne0.
\]
The use of automorphic forms, Hecke values, or Galois representations
inside the proof does not expand this exported quantifier to arbitrary
Artin or automorphic \(L\)-functions.\cite{statements}
Throughout this report, the unqualified source endpoint means
\emph{Dirichlet} GRH; the broader problems are stated separately.

\subsection{What was verified in this investigation}

The static source scan covered 803 readable-edition and 794 compact-edition
Lean source files. After removing comments and string literals, it found
none of the listed proof-placeholder or trust-sensitive tokens in either
\path{src} tree. The intentional Comparator placeholders in
\path{Challenge.lean} are outside those trees and are not imported by
the endpoint sources. The scan is reproducible from the supplied
\path{checks/upstream_audit.py} and JSON certificate.

The release and independent verification pages report successful
compilation, standard-axiom checks, and kernel replays. We read those
reports; we did \emph{not} repeat the complete Lean build or independent
kernel replay here.\cite{upstream,sutherland,rubinstein}
The distinction matters: a static scan is useful evidence about source
structure, but is not a replacement for a compiled axiom check.

Our positive mathematical claims consist of full paper proofs below,
classical arithmetic inputs cited at their point of use, and exact
computations checking concrete certificates. There is no claim that the
new refinements have already been formalized in Lean.

\subsection{What advances the supplied work}

The earlier structural report already isolated rational conjugate
self-duality, the general Smith-factor formula, and symmetric mirror
targets. It also showed that anticyclotomic twisting preserves the
self-duality obstruction.\cite{priorstruct}
We retain those findings. The additional work here treats the
\emph{finite-depth} case: when no rational intertwiner survives, the
global term is still exactly recoverable from traces under integral
matrix span. This makes the following progression concrete:
\[
 \text{trace discrepancy}
 \ \Longrightarrow\
 \text{exact congruence length}
 \ \Longrightarrow\
 \text{sharper denominator input}.
\]
It does not bypass the construction of a Selmer datum or an arithmetic
lattice. The distinction is tested by the Ramanujan example, where the
global term is completely determined but the source's auxiliary
scalar-inertia hypothesis is still absent.

Carayol's trace rigidity and elementary divisor theory underlie the
algebraic arguments.\cite{carayol}
The contribution is their exact application to the source's internal
quantity, the arithmetic example, and a precise account of what remains
before an open-conjecture transfer could be claimed.
