\paragraph{Abstract.}
We inspect the pinned \emph{grh-lean} source and inventory all 46 supplied
ProveIt transfer archives. The source's most reusable structure is an
arithmetic comparison of one corrected lattice covolume from above and
below. Its global Selmer congruence term admits an exact refinement:
under the source's full integral matrix-span hypothesis, finite-level
intertwiner modules are cyclic, with length equal to the depth of trace
congruence. This removes a rank factor from an internal scalar bound,
permits finite trace certificates, and gives a common denominator ideal.
Using classical facts about the Ramanujan representation, we evaluate
this term for every Tate twist over \(\Q(\sqrt{-23})\), at all
nonexceptional coefficient primes. A nonmaximal-order example proves
that rational irreducibility is insufficient for the refinement.
A rank-two determinant lemma clarifies the Euler-factor interface needed
for the conductor-23 nonabelian target. We also independently recheck a
completed density-gap result in the supplied corpus. These deductions
reuse proof ingredients rather than familiar consequences of RH/GRH.
They do not establish nonabelian RH, BSD, Bloch--Kato, or a new
\(K\)-group vanishing theorem; the remaining arithmetic hypotheses are
specified explicitly. The representation-theoretic ingredients are
classical, and no worldwide priority claim is made.
