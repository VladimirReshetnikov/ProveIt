# Sources and scope

## The three requested sources

1. The readable edition of Luke Sernau's repository is pinned to commit `72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207`. Its public statements use Mathlib's zeta function and Dirichlet L-functions. The half-plane form covers every Dirichlet character modulo a positive integer, with the principal pole at 1 excepted. Its critical-strip form includes primitive and imprimitive characters. The internal reduction obtains right-half-plane nonvanishing from a one-sided estimate for a weighted von Mangoldt sum. [Statements](https://github.com/lsernau/grh-lean/blob/72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207/grh/src/Statements.lean#L1-L16) · [Main theorems](https://github.com/lsernau/grh-lean/blob/72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207/grh/src/Main.lean#L4-L22) · [Reduction](https://github.com/lsernau/grh-lean/blob/72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207/grh/src/New/Reduction/OneSidedBound.lean#L8-L18).

It reports the three axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`, and acceptance of the dependency export by nanoda, con-leche and con-ron. ## Immediate antecedents of the zero-packet paper

- ProveIt's *What zero differences forget about Dirichlet characters* identifies balanced products of fixed-frequency zero sums at one prime with zero-sum monomials in a cyclic Fourier transform. It classifies equality of all such limits by local rotations, gives a criterion for global gluing at coprime mass, and exhibits one cubic measurement for odd prime exponent. Its final paragraph asks for a shortest measurement list for composite exponents. [Measurement model and gluing](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/b29490a150d3947b114d6c9daf3398300537eb64/Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis/Research/dirichlet_zero_packet_homometry/zero_packets.tex#L65-L113) · [Prime-exponent cubic](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/b29490a150d3947b114d6c9daf3398300537eb64/Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis/Research/dirichlet_zero_packet_homometry/zero_packets.tex#L186-L209) · [Composite-exponent question](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/b29490a150d3947b114d6c9daf3398300537eb64/Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis/Research/dirichlet_zero_packet_homometry/zero_packets.tex#L234).
- William D. Banks, *Comparing zeros of distinct Dirichlet L-functions*, Theorem 1.2, supplies the Dirichlet-character version of Landau's formula used to evaluate each prime-power probe. The arithmetic reconstruction above this input is finite algebra. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07073).
- F. A. Grünbaum and C. C. Moore, *The use of higher-order invariants in the determination of generalized Patterson cyclotomic sets*, Acta Cryst. A51 (1995), 310–323, established degree six for integer cyclic sequences and degree four at odd order. Their argument treats classes of frequencies with a common order, phase compatibility, and examples with two order classes and inconsistent shifts. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1107/S0108767394009827).
- Luke Pebody, *The reconstructibility of finite abelian groups*, Combin. Probab. Comput. 13 (2004), 867–892, studies the optimal deck order for multisets. Tamás Keleti and Mihail N. Kolountzakis describe the distinct classifications for rational nonnegative functions and for indicators. In particular, a full triple deck determines all such rational functions for odd prime powers and products of two odd primes. [Pebody](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/combinatorics-probability-and-computing/article/abs/reconstructibility-of-finite-abelian-groups/AC34CE37E4EADC905C023A24E93C9951) · [Keleti–Kolountzakis](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0603415).
- W. Riley Casper and Bobby Orozco, *Reconstructing rational functions on finite Abelian groups with higher autocorrelations*, give a constructive algorithm using Galois conjugacy, doubling orbits, decompositions into units, and local phase alignment. Theorems 3.1 and 3.8 provide close precedents for the algebraic ingredients of the protocol. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21022).
- B. Schefler, K. Zhao and Q. Zhong, *On separating sets of polynomial invariants of finite abelian group actions*, investigate the minimum size of monomial separating sets on complex representations. This is a direct methodological precedent for the fixed-list question. The rational histogram constraint in the present setting makes all frequencies of a given order Galois conjugates and permits one measurement to control an entire such class. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/00927872.2026.2644621).
- *Autocorrelation Phase Lattices on Cyclic Groups* studies least closing degrees for specific supports and the binary reconstruction problem for groups of order twice the product of two distinct odd primes. Its unit-support analysis includes obstructions through degree five. [Preprint](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00241v1).

The novelty comparison concerns the minimum **number of individual, exact, balanced Fourier monomials** sufficient for every integral histogram of known mass, with a distinction between fixed and adaptive designs. The cited classical results already establish bounded reconstruction order, the importance of Galois conjugacy, and the need to align phase information across order classes. The literature search establishes no general priority guarantee.

## Antecedents of the prime-trace paper

- T. Kasami and N. Tokura, *On the weight structure of Reed–Muller codes*, IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 16 (1970), 752–759. The low-weight classification supplies the candidate support sizes once the quadratic trace disagreement has been converted into a Boolean polynomial. P. Gopalan, A. Klivans and D. Zuckerman reproduce the needed normal forms in their work on list decoding. [Kasami–Tokura](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.1970.1054545) · [Gopalan–Klivans–Zuckerman](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~klivans/rm.pdf).
- K. Martin and N. Walji, *Distinguishing finite group characters and refined local-global phenomena*, study agreement densities for irreducible characters. The companion paper concerns sums of quadratic characters, and uses their integer multiplicities and the two possible character values at unramified primes. [Paper](https://kimballmartin.github.io/papers/dist-artin.pdf).
