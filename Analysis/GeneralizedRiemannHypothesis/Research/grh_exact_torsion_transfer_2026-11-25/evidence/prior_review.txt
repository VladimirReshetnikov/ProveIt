SCOPE AND VERIFICATION

All 26 ZIP archives in the pinned incoming directory were downloaded,
checked against their Git blob SHA-1, and safely extracted. SHA-256 values,
exact archive sizes, and manuscript paths are recorded in prior_inventory.json.
The archive scripts were not executed as part of this corpus review.

This was a comparison and targeted mathematical review, not independent
verification of every prior proof or finite computation. In particular,
the CM classification and large Hecke certificates were not all replayed.
No contradiction was found in the targeted K-theory caveats, torsion
statements, and nonabelian reductions inspected. Prior claims remain claims
of their respective reports unless separately checked in the new report.

WHAT WAS ALREADY COVERED

The corpus already contains generic Mellin-Landau one-sided detectors;
sparse scale and twist families; finite-difference constant removal;
averaged positive-part criteria; moving windows and linear logarithmic
auxiliary windows. It also contains nonabelian reductions for S3, S4,
A5, A6, PSL2(7), PSL2(2^r), general finite Galois groups, and prime-index
Kummer targets for Artin's primitive-root conjecture. These reductions do
not prove the new arithmetic prime bounds they require.

Integral bounded-power-series extensions, countable finite-budget torsion
families, determinant/Fitting ideal specialization, and a polynomial
shell-exponent bound are also already present. The newest torsion report
controls individual valuations with positive tolerances and gives a good-root
fraction. The exact scalar/aggregate bound in the new report is a further
result, with different scope; it does not subsume the individual-constraint
and density conclusions of that predecessor.

The distinction between the original Step 17 and the shorter repository,
the omission of Vandiver's even classes from a denominator estimate, the
pure-Tate inertia-scalar obstruction, and fixed-field versus diagonal
cyclotomic quantifiers were already identified. They are essential context,
not new discoveries of this review.

K-THEORY: THE PREDECESSOR'S ACTUAL FRONTIER

The latest method-transfers archive, with suffix '(2)', distinguishes
Mok-Zheng's claimed vanishing through degree 120 from Pintar's conditional
range through degree 104. It records the incomplete rebuild of the modified
Gaussian entry point and the different common-versus-pointwise witness
hypotheses. Its original-source interface is UpperTheory.V.Z.J.OneCharU_s,
with caller SmallClassNumbers/CorGKOf.lean, in the larger original development;
those names are absent from the shorter repository.

It already computes Bernoulli-unit screening for even e=54,...,80. For
e=62,64, corresponding to K-degrees 124,128, the two-field numerator gcd is
4. It records Eisenstein negative-space ranks 550,605, Gaussian ranks
1240,1360, and independent small Hecke operators at e=6,8. A meaningful next
step needs a new large determinant together with its correct strict-Selmer
interpretation; further screening tables alone do not settle K-group vanishing.

The beyond-Dirichlet-GRH archive proves a paper-level identification, for
odd coefficient primes and positive e, of the direct limit of the source's
odd cocycles with the natural conjugation eigenspace

  Sha^1(E,(F/O)(-e))^{c=(-1)^(e+1)}.

It transfers a finite length bound and proves integral odd-cocycle vanishing.
It excludes coefficient prime 2 and e=0. For even e its natural sign is minus,
whereas the direct rational K_{4m} obstruction lies in the plus sector. It
does not close the K-theory or Vandiver bridge.

COMPLETE ARCHIVE MAP

   results, and syndetic progressions.

   additive prime-cube thresholds, alternating subset fields, and
   congruence-versus-splitting obstructions.

3. grh-exponent-six-and-transfers.zip
   Exponent-six class-group exclusions, reflected quintic families,
   local and joint splitting computations, and graph-related applications.

4. grh-lean-transfers.zip

   class-three constraints, cubic and quintic targets, and prime races.

5. grh-transfer.zip
