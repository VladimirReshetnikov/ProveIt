# Independent review: projective Hermitian frames and the unramified cubic application

Reviewed input: research/notes/projective_frames.md.
Reviewer: source_audit agent.
Verdict: the projective-frame and local-generator arguments are valid, subject to the small normalization/degenerate-dimension qualifications below. They are qualitative mathematical proofs, not a compiled Lean formalization or a completed nonabelian GRH argument.

## 1. Projective dual generators

A finite projective O_K-module A has a finite dual system x_i, ell_i with sum x_i ell_i=Id. Extending ell_i to K and representing it by h(y_i,-) is legitimate for a nondegenerate Hermitian form. Self-duality places y_i in A. The adjoint identity is correct with the stated conjugate-linear-first convention.

## 2. Integer polarization

The displayed identity
theta_(x+beta*y)-theta_x-N(beta)*theta_y =
bar(beta)*theta_(x,y)+beta*theta_(y,x)
is correct. Summing over the dual system yields I because beta+bar(beta)=1. Norm(beta) is a nonnegative rational integer. Consequently I belongs to the INTEGER span of finitely many rank-one operators on A. There is no hidden division by 2.

This is the step that ordinary trace-integral lattices need not satisfy: their lack of self-duality is a genuine obstruction. The imaginary-quadratic trace-one element also matters; the same proof cannot be copied unchanged to a real base with trivial involution.

## 3. Positive reserve

Choose any K-basis in A and identify the complexification with an orthonormal complex space. Its column matrix B has H=B*B in Herm_n(K), so I=B H^(-1) B*. The residual H^(-1)-epsilon*sum(c_v c_v*) is positive for sufficiently small positive rational epsilon.

Hermitian LDL* elimination over K has positive diagonal entries in K intersect R=Q. After scaling its columns by positive integers, the corresponding vectors lie in A, and the coefficients remain positive rational. Clearing denominators therefore yields a genuine positive integer frame qI=sum c_v theta_v containing every prescribed vector. This is exact rational algebra, not an approximation argument.

The zero-dimensional case should be stated separately or excluded, since an empty support makes max over T undefined. It can be handled by adjoining the zero vector with coefficient 1; the application to number fields has positive dimension.

## 4. Every sufficiently large frame bound

Write M=kq+r, 0<=r<q. For the signed certificate I=sum z_v theta_v and positive reserve qI=sum c_v theta_v, the coefficients m_v=k c_v+r z_v obey
m_v >= k c_v-(q-1)max(0,-z_v).
The displayed ceiling threshold therefore ensures m_v>=R, including q=1. The proof is valid.

Once these two certificates are fixed, the same finite family works for every R, with threshold growing linearly in R. The certificates may depend very badly on the field and the lattice. No uniform control in a growing cyclotomic tower follows from mere existence.

## 5. Local-generator lemma

For y0 in A nonzero, (y0)A^(-1) is an integral ideal with finite support. Outside the finitely many rational primes beneath this support, y0 generates every local A_P. At each remaining rational p, the CRT map
A -> product_(P|p) A/P A
is onto. Since A is invertible, each quotient is one-dimensional over the residue field. Choosing nonzero residue in each factor gives y_p with v_P(y_p)=v_P(A) simultaneously for every P|p.

Thus one finite set S works at all rational primes. If E/K is Galois and A is stable, each K-automorphism preserves the normalized valuations. Relative to local ideal generators alpha_P, all ratios sigma(y_p)/alpha_P are units. Identifying these local normalizations with a common complex periodFactor still needs the source normalization theorem.

## 6. Existing upstream Weil-data theorem

For E0=K Q(zeta_q), E=F E0 and 229|q, this theorem applies with induced CM type. Its hypothesis only excludes relative ramification at primes fixed by conjugation. The unramified fact is sound: only 229 ramifies in the S3 splitting field F of X^3-4X+1; inertia there is the order-two subgroup generated by a transposition. Its intersection with Gal(F/Q(sqrt229))=A3 is trivial. Since Q(sqrt229) is contained in Q(zeta_q), unramifiedness persists after base change. Hence the relative different in E/E0 is the unit ideal.

This also proves

and rootdisc(E)=rootdisc(E0). It permits extension of the old finite idele alpha, rather than the arbitrary half-order selection made by the generic theorem. The old different generator remains a generator after extension; this is a verified property of this field, not a general assumption about nonabelian fields.

## 7. Exact period normalization caveat

The source vacuumRoot is defined as the PRINCIPAL f-th root of chiFin(alpha)^(-1):

- New/Derivatives/Field/WeilData.lean:469–475.

If E/E0 has degree 3, extension of alpha and norm-pullback of the character give
chiFin_E(alpha_E)=chiFin_E0(alpha_0)^3,
and f_E=3 f_0. The old vacuumRoot is a valid 3f_0-th root of the new quantity. But recomputing the principal 3f_0-th root need not give the same complex number because the principal argument can wrap.

Two valid formulations:
1. Specify the inherited old vacuumRoot as the new normalization. It satisfies the required power equation exactly.
2. Keep a newly computed principal root and state that its ratio to the old root is a root of unity.

In formulation 2 all archimedean and p-adic absolute values remain the same, including after every field embedding. Therefore all norm assertions and HasUnitLinearForm assertions pull up exactly. Literal equality of principal-root values is not automatic.

The Chowla–Selberg unit is attached to the fixed K-curve and can be retained, so the same qualification applies to periodFactor.

## 8. The two old unit witnesses suffice

The source gives only two candidate vectors, 1 and zeta*dualVector(phi(q)-1), and for every p-adic embedding one of them makes every CM-coordinate coefficient a unit:

- New/Derivatives/Vacuum/ShortVectors.lean:557–584,
  exists_hasUnitLinearForm.
- New/Derivatives/Vacuum/TightFrames.lean:324–327,
  norm_iota_periodFactor.

Every new CM embedding restricts to an old one. Both vectors lie in A0 and hence in A0 O_E. With inherited normalization (or its root-of-unity equivalent), their old unit equalities remain true for every new embedding. Include both in the prescribed finite S of the projective-frame theorem. This gives qualitative frames of every sufficiently large bound, with arbitrary required minimum multiplicity AND a suitable unit witness at every p-adic embedding.

Still missing is the estimate putting the required frame bounds, vector sizes and multiplicities inside the actual source regime (h<=b and M=p0*p1 with M in [b^20,b^22]). The qualitative threshold cannot be silently substituted for that quantitative hypothesis.

## 9. Remaining boundaries

- This application strengthens the earlier synthesis: existence of suitable fractional Weil data and qualitative coprime frames need no longer be listed as wholly missing.
- It does not yield the prescribed quantitative frame estimates.
- It does not yield the odd-cocycle bound, new local crystalline-defect estimates, quantitative nonabelian split level-prime selection, or the final spanning/covolume comparison.
- Neither nonabelian GRH nor a new general Selmer or K-group theorem follows.
