## The hidden-form question

Primary source: [Article/sections/spn.tex](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/00cadb961bbe77c74e5d082c4948f8884d58a970/Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis/Article/sections/spn.tex), especially `spn:thm:wide`, `spn:thm:wide-sample`, and `spn:q:symplectic`.

The source gives exact laws for matrix compressions with specified source and target forms. In the wide Lagrangian setting, admissible kernels must be isotropic for the source form. Its open question explicitly includes joint observations and an unknown source form. The current `rectangular_symplectic_blocks`, `uniform_symplectic_blocks`, `grh_uniform_extensions_2026-11-27`, and `grh-two-open-questions` packets extend rank and defect distributions, or particular known-source regimes. They do not contain the shared uniformly hidden-form experiment and additive recovery threshold proved here.

The new paper fixes a concrete case where each full-row-rank matrix has a two-dimensional kernel. It proves that the conditional law given the kernel has the same uniform fibre in the planted and null experiments. Thus the kernel is a sufficient statistic, and the hidden-form learning model is an exact transfer of the source construction.

| Issue | Established source material | Advance in this package |
|---|---|---|
| One known-source observation | Exact compression law and isotropy constraint | Exact equality of the one-observation *mixture* law with the uniform law |
| Shared unknown source | Identified as an open direction | Sharp sample threshold at binomial(n,2)−1, with a bounded accuracy window |
| Information lower bound | No matching hidden-mixture threshold found | Total variation tends to zero and optimal exact recovery fails below the threshold |
| Constructive recovery | Linear constraints are available | Gaussian elimination succeeds above the same threshold, uniformly in the source form |
| Critical window | Not treated for this experiment | Exact limiting chi-square divergence q^a/c_q at a fixed additive offset a |

The bridge retains full-row-rank conditioning. An unconditioned matrix threshold, larger growing kernel dimensions, and the complete limiting distribution in the critical window remain outside the theorem.

## The higher-order positive-filter question

Primary source: [Article/sections/fil.tex](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/00cadb961bbe77c74e5d082c4948f8884d58a970/Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis/Article/sections/fil.tex), especially `fil:thm:split`, `fil:thm:differential`, and `fil:sec:open`.

The source proves the unique analytic shortest positive filter near a repeated frequency, allows frequency splitting with combined orders at coincidences, and gives the width N*pi/mean + O(relative_radius^3/mean). It also supplies a first-variation formula at reflected configurations. Its open questions ask for the first nonzero higher term and its sign for asymmetric clusters. The current half-dyadic, zero-filter tradeoff, and zero-stencil additions address different optimization regimes; they do not give the sixth-order coefficient derived here.

The new paper re-proves the local analytic optimizer in independent polynomial coefficient coordinates. This is essential: ordinary root coordinates alone would not justify the auxiliary coefficient path used in the second variation. A positive dual certificate proves global optimality among all probability measures, not merely among nearby atomic candidates.

Mirror stationarity puts the centered width excess in the square of the ideal generated by odd elementary symmetric coordinates. Its first possible monomial is e_3^2, of weighted degree six. An exact second-variation calculation gives its coefficient N*pi^5/[20(N−1)(N−2)]. Since sum(delta_j^3)=3e_3 when the mean is fixed, this proves the coefficient in the paper's main formula, with a remainder of order eight. For N=3 or 4, e_3 is the only odd coordinate and gives the exact local sign and equality characterization.

The theorem does not make the local neighborhood explicit. Nor does it settle every direction with vanishing cubic moment for N≥5. Repeated entries prescribe filter derivative constraints; arithmetic zero multiplicity is a separate issue.

## Related primary literature checked

The geometric incidence and positive-filter subjects predate this work. The proofs and novelty claims are limited accordingly.

1. I. Cardinali and L. Giuzzi, *Minimum distance of symplectic Grassmann codes*, Linear Algebra and its Applications 488 (2016), 124–134. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.05456), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2015.09.031). Its alternating-form intersection counts and eigenspace analysis are closely related to the pair-incidence ingredient. The count is not claimed as new; the statistical experiment, sharp additive threshold, and critical-window moment are the extensions here.
2. R. J. Blok, *The generating rank of the symplectic line-Grassmannian*, Beiträge zur Algebra und Geometrie 44 (2003), 575–580. [Journal page](https://www.maths.tcd.ie/EMIS/journals/BAG/vol.44/no.2/26.html). This concerns generating rank, rather than the shared-hidden-form random observation threshold.
3. I. Cardinali and L. Giuzzi, *Enumerative coding for line polar Grassmannians with applications to codes*, Finite Fields and Their Applications 46 (2017), 107–138. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ffa.2017.03.005). This is related finite-geometric coding work, not a source for the present recovery theorem.
4. E. Crain, W. Singhose and W. Seering, *Derivation and properties of convolved and simultaneous two-mode input shapers*, IFAC Proceedings Volumes 29 (1996), 7278–7283. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-6670(17)58856-4). The authors' [MIT publication list](https://web.mit.edu/shaping/www/onlinepapers.html) confirms the paper. The publisher full text was not accessible in this session.
5. W. Singhose, E. Crain and W. Seering, *Convolved and simultaneous two-mode input shapers*, IEE Proceedings—Control Theory and Applications 144 (1997), 515–520. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1049/ip-cta:19971439). This is established two-mode filter work cited by the supplied source; the publisher full text was not accessible in this session.

Targeted searches included hidden alternating/symplectic form recovery from isotropic subspaces; random spanning in symplectic Grassmann codes; positive input shaping near coincident frequencies; higher-order width expansions; and the titles above. They did not locate either exact new statement. This is a bounded literature check, not certification of worldwide priority. A publication submission should carry out a broader bibliographic review.
