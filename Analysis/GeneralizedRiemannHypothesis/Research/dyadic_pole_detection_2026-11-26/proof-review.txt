INDEPENDENT PROOF REVIEW
Dyadic pole detection
26 November 2026

Scope
-----
A separate reasoning pass checked the mathematics against the stated formulas.
The code in code/verify_results.py was executed successfully. It requires only
Python, Sympy, and mpmath, and performs no network access.

Even number of dyadic filters
-----------------------------
For every even N>=2, put L=2^(N-1), and let r in (0,1) satisfy r+r^L=1.
The exact constant sqrt(1-r+r^2) and the two equality points
r exp(+-2*pi*i/3) were independently verified.

The proof checks included:
* All iterates lie in the open left half-plane when their filtered moduli
  are at most the proposed constant, which is strictly less than 1.
* The argument recurrence alpha_(j+1)=pi-2*alpha_j is valid with those
  branch restrictions.
* For even N, L*alpha_0+alpha_(N-1)=(L+1)*pi/3.
* The logarithmic angle function has a convex interval as its domain,
  is symmetric about half log(d), and is strictly concave.
* Both points used in weighted Jensen's inequality belong to that domain.
* Equality in strict Jensen fixes the radius; equality in the angle bound
  fixes precisely the two stated directions.
* The displayed points attain the bound for every intermediate filter.

Three filters
-------------
The quartic u^4-2*u^2-3*u+2=0 has a unique root in (1/2,13/25).
The squared sharp constant is u+u^2. Its equality points are
(u^2-1 +- i*sqrt(u+1))/2.

Two independent global arguments were checked: the interval feasibility
reduction in the main proof, and the weighted-objective Hessian certificate.
The latter proves strong convexity on the convex domain containing every
possible improvement over 1. The stationary equations and the Hessian
determinant decomposition were checked exactly modulo the quartic.

Transform and stability checks
------------------------------
A forward shift creates an entire compact-endpoint correction in the
Laplace transform. That correction does not change pole residues.
For the repository's Mellin normalization the symbol is
1+a^k exp(k*h*(s-1)); the shift by 1 is necessary.

Positive filter coefficients preserve the sign of residues at real points.
Classical one-sided Landau pole exclusion therefore applies to each
filtered transform under the hypotheses in the paper. Already the first
two filters have no common zero by the displayed Bezout identity.
The longer families improve the sharp quantitative constant.

The optimal uniform error amplification 1/c_N holds for arbitrary
estimators, not only linear estimators: at an extremal symbol value, zero
observations can arise from two opposite residues with admissible bounded
errors. The selector estimator supplies the matching upper bound.

Finite-offset approximations require bounds on the analytic regular part
and on the compact-endpoint integrals. No universal finite-offset
stability estimate follows from a residue statement alone.

Executed checks
---------------
25 exact checks passed, including the algebraic certificate, root
isolation, the concavity derivative, and the Bezout identity.
High-precision constants/extremizers were computed for N=2,4,6,8,10,12
and for the three-filter case.
Seven deterministic grids, each with 14,640 points, found no contradiction.
Those finite grids are sanity checks and are not global proofs.
See data/verification.json and data/constants.csv for the exact outputs
and environment versions.

Limits
------
No full Lean proof of the new theorem was produced.
The upstream RH/Dirichlet-GRH repository was inspected at a pinned commit;
its complete build and independent-kernel replay were not performed here.
The review establishes correctness of the supplied arguments to the extent
of independent mathematical checking. It is not a certificate of publication
priority. In particular, the N=2 constant is known prior work, and the
qualitative Bezout/Landau transfer uses classical principles.
