The reading guide identifies the comparison of lattice bounds in MainInequality
and the passage through a one-sided smoothed estimate to the Dirichlet theorem
in OneSidedBound. The arithmetical argument in the present paper uses the
Dirichlet conclusion in the explicit formula. Its combinatorial proof is independent.

56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679, and dependence on propext,

comparison checks. Our work inspected this interface and used the theorem as

verification procedures. The new paper is a mathematical proof with finite
certificates. We did not run an upstream Lean build or formalize the new proof.

ProveIt collection
https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/tree/2c45d198070f93209d6f0faa2d387ef0a2c4e72c/Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis
The index at the cited revision lists 61 research packets. The filing operation
did not rerun or formally check their mathematical arguments. The review of
overlap used their README descriptions and those of the 42 additional ZIPs in
the directory's docs/incoming. Relevant comparisons appeared in two packets.
The first optimizes the mean of a nonnegative Sato–Tate character polynomial.
The second studies one-sided character-weighted prime estimates. These
provided a reason to look for an optimal polynomial specific to Hamming caps.

Requested background
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_hypothesis
The retrieved article describes location of nontrivial zeros and their role
in prime counting. The paper uses the pinned theorem statement and the
degree-one explicit formula for its analytic input.
