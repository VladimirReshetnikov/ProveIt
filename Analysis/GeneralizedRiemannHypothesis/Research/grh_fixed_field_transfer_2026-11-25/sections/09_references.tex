\section{Sources and literature}
\label{sec:sources}
The mathematical source audit is anchored in a pinned commit, not a floating branch. All prior-report links in this manuscript likewise identify the pinned snapshot. The following literature entries supply context for fixed divisors and torsion specialization; the new proofs are complete in Sections~\ref{sec:scalar}--\ref{sec:multi}.

The upstream torsion file explicitly relates its argument to Kriz--Nordentoft and Serban. Horizontal $p$-adic $L$-functions provide an arithmetic setting where torsion-valued interpolation is relevant \cite{kriz}; Serban studies torsion points and formal multiplicative groups \cite{serban}. These relationships motivate further applications. No new improvement to a stated arithmetic nonvanishing theorem in those papers is asserted here.
