{\Large Sharp torsion specialization and the\\remaining arithmetic barriers\par}

{\large Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov\par}
\vspace{13mm}
\begin{abstract}
We examine the internal methods of \texttt{lsernau/grh-lean}, together with 31 earlier research archives in \texttt{ProveIt/docs/incoming}. The principal advance is a solution to the earlier reports' multivariable specialization question: for integral polynomials over a fixed finite extension of $\Q_p$, separate exact valuation bounds can be preserved at one torsion tuple with an explicit bound on the order exponents that has no iterated exponential tower. The proof uses finitely many multiplicative substitutions, monic division, and Gauss-valued Lagrange interpolation, keeping the coefficient field fixed until the final scalar specialization.

We also determine the exact zero-tolerance obstruction cost over every finite coefficient field. It depends only on the $p$-part of its absolute ramification index, and is attained by explicit binomials. Both results extend to integral formal power series. These are local theorems proved here, independent of RH, inspired by the repository's simultaneous twisting argument.

For transfers to major global conjectures, the audit identifies a quantitative requirement sometimes hidden by the final inequality: almost all target dimensions must be spanned, unless auxiliary completion vectors become much cheaper. The new local results improve one interface of the proof; they do not supply a nonabelian Frobenius-sensitive lattice or resolve a further named global conjecture.
\end{abstract}
