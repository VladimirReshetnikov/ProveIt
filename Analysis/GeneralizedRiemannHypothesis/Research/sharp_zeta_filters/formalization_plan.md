# Lean integration blueprint (not checked Lean code)

The source's public RH theorem is `riemannHypothesis : RiemannHypothesis` in `src/Main.lean`, under the vendored package's Lean 4.35.0 toolchain. Do not mix with ProveIt's root Mathlib version. The report uses no new GRH axiom; it has one explicitly RH-dependent section, the prime-ramp application.

Suggested modules, in dependency order:

1. **ExponentialRecurrence**: a sequence `a : ℕ → ℂ` annihilated by ∏(shift − zᵢ), for a finite list of roots with repetitions. Define its denominator polynomial and finite coefficient identities for the generating series. Prove the coefficient bound `‖a k‖ ≤ n * 2^n * (Nat.choose (k+n-1) (n-1)) * maxInitial`, for `k ≥ n` and `‖zᵢ‖ ≤ 1`. The proof is algebraic and accepts coincident roots.
2. **IntegratedExtrapolation**: for `p t = ∑ Pⱼ(t) exp(λⱼ t)` with `∑ (deg Pⱼ + 1) ≤ n` and real parts in `[-a,a]`, prove report (7) using the rescaled sampled sequence, monotone change of variable, and sum bound. Handle the zero polynomial separately and permit padding to the advertised order by zero coefficients.
3. **PositiveMomentCompression**: assume an even positive integrable density `w` obeying report (8), a real finite-dimensional reflection-invariant space `V` containing `1`, and exponential-polynomial bounds. Prove (9) by the explicit estimates. Define the tail functional on the restriction subspace in real `L¹(w dt)`, extend by Hahn–Banach, then represent by a real `L∞` function with norm ≤ `exp(-100*n)`. Verify even symmetrization, normalization, and exact moments. If the requisite `L¹` duality interface is missing, prove the finite-measure Radon–Nikodym argument separately (bounded functional on indicators yields an absolutely continuous signed measure with bounded density).
4. **XiKernel**: define the positive theta kernel and establish its bilateral transform as `xi (1/2 + I*z)`. Prove the explicit lower and upper theta bounds. Show all zeros are in the horizontal strip |im z|<1/2 by classical zeta theory, count disk zeros with multiplicity, and apply layer 3 to real and imaginary components of the derivative moments and two extra exponentials. This module is RH-independent.
5. **SupportLowerBound**: apply Jensen's formula to the entire characteristic function; use the classical Riemann–von Mangoldt count and partial summation to obtain the leading constant. For the stopband variant, formalize the harmonic-measure estimate on the semicircle (subtended angle π/2) and bounded holomorphic two-constants theorem. Obtain the upper bound via the two-state Markov chain, or use an explicit positive Bessel-series expansion of its transform instead.
6. **PrimeRampAverage**: import the source theorem (or parameterize by `hRH : RiemannHypothesis` and discharge with `riemannHypothesis`). Establish the ramp Mellin formula and contour shift to Re s = -1/2, with residual at s=0. Use the absolutely summable zero series and exact derivative moments from layer 4. The uniform bound on the tilted density is crucial to avoid an extra logarithmic factor in the contour remainder.

Proof obligations likely requiring additional analytic libraries: the theta Mellin identity in this normalization, Riemann–von Mangoldt with multiplicity, Jensen's formula, two-constants theorem, and Mellin contour estimates. The proof of the recurrence and tail bound can be checked independently before these are available. No claim of a fully elaborated statement or a kernel-checked formalization is made.
