\noindent\textbf{Abstract.}
We analyze the methods of \texttt{lsernau/grh-lean} at a pinned revision,
compare all 52 archives in the current ProveIt incoming snapshot, and develop
three complete extensions beyond standard consequences of RH and Dirichlet GRH.
First, we strengthen an incoming solution of McNew's density-separation question to
$\beta-\alpha>0.0001351$, with two exact finite verifiers and a replayable certificate.
Second, over $K=\Q_p((pu)^{1/p})$ for every odd prime $p$ and every $p$-adic unit $u$,
we determine the exact simultaneous torsion-specialization obstruction at every
primitive $p$-power order. Third, for two-dimensional Cayley graph towers with an
even number $m$ of steps and $p>2m$, $p\equiv1\pmod{2m}$, we prove the exact maximum
$m(p+1+m)(p^n-1)/(p-1)-2n$ for the $p$-adic valuation of the spanning-tree count
at every layer. The latter two results extend explicitly unresolved questions in
the supplied reports. The source audit also corrects an earlier overly broad
finite-precision objection: the repository's twisting theorem controls the full
divisible Selmer module. A new proof of nonabelian RH, BSD, Vandiver, or a new
algebraic $K$-group vanishing statement is not obtained; we specify the arithmetic
estimates still needed for those transfers.

\vspace{0.15cm}
\noindent\textbf{Status of the mathematics.}
The new statements here have human-readable proofs and exact computational checks.
They are not newly formalized or kernel-checked in Lean. The full upstream Lean
build was not repeated. Novelty claims are relative to the inspected incoming
corpus; historical publication priority has not been established.
