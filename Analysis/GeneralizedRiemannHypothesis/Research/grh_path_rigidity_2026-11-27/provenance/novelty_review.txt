Scope of repository review
--------------------------
All 21 section sources of the 689-page article were fetched at the pinned
ProveIt commit and matched against their Git blob hashes. The section inventory,
statement headings and open-question lists were examined, with detailed reading
of the directly relevant analytic, Li-process, reconstruction, graph and spanning
arguments. This is not an independent verification of all 869 results in the
merged article. The article itself describes a merge whose claims were not
independently recomputed as part of that merge.

New results in this package relative to that source
--------------------------------------------------
1. The explicit Poisson jump-band decoder of real character values at every
   prime, almost-sure path recovery, exact variance constant and independent
   Gaussian prime-coordinate limits.
2. Equality versus mutual singularity of the full path laws, classified by
   conjugacy of primitive characters; finite-grid equivalence; recovery from
   every interval and from the initial germ.
3. A quantitative lower bound on the Hellinger information in one small-jump
   band, with an explicit separating-prime constant and a binary error bound.
4. Unique product-weight and square-root-network optimizers for worst and mean
   zero displacement, explicit square-root conductances, contrast compression,
   and exact/quantitative stability identities.
5. Feasibility and the complete integer spectrum of possible total orbit ranks
   at fixed (d,s,u,v), with explicit cyclic Fourier constructions and equality
   classification.

Classical ingredients are not claimed as new
-------------------------------------------
Landau's explicit formula, zero counting, Dirichlet's theorem, character
orthogonality, Levy-Khintchine, Poisson moment/cumulant identities,
Borel-Cantelli, Gaussian convergence by characteristic functions, Poisson
Hellinger affinity, matrix Cauchy-Schwarz, matrix square roots and Fourier
equalization are standard inputs. The main paper proves the elementary
probabilistic steps and states its analytic inputs precisely.
