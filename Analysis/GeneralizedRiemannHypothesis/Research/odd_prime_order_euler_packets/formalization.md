# Lean integration roadmap (not a verified Lean file)

The finite geometry and analytic portions can be separated. No new Lean declarations are claimed compiled in this archive.

1. Define `G` as a finite-dimensional vector space over `ZMod ℓ`, `V` as its dual, and an integer function `c : G → ℤ` of total sum zero. Define signed projection counts `R_v t = ∑ a with v(a)=t, c a`, positive mass `m`, and `S = {v | ∃ t, R_v t ≠ 0}`. This definition avoids analytic functions and complex roots entirely.
2. Formalize the anchored identity by interchanging finite sums. For a nonzero `b-a`, evaluation at `b-a` is a surjective linear functional on the dual; each fiber has `|V|/ℓ` elements. Then `∑ v, R_v (v a) = (|V| - |V|/ℓ) * c a`. Apply the mass bound `|R_v(t)| ≤ m` to obtain the exact integer inequality. Scalar invariance gives divisibility of `|S|` by `ℓ-1`.
3. Equality implies all summands in the nonnegative finite deficit vanish. For each positive or negative support point this forces the collapse of its entire side into a single projection bin on `S`. Characterize `S` through annihilators of the spans of within-packet differences, then use the dimension/cardinality formula to identify affine cosets.
4. Prove the union bound by adding hyperplanes outside the first, and equality using disjointness of the added pieces. Formalize the planar criterion and the two dihedral constructions separately. The split case needs a cyclic subgroup of the multiplicative group of the base field; the nonsplit case uses its quadratic extension, norm-one subgroup, and Frobenius. The exceptional case can be encoded as a closed finite computation over 7776 choices and checked with a suitable verified decision procedure.
5. Connect projection counts to traces via the cyclotomic polynomial of prime order, and to actual Dirichlet characters through a surjective evaluation map from units modulo `Q` onto the finite dual. The unconditional density result then uses the prime number theorem in progressions.
6. For the effective theorem, isolate the smoothed character-sum estimate as an analytic lemma quantified over a fixed smooth test function, uniform in the modulus and character. Its proof requires a Mellin explicit formula and uniform zero counts. Import the vendored package's `Main` in its own pinned Lean/Mathlib environment, and use `LFunction_eq_zero_re` and `LFunction_ne_zero_of_half_lt_re` for the zero location and boundary. The finite Fourier expansion and minorants then complete the effective theorem. Formalizing the explicit formula is a nontrivial remaining step, not an assumption silently discharged by Lean.
