2. Upstream RH and Dirichlet GRH

The vendored release is lsernau/grh-lean revision
72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207.
Its statements include Mathlib RiemannHypothesis and the stated critical-line
results for primitive and imprimitive Dirichlet L-functions. This package
does not extend that scope to general Artin or automorphic L-functions.

The primary verification record inspected was:
https://math.mit.edu/~drew/shortgrh/
The record was updated 27 November 2026, 17:27 UTC and reports builds,
statement comparisons, kernel replay and independent proof-checker runs.
These reports were inspected; none of those Lean or external-checker builds
was rerun here. The new papers are ordinary mathematical proofs, not Lean
formalizations.

The proof program combines smoothed prime sums and positivity, an integral
target-lattice argument, and an analytic continuation/Landau endgame. The
continuations in this package use two structures developed in its downstream
work: finite-contrast spectral inversion and finite-quotient allocation laws.

3. Five-point paper: existing results credited

Merged source:
Article/sections/zer.tex
Labels zer:thm:stencilbank, zer:thm:fourpoint, zer:thm:missingfreq;
also zer:lem:ramp and zer:sec:coalesce for the prime explicit formula.

The merged article already provides the finite-bank Mellin criterion,
the four-point classification, recovery from any two distinct four-point
shapes, the one-frequency tangent description and its two-dilation repair,
and a five-point example with infinitely many blind frequencies.

Incoming source at the same pin:
docs/incoming/dirichlet-coupled-zeros-and-prime-variances.zip
Member: five_node_filters/five_node_frequencies.tex
Title: The missing frequencies of five-point prime variances
Its Theorem 1 already proves the quadratic/phase criterion and the upper
bound of two missing positive frequencies. Theorem 2 proves attainment
by genuine five-weight filters and a stronger local density statement.
None of those results is claimed as new in this package.

New relative to these inspected sources:
- Any two distinct normalized five-point response shapes have disjoint
  real blind sets, hence jointly recover the spectral counting limit.
- The exact three-line criterion for a uniform joint finite-symbol gap.
- Uniqueness of the normalized infinitely blind shape.
- The full positive anti-periodic tangent classification for that shape
  and its universal two-dilation repair.
- The extension to two invisible modes, with at most four dilation averages.
- The exact feasible shape domain and global smallness of defective shapes.

The rational two-frequency certificate is an explicit independent witness;
it is not the first proof that the two-frequency bound is attained.
Classical Mellin-Tauberian theory is credited in the paper, including Wiener
and Bingham--Inoue. Uniform separation of finite symbols is not asserted to
give a full noisy inversion theorem.

4. Parity paper: existing results credited

Merged source:
Article/sections/dir.tex
Labels dir:lem:disjoint-iid, dir:prop:sparseexc,
dir:thm:disjoint-growing and dir:thm:disjoint-mixing.
The last growing-block lifting theorem is a stated arithmetic input,
not reproved here. The entropy calculation for fixed blocks is inherited;
the paper makes its dependence on the number of blocks explicit.

Incoming source at the same pin:
docs/incoming/grh_extensions_2026-11-27(2).zip
Member: grh_extensions/prime_product_limits.tex
Title: Every sparse prime-product transition is compound Poisson

That report controls one sparse sampled product in a bounded quotient and
proves a uniform finite-population expansion in a bounded expected-defect
window. Its classification expressly excludes dense quotas. It does not
give the full parity-vector comparison when the number of blocks diverges.
Encoding the vector in C_2^r gives all nonidentity labels, outside its stated
defect-count hypothesis; translating one block to the identity still leaves
rn-n defects, more than half the population for r>2.

New relative to these inspected sources:
- Total variation equivalence to parity-conditioned independent signs for
  every sequence n_r >= 2 under only a_r=(1-2s/(rn))^n -> 0.
- The exact parameter sqrt(r)*a_r, including fixed capacities and sampling
  fractions tending to one half.
- The finite-capacity transition location and the intermediate-capacity
  correction when n/(log r)^2 has a finite positive limit.
- The resulting joint quadratic-character prime-product profile under the
  source's logarithmic-block hypotheses.

The Gaussian shape for ordinary hypercube walks is classical; the paper
cites Diaconis--Graham--Morrison (1990). Alves et al., arXiv:2410.00831v2,
study a related full-label comparison with a fixed number of suits; their
observed object and asymptotic regime differ from the present theorem.

5. Literature and verification limits

Targeted primary-source searches covered Mellin cancellation/inverse-filter
theorems, Allan-variance ambiguity, hypercube profiles, parity observations,
and sampling without replacement. Such searches do not certify global
historical priority. Publication novelty remains a claim for specialist
assessment, separate from the self-contained proofs given here.

Internal mathematical reviews checked the five-point algebra, tangent
argument, finite-population conditioning argument, and the arithmetic
compatibility/fibre counts. These are internal reviews, not external peer
review or formal proof checking.

The rational certificate is an exact existence argument. The symbolic
checks establish the displayed finite identities. The finite parity
enumerations check examples; they are not proofs of the asymptotic claims.
