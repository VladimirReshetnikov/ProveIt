FOUNDATIONAL SCOPE

Article/sections/src.tex identifies the input as RH and GRH for Dirichlet
L-functions. That scope is retained here; it does not automatically supply
general automorphic or Artin GRH, or simplicity of all critical-line zeros.

The upstream formalization is lsernau/grh-lean at commit
72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207, using Lean 4.35.0 and Mathlib
56a00585. Its README and Andrew Sutherland's independent verification page
were inspected. The latter reports completed kernel replay, statement and
axiom checks, and acceptance by multiple external checkers for the five

Primary links:
  https://github.com/lsernau/grh-lean

BRANCH 1: SPARSE POLYNOMIALS IN LOCAL DISKS

Read inputs:
  Article/sections/smd.tex
  Research/padic-support-rank/report.tex
  Research/sparse-padic-roots-and-graph-towers/fewnomial/paper.tex

The article develops moment normalization and recurrence arguments. The rank
packet gives exact support-sensitive root counts in closed exponential disks
for at most p exponents. The later packet gives sharp open-disk results
through p+2 terms, including the jump to p^2 at t=p+2.

New development in papers/sparse_roots.tex:

1. An exact minimum-valuation-basis formula for arbitrary support size and
   every positive disk depth. Lenstra's 1999 local cutoff reduces the
   otherwise degree-dependent Taylor matrix to a uniform finite matrix.

2. An explicit finite precision P such that the multiset of exponent
   residues modulo p^P determines the whole optimum profile above a fixed
   positive depth floor. Enumerating those residue multisets computes the
   least possible uniform local geometric root bound, not just an upper
   bound. Rational witnesses are constructed by a dual basis; perturbation
   and the Chinese remainder theorem impose full support and Eisenstein
   irreducibility for at least two terms.

3. Frobenius compression gives a smaller finite cutoff valid simultaneously
   throughout the exponential region, including its open boundary limit.

4. For odd p and p+2 <= t <= 2p-1, the sharp open-disk optimum is
       p^2 + floor((t-p-2)/2).
   The p+2 endpoint agrees with the source. The further values extend it.

5. Exact binary profiles at t=5,6,7, with eight-root thresholds 4/3 and 3/2
   for six and seven terms respectively. Explicit squarefree integer
   polynomials certify both endpoints.

Prior work and limits:
  A degree-independent effective local upper bound at arbitrary positive
  depth is already in Lenstra's Theorem 3; that is not new. Valuated basis
  exchange is also standard linear algebra. The proposed contribution is
  exact optimization, the finite residue classification and its consequences,
  and the new sharp formulas. See literature_fewnomials.txt for source details.
  These counts are geometric roots in C_p. They do not solve Lenstra's
  separate global best-bound problem for roots in a specified finite
  extension K/Q_p throughout K^times. A closed formula for all p,t is not
  proved. The related six-term singular-modulus problem needs additional
  information about conjugates and is not resolved here.

BRANCH 2: FINITELY MANY TORSION TESTS AT POSITIVE TOLERANCE

Read input:
  Article/sections/cost.tex, especially the root reduction, fixed-cardinality
  theorem, and Question cost:q:cardinality(b).

The source proves a tower law when the tolerance is zero. It also treats
positive tolerance when the number of tests is unrestricted. It explicitly
asks for the asymptotic with a fixed number n of tests and fixed delta>0.

New development in papers/torsion_phase_transition.tex:
  For fixed p,h0,n and delta>0, as B tends to infinity,
      c B^n <= H_n(B,delta) <= C B^n (log B)^2 log log B.
  Hence log H_n(B,delta)/log B tends to n, and the minimum blocking budget
  through shell H is H^(1/n+o(1)). For n=1 the sharper order Theta(B) holds.
  H is the exponent in the torsion order p^H.

The new upper-bound step shifts accumulated costs by delta, uses a capped
one-root average, and controls increments of a product potential. An explicit
sequence of consecutive shell blocks supplies the matching lower exponent.
This resolves the growth-exponent aspect of the repository's question.
Whether the logarithmic loss can be removed for n>=2, whether a leading
constant exists, and the joint limit delta -> 0 remain open.

BRANCH 3: ATTEMPT ON A NAMED CANONICAL-LINE CONJECTURE

Read input:
  Article/sections/gra.tex, finite-width Ehrhart theorem and all-width question.

The relevant literature is Higashitani--Kummer--Michalek's interlacing and
canonical-line program for Ehrhart polynomials of symmetric edge polytopes,
and Koelbl's extensions for complete multipartite graphs:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07538
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02136

The pinned repository proves the complete-bipartite line property and the
specified interlacings through width 32, with a further line-property result
at width 33. Its certificates use positive Bezoutians. We investigated an
extension to arbitrary width, looking for a uniform positive representation
or an interlacing mechanism replacing the width-by-width certificates.
No all-width positivity argument was obtained. The finite-width certificates
do not imply the infinite family, so no resolution is claimed.
