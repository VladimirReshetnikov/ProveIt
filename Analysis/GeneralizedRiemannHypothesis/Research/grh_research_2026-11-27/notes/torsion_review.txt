Claim reviewed
For fixed prime p, starting shell h0, number n of integral p-adic
polynomials, and positive tolerance delta, the least universally sufficient
torsion exponent H_n(B,delta) satisfies
    c B^n <= H_n(B,delta) <= C B^n (log B)^2 log log B.
Thus log H_n(B,delta)/log B tends to n. Constants can depend on the fixed
parameters; the estimate is uniform over degrees and coefficient ramification.
This concerns the exponent h in order p^h, not the torsion order itself.

Scope and limitations
- No RH or GRH assumption enters the theorem or its proof.
- This answers the growth-exponent aspect of cost:q:cardinality(b) in the
  pinned repository. It is not a resolution of a prominent named conjecture.

  joint limits as delta tends to zero remain open in this note.
