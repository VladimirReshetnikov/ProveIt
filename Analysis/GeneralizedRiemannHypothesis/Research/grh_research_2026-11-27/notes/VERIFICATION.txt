REPRODUCTION RUN

Command, runnable from any working directory:
    python3 /path/to/archive/code/run_checks.py

Final packaged run: ALL ARCHIVE CHECKS PASSED.
Runtime: Python 3.12.14; integer, Fraction and finite-field arithmetic only.
All random sampling uses a fixed seed. No floating-point root approximation
is used. The machine-readable summary is results/reproduction_summary.json.

1. General all-depth determinant solver
   245 prescribed-support/depth cases compared with exhaustive determinants
   of the full ORIGINAL binomial matrix, without the quotient-algebra cutoff.
   Every solver witness was checked by direct expansion at X=1. In the
   exponential region the falling-factorial and power realizations were also
   compared. Cases include the convergence boundary and shallower disks.

2. Odd-prime sharp range
   1,540 exhaustive residue-multiplicity profiles at p=5, t=7,8,9.
   44 attaining profiles across primes 3 through 17.
   400 deterministically seeded residue profiles.
   233,770 evaluations of the explicit finite-field functional.
   An explicit nine-term integer polynomial at p=5, depth 53/208, has last
   Newton minimum at index 26. The coefficient list is Eisenstein at 2;
   it is therefore irreducible over Q and has simple roots.

3. Binary profiles
   The two explicit six- and seven-term degree-eight polynomials were
   expanded exactly at 1. Their Newton polygons give the claimed depth
   groups and retain all eight roots at the closed endpoints 4/3 and 3/2.
   Modular Bezout identities certify squarefreeness.

4. Universal residue enumeration
   Every one of the 84 multisets of six residues modulo 4 was enumerated.
   The largest open binary-disk count is 8. There are 60 profiles with
   maximum 5 and 24 profiles with maximum 8.
   Code review checked the enumeration count, realization by distinct
   integer exponents, translation invariance, and refusal of partial answers
   when the configured profile budget is exceeded.

5. Fixed-support depth transitions
   For E={0,1,5,6,10,15,20,25,30} at p=5, the exact profile is:
       (1/4,5/19]    26
       (5/19,4/15]   25
       (4/15,1/2]    10
       (1/2,infinity) 8
   Transition calculations use rational basis inequalities, not sampled
   floating-point depths.

6. Positive-tolerance torsion theorem
   267 exact greedy shell-capacity instances.
   52 capped-root-average instances.
   176 explicit late-block constructions.
   All 16,384 two-member subset-root packets at p=2 on shells 1,2,3, with
   tolerance 1/4. Of these, 5,363 block every test point, and the minimum
   cost among these subset-root packets is 2.

WHAT FINITE CHECKS DO AND DO NOT ESTABLISH

These calculations verify the finite examples and help detect implementation
and proof errors. They do not prove an asymptotic law by extrapolation, prove
an arbitrary-prime assertion by sampling, or establish publication priority.
Those mathematical claims are justified in the written arguments, with the
classical input and remaining open questions expressly identified.
