INDEPENDENT INTERNAL MATHEMATICAL REVIEW
27 November 2026

This is an internal parallel-agent review, not external peer review or a proof-assistant check.

1. General sparse-root theorem

The minimum-basis formula was checked by reducing the coefficient row space using a basis with least weighted determinant valuation. The resulting identity columns certify its integral lattice. A column survives reduction exactly when it belongs to some minimum basis, so the largest such index is both an upper bound for every Weierstrass degree and an attained value.

Rational witnesses do not require rational approximations to arbitrary algebraic coefficients: if B_J=C_J diag(b^j/j!), each normalized row is a scalar j!b^{-j} times a rational functional in the original exponent algebra. Lagrange interpolation recovers rational sparse coefficients. Perturbing in a sufficiently small rational p-adic ball preserves the root count and permits all support coefficients to be nonzero and all roots simple. Rational-coefficient assertions at a center other than one require the center to be rational, or require enlarging the coefficient field to contain the center.

The all-depth falling-factorial representation was checked. Its first t columns are a unit triangular integer basis. The stated residue precision excludes every determinant valuation beyond the truncation: the factorial contribution is largest on the last t indices, while every t-subset has index sum at least 0+...+(t-1). Remainder coefficients are integral polynomials in the exponents, so congruent multisets give identical truncated determinant valuations. This avoids dividing a Vandermonde determinant modulo p.

Lenstra-cutoff computation was checked against its defining inequality. For m in [p^j,p^{j+1}), a_k(m)<=k*j, so r*p^j-k*j is a valid lower bound. Once this exceeds the threshold and r*p^j*(p-1)>k, the lower bound increases forever. The finite search implemented in lenstra_cutoff therefore misses no eligible index.

The sharper deep-disk cutoff was checked independently. Frobenius annihilates the exponent quotient modulo p after p^d>=t, repeated pth powers lift the identity to p^M, and collapsing higher base-p digits preserves the low-valuation moment while reducing its factorial weight. The strict depth assumption is essential. The t=1 case is treated separately by the code's H<=0 branch.

2. Independent computations

code/independent_binomial_check.py compares 245 fixed-seed small instances to exhaustive determinants of the FULL binomial coefficient matrix of F(1+Y), not the truncated quotient matrix used in the implementation. It includes shallow radii, the exponential boundary, the open exponential disk, and deeper radii at p=2,3,5. Every extremum agrees. Each rational witness is checked directly by its finite Newton coefficients, and the exponential and binomial methods agree on their common domain. The output is results/independent_binomial_check.json. These finite tests check implementations; the written argument supplies the general proof.

3. Explicit binary profiles

papers/binary_profile.tex proves the complete five-, six-, and seven-term binary profiles for depths r>1. The proof uses the cutoff and an explicit parity-algebra argument for five terms. All sharp upper-branch witnesses have degree eight. Their Newton polygons, exact supports, and modular Bezout squarefreeness certificates are checked by code/check_binary_profile.py; results/check_binary_profile.json records the output. The checker is standard-library Python and does not call a numerical root finder.

4. Torsion specialization theorem

The complete torsion_phase_transition manuscript was checked against the source packet definition and cost:q:cardinality(b). The root reduction, capped one-root average, normalized-loss inequality, exceptional-shell count, shifted product potential, telescope, logarithmic summation, and optimized upper bound are consistent. The lower construction's greedy residue counts, cyclotomic distance identity, rounding tail and recursive block budget were also checked. No mathematical gap was found. Its conclusion determines a growth exponent, B^{n+o(1)}, not a leading constant or the joint transition as tolerance tends to zero.

5. A named conjecture route investigated but not solved

The complete-bipartite canonical-line/interlacing question of Higashitani--Kummer--Michalek was examined via the repository's gra.tex, especially the short A,B-polynomial and Bezoutian reduction. Symbolic A,B pairs through width seven do not yield a simple three-term recurrence in width; a nonzero recurrence remainder already appears at the transition from short degree three to four. No all-width positivity proof was obtained. No claim to solve that conjecture is included.
