{\Huge\bfseries\color{navy} Beyond Dirichlet GRH\par}

{\LARGE Validated transfers, a sharper density certificate,\\[3pt]
and a concrete Hecke target\par}
\vspace{0.45in}
{\large Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov\par}
{\large 25 November 2026 (Pacific time)\par}
\vspace{0.45in}
\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The useful output of a new proof can include its internal tools, not only
consequences of its final theorem. We inspect the shortened Lean development
of RH and Dirichlet GRH, compare it with 39 earlier ProveIt archives, and
separate proved transfers from missing research steps.

The strongest concrete result is an independently rechecked counterexample,
already present in an incoming ProveIt report, to a published conjecture on
geometric-progression-free subsets of polynomial rings. It works over every
finite field and does not use RH or GRH. We improve its explicit binary
density margin from a guarantee of \(2^{-2^{494}}\) to
\(2^{-2^{476}}\), by replacing a first-moment truncation with a variance
bound. We also derive a hierarchy of kernel-frame moments and rank tails
from the proof's positive-type Fourier estimate. Finally, we give a fully
specified conditional route to RH for
\(\eta(z)\eta(23z)\), together with rigorous obstructions to an automatic
Dirichlet reduction. The new arithmetic estimate required for that route
is not proved here.
\end{abstract}
