1. THE INHERITED POLYNOMIAL-DENSITY COUNTEREXAMPLE

Archive: grh-methods-polynomial-density.zip
Relevant members: grh-methods/counterexample.tex, polynomials.tex,
small_pattern.tex, tilted.tex, supremum.tex; check_sphere.py,
check_small_pattern.py, check_tilted.py, check_polynomial_fibers.py.

The main counterexample to Asada et al. Conjecture 6.3 was independently reviewed
in detail. The paper argument is valid for nonconstant polynomial ratios and
upper density. It is inherited work, not newly discovered in this report. The
classical carry-free Behrend sphere argument is the connection to the internal
Lean lemma exists_three_term_free. The counterexample needs neither RH nor GRH.

The present report improves its explicit smooth-degree cutoff using the variance
of a sum of geometric random variables. This new quantitative refinement was
independently checked, including the exact q=2 integer. See density_review.txt and
the three data/prior_density_*.json files. No worldwide priority or new Lean
formalization is claimed.

2. A PRIOR POSITIVE-TYPE COMPRESSION TRANSFER

Archive: grh_sharp_torsion_and_symplectic_blocks.zip
Relevant member: grh_sharp_transfers/symplectic.tex.

This report already applies the Fourier estimate from
New/Spanning/SecondMoment/PositiveType.lean to ordered j-frames and to the full law
of a rectangular compression. Its general estimate is

  dTV(M,U) <= (1/2) sqrt(sum_j R_j(a,b) q^(nj) alpha_j beta_j),

where alpha_j and beta_j control pointwise j-frame stabilizer masses. It also
derives a uniform Sp_n compression regime when n-a-b tends to infinity and exact
fixed-deficit isotropic rank/total-variation limits. A new compression result must
be compared with this work. An exponential-nullity moment using only single-vector
stabilizer masses/profile is a different, potentially useful extraction; a first
general positive-type transfer claim would be inaccurate.

3. K-THEORY PREPARATION IS ALREADY ADVANCED

Archive: grh_method_transfers_2026-11-25 (2).zip
Relevant member: grh_method_transfers/sections/ktheory.tex.

This manuscript computes exact Bernoulli-unit screening for even e=54,...,80.
At e=62 and 64, corresponding to proposed K124 and K128 calculations, the two-field
numerator-product gcd is 4, with denominator primes only 3 and 7. It computes
negative comparison-space dimensions 550 and 605 in the Eisenstein setting,
1240 and 1360 in the Gaussian setting, and reconstructs two small Hecke operators.
It does not supply the large new K124/K128 Hecke certificates.

Archive: grh_exact_torsion_transfer_2026-11-25.zip
Relevant member: grh_exact_torsion_transfer/sections/targets.tex.

This report preserves the original strict-Selmer interface
UpperTheory.V.Z.J.OneCharU_s and its caller SmallClassNumbers/CorGKOf.lean, from
the much larger original development. The shortened repository does not export
that interface. Pointwise Frobenius witnesses can depend on the probe: products
across witness branches must precede gcds across probes. Spectral coverage, local
admissibility and the nonzero-difference hypotheses remain essential.

Archive: grh_beyond_dirichlet_grh_2026-11-25.zip
Relevant member: grh_beyond_transfer/sections/odd_sha.tex.

This manuscript already gives a paper-level comparison of the shortened odd
cocycle model with the c=(-1)^(e+1) part of the ordinary local H1 kernel, for odd
coefficient primes and positive Tate exponent after passing to divisible
coefficients. It derives finite length, an annihilator, stabilization and integral
odd-cocycle vanishing. The even Tate exponents needed in the direct K_(4m)(Z)
route occupy the opposite conjugation parity. Exceptional coefficient primes and
fixed-field versus varying-cyclotomic-field quantifiers are further obstructions.
No new K-group vanishing is certified by this prior-work review.

4. GLOBAL AND LOCAL NONABELIAN INPUTS ALREADY EXAMINED

Archive: grh_transfer_synthesis_2026-11-25.zip
Relevant member: grh_transfer_synthesis/sections/nonabelian_frame.tex.

This report constructs an explicit trace frame for the real S3 splitting field
of X^3-4X+1, handles its quadratic intersection with the cyclotomic field, and
proves a local Frobenius selector. It does not construct compatible global Weil
data, prescribed coprime frame bounds, quantitative level primes, the required
noncyclotomic odd-cocycle bound, crystalline losses, or quantitative spanning.
Thus it is a concrete local arithmetic advance, not a proof of nonabelian RH.

Archive: grh_fixed_field_transfer_2026-11-25.zip
Relevant member: grh_fixed_field_transfer_2026-11-25/sections/06_global.tex.

This manuscript already records that integral_forms_long needs
n >= (1-4/b^2)D, with completion cost 2*x1^2*b^2+b for a missing basis vector.
Its determinant comparison gives

  n*m*R <= n*U + 2*I + V + r*(K-A),   D=n+r.

A half-rank supply or a generic determinant-sandwich identity alone therefore
does not meet the source's quantitative global requirements.

5. OTHER STRANDS: INVENTORY, NOT A FRESH CERTIFICATION

grh_method_transfers_research.zip develops arbitrary finite ultrametric torsion
sets via P-orderings, an explicit ramified supersingular elliptic example, eventual
geometric budget scaling, finite-ring density bounds, sparse Li criteria and
Li-coefficient rigidity. The fixed-field and sharp-transfer reports substantially
develop exact torsion obstructions and coefficient-field restrictions.

The nonabelian detector corpus already treats S3/S4, A5/A6, commutator-Sylow and
cyclic-generator filters, reflection fields, PSL2(F7), PSL2(2^r), Kummer blocks,
symmetric powers and Gaussian Hecke characters. Their genuinely new arithmetic
prime estimates are generally explicitly left unproved. The arithmetic corpus
also reports quadratic class-group classifications, heavenly/isogeny bounds,
CM coefficient fields, sparse singular-modulus equations and additive prime
representations. Many use the exported Dirichlet GRH theorem plus external
results, rather than an internal proof mechanism. They are not recertified here.
