NOVELTY REVIEW: quantitative Hermite defect and root height
Date: 2026-11-26

PROPOSED CONTRIBUTION
For n>=2 conjugation-invariant roots in the unit disk, H=(sum z_j^(a+b))
and b=max |Im z_j|, put D=max(0,-lambda_min H). The proved estimate is

  2 [b/(4 floor(n/2))]^(2n-2) <= D <= n^2(n-1)(7n-11)b^2/6.

Both powers of b are optimal, uniformly over all such root multisets. The
lower bound allows collisions and repeated roots, with no separation parameter.
Adding matrix uncertainty nu and certified slack tau gives b<=4 floor(n/2)
[(tau+nu)/2]^(1/(2n-2)). The strongest new ingredient is grouping roots by an
imaginary-height gap before interpolation, so divided differences stay bounded
even when roots inside a group coalesce. The upper bound is a straightforward
second-order Taylor estimate; it is not presented independently as a major new result. A single highest-moment repair proves lower-exponent sharpness for
positivity-based certificates, not an information-theoretic recovery barrier.

Nathanson, arXiv:1911.01745v2 (2019); Mathematical Gazette 105 (2021), 122-125;
DOI 10.1017/mag.2021.19.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.01745
All 4 pages read. Qualitative Hermite--Sylvester equivalence via a polynomial
taking i,-i on one conjugate pair and zero elsewhere; repeated roots included.
No interpolation coefficient norm, noisy PSD strip bound, or optimal exponent.
This is the closest proof ancestor and receives explicit credit.

Ayyildiz Akoglu and Szanto, arXiv:2110.10313 (2021); Journal of Symbolic
Computation 117 (2023), 101-118; DOI 10.1016/j.jsc.2022.12.001.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.10313
Abstract, introduction, Prop.3.2, Thm.3.3, Section 7, and references checked.
Approximate root coordinates -> bounds on moments -> exact rational Hermite
reconstruction; signatures certify real roots in a ball. No general theorem
bounding maximum imaginary height from a small negative Hermite eigenvalue
was found in the inspected material. Their earlier seven-page univariate paper
is MACIS 2019 / LNCS11989 (2020), 3-9, DOI10.1007/978-3-030-43120-4_1; indexed
abstract checked, but full text fetch failed. Only the later paper was reviewed beyond abstract level.

Bickel, Pascoe and Sargent, "Zero-free regions near a line", arXiv:2108.04807
(2021); Mathematische Zeitschrift 305:39 (2023), DOI10.1007/s00209-023-03364-w.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.04807
Abstract, setup, main Theorems1.1/1.2/1.4, proof strategy, and RH application
read (pages1-8). Their truncated inverse-power Hankel conditions at every real
center, together with a positive spacing parameter, force nonreal zeros away
from the real axis. This is a different finite-order relaxation for infinitely
many zeros. Our bounded finite multiset/full-order approximate-PSD statement
controls maximum height toward the line without any spacing hypothesis. Their
conceptual positivity-to-geometry connection is relevant prior art; no exact
two-sided defect law was found in the inspected material.

Deng, Yang and Lu, "Contour Hankel dynamics and indicator fields for the
Riemann Xi-function", arXiv:2608.11520 (12 August2026).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11520
Indexed arXiv abstract retrieved by primary-source search; direct abstract,
HTML and PDF opens failed with DisabledError. The abstract establishes local
contour power moments, exact rank/inertia and RH positivity reformulation,
plus moving-contour congruence flows and finite-rank zero-crossing jumps.
Therefore contour-Hankel encoding and an RH positivity criterion are NOT new
contributions here. The abstract does not state our separation-free defect
bound or its sharp exponents. Full text was not accessible, so precise novelty
against this paper remains an explicit review limitation.
