# Lean formalization roadmap (not compiled)

The new finite theorems should be formalized separately from the analytic transfer. This is a proof-engineering outline, not a claim of kernel verification.

1. Model labelled items by `Fin m`, with a colour function into a finite additive commutative group `Γ`; allocations are functions `Fin m → Fin (r+1)`. Define quotas as cardinalities of inverse images and corrected sums as finite sums over these fibres. Use the reserve as index 0. Translation changes each block sum by its quota times the baseline.
2. Prove short witnesses: among a witness's sub-finsets with the desired sum choose one of minimum cardinality. A nonempty zero-sum sub-finset would contradict minimality. If its cardinality is at least `Fintype.card Γ`, order that many items and use pigeonhole on `d+1` partial sums. This produces a nonempty zero-sum interval, contradiction.
3. Completion: maintain per-block nonnegative integer capacities and partition the leftover items of each nonzero colour into packets whose lengths are its additive order and a remainder. The reserve bound pays for remainders. At each full packet, if no capacity is sufficient then the sum of capacities is at most `(d-1)*(r+1)`, contradicting the invariant that all zero-colour items and at least the pending packet are unplaced. Fill remaining slots by zeros. Prove termination by number of packets.
4. Direct matching: maximize number of singleton witnesses in the finite space of disjoint witness collections. The exchange of a witness with a same-sum item in another block is a finite-set operation; disjointness and sum identities are the essential lemmas.
5. Specialize to `ZMod 4` and `ZMod 2 × ZMod 2`. Enumerate the zero-sum-free multisets of length ≤3, then prove necessity via the listed nonnegative weights. Sufficiency is a signed-surplus case split over integers; in the odd-pair case, use integer division by 2. Full-coverage formula follows by maximizing the linear right-hand sides and the displayed floor estimates.
6. For the analytic bridge, create a theorem taking as input a finite-group prime-pool bias bound, the finite quotient Gram estimate, the partition Fourier estimate and the table-repair bound. The only part involving Dirichlet L-functions is the integrated explicit formula under `DirichletLZerosOnLine`, supplemented by PNT and partial summation. A full formalization would need to locate or prove that integrated estimate in the pinned Mathlib environment; no such downstream Lean bridge is provided here.

`SourceInterface.lean` checks only the upstream interface. The vendored Lake package has a distinct toolchain; run it from the inner `grh` directory, not the ProveIt root.
