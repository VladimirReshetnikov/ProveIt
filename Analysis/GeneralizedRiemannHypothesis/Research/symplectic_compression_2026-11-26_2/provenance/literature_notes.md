## Closest primary paper found

Maarten De Boeck and Geertrui Van de Voorde,
"Anzahl theorems for trivially intersecting subspaces generating a
non-singular subspace I: symplectic and hermitian forms,"
Linear Algebra and its Applications 699 (2024), 367--402.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2024.07.004
ArXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07486
Full HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2407.07486v3

The paper's explicit scope is: given a fixed possibly singular subspace,
count nonsingular subspaces which meet it trivially and whose sum with it
is nonsingular. The introduction identifies the symplectic result as
Theorem 4.7. This is directly relevant prior art in subspace enumeration,
but its stated main result counts subspaces under disjointness and
nonsingular-sum constraints. It does not in the inspected sections state
the probability of every specified cross-pairing matrix for arbitrary
possibly degenerate frames, or the optimal matrix/rank statistical tests
in the plane corollary. Do not imply that all symplectic subspace counting
is new.
