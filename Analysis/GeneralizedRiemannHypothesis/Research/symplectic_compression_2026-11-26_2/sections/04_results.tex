\subsection{Independent finite verification}

The code uses Python's standard library, integers, and
\texttt{fractions.Fraction}. The independent verifier generates
symplectic bases solely from their defining pairings. It does not
generate observations using the proposed probability formula.
For each selected dimension/form type it compares every possible
matrix, including impossible ones.

\section{Resolution and remaining directions}

The exact finite law answers the article's
\texttt{spn:q:symplectic}: both restricted forms may be degenerate
and neither subspace must be Lagrangian. The least-ambient-dimension
criterion is a separate extremal consequence. The boundary theorem
identifies the full limiting discrepancy for arbitrary fixed source
form and nondegenerate target with fixed-dimensional complement,
and proves that rank loses the surviving information.

The result does not classify all asymptotic regimes where source,
target, radicals, and complement dimensions grow simultaneously.
Formula \eqref{eq:law} supplies their exact starting point, but
uniform asymptotic analysis remains to be done. Another concrete
direction is to extend the reference law to the nonuniform
positive-type weights in the original spanning argument.
Analogous full laws for orthogonal and unitary groups would require
the appropriate additional form invariants.

The advance established here is a complete finite distribution
beyond the article's Lagrangian cases, together with a universal,
explicitly computable discrepancy that rank statistics miss.
