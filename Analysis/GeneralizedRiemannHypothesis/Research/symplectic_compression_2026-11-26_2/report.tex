\begin{abstract}
The finite-field spanning argument in \texttt{lsernau/grh-lean} estimates singular
compressions of a group representation. The ProveIt synthesis extends this
argument to symplectic matrices, but explicitly leaves open the distribution
for two arbitrary compressed subspaces. We give the full finite distribution
over every finite field, allowing arbitrary radicals in both restricted
alternating forms. Each matrix probability is a positive finite sum depending
on three ranks. A companion criterion gives the exact least ambient
symplectic dimension needed to realize a prescribed matrix.

We also prove a boundary limit: for a fixed source dimension and a
nondegenerate target whose complement has fixed dimension, the discrepancy
from a uniform matrix converges to the discrepancy of a random alternating
Gram matrix from the uniform alternating law. The limit is independent of
the source form, is explicitly computable, and is positive for source
dimension at least two. Nevertheless, every test based only on rank becomes
asymptotically powerless. For a symplectic plane source, exact formulas
identify the optimal full-matrix and rank-only tests.

These are unconditional finite-field theorems inspired by the proof's
method; they do not require RH or GRH. Complete proofs and independent
exhaustive checks are supplied. The precise novelty claim is an extension
beyond the pinned ProveIt article, including an answer to its stated open
question; worldwide publication priority is not asserted.
\end{abstract}
