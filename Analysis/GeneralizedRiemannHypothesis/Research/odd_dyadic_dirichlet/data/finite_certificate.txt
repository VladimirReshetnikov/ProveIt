Dyadic interval certificates for odd n from 5 through 19
Working precision: 256 binary fractional bits.
For each row, s is bracketed by [k, k+1]/2^24.
s=|z|^(2^(n-2)); d_j=1-|1+z^(2^j)|^2.
Each bracket also satisfies 2*s<1 and 2*|z|^2>1.
The last four columns give exponents e of certified lower bounds 2^e.
left: d_0-d_last at k; right: d_last-d_0 at k+1.
det: minimum of three negated oriented gradient determinants.
slack: minimum d_j-d_last over all inactive indices.
