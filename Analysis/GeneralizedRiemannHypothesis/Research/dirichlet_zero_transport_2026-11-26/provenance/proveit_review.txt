- Theorem 'Exact compression and electrical transport', label thm:electrical.
  For connected weighted graph Laplacian L and monic real-rooted p_i of
  degrees d_i, N=sum d_i, the rooted-forest determinant
  P_G=det(L diag(p_i)+diag(p_i')) is N tau(G) times the characteristic
  polynomial of a rank-(N-1) Hermitian compression K_G. Its roots lie to
  the left of those of (product_i p_i)' in ordered coupling. Their total
  displacement is sum_{i<j} d_i d_j R_ij/N, where R is effective resistance.
  The normalized W_1 distance is that expression divided by N-1.
- Corollary 'Electrical barycentre law', label cor:barycentre.
- Theorem 'Exact edge law', label thm:edge-law. Adding conductance h to
  an edge with incidence b gives a positive rank-one update
  K_{G+h}=K_G+h qq^*/(1+h b^*L^+b), q=QV L^+b. It gives exact total root
  displacement and ordered rightward motion.
- Corollary 'Small-coupling limit', label cor:small-coupling.

sections/entire.tex:
- Theorem 'Real zeros under Hermitian coupling', thm:entire-real-zeros.
  For genus-one real entire inputs F_i with only real zeros, Hermitian A
  preserves real zeros in P_A=det(diag(F_i)A+diag(F_i')).
- Theorem 'Exact collision order', thm:collision-order. At an input zero,
  it combines reduced input multiplicities with nullity of the complementary
  principal block of A+diag(F_i'/F_i).
- Theorem 'Exact zero-count identity', thm:entire-count. On regular
  endpoints, N_{P_A}(a,b)=sum_i N_{F_i}(a,b)+n_+(M_A(a))-n_+(M_A(b)); hence

- Corollary 'Graph-coupled completed Dirichlet functions',
  cor:dirichlet-graphs, applies the preceding results via Dirichlet GRH.

The explicit open problem is in sections/verification.tex: finding a
canonical regularization of all completed Dirichlet zero displacements
that gives an infinite analogue of the resistance identity. The text also
asks for optimal maximum displacement under a conductance budget. It does
not assert a convergent unweighted sum over all Dirichlet zeros; sections
electrical.tex and entire.tex explicitly warn that the finite identity
cannot be extended to that infinite sum without a convergence argument.

Consequently the new spectral-shift asymptotic should cite this packet as
the direct predecessor, reuse its real-zero and count theorems as prior
work, and explain precisely how the nonnegative displacement density,
Poisson regularization, and arithmetic growth settle the first open issue.

4. NOVELTY POSITION

The meaningful candidate extension is the arithmetic theorem for positive
Hermitian changes B of M_A=A+diag(F_i'/F_i): the bounded nonnegative integer
function xi=n_+(M_{A+B})-n_+(M_A) is a root-displacement density with exact
Poisson log-determinant representation and cumulative mass
integral_{-T}^T xi(t)dt ~ 4 tr(B) T/(pi log T).
For a strengthened graph edge of conductance h, tr(B)=2h, giving 8h/pi.
The coefficient is independent of the background network and the chosen
primitive Dirichlet characters. Its divergence also rules out a finite
unweighted total displacement for nonzero B.

Basic Hermitian real-zero preservation, interlacing, spectral shifts,
log-determinant representation, and Karamata theory are established
background. The arithmetic coefficient, all-orders Poisson expansion,
and the stated resolution of the direct predecessor's infinite-transport
problem are the candidate new contribution. Publication priority remains
unverified. See novelty_literature.txt for precise primary-source contrasts,
including the substantial scalar Cauchy-law precedents.
