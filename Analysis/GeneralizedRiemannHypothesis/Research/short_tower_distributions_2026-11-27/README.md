# Three- and four-step graph towers

The PDF proves the all-prime three-step distribution and extremizer census,
and the four-step distribution within the vanishing-quadratic subclass.
The previously known four-step maximum and its exceptional primes are
credited in the manuscript.

To rebuild: pdflatex short_tower_distributions.tex (twice).
To check sample primes independently: python3 verify.py
The checker uses direct incidence enumeration in dimension three and
both quadric rulings in dimension four, plus a separate chart count of C.
The observed sample output is in verification.txt.
