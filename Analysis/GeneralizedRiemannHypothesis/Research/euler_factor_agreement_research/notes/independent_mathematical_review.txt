FINAL UNIFIED THEOREM

LetG be a compact Hausdorff group with normalized Haar probability measurem, and let rho,sigma be inequivalent continuous three-dimensional unitary representations. They need NOT be irreducible. Define the open set
 D={g:det(TI-rho(g))!=det(TI-sigma(g))}, delta=m(D).
Then:
 1. delta>=2/27, sharply.
 2. If their determinant characters differ, delta>=1/2.
 3. If at least one representation is reducible, delta>=1/8, sharply.
 4. Equalitydelta=2/27 occurs exactly when both are irreducible, determinants agree, and their scalar kernels coincide in an open subgroupK of index9 with distinct scalar characterslambda,mu. ThenG/K=C3xC3, lambda/mu has order3, the traces vanish outsideK, andD=K\ker(lambda/mu).
 5. Ifdelta=2/27+epsilon<1/2, then
    integral_D [d_sc(rho(g))^2+d_sc(sigma(g))^2] dm <=12epsilon,
    where d_sc(U)^2=inf_{|z|=1}||U-zI||_F^2=6-2|trU|.
 6. For this family of complete-characteristic-polynomial codewords, any erased masss and error masse obey unique decoding whenever2e+s<2/27. Error-only ambiguity occurs exactly at e=1/27 in the finiteH27 example; erasure-only ambiguity occurs at s=2/27. The full mixed boundary is attainable after taking the direct product with a circle to permit arbitrary measurable splitting of the discrepancy set.

PROOF CHECKPOINTS

A. Unitarization: finite-group complex representations are equivalent to unitary representations by averaging; compact continuous representations are likewise unitarizable. This does not change characteristic polynomials.

B. Triangle lemma: forU inSU3 with eigenvaluesa,b,(ab)^(-1),
 3+2Re trU=|1+a+ab|^2>=0.
Apply also toomega U andomega^2U. The trace belongs to the closed equilateral triangleDelta=conv{3,3omega,3omega^2}. Its diameter is3sqrt3, attained only at distinct vertices; unitary trace modulus3 forces a scalar matrix.

C. Equal determinants must be normalized pointwise. Choose a unit cube rootalpha(g) of the common determinant for eachg. No continuous cube-root choice or character square/cube root is needed or claimed. Both normalized matrices lie inSU3, yielding|chi_rho-chi_sigma|^2<=27.

D. Different determinant characters: pushforward Haar to their quotient's image inU1. A nontrivial compact image is either finite cyclic of orderr>=2 or the entire circle. Thus the kernel has mass1/r<=1/2 or0, respectively. Determinant disagreement lies inD.

E. General character energyN is a positive integer sum of squared multiplicity differences andN>=2 by equal total dimensions. Therefore2<=N<=27delta. EqualityforcesN=2 and a differencealpha-beta of equal-degree irreducibles. Degrees1,2 give delta>=1/(2d^2)>=1/8; therefore global equality comes only from inequivalent irreducible degree3 representations.

F. Equality in the compact case is pointwise. The function27-|chi_rho-chi_sigma|^2 is continuous and nonnegative on openD; zero integral implies pointwise zero there because Haar has full support. Both matrices are scalar onD. Scalar kernels coincide since equality of characteristic polynomials preserves scalar matrices outsideD. The ratio of scalar characters is a nontrivial cube-root character, so delta=(2/3)m(K). Positive-mass closed subgroupK is finite-index and open; delta2/27 forcesindex9. The quotient cannot be cyclic because a cyclic projective image has an invariant eigenline. Conversely scalar kernelindex9 and character norm1 force trace0 outsideK, pointwise by continuity, and equal determinants imply equal polynomials outsideK via
 det(TI-U)=T^3-(trU)T^2+(detU)conjugate(trU)T-detU.

G. Reducible gap: every reducible cubic has a one-dimensional constituent. If any one-dimensional character's multiplicities differ, inner product with that character gives1<=3sqrt3delta, stronger than1/8. If all those multiplicities agree, inequivalence forces a common one-dimensional summand plus two different irreducible two-dimensional summands. Their energy2 and squared trace-distance bound16 give1/8. No classification of reducible equality is needed or asserted.

H. Strengthened stabilityconstant12: forz,w inDelta putr=|z|,s=|w|,a=r+s<=6. The real bilinear minimum overDelta xDelta isRe(z conjugate(w))>=-9/2. Ifa<=9/2, then|z-w|^2<=a^2<=(9/2)a. If9/2<=a<=6, thenr^2+s^2<=9+(a-3)^2, so
 |z-w|^2<=a^2-6a+27<=(9/2)a,
the last inequality being(a-9/2)(a-6)<=0. Therefore
 27-|z-w|^2 >=(9/4)[(6-2r)+(6-2s)].
Integrating onD gives the stronger bound
 integral_D[d_sc(rho)^2+d_sc(sigma)^2]dm <=(4/9)(27delta-N)<=12epsilon.
Earlier working notes used the valid but weaker18epsilon; the final theorem should consistently use12.

I. Decoding: for any two candidate codewords consistent with the same corrupted observation, their disagreement mass is at mosts+e_1+e_2<=s+2e. The strict bound contradictsdelta>=2/27. The finiteH27 disagreement has two equal-mass central points, so choose the received polynomial from one representation on one point and the other on the other. Then each candidate is exactly1/27 away. Erasing both points gives the2/27 erasure obstruction. Do not claim an efficient reconstruction algorithm merely from this uniqueness bound.

ARITHMETIC TRANSFER

For continuous finite-image cubic complex Galois representations, take the finite common quotientG and apply Chebotarev to the conjugacy-invariant discrepancy set. The density exists and equalsdelta. Hence agreement of complete local Euler factors on density>25/27 forces isomorphism; if one is reducible, density>7/8 already forces isomorphism. The full equality classification transfers to the common finite image. This needs neither Dirichlet GRH nor nonabelian GRH or Artin holomorphy. Sharpness over some number field follows from realizingH27 over a suitable base; sharpness overQ may cite the solvable inverse-Galois theorem, or a verified explicitH27 field.
