AN EXPLICIT SHARP ARITHMETIC EXAMPLE OVER Q(sqrt(-3))

Let omega satisfy omega^2+omega+1=0. Set F=Q(omega), and choose
  x^3=6, y^3=7, alpha=1+x,
  beta=(1+x)^2(1+omega*x), z^3=beta.
Put E=F(x), K=E(y), L=K(z).

Theorem. L/F is Galois of degree27 with group the exponent3 Heisenberg
group. Consequently the two cubic representations in the main theorem
give an explicit arithmetic sharpness example over F: their unramified
Euler factors agree at natural and Dirichlet density25/27 of prime ideals.

Proof of the degree.
The prime2 is unramified in F and v_p(6)=1 at a prime p above2. Hence6 is
not a cube in F. Therefore [E:F]=3 and E/F is cyclic, generated by
sigma(x)=omega*x.

Choose either prime of F above7. Its residue field is F_7 because7=1 mod3.
For example (3+omega) has norm7 and omega has residue4. The polynomial
X^3-6 has the3 distinct residue roots3,5,6. Its discriminant is-27*36,
which is not divisible by7; the chosen prime thus splits into3 distinct
unramified primes q_0,q_1,q_2 of E. Label them so that
  alpha_i=1+omega^i*x
has positive valuation at q_i. The other two alpha_j are units there.
Since alpha_0*alpha_1*alpha_2=1+x^3=7 and v_{q_i}(7)=1, their valuations
are exactly v_{q_i}(alpha_j)=1 if i=j and0 otherwise.

In particular7 has valuation1, so is not a cube in E. Hence[K:E]=3,
with cyclic generator tau(y)=omega*y. The valuation vector of
beta=alpha_0^2 alpha_1 at these3 primes is(2,1,0).

We claim beta is not a cube in K. If u^3=beta for u in K, then
(tau(u)/u)^3=1, so tau(u)=omega^k u for some k=0,1,2. In the basis1,y,y^2
of K/E, the eigenspace of tau for omega^k is exactly E*y^k. Thus u=c*y^k
with c in E, and beta=c^3*7^k. Taking the three valuations would force
(2-k,1-k,-k) all divisible by3, which is impossible. This proves the claim,
and[L:K]=3. Therefore[L:F]=27.

Explicit automorphisms.
Define maps on generators by
  sigma:x->omega*x, y->y, z->y*z/(1+x);
  tau:x->x, y->omega*y, z->z;
  kappa:x->x, y->y, z->omega*z.
The identity
  sigma(beta)/beta
    = (alpha_1^2 alpha_2)/(alpha_0^2 alpha_1)
    = alpha_1 alpha_2/alpha_0^2
    = 7/alpha_0^3
shows sigma(z)^3=sigma(beta), so sigma extends to an F-automorphism of L.
The same check for tau and kappa is immediate. The degrees proved above
ensure that the defining radical equations introduce no extra relation
which could invalidate these maps.

Their cubes are the identity. The only calculation that is not immediate
is sigma^3(z): it equals
  y^3*z/(alpha_0 alpha_1 alpha_2)=7*z/7=z.
Kappa commutes with sigma and tau. On z,
  tau sigma(z)=omega*y*z/alpha_0,
  sigma tau(z)=y*z/alpha_0,
and on x and y the two composites coincide. Thus
  tau sigma = kappa sigma tau.
The restrictions of sigma and tau to K generate Gal(K/F)=C3 x C3, and
kappa is a nontrivial element fixing K. Consequently they generate at
least27 distinct F-automorphisms of L. Since[L:F]=27, the extension is
Galois and these are all its automorphisms. The order3 relations and the
nontrivial central commutator identify the group with H27. For an exact
coordinate law (a,b,c)*(a',b',c')=(a+a',b+b',c+c'+a*b'), one can use the
normal form tau^b sigma^a kappa^{-c}; switching generator conventions only
changes the sign of the central coordinate.

Euler-factor density.
Apply the two inequivalent cubic Heisenberg representations with central
characters omega and omega^2 to Gal(L/F). At the24 noncentral elements,
both characteristic polynomials are T^3-1; they also agree at the identity.
They disagree at the2 remaining central elements. Chebotarev therefore
gives disagreement density2/27 and agreement density25/27. This is an
unconditional Artin-Euler-factor statement; no holomorphy of Artin L-series
or Riemann hypothesis is used.

Automorphic realization, if included.
A maximal abelian subgroup A of H27 has order9 and is normal of index3.
The two cubic representations are induced from one-dimensional characters
of A. The fixed field L^A/F is cyclic cubic. By class field theory these
characters are finite-order Hecke characters of L^A; by cyclic automorphic
induction their inductions are cuspidal automorphic representations of
GL3 over F, because the inducing characters have a3-element conjugacy
orbit. Their unramified Satake eigenvalues are roots of unity, so they are
tempered there. This identifies a sharp automorphic pair whenever the
standard cyclic automorphic-induction theorem is cited explicitly.

Optional explicit polynomial.
The conjugates of beta over F have sum3+18omega, pairwise-product sum
-105-126omega and product343. Therefore z is a root of
  T^9-(3+18omega)T^6-(105+126omega)T^3-343.
Taking its coefficient-field norm gives the rational polynomial
  T^18+12T^15+195T^12+2212T^9+9555T^6+28812T^3+117649.
The checker also verifies its vanishing at z. The radical presentation,
not this polynomial alone, specifies L and its automorphisms.
