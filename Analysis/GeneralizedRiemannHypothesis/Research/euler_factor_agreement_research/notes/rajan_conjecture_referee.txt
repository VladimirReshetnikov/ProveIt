1. Source and exact scope

C. S. Rajan, "Refinement of strong multiplicity one for automorphic
representations of GL(n)", Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society
128 (2000), 691-700, DOI 10.1090/S0002-9939-99-05616-6.
Primary author-institution copy:
https://repository.ias.ac.in/95366/1/Refinement.pdf

I independently read the primary PDF's indexed text, especially pp. 694-695
and p. 698. Conjecture 3 concerns A_B: irreducible automorphic constituents of
Borel induction from idele class characters. It does not require cuspidality.
For rank n it predicts that local isomorphism on density greater than
1 - 2^(-floor(log_2(2n))) forces equality of the inducing character multisets.
The paragraph immediately before the conjecture explicitly distinguishes
complete local isomorphism from equality of traces. This distinction is
essential; the construction below meets the complete-local-isomorphism
hypothesis.

2. Independent mathematical verdict

The literal statement of Conjecture 3 is false. The rank-three construction
below gives agreement density 7/9, greater than its predicted threshold 3/4.
These are Borel-induced irreducible automorphic representations, not cuspidal
representations. Reducibility of the associated three-dimensional Galois
parameter does not contradict irreducibility of the automorphic constituent.
There is no dependence on RH, GRH, Artin holomorphy, or a conjectural
automorphy statement.

The mathematical refutation is certain subject only to the ordinary meaning
of the explicitly printed conjecture. Publication priority and whether an
earlier counterexample exists have not been established. Searches combining
Rajan, Conjecture 3, Borel, counterexample, and 7/9 found no indexed earlier
refutation, but this is not evidence sufficient to assert priority. The named
target is a specialist conjecture; it should not be advertised as a resolution
of a widely famous problem such as RH, GRH, or Ramanujan.

3. Explicit rank-three construction over Q

Put omega = exp(2 pi i/3). Let chi be the primitive cubic Dirichlet character
modulo 7 with chi(3)=omega, and let psi be the primitive cubic character modulo
13 with psi(2)=omega. Their conductors are coprime, and the Chinese remainder
theorem makes their joint image C_3 x C_3. Both characters are even.

Consider the two character multisets
    P = {1, chi, chi^2},
    Q = {psi, psi chi, psi chi^2}.
They are disjoint and each has three distinct elements. Their products are
both 1, so they have the same trivial determinant/central character.

For a prime p not dividing 91, write u=chi(p), v=psi(p). The local eigenvalue
multisets are {1,u,u^2} and {v,vu,vu^2}. If u is nontrivial, the first multiset
is all of mu_3 and multiplication by v permutes it. If u=1, the two multisets
are {1,1,1} and {v,v,v}; equality then holds exactly when v=1. Consequently
the complete local Euler factors disagree exactly on
    D = {p: chi(p)=1, psi(p) != 1}.
The joint image has nine equally frequent values, and two are in D. Dirichlet's
theorem/PNT in arithmetic progressions gives natural and Dirichlet density
2/9 for disagreement and 7/9 for agreement.

An independent exact integer check modulo 91 gives 72 reduced residue classes,
with precisely the following 16 disagreement classes:
    6,15,20,22,29,36,41,43,48,50,55,62,69,71,76,85.
The agreement count is 56, and 56/72=7/9.

Attach to P and Q the standard isobaric automorphic sums of the three finite
order Hecke characters, or equivalently the irreducible automorphic
constituents in Rajan's A_B notation. At unramified primes, equality of their
Satake multisets is exactly equality of the complete local factors and local
spherical representations. Their inducing packets are distinct, and their
local components differ at positive density, so they are inequivalent.
No assertion that a particular unnormalized principal-series model is
irreducible at every archimedean place is needed.

4. Infinite cubic/quadratic family

Let eta_1,...,eta_k be independent quadratic Dirichlet characters, independent
also from chi and psi. For example, take quadratic characters of distinct
prime conductors q_i congruent to 1 modulo 4, with q_i not equal to 7 or 13.
The Chinese remainder theorem gives joint image C_3^2 x C_2^k. For a subset
S of {1,...,k}, put eta_S=product_{i in S} eta_i. Expand the virtual character
    F=(1+chi+chi^2)(1-psi) product_i(1-eta_i).
Every term is a distinct character. Terms indexed by chi^j psi^b eta_S have
coefficient (-1)^(b+|S|), for j=0,1,2 and b=0,1. The positive and negative
packets each have n=3*2^k characters and have no common term.

For g in the joint finite group, write u=chi(g), v=psi(g), and e_i=eta_i(g).
For every positive integer r,
    F(g^r)=(1+u^r+u^(2r))(1-v^r) product_i(1-e_i^r).
Define
    D={g: u=1, v!=1, e_i=-1 for every i}.
If v=1 or some e_i=1, this expression vanishes for every r. If u!=1, it
vanishes for r not divisible by 3 because its first factor is zero, and for r
divisible by 3 because its second factor is zero. Thus outside D all power
traces of the positive and negative packets agree. Newton identities (already
the first n power traces suffice) imply equality of their degree-n
characteristic polynomials and hence of their full local Euler factors.
On D, F(g)=3(1-v)2^k is nonzero, so even the first traces differ.
Therefore the full-factor disagreement set is exactly D, not just a subset.

There are precisely two such g in the group of order 9*2^k. The resulting
agreement density is
    1 - 2/(9*2^k).
But floor(log_2(2n))=k+2, so the conjectured threshold is
    1 - 1/(4*2^k).
The former exceeds the latter by 1/(36*2^k). This proves failure in every
rank n=3*2^k. Adding the same further character packet to both sides preserves
the exact disagreement set, and proves failure throughout each interval
    3*2^k <= n < 4*2^k.

5. Exact corrected rank-three bound for compact abelian groups

Let P,Q be inequivalent sums of three continuous unitary characters of a
compact abelian group G, with normalized Haar measure. Let delta be the
measure where the complete characteristic polynomials differ. Then
    delta >= 2/9,
and the explicit cubic packets attain this value.

Proof. If determinant characters differ, their ratio is a nontrivial character
and its kernel has Haar measure at most 1/2; hence delta>=1/2. Assume the
determinants agree, and cancel common characters from P and Q. Cancellation
preserves both the full-factor disagreement set and determinant equality.
If reduced rank is one, determinant equality makes the packets identical,
which is excluded. If reduced rank is two, orthogonality gives squared L2
norm at least 4 for the trace difference, while its pointwise square is at
most 16. Thus delta>=1/4. In reduced rank three, all character multiplicities
on opposite sides are disjoint; orthogonality gives squared L2 norm M>=6.
The equal-determinant SU(3) trace-triangle inequality bounds the squared trace
difference by 27. It vanishes outside the full-factor disagreement set, so
6<=M<=27 delta and delta>=2/9. This exhausts the possibilities.

Equality classification. If delta=2/9, the preceding inequalities force
reduced rank three, M=6, and distinct characters within each packet. Every
disagreement point saturates the SU(3) trace-diameter inequality: both
representations are scalar there, and their scalar ratio is a nontrivial
cube root of unity. The saturation conclusion initially holds almost
everywhere; continuity and positivity of Haar measure on nonempty open sets
upgrade it throughout the open disagreement set.

Let K be the common scalar locus. It is a closed subgroup, and D is contained
in K. On K write the two scalar characters as a and b. Equal determinants
give (b/a)^3=1; the ratio is nontrivial, so D consists of exactly two of its
three cosets in K. Consequently delta=(2/3) measure(K), forcing [G:K]=3.
All within-packet ratios lie in Ann(K), which has order three. Since each
packet contains three distinct characters, each is a full coset of Ann(K).
Thus, after a common unitary twist alpha, the packets are exactly
    {1,chi,chi^2}, {psi,psi chi,psi chi^2},
where chi and psi are independent cubic characters. Equal determinants force
psi^3=1 globally; nontrivial restriction of psi to K gives independence.
Conversely every such pair attains equality. The common twist alpha may have
infinite order.

For finite-order Hecke packets this yields the unconditional arithmetic
sharp agreement bound 7/9 by Chebotarev (or by arithmetic progressions over
Q). Extension to all unitary Hecke packets can be proved using prime-average
orthogonality/nonvanishing for Hecke L-functions, or the finite-order
equivalence-class reduction in Rajan's Theorem 1; that extension requires its
own explicitly stated analytic argument rather than identifying arbitrary
Hecke characters with a finite Galois group.

6. Repository attribution and novelty boundary

Article/sections/rec.tex already contains the odd-prime coset-packet
construction (rec:con:opack) with disagreement (ell-1)/ell^(s+1). Its case
ell=3, s=1 is exactly the basic rank-three cubic construction used above.
It must be credited as an input, not presented as discovered in this session.
The repository also gives cubic-packet minima (rec:cor:ocubicmin).

The newly identified consequence is that this construction contradicts the
literal named Conjecture 3. The mixed cubic/quadratic amplification gives an
infinite family of violating ranks. The compact-abelian rank-three argument
above removes the restriction to characters of exponent three, and gives an
exact replacement with a complete extremizer classification. Whether any
of these statements have already appeared elsewhere remains an external
priority question; the proof does not depend on the answer.
