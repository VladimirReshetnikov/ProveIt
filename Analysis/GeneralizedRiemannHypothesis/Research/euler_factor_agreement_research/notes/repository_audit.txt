2. Audited mechanism.
Article/sections/src.tex, src:sec:mechanism:
lambda_{d,q}(n)=Lambda(n) 1_{n=1 mod q}(1-chi_d(n))>=0.
R_{d,q}(x)=sum_{n<=x}lambda(n)(1/n-1/x)-log(x)/phi(q)-c_{d,q}.
A common lattice supplies a short-form upper covolume bound, an almost-full-dimensional spanning estimate, and an integral lower covolume bound. Their common main term cancels.
With a theta-lift chain of length m asymptotic sqrt(x), this gives the eventual one-sided bound
R_{d,q}(x)<=x^(-1/2+epsilon).
Its Mellin transform has residues
[sum_chi ord_rho L^circ(s,chi)-sum_chi ord_rho L^circ(s,chi chi_d)]
 / [phi(q) rho(1-rho)].
Quadratic-twist zero density chooses d AFTER a hypothetical off-line zero, avoiding cancellation by all twists. Landau positivity then excludes the remaining pole.
Quantifier dependence and matching integral lattices are essential; a character-table substitution does not create the needed arithmetic estimate.

3. Existing nonabelian transfer boundary.
Article/sections/nab.tex reduces many nonabelian RH targets to one-sided weighted Frobenius-prime bounds. Its final open question nab:q:arithmetic explicitly asks for any one such inequality for a nonabelian field, with an arithmetic lattice whose local divisibility sees nonlinear Frobenius data.
The periodic approximation theorem states that a nonlinear irreducible trace has minimum prime mean-square error exactly 1 against every fixed congruence modulus (for its unnormalized trace). More generally the best congruence predictor is the projection onto abelian constituents. Therefore fixed Dirichlet-character linear combinations cannot supply nonlinear Frobenius weights.
This is not a logical independence theorem, and says nothing with a modulus growing with the prime cutoff.

4. Prominent conjectures not settled by the inspected inputs.
- General nonabelian Artin/Hecke RH: needs a new arithmetic one-sided estimate; the analytic detection reductions already exist.
- Chowla central nonvanishing for quadratic characters: the input allows central zeros of even multiplicity. hec:thm:chowla and hec:thm:kernels are finite certificate criteria, not uniform nonvanishing.
- Pair correlation or simplicity: the zero-location input has no spacing or multiplicity conclusion.
- Hooley's route to Artin's primitive-root conjecture requires the relevant nonabelian Kummer-field RH statements, not just Dirichlet GRH.
These are boundaries of this analysis, not proofs that no other route exists.

5. A concrete unresolved finite-character question.
nab:q:un asks whether, for n>=13,
U_n=(n-1)Ind_{<t>}^{S_n}(sign)-Reg_{S_n}+1
is a positive rational sum of characters induced from linear characters of subgroups.
The article certifies n<=12. A uniform identity would prove unconditional holomorphy on C\{1} of
zeta_{E^{<t>}}(s)^(n-1) zeta_{E^{A_n}}(s)/(zeta_E(s)zeta_k(s))
for S_n extensions E/k and the sharp off-line multiplicity bound n-1 over Q under Dirichlet GRH.
A useful Foulkes-character identity was derived, but does not close monomial positivity; see notes/foulkes_boundary.txt.

6. Chosen downstream direction.
The full-Euler-factor cubic separation theorem is a clean new extension of local-factor reconstruction. Its finite/compact proof needs only unitary trace geometry and orthogonality; its arithmetic transfer uses Chebotarev. The tempered automorphic extension uses classical Rankin-Selberg theory and is detailed in cubic_euler_agreement.tex, Section 8.
It does not invoke RH or GRH, though it follows the repository's method-transfer program and moves its one-dimensional local-factor comparisons to irreducible dimension three.
