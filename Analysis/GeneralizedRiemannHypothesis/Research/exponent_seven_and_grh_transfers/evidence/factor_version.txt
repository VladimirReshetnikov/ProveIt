In this version PROVE_PRIMALITY defaults to 1. After rejecting composites by
Miller-Rabin, the functions prime_p and prime2_p recursively factor n-1 for a
Lucas order test before accepting a large prime. Small numbers surviving trial
division are prime by the size of the first omitted trial divisor. The GMP
path uses the corresponding proof for numbers beyond the two-word case.

A reproduction should use coreutils 9.7 with its default proof-enabled build
or another factorization routine which certifies each prime output.
