DIRECT ARTICLE STARTING POINTS

1. Article/sections/spn.tex
   Labels spn:thm:lagrangian and spn:thm:rank-blind.
   These give the finite-field Lagrangian-projection law and the
   contrast between rank-only testing and complete-matrix testing.
   The new paper extends the comparison to all finite valuation-ring
   precisions and complete Smith/cokernel data, proves the exact
   likelihood and precision recurrence, and determines the sharp
   precision exponent five.

   https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/3d8689ceae76501a5c50f67da37e85369aac4fb6/Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis/Article/sections/spn.tex

2. Article/sections/zer.tex
   Label zer:thm:electrical gives the rooted-forest compression and
   electrical zero-transport theorem. The final item of zer:sec:open
   asks for the optimal graph under a conductance budget and fixed
   input degrees. The new paper gives its largest-displacement
   solution, the full Wasserstein-p family, unique feasible optimizers,
   and an exact mean-transport stability identity. Topology is free;
   sparse prescribed topologies and edge caps are different problems.

   https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/3d8689ceae76501a5c50f67da37e85369aac4fb6/Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis/Article/sections/zer.tex

   verification or a new formalization. The new proofs in this packet

SCOPE OF NEW CLAIMS

The local random-matrix extension and finite graph optimization are
unconditional. Their relation to the requested proof package is through
its mathematical methods and the supplied Article's existing results.
Dirichlet GRH is used only when real-rooted polynomial inputs are formed
from finite packets of Dirichlet zero ordinates.

No assertion is made about simple or independent zero ordinates,
arbitrary Artin/automorphic GRH, an infinite-spectrum minimax optimum,
or an arithmetic distribution of number-field cokernels.

The new statements are distinct extensions relative to the pinned
Article. Targeted literature comparison did not identify the combined
statements. This is not certification of worldwide priority. Primary
literature citations are provided in the paper's bibliography.
