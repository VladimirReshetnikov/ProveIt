FORMALIZATION ROADMAP -- NOT A COMPILED LEAN DEVELOPMENT

This archive contains human-readable mathematical proofs and executable
finite checkers. No .lean file is supplied and no Lean build was performed.
The roadmap below separates reusable finite lemmas from the limit arguments.

1. Finite subspace counting
   Define the Gaussian coefficient as the number of r-dimensional subspaces.
   Establish its product formula, flag identity, and partial Mobius identity
     sum_(j=0)^L (-1)^j q^(j(j-1)/2) [m choose j]_q
       = (-1)^L q^(L(L+1)/2) [m-1 choose L]_q, m>=1.
   Derive the summed, sign-controlled inversion remainder in the paper.

2. Fourier containment probabilities
   For an E*-valued random variable and a fixed nontrivial additive character,
   prove P(row vanishes on S)=q^(-dim S) sum_(A in S) Fourier(row)(A).
   Prove the exceptional-direction stratification by
     r = dim(span(S intersect B)),
   including the count |B|^r q^((s-r)D).

3. Abstract asymptotic theorem
   Work with sequences of finite coefficient spaces of dimensions D_n.
   Prove summed absolute errors for every fixed-dimensional containment law.
   Apply finite Mobius truncation first at fixed L, take n->infinity, then
   let L->infinity. Establish tightness from the first Gaussian moment.
   The conclusion concerns the entire subspace measure on each E_n, not
   merely the dimension pushforward.

4. Quadratic and plane Fourier lemmas
   Treat characteristic two explicitly. The polar radical can support a
   nonzero pure-square form; its additive-character mean is zero.
   Count low-polar-rank forms by choosing a radical subspace and then a form
   on the quotient, accounting for the n-dimensional kernel of the polar
   map in characteristic two.
   For plane constraints, subtract dependent ordered pairs from the exact
   bilinear character sum. The result is equation (plane-fourier).

5. Code-pair sampling
   Define coordinatewise product and its linear span.
   Use shared and private generator coordinates to define E_(k,l,i).
   Prove D=kl-binomial(i,2) and the adapted-basis preimage count.
   Formalize the conditional generator-rank comparison by the explicit
   subspace count; unconditional high probability alone is insufficient
   after a rare intersection event.

6. Alternating pencils
   Prove the strict tensor dimension surplus, including extension of scalars
   to an infinite field and the eigenspace-directness argument.
   Count direct tuples of injected radical frames exactly; bound nondirect
   tuples by their strict dimension surplus. This gives mixed moments.
   Use the explicit even-nullity Lagrange interpolation majorants. Do not
   replace this step by an unsupported moment-determinacy invocation.

7. Hidden-form experiment
   Define null and planted finite distributions for even n>=4.
   Count projective nondegenerate forms and isotropic planes.
   Prove the exact likelihood Z/lambda_n, uniform posterior, and recovery
   identity. Transfer null limits, then size-bias using preserved total mass.
   Derive full-TV, dimension-only-TV and recovery formulas.

8. Certification boundary
   The Python finite checks verify examples of these identities. They do not
   constitute formal proofs of universally quantified lemmas.
   The interval computation certifies numerical evaluations of the infinite
   formulas only after the mathematical formulas and tail inequalities have
   been proved. Its output is not a finite-n approximation theorem.

No upstream RH/GRH theorem is a dependency of any module listed above.
