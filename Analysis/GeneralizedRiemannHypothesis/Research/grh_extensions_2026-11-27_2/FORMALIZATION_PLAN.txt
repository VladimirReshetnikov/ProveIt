FORMALIZATION PLAN — NO COMPILED LEAN RESULT IS CLAIMED

A. UNEQUAL BRANCH RECORDS

1. Work over a field F. Represent branches u_b in X F[[X]], their finite contact
   valuations, target quotient F[[X,Y]]/(X,Y)^d, and finite records F[[X]]/X^n_b.
   Prove that truncation in total degree L >= max n_b preserves every record.
   This turns all record and target maps into finite-dimensional linear maps.

2. Product domination is the main algebraic obligation. Extend the discrete
   valuation on F((X)) to a splitting field of a polynomial in Y. Prove Gauss
   valuation multiplicativity, also after Y -> X^kappa Z. Separate roots by
   valuation >= kappa; show the residual scalar exponent is a nonnegative
   integer even when root valuations are fractional or negative. Replace each
   high root by a nearest observed branch using the ultrametric inequality.
   Formal-series domination then follows from finite truncation and padding.

3. Establish the exact distance by two inequalities between finite zero sets.
   Formalize error/erasure uniqueness as a Hamming-distance argument on records.
   Define the compressed tree via the contact ultrametric; prove the recurrence
   by decomposing shared path lengths. The mathematical operation count needs
   cached max-convolutions at each common inherited value.

4. Componentwise saturation can be formalized independently of the root lemma:
   use the product through the d-1 closest neighbors for necessity, and monic
   division plus Lagrange interpolation over F((X)) for sufficiency. Each
   interpolation scalar has valuation >= 1. The weighted proof replaces this
   threshold by a+1 and uses k+1 branches when d-1=a+kappa*k.

5. For the decoder, make finite truncation of the monic error locator explicit.
   The first system is consistent because an actual locator exists. Any solution
   has Q-EH with at most w nonzero records, so D_(d+w)>w forces Q-EH into
   (X,Y)^(d+w). Use that the associated graded ring F[X,Y] is a domain to
   prove multiplication by E is injective on the target jet. The second system
   therefore has exactly one answer. This is a sufficient decoder condition,
   not a proof of decoding at the whole optimal uniqueness radius.

B. TWO-FREQUENCY POSITIVE MEASURES

1. Normalize two positive frequencies to 1 and r>1. Establish invariance under
   translation and scaling, and the single-frequency diameter lower bound pi.

2. Prove the last-positive-lobe comparison for -s cos(rt)/cos(t). Formalize the
   unique critical point using the fact that every zero of r tan(rt)-tan(t)
   has positive derivative r^2-1. Establish strict monotonicity of the outer
   ratio and existence of H. Prove the dual function q=M cos(t)+s cos(rt)
   is nonnegative and determine its zero set exactly.

3. For a nonnegative continuous function with zero integral, prove support
   containment in its zero set. Solve the two sine equations and mass/cosine
   equations; the determinant is nonzero because q'(H)<0. This gives uniqueness
   for arbitrary probability measures, without assuming symmetry or finiteness.

4. At odd resonance, first prove u,v -> 0 by compactness and strict sine-ratio
   inequalities. Then establish two-sided scale bounds before using Taylor
   expansions, obtaining eta ~ m(m^2-1)u^3/3 and v/u -> 2. Uniform local
   remainders are needed; numerical samples are not a substitute.

5. Formalize the perturbed dual and perturbed masses for shortening, with
   O(h^2) constants for each fixed r. At the odd ratio use the preceding-lobe
   certificate. The constants need not be uniform as r approaches resonance.

6. The prime-error application uses the source ramp explicit formula and its
   absolute convergence. Termwise integration supplies the Fourier multiplier.
   If relying on the supplied Lean Dirichlet-GRH theorem, import its exact
   exported statement only after checking a compatible toolchain and dependencies.

PRACTICAL ORDER
Start with finite saturation and the finite distance/uniqueness interfaces.
Next formalize the valued root-domination lemma. Separately formalize the
analytic dual certificate; delay singular asymptotics until the global
optimization and equality case are complete. Pin a Lean/Mathlib revision in
an isolated package before implementation; no untested Lean skeleton is included.
