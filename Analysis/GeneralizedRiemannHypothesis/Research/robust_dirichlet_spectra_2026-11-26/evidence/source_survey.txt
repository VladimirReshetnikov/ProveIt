CLOSEST PREDECESSOR: QUADRATIC_SPECTRA
Archive path:
Analysis/GeneralizedRiemannHypothesis/docs/incoming/quadratic_spectra.zip
Git blob: 475c19974b1296ba83633f82c3d1e3a9cf6662c8
Title: Quadratic characters from finitely many zeros.
TeX member within archive: quadratic_spectra/quadratic_spectra.tex

Its Theorem 3.1 already uses a high-order B-spline supported near log n:
h=1/(8X), even order r, T=16rX, and transform sinc(h gamma/r)^r.
It recovers all prime-power values of a primitive quadratic character up to X
from all signed zero ordinates |gamma|<=T. It includes an aggregate ordinate
perturbation budget and a height
T << X (log(2X)+log log(3Q)).
Its Corollary 3.2 gives a uniform identifying cutoff
T_Q << (log(2Q))^2 log log(3Q)
for primitive nonprincipal quadratic characters of conductor <=Q.
Its Theorem 3.3 refines the B-spline by a sinc-squared factor in the logarithmic
variable to obtain displacement at least c H/log(2Q) for distinct quadratic
characters. It assumes equal zero-list cardinalities and a full bijection.
Both the B-spline construction and the high-cutoff sinc-squared refinement
must be explicitly credited as inherited ideas.

The paper's second further-research question explicitly asks how much
low-zero separation identifies primitive characters of bounded order >2,
including conjugate pairs; its cosine observation loses orientation.

PROPOSED NEW CONTRIBUTION, RELATIVE TO THE INSPECTED CORPUS
A theorem for all primitive complex characters of conductor <=Q, with no
bound on their orders, the same identifying height order, and a lower bound
for a metric that allows unequal zero counts and partial matching:
  unmatched occurrences + sum min(1, |gamma-gamma'|).
The key extra arithmetic input is a small prime whose quotient-character
value is separated from 1 by an absolute angle, avoiding losses from tiny
distances between high-order roots of unity.
This is more than the formal replacement of cosine by a complex exponential:
the phase separation must be independent of character order, and the
spectral estimate must control the sup norm as well as the Lipschitz norm
to admit missing and spurious occurrences.

No matching formulation was found in the 39 newer packets' TeX sources or
the 61 older delivery summaries and targeted analytic sources. This supports
novelty relative to the reviewed repository, not worldwide publication
priority. The angular small-prime lemma should be described as an
Ankeny-type consequence proved for the application, not asserted novel
without a separate exhaustive literature study.

  Git blob 6667e024a62e1ae82c3ad94d08e15855fdb36f5e.
  Recovering prime weights from damaged residue data.
  Recovers weights from residues at calibrated joint multiplicity vectors,
  with a positive fraction of corruptions/erasures in large dyadic bands.
  This is a different observation model from a noisy unlabeled ordinate list.
  Its Bombieri-Perelli input and high-window density are not needed merely
  to use a bounded-Lipschitz prime-isolating spectral observable.
* dirichlet_li_rigidity.zip
  Git blob afca9a2850c764af5502682300f1af377dbaafb2.
  Identifies a primitive character, integer stride, and unknown finite filter
  from infinite Li samples, even after polynomial fitting and sparse
  corruption. Constants depend on the competing models; no uniform
  bounded-conductor finite ordinate cutoff is proved.
* dirichlet_zero_packet_homometry.zip
  Git blob de449402c5ff83d0d33e2dc6fe5cf3f2da89ca47.
  Classifies balanced Fourier measurements of pooled zero differences via
  local character histograms and common twists. Includes a uniform Landau
  formula through a growing frequency band and explicit homometric packets.
  A complete oriented individual-character spectrum is different data.
* prime_zero_reconstruction.zip
  Git blob bc3e70ab4833cc808dc7dae11091f54cfd249817.
  One prime signal for all Dirichlet zeros. The direction is prime
  autocorrelation to zero multiplicities, using infinite exact limits;
  finite window error estimates require spectral separation.
* one_gap_dirichlet_zeros.zip
  Git blob 94166bce91c41903c8ce73e0d203fe97ed826ea1.
  Recovers an entire spectrum from locations of high-order derivative
  observations in one gap. Infinite exact data; character recovery afterward

* grh_spectral_rigidity.zip
  Git blob a781cd40910a2308fb8c5fe203612d46cfaf9dec.

CURRENT PRIMARY LITERATURE COMPARISON
William D. Banks, Comparing zeros of distinct Dirichlet L-functions,
arXiv:2302.07073v2 (revised 17 May 2024), also published by Publ. Math.
Debrecen. The abstract states an unconditional nonidentity theorem for
distinct primitive characters modulo the same q in high windows:
T >= c1 q^theta and length c2 q^theta log T, theta>1/3; theta>1/4 for
cubefree q. The new target concerns low starting cutoff, differing
conductors, use of Dirichlet GRH, and quantitative tolerance to
positions, missing occurrences, and spurious occurrences.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07073

Searches using both web engines for Dirichlet zero-spectrum reconstruction,
noisy zeros, transport/Wasserstein distance, bounded Lipschitz tests,
angular small-prime separation and Ankeny variants found this and related
zero-independence work but no identical combined theorem. Negative search
results do not establish historical priority.
