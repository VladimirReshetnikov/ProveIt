the proof that Taylor coefficients give M_j and its rigorous scaled
moments algorithm. The generator uses rational Euler--Maclaurin bounds,
high-precision decimal intervals and Taylor coefficients of elementary
factors and the gamma function. It uses no tabulated zero heights.

To regenerate and independently compare the four input intervals:
1. Extract the archive into any directory.
2. Run python3 verification/certify_moments.py from that directory.
3. Run python3 /path/to/zeta_finite.py verification/certified_moments.json.
With no input-file argument zeta_finite.py verifies the inequalities
from the four explicit rational intervals embedded in it.
