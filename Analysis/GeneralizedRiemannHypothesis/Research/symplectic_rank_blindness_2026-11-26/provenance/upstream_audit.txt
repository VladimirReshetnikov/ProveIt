Main proof architecture
1. Theorem A: span_short_integralRamified_eq_top and
   indexLe_normalizedPoincare supply short integral source forms and an index.
2. Theorem B: enough_forms supplies sufficiently many independent translates.
3. Theorem C: integral_forms_long supplies a lower bound on the same corrected
   covolume via twists, determinant ratios and product-formula heights.
4. star_inequality cancels the common main term to give
   D.m * D.totalRemainder <= D.f * D.a * D.logB^5.
5. eventually_primeRemainder_le_rpow gives the one-sided bound
   R(y)<=y^(-1/2+epsilon) eventually for a specially arranged modulus/twist.
6. LFunction_ne_zero_of_primeRemainder_le uses a polar Landau criterion and
   selection of a quadratic twist with no interfering zeros, converting
   that bound into zero-free half-planes.
7. New.zeros_on_line and New.zeta_zeros_on_line use the functional equation.

Ishizuka Sections 3.2 and 4 were read. The paper treats exact uniform
sampling of form-prescribed code subspaces by the isometry group,

The title/abstract and relevant sections of these sources support a scoped

INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION OF THE NEW FIXED-PROJECTION COUNT
The block-count derivation was checked independently, including q even.
For A of nullity k, K=ker A, t=dim(K intersect rad H), the necessary
condition is H|K=0. Then full B extensions number
q^{n(n+1)/2+k(k-1)/2} product_{j=k-t+1}^k(1-q^-j).
The reason is B11 has a prescribed alternating difference, B12 is fixed
of rank k-t, B21 is free, and B22 must be injective on ker B12.
The normalization from symplectic frame orbits is
q^{n(3n+1)/2} product_{i=s+1}^n(1-q^-2i), where rank H=2s.
The script symplectic_count_check.py directly enumerates all A,B at
(q,n)=(2,2),(3,2),(2,3), computing full rank and Gram matrices separately.
It verifies 4371 exact (A,H) counts across 268961 pairs. All pass.
The enumeration is evidence for small cases, not a substitute for proof.

INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE NONDEGENERATE-H MAIN THEOREM
Let n>=2 be even and H nondegenerate alternating of size n. Then t=0,
s=n/2, and C=product_{j=n/2+1}^n(1-q^-2j). The likelihood ratio is
  1_{ker A H-isotropic} q^{k(k-1)/2}/C,
which is at least 1 on its support. Therefore the full matrix-law total
variation distance from uniform is exactly the uniform probability that
ker A is not H-isotropic. No approximation is used in this identity.

The conditional probability that a uniform k-subspace is H-isotropic is
  a_n(k)=q^{-k(k-1)/2} product_{i=0}^{k-1}
           (1-q^{2i-n})/(1-q^{i-n})
for k<=n/2, and 0 otherwise. This follows by dividing the counts of ordered
isotropic and unrestricted independent k-tuples. The rank-law likelihood
is q^{k(k-1)/2}a_n(k)/C; it is 1/C at k=0,1 and below 1 at every k>=2.
For the latter assertion, the product decreases with k and its k=2 value
A2 satisfies
  1-A2=(q^2-q)q^-n/(1-q^{1-n}),
  1-C<q^-n/(q^2-1).
Thus A2<C. When k exceeds n/2 the likelihood is simply zero.

Writing u_n(k) for the uniform square-matrix corank law, exact rank TV is
  (u_n(0)+u_n(1))(C^-1-1).
Since C^-1-1~q^-n/(q^2-1), its first-order asymptotic is
  [(p_q(0)+p_q(1))/(q^2-1)] q^-n,
where p_q(k)=q^-k^2 P_infinity/P_k^2 and P_k=product_{j=1}^k(1-q^-j).
Meanwhile full matrix TV tends to
  delta_q=sum_{k>=2}p_q(k)(1-q^{-k(k-1)/2})>0.
Dominated convergence is justified, for example, by
u_n(k)<=q^-k^2/P_infinity^2, a summable majorant independent of n.

Targeted exact-phrase web searches on random symplectic submatrices,
symplectic block rank distributions, isotropic kernels, and total variation
found no matching rank-versus-full-matrix-law separation theorem. The
nearest primary sources were the classical counts above, Ishizuka's
samplers, and Fulman--Goldstein's rank distributions for independent or
symmetry-constrained matrices (arXiv:1211.0504). This supports only a
scoped novelty claim; it is not an exhaustive literature certificate.
