\paragraph{Editorial note (proposed, 12 October 2026).}
The fixed-parameter quadratic boundary regimes proposed in this question
are proved in \emph{Critical Boundaries of Quadratic Polylogarithm
Feedback}~\cite{QuadraticBoundary2026}, Theorem 1.1, with proofs in Sections 3--9.
The strict-boundary constant is established for every real $s>2$, including
integer-order logarithms and unique complex dominance. At criticality the
article supplies convergent noninteger charts, the marginal Lambert
normalization and every fixed inverse-logarithmic coefficient order, and
the finite-curvature law with its integer logarithmic obstruction.
These are fixed-parameter results. Uniform joint limits in the core and
order parameters remain separate research questions. The cited contribution
is an unrefereed manuscript with conventional proofs, not a new Lean
formalization.
