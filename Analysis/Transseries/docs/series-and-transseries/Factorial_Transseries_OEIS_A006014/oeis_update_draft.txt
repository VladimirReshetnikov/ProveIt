Suggested FORMULA/COMMENT addition for A006014

Let alpha=(1+i*sqrt(3))/2 and beta=(1-i*sqrt(3))/2, and interpret 2F0 as a
formal power series. Then

  Sum_{n>=1} a(n)*x^n = x*d/dx log(2F0(alpha,beta;;x)).

If u(n)=(alpha)_n*(beta)_n/n!, then

  n!*u(n) = A130032(n),
  Sum_{k=1}^n a(k)*u(n-k) = n*u(n).

Moreover a(n)/n! tends nondecreasingly to

  1/(Gamma(alpha)*Gamma(beta)) = cosh(Pi*sqrt(3)/2)/Pi.

An all-orders expansion begins

  a(n) ~ cosh(Pi*sqrt(3)/2)/Pi *
         (n! - 2*(n-1)! - 2*(n-3)! - 8*(n-4)!
          - (204/5)*(n-5)! - ...).

Put U(x)=2F0(alpha,beta;;x) and R(x)=U(-x)/U(x). The later coefficients phi(j)
are generated by

  Sum_{j>=0} phi(j)*x^(j-1) = R(x)/x + x*R'(x).

Suggested CROSSREF addition

Cf. A130032: its normalized product is the hypergeometric carrier whose
logarithmic derivative produces A006014. Cf. A073017 for the limiting constant.

Caution: 2F0 and the ordinary generating series are divergent formal series
(radius of convergence zero); they may alternatively be Borel summed in
appropriate sectors.
