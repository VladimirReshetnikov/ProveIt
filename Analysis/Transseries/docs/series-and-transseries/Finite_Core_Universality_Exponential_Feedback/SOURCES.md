## Novelty boundary

Condensation, finite background clouds, Lagrange inversion, saddle-point
asymptotics, and rapid-series composition methods are prior mathematics.
The proposed contribution is the sharp model-specific finite-core reduction,
its multiplicative saddle formula, the associated fluctuation and least-term
laws, and the application of a separately proved rapid-inverse lemma.
Global priority is not established.
