## Boundaries of the research claim

The report proves its stated results but does not certify exhaustive
historical novelty. The strongest proposed contributions are the
quantitative nonlinear synthesis in Sections 6–8, not the rediscovery of
classical moment-indeterminate measures. The general repository
Hahn-series realization question remains outside the scope of the results.
