## Primary mathematical literature

I. M. Gessel, *Lagrange inversion*, Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A
144 (2016), 212-249.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2016.06.018
https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.05988
Used to contextualize the classical formal inversion method, not as a source
of the signed quadratic asymptotic theorem.

M. Borinsky, *Generating asymptotics for factorially divergent sequences*,
arXiv:1603.01236, version 4 (8 October 2018).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.01236
https://arxiv.org/html/1603.01236v4
Used for the comparison with fixed positive-alpha gamma-scale asymptotic
algebras. The relevant classes admit an all-zero expansion. The article
correctly describes this inverse as flat in those fixed scales, not as
excluded from the classes.

D. Sauzin, *Introduction to 1-summability and resurgence*, arXiv:1405.0356 (2014).
https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.0356
Used for the distinction between Borel continuation and the growth needed
for directional Laplace summation.

G. A. Edgar, *Transseries for beginners*, arXiv:0801.4877 (2008).
https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.4877
Used for general transseries background and the formal/analytic distinction.

These sources are acknowledged in the article's embedded bibliography.
