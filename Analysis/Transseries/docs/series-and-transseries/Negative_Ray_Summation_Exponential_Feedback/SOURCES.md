## Novelty and verification scope

The negative-direction fine-summability equivalence, its exact inverse kernel
construction, and the uniform quadratic error/certificate results are the
proposed contribution of this draft. Targeted searches and predecessor review
are not a certification of global novelty or priority. No named longstanding
conjecture is claimed resolved, and no theorem is described as Lean-verified.

The exact numerical certificate files concern explicitly defined analytic
branches whose identities and derivative bounds are proved in the article.
Floating-point experiments are stored separately and are not mathematical
certificates. The repository was not modified.
