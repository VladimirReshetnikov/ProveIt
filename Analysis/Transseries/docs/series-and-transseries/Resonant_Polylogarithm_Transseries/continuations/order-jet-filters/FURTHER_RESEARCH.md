# Further research directions

The article's Section 9 states eight concrete next steps and their present limits.

1. Construct global higher-order Borel normal forms and distinguish cancellation
   of an action coefficient from removal of a local singular germ on all sheets.
2. Analyze coefficient norms and interpolation conditioning on -log(n) +/- i*pi;
   optimize filters under arithmetic and size restrictions, not only degree.
3. Prove a joint x,N limit for deep filters and neutral curves. The delivered
   theorem fixes N and does not establish growing-depth uniformity.
4. Transport the exact moving-order neutral curve through nonlinear inversion,
   retaining its order dependence and the nonzero physical completion.
5. Determine the exact complex convergence radius of the small-target inverse
   of H. The delivered radius sqrt(3)/40 is sufficient only.
6. Classify monotonicity, branches, and doubly exponential inverse scales for
   higher order-zero jets; positivity is proved here only for the first jet.
7. Optimize combined periodic-weight and order-jet filters while retaining the
   zero Fourier mode and distinguishing two different arithmetic freedoms.
8. Formalize the finite polynomial ideal and certify the analytic computations
   using interval contour and tail bounds. The retained checks are not such
   certificates.
