## Primary mathematical sources

1. David Sauzin, *Nonlinear analysis with resurgent functions*, Annales
   scientifiques de l'Ecole Normale Superieure (4), 48 (2015), no. 3, 667–702.
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.24033/asens.2255
   Author manuscript: https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.4477
   HTML consulted: https://arxiv.org/html/1212.4477
   Used specifically for the resurgent implicit-function theorem (Theorem 4)
   and the summable implicit-function theorem (Theorem 4'). The report
   verifies their analytic-parameter hypotheses for its explicit Borel
   kernel rather than claiming those closure theorems as new.

2. David Sauzin, *Introduction to 1-summability and resurgence* (2014).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.0356
   Background for Borel–Laplace summation and resurgence conventions.

3. Gerald A. Edgar, *Transseries: Composition, Recursion, and Convergence*
   (2009). https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.1259
   Context for established transseries composition and inversion.

4. NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, Section 4.13,
   *Lambert W-Function*. https://dlmf.nist.gov/4.13
   Used for the established Lambert inverse and branch-point background.
