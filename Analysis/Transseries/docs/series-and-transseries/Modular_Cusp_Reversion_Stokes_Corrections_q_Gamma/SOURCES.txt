The counterexample refutes the clause requiring every nonsingular convergent
directional Borel sum to equal the same fixed product. It does not refute the
Gevrey, meromorphic-continuation, or positive-ray reconstruction statements.
The article proves the latter in the stated fixed-argument disk and retains
a separate open problem for uniform parameter-dependent resurgent inversion.

Established results credited in the article include formal transseries
composition/reversion, analytic implicit inversion, Lagrange-Burmann reversion,
eta transformations, rational q-gamma multiplication, theta product identities,
and existing root-of-unity and q-Pochhammer resurgence. Primary references
include van der Hoeven, Edgar, Sauzin, Marmi--Sauzin, Garoufalidis--Kashaev,
Fantini--Rella, Kong--Teo, and the NIST DLMF. The full bibliography is in the
PDF and sections/09_references.tex.

The explicit development supplied here consists of:
1. A correctly normalized elementary Borel calculation and a nonzero Stokes
   correction for the repository's named directional assertion.
2. A convergent flat-cusp inverse with every coefficient, all small-nome
   logarithm sheets, and a uniform truncation bound.
3. Minimal three-factor eta and four-factor raw Euler-product constructions
   with arbitrary finite ramification and primitive supports.
4. A regular finite-endpoint inverse theorem with sharp Q0/t0^2 feedback,
   exact leading poles, and Lambert-W coefficients.
5. Rational q-gamma completions, a sparse theta inverse, and explicit
   exponentially small corrections to the original half-q-gamma inverse.

The manuscript does not make a worldwide priority claim or purport to solve
unrestricted complex transseries inversion. All theorems specify their formal
or analytic setting. Eleven research questions identify remaining boundaries.
No upstream repository files were changed or submitted as part of this work.
