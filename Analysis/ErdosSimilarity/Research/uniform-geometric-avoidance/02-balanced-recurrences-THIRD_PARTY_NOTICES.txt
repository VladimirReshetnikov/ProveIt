CHANGES IN THE PRESENT CONTINUATION

The avoidance article introduces signed block-crossing samples for stable
recurrence states, a rational parameter encoding including negative
denominators and exact zeros, compact exhaustions for balanced polynomial
recurrences, an outer-approximation certificate argument, and a
subexponential degree-growth extension. It provides a new full exposition,
exact finite checks, an explanatory figure, and research questions.
The inherited probabilistic mechanism is credited and reproved.
