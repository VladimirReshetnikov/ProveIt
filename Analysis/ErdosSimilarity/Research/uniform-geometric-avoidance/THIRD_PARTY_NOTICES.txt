CHANGES AND ADDITIONS IN THIS PACKAGE

The new article selects ratio-dependent original exponents at common
spatial scales, fixes all random tables independently of the varying
ratio, proves a two-parameter arrangement count including boundaries,
and uses the continuous original prefix to repair residual centers.
It gives full proofs of a polynomial-family extension, a simultaneous
signed exponential-polynomial avoidance theorem, and consequences for
positive-root linear recurrences. It also develops periodic tail
assembly, effective closedness and computable measure, and terminating
searches for finite robust certificates.

The exposition has been reorganized and rewritten for the uniform
theorems. The original explanatory figure, exact finite verification
program, proof-audit notes, and research questions were prepared for
this package.
