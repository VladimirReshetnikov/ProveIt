\section{Reproducible verification and integration}
\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{What establishes equality}

The logical dependencies are explicit. Theorem~\ref{thm:gaussian-inversion}
and Corollary~\ref{cor:gaussian-coefficient-rule} prove the Gaussian
double rows. Theorem~\ref{one2:thm:closed}, together with the two proved
half-point evaluations, proves the three triples and their extensions.
Theorem~\ref{thm:inverse-color} proves the mixed-root reductions.
The exact coefficient checks then verify that the printed polynomials
agree with these finite formulas. Neither high-precision quadrature
nor a small certified residual is used as a substitute for any of
these analytic arguments.

There is one useful distinction concerning numerical certificates.
An interval excluding zero disproves a proposed equality. An interval
containing zero, however narrow, cannot by itself prove equality of
arbitrary transcendental constants. Here the exact enclosures provide
independent reproducibility and error detection for identities proved
by other means. For the mixed diagonal counterexample, the entire
enclosure is strictly positive, which independently certifies its
nonvanishing as well as the integral proof.

\subsection{The exact pipeline}

The core evaluator uses only the Python standard library's rational
arithmetic. Symbolic coefficient extraction uses SymPy. No quadrature,
floating-point MPL routine, or black-box integer-relation search is
called by the exact certificate producer. The dependency chain is:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Convert the MPL indices and colors to the GPL word by
\eqref{eq:mpl-gpl}, checking the endpoint and letter hypotheses exactly.
\item Choose the least cutoff passing the word-specific sum of the
proved binomial tails. The explicit bound in
\eqref{audit:eq:Nchoice} supplies a guaranteed finite search endpoint.
\item Compute all H\"older factors through two shared chains of exact
Taylor coefficients, form the rational center, and record the exact
rational error radius.
\item Enclose the real and imaginary coordinates of all required atoms.
Evaluate each polynomial with exact rational interval operations.
Every product and power is enclosed by its interval extremal values.
\item Independently reproduce each H\"older factor by finite nested
sums and cumulative inner sums, rather than by the differential
recurrence used by the producer. Recompute the tail budget and require
exact equality of every rational center and radius.
\end{enumerate}

The encoded interpretation of a record is
\[
 \left|\texttt{prefactor}\,G(\texttt{word};1)
                 -\texttt{center}\right|\le\texttt{radius},
 \qquad\texttt{prefactor}\in\{-1,1\}.
\]
The prefactor incorporates the MPL-to-GPL sign once. The center already
includes it. The replay checker independently validates the cutoff,
endpoint hypotheses, permitted letters, and sign before accepting a
record. Names such as \texttt{G} or \texttt{beta\_6} in the atom catalog
refer to the imaginary coordinate of a certificate for the full
complex value $\Li_2(i)$ or $\Li_6(i)$; the exact GPL word and the
displayed convention determine the underlying complex value.

The scientific-notation residual bound in the table is rounded upward.
The authoritative bounds in the JSON files are exact fractions.
Thus the claims do not depend on decimal formatting or a chosen
floating-point precision. The replay of the saved centers uses a
different finite formula but shares the elementary rational arithmetic
class; it is an independent mathematical implementation, not a claim
of independent hardware or a formally verified arithmetic library.

\subsection{Independent high-precision checks}

The package also retains the independent computations developed during
the proofs. These include two exact parity coefficient implementations
through every even weight $2,4,\ldots,20$ (100 pairs), the differential
derivation of the twelve displayed weight-six and weight-eight rows,
and exact extraction of all 24 real and imaginary one-$2$ rows at
weights four, five, and six.

Separate quadratures compare the twelve Gaussian double rows at 90
decimal digits, the four mixed-root correction rows at 60 digits,
and the all-position one-$2$ formula at 100 digits. For the last family,
the 112 moment comparisons cover all positions at weights two through
eight at four distinct endpoints, and additional direct nested-series
checks use interior points of the disk. The separate sixth-root
script adds 56 moment comparisons at the two conjugate primitive sixth
roots and four explicit example checks. Fifteen shifted-polygamma
checks and 160 exact checks of the explicit tail prescription are
included. These are described as numerical cross-checks where
appropriate; their recorded small residuals are not mislabeled as
rigorous quadrature errors.

\subsection{Files, commands, and proposed source changes}

The article is built from a standalone root \TeX\ file and modular
section files. The package includes a \texttt{Makefile}, dependency
versions, exact JSON results, source hashes, the independent scripts,
and a unified patch against the pinned manuscript. In a terminal
opened at the extracted package root, the principal commands are
\begin{verbatim}
make pdf
python3 code/verify.py --bits 384
python3 code/replay_certificates.py
python3 code/run_independent.py --quick
python3 code/run_independent.py
python3 code/independent/one_two/sixth_root.py --digits 100
\end{verbatim}
The first two Python commands regenerate the core evidence. The quick
independent run performs symbolic and exact finite checks; the final
independent runner also runs its quadratures at their original
precisions; the final command checks the sixth-root specialization. These scripts locate outputs relative to their own paths,
so they do not require a particular current working directory or any
network service. The compiled PDF is supplied, so compilation is
optional for reading the article.

The correction patch is a proposed change for review. It updates the
proof status of the eight displayed identities, supplies the four
mixed reductions, fixes the color-exchange statement and the diagonal
example, and corrects the shifted-polygamma commentary. It deliberately
does not replace a reported formal residual count with an invented
numerical dimension. Those relation matrices need a separate exact
recomputation. The patch has been checked for applicability to the
pinned source; integration into a later revision requires resolving
any intervening editorial changes.

The package's \texttt{INTEGRATION.md} gives the intended companion
location, the theorem labels needed for proof pointers, and the source
hashes. The existing source files were read and audited; the repository
was not changed as part of preparing this deliverable.
