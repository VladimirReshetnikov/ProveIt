## Additional corrections made in consolidation

1. Absolute convergence at boundary s1=1 was falsely inferred from oscillation.
2. The generalized-polylogarithm letters used suffix products despite an
   outer-first/decreasing-index convention; they must use prefix products.
3. HPL integration with a leading zero is usually convergent. The all-zero
   word, not every leading-zero word, requires endpoint regularization.
4. Reflection and squaring do not preserve the entire {0,1,-1} alphabet.
5. The Nielsen integral sign description and the word for S(1,2) were wrong.
6. MZV numerical dimensions and Gaussian period lower bounds were conflated
   with formal spanning or motivic dimensions. These are now separated.
7. The even-weight odd-Euler-sum formulas now have an all-order analytic proof.
8. A canary coefficient of zero is not a proof, and its algebraic independence
   from the search basket was never established.
9. Exact row cancellation with a numerical branch guard leaves a separate
   branch-domain obligation.
10. Fixed finite ladder seeds do not prove global non-existence of a higher
    ladder. The modified-polylogarithm sum ends at m-1, not m.
11. The Herglotz denominator-only collapse criterion has the direct F(1/7)
    counterexample; literal cotangent at integer arguments is undefined.
12. The fresh Clausen check confirms the original W(12) = 11 sqrt(3) Cl2(pi/3)/9.
    An erroneous proposed editorial correction was caught and discarded.
13. Cubic subfields, their degree-six normal closures and the quadratic class
    field extensions are distinct fields. Signed unit-log determinants must
    not be replaced by absolute values without changing coefficients.
14. Distinguish the odd-weight sine series from the usual cosine Clausen
    convention, and retain the 2/3 factor relating Cl2(2pi/3) to Gieseking.
15. The trivial-zero L-function bridge uses L''(-k)/(2 L'(-k)); conductor-one
    principal functions require their meromorphic completed equation.
16. The Nielsen inversion paragraph confused an insufficient search basket
    with failure of closure of the whole family. A finite coefficient formula,
    derived from its differential ladder and endpoint data at -1, supplies the
    exact inversion and the missing mixed-log terms. Four defining-integral
    comparisons check its normalization; no new independence claim is made.
