\subsection{Mixed integer tail moments and reduced higher anchors}
\label{mta:section}
The existing anchors admit the explicit reductions
\begin{align}
 J_7&=21\zeta(7)-8\zeta(2)\zeta(5)-4\zeta(3)\zeta(4),\label{mta:J7}\\
 K_8&=10\zeta(2)\zeta(3)^2-50\zeta(3)\zeta(5)
                 -10\zeta(5,3)+\frac{4927}{96}\zeta(8),\label{mta:K8}\\
 K_9&=1086\zeta(9)-222\zeta(2)\zeta(7)
                  +171\zeta(3)\zeta(6)-858\zeta(4)\zeta(5).
 \label{mta:K9}
\end{align}
Their exact certificates use respectively three, seventeen, and fifty-four
classical relation rows. This does not prove arithmetic independence.

For integer tail orders $p_i\ge2$, put $W=\sum_i p_i$, $R=W-d$,
and $T_n=\prod_i\zeta(p_i,n+1)$. Every regular centered moment
of degree $m\equiv R\pmod2$, with the prescribed polynomial subtraction,
has a finite rational MZV expression of weight at most $W-1$;
for odd $m$ the ceiling is $W-2$. Odd and unequal orders are allowed.
The already proved even-order recurrences retain their original attribution.

A direct telescoping specialization, with $x=n+1/2$, is
\[
 \sum_{n\ge0}\left[(23x+232x^3+240x^5)
 \zeta(2,n+1)\zeta(3,n+1)-120x^2-76\right]=-15.
\]
The bracket is an ordinary absolutely summable term.

With $\Delta_n=T_{n-1}-T_n$, every $q\ge0$ and $a>0$ satisfy
\[
 \sum_{n\ge1}\Delta_n\gamma_q(n+a)
 =T_0\gamma_q(a)-\sum_{k\ge0}T_k\frac{\log^q(k+a)}{k+a}.
\]
The right side has a finite multiple-polylogarithm Mellin representation and,
at $a=1$, a finite expression in final-index derivatives of convergent MZVs.
Anchored Gamma-ratio identities and every Hurwitz-zero order-derivative
primitive follow with all additive constants fixed.
