# Artifact map

- `article.tex`, `article.pdf`: complete standalone article and its rendered PDF.
- `certificates/`: exact J7, K8, and K9 relation coefficients.
- `verification/`: exact compiler, relation generators, independent checker, numerical diagnostics, CLI, and runner.
- `results/`: original exact/numerical receipts, formula tables, environment and PDF build/review evidence.
- `integration/`: proposed placement, status wording and optional TeX fragment.
- `SOURCE_AUDIT.md`, `provenance.json`, `CLAIM_STATUS.json`: precise source and claim boundaries.
- `README.md`, `requirements.txt`, `build.py`: use and reproduction.

The source repository has not been modified. No font files or third-party paper PDFs are distributed.
