\paragraph{Finite-part conventions.}
An endpoint constant must specify its limiting variable and subtraction
polynomial. For example, with
\[
 Z_2(s)=\frac{\zeta(s)^2-\zeta(2s)}{2},
\]
the constant of the cutoff harmonic sum in the variable $\log N$ is
$(\gamma^2-\zeta(2))/2$, the Abel endpoint constant of
$\operatorname{Li}_{1,1}(z,1)$ in $-\log(1-z)$ is zero, and the Laurent
constant of $Z_2(1+t)$ is
$(\gamma^2-\zeta(2))/2-\gamma_1$. These are distinct operations.
A root change $w=z^{1/q}$ also changes the endpoint logarithmic variable
by $\log q$; the entire subtraction polynomial must be shifted before its
constant is compared. Spectral differentiation of $\zeta(s,a)$ is denoted
by primes, whereas differentiation of its Hurwitz parameter is written
explicitly as $\partial_a$.
