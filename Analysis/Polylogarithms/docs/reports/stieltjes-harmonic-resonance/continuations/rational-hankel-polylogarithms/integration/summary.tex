\section{Rational Hankel sums and a finite polylogarithm alphabet}
\label{rhp:section}
Let
\[
 R(x)=P(x)+\sum_{a\in A}\sum_{\ell=1}^{s_a}
       c_{a,\ell}(x+a)^{-\ell},\qquad A\subset\mathbb Z_{\ge0},
\]
and put
\[
 D_{r,M}=\det\left(\sum_{n=1}^M n^{i+j}R(n)\right)_{0\le i,j<r}.
\]
For a pole of order $s$, retain the positive entries of
$s,s-2,s-4,\ldots$. Let $W_r$ be the sum of the $r$ largest entries in
the multiset obtained from all poles, or all entries if fewer than $r$
are available.

\begin{theorem}[Confluent pole allocation and exact reduction]
\label{rhp:compiler}
The generating function $\sum_{M\ge1}D_{r,M}z^M$ is a finite rational
linear combination of $1$ and positive-index multiple polylogarithms
of weight at most $W_r$. Rational prefactors have poles only at $0,1$,
and the identity holds for $|z|<1$ with removable values at zero.
In particular, every integral quadratic denominator gives, at every
rank, an expression of the form
\[
 A(z)+B(z)L+C(z)L^2+E(z)\operatorname{Li}_2(z),
       \qquad L=-\log(1-z),
\]
with rational coefficients, even with an arbitrary polynomial part.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof mechanism]
The local harmonic block at $-a$ factors through the derivative columns
$\binom ip(-a)^{i-p}$. Its $(p,q)$ entry is
$\sum_{\ell>p+q}c_{a,\ell}h_{a,\ell-p-q}$ and has harmonic weight at
most $s_a-p-q$. A minor selecting $k_a$ rows and columns at that pole
has weight at most $k_a(s_a-k_a+1)$. Cauchy--Binet and optimization give
$W_r$. Integer shift corrections preserve the sum of harmonic weight
and inverse-cutoff order. Finite harmonic quasi-shuffles, followed by
anchored operators
\[
 \mathcal J_hF(z)=z^{-h}\int_0^z t^{h-1}F(t)\,dt,
\]
and rational Hermite reduction produce the stated finite expression.
The complete report gives the full proof, exact algorithm, Gamma-product
sharp coefficient, endpoint cancellation jets, and all-rank spectral
Stieltjes identities.
\end{proof}

As explicit convergent examples, the report proves
\[
 \sum_{M\ge1}\frac{H_M H_M^{(3)}-(H_M^{(2)})^2}{M(M+1)}
       =\frac{35}{2}\zeta(5)-9\zeta(2)\zeta(3)
\]
and
\[
 \sum_{M\ge1}\frac{M(H_{M+1}^{(2)}-1)-(H_{M+1}-1)^2}
                        {M(M+1)(M+2)}
       =\frac52\zeta(3)-\frac32\zeta(2)-\frac12.
\]
The familiar low-weight zeta reductions are illustrative consequences,
not claims of historical novelty. Formal harmonic sharpness is distinct
from minimal functional weight, and neither this result nor its exact
checks resolves the unrestricted moment-prefix, $S_6$, or revised
$S_8$ conjectures.
