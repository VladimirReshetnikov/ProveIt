\section{Affine Gamma squares and Bernoulli selectors}
\label{agint:section}
Let $D u_j=u_{j+1}$ and let $\mathcal N$ be the canonical linear
projection onto functional monomials: powers of $u_0$, or monomials
whose largest positive index is repeated. Set
$A_0=1$, $A_{m+1}=u_0A_m-DA_m$, and $N_m=\mathcal N(A_m)$.
\begin{theorem}[All derivative squares]\label{agint:squares}
For $r\ge2$, $a,k\ge0$, and $m=a+2k+2r+2$,
\[
 [u_0^au_1^ku_r^2]N_m=
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\frac{(-1)^rm!}{a!2^{k+r+1}k!(r+1)!
                      \prod_{j=0}^r(k+2j+1)},&k\text{ even},\\[5pt]
 0,&k\text{ odd}.
 \end{cases}
\]
\end{theorem}
The proof in \emph{Affine Polarization of Gamma Normal Forms},
Sections 2--3, includes the Appell primitive lemma and constant-coefficient
weighted quadratic symbol. The pure-square and $r=2$ cases are inherited.
The corresponding even entire function is
\[
 g_r(y)=\frac{(-1)^r}{(r+1)!}
       \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial(y^2)}\right)^{r+1}\cosh y
 =\frac{(-1)^r}{2^{2r+1}r!(r+1)!}
       \int_0^1(1-v^2)^r\cosh(yv)\,dv.
\]
It yields finite Hurwitz/Lerch endpoint formulas and anchored Stieltjes
primitives; see the full article for domains and removable-pole rules.

For $r\ge2$ set $P_r(v)=B_{2r}((v+1)/2)-B_{2r}$ and
$d_{rj}=(2r)!B_{2r-j}(1)/(2^j(2r-j)!)$.
\begin{theorem}[Single-zeta selectors]\label{agint:selectors}
As meromorphic functions of $s$,
\[
 \int_1^3P_r(x-2)\zeta(s,x)\,dx
 =-\frac{(2r)!\zeta(s-2r)}{2^{2r-1}(1-s)_{2r}}
  -\sum_{j=2}^{2r}\frac{(-1)^jd_{rj}(1+2^{j+1-s})}{(1-s)_{j+1}}.
\]
For even $P$ of degree at most $2R$ with $P(1)=P'(1)=0$, a nonzero
transform of this form with exactly one prescribed shifted zeta
$\zeta(s-2r)$ and remainder in $\mathbb C(s)+2^{-s}\mathbb C(s)$
occurs exactly when $P$ is a nonzero scalar multiple of $P_r$.
\end{theorem}
The proof and the explicit selector expansion are in Section 9 of the
full article. The classification is functional over $\mathbb C(s)$;
it does not assert independence of numerical special values.
A particularly simple consequence is the ordinary convergent identity
\[
 \int_1^3(x-1)^2(3-x)^2\psi'(x)\,dx=\frac{6\zeta(3)}{\pi^2}.
\]
Neither theorem resolves the general unequal-tail optimal-prefix
conjecture or the Gaussian $S_6$ and revised $S_8$ candidates.
