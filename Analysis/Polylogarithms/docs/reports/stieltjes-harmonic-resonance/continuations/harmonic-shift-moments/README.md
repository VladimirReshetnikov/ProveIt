# Harmonic Shift Singularities and Exact Moment Identities

Research continuation for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project, dated 11 October 2026.

The complete article is `Harmonic_Shift_Singularities_and_Exact_Moment_Identities.pdf`. Its portable, single-file source is `Harmonic_Shift_Singularities_and_Exact_Moment_Identities.tex`. The same article is also supplied as modular TeX for integration.

## Principal results

1. **Exact harmonic shift germs.** The Gamma-normalized difference is identified with an existing relative ordered Hurwitz character. Its first nonremovable shift, full singular amplitude, Arakawa–Kaneko boundary constant, normalized Lerch/polylogarithm primitives, exact Taylor radius, and functional rank transition are proved.
2. **Mixed polygamma moments.** In every weight, the Bell-row span has rank `floor(w/2)`. At weights at least six, the ordinary summation image has rank three. Exact matrices at weights six and eight give the full elimination and an infinite family of zero identities, including a compact quartic identity and a convergent generalized-harmonic tail-square sum equal to `2*zeta(7)`.
3. **All centered harmonic-power moments.** For fixed power `p`, all values `F_p(-2r,1/2)` lie in a single finite rational span of Euler sums of weight at most `p-1`, independent of `r`. A triangular generator gives all powers and all centered even moments; the first two powers have all-moment Bernoulli formulas. Odd generalized harmonic orders also admit a sufficient parity theorem and finite ordinary-zeta evaluations.
4. **Exact geometric polygamma collisions.** A finite principal-part correction leaves a remainder jointly holomorphic in every shift. It gives oriented nested-rate harmonic laws, quartic constants, and a fully evaluated derivative example equal to `2*EulerGamma*zeta(2) - zeta(3)*log(2)`.

The article contains detailed proofs, explicit branch and normalization conventions, source attribution, a focused audit, and thirteen further research questions. Claims of advancement are relative to the inspected ProveIt material; literature-wide priority is not asserted for individual special-value consequences.

The current Gaussian `S6` and revised `S8` conjectures remain open. Positive Stieltjes indices in the geometric collision theorem, minimal arithmetic Euler-sum coordinates, and general spectral derivatives of the raw harmonic-power values also remain open.

## Build the PDF

Use a standard TeX Live installation with `latexmk`, Latin Modern, AMS packages, `mathrsfs`, `microtype`, `booktabs`, `longtable`, `enumitem`, `fancyhdr`, `hyperref`, and `cleveref`.

From this directory:

```bash
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error Harmonic_Shift_Singularities_and_Exact_Moment_Identities.tex
```

To edit the modular source and regenerate the standalone article:

```bash
python3 code/build_standalone.py
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error Harmonic_Shift_Singularities_and_Exact_Moment_Identities.tex
```

Alternatively, compile `article.tex` directly. No shell escape, external figures, bibliography processor, or network connection is required to typeset the article.

## Reproduce the checks

The programs use Python 3, SymPy, and mpmath. Install `requirements.txt` in your preferred Python environment, then run:

```bash
python3 code/run_all.py
```

The independent high-precision quadratures and symbolic coefficient calculations take longer than typesetting. Each checker writes its own JSON result. The driver additionally writes a log per checker and `results/verification_run.json` for that invocation. The bundled mathematical result files were generated by individual completed checker runs; the driver is a convenience for replaying them together.

To list the checkers or run one family:

```bash
python3 code/run_all.py --list
python3 code/run_all.py --only verify_harmonic_powers
```

`derive_polygamma_collisions.py` includes an exact assertion for the displayed derivative constant and prints several further formal coefficient calculations. Its printed rows are supplementary computations, not additional theorems asserted without proof.

### Meaning of the evidence

The finite rational matrices and polynomial coefficient calculations are exact. The infinite identities are justified by the analytic proofs in the article. Floating-point tail subtractions, Cauchy extraction, and finite-part quadratures are independent diagnostics; they are not rigorous interval enclosures. Collision diagnostics approach a holomorphic limit and are not expected to vanish at a nonzero shift.

An internal independent review checked the shift, Bell, harmonic-power, generalized-harmonic, and collision proofs. No external peer review or proof-assistant formalization is claimed.
