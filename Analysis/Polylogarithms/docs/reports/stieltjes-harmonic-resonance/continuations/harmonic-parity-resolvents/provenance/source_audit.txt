3. Precisely answered questions.

Independent Orders, Section 7, Question 2: quartic and higher directional
layers. Cubic Harmonic Mixed Orders, Section 7, research problem 4:
complete quartic directional layer.

The new theorem gives every quartic derivative in the three coordinates
Lambda = d^4/dt^4 T(t,t,t)|0,
U = R_AAA(0), Xi = R_AAB(0),
where the elementary polar normalization R is inherited from Independent
Orders. U is a j=3 specialization of an existing convergent Gamma moment
formula. Xi receives a new explicit absolutely convergent subtracted
polylogarithmic integral. All other quartic coefficients are ordinary
zeta derivatives at 0 and -1.

Independent Orders, Section 7, Question 3: systematic cancellation.
The cubic cyclic cancellation extends to quartic:

  sum_cyclic W_(1,2,3)^(4)(0) - 36 Lambda
    = -184 log(2 pi) zeta'''(0)
      + 156 zeta''(0)^2 - 386 zeta''''(0).

At every remainder degree d >= 2 the homogeneous kernel of the named
symmetry, C-axis, and Euler-plane constraints is precisely

  A(A-C) S(A,C) + B(B-C) S(B,C) + AB Q(A,B,C),

with S symmetric homogeneous degree d-2 and Q homogeneous degree d-2
symmetric in A,B. Its dimension is floor(d/2)+floor(d^2/4).
The cyclic image is ABC times fully symmetric degree d-2 polynomials,
with dimension floor(((d+1)^2+3)/12). An explicit deformation at d=4
shows that diagonal fifth-derivative data cannot determine all cyclic
fifth derivatives using these relations alone.
