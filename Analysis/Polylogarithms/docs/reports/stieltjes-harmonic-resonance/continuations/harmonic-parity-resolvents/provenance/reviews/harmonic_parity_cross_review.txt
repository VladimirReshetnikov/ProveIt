1. General-a Bernoulli expansion.

For x=n+a, H_n=psi(x+1-a)+gamma. The shifted digamma expansion gives
  psi(x+1-a) ~ log x - sum_{k>=1} B_k(a)/(k x^k),
using B_k(1-a)=(-1)^k B_k(a). Thus the uniform negative sign in A(z,a)
is correct. Differentiating r-1 times and multiplying by

  z^(r-1)/(r-1)
  + sum_{k>=1} binom(k+r-2,r-2) B_k(a) z^(k+r-1)/(r-1),
so every coefficient and sign in T_r is correct.

ellth derivative of 1/(s+j-1). The remaining coefficient is ell! q.

normal convergence in Re s>-J as claimed.

2. Formal injectivity and invariant-ring classification.

The centered formal trigamma f has the displayed expansion and satisfies
  f(x+1)-f(x)=-(x+1/2)^(-2).
The second-order homogeneous equation is correctly obtained by taking
the ratio of this rational inhomogeneous term under x -> x+1.

The rational-solution obstruction is valid. In each nonempty finite
pole orbit modulo Z, the smallest pole creates an uncancelled pole one
step earlier in the forward difference, while the largest creates an
uncancelled pole at itself. These are distinct. The right side has one
pole in exactly one orbit, so no rational solution exists.

Under the verified ADH theorem, f is differentially transcendental
over C(x), hence all finite derivative tuples are algebraically
independent. The higher harmonic tails are invertible affine multiples
of these derivatives. Adding X_1 uses the independent formal symbol L;
the highest L coefficient of a nonzero polynomial cannot vanish.
This establishes injectivity over C without assuming numerical
independence of Euler's constant or zeta values.

At the center, parity is simultaneous sign reversal of all even-order
tails. Injectivity converts vanishing of all odd expansion coefficients
