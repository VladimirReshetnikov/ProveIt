1. Mittag-Leffler entire remainder.
The contour |w|=N+1/2 stays at least distance 1/2 from EVERY integer, by the reverse triangle inequality. On the right half the standard digamma expansion is uniform. On the left, reflection sends 1-w into Re>=1, and cot(pi w) is uniformly bounded on these circles. Thus f(w)=psi(w)^4-P0(w)=O(log^4 N). The integrand z f(w)/(w(w-z)) has integral O(log^4 N/N). Residues at z and 0 are f(z),-f(0); at -n they are -(Pn(z)-Pn(0)). Hence there is no undetermined entire remainder. Normal convergence follows from O(log^3 n/n^2) of the slowest subtracted term.
