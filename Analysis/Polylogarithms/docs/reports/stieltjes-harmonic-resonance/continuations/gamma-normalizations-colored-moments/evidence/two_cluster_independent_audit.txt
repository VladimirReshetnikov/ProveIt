INDEPENDENT AUDIT: TWO-CLUSTER SPECTRAL FACES

Audited draft: sections/04_clusters.tex (draft before the optional exposition refinement).
Audit scope: ordinary face normalization, finite polylogarithm coefficients,
continuation for either ordering of nonnegative B,C, mass derivatives, and
the anchored primitive. No source edits were made.

Conclusion: no substantive mathematical error or required correction found.

1. The simplex measure is the coordinate measure dt_1...dt_{k-1}, with no
surface-area factor. The independent Dirichlet regulator identity has the
correct Gamma normalization. Aggregation of the B and C regulators and
squarefree extraction give exactly r!s!, followed by the coefficient
[X^(r-1)Y^(s-1)] of the chord-subtracted beta integral.

2. In the finite product, choosing d nonconstant factors supplies (-Y)^d.
The denominator expansions have total degree r+s-d-1, so the overall sign
is (-1)^(r+s-1) and the binomial coefficient is
binomial(r+s-d-1,r-1). The positive compositions have weight r+s-1.
This confirms every coefficient in P_{r,s}, the stated depth bound, the
collision coefficient, and the r=s=2 worked-integral normalization.

3. The chord-subtracted logarithmic integral and the stated outer-color
multiple polylogarithms are holomorphic on C minus [1,infinity). Thus the
identity continues to every negative q, including q<-1. For x>0 and
B,C>=0, q=(C-B)/(x^2+C)<1. Exchanging clusters yields the stated Landen
transformation q -> q/(q-1) on the same branch domain. Each P summand is
O(q^2) at zero, giving the claimed O(n^-4) spectral tail. No missing
small-displacement restriction was found.

4. P'_{r,s}(q)=R_{r,s}(q)/(1-q). The chain rule gives the denominator
(n+a)^2+C for the C derivative and (n+a)^2+B for the B derivative, with
exactly the displayed signs. These derivatives move the full repeated
cluster. The primitive is anchored correctly because q=0 at C=B and
P_{r,s}(0)=0. The oriented integral remains correct when C<B.

Optional exposition refinement only: explicitly say that every proper-subset
Dirichlet term has a holomorphic extension by the same chord subtraction,
or is linear when it contains only one cluster. This is already implicit
in the proof and does not require a change to any theorem.
