INDEPENDENT MATHEMATICAL AUDIT OF sections/03_colored.tex

sections/03_colored.tex (draft before the listed wording refinements)

Result: no incorrect main formula found. The following were independently
derived and checked before and after reading the section.

1. Uniform tail / normality
Let h(t)=exp(-a t)/(1-omega exp(-t)). After u=x t,
Fomega=x^(-1)/Gamma(1+z) integral_0^infinity u^z exp(-u)h(u/x) du.
Taylor expansion gives h^(j)(0)(1+z)_j/(j! x^(j+1)). Since the rising
factorial has degree j, the qth jet cancels every j<q term. The leading
term is (-1)^q Phi(omega,-q,a)x^(-q-1), as printed.
On compact color sets outside [1,infinity), the denominator is uniformly
bounded away from zero for t>=0; fixed t-derivatives are uniformly bounded,
and the tail decays exponentially for positive a in a compact interval.
Taylor remainder and Cauchy differentiation give the stated local normality.

2. Mellin formula
Absolute Fubini works directly for every permitted color, without first
expanding a potentially divergent omega-power series. Initially 0<Re u<1
and Re(u+z)>0. It gives Gamma(u+z)Gamma(1-u)/Gamma(1+z)
times Phi(omega,1-u,a), and hence the stated Qq factor. Differentiated
kernel decay extends to 0<Re u<q+1. All signs are correct.

3. Ordinary positive-integer moments
The substitution t=x/(x+c) gives exactly
c^(m-1) integral_0^1 t^(m-1)log(t)^q/(1-t)^m dt.
Expansion of (1-t)^(-m) gives the positive series for L_mq with sign
(-1)^q. Its finite zeta expression in the section agrees coefficientwise.
At the Mellin pole, the factor is (-1)^m Qq'(m), as printed.

4. Log insertions and Stieltjes dictionary
The (-1)^m factor and r! extraction in col:log-moments are correct.
The root-of-unity scaling is exactly 1/d, with x/d inside the uncolored
kernel; the pole and extra log-polynomial terms cancel by sum omega^r=0.
The alternating C1 formula has the correct gamma_1(a_plus)-gamma_1(a_minus)
sign. Its integral is -pi^2/12.

5. Exceptional moments
For q=m-1, A_(m-1)=0 improves decay from x^(-m) to O(x^(-m-1)).
The order derivative of Phi has a minus sign because s=1-u, so the
answer is (-1)^(m+1) Phi_s(omega,1-m,a), exactly as printed.
The proof could explicitly say that improved decay makes the Mellin
integral holomorphic for 0<Re u<m+1, allowing evaluation at u=m.
This is an optional explanatory addition, not a correction to the result.

6. Alternating half shift / beta
Phi(-1,s,1/2)=2^s beta(s). The functional equation gives
beta'(1-2r)=(-1)^(r-1)2^(2r-1)(2r-1)! pi^(1-2r)beta(2r).
After the exceptional-moment minus sign and 2^(1-2r) factor, this gives
(-1)^r(2r-1)!beta(2r)/pi^(2r-1), including -Catalan/pi at r=1.

7. Anchored primitive
The difference Phi(omega,z,x+a)-Phi(omega,z,x+a0) vanishes at z=0.
The printed -q![z^q]x^z/z formula differentiates to Cq exactly.
At a root of unity, the log(d) term cancels in the anchored difference,
so the Gamma primitive has no extra factor 1/d. Its sign is correct.

8. Color-one expansion
The centered function e^(-a lambda)Phi(e^(-lambda),1-m,a) has principal
part Gamma(m)lambda^(-m), constant -B_m(a)/m, and the printed convergent
Hurwitz series for |lambda|<2pi. Multiplying by exp(a lambda) contributes
a^m/m to the raw constant term. Both normalization signs are correct.

9. Uniqueness hypothesis
The proof is sound with an interval of positive length: the kth density
coefficient involves d^(r)(-1)/r! times the rth derivative of (s)_k and
the polynomial A_k(omega,a). Successive k=r terms enforce d^(r)(-1)=0.
Recommended wording change: replace 'nonempty positive interval' by
'nonempty open interval contained in (0,infinity)' to exclude singleton
intervals, where the polynomial nonvanishing argument does not apply.

Pointwise uniqueness really can fail, not merely because of wording:
at a=(1+sqrt(2))/2, omega=-3-2sqrt(2), A2=A3=0 and A4=(2-sqrt(2))/64.
Therefore 2 C3-log(x)C2 has coefficient vector (1,4,5,2), satisfies the
same d0=1 normalization, and is O(x^-4). It differs from the canonical
vector (1,3,3,1). The exact counterexample is included in sections/03_colored.tex and
verified by verification/verify_colored_moments.py.

The author incorporated the suggested clarification and counterexample.
