PROOF SCOPE

The finite construction preserves the cumulative root alphabet, handles
coincident colors, terminates by strict depth decrease, and leaves only
the first spectral order free.  Its output depth is at most P+1 in the
mixed case, P in the all-positive case, and one in the all-nonpositive
case.  This is an upper bound for the construction, not a minimal-depth
or independence assertion.  The continuation proof correctly distinguishes
interior convergence, boundary absolute convergence, and subsequent
meromorphic continuation.  If every original color differs from one,
each output term is individually entire in the free outer order.

At nonpositive outer integers, reciprocal-Gamma zeros remove the
holomorphic Mellin remainder from the constant Laurent coefficient.
They do not remove it from all higher regular coefficients or from
the constant coefficient of an outer-order derivative.  The article
states this boundary correctly.  The logarithmic budget P_1 counts
positive-order original factors whose color is one.

The C=4 evaluation is supported by a complete beta-integral proof of
the classical height-one identity, with Aomoto--Drinfel'd attribution
through Kaneko--Ohno, Eq. (5).  The U pole theorem establishes actual
orders, using nonzero even Bernoulli numbers, rather than only possible
pole locations.  The U(-1) Laurent expansion and the U(-2) Laurent
constant have the correct signs and reciprocal-Gamma factors.
