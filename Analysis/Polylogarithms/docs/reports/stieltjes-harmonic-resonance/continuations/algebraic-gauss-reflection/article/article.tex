\begin{abstract}
An explicit research question in the repository asks for a finite algebraic
proof of a Bernoulli-coefficient identity previously obtained from residues
of Gauss summation. We give such a proof, using integer-root interpolation
and a degree argument. We then evaluate the entire resonant coefficient
tail: its normalized exponential generating function is
$\e^{ct}{}_2F_1(a,b;r+1;1-\e^t)$, and every coefficient has a finite
Stirling-number formula. The coefficients form an Appell family in the
comparison shift.

A second development uses the moving reflection $a\mapsto1+u-a$ in the
complex-order Barnes--Hurwitz family. Its centered harmonic derivatives
have a common holomorphic zero at every nonpositive even integer. We prove
that the centering is uniquely forced by the corresponding all-depth
condition; for affine shifts, only the first two depths are needed.
An exact Gamma quotient evaluates an infinite family of convergent
harmonic sums at outer exponent two, with an explicit zeta-cumulant
formula at every depth. We also give shifted, all-order logarithmic
polygamma sums involving generalized Stieltjes constants and Hurwitz
zeta derivatives, ordinary antiderivatives, and an unequal-frequency
multivariate reflection theorem.

The existing fixed-shift Laurent theorem is acknowledged as prior work,
not relabeled as a new result. A version-specific Stieltjes indexing
error in a cited preprint is corrected. All-index claims are proved analytically
or algebraically. The accompanying 436 exact checks and 30 numerical
comparisons are separate regression evidence. Global priority is not
asserted, and neither S6 nor S8 is proved here.
\end{abstract}
