# PDF quality audit

The final article was compiled from the modular source with `latexmk` and
checked with the repository-independent PDF tools prescribed for this task.

- Pages: 24.
- Encryption: none.
- Fonts: all embedded.
- Outline entries: 40.
- Forms: none.
- Preflight warnings: none.
- Raster inspection: all pages rendered at 170 dpi; representative pages
  covering the title, contents, dense determinant proofs, long displayed
  formulas, tables, appendices, and bibliography were inspected. No clipping,
  overlap, broken glyphs, or unreadable text was found.
- LaTeX log: no undefined references or citations and no overfull boxes; only
  harmless underfull boxes in bibliography URLs.

The rendered page images and transient inspection JSON were deliberately not
retained in the delivery archive.
