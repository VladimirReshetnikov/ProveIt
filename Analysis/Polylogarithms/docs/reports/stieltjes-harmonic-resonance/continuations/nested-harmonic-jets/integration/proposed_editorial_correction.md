## Inspected wording

> Basis atoms must be Q-independent.

The source formats Q mathematically. This sentence occurs in the historical discussion of a negative relation-search result after the low-order log-Gamma integral material.

## Proposed replacement

> Remove proved dependencies from the comparison basket, or work in an explicitly specified quotient. Independence of the retained evaluated constants need not be known. A returned relation must be checked for its target coefficient and then subjected to an independent proof or a clearly labeled numerical validation.

## Reason

A search can be useful without a proof of numerical independence of every retained atom. Known dependencies can obscure the intended target and should be removed or accounted for. Unknown numerical dependencies are not grounds to declare the search mathematically impossible. Conversely, a failed bounded-height search is not a proof of independence.
