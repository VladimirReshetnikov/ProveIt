## Main results

1. **Differential-polynomial finite parts.** For every polynomial in digamma and its derivatives, the nondecaying residue and the large-circle constant depend only on the scalar digamma projection. A polynomial with at least one positive derivative in every monomial has a finite reduction to individually convergent multiple-zeta values and spectral derivatives. A monomial with \(K\) factors requires depth at most \(K-1\).

2. **Canonical integration by parts.** At weight \(w\ge2\), the differential-polynomial algebra has a direct-sum complement to total derivatives. Its normal monomials are the pure digamma power and those whose highest derivative occurs at least twice. Its dimension is \(p(w)-p(w-1)\). Every reduction retains the exact endpoint constant. This is a formal algebraic dimension, not an arithmetic independence statement.

3. **Primitives and common shifts.** Normally convergent partial-fraction primitives are anchored at one. Their shifted endpoint expansion recovers precisely the original-coordinate finite part. Multiple-polylogarithm Mellin kernels and an explicit formula for every bilinear polygamma integral are included.

4. **Gamma–Appell moments.** At every degree, the normalization generated by \(\Gamma(1-t)e^{tY}\) leaves only the highest height-one Laurent coordinate singular. The remaining correction uses convergent derivatives of depth at most \(m-3\). Both the sixth and seventh moments are explicit, with the lower inputs and inverse convolution needed to recover raw powers.

5. **Odd-lattice Lerch derivatives.** Every order derivative and mixed depth derivative of the centered continuous-depth family has an explicit local Gamma–Bell formula. A normally convergent Euler series evaluates every reflected jet for all complex orders and positive real part of depth. The theorem extends to ordinary Gamma-weighted polylogarithm derivatives and their polygamma-weighted depth derivatives.

6. **A conjecture proved for an infinite family.** The optimal initial-moment conjecture of the canonical fifth-moment report is proved for every neighboring pair of even tail orders \(p=q-2\). An explicit positive rational recurrence gives the determinant. A fixed-gap boundary determinant reduction is proved for the remaining cases. The ordinary-series consequence includes a concrete rational evaluation at orders four and six, with a separate finite telescoping certificate.

## Rebuilding

Python 3.10 or later is sufficient for the scripts. The recorded environment used Python 3.12, SymPy 1.14.0, mpmath 1.3.0, and pdfTeX from TeX Live 2023. PyMuPDF and Pillow are optional for PDF metadata and visual-review tooling.

Install the Python dependencies and run:

~~~sh
python3 build.py
~~~

This generates the standalone TeX, runs three serial pdfLaTeX passes, and writes the PDF and **results/build_record.json**. LuaLaTeX is also supported:

~~~sh
python3 build.py --engine lualatex
~~~

The TeX dependencies are ordinary packages: amsmath, amssymb, amsthm, mathtools, mathrsfs, lmodern, geometry, booktabs, longtable, array, enumitem, microtype, xcolor, fancyhdr, xurl, hyperref, and bookmark.

To regenerate only the standalone source:

~~~sh
python3 build.py --source-only
~~~

The standalone TeX also compiles directly; no incoming ZIPs, repository checkout, network access, or additional source fragments are needed.

## Replaying the mathematical checks

The default replay runs the finite exact suites:

~~~sh
python3 verify_all.py
~~~

For all exact checks and all numerical diagnostics:

~~~sh
python3 verify_all.py --full
~~~

The full Lerch and Gamma integrations can take several minutes. The scripts write fresh receipts; they do not preserve old hashes after a modified run. Regenerate the package manifest if intentionally rebuilding a changed artifact.

These are arbitrary-precision floating-point diagnostics, not interval certificates. The all-order statements rely on the analytic and algebraic proofs in the article. No proof-assistant formalization is claimed.
