\begin{abstract}
We develop exact identities extending the periodic Hurwitz--Stieltjes
calculus in the ProveIt manuscript and its incoming research reports.
For two affine integer frequencies we prove a complete reduction to the
unit-frequency shifted correlation, including every Stieltjes index and
the finite polynomial that records changes of local cutoff scale and
argument differentiation.  For three distinct translations we give a
six-term colored Tornheim formula, prove its entire kernel continuation,
and extract all finite-part Stieltjes products and ordinary correlations
of their normalized antiderivatives.  A separate Gamma-quotient calculus
reduces shifted elementary harmonic sums with arbitrary convergent rational
kernels to finite polygamma expressions and determines their complete
principal parts and constants at the moving spectral pole.  Gauss
summation supplies convergent series for every generalized Stieltjes
constant, all mixed parameter derivatives, and successive negative-integer
resonances.  Finally, one generating identity proves corrected versions
of two displayed harmonic conjecture families in a 2014 paper and
identifies the accompanying normalization and harmonic-order errors.
Every principal result has an analytic proof.  Exact symbolic generators,
independent numerical diagnostics, a source audit, and a prioritized
research agenda accompany the article.  Classical ingredients and results
already in the repository are explicitly distinguished from the present
extensions; historical priority is not asserted.
\end{abstract}
