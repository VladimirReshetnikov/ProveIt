REVIEW NOTES

Mathematical checking

The four proof sections were developed from the pinned sources, then
cross-checked within this assistant session. The harmonic and Dougall
sections were checked in both directions by separate parallel reviewers.
The collision section received a separate audit of its subset numerators,
geometric constants, and Laurent chambers. The unequal-twist scalar
classification, branch crossings, tail convergence, and contact terms
were separately reviewed. The final authoring pass checked all sections.
This is internal mathematical checking, not external peer review or a
proof-assistant formalization.

Particular proof issues checked

1. The finite-difference Mellin integral holds on Re s>1-j, so sufficiently
   large j covers any prescribed compact spectral set. Its logarithmic
   growth bound combines with the uniform binomial bound to give Re a>-r.
2. The multiple Arakawa--Kaneko shift jets have the sign (-1)^(r+p) and
   factor p!, with the source index convention reversed explicitly.
3. The generalized boundary has the sign -s*zeta(s+1,x). Its Stieltjes
   coefficient and repeated antiderivative denominator were checked.
4. Dougall's finite-head zeros remain in the Gamma quotient before mixed
   differentiation. The divided remainder is justified by holomorphic
   factorization and Cauchy bounds, not by dividing two zero-valued sums.
5. The all-order spectral formula has the factor 1/[2(m+1)], the isolated
   constant -1, and the correct (-2)^m Stieltjes signs.
6. The unequal-twist tail removes every mode that could cross a logarithm
   branch. A below-pole left-to-right indentation contributes +pi*i.
7. The finite scalar classification uses monodromy and an Euler-operator
   annihilator. Its scope is a functional identity, not sampled arithmetic.
8. The resonant-subset completion removes the whole numerator A/Lambda.
   The Taylor remainder has an integrable exponent near the origin.
9. The triple collision's finite term is zeta(2)*log(2)/2 for rates(0,1,2).
   It comes from two singular factors times a linear regular factor.
10. The one-argument-derivative chamber constants are +zeta(3), 0, -zeta(3).
    The zero-derivative special case has no analogous rectangular constant
    dependence; spectral-ray constants are a separate operation.
11. The total-derivative projection follows from the exact endpoint
    primitive. Equal positive Stieltjes indices give zero at every number
    of factors under the stated ambient finite-part prescription.

Reproducibility and layout

The scripts in code/ were replayed from the final package structure.
results/replay_summary.json records their successful exit codes and actual
runtime. Exact finite assertions are separate from numerical comparisons.
No floating calculation is asserted to be an interval certificate.

The TeX source was compiled with latexmk. The final log has no undefined
references or citations, no duplicate labels, and no overfull/underfull
boxes. All 40 rendered pages were visually checked; changed pages were
rechecked after final clarity edits. The standalone source was compiled
separately and its extracted PDF text compared with the modular build.

The two proposed source edits were checked with git apply --check against
the pinned repository snapshot. The patch was not applied to the repo.
