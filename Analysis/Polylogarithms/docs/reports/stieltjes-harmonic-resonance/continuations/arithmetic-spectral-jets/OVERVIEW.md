# Reconciliation with the Stieltjes-harmonic-resonance spine

This continuation belongs with the spectral-product part of
`resonant-jets-and-shifts`, not with a new proof of the Gaussian S6/S8 identities.
It explicitly develops that report's arithmetic-multiplicity research question
Q10. The title is **Arithmetic Spectral Jets**.

The earlier report handles polynomial weights, all normalized spectral jets,
and polynomial times a fixed power of a logarithm. The new report admits a
base Dirichlet series with a finite collection of relevant integer principal
parts, and proves a normally convergent transfer from them to all local jet
identities. Quasi-polynomial weights, independent multiplicative Hurwitz
lattices, divisor weights, and a sum-of-divisors two-pole example are explicit
unconditional classes.

The main exact-identity additions are higher-pole alternants, centered Gamma
reciprocity and its polygamma/primitive calculus, all harmonic single-shift
Laurent coefficients, and the finite compensation for removing a geometric
polylogarithmic twist. The polynomial/logarithmic results and classical
simple-pole anomaly remain antecedents.
