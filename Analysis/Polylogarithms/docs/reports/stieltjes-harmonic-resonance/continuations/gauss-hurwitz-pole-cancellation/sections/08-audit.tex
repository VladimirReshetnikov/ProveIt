\section{Corrections, proof status, and reproducible evidence}\label{sec:audit}

\subsection{A narrowly scoped manuscript wording correction}
The pinned source \src{chapters/07-integration.tex}, lines 41--44 in the
inspected file, describes a PSLQ run detecting a relation among supplied
constants rather than a relation involving the target.  It then states:
``Basis atoms must be $\mathbb Q$-independent'' \cite{repo-integration}.
That is too strong as a requirement on PSLQ.  An integer-relation algorithm
accepts a vector whose entries may be dependent; finding a dependence is
precisely its purpose \cite{pslq}.  The practical difficulty is that a
pre-existing relation can have zero coefficient on the target, making that
output uninformative for the intended evaluation.

The proposed replacement is:
\begin{quote}
Known rational dependencies among the supplied constants should be removed
or modeled explicitly.  Otherwise PSLQ may return a pre-existing relation
with zero target coefficient instead of an evaluation of the target.
\end{quote}
This is an editorial correction to the inference being drawn, not a change
to the recorded special-function value.  Related cautions already occur
in earlier continuation material; no priority is claimed for noticing it.
The package includes a minimal patch tied to the inspected context, but it
has not been applied to the repository.

\subsection{Three guard conditions for the new calculus}
The first guard is the reciprocal-gamma issue of
\eqref{eq:piwarning}: evaluating at a zero before differentiating loses
nonzero Taylor coefficients.  The second is normalization: our jets are
coefficients, while \eqref{eq:Bellzero} is a derivative formula.  For
example the half-parameter values are $L_3=26\zeta(3)$ and
$\lambda_3=24\zeta(3)$, so the cubic coefficient in the normalized gamma
quotient is not obtained by dividing an incorrectly shared Bell parameter.
The third guard is the combined nature of every subtraction.  Neither
the harmonic tails in \eqref{eq:Un} nor the Stieltjes counterterms in
\eqref{eq:jetseries} can be split into independently summed divergent
pieces.

These are explicitly proved implementation safeguards.  Apart from the
wording correction just identified, they are not asserted to be newly
found flaws in the canonical manuscript or in the uninspected incoming
archives.

\subsection{Analytic proofs versus finite checks}
All theorems in this article have analytic proofs.  The proof of normal
convergence and coefficient extraction does not use a numerical tolerance.
The exact test suite verifies polynomial formulas and selected
specializations; the numerical suite is an independent diagnostic of
normalization, signs, degeneracies, and transcriptions.

The delivered exact run completed \textbf{512 checks}.  They include generic
polynomial identities through $A_4$, the finite reference-change identity,
residues and the Bernoulli cancellation at rational parameter pairs through
$N=8$, a second path-jet generator, and the reciprocal-gamma polynomial
factor.  The central-binomial counterterm table is generated through
$N=10$.  The checks use rational arithmetic and symbolic polynomial
identities; they are not a proof-assistant formalization.

The delivered numerical run completed \textbf{64 diagnostics at 65 decimal
working digits}.  The primary series evaluation uses 108 actual terms
and asymptotic coefficients through $j=25$.  It is repeated with 72 terms
and coefficients through $j=21$.  The closed side is evaluated from the
finite gamma/Stieltjes expression.  The tests include spectral jets through
order three at resonances $N=0,1,2,3$, non-half rational parameters,
positive integer degeneracies, the harmonic-tail identities through
$k=3$, interior polylogarithmic identities, and the primitive telescope.
The largest recorded scaled discrepancy was
\begin{equation}\label{eq:diagnosticerror}
 1.80742922933\times10^{-43},\qquad
 \text{scaled error}=\frac{|\text{left}-\text{right}|}
 {\max(1,|\text{right}|)}.
\end{equation}
These are \emph{not rigorous interval enclosures}.  In particular,
\eqref{eq:diagnosticerror} is not a certificate of 43 correct decimal places.
The analytic identities are proved in the text; the diagnostics verify
that independently constructed evaluations agree to the reported precision.
Full parameter choices and residuals are in
\src{results/numerical_checks.json}.

\subsection{Integration status}
The manuscript-ready mathematical components are the subtraction theorem,
the coefficient differential law, the all-resonance evaluation, the
all-order Stieltjes formula, the harmonic-tail theorem, and the two
primitive/endpoint calculi.  The detailed sections can be integrated after
the canonical Hurwitz-jet material, with a cross-reference from the
harmonic-number and polylogarithm sections.  Formula labels and the local
coefficient convention should be preserved during integration.

Nothing here changes the status of $S_6$, $S_8$, or any numerical
independence question.  The package contains sources and suggested
integration instructions only.  No commit, pull request, or automatic
modification of ProveIt has been made.
