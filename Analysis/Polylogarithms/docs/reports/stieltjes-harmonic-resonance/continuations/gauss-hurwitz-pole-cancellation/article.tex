\begin{abstract}
We construct a common exact calculus for a Gauss gamma-ratio series and its
Hurwitz-zeta counterterms.  Subtracting the first $K$ explicit asymptotic
coefficients gives an ordinary normally convergent series on
$\Rea u>-K$.  At every nonpositive integer excess $u=-N$, its value is
\[
 \frac{(-1)^N(1-a)_N(1-b)_N}{N!}
 \bigl(H_N-\gamma-\psi(a)-\psi(b)+\psi(c)\bigr).
\]
A coefficient differential identity explains the otherwise substantial
Bernoulli-polynomial cancellation.  An all-order Taylor formula then
expresses every resonant spectral derivative in a finite collection of
generalized Stieltjes constants, nonpositive-integer Hurwitz-zeta derivatives,
and gamma/polygamma data.  Reciprocal-gamma zeros are handled by entire
Taylor expansions; terms vanishing at resonance can contribute to the
first and subsequent derivatives.

A second consequence is an all-weight identity for gamma-weighted complete
harmonic tails: the entire family collapses to $2\zeta(2k+1,x)$.  In the
central-binomial-square case this gives, for example, a trigamma-tail sum
of value $14\zeta(3)$ and a quadratic-tail sum of value $62\zeta(5)$.
We also prove an exact telescoping antiderivative formula and a
polylogarithmic endpoint-subtraction theorem, including all spectral jets.

The underlying Gauss summation and hypergeometric constant-term theory are
classical.  Their overlap with B\"uhring's work is explicitly acknowledged;
no global priority claim is made.  The contribution here is a self-contained
all-resonance, all-jet synthesis with exact coefficient recurrences,
explicit identities, and reproducible checks for integration into ProveIt.
\end{abstract}
