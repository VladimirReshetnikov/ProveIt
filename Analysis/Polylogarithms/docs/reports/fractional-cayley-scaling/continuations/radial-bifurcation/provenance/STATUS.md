# Mathematical status and audit limits

The article's Theorems 5.1 and 6.1 are computer-assisted: universal
analytic conclusions follow from a finite exact-arithmetic certificate.
Theorem 8.1 certifies an explicit infinite-series witness, including tail
bounds. Theorems 7.1 and 9.1 and the coefficient/transport identities are
proved analytically using those established signs where indicated.

The quadratic classification in Theorem 4.1 is inherited from the
manuscript, reproduced and credited, not claimed as new. The earlier
separate maximum and minimum examples also retain their attribution.

The optional symbolic checks test finitely many identities, but the
universal identities are proved in the article. The mesh generator uses
floating point only to propose boxes. Every proposal used by the proof is
checked by a separate standard-library verifier. Passing tests are not
called proof-assistant formalization. An independent implementation or
formal verification would further reduce implementation risk.

No algebraic mistake was identified in the selected source formulas for
K or Q. The integration change advances an explicitly unresolved status;
it does not invent an error in the earlier careful statement. This was
not a full audit of every manuscript chapter or every incoming report.
The source baseline was inspected through the GitHub connector; a local
repository was not edited. The proposed migration checks the exact old
blob before printing or applying its replacement.

Still unresolved here: full-disk turning-point bounds, the full-radius
integer-outer-order conjecture, constant curves for 0<a<1, S6/S8 period
identities, and numerical period independence. Seven further research
directions appear in Section 11. No literature-wide priority claim is
made for these new-to-this-continuation results.
