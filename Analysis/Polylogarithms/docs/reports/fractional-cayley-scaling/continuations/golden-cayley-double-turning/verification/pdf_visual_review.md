# Final PDF and package review

The final article has 36 pages. The complete LaTeX build finished with no
overfull or underfull box warnings, undefined references, missing glyph
warnings, or other LaTeX warnings. Text extraction found no unresolved
`??` placeholders.

All 36 pages were rendered with Poppler and visually inspected, using
page images at a longest dimension of 1,400 pixels and contact sheets.
The two principal weight-five identities, 66-row combined certificate
table, endpoint constants, lower-weight formulas, twelve-row first
certificate, and canonical ladder definitions received a separate
visual pass. The radial rational rectangle, strict interval bounds,
fold cases, and nested-root scaling formulas also received a separate
visual pass.

The one visible figure defect was corrected before delivery: the right
panel of the radial local-model plot initially clipped the teal local
maximum. The vertical range and annotation position were adjusted, the
figure and PDF were rebuilt, and the corrected page was inspected at
page resolution. Both turning points and their labels are now visible.
The caption states explicitly that the figure depicts the limiting
local model, with higher-order corrections in the exact theorem.

The final layout has readable equation numbers, repeated continuation
headers on the long coefficient table, consistent headers and footers,
and no detected overlaps or clipped text. The PDF contains selectable
text and vector mathematical content; the plotted figures are included
as vector PDFs.

Mathematical verification is recorded separately in
`verification/replay_summary.json`, the exact data receipts, and the
independent review notes. Visual inspection is not a mathematical proof.
