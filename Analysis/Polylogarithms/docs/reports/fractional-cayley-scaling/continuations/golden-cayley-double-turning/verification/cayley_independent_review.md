# Independent mathematical review of the Cayley construction

**Conclusion:** the all-weight projector and its kernel identification are
mathematically valid. The new all-weight separating-coordinate lemma is
also valid. No substantive gap was found. This review is a written
mathematical audit; it does not present finite-weight tests as a proof of
the all-weight assertions.

## 1. The endpoint quotient is an equivariant polynomial retraction

With the order `z<a<b<abar<c`, every Lyndon word of length at least two
is admissible. Conversely, the decreasing Lyndon factorization of an
admissible word cannot contain a singleton `c`, which would occur first,
or a singleton `z`, which would occur last. The triangular shuffle
factorization therefore gives the claimed polynomial decomposition
`A_tilde=A[z,c]`.

The shifted variables `x=z+b/2` and `y=c-b/2` satisfy `Cx=y`, `Cy=x`.
Thus `A_tilde=A[x,y]`, and setting `x=y=0` defines a unique algebra
retraction with kernel `(x,y)`. Both `C` and `K` preserve `A` and this
kernel, so the retraction commutes with both involutions. This corrects
the non-equivariance of the unshifted projection without assigning any
analytic value to a divergent word.

The stated explicit formula is also valid. Deletion of one initial `c`
and deletion of one terminal `z` are commuting shuffle derivations. They
annihilate `A` and act as the respective partial derivatives in the
polynomial variables `c,z`. Their finite Taylor substitution formulas
give the two endpoint substitutions. The shuffle power of a single
linear form of degree one is its concatenation power times a factorial,
which cancels the Taylor denominator. The powers in the displayed
formula must therefore remain concatenation powers, as the section says.

## 2. Eulerian generators genuinely split the indecomposables

The convolution logarithm `e=log_*(id)` is taken in the connected
commutative shuffle Hopf algebra, with deconcatenation coproduct.
It annihilates shuffle products of two positive-degree elements. One
way to see this without a separate structural theorem is to consider
the convolution powers `id^{*m}`. For nonnegative integers `m`, they
are characters because the target shuffle algebra is commutative.
In each fixed weight they depend polynomially on `m`, so the character
identity holds as a polynomial identity in a rational variable.
Differentiating at zero gives the infinitesimal-character identity for
`e`, and hence its vanishing on positive-degree products.

The first term of the logarithm is the identity; all remaining terms
are decomposable. Therefore `e(f)-f` is decomposable in positive degree.
This and product annihilation imply `e^2=e` directly. The coproduct need
not be cocommutative for this argument.

Although `C` reverses deconcatenation, `e` still commutes with `C`:
reversing a consecutive block decomposition reverses the order of its
factors, and shuffle multiplication is commutative. The letter map and
conjugation commute with this formula as well.

Consequently `E=R e` on `A` kills decomposables and is congruent to the
identity modulo decomposables. Its image `U` is a genuine homogeneous
section of the indecomposable quotient, not only a spanning collection
verified in low weights. Since `A` is polynomial, lifts of a homogeneous
basis of that quotient freely generate it: the change from Lyndon
generators is degree triangular with invertible linear part.

## 3. The explicit formula has exactly the full Cayley ideal as kernel

The `C`-stable generator space has the direct sum `U=U_+ + U_-`.
The algebra map fixing `U_+` and killing `U_-` has kernel `(U_-)`.
This is exactly the ideal generated by all `f-Cf`: every negative
generator satisfies `u-Cu=2u`, and after the negative generators vanish,
`C` acts identically on the entire remaining polynomial algebra.

It is important that this kills the *ideal* of negative generators,
including their products. Merely taking the linear average `(1+C)/2`
on all words would retain even products of negative generators and
would give the wrong kernel. The construction in the section avoids
that error.

The equation `E(w)=E(R(w))` used for nonadmissible blocks is valid.
In degree at least two, `w-R(w)` lies in `(x,y)` and every monomial in
that difference has at least two positive-degree factors. The Eulerian
operator kills it. In degree one, applying `R e` kills the linear
combination of `x,y` directly. Thus every factor `E(w_j)` in the
explicit convolution exponential belongs to `U`.

Applying `R` and then the generator projection to
`exp_*(e)=id` proves the displayed formula `Pi=P R`. This establishes
all-weight multiplicativity, idempotence, commutation with conjugation,
and `ker(Pi|A)=J`. None of these conclusions depends on a finite rank
computation.

## 4. The all-weight separating-coordinate calculation is complete

Here the weights and the exponents are integers, with `w>=3` and
`N=w-2>0`. For a word over `{z,a,abar}`, the operations `e,R` introduce
no `c`; therefore the `C E` part of a projected block produces no `z`.
A block made only of zeros has zero Eulerian image if its length is
greater than one, while a singleton `z` projects to `-b/2`. Hence a
contribution retaining all `N` zeros and two nonreal letters can contain
only one or two blocks. This rules out the omitted higher-block terms.

For a one-nonreal-letter block, its component with all zeros retained is

`z^u a z^v -> (-1)^v binom(u+v,v) z^(u+v) a`.

This is the ordinary right-endpoint shuffle regularization on the
component with no `b` letters. Every decomposable term of the Eulerian
logarithm has a pure-zero factor in this situation, so it vanishes in
that component after the endpoint substitution.

For the same-color double word, the two-block coefficient sum is

`Sigma=2 sum_(j=0)^(s-1) (-1)^j binom(r-1+j,j) binom(N,r-1+j)`.

The exact cancellation uses

`binom(r-1+j,j) binom(N,r-1+j)
 =binom(N,r-1) binom(s-1,j)`.

Thus `Sigma=2 delta_(s,1)`. Convolution exponentiation before the
Cayley projection gives the one-block coefficient
`delta_(s,1)-Sigma/2`. After projection, the one-block contribution is
halved and the two-block sum receives the factor `1/(2!*2*2)=1/8`.
The result is `delta_(s,1)/4`, as claimed.

There is no missing conjugate-coordinate contribution: producing both
conjugate letters requires the `C E` part of both relevant blocks, and
at least one of those blocks contains a zero because `N>0`.

For `z^N a abar`, the only possible cut is `z^N a | abar`. Its two
factors contribute `z^N a/2` and `-a/2`; the shuffle coefficient is 2
and the convolution exponential contributes `1/2!`. The result is
`-1/4`. The same retained-zero argument excludes the conjugate output.

Finally `e`, `R`, and `C` never increase the number of nonreal letters.
The asserted vanishing on words with at most one such letter therefore
holds, including every word occurring in the three product terms of
the frozen target.

## 5. The frozen S6 normalization agrees with the baseline

The manuscript's primitive relation vector is

`(485683200, -665395200, -36864000, 401080320,
  -258247, 11750400, 109347840, 971366400)`.

The harmonic parity split is
`S6=Im(Li_(6,1)(i,1)+Li_(6,1)(i,-1))`, so the coefficient of
`z^5 a a` after combining the S6 and `g_(6,1)` terms is
`485683200-665395200=-179712000`. The cumulative-color encoding sends
the other double to `z^5 a abar`, exactly as the code does.

The conversion `beta(7)=61*pi^7/184320` gives the rational coefficient
`-258247*184320/61=-47600087040/61`. Also `H(b)=Li_1(-1)=-log(2)`,
so the coefficient of the `z^5 a shuffle b` term must be negative to
produce the baseline's positive `beta(6) log(2)` term. Both signs in
the section and the implementation are correct.

The all-weight coordinate lemma then proves the target value without
depending on the stored full normal form:

`ell(F)=(-179712000-485683200)/4=-166348800`.

Because `Pi` commutes with `K` and the coordinate reduction uses paired
coefficient differences, the same nonzero value detects the
anti-invariant lift `(F-KF)/2`. Thus the stated conclusion about the
imaginary target follows, rather than merely nonmembership of a lift
with irrelevant real components.

## Scope of the conclusion

The kernel is the full shuffle ideal of convergent Cayley relations.
It omits double shuffle, distribution, and other functional equations.
The separating functional therefore neither disproves the numerical S6
conjecture nor proves any arithmetic independence. The section states
these limitations correctly. The earlier value 7518 is the dimension
of the formal imaginary quotient, not a claim about the number of
independent numerical periods.
