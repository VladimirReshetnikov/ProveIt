\section{Extremal bounds, cyclic torsion, and Lerch branch shape}
\label{extremal20261010:overview}

The companion continuation \cite{extremal20261010} proves the following
results with full hypotheses and reproducible exact certificates.
Throughout, $g_{a,b}=\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Li}_{a,b}(i,1)$ uses
analytic continuation for positive real orders.

For fixed $w>0$, the function $a\mapsto-g_{a,w-a}$ has strictly negative
derivative on $(0,w)$, and its sharp endpoint bounds are
\[
 \beta(w)-\beta(w-1)<-g_{a,w-a}
 <\frac{\beta(w)}2+2^{-w-1}\eta(w).
\]
In particular the incoming critical Euler conjecture is a theorem:
for $a+b=1$ and $N\ge1$,
\[
 0<E_N-g_{a,b}<\left(\frac\pi4+\frac{\log2}{2}\right)2^{-N},
\]
with optimal uniform constant. The normalized errors decrease on this
critical line. The Euler transform itself converges absolutely for
every $a,b>0$, including below the signed-measure threshold.

The sharp envelope $C(w)=\beta(w)+2^{-w}\eta(w)$ has a unique
nondegenerate maximum at $w_*=1.30221658710124\ldots$, with
$C_*=1.13656110333950\ldots$. Exact rational enclosures are in the
report. This bounds $-2g_{a,b}$ globally; it is not asserted to be the
all-$N$ Euler constant away from the critical line.

For the unanchored weighted distribution module $D_q$, let a unit $u$
have prime order $\ell$ and assume $q=md$, $(m,d)=1$, $d>1$,
$u\equiv1\pmod m$, $(u-1,d)=1$, and $\ell\nmid\varphi(m)$.
Let $H=\langle p\bmod m:p\mid d\rangle$. Set
$c=\varphi(m)/|H|$ if the assignments
$p\mapsto a_p\bmod\ell$ extend to a character
$H\to\mathbb F_\ell^\times$, and $c=0$ otherwise. Then
\[
 D_q/(u-1)D_q\simeq\mathbb Z^{\varphi(q)/\ell}
  \oplus(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)^{c2^{\omega(d)-1}}.
\]
The companion report gives the full finite-jet extension and its
module structure. These are formal integral coinvariants, not
independence claims about complex-valued periods.

For the Stieltjes--Lerch family $F_{n,k}^{(\rho)}$, a finite resolvent
comparison moves the largest positive zero strictly left as $\rho$
increases, without a saturation assumption. At index two the lower
branch has at most one stationary point, necessarily a nondegenerate
minimum. An endpoint sign criterion proves that the order-two branch
has that minimum, while both order-three branches decrease. The
persistent statement for all higher derivative orders remains open.

Finally, an integer separator excludes the $S_6$ target from a specified
family of 5,131 weight-seven relations through depth four. The supplied
checker independently enumerates and verifies the entire family.
The $S_6$ period identity remains conjectural; no universal depth
lower bound is claimed.
