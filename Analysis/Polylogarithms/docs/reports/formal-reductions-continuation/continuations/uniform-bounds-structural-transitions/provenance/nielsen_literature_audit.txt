Convention: our Li_{n+1,1,...,1}(z,1,...,1) is their Nielsen S_{n,p}(z),
with p-1 trailing ones. Thus T_{w,h} corresponds to S_{w-h,h}.

Precise correspondence:
1. Lemma 6 and Section 3.3, printed pp. 5-6: the Nielsen quotient is
   modeled by degree w-2 binary forms tensored with the sign representation.
   This is our unique j=1 component, not a new height-one representation.
2. Theorem 7, printed pp. 6-7: basis and uniform Nielsen depth bound
   floor((w+1)/3)=ceil((w-1)/3). Our individual minimum formula is an
   explicit fixed-presentation membership consequence of this structure,
   proved independently by interpolation; do not advertise its analytic
   upper bound as new. Remark 8 discusses motivic depth lower bounds.
3. Proposition 28, printed p. 25: EXACTLY our weight-seven congruence,
   with all six coefficients unchanged after translating notation.
   Our 864-term product file is an independently constructed explicit
   completion/replay certificate, not a claim to a new identity.
4. Section 9, preconsiderations, printed p. 28: S_{5,3}=Li_{6,1,1}
   cannot reduce to Nielsen S_{6,2} motivically, even allowing more
   complicated arguments. This is distinct from a claim about all
   multiple-polylogarithm doubles at arbitrary arguments.
   Our normalized primitive Omega_{8,2} verifies nonmembership against
   ALL binary words of depth <=2 at the six fixed argument changes.
   The exact Lie numerator and exhaustive certificate were constructed
   independently here, but the height-one nonreduction phenomenon is known.

Supported contribution: the full-binary finite-direction rank formula,
including all GL2 irreducible components rather than just the Nielsen
component, its finite-vs-full-linear orbit distinction starting at weight
7, and independently replayable exact certificates. The inspected paper
does not state this all-component formula. This comparison is not a global
priority search; no worldwide novelty assertion is justified.

No concrete height-one identity or height-one nonreduction in this section
should be advertised as previously unknown. The particular certificate
normalizations and product encoding may be new artifacts, not new-period
or new-functional-equation claims.
