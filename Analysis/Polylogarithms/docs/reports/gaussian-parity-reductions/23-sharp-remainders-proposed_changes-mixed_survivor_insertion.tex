\section{Regularized inverse-argument reductions}
\label{mixed:sec:regularized-correction}

The earlier candidate count at the inverse-argument mixed points omitted
regularized single-product rows. In particular the proposed imaginary
weight-six survivors contribute no new direction over the stated
single products and $(z,1)$ double values. The general reduction
mechanism is already encoded in the report-10 routine
\texttt{mixed\_coefficients}; the following endpoint case gives a short
direct derivation and explicit corrections.

\begin{theorem}[Inverse-argument endpoint]
For $p\ge1$ and $z=\iu$ or $\rr=e^{2\pi\iu/3}$,
\[
 \Li_{p,1}(z,z^{-1})
 =\sum_{a=1}^{p}\Li_{a,p+1-a}(z,1)+p\Li_{p+1}(z)
       -\sum_{j=2}^{p}\zeta(j)\Li_{p+1-j}(z).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Regularize $\Li_1(1)$ by the same real parameter $T$ in the shuffle
and stuffle product with $\Li_p(z)$. Subtraction cancels the
divergent leading term and gives
\[
 \Li_{p,1}(z,z^{-1})
 =\Li_{p,1}(z,1)+\Li_{p+1}(z)
       -\sum_{a=2}^{p}\Li_{a,p+1-a}(1,z).
\]
Apply the convergent stuffle
\[
 \Li_{a,p+1-a}(1,z)
 =\zeta(a)\Li_{p+1-a}(z)-\Li_{p+1-a,a}(z,1)-\Li_{p+1}(z)
\]
to every term of the finite sum.
\end{proof}

With the proved Gaussian weight-six $g$ reductions, this gives
\[
 \Imag\Li_{5,1}(\iu,-\iu)
 =3\beta(6)-\frac{\pi^2}{6}\beta(4)
       -\frac{\pi^4}{90}G-\frac{5\pi^5}{3072}\log2.
\]
For $\rr=e^{2\pi\iu/3}$ define $C_s=\Imag\Li_s(\rr)$ for every $s$.
Then
\begin{align*}
 \Imag\Li_{5,1}(\rr,\rr^2)
 &=\sum_{a=1}^{5}\Imag\Li_{a,6-a}(\rr,1)
       +5C_6-\frac{\pi^2}{6}C_4-\frac{\pi^4}{90}C_2\\
 &\quad-\frac{2\pi^3}{81}\zeta(3)-\frac{\pi}{6}\zeta(5).
\end{align*}
Thus the imaginary weight-six candidate in each tower must be removed
from the corresponding survivor summary. The real weight-five
candidate requires a separate analysis; no independence assertion
for it is made here.
