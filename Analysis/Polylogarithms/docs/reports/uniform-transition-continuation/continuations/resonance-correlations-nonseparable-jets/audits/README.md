# Proof review and numerical scope

The article is the authoritative final statement of every theorem. These
notes preserve selected arguments and source checks that are useful when
integrating or independently reviewing the results.

- `resonance_uniformity_audit.md` expands the Hermite-integral majorant and
  the dominated-convergence step for the Stieltjes shift difference.
- `nonseparable_research_notes.md` records the Frobenius argument, concrete
  module obstruction, and independently constructed exact rank checks.
- `external_lerch_normalization_audit.md` gives the published page locator,
  elementary reindexing proof, and comparison with the correct repository
  normalization.

The analytic fragments received separate internal proof reviews. Those
reviews checked the phase signs, convergence domains, cancellation
normalizations, finite remainder constants, branch choices, and the exact
algebraic hypotheses. The explicit Gamma cusp polynomials were regenerated
symbolically in a separate calculation. This is an internal mathematical
audit, not a claim of external peer review or proof-assistant certification.

Corrections made during preparation were incorporated into the final source:
the direction of the harmonic-sequence monotonicity in the resonance proof;
an explicit Hermite majorant and shift-difference domination; explicit
parameter-differentiation bounds for the Herglotz mixture; and the
qualification that the imaginary-zero theorem concerns the centered
reciprocal profile. Two LaTeX delimiter typos were also repaired. These
were draft corrections, not errors attributed to the pinned manuscript.

The numerical checks supplement the proofs. They do not turn a small
residual into a theorem, and their high-precision decimals are not
interval-certified unless expressly stated otherwise. No such interval
certification is claimed for the accompanying numerical tables.
