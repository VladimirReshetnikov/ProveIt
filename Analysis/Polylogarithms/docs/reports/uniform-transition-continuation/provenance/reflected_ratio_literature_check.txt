BOUNDED LITERATURE CHECK: REFLECTED LOG-GAMMA RATIO
Date: 2026-10-10

Target
  J(x) = [-psi(x) log Gamma(1-x)] /
         [-psi(1-x) log Gamma(x)],  0 < x < 1.
The present research package proves that J is strictly increasing by a
direct endpoint argument and an exact finite interval certificate.

Search conclusion
No exact match for the ratio or a verified earlier theorem implying its
monotonicity was located in this bounded search. Searches included reflected
log-gamma products, maxima of log Gamma(x) log Gamma(1-x), ratios involving
psi and log Gamma at reflected arguments, and logarithmic/geometric
convexity. Search coverage is not exhaustive. Absence of a retrieved match
does not support a priority claim.

Inspected primary sources and distinctions

1. G. J. O. Jameson, "Convexity of Gamma(x)Gamma(1/x)," Mathematical
   Inequalities & Applications 19 (2016), 949-953.
   DOI: 10.7153/mia-19-69
   https://files.ele-math.com/articles/mia-19-69.pdf
   Theorem 1 proves convexity of log Gamma(x)+log Gamma(1/x).
   This is a sum of logarithms at reciprocal arguments. It does not
   establish the present product of logarithms at reflected arguments.

2. Feng Qi and Chao-Ping Chen, "Monotonicity and convexity results for
   functions involving the gamma function."
   https://rgmia.org/papers/v6n4/mrigf-II.pdf
   Theorems 1 and 2 concern [Gamma(1+x)]^(1/x)/(1+x) and
   [Gamma(1+x)]^(1/x)/sqrt(1+x), respectively. No implication for J was
   verified.

3. Martin Himmel, "Directional convexity and characterizations of Beta
   and Gamma functions," arXiv:1505.01472 (2015).
   https://arxiv.org/html/1505.01472v1
   The results inspected concern functional-equation characterizations
   using directional or geometric convexity of gamma/beta functions.
   They do not give convexity properties of log log Gamma needed here.

Elementary caution about possible general-convexity shortcuts
For a positive decreasing function f on (0,1), put a_f=-f'/f and
J_f(x)=a_f(x)/a_f(1-x). Strict convexity of f is not sufficient for the
desired increasing direction. Indeed, f(x)=x^(-2)-1 is positive, strictly
decreasing and strictly convex on (0,1), with f(1)=0 and f(0+)=infinity,
whereas J_f(x)=(2-x)/(1+x) is strictly decreasing. In addition,
log log Gamma(x) has positive second derivative for sufficiently small
x>0, since its leading behavior is log(-log x). Thus its global
log-concavity cannot be invoked.
