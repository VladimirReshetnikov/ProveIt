\subsection{Beta transfer and the sharp critical Euler constant}
\label{betatransfer:section}

For positive real orders, write
\[
 F_{a,b}(z)=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{H_{n-1}^{(b)}}{n^a}z^n,
 \qquad g_{a,b}=\operatorname{Im}F_{a,b}(\mathrm i),
\]
using the principal analytic continuation at the boundary. Define the
profile
\[
 \mathcal H_w(v;z)=\frac1{\Gamma(w)}\int_0^\infty
 T^{w-1}\frac{z^2e^{-(1+v)T}}
 {(1-ze^{-vT})(1-ze^{-T})}\,\mathrm dT.
\]
Changing variables in the positive double gamma integral gives
\begin{equation}\label{betatransfer:beta}
 F_{a,b}(z)=\frac1{\mathrm B(a,b)}\int_0^1
 v^{a-1}(1-v)^{b-1}\mathcal H_{a+b}(v;z)\,\mathrm dv.
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Order transfer and the critical constant]
\label{betatransfer:main}
For every $w>0$ and $0<\rho\le\sqrt3$, the function
$a\mapsto-\operatorname{Im}F_{a,w-a}(\mathrm i\rho)$ decreases
strictly on $(0,w)$. On the critical line,
\[
 \frac\pi2-1<-2g_{a,1-a}<\frac\pi4+\frac{\log2}{2}
 \quad(0<a<1).
\]
Let
\[
 f(n)=\frac{H_{2n}^{(1-a)}}{(2n+1)^a},\qquad
 E_N=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}\frac1{2^{k+1}}
       \sum_{n=0}^k(-1)^n\binom kn f(n).
\]
Then, uniformly over $0<a<1$ and $N\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{betatransfer:criticalbound}
 0<E_N-g_{a,1-a}
 <\left(\frac\pi4+\frac{\log2}{2}\right)2^{-N}.
\end{equation}
The constant is optimal. This resolves Conjecture 12.3
(\texttt{conj:constant}) of the preserved real-order-threshold report.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The negative imaginary part of the elementary Gaussian kernel is
\[
 \frac{\rho^3xy(x+y)}{(1+\rho^2x^2)(1+\rho^2y^2)},\qquad 0<y<x<1.
\]
Its derivative in $x$ has the sign of $2x+y-\rho^2x^2y$, positive
for $\rho\le\sqrt3$. Therefore the negative imaginary profile
strictly decreases with $v$. The beta density in
\eqref{betatransfer:beta} shifts to the right in monotone likelihood
ratio order as $a$ increases at fixed $a+b=w$; its derivative formula
is the strictly negative covariance with $\log(v/(1-v))$.
The endpoint profiles are
$z\operatorname{Li}_w(z)/(1-z)$ and
$\operatorname{Li}_{w-1}(z)-\operatorname{Li}_w(z)$, giving the
critical endpoint constants.

The detailed report constructs
$\mu_a=a^{-1}\delta_1-\nu_a(-\log u)\,\mathrm du$ with
$\nu_a(T)>1$ and the required critical moments. Consequently
\[
 2^N(E_N-g_{a,1-a})
 =\int_0^1\frac{(1-u^2)^N}{1+u^2}\nu_a(-\log u)\,\mathrm du
 \le -2g_{a,1-a}.
\]
Strict positivity and the endpoint comparison prove the bound.
Its optimality follows from $N=1$ and $a\downarrow0$.
\end{proof}

The same report proves one-sided Euler convergence for all $a,b>0$
with the elementary bound $0<E_N-g_{a,b}<2(N+1)2^{-N}$, by integrating
positive divided differences. Below $a+b=1$ this is not a signed
Hausdorff-measure proof. The boundary values on and below that line
are analytic/Abel values, not ordinarily convergent boundary sums.
The stronger assertion that every $2^N(E_N-g_{a,1-a})$ decreases with
$a$ is false: an exact rational certificate gives
$A_8(1/10)-A_8(0)>13/10000$, where the value at $a=0$ is an endpoint
limit. Neither the broad angular-zero conjectures nor the $S_6$
identity is resolved by these results.
