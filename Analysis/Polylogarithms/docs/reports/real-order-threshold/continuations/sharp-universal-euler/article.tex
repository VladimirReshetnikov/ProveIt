\begin{abstract}
For $a,b>0$ let $g_{a,b}=\Imag\Li_{a,b}(\ii,1)$, interpreted by principal analytic continuation, and let $E_N(a,b)$ be the $N$-term Euler transform of its odd harmonic coefficient sequence. We prove the parameter-free bound
\[
0<E_N(a,b)-g_{a,b}<\frac98\,2^{-N}\qquad(N\ge2).
\]
The proof reduces the entire parameter range to a two-variable rational kernel. Two finite polynomial positivity certificates and one elementary envelope argument treat every truncation length; the certificates comprise 1542 strictly positive rational Bernstein coefficients.
Consequently the sharp constant uniform in $a,b>0$ and $N\ge1$ is
\[
\Cs=\max_{b>0}\{\beta(b)+2^{-b}\eta(b)\},
\]
where $\beta$ and $\eta$ are the Dirichlet beta and eta functions. This identifies the previously unresolved global Euler constant with the manuscript's already established Gaussian axis maximum. Exact arithmetic additionally proves
$1.3021<b_*<1.3023$ and
$1.1365611033<\Cs<1.1365611046$.
We derive a generating function for all Euler errors and exact binomial-moment identities that lower the outer polylogarithm order. They give new explicit convergent identities and extend Gaussian negativity to every real outer order at least $-1$. The report distinguishes inherited results, new proofs, exact computation and numerical diagnostics, and supplies proposed manuscript edits and independent verifiers. The mixed-period $S_6$ conjecture is not resolved here.
\end{abstract}
