# Polylogarithm Special-Value Questions on Math StackExchange and MathOverflow

## Executive summary

I screened primary-source questions on Math StackExchange and MathOverflow for the specific combination you asked for: explicit **polylogarithm special-value evaluations** or **identities among several special values**, with the additional filter that the **question body itself had to write down at least one example identity** and the thread had to have **at least one meaningful answer**. Under that filter, the high-confidence corpus is dominated by **Math StackExchange**, while **MathOverflow contributes only a small number of clear matches**. That asymmetry is visible already at the tag-page level: MSE’s polylogarithm tag contains hundreds of questions, whereas MO’s polylogarithms tag is much smaller. citeturn26view0turn39view0

The retained questions cluster into three recurring themes. One cluster is about **rational-point ladders and mixed-value identities** such as relations among \(\operatorname{Li}_n(1/3)\), \(\operatorname{Li}_n(2/3)\), \(\operatorname{Li}_n(1/4)\), and similar values. A second cluster treats **single special points with explicit closed forms** at algebraic or complex arguments such as \((1+i)/2\), \(1+i\), \(1\pm i\sqrt3\), \(\frac12+\frac{i}{6}\), and \(\sqrt{2-\sqrt3}\,e^{i\pi/12}\). A third cluster concerns **golden-ratio and tribonacci ladders**, which are especially prominent in both sites’ retained results. citeturn25view0turn44view0turn46view0turn48view2turn56view2turn60view0turn43view2

A few included threads do **not** end in a polished final proof, but they still pass the “meaningful answers” filter because the answers give a substantive derivation, a strong reduction to standard identities, or a literature-based route that materially advances the question. I flag those cases explicitly in the table. citeturn46view0turn59view0turn50view0turn53view0

## Screening approach

I used a conservative inclusion rule. I kept a question only if its body explicitly wrote down one or more sample identities, conjectured evaluations, or finite relations among named polylogarithm values at special points, and if the thread contained at least one nontrivial answer. I excluded near-misses such as general functional-equation threads, broad “what is special about polylogarithms?” prompts, and no-answer posts, even when they were mathematically adjacent.

I also deduplicated near-duplicates by keeping the most directly on-point formulation. For example, I kept both the \(\operatorname{Li}_3((1+i)/2)\) and \(\operatorname{Li}_4(1+i)\) threads because they target distinct special points and distinct orders, but I did not expand the list with structurally similar integral-evaluation posts unless the **question itself** foregrounded the special-value identity.

## Observations

The **rational-point identity** subfamily is especially strong on MSE: the threads centered on \(\operatorname{Li}_n(1/3)\), \(\operatorname{Li}_n(2/3)\), \(\operatorname{Li}_n(1/4)\), and related values form a natural chain from broad “are there more ladders?” questions to highly specific mixed-order identities. In practice, the most reusable nodes in that chain are the 2014 rational-point ladder question, the 2014 \(\operatorname{Li}_3(-1/3)-2\operatorname{Li}_3(1/3)\) thread, and the 2015 mixed \(\operatorname{Li}_3/\operatorname{Li}_2\) identity thread. citeturn25view0turn56view1turn49view0

The **special complex point** subgroup is also rich on MSE. The strongest examples are the \((1+i)/2\), \(1+i\), \(1\pm i\sqrt3\), and \(\frac12+\frac{i}{6}\) threads, because the prompts themselves already state candidate closed forms or immediate companion identities, and the answers are mathematically substantial rather than vacant commentary. citeturn44view0turn46view0turn53view2turn57view0turn48view2

MathOverflow is much sparser **under your exact filter**. Several visually close MO posts fail because they currently have no answers. For example, the 2025 “Deriving a 4-term functional dilogarithm identity by Kirillov” thread contains an explicit identity in the question body but still has zero answers, so I excluded it; similarly, the MO tag page shows no-answer special-value posts such as “Proving identity involving dilogarithms and \(\pi/9\)” and “The relationship between the dilogarithm and the golden ratio,” which are topically close but do not meet your answer-quality requirement. citeturn61view0turn43view0turn43view2

## Open questions and limitations

This report is intentionally conservative. I did **not** include many polylogarithm-heavy integral questions unless the question body itself foregrounded a special-value equality or finite identity among named polylogarithm values. If you later decide to relax that filter, the candidate pool on MSE expands substantially.

The MathOverflow section is likely the most incomplete part of the landscape, not because relevant MO mathematics is absent, but because many MO polylogarithm threads lean toward **functional equations, Bloch-group structure, hyperbolic geometry, or broad conceptual questions** rather than explicit special-point evaluations written out in the question body, and several of the most promising special-value-looking posts currently have **no answers**. citeturn39view0turn61view0turn43view0
