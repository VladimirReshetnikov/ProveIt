\begin{abstract}
The ProveIt polylogarithm manuscript proves freeness of a weighted finite
cyclotomic distribution module over a characteristic-zero character
splitting field and leaves its integral and modular structure for further
research. We give a division-free construction over
$\Z[A_p:p\mid q]$, including an explicit raw-point basis, a maximal relation
minor equal to one, and a finite exact resolution. These tools distinguish
unreflected distribution relations, which have no torsion after any integer
weight specialization, from reflection coinvariants, which can acquire
precisely determined $2$-torsion.

For $q>2$, put $n=\varphi(q)/2$,
$r=\#\{p>2:p\mid q\}+\one_{4\mid q}$ and $h=2^{r-1}$.
Both integer reflection signs give $\Z^n\oplus(\Z/2)^h$ when all odd-prime
weights are odd, and $\Z^n$ otherwise. In characteristic two, the entire
obstruction is a Koszul complex on the odd-prime quantities $A_p-1$,
together with $A_2(A_2-1)$ when $4\mid q$. In particular its universal
homology is the coordinate ring of an explicit reduced complete
intersection. Along a one-parameter deformation with contact order $d$,
the reflection matrix has $h$ nonunit Smith factors $t^d$, and its
length-$L$ jet cokernel has dimension $nL+h\min(d,L)$.

Three short polynomial certificates also produce all-spectral-order
cyclotomic identities and an all-derivative level-$15$ family involving
Stieltjes constants, with the pole at order one cancelled explicitly.
The ordinary distribution and its ordinary sign cohomology are classical;
we identify these antecedents rather than claiming their rediscovery as
new. Full proofs, exact replay programs, numerical diagnostics, a focused
manuscript correction, and integration notes accompany the article.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Scope.} The results concern explicitly presented modules
and exact functional identities, not numerical independence of periods.
No proof-assistant verification or exhaustive priority claim is made.
The existing $S_6$ conjecture is not proved in this contribution.
