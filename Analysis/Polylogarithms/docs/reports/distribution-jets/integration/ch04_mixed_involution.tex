The symmetric combination determined by stuffle uses the paired
involution $(a,b;x,y)\mapsto(b,a;y,x)$. At an equal-argument point the
complementary antisymmetric part is
$\Li_{a,b}(x,x)-\Li_{b,a}(x,x)$ and vanishes when $a=b$.
At a mixed point it is instead
$\Li_{a,b}(x,y)-\Li_{b,a}(y,x)$; equality of indices alone does not
make this difference zero. In particular,
\[
\operatorname{Im}\!\left(\Li_{2,2}(-1,i)-\Li_{2,2}(i,-1)\right)>0,
\]
as follows from the strictly positive integral in the companion
article. This statement does not assert independence from products of
single values. Any dimension count must specify which paired
coordinates and which additional relations are included.
