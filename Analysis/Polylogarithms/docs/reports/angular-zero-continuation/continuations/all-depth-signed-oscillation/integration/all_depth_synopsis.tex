\section{All-depth signed oscillation and minimal positive compensation}
\label{ado:integration:section}
This section records the all-depth extension proved in the report
\emph{Signed-Kernel Oscillation at Every Depth: Minimal Pick Compensation,
Zero Geometry, and Certified Polylogarithmic Identities} (October 10, 2026).
The full proof and replay artifacts belong under
\texttt{docs/reports/all-depth-signed-oscillation/}.

Use strict decreasing summation indices and the convention
\[
 L_{s_1,\ldots,s_d}(z)=
 \sum_{n_1>\cdots>n_d\ge1}
 \frac{z^{n_1}}{n_1^{s_1}\cdots n_d^{s_d}},
 \qquad s_1,\ldots,s_{d-1}\in\mathbb Z_{\ge1},\quad s_d=b>0.
\]
For $f\in L^1(0,1)$ put
\[
 (\ADOA f)(u)=\int_u^1\frac{f(v)}v\,dv,
 \qquad
 (\ADOB f)(u)=\int_0^u\frac{f(v)}{1-v}\,dv-
                         \int_u^1\frac{f(v)}v\,dv.
\]
If $m_n(f)=\int_0^1u^{n-1}f(u)\,du$, then
\[
 m_n(\ADOA f)=\frac{m_n(f)}n,
 \qquad m_n(\ADOB f)=\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}m_j(f),
 \qquad \|\ADOA f\|_1\le\|f\|_1,\quad
 \|\ADOB f\|_1\le2\|f\|_1.
\]
Thus, starting with $f_b(u)=(-\log u)^{b-1}/\Gamma(b)$, the density
\[
 \kappa=\ADOA^{s_1-1}\ADOB\cdots
             \ADOA^{s_{d-1}-1}\ADOB f_b
\]
realizes the coefficients of $L$ as its moments and satisfies
$\|\kappa\|_1\le2^{d-1}$.

\begin{theorem}[All-depth oscillation and compensation]
\label{ado:integration:compensation}
The density $\kappa$ has exactly $d-1$ simple zeros
$0<r_1<\cdots<r_{d-1}<1$, alternating signs, and positive sign near one.
Set $P(u)=\prod_j(u-r_j)$ and $D(z)=\prod_j(1-r_jz)$. Then
$P\kappa\,du$ is a positive measure and
\[
 L(z)=\frac{z^d}{D(z)}H(z),\qquad
 H(z)=\int_0^1\frac{P(u)\kappa(u)}{1-zu}\,du,
 \qquad z\in\mathbb C\setminus[1,\infty).
\]
Its mass is $[d^{s_1}(d-1)^{s_2}\cdots2^{s_{d-1}}]^{-1}$.
The only zero of $L$ on the slit plane is its order-$d$ zero at zero.
Among real polynomials $E$ with $E(0)=1$ making $EL/z^d$ a finite
positive Hausdorff--Stieltjes transform, $D$ is the unique polynomial
of least degree; that degree is $d-1$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof structure]
The first $d-1$ moments vanish. These force at least $d-1$ sign changes
by orthogonality against the polynomial of any proposed smaller set of
changes. The derivative identities
$(\ADOA f)'=-f/u$ and $(\ADOB f)'=f/[u(1-u)]$, together with
$(\ADOA f)(1)=0$, give the matching upper bound by Rolle's theorem.
They also give simplicity and strict interlacing. Multiplication by the
monic zero polynomial makes the density positive. The initial zero
moments give $DL=z^dH$ by finite polynomial cancellation. Positive
transform signs prove nonvanishing. For any polynomial $E$ of degree
at most $d-1$, the unique signed representing density of $EL/z^d$ is
$u^{d-1}E(1/u)\kappa(u)$. Positivity forces a root of $E$ at every
$1/r_j$, proving minimality and uniqueness. The report gives the endpoint
and integrability details of these steps.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Complete angular count and strict radial motion]
\label{ado:integration:angular}
For every $0<\rho\le1$, $\ADOIm L(\rho e^{i\theta})$ has exactly
$d-1$ simple zeros $0<\theta_1<\cdots<\theta_{d-1}<\pi$. For
$\alpha_k=k\pi/d$,
\[
 \alpha_k-\arcsin(\rho\sin\alpha_k)<\theta_k(\rho)<\alpha_k,
 \qquad \theta_k'(\rho)<0\quad(d\ge2).
\]
The imaginary part starts positive and alternates sign. The value of $L$
at the $k$th crossing is real with sign $(-1)^k$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof structure]
The imaginary part divided by $\rho\sin\theta$ is the signed Cauchy
kernel integral with denominator $1-2\rho u\cos\theta+\rho^2u^2$.
The change of variables $t=(1+\rho^2u^2)/(2\rho u)$ is strictly
monotone for $\rho\le1$. A confluent partial-fraction argument bounds
the number of zeros, including multiplicities, by the $d-1$ density
sign changes. The factorization supplies a continuous unwrapped phase
\[
 d\theta+\sum_j[-\arg(1-\rho r_je^{i\theta})]
                   +\arg H(\rho e^{i\theta}).
\]
It starts below $\pi$, including at unit radius by a positive-transform
sector bound, and tends to $d\pi$ at the far endpoint. This forces all
$d-1$ crossings. Positive atomic Stieltjes transforms have interlacing
numerator zeros and denominator poles; pairing those factors proves
nonnegative radial derivative of their arguments for $\rho\le1$.
Weak approximation extends the inequality to $H$. The $d-1$ compensator
factors make the radial phase derivative strictly positive, and implicit
differentiation at the simple upward crossings gives $\theta_k'<0$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope and evidence.}
The result also holds for a smooth strictly positive integrable terminal
seed, including finite positive terminal-order mixtures and the terminal
shift $(n_d+\lambda)^{-b}$ with $\lambda>-1$. It does not assert the
same signed-density construction for arbitrary fractional leading indices.
The all-depth report contains explicit positive logistic integral identities,
24 rational angular brackets, eight stored Gaussian enclosures, and
5,713 finite exact check groups. These are not proof-assistant certificates.
The $S_6$ and $S_8$ arithmetic candidates and the normalized-radius
conjecture retain their prior status.
