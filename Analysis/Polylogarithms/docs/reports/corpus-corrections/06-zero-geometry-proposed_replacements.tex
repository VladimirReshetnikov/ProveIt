\section{Unreduced second-derivative generators and numerical searches}
The six second-order derivative constants are retained as formal generators
in the chosen representation. The numerical searches described here found
no relation in the tested bases at the stated precision. This is a report
of search outcomes, not a proof of rational linear independence, algebraic
independence, or irreducibility. A complete record should state the tested
bases, precision, coefficient-search limits, and controls. No unrestricted
independence conclusion is drawn from the negative searches.

\paragraph{Computed rank and a conjectural general pattern.}
Exact row reduction on the distribution matrices used in the computations
was performed for $3\le q\le30$ and $k=1,2,3$. The reported residual dimension
in that finite range is $\varphi(q)-1$. The same formula for arbitrary $q,k$
is a conjectural extension unless a general proof is supplied. To make the
claim precise, one must specify the formal coordinate module, the treatment
of the inhomogeneous known terms, and the complete set of generating
relations. Formal residual dimension must not be identified with arithmetic
independence of the evaluated special constants.

For a primitive character $\chi$ and $k\ge1$ with $L(-k,\chi)=0$,
the normalized first bridge is
\[
 \frac{L'(k+1,\chi)}{L(k+1,\chi)}
 +\overline{\left(\frac{L''(-k,\chi)}{2L'(-k,\chi)}\right)}
 =\gamma+\log\frac{2\pi}{q}-H_k.
\]
The factor $1/2$ comes from regularizing the simple zero:
$L(-k+z,\chi)/z=L'(-k,\chi)+\tfrac12L''(-k,\chi)z+O(z^2)$.
For all higher jets, set
$D(z)=L(-k+z,\overline\chi)/z^\nu$, where $\nu=1$ at a trivial zero
and $\nu=0$ otherwise. Then
\[
 D^{(j)}(0)=\frac{j!}{(j+\nu)!}L^{(j+\nu)}(-k,\overline\chi).
\]
These factorial normalizations and the character conjugation must be kept
when translating between derivative layers.
