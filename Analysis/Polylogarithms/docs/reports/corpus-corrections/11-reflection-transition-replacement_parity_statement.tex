\begin{theorem}[Uniform even-weight reduction]
Let
\[
 S_p=\sum_{n\ge0}\frac{(-1)^nH_n}{(2n+1)^p},\qquad
 \sec(\pi z/2)=\sum_{j\ge0}c_jz^{2j}.
\]
For every integer $m\ge0$,
\[
 S_{2m+1}=(2m+1)\beta(2m+2)
 -\frac\pi2\left(c_m\log2+
 \sum_{j=1}^m c_{m-j}(1-2^{-2j-1})\zeta(2j+1)\right).
\]
In particular, these values belong to
$\mathbb Q[\pi,\log2,\zeta(3),\zeta(5),\ldots,\beta(2),\beta(4),\ldots]$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put
\[
 F(z,u)=\frac12\int_0^1
 t^{(1-z)/2-1}(1+t)^{-1-u}\,dt.
\]
Splitting the beta integral at one and inverting the second half gives
\[
 F(z,u)+F(-z-2u,u)
 =\frac{\Gamma((1-z)/2)\Gamma((1+z)/2+u)}{2\Gamma(1+u)}.
\]
Here $[u]F(z,u)=\sum_{p\ge1}S_pz^{p-1}$ and
$F(z,0)=\sum_{p\ge1}\beta(p)z^{p-1}$. Differentiate in $u$ and extract
$z^{2m}$. The even part of $\psi((1+z)/2)+\gamma$ is
$-2\log2-2\sum_{j\ge1}(1-2^{-2j-1})\zeta(2j+1)z^{2j}$,
which gives the formula.
\end{proof}
\paragraph{Status of the converse.}
The preceding theorem proves a reduction, not an irreducibility result.
Non-membership of the complementary even-$p$ values in this specified algebra
has not been proved by the numerical searches reported in the draft. Those
searches should be described as evidence about the candidate bases and
coefficient ranges used, not as unconditional depth lower bounds.
