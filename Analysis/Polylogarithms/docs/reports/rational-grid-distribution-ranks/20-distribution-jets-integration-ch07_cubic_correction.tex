\begin{result}[The cubic moment in Tornheim--Witten coordinates]
\label{integral:neg:cubic}
The cubic moment has an established evaluation in derivatives of the
Tornheim--Witten zeta function: see Bailey--Borwein--Borwein,
Theorem~6 and equation~(11) \cite{distjets:BBB2015}.
With
\[
 T(a,b,c)=\sum_{m,n\ge1}\frac1{m^a n^b(m+n)^c},\qquad
 L=\log(2\pi),\quad A=\gamma+L,
\]
and with every partial derivative of $T$ below evaluated at $(1,1,1)$,
\begin{align*}
\int_0^1\log^3\Gamma(x)\,dx
={}&\frac{L^3}{8}+\frac{L\pi^2}{32}+\frac{LA^2}{8}
-\frac{3LA\zeta'(2)}{2\pi^2}+\frac{3L\zeta''(2)}{4\pi^2}
+\frac{3\zeta(3)}{16}\\
&+\frac3{8\pi^2}
\bigl[2A^2\zeta(3)-2A T_c+2T_{ac}-T_{ab}\bigr].
\end{align*}
Thus the named-atom problem is not open in the enlarged Tornheim--Witten
vocabulary. The previous negative integer-relation experiment remains a
statement about its smaller chosen basket. Whether the displayed
Tornheim derivatives admit a useful further reduction is a distinct
research question. The companion distribution-jet article includes an
independent Fourier derivation and its convergence justification.
\end{result}
