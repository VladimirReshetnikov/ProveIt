\begin{remark}[Status and a finite proof-search obstruction]
The displayed $S_4$ evaluation remains conjectural in the companion
contribution. Its two sides agree in a fresh 55-digit integral
comparison, but a rational annihilator proves that the target is not in
the span of the explicitly generated 96 weight-five shuffle/stuffle
rows coming from products of two single polylogarithms at fourth roots
of unity, with the specified linear regularizations. The row matrix has
rank 20 and the augmented matrix rank 21. This is a limitation of that
finite presentation, not a disproof of the evaluation or an assertion
about all double-shuffle relations. The certificate and row generator
are provided for enlarged proof searches.
\end{remark}
