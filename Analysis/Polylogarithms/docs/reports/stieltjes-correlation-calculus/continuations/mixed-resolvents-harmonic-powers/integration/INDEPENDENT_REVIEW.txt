Method
------
The review checked branches, convergence domains, endpoint amplitudes,
residue signs, finite summation-by-parts identities, strict harmonic-word
products, asymptotic regularization constants, and the reduction of the
fifth-power formula. Numerical agreement was used only as supporting
diagnostic evidence. The mathematical conclusions below came from the
analytic and finite algebraic calculations.

Findings: all digamma powers
---------------------------
1. The Mittag-Leffler contour has the correct sign. For the kernel
   z/[w(w-z)], the residue at w=-n is the negative of the corrected
   principal-part contribution P_{k,n}(z)-P_{k,n}(0). The residues at
   z and 0 therefore produce the stated formula with f(0)=kappa_k.

2. The uniform O(log^k N) bound on the circles |w|=N+1/2 is valid.
   Reflection reduces the left half-plane to the right; cotangent is
   bounded on the circles, including their short near-real arcs.
   The contour consequently tends to zero at O(log^k N/N).
   This fixes the entire remainder rather than merely its principal
   parts.

3. The normalized primitive has zero constant in its expansion at the
   origin. The corrected summands converge normally on compact sets,
   with the necessary parameter derivatives.

4. The finite Abel summation identity gives Q_k=B_k-C_k+[u^1]E_k(u).
   In particular,
     [u^1](-1)^r zeta^(r)(1+u) = -gamma_{r+1},
   which confirms the sign of the asymptotic constant.

5. The leading-one regularization recurrence is terminating. Its finite
   harmonic-sum identity is distinguished from the subsequent replacement
   of convergent terminal words by limits. Their O(N^-1 log^M N) tails
   remain negligible after every fixed polynomial factor in H_N.

6. The highest-depth coefficient is -k!: the leading residue monomial
   -k H_n^(k-1) contributes -k(k-1) H_{n-1}^(k-2)/n, whose strict
   all-one word has coefficient (k-2)!.

Findings: exact fifth-power bridge
---------------------------------
The independent algebra checked all printed principal coefficients,
kappa_5, the exact difference of r_n, each B_{5,j}, the full finite
identity for sum r_n/n, the leading-one regularization, C_r, B_5-C_5,
the moving-order identity E_r, and the final polynomial cancellation.

The nontrivial constants confirmed include:
  C_r = -gamma^5/5 + 6 gamma^3 zeta(2) - 16 gamma^2 zeta(3)
        -13 gamma zeta(4)/2 + 12 zeta(2) zeta(3) -69 zeta(5)/5;

  B_5-C_5 = 30 gamma^2 zeta(2) - 60 gamma zeta(3)
            +35 zeta(4)/3.

The additional depth-two derivatives combine with exactly the signs
specified by
  chi_4 = 2(partial_1-partial_2) Z_2(3,1) + partial_1 Z_2(2,2).

The ordinary polygamma series has the stated subtraction and
O(n^-2 log^3 n) summand. In the Mellin representation of phi, the
subtracted regular remainder has value zero at order one. Thus the
inverse-Gamma contribution is exactly [u^5]1/Gamma(1+u), with no
omitted lower coefficient.

The fifth-degree polynomial cancels eta and theta but retains chi_4.
The theorem correctly avoids claiming the stronger universal
one-coordinate reduction.

Findings: resolvents and mixed logarithms
----------------------------------------
The global sine factorization, Gamma Fourier coefficients, ordinary
integral domains, Fourier orientation signs, and log-Gamma order
differentiation agree with the endpoint-normalized branches.

At the right endpoint the completed smooth Hurwitz factor is h_p(s,-y).
Its coefficient is exp(pi*i*E)(-1)^j b_j. No additional (-1)^p is
present. Both complete local quotients in the resonance germ are
necessary, and deleting them gives the stated coordinate finite parts.
Only integrality of the total base exponent is required.

The special-point mean, residue polynomial, transverse and curved-path
limits, and negative-resonance logarithmic family are consistent with
the complete endpoint correction.

The mixed-log Fourier convolution is absolutely convergent in Re u>0.
The depth-two reduction follows from an exact partial fraction, using
the combined strict value at colors (1,1). The local subtraction
retains every logarithmic amplitude and every coefficient of A_p(s).
There is no additional right-endpoint resonance for the logarithmic
kernel alone.

The eta special-point identity was checked by direct Laurent
multiplication, including
  -(gamma^3+log^3(2*pi))/3,
  -gamma*zeta(2)-log(2*pi)*zeta(2)/2,
  -2*zeta(3)/3.
The ordinary triple-log identity, Gamma moment generator, displayed
mixed moments, and completed two-parameter primitive were also checked.
