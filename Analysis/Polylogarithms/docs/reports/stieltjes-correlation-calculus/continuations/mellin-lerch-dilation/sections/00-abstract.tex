\begin{abstract}
We develop three exact identity calculi that continue the ProveIt manuscript and its five latest incoming reports. First, a Mellin transform of a polylogarithm against $(1+x)^{-N}$ is reduced, for every positive integer $N$ and every complex spectral order, to a finite sum of differences of Lerch transcendents. The differences have cancelling positive-axis jumps. All integer Mellin parameters in the convergence strip are removable resonances, and every logarithmic moment there reduces to finitely many ordinary polylogarithms. At unit argument, spectral differentiation gives integral identities for log-gamma, polygamma, and generalized Stieltjes constants, with explicit harmonic coefficients at zeta poles. Second, we give a two-variable digamma reflection formula for shifted generalized harmonic sums and an exact coefficient procedure. Its unshifted parity specializations have published antecedents and are credited accordingly. Third, we resolve the incoming question about integer dilation and finite-part normalization. Explicit local polynomials describe the difference between distributional pullback and constant-term subtraction in the ambient coordinate. Unequal dilations reduce to one shift governed by their greatest common divisor; the resulting polygamma product formula involves only Hurwitz zeta and its first spectral derivative, with a necessary logarithm of the least common multiple. All assertions include analytic proofs. The accompanying computations check finite algebra and compare independent integral or series representations; they do not replace those proofs. The Gaussian $S_6$ and $S_8$ evaluations remain conjectural.
\end{abstract}
