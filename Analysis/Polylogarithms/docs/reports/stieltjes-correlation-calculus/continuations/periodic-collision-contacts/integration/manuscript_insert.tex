\section{Periodic collision contacts in a fixed coordinate}
\label{pcc:section}
An off-point collision formula does not determine a periodic distributional
extension. The following comparison uses unit-coordinate Hadamard finite
parts: the coordinate is $x$ for each factor, and the coordinates are $a$ and
$1-a$ for the separated correlation as a function of its shift. The full
analytic subtraction lemma, its proof, and reproducible checks are supplied
in the accompanying report \emph{Periodic Stieltjes Collisions}.

Put $T_{m,p}=\operatorname{Fp}_x\gamma_m^{(p)}(x)$ on
$\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$, and let $R$ denote reflection. Write $J_{mn}^{p,q}(a)$
for the separated correlation with the specified local coordinate finite
parts, and $\pccH[J]$ for its two-endpoint Hadamard extension in $a$.
Normalize $\langle\delta^{(r)},\varphi\rangle=(-1)^r\varphi^{(r)}(0)$.
Set
\[
 P_j(t)=\frac{(1+t)_j}{j!},\qquad
 A(u,v)=\frac{\Gamma(1-u)\Gamma(1-v)}{\Gamma(1-u-v)}
 \frac{\cos(\pi(u-v)/2)}{\cos(\pi(u+v)/2)}.
\]
\begin{theorem}[All-index collision contact]
\label{pcc:theorem}
For $r=p+q$ and $N=m+n$,
\[
 (RT_{m,p})*T_{n,q}=\pccH[J_{mn}^{p,q}]
       +\kappa_{mn}^{p,q}\delta^{(r)},
\]
where
\begin{align*}
\pccB_{pq}(u,v)={}&A(u,v)P_r(u+v)+P_p(u)P_q(v)\\
 &-P_p(u)P_r(v)-P_q(v)P_r(u),\\
\kappa_{mn}^{p,q}={}&(-1)^{p+N}m!n!
 [u^{m+1}v^{n+1}]\pccB_{pq}(u,v).
\end{align*}
No lower delta derivatives occur in this comparison. At Stieltjes index zero,
\[
 \kappa_{00}^{p,q}=(-1)^p\left[2\zeta(2)
 +(H_r-H_p)(H_r-H_q)-H_r^{(2)}\right].
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $\mathcal Z(t)$ be the meromorphic periodic Hurwitz family representing
$\zeta(1+t,x)$, with zero Fourier coefficient at frequency zero and
$\Gamma(-t)(2\pi\mathrm i k)^t$ at $k\ne0$. Its residue at zero is
$1-\delta$. Full coordinate subtraction gives the holomorphic family
\[
 \mathcal T_p(t)=\pccD^p\mathcal Z(t)-\frac{\delta_{p0}}t
                  +\frac{P_p(t)}t\delta^{(p)}.
\]
Indeed a local term $c(t)x^{-r-1-t}$ paired with a test function has resonant
contribution
\[-\frac{c(t)\varphi^{(r)}(0)}{r!t}.\]
Its entire numerator must be
subtracted, because the coordinate finite part of every
$x^{-1}\log^j x$ is zero. The remaining Taylor-subtracted integral and all
fixed spectral derivatives are dominated by $x^{-\eta}(1+|\log x|^M)$,
$\eta<1$, so coefficient extraction is valid. Thus
\[(-1)^m m![t^m]\mathcal T_p(t)=T_{m,p}.\]

Write $E(u,v)=\Gamma(1-u)\Gamma(1+u+v)/\Gamma(1+v)$. Gamma reflection gives
$vE(u,v)+uE(v,u)=(u+v)A(u,v)$. Let $\mathcal J^{\rm mer}_{pq}$ be the
meromorphic separated generator before its shift-coordinate completion.
For $r>0$, its completion replaces
$W_r(t)=(-1)^r\pccD^r\mathcal Z(t)$ by
$W_r(t)+(-1)^rP_r(t)\delta^{(r)}/t$ in each Hurwitz term, with reflection
in the right-endpoint term. The resulting added coefficient is
\[
 \frac{(-1)^p}{uv}
 \big(P_p(u)P_r(v)+P_q(v)P_r(u)-A(u,v)P_r(u+v)\big)
 \delta^{(r)}.
\]
Expanding $(R\mathcal T_p(u))*\mathcal T_q(v)$ instead gives the same
$\mathcal J^{\rm mer}_{pq}$, plus
$(-1)^pP_p(u)P_q(v)\delta^{(r)}/(uv)$. At $r=0$, the constant terms from
subtracting $1/t$ are retained in both generators and the same comparison
holds. Subtraction gives $(-1)^p\pccB_{pq}(u,v)\delta^{(r)}/(uv)$.
Since $A(u,0)=A(0,v)=1$ and $P_j(0)=1$, the numerator vanishes on both axes;
coefficient extraction proves the theorem. Expanding
$A=1+2\zeta(2)uv+O((|u|+|v|)^3)$ and
$P_j=1+H_jt+(H_j^2-H_j^{(2)})t^2/2+O(t^3)$ proves the specialization.
\end{proof}

In particular,
\[
 (RT_{0,0})*T_{0,0}=T_{1,0}+RT_{1,0}+2\zeta(2)(\delta-1).
\]
Omitting the atom would give nonzero mean, although both factors have zero
mean. The scalar finite constant of the prior off-point collision remainder
is a different quantity and is unchanged. Under $h$ mean-zero integrations,
$\pccD^{-h}(\delta-1)=-B_h/h!$, so the contact term becomes an explicit
Bernoulli correction in ordinary primitive and log-Gamma correlations.
