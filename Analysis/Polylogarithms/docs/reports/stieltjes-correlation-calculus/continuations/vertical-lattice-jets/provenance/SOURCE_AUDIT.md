# Source and novelty audit

## Reading scope

The requested manuscript and incoming paths were inspected through the connected
GitHub tool. The manuscript README and selected harmonic-tail reports identified
which developments were already present. The continuous vertical-pairing README
was read at the pinned commit in `sources.json`; its stated subject is ordinary
vertical integrals, not discrete lattice sums. This package does not claim to
have audited every theorem of that report or every page of the collective book.

The main branch changed during the investigation. Returned blob identifiers
are therefore recorded individually. Sources with a `main` read are not silently
attributed to the separately pinned commit.

Five incoming ZIPs were listed. Their names and Git blob IDs appear in the source
map, but their interior files were not inspected. Binary retrieval was not
successful in the available path. No theorem from those unseen interiors is
used as a premise, and no assertion is made that the delivered results are absent
from every such archive.

## Overlap found and honored

An early calculation rediscovered the order-two harmonic-tail determinant. A
later repository report already proves that family (and smaller orders four
and six). That calculation was not promoted as a new contribution.

The external literature check found Arash Arabi Ardehali and Hjalmar Rosengren,
*A New Product Formula for (z;q)_infinity, with Applications to Asymptotics*,
Constructive Approximation, published 19 September 2026,
DOI 10.1007/s00365-026-09783-2. Its Equation (13) is exactly the Gamma
specialization of the present spectral family after y=tau*a and beta=tau.
The paper also gives the Binet/Poisson proof and higher Stirling subtractions.
The Gamma specialization and its tau/24 constant are explicitly credited.
The present proposed additions focus on full spectral/Stieltjes jets,
negative-integer contacts and their harmonic compiler, and discrete product images.

Poisson summation, Euler–Maclaurin, Mellin transforms, and reciprocal-Gamma Bell
expansions are classical mechanisms, not claimed inventions. Coffey's 2010
Stieltjes summation paper is included as background; no sweeping claim that
Stieltjes summation itself is new is made.

## Corrections and limits

A repeated README passage is the concrete editorial correction. The proposed
local-copy-only script is guarded and does not change the repository.

The omission of a resonant contact is analyzed as an invalid interchange and
used as a corruption control. No inspected canonical theorem was established to
make that error. It must not be attributed to a source without a matching
occurrence.

No exhaustive historical priority, proof-assistant formalization, period
independence, S6/S8 proof, or complete unequal-tail prefix proof is claimed.
The analytic proofs in the article establish the stated identities independently
of pending repository claims; numerical diagnostics do not confer proof status.
