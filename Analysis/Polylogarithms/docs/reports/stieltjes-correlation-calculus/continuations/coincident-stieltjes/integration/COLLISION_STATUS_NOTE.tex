\paragraph{Collision question: function-level finite constants resolved.}
The continuation \emph{Coincident-Point Stieltjes Calculus} fixes the
unit-coordinate Hadamard finite part and proves, at all nonnegative
Stieltjes indices $m,n$ and argument-derivative orders $p,q$, the exact
splitting
\[
 J_{mn}^{p,q}(a)
 =a^{-p-q-1}\mathcal P_{mn}^{p,q}(\log a)+R_{mn}^{p,q}(a),
 \qquad 0<a<1.
\]
The polynomial has degree at most $m+n+1$, the remainder is analytic for
$|a|<1$, and $R_{mn}^{p,q}(0)$ equals the directly defined same-point
Hadamard moment. Explicit gamma-quotient coefficient formulas determine
both the singular polynomial and that moment. This resolves the off-point
collision expansion and the finite-constant comparison. It does not yet
identify all delta-supported terms in a periodic distributional extension;
that part of the earlier question remains separate.
