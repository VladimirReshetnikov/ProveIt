\section*{Audit: beta--Stieltjes jets at the canceled total-order pole}

The proposed identity is correct.  Assume $\Re a>0$, $\Re b>-1$ and
$0<\Re\sigma<1$; the originally proposed $\Re b>0$ is a sufficient
smaller domain.  Put $y=b+1$, $\delta=a-b$, and
\[
 A_\pm(u)=y+(\delta\pm1)u.
\]
For $m\ge0$, define the ordinary, locally normally convergent series
\[
 T_m=\sum_{k\ge0}\bigl\{(k+a+2)^{-\sigma}-(k+a)^{-\sigma}\bigr\}
 (k+y)^{\sigma-1}\{-\log(k+y)\}^m.
\]
The canceled difference makes its summand
$O(k^{-2}\log^m(k+1))$, uniformly on compact parameter sets.  In fact
this series is entire in $\sigma$, while the unregularized beta integral
below is asserted only on the stated strip.

Set
\[
 \ell_1(u)=\log(1-u)+\psi(1)-\psi(1-\sigma),\qquad
 \ell_j=\psi^{(j-1)}(1)-\psi^{(j-1)}(1-\sigma),\quad j\ge2,
\]
and let $\mathcal B_j$ denote the complete exponential Bell polynomial,
with $\mathcal B_0=1$.  Then
\[
 T_m=\frac{\sin(\pi\sigma)}\pi
 \int_0^1u^{\sigma-1}(1-u)^{-\sigma}
 \sum_{j=0}^m\binom mj(-1)^j
 \mathcal B_{m-j}(\ell_1,\ldots,\ell_{m-j})
 \{\gamma_j(A_+(u))-\gamma_j(A_-(u))\}\,du.
\]

\paragraph{Proof and the required order of operations.}
Start with $t=1-\sigma+v$, where $\Re v>0$ is sufficiently small, so
both individual Feynman kernel integrals and series converge.  Their
difference is
\[
 \frac{\Gamma(1+v)}{\Gamma(\sigma)\Gamma(1-\sigma+v)}
 \int_0^1 u^{\sigma-1}(1-u)^{v-\sigma}
 \{\zeta(1+v,A_+(u))-\zeta(1+v,A_-(u))\}\,du.
\]
The two Hurwitz poles must be subtracted before setting $v=0$ or
differentiating.  Their residues are equal to one, so the difference
is holomorphic at $v=0$.  The shift arguments remain on a compact
subset of the right half-plane as $u$ ranges over $[0,1]$ and the
parameters range over compact subsets of the domain.  Uniform
holomorphy of the regularized Hurwitz germ, together with the integrable
weights
$u^{\Re\sigma-1}(1-u)^{-\Re\sigma}|\log(1-u)|^m$, proves local
normality and differentiation under the integral.

The logarithmic derivatives at $v=0$ of
$\Gamma(1+v)(1-u)^v/\Gamma(1-\sigma+v)$ are exactly the displayed
$\ell_j$.  The $j$th derivative of the regularized Hurwitz difference
is $(-1)^j\{\gamma_j(A_+)-\gamma_j(A_-)\}$.  Leibniz's rule and
Euler's reflection formula give the proposed identity, including all
signs and binomial factors.

\paragraph{Specialization and a useful extension.}
At $a=b=1$, for every complex $\sigma$, the direct binomial expansion
of the ordinary series gives the normally convergent formula
\[
 T_m=-2\sum_{h\ge0}\frac{(\sigma)_{2h+1}}{(2h+1)!}
        \zeta^{(m)}(2h+2,2).
\]
Thus the displayed half-order expansion is correct and is the
$\sigma=1/2$ instance of a formula valid for all $\sigma$.
At $\sigma=1/2$ and any common shift $a=b$,
\[
 T_0=\sum_{k\ge0}\left\{
 \frac1{\sqrt{(k+a+1)(k+a+2)}}
 -\frac1{\sqrt{(k+a)(k+a+1)}}\right\}
 =-\frac1{\sqrt{a(a+1)}}.
\]
Square roots use the consistent right-half-plane branch.  In particular
$T_0=-1/\sqrt2$ at $a=1$.

\paragraph{Numerical verification strategy.}
The accompanying script checks Bell--Leibniz coefficients exactly
through order four.  For $a=b=1$, it evaluates the Stieltjes differences
inside the beta integral by uniformly convergent Taylor expansions in
$u$.  These are independently spot-checked against direct
\texttt{mpmath.stieltjes} calls.  The three differences needed for
$m\le2$ have odd-power coefficients, for odd $r$, given by
\begin{align*}
 c_{0,r}&=-2\zeta(r+1,2),\\
 c_{1,r}&=2\{H_r\zeta(r+1,2)+\zeta'(r+1,2)\},\\
 c_{2,r}&=-2\{(H_r^2-H_r^{(2)})\zeta(r+1,2)
              +2H_r\zeta'(r+1,2)+\zeta''(r+1,2)\}.
\end{align*}
Terms through $r=201$ are retained.  Elementary integral estimates for
$\sum_{n\ge2}\log^j(n)n^{-r-1}$ bound the omitted second-jet tail by
$\sum_{r\ge202}(3r^2+10r+15)2^{-r}$, less than $10^{-54}$ uniformly
for $0\le u\le1$; lower jets have smaller bounds.  Numerical rounding
is separately governed by the stated working precision.

The integral is evaluated after the logistic substitution
$u=e^z/(1+e^z)$, which removes the algebraic endpoint singularities.
It is compared with the separately evaluated Hurwitz-series expression
at $\sigma=0.3,0.5,0.7$, $m=0,1,2$.  The half-order zeroth jet is
also checked against its exact telescoping value.  A further three
integrals use independently shifted parameters
$a=0.7$, $b=1.1$, $\sigma=0.4$, $m=0,1,2$.  Here the two
Taylor displacements about $b+1=2.1$ are $0.6u$ and $-1.4u$;
all powers through degree $351$ are retained.  The uniform omitted
second-jet tail is bounded by
$\sum_{r\ge352}(4r^2+10r+18)(2/3)^r<1.6\cdot10^{-56}$.
These additional integrands are again spot-checked against direct
Stieltjes evaluations.  Across all twelve numerical beta integrals,
the largest discrepancy at $42$ decimal digits is less than
$3.6\cdot10^{-42}$.  These are numerical diagnostics, not
interval-certified proofs.  No domain or sign flaw was found.
