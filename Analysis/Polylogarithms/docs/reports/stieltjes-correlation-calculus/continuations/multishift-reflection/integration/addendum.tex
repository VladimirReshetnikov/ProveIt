\section{Completed multishift reflection and resonant spectral jets}
\label{mrc-add:sec}
The report \cite{multishift-reflection-2026} continues the independent-shift
centered-reflection and spectral-jet questions in the incoming continuous-depth
Lerch work. Let $g(p)=\pi/\sin(\pi p)$, with its logarithm real on $(0,1)$.
For $\operatorname{Re}R>0$ and $\operatorname{Re}a_j>0$, put
\[
 \mathcal M_R(\boldsymbol s;\boldsymbol a)
 =\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{c-i\infty}^{c+i\infty}
 g(p)^R\prod_j(p+a_j-1)^{-s_j}\,dp,
 \quad \max(0,1-\min_j\operatorname{Re}a_j)<c<1.
\]
It is entire in every order and holomorphic in every right-half-plane shift.

\paragraph{Completed reflection, real-shift display.}
For real $a_j>0$, initially excluding $a_j=1/2$, the centered integral
must be supplemented by
\begin{align*}
 \mathcal M_R={}&\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb R}
 \left(\frac\pi{\cosh\pi t}\right)^R
 \prod_j(a_j-1/2+it)^{-s_j}\,dt\\
 &+\frac1\pi\int_{1/2}^{\max_j(1-a_j)}
 g(x)^R\sin\left(\pi\sum_{x<1-a_j}s_j\right)
       \prod_j|x+a_j-1|^{-s_j}\,dx.
\end{align*}
The second integral is zero when its upper endpoint is at most $1/2$.
Split at all branch points. For a coincident block $B$ to the right of
$1/2$, the initial ordinary domain is
$\operatorname{Re}\sum_{j\in B}s_j<1$. The complete sum then continues
entirely in all orders and holomorphically through every collision.
The full report gives the corresponding horizontal-cut formula for arbitrary
complex shifts; cut heights can merge without an ordering ambiguity.

\paragraph{Mixed logarithmic overlap.}
Put $\ell_j=\log|x+a_j-1|$ and $\chi_j=\mathbf1_{x<1-a_j}$.
At total zero order, every spectral jet is an ordinary logarithmic integral.
The degree-three cut polynomial, divided by $\pi$, is
\[
 \chi_1\ell_2\ell_3+\chi_2\ell_1\ell_3+\chi_3\ell_1\ell_2
       -\pi^2\chi_1\chi_2\chi_3.
\]
The final overlap term cannot be omitted. At common shift and depth two,
the completed degree-$Q$ moment is $-Q\gamma_{Q-1}(a)$, for $Q\ge1$.
For two shifts $b=a+h$, $a>0$, $|h|<a$, the completed bilogarithmic
moment has the normally convergent expression
\[
 \Lambda_{2,(1,1)}(a,b)
 =-2\gamma_1(a)+\sum_{k\ge1}(-h)^k
  \left(\zeta^{[1]}(k+1,a)+\frac{\zeta(k+1,a)}k\right).
\]
The general mixed-jet formula uses unsigned Stirling coefficients and
one anchor Stieltjes constant plus positive-integer Hurwitz-zeta jets.

\paragraph{Integer contacts and Hermite reflection.}
For positive integer orders $n_j$, $N=\sum_jn_j$, and
$0<\operatorname{Re}a_j<1$,
\[
 \mathcal M_R(\boldsymbol n;\boldsymbol a)
 -(-1)^N\mathcal M_R(\boldsymbol n;\boldsymbol1-\boldsymbol a)
 =\sum_p\operatorname*{Res}_{z=p}
          \left(g(z)^R\prod_j(z+a_j-1)^{-n_j}\right).
\]
The residues are confluent Hermite divided differences. Centering an
integer pole contributes half its residue, in addition to its symmetric
Hadamard finite part. In particular
$\mathcal M_R(1;1/2)=\pi^R/2$, not zero.

\paragraph{All centered spectral jets.}
The entire alternating family is the continuation of
\[
 Z_R(v)=\frac1{\Gamma(v)}\int_0^\infty
          t^{v-1}(2\cosh(t/2))^{-R}\,dt.
\]
For $F_R(w)=\mathcal M_R(w;1/2)$, set $L=\log(2\pi)$ and
$C^{\rm o}_{R,m}=(-1)^m\pi(2\pi)^{R+2m-1}/(2m)!$.
The first odd spectral derivative is
\[
 F_R^{[1]}(2m+1)=C^{\rm o}_{R,m}
 \left((L+\gamma-H_{2m})Z_R(-2m)-Z_R^{[1]}(-2m)\right).
\]
Every higher odd or even derivative has an explicit finite Bell formula
in the report. The necessary $Z_R$ derivatives are retained; discrete
odd-value formulas alone do not determine them.

\paragraph{Gamma--polylogarithm consequence.}
Writing
$G_R(x)=\Gamma(R/2+ix/(2\pi))\Gamma(R/2-ix/(2\pi))$, one has the entire
spectral identity
\begin{align*}
 &\int_{\mathbb R}G_R(x)
 \frac{\operatorname{Li}_s(ie^{-x/2})-
       \operatorname{Li}_s(-ie^{-x/2})}{i}\,dx\\
 &\qquad=8\Gamma(R)\pi^{s+1}\Gamma(1-s)
                      \cos(\pi s/2)Z_{R+1}(1-s),
\end{align*}
with removable resonances. All fixed order and depth derivatives pass
under the ordinary integral. At $R=1$ the value simplifies to
$4\pi s(2-2^{-s})\zeta(s+1)$, giving explicit Stieltjes-valued order jets.
