\subsection{Higher quadratic spectral jets: continuation overview}
\label{hqj:integration:overview}
For $x_n=n+a$, $a,b>0$, let $Z_W(s)=\sum_{n\ge0}W(x_n)(x_n^2+b^2)^{-s}$
and $F_{W,\pm}(s)=\sum_{n\ge0}W(x_n)(x_n\pm ib)^{-s}$, by continuation.
The report \emph{Higher Quadratic Spectral Jets} (11 October 2026) evaluates
\[
\Delta_r=2^{r-1}\bigl(F_{W,+}^{(r)}(0)+F_{W,-}^{(r)}(0)\bigr)-Z_W^{(r)}(0)
\]
at every order by a normally convergent harmonic--Hurwitz series with
explicit Stieltjes contact terms. The finite coefficient polynomial is
\[
C_j(s)=\frac{(2s)_{2j}}{(2j)!}-\frac{(s)_j}{j!}.
\]
At a colliding Hurwitz argument, its coefficient $[s^{r+1}]C_j/2$ must
be retained. Even weights remove the local residue, not the higher-order
defect. In particular,
\[
\Delta_2(1;a,b)=-4\sum_{n\ge0}\arctan^2\!\left(\frac{b}{n+a}\right).
\]
For fixed $a>0$ and fixed $r\ge2$, vanishing at every $b>0$ forces $W=0$.

For $E(s;a,b)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}((n+a)^2+b^2)^{-s}$, the report gives
all second jets $E''(-m)$ in finite polylogarithm, order-derivative, and
ordinary integral coordinates. The normalized germ
\[
\mathfrak A_m(\varepsilon;a,u)=
\frac{m!\Gamma(1-\varepsilon)}{\Gamma(m+1-\varepsilon)}
E(-m+\varepsilon;a,\sqrt u)
\]
satisfies $\partial_u\mathfrak A_m=m\mathfrak A_{m-1}$.
A complete polynomially weighted two-mass second-jet formula and its
convergent collision expansion are proved as well. Every collision
coefficient has a finite polygamma representation.

They do not prove minimality of the added integral coordinate or change the
