## Corrections and safeguards

The exact README version above repeats a passage beginning “The complete
1,032-input first-letter X family” consecutively. Removing its second copy is
a source-specific editorial proposal. No false canonical mathematical theorem
was established by the targeted review.

The article's warnings about subtraction before spectral restriction, the
order-m-minus-one connected anomaly, principal logarithms, mixed pi-squared
jump terms, Euler-constant boundary corrections, and elementary versus complete
harmonic coefficients are mathematical safeguards for the new results. They
are not descriptions of mistakes supposedly found in an unread report.

## Unsettled targets

The general unequal-tail optimal first-q conjecture, the Gaussian S6 and revised
S8 conjectures, and a general restriction theorem for singular distributional
collisions are not proved here. This package supplies new exact identities in
its explicitly defined family, not a claim that those named targets have been
resolved. Finite determinant experiments are not used as an infinite-family
proof, and numerical residuals are not used as exact-equality proofs.
