\begin{abstract}
We develop an exact calculus for translated, bilinear correlations of Hurwitz-zeta jets. The ordinary shifted Hurwitz correlation has both a polylogarithmic Fourier representation and a two-term beta--Hurwitz representation. Expanding the latter at the double pole proves that every canonically regularized product of two generalized Stieltjes functions is a \emph{linear} combination of single Stieltjes functions, with coefficients polynomial in positive integer zeta values. In particular,
\[
\FP\int_0^1\psi(x)\psi(\{x+a\})\dd x
=\gamma_1(a)+\gamma_1(1-a)-\frac{\pi^2}{3},\qquad 0<a<1.
\]
We determine the contact terms needed to differentiate these identities: periodic finite-part extension and ordinary parameter differentiation do not commute. The resulting all-order formula reduces every shifted polygamma product to first spectral derivatives of Hurwitz zeta. A second-primitive lifting theorem converts the entire Stieltjes closure into \emph{ordinary convergent} integrals of Stieltjes antiderivatives. Its first case evaluates the translated log-gamma correlation using first and second Hurwitz-zeta derivatives at $-1$ and a Bernoulli polynomial. Explicit rational specializations, exact coefficient code, numerical cross-checks, and integration notes accompany the proofs. Classical antecedents are credited separately from the continuation developed here; no claim of global priority or resolution of the repository's $S_6$ and $S_8$ conjectures is made.
\end{abstract}
