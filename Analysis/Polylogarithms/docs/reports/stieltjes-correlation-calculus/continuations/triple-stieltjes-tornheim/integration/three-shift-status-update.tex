\paragraph{Three separated Stieltjes factors: representation question.}
The companion report \emph{Triple Stieltjes Correlations: Colored Tornheim
Jets, Harmonic Contact Terms, and Exact Gamma Reductions} gives an
all-index representation for the canonical product of three separated
Stieltjes singularities. Its Theorems 4.1--4.2 use six entire colored
Tornheim functions, and Theorem 5.1 gives three ordinary convergent
polylogarithmic Mellin integrals with explicit endpoint constants. The
three-digamma specialization is fully regularized. Thus the representation
question posed here has an affirmative answer in the report's stated
scale-one normalization, subject to canonical analytic review.

This does not establish a single-Stieltjes reduction for every unreflected
triple, numerical period independence, a generic collision law, or the
remaining $S_6$ and $S_8$ special-value conjectures. Those stronger questions
remain separate. The original question is retained as historical context.
