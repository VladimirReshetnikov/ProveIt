Independent mathematical audit of the S6 rational residual certificate

Mathematical checks
------------------
1. With v=t^2, z=t^2*u^q and the positive measure in Section 4.8,
   c_n = integral(v^n-z^n) and integral(v-z)=c_1=H_q^(b)/3^a.
   Although the measure has infinite total mass, v-z is integrable.
2. For k>=1, the backward finite-difference numerator is
   integral[(1-v)^k-(1-z)^k]. It is nonpositive and has absolute value
   at most k*c_1. Hence all Euler-tail terms have the asserted sign,
   and summing k>=N gives (N+1)*2^(-N)*c_1 exactly. The alternative
   derivative bound for the finite Euler-tail kernel gives the same result.
3. For elementary coefficients 1/(d*n+1)^s, the moment measure is
   positive with total mass 1. Euler-tail terms are nonnegative and
   their sum is at most 2^(-N).
4. The conversions zeta(3)=(4/3)eta(3), zeta(5)=(16/15)eta(5),
   G=beta(2), and log(2)=eta(1) are correct.
5. Machin's identity has the correct factors and sign. Exact rational
   tangent arithmetic gives tan(4 atan(1/5)-atan(1/239))=1; the angle
   lies in (0,pi/2). Both used truncation counts are even, so their
   arctangent partial sums are lower bounds. Scaling the second interval
   by -4 correctly reverses its endpoints.
6. All interval multiplication extrema are taken over the four corners;
   powers use repeated sound multiplication; negative coefficient
   scalings reverse endpoints. Shared constants cause no validity issue
   because this merely enlarges an enclosure.
7. Denominator divisibility is correct for q,d in {1,2}: every divisor
   in the N input terms is at most 2N and divides lcm(1,...,2N).
8. The integer tail-weight recurrence gives sum_{j=n+1}^N binom(N,j).
   These are the exact Euler weights for k=0,...,N-1.
9. Signed floor and ceiling implement outward decimal rounding. Every
   endpoint is inclusive. The claimed absolute bound is strict.

Independent execution checks
----------------------------
The original source was executed unchanged in a separate audit directory,
leaving the source and its original certificate intact.
The regenerated report is identical to the original report. Every basket
and residual decimal interval was checked against the underlying exact
Fraction endpoints for outward enclosure and rounding width <10^-280.
All 96 independent finite-difference-versus-binomial-weight comparisons
for the four harmonic baskets and N=1,...,24 agreed exactly.

The residual enclosure, displayed here to 20 significant digits, is
[-5.0156638589421246319e-261, 3.844034286695850983e-262].
The complete 280-decimal-place outward-rounded endpoints are in
data/s6_rational_certificate.json. Both the exact rational interval and
those full exported endpoints lie strictly between -10^-260 and 10^-260.

Assertion hardening completed
-----------------------------
The final implementation requires digits_bound>=260 and directly checks
both rational endpoints against +/-10^-260. These assertions strengthen
the release gate; they do not change the computed interval. The hardened
implementation was rerun successfully, including through make exact.
Its delivered checksum is recorded in SHA256SUMS.
