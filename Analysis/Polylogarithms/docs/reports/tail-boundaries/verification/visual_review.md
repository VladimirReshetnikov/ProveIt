# Final PDF review

The final deliverable is a 40-page US-letter PDF: one cover, two contents
pages, and 37 numbered article/reference pages. The modular source and the
single-file source were compared after flattening and agree exactly.

Every page of the initial complete rendering was visually inspected in seven
contact sheets. Long equations were rebroken, title-page hyperlink anchors
were separated from the numbered pages, and the bibliography was set at a
readable smaller size to remove an otherwise nearly empty final page. The
changed contents, leading-determinant page, and complete final bibliography
were inspected again at full image size. The order-ten residue table and
five-by-five matrix were also inspected at full size.

Final compilation has no overfull boxes, underfull boxes, unresolved references,
duplicate labels, or duplicate page destinations. Text-position inspection of
all 40 final pages found no words outside the review margins. Equation numbers,
matrices, subscripts, bibliography links, headers, and footers are readable and
unclipped. This is a document-layout review; mathematical evidence is recorded
in the separate verification outputs and the article's proofs.
