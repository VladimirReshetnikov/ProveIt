## The same theorems, delivered several times

- **Zero theorem** (three deliveries: 06, 12, 13). `γ_n^{(k)}(a)` has at most
  `n` positive zeros for every `k ≥ 1`, exactly `n` simple zeros for large
  `k`, with complete ray expansions. **Base: 13**, which proves it uniformly
  in a positive Lerch deformation `0 ≤ ρ ≤ 1`, with a large-`k` threshold
  uniform in `ρ`. Credit 06 and 12 for independent proofs. 06 alone adds
  the exact count `n` for every `k` when `n ≤ 4` (computer-assisted: 14
  exact sign certificates at `k = 1`) and the three-term law
  `α_k = e^γ k + d + c/(k+1) + O(k⁻²)`; 06 and 12 add persistence and
  interlacing.
- **The `n = 1` bracket.** Cite the half-unit bracket
  `e^{H_{k−1}} < α_k < e^{H_{k−1}} + 1/2` (06, 12; purely analytic in 06).
  13's upper bound `α_k ≤ e^{H_k}` is weaker for every `k` (the dossier's
  argument: `e^{H_k} − e^{H_{k−1}} > e^{H_{k−1}}/k ≥ 1`); keep 13 for the
  monotonicity of `α_k` in `ρ` and `k`.
- **Trivial-zero bridge with the factor `1/2`** (four deliveries: 06, 10, 12,
  13). The normalized functional equation at the trivial zeros, with first
  logarithmic derivative `L″(−k)/(2L′(−k))`; printed once in this report,
  all four credited.
- The **distribution-rank theorem** that 13 also proves (for `Re s > 0`) is
  based in [`../rational-grid-distribution-ranks/`](../rational-grid-distribution-ranks/)
  (base 10).

These are the intake dossier's (dossier138_POLYLOG) findings, with its own
numerical checks; they are not re-proved in this note.
