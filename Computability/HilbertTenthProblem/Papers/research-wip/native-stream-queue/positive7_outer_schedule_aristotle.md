## 4. Common column sums reconstruct the seventh output

For the accepted weighted7 alphabet, every column of every A_sigma sums to the same fixed C. Compute V1,...,V6 densely, then

    Vsum6=V1+...+V6,

The six dense rows cost42mM+(42m-6)A. Equation(5) costs one C-product, five additions and one subtraction, giving the combined action cost

    (42m+1)M+42mA.                                          (6)

At every typed one-hot native zero, exact selection gives Ztot=S. The actual full seven-row selected action satisfies

    sum_i V_i_actual=C*Ztot=C*S.

Consequently(5) equals V7_actual at every such zero. The native packs and their positive-domain proof do not depend on any V_i or terminal coordinate. Thus they can be decoded first, without assuming the recurrence for the reconstructed output. Retaining the seven recurrences then gives exactly the same genuine trajectory and endpoint as Dense7. Conversely every Dense7 zero has the same native/outer data and satisfies(5) with the true seventh update. This is positive-zero equivalence with all supplied witness coordinates preserved.

**Review remark 1 (this row reconstruction is not an all-ring identity).** On arbitrary untyped selected fields, the sum of the full action rows is C*Ztot, not C*S. The reconstructed seventh row differs from the dense row by C*(S-Ztot). Nothing in this schedule identifies those polynomials on all assignments. Their equality is proved on complete native zeros by the one-hot selection theorem. A potentially negative off-zero computed V7 is harmless: it is not a supplied positive witness or a native input port.

Only the42m retained row1--6 coefficient roles, the common C and the existing program/radix numerals need be declared as live fixed ports in this variant. The omitted seventh-row coefficients must not be advertised as live source inputs. ## 5. A stronger hatted global chart, proved but not adopted

The frozen geometry explicitly left the stronger comparison

    S+sum_(sigma,k) Zhat_sigma,k+g_new=P                      (8)

unproved, and used the correct offset version instead. Under this specific positive7 recipe, (8) is in fact a valid alternative positive chart. Root proposed the offset-removal direction; the margin proof below was independently developed and challenged by Aristotle and Riemann. It is not part of ledgers(4),(7).

Let g_old=P-S-Ztot at a full zero of the adopted source. Exact one-hot selection gives Ztot=S, and the aggregate range gives S<= (D-1)J. Hence

    g_old=P-2S>=((K-2)D+1)J+1.

Every strictly positive7-by-7 letter has each column sum at least7, so K>Lambda and dyadic K imply K>=8. Positive initial mass is at least7 and the height slack is positive, so D>=8. Also m<K and J>=1. Therefore

    g_old-7m
       >=(K-2)D+2-7(K-1)
        =(K-2)(D-7)-5
       >=K-7>=1.                                            (9)

Thus g_new=g_old-7m is positive at every old full zero. Conversely any new positive tuple restores g_old=g_new+7m>0. Since Ztot=sum Zhat-7m, these substitutions make the global residual identical on all integer assignments, and all other values are unchanged. This proves a bijection of full positive zero sets for the alternative chart, including the previously unproved inverse positivity.

**Review remark 2 (scope of the hatted offset removal).** The original geometry's warning was correct within its stated proof: no-offset hatted completeness was not established there. Equation(9) supplies that missing margin using the positive7, K>=8,D>=8,m<K hypotheses. It is not a general permission to drop hat offsets in arbitrary packed certificates. Since the current schedules already compute every Z unhat for packing and action arithmetic, replacing the unhat total by a hat total alone does not establish a row saving; further packed-hat sharing would need a separate literal schedule and consumer audit.
