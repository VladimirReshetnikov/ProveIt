## Reports 35 and 36: checked publication claims

The new Part XX scope, finite-prism formulas, binary-legality argument,
real predecessor-rigidity argument, loader conclusions, and the complete
section recording the research programme's review and shared arithmetic
were read directly. The checked claims agree with the frozen reports and
[sandpile arithmetic author](sandpile_shared_arithmetic35_36.md) and
[independent review](review_sandpile_shared_arithmetic35_36.md).

The summary theorem now explicitly requires natural initial heights,
an initially stable exterior, and all finite additions inside the prism.
This is a necessary standing hypothesis already present in the detailed
finite-prism argument. The publication discloses the clarification; the
loader satisfies it through its stable background and interior seeds.
It does not change the archived proof bytes.

For a nonempty rectangular prism, the formulas
`V=abc`, `S=ab+ac+bc`, `E=3V-S`, `H=2S`,
`W=8V+6E+H`, and `8V+7E+H` displayed summands retain their original scope:
a unique natural zero exactly for a legal global stabilization with binary
odometer supported in the prism. General nonbinary odometers are excluded.
The real upgrade is `Qreal=Q+sum f(k+c)`. The proof forces integrality by
local edge alternatives and finite predecessor descent, without assuming
integral ranks. After the shift `w=p-1`, its real theorem applies to
`p>=1` componentwise, not to every positive real tuple.

The moment formula keeps the actual selector sum rather than substituting
its on-zero value. With `g=sigma+2` and
`S=lo+neg+eq+pos+hi`, it is the all-ring identity

    lo(d+g)^2+neg(d+1)^2+eq*d^2+pos(d-1)^2+hi(d-g)^2
      = S*d^2-2*d*(g*(hi-lo)+(pos-neg))+g^2*(lo+hi)+neg+pos.

Together with `(f+k)*beta+(f+k)*h=(f+k)*(beta+h)`, this saves
`(V+4E)` multiplications and `E` additions against the declared shared
direct schedule. The publication correctly distinguishes this convention
from the original expanded-evaluation ledger. Its natural fixture totals
54 to 53, 143 to 136, and 831 to 763, and real totals 55 to 54,
145 to 138, and 839 to 771 agree with the frozen packet. Its claims of
24 complete sources, 8,788 paid gates, 38,012 independently compared
coefficient entries, and 24 zero tuples from three legal stabilizations
retain the original finite-check scope.

The loader asserts finitely many total topplings iff halting; one-shot
behaviour at each site also holds along nonhalting runs. The stated prism
bound is conditional on an actual halting time, and the ordinary program
to finite universal-machine tape encoder remains an inherited dependency.
The publication does not turn the unbounded prism family into a fixed-arity
ordinary-input Diophantine polynomial. Its real-algebraic obstruction is
the standard univariate semialgebraic finite/cofinite argument, not a new
sandpile theorem or an arithmetic lower bound. The current 84-operation
universal construction is not improved by these finite-prism calculations.
