## 4. Exact finite cost and degree objective

Let N,G,U,L,Q be the five indicators, with N=1 meaning normalized strong and
G=1 the global unit. The factor core has

    C=358+N+G+L+Q gates,
    n=11+N+G+U+L+Q individual factors,
    m=5-N-G-U-L-Q ordinary comparisons.              (2)

For g nonempty groups, their products require n-g multiplications. A normal
SOS or anchored finalizer gives complete cost

    C+n+3m-1+2g
      =383-N-G-2U-L-Q+2g.                           (3)

There is one explicit boundary case: with m=0, g=1 and an anchor, no ordinary
or nonanchor residual remains. The source emits the sole product minus1
directly, at cost C+n, one below(3). This case occurs only when all five
indicators are1. Its source costs378; its SOS version costs379 and doubles
the degree bound from4713 to9426, so SOS is strictly dominated. For g>1,
or whenever m>0, equation(3) applies without alteration. No empty sum of
squares, addition of zero or multiplication by one is silently free.

Let the individual positive propagated weights be w_i and let r be the maximum
ordinary residual degree, with r=0 if none remain. For group weights W_j,

    SOS degree <= 2 max(r,W_1,...,W_g),
    anchor a degree <= W_a+2 max(r,{W_j:j!=a}).       (4)

The actual literal degree dictionary verifies(4) for every emitted schedule,
including the empty-residual case. It retains the two guarded native/main
norm cancellations. These remain upper bounds, not claims about a completely
expanded polynomial or cancellation between finalizer terms.

The inherited exact subset dynamic program uses

    opt(S,k)=min max(sum(T), opt(S\T,k-1)),

where T runs over nonempty subsets containing the least element of S and
leaving enough elements for k-1 further groups. This removes only permutation
symmetry. For SOS it computes the minimum largest group weight. For anchors
it checks every possible nonempty distinguished group and solves the same
maximum objective on the complement. Greedy partitions provide upper bounds;
pruning uses the largest remaining item and ceiling(total/k) lower bounds.
Thus no improving partition is discarded. All positive weights and r>=0
satisfy the implementation's hypotheses, including r=0 here.

For a fixed base and g, cost(3) is independent of the partition and anchor,
so the least degree in(4) dominates every other such plan. The exceptional
m=0,g=1 case also has a unique full factor group, with the anchored form
strictly dominating SOS in both objectives. Hence432 emitted group-count
optima exhaust the 32-base tradeoff objective. The constructor additionally
accepts arbitrary legal ordered plans, so the theorem is not restricted to
the saved winners.

Finally, for any anchor subset A, its complement's largest group weight is
at least its largest individual weight. The unrestricted floor certificate
checks every nonempty A against

    sum(A)+2 max(r,{w_i:i outside A}),

along with the SOS floor2 max(r,w_i). Splitting the complement into singleton
groups attains that lower bound for the chosen A. The receipt separately
finds an emitted attaining plan. Across the32 bases the normalized floor is
3608 and the ordinary floor1664; the displayed386 source attains the latter.
This is exhaustive for this finite source/grouping architecture only.
