## 3. Paid center recovery, induction and positive extension

For each relator sign compute Q_sigma=hcenter*S_sigma once and use it in both differences Z_sigma,j-Q_sigma. Once geometry and selectors are typed, hcenter=lambda*D<B and selector digits are 0/1. Hence Q_sigma has exactly the constant center in each selected cell, with no carries. The algebraic identity K*Q_sigma=lambda*((B-1)S_sigma+S_sigma) does not make division by K free; the actual product is paid.

Every action word is a fixed linear combination of history, selected-form and selected-center words. Its formal base-B coefficients may be signed in the unrecovered suffix. At the lowest cell the recurrences BV_i=H_i+Pscale*F_i-z_i recover H_i(0)=z_i, since S0<D<B. If a recovered state has mass a<D, the true affine coefficient satisfies

    0<lambda*D-nminus*a
      <=ell_j h_(4,5,6,7)+lambda*D
      <=lambda*D+pplus*a <=(lambda+pplus)D<B.             (6)

Thus at cell zero there is neither a form carry nor a borrow. Selection, shared-center subtraction and (2) give the actual positive action, whose next total is Cmass*a<B.

Inductively subtract the known lower recurrence coefficients. The preceding actual positive action has total at most Cmass*(D-1)<B, so it uniquely recovers the next history digits without wrap. Earlier state masses were below D; hence the aggregate has no incoming carry. Its current digit equals the recovered mass, and the aggregate AND lane forces that mass below D. Only afterward use (6), together with the already carry-free lower form coefficients, to identify the current affine digits. Uncentering then gives the true action at this cell. Higher unrecovered coefficients are multiples of higher powers of B and cannot affect this modular argument. The top recurrence coefficient supplies the genuine positive terminal. F7=F1 gives the same acceptance condition, and the initial unequal coordinates 3 and1 exclude an accepting empty word.

For positive completion, fix an accepted finite trajectory and let a_max bound every state mass. For each pair let p_j bound its row's positive coefficients, and put G=max(1,all(p_1+p_2)). Choose dyadic D>(Cg+G)*a_max. This fixed-rule multiple controls an arbitrary finite trajectory without adding a duration certificate. At a paired cell the retained selected total is at most its mass a. At a relator cell it is at most G*a+2lambda*D. In both cases the slack digit obeys

    Cg*(D-a)-zsel >= D-(Cg+G)*a>0.                       (7)

Packing these digits gives the exact positive global slack in (4). The intended form digits satisfy (6), the correct native lanes hold, absent selections have hat1, and the native converse supplies fresh positive auxiliaries. All histories and terminals are positive. The proof therefore establishes the same nonempty accepted-word/ordinary-input projection, not an all-ring polynomial identity or a reuse map for old positive witnesses.
