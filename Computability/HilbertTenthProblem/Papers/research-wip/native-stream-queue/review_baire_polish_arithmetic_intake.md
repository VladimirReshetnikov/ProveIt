# Six Baire/Polish arithmetic archives: scoped research triage

This is a provenance and research-routing review of six archives introduced by
commit `fa0a0576e5cd7ff8a2978d6ad0aefd4d24511808`. It is **not full manuscript
approval**. The inspected material supplies no new emitted universal polynomial,
complete arithmetic schedule, or paid operation improvement. The current
84-operation universal polynomial and the separate arithmetic frontier remain
unchanged. This finding does not exclude future computational uses of these
structures or claim that every unread section has been reviewed.

The [receipt](review_baire_polish_arithmetic_intake.json) authenticates the exact
arrival bytes independently of later publication moves. It records all six
Git blob IDs, archive sizes and SHA256 values, all 49 ZIP member names/sizes/CRC32
values/SHA256 values, and the exact manuscript read spans and their hashes.
Every historical Git blob ID was recomputed from its bytes; all six archive
SHA256 pins and ZIP CRC checks passed. No archive code or build command ran.

All six complete READMEs were read. For each LaTeX manuscript, the complete
heading list and an explicitly recorded keyword scan were inspected; only the
selected spans below were read as continuous text. PDF bytes and programs were
hashed but not read or executed. Package-reported test counts, external
literature, historical priority, and proof-assistant claims were not independently
verified. This batch is distinct from the earlier incoming-archive triages.

The first two packages claim closely related Baire/ccc rigidity theorems for
signed additive-group topologies on discretely ordered rings, without regularity
of multiplication. One emphasizes disjoint open translates, the other a
principal-hull quotient embedding. Their boundaries explicitly distinguish
positive monoids from signed topological groups. These are structural
obstructions for uncountable presentations, not a compiler for finite ordinary
inputs. The archives have distinct bytes and proofs; this review does not
identify them as duplicates or certify the general topological theorems.

The ATR₀ package is the most direct Diophantine lead. Its exact internal-MRDP
input at lines 352–385 is an established existence theorem for polynomial
representations of fixed arithmetic formulas, with correctness proved inside a
specified weak arithmetic. The automatic-Borel argument at lines 424–563 then
projects polynomial equality over the Polish model's carrier, using totality
and uniqueness of the output. It explicitly does **not** select Diophantine
witnesses. The distinction between standard-natural correctness and internal
correctness in nonstandard models is useful for formalization, but no new
coefficient array, variable count, or operation ledger is supplied by this
argument. Its claimed affirmative ATR₀ answer depends on the stated coded
model and descriptive-set-theoretic inputs. This triage read that convention
and the reverse-mathematical audit, but did not validate the full fusion,
cofinality, or coding proof. It therefore does not independently certify the
claimed resolution of Glazer's question.

The box-game package gives two concrete computational statements in the read
sections. Legal finite binary decision trees correspond to recursively
sliceable matchings; arbitrary blind output tables need not be executable.
For oracle programs promised total and legal on every finite-alphabet stream,
compactness supplies a finite common horizon, and a partial search finds a
finite table and a losing team prefix. The totality promise is essential.
Lines 805–819 explicitly rule out a computable horizon bound from program
length and make the sharper Busy Beaver comparison conditional on an encoding
and additive-overhead compiler. These are useful distinctions between finite
semantic tables and executable programs. They do not give a fixed-size
Diophantine history certificate or a paid arithmetic schedule. The full
probability, matching enumeration, and asymptotic claims were not reviewed.

The general Polish-arithmetic package studies inherited positive cones,
local compactness, multiplicative expansion obstructions, and the boundary
between monoid and group topologies. Its introduction explicitly retains that
boundary and attributes the earlier affirmative Presburger construction to
prior work. It supplies no candidate computational representation in the read
scope; the universal carrier/classification terminology is not machine
universality. Its classification and cardinality proofs were not reviewed.

The Presburger-frontiers package has a genuine algorithmic refinement, but a
different cost model. On rational streams, the read Section 10 bounds truth
revisions by the rank of the affine order/equality tests and gives a finite
sign-poset computation of the exact optimum for a parameter-free formula.
Each stage may inspect an input-dependent finite prefix. The number of
revisions is not a bound on time, number of stream coordinates, or arithmetic
operations, and the manuscript explicitly provides no known final stopping
stage. With a fixed arbitrary computable stream parameter, determining the
optimum can encode halting (lines 2195–2267). This is not a finite ordinary-input
halting loader: the parameter is an infinite stream, and computing its optimum
is exactly the problem declared noncomputable. The continuity procedure's
output is an expanded presentation language rather than a new definition in
Presburger arithmetic (lines 2456–2484). No complete continuity compiler or
infinitary theorem is certified by its finite diagnostics, as the package
itself states at lines 2708–2723.

The useful follow-ups are therefore formalization of the internal-MRDP/Borel
interface, executable finite-table reflection under its promise, and exact
stream revision analysis. Promoting any of them to the present arithmetic
complexity search would still require a finite ordinary-input representation,
a proved finite existential certificate, and a complete paid source. None was
found in the inspected material. Repository files and all existing frozen
artifacts were left unchanged.
