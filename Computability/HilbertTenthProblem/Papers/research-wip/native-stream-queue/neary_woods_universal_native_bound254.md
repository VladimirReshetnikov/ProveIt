## 1. An existing expression is a sufficient native bound

Write q for the joint padded native scale, A and B for its padded input
ports, and Z for its padded output port. Here B is the native input port,
not either of the two outer radices. The already paid factored source has

    S=A+1+(q+1)(B+(q-1)Z),
    r=(q-1)S.                                        (1)

Their registers are `factored_pack_inner` and `and__bs_packed`.
This is the exact integer factorization of the four truth-field packing;
it uses no equation or bit interpretation.

The parent's joint positive-scale projection computes

    X=q(r+beta_old).

Keep both of its gates but replace the addition's first operand by S:

    X=q(S+beta), beta>0.                              (2)

No new register, multiplication, subtraction, comparison or witness is
introduced. In particular S is computed once by the existing packing
graph and retains its existing consumer in(1).

The initial254 pretyping proof is unchanged until the native X bound is
needed. Its paid loader, input/duration floor, signed mask unit, positive
history scale and signed history repunit give positive computed truth
fields with checksum one. In particular, before dyadic typing,

    q>=16, 0<A<q, 0<B<q, Z>=8,
    q^3+q^2+q+1<=r<q^4.                              (3)

These conclusions concern the outer ports and do not use the joint
bound witness. Identity(1), q>1 and r>0 give S>0. Consequently

    qS-r=S>0, X=q(S+beta)>r.                          (4)

Thus the original strict native bound is preserved. No weaker raw
population theorem, circular AND assumption, or uncharged inequality is
being substituted for it. Also

    r-S=(q-2)S>0.                                    (5)

## 3. Exact graph identity and positive maps

The only changed supplied coordinate is the private joint bound witness.
At fixed outer coordinates define

    beta_old=beta+S-r,
    beta=beta_old+r-S.                               (7)

Both r and S are polynomial expressions independent of either bound
witness. Therefore(7) consists of inverse triangular polynomial maps,
not affine maps in all supplied coordinates. The source guard checks
this independence through the entire dependency cone.

On arbitrary integer assignments, (7) makes

    r+beta_old=S+beta.

Hence every retained register, native factor, group product, comparison
and complete final polynomial has exactly its parent value. This identity
holds for signed inputs and signed substituted fixed numerals; it is not
an equality under the identity map on supplied tuples.

At a positive new zero on a valid slice, Sections1–2 prove(6). The restored
old coordinate is

    beta_old=X/q-r>0,

because q<r and 2^(2r+1)>r^2>qr. It is an integer by q|X. Thus(7) gives
a positive parent zero, after which the unchanged complete initial254
theorem supplies the accepted-input conclusion.

Conversely, at any positive parent zero on a valid slice, (3) and (5)
hold. Its new coordinate beta=beta_old+r-S is strictly positive. The graph
identity proves that every new group and comparison holds. The two maps
are inverse and preserve every other supplied coordinate. Completeness
therefore transfers every valid parent zero; it does not require choosing
new padding or rebuilding private Pell coordinates.

The source stores the complete old packet under `native_port_bound_parent`.
An older coordinate-restoration helper must be called on that packet after
applying(7), since its bound witness has the old meaning. No historical
parent source, interface or receipt is modified.

## 4. Guards, literal degrees and supported schedules

The rewrite accepts a complete guarded initial254 packet whose joint
native core already uses the positive-scale projection. It reconstructs

provenance added by the reviewed initial-bound partition compiler. It then
checks all rows in(1), the two X rows, the sole private bound-witness

All source gates must reach the complete output.

There are eight supported native bases: each of the four ordinary versus
