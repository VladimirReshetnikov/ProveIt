**R2 — an overgeneral reference to the root-closure corollary.** Article 6240–6246 cites `pma:spc:cor:roots` for the coanalytic-complete full-field code set over every dense exponent group. That corollary, 40135–40143, assumes a positive element together with all its dyadic divisions. Density does not imply this: `Gamma=Z+sqrt(2)Z` is dense, but if a nonzero `u=a+b sqrt(2)` had `u/2^n` in the group for every n, uniqueness of its two integer coordinates would force both a and b divisible by every power of 2, hence zero. The conclusion about every countable dense group is nevertheless correct by `pma:spc:thm:groups`. Changing the citation supplies the appropriate hypothesis boundary; no classification theorem is refuted.

The stated halting-array observations correctly distinguish recognizing arbitrary raw validity from manipulating a supplied legal name. The selected Part XXI proof derives coefficient analyticity for Borel one-parameter subgroups, then joint regularity for connected finite-dimensional Lie groups using local products of those subgroups and the product-analytic support lemma. The latter uses countable coefficient supports, Baire category and analyticity to obtain a uniform finite support below each cutoff; it does not assume a finite-dimensional entire exponent window. The argument passes relative to its stated classical Lie theory and inherited rank-one results. Those external results were not independently reread here.
