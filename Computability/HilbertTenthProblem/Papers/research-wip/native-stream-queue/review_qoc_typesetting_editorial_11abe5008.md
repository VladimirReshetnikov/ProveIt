## Finding

**P3 — qualify the real-zero claim in README lines 43–46.** The summary says that source 17's certificates have strong gates “so for natural parameters every zero over the real nonnegative orthant is natural.” This is true of its source, generic-trace and minimum-outcome certificates, but not of its all-duration padding variant. The source itself gives the counterexample: at the valid `(L,R)=(6,0), h=328` source-plus-peak witness, set the natural duration input `N=389` and padding witness `z=1/2`; the duration row vanishes although there is no natural accepting word at 389. The full article states this at lines 5274–5289, and the original reset review confirms it. Thus the blanket summary needs “apart from the all-duration padding variant,” or should explicitly name only the source/trace/minimum-outcome certificates. Article line 185 contains a similarly broad first sentence but immediately states the padding exception; the README postpones it to lines 488 and 558. This is a summary-scope defect, not a defect in the theorem or its proof.
