# The unchanged84 source admits a compiler at every odd prime at least five

Fix an odd prime ell>=5 **before** compiling a program. For every c.e. set of positive integers, the actual native window recipe can be modified to choose its fixed radix dimensions as powers of ell rather than powers of five. The resulting newly compiled numerals give the same ordinary-input language in the unchanged [84-operation polynomial](complete84_scaled_strong_output.md), with its 18 positive witnesses and exact degree187. No operation, condition or supplied coordinate is removed.

After the harmless parity normalization, every accepted input has genuine histories realizing, for each fixed finite u>=1 and adequate spatial ell-power h, with E=dh,

    3^u E | R,
    gcd(Delta,2^(2*3^u E)-1)=3,
    gcd(Delta,(2^(2*3^u E)-1)/3^(u+1))=1,
    v3(Delta)=1 and v3(m)<=1.

Here R is the actual packed main index, a is its native half-binomial parameter, Delta=(a+1)(a+3), and m is the independent-gamma alias modulus. This extends the available finite prime classes to orders dividing 2*3^u*ell^j for a newly compiled recipe. For example ell=7 covers primes127 and43, whose exact orders of2 are7 and14. It does not control every prime or bound m. The independent-gamma83 ordinary-input language remains unresolved.

The new prime changes program constants. It is not an additional witness and cannot vary with the ordinary input in one fixed representation. No claim is made that an old numeric compiler instance supports a different prime. The [helper](complete84_odd_prime_compiler_transfer.py) implements bounded fresh sparse instances of the new recipe and checks the unchanged source as inert data; it does not run a predecessor compiler or materialize a universal program's huge constants.

## 1. Where the old choice of five enters

The dependency audit distinguishes geometric, arithmetic and executable interfaces.

* The [81 helical completeness proof](../../1980/FIXED_RAW_UNIVERSAL_81_PROOF.md), Section2, permits every sufficiently large spatial width and independently every sufficiently large time height. For a finite halting run it chooses a freely large left padding a, width a+input_run+4, and a freely large time height; its seam formula is unchanged. Thus sufficiently large ell-powers are legitimate widths and heights. No prime enters this theorem.
* The [76 compiler](../../verification/explore_fixed_raw_universal_76.py), lines94–148, uses powers of five in its rounding routines and in one modular test selecting an unchecked high monomial. Its native positions, coefficients, bands, anchor differences, window API and support inequalities do not depend on five. These two executable choices must be replaced; calling its frozen API unchanged would still compile at five.
* The [modified75 compiler proof](complete75_half_binomial_compiler.md), Sections1–5, uses odd radix dimensions, fixed size/separation bounds, two ordinary bits at an ignored digit and the literal shifted masks. Its Section6 is the old five-adic alignment argument, replaced below.
* The [half-binomial positive converse](pell_kernel_half_binomial42.md#6-exact-valuation-and-the-positive-converse) requires powers of two, the actual index/population bounds and R=3 modulo4. The [full75 composition](../../1980/FIXED_RAW_UNIVERSAL_75_PROOF.md#6-full-positive-completeness-composition) additionally uses the exact temporal congruence and odd input exponent. Neither requires five once those hypotheses have been supplied.
* The normalized strong, positive coordinate and auxiliary quotient transformations leading to84 are arithmetic statements on those positive zeros. The current84 proof and its pinned parents retain these same compiler conditions. They do not charge or execute the witness-construction algorithms.

The statements below are a new parametric fixed-numeral recipe, not a mutation of any of those frozen artifacts.

## 2. New fixed constants and the actual high correction

Start with the same fixed machine for the doubled input language, the same ordered allowed windows, Start selector0 and End selector1. Preserve the complete native layout, all center clauses and copies, four anchors and at least one ignored dummy. If the finite-prime conclusion is wanted, first apply [the proved window padding](gamma_parity_padding_scout.md): the selector count is even and the tile-alphabet size is odd, without changing the Start-containing language or the distance2x to End. Its exact marked-language argument is independent of numeric radices.

Use r0 for the inner radix to avoid confusing it with the packed index R. Let K be the number of native positions, Emax their largest exponent, mu the clause mask, and c_e the center coefficients. Retain the literal quantities Hfield,T1,T2 and

    g_high=T2+Emax+1.

Choose L to be an ell-power strictly exceeding g_high+Emax. Choose b to be an ell-power large enough that

    r0=2^b >= max(4(K+2)(2 sum_e c_e+6)+8, 2mu+4, 32).

Put d=bL and B=2^d. Thus b,L,d are odd ell-powers. The extra lower bound32 is harmless fixed padding. All choices are determined by the finite program and ell, independently of x.

Build DC without the optional high monomial and keep DR=r0^Hfield. Since ell is prime and ell-powers are1 modulo ell-1,

    r0=B=q=B^h=2 modulo ell

for every canonical ell-power h,N with q=B^N. Therefore the required bracket is

    1+q(DC+B DR+B^h)=5+2DC+4DR modulo ell.             (1)

This is not the old expression 2DC-DR, which agrees only at ell=5. If (1) is nonzero add nothing; if it is zero add r0^g_high to DC. Its increment in (1) is 2*r0^g_high, a nonzero residue. This choice chi in {0,1} is fixed once for the compiler.

The high monomial still lies above every tested field and below L after multiplication by any permitted native basis position. It adds at most K+2 to the raw mass already allowed by the displayed bound. All geometry remains separated. The modified masks are exactly

    MC=MC_old-2*r0^e_star,
    MF_native=MF_old+4,
    MF_source=MF_native+B-1,

where e_star is the first ignored dummy. The bit at e_star has digits0 through3; all meaningful positions remain Boolean. The same sparse support proof gives

    0<MC,MF_native<B-1,
    MC=2 mod4, MF_native=4 mod8,
    popcount(MC)+popcount(MF_native)=d.

The source's six fixed ports are Bm1=B-1, Kconstant=DC+B DR, twice_cell_bits=2d, inner_bits=b, MC as above, and MF=MF_source. In particular its shifted MF port must not be confused with the smaller native mask.

    2C+F <= (r0/4+7)(q-1)/(r0-1) < q/2.

## 4. General prime Boolean subset lemma

Fix ell>=5 and define the fixed integers

    S=lcm(2,ord_ell(2)),
    lambda=v_ell(2^S-1), F0=ell^lambda,
    w=1+v3(S).

Because ord_ell(2) divides ell-1, S divides ell-1, so 2<=S<=ell-1<F0 and S is even. Let d,N be ell-powers, with

    N>3^w*ell^(2lambda)*d,
    M=dN, T=N/F0, B=2^d.

Every pair of residues modulo M and3^w is realized by a Boolean subset of the physical weights

    {B^(Sj): 0<=j<T} union {B}.                         (2)

LTE gives v_ell(B^S-1)=v_ell(d)+lambda, and B^S has exact order T modulo M. Its T powers therefore enumerate precisely the T residues 1+F0*d*t, t modulo T. Also v3(B^S-1)=w, since d is prime to3, so every one of these powers is1 modulo3^w.

For a target z modulo M and v modulo3^w, choose epsilon in {0,1} so z-epsilon B is an ell-unit. The two possibilities differ by a unit, so this is possible. Choose k by CRT with

    k=z-epsilon B mod(F0*d),
    k=v-epsilon B mod3^w,
    0<k<3^w*F0*d<T.

The positive representative exists because its ell-residue is nonzero; it satisfies gcd(k,T)=1. Write sigma=(z-epsilon B-k)/(F0*d) modulo T and solve

    k*t0+k(k-1)/2=sigma modulo T.

Choose the k distinct cyclic t-values t0 through t0+k-1. Their unique indices j in the displayed subgroup enumeration specify k distinct cells Sj. Add the optional cell1 if epsilon=1; it is distinct because S>=2. This subset has exactly both requested residues. The argument is Boolean, not an unrestricted signed linear combination.

Every lower slot is at most S(N/F0-1). Choosing Htime>F0 makes its shifts by1 and h internal, because S<=F0-1. The original negative shift of the packed index therefore applies simultaneously to this whole subset.

## 5. Actual coefficient and simultaneous baseline alignment

At the actual canonical word let

    Gamma=r0^e_star*(q^2-1)*(1+q(DC+B DR+B^h)).

Since q=2 modulo ell, q^2-1=3 modulo ell is a unit. Equation(1) makes the other bracket a unit. Thus Gamma is a unit modulo dN, as required to align the actual index. This is exactly the step for which the old compiler's optional high monomial has been replaced.

The ternary valuation is also exact. All b,L,d,h,N are odd, so r0,B,q,B^h=-1 modulo3. The unchanged layout has odd anchor unit, even g_high and paired center bands of opposite parity. Hence the actual identity

    DC-DR=chi modulo3

still holds for the newly chosen correction. It gives bracket 2-chi modulo3, nonzero for either chi. Since 3 does not divide dN,

    v3(Gamma)=1.                                        (3)

The actual mask parity table is likewise unchanged: for even selector count and odd tile alphabet, MC=0 modulo3 and MF_native=2 modulo3. On canonical odd N, the already shifted source MF then gives R=0 modulo3 for every baseline word. The optional high correction does not enter the masks.

After presetting arbitrary upper bits, leave lower bits zero and call the actual packed index R0. A Boolean lower subset with sum K changes R to R0-Gamma K. Set

    K=(R0-dh)*Gamma^(-1) modulo dN,
    K=(R0/3)*(Gamma/3)^(-1) modulo3^w.

Both inverses exist in their stated rings. Section4 realizes these residues in the actual slots. The resulting baseline satisfies

    Rbase=dh modulo dN,
    Rbase=0 modulo3^(w+1).                              (4)

This uses the parity-normalized recipe; lower bits cannot change a baseline's residue modulo3. The padding theorem supplies this recipe for every c.e. language but does not silently alter an already fixed coefficient instance.

## 6. Arbitrary finite ternary power and native prime filter

Fix an accepted input, adequate ell-power spatial h and finite u>=1. Put

    E=dh, n=3^u E,
    Aminus=2^n-1,
    j=max(u-w-1,0), Q=3^j*Aminus,
    spacing=2*3^u*h.

These quantities are fixed before increasing Htime. Choose its ell-power sufficiently large for the original run, N>2x, the Boolean size condition, and

    Htime>F0,
    Htime>F0*((spacing/h)*(Q-2)+1).                     (5)

Use swap distance Lswap=S*N/F0 and preset upper bits at i_t=spacing*t, 0<=t<=Q-2, with destination upper bits initially zero. With i_max=spacing*(Q-2), (5) yields

    i_max+h<N/F0<=min(Lswap,N-Lswap).

Thus the source and destination sets are disjoint, and every field translation by1 or h is nonwrapping. This checks disjointness even when Lswap<N/2. The lower bits remain independently usable at the same cells. Apply Section5 after this upper pattern is preset.

Let G=Gamma*(B^Lswap-1). LTE at ell and3 gives

    v_ell(B^Lswap-1)=v_ell(dN),
    v3(B^Lswap-1)=w,
    v3(G)=w+1.                                        (6)

Moving the first k upper bits preserves the temporal congruence and gives

    R_k=Rbase-2G*sum_(t<k) B^(spacing*t).               (7)

The spacing has v3(B^spacing-1)=u+1. When j>0, choose

    k=(Rbase/3^(w+1))*(2G/3^(w+1))^(-1) modulo3^j.

The geometric sum is k modulo3^j, and (7) then gives 3^u|R_k. If j=0, this divisibility already follows from (4), so no ternary condition is imposed.

For each prime p dividing Aminus, p is odd and different from3, and B^spacing=2^(2n)=1 modulo p. Put s_p=v_p(G) after the actual time and baseline have been chosen. Equation(7) gives

    (R_k-1)/2=(Rbase-1)/2-G*k modulo p^(s_p+1).

Here only the geometric sum modulo p is used; the multiplier G supplies the higher modulus. A single residue k modulo p sets the s_p-th base-p digit to p-1, forcing a central-binomial carry. The CRT combines these conditions with the possible ternary one; its modulus is 3^j*rad(Aminus)<=Q. Its representative 0<=k<Q fits the predetermined grid. No bound on the later valuations s_p is needed, and no CRT modulus depends circularly on the chosen time.

The final actual index is divisible by3^u and E; they are coprime, and R/n is odd. The exact native formula is

    X=2^R, r=(R-1)/2,
    2Y=sum_(i=0)^r binom(2r,r+i)*X^i,
    a=Y(X+1).

At the plus factor, 2^n+1 divides a, giving Delta=3 modulo that factor and exact gcd3. Its ternary valuation is u+1, so a is divisible by9 and v3(Delta)=1. At each p dividing the minus factor, X=1 and the imposed carry makes binom(2r,r)=0 modulo p. Symmetry of the full binomial row gives a=1/4 and Delta=65/16 modulo p. Neither5 nor13 divides 2^n-1, because their orders4 and12 cannot divide odd n. Thus every minus-factor residue is nonzero.

The plus/minus factors are coprime. This proves the headline gcd3 and the coprime quotient after dividing by the **entire** factor3^(u+1). Section3 supplies fresh positive84 witnesses at this genuine modified history; all prior semantic and positive conditions survive. Since m divides2Delta, the same odd quotient is coprime to m and v3(m)<=1.

For a finite set of primes whose orders divide some 2*3^u*ell^j, fix a large enough u and legitimate spatial h first, then choose time as above. This remains one finite history for each finite request. The choice of ell belongs to the fixed compiler, not to these later witnesses. The argument does not combine different fixed-ell representations into one instance, infer simultaneous avoidance of infinitely many primes, or control the remaining intersection with ord_H(2). In particular even-order classes with a factor4, including order8 at17, remain outside this divisibility construction.
