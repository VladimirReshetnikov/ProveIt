## Finite grammar and exact all-value proof

For each of `r,j,h,zquot`, choose a shift in `{-1,0,1}`. The supplied child port denotes the old port plus that shift. A nonzero shift is ordinarily restored by one paid addition or subtraction, after which every old consumer uses that restored value. Two concrete cancellations are included.

For shift `j_new=j_old+1`, put

```
Hpre = j_new*c-r_old
H17  = Hpre-c.
```

Keep this restored `H17` in its norm. Replace the linear comparison

```

```

by `Hpre=of`, and delete the private `of-c` subtraction. The two exact identities are

\[
 (j_{new}-1)c-r_{old}=j_{new}c-r_{old}-c,
\]
\[
 ((j_{new}-1)c-r_{old})-(of-c)
   =(j_{new}c-r_{old})-of.
\]

Moving the subtraction from the linear comparison into the norm again ties74. Omitting the norm restoration would be a different polynomial and is not a valid reduction. ## Positive-zero restoration for the twelve minimum schedules

The twelve minima have `sr,sj` in `{0,1}` and `sh=sz=0`. These four choices in each mode preserve the full positive zero set under the coordinate translation. Forward positivity follows from adding0 or1. Reverse positivity is proved before invoking the parent theorem.

The authenticated compiler restrictions include `0<MC,MF<B-1`, `MC>=2`, and `MF>=4`. They imply, without assuming q is a power or any history is typed,

\[
 T_C=MC\,J+1,\quad T_F=MF\,J-1,\quad
 0<T'=T_C+qT_F<q^2-1.
\]

The literal packing right side has remainder T′ modulo `q²−1`. Thus the packing equation cannot have `r_old=0`. Consequently `r_old>0`. For `sr=0` positivity is supplied directly.

For `sj=1`, the restored `j_old=j_new-1` is initially only nonnegative. All three sources still give `c=kY+eta>1` at a zero, either by retained comparisons or by their already computed definitions. Let the mathematical abbreviation `A0=a+2`; the source register called `A` is its discriminant `Delta=A0²−1`. The main equation and positivity give

\[

## Why fixed-mask renaming alone cannot remove the unit shift

On the repunit slice `q=(B-1)J+1`, both `q²−1` and the literal mask contribution `(MC+q*MF_source)J` are divisible by J. The complete packed index is therefore divisible by J. Replacing only fixed MC/MF numerals changes it by `(delta_MC+q*delta_MF)J`, still divisible by J. For `J>1`, this cannot add1 or subtract1. Equivalently, on the polynomial repunit slice `J=0,q=1`, every such packed expression vanishes, so no identity differing by the constant1 exists. This is a narrow mask-only obstruction; it is not a statement about changing the history protocol or adding new arithmetic.

Similarly, h and j unit shifts alter `hE` and `jc` by E and c, respectively, not by1. Neither may be used as the unit r shift without paying and proving its effects on all the other consumers. The actual counted constructions above do pay those effects.
