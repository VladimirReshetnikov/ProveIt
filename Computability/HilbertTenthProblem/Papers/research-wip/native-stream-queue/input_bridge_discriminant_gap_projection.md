# The current input bridge without its Pell-index gap

This research note removes exactly `c=kappa+phi` from the established
[76-operation certificate](../../1980/FIXED_RAW_UNIVERSAL_76_PROOF.md).
The resulting source has **75=41M+34A**, 29 strictly positive existential
coordinates and 18 equations. Its full universal soundness is **open**.
It is different from the separate 75-operation auxiliary-scale candidate.
No established complete bound changes.

The result here is an exact projection theorem for the remaining input
bridge, including a parity branch absent from the older
[a+1-modulus analysis](../../1980/EXPLORATION_INPUT_PELL_GAP_PROJECTION.md).
It gives the precise arithmetic condition for transferring a genuine
76 witness to a different ordinary input. It also gives a rigorous bridge
counterexample obeying strong size bounds; its parameter is not asserted
to be a parameter of a complete compiled kernel.

## Exact two-coset projection

Put

    A=a+2, D=A^2-1, H=4a+3,
    u=2d*x+b,

where a is even, b is odd and u is positive. In the actual 76 source,
a is even because a=Y(X+1) and Y is divisible by q^3. After deleting the
gap, the remaining bridge is

    kappa=u+delta*D,
    mu^2=1+D*kappa^2,
    mu=W+a*kappa+rho*H,                                  (1)

with kappa, delta, mu and rho strictly positive. Write T for the
multiplicative order of 2 modulo H and G for the subgroup of units modulo
H generated by 2^(2D). Neither order nor subgroup is a free certificate
operation: they describe the exact existential projection.

For every positive odd u and every positive W, system (1) is solvable
if and only if

    W modulo H belongs to 2^u G union 2^(A*u) G.          (2)

To prove necessity, positive Pell solutions have

    kappa=psi_A(v), mu=chi_A(v), v>0.

The recurrence or binomial formula gives

    psi_A(v) = v*A^(v-1) modulo D,
    chi_A(v)-a*psi_A(v) = 2^v modulo H.                  (3)

Since A is even and D is odd, the first congruence in (1) is equivalent to
exactly one of

    v = u modulo 2D       (v odd),
    v = A*u modulo 2D     (v even).                      (4)

Here A^2=1 modulo D, so A is its own inverse modulo D. Oddness of D
lifts the first residue to the unique odd class modulo 2D and the second
to the unique even class. Equations (3)--(4) prove (2).

Conversely, (2) supplies an index class in (4) with 2^v=W modulo H.
Adding arbitrary positive multiples of `lcm(2D,T)` preserves its parity
and both congruences. Choose v large enough that

    psi_A(v)>max(u,W).

Then set

    kappa=psi_A(v), mu=chi_A(v),
    delta=(kappa-u)/D,
    rho=(mu-a*kappa-W)/H.

All are positive integers: the congruences give integrality, and

    mu-a*kappa=2*psi_A(v)-psi_A(v-1)>psi_A(v)>W

gives rho>0. This proves positive sufficiency without any finite-search
assumption.

When W=2^w, put `g=gcd(2D,T)`. The criterion becomes

    g divides w-u OR g divides w-A*u.                    (5)

This follows by solving a pair of index congruences modulo 2D and T.

## Exact fibers above a genuine complete 76 witness

Fix any genuine 76 witness at an accepted raw input y. Retain all its
outer words, compiler numerals and actual kernel coordinates, so

    W=2^(b+2d*y), c=psi_A(2r+1).

For another positive raw input x, change only the four input Pell
coordinates and set

    alpha_x=q-C-2d*x.

In this actual kernel, X=2^(2r+1), so X+1 is divisible by 3. Consequently
a=Y(X+1) is divisible by 3, H is divisible by 3, and T is even. Thus g is
even. The exponent w=b+2d*y and u=b+2d*x are odd, whereas A*u is even.
The second branch of (5) is therefore impossible. The retained complete
source above these fixed outer/kernel coordinates has positive witnesses
at x **if and only if**

    2d*x<q-C and g divides 2d*(y-x).                      (6)

Equivalently, with `e=g/gcd(g,2d)`, it requires

    x=y modulo e and 2d*x<q-C.                            (7)

The proof is both directions of (2), together with positivity of alpha_x.
Every other retained equation is unchanged. Formula (6) is a precise
remaining task, not a claimed small bound on g. Producing a false input
requires proving (6) at an **actual** compiled kernel and choosing x
outside that fixed machine's accepted set. No such assertion is made here.

Even at the same accepted x, infinitely many lifted indices

    v=u+ell*lcm(2D,T)>2r+1

satisfy all retained equations and have kappa>c. Thus the deleted gap is
not implied by the other full equations. This nonredundancy statement
keeps the accepted input and is not an existential-language refutation.

## A rigorous strong-bound bridge alias

A counterexample to a bridge-only proof based on the following size bounds
can be specified without materializing its enormous Pell tuple. Let

    p=133*2^600+1,
    a=3*133*2^598,
    A=a+2, D=A^2-1, H=4a+3=3p.

The checker proves the Proth certificate

    3^((p-1)/2) = -1 modulo p.

This also gives an elementary primality proof: for every prime ell dividing
p, the displayed half-power forces the order of 3 modulo ell to have
2-adic valuation exactly 600. Thus ell=1 modulo 2^600 and
ell>=2^600+1>sqrt(p), since 133<2^600. A composite p would have a prime
divisor at most sqrt(p), a contradiction. Put N=p-1. Fermat's theorem and reduction modulo 3 give 2^N=1 modulo H.
Moreover D is odd and `gcd(D,133)=1` because a is divisible by 133 and
D=a^2+4a+3 is 3 modulo 133. Hence

    gcd(2D,N)=2.

The exact order T divides N and is even because 3 divides H. Therefore
`gcd(2D,T)=2`, without factoring N or computing T.

Take

    q=16, J0=539, u=5, w=3, W=8.

Then

    0<u<q<J0<a, 2^J0<a, 0<W<q<H, W!=2^u.

Set

    t = -D^(-1) modulo (N/2), 0<=t<N/2,
    v = u+2D*t.

The exact modular checks give v>J0, v=u modulo 2D and v=3 modulo N.
Consequently `2^v=W modulo H`. The sufficiency proof supplies positive
Pell witnesses for (1) with this wrong endpoint. For d=b=1, these are
input exponent 5 (raw x=2) and endpoint exponent 3 (raw y=1).

This parameter matches the displayed bridge/kernel size inequalities,
including exponential growth, and a is even and divisible by 3. It has
**not** been shown to equal the special kernel value
`floor((X+1)^(2r)/X^r)*(X+1)` at X=2^(2r+1), and no compiler words or
remaining kernel equations are supplied. It therefore refutes only that
bounds-based bridge argument, not the complete gap deletion.
