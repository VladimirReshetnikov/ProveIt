## Literal machine and external universality boundary

Report40 fixes the ordinary binary RL ant, initial state `(288650,75,E)`, a periodic background of periods `(576000,481238074400)`, and finite nearest-head-first tape words. Its loader changes exactly `2812+4(popcount(left)+popcount(right))` cells. The accepting predicate is a pre-departure residue/heading event; the ant does not physically halt. The read finite-history/resource-ownership induction addresses growing layers, first-before-second crossing use, and at most two visits to each cell even after simulated halting. The actual finite template traces and every atlas entry were not independently regenerated here.

The qualitative boundary agrees with [Maldonado et al., Theorems2.1/3.1 and §5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.05547): a periodic infinite hardware background plus a finite input perturbation supports universal simulation and their construction uses at most two visits. That is not universality from an otherwise blank finite-support board, and the separate time-bounded P-completeness theorem does not imply undecidable bounded-time prediction.

The [Neary–Woods author paper](https://mural.maynoothuniversity.ie/id/eprint/12416/1/Woods_FourSmall_2009.pdf), Definition3.1/Table1, starts U15 in state `u1` over the rightmost symbol of `G=bc`, on a `c`-blank tape. Thus `c=0,b=1,u1=A` gives precisely the required A-scanning-zero configuration with finite left/right words. Table16 matches all30 Report40 table entries under `u1..u15=A..O`; its unique missing entry is `(u10,b)=J1`. Theorem2.1 and §3.5 supply the cited machine-simulation dependency. [Publisher metadata](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3233/FI-2009-0036) confirms DOI `10.3233/FI-2009-0036`, volume91(1), pp123–144; the repository warns that the PDF's front-matter pagination is wrong.

There is a small additional primary-source inconsistency: the final §3.5 sentence names `c` in the halting transition, although Table16 defines that transition and omits `b`. This intake follows the explicit table, which is the report's pinned machine. It does not claim a new independent proof of every encoded bi-tag simulation case. Neither the numerical ant atlas nor an arithmetic arbitrary-program-to-U15 compiler follows merely from reading the published universality theorem.
