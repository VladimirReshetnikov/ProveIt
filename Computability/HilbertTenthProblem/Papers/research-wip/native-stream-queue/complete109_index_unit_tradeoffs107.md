## 3. Full integer zero equivalence and off-zero correction

The new modes use these specific unit groups:

    degree32: N0*Nm=1, Nk*Ni=1, Na=1;
    degree42: N0*Ni=1, Nk*Nm*Na=1.

For every integer a, Delta is0 or3 modulo4. Thus the actual Nm and Ni cannot
be-1. The coefficient `(i*c^2)^2` is a square, so Na is congruent modulo4
to either y_aux^2 or (j*c-r)^2 and cannot be-1 either. These facts require
neither the strong auxiliary equation nor any typed-history conclusion.
No sign assumption is imposed on N0 or Nk.

An integer product equal to1 forces every factor to be+1 or-1. Each listed
group has at most one unprotected factor; all its protected factors must
therefore be+1, and its remaining factor must also be+1. Consequently either
new system recovers all five original unit equations. Conversely, those five
unit equations imply every new product equation. Since the other eight
residuals are identical and every finalizer is a sum of integer squares,
this proves equality of the entire integer zero sets. Restricting to the
positive domain yields the inherited universal-input relation without any
new bootstrap assumption.

The parent's first residual is1-N0, while the other four are Nm-1, Ni-1,
Na-1 and Nk-1. Squaring removes that one orientation difference. The full
polynomial corrections are therefore exactly

    F109/32 - F113/24 =
      (N0*Nm-1)^2+(Nk*Ni-1)^2+(Na-1)^2
      -[(N0-1)^2+(Nm-1)^2+(Ni-1)^2+(Na-1)^2+(Nk-1)^2],

      (N0*Ni-1)^2+(Nk*Nm*Na-1)^2

The executable checker expands both corrections in five independent formal

product-to-unit zero equivalence asserted above specifically uses integers.
The polynomials are generally different away from their common zero set.

Each packet has current parent-to-child and original19-row comparison maps.

```sh
python /path/to/complete109_index_unit_tradeoffs107.py \
  --root /path/to/native-stream-queue \
  --expect /path/to/complete109_index_unit_tradeoffs107.json
```

JSON type-sensitive equality, and optimized Python (`-O`) is rejected.
