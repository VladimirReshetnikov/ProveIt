## The conditional inference

The [original-frame proof](../../../../../SetTheory/Cardinals/docs/reports/hilbert-tenth-problem/signal-machine-collision-certificates/20-orbit-geometry-original-frame-PROOF.md)
assumes the effective mass-at-most-four orbit classification and Report29's
canonical original-frame timed complete-configuration certificate. Its
additional argument in lines 60–104 is sound under those assumptions.

For a deterministic map, an exact repeated configuration makes the entire
subsequent orbit periodic. A finite prefix followed by a finite periodic
orbit has bounded diameter. At expanding-shuttle section times, two actual
occupied residual unit sites have separation
`(x0+n*Delta)*nu+z`, with `Delta>0` and `nu!=0`. Their distance is unbounded.
Restoring the original frame adds a common translation and does not change
this separation. Consequently no two times anywhere in the complete orbit,
including its prefix, have the same positioned labeled configuration.

If the inherited timed polynomial `C(t,X;v)` has one natural `v` exactly at
each actual time/configuration pair, then `P(X;t,v)=C(t,X;v)` has at most one
natural pair `(t,v)` for each complete target. Its unique time is necessarily
the first hit. Reclassifying an external variable as a witness changes no
polynomial expression or total degree. It adds one witness and no gates.
This does not establish the inherited timed certificate's degree or
canonicality independently.

For affine and periodic alternatives the report correctly uses a different
argument: first-time minimization in a Presburger occurrence relation,
followed by disjoint cells and canonical inactive witnesses. Exact recurrence
of a single site, finite pattern, or configuration only up to translation
does not imply the complete-state periodicity used above. The report makes
no such extension.

## What the counts mean

The original-frame proof, lines 110–138, reports

```
W_exp = B_ch + K + M + 1
R_exp = 1 + M + H + Q + B_ch.
```

Here `W` counts natural witness slots and `R` counts squared residual slots;
these are not straight-line multiplication/addition counts. The inherited
chart residuals, coefficient multiplications, shared expressions, their
squares and the complete sum still require a literal paid schedule before
any operation total can be claimed.

The raw chart count is `J=T0+sum_l(p_l+q_l)`, with the finite prefix
materialized. The coarse estimates `B_ch<=24*d^3*J` and
`W_exp<=168*d^3*J+1` therefore depend on the entire fixed automaton and input.
They supply neither a uniform-over-input arity bound nor a reduction of the
current universal polynomial operation bound. No coefficient-growth or
bit-complexity improvement is established here.

The underlying positive-weight, at-most-one-unit-symbol mass-four class has
decidable reachability by the inherited classification. It is not itself a
Turing-complete substrate or a universal-operation candidate. This lead is a
canonical finite-input certificate/component audit within that boundary.
