## Proof and scope audit

**Complete chronology.** Both constructions use stable segment identities, atomically replace all blocks of a simultaneous batch, and sort outputs by strictly increasing velocity. A positive-duration adjacent-pair lifetime starts at equality only for a common birth, and ends at equality only for a common death. Every other endpoint is strict, including a surviving pair at the final cut. Affineness plus those typed endpoints excludes every omitted interior collision. At a batch, adjacent strict gaps separate distinct event blocks and through-signals, so all incident signals and all spatially independent simultaneous sites are included. An erased block remains in the pre-event order and therefore cannot conceal a crossing. Final-born prefix pairs need no positive-duration check: certified distinct incoming block positions and speed-sorted output fans already establish their right-limit order. The detailed boundary proof in Direct Certificates is particularly useful as a reusable statement.

**Collision Geometry.** Intercepts, event times and positions are computed homogeneous rational linear forms in the initial positive gaps. Only initial gaps and inequality slacks are Diophantine coordinates. With n initial signals, m distinct velocities and E sites, the raw condition count is at most `3(n−1)+(3m+1)E`; eliminating coordinates can make individual rows dense, so this is not a constant-width gate bound. Primitive inequality normalization uses only positive scaling; equality rows may change sign. The exact natural polynomial is `||Eg||²+||Gg−1−z||²`, with one uniquely determined slack per strict row. Empty skeleton means the zero-length prefix, not halting. The compiler supports n≥2, transparent or explicitly forbidden absent rules, and arbitrary fixed rational signed velocities.

Its further proofs are sound under their stated assumptions: causal depth bounds coefficients' denominators by D^h after integer-speed normalization; coefficient height grows exponentially in magnitude but linearly in bits with depth; Cramer's rule and a bounded vertex yield a small integer realization; nonempty homogeneous rational chambers have equivalent real/rational/integer *existence*. That equivalence does not preserve a fixed physical distance, deadline or prescribed rational seed. The synchronization rank/relative robustness result, finite first-hit disjointness, one-hot natural-selector quartic, and inclusion–exclusion/Ehrhart quasi-polynomial count all retain the required homogeneity and finite event budget. The global-nonnegative quadratic barrier is correctly restricted to a polynomial nonnegative on all real coordinates: its zero set is a rational affine subspace, so natural projection is decidable. It does not classify arbitrary quadratics.

**Direct Certificates.** Machine velocities and seed positions are externally normalized to nonnegative integers. The affine change `X=q(x+a−ct), T=(q/m)t` has new speed m(u−c), preserving order, incidence and time direction. The normalization constants are fixed outside the existential tuple; unknown denominators or scales would change degree and multiplicity. B=D^K is a compiled integer constant, not an uncharged variable; causal depth H≤K would suffice. Full-layer counts are exactly `W=(n−1)+sum_i(N_i+g_i)` and `R=(n−1)+sum_i(2N_i−1)` for n≥1, with the stated max(n−1,0) convention. Sparse counts are `W≤E+2K+2L+max(n−1,0)` and `R≤I+K+2L+max(n−1,0)`; `L≤max(n−1,0)+sum_e(q_e+1)`. The witness-rank proof genuinely works over the reals, including infeasible structurally admitted schemas, so strict witness convexity is stronger than integer uniqueness alone. Zero-witness empty/singleton cases are handled separately.

The sparse halting option checks every final adjacent relative velocity and emits terminal rays. It is distinct from a finite prefix. The four-speed bound `60^K max(1,M0)` applies to canonical zeros, and its O(K+log(M0+1)) bit length is correct. The shuttle's denominator grows as 3^(k−1) after 2k batches, without invoking a post-accumulation computation. Initial-coordinate positivity and positive-witness translation are treated separately; no free operation savings are asserted for either conversion.

**Universality boundary.** Initial labels, length, machine/rules and finite schema are compiler data. Direct Certificates exports full-layer and sparse fixed-schema polynomials. Collision Geometry also exports a finite-family union formula, but does not implement an exhaustive unknown-skeleton enumerator. The number of candidate chambers/selectors can grow rapidly with the externally fixed site budget. Neither archive transcribes the published universal rule table or its ordinary-number loader, nor compresses arbitrary chronology into fixed arity. Neither gives semantics through a Zeno accumulation. A physical-time bound is not an event bound.

The primary publication states a halting machine with **13 labels and 21 nonblank rules**, uses rational normalized placement for its halting arrangement, and distinguishes the need for exact three-signal synchronization. This supports the reports' substrate context, not a claim that their toy tables are themselves universal. [Durand-Lose 2009, Theorem 5 and §5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0906.3225).

The conservative construction's positive energy bounds the live population for a fixed input, while leaving collision count unbounded; the reports preserve that distinction. [Durand-Lose 2006, Definition 3](https://www.univ-orleans.fr/lifo/membres/Jerome.Durand-Lose/Recherche/Publications/2006_CiE.pdf).

The cited three-speed rationality restriction and four-speed accumulation example agree with the primary paper. They do not extend the finite-prefix certificates past their limits. [Becker et al. 2013](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.6468).

## Transfer A: remove endpoint equalities and recover initial-order slacks

Actual emitter: `signal-diophantine-release/scripts/signal_sparse.py:35`–43 and 59–60. Input-order rows are at 32–33; halting terminal rows at 68–75.

Write a flight row for segment s dying at event e as `F_e,s=X_e−Z_s(T_e)`. Then:

1. A same-birth lower endpoint row is the **zero polynomial**, because both birth positions and birth times are the same.
2. A same-death upper gap is exactly `Z_right(T_e)−Z_left(T_e)=F_e,left−F_e,right`. Both flight rows are retained, so this gap row can be deleted without changing any complete zero set, even over the reals.

There are precisely `sum_e(p_e−1)=I−E` same-death rows. Let Z_birth count recorded same-birth lower rows, including terminal rays. Deleting these equations reduces R by `(I−E)+Z_birth`, retains all W coordinates, keeps affine residuals and degree at most two, and preserves the full witness rank proof because all flight/time equations and every slack definition remain.

If **K>0 or halt=True**, every initial adjacent pair has a recorded lower endpoint at time zero. Its retained strict row is

```
B(p_{j+1}−p_j)−h_j−1=0,  B>=1,  h_j in N.
```

This implies a strictly positive integer gap, hence `p_{j+1}−p_j>=1`. Delete the separate initial-order row `p_{j+1}−p_j−u_j−1=0` and its private coordinate u_j, restoring it by `u_j=p_{j+1}−p_j−1`. Its naturalness follows from the retained lower row and input integrality. No other row consumes u_j. This gives a bijection between the complete natural zero sets on all retained inputs/witnesses. For a **K=0 prefix**, keep the input-order packet; no lifetime rows were emitted. For n≤1 there is nothing to erase.

With J=max(n−1,0) when K>0 or halt=True, otherwise J=0, exact counts are

```
R_new = R_old − (I−E) − Z_birth − J
W_new = W_old − J.
```

The worked two-signal annihilation becomes **6→4 residuals, 5→4 witnesses**, with the same input positions and degree two. Its remaining coordinates are T,X,d,h. This is a counted source transformation of the actual compiler output, not a proposed replacement for an arbitrary finite trace.

Off zero, the old SOS is not identical to the reduced SOS. After restoring the erased u fields, the input-order residuals vanish identically, the birth rows are identically zero, and

```
P_old(restore(v)) − P_new(v)
  = sum_same_death_pairs (F_e,left(v)−F_e,right(v))².
```

The identity holds for every signed integer assignment. At zeros, it vanishes because the retained flight rows vanish. The independent checker verifies literal row-dictionary identities and retains every flight row.

**Rational-domain caveat, complete source fixture:** use speeds r=1,s=0,q=3 (q unused), rule {r,s}→empty, initial labels r,s, one halting collision. The actual source compiler chooses D=B=6. The reduced equations have the nonnegative rational zero

```
p0=0, p1=1/2, T=3, d=2, X=3, h=2.
```

The only possible restored input slack is u=−1/2, so the original system has no nonnegative-rational extension. Thus the coordinate-erasing step is a **natural-integer** zero-bijection theorem, not a nonnegative-rational one. This fixture is produced and verified against the actual emitted rows in the independent checker. The geometric reports themselves do not claim this proposed reduction.
