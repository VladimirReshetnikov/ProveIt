# Hierarchical binary selection in the actual U9 compiler: 250 operations

The current complete U9 tag compiler has a **250 = 130M + 120A** successor,
with **43 strictly positive witnesses**, one product comparison, and
ordinary positive input `x`. Its total degree is **at most 926**. The
certificate before the final subtraction costs249=130M+119A. The default
retains four fixed positive program parameters; the separately bounded
five-program-parameter interface has the same ledger. Computation time
remains existential and unbounded.

This removes three actual operations from each saved
`neary_woods_universal_tail_quotient253` source. The same U9 machine,
ordinary-input loader, valid shifted program slices, fixed numerals,
physical counter, chronology and terminal condition remain paid. It is
an improvement of that alternative universal construction, not a new
best overall universal bound or a proof about two-branch Collatz
universality. The existing separate universal84 construction is unchanged.

The saving extends the binary-complement idea to a small tree. The
physical slope-class mask already computes exactly the three selectors
needed by that tree, so no new selected-history word or witness is
introduced. The source's signed repunit relation requires a separate
pretyping argument; the new mask is not silently replaced by its
on-zero interpretation.

The source is read only as data; no historical builder or array is run.

## 3. Signed pretyping bounds before AND or any sign recovery

At a positive zero all sixteen integer factors are units. In particular
`epsilon_G` in (1) is either sign, while its global-sum definition gives

    P>=6, J>=1, b<=P+2, J<=P/6.                     (8)

For the last bound, `(b-1)J=P-epsilon_G<=P+1` and `b>=32`
give `J<=(P+1)/31<=P/6`. If `J=0`, the signed relation would give
`P=+1` or `-1`, contradicting the positive sum. Every history and product
hat is belowP. All selectors and both groups are nonnegative and at mostJ.
Also `R>=0`, including atD=1, and

    c_h R=(b-c_h)J<(b-1)J<=P+1,

so the integer `R<P`.

Expanding the actual mask (4) with the signed relation gives the exact
identity on that relation

    Mtree=J+P*(G03+(1-epsilon_G)G12)+P^2*G12.        (9)

Every one of its three coefficients is nonnegative and at most `3J<=P/2`.
Since `P>=6`, each is at mostP-2. It follows in particular that

    Ctree<P^3, Mtree+2<P^3.                         (10)

The first inequality uses only the bounds on the three coefficients in
(2), not their bit typing. For the second, the coarse integer bound
`Mtree<=(P-2)(1+P+P^2)` already suffices.

The physical mask coefficients are at most
`(b-1)J=P-epsilon_G<=P+1`, so

    0<=Mb<2P^3.

Here `(P+1)(1+P+P^2)<2P^3` forP>=6. The histories and decoded products
give `Hb,Zb<P^3`, while `Hr,Mr<P^2`. Therefore

    Hb+P^3*Ctree<P^6,
    Mb+P^3*Mtree<P^3*(Mtree+2)<P^6,
    Zb+P^3*Ctree<P^6.

Adding the range regions proves, for either sign and even atD=1,

    0<=H0,M0,Z<T.                                   (11)

This handles possible carries in the untyped physical masks explicitly.
It is not a premature per-lane split. The identical shared controller
and range regions ofH0 andZ also imply

    H-Z>=T+1, M-Z>=1,
    Qhigh-H-M+Z>=(b-5)T+2>=2.                       (12)

For example `H-Z=Hb-Zb+2T>=2T-P^3+1>=T+1`.
The other two inequalities follow directly from (11).

The inherited signed low-recoder proof gives `0<a0,b0,z0<q0` before its
mask/index signs are recovered. It has no dependency on the changed
history rows. Equations (11)--(12) therefore give strictly positive
implicit truth fields

    F0=q-A-Bport+F3-1, F1=A-F3, F2=Bport-F3, F3>0.

Their sum is identicallyq-1, residues are(1,4,2,8) modulo16, and each
field is belowq. These are exactly the scalar hypotheses used by the
current tail-quotient253 proof; neither hierarchical typing nor history
transport has been used.

**Remark 1 (the mask is not an off-zero identity).** One must not claim
that the emitted mask (4) always equals `J+P*G03+P^2*G12`.
For `b=32,J=G12=1,G03=0,P=30`, the repunit sign is-1, and those two
expressions are961 and901. Their difference is60=2P. These are scalar
pretyping data, not full compiler zeros. Equation(9), its bounds, and the
independent sign argument below are necessary for the cheaper five-row
mask. Computing an unconditional literal tree mask instead takes one
extra subtraction in this schedule and yields a251-operation variant,
which is not emitted here.

## 4. Native recovery, then tree typing, then true chronology

The new source retains the parent's literal native norms, normalized
strong coefficient, both positive ratio slacks, index/linear factors,
positive odd multiplier inY, padded fields and tail bound (7). Thus the
local native proof of `tail_quotient253`, Sections2--3, applies using
only the scalar hypotheses just verified. For clarity its order is:

- With `r=sum q^j F_j`, one has `r>=4369`, `r<q^3(F3+1)`, `F3>=8`
  and `r=1 mod16`. Formula(7) and `Y>=3q` give `XY>2r+3`,
  `a=Y(X+1)>2r+3`, `X>=16`, before assuming `X>r`.
- All norm signs are+1 by the unchanged congruences. The normalized
  strong factor restores the full ordinary rank hypothesis with the
  proof-only `i_ordinary=Delta*i`. The rank and strict signed step-down
  recover the common indices; strict duplication fixes the linear sign.
- The lower ratio recovers `X=2^(2r'+1)` without first using `X>r'`.
  The full ratio/population theorem gives `q=2^popcount(r')`.
  The unchanged low residue1mod16 and positive checksum fields exclude
  `r'=r-2`, so the joint index sign is positive and the complete joint
  AND holds at the originalr.

These are applications of the accepted local native lemmas with their
hypotheses checked, not an invocation of the old full compiler before
its high-lane semantics. The changed power8 does not occur in those
local lemmas.

The crucial new factorization is (6): all factors are positive integers,
so dyadicq forces the same low recoder factors and bothb andP dyadic.
The parent's paid loader/population argument excludes the negative
geometry index, and the low Mersenne argument fixes the low mask sign;
neither uses the history exponent9. If `epsilon_G=-1`, dyadicb=2^v

    epsilon_G=1, P=(b-1)J+1=b^t, t>=1.             (13)

before interpreting the mask in(9).

The low/high split of the complete joint AND is valid by the strict
low-port bounds. SinceT is dyadic and the lower blocks satisfy(11),
the disjoint high tags2 and1 disappear in the high AND, leaving
`H0 AND M0=Z`. With(13), every physical coefficient is now belowP and
(9) is the genuine controller mask. The controller region splits into
exactly the three equations(3).

A subset of an integer bitmask has a nonnegative, borrow-free complement
inside that mask. First `G12 subset J` gives `G03=J-G12 subset J`.
Then `S0 subset G03` gives `S3=G03-S0` as its disjoint complement;
`S1 subset G12` gives `S2=G12-S1`. Hence all four original selectors

satisfy(3). No three-selector overlap shortcut is used.

The physical and range regions remain precisely the three slope-class

All hypotheses of `initial_bound254`, Sections2--3, are therefore restored:

## 7. Prior-art boundary and verification scope

The earlier128 orbit result applies to one fixed two-residue map and
alone supplies no proven universal substrate. Here the three-lane tree
uses an already compiled universal binary-tag host and its actual paid
input and control interfaces. The group's selected-product pack is reused
rather than assumed free. Merely removing the fourth flat selector test
would be invalid: `J=5,S0=S1=1,S2=0,S3=3` has the first three as subsets
ofJ and the correct sum, but the last is not a subset. The hierarchical
conditions specifically exclude that overlap.

The initial377-source sketch is not the reported arithmetic frontier.
The census found its later shared-selector and joint-native253 successors;
this packet works directly on the two current tail-quotient253 arrays.
No historical finalizer-partition search is rerun, and no bound for that
entire optimization family is asserted.

Fresh evidence is restricted to static source structure and independently
handwritten scalar formulas. It checks hierarchical partition equivalence,
both signed-mask expansions and full lower-block/field margins including
height1, plus genuine affine histories on a small explicit illustrative
table. Those histories are not substitutions for the actual U9 table;
no finite check is claimed to materialize a full native Pell zero or
establish universality. The proof transfers the pinned full parent theorem.
The helper imports no supplied/frozen code and has no source-array
interpreter or degree propagator.

The full parent253 source arrays and proof, product-scale253 proof,
initial-bound254 proof, history-units260 proof, U9 program/input proof,
slope-class history and four-tile theorem were read inertly. The
shifted-offset258 proof was also read in full. The separate371 sketch's
377/387 interfaces and the newer tail-partition summary were read only
for route selection; no old program or partition search was executed.
External machine-simulation and Pell lemmas remain inherited through

The fresh writer and separate normal and optimized (`-O`) receipt checks
ran from `/` and passed before freezing. Both complete arrays were rebuilt

These have precisely the limited meanings stated above.
