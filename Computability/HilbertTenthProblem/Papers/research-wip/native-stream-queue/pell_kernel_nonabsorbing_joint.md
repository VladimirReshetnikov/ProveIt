# The nonabsorbing determinant theorem with a joint append bound

The [independent-bound decision theorem](pell_kernel_nonabsorbing_determinant.md)
extends to the joint bound. Fix radix b>=2 and integer coefficients with
`Delta=alpha*delta-beta*gamma!=0`. Given a numerical input x and an
effectively computed integer eta(x), existence of powers W=b^m,L=b^n,
n>=1, satisfying a specified effective lower bound on W and

    A>=1, B>=1, A+B<=L-1,
    (alpha W+beta)A+(gamma W+delta)B
        +epsilon WL+zeta W+eta(x)=0                 (1)

is decidable. This extension is proved separately because complementing
one box coordinate does not preserve the joint bound.

For the joint-bound [ternary paired FIFO](native_ternary_pair_fifo50.md),
transport gives D0+D1=x+W(A+B). With 0<x<W, its paid strict bound
D0+D1<WL is exactly A+B<=L-1. The global affine carry equation is
equation(4) of the independent-bound note, with determinant nonzero
exactly when `c0*c3-c1*c2!=0`. Thus the same decidability conclusion
holds for that whole unfiltered nonabsorbing architecture, including its
[strong42 composition](pell_kernel_power_geometry42.md). No extra code
filter or general determinant-zero controller is covered.

## 1. A triangular bounded-coin lemma

Let 0<a<c be integers, d=gcd(a,c), M,N integers, and set

    C=c*max(a,c-a)/d,
    B0=C+3c+|N|+3.                                  (2)

For every integer L>=B0, the equation

    au+cv=ML+N,  u,v>=0, u+v<=U=L-3                (3)

has this exact classification:

* M<0 or M>c: no solution.
* 0<M<c: a solution exactly when d divides ML+N.
* M=0: a solution exactly when N belongs to the nonnegative semigroup
  generated by a,c.
* M=c: a solution exactly when -3c-N belongs to the nonnegative semigroup
  generated by c-a,c.

For the interior case write T=ML+N. The threshold gives
T>=C and cU-T>=C. If d|T, choose the unique residue u in[0,c/d-1]
with au=T modulo c. Then au<C and (c-a)u<C. Thus

    v=(T-au)/c>=0,
    u+v=(T+(c-a)u)/c<=U,

and v is integral. Conversely divisibility is necessary. Outside[0,c]
the target T or its complement cU-T is negative by(2).

At M=0, a nonnegative representation of N has u+v<=N<=U, so the
semigroup condition is exact. At M=c, introduce w=U-u-v. The complementary
equation is `(c-a)u+cw=-3c-N`; any nonnegative representation has
u+w<=-3c-N<=U by(2), recovering a valid v. Negative endpoint targets
fail. Both semigroup questions are finite enumerations, including zero
targets. The distinct positive coin coefficients exclude all zero-weight
ambiguity inside this lemma.

## 2. Exact sorted-vertex substitution

At a fixed W, write a0=alpha W+beta and b0=gamma W+delta. For now
assume that the three integers 0,a0,b0 are pairwise distinct. Put

    U=L-3,  x1=A-1, x2=B-1, x0=U-x1-x2.

The domain in(1) is equivalent to x0,x1,x2>=0 with sum U. Assign the
weights c0=0,c1=a0,c2=b0. Let sigma order these weights strictly, and
write

    m0=c_sigma(0),
    a=c_sigma(1)-m0, c=c_sigma(2)-m0,
    u=x_sigma(1), v=x_sigma(2).

The unused coordinate x_sigma(0)=U-u-v is nonnegative. Conversely every
u,v in the triangle of(3) reconstructs all three x coordinates and then
A=1+x1,B=1+x2. This is an exact bijection, regardless of the signs of
a0,b0 or which vertex has minimum weight.

The weighted sum is

    a0*x1+b0*x2=m0*U+a*u+c*v.

Consequently(1) becomes(3), with

    M=-epsilon W-m0,
    N=-zeta W-eta(x)-a0-b0+3m0.                      (4)

The coefficient gcd is unchanged by the permutation and subtraction:

    gcd(a,c)=gcd(a0,b0), hence gcd(a,c) divides |Delta|. (5)

This last assertion follows by taking differences of the three weights
and from the same fixed determinant combinations used in the independent
case. No variable modulus is silently replaced by a constant.

## 3. Uniform duration constants and exceptional widths

Set D=|Delta| and use

    S=1+|alpha|+|beta|+|gamma|+|delta|+|epsilon|
        +|zeta|+|eta(x)|,
    K=S^2+8S+3.

For every nonexceptional W, the sorted coefficients satisfy
0<a<c<=S(W+1), and(4) gives |N|<=5S(W+1). Therefore the threshold in(2)
satisfies

    B0<=K(W+1)^2.                                   (6)

Above B0 every possible case is either impossible, independent of L,
or determined by L modulo d=gcd(a,c)<=D. The repaired modular-window
argument in Section3 of the independent note applies verbatim: powers
below B0 or with exponent n<D are already bounded; otherwise the admitted
residue is in the eventual cycle modulo d. If n0 is the first exponent
with b^n0>=B0, the next D exponents contain the whole cycle, because its
preperiod plus period is at most d. Hence any admitted width has an
admitted duration satisfying

    L<=b^(D+1)*K(W+1)^2.                             (7)

For W>=4*b^(D+1)*K this permits L<=W^3. The assumptions 0,a0,b0
pairwise distinct fail only at finitely many W: none of a0,b0,a0-b0
is the zero polynomial when Delta!=0. Every possible positive integer
root is below 1+|beta|+|delta|. Thus retaining all powers below

    W0=max(2,4*b^(D+1)*K,1+|beta|+|delta|)            (8)

handles all coincidences as well as all small widths. At each such fixed
W the entire remaining formula, including its triangular domain, is
ordinary Presburger arithmetic in A,B,L. Elimination followed by the
one-power residue algorithm decides it. This covers constant and zero
coefficient degeneracies at the exceptional widths.

## 4. The bounded slack equation

For the remaining W use the same four duration branches as before:
`L=W^k V` for k=0,1,2 with 1<=V<W a power of b, or k=3,V=1.
Introduce the nonnegative slack

    Z=L-1-A-B,  A+B+Z+1=L.                          (9)

Every original witness has 0<=Z<L, and conversely(9), A,B>=1 and
Z>=0 give exactly the joint bound. Expand A,B,Z into at most four
base-W digits with the common bounds for values less than L. Retain
positive digit sums for A,B; no positivity condition is placed on Z.

The main equation has the same five coefficient slots and finitely
bounded carries as in the independent theorem. Equation(9) has coefficients

    Hj=a_j+b_j+z_j+[j=0]-V*[j=k], 0<=j<=4,          (10)

with missing digits zero. Its sum `sum Hj W^j` is zero. Since |Hj|<=5W,
all successive carries in `Hj+e_j=W e_(j+1)` lie in[-10,10], starting
and ending at zero. The integer-carry equivalence follows by successive
reduction modulo W, exactly as for the main equation.

Enumerate both finite carry tuples. The digit bounds, their positivity,
and both systems of column equations are now ordinary Presburger
conditions with free W,V. Eliminate the digits and apply the explicit
two-power finite rectangle algorithm of the independent note. This proves
the decision theorem without extending a one-parameter parametric result
to two unbounded powers.

## 5. Evidence and scope

The [checker](pell_kernel_nonabsorbing_joint.py) audits the triangular
saturation lemma in every endpoint/sign regime, the sorted-vertex
bijection for every ordering of 0,a0,b0, the gcd and uniform threshold,
the modular duration replacement, and the bounded slack-digit equations.
It imports the already audited elementary power-orbit routine; general
Presburger elimination is not implemented. The mathematical proof is
uniform, whereas these executable checks are finite evidence for its
elementary steps. Default execution compares the
[receipt](pell_kernel_nonabsorbing_joint.json).

Independent full proof/source/default review passed, including every
vertex ordering, both endpoint semigroups, the uniform duration constant,
and zero-slack carry paths. General proportional coefficients and
additional controller filters remain outside the theorem. No complete
universal operation bound is improved by this obstruction.
