## Why there is no new paid compiler in this scope

The strongest Diophantine connection is Part VIII's theorem at lines 17338–17375: for each fixed bounded or Σ₁ formula, an established internal-MRDP input supplies polynomial terms P,Q with a correctness proof in the specified weak arithmetic. The automatic-Borel argument at lines 17421–17560 evaluates those fixed terms in the model, projects the auxiliary variables, and uses totality/uniqueness of the output. It explicitly requires no selection of polynomial solutions. The ordinary remainder-based exponentiation formula is displayed, but the MRDP translation is not emitted as a coefficient array or complete paid circuit. No new witness count, gate count, fixed ordinary-input loader or universal history encoding is obtained. Internal correctness in nonstandard models is a useful formalization distinction, not an arithmetic-cost improvement.

Part VII gives a genuine finite algorithm for parameter-free stream-revision optimization, using integer Presburger regimes and rational linear sign feasibility. Its separate halting argument places a single nonzero coefficient at a machine's halting time in an infinite computable stream. Computing the optimum from an index of that stream is the task proved impossible. It is not a finite ordinary-input existential certificate. The publication expressly preserves this distinction at lines 16608–16641 and describes a full continuity implementation as future work at lines 16826–16892.

Parts VI and IX are structural results about uncountable topological presentations. Their packing cardinalities, model counts and finite-normal-form omnific realizations do not supply a finite arithmetic history compiler. The claims concern different carriers and cost models from the fixed positive-integer polynomial search.

No substantive publication-scope conflict was found in the exact text read, and no correction is requested. A new computational contribution would still need an explicit ordinary-input encoding, a proved finite existential history relation and a complete operation ledger. The current 84 universal polynomial and the existing arithmetic frontier are unchanged by this review. Unread proofs, PDF layout and broader theorem validity remain outside its scope.
