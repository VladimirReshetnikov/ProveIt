# Exact scalar rank obstructs compressing the paired squares and their guard

The six physical coordinates in the
[9D affine mortality construction](group_affine_guarded_mortality9.md)
cannot be replaced by fewer coordinates while preserving its scalar
series by linear letter actions. More strongly, the complete guarded
scalar series has exact linear rank nine. This includes attempts to
merge physical and guard coordinates; it is stronger than the parent's
rank-three statement about the guard alone.

Both statements apply to the actual abstract universal fibre subgroup,
not just to all of SL2 times SL2 or to an unrelated numerical alphabet.
The proof is elementary: opposite unipotents make the symmetric-square
action irreducible, a nontrivial normal subgroup isolates both physical
blocks, and commutators remove the commuting control action.

These are lower bounds for the **identical scalar series**. They do not
rule out another scalar having the same zero set, a different guard,
another mortality construction, or a nonlinear arithmetic certificate.
In particular they give no lower bound on the operation count of a
Diophantine equation. The existing paid four-vector compiler is fully
consistent with this result: it certifies the same existence predicate
without reproducing the guarded scalar on every word.

## 1. Subgroup hypotheses, including the universal case

Let Gamma be a subgroup of SL2(Z) containing

    U=[1,1;0,1], B=[1,0;12,1],

and not containing -I. Let N be a nontrivial normal subgroup of Gamma.
Let K be a subgroup of Gamma times Gamma which projects onto each factor
and contains N times N. Take any finite alphabet of paired matrices
which generates K as a group. A signed generating alphabet is allowed,
as in the mortality construction; the rank proof only needs group
generation and invertibility.

The [projective universal group](group_projective_zero_mortality6.md)
satisfies these hypotheses. Its Gamma is the faithful rank-four Schreier
image generated by U,B and two further conjugates of U. Its matrices
have diagonal entries 1 modulo four, excluding -I. If
pi:F4 -> H is its presentation quotient, identify N=ker(pi) with its
faithful image in Gamma. The conjugated fibre subgroup

    K=(a,1){(g,h):pi(g)=pi(h)}(a^-1,1)

is subdirect and contains N times N because N is normal.

Here N really is nontrivial. The fixed universal r.e. set has some
positive member y. The free word [b^-y a b^y,a] is nontrivial in the
free group on the named a,b, while its image is the identity in the
embedded group G_U and hence in H. Faithfulness of the matrix image
makes this a nonidentity element of N. No property of the word problem
of H or numerical implementation of the embedding is assumed in this
argument.

The numerical fixtures below instead use the rank-two group <U,B> and
one chosen relator. That smaller group already supplies the same two
unipotents; it is not confused with the full Schreier image, and those
fixtures are not claimed to be universal.

## 2. Two elementary linear-algebra facts

Use the parent's representation on symmetric coordinates (a,b,c) for
the matrix [a,-b;-b,c]:

    S([p,q;r,s])=[p^2,-2pq,q^2; -pr,ps+qr,-qs; r^2,-2rs,s^2].

This acts by G -> A G A^T. It is a homomorphism, and S(A)=I implies
A=I or -I: the first and last diagonal-coordinate equations give q=r=0
and p^2=s^2=1, while determinant one gives p=s. On Gamma its kernel is
therefore trivial.

First, V=Q^3 is irreducible under S(Gamma). Write J=S(U)-I. Then

    J=[0,-2,1;0,0,-1;0,0,0],
    J^2=[0,0,2;0,0,0;0,0,0], J^3=0.

Any nonzero invariant subspace contains e1: apply J^2 if its vector's
third coordinate is nonzero, or J if only the second is nonzero; a
vector supported in the first coordinate already suffices. The three
vectors S(B)^k e1 for k=0,1,2 are

    (1,0,0), (1,-12,144), (1,-24,576),

whose column determinant is -2*12^3. Thus the subspace is all V.
The dual representation is also irreducible, since the annihilator of
a proper nonzero invariant dual subspace would contradict this result.

Second, for any nontrivial normal subgroup M of Gamma,

    W_M=sum_(n in M) image(S(n)-I)=V.                  (1)

This sum is nonzero because S is faithful. It is Gamma-invariant since

    S(g)(S(n)-I)=(S(gng^-1)-I)S(g).

Irreducibility proves (1). The corresponding span of rows
`v(S(n)-I)` is all V*, by the same normality argument on the dual.

We will use rational inverse words only as linear-span arguments.
If a finite-dimensional subspace R is invariant under an invertible
letter matrix A, then A restricted to R is injective and hence onto R;
therefore R is invariant under A^-1 too. The forward reachable span
and forward observable span are thus invariant under all group words
in the letter matrices. This does not add an inverse matrix as a
permitted stream letter, and does not assume its control action equals
the action of the alphabet's separately supplied inverse physical letter.

## 3. The paired-square series has exact rank six

Fix an integer t, put c_t=(1,t,t^2), and define, on chronological words,

    rho(P,Q)=diag(S(P),S(Q)),
    v_t=(c_t,c_t), u=(1,0,0,1,0,0),
    f_t(w)=u rho(w) v_t.

Thus f_t(w) is the sum of the two first-coordinate squares at input
(-1,t). Later chronological letters multiply on the left.

Let R be the span of all forward reachable vectors rho(w)v_t. Each
block projection of R is all V: K projects onto Gamma, c_t is nonzero,
and the representation is irreducible. For n in N, subtracting the
identity from rho(n,1) isolates the first block. Since that block's
projection already ranges over V, (1) implies V times zero is contained
in R. The pair (1,n) similarly supplies zero times V. Hence R=Q^6.

The same reasoning starts with the row u. Each block projection of its
observable span is all V* by dual irreducibility. The normal difference
operators isolate both dual blocks, giving the entire six-dimensional
observable span.

For clarity, the finite Hankel argument now proves the lower bound.
Choose six forward prefixes whose reachable columns form a basis, and
six forward suffixes whose observed rows form a basis. The matrix of
values f_t(prefix followed by suffix) is the product of these two
invertible basis matrices, so has rank six. Every d-dimensional linear
representation of the same word scalar factors this matrix through Q^d;
therefore d>=6. The displayed representation attains six.

This holds for every t, not merely generic values. A five-coordinate
quotient of the physical action, a change of basis removing one shared
coordinate, or a wholly different linear realization of this same
scalar series is therefore impossible under the stated hypotheses.

## 4. The full guarded series has exact rank nine

Fix t>=3 and use precisely the letters and boundary vectors of the
9D construction:

    C_T=[1,0,0;0,1,0;1,1,1],
    C_F=[1,0,0;3,1,0;0,0,1],
    T=diag(T3(t),T3(t),t C_T),
    D_i=diag(S(P_i),S(Q_i),lambda_i C_F),
    z=(e1,e1,1,0,-2), u9=(e1^T,e1^T,0,0,4).

Here T3(t) is the parent's affine physical input matrix, and lambda_i
are its fixed positive weighted bounds. Every letter is invertible.
The theorem concerns the full scalar `F_t(w)=u9 D(w)z`, before adjoining
the constant rank-one reset. Including the reset as another letter
cannot lower the rank already forced by this subalphabet.

All the fixed control matrices lambda_i C_F commute, including their
rational inverses. Therefore a group commutator of fixed lifted words
has identity control block, regardless of the particular lift. This
permits an additional isolation argument without changing any alphabet.

Let M=[N,Gamma], the subgroup generated by all commutators [n,g]. It
is normal in Gamma and nontrivial. Indeed if M were trivial, every
n in N would commute with both U and B. Direct matrix multiplication
shows their common centralizer in SL2(Q) is {I,-I}; Gamma excludes -I,
contradicting nontrivial N.

For n in N, take a fixed group-word lift of (n,1). For g in Gamma,
subdirectness supplies (g,h) in K and a lift of that pair. Their lifted
commutator acts as

    diag(S([n,g]),I3,I3).                            (2)

The control is exactly I3 because its image is abelian. Products give
such a lift for every member of M; the second block has the symmetric
construction. The inverse-span observation in Section2 makes all these
operators legitimate for reachable and observable spans of forward
words, even though the lifts can use rational inverses.

The first and second physical projections of both spans are full V and
V*, just as before. Apply (2) minus the identity and use (1) with M.
This puts all six pure physical coordinate directions, and all six
pure physical dual directions, inside those spans.

It remains to examine the control quotient. Its initial column is
c=(1,0,-2). Dividing the nonzero scalar letter factors in Q, the three
reachable control columns

    c, C_F c, C_T c

have determinant3. Thus the reachable quotient is all Q^3. Its output
row ell=(0,0,4) has the three observable rows

    ell, ell C_T, ell C_T C_F,

whose determinant is -192. The observable quotient is also all Q^3.
Together with the pure physical subspaces, both full spans are Q^9.

The Hankel factorization from Section3 therefore gives a nonsingular
nine-by-nine minor. Any linear representation of this identical
guarded scalar series has dimension at least nine, and the existing
construction attains it. This holds at each fixed t>=3, so even allowing
an alternative realization to depend arbitrarily on t cannot evade the
dimension bound for the same function of words.

## 5. What this rules out, and what remains open

The argument rules out exact linear compression of the paired squares
to four or five coordinates, and exact linear compression of the entire
current guard-plus-physical scalar to eight coordinates. It also rules
out finding a common physical/control coordinate by a clever basis
change: rank is independent of the chosen coordinates.

It does not assert that every universal mortality predicate needs nine
dimensions. A scalar with the same zeros can have a different Hankel
rank. A different sign-separation guard, a non-rank-one reset, a changed
word interface, or a nonlinear arithmetic endpoint lies outside this
theorem. No new universal operation bound follows. The two-operation
affine loader, fixed alphabet, and ordinary-input contract remain those
of the parent; this packet only identifies a precise obstruction to
compressing that implementation unchanged.

The nontrivial normal subgroup hypothesis is essential to this proof.
For the pure diagonal subgroup {(P,P)}, the same equal initial blocks
give just twice one square series, of rank three. Its full duplicated
physical-plus-control series has rank six in the supplied fixture.
Surjectivity onto both factors alone would not justify the rank-six or
rank-nine conclusions.

## 6. Executable evidence

The [source](group_guarded_mortality_rank_obstruction.py) and
[receipt](group_guarded_mortality_rank_obstruction.json) construct exact
forward reachable and observable bases using rational elimination. No
bounded word search is treated as a proof of rank deficiency: dependent
vectors need not be expanded because their letter images are linear
combinations of images of the retained basis, and the worklist closes
under every letter before stopping.

Three finite one-relator fibre alphabets use relators
`[b^-y a b^y,a]` for y=1,2,3. For each at t=3,13,37,61, the checker
constructs nonzero rank-six and rank-nine Hankel minors, and independently
evaluates every prefix/suffix concatenation. One complete pair of minors
and their words is stored; the other exact determinants have compact
bit-length/hash summaries. The receipt also checks twelve rational
commutator lifts, the elementary unipotent and control determinants,
384 direct projective square identities, and the diagonal rank3/rank6
boundary example.

These fixtures do not instantiate a universal numerical alphabet. The
abstract subgroup proof establishes the universal case; the finite
checks audit its matrix conventions, chronology and rank mechanism.
Run the source with the research Python environment. The optional
`--write-receipt` flag regenerates the deterministic receipt, and the
default run compares it.
