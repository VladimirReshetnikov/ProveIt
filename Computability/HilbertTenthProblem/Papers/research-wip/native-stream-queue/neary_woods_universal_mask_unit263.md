# A positive repunit unit gives263 operations

The [history-unit successor](neary_woods_universal_history_units260.md) lowers the
complete U9 polynomial to260=134M+126A,2 comparisons and43 positive witnesses, with
degree at most3866. Its positive slack bijection preserves the full relation;
mapped270/608 has44 witnesses. The263 source and proof below remain unchanged.

The explicit U9 universal polynomial has a **263=134M+129A** successor
to the [shared-history265 source](neary_woods_universal_shared_history265.md).
Its certificate costs **252=130M+122A**, with **four comparisons**,
**43 positive existential witnesses**, the same four positive program
parameters and ordinary positive input. Its degree is **at most3857**.
A separate mapped schedule reaches degree at most608 with273 operations
and44 witnesses.

The change absorbs the second recoder repunit equation into the existing
integer unit product. A negative new unit would require a power of two
to equal minus one modulo a Mersenne number; the native core excludes
that possibility. The complete positive zero sets are identical on the
same supplied coordinates. The off-zero polynomials are generally
different, with exact correction identities given below.

The [source](neary_woods_universal_mask_unit263.py) and
[receipt](neary_woods_universal_mask_unit263.json) retain the actual fixed
U9 machine, numeral recipes, paid loader, valid program slices and exact
counter initialization. Both the four-program interface and the separate
fifth duration-bound interface are supported. No fixed numeral
multiplication becomes free. The separate75-operation certificate and
87-operation universal polynomial are unchanged.

## 1. The new unit and the unchanged positive domain

Use the recoder notation of the
[computed-port proof](neary_woods_universal_computed_ports275.md).
Here q0 is the recoder input scale, while q below is the much larger
joined native scale. With positive supplied coordinates, the literal
outer source defines

    ell=program_duration_bound,
    q0=x+ell+input_slack,
    rload=q0+z+power_gap,
    Q=(2^D-1)rload+1, B=2^(D-1)Q,
    J=(B-1)duration_quotient+ell,
    P=(B-1)J+1, S=q0*P, copies=xJ,
    K=quotient_hat+fusion_output_slack.

The actual fixed U9 width D is at least3. These positive definitions
give Q>q0>=3, B>=4Q, 1<=ell<B-1, J>0, J<P and K>=2 before any
native equation. Write h=quotient_hat, so K>h>=1. The projected output
hat is the proof expression

    Ahat=(Q-1)(h-1)+z+1.

The old comparison and the new factor are respectively

    mask_scale=(2B-1)K+1=S,
    Nmask=S-(2B-1)K.                                  (1)

Delete the old one-addition mask_scale register and its comparison.
Use one subtraction for Nmask, and multiply Nmask into one of the
already paid factor groups. The source reuses mask_product=(2B-1)K.
Thus the certificate adds exactly one multiplication, loses one
comparison and changes no supplied coordinate.

A zero of either supported grouped finalizer makes every retained
ordinary residual zero and each group product1. For an unsquared anchor
this follows from

    anchor*(1+sum residual^2)=1

over integers; the positive second factor must be1. In an all-SOS
finalizer the assertion is immediate. Consequently every individual
integer factor, including Nmask, is a unit. Put

    Nmask=sigma in{1,-1},
    S=(2B-1)K+sigma.                                  (2)

We must prove sigma=1 without presuming the old repunit equation.

The guard reconstructs the complete frozen parent from its strong
treatment, positive-scale choices, fixed factor partition and program
interface. It accepts the two literal frozen product-label conventions,
but does not accept altered outer rows, comparison lists, domains,
fixed numeral roles or nested public interfaces. Private-consumer checks
also ensure that mask_scale has no source consumer or active export
beyond its removed comparison. Both complete strong equations and all
four ratio slacks remain present.

## 2. The computed fields stay positive for either sign

For B>=4 and K>=1, even the negative choice in(2) gives

    S-BK=(B-1)K+sigma>0,
    S-(K+1)=(2B-2)K+sigma-1>=0.                     (3)

These are the only bounds from the old mask equation used in the
computed-port positivity proof. In particular all its low-port bounds
remain valid. With L=16BS, the literal low values are

    A_lo=16B*copies+16ell+12,
    B_lo=16BK+16ell-6,
    Z_lo=16B(Ahat-1)+8.

Since q0>x and J<P, copies<S. Thus

    0<A_lo<16B(copies+1)<=L,
    0<B_lo<16B(K+1)<=L.

The paid loader gives Q-1>z+1, so

    1<=Ahat<(Q-1)h<Bh<BK<S.

This proves 0<Z_lo<L as well, without treating Ahat as an already typed
AND result.

The high history source is unchanged. Let b be its radix, p its scale,
and H_h,M_h,Z_h its three joined words. The retained global comparison
has six positive terms summing to p. Together with the selector-hat
definitions it proves p>=6, b>=4, and the ordinary integer margins

    H_h-Z_h>=1,
    M_h-Z_h>1,
    p^11-H_h-M_h+Z_h>=2.                              (4)

These are exactly the untyped inequalities in Sections1--2 of the
computed-port proof; none uses the recoder mask equation. They follow
by bounding each high lane below its power of p, with the identical
controller/range terms of H_h and Z_h cancelled before estimating.

The factored265 source has the exact implicit ports and truth fields

    A=A_lo+L*H_h, Bp=B_lo+L*M_h, Zp=Z_lo+L*Z_h,
    q=L*p^11,
    F1=A-Zp, F2=Bp-Zp, F0=q-1-A-Bp+Zp, F3=Zp.        (5)

The deleted field definitions are mathematical reconstructions, not
newly paid gates. The parent's exact factoring identity still gives

    r=F0+q*F1+q^2*F2+q^3*F3.

By the low bounds and(4), every F_i is strictly positive. For example
F0>=2L-A_lo-B_lo+Z_lo-1>=Z_lo+1>0. Also

    sum_i F_i=q-1,
    (F0,F1,F2,F3)=(1,4,2,8) modulo16,
    0<F_i<q,
    q^3+q^2+q+1<=r<q^4.                              (6)

Thus the checksum is identically+1. No power-of-two or bitwise
interpretation has yet been used.

## 3. Obtain the dyadic scale before choosing any index sign

The local joint-core part of the
[coupled native proof](neary_woods_universal_joint_and_coupled.md),
Sections2--3, applies with either sign of its index unit. Its required
pretyping hypotheses are still available from(6), the source's positive
odd multiplier, both ratio slacks and the unchanged positive X bound:

    q>=16, r>=4369, X>r, Y>=16,
    E=XY>2r+3, Y(r-1)>2(2r+3).

For a computed positive scale, X=q(r+bound_beta)>r directly. In an
unmodified scale choice the retained comparison supplies X>r.

For completeness, the order of the local argument is as follows.
Every first, main, auxiliary and normalized-strong norm excludes-1
modulo4; any unnormalized strong comparison is a retained residual.
Thus the full strong equation is restored before applying its rank
theorem. If the index and coupled linear signs are epsilon and lambda,
then K_native=r+epsilon and the linear target is
2K_native-lambda. The first/main Pell classifications, strict ratios
and r bounds give a first index n>=r-1, a main index p_main>n,
c>Delta^2*A, and c>2(2r+3). Strong rank makes the auxiliary root
of-c positive. The signed step-down then gives
p_main=2K_native-lambda and n=K_native. The choice lambda=-1 would
give p_main=2n+1, contradicting the retained upper ratio by Pell
duplication. Hence lambda=1.

All raw native equations are consequently restored at

    r'=r+epsilon-1,                                  (7)

which is either r or r-2. Increasing the proof-only old X-bound slack
by1-epsilon retains positivity. The raw population theorem applies
because r'>q and r'>=9. It gives

    q=2^popcount(r').                                (8)

This local argument requires neither sigma=1 nor a relation between
the geometry and joint index signs. In particular we do not reuse the
old total-product sign relation after inserting the new factor.
The geometry index need not be identified yet. Even the choice
epsilon=-1 in(7) already gives the dyadic q in(8).

All factors of q=16BS*p^11 are positive integers. Therefore B and S
are powers of two. Write B=2^b and S=2^e; here b>=2. If sigma=-1,
equation(2) implies

    2^e=-1 modulo(2^(b+1)-1).                         (9)

Reducing e modulo b+1, the left side has least positive residue2^j
for some0<=j<=b. But

    2^j<=2^b<2^(b+1)-2

when b>=2, so it cannot be the residue of-1. This contradiction proves
**sigma=1** and restores the exact old mask comparison.

Every old group product now equals its new value, namely1. All other
comparisons and supplied coordinates are unchanged. Hence the same
tuple is a complete positive zero of the265 parent. Its existing full
theorem restores all remaining signs, geometry, synchronized duration,
loader relation and chronological history. There is no circular use of
those conclusions in the mask-sign proof.

Conversely, every parent positive zero has Nmask=1 by its mask equation,
so inserting Nmask into any group changes no group value and removing
the old comparison loses nothing. The complete positive zero sets are
therefore identical, on the same supplied tuple. The universal theorem
on the inherited valid program slices, including every required
leading-zero padding and the exact initial counter, is preserved.

## 4. Exact corrections outside the zero set

Let G_j be the old group products, let a be the anchor if one is used,
and let g be the group receiving Nmask=N. The removed old residual is
1-N. All other old certificate registers except mask_scale retain their
values at every integer assignment. The changed group value is NG_g.

If g=a, the complete old and new polynomials obey

    P_new=N(P_old+1)-N*G_a*(1-N)^2-1.                 (10)

If g is not the anchor, put W=G_a for an anchored finalizer and W=1
for an all-SOS finalizer. Then

    P_new=P_old+W*((NG_g-1)^2-(G_g-1)^2-(1-N)^2).     (11)

These are all-integer polynomial identities with no division or sign
assumption. In particular N=1 gives equal complete outputs even away
from all other equations. They are not an assertion that P_old and
P_new are unconditionally identical. The checker evaluates the whole
literal finalizers and these corrections independently, including
signed supplied coordinates and signed stand-ins for fixed numeral
roles. Such stand-ins test algebra only; the positive theorem uses the
actual fixed U9 numerals and their width/radix relations.

## 5. Literal costs, degrees and selected schedules

Replacing mask_scale by Nmask keeps one addition/subtraction. Its new
group multiplication adds1M. Removing an outer comparison removes one
square and two additions in either supported finalizer. The net change
is therefore **two fewer additions**, with no multiplication or witness
change in the complete polynomial.

The default has252 certificate operations and four comparisons, hence
252+3*4-1=263 polynomial operations. Nmask has degree bound4. The
default group bound increases from3843 to3847, and the other largest
residual still has degree bound5, giving3847+2*5=3857. All degree bounds
come from the actual new source, including only the parent's guarded
exact main-norm cancellation; no equality valid only at zeros lowers
the degree.
