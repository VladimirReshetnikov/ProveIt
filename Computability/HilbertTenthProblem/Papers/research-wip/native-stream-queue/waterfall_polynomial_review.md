## Mathematical result and domain

For each **externally fixed** integer `k >= 1`, the construction has 35k natural witnesses, 5k+4 squared linear residuals, and 2k unsquared quadratic complementarity terms. Its exact degree is two because the coefficient of tau squared is one. The four supplied natural parameters are L0, R0, C and tau. The timestamp is `1 + 2C + 7k`, and C is the sum of the macrostep counts `6 + 3Q + r + 3Y`.

The source agrees with the article up to the irrelevant sign of the squared count residual. Every complementarity factor is a nonnegative linear combination of natural coordinates on **all** assignments. Thus a zero annihilates every residual and product before any one-hot assumptions are used. The natural selector sum forces exactly one rule. Complementarity then sets the inactive direction's three coordinates to zero. The next selected source bit forces each preceding active remainder into {0,1}; the final head residual supplies the last bit. Input balances consequently force the unique binary quotient and remainder. Induction recovers the actual deterministic source-machine history.

The selector table omits the sole undefined state/symbol pair (J,1). Hence an earlier halt cannot be crossed, and the terminal state/head equations force the first halt exactly at k. Conversely the actual history supplies a unique natural witness tuple. No separate remainder Boolean constraints are missing. These arguments use naturalness; the theorem is not about signed integer or arbitrary rational zeros.

The article separately defines F0=1; the compiler intentionally accepts only positive horizons. Fixed-k representability is effectively semilinear and does not give a fixed-arity universal polynomial. The 46-clock machine, four-operation half-tape loader and six-operation serialized loader do not pay for a general program-to-half-tape encoding or for an unbounded sequence representation. The report explicitly leaves those costs open. Neither its quadratic family nor the reduction below improves the current unbounded universal arithmetic frontier.

## Concrete API findings and narrow repair

1. **Exact natural input contracts were unenforced.** Original `Base.energy` (line 67) accepts float parameters and Boolean witnesses. At the actual k=7 zero for (L0,R0)=(6,0), replacing L0 with 6.0 yields zero of type float, and replacing the selected first selector by True still yields zero. Original `lift(6.0,0)` itself generates mixed float/int witnesses. The low-level polynomial constructor also accepted noninteger coefficients, and its evaluator accepted noninteger variable values. Original horizon and early-halt guards used assertions, which disappear under `python -O`.
2. **Exported metadata aliased live compiler lists.** Original `dump()` (lines 73–75) returns `parameters` and `witnesses` directly. Clearing `dump()['witnesses']` empties the compiler's witness declaration while leaving all residuals in place. This is an export integrity defect, not a counterexample to the integer polynomial theorem.

[waterfall_grouped_exact_domains.patch](waterfall_grouped_exact_domains.patch) adds exact integer coefficient and formal-value validation, exact natural guards at the certificate/simulator/lift boundary, explicit positive-horizon and early-halt errors, exact declared coordinate sets in `energy`, and detached export name lists. Formal signed integer evaluations remain available through `P.at`; the natural zero theorem is not extended to that interface. Input polynomial term dictionaries are copied. The patch does not promise immutable arbitrary user-mutated compiler internals or authenticate user replacement of Python methods/globals.

## Independent affine projection proof

This is a reduction of the existing **fixed-horizon** polynomial family. It restores deleted coordinates by nonnegative affine expressions, so it provides a natural-domain graph identity and a bijection on zero sets. No hidden existential condition or division is introduced.

The first instruction is A0 -> (0,R,B). Set its 29 selectors accordingly, set QL0=rL0=YL0=0 and YR0=L0. This deletes 33 coordinates, four identically zero squared residuals and both first-step products. For k>=2 also set `rR0 = S1`, the next source-bit selector sum; this is nonnegative on every natural assignment, even before the next one-hot equation holds. The residual `head1` then disappears. Thus the prefix form has 35k−34 witnesses, 5k−1 squares and 2k−2 products for k>=2. At k=1 it has two witnesses, five squares and no products. Constant nonzero terminal residuals are retained, so these small nonhalting horizons are not mistakenly accepted.

For k>=4 the first step is disjoint from the last three instructions. Inspection of **all 29 actual source rules** shows that I1 is the unique instruction entering J, H0 the unique instruction entering I, and G0 the unique instruction entering H. Consequently every parent natural zero ends in G0,H0,I1, all left moves. Their active residues are respectively 0,1,1, forced by the following source bits and final halt bit. Their inactive right triples are zero.

Write g=k−3, h=k−2, i=k−1. Let R_boundary be the output-right expression of step g−1. The input-right equations give

```
YLg = R_boundary,
YLh = 2 R_boundary,
YLi = 4 R_boundary + 1.
```

The last two input-left equations give

```
QLh = 2 QLi + 1,
QLg = 4 QLi + 3.
```

Hence the suffix's incoming left half is `8 QLi + 6` and its final right half is `8 R_boundary + 3`. Every restored coordinate is an affine polynomial with nonnegative integer coefficients in retained natural coordinates. Together with the prefix substitutions, exactly 138 coordinates, 19 squares and eight complementarity products disappear. For k>=4 the resulting counts are

| Quantity | Parent | Forced boundaries |
|---|---:|---:|
| Natural witnesses | 35k | 35k−138 |
| Squared linear residuals | 5k+4 | 5k−15 |
| Raw quadratic products | 2k | 2k−8 |
| Degree | 2 | 2 |

At k=4 this leaves two witnesses and five squares, but `state1=6−1=5` remains a constant nonzero residual. Thus there is no spurious k=4 halt. The disjoint-boundaries construction rejects k<4; the prefix form remains defined there.

Let rho be this affine restoration and let F'_k be the parent polynomial after substitution with its identically zero summands deleted. The complete ring identity is `F'_k(z)=F_k(rho(z))`, valid also for arbitrary signed values. For natural z, rho(z) is natural, so every child zero lifts to a parent zero. Conversely every parent natural zero satisfies all the displayed forced-boundary equations, so projection followed by rho returns that same parent tuple. Projection and restoration are therefore inverse bijections on natural zero sets for each fixed supplied parameter tuple. The singleton-fiber property is preserved.

The initial-plus-suffix improvement is a uniform constant saving of 138 witnesses in this growing family. It does not remove the external horizon or lower its asymptotic 35k witness coefficient. Parent's separately emitted full arithmetic schedule accounts for evaluating all surviving affine forms; square/product counts alone are not operation counts.
