## 3. Dense projection and the six-row zero equivalence

For dense7, every selected matrix action has the required 7m fixed-coefficient products and their sum. The previously reviewed geometry theorem, the explicit terminal sharing map and all seven recurrences therefore prove the exact nonempty-word projection. Positive completeness chooses a sufficiently large dyadic D, actual selected histories and global_slack=P-2S>0, then uses the full positive native converse. The fixed positive7 initial coordinates 3 and 1 reject the empty word. Inherited valid alpha/gamma program slices therefore preserve the ordinary-input language.

At every full native/global zero, exact exclusive selection gives Ztot=S independently of the history recurrence equations. Since each actual alphabet column sums to C, the sum of all seven actual selected actions is C*Ztot=C*S. Thus the computed V7 agrees with the actual seventh action before using its recurrence.

All native packs and their positivity proofs are independent of V_i. The reconstructed V7 may be negative on other assignments without violating any supplied positive domain. Under the same valid constant-column fixed data, the two sources have exactly the same positive zero tuples on their common witness coordinates. ## 5. The separate schedule proof and unadopted slack chart

Its alternative hatted global chart is correct but is not used in the frozen source. On old full zeros, g_old=P-2S and S<=(D-1)J. Strictly positive seven-dimensional letters give Lambda>=7, hence dyadic K>=8; also D>=8, m<=K-1 and J>=1. Therefore

    g_old-7m >= (K-2)D+2-7(K-1)

The change g_new=g_old-7m has a positive inverse g_old=g_new+7m and makes the alternative global residual identical under that substitution. It proves the previously unproved margin in this particular domain. It neither licenses arbitrary hat-offset deletion nor proves any extra row saving in the current arrays, which still compute all selected unhats. The two variants' published counts exclude this alternative.
