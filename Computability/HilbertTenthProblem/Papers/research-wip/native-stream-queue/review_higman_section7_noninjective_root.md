# A noninjective map in the printed final Higman embedding recipe

The printed Section 7.4 construction of Mikaelian v8 does not provide its
claimed injective amalgam. Four independent source generators are sent to
four dependent target words. Moreover, the finite presentation (7.10)
already kills the same nontrivial word in its prescribed free base group.
This invalidates that specific embedding step. The earlier 499-generator,
17,678-relator benign-pair schedule does not use it and remains separate.

The previously recorded 20,808 downstream relation slots are still the
arithmetic result of a syntactic ledger. They cannot now be presented as a
validated faithful embedding route merely awaiting word materialization.
An explicit replacement embedding, with a new audited ledger, is open.

## 1. Exact source and notation

The source is [V. H. Mikaelian, *An explicit algorithm for the Higman
Embedding Theorem*, arXiv:2507.04347v8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.04347v8),
Section 7.4, printed pages 52–54, and equation (7.10), page 55. The exact
cached PDF is pinned in the companion metadata. Root and Aristotle each
read the relevant text and visually checked printed page 53. Root also
visually checked page 55. In particular the target is the product
`v · w(p,q)`, not a conjugate of v; the source is the nested conjugate
`u^(z^w(r,s))`.

Use right conjugation x^y=y^-1 x y. In the prescribed free subgroup

    B=Free(z,r,s,u)=Free(z,r,s)*<u>,

put U_w=u^(z^w) for each word w in Free(r,s). The source declares a map
from the subgroup J1 generated by these U_w to the subgroup J2 generated
by v*w(p,q) inside Free(p,q,v), sending every U_w to that indicated word.
It claims this map is an isomorphism and uses it for amalgamation.

## 2. A four-letter kernel word

**Remark 1 (the asserted isomorphism is not injective).** The normal
closure of u in B has free basis

    {u^h : h in Free(z,r,s)}.

This follows directly from the free-product normal form, or the kernel
normal form for the retraction B to Free(z,r,s) killing u. The four reduced
words

    z^r=r^-1 z r,  z,  z^s=s^-1 z s,
    z^(rs)=s^-1 r^-1 z r s

are distinct. Consequently U_r, U_1, U_s and U_(rs) are four different
members of that free basis. The word

    W=U_r U_1^-1 U_s U_(rs)^-1                         (1)

is a nonempty reduced word in that basis, so W is nontrivial in B and J1.
The prescribed map sends it to

    (v p) v^-1 (v q) (v p q)^-1
      =v p q q^-1 p^-1 v^-1
      =1.                                               (2)

Thus the proposed map cannot be injective. Its target family is not a
free basis. Indeed the cases w=1,r,s give v,vp,vq, which generate all of
Free(p,q,v); the extra indexed words satisfy relations such as (2).
This is a concrete kernel element, independent of the recursively
enumerable set X and of the subgroup list supplied to the construction.

The source words U_w form an infinite free family: the distinct conjugates
z^w in Free(z,r,s) have distinct reduced spellings for w in Free(r,s).
There is no isomorphism with the displayed target family, whose generated
group has rank three. A group pushout along a noninjective map can still
be defined, but it does not supply the asserted injective amalgam or its
normal-form consequences.

## 3. The finite relations already force the collapse

**Remark 2 (equation (7.10) also kills W).** This failure is present in the
finite presentation, not just the preceding infinite-family argument.
The newly imposed relations in (7.10) are

    u^z=v;
    z^e1=z^r, r^e1=r, s^e1=s, u^e1=u,
    p^e1=p, q^e1=q, v^e1=v p;
    z^e2=z^s, r^e2=r, s^e2=s, u^e2=u,
    p^e2=p, q^e2=q, v^e2=v q.                          (3)

These are one plus fourteen relations. Conjugating the first relation by
e1 and by e2 gives respectively

    U_1=v,  U_r=v p,  U_s=v q.

For the fourth word, the order e2 e1 is important:

    z^(e2 e1)=(z^s)^e1=(z^r)^s=z^(rs),
    u^(e2 e1)=u,
    v^(e2 e1)=(v q)^e1=v p q.

Hence U_(rs)=(u^z)^(e2 e1)=v p q, so (2) proves W=1 using only the
finite relations (3). Since W was nontrivial in B, the prescribed map
from B into this finite presentation is not injective.

The construction starts with an embedded Free(z,r,s) in K_ZX and then
takes its free product with <u>. That contains the prescribed free B,
so the same counterexample applies to this base in every such instance.
It does not by itself prove that K_ZX alone fails to embed, nor that
every subsequently presented T_X fails to embed by every possible map.
The precise refutation is the claimed base-B injection in this recipe.

## 4. Effect on the repository's conditional counts

The frozen gap note, Section 3, counted the source's later displayed
relation slots as

    r_cond=n_H+6 m_H+k_H+133.                            (4)

It explicitly did not audit all supporting HNN constructions. The later
shared schedule substituted the independently hand-counted preembedding
data (m_H,n_H,k_H)=(499,17678,3), giving 20,808. Its three weaker finishes
gave 34,232, 37,937 and 49,792. Those are still correct substitutions into
the historical syntactic ledger; they are not certified embedding sizes.
The premise missing from that ledger is now explicitly refuted for the
printed Section 7.4/(7.10) construction, rather than simply unreviewed.

The affine recognizer, shared-ambient theorem and two-relator affine-line
transducer end before this defective step. Their mathematical scopes and
the 499/17678/3 schedule are unchanged by this counterexample. In
particular the local transducer uses an independently proved free pair;
it does not identify an independent orbit family with the dependent
product family in (2). Its materialization and word audit can proceed.

The established universal arithmetic result, 84 operations, degree 187
and 18 positive witnesses, is also unaffected. No group-based numerical
universal arithmetic improvement had been established from (4).

**Remark 3 (a conjugation substitution is not a completed repair).** The
tempting family v^(w(p,q)) is freely independent, but replacing the printed
product family by those conjugates changes the construction. In particular
v^-1*v^p=v^-1 p^-1 v p is not p in Free(p,q,v), so the word-extraction
identity used after the printed product assignment is lost. Merely changing
the multiplication dot to a superscript does not preserve the later proof
or any of its presentation counts.

**Open question 1 (replacement final embedding, credited to the source
construction and root's review).** Starting with the actual benign pair
for X_U, give an explicit faithful route to the required recursively
presented two-generator group, including the named images, complete finite
relators and exact matrix alphabet. The proposed route in source Section
7.4 was an isomorphism between the displayed indexed families followed by
finite HNN relations and subgroup extraction. Equations (1)–(3) show why
that argument does not suffice. A replacement normal-form argument or a
different effective embedding is needed before a downstream arithmetic
parameter is instantiated. This note does not refute the classical Higman
embedding theorem or rule out other constructions.
