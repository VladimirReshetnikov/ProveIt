## The useful new mechanism and its exact boundary

The first archive's `standard_integers_in_infinite_interval/article.tex` was read at lines182–367 (hypotheses and dyadic/standard-cut proof),666–711 (computable ring), and830–1048 (complete compiler, truth-complexity argument and Diophantine boundary), as well as its abstract and headings. Its complete README and SOURCE_AUDIT were also read. No external literature claim, correction to another paper, priority claim or archived test total was independently certified.

The ring hypotheses are a discretely ordered commutative unital ring, division by2 and3, and a normalized additive map to the actual ordinary integers. The manuscript's predicate

`Pow(y) := y>0 and forall u,v<=y, y != (2u+3)v`

recognizes the ordinary powers of two under those hypotheses. The derived `Std(x)` is zero or asks for such a power between x/2 and x. The supplied proof treats both zero and negative values of the additive character; it does not incorrectly assume that the character preserves order. This scoped elementary argument and the subsequent structural relativization read coherently.

With one positive infinite H, every source quantifier is relativized to `x<=H` guarded by `Std(x)`. Gate variables flatten source terms into equations `z=x+y` and `z=xy`. The manuscript explicitly keeps those existential gate blocks inside each atomic replacement, including under negation; it does not silently convert arbitrary Boolean formulas into a positive existential conjunction. All syntactic bounds become H, and every atom has degree at most two.

This is a new representation mechanism relative to the earlier README-only stream/topology triage, but its resource model differs fundamentally from the current paid-circuit task:

1. H is a positive infinite element of a nonstandard ordered ring, not a fixed natural numeral. The interval below it is externally infinite.
2. `Std` already contains bounded universal quantifiers. Negations and quantifier alternations remain in the compiled formula.
3. Each source sentence produces a different formula, with input-dependent syntax size, gate-variable count and quantifier depth. There is no single fixed formula taking arbitrary syntax codes as an ordinary input.
4. The example ring `Z+X Q[X]` has finite computable element codes, but their polynomial degrees and coefficient descriptions are unbounded. One ring multiplication is not identified with one elementary operation on a fixed tuple of ordinary integers.
5. “Quadratic atoms” does not price a full finalizer, standardness guards, domain coding or elimination of universal quantifiers. No complete fixed-arity existential natural-witness polynomial and operation ledger is supplied in these read sections.

The manuscript itself distinguishes equality-only Diophantine retractions from this alternating bounded truth problem at lines1033–1047. Conditional on its stated true-arithmetic equivalence, an effective uniform conversion of the entire fragment to ordinary existential polynomial solvability would be impossible: the latter is computably enumerable, while full true arithmetic is not. This observation is a boundary for that whole fragment, not an obstruction to useful restricted components.

The one modest transferable idea is the explicit local gate construction with correct placement beneath Boolean connectives. It is relevant to proof discipline for occurrence/query compilers, but it supplies no new arithmetic sharing identity or lower paid count by itself. Preserve this item as a scoped nonstandard-arithmetic compiler lead; do not prioritize a full topology audit for the current84-operation search. A future constructive proposal would need to identify a restricted existential interface and pay its ordinary finite coordinate representation explicitly.
