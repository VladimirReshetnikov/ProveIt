## 5. Retained correction, counterexamples and open scope

**Review remark 1 (support equality is stronger than the uniform template permits).** Primary proof line 60 states: “These are precisely the nonzero supports of the full signed action difference after the decoder”. This is too strong for the uniform relator template. A relator word representing the identity in the free-group matrix encoding, for example a redundant freely cancelling word aa^-1, has S(rho(R))-I3=0. Its actual difference support is empty, but the uniform source still retains ports 4,5,6,7 and charges their zero products. Such a redundant relation may also be adjoined without changing the presented group. The correct statement is: the retained sets contain all nonzero supports, and every omitted port has zero coefficient. The primary proof already states that zero relator products remain charged. Root accepted this correction and requested that its already frozen bytes remain unchanged, with this numbered correction attached. The valid support-containment statement is sufficient for every omission, identity, projection and count in this review.

**Review remark 2 (retained sparse mass counterexample).** The dense identity Ztot=S is false here: a single a+ cell with all seven coordinates 1 retains mass 6 instead of 7. This is a local selector-interface example, not an asserted accepted universal slice. The valid completeness proof uses Ztot<=S and the explicit positive global margin above. This preserves the primary and paid-action reports' numbered counterexample.

**Review remark 3 (witness correspondence remains scoped).** Sparse and dense ordinary-input projections agree, but their declared coordinates, packs and prescribed native scales differ. Fresh native witnesses and a different slack can be needed. A same-tuple correspondence is not established. Within either fixed wrapper, deleting or restoring the equal terminal copy F7=F1 remains a positive-coordinate bijection. No impossibility of other abstract encodings is claimed.

**Open question 1 (numerical universality and degree).** The actual universal relator list, its numerical r, and fixed coefficient values still need materialization. The present result closes the complete emitted-source and binding portion of the paid sparse-action packet's prior question, while preserving its numerical-instantiation portion. No new general84 bound, degree bound or arithmetic optimality claim follows. External group-universality and native foundation proofs are inherited, not re-audited here.

**Open question 2 (credited further savings).** Pascal's paired-generator/transvection sharing proposal and the earlier hatted global chart remain separate possible successors. Neither is charged into or deducted from this frozen source. Any saving requires its own exact action proof, private-consumer closure, positive-domain argument and complete source count.
