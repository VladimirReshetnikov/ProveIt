# A decidable nonabsorbing paired-carry class

Fix a radix b>=2 and integer coefficients alpha,beta,gamma,delta,epsilon,
zeta. For a numerical input x let eta(x) be any effectively computed integer.
Suppose

    Delta=alpha*delta-beta*gamma != 0.                  (1)

The existence of powers W=b^m and L=b^n, with a specified effective lower
bound on W, n>=1, and integers A,B satisfying

    1<=A,B<=L-1,
    (alpha*W+beta)A+(gamma*W+delta)B
        +epsilon*W*L+zeta*W+eta(x)=0                  (2)

is decidable, uniformly in these data. The endpoint coefficient epsilon
need not vanish. This is an exact theorem for independent append bounds;
it does not cover the additional joint bound A+B<L, coefficient
determinant zero, extra label filters, or nonlinear controller equations.

The proof supplies an effective polynomial bound on the duration power L
when Delta is nonzero, then reduces to ordinary Presburger arithmetic with
two powers as its only free coordinates. No two-parameter parametric
Presburger theorem is assumed.

## 1. Application to the paired FIFO components

For the [binary paired component](native_binary_pair_fifo52.md), put W=2K.
Twice its global carry equation after transport substitution is

    (2c0*W+2c2)A+(2c1*W+2c3)B
        +2(h-cf)WL+c1*W+2c0*x+2(cs-h)=0.              (3)

Thus Delta is four times `c0*c3-c1*c2`. The paid bounds and initialization
give exactly 1<=A,B<=L-1 and W>2x; every such pair supplies positive bounded
read streams. The power geometry supplies the remaining witnesses.

For the separate-bound [ternary component](native_ternary_pair_fifo50.md),
with initial pair(x,0), the equation is

    (2c0*W+2c2)A+(2c1*W+2c3)B
        +(h-2cf)WL+2c0*x+2cs-h=0.                    (4)

Here W>x. Both append streams are positive, so both read streams are
positive, including the coordinate initialized at zero. Consequently
(1) proves decidability of these entire unfiltered affine architectures
whenever `c0*c3-c1*c2!=0`, for arbitrary endpoints. It applies equally
after the [strong42 geometry saving](pell_kernel_power_geometry42.md).
It is not a claim about a controller with an independently paid edge filter.

## 2. Exact bounded-coin saturation, including the endpoints

Let a,b be positive integers, d=gcd(a,b), and M,N arbitrary integers.
Define

    C=ab/d,
    B0=C+2(a+b)+|N|+3.                               (5)

For every integer L>=B0, the bounded equation

    a*u+b*v=M*L+N,  0<=u,v<=L-2                     (6)

has the following exact classification:

* M<0 or M>a+b: no solution.
* 0<M<a+b: a solution exactly when d divides ML+N.
* M=0: a solution exactly when N is in the nonnegative semigroup
  generated by a,b.
* M=a+b: a solution exactly when -2(a+b)-N is in that semigroup.

Every endpoint semigroup question here is a finite calculation: a negative
target fails, and a nonnegative target H permits testing
0<=u<=floor(H/a). In particular no unbounded search is hidden at a zero
coefficient of L.

To prove the interior assertion set U=L-2 and T=ML+N. If

    U>=max(a,b)/d,  C<=T<=(a+b)U-C,  d|T,             (7)

the permitted real interval for v is

    [max(0,(T-aU)/b), min(U,T/b)].                    (8)

Its length is at least a/d: the four differences between its candidate
endpoints are U, T/b, ((a+b)U-T)/b and aU/b, all at least a/d by(7).
The congruence bv=T modulo a has one residue class modulo a/d. Interval(8)
therefore contains an admissible integer v in that class, and
u=(T-bv)/a is an integer in[0,U]. Condition(5) implies(7) whenever
0<M<a+b and d|T. Necessity of divisibility is immediate.

If M<0 then T<=-L+|N|<0. If M>a+b, the complementary target
`(a+b)U-T=(a+b-M)L-2(a+b)-N` is negative. At M=0 the target is N;
any nonnegative representation has u,v<=N<=U by(5). At M=a+b use
the complement (U-u,U-v), whose target is -2(a+b)-N, and the same bound.
The two endpoint cases cannot coincide since a+b>0. This proves all
cases, with the explicit common threshold(5).

## 3. An effective polynomial duration bound

First consider W for which both a0=alpha W+beta and b0=gamma W+delta
are nonzero. Put a=|a0|, b'=|b0| and d=gcd(a,b'). Condition(1) gives

    d divides |Delta|,                               (9)

by taking the two fixed integer linear combinations of a0,b0. Let
`neg_a=max(-a0,0)` and `neg_b=max(-b0,0)`. If a0>0 substitute A=1+u;
if a0<0 substitute A=L-1-u, and do the analogous substitution for B.
The independent bounds in(2) become 0<=u,v<=L-2, and the equation becomes

    a*u+b'*v=M(W)L+N(W,x),
    M=neg_a+neg_b-epsilon*W,
    N=-(a+b')-zeta*W-eta(x).                          (10)

All signs and endpoint cases of M are covered by Section2. Write
D=|Delta| and choose the explicit integer

    S=1+|alpha|+|beta|+|gamma|+|delta|+|epsilon|
        +|zeta|+|eta(x)|,
    K=S^2+4S+3.

Since a+b'<=S(W+1) and |N|<=2S(W+1), the threshold B0 in(5) satisfies

    B0 <= K(W+1)^2.                                  (11)

If an admitted power L is below B0 it is already bounded. The same is
true if its exponent n<D, since then L<b^D. In every remaining interior
case, admissibility depends only on L modulo d. The sequence b^n modulo d
has preperiod mu and period p with **mu+p<=d<=D**, since every state
before the first repetition is distinct. Set n0 to the first exponent
with b^n0>=B0, with no additional lower bound on n0. Every eventual-cycle
residue occurs among exponents n0,...,n0+D-1: if n0 precedes the cycle,
the remaining transient plus a full cycle has length at most mu+p; if
it is already in the cycle, p steps suffice. The admitted exponent n>=D
is in that cycle, so its residue has a representative L' in this range.
Since b^n0<b*B0, this representative satisfies L'<b^D*B0. Endpoint cases
are independent of L once L>=B0 and can use its first member. Thus whenever
there is any solution at W, there is one with

    L <= b^(D+1)*K(W+1)^2.                            (12)

The bound also covers the separately retained exponents n<D. Choosing
W>=4*b^(D+1)*K makes its right side at most W^3. This is an effective
bound depending on the numerical input, not an assertion of a uniform
small time bound for all inputs.

Each affine coefficient is not identically zero because Delta!=0.
Consequently only finitely many W make either coefficient zero. Retain
all W below

    W0=max(2, 4*b^(D+1)*K, 1+|beta|+|delta|).         (13)

as individual cases, together with the required input lower bound. For
W>=W0 both coefficients are nonzero and(12) permits L<=W^3. At any fixed
smaller W the remaining sentence is ordinary Presburger arithmetic in
A,B,L. Eliminate A,B; its resulting one-variable semilinear formula can
be tested on powers L by the elementary residue procedure below. This
also handles zero coefficients without division by zero.

## 4. Removing multiplication by the variable width

For W=b^m>=W0 and L=b^n<=W^3, either

    L=W^k V, k in{0,1,2}, V=b^u, 1<=V<W,

or L=W^3. Use k=3,V=1 for the last case. Express A,B in base W with
digits a_j,b_j, 0<=j<=k, all lower digits in[0,W-1] and top digits
in[0,V-1]. In the last case the top digits are zero. These conditions
are exactly 0<=A,B<L; require each digit sum positive to restore A,B>=1.

Expanding(2) as a formal polynomial in W gives at most five coefficients
E_0,...,E_4. For example

    E0=beta*a0+delta*b0+eta(x),
    Ej=alpha*a_(j-1)+gamma*b_(j-1)+beta*aj+delta*bj
         +epsilon*V*[j=k+1]+zeta*[j=1]   (j>=1),      (14)

with missing digits zero. Each Ej is an ordinary linear expression in
the digits and V; W no longer multiplies a digit inside Ej. The equation
is equivalent to integer carries

    Ej+carry_j=W*carry_(j+1),
    carry_0=carry_5=0.                               (15)

Taking S as above gives |Ej|<=SW. Starting at zero and W>=2, the actual
carries in(15) satisfy |carry_j|<=2S by induction. If the original sum
is zero, successively reducing it modulo W proves that these actual
carries are integers and the final carry is zero. Conversely(15)
telescopes to the original sum.

Enumerate the finitely many carries in[-2S,2S]. Each resulting system,
including its digit bounds, is ordinary Presburger arithmetic with free
coordinates W,V: the products W*carry are by enumerated constants.
Effective ordinary Presburger elimination of the digits gives a finite
Boolean combination of affine comparisons and divisibility by fixed
integers in W,V. The separate last case has V=1. All omitted small W
were handled in Section3; no unbounded digit length remains.

## 5. Effective restriction of an affine formula to two powers

Here is an elementary terminal algorithm. Given any finite Boolean
combination of atoms

    aW+cV+e>=0,    aW+cV+e=0,    M divides aW+cV+e,

decide it on W=b^(u+d), V=b^u, u>=0,d>=1. Equalities can be expressed
by two inequalities. For all comparison atoms choose D0>=1 so that
`b^D0>|c|+|e|`, and U0>=0 so that `b^U0>|e|`.

For d>=D0 and a!=0, the sign of
`(a*b^d+c)*b^u+e` is the sign of a, uniformly in u. If a=0 its sign
is constant for u>=U0, except for the directly evaluated zero coefficient.
For each d<D0 the integer a*b^d+c is fixed; if nonzero its sign controls
the expression for every u>=U0, and if zero the expression is just e.
These assertions follow from strict domination by the stated thresholds.

Let P be the least common multiple of the positive fixed moduli, with
P=1 if there are none. Compute the preperiod mu and period p of
`b^j mod P` by finite iteration. Increase U0,D0 to at least mu. In each
of the four regions (u below/above U0, d below/above D0), comparison truth
is fixed along the unbounded directions; congruence truth is periodic
with period p in those directions. It therefore suffices to test

    0<=u<U0+p,  1<=d<D0+p.                            (16)

This is a computable finite rectangle, with no coprimality assumption
on b and P. The one-power fixed-W case is the same argument in one
coordinate. Ordinary Presburger elimination is a standard effective
integer-linear quantifier elimination, not a parametric result; the
proof above explicitly handles the subsequent exponential restriction.
Together Sections2–5 prove the decision theorem.

## 6. What remains outside the theorem

When Delta=0, the two affine append coefficients can share a nonconstant
factor G(W). In that case their gcd need not have a fixed bound, and the
modular replacement in Section3 fails. After suitable fixed integer
scaling, the unresolved divisibility can take the shape

    vW+w divides a*L+c,

with W,L both radix powers. No decision theorem or universality claim
for that case is made here. Likewise the sign substitutions in(10) turn
a joint bound into a different polygon; the independent-box saturation
proof does not silently establish the joint-bound variant.

The [checker](pell_kernel_nonabsorbing_determinant.py) independently tests
the exact four-case saturation lemma, its sign normalization, gcd bound,
finite modular replacement, base-W carry identities, and the terminal
two-power algorithm. It does not implement general Presburger quantifier
elimination. The full decision procedure is proved above; finite tests
only check its specified elementary components. Default execution checks
the [receipt](pell_kernel_nonabsorbing_determinant.json). Independent full
proof/source/default review passed, including the repaired modular window
bound, all endpoint cases and the two-power terminal algorithm. No new
complete universal bound is claimed.
