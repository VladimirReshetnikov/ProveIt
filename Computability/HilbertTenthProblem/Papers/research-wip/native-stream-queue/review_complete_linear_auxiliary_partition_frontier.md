confirms six additional points in the explicit historical 18-witness union:
**90/98, 92/76, 93/60, 94/56, 95/50 and 96/44** (complete operations/exact
degree). The ten actual saved winner circuits and their complete leading

disclosed. The enumeration below is a separately implemented subset-mask

## 2. Independent enumeration and arithmetic objectives

The subset algorithm chooses the least remaining factor, selects every subset
of the other remaining factors to join its block, and recurses on the
complement. Each partition appears exactly once: its least-element block is
uniquely determined, and the same argument applies recursively. All 127
nonempty subset masks are available; no cost pruning occurs.

For seven factors this gives 877 partitions. Their counts by block count
1 through 7 are `1,63,301,350,140,21,1`. The sum of the block counts is 3263.
Every partition has one SOS and one anchor for each block, so each core has
4140 plans and the four cores have 16,560.

For k blocks, the block products consume `7−k` multiplications. The full SOS
uses k residual subtractions, k squares and k−1 additions. Consequently its
unfolded charge is `Mc+7` multiplications and `Ac+2k−1` additions. An anchor
with k>1 has the same charge: k−1 residuals/squares, k−2 summations, one +1,
one anchor multiplication and one final −1. The one-block anchor is exactly
`Mc+6,Ac+1`.

When Ns is a singleton, the identities

    (Ns−1)^2 = D^2,
    Ns*(1+S)−1 = D*(1+S)+S,    D=strong_difference

remove 2 additions in the squared/nonanchor case or 1 addition when Ns is the
anchor. The literal private Ns row justifies the first part of each saving.
Each core has 877 squared-fold plans, 203 anchor-fold plans and 3060 no-fold
plans. These counts, the complete operation/degree histogram, all cost minima,
and the finer author strata agree exactly.

If the block weights are d_j, the degree is `2 max d_j` for SOS,
`d_anchor+2 max_(other blocks) d_j` for a nontrivial anchor, and the total
weight for one block. The exactness proof is uniform: each group leader is a
product of nonzero actual factor leaders; the maximal square terms cannot
cancel as a sum of real polynomial squares. An anchor leader multiplies that
nonzero sum in an integral polynomial ring. Both singleton folds preserve the
polynomial, hence also its degree. This reasoning applies to every weighted
plan, not only the ten saved representatives.

## 4. Positive-zero transfer and historical comparison

At an SOS zero all group products equal 1. At an anchored integer zero,
`A*(1+S)=1` with S a nonnegative integer sum of squares, so A=1 and S=0.
Thus the full parent factor product is 1. On the unchanged positive supplied
domain, the parent theorem applies and gives all seven individual factors +1.
Conversely those seven equalities at any positive parent zero make every
selected finalizer vanish. The new and parent **positive zero tuples are
identical**, including ordinary input and every supplied coordinate.

The proof does not assume that an arbitrary integer product 1 makes all
factors positive. It does not claim general signed-integer or real zero-set
equivalence, nor full off-zero equality to the parent except for its
single-block product output. Grouping performs no new native positivity or
coordinate restoration step.

Taking only the pinned earlier 18-witness frontier and these four cores yields

    85/175,86/131,87/119,88/109,89/103,90/98,
    91/80,92/76,93/60,94/56,95/50,96/44.

The saved 91/90 source is dominated by the older 91/80 source in this union.
The six additions are the opening list. Other witness counts, unenumerated
coordinate changes and unrestricted circuit optimization are outside scope.
