## 2. Unique main congruence on the entire polynomial curve

Let R=2r+1 be any odd integer at least3 and define

    M_R(X)=sum_(j=0)^r binom(2r,r+j) X^j,
    Y_R(X)=M_R(X)/2,
    H_R(X)=4Y_R(X)(X+1)+3=2(X+1)M_R(X)+3.

For every integer X>=1, all coefficients of M_R are nonnegative and

    M_R(X)>=M_R(1)=(4^r+binom(2r,r))/2.

Consequently

    H_R(X)>=2*4^r+2*binom(2r,r)+3>2^R.

Also H_R(X)>X because M_R(X)>=1. Therefore

    |2^R-X|<H_R(X),
    H_R(X) divides 2^R-X  iff  X=2^R.                (1)

This proves the statement for every positive X, including X below2^R. It does not assume a compiler, Pell tuple, large-X estimate or integrality of Y_R(X). At even X, Y_R(X) is an integer and gives the half-binomial curve used in the80 construction.

Why this is exactly the missing main condition: at parameter A=a+2, the sequence E_A(j)=chi_A(j)-a*psi_A(j) has E_A(j)=2^j modulo H=4a+3. Once the retained first/main/auxiliary rank conditions identify c=psi_A(R), D=chi_A(R), an integral main quotient requires

    H divides D-a*c-X, equivalently H divides 2^R-X.

On this polynomial curve, (1) leaves no CRT room to alter X. The explicit80 family chose X strictly above2^R, so every such chosen member already fails the main quotient divisibility. Merely choosing a different small positive X on the same curve cannot help.

## 4. No dyadic rotation has transport target4

Take q=B^N for any positive N and an ordinary x>0 such that

    C=1+2^u<q, u=2d*x+b.

Then u<dN and b>0 imply 2x<N. The word is exactly

    C=1+r0*B^(2x),

with a Start bit at the origin and an End bit at cell2x. It is native typed, but no valid computation is claimed. Let e be any cyclic binary exponent; it suffices to take 0<=e<dN. Put

    Rword=B*C mod(q-1), Yword=2^e*C mod(q-1),
    Factual=DC*C+DR*Rword+Yword.                       (3)

Rword is a whole-cell cyclic shift and remains native typed. Its End may wrap to the origin when 2x+1=N; this is allowed. Yword is an arbitrary binary rotation, including within-inner-digit shifts.

The DC*C term itself does **not** wrap. Each of its terms is in cell0 or cell2x, with within-cell exponent at most maxdeg(DC)+1<=maxdeg(DC)+Emax<L. Since 2x<N, every term lies below the q boundary. Its coefficients are nonnegative and C has constant term1, so

    Factual>=DC*C>=DC>4.                              (4)

Section3's raw coefficient bound gives Factual<q-1. Moreover, because K=DC+B*DR,

    Factual=(K+2^e)C mod(q-1),                        (5)

where Factual is the strictly positive least representative. Equations (4)–(5) show

    (K+2^e)C != 4 mod(q-1) for every e.                (6)

The exceptional origin tests in the modified field mask do not invalidate this argument. They deliberately allow the supplied word F=4 because bit2 of MF0*J-1 is zero. Equation (6) uses the actual nonnegative field and its uniform coefficient bound, independently of which mask bits are tested. Allowing F=4 through the mask alone does not make it the field produced by transport.

For the root-gap80 family N and d are odd, so gcd(C,q-1)=1. In that special case (6) says its unique solved multiplier
