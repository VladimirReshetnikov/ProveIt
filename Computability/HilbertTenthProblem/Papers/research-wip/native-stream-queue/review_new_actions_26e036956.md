## Commuting injections: complete manuscript read

The classification treats nonempty connected **injective** actions, not necessarily faithful actions, of a finitely generated **commutative** monoid. Isomorphisms preserve named generators. Cancellativization is justified by injectivity, and localization gives invariant subsets of abelian quotients `G/H`, with isomorphisms exactly translations at the same H. Removing actual effective units, rather than merely syntactically invertible original generators, is necessary.

I checked the positive-height argument including torsion, the low-rank threshold description, the rigid Cantor construction and its finite-coordinate continuity, and the fixed-isotropy finite/continuum refinement. The continuum construction's height-zero trace fixes translation ambiguity. The finite-generation assumption is used essentially; the stated rational-ray counterexample correctly separates the nongenerated case.

The exact presentation test uses the nonnegative rational kernel of the relation matrix. Its unit certificate is an integer identity `A0^T z=e_j+v`, with v nonnegative. Clearing denominators from a rational separating certificate remains valid: an extra positive multiple of e_j is absorbed into v. The positive-circuit support argument and the span/rank formula are sound. The procedure decides the algebraic type-count branch; it does not decide arbitrary cardinal cofinality comparisons or arbitrary noncommutative group presentations. The manuscript explicitly leaves bit complexity and compact certificate size as further questions (lines1631–1638).

I also checked the finite-subgroup divisor count, infinite common-power threshold/gap normal form, necklace count, repetition/automorphism formula, defects, and labelled/unlabelled generating functions. The finite point count and infinite component count are different enumerations; no finite exhaustive table is used as a proof of infinite classification.

For the foundational applications, the ambient setting is a well-founded universe with an external atom set and Choice. The named operations are recursive lifts in a finite signature. The Replacement criterion is about the cardinality of the **union of entire small type blocks**; it cannot be shortened to merely having few component types. At the finite cutoff every component must additionally be finite. I checked the uniform recognition, finite-word counterexample, component-swap argument and singular-cardinal accounting. Collection/reflection use the stronger successor-cutoff/type-count criterion and growing reservoirs. The proof does not replace those reservoirs by a single fixed supply or import unrestricted truth for a proper-class structure. Universal statements over every action are kept distinct from the criterion for an individual action.

The forcing theorem is about new connected types for a fixed finite commutative presentation. It does not assert preservation of an individual kernel model's schemes when forcing changes cardinalities or cofinalities. No counterexample or unproved transition was found in this complete proof read. This remains an ordinary mathematical review, not formal certification or an independent literature-priority audit.
