height is unsound for the actual inc2/dec2 source. The three sources whose

## Entire source and identity checks

For all four actual sources, independent consumer scans establish that the
maintained time input occurs only in the height and clock right side. The
private height register has no comparison consumer. Replacing the two
height additions by the one addition `h=n0+eta` retains every other source
row, all 19 comparisons and the exact 56-operation SOS finalizer.

The four syntactic complete totals are 591, 466, 464 and 467, with all 1,988
paid gates live. These counts describe the proposed circuits, not a claim
that the incdec circuit has the intended clock semantics. No maintained
unsafe compiler is added.

At the old height substitute `eta_old=eta-T`. Independent exact coefficient
expansion proves that its value becomes `n0+eta`. The sole-consumer checks
then transfer this identity through every downstream operand and the whole
polynomial. This is a signed identity; negative restored slack is allowed
for the algebra and does not license using a positive-parent theorem there.

Separately, the actual private clock cone is

    clock_word = (B-1)*(clock_quotient_hat-1)+T.

The new height, radix and all other source cones are independent of `T`
and `clock_quotient_hat`. Independent coefficient expansion gives

    (B-1)*(clock_quotient_hat-2)+T+(B-1)
      = (B-1)*(clock_quotient_hat-1)+T.

Therefore `T -> T+(B-1)` and `clock_quotient_hat -> clock_quotient_hat-1`
preserve the entire proposed polynomial and every native input. Positivity
is retained whenever the starting hat is at least two. The checker also
performs 32 independent complete numerical identity checks, including eight
rational assignments, checking each retained comparison difference as well
as the final output. These supplement the exact local identities and
literal source argument.

    T = 616+(B-1) = 137438954087,

## One-step scope and replay

The actual zero3, nop and positive3 tables have a different structure:
initial control goes directly to halt or trap, and every noninitial
control goes to trap. Their slopes are divisible by the control modulus,
so this control statement is independent of the residue quotient. A path
ending at halt therefore has exactly one step. Its tick is below `B-1`;
natural `T` and a positive quotient hat force hat one and the correct time.
The clock shift would leave the positive domain. A separate bounded
one-step source proof can certify these exceptions; they add no
unbounded-computation benefit.
