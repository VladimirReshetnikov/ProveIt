# Seven-dimensional unrestricted mortality with a two-operation affine input

The universal paired-vector construction has a **seven-dimensional**
unrestricted mortality adapter whose single varying matrix is loaded in
**2=1M+1A** operations from the ordinary positive input. This reduces the
dimension of the [9D affine adapter](group_affine_guarded_mortality9.md)
without increasing its loader cost.

The physical scalar changes: instead of two squares, it is an asymmetric
weighted sum of the two signed projective coordinates. A strict bound
prevents cancellation unless both coordinates vanish. Four physical
coordinates therefore suffice. Three more coordinates retain the same
loading-word guard, with its magnitude proved by bounds on entire runs
of input letters. A one-letter norm bound would be false.

This is compatible with the
[exact-series rank-nine obstruction](group_guarded_mortality_rank_obstruction.md):
the new scalar has the same relevant zeros, but different values. No
minimal-dimension assertion or improvement to the numerical75/88
Diophantine bounds is made. The universal numerical alphabet is still
inherited abstractly. Section6 states the paid arithmetic and remaining
word-certificate boundaries.

## 1. The fixed paired alphabet and the new separation scalar

Import the exact ordinary-input contract from
[the projective group theorem](group_projective_zero_mortality6.md).
It supplies one finite signed alphabet of pairs (P_i,Q_i) in Gamma times
Gamma, generating a fixed subgroup K_U. Gamma has diagonal entries1
modulo4 and lower-left entry0 modulo4. For a fixed program p and x>0,

    alpha=12*2^(p+1), beta=12*2^p,
    t=alpha*x+beta+1, v=(-1,t),

the program accepts x exactly when some paired word satisfies

    (P_word v)_1=(Q_word v)_1=0,
    equivalently P_word v=Q_word v=e2.                  (1)

The construction below needs only t>=3 for its bounds. The universal
corollary retains these compatible fixed program constants and K_U.

For a matrix M let ||M||_infinity be its maximum absolute row sum.
Precompute the positive integer constants

    lambda_i=||P_i||_infinity,
    F_i=diag(P_i,lambda_i Q_i).                         (2)

For a selected word, write Lambda=product(lambda_i) along its letters.
Its four-dimensional physical action is
`diag(P_word,Lambda Q_word)`. Submultiplicativity gives
`||P_word||_infinity<=Lambda`.

Crucially, write the first row of P_word as (p,q). Its p is1 modulo4,
so p is nonzero. Because t>1,

    |(P_word v)_1|=|-p+tq|
       <=|p|+t|q|<t(|p|+|q|)
       <=t||P_word||_infinity<=t Lambda.                (3)

Consequently the integer scalar

    s=(P_word v)_1+t Lambda*(Q_word v)_1                (4)

is zero exactly when both coordinates in (1) vanish. If the second is
nonzero, its weighted absolute value is at least t Lambda, which strictly
exceeds the first. If the second is zero, (4) is the first coordinate.
The scalar need not be nonnegative away from its zeros.

The subgroup restriction is substantive. For
`P=[0,-1;1,0]`, `Q=-I`, one has lambda=1 and the two first coordinates
are (-t,1). Their scalar (4) is zero for every t, while neither coordinate
is zero. These matrices do not satisfy the required first-block subgroup
condition. The construction is not asserted for arbitrary SL2 pairs.

## 2. One affine input letter loads both vectors

Define the two invertible integral input blocks

    A_t=[0,-1;1,t],    B_t=[0,-1;t,t],
    T4(t)=diag(A_t,B_t), z4=(1,0,1,0), u4=(1,0,1,0).

Their determinants are1 and t, and direct multiplication gives

    A_t^2 e1=(-1,t)=v,
    B_t^2 e1=(-t,t^2)=t v.                            (5)

Thus a chronological prefix of exactly two T4 letters, followed by any
fixed F_i word, yields precisely (4) under the row u4. Only the entries
0,-1,1,t occur in T4; no t-squared or t Lambda entry has to be loaded.
The quadratic coordinate in (5) is generated by the word itself.

Later chronological letters multiply on the left. Every fixed F_i is
invertible over Q, with determinant lambda_i^2. It is an integer matrix;
integer unimodularity is not needed for the rank-one reset lemma.
An original inverse group letter is encoded with its own positive
lambda_i, rather than by taking the inverse of a scaled F_i. The
original paired products still range over K_U, and Lambda records the
additional scalar factors exactly.

## 3. The run bounds which avoid another input gate

For every t>=3 and k>=0,

    ||T4(t)^k||_infinity<=2t^k,
    ||T4(t)^k z4||_infinity<=t^k.                      (6)

Here the vector norm is maximum absolute coordinate. We prove the
estimates directly, including the boundary t=3.

Let a_0=0,a_1=1 and a_(k+1)=t a_k-a_(k-1). Induction gives
`0<=a_k<=t^(k-1)` for k>=1: the sequence is positive and increasing,
and the subtracted previous term is nonnegative. For k>=1,

    A_t^k=[-a_(k-1),-a_k; a_k,a_(k+1)].                (7)

Now let b_0=0,b_1=1 and b_(k+1)=t(b_k-b_(k-1)). For t>=4,
`b_k>=2b_(k-1)>=0` propagates by induction. Hence
`0<=b_(k+1)<=t b_k`, and `b_k<=t^(k-1)` for k>=1. Its matrix identity is

    B_t^k=[-t b_(k-1),-b_k; t b_k,b_(k+1)].            (8)

For t=3 the terms can change sign, so this positive argument is not used.
Instead B_3^6=-27I. Equivalently `b_(k+6)=-27b_k`; the initial values
`b_1,...,b_6=1,3,6,9,9,0` satisfy `|b_k|<=3^(k-1)`.
Each six-step advance multiplies the absolute value by27, whereas the
bound grows by3^6. Thus the same absolute bound holds for every k.

The absolute row sums of (7)-(8) are at most2t^k. Their first columns
are bounded by t^k, proving (6), with k=0 checked directly.
It would be incorrect to replace this proof by `||T4(t)||<=t`:
its second block has an absolute row sum of2t.

For each fixed physical letter precompute

    mu_i=2||F_i||_infinity
        =2||P_i||_infinity||Q_i||_infinity.             (9)

Consider an arbitrary chronological word, including wrong loading
orders, and split it into runs

    T4^k0 F_i1 T4^k1 ... F_im T4^km.

Apply the initial-column bound in (6) to its first run. At each subsequent
run apply the operator bound, charging its factor2 to the preceding
fixed letter through (9). Zero-length runs merely leave slack. This gives

    ||physical_word z4||_infinity<=rho,
    |u4 physical_word z4|<=2rho,
    rho=t^(number of input letters)*product(mu_i).     (10)

No run length or product rho is supplied as an untyped arithmetic
witness here. These are exact properties of the matrix word and its
control block. All lambda_i and mu_i are fixed compiler numerals.

## 4. Three guard coordinates handle every word

Use the previous guard matrices

    C_T=[1,0,0;0,1,0;1,1,1],
    C_F=[1,0,0;3,1,0;0,0,1], c=(1,0,-2).

If n is the number of input letters, f the number of fixed letters, and
I the number of ordered fixed-before-input pairs, their product sends
c to `(1,3f,G)`, where `G=n-2+3I`. The integer G is zero exactly for
chronological words `T T F*`: n=0,1 cannot make it zero; n>=3 makes
it positive; n=2 requires I=0. Both control matrices have determinant1.

The full seven-dimensional letters and boundaries are

    D_T(t)=diag(T4(t),t C_T),
    D_i=diag(F_i,mu_i C_F),
    z7=(z4,1,0,-2), u7=(u4,0,0,4), R=z7 u7.           (11)

Their exact scalar identity on every word is

    u7 D(word) z7=u4 physical_word z4+4rho G.           (12)

If G is nonzero, (10) makes the second term strictly larger in absolute
value than the first. Thus every incorrectly loaded word has nonzero
scalar, including words with zero or one input letter and words with
negative guard. If G=0, (5) and (4) show that the scalar vanishes exactly
for a paired word satisfying (1). The empty F suffix gives `-(t+1)`,
so it does not accept. This contrasts with scalar2 for the same `TT`
word in the 9D adapter, explicitly demonstrating why its exact-series
rank obstruction does not apply to the new function.

The input determinant is t^4. Every other non-reset letter is also
invertible, and the fixed rank-one reset satisfies

    u7 z7=-6, R^2=-6R.

For a product with resets, each interior segment contributes the scalar
in (12). Its invertible outer segments give nonzero exterior vectors.
The whole product is zero if and only if at least one interior scalar
is zero. Products with zero or one reset cannot be zero. Conversely
a zero scalar gives `R D(word) R=0`. Therefore arbitrary repeated resets
preserve the same common paired witness; different one-block zero
words cannot erase different blocks independently.

Combining (1)-(12) proves, for the one fixed universal alphabet,

    x is accepted by program p
      iff {D_i,R,D_T(alpha*x+beta+1)} generates the zero matrix.

If the inherited signed alphabet has s letters, there are s+1 fixed
matrices and exactly one input-dependent matrix. All are integral.
Their dimension is seven, their input dependence is affine, and no
restriction on the number or positions of resets is imposed.

## 5. Exact loader cost and fixed-data conventions

The entire49-entry input matrix in (11) uses only0,-1,1,t. Its literal
input source is

    scaled=alpha*x, t=scaled+(beta+1),

costing **2=1M+1A**, with zero existential witnesses or comparisons.
All repeated entries are register copies. Fixed signed integer numerals
are allowed, as in the parent matrix packets; no input-dependent
negation or multiplication by a fixed numeral is omitted. Computing
beta+1, lambda_i and mu_i is compilation of fixed program data. In
particular the growth factor is t, not a secretly uncharged2t.

The construction improves the 9D affine-loader2 interface to7D at the
same loader cost. The older6D interface still has a three-operation
quadratic varying matrix; these are different dimension/input tradeoffs.
No claim about a globally smallest mortality dimension follows.

## 6. Uniform Diophantine boundary and finite evidence

The proof reduces unbounded mortality existence exactly to the same
paired-vector endpoint used by the paid four-history compiler. Thus its
uniform arithmetic theorem transfers without adding witnesses for
Lambda, rho or the three guard coordinates: these quantities have been
eliminated from the existence predicate by (3), (10) and (12).

For example, the named
[shared-history/strong-unit compiler](group_projective_strong_unit_product.md)
gives its existing279-operation, degree3502 illustration with42 positive
witnesses under the same fixed-table and padded-program hypotheses.
This is an imported compiler result, not a new gate saving or a numerical
universal alphabet. The 9D packet's paid selected-duration certificates
also remain available for the equivalent paired endpoint. Neither
transfer certifies a separately supplied arbitrary7D matrix word, its
duration, or all its reset positions. Such an explicit word interface
would still need its own paid selected-history encoding.
