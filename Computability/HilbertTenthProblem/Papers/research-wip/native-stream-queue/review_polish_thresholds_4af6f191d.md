## Mathematical challenge and exact hypotheses

**Part XIII.** The normal form uses a countable torsion-free pointed group `(E,u)` with torsion-free divisible `E/Zu`, the usual-real/discrete product topology, and a Baire-property positive cone. The Baire real-order argument supplies the usual or reverse real orientation. The real convex hull and relative standard part give the pure subgroup `A`, quotient order and real cut offsets; countable extension of the real homomorphism removes those offsets by a continuous shear. No splitting of an arbitrary divisible quotient is assumed. The isomorphism proof recovers the ordered extension, and the displayed positive-real dilations establish non-rigidity in this presentation class.

The linear-order reduction uses finite supports and locally constant enumeration choices. Its complexity conclusion is Borel completeness **for isomorphism of countable structures in the explicit normal-data codes**, with a lower bound on the continuous-operation codes. It is not classification of every raw Borel presentation or a computable equality test. Friedman–Stanley/non-Borelness are imported foundations, not reverified here; the fixed-manifold theorem invoked in the reduction is outside the selected proof-read spans.

Section 182's new unit shear and straightening of abstract isomorphisms check algebraically. “Abstract isomorphism is topological” means existence of a continuous isomorphism when an abstract one exists; the text explicitly does not claim that every abstract isomorphism is continuous. Its locally compact classification retains **continuous partial subtraction**, and depends on earlier Parts IX/XII group-completion and inherited-cone classification theorems. I read the new composition proof, not a fresh proof audit of those earlier theorems. No conclusion for all locally compact additive monoids is inferred.

The finite-witness lemma correctly assumes invariance under the entire coset `mG`, not merely standard shifts. The quantifier-elimination rescaling uses torsion-free cancellation and ordinary fixed integer moduli. It transfers additive first-order arithmetic uniformly over the stated groups. The omnific realization is explicitly additive and fails ring closure (`omega^3` is present, `omega^6` is absent).

The complete valuation topology is Polish exactly for countable exponent groups. Real closedness uses the explicitly imported residue-characteristic-zero defectless theorem; elementary-subfield claims additionally use real-closed-field quantifier elimination. Those external inputs and Conway normal-form foundations remain dependencies.

Given those inputs, rational isolating intervals force coefficient fixing, the natural valuation gives positive scalar rescaling, and the exact metric relation gives closed image. The finite-rank argument has a genuine uniform positive valuation increment over a finite basis, so its Neumann inverse converges.
