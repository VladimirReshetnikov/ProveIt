## Full zero relation and degrees

The actual complete synchronized source is

    Q(z)^2 + ||DX' - X L_K(z)||^2 + ||DY' - Y L_G(z)||^2.

Over the reals every term is nonnegative. A zero forces one of the96
nodes, then D>0 cancels from all four residuals. Thus the same index
selects K and G, and precisely the paired table action is recovered.
Conversely every table action has its integer node as a witness. This
proves both real and integer existential equivalence. It does not require
unique tile recovery from endpoints, nor equality of the old and new
polynomials away from zero.

negative Q at integer nonnodes. The packet retains it in both domains.
