# What deleting the input Pell gap actually permits

Deleting `c=kappa+phi` from the complete
[81-operation source](FIXED_RAW_UNIVERSAL_81_PROOF.md) removes one
addition, one positive coordinate and one equation. The remaining source
has **80 operations: 44M+36A, 32 positive coordinates and 20 equations**.
This note proves its exact input-bridge projection and proves that the
deleted inequality is not implied by the remaining full source.

It does **not** establish an 80-operation universal certificate, and it
does **not** exhibit a false raw input for that deleted source. Allowing
a larger Pell index is different from accepting a different raw input.
The distinction is essential here: the main parameter is fixed by the
actual packed index, rather than freely chosen after an alias is found.

The [checker](../verification/explore_input_pell_gap_projection.py) and
[receipt](../verification/explore_input_pell_gap_projection.json) verify
the entire deleted source, finite instances of the exact projection,
positive larger-index witnesses, and two small actual-parameter cases.
The published 81-operation files are unchanged.

## 1. The retained equations

Use the notation of the 81-operation proof, and put

    A=a+2, Delta=A^2-1, M=a+1, H=4a+3,
    J0=2r+1, c=psi_A(J0), u=d*x,

where d is the fixed cell bit width, rather than the main kernel's
coordinate named `d`. The unchanged outer bounds and main kernel give

    0<u<q<J0<M, 0<W<q<H, 2^u<2^q<a<H.             (1)

Only the gap equation and its positive coordinate phi are removed.
The remaining input equations are

    kappa=u+delta*M,
    mu^2=1+Delta*kappa^2,
    mu=W+a*kappa+rho*H,                             (2)

with kappa, mu, delta and rho positive integers. All other equations,
including the raw bound `C+alpha+d*x=q`, are retained. No replacement
inequality is supplied for free.

Let T be the multiplicative order of 2 modulo the odd positive integer
H, and let g=gcd(M,T). These numbers are used to describe the projection;
computing them is not part of a proposed certificate.

## 2. Exact projection of the gap-free bridge

For fixed a>=2 and positive u,W, equations (2) have positive integer
witnesses if and only if

    W belongs to 2^u <2^M> modulo H.                (3)

The notation on the right denotes the coset obtained by multiplying
2^u by the subgroup generated by 2^M in the units modulo H. Equivalently,
there is an integer v with

    v=u mod M, 2^v=W mod H.                         (4)

These equivalences hold without imposing the upper bounds (1); those
bounds remain important when interpreting this statement in the full
source.

For necessity, positive solutions of the Pell norm have

    kappa=psi_A(v), mu=chi_A(v), v>0.

The standard recurrences, with psi_A(0)=0 and psi_A(1)=1, give

    psi_A(v)=v mod M,
    chi_A(v)-a*psi_A(v)=2^v mod H.                  (5)

For the first congruence, A=1 modulo M. For the second, substitute a
for the formal square root of Delta, since Delta-a^2=H and A-a=2.
Reducing (2) therefore gives (4), which is equivalent to (3).

Conversely choose any integer v satisfying (4). Adding an arbitrary
multiple of L=lcm(M,T) preserves both congruences. Choose the multiple
large enough that v is positive and

    psi_A(v)>max(u,W).

Set kappa=psi_A(v), mu=chi_A(v), and

    delta=(kappa-u)/M,
    rho=(mu-a*kappa-W)/H.                           (6)

Congruences (5) make these integers. Strict positivity of delta is
immediate, while

    mu-a*kappa=2*psi_A(v)-psi_A(v-1)>psi_A(v)>W

gives rho>0. The Pell norm holds by construction. This proves full
positive sufficiency, rather than merely a modular necessary condition.

If W is already known to be a power 2^w, criterion (3) simplifies to

    g divides w-u.                                  (7)

Indeed (4) becomes the pair v=u mod M, v=w mod T, whose exact Chinese
remainder compatibility condition is (7). A residue class can always
be lifted to a sufficiently large positive v, so the positivity in
(6) introduces no extra restriction.

## 3. What this says about actual raw inputs

Fix any genuine 81-operation witness for accepted raw input y. Keep
its entire encoded cyclic word and its actual main kernel fixed. In
particular keep q,C,W, a, c, r and all compiler numerals fixed. Then

    W=B^y=2^(d*y).

For another positive raw input x, the only necessary change outside
the input Pell coordinates is

    alpha=q-C-d*x.

The deleted source admits positive replacement input coordinates
exactly when

    d*x<q-C,  g divides d*(y-x).                    (8)

Equivalently, put e=g/gcd(g,d). The allowed raw inputs in this fixed
outer fiber are precisely the positive x satisfying

    x=y mod e, d*x<q-C.                             (9)

Necessity and sufficiency both follow from Section 2 and the retained
strict raw bound. No main kernel coordinate needs to change; its a is
the particular value determined by this witness's packed r.

If an x satisfying (8) lay outside the accepted set of this same fixed
compiler, it would be a complete false-input witness for the deleted
source. This note does not establish such an x for any actual compiler
witness. General small-parameter examples, or a freely chosen prime H,
cannot supply that missing assertion. Nor is (9) claimed to classify
all witnesses of the deleted source: it describes the fibers above
genuine original outer witnesses, for which W is already a canonical
power. Criterion (3) remains valid for arbitrary retained tuples.

## 4. The gap is not implied by the other full equations

There is nevertheless an unconditional statement at the actual main
parameter of every genuine 81-operation witness. Keep the same raw
input x, endpoint W=B^x and all outer and main-kernel coordinates.
For any sufficiently large positive integer ell choose

    v=u+ell*lcm(M,T)>J0.

Use the Pell coordinates and quotients (6). They are all positive,
satisfy every retained equation, and have

    kappa=psi_A(v)>psi_A(J0)=c.

Consequently no positive phi can restore `c=kappa+phi` for this tuple.
There are infinitely many such retained-source witnesses. Thus the
gap is genuinely not a consequence of the other full-source equations,
even when a comes from the exact special main kernel and the compiler
and raw input are fixed.

This construction keeps the correct endpoint power and the accepted
raw input. It proves nonredundancy of a witness-size constraint, not
failure of existential input-set equivalence. In particular it does
not settle whether the 80-operation deletion is a universal encoding.

## 5. The exact source and bounded checks

The executable schedule imports the frozen 81-operation schedule and
removes precisely

    pell_gap=kappa+phi,
    c=pell_gap,
    phi from the positive coordinate list.

Every one of the remaining 80 primitive instructions and 20 source
residuals is checked symbolically. The unchanged auxiliary Pell
residual still uses the same acyclic correction by the preceding norm
equation. All 44 multiplications remain; additions/subtractions fall
from 37 to 36. Fixed numerals and equality comparisons remain free.

The finite projection checks cover a=2,...,89, bounded u,W and exponent
pairs. Direct residue enumeration agrees with (3) and with the CRT
criterion (7). Materialized Pell examples at a=64,90,128,256,512 keep
the endpoint correct and give positive quotients with kappa>c. Their
index bounds are u<q<J0<M, but these illustrative parameters are not
asserted to be full compiler witnesses.

A separate general-parameter example uses

    a=1024, M=1025, H=4099, T=4098, g=1,
    u=4, W=256=2^8, q=512, J0=600.

It satisfies u<q<J0<M, W<q<A<H and the direct bound 2^u<A. CRT gives
a positive Pell index with v=4 mod1025 and v=8 mod4098, producing the
wrong endpoint for u=4. The checker verifies the two Pell congruences
by modular powering; Section 2 supplies positive full coordinates.
This parameter does not satisfy the actual main growth bound 2^q<a
and is not a full packed-kernel counterexample.

Finally two exact special parameters are obtained from

    X=2^(2r+1),
    Y=sum(j=0,...,r) binom(2r,j)*X^(r-j),
    a=Y*(X+1).

At r=1 one gets a=90 and g=1. At r=3 one gets g=5. The checker records
the exact integers, factors H and verifies the minimal order T. These
cases disprove the blanket claim that the special shape of a always
forces g=1. Both packed indices are outside the native compiler's
preliminary bounds, so neither is evidence of a false raw input for
the full deleted source.

This projection result does not lower the established complete universal
bound; the separate [80-operation construction](FIXED_RAW_UNIVERSAL_80_PROOF.md)
retains the gap. Deciding whether
the deleted source has false raw inputs requires control of (8) at
actual compiler-scale parameters, or a different analysis of the
remaining outer equations. That issue is unresolved here.
