# A six-operation Rule 110 component with one auxiliary field

This is a local arithmetic improvement, not a complete history or universal
certificate. The four input/output planes are already assumed to be aligned
Boolean radix-sixteen words. Their Booleanity, alignment, geometry, initial
and terminal interfaces, and the supplied repunit are not charged in the
six local operations below. Numerals and equality tests are free.

The exact checker is
`../verification/explore_rule110_three_bit_field.py`, with an adjacent JSON
receipt. No packed multiplication is interpreted as coordinatewise AND.
Author and two independent complete proof/source/dependency reviews and
fresh checks pass, with no findings. The review scope is the conditional
local and mask components proved here.

## 1. One scalar membership predicate

For Boolean a,b,c,y, put

    u=1+a+3(b+c)+4y,
    S={0,1,2,3,8,9,10,11}.                       (1)

Then u belongs to S if and only if y is Rule 110's output. The complete
table gives both possible outputs, so it proves both implications:

| a b c | Required y | u at required y | u at opposite y |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 0 0 | 0 | 1 | 5 |
| 0 0 1 | 1 | 8 | 4 |
| 0 1 0 | 1 | 8 | 4 |
| 0 1 1 | 1 | 11 | 7 |
| 1 0 0 | 0 | 2 | 6 |
| 1 0 1 | 1 | 9 | 5 |
| 1 1 0 | 1 | 9 | 5 |
| 1 1 1 | 0 | 8 | 12 |

The set S consists exactly of the four-bit digits with bit 2 absent. Its
other three bits are unrestricted. Thus this uses one auxiliary digit field
with eight allowed values, rather than two separately Boolean fields.
It does not contradict the classification in
`EXPLORATION_RULE110_ONE_MASK_FIELD.md`, which concerns only a four-value
set generated by two independently allowed bit positions.

## 2. Exact packed equation and local cost

Let N>=1, q=16^N and J=(q-1)/15. Suppose A,B,C,Y have N radix-sixteen
digits, each zero or one. Define

    U=J+A+3(B+C)+4Y.                              (2)

Each raw digit on the right lies between 1 and 12. There is no carry, and
0<U<q. Therefore (2), together with

    U & (4J) = 0,                                (3)

is equivalent to Rule 110 at every corresponding cell. The ampersand in
(3) denotes an ordinary binary bitwise intersection predicate; it is not
one of the paid arithmetic operations. Its implementation is additional.
The unique U is positive even if all four supplied planes are zero.

The explicit straight-line schedule is

    T=B+C, T3=3T, Y4=4Y,
    V=A+T3, VY=V+Y4, U=J+VY.

It costs **6=2 multiplications + 4 additions**. The older independent
aligned-plane component cost 7=1 multiplication + 6 additions and supplied
four Boolean auxiliaries. This comparison excludes both constructions'
digit-test and repunit costs. Fewer fields do not by themselves prove a
smaller total mask/kernel cost.

If J is supplied and q-1 is not already available, its definition takes
one subtraction q-1 and one multiplication 15J, followed by their free
equality test. It is not a free numeral because N varies.

If the spatial shifts A=16B and B=16C have already been established,
substitution into (2) gives

    U=307C+4Y+J.                                 (4)

Computing (4) costs **4=2M+2A**. This identity is literal integer
substitution; the existing alignment equations and appropriate zero
boundaries must still be retained. For N-digit Boolean A,B,C, the highest
two cells of C must be blank. Equation (4) alone does not establish that
hypothesis or the shifts. This is separate from the published
79-operation radix-eight endpoint system; no endpoint bijection or new
complete-system count is asserted here.

## 3. A single explicit mask can accommodate the different digit sets

The unequal allowed digit sets do not require logically separate bitwise
tests. Suppose 0<=U,Y,W<q, where W is a further plane whose desired
radix-sixteen digits are zero or one. Put

    P=U+qY+q^2W,
    M=J(4+14q+14q^2), L=q^3.                     (5)

There is no overlap between these bounded fields. Consequently

    P & M = 0

holds exactly when every digit of U belongs to S and every digit of Y,W
belongs to {0,1}. The first field forbids bit 2; the other fields forbid
bits 1,2,3. In particular, popcount(M)=7N and log2(L)=12N.
Each inequality in the premise matters: (5) is not a proof of those field
bounds, and computing P and M is additional arithmetic.

The following literal schedules make that additional cost explicit:

    qW=q*W, YqW=Y+qW, qYqW=q*YqW, P=U+qYqW;
    qp1=q+1, qqp1=q*qp1, M14=14*qqp1,
    Mfactor=M14+4, M=J*Mfactor.

Packing costs 4=2M+2A, and forming the mask costs 5=3M+2A, for
**9=5M+4A** together.
If q^2 is already available for a separately charged power schedule,
replace the first two mask instructions by q2pq=q^2+q. The mask then
costs 4=2M+2A, and the combined conditional cost is **8=4M+4A**.
Neither number includes the repunit, bounds or disjointness test itself.

Alternatively, if n=q^3 is already computed, supplying positive M and
checking

    15M+10q+4=14n

costs 5=3M+2A. This follows by multiplying (5) by 15 and cancelling
(q-1)(4+14q+14q^2)=14q^3-10q-4. For integer q>=2 the right-hand
solution for M is positive. The identity alone does not force q to be a
power of sixteen, and the local formula still needs J. These counts are
alternative conditional mask interfaces, not terms of a finished total.

For completeness, the exact existing central-binomial interface applies
to this nonuniform mask too. Define

    r=(L-P)(L-1)+M, D0=2^(19N).                   (6)

For 0<=P<L, one has

    D0 divides binom(2r,r) iff P & M = 0.         (7)

Here is a proof that includes overflow. Write b=log2(L) and pc for
popcount. If P+M<L, then

    r=(L-P-1)L+(P+M),
    pc(r)=b-pc(P)+pc(P+M)<=b+pc(M).

Equality holds exactly when adding P and M produces no carries, namely
P & M=0. If P+M>=L, then P>0 and

    r=(L-P)L+(P+M-L),
    pc(r)=b-pc(P-1)+pc(P+M)-1.

Since pc(P-1)=pc(P)-1+v2(P), and the overflowing addition has at least one
carry, this is at most b+pc(M)-1-v2(P), strictly below the threshold.
Finally v2(binom(2r,r))=pc(r), by the factorial valuation formula. This
proves (7) with threshold b+pc(M)=19N.

The exponent 19N is intentional. With q=16^N, D0 is not an integral
power of q. An eventual arithmetic realization must pay for appropriate
power coordinates, the heterogeneous mask M, every required bound,
Pell-kernel prerequisites and its converse parity. Formula (7) supplies
none of those for free. In particular, it is not a completed replacement
for the mask used in the existing 79-operation history.

## 4. A related native ternary digit option

The same exact finite search also found

    z=3+a+4b+4c+2y in {0,1,3,4,9,10,12,13}        (8)

if and only if the Boolean tuple is a Rule 110 transition. The allowed
set is precisely the three-trit words with every trit zero or one. The
raw side is at most 14, so packing the cells in radix 27 is carry-free.
Writing J27=(27^N-1)/26 gives

    Z=3J27+A+4(B+C)+2Y.

This costs 7=3M+4A when J27 is available, or 5=3M+2A after valid spatial
shifts A=27B, B=27C absorb the neighbor expression into 841C.

The required A,B,C,Y have digits {0,1} in radix 27. Testing them only
for ordinary ternary Booleanity would allow digits 3,4,9,10,12,13 within
each cell and invalidate the Boolean truth-table argument. Thus (8) is
not a direct solution to the known failed ternary Rule 110 transplant.

## 5. Evidence and scope

The checker exhaustively enumerates all affine projections into each of
the two fixed eight-value sets above. The values of the true rows 0000,
0011,0101,0111,1000 determine all five affine coefficients uniquely.
There are 8^5=32,768 assignments for either set; it finds 66 binary-set
projections and 18 ternary-set projections, including (1) and (8).
This is complete for these fixed sets, not a lower bound on other sets,
nonlinear relations or whole certificates.

It also checks both scalar truth tables, every three-cell input/output
assignment for (2) and its ternary counterpart, the spatially absorbed
identities on finite blank-padded rows, the literal schedules and their
counts, all 4,096 one-cell three-field mixed-mask words, and multi-cell
valid masks with every forbidden-bit corruption. The receipt separates
these finite checks from the general proofs above. The whole universal
interface and any complete operation total remain **OPEN**.
