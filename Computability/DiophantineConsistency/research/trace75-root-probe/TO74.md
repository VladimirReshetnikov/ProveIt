# Trace75 positive zeros and guarded trace74 zeros

`Trace75To74.positive_zero_iff_guarded` is Lean checked in every `IΣ₁` model.
For arbitrary natural ports with beta positive **in the model**, it proves

```
(there is a positive trace75 ring zero)
  iff
(there is a positive trace74 ring zero with F+C<q).
```

Both ring zeros are evaluated in the model's difference ring at the indicated
natural-model witnesses. The old trace75 positivity guard `F+alpha<q` is now
derived from its positive zero using `Trace75Transport`; the forward witness
is the transformed mask C. The reverse map uses the explicit `F+C<q` guard
to obtain positive old alpha. The guard remains **inside the trace74
existential**. No proof that arbitrary positive trace74 zeros obey it, nor any
counterexample, is provided here. This is not an unguarded 74/75 equivalence,
a halt/iPRA theorem, or a Lean binding of JSON/PDF/fixed payload bytes.
