# Native endpoint for the 47-variable quartic

`IPRANamedValues47Native.literal_equivalence` constructs a derivation in the
repository's `nativeTheory` of equivalence between the no-zero formula for the
positive shift of the fixed-port 47-variable literal and the negation of the
mapped native refutation predicate. It transfers the existing 49-variable
bridge through the model equivalence for the two deleted named coordinates.

The focused native replays share the historical native-49 peer provider. All
seven 47-variable model/check modules, including a fresh build of the generic
square-fold helper, are rebuilt on it and required to match the independently
matched 47-model replay; its receipt and observed process exit are pinned before
these runs. The original 258-module project ancestry, 848 author-only ancestry,
package-transitive ancestry and dynamic imports remain inherited. This is not a
full current-source closure. Identification of the mapped refutation predicate
with ordinary Con(ZFC) depends on the separate interpretation and refutation
bridge; this endpoint alone does not close that broader goal.
