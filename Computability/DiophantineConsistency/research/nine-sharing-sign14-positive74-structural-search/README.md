# Positive74 whole-DAG structural search

Both scripts start from the SHA-256-pinned Lean-checked 74-gate circuit and count gates live at its output, merging commutative binary additions and multiplications. They explore algebraic rewrites across the whole shared graph rather than replacing only one gate. All inputs and fixed integer ports remain unchanged, and integers are free circuit terminals.

- `search_canonical.py` uses exact ring-identity templates for commutativity, reassociation, distribution, and common-product factoring. It retains at most 600 states per depth for 12 depths and permits intermediate circuits of at most 76 live gates.
- `search_basis.py` starts from all 40 changes of one residual `Ri` to `Ri + Rj` or `Ri − Rj` for distinct indices and explores the same structural templates, keeping at most 400 states per depth for 10 depths and allowing up to 77 live gates. Over the integers, a sum of squares vanishes exactly when every residual does, and each such residual change is invertible; this makes the new equation zero-equivalent. Its output polynomial need not be identical.

Each script checks a three-gate factoring positive control reduces to two gates. `exact-template-check.py` independently evaluates 1,056 generated local rewrite instances as exact integer polynomials in formal indeterminates, across 731 generic cases; both search scripts use the same rewrite engine. The recorded reruns found no circuit below the 74-gate incumbent. In the basis search, the incumbent is the original circuit; the result does not claim that the transformed circuits themselves attain 74 gates. These are bounded beam searches, not exhaustive proofs or minimality claims. No candidate was found, so there is no new formula or exact candidate-equivalence theorem; the external graph/PDF binding remains outside Lean. Run from repository root using `python3 -I -S -B`.
