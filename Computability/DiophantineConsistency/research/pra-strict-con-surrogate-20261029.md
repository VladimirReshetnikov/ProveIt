# Candidate PRA and the bounded consistency surrogate

The maintained equation's separate HA proof-existence theorem concerns Foundation's original ZFC consistency sentence. This note records an additional candidate-PRA result for a different, bounded-witness consistency sentence; it does not replace the HA theorem or identify the two consistency sentences in candidate PRA.

The backward implication is not known here in candidate PRA; it is related to the bounded-collection issue. The conditional source-model and language-reduct details remain to be checked before any nonprovability claim.
