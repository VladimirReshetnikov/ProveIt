This is an **unelaborated source-only proposal** for the `DirectFY` cut in
`../ordinary13-natural89-to-positive80-model-draft/`. It does not prove an
r-positive replacement, the positive-80 bridge or an iPRA endpoint.

its own signed strong factor, using the separate graph-to-natural strong norm
and `Delta=3+4a+a²≥3`. For y, the proof uses the prototype's **raw signed
auxiliary ordinate**, so it does not need a natural rank or a positive-C gap.

divide one in the difference ring. The existing
`Trace75IntegralTransport.natural_dvd_of_signed` reflects this to the model
naturals, where `Delta≥3` contradicts `Delta∣1`. No positivity of eta, beta,
X, f, y, or the signed rank is assumed to prove the y branch. All four ports
are casts of natural inputs, and the proposed unit-factor bridge and casts
remain dependencies.

into this 85 prototype. Those shifts leave direct f=coordinate 3 and
y=coordinate 11 unchanged. Thus the final source proposes the exact
all-model `Natural89Positive80TransportDraft.DirectFY K` interface. It does
not assume or derive r positivity; r=8 still needs the separate actual
first-root switch/positive replacement. The natural K0 condition needed for
prototype factor extraction is retained, and every generated graph map,
