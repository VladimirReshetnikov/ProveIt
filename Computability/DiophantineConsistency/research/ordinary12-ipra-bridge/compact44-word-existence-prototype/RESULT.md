# Exploratory equivalence for supplied definable cyclic words

`CompactWordExistence.supplied_word_equivalence` states, for each fixed `N` and `s` in any `IΣ₁` model, that existence of a Σ₁-definable old `MarkedWord` is equivalent to existence of a Σ₁-definable compact `RawCompactWord`. Both are external quantifications over model functions. The old word assumes a positive period, <942 rows, marker and the specified cyclic east/south matches. The compact word assumes the same positive period and marker, <943 rows and its specified cyclic east/south matches. The forward map uses the row lookup; the reverse uses the inverse and derives exclusion of row 1 from the supplied positive-period east matching.
