# Proposed paired graph-derived radix replay

`replay.py` freezes the three independently reviewed closure sources and separately rebuilds them over the audited fixed-graph author and peer providers. It pins the exact closure audit receipt and observation, reruns the fixed-graph parent audit and source scan, checks both 140-object inventories, enforces same-side project imports and a regular Init object, rejects warnings/errors/sorryAx, unexpected declaration names or axiom sets, and non-regular or symlink outputs. Direct objects and their present/absent companion slots are recorded with realpaths and hashes before each step and checked afterward; all frozen sources are rechecked through the final parent audit. Paired outputs must match.
