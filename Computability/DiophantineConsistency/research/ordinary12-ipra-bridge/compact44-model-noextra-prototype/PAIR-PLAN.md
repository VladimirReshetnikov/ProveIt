# Conditional compact-row exclusion paired replay plan

This unlaunched driver rebuilds the exact previously independently audited `CompactForbidden.lean` source separately over the author and peer sides of the independently audited row-matching pair, then compiles `CompactNoExtra.lean` on each corresponding side. It does not import a forbidden object from the other provider. It pins both exact sources, both audit receipts and audit scripts, the matching pair receipt, Lean and the sampled monitor; reruns both complete independent parent audits before and after and compares them with pinned JSON; guards exact direct dependencies and companions; and checks all seven named axiom reports, warning/admission absence, compiler exits and equal paired object/log hashes. The forbidden source's first two reports have no axioms; its remaining three have `[propext, Quot.sound]`. The two model-client reports have `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

Launch only after independent prelaunch review and external sole-Lean/stable-HEAD coordination. Start with a trusted clean native-loader environment, e.g. `env -u LD_PRELOAD -u LD_LIBRARY_PATH -u LD_AUDIT python3 -I -S -B replay.py --output /tmp/ipra-compact-model-noextra-pair-<fresh-suffix>`. Parent audits can be lengthy; actual outer and child exits must be observed and recorded. Compiler guards are sampled, while `lean --deps` has a leader timeout without a process-group/RSS guarantee. Package, transitive/dynamic dependency and between-check limitations are inherited from the parent.

The local theorem assumes a supplied bounded left row and its matching edge; the cyclic theorem assumes an already supplied positive period, bounded rows and cyclic east-matching condition. Neither constructs a word or its neighbors, proves reverse whole-tiling transport or definability, calibrates the compact checker, or proves a 12-variable iPRA endpoint. Lean metatheory axiom reports are distinct from the encoded theory's axioms.
