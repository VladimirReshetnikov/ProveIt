It is pinned to the reviewed [source plan](../compact44-halt-compact-audited-plan/README.md), independent source review `adcaef253d`, and the independently artifact-audited **single-author** suffix. It reruns the exact parent and source-closure audits before and after the build and requires their JSON to equal the pinned CHECK files. Those audits do not authenticate the parent’s inherited transitive, dynamic or package ancestry and do not turn this into a paired replay.

The runner freezes the exact five tiling sources and candidate client, checks the source plan and diagnostic-policy pins, and snapshots both seed and package roots' direct object/companion slots. It compares these to the reviewed source scan, rejects conflicting providers and target collisions, pins each child's actual direct dependency resolution, rejects directory redirection, unexpected companions and source/output drift, and uses the exact source-matched historical diagnostic policy. The five historical outputs must match in full after the specific source-path normalization; their warning counts are 9, 0, 3, 1, 9. The client must emit exactly its named axiom report with a nonempty subset of the three allowed axioms; its exact set is not pre-pinned. The child monitor uses `-M3000 -j1 -DElab.async=false`, a 600-second timeout, sampled 3.5 GiB RSS and 4 GiB free-disk limits. Dependency queries and audit subprocesses have timeouts but no equivalent RSS monitor. Checks are point-in-time or sampled, not atomic.
