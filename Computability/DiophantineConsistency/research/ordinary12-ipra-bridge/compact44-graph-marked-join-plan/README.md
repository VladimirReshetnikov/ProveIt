## Why the old objects cannot simply be overlaid

In this point-in-time scan, `ModelDifferenceRing.olean` resolved in the graph overlay to SHA `d0fb8f9664675190dbfc25915a687cef203b8362681d9256606f1022a6dbde74`, and in the contextual inverse author overlay to SHA `7a0e76614c80e4eeb903dacb5df76f2f47dbf7ac864fda1ce80e5e6099185d9e`. Both paths exist, but their distinct bytes and dependencies rule out treating the two provider graphs as one coherent build without rebuilding or independently establishing a complete compatibility boundary. The contextual inverse author directory had 786 `*.olean` slots, 473 dangling symlinks at inspection; its row-transport ancestor likewise had 473 dangling `*.olean` links. These numbers are observations, not a promise that recreating links would reconstruct a coherent provider. In particular, `ArithmeticWangPlane` and `ArithmeticWangRowStep` are among the broken historical links. The graph overlay had 338 such slots and no dangling `*.olean` symlinks in that directory; that does not authenticate it or all its transitive dependencies. The scan deliberately does not take old inverse/word/soundness objects as seeds.

## New theorem still needed

`FixedGraphAnchoredMatch.graph_anchored_match` gives the **same** model `N,W,s` from positive 12-variable values and the fixed symbolic graph-zero hypothesis, with `0<N`, `s<N`, marker row zero, `b h := OrbitRowWord.row C N 1 h < 943`, `b h ≠ 1`, and two finite table-coordinate equalities at the cyclic predecessors. `OrbitRowWord.row_definable` supplies a Σ₁ function instance for this row. `CompactFieldCastBridge.lookup_matches` already converts those finite-coordinate equalities to east/south matching for any lookup satisfying its stated cast law; `CompactRowTransport.compactField_cast` is the intended concrete law. The latter sources have not yet been combined on this graph provider.

The next concrete client should import the rebuilt `CompactMarkedTransport`, `CompactWordDefinability` (which imports reverse transport), and the graph-side `CompactFieldCastBridge`, instantiate its lookup law with `CompactRowTransport.compactField_cast`, and construct `CompactMarkedTransport.CompactWord (OrbitRowWord.row C N 1) N s` using the *same* witnesses. Check all four field orientations and modular predecessor equalities, including arbitrary `s` and wrap. Then install `OrbitRowWord.row_definable`, apply `CompactWordDefinability.inverse_composition`, and apply `CompactMarkedReverse.reverse_marked_word` to obtain a represented old `MarkedWord`; apply `ArithmeticWangSoundness.marked_halts` only after its exact same-provider import and hypotheses are verified. This proposed client has not been written or compiled. `CompactCyclicBridge`/`CompactWordExistence` are useful optional supplied-word equivalence clients; the direct reverse route can use the graph's explicit row-1 exclusion rather than asserting word existence from the equivalence.

The graph-zero and positivity assumptions remain explicit; this plan does not prove that graph zero exists. A successful model-level represented halt implication would still not bind the symbolic K or other retained bytes to the displayed tuple, establish compact table/controller calibration, give the converse machine-to-graph direction, or constitute an internal iPRA derivation or the desired 12-variable endpoint.
