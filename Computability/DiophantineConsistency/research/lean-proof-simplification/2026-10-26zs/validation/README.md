# Validation scope

All validation here is non-Lean. Source and declaration identity, exact patch
replay, arithmetic residual calculations, finite samples, and PDF rebuilds are
separate checks. None is kernel elaboration, an axiom audit, performance evidence,
or a full dependency-closure build. The selected candidate changes one proof;
excluded growth and combined artifacts are retained only for comparison.

The final sealed replay receipt, maintained alongside the distributed archive,
records exact executed commands and their outcomes. The bundle manifest checks
consistency with these bytes, not publisher authenticity or mathematical truth.
