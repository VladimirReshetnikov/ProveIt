## Candidate-specific judgments communicated to the main review

### Fixed-controller annihilating exponents: retain, subject to correctness

Candidate: replace dependence on the *least* multiplicative orders of the two
fixed controller elements by the actual needed equations `x^23 = 1` and
`x^402 = 1`. Derive fixed-power identities from these and exponent congruence;
retain genuinely necessary prime-power exact-order facts elsewhere.

No matching proposal was found in principal manuscripts, supplementary notes,
or non-baseline code/prototype material using searches for `orderOf`, `B_order`,
`power_dimension`, `generator_cast`, annihilating exponents, multiplicative order,
torsion, Fermat, or order minimality. The nearest precedent is V12's generic
finite-controller certificate interface, which does not remove least-order
proofs from these concrete algebraic clients. The new contribution must be
described as the precise hypothesis/dependency reduction, not as general period
calculus (which is old in the helical geometry proposals).

Independent source-dependency check found exactly four production consumers of
the fixed `B_order_47`/`B_order_2011` facts: the two `power_dimension_*` theorems
in `CurrentControllerUnits2011` and the two `generator_cast_*` theorems in
`CurrentPowerCoordinates2011`. The `two_order_*` results feed only `B_order_*`
and the dedicated `CurrentControllerData2011Checks` module. The proper-divisor
residue checks are likewise part of that fixed-order diagnostic layer.

This is a **proof-dependency** reduction without moving files. The old data
module would still compile its exact-order declarations. A claimed compilation
dependency reduction requires a separate `ExactOrders` module or analogous
split, plus an explicit compatibility decision for existing imports. The
auxiliary exact order is different: `generator_order_succ` supplies
`full_order`, then `block_order`, then `exists_coordinates`. The last theorem
constructs an actual bijection; a mere annihilating exponent would not prove
the needed injectivity/cardinality. The nonzero first auxiliary lift and exact
prime-power order must remain.

### Executable Jones sums by weak compositions: retain as an optional syntax change

Candidate: replace enumeration of all `Fin n → Fin 5` vectors followed by the
sum-four test with the already executable `List.Nat.antidiagonalTuple n 4`.
The existing Mathlib file `Mathlib/Data/Fin/Tuple/NatAntidiagonal.lean` at the
repository's pinned Mathlib revision already provides the remaining-degree
recursion, no-duplicate theorem, lexicographic ordering, and exact membership
characterization. The source-specific gain is recognizing the Jones `star`
as this finite family and removing the bounded-coordinate lift/bijection
proof, not designing another generic finite enumerator.

No earlier non-baseline manuscript, note, or prototype mentions
`ExecutableJones`, `JonesSyntax`, `vectorSum`, `starSum`,
`sum_bounded_eq_star`, weak compositions, stars and bars, or enumeration of
fixed-total-degree homogeneous monomials. Earlier sparse coefficient/row
certificates concern a different fixed catalog and are not this argument.
The family has binomial(n+3,4) outputs rather than 5^n candidate tuples; those
are exact combinatorial counts, not measured Lean timings or a claim about
the entire Jones construction's complexity.

The change affects executable `Expr` association/order and removes zero
branches. Semantic sum equality is sufficient for evaluation correctness but
does not prove `rfl` equality of stored syntax, unchanged gate counts, or
byte-identical emitted expressions. Keep this as an explicit optional
executable change; retain a proof-only finalizer proposal separately.

### One affine sign separator for the thirteen-coordinate carrier: retain

Candidate: share the exact arithmetic fact behind
`ThirteenFreshSeed.carrier_exists` and
`ThirteenCarrierSoundness.transport_nonneg`. Both use opposite-direction sign
consequences of the identity

`y = 1 + 4 * (b*m - (1 + b*V^2)*G)`

together with the small-magnitude condition on y. A sharp domination estimate
`2*|V| + 1 < 4*(1 + b*V^2)` for positive integral b can support one affine
separation lemma and remove two independent quadratic-growth chains.

Earlier mentions of these modules discuss import footprint or already
maintained progress. None proposes this affine sign mechanism. Signed Pell
congruence, exact rational-root descent, and generic zero-set transport are
nearest precedents, but they do not establish either of these concrete sign
inequalities. Retain only the exact integral hypotheses actually needed and
check the b=1, V=0, G=0, and m=0 boundary choices against each caller's scope.
No unsupported claim of a broader Pell simplification follows from this helper.

### Do not add a generic fifth proposal merely to increase the count

The four retained mechanisms above supply distinct concrete obligations.
Dedicated natural-semiring evaluation, generic raw DAG invariants, support-local
evaluation/frame rules, witness layouts, graph-interface packaging, and another
first-failure/periodic-row abstraction are already covered. A topology-free
raw DAG nonnegativity theorem or a precise witness-restriction equivalence
could be investigated separately, but no measured residual consumer saving
has yet justified including either as a fifth recommendation in this report.

### Project a bound-certificate graph to formula validity: reject on semantics

The syntax agent explored deriving `IsFormCodeSem H c` from `BoundCodeRel H c b`
to remove an explicit domain guard. This particular strengthening was not found
in earlier proposals: j retains `IsFormCodeSem` in its unique-existence theorem,
and q/s supply only local certificate union/insertion laws. However, the current
guard is substantive. `IsFormCodeSem` is inductively generated/intersection-based,
whereas `BoundClosed` is a postfixed observation graph. Without an additional
well-foundedness/Foundation argument, the proposed projection does not justify
identifying the two. Do not recommend it merely because it is unclaimed.
