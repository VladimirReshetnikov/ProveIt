NEW DELTA
Treat all three as instances of a single fixed-point statement in an arbitrary monoid:
  x^a=x  ==>  for every n:Nat, x^(a^n)=x.
The n=0 case is x^1=x. For n+1, use
  x^(a^(n+1)) = x^((a^n)*a) = (x^(a^n))^a = x^a = x.
This needs neither commutativity, invertibility nor primality. It is not an assertion that a Frobenius map is a homomorphism over a composite modulus. It is a fixed-point identity for one distinguished base.

The exact one-step residues are:
  (37:ZMod 47)^120199 = 37;
  (1283:ZMod 2011)^120199 = 1283;
  (37435:ZMod 120199)^120199 = 37435.
All three seeds were checked with reduce_mod_char, and the symbolic-B wrappers use only the already existing B_cast_* facts. The original huge B need not be evaluated by any new lemma. The seed exponent is a fixed integer; computation is modular repeated squaring, not expansion of the natural power B^120199.
