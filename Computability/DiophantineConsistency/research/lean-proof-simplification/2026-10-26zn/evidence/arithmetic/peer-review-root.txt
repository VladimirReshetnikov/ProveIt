Independent static review of weighted-update and origin-reuse proposals

Reviewed current root/WeightedUpdates.lean and root/OriginReuse.lean against
CurrentDummyMutation2011.lean, NativeCyclicConvolution.lean,
CurrentWordOrigin2011.lean, and the retained CurrentReflectedGeometry interface.

Weighted update: the finite sum identity is valid over Nat without subtraction,
digit bounds, or carry assumptions. Every unaffected coordinate is preserved;
the selected contribution rises by delta*w(i). Boolean update is valid for every
ZMod N including N=0 because it requires only the existing pointwise bound. The
current source mutation transports and ordinary nonwrapping premise remain
unchanged. Earlier unannotated field projection in the fixture was corrected;
the final compiler status is owned by root. The standalone word expression is
identical to production, though the complete declaration has moved section
parameters into explicit binders and therefore is not literally byte-for-byte
identical as a declaration.

Origin reuse: reflected_source with h=0 and p<=985 has false condition
1005<=p, so the reflected coefficient is zero. This covers a one-cell cycle as well as larger positive dimensions. It uses exactly the
existing periodic support and aligned-base assumptions, no added matching,
marker, or origin-cleanliness premise. Finite summation then yields zero for the
reflected component. The three bare taps still require their existing source
lemmas, which are proved in origin_bare_zero and retained by the integration
plan. The normalized-origin carry and mask obligations remain downstream and
are not silently discharged by this simplification.

Conclusion: both proof mechanisms are sound as stated. This is independent
source/mathematical review, not a substitute for final Lean compilation or a
claim that production clients were rebuilt.
