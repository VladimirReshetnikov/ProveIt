1. SCOPE AND EVIDENCE

The requested exclusion corpus consists of all files under
Computability/DiophantineConsistency/research/lean-proof-simplification
(rounds 2026-10-26a through 2026-10-26w, plus README.md and ADOPTION.md),
and all contents of the five ZIP archives present in docs/incoming at the pin.

All 1,286 files in the first corpus were independently verified against their
pinned Git blob SHA-1. All five incoming ZIPs were likewise independently
verified. SHA-256 values are recorded as an independent content digest. The ZIPs
contain 1,042 regular members, whose decompressed content hashes are recorded.
The accompanying exclusion_corpus_receipt.json and incoming_members.json contain
these receipts. Archived baseline sources and evidence logs count in the byte
inventory but are not additional proposals merely because they are present.

Review scope: principal proposal sections in all 23 rounds and all five incoming
archives were read; cumulative exclusion ledgers and supplementary source notes
were inspected; narrative/proposal/Lean artifacts were searched for candidate
mechanisms. This is not a claim that every log or baseline source line was read
individually. Byte verification establishes the reviewed corpus identity, not
perfect semantic completeness. Novelty findings are scoped to this corpus, not
claims of original mathematics or prior-art searches across Lean generally.

All proposed ideas are excluded regardless of adopted, pending, deferred,
uncompiled, optional, or future-work status. New names, extra applications of a
previously proposed generic interface, and merely applying pending patches are
not enough to count as new. Actual savings against the pinned source must not be
reported as total marginal savings after a nonexistent unique counterfactual
implementation of all earlier alternative designs.

6. CANDIDATE DECISIONS DURING THIS REVIEW

ACCEPT (SCOPED NOVELTY SCREEN): common-modulus residue certification in
CurrentKernelResidues2011. One newly checked table in ZMod(47*2011*120199) can
supply three old tables by divisibility projections. No composite-modulus or
product-ring simultaneous certificate proposal was found. Nearest prior ideas
are stronger-to-weaker congruence (a/g), chunk assembly (b), and ring-hom coefficient
naturality (f). Those are assumed already available; the new contribution must
be consolidation of independent certificates, with exact data and net overhead
measured. No Chinese remainder reconstruction or coprimality is required for
projection from the product modulus.

ACCEPT (SCOPED NOVELTY SCREEN): exact weighted point-update law for
CurrentDummyMutation2011.word_update_increment using finite-sum update machinery.
No Function.update / Finset.sum_update / point-increment refactor was found.
Prior word linearity and convolution with a point mass remain assumed available.
The new claim should be an actual localized replacement, with coefficient delta
and nonnegative arithmetic handled precisely; generic linearity is not new.

REJECTED AFTER API CHECK: AnnotatedProofChecker.axiomTags_exists via the suggested
List.exists_map_eq_iff. The named lemma does not exist in the pinned environment;
introducing a replacement helper did not establish a net simplification. This is
not a recommendation and is omitted from the four delivered ideas. The earlier
novelty screen only ruled out a matching prior proposal; it did not validate the
suggested library name or implementation.

EXCLUDE: redundant stored carry recurrence converted to derived methods, even in
new CurrentCarryConstruction2011.Facts. This is explicitly b section 9.1.

EXCLUDE: moving/reusing RepresentationSize witness-count constructor lemmas in
ExpressionSize as a new generic idea. Early count projections are b section 4.3.
A distinct source-specific delta would need more than another wrapper application.

EXCLUDE OR QUALIFY VERY STRICTLY: ObjectResidualSquares via termwise AM-GM and
finite-sum equality saturation. Aggregation by Finset.sum_eq_sum_iff_of_le is q's
explicit mechanism; only a materially new local semiring equality argument could
remain. Import reorganization and ordered-semiring availability are also prior.

EXCLUDE: internal BoundStep inversion by tag shape; j explicitly supplies that
arbitrary-carrier constructor view and fused unique-existence plan.

NOT FOUND, BUT NOT YET AN ENDORSED PROPOSAL: ordered-ring/Grothendieck completion
for internal residual semantics. No direct precedent found, but substantial
instance/order construction could outweigh the removed proof. Generic ordered
semiring division uniqueness also needs comparison with k's explicit natural
Euclidean certificate/uniqueness before claiming a genuine incremental mechanism.
