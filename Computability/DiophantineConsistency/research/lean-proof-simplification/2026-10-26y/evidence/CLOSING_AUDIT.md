relevant independent-review and freshness notes, optional theorem statements,
and the affected specifications patch in s. Targeted searches covered the
materialized s/t text and the u manuscript, notes and prototype sources.

an object; the needed source text was then fetched directly from the pinned

retrieval detail, not a gap in the principal-report audit.

`differenceStage_spec` within ObjectNaturalOrder; neither edits the strict
order or its instance. The t coefficient patch calls the unchanged
`SidonPositions.weight_sum_eq_iff`. The u shared-edge prototype provides
row-edge matching and old/new-head bridge cases; it has no helical-period
or strip-width theorem. These source-level checks support the distinctions
above beyond keyword absence alone.

Files under `closing-new/` are working evidence copies of the prior corpus,
not new deliverables to republish. Package this addendum, its JSON and the
continuity record with the main exclusion ledger; the old manuscripts need
not be included again.
