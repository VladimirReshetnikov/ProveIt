1. SPECIALIZE THE ALREADY PROVED LEFT-SUCCESSOR LAW AT ZERO

Target: ObjectOmegaSemiring.lean, mulRel_one_left, original lines 20-35.
The current theorem establishes MulRel H (natV H 1) y y for every internal
natural y. It builds a formula expressing the assertion and applies internal
omega induction. Its induction hypothesis is used, so this is not the earlier
proposal to delete vacuous inductions or unused induction hypotheses.

The already imported ObjectMultiplicationLaws.mulRel_succ_left states

  MulRel H x y z -> AddRel H z y w -> MulRel H (vsucc H x) y w.

Specialize x=z=vempty H and w=y. Its two premises are exactly the existing
mulRel_zero_left H hy and addRel_zero_left H hy. The result is definitionally
the desired theorem because natV H 1 is vsucc H (vempty H).

The complete replacement body is the single term

  mulRel_succ_left H (mulRel_zero_left H hy) (addRel_zero_left H hy).

An independent shorter route starts from mulRel_zero H hy, applies the
right-successor law once, rewrites addValue_zero_left, then uses mulRel_comm.
Both versions are in UnitMultiplicationCandidate.lean. The exact patch uses
the first, whose premises can be checked directly against the source types.

Why this remains correct for nonstandard models: the arbitrary input y is
never externally enumerated. The two imported relational laws already hold
on the entire internal omega. Specializing a law at zero is an ordinary
logical consequence of those arbitrary-model statements. No identification
of Natural H with the metatheory's Nat is used.

No dependency cycle is introduced. ObjectOmegaSemiring already imports
ObjectDistributivity, which imports ObjectMultiplicationLaws; the latter
imports ObjectAdditionCancellation and then ObjectAdditionLaws. The cited
laws precede the construction of the CommSemiring instance. In particular,
the replacement does not appeal to the instance's one_mul field, which is
itself built using mulRel_one_left. All imports, raw arithmetic relations,
values, formula generators and instance definitions remain byte-identical.

Measured source delta: 16-line declaration -> 3-line declaration, a net
13 physical lines and 691 UTF-8 bytes removed. These are prospective source

2. DERIVE DISCRETE ARITHMETIC DIRECTLY FROM SET SUCCESSOR MEMBERSHIP

Main observation: the model already provides the defining semantic law

  mem u (vsucc H v) <-> mem u v OR u=v.

The current internal arithmetic API proves a strict gap statement by first
extracting an additive difference, proving the difference nonzero, taking
its predecessor, and converting back to addition. That route reconstructs
the same discreteness already present in set successor membership.

Add the following exact bridge after the CommSemiring instance, in
ObjectOmegaSemiring.Natural:

  (x+1).val = vsucc H x.val.

Its four-line proof is just the existing addValue_succ_right and
addValue_zero_right equations. This is not a new arithmetic definition.

Now for arbitrary internal naturals a and b,

  a < b+1
  <-> mem a.val (vsucc H b.val)
  <-> mem a.val b.val OR a.val=b.val
  <-> a<b OR a=b
  <-> a<=b.

The equality step uses Subtype.ext and congrArg Subtype.val. The final step
uses le_iff_lt_or_eq, so the proof does not depend on reducing the current
implementation of <= to a particular disjunction. This matters because the
incoming further-deltas packet already proposes reconstructing LinearOrder
from its strict part. Its < relation remains the same, and the bridge here
is compatible with either presentation of <=.

Taking complements in the linear order gives

  a+1<=b <-> NOT (b<a+1) <-> NOT (b<=a) <-> a<b.

The old lt_iff_exists_add_one theorem then follows in four body lines: rewrite
strict comparison as x+1<=y, apply the already proved canonical-additive-order
characterization, and commute the final +1 past the existential slack.
Its public statement is preserved exactly.

This is stronger than introducing a helper for the repeated witness code:
it reverses the mathematical dependency. Discrete successor comparison
comes directly from the set model, before any predecessor decomposition of
an additive difference. No external induction, Noetherian property,
WellFoundedLT instance or standard-Nat identification is introduced.

The exact five-file patch makes these clients shorter:

  ObjectNaturalDiscrete.lt_iff_exists_add_one, original lines 30-48:
    replace the nonzero-gap/predecessor proof by the two comparison bridges
    and canonical-order witness transport.

  ObjectPairingSurjective.add_one_le_of_lt, original lines 19-22:
    retain the private wrapper signature and apply add_one_le_iff.

  ObjectPairingSurjective.pair_successor, original lines 45-46:
    get 0<a+1 directly from lt_add_one_iff and zero_le.

  ObjectPairingSurjective.pair_surjective, original lines 80-84:
    reuse val_add_one instead of unfolding and rewriting chosen addition.

  ObjectPairingInjective.pair_eq_pair_iff, original lines 96-102:

  ObjectDivision.successor_step, original lines 59-65:

OPTIONAL STANDARD-API FOLLOW-UP

The verified pinned mathlib API offers SuccOrder.ofSuccLeIff and SuccAddOrder.
After the local bridges compile, one may register a coherent SuccAddOrder
instance with successor x+1 and a NoMaxOrder witness x<x+1. This gives the
standard Order.add_one_le_of_lt, Order.add_one_le_iff, Order.lt_add_one_iff and
Order.one_le_iff_pos APIs. InternalSuccessorCandidate.lean contains the actual
prototype with local instances. This optional overhead is excluded from the
16-line reduction and from the main patch. No PredSubOrder, subtraction,
well-foundedness or locally finite interval instance is proposed: those would
need additional structure and could misrepresent nonstandard models.
