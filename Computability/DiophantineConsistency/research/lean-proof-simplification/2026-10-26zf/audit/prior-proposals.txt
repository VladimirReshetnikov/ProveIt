# Non-repetition audit for the 26 October 2026 review

## Scope and decision rule

The candidate source snapshot is ProveIt commit
`89226e3921bce3e8949fadd12d3efb6914c7630c`. The exclusion corpus was refreshed
through `7df43cdfacb490cd3ca3acd23e39027efebf8801`. The eight candidate target
files are unchanged between these commits; `freshness-compare.json` records
the comparison. This freshness check is not a new compiler run.

The directly reviewed proposal corpus consists of **23 placed packets, a through w,
and five current incoming ZIP archives: 28 packets in total**. These counts are
packet counts, not counts of independent ideas. Many packets overlap.

The review treats every substantive prior suggestion as already available,
including optional, uncompiled, deferred, withdrawn-for-overlap and notes-only
directions. Implementing an old suggestion, moving it to a new helper, or checking
it for the first time does not create a new recommendation. Mutually exclusive
old patches are interpreted through their common mathematical outcome; this
audit does not pretend they form a simultaneously compiled tree.

Packet README files and principal manuscript sections were read; cumulative
exclusion ledgers and proposal-bearing notes were inspected; candidate terms and
nearby concepts were searched across all materialized text. The five incoming
archives were read as ZIPs without executing their programs. Copied baseline
source trees were excluded from the extraction because they are source evidence,
not thousands of separate proposals. Relevant Lean candidates and patches were
retained for checking precise overlaps. `corpus-manifest.json` records file hashes
and the incoming archive/member identities. This is a scoped semantic audit, not
a global originality claim or a claim to have reread every historical paper
transitively linked by the large incoming index.

In the table below, **P** abbreviates
`Computability/DiophantineConsistency/research/lean-proof-simplification`.

## Important examples of strict screening

The latest incoming files invalidate several attractive candidates that would
appear new from the maintained Lean files alone. For example, the list-head
order simplification and the canonical band proof sharing are already concrete
proposals in those archives. Notes-only proposals also matter: finite rank reuse
and certified trace-row composition were found in supplementary audits rather
than main recommendation lists. The new report should cite these as excluded
ideas, not count their implementation as progress beyond the user's baseline.

No prior packet's successful Lean checks validate a new replacement produced in
this review. New compiler status must be reported separately, and any source
reduction must charge its new imports, helper statements and compatibility
adapters.

### Final focused comparison of the leading four candidates

The proposed unit calculation, direct successor-membership route, iterated
convolution estimate, and shared-base power inequality were additionally checked
against all five incoming manuscript bodies, their proposal code/patches, and
targeted terms across the placed supplementary notes. No matching substantive
proposal was located. This conclusion is relative to the pinned direct corpus.
The closest earlier methods and the residual obligations are specified above;
these candidates should not be advertised as new mathematical discoveries.

The fifth incoming packet was added during the final freshness check. Its full
manuscript, proposal-bearing arithmetic/helical review notes, nonduplication audit,
and six-idea candidate code were examined. None adds a direct successor-membership
order bridge, a zero-specialized multiplication-by-one calculation, a repeated
linear coefficient operator bound, or the shared-base proof of sixteen_pow_bound.
Its geometric-series change is in PolynomialRangeBounds and leaves coefficient
bounds unchanged; its square-to-interval change is in MarginSeparation and does
not normalize exponential bases. These concrete distinctions preserve all four
recommendations after the refreshed exclusion baseline is assumed applied.
