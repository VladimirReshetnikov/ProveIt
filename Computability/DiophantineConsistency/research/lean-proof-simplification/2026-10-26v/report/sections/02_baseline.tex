\section{The assumed applied baseline}

\textbf{The baseline is a union of promised outcomes, not a pile of patches.} Several earlier packets offer competing implementations of the same result. Applying them literally together would conflict. For this review, their strongest compatible outcome is available: a shared theorem, a usable adapter, or a completed refactoring with the original semantic guards. A design-only proposal is not fresh merely because its implementation was never supplied.

The audit binds six placed packets in the repository research directory, five fresh incoming archives, and the nine recommendations from the preceding delivered study. Its 75 legacy rows, 31 additional or strengthened incoming contracts, and nine previous-study rows \emph{overlap}; 115 ledger rows do not mean 115 independent improvements. The audit checks 321 current placed blobs and rehashes all eleven archive streams.

\subsection{Three distinctions keep the review honest}

The adoption record and the pin are distinct: the record mentions queued \code{NatIterate} work, but no such module is present at the pin. Its outcome still belongs to the virtual baseline. A broad library-reuse suggestion does not reserve every later client, whereas an advertised adapter does. Finally, local controller and north-color views do not imply compatibility of neighboring positions in a newly constructed row.
