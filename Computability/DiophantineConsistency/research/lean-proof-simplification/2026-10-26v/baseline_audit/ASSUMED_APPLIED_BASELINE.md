# Assumed-applied proof baseline

Pinned repository: `22e020937caa81d0cb72a4f63071d6c0bcc1eba9`.

Counterfactual union of prior promised outcomes/APIs. Not a simultaneous patch set; not a claim these Lean declarations currently exist. Design-only proposals and proposed adapters are assumed completed for novelty screening.

The 75 legacy rows, 31 additional or strengthened incoming contracts, and nine previous-study rows overlap. They are an audit ledger, **not** a count of independent improvements.

## Virtual APIs and removed obligations

### V01 — Lookup, parser and exact decoding

Optional list lookup supplies membership and witnesses; Option.map preserves injectivity under an injective code; successful bind exposes children; tree decoding has an exact quote/depth graph and old projection APIs. Total judgment-list decoding is separate from partial formula parsing. Parse-then-check success is composed with typed correctness; partial-decoder left inverses supply encoding injectivity.

**Already removed:** Repeated list inductions, impossible Option failure branches, redundant quote/depth traversals, and repeated parse-success unpacking are removed virtually.

Legacy families: EX001, EX002.

### V02 — Scanner and validated-history semantics

A proof-only Nat.rec congruence/iteration layer supplies seed/zero/successor/fixed-point laws, reject/done stability, semiconjugacy, and parameterized dynamic-seed PR construction. Guarded-history fold soundness and append/step laws are available for different input/output types. Exact numeric Boolean reflection and guarded tag dispatch are available.

**Already removed:** Do not propose new scanner inductions, a renamed iterateFrom, or another typed/encoded simulation for the advertised scanners.

Legacy families: EX003, EX007, EX008, EX009, EX010, EX011, EX012.

### V03 — Representation and witness layout

natAssignment composition with Sum.map and Expr.eval_rename is available in a low dependency layer; Fin witness blocks split/recombine including zero lengths; constructor witness-count projections are available before their clients. Existing expressions, counts and stored PR programs are preserved, with whole-value rfl compatibility when applicable.

**Already removed:** Constructor correctness no longer repeats assignment extensionality or finite witness splitting. Proof-only reuse may not alter encoded data.

Legacy families: EX004, EX005, EX006.

### V04 — Algebraic finalizer and carrier local facts

Carrier coordinate positivity and canonical-zero normal forms, integer-unit extraction, residual-square zero equivalence, whole-conclusion casts, nine-coordinate existential packaging and named conjunction projections are available. half_base_bound_31 is a direct general-theorem specialization. New denominator descent retains exact chosen rational roots.

**Already removed:** Remove coordinate-by-coordinate branch recreation, sign reconstruction of newly chosen roots, repeated product-unit complements, and copied coordinate existential packaging from the virtual checkout.

Legacy families: EX013, EX014, EX015, EX016, EX023, EX028, EX029, EX066.

### V05 — Arithmetic certificates and inverse graphs

The positive-denominator Euclidean certificate has one neutral proof shared by register/quadratic backends, including quotient/remainder/slack uniqueness. Product-equivalence projection lemmas derive inverse-coordinate graphs from actual pairing equivalences; existing certificate and tuple normalization facts remain prerequisites.

**Already removed:** Do not count re-proving divMod semantics or recovering a missing pairing coordinate as fresh. Zero-denominator guards and existing witness counts remain.

Legacy families: EX024.

### V06 — Compiler residual, degree and cost interfaces

Shared executable finite-residual evaluation, integer sum-square zero equivalence, degree <=2d and generic per-term cost bounds are available. Common semiring gate semantics and earlier expression rename/syntax-degree laws are available without cycles. Existing internal semiring and finite-row conjunction APIs remain model-general.

**Already removed:** Repeated residual folds, generic degree inductions, add/mul identical branches and standard/internal row quantifier plumbing have already disappeared.

Legacy families: EX017, EX018, EX019, EX020, EX021, EX065.

### V07 — Carrier transport, scalar tails and source windows

Shared ratioValue semantics and identical 99-node tail are available with exact embeddings. Guarded existential zero-set transport does not require assignment bijection. Low/high source windows share one restored actual source assignment and scalar window; a lightweight base interface precedes completeness. Split/Gap sharp degree profiles retain their distinct constants.

**Already removed:** No new headline for copying tail factoring, carrier zero-set congruence, coherent source windows or importing a light facade.

Legacy families: EX025, EX026, EX027, EX030, EX031, EX032.

### V08 — Coefficient tables and row certificates

Coefficient-envelope proofs use a common nonnegative monomial/sign basis or a checked finite sparse catalog; row coefficient maps are homomorphic, source-row equality is checked in a small sparse quadratic syntax, and elementary row operations preserve zero sets. Sharp K dependence remains. Factor-degree bounds imply out-of-range univariate coefficients vanish.

**Already removed:** No fresh savings from the old 22 row envelopes, coefficient table map cases, sparse row reflection or row-operation equivalence. Competing tactical/catalog implementations count as one outcome.

Legacy families: EX033, EX034, EX035, EX036, EX037.

### V13 — Extensional computability and enumeration

### V14 — Internal object semantics and induction

All-model completeness/sealing and ZFC-to-ZF extraction live in a low common bridge; closed transport is arity polymorphic. Internal recursive graphs have preservation/fusion under exact definability and totality/functionality hypotheses.

**Already removed:** No replacement by external Nat or unrestricted induction over external predicates. The remaining task cannot be merely to supply a missing adapter already proposed as part of these APIs.

Legacy families: EX062, EX063, EX064.

### V15 — Module boundaries and validation

Thematic imports, retained facade names, affected Checks/Audit targets and direct theorem/endpoint axiom audits are part of the assumed refactoring baseline.

**Already removed:** A slimmer import or replay manifest alone is supporting infrastructure, not a new proof simplification.

Legacy families: EX075.

### V16 — Signed Pell and elementary residue arithmetic

Pell oddness reuses odd_index_of_base_dvd; congruence up to sign is a transitive symmetric relation compatible with negation/natAbs and exposes divisibility only at endpoints. eq_of_residues uses direct supported Nat/Int omega normalization; huge powers stay symbolic; rational roots descend without replacing them.

**Already removed:** Four-way sign-chasing, the copied oddness proof, the explicit cast scaffolding and arithmetic finalizer root-sign cases are all presumed removed.

Legacy families: Current incoming / previous delivered study.

### V17 — Depth and resource budgets

Composition uses a common affine witness/depth budget, including zero-arity ordinary composition where valid, and a proof-producing max-plus depth certificate layer with actual GraphRep adapters/traversal/sharing. A Fin2 depth helper already handles binary bookkeeping. This is assumed usable even where historical artifacts supplied only arithmetic prototypes.

**Already removed:** No new credit for implementing the previously unimplemented GraphRep adapters, max-plus traversal, or specializing their advertised BoundedForallDepth clients.

Legacy families: Current incoming / previous delivered study.

### V18 — Spatial/word boundaries and small views

One ZMod/Fin/list interface handles word evaluation, lookup and finite sums with all modulus/length/digit guards. Injective sparse placement has image/off-image lookup, bounds, exact word and population laws; arbitrary folding still combines collisions. Word congruence lives beside binaryWord. Direct South prefixes and zero-base propagation use existing general APIs.

**Already removed:** Do not reopen sum syntax, repeat known prefix offset arithmetic, or present Fin/ZMod coercion bundling as a fresh proposal.

Legacy families: Current incoming / previous delivered study.
