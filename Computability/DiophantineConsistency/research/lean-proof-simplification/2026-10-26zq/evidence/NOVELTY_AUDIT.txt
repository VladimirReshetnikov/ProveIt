Candidate decisions
-------------------
N5: fixed-factor annihilation versus auxiliary exact order.
  Provisionally distinct and the strongest of the order-interface suggestions.
  The actual fixed-prime production clients need B^23=1 and B^402=1, whereas
  auxiliary prime-power exact order remains necessary for injectivity. Do not
  claim novelty for the general advice to weaken unused assumptions. The new
  claim is this exact dependency separation in the current controller proof.

N1: finite prime-support exact-order certificate.
  Provisionally distinct as a small new client simplification: use the support
  of 402 and its three quotient tests, reusing the existing mathlib criterion.
  Generic finite checking is old. If N5 removes this proof from production,
  count N1 only as an optional diagnostic/API proof improvement; do not add
  its saving to the N5 production saving.

N2: whole-Gamma fixed radical residue.
  Provisionally distinct from prior modulus projection, integer-product units
  and the incoming coprime cancellation theorem. The new idea is to collapse
  all q/width-dependent factors to one fixed residue modulo d*p and transport
  coprimality to d*p^n. Preserve both exponent-zero cases, independent
  exponents, actual constants and all natural-subtraction guards.

N3: first-lift residue seed interface.
  Provisionally distinct. The exact order theorem is already implemented;
  do not propose it again. The new contract is g mod p^2=1+p*c with 0<c<p,
  from which the repeated positivity/divisibility/not-square bridges are
  derived once for the current auxiliary generator.

N4: arbitrary-seed translation for fixed-cardinality subset sums.
  Provisionally distinct from finite invariant subsets, sparse placement and
  color-moment methods. Sum(t+S)=card(S)*t+sum(S), together with invertibility
  of multiplication by card(S), realizes every sum without interval-specific
  triangular-number arithmetic. Translation does not itself preserve safe
  physical placement. Keep that independent obligation explicit.
