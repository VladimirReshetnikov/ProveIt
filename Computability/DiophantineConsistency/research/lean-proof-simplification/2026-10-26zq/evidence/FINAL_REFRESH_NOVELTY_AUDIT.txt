Candidate-by-candidate decisions
-------------------------------

P1: RETAIN.
Nearest new precedents are zh/report/04-controller.tex and zj/report.tex
sections P2-P4 (lines 115-187). Both retain the Boolean choice, cardinality
0<a<C0, and gcd(a,p^n)=1. They simplify representative/cast/nondivisibility
proofs, not the coverage theorem. The new restricted-subset theorem allows
every 0<a<T with no coprimality hypothesis. Its affine client chooses the
positive projection representative 0<a<=C0, using a=C0 when the projection
is zero, and sets the Boolean correction to zero. This is the decisive extra
mathematical contract. Reusing or extending the already proposed coordinate
normal form is infrastructure, not a separate novelty claim.
The a<=C0 endpoint must be stated accurately; do not silently claim the old
strict cardinality bound. Require C0<T, and preserve the actual orbit/position
and safety premises when integrating. New concrete source modules at f00
still call the Boolean/gcd construction; their geometric support statements
can accept the replacement, but a complete migrated controller is not claimed.

P2: RETAIN, limited to the fixed-versus-auxiliary distinction.
Packet zd/report.tex, lines 255-372, makes exact orders easier to certify; it
does not remove minimality from production clients. It explicitly preserves
the order facts for B and the auxiliary lift. Even with that finite interface
assumed applied, the fixed-modulus uses only need annihilating powers, whereas
the auxiliary prime-power orbit still needs exact order for injection. This
is a different proof dependency change. No novelty is claimed for general
period transport or the elementary pow_mul law. No fixed-order diagnostic is
required to be deleted, and compilation savings need a genuine module split.

P3: RETAIN.
Packet zh/report/05-hs.tex and controller/README.md replace one helper by
three direct conditional Hs residue proofs at 47, 2011, 120199. They do not
show the entire Gamma(rw,n) has one residue modulo d*p for both independent
exponents, nor transport a single coprimality certificate to d*p^n.
Earlier polynomial-before-evaluation and congruence interfaces remain assumed
infrastructure. The extra contract is the common whole-Gamma seed and radical
unit transfer, including rw=0,n=0. At n=0 the transfer is one way; do not
assert coprimality with d iff coprimality with d*p. Retain all actual K residue
hypotheses and the guards for natural subtraction.

P4 and lift seed: RETAIN.
Packet zg/report/sections/03-local.tex, lines 175-260, concerns the positive
order of ordinary natural powers and recovery of a block radix from
p^d-1 divisibility. It is not a p-adic valuation/prime-power multiplicative
order refactor. No new report or supporting textual member contains the
full LTE annihilator law or the first-lift seed interface. The proposed law
p^(k+1) | (g^m-1) iff p^k | m supplies one uniform proof view, including
m=0,k=0, from which exact order follows by mutual divisibility. The exact
order endpoint already exists and logically determines the same power
spectrum; claim proof reorganization, not a new mathematical endpoint.
Primality, oddness, first-lift nonvanishing, and natural-subtraction guards
remain substantive. The bound c<p follows from the checked remainder and
need not be introduced as an independent premise.

Translation companion: RETAIN AS OPTIONAL, without double-counted savings.
zj's interval-injectivity cleanup cancels an existing common translation and
uses uniqueness of bounded representatives; it does not state arbitrary-seed
sum translation under a surjective cardinality action, nor preservation of a
translation-invariant seed property. The companion's gcd/action hypothesis is
essential for one fixed seed. P1 uses arbitrary subsets to remove that gcd
restriction for coverage; these two mechanisms should not be conflated.
Translation-invariant does not imply physically safe placement.
