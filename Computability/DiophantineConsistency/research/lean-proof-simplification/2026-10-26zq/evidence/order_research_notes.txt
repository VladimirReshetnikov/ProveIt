Mathematical mechanism:
Let p=120199, d=94517=47*2011, R=d*p. Let B=2^94517, r=radix^977=2^45919.
For any monoid, x^p=x implies x^(p^n)=x by induction (no commutativity needed).
Kernel-checked modular exponentiation proves (B:ZMod R)^p=B. Hence both
q=B^(p^n) and widthPower=B^(p^rw) have the fixed residue B moduloR.
For K the SAME maintained kernel, write
Gamma0=r*(B^2-1)*(1+B*K+B^2).
Then Gamma(rw,n)=r*(q^2-1)*(1+q*(K+widthPower)) is congruent toGamma0 moduloR.
This transports the whole expression once; it does not transport each factor
separately at every prime. The natural subtraction cast needs1<=B, derived
once; this supplies1<=q^2.

If a≡b mod(d*p) and Coprime b (d*p), then Coprime a (d*p^n) for every n.
Proof: ModEq.gcd_eq transports coprimality at d*p; split into d and p;
raise the latter coprimality to p^n; recombine. No primality or squarefree
assumption is required for this implication. In the actual application R
is the radical of d*p^n for n>0; n=0 has smaller prime support and the same
one-sided proof remains valid. Do NOT assert the converse for n=0.
