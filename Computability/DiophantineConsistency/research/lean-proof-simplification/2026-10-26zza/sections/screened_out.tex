\section{A larger candidate withdrawn after the closing review}
\label{sec:withdrawn}
An initially promising candidate targeted \src{CurrentFiniteDummyMutation2011}{exists_normalization_sum}: establish a bound for the entire set of safe dummy updates, then normalize the final Boolean word once instead of constructing an existential normalization at every finite-set insertion. A standalone quantitative theorem and an endpoint transport theorem were successfully checked.

Write $M=2011N$, let $\rho\ge2$, and suppose $K<\rho^{2022}$. For a finite set $S$ of cells satisfying the actual safety inequalities
\[
 j.\mathrm{val}+1<N,\qquad j.\mathrm{val}+s<N,
\]
the checked estimate is
\[
 (K+\rho^{2011s})\left(\rho^{977}\sum_{j\in S}
       (\rho^{2011})^{j.\mathrm{val}}\right)<\rho^{M-2}.
\]
It follows by bounding the kernel and temporal parts using injective exponent maps and a geometric sum. It needs no cardinality limit on $S$ and includes $N=1$, when safety forces $S$ to be empty. The numerical statement is sound, and distinct from the earlier point-update equality by itself.

However, a new incoming review added during this task proposes exact boundary-carry conservation. Under the user's required counterfactual baseline, that conservation contract is already available. Its route can remove the same normalization/nonwrap bookkeeping without introducing the aggregate numerical bound. Thus the initially advertised architectural benefit is not a sound basis for another simplification recommendation.

\textbf{Decision: withdraw this candidate from the recommendation set.} The fact that its numerical theorem is different does not suffice when its proposed simplification payoff is already supplied by the assumed baseline. It is also not a source-compression win as delivered: the client adapter removes 25 lines but the standalone helper costs 122, yielding 97 extra lines if copied literally. Its actual project adapter was not compiled.

The archive retains this work only in \code{screened_out/}, together with the late novelty decision. It is excluded from every count of recommended changes and savings. The two-window capacity lemma and ordinary geometric identities reproduced inside that prototype are explicitly baseline infrastructure, not new proposals. This record is included to make the novelty screen auditable, rather than silently relabeling a superseded idea.
