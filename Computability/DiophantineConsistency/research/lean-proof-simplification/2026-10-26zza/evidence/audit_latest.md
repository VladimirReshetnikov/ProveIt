**Aggregate safe-support bound eliminates sequential normalization construction.**
Target `Lean/DiophantineConsistency/CurrentFiniteDummyMutation2011.lean`, especially
`exists_normalization_sum`, `finite_normalized_increment`, and
`exists_finite_normalized_increment`.

Current code constructs a normalization at every insertion of a safe finite set,
then uses final modular uniqueness. Instead, prove a bound for the entire
geometric place-value sum over safe distinct cells, normalize the final Boolean
word once with exists_assembled_normalization, and apply transport_increment

locality (zo): it removes the finite-set induction over existential carry
witnesses by certifying all updates at once. Search of entire requested text
corpus found no preceding aggregate nonwrap construction. The standalone generic kernel and endpoint were subsequently checked successfully; the actual project adapter remains uncompiled. The closing audit withdrew this candidate from the recommendations; see late-head-audit.md.
