## Scope and conclusion

All three proposed mathematical reductions are valid under the hypotheses
of their intended production targets. The sharp rank bound is the largest
new mathematical contribution; the zero-test change removes a redundant
logical premise; the controller change is a small endpoint dependency
reduction using standard coprimality facts.

This review inspected the independent Lean kernels, proposed patches, the
relevant maintained definitions and theorem signatures, and the available
compilation/axiom logs. It does not claim a separate complete project build.
Source-body fixtures intentionally take already proved source identities
as theorem parameters. Their successful elaboration checks the binding

## 1. Sharp upper native-rank envelope

Let all variables be integers, let

```
q = 1 + J*beta,
C = q-F-alpha,
R = ((q-1)*(q-F)+alpha)*(q^2-1) + (MC+MF*q)*J,
U = q^4-q^3-q-1.
```

The kernel assumes `C>0`, `F>0`, `alpha>0`, `J>0`, `MC<beta`, and
`MF<2*beta`. These are sufficient. In particular:

- Integer discreteness gives `F,C,J >= 1`, `beta-MC-1 >= 0`, and
  `2*beta-MF-1 >= 0`. The proof must not be generalized to a dense ordered
  field with the same strict hypotheses: the unit slack estimates would fail.
- Since `q=C+F+alpha`, integer positivity gives `q>=3`, so `q`, `q+1`,
  and `q^2-1` are nonnegative. No lower bound on either mask port is needed.
- The following exact identity has the correct signs and coefficients:

```
U-R = q*(q^2-1)*(F-1) + (q^2-1)*(C-1) + (q+1)*(J-1)
      + J*((beta-MC-1)+q*(2*beta-MF-1)).
```

Replacing `beta*J` by `q-1` verifies the identity directly. Every summand is
nonnegative, yielding `R<=U`. Since `q>=3`, `R+1<=q^4-q^3-q<q^4`.
Consequently the existing inequality `q^4<=E` proves `R+1<E`.

### Sharpness and its scope

For every integer `q>=3`, take

```
F=C=J=1, alpha=q-2, beta=q-1, MC=q-2, MF=2*q-3.
```

Every stated box hypothesis holds and every slack term vanishes. Thus `U`
is the exact maximum for this reduced arithmetic box. The kernel's
`sharp_value` and `sharp_box` separately establish the value and admissibility.
No conclusion follows that these assignments extend to a solution of all
22 source equations and all 31 positive source coordinates. Describing the
bound as sharp for actual full source solutions would be unsupported.

## 2. Zero-test soundness from annihilation and balance

In a cancellative commutative semiring with zero, suppose

```
bit*n=0,   n+bit*unit=unit,   unit != 0.
```

If `n=0`, balance says `bit*unit=1*unit`, so right cancellation gives
`bit=1`. If `n!=0`, the annihilation equation and absence of zero divisors
give `bit=0`. The idempotence equation is not involved in either branch.

The nonzero guard is essential: with `n=unit=0`, both retained equations
hold for every bit. The supplied natural-number example with bit two
correctly demonstrates the failure without this guard.

For the external certificate, `unit=(slack:Int)+1` is nonzero because
`slack:Nat`. Its third row
`n-(1-bit)*unit=0` is algebraically equivalent to the balance equation.
Integer no-zero-divisor and cancellation laws apply; casting back to natural
numbers preserves equality and the zero test.

For the internal certificate, `unit=slack+1` is nonzero by the existing
`ObjectNaturalDiscrete.add_one_ne_zero H slack`. This theorem is valid for
every element of `ObjectOmegaSemiring.Natural H`, including nonstandard
elements. No external induction, surjectivity of natural-number casts, or
identification of the internal carrier with `Nat` is used.

The source import closure provides `CommSemiring`, `LinearOrder`,
`CanonicallyOrderedAdd`, and `IsStrictOrderedRing` on the internal carrier.
The latter comes through `ObjectPairingInjective` and
`ObjectNaturalOrderedSemiring`. Mathlib's
`IsStrictOrderedRing.isDomain` supplies the cancellation hierarchy using
`ExistsAddOfLE`, which is supplied by `CanonicallyOrderedAdd`. The final
standalone `inferInstance` bridge under exactly these classes compiled
successfully.
The source also already provides `NoZeroDivisors` directly.

The production definitions and all three stored polynomial rows remain
unchanged. The final proof deliberately clears the first conjunct before
`simp_all` with `rcases h with ⟨-, hn, hs⟩`. An anonymous underscore could
leave an inaccessible hypothesis available to the simplifier. The final
direct fixtures and source patch use the explicit clearing pattern, and
the final fixture compilation succeeded. The generic
two-premise theorem already demonstrates mathematical independence; clearing
the conjunct also makes that independence true of the short direct proof.

## 3. Actual controller base is a unit from oddness

The maintained definitions give `d=47*2011=94517`, `auxiliary=120199`,
and `B=2^94517`. Both `d` and `auxiliary` are odd. Thus the entire modulus

```
(d*auxiliary)*auxiliary^k
```

is odd for every natural `k`, including zero. Two is coprime to every odd
natural modulus, hence its class in this `ZMod` is a unit. Raising that unit
to the power 94517 gives the class of `B` after `B_eq` and `Nat.cast_pow`.
No primality, exact multiplicative order, LTE, or positive-power assumption
is needed for this theorem.

The corrected proof keeps the enormous base symbolic and rewrites its
power representation rather than evaluating it. The updated comment now
describes the odd-modulus argument. `ControllerBaseSourceFixture.lean`
uses the same production proof body with the three established constant/
oddness facts as explicit theorem parameters; its log records a successful
proof with standard axioms only.

This removes the exact-order dependency from this theorem's proof.
It does not remove the exact-order theory from the whole module: actual
power-orbit bijections elsewhere still use it. The recent zq proposal
explicitly retains both orbit and base-unit proofs after its subset changes.

## Remaining validation boundary

The independent kernels and fixtures have normal Lean proofs; inspected
axiom outputs list only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`
(some pure identities use fewer). The proposal is not evidence of a
whole-repository build, complete downstream migration, or compilation-time
improvement. The exact corrected patches should be applied and checked
in the repository's pinned environment before adoption. This is an
integration requirement, not a remaining mathematical gap in the three
arguments reviewed here.
