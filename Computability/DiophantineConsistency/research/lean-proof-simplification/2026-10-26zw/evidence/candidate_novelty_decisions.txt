## A. Zero-test certificate without its idempotence premise: retain

Target: `ObjectZeroTest.certificate_sound` and corresponding natural certificate.
New obligation: from `bit * n = 0`, `n + bit * s = s`, and `s != 0`, derive
`bit = if n = 0 then 1 else 0`. If n=0, cancel s from bit*s=s; if n!=0,
no-zero-divisors gives bit=0. The additional Boolean/idempotent equation is
therefore not needed by soundness. Primary candidate should retain every
stored polynomial row and all executable constructors.

Nearest prior work:
- x/report/lean-proof-refinements.tex:206-217 simplifies `x*x=x` into a
  zero-or-one conclusion by cancellation; it still uses idempotence.
- m/report/additional_lean_simplifications.tex:581 onward consumes
  exists_zero_certificate_iff in the zero/conditional clients; it does not
  prove one row redundant.
- zg/report/sections/02-polynomial.tex:138 and
  zg/evidence/polynomial_transport_review.md:54 discuss semantic normalization
  of zero-test rows in cancellative semirings; they preserve the rows and do
  not derive the bit from the other two equations.

Search terms: ZeroTest, zero-test, idempotent, redundant Boolean, bit+slack,
selector redundancy. No matching premise-removal mechanism found. Credit only
the concrete logical dependency reduction. Ordinary cancellation is prior.

## B. Sharp upper envelope for the native rank: retain

Target: `NativePellBootstrap.source_window_bound`.
Use q=1+beta*J, C=q-F-alpha, positive integer F,C,J, MC<beta, MF<2*beta,
and R=(q*(q-F)-C)*(q^2-1)+(MC+MF*q)*J. Then

    U(q) = q^4-q^3-q-1
    U(q)-R = q*(q^2-1)*(F-1) + (q^2-1)*(C-1)
      + (q+1)*(J-1)
      + J*((beta-MC-1)+q*(2*beta-MF-1)).

Each term is nonnegative for q>=3; equality holds in the reduced arithmetic
box at F=C=J=1, alpha=q-2, beta=q-1, MC=q-2, MF=2*q-3. This is NOT an
attainability assertion for all original source equations.

Nearest prior work:
- u/report.tex:198-268 proves the LOWER envelope
  `(2*q-1)*(q^2-1) <= R`, shared by rank>=40 and rank>3*q clients.
- zb/report/report.tex:344-407 uses exact affine rank dependence on C+qF
  to compare two ranks with common q,J and ports. It explicitly distinguishes
  its comparison API from the lower envelope; no upper-envelope client.
- Earlier source-register decoding is assumed already available. Its source
  identity supplies the new inequality rather than constituting new credit.

Searches: source_window_bound, upper/rank in both orders, sharp upper,
slack/rank, q^4-q^3. Direct target hits are old unchanged source snapshots,
not proposed edits. Retain this exact stronger inequality and window client.

not a new generic unit API. Keep B opaque except for its symbolic equality.
