## A. Replace the native window estimate by a sharp upper deficit identity

**Target:** `Computability/DiophantineConsistency/Lean/DiophantineConsistency/NativePellBootstrap.lean`, `source_window_bound`, baseline lines 514–563.

The existing proof spends most of its body bounding the coefficient `G`, each mask contribution, their sum, and finally a fourth-degree expression. The older packet `u` concerns a sharper *lower* rank envelope. It leaves this upper-window argument in place, so the present target remains after that proposal is assumed applied.

Write the actual source-derived quantities as

\[
 q=1+J\beta,\quad C=q-F-\alpha,\quad
 R=((q-1)(q-F)+\alpha)(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J.
\]

All values here are integers. The existing theorem already supplies

\[
F,\alpha,J,C\ge1,\qquad M_C<\beta,\qquad M_F<2\beta.
\]

In particular, `q ≥ 3`. There is no need to add nonnegativity assumptions on either mask port.

The useful new exact identity is

\[
\begin{aligned}
q^4-q^3-q-1-R={}&q(q^2-1)(F-1)
 +(q^2-1)(C-1)\\
&+(q+1)(J-1)\\
&+J\big((\beta-M_C-1)+q(2\beta-M_F-1)\big).
\end{aligned}
\]

Every term on the right is nonnegative. Thus

\[
R\le q^4-q^3-q-1.
\]

The already established scale identity is

\[
E=q^4ws,\qquad w,s\ge1,
\]

so `q⁴ ≤ E`. The window inequality `R+1<E` now follows from `q³+q>0`. This removes the old sequence of mask upper bounds and the auxiliary `q²<q³` comparison.

### Why the identity is the right abstraction

The rank coefficient simplifies to `q(q−F)−C`. Its maximal value at fixed `q` occurs when `F=C=1`. The largest mask contribution occurs at `J=1` and the two greatest integral port values. The four summands in the deficit are precisely the losses from these boundary values. Thus the proof gives a transparent reason for the bound and needs one polynomial identity instead of several interacting nonlinear inequalities.

The upper envelope is sharp for the **reduced arithmetic box**. For any integer `q≥3`, take

\[
J=F=C=1,\quad \alpha=q-2,\quad \beta=q-1,
\quad M_C=q-2,\quad M_F=2q-3.
\]

These assignments attain equality. They do not demonstrate a solution to the complete Diophantine source system. Integrality matters: the strict port bounds yield integer slacks of at least one. No analogous real-variable claim is made.

## B. Derive the actual controller base unit from oddness

**Target:** `Computability/DiophantineConsistency/Lean/DiophantineConsistency/CurrentControllerSubsets2011.lean`, `block_base_isUnit`, baseline lines 25–32.

The current proof obtains positive multiplicative order of `B^(auxiliary−1)`, turns that into an `IsUnit` fact, and then cancels the power. The desired statement only needs the much simpler facts already proved in `CurrentControllerData2011`:

\[
B=2^{94517},\qquad d\text{ is odd},\qquad \text{auxiliary is odd}.
\]

Therefore `(d*auxiliary)*auxiliary^k` is odd for every natural `k`, including `k=0`, and two is coprime to it. The existing mathlib theorem `ZMod.isUnit_iff_coprime` gives that two is a unit; `.pow 94517` gives the required base unit.

The exact production replacement is:

```lean
  rw [B_eq, Nat.cast_pow]
  exact ((ZMod.isUnit_iff_coprime 2 _).mpr (Nat.coprime_two_left.mpr
    ((d_odd.mul auxiliary_odd).mul auxiliary_odd.pow))).pow 94517
```

Rewriting `Nat.cast_pow` before applying the unit theorem deliberately leaves the enormous exponentiation in `ZMod`; it avoids attempting to evaluate the natural number `2^94517`. The checked exact fixture produced no exponentiation-threshold warning.

The proof body shrinks from **6 to 3 lines**, with a full declaration decrease **8 to 5**, and a full-file decrease **3 lines**. The descriptive comment is also corrected: the unit now follows from parity, not positive order. There is no new production helper or import.

### What this changes and what it does not establish

This is a local dependency simplification. `block_base_isUnit` no longer uses the auxiliary exact-order/LTE chain. The containing file still imports and uses power-coordinate results elsewhere; no file-level import-removal or global speedup is claimed. The proof does not construct an orbit, a finite subset, or a safe arithmetic mutation.

The parity guard is essential for this route. For an even modulus, a positive power of two need not be a unit. At `k=0` the actual modulus is still odd and positive, so no special case or stronger exponent assumption is needed.

### Novelty boundary

Prior packets `zl`, `zn`, `zo`, and later `zp`/`zq` already contain composite-modulus unit assembly and fixed-reference Gamma certificates. Those ideas are excluded. The retained recommendation is only the newly available exact endpoint `block_base_isUnit`: replace its order-dependent proof with the actual power-of-two/odd-modulus facts. The later `zq/late_head_scope_addendum.md` explicitly retains that base-unit proof after its new Boolean-free subset coverage, so this residual obligation survives the assumed prior changes.

`ControllerBaseUnit.lean` checks the generic parity theorem and the literal modulus shape. `ControllerBaseSourceFixture.lean` checks the **byte-identical production proof body** with `B_eq`, `d_odd`, and `auxiliary_odd` as explicit theorem hypotheses. Again, this is a checked source-body adapter, not a complete repository rebuild.
