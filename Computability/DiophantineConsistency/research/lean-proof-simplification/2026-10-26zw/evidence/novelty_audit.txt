## Rejected candidate (full corpus search)

`CurrentCellLabels2011.sum_le_one_cases` currently reconstructs the
sum-zero/sum-one dichotomy, proves existence of a positive term, erases that
term and bounds every remainder term (~30 lines). The standard library
`Finset.sum_le_one_iff` support characterization eliminates that scaffolding
without changing `Selected` or downstream witnesses.
This exact mechanism was omitted from the 155-entry synopsis but is explicitly
proposed in `g/report.tex:201`, `h/evidence/arithmetic/findings.txt:82`,
`k/report.tex:148`, `k/native-proofs/OneHotSupport.lean`, and
`n/report/sections/05_finite_and_cycle.tex`. **Exclude it.** This is evidence
that a summary ledger or keyword absence alone is insufficient.

## Caveat on all-applied baselines

Do not add textual savings from arbitrary old source snapshots. Semantic
novelty can be assessed under every earlier mechanism being available, but
incompatible alternative patches need not define a single realizable baseline.
Any measured source saving should identify exact current source and include
helper/import/wrapper cost. Fresh proof ideas cannot consist merely of
compiling or reimplementing an earlier uncompiled idea.

## Final refresh and retained candidates

The zp and zq report sections, source experiments, READMEs and late scope
notes were checked against all three retained ideas. Neither adds the sharp
upper native-rank envelope or the two-row zero-test soundness argument.

mechanisms: it changes one current endpoint to avoid exact-order input.

power-orbit and base-unit proofs after Boolean-free subset coverage.
