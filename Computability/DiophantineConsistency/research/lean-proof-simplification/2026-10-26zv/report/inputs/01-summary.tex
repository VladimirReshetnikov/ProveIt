\textbf{The source has moved beyond the previous open boundary.} It already contains actual controller positions, a one-dummy ordinary no-wrap theorem under physical position bounds, exact packing population and rank bounds, and source-preserving rephasing. These constructions are baseline, not discoveries of this report. The new work is only the residual proof deletion after those results and all prior proposal contracts are assumed available.

The three changes have different mathematical reasons. Fixed actual coefficients turn position inequalities into linear integer arithmetic. The rank comparison follows directly from $2u\le3u<2^{3u}$. Rephasing reuses the exact recurrence and decomposition fields by rewriting their rotation inputs, while leaving the distinct scalar transport proof intact. Each is explained against its unchanged domain and witnesses below.

\textbf{All new Lean candidates are UNCOMPILED.} No Lean, Lake or Elan executable is available in this task environment, and no toolchain was installed. Static audits, exact patches, source inspection and finite arithmetic checks do not establish elaboration, kernel acceptance, axiom equality or a speed improvement. The PDF is compiled separately; its successful build says nothing about Lean acceptance.

The pinned active Lean tree contains 702 files and 87,415 physical lines. The final proposal baseline contains both excluded collections and 43 placed manuscripts, including the delivered fifth report. Three tempting optional-support proof deletions were withdrawn after the final comparison. Novelty is assessed against their useful contracts, not merely matching filenames. This report does not claim a new general theorem or completion of the full polynomial--$\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$ equivalence.
