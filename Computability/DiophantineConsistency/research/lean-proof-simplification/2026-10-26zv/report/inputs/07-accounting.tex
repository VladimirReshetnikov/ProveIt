The population and rephasing models do not evaluate the complete literal 2011/47 construction. They test the arithmetic contracts and their edge cases, not universal quantifiers or Lean syntax. The models include $u=0$, unrestricted top blocks, non-Boolean source digits, and one-cell cyclic wrap where applicable; they do not enlarge the public hypotheses.

Negative controls reject missing disjointness or strict block bounds, unsafe final/temporal positions, a nonperiodic rephase, and identifying ordinary multipliers across equal rotations. One reduced-radix example has $\rho=4$, $n=1$, $K=C=1$, $F=2$, and the same carry $0$: rephasing $W=0$ to $W'=2$ keeps the rotation and words but changes $2^W$ from $1$ to $4$ and the positive quotient from $1$ to $2$. This illustrates why the retained transport and quotient logic cannot be deleted.
