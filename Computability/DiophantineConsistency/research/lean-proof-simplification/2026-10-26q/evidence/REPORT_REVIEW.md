## Mathematical checks

- Internal addition zero: successor decomposition plus totality, uniqueness,
  and nonemptiness of successor gives the contradiction without an induction
  hypothesis. It ranges over the entire internal omega.
- Internal multiplication zero: the successor law reduces the result to an
  addition-zero statement. No predecessor induction claim is needed.
- Pell: the explicit unit power has first coordinate 1 modulo 2n. Squaring a
  negative-norm solution gives `(2*x^2+1, |2*x*y|)`. Classification and the
  original norm equation imply `2n | 2`, contradicting `n >= 2`.
- Discriminant transport: `a^2+4*a+3 = (a+2)^2-1`. The unshifted norm cannot
  equal -1 modulo four for arbitrary integer parameters; no positivity is
  missing. The auxiliary product and shifted coordinates normalize by ring
  identities.
- Saturation: retain `na^2+W^2` as one block with lower bound 1. Splitting it
  into two independent unit minima would be false. The five-block equality
  theorem has all necessary lower-bound and equality-case hypotheses.
- Closure: only Step is monotone. Closed is not upward-closed under arbitrary
  enlargement. The generic interface establishes no finiteness, well-foundedness,
  totality, uniqueness, absence of cycles, or least-fixed-point characterization.
