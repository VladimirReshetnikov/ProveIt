DECISION
Round2 overlaps the rail-prefix part of the finite-cut proposal through its
screening entry N08. Root's conservative decision to withdraw the entire
finite-cut family is justified. Round2 does not overlap the scaled Pell ratio,
integer interval extraction, compositional Sat specifications, or observed-set
certificate closure directions. It also does not itself overlap the first-unit
proof, but a separate late archive audited by novelty_audit does; that first-unit
proposal is withdrawn independently and is not to be included in the final packet.

SIX-PROPOSAL COMPARISON
1. Compositional Sat normalization: no Round2 collision. A2 fuses two traversals
   of an external Nat/Option tree decoder, not object-formula interpretation
   under binders. C2's simpa-only normalization is for numeric prefix offsets.
   Using the same tactic is not the same mathematical/proof obligation.
2. Scale-parametric Pell ratio: no Round2 collision. No proposal names
   PellRatio90, upper_estimate_scaled, lower_key, firstUnit, or a real positive
   scaling budget. The old exclusion catalog's EX050 is source-register
   equation decoding, not sharing the ordinary and doubled ratio estimates.
3. Finite cuts: DROP, due N08 above. The separate late New_Deltas archive also
   has a rail-rank research suggestion (reported independently by novelty_audit).
4. Small-square first unit: no Round2 collision, but DROP due another late
   archive's exact firstUnit_fundamental replacement via
   Pell.isFundamental_of_y_le_three. Do not keep our alternative 19-line proof
   after assuming that shorter prior replacement already applied.
5. Integer error-budget extraction: no Round2 collision. Prefix-bit identities
   C2 and old EX047 concern exact natural modular arithmetic; none combines two
   real errors to identify an integer unit interval or generalizes the real
   denominator s>=2.
6. Observed certificate-set closure: no Round2 collision. N12 (CSV:13) proposes
   Nat.unpair existential projection and bounded Fin witness assembly. That is
   different from closure of arbitrary internal sets under union/singleton
   insertion as observed through their encodings. No union/postfixed-set API
   is proposed by this archive.
