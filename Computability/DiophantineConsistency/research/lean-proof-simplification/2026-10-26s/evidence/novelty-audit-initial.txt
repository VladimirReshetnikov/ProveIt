Policy used
===========
The user's counterfactual baseline includes every suggestion in the two named
locations, including pending, deferred, uncompiled and further-research ideas.
The adoption record is evidence of provenance, not permission to rediscover a
pending idea. A new name, different client or fresh proof of an old helper is
not by itself a distinct simplification. Claims below concern the actual
proof-simplification corpus at the pinned commit. The incoming index links
hundreds of older mathematics reports outside its directory; this audit does
not claim a complete semantic reading of every transitively linked archive.

Candidate-specific novelty decisions communicated to the team
============================================================
1. Object-level satisfaction/specification normalization under binders appears
distinct. The prior omega_ind and completeness wrappers consume a Sat iff spec;
the proposed narrow simp-only method derives those specs and reduces de Bruijn
environment projections. Concrete ObjectCodeBound/ObjectCodeSeal clients and
checked unchanged statements are needed. Generic 'use simp' is not the claim.

2. A shared finite-prefix cardinality/rank theorem for strictUpper/strictRank
and target_prefix_card_lt/rail_strict_mono was not found as a concrete proposal.
D:612-625 and E:348 praise the existing structural rank/inverse as good and
warn against expensive sorting caches. I's broad order-embedding search direction
is the nearest mention. A defensible incremental claim is eliminating duplicated
finite cardinality inclusions while retaining the original structural rank.

3. Functional certificate continuation/elimination has a near-overlap with
E52's broad functional-graph uniqueness/witness-elimination direction. No exact
AnnotatedReplacement exists_chain/exists_sealValue compositional rewrite was
found, but a conservative report should not count broad certificate elimination
as independently novel without explicit qualification of the difference.

4. A mixed-radix bounded-digit reconstruction theorem is distinct from the
specific Nat.pair inverse-graph proof in E46 only if its finite digit bounds,
whole code injectivity and concrete tile-table burden add materially new content.
Do not merely rename the product-equivalence projection helper.

5. NativePellBootstrap.firstUnit_fundamental via Pell y_ne_zero and
Int.sq_eq_one_of_sq_lt_four is not found in the earlier proposals. This removes
the integer y=-1,0,1 enumeration using the existing Pell positivity invariant;
it is separate from Pell parity reuse and congruence-up-to-sign algebra.

6. A lambda-scaled upper-estimate theorem sharing NativeHalfBinomial with
HilbertTenthProblem/Paper1980/PellRatio90 is not found in the prior corpus.
The nearest occurrence, K evidence/arithmetic/findings.txt section 8:169-176,
only recommends replacing trivial existential aliases xi,X,Z,epsilon with set.
The new sharing can therefore be measured after those alias changes. F's
later upper-packing-estimate suggestion concerns source degree profiles and
does not describe this Pell approximation family.

7. Integer interval extraction for half_binomial_of_ratio was not found as
a prior proposal. Given integral t,Y, s>=2, xi=s*t+f with 0<f<1, and
xi/s<rho<xi/s+1/s together with Y<rho<Y+1, infer Y=t by uniqueness of the
containing integral unit interval. The nearby earlier floor/carry and modular
half-interval facts concern different obligations. An actual second client
should justify any shared helper rather than merely moving a short proof.

Scope of the novelty claim
=========================
The result is a source-grounded exclusion comparison, not a theorem that an
idea has never appeared anywhere. Absence from keyword search alone is weaker
than this audit; positive overlap decisions above cite exact report sections or
supporting notes. User-requested counterfactual adoption means any new source
reduction must be measured after relevant old ideas, or reported as a separate
proof-of-concept without adding earlier savings to its total.
