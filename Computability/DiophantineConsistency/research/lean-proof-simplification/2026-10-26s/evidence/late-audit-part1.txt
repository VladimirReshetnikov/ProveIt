   among the remaining 20 proofs appears in A/B proposals.
   Decision: retain, subject to other agents' exclusions; do not claim
   general existential certificate elimination as part of the new idea.

2. Positive operator / post-fixed certificate closure.
   A sections 5 and 8 reuse an existing finite cyclic propagation theorem
   and Selected.unique, respectively. Neither states union/insert closure
   of S<=F(S) for a monotone certificate operator. B certificate work is
   existential projection, unrelated to monotonicity of certificate sets.
   Searches of proposal-bearing notes/code and substantive review found
   no BoundClosed closure, postfixed operator, or union/insert rule proposal.
   Decision: retain with concrete BoundStep/BoundClosed clients and helper
   cost; avoid claiming generic fixed-point theory as new mathematics.

3. Lambda-scaled common Pell upper estimate.
   B's Pell work concerns fundamental-unit classification, odd-index theorem
   reuse, and signed modular composition (sections 3–5). Neither archive
   proposes a common upper_estimate theorem relating NativeHalfBinomial
   to H10/Paper1980/PellRatio90, nor parameterizing xi<lambda*(Y+1).
   A's "ratio tails" exclusion is older polynomial/register tail work.
   Decision: retain; account separately for already-proposed trivial alias
   cleanup in original archive K and current baseline changes.

4. Integer unit-interval extraction.
   B section 2 handles exact integrality of a rational root via denominators
   and preserves signs; it does not isolate an integer from two narrow
   rational intervals or use half-binomial denominator scaling.
   A deals with physical cyclic residues and word prefixes, not quotient
   approximation intervals. No matching unit-interval/snapping helper found.
   Decision: retain; state the actual strict inequalities and s>=2 premise,
   and charge helper cost. Do not conflate with cyclic carry normalization
   or signed rational-root descent, already excluded by prior corpus.
