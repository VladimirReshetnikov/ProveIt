6. OBSERVED INTERNAL CERTIFICATE CLOSURE — KEEP

The finite-permutation invariance proposal is about an external finite state space and the inverse of a bijection. Our closure interface concerns arbitrary internal sets S:V in a ZF model, monotone support predicates, union and singleton insertion; it preserves members outside the encoding image. It does not infer inverse invariance or use finiteness of V.

The screened-out constructor-algebra idea in audit/novelty_comparison.json:249-274 concerns depth-indexed AnnotatedBound/AnnotatedRename constructor soundness and certificate completeness, with code_binaryForm/renamed_binaryForm/bound_binaryForm semantic lemmas. It is explicitly narrower than a generic scanner fold but still different from cup/insertion closure of internal relation certificates. The canonical-evaluation note at lines220-246 uses an evaluator witness and existing uniqueness; it does not prove closure under unions or insertion. No observed-encoding Closed, BoundClosed_cup/insert, RenClosed_cup/insert, RankClosed_cup/insert, postfixed union or monotone-certificate set-kernel proposal appears in the new content. Keep as optional core plus six actual clients; no net saving claimed.
