## Candidate-specific closing comparisons

### R1: retained after numeric-word and local-carry proposals

The closest new predecessors are zn's weighted numeric source update, zo's finite prefix characterization between actual cell resets, and zp's all-offset reflected-source normal form. None states the factorization

`binaryRotation v W c = rotate (shiftDigits (2^v) (2^(W%v)) c) (W/v : ZMod n)`

as the two-coordinate congruence interface that removes Booleanity from the *equality of two binary rotations*. They concern, respectively, a natural weighted sum under a point update; a normalized carry after actual raw-cell reset hypotheses; and equality/zero of a reflected source address under support. Granting those results does not remove the artificial digit-bound premise in `CurrentWordRephase2011.rotation_eq`.

Specific nearby anchors are zo `report/report.tex:440–573`, zn `report.tex:232–300`, and zp `report.tex:137–215`. In particular, zo explicitly retains Boolean dummy/marker guards for its actual reset inputs and whole-cell displacement; its local characterization does not justify arbitrary-digit reset or normalization existence. R1 preserves that boundary: it transports an already supplied `Facts`, and only comparison of the rotation expressions is unbounded.

zp `evidence/controller-notes.txt` explicitly mentions that `CurrentWordRephase2011` already transports the same carries/digits for congruent strides. This is a description of maintained progress, not a proposal to remove the unnecessary Boolean premises, and it does not give the generic phase/residue congruence. The claim of new work must remain the residual hypothesis removal, not same-witness rephasing itself.

zq removes a different Boolean: the optional affine arithmetic correction epsilon in a subset-sum construction. Its Boolean-free affine endpoint changes the cardinality bound to a≤C0 and leaves physical placement/safety work intact. It does not remove digit hypotheses from binaryRotation equality. The word-level period theorem remains a companion to R1, not a separate counted headline or least-period claim.

### R2: retained after the additional arithmetic packets

None of zk–zq changes or proposes changing `ObjectDivision.quotient_not_lt`. Their order/LTE/divisibility work concerns ordinary natural exponents and residue rings, not quotient comparison in arbitrary model-internal naturals. The interval argument still supplies the distinct residual step after the x ordered-semiring and zf successor-discreteness outcomes are granted.

The earlier zf patch was inspected directly: its ObjectDivision changes concern `successor_step` and `division_exists`; its copy of `quotient_not_lt` remains the old two-gap, self-inequality witness proof. The retained patch removes the remainder-gap construction and uses the separated intervals qd+r<qd+d≤q'd≤q'd+r'. It does not identify internal naturals with host Nat or import host induction into an arbitrary model.

### R3: retained after the new positivity proposals

zl's optional `Gamma_pos` proof derives positivity of a separately calibrated natural controller coefficient from coprimality; zk's affine carrier reflection concerns a different relation y=1+4(bm−(1+bV²)G). Neither derives the signed port beta from the actual first two source rows.

Row decoding is prior infrastructure and receives no new credit. The downstream signature migration remains proposed, not performed.
