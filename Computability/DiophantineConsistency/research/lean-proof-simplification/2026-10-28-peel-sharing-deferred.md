# Deferred bounded peel-side sharing experiment

The candidate in snapshots/final shares the identical left/right run-peeling proof via one private Boolean-side helper, retaining both existing theorem statements, StepOkB's weight and clock guards, and all imports. It removes three net lines and one repeated case analysis. Both phase targets and the current-source TapeControl and TapeRun clients passed; their client output objects match by phase. Recorded target user CPU was 8.35s baseline versus 8.57s final under shared load; no performance conclusion follows.

The generated interface impact is disproportionate to this small simplification. Eleven public generated names disappear and two appear; ten private helper/generated names are added. In addition, the surviving sstep_actualB._proof_10–16, _proof_22–24 and _proof_6–9 retain their names but change types as automatic proof numbering shifts; see changed-common-names.txt and interfaces.diff. The 29,211-entry default simp and 13,320-entry instance/priority censuses are byte-identical, and none of the removed or 14 changed common names is registered in either. Exact short-form searches of Lean and Python files under Computability/DiophantineConsistency and Computability/BusyBeaver found no references to either set; other directories, other file types, untracked external consumers and compiled references were not searched. Generated names are unstable, but their churn is real. On balance, the candidate is deferred and should not be adopted from this experiment.

Six successful target/client build receipts and eight source/object-pinned probe receipts are checked by audit.py/audit.log. The first attempt failed because `false = true` was not simplified; its exact source, log, time and record were preserved as initial-failed.* before the successful rerun. Its recorded old paths were subsequently overwritten and the initial-prefixed copies match the recorded hashes. The final phase client receipt samples the old workspace target source but consumes its separately recorded final candidate object. The direct TapeBoxes, Home, ControlFrame and Runtime215 providers come from prior successful current-source receipts; transitive objects remain cached. This is not a clean full-closure build or a check of all consumers.
