\textbf{The optional three-line saving is not an unconditional recommendation.}
Deriving \code{Gamma_pos} from a calibrated coprimality theorem makes positivity depend on the literal-kernel residue chain. Its current elementary proof avoids that dependency. The same source-length caution applies to replacing a direct residue endpoint by a stronger exact-order result: shorter text is not automatically better dependency structure or faster compilation.

\textbf{Current source progress is not a new proposal here.} The literal $K$ residues and all three corresponding $H_s$ residues are now proved. Exact auxiliary prime-power order, full affine power coordinates, actual rank-step units and constructed mask/scalar consequences are also in the inspected source. In particular, this report does not carry forward the fourth review's open literal-$K$ status. Those developments create the fresh clients being reviewed; their construction is not counted as this packet's saving.

\textbf{Every new Lean candidate remains uncompiled.} No Lean, Lake or Elan executable is available in this task environment, and no toolchain was installed. Static source checks, exact patches, independent mathematical explanations and finite tests are included; none establishes Lean elaboration, kernel acceptance, an axiom footprint or a performance improvement. The PDF is compiled separately.

The pinned main tree contains 686 Lean files and 85,917 physical lines. Novelty is measured against 36 placed manuscripts at the final bounded exclusion cutoff, including the relocated incoming archives, fourth packet and seven fresh manuscripts. Earlier contracts are assumed available even when optional, deferred or mutually incompatible. The review is deliberately modest: it does not claim a new general theorem or completion of the full polynomial--$\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$ equivalence.
