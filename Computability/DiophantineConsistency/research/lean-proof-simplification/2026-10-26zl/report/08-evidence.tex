This tests the checker and integration boundary, not Lean validity. See \path{audit/integrated_results.json} and \path{audit/README.md}.

\textbf{Novelty is semantic.} The targeted name searches are supplementary: absence of a spelling is not evidence that an idea is new. The full inherited exclusions include fixed-state scanner recursors, cyclic residue and quotient normalization, signed prefix borrow, coherent word/carry views, mask-block lookup, source/witness extraction, finite invariant predecessors and generic rank interfaces. Those mechanisms remain free baseline contracts for judging the small current-client changes.

\textbf{What was not established.} No new Lean candidate was elaborated or checked by the kernel here. No additional axiom claim is certified, no compile-time benchmark was run, and no memory or runtime improvement is inferred from a shorter proof. Existing repository compilation and axiom-review statements were read, not rerun. PDF compilation and finite arithmetic checks are separate evidence classes.
