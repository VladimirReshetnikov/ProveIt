\section{The baseline includes all promised prior outcomes}

The user asked not to repeat proposals already in \pathcode{research/lean-proof-simplification} or \pathcode{docs/incoming}, and to assume they were applied. Maintained source alone is therefore an insufficient novelty test. A useful proposal must remove a residual obligation \emph{after} prior promised outcomes and their advertised adapters are available.

\subsection{What was inspected}
At the pinned head, the research directory has 21 placed packets, \code{2026-10-26a} through \code{2026-10-26u}. The incoming directory has its historical README and no ZIPs. The audit rebinds 45 previously inspected manuscript/note blobs to the exact current Git identities and reads the nine new manuscripts l, m, o, p, q, r, s, t, and u with their substantive support. It also adds this review series' six round-three outcomes, whether or not separately placed upstream.

There are 1,140 inventoried placed blobs, 894 hash-bound placed files, and 279 document/technical-source bindings. This does not claim every historical archive or receipt was rehashed or inspected.

The resulting ledger has \textbf{168 overlapping rows in 25 semantic subsystems}: 115 inherited rows, 47 new or strengthened packet rows, and six prior-round rows. These are not 168 distinct simplifications. Repeated proposals, compatible alternatives, and strengthening relationships remain visible rather than summed as independent savings.

\subsection{How the virtual baseline is used}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Grant the advertised semantic interface, including production adapters explicitly promised by a prior packet. A prototype-only historical implementation does not make its already-proposed adapter fresh work.
\item Remove the proof obligations that interface was meant to remove. Search beyond the already simplified client, not for a new spelling of the same argument.
\item Preserve the original guards, data, witnesses, and axiom qualifications. An optional change that adds choice does not silently confer an unchanged axiom footprint.
\item When variants conflict as source edits, grant their compatible mathematical outcomes rather than pretending both bodies coexist. A research direction without a specified theorem reserves its theme; it does not create a fictitious universal API.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Metric distinction.} The nine-line saving is measured against the authenticated \emph{actual pinned files}. There is no materialized fully merged virtual source tree, so this is \emph{not} a measured virtual-tree line delta. The incremental claim is semantic: the selected obligation is not already removed by the assumed prior outcomes. Its implementation cost is then reported honestly using complete actual-source candidate files.

\subsection{Nearby ideas deliberately excluded}
Residue/floor normalization, generic coordinate or word wrappers, finite tile dispatch and high-color fibers, first-failure/prefix selection, translated interval induction, and spatial shared-edge/live-configuration adapters all belong to the baseline. They are not renamed as new helical, trace, or soundness contributions here. Generic additive quotient architecture was previously considered and declined. The late packet additionally covers the concrete H1 and H2 clients, which are now excluded.
