\subsection{The new proof layer in four stages}

\textbf{Actual arithmetic words.} Commits
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/commit/3951d56119b98e5abd4789f97e211d0000e96887}{\code{3951d56119}},
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/commit/876f6b409b3a9c3297c53f9743cb9b9780f34065}{\code{876f6b409b}}
and
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/commit/7e9e9235e5d6d13f92763f91e2a47a86838e4539}{\code{7e9e9235e5}}
connect copy parity, whole-cell alignment and literal neighboring bits.
C2 and C3 simplify the proof plumbing around these exact formulas; neither
changes the formulas nor substitutes residue-only geometry.

\textbf{Selection and spatial propagation.} The classification,
cell-cycle, true-color and occupation commits
\code{df7f0c9c9b}, \code{a54685015f}, \code{2207b481bc} and
\code{db86282477} connect local tile possibilities with exact occupation.
C1 removes a manual finite-sum argument; C4 removes a special-case induction
already subsumed by the general propagation theorem.

\textbf{Cyclic word semantics.} Commits \code{98bfa794dd},
\code{cf0b056cc8} and \code{bfdd368be9} define the marked word, transport
physical shifts through its canonical coordinate, and map actual positive
polynomial zeros to it. None of the proposals alters that map, the ambient
modulus, the chosen coordinate or the word's temporal orientation.

\textbf{Boundary classification.} Commits
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/commit/9cef0257dde4cdc80f35121b7ea0450df316548c}{\code{9cef0257dd}}
and
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/commit/73d3d8e9c04d5362012dc1c1c1cce1ada97ac139}{\code{73d3d8e9c0}}
classify exact boundary colors and seam behavior. W1 factors repeated
north-color elimination without changing the literal initializer or adding
any strip, run, halting or tiling premise.
