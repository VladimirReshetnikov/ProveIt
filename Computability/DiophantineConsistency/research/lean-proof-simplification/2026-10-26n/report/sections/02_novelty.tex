\label{sec:novelty}

The exclusion corpus comprises all five ZIPs present in
\code{docs/incoming} at the review pin and the sixth retired packet explicitly
linked by that directory's README. The six manuscripts and their technical
notes were inspected, not just their filenames. Their 75 proposal families are
recorded in \pathcode{incoming_audit/EXCLUSION_CATALOG.md}; the source key

\textbf{Decision rule.} Renaming a helper or changing tactics does not make
an old proposal new. The same mechanism at the same target or announced
consumer family is excluded. Generic similarity in using induction, a named
library API or finite classification does not exclude a different theorem
that removes a different obligation. The following matrix records that
boundary explicitly.

\subsection{Rejected rediscoveries}

The inner recursor congruence proofs in \code{PRCode.Realizes.prec},
\code{ScalarPRCode.Realizes.prec} and \code{ScalarPROptimizer.eval_optimize}
were considered and rejected: packet F already gives that change and the
\code{RecursorCore.rec_congr} alternative (EX003). Likewise, this report does
not re-propose sum-of-squares aggregation, shared circuit power/node
certificates, nine-coordinate existence packaging, generic carry facts or
source-bound profiles. Supporting audit scripts are reproducibility tools,
not new proof-simplification ideas.
