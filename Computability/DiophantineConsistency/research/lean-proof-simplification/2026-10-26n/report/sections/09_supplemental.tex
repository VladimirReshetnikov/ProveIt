\subsection{S2 Pay the binary composition bookkeeping once}

\textbf{Targets.} \src{BoundedForallDepth.lean}{81}{120} repeats the same
\code{Fin 2} elimination in \code{pow_depth_le}, \code{binomial_depth_le},
\code{sub_depth_le}, \code{mod_depth_le} and \code{div_depth_le}. The generic
\src{GraphSizeBounds.lean}{91}{111}, \code{GraphRep.compose_depth_le},
gives $2n+4+h$; for $n=2$ that is $h+8$.

\begin{Lean}
theorem compose_two_depth_le {σ : Type*}
    {f : (Fin 2 → ℕ) → ℕ} {g : Fin 2 → (σ → ℕ) → ℕ}
    (r : GraphRep f) (s : ∀ i, GraphRep (g i)) {h : ℕ}
    (hh : 1 ≤ h) (hr : r.depth ≤ h)
    (h0 : (s 0).depth ≤ h) (h1 : (s 1).depth ≤ h) :
    (r.compose s).depth ≤ h + 8 := by
  refine (GraphRep.compose_depth_le r s hh hr ?_).trans (by omega)
  intro i
  fin_cases i <;> assumption
\end{Lean}

For \code{pow_depth_le}, the candidate body is:
\begin{Lean}
  unfold GraphRep.pow
  exact compose_two_depth_le _ _ (by omega)
    (BoundedForallDepth.pow_depth.trans hh) hr hs
\end{Lean}

\textbf{Limited recommendation.} The artifact supplies the helper and two
pilots, not all five replacements. Each client body is four versus two
nonblank lines in its source formatting. Extending to all five would remove
ten client-body lines, but the nine-line helper offsets nine of them; its
docstring eliminates even that nominal whole-file gain. Include this only
if a single maintenance point for further binary operations is valuable.
All graph constructors, witness counts, fixed 512 ceilings and $+8$ bounds
stay unchanged. This is neither a new bound nor a representation redesign.
