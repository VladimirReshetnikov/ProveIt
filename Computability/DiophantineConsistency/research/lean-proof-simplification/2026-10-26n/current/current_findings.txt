C1 — Use the existing one-support characterization, not erase-sum arithmetic
CurrentCellLabels2011.sum_le_one_cases, lines 131–158: 26 -> 10 body lines.
The current proof separately establishes sum=1, existence of a positive
summand, its exact value, the sum over erase i, and vanishing of every
remaining summand. Finset.sum_le_one_iff already packages the substantive
one-support fact. Its exact call shape is witnessed in the pinned repository
by NativeSelectorClause54.selectors_one (lines 74–79), so this is reuse of
an API the project already uses, not speculation about a Mathlib lemma name.

Split on whether every f i is zero. Otherwise pick i with f i != 0. The
existing characterization applied to (i,i) gives f i=1; applied to (j,i)
forces j=i whenever f j != 0. This directly constructs Selected f i and
eliminates the erase bookkeeping. The empty type and all-zero branch remain
valid: no Nonempty or inhabited assumption is introduced.

CurrentSouthShift2011.offset20_word, lines 116–140: 16 -> 6 body lines.

omega, introduce the prefix theorem, rewrite the identity, then transport

simp normal-form list; the source provides all required identities. If one
cast requires a small simp adjustment, do not replace actual source equality
