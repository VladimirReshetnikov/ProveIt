7. Natural-register representation change is optional, not first priority.
NaturalRegisterProgram.Op already restricts register references with Fin
and has only natural literals, inputs, addition and multiplication, while
Program stores an erased integer ArithmeticDAG with topological/allowed
certificates. A dedicated natural-semiring evaluator could make positivity
and casts automatic, but would require an erasure-correctness theorem and
may trade many small cast proofs for dependent type transport. Start with
the local shared-lemma changes above. Do not rewrite the entire evaluator
only to reduce displayed source lines.
