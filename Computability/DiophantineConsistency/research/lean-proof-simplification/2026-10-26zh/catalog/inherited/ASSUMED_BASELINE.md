## Non-repetition rule

- Treat all previously proposed contracts as available, whether adopted, deferred, incompatible in representation, or still source-only.
- Choose an appropriate prior alternative per proposed client; do not pretend all alternatives form a single compiled tree.
- Distinguish physical source delta at the real pin from marginal gain after the assumed prior abstraction. Count new helper/context overhead and never count an old helper implementation again.
- Library/tactic restyling, another generic view/record, or applying an old general theorem at its originally named client is not a new proposal.
- A fresh downstream theorem may be useful, but must state the additional conclusion and identify the old contracts it consumes.
