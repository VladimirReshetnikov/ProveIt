\textbf{The native endpoint already exists.} The pinned \href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/09d65a93c9df8684c1a226341d6a5120623d1cdb/Computability/DiophantineConsistency/LEAN_EQUIVALENCE_PLAN.md\#L629-L646}{equivalence plan, lines 629--646}, records the completed marked-language/actual-blank-halting equivalence and \code{no_marked_word_iff_conZFC}. \textbf{Remaining mathematical work is not hidden in these cleanups.} Actual power-orbit identification, safe dummy placement, rank correction and remaining arithmetic witness obligations are not supplied by direct cast reuse or the terminal-band formula. The \(H_s\) wrappers still assume their actual \(K\) residues. A source-level equality of a new helper does not settle the project's internal-ZFC formalization or strengthen its stated axiom policy.

\textbf{Recommendation.} Validate the 45-line proof-only core first. Treat the seven-line predecessor edit as a separate representation-preservation decision. Retain the marker-tail theorem as a fully costed optional API until a real consumer needs its stronger interval conclusion. This leaves a small, auditable adoption sequence rather than another unverified umbrella refactor.
