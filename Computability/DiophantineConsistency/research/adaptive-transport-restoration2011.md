# Restored adaptive transport

From an actual current adaptive natural zero, every selected `IΣ₁` model has
positive `w,z` with `X=q·w` and

```
q(K+w) = (F+α)(K+w) + F + (q−1)z.
```

The coefficient K is kept syntactically as `currentPorts 1` in the formal
client, which is definitionally the current kernel integer. The proof first
uses the recovered radix to obtain `q | X`. The literal first-carrier
transport gives `qA=(F+α)A+Fq+(q−1)j` with positive j. Substitution of
`X=q·w` shows `q | (q−1)j`; an explicit additive Bezout certificate for
q and q−1 gives `q | j`. Its quotient z is positive, and cancellation of
positive q yields the displayed equation.

This is the normalized transport premise used in the existing internal window
argument. It does not restore the other fourteen-variable norms or supply the
separate population lower bound needed by that window. It is not by itself
an internal HA proof-existence theorem.
