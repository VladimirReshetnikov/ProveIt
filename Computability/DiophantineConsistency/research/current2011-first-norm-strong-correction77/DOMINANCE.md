# Dominated by an existing same-port profile

The earlier [71-operation, degree-60 ordinary sphere](../current2011-ordinary71-60-candidate/README.md)
uses the same current2011 ports and fourteen positive coordinates, and has
separately recorded model and iPRA clients. It is strictly better in both
operation count and degree than this 77/64 source-only candidate.

The 77/64 graph and its uncompiled zero-set argument are retained as an
algebraic experiment, not a new frontier result. The proposed [72/46 shear](../current2011-sheared72-46-candidate/README.md)
would offer a lower degree if its unproved reverse witness map and formal
transport were established; its smaller degree is not currently an iPRA
equivalence theorem.
