# Constructive reduction of the literal bounded-refutation comparison

This note records conditional proof transformers in Lean. It does not claim an intuitionistic PRA proof of the target equivalence.

Let `rho` be the actual Foundation ZFC refutation predicate, and let `B` be `StrictSigmaOneInduction.bnd rho`; the associated bounded refutation is `C := ∃ b, B`. For any language homomorphism `f` from the arithmetic language to a target language, the native NNF reader retains positive atoms, interprets negative atoms by intuitionistic negation, and preserves conjunction, disjunction and quantifiers. In particular, its reading of the NNF body of a bounded universal remains a disjunction: no classical conversion of that disjunction to an implication is assumed.

The independently replayed bounded-erasure development gives an intuitionistic proof of `native (f C) → native (f rho)` for any target `Theoryᵢ`. Its recursive proof removes each extra existential guard in `bnd`; it uses no arithmetic axiom, bounded decidability, or model-theoretic `eval_bnd_imp`. Given this erasure, a T-proof of the exact mapped consistency equivalence follows from two additional T-proofs: the translated witness bound `(f rho)ᴺ → (f C)ᴺ`, and the bounded comparison `(f C)ᴺ → ¬¬native (f C)`.

`IPRAExistentialComparison` reduces the bounded comparison to the universal matrix comparison `∀ b, ((f B)ᴺ → native (f B))`. `IPRAStableBackward` constructs the latter's open-formula logical comparison from native stability proofs for the relevant positive atoms, disjunctions and existentials, using a syntax set closed under recursive children and fresh-variable opening. `IPRAStableBackwardReach` constructs the least such generated support from a root. `IPRAStableBackwardFinite` constructs a computable finite list covering that support; the reverse containment and an evaluated list or count for `B` have not been proved or computed.

The stability proofs must be usable with T's arithmetic axioms, not just bare logic. `IPRATheoryHilbertBridge` embeds T's closed axioms into a rewrite-closed Hilbert axiom set and transforms finite-context T-proofs and extended-Hilbert proofs of sentences in both directions; its generalization proof uses the fact that closed context formulas are invariant under shifting. `IPRATheoryInstantiation` gives an extended-Hilbert proof of an arbitrary term instance from a T-proof of a finite universal closure. The exact ZFC stability-schema client consequently takes, for each needed formula, a finite arity, an intuitionistic semisentence, a T-proof of its universal closure, a term vector, and an exact equality identifying its instance with the formula's native stability statement. No such arithmetic witnesses are constructed by these transformers.

The exact finite-list client requires those explicit stability-schema witnesses for every needed list entry, together with the translated witness-bound T-proof. It does not establish either class of inputs or identify the candidate native theory with a standard iPRA presentation. It also does not identify this native NNF reader with the separate PAHF ordinary interpretation. The narrow guarded replays and their author/peer receipts document selected source, object and log hashes; inherited HA, ZF, Foundation and package transitive source/object ancestry remains an explicit boundary. Classical choice in Lean's metatheory appears in some proof-transformer axiom reports, while the generated object calculus remains intuitionistic.
