# Sign28-to-sign31 three-cut source draft

This source-only, uncompiled three-module draft proposes the common natural lift and, in three groups, the old X, c2 and normalizedS2 residual zeros plus evaluation identities and exact list links for the five surviving affected rows E, mainRoot, Dc2, Na and scaledNs. The X group retains the explicit beta-port cast; c2 and normalizedS2 groups do not require it. All other surviving rows and both complete zero-set directions remain separate.

The source checker verifies the copied lift, all 13 old/new row texts and positions, and the explicit cast distribution. It checks source text, not Lean proof or model semantics. The new sign28 syntax/square provider and checked sign31 square provider require a guarded overlay union and direct object/guard bindings before compilation. The proposed proofs and resource fit are untested; no endpoint, degree theorem or external coefficient binding follows.
