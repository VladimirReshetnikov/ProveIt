# The witness bound for Foundation's ZFC refutation sentence

The 14-variable equation already has a separate Lean theorem asserting the existence of its HA equivalence proof. This note concerns the additional candidate-PRA comparison with **Foundation's exact** ZFC consistency sentence. It is an audit and construction plan, not a proof of the missing bound.

Let `ρ` be `StrictPiTwoCurrent2011.provSentence`, the negation of Foundation's ZFC consistency sentence. `PRAStrictConReverse.BoundCertificate ρ` asks for an actual candidate-PRA proof of the mapped implication `ρ → ∃w, bnd ρ(w)`. The conditional reverse theorem uses this certificate, but the certificate for `ρ` and the required witness-bound function are not yet constructed. In particular, the following source inspection does not prove either nonprovability or a general collection scheme.

## Actual dependency shape

* `Bootstrapping.provable` in `Foundation/FirstOrder/Bootstrapping/Syntax/Proof/Basic.lean` has the outer shape `∃ d, (proof T).sigma d φ`. Its `proof.sigma` existentially supplies a singleton-context code and invokes `derivation.sigma`; `derivation` is `(Derivation.blueprint T).fixpointDefΔ₁`.
* `Fixpoint.Blueprint.fixpointDefΔ₁.sigma` in `Foundation/FirstOrder/Arithmetic/HFS/Fixpoint.lean` existentially supplies a limit-stage set `L`, checks `limSeqDef L (d+1)`, and requires `d ∈ L`. The latter `limSeqDef` is a primitive-recursion blueprint's `resultDef`.
* In `Foundation/FirstOrder/Arithmetic/HFS/PRF.lean`, `resultDef` existentially supplies a sequence code `s`. `cseqDef` checks, among other things, `∀ i < 2*s` and `∀ z < s`, and for applicable positions requires a successor `u < s` satisfying `succDef`. Thus the original sentence can assert a family of local witnesses under bounded universals. `bnd` replaces every syntactic existential by one bounded by the common new `w`, including those occurring inside such families; the outer witnesses `d`, `L`, and `s` alone do not by themselves supply this common bound.
* `Fixpoint.Blueprint.succDef` itself has a bounded universal over a prospective next set. Its positive half requires the blueprint's `core.sigma` at each relevant element, and its other half contains `core.pi` in an antecedent. A bound proof must handle the **actual positive and negative syntactic occurrences** after elaboration of `bnd`, not just the informal positive core relation.
* `Derivation.blueprint` has cases for every logical inference and for ZFC axioms. Its sigma side calls formula-set, term and substitution/negation graphs and the specific `T.Δ₁ch.sigma`. Formula checking recursively uses term-vector checking, whose sigma clause contains another bounded universal of existential witnesses; `isUTerm` and `isUFormula` themselves use the same fixpoint construction. For ZFC, `ZFDelta1.lean` builds the theory characteristic from finite pieces and separation/replacement schema recognizers. The latter use `isUFormula`, `isSemiformula`, shifts, substitutions and formula-graph witnesses (`SeparationDelta1.sepCh` and `ReplacementDelta1.repCh`). Their model correctness instances are stated under IΣ₁.

`StrictSigmaOneInduction.bnd` bounds syntactic existentials, preserves universal quantifiers, and proves the reverse implication from a bounded instance to the original formula. Its IΣ₁ proof in the forward direction includes collection for bounded universal cases. This explains exactly why the accepted generic strict surrogate and its forward implication do not already give the desired reverse implication in candidate PRA.

## Concrete construction needed

A promising sufficient route is to define primitive-recursive codes which, from a coded derivation and suitable intermediate history data, compute explicit majorants for all existential witnesses required by the **specific expanded** `ρ`, including the nested bounded-universal instances and the polarity of graph occurrences. The induction/recursion equation proofs must hold in arbitrary models of the candidate theory; agreement on standard natural numbers is insufficient. A final candidate-PRA proof must use these codes to construct a single `w` and show the mapped `bnd ρ(w)`. A derivation-only bound is attractive if it can be proved, but no such code, majorant, or model-uniform correctness theorem is currently available here.

Existing `PR.Construction.CSeq.exists` and `CSeq.unique` use IΣ₁ induction, so they cannot simply be imported as candidate-PRA facts. Likewise `ValidatorConsistency222.iSigma1_proves_con_ZFC_iff_222` and its source-derivation translator work in IΣ₁ models: they provide important structure but do not establish an equivalence in every candidate-PRA model or a bound for Foundation's exact syntax. Replacing Foundation's proof predicate with a different primitive-recursive checker would still require a candidate-PRA proof relating that checker to the original predicate before it could settle this obligation.
