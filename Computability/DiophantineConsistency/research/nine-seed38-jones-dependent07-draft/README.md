# Candidate I/A0/A1/V short-prefix evaluations

These source-only clients propose the local `d35` I, `d36` A0, `d37` A1, `d38` V0 and `d39` V1 last-entry equations. They split P and K carries, carry Gprime, D/F/I and A0/A1 to the relevant prefixes, and leave symbolic rank, sums and coefficients opaque. These candidates are uncompiled and do not establish full-recipe or JSON/document binding.
