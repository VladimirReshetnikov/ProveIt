# Sign20 structured degree upper-bound candidate

Four source-only stages propose generic syntax and structural bounds,
individual residual bounds, and their final polynomial-degree composition. The runner
binds the current expression definition to its audited producer, and uses the
reviewed sign20 bridge environment as base. It retains `-M3600`, 4 GiB sampled
RSS, 10 GiB host floor, timeout and disk guards. No theorem is claimed before
Lean and independent terminal audit; this is not an exact lower bound or a
JSON/PDF binding.
