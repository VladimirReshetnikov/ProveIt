# Actual adaptive auxiliary norm

An actual adaptive zero yields `I²z²+y²=1+I²y²` in every selected
`IΣ₁` model. Here `I=c²i`, `z` is the magnitude of the literal signed
pair `(f²Tc, f²R+c)`, and `y` is the magnitude of that pair after adding
the literal positive-side variable `u`. Both signs are retained until the
magnitudes are taken. The equation is obtained from the actual auxiliary
factor unit after using the actual strong factor to identify
`Δ(f²−1)` with `I²`.

This is the auxiliary norm required by the generic internal half-index
argument. The positivity of y and its two sign congruences, the large-c
bound and the binding of that generic argument to the actual main index remain
separate steps. The theorem does not assume any of them or assert an index
identity.
