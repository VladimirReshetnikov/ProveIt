The proposed 13-natural-input, 84-operation independent-k graph is **not an
established reduction** of the 85-operation eta/zeta-natural graph. The
direct reverse witness map requires `k ≥ eta`. An [explicit symbolic natural
zero at the fixed compact ports](../ordinary13-independent-k-fixedpoint-full-zero/README.md)
has `eta > k`; its fixed-point calculation and full witness were
[independently audited](../../reviews/ordinary13-independent-k-generalized-witness-independent/README.md).
Thus this inequality cannot be forced at every child zero. The construction
does not determine whether the 85-operation parent has a zero, or rule out
a different map between the graphs' **existential** zero sets.

The [exact polynomial degree](../ordinary13-eta-zeta84-independent-k-degree99/README.md)
is 99 for both graphs at the compact beta; an independent leading-form
calculation verifies the degree certificate. This algebraic result does not
establish a zero equivalence. The earlier [conditional map](../ordinary13-eta-zeta84-independent-k-candidate/README.md)
is kept at its pinned historical bytes so its review receipt remains
reproducible.

The corrected source-only composition proposes that a child zero forces
`k > 0`, a weaker statement consistent with the explicit counterexample.
Its [bounded source inventory](../ordinary13-independent-k-corrected-lexical-inventory/README.md)
is not a provider or axiom audit. The composition and its dependencies have
not been coherently elaborated in Lean, and no new internal iPRA theorem is
claimed.

These compact ports use `beta=2^(67*8807)-1`; they are not the 2011 tuple
in the maintained 14-variable, 65-operation, degree-150 `equation.pdf`.
The counterexample therefore says nothing directly about the displayed
