# Pell indices from the direct graph

`DirectMainIndices.graph_indices` proves that, in every IΣ₁ model with the stated ordered-ring interface, arbitrary natural ports with positive beta and strictly positive witnesses satisfying the actual direct draft output zero yield positive `first`, `main`, `n`, and `m`. The main root has the explicit oriented balance from `DirectMainPell`; the two indices satisfy `n≤k`, `m≤c`, and all four first/main Pell coordinate equations. The complete conclusion and definitions of the parameters are in the Lean source. No old 95-graph zero is assumed.

The theorem joins the graph-specific first root and direct-pair norm to the conditional classification. It does **not** establish an index relation, the graph auxiliary conditions, model-to-halt equivalence, the reverse witness direction, or an iPRA proof-existence theorem.
