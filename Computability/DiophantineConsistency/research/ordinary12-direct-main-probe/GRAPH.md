# Direct-main graph draft and forward ring comparison

`DirectMainGraph.lean` defines an exploratory expression by copying the 91-operation XQ91 expression and replacing the intermediate `gamma+w` by an independent input at index 11. Its first, main, and ordinary residual evaluations match those of XQ91 over **any commutative ring** when the new input is assigned `gamma+w` and all other inputs are unchanged. The three Lean theorems state these equalities explicitly. Their direction describes a forward witness construction, but positivity and model-level `InternalZero` transport have not yet been checked here. In particular, no reverse implication, model-to-halt theorem, or iPRA proof is asserted.
