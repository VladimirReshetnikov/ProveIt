# Fixed-port all-model halt equivalence

`DirectMainHalt.lean` proves in every IΣ₁ model that the 90-operation direct draft at the checked symbolic fixed ports has a positive zero if and only if the internal machine halt predicate holds. It also proves the corresponding equivalence for all-natural witnesses after `positiveShift`, and its byte-port version. The proof composes the bounded-port positive-zero equivalence with the separately checked XQ91 halt theorem; the fixed port inequalities come from `CompactFixedBeta.bounds` through the generic `DirectPortBounds` helper. No giant beta numeral is expanded by the proof source.

This is an all-model Lean theorem over its selected provider. It is not yet a Lean proof that an equivalence derivation exists in iPRA. The draft has no formally bound equation PDF. Package, dynamic, and source-to-object ancestry remain inherited rather than freshly rebuilt, so this is not a clean closure-build claim.
