# First-factor transport bound for the direct draft

`DirectMainTransport.lean` first proves an exact signed-pair representation of the draft graph's first root. It then proves, in every IΣ₁ model and for arbitrary natural ports, that positive beta and positive witnesses together with an actual draft output zero imply `Bn < Bp` and `F+alpha < q`, where `q=1+J*beta`, `A=K0+w`, `Bp=q*A`, and `Bn=(F+alpha)*A+F`. The latter conclusion appears in Lean as `0 < q-(F+alpha)`. The proof uses the checked positive first root and the existing first-root bound; it does not assume an old 95-graph zero.

This establishes a subtraction bound needed to identify the direct main signed pair. That identification, Pell-index alignment, halt equivalence, reverse witness direction, and iPRA proof-existence remain open.
