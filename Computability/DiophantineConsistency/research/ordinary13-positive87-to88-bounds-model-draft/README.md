`BoundsCut` for natural ports whose beta cast is strictly positive in every
intended model. No source in this packet or its imported research drafts has
been elaborated, built, or axiom-audited. Compiled provider compatibility,
resource feasibility, compact byte/PDF binding, and the endpoint remain open.

The common zero client proposes extracting the first, main and strong graph
residuals from either literal output via the existing five-square graph
bindings. The signed first norm has scale `M=q³*(J*beta)`; the signed main
norm has scale `U=Y*q²` and includes the actual `X*Y*q` main shift. Positive
J, beta and Y supply positive scales. Applying the separate source-only
scaled-square extraction to each signed root proposes natural tau and d,
without choosing root signs. The literal strong residual supplies
`(i*c²)²+Delta=Delta*f²`; it also rewrites Q to `(i*c²)²`.

Delta identity, and the signed z field are passed to the existing

unelaborated Pell descent, the k=2 main-square exclusion and the ordered
geometry bounds. Its desired conclusions are exactly Q>1 and z²>1, with no
sign or natural representation assumption for z. `PositiveBeta` explicitly
quantifies the represented beta positivity required to turn this result into
the prior `BoundsCut`.
