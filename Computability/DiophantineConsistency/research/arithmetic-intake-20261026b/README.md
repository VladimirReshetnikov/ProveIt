## Most direct lead: a second Pell root removes one coordinate

The Centers archive contains complete13-positive-variable graphs with
69 operations/degree174 and70/170. Its exact old admissible942 tuple costs
94,689 raw bytes. These are complete equations on that tuple, not standalone
gadgets. Their39-unrestricted-integer lifts cost160/348 and161/340. The
13-positive and39-integer domains must not be conflated.

The reduction starts with Carrier65, removes both free `z` and free `y`,
introduces one positive `m`, and sets, in its notation,

    h=beta*J, B=(K+w)*C-F, S=h*i*Delta*c^2,
    L=V^2, y=1+4*(h*(m-L*B)-B).

The final product has the four norm factors, omitting the transport factor.
At a zero their unit signs are fixed before any native sign is used. The
auxiliary norm implies `y=+1 or -1 mod 4S^2`; the literal `y=1 mod4`
selects the positive residue. Since h divides S, this gives h divides B.
The bound `|y|<2|V|`, positive m and `L>=|V|` exclude negative B. Thus
`z=B/h+1>0` restores the deleted transport equation, and replacing the
parent strong multiplier by hi and its ordinate by |y| gives a parent zero.

Completeness needs a fresh strong Pell seed divisible by `h*c^2`; merely
substituting arbitrary old witnesses would not suffice. The source chooses
that seed by a finite norm-one unit period, then selects the negative
auxiliary ordinate using the actual canonical residue `R=3 mod4`.
Canonical `X=2^R`, rank growth and C>0 ensure B is a strictly positive
multiple of h, making the constructed m positive. I found no immediate
sequencing obstruction in this full argument.

This is the strongest variable-count lead for our front. A transfer to
sorted2501 should restate the uniform theorem with its complete canonical
interface, including `R=3 mod4`, `R>q(q^2-1)`, `X=2^R`, fresh strong periods
and both positive witness maps. It then needs newly authored literal-graph
and degree/liveness audits at the actual new ports and a full page cost
comparison. The archive's old port payloads must not be copied into a new
native instance. This does not reduce the65-operation frontier: its main
point trades one positive coordinate for four extra operations and a
higher degree.

## Direct degree tradeoffs:14/66/118 and14/67/94

The Orientation archive contains whole14-positive-variable equations at
the older69,685-byte block-mask2963 tuple. The scaled variant adds just
one subtraction to Carrier65: replace the auxiliary coefficient S^2 by
`Aaux=Delta*(f^2-1)`. The already computed strong factor is
`Ns=Delta*f^2-S^2`, so `Aaux=Ns+S^2-Delta`.

The source gives the exact polynomial identity

    Q118-P150 = N0*N1*Nt*Ns*(Ns-Delta)*(V^2-y^2).

At a positive zero of either polynomial the strong unit sign gives
Ns=Delta; the auxiliary coefficient is then a square, recovering its
positive norm sign and the transport sign. Consequently the two positive
zero sets coincide at the identical tuple. This is a particularly short
uniform transfer argument for our existing Carrier65 ports. A fresh
actual graph, full degree calculation and source binding are still needed
before publishing a new point. It lowers degree150 to118 at the cost of
one operation, rather than lowering operation count.

The ordinary variant uses `I=i*c^2`, `Ns=1+I^2-Aaux` and subtracts1 from
the final product. Its67-operation/degree94 soundness depends on the full
relaxed strong-norm and auxiliary step-down proofs, including nonsquarefree
Delta. It cannot be justified by silently dividing a free multiplier by
Delta. Completeness multiplies the old strong multiplier by Delta. This
transfer is materially longer than the scaled exact-zero-set identity.

## Weighted transport: related10-variable alternatives

The Signed Growth, Sparse Syntax and Pell Reuse submissions all combine
the older free quotient chart with a whole-product polynomial center.
Their compact fronts use ten positive inputs and a different native
four-port tuple whose largest integer has592,884 bits; the Pell Reuse
README gives295,388 raw bytes for all four. They are complete equations,
but neither their dimensions nor their payloads transfer automatically
through our14-variable Carrier65 proof.

Signed Growth gives119 operations/degree190; Sparse Syntax gives120/190.
Both use the positive transport weight `L=U*E*b^2`. Their full proofs retain
the auxiliary Pell index-three case and establish a signed bound before
recovering B>=0. This addresses an actual missing premise in simply
replacing an older bV transport by b^2. The polynomial center is an exact
formal-quotient expression with an integral, division-free realization;
it is not an input-dependent quotient allowed inside the equation.

Pell Reuse gives118/190 using `L=A*U^3*b^2`, folded into the existing
center. Its proof checks `E<U^2` at every zero and the signed magnitude
bound before using B,C,R or V positivity. Its cheaper companion is99/202.
These are useful degree/circuit tradeoffs within the ten-variable chart,
not an operation-count improvement over65. Applying them at sorted2501
would require the entire free-chart native interface and fresh canonical
completeness, rather than substituting four small integers solely because
they satisfy the same scalar inequalities.

## Direct syntax, spacing and fused-bound mechanisms

Prime Supported Spacing supplies a209-unrestricted-variable quartic with
150 residual squares and5,283 collected monomials. Its new growth center
uses prime524309, with cofinal horizons `t=32*524309^(2k)`. Small finite-field
annihilating indices and CRT arrange that the Pell ordinate F contains
every prime up to t while its binomial selector is odd. Reusing the selector
in `y=F*B` deletes one old spacing multiplier. The proof specifically rules
out dropping F: the selector alone need not carry the required prime support.
This is a whole direct equation, but it concerns its beta-CRT sequence
interface, not our safe-dummy subset controller. No current port shrinkage
or front-coordinate deletion follows without a new correspondence.

Sparse Syntax supplies a208-unrestricted-variable quartic with148 residual
squares and5,987 monomials. Its non-surjective pairing requires a genuine
finite-proof translation: unused numerical codes become extra atoms, which
are replaced by distinct high-depth marker trees in each finite derivation.
The source proves preservation of constructors and freshness inequalities
and changes the label growth constant accordingly. This is more than a
finite recoding trick and is not an index permutation of our fixed K215
tile table. A smaller machine/table would require a complete new compiler
or refutation-calibration construction.

Centers' fused certificate
`U*V-U*E-V*R-1=a^2+b^2+c(c+1)` restores two positive bounds when U,V
are squares, E>=1 and R>=0. Its completeness needs a strict joint reciprocal
budget; the supplied canonical argument gives a total below71/128. The
deleted three coordinates are private guard coordinates of that direct
SOS system. Our65-gate product has no corresponding private pair of
ternary guards, so this is not a current three-variable deletion.

Pell Reuse's direct theorem recycles a large Pell quotient as a modular
center witness. Its small p-adic index-permutation lemma requires p^2
dividing the chosen frame; the source explicitly supplies the necessary
powerful frame or a q^2 scaling. Signed-frame handling is proved separately.
The stated application gives an83-integer/degree624/1,789-operation
equation and other projections. It does not provide a pointwise replacement
for our positive gamma or native transport quotient without re-establishing
their congruence and sign roles.

Signed Growth's affine-horizon and integral-on-zeros scaling produce
209-variable quartic and130/116/107-variable pooled alternatives. The
new horizon is not a polynomial bijection of every old witness; delayed
sign recovery and cofinal completeness are essential. These changes live
in the direct C222 construction and do not reduce the present native
coefficient storage.

## Recommended next research boundaries

The two most concrete compatible follow-ups are the13-variable second-root
carrier and the14-variable scaled auxiliary substitution. Each should get
its own complete ordinary proof at the current tuple, newly authored inert
graph audit, exact operation/degree/liveness evidence and complete rendered

metrics. The lower-degree ordinary strong norm and the ten-variable chart
need broader retained proof interfaces and should remain separate until
those transfers are fully stated and reviewed.
