# Proposed remaining d10 preservation cuts

These source-only candidates carry e, ell, lambda, g, theta, B and b from their earlier prefixes to the same entries in `d10`. They use generic snoc-preservation, stating equalities to named earlier prefix evaluations so that the sums and coefficients remain opaque. They are split into two independent modules of three and four lemmas. The candidate proofs have not been compiled; neither the value of R nor full-recipe semantics is established here.
