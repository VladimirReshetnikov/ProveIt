# First carrier magnitude estimate for the 80 graph

`Direct80CarrierEstimate.adjacent_carrier_bound` is a conditional model lemma: an adjacent Pell norm, signed residue `SignMod F v c`, positivity, and the bounds `1<c<B`, `2c<F`, `8*tau*f≤F` imply `2*tau*f*y<v²`. It uses the adjacent-order theorem twice: first to rule out `v=c`, then to obtain `y<2v`. The signed residue then forces `F≤2v`.

`CarrierSizes.sufficient` derives the three required size bounds from `D≥16`, `1≤c≤U`, `tau≥1`, and `f²=1+D U² tau²`, with `a=D U² tau²`, `F=1+2a` and `B=D*4a(1+a)−1`.

`Direct80GraphCarrierBound.graph_carrier_bound` binds those hypotheses to an actual positive zero of the exact 80-row Lean graph with `beta>0`. It obtains positive adjacent witnesses `tau,f,V0,Z0,y`, the two graph square representations, `Z0=(M*tau*f)*y`, and **`2*(tau*f*y)<V0²`**, without rank, transport, fixed-port-size or index-alignment assumptions. This is a bound on the particular witnesses returned by that theorem; a later join must synchronize witnesses or prove a parameterized version. It does not by itself prove the signed carrier representation, the independent offset bound, transport positivity, halt or iPRA.
