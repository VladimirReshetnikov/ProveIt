Lean**. It attempts to isolate the discrete Pell bound `c≥2n`, the derived
strong-norm bound `f²>c+1`, and the auxiliary inequality `V<y`; see the
[mathematical argument](../p64-qcube76-independent-y-analysis/README.md).

missing hypotheses and tactic failures remain possible. This file is not
evidence of a proof or an iPRA endpoint.
