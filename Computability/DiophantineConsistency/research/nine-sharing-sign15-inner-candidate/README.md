# Sign15 signed-inner external candidate

This research candidate removes sign16 coordinate 16, whose retained defining row is `inner = C*T − Rank`, and substitutes that signed expression into the other six residuals. Its 15 natural witnesses yield a sum of six squared residuals. The candidate is conditional: integer substitution alone does not prove that the deleted coordinate has a natural representative at a zero of the reduced seed. The [proof plan](PROOF_PLAN.md) describes a possible noncircular derivation; it has not been formalized or independently verified. No sign15/sign16 equivalence, Lean bridge, iPRA endpoint, or binding of this JSON to a Lean expression is claimed.

The reproducible [external checker](check.py) verifies the exact structural substitution and port byte hashes. It finds syntactic residual degrees 22, 7, 22, 2, 62 and 46. When each witness is set to `t`, treating beta, MC and MF as independent symbols, the degree-62 coefficient of Na is `64*beta^34`. Because the remaining residuals have lower degrees and the audited external beta bytes encode a positive integer, the displayed sum of squares has exact degree 124 at those fixed ports. This is an external computation, not a Lean degree theorem. One tested integer lift is negative, illustrating why a natural-lift argument cannot be omitted; this test is not asserted to be a seed zero.

The first checker invocation failed because its hand-written test ports used `K` instead of `K0`; this was corrected before the successful [result](result.json). The [independent external audit](../../reviews/nine-sharing-sign15-external-independent/README.md) passed, including its own negative-lift example. The candidate bytes, builder, checker, result and proof plan are frozen pending that review.
