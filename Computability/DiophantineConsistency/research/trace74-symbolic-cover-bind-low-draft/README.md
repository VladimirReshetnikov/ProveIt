# Beta-and-low cover specialization draft

This uncompiled client proposes binding the actual low port and exact short defect identity to the separately checked beta-only conditional cover. The high value remains an input, and strict defect and high bounds remain explicit assumptions. Large powers and fixed ports are locally irreducible during elaboration; no exponentiation threshold is raised. No warning behavior is assumed.

Independent source review, a reconciled provider union including the separately produced low-port identity, prelaunch review and guarded Lean check are required. No complete symbolic cover, all-model cast, graph guard or iPRA endpoint is claimed by this draft.

The [independent source review](../../reviews/trace74-symbolic-cover-bind-low-draft-independent/README.md) found no mathematical blocker and did not run Lean. The beta parent was accepted under a narrowly scoped exact-warning policy, which remains part of its provenance.
