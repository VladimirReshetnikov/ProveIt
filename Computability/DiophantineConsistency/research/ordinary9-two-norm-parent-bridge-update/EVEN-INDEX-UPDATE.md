# Even-index update

The later [uniform Pell argument](../ordinary13-parent-even-index-unconditional-same-data-obstruction/README.md)
removes the kernel-gcd condition cited in the original status note. For any
standard-integer two-norm data with a positive even main Pell index, it
rules out a parent completion that preserves those first/main data. This
uses ordinary Pell classification and is not a Lean or iPRA theorem.

It remains unknown whether any actual compact two-norm zero has an even
main index, and whether changing its first/main witnesses could produce a
parent zero. Therefore the result does not determine the equations'
existential zero status or establish the desired consistency equivalence.
