# A concrete even-index witness at noncompact ports

The [q=4 witness](../ordinary9-even-index-q4-full-zero/README.md) makes the
even-index obstruction concrete. At `(beta,K0,MC,MF)=(3,1,1,1)`, its exact
natural inputs solve both the 9/64 and 10/61 relaxed graphs and have main
Pell index 84. The reviewed even-index argument rules out a shifted-parent
completion preserving those first/main data.

These are not the fixed compact ports. The result does not rule out a
different parent witness at these ports, establish existential inequivalence,
or supply a Lean or iPRA proof.
