# Reverse-shear signed magnitude: fact-level model draft

This unelaborated source isolates the reverse 72/46 positivity step. It assumes the **actual output zero identity** at a fact level and the represented natural bounds `1≤d,f,i,y` and `2≤c`; it does not claim these facts are extracted from a candidate graph. In the intended binding, `d=Delta`, `I=i*c²`, `A=Delta*(f²−1)`, `P=N0`, `QW=N1*Nt`, `Vr=V`, and the candidate signed ordinate is `Vr+ι y`. The identity in the hypothesis uses `A*(Vr²−(Vr+ι y)²)+(Vr+ι y)²`, equal over any commutative ring to the candidate's six sheared auxiliary rows. The final graph output is its four squared residuals minus 2. The checker pins and inspects those exact rows and the 14-variable order; it does not convert JSON to a Lean term.

If the signed ordinate vanished, `Vr=−ι y` and `Vr²≥1`. In the case `f=1`, `A=0` and `I≥2`, so the strong residual square is at least 16. For `f>1`, discreteness of the natural model gives `f≥2`; with `d≥1`, `A≥2`, so the auxiliary residual square is at least 4. Either exceeds the output's total of 2, since all other squared terms are nonnegative. Thus the signed ordinate is nonzero. The generic `positive_magnitude` client uses `ZRowsCoprimeSquares.abs_eq_ι` to represent the absolute value of any nonzero signed model element by a natural-model element; nonzero makes that element positive, and its square is unchanged. A separate ring equality records that the parent auxiliary factor depends only on that square. These arguments do not require `Vr+ι y` to be positive.

The draft uses the existing ordered-ring `shifted_ne_zero` proof shape in the separate 72/46 proof draft and proposes model-specific bounds and an absolute-value client. It has not been run through Lean; typeclass resolution, casts, `nlinarith`, `sq_abs`, `ι_le_ι`, import/provider matching and all axiom reports are open. The `#print axioms` lines are commands only. This is not yet an all-model zero map or iPRA theorem: a literal evaluator must bind `hz` to the candidate zero, prove `d≥1` and `c≥2` from the actual positive graph inputs/ports, and show the other parent graph outputs are independent of the replaced coordinate. The opposite shear direction and a coherent endpoint/provider replay are separate obligations.
