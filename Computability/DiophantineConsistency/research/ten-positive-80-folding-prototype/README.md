# Strong and auxiliary indices for the 80-operation graph

`Direct80Indices.lean` proves, in every model of IΣ₁, that a positive zero of the exact `Direct80Graph` syntax with positive beta supplies a positive strong Pell index and an odd auxiliary Pell index, using the **same** positive roots supplied by `Direct80Adjacent.graph_adjacent`. With the definitions in the theorem, `F = 1 + 2a`, `I = 2 U τ f`, and `A` is the graph's positive base, it obtains `c² ∣ I`, the strong coordinates `(F,I)`, and an auxiliary odd index whose coordinates are `(D I V₀,y)`. It also obtains `SignMod F V₀ (ycoord (2h+1) (A-1))` by the existing quotient modular theorem.

This is a necessary model-level consequence, not a machine halting equivalence or a proof inside iPRA. The graph's independent congruence between `V₀` and `c`, and an all-index folding theorem that identifies `c` with a main Pell ordinate, are not supplied here. In particular, `ArithmeticPellHalfIndex.half_index` assumes the very main norm that the folding step must establish; it is not invoked in this proof. The graph's rank expression may be negative in the difference ring; proving its residue must preserve both signs.
