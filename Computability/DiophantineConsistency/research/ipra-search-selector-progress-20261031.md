# Search selectors and intuitionistic bounded-existential stability

The candidate-PRA search function `searchCode good` returns an index below the bound satisfying `good(index, parameters) = 0`, whenever one exists. `PRABoundedSelector.PRA_proves_selector` proves the associated open selector sentence using model completeness and the previously proved search invariants. This is a classical candidate-PRA proof. `IPRABoundedSelector.selectorOpen` transfers its GG image into the native intuitionistic theory and eliminates the GG translation of the open matrix using the separately established native equality and order decisions. The result is a proof family at arbitrary open terms. Foundation's NNF implication is read natively as a disjunction; its constructive implication consequence is derived explicitly in the search binding.

`IPRASelectorStability` is a theory-independent intuitionistic Hilbert proof. A selector for a predicate, together with stability of its selected instance, gives stability of its existential closure. Its characteristic version also requires the forward pointwise correctness direction at a fresh witness and the reverse direction at the selected term. These are proof hypotheses, not automatically supplied by classical validity.

`IPRASelectorOpenCharacteristic` discharges selected-instance stability for an open characteristic from the checked native open-decision theorem. `IPRASelectorSearchBinding` binds the selector premise to the actual primitive-recursive `searchCode` and obtains existential stability with the two characteristic-correctness premises still explicit. The bound belongs to the characteristic predicate itself. Its author replay status and independent replay, if completed, are recorded in the corresponding review packet; scratch compilation alone is not a replay result.

The outstanding substantive task is a native pointwise characteristic for the exact mapped bounded Foundation ZFC refutation matrix, including its bounded quantifiers, together with both correctness directions. `PRABoundedFormula.PRA_proves_boundedBooleanCharacteristic` is a *classical* result and uses a one-valued correctness convention, whereas this search selector looks for zero: it cannot be silently used as an intuitionistic correctness theorem, and the convention must be reconciled explicitly. The syntactic hierarchy theorem `boundedMatrix_isDeltaZero` alone does not supply that native characteristic. Consequently none of these selector clients establishes the final equivalence with ordinary ZFC consistency.
