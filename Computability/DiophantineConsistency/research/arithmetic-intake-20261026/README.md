The first package's recommended endpoint has85 unrestricted integer
variables and1788 arithmetic gates. Its joint certificate replaces a
collection of private power and divisibility witnesses by one higher-degree
residual, followed by monic elimination. This is a distinct direct-C222
architecture; its operation count is not a reduction of the present
65-operation positive-variable front.

The Pell-growth package's direct endpoint has210 unrestricted integer
variables and degree4. Its principal idea replaces an exact growth power
by a divisibility-constrained Pell ordinate, proving both the needed
growth and a residue-compatible cofinal converse. The separate compact
companion has11 positive variables and104 gates. A growth lower bound
alone cannot replace an exact power or main-index identity in Carrier65;
the package's cofinal residue construction is the part worth retaining as
a possible future arithmetic tool. No such replacement has been proved
for the current front.

The CRT Round22 package's compact companions have10 positive variables
and82,85,87 gates. Its full Start4127 native specialization is a useful
example of a complete tuple transfer, but these are distinct operation /
variable / degree tradeoffs. The quartic and reciprocal-pooling branches
use a separate arithmetic architecture. This intake supplies no change
to the current four ports or their proof.

The signed-horizons package is closest to the present front. Its complete
`compact/PROOF.md`, SHA256
`0898210d5fbc2fb8338e2afda77895d1ce47b8b6d132f5b7bf0c0c1dff7b9aa1`,
replaces the old quotient h and positive auxiliary ordinate y by one
signed carrier `u=k-M*n-R`. It strengthens the strong ordinate with a
factor d satisfying `M|d^2`, authenticates the main Pell index before
recovering the removed quotient, and chooses fresh auxiliary witnesses
in completeness. Its three positive profiles have66,67,68 gates and
degrees174,158,146. The separately read `compact/INTEGER_DOMAIN.md`,
SHA256 `5c4e2020c05da76bab0f52f05f931aca6fa888d5cacca9e2dab252df059d27fc`,
allows f,n,tau to be signed and encodes the other eleven coordinates,
giving36 unrestricted variables and145 gates. Those domain statements
are explicitly proved there; they cannot be transferred by changing a
quantifier line on the present equation.

The ordinary residue-carrier sequence is coherent on inspection, but its
complete finite graphs and all inherited proofs have not been replayed
in this intake. It deletes the same old quotient h already deleted by
Carrier65. Therefore simply composing the two constructions does not
save a second quotient or another multiplication in the final product.
Identifying their new free quotient variables would impose an additional
relation between independently chosen Pell witnesses; no completeness
argument for that relation is supplied by either source.

One exact degree tradeoff can be stated for the current front without
assuming the new residue theorem. Write `I=i*c^2`, `S=Delta*I`. Since
`Ns_scaled=Delta*f^2-S^2=Delta*(f^2-S*I)`, the present polynomial is
identically Delta times the polynomial with strong factor `f^2-S*I`
and final constant1. Delta is positive on the positive domain, so this
division preserves precisely that zero set. However, `Delta*c^2` is
already shared with the main norm, and the existing graph computes S
with one further multiplication. Explicitly forming I costs one new
multiplication. Thus this direct rearrangement yields66 gates, not64;
its expected total degree is138, twelve below150. A complete literal
graph and independent leading-form audit would still be needed before
publishing that separate degree tradeoff. It does not improve the
current65-operation checkpoint.

No inspected summary claims fewer than65 arithmetic gates in a compatible
positive-variable front, and the closest full proof does not directly
produce such a reduction. This is a statement about the inspected
sources and transformations only, not an optimality theorem. The current
Carrier65 and all native proof packets remain unchanged.
