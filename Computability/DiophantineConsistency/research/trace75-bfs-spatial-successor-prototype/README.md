This source-only client adapts the 80-row occupied-block argument to trace75.
Under the exact symbolic direct-BFS ports and a positive trace75 zero it
proposes one common positive N with the radix identity, a decoder conclusion
at each block, a selected block at zero, and selected-block closure under the
cyclic successor. The independently selected lengths are identified through
their common radix. No Lean check has run.

The runner checks eleven accepted regular client roots, the 1,977-object
fresh union, 80 outside providers and captured BFS `DirectBFSBlockOrbit` and
`BlockCyclicIndex` subtrees (326 and 290 project objects), including
companions. Read-only preflight and 18 controls passed. The source, runner,
controls and pinned outer wrapper are held for independent prelaunch review
and coordinated release. Historical unrebuilt-source, package/dynamic
ancestry and external coefficient-file binding remain outside this check;
this is not a machine-halt or iPRA theorem.
