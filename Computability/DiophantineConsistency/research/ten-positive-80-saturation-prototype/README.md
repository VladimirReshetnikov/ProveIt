# No-carry saturation for the 80 graph

`Direct80Saturation.graph_saturation` proves that every positive zero of the actual 80-operation graph, in an IΣ₁ model, has one positive N satisfying the repunit, radix, population and two no-carry conclusions together. It assumes β ≥ 31, odd β, the two port bounds, d ≥ 3, β+1=2^d, and explicit repeated-mask facts: the high-port split, both masks below 2^d, the low mask even, the high mask 4 modulo 8, their populations summing to d, and the positive high-mask product. It uses the existing abstract four-block saturation and repeated-mask population theorems. These mask facts still need specialization to the intended symbolic payload; this does not yet establish decoding, halt, a converse, an iPRA endpoint, or PDF binding.
