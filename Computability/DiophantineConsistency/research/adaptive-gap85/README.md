# Conditional 85-operation gap-coordinate candidate

This is a candidate, not yet a proved Con(ZFC) equation. The pinned 86-operation parent's positive coordinate `alpha` occurs only in `C = q - F - alpha` and in the rank seed `G = alpha + (q-F)*(q-1)`. Replace `alpha` with a new positive coordinate `C` and rewrite the rank seed as `G = (q-F)*q - C`. This removes one graph operation, producing a live 85-operation circuit with the same 13 positive coordinates and four fixed ports. The 85-row `candidate.json` and `AUDIT.json` are generated by `audit.py`.

`audit.py` checks the exact sparse integer-polynomial identities under the signed substitution `alpha = q-F-C` and verifies the other graph rows remain structurally identical. It checks all 85 operations, all 13 witnesses and all four ports are live. The graph count is 47 multiplications, 20 additions and 18 subtractions. Reproduce from the repository root with `python3 Computability/DiophantineConsistency/research/adaptive-gap85/audit.py`. These checks are not a degree or minimality certificate. The independently replayed [conditional literal binding](../../reviews/adaptive-gap85-literal-independent.md) identifies the exact 86 and generated 85 zero tests under explicit signed-gap and signed-rank equivalence assumptions. The independently replayed [coordinate arithmetic](../../reviews/adaptive-gap85-arithmetic-independent.md) proves the two conditional positive-coordinate transformations and rank identity in arbitrary IΣ₁ models. The independently replayed [signed-hole bridge](../../reviews/adaptive-gap85-holes-independent.md) derives the literal equivalence from `F+alpha+C=q`, and the [witness lift](../../reviews/adaptive-gap85-lift-independent.md) constructs a positive 85 witness from an actual positive 86 zero and a positive 86 witness from an 85 zero under the explicit reverse bound. These results retain historical provider boundaries.

Positive witnesses are the unresolved issue. An actual positive 86 zero has `F+alpha<q` by `AdaptiveDirectOrdinate86Transport.expressionAt_transport_positive86`, so its new gap `C=q-(F+alpha)` is positive. Conversely, an 85 zero would recover positive `alpha=q-(F+C)` only if `F+C<q`; that inequality has **not** been established from the new equation. The reverse direction has been checked only under that bound. Until the bound itself is proved for actual 85 zeros, there is no unqualified 85-equation zero-set, machine, HA, or Con(ZFC) claim. No counterexample to the reverse bound is claimed either.
