`P84QcubeGraph.lean` is a generated literal `Expr (Fin 14)` source draft, **not an elaborated or compiled theorem**. `make_graph.py` pins the independently reviewed 84-row JSON candidate and emits the graph with proposed definitional `output_shape`; slot 6 is w, slot 7 is s, slot 8 is r, slot 12 is gamma-prime (named `gamma` in the source), and slot 13 is h. The four port slots are beta, K0, MC, MF. Its `--check` mode compares exact source bytes and does not run Lean. Parsing, elaboration, reduction, resource use, a graph-count theorem and file-to-constant semantics remain untested.

The reviewed standard-integer comparison at compact44 gives positive-zero existence equivalence with the 85/56 graph; it is not yet an arbitrary-model equivalence, iPRA endpoint, object/provider replay, or PDF/JSON binding.
