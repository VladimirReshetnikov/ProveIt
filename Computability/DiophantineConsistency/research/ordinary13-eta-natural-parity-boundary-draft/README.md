# Eta-natural parity contradiction boundary

These are **unelaborated source drafts**, not a Lean-checked contradiction,
zero equivalence or internal iPRA endpoint.

`EtaNaturalParityBoundary` assembles a fact-level eta-zero contradiction from
one coherent tuple of natural first/main norms, the actual first-index n and
quotient w, and the exact signed strong and auxiliary equations. It proposes
using `EtaNaturalNormLargeKDraft.large_first_ordinate` and
`AuxGeometryEtaNaturalDraft.geometry_of_large_k` to obtain the geometric
bounds; `EtaNaturalStrongAuxDraft` to obtain f>0 and the exact positive-offset
absdiff equation; and `ArithmeticPellClassification.solution_index` to
supply a main index for the same natural root g and ordinate c. It then passes
those witnesses to `EtaZeroIndexParityDraft.eta_zero_contradiction` with
`mu=q⁴`. No arbitrary root, alternative rank, eta-positive geometry lemma or
unproved Bézout certificate is assumed.

`EtaNaturalCompactParity` proposes a partial actual-graph client at compact
natural ports with arbitrary K. The separately drafted graph early-index
client supplies tau, n and w, and the graph natural-norm client supplies g;
the compact beta, graph X positivity, rank lower bound and rank window come
from their respective prior source drafts. It still **explicitly assumes**
the graph-to-model represented signed strong and auxiliary equations at the
natural rank. In particular, those premises must be derived by matching the
actual graph fields and using the positive-C signed/natural rank identity;
the client does not assert that those conversions have happened. Its graph
input is a proposed zero and eta=v9=0, with its usual natural-port casts.

All imported graph-factor proofs, exact early-index witness binding, compact
port assumptions, geometry, absdiff conversion, model casts, provider
closure, `#print axioms` outputs, graph zero maps and the internal endpoint
remain to be Lean checked. A proof of the resulting conditional `False` is
not asserted here.
