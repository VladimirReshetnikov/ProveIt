eta-natural prototype and its compact symbolic natural port tuple, it proposes
that a graph zero with `v9=0` leads to `False`, and consequently that such a
zero has `v9>0`. The theorem's K remains an arbitrary standard natural. Its
only explicit graph-level premises are the prototype zero and, for the first
theorem, `v9=0`; the strong/auxiliary equations are supplied by the same
graph's proposed field bindings instead of being assumed separately.

The source uses the compact beta and prior prototype J positivity to obtain
the beta sign, then the prototype gap to get `F≤q`. The latter is needed to
identify the auxiliary graph's signed rank with the **same model-natural

prior graph-level direct auxiliary equation for the same tuple and valuation.

it rewrite to the boundary's `c=Y*k`. The original free-J graph, an unrelated
rank, eta-positive geometry, and a different first root are not substituted.
