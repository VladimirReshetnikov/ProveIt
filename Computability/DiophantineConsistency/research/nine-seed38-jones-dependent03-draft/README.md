# Candidate A/Bprime/W/C short-prefix evaluations

These source-only clients propose the local `d24` A, `d25` Bprime, `d26` W and `d27` C last-entry equations. They also carry M and U to d23, R to d24, b and w to d25, and phi to d26 for later substitutions. The symbolic rank, sums and coefficients are not expanded. These candidates are uncompiled and do not establish full-recipe or JSON/document binding.
