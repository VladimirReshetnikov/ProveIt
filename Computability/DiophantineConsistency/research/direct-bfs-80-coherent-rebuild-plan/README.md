Eight historical receipts did not individually record direct-import hashes, so their producer-time direct selection cannot be reconstructed from those receipts alone; a fresh build must capture and guard it.

The fixed closure has another 101 names absent from the rebuilt-80 capture and outside this two-seed affected cone. They are potential supplements, **not automatically authenticated compatible providers**. A safe build must create a fresh namespace-aware single union, check object and all companion collisions and resolved paths, pin inputs/tool/source snapshots and actual selected direct dependencies, rebuild the 65 in dependency order against the selected 127-stage union, preserve baseline warning policies and rejected attempts, and rerun a full serialized-header/companion closure and independent review. Package-transitive and dynamic provenance, and producer-time/source ancestry of other inherited modules, remain separate obligations. No Lean build or endpoint replay is claimed here.
