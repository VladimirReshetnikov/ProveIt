# Literal 86 residue-guard graph — source-only draft

`P86ResidueGraph.lean` is generated from the pinned independently reviewed
86-row candidate with X at slot 6, Y at slot 7 and the old first-root r at
slot 8. It includes the five-residual output shape. The generator checks exact
candidate bytes and emits or verifies source without invoking Lean.

This is unelaborated source, not a graph-count theorem or model equivalence.
The q-cubed coordinate bridge needs separate ring-field identities, positive
witness maps, reverse signed-root/quotient divisibility, provider compatibility
and guarded Lean checking. The iPRA endpoint and physical-file/PDF semantics
are separate.
