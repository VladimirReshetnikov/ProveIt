# Bounded direct-BFS finite conjugacy coverage

The first monolithic `DirectBFSCoveragePilot.lean` attempted 32-row endpoints and full inverse checks in one process; `observations/directbfscoveragepilot-attempt01/` retains its guarded 180-second timeout. The separate array-backed sample packet checked only the first/last slices.

`generate-v2.py` creates 59 immutable 16-entry chunks and `coverage-manifest-v2.json`. The manifest enumerates disjoint, complete index ranges for row matching, inverse equations and forward/inverse bounds: 943 row indices, 250 horizontal colors and 439 vertical colors. The candidate map arrays and both table sizes/distinguished colors are in the adjacent base and shape packets. Coverage of index ranges in the manifest is an external generator check; it is not itself a Lean theorem.

`run-chunks-v2.py` refuses existing output/observation paths, pins Lean, the check module, candidate tables, manifest/generator and sample receipt, verifies all chunk hashes and exact manifest range coverage, resolves the direct import, and records each source, stdout, stderr, object hash, axiom report and resource samples. It enforces available memory ≥8 GiB, at most two active Lean processes and a per-stage time bound. The recorded direct pins do not establish full transitive source/object, package or dynamic ancestry. `observations/pilot03/`, `pilot04/` and `pilot05/` are individually accepted v2 samples, covering stages 0, 58 and 1 respectively; they do not prove full coverage. The earlier v1 generator, source chunks and `pilot01`/`pilot02` are retained.

`DirectBFSCoverageAggregate.lean` is generated by `generate-aggregate.py` and is **unchecked**. A separate two-chunk aggregate pilot proves only the first 32 rows. An accepted complete sweep and Lean compilation of the aggregate are both needed before any full finite conjugacy assertion; lifting finite row/color facts to internal tilings and machine runs is additional work. No iPRA, external JSON/file binding or machine equivalence is claimed here.
