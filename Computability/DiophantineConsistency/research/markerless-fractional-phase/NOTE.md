## Exact local mask challenge

Suppose preliminary count recovery has already established at most one
selector in every cell, and the origin's forced selector is Start. Its
complemented tuple is `(253,76,252,510)`, packed as

    UStart=17145810173,
    c'_0=2+2048*(2^34-UStart)=69752854530.

Let `W` be an arbitrary unsigned34-bit physical copied-label word, with
no prior equality to the Start colors. Let incoming carry range over
`0..57`, and overapproximate the other bare/spatial summands by two
independent Boolean units. For each binary phase `b=0..46`, let the
temporal contribution be `2^b*epsilon`, with Boolean epsilon. The actual
clause digit is

    (c'_0+2048*W+k+u+v+2^b*epsilon) mod rho.

The mask `mu=2^45-4` tests precisely bits2..44. It accepts exactly residues
`0,1,2,3` modulo `2^45`; taking the digit modulo rho has no effect on
these bits. For each of all47 phases, all58 carries, both Boolean units,
both temporal bits and all four accepted residues, the auditor solves
the actual congruence. Divisibility by2048 determines whether a solution
exists, and division determines its unique W in `[0,2^34)`.
This enumerates every accepted local label without assuming a decoded
label identity first. Each result is substituted into the actual mask
again.

Every accepted result has a nonzero South block. The enumeration also
derives the expected exact identity:

    W=UStart, b<11;
    W=UStart-2^(b-11)*epsilon mod2^34, b>=11.

For low phases the complete low subtotal is less than2048, so no
unaccounted carry can enter the label bits. For high phases the identity
is a complete modular statement. A subtracted power below bit25 can
reduce the South block by at most one; one at bits25..33 subtracts one
power from510, never giving zero; at bits34 and above it vanishes modulo
`2^34`. The receipt stores the exact surviving full words and South values
for every phase, plus a hash and count of every accepted local tuple.

An independent interval route instead assumes `W<2^25`, allowing every
possible assignment of its lower25 bits, and directly solves the same
mask congruence. It finds no accepted word in that interval for any
phase, carry or allowed small summand. Thus the nonzero-South conclusion
does not depend on applying the stated label identity as a premise.
