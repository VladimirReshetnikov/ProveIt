# Generic Pell interval cast transfer draft

This uncompiled source attempts a generic transfer from positive natural slacks and two finite natural Pell ordinate identities to the corresponding identities in any IΣ₁ model, preserving the same `x`, `y`, `eta`, and `zeta`. The first-base subtraction-cast identity remains an explicit hypothesis. The construction avoids applying the represented recurrence to the 462-bit concrete seed during theorem elaboration.

It does not yet instantiate the audited natural seed, prove its source X/Y coordinate identities, establish the exponential or growth conditions, or construct a 74 graph zero. The separate standard-ℕ assembly attempt failed with instance mismatch and recursion-depth diagnostics; see its [retained record](../trace74-boundary-natural-assembly-rebuild/README.md). This draft has not been checked in Lean.

The [independent source review](../../reviews/trace74-boundary-generic-cast-transfer-draft-independent/README.md) checked the shared parameters, positive slacks, both offsets/indices and explicit subtraction-cast premise. It did not run Lean.
