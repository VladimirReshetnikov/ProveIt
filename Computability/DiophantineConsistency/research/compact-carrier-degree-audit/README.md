# Why replacing q²+1 by q+1 is not yet licensed

This is a read-only mathematical lead audit, not a new equation or a size
claim. It concerns the complete ten-positive zero-offset/expanded-transport
proofs and their weighted-root, short-projection and free-J ancestry.

A tempting change is mu=q+1 instead of q²+1. At the actual complete
coefficient tuple beta is vastly larger than2025. Thus q+1>2025 would
still exclude the weighted three-root cancellation mu|2025, even though
q+1 need not be coprime to3. After q is proved even, gcd(q+1,q³(q−1)H)=1
also follows exactly as before from H=−1 modulo mu. Most local positivity
and ratio estimates only need a positive sufficiently large mu.

The complete proof has another essential use of the original choice.
After mu²|e, the first Pell congruence gives

    2*n0 = R+1 modulo mu².

The established bounds R<q⁴<mu² and n0<=R−1 force the difference
2*n0−(R+1), which lies in [0,R−3], to be zero. This identifies the
first index as (R+1)/2 before the ratio/height projection. The argument
is explicit in the copied ORIGINAL_PROOF.md §§6.9,6.13 and FREE_J_PROOF.md
§4 of candidates/ten-positive-short-projection/source/. A carrier q+1
has square only about q², so these bounds no longer identify the index.
The later q³|Y conclusion cannot be assumed to strengthen that congruence:
its present proof uses the ratio and height projection after index recovery.

Consequently neither the complete ordinary equivalence nor a lower degree
is established for that change. A successful smaller carrier would need
a fresh index argument or a compatible reduced native exponent encoding.
