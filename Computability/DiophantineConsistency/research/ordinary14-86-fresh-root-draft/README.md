# Fresh 86 first-root replacement — source draft

This unelaborated source proposal shows how, once the **explicit** equations
`Y=q³*s` and `tau²=1+ell*(ell+k)` are available, an 86 graph zero can be
replaced at slot8 by the fresh old-graph root `ell+k+tau+s`. Its signed first
term is then `−M*tau`; the four other residuals ignore slot8. No comparison
between the original root and s and no sign choice for the original root is
needed. Positive s/tau and the 85 witness transfer are separate clients.

The checker replays the 86 first-field source receipt and tests 81 finite
partial first-norm/field replacements for both original sign branches. Those
controls do not claim full graph-zero existence. All source imports, ring
rewrites, provider ancestry, iPRA endpoint and external file/PDF semantics
remain unelaborated or separate; no Lean is invoked.
