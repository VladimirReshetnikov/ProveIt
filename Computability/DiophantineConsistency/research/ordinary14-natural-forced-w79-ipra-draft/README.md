# Natural-79 iPRA endpoint clients: source proposal

`Natural79Endpoint.lean` proposes transporting the existing source-only
natural-80 endpoint to the literal [79-operation graph](../ordinary14-natural-forced-w79-lean-draft/README.md).
It and the entire graph, zero-w, model, symbolic-port and endpoint chains are
**unelaborated**. Neither this packet nor its checker establishes a Lean
model theorem, axiom closure, a derivation in iPRA, physical coefficient-byte
binding or a connection to the different Carrier65 PDF.

The proposed expression evaluates the 79 graph at the same compact symbolic
port tuple `CompactSymbolicModelIff.p` used by the [natural-80 endpoint draft](../ordinary14-natural-forced-gamma80-ipra-draft/README.md).
The `sameNoZero` client proposes these steps for each `IΣ₁` model:

1. Use the unelaborated natural-82 symbolic source declarations to represent
   port 0 by the natural model's cast of `CompactFixedBeta.betaNat` and to
   supply its positivity.
2. Use the unelaborated strict-K0 source declaration to represent port 1 by
   `(paramKernel rho : V)`. The local 79 positivity source needs that value to
   be a model natural; it does not need the separate strict-K0 positivity
   theorem. Neither representation is a theorem about external payload bytes.
3. Instantiate the unelaborated [79 w-positivity client](../ordinary14-natural79-zero-w-divisibility-client-draft/README.md)
   for every 79 zero, then supply it as the explicit premise of the
   [79-to-80 zero-existence transfer](../ordinary14-natural79-wshift-model-draft/README.md).
   These zero witnesses generally differ in slot 6. The 80-to-79 direction
   increments that coordinate; the other direction needs the proposed
   positive-w theorem and a predecessor.
4. Transport the 80 `PolynomialBridge` across `SameNoZero`; propose native,
   standard and equality-only iPRA derivability clients through the existing
   source adapters. `he.mpr` transfers an 80 zero to refute 79 no-zero, and
   `he.mp` transfers a 79 zero to refute 80 no-zero.

All cited source statements, casts, instances, definitional `change`s and
proof tactics require actual Lean checking. In particular, the positive-w
client depends on the unelaborated zero-w factor-unit, signed-root,
square-divisibility and natural-divisibility drafts; the 80 endpoint depends
on the 81/82 chain, whose original predecessor-order and `partial` helper
sources have separately documented source hazards and inert alternatives.
A future build must select and authenticate its exact coherent source and
provider set. Whether the equality-only adapter's erasure of an entire
sentence reduces to this displayed componentwise target also remains to be
checked. No source snapshot or prior review is silently replaced here.

The model-side route may use an `IΣ₁` model argument as an intermediate
metatheoretic step, but that interface alone does not establish a derivation
in iPRA. Only an elaborated and audited complete conversion to the stated
native, standard or equality-only theory can establish the intended
proof-existence claim. All five `#print axioms` commands are unexecuted.
The symbolic graph and its huge port numerals also require separate association
to external candidate data; the displayed Carrier65 equation uses a different
graph and coefficients, so its relationship needs its own checked endpoint or
equivalence argument.

The deterministic source checker pins this client and 11 directly cited
source interfaces, checks the selected port and theorem signatures and the
two witness directions, and verifies presence of the five unexecuted axiom
commands. It does not run Lean, resolve imports or providers, verify tactics,
prove absence of other assumptions, or bind files.

An [independent source review](../../reviews/ordinary14-natural-forced-w79-ipra-independent/README.md)
checks the selected port facts, zero-map directions and endpoint signatures;
it does not elaborate the client or execute its axiom reports.
