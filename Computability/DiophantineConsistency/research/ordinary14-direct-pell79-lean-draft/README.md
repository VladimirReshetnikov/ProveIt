## Intermediate 84↔80 unscale: model work still required

The reviewed 80 graph is a same-coordinate, positive-input unscaling of the 84 graph. With `M=q³*J*beta` and the signed L represented by `(ell+k,r_old)`, its residual is `A0_80=L²−D` and the 84 residual is `A0_84=M²*A0_80`. A new model client must establish this identity in `DiffRing V` from the literal 84 and 80 graph evaluations, derive nonzero M from positive J and beta, and prove cancellation by M². It must extract all five zero residuals from each sum of squares in the model/difference ring, then transfer them pointwise; it must not treat equality of outputs as the identity, since `P84−P80=(M⁴−1)*A0_80²`. This local client needs no decoder and no coordinate shift. Neither an 80 Lean graph nor this cancellation/evaluation proof is supplied here.

## Intermediate 80↔79 direct-root maps: model work still required

For an 80 zero, let the signed pair `(ell+k,r_old)` represent L and let tau be `ArithmeticSignedPairs.magnitude (ell+k,r_old)`. The 80 norm supplies `tau²=D=1+ell*(ell+k)` only after an explicit signed-pair square/evaluation bridge. `D>0` gives tau>0, so replace slot 8 with tau and preserve all other inputs; show the other four graph residuals do not depend on slot 8, and prove the 79 first residual vanishes. For a 79 zero, use `r_old=ell+k+tau>0`; the represented old L is exactly `−tau`, so the 80 first residual vanishes. Prove this identity in the difference ring/signed-pair representation and transfer the remaining residuals. The maps preserve positive-zero **existence**, not the value of slot 8 or equality of the whole polynomials. Existing `ArithmeticSignedPairs.magnitude`, `magnitude_of_add`, `magnitude_multiply` and `Equivalent` may help, but there is no compiled graph-specific squared-magnitude or witness client here.
