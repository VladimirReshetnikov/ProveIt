# Uncompiled generic gate-model and forward-witness draft

None of the Lean files has been compiled. `SharingGateModel.lean` drafts reusable algebra for representing signed ring values by pairs of natural-like semiring values. It states candidate forward identities for addition, subtraction and multiplication, one- and two-component materialized residual lemmas, a forward gate-list replay, and a reverse replay conditional on a chronological trace of residual-satisfying updates. It deliberately does not claim that the frozen seed supplies such a trace.

`ForwardWitness54.lean` and `ForwardWitness52.lean` are generated from their respective frozen seeds. They construct all 40 and 38 materialized gate coordinates from the 14 natural inputs and four semiring port values using only addition and multiplication. `check.py` checks the text of both sets of right-hand sides and their dependency order against the exact frozen residual AST, and checks both output vector orders. This is an external transcription check, not a Lean result; the constructors' residual-correctness theorems have not yet been written or checked.

To close the model bridge, one must specialize the semiring-to-ring map to the model's `ι : V →+* DiffRing V`, prove all four fixed ports have the required natural images (in particular the once-shifted high port), establish the generated witness assignments' 40 residuals vanish, and extract all 41 residual equalities from the seed's zero via ordered-ring sum-of-squares. The reverse direction still needs a proof that the single final witness assignment induces a chronological trace respecting the graph's acyclic read-before-write order, and that its final difference is the actual current 65-expression value. The externally audited source/graph comparison is not itself a Lean theorem. A complete iPRA endpoint additionally needs the separately drafted seed's degree and all-input normalization proofs plus `NineFreshJones.nine_iff` and the current all-model consistency bridge.

Run `python3 -I -S -B check.py` for the text/ordering check. No Lean process is used.
