## 8. Shared moduli are the default; a separate lowering has no known gain

The cap design RETAINS the existing shared T coordinates and their current
power congruences, with only the selected m² terms restored to m. There
are five distinct base moduli, not one private modulus per power call:
v268(F²+1),v285(B),v291(N),v307(q+1),v313(q), with defining
rows221,236,241,256,262. The q and q+1 moduli serve several calls.
Sharing must be retained unless actual measured evidence justifies another
source representation.

A separate exact identity proposed by root is valid for arbitrary integers:
introduce u=nd and replace

    X+(n-A)Y-m-(2An-n²-1)d=0

by

    X-m+d+nY-AY-2Au+nu=0.

Substitution gives the complete old residual, so both existential directions
preserve A,n,X,Y,m,d. This is an exact quadratic lowering, not a new power
theorem. All actual n ports and the derived t+1 pair are affine in this
chart, so the displayed rows are indeed quadratic. Nevertheless it uses
an nd coordinate per call and can lose shared-modulus reuse. It is NOT
part of this initial cap design and has no known endpoint advantage here.

An inert census of the accepted projected.json confirms that all five
base*.T names survive, while the old private modulus helpers
v269,v270,v286,v287,v292,v308,v309,v314,v315 do not. The accepted monic
projection already removes those lowering intermediates. Thus manually
fusing each T definition to T-2An+n²+1=0 offers no established further
saving in this source. No alternative u-presentation should be expanded
on a count prediction.

One privacy distinction remains essential: the old v175=N² occurs in
front rows158 and162 as well as the N-modulus row241. It cannot be
removed by simply deleting a supposedly private modulus subgraph. Its
name also disappears from the accepted projected chart, through the
separately certified monic substitution into every occurrence. Those
are different justifications. The cap plan leaves the original source
coordinate and its front uses intact until a full projection certificate
licenses their simultaneous replacement.

## 9. Construction/checker boundary before any execution

A future producer should take only the pinned JSON data above, generate
the new pre-guard body in memory, record every old row and coordinate,
and emit a separate literal certificate before any elimination engine
is permitted to run. The certificate must include the full primary
table, all restored linear output terms, retained t+1 recurrence,
new cap/common-product rows, affine lower guard, squared cap and joint
pool expansions, exact even frame and every retained guard specification.

An optimizer-free checker must rederive these complete identities and
validate private-variable nonescape across the deleted comparisons,
old product and old index slacks. The shared-modulus definitions stay
unchanged in this initial design. It
must replay the early front sign proof WITHOUT A/t-slack/K-slack,
the scale/cap sign chronology and every requested-index lower bound.
The ordinary proof can then be applied to the literal checked interface.

Only after independent full code/data review should deterministic
source generation, projection and complete polynomial collection run.
Their resulting actual equations and measurements would require their
own normal/optimized checks and mutations. Neither the tables here nor
the joint row(J) predict a final count, height, support or degree.
