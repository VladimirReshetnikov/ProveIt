# Signed Pell cap: ordinary proposal and construction design

[PROPOSAL.md](PROPOSAL.md) proves an abstract signed-index cap theorem,
its explicit witness construction, and a stronger joint residue theorem
that restores the sign of the scale at an even frame index. It identifies
the precise complete cap certificate needed at the absolute scale and
does not borrow an omitted nonnegative-scale guard. Root independently
read the complete proof and found no gap under its stated inherited
Pell divisibility and abscissa-residue lemmas.

This is **proposal-only work**. No successor source, generator, optimizer
run, collected quartic, equation count or performance claim is delivered.
The accepted Round15 equation and all existing pages remain unchanged.
SOURCE_PINS.json records the exact primary natural-cap theorem and signed
Round15 interfaces used. No incoming program has been executed.

The proof separates the actual large pooled square sum from its bounded
index-error residue. It retains a positive, fully indexed cap; requires
the stronger modulus; and treats every primary abscissa sign separately.
The joint variant puts the cap itself in the pooled congruence, so the
even frame detects a negative scale. The fallback positive-scale theorem
remains independently valid. Countercases show why the weaker modulus,
an odd frame, a reflected cap or an unoriented base-one call cannot be
silently substituted.

[DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) records the non-executable source-construction plan:
all fifteen original undoubled targets, one cap using the common helper,
direct affine cap lower guard, joint pooled congruence and the existing
shared power moduli. It gives exact proposed coordinate/row maps and
the staged sign/CRT witness chronology. A separate quotient-lowering
identity is valid but has no known benefit in this source; the design
keeps its shared moduli. No compiler or source has been materialized.
