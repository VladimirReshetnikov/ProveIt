# A positive C input does not supply a pointwise positive alpha

This is a bounded code-free obstruction note, not a replacement equation
or an equivalence claim. It concerns the proposed substitution

    alpha=q-F-C,
    R=[q*(q-F)-C]*(q^2-1)+(MC+q*MF)*J

in the sealed unshifted bV equations. The new independent C would be
strictly positive, while alpha would cease to be an input. Every other
formula, the ten-input domain and all four coherent fixed ports would
be retained. The one-operation saving is unlicensed: no new graph,
count, degree replay, compiler, application execution or Lean is supplied.

There is an exact conditional escape family. Starting from a finite
refutation and its accepted canonical outer data, one can construct zeros
of the proposed C-input equation with recovered alpha<0 and R<0, indeed
with both tending to minus infinity. This does not assert that a finite
refutation exists, nor exhibit an unconditional false zero. It shows that
recovering alpha>0 at every broadened zero is not an available neutral
arithmetic lemma: together with completeness, it would imply that the
accepted original equation has no positive zero.

## 1. Literal domain enlargement and the missing bound

Use all definitions and either full carrier from the pinned unshifted bV
proposal, replacing only the displayed alpha/C/R definitions above.
Let D=q^2-1 and S=MC+q*MF, so

    R=q^2*D-D*(q*F+C)+S*J,
    B=(K0+w)*C-F,
    e=k-R-1, b=c*T-R, V=f^2*b-c, L=b*V.

The full polynomial is still

    N=Q*(Z^2-M^2*G*V^2)+(M^2*G-Z^2).

Its equivalent norm is (Q-1)*Z^2=M^2*G*(Q*V^2-1).
The source has f=1+2a, f^2=Fsq and G=E*Fseed, where Fseed=1+a.
After root recovery Q=(Delta*I)^2. These are proof aliases, not new unknowns.

The parent row ledgers show that after the separately proved Tp deletion,
alpha appears only in C=qmF-alpha and Gmask=h*qmF+alpha. Replacing
the latter by q*qmF-C is an exact polynomial substitution. The former
becomes the new free input. This identity does not preserve positive
domains: F,C>0 does not imply alpha=q-F-C>0.

There is still an unconditional upper bound. The actual four-port box gives
0<MC<beta<MF<2beta and beta*J=h. Thus S*J<(2q+1)h. Since F,C>=1,

    R< (q^2-q-1)*(q^2-1)+(2q+1)*(q-1)=q^4-q^3.

There is no corresponding lower bound as F,C increase. In particular this
upper bound cannot license the signed-index theorem's exact conditions
0<R<c/2, or its conclusion p=R. Without those strict conditions, the
actual folding argument only identifies p with +R or -R modulo c.
The family below makes the nonzero multiple of c explicit.

## 2. Canonical data retained from the accepted theorem

Conditionally on a finite syntactic refutation, the accepted whole
indexed/native construction supplies positive outer data with

    q=beta*J+1, X=w*q, mu=q^2+1, h=q-1,
    Cbase=q-Fbase-alpha_base>0,
    p=R0>3q, p=3 modulo4,
    c=psi_A(p)=k*Y+eta, 0<eta<k, zeta=k-eta>0,
    E=tau^2, kappa=Kpos/H in Z_{>0},
    t0=[(K0+w)*Cbase-Fbase]/h in Z_{>=0},
    rho0=(k-p-1)/mu^2 in Z_{>=0}, Y/q^3 in Z_{>0}.

Here Fbase,Cbase,alpha_base denote the old independent F and alpha and
the old computed C. The source's product-square factor is called Fseed
throughout; it is distinct from Fbase.
The full unshifted note and repaired parent prove exactly these canonical
promises, including the actual R0>3q bound. No generic positive-R claim
is substituted for them. For the q-cube branch keep s=Y/q^3>0.

All four actual coefficient payloads remain fixed as a whole. Nothing
about the native language, controller or source interpretation is being
redefined. The next steps construct arithmetic zeros directly; they do
not pretend that the altered F,C encode the old native witness.

## 3. A simultaneous positive shift of F and C

Set ell=h*c*mu^2>0. For every integer m>=1 define new positive inputs

    Fm=Fbase+m*ell, Cm=Cbase+m*ell.

Keep J,w,Y,eta,zeta, hence q,X,mu,A,Delta,k,c,E,tau,H,Kpos and
kappa fixed. Recompute the literal C-input R,B,e. Exact identities give

    Rm=p-D*(q+1)*m*ell,
    Bm=B0+(K0+w-1)*m*ell,
    em=e0+D*(q+1)*m*ell.

In particular

    Rm=p modulo c and modulo mu^2,
    tm=Bm/h=t0+(K0+w-1)*m*c*mu^2 in Z_{>=0},
    rhom=em/mu^2=rho0+D*(q+1)*m*h*c in Z_{>=0}.

Since K0>0,w>=1, the coefficient K0+w-1 is positive. Also p<c/2
and D*(q+1)*ell>c, so Rm<0 already for m=1. The recovered alpha is

    alpha_m=q-Fm-Cm=alpha_base-2m*ell<0,

because alpha_base<q and ell>q. Both Rm and alpha_m tend to minus infinity.
In particular Bm>=0 does not restore either missing inequality. The
residue p=Rm modulo c holds, but p=Rm is false for this family.

## 4. Fresh seed and auxiliary witnesses with positive new T

The source's seed period construction is available at the unchanged
positive main index p; it does not depend on the new Rm. Choose arbitrarily
large Pell seed pairs (f0,I0) at A with c^2*tau dividing I0, and put

    i=I0/(c^2*tau)>0, U=i*c^2=I0/tau,
    a=Delta*I0^2, Fseed=1+a=f0^2, g=tau*f0,
    f=1+2a=2f0^2-1, I=2f0*I0=2U*g,
    Q=(Delta*I)^2, S0=Delta*I.

These are exactly the literal doubled product-square quantities; all
strong/seed witnesses are rebuilt together. Choose the auxiliary at the
ORIGINAL positive p, not at the negative register Rm:

    Vstar=chi_S0(p)/S0>0, ystar=psi_S0(p)>0.

Since p=3 modulo4, the explicit source61 odd-index identities give

    Q*Vstar^2-(Q-1)*ystar^2=1,
    Vstar=-c modulo f^2, Vstar=-p modulo c.

Also f^2=1 modulo c, so gcd(c,f^2)=1. As Rm=p modulo c,

    Tm=(Vstar+c+Rm*f^2)/(c*f^2)

is an integer. Its positivity is ensured by the free size of the seed,
not by incorrectly applying a positive-R lemma with Rm. Indeed p>=5
gives Vstar>8Q^2 and therefore

    Vstar/f^2 > 8*Delta^2*(f^2-1)^2/f^2 -> infinity.

For the fixed chosen m, select a sufficiently large seed so that
Vstar/f^2>|Rm|. Then Tm>0. Its literal b satisfies

    b=c*Tm-Rm=(Vstar+c)/f^2>0,
    f^2*b-c=Vstar,
    L=b*Vstar>0.

Thus every auxiliary, strong and first norm is genuine, with the same
positive main index p and a negative Rm register. This is precisely why
the omitted strict bound in the signed-index identification matters.

## 5. Complete positive carrier and norm zero

Put sigma=Y/q^3>0 for the unsheared carrier and sigma=0 for the q-cube
carrier. Both are integers in this canonical family. Increase the fresh
seed if needed so that g>kappa+sigma, and define the new independent input

    nm=g*ystar+(1+h*L)*tm+rhom-kappa-sigma.

It is a positive integer, since ystar>=1 and tm,rhom>=0. Substitution
in the entire corresponding carrier gives

    Z/M=g*ystar.

The auxiliary norm therefore makes N=0 exactly. This checks the entire
polynomial, not only selected endpoints or congruences. Every one of the
ten independent inputs is positive, including Fm,Cm,i,Tm,nm. All four
fixed ports are the original whole indexed tuple. The argument works in
both full carriers; for q-cube the independent s stays fixed and positive.
No graph/equation has been emitted or evaluated.

## 6. Exact conclusion and unresolved equivalence

The result is the conditional ordinary implication

    finite refutation => broadened C-input zeros with alpha_m<0,Rm<0.

It follows that a theorem asserting alpha=q-F-C>0 at EVERY broadened
positive zero, combined with the accepted completeness theorem, would
prove that there is no original positive zero (and hence the retained
Con(ZFC) assertion). Such a theorem is not merely a harmless domain
recovery step in an equivalence proof.

This note does NOT prove an unconditional extra zero, prove that ZFC is
inconsistent, or refute existential equivalence of the broadened equation.
It remains possible in principle that all broadened zeros could yield a
refutation by another argument, or be normalized to an old zero using a
different front/native construction. No such normalization is supplied.
The pointwise alpha-positivity route fails to provide it, and the existing
rank/native proof cannot simply be reused at the negative aliased Rm.
No one-operation endpoint is licensed. All accepted parents and the held
Tp-only finite preparation remain untouched.
