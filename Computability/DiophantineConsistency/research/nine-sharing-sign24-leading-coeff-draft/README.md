# Sign24 leading coefficient ingredients draft

These source-only modules build on the separately audited N0 axis identity.
The first proposes checking that every other seed residual has weighted
syntax degree at most two when only seed coordinates 16 and 17 have weight
one, and then uses the existing weighted-evaluation theorem to bound the
specialized one-variable polynomial degree. The distinguished seed input is
set to one by the axis, and arbitrary integer port coefficients are retained.
The second proposes computing the coefficient of `X^8` in the specialized
N0 square as one.

The sources are uncompiled. They do not yet connect the individual bounds to
the coefficient of the actual complete sum of squares, prove a lower bound,
or bind external JSON/byte/PDF data. The existing local heartbeat/resource
guards apply; an independent prelaunch review and assigned Lean slot are
required.
