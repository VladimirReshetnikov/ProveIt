has total degree at most 12 for every integer port vector, and for current
ports. A finite residual bound of six supplies the sum-of-squares bound;
removing the unused input cannot raise degree. No Lean result is claimed
until the guarded runner completes and is independently audited. This is an
upper bound, not an exact lower bound or an external JSON/PDF binding.
