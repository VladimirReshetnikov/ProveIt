# Conditional 73-operation center candidate

`candidate.json` is generated from the pinned 75-operation trace graph by removing the two rows `LBt = V2 * Bt` and `center = n - LBt`, then substituting the new input `n` for every use of `center`. It has the same ten positive witness names and four fixed coefficient ports. The resulting graph has 73 live operations: 43 multiplications, 17 additions, and 13 subtractions. All ten witnesses and four coefficient ports remain live.

The retained external check `observations/graph-check01/` recomputes the exact parent-to-candidate row mapping and checks liveness, operation count, and coefficient raw bytes. Structural degree propagation gives an upper bound of 352; evaluating the full diagonal polynomial modulo 1,000,000,007 gives degree 352 with leading coefficient 316589492. Thus the external computation certifies exact total degree 352 for the pinned coefficient payloads. It also checks 200 deterministic modular substitutions. The coefficient metadata comes from the parent graph; the checker reads separately retained gzip payloads whose **raw decompressed** hashes match that metadata, without asserting that their compressed bytes match the parent's files.

The input graph and degree computation are external artifacts, not Lean file-semantics theorems. This candidate is conditional research: positivity of the transformed witness and both directions of positive-zero equivalence remain open. The separate `ALGEBRA.md` records a checked all-ring identity for the Lean expressions; it does not yet identify the JSON rows with those expressions.
