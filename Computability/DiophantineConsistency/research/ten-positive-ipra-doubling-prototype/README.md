# Ten-positive Pell doubling and coprimality: Lean prototype

This separate prototype formalizes one arithmetic ingredient in the ordinary proof in [`../ten-positive-doubled-product-square/PROOF.md`](../ten-positive-doubled-product-square/PROOF.md), sections 2–3. That proof describes distinct ten-positive-coordinate 84-operation/degree-436 and 85-operation/degree-328 equations. This prototype has **not** interpreted either equation's JSON, shown that its ports or graphs have these shapes, or proved an all-model zero/refutation equivalence or an iPRA endpoint. It does not execute the other packet's programs.

All results below are Lean theorems for an arbitrary model `V` of `IΣ₁` with the repository's `ORingStructure` interface. Set `a=delta*U²*E`, `G=E*(1+a)`, `Fsq=1+4*a*(1+a)`, and `Q=delta*(Fsq-1)`, with subtraction interpreted as model truncated subtraction.

* `doubled_trace` proves `Fsq=(1+2*a)²` without positivity assumptions.
* `doubled_divisibility_identity` proves the subtraction-free identity `delta*Fsq=delta+G*(4*delta²*U²)`. The final theorem separately proves the exact truncated-subtraction factorization `Q=G*(4*delta²*U²)`.
* `adjacent_unit_certificates` builds natural signed Bézout certificates for `G` against `G*t-1` and `G*t*V0²-1`, provided those two unsubtracted expressions are positive. `adjacent_product_coprime` derives that every common divisor of `G` and the product of those two differences divides one, without assuming unique factorization or a gcd operation.
* `doubled_radicands_coprime` specializes that conclusion to the proposed radicands: if `Q>0` and `Q*V0²>0`, then any common divisor of `G` and `(Q-1)*(Q*V0²-1)` divides one. `positive_doubled_coprime` derives the two Q-positivity premises from positive abstract `delta`, `U`, `E`, and `V0`, where `V0` represents a positive magnitude; those ingredient bounds are not yet linked to the actual graph witnesses.

This is a coprimality component, **not** root extraction: deriving square roots from the product equation in arbitrary models, rational adjacent-Pell descent, the source-specific arithmetic, calibration to the retained indexed tuple and machine, the two existential directions, and internal iPRA proof existence all remain open.
