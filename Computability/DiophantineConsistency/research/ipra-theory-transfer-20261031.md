# Conditional classical-to-intuitionistic PRA-language transfer

The repository's `PRALanguage.language` has arity-indexed finite codes for primitive-recursive function symbols. Foundation's intuitionistic syntax and Gödel–Gentzen translation are generic over languages; `IntuitionisticPRABridge.from_classical` applies them to this expanded language. For any intuitionistic theory `T` over that language, it takes an actual classical candidate-PRA proof of a sentence and explicit `T` proofs of the negative translations of every candidate-PRA axiom, and obtains a `T` proof of the translated sentence. The finite-assumption discharge is constructed from intuitionistic finite-context deduction, conjunction and modus ponens. No theory-level cut or extra axiom is assumed.

`IntuitionisticPRACurrent.translated_current` specializes the source sentence to the exact mapped 14-variable no-zero/ordinary-Foundation-consistency equivalence, but explicitly assumes its classical candidate-PRA proof and all axiom-image proofs. `current_with_target` additionally assumes a proof from this translated sentence to a supplied intuitionistic target. These are proof-composition interfaces, not a definition of native iPRA or a proof of its equivalence. In particular, native open induction must be formulated with intuitionistic implication, and Foundation's classical `succInd` uses addition by one rather than the primitive successor symbol; naive structural embedding of its classical NNF implication would not establish the intended native induction axiom.

The [author and two independent peers](../reviews/ipra-theory-transfer-prefix-replay/README.md) match all four source, object, full-log and direct-dependency rows on a guarded 307-module prefix of the still-running fixed-source 14-equation build. The full parent is not yet a completed proof, and reconstructed-origin and external-package ancestry remain inherited. Remaining work includes specifying and verifying the native intuitionistic PRA presentation, proving every required Gödel–Gentzen axiom image in it, and proving the bridge from the translated target to the intended literal intuitionistic equivalence.
