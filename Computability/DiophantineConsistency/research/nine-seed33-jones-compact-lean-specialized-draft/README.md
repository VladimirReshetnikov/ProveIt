It is a proposed specialization, not a Lean theorem. The number 45 is a named-node count, not an arithmetic-operation count. Fixed coefficients, finite sums and enormous powers remain symbolic.

The two generated targets `WellScopedTarget` and `SemanticTarget` are definitions of propositions, not proofs. Both scripts are ordinary unverified translators; regeneration is not a verified parser or a JSON-to-Lean semantics theorem.

A bounded development can check the language alone against the audited sign33 named provider, then the recipe and Boolean scopedness. Adapt the four generic snoc/value cuts and typed prefix-embedding/lookup proof to the new language; they describe the same pattern but require new Lean checking. Next factor 45 recipe entries into named prefixes and establish short-prefix lookup/preservation and substitution lemmas in dependency order, retaining sums and coefficients as opaque expressions. Compose the resulting output equality with the checked `Sign33NamedFormula.eval_current`; the desired final statement is exactly the generated `SemanticTarget`, for arbitrary `CommRing R` and nine inputs. An independently checked source/guard survey, bounded runs, precise exits and terminal audits are required at each step. Resource fit and definitional behavior cannot be inferred from the sign38 checks.

The Lean statement would bind the specialized Lean DAG to the checked Lean sign33 expression, not this JSON, Markdown, or PDF. A separate verified parser/serializer or explicitly trusted audited deterministic translation, and a checked or independently reviewed human transcription, is needed for those boundaries. No fixed-byte coefficient or exact operation-count claim follows.
