## Meaning of the inherited translation order

The descriptive inverse_order entries distinguish additional centering from
the initial T translation. The stored centered translation certificate already
COMPOSES BOTH shifts. It can therefore be inverted ONCE directly to projected
coordinates. Alternatively one may invert the additional centering shift,
then the initial T shift; the additional shift is total minus initial.
Applying the total centered certificate and then the initial T certificate
would apply the initial shift twice and is not the intended inverse map.
The complete stored certificates are preserved unchanged, and neither new
program executes either translation or any optimizer/source compiler.
