Because (A-n)²==(A²-1) modulo T and n(2A-n)=T+1, the usual polynomial
Pell identities imply

    chi_k-(A-n)psi_k == n^k (mod T),
    n^k*(chi_k+(A-n)psi_k) == 1 (mod T).

These are integer congruences; no negative exponent is needed. If X is
negative, the actual power row therefore forces T to divide m*n^k+1.
That integer is nonzero for every signed integer m: otherwise n^k would
divide1, whereas n>=3,k>=1. Moreover

    |m*n^k+1| <= (W-1)^W+1 < W^W < A < T.

The last inequality follows from n>=3, n<W and A>W²; specifically
n²+1<W²<A, so T>5A. This excludes X<0 without assuming m>=0.
For X>0, the actual congruence instead makes T divide m-n^k. Its absolute
value is at most W-1+(W-1)^(W-1)<W^W<T. It must be zero, and hence

    m=n^k>0.

correct indexed pair would permit m=-n^k. The extension should be applied
only after the relevant ordinate signs have been recovered. The other
thirteen requests keep their inherited arguments and chronology.
