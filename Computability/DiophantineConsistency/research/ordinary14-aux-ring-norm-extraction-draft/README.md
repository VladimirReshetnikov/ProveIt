# Source-only ring residual to natural norm extraction

`RingNormExtraction.lean` is an **unelaborated, uncompiled source proposal**. It advances the 78/79 auxiliary-bound route from explicit difference-ring residual equations to the natural first, main and strong norms required by the separately reviewed `../ordinary14-aux-norms-to-bounds-draft/`. It does **not** extract those residual equations from either literal graph, or claim a graph-zero, arbitrary-model theorem, Lean/iPRA endpoint, provider replay or PDF/JSON/gzip binding.

`root_of_scaled_ring_square` proposes to handle either sign of a main ring value Z: `ModelDifferenceRing.nonneg_or_nonpos` represents Z as `ι z` or `−ι z`; after injection, `ArithmeticAdaptiveQuotients2011.scaled_quotient` supplies a natural root under the explicit positive scale U. No orientation or natural signed subtraction is assumed. `first_norm_of_ring_residual` converts the direct first-root ring equation into the natural first Pell norm by a polynomial identity. `strong_norm_of_ring_residual` adds Delta on both sides of the ring residual before injecting, yielding a natural strong equation without identifying natural truncated subtraction with ring subtraction.

`bounds_of_direct_field_residuals` proposes the fact-level composition into `1<Q ∧ 1<z²`. All positive parameter, c and Delta definitions, first/main/strong ring residuals, and signed Q/z identities are explicit hypotheses. The natural main root is supplied by the preceding proposed sign-free extraction, and the discriminant identity by a ring normalization. The imported `NormsToBounds` is a bare future-overlay name for another independently source-reviewed but uncompiled draft. These source proofs, coercions, `nonneg_or_nonpos` case rewrites, `ring` and tactic behavior have not been checked by Lean.
