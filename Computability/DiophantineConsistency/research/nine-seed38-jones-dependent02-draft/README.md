# Candidate M/P/K short-prefix evaluations

These source-only clients propose the `d21` M, `d22` P and `d23` K last-entry equations in terms of their immediately preceding prefixes. They also carry R from d18 to d20 and d22, U from d19 to d21, and h from d18 to d22 for subsequent substitutions. Rank, sums and coefficients remain opaque. The candidates are uncompiled and do not establish a full-recipe or JSON/document binding.
