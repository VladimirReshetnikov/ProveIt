## Two unconditional facts

The accepted LV13 ordinate is exactly
`y=1+4*(b*(m-L*V*G)-G)` (`ThirteenLVCarrier.y`), so y is1 mod4 and cannot
vanish. Positivity of this abbreviation is unnecessary. With positive f,
positive delta and `Theta=delta*(f²-1)`, the new generic
`NearSquareNormZero.norm_ne_zero` therefore also proves U!=0 for the signed
13-variable scaled auxiliary: f=1 gives y²; f>=2 uses
`4*Theta*U=(2*Theta*V)²-((2*Theta-1)²-1)*y²`. This is a useful genuine
extension, but does not alone reduce a paid operation.

Moreover `delta=(a+2)²-1` is0 or3 mod4, as is f²-1. Consequently Theta is
0 or1 mod4. For arbitrary integers V,y, `U=Theta*(V²-y²)+y²` is congruent
either to y² or V², hence0 or1 mod4. Thus **U!=-1 unconditionally**. No
Z=1 assumption or sign of y,V is required. The frozen sphere proof used
the sufficient weaker conditional exclusion after Z=1; it remains correct.
This observation is not a claim that U is positive: varying the carrier
coordinate can make the negative y² coefficient dominate when Theta>1.

## Paid grouping comparisons

The frozen14 common prefix costs62 operations and has factor degrees
P22,Q18,U30,Z34,W2. The present71/80 grouping
`(PQ)²+(UW-1)²+Z²-2` adds9 operations; present72/68 groups W with Q and
leaves P,U,Z as the three raw squares, adding10. All costs include constants,
squares, additions and subtraction of the right-hand constant.

Within the simple family of three squared products partitioning the five
factors into sizes2,2,1, exactly one containing W as a residual, the
71/80 arrangement minimizes the largest grouped degree: PQ40,UW32,Z34.
This is only a restricted grouping comparison, not a global lower bound.

Two squared groups can add8 operations. The best balanced grouping in that
restricted family is `(QZ)²+(PUW-1)²-1`, with grouped degrees52 and54,
thus14/70/108. QZ is unconditionally nonzero; the sphere forces QZ=1 and
PUW=1 after the negative-unit exclusions (or Z=1 before the old U lemma).
Its entire positive zero map is valid. However it is dominated by the
already-recorded prospective14/67/106 normalized product, so this note
recommends **not** materializing70/108.

For the signed13 common68-operation prefix, raw two-group spheres add6
operations. The least-degree partition is `(PQZ)²+U²-2`, with degrees152
and96, giving13/74/152. It is dominated by accepted13/72/124. The other
nonzero-factor regroupings likewise give no improvement over13/75/96;
the previous74/164 square-coefficient variant remains dominated. The
unproved V-sign shortcut is not supplied by either mod4 observation.

The normalized14 product instead adds precisely four multiplications and
one subtraction to the62-row prefix:
`P*Q*U*W*Z-1`. Its leading degree is106, and the scaled parent is exactly
delta times it. Positive delta permits mathematical cancellation with no
paid division or new variable. This67/106 point was already recorded in
`thirteen-variable-independent/NORMALIZED_LEAD.md`; the new authorized
work will bind a complete current2011 graph and external Lean endpoint,
not claim discovery of a new algebraic identity.
