# Candidate ring-variable margin and Kc cuts

These two stages propose that d42 Dm equals the symbolic `RingVars.marg`, and d43 Kc equals `dvsr * (2*marg-1)`. Rank and large sums remain opaque; the Kc proof uses the checked Bd binding without expanding it. The guarded check passed Lean compilation and its independent terminal audit. These are cut bindings, not named-evaluator equality or JSON/document binding.
