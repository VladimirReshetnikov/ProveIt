# Sign23 two-row axis coefficient and lower bound draft

These source-only modules propose composing the checked N0 and Dc2 ingredients.
The list lemma sums the eighth coefficients as the counts of **both** distinct
exceptional rows; the concrete seed is then checked to contain each once, so
its axis coefficient is two for every integer port vector. A final module
proposes identifying this specialization with the direct Fin23 polynomial and
deriving the degree lower bound eight. No cancellation is assumed: every other
residual is bounded to degree at most two on the axis.

The new modules are uncompiled and may require further proof repairs. Even if
checked, exact degree equality still needs the separately checked upper bound,
and no external JSON/port-byte or PDF correspondence follows. Independent
prelaunch review and an assigned Lean slot are required.
