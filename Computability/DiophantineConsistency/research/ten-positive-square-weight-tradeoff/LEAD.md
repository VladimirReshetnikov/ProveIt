The proposed replacement is

    Lsquare=I²+b, Wsquare=1+g*Lsquare,

where g=q-1 (original) or q(q-1) (direct). The current Lindex=kI+b
uses k=eta+zeta. The exact parent graphs ALREADY compute I2=I*I for the
strong norm, before the private kI product. That private product has only
one consumer, L. Replacing L's kI operand by I2 and deleting the kI row
therefore needs no new arithmetic gate. If fully materialized and audited,
this exact deletion would give97 operations (57 multiplications,22 additions,
18 subtractions) or102 operations (60/23/19). Those prospective graph counts
are not a substitute for a complete new literal audit.

The ordinary transfer has a direct proof. Before transport, every zero
of either proposed kernel has the same unchanged root extraction, folding
and first-index argument as the sealed proof. Thus

    c>k>=8X(muY)²+4>H, I=i*c²>=c²>k.

Consequently I²>kI>HI, so every complete negative-transport majorization
is preserved, including the direct X/q⁴ term. The weight remains integral
and equals1 modulo g, preserving all later partial fractions in their
original order. The native/compiler interface is unchanged.

For positive completeness, take any zero of the sealed index-weight
parent and set

    dL=I(I-k)>0, n_square=n_index+dL*B.

Here B means Bt in the original or the entire direct carrier B. Parent
soundness gives B>=0, so the new n is positive. As the weight difference
is g*dL, the normalized centers agree identically. Every other kernel
value and all norm arguments are unchanged. This polynomial n shear is a
universal ring identity; its inverse need not preserve positivity. The
new soundness direction uses the preceding majorization proof, not an
unjustified positive inverse map.
