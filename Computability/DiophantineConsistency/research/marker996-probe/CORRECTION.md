# Correction: the marker-996 semantic calibration is not established

The frozen probe passes its written checks, but one of those checks uses
the wrong physical label coordinate. Its proposed complete alignment and
canonical-marker conclusions are invalid. No main page or accepted native
theorem was promoted from this exploration. The maintained 2011 tuple has
a different marker with no such extra physical label contribution.

`/root/controller_certificate` found the mismatch while reading the full
ordinary proof. ROOT independently confirmed it from the actual doubled
coefficient array. In the packed complemented label word

    W = West + 2^8 North + 2^17 East + 2^25 South,

the coefficient at each physical source is 2048 times its bit weight.
The complete physical placement is:

| Physical positions | Coefficient divided by 2048 | Actual packed block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 978+j, 0<=j<8 | 2^j | West |
| 960+j, 0<=j<9 | 2^(8+j) | North |
| 969+j, 0<=j<8 | 2^(17+j) | East |
| 942+j, 0<=j<9 | 2^(25+j) | South |

In particular c'_978=2048, whereas c'_969=2^28. The forced Start has
packed complemented colors (253,76,252,510). Therefore physical bit 978
is the low bit of 253, equal to 1. The frozen probe instead inspected
the low bit of the third tile component's complement, 252, equal to 0.
The descriptive legacy slot name did not prove that coordinate equality.

At marker 996 the actual odd part is selector0 plus physical978. For
Start these units add to 2, so the tested parity is even. The claimed
forced odd temporal marker does not follow. The proposed canonical sum
Start+physical978 is also 2 at Start and is not Boolean. Consequently
the offset enumeration's forced-one premise and the claimed decoded
marker transition do not bind the actual native equation. Numeric kernel,
mask, controller and whole-port calculations do not repair this error.
This does not prove that the specialized polynomial itself has a spurious
zero; it invalidates this proposed proof and witness construction.

The original probe, receipt and note remain frozen as historical evidence.
ROOT's earlier finite review missed the same coordinate mismatch; its
semantic acceptance of the marker transitions and canonical flag is
withdrawn. The complete conditional fractional-phase lemma remains valid:
it derives the South block directly from the packed coefficients and
does not use this wrongly named horizontal bit. The published 2011 proof
uses its separate marker and is unchanged.

A separate proposal uses marker 997 and physical979, which is the bit at
position 1 of packed West. Start253 has that bit 0. It retains center995
and may retain length1997, but requires a fresh full proof, actual packed
coordinate checks, finite certificate and independent reviews. It is not
accepted by this correction. Historical 996 passing receipts must never
be presented as evidence for the repaired tuple.

It then checks the actual Start bits at 978 and 979 and rejects the
frozen marker-996 forcing premise. The original probe hash is authenticated,
