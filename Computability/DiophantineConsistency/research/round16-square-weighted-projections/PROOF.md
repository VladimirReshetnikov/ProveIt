## 3. Integral monic elimination and complete free partition

In any commutative integer algebra a residual `v-f=0` or `f-v=0`,
where f is an integer polynomial not containing v, is equivalent to
replacing every other residual p by p[v:=f]. Forward projection forgets
v; reverse extension restores it uniquely as f. No positivity of f is
required because the coordinate domain is unrestricted integers.

Every accepted restoration has degree at most two. The producer uses
exactly default support16, no separate affine cap, affine-first order
and no keep-list. It rejects a quadratic restoration if the target has
a nonlinear occurrence in any other current residual. The independent
checker does not import or replay optimizer choice code. Instead it
proves each actual defining equation coefficient by coefficient,
substitutes into every surviving residual, and rejects any degree
increase above two. It checks name/kind/degree/support metadata and
reconstructs every complete output row, label and variable record.

Zero residuals created by substitution may disappear; all are accounted
for by the full sequential replay and final original-row map. The
retained coordinates are exactly those used by final residuals. Every

step or to the complete actual unconstrained set. This disjoint partition

every monic restoration. Induction over the certificate proves all
