that exact E. Only transform, optimize, verify, expand and polynomial
helpers are called. The independent checker imports only check_direct's
polynomial, factorization, projection and literal-SOS helpers. It does
not call that file's old main, source_transform, domain, pruning,
translation or natural/compiler entry points. All copied bytes are
reviewed, even though those extra entry points are not invoked.

context and diagnostic is anchored by the accepted source seal. The

fresh whole factorization and certificate, actual Horn witness map,

old324 retained set, or a manual dimension subtraction. Weighted E_B

reviewed core/vendor overwrites, receipts inside generated data and
receipts inside a sealed author packet. Materialization requires an
empty destination. Pure rebuilds use private temporary directories and
compare the complete generated inventory; existing inputs are read-only.
