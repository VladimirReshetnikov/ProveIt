## Independent degree proof without a giant expansion

Give each unknown degree1 and the four ports degree0. Set

    q0 = beta*J,  k0 = eta+zeta,
    R0 = q0^3*(q0-F),
    Bt0 = (K0*q0+X)*(q0-F-alpha)-q0*F.

The proposed complete leading factors, derived directly from the current
literal graph, are

    L0 = -2*q0^8*k0^2*X*Y^2*Bt0,                       degree15,
    L1 = 8*q0^2*k0*X^2*Y^4*(q0^2*Y*gamma+R0),          degree13,
    La = 2*X^2*Y^2*f^4*u*R0,                          degree13,
    Ls = i^2*k0^4*Y^4.                               degree10.

The output leader is the complete product L0*L1*La*Ls, degree51. Its
monomial

    J^15 * F * eta^7 * X^6 * Y^13 * f^4 * i^2 * u * gamma * alpha

has coefficient -32*beta^15, independent of K0,MC,MF. Actual beta is
2^94517-1>0, so this coefficient survives the current specialization.
The absent T,r,zeta in this selected monomial does not prove independence
of them; their dependence requires the separate whole-graph checks below.

There are three leading cancellations that need exact identities, not
degree-max propagation. All symbols below are actual named graph cuts.

1. Write `Z=M*ell+h` with
   `h=M*(k+r)+Y*qm-Bt*W*q3`. Then

       A0=2*M*ell*h+h^2-M^2*ell*k-M^2.

   Here deg(M*ell)=8, deg(h)=7; the degree16 square terms cancel exactly
   and the degree15 leader is L0.

2. Write `Dstar=U*a*c+v` with `v=U*X+H*shift`. Since Delta=a^2+H,

       A1=2*U*a*c*v+v^2-U^2*H*c^2-U^2.

   The degree14 terms cancel exactly, leaving L1 of degree13.

3. Actual y=V+u gives `y^2-V^2=2*V*u+u^2`. This is graph gate g81;
   its degree is7, not12. With V leading -f^2*R0, the auxiliary factor
   has leader La rather than a spurious degree18.

A new standard-library sparse polynomial auditor can prove these small
identities in cut variables and independently expand the required bounded
layers in the literal graph. Every other gate uses ordinary homogeneous
sum/product propagation. It must record every actual gate's complete
leader, degree, coefficient list/hash and any cancellation, and compare the
four terminal leaders and complete output product. It must not expand the
entire degree51 output. At each gate, specialization of its complete formal
leader at the actual four ports modulo a fixed small prime supplies an
exact nonzero witness; failure at a chosen prime is not proof of zero and
must be handled explicitly. The final selected coefficient has the stronger
exact integer nonzero argument above.

For all13 unknowns, exact unequal modular graph evaluations at positive
input tuples differing only in that unknown prove genuine dependence at
the actual fixed coefficients. The analogous four-port perturbations test
formal port dependence; they are not positive-zero equivalence tests.
