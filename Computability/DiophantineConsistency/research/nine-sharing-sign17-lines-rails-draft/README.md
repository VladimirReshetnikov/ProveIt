# Source-only lines-to-target-set and rail link

This uncompiled client proposes deriving the target sets, their rail formulas, and the 430 transition-row formulas directly from the parametric decoder applied to the 715 in-Lean decimal digits. It does not itself prove all printed table/rank layout, K byte equality, PDF extraction or external filesystem semantics. Fresh guarded preflight, independent review and allocation are required.
