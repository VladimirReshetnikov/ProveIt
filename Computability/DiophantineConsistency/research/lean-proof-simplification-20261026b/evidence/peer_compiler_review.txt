Verdict: I found no mathematical circularity, changed circuit data, or lost
assumption in the four reviewed prototypes. The claims are appropriately
local. They do not establish any new end-to-end consistency equivalence.

1. WitnessLayout

The renaming lemma is just a pointwise equality of the same natAssignment
functions. The witness-splitting equivalence is constructive: restrictions
use Fin.castAdd and Fin.natAdd, and combination uses Fin.addCases. It does
not invoke choice or assume represented predicates are decidable.

conjunctionCompact builds precisely the old witnessCount r+s and the same
sum of two squares after the same renamings. disjunctionCompact builds the
same product after the same renamings. The proofs use only r.correct and
s.correct, not correctness of the old conjunction/disjunction constructors.
The examples comparing expression fields by rfl substantiate literal
expression preservation; witness-count preservation is also apparent from
the constructor definitions. Zero-filled unused witness blocks are valid
because Rep quantifies natural witnesses, including zero. The helper also
handles an empty witness block.

Permissible claim: these two representation constructors have factored
proofs with definitionally unchanged expression data and unchanged witness
counts. Do not claim they reduce polynomial degree or variable counts.

2. GapPowerCertificate and SplitPowerCertificate

Both certificates refer directly to the original naturalNodes, with no new
node list, index map, output expression, or rewiring. The imported literal
circuit modules do not import their corresponding generated Steps files.
label_steps is a finite kernel-checked proposition about the actual nodes.
It is therefore independent of the old pure-power proof chains.

The existing run_power theorem performs strong induction on node index.
Actual Topological evidence makes both multiplication operands earlier
than the current node. A positive label at a non-base node requires two
nonzero operand labels and an additive exponent equation. No power value
identity is assumed as an input. At the base, exponent one gives a
tautological identity. Hence the certificate is not circular.

Label zero means 'not certified as a power', not exponent zero. The public
power theorem correctly retains hp : label i != 0. No identity is claimed
for any zero-labelled node. The exact bounds i<484 and i<515 are converted
using each original natural_length theorem. This blocks default getD
behavior from introducing an out-of-range node into the conclusion.

The Gap example proves value(node375)=value(node20)^2200. The Split
example proves value(node407)=value(node36)^1201. These identities are
valid over every commutative ring with the same symbolic fixed parameters
and inputs as the original circuits. They do not specialize or calibrate
the fixed parameters and do not identify the entire output with a target
Diophantine equation.

Permissible claim: a checked finite exponent certificate derives the
same local pure-power facts for the literal naturalNodes, replacing the
need for their repeated algebraic chains. Do not claim every generated
step or semantic recipe theorem can be deleted: non-power nodes, named
semantic cut points, and final recipe correctness still need proofs.

3. CircuitDispatch

reg is an abbreviation through the existing value evaluator, not a freely
chosen value assignment. step specializes the already-proved
run_node_equation to the original Gap naturalNodes and its topology proof.
It therefore assumes neither the desired node equation nor the candidate
base identity. The i<484 bound rules out out-of-range nodes.

base_identity selectively expands nodes 20,18,19,10,0,9 and uses ring. This
is a local algebraic identity for the literal circuit. The step theorem is
not globally registered as a simp lemma, which avoids silently expanding
the entire DAG recursively.

Permissible claim: named local step lemmas can be obtained on demand from
the generic evaluator theorem, and the illustrated base identity follows
without a pre-generated theorem for every intermediate node. This alone
does not replace all semantic cut points or prove the full circuit recipe.

4. Remaining presentation/validation boundaries

- Cite the compiler agent's replay/axiom logs separately; this review did
  not rebuild the files.
- Count the new certificate lists and helpers when reporting net source
  reduction. Short local proof bodies are not automatically a net project
  reduction.
- Distinguish proof-only changes from expression/DAG transformations. All
  four prototypes reviewed here preserve the original relevant data.
- A coefficient/source snapshot hash records provenance but is not itself
  a proof. Soundness here comes from literal-node checks, topology, and
  Lean's theorem checking.
- No consistency, ZFC interpretation, positivity calibration, machine
  simulation, or end-to-end Diophantine equivalence is newly proved here.
