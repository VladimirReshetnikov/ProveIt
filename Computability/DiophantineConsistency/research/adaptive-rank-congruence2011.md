# Rank congruence from the adaptive zero

The actual current 13-variable zero now implies `q⁴ | |R+1−k|` in every
selected `IΣ₁` model. Here `q`, `R`, and `k` are the actual quantities in the
[rank window](adaptive-rank-window2011.md), and the absolute difference is
represented by two natural truncated differences. Both orders of `R+1` and
`k` are covered; no positive difference is assumed.

The proof combines the [weighted main-root equality](adaptive-main-congruence2011.md)
with the derived `q³ | Y` to get `q⁵ | H·|Eq+R+1−k|`. The internal
factor-three lemma loses at most one factor q, even when `3 | q`. Since
`q⁴ | Eq`, elementary congruence arithmetic removes Eq and yields the
claimed result. The factor-three proof uses explicit additive Bezout
certificates and cancellation, with no factorization, standard-model gcd,
or assumed unit.

This congruence has not yet been combined with the first Pell recurrence to
identify its index. The index comparison, main index equality, power
recovery, and existence of the full HA proof remain open.
