## First compact priority: staged product-square

ConZFC_Staged_Certificates claims ten strictly positive inputs with
84 operations/degree 436 and 85 operations/degree 328. The complete compact
proof was read. The actual supplied row lists have strict topology and full
liveness: 84=50 multiplications+19 additions+15 subtractions, and
85=51+19+15. All four entire uncompressed coefficient payloads match the
accepted directly indexed BFS943 tuple byte for byte. COMPACT_LITERAL.json
retains every raw hash and byte count; this is not merely a shared tuple name.

The useful algebra is

    a=Delta*U^2*E, F0=1+a, G=E*F0,
    Fsq=1+4*a*F0=(1+2*a)^2,
    Q=Delta*(Fsq-1)=4*Delta^2*U^2*G,
    RG=M^2*G,
    Q*(Z^2-RG*V^2)+(RG-Z^2)=0.

Thus (Q-1)*(Z/M)^2=G*(Q*V^2-1). Both other factors are -1 modulo G,
so valuations force G to be a square even when M and G share prime factors.
Since gcd(E,F0)=1, E and F0 are separately squares. The explicit rational
adjacent-Pell descent preserves the reduced denominator and strictly lowers
the nonzero absolute first coordinate to 1, recovering an integral second
root. A fresh seed Pell period, then doubling, produces the actual strong
pair. Its retained contracts must allow the doubled strong index; no odd
strong-index or I/c^2 parity restriction may be imported.

Soundness uses all-index folding before transport signs, obtaining b>c and
V>0. The 84-operation weight L=V^2 bounds the product root; the 85-operation
weight bV additionally uses the fifth auxiliary-index gap to obtain b>4g.
The later decoding only needs the recovered carrier to be integral. It does
not cancel a composite-modulus nonunit or assume an additive signed-root-sum
representation. Converse witnesses are constructed afresh.

The independent full ordinary review is
`reviews/staged-certificates-compact-ordinary-peer.md`, SHA256
2de80694e803dd2444771ffc5b62819053e4796c8cff654f7f9da1516c4cc22d.
It gives a qualified PASS with one explicit completeness repair. The generic
R>=3 description does not prove shifted T>0: at R=3 its T*=1, so T=T*-alpha
can fail for positive alpha. The actual canonical source family has R>3q;

proof must state this stronger inherited premise. The full ordinary review

A concrete leading-form error was independently reported by reduce_ten and
confirmed here by reading all of SignedFree lowdegree/PROOF.md and the copied
integer/source/FREE_SCALE_PROOF.md section 8. Both assert that only 4ULZ
contributes to degree 22 in

    A=(Z+Uk)^2+4U(LZ-U).

Their own degrees deg(U)=6 and deg(k)=5 show that (Uk)^2 also contributes.
Since k's top form is R0, the corrected forms are

    A22=U0^2*R0^2+4*U0*L0*Z0,
    P44=(U0^2*R0^2+4*U0*L0*Z0)^2.

The printed full form 16*U0^2*L0^2*Z0^2 is therefore false. Its stated
n^2 coefficient 16*U0^4*L0^2 remains correct because the omitted term is
n-free, so the degree-44 attainment witness appears repairable. This is a
proof erratum, not a found counterexample to degree 44 or zero equivalence.
No full global-leading-form approval is given. The exact two member hashes
are recorded in READ_SCOPE.json.
