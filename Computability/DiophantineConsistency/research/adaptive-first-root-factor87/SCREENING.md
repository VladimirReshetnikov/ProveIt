# Heuristic screening for an 86-gate output

The two stdlib-only scripts screen local replacements in the pinned 87-gate output DAG in `candidate.json` (SHA256 `da6b88365da3ec9db6040373c25de20b30baf63edc9fdf742de2aa4b2c06e6e8`). They count live binary operations by tracing backward from `P13` after a proposed replacement; the initial DAG has 87 live rows. The scripts require the pinned candidate hash and do not edit the candidate.

Both scripts assign independent pseudorandom values to the thirteen witness inputs and four symbolic port inputs, using seed `80281234` and three evaluation samples in the prime field modulo `2305843009213693951`. The literals 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 are available as input nodes; they carry no counted operation in this screening metric. Each original row is processed in its existing order, so replacement operands may use only inputs, literals or earlier rows. Each matching signature is only a possible algebraic identity; any reported candidate would still require exact polynomial verification, input/liveness checks and Lean binding.

[`screen-one-op.py`](screen-one-op.py) tries a single `+`, `-` or `*` operation over every earlier-node pair, including both subtraction orders, in place of each of the 87 rows. Its saved [output](screen-one-op.out) is `done`, with no candidate of fewer than 87 live operations. The script hash is `b63c4823f4359be494b6fdd11c62c993034e2f07cdd8cad4197bad21325994af`.

[`screen-two-op.py`](screen-two-op.py) stores all earlier-node representations of one-operation intermediate signatures and tries a second `+`, `-` or `*` with an earlier node, including both final subtraction orientations. It deletes dead rows after inserting the two replacement gates. Its saved [output](screen-two-op.out) reports checkpoints 10 through 80 and no candidate of fewer than 87 live operations. The script hash is `7d9c1fedf59c32e5a5f9eb6e28d73f9bf7f72dd2bc37be97e50319beb47f1d5a`.

This is **heuristic screening**, not an exhaustive proof or a minimality result. In particular, the two-operation scan excludes an intermediate whose three-sample signature equals any existing node. Recomputing such an intermediate could permit a different existing dependency subtree to become dead, so the earlier single-operation scan does not cover every excluded replacement. Multiplicative inverse matching also skips any second operand with a zero sample. Signature collisions can cause both false matches and mistaken exclusions; the scan does not prove a polynomial identity or inequality from its samples. It only replaces one target row at a time, preserves the earlier topological order, permits at most two replacement gates, and does not search multi-target rewrites, alternative equation representations, other constants, or semantic equivalences that change the polynomial.

From the repository root, reproduce the outputs with `python3 Computability/DiophantineConsistency/research/adaptive-first-root-factor87/screen-one-op.py` and `python3 Computability/DiophantineConsistency/research/adaptive-first-root-factor87/screen-two-op.py`. No 86-gate result is claimed.
