# Restricted four-operation affine-product screen

[`screen-four-affine-product.py`](screen-four-affine-product.py) screens a third local four-operation replacement family in the pinned 87-gate output DAG: `(x ± y)*(u ± v) ± w`, including both orientations of the final subtraction. It constructs two addition/subtraction rows, their product, and the final addition/subtraction row. Each operand may be a witness input, symbolic port input, literal 0–4, or an earlier row. It replaces one target row at a time and recomputes output liveness after inserting the four rows. Candidate JSON SHA-256 is `da6b88365da3ec9db6040373c25de20b30baf63edc9fdf742de2aa4b2c06e6e8`.

The screen visits target rows whose ancestor-deletion potential is at least four: four rows replacing one add three operations, so fewer than four deleted old rows cannot decrease the output count. The bound selects 18 targets. The [saved output](screen-four-affine-product.out) reports zero candidate replacements and zero encountered zero-divisor skips. Script SHA-256 `6d33b2d49c4bf93b838470dd7c74b71f17967086a1606baa915dcb000a24b6ee`; output SHA-256 `dec3764d7b13a0c9bffca96b589f743f520cae547dad9b86614affc51e125b3c`.

This is a heuristic screen, not an exhaustion or minimality proof. It evaluates three pseudorandom samples in the prime field modulo `2305843009213693951`, using seed `80281234` and treating the four fixed ports as independently sampled symbols. It excludes either affine intermediate or their product when its sample signature matches an existing input or earlier row; recomputing such an intermediate can change liveness, so earlier screens do not cover that exclusion. It also skips division by an affine intermediate with any zero sample; the reported zero skips count only those reaching the division test, because sample signatures already matching existing nodes, including zero, are excluded earlier. Signature collisions can cause missed or spurious matches. Other four-gate shapes, multi-row changes, larger expressions, other constants and different polynomials with equivalent zero sets are not screened.
