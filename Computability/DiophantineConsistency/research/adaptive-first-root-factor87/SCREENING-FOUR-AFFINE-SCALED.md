[`screen-four-affine-scaled.py`](screen-four-affine-scaled.py) screens a second local four-operation replacement shape in the pinned 87-gate output DAG: `((x*y ± u)*z) ± w`. The first multiplication, first addition or subtraction, second multiplication, and final addition or subtraction are four separate candidate rows. Each operand may be a witness input, symbolic port input, literal 0–4, or earlier row. Only one target row is replaced at a time, and output liveness is recalculated for each matching candidate. This is heuristic screening, not an exhaustion or minimality proof. It uses three pseudorandom samples in the prime field modulo `2305843009213693951`, with seed `80281234`, and treats the fixed ports as independently sampled symbols. It excludes any multiplier with a zero sample and skips a product or first affine intermediate whose sample signature equals an existing input or earlier row. Recomputing an excluded intermediate can change liveness, so earlier scans do not cover these exclusions. Sample collisions can cause missed or spurious matches. The screen also omits reverse subtraction forms `u - x*y` and `w - (...)`, other four-operation shapes, simultaneous multi-row rewrites, larger replacements, other constants, and different polynomials with equivalent zero sets.
