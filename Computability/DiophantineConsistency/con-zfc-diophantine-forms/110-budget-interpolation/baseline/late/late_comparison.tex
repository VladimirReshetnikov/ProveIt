cubic $Y$ scale.  It removes both the $v^2$ guard multiplier and
$\Lambda=4H+1$ main-root weight, obtaining degree 1072 with 190

removals are not claimed here as new to the inspected repository.

\texttt{proofs/TRACE13\_PROOF.md}.

The present twelve-variable route instead combines the earlier
centered-budget guard with deletion of its nearest-square coordinate.
The residue obstruction modulo 3 recovers the main root before
separating the other four roots.  That centered-budget reduction and
its distinct completeness map are not supplied by the inspected late
trace proof.  This is a comparison of concrete packets, not a claim
of worldwide priority.

\paragraph{The late twelve-variable construction is different.}
It retains the nearest-square coordinate and shares a positivity
Pell witness with the strong witness through $I=src^2$.

\[

$(I,f)$ first and then realizes every auxiliary residue $T\bmod I$.

degree 4832 with 248 operations and degree 16000 with 223 operations.
