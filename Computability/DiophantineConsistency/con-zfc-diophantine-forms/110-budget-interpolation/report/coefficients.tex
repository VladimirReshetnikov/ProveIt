\section{Fixed numeral definitions and reproducibility}
The main eleven-, twelve-, and fifteen-integer equations use one complete
source34 tuple. Put $\rho=2^{121}$ and $B=\rho^{8801}=2^{1064921}$.
The supplied file \texttt{guard/coefficients/recipe/wang943\_tiles.json}
is the entire fixed ordered list of $943$ tiles, in west--north--east--south
order. It is reconstructed, entry for entry, from all $430$ transitions of
the included fixed blank machine. The first two selectors are the specified
start and forbidden tiles; their order is part of the numeral definition.
No search for a halting computation enters this finite reconstruction.

For tile $i$ let $x_{it}$ be its four fixed labels. Set
\begin{align*}
 W_j&=2^{10+2j},\qquad (o_0,o_1,o_2,o_3)=(1020,1000,980,960),\\
 E&=\{0,\ldots,942\}\cup\{o_t+2j:0\le t<4,\ 0\le j<10\}\\
  &\quad\cup\{1063,1064,1095,1096,2195,3296\},\\
 c_i&=1+\sum_{t=0}^3\sum_{j=0}^9
       \operatorname{bit}_j(x_{it}+512)W_{10t+j}
       +\sum_{j=40}^{43}W_j\quad(0\le i\le942).
\end{align*}
At a copy position put $c_{o_t+2j}=W_{10t+j}$; at the four anchors
$1095,1096,2195,3296$ put $W_{40},W_{41},W_{42},W_{43}$ respectively.
The two dummy coefficients at $1063,1064$ are zero. Thus $|E|=989$.
Define the fixed integers
\begin{align*}
 \mu&=1022+\sum_{j=0}^{43}W_j,\\
 Z_p&=(2^{119}-1)\sum_{e=8792}^{8798}\rho^e
                  +(2^{27}-1)\rho^{8799},\\
 D_C&=\rho^{40}+\rho^{41}+\rho^{1100}+\rho^{2200}
       +\sum_{e\in E}c_e(\rho^{4393-e}+\rho^{5495-e}),\\
 M_C&=B-1-\sum_{e\in E,\ e\ne1}\rho^e-2\rho^{1063},\\
 M_{F,n}&=4+\mu(\rho^{4393}+\rho^{5495})
       +\sum_{j=0}^9(\rho^{1000+2j}+\rho^{1021+2j})\\
       &\quad+\rho^{2195}+\rho^{3296}+Z_p.
\end{align*}
The actual polynomial ports are
\[
 \beta=B-1,\quad K_0=D_C+B\rho,\quad M_C,\quad M_F=M_{F,n}+B-1,
\]
with the two additional cone numerals $A_c=2^{1113}$ and $B_c=2^{666104}$.
The six complete payloads total $615{,}883$ uncompressed bytes. A payload
with little-endian bytes $b_0,\ldots,b_{m-1}$ means precisely the integer
$\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}b_i256^i$, not a digest standing in for an unspecified
number. Payload hashes, finite recipe reconstruction, complete tile equality,
and the ordinary coefficient-adapter proof are all supplied.

The ten-positive construction deliberately uses a different complete
compact44 tuple, with $\beta=2^{590069}-1$. Its four whole payloads total
$295{,}388$ bytes; its own source-specific recipe, transition list, adapter,
and reconstruction check are retained under
\texttt{positive/ten\_centered/coefficients}. None of its ports is substituted
into a source34 integer construction. In both tuples $M_F$ has already been
shifted; shifting it a second time would define the wrong polynomial.
