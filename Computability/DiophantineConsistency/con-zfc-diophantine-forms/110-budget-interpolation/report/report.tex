\begin{abstract}
We combine a centered sign budget with intersective interpolation, parity,
and five-root fusion to obtain fixed ordinary polynomials in eleven and twelve unrestricted integer unknowns. Every named
coefficient is an explicit, authenticated integer; all other named registers
are polynomial abbreviations, not quantified variables. A second reduction
absorbs exact equations into a one-sided bounded positivity certificate.
The constructions preserve solvability precisely through explicit witness
maps and named ordinary native/Pell/refutation calibration theorems.
Their unsolvability is therefore provably equivalent in ZFC to syntactic
$\Con(\ZFC)$ under those inherited results. This is neither a proof of
consistency nor a new end-to-end kernel certificate. We state exact domains,
degrees, operation conventions, dependencies, and reproducible finite checks;
we make no global minimality or historical-priority claim.
\end{abstract}
