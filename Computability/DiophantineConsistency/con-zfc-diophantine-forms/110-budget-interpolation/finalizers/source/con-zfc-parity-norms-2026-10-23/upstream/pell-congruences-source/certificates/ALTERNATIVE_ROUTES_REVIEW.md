# Alternative encodings: findings from the current snapshot

Snapshot: `cf4a5218a5a4e4358d3f5314151b6b05d4aa384e` of
`VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt`.

The current repository already goes considerably beyond a proposal to
"apply DPRM to a proof checker." `ALTERNATIVE_ENCODINGS.md` records a
closed executable quartic for the actual syntactic refutation predicate,
a checked conservative bound `nu < 2^300000`, an annotated sealed-ZFC
proof checker, and direct graph compilers retaining arithmetic sharing.
Its externally checked Lean equivalence is not represented as a completed
object-language ZFC derivation.

Package 68 already proves that single-conclusion Horn derivability is
equivalent to a nonnegative finite flow certificate, and replaces formula
exponents by pairwise-coprime affine factors `1+jM`. Its preferred local
rule/accumulator source has 23 witnesses and degree 50. The variable-length
global certificate has not been materialized and measured as an ordinary
polynomial. Package 75 supplies an inert-prime CRT closure theorem and a
fixed-input boundary adapter, with the same explicit distinction between
proved interfaces and finished numerical endpoints.

Two potentially useful further directions are:

1. **A fixed finite class-theory axiom list.** A fully specified finite
   NBG presentation, with its ZFC equiconsistency translation, could replace
   the large guarded ZF schema bundle by fixed ground axiom codes. This
   could reduce the largest branch of a direct Hilbert verifier. A complete
   calculus translation, exact axiom numerals and global compilation are
   required before any final variable count is meaningful. This iteration
   supplies none of those completed artifacts and makes no record claim
   for this direction.
2. **Shared formula syntax.** A finite DAG of formula nodes with strictly
   smaller child identifiers could share repeated subformulas across all
   proof lines. Finite induction unfolds the DAG into ordinary formulas;
   canonical interning provides completeness. This reduces repeated local
   constructor evaluation but introduces a global table-consistency
   obligation. A local gate count cannot be reported as the size of the
   final Diophantine equation.

Neither idea is claimed to be unprecedented in logic or computability.
The completed improvement in the accompanying main construction instead
comes from a new combination of established guards and a proved
Pell-adapted CRT elimination. This note records why the alternative search
did not justify claiming an unrelated finished breakthrough.
