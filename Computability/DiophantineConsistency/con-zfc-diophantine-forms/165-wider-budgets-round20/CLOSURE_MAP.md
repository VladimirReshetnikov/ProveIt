# Ordinary proof and executable dependency closure

This package supplies ordinary unbounded mathematical proofs and exact finite
artifact checks. It does not decide consistency or rebuild a new end-to-end
proof-assistant certificate.

## Direct signed equations

`signed/` contains the five literal quartics, complete expansions, certified
integral coordinate and residual transformations, new source reconstruction,
and independent verification. `balanced238`, `dimension237`, and `power239`
are the selected tradeoffs. `exposed241` and `power_exposed242` are technical
sources for the height-one construction.

`signed/parent252/` is the one canonical common-remainder parent, retaining
its ordinary Pell, factorial, CRT, range and C222/ZF/ZFC calibration sources,
original arithmetic compiler and full source reconstruction. Incoming shared
factorial and factorial-free descendants use this same byte-identical parent.
`signed/inherited_shared/` and `signed/inherited_free/` retain their additional
source transformations and ordinary proof texts, including the actual final
signed-negative-radix and odd-square-beta arguments. Original notices and
source hashes are preserved. The few duplicated small parent files under
`inherited_free/parent` retain its original verifier import interface.

## Height-one equations

`height_one/` includes actual coefficient arrays, complete literal equations,
source and occurrence ledgers, incidence trees, nonnegative energy identities,
anchored-unit substitutions and internal-flow contractions. Its five exact
source bindings are checked against the enclosing source polynomials.

Two source polynomials occur in the current `signed/` component. The older
three are retained minimally in `height_sources/round19/` (exposed247) and
`height_sources/round18/` (the grouped263 and sparse285 sphere sources).
`check_height_sources.py` authenticates their original bytes, reconstructs
only the three retained outputs, and independently rejects mutations. It
adapts old file lookup in memory; archived mathematical producers and
checkers remain unchanged. Temporary copies of old source inputs are
recovered from retained exact source/translation files, not a remote archive.

The old proof files refer to their historical layouts. Their inherited
orientation, strict-budget and calibration texts are present in the common
parent under `signed/parent252/upstream/round17/`. `HEIGHT_SOURCE_BINDINGS.json`
records exact origins, sizes and hashes. The driver redirects its historical
`common252/` prefix to the canonical `signed/parent252/` directory.

## Scalar equations

`scalar/` supplies the two new eleven-integer circuits, full polynomial-center
identities and degree proofs, complete six-port numerical payloads and finite
reconstruction, and the ordinary incoming rebased/native handoff. The changed
rank progression and weaker magnitude budget are distinct from the old
fixed frame; the old degree-300 limitation is not contradicted. The new
rebased frame has its own precisely scoped degree-298 minimum theorem.

`scalar/CLOSURE_MAP.md` and `scalar/OMITTED_OPTIONAL_FILES.json` identify four
omitted optional items: two standalone rendered historical PDFs and two
repository-wide package indexes. All actual proof MD/TeX, numerical payloads,
code and licenses are retained. Unchanged nested historical manifests may
name those omitted optional documents; they are historical provenance, not
the active distribution inventory. The active root manifests and source pins
cover the selected executable and ordinary mathematical closure.

## Reproduction

`VERIFY_PLAN.json` is authoritative. The top-level verifier first checks the
exact sealed inventory, then works only in a disposable copy. Optimized
steps set PYTHONOPTIMIZE=1 as well as passing -O, so nested Python processes
inherit optimization. The optimized paths include deliberate corruption controls rather than
relying only on successful execution. Receipts should be written outside
the sealed tree. No researcher workspace or network is needed.
