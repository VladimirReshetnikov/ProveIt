\subsection{Small variable circuits and fixed coefficient architectures}
In eleven unrestricted variables, Round~19 retained the authentic
source34 degree-300, 148-operation point. Nearest factorial already
supplies degree 298 at 150 operations by altering the arithmetic budget,
as well as fourteen-variable points at degree/cost $82/113$ and $90/111$,
and a thirteen-variable point at $152/126$. The Cofinal witnesses
package supplies fourteen-variable $88/110$ and thirteen-variable
$156/117$ points. A lower degree at a different dimension is not
dominance in every metric.

The strictly positive ten-variable family is another comparison class.
Nearest factorial and Cofinal witnesses include degree/cost $382/79$;
Cofinal witnesses also gives $338/80$. Joint norm gives $454/78$,
and Lucas corrections gives $304/87$. Earlier points such as $626/75$,
$460/77$, $328/84$ and $288/89$ remain separate degree/cost choices.
These are incoming compact metrics on their declared positive domain,
not new unrestricted-integer results of this report.

Coefficient tuples must remain coherent. The positive-domain radix67
and alternate radix47 encodings, and the signed source34 tuple, have
different native meanings. Replacing one port by a smaller coefficient
from another tuple does not preserve calibration. Every selected scalar
endpoint here retains its specified complete authentic tuple; every
independent variable and every fixed integer port is counted distinctly.
Collected support and total coefficient height of the scalar equations
are not measured unless an actual expansion is supplied.

\subsection{The limits of the comparison}
The comparison universe consists of named pinned artifacts and prior
releases, not every known or possible Diophantine equation. No global
minimum in dimension, degree, coefficient height, support or circuit cost
is inferred. The direct C222 route is not a K215 simulation or an
instantiation of Jones's universal equation; this lineage fact is not a
claim to have replaced the inherited logical calibration. Separate reviews
are independent AI-assisted checks within this workflow, not external
human peer review. Preparing the artifacts does not publish, commit or
modify the repository.
