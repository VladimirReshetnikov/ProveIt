\section{A wider common norm and smaller signed quartics}
\label{sec:signed}
Every coordinate in this section is an unrestricted integer. The immediate
arithmetic input is the Shared factorial error construction, including its
signed affine radix, full fourteen-pair orientation pool, deterministic
Pell comparison center and eleven normalized digit remainders. Its source
is not the positive-radix odd-square chart of Round~19. The new argument
weakens its shared norm while keeping the signed bootstrap valid; exact
row and coordinate reductions then lower the dimension and coefficient
height. The distinction matters because the wider norm no longer proves
its denominator nonzero by itself.

The inherited arithmetic interface and the signed-base power proof are
reproduced first, with the new norm displayed explicitly. The subsequent
subsections prove every additional implication needed for the change,
including the unit-radix and zero-factorial branches. The retained logical
calibration is exactly the C222/ZF/ZFC chain described in
Section~\ref{sec:foundation}.
