\begin{abstract}
A wider common norm for factorial and digit errors gives an explicit
quartic in 238 unrestricted integers with 175 quadratic residuals,
5236 collected monomials and coefficient height 3968. The proof recovers
signed and unit radix cases before positivity, using a factorial fixed
point valid at radius three quarters. A separate factorial-free companion
uses the power $t^t$ and has 239 variables, 5137 terms and the same height.
For coefficient-height-one equations, replacing equality dummies by
products with already anchored unit aliases and contracting internal
flow edges give smaller quartics with explicit inverse witness maps,
including a 505-variable, 20491-term point and a 503-variable alternative.
Whole-expression scalar centers give eleven-integer equations at exact
degree and circuit cost $298/147$ and $300/145$, with separate sharp
fixed-frame degree bounds. Complete new ordinary proofs, literal finite
arrays, fixed coefficient payloads and independent reproducible checks
are supplied. The consistency interpretation composes with identified
inherited calibrations; no consistency proof, global optimum or new
end-to-end proof-assistant endpoint is claimed.
\end{abstract}
