# Source attribution

The numerical C222 calculus, its proof-theory calibration, direct flow and
large-carrier construction, original arithmetic representations, and current
shared-CRT/fixed-power baseline are inherited mathematical dependencies from
Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository and its credited contributors.
Their immediate source files are copied inertly in sources/, with exact pins.
The inherited ProveIt MIT No Attribution license and package notice are
preserved there as LICENSE.ProveIt and NOTICE.shared_certificates.md.

The official Mathlib PellMatiyasevic.lean source is separately pinned and
retains its source authorship header and Apache-2.0 license (LICENSE.Mathlib).

The executable scripts in this direct package are independently authored
implementations. They do not import or execute the copied upstream source.
The new arithmetic/source simplifications and their equivalence proofs are
identified in section.tex. No kernel certification, proof of consistency,
absolute minimality or worldwide novelty claim is made.
