# Attribution and boundary

This work retains the complete prior natural construction and its source
calibration under `inherited/`, including the licenses and notices there.

common square before quadratization, its exact source checker and proof,
and independently certified contractions/residual-basis companions.

calibration; the new end-to-end result has not been kernel checked.
