# Guarded trace resultants: 13/118/114 and 13/120/112

Two fixed polynomials in **thirteen unrestricted integers** have exact
degree/live-operation pairs **118/114** and **120/112**. Both strictly
dominate the incoming13/152/119 and13/156/117 points in degree and cost.
The two new points themselves trade degree against cost.

The new guarded quadratic resultant eliminates a signed strong trace from
an unsquared auxiliary Pell row. Its exceptional zero-coefficient branch
is explicitly excluded *before* trace recovery. Full signed and zero cases,
constructive maps, exact homogeneous tops, full numerals and their finite
recipe are supplied. The ordinary proof composes with the preserved
native/compiler/refutation calibration. No consistency decision, global
optimum or new end-to-end kernel certificate is claimed.

From any working directory, Python3.10+ standard library only:

```sh
python3 -B /path/to/roots/build.py --check
python3 -B /path/to/roots/check.py
python3 -B -O /path/to/roots/check.py
python3 -B /path/to/roots/reconstruct_coefficients.py
```

alternative binary operators at every live gate. The full114/112
