\begin{abstract}
We construct explicit ordinary polynomials whose lack of solutions in
specified integer domains is provably equivalent in ZFC to syntactic
consistency of ZFC. A guarded quadratic resultant removes a signed strong
trace and yields thirteen-integer equations of degrees $118$ and $120$.
An early Pell projector excludes the third main index before sign recovery,
licensing a squared transport and a ten-positive-variable degree-$190$
equation. Natural-domain coordinate charts and certified contractions
produce a $201$-variable quartic and degree-six and degree-eight alternatives.
An even affine horizon gives a $209$-integer quartic, trading one
variable and one residual against support and coefficient height.
Every result includes complete coefficients or circuits, constructive maps,
ordinary proofs and reproducible finite checks. The pinned low-padding2963
specializations retain their own whole native calibration. No consistency
assertion, global optimum or new end-to-end kernel certificate is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Objective and the comparison boundary}
For each specified domain $D$ and fixed polynomial $P$, the target is
\[
\mathrm{ZFC}\vdash
\bigl(\neg\exists\boldsymbol{x}\in D^n\;P(\boldsymbol{x})=0\bigr)
\longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\]
The proofs establish solvability equivalent to a finite syntactic
refutation. They do not assert consistency, unsolvability, or an actual
solution. The domains $\Z$, $\N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and $\Z_{>0}$ are
part of the individual theorems, not interchangeable size conventions.

\section{Reproduction and the logical boundary}
The direct constructions supply every residual and every collected
coefficient of the final sum of squares. The compact constructions supply
every live circuit gate, all actual fixed integer payloads and their finite
reconstruction recipes. Powers, fractions, factorials, recurrences and
inequalities in semantic proofs are not hidden primitives or extra
quantified variables inside a final polynomial.

Ordinary unbounded mathematics establishes sign recovery, nonzero
resultant denominators, cofinal choices and constructive maps. Independent
finite checkers bind these proofs to literal rows, inverse maps, all leading
terms, coefficient bytes and actual corruption controls. Finite sample
identities supplement, not replace, their unbounded proofs. The portable
release replay runs in fresh copies under normal and optimized Python,
propagates optimized mode to child checkers, and preserves sealed bytes.
The actual extracted ZIP is replayed separately before delivery.

The logical calibration remains the specified inherited numerical calculus
or native, compiler and refutation theorem. No transitive formal development is
claimed to have been rebuilt into a new proof-assistant theorem for every
printed coefficient. The independent reviews are separate checks within
an AI-assisted workflow, not outside human endorsements. No upstream
repository is modified, and no literature-wide priority or global
simplicity lower bound is asserted.

The low-padding2963 language and four-number tuple differ from the older
indexed943/radix67 specialization. Physical positions with zero weight
remain part of the native proof. Each complete tuple, selector map, native
proof and finite controller is authenticated separately; neither scalar
bounds nor weighted-array equality license exchanging ports or certificates.
Storage byte order and compression metadata are distinguished from the
mathematical integers. Any documented inherited gzip-header portability
mismatch is not relabeled as a successful compressed-byte rebuild.
