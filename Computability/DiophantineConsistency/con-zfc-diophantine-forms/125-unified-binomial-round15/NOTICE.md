# Attribution and proof boundary

This round-15 contribution is prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt repository. New material consists of the compatible eleven-variable powered/coprime composition and split carrier, its paired weighted-guard schedules, the unified factorial/reciprocal-product architecture, selective even-index/residue-one signed compilation, independent audits, and the report/replay packaging.

The square-output factorial fixed point and uniform low-binomial bound are inherited from the Synchronized Exponents and earlier Bootstrap line. Coprime guard fusion, powered-divisibility saturation, supported-modulus interpolation, source34 coefficients and native calibration, direct C222 source semantics, and earlier signed/CRT/Horn/projection components retain their supplied provenance. The report distinguishes new composition from inherited results.

The top-level MIT No Attribution license follows the ProveIt contribution release convention. Third-party material keeps its own license and notice files. Relevant ProveIt, Mathlib, K215, Wang-tile, source34, scalar normalization, and signed-source notices are retained beside their copied sources. The top-level license does not remove or supersede those terms.

No new end-to-end Lean/Rocq proof, encoded internal ZFC derivation, proof of Con(ZFC), or global polynomial minimum is claimed. Finite check receipts are not substituted for unbounded arithmetic arguments or historical calibration.
