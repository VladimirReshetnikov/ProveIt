# Attribution and scope

New mathematics, exposition, code, and artifact assembly were prepared
with AI assistance for Vladimir Reshetnikov and ProveIt. This release uses
the repository's MIT No Attribution terms for new material.

The constructions build on work in ProveIt, especially packages 128,
131, 132, and 133, and on the earlier arithmetic and logical interfaces
identified by each component's provenance ledger. Their ordinary proofs,
finite recipes, original notices, and licenses are retained. Mathlib source
excerpts retain the Apache 2.0 license in the corresponding component.

The title and result names describe this research packet. They do not
claim historical priority, a metric-independent minimum, or a new proof
of Con(ZFC). The mathematical assertion is a ZFC-provable equivalence
between the designated equation's unsolvability and Con(ZFC).

Independent reviews included here were performed by separate agents in
the same AI-assisted workflow. They are distinct from external human
peer review and from proof-assistant checking.
