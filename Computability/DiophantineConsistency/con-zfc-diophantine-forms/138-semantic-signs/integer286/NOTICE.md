# Attribution and proof boundary

All new research, code, and exposition were prepared with AI assistance in
this iteration for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository. Independent
review denotes a separate mathematical review within that workflow.

The direct signed source, arithmetic compiler, projection helpers, centering
code, and ordinary calibration are inherited from the exact repository files
identified in `SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json`, primarily packages 131, 125, 124,
119, 112, 95, 89, and 66. Their original notices and licenses are retained.
The linear cofinal-modulus estimate was first derived in the parallel
natural-number branch of the same iteration and transferred here.

New contributions are the parity-compatible guard removal, the joint
abscissa orientation followed by semantic recovery of the representative
and beta-code signs, the compatible odd square radix, their complete
composition, and the two exact smaller integer quartics. Standard Pell,
CRT, ternary nonnegative representations, and polynomial sum-of-squares
identities are not claimed as new.

The new ordinary equivalence proof is formalizable in ZFC. The supplied
finite verifiers do not constitute a new complete Lean/Rocq or encoded
object-ZFC proof, and no consistency statement is proved outright.
