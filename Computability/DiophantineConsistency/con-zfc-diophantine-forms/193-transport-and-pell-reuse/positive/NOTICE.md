Reshetnikov and the ProveIt project on 25 October 2026.

signed index-three analysis, and its native/K215/refutation calibration are
inherited from package 172. The precise source is frozen in the incoming

unchanged source proofs, recipes, coefficient bytes and licenses are retained.

or repository paths. Historical statements inside those source files retain
their original scope.

The new contribution is the polynomial transport weight compatible with the
free chart, its positivity proof before rank recovery, and the algebraic folding
that yields the 118-operation degree-190 equation. The 99-operation degree-202
companion, exact graph definitions, proof and finite checks are also supplied.

New material is offered under the repository's MIT No Attribution terms.
Retained source licenses and the original machine attribution remain applicable
to their corresponding components. The independent mathematical review and
principal checker are separate AI-assisted internal checks, not an external
human peer review.

Nothing here asserts a global simplest equation, historical priority, the
consistency of ZFC, an actual polynomial zero, or a new end-to-end kernel proof.
