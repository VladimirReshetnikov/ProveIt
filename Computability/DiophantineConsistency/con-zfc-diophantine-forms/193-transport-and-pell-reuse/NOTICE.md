# Attribution, scope, and licensing

Prepared with AI assistance for Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt
project on 25 October 2026.

The new mathematical contributions are the reuse of a Pell quotient as
the modular center witness, its local index-permutation and signed-frame
proof, the resulting explicit direct profiles, and the weighted transport
and exact degree-190/degree-202 equations in the positive free chart.
The report and finite verification artifacts make their dependencies
explicit.

source, Horn factorization, and prior methods of exact integer projection
are inherited from ProveIt. The positive chart, fixed native/K215 tuple,
Pell authentication, and native refutation calibration are inherited from
package 172. The polynomial center and its coefficient majorant are
inherited from package 178. Incoming Affine Horizons and Cofinal Pell
Compression are explicitly credited comparisons and antecedents.
