\section{A fully centered fifteen-integer carrier}
\label{sec:centered15}
The new endpoint uses fifteen unrestricted integer variables, has exact total degree
$48$, and admits a $108$-gate division-free evaluator
($61$ multiplications, $25$ additions, $22$ subtractions).
The retained predecessor used degree $56$ and $110$ gates at the same variable count.
This is an ordinary arithmetic equivalence to that predecessor; the inherited
native/compiler/refutation theorem, not a new consistency proof or formalized
object-language derivation, supplies the connection to $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$.

\subsection{Complete polynomial}
The variables are
\[
 j,C,z,h,r,v,w,T,c,n,f,y,p_1,p_2,p_3\in\mathbb Z.
\]
The six numerals $\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F,A_c,B_c$ are the authenticated fixed
payloads in the accompanying coefficient directory; $M_F$ is already shifted.
All names below are polynomial abbreviations, not extra witnesses:
\begin{gather*}
 J=6j+1,\quad  q_m=\beta J,\quad q=q_m+1,\quad W=12w+4,\\
 X=qW,\quad Y=qv,\quad  F=(K_0+W)C-zq_m,\\
 R=(q(q-F)-C)(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\\
 E=XY,\quad a=E+Y,\quad H=4a+3,\quad \Delta=a^2+H,\\
 k=R+1+hE,\quad \eta=c-Yk,\quad L=EYk,\\
 I=(2r+1)c^2,\quad Q=\Delta(f^2-1),\quad V=f^2(cT-R)-c,\\
 M=2q^2,\quad U=MH,\quad K_{\rm lo}=2X^2-3Xc-2,\\
 Z=Un+MK_{\rm lo}+H(v-q^2),\\
 D_{\rm err}=(A_cF-q)^2+(B_cC-q)^2,\\
 B_{\rm udget}=k^2(q^2-D_{\rm err})-4q^2\eta^2,\\
 P_{\rm os}=1+p_1^2+p_2^2+p_3(p_3+1).
\end{gather*}
Define
\begin{align*}
 C_{\rm new}&=(Z+Uk)^2+4U(LZ-U),\\
 B&=f^2-\Delta I^2-1,\\
 C_{\rm aux}&=V^2-(Q-1)y(2V+y)-1,\\
 G&=B_{\rm udget}-P_{\rm os}.
\end{align*}
The one equation is
\[
 \boxed{P=C_{\rm new}^2+B^2+C_{\rm aux}^2+G^2=0.}
\]
In particular, no inequality, rational-domain variable, division, or free
coefficient port occurs in the literal final polynomial.

\subsection{Monicity, parity, and exact inverse maps}
The pinned $\beta=2^{1064921}-1$ is odd and $1\pmod 3$.
Hence $q$ is nonzero, even, and $2\pmod3$.
Also $H$ is nonzero and odd, and $H\equiv3\pmod q$.
Thus $\gcd(H,2q^2)=1$ and $U\ne0$ on every signed assignment.
At a zero of $P$, put $t=Z/U\in\mathbb Q$.
The new carrier is equivalently
\[
 t^2+2(2L+k)t+k^2-4=0.
\]
Monicity gives $t\in\mathbb Z$ before any Pell or sign argument.
Reducing $U\mid Z$ modulo the coprime factors $M,H$ yields
\[
 v_0=\frac{v-q^2}{M}\in\mathbb Z,\qquad
 g=\frac{K_{\rm lo}}H\in\mathbb Z,\qquad t=n+g+v_0.
\]
Reduction of the monic equation modulo $2$ shows $t+k$ is even.
Therefore $s=(t+k)/2$ is an integer. Equivalently,
$(t+2L+k)^2=4[1+L(L+k)]$, and its integer square root is even.

The predecessor differs only in its carrier and its $n$ coordinate:
\[
 C_{\rm old}=Z_{\rm old}^2+(2Z_{\rm old}-Uk)UL-U^2.
\]
The universal polynomial identity
\[
 C_{\rm new}(2Z_{\rm old}-Uk)=4C_{\rm old}(Z_{\rm old})
\]
proves both directions. From a new zero keep every other input and set
\[
 n_{\rm old}=\frac{n_{\rm new}-g-v_0+k}{2}
             =s-g-v_0\in\mathbb Z.
\]
Then $Z_{\rm old}/U=s$ and $C_{\rm old}=0$.
Conversely the old carrier is monic in $Z_{\rm old}/U$;
it therefore licenses the same CRT offsets at every old zero. Set
\[
 n_{\rm new}=2n_{\rm old}+g+v_0-k.
\]
Then $Z_{\rm new}=2Z_{\rm old}-Uk$ and $C_{\rm new}=0$.
The other three residuals do not involve $n$ and remain zero in both directions.
These maps are mutually inverse on the zero sets. Divisions in the proof maps
are licensed at zeros; none is hidden in the polynomial.

\subsection{Exact degree and arithmetic accounting}
The relevant degrees are
\[
 \deg U=6,\quad \deg Z=7,\quad \deg L=11,\quad \deg k=5.
\]
The unique degree-$24$ term of $C_{\rm new}$ is $4ULZ$.
The four residual degrees are $24,14,18,16$, so $\deg P\le48$.
Writing leading homogeneous parts with subscript $0$ gives
\[
 (C_{\rm new})_{24}=4U_0^2L_0n,\qquad
 P_{48}=16U_0^4L_0^2n^2,
\]
where
\begin{align*}
 q_0&=6\beta j,& X_0&=12q_0w,&Y_0&=q_0v,\\
 U_0&=8q_0^2X_0Y_0,&
 L_0&=X_0Y_0^2(R_0+hX_0Y_0).
\end{align*}
Because $R$ is independent of $h$, the actual coefficient is
\[
 [j^{26}w^8v^{10}h^2n^2]P=2^{58}3^{34}\beta^{26}>0.
\]
Its exponents sum to $48$; the other squared residuals have smaller degree.
This proves exact degree after the huge constants are fixed.

The evaluator has $108$ live binary gates and all fifteen inputs are live.
Squares and scalar multiplications are charged. Exact register-cut identities,
source/payload hashes, leading forms, liveness, counts, and deliberately bad
mutations are checked independently of the builder, under normal and optimized
Python. These finite certificates do not replace the unbounded inherited
compiler/refutation theorem or assert global minimality.
