\section{Scope and comparison criteria}
We seek a fixed ordinary polynomial $P$ with integer coefficients for which
\[
 \mathsf{ZFC}\vdash
 \bigl(\neg\exists\mathbf x\in D^n\;P(\mathbf x)=0\bigr)
 \longleftrightarrow\operatorname{Con}(\mathsf{ZFC}).
\]
Here the domain $D$ is specified separately for each equation. Positive
integer variables are not interchangeable with unrestricted integer
variables. Every abbreviation in a displayed defining equation is a polynomial,
not an additional existential quantifier. Proof-only rational expressions
and radicals do not occur in the literal polynomial circuits.

There is no single size measure that ranks all these constructions. We
record the number of variables, exact total degree, and the number of live
binary addition, subtraction and multiplication gates. Squaring and
multiplication by a constant count as multiplication gates. This is a
shared-circuit count, not bit complexity. For the expanded quartics we
also record nonzero monomial count and maximum absolute coefficient.
Their small coefficients distinguish them from the compact Pell equations,
whose few fixed coefficients are very large.

For Pell-sequence notation used below, when $b\ge2$ and $m\ge0$ define
\[
 \chi_b(m)+\psi_b(m)\sqrt{b^2-1}
   =\bigl(b+\sqrt{b^2-1}\bigr)^m.
\]
Thus $\chi_b(m)$ and $\psi_b(m)$ are the usual integral Pell coordinates.
These proof-only sequences are not operations in the literal polynomials.

The new results are ordinary mathematical reductions, composed with
explicitly retained source and syntactic-refutation calibrations. The
checks authenticate coefficients, transcriptions, polynomial identities,
degree certificates and finite compiler transformations. They do not
replace the unbounded proofs. No new end-to-end Lean, Rocq or encoded
object-language ZFC certificate is asserted. In particular, the
equivalence above is not a proof of either side, and no global minimum
or literature-wide priority is claimed.

\section{The inspected comparison baseline}
The repository snapshot used for comparison is
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/tree/45b399ec51dfbecb891dd51d8a9e4b6b7c361ac6}{commit
\texttt{45b399ec51df}}, dated 24 October 2026 at 22:28 UTC. It includes the
previous coprime-projection packet and seven new peer packets. The source
audit authenticates 521 downloaded files against pinned Git blob hashes;
this is a selected source snapshot, not an independently rebuilt whole
repository.

The canonical displayed equation at that pin has ten positive variables,
degree 360 and 98 operations. That display is not the entire research
frontier: incoming saturated-Pell constructions include degree 254 at
99 operations, and our preceding packet includes degree 266 at 96
operations. Similarly, the accepted direct quartic has 569 integer
variables, while the incoming factorial-carrier packet reaches 517.
Comparisons below use the relevant stronger incoming predecessor, not
merely the canonical page.

Three useful new ingredients are explicitly credited. The structural
compression packet proves that auxiliary Pell growth supplies its own
transport bound, and supplies shorter universal norm evaluators. The
saturated-Pell packet centers a product of two recovered roots. The
powered-saturation factorial packet supplies the direct quartic on which
the new sign-guard transformation acts. Our previous coprime-projection
packet supplies the compact integer kernels and their complete fixed
coefficient bindings.

The newer indexed-tile enumeration shortens a constant recipe, but changes
its selector ordering and hence its $K$ coefficient. We do not splice that
coefficient into an older tuple. Each compact construction retains one
whole calibrated tuple. The archive records the exact predecessor files
and checksums so that these distinctions survive later repository edits.
