\paragraph{Soundness.}
At $I=0$, one has $S=Q=0$ and $\delta=-1$; thus the rational
clearing factor $\delta^2$ equals one and both polynomials equal
$A_c^4\ne0$, since
$(t^2+3)(t^3-19)$ has no rational root and $H\ne0$.
Thus $I,s,r,c\ne0$. The established bootstrap gives $D_f,D_a>0$.
A vanishing conjugate has no sole nonzero imaginary term, hence $D_g>0$,
which forces $\mathrm{Budget}\ge2$ and the centered signed packing bounds.
For $N_{\rm int}$, a signed rational radical sum is
$\sqrt{D_fD_a}+2\sqrt P$; for $N_{\rm rat}$, multiplication by $\delta$
gives $\sqrt{D_fD_a}+2\delta\sqrt P$, with the conjugate signs understood.
All underlying radicands and $\delta$ are odd. The distinct coefficient
$2$-adic valuations force both products to be squares.
Now $\gcd(D_\tau,D_g)=1$, while modulo $D_f$,
\[
 Q\equiv-\Delta,\quad \delta\equiv-A^2,\quad
 V\equiv-c,\quad C_y\equiv-D_m,\quad D_a\equiv A^2D_m.
\]
Since $AD_m\mid I$, one has $\gcd(D_f,D_a)=1$.
Each of $D_f,D_a,D_\tau,D_g$ is therefore a square.
The retained adjacent-coefficient Pell descent makes
$y=\sqrt{D_a}/\delta$ integral. Consequently $S\mid A_c$.
Monic prime-adic comparison yields $H\mid K$, and
$K=HXc-2[X(X+2ac)-1]$ restores the same main projection.
