\section{Reproduction and verification boundaries}
The archive includes the TeX source, compiled PDF, literal equations,
coefficient payloads, mathematical proofs, generators, independent audits
and the immutable preceding packet needed by relative source references.
The root reproduction program works in isolated temporary copies rather
than changing the delivered originals. It runs both ordinary and optimized
Python, compares deterministic rebuilt files and exercises corruption
controls. The replay receipt records exactly which commands were run and
which files were rebuilt byte-for-byte.

There are three distinct levels of evidence. First, the mathematical
proofs establish unbounded implications, including the order of sign,
index and divisibility recovery. Second, independent symbolic and compiler
checks establish identities and the literal transcription of the claimed
equations. Third, finite numerical and mutation tests provide regression
and error-detection evidence. The third level cannot replace the first.
The final report is compiled and every page visually inspected separately
from these algebraic checks.

The direct guard transformation is reviewed independently of its producer.
The fifteen-variable equivalence has an independent complete register,
degree, provenance and inverse-map audit. The positive family has an
independent six-chart transcription and proof review, including the
correct negative-transport chronology and fresh positive completeness.

theorem, not an unsupported assertion that arbitrary Pell witnesses can
be synchronized.

\section{What did not become a result}
No new nine-positive-variable or ten-unrestricted-integer endpoint was
obtained. A lower-degree carrier for the eleven-variable construction
would be valuable, but replacing its intersective quintic by a quadratic
is not justified merely by finding roots modulo some test moduli. The
strong ordinate must retain prescribed divisors, and its zero branch
must be excluded. Similarly, forcing a Pell guard ordinate to be odd
does not follow from taking powers of a unit. These are unresolved
directions, not proved universal barriers.

The completed gains are therefore specific: a twelve-pair sign
compression with a checked late-stage bootstrap, a short zero-set bijection lowering
one compact degree, two new coprime-group tradeoffs, and compatible
transport and evaluator simplifications. They do not establish a unique
``simplest'' equation across incomparable domains, degrees, heights and
circuit costs.
