# Attribution and research status

This is an AI-assisted research artifact prepared at the request of
Vladimir Reshetnikov for potential incorporation in ProveIt. It has not
been presented as reviewed or endorsed by the repository maintainers.

The new contribution is the factorial-free binomial CRT source argument
and its composition into the two explicitly emitted direct quartics.
The numerical C222 calculus and its logical calibration, signed Pell
certificate, common normalized remainders, odd-square radix idea, and
affine residual exposures have identified predecessors. The retained
source files, source ledgers, and notices preserve that attribution.

The denominator divisibility involving binomial coefficients and
lcm(1,...,t) is classical; the report credits Bakir Farhi's related lcm
identity and gives the short valuation proof needed here.
