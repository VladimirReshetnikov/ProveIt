\section{Supplement: one fewer operation at degree 24}
\label{sec:supplement-fifteen}

The pinned source-58 equation has fifteen strictly positive unknowns,
exact degree $24$, and a division-free evaluator using $90$ live binary
arithmetic operations. A positive transport-coordinate shift lowers the
last figure to $89$. This is a one-operation supplementary improvement;
the domain, variable count, degree, and fixed coefficient tuple are retained.
The coordinate shift already appears in the inherited ordinary kernel;
here it is applied to the literal source-58 integral-root evaluator.

\]

\subsection{Exact identity and positive-domain converse}

Source 58 uses a positive coordinate $z_{\rm old}$ and transport residual
\[
 A_t^{\rm old}
   =q[K_0C+(q-F)-z_{\rm old}(q-1)]+XC-q.
\]
There is an exact free-ring identity
\[
 A_t^{\rm old}\big|_{z_{\rm old}=z+1}
   =C(qK_0+X)-q[F+z(q-1)]=A_t.
\]
The five other residuals are unchanged. Thus a positive zero of the revised
polynomial gives a positive source-58 zero by $z_{\rm old}=z+1$.

For the converse, source 58's integral-root reconstruction gives
$q\mid X$ and the positive ordinary-kernel coordinate $w=X/q$.
The inherited ordinary proof then gives
\[
 C>0,\qquad X=2^R,\qquad w>q>F.
\]
Dividing the old transport equation by $q>0$ yields
\[
 (z_{\rm old}-1)(q-1)=(K_0+w)C-F>0.
\]
Since $q>1$, the integer $z_{\rm old}$ is at least two. Hence
$z=z_{\rm old}-1$ is strictly positive. The two shifts give a bijection of
the positive zero sets, proving the unconditional local arithmetic result
\[
 \exists\mathbf x\in\mathbb Z_{>0}^{15}\;
                    P_{15}^{\rm shift}(\mathbf x)=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists\mathbf x\in\mathbb Z_{>0}^{15}\;P_{58}(\mathbf x)=0.
\]
The strict bound on $z_{\rm old}$ is necessary for this positive-domain
argument; the polynomial identity alone would not establish the converse.

The consistency corollary is conditional: if the inherited source
calibration and arithmetic/compiler correctness yield an internal ZFC
proof that parent unsolvability is equivalent to the chosen consistency
sentence, then the same internal equivalence follows for the revised
equation. The current pinned \texttt{OBJECT\_ZFC.md} leaves both
internalization steps open. An external equivalence on standard natural
numbers does not itself supply the required internal ZFC derivation.

\subsection{Exact degree and arithmetic cost}

Writing $Z=X+\gamma H$ and $D_0=ac+Z$, the exact cancellations
\begin{align*}
 A_0={}&q^6[r^2+\ell(2r-k)-1]+2q^3(\ell+r)Y+Y^2,\\
 A_1={}&q^2[2acZ+Z^2-Hc^2-1]+2qD_0XH+(XH)^2
\end{align*}
give residual degree upper bounds $(11,9,4,3,12,10)$. The leading form is
\[
 \operatorname{in}_{24}(P_{15}^{\rm shift})
   =4X^4Y^6f^8u^2\bigl[(\eta+\zeta)T+hX\bigr]^2.
\]
In particular, the coefficient of $X^6Y^6f^8u^2h^2$ is $4$, independently
of the fixed ports. The concrete polynomial therefore has exact degree $24$.

Every binary addition, subtraction, and multiplication costs one operation,
including multiplication by fixed scalars; fixed leaves and previous-gate
references are free. The old transport residual takes eight gates when its
shared inputs are available. The new residual takes seven. The shared
six-residual evaluator consequently drops from $79$ to $78$ operations.
Six squarings and five additions yield the single equation, giving
\[
 90\longrightarrow89
   =47\text{ multiplications}+27\text{ additions}+15\text{ subtractions}.
\]
The separate cancellation-expanded evaluator has $101$ operations and is
used only as a degree certificate. It does not enter the $89$-operation
claim. No minimality claim is made under this metric.

At the all-one positive tuple, $c=3$, $f=1$, $U=0$, and $I=9$. Thus
$A_s=81$ and $P_{15}^{\rm shift}(1,\ldots,1)\ge6561$ independently of the
coefficient tuple. This tuple is not a solution.
