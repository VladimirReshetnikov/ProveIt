Attribution license. Existing material retains its original notices.

This contribution adds the positive source substitutions P = p+eta+zeta,
tau = p+t and L = S-1; the 86-record source and maximum-weight-100
packing; the calibrated nine-variable instances and exact metrics;
independent audits; the one-operation degree-24 transport refinement;
and the report with explicit remaining proof obligations.

source is inherited from package 31 and its ancestors. Degree-separated
packing, the free carrier, quotient shift, two-block architecture and
numerical finalizer are inherited from package 59 and its credited
FreeCoordinate contribution. The integral-root degree-24 construction
is inherited from package 58. No discovery priority is asserted for
general positive-gap substitutions or inherited constructions.

inherited. Retained notices credit ProveIt contributors and other upstream
authors in their stated scopes, including Mira, Elliot Glazer, Andrew
