# Attribution and proof scope

New synthesis, ordinary proofs, circuit refactorings, and research packaging
were prepared with OpenAI assistance for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project.
No claim of external human authorship or independent human peer review is made.

New material is offered under the repository's MIT-0 convention, reproduced
in LICENSE. Imported material retains the licences and attribution included
in each subdirectory. In particular, machine and source notices under
compiler/licenses and integer15/upstream and integer16/upstream are retained.

The signed body and coefficient recipe originate in package 29 / package 18;
the factored Jones evaluation originates in package 27. The positive polynomial
originates in package 30. The new coefficient construction combines packages
24, 25, and 27. The main article gives detailed attribution and an immutable
comparison pin. New variable reductions are ordinary mathematical proofs,
not new kernel-checked formal certificates.
