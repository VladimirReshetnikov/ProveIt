=====================

by an AI-assisted research collaboration.

are copied verbatim from package 12-gap-norm14 at the historical commit
identified in SOURCE_PINS.json. Its gap-coordinate proof is retained.

its proof/binding are copied from package 31-sun10 at commit

Original source and coefficient notices, including inherited K215 and
Andrew James Wade attribution, are retained in licenses/. Relative paths
within copied upstream notes refer to their original repository context.
Historical claims in those notes are claims of the sources; this package
does not silently claim to have rebuilt all their semantic dependencies.

The new contributions are the minimum-norm finalizers, their local proof,
the two emitted circuit schedules, and the exact finite degree auditor.
New work uses the MIT No Attribution license in LICENSE.

The reported improvements are relative to the pinned historical
degree-44 circuit. They are not global minimality claims. Internal
cross-checks by separate agents are not external human endorsement.
No consistency proof or new kernel-checked compiler endpoint is asserted.
