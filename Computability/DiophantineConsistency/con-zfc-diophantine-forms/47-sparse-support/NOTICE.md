# Provenance, credit and scope

This research package was prepared with AI assistance for Vladimir
Reshetnikov's ProveIt project on 23 October 2026. Independent audits in this
package mean separately implemented checks and separately reviewed
mathematical arguments within this work; no external human review is claimed.

The new principal contribution is supported-monomial isolation, its two
explicit weight vectors, and the reduced-degree nine-variable source
encoding. The minimum-norm appendix is a separate arithmetic refinement.

The fixed machine, compiler, native coefficient tuple, quadratic parent,
Jones arithmetic reduction, Sun arithmetic reduction, quintic merger and
norm finalizers retain their inherited provenance. See the article,
`SOURCE_PINS.json`, `construction/SOURCE_PINS.json`, `minima/SOURCE_PINS.json`,
and the included license/notice texts. In particular, the quintic merger
and the ten-integer scaled-square base are not new claims of this package.

New material is provided for incorporation into ProveIt under its existing
project arrangements. All inherited copyright and licensing texts remain
in force; no third-party terms are overridden.

This package is a mathematical research contribution, with its hypotheses
and verification boundary stated in the article. Its finite tests neither
decide consistency nor supply the documented outstanding end-to-end
compiler formalization.
