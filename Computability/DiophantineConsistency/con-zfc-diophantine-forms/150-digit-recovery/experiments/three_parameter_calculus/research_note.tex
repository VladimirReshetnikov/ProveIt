\title{Three-parameter proof calculi and modular saturation\\
\large A measured alternative that does not beat the natural-variable parent}
\author{Research artifact for ProveIt}
\date{25 October 2026}

\begin{abstract}
We replace a four-parameter implicational axiom by K, S, and Peirce and
remove its companion bounded disequality witness using an unbounded modular
inverse. A small-value decoding argument makes the inverse usable without
another binomial range certificate. We prove both directions of this change,
including a necessary lift of two encoded representatives to keep the
saturation quotient nonnegative. A literal 521-step Hilbert derivation
certifies the propositional replacement. The implementation produces a
quartic in 231 nonnegative integer variables with 183 quadratic residuals;
it loses to the retained 229-variable natural parent. It is an experimental
source-calculus alternative, not the preferred equation of the surrounding
package and not a new end-to-end formalization.
\end{abstract}

\section{A basis with at most three parameters}

Write the numerical implication constructor as
\[
 I(a,b)=2(a+b)(a+b+1)+4b,
\]
and, temporarily, write $a\imp b$ for this constructor. The source calculus
has the four-parameter axiom
\begin{equation}\label{old}
 ((c\imp d)\imp a)\imp((a\imp c)\imp(b\imp c)).
\end{equation}
Replace it by the three schemata
\begin{align*}
 \mathrm K&:\quad a\imp(b\imp a),\\
 \mathrm S&:\quad(a\imp(b\imp c))\imp((a\imp b)\imp(a\imp c)),\\
 \mathrm P&:\quad((a\imp b)\imp a)\imp a.
\end{align*}
Retain all other numerical axiom families, the guarded bundle, modus ponens,
and generalization. No new bound is imposed on the numerical instantiations.

\begin{proposition}\label{basis}
The original and replacement numerical calculi have the same derivable codes.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The retained calibration source \file{KCode222.lean} contains
\file{c222_k}, \file{c222_s}, and
\file{c222_classical}, whose Peirce component is supplied by
\file{Lukasiewicz.peirce}. Thus each new schema is derivable in the
original calculus.

For the converse, K and S support the ordinary deduction transformation.
If an axiom or an undischarged assumption proves $D$, K derives
$A\imp D$. The discharged assumption $A$ is treated by the standard K/S
proof of $A\imp A$. A modus-ponens step is discharged with S. This is a
finite syntactic transformation, valid under every interpretation of the
binary constructor.

Put $f=(c\imp d)\imp a$ and $g=a\imp c$. Under assumptions $f,g,c\imp d$,
two applications of modus ponens derive $c$. Discharging $c\imp d$ gives
$(c\imp d)\imp c$. Peirce with parameters $c,d$ then gives $c$ under $f,g$.
Add the unused assumption $b$ and discharge $b,g,f$, in that order. The result
is precisely \eqref{old}.

The supplied certificate expands all deduction transformations into 521
literal lines: 132 K instances, 128 S instances, one Peirce instance,
and 260 modus-ponens steps. The independent checker accepts only these axiom
instances and exact earlier-line references. It accepts no remaining
assumptions or deduction-theorem rule. Replacing every use of an axiom by
its finite derivation now gives both inclusions of derivable codes.
\end{proof}

The source's bundle requires $c\ne a$ and $c\ne b$. Its parameter $c$ also
occurs as the formula matrix, so simply choosing $c=a+b+1$ is not an
equivalent axiom restriction. We keep $c$ unrestricted and enforce its two
disequalities by the following separate device.

\section{The modular saturation lemma}

Let $\boldsymbol z$ denote the bounded local fields. Let $U(\boldsymbol z)$
be a product of squared residuals, one for each \emph{non-bundle} branch.
Let $V(\boldsymbol z)$ be the squared residual for the unguarded bundle
transition, and put
\[
 D(\boldsymbol z)=(c-a)^2(c-b)^2,\qquad
 L(\boldsymbol z)=U(\boldsymbol z)V(\boldsymbol z)
                  +\sum_\nu G_\nu(\boldsymbol z)^2.
\]
Here the $G_\nu$ are the two beta-sequence residuals. Consequently $U,V,L$
are nonnegative at every natural tuple. The desired local condition is
\begin{equation}\label{desired}
 G_\nu=0\text{ for every }\nu,\qquad
 U=0\quad\text{or}\quad(V=0\text{ and }D\ne0).
\end{equation}

Let $\boldsymbol A$ be an encoded tuple, with a common modulus $N$.
Add just the two global equalities
\begin{equation}\label{saturation}
 L(\boldsymbol A)=Nq_L,\qquad
 U(\boldsymbol A)D(\boldsymbol A)e
   =U(\boldsymbol A)+Nq_s.
\end{equation}
The new $e$ is an \emph{unbounded} natural coordinate. It does not receive
a range certificate or become a Pell sequence index.

\begin{lemma}[Soundness]\label{sound}
Suppose $m\mid N$, $m\ge2$, and
$\boldsymbol A\equiv\boldsymbol z\pmod m$. If \eqref{saturation} holds and
\[
 0\le L(\boldsymbol z)<m,\qquad 0\le U(\boldsymbol z)<m,
\]
then \eqref{desired} holds, regardless of the size of $e$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The first equality implies $L(\boldsymbol z)\equiv0\pmod m$; its bound
therefore gives $L(\boldsymbol z)=0$. Nonnegativity gives all $G_\nu=0$
and $UV=0$. If $D(\boldsymbol z)=0$, reduce the second equality modulo $m$.
It gives $U(\boldsymbol z)\equiv0\pmod m$, and the separate bound gives
$U(\boldsymbol z)=0$. Otherwise $D\ne0$, and $UV=0$ gives the stated
alternative.
\end{proof}

The separate upper bound on $U$ is essential: a bound on $UV$ does not bound
$U$ when $V=0$. For example, $m=3$, $U=3$, $V=D=0$ satisfies both zero
congruences with an invalid guard. The construction's majorant explicitly
includes $U$, in addition to $L$, rather than assuming this missing bound.

\begin{lemma}[CRT completeness]\label{complete}
Let $m_1,\ldots,m_t$ be pairwise coprime, $K\ge1$, and
$N=(\prod_i m_i)^K$. Suppose each local tuple $\boldsymbol z_i$ satisfies
\eqref{desired}. Assume $\gcd(D(\boldsymbol z_i),m_i)=1$ whenever
$U(\boldsymbol z_i)\ne0$. Choose an encoded tuple satisfying
$\boldsymbol A\equiv\boldsymbol z_i\pmod{m_i^{2K}}$ for every $i$.
If $D(\boldsymbol A)\ge1$, then positive $e$ and natural $q_L,q_s$ exist
satisfying \eqref{saturation}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For every row with $U(\boldsymbol z_i)\ne0$, choose
$e_i\equiv D(\boldsymbol z_i)^{-1}\pmod{m_i^{2K}}$.
For every other row choose $e_i=0$. CRT supplies an $e$ in these residue
classes, and adding a sufficiently large multiple of $N^2$ makes $e\ge1$.
For every $i$, either $U(\boldsymbol z_i)=0$ or $D(\boldsymbol z_i)e_i=1$
modulo $m_i^{2K}$. Therefore $N^2$ divides
$U(\boldsymbol A)(D(\boldsymbol A)e-1)$.
The same polynomial-congruence argument gives $N^2\mid L(\boldsymbol A)$.
Since $L,U\ge0$, $D(\boldsymbol A)\ge1$, and $e\ge1$, both quotients in
\eqref{saturation} are nonnegative integers.
\end{proof}

Nonzero guards need not be units for arbitrary moduli. For example, $D=3$
has no inverse modulo 3. The hypothesis is supplied here by the specific
reciprocal moduli, not by an invalid general converse to Lemma~\ref{sound}.

\section{Application to the reciprocal CRT compiler}

After the inherited factorial decoding, let
\[
 t\ge12,\quad F=t!,\quad m_i=F^2/i-1\ (1\le i\le t),\quad
 B=\prod_{i=1}^t m_i=\binom{F^2-1}{t},\quad N=B^x.
\]
Every $i$ divides $F$, so $m_i\equiv-1\pmod F$. Every prime dividing an
$m_i$ is therefore greater than $t$. If a prime divided both $m_i$ and
$m_j$, it would divide $i m_i-jm_j=j-i$, which is impossible for $i\ne j$.
Thus the moduli are pairwise coprime. Since $0\le a,b,c\le t$ in each
decoded row, both nonzero differences $c-a,c-b$ have absolute value at most
$t$. Their squared product is consequently a unit modulo the row modulus.
This proves the unit hypothesis of Lemma~\ref{complete}.

The bounded local columns are now exactly
\[
 a,b,c,S,S',u,v.
\]
The original eighth column $d$ was used by the four-parameter axiom and
the equality witnessing the bundle's disequality guard. Proposition~\ref{basis}
and Lemmas~\ref{sound}--\ref{complete} remove both uses.
The global index representative and derived-label sum retain their own
range requests. Together with the exact factorial binomial this gives ten
modular digit requests, one fewer than in the parent. There are six exact
power graphs, twelve independent Pell seeds, and frame offset fourteen.

For soundness, choose a prime $p\mid m_i$ and put
$\mu=v_p(m_i)$. The retained one-direction prime-power range lemma decodes
all local columns modulo $p^{x\mu}$. The two exact global equations
\eqref{saturation} are divisible by that modulus. The new majorant includes
$L,U,D$ and the label sum. Its computed exponent is $6{,}893{,}371$,
rounded upward to $E=2^{23}$, exactly the parent's existing budget. With
$R=2+t+x+y+F$, the inherited endpoints give
\[
 R<2x,\quad x>2^{32},\quad L,U<R^E<(2x)^E<2^x\le p^{x\mu}.
\]
Lemma~\ref{sound} applies. The unchanged beta compatibility and
multiplicative Horn-flow argument now recover a refutation in the new
calculus, and Proposition~\ref{basis} converts it into an original one.

For completeness, convert an original refutation to the new basis and pad
the horizon beyond all its local parameters and labels. The inherited
beta construction supplies $x,y$. Choose each positive independent encoded
representative initially in $(0,N^2]$, with the prescribed local residues
modulo $m_i^{2x}$. Then replace
\begin{equation}\label{lifts}
 A_a\longmapsto A_a+2N^2,\qquad A_b\longmapsto A_b+N^2,
\end{equation}
leaving $A_c$ in $(0,N^2]$. Both lifted representatives are strictly greater
than $A_c$, so the global $D$ is positive. All row congruences are preserved.
Lemma~\ref{complete}, with $K=x$, supplies $e,q_L,q_s$.

The two lifts are not optional bookkeeping. In a proof that never needs a
bundle branch, canonical global representatives can have $A_b=A_c$ while
global $U>0$. Then the requested natural quotient would satisfy
$0=U+Nq_s$, which is impossible. Lifting only $A_a$, as in the parent,
would not fix this case. The metadata and proof both use \eqref{lifts}.

The parent's radix is self-sized:
\[
 q=(NH)^x,\qquad H=1+\text{sum of the independent representatives}.
\]
There is no aggregate upper-bound certificate restricting the lifts.
After \eqref{lifts}, every independent representative is at most $3N^2$.
The semantic label anchor $J$ contains the term $A_a$ and its quadratic
syntax growth; in particular $J\ge2A_a^2>8N^4>6N^2$. It still exceeds
twice every nonanchor index. The retained cofinal-anchor construction can
therefore choose its Pell witnesses. The radix is chosen after the lifted
representatives, so its coefficient bounds adapt to them. The unbounded
$e$ is absent from the range and Pell-index lists. No circular enlargement
of the radix or horizon is introduced.

The stronger precision $m_i^{2x}$ supplies each binomial range divisibility
to precision $m_i^x$: one vanishing factor has the stronger valuation,
whereas the denominator factorial loses at most $k-1\le x-1$ for a range
length $k\le x$. This is the retained one-direction argument; no general
converse of the binomial range lemma is used.

\section{Polynomial export and calibration boundary}

The raw adapter emits 337 natural variables and 289 quadratic residuals.
The inherited nonnegative affine and certified quadratic contractions
eliminate 106 variables and produce the delivered 231-variable quartic.
Two restorations have negative coefficients but carry exact rational
sum-of-squares plus nonnegative-monomial certificates. The inherited
independent checker replays those restorations and independently expands
the target polynomial. The supplied standalone checker independently checks
the literal target's coefficients and the 521-line basis derivation, but
does not reconstruct the inherited arithmetic interpretation.

The ordinary proof dependency is explicit. The parent supplies the
descending-factorial bootstrap, binomial digit graph, common Pell frame,
semantic index anchor, and original numerical-calculus calibration. The
new claims proved here are the basis replacement, the modular saturation
argument, and its positive-quotient/cofinal completeness adjustment.
Together these give, within ZFC and relative to those retained arithmetic
proofs,
\[
 (\exists\boldsymbol z\in\NN^{231})\ P_{231}(\boldsymbol z)=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \text{there exists a finite syntactic ZFC refutation}.
\]
Negating gives the requested form with $\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$.
This is an equivalence, not a proof of consistency. We do not claim that
the new equation's entire interpretation has been checked by Lean.

The parent retains named calibration theorems
\file{KEndpoint.c222_iff_zf_refutation} and
\file{ConstructibleChoice.zf_refutation_iff_zfc_refutation}.
Its calibration files are pinned at repository commit
\file{08200eb10fde2db408c69dffd840d6920a569b1f}; that older calibration
pin is distinct from the current intake commit and is not silently updated.

\section{What was learned}

The inverse can safely remain unbounded because soundness reduces the
guard failure to a separately bounded nonnegative factor. This is the
useful general mechanism. One range column and one Pell seed disappear,
but K/S/Peirce introduce additional source branches and arithmetic gates,
and the saturation row also requires gates and a quotient. With this
compiler the net result is two additional natural variables and four
additional rows. Fewer source parameters do not imply a smaller literal
Diophantine equation.

No literature-priority claim is made for modular saturation. We have not
asserted an optimal implicational basis, an optimal inverse encoding, or a
new variable record. The retained artifacts make the architectural change
auditable and leave a precise target for later work: reduce the polynomial
cost of a three-parameter classical basis, or share the inverse with an
already cofinal witness using an independently proved joint existence
argument. Such sharing is not used in the delivered equation.
