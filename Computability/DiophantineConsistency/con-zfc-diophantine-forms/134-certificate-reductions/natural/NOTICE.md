## New adaptations in this component

The current construction uses the descending fixed point

    F=floor((F^2-1)^t/binom(F^2-1,t))  iff  F=t!,

on the domain stated and proved in the report. It replaces the rising
reciprocal moduli by `d_i=F^2/i-1` and changes the index congruence to
`C+1=F^2+Bq_C`. It combines the retained semantic anchor with one
nonnegative cap-and-error row, and lowers the horizon floor to 12 using
the exact emitted local and label majorants. The final measured endpoint
is 233 natural variables, 182 quadratic residuals, degree 4, 7,313
monomials and coefficient height 86,543,666.

The implementation is not described as an unmodified historical engine.
The current `build.py` supplies the combined cap, semantic lift, dominant
denominator and export. The included
`engine/direct/build_modular_factorial.py` supplies the descending
factorial/modulus interface; the included
`engine/direct/r14/architecture/build_derived_label_range.py` supplies
the parameterized horizon and descending index row. The report and
`REVIEW.md` prove the corresponding new ordinary arithmetic steps.

The engine hierarchy is shortened for portability. `ENGINE_MAP.json`
records 97 retained files with their pre-relocation staged input paths,
input hashes, released paths and released hashes. Three files have path

the actual included engine; its input hashes are not presented as Git
hashes of an entirely unmodified parent.

Historical proofs, notices, READMEs, report fragments and scripts are
retained to identify the arithmetic ancestry. Their old output counts,
ceiling formulas, cap variants and commands concern their historical
parents. The current endpoint is defined by `equation.json` and
`expanded_polynomial.json`; its current entry points are `build.py` and
`verify.py` in this directory, and its current ordinary proof is the
natural section of the main report. A successful historical check is not
advertised as verification of the new theorem.

## What is and is not certified

The supplied exact finite checks reconstruct the exported rows and
collected coefficients, replay all eliminated-coordinate restorations,
verify their natural-domain conditions, check the engine binding and
reject deliberately altered data. Reconstruction regenerates the source
and endpoint from the included literal numerical-calculus input.

Pell classification, factorial uniqueness, CRT decoding, unbounded
growth and the refutation calibration are ordinary mathematical proofs.
The named inherited logical interfaces are
`KEndpoint.c222_iff_zf_refutation` and
`ConstructibleChoice.zf_refutation_iff_zfc_refutation`. Their entire
transitive formal development is not rebuilt here, and no new Lean,
Rocq or encoded object-language ZFC certificate is claimed.

The resulting theorem is the ZFC-provable equivalence of absence of a
natural zero with Con(ZFC). It does not prove either consistency or
unsolvability separately. No global minimum or literature-wide priority
is asserted. Independently performed reviews are separate agent
derivations within this AI-assisted workflow, not external human
endorsements.
