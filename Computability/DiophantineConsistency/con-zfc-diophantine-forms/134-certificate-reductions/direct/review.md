## 2. Signed-scale growth and the deterministic center

The odd nonzero frame ordinate and the norm at base W+1 classify
`(|A|,|W(2z+1)|)` at a positive index divisible by W. Consequently
`|A|>W^W` and `|A-1|>4W^3`, without assuming a sign for A. The even
frame norm also makes A odd.

The square cap gives `|C|>W>1`. Therefore the principal and common norms
have positive indices i and l at `a=|A|`, and `C^2|J_c` forces `i<l`.
The squared-ordinate divisor lemma gives `|C||l`. Its usual stronger
conclusion follows by writing `l=ik` and reducing

    Y_l(a)/Y_i(a) = k X_i(a)^(k-1) modulo Y_i(a).

Since `gcd(X_i,Y_i)=1`, the squared divisibility implies `Y_i|k`.

The deterministic identity is valid for either sign of A:

    D=A+(1-A)H^2
     =1-(A-1)(A^2-1)J_c^2.

It gives `D=A modulo H` and `D=1 modulo 4C`. Since A is odd,
the coefficient `(A-1)(A^2-1)` is divisible by four. For `A>=3`,
the second expression is less than -1; for `A<=-3`, it exceeds 1.
Thus the comparison Pell classification is legitimate even though D
need not be positive.

The comparison ordinate cannot vanish: it is h modulo 4C, while
`0<h<W<|C|`. Signed base transfer and the inherited up-to-sign trace
residue lemma give its absolute index j satisfying `j=+/-i modulo 2l`.
The ordinate congruence gives `j=+/-h modulo 4C`. Together they imply
`i=+/-h modulo 2|C|`. Since `1<=i<=|C|` and `0<h<|C|`, the only
possibility is `i=h`. The absolute anchor index is proved before choosing
the sign of its ordinate.

## 5. The odd auxiliary quotient works for every anchor index

Choose the positive canonical pair `U=X_h(A), C=Y_h(A)`, and set
`l=hC`. The composition identity gives

    J_c=Y_l(A)=C Y_C(U).

For some integer T, the Pell polynomial expansion is

    Y_C(U)=C U^(C-1)+(U^2-1)T.

Since `U^2-1=(A^2-1)C^2`, it follows that

    J_c/C^2=U^(C-1)+(A^2-1)C T.

This is a positive odd integer. Indeed A is odd, so U is odd, while
`A^2-1` is even. No parity restriction on h is needed. Hence
`e=(J_c/C^2-1)/2` is an allowed integer and the exact new common-multiple
row is satisfied.

After defining D deterministically, the choices `S=X_h(D)` and
`Q=Y_h(D)` satisfy the comparison norm and both congruences by polynomial
base transfer. These coordinates and their congruence quotients may be
signed; their guards have not silently been reintroduced. The genuine

by the established recurrence identities and retained representations.
