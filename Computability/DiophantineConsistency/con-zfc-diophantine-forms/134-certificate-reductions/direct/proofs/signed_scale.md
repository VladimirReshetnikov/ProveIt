# An unoriented cap and signed-scale power semantics

This is a separate ordinary proposal. It changes no sealed source, program,
projection, coefficient tuple, page or Lean file. No new source is generated
and no numerical saving is claimed. The literal correspondence below is to
the accepted signed-cap source, seal
`53244a0a26bcfe2411c28adf2c6d74f7a4618c0c42c7904e8818be4e3f9a6ef5`.
The executable cap-projection packet continues to use that source unchanged.

The proposal removes the cap's own C-W Legendre graph and removes (C-W)^2
from the pooled row. It retains F>=0, the strict common comparison ordinate
Vc>0, d>=0, the frame ordinate guard, the quadratic cap graph and all other
accepted front guards. It does not compose the separate F-guard deletion or
private-helper reflection proposals. Its full ordinary conclusion is
existence equivalence at a fixed native arithmetic front, after fresh private
Pell reconstruction. It is not a fixed-whole-body guard deletion.

## 1. Abstract hypotheses and signed recurrences

For every integer A define

    X0(A)=1, Y0(A)=0,
    X_(j+1)(A)=A Xj(A)+(A^2-1)Yj(A),
    Y_(j+1)(A)=Xj(A)+A Yj(A).

For negative j use X_(-j)=Xj and Y_(-j)=-Yj. Put a=|A|>=2,
epsilon=sgn(A). For j>=1 the polynomial identities give

    Xj(A)=epsilon^j Xj(a),
    Yj(A)=epsilon^(j-1)Yj(a),
    Xj(A)^2-(A^2-1)Yj(A)^2=1,
    Yj(A)=j modulo A-1.

Assume W>=4, a-1>4W^3, 1<=m<=W-2, |ki|<W, and at least one ki is
nonzero. The actual t target, t0+16, supplies that last condition without
interpreting any power. Main ordinates yi and abscissas xi are unrestricted
integers satisfying all m norms xi^2-(A^2-1)yi^2=1.

Keep a full cap comparison certificate, with C signed:

    Xc^2-(A^2-1)C^2=1,
    Uc^2-(A^2-1)Vc^2=1,
    Sc^2-(Bc^2-1)Rc^2=1,
    Vc>0, d>=0, Vc=C^2(e+1),
    Bc=1+4Vc(d+1)=A+Uc f,
    Sc=Xc+Uc g, Rc=W+4C r.

Abscissas and e,f,g,r are initially signed; there is no sign or lower
predicate on C. Keep only these two pooled predicates:

    C^2-sum_i yi^2-1 = p^2+q^2+s(s+1),
    sum_i(yi-ki)^2 = (A-1)h, h in Z.             (P)

The first is a nonnegative quadratic cap. The second has no cap summand.
In particular h is signed while A is signed. All cap/comparison variables
remain outside the semantic frame W.

## 2. Exclude the collapsed cap before using an index theorem

The quadratic cap gives C^2>=1+sum yi^2, hence C is nonzero. A nonzero
Pell ordinate need not be large: Y1(a)=1. Therefore it would be false to
infer |C|>=a just from its norm and nonzeroness.

If |C|=1, the quadratic cap forces every yi=0. Then the actual pooled
left side is S=sum ki^2. It satisfies

    0<S<=m(W-1)^2<W^3<|A-1|.

The strict positivity uses the nonzero t target. This contradicts (P),
which makes S divisible by A-1. This argument uses the actual small sum
only in this collapsed case; it does not bound the usual huge pooled sum.

Consequently L=|C|>1. Integral Pell classification now gives unique i>=2
with L=Yi(a), |Xc|=Xi(a). In particular

    L>=Y2(a)=2a>W, and 0<i<L.                   (1)

No cap-index lower graph, positive C, positive A, main index meaning or
power meaning has been used to obtain (1).

## 3. The absolute cap anchor

Classify Vc=Y_H(a), |Uc|=X_H(a), with H>=1. The unchanged squared
divisibility lemma applies to L^2 | Vc and gives i<=H and L | H.
This is the same inherited Pell divisibility contract as in the accepted
full cap proof, applied to positive L; it is not a new arithmetic assumption
about a signed ordinate.

The constructor gives Bc>=5 and Bc=1 modulo4L. The comparison norm has
some absolute index j>=0. Its ordinate Rc cannot vanish: Rc=W modulo4C,
the ideals generated by4C and4L are equal, and 0<W<L. Hence j>=1.

Modulo |Uc| the base-transfer identity is

    Xj(Bc)=Xj(A)=epsilon^j Xj(a).

Together with Sc=Xc modulo Uc and independent abscissa signs, this gives
Xj(a)=+/-Xi(a) modulo X_H(a). The accepted up-to-sign abscissa residue
lemma therefore gives j=+/-i modulo2H. Its endpoint hypotheses hold:
i>=2 and H>=i. This does not apply the exceptional i=1 endpoint.

Modulo4L, Bc=1 gives Yj(Bc)=j, so Rc=W gives j=+/-W modulo4L.
Since L|H, combine the congruences to obtain i=+/-W modulo2L. By (1),
both i and W lie strictly between0 and L. The negative case is impossible
because 0<i+W<2L; the positive case forces i=W. Thus

    |C|=Y_W(|A|).                              (2)

This proves the required absolute cap bound. Neither sign of C is selected.
It is important that Vc>0 and d>=0 remain in the proposal: they provide
the common Pell index and Bc>=5. The optional helper reflection is separate.

## 4. The primary pool recovers signed-A canonical ordinates

Classify each primary pair by |xi|=X_(ji)(a), |yi|=Y_(ji)(a), ji>=0.
The quadratic cap and (2) imply |yi|<L=Y_W(a), hence ji<W. Write
yi=sigma_i Y_(ji)(a). At ji=0 either sign may be used. Put
tau_i=sigma_i epsilon^(ji-1) for ji>0, arbitrarily at0. Then

    yi=tau_i ji modulo A-1.

The pooled row (P) makes the finite representative

    E=sum_i(tau_i ji-ki)^2

divisible by A-1. Its bound is

    0<=E<=4m(W-1)^2<4W^3<|A-1|.

Thus E=0 and tau_i ji=ki term by term. Consequently

    yi=Y_(ki)(A), xi=+/-X_(ki)(A).              (3)

This also covers zero and negative requested indices, with the conventions
in section1. It does not interpret negative-index requests as ordinary
positive-integer powers. As before, only the finite representative E is
small; the actual sum in (P) generally is not. No sign of h is assumed.

There is now no reason to recover A>0 or choose C's sign. In particular
the sign conclusion from the old cap-in-pool row is not reused. Evenness
of W remains true of the actual frame, but the proof above does not need
it for an orientation of A.

## 5. Power orientation relative to signed A

Let 1<=k<W, 2<=n<W and |m|<W be integer semantic ports. Suppose the
frame gives n^2+1<a, a>W^W, and the actual request is

    x+(n-A)y=m+T z, T=2An-n^2-1,
    y=Y_k(A), x=+/-X_k(A).

Set X=X_k(A), Y=Y_k(A), H=X+(n-A)Y and J=X-(n-A)Y. Direct polynomial
identities valid for every integer A give

    HJ=1+T Y^2,
    H_(k+1)=n H_k+T Y_k, H_0=1.

Therefore H=n^k modulo T, without a positive-A premise. If A=a>0,
T>=4a-n^2-1>3a. If A=-a, |T|=2an+n^2+1>=4a+n^2+1. In both cases

    |T|>W^W,
    |m-n^k|<=W-1+(W-1)^(W-1)<W^W,
    |m*n^k+1|<=(W-1)^W+1<W^W.

For the canonical branch x=X, the request implies m=n^k modulo T,
so the first bound gives m=n^k. For the opposite canonical branch x=-X,
its left side is -J. Multiplying by H gives m*n^k=-1 modulo T.
The integer m*n^k+1 is nonzero since n^k>=2 cannot divide1, contradicting
the second bound. Thus the request orients x=X_k(A) and proves m=n^k>0.

The word canonical matters: X_k(A) may be negative, and Y_k(A) may be
negative. This proof makes no assertion that the actual x or y is positive.
For n=1 the opposite branch m=-1 remains possible. After a SAME-pair
n>=2 request has selected x=X_k(A), however, its n=1 request gives m=1
from |m-1|<|2A-2|. No independent base-one orientation is asserted.

The accepted square frame gives stronger bounds than required here:
W=2(2+sum37 atoms^2), so n^2<W/2 and all |m|,k<W. Its norm with the
retained Z>=1 gives a>=(W+1)^W and a-1>4W^3. Hence all stated
modulus bounds are available before interpreting any power.

## 8. Both existential directions and fresh positive private witnesses

From a zero of the proposed kernel, sections2..7 recover the genuine
arithmetic/power meanings of the unchanged native front. To construct a
zero of the sealed positive-oriented cap source at that SAME front, choose
a fresh positive A from the frame Pell family after W is fixed. Rebuild
Z, all canonical main pairs and the derived pair, all shared T gates and
power quotients using the genuine powers. Choose a fresh positive cap
C=Y_W(A), then the common positive comparison unit with its C^2 divisor
and the full finite-ring/CRT comparison witnesses. Supply its retained
private sign triples, C-W triple and quadratic-bound triple. Finally choose

    h_old=[sum_i(Y_(ki)(A)-ki)^2+(C-W)^2]/(A-1).

This is integral because every difference is divisible by A-1. The same
growth bound as in the accepted cap completeness proof supplies both cap
Legendre triples. All already fixed native front rows remain zero and all
new private defining rows are satisfied by construction. Retained front
triples can be copied; changed private triples are chosen freshly. No old
A, negative private ordinate, cap sign, quotient or full body tuple is
silently reused. This reconstructs precisely the sealed source, whose
ordinary native/refutation calibration is already accepted.

Conversely, an old sealed cap-source zero has A>0 and its canonical cap
and primary indices by its accepted ordinary theorem. Its lower graph
can be forgotten. Keep every other coordinate except the signed pool
quotient and set

    h_new=h_old-(C-W)^2/(A-1).

The quotient is integral because C=Y_W(A)=W modulo A-1. Coordinate395
has no other occurrence, so this restores exactly the new primary-only
pool. Drop the three private lower-graph witnesses. Thus the forward
existence map does not incorrectly leave the old pooled quotient fixed.

This proves the proposed kernel's ordinary existence equivalence at the
same native front, conditional only on the explicitly inherited full Pell
classification/divisibility/residue/CRT contracts already used in the
accepted source. It does not claim A or C becomes positive in every new
zero. The construction may replace their entire private witnesses.

Before any finite implementation, bind the two exact edits, all retained
graphs, the new signed-scale power proof and actual reconstruction maps
independently; then review any new code before execution. The separate
F omission and optional helper reflection need their own combined proof
and literal bindings. No projected count can be inferred by subtraction.
