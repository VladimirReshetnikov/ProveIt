## The fixed BFS tuple

The coefficient tuple in the new ten-positive equations is the **entire**
calibrated tuple from
`Computability/DiophantineConsistency/candidates/one-page-short10/`.
Its complete page, displayed equation, README, and source pins are retained.
The printed finite constant recipe and its independently written replay
construct exactly the four files in `../coefficients/compact44_bfs/`.

The zero-offset parent also shipped an original compact44 tuple. Its
payloads are retained under `coefficients_original44/` as provenance
controls. Its native `K0` differs from the BFS `K0`. No proof in this
packet identifies those two constants or substitutes one coefficient into
the other tuple. The ordinary positive-source argument is coefficient
uniform on the stated port box; the coherent state/color transfer supplies
the whole BFS calibration. The port named `MF` includes its source shift
by `beta` exactly once.

The complete native/compiler/refutation theorem links the specified
source to the same finite syntactic ZFC-refutation problem used in the
repository's K215 calibration. That unbounded theorem is inherited. The
seed decoder, all 943 tiles, byte reconstruction, and finite polynomial
checks verify the finite objects used by the theorem; they do not newly
prove the theorem itself.

## Unrestricted-integer parent

The separate `../integer12/` packet retains
`Computability/DiophantineConsistency/candidates/scalar-carrier12/`, its
source proofs, all six source34 coefficient ports, their exact recipe,
and independent source pins. Its tuple is named
`mixed-parity-unrounded-1064921` and is not mixed with the BFS tuple.

The centered equations are exact polynomial substitutions into these
parents. Consequently their entire integer zero sets are related by the
displayed invertible coordinate shears, including exceptional tuples.

The new `../integer12_saturated/` packet uses the same complete parent and
tuple. Its proof imports the generalized signed native-kernel interface,
the prime-order separation lemma, and the full-residue interpolation
theorem with an arbitrary positive modulus parameter. It explicitly
restores `I=U tau` and `T_old=tau T`. This is not a pointwise substitution
into the full old scalar carrier, whose auxiliary coordinate differs.
The supplied independent semantic audit reviews that distinction and
the order of all completeness witness choices.

## Earlier ideas and new contribution

Pell-period divisibility, coprime saturation in other families, root
centering, and distributed transport numerators have precedents in the
inspected collection. In particular the incoming dyadic submission already
expands the transport numerator and separately centers a strong root.
The new result here is their specific combination in the ten-positive
first/strong Pell kernel, the congruence excluding the exceptional two-root
cancellation, the complete changed-witness construction, and the resulting
four literal improvements. The paper makes no worldwide-priority claim
for the individual algebraic ingredients.

The relation-algebra appendix credits the earlier finite-set-theory and
algebraic-logic provability approaches. Its local inference kernel is
explicit, but no complete replacement source encoding is claimed.
