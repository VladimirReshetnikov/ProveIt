# Provenance and attribution

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt project, 23 October 2026.
This is AI-assisted mathematical research with independent internal reviews;
it is not a claim of external peer review or worldwide priority.

The new contribution is the use of fixed-prime valuation separation to combine
the compatible Pell and positivity square conditions, its normalized canonical
adapter, and the resulting literal 13-, 14-, and 15-integer polynomials.

The three-generalized-pentagonal representation and norm-9 lifting are classical.
The report credits Guy, Sun, Réalis' identity as discussed by Sun, and Oh. The
joint positivity argument, rational Pell descent, native coefficient constructor,
determinant guard, source orientation, CRT parent, and K215 calibration are
inherited from the pinned ProveIt work and are identified in the source ledger.

The root license is the inherited ProveIt MIT No Attribution license, applied
also to the newly authored code and text in this contribution. Retained component
notices and licenses under `licenses/`, `upstream/`, and the original parent ZIP
remain in force. The embedded original archive is retained byte for byte.
