\begin{abstract}
This iteration combines six newly delivered constructions and also investigates
architectures that do not assume K215 or Jones as a required starting point.
The principal new structural result is a quartic in42 unrestricted integer
unknowns, improving45: a cofinal factor-four square scale forces a large
modulus before Pell rank recovery, allowing two positivity guards to be
replaced by one. Exact monic projections give36-integer sextic and32-integer
octic companions. A smaller degree-separated source gives a nine-positive
polynomial of degree $1\,731\,700$ in462 operations and a lower-degree
$1\,494\,006$/487 tradeoff. Congruence norms and a two-bit carry trim give
ten integers, degree $3\,327\,556$, in519 operations. An adaptive divisor
modulus gives fourteen positive unknowns of degree30 in87 operations.
All are explicit fixed polynomials with complete coefficient bindings.
The proofs are ordinary mathematics formalizable in ZFC, with identified
inherited arithmetic/compiler dependencies and finite exact certificates;
no consistency decision, global optimum, or new end-to-end kernel proof is
claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem, scope and immutable baseline}
We seek an explicit polynomial $F$ with integer coefficients and a stated
integer domain $D$ for which ordinary mathematics establishes
\[
 \mathrm{ZFC}\vdash
 \bigl(\neg\exists\mathbf x\in D^n\ F(\mathbf x)=0\bigr)
 \longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\]
Here $\N$ includes zero; strictly positive and unrestricted integer domains
are distinguished everywhere. Intermediate registers are polynomial
abbreviations, not hidden quantified unknowns. Fixed numerals are supplied
as actual payloads or explicit finite integer recipes, not selected according
to whether consistency holds.

The six original incoming archives were retrieved at
\texttt{7331974b6966}, placed at \texttt{beb0192cb8dc}. Full immutable IDs,
Git blob hashes, archive SHA256 values and byte counts appear in
\texttt{INCOMING\_PINS.json}. The project is
\begin{center}\small
\url{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/tree/main/Computability/DiophantineConsistency/con-zfc-diophantine-forms}.
\end{center}
All six original root replays passed in fresh copies; the original trees
were left unchanged. The input packets are Free Coordinate, Integral Root,
Separated Degrees, our preceding Degree Separated packet, Calibrated Carry,
and Congruence Norms. We use their actual archived circuits and proofs,
not a possibly incomplete placed tree. Independently recomputed live-gate
counts and the reviewed formal-status snapshot accompany the report.

Neither K215 nor Jones is a requirement of the problem. We considered direct
proof predicates, other constructive DPRM compilers, and fixed-input
universality. Those alternatives are discussed separately; none is passed
off as a validated smaller endpoint. The successful low-degree quartic and
its projections do not use the Jones universal expression or Sun's reduction,
although their source calibration still uses the established native compiler.

\paragraph{What is genuinely new.}
The quartic improvement uses cofinal witness flexibility to establish
$|E|>3R+1$ before rank recovery; it is not merely a gate optimization.
The source improvement combines incoming108-position weights with the
previous row shear, fresh target placement and a global sign reversal.
All three reversed kernels now have positive leaders, simplifying the
unconditional positivity argument. The nine-variable alternatives exploit
higher Pell majorants while retaining positivity-first free-coordinate
soundness. The ten-integer construction combines the smaller source with
incoming two-root congruence separation and removes two provably redundant
population bits. The fourteen-positive construction uses the recovered
scale divisibility to replace a fixed modulus by $4qY$.

\paragraph{Coefficient coherence.}

No new coefficient-storage record is claimed.

\section{Formalization boundary and validation}
The recent \texttt{dabf4e39f103} commit reports local Lean verification of
package52's literal60-gate/22-row source,136 shifted coefficient records,
position/kernel bounds, sparse low-mask decoding, cofinal estimates,
ratio substitution and a margin-first finalizer. The source adapter,
actual native calibration, assembled closed replacement expression and
internal ZFC equivalence are still expressly separate obligations. We read
the reported3709-job build and axiom audit; we did not rerun that Lean build.
Existing closed \texttt{ExplicitZFC} and \texttt{NativeZFC} endpoints are
not automatically certificates for the smaller circuits in this archive.

The arithmetic proofs here establish new equivalences using precisely
identified inherited compiler/Pell contracts. Their ordinary ZFC-formalizable
status is distinguished from a checked external Lean theorem and from an
exported object-language ZFC derivation. Only the ten-integer route additionally
imports Sun's2026 result. No use is made of an assumption that ZFC is
arithmetically sound or that either side of the consistency equivalence is true.

The complete release is hash-authenticated, copied to an isolated directory,
and replayed without network access. Exact polynomial identities, source
isolation, coefficient payloads, gate liveness, cancelled degree upper bounds
and nonzero leading witnesses are checked. The two quartic projections have
unit leading coefficients on the actual fixed slice. Component mutation
tests are distinguished from root byte-integrity corruption controls.
Normal and optimized Python runs are recorded separately; no disabled
assertion run is silently reported as a proof.

The root receipt lists every executed stage and every regenerated file
compared byte-for-byte. PDF generation is independent of the mathematical
replay; PDF metadata can differ when recompiled. The distributed ZIP is
extracted and the complete replay repeated before delivery. These finite
checks support explicit arithmetic/data claims and implementation fidelity;
they do not replace the universal proofs or certify an unbounded compiler
by testing finitely many assignments.

\paragraph{Corrections and rejected shortcuts.}
The incoming Q45 prose writes a transport quotient shifted by one, while its
literal Q45 and Q50 row is $(K_0+w)C=F+z(q-1)$. We retain the literal row;
the corrected late argument gives $z>0$, exactly what is needed. The
original files are preserved and the correction is documented separately.
The unsuccessful shared-sign proposal with only $w=u^2$ is not claimed;
its negative branch lacked the early scale gap. Likewise the initial
positive14 draft degree34 is superseded by the adaptive-modulus degree30
form, not counted as an additional frontier improvement. No unproved
cofinal restriction on a canonical transport witness is used.
