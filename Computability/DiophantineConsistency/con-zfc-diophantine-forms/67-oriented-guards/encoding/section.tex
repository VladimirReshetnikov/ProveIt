\label{sec:source108}

incoming \emph{Separated Degrees} construction, our earlier two row shears,
a new target placement, and a global reversal of sign. The last operation
makes all three kernel leaders positive. The source has 89 records and
convolution height 1954, versus 89 records and height 2161 in our previous
source. These are source-code parameters, not additional Diophantine unknowns.

\paragraph{A simpler unconditional sign proof.}
Every kernel coefficient has absolute value below $C=2^{2134927}$.
Fix $\kappa=2134940$ and set
\[
 B=2^\kappa b^2=8192Cb^2,\quad \ell=\sum_iB^{w_i},\quad U=\ell+g,
 \quad G=D_2(B)U^2+D_1(B)U+D_0(B).
\]
For a kernel with degree $h$ and positive leader $a$, the absolute lower-tail
contribution is less than $CB^h/(B-1)$. Since $B>2C$, each kernel is positive.
Thus $G>0$ whenever $b,g\ge1$, without a binary-base assumption or a
final-equation hypothesis. No cancellation between the three kernels is needed.

Define
\[
\begin{gathered}
 A_* =\sum_jB^{q_j},\qquad N_0=B^{109},\qquad Z=B^{1955},\\
 M=N_0-1-(b-1)\ell,\qquad J_*=2G+A_*,\\
 S=g+N_0J_*,\qquad T=M+N_0(B-2)A_*,\qquad G_{\rm cap}=32bB^{108}.
\end{gathered}
\]
Then $S>0$ and $T\ge0$ unconditionally. On the ratio-derived range
$g<4bB^{108}$, one has $U<6bB^{108}$ and
\[
 G<\frac{C}{B-1}
 \bigl(36b^2B^{1955}+6bB^{1951}+B^{1940}\bigr),\qquad
 \frac{2G}{Z}<\frac{86Cb^2}{B-1}<\frac1{64}.
\]
Also $A_*<Z/64$, $(B-2)A_*<Z/64$, and $g<N_0$. Hence
\[
 S+T<N_0Z=B^{2064}.
\]
This is proved on the full necessary range, not merely on the smaller
range occupied by canonical witnesses.

\paragraph{Signed-digit decoding without spurious zeros.}
For $b=2^k$ and $B=2^v$, the low mask $M$ has digit $B-b$ at each of the
31 active positions, and digit $B-1$ elsewhere below position109.
Thus disjointness of $g$ and $M$ is equivalent to
$g=\sum_i z_iB^{w_i}$ with $0\le z_i<b$. The word $U$ then encodes
$p_i=z_i+1>0$. Every convolution coefficient $h_t$ satisfies
\[
 |h_t|<C\bigl((\textstyle\sum_i p_i)^2+\sum_i p_i+1\bigr)
 \le993Cb^2<B/8.
\]
The formal coefficients of $J_*$ are $2h_t+\mathbf1_{\{t\text{ target}\}}$.
Base-$B$ normalization therefore uses only incoming borrows $0,-1$.
At a target, $h_t=0$ produces digit $1$ or $0$, both disjoint from $B-2$.
For $h_t>0$ the digit lies between $2$ and $B/2$; for $h_t<0$ it lies
between $B/2$ and $B-1$. Both nonzero cases intersect $B-2$.
Consequently
\[
 S\mathbin{\&}T=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 g\text{ encodes a positive parent zero}.
\]
Conversely every parent zero is encoded for all sufficiently large binary
$b$, with $0<g<bB^{108}$ and $32g<G_{\rm cap}$.
The all-one parent tuple is not a zero, since $r_5(1,\ldots,1)=1$;
this proves the strict lower bound on $g$. Every weight is positive, so
canonical $g$ is divisible by $B$ and hence by $b^2$.

\paragraph{Exact recalibration for the free-coordinate finalizer.}
Writing $v=\kappa+2k$, the mask population is
\[
 \operatorname{pop}(T)=131v-31k-22.
\]
Set
\[
 L=bB^{2064},\quad R=(S+T)L+L-1-S,\quad
 c=2^{15\kappa/2-11}=2^{16012039},\quad N=cB^{1090}.
\]
The even choice of $\kappa$ ensures that $c$ is an integer. Unconditionally
$R=(L-1)S+LT+L-1\ge8$. On the bounded branch $S+T<L$; if
$u=\log_2L=k+2064v$, then
\[
 \operatorname{pop}(R)=u+\operatorname{pop}(T)-\tau,
 \qquad \tau=\operatorname{pop}(S)+\operatorname{pop}(T)
                  -\operatorname{pop}(S+T)\ge0.
\]
Here $\tau=0$ exactly when $S\mathbin{\&}T=0$, and
\[
 u+\operatorname{pop}(T)=2195v-30k-22=2\log_2N.
\]
Legendre's valuation identity proves
$N^2\mid\binom{2R}{R}$ exactly when the source is satisfied.
The degrees entering the arithmetic finalizer are $\deg R=8255$ and
$\deg N=2180$; intermediate circuit registers are not additional variables.
