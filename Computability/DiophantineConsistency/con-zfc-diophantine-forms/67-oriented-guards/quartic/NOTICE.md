The literal construction starts from the Separated Degrees Q45 packet,
whose original source and notices are retained under upstream/q45.
Its ternary guards, dyadic radix guard and coherent coefficient compiler
are inherited. The new contribution is the cofinal factor-four square
scale and the ordered sign-orientation lemma eliminating three unknowns.
The exact sparse parser follows the transparent arithmetic style of the
retained Q45 builder; no imported builder is executed. AI-assisted internal
reviews are not external human endorsement or a kernel certificate.
