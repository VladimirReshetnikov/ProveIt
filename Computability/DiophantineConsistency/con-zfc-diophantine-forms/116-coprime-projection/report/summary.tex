\begin{abstract}
This iteration replaces several independent square-root constraints by
coprime products, and makes an existing rational Pell root carry the
congruences formerly enforced by separate coordinates. The resulting
polynomials retain explicit fixed coefficients and ordinary two-direction
proofs of their zero-existence equivalence to the calibrated source.

The completed integer constructions include eleven variables at degree
$688$ and $126$ arithmetic operations, compared with the previous
$2240/206$; a lower-operation eleven-variable alternative has degree
$1632$ and $114$ operations. A thirteen-integer companion reaches degree
$274$ with $129$ operations, versus the recent $536/135$ construction.
Twelve- and fourteen-integer tradeoffs have degree/operation pairs
$500/148$ and $108/119$, respectively.
A rational first-root projection gives fifteen
integer variables at degree $56$ and $110$ operations, compared with the
recent $172/123$. A distinct coprime fusion gives ten positive variables
at degree $266$ and $96$ operations, improving both late $340/103$ and
$368/98$ alternatives.

A separate reusable result represents positive exponentiation with nine
natural auxiliary variables, and shares the same frame among several
power graphs. Its generic tiling composition is distinguished from a
calibrated consistency equation. No global minimality, consistency proof
or new end-to-end proof-assistant certificate is claimed.
\end{abstract}
