\section{A triple-centered companion: thirteen integers, degree 274}
\label{sec:triple-carrier}
A third endpoint removes the auxiliary coordinate $y$ as well as $f$.
It has \textbf{13 unrestricted integer variables, exact degree 274 and 129
live operations}, improving both degree and evaluator cost over the inspected
13-variable degree 536/135-gate equation.

Keep the shared definitions with $D_f=1+\Delta I^2$, $Q=\Delta(D_f-1)$,
$V=D_f(cT-R)-c$, and $Z=Un+MK_*+H(v-q^2)$.  Set
\begin{align*}
 D_\tau&=1+L(L+k),&D_a&=(Q-1)(QV^2-1),\\
 A&=2L+k,&B&=6(a+2)I,\\
 C_*&=9(2Q-1)V,&Z_*&=Z+U(A+B+C_*),\\
 s&=Z_*^2,&u&=4U^2D_\tau,\\
 v_*&=36U^2D_f,&w_*&=324U^2D_a.
\end{align*}
Define
\[
 N=[(s+w_*-u-v_*)^2-4(sw_*+uv_*)]^2-64sw_*uv_*.
\]
With $G=B_{\rm sign}-P_{\rm pos}$, the complete equation is
\[
 \boxed{P_{13}=N+G^2+NG^2=0.}
\]
The unit-product identity $(1+N)(1+G^2)=1$ proves $N=G=0$ without any
assumption about the sign of $N$.

\paragraph{Root recovery and complete maps.}
The norm is monic in the rational total center $Z_*/U$, so integer-root
monicity again licenses CRT before root extraction.  Unconditionally,
\[
 D_\tau\equiv D_f\equiv D_a\equiv1\pmod3.
\]
The root weights $2,6,18$ therefore have distinct three-adic orders.
In any nonrational squareclass, the least-order term cannot cancel the
others modulo 3; independence of squareclasses forces all three roots to be
integers.  If $e^2=D_a$, the rational adjacent-Pell root
$e/(Q-1)$ is integral by the ordinary denominator-descent lemma, explicitly
proved in the component proof.  Restoring
\[
 f_{15}=\sqrt{D_f},\quad
 y_{15}=e/(Q-1)-V,\quad
 n_{15}=\sqrt{D_\tau}-L-K_*/H-(v-q^2)/M
\]
gives a literal 15-variable zero.  Conversely every 15-variable zero gives
an integral $e=(Q-1)(V+y)$ and an integral shift of $n$ making a norm factor
zero.  No new canonical-choice or strong-index tuning is required.

\paragraph{Why triple centering matters.}
The root-center defects are
\[
 4D_\tau-A^2=4-k^2,\quad
 36D_f-B^2=36(1-I^2),\quad
 324D_a-C_*^2=81(4-4Q-V^2).
\]
They expose cancellations hidden by the compact direct evaluator.  The
independent generic expansion has 568 terms and exact weighted leading
monomial $128ZU^7A^2BC_*^4$, of degree 242.  Hence the signed norm itself
has degree 242 and the unit-product finalizer has degree 274.  The actual
fixed-coefficient leading witness is
\[
 [j^{124}w^{41}v^{47}h^6z^4r^{17}c^{34}n]P_{13}
 =2^{265}3^{174}\beta^{124}>0.
\]
The 129-gate direct evaluator has 76 multiplications, 31 additions and 22
subtractions.  The longer Horner evaluator is retained solely as an exact
identity/degree comparison.  Full formulas, ordinary maps and independent
checks are under \texttt{lowdegree/thirteen/}.
