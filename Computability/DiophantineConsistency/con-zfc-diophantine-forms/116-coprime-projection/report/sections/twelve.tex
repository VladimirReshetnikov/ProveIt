\section{A four-root companion: twelve integers, degree 500}
\label{sec:four-carrier}
The final member removes $p_1$ as well, leaving \textbf{12 unrestricted
integer variables, exact degree 500 and 148 live operations}.  It improves
both measures over the inspected degree 1000/180-gate twelve-variable
endpoint. The latest degree-$1200$, $145$-operation and degree-$1456$,
$141$-operation twelve-variable circuits remain cheaper, so the new
$500/148$ endpoint is a degree--cost tradeoff against those comparators.
The guard is now a square radicand inside the norm itself, not an
additional inequality or hidden witness.

Keep the 13-variable definitions and set
\[
 G_0=B_{\rm sign}-1-p_2^2-p_3(p_3+1),\qquad
 Z_*=Z+U(A+B+C_*).
\]
Define the four scaled radicands
\[
 a_r=4U^2D_\tau,\quad b_r=36U^2D_f,\quad
 c_r=324U^2D_a,\quad e_r=729U^2G_0,
\]
and the direct four-root evaluator
\begin{align*}
 p&=a_rb_r,&q_*&=c_re_r,&u_*&=a_r+b_r,&v_*&=c_r+e_r,\\
 s_*&=Z_*^2-u_*-v_*,\\
 k_*&=s_*^2-4u_*v_*+4(p+q_*),\\
 \ell_*&=s_*+2v_*,&m_*&=s_*+2u_*,\\
 E_*&=k_*^2+16[p\ell_*^2-q_*(m_*^2+4p)],\\
 O_*&=k_*\ell_*+8q_*m_*.
\end{align*}
The complete equation is
\[
 \boxed{P_{12}=E_*^2-64pO_*^2=0.}
\]
It is exactly the product over all 16 signed sums of the four roots, with
no finalizer needed.

\paragraph{Why the guard can join the roots.}
Monicity of the 16-sign norm first makes the rational total center integral,
so the same coprime CRT recovers the cubic scale.  The first three radicands
remain units modulo 3.  Their root weights $2,6,18$ have respective
three-adic coefficient orders $0,1,2$; the fourth weight $27$ has order
at least 3 after the squarefree decomposition of nonzero $G_0$.
Therefore no nonrational squareclass can cancel.  If $G_0=0$, that root is
simply omitted; a negative $G_0$ is excluded by the same squareclass argument,
not assumed away.  Thus $G_0=g^2$ for an integer $g$ and
\[
 B_{\rm sign}=1+p_2^2+p_3(p_3+1)+g^2>0.
\]
Set $p_{1,15}=g$ and restore the other three integer roots exactly as in
the 13-variable proof.  The adjacent-Pell denominator lemma restores the
auxiliary coordinate.  Conversely every 15-variable zero gives this norm
zero after an integral shift of $n$ incorporating $27p_1$.  Both maps apply
to every zero, without extra canonical choices.

\paragraph{Exact degree.}
The triple-centered root defects are unchanged.  The cleared 16 factors
have degrees $47$ eight times, $17$ four times and $14$ four times,
so their product has exact degree 500.  Its actual leading witness is
\[
 [j^{228}w^{76}v^{84}h^8z^8r^{32}c^{64}]P_{12}
   =2^{488}3^{332}\beta^{228}>0.
\]
The 148 live gates are 90 multiplications, 35 additions and 23 subtractions.
The complete scalar definitions, ordinary proof and independent certificate
are under \texttt{lowdegree/twelve/}.
