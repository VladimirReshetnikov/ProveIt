These rows do not exhaust the Pareto frontier. The repository also has
lower-variable, much higher-degree equations; low-operation formulas with
more variables; and direct quartic compiler constructions. At the initial comparison pin the direct projected quartic has $604$
unrestricted integer variables. A bounded late review through commit
\texttt{56b4d15d128b} finds a completed $601$-integer projected quartic.
The subsequent $73$-predicate source theorem does not yet supply a measured
smaller projected quartic. Such expanded quartic sizes are not comparable
to short circuit counts without
also accounting for expanded support and coefficient height.

The scalar intersective carrier, signed absolute Pell folding,
full-residue interpolation and centered budget are inherited ideas and are
credited as such. The new contribution is their use with deliberately
coprime square products and, separately, with a rational first-root
projection carrying two congruences. We do not claim historical priority
for the elementary square-product lemma or a literature-wide minimum.

The archive's baseline comparison records exact source paths and hashes.
All original source files and older candidate polynomials remain unchanged.
Original-label source34 and compact44 coefficients are kept in separate
coherent sets. In particular no original-label initialization coefficient
is identified with the repaired BFS-renamed initialization coefficient.

A late transport expansion in upstream commit \texttt{49379c31d969}
reduces the two ten-positive comparator circuits to $103/340$ and $98/368$
(operations/degree). Applying that exact identity to the new fused
polynomial gives $96/266$, without changing its coefficients, degree or
zero set. This late simplification is credited to the upstream construction.
The initial comparison table remains a record of the frozen starting pin;
the final positive comparison uses the late figures.
