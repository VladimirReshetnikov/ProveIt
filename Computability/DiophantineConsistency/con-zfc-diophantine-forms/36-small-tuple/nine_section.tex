\section{Combining the 31-variable quartic with the faster Jones evaluator}

\subsection{The parent quartic is explicit}

Package 30 eliminates one packing register from the earlier 32-variable
quartic. Its 31 positive coordinates are
\[
\begin{gathered}
 J,F,\alpha,z,f,h,i,o,j,s,w,\tau,\eta,\zeta,y,\gamma,\\
 N,E,P,c,a,X,D,C_2,L,F_2,V,g,I,B_a,\Delta.
\end{gathered}
\]
With affine abbreviations $q=\beta J+1$, $k=\eta+\zeta$,
$H=4a+3$, and $C=q-F-\alpha$, the 22 equations are
\begin{align*}
 L&=q^2-1,&N&=q(L+1),&X&=wq,\\
 a&=E+sN,&E&=X(a-E),&c&=k(a-E)+\eta,\\
 P&=E(c-\eta)+k,&\tau^2&=P(P-k)+1,\\
 D&=X+ac+\gamma H,&\Delta&=a^2+H,&C_2&=c^2,\\
 D^2&=\Delta C_2+1,&k&=jc-V+1+hE.
\end{align*}
\begin{gather*}
 jc-V=(q-F)(N-q)-CL+(M_C+qM_F)J,\\
 (K+w)C+(q-F)-z(q-1)=1.
\end{gather*}
\begin{align*}
 F_2&=f^2,&V&=of-c,&g&=y^2-V^2,\\
 I&=iC_2,&B_a&=I^2,&B_a&=\Delta(F_2-1),\\
 y^2-B_ag&=1.
\end{align*}
Let $Q_{31}$ be the sum of the squares of their left-minus-right residuals.
Every residual is quadratic, so $Q_{31}$ is quartic. The coefficient of
$\tau^4$ is 1, proving exact degree four for every valid fixed tuple.
At the positive all-one tuple the residual $E-X(a-E)$ is 1; hence shifting
every positive coordinate by $+1$ gives a quartic over $\N^{31}$ with
strictly positive constant term, as required by the Jones normalization.
The complete sparse polynomial is retained in
\path{nine/source/quartic31.json}.

The one-register elimination is an exact positive zero-set bijection. The
omitted register was $G=q(q-F)-C$, and the identity
\[
 L[q(q-F)-C]-[(q-F)(N-q)-CL]=(q-F)[q(L+1)-N]
\]
restores the packing equation. Transport forces $C>0$, and then
$G=(q-1)C+q\alpha>0$. We did not find a justified second elimination that
preserves quadratic residuals. It would be incorrect to report a
30-variable quartic merely by dropping a positivity obligation or raising
the residual degree.

\subsection{All Jones index constants are specified}

Introduce one unused input coordinate, fixed to 1, and let $p_\alpha$ be
the coefficients of the shifted quartic, with $\alpha\in\N^{32}$ and
$|\alpha|\le4$. Write
\[
 c_\alpha=(4-|\alpha|)!\left(\prod_{j=0}^{31}\alpha_j!\right)p_\alpha,
 \qquad e(\alpha)=\sum_{j=0}^{31}\alpha_j5^j,
 \qquad L_J=5^{32}.
\]
There are 402 nonzero coefficient records; each is a specified polynomial
in the four fixed compiler ports. Every omitted record is explicitly zero.

Let the port bit lengths, in the order $\beta,K,M_C,M_F$, be
\[
 (b_1,b_2,b_3,b_4)=(1012115,1016946,1012115,1012116).
\]
For a record $c_\alpha=\sum_t a_t\prod_r\mathrm{port}_r^{e_{tr}}$,
define the exact integer upper bound
\[
 H_\alpha=\sum_t|a_t|2^{\sum_r b_re_{tr}}.
\]
All records satisfy $|c_\alpha|\le H_\alpha$, and exact finite arithmetic
checks $H_\alpha+1<2^{4,058,127}$. Therefore set
\begin{equation}\boxed{z_J=2^{4,058,127}.}\label{eq:jones-z}
\end{equation}
The other essential fixed index is
\begin{equation}
 y_J=\sum_{\substack{\alpha\in\N^{32}\\|\alpha|\le4}}
        (z_J+c_\alpha)(2z_J)^{L_J-e(\alpha)}.
 \label{eq:jones-y}
\end{equation}
This is a bounded sum of exactly $\binom{36}{4}=58,905$ terms. Its digits
are between 1 and $2z_J-1$, and their positions are distinct. Both $y_J$
and $z_J$ are fixed integers, not unknowns, oracles, or unspecified
enumeration indices. A conservative binary-length bound for $y_J$ is
$4,058,128(5^{32}+1)$. Its enormous size is a genuine cost of the
nine-variable presentation and is not hidden by the short recipe.

The actual $z_J$ is supplied in compressed binary form: 507,266 raw bytes,
527 gzip bytes. The previous instance used $z_J=2^{45,933,757}$.
The new bound is recomputed from the new tuple. Carrying over the old
coefficient records numerically while changing only the four compiler ports
would not be a coherent instance.

\subsection{The evaluation identity}

The expensive fixed digit polynomial in a variable base $\mathcal B$ is
\[
 e_J(\mathcal B)=\sum_{|\alpha|\le4}
                 (z_J+c_\alpha)\mathcal B^{L_J-e(\alpha)}.
\]
Set $T_J=5^{31}$ and introduce computed registers
\[
 t_j=\mathcal B^{5^j}\quad(0\le j\le32),\qquad
 v_j=\mathcal B^{T_J-5^j}\quad(0\le j\le31).
\]
These are fixed-exponent polynomials; none is an extra witness. They are
computed through $t_{j+1}=t_j^4t_j$, $v_{31}=1$, and
$v_j=t_j^4v_{j+1}$. The elementary exponent identity
\[
 \mathcal B^{L_J-e(\alpha)}
 =t_{31}\,t_{31}^{4-|\alpha|}\prod_jv_j^{\alpha_j}
\]
has no negative exponent or division. If $h_4$ denotes the complete
homogeneous polynomial of degree four, it gives
\begin{equation}
 e_J(\mathcal B)=t_{31}\left[
 z_Jh_4(t_{31},v_0,\ldots,v_{31})+
 \sum_\alpha c_\alpha t_{31}^{4-|\alpha|}\prod_jv_j^{\alpha_j}
 \right].\label{eq:homogeneous}
\end{equation}

The complete homogeneous term includes every zero coefficient position
automatically. It is evaluated by the usual ascending-order updates
$h_r\gets h_r+wh_{r-1}$ for $r=1,\ldots,4$, initialized with $h_0=1$
and the other registers zero. The sparse correction is evaluated by
multivariate Horner factorization. Literal records become literals, and
identical coefficient recipes share a fixed leaf. Both transformations are
exact identities in the compiler ports. In total, the new DAG has only
80 live fixed leaves: $z_J$, $y_J$, and 78 distinct nonliteral recipes.

The geometric register also factors without division. With
$q_J=\mathcal B^{L_J}$,
\[
 \sum_{r=0}^{4L_J-1}\mathcal B^r
 =\left[\prod_{j=0}^{31}(1+t_j+t_j^2+t_j^3+t_j^4)\right]
     (1+q_J)(1+q_J^2),
\]
and each five-term factor equals $1+t_j(1+t_j)(1+t_j^2)$.

All remaining Jones expressions and the source's tight two-root finalizer
are retained verbatim as polynomial data. The complete executable
definition, with exactly nine supplied inputs, is
\path{nine/compact/nine31_natural.json}. The affine positive-coordinate
version is \path{nine/compact/nine31_positive.json}.
The finalizer has the form
\[
 [\Theta^2-\Omega^2(a_0+V_0^2a_1)]^2
       -4\Omega^4V_0^2a_0a_1.
\]
Its input expressions are all defined in those literal DAGs; the source
proof bounds both radicands before imposing square conditions and treats
negative or coincident radical cases. We preserve this finalizer rather
than replacing its proof with generic claims about independent radicals.

\subsection{Metrics, proof, and remaining cost}

Exact expansion verifies the sparse correction in
\eqref{eq:homogeneous} as 675 matching symbolic terms over
$\Z[\mathcal B,\beta,K,M_C,M_F]$. The recurrence and exponent identity
prove its remaining baseline. This gives the exact original polynomial at
the old binding, in 1587 operations instead of 1840: a 253-operation, or
13.75\%, saving. The gate breakdown is 857 multiplications and 730
additions/subtractions. The source's leading-degree certificate is uniform
in the index, with diagonal leading coefficient $2^{274}z_J^2>0$.
Thus coherent rebinding preserves the exact degree as well.

Replacing each nonnegative supplied coordinate by a positive coordinate
minus 1 cancels several internal shifts. The resulting evaluator costs
1582 operations and has the same degree and zero-existence condition. The
transformation is an explicit affine bijection, not an informal domain
change.

Jones's nine-variable reduction \cite{jones,repojones}, the normalized
quartic, and the strict index bounds give the semantic theorem. The complete
coefficient recipes and proof are in \path{nine/NINE_PROOF.md} and
\path{nine/compact/CONSTANTS.json}. Although every coefficient is effectively
specified, $y_J$ is not materialized. The nine-variable form is consequently
the coordinate-minimizing choice among the recommendations, not the
smallest actual numerical object or the easiest equation for human review.
