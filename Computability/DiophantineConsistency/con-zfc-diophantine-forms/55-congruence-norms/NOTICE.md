# Attribution and scope

This is an AI-assisted research contribution prepared for incorporation into
Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository at the user's request. The report,
new arithmetic reductions, circuit adapters, and independent finite checkers
are supplied with this archive. The top-level license follows ProveIt's
MIT No Attribution license. All third-party licenses and notices retained in
component directories continue to apply to their respective material.

The fixed K215 transition table and its source author attribution, native
Wang-language compiler, package-31 coefficient adapter and source system,
Jones ratio interfaces, sparse carry machinery, higher polynomial majorant,
quintic merger, and quotient absorber have inherited origins. Their precise
source pins and notices are retained. The modified Lucas bridge and full
residue interpolation used by the ten-integer construction are due to
Zhi-Wei Sun and are cited as mathematical dependencies.

The new contributions claimed here are the combined coprime guard, the
congruence-preserving two-root reductions, the associated removal of redundant
nonvanishing/padding factors, positive-word source fusion with its repaired
support code, the resulting exact-degree reductions, and the fourteen-variable
combination using a stronger canonical auxiliary congruence. The individual
Pell, norm, and finite-field facts are classical. No exhaustive priority search
or global minimality theorem is claimed.

The independent reviews were conducted by separate AI-assisted work streams
within this session. They are not external human peer review. The new literal
equations are not accompanied by a newly rebuilt end-to-end kernel certificate.
The inherited K215 Lean source closure is included separately; its availability
does not certify the new coefficient and polynomial transformations.
