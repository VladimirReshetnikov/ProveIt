# Provenance

This component was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt research task.
New text, majorant tables, exact-algebra checkers, and construction scripts are
AI-assisted mathematical research, supplied under the included MIT No
Attribution license for repository incorporation.

Repository pin: cf4a5218a5a4e4358d3f5314151b6b05d4aa384e.

Inherited files in `upstream/` retain their exact source directory names and
their notices. The source/pell DAG and degree ledger are from package71;
source kernels and the proof are from package65; free-carrier soundness is
from package59; all four coherent native coefficient payloads and their finite
generator are from package31. The arithmetic helper `circuit_helper.py` is
copied from package68's `norm/final10/circuit.py`, whose short header is retained.

The new contribution is the full-radical polynomial part and its complete
positive-cone proof, followed by its explicit nine-positive-variable
application. It uses the established native compiler and K215 consistency
calibration. The imported results are not relabeled as new discoveries or new
Lean/Rocq certificates.
