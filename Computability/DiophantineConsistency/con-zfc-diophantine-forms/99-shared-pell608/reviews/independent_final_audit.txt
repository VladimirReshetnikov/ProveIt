INDEPENDENT FINITE AUDIT OF THE FINAL 608-NATURAL / 1685-INTEGER PAIR

Audited directory: research/optimized/final.

The affine checker does not replay the constructor's gate-selection heuristic.
It identifies the proposed retained variables by their raw metadata, checks
every raw defining equation independently, and reconstructs the omitted
affine wires needed by every semantic constraint and retained definition.
Exact sparse substitution reproduces all 700 rows of before_terminal.json.
All 1,182 raw gate definitions are monic, triangular, and coefficientwise
nonnegative after solving for their output. There are 724 needed omitted
affine gates, each with at most four terms in its fully substituted affine
recipe. No free input or otherwise dead gate was omitted. Thus the raw
1,541-variable / 1,424-row system and its 817-variable / 700-row affine
export have bijective natural zero sets, via unique polynomial extension.

The independent terminal checker derives and checks all 209 reconstruction
recipes directly from the then-current defining rows. It scans every other
row for nonlinear occurrences, checks 218 consumer substitutions, and
reproduces the 608-variable / 491-row residual system exactly. Independent
ordered convolution of those residuals produces exactly 7,582 nonzero
monomials, maximum absolute coefficient 79,824,571, and total degree four.
No unused variables were dropped. This stage also gives a bijection of
natural zero sets by the terminal-elimination lemma.

INTEGER DOMAIN CONVERSION

There are exactly 359 original raw variables without a defining gate:
five outer variables and 354 additional arithmetic witnesses. Every one
is still present among the final 608 natural variables. The other 249
final variables are retained gates. For each of the 359 free inputs v,
the integer export adds three fresh integer variables a_v,b_v,c_v and
the quadratic residual

             8v + 3 - a_v^2 - b_v^2 - c_v^2.

The final integer equation is the sum of squares of the original 491
residuals and these 359 additional residuals. It therefore has 1,685
unrestricted integer variables and 850 residuals. Independent expansion
gives exactly 12,082 nonzero monomials and maximum absolute coefficient
79,827,802. Its total degree is exactly four. In particular each fresh
square witness occurs with coefficient one in its pure fourth-power term.

Completeness of the conversion follows from Legendre's three-square
theorem: if v>=0, then 8v+3 is positive and is not of the excluded form
4^a(8b+7), so it is a sum of three integer squares. The new witnesses
for different inputs are disjoint, hence all 359 guards can be satisfied
simultaneously without modifying the natural solution.

For soundness, an integer zero makes every residual zero. Each guard
implies 8v+3>=0. Since v is an integer, this forces v>=0. Thus all 359
original free inputs are nonnegative. At this point the retained gate
variables have not yet been assumed nonnegative; that would be an invalid
shortcut. Instead, lift the integer solution through the terminal and
affine eliminations. Both lifting maps are polynomial identities over Z,
so the result satisfies every original raw equation over Z. Process the
raw gate definitions in increasing output-variable order. Each output
is a polynomial with nonnegative integer coefficients in earlier values.
The free values are nonnegative, and induction therefore makes every raw
gate value nonnegative as well. This proves that the lifted assignment is
a natural-valued raw solution. Projecting it gives the required natural
608-variable solution. Hence the natural and integer equations have
equivalent solvability, with no positivity assumption on unguarded gate
variables used before the induction establishes it.

VERIFICATION SCOPE

All three independent programs ran successfully with ordinary Python and
with python -O. None imports the constructor, exporter, compressor, or
arithmetic-macro implementation. The integer checker imports only the two
other independently authored checkers and rechecks the full finite chain.
Eight deliberate corruptions were rejected: an affine export coefficient,
a nonunit raw gate coefficient, a terminal reconstruction coefficient,
a final natural coefficient, a nonlinear terminal consumer, an omitted
free-input guard, an incorrect guard constant, and an incorrect final
integer coefficient.

The programs prove finite polynomial identities, reconstruction hypotheses,
input-guard coverage, and exact polynomial exports. The ordinary proof
of the arithmetic macros and the source's syntactic ZFC-refutation
calibration remain separate mathematical dependencies. These checks are
not a newly generated Lean/Rocq proof or an encoded object-ZFC derivation.
