\begin{abstract}
This report gives new explicit polynomial presentations of syntactic
ZFC refutation, and hence consistency via universal nonvanishing, with the
logical calibration explicitly inherited. Spherical low-digit bounds and an
odd-square radix produce quartics in 258 unrestricted integers, with sparse
companions in 263, 285 and 286 integers, all of coefficient height 128446.
Weighted natural digit pools yield quartics in 221--224 nonnegative integers,
with dimension, support and height tradeoffs disclosed. Certified coefficient
normalization, row-sign balancing and direct pairing of opposite monomial
occurrences give height-one quartics in 571, 593, 646 and 742 unrestricted
integers. Finally, fixed even radical weights and integral polynomial centers
give eleven-integer equations at exact degree and binary-operation pairs
316/133, 308/140, 304/144 and 300/148. Complete new witness arguments,
literal equations, authentic coefficient payloads and independent exact
checks are supplied. The results are compared with six authenticated incoming
packages at a fixed ProveIt snapshot. No proof of consistency, global
minimality, or new end-to-end formal-kernel calibration is claimed.
\end{abstract}
