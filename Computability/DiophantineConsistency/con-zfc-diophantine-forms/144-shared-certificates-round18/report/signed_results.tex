Every coordinate ranges over $\Z$. All four selected endpoints use the
odd-square radix. The first three merge the $t$ and $t+1$ spheres using
two explicit product wires. The last keeps four spheres and thereby trades
one additional variable for one fewer row and six fewer monomials.
The 258-, 263-, and 285-variable points each improve variables, rows and
support over their respective Round~17 282-, 287-, and 309-variable parents
at unchanged coefficient height. These improvements do not require a
new refutation calibration.
