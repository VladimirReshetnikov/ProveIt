\section{Results and interpretation}
\label{sec:results}
The new reductions use spare capacity already present in explicit arithmetic
certificates. The signed construction pools low-digit bounds into spheres;
the natural construction pools adjacent digit levels and can absorb the
exact output bound. The height-one construction connects opposite monomial
occurrences directly between rows and certifies fixed row-sign choices.
The scalar construction changes a free integer coordinate by an integral
polynomial shear. These are structural zero-set arguments, not assertions
that a heuristic search has found a global optimum.

All numbers below refer to complete emitted artifacts on their stated
domains. The direct and height-one polynomials have exact degree four.
The scalar degree and live binary graph cost are separate measures.
