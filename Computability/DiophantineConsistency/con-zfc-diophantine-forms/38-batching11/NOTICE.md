# Attribution and scope

This package was prepared for incorporation in Vladimir Reshetnikov's
ProveIt report family, using revision
22e75dd6b87b1b199bb08c778d7cf075ae5aae08 as the comparison and source pin.

The new contributions are direct simultaneous quadratic coefficient
packing with an explicit finite Sidon certificate; its Jones and Sun
applications; the eleven-positive index-absorption argument and optional
positive carrier translation; and the complemented whole-label native
compiler with its recomputed full coefficient tuple/rank controller.

The quadratic parent, the Jones ratio and finalizer, the direct Pell
norm architecture, Sun's Lucas interfaces, the native-window compiler,
the K215 machine and its logical calibration retain their original
attribution. The relevant upstream licenses and notices are preserved
in `compiler/licenses`, `signed10/inherited`, and `eleven/source_proofs`.
Root manuscript and new code are supplied under MIT-0, consistent with
the report family. This does not replace any inherited attribution
requirements.

Development and local independent reviews are AI-assisted. The package
does not assert independent human peer review, an end-to-end new Lean
certificate, a consistency proof, or global optimality. The manuscript
identifies every inherited mathematical dependency used by the new forms.
