## Credited predecessor work

The direct branch inherits the numerical C222 calculus and refutation
calibration, the arithmetic and CRT compiler, the signed Pell frame and
semantic anchor, both-coordinate orientation, the strict amplification
budget, common normalized remainders, odd-square radix construction,
Horn factorization, monic projection, coordinate shears, and affine residual
consequences. The complete new nearest-factorial proof identifies where
these results are used and where the earlier front-sign schedule is replaced.

The signed and compact branches retain their respective native/compiler/
refutation ancestries and exact fixed coefficient tuples. The compact
79-operation endpoint computes the same polynomial as its 80-operation
parent by sharing an existing multiplication; neither multiplication
associativity nor the sharing technique is claimed as a new invention.

Immediate sources include the relevant incoming packages at ProveIt commit
`3ecd6a6cf53d47519228328567f45fb7ee9781d9`. Exact source identities, archive
hashes, copied notices, and coefficient bindings are preserved within the
components. The six coordinate shears and four affine consequences are
credited to the retained residual-basis lineage; their new composition is
separately certified.

## Verification and research claims

The ordinary mathematical arguments are formalizable in ZFC and compose
with the explicitly identified predecessor calibration. The exact finite
checkers authenticate the equations, coefficients, transformations, and
metrics. Separately tasked AI-assisted reviews examine the mathematical
arguments and the internalization boundary.

No claim is made of outside human peer review, institutional endorsement,
literature-wide priority, a global simplicity minimum, or a proof of
Con(ZFC). The package supplies no newly compiled end-to-end Lean/Rocq proof
or emitted object-ZFC proof term for the complete new endpoint. Retained
formal-interface sources are not a claim that their full transitive closure
was rebuilt in this work.
