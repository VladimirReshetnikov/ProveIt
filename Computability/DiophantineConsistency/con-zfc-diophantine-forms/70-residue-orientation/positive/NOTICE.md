# Attribution and scope

The common arithmetic prefix, normalized source interface, positive auxiliary
reconstruction and sparse-verification approach derive from ProveIt package
61 (`61-congruence-carriers`) at commit
`9cca523d9f27ad39d52993f4065cbd7e58f8bdd4`.

The adaptive main modulus derives from package 65 (`65-shared-taps`), at
the same pin. The four fixed coefficient payloads, exact numeric manifest
and native source proof derive from package 44 (`44-b4-sparse`). Their
machine lineage, upstream authorship and licenses are preserved in `licenses/`
and the unchanged source records. These dependencies are not claimed as new.

The new twelve-variable reduction transfers the strong Pell root into the
transport gap, proves a strict radicand separation, handles both recovered
root signs, and strengthens the existing transport multiplier to prove
completeness. Its concrete equation, degree/cost certificates and new proof
are supplied here. The thirteen-variable equation is an explicitly proved
composition of the cited transport and adaptive carriers.
