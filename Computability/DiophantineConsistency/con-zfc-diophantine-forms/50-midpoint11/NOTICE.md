# Attribution, provenance, and scope

This is a derived research contribution prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's
ProveIt project on 23 October 2026, against commit
`124c07c0b77a9e20e84676514d8b1ff78536ea3f`.

New material comprises the support-dependent isolation theorem and weight
assignment, its specialized circuits, the refined auxiliary-root estimate,
the small-modulus midpoint and ordinary-strong quotient absorbers, their
proofs, and the associated finite verification and integration files.
New material is supplied under the root MIT No Attribution (MIT-0) license.

The four-port compiler tuple and its finite recipe, source compiler and
Pell interfaces, and K215 syntactic-refutation calibration are inherited.
The nine-variable finalizer follows the repository's Jones construction;
the unrestricted ten-variable finalizer follows Sun's theorem as adapted
by the repository. These results are not claimed as new here. The source
ledgers identify the copied and adapted files and their exact revisions.

Copies under `provenance/`, `coefficients/upstream/`, and the packing
subtree retain all applicable source notices. MIT, MIT-0, and source-specific
notices remain effective for their respective inherited material. In
particular, the K215 attribution and Wade license are preserved. The
original journal and preprint articles are cited rather than bundled.

The mathematical and code reviews were conducted by independent AI-assisted
review tasks during this work. They are not external human peer review.
Finite verification receipts certify their specified finite properties;
they are neither an end-to-end proof-assistant certificate of the compiler
nor a proof of the consistency of ZFC.
