The native four-port coefficients and positive source interface are derived
from VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt at the pinned source and archived inputs
identified in ../native/COEFFICIENT_BINDING.json and ../review/SOURCE_PINS.json.
Source-support and sparse carry ideas are adapted from incoming research
artifacts at that pin. Original licenses and notices are retained in ../native/licenses.

The general ten-unrestricted-integer representation is due to Zhi-Wei Sun,
"Ten unknowns for Hilbert's tenth problem over the integers", arXiv:2609.01594v2,
with source coding and arithmetic dependencies from "Further results on
Hilbert's Tenth Problem", arXiv:1704.03504v7. No general unknown-count
improvement is claimed here. The new equal-weight conditional separator,
source adaptation, literal circuits, and independent verification are local
research contributions; no upstream script was executed.
