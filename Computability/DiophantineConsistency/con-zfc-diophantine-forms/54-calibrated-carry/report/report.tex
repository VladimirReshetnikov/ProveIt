\begin{abstract}
We combine independently useful advances from all eight incoming wave-7
archives, then improve their composition. A positivity-recovery bijection
shifts only twelve of the thirty-one quadratic coordinates. A signed-borrow
source uses sparse one-bit padding, and its exact binary mask population
sets the central-binomial threshold. Calibrating that threshold separately
for the Jones and Sun interfaces avoids an unnecessary degree penalty.
A new quotient-cancelled radical majorant gives nine positive unknowns at
\NineOps{} binary operations and exact degree \NineDegree{}, improving
both coordinates of the best-degree reviewed incoming equation. A
conditional three-root separation argument gives ten unrestricted integers
at 554 operations and degree $14\,699\,152$. A constant-cancelled norm
minimum gives fifteen positive variables at 72 operations and degree 64,
with several explicit tradeoffs. All equations bind the complete inherited
215492-byte native tuple, not a mixture of coefficient instances.
The new proofs compose with identified inherited compiler, K215, Jones,
and Sun theorems to give ZFC-provable unsolvability equivalences to the
syntactic $\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$ predicate. The archive contains literal
arithmetic circuits, exact fixed payloads, source pins, mathematical proofs,
and independently authored finite verifiers. These are not claims of global
minimality, fewer unknowns, a consistency decision, or a new end-to-end
proof-assistant certificate of these particular equations.
\end{abstract}

\section{Results and the precise comparison}
Throughout $\N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, $\Pos=\{1,2,\ldots\}$ and $\Z$ denotes
all integers. The domain is part of an equation's specification.

\begin{theorem}[Fixed consistency equations]
Instantiate every fixed port and derived coefficient by the payloads and
recipes in the accompanying release. For every retained circuit of its
stated family, the mathematical arguments below and the identified inherited
theorems establish in ZFC
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \neg\exists\mathbf x\in\Pos^9\ F_9(\mathbf x)=0
   &\ \longleftrightarrow\ \Con(\mathrm{ZFC}),\\
 \neg\exists\mathbf x\in\Z^{10}\ F_{10}(\mathbf x)=0
   &\ \longleftrightarrow\ \Con(\mathrm{ZFC}),\\
 \neg\exists\mathbf x\in\Pos^{15}\ P_{15}(\mathbf x)=0
   &\ \longleftrightarrow\ \Con(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\end{aligned}
\]
The nine-nonnegative circuits give the same assertion over $\N^9$ by an
explicit coordinate translation. No other domain conversion is implicit.
\end{theorem}
The consistency predicate means the absence of a finite syntactic ZFC
refutation under the inherited K215 calibration. Proving this equivalence
is not proving either side. In particular we infer no unconditional
independence theorem merely from consistency.

\subsection{What is new and what is inherited}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Selective positivity recovery.} Twelve guarded coordinates
suffice. This is a proved zero-set bijection and reduces the encoding to
109 weighted records on 62 active monomials, without deleting unknowns.
\item \textbf{A calibrated source composition.} Sparse $+1$ target padding
and the actual-popcount threshold come from the Selective Quadratic
arrival. We compose them with a stronger selective source, interior padding,
and distinct natural/integer threshold interfaces, proving all pre-decoding
bounds and the signed-borrow cases.
\item \textbf{A lower-defect majorant.} Exact quotient cancellations and an
additional finite radical estimate lower the polynomial error of the
inherited $T_6$ majorant. The degree certificate applies to the actual
primary norm polynomial, not a polynomial multiplied by a denominator power.
\item \textbf{Equal integer weights.} The incoming $(1,b,bK)$ separator can
be shortened to $(1,b,b)$: after recovering the first square, the actual
margin forces the remaining radicands to be distinct.
\item \textbf{Short positive equations.} The squared-modulus quotient and
norm-minimum ideas combine; the extra cancellation
$(B-1)^2-1=B(B-2)$ then gives the new short-circuit frontier.
\end{enumerate}
The complete native tuple with base $2^{429959}$ is inherited from the
selective arrival. Its 215492-byte size is not a new record. What is new
here is rebinding all ports and every derived leaf of these compositions
and independently checking that binding. Search results are not optimality
certificates, and a shorter arithmetic circuit is not necessarily a shorter
binary description or expanded polynomial.

\section{Further research directions}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Optimize actual gate count and exact final degree jointly with the
source support, row signs, padding position and target shifts. A smaller
maximum weight can lose to a layout permitting shared homogeneous evaluation.
The nine small isolation certificates supply a stable proof-output format.
\item Seek a still lower polynomial defect in $T^2-qDFG^2$, rather than a
numerically tighter square-root estimate alone. Any improvement must prove
strict positivity before squarehood or divisibility is known and must
certify the primary equation after leading cancellations.
\item Extend the twelve-coordinate positivity recovery by proving a further
unguarded coordinate positive from the exact source equations. An apparent
support saving without a two-way domain proof is not an improvement.
\item The squared-modulus quotient removes a square-root occurrence needed
by the previous eleven-/twelve-variable radical norm. Find a replacement
coupling rather than silently deleting that strong-square condition. The
included explicit Pell-fiber counterexample rules out the naive inference.
\item Connect the closed, smaller simultaneous-quadratic adapter to the
current executable compiler and then to an object-level ZFC derivation.
The later pinned project progress is relevant infrastructure, not a kernel
certificate of the literal polynomials delivered here.
\item Prove finite-catalogue or restricted-family lower bounds for circuit
cost, target placement, or unknown count. No present heuristic search gives
a global simplicity theorem.
\end{enumerate}
These are specific open directions, not conditions needed to specify or
replay the present equations.
