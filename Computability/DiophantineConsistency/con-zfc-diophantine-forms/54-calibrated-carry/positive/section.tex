\section{A constant-cancelled positive norm minimum}
The short-equation frontier gains a fifteen-positive-variable polynomial
of degree $64$ evaluated in $72$ binary arithmetic operations, and a
$70$-operation degree-$80$ version. The inherited $66$-operation,
degree-$157$ formula remains the operation-count comparison. All four
ports are rebound together to the verified native tuple with base
$2^{429959}$; the high mask is already shifted.

For the $72/64$ version the positive inputs are
$J,F,\alpha,z,f,h,i,T,s,w,r,\eta,\zeta,u,\gamma$. Define
\begin{align*}
 q&=\beta J+1,&X&=wq,&Y&=sq^3,&E&=XY,\\
 k&=\eta+\zeta,&c&=kY+\eta,&a&=E+Y,&H&=4a+3.
\end{align*}
\begin{align*}
 \Delta&=a^2+H,&D&=X+ac+\gamma H,&\ell&=EkY,\\
 V&=f^2(cT-R)-c,&I&=ic^2,&A_0&=\Delta(f^2-1).
\end{align*}
\[
 R=((q-1)(q-F)+\alpha)(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J.
\]
\begin{align*}
 N_0&=r^2+\ell(2r-k),&N_1&=D^2-\Delta c^2,\\
 N_a&=(V+u)^2-A_0u(2V+u),&N_k&=k-hE-R.
\end{align*}
\[
 N_t=(K_0+w)(q-F-\alpha)+(q-F)-z(q-1).
\]
Put $A=N_0N_1$, $B=N_aN_kN_t$, and $C=I^2-A_0$. The equation is
\[
                    A^2+B(B-2)+C^2=0.                 \tag{S64}
\]
All displayed auxiliary letters denote polynomial abbreviations, not new
witnesses. The literal division-free DAG uses $40$ multiplications,
$18$ additions and $14$ subtractions, including all literal operations.

\paragraph{Soundness.}
The first and main norms cannot vanish on the positive domain: their
discriminants $XY^2(XY^2+1)$ and $(a+2)^2-1$ are nonsquares. Hence
$A^2\geq1$. Completing the square in (S64) gives
$A^2+(B-1)^2+C^2=1$, so $A^2=1$, $B=1$, $C=0$.
The consecutive-product negative-Pell obstruction and the modulo-four
main-norm obstruction give $N_0=N_1=1$. Now $A_0=I^2>1$ and the
auxiliary norm has square residue modulo four, giving $N_a=1$.
Restore $\tau=\ell+r>0$ and $y=|V+u|>0$; the latter cannot vanish
since its norm is one. Set $T_{\rm old}=fT$. The resulting tuple solves
the inherited ordinary product equation, with $N_kN_t=1$. Its signed
strong-rank theorem recovers both unit signs, including the weak
transport boundary, and extracts the blank K215 computation. No division
of a supplied multiplier by $\Delta$ is made.

\paragraph{Positive completeness.}
For a canonical normalized compiler witness,
$c=\psi_{a+2}(R)$ and $f^2=1+\Delta j^2c^4$. Choose $i=\Delta j$.
The verified native low-mask phase gives $R\equiv3\pmod4$.
For the odd-index Pell polynomials,
\[
 \chi_t(2v+1)=tQ_v(t^2),\quad Q_v(0)=(-1)^v(2v+1),\quad
 Q_v(1-(a+2)^2)=(-1)^v\psi_{a+2}(2v+1).
\]
With $S=ic^2$, choose $V=\chi_S(R)/S$ and $y=\psi_S(R)$.
Since $S^2=\Delta(f^2-1)$, these identities give
$V\equiv-c\pmod{f^2}$ and $V\equiv-R\pmod c$. Also
$f^2\equiv1\pmod c$, so
\[
                 T=\frac{V+c+Rf^2}{cf^2}
\]
is a positive integer. Moreover $V\geq4S^2-3>1$ and the auxiliary
norm implies $y>V$, giving positive $u=y-V$; the first norm similarly
gives positive $r=\tau-\ell$. Thus $A=B=1$, $C=0$.
This reconstructs auxiliary coordinates; it does not assert divisibility
of every old quotient by $f$.

\paragraph{Exact degree.}
Let $q_0=\beta J$, $k_0=\eta+\zeta$,
$E_0=wsq_0^4$, $c_0=k_0sq_0^3$, and
$T_0=w(q_0-F-\alpha)-zq_0$.
The degrees of $A,B,C$ are $30,32,22$, respectively.
The unique degree-$64$ term is $B^2$, with leader
\[
               4h^2E_0^6c_0^2T^2f^8u^2T_0^2.
\]
The coefficient of
$J^{32}h^2\eta^2w^6s^8T^2f^8u^2z^2$ is $4\beta^{32}\ne0$.
Without either gap the same finalizer has $70$ gates and degree $80$.
With both gaps but old $V=c(Tf-1)-Rf^2$, it has $73$ gates and degree
$62$. The target cancellation $(B-1)^2-1=B(B-2)$ saves one operation.

corroborate the unbounded proof; inherited compiler and rank theorems
