# Attribution and proof boundary

The divisibility carrier, its earlier bound, and the degree-629 local reduction
were developed in this iteration with AI assistance. No independent human
review or proof-assistant kernel verification is asserted.

`build_p12.py` adapts the common norm evaluator and circuit representation from
package 52, whose exact builder and MIT-0 license are preserved in `upstream/`.
`check_p12.py` independently multiplies formal radical factors.
The established lean norm, rational Pell saturation, signed compiler, native
coefficient construction, and K215/refutation calibration remain inherited
mathematical dependencies. All four coefficient ports are bound together by
`../coefficients/COEFFICIENT_BINDING.json`; MF is the already-shifted port.

`SOURCE_PINS.json` records exact repository paths, commit, Git blob identifiers,
and SHA-256 hashes. The upstream files are inert references, not new proofs.
The checker reads the old JSON polynomial but never executes upstream code.
