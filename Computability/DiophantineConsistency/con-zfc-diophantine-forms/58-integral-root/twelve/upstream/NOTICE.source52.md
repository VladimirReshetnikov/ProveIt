# Provenance and scope

The new carrier, smaller Y-modulus absorption, proofs, straight-line circuit
builder and independent checker were authored for this research iteration.
The existing normalized and ordinary signed compiler theorems, Pell-saturation
lemma, inner-separator-free norm identity and calibration remain inherited.

The texts in upstream are copied as inert references from VladimirReshetnikov/
ProveIt at commit 124c07c0b77a9e20e84676514d8b1ff78536ea3f. Their immutable blob
identifiers and local hashes are recorded in SOURCE_PINS.json. The source
package35 LICENSE and NOTICE are preserved there. No upstream executable
was imported or run. This release does not claim to have originated the
inherited compiler, representation theorems, or earlier eleven-variable bound.
