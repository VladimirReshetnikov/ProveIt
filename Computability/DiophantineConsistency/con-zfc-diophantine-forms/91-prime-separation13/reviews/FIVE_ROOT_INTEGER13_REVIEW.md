# Independent review: a fifth norm removes a positivity witness

## Conclusion and scope

The proposed extension from the normalized fourteen-integer construction
to a single polynomial in thirteen unrestricted integers passes this
mathematical review. Its key requirement is to make c odd syntactically
before deleting the guard. That change proves Q>1 without using any guard
equation, so the root-recovery argument is noncircular.

The review covers the polynomial construction, signed soundness, canonical
completeness, and all relevant denominator/nonvanishing issues. It does
not independently certify the implementation, operation count, exact total
degree, or inherited machine/compiler/refutation calibration.

## 1. Precise proposed construction

Start with the raw four-root norm N14 from the preceding construction,
before its product finalizer. Substitute

    c=2c1+1

throughout. Let det, eta, k and pair_root retain their preceding polynomial
definitions. Delete p1 from the coordinate list and put

    Bguard = det*eta*(k-eta)
             -(pair_root^2-det-eta*(k-eta)+1)^2
             -1-p2^2-p3*(p3+1).

Let w be a proof-only algebraic root of w^2=Bguard. Define

    N13(xi)=Norm_w [ N14(xi-54w) ]
           =Res_w(w^2-Bguard, N14(xi-54w)).

The resultant convention displayed here has the monic polynomial first,
so it is exactly the product over the two roots of w^2-Bguard, with
multiplicity. Equivalently reduce N14(xi-54w) to E(xi)+O(xi)w using
w^2=Bguard, then set

    N13=E(xi)^2-Bguard*O(xi)^2.

This is an ordinary integer polynomial. The auxiliary symbol w is not a
quantified coordinate. The thirteen coordinates are

    J6,C,z,h,r,v_scale,w3,c1,T,pair_root,p2,p3,xi.

The use of raw N14 is essential to this definition: the old finalized
expression N14+G^2+N14*G^2 should not be substituted in its place without
a different proof.

## 2. Unconditional arithmetic bounds replace the deleted guard

The fixed source coefficient is beta=2^1064921-1. In particular beta is
odd, beta>=3 and beta=1 modulo 3. On every integer assignment,

    J=6J6+1, q=beta J+1, W=4(3w3+1),
    X=qW, Y=(2v_scale+1)q^3, a=(X+1)Y.

J is nonzero and odd. Thus q is even, and

    |q|>=beta-1>=2.

Also |W|>=4 and |2v_scale+1|>=1. Therefore

    |X+1|>=|X|-1>=7, |Y|>=8, |a|>=56.

It follows that

    Delta=(a+2)^2-1 >= 54^2-1 =2915>1.

The syntactic c=2c1+1 is nonzero. Consequently

    L=(2r+1)c^2 != 0,
    Q=Delta^2 L^2>1, delta=Q-1>0.

H=4a+3 is odd and nonzero. None of these statements uses Bguard being a
square, any guard equation, or any deleted root equation.

The radicands F2=1+Delta L^2 and Dm=1+Delta c^2 are positive integers.
The first radicand A=u(u+k)+1 is at least one, because u=bk with
b=XY^2 and A=b(b+1)k^2+1. The remaining rational radicand

    B=(QV^2-1)/(Q-1)

is nonzero: QV^2=1 is impossible for integral V and integer Q>1.

## 3. Valuations are established before any guard recovery

Modulo 3, beta,J,W are 1, q,X are 2, and a=(X+1)Y is 0. Thus

    Delta=0 modulo 3, H=0 modulo 3,
    F2=1 modulo 3, Dm=1 modulo 3, Q=0 modulo 9.

Writing b=XY^2 again shows b is 0 or 2 modulo 3, so A=1 modulo 3.
Both numerator and denominator of B are -1 modulo 9. It follows that
the four old radical terms

    18sqrt(F2), 6sqrt(A), 2sqrt(B), 2sqrt(Dm)/H

have respective 3-adic valuations

    2, 1, 0, -v_3(H).

These values are pairwise distinct and all at most 2.

The integer polynomial Bguard may have any sign and may vanish. If it
does not vanish, w^2=Bguard gives

    v_3(w)=v_3(Bguard)/2>=0,
    v_3(54w)>=3.

Thus the fifth term has a valuation strictly greater than every old
term. If Bguard=0, then w=0 and the fifth term is simply omitted.

All assertions concern any extension of the valuation to the finite
multiquadratic field generated by the five radicals. Negative radicands
do not invalidate the argument.

## 4. Soundness of the norm equation

Suppose an unrestricted integer tuple satisfies N13=0. Because the
quadratic w^2-Bguard is monic, there is an algebraic root w for which

    N14(xi-54w)=0.

The algebraic factorization of the raw four-root norm gives a signed
root identity of the form

    xi+3(2u+k)+2V+18(a+2)L+2D0/H
      = +/-18sqrt(F2) +/-6sqrt(A) +/-2sqrt(B)
        +/-2sqrt(Dm)/H +54w,

where D0=X+ac. The left side is rational. The valuation separation lemma
now applies to all five nonzero terms. Alternatively apply its Galois
proof directly: changing any old radical gives a unique least-valued
term of valuation at most 2, while the changed fifth term has valuation
at least 3, so cancellation is impossible. Thus all old roots are fixed
by every Galois automorphism and are rational. The signed-root identity
then makes w rational as well. This also covers w=0.

Monicity makes f=sqrt(F2), tau=sqrt(A), d=sqrt(Dm), and w integral after
choosing the signs supplied by the identity. The adjacent-coefficient
descent makes y=sqrt(B) integral from

    QV^2-(Q-1)y^2=1.

The carrier implies 2gamma integral for gamma=(d-D0)/H. Hgamma=d-D0 is
integral and H is odd, so gamma is integral. Restoring p1=w gives

    G=Bguard-p1^2=0.

At this point all retained and deleted conditions of the fourteen-variable
construction hold, and its previously reviewed polynomial restoration
recovers the original unrestricted-integer interface. In particular

    i_old=Delta(2r+1), r_old=(i_old-1)/2,
    o=f(cT-R),
    j_old=F2*T-1-Delta(2r+1)^2*c^3*R

are integers and satisfy all original strong, auxiliary, and congruence
rows. There is no appeal to the guard to justify the radicals before the
guard has itself been recovered.

## 5. Completeness and the new odd-c restriction

The source-34 canonical construction used in the fourteen-variable proof
has A_star=a+2 even, R odd, and c=psi_A_star(R) odd. Thus

    c1=(c-1)/2

is an integer for every canonical witness used in that completeness
argument. The syntactic restriction does not require an additional
cofinality, density, or number-theoretic existence theorem.

That construction supplies integer p1,p2,p3 and pair_root for the joint
guard, hence Bguard=p1^2, and supplies an integer xi14 with N14(xi14)=0.
Set

    xi13=xi14+54p1.

Then w=p1 is a common root of w^2-Bguard and N14(xi13-54w), so N13=0.
This is the complete lift. It changes only the carrier and the syntactic
coordinate used for c.

## 6. Leading-coefficient and specialization check

The first two-root norm has leading coefficient delta^2 as a polynomial
in its carrier. The denominator-cleared main-root norm has leading
coefficient delta^4 H^8. The centered strong-root norm N14 therefore has
leading coefficient

    delta^8 H^16 != 0.

The final fifth-root norm has leading coefficient

    delta^16 H^32 != 0

and degree 32 in xi. These are degrees in the carrier, not claims about
the total degree after substituting the source polynomials.

The w-polynomial N14(xi-54w) also retains its degree, with nonzero leading
coefficient 54^16 delta^8 H^16. Thus neither nonzero-denominator clearing
nor degeneration of the carrier polynomial can create a spurious zero.
When Bguard=0 the final norm is N14(xi)^2, which gives the same valid
recovery with p1=0.
