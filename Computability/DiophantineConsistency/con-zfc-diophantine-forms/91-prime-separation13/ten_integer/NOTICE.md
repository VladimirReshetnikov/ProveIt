# Attribution and scope

The four parent polynomials, their coefficients and source kernels,
the norm-saturated H1868 predecessor, polynomial shears, exact-degree
certificates, and compiler/refutation calibration are inherited from the
supplied ProveIt `con_zfc_polynomial_shears` package and its pinned sources.
Its component and source report are retained under `upstream/` and
`provenance/`, with their license and notice files unmodified.

The contribution here is the composition of three elementary ring/circuit
rewrites, explicit optimized graphs, and a standalone exact-context audit.
The improvements preserve the complete parent polynomials. They do not
establish a new machine-independent calibration, a globally smallest
equation, or historical priority. The mathematical and computational review
was performed within this AI-assisted collaboration, not by external human
peer review.

New material in this component is offered under `LICENSE.NEW` (MIT No
Attribution), subject to the inherited terms governing copied material.
