\section{Monic projection before positional encoding}
A different architecture is to eliminate source registers before compressing
source coordinates.  The transformed source need not remain quadratic.
This yields a rigorously verified positive-domain low-degree tradeoff even
before a compact positional code is found.

Let $q=\beta J+1$.  From the frozen 31-coordinate source, eliminate
\[
 L=q^2-1,\quad N=q^3,\quad X=qw,\quad
 \Delta=a^2+4a+3,\quad C_2=c^2,\quad F_2=f^2,
\]
\[
 D=(4a+3)\gamma+ac+X,\quad B_{\rm aux}=I^2,
 \quad P=E(c-\eta)+\eta+\zeta,
 \quad V=jc+hE-\eta-\zeta+1.
\]
The first eight reconstructed coordinates are visibly positive. For the last
two use the retained equations $a-E=sN$ and
$c=(\eta+\zeta)(a-E)+\eta$, which imply $c>\eta+\zeta$.
Each eliminated equation is monic, so substitution and reconstruction give
inverse bijections of positive common-zero sets.
The exact residual list in \texttt{source21.json} consists of 12 polynomials
$r_j$ in 21 positive variables, with maximum degree 4 and 100 monomials in total.
Thus the explicit equation
\[
                  \sum_{j=1}^{12}r_j^2=0
\]
is an octic in 21 positive unknowns with the inherited consistency equivalence.
The compiler constants are coefficients, not unknowns. For the fixed instance,
\texttt{COEFFICIENT\_BINDING.json} selects the complete inherited old31 tuple
with $\beta=2^{1064921}-1$, with $K_0=K$ and the already-shifted $M_F$.
The frozen source hash agrees with the prior package's explicit old31 selection;
the separate adaptive-carrier compact44 tuple is not substituted here. This is a mathematical
composition with the inherited source theorem, not a new kernel certificate.
Deleting only $C_2,F_2,L,X,B_{\rm aux}$ instead gives 17 cubic residuals in 26
positive unknowns, hence a sextic in 26 positive unknowns.
Both are dominated in unknown count at the same degree by existing
positive-domain endpoints: a 20-positive octic and a 25-positive sextic.
They are source-transformation demonstrations, not new low-degree records.
No minimum or improvement over every repository tradeoff is asserted.

For mixed-degree positional compression, let $d$ be the maximum degree,
$m=d!$, $W_\alpha=\sum_i\alpha_iw_i$, and $U=\sum_i x_iZ^{w_i}$. Set
\[
 D_e(Z)=\sum_{j,\,|\alpha|=e}
 \frac{m}{e!}\Bigl(\prod_i\alpha_i!\Bigr)c_{j,\alpha}
 Z^{q_j-W_\alpha}.
\]
Under same-degree isolation against \emph{every} degree-$e$ monomial,
the coefficient at $q_j$ in $\sum_eD_eU^e$ equals $mr_j$ by the multinomial
theorem. Different degree channels may overlap freely. This removes dummy
homogenizing coordinates. However, decoded coordinates bounded by $b$ now
require coefficient bounds of order $b^d$, not$b^2$. A new code must compensate
for that increased radix exponent. The projection alone does not establish
a better nine-variable finalizer.
