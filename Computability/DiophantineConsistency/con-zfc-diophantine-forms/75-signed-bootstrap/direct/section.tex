\section{A fixed input boundary without a variable-count penalty}
\label{sec:fixedinput}
This section proves a compiler-interface advance, not a newly instantiated
small consistency equation. The unresolved work is an explicit small universal
machine, its compiled refuter input, and the new complete coefficient tuple.

\paragraph{The boundary lemma.}
Suppose the native compiler recovers
\[
 C=\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}c_iB^i,\qquad 0\le c_i<B,
\]
where $B$ is fixed and increasing $i$ moves leftward from the marked cell.
Let $t_0,\ldots,t_{m-1}$ be prescribed initializer tiles, encoded with all
free dummy digits zero, and let
\[
 D=\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\operatorname{enc}(t_i)B^i,
 \qquad M=B^m.
\]
Assume the last digit is nonzero. Then the single linear equation
\[
 C=D+MV,\qquad V\in\mathbb Z,
\]
is equivalent to prescribing those $m$ digits. Indeed $0\le D<M$,
$C\ge0$ imply $V\ge0$, and uniqueness of positional expansion proves
both directions. Also $C\ge D\ge B^{m-1}$ forces $N\ge m$.
The coefficient $M$ is fixed: no unknown occurs as an exponent.

\paragraph{Affine absorption.}
The lifted native source has $C=q-F-\alpha$ with
$q=\beta J+1$ and an independent coordinate $\alpha$. Substitute
\[
 \boxed{\alpha:=q-F-D-MV}
\]
everywhere, including in every old positivity guard, and replace the
coordinate $\alpha$ by $V$. There is no new residual. The new solutions
are in bijection with the old solutions obeying the boundary constraint,
via $V=(C-D)/M$. Affine substitution increases neither variable count nor
maximum degree. In particular the existing unrestricted quartic architecture
can accept fixed finite inputs without a variable-count or degree penalty.
Its coefficients are not the old K215 coefficients: the entire new tuple
must be regenerated. The bitlength of $M$ is $1+\lfloor m\log_2B\rfloor$ (equal to
$1+m\log_2B$ when $B$ is a power of two), an explicit
cost rather than an uncharged claim of compression.

\paragraph{A constant-size initializer.}
For an alphabet $\Gamma$ with blank $0$, use horizontal phases $L,D,R$,
vertical reset symbols $I,Q$, and the tiles
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
(L,I,L,0),\ (R,I,R,0),&\text{blank margins},\\
(L,I,D,\operatorname{head}(q_0,a)),\ a\in\Gamma,
 &\text{first symbol},\\
(D,I,D,a),\ a\in\Gamma,&\text{remaining symbols},\\
(D,Q,R,0),&\text{marked right delimiter}.
\end{array}
\]
For input $a_0\cdots a_{\ell-1}$, prescribe the delimiter, the data tiles
for $a_{\ell-1},\ldots,a_1$, and the first-symbol tile for $a_0$, in that
right-to-left order. Thus $m=\ell+1$. An empty input is treated as the
single blank symbol. Blank-phase matching and horizontal periodicity force
blank margins ending at seams. The ordinary directed-signal transition,
finish and reset argument then gives exactly a finite halting computation.
The initializer uses $2|\Gamma|+3$ tiles, independently of input length.
Including resets, seams and the impossible forbidden tile, a generous total
bound is
\[
 ns+s(|S_R|+|S_L|)+6s+15,
\]
for $n$ working states and $s$ symbols. A total five-working-state,
five-symbol machine with one extra halt state therefore needs at most
$130$ tiles. This is a conditional bound, not a pinned 130-tile ZFC instance.

\paragraph{The rank controller survives a frozen prefix.}
The existing Boolean controller has an odd prime $\lambda$, $N=\lambda^n$, $T=N/\lambda$,
$C_0=d\lambda$, $k=\lambda-1>1$, and the orbit identity
\[
 \{B^{kj}\bmod C_0T:0\le j<T\}
 =\{1+C_0z\bmod C_0T:0\le z<T\}.
\]
Retain its coprimality hypotheses and $T>C_0$. Translate its mutable
positions to $a+kj$ and the extra position $a+1$, with fixed $a\ge m$.
To represent $r$, first divide by the unit $B^a$. Choose
$\epsilon\in\{0,1\}$ so that $rB^{-a}-\epsilon B$ is nonzero modulo
$\lambda$. Its residue $b$ modulo $C_0$ satisfies
$1\le b<C_0<T$ and $\gcd(b,T)=1$. Write the remaining residue as
$b+C_0u$. Solve
\[
 bz_0+\frac{b(b-1)}2\equiv u\pmod T
\]
and select the $b$ orbit coordinates $z_0,\ldots,z_0+b-1$, plus the extra
position iff $\epsilon=1$. Their sum is $r$. They are distinct, and none
lies in the frozen prefix. The maximal orbit position is $a+k(T-1)$,
leaving margin $T+k-a$. Arbitrarily large independent padded widths and
heights make the required translated positions fit as well. Consequently
canonical rank and parity control remains compatible with the fixed input.

The finite replay checks $113{,}748$ positional cases and $108$ instances
of the translated Boolean controller, as well as $92$ finite initialization strips. These checks are not substitutes for
the universal argument above. They do not instantiate the missing universal
machine, input word, or regenerated arithmetic-controller certificate.
