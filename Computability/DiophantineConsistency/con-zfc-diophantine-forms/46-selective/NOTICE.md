# Attribution and source boundary

This research package was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project
on 23 October 2026, with AI assistance. It reviews repository commit
`124c07c0b77a9e20e84676514d8b1ff78536ea3f`. It is an ordinary mathematical
research contribution with reproducible finite certificates, not a claim
of global optimality or a new end-to-end proof-assistant certificate.

The main quadratic parent, coherent native coefficient tuple, and signed
ten-variable finalizer are inherited from ProveIt packages 31 and 40.
The nine-variable numerical bridge and finalizer are inherited from
package 45 and the repository's Jones1982 formalization. The short local
equation uses the current P75/P69/P67 factor definitions and its own whole
source-36 coefficient convention. The new native adapter builds on the
factorized receiver construction in package 40 and complement/interface
arguments in package 44. Its own source pins and notices are retained.

The K215 machine, native compiler, Wang language, Pell arithmetic,
ZFC-refutability calibration, and Sun/Jones theorems are not new results
of this package. Sun's 2021 paper and 2026 preprint, Jones's 1982 paper,
and the relevant repository materials are cited in the article. The
native-window compiler remains an inherited ordinary mathematical
dependency; the current active repository distinguishes it from local
Lean polynomial equivalences.

New work consists of the explicit selective support certificate, shared
packed-word positivity substitution, nonuniform target layout and common
translation, proofs of the modified source contracts, their resulting
literal circuits and exact-degree certificates, the local P74 norm-minimum
argument, and the complemented receiver/parity-anchor coefficient adapter.
Package 40 had already suggested support-sensitive separation and
nonuniform targets as research directions. No claim of global priority
for sparse coefficient coding or small-variable universality is made.

`code/build.py` adapts the source/finalizer organization of package 40.
`code/circuit.py` is the retained package-31 circuit helper.
`nine/build_nine.py` adapts package 45's numerical tail; its preserved
original builder/helper/proof appear under `nine/upstream/`.
The independent auditors reconstruct the relevant finite identities and
literal formulas without importing the corresponding new builders.

Files under `upstream/` and component upstream directories retain their
original text, dates, historical source commits, and mathematical claims
for provenance. Their directory-relative historical links may refer to
the original repository layout. Exact retained source bytes are pinned
in `SOURCES.json` and component provenance records. Newly authored files
use the included ProveIt MIT No Attribution license. Original licenses
and K215 source notices are preserved under `licenses/` and in the
coefficient adapter. Their original attribution and source conventions
must remain clear when integrating this report.
