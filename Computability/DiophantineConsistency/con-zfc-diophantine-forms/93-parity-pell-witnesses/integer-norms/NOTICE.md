were prepared with OpenAI ChatGPT for Vladimir Reshetnikov and ProveIt.

inherited notices retained in `licenses/`.

The 17-variable parent, coherent source34 coefficient binding, common source
registers and arithmetic DAG emitter come from the inspected
`ConZFC_Saturated_Certificates_2026-10-24.zip` packet. The parent has its own
explicit dependencies, including source71's calibrated determinant, source68's
joint sign guard, source70's residue orientation, source69's rational Pell
descent, and the ordinary K215/native compiler/refutation calibration.
This contribution leaves those fixed ports and inherited proof obligations
unchanged.

The elementary two- and three-root norm identities and multiquadratic
square-class reasoning are classical algebra. No historical priority claim
is made for those identities. The new combination is the parity restriction
that makes the retained odd strong multiplier recoverable without its own
witness coordinate, followed by the specified concrete projections.

The separate degree checker in `independent_degree/` was produced in a
parallel AI review and uses a retained exact sparse-ring implementation from
ProveIt's centered-joint22 checker. Its purpose is algebraic verification,
not a new semantic or consistency theorem.
