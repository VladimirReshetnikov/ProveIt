# Attribution and contribution boundary

Prepared with AI assistance for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project,
2026-10-23. New material follows the repository's MIT No Attribution license
in `LICENSE`. Preserved third-party notices and licenses continue to apply
to their corresponding inherited files.

The new work in this packet is the degree-separated quadratic source,
its frozen finite certificate and unconditional positive carrier; its
composition with the calibrated 9/10 arithmetic wrappers; the two separate
Pell norms and balanced auxiliary-ordinate quotient; the 45-integer quartic
guard reduction; and the accompanying proofs and independent audits.

The native K215 machine, its consistency calibration, the Wang943 language,
native-window compiler, normalized and signed Pell interfaces, original
quadratic parents, compact whole coefficient tuple, and public Jones/Sun
theorems are inherited. They are attributed in their preserved notices and
the report. The compact tuple comes from package39/44's bounded-checksum
adapter; its original provenance points to package36 and earlier compiler
sources. This packet supplies its full existing four-port binding, not a
new native coefficient compiler.

The incoming wave-7 packages already supply selective support isolation,
the shifted packed word, one-bit borrow-tolerant masks, exact mask-population
thresholds, CRT quotient substitution, polynomial Pell majorants,
margin-first radical separation, and the Sun structural-weight finalizer.
These are used with attribution, not claimed as discoveries of this packet.
The new source changes which degrees must be separated and adds a globally
positive carrier. The 9-variable two-block calibration also removes an
unneeded leading block. The direct low-degree route uses a known canonical
squared-modulus auxiliary reconstruction to justify its new ordinate quotient.

`circuit.py` retains the small division-free DAG infrastructure from the
incoming constructions. Preserved historical Python files in `upstream/`
are provenance documents, not additional reproduction entry points; their
original relative paths describe their source packages. The current
entry points are those in `README.md`.

The independent reviews were internal checks of this research contribution,
not external peer review. They separately examined source identities,
Pell index recovery and completeness, the quartic proof order, and the
replacement ten-integer source contract. Finite exact audits accompany the
unbounded mathematical arguments; they do not prove consistency or close
the repository's object-language formalization obligation.
