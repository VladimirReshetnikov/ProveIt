\section{Comparison with the pinned frontier}
The comparison is frozen at public repository commit
\texttt{84e16e43092614bae262ca59651cb9d77e31a255}
(24 October 2026).  The active research is substantially ahead of the
archival manuscript-collection README.  We also include the previously
supplied prime-power and square-shell/reciprocal packages, which are not
yet incorporated in that public snapshot.

Let $\mathbb N=\{0,1,\ldots\}$.  The following complete direct polynomials
are quartics.  Here $r$ is the number of quadratic residuals, $s$ the
number of collected monomials, and $h$ the maximum absolute coefficient
after expanding the single sum of squares.  A residual is not an
arithmetic operation.

Every entry in this table was independently recomputed from the literal
JSON residuals and compared coefficient by coefficient with the supplied
expanded polynomial.  The data-only check executes no inherited script.
It checks algebra and metrics, not the unbounded semantic calibration.

The363-variable natural equation dominates the375-variable natural
equation in all displayed measures.  The364-variable shell alternative
uses one more variable but has fewer monomials and much smaller height.
Over integers,553 variables trades against99-bit coefficient height.
The public609-variable equation improves on660 variables and its
coefficient height, but has more monomials,8823 versus6408.  Accordingly
there is no single record independent of the chosen objective.

Nor are these the smallest-variable quartics without qualification:
the calibrated native-compiler route already has a37-integer quartic,
a31-integer sextic and a26-integer octic, using very large fixed
coefficient payloads.  Payload byte length and expanded coefficient
height are different quantities.

\paragraph{Proof boundary.}
Ordinary mathematical proofs, finite algebra checks, and a newly
kernel-checked or encoded object-language ZFC derivation must not be
conflated.  The cited constructions inherit named Pell, compiler and
syntactic-calibration results; current internal formalization remains
open.  The scalar12 pre-projection rank proof was checked against its
retained signed-quadratic and interpolation sources: absolute main-Pell
classification, rank bounds and the whole-product bound precede recovery
of $\gamma=(d-X-ac)/H$.  No circular use of that projection was found in
this prefix.  This does not replace an audit of every transitive
calibration dependency.

\paragraph{Rejected shortcut.}
The shared-projection83 proposal is refuted for its original inherited
compiler recipe by positive zeros at unbounded ordinary inputs for a
genuine fixed empty-language program.  It is not a valid baseline.
The established universal operation point is84 operations, degree187
and18 positive witnesses;92 operations, degree325 and17 witnesses is a
different valid tradeoff.  This rejection does not rule out all possible
83-operation constructions or other coefficient recipes.
