\subsection{What is and is not dominated}
The $519$-integer quartic strictly improves the supplied $553$-integer and $660$-integer equations, and the pinned public $609$-integer equation, simultaneously in variables, collected monomials, and coefficient height, at unchanged degree. Its $411060$ bound applies to every expanded coefficient, not only a radix or construction constant. The $517$-integer companion saves two more unknowns but has $521$ more monomials and a higher coefficient bound; neither new integer endpoint dominates the other in all three measures.

The $349$-natural endpoint saves fourteen variables relative to the $363$-natural reciprocal equation but has $6094$ more terms and slightly larger height. Its intermediate $350$-natural factorial-slack companion has $11472$ terms and height $86523090$, another explicit tradeoff. These domains must not be merged: nonnegative unknowns are not unrestricted integer unknowns without a further proved transformation.

The native-compiler route already retains a quartic in $37$ unrestricted integer variables, with $31$-variable sextic and $26$-variable octic companions, at very large fixed coefficients. Thus neither $519$ nor $517$ is a global fewest-variable quartic record. Payload bytes, coefficient bit lengths, and the height of the expanded output polynomial are different quantities.

\subsection{Rejected shortcuts and remaining questions}
The shared-projection $83$-operation proposal is refuted for its original weakened interface by zeros at unbounded ordinary inputs for a fixed empty-language program. The valid universal operation baseline remains $84$ operations, degree $187$, and $18$ positive witnesses; $92$ operations, degree $325$, and $17$ witnesses is another tradeoff. These are universal input/witness counts, not direct fixed-ZFC certificate counts. The refutation does not refute every possible $83$-operation construction, the modified75 compiler, or the unchanged source34 tuple.

Further research should target specific verifiable improvements:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can a lower-degree intersective carrier satisfy the required prime-local conditions while preserving a no-rational-root bootstrap? The general saturation lemma makes the exact allowable degree and rank margin explicit.
\item Can a further integral shear reduce the degree below $768$ without increasing the live operation count? Any candidate needs a polynomial identity and a nonzero specialized leading coefficient, not just a syntactic estimate.
\item Can the $517$-integer branch inherit the $519$-integer branch's small coefficient height? Integral shifts and monic projections should be compared using a reproducible Pareto search rather than an unqualified ``simplest'' score.
\item Can another positive label constructor or exact local bound improve the $349$-natural support while keeping the shared-factorial proof? The derived label must remain outside the aggregate representative bound.
\item Can the signed scalar folding, full supported-modulus interpolation, and direct C222 calibration be internalized in the proof assistant? This would close a different proof obligation from finite coefficient checking.
\end{enumerate}
No lower-bound theorem, global minimum, or historical priority claim is made.
