# Attribution and scope

The signed direct construction composes the supplied Prime Power 375 553
and Square Shell Reciprocal 660 arithmetic and logical interfaces with the
round14 reciprocal-prime-power source. The generic signed indexed-Pell and
abscissa-orientation lemmas are inherited, not claimed as new here.

The local square factorization, its exact source transformer, the presemantic
positive-index proof deleting the auxiliary nonzero guard, the concrete
integer endpoints and integral translations are the round14 contribution.

terminal_elimination.py is copied unchanged from the Prime Power 375 553
package's inherited parent407/code/integer_pipeline/terminal_elimination.py.
It was inspected before use. Preserve the parent package's license and
attribution notices when redistributing this file.

build_signed.py adapts the already supplied square-budget and sign-closure
algorithms into a standard-library-only builder without executing inherited
modules. All other code in this directory was authored for this round.

The equations certify refutability, not the existence or absence of an actual
refutation. Finite exact checks are not substitutes for the unbounded proof,
nor a claim of a newly checked complete Lean or Rocq endpoint.
