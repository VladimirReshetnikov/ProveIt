# Proof entry point

square recovery, auxiliary denominator descent, smaller index modulus,
fifth-power divisibility, supported-modulus completeness, and exact degree
proof appear in `../report/norm_section.tex`, included in the compiled PDF.

An independent full ordinary proof review is retained at
`../reviews/SYNTHESIS11_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md`.

Inherited unbounded arguments are retained in `source/`, especially:

- `SELECTIVE_FUSION_PARENT.md`: incoming eleven-variable parent and source boundary.
- `POWERED_SATURATION_PARENT.md`: powered divisibility and full interpolation modulo every modulus supported on the fixed strong ordinate.
- `centered11-proof.md`: centered budget, signed index recovery, and native source restoration.
- `signed-quadratic-proof.md`: signed folding and exact main projection.
- `PRIME_TWELVE.md`: intersectivity of `(x^2+3)(x^3-19)`.

Relative paths inside unchanged inherited files refer to their original
package context; the matching source texts are retained alongside them or
under `source/provenance`. Their historical statements and numerical counts
are not relabeled as new results.

The finite check receipts establish the literal arithmetic graph and its
degree, not consistency or the unbounded source calibration. The new result
is an ordinary proof formalizable in ZFC, with the inherited semantic
calibration explicit, rather than a new end-to-end proof-assistant certificate.
