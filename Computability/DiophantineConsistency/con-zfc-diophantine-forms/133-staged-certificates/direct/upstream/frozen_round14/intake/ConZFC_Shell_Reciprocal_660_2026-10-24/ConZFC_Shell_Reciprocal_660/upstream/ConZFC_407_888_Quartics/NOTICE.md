# Provenance and attribution

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project through AI-assisted
mathematical research and independently implemented exact algebra checks.

The inherited numerical calculus, its calibration to ZF and ZFC refutation,
proof-flow and beta-coding methods, arithmetic graph identities, and shared
Pell infrastructure come from ProveIt and its incoming contributions.
Original notices and licenses are retained under `upstream/`.

The immediate base is
`ConZFC_Synchronized_Certificates_2026-10-24.zip`, as published in ProveIt
commit `85ae4070019efd66ea3e58e9a67d287528094e8b`. The eight-column
projection is inherited from `ConZFC_Direct_Projection_7617.zip`. The signed
terminal elimination method follows the batched-certificate contribution
`con_zfc_batched_certificates_2026-10-23.zip`. The article distinguishes
these inherited ingredients from the transformations developed here.

The immutable archive inventory is `provenance/incoming_index.json`.
`provenance/UPSTREAM_SHA256.json` authenticates every retained inherited
file. Logical calibration pins appear additionally in
`upstream/provenance/upstream/calibration/CALIBRATION_PINS.json`.

The main mathematical changes in this contribution are residue-only
binomial graphs, lower-digit bounds with a reused factorial numerator,
nonnegative difference-square gates, additive Pell-index reuse, exact
nonnegative restoration certificates, and selective sign guarding. Their
application here is explicit and reproducible; no worldwide novelty claim
is made for the general identities.

New code and accompanying documentation are provided under the MIT No
Attribution terms in `LICENSE`, consistent with the retained immediate
upstream package. Third-party notices remain applicable to their files.
