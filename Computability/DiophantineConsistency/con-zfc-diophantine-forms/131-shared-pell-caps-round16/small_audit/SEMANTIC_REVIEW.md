- Fstrong is 1 modulo D, while delta and Dnum are -1 modulo D.
- Modulo Fstrong, delta is -A^2 and Dnum is -Kc.
- Fstrong is 1 modulo both A and Kc, because both divide E.

Thus D and Fstrong are each coprime to the product of the other relevant factors. All factors used in the square extraction are positive: the positive input domain gives A>6, c>=3, Fstrong>c+2, and V=Fstrong b-c cannot have absolute value at most one for integral b. In particular Dnum>0. Multiplying N=0 by delta and dividing by the nonzero square M^2 makes D Fstrong delta Dnum a rational square, hence an integer square. Unique factorization gives D=tau^2 and Fstrong=f^2 separately and leaves delta Dnum square. The rational auxiliary root then satisfies the stated adjacent-coefficient Pell equation with Q=(Delta tau E)^2.
