Each m_i has its own two fresh aliases; each w_i is private. All quadratic row-energy terms involve only w variables, leaving alias squares/edges free for agreement cycles. Ordinary alias groups of size two acquire a third dummy, avoiding the forbidden double edge; unit aliases use one anchored path with distinct endpoints. Degree partition plus private supports excludes all collisions. Binary constant/scale definitions are triangular monic equations and preserve a degree-at-most-two lifting map from the original source. No hidden positivity restrictions occur. The identity P(L(x))=sum R_j(x)^2 holds for every source tuple. Global height-one optimality is only the tautological coefficient-height optimum among nonzero integer polynomials, not a simplicity optimum or a new worldwide theorem claim.
