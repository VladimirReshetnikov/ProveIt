# Source attribution

This component derives its direct polynomial source and proof closure from
Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository at commit
`3ecd6a6cf53d47519228328567f45fb7ee9781d9`.

The complete common-remainder parent, including its original notice and
MIT No Attribution license, is retained in `parent/`. Prior odd-square radix and
affine-exposure proofs and their provenance are retained in `sources/`.
`SOURCE_LEDGER.json` records the exact source-archive SHA-256 values.

The factorial-free source mutation, its independent finite checker, the selected
residual bases, readable exports, reproduction driver, and independent power
interface review were prepared for the present contribution. The new unbounded
mathematical claims are explained and qualified in the accompanying report.
