# Attribution and proof boundary

This research was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project with
AI assistance. New text, code, equations and certificate applications follow
the accompanying MIT No Attribution license. Original third-party notices
are retained in the compact source closure and the unchanged direct-source
archives. Those notices apply to their respective material.

Immediate arithmetic parents are the package-142 coprime-product equations,
the incoming Factorial-Free Binomial CRT source, and the incoming Pooled
Powers source. The incoming Quadratic Projection 234 package supplies the
common-quadratic-direction projection method and its independent checker.
The incoming Linear CRT package precedes this work's diagonal growth and
linear beta choices. Rational adjacent-Pell integrality, coprime root
extraction, finite matrix-period completion and signed Pell-index lemmas are
also inherited, with their ordinary sources supplied.

The contributions claimed here are the specified new compact quotient
carriers, the smaller-modulus refinement, the affine residue-class bootstrap,
and the exact composed equations and their verified certificates. No broader
priority or global optimum is asserted.

The logical conclusion is proved by ordinary mathematics formalizable in ZFC,
composed with the explicitly identified inherited refutation calibration.
The finite programs establish exact artifact properties and source identity.
They do not constitute a new end-to-end proof-assistant derivation or an
encoded ZFC proof term, and they do not decide Con(ZFC).
