# Selected positive10 proof and numerical closure

The active release manifest is `MANIFEST_SHA256.json`; `SOURCE_PINS.json`
authenticates the unchanged selected source files. Root `replay.py` is the
portable entry point. Nested historical receipts/provenance are retained
as evidence, not authoritative manifests for this new package.

## Ordinary source lemmas and calibration

* `inherited/strict/PROOF.md`: free positive quotient, lambda=q, late full
  denominator recovery, native restoration, and fresh positive construction.
* `inherited/strict/parent_cofinal_proof.md`: projection-before-denominator
  arithmetic and canonical source witnesses.
* `inherited/strict/upstream_lucas/PROOF.md`: complete second-order recurrence,
  prime2/prime3 index authentication and Lucas chart.
* `inherited/strict/upstream_lucas/parent/PROOF.md`: signed all-index folding,
  main-rank recovery, original positive carrier and native restoration.
* `inherited/strict/upstream_lucas/parent/proof_sources/uniform_ratio.md`:
  theorem uniform for lambda>=1,Y>=1; essential for free Y=1.
* `inherited/strict/upstream_lucas/parent/proof_sources/universal_pell.tex`:
  Pell classification, normalized strong rank, signed auxiliary index,
  positive auxiliary reconstruction. Other proof texts in that directory
  retain the named arithmetic/native ancestry; none is omitted.
* `inherited/TRACE_PROOF.md`, `TRACE_REVIEW.md`: explicit strong-trace change
  and positive auxiliary reconstruction. Their historical `inherited/lucas`
  path resolves here to `inherited/strict/upstream_lucas`. Their historical
  cofinal proof is represented by the strict parent's cofinal proof and its
  complete universal/native dependencies, not by the separate unrelated
  direct C222 family.
* `inherited/INVERSE_PROOF.md`, `INVERSE_REVIEW.md`: inverse cubic, exact
  prime3 obstruction, power-of-two repair and negative-rho positive converse.
  Their historical `inherited_lucas` resolves to the same selected
  `inherited/strict/upstream_lucas`; their `inherited_projection.md` arithmetic
  is retained as the strict `parent_cofinal_proof.md`.

## Literal equations and coherent numerical tuple

The three root graphs are the equations. Their coefficient_binding fields
resolve to `inherited/strict/upstream_lucas/parent/coefficients/`, containing
all four complete gzip payloads. The adjacent `rebuild_coefficients.py`
reconstructs all bytes from the printed finite indexed-tile recipe. Its
inline machine string and full943-tile construction are retained. No
coefficient is represented only by a hash or chosen independently from the
other three. `beta=2^590069−1`; the largest port has592,884 bits.

The two strict source graphs and inverse_bV.json are retained. Their complete
coefficient bindings agree byte-for-byte. New `build.py` reads them as data,
not executable code. All relevant licenses and source notices are present
under the inherited parent licenses directory, with root LICENSE/NOTICE.md.

The unbounded logical theorem remains the expressly named ordinary
native/refutation calibration of these sources. Neither this package nor
its finite checker claims a newly rebuilt end-to-end Lean development.
No optional historical rendered PDFs, repository-wide aggregate indexes,
or unrelated direct-quartic/natural-number components were copied.
