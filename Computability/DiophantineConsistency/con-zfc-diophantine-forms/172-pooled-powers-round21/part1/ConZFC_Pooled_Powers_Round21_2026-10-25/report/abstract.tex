\begin{abstract}
An explicit quartic in 217 unrestricted integers is given as the sum of
154 literal quadratic squares, with 7396 collected monomials and
coefficient height 8880. It combines common-base power pooling with a
strict-horizon factorial-free source, an odd beta multiplier, a wider
remainder norm and certified integral projections. A 216-variable
companion and a height-3200 companion retain different tradeoffs.
The complete signed bootstrap is proved without assuming the radix or
beta signs before they are recovered. Unit-homogenized equality cycles and shared coefficient factors
strictly reduce the support of six matched coefficient-height-one
quartics without adding variables. A separate ten-positive-variable
construction combines linear trace, free quotient and inverse-Lucas
correction, attaining exact degree 214 at 87 binary operations, with
232/85 and 240/82 companions. Two eleven-integer circuits retain degrees
298 and 300 at costs 146 and 144. Complete new ordinary arguments,
literal arrays, coherent numerical payloads and independent finite checks
are supplied. Their consistency interpretation composes with explicitly
identified inherited calibrations; no consistency proof, global minimum
or new end-to-end formal-kernel endpoint is claimed.
\end{abstract}
