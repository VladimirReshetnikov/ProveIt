All six have exact degree four, coefficient height one, constant coefficient
one, no equality dummy coordinates, and every declared unrestricted
coordinate present. Each new point strictly improves support over its
matched Round~20 arithmetic source and dimension, with height unchanged. These
small support gains are not described as new dimension records.

The first source is the retained 241-variable shared-error source;
the next two use the separate 242-variable power-base source. The last
three retain the grouped 263-variable or sparse 285-variable sources.
Their original source bindings and affine refactorizations are
unchanged. The refined 505- and 512-variable points use the new split-factor
normalization, with 342 normalized coordinates instead of 343; their
final graph counts remain 505 and 512. The other four retain their
constructor chains. In particular none is silently relabeled as the new pooled
principal. Trials of a transfer to newer arithmetic sources did not give
a selected improvement and are not counted as delivered equations.

To isolate the cycle change from normalization, the frozen cycle-only
controls at 505 and 512 variables have 20483 and 18247 terms. Together
with the other four endpoints these give the six matched cycle-only
reductions $10,8,8,8,4,4$. The refined selected graphs further save
78 and 63 terms against those two controls. Controls are replay evidence,
not extra selected deliverables.

The release contains each literal full coefficient array, graph
certificate, explicit source binding and frozen operation sequence.
Independent reconstruction checks the new cycle identity and universal
source lift as well as every expanded coefficient. The variable/support
tradeoffs among these six selected outputs remain distinct; no exhaustive
Pareto frontier or graph optimum is claimed.
