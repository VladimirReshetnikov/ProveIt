\section{Further questions}
\subsection{Can algebraically related bases share one pool}
The proved two-row module shares one base and one modulus
$2An-n^2-1$. Can the requests at $B$ and $BH$ be pooled using their
algebraic relation, without increasing residual degree or introducing
more coordinates than are saved? A sum of errors from different moduli
has no present exactness proof. This is not achieved in the delivered
construction.

\subsection{Can the next direction preserve quadratic degree integrally}
The released reductions use common Hessian-kernel directions, unit slopes
and affine consequences. General primitive directions could require a
unimodular completion and a Bezout combination of slopes. Can a larger
finite search find another certified quadratic-preserving projection,
while controlling the collected height and support? Absence of one of
the present pivot types is only a restriction on that search family,
not a lower bound on all Diophantine presentations.

\subsection{Can equality cycles and flow contractions be selected jointly}
A cycle change modifies the monomial collisions available to an internal
flow contraction. A contraction can in turn make a previously rejected
cycle safe. Joint optimization of the frozen graph, row signs and unit
choices may give more than the small separate support savings here.
Every proposed move needs its zero-set proof and full coefficient test;
an uncollected count of negative terms is not a height certificate.

\subsection{Can the positive scalar degree fall without losing its sign proof}
The degree-214 point uses an unshifted $b=cT-R$ and a uniform bound on
$|b|$ before $R$ is known positive. Can a different carrier lower the
remaining degree while preserving this bootstrap, the prime-three
correction and the positive witness lift? A circuit identity alone is
insufficient if the proposed coordinate replacement leaves the positive
orthant. The fixed-frame degree floors for the eleven-integer branch
place no bound on this different ten-positive construction.

\subsection{Can an emitted array be bound to a kernel checked calibration}
A focused formalization target is the actual two-row aggregate identity,
the signed small-error theorem, the exact projected residual arrays and
the graph-energy transfer. A complete transitive proof-assistant build
could then bind those finite arrays to the retained syntactic refutation
calibration. Successful coefficient replay and authenticated theorem-name
source files do not by themselves supply that stronger endpoint.
