# Complete release closure and two-part layout

Both ZIP parts populate one common release folder. The partition is a storage
choice only: no source path, numerical tuple or mathematical interface is
changed. Extract both before invoking any advertised root verification.

## Current direct equations

`signed/` contains all three literal residual systems and expansions, complete
new proof, frozen transformation certificates and independent verifiers.
`signed/inherited_strict/` retains the selected Strict Horizons source and
its ordinary proof/code ancestry, including the factorial-free binomial CRT
and C222/ZF/ZFC calibration. `signed/pooled_module/` contains credited reusable
pooling code; `signed/proofs/` and the main proof retain the corresponding
unbounded argument. The module's historical source is not being substituted
for the selected Strict Horizons calibration.

The verifier authenticates the actual 239-coordinate input, forward and
reverse private-wire identities, all 34 frame atoms and all 17 power ports,
the two pools, cofinal affine beta change, inverse coordinate maps, and every
collected quartic coefficient. Original notices and exact source identities
are retained. Formal interface sources are not a newly rebuilt transitive
Lean/Rocq proof object.

## Height-one equations and retained ancestors

`height_one/` supplies complete final coefficient arrays and literal text,
monic normalization/source pullbacks, incidence and equality certificates,
ordinary witness proofs and exact historical comparisons. Current original
source files are byte-bound through `SOURCE_BINDINGS.json` to the retained
calibrated equations under `inherited_round20/`.

`inherited_round20/signed/` is the complete previously delivered signed
component. `inherited_round20/height_sources/` retains the three older source
systems used in its height-one lineage. The unchanged adapter
`inherited_round20/check_height_sources.py` reconstructs those selected old
sources using its retained parent and exact input records. The top-level plan
sets its working directory and arguments explicitly. Old notices and closure
maps describe their historical full release; they are provenance, not a claim
that unrelated old report/scalar/height outputs are all copied here.

The selected new height-one polynomials keep the old dimensions and reduce
support. Pooled-source transfers were explored but were not selected.

## Separate scalar domains and coefficient tuples

`scalar/positive10/` contains three equations over strictly positive integers,
complete four-port numerical payloads and finite reconstruction, and full
ordinary trace, signed-transport, inverse-correction and native handoff proofs.
Its `inherited/` subtree supplies the selected antecedents. A complete signed
argument for intermediate expressions does not change the quantified domain
of its ten original variables.

`scalar/integer11/` contains two equations over unrestricted integers, the
separate six-port tuple, exact evaluator and polynomial-shear identities,
leading-form proofs and the full retained rebased/native arithmetic closure.
Its `CLOSURE_MAP.md` and omission ledger preserve the prior boundary: only
historical rendered PDFs and repository-wide aggregate indexes were omitted;
all required ordinary proof texts, code, numerical payloads and licenses remain.

The four-port and six-port payloads must not be partially interchanged, even
when labels or a machine ancestor look similar. Root independent audits
reconstruct both coherent tuples separately.

## Active checks and historical inventories

The active distribution inventory is root `MANIFEST_SHA256.json`. Component
manifests and source pins additionally bind their selected inputs. Unchanged
nested historical inventories may list documented optional files that are
not part of the selected closure; the root plan does not advertise execution
of an omitted historical whole-release entrypoint.

`VERIFY_PLAN.json` executes every current mathematical component and the
retained source checks in a disposable copy, both normally and with inherited
PYTHONOPTIMIZE=1. Portable no-producer checks in `audit/` independently collect
all polynomial coefficients, validate cross-component source bindings,
reconstruct both numeral tuples and parse the printed principal residuals.
Supplemental research-workspace scripts are not needed for this release.

The ordinary unbounded proofs and finite artifact audits are distinct. The
package proves the stated ZFC equivalences through named calibrations; it
does not decide consistency, supply a new formal-kernel endpoint or establish
a global simplicity minimum.
