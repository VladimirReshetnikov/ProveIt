were developed with AI assistance for Vladimir Reshetnikov on 25 October
2026. New material follows the retained MIT No Attribution license.

The immediate source is the `compact/` branch of
`ConZFC_Certificate_Reductions_2026-10-25.zip` in ProveIt's `docs/incoming`.
Its immutable SHA-256 identity is in `PROVENANCE.json`. Its original proof,
checker, builder, equations, and notices remain unchanged under `parent/`.

and the retained ordinary mathematical source files are unchanged copies.
Their original source ledger and notices are included. The verifier is
adapted from the frozen predecessor verifier; it is independent of the new
builder but is not presented as a separately invented checking algorithm.

The inherited K215/native endpoint, indexed tile recipe, universal Pell
lemmas, product-square method, and staged index recovery are explicitly
credited. The new contribution is the denominators `XY` and `q^4` and the shared
evaluation that it permits. No historical priority or global minimum is
claimed. No human or institutional endorsement is implied.
