# Attribution and verification scope

This submission was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt repository
using AI-assisted mathematical exploration and independent algebra and proof review.

New contributions in this package are the square-radius and output-linear factorial
fixed-point lemmas and their shared-radius implementation; the nine-column branch
projection; the integer sign schedule, centered guards and exact projections;
the nine-variable local majorant and its complete transplant; and the shorter
cleared radical norm circuits. The report, packaging and release driver are new.

The numerical C222 calculus, its ZF/ZFC calibration, earlier direct finite-flow
and large-carrier encoding, shared power/index/binomial machinery, fixed universal
source numeral constructions, and exact parent equations are inherited. Their
source files, hashes, ordinary proofs and licenses are retained and cited in the
component provenance ledgers. Historical upstream notices describe their original
submissions. The projection optimizer is a local adapter of the incoming batched
certificate code, with an independent builder-free checker of all actual steps.

New material is offered under MIT No Attribution (LICENSE), matching the target
repository. Inherited notices and licenses remain in force for copied material.
No global minimality, historical priority, proof of consistency, or new complete
proof-assistant/object-ZFC certificate is claimed.
