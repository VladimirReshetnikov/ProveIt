# Attribution and scope

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project with AI-assisted
mathematical exploration, independent proof reviews, and exact algebra audits.

The C222 numerical calculus and its ZF/ZFC calibration, the commutative-flow
criterion, large polynomial carrier, beta/CRT compiler, Pell representation,
shared power frame, common binomial radix, and nonnegative contraction
algorithm are inherited from the ProveIt contributors and the identified
Synchronized Certificates package. Original notices and licenses are retained
under `provenance/`.

New material in this component is the nine-column branch projection,
polynomial factorial fixed-point argument, shared bootstrap radius,
materialized resulting equations, and scoped audit/rebuild files. These
changes are compared with the inspected repository snapshot and incoming
packages. No claim of worldwide priority, global minimality, ZFC consistency,
or absence of polynomial solutions is made.

New material is supplied under the included MIT No Attribution license.
