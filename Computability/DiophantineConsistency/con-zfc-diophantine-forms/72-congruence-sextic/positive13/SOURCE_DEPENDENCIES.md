   and the R=3 mod4 property. It explicitly composes with the inherited
   native compiler, Pell converse, Wang-language theorem and K215 endpoint.

   This gives the normalized source's exact Pell hypotheses and their
   use in the first/main Pell-to-binomial bridge. It includes first-Pell

   strong rank, and the positive converse at the actual packed index.

   This is the underlying normalized blank-input arithmetic/native proof.
   The source44 compact recipe changes the whole coefficient tuple while

## Boundary of "standalone"

Including these texts and fixed numerals makes the newly proposed circuit,
local proofs and their immediate source contracts reviewable offline. The
entire transitive K215 formal development and a proof-assistant kernel are
not thereby rebuilt or included. A report should say precisely that its
ordinary mathematical proof composes the new reduction with named pinned
native and syntactic-calibration results. It should not claim that finite
replays certify those unbounded source theorems or that the repository's
separate closed Lean consistency equation automatically certifies this DAG.
