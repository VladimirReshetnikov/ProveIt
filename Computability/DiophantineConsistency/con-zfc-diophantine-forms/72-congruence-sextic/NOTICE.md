# Provenance and attribution

This research package was prepared with OpenAI ChatGPT for Vladimir
Reshetnikov's ProveIt project, for mathematical review and possible
incorporation. Its comparison snapshot is ProveIt commit
9cca523d9f27ad39d52993f4065cbd7e58f8bdd4.

New material comprises the positive-coordinate fixed-congruence reduction,
its 13-positive degree-28 circuit and ordered proof, its fourteen-positive
degree-24 product-register lift, the 34-integer sextic
reduction with its retained-row correction and polynomial restorations,
the local verification programs and independent audits, and the paper
and integration wrapper. The general rational-root and polynomial
row-operation facts are elementary and are not claimed as new theorems.

The native encodings, source coefficient recipes, literal K215 machine,
machine/refutation calibration, source quartic, and inherited mathematical
proofs are not original to this package. Their unchanged files are identified
in SOURCE_MANIFEST.json. Their supplied licensing and attribution texts
are preserved under positive13/native/licenses/, integer34/licenses/,
and provenance/. Historical notices can describe older coefficient layouts;
the active bindings, exact bytes and mathematical proofs identify the
tuples actually used here.

Locally copied independent checkers have only their relative default paths
adapted to this archive. The positive LaTeX section has a typographical
exponent correction. The frozen literal positive circuit is unchanged.
All substantive mathematical claims remain subject to ordinary review;
the included internal independent reviews are not external peer review.

The package uses the target repository's MIT No Attribution license.
Inherited third-party notices retain their original terms. No additional
attribution requirement is imposed by this package.
