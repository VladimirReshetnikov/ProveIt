# Attribution and scope

This is AI-assisted research prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov for
integration into ProveIt. The new contribution comprises the combined
signed-affine front, guard-free horizon argument, discriminant contraction,
their explicit polynomial artifacts, and associated proofs and checks.

The factorial-free arithmetic source, shared signed Pell machinery,
generalized binomial CRT, local C222 calculus, and logical calibration are
inherited from the exact source package preserved in `provenance/`.
All original notices remain in that unmodified archive. The separate
shared-factorial-error incoming package provided the affine-radix precedent
and comparison data. No licensing change to inherited material is proposed.

Several agents contributed separate mathematical and finite-artifact
reviews. These are internal independent checks, not external peer review
or a proof-assistant certification of the complete theorem.

The deliverable establishes ordinary mathematical equivalences using
identified inherited results. It does not decide ZFC consistency or claim
an absolute minimum among all possible Diophantine equations.
