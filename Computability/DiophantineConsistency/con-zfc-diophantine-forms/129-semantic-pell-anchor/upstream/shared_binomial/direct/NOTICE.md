# Attribution and scope

The new compiler composition, independent radix bound, mathematical report,
finite checks and generated equations are prepared for Vladimir
Reshetnikov's ProveIt project. New material is released under the included
MIT No Attribution license. Inherited files retain their original licenses.

The squared-radius factorial fixed-point theorem is inherited from
package105-bootstrap-certificates, direct/proofs/NEW_RESULTS.md Section 2.
The common Pell residue construction is inherited from package108-pell-residues.
The numerical C222 calculus and calibration, Horn-flow source, reciprocal
prime-power CRT, derived label range, simultaneous indexed-Pell compiler,
and rational sum-of-squares contraction are inherited from the frozen
Round 14 packet and the dependencies whose notices it retains.

The independent contraction verifier is copied without modification as
check_contraction.py from the retained Round 14 dependency tree. The new
verify.py wraps it with complete coefficient comparisons and corruption
controls. No global minimum, worldwide priority, formal proof-assistant
endpoint, or determination of consistency is asserted.
