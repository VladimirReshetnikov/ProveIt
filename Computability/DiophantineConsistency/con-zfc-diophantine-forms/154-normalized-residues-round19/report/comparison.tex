\subsection{Few variable circuits and coefficient architectures}
The Round~18 eleven-integer degree/cost pairs were
\[
 (316,133),\quad(308,140),\quad(304,144),\quad(300,148).
\]
They use the same authentic source34 six-port tuple as the retained eleven-integer
control, so degree and live graph cost are directly comparable.
The earlier Round~17 alternatives $(328,130)$, $(340,129)$, $(352,125)$,
and $(398,121)$ remain relevant lower-cost choices. A degree reduction at
higher cost does not dominate them.

The newest Residual bases package reports unrestricted-integer points
with fourteen variables at degree/cost $86/112$ and $94/110$, and thirteen
variables at $156/125$. Their smaller degrees do not make them
improvements in every metric over an eleven-variable equation.

The positive-domain results form another comparison class. Centered residues
reports ten strictly positive variables at cost/degree $75/626$, $76/464$
and $77/460$. Coprime products reports ten-positive points $76/626$,
$77/464$ and $80/382$, fourteen-positive points $70/189$ and $71/157$,
and a thirteen-positive point $72/433$. Symmetric remainders gives ten-positive points $77/626$, $78/464$
and $79/460$. These are reported compact metrics from the inspected packages;
the direct coefficient recollection does not itself verify their unbounded
positive-domain theorems.

The Common remainders package also supplies a coherent radix47 native
coefficient tuple totaling 207214 raw bytes. This concerns a different
native encoding. It is not a license to replace one or more ports in the
source34 tuple while retaining its proof. A new coherent specialization
requires the complete encoding and calibration bridge. The present scalar
proof uses its own authentic unchanged six-port tuple throughout.

\subsection{Limits of the comparison}
The comparison universe is the named pinned artifacts and prior releases,
not all published or possible Diophantine equations. The tables report
achieved objects; they do not establish global dimension minima, arithmetic
circuit lower bounds, coefficient-optimality, or literature-wide priority.
Separate audits in this workflow are AI-assisted independent reasoning and
implementations, not outside human peer review. No upstream commit, pull
request or publication is part of preparing these artifacts.
