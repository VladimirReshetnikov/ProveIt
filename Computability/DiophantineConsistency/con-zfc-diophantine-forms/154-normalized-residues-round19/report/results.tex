\section{Results and interpretation}
\label{sec:results}
The main direct equation is now an explicit quartic in 244 unrestricted
integer variables, with 176 quadratic residuals, 5212 collected monomials
and coefficient height 3968. Compared with the newest incoming
252-variable common-remainder source, it improves all four varying
measures. Compared with Round~18's 258-variable principal equation, it
saves fourteen variables, ten residuals and 152 monomials while
lowering coefficient height from 128446 to 3968.

The equation is fully specified by its finite residual array and its
collected coefficient array. Appendix~\ref{sec:residuals} prints all 176
residuals. It contains no variable exponent, factorial, binomial operator,
unspecified coefficient or external predicate. Those notions occur only
in the proof explaining its integer zeros.

All five have exact degree four on $\Z$. The 243-variable choice is much
denser, and the height-3840 choice nearly doubles the balanced support.
They are genuine tradeoffs, not simultaneous improvements in every metric.
The sparse choice retains 4770 terms while improving variables, rows and
height over the incoming Shared bounds principal.

The new height-one transformation changes prescribed square labels to
products of two aliases for the same logical value. It then replaces a
three-coordinate equality cycle by a two-coordinate quartic equality
penalty. A complete nonnegative energy argument preserves the source zero
set; full coefficient collection, including all equality terms, certifies
which particular polarizations retain height one.

The 534-variable point improves both dimension and support over Round~18's
571-variable compact point; the 528-variable alternative trades larger
support for six fewer coordinates. Matched-source replays separately isolate
the effect of polarization from the new arithmetic source.

The scalar analysis distinguishes a limitation of the inherited frame from
an unrestricted lower bound. With every other register fixed, no nonzero
constant radical weight and integral polynomial center can reduce its output
degree below 300. This theorem concerns that exact family only, not all
eleven-variable polynomials or other arithmetic frames.

The logical meaning is a correspondence with a finite syntactic ZFC
refutation, not an independent assertion that the equations have no roots.
Each new witness implication is proved in both directions. The ordinary
calibration is retained explicitly; finite coefficient checks and successful
program execution do not become a new proof-assistant kernel theorem.
