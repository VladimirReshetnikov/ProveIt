\begin{abstract}
This report develops explicit Diophantine presentations whose unsolvability
is provably equivalent in ZFC to syntactic ZFC consistency through identified
inherited calibrations. A single normalized spherical remainder bound,
a compatible odd-square radix, and certified monic eliminations give a
balanced quartic in 244 unrestricted integers with 5212 collected monomials
and coefficient height 3968. Companion equations trade dimension, sparsity
and height. Polarizing same-logical square aliases removes equality dummies
from coefficient-height-one quartics. A fixed-frame degree theorem identifies
a precise obstruction to further scalar improvement by center changes alone.
Complete new witness arguments, literal coefficient arrays, exact finite
certificates and a reproducible offline package accompany the report.
All domains and comparisons with Round~18 and the newest pinned inputs are
explicit. No proof of consistency, global minimality or new end-to-end
formal-kernel calibration is claimed.
\end{abstract}
