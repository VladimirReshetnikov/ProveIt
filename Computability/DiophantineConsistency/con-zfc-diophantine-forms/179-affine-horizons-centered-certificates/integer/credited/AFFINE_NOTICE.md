# Attribution and proof scope

This contribution was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov using AI-assisted mathematical research, code generation and separately conducted checks. Independent reviews refer to separate agents and checking implementations within that workflow; they are not external human peer review.

The inherited factorial-free packet is retained under `inherited_factorial_free/`, including its source notices and MIT No Attribution license. Its original files are unchanged. The ProveIt source snapshot and all seven incoming archive identities are recorded in `PROVENANCE.json`.

Inherited components include the C222 numerical proof calculus, the C222/ZF/ZFC calibration, local rule encoding, factorial-free binomial CRT architecture, signed Pell power graphs, shared orientation framework, normalized remainder construction, cofinal parity choices, and earlier polynomial factoring and affine reductions.

New contributions are the compatible affine radix/beta/horizon bootstrap, the linear selector offset justified by an LCM bound, the 33-coordinate frame for 37 affine atoms, the difference of orientation errors, the smaller growth threshold, their composition proofs, and the frozen 239/238/237-variable endpoints with exact certificates. Elementary LCM estimates are classical; the report does not claim their invention. Novelty is asserted only as a new contribution relative to the inspected repository snapshot, not as an exhaustive literature priority claim.

The new theorem is supported by ordinary mathematical proofs composed with the retained logical calibration. Finite polynomial data were checked exactly. This packet does not supply a fresh end-to-end Lean/Rocq proof or a newly kernel-checked object-language ZFC derivation, does not decide consistency, and does not establish a global minimum for Diophantine simplicity.

All new original material is distributed under the root MIT No Attribution license. Upstream attributions and notices remain applicable to the retained material.
