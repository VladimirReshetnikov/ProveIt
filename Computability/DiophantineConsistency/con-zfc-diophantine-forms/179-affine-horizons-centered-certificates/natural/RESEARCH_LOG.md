# Research record

The starting point was the completed natural202 round21 theorem, including
its three-wire q-power aggregate and the restored inverse-power orientation.
An exact rational elimination found homogeneous quadratic rank 154 among its
154 rows; a mere constant linear dependency was not available.

A naive aggregate of modular remainders was not adopted: divisibility of a
sum of squared errors by the shared binomial modulus does not authenticate
each residue without an additional bound or prime-power argument. The
q-power aggregate's huge-Pell-modulus proof cannot simply be copied there.

Gate-only translations reduced height modestly. A more useful search found
that nonnegative affine accumulator coordinates dramatically sparse the
syntax rows. Twelve elementary shears were selected, with no semantic
coordinate translated. Exact natural restoration follows from the original
acyclic positive gate equations, not from a globally positive affine inverse.
The literal pre-basis chart202 is frozen at 9,652 terms/H13,654,244.

Residual-basis refinements are separately certified. Their search is only a
finite heuristic selection; no optimality or global lower bound is claimed.
The optional 200-variable degree-eight companion is an explicitly labeled
tradeoff obtained by eliminating the two linearization witnesses; it is not
a quartic-dimension advance.
