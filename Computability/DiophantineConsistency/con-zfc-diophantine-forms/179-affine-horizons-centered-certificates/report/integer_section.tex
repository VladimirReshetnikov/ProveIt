\subsection*{Pooling with absolute bounds}

$|n|,|m_i|,|k_i|<W$, with canonical signed Pell pairs $X_i,Y_i$ at $A$ and
retained gates $Z_i=AY_i$.  Write
\[
\begin{split}
 T&=2An-n^2-1,\\

\end{split}
\]

\[
 K-2H-(2A-n)L=0,\qquad S-nK+L=0.\tag{PP}

\[
