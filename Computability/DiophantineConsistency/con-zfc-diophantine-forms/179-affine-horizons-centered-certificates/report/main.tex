\begin{abstract}
We give explicit polynomial equations whose lack of solutions in their
specified domains is provably equivalent in ZFC to syntactic consistency
of ZFC. The direct integer construction combines an affine proof horizon
with a larger cofinal Pell frame and exact private-coordinate elimination.
For natural variables, triangular changes of gate coordinates preserve
nonnegative solutions while sharply reducing collected support and
coefficient height. Centering a positive-domain carrier at a polynomial
approximation to its extracted square root lowers its degree; a signed
unsquared trace also improves two fourteen-integer circuits.
The report supplies complete formulas, constructive witness maps,
ordinary proofs and reproducible finite artifacts. It includes a separately
calibrated pinned Start-marker4177 coefficient specialization and a general
lemma for separating dependent radicals. No consistency decision, global
optimum or new end-to-end kernel certificate is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Objective and comparison boundary}
For a fixed polynomial $P\in\Z[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ and its specified domain $D$,
our target is
\[
\mathrm{ZFC}\vdash
\bigl(\neg\exists\boldsymbol{x}\in D^n\;P(\boldsymbol{x})=0\bigr)
\longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\]
The proofs establish solvability equivalent to a finite syntactic
refutation, without asserting either consistency or a solution.
The domains $\Z$, $\N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and $\Z_{>0}$ are essential
parts of the individual statements.

It includes the new fixed-base modular, integral projection, reciprocal
pooling, quotient-carrier and pooled-projection packets, as well as our
preceding release. The baseline audit independently recollects the literal
quartics and distinguishes placed work from incoming archives.
In particular, the incoming direct quartic already reaches $216$ integer
variables. The $215$-variable reciprocal companion has degree ten and must
not be confused with a quartic.

Its free-quotient circuit at $87$ operations and degree $214$ superseded

Complexity measures are complete quantified variable count, exact total
degree, quadratic residual count, collected support, maximum absolute
coefficient, and live binary operation count. Addition, subtraction and
multiplication each cost one, including squares and scalar multiplication;
reuse is free. Circuit counts use integer leaves, not free rational
constants. Fixed coefficients are not unknowns, but their complete coherent
numerical tuple is part of every equation. These measures are not bit
complexity and do not define a unique globally simplest equation.

\section{Reproduction and mathematical scope}
The direct equations are sums of the squares of literal residual lists.
Their complete collected coefficients and readable expressions are supplied.
The compact equations are fully specified polynomial circuits with all
fixed numeral payloads and reconstruction recipes. Proof-only factorials,
Pell sequences, fractions, inequalities and variable exponents are not
hidden primitives of any final polynomial.

The unbounded proofs establish sign recovery, divisibility, cofinal
completeness, source restoration and domain preservation. Independent finite
checkers bind them to actual rows and gates, full leading forms, coefficient
bytes and inverse identities. Finite test cases supplement those proofs;
they do not replace them. The portable replay runs in independent temporary
copies under both normal and optimized Python and verifies that all sealed
bytes remain unchanged. The final extracted ZIP is replayed separately.

Logical equivalence retains the specified numerical-calculus or native
compiler and syntactic-refutation calibration. These inherited interfaces
are explicit, not inferred merely from numerical coefficient bounds. No
transitive proof-assistant build, encoded object-language ZFC derivation,
consistency decision or global lower bound is claimed. The independent
reviews are separate checks in this AI-assisted workflow, not outside
human endorsements. No upstream repository is modified.

The Start-marker4177 tuple has its own complete native proof, physical
support and finite controller certificate. Zero coefficient weight does
not mean a physical marker may be removed. Its four ports are not mixed
with earlier specializations; storage endianness and compression metadata
are distinguished from the mathematical integers.
