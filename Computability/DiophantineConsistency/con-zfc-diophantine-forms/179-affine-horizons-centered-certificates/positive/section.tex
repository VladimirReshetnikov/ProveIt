\section{Constructive strictly positive completeness}

outer data, ratio construction, strong period seed, and unsquared-trace
auxiliary construction in the retained parent proof. In particular $V>0$.
Keep every independent input except $n$ and set
\[
 n_{\rm new}=n_{\rm old}+g_0V.
\]

\[
 n_{\rm new}-V(bB+g_0)=n_{\rm old}-bVB.
\]

no extra period/cofinal choice or new size estimate is needed.
