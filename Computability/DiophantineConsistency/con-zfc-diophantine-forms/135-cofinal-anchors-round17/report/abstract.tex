\begin{abstract}
This report gives explicit polynomial equations whose unsolvability is equivalent to syntactic ZFC consistency, relative to the identified inherited ordinary calibrations. A cofinal semantic Pell anchor and strict quotient bounds yield natural-domain quartics with 229 or 230 variables; a 228-variable companion trades a much larger coefficient height for one fewer coordinate. A separate signed construction removes front sign guards only after an orientation-first recovery argument, yielding quartics in 282, 287 or 309 unrestricted integers with coefficient height 128446. Incidence-local alias contraction, integral affine refactorization and a quartic two-alias equality polynomial give height-one quartics in 793, 806 or 994 unrestricted integers, with explicit bijective witness maps. Finally, a linear cofactor saturation gives eleven-integer equations at exact degree and operation pairs 328/130, 340/129, 352/125 and 398/121. These are different domain-specific Pareto objectives, not a single simplicity optimum. Complete new unbounded proofs, literal row and coefficient arrays, arithmetic graphs, authentic numerical ports, deterministic builders and independent finite checkers accompany the report. Neither consistency nor a new end-to-end proof-assistant endpoint is asserted.
\end{abstract}
