# Reuse and provenance

New mathematical research text, literal polynomial data and independently
authored checkers are supplied for incorporation into ProveIt. Inherited
source material retains its original notices and licenses; see
`integer_system_audit/licenses/`, the free-carrier baseline licenses, and
component source manifests. `PROVENANCE.json` identifies byte-exact members
of the eight reviewed archives. The main repository pin is
9cca523d9f27ad39d52993f4065cbd7e58f8bdd4; earlier calibration snapshots retain
their own explicit pins rather than being mislabeled as new main files.

No complete third-party copyrighted article is redistributed. The JSWW 1976
article is cited and hashed as audit evidence only. No incoming executable
is run by the provided replay. The primary mathematical equivalence depends
on the explicitly named inherited source, compiler and Pell theorems. New
Lean proposals are clearly uncompiled, and are not kernel certificates.
