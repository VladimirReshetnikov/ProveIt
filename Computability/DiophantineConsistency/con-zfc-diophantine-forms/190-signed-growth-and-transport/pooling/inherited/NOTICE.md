## New work in this packet

The new direct work is the completed oriented rational-integrality theorem
for up to five bases and seventeen power requests, including temporary
zero bases, the simultaneous bootstrap, three literal grouping variants,
and the twelve supplied acyclic unit-pivot projections. Exact circuits,
reconstruction maps, proof arguments and independent finite checkers are
provided. The scalar constants in the direct circuits are small; expanded
coefficient bounds are reported separately.

The compact work combines the inverse-Lucas correction with the unsquared
strong trace, proves the square-transport estimate left open by the trace
source, and introduces the integral root center with a constructive cofinal
positive lift. It includes the degree-224 circuit, exact cancellation and
leading form, independent finite checks, and reconstruction of the actual
fixed coefficient tuple.

## What has and has not been established

The supplied new arguments are ordinary mathematical proofs. The finite
checkers authenticate exact algebra, literal circuits, coefficient identities,
degree and height certificates, and integral projection maps. They do not
replace the unbounded signed-Pell, positivity, or logical calibration proofs.

The direct consistency corollary uses the named inherited interfaces
`KEndpoint.c222_iff_zf_refutation` and
`ConstructibleChoice.zf_refutation_iff_zfc_refutation`, together with the
ordinary arithmetic and constructor-recoding proofs. The semantic
calibration uses completeness and model constructions; no newly implemented
primitive-recursive translator for the whole calibration is claimed.

The full transitive historical proof and Lean import closure is not bundled
or freshly compiled here. No new end-to-end Lean/Rocq theorem, encoded
object-language ZFC derivation, or consistency proof is claimed. Separate
AI-assisted mathematical reviews are not external human peer review.

Comparisons are to identified repository constructions at the pinned
snapshot. No global minimum, universally best profile, or literature-wide
priority is asserted. Domains, degree, coefficient measures and operation
counts remain separate parts of every comparison.
