\begin{abstract}
We give explicitly specified ordinary polynomials whose nonsolvability, in
their stated domains, is provably equivalent in ZFC to syntactic
\(\Con(\ZFC)\). A signed affine horizon, followed by a divisibility-justified
rescaling, yields a quartic in 209 unrestricted integers. A separate
construction combines a free quotient with an integer polynomial center
and a new transport bound to obtain degree 190 in ten positive integers.
Three compositions with oriented pooling give further unrestricted-integer
tradeoffs. Every equation is accompanied by its literal coefficient or
circuit data, constructive reductions, fixed-source provenance, and
reproducible exact checks. We also identify why a tempting additional
variable elimination fails: it limits the capacity of the proof-state
encoding. The results improve specified constructions and do not establish
a global minimum for Diophantine consistency equations.
\end{abstract}
