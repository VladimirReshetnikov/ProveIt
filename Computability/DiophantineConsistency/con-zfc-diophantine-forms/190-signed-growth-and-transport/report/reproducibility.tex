\section{Logical calibration and the proof boundary}
\label{sec:boundary}

The direct quartic and pooled constructions encode the numerical calculus
$\Ccal$.  Their new arithmetic proofs establish, in both directions,
equivalence between an integer zero and a finite refutation in that
calculus.  The final logical step uses the repository's explicitly named
inherited endpoints
\begin{quote}\small
\path{KEndpoint.c222_iff_zf_refutation},\\
\path{ConstructibleChoice.zf_refutation_iff_zfc_refutation}.
\end{quote}
The first identifies numerical-calculus refutability with the fixed ZF
refutation presentation; the second transfers refutability between the
identified ZF and ZFC presentations.  Together they supply the stated
syntactic ZFC endpoint.  The arithmetic reductions in this article preserve
that endpoint rather than introducing a new interpretation of
$\Con(\ZFC)$.

The ten-positive-variable construction has a different inherited boundary.
It retains the entire authenticated BFS943/radix67 coefficient tuple and
the ordinary native/compiler/refutation calibration used by source 172.
The selected ordinary proof closure is in
\path{compact/source/upstream172/}.  Its
\path{inherited/strict/upstream_lucas/parent/proof_sources/} directory
contains the Pell classification and signed-index arguments in
\path{universal_pell.tex}, the uniform projector in
\path{uniform_ratio.md}, the named source theorem in
\path{scaled_native.md}, and the coherent-tuple calibration in
\path{indexed_native.md}.  The local source ledger
\path{compact/source/SELECTED_PROOF_SOURCES.json} and
\path{compact/SOURCE_LINKS.json} identify those retained texts and their
immutable repository sources.  Reconstructing the coefficient bytes
authenticates which instance is used; it does not independently prove its
machine or native calibration.

Three kinds of evidence must therefore be distinguished.  The new
unbounded proofs establish sign recovery, the cofinal existence of
witnesses, growth, exact-power interpretation, and the arithmetic
restorations.  Finite exact checks authenticate the literal equations,
substitutions, fixed numerals, degree certificates, and measured size
claims.  The named inherited calibrations supply the logical meaning of
the numerical or native endpoint.  Neither finite sampling nor successful
coefficient reconstruction replaces an unbounded proof.

All new deductions are ordinary mathematics formalizable in ZFC.  This
package does not supply a newly compiled proof object for the complete
chain, rebuild the entire historical Lean/Rocq import closure, or emit an
object-language ZFC derivation.  Historical replay statements inside
preserved source documents belong to their original releases.  The new
internal reviews, including
\path{reviews/QUARTIC_ALGEBRA_REVIEW.md} and
\path{reviews/SIGNED_HORIZON_SECOND_REVIEW.md}, are AI-assisted mathematical
checks, not external human peer review.  No consistency assumption is
used to select a coefficient or witness family.

\section{Artifacts and reproducibility}
\label{sec:reproduce}

The JSON specifications are complete finite definitions of the
polynomials.  Named gates are shared expressions, not additional
quantified variables.  The principal artifact locations are as follows.
\begin{itemize}
\item The two quartic directories are
  \path{quartic/equations/affine209_F64/} and
  \path{quartic/equations/affine209_F128_basis/}.
  Each contains \path{equation.json},
  \path{expanded_polynomial.json}, \path{residuals.txt},
  \path{metrics.json}, and \path{certificate.json}.
  The exact affine-source and scaling argument is in
  \path{quartic/ALGEBRA_PROOF.md}.
\item The principal pooled circuits are
  \path{pooling/equations/integer116_degree16_staged.json} and
  \path{pooling/equations/integer107_degree32_all4.json}.
  The degree-12 companion is
  \path{pooling/equations/integer130_degree12_staged.json}.
  Their literal source bindings and acyclic restoration certificates are
  under \path{pooling/sources/} and \path{pooling/certificates/};
  \path{pooling/PROOF.md} gives the unbounded arithmetic argument.
\item The compact circuit is \path{compact/equation.json}, with its proof
  in \path{compact/PROOF.md}.  All center and norm-defect coefficients are
  recorded in \path{compact/exact_center_polynomials.json}.
  The four complete fixed numerical payloads are under
  \path{compact/source/coefficients/};
  \path{compact/source/rebuild_coefficients.py} reconstructs them by the
  printed finite recipe.  They are not represented only by checksums.
\end{itemize}

Python 3.10 or later and its standard library suffice for the exact
checks.  From the extracted package root, run
\begin{lstlisting}
python3 -B verify_all.py
\end{lstlisting}
The component entry points can also be run separately:
\begin{lstlisting}
python3 -B quartic/verify.py
python3 -B pooling/verify.py
python3 -B compact/verify.py
\end{lstlisting}
The same component commands accept Python's \path{-O} switch before the
script name; their acceptance conditions do not depend on removable
\path{assert} statements.  The root verifier and retained receipts state
which checks were actually replayed.

The quartic verifier independently expands each coordinate substitution
and residual-basis identity, collects the sums of squares, and checks
every coefficient, support count, and height-attaining quartic monomial.
The pooling verifier checks the actual request/frame bindings, cleared
denominator identities, and acyclic polynomial restoration maps; exact
nonzero homogeneous specializations certify degree.  The compact verifier
imports no circuit producer: it checks all center gates, the complete norm
defect, the actual highest form, and the four fixed numerals by independent
finite reconstruction.  Nonzero modular derivatives certify genuine
occurrence of the circuit inputs.  These modular checks are finite
algebraic certificates, not evidence for or against an integer zero.

The main article source is \path{report/main.tex}; every included
\path{.tex} fragment is supplied.  With a standard TeX Live installation,
change into the \path{report/} directory and run
\begin{lstlisting}
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
\end{lstlisting}
PDF metadata can vary between TeX installations.  The literal equation
data, integer coefficients, and exact certificates are the stable
mathematical definitions.

For repository integration, retain this package as an additive submission
and cite the specific profile, domain, degree, and cost being compared.
Preserve the source notices and the distinction between the two calibration
routes.  The results support the measured comparisons in this article;
they do not establish a global simplicity minimum or historical priority.
