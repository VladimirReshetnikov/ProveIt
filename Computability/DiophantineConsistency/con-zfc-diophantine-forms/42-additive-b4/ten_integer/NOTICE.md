# Attribution and scope

This mathematical synthesis and its new code were prepared with AI
assistance for the ProveIt research program. They make no assertion of
human authorship of generated material and no claim of publication priority.

The new work is the B4 source-code adapter, its complete ordinary proof,
the explicit finite-field/exhaustive code certificate, and the generated
and checked ten-integer instance with reduced degree.

Bose-Chowla additive codes are classical. Sun's 2021 explicit source
construction and his 2026 preprint ten-integer reduction are attributed
in PROOF.md. The relation-combining norm comes from Matiyasevich and
Robinson as presented by Sun. The local quintic, positivity and separating
weight improvements are inherited from ProveIt's package 31; they are
not new claims of this adapter.

The circuit infrastructure, coefficient recipes, parent quartic,
compressed four-port tuple, bridge formulas and much of the finite
verification code are adapted from the package `31-sun10/signed10` at
commit 22e75dd6b87b1b199bb08c778d7cf075ae5aae08. SOURCE_PINS.json records
that origin and the changes. Earlier compiler, machine and source-coding
contributions retain their notices in NOTICE.upstream31.md,
upstream_NOTICE.md, LICENSE.upstream31 and licenses/. Third-party material
is not relicensed by this notice.
