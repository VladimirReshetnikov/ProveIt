# Attribution, scope, and reuse

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt research program with AI assistance,
23 October 2026. The new mathematical contributions presented here are:

1. A fully explicit B4-coded source adapter for the inherited ten-integer
   consistency equation, including its reverse implication and exact circuit.
2. The nonzero-edge-word occupancy argument, shorter native support geometry,
   complete fixed coefficient tuple, and actual-rank controller audit.
3. Concrete positive equation/system bindings, independent checks, and the
   accompanying synthesis and reproducible report.

The Bose–Chowla construction, Sun's universal Diophantine bridge, Pell
machinery, the K215/refutation calibration, native compiler and Wang language,
the positive arithmetic forms, and the package-31 outer finalizer are inherited.
Their authorship and sources remain identified in the report and source
provenance files. Worldwide novelty or priority is not established by this work.

The complete inherited machine and compiler attribution, license notices,
and original source licenses are retained under the subpackage license
directories. In particular, the original machine credits and Wade license
must accompany their redistributed data and derivatives.

No new license restriction is imposed on this report or the newly written
contribution. Retained upstream material remains subject to its original
license terms. This note does not replace those terms or change ownership.

Historical source copies describe their original package and may contain
relative paths or reproduction commands that are not active entry points in
this archive. The top-level README and reproduce.py identify active commands.
Local copies may have a normalized final newline; local content hashes and
upstream Git blob identifiers are distinguished in the provenance records.

These are proofs of equivalence with consistency, not proofs of consistency.
The ordinary proof is explicit about inherited mathematical dependencies.
Finite Python audits are not represented as a new end-to-end proof-assistant
certificate.
