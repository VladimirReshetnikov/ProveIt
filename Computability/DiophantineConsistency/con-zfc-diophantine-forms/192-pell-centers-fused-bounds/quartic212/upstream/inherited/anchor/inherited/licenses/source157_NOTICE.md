# Attribution and proof scope

This research contribution was prepared with AI assistance for Vladimir
Reshetnikov and the ProveIt repository on 25 October 2026.

The new contribution comprises the joint choices F=t^t, R=F, an even
actual index chart, a universally nonunit affine majorant, an affine odd
beta multiplier, and the proofs connecting those choices to the retained
source. It also includes the emitted smaller polynomials, the frozen
additional integer row reductions, the new report, and independent finite
checks of the changed construction.

The immediate parent is the incoming report "Removing factorial
authenticity: binomial prime-power certificates for Diophantine consistency"
from ProveIt commit 7a8da414376a80b90ecf799f37ceb26fb9ac674e. Its files and
notices are retained under inherited/factorial_free. The numerical C222
calculus, logical calibration, Pell/frame interface, generalized binomial
CRT compiler, normalized remainder construction, Horn factoring identities,
and six affine exposure shears are inherited. The original sources and
their licenses remain identifiable.

The LCM and coefficient arguments are elementary mathematical statements;
this release claims their useful application in this specific construction,
not literature-wide priority for the underlying facts.

The separate mathematical and finite algebra reviews were performed in
the AI-assisted workflow. They are not represented as external human
peer review. Finite diagnostics do not replace unbounded proofs. The
complete transitive proof-assistant environment of the inherited logical
interfaces was not rebuilt. The new proof is an ordinary mathematical
argument formalizable in ZFC, not a newly emitted formal proof object.

The inherited MIT No Attribution license is retained in LICENSE. Original
subtree notices and license files are preserved.
