# Sources and authorship

This package was prepared 25 October 2026 for Vladimir Reshetnikov and ProveIt
with AI assistance. The first-Pell-root quotient reduction, its detailed
proof, the reverse-discovery digit representation, and the new checks were
developed in this research session.

The fixed action language, native tuple, current parent equation, and inherited
consistency calibration come from VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt at commit
02a78a08603cbae248c19c23a75907c6c6bd6443. Exact immediate source paths and
SHA256 values are in sources/SOURCE_PINS.json.

The package retains ProveIt's MIT No Attribution license, the inherited
Wade MIT No Attribution license, and the K215 attribution notice in licenses/.
The new material is offered under the same MIT No Attribution terms.

The uniform positive P66 proof copied from source174 describes a historical
coefficient specialization. It is used through its uniform arithmetic
interface, together with the complete current admissible942 proof. Those
distinct coefficient tuples are not identified.

Mixed-radix target packing was previously investigated in the repository's
research/mixed-radix-bfs-seed work. Source170 describes related quotient
carriers. The report states precisely how the new constructions differ;
neither general idea is claimed as new here.

The new and inherited reviews in this archive are internal AI-assisted
research checks. They are not external human peer reviews or endorsements.
