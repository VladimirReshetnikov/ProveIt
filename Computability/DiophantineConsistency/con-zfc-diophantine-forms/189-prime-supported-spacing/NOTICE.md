# Origin and scope

This release was prepared with AI assistance for Vladimir Reshetnikov.
The new contribution is the prime-supported Pell index construction,
the reuse of the binomial selector as beta spacing, and its literal
209-variable implementation and verification.

The immediate parent archive is preserved without modification under
`inherited/ConZFC_Pell_Growth_210_2026-10-25.zip`. It includes the direct
C222 arithmetic sources, earlier arithmetic proofs and notices, and selected
logical calibration sources. Its own inherited licenses and notices remain
inside the archive. The standalone parent equation is copied byte-for-byte.

The polynomial changes are specified in `equations/transformation.json`.
The parent residual-row shear is undone before replacing the beta row;
all other unchanged coefficients are retained. Semantic changes are proved
in the article, not asserted to follow merely from coefficient equality.

The reports were independently reviewed by separate AI tasks. That review
is not described as external human peer review. No global minimality,
worldwide novelty, formal proof-assistant endpoint, or consistency proof
is claimed.
