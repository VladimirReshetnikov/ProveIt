\begin{abstract}
We give explicit, fixed-integer-coefficient polynomial equations whose
absence of solutions is provably equivalent in ZFC to its syntactic
consistency, using the repository's established K215/compiler calibration.
The principal new step replaces a full Sidon condition in quadratic
digit coding by isolation only of monomials actually used by the source
equations. A certified list of 32 labels in $[0,200]$ isolates all 71
active pairs, although many of the other 528 pairs collide. Compared with
the wave-6 constructions at the pinned source commit, this gives nine
positive unknowns at degree $32\,568\,568$ in 652 binary arithmetic
operations, and ten unrestricted integer unknowns at degree $59\,564\,744$
in 653 operations. The latter uses Sun's cited ten-integer bridge.
A separate root estimate permits index-quotient absorption and the
removal of an inner radical separator to coexist: eleven positive
unknowns then suffice at degree 990 in 117 operations, or degree 994
in 115 operations. A small, historically qualified circuit refinement
also attains degree 44 with fifteen positive unknowns in 76 operations.
All coefficients, arithmetic circuits, finite certificates, reconstruction
scripts and source notices accompany the article. These are ordinary
mathematical proofs with explicit inherited interfaces, not a new
end-to-end proof-assistant certificate, a proof of consistency, or a
claim of absolute minimality.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, baseline and the exact claim}

\subsection{What ``simplest'' can mean}
Variable count, degree, evaluator length and coefficient size are different
objectives. No known argument identifies a globally simplest consistency
equation under all of them. This package constructs improvements with
precise, comparable metrics. The underlying logical assertion is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:logicaltarget}
 \ZFC\vdash
 \bigl(\neg\exists\mathbf x\in\mathcal A\;P(\mathbf x)=0\bigr)
 \ \longleftrightarrow\ \Con(\ZFC),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal A$ is stated separately for every polynomial. Here
$\N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, and $\Con(\ZFC)$ means that the repository's
fixed, recursively specified ZFC proof calculus has no finite derivation
of falsity. No assertion is made that a positive-domain polynomial has
the same meaning over all integers.

The source is pinned to \pin. This commit places wave 6 beside wave 5;
the collection README at that commit still principally describes wave 5.
Consequently the comparison uses the actual new packages 38--45 as well
as the active research documents. A comparison only with wave 5 would
substantially overstate the improvements \cite{repo,package38,package40,package44,package45}.

The nine-variable degree decreases by approximately $74.92\%$ and its

approximately $74.92\%$ and its operation count by $16.50\%$ against
package 40. These are substantial simplifications of explicit instances,
not new general nine- or ten-variable representation theorems. The
historical 67-operation equation remains shorter when evaluator length
alone is the objective. The new constructions do not decrease the
known variable-count bounds.

\subsection{Proof layers and what was checked}
The mathematical composition has four layers:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item the literal K215 machine halts on blank input exactly when there is
 a finite ZFC refutation;
\item its marked-tile/native-window/Pell compiler gives the fixed positive
 source equations and coherent coefficient tuple;
\item the new support-aware coding or the new eleven-variable root
 estimate transforms those equations;
\item the existing Jones ratio or Sun Lucas bridge, where used, and an
 explicit finalizer give a single polynomial.
\end{enumerate}
Layers 1 and 2 are inherited mathematical dependencies. Their source
proofs and immutable repository references are identified in the archive.
The new local arguments are proved here. The finite programs check
literal coefficient reconstruction, symbolic extraction identities,
arithmetic circuits, liveness and degree certificates. They cannot replace
the unbounded compiler arguments or decide either side of
\eqref{eq:logicaltarget}.

The repository separately contains an unconditional Lean equivalence with
a nine-natural-variable polynomial selected through
\texttt{Classical.choose}. That milestone has different coefficient
explicitness from the materialized constructions here. A Lean theorem
about a syntactic proof predicate also differs from an already checked
derivation inside the object-language ZFC calculus. We do not conflate
these claims or claim a new Lean/Rocq build \cite{active}.

\subsection{Exact coefficients and operation convention}
All constructions use the \emph{whole} package-31 coefficient tuple
$(\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F)$, where $M_F$ is already shifted. Its bit lengths are
\[
1064921,\quad1070004,\quad1064921,\quad1064922.
\]
The four raw payloads total 533099 bytes; their gzip encodings total
10485 bytes. They are independently reconstructed from the finite recipe
in Appendix~\ref{sec:coefficients}. The recipe does not search for a
refutation or depend on a truth value of consistency.

An operation is one binary addition, subtraction or multiplication of a
variable-dependent expression, including multiplication by a numeral.
Reuse and fixed coefficient leaves are free. Fixed powers are expanded
into charged multiplication gates. Arithmetic involving only fixed
constants is part of coefficient construction, as in the compared
packages. Equality comparisons are free when reporting systems. The
literal DAGs, rather than uncharged abbreviations, determine all counts.
