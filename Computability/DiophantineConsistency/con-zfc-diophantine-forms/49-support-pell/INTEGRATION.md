## Logical and formal status

The theorem claimed is an equivalence inside ZFC, by ordinary mathematics
formalizable there, between absence of solutions in each explicitly stated
domain and syntactic `Con(ZFC)`. The construction neither proves consistency
nor produces a refutation.

The compiler/K215 calibration and the applicable Jones or Sun bridge are
explicit inherited dependencies. The new proofs establish the local
coding, root-estimate, and finalizer transformations. The finite scripts
audit coefficient reconstruction, source supports, exact identities,
literal arithmetic schedules, live gates and ports, and degree
certificates. Finite computations do not certify the unbounded compiler
semantics or either truth value of consistency.

No new Lean/Rocq build, end-to-end kernel-checked explicit-coefficient
theorem, or checked derivation in the repository's object-language ZFC
calculus is claimed. The repository's separate `Classical.choose`
nine-variable Lean theorem has a different coefficient-explicitness status.
Preserve this distinction in the collection README and active research
status documents.

For the short degree-44 polynomial, the multiplier `i` satisfies the
**ordinary** strong equation `(i*c^2)^2 = Delta*(f^2-1)`. Completeness maps a
normalized witness by `i_ordinary = Delta*i_normalized`. Soundness uses the
ordinary strong-rank theorem; it never divides an arbitrary supplied
ordinary multiplier by `Delta`.
