# Attribution and proof scope

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project on 23 October 2026.
This is AI-assisted mathematical and software work. An independent agent
review is not an external human endorsement or a proof-assistant build.

New contributions are the support-separation criterion, its explicit
maximum-weight 200 certificate for the actual quadratic support, the
corresponding nine- and ten-variable instances, and their authored
proofs and finite certificates. The source comparison is pinned to commit
124c07c0b77a9e20e84676514d8b1ff78536ea3f.

The quadratic parent, coherent native tuple, machine calibration,
multi-target carry arrangement, Jones ratio bridge and arithmetic
finalizers are inherited. Their exact attributions appear in
upstream/NOTICE_PACKAGE40.md, upstream/NOTICE_PACKAGE45.md and
coefficients/licenses/. The nine-variable tail is adapted from package45;
the ten-variable tail is adapted from package40 and ultimately package31,
whose general ten-integer reduction is credited to Zhi-Wei Sun.

The code and proof copies in upstream/ are historical provenance and
retain their original numerical claims. They are not the active
definition or current verification driver. Read README.md and
SPARSE_PACKING_PROOF.md for the new instances.

New material is provided for incorporation into ProveIt under the
repository's MIT No Attribution terms. Existing third-party notices and
licenses are preserved and are not replaced by this statement.

The combined archive also contains a new relative-error proof and evaluator
combining package38 quotient absorption with the package44 smaller norm,
and a new 76-operation regrouping of historically existing Pell-root gaps.
The degree-44 and root-gap precedents are explicitly credited in the main
article and short/historical/. Additional source copies are pinned in
upstream/ADDITIONAL_SOURCE_PINS.json. Active documentation and path metadata
are adapted for this self-contained archive; immutable upstream proof and
payload copies retain their original bytes and notices.
