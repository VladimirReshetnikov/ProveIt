\section{Support separation instead of full Sidon separation}

The current wave-6 constructions encode a system of twenty-two quadratic
residuals in thirty-one source coordinates.  Their positional code separates
all $\binom{33}{2}=528$ homogenized quadratic monomials, although only
seventy-one distinct monomials occur in the residuals.  The following
weaker condition is sufficient and admits a substantially shorter explicit
code.

\paragraph{Definition.}
Let $p_1,\ldots,p_r\in\mathbb Z[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ have degree at most two.
Pad a monomial of degree less than two by copies of $X_0=1$, and let
$\mathcal A$ be the union of the resulting supported unordered pairs.
Weights $w_0=0,w_1,\ldots,w_n$ are \emph{support separating} if, for every
$(i,j)\in\mathcal A$, the equality
$w_i+w_j=w_k+w_l$ with $0\le k\le l\le n$ implies $(i,j)=(k,l)$.
No condition is imposed on a sum shared only by unsupported pairs.
Uniqueness merely among supported pairs would be insufficient.

\paragraph{Support extraction theorem.}
Write $W=\max_iw_i$, choose
$E_j=2W+(j-1)(2W+1)$, and put
\[
 p_j(X)=\sum_{|\alpha|\le2}a_{j,\alpha}X^\alpha,\qquad
 c_{j,\alpha}=(2-|\alpha|)!\prod_i\alpha_i!\,a_{j,\alpha}.
\]
Define
\[
 D(Z)=\sum_{j,\alpha}c_{j,\alpha}Z^{E_j-\alpha\cdot w},
 \qquad C_X(Z)=\left(1+\sum_iX_iZ^{w_i}\right)^2.
\]
Then
\[
 [Z^{E_j}]C_X(Z)D(Z)=2p_j(X)\qquad(1\le j\le r).
\]
If every $|c_{j,\alpha}|\le M$, then for nonnegative $X_i$ every
coefficient of $C_XD$ has absolute value at most
$M(1+\sum_iX_i)^2$.

\paragraph{Proof.}
At the sum of a supported padded pair, support separation identifies exactly
one unordered pair in $C_X$. Its multinomial coefficient is canceled by the
factorial in $c_{j,\alpha}$. A contribution from a different row would equate
a nonzero target difference, of absolute value at least $2W+1$, to a
difference of two pair sums, of absolute value at most $2W$. This is
impossible. The reversed row kernels lie in disjoint intervals
$[E_j-2W,E_j]$, so every coefficient of $D$ is bounded by $M$.
The coefficients of $C_X$ are nonnegative even when unsupported pairs
collide, and their sum is $C_X(1)=(1+\sum_iX_i)^2$. Convolution proves the
last bound. This also explains why collisions away from the tested digits
do not create a hidden carry problem.\hfill$\square$

\subsection{The explicit certificate}

In the parent coordinate order
\[
\begin{gathered}
J,F,\alpha,z,f,h,i,o,j,s,w,\tau,\eta,\zeta,y,\gamma,\\
N,E,P,c,a,X,D,C_2,L,F_2,V,g,I,B_{\mathrm{aux}},\Delta,
\end{gathered}
\]
take the weights
\[
\begin{split}
(w_1,\ldots,w_{31})={}&(200,199,193,166,11,57,125,37,93,113,153,16,\\
&158,192,120,64,139,198,144,23,15,43,18,165,179,\\
&112,10,44,117,71,119).
\end{split}
\]
The exhaustive certificate finds $285$ distinct sums among $528$ pairs,
with $130$ collided sum positions and maximum multiplicity seven.
Nevertheless all $71$ supported pairs are uniquely represented among all
$528$ pairs. Thus this is deliberately not a Sidon set. The literal vector
and exhaustive check, rather than its heuristic discovery method, certify
the construction.

The largest weight is $W=200$, compared with $799$ in the inherited code.
For $r=22$, the targets are $400+401j$ for $0\le j\le21$, their maximum
is $8821$, and the product support bound is $9221$. The exact symbolic
audit checks the forty-four extraction identities for the two inherited
parent coordinate conventions, with all four native ports left symbolic.
Those parents differ by the explicitly checked transport shift
$z_{\rm old}=z+1$; their supports coincide.

\subsection{The resulting equations and exact metrics}

The nine-variable source uses the existing coherent package-31 tuple,
$b=2^{2134939}+\epsilon$, $B=b^3$,
$N_0=B^{201}$, $N_1=4B^{9222}$ and $N=N_0N_1$.
Its coefficient kernel is the $D(B)$ above. All carry bounds and the
numerical Jones ratio hypotheses are preserved by the support-extraction
theorem. The inherited finalizer remains the polynomial
\[
 F_9=\bigl(\Theta^2-\Omega^2(a_0+V_0^2a_1)\bigr)^2
       -4\Omega^4V_0^2a_0a_1.
\]
Its full arithmetic registers and fixed coefficient recipes appear in
the accompanying proof and literal division-free DAG. They are
abbreviations, not additional unknowns. The positive domain uses the
usual successor translations of $\epsilon,\phi,n$; it must not be
reinterpreted as an equation over unrestricted integers.

With $t=\deg N=3(8821+3\cdot200+2)=28269$, the inherited exact
cancellation analysis gives
\[
 \deg F_9=1152t+12W+280=32\,568\,568.
\]
The diagonal leading coefficient is $2^{2050}\ne0$. The cancellation
is resolved by the exact identity
\[
\begin{split}
 V_1^2-DFI={}&(C^2-1)(AE)^2(GH)^2\\
 &+D(E^2-1)(GH)^2+DF(H^2-1),
\end{split}
\]
which is also checked by symbolic coefficient comparison.
The primary positive evaluator has $652$ live binary operations;
the translated natural evaluator has $655$.

The unchanged Sun finalizer with the corresponding package-40 source
produces a ten-unrestricted-integer companion of exact degree
