\section{A literal finite recipe for the fixed coefficient tuple}\label{sec:coefficients}

The following restates the package-31 coefficient recipe in a form that
can be reconstructed independently of its original builder
\cite{package31}. All the numbers in this appendix are fixed constants,
not additional Diophantine unknowns. The attached literal table contains
943 ordered Wang tiles. Its first two entries are
$(2,435,3,1)$ and $(5,0,0,0)$; the remaining entries are sorted
lexicographically. Each entry lists west, north, east and south labels,
all in $\{0,\ldots,511\}$. The full finite table is part of the exact
specification, at \file{coefficients/upstream/wang943_tiles.json}.

Set
\[
 \rho=2^{121},\quad L_c=8801,\quad d_c=121\cdot8801=1064921,
 \quad B_c=2^{d_c},\quad W_j=2^{10+2j}\quad(0\le j\le43).
\]
Write $\operatorname{bit}_j(n)$ for the $j$th binary digit of a
nonnegative integer. If the tile with selector $e$ has labels
$x_{e,0},\ldots,x_{e,3}$, put
\[
 c_e=1+\sum_{t=0}^3\sum_{j=0}^9
       \operatorname{bit}_j(x_{e,t}+512)W_{10t+j}
       +\sum_{j=40}^{43}W_j \qquad (0\le e\le942).
\]
The additional copy positions are defined using
$(o_0,o_1,o_2,o_3)=(1023,1013,1003,983)$:
\[
 c_{o_t+j}=W_{10t+j}\quad(0\le t\le3,\ 0\le j\le9).
\]
Put
\[
 (c_{1095},c_{1096},c_{2195},c_{3296})=(W_{40},W_{41},W_{42},W_{43}),
 \qquad c_{1063}=c_{1064}=0.
\]
The complete 989-element selector set is
\begin{align*}
 \mathcal E={}&\{0,\ldots,942\}\cup\{983,\ldots,992\}
 \cup\{1003,\ldots,1032\}\\
 &\cup\{1063,1064,1095,1096,2195,3296\}.
\end{align*}
Define
\begin{align*}
 \mu&=1022+\sum_{j=0}^{43}W_j,\\
 Z_{\rm pad}&=(2^{119}-1)\sum_{e=8792}^{8798}\rho^e
                 +(2^{27}-1)\rho^{8799},\\
 D_C&=\rho^{30}+\rho^{62}+\rho^{1100}+\rho^{2200}
       +\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}c_e
                  (\rho^{4393-e}+\rho^{5495-e}),\\
 D_R&=\rho^{42},\\
 M_C&=B_c-1-\sum_{\substack{e\in\mathcal E\\e\ne1}}\rho^e
                    -2\rho^{1063},\\
 M_F^{\rm native}&=4+\mu(\rho^{4393}+\rho^{5495})
       +\sum_{j=0}^9(\rho^{1013+j}+\rho^{1065+j})
       +\rho^{2195}+\rho^{3296}+Z_{\rm pad}.
\end{align*}
The four ports used throughout the article are exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:coefficienttuple}
 \boxed{\beta=B_c-1,\qquad K_0=D_C+B_cD_R,\qquad
        M_C=M_C,\qquad M_F=M_F^{\rm native}+B_c-1.}
\end{equation}
In particular, the final port is the \emph{shifted} mask. Substituting a
native mask in its place, or mixing coefficients from different packages,
would define a different polynomial and invalidate the inherited binding.

\subsection{Byte and arithmetic checks}
The independent reconstruction uses only this recipe and the literal
tile table; it does not import the original builder. It checks the
exact integer values against all four supplied little-endian unsigned
payloads, both compressed and decompressed SHA-256 hashes, and the
dimensions recorded in the binding manifest. It also verifies
\[
0<M_C<\beta,\quad 0<M_F^{\rm native}<\beta,\quad
M_C\equiv2\pmod4,\quad M_F^{\rm native}\equiv4\pmod8,
\]
and population counts
\[
 \pc(M_C)=1064921-989,\qquad \pc(M_F^{\rm native})=989.
\]
The literal carry threshold is
\[
 789998487493936316431751375694647416,
\]
which lies strictly between $2^{119}$ and $2^{120}$. Finite controller
unit residues and the multiplicative orders $10,12,52,60$ modulo
$121,13,677,2341$, respectively, are checked as well. These are finite
arithmetic checks of the coefficient recipe. The interpretation of
arbitrary solution words is the separate inherited compiler theorem.

The archive also materializes all nonliteral fixed leaves introduced
by the two packed evaluators: 17 for the nine-positive graph and 16
for the ten-integer graph. Their signed encodings, recipes and hashes
are recorded in \file{fixed/FIXED_LEAVES.json}. Fixed leaves are not
counted as variables or charged as variable-dependent arithmetic.
The size of a fully expanded polynomial coefficient list is neither
materialized nor claimed as an improved metric.
