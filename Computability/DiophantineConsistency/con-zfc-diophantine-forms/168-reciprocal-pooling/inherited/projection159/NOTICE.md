# Attribution and scope

AI-assisted mathematical research prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov and the
ProveIt project, 25 October 2026.

The common-radical elimination and affine projection proofs are elementary
linear and polynomial algebra. The contribution is their systematic application,
exact integer certificates, and new explicit equations at the stated snapshot;
no general linear-algebra novelty or worldwide priority is asserted.

The numerical C222 calculus, its ZF/ZFC refutation calibration, the shared Pell
compiler, normalized digit arithmetic, the shared factorial-error construction,
and the factorial-free binomial CRT construction are inherited from the pinned
ProveIt submissions. Their original texts, notices and licenses remain in the
two unchanged archives in `sources/`.

The new cofinal beta-spacing proof is an ordinary unbounded argument. Exact
finite verification concerns coefficient identities and supplied artifacts.
Neither should be described as a completed end-to-end proof-assistant proof.
