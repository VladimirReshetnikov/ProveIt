# Attribution and proof provenance

This archive contains new AI-assisted research prepared for Vladimir
Reshetnikov and exact copies of selected ProveIt source materials.

New material comprises the fifteen-variable integral-root constructions,
their builders and independent checks, the twelve-variable companion
reduction, the main report, and supporting documentation. It is supplied
under the repository's MIT No Attribution terms in `LICENSE`.

The ordinary strong/relaxed Pell kernel, K215 calibration, native clause
compiler, compact coefficient tuple, squared-modulus quotient, historical
root-gap results, and lean formal-norm evaluator are inherited, not claimed
as newly invented in this contribution. `PROVENANCE.json` and
`twelve/SOURCE_PINS.json` identify the exact copied files and repository
snapshot. Copied files are preserved with their applicable license/notice
documents in `coefficients/licenses/`, `twelve/upstream/`, and `upstream/`.

retains its historical `source_commit` field. The final archive's read-only
source snapshot is `6d375f2d2deac0519e659bbce5b48087023c1430`; these identify
different provenance layers and are not competing coefficient tuples.
