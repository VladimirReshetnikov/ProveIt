# Attribution and proof provenance

New AI-assisted composition and exact verification: transport-coordinate
projection of four incoming joint-sign polynomials, prepared for Vladimir
Reshetnikov's ProveIt project. New files are offered under the repository's
MIT No Attribution license, retained as LICENSE.

The complement-coordinate / monic transport method is prior work in
ProveIt source73, *Guard reanchoring*, not a new general lemma of this packet.
Its complete report source is retained as upstream/source73_report.tex.

The joint-sign parent polynomials, DAGs, sparse ring, and coefficient binding
come from the incoming ZFC_Diophantine_Joint_Guards_2026-10-24.zip archive.
The new builder is adapted from its shared DAG builder; the independent
verifier reuses its exact sparse ring and independently checked parent-DAG
binding. Every retained source byte is authenticated in SOURCE_PINS.json.

All inherited source34, K215, Wade, and ProveIt notices are retained under
upstream/guards/upstream/. Those licenses and notices continue to govern
their respective files. The finite coefficient recipe preserves the whole
source34 tuple, not a mixture of separately optimized ports.
