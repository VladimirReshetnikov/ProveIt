\subsection{Hybrid evaluation of the source without moving any target}
The coefficient specification and the evaluator are separate objects.
For evaluation, put $u_0=B^{183}$ and $u_i=B^{183-w_i}$.
Homogenize the inherited sixty-operation quadratic source at the coordinates
$u_i+u_0$. Align additions by powers of $u_0$ and make every comparison
homogeneous of degree two. The fifteenth residual of that sixty-operation
source uses the shifted transport convention; adding $\beta J u_0$ restores
the original package-31 residual used by the selective certificate.

For a quadratic monomial the factorial weight is two on a diagonal, including
$u_0^2$, and one off the diagonal. Add one further copy of every diagonal
coefficient to the homogeneous residual. A row with target $h\ge366$ can then
be multiplied by $B^{h-366}$ to obtain its exact reversed coefficient block.
Only targets $70$ and $86$ fail this inequality. Evaluate those two rows
directly, and evaluate the remaining twenty through the shared homogeneous
circuit. Sparse Horner evaluation combines the blocks. No target is shifted,
no negative exponent is introduced, and the polynomial is unchanged.

The independent auditor expands all twenty-two homogeneous comparisons in
all thirty-five symbols and checks the original residuals exactly. It also
reconstructs the shifted source independently and verifies all $71{,}808$
source/expansion interactions. The hybrid evaluator saves $161$ live binary
operations relative to this component's direct term evaluator. The separate
cancellation-resolved degree certificate deliberately uses the direct source
evaluator, making its degree argument independent of the gate-saving layout.
