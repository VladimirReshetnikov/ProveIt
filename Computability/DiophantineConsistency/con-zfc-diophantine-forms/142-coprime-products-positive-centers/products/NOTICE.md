# Sources and scope

New mathematical composition, code and exposition were developed with AI
assistance for Vladimir Reshetnikov on 25 October 2026. The package does
not imply external peer review or a new proof-assistant certificate.

The unchanged parent P66 circuit, ordinary proof, coefficient binding and
four coefficient payloads are copied from ProveIt commit
`e0c98c629bf10d30b695884f6b1d2ab361bdf513`, directory
`Computability/DiophantineConsistency/candidates/one-page-compact66`.
The payloads implement the indexed943/radix67 tuple, not a later smaller
radix tuple. The actual parent source is frozen under `parents/`.

Pell cofinality and product-square/coprimality methods have predecessors in
packages 123 and 133; the report gives their comparison and credit. We
claim only the new selective composition and its verified literal graphs,
not a new general theorem that products of coprime squares can be decoded.
