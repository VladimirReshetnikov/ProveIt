The mathematical development, implementation, and internal review in this
packet were carried out with OpenAI ChatGPT and cooperating AI research
agents. No external human peer review or new end-to-end proof-assistant
verification is represented.

New code and exposition are offered under the MIT No Attribution license
included as LICENSE. Retained upstream files keep their existing licenses,
notices, and attributions. In particular, preserve the notices and licenses
under direct/, graph/inherited/, and provenance/.
