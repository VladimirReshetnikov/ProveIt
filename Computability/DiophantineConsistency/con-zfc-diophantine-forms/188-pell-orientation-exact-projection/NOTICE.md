# Attribution and scope

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt project, 25 October 2026,
with AI-assisted mathematical development, code, exposition, and internal review.

The new files assemble and verify concrete equations from explicitly credited
ProveIt research. The affine orientation lemma is prior work in source136.
The C222 logical calibration, source173 anchor, cofinal Pell construction,
five-base pooling, Carrier65 transformation, and ordinary strong-rank and
native coefficient theorems are inherited dependencies. The report identifies
the exact new compositions and their matched comparisons.

Retained original files keep their own copyright and license notices.
The complete unmodified Cofinal Pell dependency archive is included under
`direct/inherited/`; it contains its original notices and licenses. The native packet
contains the ProveIt and K215 source notices and licenses. Source136 attribution
and its license are retained under `provenance/`.

No claim of worldwide priority, global optimality, a decision of ZFC
consistency, or a newly completed proof-assistant development is made.
Independent reviews in this package mean separately performed reviews within
the same AI-assisted research session.
