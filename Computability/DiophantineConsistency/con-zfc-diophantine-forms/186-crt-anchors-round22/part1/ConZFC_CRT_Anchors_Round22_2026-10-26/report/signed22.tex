\subsection{A literal local majorant without a derived range}
The removal of the derived-label range also requires an independent bound
for the actual local Horn polynomial. Here is an explicit circuit argument.
Put $R_0=2+t+x+y+F$. On a decoded local tuple, the genuine inputs
$t,x,F,y+1,a+1,b,c,d,S,S',u,v_q,C$ are smaller than $R_0$; here $C$
is the \emph{local residue}, not its enlarged global representative.
The extra product $FS$ is bounded by $R_0^2$.

For a monic gate $v=\sum_m c_m\prod_jv_{mj}$, suppose
$|v_{mj}|\le R_0^{e_{mj}}$ and there are $n$ nonzero summands.
Since $R_0\ge2$, the triangle inequality gives
\[
 |v|\le R_0^e,\qquad
 e=\max_m\left(\lceil\log_2|c_m|\rceil+\sum_je_{mj}\right)
       +\lceil\log_2n\rceil.
\]
Induction follows the actual monic dependencies, not a guessed syntactic
name. The independent ledger checks coordinates 20 through 99 using
rows 5 through 83 and 85 of the literal parent225 circuit. Removing exactly
the $N$-quotient monomial from row 84 isolates the local constraint.
Its resulting exponent is $5339613<2^{23}=8388608$. Thus the local
nonnegative constraint is below $R_0^{2^{23}}$ without the removed
range test. As already proved, $R_0<2x$ and $x>2^{32}$ imply
$R_0^{2^{23}}<2^x$. This is precisely the strict modulus margin needed
for exact decoding, and is independent of the inflated global $C$.

\subsection{More detail on the signed anchor witnesses}
For completeness we spell out the two sensitive divisions in the anchor
argument. In the notation of Proposition~\ref{prop:anchor}, principal
Pell classification at $|A|$ gives positive indices $i,l$ with
$|Y|=Y_i(|A|)$ and $|J_Y|=Y_l(|A|)$. Strong divisibility gives $i\mid l$.
Write $l=is$. Composition gives
\[
 Y_l(|A|)=Y_i(|A|)Y_s(X_i(|A|)),\qquad
 Y_s(X_i)\equiv sX_i^{s-1}\pmod{Y_i}.
\]
The Pell norm makes $X_i$ a unit modulo $Y_i$. Since $Y_i^2\mid Y_l$,
we get $Y_i\mid s$ and consequently $|Y|\mid l$. The independent
abscissa comparison gives $j\equiv\pm i\pmod{2l}$ for the comparison
index, while $D\equiv1\pmod{4Y}$ and $V_Y\equiv C\pmod{4Y}$ give
$j\equiv\pm C\pmod{4|Y|}$. Hence
$i\equiv\pm C\pmod{2|Y|}$. Together with
$1\le i\le|Y|$ and $|C|<|Y|$, this proves $C\ne0$ and $i=|C|$.
Neither nonnegativity nor a formula-label interpretation of $C$ is used.

Conversely choose the positive canonical center and even $C$. With
$Y=Y_C(A)$, $U=X_C(A)$, use $l=CY$ and $J_Y=Y_l(A)$.
Composition and the polynomial expansion modulo $Y^2$ show
\[
 J_Y/Y^2\equiv U^{Y-1}\pmod Y.
\]
Since $Y$ is even and $U$ odd, this quotient is odd. Thus
$e=(J_Y/Y^2-1)/2$ is integral. The index $l$ is even, so $J_X$ is odd;
the norm gives $\gcd(J_X,8J_Y)=1$. CRT therefore solves
\[
 D\equiv A\pmod{J_X},\qquad D\equiv1+4J_Y\pmod{8J_Y}
\]
with $D\ge2$. Evaluating the Pell polynomials at $D$ gives all remaining
comparison quotients integrally. The dominant index makes the cap slack
nonnegative, and its ternary-square representation completes the cap.
These are actual integer witnesses, not a divisibility assertion with an
unjustified quotient.

\subsection{Exact transport followed by two radical projections}
The Round21 chart supplies proposed operations, not a semantic theorem
about the new coordinate names. The literal emitted sequence retains
only operations checked on the actual source:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Integer shears $x_j=y_j+ky_i$, $i\ne j$, with inverse
$y_j=x_j-kx_i$.
\item Integer row additions $R_i\leftarrow R_i+kR_j$, with inverse
obtained by replacing $k$ by $-k$.
\item Monic definitions $x_v=f(\widehat{x_v})$, followed by deletion of
that row and coordinate. A quadratic restoration is used only when the
old coordinate occurs linearly everywhere else and every final row
remains quadratic. Inserting $f$ is the exact integral inverse on zeros.
\end{enumerate}
Each profile then admits two common-Hessian projections from
Theorem~\ref{thm:radical-projection}. Their actual direction and row
certificates satisfy the literal unit conditions, and the independent
checker reconstructs both directions without importing the producer.
Weighted growth gives 215 coordinates and 154 rows; quadratic growth gives
214 coordinates and 153 rows. Subsequent support/height changes are
invertible integer row operations. The sum of their squares may change
away from the common zero set; no identity of those sums is asserted.

The source change for the quadratic-growth companion deletes precisely
the private $T_2=t^2$ coordinate and defining row, while changing
$K=cT_2^2$ to $K=1024t^2$. The existing request is at actual $K$ and
the frame keeps that actual value. This is not deleting a wire while
leaving its old occurrences in the circuit.
