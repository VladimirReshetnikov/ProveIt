\section{Reproduction and evidence boundaries}
\label{sec:verification}
The complete release is supplied in ordinary disjoint ZIP parts, each
within the requested 4 MB limit, sharing one top-level directory. Extract
all parts into the same parent directory before running the verifier.
No individual part is advertised as a complete source closure.

The normative mathematical definitions are the complete finite residual,
coefficient and graph arrays, not PDF timestamps. Every selected direct
endpoint contains its ordered variables, all residuals and the fully
collected polynomial. Every selected height-one endpoint contains its full
coefficient array and semantic graph certificate. The scalar endpoints
contain their complete acyclic graphs, all four actual coefficient words,
the finite language decoder and all native-interface data. A digest is
an identity check, never a substitute for a missing coefficient.

\subsection{Component commands and report rebuild}
From the combined extracted root, the principal finite checks are
\begin{quote}\ttfamily
python3 -B signed/build.py --check\\
python3 -B signed/verify.py\\
python3 -B reciprocal/verify.py --deep\\
python3 -B height\_one/verify.py\\
python3 -B scalar/positive10/build.py --check\\
python3 -B scalar/positive10/verify.py --deep
\end{quote}
The release README gives the integrated verifier and exact final command
plan. Its receipt records terminal outcomes and source hashes. An
integrity-only pass verifies bytes, not mathematical reconstruction.
The Python finite checks use the standard library and do not require a
network or a proof-assistant installation; the full inherited arithmetic
and logical statements remain separate unbounded proofs.

Rebuild the native report with
\begin{quote}\ttfamily
sh report/build\_report.sh
\end{quote}
The script uses pdfLaTeX in a fresh temporary directory, compiles three
passes, and rejects unresolved references and overfull boxes. The
principal appendix is generated directly from its literal residual array
by \file{report/render_residuals.py}; \file{RESIDUAL_BINDING.json}
binds its source and generated TeX hashes. Independent re-parsing checks
the complete displayed residual list, coefficient by coefficient.
All final pages are rendered and visually inspected; the QA receipt
records the exact PDF hash, page count and inspected render inventory.

\subsection{What is independently checked}
\begin{itemize}
\item The direct source-delta checker compares every unchanged row and
coordinate, checks the weighted or quadratic growth definitions and
actual frame retention, then checks every transported operation,
common-Hessian direction and full inverse map. It recollects all final
quartics and verifies dimension, residual count, support, exact degree,
height and coordinate participation.
\item The separate reciprocal checker authenticates the complete

collected degree-ten coefficient. The independent audit rejects seven
corruption classes. The component \file{reciprocal/} explicitly
requires the bound sibling \file{signed/}; it is not a standalone
subtree with missing inherited files.
\item The separate local-majorant checker follows the literal monic gates
without importing the source producer, proving its finite exponent ledger
and isolating the exact local row used in the unbounded modulus proof.
\item The network checks reconstruct all divergence and equality squares,
their integral half-sum and every final collected coefficient. They check
all monic constructor and source pullbacks, graph occurrence coverage,
logical aliases, unit anchoring and the symbolic universal lift including
its hub term. Separate normalization checks independently verify every
new multiplicative constant and chained scale.
\item The scalar checks import no producer, verify every free-ring gate
identity and complete leading form, bind all four new ports together,
and independently reconstruct all language and coefficient bytes.
The deep native checks include carry envelopes, complete alignment
cases, origin/padding masks and the actual new controller premises.
All508 single-operator corruptions of the 254 arithmetic gates are
rejected. Normal and optimized-Python checks remain active with
assertions disabled; byte-identical replay checks reconstruction.
\end{itemize}
These are finite proofs of exact arrays, identities and explicit numerical
premises. They do not prove an unbounded Pell theorem by sampling,
establish the truth of consistency, or replace the syntactic calibration.
The ordinary mathematical reviews are AI-assisted independent reviews,
not external human refereeing or a new formal-kernel certificate.
