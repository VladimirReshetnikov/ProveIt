This round gives three complementary reductions of explicit polynomial
presentations of syntactic ZFC consistency. Reusing an even CRT row-index
representative as the semantic Pell anchor permits removal of a formula-label
range test; weighted polynomial growth and exact integral projection then
give a principal quartic in 215 unrestricted integers, with 7081 monomials
and coefficient height 8880. A separate reciprocal-pooling composition
reaches 212 unrestricted variables at degree 10. Monic product and chained-scale
constructors reduce the smallest selected coefficient-height-one equation
from 503 to 493 unrestricted integer variables. Finally, a full native-interface
proof transports all three ten-positive-variable circuits to one coherent
Start-only4127 coefficient tuple, reducing their four literal coefficient
payloads from 295388 to 97046 bytes without changing the degree/cost pairs
214/87, 232/85 and 240/82.

Every equation is an ordinary fixed polynomial on its explicitly stated
domain. The report provides complete witness arguments for the changes,
prints every principal residual, and retains the native coefficient proof
and all inherited source interfaces. The reproducible arrays and finite
checkers establish exact identities and metrics; they do not replace the
unbounded arithmetic or logical calibration. The result is a provable
equivalence with consistency, not a proof of consistency, unsolvability,
global minimality or a new end-to-end proof-assistant certificate.
\end{abstract}
