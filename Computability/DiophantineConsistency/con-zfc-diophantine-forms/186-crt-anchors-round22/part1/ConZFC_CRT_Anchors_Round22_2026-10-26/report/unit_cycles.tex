\subsection{Unit homogenized equality cycles}
The retained Round21 support reduction generalizes the anchored three-cycle without
adding a coordinate. Let $a,b$ be the two aliases of one non-unit logical
class, and let $u_1,\ldots,u_\ell$ be existing unit aliases, already
anchored by the unchanged unit energy $U$. Choose three expressions
$f_1,f_2,f_3$, each equal to $a$, $b$, $au_j$ or $bu_j$, with both
underlying aliases $a$ and $b$ represented. Replace that class's equality
penalty by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:unit-cycle}
 C(f_1,f_2,f_3)=f_1^2+f_2^2+f_3^2-f_1f_2-f_2f_3-f_3f_1.
\end{equation}
No other incidence label or unit relation is changed.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:unit-cycle}
The replacement preserves the calibrated integer zero set and the
universal source lift. It introduces no variable and has degree at most
four. Any frozen instance whose complete coefficient collection has
height one and exact degree four is a height-one quartic on the same
independent coordinate set.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The polynomial is nonnegative for every real tuple because
\[
 2C(f_1,f_2,f_3)=(f_1-f_2)^2+(f_2-f_3)^2+(f_3-f_1)^2.
\]
At a zero of the complete energy, the separately nonnegative unit
summand gives $u_j=1$ for every unit alias. The three expressions then
reduce to copies of $a$ and $b$, with both present. Their equality is
therefore exactly $a=b$. Conversely $a=b$ and all $u_j=1$ make the
new penalty vanish. All other summands and the source-block divergence
identity are unchanged. The old and new energies have literally the same zero set in the same
coordinate space, so this step has the identity map as both witness maps.
The same source projection and unique tree-flow lift also remain inverse
on zeros.

On the canonical lift of an arbitrary source tuple, all units are one
and all aliases equal their logical values. Thus the new penalty
vanishes identically there, not merely over source zeros. This proves
that \eqref{eq:networklift} is unchanged. Each $f_i$ has degree at most
two, so the new penalty has degree at most four.

These facts do not by themselves prove height one or decreased support.
A product $au_j$ can create monomials already present elsewhere. The
complete energy is therefore collected after each proposed change,
including incidence, other equality penalties and the unit path.
The accepted frozen certificate must pass exact checks of every
coefficient, degree, constant and live coordinate. No choice of
$f_i$ is assumed universally safe for height.
\end{proof}
This changes the polynomial away from its zeros, but retains the same
source witnesses. The tested cycles save support through actual signed
monomial collisions in the full energy, not through an uncollected
count of terms in one penalty. The finite selection procedure is not an
exhaustive search over height-one equations.
