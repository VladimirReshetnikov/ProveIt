\section{Further questions with explicit proof obligations}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can another growth polynomial $K(t)\ge2^{20}t$ reduce the remaining
height cost? A lower-degree index can save a coordinate, but the emitted
quadratic rows, actual frame atom and cofinal choice order must all be
rechecked. Reducing a coefficient below the stated budget without a
sharper label bound would break product separation.
\item Can sharper literal constructor bounds reduce $2^{20}$ or the
local majorant without reinstating a derived-label range? The relevant
objects are the actual schema trees and local residual, not a loose
count of constructor names. The boundary $F=L^t$ is already allowed.
\item Can the 214-variable chart be brought below its current height
without losing its variable advantage? Its large coefficient is not an
arithmetic necessity theorem. Exact reversible row operations or integral
radical directions are admissible, but nonunit slopes require additional
divisibility and cannot simply be divided out.
\item Can joint choice of coefficient factors, product constants and
chained scales improve the 493-variable network? The heuristic graph
cost is not the final support. Every proposal needs the monic triangular
source proof and a full coefficient-height-one collection test, including
all equality and unit terms.
\item Can the newer arithmetic sources improve the height-one catalog?
A source transfer must be implemented and measured; the current catalog
retains the old source families. Neither a lower direct dimension nor
fewer residuals guarantees fewer aliases after normalization.
\item Can Start-only geometry be shortened further while preserving the
complete native interface? Clean clauses must precede alignment; a flag
must not be mistaken for occupation; all carry resets, mask populations
and cofinal controller conditions must be proved at the new tuple.
\item Can the positive circuits reduce degree or gates as well as
coefficient storage? The whole-tuple transfer changes neither. Replacing
$bV$ by $b^2$ in a free chart is not licensed by its current transport
bound, and early cancellation through an unproved cubic scale remains
invalid. The separate eleven-integer fixed-frame bounds are irrelevant
to a new positive-chart architecture unless a bridge is proved.
\end{enumerate}
These are concrete open optimization directions, not claimed improvements
or universal lower bounds. Negative searches recorded in the source
packages apply only to their stated finite families.
