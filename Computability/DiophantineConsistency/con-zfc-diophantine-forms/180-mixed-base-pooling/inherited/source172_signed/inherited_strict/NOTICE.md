# Attribution and scope

Prepared with AI assistance for Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt project,
25 October 2026.

The new material consists of the strict-horizon source edit, the composition
of a self-power majorant and linear CRT radius with signed affine-radix
recovery, the new compact free-quotient Pell constructions, their proofs,
the final report, and the accompanying integration and verification work.
The direct coordinate shears, residual balancing method, and much of the
arithmetic compiler are retained from the identified incoming sources.

The direct dependency tree is the supplied **Factorial Free Binomial CRT**
submission, itself retaining C222 logical calibration, Pell representations,
centered and normalized remainder constructions, signed Horn factorization,
and exact polynomial elimination. The signed-binomial comparison also
credits **Shared Factorial Error**. The compact branch retains material from
**Cofinal Witnesses and Centered Carriers** and **Lucas Corrections and
Witness Elimination**, including the fixed BFS943 radix67 coefficient tuple
and its machine ancestry. These contributions are not attributed to the
present work as original discoveries.

The new direct equation uses the numerical C222 rules directly; it does
not compile a K215 run or instantiate Jones's universal expression. The
compact equations have a different ancestry and retain their original
large constants. The complete supplied coefficient payloads and their
reconstruction must accompany those equations.

Original licenses and notices remain in sources/factorial_free/,
compact/, and their dependency directories. The root LICENSE is the
ProveIt MIT No Attribution license used for new material in this package.
Retained third-party material remains subject to its own included notices.

The mathematical proofs establish the stated consistency equivalences by
composition with named inherited theorems. The programs check exact finite
algebra and data. They are not a new end-to-end Lean/Rocq verification or
an encoded internal-ZFC proof object. The retained Lean source excerpts
identify the logical calibration; their entire import closure is obtained
from the pinned repository, not claimed as a standalone project here.

Independent reviews in review/ were carried out by separate AI-assisted
research passes. They are additional scrutiny, not external peer review.
Neither global minimality, historical priority, nor the truth of
Con(ZFC) itself is claimed.
