# Attribution and scope

This research contribution was prepared with AI assistance for Vladimir
Reshetnikov and the ProveIt repository on 25 October 2026.

New work in this package:

- The two-integrality-step theorem for unweighted reciprocal pooling
  across mixed-sign and zero power bases, its literal five-block
  instantiation, and witness recovery.
- The selected compositions with monic elimination, the corresponding
  explicit circuits, exact degree and height-bound certificates, and
  the matched 116/24 versus 112/24 comparison.
- The report, public evaluator, reconstruction and independent checks.

Inherited and credited work:

- Source 172 supplies the actual calibrated 216-variable source and its
  signed arithmetic proof, preceding transformations, and C222/ZF/ZFC
  logical interfaces.
- Source 158 supplies same-base power pooling.
- Source 168 supplies the earlier two-consecutive-base reciprocal pool
  and discusses the oriented multi-base direction. The oriented idea is
  not presented as a literature-wide first here.
- Source 152 supplies the monic-elimination method and the prior degree
  tradeoffs. Its variable-name priorities inform the finite search.
- Earlier C222, Pell, binomial-CRT, and formal-interface contributions
  retain their original notices and licenses in the inherited tree.

Every inherited file is mapped to a precise repository path and content
hash in inherited/source172_PROVENANCE.json. Some files were deduplicated
at repository intake; their exact retained copies were restored using the
repository's placement ledger. They were not rewritten to hide ancestry.

Independent reviews in this release are separate AI-assisted reviews,
not outside human peer review. The report distinguishes ordinary proofs
formalizable in ZFC, finite computational checking, and the absence of a
new end-to-end proof-assistant compilation.

No claim is made that these equations are globally simplest or that ZFC
is consistent. All equivalences inherit the explicitly identified effective
ZFC presentation and ordinary calibration from the authenticated source.
