# Provenance and reuse

The certificate-budget theorem, linear-factor construction, explanatory text,
and standalone checking code in this directory were developed in this
research iteration for incorporation into ProveIt.

The literal C222 branch rows in `horn_source_rows.json` are extracted without
changing coefficients from
`Computability/DiophantineConsistency/con-zfc-diophantine-forms/68-joint-positivity/direct/horn_shared_source.json`
in the user-owned ProveIt repository. The exact commit and SHA-256 are in its
provenance block. The original source's expanded sums of squares were omitted
because they are regenerated by `build.py`. Inherited material retains the
repository and original-source licenses and notices; none is relicensed by
this notice. Source 68's NOTICE is included as `NOTICE-source68.md`.

The general single-conclusion flow theorem and the earlier affine-coprime
construction are credited to source 68's `COMMUTATIVE_CERTIFICATES.md` and
`AFFINE_PRODUCT_CERTIFICATES.md`. The new contribution is the linear-factor
coefficient budget and its exact branch-shared local source. The ordinary
mathematical proof is provided; no new Lean or Rocq certificate is claimed.
