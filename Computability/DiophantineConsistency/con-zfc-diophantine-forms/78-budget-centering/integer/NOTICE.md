# Attribution and scope

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt research program, October 23, 2026, with ChatGPT assistance. New prose, constructions, and verification code follow the repository's MIT No Attribution license, retained in `LICENSE`.

The nearest-square simultaneous-positivity lemma is inherited from package68. The determinant guard, complete six-numeral coefficient binding, and literal Q38 parent are inherited from package71. Its arithmetic parent is package64, and its complete native coefficient tuple is source34. The exact sparse-polynomial engine `polynomial.py` is copied from source71 without modification. `coefficients/recipe/build_coefficients.py` is copied from source34 without modification. Literal machine and tile data retain their inherited notices.

The additional constructions in this package combine those sibling results and use two Pell-root translations plus the paired elimination of c and V to obtain a 24-integer degree-ten polynomial. The 26-integer octic and 23-integer degree-sixteen companion are separate variable/degree choices. No claim of global mathematical priority or global minimality is made. Internal AI-assisted checks are not external human peer review.

Retain all notices and licenses under `licenses/`. In particular, the inherited compiler and machine work includes the attributions specified there to ProveIt contributors, Mira-Cult, Elliot Glazer, Jeff Ketchersid and contributors, Andrew James Wade, BusyCoq, and the Coq Library of Undecidability, each within the scope of the original notice. This file does not relicense inherited material.

The new exact algebra is distinguished from the inherited semantic theorem. No end-to-end proof-assistant certificate, internally encoded ZFC derivation, proof of consistency, or proof of inconsistency is asserted by this package.
