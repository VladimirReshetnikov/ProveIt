# Attribution and scope

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project through AI-assisted
mathematical research and separately implemented exact algebra checks.

The new material is the centered factorial fixed-point theorem, its odd
specialization, the two-numerator common radix proof, their literal
composition with the direct proof source, and the resulting395-natural
and853-integer quartics. No literature-wide priority is claimed.

The entire immediate parent is preserved in ConZFC_407_888_Quartics/ with its
original LICENSE, NOTICE.md, report, coefficient data, and upstream sources.
The C222 calculus calibration, finite proof-flow interpretation, CRT
compilation, shared Pell graphs, difference-square gates, and certified
contraction/sign-inference methods are inherited from that package and
the sources it identifies.
