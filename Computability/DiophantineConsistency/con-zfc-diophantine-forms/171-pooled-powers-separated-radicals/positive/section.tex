\section{An inverse correction composed with the explicit Pell trace}
\label{sec:inverse-trace}
The supplied polynomial has ten independently strictly positive integer
unknowns, exact total degree $232$, and an evaluator with $85$ live binary
operations: $51$ multiplications, $18$ additions and $16$ subtractions.
Squares and fixed scalar multiplications are charged; all leaves are
integers.  The matched incoming \emph{Trace Radical} graph has $87$
operations at the same degree.  This is an ordinary proof composition at
the explicitly retained native/syntactic-refutation calibration, not a
consistency proof, global minimum theorem, or new formal-kernel object.

\subsection{Complete equation}
The inputs are $J,F,\alpha,i,T,w,s,n,\eta,\zeta>0$.  The four fixed
integers $\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F$ are supplied as complete payloads and by a
finite seed recipe.  Every following symbol is a polynomial abbreviation,
not an additional unknown:
\begin{gather*}
h=\beta J,\qquad q=h+1,\qquad C=q-F-\alpha,\\
X=qw,\qquad Y=qs,\qquad k=\eta+\zeta,\qquad c=kY+\eta,\\
A=Y(X+1)+1,\qquad \Delta=A^2-1,\qquad H=4A-5,\\
\ell=(XY)(kY),\qquad E=1+\ell(\ell+k),\\
R=[h(q-F)+\alpha](q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\\
B=(K_0+w)C-F,\qquad U=ic^2,\qquad a=\Delta U^2E,\\
F_0=1+a,\qquad G=EF_0,\qquad f=a+F_0,\qquad Q=4\Delta aF_0,\\
b=cT-R,\qquad V=fb-c,\qquad L=bV,\qquad K=X(3c-2X)+2,\\
e_6=6(k-R-1)-\ell(k^2-4),\qquad D_6=6q^6,\qquad M=hD_6H,\\
Z=hD_6[H(n-LB)+K]-H[D_6B+h(e_6-6q^4s)].
\end{gather*}
The single ordinary integer polynomial equation is
\[
 \boxed{\quad P=Q(Z^2-M^2GV^2)+(M^2G-Z^2)=0.\quad}
\]
There are no division gates, variable powers, binomial primitives or
hidden quantified variables.  For proof only put
\[
v=XY^2,\quad e_*=k-R-1-\frac{vk(k^2-4)}6,\quad
\rho=\frac{e_*}{q^6},\quad \sigma=\frac{s}{q^2},\quad
\kappa=\frac KH,\quad t=\frac Bh,\quad W=1+hL.
\]
Before assuming any divisibility, the literal carrier identity is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:inverse-carrier}
 z:=Z/M=n+\kappa+\sigma-Wt-\rho.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Signed soundness and the corrected index}
The inherited trace-linear root extraction uses
\[
f^2=1+4aF_0,\quad Q=4\Delta^2U^2G,\quad \gcd(E,F_0)=1.
\]
At a zero it yields $G=g^2$, $E=\tau^2$, $F_0=f_0^2$, integral
$z=\varepsilon gy$, and $QV^2-(Q-1)y^2=1$.  The pair
$(f,I)=(1+2a,2Ug)$ satisfies $f^2-\Delta I^2=1$ and $c^2\mid I$.
Moreover $f\equiv1\pmod c$ identically.  Signed folding gives
$c=\psi_A(p)$ with positive odd $p$, and
$k=2\psi_{1+2v}(m)$ with $m\ge2$.  Thus
$c>8XY^3\ge8q^4$ and $K>0$.

The retained main-index-three exclusion gives $c>c_3=4\Delta+3$.
The trace-linear auxiliary-index-three identities are
\[
f(b-4\Delta f)=c-c_3\qquad\hbox{or}\qquad
f(b+4\Delta f)=c+c_3.
\]
Their right sides are strictly between $0$ and $2c<f$; hence the
auxiliary index is at least five.  The consequent bounds give
$bV>0$, $gy<L/2$ and $|b|/q^6<L/2$.
Because $k\ge2$, the inverse correction is nonnegative before its
subtraction: $e_*\le k-R-1<b$, regardless of the sign of $R$.
If $B\le-1$, then $Wt<-L$ and \eqref{eq:inverse-carrier} forces
$n<L/2-L+|b|/q^6<0$.  Therefore $B\ge0$ and $C>0$.
Only now use the calibrated mask estimate $3q<R<q^4-2$.
The residues $V\equiv-c\pmod f$ and $V\equiv-R\pmod c$ give $p=R$;
then $R$ is odd and $q$ is even.

The unbounded Lucas expansion is
\[
k=2m+8vC_m+32v^2Z_m,\qquad C_m=\binom{m+1}{3},\quad Z_m\ge0.
\]
Since $k$ is even, $k(k^2-4)/6$ and $e_*$ are integral.
Reduce the literal $Z$ modulo $D_6$, using $Z=Mz$; cancel the common
ordinary integer factor $6$, not a residue-ring unit.  Since
$\gcd(q,hH)=1$, this proves
\[
q^6\mid e_*-q^4s,\qquad q^4\mid e_*.
\]
For $t_0=2m$, $d=2m-R-1$, $A_m=C_m+4vZ_m$ and
$S_m=k^2+kt_0+t_0^2-4$, the exact cubic difference identity gives
\[
3(e_*-d)=4v^2(24Z_m-A_mS_m).
\]
As $v=q^3ws^2$, every prime of $q$ gives
$\operatorname{ord}_p(e_*-d)\ge6\operatorname{ord}_p(q)
-\operatorname{ord}_p(3)\ge4\operatorname{ord}_p(q)$.
Monotonicity yields $m<R$; consequently $|d|<R<q^4$ and $d=0$.
It is not yet legitimate to infer $q^6\mid e_*$.

\subsection{Project before recovering the full denominator}
Reduction modulo $H$ gives $H\mid6hK$.  Write $R=2r+1$.
The authenticated ratio theorem gives $r\ge48$, $Y>X^r/3$ and
$X^{r+1}<H$.  Universal projection yields
\[
H\mid6h(X-2^R)(2^RX+1).
\]
With $u=2^R=2\cdot4^r$, the whole product satisfies
\[
6h|(X-u)(uX+1)|
 <X^4(X+u)(uX+1)
 \le4\cdot4^rX^6+8\cdot16^rX^5<X^{r+1}<H.
\]
The last strict inequality follows at the minimum $(X,r)=(32,48)$
from $2^{-117}+2^{-25}<1$, and improves thereafter.
Thus $X=2^R$, $H\mid K$, and $q$ is a power of two.
Now $3$ is coprime to $q$, so the corrected-index identity proves
$q^6\mid e_*$.  The carrier then gives $q^2\mid s$ and $h\mid B$.
Hence $q^3\mid Y$ and $t\ge0$ is integral, exactly the retained
native reconstruction interface at source base $Y(X+1)+2$.

\subsection{Strictly positive completeness}
A finite refutation supplies fresh canonical outer data.  Choose a
positive Pell period seed with $c^2\tau\mid I_0$ and set
$i=I_0/(c^2\tau)$.  The inherited positive auxiliary pair can be kept
while replacing its old input by
\[
 T=\frac{V+c+Rf}{cf}
   =fT_{\rm old}-\frac{f-1}{c}R>0.
\]
It is integral because $c\mid f-1$ and satisfies $V=f(cT-R)-c$.
The inverse correction can make $\rho$ negative, but the explicit bounds
\[
 |\rho|<vk^3/3<g/3,\qquad
 \kappa+\sigma<g/2
\]
show that
\[
 n=gy+(1+hL)t+\rho-\kappa-\sigma
\]
is a strictly positive integer.  The carrier is $z=gy$, proving $P=0$.
Both existential directions compose with the retained calibrated
syntactic-refutation theorem; negation gives the equivalence with
$\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$.

 [P]_{232}=-9\cdot2^{10}q_0^{100}w^{26}s^{58}i^{10}k_0^{32}B_0^2.
