## F=t^t independent mathematical assessment

The parent-requested argument is valid but already supplied in157 `proofs/MAIN_PROOF.md` §§5–6:

- In unequal product counts, the smaller count `r≤t`; the larger side is at least `(F+1)^(r+1)`, the smaller at most `t^r(F+1)^r`; `F+1=t^t+1>t^r` forces equal counts `m≤t`.
- Every coefficient of either equal-degree positive-factor polynomial lies in `[1,t^m]⊆[1,F]`. Therefore each difference has absolute value `<F`, even at the boundary where a coefficient itself equalsF. The least nonzero coefficient moduloF excludes an accidental equality atF.
- `lcm(1,…,t)≤4^(t−1)` and `t≥16` imply `lcm²<F=t^t`, so the generalized binomial-CRT radius can beR=F. Both row coverage and reverse coverage of *every* full prime power ofB must be retained.
- For `t=16+h²`, completeness may choose evenh: then `v2(F)=t v2(t)≥t>v2(t!)`, and `v2(F)>floor(log2t)` implies `binom(F+t,t)` odd. Odd-part multiplier freedom supplies an arbitrarily large even multiple of t!, retaining beta-CRT parity and cofinal representative choices.
- These inequalities alone are not a source proof. The actual source must retain the signed canonical Pell interface, every changed frame atom, representative/radix sign bootstrap, lower-binomial digit recovery, label-product/no-carry step, exceptional beta endpoint handling, and canonical witness order.157 supplies these for its own chart `t=16+4d²`, `C=2c`, `H=2+4(C+ΣAj)`, affine `y=F(2v+1)`, with no odd-square restriction onq. A differently fixed chart requires its own compatibility argument.
