\subsection{The four-root identity and denominator clearing}
Let $\mathcal H_4(S,A,B,C_*,D)$ denote the final schedule. It is homogeneous
of degree eight in its five arguments and satisfies the formal identity
\[
 \mathcal H_4(z^2,A,B,C_*,D)=
 \prod_{\epsilon\in\{-1,1\}^4}
 (z-\epsilon_1\sqrt A-\epsilon_2\sqrt B
       -\epsilon_3\sqrt{C_*}-\epsilon_4\sqrt D).
\]
This holds for arbitrary complex roots, including coincident square classes.
The independent standard-library checker multiplies all sixteen factors
exactly and compares all $495$ coefficients.

Unconditionally, $q$ is even and nonzero, $|X|\ge8$, and
$a=(X+1)Y$ is zero or has absolute value at least seven. Thus
$\Delta=(a+2)^2-1\ge3$, $H$ is odd and nonzero, and $U\ne0$.
Moreover $D_t,F_s\ge1$ because
$D_t=1+\ell(\ell+1)k^2$ and $\ell$ is even.
As $Q=(\Delta I)^2$, either $Q=0$ or $Q\ge9$;
hence $\delta,C_y$ never vanish. Also $G\equiv1\pmod4$, so $G\ne0$.
With $\rho=Z_c/U$, the exact proof identity is
\[
 P=\delta^8U^{16}\!\prod_{\epsilon\in\{-1,1\}^4}
 \left(\rho-\epsilon_1\sqrt{F_s}-3\epsilon_2\sqrt{D_t}
       -27\epsilon_3\sqrt G-9\epsilon_4\sqrt{C_y/\delta}\right).
\]
The factor $\delta^8$ is already cleared in the literal polynomial; the
quotient above is proof notation, not a division gate.

\subsection{Separation and rational Pell integrality}
The authentic $\beta$ is $1\pmod3$. For every integer assignment,
\[
 q=X=-1,\quad W=1,\quad a=H=\Delta=0\pmod3,
\]
so $F_s=D_t=1$ and $\delta=C_y=-1\pmod3$. For $D_t$, use
$\ell=-Y^2$ and $Y^4-Y^2=0\pmod3$. In the order
\[
 \sqrt{F_s},\quad3\sqrt{D_t},\quad
 9\sqrt{C_y/\delta},\quad27\sqrt G,
\]
the squared $3$-adic valuations are $0,2,4,6+v_3(G)\ge6$.

Write each nonzero rational radicand as $du^2$ with $d$ a signed squarefree
integer and $u\in\mathbb Q^\times$. Distinct $\sqrt d$ are linearly independent
over $\mathbb Q$, by sign-change character projections in a multiquadratic
extension (including $\sqrt{-1}$). Consider only an irrational class $d\ne1$;
the rational carrier belongs to class $1$. Any class meeting the first three
roots has $v_3(d)=0$, and its coefficients have a unique least valuation among
$0,1,2,\ge3$. Their signed sum cannot vanish. A class containing only the guard
cannot vanish either. Consequently every root in a vanishing norm factor is
rational. This includes negative radicands and dependent square classes; it
does not assume that at most one term is imaginary.

At a zero there are signed rationals $f,\tau,t,g$ such that
\[
 f^2=F_s,\quad\tau^2=D_t,\quad t^2=C_y/\delta,\quad g^2=G,
 \qquad \rho=f+3\tau+9t+27g.
\]
The first, second and fourth are integers, and $g$ is odd. For the third,
$QV^2-(Q-1)t^2=1$. If $Q=0$, then $t=\pm1$. If $Q>1$, rational Pell
integrality follows by descent. Indeed, write $t=r/m$ in lowest terms;
$m^2\mid Q-1$. After sign reflection, $u=|V|>1$ implies
$u<t<Qu/(Q-1)$. The norm-preserving map
\[
 u'=(2Q-1)u-2(Q-1)t,\qquad t'=(2Q-1)t-2Qu
\]
has $u'\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$, $|u'|<u$, and retains the reduced
denominator $m$ because $2Q-1\equiv1\pmod m$. Descent reaches $u=1,t=1$,
forcing $m=1$. Thus all four extracted witnesses are integers.

\[

\[
 (Z+Uk)^2+4U(LZ-U)=(Z+U(2L+k))^2-4U^2D_t
