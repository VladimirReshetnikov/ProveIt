\subsection{A doubled lower Pell frame at no coordinate cost}
The retained semantic frame $W$ is fixed after the intended powers and
representatives. Write $A=\ell+\sigma$, with $\ell,\sigma\ge0$. Its lower
graph originally used
\[
 P=W(z_{\rm old}+1),\qquad R=(W+1)P,\qquad
 \ell^2+P^2-R^2=1.
\]
Substitute $z_{\rm old}=2z+1$. Thus $P=2W(z+1)>0$ with the same
coordinates and degree. Classification of the lower Pell norm gives
\[
 \ell=X_e(W+1),\qquad P=Y_e(W+1),\qquad e>0.
\]
The second-order recurrence for $Y_e$ reduces modulo $2W$ to the recurrence
for $e$, since $2(W+1)\equiv2\pmod{2W}$. Hence
$Y_e(W+1)\equiv e\pmod{2W}$, so $e\ge2W$. For $W\ge2$,
\[
 A\ge\ell\ge(W+1)^{2W}>4W^{2W}.                 \tag{N1}
\]
Indeed, the first three binomial terms of $(1+1/W)^{2W}$ already sum to
$5-1/W>4$. For completeness choose $e=2W$; the quotient
$Y_{2W}(W+1)/(2W)$ is a positive integer. The subsequent common-root
congruence still permits choosing $A$ arbitrarily large above $\ell$.
This is a cofinal auxiliary-witness strengthening, not a bijection on all
old lower Pell tuples.

\subsection{A three-coordinate natural pool}
The three exact requests at base $n=q$ have indices and outputs
\[
 (k_0,k_1,k_2)=(t,c,x),\qquad c=y+1,\qquad
 (m_0,m_1,m_2)=(D_t,D_y,D_x).
\]
In the emitted charts each $m_i$ is the affine sum of its pooled remainders,
its pooled slack, and $1$. Their natural consecutive pairs $(Y_i,Z_i)$
retain all three norms. The old rows used a modulus coordinate
$T=2An-n^2-1$ and three natural quotients $h_i$:
\[
 nY_i-Z_i=m_i+Th_i.                                      \tag{N2}
\]
Delete these four coordinates and four rows. Introduce only three natural
coordinates $U,M_p,K$ with
\begin{align}
 U&=\sum_iY_i^2,\nonumber\\
 K&=2\sum_iY_i(m_i+Z_i)+(2A-n)M_p-nU,\nonumber\\
 \sum_i(m_i+Z_i)^2+M_p&=nK.                              \tag{N3}
\end{align}
All three rows are quadratic. They imply the exact polynomial identity
\[
 \sum_i(m_i+Z_i-nY_i)^2=T M_p.                           \tag{N4}
\]
If their residuals are $S_0,S_1,S_2$ in displayed order, the left side
minus $TM_p$ equals $S_2+nS_1-n^2S_0$.

For canonical true powers define
\[
 U=\sum_iY_i^2,\qquad M_p=T\sum_i h_i^2,
\]
and define $K$ by the middle row of (N3). It is an integer, and the final
row yields
\[
 nK=\sum_i(m_i+Z_i)^2+M_p\ge0.
\]
Thus $K$ is natural when $n,T>0$. No signed-coordinate relaxation is
hidden in the pooling construction.

\subsection{The orientation issue and its exact repair}
Deleting (N2) removes the sole predecessor-orientation incidence for the
$c=y+1$ seed. It is therefore invalid simply to assume all three consecutive
pairs remain predecessors. Here is the noncircular repair.

The stronger frame first authenticates the complete semantic anchor.
For any consecutive pair, $X=AY-Z$ satisfies
$X^2-(A^2-1)Y^2=1$. Classification applied to $(|X|,Y)$ supplies
$Y=Y_j(A)$ even when the sign of $X$ is unknown. The retained merged
error-square cap depends only on these ordinates; its original small-error
argument still proves $j=k$. The anchor itself retains positive ordinary
coordinates and every private witness.

The $t$ and $x$ pairs retain their individual positive-output rows at bases
$F^2-1$ and $B$, respectively. A row $bY=Z+R$ with $R\ge0$ and
$1\le b<A$ excludes $Y=0$ and implies $AY-Z>0$. Their predecessor
orientations therefore survive. The three unpooled power requests recover
$V,N,q$ by the old argument, so $n=q\ge1$ before the pool is interpreted.
The unchanged literal frame bounds $n,m_i,k_i\le W$, giving
\[
 T\ge2A-W^2-1>A>4W^{2W}>0.                              \tag{N5}
\]

The decisive additive, rather than merely maximum, bounds are
\[
 c+t\le W-1,\qquad c+x\le W-1,\qquad
 2c\le W-1,\qquad c+1\le W-1.                          \tag{N6}
\]
The independent verifier checks all four as exact coefficientwise
nonnegative differences in the literal frame. For the sparse charts it
first expands the retained \texttt{add.594} row and uses the retained
cofinal/floor identity $F+Fr+Fs+r=y+1$. No factorial or power interpretation
is used in checking these inequalities.

Modulo $T$, the standard Pell recurrence gives
$X_k+(n-A)Y_k\equiv n^k$. Its conjugate is the inverse, because
$(n-A)^2\equiv A^2-1\pmod T$. Thus the actual pair has
\[
 nY-Z\equiv
 \begin{cases}
 n^k&\text{if }X=X_k,\\
 -n^{-k}&\text{if }X=-X_k.
 \end{cases}
 \pmod T
\]
Here inverses exist since $T\equiv-1\pmod n$. Multiply (N4)'s errors by
$n^c$. Their ordinary integer residues are
\begin{align*}
 \delta_0&=m_0n^c-n^{c+t},&
 \delta_2&=m_2n^c-n^{c+x},\\
 \delta_1&=m_1n^c-n^{2c}&&\text{in the predecessor case},\\
 \delta_1&=m_1n^c+1&&\text{in the reversed case}.
\end{align*}
Consequently $T$ divides $E=\delta_0^2+\delta_1^2+\delta_2^2$.
By (N6), each displayed power and each $m_i n^c$ is at most $W^{W-1}$.
Therefore
\[
 0\le E\le12W^{2W-2}\le3W^{2W}<T.
\]
It follows that $E=0$. The reversed case is impossible because
$m_1n^c+1>0$. Cancellation of the positive $n^c$ now gives all three
exact powers and the missing predecessor orientation.

The old quotients are genuinely natural, not merely integral: the standard
positive-power identity is
\[
 nY_k-Y_{k-1}-n^k
 =T\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}n^{k-1-j}Y_j\ge0.
\]
Thus all four deleted coordinates in (N2) can be restored, as can the
old frame coordinate and the syntax wires. Every parent residual,
including the local nonnegative evaluation row, is recovered.
