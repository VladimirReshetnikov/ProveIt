\begin{abstract}
We construct explicit polynomial equations whose lack of solutions in their
specified domains is provably equivalent in ZFC to syntactic consistency
of ZFC. The principal quartic constructions combine pooled power certificates,
exact coordinate elimination, and reversible changes of residual basis
to reduce their numbers of quantified coordinates.
A separate four-radical construction combines an auxiliary Pell certificate
with three existing square conditions, using separated powers of three
to prevent cancellation between equal irrational square classes. This
provides a twelve-integer alternative with a particularly direct reduction
proof; it is not a global variable-degree-operation record.
An inverse Lucas correction is also composed with an unsquared Pell trace
to shorten a ten-positive-variable evaluator.
Every result is supplied with literal equations, fixed coefficients,
ordinary proofs, constructive maps, and reproducible finite checks.
Inherited logical calibration remains explicit. No consistency decision,
global optimum, or new end-to-end kernel certificate is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{The objective and the comparison snapshot}
For each specified domain $D$ and fixed polynomial $P$ the target is
\[
\mathrm{ZFC}\vdash
\bigl(\neg\exists \boldsymbol{x}\in D^n\;P(\boldsymbol{x})=0\bigr)
\longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\]
Our proofs establish solvability equivalent to existence of a finite
syntactic refutation. Neither consistency nor unsolvability is separately
asserted. The domains $\Z$, $\N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and $\Z_{>0}$ are
part of the theorems and may not be silently interchanged.

The comparison snapshot is
\texttt{892d73e1ab4bc5fd34ecca1967b06dc47ca95494} of
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt}{ProveIt}.
It includes the latest pooled-power, quadratic-projection, strict-horizon,
trace-radical and linear-CRT constructions, as well as our preceding report.
We compare with actual supplied equations rather than only earlier headline
records. The accompanying baseline audit records sources and exact metrics.

A bounded late comparison at
\texttt{fb1bed5eb7196cf35abfaa746c18c6ee7eae6cfb} found no newly supplied
endpoint dominating the new quartic dimensions or ten-positive circuit
tradeoffs. Its display had moved to the credited zero-weight6007 tuple:
$15$ positive variables, degree $157$, $66$ integer-leaf operations and
$141{,}226$ raw coefficient bytes. Our checked one-anchor7309 sidecar
remains a deliberately frozen earlier specialization, not a claim to the
latest storage record; no new tuple's calibration is silently substituted.

Operation counts use live binary addition, subtraction and multiplication
over integer leaves. Squares and fixed scalar multiplications each cost one;
register reuse is free. Fixed coefficient ports are not variables, but their
entire coherent integer tuples are part of every equation. Rational-leaf
counts use a different convention and are not silently compared. Exact total
degree, collected support, coefficient height and operation count measure
different costs; a smaller-variable equation need not improve every measure.

\section{Verification and inherited calibration}
The direct quartics are sums of squares of complete lists of quadratic
integer residuals. Their expanded polynomials are supplied coefficient by
coefficient. The compact equations are acyclic polynomial circuits over
exactly the listed free inputs and complete fixed numerals. Powers,
factorials, inequalities, divisions, Pell recurrences and binomial
expressions occurring in proofs are not hidden primitives of a final
polynomial.

Ordinary mathematical proofs and finite verification have distinct roles.
The former establish unbounded soundness and completeness, including
signs, denominator integrality, cofinal choices and arithmetic bootstraps.
The latter bind those arguments to literal rows, gates, coefficient bytes,
coordinate maps and full highest homogeneous components. Tests at finitely
many points are not substituted for a universal argument.

The release authenticates its complete inventory and replays its checks in
fresh disposable directories in normal and optimized Python. It checks
exact identities, complete expansions, source pins, coefficient recipes,
actual corruption controls and preservation of sealed bytes. The extracted
final ZIP is replayed separately before delivery. Detailed receipts state
which commands were actually run; inherited historical receipts are not
represented as fresh execution of all formal dependencies.

The direct constructions retain the numerical proof calculus and its
ZF/ZFC refutation calibration. The compact and root branches retain their
own specified native calibrations. A coherent alternate coefficient tuple
usually changes the specialized polynomial. Similar port names, byte sizes
or elementary numerical bounds never license mixing coefficients from
different encodings. Storage endianness is recorded explicitly.

The reviews were performed separately within this AI-assisted research
workflow, not by outside human referees. No upstream repository was changed.
No new end-to-end Lean or Rocq replay, encoded object-language ZFC proof,
literature-wide priority, or global minimality is asserted.
