in \pathf{lucas_h1868/fixed/}; \pathf{FIXED_COEFFICIENTS.json} specifies

every derived leaf. No undetermined parameter remains.

positions, $W=116$, height 1868 and maximum target 1836.

$-6,2,1$. The dominance gaps are 460,25,0 and target headroom is 32.

$2^{2\,134\,927}$ remains valid; the fixed extraction radix satisfies
$B_0\ge4096\cdot2^{2\,134\,927}b^2$ whenever the source scale is positive.

reduces 86 weighted records to 85 by an exact positive zero-set bijection.

is not silently substituted into the recommended equation.
