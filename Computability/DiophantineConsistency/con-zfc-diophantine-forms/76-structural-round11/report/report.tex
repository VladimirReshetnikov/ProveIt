\begin{abstract}
This report gives a norm-saturation reduction for a fixed ten-integer
Diophantine equation, lowering its exact degree from $1\,576\,864$ to
$1\,245\,324$ with 480 arithmetic operations after a certified packing improvement. A separate
coefficient-height certificate replaces an entire bounded common-multiple
block in the commutative proof source; its degree-oriented local relation
has 23 natural witnesses and degree 14 instead of 50. The latter is a
proved source-level improvement, not a materialized global consistency
polynomial. Completed companions use 37 integers at degree 4, 30 at degree
6, 26 at degree 8, 25 at degree 10, 24 at degree 12, and 23 at degree 16.
Positive-domain reductions give 11 variables at degree 277 with 111
operations and 12 at degree 78 with 101 operations. Actual fixed
coefficients, literal equations, restoration proofs, independently
written audits, and a clean replay archive accompany the report. These
are ordinary mathematical reductions over explicitly retained inherited
calibrations, not new end-to-end proof-assistant certificates or global
minimality claims.
\end{abstract}

\section{Results and exact meaning of the consistency claim}
Let $\operatorname{Ref}_{\mathrm{ZFC}}(p)$ be the fixed recursive syntactic
refutation predicate used by the retained source calculus, and set
\[
 \Con(\mathrm{ZFC})\equiv\neg\exists p\in\N\,
 \operatorname{Ref}_{\mathrm{ZFC}}(p).
\]
For every completed polynomial $P$ in this report, the mathematical
reduction establishes, in reasoning formalizable in ZFC,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:goal}
 \mathrm{ZFC}\vdash
 \bigl[\neg\exists\mathbf x\in D^n\;P(\mathbf x)=0\bigr]
 \ \longleftrightarrow\ \Con(\mathrm{ZFC}),
\end{equation}
where $D=\Z$ or $D=\Z_{>0}$ is stated explicitly. This does not prove
$\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$, decide solvability, or assume arithmetic soundness of
ZFC. It is a reduction to the same syntactic predicate, not a claim that
ZFC proves its own consistency. The inherited source/compiler and Pell
calibrations are essential mathematical dependencies. Their formal
integration remains distinct from the new algebraic arguments.

An operation means one binary addition, subtraction, or multiplication;
scalar multiplication and squaring count, fixed integer leaves and shared

Large fixed coefficients cannot be hidden when comparing overall
simplicity. Here they are retained as exact byte payloads, with their
recipes, bit lengths and hashes. A positive variable is not an unrestricted
integer variable; conversion between those domains is not free.

\paragraph{What is structurally new in this iteration.}
The ten-variable reduction removes a redundant factor of a Pell norm
inside the Lucas square certificate while proving that full residue-class
interpolation survives. The commutative source uses deterministic
coefficient-height injectivity instead of numerical pairwise coprimality.
The low-degree work proves compatible compositions and additional exact
eliminations. In particular, the incoming README already suggested the
37-variable quartic composition and a joint-positivity combination. They
are not represented here as previously unexplored ideas. The 30-variable
sextic and the transport-pivot companions are further completed reductions.
No claim of historical priority for a general mathematical technique is
made.
