\section{Evidence and reproducible verification}
\subsection{What the checks establish}
The archive distinguishes four evidential layers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Literal finite objects.} Division-free graphs and residual
arrays specify the ordinary polynomials. Variable liveness, fixed-port
bindings, ordered domains, operation counts and polynomial identities are
checked directly.
\item \textbf{Exact algebra and data.} Independent sparse-polynomial
implementations verify the seven integer transformations, centered root
identities, ideal-preserving row operation, both positive norms, and
actual-coefficient leading monomials. The full source34 and compact44
coefficient tuples are independently reconstructed from the 943-tile
finite data rather than verified solely by a producer's checksum.
\item \textbf{Unbounded mathematical proofs.} Saturated Lucas reliability,
full interpolation, rational integrality, nearest-square positivity,
coefficient-height injectivity, restoration and completeness are
mathematical arguments. Finite numerical fixtures do not replace them.
\item \textbf{Inherited semantic calibration.} Source/compiler/Pell
interfaces connect the arithmetic sources to finite syntactic ZFC
refutations. These dependencies remain explicitly retained and pinned.
No newly built Lean/Rocq kernel certificate or encoded object-language
ZFC derivation is claimed.
\end{enumerate}

The independent ten-variable audit uses different prime moduli and
nonuniform diagonal directions for exact-degree attainment. For the saturation-only control it compares
the unchanged source prefix to the previous literal equation; for the
recommended height-1868 equation it independently reconstructs and evaluates
the changed sparse source. It verifies
all actual fixed leaves, checks 480 signed modular tail assignments,
1080 local parameter cases and twelve CRT interpolation instances.
The latter are deliberately finite diagnostics, not finite evidence for
all indices. A separately authored checker also covers the entire source
formula rather than only the tail.

The independent low-degree audit verifies all seven arrays, their exact
restoration maps, explicit nonzero top coefficients, the complete four-port
reconstructions and positive11/positive12 identities. The report's compact
quartic, sextic and octic residual lists have a separate exact comparison
against the literal packets. This catches mistakes in typeset formulas,
not only in machine files.

The height certificate has its own 24,844 fixtures, 100 constructed proof
trees, twelve simultaneous beta encodings and 800 residual comparisons.
An independent implementation checks all 20,440 rule lists of length at
most three over labels $0,1,2$, including polynomial equality and
multiplicity recovery. The selector checks and literal Lean-constructor
shape checks are additional finite evidence. Shape checks are not Lean
elaboration.

\subsection{Negative controls and mathematical limits}
The saturated Lucas certificate is \emph{not} a standalone index theorem
without $|C|>1$. The slice $A=72$, $B=31$, $C=-1$ supplies a false-index
countercontrol when that hypothesis is dropped, even with the indicated
residue and range conditions. In the completed consistency equation, the
existing $\Delta>0$ approximation guard proves $|C|>1$ before reliability
is invoked. This order is part of soundness, not an optional optimization.

Likewise, removing the height bound while retaining evaluated product
balance is unsound: $1+3M=(1+M)^2$ at $M=1$ is a collision between
different label multisets. The new register rejects it. The release tests
also include mutations of coefficients and literal equations, ensuring
that verification rejects known changes instead of merely replaying
stored success receipts.

At the reviewed pin, ProveIt has the supplied numerical ZFC-refutation
checker and an internal generic quartic compiler. The reported 83,281 raw
and 76,313 optimized primitive-recursive nodes are source-program-tree
counts, not equation-variable counts. Internal syntax/proof-checker
transport remains open. On the native route, high-mask binding, phase
synchronization and global semantic calibration have not been replaced by
a complete new internal consistency biconditional. Those formal boundaries
must not be inferred away from external Lean statements or finite
coefficient checks.

\subsection{Archive contract}
The top-level \pathf{README.md} gives the precise run commands and output
contract. \pathf{MANIFEST.json} lists every shipped file with its role and
SHA-256. \pathf{verify.py} validates that inventory, copies the packet to
an isolated temporary directory, executes only the explicitly listed
authored builders and checkers, and compares generated literal artifacts
byte for byte. It runs normal and optimized Python so checks must remain
active under \pathf{-O}. No upstream program or refutation search is run.
The original delivery copy is not modified by replay.

The TeX sources and PDF are under \pathf{report/}. Rebuild with
\pathf{sh report/build.sh}; the exact mathematical replay requires Python
and SymPy, while PDF rebuilding additionally requires pdfLaTeX and the
listed standard packages. Native coefficient payloads are compressed
minimal little-endian integers; the Lucas fixed leaves additionally
carry a sign byte. These are complete integer values, not unspecified
parameters. Fixed coefficients are interpreted according to each
component's binding, not by filename alone.

Historical source snapshots retain their own notices, source pins and
original content. Their old relative links and numerical examples describe
the original packages and are not promises that every old dependency is
vendored here. The new operational instructions refer only to shipped
files. The release manifest identifies inert retained upstream programs;
none is in the executable replay allowlist. Full arrival ZIPs, search
logs, caches, abandoned drafts and copyrighted primary articles are not
redistributed.
