\section{Compact literal low degree equations}\label{app:low}
This appendix removes any ambiguity about what is meant by the completed
integer packets. Each equation is $\sum_i r_i^2=0$ for the residuals below.
The six constants are $\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F,A,B$ from
\pathf{positivity/BINDING.json}, with $A=2^{1113}$ and $B=2^{666104}$.
The symbolic $A,B$ here are unrelated to the Lucas parameters.

\subsection{The 37 integer quartic}
The 37 variables are
\[
\begin{split}
&J_3,F,\alpha,z,f,h,r,v,w_3,\tau,\eta,\zeta,y,\gamma,o,j,\\
&N,E,P,c,a,X,D,C_2,L,F_2,V,g,I,B_{\rm aux},\Delta,\\
&t_1,t_2,t_3,e_1,e_2,e_3.
\end{split}
\]
Here and below line breaks in a variable list do not indicate operations.
Put $J=3J_3+1$, $q=\beta J+1$, $W=4(3w_3+1)$,
$k=\eta+\zeta$, $H=4a+3$, $C=q-F-\alpha$ and
\[
 R=(q-F)(N-q-L)+\alpha L+(M_C+qM_F)J,\quad
 T=AFBC-(q-AF-BC)^2.
\]
With $\Pos(p,q,r)=1+p^2+q^2+r(r+1)$, the 24 residuals are
\begin{align*}
&L-q^2+1, &&N-q(L+1),\\
&a-E-(2v+1)N, &&X-Wq,\\
&E+X(E-a), &&c-\eta+(E-a)k,\\
&P-k-E(c-\eta), &&\tau^2-P^2+Pk-1,\\
&C_2-c^2, &&D-X-ac-\gamma H,\\
&\Delta-a^2-H, &&D^2-\Delta C_2-1,\\
&k-1-hE-R, &&(K_0+W)C-F-z(q-1),\\
&F_2-f^2, &&V+c-fo,\\
&V-cj+k-1-hE, &&g+V^2-y^2,\\
&I-(2r+1)C_2, &&B_{\rm aux}-I^2,\\
&B_{\rm aux}-\Delta(F_2-1), &&y^2-B_{\rm aux}g-1,\\
&T-\Pos(t_1,t_2,t_3), &&\eta\zeta-\Pos(e_1,e_2,e_3).
\end{align*}
The direct 37-variable companion instead leaves $J,w$ free, uses
$X=3X_3-1$, and removes $X$ as a coordinate. Its literal rows are retained
in \pathf{positivity/quartic37_direct.json}. Both versions delete the old
$X=2u^2$ guard only under the proved residue orientation.

\subsection{The 30 integer sextic}
Use the 30 coordinates
\[
\begin{split}
&J_3,F,\alpha,z,f,h,r,v,w_3,\tau,\eta,\zeta,y,\gamma,o,j,\\
&E,c,a,D,L,F_2,g,I,\\
&t_1,t_2,t_3,e_1,e_2,e_3.
\end{split}
\]
Define $J,q,W,k,H,C,T$ as above, $X=Wq$, and
$R=((q-1)(q-F)+\alpha)L+(M_C+qM_F)J$. The 17 residuals are
\begin{align*}
 &L-q^2+1,\\
 &a-E-(2v+1)q(L+1),\\
 &E+X(E-a),\\
 &c-\eta+(E-a)k,\\
 &\tau^2+(2\tau-k)E(c-\eta)-1,\\
 &D-X-\gamma H,\\
 &D^2+2acD-Hc^2-1,\\
 &k-1-hE-R,\\
 &(K_0+W)C-F-z(q-1),\\
 &F_2-f^2,\\
 &fo-c-cj+k-1-hE,\\
 &g+F_2o^2-2foc+c^2-y^2,\\
 &I-(2r+1)c^2,\\
 &I^2-(a^2+4a+3)(F_2-1),\\
 &y^2-I^2g-1,\\
 &T-\Pos(t_1,t_2,t_3),\\
 &\eta\zeta-\Pos(e_1,e_2,e_3).
\end{align*}
The coordinate called $D$ here is the centered root, not the old $D$.
Its restoration is $D_{\rm old}=D+ac$; likewise
$\tau_{\rm old}=\tau+E(c-\eta)$.

\subsection{The 26 integer octic and its three companions}
Use 26 variables
\[
\begin{split}
&J_3,F,\alpha,z,f,h,r,v,w_3,\tau,\eta,y,\gamma,o,j,E,c,a,V,I,\\
&t_1,t_2,t_3,e_1,e_2,e_3.
\end{split}
\]
Put
\[
\begin{gathered}
 J=3J_3+1,\quad q=\beta J+1,\quad W=4(3w_3+1),\quad X=Wq,\\
 C=q-F-\alpha,\quad k=1-V+jc+hE,\quad H=4a+3,\quad
 \Delta=a^2+H,\\
 D=X+ac+\gamma H,\quad P=k+E(c-\eta),\\
 R=((q-1)(q-F)+\alpha)(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\\
 T=AFBC-(q-AF-BC)^2,\qquad Z_+=\eta(k-\eta).
\end{gathered}
\]
The 13 residuals are
\begin{align*}
 &a-E-(2v+1)q^3,&& E+X(E-a),\\
 &c-\eta+(E-a)k,&& \tau^2-PE(c-\eta)-1,\\
 &D^2-\Delta c^2-1,&& k-1-hE-R,\\
 &(K_0+W)C-F-z(q-1),&& V+c-fo,\\
 &I-(2r+1)c^2,&& I^2-\Delta(f^2-1),\\
 &I^2(V^2-y^2)+y^2-1,&& T-\Pos(t_1,t_2,t_3),\\
 &Z_+-\Pos(e_1,e_2,e_3).&&
\end{align*}
The following literal substitutions define the companions without any
implicit existential register:
\begin{enumerate}
\item For 25 variables and degree 10, replace $\alpha$ by the free
coordinate $C$, substitute $F=(K_0+W)C-z(q-1)$ and
$\alpha=q-F-C$ everywhere, and delete the transport residual and the
coordinate $F$.
\item For 24 variables and degree 12, delete the six sign coordinates
and the two sign residuals, insert four coordinates $s,p,q_+,r_+$, and
insert the one residual
\[
 TZ_+-(s^2-T-Z_++1)^2-\Pos(p,q_+,r_+).
\]
\item For 23 variables and degree 16, perform both operations in that
order or the reverse order. Their existence proofs and exact degree
certificates were given in the main text.
\end{enumerate}
The JSON packets use \pathf{v_scale}, \pathf{cone_A}, \pathf{cone_B},
and \pathf{t} where this appendix uses $v,A,B,s$. These are renamings
only. Their ordered variable lists are authoritative for evaluators.
