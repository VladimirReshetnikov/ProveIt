# Contribution and inherited sources

This package contains new AI-assisted arithmetic constructions, ordinary proofs,
LaTeX documentation, polynomial arithmetic programs and verification scripts,
prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt project on 23 October 2026.
The primary new result is the thirteen-integer parity norm of degree 1,392,
evaluated in 211 live binary arithmetic operations.

The contribution retains the existing ProveIt source-34 fixed coefficient tuple,
source-71 determinant scales, and the incoming Pell-congruence parent. These
inherited materials remain subject to their original notices. The exact parent
package is retained at `upstream/pell-congruences-source/`, with its `LICENSE`,
`NOTICE.md`, and `licenses/` files. Relevant notices are also copied to the root
`licenses/` directory for convenient review. `SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json` pins the
repository snapshot and parent archive hash.

The included root `LICENSE` follows the inherited ProveIt MIT No Attribution
license. No new restriction is intended for the new contribution. Preserve any
additional attribution required by inherited third-party components.

The independent reviews and finite algebra checks do not claim to constitute a
new proof-assistant certificate of the complete native compiler-to-ZFC theorem.
