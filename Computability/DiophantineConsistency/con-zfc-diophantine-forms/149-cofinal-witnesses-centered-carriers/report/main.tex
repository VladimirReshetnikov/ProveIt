\begin{abstract}
We construct explicit polynomial equations whose unsolvability in their
stated integer domains is provably equivalent in ZFC to syntactic
consistency of ZFC. The direct constructions combine common remainder
certificates with an odd-square radix and exact affine elimination. A
separate natural-domain construction uses an even descending-factorial
horizon and a cofinal beta base, permitting a smaller witness system.
For ten positive variables, a small-base Pell chart delays full denominator
recovery until an exponential value has been established. Centered root
carriers shorten two unrestricted-integer evaluators without changing
variable count or degree.
The complete equations, fixed numerical tuples, reconstruction maps,
ordinary proofs and exact finite checks accompany this report. All
comparisons retain their domain and all measured costs. Inherited
calibrations are explicitly identified; no consistency decision, global
minimality theorem or new end-to-end kernel certificate is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{The objective and the comparison boundary}
For each fixed polynomial $P\in\Z[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ and stated domain $D$,
our target is
\[
\mathrm{ZFC}\vdash
\bigl(\neg\exists\boldsymbol x\in D^n\;P(\boldsymbol x)=0\bigr)
\longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\]
Here $\Con$ denotes the absence of a finite syntactic refutation in the
specified effective proof system. We prove the equivalent solvability
statement, rather than proving consistency or exhibiting an actual zero.
The domain is part of the theorem: unrestricted, nonnegative and strictly
positive integers cannot be interchanged in a complexity comparison.

The immutable comparison snapshot is
\texttt{3ecd6a6cf53d47519228328567f45fb7ee9781d9} of
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt}{ProveIt}.
This includes the seven parallel incoming packages and our preceding
\emph{Residual Bases and Denominator Engineering} package. The new
252-variable common-remainder system and cheaper compact circuits are
used as comparators instead of advertising obsolete improvements over
our previous report alone. Incoming placement is distinguished from
repository adoption and from independent mathematical verification.
The supplied baseline review authenticates the actual archive contents,
full direct polynomial expansions, and coherent fixed tuples.

We report variable counts, total degree, residual count where applicable,
collected support, maximum absolute coefficient, and live binary
arithmetic operations. Each addition, subtraction and multiplication
costs one, including a square and multiplication by a fixed constant;
reused registers are free. A short evaluator need not have small expanded
coefficients. Fixed ports are not quantified variables, but all their
numerals and their coherent binding remain part of the specification.

\section{Reproduction and the exact proof boundary}
The archive supplies the literal residual lists and full expansions for
each direct quartic, complete arithmetic DAGs for the small-variable
families, all coefficient payloads and finite recipes, inverse maps,
source hashes and ordinary proof dependencies. Its top-level replay
works in fresh disposable copies in normal and optimized Python.
Exact finite checks establish the actual polynomial identities, global
leading forms, liveness, source bindings and deliberately corrupted
negative controls. They do not replace any unbounded Pell or logical
theorem by testing finitely many inputs.

The direct integer and natural branches use the numerical C222 calculus
and its ZF/ZFC refutation calibration, not K215 simulation or Jones
universality. The positive and root branches retain their different,
explicit native coefficient calibrations. Whole tuples must be retained:
matching names, bit sizes or elementary inequalities do not justify
mixing a port from one source with another.

The repeated coordinate and residual operations have distinct meanings.
An integral triangular shear is a bijection of an unrestricted integer
lattice. Unit affine elimination gives a uniquely reconstructed coordinate.
A change of the natural horizon or a canonical cofinal witness choice is
\emph{not} an arbitrary natural-coordinate bijection; it requires the new
soundness and completeness arguments supplied in the natural section.
Likewise, alternate coherent coefficient specializations are different
integer polynomials, not identities with their predecessors.

The release includes independently performed mathematical and finite
artifact reviews within this AI-assisted research workflow. These are
not claims of outside human peer review or a new closed-kernel proof.
No upstream repository was modified. No literature-wide priority,
intrinsic arithmetic-complexity lower bound or globally simplest
Diophantine equation is claimed. The limited row-span and coordinate-shear
obstructions are stated only for the transformations they actually cover.
