\paragraph{Exact statement and proof boundary.}
For each selected residual list \(R_0,\ldots,R_{s-1}\), define the fixed
ordinary polynomial
\[
 P(\mathbf z)=\sum_{j=0}^{s-1}R_j(\mathbf z)^2.
\]
Relative to the explicitly retained ordinary C222/ZF/ZFC calibration,
\(P\) has a natural zero exactly when a finite syntactic ZFC refutation
exists.  Consequently ZFC proves that its no-natural-zero sentence is
equivalent to \(\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})\).  This is not a proof
of consistency or a newly kernel-checked end-to-end endpoint.  The finite
checkers establish the literal polynomials and exact algebra; the ordinary
unbounded arguments establish their arithmetic interpretation.  Factorials,
binomials, variable powers and Pell sequences below occur only in that
interpretation, never as primitives of the released equations.

\subsection{Compatible positive centers and pooled low digits}

The retained shift-free descending source has natural coordinates satisfying
\[
 n+1=F^2,\qquad V=BF+r,\qquad B=r+s+1.
 \tag{N1}
\]
The exact-power modulus row \(T_n+n^2+1=2An\) excludes \(n=0\) before
interpreting any power.  Thus \(F\ge2\) and \(n\ge3\).  The common frame
and semantic Pell anchor authenticate six powers,
\[
 V=n^t,\quad N=B^x,\quad q=(NS)^x,\quad
 D_t=q^t,\quad D_y=q^{y+1},\quad D_x=q^x,
 \tag{N2}
\]
where \(S\) is one plus the nine positive independent CRT representatives.
There are thirteen nonanchor seeds and eleven modular digit requests.
The anchor index \(H=J\) is the actual derived syntax-label sum.

First compose the incoming positive deterministic comparison-center
construction with this descending source.  Write its principal pair
\((X,Y)\), auxiliary pair \((U,V_c)\), and comparison pair
\((S_c,R_c)\).  Keep all three Pell norms and impose
\[
 V_c=2Y^2(e+1),\qquad T=U^2,\qquad
 C_c=A+(4Y+1-A)T,
 \tag{N3}
\]
with natural \(C_c-1\), together with
\(S_c=X+Ug\) and \(R_c=H+4Yh\).
The unchanged merged cap row gives
\[
 Y=H+\sum_i(Y_i-k_i)^2+(A-1)Q\ge H\ge2
\]
using only the raw frame \(A\ge2\) and the literal positive label expression.
It precedes index interpretation.  The auxiliary norm yields
\[
 T-1=4(A^2-1)Y^4(e+1)^2,\qquad T>A.
\]
If \(L=4Y+1-A<0\), then \(C_c=A+LT<0\), impossible.  Hence
\(L\ge0\), \(C_c\ge A\ge2\), and
\[
 C_c-1=4YT-(A-1)(T-1)\equiv0\pmod {4Y}.
\]
The old natural center witnesses are restored by
\[
 d=\frac{C_c-1}{4Y}-1,\qquad f=LU,\qquad e_{\rm old}=2e+1.
\]
Their nonnegativity follows on the retained equations; it is not claimed
coefficientwise on the entire free orthant.  Conversely, for canonical
\(Y=Y_H(A)\), choose a positive auxiliary Pell period modulo \(2Y^2\).
Then \(V_c\) is a positive multiple of \(2Y^2\); taking comparison pair
\((X_H(C_c),Y_H(C_c))\) supplies the remaining congruences and natural
quotients.  This uses fresh auxiliary witnesses, not a pointwise bijection
on all old auxiliary tuples.

Next pool each equal-denominator family into one natural linear budget:
\[
 D=\sum_{i\in G}r_i+s_G+1.
 \tag{N4}
\]
The four groups have denominators \(q^t,q^{t+1},q^{y+1},q^x\), and sizes
\(2,5,2,2\).  Soundness restores each old slack by the nonnegative
polynomial \(s_i=s_G+\sum_{j\ne i}r_j\), without any power or binomial
semantics.  The numerator rows that orient the consecutive Pell seeds
remain unchanged.

The separate factorial-output bound \(q=B+o+1\) is redundant.  Once
(N2) is recovered, (N1) implies
\[
 V<B(F+1),\qquad V\ge n^2>F(F+1),\qquad B>F\ge2,
\]
and consequently
\[
 V<B(F+1)\le B^2\le N\le q.
 \tag{N5}
\]
Thus \(o=q-B-1\ge0\) is restored \emph{before} the factorial digit is
interpreted.  Since every \(\binom nj\), \(0\le j\le t\), is at most
\(n^t=V<q\), that digit gives \(B=\binom nt>0\).
The descending floor theorem, valid for \(t\ge3\), then gives \(F=t!\).
For completeness, the canonical ordinary low coefficients are at most
\(S^x\), those for \(J\) are also at most \(S^x\) once
\(\ell(t+1)<x\), and factorial low coefficients are at most \(V<N\).
Every group coefficient sum is therefore at most
\[
 5S^x+N<N^xS^x=q
\]
for \(N\ge4,x\ge2,S\ge2\).  Its geometric low-digit expansion is at
most \(D-1\), supplying the natural pooled slack in (N4).

\subsection{A new even-horizon cofinal beta specialization}

The new structural step sets
\[
 t=2t_0+4,\qquad y+1=V,
 \tag{N6}
\]
in place of \(t=t_0+12\) and \(y=F+r_0+1\), deleting \(r_0\).
The finite coefficient change substitutes
\(t_{0,\rm old}=2t_{0,\rm new}-8\) in every raw residual.
\textbf{This is not a natural-coordinate bijection.}  Its domain theorem
is the soundness and constructive completeness argument that follows.

\paragraph{Acyclic soundness.}
The power-modulus row still gives \(F\ge2,n\ge3\), and the first endpoint
\(x=F+1+(y+1)u_0\) gives \(x\ge F+1\ge3\).
The exponents \(t,x,y+1\) are positive from the natural domains alone.
Thus the frame, restored full cap and unchanged numerator incidences
recover (N2) without first needing \(y\ge F+1\).
Afterwards
\[
 y=(F^2-1)^t-1\ge F+1.
\]
The last endpoint \(x=1+(1+(t+1)y)u_t\) has \(u_t\ge1\), so
\[
 x\ge2+(t+1)y>\max(t+1,y+1).
 \tag{N7}
\]
All required denominator divisibilities for the common-root modulus
\(Nq^x\) follow before the first binomial decoding.
The bootstrap (N5) then identifies \(F=t!\).

Now \(t\ge4,F\ge24,n\ge575\), and hence
\[
 y\ge575^4-1>2^{36},\qquad x>2^{38}.
\]
With \(R=2+t+x+y+F\), one has \(R<2x\).
The unchanged source ledger has \(E=2^{23}\); for every integer
\(x\ge2^{38}\),
\[
 (2x)^E<2^x.
\]
The base case is \(39E<2^{38}\); induction uses
\((1+1/x)^E<2\) for \(x\ge2E\), obtained by majorizing the binomial
expansion by the geometric series in \(E/x\).
The label ledger has \(\ell=2^{22}\), and \(y>\ell\) gives
\(\ell(t+1)<(t+1)y<x\).
These replace exactly the old horizon-12 majorant and label estimates.
They justify all range digits and the strict local bound below \(2^x\).

\paragraph{Constructive cofinal completeness.}
Expand a finite refutation to a finite proof tree.  Choose an \emph{even}
\(t\ge4\) at least its rule occurrences, parameters, full relevant label
sums, and truth-padding label sum, before defining \(F=t!\).
At every backward expansion stage, each pending nontruth vertex is the
root of a distinct unexpanded subtree.  There are therefore at most
\(t\) nontruth factors.  Its label \(j\le t\) has weight
\(1+jF\le1+tF<F^2-1\); truth weights are one.  Every pending product obeys
\[
 S_i\le(1+tF)^t<(F^2-1)^t=V,
\]
so \(S_i\le y=V-1\).  Also \(y\ge F+1\).
Since \(F^2-1\equiv-1\pmod F\) and \(t\) is even, \(F\mid y\).
The beta moduli \(1+(i+1)y\), \(0\le i\le t\), are pairwise coprime:
a common prime would divide an index difference at most \(t\) while
remaining coprime to \(y\), contradicting \(t!\mid y\).
CRT supplies \(x\) with exactly the prescribed pending-state residues,
including \(F+1\) initially and one finally.  Its Euclidean quotients and
all endpoint witnesses are natural.

Continue in this order: descending reciprocal moduli
\(d_i=F^2/i-1\), their product \(B\), \(N=B^x\), precision-\(N^2\)
CRT representatives with the original one-representative cofinal lift,
then \(S,q\), denominators, frame and lower Pell witness, a sufficiently
large common-root-compatible \(A\), principal and seed pairs, and fresh
positive-center auxiliary/comparison witnesses.  The original semantic
anchor dominance follows from that unchanged representative lift.
None of these later choices changes the earlier beta parameters.
The pooled slack construction above completes the new graph.  This proves
cofinal completeness rather than merely a one-way restriction of witnesses.
