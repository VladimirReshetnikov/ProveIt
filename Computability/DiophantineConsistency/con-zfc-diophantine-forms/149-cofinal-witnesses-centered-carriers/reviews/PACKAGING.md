# Packaging scope

The integrated report is the authoritative rendered article. Source TeX, mathematical artifacts, checks and provenance are retained. Standalone subsection QA PNGs and one duplicate subsection PDF were omitted, and the local root-component inventory was restricted to supplied files. This does not modify equations, proofs, source pins, coefficient recipes or executable checks. Full report visual-review evidence is summarized in report_review.md.

Every final mathematical component was replayed in separate normal and optimized disposable copies. The release inventory authenticates all delivered files; the designated top-level replay receipts and the inventory itself are excluded from their own inventory to avoid circular hashing.
