# Attribution and scope

This supplement is prepared for incorporation into Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt
repository. New exposition, scripts and local refinements are provided under the
repository's MIT-0 license in `LICENSE`. Existing source files retain their original
attribution and notices.

The fixed consistency target is the calibrated blank-input K215 machine. Preserve
`licenses/NOTICE-K215.md` and `licenses/LICENSE.Wade` with the machine data.

The source quartic and arithmetic skeletons come from ProveIt package 30. The
mixed-radix coefficient geometry comes from package 25; the general odd-factor
controller is combined with the construction in package 24. The signed Jones
interface comes from package 29. Efficient digit evaluation and shared-power
Horner identities are adapted from package 27. The source snapshot and exact
source copies are identified by the provenance ledgers.

Jones's 1982 universal-equation theorem supplies the reduction behind the
sixteen-variable presentation. Sun's 2021 explicit source coding and 2026
ten-integer construction supply the ten-variable route. We attribute the general
ten-unknowns result to Sun; the fixed consistency specialization, local mergers,
smaller weights and concrete circuit audits are the contributions here.

The ordinary mathematical proofs compose with inherited semantic proofs and the
existing K215 endpoint. The scripts do not establish end-to-end proof-assistant
verification, decide consistency, or certify global minimality.
