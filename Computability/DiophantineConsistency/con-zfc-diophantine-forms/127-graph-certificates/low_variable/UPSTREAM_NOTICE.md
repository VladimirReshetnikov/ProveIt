## Ordinary proof dependencies

The eleven-integer branch uses the native compiler and K215 calibration already
proved in the cited source chain. Its preprojection rank and folding arguments
are retained under `inherited/norm/source/`; its fixed-strong-witness interpolation
is retained as `inherited/norm/powered_saturation_proof.tex`.

The direct branch uses finite C222 refutability. The supplied endpoint texts
identify the proved theorems `KEndpoint.c222_iff_zf_refutation` and
`ConstructibleChoice.zf_refutation_iff_zfc_refutation`. These calculus equivalences
are unconditional theorem declarations; machine-specific hypotheses later in
the endpoint file do not qualify them. The ordinary factorial, exact-digit,
derived-label, and Horn-flow proofs supply the arithmetic bridge. This branch
does not encode K215 execution and does not use Jones's universal expression.

The inherited mathematical theorems are genuine dependencies of the ordinary
proof. No new end-to-end proof-assistant build is claimed. Selected historical
notes describe older incomplete compiler proposals; they should not be confused
with the completed direct arithmetic source used here. See
`direct/source/PROVENANCE_README.md` for its specific dependency map.

## New material

The main manuscript, the four new small-variable graphs and their builder, their
independent algebraic auditor, the new direct sign/frame transformation and
resulting literal quartic, new replay wrappers, and the new ordinary proofs and
reviews were prepared for this contribution. All variables of the delivered
equations are unrestricted integers.

Classical intersectivity of `(t^2+3)(t^3-19)` is credited in the manuscript to
Hyde, Lee, and Spearman, with an elementary proof included. The distinction
between this eleven-variable improvement and the known ten-variable frontier
is explicit. No global optimality or literature-wide priority assertion is made.
