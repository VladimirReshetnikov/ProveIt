# Independent mathematical review: modular digits and signed Pell auxiliaries

Reviewed the current `modular_binomial.py`, its inherited additive/shared index
builders, `MODULAR_BINOMIAL_PROOF.md`, the mixed-domain classifier in
`build_mixed.py`, and the primary Pell development retained as
`research/PellMatiyasevic.lean` (especially `modEq_of_xn_modEq`,
`xn_modEq_x2n_add`, `dvd_of_ysq_dvd`, `yn_modEq_a_sub_one`, and
`matiyasevic`). This review examines unbounded arguments; it is not another
replay of the finite projection checks.

## Assessment

The modular-binomial replacement is mathematically sound under its explicitly
inherited low-digit and direct-source calibration hypotheses. The signed
auxiliary-index relaxation is also sound, with the important interpretation
that arbitrary signed auxiliary coordinates are not required to acquire a
positive sign on the same assignment. They identify the correct main Pell
index; if a literal all-natural parent witness is required, regenerate the
auxiliary frame canonically afterwards.

I found no missing hypothesis in the combined argument, provided that this
regeneration distinction and the positivity of all requested indices are
stated. The exact variable counts belong to the separately checked emitted
polynomial artifacts, not to this mathematical review.

The S,R sign relaxation is already proved in the incoming
`ConZFC_Shared_Pell_1873.zip`,
`con_zfc_batched_pell/proofs/SIGNED_INDEX_THEOREM.md`. It must be credited
as an inherited result, not a new theorem of this iteration. The only
additional observation needed here is that a signed U can be replaced by
its absolute value for classification and congruence moduli; the present
compiler keeps V positive. The derivation below independently validates
the exact imported statement and this harmless U extension.

## 1. Independent validation of the inherited signed index criterion

Write chi_A(i), psi_A(i) for the standard positive Pell coordinates at A>=2.
Suppose the retained main coordinates X,Y are nonnegative, the requested
index k>=1, and Y>=k. The main norm gives

    X=chi_A(i), Y=psi_A(i)

for some i>=1. In particular i<=Y.

Let V>0 remain natural, while U may be any integer. The auxiliary norm gives

    |U|=chi_A(m), V=psi_A(m), m>=1.

The retained condition Y^2|V implies Y|m by the standard Lucas divisibility
lemma. It also gives Y<=V, and strict increase of psi_A then gives i<=m.
These facts do not assume a sign for U.

The second base B remains natural and at least two. In the actual builder
B=1+4V(q4+1), with q4>=0, so B≡1 modulo4Y. The equation
B=A+U*bq, with bq any integer, gives B≡A modulo |U|.

The auxiliary norm at B, with arbitrary integer S,R, gives

    |S|=chi_B(j), |R|=psi_B(j)

for some j>=0. The congruence S≡X modulo U gives

    chi_A(j)≡±chi_A(i) modulo chi_A(m),

using the recurrence's congruence in its base. If the sign is plus, the
primary theorem `modEq_of_xn_modEq` gives j≡±i modulo4m. If it is minus,
`xn_modEq_x2n_add` gives

    chi_A(j+2m)≡-chi_A(j)≡chi_A(i) modulo chi_A(m),

and the same theorem applies to j+2m. In either case

    j≡±i modulo2m,

hence j≡±i modulo2Y, since Y|m.

On the other side, psi_B(j)≡j moduloB-1. Since B≡1 modulo4Y and
R≡k modulo4Y, the possible sign of R yields

    j≡±k modulo4Y,

and therefore also modulo2Y. Combining gives either
`i≡k (mod2Y)` or `i≡-k (mod2Y)`. As 1<=i,k<=Y, the first possibility
forces i=k. The second forces i+k=2Y; its upper bound forces i=k=Y.
Thus in all cases i=k, which is exactly the index conclusion required by
the power and modular-digit arguments.

The exceptional small cases in the primary positive-index theorem cause
no gap: that theorem assumes i>0 and i<=m, which have already been proved.
In particular no inference is made from the more general unsigned
congruence statement at i=0.

## 2. Positivity of newly added indices

The modular builder requests an index for every upper binomial argument.
The direct constructor shifts each encoded column by one, so all eight
range upper arguments are positive. The two factorial upper arguments are
`rho_n+n` with n>=1 and rho_n>0. The product upper argument is `q0+t`,
with q0>=2 and t>=1. Thus every new seed has a positive requested index;
there is no unhandled n=0 case in the shared positive-index certificate.
Derived indices use the exact Pell addition law, so allowing only the
seed auxiliary roots to be signed leaves the derived main pairs correct.

## 3. The common residue root exists cofinally

For q>=2, b=q+1 and M=q^(H+1), the new condition is

    2Ab=b^2+1 (modM).

Because gcd(b,M)=1, gcd(2b,M) is either1 or2. In the even case b is odd,
so b^2+1 is even. The congruence therefore has solutions, and its residue
class contains arbitrarily large A. This validates choosing A only after
all genuine semantic powers, q, H, W and the growth lower bound L have
been fixed. Replacing the old norm for A by a norm for L, followed by
A=L+sigma with sigma>=0, is essential: without this cofinal freedom the
new congruence would not have a completeness proof.

The lower norm indeed proves A>W^W: its second coordinate WZ is positive,
the corresponding Pell index e is divisible by W, hence e>=W, and the
first coordinate is at least(W+1)^e. All retained exact power inputs and
outputs appear among the atoms defining W. Modular-only upper indices do
not need this size bound; their correct main Pell pairs come from the
independent index criterion.

## 4. Ring identity and extraction

The assignment sqrt(A^2-1) -> b-A defines a homomorphism into Z/MZ because
(b-A)^2-(A^2-1)=b^2-2Ab+1. Thus

    X_n+(b-A)Y_n ≡ b^n (modM).

This identity is valid for every nonnegative n and does not assume that
b^n has been materialized or bounded. For positive n it is a positive
integer: X_n>(A-1)Y_n, so the expression exceeds(b-1)Y_n.

For each exact requested digit with d=q^k, the retained inequalities
0<=r<d and0<=c<q identify r+dc as the unique least residue modulo qd.
The inherited low-digit bound binom(n,j)<q for0<=j<=k then gives
c=binom(n,k). For divisibility requests, B|q implies Bd|M and makes the
least residue modulo Bd equal

    r0+d*(binom(n,k) modB), 0<=r0<d.

It lies below d if and only if B divides binom(n,k). The separate
factorial and CRT arguments can therefore consume exactly their old
semantic conclusions.

## 5. Omitted quotient signs and auxiliary reconstruction

For modular quotients, the bounded residue and the positivity just proved
force the quotient nonnegative. The common root quotient is nonnegative
because the common growth frame makes A>=q+1. Ordinary power quotients
are likewise nonnegative once the exact output has been identified as the
least bounded residue of a positive Pell expression. The endpoint and CRT
quotient signs must use their stated Euclidean-remainder/source-positivity
arguments; they do not follow merely from the labels used by the compiler.

In contrast, U,S,R and their congruence quotients need not have positive
signs on an arbitrary integer zero. Absolute values can break the displayed
congruences. Soundness should use the signed-index result above directly.
If comparison with the old all-natural auxiliary frame is desired, once
all main indices have been identified, invoke the inherited simultaneous
index completeness construction at the same A and rebuild its shared
U,V,B and individual S,R. No other semantic quantity depends on those
auxiliary choices. Completeness in the other direction is immediate,
because every old positive auxiliary witness is permitted in the signed
system.

The mixed-domain sign certificate separately proves all coordinates needed
as nonnegative without depending on the omitted guards. Its exclusion of
quotient-product outputs is appropriate. This review does not replace the
separate exact check that every emitted row and classified variable matches
that certificate.

## Scope of acceptance

Accepted as ordinary mathematical reductions, formalizable in ZFC, composed
with the inherited direct proof-calculus calibration and low-digit bounds.
This review does not claim to have compiled a new Lean proof of the signed
variant, validated every transitive direct-source calibration theorem, or
proved ZFC consistency. The newly emitted finite polynomial and monic
projection certificates supply the remaining finite algebraic evidence.
