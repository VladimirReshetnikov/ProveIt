NEW WORK IN THIS PACKAGE

1. A common modular-root condition for indexed Pell residues, together
   with a cofinal choice of the Pell base above a separate growth bound.
   It removes eleven full binomial numerator power graphs while preserving
   the required exact digits and binomial-divisibility tests.
2. Explicit 655-coordinate modular source; 395- and 394-natural quartics;
   integration with inherited signed methods to produce 663- and
   687-integer quartics; all literal residuals and collected coefficients.
3. Exact binding checks for the new arithmetic graph, domain and projection
   certificates, independent checks, corruption controls, documentation,
   mathematical reviews, and deterministic offline release reproduction.
4. Two centered twelve-integer circuits, obtained by integral triangular
   coordinate translations and checked polynomial cancellation identities.

PROOF SCOPE

The article gives ordinary mathematical arguments formalizable in ZFC and
composes them with the named inherited calibration. The executable checks
establish finite graph binding, algebraic identities, exact domains,
restorations, coefficients, degrees, and counts. Finite arithmetic samples
serve only as transcription checks. No new end-to-end kernel-checked
formalization or emitted object-language ZFC derivation is claimed.
