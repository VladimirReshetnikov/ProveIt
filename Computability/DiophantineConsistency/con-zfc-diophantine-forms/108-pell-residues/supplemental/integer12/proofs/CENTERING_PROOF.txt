# Centering the auxiliary norm in twelve integer variables

## Result and inherited boundary

The incoming `ConZFC_Valuation_and_Signed_Certificates_2026-10-23.zip`
contains a rational auxiliary-root polynomial with 12 unrestricted integer
variables, exact degree 2400, and 215 binary operations, and an integral
auxiliary-root polynomial with the same variables, degree 2912, and 209
operations. Two polynomial changes of coordinates improve that pair:

| New polynomial | Integer variables | Exact degree | Live operations |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| centered rational | 12 | 2368 | 216 |
| centered integral | 12 | 2400 | 210 |

The second dominates the old 2400/215 point in arithmetic cost. The first
lowers the degree with one additional gate. These are incremental algebraic
improvements, not a variable-count breakthrough or a global simplicity
claim. The named archived mathematical endpoint, its six fixed constants,
and its native/compiler/calibration assumptions are unchanged. There is no
new end-to-end Lean or Rocq certificate.

## Entire change of variables

Retain every front-end definition in the parent `PRIME_TWELVE.md`, including
all twelve independent integers

    j,C,z,h,r,v,w,T,c,n,t,s

and the six fixed integer coefficients. In particular `S=I H^5`,
`Q=Delta^2 I^2`, `delta=Q-1`, `Cy=Q V^2-1`. The coefficients A and B of the
intersective quintic are unchanged, as are the four integer radicands

    a0=B^2 Dm, a1=9 S^2 Df, a2=81 S^2 Dt, a3=729 S^2 Dg.

The parent polynomials are respectively

    delta^16 F32(S n-A; a0,a1,a2,a3,S^2 Cy/delta),
    F32(S n-A; a0,a1,a2,a3,S^2 delta Cy),

where F32 is the ordinary polynomial obtained by multiplying the 32 signed
radical factors, as explicitly evaluated by the retained division-free
`norm5.py` circuit.

For the new rational polynomial substitute

    n_old = n + V.

For the new integral polynomial substitute

    n_old = n + Q V.

V and Q are integer polynomials in the other eleven coordinates, independent
of n. Each substitution is therefore an automorphism of the full integer
coordinate lattice, with inverse `n=n_old-V` or `n=n_old-QV`. Consequently
this is an exact bijection on zero sets, with no extra positivity, parity,
invertibility, nonvanishing, or canonical-witness hypothesis. In particular
all exceptional branches are preserved, and the parent semantic theorem
transfers without altering any Pell or proof-calibration argument.

The delivered graphs contain only integer constants, the twelve independent
variables, the six fixed coefficient leaves, and binary +, -, and *. There
are no literal divisions or square-root operations.

## Why polynomial degree falls

Set `z0=S n-A`. Introduce the two formal square roots with squares a2 and
a3 only to analyze the retained biquadratic norm circuit. The crucial
scalar cuts are exact integer-polynomial identities:

    delta (S V+z0)^2 - S^2 Cy
      = delta z0^2 + 2 delta S V z0 - S^2(V^2-1),

    (S Q V+z0)^2 - S^2 delta Cy
      = z0^2 + 2 S Q V z0 + S^2(Q V^2+delta).

The first removes the degree-150 scalar contribution; the second removes
the degree-182 contribution. This is why simple syntactic degree propagation
through the unexpanded circuit would be incorrect.

Give every independent variable degree one and every fixed coefficient
port degree zero. The unchanged front-end degree table is

    S:34, Q and delta:32, V:25, z0:35,
    a0:70, a1:88, a2:102, a3:114.

Use weights 51 and 57 for sqrt(a2) and sqrt(a3). In a four-component
biquadratic expression, the table below gives each component's polynomial
degree plus its radical's weight. An entry is an upper bound unless the
strict leading argument below identifies it as attained.

| Stage | Centered rational | Centered integral |
|---|---|---|
| Initial adjusted square q | 146,142,148,140 | 150,142,148,108 |
| First adjusted square | 296,288,294,290 | 300,292,298,290 |
| Second adjusted square | 592,584,590,586 | 600,592,598,590 |
| First final norm, scalar | 1184 | 1200 |
| First final norm, radical component | 1182 | 1198 |
| Final scalar | **2368** | **2400** |

To check the table independently, squaring four weighted components
(a,b,c,f) gives component bounds

    max(2a,2b,2c,2f), max(a+b,c+f),
    max(a+c,b+f), max(a+f,b+c).

The first scalar correction has degree at most 270 and the second at most
522. They cannot affect any scalar leading term in the displayed table.
The final two quadratic norms both have a strictly dominant scalar term.
This establishes the degree upper bounds by exact algebra.

For the rational endpoint, the unique largest initial weighted component
is `-2 Q S V sqrt(a3)`. Its square gives the unique first scalar top term;
all subsequent scalar top terms remain unique. Writing a subscript 0 for
the highest homogeneous part, the exact final top form is

    2^16 Q0^16 S0^16 V0^16 a3_0^8.

As `a3_0=-3^7 S0^2 s^2 eta0^4`, this becomes

    2^16 3^56 Q0^16 S0^32 V0^16 s^16 eta0^32.

The physical front-end leading forms are

    q0=6 beta j, W0=12 w,
    Y0=2 q0^3 v, a0_phys=2 q0^4 W0 v,
    I0=s r c^2, Q0=a0_phys^4 I0^2,
    S0=2^10 I0 a0_phys^5,
    V0=a0_phys^2 I0^2 (4 I0 T-R0),
    eta0=-h a0_phys Y0.

Here `a0_phys` is the leading form of the source coordinate a, not the first
norm radicand a0. Selecting the T term throughout the last factor yields

    [j^1248 w^288 v^320 h^32 s^128 r^112 c^224 T^16] P
       = 2^2512 3^1592 beta^1248 > 0.

Its exponents sum to 2368. This proves exact attainment after specialization
to the actual fixed coefficient tuple; other fixed ports cannot cancel it.

For the integral endpoint the unique initial scalar top term is
`S0^2 Q0 V0^2`. It remains dominant through all four squarings/norms, so the
final top form is

    S0^32 Q0^16 V0^32
      = 2^320 a0_phys^288 I0^128 (4 I0 T-R0)^32.

Therefore

    [j^1152 w^288 v^288 s^160 r^160 c^320 T^32] P
       = 2^2400 3^1440 beta^1152 > 0,

proving exact degree 2400 for the fixed tuple.

## Arithmetic cost and exact checks

The rational translation adds one addition: `S*(n+V)-A` replaces `S*n-A`.
For the integral version first evaluate `QV=Q*V` and `Cy=QV*V-1`.
This reassociation uses exactly the two multiplications previously used by
`Q*(V*V)-1`, while making QV available for the translation. Thus the
integral translation also adds only one addition, not a new multiplication.
Independent live-node counting gives 139 multiplications, 45 additions and
32 subtractions for the rational polynomial, and 133,45,32 for the integral
polynomial.

`check_centered12.py` checks the exact output-polynomial identity against
each archived graph after the stated substitution. It uses exact expression
interning modulo associativity and commutativity of + and *; it does not
rely on random evaluations or equal cryptographic hashes. It separately
expands both central cancellation identities over the integers in four
independent cut variables, checks the weighted-degree recurrence and the
strict scalar comparisons, and recounts the graph operations.

The resulting checker certifies the algebraic reduction and metrics. The
unbounded consistency equivalence remains the explicit inherited theorem,
now transferred by the elementary polynomial bijection above. Finite
checks do not decide ZFC consistency.
