\section{A signed pooled Pell cap: 352 unrestricted integers}
The new direct-calculus polynomial is the sum of 219 squared quadratic
residuals in 352 unrestricted integer variables. Its complete expansion has
9,355 monomials and coefficient height 15,020,718. Relative to the newly
reviewed 442-integer direct quartic, this saves 90 coordinates (20.4\%),
while increasing support and coefficient height. It is not a globally
smallest-variable quartic; native large-coefficient architectures are much
smaller.

The key new principle recovers signs and indices together. Suppose
$A-1>9W^3$, $1\leq k_i<W$, and $m\leq W$. Retain only the signed principal
norms $x_i^2-(A^2-1)y_i^2=1$. Certify once $V=Y_{2W}(A)$ and impose
\[
 V=2W+1+s+\sum_i y_i^2,\quad s\geq0,
 \qquad \sum_i(y_i-k_i)^2=(A-1)Q.
\]
Classification gives $y_i=\epsilon_iY_{j_i}(A)$. The squared cap implies
$j_i<2W$. Reducing the pooled congruence modulo $A-1$ therefore gives a
multiple $E=\sum_i(\epsilon_i j_i-k_i)^2$ with
$0\leq E<9W^3<A-1$. Hence $E=0$, and every ordinate is positive and has
the intended index. Completeness follows from
$Y_{2W}=2X_WY_W>2(A-1)Y_W^2$, which dominates all the squared ordinates.
The old signed-power inverse-branch argument separately orients each
abscissa; the cap alone asserts only its absolute value.

A single finite indexed-Pell certificate at $2W$ supplies $V$. Its auxiliary
ordinate can be constrained by $J=V^2(2e+1)$ with signed $e$, without a sign
guard. Indeed the canonical $V$ is even and its abscissa $U$ is odd. Choosing
$J=Y_{2WV}(A)=VY_V(U)$ gives
\[
 J/V^2=U^{V-1}+(A^2-1)VT,
\]
an odd integer. This saves three additional guard witnesses relative to the
355-integer sibling, which is also supplied.

All variables, coefficients, residuals, and monic restorations are explicit.
The checker replays 229 projections, verifies the 41-atom frame and all
19 retained power requests, and expands the complete quartic. The ordinary
proof inherits the frozen parent's C222/refutation and ZFC calibration;
it is not a new kernel proof or an assumption of consistency. The squared
signed cap extends, and credits, the incoming natural-domain pooled cap.
