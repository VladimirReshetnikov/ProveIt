# Attribution and scope

The signed squared-cap theorem and its concrete finite transformations are new work of this research round. The odd-quotient cap refinement was proposed and independently verified during the same round. The incoming natural pooled-cap construction is credited in PROOF.md and preserved as source/INCOMING_NATURAL_CAP_THEOREM.md.

The underlying direct polynomial, paired sign guards, square factorial anchor, Horn factorization, integer monic elimination, signed Pell and arithmetic proofs are inherited from the frozen prior packet. Their original attribution, source pins, and licenses are retained in inherited_proofs/ and licenses/. The copied utilities are unchanged except for their location. The numerical C222 and ZFC calibration remains an inherited ordinary mathematical dependency, not a newly formalized result.

Comparison counts refer to the direct-calculus, relatively small-coefficient quartic architecture. Other architectures have far fewer integer variables and enormous fixed coefficients. Nothing here claims a global variable-count or worldwide priority record.

No remote repository state was changed. All new programs operate locally on literal JSON and standard-library arithmetic.
