\begin{abstract}
This packet develops explicit Diophantine consistency constructions across
several size criteria. A signed pooled Pell cap compresses the direct-calculus quartic
to $352$ unrestricted integer unknowns. A free-scale carrier lowers the
fifteen-integer degree to $44$. Main-root saturation combined with the
free-scale argument gives an eleven-integer polynomial of degree $352$.
Polynomial radical centering gives ten-positive-variable equations of
degrees $253$ and $252$. A separate single-product saturation reduces both
degree and cost at two low-operation eleven-integer endpoints.

The supplied ordinary proofs are reductions relative to pinned, retained
compiler and refutation calibrations. They do not prove consistency,
global optimality or a new end-to-end formal-kernel theorem. The accompanying
archive contains literal equations and DAGs, complete coefficient payloads,
proofs and independent reviews, source provenance, and offline normal and
optimized Python replay with corruption controls.
\end{abstract}
