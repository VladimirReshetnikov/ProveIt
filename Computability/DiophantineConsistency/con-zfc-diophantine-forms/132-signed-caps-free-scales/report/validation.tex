\section{Independent verification and reproduction}
The root program \texttt{reproduce.py} verifies the immutable inventory,
the exact preceding release, and the hashes of the reviewed execution
closure. It then makes isolated copies, deletes specified generated
artifacts, rebuilds them, and compares exact bytes with the delivered files.
The entire procedure is repeated under ordinary Python and Python with
optimization enabled. Byte-identical receipts use Python 3.12.14 and
SymPy 1.14.0; mode-specific receipts use the matching mode reference. Explicit exceptions, rather than removable
\texttt{assert} statements, enforce the mathematical checks.

The direct checks reconstruct the literal cap formulas, all deleted and
retained parent rows, the complete power-request coverage, the presemantic
lower bounds, the signed Horn rewrite and each monic restoration. They
collect the complete expanded polynomial, rather than infer a quartic
degree or coefficient height from a circuit bound. The negative controls
alter live cap rows, parent data, expansion coefficients and transformation
certificates, requiring every alteration to be rejected.

The fifteen-integer audit checks every named register against an independent
formula, every graph edge and live input, the exact highest homogeneous form
and a nonzero degree-$44$ coefficient. It verifies the authentic coefficient
bytes and rejects all $111$ individual live-gate mutations. Its independent
mathematical review addresses the amplified whole-product bound, the
chronology of signed Pell recovery, late scale recovery, and constructive
completeness. Numerical examples are not substituted for these arguments.

For the positive equations, an independent free-expression normalization
proves whole-output equality with the old polynomial under the proposed
coordinate substitution. The same review checks the full closed formulas,
literal graph census, source bindings, both remainder identities, the complete
four-factor norm and leading forms. The existence direction uses a fresh even
Pell period and proves positivity of the carrier even at zero native transport.

The eleven-integer branch separates the local saturation and free-scale
arguments from inherited native/compiler semantics. Its literal checks and
independent review are distributed together with the exact six sibling
graphs and the two separately reviewed low-cost product graphs. Degree bounds must be attained at the actual fixed coefficients;
syntactic gate-degree bounds alone are not treated as exact degrees.

The archive also exercises missing-file, changed-equation, coefficient-byte,
unsafe-path and ZIP-CRC controls. Its ZIP builder uses fixed metadata and
requires two independent builds to be byte-identical. The root manifest is
an integrity inventory, not a signature. None of these finite checks decides
whether a refutation or polynomial zero exists.

\subsection{Source closure and practical use}
All current execution inputs are local. Historical source code is retained
as inert evidence and is not run by the current replay. The exact previous
release is kept under \texttt{prior/wave15}; its original manifest verifies
every listed byte. The much larger working comparison snapshot is not
bundled indiscriminately. The selected comparison evidence and its provenance
are listed explicitly in \texttt{baseline/SELECTED\_EVIDENCE.json}.

The new mathematical sections were copied from their producer sources, and
report-specific additions are separately identified. The equations are
preserved in TeX, not reconstructed from PDF text. The report is compiled
with shell escape disabled, rendered, and visually reviewed page by page.
Python replay and PDF review are separate checks with separate receipts.

\section{Boundaries and useful next directions}
No new nine-positive-variable or ten-unrestricted-integer construction is
claimed. Nor does the $352$ direct coordinate count compare universally with
the separate native-coefficient quartic family. The nonnegative, positive
and unrestricted domains continue to carry genuinely different constraints.

The gains here are concrete local transformations: a squared cap forcing
signed indices together, an explicitly constructed odd auxiliary quotient,
a free-scale magnitude guard followed by amplified projection, compatible
main-root saturation, and polynomial approximation of a radical center.
The exact equation, certificate and source pins make each transformation
available for further work without conflating it with an overall simplicity
record.

Further reciprocal terms in the positive center do not automatically remove
the remaining reciprocal-$X$ obstruction. A tempting rational center changes
the congruence needed by the old source projection and is therefore not a
proved endpoint. Similarly, a smaller universal carrier would require an
unbounded existence argument with all simultaneous divisibility and
nonvanishing requirements, not only successful modular experiments.
