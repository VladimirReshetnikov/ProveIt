\section{Complete formulas for the positive polynomial family}
To make the next section independent of a reader opening the predecessor,
we give the literal polynomial definitions here. The ten unknowns
\[
 J,F,\alpha,i,T,x,Y,n,\eta,\zeta
\]
are strictly positive integers. The four fixed ports are the original
compact44 tuple, as distinguished above. Put
\begin{gather*}
 q=\beta J+1,\quad X=q+x,\quad \mu=q^2+1,\quad p=\mu Y,\\
 A=p(X+1)+1,\quad \Delta=A^2-1,\quad H=4A-5,\\
 k=\eta+\zeta,\quad c=kY+\eta,\quad v_0=p^2X,\\
 D=v_0(v_0+1)k^2+1,\quad E_0=ic^2,\quad E=2E_0,\\
 G=(q-1)(q-F)+\alpha,\\
 R=G(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\quad e=k-R-1,\\
 C=q-F-\alpha,\quad B=(qK_0+X)C-qF,\\
 F_s=1+\Delta DE^2,\quad Q=\Delta^2DE^2,\quad \delta=Q-1,\\
 b=c(T+\alpha)-R,\quad V=F_sb-c,\quad D_{\rm num}=QV^2-1,\\
 P=q^4(q-1),\quad M=P\mu^2H,\quad K_{\rm pos}=X(3c-2X)+2.
\end{gather*}
For $j=1,2$, use
\[
 Q_1=pv_0k^2,\qquad Q_2=p(v_0-p^2)k^2,\qquad S_j=ADE-E_0Q_j.
\]
Then define
\begin{align*}
 Z_j={}&P\mu^2\bigl[H(n+V+S_j-bB)+K_{\rm pos}\bigr]\\
      &+H\bigl[\mu^2(q(q-1)Y-q^3B-(q-1)X)-Pe\bigr],\\
 s_j={}&\delta Z_j^2,\qquad u=\delta M^2DF_s,\qquad v=M^2D_{\rm num}.
\end{align*}
The sole equation for each sibling is
\[
 \boxed{N_j=(s_j-u-v)^2-4uv=0.}
\]
These expressions are exactly the formulas in \texttt{positive/PROOF.md};
the graph files \texttt{first.json} and \texttt{second.json} specify shared
evaluation schedules, not a different polynomial. In those graphs,
\texttt{I} denotes $E$, and neither symbol introduces an unknown.
