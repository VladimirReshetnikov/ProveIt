# Independent ordinary and literal audit of the round17 direct equations

## Scope and present result

This audit is independent of the producer's executable implementation. Its
checker imports only Python's standard library, reads literal JSON, constructs
its own finite polynomial operations, and does not import or execute the
producer, optimizer, factorizer, or any downloaded code. The receipt records
the precise equations and hashes actually checked. A later file change is not
covered until rechecking. The ordinary argument below is not a bounded test.

The mathematical conclusion is an ordinary, source-calibrated equivalence:
existence of an integer zero of each endpoint is equivalent to existence of a
finite ZFC refutation. Negation gives the corresponding ZFC-provable
equivalence to Con(ZFC), using the inherited complete C222/native calibration.
It is not a proof of Con(ZFC), a new end-to-end proof-assistant verification,
or a globally minimal variable/support/height claim.

The Horn audit verifies every old affine-square root by multiplication,

product(L_i^2)=(product L_i)^2 and r^2s^2=(rs)^2. Consequently rebuilding

For every projection step the checker confirms that the deleted row is

already deleted variable, and that substitution into every remaining row
keeps degree at most two. It checks the complete retained coordinate and row
maps, all metadata, and the full set of truly unused coordinates. Reverse the

nonnegative-domain assumption.

Each centering map is checked as an integral affine bijection, including the
literal identity of its predecessor equation with a checked projected source.
The complete quartic is independently collected from the final residuals and

Eight actual malformed-input controls must be rejected: odd-helper multiplier,

Normal and optimized-Python runs use explicit exceptions, not assertions.

## 2. The odd scale is sound for either sign

Write the exact frame as W=4+2 sum a_i^2. It is even, W>=516, and each
semantic atom has absolute value less than W. In particular t=16+h0^2 is
nonzero, and all fifteen proposed indices have absolute value less than W.
The scale says T=W(2z+1), E=(W+1)T and A^2+T^2-E^2=1. Hence T is
nonzero and Pell classification at W+1 gives |T|=Y_n(W+1), n>=1.
Since Y_n(W+1)=n modulo W, W divides n. Thus
|A|>=X_W(W+1)>W^W and |A|-1>4W^3. These bounds precede all power semantics.

For completeness, choose A=X_W(W+1)>0. For any odd base b, X_n(b) is
odd, Y_n(b) is odd for odd n, and Y_(2n)=2X_nY_n. Removing powers of
two from n proves v2(Y_n(b))=v2(n). Together with W|Y_W(W+1), this
makes Y_W(W+1)/W a positive odd integer. Thus the required z is integral.

## 3. Signed odd helper, including the dangerous comparison endpoints

Let a=|A|, L=|C|, and distinguish the comparison base D from the positive
factorial carrier B. The cap inequality gives C^2>=1+sum y_i^2. If L=1,
all primary ordinates are zero, so the pool makes 0<sum k_i^2<W^3 divisible
by A-1. This contradicts |A-1|>4W^3. The strict positivity uses the actual
nonzero t target, not any assumed sign of the other indices.

Therefore L>1. Pell classification gives L=Y_i(a), |U|=X_i(a), i>=2,
and L>=2a>W, 0<i<L. The new helper row J=C^2(2e+1) gives
|J|>=L^2>1. Its norm gives |H|=X_l(a), l>=2, and
|H|>=2a^2-1>a+1>=|A-1|. If d+1=0 then D=1, while D=A+Hf would
make H divide A-1, an impossibility. Hence d+1 is nonzero and
|D|=|1+4J(d+1)|>=4|J|-1>=15.

In particular D=-3 is not an overlooked allowed endpoint of a complete
zero. Its defining equation would require J(d+1)=-1, so |J|=1; then the
odd-square-divisor row forces |C|=1, already excluded by the cap and pool.
The old comparison-base-lower predicate has NOT been recovered on the
original tuple. Negative D of larger magnitude may occur, and sign reflection
of J alone cannot license the old theorem.

Now apply the inherited positive-base Pell divisibility contracts to absolute
ordinates: L^2 divides |J| implies i<=l and L|l. Since R=W modulo 4C
and 0<W<L, R is nonzero; the comparison norm at |D| has an absolute
index j>=1. For D<0, the polynomial identities
X_j(D)=sgn(D)^j X_j(|D|) and Y_j(D)=sgn(D)^(j-1)Y_j(|D|)
absorb into the independent signs of the coordinates. Thus D=A modulo H
and S=U modulo H imply X_j(a)=±X_i(a) modulo X_l(a), giving
j=±i modulo 2l. The index-one exceptional endpoint is not used: i>=2.

Also D=1 modulo 4L and R=W modulo 4C imply j=±W modulo 4L. This
uses polynomial reduction of Y_j at base one and is equally valid for
negative D and C. Since L|l, i=±W modulo 2L. Both i and W lie strictly
between zero and L, so i=W. Hence |C|=Y_W(a).

The cap bounds every primary absolute Pell index j_i below W. Put
epsilon=sgn(A), and write y_i=sigma_iY_(j_i)(a). For j_i>0 define
tau_i=sigma_i epsilon^(j_i-1); at zero either choice is harmless.
Modulo A-1 the pool is the sum of (tau_i*j_i-k_i)^2. This nonnegative
integer is less than 4*15*W^2<4W^3<|A-1| and divisible by A-1, hence
zero. Thus y_i=Y_(k_i)(A), x_i=±X_(k_i)(A), including signed/zero k_i.
Only this finite index-error representative is bounded, not the original
potentially enormous sum of squared ordinate differences.

## 4. Canonical helper completeness and genuine old-source restoration

Choose positive A after fixing the semantic front. Let U=X_W(A),
C=Y_W(A), H=X_C(U)=X_(WC)(A), and J=C*Y_C(U)=Y_(WC)(A).
Because W is even, U and H are odd and C is even. Polynomial expansion
of Y_C(U) modulo C, using U^2-1=(A^2-1)C^2, shows C|Y_C(U).
The odd-base valuation identity gives that Y_C(U)/C is odd. Therefore
J/C^2 is a positive odd integer and e=(J/C^2-1)/2 is integral.

The helper norm gives gcd(H,J)=1. Since H is odd, gcd(H,4J)=1. CRT
therefore chooses arbitrarily large positive D with D=A modulo H and
D=1 modulo 4J. It is essential to use 4J here, not just 4C: the literal
row is D=1+4J(d+1). Choose S=X_W(D), R=Y_W(D); polynomial base
transfer and reduction at one provide S=U modulo H and R=W modulo 4C.
All quotient and gate witnesses now exist, including the complete helper.

For restoring an arbitrary new zero to the old fused source, keep its A,C,U
and primary pairs, use a=|A| and L=|C| in the same construction, choose

Restore z_old=|2z+1|-1 and the corresponding positive scale ordinate gates,

## 5. Acyclic arithmetic recovery

The retained front guards give B=r+s+1>=1 and q=B+o+1>=2. At positive
t, the q-base request orients its primary pair and yields q^t. Its same
pair at base F^2+1 yields V0=(F^2+1)^t even at the possible base-one
endpoint. The ceiling FB=V0+s then gives F>=1. In fact
V0>F(F^2+1), so B>F^2+1 and q>=3. This does not presuppose a factorial.

For clarity, all signed-index branches are essential. At positive k and
n>=2, the canonical branch gives m=n^k and the opposite branch would
give mn^k=-1. At k=-l<0, the canonical abscissa branch would give
mn^l=1, impossible, and the opposite gives m=-n^l. At zero, m=±1.
These are exact consequences of the Pell power congruence and the strict
frame bounds on |m n^l±1| and |m±n^l|. Thus a q-base output >=2 forces
a positive index; a q+1-base output congruent to 1 modulo q excludes a
negative index once q>=3, but does not alone exclude zero.

The exact t+1 recurrence supplies q^(t+1). The two weighted support sums
are q^t-2 and q^(t+1)-2, hence positive. For integers u,v and q>0,
uv>=0 and qu+v>0 force both u,v>=0, because one negative factor forces
the other nonpositive. This restores the two deleted scalar pairs.

For the cofinal-y variants, y=V0(V0-1)>=F+1, so r0=y-F-1>=0 and
the y+1 power is positive. Its weighted support sum restores the third
scalar pair. In the conservative variant, the retained scalar y-support
pair gives its output >=2 directly; the signed-index case analysis yields
y>=0 and its correct power. The retained x-support pair likewise gives
x>=0 and q^(x+1). No third-pair sign is borrowed before its positive sum.

Now the eleven exact successor residue ideals supply every q+1 output
as 1 modulo q, without an early sign or power interpretation of N. They
force all nine independent representative targets to be nonnegative.
The strict P8 graph forces the first eight positive; in the conservative
variant the mixed graph also forces r0>=0. The ninth zero endpoint would
give B*v27=F^2+1, impossible because 0<F^2+1<B. Thus all nine are
strictly positive. The derived constructor is then positive by its literal
monotone source construction. All principal and derived powers are now
genuine, and all interval signs and natural quotient signs follow from
their positive divisor and remainder bounds.

## 7. Status and limitations

application to this changed signed domain, the complete canonical helper
restoration, and the checked finite compiler maps. A source-closure seal and
