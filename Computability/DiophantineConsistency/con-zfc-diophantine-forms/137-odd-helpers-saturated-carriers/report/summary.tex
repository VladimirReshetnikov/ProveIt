\begin{abstract}
We give eight explicitly specified fixed polynomials whose zero nonexistence,
in their stated domains, is equivalent to the syntactic consistency of ZFC
at the retained ordinary compiler calibration. Three small-coefficient direct
quartics use $307$, $308$ and $310$ unrestricted integer variables, with explicit
support and coefficient-height tradeoffs. Root absorption lowers the fourteen-integer degree from $108$ to $88$
at the same $119$-operation cost, and improves the thirteen-integer
degree/cost pair from $274/129$ to $156/127$.
A separate eleven-integer equation improves the degree/cost pair from
$654/117$ to $648/115$. Two ten-positive equations reduce the supplied
product-square evaluator costs by three operations, to $81$ and $82$,
at increased degrees $750$ and $560$.

The reductions use signed odd helper quotients, exact integral projections,
parity separation within common square classes, unit-product conjunctions
and saturated carriers. Complete equations, coefficient payloads, ordinary
proofs, source pins, independent audits and offline replay accompany the
report. These are arithmetic equivalences, not a proof of consistency,
a global optimality theorem or a new end-to-end proof-assistant certificate.
\end{abstract}
