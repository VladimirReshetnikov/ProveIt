\subsection{The exact new helper simplification}

\]

The cap and nonzero target exclude $|C|=1$: otherwise all primary ordinates

\[

$H\mid A-1$, a contradiction. Therefore $|D|\ge4|J|-1\ge15$.

\[

 J=C Y_C(U)=Y_{WC}(A).
\]

$Y_C(U)=CU^{C-1}+(U^2-1)T$, with
$U^2-1=(A^2-1)C^2$, also proves $C\mid Y_C(U)$.
Thus $J/C^2$ is an odd integer.
Also $H$ is odd and coprime to $4J$, so CRT supplies a large positive

the complete helper. Soundness restores the old positive helper by this
