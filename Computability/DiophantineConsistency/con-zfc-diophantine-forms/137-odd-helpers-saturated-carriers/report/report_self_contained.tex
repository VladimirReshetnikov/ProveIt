\begin{abstract}
We give eight explicitly specified fixed polynomials whose zero nonexistence,
in their stated domains, is equivalent to the syntactic consistency of ZFC
at the retained ordinary compiler calibration. Three small-coefficient direct
quartics use $307$, $308$ and $310$ unrestricted integer variables, with explicit
support and coefficient-height tradeoffs. Root absorption lowers the fourteen-integer degree from $108$ to $88$
at the same $119$-operation cost, and improves the thirteen-integer
degree/cost pair from $274/129$ to $156/127$.
A separate eleven-integer equation improves the degree/cost pair from
$654/117$ to $648/115$. Two ten-positive equations reduce the supplied
product-square evaluator costs by three operations, to $81$ and $82$,
at increased degrees $750$ and $560$.

The reductions use signed odd helper quotients, exact integral projections,
parity separation within common square classes, unit-product conjunctions
and saturated carriers. Complete equations, coefficient payloads, ordinary
proofs, source pins, independent audits and offline replay accompany the
report. These are arithmetic equivalences, not a proof of consistency,
a global optimality theorem or a new end-to-end proof-assistant certificate.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and the exact logical claim}
The objective is an explicitly specified fixed polynomial with integer
coefficients whose zero existence, in the domain stated for the construction,
is equivalent to existence of a finite syntactic refutation of ZFC.
Writing $D$ for that domain, the ordinary arithmetic equivalence has the form
\[
 \mathsf{ZFC}\vdash
 \bigl(\neg\exists\mathbf x\in D^n\;P(\mathbf x)=0\bigr)
 \longleftrightarrow\operatorname{Con}(\mathsf{ZFC}).
\]
The equivalence does not prove either side. The fixed recursive presentation
and proof coding are those of the inherited source calibration. No consistency
assumption is used to choose coefficients or establish the zero-set reductions.

The new proofs are ordinary unbounded mathematics relative to the retained
source theorems. Neither a successful program replay nor source checksums
supply a fresh end-to-end Lean, Rocq, or object-language ZFC certificate.
The inherited compiler, Pell and formal-system calibration interfaces remain
substantive mathematical dependencies. Exact finite computation checks the
new transcription, identities, degree certificates and data integrity; it does
not replace those unbounded hypotheses.

The domains $\N$, $\Z_{>0}$ and $\Z$ are distinct comparison classes.
Each construction states its domain explicitly. A named polynomial register
is an abbreviation to be substituted, not an additional existential unknown.
Proof-only divisions, Pell sequences and radicals are not operations of the
supplied division-free equations.

Circuit cost counts live binary additions, subtractions and multiplications;
a square or multiplication by a fixed numeral is one multiplication.
An exact degree is distinguished from a syntactic DAG upper bound. Fixed
coefficient bit length, variable count, total degree, circuit cost, support
and coefficient height are separate criteria. A gain in one criterion need
not dominate another construction.

\subsection{The exact new helper simplification}
Write $W$ for the even square frame, $A$ for its signed Pell parameter,
$(U,C)$ for the single cap, and $(H,J)$ for its auxiliary Pell pair. Retain
the quadratic cap and primary-only pooled congruence, but replace
\[
 J=C^2(e+1)\quad\hbox{by}\quad J=C^2(2e+1),\qquad e\in\mathbb Z.
\]
This removes the remaining comparison-base sign graph. The full proof does
not assert that its signed comparison base becomes positive.

The cap and nonzero target exclude $|C|=1$: otherwise all primary ordinates
would vanish and a strictly positive sum of small index squares would be
divisible by $A-1$. Hence $|C|>1$. The odd quotient gives $|J|\ge C^2>1$,
so Pell classification gives
\[
 |H|\ge 2|A|^2-1>|A-1|.
\]
If the comparison constructor $D=1+4J(d+1)=A+Hf$ had $d+1=0$, then
$H\mid A-1$, a contradiction. Therefore $|D|\ge4|J|-1\ge15$.
The existing index-residue proof works at negative $D$ by polynomial Pell
base transfer and independent coordinate signs. It recovers
$|C|=Y_W(|A|)$, then every canonical signed-index primary ordinate.

For completeness choose $U=X_W(A)$, $C=Y_W(A)$ at positive $A$.
Since $W$ is even, $U$ is odd and $C$ is even. Set
\[
 H=X_C(U)=X_{WC}(A),\qquad
 J=C Y_C(U)=Y_{WC}(A).
\]
For odd $U$, the doubling identity $Y_{2n}(U)=2X_n(U)Y_n(U)$ and parity
at odd indices imply $v_2(Y_n(U))=v_2(n)$. The expansion
$Y_C(U)=CU^{C-1}+(U^2-1)T$, with
$U^2-1=(A^2-1)C^2$, also proves $C\mid Y_C(U)$.
Thus $J/C^2$ is an odd integer.
Also $H$ is odd and coprime to $4J$, so CRT supplies a large positive
$D\equiv A\pmod H$, $D\equiv1\pmod{4J}$. Its index-$W$ pair supplies
the complete helper. Soundness restores the old positive helper by this
fresh construction, not by an invalid whole-tuple sign reflection.

\section{Absorbing a positivity square with fourteen integers}
\label{sec:positive-root14}
The polynomial below uses fourteen unrestricted integer variables, has exact
ordinary total degree $88$, and a division-free evaluator with $119$ binary

consistency nor a new end-to-end formal-kernel certificate.

\[

radical, inequality or variable exponent appears in its literal DAG.

\subsection{An exact, unsquared conjunction}
For integers $A,B$ with $A,B\ne-2$, the equation
$(A+1)(B+1)(C+1)=1$ is equivalent to $A=B=C=0$: all three factors must be units,
and the first two cannot be $-1$.
Here $N$ is a square modulo $4$, hence $N\ne-2$. Write $A_0=a+2$, so
$\Delta=A_0^2-1$. If $A_0$ is odd, $B\equiv f^2-1\pmod4$; if $A_0$ is even,
$B\equiv f^2+I^2-1\pmod4$. Neither can be $2\pmod4$, so $B\ne-2$.
Thus $P=0$ is exactly $N=B=C_2=0$, with no nonnegativity assumption on $N$.
For comparison, the general construction $\prod_i(3R_i+1)=1$ also encodes
$R_i=0$ over integers; the present residue exclusions avoid those extra scalar
multiplications. No novelty is claimed for that general unit-product device.

\subsection{Separation and extraction before invoking the source}
Since $\beta=2^{1064921}-1$ is odd and greater than one, $q$ is even and nonzero.
Also $H$ is odd and nonzero. Thus $U\ne0$ on every integer assignment, and
$|X|=|qW|\ge4|q|\ge8$.
Introduce proof-only quantities
\[
 D=XY^2,\quad A=4\bigl(1+D(D+1)k^2\bigr),\quad
 b=2L+k,\quad t=Z/U,\quad\rho=t+b.
\]
The exact norm identity is
\[
 N/U^4=(\rho^2-A-G)^2-4AG. \tag{L1}
\]
Because $D(D+1)\ge0$ for integer $D$, $A\ge4$. At $N=0$, (L1) forces
$G\ge0$. Therefore $B_{\rm ud}=G+1+p_2^2+p_3(p_3+1)>0$, which excludes
$Y=0$ and $k=0$. Directly, $B_{\rm ud}\le Y^2k^2q^2$. Since
