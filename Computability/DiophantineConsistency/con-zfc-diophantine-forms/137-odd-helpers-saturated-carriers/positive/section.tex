\section{Canonical saturation of the doubled Pell product square}
\label{sec:positive81}
Two new literal polynomials in ten strictly positive integer unknowns have
\[
 (\text{operations},\text{degree})=(81,750),\qquad(82,560).
\]
They combine canonical $q$-cube saturation with the incoming doubled-Pell
product-square construction. Compared with the latter's $(84,436)$ and
$(85,328)$ points, this saves three gates at a degree cost. The complete
\emph{directly indexed BFS943 compact44} coefficient tuple is unchanged.
This is an ordinary arithmetic reduction using the retained source/native
calibration, not a variable-count record, a global minimum, a consistency
proof, or a newly generated formal proof endpoint.

\subsection{Literal equations}
The independent variables are
$J,F,\alpha,i,T,w,s,n,\eta,\zeta\in\mathbb Z_{>0}$.
The four fixed numerals $\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F$ are supplied in the coefficient
payload and satisfy $\beta\ge31$ odd, $K_0>0$ and
$0<M_C<\beta<M_F<2\beta$. Define
\begin{align*}
q&=\beta J+1,& h&=q-1,& X&=wq,& \mu&=q^2+1,&Y&=q^3s,\\
k&=\eta+\zeta,&c&=kY+\eta,&A&=\mu Y(X+1)+1,&\Delta&=A^2-1,&H&=4A-5,\\
V_0&=X\mu^2Y^2,&E&=1+V_0(V_0+1)k^2,&U&=ic^2,\\
R&=[h(q-F)+\alpha](q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\\
e&=k-R-1,&C&=q-F-\alpha,&B&=(K_0+w)C-F,\\
a&=\Delta U^2E,&F_0&=1+a,&G&=EF_0,&F_{\rm sq}&=1+4aF_0,\\
Q&=\Delta(F_{\rm sq}-1),&b&=c(T+\alpha)-R,&V&=F_{\rm sq}b-c,\\
K_+&=X(3c-2X)+2,&M&=h\mu^2H.
\end{align*}
Choose $L=V^2$ for the 81-gate equation or $L=bV$ for the 82-gate equation,
and put
\begin{align*}
 Z&=h\mu^2[H(n-LB)+K_+]-H[\mu^2B+he],\\
 R_G&=M^2G,\\
 \boxed{N=Q(Z^2-R_GV^2)+(R_G-Z^2)=0.}
\end{align*}
All symbols are polynomial abbreviations, not additional unknowns.
With proof notation $\delta=Q-1$, $D_y=QV^2-1$,
$\kappa=K_+/H$, $\rho=e/\mu^2$, $t=B/h$ and $W=1+hL$,
\begin{equation}
 N=\delta Z^2-M^2GD_y,
 \qquad \frac ZM=n+\kappa-Wt-\rho.
 \label{eq:positive81-carrier}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Normalization and positive soundness}
For the matching source133 polynomial, the substitutions
$Y_{\rm old}=q^3s$ and $n_{\rm old}=n-s$ give whole-polynomial identities
\[
 Z_{\rm old}=q^3Z,\qquad M_{\rm old}=q^3M,
 \qquad N_{\rm old}=q^6N.
\]
The exponent is six, because this output is quadratic in the cleared
coordinates. The shifted $n-s$ need not be positive, so this identity alone
is not used as a positive soundness argument.

All source kernel inequalities hold because $Y>0$. On every positive
input, $G,\delta,D_y,M$ are positive. Pell doubling gives
\[
 F_{\rm sq}=(1+2a)^2,\qquad Q=4\Delta^2U^2G,
 \qquad \gcd(G,\delta D_y)=1.
\]
At a zero, $\delta(Z/M)^2=GD_y$, so prime valuations force $G=g^2$.
Since $F_0=1\pmod E$, its coprime factors are squares:
$E=\tau^2$, $F_0=f_0^2$, $g=\tau f_0$.
Rational adjacent-Pell descent makes
$y=|Z/M|/g$ an integer satisfying
\[
 QV^2-(Q-1)y^2=1,\qquad Z/M=\varepsilon gy,
 \quad \varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}.
\]
The doubled strong pair $f=1+2a$, $I=2Ug$ satisfies
$f^2-\Delta I^2=1$, $Q=(\Delta I)^2$ and $c^2\mid I$.
The retained all-index folding theorem and the first norm therefore give
$c=\psi_A(p)$ for odd $p\ge3$, $k\ge8V_0+4$, $b>c$,
$V>0$, $K_+>0$, and the one-sided bound $\rho<b/\mu^2$.
These deductions use neither the transport sign nor carrier divisibility.

For $L=V^2$, the inequalities $g<a$ and
$V>(F_{\rm sq}-1)b>ab>3g$ imply $gy<V^2/2$.
If $B\le-1$, equation~\eqref{eq:positive81-carrier} gives, for either
root sign,
\[
 n=\varepsilon gy-\kappa+Wt+\rho
 <V^2/2-V^2+V/\mu^2<0.
\]
For $L=bV$, the retained fifth-auxiliary-index argument first gives
$V>8Q^2$ and $b>4g$. Hence $gy<3bV/8<L/2$ and negative $B$ would give
\[
 n<L/2-L+b/\mu^2<0.
\]
Both contradictions concern the actual positive $n$, not $n-s$.
Thus $B\ge0$ and $C>0$.

The later arithmetic now follows in its original order: rank recovery
gives $p=R$, then parity gives even $q$; only then denominator coprimality
gives $\mu^2\mid e$. The first-index comparison and ratio bounds hold
unchanged. Clearing the integral carrier modulo $H$ gives
$H\mid hK_+$ and therefore the source's weaker premise
$H\mid q^3hK_+$. Its whole-product projection and height estimate force
$X=2^R$, without cancelling nonunits. Then $\kappa$ is integral, and
$W=1\pmod h$ gives $h\mid B$. Finally $q^3\mid Y$ is literal.
The fixed-minus parity, exact half-binomial rounding and fresh unscaled
source reconstruction yield a finite refutation through the unchanged
whole indexed calibration.

\subsection{Canonical completeness}
From a finite refutation, use the source133 canonical outer and first
Pell data, including $q^3\mid Y$. Choose its fresh seed period divisible
by $c^2\tau$, double that seed, and use its explicit odd auxiliary
witness. The source's positive carrier is
\[
 n_{\rm old}=gy+Wt+\rho-\kappa-Y/q^3>0.
\]
Set $s=Y/q^3>0$ and $n=n_{\rm old}+s>0$.
The normalization identity gives $q^6N=N_{\rm old}=0$, hence $N=0$.
Equivalently $n=gy+Wt+\rho-\kappa>0$, since $g>\kappa$ and
$Wt,\rho\ge0$. This includes native transport $t=0$ and does not
assume simultaneous solvability of unrelated Pell equations.
Thus absence of a positive zero is equivalent to $\operatorname{Con}
(\mathrm{ZFC})$ under exactly the retained ordinary calibration.

\subsection{Exact degree and reproducible arithmetic}

\[
 8,5,11,30,68,98,136,152,6,142,13,2.

$\delta Z^2-M^2GD_y$ have degrees750 and560. For $L=bV$, their
