\section{Absorbing a positivity square: fourteen integers, degree 88}
\label{sec:positive-root14}
The polynomial below uses fourteen unrestricted integer variables, has exact
ordinary total degree $88$, and a division-free evaluator with $119$ binary
operations ($67$ multiplications, $27$ additions and $25$ subtractions).
It is equivalent by explicit two-way maps on every zero to the pinned
fifteen-variable free-scale degree-$44$ source. Consequently its zero
nonexistence is equivalent in ordinary ZFC to syntactic $\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$
under that unchanged compiler/calibration theorem. This is neither a proof of
consistency nor a new end-to-end formal-kernel certificate.

\subsection{The complete fixed equation}
All unknowns are unrestricted integers:
\[
 j,C,z,h,r,v,w,T,c,n,f,y,p_2,p_3.
\]
The six fixed numerals $\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F,A_c,B_c$ are exactly the authenticated
source34 payloads, with $M_F$ already shifted. The remaining names are polynomial
abbreviations, not extra witnesses:
\begin{gather*}
 J=6j+1,\quad q_m=\beta J,\quad q=q_m+1,\quad W=12w+4,\\
 X=qW,\quad Y=v,\quad F=(K_0+W)C-zq_m,\\
 R=(q(q-F)-C)(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\\
 E=XY,\quad a=E+Y,\quad H=4a+3,\quad\Delta=a^2+H,\\
 k=R+1+hE,\quad \eta=c-Yk,\quad L=E(Yk),\\
 I=(2r+1)c^2,\quad Q=\Delta(f^2-1),\quad V=f^2(cT-R)-c,\\
 M=2q^3,\quad U=MH,\quad K_{\rm lo}=X(2X-3c)-2,\\
 Z=Un+MK_{\rm lo}+H(Y-q^3),\\
 D_{\rm err}=(A_cF-q)^2+(B_cC-q)^2,\\
 B_{\rm ud}=(Yk)^2(q^2-D_{\rm err})
       -q^2\bigl(4(Y\eta)^2+(kq^3)^2\bigr),\\
 G=B_{\rm ud}-1-p_2^2-p_3(p_3+1).
\end{gather*}
Set
\begin{align*}
 C_1&=(Z+Uk)^2+4U(LZ-U),\\
 B&=f^2-\Delta I^2-1,\\
 C_2&=V^2-(Q-1)y(2V+y)-1,\\
 N&=(C_1-U^2G)^2-16U^4G\bigl(1+L(L+k)\bigr).
\end{align*}
The single equation is
\[
 \boxed{P=(N+1)(B+1)(C_2+1)-1=0.}
\]
The evaluator shares $U^2G$, $q^3$ and the already-counted registers. No division,
radical, inequality or variable exponent appears in its literal DAG.

\subsection{An exact, unsquared conjunction}
For integers $A,B$ with $A,B\ne-2$, the equation
$(A+1)(B+1)(C+1)=1$ is equivalent to $A=B=C=0$: all three factors must be units,
and the first two cannot be $-1$.
Here $N$ is a square modulo $4$, hence $N\ne-2$. Write $A_0=a+2$, so
$\Delta=A_0^2-1$. If $A_0$ is odd, $B\equiv f^2-1\pmod4$; if $A_0$ is even,
$B\equiv f^2+I^2-1\pmod4$. Neither can be $2\pmod4$, so $B\ne-2$.
Thus $P=0$ is exactly $N=B=C_2=0$, with no nonnegativity assumption on $N$.
For comparison, the general construction $\prod_i(3R_i+1)=1$ also encodes
$R_i=0$ over integers; the present residue exclusions avoid those extra scalar
multiplications. No novelty is claimed for that general unit-product device.

\subsection{Separation and extraction before invoking the source}
Since $\beta=2^{1064921}-1$ is odd and greater than one, $q$ is even and nonzero.
Also $H$ is odd and nonzero. Thus $U\ne0$ on every integer assignment, and
$|X|=|qW|\ge4|q|\ge8$.
Introduce proof-only quantities
\[
 D=XY^2,\quad A=4\bigl(1+D(D+1)k^2\bigr),\quad
 b=2L+k,\quad t=Z/U,\quad\rho=t+b.
\]
The exact norm identity is
\[
 N/U^4=(\rho^2-A-G)^2-4AG. \tag{1}
\]
Because $D(D+1)\ge0$ for integer $D$, $A\ge4$. At $N=0$, (1) forces
$G\ge0$. Therefore $B_{\rm ud}=G+1+p_2^2+p_3(p_3+1)>0$, which excludes
$Y=0$ and $k=0$. Directly, $B_{\rm ud}\le Y^2k^2q^2$. Since
$|D|\ge8$, one has $D(D+1)\ge D^2/2$, and hence
\[
 A\ge4+2X^2Y^4k^2\ge4+32q^2Y^4k^2
   >Y^2k^2q^2\ge B_{\rm ud}>G. \tag{2}
\]
In particular $\rho\ne0$, since (1) with $\rho=0$ would force $A=G$.
The rational numbers
\[
 p=\frac{\rho^2-A+G}{2\rho},\qquad u=\rho-p
\]
satisfy $p^2=G$ and $u^2=A$. A rational square root of an integer is integral,
so $p,u\in\mathbb Z$, and $u$ is even. It follows that
\[
 t_{\rm old}=t-p=u-b\in\mathbb Z,\qquad
 t_{\rm old}^2+2(2L+k)t_{\rm old}+k^2-4=0. \tag{3}
\]
The argument explicitly handles equal square classes: for two roots, the
separation $A>G$ rules out the sole cancelling-sum obstruction. It does not
claim that pairwise distinctness suffices for a norm with more than two roots.

\subsection{Complete maps in both directions}
At any new zero, retain all shared coordinates and set
\[
 p_{1,\rm old}=p,\qquad n_{\rm old}=n-p.
\]
Since $Z$ is affine in $n$ with coefficient $U$, (3) makes the old first carrier
zero. Its strong and auxiliary residuals are unchanged, and its guard is zero
because $B_{\rm ud}=p^2+1+p_2^2+p_3(p_3+1)$. This is a literal zero of the
pinned fifteen-variable polynomial, so its complete ordinary soundness theorem
now applies. No premature CRT or canonical assumption has been used.

Conversely, from any old zero, delete $p_1$ and set
$n_{\rm new}=n_{\rm old}+p_1$. Then $G=p_1^2$ and
$Z_{\rm new}=Z_{\rm old}+Up_1$. The universal identity
\[
 N_{\rm new}=C_{1,\rm old}
 \bigl[C_{1,\rm old}+4Up_1(Z_{\rm old}+Ub)+4U^2p_1^2\bigr]
\]
gives $N_{\rm new}=0$, with the other two residuals unchanged.
This applies, in particular, to every member of the authentic canonical source34
family supplied from a finite ZFC refutation. No new rank-tuning or two-square
representation theorem is required.

\subsection{Exact coefficient, cost and boundary}
The degrees of $C_1,B,C_2,G,N$ are $22,12,16,18,60$.
For the leading homogeneous forms, write
\begin{gather*}
 q_0=6\beta j,\quad X_0=12q_0w,\quad Y_0=v,\quad
 R_0=q_0^4z-12q_0^3wC,\\
 a_0=X_0Y_0,\quad U_0=8q_0^3X_0Y_0.
\end{gather*}
Then
\begin{gather*}
 N_{60}=U_0^4R_0^4q_0^8(q_0^8+16X_0^2Y_0^4),\\
 B_{12}=-4a_0^2r^2c^4,\qquad (C_2)_{16}=2a_0^2f^4R_0y.
\end{gather*}
Their product is the unique degree-$88$ part of $P$. In particular,
\[
 [j^{56}w^8v^8z^5r^2c^4f^4y]P
       =-2^{87}3^{64}\beta^{56}\ne0.
\]
Thus the degree is attained on the actual fixed coefficients. The $119$-gate
count includes all scalar multiplications and squares. This lowers the historical
fourteen-variable degree-$108$/119-gate endpoint's degree at unchanged evaluator
cost; it is a different tradeoff from the fifteen-variable degree-$44$/111-gate
source.

The independent audit checks all 33 named registers, literal liveness and gate
counts, the nonzero coefficient, complete coefficient bytes, source pins,
identities, residue cases, signed bounds, and deliberate gate mutations. Normal
and optimized runs pass. These finite checks do not replace the unbounded source34
compiler/refutation theorem, whose source closure remains an explicit dependency.
