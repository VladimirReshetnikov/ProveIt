# Additional routes examined, not claimed as results

The validated advance is eleven unrestricted integers, exact degree648 and115 gates. Its combination of one-product saturation with a weak carrier improves the recovered654/117 endpoint. Free-scale and centered variants are fully audited comparisons, not frontier claims.

A further root or positivity-parameter elimination was investigated but not validated. In the one-product argument BP=Dm*P is saturated into the strong ordinate specifically so BP,Df,Da are pairwise coprime. Removing P from this saturation destroys the argument: the product being square does not imply its individual factors are squares when an uncontrolled common prime can occur. Early folding alone does not repair this because it requires the auxiliary norm, whose root extraction is precisely what the common-squarefactor obstruction prevents.

A redesigned guard congruent to1 modulo the unsaturated strong and auxiliary radicands would restore the needed coprimality, but inserting Da directly into the guard raises degree substantially and makes the guard/strong completeness choices coupled. No claim is made that arbitrary fixed-strong-pair interpolation resolves that coupled selection problem.

Higher polynomial centers also were considered. Increasing the A-power in U allows more terms of the formal square-root expansion to be cleared, but increases the strong/auxiliary degrees faster than the resulting leading cancellation in the straightforward construction. No lower-than352-degree eleven-integer endpoint or ten-variable equation was established.

These are local obstructions to particular attempted simplifications, not impossibility theorems for other architectures.
