# Attribution and scope

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt research project with AI assistance.
New material in this component follows the supplied root MIT No Attribution
license. Original third-party license and notice texts remain in licenses/.

The 78- and 79-operation circuits build on package 133, Staged Pell certificates
and a product-square equation for Con(ZFC), at pinned ProveIt commit
1c5367452a0bd4dd7e9e4bceb1508b62dbd2ff61. Pell doubling, the coprime
product-square finalizer, and the two transport weights are inherited from
that work. The Y=q^3*s quotient and absorption into the positive padding
coordinate have predecessors in the q-cube-normalization research and package
130. The uniform zero-offset ratio estimate is inherited from ProveIt's
zero-offset research. The fixed indexed BFS943 tuple and its finite 436-character
specification are unchanged mathematical data from the indexed-tile work.

The new contribution is the staged index recovery using the already evaluated
denominator Y^2, its proof at scale one with both existential directions, and
the resulting complete 78/630 and 79/468 arithmetic circuits. Historical
priority over the broader literature and global minimality are not asserted.

The coefficient recipe here was independently transcribed from the retained
printed specification. It reconstructs all four raw integers exactly; gzip
container bytes were regenerated deliberately with fixed metadata. Parent
and new compressed hashes are recorded separately. No arbitrary coefficient
ports from different native tuples have been mixed.

Separately performed review in this workflow means an independent agent
derivation and assessment. It does not mean external human refereeing or
proof-assistant certification. The finite checker verifies identities,
circuits, coefficients, and degrees. The unbounded logical conclusion uses
the named ordinary inherited calibration, and does not assert consistency
or the absence of solutions as a proved fact.
