# Attribution and proof boundary

This package builds on the normalized-remainder direct C222 construction in
`ConZFC_Common_Remainders_Radix47_2026-10-25.zip`, present at ProveIt commit
`3ecd6a6cf53d47519228328567f45fb7ee9781d9`.

The eleven normalized digit coordinates, common denominator, spherical
remainder bound, signed Pell frame and orientation, cofinal semantic anchor,
strict exponent schedule, C222 arithmetic encoding, and ZF/ZFC logical
calibration are inherited contributions. The complete parent direct
component is retained unchanged under `inherited/normalized252/`.

The new contributions are the joint ceiling-and-digit norm, the odd
nonzero radix multiplier, removal of all three initial sign guards, the
signed bootstrap and nearest-integer factorial proof including the zero
case, and the resulting literal 245-integer quartic and its exact checks.

New code and exposition are provided under the included MIT No Attribution
license. The work was prepared with AI assistance. No external human peer
review or new end-to-end proof-assistant verification is implied.
