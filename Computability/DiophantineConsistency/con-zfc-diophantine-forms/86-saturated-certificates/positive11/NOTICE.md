(America/Los_Angeles), for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project.

New material: the conditional strong-Pell growth budget, its positivity
bootstrap, double centering at the strong and auxiliary roots, the
degree-213 literal circuit, and the associated exact audit.

`build.py` adapts the arithmetic-DAG framework and unchanged source/norm
prefixes from the incoming `ZFC_Diophantine_Joint_Guards/pell/build_centered.py`.
`exact_ring.py` is copied from that packet. The retained source files and
byte digests are in `provenance/`. The compact44 coefficient payloads are
unchanged, not freshly inferred or mixed with another tuple. Their
underlying work includes the ProveIt native compiler and K215 source.

The ordinary proof inherits the complete source calibration identified
in `PROOF.md`; it does not claim a new machine-checked semantic endpoint,
historical priority over all literature, or a globally simplest equation.

their corresponding retained materials.
