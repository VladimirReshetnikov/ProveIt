# Logical and verification status

## Ordinary theorem

Fix the syntactic ZFC calculus used by the repository. Let Ref_ZFC(p) be its finite refutation predicate, and define Con(ZFC) as not exists p Ref_ZFC(p). The target is a biconditional provable in ZFC; it is not an unconditional proof of either consistency or inconsistency.

Each new component proves an existence equivalence with an identified older arithmetic source. Composing those reductions with the older ordinary native/compiler and syntactic-refutation calibration gives the stated ordinary ZFC proof of

    not exists x in the declared domain P(x)=0  iff  Con(ZFC).

The inherited source theorems are used as mathematical lemmas. This package does not claim to have independently re-proved their complete native transition, Wang-window, rank-controller, and numerical-calculus chain. The retained proofs state unbounded soundness and completeness, rather than merely bounded experimental evidence.

## What the programs establish

The standard-library verifiers check exact finite polynomial identities; recover every retained source arithmetic row; authenticate supplied coefficient bytes; bind literal DAGs to formulas; count live operations and variables; and verify leading coefficients that attain the reported degrees. The nine-positive shifted-defect certificate is an exact universal polynomial identity with positive coefficients, not a numerical sample of an inequality.

Small Pell fixtures and modular evaluations are explicitly labeled diagnostics. They help detect transcription mistakes. They do not prove the universal semantic source/compiler claims, consistency of ZFC, or a new object-language proof.

## Formalization boundary

The repository distinguishes external Lean/Rocq theorems about the standard arithmetic predicate from a derivation inside its encoded ZFC calculus. The exact K215 machine has existing external Lean/Rocq correctness theorems. More recent native polynomial/compiler and internal-ZFC interfaces still have open formalization work at the reviewed pin. See the retained status documents under `provenance/`, especially `OBJECT_ZFC_AT_PIN.md` and `REPOSITORY_STATUS.md`.

This contribution adds no end-to-end proof-assistant certificate and no exported `BProv ZFCax_s [] (...)` derivation. Its theorem statement is an ordinary mathematical composition of named source lemmas with the new proofs, which is the research level currently requested by the repository's paused-formalization plan. It must not be advertised as a newly kernel-certified consistency equation.
