# Attribution and proof status

This proposed research contribution was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov
and ProveIt on 23 October 2026 with AI assistance. Its mathematical review is
internal, not external peer review. New material follows the repository's
MIT No Attribution license; see `LICENSE`.

The new contributions are the adaptive `Y^2` quotient modulus and its
14-positive composition; the absolute-radix bootstrap and delayed sign
recovery; the scaled product guard and 41-integer composition; exact
36/31-variable elimination companions; the supplied 111-position finite
packing certificate; the integrated article, review and finite auditors.
Global novelty beyond the inspected sources has not been established.

The constructions retain substantial earlier mathematics: the calibrated
K215 endpoint; the native Wang-window/compiler construction and its complete
coefficient adapters; Pell classification, ordinary strong-rank and signed
auxiliary lemmas; the integral-root carrier; the squared-modulus auxiliary
construction; the low-degree lifting scheme; degree-separated coefficient
extraction; and the inherited nine-variable finalizer.

Exact copied sources and their identities are recorded in each component's
`SOURCE_PINS.json`. All inherited license and notice files remain in their
component directories. In particular, original machine-source and Wade
notices must be retained on redistribution. The root license does not
replace any more specific inherited notice.

The three complete coefficient tuples are not interchangeable. Their binding
manifests and raw payload digests are part of each equation's definition.
Nothing in this package claims consistency or inconsistency of ZFC, a
globally simplest equation, an end-to-end formal certificate for the new
equations, or an exported object-language ZFC proof.
