\section{Selective quadratic isolation with sparse target padding}
\label{sec:selective-isolation}

This section proves the new source encoding, independently of the
number-theoretic finalizers.  Its only finite input is the explicit tuple
in \texttt{code\_v1.json} and the 22 source residuals identified below.
The proof does not assume a full Sidon ($B_2$) property, disjoint source
intervals, or any unverified output of the search program.

\subsection{The exact source and coefficient convention}
Let $r_1,\ldots,r_{22}$ be the quadratic residuals of the original
package31 positive-variable source.  Every original variable $X_i$ is
replaced by $x_i+1$, where $x_i\in\mathbb N=\{0,1,\ldots\}$.
We use the following coordinate order:
\[
\begin{gathered}
J,F,\alpha,z,f,h,i,o,j,s,w,\tau,\eta,\zeta,y,\gamma,\mathsf N,\mathsf E,\mathsf P,c,a,\\
X,D,C_2,L,F_2,V,g,I,B_{\rm aux},\Delta.
\end{gathered}
\]
Here the displayed names label source coordinates, not the variables of a
final nine- or ten-variable polynomial.  In this section the coefficients
may first be regarded as elements of
$\mathbb Z[\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F]$; the final construction must substitute its
specified, fixed compiler tuple.  These four symbols are not additional
Diophantine unknowns.  The isolation identities below hold under every
integer specialization of the ports.

The exact source bytes are \texttt{upstream\_parent\_quartic31.json},
fetched from repository commit
\begin{center}\texttt{124c07c0b77a9e20e84676514d8b1ff78536ea3f}.\end{center}
The accompanying \texttt{SOURCE\_PINS.json} records repository paths,
SHA-256 hashes and Git blob hashes.  The selected source is the original
positive-transport version, not a silent substitution of package45's
transport variant.  After shifting coordinates, package45's row 15 is
precisely the selected row 15 minus $\beta(x_J+1)$.  Thus its weighted
constant coefficient differs by $-2\beta$, and its weighted $x_J$
coefficient by $-\beta$; the other weighted coefficients agree.
All 136 supports are the same.  The checker verifies this exact difference.

\subsection{A support-selective isolation theorem}
For $n$ coordinates write
\[
 r_k(x)=\sum_{\alpha\in S_k}p_{k,\alpha}x^\alpha,
 \qquad |\alpha|\leq2.
\]
Choose distinct positive integer weights $w_1,\ldots,w_n$ and set
$w_0=0$.  Pad each multi-index $\alpha$ with $2-|\alpha|$ copies of
index 0 to obtain an unordered pair.  This is a bijection between
multi-indices of degree at most two and the $\binom{n+2}{2}$ unordered
pairs from $\{0,\ldots,n\}$.  Put $e(\alpha)=\sum_i\alpha_iw_i$.
Choose distinct targets $q_k\in\mathbb N$ satisfying
\begin{align}
 q_k-e(\alpha)&\geq0 &&(\alpha\in S_k),\label{eq:selective-nonnegative}\\
 q_k-e(\alpha)+e(\gamma)=q_j
 &\ \Longrightarrow\ k=j\ \text{and}\ \alpha=\gamma
 &&(\alpha\in S_k,\ |\gamma|\leq2).\label{eq:selective-isolation}
\end{align}
Only the first monomial in this condition is required to occur in a
residual.  Distinct absent monomials are allowed to have equal weights.
Define
\[
 f_\alpha=(2-|\alpha|)!\prod_i\alpha_i!,\qquad
 c_{k,\alpha}=f_\alpha p_{k,\alpha},\qquad
 D(t)=\sum_{k,\alpha\in S_k}c_{k,\alpha}t^{q_k-e(\alpha)},
\]
and $C_x(t)=(1+\sum_i x_i t^{w_i})^2$.
Then, identically in all the $x_i$ and all coefficient ports,
\begin{equation}
 [t^{q_j}]C_x(t)D(t)=2r_j(x).\label{eq:selective-extraction}
\end{equation}
Indeed, the contribution from monomial $x^\gamma$ in the square has
multinomial coefficient $2/f_\gamma$.  Condition
\eqref{eq:selective-isolation} permits it to reach target $q_j$ only when
$k=j$ and $\gamma=\alpha$.  Its product with $f_\alpha p_{j,\alpha}$
is $2p_{j,\alpha}$.  Summing proves the identity, including all signs.
This argument is a polynomial identity, not a test on bounded witnesses.

\paragraph{The coefficient bound does not acquire a factor 22.}
The reversed exponents $q_k-e(\alpha)$ are injective.  To see this, suppose
$q_k-e(\alpha)=q_j-e(\gamma)$ with $\alpha\in S_k$ and
$\gamma\in S_j$.  Adding $e(\gamma)$ makes the left side reach $q_j$;
\eqref{eq:selective-isolation} forces $k=j$ and $\alpha=\gamma$.
Consequently, after fixing the integer ports, if
$A=\max_{k,\alpha}|c_{k,\alpha}|$, each coefficient of the entire
polynomial $D$ has magnitude at most $A$.  For $x_i\geq0$,
\begin{equation}
 \bigl|[t^s]C_x(t)D(t)\bigr|
 \leq A\left(1+\sum_i x_i\right)^2.\label{eq:selective-bound}
\end{equation}
For a fixed coefficient of the product, sum over the nonnegative
coefficients of $C_x$ and use the bound $A$ for the single corresponding
coefficient of $D$.  Their sum is $C_x(1)$.  Collisions between absent
monomials in $C_x$, or overlaps between source intervals, do not change
this argument.

\paragraph{Leading coefficient and positivity.}
Let $M=\max_i w_i$ and $Q=\max_kq_k$.  In the explicit certificate the
unique row at target $Q$ is original row 5.  Its shifted polynomial is
\[
 r_5(x)=1+2x_E-x_a+x_Ex_X-x_ax_X.
\]
It has constant coefficient 1.  Positivity of every input weight implies
that the coefficient of $t^Q$ in $D$ is exactly 2, and no term has higher
degree.  If $B>A+1$, then
\[
 \left|\sum_{s<Q}[t^s]D(t)B^s\right|
 \leq A\frac{B^Q-1}{B-1}<B^Q,
 \qquad D(B)>0.
\]
Also $D(B)<2AB^Q$ for $B\geq2$.  Row 5 shows that the all-zero source
vector is not a common zero.  These facts hold independently of the
values of the fixed ports.

\subsection{Sparse target padding: one bit absorbs incoming borrow}
The same selective code permits a smaller source wrapper than dense
centering.  Let $B\geq4$ be a power of two and let
\[
 F(t)=\sum_{s=0}^H a_st^s\in\mathbb Z[t],\qquad
 |a_s|<B/4,\qquad F(B)>0.
\]
For a target set $\mathcal Q\subseteq\{0,\ldots,H\}$ put
\[
 A_{\mathcal Q}=\sum_{q\in\mathcal Q}B^q,\qquad
 J=2F(B)+A_{\mathcal Q}.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}
 \tau_2\bigl(J,(B-2)A_{\mathcal Q}\bigr)=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 a_q=0\quad\hbox{for all }q\in\mathcal Q,\label{eq:sparse-carry}
\end{equation}
where $\tau_2(u,v)=0$ means that binary addition of the nonnegative
integers $u,v$ is carry-free.

For completeness, write $\delta_s=1$ at a target and 0 elsewhere, and
form the signed carry recurrence
\[
 c_0=0,\quad
 c_{s+1}=\left\lfloor\frac{2a_s+\delta_s+c_s}{B}\right\rfloor,
 \quad d_s=2a_s+\delta_s+c_s-Bc_{s+1}.
\]
Induction gives $c_s\in\{-1,0\}$ and $0\leq d_s<B$: the numerator
lies strictly between $-B$ and $B$.  Because $J>0$, telescoping forces
the final carry $c_{H+1}=0$; otherwise
$J=\sum_{s=0}^H d_sB^s-B^{H+1}<0$.  Thus the $d_s$ are the actual
base-$B$ digits of $J$.

At a target the mask digit is $B-2$, whose binary digits forbid all bits
except the least significant bit.  Hence carry-freeness requires
$d_s\in\{0,1\}$.  If the outgoing carry were $-1$, the numerator would
be $d_s-B\leq1-B$, contradicting
$2a_s+1+c_s>-B/2-1\geq1-B$.  The outgoing carry is therefore zero.
Now $2a_s=d_s-1-c_s$, and the only even integer allowed by
$d_s\in\{0,1\}$ and $c_s\in\{-1,0\}$ is 0.  Conversely, $a_s=0$
gives $d_s=1+c_s\in\{0,1\}$ and outgoing carry zero.  This proves
\eqref{eq:sparse-carry}; in particular, an incoming borrow cannot produce
a false zero test.  No test is imposed on an absent target.

\subsection{Source wrapper and size estimates before power-of-two decoding}
Choose an integer $b\geq1$ and an integer base
$B>4096Ab^2$.  This assumption is a numerical coefficient majorant,
not yet a power-of-two assumption.  Define
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \ell&=\sum_{i=1}^{31}B^{w_i},& A_{\mathcal Q}&=\sum_{k=1}^{22}B^{q_k},\\
 N_0&=B^{M+1},&N_1&=B^{H+1},\\
 N_{\rm src}&=N_0N_1=B^{5741},&\mathcal C&=bB^M,\\
 M_0&=N_0-1-(b-1)\ell,&J(g)&=2(1+g)^2D(B)+A_{\mathcal Q},\\
 S(g)&=g+N_0J(g),& T&=M_0+N_0(B-2)A_{\mathcal Q}.
\end{aligned}
\]
For every integer $0\leq g<2\mathcal C$, all of these expressions
needed by the no-carry test are nonnegative, with $J(g),T>0$, and
\begin{equation}
 S(g)+T<N_{\rm src}.\label{eq:source-pair-bound}
\end{equation}
Indeed, $B>2b$ implies $g<N_0$, and the base-$B$ digits of $M_0$ are
$B-b$ at the distinct marked input positions and $B-1$ elsewhere, so
$0\leq M_0<N_0$.  As $g$ is integral, $1+g\leq2bB^M$.  Consequently
\[
 0<2(1+g)^2D(B)<16Ab^2B^H<N_1/256.
\]
Furthermore, using $M\geq1$,
\[
 (B-1)A_{\mathcal Q}\leq B^{Q+1}-1<N_1/B^2.
\]
It follows that
\[
 S(g)+T<N_0\left(2+N_1/256+N_1/B^2\right)<N_0N_1.
\]
The last inequality follows already from $B\geq4$ and $N_1\geq4$.
These estimates hold before any bridge has deduced that $b$ is a power
of two.  They therefore do not introduce circular reasoning into that
bridge.  The displayed $N_{\rm src}$ has no factor 4; the old dense-padding
wrapper $4B^{5741}$ is not the wrapper used here.

If now $b$ and $B$ are powers of two, binary carry-freeness of $g$ and
$M_0$ is equivalent to a unique representation
\[
 g=\sum_{i=1}^{31}x_iB^{w_i},\qquad 0\leq x_i<b.
\]
The mask $B-b$ allows precisely the lowest $\log_2 b$ bits in each
marked cell and no bits in an unmarked cell.  For this representation,
$(1+g)^2D(B)=C_x(B)D(B)$.  By \eqref{eq:selective-bound},
\[
 |a_s|\leq A\bigl(1+31(b-1)\bigr)^2
 \leq A(31b)^2<B/4.
\]
The low and high words split at the power of two $N_0$.  Combining the
low-word mask, \eqref{eq:sparse-carry} and
\eqref{eq:selective-extraction} proves
\[
 \tau_2\bigl(S(g),T\bigr)=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \begin{cases}
 g=\sum_i x_iB^{w_i},\quad 0\leq x_i<b,\\
 r_1(x)=\cdots=r_{22}(x)=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
A common source zero is nonzero because $r_5(0)=1$.  Choosing $b$ from
any cofinal allowed power-of-two family larger than every $x_i$ gives
$B\leq g<\mathcal C$, since all weights are positive and distinct.
Thus the usual cofinal source-witness requirement is preserved.
The arithmetic finalizers must still prove their own bridge hypotheses,
fixed coefficient specialization and final variable-domain equivalences.

\subsection{Independent finite verification and limits}
The standard-library program \texttt{verify\_selective.py} performs the
following checks without executing any upstream code or search binary:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Check source SHA-256 and Git blob hashes against the current pinned
repository receipts, and the frozen code's exact byte hash.
\item Parse all 22 parent residual formulas, including affine
abbreviations, in an independently implemented sparse polynomial ring;
compare them with the supplied sparse terms.
\item Apply the 31 binomial shifts, verify every weighted coefficient in
\texttt{source\_support.json}, and compare all 136 terms to package45
with precisely the two row-15 corrections described above.
\item Enumerate all $136\cdot528=71\,808$ source-term/padded-pair
interactions.  Exactly 136 land at marked targets, each at its intended
row and monomial.  Reconstruct the 22 formal target polynomials and
verify that each is twice its residual.
\item Check all reversed exponents are nonnegative and distinct, the
leading coefficient is 2, and the displayed maxima are exact.
\end{enumerate}
The replay command is \texttt{python3 verify\_selective.py}; it also
passes with Python's \texttt{-O} switch, because validity checks do not
use removable assertions.  The finite certificate supplies the complete
finite premise of the isolation proof.  It does not purport to prove the
inherited machine compiler or the Sun/Jones number-theoretic theorems.

The maximum target decreases from the package45 value 35178 to 5190,
and the maximum input weight from 799 to 183.  These are source exponent
spans, not final polynomial degrees or operation counts; those metrics
must be computed from each compiled finalizer.  The tuple is not claimed
globally optimal.  The construction gives no new proof of
$\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$, and no assertion that the consistency
question has been decided.  Its contribution is an explicit, rigorously
checkable support-selective code and a sparse padding lemma that can be
composed with the separately stated consistency-to-source theorem.
