\begin{abstract}
An explicit selective source code, sparse signed-borrow padding, and a new
parity calibration of the binary block give a polynomial of exact degree
7,344,328 in nine nonnegative or positive unknowns. Its positive form uses
738 binary arithmetic operations. Relative to a repository 129,875,980-degree,
788-operation nine-positive-variable comparator, the degree falls by a factor
of about 17.68 and operations by 6.35\%. The equivalence with absence of a
finite ZFC refutation uses explicitly identified inherited compiler and Jones
ratio theorems; no new proof of consistency or end-to-end kernel certificate
is claimed.
\end{abstract}
