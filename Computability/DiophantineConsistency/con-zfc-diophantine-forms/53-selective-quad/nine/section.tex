\section{Nine unknowns through a parity-calibrated carry block}
\label{sec:nine-calibrated}
The explicit polynomial below has nine nonnegative integer unknowns,
exact degree \(7\,344\,328\), and 744 binary arithmetic operations.
Its positive-coordinate translate has the same degree and 738 operations
(211 additions, 26 subtractions, 501 multiplications).
All intermediate names denote polynomial registers, not quantified variables.
With the identified inherited compiler and Jones ratio theorems, ordinary
mathematics formalizable in ZFC proves
\[
  \neg\exists\mathbf x\in\mathbb N^9\ F_9(\mathbf x)=0
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\]
This is not a proof of consistency, a claim of minimum complexity, an
unrestricted-integer nine-variable result, or a new end-to-end kernel proof.

\subsection{Sparse target padding and source bounds}
Use \((\epsilon,g_0,h_0,s_0,w_0,\phi,i_0,j_0,n)\in\mathbb N^9\),
where \(\mathbb N=\{0,1,\ldots\}\). Put
\(g=g_0+1,h=h_0+1,s=s_0+1,w=w_0+1,i=i_0+1,j=j_0+1\), and define
\begin{align*}
 b&=\epsilon+2,& B&=ab^2,& \ell&=\sum_iB^{w_i},& A_t&=\sum_jB^{q_j},\\
 N_0&=B^{184},& Z&=B^{5557},& L&=2bB^{5741},& N&=cB^{2966},\\
 M_c&=N_0-1-(b-1)\ell,&
 J_c&=2(1+g)^2D_c(B)+A_t,\\
 S_c&=g+N_0J_c,&T_c&=M_c+N_0(B-2)A_t,\\
 R&=L^2+(S_c+T_c)L+L-1-S_c,&G_{\rm cap}&=32bB^{183}.
\end{align*}
The block length \(L\) and ratio threshold root \(N\) are different.
On every outer assignment, \(J_c,S_c>0\), and the base-\(B\) digits of
\(M_c\) are \(B-b\) at the weights and \(B-1\) elsewhere through \(W\).
Hence \(0\leq M_c<N_0\), \(T_c\geq0\), and
\[
 R=L^2+(L-1)S_c+LT_c+L-1\geq8,\qquad
 0<b\leq R,N,\quad N\geq8,\quad G_{\rm cap}>0.
\]
These facts do not presuppose a power-of-two base or a bound on \(g\).

If \(g<4bB^{183}\), integrality yields \(1+g\leq4bB^{183}\) and
\(g<N_0\). Therefore
\[
2(1+g)^2D_c(B)<\frac{32Mb^2}{B-1}Z
 =\frac{B}{256(B-1)}Z<\frac Z{128},\qquad
 A_t<\frac Z{128}.
\]
Thus \(0<J_c<Z/64<Z\), while \((B-2)A_t<B^{5191}\leq Z\).
Using integrality again gives
\[
 0\leq S_c,T_c<N_0Z=B^{5741},\qquad S_c+T_c<2B^{5741}<L.
\]

Once \(b\) is a power of two, so is \(B\). Binary disjointness of
\(g\) and \(M_c\) is exactly
\(g=\sum_i x_iB^{w_i}\) with \(0\leq x_i<b\).
Let \(h_r=[t^r](C_{\mathbf x}D_c)\). Then
\[
 |h_r|<M(1+\sum x_i)^2<1024Mb^2=B/8,
 \qquad J_c=\sum_{r=0}^{5556}(2h_r+\delta_r)B^r,
\]
where \(\delta_r\) is 1 at a target and 0 elsewhere. In signed-digit
normalization the incoming borrow is always \(-1\) or 0: the coefficient
bound preserves this by induction. At a target the normalized digit is
\((2h_r+1+\text{borrow})\bmod B\). It avoids the mask \(B-2\) precisely
when \(h_r=0\): for zero it is 0 or 1; for positive \(h_r\) it is at
least 2 and below \(B/2\); for negative \(h_r\) it is above \(B/2\).
Consequently
\[
 S_c\mathbin{\&}T_c=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 g\text{ codes a common nonnegative zero of all }p_j.
\]
The symbol \(\&\) denotes ordinary bitwise intersection of nonnegative
integers. It occurs only in the proof, not in the polynomial circuit.

\subsection{The new block-parity calibration}
For \(S,T\geq0\), \(S+T<L=2^u\), the polynomial
\[
 X=L^2+(S+T)L+(L-1-S)
\]
has disjoint top, middle and bottom binary blocks. Thus
\[
\operatorname{pop}(X)=u+\operatorname{pop}(T)+1
 -\bigl[\operatorname{pop}(S)+\operatorname{pop}(T)-\operatorname{pop}(S+T)\bigr].
\]
The bracket is nonnegative and vanishes exactly if \(S\mathbin{\&}T=0\).
Legendre's formula gives
\(v_2\binom{2X}{X}=\operatorname{pop}(X)\), so
\[
 2^{u+\operatorname{pop}(T)+1}\mid\binom{2X}{X}
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad S\mathbin{\&}T=0.
\]
Put \(b=2^k,B=2^v\), with \(v=\kappa+2k\).
The low mask contributes \(184v-31k\) bits; the 22 high target digits
contribute \(22(v-1)\). Hence
\[
 \operatorname{pop}(T_c)=206v-31k-22.
\]
Choosing \(L=2bB^{5741}\) gives \(u=1+k+5741v\), and therefore
\begin{align*}
 u+\operatorname{pop}(T_c)+1
 &=5947v-30k-20\\
 &=5947\kappa+11864k-20\\
 &=2\bigl(15\kappa/2-10+2966v\bigr)=2\log_2N.
\end{align*}
Thus the tested threshold is exactly \(N^2\), even with \(b\) linear
in the input. No extra unknown, dummy digit or square-base restriction is
needed. This is the new parity-calibration step.

Conversely, for a source zero choose any sufficiently large power of two
\(b>\max x_i\), and \(\epsilon=b-2\). Since \(p_5(0)=1\), its code
\(g\) is positive. Moreover
\(g\leq(b-1)\ell<bB^{183}\), so \(32g<G_{\rm cap}\).
Both masks are disjoint and \(N^2\mid\binom{2R}{R}\).
Every sufficiently large power of two is available, eliminating any hidden
cofinality assumption.

\subsection{Ratio registers and the near-square finalizer}
Use the inherited Jones ratio registers
\begin{gather*}
 U=N^2w,\quad Y=N^2s,\quad M_0=RY,\quad P=2M_0^2U,\\
 K=R+1+h(P-1),\quad A=M_0(U+1),\quad B_1=2R+1,\quad W_1=bw,\\
 C=B_1+W_1+\phi,\quad J=4A-5,\quad Q=3W_1C-2(W_1^2-1),\\
 D=(A^2-1)C^2+1,\quad E=iJDC^2,\quad F=(A^2-1)E^2+1,\\
 G=A+F(CD+1-A),\quad H=B_1+jC,\quad I=(G^2-1)H^2+1,\\
 a_0=(P^2-1)K^2+1,\quad a_1=64DFI,\quad u_0=JF,\quad v_0=QF+J(H-C),\\
 \mu=K^2G_{\rm cap}-4(C-KY)^2G_{\rm cap}-8gK^2.
\end{gather*}
Here \(u_0,v_0\) avoid conflict with the binary block exponent.
The imported \texttt{ratio\_sound} and \texttt{ratio\_complete} in
\path{upstream/NineRatio.lean} relate the four conditions
\[
 a_0\text{ square},\quad DFI\text{ square},\quad u_0\mid v_0,\quad\mu>0
\]
to the central-binomial divisibility and \(b\) a power of two. Soundness
gives \(8g<G_{\rm cap}\); completeness requires \(32g<G_{\rm cap}\).
All numerical hypotheses were proved unconditionally above. Multiplying
\(DFI\) by 64 does not change its square predicate.

For every outer assignment, \(A\geq2,J\geq3,D\geq A^2,K>1\), all Pell
registers are positive, and \(Q,H-C>0\). Thus
\[
 0<\frac{v_0}{u_0}=\frac QJ+\frac{H-C}{F}\leq Q+H-C=:M_d.
\]
Put
\[
 V_0=PK,\quad t_0=iJC,\quad S_0=t_0(8D^2-4D-1),\quad V_1=S_0GH.
\]
As \(E=t_0DC\), exact identities yield
\[
 DF=t_0^2D^3(D-1)+D,\qquad
 S_0^2-64DF=t_0^2(8D+1)-64D\geq8D+9>0.
\]
Since \(I\leq G^2H^2\) and \(V_0^2-a_0=K^2-1>0\), we have
\(0<a_r<V_r^2\) for \(r=0,1\).
Define the requested single polynomial by
\begin{gather*}
 \Omega=u_0(2\mu-1),\qquad
 \Theta=u_0n+v_0-\Omega(M_d+V_0V_1),\\
 \boxed{F_9=\left(\Theta^2-\Omega^2(a_0+V_0^2a_1)\right)^2
                    -4\Omega^4V_0^2a_0a_1.}
\end{gather*}
The four-factor identity implies, at any zero,
\(\Theta/\Omega=r_0+V_0r_1\) with \(r_i^2=a_i\).
This rational number cannot be zero, because then
\(|r_1|=|r_0|/V_0<1\), contrary to the positive integer \(a_1\).
The identity
\(2(\Theta/\Omega)r_0=(\Theta/\Omega)^2+a_0-V_0^2a_1\)
forces both roots rational and thus integral. This argument does not assume
independence of square classes.
The strict bounds give
\[
 Z_0=M_d+V_0V_1+r_0+V_0r_1\geq M_d+1>0.
\]
The factor equation is \(n+v_0/u_0=(2\mu-1)Z_0\).
Its positive left side forces \(\mu>0\), and its integral right side
forces \(u_0\mid v_0\). Conversely the four ratio conditions yield an
integer \(n=(2\mu-1)Z_0-v_0/u_0\geq1\) and hence a zero.
This completes both directions of the stated reduction.

\subsection{Exact degree and audited implementation}
A raw degree walk would miss a leading cancellation. The certificate uses
\begin{align*}
 \Delta_V=V_1^2-a_1
 &=[t_0^2(8D+1)-64D]G^2H^2+64DF(H^2-1),\\
 E_0&=u_0n+v_0-\Omega M_d,\\
 \Theta^2-\Omega^2(a_0+V_0^2a_1)
 &=E_0^2-2E_0\Omega V_0V_1+\Omega^2V_0^2\Delta_V-\Omega^2a_0.
\end{align*}
The first summand of \(\Delta_V\) has degree \(1\,763\,760\) and
leader \(2^{147}a^{881800}c^{96}\). The third summand of the last display
dominates, with degree \(3\,672\,164\) and leader
\(2^{299}a^{1835914}c^{224}\). Finally
\(4\Omega^4V_0^2a_0a_1\) has degree \(5\,998\,540\), strictly less than
twice the dominant degree. Thus
\[
 \deg F_9=7\,344\,328,\qquad
 \operatorname{LC}_{\rm diagonal}(F_9)=2^{598}a^{3671828}c^{448}>0.
\]
The diagonal attains the full multivariate total-degree bound. The
cancellation-resolved certificate records every decisive exact leader;
coefficient-dependent lower heads are left unknown, not guessed.

Replacing every natural input by a positive input minus one is a bijection
and preserves degree. In particular \(b=\epsilon_++1\),
\(\phi=\phi_+-1\), and \(n=n_+-1\).
The conservative three-block comparison has degree \(12\,023\,320\) and
734 positive operations. A square-base uncalibrated two-block comparison
has degree \(14\,687\,132\) and 740 operations. Its admissible bases
\(b=2^{2r+1}\) are explicitly cofinal. Both comparison circuits and their
proof details are included; the parity-calibrated version is primary.
