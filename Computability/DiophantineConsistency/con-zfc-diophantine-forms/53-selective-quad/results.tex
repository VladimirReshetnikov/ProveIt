\section{Results and fair comparisons}
An operation is a charged binary addition, subtraction, or multiplication,
including multiplication by a fixed numeral and every multiplication used
for a fixed power. Shared registers and fixed integer leaves are not
charged again. Degrees are total degrees in the quantified unknowns; the
large coefficients are fixed. This arithmetic-circuit measure is not a
bit-complexity measure or an expanded-monomial count.

The nine-natural versions use 744 and 740 operations, respectively, at
the same degrees. The eleven-positive two-equation shifted systems have
degrees $(2,956)$ at 112 operations, or $(2,960)$ at 110 operations. The
systems are secondary presentations; the main claims above are single
polynomial equations. The unseeded thirteen-integer comparator has degree
$71\,382\,096$ and 770 operations.

These rows are not ordered by one universal notion of ``simplest.'' Sun's
2026 theorem is used only for the ten-unrestricted-integer row. The nine,
thirteen, eleven, and fifteen-variable routes do not use that theorem.
The thirteen-variable row is therefore a dependency-reduced alternative,
not a competitor on unknown count with the ten-integer row. Positive and
unrestricted domains must not be conflated.

The known 67-operation scalar equation and 59-operation system remain
unbeaten on operation count alone. Our previous twelve-positive equation
has smaller degree $901$ than the new eleven-positive equation, at the
cost of an extra unknown. Historical fifteen-positive points $99/69$,
$93/70$, $83/71$, $56/74$, and $44/78$ also remain relevant; the present
$56/74$ regrouping is explicitly not claimed as new. The eight earlier
packages were reviewed as mathematical sources, not all rerun wholesale.
New circuit properties are independently audited in this archive.

\paragraph{Coefficient cost is not hidden.}
All active constructions in this release use the complete source31
Wang943 $121\times8801$ tuple: $B_{\rm native}=2^{1064921}$,
$\beta=B_{\rm native}-1$, and the three matching ports $K,M_C,M_F$.
The fourth port is already shifted. The four uncompressed payloads total
533,099 bytes. This is larger than source44's alternative 295,388-byte
tuple, which is not silently substituted. The source constant
$2^{2134940}$ and derived fixed leaves have exact finite recipes from this
selected tuple; their cost and bit lengths are disclosed separately.
The new wins are in exponent ranges, degree, arithmetic circuits, and
some proof interfaces, not a claim to new coefficient-byte records.
