# Attribution and scope

This authored mathematical research artifact builds on the ProveIt repository
by Vladimir Reshetnikov and its attributed compiler, proof-checker, and Jones
1982 developments. Exact imported source paths and hashes are in
SOURCE_PINS.json. Preserved upstream licenses are in licenses/; LICENSE is
also supplied at this directory's root.

The semantic endpoint is the original package31 quadratic system with the
complete 121-by-8801, 943-tile coefficient tuple, not arbitrary independently
chosen ports. The additional source coding, parity calibration, and seeded
square-condition merger are proved in section.tex. Search logs and finite
checks are not substitutes for those proofs.

No end-to-end Lean compilation of this concrete polynomial, proof of ZFC
consistency, globally optimal equation, or unrestricted-integer variable-count
record is claimed. The Jones branch does not use Sun's 2026 arithmetic bridge;
the inherited SIGNED10_PROOF.md is retained for its source/tuple description,
not as a claim that this finalizer uses all of its later dependencies.
