\section{The ten-unrestricted-integer companion}
\label{sec:sparse-sun}
The sparse quadratic code can be composed with the existing ten-integer
finalizer, without a new unknown or a change to its logical interface.
The variables are exactly
\[
(f,g,h,k,l,w,x,y,z,m)\in\mathbb Z^{10}.
\]
The coefficient tuple is the coherent tuple specified in the coefficient
manifest.  All expressions below are polynomial abbreviations, not extra
quantified variables.  Our new work is sparse support isolation, an
interior padding digit, and a shared-source evaluator.  The universal
ten-integer reduction itself is due to Sun.

\subsection{A source interface with arbitrary padding position}
Let $p$ be the padding position and $w_1,\ldots,w_{31}$ the distinct
variable positions. They are distinct from $p$, nonnegative, and at most
$W$. For $|\alpha|\le2$ put
\[
 e_p(\alpha)=(2-|\alpha|)p+\sum_i\alpha_iw_i,
 \qquad c_{j,\alpha}=(2-|\alpha|)!\prod_i\alpha_i!\,p_{j,\alpha},
\]
where $r_j(n)=\sum p_{j,\alpha}n^\alpha$ is the shifted positive parent.
The certificate gives targets $H_j$ such that
\[
 H_j-e_p(\alpha)+e_p(\gamma)=H_k
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (j,\alpha)=(k,\gamma)
\]
for each nonzero source coefficient and each quadratic monomial $\gamma$.
In particular,
\[
 [T^{H_j}]\left(T^p+\sum_i n_iT^{w_i}\right)^2
       \sum_{k,\alpha}c_{k,\alpha}T^{H_k-e_p(\alpha)}=2r_j(n).
\]
This condition is weaker than requiring all quadratic monomial positions
to be distinct: only the source's active support must be isolated.
Write
\[
 D(T)=\sum_{j,\alpha}c_{j,\alpha}T^{H_j-e_p(\alpha)},\quad
 Z(T)=\sum_jT^{H_j},\quad \ell(T)=\sum_iT^{w_i},\quad
 K_c=\deg D+2W.
\]
All the exponents displayed are certified nonnegative.

The exact fixed integer
$C_0=1+\sum_{j,\alpha}|c_{j,\alpha}|$ has bit length $1\,182\,954$.
Independent reconstruction verifies
\[
 \beta_S=2^{1\,182\,966}>2048C_0.
\]
In particular the fixed base multiplier is $1$ modulo $3$.
Define
\begin{align*}
 b&=1+3f,& \mathcal B&=\beta_Sb^2,& \mathcal C&=b\mathcal B^W,\\
 N_0&=\mathcal B^{W+1},&N_1&=4\mathcal B^{K_c+1},&N&=N_0N_1,\\
 M_c&=N_0-1-(b-1)\ell(\mathcal B),\\
 T_c&=M_c+N_0(\mathcal B-2)Z(\mathcal B),\\
 J_c&=2(\mathcal B^p+g)^2D(\mathcal B)
            +\mathcal B\sum_{i=0}^{K_c}\mathcal B^i,\\
 S_c&=g+N_0J_c,&R_c&=(S_c+T_c+1)N+T_c+1,\\
 X&=(N-1)R_c,&Y&=N^2.
\end{align*}
Suppose $f\ne0$, $b$ is a square and $0\le g<2\mathcal C$.
Then $b\ge4$ and $\mathcal B>2048C_0b^2$. Since $p\le W$,
\[
 \left|2(\mathcal B^p+g)^2D(\mathcal B)\right|
 <18C_0b^2\mathcal B^{K_c}<\mathcal B^{K_c+1}.
\]
Hence $0<J_c<3\mathcal B^{K_c+1}<N_1$ and the block bounds give
$0\le S_c,T_c<N$. Consequently
\[
 X\ge N^2-1>3b,\qquad Y>\max\{b,256\},\qquad 3\mid X.
\]
These statements precede, and do not assume, the power-of-two condition.

If $b$ is also a power of two, Sun's binomial carry lemma identifies
$Y\mid\binom{2X}{X}$ with the absence of binary carries in $S_c+T_c$.
The low block decodes
$g=\sum_i n_i\mathcal B^{w_i}$ with $0\le n_i<b$.
Every coefficient $A_t$ of
$(T^p+\sum n_iT^{w_i})^2D(T)$ satisfies
\[
 |A_t|\le C_0(1+\textstyle\sum n_i)^2
 <1024C_0b^2<\mathcal B/2.
\]
Thus the baseline $\mathcal B/2$ produces actual, strictly interior
base-$\mathcal B$ digits, without interdigit carrying. At target $H_j$,
the digit is $\mathcal B/2+2r_j(n)$. Its addition to
$\mathcal B/2-1$ has no binary carries precisely when $r_j(n)=0$.
This proves soundness.

Conversely, choose a natural source zero, then choose $b=4^q$ arbitrarily
large and greater than all its coordinates.  The same calculation gives
the binomial divisibility and $g<\mathcal C$. In the interior-padding
certificate the sole zero variable position is the coordinate $i$,
whereas the fifth residual has constant term $1$ and depends only on
$E,a,X$, whose positions are positive. Some positive-position coordinate
must therefore be nonzero. Thus $g\ge\mathcal B\ge b$. The original
padding-zero certificate has all variable positions positive, and the
same conclusion follows because the zero vector fails the fifth residual.
This proves the full source interface required by the inherited bridge.

\subsection{Two layers of evaluator factoring}
The tail polynomial is unchanged by the exact identities
\[
 G=(a-1)(F-1)-1,\quad a=(LX)(V+1),\qquad
 H=C+F(B_1-C+16Ey),
\]
\[
 t^3-tS^2-S^3=t(t^2-S^2)-S^3,
 \qquad f^2l^2=(fl)^2,\quad b^2z^2=(bz)^2.
\]
The equality $A=2a$ is built polynomially, without division. Sharing
$\Sigma^2r_0^2$ between $a_1$ and $a_2$ saves another operation.
Independent symbolic expansions verify these identities and the norm.

There is a more substantial source-level saving. Let
$Q_j(u_0,\ldots,u_{31})$ be the degree-two homogenization of the shifted
residual, and let $\operatorname{diag}(Q_j)$ be its sum of pure-square
monomials. The weighted form is exactly
\[
 V_j=Q_j+\operatorname{diag}(Q_j).
\]
Indeed a degree-two multinomial has weight $1$ on cross monomials and
weight $2$ on pure squares.  Evaluate every $Q_j$ by homogenizing the
\emph{shared sixty-gate parent}, not by expanding its monomials. At
\[
 u_0=\mathcal B^{W-p},\qquad
 u_i=\mathcal B^{W-w_i},
\]
we have
$D(\mathcal B)=\sum_j\mathcal B^{H_j-2W}V_j$ whenever every
$H_j\ge2W$.  Only the sparse diagonal correction is added afterward.
This preserves sharing between different quadratic equations and sharply
reduces the operation count. Uniformly translating all target positions
preserves their isolation property, so it can be used to ensure this
nonnegative-offset condition.

All operation counts are live binary $+,-,\times$ gates, including scalar
multiplication; fixed coefficients and reuse are free. The supplied JSON
circuits and receipts give the exact counts for each retained tradeoff.
Exact degree is established by an upper bound from circuit propagation
and a nonzero coefficient at that same bound after diagonal specialization
modulo a prime. Three independent primes are retained for redundancy.
The verifier also checks actual fixed bytes, all coefficient bounds and
literal source-and-tail replay. These finite checks do not replace the
inherited unbounded arithmetic theorems, nor supply a new end-to-end formal
proof assistant development.
