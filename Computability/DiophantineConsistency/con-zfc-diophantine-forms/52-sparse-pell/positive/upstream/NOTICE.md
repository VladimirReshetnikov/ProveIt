# Attribution, new work, and scope

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository through an
OpenAI-assisted mathematical research collaboration. Separate agents
investigated signed variables, positive-variable norms, low-degree systems,
and independent reviews; the primary agent combined the coefficient
construction, checked the interfaces, and assembled the release. No human
authorship, outside endorsement, or discovery-priority claim is implied.

The work combines the repository's packages 24-integer24, 25-hybrid12,
26-ratio12, 27-wave4, 28-compact-norms, 29-simpler-forms, and 30-quartic31
at commit 8c6eec9d19ef80538c8e61ff6e0180040583446a. The exact source map and
inherited attribution are preserved in sources/ and licenses/.

New contributions in this delivery are the general-padding mixed121
coefficient binding, the cofinal square-radix signed16 construction, the
smaller signed separator, the compact signed17 companion, the degree1065
positive cancellation, the economical norm reassociation, the three-shift
62-operation system, and their proofs, generators, audits, and report.
The finite K215 machine and 943-tile language are inherited unchanged.

The sources retain the attribution of their underlying machine, compiler,
proof, and mathematical work, including the Jones reduction and earlier
compact theory/K215 developments. Historical notices can mention paths in
their original packages; the current paths are defined by README.md and
sources/SOURCE_MAP.json. The repository's MIT No Attribution license is
retained as LICENSE and applies to the new repository material. Inherited
third-party material retains its original terms, including LICENSE.Wade.

This is an ordinary mathematical equivalence formalizable in ZFC, with
identified inherited dependencies and finite implementation evidence. It
is not an end-to-end new proof-assistant certificate, a proof of ZFC
consistency, or a claim of a global minimum.
