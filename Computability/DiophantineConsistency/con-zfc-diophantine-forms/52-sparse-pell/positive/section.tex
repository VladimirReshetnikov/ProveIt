\section{A new lean Pell carrier: eleven positive variables}
\label{sec:positive-lean}

\paragraph{Result.}
There is an explicit polynomial in eleven strictly positive integer variables
of exact total degree $954$, evaluated by $113$ binary arithmetic operations,
whose absence of positive solutions is equivalent in ZFC to $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$.
The associated two-equation system has maximum degree $952$ and costs $108$
operations after a valid positive transport-coordinate shift. A twelve-positive
companion has degree $897$ and $108$ operations, or a three-equation system of
maximum degree $888$ and $102$ operations. These are ordinary mathematical
reductions using the inherited normalized compiler and calibrated K215 machine,
not new end-to-end proof-assistant certificates or proofs of consistency.

Compared with the pinned eleven-positive degree-$1140$/129-operation companion,
the new equation removes $186$ degrees ($16.32\%$) and $16$ operations.
The new twelve-positive companion strictly improves the pinned
degree-$901$/112-operation equation on both measures.

\paragraph{Fixed coefficients and explicit equation.}
Use the entire coherent four-port tuple $(\beta,K_0,M_C,M_F)$ bound in the
supplied coefficient manifest, with $\beta=2^{590069}-1$ and $M_F$ already
shifted by $\beta$. All four ports are fixed integer numerals. They are not
existential parameters and may not be mixed with ports from another tuple.
The eleven positive unknowns are
\[
 J,F,\alpha,z,i,T,s,w,\eta,\zeta,t.
\]
Define computed polynomial abbreviations
\begin{align*}
q&=\beta J+1,&s_0&=s+3q,& C&=q-F-\alpha,\\
X&=wq,&Y&=s_0q^3,&E&=XY,\\
k&=\eta+\zeta,&c&=kY+\eta,&a&=E+Y,\\

A&=1+\ell(\ell+k),&G&=1+\Delta c^2,&b_0&=X+ac,\\

N_t&=(K_0+w)C+q-F-z(q-1),\\
I&=ic^2,&D&=1+\Delta I^2,&Q&=(\Delta I)^2,\\
d&=Q-1,&v&=cT,&b&=c+RD,\\
e&=Q(Dv^2+b^2)-1,&B_a&=2Qvb.&&
\end{align*}
The new lean carrier and smaller absorption modulus are
\[
 K=IT,\quad u=k-R-1,\quad S=HY,\quad
 L=Y(1+HK),\quad W=Yb_0+H\bigl[Y(t+b+\ell)+u\bigr].
\]
Set
\[
 N=\prod_{\substack{g=\pm\sqrt G\\ \tau=\pm\sqrt A\\ f=\pm\sqrt D}}
 \left[d(W-Lg-S\tau)^2-S^2e+S^2B_af\right].
\]
This is a formal conjugate product in an integer polynomial ring, not an
assertion of independent specialized Galois automorphisms. The displayed
radicals add no unknowns. The recommended equation is
\[
 \boxed{\ N_t(1+N)-1=0.\ }
\]
Every power and conjugate product is replaced by additions, subtractions,
and multiplications in the supplied division-free JSON circuit. In particular,
the claimed $113$ operations include evaluation of every abbreviation.

\paragraph{Why the omitted index quotient is recoverable.}
The norm obeys a square congruence modulo $4$. Thus the product equation
forces $N_t=1$ and $N=0$. The authentic outer interface supplies
\[
q\ge16,\quad 3q<R<q^4-2,\quad X\ge q,\quad
Y>3R,\quad E>R+1,\quad k\ge2,\quad kY<c<k(Y+1).
\]
No premature assumption $k>E$ is used. For the positive real roots
$g=\sqrt G$, $f=\sqrt D$, $\tau=\sqrt A$, the exact identity
\[
 (Ig)^2-(cf)^2=I^2-c^2
\]
and elementary bounds give
\[
 \frac{b+cf}{d}<\frac{12}{E}
 <\frac{ic}{8E}<Ig-cf.
\]
Every coherent auxiliary root obeys $|y|<b+ITg$, including complex roots.
Hence every inner norm root has $|x|<\tau+b+ITg$. A negative main root,
on the other hand, would give
\[
 x=b+ITg+(t+u/Y)+\ell+\frac{b_0+g}{H}
   >b+ITg+\tau,
\]
because $t+u/Y>0$, $(b_0+g)/H>c/3>k$ and $\tau<\ell+k$.
Actual quadratic conjugation now forces $g$ integral and $x$ rational.

The inherited modulus-$Q$ exceptional-root exclusion uses
$1<A<\Delta I=\sqrt Q$, not a large inner separator. It proves that the
rational reconstruction denominators are nonzero and recovers integral
$\tau,f$. Rational Pell saturation then makes $y$ and $x$ integral.
Reducing the carrier identity modulo $Y$ gives
\[
 Y\mid H(k-R-1).
\]
The unchanged signed auxiliary argument recovers main Pell index $p=R$,
and the first Pell norm gives $k=2\psi_{2EY+1}(n)$ with $1\le n<R$.
Now
\[
 \mu=\frac{Y}{\gcd(Y,H)}=\frac{Y}{\gcd(Y,3)}>R,
 \qquad k\equiv2n\pmod Y.
\]
It follows that $2n-R-1\equiv0\pmod\mu$; its absolute value is below $R$,
so $2n=R+1$. As the same recurrence also gives $k\equiv2n\pmod E$, the
full omitted quotient
\[
 h=\frac{k-R-1}{E}
\]
is a positive integer. Dividing the carrier identity by $Y$ then recovers
$g\equiv b_0\pmod H$ and a positive $\gamma=(g-b_0)/H$.

The exact bridge to the ordinary signed compiler sets
$i_{\rm ordinary}=\Delta i$. Then
$(i_{\rm ordinary}c^2)^2=\Delta(f^2-1)=Q$, so its strong and auxiliary
equations hold literally. The pinned signed theorem permits the resulting
signed auxiliary quotient and extracts a blank K215 halt.

\paragraph{Constructive positivity.}
A canonical compiler witness has $X=2^R$, $Y>2^R$ and $R>3q$, so
$s=s_{\rm old}-3q>0$. Set
\[
 t=\gamma+ITg+\tau+y-b-\ell-hX.
\]
The auxiliary Pell equation yields $y\ge b-vf$, while
$\tau-\ell>k/3$ and $hX<k/Y<k/3$. Therefore
\[
 t\ge\gamma+T(Ig-cf)+(\tau-\ell)-hX>0.
\]
The same witness satisfies a coherent zero factor of $N$. This establishes
both directions without an unproved enlargement of a Pell index.

\paragraph{Exact degree.}
Before absorption, the eight factors have exact degrees
\[
 119,119,119,119,107,107,101,97,
\]
with nonzero leading factors, so the lean norm has degree $888$.
Writing $N_{\rm lean}$ for that unabsorbed norm, the exact identity
\[
 N=Y^{16}N_{\rm lean}\!\left(t+\frac{k-R-1}{Y}\right),
 \qquad s_{\rm old}=s+3q,
\]
adds $64$ degrees. The rational shift has degree $0$, below $t$'s degree $1$,
and cannot alter the leading form. The transport factor adds $2$, giving
$954$. The full proof supplies every leading factor and a nonvanishing
certificate uniform in every authentic coefficient tuple.

\paragraph{Twelve-positive companion.}
Retain positive $h$, use $Y=sq^3$, set
$S=H$, $L=1+HIT$, $W=b_0+H(t+b+\ell)$ and $N_k=k-hE-R$.
Then $N_kN_t(1+N)-1=0$ has exact degree $888+7+2=897$ and costs
$108$ operations. Completeness uses the preceding formula for $t$ without
the term $hX$, and soundness uses the inherited common-unit signed theorem.

\paragraph{Validation and limits.}
Independent checks multiply the formal radicals, replay complete univariate
polynomials over three prime fields using the actual fixed coefficient tuple,
verify all coefficient payload hashes, and stress the no-index-equation
bounds at $k=2$ and arbitrarily larger $X$. A separate reviewer reproduced
the analytic leading coefficient at nine points over three primes. Finite
checks corroborate the identities and transcription; they do not replace
the inherited unbounded compiler proof. The complete proof, its pinned source
texts, source code, inert circuits, and replay receipts accompany this section.
