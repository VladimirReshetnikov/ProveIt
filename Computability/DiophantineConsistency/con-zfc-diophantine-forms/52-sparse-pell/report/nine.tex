\section{The nine-variable conditional-separation construction}
\label{sec:nine}
We first work in the mixed domain
\[
 b,g,h,s,w,i\in\Pos,\qquad \phi,j,n\in\N.
\]
These are exactly nine unknowns. Every other name below is a computed
polynomial, not an additional quantified variable. Use any supplied valid
packing certificate, in particular the interior packed certificate for the
lowest degree. Let $p$ be its padding position, $W$ its maximum position,
$D_c(T)$ its weighted code, $d_c=\deg D_c$ and $K_c=d_c+2W$.

\subsection{A quadratic radix with unconditional positivity}
Set $\lambda=2^{1182967}$ and define
\begin{align*}
 B&=\lambda b^2,&\ell&=\sum_i B^{w_i},&\mathcal T&=\sum_jB^{h_j},\\
 N_0&=B^{W+1},&N_1&=4B^{K_c+1},&N&=N_0N_1,\\
 M_c&=N_0-1-(b-1)\ell,&&&\\
 T_c&=M_c+(B-2)\mathcal T N_0,&&&\\
 J_c&=2(B^p+g)^2D_c(B)+B\sum_{e=0}^{K_c}B^e,&&&\\
 S_c&=g+J_cN_0,&&&\\
 R&=(N-1)\bigl[(S_c+T_c+1)N+T_c+1\bigr],&&&\\
 G_c&=32bB^W.&&&
\end{align*}
Every exponent and sum limit is a fixed nonnegative integer. The scale is
an ordinary fixed coefficient; there is no enormous additive floor on $b$.
Since $D_c$ has leading coefficient 2 and all lower coefficients have
absolute value below $M=2^{1182955}$, while $B-1>M$,
\[
 D_c(B)>B^{d_c}>0,\qquad D_c(B)<\frac{MB^{d_c+1}}{B-1}.
\]
Also $M_c>0$. Thus the registers satisfy $R\ge N\ge8$ and
$0<b\le N\le R$, unconditionally, before any polynomial zero is assumed.

On the branch $g<4bB^W$, interior padding needs the estimate
$B^p+g<(4b+1)B^W\le5bB^W$, rather than the old zero-padding estimate.
Consequently
\[
 2(B^p+g)^2D_c(B)
 <\frac{50Mb^2B^{K_c+1}}{B-1}
 \le\frac{B^{K_c+2}}{64(B-1)}.
\]
The geometric summand is less than $2B^{K_c+1}$, proving $0<J_c<N_1$.
Moreover $4b<B$ gives $g<N_0$, and the distinct targets satisfy
$\max h_j<K_c$, so $0\le T_c<N$ and $0\le S_c<N$.

\begin{lemma}[Exact binary carry identity]\label{lem:carry7}
For $N=2^r$, $0\le S,T<N$, and
$X=(N-1)((S+T+1)N+T+1)$,
\[
 N^2\mid\binom{2X}{X}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \text{the binary addition of $S$ and $T$ has no carries}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The exact identity
$X=(S+T)N^2+(N-1-S)N+(N-1-T)$ has disjoint lower blocks even if
$S+T\ge N$. Therefore
\[
\pc(X)=\pc(S+T)+2r-\pc(S)-\pc(T)=2r-\tau_2(S,T),
\]
where $\tau_2$ is the total number of binary carries. Legendre's formula
says $v_2\binom{2X}{X}=\pc(X)$, proving both directions.
\end{proof}

When $b$ is a power of two, so are $B,N_0,N_1,N$. Lemma~\ref{lem:carry7}
splits the binomial divisibility into the low and high block carry tests.
The low mask has digit $B-b$ at every variable position, and $B-1$ at every
other position. It therefore forces exactly
\[
 g=\sum_i z_iB^{w_i},\qquad 0\le z_i<b.
\]
It excludes the distinct padding position, so the coefficient of $T^p$ in
the encoded linear form remains 1. For this decoded vector, every
coefficient of $(T^p+\sum_i z_iT^{w_i})^2D_c(T)$ has absolute value below
$M(1+\sum_i z_i)^2\le M(32b)^2=B/4$. Thus
\[
 J_c=2\sum_{e=0}^{K_c}a_eB^e,\qquad
 a_e=[T^e]\bigl((T^p+\sum_i z_iT^{w_i})^2D_c(T)\bigr)+B/2,
\]
with $0<a_e<B$. Dividing both high-block operands by 2 removes a common
binary shift. At each target, the mask becomes $B/2-1$, whose lower bits
are all 1; its addition to $a_{h_j}$ is carry-free exactly when
$a_{h_j}=B/2$. Sparse extraction makes this equivalent to $r_j(\mathbf z)=0$.
The other positions impose no condition.

Conversely, from a source zero choose a power of two $b$ greater than every
coordinate, as large as needed, and use the displayed digit encoding for $g$.
Distinct positions give $g<bB^W$. The source zero is nonzero, so $g>0$;
all carries vanish and $32g<G_c$. This stronger bound supplies completeness
of the inherited numerical ratio theorem.

\subsection{Literal ratio registers and exact quotient removal}
Define
\begin{align*}
 U&=N^2w,&Y&=N^2s,&M_0&=RY,\\
 P&=2M_0^2U,&K&=R+1+h(P-1),&A&=M_0(U+1),\\
 B_1&=2R+1,&W_1&=bw,&C&=B_1+W_1+\phi,\\
 J&=4A-5,&Q&=3W_1C-2(W_1^2-1),&&\\
 D&=(A^2-1)C^2+1,&k&=iJC,&L&=kD,\\
 E&=LC,&F&=(A^2-1)E^2+1,&&\\
 G&=A+F(CD+1-A),&H&=C+F(B_1+jC),&&\\
 I&=(G^2-1)H^2+1,&a_0&=(P^2-1)K^2+1,&a_1&=DFI,\\
 m&=[K^2-4(C-KY)^2]G_c-8gK^2.&&&&
\end{align*}
The inherited ratio theorem instead has $H_{\rm old}=B_1+j_{\rm old}C$,
$j_{\rm old}\ge1$, and divisibility
$JF\mid QF+J(H_{\rm old}-C)$. Since $F\equiv1\pmod J$ and
$F\equiv1\pmod C$, $\gcd(F,J)=1$, so that divisibility is equivalent to
\[
 J\mid Q\quad\hbox{and}\quad F\mid H_{\rm old}-C.
\]
If the second holds, $z=(H_{\rm old}-C)/F$ is positive and congruent to
$B_1$ modulo $C$. As $0<B_1<C$, uniquely $z=B_1+jC$ for $j\ge0$.
This is exactly the new $H$. Conversely every such new $j$ gives the old
positive integer
\[
 j_{\rm old}=1+\frac{F-1}{C}B_1+Fj.
\]
The quotient is an integer because $C\mid F-1$. This is a bijection of the
old divisible witnesses with the new unrestricted nonnegative witnesses,
preserving $H$ itself. No cofinal-witness assertion or extra hypothesis is
introduced. The resulting ratio conditions are precisely
\[
 a_0\text{ square},\quad a_1\text{ square},\quad J\mid Q,\quad m>0.
\]
The numerical Jones theorem gives from these conditions that $b$ is a power
of two, $8g<G_c$, and $N^2\mid\binom{2R}{R}$. Conversely the stronger
source bound $32g<G_c$ produces the requisite ratio witnesses.

\subsection{A general conditional-separation norm theorem}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:conditional}
Let $u,v,m,a_0,a_1,V_0,V_1$ be integers with $u>0$,
$0<v/u<V_0+V_1$, $a_i>0$, $V_i>0$, $a_i<V_i^2$, and
$m>0\Rightarrow a_0\ne a_1$. Put
\[
 \Omega=um,\qquad \Theta=un+v-\Omega(V_0+V_1).
\]
Then there exists $n\in\N$ satisfying
\[
 (\Theta^2-\Omega^2(a_0+a_1))^2-4\Omega^4a_0a_1=0
\]
if and only if $m>0$, $a_0,a_1$ are integer squares, and $u\mid v$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $m=0$, $\Theta=un+v>0$, excluding a zero. Otherwise factor the norm
over the reals to obtain signed roots $r_i^2=a_i$ with
$\Theta/\Omega=r_0+r_1$. Rearrangement gives
\[
 n+v/u=mZ,\qquad Z=V_0+V_1+r_0+r_1>0.
\]
The left side is positive, so $m>0$ \emph{before} integrality of either
root is known. Conditional separation now makes $x=r_0+r_1$ nonzero;
otherwise $a_0=a_1$. Since $x$ is rational,
$2xr_0=x^2+a_0-a_1$ shows $r_0,r_1\in\mathbb Q$.
A rational number whose square is an integer is an integer, as follows
by writing it as a reduced fraction. Hence $Z\in\Z$ and $u\mid v$.
Conversely choose the positive integer roots and put $n=mZ-v/u$.
Then $n\in\Z$ and $n\ge Z-v/u>V_0+V_1-v/u>0$.
Substitution makes a linear factor zero. The quotient bound is needed only
for completeness, not for the positivity-first soundness argument.
\end{proof}
Positive majorants are essential, not just $V_i^2>a_i$. Without separation,
$a_0=a_1=2$, $V_0=V_1=2$, $u=v=2$, $m=1$, $n=3$ is a false square
witness. Without positive majorants, $a_0=1,a_1=4,V_0=-2,V_1=4$,
$u=v=1,m=-1,n=0$ is a false positive-margin witness, even though the
squared-majorant and quotient inequalities hold. These are retained
as negative controls.

\subsection{Sharper Pell majorant and the actual equation}
Unconditionally $A\ge2$ and $P,K,C,J,D,E,F,G,H>1$. Define
\[
 V_0=PK,\qquad T=k(8D^2-4D-1),\qquad V_1=TGH.
\]
The identities $E=CL$, $L=kD$ give $F=(D-1)k^2D^2+1$. Direct expansion
then yields
\[
 V_0^2-a_0=K^2-1>0,\qquad
 T^2-64DF=(8D+1)k^2-64D=8D(k^2-8)+k^2>0,
\]
because $k=iJC\ge3$. Therefore the strictly positive majorant $V_1$ has
\[
 \Delta_1=V_1^2-64a_1
 =[(8D+1)k^2-64D](GH)^2+64DF(H^2-1)>0.
\]
The lower degree of this polynomial defect, rather than simply a smaller
numerical bound, is the degree improvement.

If $m>0$, its defining margin implies $4(C-KY)^2<K^2$, so
$C>K(Y-1/2)>K$ because $Y\ge64$. Also
\[
 A^2-P=M_0^2(U^2+1)>0,\quad D>C^2,\quad F\ge A^2,\quad
 G\ge A,\quad I\ge G^2\ge A^2.
\]
For $G\ge A$, use $G=A+F(CD+1-A)$ and $D\ge A^2$, $C\ge1$.
It follows that
\[
 a_1=DFI>C^2A^4>C^2P^2>K^2P^2>a_0.
\]
This separation is established without assuming squarehood or divisibility.
The same positivity-first proof works without the auxiliary quotient offsets.
Indeed $Q<3C^2$, $D>C^2$ and $T>3D$ imply $V_1>Q$.
The promised fixed polynomial is
\begin{align*}
 \Omega&=Jm,\\
 \Theta&=Jn+Q-\Omega(V_0+V_1),\\
 F_9&=[\Theta^2-\Omega^2(a_0+64a_1)]^2
                   -256\Omega^4a_0a_1=0.
\end{align*}
To check the offset-free form explicitly, $m=0$ gives $\Theta=Jn+Q>0$.
Otherwise a zero factor gives $n+Q/J=mZ$ with
$Z=V_0+V_1+r_0+r_1>0$. Thus $m>0$, and the separation just proved
makes $r_0+r_1$ nonzero. The rational reconstruction from
Theorem~\ref{thm:conditional} gives integer roots and $J\mid Q$.
Conversely choose the positive integer roots and set $n=mZ-Q/J$.
As $Z>V_1>Q>Q/J$, this is a positive integer and a factor vanishes.

Squarehood of $64a_1$ is equivalent to squarehood of $a_1$: an integer
square root divided by 8 is a rational square root of an integer, hence
an integer. The finalizer is therefore equivalent to exactly the four
ratio conditions. Composing both directions proves the desired source
solvability equivalence.

For the positive-domain polynomial, replace $\phi,j,n$ in this mixed-domain
formula by the corresponding positive coordinates minus 1. For the
nonnegative-domain polynomial, replace $b,g,h,s,w,i$ by the corresponding
nonnegative coordinates plus 1. These are bijections; the supplied circuit
ledgers charge their operations. They must not be omitted when stating the
domain.

\subsection{Exact degree and the retained evaluator frontier}
Let $\ell_0=K_c+W+2$ and $t=2\ell_0=\deg N$. On the all-variable
diagonal, the leading coefficients of $N,R$ are respectively
$4\lambda^{\ell_0}$ and $16\lambda^{3\ell_0}$. The geometric summand
uniquely dominates $J_c$. Direct propagation gives
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{ll@{\qquad}ll}
\toprule
Register&Degree&Register&Degree\\\midrule
$P$&$12t+3$&$K$&$12t+4$\\
$A,J$&$7t+2$&$C$&$3t$\\
$D$&$20t+4$&$k$&$10t+3$\\
$L$&$30t+7$&$E$&$33t+7$\\
$F$&$80t+18$&$G$&$103t+22$\\
$H$&$83t+19$&$I$&$372t+82$\\
$a_0$&$48t+14$&$a_1$&$472t+104$\\
$m$&$28t+2W+11$&$\Omega$&$35t+2W+13$\\
$V_0$&$24t+7$&$V_1$&$236t+52$\\
$\Delta_1$&$412t+92$&&\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The $\Delta_1$ degree follows from its positive decomposition, whose first
summand uniquely dominates. Put $E_0=Jn+Q$. The exact identity
\[
\begin{split}
\Theta^2-\Omega^2(a_0+64a_1)
={}&E_0^2-2E_0\Omega(V_0+V_1)\\
&+\Omega^2[(K^2-1)+\Delta_1+2V_0V_1]
\end{split}
\]
resolves the leading cancellation. The uniquely dominant term is
$\Omega^2\Delta_1$, of degree $482t+4W+118$. Its square dominates the
subtracted norm term. Thus
\[
 \deg F_9=964t+8W+236,
 \qquad\operatorname{lc}_{\rm diag}(F_9)=2^{1730}\lambda^{964\ell_0+4W}.
\]
This is nonzero for the actual tuple, and a multivariate upper bound matches
this diagonal lower bound, proving exact total degree.

The regular interior layout costs 548 operations at degree 15502748.
Uniformly shifting the packed interior targets by 186 makes their minimum
$2W$, allowing the shared homogeneous source evaluator described in the next
