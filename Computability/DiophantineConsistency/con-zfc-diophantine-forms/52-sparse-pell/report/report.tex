\begin{abstract}
We improve the explicit consistency equations in ProveIt packages 38--45
by using only the active quadratic source support, overlapping target digits,
and an interior padding digit. The largest positional weight falls from 799
to 174. A new conditional-separation norm theorem, a second-order polynomial
Pell majorant, and an exact CRT witness reparameterization substantially
simplify the nine-variable finalizer. The recommended compromise
uses nine positive unknowns, degree \NineDegree{}, and \NineOps{} live binary
operations; a 655-operation companion lowers the degree to $10\,127\,484$. A ten-unrestricted-integer companion has degree $33\,202\,776$
in 689 operations; shared homogenization also gives degree $50\,822\,936$
in only 582 operations. An independent lean-carrier construction gives
eleven positive unknowns at degree 954 in 113 operations and twelve at
degree 897 in 108 operations. Each lack-of-solutions assertion is
provably equivalent in ZFC by the mathematical arguments below to the inherited syntactic
$\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$ predicate. We give the new proofs, literal polynomial
registers, complete fixed coefficient payloads, finite positional certificates,
and isolated reproducibility checks. We do not claim global minimality,
a consistency decision, a new unknown-count theorem, or a newly checked
end-to-end Lean, Rocq, or object-level ZFC derivation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Results and the meaning of simplest}
The domain is part of an equation's specification. Throughout
$\N=\{0,1,\ldots\}$ and $\Pos=\{1,2,\ldots\}$. An equation over
$\Pos^9$ is not silently an equation over $\Z^9$.

\begin{theorem}[Consistency presentations]
Let the fixed integer polynomials in this report be instantiated by the
complete coherent tuple in \path{coefficients/COEFFICIENT_BINDING.json}.
Then, with the inherited compiler and arithmetic dependencies identified
below, ZFC proves each of
\[
\begin{aligned}
\neg\exists\mathbf x\in\Pos^9\ F_9(\mathbf x)=0
 &\longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}),\\
\neg\exists\mathbf x\in\Z^{10}\ F_{10}(\mathbf x)=0
 &\longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}),\\
\neg\exists\mathbf x\in\Pos^{11}\ P_{11}(\mathbf x)=0
 &\longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}),\\
\neg\exists\mathbf x\in\Pos^{12}\ P_{12}(\mathbf x)=0
 &\longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\end{aligned}
\]
A separate coordinate translation gives the supplied nine-nonnegative form.
\end{theorem}
This is an equivalence theorem, not a proof of either truth value. The
consistency predicate means the absence of a finite syntactic ZFC refutation
under the pinned K215 calibration. No unconditional independence claim is
inferred merely from consistency.

There is no single numerical simplicity order. The nine-variable result is
recommended when the number of positive unknowns matters. For unrestricted
integers, retain the ten-variable Pareto alternatives rather than hiding the
cost--degree tradeoff. The eleven- and twelve-positive equations are useful
when low total degree and short evaluation are preferred. The historical
67-operation scalar and 59-operation system remain unbeaten here on their
operation measures alone.

\subsection{New mechanisms}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Isolate only the 71 active quadratic monomials from all 528 possible
monomials, allowing irrelevant collisions. Overlapping target bands and an
interior padding position further shorten the source.
\item In the nine-variable norm, infer a positive margin before recovering
integer roots. That margin itself separates the radicands, eliminating a
large multiplicative separator.
\item Use a second-order Pell square-root majorant whose polynomial defect
has lower degree, and absorb an entire divisibility quotient by an exact CRT
reparameterization of an existing witness.
\item Evaluate the sparse source by homogenizing the shared sixty-gate
parent and adding only its diagonal correction, instead of expanding every
coefficient independently.
\item For eleven positive variables, combine a lean Pell carrier with a
smaller absorption modulus $Y$, proving the missing quotient only after a
short Pell-index interval is recovered.
\end{enumerate}
The smaller compiler tuple is reused as a \emph{whole} from packages 39/44;
its coefficient size is not a new record. The support certificates are
explicit verified constructions, not optimality certificates.

\section{Further research questions}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can support-local collision avoidance and target placement be optimized
jointly with addition-chain cost, rather than minimizing only the maximum
position or target span? A proof-producing search can emit the same small
isolation certificate independently of its heuristic.
\item Can a third-order or asymmetrically scaled polynomial Pell majorant
improve the exact nine-variable degree after all cancellation terms are
charged? Its strict positivity must hold before squarehood is known.
\item Is there a safe analogue of conditional separation for the
unrestricted-integer three-radical norm? The negative-radicand and zero-scale
branches make a naive removal of its separators invalid.
\item Can an additional quotient be absorbed without enlarging the witness
count, while preserving both directions and the exact positive domains?
The CRT change here is a bijection, not a claim of cofinal Pell witnesses.
\item Can package 40's 457-selector factorized-receiver candidate be made a
complete compiler? Its empty cells may retain payload bits; an occupancy-first
interface proof is needed before its coefficients may replace a complete tuple.
\item Can the new sparse quadratic interface be connected directly to the
post-pin executable supplied-data Jones compiler, avoiding the quartic
sum-of-squares detour? Concrete refutation-program extraction and the
object-level ZFC derivation are distinct remaining tasks.
\end{enumerate}
These are bounded next directions, not completed improvements. The present
release already specifies the exact equations and their reproducible finite
certificates without depending on any future success.
