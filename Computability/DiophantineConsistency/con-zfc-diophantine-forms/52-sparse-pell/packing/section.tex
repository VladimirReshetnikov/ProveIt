\section{Sparse active isolation and overlapping target bands}
The full $B_2$ requirement is unnecessarily strong. The shifted quadratic
source has only $71$ distinct active monomials, although its universal
quadratic expansion has $528$. We require each active monomial to be isolated
from that expansion, but allow all irrelevant monomials to collide.

Let $q_j=\sum_\alpha p_{j,\alpha}X^\alpha$ be the shifted residuals,
$w(\alpha)=\sum_i\alpha_iw_i$, and
$f(\alpha)=(2-|\alpha|)!\prod_i\alpha_i!$. Put
\[
 C_{\mathbf z}(T)=\left(1+\sum_i z_iT^{w_i}\right)^2,
 \qquad D(T)=\sum_{j,\alpha}f(\alpha)p_{j,\alpha}
                       T^{H_j-w(\alpha)}.
\]
All source exponents are nonnegative. Our exact finite separation condition is
\[
 H_j-w(\alpha)+w(\gamma)=H_k
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad j=k\ \hbox{and}\ \alpha=\gamma,
 \tag{S}
\]
for every active source record $(j,\alpha)$, every monomial $\gamma$ of
degree at most two, and every target $H_k$.

\begin{theorem}[Sparse simultaneous extraction]
Under \textup{(S)}, for every integer tuple $\mathbf z$,
$[T^{H_j}](C_{\mathbf z}D)=2q_j(\mathbf z)$ for every $j$.
Moreover, distinct source records occupy distinct exponents in $D$.
If every weighted source coefficient is bounded in absolute value by $M$,
then every coefficient of $D$ is so bounded, and for nonnegative $\mathbf z$
every coefficient of $C_{\mathbf z}D$ is bounded by
$M(1+\sum_i z_i)^2$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Expand the square retaining its formal multinomial labels, even if some
irrelevant labels have the same exponent. Condition \textup{(S)} permits
only the intended label at a target, whose multinomial multiplier
$2/f(\alpha)$ cancels the weighting in $D$. If two source records occupied
the same exponent, multiplying by either record's monomial would violate
\textup{(S)} at its target. Hence source exponents are distinct.
Finally the coefficients of $C_{\mathbf z}$ are nonnegative and sum to
$(1+\sum_i z_i)^2$, giving the asserted convolution bound.
\end{proof}

The certified variable weights, in source order, are
\[
\begin{split}
(&9,89,38,43,2,118,82,27,88,83,23,163,123,148,3,108,\\
 &144,155,137,173,174,68,17,107,63,57,169,168,28,133,102).
\end{split}
\]
Their maximum is $174$, versus $799$ previously. The $528$ monomials
occupy only $265$ positions: this is deliberately not a Sidon set.
All $71$ active monomials are nevertheless isolated. With rows ordered
$1,2,3,4,6,\ldots,22,5$, regular targets $H_j=349j$ yield
$D_{\max}=7678$ and $K_{\rm code}=8026$.

An independently checked overlapping placement has targets
\[
\begin{split}
(&63,3297,4744,1100,752,3094,2209,3586,912,4689,2437,\\
 &4168,3833,2764,78,4364,425,1347,1548,1614,1882,5074),
\end{split}
\]
yielding $D_{\max}=5074$ and $K_{\rm code}=5422$.
In both cases the largest target belongs to shifted row $5$, whose constant
coefficient is $1$, so $D$ has leading coefficient $2$ and is positive at
all sufficiently large positive bases. The existing target mask uses only
distinctness of its target positions, not their equal spacing. Thus the
same no-carry theorem applies without an extra factor $22$ in its
coefficient bound. Both layouts are retained because irregular exponent
arithmetic can trade lower degree for more operations.

The supplied standard-library verifier reconstructs the source symbolically
and checks all $71\,808$ source-term/expansion pairs. The search itself is
not trusted and no global optimality is claimed. An additional certified layout puts the padding position at $81$, with
one variable position at $0$, and replaces $(1+g)^2$ by $(B^{81}+g)^2$.
Its packed layout has $D_{\max}=4728$, $K_{\rm code}=5076$, and largest
target $4890$. The leading coefficient remains $2$. The decoding and
coefficient bounds are unchanged. Completeness still gives $g\ge b$:
no source zero can be supported on its sole zero-position variable $i$,
as shifted row $5$ is independent of $i$ and has constant $1$.
All bounds use $D_{\max}$ rather than the largest target, which is different
for interior padding. Full vectors and a checked translation-normalization
certificate are included alongside the general proof.
For the wider nine-variable witness interval $0\le g<4bB^W$,
$B^{81}+g<(4b+1)B^W\le5bB^W$; for Sun's interval
$0\le g<2bB^W$, the corresponding bound is $3bB^W$.
Thus interior padding preserves the respective coefficient budgets.
Uniformly raising all packed interior targets by $186$ makes their minimum
$348=2W$, permitting a shared homogeneous evaluator with no negative powers.
This multiplies $D$ by $T^{186}$, preserves every extraction identity, and
gives $D_{\max}=4914$ and $K_{\rm code}=5262$. It is supplied as a separately
verified degree--operation tradeoff.
