# Coefficient bundle provenance

This directory preserves the complete compact native coefficient tuple
and its reconstruction, audit, proof, and license dependencies from
VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt at commit
`9cca523d9f27ad39d52993f4065cbd7e58f8bdd4`.

All upstream bytes are listed in `UPSTREAM_FILES.json`; their original
copyright notices and license texts remain intact. The source44 builder
and independent auditor are unchanged. The four compressed coefficient
files are the exact upstream payloads, independently regenerated and
verified by the included wrapper.

New files are `verify.py`, `INTEGRATION.md`, `POSITIVE_PORTS.json`, this
notice, the retrieval manifest, and the local reconstruction receipts.
The new wrapper and integration material are available under the MIT No
Attribution license in `LICENSE`.

See `INTEGRATION.md` for the active whole-tuple convention and the scope
of the executable audit and inherited mathematical proof dependencies.
