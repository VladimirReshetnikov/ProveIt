# A positive lower-degree projection numerator

This ordinary arithmetic sibling keeps the ten strictly positive integer
unknowns, all three root weights and the complete coherent four-port
coefficient tuple of the frozen102-operation degree368 endpoint. It
replaces only its projection numerator by

    Kpos=X*(3c-2X)+2.

The evaluator has101 live operations and exact total degree368. Every

This is an ordinary unbounded arithmetic reduction, not a new Lean or
encoded object-ZFC proof, proof of consistency, or optimality claim.

## 1. Complete definitions and elementary identities

Use exactly the ten positive coordinates and all definitions of
`source/WEIGHTED_PROOF.md`, except replace Kquad by Kpos in its center:

    J,F,alpha,i,T,w,Y,n,eta,zeta in Z_{>0},

    F8(delta*Z^2;9*delta*M^2*Dtau,delta*M^2*Df,M^2*Dnum)=0.

Here A denotes the full main Pell parameter, H=4A-5,
P=q^3(q-1), and all other abbreviations are retained verbatim.
The required port box is unchanged:

    beta odd, beta>=31, K0>0, 0<MC<beta<MF<2beta.

The original whole compact44 tuple is used in the literal evaluator.
The separately calibrated whole BFS page tuple is also licensed by
this uniform arithmetic proof; their different K0 values are not

Define only in the proof the old projection numerator

    Kquad=X*(X+2*(A-2)*c)-1.

Two exact ring identities are

    Kpos=H*X*c-2*Kquad,                                    (2)
    3*Kquad-H*X*c=3*(X^2-1)+(2A-7)*X*c>0.                  (3)

The strict inequality holds at every positive input: X>=32 and A>4.
H is positive and odd, so gcd(H,2)=1. For any integer P, (2) implies

    H|P*Kpos  if and only if  H|P*Kquad.                   (4)

Only2 is cancelled modulo H; P is not cancelled and need not be
coprime to H. No primality or primewise choice of a root is used.

## 3. Completeness with a strict positive witness map

Take any positive zero of the frozen weighted102/368 endpoint. Its
proved soundness supplies the positive integer

    kappa=Kquad/H.

Keep every input other than n, and put

    n_new=n_old+3*kappa-X*c.                               (6)

    Kpos/H=Xc-2*kappa,
    n_new+Kpos/H=n_old+kappa.

    H*(n+3u-Xc)+Kpos-(H*n+Kquad)=3*(H*u-Kquad).              (7)
