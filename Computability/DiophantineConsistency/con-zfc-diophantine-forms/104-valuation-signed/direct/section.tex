\section{A smaller direct-calculus quartic over the integers}
\label{sec:direct1190}

The second construction improves a different part of the repository's
frontier. It uses the received synchronized numerical-calculus equation,
which is independent of K215 and Jones. Its coefficients are ordinary
small integers. The new result is
\[
 P_{1190}(x_0,\ldots,x_{1189})
   =\sum_{j=0}^{599}R_j(x_0,\ldots,x_{1189})^2,
 \qquad x_i\in\mathbb Z,
\]
where the entire list of quadratic residuals $R_j$ is supplied in
\texttt{direct/output/equation.json}. The adjacent expanded coefficient
file contains the same polynomial as 26,411 collected monomials.
Its degree is exactly four, its coefficient height is 83,900,628, and the
largest absolute coefficient of a residual is 4,556. Thus no arithmetic
macro or unspecified coefficient is hidden in this presentation.

\paragraph{The fixed source and inherited theorem.}
The source is the received synchronized equation in 522 nonnegative
coordinates and 400 quadratic residuals. The complete upstream construction
is included with its proofs and calibration sources. Its finite proof
certificates encode the numerical calculus $C_{222}$ directly, using a
commutative Horn flow, an accumulator path, CRT, and synchronized Pell
certificates. The named calibration interfaces are
\texttt{KEndpoint.c222\_iff\_zf\_refutation} and
\texttt{ConstructibleChoice.zf\_refutation\_iff\_zfc\_refutation}.
We preserve the entire natural source and its ordinary theorem.

\paragraph{Inferring signs instead of guarding every coordinate.}
Consider a residual
\[
 c x+Q(\boldsymbol y)=0,\qquad c\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\},
\]
where $x$ does not occur in $Q$ and every coefficient of $-cQ$ is
nonnegative. The equation implies $c^2x=-cQ$. In each monomial on the
right, even powers are nonnegative for arbitrary integer inputs.
Consequently it suffices to know that every variable occurring to an odd
power is nonnegative. This makes the right side nonnegative and proves
$x\ge0$, because $c^2>0$.

This is solely an inference about signs. It makes no claim that division
by $c$ is an integral polynomial substitution. Its monic special case is
the familiar observation that $x=F(\boldsymbol y)$ is nonnegative when
$F$ has nonnegative coefficients and the needed inputs are nonnegative.
One nonmonic instance in the actual certificate is the row
$16A=20+4T$, which proves $A\ge0$ once $T\ge0$.

Applied to the frozen source, the procedure selects 234 coordinates and
supplies an ordered list of 288 sign inferences. Every dependency in an
inference occurs earlier or is one of the selected coordinates. Thus no
circular implication is used. The selected coordinates are exactly those
that occur as the target of no eligible single-row inference of this kind;
their closure reaches all 522 coordinates. The guard count is therefore
minimal within this particular inference rule system. No broader
optimality claim follows.

For each selected coordinate $x_i$, introduce three fresh unrestricted
integers $a_i,b_i,c_i$ and append
\[
 x_i-a_i^2-b_i^2-c_i^2-c_i=0.                 \tag{DG}
\]
For every integer $c_i$, $c_i(c_i+1)\ge0$, so (DG) forces $x_i\ge0$.
Conversely, every nonnegative $x_i$ has such a representation. Legendre's
three-square theorem applies to $4x_i+1$, which is $1$ or $5$ modulo $8$
and is not divisible by $4$. Exactly one representing square is odd,
so, after reordering,
\[
 4x_i+1=(2a_i)^2+(2b_i)^2+(2c_i+1)^2.
\]
Subtracting one and dividing by four proves (DG).

The integer checkpoint consequently has
\[
 522+3\cdot234=1224\quad\hbox{coordinates},
 \qquad 400+234=634\quad\hbox{quadratic residuals}.
\]
At any integer zero, the guards and ordered inferences force every
original coordinate to be natural. The unchanged source equations then
give a natural parent zero. Conversely, every natural parent zero extends
by the three-square representations. This proves existence equivalence
in both directions; uniqueness of guard witnesses is unnecessary.

\paragraph{Signed monic projection.}
Over the integers, a residual $v-F(\boldsymbol w)=0$, or its negative,
allows substitution of $F$ for $v$ in every other residual. If $v$ does
not occur in $F$, projection is a bijection on integer zero sets, with
inverse $v=F(\boldsymbol w)$. Negative coefficients in $F$ cause no
problem. Every sign constraint must be preserved either as a substituted
residual or through the exact restoration formula of a deleted guard row.

The received exact projection algorithm removes 34 coordinates from
the checkpoint: 23 affine restorations and 11 quadratic restorations.
An affine restoration preserves the quadratic row bound. A quadratic
restoration is used only when the deleted coordinate occurs only as a
scalar multiple, never in a product with another coordinate, in every
other row. Every operation is recorded, every resulting row
is checked to remain quadratic, and no unconstrained coordinate is
discarded. Reverse restoration recovers the complete guarded checkpoint
before the nonnegativity argument is applied.

We obtain $1224-34=1190$ coordinates and $634-34=600$ residuals.
Since an integer sum of squares vanishes exactly when each square does,
the preceding equivalences and the inherited calibration establish
\[
 \mathrm{ZFC}\ \vdash\quad
 \bigl(\forall\boldsymbol x\in\mathbb Z^{1190},\,
              P_{1190}(\boldsymbol x)\ne0\bigr)
       \ \longleftrightarrow\ \operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\]
These are finite ordinary algebraic arguments formalizable in ZFC,
including its internal natural and integer domains. They supply no new
complete Lean proof term or encoded object-language ZFC derivation.

\paragraph{Verification and scope.}
A separately authored standard-library checker imports neither the
producer nor the optimizer. It authenticates the frozen source, checks
the entire unchanged prefix, every guard and ordered sign inference,
all 34 substitutions and inverse maps, and both complete coefficient
dictionaries. Four deliberately corrupted artifacts are rejected without
using hash failure as the reason. The preserved parent release has its
own complete replay. These checks establish finite artifact identities;
the unbounded Pell, CRT, and calculus theorems remain the explicitly
identified mathematical dependencies.

The contribution is a new explicit reduction of received constructions,
not a new three-square theorem or a claim of global simplicity. Other
repository equations have far fewer variables and very different degrees
or coefficient payloads. Here the gain is a smaller complete direct-calculus
quartic with literal coefficients. Further improvements could use multi-row
sign implications, exact Pell sign symmetries after contraction, or a
simultaneous frame designed directly for signed quotients.
