# Attribution and proof boundary

New material: the nested-modulus main-root carrier, its ordered existence
proof, degree26 and degree30 fourteen-positive equations, literal circuits,
and their finite verification code. Prepared in an AI-assisted research
session for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt collection,23October2026.

The construction explicitly builds on ProveIt packages44,58 and59.
Package44 supplies the whole compact Wang943 tuple and native adapter.
Package58 supplies integral-root desaturation and the fresh squared-modulus
auxiliary witnesses. Package59 supplies ordered main-root index absorption.
The ordinary strong-rank/signed-index proof and Pell audit are inherited
from the pinned source collection. The precise files and Git blob hashes
are recorded in `SOURCE_PINS.json`; the compact adapter preserves its own
older dependency ledger under `coefficients/SOURCE_PINS.json`.

All inherited licenses and notices are retained in `coefficients/licenses/`.
New material is provided under the repository's MIT No Attribution license,
copied as `LICENSE`. The machine/compiler dependencies retain the credits
and notices in the accompanying source data. The new mathematical review
is internal AI-assisted review, not external human peer review.

An ordinary mathematical proof formalizable in ZFC is supplied for the new
transformation. It is not a new end-to-end Lean/Rocq development or an
object-language ZFC derivation file. The existing repository's closed
nine-variable Lean equation does not automatically certify these different
literal fourteen-variable circuits.
