\section{A thirteen-positive degree-126 auxiliary quotient}
\label{sec:positive13-quotient}
This additional construction has thirteen positive unknowns, exact degree
$126$, and $91$ binary arithmetic operations ($51$ multiplications and
$40$ additions/subtractions). It is a degree--operation tradeoff against the
late incoming degree-$132$, $83$-operation construction, not a dominance
claim. It uses that construction's entire fixed native-window coefficient
tuple, with $\beta=2^{590069}-1$ and the already shifted $M_F$.

Supply exactly $J,F,\alpha,z,f,i,T,s,w,\eta,\zeta,y,t>0$. Define
\begin{align*}
q&=\beta J+1,&X&=wq,&Y&=sq^3,&E&=XY,\\
k&=\eta+\zeta,&c&=kY+\eta,&a&=E+Y,&H&=4a+3,\\
\Delta&=a^2+H=(a+1)(a+3),&\ell&=EkY,\\
A&=1+\ell(\ell+k),&G&=1+\Delta c^2,&b_0&=X+ac.
\end{align*}
Set
\begin{align*}
R&=((q-1)(q-F)+\alpha)(q^2-1)+(M_C+qM_F)J,\\
N_t&=(K_0+w)(q-F-\alpha)+q-F-z(q-1),\\
U&=4q^4,\qquad r=k-R-1,\qquad S=UH,\\
W&=Ub_0+H\bigl(U(t+\ell)+r\bigr),\\
N&=(W^2+U^2G-S^2A)^2-4W^2U^2G.
\end{align*}
These abbreviations are the late parent's separate main/first-Pell norm.
The new auxiliary abbreviations are
\begin{align*}
I&=ic^2,&N_s&=f^2-\Delta I^2,&Q&=\Delta(f^2-1),\\
V&=c(Tf-1)-Rf^2,&M&=a+1,&C_*&=(a+3)(f^2-1),\\
Z&=C_*(V^2-1)+(Q-1)y(2-My).
\end{align*}
In particular $MC_*=Q$; the notation $C_*$ is unrelated to the fixed
coefficient port $M_C$. The single equation is
\[
\boxed{\quad P=N_t(1+N)N_s(1+Z)-1=0.\quad}
\]
Every abbreviation is polynomial and introduces no quantified unknown.

\paragraph{The quotient identity and the sign gap.}
Direct expansion gives the universal polynomial identity
\[
MZ=QV^2-(Q-1)(My-1)^2-1.                       \tag{*}
\]
For integers $m\ge2$ and $0<b<2m-2$, the equation
$x^2-(m^2-1)v^2=-b$ has no integer solution. Indeed, after taking absolute
values, $v>0$ and $(m-1)v<x<mv$, because
$(2m-2)v^2>b$. Multiplication by the norm-one element
$m-\sqrt{m^2-1}$ gives another solution with strictly smaller positive
ordinate $mv-x$. Infinite descent proves the assertion.

\paragraph{Soundness, with the order explicit.}
If $P=0$, its four integer factors are units. Since $N$ is a square modulo
$4$, the factor $1+N$ must be $1$. The negative choice for $N_s$ is excluded
by the preceding lemma with $m=a+2$ and $b=1$, since
$\Delta=(a+2)^2-1$. Thus $N_s=1$ and
$Q=(\Delta I)^2$. Put $m=\Delta I\ge2$.
If $1+Z=-1$, identity~(*) gives
\[
(mV)^2-(m^2-1)(My-1)^2=-(2a+1).
\]
This is impossible because
$0<2a+1<2\Delta I-2$: already $I\ge1$, $a>0$, and
$2\Delta-2-(2a+1)=2a^2+6a+3>0$.
Therefore $Z=0$ and $N_t=1$. Notice that no transport bound was needed
before this recovery. Now $y_{\rm old}=My-1>0$ restores exactly the late
parent's thirteen-positive system:
\[
N_t=1,\quad N=0,\quad N_s=1,\quad
QV^2-(Q-1)y_{\rm old}^2=1.
\]
The parent's main-norm recovery and normalized strong/auxiliary rank proof
therefore restore the full compiler interface, giving a blank K215 halt
and a finite syntactic ZFC refutation.

\paragraph{Completeness and the residue.}
Start with canonical normalized compiler witnesses for an accepting
computation, keeping the outer variables, $f,i$ and the parent's positive
carrier $t$. The whole fixed tuple gives $R\equiv3\pmod4$.
Write $m=\Delta I$, so $Q=m^2$, and choose
\[
V=\frac{\chi_m(R)}m,\qquad y_{\rm old}=\psi_m(R).
\]
The parent's explicit odd-index reconstruction proves
$V\equiv-c\pmod{f^2}$, $V\equiv-R\pmod c$ and
$y_{\rm old}\equiv-1\pmod Q$.
The last congruence also follows directly because $\psi_m(R)$ is an even
polynomial in $m$ with constant term $(-1)^{(R-1)/2}=-1$.
Consequently
\[
T=\frac{V+c+Rf^2}{cf}>0,
\qquad y=\frac{y_{\rm old}+1}{M}>0
\]
are integers, using $\gcd(c,f)=1$, $f^2\equiv1\pmod c$, and $M\mid Q$.
The Pell identity and~(*) give $Z=0$. The other three factors are $1$, so
$P=0$. This changes the meaning of $y$ and may replace $T$; it does not
assert the residue for every old auxiliary witness.

\paragraph{Exact degree.}
Let $q_0=\beta J$, $Y_0=sq_0^3$, $E_0=wq_0Y_0$,
$k_0=\eta+\zeta$, $c_0=k_0Y_0$, $\ell_0=E_0c_0$,
$U_0=4q_0^4$, $H_0=4E_0$, $I_0=ic_0^2$ and
$N_{t,2}=w(q_0-F-\alpha)-zq_0$.
The parent's exact cancellation identity gives degree $68$ for $N$, with
\[
N_{68}=4U_0^4H_0^3\ell_0^3(2t-k_0).
\]
The strong factor has degree $34$ and leading form $-E_0^2I_0^2$.
The two summands of $Z$ each have degree at most $22$, and their combined
highest part is
\[
Z_{22}=E_0f^2(c_0^2T^2f^2-E_0^2y^2)\ne0.
\]
Thus $\deg P=2+68+34+22=126$, with nonzero leading form
\[
-N_{68}E_0^3I_0^2f^2(c_0^2T^2f^2-E_0^2y^2)N_{t,2}.
\]
This is a universal degree proof, not an inference from sampled rays.

\paragraph{Logical and reproducibility boundary.}
Composing both directions with the inherited compiler and syntactic
calibration yields, in ZFC,
\[
\text{$P$ has no strictly positive integer zero}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC}).
\]
No global minimality or consistency decision is claimed. The literal DAG,
parent and coefficient hashes, complete proof and independent finite
checks accompany this section. The checker verifies polynomial identities,
all live gates, the $91$-operation count and actual-coefficient degree rays;
these checks do not replace the unbounded compiler proof.
