\begin{abstract}
Combining three previously established coordinate reductions gives nine
new local fifteen-positive-variable degree/operation tradeoffs and lowers
the degree of a fourteen-variable, 79-operation equation from 56 to 52.
The inherited absolute operation record remains 66; the inherited degree-44
point remains unchanged. We give exact literal polynomials, positive
existence reductions, coherent materialized coefficients and independent
finite arithmetic audits, while explicitly retaining the inherited
compiler proof boundary.
\end{abstract}
