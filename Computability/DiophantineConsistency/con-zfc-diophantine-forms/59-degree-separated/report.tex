\begin{abstract}
We review all eight newly delivered wave-seven packages and combine their
ideas with a different coefficient-extraction architecture. Quadratic,
linear and constant source terms are encoded in separate kernels, so their
labels may collide across degrees without corrupting a tested coefficient.
This reduces the source convolution height from 5556 to 2161. Combined
with calibrated carry blocks, exact CRT parametrization and sharper Pell
majorants, it yields smaller explicit nine-natural and ten-integer
polynomials. A coprime spectator guard removes an unnecessary radical
weight in the thirteen-integer route. Independently, retaining the ordinary
Pell root rather than its multiplier yields a twelve-positive-variable
polynomial of degree 316. A late composition with a parallel free-coordinate
construction gives the recommended nine-positive equation at degree
$1\,910\,404$ and 473 operations. All coefficients are fixed by explicit recipes
and materialized native payloads. The proofs are ordinary mathematical
reductions with identified inherited dependencies, accompanied by finite
exact certificates; no consistency proof, global minimum or new
end-to-end formal-kernel certificate is asserted.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and reproducible review boundary}
The task is to construct an explicit integer-coefficient polynomial $F$
for which ZFC proves
\[
 \left(\neg\exists\mathbf x\in D^r\ F(\mathbf x)=0\right)
 \longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{ZFC}),
\]
with a small description, low degree, few unknowns or a short arithmetic
circuit. These are different size objectives. We state the domain $D$
explicitly: $\N$ includes zero, positive integers exclude zero, and
unrestricted integers form a third domain. A positive-domain equation is
not silently reinterpreted over $\Z$.

The eight input archives were retrieved at arrival commit
\texttt{4b4349064a75}, verified against their Git blob hashes, and inspected
as original delivered packages. Their placement commit
\texttt{6d375f2d2dea} assigns sources 46--53 under
\begin{center}\small
\url{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/tree/main/Computability/DiophantineConsistency/con-zfc-diophantine-forms}.
\end{center}
Full commit IDs, archive hashes and member provenance are in
\texttt{INCOMING\_PINS.json} and the component ledgers. The catalogue was
not yet reconciled at this placement, so comparisons use the original
packets rather than an outdated summary. Source53 is the preceding
32-page delivery in this conversation.

Every register below abbreviates a polynomial expression, not an additional
existential unknown. All exponents in the final circuits are fixed
nonnegative integers. Rational quantities, square roots, valuations and
divisibility appear in the proofs, not as permitted operations in the
polynomial. The route is finite ZFC refutation $\leftrightarrow$ calibrated
blank K215 halting $\leftrightarrow$ a native-window/Pell source solution
$\leftrightarrow$ a zero of the explicit new polynomial. Negation supplies
the requested equivalence without deciding either side.

\section{What changes mathematically}
The previous selective code still homogenized a quadratic system by adding
a dummy coordinate. It therefore separated linear from quadratic labels
although no source equation needed those cross-degree products. We instead
use
\[
 G(B,U)=D_2(B)U^2+D_1(B)U+D_0(B).
\]
Only labels within the same homogeneous degree can interfere. The resulting
finite certificate has 89 source records, 22 exact target identities and
22,544 interactions, rather than the preceding 136 records and 71,808
interactions. It deliberately permits a linear label to equal a quadratic
label. A signed leading-layer estimate is needed: positive input coordinates
do not by themselves make a signed kernel positive.

The arithmetic finalizers also impose only the relations actually needed.
The mask's binary population calibrates the binomial divisibility threshold;
a small factor in the block length makes that threshold a polynomial square.
An exact CRT parametrization removes one divisibility quotient. A spectator
radicand in the last Pell guard prevents coincident radicals without a large
external separator. Finally, choosing which Pell witness to retain changes
the direct moderate-degree construction dramatically: keeping $f$ makes
$Q=\Delta(f^2-1)$ degree 14 instead of the normalized degree 46 expression.
The new merger restores integrality before invoking strong-rank results.

\section{Proof status and relationship to the formal developments}
At the reviewed placement snapshot, the repository reports a closed
executable \texttt{ExplicitZFC} expression, a direct arithmetic-graph
\texttt{NativeZFC} equivalence, and a \texttt{PositiveZFC} natural-to-positive
coordinate shift. Numerical coefficient payloads of those formal expressions
remain unmaterialized. The generic quadratic extraction foundations are
reported checked, but their complete adaptation to the final Jones theorem
is separate. The object-language ZFC development has actual addition
formulas and derivations, while its full internal consistency bridge is
still open. We inspected this status; we did not rerun those Lean builds.

Those results are not kernel certificates for the smaller fixed circuits
in this report. Conversely, the existence of noncomputable historical
polynomial selections does not make all newer executable constructions
noncomputable. We distinguish explicit coefficient data, external Lean
semantic equivalences, object-language derivations, and ordinary proofs
formalizable in ZFC. None may be substituted for another without an actual
bridge theorem.

The literal K215 logical calibration, native compiler, Pell rank and Jones
ratio results are identified inherited dependencies. Only the ten-integer
route additionally uses Sun's 2026 bridge. Local finite tests certify exact
identities, bindings, circuit sizes and degree attainment; they do not
certify an unbounded compiler by testing a finite number of assignments.

\section{Limits, rejected shortcuts, and further targets}
The new code is certified, not proved optimal. Its search history is not a
semantic dependency: the finite tuple and exact isolation checker are
sufficient. More aggressive fixed-port row operations or a different
source presentation could improve it further, but require new coefficient,
positivity and degree audits.

No twelve-unrestricted-integer theorem or ten-positive-variable theorem is
claimed. In the signed construction the zero-spectator branch is handled
by recovering the first two guards and using source positivity for every
signed packed input before the final guard is read. Omitting that ordering
would permit an invalid argument. In the ordinary-root merger, integrality
comes from Pell saturation followed by the carrier identity. A tempting
shortcut from $c^2\mid d\rho$ and $\gcd(d,\rho)=1$ to $c^2\mid\rho$ is
invalid and is not used.

The most useful formalization targets are the degree-specific extraction
certificate, sparse-borrow normalization, and the ordinary-root merger's
ordered saturation proof. They are reusable arithmetic interfaces rather
than assertions about consistency itself. The supplied source maps,
explicit gates and independent algebraic checkers are intended to make
such work easier, not to present it as already completed.
