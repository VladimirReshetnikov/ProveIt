The new nine- and ten-variable primary points improve not just the preceding
package in this conversation but both extremes of the reviewed frontier.

polynomial is especially concise at moderate degree, but it does not beat
the source52 eleven-positive unknown count: the latter remains degree

\paragraph{What was reviewed.}
Sources46--49 contribute shared positive-coordinate words, selective support
codes and sharper radical majorants. Source50 supplies midpoint/index
absorption and the ordinary-strong fourteen-variable bootstrap. Source51
supplies squared-modulus quotient reconstruction and the 66-operation
record. Source52 combines CRT removal, positivity-first separation, better
majorants and lean direct Pell carriers. Source53 supplies our previous
calibrated carry and seeded-guard constructions. We inspected every entry
point and the source proofs used here; we did not claim to rerun every
upstream archive wholesale. New local data and circuit properties are
recomputed by this release's independent finite audits.
