      coefficients and independent finite arithmetic audits, while explicitly retaining the inherited compiler
      proof boundary.

1.4   Arithmetic verification and proof boundary
The independent standard-library auditor imports no generator or upstream Python code. It expands each
literal main-norm ancestor cone exactly, then propagates symbolic leading forms, preserving cancellations
rather than using only naive degree bounds. The factor degrees are

             (deg N0 , deg N1 , deg Na , deg Nk , deg Nt , deg S) = (22 or 12, 18, 30 or 23, 7, 2, 22).

The scaled factor has degree 46. All output leading forms have an exhibited monomial with nonzero
coefficient depending only on β > 0, so the proof applies to the actual bound tuple. All gates, unknowns
and ports are live. Raw/compressed coefficient hashes, ranges, mask populations and source-proof hashes
are checked, along with five universal local polynomial identities. Each of the eleven circuits passes 100
signed/degenerate literal-formula diagnostics; five exact canonical Pell examples check reconstruction and
positive gaps. Checks pass normally and under python3 -O.
    These are finite artifact checks, separate from the unbounded Pell and compiler proofs. No finite
experiment tests Con(ZFC). The contribution is the explicit combined frontier, reproducible circuits and
reduction proof, not a claim of a foundational breakthrough.
