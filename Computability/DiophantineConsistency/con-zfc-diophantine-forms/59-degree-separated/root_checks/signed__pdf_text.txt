For every integer assignment, u = J = 4A − 5 ̸= 0. The polynomial

                                   Π(x) = (x3 − x − 1)(x2 + 23)

has no rational root and has a root modulo every positive integer. For an odd prime other than
23, either the cubic has a simple root or it is irreducible, whose Frobenius three-cycle makes its
nonzero discriminant −23 a square. The quadratic then has a simple root. At 23 the cubic has
the simple root 3; at 2, −23 ≡ 1 (mod 8) admits compatible square roots. Hensel lifting and CRT
finish the assertion. Thus Θ = u5 Π(t/u + r) ̸= 0 for every assignment, and

                              ∃r   Zu5 | Θ       ⇐⇒       Z ̸= 0 and u | t.

Indeed a root modulo |Z| proves the reverse implication once u | t. If vp (u) = α > β = vp (t), the
two factors of Θ have valuations 3β and 2β, contrary to u5 | Θ. If Z = 0, divisibility would require
Θ = 0, already impossible.

particular ϵ, w, i, z1 , z2 , z3 , a0 are nonzero. Every dj ≡ 2 (mod 3) is nonzero, and every Bj∗ ≡ 1
