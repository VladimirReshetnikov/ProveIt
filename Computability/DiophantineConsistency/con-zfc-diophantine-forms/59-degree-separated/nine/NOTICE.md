# Attribution and source separation

The original package31 positive compiler, coherent coefficient tuple,
Jones ratio interface, and preceding carry-block constructions originate
in Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository and its cited mathematical
sources. The complete inherited proof texts and source bindings are kept
with their source hashes. The repository snapshot compared in this wave
is 6d375f2d2deac0519e659bbce5b48087023c1430.

The supplied packet52 contributed the positivity-first conditional radical
separation, exact CRT reparameterization, second-order Pell majorant, and
shared homogeneous comparison evaluator. The previous local wave7
contributed sparse +1 borrow padding and parity calibration. This wave
adds the degree-separated raw-positive source and its new finite layout,
composes it with those tools, extends parity calibration to the even
parameter, and independently rebuilds and audits the resulting circuits.

This directory's builder and verifier are newly authored and standard-library
only. They parse mathematical JSON specifications and integer payloads;
they do not execute upstream scripts. `circuit.py` is the reviewed preceding
local emitter, extended through authored power scheduling in the builder.
The search that found a finite certificate is not a theorem dependency.

The primary old31 tuple and the native52 comparison tuple are separate
four-port bindings. Their ports must never be mixed. In particular their
source residual15 conventions differ by -beta*J.

The copied license is retained as `LICENSE`. No new global optimality,
literature-priority, consistency, or end-to-end kernel-checking claim is made.
