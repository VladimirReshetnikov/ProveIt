\begin{abstract}
A new degree-separated source code combines with sparse carry padding,
an even-parameter parity calibration, and a positivity-first Pell finalizer
to produce an explicit integer polynomial in nine positive unknowns of exact
degree 2,676,548.  Its supplied circuit uses 476 binary arithmetic operations.
Against the previous degree 7,344,328 and 738-operation construction, these are
reductions of 63.56\% and 35.50\%.  All fixed coefficient payloads, finite
certificates, and authored rebuilding and checking programs are supplied.
Absence of zeros is provably equivalent in ZFC to Con(ZFC), using the
identified inherited compiler and Jones ratio theorems.  No proof of
consistency or global minimality is claimed.
\end{abstract}
