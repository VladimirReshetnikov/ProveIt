# Incorporation notes

This is one complete research package, containing two independently
calibrated equation families. Retain the top-level article and both
component proof boundaries when incorporating it.

Suggested descriptive directory name:

`dyadic-descaling-and-delayed-pell-signs`

The package does not assign itself a permanent numbered slot in the
collection. The receiving repository can choose its next slot without
rewriting source pins.

## Results appropriate for a catalogue

1. **Ten positive integers, degree 706, 133 operations.** Compared with the
   same-tuple parent 816/124, the degree is lower and the arithmetic circuit
   is longer. The fixed compact44 payloads remain whole. The coefficient
   `MF` is already shifted. This result uses the existing K215/native
   calibration through package61.
2. **780 unrestricted integers, quartic, 9,628 monomials, height
   2,445,790,974,704.** Compared with the direct888 parent, 36 sign guards
   and 108 integer coordinates are removed. The original direct407
   natural equation is an inherited comparator. This result uses the
   independent C222 direct certificate calibration and avoids K215 and
   Jones's universal equation.

The logical theorem for each is zero-existence iff syntactic ZFC
refutability, with the new transformation proved in ordinary mathematics
formalizable in ZFC. Do not catalogue the finite replay as an end-to-end
Lean/Rocq or internal-ZFC certificate, or call either equation globally
simplest.
