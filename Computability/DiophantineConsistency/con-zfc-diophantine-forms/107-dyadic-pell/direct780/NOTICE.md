# Provenance and attribution

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project through AI-assisted
mathematical research, independently implemented exact checks, and a
separate adversarial mathematical review.

The direct C222 calculus and its calibration, source arithmetic circuits,
Pell-index infrastructure, shared frame, common auxiliary Pell certificate,
and original quotient recurrence are inherited from ProveIt and the
incoming contributions retained in the immutable parent archive. The
immediate source is `ConZFC_407_888_Quartics_2026-10-24.zip` at commit
`ec80841c07e91be8956ce6ed4af9a9c0b782d3d9`; its original notices and complete
archive are preserved in `upstream/`. Exact member pins are in
`SOURCE_PINS.json`.

The contribution here is the delayed sign-recovery application of that
recurrence, the staged certificate that makes all earlier premises
independent of the omitted signs, stronger elementary factor inference,
and the resulting literal 780-integer quartic with independent finite
verification. No worldwide priority for the underlying Pell identities
is claimed.

`vendor/terminal_elimination.py` is unmodified from the immediate source.
Its original license remains applicable. New code and documentation are
provided under the MIT No Attribution terms in `LICENSE`, consistently
with the immediate parent's terms.
