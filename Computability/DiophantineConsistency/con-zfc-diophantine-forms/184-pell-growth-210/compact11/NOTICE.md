# Attribution and scope

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt research project with AI assistance.
New material follows the supplied MIT No Attribution license. Retained source
files and their original third-party notices are preserved byte-for-byte.

This component builds on the repository's native BFS943/radix67 calibration,
universal Pell projector, uniform ratio estimates, normalized strong-rank
and signed folding lemmas, product-square root extraction, and quotient
carriers. These inherited ideas are not presented as new.

The new contribution is the specific fixed-dilation 210, third-order integral
inverse Lucas application, with a separate integral projector quotient,
fresh early c-bound and cofinal-seed completeness argument, and its full
eleven-positive/degree-174/104-operation polynomial. Formal inverse series
are classical; no broad historical novelty or global optimum is claimed.

The direct quartic elsewhere in the accompanying submission uses a different
coefficient architecture. This companion continues to use the retained native
and K215 ancestry. Every source snapshot and archive-member provenance is
recorded in PROVENANCE.json, and all shipped bytes are bound by SHA256SUMS.

The review was independently assigned within this AI-assisted workflow.
The finite checks certify the literal algebra and fixed coefficients; they
do not certify an end-to-end formal derivation or decide consistency.
