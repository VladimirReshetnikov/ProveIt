The included LICENSE is ProveIt's MIT No Attribution license.
The native coefficient route ultimately uses the K215 compiler calibration;
the new work neither replaces nor claims to recompile that full proof closure.
The two pinned mathematical source reports explicitly identify their inherited
folding/native/refutation interfaces. They are used as established source
theorems, with their scope stated in PROOF_COMPACT.md.

The new coefficient constructor reproduces all four numerical payloads from
the published finite recipe; raw and deterministic compressed hashes match
the parent. This is finite reconstruction, not a machine execution or proof
search. The calibration of those fixed numerical payloads is inherited.

The finite checks concern literal algebra, degrees, topology, coefficients,
and targeted corruptions. They do not prove the unbounded number-theoretic
arguments or the consistency of ZFC, and are not advertised as a fresh
Lean/Rocq kernel theorem.
