# Attribution and mathematical scope

Prepared with AI assistance for Vladimir Reshetnikov, 25 October 2026.
New code and exposition are supplied under the included MIT No Attribution
license, matching the immediate parent. Original inherited notices and
source files are retained.

The direct proof-calculus encoding, C222 calibration, square-shell syntax
recoding, multiplicative Horn flow, finite CRT construction, factorial
representation, signed Pell frame and cap, canonical orientation,
cofinal semantic anchor, and joint factorial/digit norm are inherited.
Their immediate source is the direct component of
`ConZFC_Joint_Norm_2026-10-25.zip`, retrieved at ProveIt commit
`a0ef2df5bb1f73c35cd334bbc7f53553350ef06a`. The recoding supplement is
identified separately in the provenance ledger.

The contributions of this package are:

1. Two quadratic equations and two integer auxiliaries for arbitrarily
   many requests at one common base when the stated frame bound is
   sufficient; in the literal construction the groups have eleven and
   three members. The modulus is eliminated as a coordinate.
2. The signed simultaneous exactness proof using the original squared
   atom bounds, including unit bases and exclusion of negative indices
   at nonunit bases, integrated into a noncircular bootstrap.
3. Cofinal strict local ranges, removal of the `q^(t+1)` request, and
   the adjusted factorial-digit estimates.
4. The beta-base refinement `y=V-1` and its pending-multiset bound.
5. Three exact private-wire eliminations and integral Pell difference
   coordinates, with explicit residual identities and inverse maps.
6. The composed literal 225-variable quartic, its independent finite
   verifier, complete expansion, printed residual specification, and
   reproducible transformation certificates.

Elementary identities are not claimed to be new as isolated algebra.
The new combination and reduction are compared with the inspected pinned
repository sources. No absolute simplest equation or global priority
claim is asserted. Smaller-variable representations with other degrees
and coefficient tradeoffs already exist.

The report gives ordinary mathematical proofs formalizable in ZFC and
composes them with the identified inherited logical calibration. It does
not assume consistency, prove consistency, or decide whether a zero exists.
The supplied checks authenticate finite polynomial data and transformations.
No newly compiled end-to-end Lean/Rocq proof or external human peer review
is claimed. Independent reviews in this package were assigned to other
AI research agents and were used to correct the exposition and check
the changed mathematics.
