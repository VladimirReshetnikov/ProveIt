# Attribution and scope

This component's new material is the explicit composition of the signed-power
bootstrap, calibrated determinant guard, and simultaneous-positivity lemma,
the resulting 31-integer sextic, 26-integer octic, and 23-integer degree-twelve
equation, the further nonzero-scale main-norm replacement giving 24 integers
at degree ten, the auxiliary strong-norm row operation and root shear giving
22 integers at degree fourteen, and their exact new builders, straight-line
evaluators, proofs, and checks.

The named mechanisms and source equations are inherited from ProveIt packages
75-signed-bootstrap, 71-determinant-guards, 68-joint-positivity, and
64-structural-reductions. The native coefficient tuple and its computational
meaning come from 34-integer14 and the earlier compiler/K215 contributions.
The source71 sparse-polynomial utility is copied as `polynomial.py`, with its
original license retained. The original source files, source pins, licenses,
and notices are retained separately in `upstream/`, `SOURCE_PINS.json`, and
`licenses/`.

The new proof is ordinary mathematics formalizable in ZFC, composed with the
explicitly named inherited source theorems. No new end-to-end Lean or Rocq
certificate, object-language ZFC derivation, global minimum, worldwide novelty,
or external human peer review is claimed.
