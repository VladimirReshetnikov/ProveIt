# Authorship, source use, and proof scope

This is an AI-assisted research contribution prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov.
The main inherited project is ProveIt at commit
a88aefd7ddab4771534bb049c67350e397c91b9a.

New contributions are the completed retention/unit-product integer family,
the eleven-coordinate positive-power specialization and its fully compiled
direct quartics, the free-J domain extension, and the separately stated
product-budget source lemma. Classical Pell representation, radical
relation-combining, three-square/pentagonal representation, and the existing
calibrations are attributed rather than claimed as new.

Component notices identify inherited source files and archives. The fixed
coefficient recipe includes upstream machine/tile material with its original
notices. The pinned mathlib source retains its Apache2.0 license. These
notices are preserved in addition to the root MIT No Attribution license.

Independent reviews in this package are AI-assisted reviews of the stated
steps and finite data, not external peer review. Ordinary proof,
computational verification, and end-to-end formalization are distinct.
