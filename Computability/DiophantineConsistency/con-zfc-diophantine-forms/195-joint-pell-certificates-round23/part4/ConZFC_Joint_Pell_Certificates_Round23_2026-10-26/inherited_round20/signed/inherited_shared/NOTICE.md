# Attribution and scope

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt project on 25 October 2026,
with mathematical development, code, and internal reviews assisted by OpenAI
ChatGPT.

The new research contribution is the shared signed factorial-error certificate,
its nearest-integer factorial proof and acyclic signed radix bootstrap, and the
fully emitted compatible direct equations. The C222 proof calculus and logical
calibration, reciprocal CRT and Horn-flow arithmetic, Pell frame and index
theorems, common normalized remainder method, deterministic comparison center,
integer projection, and affine-exposure methodology are inherited and credited.

The final residual-row operations are explicit finite transformations with
checked inverses. Their measured improvements do not imply global optimality.
The internal independent reviews are AI-assisted; no external human peer review
is asserted.

The repository's MIT No Attribution license is included. All retained source
notices and provenance files remain with their source material.
