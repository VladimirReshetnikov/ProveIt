\begin{abstract}
This round improves several explicit polynomial presentations of syntactic
ZFC consistency, while keeping their domains and size objectives separate.
The principal unrestricted-integer quartic has 210 variables, 150 quadratic
residuals, 5276 collected monomials and coefficient height $2^{24}$.
A 209-variable quartic trades a larger height for one fewer coordinate.

A new unweighted joint Pell certificate authenticates signed indices and
power requests with two successive integrality arguments. Its selected joint equation reaches 113 variables at degree 16. A separate
unfused monic-elimination control reaches 138 variables at degree eight;
the smaller count has a substantially larger expanded support. Constructor fusion and inlining give a height-one quartic
in 484 unrestricted integers, and reduce support at 493 variables from
24828 to 17058. Two complete ten-positive-integer circuits have exact
degree/gate pairs $202/99$ and $190/120$, using the unchanged coherent
Start-only coefficient tuple. Their lower degrees cost additional gates.

The report gives the unbounded witness arguments, precise inherited
calibration boundary, complete native equations or finite substitution
specifications, actual coefficient artifacts, and reproducible finite
checks. It compares all nine incoming packages and selected current
repository sources at a pinned snapshot. Ordinary ZFC proves that each
selected equation's unsolvability on its stated domain is equivalent to
$\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$ through the identified source calibration. No truth of
consistency, globally simplest equation, external refereeing or new
end-to-end proof-assistant build is claimed.
\end{abstract}
