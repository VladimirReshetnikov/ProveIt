\subsection{The seeds before elimination}
For a fixed ordered block set $I$ and a new variable $z_I$, write
\[\mathcal P_I=\sum_{j\in I}\widehat E_j\prod_{h\in I\setminus\{j\}}T_h-z_I\prod_{j\in I}T_j.\]
The sparse 114 seed retains all source rows except $96,97,98,99,141,142,143,144$, deletes coordinates $137,138,139,140,201,202,203,204$, and appends $\mathcal P_{\{0,1\}}$ followed by $\mathcal P_{\{2,3\}}$. The other retained rows preserve ascending original order. First retain coordinates in ascending original order, then append the two new pool variables in that same pool order. This gives 203 coordinates and 143 rows.
The joint 113 seed additionally deletes row 135 and coordinate 191. Its appended rows, in order, are
\[\Omega^{\prime} T_0+d\mathcal P_{\{0\}},\qquad\mathcal P_{\{1,2,3\}}.\]
Use the same ascending retained-coordinate order and then the two new pool variables. This gives 202 coordinates and 142 rows. The matched unfused control instead keeps row 135 and coordinate 191 and appends $\mathcal P_{\{0\}},\mathcal P_{\{1,2,3\}}$.
The 138-variable endpoint starts directly from the complete 209-coordinate, 149-row conservative source, with no new pool seed. Its 71 monic substitutions are therefore generic exact eliminations, not a gain credited to the new joint theorem.
\subsection{How to read the complete ordered tables}
In each following table, $(j,r,v,\epsilon)$ means: at ordinal $j$, the current row with original seed label $r$ has the form $\epsilon u_v+f$, with no other $u_v$ term. Replace $u_v$ by $-\epsilon f$ in every other current row and delete row $r$. Row and coordinate labels remain the initial seed labels throughout; do not compact after a step. At the end retain the undeleted row labels in ascending order, and compact the undeleted coordinate labels in ascending order. The sum of squares of those remaining rows is the literal final polynomial. Thus the tables and fixed seeds alone specify every coefficient, with no optimizer or choice left to execute.
The adjacent \file{NATIVE_DEFINITION.json} files also give every resulting pivot polynomial explicitly; independent replay verifies each unit coefficient and the complete final residual arrays. The tables below are parsed independently against those files.
