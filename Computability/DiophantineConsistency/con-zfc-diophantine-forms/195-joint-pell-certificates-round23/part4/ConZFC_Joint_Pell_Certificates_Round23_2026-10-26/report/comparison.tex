\subsection{What these comparisons do and do not imply}
The incoming Direct Integer Elimination 85-variable equation has reported
exact degree 16964 and 1788 live gates; its 84-variable alternative has degree
2097732. Those lower variable counts are not quartic improvements. Their
maximum absolute literal is $1099511627780$, while their expanded coefficient
height bounds are respectively $2^{59805}$ and $2^{7342557}$. A literal maximum
must not be substituted for an expanded height. Similarly, the Mixed Base
112- and 143-variable controls have exact degrees 24 and 8, maximum literal
60, and reported expanded-height bounds strictly below $2^{79}$ and $2^{43}$.
Their supports are unreported here, not zero.

The Affine Horizons 202-variable quartics are over $\N$, not $\Z$.
Their natural triangular-source restoration argument is a genuine domain
proof, not an invertible affine map preserving every point of the orthant.
Its 200-variable degree-eight point is neither another quartic nor an
unrestricted-integer point. Positive Pell Carrier 65, unrestricted variants,
and the positive scalar charts must likewise remain in separate domain rows.

The incoming Cofinal Pell construction's smaller frame uses an order-eight
local square-class argument and a cofinal even-frame choice. Its completeness
is not a pointwise inverse for arbitrary two-square witnesses. That source's
206-variable degree-24 and 207-variable degree-16 points are meaningful
comparators to higher-degree direct systems, but not to fixed-degree quartics
without reporting degree. The selected new reciprocal family uses its explicitly pinned conservative
$m=16$ square-frame 209-coordinate source. The weighted family remains a
theorem instance and control, not the selected arithmetic source. A newer
source is not transferred merely by renaming its requests.

The source 176 joint orientation theorem is a genuine mathematical input:
it controls raw classified signed indices before canonical orientation.
The unweighted joint theorem of Section~\ref{sec:reciprocal23} is not claimed as the invention of
joint orientation itself. Source 178's whole-product centering is a distinct
positive route with its own calibrated coefficient tuple. Its 208/116
(degree/gate) control is already dominated in those two measures by the
incoming Affine Horizons 206/90 control at ten positive variables. Matching
port names or coefficient-size boxes does not equate their calibrations.

The consolidated fetch is a selected, authenticated comparison subset, not
an assertion that each extracted source subtree is independently standalone.
The release source-closure maps distinguish retained prerequisites from
comparison-only material. All ordinary mathematical reviews in this round
are AI-assisted independent reviews; they are not external human refereeing
or a new full Lean or Rocq dependency build.
