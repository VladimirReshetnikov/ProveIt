\section{Further questions with concrete proof obligations}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can the affine horizon use a smaller progression than the selected
threshold-four family? Every proposed change must reprove the initial
signed bootstrap, exclude exceptional zero or unit bases, retain the actual
$q^t$ request, and construct an odd binomial selector cofinally. A formal
coefficient saving without those conditions is not an equation for the
same arithmetic relation.
\item Can a different integral basis for the affine frame lower coefficients
without weakening the error budget too far? The relevant tests are the full
actual-atom ledger, raw classified-index bounds, $|A|>W^W$, the orientation
residue sum, and the appropriately strengthened power or joint theorem.
Bounding only the nominal requests is not enough before orientation.
\item Can the unweighted joint certificate be composed with a lower-height
source or another block partition before monic elimination? The matched
unfused control isolates the current orientation saving; further apparent
dimension improvements must report degree, collected support and height as
well. At zero or mixed-sign bases, the second integrality step cannot be
replaced by a direct nonnegative-sum argument.
\item Can exact elimination retain the joint theorem's small degree while
avoiding its large expansion? A compact arithmetic DAG and a sparse expanded
polynomial measure different resources. Any further deletion needs its unit
pivot, acyclic restoration and attained final degree checked on the actual
emitted source, not only on a symbolic model.
\item Can a joint search over constructor fusion, additive inlining, scale
expansion and graph aliases improve the height-one catalog? The search cost
is not final support, and individual changes can increase support. Every
candidate still needs the complete triangular pullbacks, occurrence coverage,
unit anchoring, integral energy and universal lift including the hub term.
\item Can the newer direct arithmetic fronts improve those height-one
networks? Their smaller original dimensions do not imply fewer aliases or
occurrences after normalization. Such a transfer needs a new measured graph,
not a relabelled inherited source theorem.
\item Can higher quotient centers reduce the positive scalar degree at a
reasonable gate cost? The key obligations are an exact division-free integer
polynomial evaluator, an all-sign pre-rank center bound, a transport valid
at the auxiliary index-three branch, and a strictly positive completeness
lift. Bare $b^2$ is not licensed by the proved $UEb^2$ inequality.
\item Can these ordinary compositions be given a complete formal-kernel
replay? This would require the actual transitive logical and arithmetic
source closure and explicit port bindings. Finite coefficient reconstruction,
mutation rejection and byte replay are useful prerequisites, not substitutes
for such a formalization.
\end{enumerate}
These are bounded research directions with named proof obligations. They are
not claims of achievable savings, exhaustive negative results, global lower
bounds or mathematical priority.
