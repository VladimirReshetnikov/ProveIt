\section{Selected results and what changed}
\label{sec:results}
The useful results are four distinct improvements, not a single ordering
of ``simplicity.'' Every coefficient, coordinate and arithmetic gate is
fixed in the supplied artifacts. All statements of equivalence use ordinary
unbounded proofs composed with the identified inherited logical calibration.

\subsection{Unrestricted integer quartics}
All four selected direct equations are sums of squares of quadratic
polynomials on $\Z$ and have exact degree four.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}\toprule
Endpoint & Variables & Rows & Terms & Height\\\midrule
Principal &210&150&5276&$2^{24}$\\
Dimension &209&149&5278&$2^{46}$\\
Balanced &211&151&5401&65537\\
Lower height &212&152&18381&5200\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The principal has the same dimension and row count as incoming Pell Growth
210, but six fewer collected terms and a height reduction from $2^{40}$ to
$2^{24}$. The 209-variable point is a dimension tradeoff, not a universal
improvement: its height is larger. The balanced 211-variable point improves
the incoming balanced 214-variable control's dimension, rows, support and
height. The 212-variable point does not beat the retained Round22 heights
4096 and 2304; those remain genuine alternatives.

The new arithmetic work replaces a positive quadratic horizon by a signed
affine horizon whose sign is recovered from an existing power request.
Threshold four needs new local-budget and odd-selector completeness
arguments. A separate, explicitly certified affine-atom frame and difference
orientation lower coefficients. Monic growth splitting then makes a finite
variable/height tradeoff. Appendix~\ref{sec:residuals} prints every residual
of the principal equation.

\subsection{Joint pooling and exact elimination}
The selected reciprocal equations also have unrestricted integer domains.
Their degree is not four.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}\toprule

Joint dimension &113&53&16&1030202\\
Sparse companion &114&54&16&127818\\
Lower degree &138&78&8&12099\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The two degree-16 heights are $16250018885420533678080$; the degree-eight
height is $2^{50}$. The million-term endpoint is not advertised as sparse.
The complete coefficients are supplied despite the compact proof/circuit
presentation. The new theorem avoids a premature sign assumption by using
one small integer sum, then a second integer sum whose nonzero summands have
a common sign. A retained-frame-row numerator change allows one further
monic elimination without increasing the selected degree.

The 138-variable point is an unfused monic-elimination control, not a saving
credited to the new joint theorem. These endpoints use the conservative
209-variable square-frame source,
not the weak-frame quartic source above. Appendix~\ref{sec:conservative-residuals}
prints that exact source, and the following finite ledger specifies the
pools and all ordered substitutions. The incoming Mixed Base 112-variable
point has degree 24; its smaller count does not make it a degree-16
comparison. Its 143-variable degree-eight point has a lower reported height
bound than the new 138-variable equation, so the latter is not a uniform
size improvement.

\subsection{Coefficient height one}
The new minimum has 484 variables and 24131 terms, versus the retained
493-variable, 24828-term point. At exactly 493 variables, the new support
is 17058, a reduction of 7770 terms, about 31.3\%. The new catalog also
includes $533/11985$, $600/8241$ and $696/7113$ variable/support tradeoffs.
Every endpoint has exact degree four, unrestricted integer coordinates,
constant one and all nonzero collected coefficients equal to $\pm1$.

The new operations fuse available constant and scale products into source
rows, prune unused monic constructors, inline low-use constants and expand
rare scales. Their exact source maps precede the unchanged nonnegative
network energy. All nine selected Round22 height-one endpoints are dominated
by members of the new finite catalog; no exhaustive frontier is claimed.

\subsection{Ten strictly positive inputs}
The complete new scalar equations have these exact live-circuit metrics:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrr@{}}\toprule

Scalar quotient center, $L=bV$ &10&202&99\\
Whole-product center, $L=UEb^2$ &10&190&120\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Both keep all four authentic Start-only 4127 numerical ports, totaling
97046 raw bytes. The new factor $UE$ makes the squared transport valid even
at the pre-rank auxiliary index-three case. Exact cancellation in the full
norm defect, followed by actual leading-form substitution, establishes the
degrees. The retained $214/87$ and incoming $206/90$ and $210/89$ points
remain lower-cost alternatives. None of these is a ten-unrestricted-integer
result.

The source snapshot, complete comparison inventory, proof boundaries and
rebuild commands appear later. In particular, a finite verification receipt
is evidence about the emitted algebra and bytes, not a proof that ZFC is
consistent or a replacement for an unbounded source theorem.
