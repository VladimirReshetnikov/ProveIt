# Attribution and scope

New material in this component was prepared with AI assistance for Vladimir
Reshetnikov and ProveIt on 25 October 2026. It follows the repository's MIT
No Attribution license, retained as `LICENSE`.

The literal parent is package 164's inverse-Lucas equation. Package 163
supplies the unsquared trace, signed auxiliary bounds, and the identified
open square-transport question. This component composes those results,
proves square transport and polynomial centers, and supplies new literal
circuits, exact algebra checks and degree proofs. The degree-220 quotient
center was developed during an independent internal mathematical review.

`polynomial.py` is retained from package 163 with its attribution. The finite
coefficient recipe is retained from package 140; its earlier lineage and
the native/K215 notices remain under `licenses/`. All inherited source
identities and canonical locations are recorded in `SOURCE_LEDGER.json`.

end-to-end proof-assistant formalization is claimed.
