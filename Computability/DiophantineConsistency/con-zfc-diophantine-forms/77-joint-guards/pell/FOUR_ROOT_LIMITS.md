# Four-root extension: two distinct unresolved obligations

The degree-277 and centered degree-257 eleven-positive constructions are
proved. The proposed deletion of a fourth coordinate is not a theorem.

1. The three-root rationality lemma does not extend by merely requiring
   four distinct positive radicands and no zero full signed sum. For example,

       sqrt(2)+sqrt(8)-sqrt(18)+sqrt(10000)=100.

   The first three irrational roots cancel. All four radicands are distinct,
   and every full signed sum is nonzero: it is an integer multiple of sqrt(2)
   plus or minus100. The last root is strictly larger than the sum of the
   first three. Thus even one globally dominant root does not suffice.
   A sufficient repair would exclude signed cancellation in every nonempty
   subset, for example by a fully superincreasing hierarchy, but such a
   repair still has to respect the literal residue interface.

2. Keeping the main-root residue D=X+ac modulo H while absorbing the first
   transport root multiplies its congruence by H in the naive merged
   carrier. It gives (q-1)|H Bt rather than (q-1)|Bt. Adjacent-Pell descent
   makes radical roots integral; it does not cancel this independent
   integer residue obstruction. The concrete control q=32,Y=q^3,X=192 gives
   H=4Y(X+1)+3=25296899=31*816029. For toy K0=F=C=1 and alpha=30,
   Bt=192 and 31 does not divide Bt, although31 divides H Bt.

Neither example is a zero or counterexample to a full consistency polynomial.
They refute precisely the tempting intermediate inference. No conjectured
endpoint or unsupported extra cofinal congruence is included in the frontier.
