# Attribution and verification scope

The new normalized-remainder transformation, its explicit 252-variable
quartic, proofs, and computational artifacts were developed with AI assistance
for Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt repository on 25 October 2026.

The C222 calculus, its refutation calibration, arithmetic/CRT compilation,
square-output factorial fixed point, cofinal witness choices, signed Pell
frame, semantic anchor, pooled orientation, strict amplification budget,
Horn factorization, integer monic projection, and centering machinery are
inherited. Their retained source files and notices are under `upstream/`.
The portable factorization, projection, translation, and centering modules
retain the parent implementation except for the centering helper import path.

`code/check.py` was written independently of the new builder. Its inherited
Horn factoring check uses a separately retained independent checker, not the
producing factorization module. Exact finite validation and the ordinary
unbounded proof are distinguished throughout. No global optimality,
literature-wide novelty, new complete formalization, or consistency proof is
asserted.
