# Attribution and proof boundaries

This is AI-assisted mathematical research prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's
ProveIt project. The direct C222 encoding, signed Pell arithmetic, logical
C222/ZF/ZFC calibration, and the general monic-projection and radical-projection
methods are inherited from the identified ProveIt developments.

New contributions in this release are the literal 92-variable degree-692
endpoint, its source-bound integral restoration and finite degree, occurrence,
and height certificates, the lower-degree literal contraction profiles, the
proved b-squared transport refinement, and the explicitly delimited supporting
arithmetic results. No global optimum or exhaustive literature-priority claim
is made.

The exact compact bV graph was found in a late incoming source. It is retained
for comparison and attributed in `compact/LATE_COMPARISON.json`; the 85-operation
degree-232 pair is not claimed as a new discovery. The historical radix47
coefficient specialization is not claimed to beat newer coefficient tuples.
The oriented-weight idea was already suggested in the reciprocal-pooling source;
the completed signed bounds and concrete implementation are the contribution
here.

The MIT No Attribution license follows the included source license. Original
archives and their notices are preserved under `inherited/`. Source identities
are recorded in `SOURCE_LEDGER.json` and component provenance ledgers. No claim
of external human peer review or newly compiled end-to-end proof-assistant
verification is made.
