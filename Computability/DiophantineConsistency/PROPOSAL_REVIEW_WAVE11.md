## Review scope

* [Positive review](PROPOSAL_REVIEW_WAVE11_POSITIVE.md): eight delivered
  literal circuits, actual whole compact44 ports and specialized degrees;
  26 selected normal/optimized author stages. Three-root ordinary descent
  justifies deleting q(q-1). The arriving101/78 circuit matches our
  independently reconstructed checkpoint. The report-only100/70 argument
  is now materialized, with its early rank proof and complete witness maps
  in [ordinary-unit-products](research/ordinary-unit-products/README.md).
* [Integer review](PROPOSAL_REVIEW_WAVE11_INTEGER.md): full-domain sign
  guards, compatible canonical witnesses, monic restorations, nonzero-scale
  main norm and centered auxiliary row. Selected joint and centered replays
  authenticated425 sealed files plus eight inherited ZIPs, passed28 stages
  and reproduced36 artifacts. Our subsequent
  [joint22-centered](candidates/joint22-centered/README.md) candidate proves
  an integer-polynomial isomorphism to its parent at119 operations.
* [Structural/direct review](PROPOSAL_REVIEW_WAVE11_STRUCTURAL.md): full
  structural release normal/optimized replay,30 commands and46 byte-identical
  rebuilt artifacts per mode. The Lucas theorem uses its required`|C|>1`
  hypothesis only after the full guard proves it. The separate budget/height
  constructions are ordinary source theorems; their local counts do not
  measure a finished global polynomial.
* [Nine-variable review](PROPOSAL_REVIEW_WAVE11_NINE.md): the radical/guard
  release passed all eight stages per mode and reproduced all170 members.
  The polynomial-part majorant and positive shifted defect certificates
  support an unbounded strict inequality. Our
  [D-register reduction](candidates/radical-d-shared/README.md) preserves
  the exact polynomial while reducing1,753 gates to1,065; an independent
  peer checked both rewrite stages over integer polynomials.

The report-only811-operation nine-variable claim is not accepted as a
checked point: its ZIP omits the referenced nine-variable section and literal
artifacts. Its separately supplied twelve-variable argument is unaffected.

## Domains, coefficients and alternate encodings

Positive11/12/13 forms here use the whole compact44 tuple. Integer guard
forms use source34's six-port determinant tuple. Radical-nine uses the
whole original31 tuple. Their fixed ports are not interchanged, and MF is
not shifted twice. Our separate [771-digit complete page](SEED_COMPRESSION54.md)
uses its own state-renamed compact54 tuple, with the changed K explicit.

Our [direct-flow source](candidates/direct-flow-large-carrier/README.md)
uses a bounded-factor carrier and shared quadratic beta equations, giving
47,439,839 representation witnesses before quartic flattening. The new
local20/50,21/20,23/14 height tradeoffs do not automatically improve that:
their literal nested beta constructors give roughly2.7trillion representation
witnesses at the same stage. These unmaterialized compiler outputs remain
separate from the finished small native equations.

The user's pointer to`Computability/HilbertTenthProblem` led to a specific
[strong-root elimination transfer](SIZE_RESEARCH_H10.md). Candidate local
counts are14 positive unknowns at73/342 or75/293, pending the fixed-blank
signed-positivity review and literal-construction check. They are not counted
as calibrated endpoints here. Frozen helper programs and scientific arrays
were read as inert metadata and were not executed.

The ordinary arguments target a biconditional provable in ZFC without
assuming consistency. Finite checks prove their stated algebra/data claims;
they do not supply full native calibration or an encoded object-ZFC
derivation. No work on Lean formalization resumed.
