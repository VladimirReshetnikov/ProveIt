# Independent review: restricted 73/75 positive-zero equivalence

`restricted_iff` quantifies an arbitrary supported IΣ₁ model and the same external natural port tuple. On the 75 side it requires, inside the existential for the very same positive zero tuple `v`, both `0≤shift p v` and `shift p v<ι(v 7)`. On the literal-73 side it requires, inside that side's existential for its positive zero tuple, `0≤shift p v`. The proof obtains a model-semiring representative `d` of the nonnegative shift and updates slot 7 to `v 7-d` or `v 7+d` respectively. It uses shift invariance under slot-7 update to carry the sign condition to the constructed witness and proves the reverse direction's strict bound from source positivity.

`check.py` checked the exact witness-side formulas, both explicit updates and the parent substitution, and rehashed the accepted source, direct imported sign provider, all recorded inputs, output/runner/logs and `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` axiom report. No independent Lean replay or enclosing-process exit observation was performed. Neither side's required sign/strict bound is established for arbitrary graph zeros by this theorem; it therefore does not prove an unrestricted positive-zero iff, an iPRA result, or formal JSON/PDF semantics. Historical project/package/dynamic ancestry remains inherited.
