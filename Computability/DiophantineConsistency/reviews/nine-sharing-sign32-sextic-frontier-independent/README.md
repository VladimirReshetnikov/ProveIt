`python3 -I -S -B check.py` reproduces `result.json`. This read-only check pins the v2 candidate and prior 33-seed and independently checks each of the 23 transformed ASTs, all metadata, variable indices, four compressed and raw coefficient hashes, byte lengths, positivity and bit lengths. The v2 coefficient path resolves from the candidate to the inherited original graph's coefficient directory. The earlier v1 had an inherited `coefficients/` path that did not resolve relative to the candidate; v1 remains a separate historical artifact. The `MF_source` bytes are the source high mask; correspondence to the model's effective high-plus-beta port is a separate inherited binding, not established by this check.

The removed q2 residual is `old[15] - q^2` where `q = beta * (old[1]+1)+1`. The remaining residuals replace old 15 by `q^2`, leave 0..14 fixed, and map old 16..33 to new 15..32. The displayed output is their sum of squares. All witnesses 1..32 occur; input coordinate 0 is unused. Each residual has witness degree at most 3; q3, tau and cTminusR attain syntactic degree 3. The q3 residual, after setting all but witness 1 to zero, is exactly `-(beta*(x1+1)+1)^3`. Its x1^3 coefficient is `-beta^3`. The x1^6 coefficient in the total sum is the sum of the squares of the x1^3 coefficients of all residuals, hence is positive for the fixed positive integer beta. Therefore the fixed-coefficient integer polynomial has exact witness degree 6. At zero witnesses its q3 residual is `-(beta+1)^3`, proving nonzero normalization.

The mathematical bridge remains to be formalized. In an IΣ₁ model, the new-to-old construction inserts the model-natural `q^2` at old coordinate 15; natural-to-ring cast homomorphism gives the displayed substituted expressions and the deleted residual vanishes. Conversely, zero old sum of squares forces the deleted residual to vanish in the ordered ring, and injectivity of the model-natural embedding recovers old coordinate 15 equal to model-natural `q^2`; projecting it gives the new zero. These implications require the usual ordered-ring sum-of-squares, cast and embedding hypotheses, and neither direction was compiled here. This is a 32-witness sextic *seed candidate*; the quartic Jones construction cannot simply be applied to it. No 32-witness iPRA endpoint, nine-variable endpoint, or global lower bound is claimed by this audit.
