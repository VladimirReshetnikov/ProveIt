# Independent review: warning inventory and 127-source plan

`check.py` verifies the saved warning inventory `reviews/ten-positive-80-warning-inventory/observations/attempt01.json`, its pinned input hashes, exact one-based stage order, and the chosen historical evidence for every one of the 127 planned modules. It also runs the earlier independent 127-source/hash/header/topological and drift-cone checks. The inventory is an account of **selected retained records**, not a prediction of fresh compilation diagnostics or authorization to suppress them.

The chosen evidence comprises 110 matched single-object receipts whose stdout/stderr bytes are individually rehashed and scanned for coded/uncoded warning/error markers; 5 nine-prefix case warning-policy fields compared to their original producer receipts; 7 adaptive author-record `warnings` fields; and 5 HA87 author-record `warning_lines` fields. All 127 names receive one of those categories; the 110 inspected single-object logs have no warning/error markers. The remaining 17 are **structured fields**, not a comprehensive independent inspection of every historical raw log. Missing/default-empty fields must not be interpreted as proof that no historical diagnostic or future warning exists; the selection precedence also does not enumerate every possible byte-matched producer record.

The inventory's reported nonempty policies correspond to the plan's stage 3 `ArithmeticPellPigeonhole` (three warnings), stage 5 `ModelArithmetic` (three), and stage 13 `ModelRatioBounds` (four). It makes no exception for stage 13. Actual v5 attempt03 has since terminated separately at stage 13 and is independently assessed in `reviews/ten-positive-80-rebuild-attempt03-independent/`.

The 127-source plan is source-hash and captured direct-header/topology evidence only. It is not a build, proof of unique historical origin, or a guarantee that all project/package ancestry or later warnings are known. No Lean replay was performed.
