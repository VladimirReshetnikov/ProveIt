# Scope audit of the proposed 86- and 87-operation iPRA endpoints

The [frozen endpoint source](ipra-adaptive-native-prefix-continuation/sources/DiophantineConsistency/IPRAAdaptiveNativeZFC.lean) states, for each of the two literals, that `nativeTheory` proves its no-zero sentence equivalent to `¬mappedNativeRefutation embedding`. These two declarations have no additional theorem hypotheses. Their intended composition uses the independently checked translated-refutation clients and bounded-matrix comparison, but the earlier combined source-directed build [failed](ipra-adaptive-native-zfc-failed.md) at a stale helper object. This audit of statements does **not** certify that combined build or assert a complete current-source closure.

## Equation and positivity

`noZero86` and `noZero87` universally close the inequality of the two natural parts of their literal expression over 13 natural coordinates. The exact literals are [AdaptiveDirectOrdinate86Literal.lean](../Lean/DiophantineConsistency/AdaptiveDirectOrdinate86Literal.lean) and [AdaptiveFirstRootFactor87Literal.lean](../Lean/DiophantineConsistency/AdaptiveFirstRootFactor87Literal.lean). Each defines its natural expression as `positiveShift` of the unshifted integer expression. The theorem `split_positiveShift` in [AdaptiveEquationSyntax2011.lean](../Lean/DiophantineConsistency/AdaptiveEquationSyntax2011.lean) proves the natural parts evaluate at each input plus one. Thus, in the standard naturals, the 13 quantified coordinates correspond to the 13 **strictly positive** unknowns; the statement is not a no-solution claim over arbitrary integers. The signed expression is represented by equality of its two natural parts, not by natural truncated subtraction.

The 86 literal fixes its four ports using `CurrentWindowCarry2011.ports`; [CurrentKernelData2011.lean](../Lean/DiophantineConsistency/CurrentKernelData2011.lean) and [CurrentMaskData2011.lean](../Lean/DiophantineConsistency/CurrentMaskData2011.lean) define these respectively as `beta`, `K`, `lowPort`, and `highPort + beta`. The same 13-variable positive shift applies to the 87 literal. The internal all-model change of ordinate for the two literals requires the sign, norm and fixed-port bounds; it is not a consequence of a formal signed substitution alone. See the candidate [README](../research/adaptive-direct-ordinate86/README.md) for those independently replayed snapshot qualifications.

On this audit, `python3 Computability/DiophantineConsistency/research/adaptive-direct-ordinate86/verify_equation86.py` passed: it checks 24 exact sparse-polynomial identities, the final factor wiring, the pinned TeX display block, all 86 parsed circuit rows against the pinned JSON, and byte-for-byte copying of the constant block from `equation.tex`. This is external Python evidence, not a Lean parser for the PDF or the TeX, and copying the constant block alone is not an independent proof that its decoder and every printed constant equal the Lean ports. The Lean literal, the candidate JSON, the textual display, and PDF rendering remain distinct artifacts with their respective existing checks. No separate 87-operation PDF is claimed by this audit. The degree certificates are likewise separate exact computational arguments, not Lean degree proofs or minimality proofs.

## Refutation and interpretation

[FoundationHAConsistencyNormalization.lean](../Lean/DiophantineConsistency/FoundationHAConsistencyNormalization.lean) defines `rho` to be `provabilityPred ZermeloFraenkelChoice ⊥` and proves `source_eq_neg_rho` by reflexivity. [IPRAFoundationZFCMappedNegation.lean](../Lean/DiophantineConsistency/IPRAFoundationZFCMappedNegation.lean) defines `mappedNativeRefutation embedding` as the native intuitionistic NNF reading of the *mapped positive* `rho`. The target is the intuitionistic negation of that reading. It must not be silently identified with the direct native reading of classical NNF negation. [IPRAEquationComposition.lean](../Lean/DiophantineConsistency/IPRAEquationComposition.lean) explicitly bridges the negated GG translation and translated source negation using intuitionistic double-negation logic; [IPRABoundedRhoZFC.lean](../Lean/DiophantineConsistency/IPRABoundedRhoZFC.lean) supplies the separate bounded-matrix comparison with no proof premise.

[FoundationZFCConsistencyNat.lean](../Lean/DiophantineConsistency/FoundationZFCConsistencyNat.lean) independently states a standard-natural semantic bridge from the Foundation source sentence to nonexistence of a refutation in its separately formalized `BProv` ZFC checker, using the validator and ZF/ZFC comparison. This is an external standard-model statement; the desired final wrapper is an object-theory derivability statement. Comparing other proof codings or identifying the explicitly defined candidate `nativeTheory` with any particular textbook presentation requires additional interpretation results. The successor-step schema conversions address one presentation difference only. Lean metatheory axioms reported for checks are not object-level classical-logic axioms of `nativeTheory`.
