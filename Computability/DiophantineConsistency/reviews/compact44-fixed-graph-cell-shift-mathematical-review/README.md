# Source-level mathematical review of the whole-cell shift

Reviewed commit `283232f28ea22a87169a52feb37743a12bfa20f6`, including all four new theorem sources and the key inherited statements `FixedGraphAlignedShift.graph_aligned_shift`, `FixedGraphAnchorParity.graph_anchor_parity`, `FixedGraphSupportCarry.graph_support_carry`, `FixedGraphTemporal.fixed_graph_temporal`, `TemporalSourceUnified.digit`, `TemporalCarryReset.clean`, `CyclicAnyPhase.cyc_period` and the `CleanGeometry.Bad` definition and bounded-standard-residue lift. The preceding alignment argument has a separate source review in `compact44-fixed-graph-aligned-shift-mathematical-review`. No witness mismatch, phase-case gap or bounded-quotient error was found in the inspected arguments. This is not an independent reconstruction of all transitive proofs.

The theorem assumes an `ORingStructure` model of `IΣ₁`, positive graph coordinates and `InternalZero` for the fixed literal symbolic ports with the distinct `paramKernel rho`. It concludes existence of `N,w,s`, retaining the original graph and indexed `b = 1` carry hypotheses, such that `s < N`, `E%67 = 0`, and `(E/67)%(8807*N) = 8807*s`, where `E = R - 67*(8807*N)` uses truncated subtraction. Its graph-witness premises also include `67*8807*N ≤ R`. It makes no existential assertion that such a graph zero exists.

Four graph theorems are invoked separately for alignment, anchor parity, support and source digit bounds. They have the same graph tuple, ports and `q`; injectivity of model exponentiation and cancellation of the positive constant prove equality of their `N`s before any facts are combined. The returned `w` and carry input are taken from the alignment witness. The facts taken from the other invocations depend on the now-common `N` and fixed tuple, not on their separately chosen `w`s; their equality is not assumed. The parity equation and carry reset apply to the same `C`, `E`, length, carry input and anchor after substitution.

From `E%67 = 0`, the unified arbitrary-model rotation formula reduces each temporal digit to the source digit at `cyc n t ((E/67)%n)`, with `n = 8807*N`. The source bound `≤3` bounds the predecessor temporal digit by 3 and hence by `2^66`; the coefficient-room inequality and `b = 1` carry input then force the anchor's incoming carry to zero. The anchor parity equation forces the target temporal digit, and hence its source digit, to be nonzero. The support theorem says digits outside `Bad` vanish, so both cyclic source phases lie in `Bad`.

`Bad` consists of phases `0..1065`, `1095`, `2195`, and `3296`. The cyclic-period lemma reduces the two anchors to the corresponding phases modulo 8807, including wrap and arbitrary model `N`. The model proof takes the remainder of the displacement modulo the standard numeral 8807, lifts that bounded model value to a standard natural, and applies the finite phase theorem. Independent enumeration in `check_cases.py` finds 1,069 bad shifts for each anchor and their intersection exactly `{0}`. This standard finite check supports the Lean argument; the bounded lift, not enumeration of a nonstandard word, is what makes it applicable in arbitrary models.

With `D = (E/67)%n`, the phase result gives `D%8807 = 0`, and positivity gives `D < 8807*N`. Division with remainder yields `D = 8807*(D/8807)` and the strict bound yields `D/8807 < N`; the proof chooses `s = D/8807`. In particular `s = 0` remains possible, and the conclusion bounds the reduced displacement rather than asserting `E/67 < 8807*N`.

The result does not establish a graph zero or contradiction, identify `s` as a valid semantic space/time stride, establish a global row decoder, bind the symbolic kernel or ports to retained equation bytes, prove machine correctness, or establish existence of an internal PRA proof. The recorded Lean runs are single-author exploratory builds with the listed classical Lean axioms; this mathematical source review runs no Lean and is neither an independently coherent provider replay nor a paired artifact audit.
