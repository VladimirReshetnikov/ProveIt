# Independent review: 59-witness affine-inlined quartic seed candidate

The pinned source graph is the current 14-variable 65-gate graph. An independent source checker reconstructs the complete graph and Lean `expressionAt` as canonical DAGs modulo associative/commutative multiplication and signed addition; it checks all fourteen indices, all live rows, port ordering and an output-sign mutation. This is an external source comparison, not a Lean graph theorem.

The seed checker separately reconstructs every one of the 65 signed-pair gate identities over a free commutative ring. It checks that all natural witness-construction components have nonnegative coefficients, every inline pair has degree at most one, every materialized pair has degree at most two, each recorded residual equals its intended gate equation, and all input/witness coordinates are distinct and live. The exact counts are 30 inlined gates, 34 materialized gates, 45 materialized-gate witnesses plus 14 shifted original witnesses, and 46 residuals. The distinguished coordinate 0 is unused, yielding 59 witnesses and 60 seed coordinates. Every residual has degree at most two, so the external sum of their squares has degree at most four.

With all 59 witnesses zero, the first residual is exactly `−(beta+1)^2`, independent of the distinguished input. The actual fixed beta bytes are positive, so that residual is nonzero and the sum of integer squares is nonzero. All four fixed-port gzip hashes, raw hashes, lengths and big-endian bit lengths were checked. The author's seed and source-binding checkers both reran byte-for-byte.

This is external finite graph/syntax and ring-identity evidence. It does not prove the gate construction or zero equivalence in all IΣ₁ models, the quartic degree or normalization in Lean, instantiate Jones, or establish a new nine-variable iPRA endpoint. It also does not prove file/PDF semantics or give a final nine-variable graph or degree. No Lean replay was performed.
