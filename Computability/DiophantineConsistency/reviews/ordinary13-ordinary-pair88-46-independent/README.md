# Independent review: ordinary free-J 88/46 and P73-parameterized 90/46

Both source-only graphs pass review. The 88-gate graph is an exact edit of package 115's 87/51 free-J graph: it factors the auxiliary difference of squares and replaces the four-factor finalizer by `A0²+A1²+(Fa*Fs−1)²`. All 88 operations, 13 coordinates and four original fixed ports are live; there is no new fixed leaf. The separate P73-parameterized graph has 90 live operations. Exact sparse-polynomial checks compare its A0, A1, Fa and Fs against the existing **ordinary-unit** P73-parameterized graph; this is not a pointwise comparison with the normalized-strong P73 graph or with free J.

The free-J zero equivalence is global over integer inputs and integer ports. Because q and qm are consecutive, `M=q³*qm` is even, so `1+A0` cannot be −1 modulo 4. Since `Delta=(a+2)²−1` is 0 or 3 modulo 4, `1+A1` also cannot be −1. The product `Q=Delta*(f²−1)` is 0 or 1 modulo 4, so Fa is congruent to a square and `Fs=1+I²−Q` is 0, 1 or 2; neither is −1. An integer product equal to 1 has only unit factors, hence all four original factors equal 1. Conversely, a zero of the new sum of squares forces A0=A1=0 and Fa*Fs=1, and the same sign exclusions force both tail factors to 1. Neither direction assumes tail factors nonzero before the product equation.

The 88- and 90-gate graphs have syntactic degree upper bound 46: the factored gap has degree at most 7, Fa at most 13, and Fs at most 10. Authenticated P72 compressed and little-endian raw port bytes match the pinned P115 binding hashes. Independently evaluating each full graph on a weighted affine ray gives nonzero degree-46 leading coefficient modulo 1,000,000,007, establishing exact specialized degree 46. The author receipt reproduces byte for byte.

The 88/46 result is one operation more and five degrees lower than its 87/51 parent, and improves the inspected 90/50 count/degree record. The 91/30 point remains a different tradeoff. These are named-record comparisons, not a global frontier.
