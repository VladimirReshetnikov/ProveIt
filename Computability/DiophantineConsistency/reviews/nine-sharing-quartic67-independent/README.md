# Independent review: 110-witness sharing-aware quartic seed

The candidate faithfully describes the pinned 67-gate current2011 graph. This independent checker reconstructs every gate's signed pair in a free commutative polynomial ring, verifies each submitted residual exactly, reclassifies syntactically nonnegative gates from positive inputs and verified fixed ports, and checks all coordinates and constraints. The 66 internal gates require 37 single and 29 paired witnesses; with fourteen shifted input witnesses and one guard this gives 110 quartic witnesses and 111 total seed coordinates. The guard, 95 internal constraints and final output constraint give 97 residuals.

Every residual has syntactic witness-degree at most two, so the external sum-of-squares seed has degree at most four. The distinguished coordinate 0 is unused. When all 110 witnesses are zero, the explicit guard residual is −1, so its square alone makes the integer seed nonzero. Both current graph ports and their positive, big-endian decoded byte values are separately checked against their pinned gzip/raw hashes. The author's checker also reproduced its result byte-for-byte.

This establishes external universal gate identities and the candidate's structural counts. It does not prove the zero-set construction in every IΣ₁ model, formalize its degree or normalization in Lean, instantiate the Jones theorem, provide a new iPRA endpoint, or identify a final nine-variable graph/degree. The candidate uses the 67/94 graph, not the original 65-operation displayed graph. No Lean replay was performed.
