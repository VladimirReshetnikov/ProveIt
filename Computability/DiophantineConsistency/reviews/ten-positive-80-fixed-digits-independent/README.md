# Independent review: fixed 80-graph digits, slots, and V positivity

`graph_fixed_digits` and `graph_fixed_slots` each assume an actual positive zero of the exact Lean 80-row graph and the three symbolic port equalities `p₀=betaNat`, `p₂=lowNat`, and `p₃=betaNat+highNat`; `p₁` remains arbitrary. Each takes its N from the fixed saturation result and retains that same N in the repunit/radix and low-mask conclusions. Natural transport is obtained independently for the same graph input and deterministic `C=q−F−alpha`; the digits theorem also returns its natural transport witness and equation. It does not identify the unrelated existential transport witness with the length N.

The digits theorem invokes the selected `CompactConcreteIncrement.recipe_digits` object on the same N, C and low-mask no-carry statement, giving origin digit 1 and digits at most 3 for all slots below `8807*N`; it retains the high-mask no-carry statement. The slots theorem separately invokes its locally reproduced `increment_digits` proof on the same N, C and mask. Comparing theorem text through the end of that proof with the historical `CompactPositionCarry.lean` shows an exact byte-for-byte match after stripping surrounding whitespace; the following historical documentation comment is outside the theorem. The local theorem is compiled in the selected slots object, not imported from the historical object. Its conclusion is slotwise no-carry for nonzero slots below `8807*N` plus the origin digit and high-mask no-carry; it does not establish a complete decoder or machine theorem.

`graph_v_positive` proves that the actual graph's signed V expression is positive in the model difference ring. Its explicit assumptions are beta positive, the MC/MF port inequalities, positive graph variables and zero output; it does not assume odd beta. The proof uses the graph's rank window and `2q⁴<c` to derive natural rank `<c`, obtains `c*T−rank≥1` by discreteness, and combines the actual graph identity `V=F²*(c*T−rank)−c` with `F²>c+1`. This is a necessary model-level condition, not a phase, halt, converse, iPRA, or external-payload theorem.

The captured `check.py` verifies five selected successful producer receipts, current source/runner/Lean and object hashes, selected predecessor pins, direct import resolution and matching producer objects, diagnostics, axiom reports and sampled resources. It explicitly checks that the selected `CompactConcreteIncrement` and `CompactMixedIncrement` direct objects occur in their runners' pinned inputs. Eleven terminal histories are inventoried with archived source/log hashes; slots attempts01–02 and V-positive attempt01 remain failures. Paths and hashes are in `observations/attempt01/stdout`.

This read-only audit does not replay Lean or reconstruct the complete transitive serialized, package/dynamic and historical source-to-object ancestry. Lean axiom reports are metatheory dependencies, not object-theory iPRA axioms. Resource checks are sampled, and external graph/PDF or byte binding is separate.
