`ModelDigitTransfer` as stages 18–20. The warning-bearing first module was
retried against its source-bound complete warning baseline. The retained
receipts, source snapshots, dependency listings, logs, rejected attempt and
baseline are under `author/`; output objects remain in the live temporary
overlay. `capture.py` checked their hashes when retaining the packet.

This is a point-in-time author observation, not an independent artifact audit,
paired replay, authenticated transitive closure, or proof of either endpoint.
