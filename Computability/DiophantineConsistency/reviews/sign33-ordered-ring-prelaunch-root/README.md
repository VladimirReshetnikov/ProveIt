# Independent prelaunch review: sign33 ordered-ring implications

The client copies the ordered-ring section of the independently reviewed
sign33 draft and retains its explicit hypotheses. The inequalities proposed
for the gap, tau, inner difference, V and auxiliary squared difference follow
mathematically from those hypotheses. In particular the V implication uses
both the half-window bound and `f ≥ 2`; no claim is made here that model
solutions satisfy those assumptions or that natural witnesses exist.

The runner checks the copied section, pins its source, tool and 60 direct
candidate slots, requires the scanned dependency paths to match those pins,
and repeats the point guards after successful compilation. It does not check
all transitive or dynamic inputs, and its resource observations are sampled.
The independent checker reruns the no-Lean preflight; elaboration, imports,
resource fit and axiom reports remain untested. No Lean was run in this
review, and no sign33 bridge or iPRA endpoint follows.
