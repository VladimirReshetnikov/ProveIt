The accepted frozen packets establish the **guarded** model-level equivalence stated in their Lean sources. `check.py` independently rehashes source snapshots, runners, all recorded direct input pins, Lean binary, output objects and captured logs, and resolves the selected direct and key overlay modules in the recorded search paths. For any `ORingStructure V` model of IΣ₁ and any `p : Fin 4 → ℕ`, with no separate positivity hypothesis on `p`, `Trace74GuardedIff.guarded_positive_zero_iff` equates:

* existence of a tuple `v : Fin 10 → V` whose every coordinate is positive, with **old-chart** guard `v 1 + v 2 < q p v`, and whose `TenPositiveTrace75.value75` equals zero in `DiffRing V` after casting ports to that ring and applying `ι` to coordinates; and
* existence of a positive tuple with its **new-chart** guard `v 1 + v 2 < q p v`, and zero of `Trace74Mask.value74` in the same difference ring under the same casts.

`q p v = (p 0 : V) * v 0 + 1`. Each direction uses `flip p v`, preserving the other nine coordinates and replacing slot 2 by the model's truncated subtraction `q p v − v 1 − v 2`. A guard makes that coordinate positive and justifies its cast to ring subtraction; original positivity gives the flipped tuple its corresponding strict guard. The code proves flip involutive under the bound, then applies the previously checked all-ring coordinate identity. The one-way `Trace74Guarded.guarded_to_old` assumes a positive guarded new tuple with its difference-ring 74 zero, and produces a positive old tuple with a difference-ring 75 zero; the iff wrapper separately derives the old-chart guard.

The guards are not consequences of arbitrary positive zeros in these packets. The theorem is about the Lean-defined values and their difference-ring evaluations, not a theorem that parses the external JSON, specializes the four file coefficients, derives a halting equivalence, or constructs an iPRA proof. The direct-BFS tuple and the distinct original-order symbolic kernel must not be conflated.
