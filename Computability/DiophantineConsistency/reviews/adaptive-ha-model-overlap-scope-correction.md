# HA/model overlap scan: symlink scope correction

The author and independent `audit_ha_model_overlap.py` runs used `Path.rglob('*.olean')`. That traversal does not follow symlinked directories. In particular, the generic HA assembler overlay has symlinked BusyBeaver, Foundation, FirstOrder, ZFCinPA, PAHF, BoundedPAConsistency and BoundedZFCConsistency namespace directories. The reported 463 and 687 are counts of objects actually traversed, and the 119 common paths with 118 different hashes are valid recorded observations **only for that traversed subset**. They are not a complete project-object inventory or a complete difference count.

The original author and peer state files, `result.json`, provider paths, hashes and full evidence remain unchanged. The author and independent receipts now carry this explicit qualification. A separate symlink-aware, dependency-directed source-closure audit is required before a unified HA/model provider is selected. A preliminary independent source-closure analysis has found additional differing BusyBeaver, FirstOrder and PAHF objects, but its full evidence and provenance require separate review. Unequal object bytes do not by themselves prove semantic inequivalence.
