Given these, it constructs the **same** `N,s` with `0<N`, the stated radix/size and carry data, `E%67=0`, `s<N`, `(E/67)%n=8807*s`, and:

* For all standard `u<10` and internal `k<n` with `k%8807=1013+u`, equality of digits at cyclic offsets 30 and `(E/67)%n`.
* For all standard `u<9` and internal `k<n` with `k%8807=1065+u`, equality of digits at offsets 62 and 8849.

Here `n=8807*N` and `E=rankNat p v-67*n`. The proof reconciles independently obtained N witnesses by equality of exponentials; it adds marker cases 1022 and 1073 to the existing ordinary field parity families.
