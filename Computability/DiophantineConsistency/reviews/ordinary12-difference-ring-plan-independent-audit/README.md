The import survey parses source headers; Lean's actual dependency resolution, any new bridge closure and package-transitive or dynamic loads remain to be checked by a build.

The historical 62 and 27 artifact totals independently recompute to 42,965,976 and 19,274,200 bytes from authenticated objects and logs. The plan's earlier free-space figure and seven-object total are recorded point-in-time observations; the seven object identities and a contemporaneous filesystem measurement are not preserved in the plan, so this audit does not independently reproduce those two numbers. The independent receipt records a later free-space measurement, which can change at any time. Source sizes are not reliable upper bounds on compiler memory, temporary files, or object sizes.

The requirement to compile the complete frozen project closure into two empty sibling trees under one authenticated provider is necessary: no old project objects or previously built Free-J objects should be reused across the NaturalExpression generation change. Before use, a new driver must bind exact source and configuration bytes, compiler, package and provider inputs; check actual resolved direct dependencies, source shadows and generated companions; preserve real process exits and axiom reports; and subject the completed artifacts to independent review. Provider wrapper exit, package-transitive ancestry and dynamic loads remain qualified unless separately observed and authenticated.

The revised plan describes 512 MiB per sibling as a planning budget and 4 GiB as a **sampled** stop threshold. It does not claim a continuous guarantee. At the independent survey's later measurement, `/tmp` had 6,085,373,952 bytes free: 1,790,406,656 bytes above 4 GiB. If two siblings actually used 512 MiB each, that would leave 716,664,832 bytes above the threshold for every other write and uncounted temporary file. These calculations are point-in-time estimates, not enforced allocations; free space may already have changed.

There is no measured source-current build size or runtime, no tested production stopping driver, and no bound on compiler temporaries or concurrent writers. A compiler can write past the sampled threshold before the next observation; a signal sent only to a parent can also leave children writing. The proposed implementation needs to account for logs and subprocess temporary locations, sample during compilation, terminate and reap the complete process group on a failed sample or other guard, preserve partial evidence, and be tested for threshold crossing and interruption. These are concrete unverified behaviors, not evidence that a run must exhaust the available space. An enforceable quota or isolated filesystem would provide a stronger guarantee, but is not claimed by this sampled plan. The single 1 MiB namespace probe does not test a 512 MiB build sandbox, complete write confinement, or host-free-space protection.

Subject to implementing and reviewing that stop behavior, authenticating actual Lean dependencies and the complete source-current closure, and remeasuring space immediately before a run, the survey identifies no demonstrated mathematical or dependency contradiction that makes the proposed approach impossible. It also does not show that a build will fit or complete within the observed headroom.

This review verifies a source and planning survey only. It does not authorize or demonstrate a build, establish the difference-ring bridge or ordinary integrality, recover the strong norm, interpret compact44 ports, or prove an internal iPRA derivation.
