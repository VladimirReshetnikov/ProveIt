# Independent review of P73 91/30 and 90/50 circuits

The two primary external graphs pass review. Their 13 positive inputs and four fixed integer ports match the P73 graph, no fixed leaf is added, and all operations and inputs are live. The 91-gate graph computes exactly the parent polynomial: `(1+k*qm)*q³=q³+k*(q³*qm)` in any commutative ring, and the old W node has no other consumer. The 90-gate graph has the same positive-input zero set at each valuation.

The actual transformed subgraphs were separately evaluated as sparse integer polynomials over common symbolic cuts. They satisfy `As_new=Delta*As` and `Aa_new=Aa+Delta*(y²−V²)*As`; the four-square output and the unchanged carrier rows were checked. Since `Delta=(a+2)²−1>0` for positive inputs, the strong residuals vanish together, at which point the auxiliary residuals agree. Four integer squares sum to zero exactly when all four residuals vanish. The same check applies to the 91-gate P73 and 92-gate P61 intermediate transfers.

Both 50-degree P73 graphs and the P61 intermediate have syntactic upper degree 50. For the exact-polynomial 91-gate graph, the verified cancellation `A0=d²+M*ell*(2*d−M*k)−M²` bounds A0 by 15, while `y²−V²=u*(y+V)` bounds Aa by 13; the other residuals are at most 14. Thus the full degree is at most 30. The complete graphs were independently evaluated on a weighted affine ray, distinct from the author's all-t ray, using the authenticated compact44 port bytes modulo 1,000,000,007. Nonzero leading coefficients establish exact degrees 30 and 50 respectively. The author receipt also reproduces byte for byte.
