# Foundation derivationOf: concrete first-index binding

`derivationOf_sigma_shape` identifies the exact first-index occurrence in the
sigma presentation of Foundation's `derivationOf T`, for every theory with
its stated Δ₁ axiom representation. `fstIdx_inline_succ` bounds that actual
argument-permuted graph in every IOpen model by `d+1`.
`derivationOf_bnd_of_local` and its candidate-model wrapper bind the same
local component to the exact bounded `derivationOf` formula. They explicitly
assume the separate bounded derivation/fixpoint conjunct and a common bound
at least `d+1`.

`candidate_proves_derivationBoundSentence` is an actual candidate-PRA proof
of the universally closed conditional implication. It does not construct the
bounded fixpoint conjunct, the full refutation bound certificate or the
PRA-equivalence with Foundation's literal ZFC consistency sentence. Foundation
and Mathlib remain inherited; no IΣ₁ assumption is made of candidate-PRA models.

The author build `/tmp/pra-fstidx-derivation-bound-qqxa5_ww` passed both
source/check modules and final authentication on the post-five-induction
35-module author provider `/tmp/pra-after-five-induction-refresh-j0iq7uvk/lib`.
The inputs pin the accepted local parent-manifest source/object graph plus the
exact inherited Foundation HFS Basic and Bootstrapping Proof Basic source/object
pairs. Hashing those Foundation files does not authenticate their full
transitive source closure. `author/` retains source snapshots, full logs and the
terminal state. The check reports only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and
`Quot.sound` as Lean axioms.

its entire recorded local source/object graph and the two stated Foundation
pairs before and after, and compares all client source/object/full-log hashes
against the author. Each replay retains its own parent-state hash and commands.
