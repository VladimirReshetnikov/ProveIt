`PowerRepresentation`, `RemainderRepresentation`, `FactorialRepresentation`
and `BoundedForallRepresentation` as stages 36–40. Their retained receipts,

objects remain in the live temporary overlay. `capture.py` checked their hashes

paired replay, authenticated transitive closure, or proof of either endpoint.
