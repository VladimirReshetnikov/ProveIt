# Independent source review: signed parent quotient facts

The signed pair represents `M(P+r)+bY−WQ*Bt` with Bt=Bp−Bn. The q³ congruence and Bezout cancellation yield q³|Y; `WQ=1+bL` and the b congruence yield b dividing the magnitude of Bt. Positive b, Y and radicand give positive quotient and root. Both signs of Bt remain allowed; neither its positivity nor a positive guard quotient is proved here.

The checker constructs nonvacuous scaled-square cases with both Bp>Bn and Bn>Bp, pins the prior independent quotient review, and replays the author receipt. It does not rerun that prior review, elaborate Lean or bind these facts to a graph. Run `python3 -I -S -B check.py` and compare with `result.json`.
