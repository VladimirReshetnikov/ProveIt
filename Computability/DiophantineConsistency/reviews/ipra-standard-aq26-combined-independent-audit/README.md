It checks that the recorded addition outputs occupy a single overlay and that the additions are ordered after their dependencies. Exact source statements are checked for the AQ28, AQ27 and AQ26 native endpoints, the standard/native derivability equivalence, and the three AQ26 standard endpoints. The latter concern derivability of the positive-shifted literal's no-zero statement versus the mapped native refutation predicate and the Gödel–Gentzen translation of the selected Foundation consistency sentence; they do not assert derivability of the untranslated classical sentence.

This evidence concerns one historical author-only trial extending the audited AQ29 author provider. The inherited Foundation build wrapper's outer exit remains unobserved. Lean metatheory axioms in diagnostic output should not be confused with axioms of the object theory.
