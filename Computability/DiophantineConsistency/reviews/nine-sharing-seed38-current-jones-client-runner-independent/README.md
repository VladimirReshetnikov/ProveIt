# Independent prelaunch review of the current-port Jones client

No material launch blocker was found. The pinned client specializes the
checked 38-seed degree and normalization statements to current ports, proves
the first port positive, and proposes `NineFreshJones.nine_iff` at input 1.
Its exact statement is the nine-variable Jones expression's zero equivalence
with existence of a 38-witness seed zero; it does not assert that this seed
matches the maintained equation or an iPRA endpoint. The proposed beta proof
and new client have not been checked by Lean.

The runner checks the ten-stage weighted-retry source/object/log records,
direct-provider guards and inherited overlays, then installs those ten objects
as a fresh overlay and captures the new client's direct imports and guards.
The independent `check.py` confirms all ten recorded source/object hashes and
no output-name collisions. An independent no-Lean preflight was run and
returned `PASS_NO_LEAN_PREFLIGHT`; no Lean compile was launched by this review.

Provider checks are point observations, not a complete package-transitive or
dynamic-load audit. The sampled session and host memory limits and process
scan are not atomic guarantees. A later successful compile and actual terminal
receipt are needed to establish the client theorem.
