## Signed-T proof: hypotheses challenged

1. **No unlicensed domain widening.** `SIGNED_DOMAIN_29.md` states an
   existence theorem with odd i and square w. It cannot simply be invoked
   for arbitrary positive i,w. The new proof instead repeats its soundness
   steps: only `i!=0` and `w>0` are needed, and the retained supplied
   i,w are positive. Its normalized rank proof uses
   `psi_A(m)=ic²`, not an ordinary strong equation requiring an additional
   relaxed-rank hypothesis. Odd-i and square-w restrictions were used by
   the old completeness construction, which is not imported here.

2. **The C=0 boundary is real.** Weak transport only gives C>=0. Its
   remaining term is `1-F-(z-1)(q-1)<=0`, while K0+w>=2 excludes C<0.
   At C=0, the tuple `F=2,z=1,alpha=q-2` can indeed have Nt=-1.
   The proof retains this possibility while using the corrected bounds
   `R<q⁴-2`, E>R+2 and a>R+2. It does not use the stronger historical
   margin `R<q⁴-q³`.

3. **Index recovery precedes auxiliary positivity.** The first/main Pell
   equations and positive h give `p>n>=(R-1)/2>=24`, `c>2p,2R`.
   The normalized strong recurrence proves `pc|m` and f>2c without
   T>0. The signed auxiliary norm then recovers p=R using absolute roots
   and the two source congruences, initially squaring only the f congruence.
   The reduction `V=-R (mod c)` uses the already recovered strong equation
   `f²=1 (mod c)`. This is available at that point.

4. **No wrap and C>0 are proved in order.** Since n<p=R and E>R+2,
   the wrap multiplier vanishes. The epsilon=-1 branch contradicts
   `kY<c<k(Y+1)` by Pell duplication. Thus Nk=Nt=+1 and only now is
   C=0 excluded. The actual R is odd before any compiler mask is decoded.

5. **Power/half-binomial extraction does not assume R=3 modulo4.** With
   r=(R-1)/2, the lower ratio uses only positive X,Y and the odd index.
   It yields `Y>X^r/3` and `a>X^(r+1)/3`. The main-root projection and
   strict range then force X=2^R. The upper ratio gives
   `0<c/k-xi/2<16r/(X+1)<1/2`. Expanding xi=M+theta has even M and
   `0<theta<1/4`, so Y=M/2. The valuation yields
   `popcount(R)>=3t+2` for q=2^t. No sign of V,T or input-index
   equation enters these inequalities.

6. **The low word is actual C, not an omitted input port.** The exact
   shifted-source identity is
   `R=(q²-Sword)(q²-1)+Tword` with
   `Sword=(C-1)+qF` and
   `Tword=(MC J+1)+q(MF_native J-1)`.
   It follows directly using beta J=q-1 and MF_source=MF_native+beta.
   The independent check verifies this identity. C,F>0 and C+F<q give
   `1<=Sword<q²`; the actual mask parities give
   `0<Tword<q²-1` and population t+2. Equality in inverse population
   forces `(C-1) AND (MC J+1)=0`. As MC J+1 is three modulo four,
   C=1 modulo four, and the literal R is C+MC J=3 modulo four.
   No input norm, input marker, five-power dimension assumption or
   accepting history was used to get this parity.

7. **Both unsquared congruences are necessary and supplied.** The stronger
   plus-sign step-down gives ell=epsilon R+2m z0. The recurrence gives
   `psi_A(ell)=epsilon(-1)^z0 c (mod f)`. Keeping the sign e=sign(V),
   the c and f congruences yield respectively
   `e(-1)^(r+m z0)R=-R (mod c)` and
   `e(-1)^(r+(m+1)z0)c=-c (mod f)`.
   The bounds c>2R,f>2c make both signs exactly -1. Their quotient
   forces z0 even regardless of m's parity. Hence e(-1)^r=-1;
   R=3 modulo four gives e=+1. Thus V>0 and then T>0.
   Using just the squared f congruence would not close this step.

This proves signed-T positivity on the arithmetic port contract before
applying any positive compiler theorem. A useful corollary permits signed

## Algebraic inverse and completeness

The new strong radicand D is an integer polynomial,
`D=1+(ic²)S=1+Delta i²c⁴`, rather than a quotient of computed ports.
It satisfies D>c>0 before imposing a zero. For every integer R,
`b=c+RD` is positive if R>=0 and negative if R<=-1, so it never vanishes.
Thus `Brat=2Q(cT)b` is nonzero on the retained positive domain without
assuming R>0 or compiler soundness.

The independent algebra audit verifies both literal tail outputs, the exact
auxiliary remainder identity, and their complete-polynomial relationship:

```text
Na-1=Arat-Brat*f+(f²-D)qaux,
F73=P*F75+(P-1)(2P*Arat-1).
```

At a field73 zero, P!=0 and the displayed rational inverse has square D,
so it is an integer. Substitution gives P*Na=1; since Q is a square,
Na cannot be -1 modulo four, hence P=Na=1 and the parent is restored.
At a unit75 zero, the guard `Arat²-D Brat²+1` is one or two modulo four,
so it must be +1. Thus P=1 and the same integral inverse restores the
parent. D>0 excludes f=0. The signed-T theorem then forces f>0 since
retained T>0. No extra divisibility witness is assumed.

Conversely, at every positive parent zero the normalized strong factor
is +1 and Na=1, P=1 by the modulo-four unit arguments. Then f²=D and
Arat=Brat*f, so both new outputs vanish. This proves exact positive-zero
projection and uniqueness of the restored root. It preserves every other
coordinate and the whole fixed tuple, so original positive completeness
transfers without an extra congruence, padding choice or native completion.
