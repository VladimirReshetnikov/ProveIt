# A direct positive base coordinate and a delayed divisibility carrier

This ordinary arithmetic ten-positive endpoint has a complete107-operation
evaluator and exact degree340. No accepted endpoint, page,
common document, incoming package or Lean file is edited. The construction
is a cost/degree tradeoff against101/368, not a replacement claim. It does
not supply a new Lean or encoded object-ZFC derivation.

## 1. The polynomial definitions

Retain the frozen ten-positive-short-projection definitions and coherent
four fixed ports, except replace its independent input w by x>0 and put

    X=q+x,
    B=(q*K0+X)*C-q*F,
    D=q*(q-1), W=1+D*L, P=q^4*(q-1), M=P*mu^2*H,
    Z=P*mu^2*[H*(n+V)+Kpos]
          +H*[mu^2*(q*(q-1)*Y-q^3*W*B-(q-1)*X)-P*e].      (1)

The only unknowns remain ten positive integers:

    J,F,alpha,i,T,x,Y,n,eta,zeta.

Here q=beta*J+1, C=q-F-alpha, I=i*c^2, L=HI+b and
Kpos=X(3c-2X)+2. Every other kernel definition is unchanged. In particular
A=mu*(Y(X+1)+2)+1 is the full main Pell parameter, H=4A-5,
mu=q^2+1, V0=X*mu^2*Y^2 and the root weights are3,1,1.
The equation is the same division-free octic

    F8(delta*Z^2;9*delta*M^2*Dtau,delta*M^2*Df,M^2*Dnum)=0.

The exact normalized center now is

    Z/M=n+V+Kpos/H+Y/q^3-W*B/[q(q-1)]-X/q^4-e/mu^2.       (2)

All denominators here are proof notation, not evaluation operations.
The port box remains beta odd,beta>=31,K0>0 and0<MC<beta<MF<2beta.
Thus q>=32 and X=q+x>q. No integrality of X/q is assumed.

## 2. Root extraction, rank and the transport sign

All unconditional kernel bounds used by the old proof remain valid,
since X>q>=32. The cleared-modulus(3tau,f,y) noncancellation argument,
integer-square and adjacent-coefficient descents recover integral roots.
The all-index main fold gives c=psi_A(p) with odd p. The first norm then
excludes n0=1 and yields

    c>8Xmu^2Y^2>=8q^5.

Consequently b>0,V>0,0<y-V<b/2, and the still rational
rho=e/mu^2 satisfies |rho|<b/2. The old weighted bound is

    3tau+f<(2A-1)I.

Also A>X+2 and I>=1, so X/q^4<X<(2A-4)I. Therefore

    3tau+f+X/q^4<HI.                                      (3)

The new signed-center relation from(2) is

    n=eps1*3tau+eps2*f+(eps3*y-V)-Kpos/H-Y/q^3
          +W*B/[q(q-1)]+X/q^4+rho.                        (4)

At this point c>2X already gives Kpos>0. If B<=-1, then L>0 and
W*B/[q(q-1)]<-L because W=1+q(q-1)L. Equations(3),(4) give
n<HI+b-L=0, contradiction. Hence B>=0. Since qK0+X>0 and F>0,
this forces C>0 and the old outer-mask bounds

    3q<R<q^4-2.

The normalized strong rank and signed auxiliary-index argument give p=R.
Odd p forces q even exactly as before. No step has divided X by q or
used the integrality of rho.

## 3. Primitive denominators and the first index

Only now use

    gcd(mu^2,q^4*(q-1)*H)=1.

The q/q-1 coprimality with mu is exactly the old q-even calculation,
and H=-1 modulo mu. Every other fraction in(2) has denominator dividing
q^4*(q-1)*H. Since the weighted roots and all other integer terms are
integral, partial fractions give mu^2|e. There is no assumption that q,
q^3 and q^4 are pairwise coprime; only the single modulus mu^2 is
separated from their common denominator.

The unchanged first Pell congruence, mu^2|4V0 and mu^2>q^4 now give
n0=(R+1)/2. Put R=2r+1. The old ratio estimates give

    r>=48, X^(r+1)<3a<H.

All first-index and ratio hypotheses have their original order; no
whole-product or q-divisibility conclusion has been used yet.

## 4. A height bound for the enlarged carrier

The integral signed relation implies H|P*Kpos with P=q^4(q-1).
Since H is odd and Kpos=HXc-2Kquad, this implies H|P*Kquad.
The retained projection identity makes H divide

    P*(X-2^R)*(2^R*X+1).                                  (5)

Here is the needed larger-carrier height bound, uniformly rather than
asymptotically. If X>=q>=32,R=2r+1 and r>=35, then

    X^(r-1)>=q^5*32^(r-6)=q^5*2^(5r-30)
                  >q^5*2^(4r+4)=q^5*2^(2R+2),

because5r-30>4r+4. Thus

    q^5*2^(2R+2)*X^2<X^(r+1)<H.

Our r>=48 satisfies the lemma. Since P<q^5, the absolute value in(5)
is strictly less than H. Its vanishing forces X=2^R; the other factor
is positive. This does not cancel P through H. The ordinary projection
identity now gives H|Kquad and H|Kpos directly, without P, and therefore
kappa_pos=Kpos/H is an integer.

## 5. Recover q|X only after the projection

With rho and kappa_pos integral, subtract all integer terms in(2):

    Y/q^3-W*B/[q(q-1)]-X/q^4 is an integer.                 (6)

Multiplying by P gives

    q(q-1)Y-q^3WB-(q-1)X is a multiple of q^4(q-1).

Reduction modulo q proves q|X. The H denominator has already been
removed by Section4; this argument does not illicitly cancel H modulo q.
Since X=2^R and q>0, q=2^kappa for some kappa>=5. The elementary bound
4kappa<=2^kappa=q (kappa>=4), together with R>3q, gives R>4kappa.
Consequently

    q^4|X and X/q^4 is a positive integer.                 (7)

Now set the proof abbreviation w=X/q>0. Then

    B=q*Bt, Bt=(K0+w)C-F, B/[q(q-1)]=Bt/(q-1).

Subtract the integer X/q^4 in(6). Coprimality of q^3 and q-1 gives
q^3|Y and q-1|W*Bt. Since W=1 modulo q-1, q-1|Bt. The quotient is
nonnegative because B>=0. The fixed-minus parity and half-binomial
rounding arguments remain unchanged, as their kernels have the same
X,Y,R. Fresh unscaled source61 witnesses use this actual positive
integer w and z=1+Bt/(q-1). They give the same finite-refutation
soundness, including J=1, with the same whole-tuple native calibration.

## 6. Completeness and positive maps

Take any positive zero of the frozen101/368 endpoint. Its soundness
supplies X=2^R,q=2^kappa,R>3q, q^4|X and t=Bt/(q-1)>=0 integral.
In particular X>q. Retain all inputs except replace w by

    x=X-q>0.

All kernel parameters, roots, b,L and the projection are identical.
Write W_old=1+(q-1)L and W_new=1+q(q-1)L. Their difference is
(q-1)^2L>0. Set

    n_new=n_old+(W_new-W_old)*t+X/q^4.                      (8)

This is a strictly positive integer. Since B=q*Bt, equation(8)
preserves the exact normalized center(2). The new M is q times old M,
so the integer Z is q times old Z and the full polynomial output is
q^8 times the old output. Thus it vanishes. This argument does not
require a particular signed root factor.

Conversely Sections2--5 extract a finite refutation from any new positive
zero and hence old canonical positive witnesses. A positive pointwise
inverse n map is not asserted. All proof abbreviations w,t,X/q^4 above
are recovered integers; none is an additional unknown in the equation.

## 7. Exact degree340 and operation tradeoff

The direct base has degree1. The front degrees become

    A4,Delta8,I5,Df18,Q26,V22,L9,W11,P5,M13,Z35.

Write Z=MV+Z0. Its unique leading Z0 term is the transport term,
of degree24. The squared-root argument degrees are96,68,70,96.
The centered difference omega has unique degree85 term
2Q0*Z00*M0*V00. Its fourth power uniquely has degree340; competing
first-bracket terms have degree at most166 versus170, and the last
norm product has degree330<340.

For an attained coefficient let q0=beta*J and X0=q0+x. The complete
leading form simplifies to

    omega0=2^7*q0^39*X0^9*i^5*Y^19*(eta+zeta)^10
                       *B0*(q0-F),
    B0=(q0*K0+X0)*(q0-F-alpha)-q0*F.

In omega0^4 the highest possible x exponent is40. It requires taking
x from every X0 factor and the x term from each B0 factor. Selecting

    [J^156*x^40*i^20*Y^76*eta^40*alpha^4*F^4]P
        =2^28*beta^156>0.

The exponents sum to340. The other three ports cannot cancel this
coefficient. The complete shared schedule has107 operations:
64 multiplications,25 additions and18 subtractions. This preserves
