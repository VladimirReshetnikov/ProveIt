# Carrier composition with the materialized round12 source

Ordinary arithmetic lead, 2026-10-23; Lean remains paused. This records the source transformation before the newer shared-direct archive takes priority. No new large circuit or polynomial has been generated by this note.

The carrier proof in this directory generalizes slightly. For `m,N>=1`, **any integer M >= (m+1)^N** has the required injectivity on the two affine products, conditional on their equal evaluation. If the premise product had actual degree at least N+1, it would have value at least `M^(N+1)`, while

```text
A(M) <= (1+mM)^N < ((m+1)M)^N <= M^(N+1).
```

Therefore both products have at most N nonconstant factors. Their coefficient sums are at most `(m+1)^N <= M`. The constant coefficient is 1, so every coefficient is strictly below M (including the constant-polynomial case since M>=2). The least-nonzero-coefficient argument then proves polynomial identity and exact flow balance.

The round12 direct source has five outer naturals `N,x,y,m,M0`, uses `M=M0+1`, and currently imposes `M0=2^(2mN+1)`. Two directly compatible replacements are:

- `M0=2^(mN)`, keeping `M=M0+1` and every local row/endpoint unchanged.
- `M0+1=(m+1)^N`, keeping the same local row/endpoint and changing only the height graph's target, base, and exponent.

Both satisfy the generalized carrier theorem. The second is an integer predecessor equation, not an illicit subtraction in a natural expression: its right side is at least 1, so a natural `M0` always exists.

The degenerate cases are excluded before invoking injectivity. `N=0` makes the two endpoint remainders identical but requires `M+1=1`, impossible since either replacement has M>0. If `m=0`, all three rule labels are zero; every update preserves the accumulator and contradicts the endpoints. Thus no extra existential guard or positivity branch is required. Completeness still chooses finite proof labels/N first, then the carrier, trace, beta code, and independent CRT witness bound U.

There are two further measurable variants: use `M=M0` with `M0=2^(mN)` and change the endpoint to `beta(0)=M0+1`, or retain the plus-one carrier with `M0=(m+1)^N`. They are mathematically valid by the same argument. Their local signed-split polynomials differ, so a literal comparison must rebuild the full bounded-CRT/affine pipeline rather than change a numeric summary.

Expected implementation tradeoffs, not measured results: replacing `2mN+1` by `mN` removes a scalar product and an addition, which the affine compiler may already eliminate; witness count therefore need not fall. A variable power base `m+1` avoids the nonlinear `mN` exponent wire, but may introduce nonlinear `a*(m+1)` and `(m+1)^2` wires inside the general power certificate, where base 2 previously permitted scalar folding. Only full final variable/term/coefficient counts can decide the better representation.
