## The critical order of recovery

The proof uses positive unknowns throughout, with X=wq, Y free and
mu=q^2+1. The index modulus is mu^2, not the old q-adic one. The claimed
coprimality is precisely valid: q even implies q-1 odd, so mu^2 is prime
to q^3(q-1); H'=4mu(a+2)-1 is minus one modulo every prime of mu.
Consequently the integer signed-root relation recovers mu^2|e before
assuming C>0, q^3|Y or a transport quotient. No coprimality of H' with
q or q-1 is used.

The rational-root argument is also licensed before source interpretation.
For all positive assignments, the elementary root brackets cover both
b'=0 and b'!=0. They give superincreasing orders d<y<tau<f or
d<tau<f<y. In either order the largest member of a nonempty signed
subsum exceeds the sum of all smaller members. The square-class
character projection therefore makes each root rational. The first,
strong and main roots are then integral because their squares are
integers; the auxiliary rational root is integral by the explicit
adjacent-coefficient descent with Q>1. The descent preserves its reduced
denominator and strictly decreases the absolute integer coordinate.

The first Pell norm has fundamental positive solution (2V0+1,2): a
putative ordinate one would require V0^2+V0+1 to be a square, which
lies strictly between consecutive squares. Thus k=2*psi_(2V0+1)(n0).
The interval c=kY+eta with0<eta<k excludes n0=1 once the main norm is
classified, and gives c>8q^5. This inequality, together with the mask
bound valid even for C<=0, makes c(T+alpha)>R. Hence b'>0 and V>0
are proved before the transport-sign step. The offset alpha really is
sufficient; F is not needed in that offset.

For Bt<=-1, the rational expression for n is bounded using
tau+f+2c+2<3(A'-1)I, the estimate on y-V, the explicit two cases R>=0
and R<0 for |e|/mu^2, and |g|<2c+1. The chosen L exceeds the sum,
forcing n<0. This proves Bt>=0, then C>0, and only then licenses
3q<R<q^4-2. It does not assume g,s,t integral while applying this bound.

The recovered mu^2 divisibility gives 2n0>=R+1. The main/strong rank
lemmas apply with their stated size hypotheses: c>2R, c>2p,
c^2|I and the reconstructed strong index m. They give p=R; comparing
P'>A' then forces n0<R and hence 2n0=R+1 exactly. The fixed-minus
parity argument correctly handles signed indices and yields R=3 modulo4.
Its plus-sign step-down includes the residue-zero k=m boundary, and the
half-interval bounds exclude the wrong signs in both congruences.

## Archimedean separation and source reconstruction

The ratio estimate with the scaled Pell parameters gives
Y>X^r/3 and a>X^(r+1)/3, with R=2r+1 and r>=24. It follows that

```
q^4 * 2^(R+1) * X < X^(r+1) < 3a < H'.
```

This is sufficient even when H' shares factors with q^3(q-1). The
negative main-root sign makes a **positive** multiple of H', namely
q^3(q-1)(2^R X+1), strictly smaller than H'. Thus that sign is
impossible. The positive sign gives an integer multiple of H' with
absolute value below H', so X=2^R. No cancellation of a potentially
noncoprime multiplier was used. This is the decisive inverse-projection
step. The same relation now gives q^3|Y and q-1|Bt.

The scaled ratio and the half-binomial expansion then identify Y=M/2
exactly. The small error estimates place the ratio inside the same open
unit interval as the integer Y. This permits a **fresh unscaled** kernel
with the same R,X,Y and the original source's parameter A=a+2. The
unscaled ratio proves positive eta_o,zeta_o; its index congruence gives
the positive h quotient; its projection and growth give positive gamma_o.
The strong pair at R*c_o supplies c_o^2 divisibility, and the cited
explicit auxiliary completion gives the old transport convention and
all fifteen positive coordinates of the exact package61 system. The
scaled kernel is not passed to an unscaled compiler theorem.

Conversely, use the source's actual canonical promises before changing
the kernel. They give positive j=(J-1)/2, the same half-binomial Y,
and the new scaled interval. Choosing the strong index R*c*mu makes
mu*c^2 divide I: the first factor already has c^2, and the composition
factor psi_D(mu) is divisible by mu since mu|A'-1|D^2-1. This does
not require coprimality of mu and c. The explicit auxiliary witness
has T*>alpha by the displayed Q_v lower bound, so T=T*-alpha stays
positive. Finally the sum-variable n is positive because tau>k>Y>=s.
W is evaluated only after these witnesses are chosen and imposes no
extra equation. The all-positive norm factor then vanishes.

The use of package61's source theorem is an explicit inherited ordinary
calibration dependency. The proof also cites its rank/index/auxiliary
lemmas and standard Pell facts; those dependencies have not disappeared
merely because there is only one compiler/calibration theorem.
