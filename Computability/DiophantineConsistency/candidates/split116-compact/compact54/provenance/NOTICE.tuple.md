This work synthesizes all eight wave-7 incoming archives at commit
4b4349064a750ba6347293b55874a7f92abdd0db. In particular:

- Selective Quadratic supplied sparse signed-borrow target padding and the
  exact mask-popcount threshold.

  and norm-minimum ideas. Its original tuple was not yet composed into its
  own main equations; this release rebinds it as a whole.
- Support Majorants supplied the T6 radical expression and coarser correction.
- Sparse Arithmetic supplied squared-modulus quotient rebuilding and the
  (1,b,bK) integer radical separator.
- The previous sparse-Pell iteration supplied support packing, exact CRT
  quotient removal and positivity-first conditional norm separation.

The new contributions are the twelve-guard positivity bijection, the
calibrated selective/interior source compositions, quotient-cancelled
majorant correction, equal (1,b,b) integer separator, and constant-cancelled
short positive norm minimum, with explicit coefficient-specific circuits.
No inherited discovery is represented as a new native coefficient-size
record or a new unknown-count theorem. All source archive names, exact
pins, hashes, selected-member identities and retained direct Git blobs are
recorded separately in PROVENANCE.json and the review ledgers.

The original native machine and Wang-language sources retain the ProveIt,
Wade, normalized-source and K215 notices under native/licenses/. The
inherited historical proof texts may refer to their own coefficient tuples
or package paths; active bindings in this release are those of native/,
nine/FIXED_BINDING.json and integer/FIXED_BINDING.json only.

The inherited code texts, if any, are inert references. No upstream program
is imported or executed by verify_release.py. The authored mathematical
