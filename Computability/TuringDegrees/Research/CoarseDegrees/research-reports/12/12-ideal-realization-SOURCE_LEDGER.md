## Published ingredients and closely related constructions

1. D. R. Hirschfeldt, C. G. Jockusch Jr., R. Kuyper, P. E. Schupp,
   “Coarse reducibility and algorithmic randomness,” JSL 81 (2016), 1028–1046.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.01707
   https://doi.org/10.1017/jsl.2015.70
   - Section 2: uniform coarsenings.
   - Theorem 3.7: cone-avoiding compactness / robust radius.
   - Theorem 4.3: gamma=1 implies trivial common information (credited to Igusa).

2. C. G. Jockusch Jr., P. E. Schupp,
   “Generic computability, Turing degrees, and asymptotic density,”
   JLMS 85(2) (2012), 472–490.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.5212
   https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/jdr051
   - Theorem 2.19: dyadic code coarsely computable iff the input is Delta^0_2;
     its relativization yields the jump-cone spectrum used here.

3. D. R. Hirschfeldt, C. G. Jockusch Jr., P. E. Schupp,
   “Coarse computability, the density metric, Hausdorff distances between
   Turing degrees, perfect trees, and reverse mathematics,” preprint (2021).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.13118
   - Lemma 3.10: extraction from positive-density columns.
   - Theorem 3.11: a close antecedent using robust codes for a countable ideal
     and finite-column truncations. This prevents claiming the column mechanism
     as a new discovery.
   - Section 3 also discusses relative jump inversion in density-metric work.

4. D. D. Dzhafarov, G. Igusa,
   “Notions of robust information coding,” Computability 6(2) (2017), 105–124.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2982
   https://doi.org/10.3233/COM-160059
   - Background framework for robust coding and uniformity distinctions.

5. P. M. Gerdes,
   “Comparing Notions of Dense Computability on omega^omega and 2^omega,”
   arXiv:2508.06925v1 (2025).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06925v1
   - Question 7: least representatives and broader representative spectra.
   - The effective-dense part is not resolved by this manuscript.

## Search limitation

The search was targeted to the repository and these primary sources. It does
not establish absence of the present conclusions from every paper, thesis,
preprint, or unpublished note. “New” should not be read as an independently
verified priority claim. The complete proofs, the precise repository target,
and the stated scope can be assessed independently of priority.
