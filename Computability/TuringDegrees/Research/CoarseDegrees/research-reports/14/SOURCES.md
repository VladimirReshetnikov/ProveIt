## Peter M. Gerdes

*Comparing Notions of Dense Computability on omega^omega and 2^omega*.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.06925v1
https://arxiv.org/html/2508.06925v1

The arXiv record showed v1, submitted 9 August 2025. Section 7, Question 7
asks whether the asymptotic degrees considered have representatives of
least ordinary Turing degree. The draft targets its effective-dense part,
in both uniform and nonuniform versions. Questions 5 and 6 concern other
issues about binary representatives; our theorem does not solve them.

## Astor--Hirschfeldt--Jockusch

*Dense computability, upper cones, and minimal pairs*, Computability 8
(2019), 155--177. DOI 10.3233/COM-180231.

https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~drh/Papers/dense.html
https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~drh/Papers/Papers/dense.pdf

Definition 1.4 gives effective-dense descriptions. Definition 3.1 gives
uniform and nonuniform effective-dense reductions. Theorem 5.9 concerns
quasiminimality in asymptotic degree structures, not a least ordinary
Turing degree inside one equivalence class. Proposition 5.5 and its
surrounding discussion recall generalized lowness of 1-generics.
Relevant formula pages were inspected visually as well as through text.

## Kjos-Hanssen--Merkle--Stephan

*Kolmogorov complexity and the Recursion Theorem*, Transactions of the
American Mathematical Society 363 (2011), 5465--5480.

https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.3933v2
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0901.3933

Theorem 5.1 was checked directly, including its PDF page image: an oracle
computes an eventually different function exactly when it is high or of
DNR degree. This is the draft's named external input for the low refinement.
The theorem and its priority are not claimed as new.

## Brendle--Brooke-Taylor--Ng--Nies

*An analogy between cardinal characteristics and highness properties of
oracles*, arXiv:1404.2839v2 (2014).

https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.2839v2
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1404.2839

Section 3.2 records the high-or-DNR characterization. Theorem 4.2 relates
eventual difference to weak meager engulfing. Section 4, item (5), records
Rupprecht's result excluding 2-generics from that class. The draft gives a
direct proof of the particular 2-generic fact it needs.

## Novelty limitation

Searches were aimed at effective-dense least representatives, generic
coordinate erasures, and eventually different functions. They did not
establish a prior occurrence of the exact application developed here.
The absence of a matching result in this search is not a proof of novelty.
No claim of peer-reviewed acceptance, independent review, or exhaustive
priority certification is made.
