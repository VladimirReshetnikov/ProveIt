\section{Result and comparison target}

The requested target is exclusively 3-SAT. Exact counting is used as a
subroutine because the reviewed algorithms decide satisfiability by testing
whether their count is positive. We keep three quantities separate:
the number \(n\) of Boolean variables, the number \(m\) of clauses, and
the wire parameter \(s\) of the tensor contractor. A coefficient in the
last parameter is not automatically a coefficient per SAT variable.

\begin{theorem}[New certified contraction bound]\label{thm:main}
Assume the normalized exact contraction interface described in
Section~\ref{sec:interface}: at most \(s\) Boolean wires, maximum degree
\(\lceil s^{9/10}\rceil\), the specified coefficient ring and entry access,
and the inherited exact packing, deformation, interpolation, and mode
identities. There is a deterministic choice of contraction plans with
\[
 T(s,\mathcal L),\ S(s,\mathcal L)
 \le (s+\mathcal L)^{O(1)}
       2^{(C_*+o(1))s/\sqrt{\log_2 s}},
\]
where
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mainconstant}
 C_*=\frac{\pi\sqrt2}{\ln2}
          \sqrt{\frac{20000}{301}\,B_*},
 \qquad
 B_*=\NewBeta,
 \qquad C_*=\NewC\ldots.
\end{equation}
Here \(\mathcal L\) is the description length and all hidden entry-access
constants are fixed. The constant \(29.149\) is an eventual upper-bound
coefficient for both resources.
\end{theorem}

The proof occupies Sections~\ref{sec:interface}--\ref{sec:schedule}.
The finite inequality behind \(B_*\) is proved by a reproducible exhaustive
certificate using arbitrary-precision integers. The infinite recursion is
handled analytically. The theorem imports the same analytic contractor
lemmas used by the preceding packets; it does not require a new unproved
mode-summation hypothesis.

\subsection{What improved}

The incoming pairwise certificate uses nine plans and bounds a quadratic
norm by maximizing each plan pair independently. A plan can consequently
be charged for different operations in its comparisons with different
partners. Our certificate enforces one support choice per plan throughout
the whole quadratic expression. This stronger analysis makes a twelve-plan
affine construction competitive even though its independent pair bound
is worse.

\subsection{What remains unresolved}

For unrestricted 3-SAT, the supported worst-case rate remains
\begin{equation}\label{eq:satrate}
 (n+m)^{O(1)}
 2^{O(n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n})}.
\end{equation}
The existing density-sensitive refinement is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:density}
 (n+m)^{O(1)}
 2^{O\left(\frac{n}{\sqrt{\log n}}
       \min\{\log(2+m/n),\log\log n\}\right)}.
\end{equation}
At fixed clause density this becomes
\((n+m)^{O(1)}2^{O(n/\sqrt{\log n})}\)
\cite{source,density,density2}.
Our change improves an explicit contractor constant inside these
reductions. It does not remove the \(\log\log n\) factor or improve the
\(\sqrt{\log n}\) denominator. The cached construction uses subexponential
space; the previous polynomial-space branch remains available with its
previous guarantee.

\subsection{The linked Lean development is a genuine machine theorem}

The user's linked commit is
\[
 \texttt{anthropics/formal-math@1a6eee293b9e00cb432eef6018a21f523623a9af}.
\]
Its independently stated \texttt{MainResults.MainTheorem} quantifies one
deterministic Mathlib multi-stack Turing machine with finite stack
alphabets, before quantifying \(k,n\), and the formula. It computes the
complete satisfying-assignment count within the stated bound
\[
 2^{C\min\{km/\sqrt{\log_2m},
              \ k^2n\log_2\log_2n/\sqrt{\log_2n}\}},
\]
subject to the explicit normalization premises: \(k\ge1\), \(n\ge4\),
at least two clauses, no repeated clause, and every variable occurring.
Deduplication, unused variables, and small inputs need the usual
polynomial-time front end. At fixed width three the rate agrees with
\eqref{eq:satrate} \cite{anthropic-main,anthropic-solution}.

\texttt{Solution.lean} supplies proofs of the public propositions without
additional theorem hypotheses. A source scan found the two expected
\texttt{sorry} occurrences in the separate \texttt{Challenge.lean}
specification, and no source declarations of additional axioms or
unsafe proof shortcuts in the project. The exact linked commit has a
successful \texttt{verify (eth)} GitHub check. Its README and Comparator
configuration describe the standard three Lean axioms and the nanoda check
\cite{anthropic-solution,anthropic-ci}.

We inspected these sources and exact-commit CI evidence. We did not
independently rebuild the full Lean project or rerun its alternate kernel.
Polynomial space is not a conjunct of the exposed Anthropic main theorem,
so it must not be inferred from that theorem alone. Its unspecified
machine constant also cannot be compared numerically with a normalized
wire coefficient. The present contribution is a new finite contraction
certificate and plan family, not a revision of that formalization.
