\title{\color{navy}\bfseries Compatible affine contraction plans for 3-SAT\\[5pt]
\large A stricter certified exponential bound, exact finite certificates,\\
and the remaining logarithmic bottleneck}
\author{Research prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov}
\date{October 26, 2026 (Pacific time)}

\begin{abstract}
We review the latest 3-SAT research at ProveIt commit
\texttt{8e76d515063c}, including the five incoming archives added at
\texttt{26bd83d4b37d}, and the linked Anthropic formalization.
The smallest incoming general-degree cached contraction coefficient is
\(\OldC\), on the scale \(s/\sqrt{\log_2 s}\), where \(s\) is a normalized
tensor-network wire budget. We reduce this coefficient to
\(\NewC\), with a safe eventual coefficient \(29.149\).

The new construction uses twelve ten-group contraction plans obtained by
adding a tenth point to the parallel lines of the affine plane over
\(\F_3\). Its proof keeps one operation choice consistent across every
pairwise comparison. An exact integer elimination and branch-and-bound
certificate checks all \(96^{12}\) support choices without enumerating them.
A separately written checker reconstructs the supports and recomputes the
search bounds. A sharper, already published rectangular multiplication
envelope gives the final coefficient; a separate exhaustive certificate
improves the next recursion stage. The new plans alone give \(29.640645\)
with the previous backend.

This is an improvement of an explicit asymptotic upper bound, relative to
the imported contraction and encoding lemmas. It does not establish a
smaller unrestricted 3-SAT exponent order: the strongest supported order
remains \(n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n}\), with the existing density-sensitive
refinement. No end-to-end solver speedup or new Lean formalization is
claimed. We also give a sharp limitation for the preceding nine-plan
entropy model and two scoped obstructions encountered while pursuing an
order improvement.
\end{abstract}
