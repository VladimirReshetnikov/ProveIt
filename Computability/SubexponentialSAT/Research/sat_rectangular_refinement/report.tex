\begin{abstract}
We review the 3-SAT research at a fixed ProveIt revision, including all five
new incoming archives absent from its 29-packet index. The strongest
comparable generic cached contraction coefficient is $\oldC$, multiplying
$s/\sqrt{\ceil{\log_2s}}$ in a base-two exponent. A weighted triple
certificate lowers that coefficient to $\sameC$ with the same matrix
backend and the same recursion schedule. Incorporating newer, explicitly
identified characteristic-zero matrix-multiplication results and a newly
certified schedule gives coefficients $\NewC$ for time and $\NewS$ for
space. Every recursive table entry is charged. We prove a finite-base
arithmetic transfer that permits the imported matrix algorithms over the
two-adic coefficient rings without a map from those rings to odd prime
fields. We also prove a restricted rectangular-grid minimax obstruction
for the Anthropic crossing recursion and supply exact finite operator-rank
probes. These results improve explicit upper-bound coefficients. They do
not prove a smaller general 3-SAT exponent order than
$n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n}$, or an asymptotic improvement over the
unspecified constants of the independent Anthropic algorithm. No
end-to-end SAT implementation or new Lean formalization is claimed.
\end{abstract}
