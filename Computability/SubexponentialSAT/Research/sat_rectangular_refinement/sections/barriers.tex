\section{Attempts to improve the logarithmic order}
\label{sec:barriers}

\subsection{The effective crossing interface}

The Anthropic paper's strongest recurrence is in Appendices A--B, rather
than its weaker introductory recurrence. For an alphabet of size
$2^\mu$, it decomposes the crossing tensor
\[
 \mathsf C(x,y,u,v)=[x=v][y=u]
\]
as $d\mathsf C=\sum_{j\in I}A_j$. Each piece has integer row and column
factorizations through $c_j^{\rm row}$ and $c_j^{\rm col}$ bits, with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:crosscontract}
 c_j^{\rm row}+c_j^{\rm col}
 \le4\rho\mu+O(\log\mu).
\end{equation}
There must be polynomially many pieces, polynomial coefficient bit
lengths, and a single $2^{O(\mu)}$ evaluation procedure. These effectiveness
conditions are essential.

Its parameters satisfy
\[
 K_{l+1}=g_l^2,\qquad
 g_l=2^{3l+x_0}K_l,\qquad
 e_{l+1}=(\rho+3\zeta_l)e_l+3/K_l,
 \quad\zeta_l=2^{-l-5}.
\]
Therefore $e_l=\Theta((\log K_l)^{-\alpha})$ with
$\alpha=\log_2(1/\rho)$. The partition/vertex-operator pieces give
$\rho=1/\sqrt2$ and $\alpha=1/2$~\cite{anthropicpaper}.

A successful construction with \eqref{eq:crosscontract} for
$\rho<1/\sqrt2$, preserving every stated effectiveness condition,
would yield a genuine improvement in the logarithmic denominator power
for 3-SAT. The finite probes below do not construct such a family.

\subsection{A new aspect-ratio obstruction for the existing two-way bound}

An apparently simple way to improve the recursion is to replace its square
grid by a rectangle. The following lemma shows precisely why this does
not follow from the current total-cost guarantee alone.

\begin{proposition}[Rectangular-grid minimax]\label{prop:rectbarrier}
Let $a,b>1$, $N=ab$, $0<\alpha<1$, $C>0$, and $0<\rho<1$.
Suppose the only information about two nonnegative costs is
\[
 U_R+U_C\le2\rho N^2\mu.
\]
Grant the optimistic recursive rates $e(t)=C/(\log_2t)^\alpha$ and
ignore all additional overhead. The worst certified cost of choosing
rows or columns is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:minimax}
 \max_{U_R+U_C\le2\rho N^2\mu}
       \min\{e(a)U_R,e(b)U_C\}
 =\frac{2\rho C N^2\mu}
        { (\log_2a)^\alpha+(\log_2b)^\alpha}.
\end{equation}
Among aspect ratios with product $N$, its minimum is attained by
$a=b=\sqrt N$. Relative to the target rate
$CN^2\mu/(\log_2N)^\alpha$, the best factor remains
$\rho2^\alpha$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For fixed positive multipliers $c_R,c_C$ and budget $B$, maximizing
$\min(c_RU_R,c_CU_C)$ uses all of $B$ and equalizes the two costs.
The resulting value is $B/(1/c_R+1/c_C)$, proving~\eqref{eq:minimax}.
Write $x=\log_2a$, $y=\log_2b$, so $x+y=\log_2N$. By concavity,
\[
 x^\alpha+y^\alpha
 \le2\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)^\alpha
 =2^{1-\alpha}(\log_2N)^\alpha,
\]
with equality at $x=y$. Substitution gives the stated factor.
\end{proof}

Thus the existing two-way guarantee cannot by itself certify progress
with $\alpha>\log_2(1/\rho)$; for the current pieces this threshold is
$1/2$. This proposition is a minimax statement about an analytic
relaxation. It does not assert that every extremal cost pair is realizable
by an actual crossing instance, and is not a lower bound for 3-SAT. Extra
correlations between row and column costs, or a different crossing
identity, could escape its hypotheses.

\subsection{Why the straightforward compositions do not close the gap}

The newest incoming packets already prove several useful restricted
obstructions~\cite{pairwise,affineplane,affinecauchy}. We checked them
before proposing another version of the same route.

\paragraph{Cauchy modes followed by the clique engine.}
The explicit labeling layout requires $u\log_2K=O(\log s)$ for a
growing number of groups $K$. Its information scale is $M/\sqrt u$,
whereas the current clique coefficient is
$\Theta((\log K)^{-1/2})$. Even an ideal, cost-free interface between
them would leave the certificate scale
\[
 \Theta\left(\frac{M}{\sqrt{u\log K}}\right)
 =\Omega\left(\frac{M}{\sqrt{\log s}}\right).
\]
In an actual implementation the local entry routines and precision also
have to be paid. This is a limitation of these two certificate scales,
not a general runtime lower bound.

\paragraph{Sparse graded spectra in the same energy window.}
The crossing has row-flattening rank $2^{2\mu}$. If polynomially many
pieces factor through polynomially many graded spaces of energies at
most $4h$, and
$\mu\sim c h^\theta$ while uniformly
$\log_2\dim W_E\le cE^\theta+o(h^\theta)$, rank subadditivity implies
\[
 2\mu\le O(\log h)+c(4h)^\theta+o(h^\theta),
 \qquad 2\le4^\theta.
\]
Hence $\theta\ge1/2$. Substituting a thinner Hilbert series into the
same intermediate-space proof cannot produce the hoped-for improvement.
Allowing exponentially many pieces instead makes the existing Cartesian
piece enumeration exponential in the original information budget.
Structured summation would be a new theorem, not an automatic repair.

\paragraph{Lower initial precision and a conserved degree.}
The dense reduction has $O(n\log\log n)$ wires and counting precision
$n+1$. Its dominant Hermite-recovery increment is nevertheless
$6MD/u$, so lowering the initial precision alone does not change the
leading entropy. A representation preserving incidence degree $K=O(n)$
through every recursive interface could instead target
$\sqrt{MK/u}$. The prior degree-flow lemma correctly conserves total
degree, but does not supply efficient local coefficient queries on the
compressed binary indices. Those obligations remain open.

\paragraph{Nonbinary leading digits and separator trees.}
The incoming odd-prime calculation shows that restricting to one leading
digit loses the binary taper cancellation; one fixed nontrivial grouping
cannot localize two distinct shifts. Node-dependent groupings are outside
that proof but need a new counted layout. The incoming star-weight witness
also proves optimality of the flag coefficient among arbitrary separator
trees at the used powers of two. Tree imbalance by itself does not improve
that polynomial-space certificate.

\subsection{Finite rank probes of the actual crossing pieces}

The archive implements the Appendix B operator formulas, not merely a
dimension surrogate. For $h=1,\ldots,6$, it computes every piece and checks
\[
 \sum_{s=-2h}^{2h}A_s=h![x=v][y=u]
 \pmod{1000003}.
\]
For $h=1,\ldots,8$, the central piece $A_0$ has the following row and
column ranks over that prime field:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}rrrrrrrrr@{}}
\toprule $h$&1&2&3&4&5&6&7&8\\
\midrule $p(h)$&1&2&3&5&7&11&15&22\\
Row rank&1&4&9&25&49&121&225&484\\
Column rank&1&4&9&25&49&121&225&484\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
These integer matrices therefore have full rank over $\Q$ as well.
The probes use all $p(h)$ partition labels; the binary theorem selects
$2^\mu$ labels, so this statement must not be transferred to every binary
submatrix. Also, at these small values the ambient matrix dimension is
smaller than the source's intermediate-space upper bound. The experiment
does not settle the asymptotic image-rank question. It does reject an
unsupported claim of an already visible small-degree rank collapse.

\subsection{A further proved local inequality}

The supplementary mathematical fragment proves, for sorted
$0\le a\le b\le c$, the symmetric quadratic bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:quadratic}
 U(a,b,c)^2\le\frac{55}{96}
 \left(7(a^2+b^2+c^2)-4(ab+ac+bc)\right),
\end{equation}
where
\[
 U(a,b,c)=
 \begin{cases}
 b+c,&2a\le b,\\
 c+a+b^2/(4a),&2a\ge b,\ a>0.
 \end{cases}
\]
This is the equal-outer rectangular curve followed by blocking, rather
than the strongest general three-dimensional bound. Equality in
\eqref{eq:quadratic} occurs at $(1,1,20/17)$ and its scaled permutations.
An exact affine-plane spectral calculation gives final-triangle envelope
coefficient squared $55/192$. The proof and rational checker are included
as a separate appendix source.

This last statement concerns three \emph{already formed} blocks. It does
not pay for their internal merges or recursive entries, and is not used
in $C_T$ or $C_S$. A fixed-slack algorithmic interpretation would also
charge the finite-library approximation slack. The result is a completed
local inequality and a possible ingredient for future work, not an
additional global SAT improvement.
