\subsection{Closing the depth and precision induction}

To avoid assuming the desired contraction before proving packing, verify
the gates provisionally through tail depth
$\log_{20/11}\lambda+O(1)$. On this whole range the smallest tail taper
has order
\[
 \lambda^{\delta+o(1)},\qquad
 \delta=1+\frac{\ln(63/100)}{\ln(20/11)}>0,
\]
because $63/100>11/20$. Hence the uniform packing estimates apply through
the provisional depth. They give actual tail child ratio
$1/2+o(1)<11/20$, so an active path cannot exceed that depth. This closes
the induction. There are $O(\log\lambda)$ levels and accumulated relative
padding error $1+o(1)$.

At a retained interpolation node,
$D\ge2^{a_{d-1}+B+1}$. The inherited creation-precision bound
$O(s\lambda^2 2^{a_{d-1}})$ is at most
\[
 O(\lambda^4 2^{-B})=o(1)
\]
times $MD/u_d$, using $s/M\le\lambda$ and $u_d\le\lambda$.
Thus the precision and entropy bounds in Section~\ref{sec:interface}
apply uniformly. Moreover
$a_d\le A_\infty\lambda+O((\log\lambda)^2)$, so all precisions have
polynomial magnitude and polynomial bit length.

The leading wire masses at depths zero through six are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:masses}
 (m_0,\ldots,m_6)
 =\left(1,\frac15,\frac2{65},\frac4{585},
          \frac8{4095},\frac8{12285},\frac{16}{61425}\right),
 \qquad M_d\le(1+o(1))m_ds.
\end{equation}
Later masses halve. Original entry routines cost
$2^{O(s^{9/10})}$ times a polynomial, new packet routines cost
$\exp(s^{A_\infty+o(1)})$ times a polynomial, and direct leaves cost
$2^{O(s/\lambda)}$ times their entry overhead. All are lower order on
$s/\sqrt\lambda$. In the normalized-work recurrence~\eqref{eq:paid},
multiplying the $\poly(s)$ overheads over
$O(\log\lambda)$ levels adds only $O(\lambda\log\lambda)$ to the
exponent. Restoring the single factor $P(s+\mathcal L)$ from
Section~\ref{sec:interface} gives the polynomial description-length
factor in the theorem. The imported immutable-entry representation is retained
throughout; an entry description is not recursively expanded into an
exponentially large expression.

\subsection{Exact time and space constants}

Define
\[
 K_d^*=\kappa\frac{m_d}{\sqrt{\eta_d}}\quad(0\le d\le6),
 \qquad \gamma=\frac{1/2}{\sqrt{63/100}}=\frac5{\sqrt{63}}<1.
\]
At a finite stage $i\le5$, the aggregation branch of~\eqref{eq:paid}
has coefficient
\[
 C_i=\sum_{j<i}\alpha_{r_j}K_j^*+\beta_{r_i}K_i^*.
\]
For the tail, $\alpha_4/(1-\gamma)>\beta_4$. Its finite aggregation
branches interpolate between the first-tail aggregation and the limiting
all-cache route. Consequently the exact global time coefficient is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:CT}
 \boxed{C_T=\sum_{j=0}^{5}\alpha_{r_j}K_j^*
           +\frac{\alpha_4 K_6^*}{1-5/\sqrt{63}}.}
\end{equation}
The finite checker proves that this is larger than every $C_i$. For
orientation, the complete list is

Ancestor and descendant tables coexist, so their storage sizes add.
There are only $O(\log\lambda)$ active frames, and hence their leading

sum. The largest local coefficient is at the root:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:CS}

\end{equation}
The later local coefficients are approximately $6.25666$, $1.57435$,
$0.38609$, $0.12996$, $0.03741$, and $0.02331$, and then decay
geometrically. Polynomial matrix workspace and CRT overhead do not change
these coefficients.

Equations~\eqref{eq:CT}--\eqref{eq:CS}, with the literal rational data,
define the headline values exactly. Rational interval arithmetic proves
\[
 C_T<\SafeC<\oldC,\qquad C_S<\SafeS.
\]
All finite branches, terminal work, packing and entry costs have now been
charged, proving Theorem~\ref{thm:main}. The separate incoming
$30.257036\ldots/25.989068\ldots$ memory point remains useful when its
smaller space exponent is preferred.
