COMMON NORMALIZATION
All coefficients below multiply s/sqrt(log2(s)) (or its equivalent ceiling-log
version) in a base-two exponent, with fixed polynomial input factors and
uniform lower-order exponent terms. s is a Boolean-wire budget, not a SAT
variable count. Generic input premise: root degree at most ceil(s^(9/10)),
initial precision/truncation at most s, inherited admissible entry-access and
immutable creation-precision conventions. Cached branches use subexponential
space. All nine retain the source exact deformation, recovery, packet, mode,
and SAT-reduction dependencies. No nine-packet result changes the unrestricted
SAT logarithmic order, supplies a full SAT solver, or independently verifies
the original ETH claim.

ASYMPTOTIC-ORDER LIMIT
The supported SAT density envelope remains
poly_k(n,m)*2^{O_k((n/sqrt(log n))*min{log(2+m/n),loglog(n)})}.
This remains2^{O_k(n loglog(n)/sqrt(log n))} in the worst case and
2^{O_{k,D}(n/sqrt(log n))} at fixed clause density.
No incoming packet closes the unproved structured-mode summation interface
needed for an s/log(s)-type contraction order. Changing the support menu or
matrix exponent alone still leaves the s/sqrt(log s) scale.

The explicit full-table width theorem in item7 is exactly
W_r=2*ceil(r/3)*(r-ceil(r/3)) for complete directed pair graphs.
It yields exp(Omega(M/sqrt u)) output work for a feasible abstract symmetric
mode inventory. That lower example is expressly not shown to occur for
every actual SAT instance; sparse/implicit/cancellation representations lie
outside its scope. It does not prove a SAT lower bound.

POTENTIAL NEXT STEP IDENTIFIED DURING REVIEW
The three-plan support-averaging bound is conservative. For finite support
sets V_j of nonnegative vectors, exact pointwise independence of the choices
and bilinear minimax give
 max_{h>=0,||h||<=1} min_j max_{v in V_j} v.h
 = max_{(v_j)} min_{lambda>=0,sum lambda=1} ||sum_j lambda_j v_j||.
Thus closest-convex-hull certificates can sharpen equal averaging. In the
original r9 menu the maximum-Q witnesses have unequal per-plan scores
1062,1121,1121; in the r13 menu its unique maximum witness has equal2358
scores and is compatible, so that particular menu's equal averaging is
already sharp. Better r13 menus require genuinely changed plans.
