MainResults.lean imports only Mathlib and defines the two claimed

* MainResults.mainTheorem is CountedRoute.mainTheoremHolds.
* MainResults.ethIsFalse derives the ETH negation through NamesInOrder and

MainTheorem quantifies a single finite-alphabet Mathlib FinTM2 machine and
a single natural constant C before k, n and the formula. It assumes k>=1,
n>=4, at least two clauses, no identical clause-list repeated, and every
variable occurring. Its exact count is output in n+1 bits within

    floor 2^(C min{k m/sqrt(log2 m),
                   k^2 n log2(log2 n)/sqrt(log2 n)}).

No polynomial-space guarantee occurs in that proposition. It does not
provide a matched numerical coefficient in ProveIt's normalized Boolean
wire model. Therefore a smaller Cauchy-engine constant cannot be asserted
to dominate this distinct complete algorithm.

ETHIsFalse is the negation of 0<s_3, where s_3 is the infimum of exponents
delta admitted by finite-alphabet machines deciding canonically encoded
3-CNF. MainTheorem is stronger in uniformity: one counting machine works
before k and the input are chosen.

## Why a direct hybrid does not immediately change the logarithmic order

Appendix A, Definition 7 and Theorem A, expose a general crossing interface.
A polynomial-size family of exact crossing pieces whose row and column
factorization bit counts sum to at most

    4 rho lambda + O(log lambda)

gives logarithmic denominator power alpha'=log2(1/rho). Appendix B proves
rho=1/sqrt(2) using a graded Heisenberg polynomial representation and
ordinary partition counts. Its piece parameter counts are polynomial, its
exact arithmetic sizes and evaluation costs are explicitly included, and
adaptive selection takes the less expensive of row and column aggregation.

Appendix H explicitly observes that a compatible graded representation
with log(dim V_E) proportional to E^alpha would give rho=2^(alpha-1).
This is the author's stated research direction, not a new observation of
this review. In particular, the prospect alpha<1/2 is already identified.
Naively applying Cauchy modes to these rank indices supplies neither the
new two-sided factorization inequality nor the required entry-cost closure.

## A scoped obstruction to deleting oscillator modes

A tempting way to reduce graded dimensions is to keep only oscillators
whose weights are perfect squares. The resulting monomial count has
logarithm Theta(E^(1/3)). For the lower bound, independently choose parts
2^2,3^2,...,J^2 with J=Theta(E^(1/3)) and fill the remaining weight with
ones; these are distinct partitions of E. For the upper bound, exponential
tilting of product_{j>=1}(1-exp(-theta*j^2))^(-1), followed by the Gaussian
sum bound, gives log count <= theta E+O(theta^(-1/2)); set
theta=Theta(E^(-2/3)).

But merely deleting the other oscillators destroys finite-pole locality,
the ingredient behind Appendix B's equality of the two operator orders.
Here is a short all-size proof, independent of any asymptotic estimate.

Let S be an infinite subset of the positive integers with arbitrarily long
gaps, and put a_m=m when |m| is in S, otherwise a_m=0. The scalar formal
commutator of the corresponding restricted Heisenberg current is

    C_S(z,w)=sum_{m in Z} a_m z^(-m-1) w^(m-1).

If (z-w)^H C_S(z,w)=0 for some finite H, coefficient comparison gives

    sum_{j=0}^H (-1)^j binom(H,j) a_(n-j)=0

for every integer n. Thus H consecutive zero values force every later
value to be zero by a forward recurrence. An arbitrarily long gap followed
by another element of S contradicts this. For square S, choose
n=(H+1)^2. The preceding H indices are nonsquares, so the displayed
coefficient equals a_n=n, not zero. The case H=0 is immediate.

This rules out the naive lacunary-current substitution, even on the
vacuum scalar commutator. It does not rule out all operator families with
smaller graded growth, all vertex-algebra approaches, or all improvements
to the Anthropic crossing pieces. The exact missing interface remains
a polynomial-size, efficiently evaluable, two-sided decomposition with
rho<1/sqrt(2), or another algorithm whose full resource accounting is
better. This review constructs no such decomposition.
