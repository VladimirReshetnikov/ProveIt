# Exact convolution for packet-Cauchy actions

## Claim and scope

For an L-slot packet, its Cauchy deformation matrix is Toeplitz. Given its
2L-1 distinct diagonals, its complete action on an L-vector over
R_p=(Z/2^p Z)[U]/(U^2+U+1) can be computed with three exact integer
convolutions. An actual Boolean column input needs two narrower products.
The resulting bit work is O(M_int(L(p+log L))) plus linear packing and
unpacking; M_int denotes the cost of exact integer multiplication. With a
quasi-linear integer multiplier this is quasi-linear in that packed length.
The included Python prototype uses the host's big-integer multiplier; it
does not implement or claim an asymptotically optimal integer multiplier.

More explicitly, for the implemented fixed quadratic ring the action costs
O(M_int(L(p+log L))+L(p+log L)) bit operations, includes reading the
diagonals/vector and writing all L output pairs, and uses
O(L(p+log L)) working bits. Diagonal preparation costs O(L) ring
multiplications plus two unit inversions, hence O(L M_int(p)+I(p)) bit
operations for fixed quadratic degree, apart from the exact input
normalization cost stated below. Thus preparing and applying a packet costs
O(L M_int(p)+I(p)+M_int(L(p+log L))), with linear terms understood.
The dense general-ring control uses L^2 quadratic-ring multiplications and
O(L^2) additions. The stronger masked Boolean control uses only additions
on the selected row/column pairs and can be faster at small sizes.

For the source's unramified extension degree e and nilpotent length ell,
the Toeplitz identity remains exact. Because W itself has no V dependence,
one conservative extension of the action is e^2*ell scalar convolutions,
followed by coefficient reduction modulo the defining polynomial. For
bounded-size defining coefficients this gives
O(e^2*ell*M_int(L(p+log L))+L*e^2*ell*p) bit work, in addition to kernel
preparation in that extension. This is a direct coordinate algorithm, not
an implemented arbitrary-extension API. The delivered code fixes e=2 and
ell=1; its measured ratios are only for that ring.

This improves the quadratic dense-action subroutine. It does not change the
number of weighted mode tuples, prove a new SAT exponent order, construct a
complete contraction engine, or replace the full Cauchy feature table when
an application actually needs every feature. Toeplitz-to-convolution and
Kronecker substitution are classical techniques. The contribution relative
to the reviewed corpus is their exact, ring-safe application to this packet
action, an implementation and matched controls.

## Algebraic identity

Use the source's notation L=2^a, S={-L,...,L-1}, alpha=2L,
C(T)=product_{q in S}(T-q), and T=t+alpha*zeta, with t in S. Put

    w_d = product_{q in S, q != d}(T-q) / C'(0), -L < d < L.

Then W_xy(T)=w_(y-x). For a vector v_y define the polynomial arrays

    A(X)=sum_{d=-L+1}^{L-1} w_d X^(d+L-1),
    B(X)=sum_{y=0}^{L-1} v_y X^(L-1-y).

Their product has coefficient

    [X^(2L-2-x)] A(X) B(X) = sum_y w_(y-x) v_y.

This proves the action formula over any commutative coefficient ring; no
field inverse or division by L is involved. For a masked local block,
replace v_y by zero on excluded columns and discard excluded output rows.
At a fixed source valuation level a packet has at most two group symbols,
so all its local blocks need at most two such masked actions.

There is a useful exact specialization when both slot sets are arithmetic
progressions. Rows r+hj and columns c+hk give the smaller Toeplitz matrix
w_(c-r+h(k-j)). Thus the bit-0 local groups, with opposite row/column
parities, require a convolution of dimension L/2, not L. The routine
strided_boolean_action implements this specialization and restores zeros
at all excluded output positions. This reduces practical overhead; it
does not change the already stated asymptotic order.

## Integral preparation in O(L) ring operations

The concrete implementation supports exact integer coordinates of zeta in
Z[U]/(U^2+U+1), then reduces outputs modulo 2^p. The reduction modulo 2 of
U^2+U+1 is irreducible. Thus an element is a unit exactly when its two
integer coordinates are not both even, and its 2-adic valuation is the
minimum coordinate valuation. The latter is multiplicative on nonzero
elements in the associated unramified local ring.

For d != t, v_2(T-d)=v_2(t-d)<=a. If zeta is nonzero, the remaining factor
T-t has valuation a+1+v_2(zeta). The complete residue grid gives

    sum_{q in S, q != t} v_2(t-q) = v_2(C'(0))

independently of t. Consequently

    v_2(C(T)/C'(0)) = a+1+v_2(zeta),

and cancellation of any one T-d leaves a nonnegative valuation. To compute
all w_d, split each exact factor T-q into a power of two and a unit before
reduction. Multiply the units, cancel the scalar odd part of C'(0), and
batch-invert the O(L) factor units. For each d, subtract its exact valuation
and multiply by its inverse unit. Only nonnegative final powers of two are
restored. Nothing divides a residue by two.

If zeta=0, exactly one factor T-t is zero. Every w_d except d=t is zero;
w_t=C'(t)/C'(0) is computed after cancelling the zero factor symbolically.
If t=-L, that diagonal is outside the L by L matrix, so all entries vanish.
The code handles this branch explicitly. Unit inversion in R_p uses

    (a+bU)^(-1) = ((a-b)-bU)/(a^2-ab+b^2),

whose scalar denominator is odd precisely for a unit. Preparation uses
O(L) p-bit ring operations and two unit inversions, plus exact input
normalization/valuation bookkeeping. Arbitrarily long input coordinates
also incur their input-reading and normalization cost.

## No-carry convolution

After choosing canonical residues, let A=max a_i, B=max b_j and
t=min(number of a_i, number of b_j). Every unreduced scalar convolution
coefficient is at most tAB. Choose a byte-aligned power Q of two strictly
larger than tAB. Form integers sum_i a_i Q^i and sum_j b_j Q^j. Their
ordinary product contains the exact convolution coefficients as radix-Q
digits, because none reaches Q. Read those digits and reduce modulo 2^p.
The zero-input case is handled before choosing Q. Byte-array packing and
unpacking are linear in the packed bit length and avoid quadratic repeated
shifting of large integers.

For pairs representing a+bU and c+dU, take the scalar convolutions

    A=a*c, B=b*d, C=(a+b)*(c+d).

The answer has real coordinate A-B and U-coordinate C-A-2B, since
U^2=-U-1. All coordinate arrays can first be reduced modulo 2^p. For a
Boolean column vector, compute its convolution with each diagonal
coordinate separately; no cross-coordinate product is necessary. This
also reduces the required radix from roughly 2p+log L bits to p+log L bits.

## Independent evidence

The cancelled-polynomial oracle forms each w_d directly as a product of
2L-1 factors at precision p+v_2(C'(0)), then removes the known scalar power
of two. It shares no unit-product preparation, batch inverses, or Toeplitz
convolution with the production routine. The action oracle is literal
matrix-vector summation. A stronger Boolean control skips zero columns and
masked rows and performs no multiplication by Boolean bits.

The verifier checks 1,048 packet cases, 12,328 independently prepared
diagonals, 2,096 general vector actions, 149 arbitrary masked actions,
1,048 Boolean masked actions, 1,048 parity-strided actions, 350 unrelated
packed-convolution cases and
10 invalid inputs. It includes signed 60-bit extension parameters, zero and
nonunit parameters, scalar and extension coordinates, p=1 and p=129, and
the entire retained/interpolating grid for small L. Normal and optimized
Python produce identical digests. The finite tests corroborate the
implementation; the all-size proof is the identity and arithmetic argument
above.

Run:

    python3 check_toeplitz.py
    python3 -O check_toeplitz.py
    python3 check_toeplitz.py --benchmark --output benchmarks_rerun.json

Benchmarks are paired five-repeat synthetic subroutine timings. General
ring-vector measurements and the stronger Boolean-mask measurements are
reported separately in benchmarks.json. They compare identical outputs
and arithmetic domains; they do not compare complete SAT solver runtimes.
The generic full-grid convolution is slower than the stronger Boolean
control on the sampled small fixtures; that negative result is retained.
The strided specialization is also measured against this control, without
using a multiplication-heavy Boolean baseline.
