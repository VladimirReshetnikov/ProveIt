SUGGESTED INDEX ENTRY

Adaptive evaluation of the subexponential SAT mode graph. Adaptive quartet
conditioning lowers the common-interface polynomial-space time coefficient
to C_Q = 50.30468921410. A joint covariance objective for block construction
and matrix contraction lowers both cached time and space coefficients to
C_J = 31.25239731266. Coefficients multiply s/sqrt(log2 s), where s counts
normalized Boolean wires. The general-density and fixed-density SAT
logarithmic orders remain unchanged. The packet also proves a conserved
degree-flow identity and identifies its unresolved recursive-representation
requirements. Source review covers twenty committed packets through

IMPORTANT REPRODUCTION DISTINCTIONS

The finite evaluators compute exact mode-graph contractions over nonfields.
The asymptotic cached theorem imports a fast matrix-multiplication backend
through integer coordinates and CRT. The finite prototype deliberately
uses literal final summation to validate algebra and work accounting; it
does not benchmark that backend. No SAT-level speedup was measured.

The new upper-bound functions are smaller by exponential factors on the
same E(s) scale. They do not change the Big-O order of the SAT exponent.
Conditional n/log n targets and algorithms requiring stronger degree
hypotheses remain distinct comparison classes.

The main run_checks.py is the portable replay entry point. Supplementary
checkers are self-contained optional historical calculations; their output
paths are beside those scripts. Run verify_manifest.py before rebuilding
or editing, since regenerating a PDF may change its binary metadata.
