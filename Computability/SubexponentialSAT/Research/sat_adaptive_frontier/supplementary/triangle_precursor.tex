\section{Adaptive matrix multiplication by balanced partition covariance}
\label{sec:adaptive-mm}

We use the exact mode-graph identity~\eqref{eq:mode-graph} and the
uniform entropy norm of Lemma~\ref{lem:norm}. Write $K(M,u)=\K(M,u)$
in this section. The inherited pair-index and matrix-arithmetic interface
is developed in the incoming contraction-tree report~\cite{incomingtree}.
\begin{equation}\label{eq:adaptive-norm}
 \|h\|_2\le(1/r+o(1))K(M,u).
\end{equation}
The new step chooses the three final blocks by their actual index sizes.
The earlier adaptive packet minimized their total crossing weight and
summed it directly.  Here the chosen objective is the cost of rectangular
matrix multiplication.  The coefficient improvement is not obtained by
assuming equal actual dimensions.

\begin{lemma}[Covariance bound for adaptive triangle contraction]
\label{lem:adaptive-mm}
Let $r=3k$ with $k\ge2$, and let $h_{gh}\ge0$ be arbitrary directed pair
weights.  Put $H=\sum_{g\ne h}h_{gh}$ and $N=r(r-1)$.
For an equipartition $\mathcal P=(A,B,C)$ into blocks of size $k$, write
$H_{AB},H_{AC},H_{BC}$ for the sums on the directed pairs joining the
indicated blocks.  For $0\le\delta\le1$ define
\[
 F_\delta(\mathcal P)=H_{AB}+H_{AC}+H_{BC}
            -\delta\min(H_{AB},H_{AC},H_{BC}).
\]
There is an equipartition for which
\begin{equation}\label{eq:adaptive-mm-general}
 F_\delta(\mathcal P)
 \le \sqrt{A_\delta^2N+B_\delta^2}\,\|h\|_2,
 \quad
 A_\delta=\left(1-\frac\delta3\right)\frac{2k}{r-1},
 \quad B_\delta^2=\frac{4\delta^2}{3}
             \frac{2k^3}{(r-1)(r-2)}.
\end{equation}
For $\delta=3/5$, equations~\eqref{eq:adaptive-norm} and
\eqref{eq:adaptive-mm-general} give local coefficient
\begin{equation}\label{eq:adaptive-triangle-coefficient}
 \beta_{\triangle}(3k)^2
 =\frac{8k(8k-5)}{25(3k-1)(3k-2)}
\end{equation}
on $K(M,u)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Combine opposite directed weights into undirected weights
$x_{\{g,h\}}=h_{gh}+h_{hg}$.  Choose a uniformly random labelled
partition into equal blocks.  Its three crossing classes have total
$S=H_{AB}+H_{AC}+H_{BC}$ and variance sum
\[
 V=\sum_{J\in\{AB,AC,BC\}}(H_J-S/3)^2.
\]
The mean crossing sum is $\mathbb ES=2kH/(r-1)$.
Also $\min_JH_J\ge S/3-\sqrt{2V/3}$, since three deviations sum to
zero.  Jensen's inequality therefore gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:adaptive-jensen}
 \mathbb EF_\delta\le A_\delta H+
                         \delta\sqrt{\frac23\mathbb EV}.
\end{equation}

For an undirected edge $e$, set
$Y_e=\mathbf1_{e\text{ joins }A,B}-\mathbf1_{e\text{ joins }A,C}$.
By symmetry among the three classes,
$\mathbb EV=\mathbb E(H_{AB}-H_{AC})^2$.
The covariances $\mathbb EY_eY_f$ depend only on whether the edges are
equal, share one endpoint, or are disjoint.  Counting ordered colors of
the two, three, or four endpoints gives, respectively,
\[
 c_0=\frac{4k^2}{r(r-1)},\qquad
 c_1=\frac{2k^2(k-2)}{r(r-1)(r-2)},\qquad
 c_2=-\frac{8k^2(k-1)}{r(r-1)(r-2)(r-3)}.
\]
Here the equal-block restriction is used; independent vertex coloring
would have different covariances.

Let $m=\binom r2$, center $x$ by $x'_e=x_e-H/m$, and put
$d'_v=\sum_{e\ni v}x'_e$.  The covariance matrix kills the constant
vector because every partition has the same number $k^2$ of edges in
each of the classes $AB$ and $AC$.  Expanding by the three edge-pair
types gives
\[
 \mathbb EV=(c_0-2c_1+c_2)\sum_e(x'_e)^2
                   +(c_1-c_2)\sum_v(d'_v)^2.
\]
Because $\sum_ex'_e=\sum_vd'_v=0$, Cauchy--Schwarz yields
\[
 \sum_v(d'_v)^2
 =\sum_{\{v,w\}}x'_{vw}(d'_v+d'_w)
 \le\|x'\|_2\sqrt{(r-2)\sum_v(d'_v)^2},
\]
and hence $\sum_v(d'_v)^2\le(r-2)\|x'\|_2^2$.
Since $c_1-c_2\ge0$ for $k\ge2$, it follows that
\[
 \mathbb EV\le\Lambda\left(\|x\|_2^2-\frac{H^2}{m}\right),
 \qquad
 \Lambda=c_0+(r-4)c_1-(r-3)c_2
        =\frac{2k^3}{(r-1)(r-2)}.
\]
Finally $\|x\|_2^2\le2\|h\|_2^2$, so
\[
 \mathbb EV\le2\Lambda\left(\|h\|_2^2-\frac{H^2}{N}\right).
\]
Insert this into~\eqref{eq:adaptive-jensen} and apply Cauchy--Schwarz to
$A_\delta H+B_\delta\sqrt{\|h\|_2^2-H^2/N}$.  The result is
\eqref{eq:adaptive-mm-general}.  The minimum partition cost is no larger
than its mean.  Substitution of $\delta=3/5$, $r=3k$, and simplification
give~\eqref{eq:adaptive-triangle-coefficient}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact algorithm and ring arithmetic.}
For each of the finitely many equipartitions, compute the integer class
dimensions $D_{AB},D_{AC},D_{BC}$ from the exact list sizes.  Choose one
minimizing the rational score
\[
 \frac{(D_{AB}D_{AC}D_{BC})^5}
      {\min(D_{AB},D_{AC},D_{BC})^3}.
\]
The logarithm of this score is $5F_{3/5}$.  Comparisons therefore use
exact integers with polynomial bit length, not approximate logarithms.
There are $280$ unlabelled equipartitions for $r=9$ and $5775$ for $r=12$;
these are constants.  Build the chosen block tables by binary matrix
merges as in the cached-tree packet, and contract their triangle using
matrix multiplication of exponent $12/5$.  The integer-coordinate/CRT bridge from the incoming report makes this
valid over the actual power-of-two quotient ring~\cite{incomingtree}.
Here is the arithmetic reason. The ring is a free module of polynomial
rank over $\mathbb Z/2^p\mathbb Z$, with a polynomial-size integer
multiplication table. Lift input coordinates to integers and multiply each
coordinate pair of matrices modulo sufficiently many nonexceptional odd
primes. Reconstruct their integer products by CRT, apply the structure
constants, and reduce modulo $2^p$. An unreduced coordinate product has
bit length $O(p+\log d)$, where $d$ is an inner dimension; this is
polynomial in $s$. Thus only polynomial bit overhead is incurred. A fixed
rational bilinear algorithm of exponent below $12/5$ exists by the
established field result~\cite{mm}; excluding its finitely many
constant-denominator primes makes its identities valid in these fields.
Computing recursive products sequentially bounds temporary storage by a
polynomial factor times the input and output tables. This argument never
inverts two or a nilpotent in the source quotient ring.

\subsection{Paid block construction and child evaluation}
The adaptive partition does not make table construction free.  Cache all
$r$ group functions, evaluating every entry by a real recursive child
contraction.  Their incident-pair coefficient is
$a_r=\sqrt{2(r-1)}/r$.  To merge blocks of sizes $a,b$, with $q=a+b$,
the three matrix index classes have cardinalities
\[
 2a(r-q),\quad 2ab,\quad 2b(r-q).
\]
The inherited weighted entropy bound and the $12/5$ backend give squared
numerator $v_1+v_2+v_3-(21/25)\min(v_1,v_2,v_3)$.
All these bounds hold for every chosen partition.

For $r=9$ use blocks $3,3,3$.  The $1+2$ merge has classes $12,4,24$,
so its squared numerator is $916/25$.  The smaller $1+1$ merge has
$708/25$, and the adaptive final triangle has squared coefficient
$57/175$, strictly smaller than $916/2025$.  For $r=12$ use blocks
$4,4,4$; the largest $2+2$ merge has classes $32,8,32$ and squared
numerator $1632/25$, while the adaptive triangle has squared coefficient
$432/1375$.  Consequently the complete local recurrence is
\[
 T_d\le\operatorname{poly}(s)\left(
       2^{(a_{r_d}+o(1))K_d}T_{d+1}
       +2^{(b_{r_d}+o(1))K_d}\right),
\]
with
\[
 a_9=\frac49,\quad b_9=\frac{2\sqrt{229}}{45},\quad j_9=\frac23;
 \qquad
 a_{12}=\frac{\sqrt{22}}{12},\quad
 b_{12}=\frac{\sqrt{102}}{15},\quad j_{12}=\frac23.
\]
Here $j_r$ is the largest storage coefficient on $K_d$; output boundary
sizes are $36$ and $64$ respectively.  The incoming sorting/streaming
layout and coordinate bridge pay polynomial bit overhead per record.

\subsection{A full schedule and its certified coefficient}
Put $\lambda=\lceil\log_2s\rceil$, $g=\lceil\sqrt\lambda\rceil$,
$b=\lceil6\log_2\lambda\rceil$, and define
\[
 B=\left\lfloor\frac{\lambda-g}{20\log_2 9}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad v=\left\lfloor\frac56\left(\frac\lambda{36}-B\right)\right\rfloor.
\]
The first floor can be computed exactly by the largest integer $B$ with
$9^{20B}\le2^{\lambda-g}$; no transcendental test is required.  Set
\[
 r_0=9,\quad u_0=B,\qquad
 r_d=12,\quad u_d=\lfloor v/6^{d-1}\rfloor\ (d\ge1),\qquad
 a_d=\sum_{j\le d}(u_j+b),\quad H=\lceil s/\lambda^{3/4}\rceil.
\]
Then $\sum_du_d\le B+6v/5\le\lambda/36$.
At the root, the symmetric packing term $Q_0^2\Delta$ has binary
logarithm at most $\lambda-g/10+O(\log\lambda)$.  Below it the
retained-side term $Q_d\Delta$ has exponent at most
$9/10+\log_2(12)/36<1$ in $s$, and the fresh-side and padding terms
have exponent at most $(2\log_2(12)+1)/36<1$.  For an exact rational
certificate, $12^{100}<2^{359}$ leaves margin $1/3600$ in the former
inequality.  Fresh packet degrees have exponent at most $1/36+o(1)$.

As in the incoming cached proof, consider hypothetical active depths up
to $\tfrac34\log_5\lambda+O(1)$.  The retained ranges satisfy
$u_d\ge\lambda^{\eta+o(1)}$, where
$\eta=1-3\ln6/(4\ln5)>0$; the latter is certified by $6^3<5^4$.
The packing gates hold uniformly there, and give a coarse tail shrinkage
of $1/5$, closing the depth bootstrap.  The sharp sizes are
\[
 M_0\le s,\qquad M_d\le(1+o(1))^d\frac{2s}{9\,6^{d-1}}\quad(d\ge1).
\]
The buffer gives $D\ge2^{a_{d-1}+b+1}$ and absorbs inherited precision:
the source bound $O(s\lambda^2 2^{a_{d-1}})$ is
$o(MD/u)$ since $s/M\le\lambda^{3/4}$ and
$\lambda^{15/4}2^{-b}\to0$.  Thus all local entropy bounds are uniform.

Let
\[
 \kappa=\frac{\pi\sqrt2}{\ln2},\quad
 A=\frac1{20\log_2 9},\quad
 W=\frac56\left(\frac1{36}-A\right),\quad
 K_0^*=\frac\kappa{\sqrt A},\quad K_1^*=\frac{2\kappa}{9\sqrt W},
 \quad\gamma=\frac1{\sqrt6}.
\]
The root $K$ coefficient is $K_0^*$, and the tail coefficients are bounded
by $(1+o(1))K_1^*\gamma^{d-1}$.  Expanding the paid recurrence yields
\begin{align*}
 C_T&=\max\left\{b_9K_0^*,\quad
      a_9K_0^*+K_1^*\max\left(b_{12},\frac{a_{12}}{1-\gamma}\right)\right\}\\
 &=\max\{34.32528279728\ldots,\ 32.27135203916\ldots\}
  =34.32528279728\ldots,\\
 C_S&=\max\{j_9K_0^*,j_{12}K_1^*\}
   =34.02417800749\ldots.
\end{align*}
Ancestor table sizes add rather than multiply.  Polynomial per-level
factors contribute only $O(\lambda\log\lambda)$ to the exponent.
Leaves, original entries, and fresh packet routines have respective
local work $2^{O(s/\lambda^{3/4})}$, $2^{O(s^{9/10})}$, and
$\exp(s^{1/36+o(1)})$, all lower order after the paid child recurrence.
For bounded exceptional inputs or failed explicit parameter gates use
direct contraction.  We conclude, relative to the inherited interface,
\[
 T(s,\mathcal L)\le\operatorname{poly}(s+\mathcal L)
 2^{(34.32528279728\ldots+o(1))s/\sqrt{\log_2s}},\qquad
 S(s,\mathcal L)\le\operatorname{poly}(s+\mathcal L)
 2^{(34.02417800749\ldots+o(1))s/\sqrt{\log_2s}}.
\]
In particular $34.326$ and $34.025$ are safe eventual coefficients.
These improve the incoming $35.3545880584\ldots$ time and
$34.2748030050\ldots$ space certificates, while retaining subexponential
space.  The logarithmic order of the SAT bound is unchanged.
