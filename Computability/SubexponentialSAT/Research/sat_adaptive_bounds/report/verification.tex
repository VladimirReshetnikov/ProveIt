\section{Verification, deliverables, and integration}
\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{What the finite checks establish}

The accompanying programs use only the Python standard library.
Every verification gate raises an explicit exception and remains active
under \texttt{python -O}. Normal and optimized runs give the same
mathematical results; only elapsed-time metadata may differ.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Flag combinatorics.} The checker enumerates all complete
flags at $r=4,8,16,32$ and verifies the path sizes and uniform
common-origin average exactly. It checks 798,192 exhaustive
relative-leaf intersection cases for $r\le16$, and 64,000 further
seeded cases at $r=32$. The latter are consistency tests, not an
exhaustive proof for that size.

\item \textbf{Adaptive flag evaluation.} Nine integer-weight profiles
exercise exact tree selection, including an extremal profile attaining
the balanced-tree entropy bound. Fifteen small contractions over
$\mathbb Z/8\mathbb Z$ compare streamed conditioning against full
enumeration, covering a ring with zero divisors.

\item \textbf{Cached partitions.} The checker enumerates all 280
partitions of nine groups into triples, and all 126,126 partitions
of fifteen groups into three blocks of five. Every undirected edge
crosses exactly 210 and 90,090 of these partitions, respectively.
It checks 64 arbitrary nonnegative integer-weight instances of the
centered triangle estimate over all 280 nine-group partitions.

\item \textbf{Exact local and global certificates.} Every merge score,
boundary count, rational schedule identity, recurrence-dominance
inequality, and displayed strict packing inequality is checked.
Coefficient bounds use rational enclosures for $\pi$, logarithms,
and square roots, independently of decimal approximations.
In particular the flag constant lies in
$[46.184845586730,46.184845586731]$.

\item \textbf{Independent audit.} A separate review checked the new
proofs, their dependencies, and the charged recursive costs.
Its exact-arithmetic spot checks and written review are included.
\end{itemize}

The finite programs do not prove the asymptotic analytic estimates by
exhaustion. Those proofs are in the text. They also do not recheck the
entire upstream deformation, Hermite recovery, SAT reductions, or the
fast rational matrix algorithm. No new formal Lean or Rocq build, full
SAT implementation, or SAT benchmark is claimed.

\subsection{Reproduction}

From the archive's top directory, run
\begin{verbatim}
python3 run_checks.py
python3 build_report.py
python3 verify_manifest.py
\end{verbatim}
The first command runs both new mathematical checkers normally and under
optimization, compares their results after excluding timing metadata,
and writes a new verification summary. The second uses
\texttt{pdflatex} twice; the third checks the delivered file hashes.
For an untouched archive, run the manifest check first. Rebuilding the
PDF or rerunning timed checks can change its delivered hashes without
changing the mathematical content.

The complete report is \path{report/report.tex}, with its included
section sources and compiled \path{report/report.pdf}.
The \path{code/} directory contains the checkers, \path{results/}
their outputs, \path{audit/} the independent review, and
\path{provenance/} the reviewed source identities, arrival commits,
and closing relocation check. No large upstream manuscripts or
duplicate input ZIPs are required in the deliverable.

\subsection{Suggested repository integration}

The packet can be filed under
\path{Computability/SubexponentialSAT/Research/eth_adaptive_bounds/}.
Its new interface-compatible modules are the centered adaptive
triangle estimate, recursively built cached tables with the two
certified schedules, and adaptive linear-flag streamed separators.
The integration note identifies their imports and recommended README
claims.

Preserve the resource distinctions in any repository summary.
The polynomial-space coefficient is $46.1848456$; the faster general
cached branch is $34.3252828$ time with $34.0241780$ space;
the $13.3796491$ time coefficient requires the lower root degree.
The worst-case SAT logarithmic order and the conditional status of
the denominator-$\log n$ target remain unchanged.
