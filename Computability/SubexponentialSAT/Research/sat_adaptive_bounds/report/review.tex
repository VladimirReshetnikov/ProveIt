\subsection{Three bounds that improve the reviewed frontier}

Write
\[
 E(s)=\frac{s}{\sqrt{\log_2s}}.
\]
All numerical coefficients in Table~\ref{tab:outcomes} multiply $E(s)$
in a base-two exponent, with a multiplicative polynomial in input size and
an additive $o(E(s))$ exponent. The main general interface has root
degree at most $\ceil{s^{9/10}}$; the last row has a stronger premise.

The decreases in the respective \emph{time coefficients} are approximately
$17.10\%$, $2.91\%$, and $13.07\%$. They are not empirical speedups.
For example, the new and old general cached certificate functions have
ratio
\[
 2^{-(1.02930526+o(1))E(s)}\longrightarrow0.
\]
This establishes an asymptotic separation between the displayed upper-bound
functions. It does not establish a lower bound on any older implementation's
actual running time.

The new general cached space coefficient is also below the previous
\emph{fastest} cached branch's $34.2748030$. Other slower branches have
smaller space coefficients; the full time--space frontier is not reduced
to a single point.

\subsection{What is proved here and what is inherited}

The new arguments prove adaptive summation bounds and their schedules.
They inherit the source's exact deformation, local integrality, Hermite
recovery, creation-precision convention, and SAT reductions. They also
inherit the reviewed asymmetric packing and full-orientation Cauchy
factorization. Section~\ref{sec:interface} states the precise interface.
This report does not independently reprove the entire upstream ETH result,
run the upstream Lean development, or supply an end-to-end SAT solver.

The phrase \emph{strongest established bound} in this review means a
displayed bound supported by a mathematical argument in the reviewed
packets, relative to their declared imports. The repository itself labels
the packets external and unchecked in ProveIt. We preserve that distinction.
The unproved algorithmic hypotheses of earlier packets are not promoted
to established algorithms by including them in a comparison.

\subsection{The comparison envelope}

Taking the best compatible \emph{proved claims} across the corpus leaves
the general SAT order
\[
 \poly_k(n,m)\,
 2^{O_k(n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n})}
\]
and the fixed-density order
$\poly_{k,D}(n,m)2^{O_{k,D}(n/\sqrt{\log n})}$.
The density-sensitive refinement remains available by taking the better
applicable algorithm. The best displayed general polynomial-space and
cached contraction coefficients before this report are the first two
entries in Table~\ref{tab:outcomes}.

The conditional $n\log\log n/\log n$ proposal is asymptotically smaller
than every constant refinement here, but its needed summation procedure
is absent. We have not proved that hypothesis or found a valid replacement.
Consequently our result improves the supported normalized contraction
envelope; it does not dominate that conditional target.
