PROOF STATUS AND DEPENDENCIES

1. IMPORTED RESULTS

The report relies on the OpenAI preprint at the commit recorded in
source_manifest.json for:

  - its Boolean tensor-contraction theorem, with time
    poly(s+description_length) * 2^{O(s/sqrt(log s))};
  - its total-order parity-lift identity and associated incidence-degree
    bound K = k(a+2n);
  - its monomial-to-tensor-network representation and tensor-entry formulas;
  - its general-density exact #k-SAT counter for the high-density branch.

It relies on the classical Margulis-Gabber-Galil spectral theorem for the
explicit expander family. The cited Isabelle AFP theory supplies the graph
definition and an independently accessible formal spectral proof.

The original preprint's full proof/formalization and the AFP session were
not rebuilt or independently reverified in this task.

2. DERIVATIONS IN THIS REPORT

  - Uniform sparse-system count depending on actual monomial bound S,
    with time poly(n) * 2^{O(n log(S+2)/sqrt(log n))}.
  - Explicit powered-lazy-expander checks whose support is polynomial in
    density B, with no log n term in that support bound.
  - Exact local-code existence, row budget, distance, padding and shortening
    bounds, with fully integer parameter selection.
  - Greedy elimination as an oracle reduction and its uniform integration
    with the sparse-system counter.
  - The density-dependent hybrid time bound and polynomial-space accounting.
  - A combinatorial obstruction to improving the mode count using only its
    weight and ordinary-degree budgets. This is not a computational lower bound.

These are mathematical arguments in the LaTeX/PDF. They have received
independent adversarial checks within this research task. They have not
been mechanized in Lean, Rocq, or Isabelle, and have not undergone external
peer review.

3. EXECUTED FINITE COMPONENT CHECKS

All passed using exact integer or rational arithmetic:

  3,248 inverse-port identities on lazy MGG graphs, sides 2 through 8;
  4 exact rational spectral certificates, sides 2 through 5;
  5 exhaustive small local-code searches;
  156 shortening and unique zero-extension checks;
  2,047 subset rank/unrank cases, all N <= 10;
  30,000 wire and incidence-encoding inequalities;
  72 greedy parity rounds;
  1,000 exact parameter cases, B = 1,...,1000;
  27,696 padding and row-budget checks.

The script does not instantiate the enormous theorem-sized expander degrees.
It computes their integer parameters symbolically as arbitrary-precision
integers. The toy code examples are clearly separated from the asymptotic
parameter choices. No component test is presented as a proof for all sizes.

4. IMPLEMENTATION STATUS

The archive contains reference routines and finite checks for the new
components. It does not contain a complete implementation of the source
tensor-contraction engine or of the entire subexponential #k-SAT solver.
No measured speedup or realistic crossover size has been established.

5. EXACT SCOPE OF THE IMPROVEMENT

At m = Theta(n log log n), the exponent improves from
O(n log log n/sqrt(log n)) to O(n log log log n/sqrt(log n)), with k fixed.
More generally it improves the source's combined upper bounds whenever
B tends to infinity and log B = o(log log n).

The unrestricted worst-case n-only exponent remains
O(n log log n/sqrt(log n)). The fixed-density exponent remains
O(n/sqrt(log n)). Further improvement of either is an open target of this
work, not an established result.
