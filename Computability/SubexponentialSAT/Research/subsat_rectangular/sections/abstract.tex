\begin{abstract}
We review ProveIt at commit \pin: twenty SubexponentialSAT

manuscripts. We obtain smaller explicit time upper bounds in both polynomial-space and

the common normalized Boolean tensor-contraction problem. Let
\[
 \Escale(s)=\frac{s}{\sqrt{\lceil\log_2s\rceil}},\qquad
 C_S=\frac{\pi}{\ln2}\sqrt{\frac{2812500}{49997}},\qquad
 C_T=\left(1+\frac1{200000}\right)C_S.
\]
Then, relative to the imported contraction identities and oracle interface,

\[
 (s+\mathcal L)^{O(1)}2^{(C_T+o(1))\Escale(s)}
 \quad\hbox{time},\qquad
 (s+\mathcal L)^{O(1)}2^{(C_S+o(1))\Escale(s)}
 \quad\hbox{space},
\]

The improvement combines a genuine rectangular matrix bound with a
fully charged multistage cache recurrence. It includes the cost of
constructing every child and block table, and uses exact arithmetic over
the actual power-of-two quotient rings.

This is an improvement of an exponential coefficient, not of the
logarithmic order. We do not establish a smaller unrestricted SAT order
than $n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n}$, or close any of the conditional
