\section{An arity-uniform barrier for the full-table certificate model}
\label{sec:cached-tree-barrier}

The following result identifies a limit of the certificate used above, not
a lower bound for SAT. It is useful because changing grouping arities or
choosing a contraction tree adaptively might otherwise appear to evade the
weighted-partition obstruction.

Consider the abstract leading entropy relaxation on the $r(r-1)$ directed
pairs of $r\ge3$ groups,
\[
 \mathcal H_r(K)=\{h\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{r(r-1)}:
                       \|h\|_2\le K/r\}.
\]
This is the norm-ball envelope corresponding to the common-tilt
Cauchy--Schwarz certificates; it intentionally forgets more detailed
realizability constraints of actual mode lists. A full table for a subset
$B$ of $k$ groups has logarithmic size
\[
 L_B(h)=\sum_{e\in\partial B}h_e,\qquad
 |\partial B|=2k(r-k).
\]
A full-table binary contraction tree stores the prescribed table for each
merged subset. The model includes the binary table-building schemes and
their final triangle contraction used in the reviewed packets.

\begin{proposition}[Fixed and adaptive table certificates]
\label{prop:cached-tree-barrier}
For every fixed full-table binary tree $\mathcal T$,
\[
 \sup_{h\in\mathcal H_r(K)}\ \max_{B\in\mathcal T}L_B(h)
        \ge\frac23K.
\]
Even if the tree is chosen after $h$ is known,
\[
 \sup_{h\in\mathcal H_r(K)}\ \min_{\mathcal T}\
            \max_{B\in\mathcal T}L_B(h)
 \ge\frac{4}{9\sqrt{1-1/r}}K\ge\frac49K.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Every full binary tree on $r$ leaves has a node with leaf count
$k\in[r/3,2r/3]$. One proof starts at the root and descends while a child
has more than $2r/3$ leaves. At the last such node its children both have
at most $2r/3$ leaves, and at least one has at least $r/3$ leaves.
For that node, $2k(r-k)\ge4r^2/9$. The support function of the nonnegative
norm ball gives
\[
 \sup_{h\in\mathcal H_r(K)}L_B(h)
       =\frac Kr\sqrt{2k(r-k)}\ge\frac23K,
\]
proving the fixed-tree statement.

That argument cannot interchange the supremum over $h$ with the minimum
over adaptively chosen trees. For the adaptive statement instead use the
single vector
\[
 h_e=\frac{K}{r\sqrt{r(r-1)}}\quad\hbox{for every directed pair }e.
\]
It belongs to $\mathcal H_r(K)$ and works simultaneously against every
tree. At each tree's balanced node,
\[
 L_B(h)\ge\frac{4r^2}{9}\frac{K}{r\sqrt{r(r-1)}}
       =\frac{4K}{9\sqrt{1-1/r}}.
\]
\end{proof}

The reviewed root packing certificate requires
\[
 2a_0\log_2r+\frac9{10}\log_2s
     \le(1-o(1))\log_2s,\qquad u_0\le a_0.
\]
Consequently, within that certificate,
\[
 u_0\le\left(\frac1{20}+o(1)\right)
                  \frac{\log_2s}{\log_2r},\qquad
 K(s,u_0)=\Omega\left(
          \frac{s\sqrt{\log r}}{\sqrt{\log s}}\right).
\]
Proposition~\ref{prop:cached-tree-barrier} therefore prevents a worst-case
$o(s/\sqrt{\log s})$ certificate from full-table tree selection, arity
retuning, or a faster matrix backend alone. The constants are uniform in
$r$. This remains an obstruction in the idealized real entropy model even
if one grants growing arities and ignores their extra budget-enumeration
costs.

The statement is deliberately scoped. It does not claim that every point
of $\mathcal H_r(K)$ is realized by an actual crossing network, that every
instance materializes a table of this size, or that the packing inequality
is necessary for all conceivable packing algorithms. It is a lower bound
on this relaxed certificate model. Additional constraints on real mode
lists, a nonmaterializing summation method, or a different packing or SAT
representation may evade it. It supplies no complexity lower bound for
SAT or for general tensor contraction.

\paragraph{The newly filed streamed separator branch.}
The recursive-conditioning certificate in Section~\ref{sec:streamed-new}
also retains this order, by a separate elementary calculation. Its root cut
alone gives
\[
 v(r)\ge2\lfloor r/2\rfloor\lceil r/2\rceil
       \ge\frac{r^2-1}{2},\qquad
 \beta(r)=\frac{\sqrt{v(r)}}r
       \ge\sqrt{\frac{1-r^{-2}}2}\ge\frac23\quad(r\ge3).
\]
Consequently its local subset-entropy certificate is a positive uniform
multiple of $K(s,u_0)$ even when the arity changes. This does not extend the
full-table proposition to all streaming algorithms; it verifies the same
order obstruction for this particular balanced recursive-conditioning
profile. An exact summation method exploiting additional structure remains
outside both scoped arguments.
