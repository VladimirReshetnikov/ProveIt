\section{A polynomial-space coefficient below 54}
\label{sec:streamed-new}

A closing repository check added the packet \path{sat_adaptive_contraction}~\cite{adaptive}.
Its recursive-conditioning result changes the polynomial-space comparison
frontier. The factor $\sqrt{17/24}$ in that packet reduces the number of
paid child evaluations for streamed summation at arity sixteen; it is not
a uniform improvement of the pair entropy bound and cannot multiply the
cached coefficient from Section~\ref{sec:rectangular-new}. We instead compose
its proved streamed identity with a new multistage retained-range schedule.

\subsection{The appropriate composed comparator}

The new packet's displayed polynomial-space coefficient is
$55.70947831890997\ldots$. Combining its conditioning identity with the
previously filed \path{subexponential_sat_frontier} schedule~\cite{frontier} already gives
the stronger bound $55.40466590661055\ldots$. That prior schedule has arities
$(13,14,8,6,4,4,\ldots)$ and fractions
\[
 \frac{27}{2000},\quad\frac{51}{4000},\quad
 \frac{141}{20000},\quad\frac{107}{20000},\quad
 \frac{27}{6400}\left(\frac58\right)^j\quad(j\ge0).
\]
We use the stronger composed comparator below. Neither it nor our new
schedule is asserted to optimize all possible prior parameters.

\subsection{Stream exact pair lists using balanced separators}

Retain the exact pair-budget arrays and the inherited entropy scale
\[
 K(M,u)=\kappa\frac M{\sqrt u},\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{\pi\sqrt2}{\ln2}.
\]
Fix a balanced binary tree on the $r$ groups. At a split $B=B_1\sqcup B_2$,
enumerate the pair indices crossing the split, recursively sum the two sides
conditional on these indices, and multiply the conditional answers.
Ancestor indices remain fixed parameters. Finite distributivity proves the
identity: after fixing crossing indices, no factor in one side depends on
an internal index of the other side. A singleton evaluates its group factor
by a genuine recursive tensor contraction.

The sets of cut pairs on a root-to-leaf path are disjoint. If their union
is $E_\ell$, the number of paid group-factor evaluations is at most
\[
 \sum_{\ell=1}^r\prod_{e\in E_\ell}|\mathcal I_e|.
\]
Sibling internal sums add to this work rather than multiplying through a
leaf evaluation. The largest cardinality of $E_\ell$ is
\[
 v(1)=0,\qquad
 v(r)=2\lfloor r/2\rfloor\lceil r/2\rceil+
       \max\{v(\lfloor r/2\rfloor),v(\lceil r/2\rceil)\}.
\]
Thus, with
\[
 \beta(r)=\frac{\sqrt{v(r)}}r,
\]
the inherited subset-entropy estimate gives the fully paid recurrence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:streamed-new-recurrence}
 T_i\le\operatorname{poly}(s)\,
       2^{(\beta(r_i)+o(1))K_i}T_{i+1}.
\end{equation}
This is the new packet's proved separator identity, applied at the arities
below. It uses no field multiplication algorithm or cached exponential table.
The separator assignment ranks, finite-ring accumulators and recursion
frames have polynomial total size. All expensive group evaluations remain
explicitly charged.

\subsection{A strict multistage schedule}

Let $\lambda=\lceil\log_2s\rceil$, $H=\lceil s/\lambda\rceil$,
$b=\lceil6\log_2\lambda\rceil$, and
\[
 u_i=\lfloor f_i\lambda\rfloor,\qquad
 a_i=\sum_{t=0}^i(u_t+b).
\]
Choose
\[
 (r_0,r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4)=(16,16,8,6,5),\qquad
 r_{5+j}=4\quad(j\ge0),
\]
with
\begin{align*}
 f_0=f_1&=\frac{12499}{1000000},&
 f_2&=\frac{2083}{250000},&
 f_3&=\frac{107}{20000},\\
 f_4&=\frac{219}{50000},&
 f_{5+j}&=\frac{513}{200000}\left(\frac58\right)^j.
\end{align*}
The leading cumulative capacities at the first five levels and in the tail
are exactly
\[
 0.012499,\quad0.024998,\quad0.033330,\quad
 0.038680,\quad0.043060,\quad A_\infty=0.049900.
\]
All displayed decimals are terminating rational numbers, not numerical
approximations used in place of inequalities.

The root high-degree packing constraint is
$9/10+2A_0\log_2r_0<1$. At later calls the old-side constraint is
$9/10+A_i\log_2r_i<1$. Using the exact integer comparisons
\[
 6^{200}<2^{517},\qquad5^{500}<2^{1161},
\]
the six regime bounds are, respectively,
\[
 \frac{124999}{125000},\quad
 \frac{124999}{125000},\quad
 \frac{99999}{100000},\quad
 \frac{4999939}{5000000},\quad
 \frac{24999633}{25000000},\quad
 \frac{4999}{5000}.
\]
Every one is strictly below one. The fresh-side and packet-padding
exponents are bounded by $(2\log_2r_i+1)A_i<1/4$ in all six regimes.
New degrees are at most $s^{0.0499+o(1)}$, below the maintained
$s^{9/10}$ bound. Consequently all packing gates tolerate the additive
buffers and any fixed polylogarithmic slack.

As before, first check all hypothetical active levels through
$5+\lceil\log_{20/11}\lambda\rceil+O(1)$. The smallest taper in this range
is at least $\lambda^{\eta+o(1)}$, with
\[
 \eta=1-\frac{\ln(8/5)}{\ln(20/11)}>0.
\]
All arities are at least four, so nearly lossless padding implies eventual
child shrinkage at most $11/20$, closing the depth bootstrap. Padding may be
chosen with relative error $O(\lambda^{-2})$, whose accumulation tends to
zero over this depth. The sharper limiting wire masses are
\[
 (m_0,m_1,m_2,m_3,m_4,m_5)=
 \left(1,\frac18,\frac1{64},\frac1{256},
           \frac1{768},\frac1{1920}\right),
 \qquad m_{5+j}=m_5\,2^{-j}.
\]
The six cut profiles are
\[
 (v(16),v(16),v(8),v(6),v(5),v(4))
       =(170,170,42,24,18,10).
\]
The same precision guard gives inherited-to-local precision ratio
$O(\lambda^4 2^{-b})=o(1)$, preserving the coefficient-six precision budget
and the common-tilt entropy estimate uniformly.

\subsection{Exact summation of the new certificate}

Unroll \eqref{eq:streamed-new-recurrence}. With
$\gamma=(1/2)/\sqrt{5/8}=\sqrt{2/5}$, the resulting coefficient is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:streamed-new-coefficient}
 C_{\rm ps}=\kappa\left[
 \sum_{i=0}^4\frac{m_i\beta(r_i)}{\sqrt{f_i}}
 +\frac{m_5\beta(4)}{\sqrt{f_5}(1-\gamma)}
 \right]
 =53.97753119806873\ldots.
\end{equation}
Its six contributions are approximately
\[
 46.7203678473,\quad5.8400459809,\quad0.8888315523,\quad
 0.2794968539,\quad0.1070058158,\quad0.1417831479.
\]
The accompanying standard-library script verifies the profile recursion,
wire masses, capacities, every strict packing gate, and rational interval
bounds for all square roots, $\pi$, and $\ln2$. It proves
\[
 53.97753119806<C_{\rm ps}<53.97753119808<54
      <55.40466590660.
\]
These finite interval checks certify the arithmetic in the all-size proof;
they do not stand in for the imported contraction identities.

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial-space multistage conditioning]
\label{thm:streamed-new}
Under the normalized tensor interface and imported identities, the above
streamed algorithm has
\[
 T(s,\mathcal L)\le(s+\mathcal L)^{O(1)}
       2^{(C_{\rm ps}+o(1))s/\sqrt{\log_2s}},
 \qquad C_{\rm ps}=53.97753119806873\ldots,
\]
and polynomial space. In particular, 54 is a valid eventual time
coefficient.
\end{theorem}

The proof's remaining costs are unchanged: direct leaves take
$2^{O(s/\lambda)}$, initial entry routines take $2^{O(s^{9/10})}$, and fresh
packet routines take $\exp(s^{0.0499+o(1)})$. All are lower order after
charging the ancestor separator enumerations. Multiplicity polynomials
depend on $s$ alone and contribute $O(\lambda\log\lambda)$ to the exponent
across the active depths. A single fixed polynomial in the description
length pays for processing at each visited call. Depth-first separator
summation and tensor recursion retain polynomial space throughout.

The new coefficient is about $3.11\%$ below the newest packet's displayed
$55.7094783189\ldots$, and about $2.58\%$ below the stronger prior composition
$55.4046659066\ldots$. It complements the cached bound below 34 by improving
the polynomial-space alternative. Both retain the same logarithmic order;
neither proves the unresolved $n/\log n$-type targets or supplies a numerical
coefficient per SAT variable.
