\section{Why the exponent order is still unresolved}
\label{sec:research}

The positive contraction theorem decreases explicit coefficients in
$2^{(C+o(1))\Escale(s)}$. It does not replace $\Escale(s)$ by a smaller
order. The new Hermite theorem and the representation barriers identify
several changes that cannot, on their own, supply that replacement.

\subsection{Precision peeling does not justify a free reset}

The incoming frontier packet already proves precision peeling:
a mode tuple of total guaranteed weight $W<P$ can contribute as
$2^W$ times a product of normalized child contractions modulo $2^{P-W}$.
It also supplies the required creation-time precision caps~\cite{frontier}.
This identity is prior work, not a new contribution here.

A tempting further step is to reset the cumulative packet exponent
because most tuples have small residual precision. The present
upper-bound analysis does not justify that step. If its partition
certificate at budget $P$ is $S(P)=c\sqrt P$ with the other parameters
fixed, then for any fixed $B>0$,
\[
 S\!\left(P-\frac{P}{(\log s)^B}\right)
 =S(P)\sqrt{1-(\log s)^{-B}}
 =(1-o(1))S(P).
\]
Thus the high-residual part, $P-W\ge P/(\log s)^B$, still has the
same leading counting certificate. Meanwhile
\[
 \log_2(P-W)\ge\log_2P-O(\log\log s).
\]
It retains the same leading inherited precision exponent.
That change can recover an additive $O(\log\log s)$ in the packet
exponent, not a new range of order $\log s$.
Typical-residual reasoning or a split into a few fixed fractional
bins misses this obstruction. The calculation concerns the current
certificate; it is not a lower bound on actual evaluation times.
A successful reset would require collective handling or new structure
in that high-residual part.

\subsection{Why binary tuple indices do not close the recursive interface}

The exact-weight mode lists can be ranked and unranked with polynomial
work. This gives a compact bit string describing a mode tuple, but a
group function at fixed grouping arity can still depend on a linear
fraction of all the new bits. Its degree then exceeds the
$s^{9/10}$ entry-oracle premise of the contraction theorem at that
new size. Evaluating such an entry recursively does not split its
ports at low wire cost.

The conditional structured-summation and degree-preserving proposals
correctly isolate this missing factorization~\cite{precision,scalar,analysis}.
Applying the contraction theorem a second time without constructing
it would be circular. The finite rank/unrank dynamic program is
necessary bookkeeping, not the missing algorithm.

\subsection{Research questions with a concrete success criterion}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Construct a small representation of a group function.}
For a compressed mode network with $B$ index bits, exhibit an exact
network with $O(B)$ wires, sublinear maximum degree, and entry evaluation
cost compatible with the second contraction. The representation must
encode the entire group function, including coefficient extraction,
not just its tuple indices. This is the most direct route to the
existing $s/\log s$ conditional target.

\item \textbf{Exploit correlations among attainable Hermite jets.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-hermite} optimizes independent perturbations
of the ordinary recovery blocks. It leaves open the lattice of jet
vectors actually produced by the deformed contraction. Identify a
uniform relation in that lattice and an exact recovery algorithm
whose precision analysis uses it. Moving multiplicities among nodes
alone now has a precise negative answer.

\item \textbf{Avoid materializing formal exact-weight tables.}
The inventory barrier does not prove that their entries are independent,
nonzero, or information-theoretically incompressible. A representation
that supports multiplication and summation without constructing every
formal cell could evade it. The Cauchy kernel's structure must be shown
to survive contraction with arbitrary allowed vertex tensors; that
survival cannot be assumed.

\item \textbf{Remove incidence entropy in the dense reduction.}
The reviewed reduction uses $O(n)$ selected incidences among
$n\,\polylog n$ possible incidences, leading to $O(n\log\log n)$
index wires. A direct $O(n)$-wire representation with the required
oracle bounds would improve the unrestricted SAT order even if the
contraction's square-root logarithm stayed unchanged. Polynomial degree
in incidence variables does not automatically imply low complexity in
the binary rank bits.

\item \textbf{Optimize time and space together under explicit premises.}
The new rectangular and streamed algorithms can coexist with the
lower-degree and lower-memory branches. Further schedule or hybrid
separator/cache optimization may yield smaller coefficients.
Such a result should be reported as a coefficient improvement unless
it actually changes the logarithmic scale.
\end{enumerate}

No new affirmative algorithm for these open interfaces is supplied in
this packet. The established refinements, their imported dependencies,
and the still-open stronger target are recorded separately in
\texttt{PROOF\_STATUS.json}.
