\section{Independent audit of the cached global schedule}

\paragraph{Scope and finding.}
This audit checks the proposed $8,12,8,4,4,\ldots$ schedule against the
inherited asymmetric packing, sharp Hermite precision, full-block Cauchy
modes, and genuine recursive group-entry evaluation interfaces. It finds
the displayed time and space constants consistent with those interfaces.
This is a relative algorithmic proof audit, not a verification of the
complete upstream SAT theorem, a formal proof, or an implemented fast SAT
solver. The exact finite checker accompanying this note verifies the scalar
inequalities and recurrence comparisons described below.

\subsection{Parameters and the equality-boundary reserve}

Put $\lambda=\lceil\log_2s\rceil$,
$\Lambda=\lambda-\lceil\sqrt\lambda\rceil$, and
$b_0=\lceil6\log_2\lambda\rceil$. A fixed exceptional range is handled
directly. Let
\[
 (f_0,f_1,f_2)=\left(\frac1{60},\frac1{90},\frac1{180}\right),
 \qquad f_{3+k}=\frac1{160}\left(\frac58\right)^k,
 \qquad u_d=\lfloor f_d\Lambda\rfloor,
 \qquad a_d=\sum_{j=0}^d u_j+(d+1)b_0.
\]
The alphabets are $r_0=8,r_1=12,r_2=8$, and $r_d=4$ thereafter.
The cumulative leading ranges are exactly
\[
 A_0=\frac1{60},\quad A_1=\frac1{36},\quad
 A_2=\frac1{30},\quad A_\infty=\frac1{20}.
\]
At the root, the limiting symmetric high-degree condition is an equality:
$2A_0\log_2 8+9/10=1$. The retained-side condition at depth two and in
the limiting tail is also an equality:
$A_2\log_2 8+9/10=A_\infty\log_2 4+9/10=1$.
These equalities alone would not prove nearly lossless packing.
The reserve in $\Lambda$ supplies, uniformly through logarithmic depth,
\[
 \log_2\left(\frac{Q_0^2\Delta\lambda^2}{M}\right)
 \le-\frac1{10}\sqrt\lambda+O((\log\lambda)^2),
 \qquad
 \log_2\left(\frac{Q_d\Delta}{M}\right)
 \le-\frac1{10}\sqrt\lambda+O((\log\lambda)^2)
\]
at the equality-boundary regimes, for active $M>s/\lambda$.
Here $Q_d=r_d^{a_d}$ and $\Delta=\lceil s^{9/10}\rceil$.
The depth-one condition has a fixed positive power margin because
$12^5<2^{18}$ implies $A_1\log_2 12<1/10$.

The fresh-side and slot-rounding leading exponents are at most $1/4$:
\[
 2A_d\log_2r_d+A_{d-1}\le\frac14,
 \qquad A_d(1+2\log_2r_d)\le\frac14.
\]
They therefore have substantial margins below one. Fresh packet degrees
are at most $2L_d=s^{1/20+o(1)}<\Delta$. The balancing lemma gives a
uniform padding error bounded by
\[
 \delta_s=O(1/\lambda)+2^{-c\sqrt\lambda+O((\log\lambda)^2)}
\]
for a fixed $c>0$. In particular $\delta_s\log\lambda=o(1)$,
which is needed before multiplying the shrinkage errors over all depths.

\subsection{Depth, precision, and entry evaluation}

First verify the gates for hypothetical depths up to
$\log_{20/11}\lambda+O(1)$. Throughout this range the least retained
taper length satisfies
\[
 u_d\ge\lambda^{\eta+o(1)},\qquad
 \eta=1-\frac{\log(8/5)}{\log(20/11)}>0,
\]
because $8/5<20/11$. Thus all floors are uniformly negligible.
The uniform padding estimate makes every child shrink by at most $11/20$
eventually. This closes the depth bootstrap with leaf threshold
$H=\lceil s/\lambda\rceil$, without assuming shrinkage before establishing
the packing gates. The sharper bounds are
\[
 M_d\le(1+o(1))s m_d,
 \quad (m_0,m_1,m_2)=\left(1,\frac14,\frac1{24}\right),
 \quad m_{3+k}=\frac1{96}2^{-k}.
\]

The precision argument remains valid uniformly. With
$D=2^{a_d-i}$ and $i<u_d$, we have
$D\ge2^{a_{d-1}+b_0+1}$. Inherited precision is at most
$O(s\lambda^2 2^{a_{d-1}})$, so its ratio to $MD/u_d$ is
$O(\lambda^4 2^{-b_0})=o(1)$. The jet-evaluation loss is lower order too.
The exact sufficient linear taper therefore gives
\[
 P_i\le(6+o(1))MD/u_d.
\]
All precisions, ring-coordinate counts, interpolation degrees, and stored
descriptions remain polynomial in $s$. The original extension of degree
$4\lambda$ still supplies enough residue-distinct interpolation points.

The original entry cost $2^{O(s^{9/10})}$, new packet-entry cost
$\exp(O(L_d\log s))=\exp(s^{1/20+o(1)})$, and direct leaf cost
$2^{O(s/\lambda)}$ are all lower order on the scale $s/\sqrt\lambda$.
Keep immutable creation-time tensor descriptions. In particular, generating
a new packet descriptor must not turn evaluation of one packet entry into
an uncharged recursive evaluation of a previous tensor network.
Cache entries are paid child contractions, not free oracles.

The number of branching choices depends on $s$, not on the root description
length $\mathcal L$. Count nodes first, then charge a fixed polynomial in
$s+\mathcal L$ per node or original entry. Multiplying an arbitrary
$\mathcal L$-dependent factor afresh into every recursive branching bound
would not justify a final fixed polynomial in $\mathcal L$.

\subsection{Local table construction and storage}

Let $\kappa=\pi\sqrt2/\ln2$, $K=\kappa M/\sqrt u$, and
\[
 b=\sqrt{61/150},\qquad
 \alpha_r=\frac{\sqrt{2(r-1)}}r.
\]
The adaptive four-block lemma bounds the final contraction by
$2^{(b+o(1))K}$. For eight groups, formation of four pairs costs at most
$2^{(\sqrt{608/25}/8+o(1))K}$. For twelve groups, formation of four
triples costs at most $2^{(\sqrt{1366/25}/12+o(1))K}$.
The respective coefficients are $0.6164414003\ldots$ and
$0.6159906204\ldots$, strictly below $b=0.6377042157\ldots$.
Thus input-block formation is included, rather than omitted from the
local bound. The singleton caches have coefficient $\alpha_r<b$ for
every alphabet used here.

There is a convenient way to avoid a subtle uniformity issue in invoking
the inherited weighted-entropy lemma. That lemma is phrased for fixed
coefficient vectors, whereas choosing its dual vector proportional to the
observed index-length vector would make the choice data dependent. Instead
apply the lemma to the finite set of $216$ mean coefficient vectors in the
four-block proof. Aggregation of the directed pairs repeats each block-edge
coefficient $2(r/4)^2$ times. Every resulting vector has Euclidean norm at
most $\sqrt2(r/4)\sqrt{244/75}$. Maximizing the error over this finite set
is uniform, and dividing by $r$ gives exactly $\sqrt{61/150}$. This proves
the adaptive bound without requiring a separate dual-norm uniformity claim.

For the chosen perfect matching $M$, its crossing-boundary size has
logarithm $C_M\le F_M$. Hence the selected intermediate pair tables fit
in $2^{(b+o(1))K}$ space. The cruder boundary-cardinality bound
$\sqrt{1/2}K$ is larger and must not be substituted here while retaining
the claimed space constant. The adaptive cost certificate itself supplies
the needed sharper storage bound. Standard sequential fixed-base matrix
multiplication, record reshaping, and coordinate/CRT recovery add only
polynomial factors in the record and index lengths.

The exponent $12/5$ requires the exact integer-coordinate bridge inherited
from the contraction-tree packet. An arbitrary field-multiplication theorem
cannot simply be applied inside a ring with noninvertible two and nilpotents.
The bridge fixes a rational bilinear backend with exponent below $12/5$,
uses nonexceptional odd primes on canonical integer coordinate matrices,
reconstructs their exact products, and then reduces in the target ring.
Its coordinate rank, input precision, and matrix-index lengths are all
polynomial in $s$. It therefore preserves the claimed leading exponent.

\subsection{Complete recurrence and exact constants}

After paying for all cached group entries, the recurrence is
\[
 T_d\le 2^{(\alpha_{r_d}+o(1))K_d}T_{d+1}
             +2^{(b+o(1))K_d},
 \qquad S_d\le2^{(b+o(1))K_d}+S_{d+1},
\]
up to already accounted-for polynomial factors. On the scale
$s/\sqrt\lambda$, the first four $K_d$ coefficients are
\[
 K_0^*=\kappa\sqrt{60},\quad
 K_1^*=\frac\kappa4\sqrt{90},\quad
 K_2^*=\frac\kappa{24}\sqrt{180},\quad
 K_3^*=\frac\kappa{96}\sqrt{160}.
\]
The subsequent ratio is
$\gamma=(1/2)/\sqrt{5/8}=\sqrt{2/5}$, not $\sqrt2/5$.
Unrolling the recurrence gives the local candidates
\[
 bK_0^*,\quad \alpha_8K_0^*+bK_1^*,\quad
 \alpha_8K_0^*+\alpha_{12}K_1^*+bK_2^*,\quad
 \alpha_8K_0^*+\alpha_{12}K_1^*+\alpha_8K_2^*+bK_3^*.
\]
Their values are respectively
$31.6617067862\ldots$, $32.9158138649\ldots$,
$31.4483967553\ldots$, and $31.3778492206\ldots$.
Since $\alpha_4/(1-\gamma)>b$, later tail candidates increase toward
\[
 \alpha_8K_0^*+\alpha_{12}K_1^*+\alpha_8K_2^*
       +\frac{\alpha_4K_3^*}{1-\gamma}
 =32.2464016431\ldots,
\]
still below the depth-one candidate. Leaves only add a lower-order
exponent. The verified constants are therefore
\[
 C_T=\frac\pi{\ln2}
       \left(\frac{\sqrt{105}}2+\sqrt{\frac{183}{40}}\right),
 \qquad C_S=\frac\pi{\ln2}\sqrt{\frac{244}{5}}.
\]
Ancestor and descendant stored arrays add, rather than multiply; the
largest local space exponent is at the root. Rational interval arithmetic
independently establishes
\[
 32.91581386492429831439<C_T<32.91581386492429831440,
\]
\[
 31.66170678619398804166<C_S<31.66170678619398804167.
\]
The checker uses exact fractions, integer-square-root enclosures, Machin's
formula for $\pi$, and a positive series for $\ln2$; floating-point output
does not decide any comparison.

\subsection{Limits relevant to the research objective}

This theorem improves a leading coefficient in a normalized wire-level
exponential upper bound and uses subexponential space. It does not improve
the logarithmic order of the SAT bound or supply a coefficient per SAT
variable. The inherited encoding constants and the source theorem remain
separate dependencies.

Precision peeling leaves the set of enumerated mode tuples unchanged and
reduces only inherited precision, already lower order compared with the
next Hermite loss under the above schedule. The incoming frontier packet
already observes this limitation. Similarly, applying the tensor engine
to a rank-compressed mode network with only $r$ group vertices faces high
arity: its usable next range is $O(\log r)$, whereas initial packing costs
$O(\log M/\log r)$. The apparent two square-root savings cancel.
The previously proposed degree-preserving exterior-power interface also
still needs compressed mode multiplicity and recursive closure. Neither
that interface nor the conditional structured summation algorithm is
established by this schedule or this audit.
