\section{Review inventory and source coverage}
\label{app:inventory}

The frozen snapshot contains twenty-nine filed refinement packets and five
new incoming archives. The two primary manuscripts are additional sources,
not part of this count. Every packet's title, headline claims, and available
status material were screened. This identifies the claimed comparison
frontier; it is not an independent proof audit of every packet.

Detailed examination focused on the exact mode and precision interface,
asymmetric packing, adaptive flags, complete contraction trees, the
rectangular backend, and the strongest compatible incoming bounds.
The verification section distinguishes executable finite checks from
imported analytic theorems and source inspection of the Lean projects.

The tables record author-stated contributions and claim scope.
A coefficient refinement preserves the logarithmic exponent order relative
to imported identities. A conditional stronger order requires its named
missing interface. Kernel measurements are not full SAT measurements.
Generic coefficients refer to the maximum-degree promise
$\Delta\le\lceil s^{9/10}\rceil$; smaller-degree bounds must be compared
separately, including where an actual 3-SAT reduction supplies that stronger
promise. Each full identifier links to the frozen directory or archive.
All applications in this report are to 3-SAT, even where older packets
treat broader clause widths.
