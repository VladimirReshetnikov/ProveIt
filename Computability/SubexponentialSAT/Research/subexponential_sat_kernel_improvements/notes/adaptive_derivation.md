# Adaptive separation on the actual crossing block

## Status and contribution

This is a new, finite, instance-dependent improvement to the separated-mode
enumeration envelope. It does not lower the worst-case logarithmic order.
It can be composed with the latest full-orientation Cauchy factorization,
asymmetric packing, precision guards, and the new packet extractor.

The `eth_joint_cauchy` packet proves the optimal Smith profile of the **full
padded orientation block**, but explicitly excludes sparse real-port
restrictions from its optimality claim. Its executable is a fixed full-block
factorization. I did not find an actual-port valuation-pivoted separator in the
inspected Cauchy and packing packets. The parent review now covers all fifteen
packets at ProveIt `6d3adc45061535ebe6fa7cd3d9b37dff7dcfc22a`; the additional
matching-packing packet does not change this kernel interface. The general mathematical method is ordinary Smith
elimination over a finite chain ring; novelty claimed here is its precise use
at this algorithmic interface, with dominance against even a restricted and
valuation-normalized padded basis. Do not claim invention of Smith normal form.

Implementation and check results are under `code/`:

- `adaptive_modes.py`: finite unramified-ring arithmetic; adaptive separator;
  actual crossing kernel; strengthened restricted padded comparator; exact
  abstract tuple counts.
- `check_adaptive_modes.py`: independent determinant-minor checks, finite
  reconstruction checks, actual-domain examples over F2/F4/F8 extensions.
- `adaptive_mode_results.json`: recorded results.

## Setting

Let O be the ring of integers in an unramified extension of Q2, with residue
field k, and let R_p = O/(2^p). The crossing matrix W has entries in R_p. The
tensor coefficient ring may additionally contain a truncated V variable, but
W itself is scalar in O. Apply the algorithm to W, not to arbitrary matrices
over O[V]/(V^ell), which need not be a principal ideal ring.

Choose one packet and one crossing orientation at a fixed interpolation node
and value. Its real rows X all lie in one target group, and its real columns Y
all lie in one source group. Construct only the |X| by |Y| matrix

    A_xy = Gamma(T)/(T+x-y) mod 2^p.

Here Gamma(T)=C(T)/C'(0), T=t+2^(a+1) zeta, i=v2(t), and the slots have the same
bit at position i. Every denominator has valuation at most i, hence every entry
has valuation at least a+1-i. The kernel preparation must retain its existing
integral-evaluation precision guarantees. The separator below, once A mod 2^p
is available, never asks for a higher precision.

## Exact algorithm without additional precision

Maintain the current residual B modulo 2^p. If it is zero, stop. Choose a
nonzero entry B_ab of minimum valuation e and write B_ab=2^e u with u a unit.
Every coordinate of every B_ij is divisible by 2^e. In R_(p-e), compute

    v_j = (B_aj/2^e) * (B_ab/2^e)^(-1).

Lift each v_j canonically to R_p, and set c_i=B_ib. Record the rank-one mode
c v^T, then replace B by B-c v^T. Its pivot row and column are now zero modulo
2^p. Restrict to the remaining rows and columns and repeat.

This is well-defined: choosing a different lift of v changes its product with
c by a multiple of 2^p, since all c_i are divisible by 2^e. Only the normalized
unit u is inverted. The target vector has minimum valuation exactly e, and
the source vector has a unit at the pivot column. Subsequent minimum valuations
cannot decrease. At most min(|X|,|Y|) pivots occur.

Each step is integral elementary Gaussian elimination, together with row and
column permutations. Consequently the recorded exponents

    0 <= lambda_1 <= ... <= lambda_r < p

are exactly the nonzero Smith valuations of A over R_p. More explicitly,
unipotent row and column operations transform the pivot row and column into a
single entry 2^e times a unit, leaving precisely the Schur residual just
computed. Repeating gives an integral equivalence to the diagonal Smith form.
The recorded outer products give an exact representation of A modulo 2^p
in the original row and column coordinates.

## Finite optimality theorem

For each q in {1,...,p}, put

    r_A(q) = #{j : lambda_j < q}.

Then r_A(q) is the least inner dimension of an integral factorization
A mod 2^q = BC over R_q. The Smith form gives such a factorization with
r_A(q) columns. Conversely, the image module of the Smith matrix requires
r_A(q) generators. If A=BC with r inner coordinates, im(A) is a submodule
of im(B), which is generated by at most r elements. Over a finite chain ring,
the number of generators of a submodule cannot exceed that of its ambient
module: by the elementary divisor classification, both equal the residue-field
dimension of the submodule annihilated by 2, and inclusion injects these
socles. Hence r_A(q)<=r.

Now consider **any** exact integral separated representation on this same
finite block,

    A = sum_{j=1}^m 2^(w_j) u_j v_j^T mod 2^p,

with positive certified weights w_j; delete terms of weight >=p. Reducing
modulo 2^q leaves at most #{j:w_j<q} rank-one terms, so

    r_A(q) <= #{j:w_j<q}  for every q<=p.

Sort the w_j and append infinite valuations to the Smith list if needed. Then

    lambda_j >= w_j  for every matched j.

This proves a strong finite inventory dominance statement:

    #{beta>=0 : sum lambda_j beta_j < p}
      <= #{beta>=0 : sum w_j beta_j < p}.

Map the shorter exponent tuple to the same sorted coordinates and set omitted
coordinates to zero. The assertion also holds after imposing the common
total-degree cap. It concerns this abstract mode inventory, not every
possible cancellation or pruning heuristic in a complete solver.

In particular the theorem applies to geometric modes, factorial/annulus
modes, full padded joint modes restricted to real ports, and that restricted
basis after deleting zero modes and transferring every common source
valuation to the target. It therefore improves over simply observing that
the sparse submatrix has fewer rows. No new full-matrix slope beyond four is
claimed; dense padded blocks recover the existing exact profile.

## Locality, recursion, cost, and limitations

Each recorded source mode is the linear form sum_y v_y X_y. Its columns all
belong to the original source group. Its target coefficients c_x read only
the original target rows. Mixing rows or columns inside this one orientation
does not join groups, packets, or tensor vertices. The same coefficient
extraction identity and child graph therefore apply unchanged, modulo the
working precision. Fresh tensor coefficients are frozen as canonical
representatives at their creation precision, just as in the existing source.

The separator uses O(m n min(m,n)) ring operations, O(mn) stored ring elements
including the resulting mode vectors, and at most min(m,n) unit inversions.
For m,n<=L=poly(s) and polynomial precision and extension degree, this is
polynomial work and space in the root size. It creates at most as many modes
as the full padded basis. Its finite polynomial overhead may outweigh its
enumeration savings in tiny blocks; the guarantee is an improved weighted
enumeration certificate, not a universal measured runtime dominance.

The prototype is a direct dense implementation. A production implementation
can choose the padded factorization for fully populated blocks and use the
adaptive separator for genuinely sparse blocks, or compare estimates. The
Smith theorem guarantees the adaptive inventory is no worse, but it does not
select the best wall-clock algorithm automatically. No complete SAT run is
claimed.

## A closed-form sparse family

If the real row and column slots are x_j=x_0+Hj, j=0,...,m-1, with
v2(H)=i+h and h>=1, normalize T=2^i tau, tau a unit. The normalized block is

    tau/(tau+2^h h_odd (j-k)).

Its standard Cauchy telescoping factors at the actual nodes have valuation
h b+v2(b!) on both sides. After dividing these factors by that power of two,
their triangular diagonals are units. Hence the exact normalized Smith
valuations are

    2 h b + 2 v2(b!) = (2h+2)b - 2 popcount(b).

For unit zeta, multiply by Gamma(T)/T to obtain the exact W valuations

    a+1-i + (2h+2)b - 2 popcount(b).

The full orientation i=0 has h=1, recovering the existing slope four. Taking
every other real slot gives h=2 and slope six. Taking every fourth gives h=3
and slope eight. This is an instance-dependent sparse-block effect; creating
sparsity by padding also changes packing/precision costs and is not by itself
a new global asymptotic theorem.

## Verified finite evidence

The deterministic checker completed:

- 720 arbitrary matrices over R_p with residue fields F2, F4, F8, including
  zero and repeated-row cases;
- 6,840 independently evaluated determinant minors, checking the capped
  determinantal ideals against the sum of computed Smith valuations;
- 720 actual crossing matrices, checking adaptive reconstruction, the
  strengthened padded comparator reconstruction, sorted-weight dominance,
  and exact tuple-count dominance;
- 180 full padded profile checks against the existing formula.

The six recorded sparse examples use zeta=U in
(Z/2^p)[U]/(U^2+U+1), so the tests do not assume interpolation points are
ordinary rational integers. Their baseline is the strengthened restricted
padded basis, not its loose published uniform weights.
