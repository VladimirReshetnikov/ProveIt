1. The exact finite minimax identity is valid
-------------------------------------------
Let E be a finite coordinate set and V_1,...,V_q finite nonempty subsets
of R_{≥0}^E. Then

  max_{g≥0, ||g||_2≤1} min_j max_{v in V_j} <v,g>
    = max_{v_j in V_j} min_{p≥0, sum p=1} ||sum_j p_j v_j||_2.

For fixed g, choosing an independent maximizing support vector in each
V_j proves min_j max_v <v,g> = max_{(v_1,...,v_q)} min_j <v_j,g>.
The finite maximum commutes with the maximum over the compact ball.
For each tuple, min_j <v_j,g> is the minimum over the probability simplex.
The elementary compact convex bilinear minimax theorem exchanges this
minimum and the ball maximum. Every convex combination of the support
vectors is nonnegative, so the support function of the nonnegative unit
ball at that vector is its ordinary Euclidean norm. These steps give the
identity. Nonnegativity is needed at the final support-function step.

A closest-hull certificate is rational when the supports are rational.
For weights p, put w=sum p_j v_j. The exact sufficient and necessary
conditions are p_j≥0, sum p_j=1, <v_j,w>≥||w||^2 for every j, with
equality whenever p_j>0. They follow from first-order optimality of the
squared norm on the convex hull. A maximizing tuple also gives a sharp
lower witness g=w/||w|| if w is nonzero. A closest point has a representation
on an affinely independent subset of support vectors. Solving the augmented rational system
G p = t 1, 1^T p=1 on each such subset and checking all inequalities therefore
produces a finite exact certificate; the squared optimum is rational.

minimax_exact.py implements this argument with Python Fraction arithmetic.
The six synthetic cases include zero and duplicate vectors and reject
nonoptimal equal averaging. For vectors (2,0),(0,1), equal averaging has
squared norm 5/4, while the closest hull point has squared norm 4/5 and
weights 1/5,4/5. Thus weighting can strictly improve equal averaging.

4. The matrix-arithmetic bridge is adequate with fixed positive slack
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Reviewed files in the main report: sections/backend.tex and interface.tex.
Primary additional sources in the source collection are mm_square.tex,
Theorem 1 and the finite-algebraic-coefficient remark near source line 690,
and mm_rectangle_intro.tex, main theorem and characteristic-zero transfer.
Their claims are imported here, not independently reproved.

For each fixed positive exponent tolerance, use a finite exact bilinear
rank scheme. Its rational polynomial equations have an algebraic solution
whenever they have a complex solution, so finitely many fixed coefficients
lie in a fixed number field. Fixed rational coordinates simulate it exactly.
The recursion has logarithmic depth, fixed linear combinations before the
bilinear leaf products, and fixed linear combinations afterward. Coefficient
heights therefore have polynomial overhead in input bit length and log
matrix dimension. This is sufficient for the bit model used in the report.

To multiply over a finite free (Z/2^p)-algebra, lift module coordinates to
integers, perform polynomially many integer matrix products by that exact
implementation, combine with the algebra's multiplication table, then
reduce modulo 2^p. No number-field embedding into characteristic two and
no inversion of a nonunit in the target ring is needed. Products may be
streamed, retaining polynomially many input/output-sized arrays.

The endpoint omega<=9/4 means arbitrarily small positive fixed slack,
not automatically a single fixed algorithm of cost N^(9/4+o(1)). The
report correctly charges relative slack 10^-5. The schemes and their
constants may be enormous; no practical implementation claim follows.

-----------------------------------------------------------------

6. Scope of the actual-piece rank probes
--------------------------------------
rank_probes/probe_boson.py implements the (O2) operator recursion and the
Appendix B Q'_s factorization modulo 1009. Source definitions can also be
found at eth/proof/rates/ClauseBound/Boson/Defs.lean. It uses all p(h)
external partitions of h, whereas the algorithm selects 2^lambda of them.
Replacing b_mu=(h!/z_mu)t_mu by t_mu is an invertible external diagonal
rescaling for the tested h<1009, so it preserves each flattening rank.

Central-piece (s=0) ranks over F_1009:

  h=4, p(h)=5:  row 25, column 25.

7. Rectangular-grid certificate identity
---------------------------------------
For h>0 and positive e_1,e_2, direct equalization gives

 max_{-2h<=t<=2h} min(e_1 sqrt(2h+t), e_2 sqrt(2h-t))
   = 2 sqrt(h) / sqrt(e_1^(-2)+e_2^(-2)).

The increasing and decreasing terms meet at
t=2h(e_2^2-e_1^2)/(e_1^2+e_2^2), which lies in the specified interval.
After the source normalization, e_out=(e_1^(-2)+e_2^(-2))^(-1/2).
For e(K)=C/sqrt(log K) and K=K_1 K_2 this is precisely e(K).
Thus rectangularizing that displayed partition envelope alone does not
change its logarithmic power. This is an identity for the certificate
family, not a lower bound for an algorithm or for SAT.

8. Essential modular-CRT correction for the open rank criterion
--------------------------------------------------------------
The finite-field rank compiler itself is valid. The improved rank bound
rho<1/sqrt(2) remains unproved. A rational-rank upper bound transfers to
every prime because all larger minors are exactly zero as integers.

For the direct clause-count reduction, a run modulo a good prime returns
the count modulo that prime, and a prime product >2^n suffices. However,
the sharper variable-count reduction in paper Appendix D uses a truncated
parity expansion valid only modulo powers of two (Lemma 12, (64)-(65)).
It is incorrect to run that truncated reduction over odd prime fields
and identify its result with the count modulo those primes.

The repair is to supply an exact signed integer clique oracle first.
For a (K,lambda)-clique with |W_v|<2^B, elementary summation gives

  |Z| <= 2^[binom(K,2)*lambda + K*B].

Compute its value modulo sufficiently many good primes with product
greater than 2^[binom(K,2)*lambda+K*B+1], then use signed CRT to recover
Z exactly. Appendix D's original 2-adic truncation and exact potential
comparisons can then be used unchanged. The internal modular recursion
values need not be reconstructed separately. K,lambda,B and every reached
h have polynomial bounds in the original input, so polynomially many
polynomial-size primes suffice. Taking primes above every reached h
ensures that the factorial normalizers are invertible. This correction
preserves the conditional logarithmic-power consequence, but it is an
essential arithmetic interface condition rather than an optional detail.

Overall conclusion
------------------
The new finite minimax method, its four replayed certificates, and the
two charged schedules withstand this independent audit. They give improved
explicit coefficients within the imported contraction framework. No
strictly better power of log n was proved. The modular actual-rank route
is a precise conditional research direction after the signed-CRT repair;
the required uniform rank theorem is still open.
