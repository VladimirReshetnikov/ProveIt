\subsection{Orders, constants, and unresolved targets}

No new incoming packet supplies a proved smaller unrestricted logarithmic
order. At fixed density the common order is $n/\sqrt{\log n}$; in the
worst case it is $n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n}$. At
$m=\Theta(n\log\log n)$, the density-sensitive construction improves the
numerator to $n\log\log\log n$. This genuine order improvement was
already present in the first round and is not a new result here.

The strongest smaller-order targets still require an unproved algorithmic
interface, notably the structured summation in
\path{3sat_precision_refinement}. A numerical coefficient cannot dominate
an unproved $n/\log n$ target as though it were an implemented algorithm.
Likewise, the independent Anthropic theorem has an unspecified big-$O$
constant in its machine model. It is not numerically comparable with a
coefficient per normalized Cauchy wire. Section~\ref{sec:formal} gives the
updated public formalization status.
