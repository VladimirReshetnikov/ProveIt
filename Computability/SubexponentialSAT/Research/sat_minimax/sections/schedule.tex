\section{A fully paid multistage schedule}
\label{sec:paid-new-schedule}

Let $\lambda=\lceil\log_2s\rceil$, use
$u_d=\lfloor\eta_d\lambda\rfloor$, and put
\[
 a_d=\sum_{j\le d}u_j+(d+1)\lceil6\log_2\lambda\rceil,
 \qquad H=\lceil s/\lambda\rceil.
\]
Stop when the child wire count is at most $H$; use direct contraction for
bounded exceptional sizes or failed finite admissibility tests.
The classical prefix is
\[
(r_0,\ldots,r_7)=(9,12,8,6,5,4,4,4),
\]
\[
(\eta_0,\ldots,\eta_7)=
(0.01577,0.01212,0.00544,0.00535,0.00438,0.00342,0.00215,0.00136).
\]
Every displayed decimal is an exact terminating rational. For $d\ge8$ take
\[
 r_d=3,\qquad\eta_d=\frac{30107}{10^7}
                   \left(\frac{77}{100}\right)^{d-8}.
\]
The total retained fraction is $1577/25000=0.06308$.
For the optional newer backend replace only
\[
 r_0=10,\qquad\eta_0=0.01505,\qquad\eta_1=0.01284.
\]
Both prefixes have cumulative range $0.02789$ at depth one and $0.04999$
at depth seven.

Let $A_d=\sum_{j\le d}\eta_j$. At the root the strict leading packing
gate is $9/10+2A_0\log_2r_0<1$. Later gates are
\[
9/10+A_d\log_2r_d<1,\qquad
2A_d\log_2r_d+A_{d-1}<1,\qquad
(2\log_2r_d+1)A_d<1.
\]
The accompanying interval checker proves all of them, including their tail
limits, by exact rational enclosures of the logarithms. The strict positive
margins absorb all polylogarithmic padding and precision factors.
All fresh tensor degrees are at most $s^{0.06308+o(1)}<s^{9/10}$.

For a noncircular depth argument, first verify these inequalities through
$\log_{600/431}\lambda+O(1)$ hypothetical active levels. The minimum taper is
at least $\lambda^{1+\ln(77/100)/\ln(600/431)+o(1)}$, which diverges.
Nearly lossless padding then gives eventual tail child ratio at most $431/600$,
since its sharp ratio is $2/3$, closing the depth bootstrap. Padding tolerance
$O(\lambda^{-3})$ makes the accumulated relative padding error $1+o(1)$.
The six-logarithm buffer absorbs inherited precision and gives the uniform
local leading precision $(6+o(1))MD/u$.

Every group cache calls its true child contractor. Thus the paid recurrence is
\[
 T_d\le\operatorname{poly}(s)
 \left[2^{(\alpha_{r_d}+o(1))K_d}T_{d+1}
       +2^{((1+\varepsilon)\beta_{r_d}+o(1))K_d}\right],
 \qquad\alpha_r=\frac{\sqrt{2(r-1)}}r.
\]
Use the certified menus at the first two depths and fixed balanced
three-block trees thereafter. The latter have
\[
 \beta_8^2=15/32,\quad\beta_6^2=4/9,\quad\beta_5^2=12/25,
 \quad\beta_4^2=1/2,\quad\beta_3^2=4/9
\]
under either backend. Their input/output support bounds dominate the
backend-dependent arithmetic supports.

Put $m_0=1$, $m_d=\prod_{j<d}2/r_j$, and
$K_d^*=(\pi\sqrt2/\ln2)m_d/\sqrt{\eta_d}$.
The prefix branch at depth $d$ has coefficient
\[
 \sum_{j<d}\alpha_{r_j}K_j^*+(1+\varepsilon)\beta_{r_d}K_d^*.
\]
For the tail use $\gamma=(2/3)/\sqrt{77/100}$ and append
\[
 \sum_{j<8}\alpha_{r_j}K_j^*
 +K_8^*\max\{(1+\varepsilon)\beta_3,\alpha_3/(1-\gamma)\}.
\]
The space coefficient is $\max_d\beta_{r_d}K_d^*$: live ancestor and child
tables add in storage rather than multiply. Exact rational interval arithmetic
gives
\[
\begin{array}{lrr}
& C_T&C_S\\
\text{Classical backend}&30.0872056033774679\ldots&28.8528994149423854\ldots\\
\text{Optional newer backend}&28.9729416032324588\ldots&28.9726518767136916\ldots.
\end{array}
\]
Therefore eventual coefficients $(30.09,28.86)$ and $(28.98,28.98)$ are valid.
The classical time maximum occurs at depth one; the optional time maximum
occurs at the root. Neither time statement uses polynomial space.

Direct leaves cost $2^{O(s/\lambda)}$; permitted original entries cost
$2^{O(s^{9/10})}$ times a fixed polynomial; fresh entries cost
$\exp(s^{0.06308+o(1)})$. These are lower order after all ancestor calls
have been charged. Polynomial work over $O(\log\lambda)$ levels adds
$O(\lambda\log\lambda)$ to the exponent. Ring dimensions, precisions and
record lengths stay polynomial in $s$. Sorting and scanning the tables in
index order retains the reviewed bit-cost model.

\subsection{Fair comparisons and the remaining goal}

All rows below use the same normalized-wire interface. ``Old'' means
the reviewed three-plan method of \cite{treenew}, not an arbitrary
optimization over all previous methods. Backend slack is paid in the
newly composed constructions.

On the old published schedule, merely inserting the newer backend gives
$30.512927432781\ldots$ time; the explicit tail retuning gives the
$30.273960451080\ldots$ row. Thus the new time improvement survives equal
backend upgrades. That retuned old-menu row has a smaller space coefficient
than the new fastest construction; retain it when memory is the priority.
These are explicit compatible comparisons, not a global optimization of
every possible composition of previous algorithms.

The new estimates remain
$\operatorname{poly}(s+\mathcal L)2^{(C+o(1))s/\sqrt{\log_2s}}$.
They do not improve the worst-case SAT order
$n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n}$, prove the conditional $n/\log n$ interfaces,
or supply a complete SAT solver. The polynomial-space frontier
$46.184845586731\ldots$ remains a separate resource option. The exact
minimax identity and its certificate compiler are new mathematical tools
for obtaining the best constant of a fixed finite strategy menu; they do
not by themselves remove the square-root logarithm.
