\begin{abstract}
We review twenty-nine refinement packets in the two requested ProveIt
paths, including the nine latest arrivals and their subsequent placement,
and incorporate the supplied public Anthropic formalization. A new exact
finite minimax certificate, explicit four-plan contraction menus, rectangular
matrix multiplication and a fully paid recursion schedule improve the
best reviewed generic cached contraction bound.

For the shared normalized-wire interface and
$E(s)=s/\sqrt{\lceil\log_2s\rceil}$, the classical-backend construction has
time $\poly(s+\mathcal L)2^{(30.087205604+o(1))E(s)}$ and space
$\poly(s+\mathcal L)2^{(28.852899415+o(1))E(s)}$.
Here $\mathcal L$ is the input-description length.
Adding the published 2026 characteristic-zero matrix bounds gives
coefficients $28.972941604$ and $28.972651877$, respectively.
These improve the reviewed displayed cached pair
$30.891018921/30.753912833$ under the same inherited interface.
The gains also survive explicit comparisons that upgrade the older
three-plan menus with the same matrix backends and a retuned tail.

The mathematical tool characterizes the exact worst-case support cost of
a finite strategy menu as the maximum, over one support chosen from each
plan, of the distance from zero to their convex hull. Its rational
certificates cover $173{,}846{,}817$ support tuples. A separately written
enumerator reproduces the certificates. Exact rational interval arithmetic
checks all schedule gates and the resulting constants.

\textbf{The logarithmic-order breakthrough is not established.}
The new algorithms still have exponent of order
$s/\sqrt{\log s}$, use subexponential memory, and retain the general-density
SAT order $n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n}$. We give a separate, explicitly
conditional route through the actual ranks of Anthropic's completed
crossing pieces. A concrete rank-product target would improve the
denominator power to $\log_2(3/2)>1/2$, but that rank theorem remains open.
The archive includes the proofs, exact certificates, independently written
checks, reproducible source pins and this report's complete LaTeX sources.
\end{abstract}
