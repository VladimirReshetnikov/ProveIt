ALGORITHMIC CONTENT AND BREAKTHROUGH LEVER
The public paper is a different contraction method, not merely the OpenAI deformation under another formalization. It places nodes on a grid, rewrites crossingSWAPs into signed pieces, then merges rows or columns globally per term and groups into balanced classes. AppendixA's effective piece contract with row+column bit budget4*rho*lambda+O(loglambda) yields exponent-log poweralpha'=log2(1/rho). AppendixB uses the Heisenberg/Fock partition space dim(V_E)=p(E) with logdim~sqrtE, givingrho=1/sqrt2 andalpha'=1/2. AppendixD's sparse parity checks and profile mark encoding give the nloglogn factor.
AppendixH explicitly identifies a structural lever: if a valid effective replacement had logdim(V_E)~CE^alpha, with all crossing identities, both factorizations, polynomially many pieces, polynomial bit lengths, and2^{O(lambda)} evaluation intact, thenrho=2^{alpha-1}, hencealpha'=1-alpha. Alpha<1/2 would improve denominator power. This is already proposed in the paper and already discussed in3sat_round2_refinement; it is not itself a new result.
Existing3sat_round2_refinement report proves a characteristic-zero vacuum representation retaining all exact Heisenberg relations must have dimV_E>=p(E), and thinning infinitely many positive modes breaks ordinary finite-order locality; nonlinear regrading breaks homogeneous shifts. A successful replacement must change more than the Hilbert-series count.
