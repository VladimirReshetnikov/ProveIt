   571	The number of interpolation nodes, values, and groups at a call
   572	is polynomial in $s$, independently of $\mathcal L$.
   573	By \eqref{eq:tensor-partition-count}, the number of children of
   574	an active depth-$d$ call is at most
   575	\[
   576	 s^{O(1)}\exp\left(
   577	       O_C\left(\frac{s}{\sqrt\lambda}
   578	                    \left(\frac{\sqrt2}{8}\right)^d\right)\right).
   579	\]

   581	$u_d=\Theta_C(\lambda/2^d)$.  Multiplying these bounds over the
   582	$O_C(\log\lambda)$ depths, or bounding the number of nodes on
   583	each level in this way, gives at most
   584	\[
   585	 \exp\left(O_C(s/\sqrt\lambda)
   586	                   +O_C(\lambda\log\lambda)\right)
   587	       =\exp(O_C(s/\sqrt\lambda))
   588	\]
   589	calls in total.  At a leaf, direct summation uses at most $2^H$
   590	assignments and polynomially many entry evaluations per assignment.
   591	Its cost is bounded by a polynomial in $s+\mathcal L$ times
   592	\[
   593	 \exp(O_C(H)+O_{C,A}(s^{9/10}))
   594	   =\exp(O_{C,A}(s/\sqrt\lambda)).
   595	\]
   596	Local arithmetic, packing, descriptions, and recovery use
   597	polynomial time per generated term, apart from the already
   598	bounded entry computations.  The description-dependent factor
   599	bounds the work per call. Since the branching bound is independent of
   600	$\mathcal L$, the total work retains a fixed polynomial factor in $s+\mathcal L$.
