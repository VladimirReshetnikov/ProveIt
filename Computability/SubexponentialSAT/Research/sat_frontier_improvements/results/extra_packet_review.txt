Proof-focused findings
----------------------
The principal theorem (report Section 2, label thm:main) gives normalized
polynomial-space time coefficient 94.74820225953415 when root M<=s and
maximum degree<=ceil(s^0.9). For a root with M<=C*s it explicitly pays C
multiplying that coefficient. This matches the generic coefficient already
present in the balanced-packing and higher-moment packets; it is weaker
than the previous asymmetric coefficient 76.9089949516.

Section 3 proves exact rational derandomization using at most c=2*Delta
matchings, label-pair count D, and tau=ceil(log2(8*c*D)). The potential is
sum over matching/cell pairs of t^(-H_k) times a product of conditional
edge factors, with t=1+1/lambda. Independence is used only within each
matching. Its initial expectation is at most 1/8, and exact conditional
minimization preserves this. Consequently
  X_max < (1+1/lambda)*M/D + 2*c*lambda*tau.
Uniform padding has relative mass below
  1+1/lambda + D*(2*c*lambda*tau+L)/M.
The exact guard M>=lambda*D*(2*c*lambda*tau+L) yields padding below
1+2/lambda and child ratio (2/r)*(1+2/lambda).
This is valid for multigraphs. Common rational denominator bit length and
all candidate counts are explicitly polynomial in the application.

Section 4 fixes r=54. Its exact cap A_lambda=floor(lambda/(20*log2(54)))
reserves (d_max+1)*g+ceil(log2(lambda))^2 bits, with
  d_max=min{d:18^d>=ceil(C)*lambda}, g=6*ceil(log2(lambda)),
  u_d=floor(8*U/9^(d+1)), a_d=sum_{j<=d}u_j+(d+1)*g.
The square-log reserve makes D*Delta/M decay faster than any inverse
polynomial in lambda. The child ratio is at most 1/18 for a noncircular
bootstrap, and minimum taper grows with exponent 1-ln(9)/ln(18)>0.

Sections 5--6 reuse the leading precision bound 6*M*D_i/u and the older
factorial weights 1+3*b-2*popcount(b). They pay inherited precision using
the additive gap. They do not supply the full-orientation slope-four
basis, asymmetric side-degree exploitation, multistage arity, cached group
tables, a new SAT logarithmic order, or a missing structured-sum oracle.
The global constant follows from child ratio 1/27 and taper ratio 1/9.
I found no substantive gap in these new proof components during this
source-level review; the unchanged upstream proof was not recertified.

There is no stronger-premise degree specialization or practical benchmark
in this packet. code/refinement.py contains small exact component routines
for matching coloring, conditional potentials, labeling, log intervals,
integer budgets, schedules, inherited Hermite precision, and abstract
factorial-mode tuple counts. It contains no complete contraction or SAT
solver and no accelerated full-Cauchy feature construction. The report
explicitly disclaims a measured speedup. Its schedule basic_guard is only
a basic positivity/taper test, not a full finite contraction guard.
