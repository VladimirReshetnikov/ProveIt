The recorded verification covers the following finite obligations:
\begin{itemize}
\item Exact rational capacity and coefficient checks for the $64$ and $24$ polynomial-space schedules, including deliberate false-capacity and false-threshold controls that must fail under both normal and optimized Python.
\item Exact rational $38/34$ and $14.3/12.4$ cached certificates, their block counts and geometric recurrence; false smaller thresholds are rejected in both modes.
\item The 200-configuration Cauchy-feature audit described in Section~\ref{sec:cauchy}.
\item The balancer's 140 configurations and 470 enumerated completions, plus an independently written audit with 696 fixtures, 3,480 candidate means and 7,600 completion evaluations. The latter includes all $2$ by $2$ matrices over $\{0,1,2\}$ for $Q=1,2,3,4$ and two rational-base scales.
\item The packet-extraction checks described in Section~\ref{sec:packet}, including independent direct-product coefficients, finite free quotient rings, precision guard failures and creation-precision controls. A separately written identity audit adds 183 centered cases, 63 cube-root ring cases and 1,572 monomial orthogonality checks; it does not import either candidate or upstream executable code.
\end{itemize}
Every failure predicate in the authored checkers uses an explicit exception rather than a removable Python assertion. Normal and \texttt{-O} outputs agree after excluding elapsed-time fields. Recorded benchmark samples are retained; replay validates exact outputs but does not require identical wall-clock timings.
