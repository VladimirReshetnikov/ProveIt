\begin{abstract}
We review the thirty-four refinement packets present in ProveIt's
SubexponentialSAT project through commit \texttt{324f3d9b009a}, together
with the OpenAI manuscript and the public Anthropic Lean development.
The latest five packets already contain affine-plane menus, coupled
support certificates, rectangular backends, and joint time/storage
selection. The new contribution is a controlled partial duplication of
the nine-point affine plane and a mixed-arity recursion that pays for
every child entry.

Relative to the corpus's stated exact contraction interface, the new
fifteen-group construction gives time and space coefficients
\[
 C_T=\frac\pi{\ln2}\sqrt{\frac{32012591492467}{5556600000000}}
 =10.87878166668\ldots,\qquad
 C_S=C_T\sqrt{\frac{200}{231}}=10.12253456921\ldots
\]
on the scale $L/\sqrt{\log_2 L}$ for 3-SAT, where $L\le3m$ is the
normalized literal-occurrence count. A fully doubled eighteen-group
construction gives the alternative pair
\[
 (C_T,C_S)_{18}=(11.07682383502\ldots,9.97733739357\ldots).
\]
The first time coefficient is about $3.42\%$ below the strongest compatible
composition of prior ingredients constructed in this review,
$11.26420175559\ldots$, and about $18.69\%$ below the smallest printed
comparable low-degree time coefficient.

The finite geometry and joint selectors have independent exact checks;
the uniform recursion and 3-SAT translation are proved here.
The new code is not a complete SAT solver or a proof-assistant formalization.
The unrestricted $n$-only order
$n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n}$ remains unchanged. A breakthrough in
logarithmic order has not been established.
\end{abstract}

\section{Results, comparisons, and proof status}
\label{sec:results}

The problem throughout is ordinary 3-SAT. Exact counting is used as a
method for deciding satisfiability. Let $n$ be the number of declared
variables, $m$ the number of clauses, and $L$ the number of literal
occurrences after removing tautologies and duplicate literals within a
clause. Numerical exponent coefficients use base-two logarithms;
$\ln$ denotes the natural logarithm.

\begin{theorem}[Explicit occurrence bounds for 3-SAT]
\label{thm:sat}
Under the inherited exact contraction premises in Section~\ref{sec:interface},
there are deterministic algorithms deciding 3-SAT, and in fact returning
its exact number of satisfying assignments, with
\[
 T\le\poly(n,m)\,2^{(C_T+o(1))L/\sqrt{\log_2 L}},\qquad
 S\le\poly(n,m)\,2^{(C_S+o(1))L/\sqrt{\log_2 L}},
\]
for either of the following simultaneous pairs:
\begin{align*}
 (C_T,C_S)_{15}&=\frac\pi{\ln2}
 \left(\sqrt{\frac{32012591492467}{5556600000000}},
       \sqrt{\frac{32012591492467}{6417873000000}}\right),\\
 (C_T,C_S)_{18}&=\frac\pi{\ln2}
 \left(\sqrt{\frac{563681831683}{94374000000}},
       \sqrt{\frac{14000}{2889}}\right).
\end{align*}
The error terms are uniform over normalized formulas. Empty and
bounded-size cases are handled directly. The pairs are alternative algorithms.
\end{theorem}

The normalized contraction theorem starts with at most $s$ wires, maximum
degree $\lceil s^{1/12}\rceil$, and precision and truncation order at most
$s$. Section~\ref{sec:occurrence-bridge} proves that fixing variables with
more than $\lceil\log_2(L+1)\rceil$ occurrences meets this degree condition
at lower-order cost, with $s=L+1$. This threshold choice composes an
existing incidence construction; it is not a new SAT encoding.

Applying newer ingredients to an old menu can improve a printed number
without a new construction. We therefore reconstruct the stronger
$11.264202$ comparison before comparing the new geometry. It is a concrete
certified bound, not a globally optimized prior frontier. Its equal
time/space bound is safe; existing ingredients may permit further memory
tradeoffs. A different printed low-degree pair has space
$12.36130\ldots$~\cite{project}. Both new algorithms use subexponential
space; the latest low-degree polynomial-space coefficient
$16.81223\ldots$ remains a different resource guarantee~\cite{pairwise}.

The main time coefficient is smaller than the constructed comparison by
$0.38542008891\ldots$, or about $3.42\%$. For the corresponding upper-bound
expressions $U_{\rm prior}$ and $U_{15}$,
\[
 \log_2\frac{U_{\rm prior}}{U_{15}}
 =\bigl(0.38542008891\ldots+o(1)\bigr)
       \frac{L}{\sqrt{\log_2L}}.
\]
This is a comparison of upper-bound functions, not a lower bound on an
earlier algorithm or an empirical SAT speedup. Since $L\le3m$, the main
pair gives coefficients
$(32.63634500004\ldots,30.36760370765\ldots)$ on
$m/\sqrt{\log_2m}$. At fixed density $m\le Dn$, multiply the normalized
constants by $3D$ for an $n/\sqrt{\log_2n}$ bound. At arbitrary density
retain the existing compressed and density-sensitive branches. The
worst-case $n$-only order remains
\[
 \poly(n,m)\,2^{O(n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n})}.
\]
No comparison is made with an unspecified constant in the independent
Anthropic theorem.

\paragraph{Dependency boundary.}
The new finite inequalities, joint integer selectors, strict schedule
arithmetic, recurrence analysis and occurrence-threshold composition are
supplied here. The deformation, exact recovery, packing, mode-entropy and
matrix-ring interfaces are imported mathematical premises. This is a
research proof relative to those interfaces, not an independent end-to-end
verification of the source preprint. The source repository itself records
its packets as unverified claims~\cite{project}.

\subsection{The public formalization}

At Anthropic commit
\texttt{1a6eee293b9e00cb432eef6018a21f523623a9af},
\path{eth/MainResults.lean} states the theorem using a deterministic
finite-alphabet Mathlib multi-stack Turing machine, and
\path{eth/Solution.lean} supplies its proof entry points~\cite{anthropic}.
For clause width three its quantitative order is
\[
 2^{O\left(\min\{m/\sqrt{\log m},\,
                    n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n}\}\right)}.
\]
The input conventions include occurrence of every variable and no repeated
clause lists; normalization supplies input-polynomial overhead. We inspected
these statements, proof entry points and the paper, without rebuilding the
full Lean development. Its public availability corrects older descriptions
as unpublished. It does not formalize the new contractor or automatically
transfer our explicit constants through a different proof.
