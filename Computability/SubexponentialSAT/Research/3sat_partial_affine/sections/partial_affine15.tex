\section{Partial affine duplication: the main improvement}
\label{sec:partial15}

contains affine-nine plans, coupled support-pair bounds, the rectangular

The new construction duplicates six points of the affine plane, producing
a fifteen-group root menu with a more favorable balance between local
contraction and recursive child costs.

For the low-degree normalized interface, the resulting coefficients are
\begin{align*}
 C_T&=\frac\pi{\ln2}
 \sqrt{\frac{32012591492467}{5556600000000}}

 C_S&=\frac\pi{\ln2}

\subsection{The explicit root plans}

Start with the nine points $3x+y$ of $\mathbb F_3^2$. Duplicate points
$0,1,2,3,4,5$, adding twins $9,10,11,12,13,14$, respectively. These are
the two parallel lines $x=0$ and $x=1$; the line $x=2$ remains undoubled.
In every affine parallel class, append each selected point's twin to the
end of its block, in increasing original-point order. The four ordered
plans are
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
(0,1,2,9,10,11)&(3,4,5,12,13,14)&(6,7,8),\\
(0,3,6,9,12)&(1,4,7,10,13)&(2,5,8,11,14),\\
(0,4,8,9,13)&(1,5,6,10,14)&(2,3,7,11,12),\\
(0,5,7,9,14)&(1,3,8,10,12)&(2,4,6,11,13).
\end{array}
\]
Each block is recursively split after $\lfloor k/2\rfloor$ entries.
The two resulting boundary tables are multiplied, and the three final
block tables are contracted. The first plan has block sizes $(6,6,3)$;
the other three have sizes $(5,5,5)$. Every intermediate merge is included
in the following certificate.

Let $D_*=1400000$. Use the round-five rectangular cost with the nine
integer coefficient triples consisting of the distinct permutations of
\[
 (1400007,1400007,0),\qquad(1400000,1175014,750000).
\]
For each operation, assign the three coefficients to its three edge
classes, and zero inside each of its three parts. This gives
$9(15-2)=117$ time supports $v$ per plan, in dimension $\binom{15}{2}=105$.
The corresponding normalized local cost is
$F_j(g)=D_*^{-1}\max_{v\in\mathcal V_j}v\cdot g$, where
$g_{\{i,j\}}=\log_2(|\mathcal I_{ij}|\,|\mathcal I_{ji}|)$.

The exact coupled pair maxima
\[
 A_{ij}=\max_{v\in\mathcal V_i,w\in\mathcal V_j}
       (\|v\|_2^2+\|w\|_2^2+6v\cdot w)
\]
are, in pair order $01,02,03,12,13,23$,
\[
 (A,A,A,B,B,B),\qquad
 A=580173494513132,\quad B=572279799215680.
\]
The standalone standard-library checker reconstructs every support and
checks all $6\cdot117^2=82134$ pairs. By the imported coupled-pair identity,
\[
 \left\|\frac14\sum_{j=0}^3v_j\right\|_2^2
 \le\frac1{48}\sum_{i<j}A_{ij}.
\]
The pointwise inequality $\min_jF_j(g)\le\tfrac14\sum_jF_j(g)$ and the
inherited entropy bound therefore give root local time coefficient
\[
 \beta_{15}^2=
 \frac{2\sum_{i<j}A_{ij}}{48D_*^2\,15^2}
 =\frac{32012591492467}{98000000000000}.
\]
This is a rigorous uniform support certificate. Unlike the doubled
eighteen-point symmetric menu, no matching local minimax assertion is
needed or claimed for the partially doubled menu.

\subsection{Joint selection and its exact storage certificate}

Each stored table corresponds to a subset appearing in one of the three
binary block trees. Its boundary support $b$ is one on edges crossing
that subset and zero elsewhere. There are $2r-3=27$ distinct boundary
supports per root plan. Write $\mathcal B_j$ for this boundary family and
$B_j(g)=\max_{b\in\mathcal B_j}b\cdot g$. Set $\rho^2=231/200$ and augment every time-support
family by its scaled boundary supports: use vectors $v/D_*$ and $\rho b$.

The time/time pair bound is the displayed $A_{ij}/D_*^2$. For two boundary
supports, the unscaled coupled maxima are
\[
 \max(\|b\|^2+\|c\|^2+6b\cdot c)
 =(248,248,248,238,238,238).
\]
For each pair these numbers $B_{ij}$ obey
$231D_*^2B_{ij}\le200A_{ij}$. For a mixed time/boundary pair, define
\[
 \Delta=200A_{ij}-200\|v\|^2-231D_*^2\|b\|^2.
\]
The independent checker verifies, for both orientations of every plan pair,
\[
 \Delta\ge0,\qquad
 \Delta^2\ge36\cdot200\cdot231D_*^2(v\cdot b)^2.
\]
These are exact integer inequalities, and checking the first inequality
before squaring is essential. They imply
\[
 \|v/D_*\|^2+\|\rho b\|^2
       +6(v/D_*)\cdot(\rho b)\le A_{ij}/D_*^2.
\]
All $4374$ boundary/boundary pairs and $37908$ mixed pairs pass.
The same coupled-pair proof now applies to the augmented families.
Selecting the plan with smallest joint objective
$\max\{F_j(g),\rho B_j(g)\}$ bounds both time and $\rho$ times boundary
storage by $(\beta_{15}+o(1))K$. Hence the storage coefficient is
\[
 \sigma_{15}^2=\beta_{15}^2/\rho^2
 =\frac{32012591492467}{113190000000000}.
\]

The implemented specification avoids irrational comparisons. For each
positive integer pair-list product $d_e$, set
$\widehat g_e=(d_e-1).\mathrm{bit\_length}=\lceil\log_2d_e\rceil$.
For plan $j$ compute
\[
 \widehat T_j=\max_{v\in\mathcal V_j}v\cdot\widehat g,
 \qquad \widehat B_j=\max_{b\in\mathcal B_j}b\cdot\widehat g,
\]
and minimize the integer
\[
 \max\{\widehat T_j^2,\;2263800000000\,\widehat B_j^2\}.
\]
Since $\rho^2D_*^2=2263800000000$, this selects the least rounded joint
objective exactly. Every coordinate is rounded upward by less than one;
the support family and all coefficients are fixed. Thus the selected true
objective exceeds its exact minimum by at most $O(1)$, a constant additive
exponent that is negligible on the scale $K$. Empty exact-weight lists
are skipped before selection. The integer bit lengths are polynomial.

\subsection{Charging all recursive entries and storage}

\[

 \qquad K_d^*=\frac{\pi\sqrt2}{\ln2}\frac{\mu_d}{\sqrt{\eta_d}}

$\gamma=(1/2)/\sqrt{63/100}=5/\sqrt{63}$.

\begin{align*}
 C_0&=\beta_{15}K_0^*,\\
 C_1&=\alpha_{15}K_0^*+\beta_{10}K_1^*,\\

every tail aggregation depth as well as the limiting full child-entry

\text{Coefficient}&10.878781667&10.411338011&10.185959328&10.658059532
