\section{Full doubling: a lower-memory alternative}
\label{sec:double18}

The newly placed round-5 packets change the comparison baseline. The
weighted coupled-pair certificate of \emph{sat\_pairwise\_certificates} and
the joint time--space selector of \emph{sat\_affine\_contraction} are used
here as prior methods. In particular, the nine-point affine construction
is no longer a new contribution. The new object is its explicitly ordered
eighteen-point doubling and the compatible low-degree composition below.

Identify point $3x+y$ with $(x,y)\in\mathbb F_3^2$ and add its twin
$9+3x+y$. Order each line by its three original labels, followed by their
three twins, and split blocks by the same midpoint rule. The four plans are
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
(0,1,2,9,10,11)&(3,4,5,12,13,14)&(6,7,8,15,16,17),\\
(0,3,6,9,12,15)&(1,4,7,10,13,16)&(2,5,8,11,14,17),\\
(0,4,8,9,13,17)&(1,5,6,10,14,15)&(2,3,7,11,12,16),\\
(0,5,7,9,14,16)&(1,3,8,10,12,17)&(2,4,6,11,13,15).
\end{array}
\]

Let $D_*=1{,}400{,}000$. The rectangular backend has nine integer supports
per operation, the distinct permutations of
\[
 (1400007,1400007,0),\qquad (1400000,1175014,750000).
\]
For each of the four doubled affine plans, let $\mathcal T_j$ be its
$144$ time masks and $\mathcal B_j$ its $33$ zero--one boundary masks.
The boundary family includes every singleton, two-vertex table,
three-vertex table, and six-vertex block table; the last output boundary
is already represented by the complementary final block.

Write
\[
 A=811701837623520,
 \qquad \rho^2=\frac{A}{336D_*^2}
              =\frac{1691045495049}{1372000000000}.
\]
The independently reconstructed supports satisfy, for every two distinct
plans and all corresponding supports,
\begin{align}
 \|v\|^2+\|w\|^2+6v\cdot w&\le A,
       &&v\in\mathcal T_i,\ w\in\mathcal T_j,\label{eq:audit-tt}\\
 \|b\|^2&\le72,\qquad b\cdot c\le32,
       &&b\in\mathcal B_i,\ c\in\mathcal B_j.\label{eq:audit-bb}
\end{align}
Every one of the six time-pair maxima in \eqref{eq:audit-tt} equals $A$.
For a direct formula for this value, put
$y=(1400000,1175014,750000)$. A final mask has squared norm
$36\sum y_i^2$, and two transverse final masks have dot product
$8(\sum y_i)^2$. Thus
\[
 A=72\sum_i y_i^2+48\left(\sum_i y_i\right)^2.
\]

Mixed time--boundary supports obey the additional bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:audit-tb}
 \|v/D_*\|^2+\rho^2\|b\|^2+6\rho(v/D_*)\cdot b\le A/D_*^2.
\end{equation}
This is certified without approximating $\rho$: set
\[
 D=A(336-\|b\|^2)-336\|v\|^2.
\]
For each mixed pair the verifier checks the exact integer inequalities
\[
 D\ge0,\qquad D^2\ge36\cdot336\cdot A\,(v\cdot b)^2.
\]
After taking nonnegative square roots these are precisely
\eqref{eq:audit-tb}. The finite audit checks $124{,}416$ time pairs,
$6{,}534$ boundary pairs, and $57{,}024$ mixed pairs, with no violation.

For illustration, the largest structured final-operation mixed case has
a short algebraic explanation. Put $S=(y_1+y_2+y_3 )/D_*$ and
$a=36\sum_i(y_i/D_*)^2$. Then
$A/D_*^2=2a+48S^2=336\rho^2$. Its mixed expression is
$a+72\rho^2+96S\rho$, and the explicit nonnegative difference is
\[
 A/D_*^2-(a+72\rho^2+96S\rho)=24(2\rho-S)^2.
\] The exhaustive mixed checks also include all intermediate
operations and smaller boundaries.

For nonnegative entropy vector $g$, define the actual local exponents
\[
 F_j(g)=D_*^{-1}\max_{v\in\mathcal T_j}v\cdot g,
 \qquad G_j(g)=\max_{b\in\mathcal B_j}b\cdot g,
 \qquad J_j(g)=\max\{F_j(g),\rho G_j(g)\}.
\]
The support family for $J_j$ is
$\mathcal T_j/D_*\ \cup\ \rho\mathcal B_j$.
For any selection of one vector $z_j$ from each such family,
\[
 \left\|\sum_{j=1}^4z_j\right\|^2
 =\frac13\sum_{i<j}
       (\|z_i\|^2+\|z_j\|^2+6z_i\cdot z_j)
 \le\frac{2A}{D_*^2}.
\]
Here the time pairs use \eqref{eq:audit-tt}, boundary pairs use
$\rho^2(2\cdot72+6\cdot32)=A/D_*^2$, and mixed pairs use
\eqref{eq:audit-tb}. The support-averaging and inherited entropy estimate
therefore give a simultaneous local time--space pair
\begin{equation}\label{eq:audit-joint-local}
 \beta^2=\frac{A}{4D_*^2\cdot18^2}
        =\frac{563681831683}{1764000000000},
 \qquad \sigma^2=\frac{\beta^2}{\rho^2}=\frac7{27}.
\end{equation}

The time certificate is exact for this fixed menu under the Euclidean
entropy relaxation. Let $g$ be the sum of one final-operation support
with coefficient order $(1400000,1175014,750000)$ from each plan.
The independent time checker verifies
\[
 \|g\|^2=1623403675247040=2A,\qquad
 \max_{v\in\mathcal T_j}v\cdot g=405850918811760=A/2
\]
for each plan, including all internal operations. Thus the normalized
local time bound is attained in that relaxation. No realizability by an
actual SAT input, or lower bound for a different algorithm, is asserted.

\paragraph{An executable integer selector.}
Exact comparison of expressions involving logarithms or an irrational
coefficient is unnecessary. For every nonempty exact mode-list product
$D_e=|\mathcal I_{gh}|\,|\mathcal I_{hg}|$, compute
\[
 \widehat g_e=(D_e-1).\texttt{bit\_length}()
             =\lceil\log_2D_e\rceil.
\]
This equals zero for $D_e=1$, and satisfies
$g_e\le\widehat g_e<g_e+1$. Define the integers
\[
 \widehat T_j=\max_{v\in\mathcal T_j}v\cdot\widehat g,
 \qquad \widehat B_j=\max_{b\in\mathcal B_j}b\cdot\widehat g.
\]
Choose the first plan minimizing the integer score
\begin{equation}\label{eq:audit-selector}
 \max\{336\widehat T_j^2,\ A\widehat B_j^2\}.
\end{equation}
This exactly minimizes $J_j(\widehat g)$, since its square times
$336D_*^2$ is the score. Because the finite support families and $r=18$ are
fixed, $0\le J_j(\widehat g)-J_j(g)\le C_0$ for one constant $C_0$.
Thus the selected plan satisfies
\[
 J_{\rm selected}(g)\le\min_jJ_j(g)+C_0.
\]
The additive constant is lower order in the exponent and preserves both
coefficients in \eqref{eq:audit-joint-local}. This selector, implemented
as \texttt{select\_joint} in the independent checker, is the algorithm
used for the joint theorem. A selector that minimizes time alone is not
asserted to have the sharper space bound.

\subsection{The low-degree $18\to9$ composition}

Under the normalized root degree premise
$\Delta\le\lceil s^{1/12}\rceil$, take
\[
 r_0=18,\quad u_0=\left\lfloor\frac{107}{1000}\lambda\right\rfloor,
 \qquad
 r_d=9,\quad u_d=\left\lfloor
       \frac{29}{1600}\left(\frac38\right)^{d-1}\lambda
                         \right\rfloor\quad(d\ge1).
\]
Use the same logarithmic guards, immutable precision conventions, near-unit
padding, and leaf threshold $s/\lambda$ as in the audited interface.
The total leading budget is
$107/1000+(29/1600)/(1-3/8)=17/125$.
The exact capacity witnesses are
\[
 648^{107}<2^{1000},\qquad162^{17}<2^{125}.
\]
The root budget exceeds $1/12$ and all cumulative budgets increase, so
original vertices and all retained older packets are dominated by the
latest packet scale after the root. These strict capacity margins cover
the resulting symmetric packing gates and all additive guards. A tail
coarse child ratio $1/4$ and geometric taper ratio $3/8$ give a growing
least taper through the hypothetical active depth
$\log_4\lambda+O(1)$, closing the usual noncircular depth argument.

Use the new joint selector at the root and the prior round-5 nine-point
joint selector in the tail. The latter has local time $1/\sqrt3$ and
local space $\sqrt{7/27}$. This slightly weaker tail time bound is already
enough, so no new tail rectangular certificate is needed for this theorem.
For this schedule, redefine its normalized scales by
\[
 K_0^*=\frac\kappa{\sqrt{107/1000}},\qquad
 K_1^*=\frac\kappa{9\sqrt{29/1600}}.
\]
With $\alpha_{18}=\sqrt{34}/18$, $\alpha_9=4/9$,
\[
 \gamma=\frac{2/9}{\sqrt{3/8}}=\sqrt{\frac{32}{243}},\qquad
 x=\frac{K_1^*}{K_0^*}=\sqrt{\frac{856}{11745}},
\]
the tail's paid contribution is at most
\[
 K_0^*\left(\alpha_{18}
      +x\max\left\{\frac1{\sqrt3},\frac{\alpha_9}{1-\gamma}\right\}\right).
\]
Exact rational bounds give
\[
 \alpha_{18}<\frac{13}{40},\quad
 x<\frac{27}{100},\quad
 \gamma<\frac{23}{63},\quad
 \frac1{\sqrt3}<\frac7{10},\quad
 \frac{\alpha_9}{1-\gamma}<\frac7{10}.
\]
Consequently this branch is below
$(257/500)K_0^*<(14/25)K_0^*<\beta K_0^*$.
Every child entry is therefore paid, and root local work dominates.

Since $x<1$ and $\gamma<1$, the largest local storage exponent is also at
the root. The boundary exponent of simultaneous ancestor tables adds in
storage size, not in the exponent; there are only $O(\log\lambda)$ levels.
This gives
\begin{align*}
 C_T&=\frac\pi{\ln2}\sqrt{\frac{563681831683}{94374000000}}
      =11.0768238350204\ldots,\\
 C_S&=\frac\pi{\ln2}\sqrt{\frac{14000}{2889}}
      =9.97733739357645\ldots.
\end{align*}
These coefficients multiply $s/\sqrt\lambda$ in the respective base-two
exponents. They are normalized-wire coefficients under the stated degree
premise, not coefficients on the number of arbitrary 3-SAT variables.
