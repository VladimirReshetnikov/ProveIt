\section{Linear-work evaluation of full-crossing Cauchy features}
Let $L=2^a$, $R=2^i$, $0\leq i<a$, and $N=L/2$.  Define
\[
 s_j=(j\bmod R)+2R\lfloor j/R\rfloor,\qquad 0\leq j<N,
 \quad T=t+2^{a+1}\zeta,\quad v_2(t)=i,\quad -L\leq t<L,
\]
where $\zeta$ is integral in an unramified extension of $\mathbb Q_2$.
Put $q_b=\lfloor b/R\rfloor$ and
$E_b=q_b+v_2(q_b!)=2q_b-\operatorname{popcount}(q_b)$.
For a fixed port $j$, the reviewed normalized factors are
\[
 f_b(j)=2^{-E_b}\frac{T}{T+s_j-s_b}
          \prod_{h<b}\frac{s_j-s_h}{T+s_j-s_h},\qquad
 g_b(j)=2^{-E_b}\frac{T}{T+s_b-s_j}
          \prod_{h<b}\frac{s_h-s_j}{T+s_h-s_j}.
\]
Both factors vanish when $j<b$.  Writing
$C(X)=\prod_{q=-L}^{L-1}(X-q)$ and
$A=C(T)/(C'(0)T)$, the target/source pair used in the kernel is
\[
 (A2^{2E_b}f_b(j),\ g_b(j)).
\]
This section changes only how these formulas are evaluated. Their algebraic
identity and the global contraction reductions remain the reviewed baseline.

\paragraph{Streaming recurrence.}
Set $P_0^+(j)=P_0^-(j)=1$ and update
\[
 P_{b+1}^{\pm}(j)=P_b^{\pm}(j)
 \frac{\pm(s_j-s_b)}{T\pm(s_j-s_b)}.
\]
Then
\[
 f_b(j)=2^{-E_b}P_b^+(j)\frac{T}{T+s_j-s_b},\qquad
 g_b(j)=2^{-E_b}P_b^-(j)\frac{T}{T-s_j+s_b}.
\]
The update is stopped at $b=j$; all subsequent features are zero.
This handles zero products explicitly instead of assigning a finite valuation
to zero.

\paragraph{Integrality.}
For two slots in the same orientation, a nonzero difference $d=s_j-s_h$
has either $v_2(d)<i$ or $v_2(d)\geq i+1$. Therefore
$v_2(T\pm d)=\min\{i,v_2(d)\}$, and both
$d/(T+d)$ and $T/(T+d)$ are integral.  Among the indices $h<b$ with
$h\equiv j\pmod R$, there are $c\geq q_b$ indices, and their quotient
indices are consecutive integers starting at zero. Their contribution to
$v_2(P_b^\pm(j))$ is
\[
 c+v_2\bigl(q_j(q_j-1)\cdots(q_j-c+1)\bigr)
 \ \geq\ c+v_2(c!)\ \geq\ E_b.
\]
The first inequality follows because the falling factorial is divisible by
$c!$. All other factors have nonnegative valuation, as does
$T/(T\pm(s_j-s_b))$. Thus the final exponent after removing $E_b$ is
nonnegative. This argument applies for $b\leq j$; the remaining features
are identically zero.

\paragraph{Fixed precision.}
Store each nonzero prefix as a pair $(v,u)$ representing $2^v u$, with the
unit $u$ reduced modulo $2^p$ and $v$ stored exactly. Factor every numerator
and denominator before modular reduction. Invert only the unit part of a
denominator. When emitting a feature, subtract the known $E_b$ from the
exact valuation and multiply the stored unit by the resulting nonnegative
power of two. No division of a previously reduced modular residue occurs.
A unit approximation modulo $2^p$ remains sufficient after multiplication
by any nonnegative power of two, so the method needs no extra precision.
The prefactor is treated identically after cancelling $T$:
\[
 A=\prod_{\substack{-L\leq q<L\\q\ne0}}\frac{T-q}{-q}.
\]
A zero numerator gives $A=0$ exactly. For nonzero $\zeta$, factorial
cancellation gives
$v_2(A)=a+1-i+v_2(\zeta)\geq0$.

\paragraph{Cost and compatibility.}
For $P$ requested ports and $B\leq N$ modes, separate evaluation of every
product costs $O(L+PB^2)$ ring operations and unit inversions. The recurrence
costs $O(L+PB)$ of each: the common prefactor costs $O(L)$, and every emitted
feature and prefix update takes constant work. These are arithmetic counts
at the same precision and in the same coefficient ring. Per-port streaming
uses $O(1)$ auxiliary ring elements and integer counters; storing all outputs
uses $O(PB)$ elements. Features of weight
$a+1-i+2E_b\geq p$ may be dropped exactly, since their targets vanish modulo
$2^p$. The weights are nondecreasing, so dropping the tail does not interrupt
a later retained prefix. The result composes with the full-orientation
Cauchy modes and any of the reviewed packing or precision schedules.
It improves a local polynomial factor; it does not establish a change in
the global subexponential exponent or an end-to-end SAT runtime speedup.
