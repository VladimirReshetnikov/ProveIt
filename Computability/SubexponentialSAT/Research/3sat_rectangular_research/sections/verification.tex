\section{Reproducibility and proof status}\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{What the accompanying programs verify}

The archive is self-contained for its finite certificate checks and its
SAT normalization tests. Those programs require only the Python standard
library. Their acceptance conditions use explicit exceptions, so running
Python with optimization does not disable the checks.

There are $2\,460\,861$ exhaustive pair comparisons in the two support
suites together. The backend-support checker also tests 2,197 small
integer dimension-log triples against the sorted formula. These samples
are a regression check; the rearrangement and interpolation proofs,
rather than the samples, establish the formula for all dimensions.

The normalization suite uses 171 raw formulas and constructs 684
networks. It directly evaluates 664 complete integer tensor contractions,
enumerating 361,624 edge assignments, and compares with 8,376 original
variable assignments. Separately, 40 equality gadgets are checked on
2,550 boundary conditions, using 310,950 boundary/internal assignments.
Twenty-five larger structural cases reach 3,994 wires. All eight invalid
inputs are rejected; incorrect literal signs and broken equality are
detected. These finite tests accompany the general proof in
Theorem~\ref{thm:equality}; they do not substitute for it.

The new fifteen-plan certificate also has an independent reconstruction
of its trees and all 1,437,345 pair comparisons. Its fixed-width integer
implementation proves that the largest possible intermediate value is
below $2^{63}$, then recomputes maximizing witnesses with arbitrary-size
integers. The main supplied checker uses arbitrary-size integers throughout.

The suites passed both ordinary execution and \texttt{python3 -O}, with
identical mathematical outputs. The final archive reruns the portable
entry points. Exact result receipts and negative-control evidence are
included. Neither a floating-point optimizer nor an unfinished exploratory
search supplies any accepted theorem or inequality.

\subsection{What is proved, imported, and not implemented}

The new mathematical content is the exact characteristic-zero backend
bridge, the updated finite support certificates including the cyclic
fifteen-plan construction, the fully charged integer schedules, the
growing-degree equality normalization, and the scoped locality lemma.
The source Cauchy/Hermite contraction, packing and entropy interfaces are
imported with their hypotheses; the two characteristic-zero matrix
exponents are primary-source theorem dependencies. This report does not
independently reprove all of those sources.

The archive does not contain a full subexponential SAT solver, explicit
large matrix schemes achieving the imported exponents, or a Lean
formalization of the new results. The fixed algebraic-scheme search proves
effectivity, but it may have an enormous fixed cost. The packing margins
and root-degree comparisons are eventual asymptotic statements, not
useful finite-size thresholds or benchmark predictions.

The audit of the supplied Anthropic development records a successful
upstream CI build at the requested SHA and checks its theorem statement
and selected interfaces. It is not a new local Lean build. In particular,
its big-$O$ machine theorem must not be cited as formal verification of
the new numerical coefficients.

\subsection{Archive guide}

\texttt{report.tex} and \texttt{sections/} contain the complete source;
\texttt{report.pdf} is the compiled report. \texttt{code/} contains the
portable checks and equality-network compiler, \texttt{data/} the exact
plans and weights, and \texttt{results/} the reproduced outputs.
\texttt{audit/} records independent and formal-source evidence.
\texttt{provenance.json} pins the reviewed repositories and incoming
archives. \texttt{PROOF\_STATUS.json} separates theorem dependencies,
implemented components, and unresolved objectives.

Run \texttt{python3 code/run\_checks.py} from any directory to regenerate
the main certificates. Add \texttt{--optimized} to run the suites with
Python optimization. Run \texttt{sh build\_report.sh} to rebuild the PDF
using a normal LaTeX installation. Detailed commands and component scope
are in \texttt{README.txt}. No downloaded prior research packet is needed
to execute these finite checks.

The principal remaining objective is mathematical: produce a compatible
replacement that improves the logarithmic exponent order. The exact
certificates here make the improved coefficient bounds reviewable while
leaving that objective explicitly open.
