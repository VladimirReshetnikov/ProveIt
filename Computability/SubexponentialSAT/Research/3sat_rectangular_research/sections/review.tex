\subsection{What the cited Lean development establishes}

The supplied Anthropic SHA
\texttt{1a6eee293b9e00cb432eef6018a21f523623a9af} is the exact PR~31 head.
It was merged as \texttt{16a5ce41304fcdaea4e0be7c132e1c361dc26da5}.
The formal statement quantifies one deterministic finite-alphabet machine
and one constant before the formula and clause width. Specializing to
3-SAT gives exact counting within
\[
 2^{O\left(\min\left\{
 m/\sqrt{\log m},\ n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n}\right\}\right)}.
\]
The formal promises include distinct clause lists, every variable occurring,
$n\ge4$, and $m\ge2$; elementary preprocessing and the separate naming
frontend handle general encodings. This is a uniform machine theorem,
not merely a different machine for each desired exponential rate
\cite{anthropicmain,anthropicsolution}.

The exact SHA has a successful GitHub Actions verification run, including
the build and the declared Comparator checks \cite{anthropicci}.
A static source scan found the two intended challenge placeholders and
no additional occurrences of \texttt{sorry}, \texttt{axiom},
\texttt{admit}, or \texttt{unsafe}. Lean and Lake are unavailable in this
execution environment, so this work did not perform a fresh kernel build.
The audit examines the endpoint and its principal interfaces, not each of
the roughly 1,050 proof modules.

The formal proof does not certify the numeric coefficients of any ProveIt
refinement. Its intermediate machine simulation can cost a fixed power of
an operation budget, which preserves the stated big-$O$ order but changes
the constant in the exponent. The explicit comments in
\texttt{Solution.lean} explain this distinction. Our coefficients instead
come from the direct bit-operation accounting in the stated contraction
and matrix interfaces.
