\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{A smaller unrestricted logarithmic order is not established.}
The user's target is stronger than the paper's exponent
$O(n\log\log n/\sqrt{\log n})$, not merely a smaller constant or a new proof of that rate.
The recent ProveIt arrivals preserve this order; their stronger targets remain conditional.
We derive a concrete improvement on their normalized tensor interface:
\[
 (s+\mathcal L)^{O(1)}
 2^{(84.0662714943\ldots+o(1))s/\sqrt{\lceil\log_2s\rceil}}
 \quad\text{with polynomial space},
\]
with a conservative eventual coefficient $85$, compared with the supplied $648.4411$ certificate.
The new ingredients are deterministic matching-based collision packing, a thinner precision gap,
exact crossing-packet accounting, and a two-stage grouping schedule.
This is a mathematical derivation relative to unchanged source identities, not an independently
verified full SAT algorithm, a Lean theorem, a practical benchmark, or a coefficient directly on $n$.
We also identify precise missing interfaces for a true order improvement and prove narrow barriers
to several shortcuts. The canonical source relocation is byte-preserving and adds no new theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{What this review establishes}
Fix clause width $k=3$. Write $n$ for the number of variables, $m$ for the
number of clauses, $L_n=\log_2 n$, and $\ell_n=\log_2\log_2 n$; small inputs
are handled separately. Polynomial factors include input reading and exact arithmetic.
The imported OpenAI manuscript claims an exact-counting, deterministic polynomial-space bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:baseline}
 \poly(n,m)2^{O(n\ell_n/\sqrt{L_n})},
\end{equation}
and, at each fixed density $m\le Dn$, the stronger exponent
$O_D(n/\sqrt{L_n})$ \cite{openai}.
The meaningful primary target here is an unrestricted exponent
$o(n\ell_n/\sqrt{L_n})$, with all algorithmic and space costs paid.

\textbf{The strongest available new conclusion is a coefficient improvement.}
Section~\ref{sec:combined} proves the displayed $84.0663$ contraction certificate on exactly the
normalized interface used by the supplied $648.4411$ certificate.
At most $s$ edges, maximum degree $\lceil s^{0.9}\rceil$, initial precision and
truncation parameter at most $s$, and the source's entry-oracle allowance are essential
normalizations. Replacing $s$ by $O(n\log\log n)$ is not a license to put $84.0663$
before $n\log\log n$: the reduction's hidden constants remain.

The stronger proposal already present in the precision packet would give
$O(n\ell_n/L_n)$ if its structured-summation hypothesis were proved.
That is asymptotically stronger than our coefficient result and remains unproved.
We do not call a successful synthesis of constants a solution to the user's stronger-order request.

\textbf{Trust boundary.} ``Derived'' and ``proved relative to the source'' below refer to
ordinary mathematical arguments conditional on the unchanged source deformation, integrality,
recovery and recursive-factorization identities. Local exact checks are regression evidence,
not a substitute for an asymptotic proof. No imported program, proof build or installer was run.
No full SAT solver or full formal proof was constructed or executed.

\subsection{The improvements that can be composed}
Exact taper and precision improvements, integral factorial-Cauchy modes, and the new packing
act on different parts of the contraction argument and can be composed after checking their
shared precision and recursive interface. The scalar parity reduction may independently set
the truncated coefficient length to one. The density refinement independently reduces the
Boolean wire budget to
\[
 O_k\!\left(n\min\{\log(2+m/n),\log\log n\}\right).
\]
Neither reduction should be multiplied blindly into a numerical coefficient on the normalized
$s$ interface. The two density packets mostly repeat the same mathematical mechanism.

The inspected density proof chain uses a lazy Margulis--Gabber--Galil walk, powering for small
spectral error, exhaustive local-code search, shortening, and greedy exact-count minimization.
The cost of local-code search is explicitly $2^{O(d^2)}$, not polynomial. After choosing
polylogarithmic parameters, it is lower order than $2^{O(n/\sqrt{L_n})}$.
High-incidence branching and degree-array encoding preserve polynomial space and yield
$O(n\log(S+2))$ wires for $S$ actual monomials per parity equation.
This source audit found no fatal gap in that chain relative to the imported identities;
it did not rebuild a solver or formal proof. The external expander theorem is an input,
not established by tiny graph tests \cite{mgg}.

\subsection{Why the conditional targets are not achieved}
The precision packet's exact rank network has only $O(M/\sqrt u+r^2)$ wires in its
compressed representation, but the group tensors have excessive degree. Their subexponential
entry evaluators do not make them bounded-degree factors. Equality-tree splitting cannot
represent an arbitrary high-arity tensor, and restoring the original child network loses
the compression. Its missing summation theorem is a real algorithmic obligation.

Similarly, the parity packet's compact-factorization assumption must cover every generated
group tensor, invalid-rank zeros included, with $O(W)$ total Boolean edges, maximum degree
$W^{0.9}$, correct immutable creation precision, and construction/entry time
$\poly(s)\exp(O(s/\log s+s^{0.9}))$. No such construction is supplied.
The prior degree-kernel route also needs a deformation degree bound and a mode-family
count measured by the compressed wire universe at every recursive child. A small root
incidence degree alone does not establish these properties.

\section{What the stronger order problem still requires}\label{sec:frontier}
There are three distinct constructive routes, not three established ingredients.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A degree-sensitive compressed deformation.} Produce a recursively closed
kernel with global degree $K$ and mode multiplicity $O(M/\Dt)$ where $M$ is the compressed
wire count. An exterior-power identity with degree $K=O(n)$ is insufficient if its modes
still count the uncompressed incidence universe.
\item \textbf{Structured mode aggregation.} Factor or sum the genuine high-arity rank tensors
within the precision packet's target cost, preserving invalid-index zeros, bounded degree,
polynomial descriptions, exact entry costs and polynomial space.
\item \textbf{A different effective crossing algebra.} In the alternate manuscript's framework,
construct a graded family with entropy exponent below $1/2$ while preserving exact crossing
reconstruction, both factorizations, locality or a valid replacement, and effective integer costs.
\end{enumerate}
Any proposed improvement must retain deterministic exact arithmetic, polynomial-sized
coordinate precision, a fixed polynomial dependence on description length, and immutable
creation-time representatives. Reducing a leaf cutoff does not remove the root entropy.
Exponentially many simultaneously stored tensor entries are not compatible with polynomial space.
These are mathematical open interfaces, not denied permissions or missing tool access.

\subsection{A genuine entropy lever and a restricted obstruction}
Appendix H observes that if the full effective construction worked with
$\log\dim V_E\sim C E^\alpha$, its concavity argument would yield
\[
 \rho=2^{\alpha-1},\qquad \log_2(1/\rho)=1-\alpha.
\]
Thus $\alpha<1/2$ could improve the actual denominator power, if every required identity and
cost bound survived. The manuscript does not construct such a replacement.
The following new elementary deductions show why simply thinning the existing space is not enough.

\begin{lemma}[Partition states forced by the same Heisenberg relations]
Let $W=\bigoplus_E W_E$ be a graded vector space over a characteristic-zero field, with
$0\ne v\in W_0$. For every positive integer $m$, suppose commuting creation operators
$a_{-m}$ have degree $m$, commuting annihilators $a_m$ have degree $-m$,
$[a_m,a_{-j}]=m\delta_{mj}I$, and $a_mv=0$. Then $\dim W_E\ge p(E)$,
the ordinary partition number.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each finite-support vector $\beta\ge0$ with $\sum_m m\beta_m=E$, set
$v_\beta=\prod_m a_{-m}^{\beta_m}v$. In a linear relation among these vectors,
apply $A_\beta=\prod_m a_m^{\beta_m}$. If $\gamma\ne\beta$ has the same weighted
sum, some coordinate satisfies $\beta_m>\gamma_m$; commuting annihilators rightward
then kills $v_\gamma$. The remaining term is
\[
 A_\beta v_\beta=\left(\prod_m m^{\beta_m}\beta_m!\right)v\ne0.
\]
Thus the coefficient of $v_\beta$ is zero. This holds for all $\beta$, proving independence.
\end{proof}
An elementary bound suffices: choose $r$ with $\sum_{j=2}^r j\le E$. Every subset of $\{2,\ldots,r\}$, padded to energy $E$ with ones, gives a distinct partition. Hence $p(E)\ge2^{r-1}$ with $r=\Theta(\sqrt E)$, and $\log\dim W_E=\Omega(\sqrt E)$.
A nonzero quotient preserving all these exact relations and the vacuum cannot achieve
$\alpha<1/2$. This is not a lower bound for SAT, tensor contraction, all graded algebras,
or all crossing decompositions.

\begin{lemma}[Naive infinite thinning loses ordinary finite-order locality]
Retain only modes in a set $S\subseteq\mathbb Z_{>0}$ in the standard Heisenberg current.
If $S$ and its positive complement are both infinite, no finite $H$ makes its scalar
commutator vanish after multiplication by $(z-w)^H$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Its coefficient sequence is $c_j=j\mathbf1_{|j|\in S}$, with $c_0=0$.
Multiplication by $z-w$ takes a forward difference after reindexing, so locality would give
$\Delta^Hc_j=0$ for every integer $j$. In characteristic zero this is a polynomial sequence
of degree at most $H-1$. Infinitely many missing positive modes force infinitely many roots,
so the polynomial vanishes identically; retained positive modes contradict this.
\end{proof}
Periodic thinning may yield other diagonals of locality, but not the ordinary locality
used by the source. Nonlinear regrading, for example $\deg t_m=m^2$, also breaks the
printed homogeneous-shift rule: the degree of a product is no longer determined by
its linear mode-index sum. Linear rescaling preserves the square-root entropy power.
A new construction could escape these assumptions, but must reprove exact reconstruction,
both integer factorizations, polynomially many pieces, polynomial bit lengths, and effective
$2^{O(\lambda)}$ evaluation. A smaller Hilbert series alone is not an algorithm.

\section{Recommended next research steps}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{For a genuine order improvement, prioritize structured summation.} Try to
factor the actual generated rank tensors rather than a generic high-arity tensor. A candidate
must state exact construction, Boolean edge count, degree, entry bit-cost, precision lifting,
invalid-index handling and recursion closure before any improved rate is claimed.
\item \textbf{Explore actual-row separation only as a constant refinement.} The slope-four
Smith profile motivates a better integral construction on the restricted reciprocal rows,
but the family multiplicity and recursive compatibility must be paid. It cannot by itself
change the logarithmic order.
\item \textbf{Optimize depth-dependent schedules only after preserving finite guards.}
The two-stage schedule is not claimed optimal. Extra stages may lower the coefficient,
but must respect the initial degree exponent, packing slack, cumulative precision gap,
and uniform leaf-depth argument. Such optimization still does not solve the primary target.
\item \textbf{Request the alternate proof sources before treating its formal claims as verified.}
Check the actual theorem type against its strongest printed statement, pinned dependencies,
axioms, full kernel/checker logs and machine-cost correspondence. A placeholder link is not evidence.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Finite parameter conditions and crossover caution}
The new schedule has exceptionally small packing slack:
\[
 1-\left(0.9+\frac{20\log_2 37}{1043}\right)
 =0.000106359240\ldots,
\]
\[
 1-(0.9+6A)=\frac{31}{307685}=0.000100752392\ldots.
\]
Thus factors such as $s^{-0.00010075}\polylog(s)$ tend to zero extremely slowly.
The proof is an eventual asymptotic certificate, not an optimized finite threshold.
No numerical global crossover $s_0$ is claimed.

For a finite implementation, test the actual integer parameters: all retained taper levels
must be positive; their count must fit the packet exponent; the matching potential must start
below one; padding must satisfy the desired child-size guard; the new degree $2L$ must fit the
interface; the field extension must dominate deformation degree; and every precision and
entry-cost bound must hold. Direct summation handles exceptional sizes or failed guards.
The proof shows these guards eventually hold uniformly, so fallback is confined to a finite
exceptional range for fixed interface constants. This is an existence/complexity statement,
not a supplied finite solver implementation.

The additive gap $g=(\lceil\log_2\lambda\rceil)^2$ is large enough to make
$\lambda^B2^{-g}=o(1)$ for every fixed $B$, yet its cumulative cost
$O((\log\lambda)^3)$ is $o(\lambda)$. This separation is central: one must not
replace the gap by a fixed constant without a new inherited-precision proof.
