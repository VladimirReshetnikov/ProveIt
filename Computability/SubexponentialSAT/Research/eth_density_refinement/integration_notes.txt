1. build/main_full_version.tex, lemma "sparse-checks"

  w = ceil(log2(2B))
  delta = 1/(1024 B w)
  P = 2(1024 B w)^2

Use a lazy MGG expander power and a local code with ceil(d/(32B))

also be retained; either approach gives the same asymptotic order.
