THE STRICT RATIONAL 9 -> 13 -> 7 SCHEDULE
Independent derivation for the adaptive contraction-tree report

Status. This is a relative refinement of the normalized tensor-contraction
interface in the supplied ProveIt packets. It imports their deformation,
canonical creation-precision convention, slope-four modes, sharp Hermite
precision, asymmetric near-lossless packing, weighted pair entropy, and
exact matrix-multiplication bridge. The only additional inputs are the
independently checkable three-tree certificates
  b_9^2 = 6608/18225,   b_13^2 = 1572/4225.
It does not independently certify the upstream ETH manuscript, change the
logarithmic order, or supply an end-to-end practical SAT implementation.

1. Normalization and exact local constants

Write lambda=ceil(log_2 s), kappa=pi sqrt(2)/ln 2. A call with M wires and
taper u has K=kappa M/sqrt(u). Let
  a_r = sqrt(2(r-1))/r.
For an r-group call, cache the group functions and then select the best
available tree using the exact pair-index list lengths. The paid time
recurrence is
  T_d <= poly(s) [2^{(a_{r_d}+o(1))K_d} T_{d+1}
                         +2^{(b_{r_d}+o(1))K_d}],
apart from one overall fixed polynomial in s+L accounting for input entry
routines and descriptions. The exponential table-construction part does
not treat a group function as a unit-cost oracle.

At r=9 and r=13 use the adaptive portfolios and the displayed b-values.
At r=7 an ordinary fixed balanced tree suffices: blocks (3,2,2), splitting
the size-three block as 1+2. Its largest triangle cost is
  32-(21/25)*8=632/25,
so one can safely use
  b_7^2 = 632/1225, b_7 = sqrt(632)/35.
(The sharper optimal binary tree gives 624/1225, but this schedule does
not need that optimization.) Both satisfy b_7^2<3/4. The singleton
cache coefficient is below b_r for each of r=9,13,7.

Every table that the selected tree stores is an input or output of a
matrix multiplication whose time exponent is at least that table's size
exponent. Thus its total space is at most poly(s+L)2^{(b_r+o(1))K}, including
cached singleton tables. This avoids reusing an obsolete fixed-tree space
constant after adaptively changing the tree.

2. Parameters

Put
  r_0=9,       u_0=floor((63/4000)lambda),
  r_1=13,      u_1=floor((9/800)lambda),
  r_d=7,       u_d=floor((77/16200)lambda (4/9)^{d-2}), d>=2.
Also put
  b=ceil(6 log_2 lambda),
  a_d=sum_{j=0}^d u_j+(d+1)b,
  L_d=2^{a_d},
  H=ceil(s/lambda^{3/4}).
A call with M<=H is evaluated directly. Use the near-lossless padding
construction with relative slack, for example, lambda^{-3}. This is
permitted by the strict polynomial capacity margins below and ensures
that products of per-level 1+o(1) losses over O(log lambda) levels remain
1+o(1), rather than merely assuming that fact.

The exact identities behind the capacity allocation are
  63/4000+9/800 = 27/1000,
  63/4000+9/800+(77/16200)/(1-4/9) = 8/225.
Therefore
  a_0=(63/4000)lambda+O(log lambda),
  a_1=(27/1000)lambda+O(log lambda),
  a_d <= (8/225)lambda+O((log lambda)^2)
throughout a logarithmic-depth path.

3. Noncircular depth bootstrap and degree invariant

Hypothesize active depths only up to
  D_max=3+ceil(3 log(lambda)/(4 log 3)).
At all these depths the deepest taper is at least lambda^{eta+o(1)}, where
  eta = 1 - 3 log(9/4)/(4 log 3) > 0.44.
Thus every active taper tends uniformly to infinity. The floor errors,
buffers and number of levels contribute only O((log lambda)^2) to a_d.
All initial degrees are <=ceil(s^{9/10}), and each new packet vertex has
at most 2L_d=s^{8/225+o(1)} ports. Hence the global degree invariant remains
s^{9/10+o(1)} (and, eventually, new packet degrees are much less than the
original cap).

At the root both sides have degree at most s^{9/10+o(1)}. With
Q_0=9^{a_0}, the root packing condition follows from
  9/10+2*(63/4000)log_2 9 < 1.
At depth 1, one side consists of retained old vertices of degree at most
s^{9/10+o(1)}, while the newly created opposite side has degree at most
2^{a_0+1}. With Q_1=13^{a_1}, the potentially tight asymmetric condition is
  9/10+(27/1000)log_2 13 < 1.
For d>=2 the analogous tight condition is
  9/10+(8/225)log_2 7 < 1.
The conditions Q_d^2 C_d polylog(s)=o(M) and L_d Q_d^2=o(M) are much
looser: their exponents are bounded above by
  (8/225)(2log_2 13+1)<1.
Indeed C_d<=2^{a_{d-1}+1} for the fresh side. The M denominator costs at
most lambda^{3/4} at an active call. All other factors are logarithmic.

Entirely rational certificates are
  9^100 < 2^317,
  13^1000 < 2^3701,
  7^100 < 2^281.
These give respective exponent gaps of at least
  29/200000, 73/1000000, 1/11250.
They absorb arbitrary fixed powers of logarithms, including the
lambda^{-3} packing slack and all buffer terms. The auxiliary exponent is
bounded using log_2 13<3701/1000.

Consequently packing holds uniformly in the hypothesized depth range.
Children have (2/r_d+O(lambda^{-3}))M wires. In particular, tail children
have at most M/3 wires for all sufficiently large s. If an active call
persisted beyond D_max, this shrinkage would give M<=H, a contradiction.
This closes the induction without presupposing the depth bound needed
for the packing estimates. The sharper ratios now imply uniformly
  M_0<=s,
  M_1<=(1+o(1))(2/9)s,
  M_d<=(1+o(1))(4/117)(2/7)^{d-2}s, d>=2.

4. Uniform precision and the slope-four entropy interface

At active interpolation nodes i<u_d, let D_t=2^{a_d-i}. The buffer gives
  D_t >= 2^{a_{d-1}+b+1},  a_{-1}=0.
The sum of inherited precision increments is bounded by
  p_d=O(s lambda^2 2^{a_{d-1}}).
This coarse bound follows by summing O(log lambda) previous increments,
using M<=s, u>=1, a=O(lambda), and D<=2^a. Relative to M D_t/u_d it is
  O(lambda^{2+1+3/4} 2^{-b}) = O(lambda^{-9/4}) = o(1).
The O(M a_d) evaluation loss is also lower order, since its relative
size is O(lambda^2 2^{-b}). Rounding the multiplicities costs only a
relative O(u_d/M)=o(1). Therefore the sharp taper estimate applies with
  p_t <= (6+o(1)) M D_t/u_d
uniformly at every active depth.

The mode offset, slope-four weighted partition calculation and exact
pair-index construction can therefore be imported with their uniform
error terms. The increasing u_d and the additive buffer prevent a
nonuniform error near the last active level. All precision bit lengths
are polynomial in s: p_d<=s^{1+8/225+o(1)}. The extension/truncation ring
has polynomial rank and polynomial-size structure constants.

5. Enumeration and implementation costs

For fixed r<=13, arrays of exact pair weights number at most
(p_t+1)^{r(r-1)}, polynomial in s. Their exact-weight index lists are
counted/ranked/unranked with the integer dynamic program already given
in the supplied cached-tree packet. List cardinalities have polynomial
bit length. Empty lists contribute zero. Each of the finitely many tree
candidates has finitely many linear logarithmic cost expressions with
weights 0,2/5,1. Candidate selection is exact: multiply the list lengths
raised to the integer weights 0,2,5 and compare the resulting integers.
No floating-point logarithm is needed to select a tree.

The number of interpolation nodes, values, pair-budget arrays and groups
per call is poly(s), independent of the input-description length L.
Products of such factors over O(log lambda) levels contribute only
2^{O(lambda log lambda)}. Work for each generated entry or arithmetic
operation has one fixed poly(s+L) factor; it does not multiply by a new
power of L at every recursive level.

Original entry routines take poly(s+L)2^{O(s^{9/10})} time. New packet
routines take exp(O(2^{a_d}log s))=exp(s^{8/225+o(1)}) time. Both are
lower order on the scale s/sqrt(lambda). A leaf enumerates at most
2^H assignments, so its total work is also 2^{o(s/sqrt(lambda))} apart
from a fixed polynomial factor. Original and packet tensor descriptions
are immutable and tagged with creation precision, exactly as in the
source algorithm. Retained descriptions are shared by reference, so
entry routines are not recursively composed or silently re-lifted.

6. Full paid time recurrence

Define
  K0 = kappa /sqrt(63/4000),
  K1 = kappa (2/9)/sqrt(9/800),
  K2 = kappa (4/117)/sqrt(77/16200),
  gamma = (2/7)/sqrt(4/9)=3/7.
On the common scale s/sqrt(lambda), the K coefficients are at most
K0,K1,K2 gamma^{d-2} (up to uniform 1+o(1) losses).

Unrolling the recurrence, including all paid child tables and the final
leaf work, gives
 C_T=max{b9 K0,
         a9 K0+b13 K1,
         a9 K0+a13 K1+K2 max(b7,a7/(1-gamma))}.
For the tail, the expression after j stages is
 a7(1-gamma^j)/(1-gamma)+b7 gamma^j;
its maximum is max(b7,a7/(1-gamma)). The infinite sum also covers leaves.
Here
  a7/(1-gamma)=sqrt(3)/2 > b7,
so the three numerical candidates are
  30.753912832054975354229948988135...,
  30.891018920366092231453928171727...,
  30.512927432780512909205333163937....
The middle one is strictly largest. Equivalently,
 C_T = (pi/ln 2)(sqrt(128000/5103)+sqrt(134144/41067))
     = (pi/ln 2)(160 sqrt(35)/189+32 sqrt(393)/351)
     <30.892.

7. Space

At a given call, the cached singleton entries, selected-tree tables and
matrix-multiplication working storage fit within
poly(s+L)2^{(b_r+o(1))K}. Child cache entries are constructed sequentially.
Tables retained on ancestor stack frames occupy a sum of memory sizes,
not a product. The polynomial number of stack frames changes the
exponent only by a lower-order term. The maximum local coefficient is
at the root:
 C_S=b9 K0=(pi/ln 2)sqrt(302080/6561)
             =32 pi sqrt(295)/(81 ln 2)
             =30.753912832054975354229948988135...<30.754.
The depth-1 coefficient is 8.19150213324197..., and the depth>=2 maximum
is less than 2.3 even using the safe fixed r=7 tree. CRT records, sorting
and coefficient coordinates add polynomial factors per table cell, as
in the source matrix-multiplication bridge.

Thus the relative algorithmic theorem is
 time  (s+L)^{O(1)} 2^{(C_T+o(1))s/sqrt(log_2 s)},
 space (s+L)^{O(1)} 2^{(C_S+o(1))s/sqrt(log_2 s)}.
It improves the certified time coefficient in the supplied normalized
interface. Its transfer through the existing SAT reductions retains
n log log n/sqrt(log n) in the general case and n/sqrt(log n) at fixed
density. Constants per normalized wire must not be mislabeled constants
per original SAT variable. Conditional n/log n targets remain open.

8. Exact numerical certificates

schedule_checks.py uses only Python's standard library. Every assertion
uses integer or Fraction arithmetic. Logarithm capacity inequalities are
checked by integer powers. Square roots are enclosed using integer square
roots on a 10^50 grid. Pi is enclosed through Machin's identity and the
alternating arctangent series; ln 2 is enclosed by a positive atanh series
and an explicit geometric tail bound. Decimal formatting happens only
after all assertions have succeeded. This certificate assumes the two
local adaptive b^2 inputs and does not replace the separate finite
portfolio audit.
