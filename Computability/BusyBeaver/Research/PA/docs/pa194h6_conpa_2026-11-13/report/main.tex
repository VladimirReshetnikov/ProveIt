\begin{abstract}
No machine with fewer than 194 working states is established in this report.
The delivered fallback is PA194H6: a fully specified deterministic binary,
single-tape machine, starting on a blank two-way infinite tape, with 194
working states and a separate halting label. It combines coefficient-six
formula construction, no-push modus ponens, and a cache-only Shift test.
The individual ideas occur in earlier packets, but this composition has a
new literal machine graph and a different command behavior from its
coefficient-six predecessor. The ordinary mathematical argument, reusing
the explicitly identified numerical-calculus calibration, gives
\(\PA\vdash\neg\Halt(\mathrm{PA194H6},\mathrm{blank})\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\).
Two symbolic physical checks cover all 4,876 source outcomes, and the
complete blank initialization is checked for 58,088 transitions. No new
end-to-end Lean, Rocq, or iPRA theorem is claimed.

The audit pins ProveIt commit \texttt{3cc7fbf75ba1}. It covers the 47 filed
research packets and all four incoming archives. A fresh literal-table
census recognizes 2,049 complete numeric tables, with 683 byte identities;
the 194-state frontier has 34 classes under the stronger comparison
allowing state renaming, spanning roots, tape reflection, and global bit
exchange. Reported constructions, auxiliary search inputs, and rejected
machines are distinguished. PA194H6 matches none of those classes.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, machine convention, and scope}

The requested strict reduction was not obtained. PA194H6 meets the alternative
of a materially different 194-state design. This is a composition result,
not a claim that its three component ideas are individually new and not a
claim that 194 is minimal.

The definitive table is \file{machine/machine.tm}. Its state set is
\(\{0,\ldots,193\}\), its initial state is 0, and its terminal label is 194.
The head initially scans coordinate 0 of an all-zero tape indexed by
\(\mathbb Z\). Each of its 388 transitions writes 0 or 1 and moves one cell
left or right. The terminal label has no outgoing transitions and is excluded
from the state count. Every working state is reachable in the directed
transition graph from the start; graph reachability is not asserted to be

\subsection{Why PA194H6 is materially different}

The changes operate during the repeated proof search. They are not confined
to initialization, unreachable rows, state names, tape reflection, or a
rewriting of the final halting action. Relative to N6, the Shift test changes
from \(t+h=1\) to \(h=1\). Thus N6 halts on Shift from
\((t,w,d,h)=(1,0,0,0)\), whereas H6 clears that store without halting.
A proof of contradiction in the visible top must therefore be exposed by
\(\mathsf{Pop}\), not by a single final Shift.

Relative to the multiplier-four hybrid H, the formula constructor and MP
comparison change. Since \(p(0,2)=5\), H accepts the MP comparison at
\((0,w,0,20)\) with parameter 2, while H6 rejects it; H6 instead accepts
cache 30. The Cantor stack and word scheduler still use unscaled pairing.
Therefore changing formula codes is not a relabeling of complete stores or
an assertion that their arithmetic execution traces are conjugate.

These examples distinguish specified command functions on natural stores;
we do not use them to claim that the same stores occur on the actual blank
run under a consistency assumption. Independent full-graph comparison
establishes the literal novelty against all 34 old frontier classes.

\section{The numerical calculus and its PA calibration}
\label{sec:logic}

This section specifies the logical system searched by H6 and summarizes the
ordinary proof dependency. The complete inherited calibration texts are
preserved, including the diagonal replacement, the separate-head interface,
the radix proof and the original PA195 calibration.

Let \(p(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b\) and define
\[
 I(a,b)=6p(a,b),\quad M(a,b)=6p(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(i,a)=6p(a,i)+2,\quad E(a,b)=6p(a,b)+3.
\]
The numerical formulas are all natural numbers, with code 0 interpreted as
truth and code 1 as the designated contradiction. Write \(A\to B=I(A,B)\),
\(\neg A=I(A,1)\), \(A\wedge B=\neg(A\to\neg B)\), and
\(\exists_i A=\neg U(i,\neg A)\). Codes with residue four or five are allowed;
the soundness interpretation maps them to truth.

The six universal axiom families are
\begin{align*}
 L_1(D,H)&=(\exists_D U(D,H))\to H,\\
 L_2(D,A,H)&=U(D,U(A,H))\to U(A,U(D,H)),\\
 L_3(D,A,H)&=M(H,D)\to(E(H,A)\to M(A,D)),\\
 L_4(D,A,H)&=E(H,D)\to(M(A,H)\to M(A,D)),\\
 L_5(D,H)&=\exists_D E(H,D),\\
 L_6(D,A,H)&=U(D,A)\to(U(D,A\to H)\to U(D,H)).
\end{align*}
Add the dominated implicational family
\[
 \rho_8(D,A,H,u)=((H\to u)\to D)\to((D\to H)\to(A\to H)),
 \qquad u\le D,
\]
and every diagonal-vacuity formula
\(\Gamma(B)=B\to U(B,B)\).
For the final axiom family put
\begin{align*}
 V(a,b,c)&=M(a,b)\to U(c,M(a,b)),&P(a,b,c)&=c\to M(c,b),\\
 S(a,b,c)&=U\bigl(b,(M(a,b)\to P(a,b,c))\wedge(M(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
 Q(a,b,c)&=U(c,S(a,b,c)\to\neg P(a,b,c)),\\
 G(a,b,c)&=\neg(V(a,b,c)\to Q(a,b,c)).
\end{align*}
The gate \(G(a,b,c)\) is an axiom when \(c\ne a\) and \(c\ne b\).
The only inference rules are modus ponens and theorem generalization
\(B\mapsto U(i,B)\). There are no undischarged hypotheses in this numerical
proof system. Code zero has a fixed derivation from
\(\rho_8(0,0,0,0)=0\).

\subsection{Why the calibration survives multiplier six}

The dependency is a syntactic, PA-verifiable pair of refutation translations,
not merely a standard-model equiconsistency assertion. Four points prevent
incorrect shortcuts.

First, formula codes also serve as variable labels in \(\Gamma(B)\).
The hereditary recoding \(F\) from coefficient four to coefficient six is
\[
 F(0)=0,\qquad F(4p(a,b)+r)=6p(F(a),F(b))+r\quad(n>0,\ 0\le r<4).
\]
Every recursive argument is smaller than its input, so course-of-values
primitive recursion defines \(F\). A similarly recursive partial inverse
proves injectivity. It follows that constructors, binding, repeated labels,
guards and diagonal heads are preserved. Keeping labels fixed while
recoding formulas would fail to preserve the diagonal head.

Second, \(F\) need not preserve the side condition \(u\le D\).
For example, \(40<44\), but \(F(40)=126>66=F(44)\).
The retained finite \file{RestrictedImplication.lean} proof template derives
unrestricted \(\rho_8\) from the dominated schema using only reflexivity and
closure of domination under either implication argument. At coefficient six,
these follow from
\(a,b\le p(a,b)\le6p(a,b)\).
Translate each old dominated leaf to that finite unrestricted derivation,
whose new restricted leaves satisfy their own target inequalities. No
monotonicity of \(F\) is assumed.

Third, the translation back into PA interprets membership by the arithmetic
bit predicate, equality by equality, the quantifier by ordinary universal
quantification, and residues four and five by truth. Every label in a decoded
formula of nonzero code \(B\) is less than \(B\), so the diagonal head has a
uniform vacuity proof. The numerical alias \(I(0,0)=0\) is handled by a proof
of \((0=0)\leftrightarrow((0=0)\to(0=0))\), rather than a false assertion of
syntactic identity.

Fourth, the guarded gate has a uniform PA proof supplier using finite bit-set
comprehension. For each arithmetic formula \(\theta\), PA proves
\[
 \forall n\;\exists s<2^n\;\forall z<n\;
       (\operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\leftrightarrow\theta(z)).
\]
A supplier inserts the appropriate induction instance and excluded-middle
case split. It does not decide \(\theta\) and does not use a truth or reflection
principle. The separate case \(a=b\) in the gate uses the irreflexivity
\(\neg\operatorname{Bit}(z,z)\), and the guard makes the fresh label usable.
These cases and the full matrices are written out in the retained radix
calibration.

The coefficient-four completeness construction is reused through its three
required equality interfaces: two-way membership rewriting, neighborhood
equality, and an equality witness. The diagonal replacement recovers those
interfaces with explicit proof templates, using the code of a negated target
formula as its fresh label. The independent head \(\Gamma\) and the gate
are interderivable with the earlier combined gate by classical implication
proofs. This identifies precisely where the earlier proof-producing PA
compiler is used~\cite{upstream,diagonal-packet,radix-packet}.

Thus there are primitive-recursive proof transformations \(\alpha,\beta\)
whose correctness is provable in PA:
\[
 \operatorname{Prf}_{\PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner)
 \Longrightarrow\operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal L_6}(\alpha(p),1),\qquad
 \operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal L_6}(q,1)
 \Longrightarrow\operatorname{Prf}_{\PA}(\beta(q),\ulcorner0=1\urcorner).
\]
These are the logical inputs to the following machine-specific proof.

\section{Reduction attempts and their limits}
\label{sec:research}

The comparison prevents three misleading ways of claiming a new reduction.
One cannot add savings from folds that identify overlapping source roles;
one cannot subtract a control needed by blank initialization just because
it is absent from a later invariant; and one cannot count a shorter source
word as a state saving before constructing and checking its actual decoder.
Several rejected tables in the supplied corpus illustrate these issues.

\subsection{The six-zero scanner obstruction}

The new candidate still uses a scanner recognizing a run of seven zeros.
Deleting native state 34, redirecting its incoming target to 75, would leave
193 working states. This straightforward proposal fails the old scanner
contract even at a context reached by the parent from blank tape.

After the checked 58,088-step startup, the parent is at source site 25,
whose path is \texttt{1000000}; the instruction is decrement of logical
register 8, physically register 1, which is zero in that seed. After another
56 parent transitions, the left scan enters native state 2 at coordinate 44.
Starting from that exact complete tape:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
Scanner & Steps to its exit & Exit state & Exit head\\\midrule
Seven-zero parent & 64 & 84 & $-2$\\
Six-zero deletion & 47 & 84 & $-1$\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The two exit tapes are identical; the head position is wrong by one cell.
The longer source test returns to the intended next canonical frame after
438 parent steps. The local finite witness already suffices to reject the
claim that this deletion preserves the specified scanner operation. It is
not an assertion about the complete altered machine's eventual halting.
The standalone script \file{research/scanner_probe.py} reproduces every cell
and step of the witness.

The path \texttt{1000000} is in the fixed scheduler scaffold outside AX.
Consequently changing only the AX grammar or its head order cannot make the
unchanged six-zero scanner contract valid. A successful scanner reduction
must also change that scaffold, its addressing convention, or the scanner
implementation. This is a limitation of that proposal, not a lower bound
for all 193-state machines.

\subsection{Ideas from the ZFC and ConciseIPRA work}

The useful transferable ideas are semantic interfaces and verified compilation:
remove an unnecessary stack push, use a known-zero register to implement a
jump, separate a short logical basis from its search procedure, and exploit
context when allocating decoder states. The inspected ZFC work already uses
related no-push and context-fold techniques; the present report does not
claim those in isolation as new~\cite{zfc}.

The numerical fresh-label trick and separated diagonal head are inherited
from the PA196/PA194 line. ConciseIPRA supports the broader strategy of
replacing a large presentation by a smaller derivably equivalent interface,
but its target is iPRA. Merely searching for an iPRA refutation would not
establish the requested PA-consistency equivalence. A transfer needs explicit
PA-refutation translations and their compiled cost. Likewise, substituting
a ZFC-consistency search changes the target strength~\cite{concise}.

\section{Reproduction and package structure}

The accepted construction uses Python 3.10 or later and the standard library.
No external checkout, network connection, proof assistant, or heuristic search
is needed for the package's default verification command. Run with assertions
enabled:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -B verify.py
\end{verbatim}
The verifier rebuilds the table in a temporary directory, checks its expected
bytes and source graph, checks local folds, runs both symbolic physical
implementations, checks the complete startup, and verifies the new command
and source-change identities. The separate comparison verifier checks novelty
against the bundled old 194-state tables. The optional discovery searches are
not a premise of the accepted mathematical result.

To rebuild the report from its source directory:
\begin{verbatim}
cd report
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
\end{verbatim}
The package retains licenses and source provenance for the inherited compiler,
symbolic engines and proof fragments. \file{MANIFEST.json} records the SHA-256
of every delivered file other than itself. The report's LaTeX source, compiled
PDF, complete table, verifiers, comparison ledger and relevant research evidence
are all contained in the ZIP. Historical claims about earlier packages are
identified as such; their entire verification suites were not all rerun.
