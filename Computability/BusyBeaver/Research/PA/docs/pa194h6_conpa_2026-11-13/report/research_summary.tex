\subsection{Additional decoder and contraction searches}

Fresh bounded searches combined seven-head permutation and bijective parameter
renaming with continuing shared and occurrence-level binary-tree reassociation.
Earlier packets already considered seven-head permutations in fixed shapes;
the added scope is that combination with reassociation and several controller
parents. For coefficient six, the search also
recognized the existing three-increment reader at native state 330, which
was absent from the earlier grammar-interning map. It allowed eligible
contextual folds involving that fixed reader. The recorded best effective
counts remained compatible with 194 states; no verified 193-state table
resulted. The exact proposal counts, pseudorandom seeds, objective values,
bounds and search inputs are supplied in \file{research/decoder/}.
The five completed runs used 36,567,758 proposal slots. These are not counts
of distinct machines. All 301 saved grammar words were independently checked
to belong to the stated seven-head mutation domain; that check does not
certify their physical execution.

A whole-source-contract contraction screen on H6 considered 62,073 specified
row-choice merges while protecting the arithmetic block. It found no survivor
at its stated symbolic fuel bound. This rules out those tested contractions
under that proof interface, not contractions requiring a changed source
invariant or a different interpreter. Two further finite local screens used
eleven-cell windows and at most 300 local transitions: arbitrary common
replacement actions processed all 194 controls, and independent incoming-edge
bypasses processed 172 eligible controls. Neither returned a contraction. The saved code and receipts define the
search space more precisely than a claim that the machine is irreducible.

There is a further non-additivity obstacle. The supplied symmetric hybrid
uses a new reader at native label 330 and a fold consuming state 41. The
coefficient-six constructor already uses 330 for three increments and its
allocation also consumes state 41. Renaming the conflicting reader to 331
produces a source-correct 195-state symmetric H6 implementation. Blindly
adding the two advertised savings double-counts the same available control.
The accepted H6 uses the ordinary cached-only comparison, with its separately
verified eightfold allocation.
