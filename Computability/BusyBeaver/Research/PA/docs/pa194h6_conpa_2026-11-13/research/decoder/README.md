# Bounded reduction research

These searches did not produce a verified machine with fewer than 194 working states. They are optional research artifacts, not prerequisites for checking PA194H6.

- `seven/README.md`: five bounded decoder-grammar searches, 36,567,758 proposal slots in total; these are not counts of distinct machines. Sources, seeds, saved grammars, logs, summaries and an independent mutation-domain checker are included.
- `h6merge/README.md`: universal source-contract screening of 62,073 row-choice contractions of the delivered H6 table; no survivor within the stated limits.
- `h6local/README.md`: separate bounded common-action and incoming-edge bypass screens of H6; no survivor under the recorded neighborhoods and bounds.

The saved negative results establish only these search outcomes. They do not prove a lower bound or minimality. The sibling `../scanner_probe.py` gives a concrete failure of a proposed six-zero scanner shortcut on a configuration reached by the parent machine from blank.

`MANIFEST.json` in this directory records the original research bundle; the root release manifest additionally covers this index and every delivered file.
