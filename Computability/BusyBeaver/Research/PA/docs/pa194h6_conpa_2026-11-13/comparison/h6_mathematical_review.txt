Conclusion and scope
--------------------
No mathematical defect was found in the new H6 composition reviewed here.
Its new controller identities, proof-tree compiler, arbitrary-carried-store
interface, and coefficient-six source binding fit the inherited ordinary
PA calibration. This supports the proposed 194-state fallback. It does not
establish a machine below 194 states, a state lower bound, a new kernel-checked
PA theorem, or an iPRA consistency equivalence.

This review checked the ordinary argument in proof/pa194h6_preservation.tex,
the actual source/data and command checker, and the retained radix, constructor,
and hybrid-controller proofs. It did not rerun the long universal physical
checks, which have separate receipts, and did not re-elaborate the upstream
Lean library or every historical logical lemma.

1. Independent source and literal table check
--------------------------------------------
review_h6_structure.py imports no builder, compiler, allocator, or tape-runtime
module. It expands the named source trees itself, constructs a binary trie,
and independently resolves successor, decrement, break, and continue targets.
The result agrees with all 3,775 delivered H6 primitive instructions and every
source address. Restoring the four-increment multiplier yields exactly the
supplied 3,653-instruction H4 graph.

All 61 node61 occurrences are the relative 1110 bodies of distinct node65 Cons
occurrences. The AX grammar does not directly use node61 as a literal tag.
Every Cons occurrence has the full 29-instruction coefficient-six pattern in
the generic constructor proof. All 3,409 instructions outside multiplier bodies
and all their successor edges agree under the recorded relocation. No outside
edge enters a replacement-body interior. This closes a possible gap between
changing61 multiplication loops and merely asserting a changed formula radix.

The source changes therefore alter formula multiplication 4 -> 6 everywhere it
is required, including MP's implication comparison, while leaving Cantor stack
pairing and the candidate scheduler's pairing conventions unchanged.

2. Controller identities, including degenerate codes
---------------------------------------------------
Write [a;w]=p(a,w), I(a,b)=6p(a,b), U(i,a)=6p(a,i)+2. The numerical stack is
not a free list: [0;0]=0, and I(0,0)=0. Every identity below uses total Cantor
projections, so these aliases cause no exception.

The hybrid Shift halts exactly if the old cache h=1; otherwise it returns
(h,tl(w),d,hd(w)). Successful MP_x with b=d+x returns(b,w,b,0), and failed MP
returns(0,w,b,0). Consequently MP_0 on d=0 returns(0,w,0,0) for every old top
and cache, regardless of whether its comparison succeeds. This Discard rule
is valid without assuming that the old cache is derivable or nonzero.

AX pushes the nine generated heads and saved old top and clears d,h. The first
eight generated heads and gate are 0 modulo 6; the final universal head is 2
modulo 6. None is 1. Thus AX;S^9 and AX;S^10 never test the arbitrary saved old
top. They produce the exact interfaces displayed in the report.

Push=AX(0,0,0);S^9;MP_0 works for every old(t,w,d,h), giving(0,[t;w],0,0).
Pop=AX(0,0,0);S^11 preserves the old top, clears d, and pops the old stack if
the top is not1; its last Shift halts if that top is 1. A plain final Shift
would fail to detect top 1 with cache 0. The report correctly uses Pop.

The axiom/gate selectors consume exactly the generated heads and saved zero.
Their fixed visible output is never1, so the intervening Pops are safe. The
dominated-rho selector uses an offset u and parameter a-u under u<=a, which
correctly realizes D=a. Generalization works even with visible premise1:
its initial Shift tests cleared cache 0, and AX consumes the newly loaded
premise before any further test could see it.

For MP, reserving zero BEFORE the two compiled premises is essential. After
major proof, Push, minor proof and Push, the suffix sees
(0,[a,I(a,b),0;w],0,0). Two Shifts expose the premises; MP gives
(b,[0;w],b,0); final Pop consumes the reserved zero and clears the offset.
It halts if a=1 or b=1, otherwise returns(b,w,0,0). The all-zero alias case
still gives precisely the required numerical equality. Without the reserved
zero the suffix could consume an arbitrary old stack entry; the submitted
compiler includes it.

3. Dirty-store compilation and scheduler completeness
-----------------------------------------------------
The compiler invariant quantifies over every t,w with d=h=0, rather than only
an empty stack. At every proof node it either halts early or returns its
conclusion while preserving w and clearing d,h. A preliminary AX clears any
arbitrary carried d,h without needing the prior store to be logically sound.
Thus AX;W_p;Pop halts from EVERY incoming numerical store when p proves 1.
This is the correct interface for a scheduler that carries its store between
candidate words.

The continuation encoding E(c::w)=1+B_c(E(w)) must be distinguished from a
fresh candidate's outer command code. The report does this. Prefixing the
universal halting word by AX(k,0,0) gives cofinally many candidate codes
p(k+2,p(0,p(0,E(word)))); they are increasing and unbounded. Starting the fresh
counter at 2 therefore loses none of the necessary coverage. Positive residual
continuations decrease, and each fixed arithmetic macro has a PA-provably
terminating primitive-recursive bound. The finite induction through earlier
candidate numbers reaches a chosen halting word unless an earlier halt occurs.
No reset-to-empty hypothesis or standard-model-only enumeration argument is
being substituted for this step.

4. Radix calibration and the numerical-order obstruction
--------------------------------------------------------
Recoding ONLY formula syntax while keeping numerical variable labels fixed
would break diagonal freshness. The retained radix_calibration.tex instead
uses hereditary recoding F of both formula codes and labels, with a primitive-
recursive left inverse proving injectivity. It fixes 0 and 1, preserves all
constructor identities, and hence carries Gamma4(B) to Gamma6(F(B)).

F is not order preserving. In the coefficient-six instance,
40<44 but F(40)=126>66=F(44). A restricted propositional axiom cannot simply
be recoded to another restricted axiom leaf. The proof correctly replaces
that leaf with the unrestricted rho8All template. The actual retained
RestrictedImplication.lean defines this template for implication and a
relation satisfying reflexivity and the two argument bounds. Natural <=
with I6 satisfies those premises because a,b<=p(a,b)<=6p(a,b).

For the reverse translation, unused residues 4 and 5 are interpreted as closed
truth; they are not silently excluded from arbitrary numerical command
parameters. The source label B is absent from the interpretation of code B,
because recursive components and occurring labels are strictly smaller.
Truth code 0 and the I(0,0) alias receive explicit fixed proof translations.
Code 1 translates to Bit(x0,x0), whose negation PA proves.

The gate supplier uses finite bit comprehension for an arbitrary formula,
with a syntactic proof supplier using one corresponding PA induction instance.
It does not claim a uniform truth predicate or reflection theorem. The
coincident-variable-label case is handled separately. The fixed logical
translation therefore supports all source naturals, including the two unused
residue classes, and supplies the intended PA refutation equivalence.

The new H6 report relies on these existing ordinary proof-producing
calibrations. They have not all been converted here to a new end-to-end Lean
or Rocq declaration. The imported PA196 declaration proves one internal PA
direction; its external standard-model theorem alone must not be described
as supplying the converse. The retained ordinary syntactic translation is
what supplies that converse in this argument.

5. Halting reflection and proof status
--------------------------------------
The logical soundness invariant annotates top, cache and every canonical live
stack entry with derivations; the numerical offset has no such annotation.
Zero supplies the virtual tail. AX, MP and Shift preserve these annotations,
and a Shift halt exposes a derivation of 1. Canonical Cantor tails decrease
at every positive stack value, giving bounded finite annotations.

Exact positive source-to-tape contracts are needed in both directions. An
arbitrary literal tape halt cannot be reflected just by saying that source
halts are simulated. The retained argument uses positivity and determinism:
if a literal halt occurred at time T while source execution remained nonhalting,
T+1 completed positive source segments would provide a longer run from the
same frame, a contradiction. Macro termination and the whole blank startup
close the gaps before the first frame and inside command execution. These
are PA-formalizable finite inductions with the supplied uniform witnesses.

The finite table/CFG/startup and universal symbolic runtime certificates are
support for this ordinary mathematical composition. They do not themselves
encode the complete proof of the PA biconditional. Actual nonhalting is the
further conclusion under Con(PA); the biconditional itself does not assume
PA consistency.
