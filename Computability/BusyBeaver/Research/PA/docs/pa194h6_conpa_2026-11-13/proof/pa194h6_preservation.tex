\section{PA194H6: the coefficient-six cached-test controller}
\label{sec:h6}

PA194H6 combines coefficient-six formula constructors with the no-push
modus-ponens rule and a Shift command that tests only the cached formula.
Coefficient six and no-push modus ponens occur together in the supplied
PA194N6.  The cached-test/no-push interface occurs at coefficient four in
PA194H and PA194HS/NS.  The contribution here is their verified composition,
with a new literal graph at 194 states.  The component ideas are inherited.
The composition does not lower the state count.

\subsection{Numerical semantics and inherited logical calibration}

Put
\[
 p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b,\qquad [a;w]=p(a,w),\qquad
 I(a,b)=6p(a,b),\qquad U(i,a)=6p(a,i)+2.
\]
Membership and equality codes are $M(a,b)=6p(a,b)+1$ and
$E(a,b)=6p(a,b)+3$.  Negation is $N(a)=I(a,1)$; conjunction and existential
quantification have the inherited definitions
$C(a,b)=N(I(a,N(b)))$ and $\exists_i a=N(U(i,N(a)))$.
The codes zero and one are truth and the target contradiction.
The unused residue classes four and five are interpreted by the inherited
radix calibration; they are not silently assumed to be ordinary formula
constructors.  In particular $I(0,0)=0$ remains a numerical alias.

Let $\mathcal L_6$ denote the supplied separated-head numerical calculus
with these coefficient-six constructors.  The logical dependency used here
is the pair of primitive-recursive proof translations $\alpha,\beta$ whose
correctness is provable in PA:
\begin{align*}
 \operatorname{Prf}_{\rm PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner)
     &\longrightarrow \operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal L_6}(\alpha(p),1),\\
 \operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal L_6}(q,1)
     &\longrightarrow \operatorname{Prf}_{\rm PA}(\beta(q),\ulcorner0=1\urcorner).
\end{align*}
The retained \texttt{radix\_calibration.tex},
\texttt{separate\_head\_calculus.tex}, and original calibration give this
ordinary mathematical interface.  The retained
\texttt{RestrictedImplication.lean} supplies the explicit unrestricted
$\rho_8$ proof template (\texttt{rho8All}) needed by the recoding argument;
this avoids assuming that recoding preserves the numerical dominance order.
This release does not supply a newly
compiled end-to-end Lean or Rocq proof of it, nor an iPRA endpoint.
Nothing in the new argument assumes PA's consistency or its own soundness.

A command store is $(t,w,d,h)$, with current top, stack, numerical offset,
and cached formula.  For $b=d+x$ the two controller operations are
\[
 S(t,w,d,h)=\begin{cases}
 \mathsf{halt},&h=1,\\
 (h,\operatorname{tl}w,d,\operatorname{hd}w),&h\ne1,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 \mathsf{MP}_{x}(t,w,d,h)=\begin{cases}
 (b,w,b,0),&I(t,b)=h,\\
 (0,w,b,0),&I(t,b)\ne h.
 \end{cases}
\]
The total Cantor projections obey
$\operatorname{hd}[a;w]=a$, $\operatorname{tl}[a;w]=w$, and
$\operatorname{hd}0=\operatorname{tl}0=0$.  Thus $[0;0]=0$; no proof below
treats the numerical stack as a free list.

For $D=d+x$, $H=h+y$, and $A=c$, the unchanged AX operation returns
\[
 \mathsf{AX}_{c,x,y}(t,w,d,h)=
 (G(H,D,A),[q_1,\ldots,q_9,t;w],0,0).
\]
Its first head is $q_1=I(A,U(A,A))$, its next six heads are the inherited
universal heads in reverse emission order, and
\[
 q_8=I(I(I(H,d),D),I(I(D,H),I(A,H))),\qquad q_9=U(d,h).
\]
The guarded gate $G$ is exactly the coefficient-six parent's gate.
The complete free-constructor emitter is checked in
\texttt{proof/check\_commands.py}.  All $q_1,\ldots,q_8,G$ are zero modulo
six, whereas $q_9$ is two modulo six and at least two.  None is one.
This constructor observation is valid for arbitrary input naturals and
requires no semantic consistency assumption.

\subsection{A complete command compiler}

Write $A_0=\mathsf{AX}_{0,0,0}$ and execute words left to right.  The exact
identities
\[
 \mathsf{AX}_{c,x,y};S^9(t,w,d,h)=(q_8,[t;w],0,U(d,h)),\qquad
 \mathsf{AX}_{c,x,y};S^{10}(t,w,d,h)=(U(d,h),w,0,t)
\]
never halt.  Their tests encounter generated non-one heads and the cleared
cache, not the arbitrary saved top.  Define
\[
 \mathsf{Push}=A_0;S^9;\mathsf{MP}_0,\quad
 \mathsf{Pop}=A_0;S^{11},\quad
 \mathsf{Discard}=\mathsf{MP}_0,\quad
 \mathsf{Offset}_i=\mathsf{MP}_i.
\]
They satisfy, for every displayed natural store,
\begin{align*}
 \mathsf{Push}(t,w,d,h)&=(0,[t;w],0,0),\\
 \mathsf{Pop}(t,[a;w],d,h)&=
  \begin{cases}\mathsf{halt},&t=1,\\(t,w,0,a),&t\ne1,\end{cases}\\
 \mathsf{Discard}(t,w,0,h)&=(0,w,0,0),\\
 \mathsf{Offset}_i(0,w,0,0)&=(0,w,i,0).
\end{align*}
Push's final comparison fails by the residues zero and two.  Pop's eleventh
Shift tests precisely the saved top.  The two MP branches coincide when
the candidate conclusion is zero, proving Discard even with arbitrary old
cache.  Offset includes the zero case.  All identities continue to hold
when a zero push collapses into the empty stack.

From $(t,w,0,0)$, the word
$\mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{AX}_{A,D,H};S^{j+1};\mathsf{Pop}^{9-j}$ selects
$q_j$ for $1\le j\le7$, preserving $w$ and clearing both caches.  The gate
uses $\mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{AX}_{c,b,a};\mathsf{Pop}^{10}$.
For $u\le a$, the dominated implicational head is selected by
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Offset}_u;
 \mathsf{AX}_{b,a-u,c};S^9;\mathsf{Pop}.
\]
Generalization is
\[
 \mathsf{Gen}_i=\mathsf{Push};\mathsf{Offset}_i;S;
                  \mathsf{AX}_{i,0,0};S^{10},
\]
which sends $(a,w,0,0)$ to $(U(i,a),w,0,0)$, including $a=1$.
These are the inherited hybrid words; their proof uses only the constructor
properties just verified for coefficient six.

The MP suffix $S;S;\mathsf{MP}_b;\mathsf{Pop}$ sends
$(0,[a,I(a,b),0;w],0,0)$ to $(b,w,0,0)$ when $a,b\ne1$, and halts when
$a=1$ or $b=1$.  Indeed the two Shifts expose the premises; successful MP
leaves $(b,[0;w],b,0)$; Pop consumes the reserved zero and clears the offset,
or detects the false conclusion.  The reserved zero must be supplied by
the proof compiler, since MP no longer pushes it.

For a finite proof tree $p$, compile axioms by these selectors and a
universal-introduction node by appending $\mathsf{Gen}_i$.  At an MP node
with subproofs $p_0$ of $a$ and $p_1$ of $I(a,b)$, use
\[
 W_p=\mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Push};W_{p_1};\mathsf{Push};
 W_{p_0};\mathsf{Push};S;S;\mathsf{MP}_b;\mathsf{Pop}.
\]
Induction on $p$ proves that execution from every $(t,w,0,0)$ either halts
or returns $(\operatorname{concl}p,w,0,0)$.  The first two commands reserve
the zero needed by the suffix.  If $p$ proves one, the word
$A_0;W_p;\mathsf{Pop}$ therefore halts from every incoming store.
The initial AX clears arbitrary carried offsets and cache; the final Pop
tests one before using the old underlying stack.  A plain final Shift
would be incorrect: here $S(1,0,0,0)=(0,0,0,0)$.

\subsection{Exhaustiveness, soundness, and the PA theorem}

The unchanged scheduler tests finite words while carrying the store between
trials.  The preceding all-store compiler is consequently essential.
Its continuation convention is $E(\epsilon)=0$ and
$E(c::w)=1+B_c(E(w))$, where
\[
 B_S(z)=p(0,z),\quad B_{\mathsf{MP}_x}(z)=p(1,p(x,z)),\quad
 B_{\mathsf{AX}_{c,x,y}}(z)=p(c+2,p(y,p(x,z))).
\]
Let $z=E(A_0;W_p;\mathsf{Pop})$.  Prefixing the halting word by
$\mathsf{AX}_{k,0,0}$ gives the fresh-candidate codes
$n_k=p(k+2,p(0,p(0,z)))$.  They are increasing and unbounded.  Each prefix
may alter the store arbitrarily because the following word halts from
every store.  The fresh counter begins at two; a member of this cofinal
family will be reached unless an earlier halt occurs.  Positive
continuations strictly decrease during decoding.  The inherited source
arithmetic loops terminate with primitive-recursive bounds, so each earlier
finite trial also terminates or halts.  These are finite coding inductions
formalizable in PA, not an appeal to a consistent standard model.

For soundness, annotate the current top, cache and each canonical Cantor
stack entry by a derivation in $\mathcal L_6$.  Zero has a fixed derivation
and supplies the virtual zero tail.  AX preserves the annotations by its
axioms and generalization; successful MP uses its two annotated premises;
failed MP returns zero; Shift moves annotations and halts only when an
annotated cache equals one.  The canonical stack has a finite bounded
decoding because its tail decreases at each positive value.
Proof annotations can therefore be updated by primitive recursion on a
finite execution.  PA proves the soundness invariant and the proof-tree
compiler by ordinary induction.

Composing these two syntactic constructions with $\alpha,\beta$, the exact
source binding, the positive universal physical simulations, and the
complete blank initialization gives the ordinary mathematical theorem
\[
 \mathrm{PA}\ \vdash\
 \operatorname{Halt}(\mathrm{PA194H6},\mathrm{blank})
 \ \longleftrightarrow\
 \exists p\,\operatorname{Prf}_{\rm PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner),
\]
and hence
\[
 \mathrm{PA}\ \vdash\
 \neg\operatorname{Halt}(\mathrm{PA194H6},\mathrm{blank})
 \ \longleftrightarrow\ \operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA}).
\]
The logical calibration is an explicitly inherited ordinary-proof dependency.
The new physical and symbolic checks support the composition and do not
constitute an end-to-end proof-assistant theorem.  Consistency is needed only
for the further external conclusion that the machine actually never halts.
