This is a useful positive control for the duplicate detector, and confirms

QH is the combination that is absent from the pinned literal corpus:
- Q's exchanged-curried-premise quantifier-distribution emitter;
- H's no-push MP result in the visible top and retained offset;
- H's ordinary, unsymmetric cached-only Shift guard h=1.

Relative to Q, recurring successful MP changes from (0,w,0,b) to (b,w,b,0),
where b=d+x. This changes which command words compile a proof and how a
contradiction in the top is detected. Relative to QN, Shift tests h=1 instead
of t+h=1. For example, from (t,w,d,h)=(1,0,0,0), QN's Shift halts while QH's
does not. Relative to H, the emitted quantifier-distribution head exchanges
its curried premises; the explicit K/S/MP exchange certificate supplies the
finite proof translation. Relative to H6, QH also retains constructor
coefficient four rather than six. Relative to DHS, QH retains Gamma(A), uses
ordinary rather than symmetric Shift comparison, and exchanges the designated
quantifier head. These are changes of recurring source/command behavior.

Thus the fallback is a new concrete composition of established mechanisms.
It is not a new logical basis, a new arithmetic engine, a state reduction,
or a mathematical breakthrough beyond the cited preservation proofs. Its
materiality comes from the changed proof-search interface and exact compiler,
plus a control graph absent from the complete pinned 42-class frontier.
Structural difference alone would not suffice, so both comparisons matter.
