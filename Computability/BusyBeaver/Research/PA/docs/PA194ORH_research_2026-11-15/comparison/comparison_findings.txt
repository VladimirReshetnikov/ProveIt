Main mathematical families
-------------------------
All principal current constructions are descendants of the same numerical
PA-refutation search architecture. Their substantive changes fit these axes:

1. Logical coding: constructor coefficients four, five, six; exchanged
   curried premises in the universal-distribution head (Q); diagonal head
   Gamma(A) versus Gamma(D); explicit AX operand c versus inherited offset
   d as the generalization binder.
2. Command protocol: successful MP may push a dummy zero, retain the
   consequent visibly without a push, or place it in the cache. AX emits
   ten stored entries in the old interface, or eleven and consumes a fourth
   operand by falling through the full MP reader in AX11.
3. Contradiction detector: Shift tests t+h=1 or only h=1. These differ on
   arbitrary carried stores; a final visible contradiction with h=0 does
   not halt under one cached-only Shift. A corrected compiler uses Pop or
   an equivalent explicitly proved exposing word.
4. Source algorithms: symmetric destructive comparisons remove restoration
   work; known-zero decrements replace particular source jumps. These
   require all-store or reachability invariants rather than a sampled run.
5. Physical layout: source grammar reassociation, register block
   permutations, history selectors, and loop-continuation reassociation.
   They can preserve every resolved source instruction while changing
   recurring physical traces. They are stronger changes than state renaming.
6. Physical timing: incoming-edge invariants permit three-step paths to
   be replaced by one transition. These give exact relative time maps but
   retain the number of working controls.

Critical duplication and naming observations
-------------------------------------------
PA194AX11H from the revised second AX11H packet has exactly the same dense
bytes as the earlier AX11H packet (c5dfe1026d077d3e...); its contribution is
expanded written arithmetic calibration. PA194QAX11H is the earlier
PA194AX11QH table (19e215df20429fa6...). PA194QN and PA194NQ are aliases;
several PA194C submissions are duplicates; QN6 and QH5 recur in multiple
packets. The string PA194NS is ambiguous across historical submissions:
one denotes the symmetric sum guard, while a filed packet with that name
duplicates PA194HS's cached-only guard. Names alone do not identify a machine.

Proof status must not be flattened
---------------------------------
The central PA README says the end-to-end literal machine-specific iPRA
formalization remains open. That does not imply that no packet claims a
written mathematical iPRA theorem. In particular, the second filed packet
pa194_ax11h_research_2026-11-14_2 contains a substantial claimed completion:
physical_ipra_audit.tex, source_command_completion.tex,
foundation_paeq_completion.tex, term_leaf_completion.tex,
forward_calibration_completion.tex, and consistency_equivalence.tex.
It claims the full written iPRA equivalence for AX11H and QAX11H, with
explicit weak-arithmetic clocks, finite request trees replacing full PA
induction, current Foundation proof coding, and the supplied I-Sigma-1
transfer. It explicitly does not claim a new complete Lean/Rocq build.

Several later packets, including all four current incoming deliveries,
import that exact revised AX11H written calibration (archive SHA-256
93ee082ef035b2d32e71d6af5239b25b4bea1773f03bea4d2c39810a7fe15075).
Their new statements are generally relative physical/source equivalences;
absolute Con(PA) claims depend on that mathematical calibration. An audit
must distinguish checking a finite simulation certificate, reviewing a
paper-level uniform arithmetic compiler, and completing a kernel proof.
The table census itself establishes none of those consistency equivalences.

Rejected smaller machines
-------------------------
The presence of 193-state files is not a successful reduction. The most
studied 193-control runtime splice 301->88 preserves a runtime fragment but
breaks blank initialization. Newer packets supply exact finite traces into
outward rays, including all 9! physical register permutations in the specified
selector-only family. A trivially proved infinite ray cannot serve as a
Con(PA) search machine under the intended weak-arithmetic equivalence.
These exclusions apply only to the specified transformations. None of the
negative searches, local context envelopes, or reader scores proves a
general 194-state lower bound.

Recognized-format boundary
--------------------------
The census recognizes complete numeric five-column binary L/R tables and
the explicitly documented JSON schemas. It does not execute arbitrary
parameterized generators or parse TeX-only transition descriptions, JSONL,
or opaque binary encodings. The packet ledger and mechanism review cover
the named designs beyond this automatic recognition boundary. Complete
spanning canonical forms compare all possible spanning roots; disconnected
diagnostics are kept separate. Broad comparison also admits reflection,
global bit exchange, and ignored write/move effects on halt-entry edges.
Those are structural novelty checks, not blank-zero behavioral equivalences.
