Independent review of the revised AX11H written calibration
=========================================================

Review date: 15 November 2026.
Repository snapshot: 43d9041f5571936c45c583f7f9d7f6eed9e0fd84.
Reviewed packet P:
  Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/docs/pa194_ax11h_research_2026-11-14_2/
All paths beginning P/ below are relative to that exact directory. All ten
P/proof/*.tex files were read, not only the packet's claims or summaries.

Verdict
-------

I found no concrete fatal flaw in the finite-demand repair, the explicit
Foundation adapter, or the physical/source clock argument. The central repairs
are substantive: they replace impermissible all-environment inductions and
unspecified witness suppliers by actual finite algorithms and appropriate
bounded invariants. The manuscript supplies an ordinary written argument for
the two literal machines' iPRA consistency equivalence. It is inaccurate to
describe this revised packet as supplying only a conditional simulation or no
written PA calibration.

This is not a certification of a new end-to-end Lean theorem. The review did
not rebuild Lean and did not independently rederive every one of the hundreds
of cited source recipes. The largest remaining verification boundary is the
interpretation and composition of those finite recipes as direct C4 proof
lists, especially the enormous fixed relational seeds. The manuscript states
this boundary explicitly. Failure to evaluate their huge numeral codes is not
by itself a mathematical gap: a supplied finite derivation object can be used
as fixed data. It is a reason to distinguish a written proof from a completed
machine-checked implementation.

1. Exact proof strengths and the final transfer
---------------------------------------------

The proof does not obtain an iPRA theorem simply by relabelling a PA theorem.
Its layers have different stated strengths:

* P/proof/physical_ipra_audit.tex and source_command_completion.tex prove
  arithmetic simulation identities by quantifier-free induction, in usual
  iPRA with symbols for primitive recursive functions.
* P/proof/ax11h_preservation.tex proves command-word compilation, exhaustive
  scheduling, and finite proof-annotation soundness in iPRA.
* P/proof/forward_calibration_completion.tex, statement (F1), is explicitly
  an I-Sigma-1 proof of OrdinaryPAeqProof(d,false) -> C4Proof(F(d),1), with F
  primitive recursive. The Foundation adapter is likewise stated in the
  current I-Sigma-1 theory, not asserted without qualification in iPRA.
* P/proof/consistency_implies_nonhalting.tex uses a Sigma-1 invariant of
  internal PA proof witnesses in arbitrary candidate-PRA models whose
  arithmetic reduct satisfies I-Sigma-1. It correctly does not call the
  predicate Good(a) = Prov_PA(Tr(a)) quantifier free.
* P/proof/consistency_equivalence.tex applies the existing one-way transfer
  theorems to the atomic matrix chi_M(t)=1. Its internal halting/refutation
  implications are model-uniform, not merely statements about standard N.

I inspected the retained references/reverse_soundness/isigma1-machine/
ConPAISigma1Directions.lean and ConPAUnconditional.lean. The former really has
the two advertised endpoint theorems. The nonhalt-to-consistency direction
requires both openness and boundedness of the halt matrix; the chosen atomic
matrix satisfies both. The latter discharges the fixed PA witness-bound
certificate through PA196PACertificateComplete.certificate. The negative
translation proof yields the negative endpoint and does not use Markov's
principle to remove a double negation from an existential halting claim.

Consequently, a new literal table still needs its own uniform execution
binding. The existing endpoint cannot certify a table merely because its
ordinary finite tests resemble those of AX11H.

2. Why the finite-demand construction is an allowed repair
--------------------------------------------------------

The precise argument is P/proof/forward_calibration_completion.tex,
"Finite demand instead of induction over all environments", with its actual
child maps in (F4), (F9)-(F16), and P/proof/term_leaf_completion.tex (T1)-(T16).

A fixed request has a rank r and a finite parameter packet. Generate the full
binary tree of depth r+1, including inactive slots, by a bounded forward loop.
There are at most 2^(r+2)-1 slots. For each active slot, child queries are
computed from syntax and input parameters before child proof values exist.
Same-stratum ranks strictly decrease. A reverse bounded loop constructs proof
lists from previously evaluated children and already completed lower strata.

This is primitive recursive even when label reserves, shifted syntax, or proof
codes grow rapidly: both loop lengths are primitive recursive in the original
input, and the fixed local operations are primitive recursive. No uniform
small bound on proof size, no unrestricted minimization, and no inference from
"for all i<k there exists e_i" to a chosen PR function are necessary. One may
use the constructed output table itself in the checking invariant. Bounded
conjunctions of the concrete validity/correctness tests have PR characteristic
functions, so forward and reverse correctness use quantifier-free induction.

I checked these potentially problematic cases in detail:

* Allocator relocation has two recursive calls on the quantified body, at
  different allocator parameters. These are exactly the calls in the pinned
  PA196/Arithmetic/Transport/FoundationRelationalSequentAllocator.lean,
  allocator_source_step. Its original formula_allocator_certificates theorem
  explicitly uses full PA induction; the manuscript uses the local step
  recipes instead. The common allocator max(lo,other)+1 preserves coverage
  because each body code is strictly smaller than its quantified parent.
* Term vector realization (T14) invokes uniqueness on a replacement term
  w_i which can be larger than the variable being realized. The manuscript
  does not assert the false inequality w_i < variableCode. Uniqueness is
  completed before vector realization, so this is a lower-stratum call.
* Formula realization (F11)-(F12) recurses only on original formula children.
  Large substituted terms are handled by the completed term algorithms.
  The quantified child carries the explicitly shifted and extended vectors.
* Universal induction-matrix closure (F15) retains the whole canonical
  witness packet (m,p,K), uses the invariant s+k=m, and decreases k. Its
  zero case therefore supplies only the recorded induction matrix, not an
  arbitrary matrix of the same arity. This prevents a hidden general-purpose
  matrix oracle at the base case.
* The outer PAeq compiler's universal map (F16) is fixed by formula syntax
  and labels, not by a certificate that a recursive call later returns.
* Guard collection and canonical input in the term manuscript explicitly
  supply an array of entry proofs and an iterated maximum of required code
  bounds. The repeated-environment supplier uses identical explicit identity
  proofs. Neither uses unrestricted choice to collect arbitrary existentials.

These checks justify the key induction mechanism. A future implementation
should preserve this request/output separation: if a new child query were
chosen from the size or contents of a returned same-stratum proof, the
present bounded-tree proof would no longer apply automatically.

3. The diagonal/equality interface is a genuine issue, and is addressed
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The delivered calculus uses Gamma(B)=I(B,U(B,B)) and the old guarded gate,
whereas the cited PA196 library uses a bundled primitive equality-vacuity
axiom. The manuscript does not claim a translation of arbitrary proofs using
that obsolete axiom. Its (F2)-(F3) replaces the exact interfaces required by
the relational construction.

For reverse membership, c is the code of the complete negated conclusion
phi, not an arbitrary fresh name. Constructor inequalities imply c>a,b and
the required fixed membership label. Under phi, classical propositional
constructors expose the equality, the positive membership premise, and the
negative target. Pointed separation and direct membership rewriting give a
contradiction; Gamma(phi) provides precisely the vacuity needed to generalize
the remaining assumption at c. This does not silently generalize an equality
assumption at an arbitrary label. Coincident labels are handled separately.

I independently searched the actual 60-file PA196/Logic tree. The only
downstream consumers of primitive equalityVacuity are Reverse.reverseChi and
Neighborhood.primitiveToNeighborhood, as claimed. I read those definitions
and the primitive Basic.lean calculus. The replacement is therefore at a
meaningful dependency boundary. Membership-only neighborhood vacuity is a
derived property of the defined relation and is a different interface.

The fixed relational seeds were spot-checked in
PA196/Arithmetic/Transport/RelationalClosedCertificateSeeds.lean and
PAInterpretedFixedCertificateSeeds.lean. They supply actual Derivation
objects at explicitly constructed standard reserves. They are not simply
postulates that PA proves an appropriate sentence. I found no sorry, admit,
or new axiom in the scanned logical core and these seed modules. This scan
does not replace a dependency build or exhaustive examination of all imports.

4. Foundation adapter: the traps checked
---------------------------------------

P/proof/foundation_paeq_completion.tex is substantially more specific than
an appeal to the informal equivalence of presentations of PA.

Its "Two explicit proof constructors" gives OpenOne using shift, weakening,
excluded middle, existential introduction, and cut, and CloseOne using the
literal universal rule. I checked their sequent/binder shapes. The opening
and closing loops fix the original matrix and witness packet as parameters;
their induction predicates are Delta-1/Sigma-1 checks of actual constructed
proofs. They do not require induction over all matrices or environments.

The induction recognizer searches m,b <= c but searches the property K up to
the computed opened-body code. It does not incorrectly bound K by c. The
canonical induction closure may use fewer parameters than the original
accepted closure. The proof uses the actual support m_0 and then the explicit
opening/closing constructors, rather than assuming that every padded closure
is itself recognized as a primitive induction axiom.

I also inspected the retained FoundationPAeqCodeEqualityFormula.lean,
replace_aux. The binder case shifts/opens its body before invoking the
induction hypothesis; a raw-code-decreasing recursion would be unsafe here.
The adapter's separate PR construction ranks these queries by connective and
quantifier count, which renaming preserves and taking a body decreases.

The current Foundation comparison does not assert identical axiom predicates.
It identifies 25 common finite leaves and explicitly adapts addEqOfLt from
the historical bounded existential to the current unbounded existential.
The six-node existential projection is correct. The reverse finite-base
derivation supplies z<y+1 from x+z=y using zeroLe, addition monotonicity,
and y<y+1; it uses no induction. This matters because historical I-Sigma-1
syntax lemmas require the old arithmetic base, not just old PA leaf proofs.

The hierarchy comparison explicitly embeds historical bounded/prenex
Sigma-1 syntax into the current Arithmetic.Hierarchy. The current recognizer
is compared at the internal-code level. The native endpoint is then the
actual mapped Foundation PA refutation predicate, via the retained
PA195RefutationBridge.mapped_refutation_iff. Raw Foundation false is code 13;
the numerical calculus's contradiction code 1 is reached by translation,
not identified with 13.

5. Positive clocks, halted data, and the exact physical frame
-----------------------------------------------------------

The generic P/proof/ipra_simulation_theorem.tex is sound under its stated
closure, endpoint, halt-flag, and positive-duration hypotheses. A physical
halt at t is reflected using a synchronized boundary at time >= t and
absorption, so no least-boundary search or Markov principle is required.

One must distinguish the two clock conventions actually used:

* The source-to-scheduler theorem sets theta=1 on data-retaining Done states.
  Its cumulative clock B(k)>=k therefore satisfies the generic positive-clock
  hypotheses literally.
* The physical theorem sets D(H)=0 after canonicalized halt. Thus that D
  does not satisfy global positivity. The physical manuscript supplies its
  own correct argument: if z_k is not halted, tau(k)>=3k; if physical halt
  occurs at t, assume z_(t+1) is not halted and compare the later boundary.
  Decidability of the halt flag yields z_(t+1)=H. This conditional progress
  argument is the appropriate justification; applying the generic globally
  positive theorem directly to D(H)=0 would be a mistake.

The physical proof also binds slot 12 to the width W(X) of the same spare
list X occurring on the tape. Treating it as an unrelated independent
natural would invalidate list events. Its word identities C(X)1=1B(X) and
their reversals have actual prefix inductions. The four list traversals
derive from literal rows, including the zero-length-list case. Complete tape
halves, both zero exteriors, zero head displacement at nonhalting boundaries,
and padded address equality are accounted for.

The source-command manuscript retains the exact halted register file. The
physical manuscript canonically forgets it only after halt. The erasure
commutes with the step functions and preserves the halt bit, so these are
compatible representations rather than a mismatch in the endpoint equation.

7. Relevant cross-source lessons
--------------------------------

ZFC/arithmetic_control_fetch254.md uses the same fresh-candidate clock
7*NP+4 and the positive-residual clock 2, with scratch restoration and the
fresh counter advanced at selection. It stresses fixing the initial whole
register file during bounded internal iteration, rather than inferring an
all-input runtime from external finite execution. That is exactly the useful
method in source_command_completion.tex. ZFC/internal_code_tuple_order.md
also distinguishes internal well-order/code induction from external
well-foundedness of a nonstandard model. Neither source supplies an automatic
state-count improvement or a PA endpoint for an unrelated binary table.

DiophantineConsistency/Lean/DiophantineConsistency/PRAPiOneImplication.lean
normalizes implications between open-matrix Pi-1 sentences to the appropriate
open existential form, the transfer mechanism used here. Its theorem is
about exact formula codes and models, not standard truth alone. The same
project's PRA_PROVABILITY_PLAN.md emphasizes witness bounds for the actual
refutation predicate. A small polynomial or a compact ZFC consistency
equation does not itself provide a smaller two-symbol PA machine: the theory
endpoint, numerical evaluator, enumeration, and tape implementation still
have to be supplied and counted.

ConciseIPRA/README.md measures complete Python proof checkers by tokens, AST
nodes, and bytes. Its rule fusion and shared-context ideas are relevant
engineering inspiration, but its target is a presentation bi-interpretable
with iPRA. Searching for a contradiction in that target does not establish
Con(PA). A replacement PA checker needs its own PA calibration; reductions
that move computation into normalization also need a uniform weak-base
account of that normalization. Small rule counts or source byte counts are
not binary-TM state counts.

Recommended use of this review
------------------------------

It is reasonable to inherit the revised AX11H written calibration, with its
exact pin and stated formalization boundary, when presenting a new machine
whose uniform execution relation to this source has actually been proved.
Do not describe the inherited theorem as newly Lean-checked, and do not
replace the necessary model-uniform bridge with a finite test receipt.
