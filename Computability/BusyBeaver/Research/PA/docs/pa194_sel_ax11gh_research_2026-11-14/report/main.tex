\begin{abstract}
No verified blank-start consistency machine with fewer than 194 working
states is obtained. We construct two distinct 194-state binary one-tape
machines and give complete tables, reproducible certificates, and their
precise proof boundaries. PA194SEL reorganizes two recurring decoder pairs
into history selectors. Its eight-row change has a direct proof in iPRA
of halting equivalence to the pinned primary PA194: an old halting witness
of length $t$ gives a new witness by $3t$, and a new witness by $t$ gives an
old witness by $t$. The proof is a whole-tape local calculation followed by
quantifier-free induction; it needs no source-calculus calibration.

PA194AX11GH instead changes the recurring command interface. It combines
the four-field, eleven-entry AX operation, an explicit-operand
generalization binder, no-push modus ponens, and cache-only halting.
Its all-store compiler treats early halts and the zero-pairing alias.
All 4,778 source outcomes pass an independent literal symbolic audit.

The comparison covers all 82 filed packets and five current incoming ZIP
deliveries at the pinned revision. It recognizes 110 spanning 194-state
graphs, including research and rejected tables; both new graphs differ
from every one even under stronger structural symmetries. The repository
explicitly leaves its absolute machine-specific iPRA calibration to
$\Con(\PA)$ open. This report preserves that obligation: SEL transfers
any established calibration of the primary directly, while AX11GH supplies
the new command implementation and its relative proof. Neither executable
certificates nor an older PA proof are relabeled as a completed absolute
iPRA theorem. No new end-to-end Lean build is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Outcome and the exact target}
\label{sec:outcome}

The requested target is a deterministic single-tape binary machine $M$, on
the entirely zero two-way tape with head coordinate zero, such that
\[
                 \ipra\vdash\Con(\PA)\leftrightarrow\neg H_M.
\]
Here $H_M$ means that a finite execution of the \emph{literal} machine from
that blank configuration reaches its distinguished terminal label.
Working states exclude that terminal label. Every transition writes a bit
and moves exactly one cell left or right. Both delivered tables use working
states $0,\ldots,193$, start state zero, and terminal label 194 with no rows.

The main state reduction was not achieved. The strongest new fallback
theorem concerns PA194SEL and the primary PA194 $M_0$:
\[
 \boxed{\ipra\vdash H_{M_0}\leftrightarrow H_{\mathrm{SEL}},
 \qquad\ipra\vdash\neg H_{M_0}\leftrightarrow\neg H_{\mathrm{SEL}}.}
\]
The complete proof appears in Section~\ref{sec:history-selector-proof}.
In particular,
\[
 \bigl(\ipra\vdash\Con(\PA)\leftrightarrow\neg H_{M_0}\bigr)
 \Longrightarrow
 \bigl(\ipra\vdash\Con(\PA)\leftrightarrow\neg H_{\mathrm{SEL}}\bigr).
\]
This is a direct transfer from the primary table, with no new logical or
source-frame premise. The antecedent is not established anew here.

The distinction is substantive. The pinned PA README states that neither
machine-specific direction of the primary iPRA calibration is established,
and lists the internal physical/compiler obligations separately from its
standard-run Lean results~\cite{pa-status}. The user correctly notes that
the Lean work is unfinished. Lack of a Lean file alone would not defeat a
complete written iPRA proof; the limitation here is that the inspected
written calibration proves a PA-level statement, while the repository
still identifies weaker-arithmetic proof obligations. This report does not
silently discard them. The inherited ordinary PA calibration is retained
as \file{references/primary_PA_calibration.tex}~\cite{primary-calibration}.

\subsection{Scope, deduplication, and evidence categories}

The filed catalogue describes 82 packets and 13,006 original literal table
occurrences, reduced to 682 classes across all sizes~\cite{catalogue}.
These historical counts are not our new census. The current checkout has
695 tracked \file{.tm} files in the complete PA subtree, and the five current
ZIPs contain another 874 such members. Every one of these 1,569 payloads is
read. The 695 tracked tables and five incoming archives are independently
bound to their exact Git blob identities: 700 byte checks in total.

The literal census has 802 distinct file contents. Of the 706 occurrences
with 194 controls and a unique terminal, seven are disconnected fixtures;
the others represent \textbf{110 distinct spanning graphs}. A spanning
root reaches every working control through the two labeled transitions.
The 110 count includes 94 previously indexed frontier classes and sixteen
additional classes in the current incoming material. Five of the added
classes are named principal constructions (AX11G, GH, GHS, AX11QH4L and
AX11QH4R); eleven are additional research tables. AX11G and GH each appear
in multiple current deliveries, so those copies do not increase the graph
count. A separate scan reads all 668 JSON files in the current archives
through an explicit-table recognizer and adds no frontier class.

Tables with no unique terminal and partial engine fragments are recorded
outside this recognizer's scope. They are not declared invalid programs
merely because they use a different fragment/terminal convention. The
scanner does not execute arbitrary generators or infer unevaluated
machines from prose. This is a comparison of all delivered named designs
and their explicit table representatives, not a claim that every historical
proof suite has been rerun. The complete scopes and source paths are in
\file{comparison/census_summary.json}, \file{census.json}, and the ledgers.

The most valuable distinction for future state reduction is between
source savings and physical savings. The recurring allocation
$134+67-7=194$ already combines a 134-control fixed engine, 67 axiom
readers, and seven simultaneously valid folds. Removing primitive source
instructions can leave all three numbers unchanged. Conversely a larger
reader grammar can be competitive if it supports more compatible folds.
The compiler must score the final simultaneous table, then verify its
resolved source graph and its startup.

\subsection{Exact novelty and ordinary quotients}

For each complete spanning table, a breadth-first traversal from a chosen
root lists both bit-labeled actions. The complete tuple of writes, moves
and newly numbered targets uniquely determines that rooted machine.
Minimization over all spanning roots removes the need to guess the start
of a native historical file. A stronger comparison also allows global
left/right reflection and bit exchange and ignores write/move on terminal
edges. Bit exchange is a structural test here; it does not automatically
preserve the zero-blank semantics.

Every comparison uses full tuples; hashes are identifiers and integrity
checks. All 110 historical ordinary and strong classes are distinct.
Both new machines differ from all 110 and from each other. The coarsest
ordinary control bisimulation of every historical table and both new
tables still has 194 classes. This rules out ordinary state identification
for those tables. It says nothing against reductions that exploit tape
context, change data representation, or redesign the source.

\section{Research toward fewer than 194 states}
\label{sec:search}

The experiments in this section did not produce an accepted smaller
blank-start machine. They are included to make the unsuccessful part of
the work reproducible and to avoid repeating it blindly. A rejection by
a bounded local proof search is not necessarily a counterexample to a
candidate; an overapproximated context set or a short search window may
fail to prove a semantically valid rewrite.

\subsection{Physical folds and selector synthesis}

A new strict local-fold search considered 4,107 oppositely witnessed
control pairs with excursions through at most fifteen transitions. It
found no additional single-control removal. A broader joint two-row
contraction search on the primary considered all 18,721 unordered control
pairs. Its radius-eight, 160-step run tried 26,284,284 single-row proposals
and 158,117 compatible joint combinations without a successful contraction.
The search uses a sound finite-window context overapproximation and does
not protect arithmetic controls from being considered.

The same method was run on the five new single-selector graph classes
and all six two-selector compositions. The former covered 93,605 pairs,
124,837,804 single-row proposals and 679,537 joint combinations; the latter
covered 112,326 pairs, 149,758,736 proposals and 794,093 joint combinations.
No smaller table passed those bounded contraction models.

SEL itself was found by an exact same-count synthesis. Sixteen pairs
have a common branch and opposing forced predecessor bits. The search
tries both choices of keeper, redirects the old selector's incoming
edges, and tests 1,204,352 provider assignments on the final simultaneous
table, with arbitrary exterior tape. Twelve successful labeled tables
form six graph classes: the already supplied B design and five new
single-selector designs. SEL composes two of the new designs. The
acceptance proof for SEL uses its eight fixed cases, not the exhaustiveness
or implementation of the discovery search.

A further search tries to share one selector between two pairs, using
the original current cell as a tag for the pair. The basic family covers
48 control/tag allocations and 1,787,952 provider assignments, with
31-step two-cell contracts. It produces no accepted 193-state table.
The expanded search and the source-grammar outcomes are recorded in the
following supplement and the accompanying research files.

\subsection{Why a 193-state runtime core is insufficient}

Several prior packets fold native 301 into 88 to obtain a 193-control
runtime. The modified marker behavior prevents the tested blank startups
from reaching the required source frame. The filed research includes
finite halts and outward-ray certificates, as well as bounded unresolved
trials; these are kept separate in the source reports. A computation that
works only from a prepared register frame is not a blank-start consistency
machine. Adding an initializer outside the counted table would change the
problem. Our new selector designs retain the startup behavior and state
count explicitly. No global lower bound of 194 is inferred from any of
these experiments.

\section{Reproduction, trust boundaries, and integration}

The main verification requires Python 3.10 or newer and only its standard
library. Assertions must remain enabled for the inherited AX11GH engines.
From the archive root run
\begin{quote}\ttfamily
python3 verify.py --full
\end{quote}
The quick mode omits the longest independent literal symbolic replay;
the complete replay and its recorded output are included. No network,
repository checkout or proof assistant is needed to rebuild the two
delivered tables or replay their frozen comparison corpus. Recollecting
the repository census requires the pinned checkout and current ZIP inputs.
Optional discovery searches use a C++17 compiler and are not prerequisites
for checking the delivered machines.

The verification boundaries are deliberately small for SEL. Its builder
and independent checker agree on the pinned parent identity, all eight
changed rows, all 380 ordinary transition cases, all eight exceptional
cases, and exact dense/native conjugacy. An independently authored second
checker reconstructs the same native bytes and verifies the whole startup.
The written generic induction consumes those finite premises. This gives
a direct route for a future Lean formalization: define the binary-zipper
step functions, prove the four entry-history implications, prove the eight
fixed local equations, define the primitive-recursive clock, and apply
quantifier-free induction. The absolute primary endpoint can then transfer
without reopening source coverage or PA-proof compilation.

AX11GH has a larger proof surface. It retains the exact axiom emitter and
distribution-exchange certificate, explicitly changes five controller
rows, and checks every relocated source successor. Its independent literal
checker derives four unary-list lemmas from those rows and covers arbitrary
register values and spare lists. Its command proof includes the early-halt
Pop, the reserved-zero MP frame, the fourth AX operand, cofinal candidate
enumeration and a bounded induction invariant for the proof compiler.
Those results should be integrated as source and physical components,
with the absolute weak-arithmetic calibration still stated separately.

No repository commit, issue, pull request, or message was published during
this work. The supplied archive is the complete reviewable deliverable.
