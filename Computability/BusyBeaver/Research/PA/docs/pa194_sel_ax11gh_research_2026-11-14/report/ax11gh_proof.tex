\section{PA194AX11GH: four-field AX, explicit generalization, and cache-only halting}
\label{sec:ax11gh}

This construction combines the AX11G axiom operation with the cache-only
Shift of the H family. It has 194 working states and 388 transitions on a
two-way infinite binary tape, with a completely blank start. The dense
start is zero and the halt label is 194, excluded from the count. Its
dense SHA-256 is
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
513e9c8b28e0dd41e6003bb8d82c80ff3dac5fb3c5274cd2f709a3bba8c3e9e1.
\end{quote}
This is a fallback construction; it is not a sub-194 result.
The absolute calibration to PA consistency remains a separate obligation.

\subsection{Exact source and physical construction}

The immediate parent is the AX11G table from the pinned incoming packet.
The emitted axiom word, its coefficient-four coding, its Q orientation of
universal distribution, and all four AX operands are unchanged.
Five native rows change:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
q&\text{read}&\text{old write/move/target}&\text{new write/move/target}\\\hline
38&0&0,R,28&0,R,11\\
38&1&1,R,20&1,R,122\\
214&0&0,R,188&0,R,312\\
214&1&1,R,312&1,R,20\\
312&0&0,R,11&0,R,188
\end{array}
\]
The corresponding source edit is
\[
\texttt{node170}: (\texttt{node36},\texttt{node169})
\quad\longmapsto\quad
((\texttt{node36},(\texttt{node167},\texttt{node145})),\texttt{node57}).
\]
An independent resolver shows that this deletes exactly the instruction
$\operatorname{inc}X$ at old source index 3661. The neighboring loop
decrements the visible top $T$. Previously it accumulated $T$ in $X$;
now it clears $T$ without adding it to $X$. The subsequent copy of the
cached head $H$ into $T$ and $X$ is unchanged. Every other primitive
instruction and successor agrees under the index relocation
$j\mapsto j-\mathbf1_{j>3661}$.

The raw machine has 134 fixed controls and 67 AX-reader controls.
The inherited seven context folds have all 28 local cases rechecked on
the final simultaneous table, giving $134+67-7=194$ controls.
The source has 3671 sites and 4778 branch outcomes. Both construction
and independent literal-tape certificates are supplied. The latter
derives its four list-sweep lemmas from the rows themselves and does not
call an arithmetic-block oracle.

The native start is 74 and the native halt label is 254. A literal
57,922-step run from the all-zero tape reaches dispatcher 84 at head
coordinate $-2$, source site 25, logical registers $(2,0,\ldots,0)$,
and 26 one-valued spare blocks. The logical-to-physical register map is
$(6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1)$. The check compares the complete finite support of
the tape, including both header markers, the source address, all nine
register blocks, the spare tail, and the zero exterior.

\subsection{Numerical calculus and the precise command equations}

Let $p(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b$ and write $[a;w]=p(a,w)$.
Cantor projections are total: $\operatorname{hd}(0)=\operatorname{tl}(0)=0$
and $[0;0]=0$. Put
\[
 I(a,b)=4p(a,b),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(i,a)=4p(a,i)+2,\quad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
Code zero is truth, code one is the contradiction target, and
$N(a)=I(a,1)$, $a\wedge b=N(I(a,N(b)))$,
$\exists_i a=N(U(i,N(a)))$.

The unchanged Q-oriented separate-diagonal calculus has truth, modus
ponens, generalization, and the following seed families:
\begin{align*}
 h_1(D,H)&=I(\exists_D U(D,H),H),\\
 h_2(D,A,H)&=I(U(D,U(A,H)),U(A,U(D,H))),\\
 h_3(D,A,H)&=I(M(H,D),I(E(H,A),M(A,D))),\\
 h_4(D,A,H)&=I(E(H,D),I(M(A,H),M(A,D))),\\
 h_5(D,H)&=\exists_D E(H,D),\\
 h_6^Q(D,A,H)&=I(U(D,I(A,H)),I(U(D,A),U(D,H))),\\
 \rho(D,A,H,u)&=I(I(I(H,u),D),I(I(D,H),I(A,H))),\qquad u\le D,\\
 \Gamma(A)&=I(A,U(A,A)).
\end{align*}
For the guarded seed, define
\begin{align*}
 P(a,b,c)&=I(c,M(c,b)),\\
 V(a,b,c)&=I(M(a,b),U(c,M(a,b))),\\
 S_0(a,b,c)&=U\bigl(b,I(M(a,b),P(a,b,c))\wedge I(M(c,b),c)\bigr),\\
 Q_0(a,b,c)&=U(c,I(S_0(a,b,c),N(P(a,b,c)))),\\
 g(a,b,c)&=\begin{cases}N(I(V(a,b,c),Q_0(a,b,c))),&c\ne a,b,\\
 0,&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
\end{align*}
The guarded seed rule supplies the nonzero displayed gate under its
guard; the zero branch is covered by truth. The seed system has the
same conclusion set as the primary distribution orientation by the
finite implicational exchange template retained in the AX11G packet.
No new arithmetic-calibration claim is required for this equality of
the numerical calculi.

A store is $(t,w,d,h)$, consisting of visible top, stack, offset and
cached formula. For AX operands $(c,x,y,z)$ put $D=d+x$, $H=h+y$ and
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
q_0&g(H,D,c)\\q_1&\Gamma(c)\\q_2&h_6^Q(D,c,H)\\q_3&h_5(D,H)\\
q_4&h_4(D,c,H)\\q_5&h_3(D,c,H)\\q_6&h_2(D,c,H)\\
q_7&h_1(D,H)\\q_8&\rho(D,c,H,d)\\q_9&U(c,h).
\end{array}
\]
All of $q_0,\ldots,q_8$ have residue zero modulo four. The last head
$q_9$ has residue two and is at least two. It generalizes the incoming
theorem cache $h$, not the arithmetic sum $H=h+y$.

The command effects are
\begin{align*}
 \mathsf A_{c,x,y,z}(t,w,d,h)&=(0,[q_0,q_1,\ldots,q_9,t;w],z,0),\\
 \mathsf M_x(t,w,d,h)&=
 \begin{cases}(d+x,w,d+x,0),&I(t,d+x)=h,\\
 (0,w,d+x,0),&I(t,d+x)\ne h,\end{cases}\\
 \mathsf S(t,w,d,h)&=
 \begin{cases}\mathsf{halt},&h=1,\\
 (h,\operatorname{tl}(w),d,\operatorname{hd}(w)),&h\ne1.
 \end{cases}
\end{align*}
The fourth operand $z$ is consumed by AX's fallthrough through the
complete MP reader. After the guarded top is pushed, the visible top,
offset and cache are zero. MP on operand $z$ returns visible zero both
when $z=0$ and when $z>0$, and retains offset $z$. Thus the fourth field
is part of the exact semantics; it cannot be erased from the decoder.

\subsection{Universal stack identities, including early halts}

Words run left to right. Let $A_0=\mathsf A_{0,0,0,0}$ and set
\[
 \mathsf{Push}=A_0;\mathsf S^{10};\mathsf M_0,
 \qquad \mathsf{Pop}=A_0;\mathsf S^{12},
 \qquad \mathsf{Discard}=\mathsf M_0.
\]
For arbitrary natural entries,
\begin{align*}
 \mathsf A_{c,x,y,0};\mathsf S^{10}(t,w,d,h)&=(q_8,[t;w],0,q_9),\\
 \mathsf A_{c,x,y,0};\mathsf S^{11}(t,w,d,h)&=(q_9,w,0,t),\\
 \mathsf{Push}(t,w,d,h)&=(0,[t;w],0,0),\\
 \mathsf{Discard}(t,w,0,h)&=(0,w,0,0),\\
 \mathsf{Pop}(t,w,d,h)&=
 \begin{cases}\mathsf{halt},&t=1,\\
 (t,\operatorname{tl}(w),0,\operatorname{hd}(w)),&t\ne1.
 \end{cases}
\end{align*}
The first eleven Shifts test zero or generated heads, never one.
The twelfth Shift tests the saved arbitrary top. This is the essential
change from AX11G, whose sum-based Pop safely returned even when $t=1$.
Push's final MP comparison fails because an implication has residue
zero and $q_9$ has residue two. Discard produces the candidate conclusion
zero on both MP branches. All these identities include the collapsed
zero stack and its virtual zero entries.

For $0\le j\le8$, the selector
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf A_{c,D,H,0};\mathsf S^{j+2};
 \mathsf{Pop}^{9-j}
\]
sends $(t,w,0,0)$ to $(q_j,w,0,0)$. Every selected head has residue zero,
so its Pops are safe. The last Pop removes the saved zero and restores
the arbitrary old stack. For a desired restricted seed with $u\le D$,
replace the AX prefix by
$\mathsf{Discard};\mathsf M_u;\mathsf A_{c,D-u,H,0}$ and use
$\mathsf S^{10};\mathsf{Pop}$. The identity
$\mathsf M_i(0,w,0,0)=(0,w,i,0)$ includes $i=0$.

The shorter generalization word is
\[
 \boxed{\mathsf{Gen}_i=
 \mathsf{Push};\mathsf S;\mathsf A_{i,0,0,0};\mathsf S^{11}.}
\]
It sends $(a,w,0,0)$ to $(U(i,a),w,0,0)$ for every $a,w,i$.
After Push and one Shift the store is $(0,w,0,a)$. That Shift tested
the preceding zero cache. AX consumes $a$ before another Shift tests it,
so even $a=1$ is covered. Its explicit operand $i$ becomes the binder.
The offset-setting MP required by AX11H has disappeared.

For modus ponens, from $(0,[a,I(a,b),0;w],0,0)$ the suffix
\[
 \mathsf S;\mathsf S;\mathsf M_b;\mathsf{Pop}
\]
halts exactly in the two cases $a=1$ or $b=1$, and otherwise returns
$(b,w,0,0)$. The second Shift tests $a$. If it does not halt, successful
MP leaves $(b,[0;w],b,0)$; Pop then tests $b$ and removes the reserved
zero on its returning branch. The case $a=b=w=0$ follows from
$I(0,0)=[0;0]=0$. No hidden nonempty-stack hypothesis is used.

\subsection{Derivation compiler and its quantifier-free induction}

Compile a checked numerical-calculus derivation $e$ into a command word
$C(e)$. Truth uses Discard, a seed uses its selector, and a generalization
appends $\mathsf{Gen}_i$ to its predecessor word. For an MP node with
major proof $e_1$ of $I(a,b)$ and minor proof $e_0$ of $a$, use
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Push};C(e_1);\mathsf{Push};
 C(e_0);\mathsf{Push};\mathsf S;\mathsf S;\mathsf M_b;\mathsf{Pop}.
\]
For every incoming $(t,w,0,0)$, the compiled word either halts or
returns $(\operatorname{concl}(e),w,0,0)$. The initial Discard and Push
reserve zero; both recursive premise words preserve their full incoming
stack, so the reserved zero survives until the last Pop.

For clarity, the unbounded phrase ``for every store'' is not used as
the induction formula in iPRA. Fix a finite proof-node table, a height
bound $F$ (leaves have height one), and the desired inputs $t,w$. Let
$K\ge1$ bound all conclusion codes in the table and define
\[
 R_0=\max(1,K,t,w),\qquad R_{j+1}=(K+R_j+3)^4.
\]
At stage $k\le F$, check every proof-table node of height at most $k$
on every incoming top and stack at most $R_{F-k}$. The assertion is
the bounded conjunction that its compiled word halts or has the stated
return value. All bounds, compiled words and length-bounded executions
are primitive recursive; the conjunction is a primitive-recursive
Boolean equality and is therefore a quantifier-free induction formula.

In a successor step, an MP node queried on stack $y\le R$ invokes
its two predecessors on $[0;y]$ and $[I(a,b),0;y]$, with input top zero.
The inequality $p(v,x)\le(v+x+1)^2$ and $I(a,b)\le K$ bound both
stacks by $(K+R+3)^4$, exactly the previous stage's allowance.
Generalization invokes its predecessor on the same input. Leaves use
the local identities above. Earlier-height nodes remain covered because
the input bounds decrease. At stage $F$, the required root instance
includes the fixed $t,w$. Universal generalization in these originally
free parameters yields the all-store compiler theorem.

The constructors also have a primitive-recursive length bound. A seed
selector has at most 121 commands, the generalizer has 25, and an MP
node adds 53 beyond its predecessor words. Thus the deliberately coarse
bound $L_k\le128(2^k-1)$ at height $k$ is valid. This is a word-length
bound, not a claim that formula codes or physical runtimes are small.

If $e$ concludes one, then
\[
 R_e=A_0;C(e);\mathsf{Pop}
\]
halts from every incoming store $(t,w,d,h)$. The first AX clears dirty
offset and cache. The compiler either halts already or returns top one;
the final Pop detects that one. A final plain Shift would be incorrect:
$\mathsf S(1,w,0,0)=(0,\operatorname{tl}(w),0,\operatorname{hd}(w))$.

\subsection{Fair enumeration, soundness and exact scope}

For continuations define $e(\epsilon)=0$,
$e(C::v)=1+b_C(e(v))$, with
\begin{align*}
 b_{\mathsf S}(r)&=p(0,r),\\
 b_{\mathsf M_x}(r)&=p(1,p(x,r)),\\
 b_{\mathsf A_{c,x,y,z}}(r)&=p(c+2,p(y,p(x,p(z,r)))).
\end{align*}
The first command of a fresh enumerated candidate omits the outer one.
The AX read order is $y,x,z$ after its tag. Total Cantor projections
recover this exact four-field code, including every zero-field alias.
After any positive continuation, the residual is strictly smaller.
Hence nonhalting candidate runs have a primitive-recursive command bound.

Prefix $R_e$ by $\mathsf A_{k,0,0,0}$. The resulting fresh candidate
codes
\[
 N_k=p(k+2,p(0,p(0,p(0,e(R_e)))))
\]
are unbounded in $k$. The prefix is total and leaves some store; the
suffix halts from every store. The scheduler tries consecutive candidates
from two while carrying its proof store, so a chosen $N_k\ge2$ supplies
a primitive-recursive finite halting witness. This does not assume that
the store resets between candidates.

Conversely annotate the top, cache and each canonical stack entry with
a derivation of its formula code. Zero's fixed proof covers virtual zero
tails. AX appends seed proofs, the saved old top and a generalization of
the incoming theorem cache; its extra MP field produces truth for every
$z$. Successful MP composes proof annotations and failure produces truth.
Shift moves annotations and can halt only when the cache has an annotated
derivation of one. Canonical stack expansion and annotation checks have
primitive-recursive bounds. Induction on a bounded run prefix is a
quantifier-free Boolean invariant, not an arithmetic truth predicate.

These two witness maps prove in iPRA that the \emph{stated command
scheduler} halts exactly when the Q-oriented numerical calculus proves
one. The exact source binding and positive symbolic tape contracts give
the intended compiler from these commands to the literal table. To
internalize the latter in iPRA, encode finite tape halves as naturals and
use quantifier-free induction for the explicit unary sweeps and register
list traversals. Positive local durations give the reverse witness map:
if the physical run halts at time $T$, more than $T$ unhalted source
steps would contradict determinism. Bounded search therefore extracts a
source halt. The supplied checkers audit these local simulations; they
are not proof-assistant kernels.

The construction is thus a relative transfer within the current machine
family. In particular, it supplies no new proof that the numerical
calculus and PA have iPRA-provably equivalent refutability. The repository
explicitly records that missing calibration and unfinished physical
formalization. If the primary machine's requested iPRA consistency
equivalence is established, and the stated physical simulation is
internalized, the relative command and tape translations transport it to
AX11GH. Neither an ordinary standard-model simulation
nor a finite Python receipt alone discharges the absolute calibration.

The source difference is substantive. AX11GH has the explicit-binder,
four-operand axiom interface of AX11G, while its Pop and MP compiler have
the early-halt behavior of AX11H. At $(1,w,0,0)$ its Shift does not halt;
at $(1,w,0,1)$ it does. AX11G has the opposite behavior on those two
stores. Relative to AX11H, the saved universal changes from $U(d,h)$
to $U(c,h)$ and the generalizer loses one command. These are recurring
command differences, not state renaming or a change of start state.
