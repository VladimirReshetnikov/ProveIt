\section{The weak-arithmetic proof boundary}
\label{sec:weak-audit}

The target is an object-theory theorem
\[
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash\operatorname{Con}(\mathsf{PA})
                  \leftrightarrow\neg H_M,
\]
where $H_M$ is the arithmetized blank-tape halting assertion for the
\emph{literal} transition table.  Four mathematically different claims
must therefore be kept separate:
\begin{enumerate}
\item equality of transition tables up to a stated renumbering;
\item equivalence of standard finite runs of two concrete machines;
\item a proof of their arithmetized halting equivalence in a weak theory;
\item calibration of one of those halting predicates to PA refutation.
\end{enumerate}
An executable finite test may establish the first assertion or check a
finite certificate used in a proof of the second or third.  Neither a long
nonhalting run nor a theorem quantified only over the metatheoretic natural
numbers supplies the fourth assertion.  A proof in PA also cannot simply
be relabelled as a proof in iPRA.

\paragraph{What the pinned repository supplies.}
At revision \texttt{603c58ca3bd6bf3222fcfc4e308e6b079f65d573},
\texttt{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/README.md} says explicitly~\cite{pa-status}
that neither machine-specific direction is established in iPRA for the
primary PA194.  It records considerable completed standard-run work and
some model-uniform components.  In particular, source coverage,
calculus/clock composition and the complete source scheduler over ordinary
natural numbers are useful inputs, but they do not by themselves provide
the arbitrary-model physical simulation or the internal compilation of
arbitrary PA refutations.

The generic transfer in
\texttt{PA/isigma1-transfer/README.md} gives a precise sufficient target~\cite{transfer}:
for a $\Sigma_1$ halting sentence $H$, an
$\mathsf{I\Sigma_1}$ proof of
\[
 \operatorname{Ref}_{\mathsf{PA}}\leftrightarrow H
\]
and the specified refutation-bound certificate give the desired iPRA
consistency equivalence.  The separate certificate is supplied by the
PA196 endpoint development.  Thus an arbitrary-model proof in
$\mathsf{I\Sigma_1}$ is sufficient; one need not reproduce the entire
machine proof directly inside intuitionistic quantifier-free induction.
It remains necessary to bind the halting sentence to the exact table and
the blank initialization.

\subsection{A precise preservation criterion}

Extend a machine's transition function to make its halt configuration
absorbing.  Let $h_M(t)$ be the decidable primitive-recursive predicate
that $M$ has halted by time $t$.  Suppose primitive-recursive functions
$u,v$ have been supplied, together with iPRA proofs of
\[
 \forall t\,[h_M(t)\to h_N(u(t))],\qquad
 \forall t\,[h_N(t)\to h_M(v(t))].                 \tag{*}
\]
Then, by intuitionistic existential introduction and elimination,
\[
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash H_M\leftrightarrow H_N
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash\neg H_M\leftrightarrow\neg H_N.
\]
No excluded middle, Markov principle, consistency assumption, or
reflection principle is used in this last inference.  Consequently any
established calibration of $M$ transfers immediately to $N$.

The word ``proofs'' in (*) is essential.  Merely exhibiting programs that
appear to implement $u,v$, or observing that all involved operations are
primitive recursive, does not establish their correctness in iPRA.
Correctness can instead follow from an explicit quantifier-free invariant
whose preservation is proved by induction, or from the repository's
$\mathsf{I\Sigma_1}$ all-model route.

\subsection{How finite local blocks produce witness maps}

For a local rewrite, encode each finite tape configuration by a natural
number.  A sufficient certificate records a decidable frame invariant,
a primitive-recursive relation between old and new checkpoint
configurations, and the following facts.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The complete blank initialization reaches related checkpoints;
      every earlier configuration is nonhalting.
\item Each source or old-machine step is represented by a positive-length
      new-machine block.  Its duration has an explicit primitive-recursive
      bound (often an affine expression in register values).
\item The entire final tape, head position and control state satisfy the
      claimed checkpoint relation.  Equality of only the inspected window
      is insufficient unless the untouched tail is quantified explicitly.
\item No strict block interior reaches halt or a conflicting rewrite site.
      If another rewrite site occurs internally, a joint simulation argument
      must cover it.
\item Halting checkpoints correspond in both directions.
\end{enumerate}

The forward clock is obtained by primitive recursion, summing block
durations along the old run.  For the reverse direction, positivity is
what prevents an infinite number of checkpoints from being compressed
into a finite new-machine run.  Within a new-machine run of length $t$,
there are at most $t$ completed positive-length blocks after the fixed
startup.  Bounded search through the first $t+1$ checkpoint times locates
the containing block.  The nonhalting-interior property forces any
observed halt to a corresponding halting checkpoint.  With absorbing
halting semantics, a bound of $t$ on the source step index is sufficient.
All clocks and bounded searches here are primitive recursive.  The
remaining proof obligations are the local identities and invariant
induction, which must be shown for arbitrary encoded values, not just a
finite sample of register inputs.

This criterion is useful for independent validation because it separates
three responsibilities: the finite table certificate, the generic
arithmetic induction that consumes that certificate, and the inherited
logical calibration.  A valid new reduction need not reprove PA's
logical calibration, but it cannot make an open calibration unconditional.

\subsection{Limits on tempting logical substitutions}

The source calculus's head
$\Diamond_a\Box_a B\to B$ is stronger in a different direction from
$\Box_a B\to B$.  The first is a symmetry principle in normal modal
logic; the second is reflexivity.  In particular, positive vacuity
$P\to\Box_a P$ together with reflexivity and normal distribution does
not entail negative vacuity
$\neg P\to\Box_a\neg P$: a reflexive accessibility relation may move
from a world where $P$ is false to one where it is true.

The existing diagonal-calculus calibration uses the first head to extend
membership-only vacuity through negation; that extension is used in
reverse membership rewriting, which is then used in the neighborhood
equality interface.  Recovering the first head from arbitrary equality
substitution by citing those same interfaces would therefore be circular.
This observation is a dependency obstruction for the existing proof, not
a countermodel to every remaining axiom of the full separation calculus.
A new separation-based derivation could still change the conclusion, but
it must be provided explicitly before a shorter emitter is accepted.

The coefficient-three collision is a separate obstruction.  With the
unchanged tags $I(a,b)=3p(a,b)$ and $E(a,b)=3p(a,b)+3$, equality and
implication collide.  The pinned
\texttt{pa194\_ax11s\_study/proof/cofinite\_collision.tex} gives an
unbounded family of refutations even after excluding finitely many
equality-witness instances.  It does not rule out a typed or otherwise
redesigned three-constructor calculus.

\subsection{What can be reused from the related projects}

The ZFC context-return packet supplies a clean model for a valid local
rewrite argument: exact whole-tape blocks, positive lengths, a check that
their interiors avoid modified rows, a complete blank initialization, and
all-natural register contracts~\cite{zfc-context}. The K217 construction
also uses a preceding written bit to distinguish two merged control
entries~\cite{zfc}. Their logical calibration concerns ZFC
and is not a PA calibration.

The DiophantineConsistency work supplies the weak-arithmetic conservation
and negative-translation machinery behind the generic transfer above~\cite{diophantine}.
Its polynomial operation counts do not translate directly into binary-TM
state counts; an evaluator and complete blank initialization still have to
be compiled and counted.

ConciseIPRA supplies examples of replacing logical primitives by derivable
interfaces~\cite{concise}. A search for refutations of its unmodified calculus concerns
iPRA consistency.  To use it for the present problem one would first need
a precisely specified stronger theory calibrated to PA, for example an
appropriate full-induction extension with a verified syntactic
negative translation.  A smaller Python checker for iPRA alone cannot
supply the requested consistency machine.
