1. Conclusion and exact evidence boundary

I found no accepted machine below 196 working states in the requested PA
directory or in any of the 20 incoming ZIPs at the pinned revision. This is an
inventory conclusion, not a lower bound. The directory-level COMPARISON.md

PA |- Nonhalt(PA196) -> Con(PA) from its missing formally checked converse.

biconditional. Several incoming manuscripts give ordinary mathematical
proof-producing routes to the converse. They do not supply a new end-to-end
kernel-checked PA biconditional. A mathematical proof may legitimately be
stronger than the shipped theorem inventory, but its unproved bridges must
be addressed, not concealed by a passed transition-table test.

4. Why the208 saving cannot just be added to196

The original nine208 tables all identify the historical native roles172
and211. Different surviving labels and helper numbers encode the same
deletion, so adding their savings double counts it. Their two source layouts
have identical3909-site ordinal CFGs,5083 nonhalting edges,720 differing
paths and maximum path lengths42 versus41. Both max-zero runs are6.

James Chang's PA209 is a different209-state parent. PA209 -> PA196 removes75
native working labels, introduces62 and retains134. Of268 rows on common
working labels,265 are identical and only3 change. Both native roles172 and

achieved by transposed quantifier coding, removal/derivation of rho7 through
guarded equality vacuity, mirrored/reordered heads and contextual decoder
sharing. PA196 already includes its six advertised AX reader folds.

PA196:3812 source sites;36 engine+13 leaf-route+147 reader states=196.
PA209:3948 sites and209 states. Original U208:3909 sites and208 states.
Thus there is no monotone relation between primitive-site count and physical
state count. The new198/199/203 implementations with shorter source illustrate
this particularly clearly.

7. Reverse PA implication: the substantive remaining bridges

Two uniform all-model assertions would suffice:
  literal halted trace -> accepted numerical checker proof of code1;
  accepted checker proof -> an ordinary PA refutation.
The shipped transport proves these in the opposite direction. External HF
truth/soundness is not the second assertion inside PA.

The strongest incoming direct route interprets membership by arithmetic
bit membership B(u,v), equality by equality, and PA196's transposed
quantifier directly. PA proves B(u,v)->v<u, hence code1 is false. A uniform
proof-producing bounded-comprehension induction template handles the gate.
Care is needed when variable LABELS coincide (not merely their assigned
values), when code0 aliases truth, and with pair(0,0)=0 stack underflow.
The repository certificate-stack note supplies a useful bounded invariant:
only nonzero numeric-stack cells require live certificates, later cells may
be ghosts, and underflow returns a fixed proof of truth. None of these ideas
alone is the missing arbitrary-model trace extraction or a compiled proof
constructor. Existing forward positive segment proofs can potentially be
reversed by internal bounded trace comparison and determinism, not by
assuming inverse state maps.

8. Relevance of iPRA and ZFC

The iPRA checker-size metric counts proof-checker source structure, whereas
this target counts binary tape states including initialization. Replacing
the PA search by a plain iPRA search changes the required strength. Useful
ideas are derivable logical interfaces, fused arithmetic/template operations
and explicit proof-producing translators. An HA-strength extension can be
equiconsistency-calibrated to PA by negative translation, but needs a full
exact compiler before it has a relevant state count.

The ZFC work supplies contextual helper folding, source grammar factoring
and proof-friendly positive-block simulation. Its exact logical consistency
target cannot simply be retained for a Con(PA) machine. The present source
geometry result uses the compiler ideas without claiming that a proof-checker
token saving is already a tape-state saving.
