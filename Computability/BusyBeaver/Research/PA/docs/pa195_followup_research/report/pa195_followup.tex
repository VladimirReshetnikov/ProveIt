\begin{abstract}
The requested construction with fewer than 195 working states is
\textbf{not established by this packet}. We compare the supplied PA
constructions, validate a distinct 195-state binary machine, and document
the exact obstruction in a genuine 194-state local quotient.
That quotient preserves the entire source control-flow graph and has eight
compatible local folds, but fails a physical instruction contract because
the old scanner mistakes seven address zeros for a separator.
One additional scanner state repairs the full runtime, giving
$135+68-8=195$ working states.

For this repaired machine, the packet supplies the complete 390-row table,
deterministic reconstruction, 32 local simulation cases, all 4,815 symbolic
primitive contracts, and an exact 59,164-step blank initialization.
A common-source argument proves its halting PA-equivalent to the supplied
PA195 machine. It consequently inherits the supplied ordinary mathematical
PA biconditional with $\Con(\PA)$; no new end-to-end Lean or Rocq theorem is
claimed. The comparison covers all 31 incoming archives and 36 selected
distinct rooted machine graphs. An independently reproduced finite
rotation census and several explicitly bounded searches find no smaller
validated machine. None is a general state lower bound.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, conventions, and evidence}
\label{sec:result}
The definitive new table is
\file{machine_195s8/pa195_scanner8.tm}.
The model is the same one used in the supplied PA195 construction:
one deterministic tape extending infinitely in both directions, alphabet
$\{0,1\}$, an initially all-zero tape, and a head initially at coordinate
zero. Working states are $0,\ldots,194$, the start is $0$, and the separate
halt label is $195$. Each working-state/read-symbol pair has exactly one
transition. Every move is left or right by one cell. The halt state has no
outgoing transition and is excluded from the count.

A line \((q,b,w,D,n)\) means read $b$, write $w$, move in direction $D$,
and enter $n$. All 195 controls are reachable in the finite transition
graph. Graph reachability is not a claim that every control occurs on the
actual blank execution.

\subsection{Three different kinds of claim}
\textbf{Finite exact facts} include table hashes, state counts, graph
reachability, the 32 bounded local traces, and the complete startup tape.
They are independently executable checks on fixed data.

\textbf{Uniform physical correctness} includes all primitive instruction
contracts for arbitrary natural register contents and all admitted gap
parameters. These are symbolic identities, with unchanged register-block
lemmas, rather than a collection of numerical test inputs.

\textbf{Arithmetic calibration} is an ordinary mathematical proof that
PA proves the desired consistency biconditional. Here it is inherited
from the supplied PA195 report by the unconditional halting equivalence
proved in Section~\ref{sec:transfer}. The included checkers are not a proof
assistant and do not turn this ordinary proof into a newly compiled
Lean or Rocq theorem.

\section{Why the 194-state local quotient is rejected}
\label{sec:failure}
The strongest new unsuccessful candidate keeps the exact 191-token
AX emitter, all 3,700 ordered primitive instructions, every ordinal
successor, and the same initial source site 25. Its unfolded machine has
202 working controls. Eight disjoint, simultaneously compatible folds
produce a literal 194-state, 388-row table. An independent parser,
compiler, and two-cell executor confirm these finite facts.

Those facts do not establish that the physical machine executes the source.
Its binary association creates seven consecutive zero bits in some source
addresses. The inherited scanner counts seven zeros as a separator.
It can therefore return to the source root at the wrong position.
The four offending decrement sites are:

\small
\begin{tabular}{rll}
\toprule
Source ordinal & Operation & Exact address\\
\midrule
1003 & decrement logical register 1 &
\texttt{101010011010100101110000000}\\
2310 & decrement logical register 1 &
\texttt{10101001101011001010000000}\\
2552 & decrement logical register 1 &
\texttt{10101001101011010000000}\\
3390 & decrement logical register 1 &
\texttt{101010011011110000000}\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The first failed raw symbolic contract is source ordinal 1003 on the
zero branch of logical register 1, which is physical register 2.
The checker completes 1,309 earlier branch contracts before encountering
this failure. The maximum full source address length is only 37;
overall address length is not the failing condition.

The rejected table and its successful local quotient certificate are
retained in \file{evidence/rejected_candidate_194/}.
The negative replay checks the failure explicitly.
They are useful together: they demonstrate why a source CFG identity
and a correct physical quotient are insufficient without a valid
source-to-parent runtime.

\subsection{Exact scanner repair}
The native left-scanning counters include
$2,3,5,9,15,23,34$. On a one the scan resets; the old transition
\[
 (34,0)\mapsto(0,L,75)
\]
recognizes the seventh zero. The repaired table inserts native control
1999:
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
(34,0)&\mapsto&(0,L,1999),\\
(1999,0)&\mapsto&(0,L,75),\\
(1999,1)&\mapsto&(1,L,2).
\end{array}
\]
Every other raw row is unchanged. The identifier 1999 is fresh and is
not one of the AX readers, whose labels start at 2000.
The longer scan costs exactly one state.

The repaired unfolded parent has 203 controls. Applying the same eight
folds gives the accepted 195-state alternative. Its maximum zero run
is seven, and its entire symbolic runtime passes. Merely relaxing the
zero-run filter would not have sufficed: another seven-zero grammar
failed a separate contract even with this scanner. Full instruction
verification remains the acceptance criterion.

\section{Construction and complete physical verification}
\label{sec:physical}
\subsection{The eight simultaneous folds}
All pairs below use native labels. The first state is deleted and mapped
to the second:
\[
\begin{array}{rrrr}
2018\to2024,&2023\to2004,&2030\to2051,&2033\to2019,\\
2042\to2028,&2054\to2043,&2056\to2035,&2062\to2037.
\end{array}
\]
The final replacement transitions, including their helpers, appear in
\file{machine_195s8/folds.json}. They are checked in the final, jointly
folded table. Selecting several individually valid folds without this
joint check would be unsound because a fold may change a helper used
by another fold.

\begin{lemma}[Context-sensitive quotient]
\label{lem:quotient}
Let $R$ be the 203-state repaired parent and $Q=\Mnew$ its folded table.
For every finitely supported initial tape, with common native start
control 74, $R$ halts if and only if $Q$ halts. PA proves the equivalence
uniformly in the finite initial-tape code.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $\sigma$ identify each deleted state with its keeper and fix all
other states. Start 74 and halt 254 are fixed. The 25 raw transitions
entering a folded pair satisfy the following history invariant: every
entry to its first state writes zero and moves right; every entry to
its second writes one and moves right. Thus the cell immediately left
of the head records the relevant old state role. Induction on the raw
run proves this invariant independently of the initial tape.

For each pair, each old role, and each current bit, the delivered local
certificate runs $Q$ from the corresponding two-cell tape and mapped
control. Its endpoint has exactly the raw transition's final tape,
head displacement, and mapped successor control. All intermediate
reads and writes stay in these two cells, so arbitrary tape extensions
are immaterial. There are 32 cases: 12 have length one, 18 length three,
and two length five. No case passes through a halt. Every other raw
transition commutes with $\sigma$ in a single step.

Concatenating these positive blocks simulates every raw step, with
between one and five folded transitions per step. A finite raw halt
therefore gives a finite folded halt.
Conversely, if the folded run halted in $T$ steps but the raw run did
not halt in its first $T+1$ steps, those raw steps would yield a folded
run longer than $T$. Determinism and the absence of halt transitions
contradict that conclusion. No inverse map on arbitrary folded
configurations is required.

Finite configurations and traces have primitive-recursive codes.
PA proves the incoming-history invariant by induction, checks the fixed
finite local identities, concatenates the bounded positive blocks,
and proves the converse by the same $T+1$ argument.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Source identity and arbitrary-register runtime}
The macro emitter is unchanged as a word in the free algebra of
formula constructors, reads, pushes, and guard operations.
The check includes the complete resulting stack and top formula;
it is not an equality test on a few numerical parameters.
Two independent successor resolvers confirm the exact source graph,
including all break and continue targets.

For every primitive site, the symbolic checker begins with the source
path, an affine zero gap, and a complete symbolic register file.
It checks both outcomes of each decrement and the exact output frame,
including all registers and the updated gap.
The underlying register permutation is
\[
(0,1,\ldots,8)\longmapsto(6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1).
\]
All 46 transition rows protecting the inherited opaque register-block
lemmas are unchanged. The supplied PA195 packet contains those reference
sources and their arithmetic induction arguments. Explicit sweep and
register-file trace constructors, together with those induction proofs,
establish the quantified premises. The finite Python execution checks
the corresponding symbolic templates; it is not by itself a
proof-assistant formalization of the universal contracts.

\subsection{Complete blank initialization}
Starting with native state 74, head coordinate zero, and an entirely
zero tape, both the repaired raw table and the folded table reach the
same clean source frame after exactly 59,164 literal transitions.
The control is native 84, the head is at $-2$, and the source ordinal
is 25 with path \texttt{100000}. The logical register vector is
$(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$.

The complete physical file has values
\[
(0,0,0,0,0,0,2,0,0,
\underbrace{1,\ldots,1}_{26\text{ spare registers}}).
\]
The header gap is 48. The certificate lists all 66 tape positions
containing a one and asserts that every other position is zero.
An independent dense byte-array simulator reproduces the same complete
tape. Matching only a nearby window or only the visible registers
would not establish this startup theorem.

\section{Transfer of the internal PA biconditional}
\label{sec:transfer}
The new work can be isolated from the inherited logical calibration.
Let $S$ denote the deterministic ordinal register program whose exact
3,700 instructions and successors are shared by PA195 and $\Mnew$.
Its initial ordinal is 25 and its logical register vector is as above.

\begin{theorem}[Common-source transfer]
\label{thm:transfer}
PA proves
\[
\Halt(\Mnew,\mathrm{blank})
\ \longleftrightarrow\
\Halt(\Mold,\mathrm{blank}).
\]
This transfer uses no consistency or soundness assumption.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For either physical machine $N$, let $E_N(i,\mathbf r,g)$ denote its
complete encoding of source ordinal $i$, register file $\mathbf r$,
and an admitted gap $g$. Source paths may differ between the two
grammars; ordinal instructions, register meanings, and successors do not.

Its fixed blank startup reaches $E_N$ of the common source seed.
For every nonhalting source instruction, its primitive contract gives
a finite positive physical segment to the encoding of the exact source
successor. For a source halt, the contract gives a finite positive
segment to the distinguished physical halt. Register-file and gap
preconditions are preserved. These statements hold for arbitrary
natural file entries, by the symbolic identities and unchanged
register-block lemmas.

PA proves by induction that a finite halting source run yields a
halting physical run. For the converse, suppose a physical run halts
in $T$ transitions. It cannot halt during the certified startup.
If $S$ failed to halt through its first $T+1$ steps, primitive
recursion would generate those source steps, and their positive
physical segments, with startup prepended, would yield a defined run
longer than $T$. This contradicts determinism at the alleged halt.
Hence PA proves
\[
\Halt(N,\mathrm{blank})\longleftrightarrow\Halt(S,\mathrm{seed})
\]
for both choices of $N$, and the displayed equivalence follows.

The argument uses finite coded configurations, induction, and
primitive-recursive generation of the finite traces and registers.
It does not require an internal truth predicate for PA.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Consistency calibration, ordinary mathematical level]
Using the supplied PA195 calibration,
\[
\PA\vdash
\neg\Halt(\Mnew,\mathrm{blank})\longleftrightarrow\Con(\PA).
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The supplied PA195 report proves the corresponding PA biconditional
for $\Mold$ by syntactic proof translations, finite bit comprehension,
a proof-carrying store invariant, and positive simulation.
Combine that theorem with Theorem~\ref{thm:transfer}.
\end{proof}
The original report and relevant reference sources are included under
\file{reference_pa195_packet/}. The ordinary arithmetic argument was
read during this audit; this is not a claim that its entirety has been
reproved by a new proof-assistant kernel run.
The imported PA196 Lean endpoint proves the internal nonhalting-to-
consistency direction. It must not be mistaken for a previously
compiled internal biconditional for this new literal table.

\section{What the searches establish}
\label{sec:search}
\subsection{An exhaustive, finite rotation neighborhood}
A move either rotates one occurrence of a binary association or rotates
all occurrences of one distinct ordered subtree. Both directions
$((a,b),c)\leftrightarrow(a,(b,c))$ are allowed. Macro leaves remain
fixed. Every first move and every second move is enumerated; invalid
intermediate encodings are retained so that a second move may repair
them.

There are 218 distinct one-move results, 24,154 results obtainable
in exactly two moves, and 24,319 distinct grammars in the union
including the seed. These sets overlap. Of the union, 12,277 pass
the stated encoding gates. Every admitted grammar is scored by exact
simultaneous compatible-fold search, with finite-state cycle detection
instead of a local fuel cutoff. The minimum allocator count is 195,
attained by 14 union grammars.

A separate Python implementation reconstructs every canonical grammar
key, the one/two-move reachability flags, the encoding properties,
and every admitted fold score. It agrees with the C++ census.
The complete key file, per-grammar census, sources, and receipts are
included in \file{search/two_rotation/}.
This is an exclusion for that neighborhood and allocator; it is not a
claim that PA195 is minimal among all grammars or all Turing machines.

\subsection{Local deletion screens and heuristic searches}
On the verified 195S8 table, the two-cell screen tried 32,010 asymmetric
aliases and 2,784 opposite-history pairs in both predecessor directions.
Allowing arbitrary finite nonrecursive helper detours gave no deletion.
A stronger two-cell screen allowed either replacement row to be
revisited recursively: 5,451,830 row assignments and 29,074,603
simulated local steps also yielded no deletion. Exact cycle detection
closes these finite local searches. A positive control rediscovers an
existing fold in the unfolded parent.

A separate discovery screen derived conservative tape windows from
the complete symbolic contracts and startup, excluding opaque and
self-sweeping controls. It considered 165 controls, 13,530 pairs,
and 258,720 initial actions. Both a five-cell window and a nine-cell
window gave no additional deletion. This screen is documented as
a particular discovery family, not as a universal no-fold theorem.

Pure control aliases, partial-provider rewrites, source rotations,
equal-word subtree crossovers, scanner shortening, and several finite
stochastic association searches were also tried.
The receipts distinguish proposal slots, actually scored grammars,
unique retained grammars, optimistic matching bounds, and genuinely
compatible folds. These quantities are not interchangeable.
In particular, several apparent 194 scores from plain bipartite
matching disappeared when all selected folds were checked together.

The source invariant using the finite domain
$\{0,1,2,3,\ge4\}$ reaches 3,697 of 3,700 sites and removes five
of the 4,815 branch contracts. It exposes no free transition row
in either PA195 or 195S8. It does not prove that every abstractly
reachable site occurs on the concrete blank execution.

\section{Ideas from ZFC and ConciseIPRA}
\label{sec:ideas}
The ZFC developments suggest optimizing physical allocation jointly
with source syntax: predecessor markers can distinguish two logical
reader roles, useful helpers can be shared, and a partial selector may
use a transition row whose other role has a proved restriction.
These are concrete compilation ideas rather than a change of target
consistency statement. The new eight-fold parent and the additional
helper searches pursue that approach.

The ZFC project also illustrates the need to keep source proofs,
literal runtime checks, startup, external correctness, and internal
arithmetic statements separate. Its documented K215 endpoint and
the internal PA result for the distinct 240-state table have different
formal scopes. Neither endpoint can simply be relabeled as a PA
consistency construction \cite{zfc}.

ConciseIPRA provides a complementary idea: factor common proof
operations, weaken a primitive rule when its full form is derivable,
and combine rule families through a shared syntactic interface.
Its clause, one-body, and evaluation-discharge presentations make
these possibilities explicit \cite{ipra}. The searches in the following
appendix apply that style of factoring to the PA195 diagonal clause:
move it to another gate parameter, or bundle it with an existing
negated equality-witness head.

The size measure in ConciseIPRA is the complete normalized proof checker,
including significant tokens, AST nodes, and bytes. A smaller such
checker does not imply a smaller binary blank-tape TM. Moreover,
bi-interpretability with its stated iPRA target alone does not supply
the internal PA calibration with $\Con(\PA)$ required here.
Both arithmetic calibration and a complete physical compiler would
still be necessary before substituting that calculus.

\section{Reproduction, limitations, and useful next targets}
\label{sec:reproduce}
The top-level command
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
python3 verify\_all.py
\end{quote}
checks the package hashes and runs the principal exact finite,
symbolic-runtime, rejected-candidate, comparison, and source-family
checks. Use ordinary Python without \texttt{-O}; the physical
reference checker deliberately refuses optimized execution.
The source-family proof checks separately exercise both interpreter
modes using explicit failure conditions.
The two-rotation census has its own longer replay instructions;
its exhaustive regeneration is not silently represented by a
quick structural check.

The complete accepted machine is reconstructible with the Python
standard library. A C++17 compiler is needed to repeat the finite
searches. The report builds with \texttt{pdflatex} from its
\file{report/} directory. Search executables are not required to
verify the accepted table.

Three research targets remain concrete:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Find a physically valid 194-state association with the original
scanner, or a 193-state reader/runtime allocation before paying for
the eighth-zero scanner. The failed 194 seed and the accepted repair
provide exact inputs and failure conditions.
\item Change a shared logical routine or macro body enough to reduce
the total compiler cost. The new 51 equal-length source words offer
proved source-calculus alternatives, but the searches here do not
turn any into a smaller accepted machine.
\item Permit allocation changes involving more than the tested
two-cell folds, one-control replacements, or bounded helper families,
while retaining a fully checkable positive simulation and exact
blank startup.
\end{enumerate}
No empirical search in this packet proves that these approaches must
fail. Conversely, no lower local score is accepted as a consistency
machine until its whole chain of proof obligations passes.
The requested strict reduction remains open in this delivery.
