\subsection{Dedicated extra routers}
A distinct allocation experiment adds a read-only helper
\[
 (h,0)\mapsto(0,R,u_0),\qquad
 (h,1)\mapsto(1,R,u_1).
\]
The helper is absent from the raw machine and appears only in the folded
implementation. A correct local certificate may execute it during a
detour. Its cost is worthwhile only if it enables more state deletions
than the number of added helpers.

For the fixed original PA195 grammar, one-helper screening finds 1,147
useful routers and at most eight optimistic folds; nine would be needed
to reach 194. For the fixed scanner-eight grammar it finds 1,287 routers
and at most nine folds; ten would be needed. Both conservative incoming
histories and the broader, unreachable-helper interpretation were tried.
Two-router screening also checks cases where the routers complete
different missing branches of the same pair. The fixed-grammar tests
still give no optimistic score below 195.

Across 172 retained admissible original-PA195 grammar ties, the
matching upper bound on fold savings yields an optimistic 194-state
count for 24 grammars with one helper, but all fail the simultaneous
compatible-fold check. The completed all-control two-helper screen
also finds optimistic 194 scores on 24 grammars, and rejects all
11,655 concrete improving router assignments by exact history and
cycle checks. Related AX-only and one-helper modes overlap with
this search; their trial counts must not be summed as distinct machines.
Across 141 distinct score-60
scanner-eight ties from two final search pools, neither one nor two
added routers gives even an optimistic 194 score. Complete sources and
receipts are included in \file{search/added_routers/}.

Finally, the start control was checked separately rather than discarded
by a generic rule against merging the start. On blank tape, native 74
has a known zero on its left, consistent with its incoming history.
General recursive two-cell start-fold searches over 52 eligible
partners in PA195 and 51 in 195S8 both fail. This closes that specific
exception to the earlier local screens, not arbitrary startup changes.

\subsection{Completed association runs}
Seven separately seeded root searches use 23,000,000 proposal slots:
three 3,000,000-slot ordinary association runs, two 3,000,000-slot
encoding-repair runs, a 3,000,000-slot scanner-eight run from the
original seed, and a 5,000,000-slot scanner-eight run from the
eight-fold seed. The repair runs preserve a 194-state local quotient
only with remaining encoding violations. The relaxed scanner runs
find no allocation below 195 after the extra scanner is counted.
An additional 3,000,000-slot equal-word crossover run has the same
negative outcome.

These figures count proposal slots, including proposals rejected before
scoring. They are not counts of distinct machines. The separate source
families in the final appendix have their own exact scoring-call totals.
Finite seeds, headers, executable source code, final pools and logs are
retained so that these empirical outcomes can be examined or repeated.
