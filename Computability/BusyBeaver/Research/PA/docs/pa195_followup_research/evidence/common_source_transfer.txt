Direct transfer from PA195 through the common source program
==========================================================

Let S be the deterministic ordinal register program whose3700 entries
are the exact primitive instructions and exact successor ordinals in
PA195/source.json. Its initial ordinal is25, its logical register vector
is the one represented by the supplied clean startup file, and its
register permutation PHYSICAL is unchanged. The new grammar must yield
exactly the same S; comparing a flattened word without successors is
insufficient.

For each binary machine N (PA195 or an accepted new machine), let E_N(i,R,g)
be the physical source frame at ordinal i with complete register file R
and the required header gap g. The path in E_N may depend on its grammar.
All other encoding conventions and the abstract register operations agree.
The startup certificate proves its blank run reaches the corresponding
initial frame. The primitive certificate proves uniformly in every valid
file and every admitted gap:
  (i) every nonhalting source step has an exact finite N segment to the
      corresponding successor frame, with the claimed updated registers;
  (ii) a source halt has an exact finite N segment to its distinguished halt;
  (iii) each segment contains at least one literal N transition;
  (iv) file/gap preconditions are preserved.
The register-block excursion lemmas are unchanged. Their arithmetic
induction proofs and the finite symbolic trace checker establish these
uniform statements in PA, not merely for sampled numerical files.

These premises prove PA ⊢ Halt(N,blank) ↔ Halt(S,initial). The forward
source-to-machine implication concatenates its source-step segments.
For the reverse, suppose N has a T-step blank halt but S has no halt in
its first T+1 steps. Primitive recursion produces these T+1 source steps
and their positive N segments, with startup attached, giving a defined N
run longer than T. Determinism and partial halting semantics contradict
the alleged T-step halt. If N allegedly halted before startup completed,
the fixed complete startup trace already contradicts it. Every operation
is finite and primitive recursive; PA proves the induction and comparison.

Applying this theorem to both PA195 and the new N gives the unconditional
relative theorem
   PA ⊢ Halt(N,blank) ↔ Halt(PA195,blank).
No consistency or soundness premise enters this transfer. Combining it
with the inherited ordinary mathematical PA195 calibration gives
   PA ⊢ Nonhalt(N,blank) ↔ Con(PA).
This presentation isolates exactly what is new. It neither silently
upgrades PA195's ordinary proof to a newly compiled Lean theorem nor
requires a second reconstruction of its arithmetic refutation compiler.
