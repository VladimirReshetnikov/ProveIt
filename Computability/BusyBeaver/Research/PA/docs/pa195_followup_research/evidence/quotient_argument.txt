Independent audit: exact context-sensitive quotient theorem
=========================================================

Let R be the unfolded table obtained from the candidate grammar, and Q be
its folded native table. Let sigma fix every control except the deleted
first member a of each fold pair (a,b), for which sigma(a)=b. All fold pairs
must be disjoint; neither start 74 nor halt 254 may occur in a pair.

Premise 1: Every R transition entering a writes 0 and moves R; every R
transition entering b writes 1 and moves R. Every folded control is reached
by at least one row. Consequently, in every configuration reachable in R
from start74, q=a implies tape(head-1)=0 and q=b implies tape(head-1)=1.
This is an induction on elapsed transitions. It applies to arbitrary
initial tapes because no folded control is an initial control.

Premise 2: For each of the 2*(2*number_of_pairs) choices of old control q
and current bit v, the delivered two-cell trace, starting Q at sigma(q)
with its required history bit at -1 and bit v at0, reproduces exactly the
one R transition from q,v. Its final control is sigma(the R successor),
its final head displacement is the R direction, and both tape cells have
exactly the R final contents. Every read and write before that endpoint
occurs at -1 or0. Therefore the identity holds under EVERY extension of
those cells to a full tape. The trace must be positive and never access a
halt row. Direct comparison verifies all remaining R rows commute with
sigma in one Q step. Simultaneous folds are checked in the final folded
table; individually valid merges alone would be insufficient.

These premises define a forward simulation on all R-reachable states,
with exactly unchanged tape and head at comparison boundaries. Each R
step is simulated by at least one and at most L Q steps, where L is the
maximum of the finite certificate lengths and1. A finite R halt therefore
yields a finite Q halt, with start and halt unchanged.

Conversely, if Q halts in T steps and R had not halted in its first T
steps, concatenate the comparison blocks for T+1 R steps. This would
give a Q run with more than T defined transitions from the same initial
configuration. Determinism and the absence of a halt row make that
impossible. Thus Q halting implies R halting. No inverse map is needed on
arbitrary Q configurations, and no claim of one-step bisimulation is made.

The finitely-supported initial-tape version is provable in PA, uniformly
in its finite initial-tape code. The assertion for arbitrary infinite tapes
is an external mathematical observation. For the internal theorem, code
finite configurations and finite
traces in the usual primitive-recursive way; prove the incoming-history
invariant by induction; use the fixed finite local identities as closed
arithmetic facts; concatenate at most L steps per parent step; and prove
the converse with bounded iteration of T+1 parent steps. No consistency
or soundness assumption is used in the theorem.

Scope and inheritance
---------------------
This quotient proves Q equivalent to its OWN unfolded parent R. To inherit
PA195's consistency theorem, one must also check: exact source word and
all ordinal successors; unchanged emitter word and arithmetic interface;
every primitive symbolic frame contract (including both decrement cases);
all protected register-engine rows; and the COMPLETE blank startup frame.
Reassociation plus matching CFG alone is not enough. The inherited binary
runtime has a finite zero-run scanner, and some grammars with the same
source CFG violate its encoding conditions. The rejected k306 candidate
is a concrete counterexample to treating the CFG identity as sufficient.

The PA195 arithmetic calibration was read in full. Its proof direction
Halt -> inconsistency is syntactic: translate numerical calculus proofs
into PA proofs using a uniform finite-bit-comprehension induction template;
maintain annotations for both visible formula registers and the canonical
Cantor stack; use positive command and physical segments to rule out a
finite physical halt under consistency. It does not claim an internal PA
truth predicate or invoke PA soundness. Its code-freshness argument, the
I(0,0)=0 alias, source false-code translation, coincident binder case a=b,
and non-resetting scheduler are all addressed explicitly. The reverse
direction reuses the PA refutation compiler with explicit diagonal
vacuity templates. No new gap was found in these displayed arguments,
but this review is not a kernel formalization of the arithmetic endpoint.
