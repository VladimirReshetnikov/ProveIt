\label{sec:comparison}

The source snapshot is ProveIt commit
\texttt{5be8b9236df6a6d308195e62a906a173dc70d00a}.
There are \emph{31 ZIP archives}, not 32: the thirty-second directory item is

\subsection{The useful distinctions}
The original nine U208 presentations represent two rooted transition graphs,
not nine independent deletions. Eighteen later helper alternatives expand that
family to twenty rooted graphs. Their final saving is the same history-sensitive
identification of the native 172/211 roles; adding the stated savings across
those papers would count the same deletion more than once. James Chang's
historical PA209 is a different parent from the native U209s of this family.

Published PA196 replaces 75 native controls of Chang's PA209 by 62 new ones,
for a net reduction of 13. Its important source changes are the transposed
Cantor-pair quantifier code, omission of the old seventh equality head, guarded
equality vacuity, and a reorganized axiom emitter. The U208 roles 172 and 211
are already absent. The six contextual folds in the published PA196 count are
also already included in its 196 states.

The strongest source simplification relevant here is the diagonal-vacuity
calculus. Its 191-token emitter has 3,700 primitive sites, compared with
201 tokens and 3,812 sites for PA196. The separate-head version has 190 tokens
and 3,673 sites, but the extra stack entry changes the command compiler.
PA195 keeps the diagonal emitter and changes its binary association, obtaining
\(134+68-7=195\) working controls. Consequently a further useful association
can preserve the existing logical calibration while changing which physical
reader controls can share a history-sensitive implementation.

A smaller source is not automatically a smaller physical machine. NEG18
implements \(4\operatorname{pair}(t,1)\) by two increments of register 7
followed by the existing \texttt{node64} core, using
\(\operatorname{pair}(t,1)=\operatorname{pair}(0,t)+2\).
It has a complete 18-instruction source proof and a scratch-zero invariant,
yet the supplied complete implementation uses 197 controls. The source-count
saving loses useful physical sharing.

\subsection{What was independently checked in this comparison}
The delivered comparison corpus contains 36 selected literal tables:
32 pairwise nonisomorphic 196-state graphs, three 197-state graphs, and PA195.
The older 25 selected 196-state tables are the PA195 packet's supported set.
The seven additions comprise four Combined/zerojump grafts, two MP-in-H
compositions, and a separate-calculus closing-R113 variant. A fresh checker
verifies every transition domain, graph reachability, unique halt, start label,
literal hash and rooted action-preserving canonical graph. None of these
36 selections is an alias of another. This is a selected physical-table corpus,
not an exhaustive census of every exploratory machine in every ZIP.

Evidence statuses remain explicit in the JSON and CSV. In particular the two
new MP-in-H compositions have full physical instruction contracts but their
packet does not complete the new arithmetic calibration. They are not silently
promoted to proved PA-consistency constructions. Two older 196-state Shift
experiments halt on blank tape after 43,563 steps and are excluded from this
selected positive/experimental corpus. The six older diagnostic 195-state
constructions likewise remain rejected; the PA195 packet is a later successful
construction, not a rehabilitation of any of those failed tables.

\subsection{Proof scope and useful prior negative results}
The imported Lean declaration proves the internal direction
\(\mathrm{PA}\vdash\operatorname{Nonhalt}(\mathrm{PA196})\to
\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})\). Its external standard-model nonhalting
proof is a different theorem. The PA195 report supplies an ordinary
mathematical proof of both internal directions, using uniform PA proof
translations, finite bit comprehension, a proof-carrying store invariant and
positive physical simulation. A new physical descendant can inherit that
ordinary theorem through a proved PA-formalizable halting equivalence.
This requires no assertion that an end-to-end Lean or Rocq biconditional has
already been exported.

Prior failures cover substantial but restricted families: direct one-row
contractions, finite-window helper repairs, specified source rotations,
parameter/head permutations, short scanner templates, and complete-instruction
endpoint filters. For example, the cross-archive packet rejects 59,928,960
one-keeper-row assignments on finite complete-instruction fixtures. The later
seven-head search records 43,033,542 scoring evaluations, still with minimum
196 in its searched family. Such results motivate changing association and
simultaneous helper allocation; none is a lower bound on all smaller machines.
