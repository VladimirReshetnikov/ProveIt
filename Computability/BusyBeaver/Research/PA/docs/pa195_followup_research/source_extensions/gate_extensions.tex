\subsection{Further gate transformations with explicit scope}
The broader search combines the three diagonal locations with the following
finite changes.  First,
\[
 \neg(X\to(Y\to Z))\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
 \neg(Y\to(X\to Z))
\]
is proved by the explicit deduction template in
\texttt{proof\_templates.json}; the reverse direction is the
same template with $X,Y$ exchanged.  This exchanges $\Gamma$ and $V$ in the
two leading antecedent positions without introducing an extra negation.
Second, exchanging the numerical parameters $a,b$ throughout a gate preserves
the guarded axiom family, since $c\ne a,b$ is symmetric and the exchange is
an involution.  The gate selector exchanges its two input increments after
the ordinary reset; no unbounded parameter search is needed.

Third, use the four separation matrices already considered by the earlier
seven-head search: the antecedents in
$M(a,b)\to(c\to M(c,b))$ may be exchanged, and its conjunction with
$M(c,b)\to c$ may be exchanged.  Each direction is a fixed propositional
proof.  To transport such an equivalence through the enclosing universal
quantifier, generalize its closed proof and use the retained universal
implication-distribution head.  Implication and negation congruence are fixed
propositional templates; repeating these operations transports the matrix
equivalence through $S$, $Q$, and the whole gate.  Thus every resulting gate
has primitive-recursive proof translations to and from the gate before the
matrix exchange.  These are proof operations on closed derivations; no
generalization under an unverified open assumption is used.

There are $3\cdot2\cdot2\cdot4=48$ distinct 67-token gate words.  Their
complete free-algebra expressions and stack effects were independently
checked by \texttt{verify\_source\_family.py}, with the unchanged PA195 gate
bound exactly as variant zero.  All full AX words retain 191 tokens, nine
pushes, the original parameter reads, and the same guard suffix.  This is a
finite source search family, not a physical-state lower bound; any proposed
small transition table still requires compatible-fold and complete scanner,
primitive-runtime, and startup verification.
