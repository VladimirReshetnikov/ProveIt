REPRODUCE
Python 3.10+ and its standard library suffice. Keep assertions enabled.
From any working directory:

  python3 -B /absolute/path/to/this/packet/reproduce.py

This uses a fresh temporary copy and leaves the packet unchanged. It rebuilds
the table, checks commands and exchange, independently resolves the source,
runs both full physical symbolic passes, compares regenerated bytes, and
checks graph novelty. Several minutes may be needed. No network, external
repository, proof assistant, or solver is required.

  python3 -B reproduce.py --quick

Quick mode omits both full physical symbolic passes and reports that scope.
It does not relabel the recorded full checks as newly rerun.
Use --work /new/nonexistent/directory to choose the replay location.
