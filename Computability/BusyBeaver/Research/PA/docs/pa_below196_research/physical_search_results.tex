\subsection{Completed wider and displaced screens}

The radius-six blank-start abstract closure has 270,067 state/window pairs.
Its seven-cell projection has 8,120 contexts. It contains an abstract route
to halt, so it is used as an overapproximation for local simulation checks,
not as a nonhalting certificate.

The wider direct-fold screen checks all 38,220 ordered deleted/retained
state pairs. Child segments may last up to 32 transitions and must stay
inside seven cells. After projecting the deleted state to the keeper, up to
two retained rows may change arbitrarily. It examines 98,072,128 first-row
assignments and 798,376,992 second-row assignments. There are no unpatched,
one-row or two-row survivors.

The displaced screen uses the full thirteen-cell contexts and offsets
$-2$ or $+2$. Its 33,735 eligible choices are 173 eligible deleted states
times 195 keepers. With one additional arbitrary helper-row replacement,
32-step positive segments and exact whole-window endpoints, it examines
47,684 helper locations and 37,384,256 replacement triples. There are no
unpatched or patched survivors.

An independent Python simulator checks 512 seven-cell contexts for a known
two-helper synthetic fold and 160 five-cell contexts for a genuine displaced
fold. The C++ engines also recover these controls. This checks that the new
templates can recognize positive examples; it is not a proof that every
possible optimization fits either template.

\subsection{Three-row screen: a recorded partial result}

A recursive failure-trace search handles all choices of up to three changed
rows for each selected pair, within five cells and sixteen child steps. This
differs from the older representative-only third-row extension. The planned
pair set contains the 348 ordered pairs sharing an original outgoing row.
Exactly 84 completed pairs are recorded here. They examine 162,081 first-row,
328,246,128 second-row and 9,582,125,364 third-row assignments, with zero
survivors. The run was stopped to pursue source specializations; it is not
exhaustive for the 348-pair set, much less for all state pairs. The exact
completed pairs are in \file{physical_search/partial_three_receipt.json}.

\subsection{A shorter H-only Shift guard}

The accepted H-only Shift source enters its comparison with scratch
register $X=1$ and arbitrary $H$. Under that explicit condition, the generic
eleven-site equality guard can be specialized to seven sites: clear $X$;
test $H$ twice; halt precisely if its entry value was one; otherwise clear
the remaining $H$ and continue. The cases $H=0$, $H=1$ and $H\geq2$, followed
by induction on the clearing loop, prove the all-natural local contract.

The independent source checker resolves both full control graphs twice,
checks the exact seven-row replacement, verifies all 3,800 exterior rows
under the ordinal shift, and rules out exterior entries into the removed
interior. The H-only source decreases from 3,811 to 3,807 sites. However,
its distinct internal nodes increase from 159 to 163, because the old
comparator's pieces are reused elsewhere. The first leaf shortens from
\texttt{000000} to \texttt{00000}, but twenty other addresses still contain
six consecutive zeros. Maximum depth remains 39. No shortened scanner,
binary allocation or new blank-start proof follows. The exact source and
ordinary proof are included in \file{physical_search/}.
