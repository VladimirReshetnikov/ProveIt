A seven-instruction specialization of the H-only Shift comparison
===============================================================

This is a source-program specialization, not a smaller Turing machine.

The incoming H-only Shift construction is in pa_consistency_reduction.zip,
member pa_consistency_reduction/comparison/audit/h_only_shift_note.tex. Its
accepted 196-state table uses source
h_catalan_node170_3_133_37_sites.json. The note explains that the modified
preparation clears T, copies H into T and X, transfers X back to H, and then
increments X. Consequently the comparison is entered with X=1 and H equal
to the saved h. The generic comparison that remains in the source halts iff
H=X and clears both registers on normal exit.

Explicit entry condition: register 6, called X, equals 1. Register 5, called
H, is any natural number. All seven other registers are arbitrary. The new
local program has this exact control-flow table (zero successor first):

  0: decrement X; zero -> 2, positive -> 1
  1: decrement H; zero -> 5, positive -> 2
  2: decrement H; zero -> 4, positive -> 3
  3: jump to 5
  4: halt
  5: decrement H; zero -> exit, positive -> 6
  6: jump to 5

Its source tree is

  (((dec X, dec H), ((dec H, break 1), halt)), (dec H, continue 1)).

Proof for all natural inputs
----------------------------
At instruction 0 the explicit condition X=1 forces the positive branch;
X becomes zero. No later instruction changes X.

If the entry H is zero, instruction 1 takes its zero branch to 5, and 5
immediately exits. The final H and X are both zero.

If the entry H is one, instruction 1 changes H to zero and continues to 2.
Instruction 2 takes its zero branch to 4, which halts.

If the entry H is at least two, instructions 1 and 2 decrement it twice;
instruction 3 reaches the loop at 5 with H equal to the entry H minus two.
Each positive iteration decreases H by one, so ordinary natural-number
induction proves termination. The zero branch exits with H=0 and X=0.
Every other register is unchanged in all cases because none is mentioned.
Thus this block halts exactly for entry H=1, and otherwise has the same
register effect as the inherited H=X comparison under X=1.

The corresponding old block can be checked by the same three cases. Its
first successful H/X pair of decrements changes X=1 to X=0. For H=1 it then
reaches its equality halt; for H>=2 it enters the H-clearing branch with
X=0. For H=0 it clears the remaining X=1 and exits. The old and new blocks
therefore have identical halt/normal outcomes under the stated condition.

Finite transcription checks
---------------------------
verify_guard.py independently resolves the complete old and new control
flows in two ways: binary-prefix cutting and interval/ancestor traversal.
It checks that the first seven new rows are exactly the proved program,
that only the old node7 subtree has changed, and that all 3,800 exterior
rows agree under ordinal transport i -> i-4. Every exterior edge into the
old guard enters at its first instruction; the checker rejects an edge
into the removed interior. A deliberately altered local successor is a
negative transcription control. The checker does not infer the proof from
testing a finite range of H.

Measured effect and limitation
------------------------------
The H-only source shrinks from 3,811 to 3,807 primitive occurrences. Its
number of distinct internal source nodes grows from 159 to 163: the old
comparison's subtrees are heavily reused elsewhere. Its first leaf address
shortens from 000000 to 00000, but 20 other addresses still contain a run
of six zeros. Maximum depth remains 39. This does not justify shortening
the return scanner, and the source-node increase is unfavorable for the
current compilation scheme.

The source entry invariant is inherited from the H-only preparation
argument, not freshly exported as a Lean theorem here. No physical table
was generated for the new tree, no complete runtime proof or blank-start
simulation is supplied, and no PA consistency biconditional is claimed.
The useful result is the explicit local specialization and the exact
reason it does not currently give a state saving.

Reproduce the finite source checks:
  python3 verify_guard.py
