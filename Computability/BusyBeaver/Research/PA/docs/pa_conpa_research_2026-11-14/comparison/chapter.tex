\subsection{What was counted}

The fresh recognizers find 908 occurrences of complete 194-state matrices:
597 in \texttt{.tm} files and 311 in supported JSON or other literal-text
representations. They collapse to 111 table classes under arbitrary
renaming of controls and forgetting the designated start. Of these, 110
admit a spanning root and one is a disconnected diagnostic. The supported
JSON representations include explicit row arrays, pairs of action triples,
flat action arrays, numeric-state dictionaries, transition-key dictionaries,
row records, and embedded complete textual tables. ZIPs are read recursively. The unified replay verifies 13,109 source files against their pinned Git blobs and reads 18 distinct ZIP payloads through 30 archive references.

printed only in mathematical prose.

\subsection{The substantive design axes}

The principal table has the state budget
\[
134\text{ fixed controls}+67\text{ AX readers}-7\text{ contextual folds}=194.
\]
Its source has 190 AX macro tokens and 3,673 resolved primitive sites. The
alternative 68-reader/eight-fold table and the many 135-fixed-control
variants illustrate why a shorter source program need not be a smaller
literal machine: helper controls and lost compatible folds matter too.

The Q axis exchanges the two curried premises of universal distribution.
N removes the dummy push from successful modus ponens. C returns the
consequent in the cache. H changes the halt guard from $t+h=1$ to $h=1$;
therefore a visible contradiction with zero cache requires Pop rather
than an immediate plain Shift. AX11 pushes the guarded gate and falls
through the complete MP operand reader, which really consumes a fourth
field. G binds the saved generalization by the explicit first AX operand
$c$, replacing the offset $d$. Symmetric comparison is an implementation
axis and is not the same change as the H guard.

Existing AX11G combines AX11, G, Q, N and the original sum Shift. Existing
GH and GHS combine G, Q, N and H with the ten-entry AX protocol. The two
new constructions combine the explicit binder, four-operand eleven-entry
AX protocol and cache-only halt guard, and provide two physical comparison
implementations of that command interface. This is a new combination of
credited mechanisms, rather than a claim to have invented each ingredient.

\subsection{Identity traps and the four incoming archives}

PA194C is repeated by five packets. PA194HS is the same graph as the
cache-only machine called PA194NS in the lowercase filed packet, but the
sum-guard PA194NS in \texttt{PA194NS\_research} is different. PA194QN and
PA194NQ coincide; the two QNS deliveries coincide; CSZ and X coincide;
N6, QH5, QN6 and AX11QH5 each occur in repeated principal deliveries.

The four current incoming archives contribute five new principal graphs:
AX11G, AX11QH4L, AX11QH4R, GH, and GHS. Their additional eleven research
graphs also enter the novelty comparison. The current incoming AX11H
archive contributes a new written-proof claim, not two new tables:
AX11H is byte-identical to the filed AX11H, and its QAX11H is the filed
AX11QH. Some older native tables were numbered from zero without making
zero their intended start. Hence start metadata is preserved separately
from the graph equivalence used for novelty.

\subsection{Exact novelty of the two delivered tables}

For each candidate and every spanning historical representative, the
checker computes the complete lexicographically least transition tuple
over every spanning root, both tape orientations, both global bit
conventions, and with terminal write/move decorations ignored. It compares
the complete tuples. The SHA-256 values merely identify the retained
evidence and are not substituted for equality checking.

Both new tables have zero historical matches and differ from each other.
They each admit a spanning root, so the disconnected diagnostic cannot
be isomorphic to either. The enlarged comparison allows arbitrary control
renaming and alternate starts as structural symmetries; global bit exchange
is deliberately stronger than a symmetry of the specified all-zero blank
execution. Operational materiality is established by the changed command
interface and repeated comparison implementation, rather than by the
novelty test alone. The offline replay is
\texttt{python3 comparison/check\_novelty.py}.

\subsection{What the 193-state experiments establish}

The stored coefficient-three shortcut is logically inconsistent with its
unchanged supplied heads. The Runtime193 family can satisfy clean-source
contracts and still fail blank initialization. Earlier reports certify
10,036 starts of 52 physical runtime-experiment graphs as 106 finite halts
and 9,930 outward rays, and another fixed-splice certificate covers all
$9!$ physical register allocations. These are attributed restricted
obstructions, not a lower bound of 194 for arbitrary consistency machines.
The verified even-only candidate scheduler currently costs 195 states;
the even-runtime194 graft is a rejected diagnostic. Fresh finite search
results in this report are stated with their own scopes and are not
inflated into universal impossibility claims.
