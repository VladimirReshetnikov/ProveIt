\paragraph{Other mathematical routes.}
The ZFC corpus contributes useful command-local readers, partial
selectors, contextual folds and initialization certificates.  Its K215
table has 215 working states and is calibrated to ZFC refutation;
neither that target nor its external correctness theorem supplies the
requested iPRA equivalence with PA consistency.  A borrowed fold must
also be rechecked in the PA source contexts and from its blank startup
(\path{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/README.md}).  The
Diophantine route has a different accounting obstacle: the 75-operation
circuit has four fixed coefficient ports, so its arithmetic-operation
count does not include a blank-tape binary enumerator, evaluator or
coefficient construction.  The annotated logical scanner likewise
has 23,017 optimized scalar-code nodes, a source size rather than a
binary-state bound, and still requires a PA-specific proof-checker
calibration (\path{Computability/DiophantineConsistency/SHORT_SYSTEM.md}
and \path{ANNOTATED_LINE_SCANNER.md} in that directory).
ConciseIPRA shows how to derive expensive primitive rules, but enumerating
refutations of its concise iPRA calculus targets $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{iPRA})$;
no inspected translation identifies that sentence with
$\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})$ in iPRA.  Adding PA-strength
axioms would require their recognition and proof translations to be
priced and proved anew
(\path{Logic/PrimitiveRecursiveArithmetic/ConciseIPRA/README.md}).
These are specific missing construction and calibration obligations;
they are not impossibility results for the three approaches.
