\begin{abstract}
The state-count target below 194 was not achieved. The delivered fallback
consists of two explicit, complete, binary, one-tape, blank-start machines,
PA194Q5 and PA194Q6, each with 194 working states and 388 transition rows.
They combine the exchanged quantifier-distribution head with formula
coefficients five and six, respectively, while retaining the original
sum-testing Shift and dummy-push modus-ponens controller. A simultaneous
eight-fold quotient has budget $135+67-8=194$. Both tables pass complete
source-step contracts and exact whole-tape initialization. A separate
literal-tape implementation and a finite implicational proof certificate
are supplied.

The comparison is pinned to ProveIt commit
\nolinkurl{317b07268681b44ff352d173a162e2a8c241b2fe}. It covers 51 placed
research packets and three incoming archives, including explicit JSON
machine encodings. Forty existing 194-state graph classes were identified.
Neither new table is equivalent to any of them under state renaming,
choice of spanning root, tape reflection, symbol complementation, or
disregard of the final write and move. The ordinary mathematical argument
establishes the PA-provable consistency equivalence using the attributed
radix-calibration and source-compiler lemmas. No new end-to-end Lean, Rocq,
or iPRA equivalence theorem is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result and exact scope}

The requested improvement would have been a verified machine with at most
193 working states. None was obtained. The positive result is the following
pair of new constructions, using the permitted 194-state fallback.

\begin{theorem}[Ordinary mathematical calibration]
For $k\in\{5,6\}$, let $M_k$ be the literal table
\file{machines/PA194Qk/machine.tm}, with the letter \texttt{k} replaced by
the indicated digit. Then
\[
 \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg\Halt(M_k,\text{blank})\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
\]
The proof uses the inherited ordinary PA calibration stated in
Section~\ref{sec:logic}, the explicit source/compiler transport below, and
the delivered finite and symbolic physical certificates.
\end{theorem}

Here $\Con(\PA)$ is the usual arithmetical assertion that there is no
finite PA proof of $0=1$ under a fixed effective proof coding. The theorem
is a statement about what PA proves, not an assumption that PA is sound or
consistent. Its proof does not use a PA reflection principle.

Each machine starts in state 0 with its head at cell 0 on a two-way
infinite all-zero tape. The alphabet is $\{0,1\}$. Working states are
$0,\ldots,193$; 194 is the separate halt label and has no rows. Every
transition writes one bit and moves exactly one cell left or right.
There are no stationary moves, preloaded input, auxiliary tapes, or
uncounted working states. All 194 working states are reachable in the
transition graph; this last fact is a graph property, not a claim that
each is visited by the particular blank computation.

\subsection{Novelty of the delivered tables}

The comparator checks complete canonical transition tuples, not merely
hashes. For every existing 194-state table it tries all graph-spanning
roots and both choices of tape reflection and bit complementation,
canonically renaming the controls. It also ignores the final write and
move into the halt label. A transformed positive control verifies that
renaming, reflection, and complementation are actually recognized.
Both new tables have no match. This establishes the stated graph novelty;
it does not establish semantic inequivalence, which would be contrary to
the intended common halting condition.

The mathematical difference is explicit. M5 and M6 use the original
quantifier-distribution head; the new machines use its premise-exchanged
form. Existing Q and QN use coefficient four. PA194Q5 and PA194Q6 change
the numerical constructors throughout the source and change the actual
emitted axiom in its original stack slot. Their derivation languages and
literal transition graphs therefore change, rather than just the names
of the controls.

\section{The revised numerical calculus}
\label{sec:logic}

Let $p(a,b)=\tfrac12(a+b)(a+b+1)+b$ be Cantor pairing. For $k=5,6$ set
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
I_k(A,B)=kp(A,B),&M_k(a,b)=kp(a,b)+1,\\
U_k(a,B)=kp(B,a)+2,&E_k(a,b)=kp(a,b)+3.
\end{array}
\]
Zero denotes truth and $1=M_k(0,0)$ denotes contradiction. Negation is
$N_k(B)=I_k(B,1)$; conjunction and existential quantification use their
classical implicational definitions. The reversal of the arguments in
$U_k$ is part of the construction. Unused residues are interpreted as
truth in the arithmetic soundness translation.

Let $\mathcal C_k$ be the inherited separate-diagonal calculus, with its
six ordinary heads, the dominated implicational head, the diagonal schema
$\Gamma_k(B)=I_k(B,U_k(B,B))$, guarded pointed separation, modus ponens,
and generalization. Let $\mathcal C_k^Q$ change only
\begin{align*}
h_{6,k}(D,A,H)
 &= U_k(D,A)\to\bigl(U_k(D,A\to H)\to U_k(D,H)\bigr)\\
\text{to }h_{6,k}^{Q}(D,A,H)
 &= U_k(D,A\to H)\to\bigl(U_k(D,A)\to U_k(D,H)\bigr).
\end{align*}
All arrows here are $I_k$. The parameter guard, other formulas, and
positions in the emitted stack remain the same.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform premise exchange]
There are primitive-recursive proof translations between
$\mathcal C_k$ and $\mathcal C_k^Q$ preserving the literal conclusion.
PA verifies their correctness.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the finite implicational theorem
\[
 (P\to(Q\to R))\to(Q\to(P\to R)).
\]
The delivered certificate derives it from K, S, and modus ponens, without
quantifier, negation, or falsity rules. Substituting
$P=U_k(D,A)$, $Q=U_k(D,A\to H)$, and $R=U_k(D,H)$ gives one direction;
interchanging $P$ and $Q$ gives the other. Replace each affected axiom
leaf by this fixed derivation and leave all other derivation constructors
unchanged. Induction on the derivation proves the translation claim.

K and S are uniformly derivable from the unchanged restricted
implicational basis. Its proof requires reflexivity of numerical
comparison and the two operand bounds
$a,b\leq I_k(a,b)$. These follow from $a,b\leq p(a,b)$ for every $k\geq1$.
Consequently the same proof applies at five and six; one must not import
only a coefficient-four conclusion without this check. Numerical aliases
such as $I_k(0,0)=0$ cause no problem: the certificate is instantiated as
a homomorphism of implication terms and uses no syntactic injectivity.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Why coefficient recoding preserves PA calibration}

For completeness, the relevant inherited radix argument is stated here.
Define $F_k(0)=0$, and for $n=4p(a,b)+r>0$ put
\[
 F_k(n)=kp(F_k(a),F_k(b))+r\qquad(0\leq r<4).
\]
Both recursive arguments are below $n$, so this is a primitive-recursive
course-of-values definition. A primitive-recursive left inverse, decoding
the first four residues and sending unused residues to zero, proves that
$F_k$ is injective. It fixes 0 and 1 and commutes with all four constructors,
\emph{including the variable labels}. In particular,
\[
 F_k(\Gamma_4(B))=\Gamma_k(F_k(B)).
\]
Recoding formulas while leaving their numerical variable labels unchanged
would fail at this diagonal schema.

There is a second obstacle: $F_k$ need not preserve order. For example,
$40<44$ while $F_5(40)=75>55=F_5(44)$. The restricted propositional head
has an inequality side condition, so a one-leaf translation is invalid.
The inherited \texttt{rho8All} proof first derives the unrestricted head
from the restricted one using only the operand bounds just stated.
Each translated restricted leaf is replaced by that finite template.
This produces a PA-verifiable translation of coefficient-four refutations
into $\mathcal C_k$ refutations.

For the converse, interpret membership by finite binary membership,
equality by equality, and $U_k$ by first-order universal quantification.
The coding inequality proves that every variable label in a nonzero
formula is smaller than its code. This supplies the diagonal axiom.
PA's full induction proves finite binary comprehension for each arithmetic
formula, supplying the pointed-separation instances. A fixed syntactic
template produces the PA proof of each translated axiom. It handles the
zero alias, unused residues, and coincident-label case separately.
Thus a finite numerical refutation produces a finite PA refutation.

These are proof-producing transformations, not an assertion of a uniform
truth predicate. The supplied ordinary calibration consequently gives
\[
\PA\vdash
\bigl(\exists d\,\Prf_{\mathcal C_k}(d,1)\bigr)
\leftrightarrow
\bigl(\exists p\,\Prf_{\PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner)\bigr).
\]
The exchange lemma replaces $\mathcal C_k$ by $\mathcal C_k^Q$ in this
equivalence. Full attributed manuscripts and the relevant generic Lean
proof template are retained in \file{calibration/}; their status is
ordinary inherited calibration, not a newly compiled endpoint.

The constructor changes each of 61 distinct four-increment multiplication

\subsection{The controller retained here}

The new machines use the \emph{original} controller, whose Shift halts
when $t+h=1$ and whose successful MP retains a dummy zero on the encoded
stack. The stack is Cantor encoded: $[a]w=p(a,w)$, with
$p(0,0)=0$ explicitly allowed. This controller choice fixes the proof
compiler; cached-controller command words cannot be substituted for it.

Writing stores as $(t,w,d,h)$, the ten-entry AX interface has the same
safe extraction positions as the original separate-head construction.
The following words and equations remain valid for $k=5,6$ and the Q head:
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{pop}&=\AX(0,0,0)\Shift^{11},&
\mathrm{pop}(t,[a]w,d,h)&=(t,w,0,a),\\
\mathrm{push}&=\AX(0,0,0)\Shift^9\MP(0),&
\mathrm{push}(t,w,d,h)&=(0,[t]w,0,0),\\
\mathrm{discard}&=\MP(1)\AX(1,0,0)\Shift^{10}\MP(0),&
\mathrm{discard}(t,w,0,0)&=(0,w,0,0).
\end{align*}
They also hold for $t=1$: intermediate Shift tests in these words are
protected by constructor residues and positivity. All six ordinary
heads and $\Gamma_k$ are implication, quantifier, or existential codes;
none is the contradiction code 1.

Let $q_1,\ldots,q_{10}$ be AX's saved entries in stack-top order:
\[
\Gamma_k(A),\ h_{6,k}^Q,\ h_5,\ h_4,\ h_3,\ h_2,\ h_1,
\ \rho_{8,k},\ U_k(d,h),\ t.
\]
For $1\leq j\leq7$, the word
\[
\mathrm{discard}\,\AX(A,D,H)\Shift^{j+1}\mathrm{pop}^{9-j}
\]
selects $q_j$ while preserving the protected stack and clearing both
caches. The Q head is selected at $j=2$, exactly the old sixth-head slot.
Dominated-head and guarded-gate selectors are inherited unchanged.

Generalization uses the inherited offset word and
$\mathrm{push}\,\operatorname{offset}(i)\Shift\AX(i,0,0)\Shift^{10}$.
For MP the correct suffix is
\[
 \mathrm{suffix}_b=\Shift\Shift\MP(b)\AX(b,0,0)\Shift^{11}.
\]
On $(0,[a][I_k(a,b)]w,0,0)$, it halts at the second Shift if $a=1$.
Otherwise MP yields $(b,[0]w,b,0)$, and the remaining AX--Shift suffix
returns $(b,w,0,0)$. This explicitly removes the original controller's
dummy zero. It is the point at which a cached-controller proof would give
the wrong command sequence.

Induction on a numerical derivation compiles it into a finite word
$C(d)$ which, from every $(t,w,0,0)$, either halts or returns its conclusion
with the protected $w$ and zero caches. A refutation gives the universally
halting word $W_d=\AX(0,0,0)C(d)\Shift$. No reset of the actual store
between candidate command words is assumed.

\subsection{Enumeration and the reverse implication}

The original command scheduler uses continuation-pointer encodings
\begin{align*}
e([])&=0,&e(\Shift::v)&=1+p(0,e(v)),\\
e(\MP(x)::v)&=1+p(1,p(x,e(v))),&
e(\AX(c,x,y)::v)&=1+p(c+2,p(y,p(x,e(v)))).
\end{align*}
Every positive residual pointer decreases under decoding. Thus each
candidate finishes in a bounded number of commands or halts, and fresh
candidates enumerate $2,3,4,\ldots$ from the verified initial seed.
The candidate coding of $W_d$ lies in that enumeration. Bounded induction
in PA shows that it is reached or an earlier halt has occurred.

For soundness, annotate $t$, $h$, and the live \emph{canonical} stack
entries with PA proofs of their arithmetic translations. Popping zero
supplies the truth proof; pushing a zero onto zero adds no canonical
cell. AX, successful or failed MP, and nonhalting Shift preserve the
annotation. If $t+h=1$, one of the two annotated formulas is code 1;
its translation is refutable in PA, yielding a PA contradiction.
These syntactic annotation operations and their correctness induction
are primitive recursive and PA-verifiable.

Consequently the source halts if and only if there is a PA refutation,
provably in PA. Section~\ref{sec:physical} connects that source to the
literal 194-state tables.

\subsection{Why the folds preserve executions}

Let $\alpha$ identify each deleted control $a$ with its retained partner
$b$. Each arrival at $a$ has just moved right leaving a 0 behind; each
arrival at $b$ has left a 1. That predecessor bit distinguishes the two
old roles. For each pair, the checker considers both roles and both
current read bits, verifies all incoming transitions, and executes the
\emph{final simultaneously folded table}. It restores the two-cell tape
and reaches exactly the old transition's projected endpoint in a positive
number of moves. None of the eight folds touches the start or halt.

All other transitions commute directly with $\alpha$. Induction on
unfolded steps, concatenating these local positive traces, proves the
forward simulation. Determinism and the absence of internal halts in a
replacement trace give the finite-halting equivalence from admissible
starts. Checking folds one at a time against different intermediate
tables would not establish this simultaneous result.

\subsection{Primitive contracts and initialization}

A source frame contains a source path, an arbitrary affine zero gap,
the nine natural-valued registers in the stated physical order, and an
arbitrary finite nonempty spare list. The header parameter is 48; the
maximum path length here is 35. Each increment, both branches of every
decrement, every jump, and the halt leaf is checked at its resolved source
address. The two accepted tables leave all inherited arithmetic-box
transition rows unchanged.

The construction checker passes 4,841 and 4,902 universal symbolic
contracts, respectively. The totals 1,546,445 and 1,571,284 in its receipts
count executor operations with symbolic loop/box summaries; they are
\emph{not} running-time bounds on arbitrary natural-valued registers.
A second implementation parses the source independently, resolves its
control flow by two methods, and checks literal-tape behavior using list
lemmas rederived from the actual transition rows.

The blank-start check is an exact finite computation of 58,320 moves.
It compares the entire set of nonzero cells, the native control 84, and

The arithmetic-loop lemmas are proved by induction on unary lengths and

and trace concatenation are primitive recursive. PA can formalize these
inductions and the candidate-specific finite checks. Every primitive
simulation has positive physical duration. Under the annotated-source

other direction, a compiled source refutation yields a finite literal

\section{Unsuccessful reductions and the useful remaining obstacles}

The bounded searches in this session are recorded in
\file{search/summary.json}. They explore premise exchanges in the third,
fourth, and sixth ordinary heads, alone and in combinations, and decoder
reassociation with the changed radix controller and the extra 41/99
fold. Their stopping conditions are finite time budgets. Their best
allocation scores did not yield a verified table below 194. These
observations imply no lower bound on the number of states.
Ten complete allocation runs performed 14,425,456 mutation iterations;
the four coefficient-five/six runs retained best projected state count
194. The frozen accepted constructions do not depend on repeating a
stochastic search.

One source experiment moves the contradiction test from Shift to the MP
operation which first creates code 1. The first-production argument is
mathematically plausible because AX and generalization do not first
produce 1. An adapted ZFC context allocator reproduced the old 194-state
positive control, but its changed 208-state compilation failed a primitive
contract. That table is explicitly rejected. Decoder reuse must respect
the interpreter's later passes, not just its first arrival at a source
leaf.

An additional physical investigation uses the existing PA194QN table.
It derives 3,236 must-zero facts, 5,924 must-positive facts, and 750
nontrivial modulo-four facts at source sites. The exact blank startup
also supplies the invariant that every spare register equals one.
Both completed collectors cover all 4,755 primitive outcomes and all
194 controls. These stronger invariants reduce the radius-five context
set from 29,479 to 24,388 windows, removing 5,091 and adding none.

Both context sets were screened for common-action state mergers,
per-incoming-edge bypasses, and a new predecessor-history-marking fold.
The latter lets predecessors overwrite their cells to distinguish two
roles, requiring the detour to restore the complete local tape. It tests
14,401 independent control pairs and 22,148,352 candidate first-action
trials per context set. All six screens completed at a 300-step detour
bound and returned zero candidates. The supplied inputs and receipts
bind these negative results to QN, not to Q5 or Q6. They exclude only
the stated finite families, not arbitrary smaller machines.

A guard-free logical proposal was also reviewed. Taking a separator
label $z=M_4(D,I_4(H,A))$ guarantees $z>D,H,A$, including the all-zero
case. This establishes arithmetic soundness of the suitably split
separator/property gate. It does not supply the arbitrary-label
membership-vacuity instances required by the present completeness proof.
Using full variable-renaming rules at that point would assume an
interface which the small calculus has not yet derived. The proposal
therefore remains a proof obligation, not an accepted consistency
construction.

The ZFC material contributes reusable decoder allocation and local
simulation methods, but its consistency strength cannot replace PA's.
Similarly, replacing the source by a small iPRA checker would search for
iPRA refutations. A route through intuitionistic arithmetic would need
full induction, a syntactic PA/HA refutation translation, and its own
complete machine compilation.

\section{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Can the Q/radix decoder use 66 AX readers with eight compatible
folds, or 67 readers with nine, while retaining the same 135 fixed
controls? Either would give 193 states. All folds must be checked in the
final table, including the fixed-controller pair.
\item Can the proven source invariants and one-valued spare-list language
justify a physical state elimination outside the completed two-control
screens? A positive answer needs an invariant closed under the actual
blank-start computation, not a finite simulation sample.
\item Can contradiction testing be moved to successful MP using a
compiler that preserves the interpreter's multi-pass source-prefix
invariants and reconstructs a valid blank seed?
\item Can higher-order predecessor histories share three or more reader
roles with a smaller compatible quotient, avoiding the limitations of
disjoint two-role folds?
\item Can a uniformly fresh separator construction recover the
arbitrary-label vacuity interface without presupposing full
first-order renaming? Resolving that logical obstruction could make
guard removal a valid source-size reduction.
\item Can the ordinary PA calibration and these explicit physical
certificates be internalized together in Lean/Rocq, and then at the
repository's stronger iPRA target? This is independent of whether the
literal state count drops below 194.
\end{enumerate}
