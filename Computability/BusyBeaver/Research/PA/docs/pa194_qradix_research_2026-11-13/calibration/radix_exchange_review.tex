\section{Verdict and proof boundary}
The quantifier-premise exchange and radix-five/radix-six formula coding
are compatible. For every fixed $k\ge4$, the exchanged numerical calculus
$\C_k^{\mathrm Q}$ and the ordinary radix-$k$ numerical calculus $\C_k$
have primitive-recursive proof translations preserving the literal
conclusion code. PA proves the translations correct. Combining these
translations with the supplied radix calibration gives PA equivalence of
their refutation predicates with the PA refutation predicate.

The concrete Q5 and Q6 source programs retain the original controller:
Shift tests $t+h=1$, and successful MP pushes zero and retains the result
in $t$ and $d$. Their command compiler must therefore be the original
ten-entry compiler. The cached-controller command section in the supplied
PA194Q report cannot be imported verbatim. Exact appropriate identities
are provided below.

This is an ordinary mathematical review and a finite symbolic certificate
replay. No new Lean kernel execution or machine-specific end-to-end
Lean theorem is claimed. The concrete physical implementation additionally
depends on the candidate-specific source bindings, universal primitive
contracts, and exact blank startup supplied with the construction.

\section{A radix-independent exchange derivation}
Write
\[
 p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b,\qquad
 I_k(a,b)=kp(a,b),\qquad U_k(d,a)=kp(a,d)+2.
\]
All arrows below mean $I_k$. Put
\[
 P=U_k(D,A),\qquad Q=U_k(D,I_k(A,H)),\qquad R=U_k(D,H).
\]
The old sixth head is $P\to(Q\to R)$; the Q head is
$Q\to(P\to R)$. All other axioms and rules remain unchanged.

The generic source \path{M6/Logic/RestrictedImplication.lean} defines
\texttt{Rules} using only modus ponens, the restricted row
\[
 ((c\to u)\to a)\to((a\to c)\to(b\to c))\qquad(u\preceq a),
\]
reflexivity of $\preceq$, and the two rules that a bound by either
operand gives a bound by their implication. Its finite proofs derive
weakening K, distribution S, and
\[
 E(X,Y,Z)=(X\to(Y\to Z))\to(Y\to(X\to Z)).       \tag{1}
\]
The declaration \texttt{exchange} uses only earlier finite proofs and MP;
it does not use the changed quantifier head. The later declaration
\texttt{s} is obtained from \texttt{exchange}, so there is no dependence
of that derivation on an assumed distribution head.

Instantiate $\preceq$ by numerical $\le$ and implication by $I_k$.
Cantor pairing gives
\[
 a,b\le p(a,b)\le kp(a,b)=I_k(a,b)\qquad(k\ge1).
\]
These bounds and transitivity discharge every generic side condition.
Thus the same fixed proof constructor derives (1) at every radix in
question. The independent 61-line archived K/S/MP certificate is a second
explicit exchange derivation; its flat replay is recorded separately.
K and S in that replay have the preceding restricted-basis derivation as
their stated dependency.

Apply MP to the new head and $E(Q,P,R)$ to derive the old head. Apply
MP to the old head and $E(P,Q,R)$ for the converse. Recursion on a
derivation replaces each sixth-head leaf by the appropriate two-part
derivation, retains every other axiom, and translates MP and
generalization recursively. Hence there are primitive-recursive maps
$F_k,G_k$ such that
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \PA&\vdash
 \operatorname{Prf}_{\C_k}(d,B)
    \to\operatorname{Prf}_{\C_k^{\mathrm Q}}(F_k(d),B),\\
 \PA&\vdash
 \operatorname{Prf}_{\C_k^{\mathrm Q}}(d,B)
    \to\operatorname{Prf}_{\C_k}(G_k(d),B).
\end{aligned}                                                    \tag{2}
\]
No first-order substitution into a freshness-restricted gate is involved.
The proof works on numerical codes, including $I_k(0,0)=0$ and formula
parameters in unused residues. It does not require free constructors or
an injective representation of arbitrary implication trees.

\section{How the PA calibration composes}
The supplied radix calibration uses hereditary recoding of both formula
codes and variable labels. For $n>0$, if $n=4p(a,b)+r$ with $r<4$, it sets
\[
 H_k(0)=0,\qquad H_k(n)=kp(H_k(a),H_k(b))+r.
\]
The recursive arguments are smaller than $n$; course-of-values recursion
is primitive recursive. A left inverse gives injectivity, so guards based
on equality or disequality and quantifier binding are preserved. In
particular the diagonal head $B\to U(B,B)$ is recoded correctly because
the same function is applied to the formula code and its binder label.

Numerical order is \emph{not} preserved: $40<44$ but
$H_5(40)=75>55=H_5(44)$. Consequently dominated propositional leaves
must be replaced by the generic \texttt{rho8All} derivation, whose own
restricted leaves satisfy valid bounds. They must not be recoded as
single restricted leaves by assuming monotonicity.

The converse calibration translates every radix-$k$ numerical formula
syntactically into PA. Residues $4,\ldots,k-1$ are interpreted as closed
truth. Membership is the finite-bit predicate; $1=M_k(0,0)$ is refutable;
the zero implication alias is handled by a fixed proof of truth's
equivalence with truth implying truth. The guarded separation gate uses
one finite-comprehension PA induction instance constructed from the input
property. This is a uniform syntactic proof supplier, not an arithmetical
truth predicate or a reflection principle.

The two supplied radix proof translations, together with (2), therefore
give
\[
 \PA\vdash
 \left(\exists d\,\operatorname{Prf}_{\C_k^{\mathrm Q}}(d,1)
 \ \leftrightarrow\
 \exists e\,\operatorname{Prf}_{\PA}(e,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner)\right).
                                                               \tag{3}
\]
One must compile a fresh target-radix command word from the translated
proof. Hereditary recoding preserves neither the Cantor stack arithmetic
nor addition of command offsets, and does not conjugate machine runs.

\section{The correct original-controller command identities}
Let $[a]w=p(a,w)$, with total projections
$\operatorname{hd}(0)=\operatorname{tl}(0)=0$. Write stores as
$(t,w,d,h)$. In Q5 and Q6,
\[
 \Sh(t,w,d,h)=
 \begin{cases}
  \mathsf{halt},&t+h=1,\\
  (h,\operatorname{tl}(w),d,\operatorname{hd}(w)),&t+h\ne1,
 \end{cases}
\]
and, with $v=d+x$,
\[
 \MP(x)(t,w,d,h)=
 \begin{cases}
   (v,[0]w,v,0),&I_k(t,v)=h,\\
   (0,w,v,0),&I_k(t,v)\ne h.
 \end{cases}                                                    \tag{4}
\]
For $D=d+x,H=h+y,A=c$, the AX command pushes, from first to last,
\[
 t,\ U_k(d,h),\ \rho_{8,k}(D,A,H,d),\ h_1,h_2,h_3,h_4,h_5,
 h_6^{\mathrm Q},\Gamma_k(A),
\]
leaves the usual guarded gate $G_k(H,D,A)$ on top, and clears $d,h$.
Let $q_1,\ldots,q_{10}$ be these entries in reverse order. The gate
and $q_1,\ldots,q_8$ have residue zero modulo $k$;
$q_9=U_k(d,h)$ has residue two and is at least two; $q_{10}=t$.
The internal Q exchange changes only $q_2$ and preserves these facts.
Thus
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \AX(c,x,y)\Sh^9(t,w,d,h)
   &=(\rho_{8,k}(D,c,H,d),[t]w,0,U_k(d,h)),\\
 \AX(c,x,y)\Sh^{10}(t,w,d,h)&=(U_k(d,h),w,0,t).
\end{aligned}                                                    \tag{5}
\]
Every tested sum differs from one. Define
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{pop}&=\AX(0,0,0)\Sh^{11},\\
 \mathrm{push}&=\AX(0,0,0)\Sh^9\MP(0),\\
 \mathrm{discard}&=\MP(1)\AX(1,0,0)\Sh^{10}\MP(0).
\end{aligned}
\]
Their identities, for arbitrary natural parameters, are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{pop}(t,[a]w,d,h)&=(t,w,0,a),\\
 \mathrm{push}(t,w,d,h)&=(0,[t]w,0,0),\\
 \mathrm{discard}(t,w,0,0)&=(0,w,0,0).
\end{aligned}                                                    \tag{6}
\]
Pop never halts even when $t=1$: its last test is
$U_k(d,h)+t\ge2$. Push's MP comparison fails by the implication and
universal residues. Discard's comparisons fail because $I_k(t,1)>0$
and $I_k(U_k(1,0),0)>0$.

For $1\le j\le7$, the selector
\[
 \mathrm{discard}\,\AX(A,D,H)\Sh^{j+1}\mathrm{pop}^{9-j}
\]
returns $(q_j,w,0,0)$ from $(t,w,0,0)$. In particular, $j=2$ selects
the exchanged sixth head. Write $\mathrm{offset}(0)$ for the empty
word and $\mathrm{offset}(i)=\MP(i)$ for $i>0$. Then generalization is
\[
 \mathrm{gen}_i=\mathrm{push}\,\mathrm{offset}(i)\Sh
                   \AX(i,0,0)\Sh^{10}.
\]
It sends $(a,w,0,0)$ to $(U_k(i,a),w,0,0)$ even if $a=1$.
The dominated head selector for $u\le D$ is
\[
 \mathrm{discard}\,\mathrm{offset}(u)\AX(A,D-u,H)\Sh^9\mathrm{pop},
\]
and the gate selector is
$\mathrm{discard}\,\AX(c,b,a)\mathrm{pop}^{10}$.

The correct MP suffix is
\[
 \mathrm{suffix}_b=\Sh\Sh\MP(b)\AX(b,0,0)\Sh^{11}.              \tag{7}
\]
On $(0,[a][I_k(a,b)]w,0,0)$ the second Shift halts if $a=1$.
Otherwise MP succeeds and yields $(b,[0]w,b,0)$; the remaining
AX--Shift word returns $(b,w,0,0)$. This calculation also covers
$a=b=w=0$ and $[0]0=0$. A cached-controller suffix
$\Sh\Sh\MP(b)\Sh$ has a different effect under (4) and must not be used.

Compile a proof node with premises $p:a$ and $q:I_k(a,b)$ as
\[
 C(q)\,\mathrm{push}\,C(p)\,\mathrm{push}\,\mathrm{suffix}_b.
\]
Together with the selectors and generalization identity, induction
proves that $C(d)$ either halts or returns its conclusion on the original
arbitrary stack, with both caches zero. Hence a refutation supplies
\[
 W_d=\AX(0,0,0)\,C(d)\,\Sh,
\]
which halts from \emph{every} incoming store. This is the property
needed by the scheduler, which carries the store between trials rather
than resetting it. All assertions in this section use residue classes,
positivity, Cantor projection identities, and finite induction; their
proofs are uniform for $k\ge4$.

\section{Binding to the concrete constructions}
The only changed arithmetic body of Cons replaces four increments by
$k$ increments. Its decrement loop sends $(T,Y)$ to $(T+k,Y-1)$ while
$Y>0$, so after $i$ iterations
\[
 T=T_0+ki,\qquad Y=Y_0-i.
\]
It terminates in exactly $(k+2)Y_0+1$ primitive source steps. Together
with the unchanged unpair/pair prefix, this yields
\[
 (t,[a]w,0,0)\longmapsto(I_k(a,t),w,0,0)
\]
on coordinates $(T,W,X,Y)$, preserving the remaining registers.
This is a new multiplier-loop induction, not an invocation of the
old times-four lemma as if its parameter had already been generalized.
The candidate-specific binding checks identify all 61 Cons occurrences,
their exact 21-site prefixes, all changed increment bodies, and the
successor relocation for all 3429 other sites. Formula construction
inside MP uses the same Cons routine; ordinary stack and scheduler
pairing retain their unmultiplied Cantor coding.

The independent free-algebra emitter audit must identify the exact
ten-element stack and guarded top above, not merely compare a few
numerical examples. Universal primitive simulation contracts and the
complete blank-tape startup then bind those source semantics to the
literal binary tables. Their positive durations are essential: under
consistency one iterates nonhalting command execution past any proposed
finite machine halt, using determinism; conversely the scheduler reaches
the all-store halting word and physical simulation yields a finite halt.
Composing this with (3) gives the desired ordinary PA-provability claim
for each certified concrete table.
