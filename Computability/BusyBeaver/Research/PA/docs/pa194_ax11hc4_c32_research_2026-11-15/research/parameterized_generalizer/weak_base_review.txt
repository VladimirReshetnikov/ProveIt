The file gives a substantive mathematical construction, not merely a
statement that ordinary PA proves the desired interpretation. It aims
to construct a primitive-recursive ordinary-PAeq-to-C4 refutation map
with correctness already in I Sigma_1, so that the negative consistency
endpoint can subsequently be transferred to iPRA using the cited
weak-base theorem.

Its important device is a finite-demand request/response tree. A node's
child requests are computed from syntax before any returned child
proofs are available. Within each stratum, recursive ranks strictly
decrease. Calls to completed lower strata may have larger arguments.
The construction first fills the finite query tree, then evaluates
proof-producing responses in reverse order. Correctness is expressed
by bounded Boolean invariants about this finite table, avoiding an
induction hypothesis of the form 'for every environment there exists a
proof'. As an algorithmic and weak-arithmetic method, this is sound.

I checked the displayed coverage budgets F4--F7 and their relocation
argument: m=max(l,l')+1 and a child budget q <= p-1 imply the stated
m+q <= z bound. The file explicitly protects the prefix of each extended
environment and takes freshness above all used variables.

I also read the arbitrary-property separation construction, requested
label maps F9--F12, the arithmetic formula/term interfaces, the induction
supplier F13--F15, and the outer proof compiler F16. The stratification
is explicit: formula realization may call already completed term
realization, and arbitrary induction is supplied by the seven stated
ordinal-induction premises rather than by an unexplained base case for
arbitrary closed matrices. Recursive children of the outer proof
compiler are smaller proof codes.

Several older, stronger-PA calibration theorems are cited as sources of
finite constructor recipes. The text explicitly instructs the reader
to implement those recipes directly, rather than importing their
stronger-theory correctness conclusions. That distinction is material
to the weak-base claim and is addressed in the written proof.

I found no concrete contradiction, rank failure, or coverage-budget
error in these parts. This review does not independently evaluate all
large native proof objects or verify every join between the many
templates. The correct status is an inherited written calibration with
unfinished kernel formalization, not an absence of an iPRA argument.
The additional source experiment separately proves its command
interface change by an exact offset-addition identity.
