\section{The arithmetic base: the updated written calibration}
\label{sec:reviewed-arithmetic-base}

There are two different historical statements in the inspected material.
The maintained PA README at commit
\nolinkurl{793c68737d94bffa4e32a75bce6cc8058d6d22f0}
records that the end-to-end machine-specific Lean proof in iPRA remains
open. The older PA194 manuscript proves its calibration in PA.
The updated incoming AX11H manuscript makes a newer, stronger claim:
an ordinary written proof in iPRA for its exact AX11H and QAX11H tables.
Its source snapshot is
\nolinkurl{1c9524d8bf2e0a27b9a16ff622359927f536e663}; the updated archive
was present at commit
\nolinkurl{1e1a41f799d4658b829cdd0eabae3775a0c8c4a0}, with SHA-256
\nolinkurl{93ee082ef035b2d32e71d6af5239b25b4bea1773f03bea4d2c39810a7fe15075}.
Copies of its ten written proof files are preserved in the incoming GD
packet under \texttt{references/AX11H\_written\_sources} and in the
incoming SF packet under \texttt{references/ax11h\_written\_ipra}.
These statements are compatible: a written mathematical proof and a
completed proof-assistant derivation have different verification status.

\subsection{What changes relative to the older PA proof}

The weakness of the old inference is substantive. The upstream
\texttt{ordinary\_derivation\_requested} and
\texttt{fuel\_invariant} proofs use full-PA induction on statements
quantifying over environments or proof-table bounds. Their old theorem
statements cannot simply be weakened to $I\Sigma_1$.
The updated manuscript instead constructs explicit proof lists in the
numerical calculus. Each recursive request consists of a syntax rank and
an environment packet. Its children are computed from that packet and
the parent syntax before any child proof is returned. Same-family ranks
strictly decrease. A complete binary request tree of depth $r+1$, with
inactive placeholders, has at most $2^{r+2}-1$ slots. Computing it forwards
and evaluating proof constructors backwards are primitive recursive.

Correctness uses two finite Boolean invariants: generated children of
valid requests satisfy their validity and coverage tests; evaluated valid
slots contain proofs of their specified conclusions. The indices of
every conjunction are bounded by the finite tree. With primitive recursive
characteristic functions these invariants are quantifier free. In the
arithmetic-language presentation they have $\Sigma_1$ witness-table forms.
This is an appropriate way to replace the older unbounded induction.
It would fail if a child rank depended on a returned child proof or if
a higher proof supplier were called recursively without a decreasing rank;
the manuscript explicitly orders the relevant supplier strata to avoid
these cases.

The inspected order is: syntactic operations and finite logical recipes;
relational translation and relocation; relational LK and fixed templates;
arithmetic term syntax, totality, coherence and uniqueness; vector and
formula realization; the induction-axiom supplier; and finally the
ordinary arithmetic proof compiler. A replacement term can be larger than
its original variable code, but its uniqueness proof is a call to an
already completed lower stratum. The Foundation adapter separately
constructs opening and closing proof chains and extracts induction-schema
witnesses by explicitly bounded search. Thus the claim does not identify
the historical ordinary-PAeq proof predicate with the current Foundation
predicate without a translation.

The maintained $I\Sigma_1$-to-iPRA transfer then supplies the negative
endpoint. Its relevant hypothesis is
\[
 I\Sigma_1\vdash\bigl(\operatorname{Ref}_{\rm PA}
                  \leftrightarrow H_M\bigr),
\]
with $H_M$ a $\Sigma_1$ halting sentence and the pinned PA witness
certificate. It yields
\[
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash
   \bigl(\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})\leftrightarrow\neg H_M\bigr).
\]
This is a conservation and negative-translation argument, not an inference
from a standard-model equivalence and not an application of Markov's
principle.

\subsection{A reusable exact-clock transfer lemma}

The physical part admits a short independent argument worth making
explicit. Represent halt by an absorbing code $\mathsf H$ in both
machines. Let $F$ be a primitive recursive source step, $E$ an encoding,
$D$ an exact duration, and $\operatorname{Run}$ the primitive recursive
physical run. Assume that iPRA proves, on a primitive recursive invariant,
\[
 \operatorname{Run}(D(z),E(z))=E(F(z)),\qquad
 D(z)\ge1\quad(z\ne\mathsf H),
\]
that $F$ preserves the invariant, and that $E(z)=\mathsf H$ exactly when
$z=\mathsf H$. Set $D(\mathsf H)=0$. Suppose a fixed nonhalting startup
reaches $E(z_0)$ at time $T_0$.
Define
\[
 z_k=F^k(z_0),\qquad \tau_0=0,\qquad
 \tau_{k+1}=\tau_k+D(z_k).
\]
Quantifier-free induction proves
\[
 \operatorname{Run}(T_0+\tau_k,c_{\rm blank})=E(z_k),
 \qquad z_k\ne\mathsf H\Longrightarrow\tau_k\ge k.
\]
If the source has halted by $k$, the first equality gives a physical
halting witness $T_0+\tau_k$. Conversely, if the physical machine has
halted at $t$, take $k=t+1$. If $z_k\ne\mathsf H$, the boundary time
$T_0+\tau_k$ lies after $t$, so absorption makes its physical state halt,
contradicting the exact encoded nonhalting boundary. Decidability of
equality with $\mathsf H$ gives $z_{t+1}=\mathsf H$ intuitionistically.
Thus both directions have explicit primitive recursive witnesses.

The same lemma composes a new implementation with the established source.
A bounded blank simulation alone is insufficient; the indispensable
hypotheses are complete boundary configurations, the arbitrary-input
invariant, and positive durations. The updated AX11H physical audit
supplies these through literal, unary-run and finite-list lemmas.
Its list-width variable denotes the width of the same list occurring in
the tape expression, avoiding an independent-width assumption.

\subsection{Scope of this review}

The finite-demand scheme, its main stratum dependencies, the Foundation
opening/closing argument, and the exact-clock physical composition were
reviewed. No specific contradiction, circular supplier dependency, or
illicit weakening of induction was found in those inspected constructions.
This review does not reconstruct every local syntactic equation or every
large fixed proof leaf in the inherited development. The updated AX11H
calibration is therefore an explicit inherited mathematical theorem;
finite runtime receipts are not substitutes for that theorem, and no new
end-to-end Lean or Rocq verification is claimed.

\subsection{What the other requested projects contribute}

The ZFC work supplies useful machine engineering: context-sensitive
decoder folds, exact symbolic tape contracts, and generalized startup
seeds. Its completed external Lean/Rocq K215 theorem has a separate
internal-arithmetic status, and $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$
cannot simply replace $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})$ in this problem.
The Diophantine development supplies weak-arithmetic model and
conservation infrastructure. Its small variable counts do not imply a
small binary-tape enumerator; a literal coefficient decoder, arithmetic
engine, search and initialization must all be counted.
ConciseIPRA supplies examples of deriving logic from a small native
calculus, but its native theory is calibrated to iPRA, not automatically
to PA. A smaller presentation of a weaker theory does not by itself solve
the present consistency-machine problem.
