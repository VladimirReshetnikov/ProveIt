Finding
-------
The new completion papers do supply explicit written primitive-recursive witness constructions and arguments covering arbitrary internal PA proof codes. They do not merely append an absolute theorem to an unchanged conditional argument. In the reviewed layers I found no definite remaining bridge silently promoted from a hypothesis to a theorem. This is an assessment of the written construction and its dependency architecture, not an independent formal verification of every cited finite derivation.

The old references/final_dependency_review.md explicitly predates the new completion papers. Its warnings about old full-PA all-environment induction are useful audit targets, but they cannot be cited as the final status of this revised release. The authoritative repository README can still correctly report that end-to-end Lean formalization is open. It should not be used to deny the existence of this new written completion claim.
