\subsection{The verified 195-state realization, PA195EvenHX}

The parity scheduler has a complete literal implementation with 195
working states and 390 rows. Its dense start state is zero, its external
halt label is 195, and its tape is initially entirely zero. It is included
as a separate research result, since its state count exceeds the requested
194-state fallback. Its algorithmic change is more substantial than the
H5 radix specialization: it permanently visits only candidates
$4,6,8,\ldots$, with completeness proved in the preceding section.

Relative to primary PA194, introduce native control 3000 and make the
following changes:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
\text{control}&\text{read 0}&\text{read 1}\\\hline
110&\text{unchanged}&1R3000\\
3000&0R14&1R8\\
21&\text{unchanged}&0R55\\
45&\text{unchanged}&0R60
\end{array}
\]
The latter two edits exchange the physical positions of logical registers
$H$ and $X$. The logical-to-physical register map becomes
$(6,2,0,4,3,8,7,5,1)$. The arithmetic engine's rows are unchanged.
This placement change is essential to the demonstrated blank bootstrap;
the first trial with the old placement halts after 62,645 steps and does
not provide a consistency machine.

An independent literal-table checker derives the four register-list
lemmas again, resolves the source by both arithmetic and interval methods,
and checks all 4,781 branch-sensitive outcomes over arbitrary registers
and a nonempty spare tail. It uses 1,068,285 symbolic operations, with a
largest case of 392 operations and minimum positive duration constant
three. Dense/native conjugacy and graph reachability are also checked.

After exactly 44,094 literal transitions from the blank tape, the machine
reaches native state 84 with head at $-2$, source ordinal 40 and logical
registers $(4,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,1)$. The complete finite-support tape equals
the prescribed frame, including all 29 one-valued spare registers and
the zero exterior. The independently replayed 48-instruction bridge
then reaches the clean source entry 25 with registers
$(4,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$. The parity-prefix theorem therefore applies with
$N_0=4$. The original source calculus, proof annotation and all-store
refutation compiler are unchanged. The same positive-duration argument
used for H5 transfers this source calibration to the literal table.

Consequently the ordinary mathematical proof establishes
\[
 \mathrm{PA}\vdash
 \neg\Halt(\mathrm{PA195EvenHX},\mathrm{blank})
 \longleftrightarrow\Con(\mathrm{PA}).
\]
