## Unary theory U: independently checked mathematical structure

The primitive language has one binary relation E and classical equality-free first-order logic on a nonempty domain. With `Sub(x,a) := forall t (E(t,x) -> E(t,a))`, U has every instance

    forall a exists b [
      forall x (E(x,b) -> phi(x)) and
      forall x (Sub(x,a) -> (phi(x) -> E(x,b))) ],

where phi has at most the free variable x. Bound variables may be numerous. In particular a is not a free matrix parameter. The schema is weak covering, not exact arbitrary-source powers or unrestricted comprehension.

The lower proof in `sections/unary_core.tex` and `sections/unary_arithmetic.tex` has the following noncircular dependency order.

1. Define coextensionality by equal member profiles. Saturation supplies its missing first-coordinate membership congruence. The numerical predicate combines saturation, transitivity, a strict linear order on member classes, hereditary saturation, and endpoints for every represented intersection with each numerical initial segment.
2. A false-matrix cover gives empty objects. Nonempty numerical objects have predecessor classes. These local facts do not assume total successors.
3. For a unary formula Q, filter failures at source n. A least failure inside n contradicts the successor premise using its predecessor. If no failure lies inside n, use n itself and its predecessor. This proves induction before successor existence. For a formula with parameters, bind them in `Q(v) := forall p (BaseAndStep(theta,p) -> theta(v,p))`; that is still a legal unary matrix.
4. Derived induction yields comparison and least numerical classes/finite tuples. No external well-foundedness or finiteness is assumed.
5. For a numerically parameterized formula chi with proved parameter invariance and a supporting source, suppose exact comprehension fails. Bind the globally least failing tuple inside a unary matrix. All its witnesses agree modulo coextensionality, so this unary predicate is exactly chi at that tuple. Applying the weak cover at a support source contradicts failure. This is a finite proof construction; it does not evaluate a least-failure predicate or introduce a truth predicate.
6. That bounded numerical comprehension first gives total successors and powers of numerical objects. Saturated singleton/pair/ordered-pair codes and numerical graph comprehension follow.
7. Canonical histories bind old history objects inside their defining formulas. Pointwise history uniqueness is proved first by the already available induction with arbitrary ambient parameters. The graph extension and restriction formulas therefore use only numerical free parameters. They give total addition and then multiplication with the PA recursion equations.
8. Translate equality by coextensionality and quantify over numerical representatives. The same derived induction proves every translated PA induction instance. There is no set of all quotient classes asserted.

The upper proof in `sections/upper_transport.tex` sends E(x,a) to binary membership `Bit(a,x)`. PA proves `Sub(x,a) -> x<=a`; formula-specific finite bit comprehension, by PA induction, supplies the cover. PA also proves global irreflexivity because `2^a>a`. The proof works formula by formula in every PA model and permits arbitrary additional matrix parameters. It does not require a uniform arithmetic truth predicate.

The fixed interpretation and schema-specific derivation templates use primitive-recursive operations on syntax. Formula induction and proof-length induction verify the two refutation transformations in PA. This supplies the claimed internal consistency equivalence, rather than relying only on an external model-equiconsistency assertion. The package explicitly does not implement a general arbitrary-PA proof translator.

## The literal machine does not parse the unary U grammar

The inherited native calculus uses a stronger machine-facing theory W of unrestricted weak covering, with extra matrix parameters permitted, together with global irreflexivity IR. This is legitimate: U embeds into W+IR, while the same bit-membership construction interprets W+IR in PA. Compose those translations with PA's interpretation into U. Thus the three theories have PA-verifiable finite-refutation reductions.

The native calculus is also different from the positive dyadic stream introduced in the unary archive. The positive fragment alone has a one-point total-relation model. Its calibrated assertion is nonderivability of its designated positive target, or consistency after adjoining the specified negative seed. None of the three U211 tables is a literal compilation of that separate nine-multiplication positive polynomial emitter.

The actual native coding uses dual Cantor pairing P, implication `I=4P`, membership `R=4P+1`, quantification `Q=4P+2`, and auxiliary equality `E_=4P+3`. Code 0 is a closed truth alias with `I(0,0)=0`; code 1 is the diagonal membership atom treated as falsity under IR. The numerical system retains seven logical rows, a dominated propositional row, MP, GEN, and a gated covering/vacuity bundle. Its pure-logic completeness requires actual arguments for the aliases, dominated-row recovery, and the reduced equality interface; it cannot be assumed from the existence of the K215 endpoint.

U222's paper and retained templates give those arguments. `DominatedCore.v`/`DomRho8.v` recover the unrestricted propositional basis; `G222.v` recovers reflexivity, the equality helper, and the Euclidean equality row; `L224E.v` and `Interp222.v` interpret auxiliary equality by two-sided membership indiscernibility. They avoid an unjustified assumption of arbitrary primitive-equality vacuity. `Complete222.v` supplies the finite quotation/refutation-transfer framework, with Theory K-specific later declarations explicitly excluded from the U argument.

## Role-a emitter and exceptional collisions

Read `U214_ConPA_Reduction_2026-10-28/proofs/role_emitter.tex` for the exact emitter that survives in all three U211 sources. Matrix code and output-variable label are a; source label is b; tested label is c. The retained gate is `c != a and c != b` and supports the atomic-vacuity conjunct `R(a,b) -> Q(c,R(a,b))`.

For positive a, all effective labels of its matrix are below a; code 0 decodes to a closed truth. The output label is therefore fresh. If a differs from b, the cover is an ordinary W instance. If a=b, the local subset-guard binder captures the source occurrence and the guard becomes

    forall v_a (E(v_a,v_c) -> E(v_a,v_a)),

which is `Emp(v_c)` under IR. An empty source, followed by an ordinary cover at that source, proves the resulting exceptional instance. This handles all representatives and assumes no extensionality or unique empty object. Small codes 0 and 1 are covered explicitly.

Completeness pads a desired matrix by double negation to a code n>1, chooses `(a,b,c)=(n,0,1)`, projects the cover, removes the padding, and restores names. The gate passes. The same bundle projects every required atomic-vacuity instance, preserving the logical proof interface. Dropping either gate test without replacing that interface is not justified.

An older U222 `theory_bridge_notes.txt` contains a provisional warning that code zero is unsafe; `references/exceptional_codes.txt` explicitly supersedes it and the final U222 paper uses the corrected unshifted construction. Quote the final paper/correction, not the obsolete provisional sentence.

## Source compiler and startup obligations retained by any reduction

The direct-transfer source transports the exact 774-primitive U214 emitter into the ST control structure, reducing the predecessor source by 823 primitives. The 3,135 exterior primitives and every emitter primitive have separately resolved control edges. The main documents are the U213 `proofs/source_transport.tex`, `command_semantics.tex`, `compiler.tex`, and `startup_suffix.tex`.

The actual blank startup is an interior AX frame, not a clean source root. After 48,140 literal transitions it reaches native84, head -2, header54, ordinal136, with logical registers `(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)=(1,0,13,0,0,1,0,0,1)`. It matches 141 source steps from ordinal106 and store `(1,0,4,0,0,1,0,0,1)`.

The incoming cached 1 is only an arithmetic selector parameter. It is not assumed theorem-valued and is not generalized as a theorem of falsity. The remaining suffix produces nine explicit theorem heads over opaque tail4, then returns to candidate1 with counter, cache, top, scratch, and residual pointer in the stated safe state. Tail4 is dangerous if exposed: `4=P(1,1)` and `1=P(1,0)`.

The all-natural Cantor-diagonal credit proof gives debt at most2 for the scheduler from candidate1. AX adds10, transfer costs1, and MP costs at most0; the nine initial heads leave margin7. A ghost explicit-list length is essential because `P(0,0)=0` is a numerical alias. The proof does retain this length and does not infer a unique physical stack depth from its integer code.

The strong proof compiler works above arbitrary old stacks after an AX reset. Successful candidate numbers are cofinal, and each unsuccessful candidate computation terminates. This proves completeness despite the finite startup cutoff and opaque protected tail. The new machine must preserve this continuation via source identity and whole-tape primitive simulation; a checksum or bounded blank prefix alone is not enough.
