U218: provenance and attribution
================================

This research package was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov on 28 October 2026.
It does not assert discovery priority, absolute minimality, or unconditional
consistency. Independent checks within this package were written by separate
assistant agents in one research collaboration, not external human reviewers.

New work
--------
The equivalent covering-clause source, bounded parenthesization search,
numerical proof translation, independent source/control-flow audit,
contextual arithmetic-routing diagnosis and repair, concrete failed-table
trace, two local state reductions and proofs, independent finite certificates,
mutation controls, literal final tables, dense renaming, deterministic
reconstruction wrapper, comparative review, and this report were developed
for the present request. The separately preserved U221 reference was also
developed during this request. No endorsement by source authors is implied.
