REVIEW SCOPE
============

Source review
-------------
The complete main LaTeX manuscripts and their included mathematical sections
from all eight supplied draft packages were reviewed. The main comparison is
Table 1 of the new report. input_manifest.json fixes the complete 240-file
input archive, including files which were not individually rerun. Relevant
numeric builders, proof enumerators and supplied audit material were inspected
to avoid claiming inherited improvements as new. Not every script from every
old archive was rerun.

The K215, ProveIt, K230 and PA238 source statements described in the bibliography
were read through primary repository sources. The two historical publications
were read for context. external_sources.json records URLs and available Git
blob identities. This is not an exhaustive record survey, and no external
proof build was reproduced.

Mathematical audit
------------------
Independent agent reviews covered:
* Pure logical properties of the saturated numerical predicate.
* Least failures below a bad source, including the empty-intersection branch.
* Arbitrary-parameter induction by a closed-premise unary matrix.
* Comparison and least tuples before successor totality.
* Numerical bounded comprehension, parameter invariance and explicit supports.
* Successor and powers of numerical sources only.
* All-representatives ordered pairs and decoding.
* History uniqueness before existence; numerical graph selectors with old
  histories bound internally; canonical extension and restriction.
* Formula-by-formula binary-membership upper interpretation and finite-proof
  transport, without any uniform truth predicate or semantic evaluator.
* Infinite orbit-model closure and failure of source-dependent exactification.
* Closed-D substitution in the positive unary theory and irreflexive supports.
* Raw exceptions at 0 and 1, padding at 27 and 33, and the calibrated target.
* The five positive logical families, deduction/generalization restrictions,
  the eight-tag stream's soundness/completeness, and exact syntax matching.

A second adversarial review checked the source-free construction for hidden
parameters, circular successor assumptions, and illegal history parameters.
Final reviews checked the integrated paper against the reviewed constructions.
These reviews are mathematical scrutiny by AI agents, not independent human
refereeing or proof-assistant kernel checks.

Executable evidence
-------------------
All executable material is Python with standard-library dependencies.
The integrated reproducer reruns all six released diagnostics in normal and
optimized modes, verifies exact output receipts and a fixed certificate
inventory, and checks release hashes before and after execution. It neither
builds nor proves the unbounded PA interpretation in a formal proof system.

The emitter checks compare separate syntax-tree construction, algebraic
programs and codec round trips. The polynomial checker reconstructs every
coefficient exactly. The theorem stream uses the division-free polynomial
AX program while the explicit certificate checker retains the constructor
reference implementation. The certificate replay fixes exact conclusions
independently of the certificate-generating function and rejects empty,
missing, additional and unrelated valid targets. Some lower-level coding and
proof-checking functions are shared; this is not a wholly independent kernel.

Finite relation and orbit-level tests are local diagnostics. The bounded
proof stream finding no target is an implementation observation only.
The literal one-tape, two-symbol TM and its transition count remain open work.
