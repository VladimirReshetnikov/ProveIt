COMPILER CHECKS

The native code identities are P(x,y)=(x+y)(x+y+1)/2+y,
I(x,y)=4P(x,y), Q_i(x)=4P(i,x)+2. The proof uses I(0,0)=0 explicitly,
and does not presume that numerical stacks encode list length injectively.

The direct S command tests the incoming cache h=1. On normal return it
produces (h,tail(w),d,head(w)). It does not test the incoming top. The
structural macro H=AX(0;0,0);S^11 instead tests the incoming top after AX
has erased the incoming cache. Its normal contract is the top-preserving
pop (t,tail(w),0,head(w)). The calibration correctly keeps these two
contracts separate.

I checked the shift counts in H, PUSH, and DISCARD against all ten AX
outputs. The first eight outputs are implication codes, the ninth is a
nonzero quantifier code, and the tenth is the saved top. Thus the first ten
S tests in H cannot halt; its eleventh tests the saved top. PUSH reaches a
quantifier code in cache and then forces MP(0) to fail by its residue modulo 4.
DISCARD's first MP(1) fails because I(t,1)>0, and its final MP(0) fails
because Q_1(0)>0. These are universal tag arguments, not bounded tests.

J(0) must be the empty word, while J(u)=MP(u) for u>0. This zero exception
is retained: MP(0) would succeed when both top and cache are zero. The
axiom-block counts S^(j+1);H^(9-j), S^9;H for the dominated row, and H^10
for the gated bundle each restore the exact old numerical stack and leave
counter/cache zero. In generalization, the premise is loaded into cache
just before AX, so X=1 does not cause an unintended earlier cache test.

For ordinary MP, major-first compilation produces (A,w,0,I(A,Y)); successful
MP gives (Y,[0;w],Y,0). The final AX(Y;0,0);S^11 either halts at Y=1 or
returns (Y,w,0,0), removing precisely the MP-pushed zero. The A=Y=0 alias
case is valid. For the additional cache-return variant, reserving a zero
below the premises is necessary; the displayed construction does so. The
accepted source uses ordinary MP.

The strong invariant quantifies over all incoming natural top/stack values,
and allows an early halt. It must not be confused with actual-run soundness
on theorem-valued explicit heads. Induction on finite derivations and
cofinally many successful candidate numbers prove completeness; ordinary
theoremhood invariance and the protected-tail credit theorem prove soundness.
The candidate number P(2,P(z,P(1,R))) has the displayed AX fields and
successor-delimited suffix, dominates z, and resets counter/cache before
the strong suffix. Cantor projections and the predecessor operation make
every unsuccessful positive command continuation strictly decrease.

TRANSFER TO U

The inherited compiler requires the AX rows' theoremhood, tag classes,
positions, gate, and uniform arithmetic contracts. It does not require
the previous bundle's set-theoretic strength. U214 supplies the changed
bundle's soundness/completeness calibration, including coincident a=b,
codes 0 and 1, and atomic vacuity recovery. The retained numerical rows and
generalization interface are unchanged. Accordingly it is legitimate to
transport the command compiler to the U calculus. It would not be legitimate
to reuse the fixed literal K215/ZFC endpoint as if its statement had changed.

STARTUP REVIEW POINT

The new startup argument uses an interior frame and a nine-head AX suffix,
not the old ST215 ten-head initializer. The source seed at ordinal 106 is
(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)=(1,0,4,0,0,1,0,0,1). At first glance L=1 might appear
to require an extra pop before the candidate 1 credit theorem. That concern
is resolved by the actual source: read176 at [158,172) unpairs L=1 into
(y,L)=(1,0), so H changes from 1 to 2; read175 at [199,213) then reads x=0
and keeps D=0. Thus the final source parameters are (A,B,C,U)=(0,2,0,0)
and the residual pointer is zero before the safe root. The ordinary
candidate 1 credit d(1)=2 is the correct one.

The incoming top 0 at ordinal 106 replaces the already constructed quantified
head with truth. It is pushed above opaque numerical tail 4, and the suffix
adds the eight logical axiom heads. Tail 4 is not declared theorem-safe.
The nine explicit theorem heads protect it, leaving margin 7 under the
inherited conservative scheduler credit. The full literal startup trace,
141-step source-prefix match to ordinal 136, exact suffix macro bindings,
and 56-cell whole-tape simulation are required separately to make this
application to the 213-state table valid. This review has checked the
mathematical interface and the two pointer reads; it does not replace those
separate exact checks.

PROOF LEVEL

The source transport, compiler, and credit arguments use finite syntax,
primitive-recursive constructions, and ordinary natural-number induction;
they are formalizable in PA. The new argument is a mathematical proof with
executable exact checks, not a newly compiled end-to-end Lean/Rocq/MM0 proof.
The U/PA consistency interpretation remains an expressly inherited
dependency, and no absolute minimality or unconditional nonhalting claim
is made.
