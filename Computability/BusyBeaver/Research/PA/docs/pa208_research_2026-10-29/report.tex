\begin{abstract}
The requested endpoint is a two-symbol, one-tape machine $M$ with fewer than
208 working states such that
$\PA\vdash\neg\Halt(M,\mathrm{blank})\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)$.
Five incoming U208 archives were examined at the same pinned repository
revision. Their nine primary and alternative tables fall into exactly two
rooted, action-preserving isomorphism classes. All five proposals implement
the same local identification of historical states 172 and 211; their
advertised savings cannot be added. Fresh verification covers 5,084 symbolic
primitive contracts and every literal transition row for each graph class.
The inherited consistency calibration was audited, including the difference
between unary Theory U, the native $W+\IR$ calculus, and the separate positive
theorem-stream proposal. New research includes a more general source
associativity theorem, finite-window inductive context analysis, a direct
18-primitive negation constructor, fixed-role cover emitters, and a proof
compiler with unnecessary successful-modus-ponens padding removed.
Four new local constructions also yield eighteen pairwise non-isomorphic
208-state machines with short halting-equivalence proofs.
None has yet yielded a verified sub-208 literal table. The negative results
are explicitly limited to their search families; they are not state-count
lower bounds.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem, conventions, and evidential standard}

The motivating K215 result is materially stronger than an informal statement
of equiconsistency: its final theorem is proved in PA and binds a particular
literal machine to a particular arithmetization of ZFC inconsistency
\cite{k215pa}. Replacing its set theory, source program, or transition table
requires new bridges at the affected levels. The K215 certificate does not
automatically certify a U-based construction.

Here a working state is a state with two binary transition rows; the halt
state is excluded from the count. A transition writes a bit, moves exactly
one square left or right, and selects the next state. The tape is two-way
infinite and initially all zero, with designated initial state 0. A smaller
alphabet count, a nonblank input encoding, or a different machine convention
would not answer the requested comparison.

There are four separate proof obligations:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A theory/calculus calibration, verified inside PA at the level of
      finite refutations, not merely an external equivalence of models.
\item Sound and complete enumeration of numerical derivations by the
      structured register program, including its startup invariant.
\item Correct implementation of every required primitive over arbitrary
      natural register values, complete tape frames, and arbitrary permitted
      exterior tape.
\item An exact state census and a binding of all those statements to the
      actual delivered transition table.
\end{enumerate}
A finite blank-tape run, finite register tests, or a small source expression
establishes none of these obligations by itself.

We use the following evidence labels. An \emph{ordinary proof} is a written
mathematical argument. \emph{PA-formalizable} means its construction uses the
specified finite syntactic transformations and induction available in PA;
it does not assert that a new PA proof object has been generated and checked.
A \emph{symbolic runtime replay} applies explicit induction schemas and
arbitrary-natural tape identities. A \emph{finite screen} is used to reject
candidates and is never promoted to an unbounded correctness theorem.

\section{The existing local 209-to-208 reduction}

The reduction identifies historical states 172 and 211. Its point is that
the surrounding tape distinguishes visits whose state names are being
identified. The complete predecessor census establishes:
\begin{enumerate}
\item On every entry to old state 172, the two preceding tape cells are
      \code{10}.
\item On entry to old state 211 while reading zero, the immediately
      preceding cell is one.
\end{enumerate}
These claims concern arbitrary initial tape runs from the specified initial
state, not only the observed blank startup.

In the class-A representative, both transitions entering 211 from state
163 are redirected to 172, the zero row at 172 is altered, and state 211
is deleted. The key rows are
\[
\begin{array}{rclcrcl}
163,0&\mapsto&(0,L,211)&\longrightarrow&163,0&\mapsto&(0,L,172),\\
163,1&\mapsto&(1,R,211)&\longrightarrow&163,1&\mapsto&(1,R,172),\\
172,0&\mapsto&(0,L,67)&\longrightarrow&172,0&\mapsto&(0,L,217).
\end{array}
\]
The four old steps at $(172,0),(172,1),(211,0),(211,1)$ are simulated by
new blocks of lengths $7,1,3,1$. Every block preserves the entire tape,
has the correct state/head endpoint under the quotient, and has no interior
halt. The other 414 old rows commute directly with the quotient.

\begin{proposition}[Bounded positive stuttering]
The old and new machines halt on exactly the same inputs. If an old run
halts after $T_{209}$ transitions, the corresponding new run has length
\[
T_{208}=T_{209}+6N_{172,0}+2N_{211,0}\leq 7T_{209},
\]
where $N_{q,b}$ counts the indicated old steps before halting.
The finite local argument and both halting implications formalize in PA.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Induct on old execution length, substituting the appropriate positive block
at each step. This constructs the new boundary configurations and the stated
time identity. For reflection, boundaries occur after positive blocks of
length at most seven. An interior point cannot be a new halt. A new halting
prefix therefore ends at a simulated old boundary; a bounded search through
the new prefix recovers the corresponding old prefix. Finite tape windows
and finite trajectories can be coded arithmetically, so the induction and
bounded recovery require no consistency assumption.
\end{proof}

This local theorem is strong evidence for the shared reduction. It must
still be composed with the theory, program, runtime, and startup bridges to
obtain the final PA-consistency statement.

\section{Audit of Theory U and the native consistency calibration}

\subsection{Three interfaces that must be kept separate}

The unary theory U is equality-free classical first-order logic over
nonempty domains, in one binary relation $E$. Define
\[
\Sub(x,a)\;:\!\equiv\;\forall t\,(E(t,x)\to E(t,a)).
\]
For every formula $\varphi$ whose only possible free variable is $x$, U has
the schema
\begin{equation}\label{eq:U}
\forall a\,\exists b\left[
 \forall x(E(x,b)\to\varphi(x))\ \land\
 \forall x\bigl(\Sub(x,a)\to(\varphi(x)\to E(x,b))\bigr)
\right].
\end{equation}
The source variable $a$ cannot occur free in $\varphi$. The output is a
weak cover: every member passes the predicate, and every passing subset of
the source is included. The axiom does not require the output's members to
be exactly the passing subsets of the source.

The actual U208 source instead uses a Cantor-coded native calculus whose
ordinary theory is $W+\IR$. Here W allows matrix parameters in the weak-cover
schema, and $\IR$ is global irreflexivity. Its numerical truth alias is 0 and
its refutation target is 1. The intended PA-verifiable consistency cycle is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cycle}
\Con(\PA)\ \Longrightarrow\ \Con(W+\IR)
\ \Longrightarrow\ \Con(U)\ \Longrightarrow\ \Con(\PA).
\end{equation}

The positive dyadic theorem stream in the original unary-theory archive is
a third interface. It targets the atom $E(v_0,v_0)$ at code 0 and uses a
negative seed concerning $D\equiv\forall v\,E(v,v)$. Its positive theory
alone has a one-object total-relation model. Its calibrated assertion is
nonderivability of the atomic target, equivalently consistency after the
negative seed is included. Equating the consistency of that positive theory
alone with $\Con(\PA)$ would be an error. It is not the source compiled by
the five current U208 packages.

\subsection{Lower interpretation: why unary matrices suffice}

The subtle direction is interpreting arithmetic in U. The supplied argument
uses coextensionality, saturation, and numerical representatives rather
than assuming global extensionality. Coextensionality is a congruence in
the second argument of membership; its use in the first argument requires
saturated containers. Numerical representatives are saturated, transitive
objects whose member classes are linearly ordered, together with a first-order
endpoint condition for represented initial segments.

The ordering of the proof is essential. Heredity, irreflexivity, greatest
predecessors, and uniqueness of existing successors are proved first.
Induction is established before totality of successor. Given a numerical
counterexample $n$, one filters the failures below $n$ using the fact that
every member of $n$ is a subset of $n$. If a failure lies inside $n$, take
its least class and use its existing predecessor. If none lies inside $n$,
apply the successor step from the greatest predecessor of $n$ to $n$.
Neither argument assumes that $n$ already has a successor.

Arbitrary ambient parameters are internalized in a legal unary matrix:
\[
Q(v)\;:\!\equiv\;\forall p\,
\bigl(\operatorname{BaseAndStep}_{\theta}(p)\to\theta(v,p)\bigr).
\]
The base and successor step for $Q$ follow from their corresponding
components. This is an actual elimination of free parameters in the
nonlogical instance, not a silent strengthening of the schema.

After comparison and least-counterexample results, bounded numerical
comprehension uses the least failing numerical tuple. For a putatively
missing filter at that tuple, use the unary matrix
\[
\exists n\,
  \bigl(\operatorname{LeastFailure}(n)\land\chi(z,n)\bigr).
\]
All parameters $n$ are bound. A separate support hypothesis supplies an
object $a$ such that every selected $z$ is a subset of $a$. The upper and
lower clauses then yield the desired exact profile. Finite tuples are
minimized by finitely many least-element arguments; tuple-coding objects
are not assumed prematurely.

Successor, exact powers of numerical sources, common bounds, and bounded
graph comprehension follow in that order. Ordered-pair profiles include
entire coextensionality classes, so equal-coordinate and representative
cases are covered. Histories are constructed with the old graph
existentially bound inside the matrix and numerical parameters alone left
free. This avoids using an illegal arbitrary graph parameter in U's schema.
Addition, multiplication, and each translated PA induction instance then
follow from these history constructions.

\subsection{Upper interpretation and internal consistency equivalence}

Interpret $E(x,a)$ as ``bit $x$ of $a$ is one.'' PA proves that the translated
$\Sub(x,a)$ implies $x\leq a$. For each fixed arithmetic formula, induction
constructs its finite binary characteristic code through $a$. That gives
an exact filter, hence the weak-cover instance. Parameters are permitted in
this PA induction, so the interpretation also validates W. PA proves
irreflexivity because $2^a>a$.

This is a formula-by-formula induction argument and works in nonstandard
PA models. It does not use a uniform arithmetical truth predicate for all
formulas. For the internal statement, the required bridge consists of
uniform proof templates: renaming, substitution, term-to-graph translation,
relativization, and finite proof concatenation. Those syntactic operations
are primitive recursive, with verification by formula and proof induction
in PA. This supplies the stated ordinary argument for
\[
\PA\vdash\Con(U)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA).
\]
The supplied work does not implement every arbitrary-proof translation or
export a new end-to-end PA kernel certificate. The audit found no fatal
mathematical defect in the proof, but this remaining formalization boundary
must be reported accurately.

\subsection{Native role emitter and its exceptional cases}

Write $I,R,Q$ for the native implication, membership, and universal
quantifier constructors, and $F=1$, $N(A)=I(A,F)$. The emitter uses
matrix/output label $a$, source label $b$, and tested label $c$:
\begin{align*}
V&=I(R(a,b),Q(c,R(a,b))), & e&=R(c,a),\\
G&=Q(a,I(R(a,c),R(a,b))), & U_0&=Q(c,I(e,a)),\\
L_0&=Q(c,I(a,I(G,e))), & H&=Q(a,I(U_0,I(L_0,F))).
\end{align*}
If $c\ne a,b$, the emitted bundle is $N(I(V,H))$; otherwise it is truth
code 0. Classically the enabled bundle is $V\land\neg H$.

For positive code $a$, every effective variable label in its decoded
formula is below $a$; zero is a closed truth alias. Thus the output binder
is fresh. For $a\ne b$, $G$ is the intended subset condition. For $a=b$,
its binder captures the source and gives
\[
\forall v_a\,[E(v_a,v_c)\to E(v_a,v_a)],
\]
which is $\Emp(v_c)$ under $\IR$. W supplies an empty source from a false
matrix and then supplies precisely the required cover at that source.
The codes $a=0$ and $a=1$ are treated explicitly as closed truth and falsity
under $\IR$. The gate is still needed for the separate atomic-vacuity
component $V$.

For completeness, rename source and test to 0 and 1, double-negate the
desired matrix to a code $n>1$, and choose $(a,b,c)=(n,0,1)$. Project the
cover, remove the double negation, and restore names. The inherited logical
rows and equality-indiscernibility translation remain necessary premises.
In particular, a supplied Boolean counterinterpretation rules out simply
deleting the dominated propositional row.

\subsection{Why the startup invariant cannot be omitted}

At exactly 48,140 blank-tape transitions, the selected class-A table reaches
native dispatcher 84 at head coordinate $-2$, source ordinal 136, with
logical registers
\[
(1,0,13,0,0,1,0,0,1).
\]
The header is at 54, there are 43 spare blocks of value one, and the complete
tape has 120 one-cells. These are exact finite data, not an extrapolation.
The source address is \code{001100110101010}.

The source then enters an interior axiom suffix. Its incoming cache 1 is
an arithmetic parameter, not an already proved falsity, and its numerical
stack tail 4 is opaque. The retained suffix proof constructs nine explicit
theorem heads above that tail. A separate ghost stack length is required
because Cantor pairing has the zero alias $P(0,0)=0$. The scheduler's maximum
initial conservative debt is two, leaving a permanent margin of seven.
This unbounded credit argument, together with the suffix contract, prevents
the opaque tail from being exposed. The finite startup trace alone does
not establish this fact.

\subsection{Simple theory shortenings that fail}

Several tempting modifications have small countermodels, uniformly
verifiable in PA:
\begin{itemize}
\item Deleting the lower clause permits a one-object empty relation.
\item Deleting the upper clause permits a two-object directed cycle, even
      with irreflexivity. Each profile is a singleton and $\Sub(x,a)$ reduces
      to $x=a$.
\item Replacing the subset guard by $E(x,a)$ again permits the one-object
      empty relation.
\item Removing the guard while retaining both clauses makes the matrix
      $\neg E(x,x)$ yield Russell's contradiction.
\end{itemize}
These are counterexamples to the specified edits, not a lower bound on
possible PA-equiconsistent schemas.

\section{New source-level transformations}

\subsection{Directional associativity}

The structured source language uses binary sequencing trees. A decrement's
zero branch and a forward break select zero-edge ancestors; backward
continues select one-edge ancestors. Let $f(X)$ and $b(X)$ be the respective
escaping control-depth bounds. For a leaf they are
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
 & f & b\\\hline
\CI r,\CH&0&0\\
\CD r&1&0\\
\CB k&k&0\\
\CC k&0&k
\end{array}
\]
and at a sequence $(X,Y)$ they satisfy
\[
f(X,Y)=\max(f(X)-1,f(Y),0),\qquad
b(X,Y)=\max(b(X),b(Y)-1,0).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Directional associativity]\label{thm:rotation}
The trees $((A,B),C)$ and $(A,(B,C))$ have the same ordered primitive word
and ordinal control graph whenever $f(A)=0$ and $b(C)=0$. No condition is
needed on the middle factor B. Halt occurrences are allowed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Within A, forward controls do not escape. The prefix changes from 00 to 0,
which changes no one-edge ancestor, so backward controls retain their
targets. Within B, compare prefixes 01 and 10. The first escaping forward
control sees the end of ABC in either tree: the old root and the new BC
interval have the same right endpoint. The first escaping backward control
sees the start of ABC: old AB and the new root have the same left endpoint.
Every later relevant ancestor is outside the changed region and agrees.
Within C, backward controls do not escape. Its prefix changes from 1 to 11,
which changes no zero-edge ancestor, so forward targets agree. Internal
targets are unchanged, as are ordinary successors and positive decrement
branches in the identical primitive word. Halting has no target to change.
\end{proof}

The supplied builders used the stronger requirement that all three factors
be completely closed and halt-free. On class A, the new theorem increases
the initially eligible single-entry pure-selector rotations from 51 to 58.
All 58 were checked against two independent CFG resolvers, including all
5,084 arithmetic target checks. Only one of the seven newly admitted literal
layouts passed the finite physical screen; it did not save a state.

This last qualification matters: a source associativity theorem is not a
theorem about the physical table. A selector state may also be called from
an arithmetic routine, where its old row still has another required role.

\subsection{Direct negation without a stack round trip}\label{sec:negation}

Let the native pairing be
\[
P(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b,\qquad I(a,b)=4P(a,b).
\]
The original negation constructor pushes a literal one and uses generic
implication. It consumes 47 source primitives and performs an unnecessary
push/pop round trip. The following identity gives a shorter construction:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pairidentity}
P(n,1)=P(0,n)+2.
\end{equation}
It is an elementary polynomial identity, valid also at $n=0$.

Use the inherited pair routine with effect
\[
(X,T,Y)\longmapsto (0,0,Y+P(X,T))
\]
and the inherited quadruple-transfer routine $Y\mapsto4T$, which clears Y
when T is initially zero. Start with $T=n$, $X=Y=0$ and perform
\[
Y\mathrel{+}=1;\quad Y\mathrel{+}=1;\quad
\operatorname{Pair};\quad\operatorname{QuadrupleTransfer}.
\]
The resulting top is $4(P(0,n)+2)=I(n,1)=N(n)$. The stack and selectors are
untouched, and both scratch registers end at zero. The routines require
$2+10+6=18$ primitives. An alternative 20-primitive version first transfers
T to X, sets T to one, then pairs and quadruples.

Four exact old 47-primitive subtrees can initially be replaced after the
protected startup prefix. Two further occurrences are hidden by source
association but are still closed 47-primitive intervals. Replacing all six
with the 18-primitive routine saves $6(47-18)=174$ occurrences, giving 3,735
primitives. The first 137 source rows are unchanged, all
exterior primitive effects and control edges are preserved, and the logical
axiom family is unchanged. The best initial literal substitutions have
198 distinct source DAG nodes, versus 197 in the baseline. Exploratory
allocation yielded a 211-state four-occurrence implementation and a
212-state six-occurrence implementation, both passing their finite primitive
screens. Thus this is a real arithmetic simplification but, by itself, not
yet a state reduction.

\subsection{Fixing cover roles while preserving the logical interface}

The completeness proof already uses source label 0 and tested label 1.
This suggests replacing those roles by constants throughout the cover,
while retaining the original atomic-vacuity term and its original gate.
With matrix/output selector $n$, define
\begin{align*}
e_n&=R(1,n),&g_n&=Q(n,I(R(n,1),R(n,0))),\\
u_n&=Q(1,I(e_n,n)),&\ell_n&=Q(1,I(n,I(g_n,e_n))),\\
h_n&=Q(n,I(u_n,I(\ell_n,F))).
\end{align*}
For $n>1$ this is the intended weak-cover shell. The small cases must be
checked, since the original vacuity gate does not necessarily exclude them.
For $n=0$, the inner guard binder also captures the fixed source; under
irreflexivity it means emptiness, and W supplies the required cover at an
empty source. For $n=1$, the matrix is false under irreflexivity and the
output/test collision makes the shell an irreflexivity instance. Thus the
enabled fixed-role bundle remains sound in $W+\IR$.

Every original atomic-vacuity instance is still projected from its enabled
bundle. For any desired W instance, padding to a matrix code $n>1$ and
choosing $(a,b,c)=(n,0,1)$ recovers its cover. The same proof templates give
the original consistency calibration.

Replacing five test-label loads by literal-one loads and one source-label
load by a literal-zero load saves 37 primitives, giving 3,872 occurrences.
The first 137 rows and the exterior interface remain unchanged. Exchanging
the upper and lower clauses inside the curried negation is classically
equivalent; exchanging the two lower-clause antecedents is also legitimate.
Using both exchanges improved the initial optimized source DAG from 206
to 203 nodes. This is still larger than the original 197-node source.

It is unsafe simply to reverse the constants without a repair: test 0,
source 1 fails at matrix code 0. Clearing the entire enabled bundle there
would also remove required atomic-vacuity instances. A sound repair sets
the cover's negative shell to F before combining it with V; then the bundle
becomes double-negated V. The explored variants record this distinction.

\subsection{Removing successful-modus-ponens padding}

The current successful MP command leaves an additional zero at the head of
the proof stack. In work-register notation $(T,W,D,H)$ its successful
postcondition is
\[
(z,P(0,w),z,0).
\]
That extra zero is a theorem but carries no information needed by the
enumerator. A revised success branch can return $(z,w,z,0)$ instead, leaving
failure unchanged. This deletes the closed PUSH0 interval
$[3847,3866)$, or 19 primitive occurrences.

Soundness is preserved because no new top, cache value, or stack entry is
introduced. The conservative stack-credit proof assigns MP weight zero
already; it never relied on the additional head. Completeness must use the
\emph{final three-command compiler}, not an earlier four-command
NIP/DROP presentation. Write $M=I(A,Y)$ and retain the established PUSH,
DISCARD, and testing-cleanup H blocks. The MP-node compiler becomes
\[
\operatorname{DISCARD};\operatorname{PUSH};B_M;\operatorname{PUSH};
B_A;\operatorname{PUSH};S;S;\operatorname{MP}'(Y);H.
\]
The initial PUSH reserves a zero. Immediately before the two S commands the
tuple is $(0,[A;M;0;w],0,0)$. The two transfers give $(A,[0;w],0,M)$;
the successful new MP gives $(Y,[0;w],Y,0)$; and H removes the reserved zero,
tests Y, and returns $(Y,w,0,0)$ or halts when $Y=1$. The other uses of MP
inside PUSH and DISCARD are forced failures, so their contracts are
unchanged. The zero exception $I(0,0)=0$ is handled by leaving $J(0)$ empty.
Induction on finite proofs therefore supplies the required strong Build
contract, cofinal enumeration, and halting of a compiled refutation.

The resulting source has 3,890 primitives and 197 distinct DAG nodes. An
exploratory literal allocation produced a 209-state implementation passing
10,118 finite primitive cases, with all 418 rows visited. This is not a
sub-208 result and is not supplied as an accepted replacement for U208.

\subsection{Direct selection and a checked zero invariant}

The old S command halts exactly when the cache $h=1$; otherwise it installs
$h$ as the current formula and pops the stack into the cache. Its 35-primitive
implementation can be replaced by a 25-primitive routine: clear T; attempt
one cache decrement; if it fails, proceed to the pop; otherwise increment T,
attempt a second decrement, and halt if that fails. If the second succeeds,
increment T again and transfer the remaining cache to T before the pop.
For $h=0$ this leaves $T=0$; for $h=1$ it halts; for $h\geq2$ the transfer
loop has invariant $T+H=h$ and decreases H to zero. The unchanged 14-primitive
pop then has its original all-natural precondition. A 26-primitive version
first copies the cache and performs the two-decrement test on scratch.
Their source DAG counts are 201 and 199, respectively, so neither count
constitutes a state improvement.

An independent product-domain analysis also proves a local zero fact useful
to MP. At every source ordinal it records which registers are zero, positive,
or unrestricted. The finite certificate checks 5,080 abstract CFG edges and
has 3,907 nonempty ordinal domains. It proves T is zero at ordinal 3847 and
excludes the positive body at 3848--3849. It includes both the canonical
source seed and the actual interior bootstrap: at ordinal 136 its only
requirements are $N>0$ and scratch $Y=0$, satisfied by the calibrated register
vector. Closure of all abstract edges makes this an induction over arbitrary
execution length. Deleting only that dead initial transfer gives 3,906
primitives but 198 source DAG nodes.

\section{Context analysis and the negative searches}

\subsection{Finite-window inductive contexts}

Finite histories constrain the tape near a state but forget information
older than the chosen history length. A complementary analysis computes a
least fixed point on states and radius-seven tape windows. The abstract
window contains 15 bits centered on the current head. Initially, every
window at state 0 is admitted, corresponding to arbitrary initial tapes.
Each transition updates the scanned bit, shifts the window by one cell,
and admits both possibilities for the newly exposed exterior bit.

\begin{proposition}[Soundness of window closure]
Any set of abstract state/window pairs containing every initial window and
closed under both exposed-bit successors contains the abstraction of every
concrete run from state 0.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Induct on run length. The concrete new exterior bit is one of the two
abstract choices. All other new window cells are determined by the old
window and the literal transition, so the concrete successor belongs to
the closed set.
\end{proof}

For class A the computed fixed point contains 961,765 abstract configurations,
compressed into 2,692 exact Boolean cylinders. Class B has 2,698 cylinders.
No working state is restricted to reading just one symbol. These are
complementary unbounded-history invariants within the chosen window
vocabulary. A long finite history can retain correlations involving a cell
that a sliding window has forgotten, so there is no blanket dominance claim.
The builder and its data are retained; independent transition-closure
verification is the appropriate certificate boundary.

\subsection{Counterexamples that prevented false positive results}

\paragraph{A dispatcher visit is not necessarily a primitive boundary.}
Cutting execution every time native state 84 is visited produced an
apparently useful invariant and the proposed deletion of state 14. That
cut is invalid: a primitive may re-enter dispatcher 84 on a temporary program
counter, before its canonical contract is complete. The finite checker
rejects the candidate at the decrement instruction with address
\code{0000100}. Those exploratory files are explicitly classified as
rejected and are not certificates.

\paragraph{Matching scanners on short words is insufficient.}
A two-sided joining search temporarily retained a $52\to155$ alias. Exact
branch analysis reaches state 32 on one side and 48 on the other. Both scan
ones to the right, but their zero transitions count different numbers of
separators. A longer unary block exposes the mismatch. Other scanner aliases
similarly fail on long zero separators.

\paragraph{A smaller decoder graph may break arithmetic.}
Fifty 207-state contractions obtained after source rotations fail finite
primitive tests even though their source graphs are correct. Selector states
are reused as arithmetic continuation code. An independent one-row repair
search over 86,008 choices did not repair the relevant failure.

\paragraph{A shorter zero detector needs a global invariant.}
Deleting state 34 from the origin detector fails an actual decrement case.
The leftward search encounters temporary phase markers as well as final
source addresses. Neither a shorter header constant nor an observed short
source address proves that a six-zero detector is sound. The explored
reassociations did not eliminate every six-zero source run in any event.

\section{What is established, and what remains open}

The comparison establishes two original U208 graphs, a single shared
historical fold, and successful fresh symbolic replay of both. The eighteen
new helper layouts have independently checked local halting-equivalence
proofs and remain at 208 states. The theory audit supports the
ordinary PA-formalizability claim while keeping its missing end-to-end
proof-assistant export explicit. The new directional associativity theorem,
the direct negation identity, and the revised MP proof-stack protocol are
mathematical contributions with precise interfaces. Fixed-role emitters
preserve the necessary native logical calibration, including exceptional
small codes and atomic vacuity.

The requested state-count breakthrough remains open in this investigation.
The central implementation obstacle is shared code: minimizing primitive
occurrences, distinct source nodes, and literal machine states are three
different optimization problems. A source routine may become shorter while
losing a physical overlap that previously saved a state. Conversely, a
slightly longer routine might enable better state sharing.

A defensible next candidate should therefore be accompanied by its literal
table at discovery time. The decisive tests are a complete state census,
the exact protected blank startup, arbitrary-natural primitive simulation
over complete frames, and the applicable source/calculus proof. For a local
quotient, a complete finite context invariant and positive bounded local
simulation may replace the full runtime replay. No amount of additional
blank-tape running substitutes for those arguments.
