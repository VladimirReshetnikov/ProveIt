\section{Binding the proof to the literal binary machine}
\label{sec:physical}

\subsection{The changed source loops}

The logical register names are $T=R_1$, $W=R_2$, $D=R_4$, $H=R_5$,
with scratch $X=R_6$, $Y=R_7$. The command-boundary invariant includes
$R_3=X=Y=0$. It leaves the four store values arbitrary. Candidate register
$R_0$ and the remaining command word $R_8$ are preserved except where
the inherited scheduler and operand decoder explicitly use them.

Here is the complete changed tail. A decrement lists the zero successor
first and the positive successor second; on the positive branch it

On a successful MP, the unchanged prelude supplies
$(T,W,D,H,X,Y)=(0,w,y,0,0,0)$ at 3661. After $k$ loop iterations,
$(D,H)=(y-k,k)$. The loop reaches 3664 in $3y+1$ source steps and
returns to fetch in one more. A failed comparison goes directly to 3664
with offset $y$ and zero cache. This proves (C1) for arbitrary naturals.

At Shift entry 3665, the first loop clears $T=t$ in $2t+1$ instructions.
The next loop transfers $H=h$ into both $T$ and $X$ in $4h+1$ steps,
and the third restores $H$ from $X$ in $3h+1$ steps. Thus the first nine
tail instructions, iterated as prescribed, reach $(T,H,X)=(h,h,0)$
after $2t+7h+3$ steps. The final two rows set $X=1$ and enter the
unchanged root guard, now comparing $h$ with one. On a nonhalting branch,
the unchanged Cantor decoder produces $\operatorname{hd}(w)$ and
$\operatorname{tl}(w)$. The elementary loop invariants give precisely
the new Shift contract for all store values. They do not rely on tests
of a bounded initial computation.

The checker verifies all unchanged prefix rows and the three exit
relocations before checking this tail. The source therefore has the
command semantics proved in Section~\ref{sec:commands}. Its census is
1,458 increments, 1,108 decrements, 1,109 resolved jumps, and one halt.
Counting both decrement outcomes gives
\[
 1458+2(1108)+1109+1=4784.
\]

\subsection{Clean tape frames and two independent checks}

A clean frame has a left marker, a binary source address, a zero-filled
separator, a right header, and a complete unary register file. A value $v$
uses the block $0\,1^{v+1}$. Logical registers $0,\ldots,8$ occupy physical
positions
\[
 (6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1).
\]
The header distance is $48+n$ for arbitrary $n\ge0$; the longest source
path here has 38 bits. The physical file has the nine logical registers
and an arbitrary finite nonempty spare suffix. Increment appends a
one-valued spare block, as in the inherited register engine. That effect
is part of the contract, rather than silently ignored.

The primary checker reconstructs original PA195 from its supplied grammar,
folds, and pinned vendor files, then reconstructs PA195DC. It checks the
source graph with two differently structured resolvers, the complete AX
free-algebra output, unchanged arithmetic-box rows, every instruction
outcome for the unfolded and final folded tables, and full initialization.
Its symbolic execution uses the inherited arbitrary-register block
contracts. It accepts 4,784 contracts for each table; the folded run has
1,573,300 symbolic steps in total and a maximum of 506 per contract.

The independent checker reads the literal final table and the delivered
source, without importing the primary compiler. It reconstructs the source
graph independently and rederives four generic register-list sweep lemmas
from that table. It then follows all source instructions with symbolic
natural-valued unary blocks and a symbolic finite spare list. It does not
call an arithmetic-box oracle. The resulting verification is:

These are different symbolic granularities, so their step totals need not
agree. Neither maximum is a bound on actual tape steps for arbitrary
register values. The symbolic formulas describe all natural register
values and finite spare lists; the counts enumerate proof cases, not small
numeric samples. The symbolic reasoning is executable mathematics and is
not asserted to have been newly checked by a proof-assistant kernel.

\subsection{Exact initialization}

Literal execution from the all-zero tape reaches the clean source-25 frame
after exactly 57,922 transitions, in native state 84 with head at $-2$.
The new source path is $\code{1000000}$; the logical register vector is
$(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$, and the spare suffix has 26 registers of value one.
The header distance is 48. Both checkers compare the entire finite support
of the tape, the state, and the head, and require every other cell to be
zero. The certificate retains the complete sorted set of one positions.
The original PA195 startup took 58,154 transitions; the saving is 232
transitions in initialization. It is not a claim of uniform runtime
improvement throughout the infinite search.

\subsection{Uniform finite-trace transfer inside PA}

Each symbolic instruction contract denotes a finite, positive literal
execution segment from a clean source frame to the exact next frame,
or to halt for the single source halt instruction. Symbolic unary scans
have natural-valued lengths; list traversals use induction on the finite
register list. Their trace constructors and duration bounds are primitive
recursive in the coded frame. The unchanged header and spare invariants
are preserved. The arithmetic loops above and the scheduler have analogous
primitive-recursive finite trace constructors.

For clarity, configurations can be coded by a control and two binary stacks
for the tape on either side of the head. A finite run is a coded finite
sequence checked by the fixed transition function. PA proves the usual
coding identities, the scan and list inductions, and the finite symbolic
identities. Since the table and the contract list are fixed finite objects,
their case distinctions require no additional schema beyond PA induction.
This internal argument concerns all coded finite traces, including
model-length traces. An external Lean theorem only about standard finite
runs would not by itself justify that step.

\begin{lemma}[Literal/source halting equivalence]\label{lem:literal-source}
PA proves that $M_{\rm DC}$ halts from blank if and only if the delivered
source program $G_{\rm DC}$ halts from the exact clean seed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Forward source-to-tape transfer concatenates the fixed startup and the
positive physical segments of a finite source trace. All constructed runs
and their bounds are primitive recursive, and PA verifies them by induction.

For the converse, suppose the literal machine halts at time $T$. If it
halted before the end of startup, the checked finite startup would already
contradict determinism. Otherwise start at the exact seed and suppose the
source does not halt within its first $T+1$ instructions. Iterating those
instructions and their contracts constructs a defined physical continuation
longer than $T$, with no premature halt. Determinism contradicts the alleged
halting trace. Thus the source halts within that bound. This does not assume
that a physical tape can be inversely decoded at arbitrary intermediate
times, or that the machine actually runs forever. Positivity of the
segments is the essential bounded argument.
\end{proof}

The dense and native tables have a separately checked row-for-row conjugacy.
Mapping each control of a finite trace along that fixed bijection gives
one-step exact conjugacy and fixes the specified start/halt pair. This
finite induction also formalizes in PA, completing the binding to the
definitive dense table rather than merely to inherited native labels.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}]
The uniform proof translations of Section~\ref{sec:pa-calibration} identify
refutability of $\mathcal C_\Gamma$ with refutability of PA, internally
in PA. Section~\ref{sec:commands} identifies the former with source
halting, using both the annotation invariant and the complete proof-word
compiler. The exact source binding and Lemma~\ref{lem:literal-source}
identify that source halt with a blank halt of the literal table. Compose
the three internal equivalences and negate them in classical PA. This
gives the stated biconditional with $\Con(\PA)$.
\end{proof}
