\subsection{What the comparisons rule out, and what they preserve}

James Chang's PA209 and the U209 parent of the U208 line are distinct programs.
The former has 3,948 source sites; PA196 has 3,812. PA196's reduction uses
transposed universal coding, guarded equality vacuity, altered head and
antecedent orders, and six complementary reader folds. Those six folds are
already included in its published budget of 36 engine states, 13 leaf routers,
and 147 reader states. U208's native 172/211 fold has a mixed incoming-direction
motif absent from PA196, so it cannot be transferred by merely renaming labels.
This excludes that specific transplant, not every history-sensitive quotient.

The six rejected 195-state graph classes must also remain separate from the
three prior positive designs. Besides the two-rotation and 714/777-failure
cases above, they include the seven-zero scanner, the separate-head short
scanner with 96 failed contracts, and a wrong-opcode zero-jump fold. The last
changes native 27/1 to \texttt{1 R 62}; source site 26 then increments register
6 and selects site 27 instead of implementing the intended zero decrement to
site 34. These witnesses refute the advertised simulations; most do not prove
that every conceivable alternative account of the table's blank behavior is
impossible.

One scanner audit corrects a particularly important diagnostic error. A tape
mismatch at the \emph{first} return to the dispatcher need not refute an
instruction implementation: the correct parent can return at 134 and 290
steps with intermediate tapes and reach its required full endpoint only at
574 steps. Positive-block simulation requires the specified complete endpoint,
not an arbitrarily selected intermediate return.

The exact corpus census, ordinary mathematical calibration arguments,
executable arbitrary-register contracts, and kernel theorems have different
roles. The imported formal PA196 endpoint establishes the internal implication
from nonhalting to $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})$. Its converse needs the
uniform proof-code and trace constructions described in the supplied
manuscripts and in this report. Neither a finite blank run nor a negative
bounded search supplies that implication or a state-count lower bound.
