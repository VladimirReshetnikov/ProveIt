\section{The search below 195 and what the negative results mean}
\label{sec:search}

No verified 194-state machine was obtained. The result is the user's
same-size alternative, with a source-level controller change and a new
literal graph. The comparison and search receipts support this statement;
they do not establish a lower bound of 195 or exhaust all possible
two-symbol machines.

\subsection{Finite-window local reductions}

We computed an inductive overapproximation of the head-centered tape
windows. A transition overwrites the central bit, shifts the window one
cell, and exposes either possible bit at its far edge. Starting from the
zero window is sound for the blank computation: introducing both possible
new edge bits only enlarges the represented set. A least fixed point on
this finite abstract graph supplies all allowed local contexts. The
radius-seven runs used 15-cell windows and gave:

\begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
\toprule
Table&Reachable abstract contexts\\\midrule
Original diagonal PA195&922,054\\
Separate-head 195S&931,579\\
New PA195DC&916,710\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

For each, we checked 37,636 ordered direct identifications retaining the
keeper's rows, with a noninitial removed state. We also checked all 18,721
unordered pairs of noninitial controls in a two-rewritten-row template:
the keeper's first step can write either bit, move either way, and enter
an existing helper. Each local simulation must reproduce one parent
transition within 63 steps, stay inside the recorded window, restore the
exact tape, and reach the projected state and head. Helper runs reject an
interior revisit to the merged keeper before the required endpoint.
Both possible starting read bits and all abstract contexts are checked.
The PA195DC run attempted 14,631,480 row actions after early rejection of
infeasible first-row choices. No pair passed this template.

As a positive control, the same program on the unfolded 202-state diagonal
parent found all seven known fold pairs. The full compatibility of the
retained simultaneous fold is verified separately by the 28-case
certificate. Radius-five arbitrary-initial-tape runs provide additional
recorded controls. The search's limited template and 63-step cutoff are
explicit; no failure is promoted to a universal state-minimality theorem.

\subsection{Program trees and alternative logical emitters}

The fold-aware grammar searches score the actual allocation of readers
after compatible folds, rather than merely minimizing source-tree nodes.
They explored binary reassociation, dynamic permutations of the six pure
axiom-head blocks, local parameter permutations, antecedent exchange in
three implicational heads, and a permutation of the two unrestricted
parameters in the dominated propositional head. Additional searches
allowed fold candidates involving the fixed core and used the new
Combined core, as well as two longer dual-diagonal clauses.
The five corrected runs proposed 39,661,078 moves and accepted 7,153,880;
none produced a sub-195 candidate. A final radius-two rotation neighborhood
contained 49,513 distinct endpoint trees and also found none. The retained
source/input files and fixed-seed logs state the finite budgets and tested
fold model. Two secondary 195-state witnesses passed full finite
implementation checks; their additional logical calibration is separate
from the primary result. The selected machine needs none of these
stochastic searches for reproduction: its exact witness is frozen and
deterministic.

Appendix~\ref{app:logical-experiments} proves the head-order change safe at
the command interface. Appendix~\ref{app:reflexivity} records a useful obstruction. Replacing the equality-witness head
$\exists x_D E(H,D)$ by the shorter reflexivity $E(H,H)$ does not preserve
the needed axiom interface: a variable-dependent-domain model validates the
other proposed axioms while refuting the witness. This rules out a simple
reuse of the old completeness proof. It does not decide the consistency
strength of the altered theory.

\subsection{What was useful from ZFC and ConciseIPRA}

The ZFC lineage supplies the unary-register engine, shared source readers,
and history-sensitive use of the adjacent tape cell \cite{zfc}. Those are
already central to the PA lineage. Transferring an optimized row from a
different encoding requires preserving its entry conditions; the corpus
contains failed examples of a blind zero-spare transplant. Here the full
table and its frame contracts were checked after composition.

ConciseIPRA emphasizes explicit syntax, proof checking, and economical
proof-theoretic interfaces \cite{ipra}. The diagonal formula-code freshness
argument is useful in that spirit. The PA calibration, however, needs
finite bit comprehension for an \emph{arbitrary} translated formula, proved
using full PA induction. A short iPRA checker cannot replace that strength
without a new equivalence proof. This report makes no identification of
$\Con(\mathrm{iPRA})$ with $\Con(\PA)$.

\section{Reproduction, provenance, and limits}
\label{sec:reproduce}

From the extracted archive root, the primary construction and full checks
run with Python 3 and its standard library:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 machine/build_verify.py
python3 independent/replay.py
python3 comparison/verify_novelty.py machine/machine.tm --start 0
\end{verbatim}
Assertions must remain enabled; do not use \code{python3 -O}. The primary
checker reconstructs its parent and the new machine, verifies all symbolic
contracts and exact startup, and compares the frozen table bytes. Adding
\code{--export} regenerates its derived source and certificates.
The independent replay checks its manifest, recomputes the physical and
command certificates, and compares the recorded outputs byte for byte.
No optimizer, solver, network connection, or theorem-prover installation
is required for these machine checks.

The independent command algebra check has 164 cases with arbitrary opaque
underlying stacks and the exact zero-tail alias. An undecided symbolic
comparison is an error, not a guessed branch. The mathematical universal
word theorem is given in Section~\ref{sec:commands}; the cases provide a
separate executable check of its exact contracts.

The source snapshot, archive hashes, exact imported paths, and unchanged
license notices are recorded. The 191-token emitter and seven folds are
from the original PA195 archive; the controller transformation and its
compiler are from the earlier Combined and cross-archive reports; 195S/195C
and the independent checker are from the latest 195-state research archive.
The present novelty is their verified diagonal-plus-Combined realization
at 195 states, its corpus comparison, and the accompanying bounded search.
It is not attributed as a new axiom system or the first Combined controller.

The remaining formalization task is to assemble the new literal-table,
source-command, and arithmetic-translation steps as one closed Lean or Rocq
theorem of the PA biconditional. The existing native 202-to-195 Lean theorem
concerns the old table; changing six rows does not automatically instantiate
it for the new one. The new finite fold certificate and the independent
physical proof support the ordinary mathematical lifting given here.
No soundness claim is inferred merely from a long nonhalting prefix, and
no state-optimality claim is made.
