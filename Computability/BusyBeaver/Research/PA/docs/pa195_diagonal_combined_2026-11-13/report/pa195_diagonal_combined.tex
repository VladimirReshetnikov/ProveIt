\begin{abstract}
We compare the current PA-machine corpus and construct a new deterministic,
single-tape, two-symbol machine with 195 working states. We did not obtain
a verified machine with fewer than 195 states. The delivered machine fulfills
the alternative target: it combines the original PA195 diagonal axiom emitter
and its seven contextual reader folds with the materially different Combined
proof-search controller. It changes six literal transition rows, removes
24 primitive source instructions, and changes the complete blank startup.
It is not isomorphic to any of the 422 spanning action-graph classes in the
pinned corpus, even after tape reflection or simultaneous bit complementation.

The construction has 390 transition rows and a 3,676-instruction source
program. Its deterministic checker reproduces the supplied PA195 parent,
the exact changes, all 4,784 symbolic source outcomes, all 28 two-cell fold
cases, and the complete 57,922-transition startup. An independent physical
checker rederives the register-list lemmas from the literal table.
The report supplies the mathematical argument that PA proves nonhalting of
the new table equivalent to $\Con(\PA)$, with explicit attribution for the
inherited logical calibration and controller compiler. A newly kernel-checked
Lean or Rocq proof of the whole biconditional is not claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, exact conventions, and proof status}
\label{sec:result}

The definitive object is \file{machine/machine.tm}. The working controls are
$0,\ldots,194$, control 0 is initial, and the separate halt label is 195.
The alphabet is $\{0,1\}$, the tape is indexed by $\mathbb Z$, the initial
head position is zero, and every initial cell is zero. A row
$(q,b,w,D,q')$ means read $b$, write $w$, move one cell in direction
$D\in\{L,R\}$, and enter $q'$. There are no stationary moves and no
outgoing transitions from halt. The halt label is excluded from the count.

Every working control is reachable in the finite transition graph from the
start. This graph statement does not assert that every control occurs in
the actual blank computation. The complete table is printed in
Appendix~\ref{app:table}. Its native-label presentation has start 74 and
halt 254; \file{machine/labels.json} is an exact dense-to-native conjugacy.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
\toprule
Quantity&Value\\\midrule
Working states / binary transitions&195 / 390\\
Unfolded working controls&202\\
Fixed controls / AX readers / folds&134 / 68 / 7\\
Source instructions / branch contracts&3,676 / 4,784\\
AX macro tokens&191\\
Largest source-address length&38\\
Exact blank startup&57,922 transitions\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The SHA-256 of the dense five-column table, including its final newline, is
\begin{quote}\small

\end{quote}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
Let $M_{\rm DC}$ be that exact table and let $H_{\rm DC}$ be the arithmetic
sentence asserting a finite halting trace from its specified blank start.
For an ordinary fixed primitive-recursive PA proof checker,
\[
 \PA\vdash H_{\rm DC}\leftrightarrow
      \exists p\,\operatorname{Prf}_{\PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner).
\]
Consequently $\PA\vdash\neg H_{\rm DC}\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)$.
\end{theorem}

The claim is a mathematical proof of internal PA provability. It is stronger
than an external statement that the machine does not halt in the standard
model. Its proof uses syntactic proof transformations and induction on coded
finite computations. In particular, it does not invoke PA's reflection
principle or assume $\Con(\PA)$ to prove the biconditional.

Three levels of evidence are distinguished throughout. The repository
contains substantial inherited Lean proofs, including the PA196 implication
from nonhalting to consistency and the original PA195 native-table quotient.
The complete new biconditional is proved in ordinary mathematical form
here by the stated interfaces. The delivered Python programs are executable
finite and symbolic certificates binding that argument to the literal table.
They are not represented as a newly completed proof-assistant theorem of
the whole endpoint. This distinction is a formalization limit, not a claim
that finite simulation alone establishes consistency equivalence.

\section{Comparison of the complete pinned corpus}
\label{sec:comparison}

The comparison uses ProveIt commit
\file{5ed5a41fa5932433111eea096311364c6087e8e3}, not a moving branch.
All 32 top-level ZIP archives in \file{docs/incoming} were inventoried,
including recursively nested archives, together with the PA research tree
\cite{corpus}. Appendix~\ref{app:archives} discusses every current archive.
The machine-readable comparison preserves archive hashes, member paths,
graph classes, state counts, and the distinction between proposed outputs,
ancestors, failed experiments, and synthetic controls.

The census finds 45 distinct ZIP payloads through 73 archive references, with
maximum nesting depth eight. It reads 2,363 occurrences of strict numeric
binary transition tables, including 14 repository files. There are 583
distinct byte strings and 573 distinct labeled transition functions.
Root-free canonicalization gives 422 action-graph classes with a vertex
reaching every working state, plus two disconnected synthetic controls of
sizes six and nine. A four-state positive control is included among the 422.
These counts are not counts of successful consistency machines.

The literal census recognizes \file{.tm} files and strict five-column
numeric tables in \file{.txt}, \file{.tsv}, and \file{.csv}; it does not
pretend to enumerate every table a Python program might generate or every
table encoded only as a Lean or JSON expression. The manuscripts and formal
proof directories were reviewed separately for their substantive designs.

\subsection{The actual 195-state frontier}

There are 37 literal 195-state occurrences, 14 labeled functions, and nine
action-graph classes. Three classes are supported constructions in the
reports; the other six are explicitly rejected diagnostics. The three
supported designs must all be included in the baseline:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrrrr@{}}
\toprule
Design&Sites&AX word&Readers&Folds&States&Startup\\\midrule
PA195, diagonal&3,700&191&68&7&195&58,154\\
195S, separate head&3,673&190&67&6&195&58,154\\
195C, separate + Combined&3,649&190&67&6&195&57,922\\
\textbf{PA195DC, this report}&\textbf{3,676}&191&68&7&\textbf{195}&57,922\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Original PA195 saves a seventh reader state in a 202-control unfolding.
195S uses a shorter, separate-diagonal-head emitter and a 201-control
unfolding with six folds. 195C combines that emitter with a changed Shift
and MP controller. The new PA195DC uses the original \emph{bundled}
diagonal emitter with the changed controller. Its source is 24 instructions
shorter than original PA195, but it is not the shortest source among the
four designs; source length and physical state count are different metrics.

The new graph is absent from all 422 spanning classes under canonical
comparison that even ignores the designated start control. Neither tape
reflection, simultaneous exchange of read/write bits, nor their combination
makes it equal to a supplied class. The retained canonical hash and complete
comparison receipt are in \file{comparison/}. The change is more than a
relabeling: the register-command transition relation itself changes.

\subsection{Larger machines and nonadditive reductions}

James Chang's PA209 predecessor and the U209 ancestor of the U208 reports
are different source programs. The U208 reduction merges native roles
172 and 211 using entry history and an existing helper. Repeated reports
of this fold do not yield repeated savings; later helper choices give
20 designated U208 action graphs. Those roles are already absent from
the PA196 lineage, so the old fold cannot simply be subtracted again.

The PA196 frontier includes alternate head orders, binary associations,
physical register exchanges, a forced-zero jump, several closing decrement
variants, H-only Shift, MP-result-in-H, and their combinations with the
diagonal and separate-head calculi. Smaller source DAGs sometimes lose a
helper or need a longer scanner. A seven-zero source address can be mistaken
for a marker; repairing the scanner can restore the original state count.
The closing CD3--CD6 proposals all change the same transition and therefore
are alternatives. A zero-jump at a different row can be composed only after
checking the resulting full machine.

Among the failed 195-state classes are a two-rotation quotient with 93 failed
source contracts, two physical folds with 714 and 777 failures, a seven-zero
scanner error, a separate-head scanner with 96 failed unrestricted contracts,
and an incorrect zero-jump opcode. These refute their advertised simulations;
they are not universal impossibility proofs about every interpretation of
those tables. Two other rejected Shift layouts give stronger evidence:
they halt on the blank tape after 43,563 steps.

The eight \file{pa195-*} proof directories develop successive formal layers
for the original PA195 quotient, not eight distinct designs. The native-table
any-tape halting theorem in \file{pa195-global} explicitly stops short of
dense conjugacy and the arithmetic consistency endpoint \cite{formalstatus}.

\section{The new literal construction}
\label{sec:construction}

\subsection{Six row changes and a shorter command tail}

Start from the supplied diagonal PA195 \cite{pa195}. Keep its complete AX
grammar, its 191-token word, all parameter assignments and emitted formulas,
and the seven contextual reader folds. In native coordinates change exactly
the following six transitions:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}rrll@{}}
\toprule
State&Read&Old action&New action\\\midrule
38&0&$0R28$&$0R11$\\
38&1&$1R20$&$1R122$\\
164&1&$1R98$&$1R130$\\
214&0&$0R188$&$0R312$\\
214&1&$1R312$&$1R20$\\
312&0&$0R11$&$0R188$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

All other native rows are unchanged. The two source DAG substitutions are
\[
 \texttt{node163}=(\texttt{node6},\texttt{node147}),
\]
\[
 \texttt{node170}=((\texttt{node36},(\texttt{node167},\texttt{node145})),
                         \texttt{node57}).
\]
These are the inherited Combined controller edits, now checked in combination
with the seven-fold diagonal allocation. Their exact binding to ordinary
register instructions is supplied, rather than inferred from the names.

Both source-graph resolvers agree on all 3,676 instructions. The first 3,661
instructions preserve the old opcode, register, and ordinal successors except
for three retargeted exits: the zero branch at 48 goes to 3665, and the jumps
at 3580 and 3660 go to 3664. The final 15 instructions implement the new MP
success continuation and Shift. No AX emission instruction changes.

\subsection{Why the count remains 195}

The unfolded allocation has 134 fixed controls and 68 AX readers. The seven
disjoint identifications, with the deleted control on the left, are
\[
\begin{gathered}
2018\mapsto2024,\quad2030\mapsto2050,\quad2038\mapsto2019,\\
2042\mapsto2028,\quad2053\mapsto2043,\quad2055\mapsto2034,
\quad2062\mapsto2036.
\end{gathered}
\]
Thus $134+68-7=195$ working controls remain. The six controller changes do
not modify any arithmetic-box row or any of the fold-state entry premises.
Nevertheless the complete folded table is rechecked; compatibility is not
assumed from the fact that each separate optimization worked elsewhere.

\begin{lemma}[Global seven-fold simulation]\label{lem:fold}
The delivered unfolded 202-state native table and PA195DC halt on exactly
the same complete binary tapes when started in native control 74 at any
integer head position. PA proves the corresponding coded finite-trace
equivalence, in particular for the blank start. The arithmetical
statement ranges over numerically coded configurations and finite traces;
the arbitrary-complete-tape formulation is external, and its local proof
allows every outside-tape extension.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $\sigma$ be the displayed quotient map. For each pair $(a,b)$, every
incoming transition to $a$ writes zero and moves right; every incoming
transition to $b$ writes one and moves right. Neither is initial. Induction
on a parent trace therefore gives $t(h-1)=0$ at $a$ and $t(h-1)=1$ at $b$.
The checker verifies all 22 incoming rows establishing these premises.

For each of the 14 controls and both current bits, the final simultaneously
folded table reproduces the parent transition's write, head displacement,
and projected successor. All reads and writes in these 28 cases remain in
the two cells $h-1,h$, and the entire endpoint tape agrees. The durations
are ten cases of length one, seventeen of length three, and one of length
five. Every other transition commutes with $\sigma$ in one step. Hence
each reachable parent step is simulated by one to five child steps.

Concatenation gives forward halting preservation. Conversely, if the child
halts at time $T$ but the parent has not halted within $T$ steps, its first
$T+1$ defined steps concatenate to a child run longer than $T$. All blocks
are positive and their intermediate controls are nonhalting. Determinism
contradicts the asserted halt. This is a bounded finite-trace argument;
its lookup, invariant, and concatenation functions are primitive recursive
and its inductions are available in PA.
\end{proof}

\section{The unchanged numerical calculus}
\label{sec:calculus}

Using the Cantor pairing $p$, formulas are numerical codes with constructors
\[
 I(A,B)=4p(A,B),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,\quad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
Zero is truth and $1=M(0,0)$ is the designated false code. Write
$A\to B$ for $I(A,B)$, $\neg A=I(A,1)$, and use the classical
abbreviations for conjunction and $\exists x_a$. The six retained heads
are the following universally parameterized numerical schemas.

\begin{table}[ht]\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}cl@{}}
\toprule Head&Formula\\\midrule
1&$(\exists x_D\,U(D,H))\to H$\\
2&$U(D,U(A,H))\to U(A,U(D,H))$\\
3&$M(H,D)\to(E(H,A)\to M(A,D))$\\
4&$E(H,D)\to(M(A,H)\to M(A,D))$\\
5&$\exists x_D\,E(H,D)$\\
6&$U(D,A)\to(U(D,A\to H)\to U(D,H))$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{The six unchanged numerical heads.}\label{tab:heads}
\end{table}

There is also the dominated propositional schema
\[
 \rho_8(D,A,H,u)=((H\to u)\to D)\to((D\to H)\to(A\to H)),
 \qquad u\le D,
\]
and the guarded diagonal bundle explicitly defined in
Section~\ref{sec:pa-calibration}. The inference rules are modus ponens and
generalization of a theorem at any numerical label. No extra equality rule,
truth oracle, or arbitrary-tautology rule is implicit in this specification.
The source verifier proves the complete emitted stack and gate as identities
in a free algebra, not by testing small numerical instances.
