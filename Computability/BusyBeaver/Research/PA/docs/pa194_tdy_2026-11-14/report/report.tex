\begin{abstract}
We give an explicit deterministic, single-tape, binary Turing machine with
194 working states, started on the entirely blank two-way tape. It relocates
the logical registers $T,D,Y$ through three physical block positions in the
primary PA194 implementation. All 388 transitions are supplied. The complete
source program is preserved under the new representation. Its 4,780 symbolic
source-step contracts pass in Python and JavaScript. A further literal-tape
checker derives the register-file sweep lemmas directly from the candidate
rows and checks all 4,780 outcomes with positive affine physical durations.
A literal 57,594-step startup reaches exactly the required whole-tape frame.
Combining this transport with the primary packet's ordinary mathematical PA
calibration gives
\[
 \PA\vdash \bigl(\neg\Halt(\mathrm{PA194TDY})\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
\]
This is the requested 194-state fallback. We did not establish a machine with
fewer than 194 states. We also isolate why the existing 193-state runtime
fold cannot be repaired merely by reallocating the physical registers: its
blank bootstrap loses the operation that creates a fresh rightmost marker.
The comparison is pinned to a specified repository snapshot and distinguishes
proposed consistency machines from auxiliary tables and rejected candidates.
The result is an ordinary mathematical proof with executable certificates;
it is not a new end-to-end Lean proof or an internal iPRA formalization.
\end{abstract}

\section{What the comparison actually compares}

The filed catalogue records 7,130 historical literal-table occurrences,
786 distinct byte strings, and 535 transition-graph classes. Of those classes,
49 have 194 states, 22 have 195 states, and 102 have 196 states. These numbers
count tables, not 49 independently established consistency theorems. Some
tables are search intermediates, rejected experiments, or quoted baselines.
The incoming archives add further occurrences and graph classes; the complete
fresh comparison is recorded in the generated comparison appendix and the
machine-readable files. The historical 786 byte strings and the fresh 780
byte forms refer to different populations: the fresh scan starts from the
535 retained filed representatives, rather than every historically omitted
byte-level alias, then adds the incoming and other scoped inputs.

The resulting corpus has 3,442 recognized table occurrences, 780 byte forms,
642 full spanning graph classes, and ten additional disconnected byte forms.
There are 82 spanning 194-state classes: 40 principal or historically supported
constructions, seven supplementary constructions, 25 research auxiliaries,
four seeds and six rejected candidates. PA194TDY matches none of the frozen
tables, including 29,294 candidate-sized reachable components in the broader
corpus. These categories record supplied evidence, rather than 82 newly
proved consistency theorems.

For each literal table, the comparison checks the rows, control count, and
external terminal targets. It canonicalizes the ordered two-successor graph by
traversal, preserving each transition's written symbol and direction. To
avoid false novelty caused by a changed starting label, it compares all
graph-spanning roots. It also tests global tape reflection and global symbol
exchange. These are finite syntactic checks, not tests of semantic halting
equivalence. A machine beginning in a different control can have a different
blank computation even when the unrooted table is identical.

This distinction matters in the supplied material: some tables are called
``dense'' even though control $0$ is not their actual start. It also matters
when reading counts of historical experiments. The presence of a table with
193 row-defined controls does not establish a 193-state blank-input
consistency machine.

\section{The changed physical design}

The underlying register program has logical register order
\[
 (N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,Z).
\]
Here $Z$ denotes the register called $L$ in the inherited primary source;
only the notation is changed.
The old and new physical files, written in order from block $0$ to block $8$,
are
\[
 (W,Z,T,D,A,Y,N,H,X)
 \quad\longrightarrow\quad
 (W,Z,Y,T,A,D,N,H,X).
\]
Thus a proof-store register, a formula-parameter register, and a work register
move together. This is a representation change in the tape-level machine;
the logical source instructions retain their original meanings.

Only four native rows change. A row is written as
$(q,b)\mapsto(w,d,q')$.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ccc@{}}
\toprule Read pair & Primary action & PA194TDY action\\\midrule
$(41,1)$ & $(0,R,25)$ & $(0,R,48)$\\
$(61,1)$ & $(0,R,25)$ & $(0,R,48)$\\
$(82,1)$ & $(0,R,33)$ & $(0,R,25)$\\
$(144,1)$ & $(0,R,48)$ & $(0,R,33)$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
All other rows retain their exact native actions. Dense renumbering is then
performed by breadth-first discovery from native control $74$, visiting the
$0$ transition before the $1$ transition. The label map is supplied.

The four edits are active even during the initial blank computation: before
the first initialized frame they occur respectively 11, 1, 33, and 2 times.
Their first occurrences, with time zero denoting the initial configuration,
are at times 9,259, 9,799, 1,438, and 15,365. Thus this construction does not
obtain a different file merely by changing an unvisited transition.

No arithmetic block has been changed. The selector entry controls for physical
positions $0,\ldots,8$ are
\[
 (51,36,25,48,32,33,22,55,60).
\]
The four edits redirect the appropriate logical register operations to the
new entries. The internal chain that scans the physical file is preserved.
This distinction is essential: globally renaming every occurrence of a
selector control would change the scanner itself and would not implement the
claimed relocation.

This changes recurring physical work, not just initialization. For example,
access to $T$ now crosses the block holding $Y$, which previously lay to its
right. The length of that block depends on the current value of $Y$. Accesses
to $D$ and $Y$ likewise traverse different variable-length prefixes. The
operational distinction is therefore a changed memory representation and
changed register-access paths. Exact graph nonidentity alone would be too
weak a reason to describe a design as materially different. This remains a
variant of the same interpreter architecture and proof calculus, and its
relation to the earlier AD allocation is explicit.

The old initialization takes 58,154 literal steps; the new one takes 57,594.
The reduction of 560 startup steps is an observed exact prefix difference,
not a claim of uniformly faster proof search. Different register values can
produce different costs for the relocated operations.

\section{Verification and its exact scope}

\subsection{Reconstruction and source graph}
The primary dense table and its label map are retained with their original
bytes. The checker converts them to native labels, applies exactly the four
listed edits, verifies the resulting native file, and independently constructs
the dense output. It checks 194 controls, 388 complete rows, the unique halt
label, the 194 graph-reachable controls, and all declared SHA-256 values.

The archive contains the primary source's 3,673 path-labelled instruction
sites and its resolved instruction array. The checker recomputes the control
flow from the tree-path rules for increment, decrement, break, continue, and
halt, then compares the entire resolved array. The source algorithm is
unchanged; the physical representation map is changed.

\subsection{All source outcomes}
For every instruction site, the symbolic checker starts from the complete
prescribed frame. A decrement contributes one zero-register outcome and one
positive-register outcome. Every other site contributes its corresponding
single contract. The result is 4,780 checked outcomes.

The symbolic tape contains exact finite bits, affine-length constant runs,
and an abstract full register file. Literal controller transitions are
executed symbolically. Register marking, increment, and decrement use the
unchanged arithmetic-block contracts. The endpoint includes the correct
source path, complete tape representation, register effect, and head phase;
it is not just a check of selected register values.

Both implementations return
\[
 \text{contracts}=4780,\quad
 \text{maximum symbolic iterations}=497,\quad
 \text{total symbolic iterations}=1{,}479{,}348.
\]
The latter two quantities count symbolic rewrite iterations, including
normalization and accelerated runs. They are not bounds of 497 literal TM
steps per source instruction. Literal durations depend on the represented
register values and header sizes.

The Python implementation reuses the supplied arithmetic-oracle checker with
an independently assembled list of new contracts. The JavaScript checker is
an independent implementation of the same symbolic rules. Agreement between
the two is useful implementation evidence. It does not turn their common
imported arithmetic-block assumptions into a new proof-assistant theorem.
The imported Lean runtime file is included for inspection and attribution;
it is not advertised as a standalone Lean build of the new machine.
Its generic symbolic-execution theorem takes \texttt{BoxLemmas} as explicit
hypotheses. Merely including that file is not a discharge of those hypotheses;
this construction uses the unchanged physical block lemmas of the inherited
engine. The additional check below derives their required tape-sweep content
directly from the candidate's rows.

\subsection{Independent literal-tape derivation}
The checker \file{verification/literal/check_tdy.py} uses the primary packet's
separate literal symbolic engine. It does not use arithmetic-box oracles.
Writing $B(v)=0\,1^{v+1}$ and $C(v)=1\,0\,1^v$, it executes the actual
candidate rows on one arbitrary block in opaque left and right contexts.
This derives four sweeps: $B\to B$ to the right, $B\to C$ to the right,
$B\to B$ to the left, and $C\to B$ to the left. Each has physical duration
$v+2$ and corresponding signed head motion. Concatenation then proves the
finite-list versions. For a finite list $A$, write $B(A)$ and $C(A)$ for
the corresponding blockwise concatenations. The word identity
$C(A)1=1B(A)$, and its reversal,
follow by the empty-list and cons cases.

Every source outcome is then derived using only literal transitions,
constant-symbol loop induction, those list sweeps and exact word identities.
Two separate control-flow resolvers also agree with the frozen instruction
array. The recorded result is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \text{source contracts}&=4{,}780,\\
 \text{symbolic iterations}&=1{,}064{,}568,\\
 \text{maximum iterations per contract}&=397,\\
 \text{minimum physical-duration constant}&=3.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Each physical duration is an affine expression in the header-gap parameter,
nine register values, the first spare value and the remaining spare-list
width. Every coefficient is nonnegative and the constant is at least three.
These are physical durations, unlike the checker's iteration counts. The
per-outcome expressions, four list-lemma traces and a separate bytearray
blank-start receipt are retained under \file{verification/literal/recorded/}.
This is a direct mathematical certificate against the new rows. It still
does not constitute a newly kernel-checked proof-assistant theorem.

\subsection{Exact blank initialization}
The literal simulator begins with an empty set of one-cells, native control
$74$, and head position $0$. At step 57,594 it reaches native control $84$,
head position $-2$, and source site 25. Its physical register values are
\[
 (0,0,0,0,0,0,2,0,0),
\]
followed by 26 spare blocks of value one. Under the new map the logical
register vector is $(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$. The complete set of one-cells equals
the constructed target frame; all other cells are zero. The receipt records
that finite set, allowing the whole-tape equality to be replayed.

\section{Why the 193-state runtime fold still fails}
\label{sec:obstruction}

The supplied runtime construction identifies native $301$ with $88$, redirects
all incoming targets accordingly, deletes control $301$, and changes
\[
 (88,0)\mapsto(0,L,51),\qquad
 (51,0)\mapsto(0,R,122).
\]
This has 193 working controls and passes the source-frame contracts. It is
not a blank-input consistency machine. The difference between a runtime
contract and a correct bootstrap is decisive here.

Originally the marker scanner's exit is
$(51,0)\mapsto(0,R,16)$. On a represented register file, the following block
starts with a $1$, and
\[
 \delta(16,1)=\delta(122,1)=(0,L,1).
\]
The change is therefore invisible in the relevant initialized-frame case.
During bootstrap, however, the next square can still be blank. The old action
\[
 \delta(16,0)=(1,L,1)
\]
creates the next marker, whereas $\delta(122,0)=(0,L,137)$ does not.

This obstruction concerns the explicitly specified fold and the family of
physical-register reallocations with the existing blank entry. It is not a
lower bound of 194 on all PA-consistency machines. Changing the boot protocol,
finding a different initialized invariant, or redesigning the arithmetic
engine may still produce a valid 193-state or smaller machine.

\section{Other reduction avenues and limits of the search}

We also explored occurrence-level reassociation of the primary AX expression
tree, scored by the number of compiled reader controls minus available local
history folds. A 7,438-proposal exploratory run did not improve the score 194.
The known local contraction template was extended to include arithmetic-box
controls and larger tape windows; no additional primary-table contraction
was found within that template. Neither computation is a global minimality
argument.

Logical changes were reviewed as a separate search dimension. Premise
exchange in the equality heads is sound because positive implication derives
\[
 (P\to(Q\to R))\to(Q\to(P\to R)).
\]
Earlier packets already search such variants, however, and their compilation
does not automatically match 194 states. Changing the witness parameter in
$\exists x_D E(H,D)$ to $\exists x_D E(A,D)$ preserves the axiom schema under
independent parameter selection, but the unchanged association compiled above
the target. The resulting formula-level observations are not delivered as new
small machines.

A tempting dualization of the first logical head was rejected. The head
$(\exists x_D\,U(D,H))\to H$ helps supply explosion for the distinguished
contradiction atom. An argument replacing it by its classical dual can
circularly assume the explosion principle it needs to recover. The research
notes give the separating-valuation analysis. No candidate relying on that
unjustified replacement is included as a consistency machine.

The ZFC work contributes reusable ideas about history-dependent folds and
exact source frames. The ConciseIPRA work contributes strategies for reducing
logical interfaces. Neither permits replacing the consistency target by a
different theory: the PA calibration used here includes proof production for
finite comprehension of arbitrary arithmetic formulas. A short equation-only
proof checker is not automatically a smaller machine for $\Con(\PA)$.

\begin{Verbatim}[fontsize=\small]
python3 verify.py
node verification/verify_tdy.cjs
python3 -B verification/literal/check_tdy.py
python3 comparison/verify_novelty.py
\end{Verbatim}

source outcomes, and exact startup. The third independently checks literal
list sweeps, positive-duration source contracts and blank initialization.
The comparison command checks the new
table against the included canonical comparison evidence; its output states
the scope of the catalogue. The research subdirectory contains the specified
193-state obstruction certificate and its replay program.
