Research reproduction and scope
===============================

From the package root:

  node verification/verify_tdy.cjs
  node research/runtime193_permutations.cjs

The first command reconstructs the four-row PA194TDY transformation,
checks the unchanged complete source instruction map, checks all 4,780
symbolic source outcomes, verifies the exact complete blank startup,
checks the two table hashes, and writes the native/dense tables and receipt.
Its symbolic arithmetic operations inherit the unchanged M6 arithmetic-box
contracts. It is not a new proof-assistant endpoint.

The second command proves the uniform finite-prefix certificate for all
9! physical layouts of the specified 193-state runtime fold at native
blank start 74. It also explicitly checks the 8! layouts fixing slot 6.
All 40,320 enumerated runs enter the certified outward-ray configuration;
there are no timeout or unclassified cases. State 51 is reached at time
79 + pi(7), and the perpetual state-35 writing loop begins at time
85 + pi(7). The included LaTeX fragment gives the mathematical argument.

Optional bounded contraction search (C++17):

  g++ -O3 -std=c++17 research/action_merge_all.cpp -o /tmp/action_merge_tdy
  /tmp/action_merge_tdy artifacts/pa194_tdy.native.tm 4 80
  /tmp/action_merge_tdy artifacts/pa194_tdy.native.tm 6 150
  /tmp/action_merge_tdy artifacts/pa194_tdy.native.tm 8 200

The observed stdout files contain zero candidate pair contractions at all
three bounds. The source adapts the supplied primary PA194 packet's
search/action_merge.cpp, allowing arithmetic-box states as potential
contracted states while continuing to exclude the start state. It uses
all-tape finite-window overapproximations and bounded exact detours.
These are negative results for this sufficient finite contraction rule;
they do not establish minimality or a general lower bound.

The runtime193_rejected.native.tm file is deliberately a rejected
blank-start candidate. Its presence must not be interpreted as a new
193-state PA-consistency construction.
