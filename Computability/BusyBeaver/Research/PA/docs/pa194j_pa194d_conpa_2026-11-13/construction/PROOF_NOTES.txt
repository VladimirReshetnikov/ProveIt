Relationship to the supplied source
-----------------------------------
The source is the supplied PA194CS symmetric-Shift source (3646 sites).
All initial source paths remain identical. Ten jump instructions become
zero tests/decrements of registers that are provably zero whenever the
instruction is reached from source25, registers(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)
=(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0).

At sites3,9,3526,3532,3544,3550,3627,3633, replace breakTo1 by decA.
At site26, replace breakTo1 by decX (the already supplied CZ idea).
At site3553, replace the final AX breakTo1 by decD.
Each new instruction's zero successor is the old jump target, and its
positive successor is the next primitive site. Source paths and total
site count stay unchanged, while the number of complete primitive
physical cases rises from4745 to4755.

These changes are generated structurally, not only patched in JSON:
node1 = (node0, decA), node22 = (decL, decX), and the single BREAK
terminal of the 67-reader AX grammar becomes decD. The Combined and
symmetric-Shift changes to nodes163,170,171 are exactly those of PA194CS.
Two independent source-path resolvers rederive the resulting full
instruction map from the generated paths.

The physical table differs from supplied PA194CS in exactly three rows:

The zero branches have identical register effects on reachable source

Proof of the zero invariant
---------------------------
For a concrete register vector v define mask(v)=sum(2^i:[v_i>0]).
The abstract states are (source site, nine-bit positivity mask).
An increment sets its register bit. A zero decrement follows the zero
branch with the mask unchanged. A positive decrement follows the positive
branch with its bit either retained or cleared. Jumps preserve the mask.
This transition relation soundly overapproximates the concrete step,
by elementary properties of natural successor and predecessor.

The builder independently recomputes the reachable abstract closure of
both original CS and new J from (25,1). They are equal and contain exactly
263180 pairs. At every pair in this set the two abstract successor lists
are identical. Every successor belongs to the set. All ten replaced
jump sites have their tested bit absent at every associated mask. The
actual per-site masks and a closure digest are stored in zero_invariant.json.

Induction on the concrete trace length keeps its mask in the finite
closed set. At a changed site the selected register is0, so the new
instruction makes exactly the old register update (none) and takes the
old successor. Other instructions are literally unchanged. Hence the
old and new source configurations are identical after every source step,
and they halt iff one another. This proof is formalizable in PA: the
closure is an explicit finite list, closure checks are fixed finite
arithmetic facts, and the sole unbounded argument is ordinary induction.
It does not use Con(PA), reflection, or a hypothesis of actual nonhalting.

Physical realization
--------------------
independent_verify.py imports neither the source compiler nor any
arithmetic-box oracle. It uses literal transitions and four generic
list-sweep inductions rederived from the actual table. It then checks all
4755 primitive source cases over nine independent arbitrary natural
register values, arbitrary natural extra header gap, arbitrary first
spare and arbitrary finite remainder of the spare list. Both outcomes of
every decrement are checked, including the ten newly introduced positive
branches. Exact whole-tape equality is required at each next source frame.
The duration certificate is positive, so no source step can be simulated
by zero physical progress. The source-frame start control is native84,
with the normal minimum header distance48.

A separate bytearray interpreter runs exactly60062 steps from the blank
start and compares the entire tape (not just registers) to source25,
head -2, N=2, all other logical registers0, and26 one-valued spare blocks.
The complete finite support is recorded in independent_blank_seed.json.

Concatenating the positive finite simulation segments maps finite source
halts to physical halts. Conversely, if the physical run first halts at
T, T+1 nonhalting source steps would produce a longer nonhalting physical
prefix by determinism, a contradiction. Thus physical halting and source
halting are equivalent by a PA-formalizable finite-trace argument. Composing
this with the preceding invariant gives PA194J and supplied PA194CS the
same blank halting behavior in PA. The logical calibration of PA194CS to
Con(PA) is inherited; these new scripts do not supply a Lean/Rocq proof of
that calibration or of the final biconditional.

What has NOT been obtained
-------------------------
No machine with fewer than194states was obtained in this subtask.
The table is not a control renaming, global reflection, or symbol-complement
version of any of the8root-free194-state graph classes found by the corpus
agent. It changes executed controller transitions and the reachable proof
search implementation. State count is unchanged; no runtime improvement
is claimed (startup is60062 vs57768 for supplied CS).

Search findings
---------------

therefore cannot justify that deletion.
