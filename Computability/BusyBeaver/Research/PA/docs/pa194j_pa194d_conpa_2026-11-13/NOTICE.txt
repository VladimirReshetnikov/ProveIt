Provenance and attribution
==========================

This research packet compares and extends material from
https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt
at revision 17f92105e2bc4317feae6c219cb2143437b482ff.
The comparison ledger records all supplied packet identities and paths.

The PA194J and PA194D tables, source refinements, new Gamma(D) association,
finite-invariant arguments, new report, and task-specific orchestration
were assembled for this research request. They incorporate the supplied
interpreter, cached controller, source language, numerical calculus, and
existing finite-history folding methods. This packet does not claim those
inherited components as new.

James Chang's PA196 Program.lean and related upstream machines are retained
as source dependencies. The original James Chang and ProveIt notices are
in construction/reference/ and logical_emitter/reference/.

The independent symbolic runtime, arithmetic/interval source resolvers,
and list-sweep proof methods are the preserved implementations from the
supplied incoming PA packets. Their README and the James Chang, ProveIt,
and Andrew James Wade license notices are retained in each reference
directory. The new drivers bind those engines to the delivered literal
tables and expose their precise proof scopes.

The C++ decoder screening and occurrence-search infrastructure is adapted
from docs/incoming/pa194C_conpa_2026-11-13 (2).zip, members under
pa194C_conpa/search/grammar/. New search inputs, logical emitter seeds,
zero-test interfaces, closing-leaf mutations, and adaptive pool admission
are documented in research/search/README.txt. No search result alone is
treated as a machine-correctness certificate.

The ordinary logical calibration texts in references/logical_calibration/
are supplied research dependencies, retained with their names and context.
The new report explicitly distinguishes these inherited arguments from
the new source invariants, selector proof, table construction, and checks.

No new end-to-end Lean or Rocq formalization is included. The Program.lean
snapshot is an input source tree, not a formal proof of either new machine.
