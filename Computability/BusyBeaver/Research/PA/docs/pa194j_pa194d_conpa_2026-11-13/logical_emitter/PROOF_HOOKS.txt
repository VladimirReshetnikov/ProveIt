Proof boundaries and arithmetic transport
=========================================

This supplementary construction changes an emitted diagonal head, not only
physical code. Its logical proof obligation is the calibration of the
separate Gamma(D) emitter. The symbolic word certificate shows exactly
three copyA -> copyD changes at macro-token indices 121,122,123, and binds
the remaining word, guards and parameter reads. The accompanying report's
logical section supplies the syntax and parameter-coverage argument.

The machine implementation is bound to that new source in four stages:

1. Expand the frozen grammar and resolve its full primitive source graph.
   A second grammar uses the original accepted association with precisely
   the same new word. Their complete instruction maps must be identical;
   word equality alone is not used to justify structured-jump preservation.

2. Relative to the Gamma(D) source with ordinary jumps, replace source26 by
   dec X and source3553 by dec D. The exact old/new positivity closures are
   computed from (site25,mask1). They are equal (263244 pairs), closed, and
   have identical successors at every pair. The tested bit is absent at
   each changed site. Concrete source configurations are therefore equal
   after every step by induction. This is a PA-formalizable finite invariant.

3. Compile to a 203-state parent (134 fixed +69 readers), then apply nine
   disjoint identifications. All incoming transitions establish the required
   history bit, and all four context/read-bit cases per fold are replayed
   against the complete final table. Each raw step has a positive finite
   folded simulation (maximum five steps in exceptional local cases).
   This is an all-tape quotient from the designated ordinary start control.

4. Independently rederive the physical list-sweep lemmas from the actual
   194-state table. Check every source branch with independent arbitrary
   natural register values and arbitrary finite spare tail. Every simulated
   source step has positive duration. Check complete blank initialization,
   including all tape cells, before source iteration begins.

A finite source halt yields a physical halt by concatenation. Conversely,
if the physical machine first halts at time T, a source prefix of T+1
nonhalting steps would produce a strictly longer nonhalting physical prefix,
contradicting determinism. This establishes source/machine halting
 equivalence by ordinary PA induction. Composing it with the separately
proved Gamma(D) calculus calibration yields the requested PA statement.

The executable certificates establish finite and symbolic mathematical
facts. They are not an exported Lean/Rocq endpoint, and do not replace the
logical calibration proof with a Python assertion.
