\begin{abstract}
A verified construction below 194 states was not obtained. The requested
fallback is supplied by PA194N6, a complete binary, one-tape, blank-start
machine with 194 working states and 388 transitions. It combines two
previously separate substantive changes: coefficient-six numerical formula
constructors and a modus-ponens command that does not push a dummy zero.
The first changes every constructor computation; the second changes the
stack effect of successful detachment. This is a new composition of supplied
ideas, not a claim to have invented either idea separately.

The new table is absent from all 30 recognized 194-state control-graph
classes in the pinned corpus, even under arbitrary control renaming,
a graph-spanning change of root, reflection, and global symbol exchange.
The comparison covers 44 placed research packets and three current incoming
archives. It also identifies the purportedly separate PA194X and PA194CSZ
machines as byte-identical.

The accompanying ordinary mathematical argument proves the PA-internal
consistency equivalence by combining the inherited numerical-calculus
calibration with a command compiler valid at coefficient six and a complete
physical binding. Two symbolic implementations check all 4,877 primitive
source outcomes. The independent implementation derives its register-list
lemmas from literal transitions and verifies the entire 58,320-step blank
startup. These results do not constitute a new end-to-end Lean or Rocq proof;
the distinction and the inherited proof dependencies are explicit.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result and the exact meaning of the claim}
\label{sec:result}

The working-state set is $\{0,\ldots,193\}$, the initial state is $0$,
and $194$ is the separate terminal halt label. The head starts at cell zero
of an all-zero tape indexed by $\mathbb Z$. Every defined transition writes
one bit and moves exactly one cell left or right. The transition function
is total on the 194 working states and the two symbols; no transition is
defined at halt. The complete dense table is printed in
Appendix~\ref{app:table} and supplied as \file{machine/machine.tm}.

\begin{theorem}[Ordinary PA-formalizable correctness]
Let $M$ be the literal PA194N6 table in this archive. For a fixed ordinary
primitive-recursive proof predicate for first-order Peano arithmetic,
\[
 \PA\vdash \Halt(M,\mathrm{blank})
       \longleftrightarrow
       \exists p\,\Prf_{\PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner).
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \boxed{\PA\vdash
       \neg\Halt(M,\mathrm{blank})\longleftrightarrow\Con(\PA).}
\]
\end{theorem}

The proof uses the supplied ordinary syntactic calibration of the
separate-diagonal-head calculus to PA, with its full argument retained and
attributed in the archive. It then supplies and checks the new composition's
command meaning, source binding, literal implementation, and initialization.
The inherited calibration is a mathematical dependency, not a newly added
axiom of PA. The theorem is an ordinary mathematical PA-provability claim;
this release does not contain a newly kernel-checked end-to-end proof of it.
In particular, Python success and a conditional iPRA endpoint are not being
presented as such a kernel proof.

The theorem concerns finite coded traces and finite coded proofs, including
their interpretation in nonstandard models of PA. It does not rely on
observing a long nonhalting run. PA consistency is not an extra hypothesis
needed to prove the biconditional. It is the usual additional assumption
under which one concludes actual nonhalting in the standard model.

in the complete proof search's running time.

\subsection{What is materially different about PA194N6}

The difference is relative to the complete pinned corpus, including its
auxiliary tables. Compared with PA194N, the new machine builds implication
codes $6\pair(a,b)$ rather than $4\pair(a,b)$ and correspondingly changes
membership, universal, and equality codes. Compared with M6, successful MP
retains the old stack instead of pushing a zero. These changes operate in
recurring logical computations. They are not merely renaming controls,
reflecting the tape, changing unreachable rows, or altering a bootstrap delay.

For a concrete arithmetic difference, $\pair(1,0)=1$, so the two implication
codes are $4$ and $6$. For a concrete stack difference, at coefficient six
consider a successful MP store with $(t,w,d,h)=(0,1,0,12)$ and parameter
$x=1$. Since $I_6(0,1)=12$, PA194N6 returns
$(1,1,1,0)$. The M6 dummy-push rule returns $(1,2,1,0)$ because
$\pair(0,1)=2$. These are command-interface examples, not claims that a
particular inconsistent store occurs in the actual consistent blank run.

The current-corpus comparison finds no match to PA194N6 among all 30 prior
194-state graphs, allowing arbitrary control names, any root that spans the
graph, left/right reflection, and global read/write symbol exchange. The
last transformation is used only to strengthen the novelty test: the actual
machine always starts on zero tape. A finite graph comparison establishes
structural distinctness; the mathematical correctness proof is separate.

\section{Literal construction and exact source binding}
\label{sec:construction}

The native labels retain the inherited interpreter's numbering. Native start
is 74, native halt is 254, and native source-frame entry is 84. The dense
table is the checked breadth-first relabeling from the start, with halt
assigned 194. Both formats and their explicit bijection are supplied.

\subsection{The coefficient-six parent and its eight simultaneous folds}

The M6 parent has 135 fixed controls, including a three-increment reader,
and 67 AX readers. Eight compatible history-sensitive folds give
\[
                 135+67-8=194.
\]
The coefficient-six multiplier uses the tree $((i,D),(i,D))$, where
$i$ is increment of the formula-top register and $D=(i,i)$. All incoming
edges to $D$ write one and move right. Another decoder, native 41, is
entered only after a written zero. This existing history bit distinguishes
the contexts and permits $41\mapsto99$ with no new marker.

The added native control 330 has actions $0R61$ and $1R99$; native 190
dispatches both bits to 330, preserving the bit and moving right. The
retained control 99 uses $0L30$ and $1L2035$. Four local cases reproduce
the old 41/99 effects in three steps, restoring the history cell. Together
with the seven inherited AX folds, all 32 local cases are checked on the
final simultaneous table. Individual fold correctness is not assumed to
imply compatibility automatically.

\subsection{The new MP bypass}

Starting with M6, change exactly one native transition:
\[
              (164,1):\quad 1R98\quad\longmapsto\quad1R114.
\]
Control 98 enters the push-and-copy offset routine; control 114 enters its
copy-offset suffix directly. The physical register arithmetic and AX reader
folds are retained. No row in a register arithmetic box changes.

At the source level the change is
\[
 \mathtt{node163}=(\mathtt{node6},\mathtt{node55})
 \quad\longmapsto\quad
 (\mathtt{node6},\mathtt{node54}),
\]
where $\mathtt{node55}=(\mathtt{node49},\mathtt{node54})$ and
\file{node49} is the stack push. The exact M6 graph has 3,795 sites.
Its interval $[3756,3775)$ consists of the 19 removed primitive push
instructions. The only incoming edge from outside this interval is
$3750\to3756$. The new graph has 3,776 sites. The builder checks all
retained instructions and all successor indices under the explicit
relocation, including bypassing that entry to the retained suffix.
It does not infer source identity merely from a macro word's text.

The retained copy suffix has two loops. The first decrements $D$ and
increments both $T$ and $X$; the second decrements $X$ and increments $D$.
From $(T,D,X)=(0,b,0)$, after $j\le b$ iterations of the first loop the
store is $(j,b-j,j)$. After $j\le b$ iterations of the second it is
$(b,j,b-j)$. Thus it returns $(b,b,0)$, preserving the arbitrary stack and
all other registers, in $7b+2$ primitive source steps. These elementary
induction invariants explain why the bypass implements exactly the changed
MP contract rather than merely making the source shorter.

\subsection{Constructor semantics}

Registers are ordered $(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)$. The constructor macro pops
a Cantor stack entry and changes
\[
 (T,W)=(a,\pair(b,w))\quad\longmapsto\quad(6\pair(b,a),w),
\]
when entered with $X=Y=0$; it restores these scratch registers and
preserves the others. Its pairing prefix is inherited. Its final loop
consumes $Y$ and increments $T$ six times per unit. For arbitrary starting
$t,y$, the invariant
\[
                    T+6Y=t+6y
\]
and induction on the initial $y$ prove termination with $T=t+6y,Y=0$.
This supplies the macro meaning beyond the primitive implementation checks.

The same constructor is used in AX and in the MP equality test. A mixed
machine with coefficient six in AX and coefficient four in MP would be
incorrect. The source binding and free-constructor emitter audit verify
that both use the same coefficient. The 61 multiplier bodies of the
original source are six increments long; the N bypass deletes a different
block and does not change those bodies.

\section{The coefficient-six numerical calculus and its PA calibration}
\label{sec:calculus}

Define Cantor pairing and four numerical constructors by
\begin{align*}
 \pair(a,b)&=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b,\\
 I_k(a,b)&=k\pair(a,b),& M_k(a,b)&=k\pair(a,b)+1,\\
 U_k(i,a)&=k\pair(a,i)+2,& E_k(a,b)&=k\pair(a,b)+3.
\end{align*}
Here $M_k$ in a two-argument expression denotes membership coding, not a
Turing machine. The actual machine uses $k=6$. Zero denotes truth and one
the target contradiction. Set $N_k(a)=I_k(a,1)$ and use the classical
implicational definitions of conjunction and existential quantification.
All variable labels are numerical, and formulas may have free variables.

The calculus $\mathcal C_k$ has modus ponens and generalization of theorems,
the six inherited PA196 heads, the separated diagonal schema
$\Gamma_k(a)=I_k(a,U_k(a,a))$, the guarded separation head, and
\[
 \rho_k(D,A,H,u)
  =((H\to u)\to D)\to((D\to H)\to(A\to H)),\qquad u\le D.
\]
Arrows here mean $I_k$. The six heads are, with existential quantification
read in the coefficient-$k$ constructors,
\begin{align*}
 h_1(D,H)&=(\exists x_D\,U_k(D,H))\to H,\\
 h_2(D,A,H)&=U_k(D,U_k(A,H))\to U_k(A,U_k(D,H)),\\
 h_3(D,A,H)&=M_k(H,D)\to(E_k(H,A)\to M_k(A,D)),\\
 h_4(D,A,H)&=E_k(H,D)\to(M_k(A,H)\to M_k(A,D)),\\
 h_5(D,H)&=\exists x_D E_k(H,D),\\
 h_6(D,A,H)&=U_k(D,A)\to(U_k(D,A\to H)\to U_k(D,H)).
\end{align*}
For the guarded head, put
\begin{align*}
 P(a,b,c)&=c\to M_k(c,b),\\
 V(a,b,c)&=M_k(a,b)\to U_k(c,M_k(a,b)),\\
 S(a,b,c)&=U_k\bigl(b,(M_k(a,b)\to P(a,b,c))
                    \wedge(M_k(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
 Q(a,b,c)&=U_k(c,S(a,b,c)\to N_k(P(a,b,c))),\\
 G(a,b,c)&=N_k(V(a,b,c)\to Q(a,b,c)).
\end{align*}
Every $G(a,b,c)$ with $c\ne a,b$ is an axiom. A failed emitter guard
returns zero. This is legitimate because
$\rho_k(0,0,0,0)=0$ already gives a derivation of zero. Generalization
maps a theorem $B$ to $U_k(i,B)$ for any numerical label $i$, with no
undischarged assumptions. This completely specifies the numerical calculus.
Its primitive proof translations are given in the retained
\file{calibration/} material. The exact emission order is also verified
as free constructor terms, including the saved incoming top and the
generalization of the incoming cache. The full calculus is part of the
deliverable; it is not an unspecified assertion that the machine checks PA.

\subsection{The inherited base and the change of numerical coding}

The supplied separated-head calibration provides primitive-recursive
translations between PA refutations and $\mathcal C_4$ refutations with
correctness provable in PA\cite{primary,calibration}. The diagonal replacement,
truth-code alias, and coincident-variable cases are included in that
dependency. The following transport to coefficient six addresses two
additional issues: variable-label recoding and a numerical order constraint.

For each fixed $k\ge4$, put $F_k(0)=0$. For $n>0$, write uniquely
$n=4\pair(a,b)+r$, with $0\le r<4$, and define
\[
                F_k(n)=k\pair(F_k(a),F_k(b))+r.
\]
Both components are smaller than $n$, so primitive-recursive
course-of-values evaluation is available. The same function recodes
formula numbers and variable labels. An inverse on its image is obtained
by the analogous recursive decoder $J_k$, returning zero on unused
residues $4,\ldots,k-1$. Induction proves $J_k(F_k(n))=n$, hence injectivity.
The map fixes 0 and 1 and commutes with all four constructors. In particular,
\[
                   F_k(\Gamma_4(a))=\Gamma_k(F_k(a)).
\]
Keeping old variable labels while recoding formulas would fail at this
diagonal schema. Injectivity also preserves the guarded head's inequalities.

The map does not preserve numerical order. For example,
$40<44$ but $F_5(40)=75>55=F_5(44)$. Thus a dominated propositional
axiom cannot always be recoded as a single dominated axiom. The inherited
generic theorem \file{rho8All} gives a fixed finite derivation of the
unrestricted schema from the restricted one\cite{restricted}. Its order
premises hold for $I_k$ because
\[
                 a,b\le\pair(a,b)\le I_k(a,b).
\]
Replace each problematic axiom leaf by this finite derivation at the
recoded parameters. This yields a proof-producing translation
\[
       \PA\vdash \Refut(\PA)\longrightarrow\Refut(\mathcal C_k).
\]
The template's own restricted leaves have valid target bounds; no false
monotonicity assumption is used.

\subsection{Syntactic translation back to PA}

Interpret numerical membership as finite binary membership,
\[
 \operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\equiv
 \exists u,v\,[s=(2u+1)2^z+v\ \wedge\ v<2^z],
\]
and interpret equality, implication, and universal quantification in their
usual first-order meanings. At coefficient six, unused tags 4 and 5 decode
as truth. This makes the arithmetic interpretation total; it does not add
all unused numbers as axioms of $\mathcal C_6$.

Every component and occurring variable label of a nonzero formula code is
smaller than that code. Therefore the variable labelled $a$ is absent from
the interpretation of formula $a$, and the diagonal schema has a uniform
first-order proof. PA proves $\operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\to z<s$, so code one,
$M_k(0,0)$, translates to the refutable formula
$\operatorname{Bit}(x_0,x_0)$.

For each arithmetic formula syntax $\theta(z,\vec u)$, PA proves finite
bit comprehension by induction on $n$:
\[
 \forall n\,\exists s<2^n\,\forall z<n\,
       (\operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\leftrightarrow\theta(z,\vec u)).
\]
The proof either retains the existing code or adds $2^n$ according to
$\theta(n,\vec u)$. Substitution into this proof template is primitive
recursive on formula syntax. It is not a uniform PA truth predicate or
a reflection principle. The retained calibration uses this template for
the separation head, handles coincident variable labels separately, and
uses capture-avoiding renaming. The remaining heads have fixed logical
proof suppliers.

Consequently PA verifies a syntactic translation
\[
             \Refut(\mathcal C_k)\longrightarrow\Refut(\PA).
\]
The exceptional equality $I_k(0,0)=0$ is handled by a proof of truth
equivalent to truth implying truth; numerical stacks and formulas are not
treated as free syntax in this case. This completes the inherited radix
calibration needed at $k=6$\cite{radix}. The present construction's additional
obligation is to compile refutations into its changed command language.

\section{From the source to the literal blank-tape machine}
\label{sec:physical}

A source frame consists of the encoded source address, a zero gap with
header parameter 48, nine arbitrary natural register values encoded in
unary blocks, and an arbitrary finite nonempty spare list. For a source
address $p$, the gap length is $48+n-|p|$ for arbitrary $n\in\mathbb N$.
The contracts quantify over these data, not over a bounded sample of small
register values. Both outcomes of every decrement are checked, even when
a particular program invariant would rule one out from the seed.

The construction executor verifies 4,877 primitive outcomes for the
3,776-site source. A separate implementation resolves the source graph in
two ways, checks native/dense conjugacy, and derives four generic list-sweep
lemmas from the actual table rows. Its primitive traces import neither the
builder nor an arithmetic-box oracle. Each nonhalting contract ends at
the exact complete successor frame without passing through halt, and its
duration has a strictly positive constant term.

\input{verification.tex}

The bootstrap is checked independently of all clean-frame invariants.
After exactly 58,320 transitions the blank machine is at native control 84
with head $-2$, source site 25, logical registers $(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$,
and 26 spare blocks each containing one. The receipt records every nonzero
tape cell. Equality includes the zero exterior, not just a finite window.

\begin{lemma}[PA-internal physical transport]
PA proves that the literal PA194N6 machine halts from blank if and only
if its source program halts from this clean seed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The fixed startup and each primitive contract are finite row calculations
and unary-list inductions. PA formalizes these calculations, their frame
relations, and concatenation of coded traces. A finite source halt gives
a finite literal halt by concatenating the positive primitive segments.

For the reverse implication, suppose a literal halt occurs by time $T$.
A halt during startup contradicts its checked trace and determinism.
Otherwise, if the source has no halt in its first $T+1$ instructions,
the total increment, decrement, and jump operations give $T+1$ completed
physical segments after startup. Every segment has positive duration and
no intermediate halt, so this deterministic physical run continues beyond
$T$, a contradiction. Thus a bounded finite source halt exists.

At source time $m$ every register is bounded by its initial value plus
$m$, since each primitive increases at most one register by one. Coded
source iteration is primitive recursive. Physical list-sweep proofs and
their finite compositions use induction on natural lengths. This is a
proof about arithmetized finite traces in PA, rather than an inference from
standard-model simulation alone.
\end{proof}

The all-store proof-word compiler of Section~\ref{sec:commands}, the
calculus translations of Section~\ref{sec:calculus}, and this physical
transport yield the theorem in Section~\ref{sec:result}.

\subsection{Proof and verification boundaries}

The fresh facts are the literal table identity, one-row M6 patch, exact

distinctness, two complete symbolic primitive checks, and exact startup.
The finite certificates rely on the mathematical soundness of their

\section{What transfers from ZFC and ConciseIPRA}
\label{sec:transfer}

The ZFC lineage supplies useful engineering methods: semantic command
changes with explicit proof compilers; sharing control states through
already-written tape bits; separate accounting of source subtrees,
physical readers, and contextual folds; and whole-tape initialization
checks. Its omit-zero variants also show that the no-push principle itself
predates this packet\cite{zfc}. The new work here is the exact composition
at 194 states and its bindings, not a new claim about the general principle.

The ZFC results must retain their theory and formalization bases. A machine
for $\Con(\mathrm{ZFC})$ cannot automatically be substituted for one for
$\Con(\PA)$ merely because it uses an attractive controller. The current
ZFC README also distinguishes externally compiled K215 equivalences from
the separate internal PA endpoint for a 240-state machine. State reductions
and logical-strength calibrations are different obligations.

ConciseIPRA demonstrates that a small native calculus can derive much of
the familiar logical infrastructure from a few computational and closure
rules\cite{ipra}. Its proof-checker compression is a useful source of ideas
for finite certificate design and future formalization. Its checker size,
however, is not a binary-TM state count. Replacing the searched theory by
iPRA would also require a separately proved consistency-strength bridge
to PA; that bridge is not supplied just by a compact presentation of iPRA.
The present machine therefore retains the calibrated PA-strength numerical
calculus.
