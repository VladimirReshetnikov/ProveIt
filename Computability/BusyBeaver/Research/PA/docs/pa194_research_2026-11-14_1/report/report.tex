\begin{abstract}
No justified blank-start machine with fewer than 194 working states was
obtained. The concrete positive result is a family of three new 194-state
binary single-tape machines, PA194GD\_SF, PA194GDH\_SF and PA194GDHS\_SF.
Each has 388 literal transitions and starts on the entirely blank tape.
They change six active interpreter rows of the corresponding GD parent,
combining an entry-history selector with two guarded return shortcuts.
Every new graph is distinct from all 522 spanning 194-state graph classes
recognized in the pinned PA and incoming corpus, even with additional
cosmetic identifications.

For each exact parent $M$ and new table $N$, an elementary written proof
establishes in iPRA the bounded implications
$H_M(t)\to H_N(3t)$ and $H_N(t)\to H_M(3t)$. The proof uses literal
local identities, primitive-recursive clocks and quantifier-free induction.
The absolute consistency corollary imports the identified GD calibration
and its revised AX11H written dependency. It is not presented as a completed
Lean proof or as an independent reconstruction of that entire calibration.

The package additionally contains a fresh full-table census, an 85-design
named ledger, stronger bounded attempts to repair the 193-state runtime,
fresh state-elimination searches, and finite Hilbert certificates for two
new consequent-parameterized axiom presentations. Those logical alternatives
did not yield a smaller certified physical machine.
\end{abstract}

\section{The result and its precise boundary}
\label{sec:result}

The repository snapshot is \snap. The scope is the complete tracked
\file{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA} tree and the five archives
then in \file{docs/incoming}. The related ZFC, DiophantineConsistency and
ConciseIPRA trees were also inspected at this pin. Pinning is essential:
the collection receives new packets and removes repeated representations
as its formalization develops. Claims of novelty below concern this
snapshot, not worldwide priority.\cite{pa,incoming,catalog}

All three delivered machines have alphabet $\{0,1\}$, blank symbol zero,
a two-way infinite tape, one head initially at coordinate zero, working
controls $0,\ldots,193$, initial control zero, and terminal label 194.
Each working control has exactly two transition rows; every move is one
cell left or right. No external word, register file, extra track or
uncounted bootstrap is assumed. Native tables instead use start 74 and
halt 254. Native label 194 is a working control and must not be confused
with the dense terminal label.

\input{metrics.tex}

The raw parent state accounts are $134+69-9=194$ for GD and GDH, and
$135+69-10=194$ for GDHS: fixed controls plus AX readers minus compatible
folds. The new physical patch preserves that count. All 194 working
controls are reachable in each transition graph; graph reachability
does not assert that every control occurs on the particular blank run.
Every one of the six changed rows does occur during blank initialization.

There are three logically separate claims.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The new tables, structural premises, all local cases and whole-tape
initialization synchronizations have fresh executable certificates.
\item The accompanying written proof proves the \emph{relative} iPRA
halting equivalences directly from those finite premises. It does not
depend on a consistency assumption or on the source calculus.
\item The \emph{absolute} nonhalting/Con(PA) corollary uses the exact
inherited GD mathematical calibration, which in turn uses the revised
AX11H written argument. The full inherited proof compiler has not been
reimplemented or kernel-checked here.
\end{enumerate}
The current top-level PA README records an incomplete machine-specific
Lean endpoint. The revised AX11H packet nevertheless supplies a substantial
written mathematical completion. Both facts are retained. A numerical
certificate is not substituted for the arithmetic theorem, and a
standard-natural-number execution theorem is not silently upgraded to
an internal theorem of iPRA.\cite{pa,ax11h,gd}

\section{The three new physical constructions}
\label{sec:construction}

Let $M$ be one of the pinned GD, GDH, GDHS parents. Construct an intermediate
table $S$ with the history selector below; then construct $N$ by the two
shortcuts. No source command, numerical formula constructor, axiom or
logical register changes. In native numbering the six differences are:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{row}&M&N\\\hline
104/1&1R124&1R127\\
127/0&0R14&0L124\\
124/0&0R62&0R8\\
124/1&1R138&1R59\\
26/1&1R42&1R91\\
101/0&0L26&0R17
\end{array}
\]
The first four rows implement the selector; the last two are fast returns.
The ingredients occur separately in the supplied corpus. The new result
is their verified compatibility on these three recent GD sources, the
new literal graphs, and the direct quantitative transfer proof. It is
not claimed that the underlying selector method was invented here.

The change is more than a relabeling, mirror image or padding. Two semantic
entry roles now share an entry control and recover their role from the
previous tape bit; the old second entry becomes a selector. The fast
returns skip actual working transitions under proved context guards.
All six altered rows execute during initialization. Selectors add steps
on some visits, so no uniform runtime speedup is claimed.

\subsection{Exact history-selector simulation}

Write configurations as $(q,h,T)$ with $T_j=T(h+j)$, and define
$f(124)=127$ while fixing other controls. The literal parent rows satisfy
\[
\delta_M(127,0)=0R14,\quad\delta_M(124,0)=0R62,\quad
\delta_M(127,1)=\delta_M(124,1)=1R138.
\]
The helper rows are $\delta_S(8,0)=0R14$ and
$\delta_S(59,0)=0R62$. Every incoming transition to 127 writes zero
and moves right; every incoming transition to 124 writes one and moves
right. The full lists are respectively $110/0$ and $104/1$.
Thus every parent run from control 74, on any complete finite-support
tape, satisfies
\[
 q=127\Rightarrow T_{-1}=0,\qquad
 q=124\Rightarrow T_{-1}=1.                 \tag{E}
\]
This follows by induction on one transition; the initial state is neither
of the two states.

At matching checkpoints map $(q,h,T)$ to $(f(q),h,T)$. A read-one step
uses the common row. On read zero, control 127 moves left into 124;
124 preserves the history bit and returns through 8 or 59; the selected
helper performs the correct original transition. This takes three steps.
The four cases inspect only cells $h-1,h$ and leave the complete tape,
head displacement and mapped next control equal to the old one-step
result. All ordinary rows are checked separately with the mapped target.

\begin{lemma}[Selector clock]
With absorbing terminal semantics there is a primitive-recursive clock
$\tau$ such that, for a common initial configuration in control 74,
\[
 \operatorname{run}_S(\tau(t))=f_*(\operatorname{run}_M(t)),\qquad
 t\le\tau(t)\le3t.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Set $\tau(0)=0$. Add three when the parent is in 124 or 127 reading zero,
and one otherwise, including after halt. Induction uses (E) and the
four local identities. The state map fixes the terminal label and maps
no working state to it. Every assertion is an equation or Boolean
combination of primitive-recursive functions.
\end{proof}

Let $H_X(t)$ mean that the absorbing run of $X$ is terminal at time $t$.
Monotonicity of absorbing halting and the clock identity give
\[
 H_M(t)\to H_S(3t),\qquad H_S(t)\to H_M(t).       \tag{S}
\]
For the second implication, evaluate the identity at $t$; the intermediate
run is still terminal at $\tau(t)\ge t$, so the parent must be terminal.

\subsection{Guarded acceleration after the selector}

The full intermediate table has
\[
 \operatorname{pred}_S(101)=\{96/0\mapsto0R101\},
\]
\[
 \operatorname{pred}_S(26)=\{11/0\mapsto0L26,\ 101/0\mapsto0L26\},
\]
and every transition into 11 writes zero. Consequently its reachable
configurations satisfy
\begin{align*}
 q=101&\Rightarrow T_{-1}=0,\\
 q=11&\Rightarrow T_{-1}=0\ \lor\ T_1=0,\\
 q=26\ \land\ T_0=1&\Rightarrow T_1=T_2=0.       \tag{G}
\end{align*}
The first two statements follow from the just-written neighboring cell.
For the last, an entry into 26 writes the cell at new offset one as zero.
Entry from 101 forces the new scanned bit to zero, so cannot be the
read-one case. Entry from 11 with new scanned bit one forces the other
neighbor of 11 to be zero by its preceding invariant; this is the new
offset-two cell. This argument covers both movement directions of
predecessors of 11.

Under (G), the following are exact identities on complete tapes:
\begin{align*}
26,1 &: \quad 1R42,\ 0R13,\ 0L91
                 &&\rightsquigarrow 1R91,\\
101,0 &: \quad 0L26,\ 0R194,\ 0R17
                 &&\rightsquigarrow 0R17.
\end{align*}
The first guard fixes the scanned one and the two right zeros. The second
fixes the scanned zero and the left zero. The checker exhausts all twelve
admissible five-cell windows: four for the first identity and eight for
the second. None touches an uninspected exterior cell or encounters halt.

Define $\sigma(0)=0$ and add three at either shortcut row of $N$, one
elsewhere. At each induction step the existing equality identifies the
new configuration with an actually reachable $S$ configuration, so the
guards (G) apply; they are not assumed independently for $N$. Thus
\[
 \operatorname{run}_N(t)=\operatorname{run}_S(\sigma(t)),\qquad
 t\le\sigma(t)\le3t.
\]
In particular,
\[
 H_S(t)\to H_N(t),\qquad H_N(t)\to H_S(3t).       \tag{A}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Direct relative iPRA result]
For each of the three literal parent/child pairs, iPRA proves
\[
 \boxed{H_M(t)\to H_N(3t),\qquad H_N(t)\to H_M(3t).}
\]
It therefore proves both $\Halt(M)\leftrightarrow\Halt(N)$ and
$\neg\Halt(M)\leftrightarrow\neg\Halt(N)$, for blank starts and,
more generally, a common coded finite-support initial tape at control 74.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose (S) and (A). The factor is three in each direction: the selector
and the shortcuts expand the opposite simulations. Existential
introduction and elimination give halting equivalence; intuitionistic
logic gives equivalence of the negations.

For explicit arithmetization let $(L,R)$ encode the tape relative to the
head. With $R=2r+b$, a right move writing $w$ gives $(2L+w,r)$.
With $L=2\ell+a$, a left move gives $(\ell,4r+2w+a)$. A signed head
counter retains absolute coordinates if desired. Lookup, iteration,
neighbor bits, clocks and comparisons are primitive recursive. Every
induction above has a quantifier-free invariant with tape parameters free.
The absorbing terminal step is used only to define the arithmetic run
function; it adds no working control to the literal table. There is no
Markov principle, unbounded search or reflection assumption.
\end{proof}

\section{Arithmetic calibration and dependencies}
\label{sec:calibration}

The parents use Cantor pairing
$p(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b$ and constructors
\[
 I(A,B)=4p(A,B),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(i,A)=4p(A,i)+2,\quad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
Truth is code zero, the contradiction target is code one, and
$\neg A=I(A,1)$. The exceptional identity $I(0,0)=0$ is retained.
The GD source generalizes a saved theorem as $U(c,h)$ and emits the
diagonal clause $\Gamma(D)=I(D,U(D,D))$ at $D=d+x$.
Its other heads and numerical calculus are unchanged. GD uses the
sum detector $t+h=1$; GDH and GDHS implement $h=1$ with different
destructive comparison arrangements. Their AX has three operands and
pushes ten entries, unlike the four-operand AX11 interface.\cite{gd}

The inherited GD proof supplies explicit all-store words for Push, Pop,
axiom selection, modus ponens and generalization. It addresses numerical
zero aliases in the Cantor stack and the different final detector words.
Its preserved physical certificates have 4,755, 4,754 and 4,750 source
outcomes respectively. The new construction changes none of these
source interfaces; the direct physical transfer is sufficient.

The revised AX11H written calibration separates five interfaces:
literal machine/source, source/scheduler, scheduler/numerical calculus,
numerical calculus/current PA refutations, and the I$\Sigma_1$-to-iPRA
negative transfer. Its forward compiler replaces induction over all
environments by an explicit finite tree of requested proof constructions.
It supplies acyclic construction strata, bounded Boolean table invariants,
primitive-recursive clocks, and a Foundation-to-ordinary-PAeq adapter.
The current PA falsity code and the numerical contradiction code are
related by translations, not identified.\cite{ax11h}

These are substantial written arguments. This investigation reviewed
their relevant physical and weak-induction interfaces, but did not
independently evaluate every large proof code, implement the complete
compiler, or rebuild the transitive Lean development. The full inherited
GD archive and the exact AX11H calibration archive are preserved, with
hashes, in \file{references/}. This is an explicit dependency boundary.

\begin{corollary}[Absolute consistency corollary, importing the written calibration]
Using the GD calibration with its revised AX11H dependency, each of the
three delivered literal tables $N$ satisfies
\[
 \ipra\vdash\neg\Halt_{\rm blank}(N)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA).
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Compose the relative nonhalting theorem with the exact parent's
calibration. The new proof is independent of whether PA is consistent.
If the inherited calibration is subsequently revised, the directly
checked relative theorem remains valid for the pinned tables.
\end{proof}

\section{Attempts to cross the 194-state threshold}
\label{sec:search}

The following results are bounded negative experiments. None proves a
state-count lower bound, and no rejected 193-state table is promoted.

\subsection{Adaptive multi-row bootstrap repair}

The historical Runtime193 core identifies native 301 with 88 and changes
two scanner/selector rows. Source-frame checks can succeed while its
ordinary blank initialization enters an outward ray. The earlier
two-row repair experiment used 346 candidate rewrites on 92 distinct
rows, 58,485 distinct-row pairs and all 193 possible starts. It found
no complete source frame. Its pool contains speculative arithmetic-box
changes, so membership in the pool is not itself a proof of neutrality.

The fresh search fixes the intended native start 74 and allows several
simultaneous distinct-row rewrites from that same pool. It branches only
when a potentially mutable row is first encountered. A branch either
keeps the original row permanently or selects one of its supplied edits.
Unvisited edits have no effect on the bounded trace, so they need not
be enumerated as different full tables. At a node with no remaining
edit budget the machine is deterministic.

Two rejection rules are sound finite arguments. An outward ray is used
only when every zero-reading row on its same-direction route is fixed,
has no available edit, or the edit budget is exhausted. Exact cycles
are recognized by a hash \emph{candidate} followed by a fresh scalar
replay and complete state/head/tape equality; a hash alone never rejects
a branch. Other runs stop at the stated bounds and remain unresolved.
The bounds are 200,000 transitions, absolute head position 20,000 and
support size 20,000. The detector asks for the entire source frame at
one of sites 25--44, permits tape translation, header width at least 48,
arbitrary nine register values and an arbitrary nonempty spare list.
A hit would still require a safe logical seed and a runtime proof.

\section{Lessons from the related programs}

The ZFC work is the closest architectural source: predecessor-bit
sharing, command-local field readers, loop reassociation, protected-tail
scheduling and independent initialization proofs. Its K215 external
Lean/Rocq endpoint and its 240-state internal PA endpoint are distinct
claims. Importing a method does not import its state saving. In particular,
the K215 relay improvement at native $81/0$ already occurs in primary
PA194, so counting it again would be incorrect.\cite{zfc}

The DiophantineConsistency work emphasizes exact refutation predicates,
explicit finite encodings and native iPRA proof-existence endpoints.
Those methods support the arithmetic discipline used here. A smaller
polynomial variable count or circuit-operation count is not yet a smaller
binary machine: coefficient generation, exact arithmetic, witness
enumeration and blank initialization must all be compiled and counted.
No such alternative interpreter was established below 194 states.\cite{dio}

ConciseIPRA makes the entire standalone proof checker the object of its
size comparison. Its weakened transport and direct-closure rules suggest
ways to organize primitive-recursive proof transformations. Searching
for inconsistency of an equivalent presentation of iPRA, however, would
change the requested target. A PA-calibrated calculus and a priced binary
implementation remain necessary. Here iPRA is the metatheory of the
relative simulation, not a substitute for the searched PA theory.\cite{concise}
