# What transfers from the ZFC machine research

All links below are pinned to the comparison revision `1e1a41f799d4658b829cdd0eabae3775a0c8c4a0`. The inspected leaf files are unchanged from the available13November snapshot; their identity was checked by a Git comparison. These are specific method transfers, not an assertion that consistency of ZFC can replace consistency of PA.

## Partial selectors are useful; their state savings are contextual

The [K216 proof](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1e1a41f799d4658b829cdd0eabae3775a0c8c4a0/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/candidate-k216/proof.md) exposes a source subtree `((A,X),(B,Y))` over4,174 primitive occurrences. A history-based selector reads only the A/B branches while X/Y remain live ordinary source code. This extra freedom makes an existing provider implement both origins of the164/183 fold; source reassociation alone would not establish physical correctness. The final proof checks both source control flow and complete arbitrary-value runtime.

That idea suggests allowing large partial providers during PA reassociation, rather than demanding that an entire helper tree duplicate a desired decoder. It does not immediately remove a PA control: the required endpoints, predecessor markers and available live branches have to occur in the PA source, and the final allocation must be recounted. No untested transplantation of the K216 fold is credited as a saving here.

## Two successful ZFC mechanisms are already present in PA

The [K215 proof, sections Physical allocation and Local81 repair](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1e1a41f799d4658b829cdd0eabae3775a0c8c4a0/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/candidate-k215/proof.md) gives the69/400 predecessor-marker fold and the all-tape three-step replacement `81/0:0L92 -> 0L111`, freeing `92/1` for decoder use.

The new AX11GH table already contains exactly these relevant native rows:

| Control/read | AX11GH action |
|---|---|
|69/0|0L213|
|69/1|1R31|
|213/0|0R92|
|213/1|1R90|
|92/0|0R104|
|90/0|0R105|
|81/0|0L111|
|111/0|0R104|
|111/1|1R128|

Its `92/1=1R185` is already assigned to other decoder work. Thus the fact that K215 saved states using these mechanisms does not supply a new numerical reduction for this PA table. The present two-row fusion search looks for additional compatible routes instead of counting an existing saving again.

## The301/88 bootstrap difficulty has an exact ZFC precedent

The [ZFC marker-relay proposal](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1e1a41f799d4658b829cdd0eabae3775a0c8c4a0/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/k218-continuation-search/marker-relay/README.md) starts with the same301-to88 identification and the same helper edits `88/0=0L51`, `51/0=0R122` as the PA193 runtime core. It adds a temporary marker through36,16,56 and redirects six decoder returns. The [local proof](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1e1a41f799d4658b829cdd0eabae3775a0c8c4a0/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/k218-continuation-search/marker-relay/proof.md) explicitly leaves the `108 ->10 ->16` route and right-zero bootstrap branch uncovered. The original ZFC packet records a concrete failed source return; it does not certify that relay as a smaller machine.

We tested the exact published relay recipe on the newly constructed AX11GH, including all six return redirects. The inherited source checker fails at source ordinal2 after four completed outcomes. Independently, literal blank execution reaches state35 with head19 and one-cells exactly `{-3,1,11,12}` after117 transitions. Since `35/0=1R35`, it then writes ones indefinitely while moving right into fresh zero cells. The complete117-step prefix is in `recorded/relay_transfer.json`. The infinite continuation follows by elementary induction and does not depend on the source oracle.

This rejects that concrete direct transfer. It leaves open changing the source return structure together with the relay, or finding a different marker scheme.

## More permissive logical seeds can help only after a physical frame is reached

The [protected-tail theorem](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1e1a41f799d4658b829cdd0eabae3775a0c8c4a0/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/k217-protected-tail-seed/README.md) permits an arbitrary initial theorem-stack suffix for the specified ZFC scheduler when its initial counter and proof-list pointer satisfy the exact stated conditions and its current formula/cache are theorems. Its stack-credit argument is more flexible than requiring every initial stored formula to be safe.

The [zero-spare transfer215 packet](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1e1a41f799d4658b829cdd0eabae3775a0c8c4a0/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/candidate-k215/alternatives/zero-spare-transfer/README.md) combines a changed physical spare policy with a different command language and a carefully justified unsafe-tail startup. It keeps215 states; `201` in its source description is a DAG-node count, not a TM-state count. The logical protected theorem heads and physical spare-register blocks are distinct objects.

For PA these methods could enlarge the acceptable startup target family, but they require PA-specific command/credit and calibration proofs. They do not turn an outward-ray computation into an enumerator, and they do not justify accepting a visually plausible but semantically uncalibrated tape. The present rejected transfers fail before this kind of logical flexibility can help.

## Formal proof boundary

The [ZFC research status](https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1e1a41f799d4658b829cdd0eabae3775a0c8c4a0/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/README.md) distinguishes the external Lean/Rocq correctness proofs for exact K215, the internal PA statement for exact240, and the uncompleted internal PA endpoint for K215. None of these is an internal iPRA calibration of a PA194 table. Transferring a local whole-tape simulation is a comparatively finite obligation; transferring a consistency claim requires retaining and proving its correct arithmetical base and exact target calculus.
