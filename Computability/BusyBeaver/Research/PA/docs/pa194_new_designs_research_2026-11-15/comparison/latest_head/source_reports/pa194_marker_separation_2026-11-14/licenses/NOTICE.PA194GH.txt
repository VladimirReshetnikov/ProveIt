PA194GH, 14 November 2026.

Adapted from Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt research packets at commit
32d0d8b7d93a63ad4106c18cdb29b37927acdad2, in particular the PA194G package
and the PA194H/HS package. The underlying arithmetic runtime and source
compiler descend from James Chang's PA196 development and earlier Wade /
K-family work; their supplied notices and licenses are retained here.

The new work combines the explicit-AX generalization binder with cached-
head-only Shift, constructs and checks its literal table, gives its
all-store command proof with explicit primitive-recursive bounds, and
adapts the independent literal checker and source search to that table.
Neither component mechanism is individually claimed as new.

The inherited compiler and symbolic runtime files retain their comments.
Their comments about earlier packages do not certify the new machine.
Verification scope and incomplete iPRA calibration are stated explicitly
in README.txt and proof.tex.
