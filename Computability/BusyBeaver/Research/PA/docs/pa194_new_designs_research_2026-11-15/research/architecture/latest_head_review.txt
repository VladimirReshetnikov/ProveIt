COUPLING DUAL-B WITH H5X CHANGES THE LOGICAL ASSESSMENT

The witness archive introduces an h5X head W(d,H)->A, where the old head's
conclusion was code1. Its 16-step proof of explosion uses h5X, diagonal,
distribution, pure implication and generalization, without old B. Therefore
our standalone dual-B countermodel must NOT be applied to this coupled
calculus: it does not validate h5X. This is a concrete new promising logical
ingredient even though it supplies no verified state saving by itself.

Let N(P)=(P->1), write U_d(P) for the coded universal, and define the two
dual schema presentations:
  D(P) : N(P) -> U_d(N(U_d(P))),
  R(P) : P -> U_d(N(U_d(N(P)))).
The archive's coupled construction uses D; our earlier experiment used R.
They are interderivable without explosion or double-negation elimination.

For D=>R, pure implication proves P->NN(P). The D instance at N(P) gives
NN(P)->U_d(N(U_d(N(P)))); compose these two implications.

For R=>D, the R instance at N(P) gives
N(P)->U_d(N(U_d(NN(P)))). Generalize P->NN(P) and use h6 distribution to
obtain U_d(P)->U_d(NN(P)). Pure contraposition gives
N(U_d(NN(P)))->N(U_d(P)). Generalize once more and apply h6 to obtain
U_d(N(U_d(NN(P))))->U_d(N(U_d(P))). Compose with the first implication.

These are fixed finite proof recipes in K/S plus h6/generalization. The
restricted-rho implementation of K/S and primitive-recursive substitution
therefore supply bounded proof translations. Together with the witness
archive's independently checked h5X explosion proof and coupled dual-B
translations, this removes the specific old-B/explosion circularity for the
coupled family. It does not invalidate the standalone countermodel.

The existing sound D+h5X grammar search in the witness archive reports

200 with 134 fixed controls; it reports no frontier hit. This is an archived
compiler-search objective, not a completed 200-state consistency machine.
The syntactically different R+h5X emitter could still have a different price,
but neither derivability alone nor our old standalone R price 68 supplies
a verified sub-194 physical construction.
