\section{The search below 194 and what it established}
\label{sec:search}

The requested strict improvement was pursued at the logical interface,
source grammar, physical register layout and literal transition levels.
None of the completed searches establishes a valid machine below 194.
Their output is useful because it identifies exact unsuccessful families
and supplies two new mathematical starting points.  It does not show
that 194 is optimal.

\subsection{Logical and grammar searches}

The original six-head parameter-permutation experiment reached effective
reader cost 60, but every one of the 30 inspected frontier seeds reverted
to identity parameter maps.  Those outputs only reassociated an existing
emitter and were not selected as a materially new fallback.  Moving the
diagonal head to $\Gamma(H)$ was also tested, with a best recorded cost
corresponding to 196 states.

The independent witness consequent is different.  With the exchanged
sixth head its best fully validated implementation has 195 states.
Restoring the ordinary sixth-head orientation changes grammar sharing
enough to obtain PA194H5X.  Its successful bounded stochastic run made
9,391,599 mutation iterations in 900.004 seconds, accepting 1,597,061
mutations.  The first effective-cost-60 hit occurred at iteration
1,537,756.  The 200 recorded frontier hits are occurrences, not a claim
of 200 distinct calibrated machines.  The deterministic delivered
builder reconstructs the selected result without repeating that search.

The coupled $J,h_5^X$ presentation first gave a validated 195-state
exchanged-head implementation.  The ordinary-head search then found the
new PA194JH5X table.  Both accepted machines have identity parameter maps
in all six logical heads; neither result relies on an unresolved schema
renaming.  Their fixed grammar trees and nine simultaneous folds are
frozen separately.

The successful ordinary-$J$ run completed 15,454,951 mutations in
900.002 seconds, accepted 2,439,490 mutations and recorded 200 frontier
occurrences.  A separate finite enumeration checked all 720 head orders
with the frozen $J$ tree, then 308,880 head-order/eight-chunk bracketings,
without reducing the count.  The ordinary-$J^A$ run completed 20,235,655
mutations with best nomination 195.  The final manifest also records
the last bounded searches with relaxed heuristic height penalties; no
such penalty is used to assert an impossibility result.  All 18 randomized
runs finished their stated budgets, totaling 187,622,892 mutation trials
and 31,317,681 accepted mutations.  These totals do not count distinct
machines.  The final height-relaxed run remained at 194 after 6,078,532
trials; the $J$-seeded $J^A$ run remained at 195 after 15,098,637; the
full-order $J$ run remained at 194 after 9,893,090.

Other runs explored radix five, consequent shifts, the coupled
contraposed head, individual-occurrence tree rotations, reusable-node
rotations, argument permutations and head-order changes.  Some of these
experiments shorten the emitted word but increase the number of distinct
reader helpers.  The frozen search manifest records seeds, programs,
inputs, stopping budgets, attained costs and whether a run actually
finished.  Wall-clock-bounded stochastic runs are reproducible as
algorithms and inputs, not promised to stop at the identical mutation
number on another CPU.  Grammar scores nominate candidates; full source,
simultaneous-fold, blank-start and logical checks remain separate gates.

The 187-token $J^A,h_5^X$ presentation is a specific unresolved target.
Its two-way derivations are already supplied.  Achieving effective reader
cost 59 with the present 134 fixed controls would yield 193 states, but
that cost has not been obtained.  A different arithmetic engine or
initialization protocol could change this budget altogether.

\subsection{Fresh complete-context physical searches}

The new literal-context collector represents every source outcome with
arbitrary registers, an arbitrary nonempty spare list and an arbitrary
gap.  To expose a list-scan interior, it splits the list into a consumed
prefix, one arbitrary current block and an unconsumed suffix; either
outer list may be empty.  It also includes every interior position of an
accelerated constant-symbol run.  Affine correlations may be discarded,
which enlarges the window set.  The complete blank startup is added
explicitly.  For PA194H5X, the resulting radius-six invariant contains
50,873 windows covering all 194 controls.

This is stronger than collecting only the entry and exit of an arithmetic
box.  It still defines a particular finite local-certificate model, not
all possible tape invariants.  The following completed screens on
PA194H5X produced no 193-state candidate:

The last screen does not establish arbitrary simultaneous mutation
soundness: a combined edit could introduce new contexts.  Any survivor
would need a fresh full source proof or a combined local simulation
certificate.  There were no survivors.  A timeout or a local step limit
is never treated as proof of nontermination.

The same quotient, incoming-elimination and shared-selector methods were
run on PA194JH5X.  Its quotient screen examined all 18,721 pairs,
25,551,656 row actions and 183,698 joint row pairs without a hit.
Its 40,320 allocations fixing the first physical-register position also
had maximum tagged matching size nine.  These secondary counts do not
silently extend the allocation conclusion to untested positions.

\subsection{Alternative physical and logical routes}

The spare-register append mechanism was investigated independently.
On the GH parent, 35,696 one-row mutations were screened against 27 small
literal register-operation cases for appended values two through eight.
Seven passed the first increment case; none passed all 27.  The run used
728,755,426 literal transitions.  A two-row local synthesis considered
all 602,176 assignments of native rows $(66,0)$ and $(198,0)$, with
existing successors, a 160-step local bound, and a fixed tape region;
none passed even the first local case.  These are bounded discovery
experiments, not universal impossibility proofs.

The shorter source addresses of PA194JH5X suggested a separate final
check: reduce the minimum address header from 48 cells.  A 36-cell
runtime interpretation passes all 4,794 outcomes, as does the associated
Runtime193 splice on already prepared frames.  The unchanged accepted
blank startup still builds the same tape; under the 36-cell convention
it has gap 12.  This does not remove a control.  In fact all 194 controls
occur in the universal source contracts, leaving no startup-only control
to discard under this scheme.  More decisively, every one of the 193
possible blank starts of that fixed spliced table is classified within
93 transitions: two halt and 191 enter certified escaping self-loop rays.
Its ten visits to the source-boundary control have at most four ones,
whereas any complete frame with nine working registers, a nonempty spare
list and two markers needs at least twelve.  This obstruction is
independent of header length.  It rules out repairing that \emph{fixed}
193 core by merely reinterpreting its header, not an additional bootstrap
rewrite or a new 193-state architecture.

Two tempting logical shortcuts were rejected before promoting a table.
First, moving the independent consequent inside the equality witness,
$U(D,E(H,D)\to A)\to A$, admits the concrete variable-capture
counterexample in the logical chapter.  Second, collapsing the dominated
implication family to the diagonal $u=D$ leaves the three-parameter basis
\[
 R_3(D,A,H)=((H\to D)\to D)\to((D\to H)\to(A\to H)).
\]
This family alone, MP and the zero truth alias cannot supply the retained
K/S/Peirce basis.  With designated value zero, use the implication table
\[
 \begin{array}{c|ccc}
 \to&0&1&2\\\hline
 0&0&1&1\\1&0&0&1\\2&0&1&0
 \end{array}.
\]
All 27 $R_3$ instances are designated, and MP preserves designation.
$I(0,0)=0$ respects the real zero alias.  K at $(2,0)$, S at $(1,0,2)$,
and Peirce at $(1,2)$ are not designated.  This is an obstruction for
that implicational basis by itself; adding every other numerical head
is outside the claimed counteralgebra.  The exhaustive finite check and
its receipt are included.

The suggested neighboring projects \cite{zfc,dio,concise} informed three
choices.  The ZFC work emphasizes complete caller histories, protected
tails and return-context invariants, which motivated the enlarged
literal-window and shared-selector work.  The Diophantine work makes
uniform PR witness maps and exact consistency coding central; a short
equation alone does not make its binary evaluator small.  ConciseIPRA
illustrates removing an apparent logical primitive through explicit
proof translations.  Here that lesson led to the witness bootstrap and
the coupled first-head change, while the enumerated theory remains PA
strength.  Replacing the theory by ZFC or by iPRA would not preserve the
requested target sentence.

The remaining promising issue is therefore concrete: retain the proved
new logical presentations while changing the source sharing, reader
protocol or arithmetic representation enough to remove one literal
control.  The artifacts isolate those interfaces so subsequent work can
start from a calibrated new source instead of repeating the old corpus
or repairing an invalid blank initialization.
