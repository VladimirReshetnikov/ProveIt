\subsection{Physical reduction attempts and a complete rejection certificate}
The physical search produced no accepted machine below 194 states. Two
restricted searches were completed before a loss of temporary research
storage: a box-inclusive offset search, and a source-macro row-choice screen.
Their raw search receipts did not survive. The accompanying JSON therefore
labels their counts as a retained previous-run record, rather than a newly
replayed exhaustive-search certificate.

The offset search considered offsets $-2,0,+2$, required at least one of the
two controls to belong to a designated arithmetic box or the dispatcher, and
allowed arbitrary replacement rows at the retained control. Its finite model
used radius-four tape windows and at most 160 microsteps per local comparison.
The recorded counts are:

$2$ & 1624 & 2214704 & 34377 & 0\\\hline
Total & 12287 & 13995160 & 80173 & 0
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The constraint windows included 398 outcomes from the first 301 source sites,
not a claimed complete source-context characterization. A separate 17424-proposal
row-choice screen recorded 11812 inconclusive exits at its 5000-step bound,
1577 early halts, and 4029 exact configuration cycles. Six candidates survived
that concrete screen and the four list lemmas, but none passed the subsequent
symbolic source check. A bounded exit or a symbolic-checker failure is not, by
itself, a proof of inequivalence. These counts establish no global lower bound.

One rejected proposal has been reconstructed and freshly checked in full.
Starting from the primary 194-state native table, delete control 16, redirect
all targets 16 to 38, and replace both rows at 38 by the old rows at 16:
\[
(38,0)\mapsto(1,L,1),\qquad
(38,1)\mapsto(0,L,1).
\]
Its 193-state native table has SHA-256
\texttt{cb53ad33e8812dc2ebfb6a1a1b452962eeba0cf6b851d398d403a192ea0e6b80}.
The blank run from control 74 and head 0 reaches a repeated complete
configuration at time 28217 and repeats after 52 further steps. The repeated
control is 1, the head is 1, and the tape has 46 ones. A canonical positive branch
frame at source site 3661 (path \texttt{101100}, logical $T=1$, other primary
registers zero, spare values $[0,1]$, header distance 48) repeats after 52 steps
with no preperiod.

The two certificates contain every literal transition, the complete finite
support of each entry tape, and an explicit declaration that the exterior tape
is zero. A second script independently reconstructs the mutated table and
checks both the prefix and the complete configuration equality after the
cycle. Thus this candidate is conclusively rejected; it is included only as a
diagnostic artifact, conspicuously marked \textbf{REJECTED}.

A separate source interval invariant was also freshly reconstructed and
checked after recovery. From source site 25 with logical register vector
$(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$, all 4774 abstract successor edges are included in the
invariant. It excludes source sites 8,3635,3636 and forces the zero branch at
site 7 (register 6) and site 3634 (register 1). This is a sound source-execution
invariant and does not establish the missing proof-theoretic calibration.
