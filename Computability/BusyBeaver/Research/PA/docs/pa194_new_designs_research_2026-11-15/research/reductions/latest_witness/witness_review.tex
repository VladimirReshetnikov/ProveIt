\subsection{The newly included witness presentations}
The latest witness archive adds PA194H5X and PA194JH5X as prior constructions.
Both have $134+69-9=194$ working controls. Put
$N(A)=I(A,1)$, $W(d,h)=U(d,N(E(h,d)))$, and
$X(d,h)=U(d,N(U(d,h)))$. H5X replaces $W(d,h)\to 1$ by
$W(d,h)\to a$, with $a$ independent of $d,h$. JH5X also replaces
$(X(d,h)\to 1)\to h$ by $(X(d,h)\to h)\to h$.
Their AX words have 189 and 188 tokens respectively, and their source-site
counts are 3677 and 3683. These logical changes are distinct from our physical
LC-return and MP-decoder changes.

The supplied independent schematic Hilbert checkers were freshly replayed.
In particular, the 16-line H5X explosion proof uses only diagonal,
distribution, H5X, K, S, closed generalization and modus ponens: neither the
old first head nor Peirce is used. The old-to-H5X and reverse templates have
13 and 1 lines; the J translations have 95 and 72 lines. Their inherited
derived-basis and revised AX 11 H calibration dependencies remain explicit.

This makes a coupled version of our previously obstructed dual-head route
valid. The witness archive proves the coupled $B+h_5^X$ presentation, where
$B_d(h)=N(h)\to U(d,N(U(d,h)))$. Our earlier family was
$R_d(h)=h\to U(d,N(U(d,N(h))))$. The schemas $B$ and $R$ are interderivable
using $h\to N(N(h))$, positive composition, contraposition, theorem
generalization and distribution. Indeed $B_d(N(h))$ gives $R_d(h)$ after
composition. Conversely, apply $R_d(N(h))$ and lift the contraposition of
$U(d,h)\to U(d,N(N(h)))$ through the outer quantifier to obtain $B_d(h)$.
The independent H5X explosion lemma therefore repairs the missing premise;
it does not invalidate the countermodel to changing the old first head alone.

The archive's already completed coupled-$B$ run records 6694768 mutations and
best effective reader score 66, a 200-control nomination. These are attributed
upstream counters. Our fresh bounded follow-up priced only two fixed tree
shapes: the archived best tree reproduces $78-12=66$ readers after optimistic
folding, while changing its first head to $R$ at the same shape gives
$81-10=71$, nominally 205 controls. Exact free-term head binding was checked;
no new full physical implementation is inferred from those prices.

The witness Runtime193 splice is also expressly rejected. Fresh literal
replay classifies all 193 blank starts: two halt and 191 enter certified rays,
within 93 transitions. Its ten visits to boundary 84 have at most four ones,
whereas any complete frame needs at least twelve. A smaller minimum-header
interpretation therefore does not initialize this fixed core. The rejection
does not exclude other bootstrap edits or other 193-state designs.

We also checked the immediate MP composition on both raw witness tables.
All nine existing folds retain their 36 simultaneous local cases, but the
specified non-box predecessor-bit matching maximum remains nine, before and
after the four-row MP edit. Thus this local composition supplies no tenth fold.
Full composite source/runtime/startup proofs were not performed. The LCSF3
shortcut does not directly transplant: the witness predecessor of 190 writes 0,
and their 190/1 row differs from the LC guarded-return path.
