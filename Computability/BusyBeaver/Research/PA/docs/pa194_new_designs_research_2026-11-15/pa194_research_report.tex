\subsection{Formal conventions}\label{formal-conventions}

A working control has two transitions, one for each symbol in \{0,1\}; each transition writes one symbol, moves exactly one cell left or right, and enters a possibly identical control. The tape is indexed by the integers and is initially all zero; the head starts at zero. There are 194 working controls, plus a separate halting control. Dense tables use start 0 and halt 194. Native tables use start 74 and halt 254: native control 194 is an ordinary working control. All transitions into halt count as the step that reaches halt. For arithmetic purposes the run is totalized by making terminal configurations absorbing.

Write H\_M(t) for terminality at time t, and NH(M) for the statement that no finite time is terminal. The selected Con(PA) is the consistency sentence for the primitive-recursive PA proof predicate fixed by the imported calibration. Its usual forms not-exists-proof and forall-proof-not are intuitionistically equivalent. Similarly, proving both positive halting-witness maps suffices to derive both halting equivalence and nonhalting equivalence by intuitionistic logic.

Finite-support tapes have a primitive-recursive binary-zipper encoding; finite configurations, step functions, instruction arrays, frame encoders, and all clocks below are primitive recursive. Assertions used for induction are equalities or primitive-recursive characteristic predicates. The written induction arguments therefore use quantifier-free induction available in iPRA. The unbounded conclusions are mathematical consequences of these arguments, not extrapolations from finite simulations.

\subsection{Attribution and scope of novelty}\label{attribution-and-scope-of-novelty}

The third guarded return used by LCSF3 was already present on the GH/GHS loop-return parents. LCSF3 establishes a new compatible composition on LC\_AX11H with its history selector. MP preserves the entire resolved instruction array but changes the physical address layout and decoder. Neither construction is just a renaming of controls, a reflection of the tape, or a change to unused terminal-entry decorations. The corpus and novelty conventions are specified below.

\subsection{Forced history bit and exact local identity}\label{forced-history-bit-and-exact-local-identity}

The only transition into native 190 is 139/1 = 1 R 190. Since the start is 74, induction on run length proves that every reachable configuration at 190 has a 1 immediately to the left of the head. The new row creates no new predecessor of 190.

The parent rows 190/1 = 1 L 73, 73/1 = 1 R 83, and 83/1 = 0 L 42 consequently execute a three-step block with the same complete tape, head, and control endpoint as the child's one-step 0 L 42. Both intermediate controls are nonterminal. For the binary zipper tape coding, with the low bit of L immediately left of the head and the low bit of R under the head, the identity is:

\[
(190,2\ell+1,2r+1)\longmapsto(73,\ell,4r+3)\longmapsto(83,2\ell+1,2r+1)\longmapsto(42,\ell,4r+1).
\]

The child performs the direct first-to-last step. The displayed equations hold for arbitrary natural l and r. Eight finite window instances independently check all free bits of the inspected local context, but the mathematical identity does not depend on a bounded exterior.

This edit is specific to the LC parent. In PA194AX11H\_SF, 190/1 is 1 R 99 and its predecessor marker differs; the shortcut cannot simply be transplanted there.

\subsection{Direct iPRA transfer for LCSF3}\label{direct-ipra-transfer-for-lcsf3}

Use absorbing totalized runs and let H\_M(t) mean that the parent is terminal at time t. Define sigma(0)=0. At child step t, increase sigma by three if the child is at native 190 reading 1, and by one otherwise. This is primitive recursion. Quantifier-free induction, using the predecessor invariant and the local identity, proves:

\[
\operatorname{Run}_N(t)=\operatorname{Run}_M(\sigma(t)),\qquad t\leq\sigma(t)\leq3t.
\]

Absorbing terminality then gives the explicit positive bounds:

\[
\mathsf{iPRA}\vdash H_M(t)\to H_N(t),\qquad
\mathsf{iPRA}\vdash H_N(t)\to H_M(3t).
\]

Existential introduction and intuitionistic logic yield both halting and nonhalting equivalence. No PA-consistency assumption, Markov principle, or unbounded minimization is used in this relative proof.

The absolute Con(PA) corollary follows through the identified chain revised AX11H → LC\_AX11H → LC\_AX11H\_SF → PA194-LCSF3. The exact revised calibration archive has SHA-256 \hash{93ee082ef035b2d32e71d6af5239b25b4bea1773f03bea4d2c39810a7fe15075}. This imports its written arithmetic calibration and the two intermediate physical transfers; it is not a new completed Lean proof.

\subsection{Common extended source for the MP proof}\label{common-extended-source-for-the-mp-proof}

Extend the common source state to \(z=(i,R_0,\ldots,R_8,n,Q)\), with n the extra header gap and Q the nonempty spare list. Increment appends a unit spare to Q; positive decrement increments n; other nonhalting source instructions leave this bookkeeping unchanged. Start at site 25, \(R=(2,0,\ldots,0)\), \(n=0\), \(Q=[1]^{26}\). Executing the halt instruction sends the tuple to an absorbing code H. Let F be this primitive-recursive step and \(z_k=F^k(z_0)\).

For each machine C, \(E_C(z)\) is its complete frame using C's own address encoding, with head -2. Independent string-carry and suffix-cut checks cover all 4,777 nonhalting outcomes for each machine: every address overflow is zero, and the computed successor padded to width 48 equals the resolved successor address padded to width 48. The 4,778 contracts for each machine supply exact affine durations in thirteen slots: constant, gap, nine logical register parameters, first spare, and remaining spare-list width. In a positive decrement case the tested parameter is R\_i-1; the zero case fixes it to zero. Case selection and evaluation are primitive recursive. Write \(D_C(z)\) for the exact duration.

\subsection{Primitive-recursive clocks and cofinality}\label{primitive-recursive-clocks-and-cofinality}

For arithmetization, collapse every physical configuration already in the halt control to the single absorbing code H. Retain the complete tape and head before halt. This preserves the literal halting predicate and adds no TM control. Set \(E_C(H)=H\) and \(D_C(H)=1\).

The uniform physical-rule proofs, literal contract instances, frame closure and exact startup give:

\[
\operatorname{Run}_C(D_C(z),E_C(z))=E_C(F(z)),\qquad 1\leq D_C(z).
\]

Define \(a(0)=57{,}922\), \(b(0)=58{,}066\) and \(a(k+1)=a(k)+D_A(z_k)\), \(b(k+1)=b(k)+D_B(z_k)\). Quantifier-free induction proves source validity and:

\[
\operatorname{Run}_A(a(k),c_{\rm blank})=E_A(z_k),\qquad
\operatorname{Run}_B(b(k),c_{\rm blank})=E_B(z_k),\qquad
k\leq a(k),\quad k\leq b(k).
\]

Every component is a primitive-recursive function or characteristic function. If \(H_A(t)\), absorption and \(a(t)\ge t\) make \(E_A(z_t)\) terminal, hence \(z_t=H\) and \(H_B(b(t))\). Interchanging the machines gives the reverse implication. No least-boundary search is needed.

\subsection{Explicit quadratic witness bound}\label{explicit-quadratic-witness-bound}

D_C(z_k)\leq 694+18k,\qquad
a(k)\leq57922+9k^2+685k,\qquad

\[
\mathsf{iPRA}\vdash H_A(t)\to H_B(\Theta(t)),\qquad
\mathsf{iPRA}\vdash H_B(t)\to H_A(\Theta(t)).

Existential introduction and intuitionistic logic yield halting and nonhalting equivalence. Composing with the exact revised AX11H written calibration gives the requested Con(PA) equivalence. The new proof does not assume PA's consistency and does not complete its Lean formalization.

\subsection{The stronger witness head repairs the coupled logical route}\label{the-stronger-witness-head-repairs-the-coupled-logical-route}

H5X replaces the head \(W(D,H)\to1\) by \(W(D,H)\to A\), with independent \(A\). JH5X also replaces \((X\to1)\to H\) by \((X\to H)\to H\). Their supplied logical certificates were freshly replayed. The archive supplies fixed finite proof translations for the sound coupled basis.

There is a useful relation between its dual presentation and the one investigated earlier. Write \(N(P)=P\to1\), \(U_d(P)=U(d,P)\), and

\[
D(P):\quad NP\to U_d(N(U_d(P))),\qquad
R(P):\quad P\to U_d(N(U_d(NP))).
\]

These schemes are mutually derivable without explosion. Instantiate \(D\) at \(NP\) and use the implicational theorem \(P\to NNP\) to obtain \(R(P)\). Conversely, instantiate \(R\) at \(NP\). Generalize \(P\to NNP\) and use distribution to obtain \(U_d(P)\to U_d(NNP)\). Contraposition, followed by generalization and outer distribution, gives

\[
U_d(N(U_d(NNP)))\to U_d(N(U_d(P))).
\]

Composing with \(R(NP)\) gives \(D(P)\). This is a fixed proof translation, so the H5X repair applies to both presentations; the earlier isolated logical obstruction remains restricted to its original theory.
