\begin{abstract}
The strict reduction below 194 working states was not obtained. We supply
the requested alternative: an explicit deterministic, binary, single-tape,
blank-start machine PA194QHS with 194 working states and 388 transitions.
Its ordinary mathematical calibration is
\[
 \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg\Halt(\QHS)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
\]
The construction combines the supplied premise-exchanged quantifier head,
visible-result no-push modus ponens, and symmetric cache-only Shift.
The component mechanisms are prior work; the exact combination, source
binding, and literal table are the contribution of this report. In particular,
the closest parent QH has the same command semantics, but QHS removes an
entire restoration loop from the implementation of Shift. This is a
change of runtime algorithm, rather than a renaming of its states.

The complete accounting is $135+67-8=194$. Both symbolic executors accept
all 4,750 source outcomes, and the independent literal-tape executor
rederives its four generic register-list lemmas from the actual transitions.
All 32 simultaneous fold cases pass. Blank execution reaches the exact
complete source frame after 57,768 transitions. The comparison covers
61 filed research packets and five current incoming archives at a pinned
commit, including nested archives, historical duplicates, and rejected
tables. The new graph is absent from that corpus even after state renaming,
choice of spanning root, tape reflection, and global symbol interchange.

The PA argument composes the supplied syntactic calibration with the
explicit proof compiler and physical refinement described here. Executable
certificates support that argument; they are not a newly kernel-checked
end-to-end Lean or Rocq theorem. No state lower bound is claimed.
\end{abstract}

$0,\ldots,193$, its initial state is 0, and its terminal halt label is 194.
The tape is indexed by $\mathbb Z$, initially zero everywhere, with the

one bit and moves exactly one cell left or right. The terminal label has

\begin{theorem}[PA consistency calibration]\label{thm:main}
For the literal table and blank start just specified,
\[
 \PA\vdash \Halt(\QHS)\leftrightarrow
       \exists e\,\Prf_{\PA}(e,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner).
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \boxed{\PA\vdash
   \bigl(\neg\Halt(\QHS)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).}
\]
\end{theorem}

The proof is given in Sections~\ref{sec:calculus}--\ref{sec:assembly}.
The inherited logical calibration is explicitly identified, and the new
finite source and physical obligations are bound to the displayed table.
Actual nonhalting follows if PA is consistent. The biconditional itself
does not assume consistency as an additional axiom of PA.

\subsection{What is new}

Three independent choices describe this machine:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Q:} exchange the two curried premises of the universal
      distribution head. This changes a generated formula and its proof
      translation, without changing its arity or its stack position.
\item \textbf{H:} successful MP leaves its conclusion in the visible top
      and in the offset, pushes no dummy zero, and clears the cache.
      Shift tests the cache alone for contradiction.
\item \textbf{S:} implement that Shift with a symmetric destructive
      equality test, removing its restoration loop and sharing an extra
      physical reader through a history-sensitive fold.
\end{enumerate}
Q, H, and S each occur in the supplied work. QH occurs in the current
incoming archive; HS occurs in the filed packets. QHS is a new concrete
combination relative to the snapshot, with fresh complete verification.
It is not presented as a new logical calculus, a novel general compiler
principle, or a reduction of the 194-state record.

The distinction from QH is algorithmic: a three-instruction loop has
been removed, and its dynamic iteration count depends on a natural
register value. Thus the difference is not merely three saved transitions
per execution. No uniform whole-program speedup is claimed: the new
contextual fold introduces its own short physical detours. Its actual
blank initialization is 154 transitions shorter than QH's
$57,922$-transition initialization.

\section{The numerical calculus}\label{sec:calculus}

\subsection{Coding, truth, and the separate diagonal head}

Use Cantor pairing
\[
 p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b
\]
and numerical constructors
\[
 I(A,B)=4p(A,B),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,\quad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
The arguments of $U$ are deliberately transposed. Write $A\to B$ for
$I(A,B)$, let $N(B)=I(B,1)$, and use the classical implicational
definitions of conjunction and existential quantification. Code zero
is truth; code one is the designated contradiction $M(0,0)$.

The exceptional identity $I(0,0)=0$ must be retained. These numerical
constructors are not a freely generated syntax. The arithmetic
translation handles this case with a fixed proof of the equivalence
between truth and truth implying truth.

The diagonal head is
\[
 \Gamma(B)=I(B,U(B,B)).
\]
Every variable label occurring in the decoded formula $B$ is strictly
less than $B$, with the truth-code case handled separately. Hence $x_B$
does not occur in its own formula and the diagonal generalization is
sound. This numerical freshness mechanism is inherited from the
separate-head calibration \cite{primary,separate}.

\subsection{All axiom heads}

With parameters $D,A,H$, the six universally instantiated logical heads
are
\begin{align*}
 L_1(D,H)&=(\exists x_D\,U(D,H))\to H,\\
 L_2(D,A,H)&=U(D,U(A,H))\to U(A,U(D,H)),\\
 L_3(D,A,H)&=M(H,D)\to(E(H,A)\to M(A,D)),\\
 L_4(D,A,H)&=E(H,D)\to(M(A,H)\to M(A,D)),\\
 L_5(D,H)&=\exists x_D E(H,D),\\
 L_6^{\mathrm Q}(D,A,H)&=U(D,A\to H)\to
                     \bigl(U(D,A)\to U(D,H)\bigr).
\end{align*}
There is also the dominated implicational head
\[
 \rho(D,A,H,d)=((H\to d)\to D)\to
               ((D\to H)\to(A\to H)),\qquad d\le D.
\]
The inference rules are numerical modus ponens and generalization of
theorems $B\mapsto U(i,B)$.

Define the pointed-separation expressions
\begin{align*}
 V(a,b,c)&=M(a,b)\to U(c,M(a,b)),\\
 P(a,b,c)&=c\to M(c,b),\\
 S_*(a,b,c)&=U\bigl(b,(M(a,b)\to P(a,b,c))
                         \wedge(M(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
 Q_*(a,b,c)&=U(c,S_*(a,b,c)\to N(P(a,b,c))),\\
 G(a,b,c)&=N(V(a,b,c)\to Q_*(a,b,c)).
\end{align*}
The guarded output is $g(a,b,c)=G(a,b,c)$ if $c\ne a,b$, and zero
otherwise. The guards cannot be dropped merely because a shorter
emitter appears possible: they are part of the soundness argument.

The calculus $\CQ$ has the six displayed $L_i$ heads, every dominated
$\rho$ instance, every guarded gate $g$, every $\Gamma(B)$, and the two
inference rules. The original separate-head calculus $\CC$ differs
only by using
\[
 L_6(D,A,H)=U(D,A)\to
              \bigl(U(D,A\to H)\to U(D,H)\bigr).
\]

\subsection{Uniform premise exchange}

\begin{lemma}[Conclusion-preserving translations]\label{lem:exchange}
There are primitive-recursive translations between $\CC$ derivations
and $\CQ$ derivations that preserve the conclusion literally. PA proves
their correctness, including conclusion zero and conclusion one.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The unchanged dominated implicational basis derives the exchange formula
\[
 (P\to(Q\to R))\to(Q\to(P\to R)).
\]
Its derivation uses only the same restricted propositional head and MP;
it does not use universal distribution. Equivalently, the inherited
derivations of $K$ and $S$ support the usual deduction construction:
from hypotheses $P\to(Q\to R)$, $Q$, and $P$, two MPs give $R$.
The inherited theorem \file{PA196/Logic/ImplicationBasis.lean}
and the included finite exchange certificate record the needed basis.

Instantiate exchange with $P=U(D,A)$, $Q=U(D,A\to H)$,
$R=U(D,H)$. It converts either distribution head into the other.
Transform each proof tree recursively, replacing only the changed
axiom leaves with this fixed exchange derivation and retaining the MP
and generalization nodes. The translation is primitive recursive, and
induction on the proof tree is formalizable in PA. No substitution
into a freshness-restricted gate is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Inherited syntactic calibration]\label{lem:inherited}
For the original separate-head calculus there are primitive-recursive
translations between its refutations and PA refutations, with PA-provable
correctness. Thus
\[
 \PA\vdash \exists e\,\Prf_{\CC}(e,1)
       \leftrightarrow \exists p\,\Prf_{\PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner).
\]
\end{lemma}

This is the supplied ordinary mathematical calibration
\cite{primary,separate,diagonal}, not a newly proved kernel endpoint.
For clarity, Section~\ref{sec:calibration-audit} explains its two
syntactic directions and their delicate cases. Combining it with
Lemma~\ref{lem:exchange} gives the same statement for $\CQ$.

\section{Commands and a complete proof compiler}\label{sec:commands}

\subsection{Exact command semantics}

Write $[a;w]=p(a,w)$ and use the total Cantor projections
$\operatorname{hd}$ and $\operatorname{tl}$. In particular,
\[
 [0;0]=0,\qquad\operatorname{hd}(0)=\operatorname{tl}(0)=0.
\]
A command store is $(t,w,d,h)$: visible theorem, theorem stack,
arithmetic offset, and cached theorem. With $b=d+x$, the MP command is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mp}
 \mathsf{MP}_x(t,w,d,h)=
 \begin{cases}
 (b,w,b,0),&I(t,b)=h,\\
 (0,w,b,0),&I(t,b)\ne h.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
The successful branch has no stack push. Shift is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shift}
 \mathsf S(t,w,d,h)=
 \begin{cases}
 \mathsf{halt},&h=1,\\
 (h,\operatorname{tl}(w),d,\operatorname{hd}(w)),&h\ne1.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
For $D=d+x$, $H=h+y$, and $A=c$, the AX command is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ax}
 \mathsf{AX}_{c,x,y}(t,w,d,h)=
 \bigl(g(H,D,A),[q_1,\ldots,q_9,t;w],0,0\bigr),
\end{equation}
where, in stack-top order,
\[
 (q_1,\ldots,q_9)=
 (\Gamma(A),L_6^{\mathrm Q},L_5,L_4,L_3,L_2,L_1,
                   \rho(D,A,H,d),U(d,h)).
\]
There are ten pushed entries, including the saved old top. All generated
entries except the last are divisible by four; $U(d,h)$ has residue two
and is at least two. Neither the gate nor a generated entry is code one.
The free-constructor command audit binds the entire emitted stack, not
only this residue observation, to the exact grammar.

\subsection{Stack operations, with the zero aliases included}

Words below execute from left to right. Put $A_0=\mathsf{AX}_{0,0,0}$ and
\[
 \mathsf{Push}=A_0;\mathsf S^9;\mathsf{MP}_0,\quad
 \mathsf{Pop}=A_0;\mathsf S^{11},\quad
 \mathsf{Discard}=\mathsf{MP}_0,\quad
 \mathsf{Offset}_i=\mathsf{MP}_i.
\]
\begin{lemma}[Uniform command contracts]\label{lem:words}
For all displayed natural values,
\begin{align*}
 \mathsf{Push}(t,w,d,h)&=(0,[t;w],0,0),\\
 \mathsf{Pop}(t,[a;w],d,h)&=
 \begin{cases}\mathsf{halt},&t=1,\\(t,w,0,a),&t\ne1,
 \end{cases}\\
 \mathsf{Discard}(t,w,0,h)&=(0,w,0,0),\\
 \mathsf{Offset}_i(0,w,0,0)&=(0,w,i,0).
\end{align*}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
After AX, nine Shifts leave
$(\rho(D,A,H,d),[t;w],0,U(d,h))$; ten leave
$(U(d,h),w,0,t)$. Their tested caches are zero or generated non-one
codes, so none halts. Push's final MP fails because an implication has
residue zero whereas the cached universal code has residue two.
Pop's eleventh Shift tests the saved old top, giving the stated cases.
For Discard, both outcomes of MP agree when $d=x=0$.
For Offset, $I(0,i)>0$ if $i>0$; at $i=0$ the successful branch has
the same required output. These calculations use total projections,
so they also cover $a=w=0$ and collapsed zero pushes.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Axiom and generalization selectors}

From every normalized store $(t,w,0,0)$, the word
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{AX}_{A,D,H};
           \mathsf S^{j+1};\mathsf{Pop}^{9-j}\qquad(1\le j\le7)
\]
returns $(q_j,w,0,0)$ for the prescribed parameters. The unselected
generated tail and the saved zero are consumed; the selected top is
preserved. No generated head equals one. For the restricted head with
$u\le D$, use
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Offset}_u;
 \mathsf{AX}_{A,D-u,H};\mathsf S^9;\mathsf{Pop}.
\]
It returns $(\rho(D,A,H,u),w,0,0)$. The gate selector is
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{AX}_{A,D,H};\mathsf{Pop}^{10}.
\]
Truth uses Discard. These are selectors for the new Q head when
$j=2$; they are not silently selectors for the old premise order.

Generalization uses
\[
 \mathsf{Gen}_i=
 \mathsf{Push};\mathsf{Offset}_i;\mathsf S;
             \mathsf{AX}_{i,0,0};\mathsf S^{10}.
\]
Starting from $(a,w,0,0)$ it returns $(U(i,a),w,0,0)$.
Immediately before AX the store is $(0,w,i,a)$, and the last ten
Shifts select $U(i,a)$ while restoring the saved top zero to the cache.
The first Shift tests cache zero, so this word is valid even when $a=1$.

\subsection{Modus ponens and the reserved zero}

For proofs $e_1$ of $I(a,b)$ and $e_0$ of $a$, define the compiled MP word
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mp-compiler}
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Push};W_{e_1};\mathsf{Push};
 W_{e_0};\mathsf{Push};\mathsf S;\mathsf S;\mathsf{MP}_b;\mathsf{Pop}.
\end{equation}
The initial Discard and Push reserve a zero below both premise
computations. If neither recursive computation has halted, the store
before the two Shifts is
\[
                 (0,[a,I(a,b),0;w],0,0).
\]
The first Shift returns $(0,[I(a,b),0;w],0,a)$. The second halts if
$a=1$; otherwise it returns $(a,[0;w],0,I(a,b))$. MP then succeeds
and returns $(b,[0;w],b,0)$. By Lemma~\ref{lem:words}, Pop halts if
$b=1$ and otherwise returns $(b,w,0,0)$. The reserved zero is consumed
exactly once. Omitting it would destroy the arbitrary underlying stack.

\begin{proposition}[Compiler valid on every underlying stack]\label{prop:compile}
For every finite $\CQ$ derivation $e$, there is a primitive-recursively
constructed finite command word $W_e$ such that, from every
$(t,w,0,0)$, it either halts or returns
$(\operatorname{concl}(e),w,0,0)$. PA proves this statement by induction
on the coded derivation.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use the selectors for axiom leaves; define
$W_{\operatorname{Gen}(e,i)}=W_e;\mathsf{Gen}_i$ for generalization;
and use equation~\eqref{eq:mp-compiler} for MP nodes. An earlier halt in a
recursive word remains a halt after adjoining its suffix. The command
calculations prove the required stack framing in every case.
\end{proof}

If $e$ is a refutation, the word
\begin{equation}\label{eq:allstore}
                  A_0;W_e;\mathsf{Pop}
\end{equation}
halts from \emph{every} incoming store, not just from a cleared store.
The initial AX clears $d,h$; Proposition~\ref{prop:compile} applies
with arbitrary new top and stack. If the compiler returns code one,
the final Pop halts. This all-store property is necessary because the
scheduler retains its store between unsuccessful candidate words.

\subsection{Fair enumeration and syntactic soundness}

The inherited scheduler decodes finite command words using Cantor
pairing. If its continuation $L$ is positive, the next continuation is
at most $L-1$. If $L=0$, it parses the fresh candidate $N$, increments
$N$, and begins draining that continuation. Thus a finite command
sequence is eventually tried unless an earlier halt has occurred.
The all-store word~\eqref{eq:allstore} can be padded to obtain candidate
codes above any initial fresh counter. Coding words, bounding a finite
continuation, and reaching a specified fresh candidate use primitive
recursion and induction in PA. This is a finite cofinality argument,
not an assumption that every trial resets the registers.

For the converse, annotate the visible top, cached head, and each live
canonical stack entry with a $\CQ$ derivation. Canonical stack expansion
terminates because the tail of a positive Cantor code is smaller than
that code; zero padding receives the fixed truth derivation, supplied by
$\rho(0,0,0,0)=0$ with the permitted bound $0\le0$. AX emits
axiom instances, a generalization of the old cached theorem, and the
saved old top. MP success applies MP to the annotated premises; failure
returns truth. Shift transports the annotations and halts only when
its cached annotation concludes code one. There is no proof obligation
on the arithmetic offset $d$ itself.

\begin{theorem}[Command calibration]\label{thm:commands}
For the initialized source scheduler $R_{\mathrm{QHS}}$, PA proves
\[
 \Halt(R_{\mathrm{QHS}})\leftrightarrow
              \exists e\,\Prf_{\CQ}(e,1).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Completeness is the all-store proof compiler and fair finite-word
scheduler. Soundness is the finite proof-store annotation invariant.
All transformations manipulate codes of finite proofs and traces.
No uniform truth predicate or reflection principle for PA is used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exact state accounting and simultaneous folds}

The source uses the Q axiom-emission grammar, with 67 raw AX readers.
The symmetric controller has 135 other working controls. The raw

Each merge identifies readers entered with distinguishable preceding
symbols. A short detour recovers that symbol and reproduces the
appropriate original transition. Every context/read-bit case is checked
against the \emph{final simultaneously folded table}, including helpers
affected by other identifications. There are four cases per merge,
hence 32 local cases. Counting individually feasible pairs without this
joint replay would not justify the quotient.

\subsection{Source boundaries and the two independent executors}

The source has 3,650 primitive instruction sites and 4,750 outcomes
when both branches of every decrement are counted. A source frame
contains an encoded instruction path, zero padding, markers, a unary
register file, and spare blocks. The path and padding together have
length $48+n$: a path of length $|p|$ has $48+n-|p|$ padding zeros. The logical registers
are $(N,T,W,A,D,H,S,R,L)$ and their physical slots are
\[
                    [6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1].
\]
The contract domain covers every natural value of the nine registers,
both outcomes of decrement, every $n\ge0$, and every nonempty finite
spare list of natural values.

The construction checker obtains all 4,750 intended effects using its
symbolic register interface. A separate literal-tape checker uses a
different source resolver and symbolic representation. It rederives four
generic register-list lemmas from the actual rows and uses no arithmetic
box oracle. Its proof rules still have an ordinary mathematical
justification by induction on unary lengths and finite lists; Python
execution is not being described as a trusted proof-assistant kernel.

\subsection{Finite-trace equivalence inside PA}

\begin{proposition}[Physical refinement]\label{prop:physical}
PA proves
\[
                 \Halt(\QHS)\leftrightarrow\Halt(R_{\mathrm{QHS}}).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For a source halt, prepend the verified startup and concatenate the
positive physical segments certified for its successive instructions.
The symbolic list and scan lemmas supply finite trace witnesses for
every natural register value and frame size. Their primitive-recursive
construction and loop inductions are formalizable in PA.

Conversely, suppose the physical machine first halts at time $T$.
If the totalized source does not halt during its first $T+1$ instructions,
the startup followed by their positive certified simulations gives a
nonhalting physical trace extending past time $T$. Determinism
contradicts the assumed first halt. Thus a bounded source computation
finds a source halt. This argument avoids any use of an infinite
standard-model source execution inside arithmetic.
\end{proof}

\section{Assembly and the inherited logical proof}\label{sec:assembly}

\subsection{Two syntactic directions of the calibration}\label{sec:calibration-audit}

The supplied forward refutation compiler translates PA refutations
into the numerical separate-head calculus. Its interfaces include
two-way membership rewriting, neighborhood equality, an equality
witness, and the retained logical heads. The diagonal-vacuity
construction replaces the older equality-vacuity axiom using the
fresh label supplied by a formula's own code. The separate-head
presentation interderives its two heads with the earlier bundled
diagonal gate by uniform propositional proof templates. These are
proof translations, not a claim of external semantic equivalence alone.

In the other direction, interpret $M(a,b)$ by finite binary membership
$\operatorname{Bit}(x_a,x_b)$, $E$ by equality, and $U$ by universal
quantification. PA proves binary membership is irreflexive, so the
translation of code one is refutable. PA also proves, for each
arithmetic formula $\theta$,
\[
 \forall n\,\exists s<2^n\,\forall z<n\,
  \bigl(\operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\leftrightarrow\theta(z,\vec u)\bigr).
\]
The proof is induction on $n$, retaining the old binary code or adding
$2^n$ according to the instance $\theta(n,\vec u)$. Substitution into
this finite induction-proof template gives a primitive-recursive
proof-producing operation whose correctness PA verifies. It does not
give an arithmetic truth predicate for all formulas.

The pointed-separation proof has a separate coincident-label case.
When its labels $a,b$ are different, the finite-comprehension matrix
uses $(\operatorname{Bit}(x_a,z)\lor z=x_b)\wedge T(c)[z/x_b]$.
When $a=b$, the binder changes both occurrences, and the correct
matrix is $(z=x_b)\wedge T(c)[z/x_b]$; the relevant self-membership
antecedent is false. Treating these cases as identical would be an
error. Similarly, $I(0,0)=0$ and $p(0,0)=0$ are handled by explicit
proof and stack conventions, not ignored.

The latest incoming ordinary-PA audit checks these exact issues and
finds the supplied calibration coherent \cite{audit}. This report
uses that calibration as a mathematical dependency; its complete
formal closure is not represented by the local tape certificates.

\subsection{The theorem follows by finite proof transformations}

Combining the proved interfaces gives
\[
 \Halt(\QHS)
 \ \longleftrightarrow\ \Halt(R_{\mathrm{QHS}})
 \ \longleftrightarrow\ \exists e\,\Prf_{\CQ}(e,1)
 \ \longleftrightarrow\ \exists p\,\Prf_{\PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner).
\]
The first equivalence is Proposition~\ref{prop:physical}; the second
is Theorem~\ref{thm:commands}; the last combines
Lemmas~\ref{lem:exchange} and \ref{lem:inherited}. All three
equivalences have finite syntactic witnesses and PA-verifiable
correctness. Negating the final existential proves
Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.

\section{What transfers from ZFC and ConciseIPRA}

The ZFC work contributes methods directly useful at the physical level:
source/implementation separation, explicit register interfaces,
history-dependent folds, complete initial-frame checks, and elimination
of unnecessary successful-MP stack effects. The no-push idea has an
explicit ZFC ancestry, and the runtime193 marker-sharing construction
is a concrete transfer attempt. These credits matter because simply
reusing an old mechanism in a new packet does not make that mechanism
new.

ConciseIPRA contributes a useful discipline: state a compact calculus
completely, count the actual checker, and keep finite proof transformations
separate from reflection claims. Its theory cannot simply replace the
PA refutation checker in this task. The requested calibration is to
$\Con(\PA)$, and the inherited arithmetic interpretation uses full
induction instances in its finite-comprehension proof templates. A
shorter checker for iPRA does not supply a PA consistency machine with
fewer states. Any new theory must come with the required PA-verifiable
two-way refutation translations and a concrete physical compilation.

\section{Reproduction and deliverable structure}

The release requires Python 3.10 or later and the standard library for
its machine and certificate replay. The main command is
\begin{quote}\ttfamily
python3 -B verify.py
\end{quote}
It rebuilds the candidate, checks the exact tables and source binding,
replays the command checks and literal-tape contracts, and compares the
candidate with the included frontier representatives. Assertions must
remain enabled; do not run the assertion-based inherited checkers with
\file{-O} or \file{PYTHONOPTIMIZE}. The top-level verifier rejects such
execution rather than silently accepting disabled checks.

The full corpus census can also be regenerated against the pinned
repository using the documented command in \file{comparison/README.txt}.

two runs of
\begin{quote}\ttfamily
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error pa194qhs.tex
\end{quote}
from \file{report/}. No proof assistant is needed for this executable
replay; this fact is consistent with the explicitly stated absence of a
new end-to-end kernel proof.
