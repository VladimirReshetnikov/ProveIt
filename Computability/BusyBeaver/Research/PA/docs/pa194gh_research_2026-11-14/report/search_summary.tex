For the paired/triple bootstrap searches, the limits are 200,000 literal
transitions, absolute head coordinate 20,000, and 20,000 one-cells. The
cycle-aware pair replay partitions the 11,287,605 runs as
\[
11,159,792\text{ rays}+117,274\text{ halts}
 +10,047\text{ exact cycles}+492\text{ bounded}.
\]
The original 346-edit pool includes 19 speculative arithmetic-box edits.
Individual screening never implies that a pair or triple is a valid
runtime implementation. The absence of a startup frame was checked even
for these speculative combinations, making the startup search a broader
filter, not a family of already proved machines.

The liveness result is a concrete obstruction only to static coloring
under the chosen conservative abstraction. Its first all-nine witness is
source site 102, a decrement of W; the abstract witness has 3,070 states.
It does not prove that this abstract path is concretely realizable or
exclude semantic reconstruction, packed registers, or a different source
algorithm. Likewise the local contraction searches require a bounded
same-tape refinement within their stated windows; they are not exhaustive
over all two-way transducers or representation changes.

Separately, the GH emitter reassociation search used 3,000,000 stochastic
slots (2,633,281 scored evaluations and 368,507 skipped slots, including
the scoring of initial seeds). Its best projected total remained 194.
The raw GH table has eight standard history-fold opportunity edges with
maximum matching seven; the raw GHS table has nine with maximum matching
eight. The copied fold sets therefore did not omit an immediately
available eighth or ninth fold of that standard form. All these scores
still require source and final simultaneous-fold verification before a
candidate can be accepted.
