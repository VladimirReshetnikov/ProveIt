1. SOUNDNESS IN W+IR
-------------------
Use classical equality-free W (unrestricted power covering) + global IR;
the machine's equality atoms may be interpreted as ordinary equality for
the soundness direction. This larger signature has the same PA consistency
calibration since identity is available in the bit-membership model.

The radix-four zero is a deliberate alias: I(0,0)=0. Decode 0 as a fixed
closed tautology. Decode positive codes recursively by their tag, except
that I(x,1) is decoded as the negation of decoded x. The atomic code1 still
decodes as E(v0,v0). All nontrivial recursive formula fields are smaller than
their code. Under IR the decoder satisfies
  D(I(x,y)) <-> (D(x)->D(y))
even in the exceptional y=1 and x=y=0 cases. This yields a uniform derivation
for the MP soundness step, not just a standard-model truth claim. Quantifier
and relation constructors have their ordinary meanings; negation/existence
expressions decode as intended. Equivalently one may use ordinary positive
decoding and prove equivalence under IR by structural induction.

When n>=2, all labels occurring in D(n) are <n (the closed zero tautology
can be chosen to use only label0). Thus n is fresh in the matrix and is
distinct from the fixed source label0 and test label1. The guard G(n) is
Sub(v1,v0). Its binder n shadows the output binder only inside the guard,
where the output object is not referenced. The emitted N(Z(n)) is exactly
the weak cover for matrix D(n), source v0, tested object v1, output vn.
The clause order is lower then upper; classical currying gives the same W.
The source may be a free parameter of D(n), which W allows.

For n=1, the matrix is F=E(v0,v0), the output binder and test binder collide.
The upper clause is forall v1 not E(v1,v1), i.e. IR. Under IR the lower clause
is true because its matrix antecedent F is false. The outer output binder1
is vacuous in both clauses, as all occurrences of label1 within them are
already bound. Therefore N(Z(1)) is equivalent to IR under W+IR, and is
sound. This is a proof of the one exceptional case, not an untested freshness
assumption. n=0 MUST NOT be admitted: its collision could demand a set
containing all its own represented subsets, contrary to IR.

Whenever the outer gate is enabled, V is ordinary atomic membership
vacuity, since c differs from a and b. It remains valid for a=b. The bundle
N(I(V,Z)) is propositionally equivalent to V and N(Z), so is sound. A failed
gate outputs the tautology alias0. All retained logical rows are sound in
W+IR, including the merged equality row G and the restricted Lukasiewicz
row. Their two alias exceptions are handled by the same decoder lemma.

Full equality vacuity is not asserted directly. The finite pure-logic
interpretation instead replaces equality by two-sided membership
indiscernibility:
  Eind(x,y) = forall r(E(r,x)<->E(r,y))
              and forall r(E(x,r)<->E(y,r)),
where r is chosen fresh. The helper plus equality existence yields
existence for Eind; membership vacuity supplies all fresh-binder vacuity of
Eind after alpha-converting its auxiliary binder. Congruence, Euclid and

k218_translation_logic.mm0, and k218_translation_vacuity.mm0.
Since W and IR are equality-free, this equality interpretation fixes their
formulas and targets. Intermediate equality in ordinary proof conversions

Explosion from F is already a derived rule: rho6 is

basis this gives classical negation and conjunction projections.

3. IR AND EVERY W INSTANCE ARE AVAILABLE
--------------------------------------
Let tau quote ordinary named formulas, with tau(not A)=I(tau(A),1),
tau(E(x,y))=R(x,y), and the other constructors homomorphic. Numerical
identity I(1,1) is tau(not E(v0,v0)); theorem generalization at v0 gives
tau(IR). This is the same finite derivation as TB_IR in
sources/busy-beaver/ZF/m254/coq/logic/Comp_Bundle254.v. No extra seed and no
machine instruction for IR are required. Soundness of interpreting the same
code as an ordinary negation is supplied by the preceding decoder argument.

For every n>=1 choose c=n-1 and a=b=n. The outer gate holds. Projecting the
bundle yields N(Z(n)), so every regular matrix code is available. Choice of
a,b here controls only the independent vacuity conjunct; it does not alter
the fixed source/test labels in Z.

To recover an arbitrary ordinary W instance with matrix psi(x,A,p):
  1. Rename free roles injectively so source A has name0, test x has name1,
     and all other parameters have distinct fresh names. First use temporary
     fresh names if 0 or1 was already occupied. Alpha-rename bound variables
     to avoid capture. These are standard finite syntactic transformations.
  2. Replace its matrix by phi=not not psi. This is logically equivalent and
     ensures its quotation n=tau(phi)>1. If working through the equality
     interpretation, phi is equality-free and unchanged by it.
  3. Every label of phi is <n, proved by structural induction from
     P(x,y)>=x,y and positive tags. Thus output n is fresh, regardless of
     the size of the matrix or parameter list.
  4. Obtain N(Z(n)) with c=n-1 as above; its code is exactly the quotation of
     the ordinary fixed-role cover for phi. Replace the double-negated matrix
     by psi using ordinary logical congruence, then universally close and
     instantiate/alpha-convert to restore the requested parameter names.
No syntax traversal, validation, padding, or renaming is executed by the
machine. These operations belong to the finite completeness proof compiler.
The soundness direction does not need D(tau(phi))=phi literally; equivalence
under IR suffices. Completeness uses exact quotation identities.

5. INTERNAL PA CLAIM AND ITS LIMITS
---------------------------------
Every transformation just listed is a structural recursive transformation on
finite formulas/derivations plus a finite template for each axiom family.
Fresh-name selection, double-negation padding, the Cantor codec, formula
traversal, and proof concatenation are primitive recursive. Their correctness
is proved in PA by induction on formula structure and proof length. The
numeric bound/freshness lemmas are elementary arithmetic. The existing MM0
files give explicit arithmetic versions of the unchanged logic templates;
their final K215/ZFC equivalence is not used as a substitute for a new emitter
proof. Combined with separately established machine/program/enumerator
simulation, the argument yields PA-provability for the new literal table.

A statement of mathematical PA-provability is legitimate if all these
inductive simulations are proved. Calling the new table 'kernel-verified'
requires recompiling or producing a new proof certificate and must not be
inferred from tests, hashes, or the inherited K215 certificate.
