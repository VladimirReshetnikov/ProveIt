# Attribution and proof scope

The original ProveIt machines, research packets, numerical calculus,
calibration arguments and proof-assistant development belong to their
respective authors. In particular, the symbolic compiler and runtime depend
on James Chang's imported PA196 development and the later ProveIt research
packets. The original licenses and runtime notices are retained under
`reference/haz/licenses/`, with additional provenance beside the theory and
reduction research code.

Files in `reference/haz/` are copied from the pinned incoming HAZ archive
without textual modifications; their original archive member names and
hashes are recorded in `reference/haz/PROVENANCE.json`. Some reference files
are relocated into the relative paths expected by the inherited runtime.

The new report, analysis, reduction experiments and reconstruction scripts
were prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov. They do not claim authorship of the
inherited material or a completed sub-194 blank-start consistency machine.

The finite checkers and ordinary mathematical arguments are described
separately. No newly compiled end-to-end Lean proof for runtime193 is claimed.
