\subsection{A gap in boundary-only local contexts}

The source runtime represents some tape segments as constant-symbol runs
whose lengths depend affinely on an arbitrary natural parameter.  Its symbolic
stepper can execute a complete same-state scan over such a run in one symbolic
step.  An observer invoked only before symbolic steps sees the scan's entry
and exit, but need not see the intermediate physical head positions.

This distinction matters for local transition synthesis.  For example, a long
zero run presents only zeros ahead of its entry, whereas its last zero presents
the following one at distance one.  A proposed replacement of a scanner row
may agree at the observed entry window and disagree near that final zero.
Observing normalization points does not supply the omitted physical positions.

The inherited boundary-only collector admitted nine apparent replacements of
native row $35/0=1R35$.  Those candidates redirected the row to controls
\[
  17,\ 21,\ 41,\ 45,\ 61,\ 82,\ 144,\ 161,\ 190.
\]
None passed the generic symbolic source checker.  They are rejected heuristic
candidates, not proved neutral transformations.

\paragraph{Correction.}
For every accelerated constant-symbol self-loop, the revised collector adds
the local windows at all distinguishable positions of its scan.  For radius
$K$, run lengths above $2K+1$ need only retain positions near each end and a
middle position: the remaining length is invisible to the window.  The bits
already traversed have the row's written symbol; the untraversed bits retain
the run's original symbol.  Possible prefixes outside the run are combined
conservatively, discarding correlations rather than assuming them.

On the runtime-valid 193-state core, the corrected collector processes
6,245 scan observations.  At radius four the envelope grows from 8,331 to
8,357 contexts, and at radius six from 29,299 to 29,421.  The nine apparent
row-35 rewrites disappear.  Both corrected radii yield the same 346 candidates:
327 outside the arithmetic boxes and 19 speculative box-row candidates.
The latter retain a separate arithmetic correctness obligation.

\paragraph{Effect on the reported searches.}
The original 336 outside-box candidates, together with the unchanged core,
were screened from every one of the 193 existing blank initial controls.
The strongest screen accepted any complete physical source frame at source
sites 25 through 44, with arbitrary values of all nine registers, arbitrary
translation, padding $48+n$, and any finite nonempty spare tail.  It found
no such frame in 65,041 simulations and 101,840,170 literal transitions.
There were 674 halts, 62,799 certified outward blank rays, and 1,568 bounded
runs.  The bounds were 200,000 transitions, head displacement 20,000, and
support size 20,000 per run.  A bounded run is not a permanent-failure theorem.

These remain accurate records of the candidates actually simulated.  They
must not be promoted into proofs that the first-stage candidates were all
neutral, or into an impossibility theorem for 193-state constructions.  The
corrected 327-candidate non-box pool is a subset of the screened pool, and
radius six adds no new candidate.  The proposed two-edit search through
row 35 was rejected before second-stage synthesis.
