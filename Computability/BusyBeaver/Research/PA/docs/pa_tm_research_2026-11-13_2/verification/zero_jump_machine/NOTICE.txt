symbolic.py is a portable adaptation of the symbolic verifier in the incoming
conpa_tm_study_2026-11-13 submission. The adaptation changes reference paths and
replaces removable Python assertions with explicit validation exceptions.
verify.py adds a conjunction of known-zero branch hypotheses, independent CFG
resolution, inductive mask reconstruction, finite row-action synthesis, exact
startup checks, and explicit counterexamples. The source-mask transfer rule
follows the repository's verify_joint_mask.py. The direct counterexample was
independently cross-checked against the machine-reduction investigation.

The two files with rejected/wrong in their names are negative controls.
They must not be represented as validated Con(PA) machines. Their state count
alone does not establish a consistency equivalence or a new Busy Beaver bound.
