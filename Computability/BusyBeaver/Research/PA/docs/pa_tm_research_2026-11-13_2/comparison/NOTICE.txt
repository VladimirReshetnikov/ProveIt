The selected machines are copied byte-for-byte from the named ProveIt archives at commit 1fe9b29810bfe3d0e507146d65f6b97692e7444a. Their James Chang PA196 ancestor is MIT-licensed; LICENSE.JamesChang preserves its notice. The archive manifests retain exact original member names, archive identities and table hashes. Graph equivalence checks do not certify the correctness of a new arithmetic machine. The selected196 set intentionally includes two experimental and two rejected controls, labelled in the manifest and comparison report. The U208 tables and helper layouts derive from the original ProveIt submissions; attribution remains in their archived source packages. The current checker is an independent direct table validator adapted in design from the supplied rooted comparison checkers.
