Named proposals and identity
----------------------------
The named roster contains 104 distinct whole transition-table classes: 91 in
100 filed PA packets and 13 principal/supplementary proposals in the six current
incoming archives. Every one was independently parsed and counted: 194 working
states, 388 binary L/R rows, and no exact action-preserving state bisimulation
quotient below 194. The 38-family assignment in families38.csv is an explanatory
taxonomy; a different grouping can have a different number of families without
changing any machine count.

The larger literal corpus also contains many source-preserving intermediate
compositions, failed candidates, runtime-only cores, and synthetic controls.
A census of all 194-state graphs must not be described as a count of established
PA-consistency machines. The target-class totals and exact coverage boundary belong to coverage.json
and final_novelty.json, rather than to this named roster. The completed literal
capture is recorded in captured_census_receipt.json; the retained corpus covers
all filed catalog193/194 classes and every incoming literal194 class. Arbitrary
generators, prose-only specifications and unrecognized encodings are not
enumerated by this finite census.

Principal axes of comparison
----------------------------
1. Formula arithmetic and axiom heads: constructor coefficients 4, 5 and 6;
   exchanged Q distribution; Gamma(A)/Gamma(D) diagonals; explicit G and GD
   generalization/diagonal operands; witness-consequent H5X and J variants.
   These changes require logical proof translations, not just tape simulation.
2. Command protocol: ten-entry versus eleven-entry AX; original dummy-push,
   cached-result, or visible no-push MP; original sum guard versus cached-head
   guard; ordinary versus symmetric destructive comparison.
3. Source-flow changes: known-zero decrements replace returns; decoder trees
   are reassociated at four-increment, constructor-loop, operand, or MP sites.
   A source-address equality is useful only if hidden selector/entry uses are
   also preserved; ORH is the explicit repaired operand example.
4. Physical allocation and sharing: A/D, H/X, T/D/Y, five-register, four-cycle,
   cycle32 and nine-register layouts; direct history selectors; marker/reader
   separation. These need complete startup and arbitrary-frame contracts.
5. Exact physical acceleration: guarded three-to-one shortcuts and combinations
   F, SF, MF, LC/F. These preserve the count and reduce recurring execution
   paths; their evidence consists of incoming-edge invariants and complete-tape
   local identities with explicit positive time bounds.

Most source-preserving constructions keep the common arithmetic/register engine
and alter reader sharing or routing. The primary budget is 134 fixed controls
plus 67 readers minus seven justified folds, giving 194. Alternate trees can
have more reader controls and more folds without improving this net count.
For example, the symmetric witness realization states 135+69-10=194. Counting
source tokens, changed source addresses, or folds alone is not a state reduction.

Aliases that prevent overcounting
--------------------------------
PA194QN and PA194NQ are one class. PA194CSZ and PA194X are one class. PA194AX11QGH
and PA194AX11GH are one class. The cache-only design called PA194NS in one packet
is the class called PA194HS elsewhere; the separate sum-guard PA194NS is a
DIFFERENT class. PA194C is repeated as a principal table by five packets. PA194N6,
PA194QN6, QNS and several QH/AX11 compositions have repeat deliveries. The roster
keeps one graph-class row and preserves the naming relationships. A dense
numbering convention does not repair a native table whose actual start is
specified elsewhere; graph comparison therefore never guesses that native 0
is the blank start.

Proof status
------------
The organized PA README reports that the machine-specific end-to-end Lean/iPRA
assembly remains unfinished. The latest incoming manuscripts separately claim
an ordinary written absolute iPRA calibration for the revised AX11H source,
retained verbatim by descendants. These are different assertions: unfinished
formalization alone does not refute a written mathematical proof, and a finite
symbolic checker alone does not establish the absolute arithmetic calibration.
The named roster attributes the manuscripts' claims and does not independently
certify all 104 calibrations. The new candidate report must identify which exact
parent calibration it imports and which relative physical equivalence is new.

193-state audit
---------------
All 55 catalogued 193-state action graphs were recovered from the retained MF
comparison packet, and each filename's claimed canonical hash was recomputed
from literal rows. Every one of the 193 possible blank start controls was then
screened independently: 10,615 cases, comprising 112 finite halts, 10,499 proved
monotone outward rays, and four caps at 100,000 steps. A cap is unresolved by
that screen and is not evidence of a consistency equivalence.

The four caps come from two already rejected source constructions. The
016dd748... graph is a physically simulated coefficient-three calculus whose
logical interface is inconsistent: E3(1,0)=I3(0,1)=6, with a retained finite
refutation of code one. The 07e29492... graph is the rejected constructor-loop
reassociation: its positive branch never returns to the decrement test, instead
increasing T by four and register 6 by one per six source instructions. These
source failures are independent reasons not to accept the capped runs as a
smaller solution. Neither the audit nor any bounded search proves a general
194-state lower bound.
