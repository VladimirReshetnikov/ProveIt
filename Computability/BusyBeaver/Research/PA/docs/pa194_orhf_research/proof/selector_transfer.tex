\subsection{The ORH--B composition and a relative iPRA theorem}
Let $P$ be the pinned PA194ORH table and $B(P)$ the delivered
PA194ORHB table.  This construction composes two mechanisms already
present in the corpus: the ORH operand-reader layout and the B dedicated
history selector.  It does not claim either mechanism as new.  The exact
combination is absent from the audited corpus, including under arbitrary
control renaming, change of root, reflection, and symbol exchange.

In native numbering, the entire change is
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
\text{row}&P&B(P)\\\hline
87/0&0R301&0R88\\
88/0&0R122&0L301\\
301/0&0R45&0R122\\
301/1&1R103&1R301.
\end{array}
\]
Both tables retain 194 working controls, start 74 and halt 254.
Dense numbering uses start 0 and halt 194.  The arithmetic marker at
control 51 is unchanged; thus the failed borrowed-marker initialization
of the earlier 193-control runtime is not being reused.

Define $\rho(301)=88$ and $\rho(q)=q$ for all other old controls.
In $P$, the only incoming row to 88 is $77/1=1R88$ and the only incoming
row to 301 is $87/0=0R301$.  Neither is the start.  Therefore whenever
one of these controls is reached after at least one transition, the cell
immediately to the left of the head records its old identity: 1 for 88,
0 for 301.  Every ordinary old row is copied exactly after applying
$\rho$ to its source and target; there are 384 such row cases.

For either old exceptional control and either scanned bit, the new
machine starts in control 88.  When the scanned bit is 1, both old
controls take $1R103$, which remains a single new transition.  When the
scanned bit is 0, the new machine first takes $0L301$.  If the history
bit is 1, its next two transitions are $1R301$ and $0R122$; the complete
three-step segment has the effect of the old $88/0$ row.  If the history
bit is 0, its next four transitions are
\[
0R122,\quad 0L137,\quad 0R30,\quad 0R45,
\]
using the unchanged rows $122/0$, $137/0$, and $30/0$.
The resulting five-step segment has the effect of the old $301/0$ row.
Each segment preserves the history cell, changes the scanned cell exactly
as the old transition does, and ends at the same head position and
$\rho$-mapped control.  No segment reads outside the two displayed cells,
and none halts before its endpoint.  The independent finite certificate
checks these four identities directly from the two literal tables.

\paragraph{Primitive recursive trace maps.}
For a finite old run, replace each ordinary transition by its copied row
and each exceptional transition by the corresponding segment above.
If $a_t$ and $b_t$ count visits to $88/0$ and $301/0$ in its first $t$
transitions, respectively, the expanded prefix has length
\[
 F(t)=t+2a_t+4b_t,\qquad t\le F(t)\le5t.
\]
The old configuration sequence, the counts, and $F$ are primitive
recursive functions of the coded initial configuration and $t$.
Quantifier-free induction verifies the endpoint relation, including the
history invariant.  Conversely every new prefix is a prefix of these
successive finite segments.  Since each segment lasts at most five
transitions and has no premature halt, a new halting witness of length
$u$ contains an old halting witness of length at most $u$.
One may obtain that witness by bounded search over the old prefixes
$0,\ldots,u$.  This is again primitive recursive.  These constructions
are intuitionistically valid and yield the ordinary written relative
arithmetic theorem
\[
 \mathrm{iPRA}\vdash
 H_{B(P)}\leftrightarrow H_P,
 \qquad
 \mathrm{iPRA}\vdash
 \neg H_{B(P)}\leftrightarrow\neg H_P.
\]
Here the arithmetic assertion concerns the coded blank computations;
the same argument works uniformly for coded finite initial tapes.
The local identities themselves hold for arbitrary exterior tape.
This is a proof by finite primitive recursive trace transformations,
not a new compiled Lean theorem.

Consequently any separately established iPRA equivalence
$\neg H_P\leftrightarrow\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{PA})$ transfers to this
composition.  The finite certificate does not by itself establish that
parent consistency equivalence.  The separately replayed literal
runtime checks derive four unary-list induction lemmas, cover all 4,778
primitive source outcomes, and verify the complete blank initialization
at 58,034 transitions.  Their role is independent validation of the
physical implementation; the relative halting proof above does not
assume those checks or the logical calibration.
