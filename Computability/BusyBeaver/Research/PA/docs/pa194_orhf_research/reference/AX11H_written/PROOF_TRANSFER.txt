Universal physical simulation
-----------------------------

For source path p, gap n>=0, nine arbitrary natural logical register values
v, and an arbitrary finite nonempty list of spare values, use the frame

  1 p 0^(48+n-|p|) 1 B(u_0)...B(u_8) B(spares), B(v)=0 1^(v+1),

with native boundary control 84 and the designated allocation u_phi(r)=v_r.
The left marker is one cell to the left of the head; the remaining exterior
is blank. Increment, positive decrement, zero decrement, jump and halt use
the same logical source transitions as the parent, with their unchanged
auxiliary spare/gap updates.

There are 4,778 cases because each decrement contributes zero and positive
outcomes. The lightweight Python and JavaScript checks both pass all cases,
recording 1,515,221 symbolic iterations with a maximum of 506. They use the
unchanged generic arithmetic-box lemmas explicitly.

The stronger literal check is verify_literal.py HC4 in the parent directory.
It uses the exact interpreter audited in the parent's physical_ipra_audit.tex.
It derives four arbitrary-list traversal lemmas from the candidate's actual
rows, resolves the complete source graph in two independent ways, and computes
an affine physical duration and exact successor frame for every case. The
completed run passed all 4,778 outcomes, with 1,095,048 symbolic events and
maximum 403 per outcome. Every affine clock has nonnegative coefficients and
constant at least 3. It also independently checks the complete blank tape
after 57,406 steps. Full clock vectors and list-lemma proofs are retained in
receipts/literal/, whose receipt reports PASS and arithmetic_box_oracles=false.

iPRA transfer, with explicit witness transformations
---------------------------------------------------

The parent's physical_ipra_audit.tex proves the soundness in iPRA of the
literal interpreter rules, including unary sweeps, arbitrary-list induction,
normalization, and composition with variable positive clocks. Those proofs
are generic in the actual transition rows checked at each event. They also
allow each physical block to carry its own independent logical value. The
new six dispatches do not change the representation algebra or any list rule.
Consequently the child's successful literal certificates instantiate the
same theorem with its new allocation.

For the clock construction, extend the source state by ghost data for the
head coordinate, header gap and complete spare list, updated by the exact
local contracts. Projection to the original site/nine-register source state
commutes with every step and preserves the halt flag; quantifier-free
induction proves this from the stated initial ghost data. The extension
introduces no physical control or tape symbol.

Here is the composition argument in primitive-recursive terms. Let s(k)
be the k-th absorbing extended source configuration starting at the common seed.
Let E_phi(s) encode its full physical frame for allocation phi, mapping the
canonical source halt to an absorbing physical halt code. The finite literal
certificates yield a primitive-recursive duration d_phi(s), positive while
s is live and zero after halt, for which iPRA proves

  Run_phi(d_phi(s),E_phi(s)) = E_phi(SourceStep(s)).

All parameters, including list widths and the changing gap, remain in s.
The clock is obtained from the proved affine duration vectors for the current
instruction and branch. It is not computed by waiting for an unspecified exit.
Define tau_phi(0)=0 and
tau_phi(k+1)=tau_phi(k)+d_phi(s(k)). Quantifier-free induction gives

  Run_phi(startup_phi+tau_phi(k),blank) = E_phi(s(k)).

If the source halts at k, this equality gives a physical witness at the
displayed primitive-recursive time. Conversely, suppose the physical machine
halts at time t. The Boolean source halt flag at s(t) is decidable. If it were
zero, source absorption would imply that all earlier source configurations
are live; hence tau_phi(t)>=t. Physical absorption and the displayed equation
would then identify a halted physical configuration with a live E_phi(s(t)),
a contradiction. Thus s(t) is halted, giving an explicit source witness t.
This argument uses finite Boolean case distinction, not Markov's principle
or an unbounded search for the first source halt.

Therefore iPRA proves Halt(child) iff Halt(the unchanged source). Applying
the parent's corresponding physical/source theorem proves

  iPRA |- Halt(child) <-> Halt(parent).

Intuitionistic propositional reasoning gives equivalence of their negations.
Finally use the written theorem of the exact AX11H parent in
reference/proof/consistency_equivalence.tex. Its PA consistency predicate is
the current Foundation PA predicate specified in that packet. The conclusion is

  iPRA |- Con(PA) <-> not Halt_blank(PA194AX11HC4).

This last theorem inherits the correctness of the parent's written logical
calibration. The new work is the fully specified physical reallocation and
the literal local proof certificates that transport that result. It is not
an independent reproof of the entire PA-to-numerical-calculus compiler, and
no new end-to-end proof-assistant theorem is claimed.

The rejected H/X composition
---------------------------

Applying the first candidate's additional H/X swap to AX11H preserves every
source contract but did not reach the exact calibrated seed within 500,000
steps. It is not presented as a solution. A bounded screen tested all 720
allocations fixing physical positions 0,1,6: 24 reached the required exact
seed, 127 halted first, and 569 reached the stated bound. Bounded failures are
not proofs of permanent failure or a global lower bound. HC4 was selected
from the 24 successful cases and then independently rechecked.
