\section{The consistency-to-nonhalting direction}
\label{sec:consistency-nonhalting}

The completed source binding allows one machine-specific consistency
direction to be obtained from existing logical soundness results in the
pinned repository.  This section states the composition precisely.  It
uses soundness independently of the forward compiler from arbitrary PA
refutations to numerical proofs, which is constructed later.  It uses the repository's already established
one-way transfer from candidate-PRA models with an $\mathrm I\Sigma_1$
arithmetic reduct.  All uses of those existing results are mathematical
citations; no new Lean build or fresh rebuild of their entire dependency
chain is claimed.

\subsection{The exact numerical soundness interface}

Work in an arbitrary model $V$ of candidate PRA whose arithmetic reduct
satisfies $\mathrm I\Sigma_1$.  The fixed source translation in the
repository assigns an internal PA formula $\operatorname{Tr}(a)$ to
every numerical code $a$, including nonstandard codes.  Define
\[
 \operatorname{Good}(a)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 V\models\operatorname{Prov}_{\PA}(\operatorname{Tr}(a)).
\]
The source translation has a $\Sigma_1$ graph in the reduct, its output
is an internal well-formed formula, and Good is a $\Sigma_1$ predicate.
These are the interfaces used in
\file{pa195-calibration/PA194SourceLineSoundness.lean} and
\file{pa194-target-source/PA194TargetSource.lean}; the latter's
\file{target_sound} proof lists the individual seed and rule
preservation results explicitly\cite{reverse-source}.

The numerical constructor identities match those in
Section~\ref{sec:ax11h-preservation} exactly:
\[
 I(a,b)=4p(a,b),\quad M(a,b)=I(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(a,b)=I(b,a)+2,\quad E(a,b)=I(a,b)+3.
\]
Here the source's Cantor function is the same triangle-plus-second-
argument function $p$.  The repository identifiers correspond as follows.

The six ordinary seeds, restricted rho seed, and diagonal are Good by the

The restriction $u\le D$ is respected.  The zero alias is also covered:

\subsection{The exchanged sixth head}

The Q case needs a small additional argument, because the pinned target
seed table uses the original sixth head.  Put
\[
 a=U(D,A),\qquad b=U(D,I(A,H)),\qquad c=U(D,H).
\]
All three codes are at least two.  Consequently every outer implication
in $I(a,I(b,c))$ and $I(b,I(a,c))$ is nonzero.
The model-uniform \file{implication_code} theorem identifies their
positive translations with
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(a)\to
      (\operatorname{Tr}(b)\to\operatorname{Tr}(c)),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}(b)\to
      (\operatorname{Tr}(a)\to\operatorname{Tr}(c)),
\]
respectively\cite{reverse-mp}.

The first has an internal PA proof by ordinary sixth-head preservation.
A fixed propositional deduction yields the second: assume
$\operatorname{Tr}(b)$, assume $\operatorname{Tr}(a)$, use the given
implication and these two assumptions by MP, then discharge the two
assumptions in reverse order.  This is one fixed proof template with
three formula holes.  Substituting arbitrary internal formula codes and
concatenating its fixed inference pieces are primitive recursive proof
operations; checking this template does not invoke induction on an
arbitrary PA induction formula.  Equivalently, it is the internal
propositional premise-exchange theorem applied to three well-formed
formula objects.  Therefore Good holds for $h_6^Q(D,A,H)$ uniformly
in every such model.  This argument does not assume an iPRA calibration
of the Q numerical calculus or import the Q machine's different MP
interface.

\subsection{From a numerical refutation to an internal PA refutation}

Let $e$ be an internal valid derivation list in $\mathcal C_4$ or
$\mathcal C_4^Q$, in exactly the line format of the abstract compiler.
Induct over its line number using the cumulative invariant that every
earlier conclusion is Good.  This induction is allowed in
$\mathrm I\Sigma_1$: explicitly, the invariant asserts the existence
of a finite vector of internal PA proof witnesses for those earlier
conclusions.  Its verification is bounded once that vector and the
translation computations are given, so it is a $\Sigma_1$ assertion.
Bounded $\Sigma_1$ collection, available in $\mathrm I\Sigma_1$, gives
the equivalent bounded conjunction formulation.  At an axiom line use
the seed supplier just listed; at MP or generalization use the respective
uniform preservation theorem.  Append the resulting PA proof witness to
the vector.  Premise indices are strictly earlier, so the cumulative
invariant contains both witnesses needed by MP.

This establishes, for arbitrary internal proof codes,
\[
 \operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal C_4}(e,1)
 \ \lor\ \operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal C_4^Q}(e,1)
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \operatorname{Good}(1).
\]
The existing contradiction translation and internal refutation lemma
turn Good$(1)$ into internal PA provability of falsum.  This is exactly
the final step of \file{PA194TargetSource.target_refutes_PA};
it does not identify code 1 with the raw PA falsum code by fiat.
Hence either numerical calculus refutation yields an internal PA
refutation in every candidate-PRA model with an $\mathrm I\Sigma_1$
reduct\cite{reverse-source}.

Notice the strength being used.  This section permits $\Sigma_1$
induction in those models, rather than calling an unbounded provability
invariant quantifier-free.  The following independent transfer theorem
is what supplies the desired intuitionistic PRA consequence.

\subsection{The literal machines and the one-way iPRA transfer}

For either delivered dense table $M$, define the primitive recursive
function
\[
 \chi_M(t)=\mathbf 1\{\operatorname{Run}_M(t,\mathrm{blank})
                         \text{ is halted}\}.
\]
The complete binary-tape representation and step equations are those
already proved in Section~\ref{sec:physical-ipra}.  Thus
$h_M(t)\equiv(\chi_M(t)=1)$ is an open atomic formula of the usual PRA
language with primitive recursive function symbols.  Its function code
is the fixed composition and primitive recursion specified there;
no unbounded termination search defines it.

The written iPRA physical/source and source/command theorems are valid
also in every classical model of candidate PRA.  The former may use a
single absorbing halt code, while the latter retains the halted register
file.  The primitive recursive map which forgets that file only at halt
commutes with every source step and preserves the halt bit.  Therefore
the two source representations have identical finite halting sentences.
Composing the two simulations with abstract soundness gives
\[
 \exists t\,h_M(t)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \exists e\,\operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal C}(e,1),
\]
where $\mathcal C$ is the appropriate one of the two calculi.
The preceding subsection then gives an internal PA refutation in every
candidate-PRA model whose reduct satisfies $\mathrm I\Sigma_1$.
The fixed refutation bridge identifies this with the mapped PA-refutation
sentence used by the repository's consistency endpoint.

Apply
\file{ConPA.Directions.ipra_consistency_implies_nonhalt}
to the open matrix $h_M$.  Its two hypotheses are precisely openness and
the all-model halting-to-refutation implication just proved.
Its PA witness certificate is already supplied by the unconditional
transfer chain, rather than being a new machine-specific assumption.
The theorem uses the established conservation and negative-translation
results to conclude the intuitionistic implication\cite{one-way-transfer}.

\begin{theorem}[One completed consistency direction]
For each of the two literal binary blank-start tables supplied here,
\[
 \mathrm{iPRA}\ \vdash\
 \operatorname{Con}(\PA)\ \longrightarrow\
                   \neg\operatorname{Halt}_{\rm blank}(M).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The complete chain of verified arithmetic interfaces, numerical seed
soundness, internal proof-list induction, and the cited one-way transfer
is the argument above.  Every hypothesis of that transfer has been
identified.  The reasoning does not use PA consistency as a metatheoretic
assumption, and it does not use Markov's principle.
\end{proof}
