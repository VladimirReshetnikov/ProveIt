Conclusion
----------
The revised AX11H packet presents a substantive ordinary written
mathematical calibration in iPRA, rather than merely claiming a future
Lean endpoint. An unfinished end-to-end Lean formalization does not show
that this written mathematical theorem is absent. No concrete flaw was
found in the portions reviewed. This conclusion is a review of the
written argument, not a new kernel verification of all native templates.

The exact AX11HC4 source is the calibrated AX11H source with a different
physical register allocation. Its new uniform relative simulation may
therefore compose with the revised packet's written absolute theorem.
C32 preserves a different, original primary source: an ordinary PA
calibration of that source cannot by itself be promoted to an iPRA
calibration. The revised packet explicitly warns against that inference.

The physical proof defines finite binary tape words and two tape halves,
proves all four list sweep rules by quantifier-free induction, and binds
literal transitions to arithmetic frame endpoints. It uses exact symbolic
normalization identities and explicit restrictions ensuring that an
opaque context is not inspected. Its padding invariant is tied to actual
available list widths. Complete successor frame closure, positive clocks,
and a fixed full blank-start computation are explicit ingredients.

Finite certificate acceptance supplies closed arithmetic facts; the
uniform rules are separately proved for arbitrary natural inputs, which
is the relevant distinction for nonstandard models. A successful finite
simulation sample is not substituted for a uniform frame theorem.

The source/command argument gives unpairing, pairing, comparison, clear,
copy, transfer and arithmetic clocks. It binds the complete literal source
instruction array to these templates and computes the full AX expression.
The four AX operands include the actual extra z field consumed by MP
fallthrough. The fresh candidate counter increments when a candidate is
selected. Stack contents are carried between candidates rather than reset.

The abstract proof compiler uses an all-store invariant with a bounded
Boolean formulation and a primitive-recursive majorant. Its induction
does not silently use an unbounded forall-store/existential-proof formula
as quantifier-free induction. The command proofs include zero aliases of
Cantor pairing. The source parser handles every natural candidate code.

Independent forward-compiler review
-----------------------------------
Another team member reviewed proof/forward_calibration_completion.tex,
including the finite-demand request/response construction, coverage
budgets F4--F7, label and term realization, induction supplier F13--F15,
and outer compiler F16.

The construction computes child requests before child responses;
same-stratum ranks strictly decrease; earlier strata are completed first.
Its correctness predicates are bounded Boolean tree predicates rather
than an unbounded forall-environment/existential-proof induction premise.
For the explicitly checked coverage step, m=max(l,l')+1 and child q<=p-1
give the required m+q<=z bound. The text replaces earlier stronger-PA
theorem invocations with their finite constructor recipes.

This review found the primitive-recursive/I-sigma-1 method sound in
outline and found no concrete flaw. It did not independently check every
join in the many native finite proof templates.

Absolute consistency endpoint
-----------------------------
The packet composes a current Foundation proof adapter, a numerical
proof compiler with the displayed weak arithmetic discipline, exact
source and physical simulations, and existing one-way negative-translation
or conservation endpoints. It does not invoke a general Markov inference
from not-not-existence to an arbitrary witness.

The endpoint is the current nativePAConsistency predicate; native false
code 13 and numerical false code 1 are translated, not identified.
The final halting bound displayed by the source is

  57922 + tau(B((N_e+2)^2)),

where e is the translated refutation, V_e=A0;W_e;Pop is its halting word,
and N_e is the explicitly nested Cantor code of the word and its fields.
The fixed 57922 prefix is the parent's full physical blank startup.
A physically reallocated child uses its own separately proved startup
and physical clock while preserving the logical/source word interface.
