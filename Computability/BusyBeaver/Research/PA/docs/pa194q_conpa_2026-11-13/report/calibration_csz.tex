\section{A second construction: symmetric Shift with a zero-register jump}
\label{sec:csz}

The optional second machine, PA194CSZ, combines the supplied symmetric-Shift
controller PA194CS with the supplied zero-register realization of one source
jump. It has 194 working controls and 388 transitions. The construction is
not a new sub-194 result. Its contribution is the new composition, a complete
common invariant certificate, and a fresh independent literal-tape audit.

\subsection{Exact source and physical changes}

Use the logical register order
\[
 (r_0,\ldots,r_8)=(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L).
\]
The supplied PA194CS source has 3,646 primitive sites. In its source tree,
\texttt{node22} has the two leaves
\texttt{dec 8} and \texttt{breakTo 1}. Replace the latter leaf by
\texttt{dec 6}. The entire resolved source map changes at just site 26:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|c}
 \text{PA194CS} & \text{PA194CSZ}\\\hline
 26:\operatorname{jump}34&
 26:\operatorname{dec}r_6\,[34,27].
 \end{array}
\]
The first decrement successor is its zero branch. Every other opcode,
register, and successor is identical. The new source still has 3,646 sites
and now has 4,746 primitive branch outcomes, one more than its parent.

The literal native tables differ in just one row:
\[
 (27,1):\quad 0L40\quad\longmapsto\quad1R21.
\]
Native start and halt are 74 and 254. The delivered dense numbering uses
start 0, working states $0,\ldots,193$, and separate halt label 194.
The head begins at cell zero on a two-way all-zero binary tape.

\subsection{A finite common invariant proves source preservation}

For a concrete register vector $r\in\mathbb N^9$, define its positivity mask
\[
 \mu(r)=\sum_{i=0}^{8}2^i[\,r_i>0\,]\in\{0,\ldots,511\}.
\]
The finite abstract transition system acts on pairs $(p,m)$ of a source
site and a positivity mask. A jump retains the mask. Incrementing $r_i$
sets bit $i$. A decrement with bit $i$ zero takes the zero successor and
retains the mask; with bit $i$ one it takes the positive successor, with
bit $i$ either retained or cleared. A halt has no successors. Every concrete
step projects to one of these abstract steps. In the positive decrement
case, the two abstract possibilities distinguish $r_i=1$ from $r_i>1$;
no bounded-register assumption is involved.

Starting with $(25,1)$, the two source graphs have the same abstract reachable
closure $R$, containing exactly \textbf{263,180 pairs}. The delivered finite
certificate checks all of the following:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $(25,1)\in R$;
\item $R$ is closed under every abstract successor for both graphs;
\item the two abstract successor sets agree at every element of $R$;
\item the 32 masks occurring in $R$ at site 26 all have bit 6 zero.
\end{enumerate}
The first checker computes the two closures independently. The second checker
reads the finite relation and verifies its seed, range, closure, common
successors, and site-26 property without importing the builder.

The concrete clean source seed is
\[
 (p,r)=(25,(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)),
\]
whose mask is 1. Induction on execution length and abstract-step soundness
therefore prove $r_6=0$ whenever either reachable execution visits site 26.
At that site, the new decrement consequently leaves the complete register
vector unchanged and goes to site 34, exactly like the old jump. All other
source instructions are equal. A second induction shows that the two
concrete source executions have identical program counters and register
vectors at every finite step, including the same halting outcome.

This proof makes no consistency assumption and uses no empirical observation
of a long blank run. The masks overapproximate unbounded register values.
The relation is finite and explicit, and the projection and induction are
primitive recursive arithmetic reasoning available in PA.

\subsection{Physical realization and exact initialization}

The fully unfolded table has 202 controls: 135 fixed controls and 67 AX
readers. The seven original decoder folds and the symmetric-Shift fold
$41\mapsto330$ are applied together, leaving
\[
                    135+67-8=194.
\]
All 32 two-cell local cases are verified against the final simultaneous
quotient, with arbitrary unchanged exterior tape; the maximum detour has
five physical transitions. Thus the new jump realization has not invalidated
an earlier fold by changing a helper action.

The original symbolic runtime verifies all 4,746 primitive outcomes for
both the raw and folded tables. For the folded table it uses 1,513,882
symbolic transitions, with at most 508 per outcome. The extra positive
branch at site 26 is checked even though the invariant excludes it in the
blank-start source execution.

A separate literal-tape checker rederives four generic list-sweep lemmas
from the actual transition rows and checks all source outcomes without the
first checker's arithmetic-block oracle. Its complete replay uses 1,100,566
symbolic transitions, at most 403 per outcome, with minimum positive-duration
constant 3. It also resolves the source graph
in two independent ways. The contracts quantify over nine arbitrary natural
register values, header distance $48+n$ with $n\ge0$ (zero gap
$48+n-|p|$ for address $p$, as in the principal physical-frame convention),
and an arbitrary nonempty finite spare list. They are not tests over
bounded numerical examples.

The folded machine reaches the complete clean source frame after exactly
\textbf{58,014} physical transitions. The independent bytearray simulator
compares the entire tape, not merely the main registers or a window. The
head is at $-2$ in native state 84, the source site is 25, the logical
register vector is the seed above, and all 26 spare registers have value
one. No earlier halt occurs. The fully unfolded table reaches its clean
seed after 57,840 transitions. The old symmetric-Shift table reaches its
seed after 57,768, so no speed improvement is claimed for this composition.

Concatenating positive physical source-step simulations, and prepending
initialization, proves that each literal table halts precisely when its
source program halts. Conversely, a literal halt after $t$ transitions
cannot coexist with $t+1$ nonhalting source steps: their positive simulations
would deterministically reproduce more than $t$ nonhalting physical steps.
Together with the common-source invariant, this gives the new transfer
\[
 \mathrm{PA}\vdash
 \operatorname{Halt}(\mathrm{PA194CSZ})
 \longleftrightarrow
 \operatorname{Halt}(\mathrm{PA194CS}).
\]
The inherited ordinary mathematical calibration of PA194CS then transfers
its nonhalting/consistency biconditional to PA194CSZ. This does not claim a
new end-to-end Lean or Rocq kernel theorem, or completion of the repository's
conditional iPRA endpoint.
