\begin{abstract}
We construct \textbf{PA194Q}, a deterministic binary single-tape machine
with 194 working states, whose blank-tape nonhalting is equivalent to
$\Con(\PA)$ by an ordinary mathematical proof formalizable in PA.
\textbf{No machine with fewer than 194 working states was established.}
The result meets the requested alternative of a materially different
194-state design.

PA194Q exchanges the two premises of the quantifier-distribution axiom
emitted by the existing separate-head source. The source transformation
was proposed in earlier larger-machine research; the new contribution is
its complete 194-state realization, decoder and compatible folds, exact
source binding, and independent literal-tape verification. Its resolved
source differs at 79 of 3,649 instructions from PA194C. Both proof calculi
have the same theorems by explicit, conclusion-preserving proof
translations. Its allocation is $134+67-7=194$.

The fresh comparison is pinned to ProveIt commit
\nolinkurl{17f92105e2bc4317feae6c219cb2143437b482ff}.
It includes 41 delivered packets and recognizes 4,417 literal-table
occurrences, 620 byte forms and 446 action-graph classes. The eight existing
194-state graphs include a rejected scanner, repeated copies of PA194C,
and previously supplied symmetric, alternative-decoder and zero-jump
variants. PA194Q is absent even under arbitrary control renaming, change
of start, tape reflection and symbol complementation.

All 4,749 arbitrary-register source outcomes pass two physical checks;
the independent checker rederives four list lemmas from literal rows.
An exact 57,922-step blank initialization reaches the intended complete
source frame. A second new 194-state machine, PA194CSZ, combines symmetric
Shift with a zero-register jump using a 263,180-pair inductive certificate.
The archive contains both complete tables, deterministic builders, source
maps, verifiers, certificates, comparison data, this PDF and its complete
LaTeX source. The logical PA calibration is explicitly inherited; no new
end-to-end Lean or Rocq theorem or completed iPRA endpoint is asserted.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result and precise machine convention}

The principal machine is \file{machine/pa194q.tm}. Its working states are
$0,\ldots,193$, initial state is 0, and terminal halt label is 194.
The single tape is two-way infinite, with alphabet $\{0,1\}$ and all
initial cells zero. The head initially scans cell zero. Each transition
writes one bit and moves exactly one cell left or right. The table has
388 rows, two for every working state and none for the halt label.
All working states are reachable in the pointed transition graph; this
is not a claim that the actual blank computation visits each state.

Each row has the form
\begin{center}\ttfamily state\quad read\quad write\quad L-or-R\quad next-state.\end{center}
The authoritative dense-table SHA-256 is
\begin{quote}\small
\nolinkurl{48e98b5504dc5b3f20a2d040cb54ed1c96bee4c5e32ea27ae98ffada4dce7ea1}.
\end{quote}
The optional second table is
\file{secondary/pa194csz/machine_csz/pa194CSZ.tm}, with the same tape,
state, start and halt conventions. Its SHA-256 is
\begin{quote}\small
\nolinkurl{62e93098caf7c09ff2938d07064f0dbbb47c71ec369892968258fff708592269}.
\end{quote}
Appendices~\ref{app:qtable} and~\ref{app:csztable} print every transition.

Let $H_M$ be the usual arithmetic sentence asserting a finite literal
halting trace from the specified blank start. The principal conclusion is
\[
 \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg H_{\mathrm{PA194Q}}
                         \leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
\]
The new preservation theorem is
$\PA\vdash H_{\mathrm{PA194Q}}\leftrightarrow H_{\mathrm{PA194C}}$.
It is proved through interderivable numerical calculi and their source
simulations, not by asserting that the two instruction graphs are equal.
The second construction instead has a step-for-step source preservation
proof on an inductive invariant. Section~\ref{sec:q-calibration} separates
these new results from the inherited PA calibration.

\subsection{The 195-, 196- and 208-state families}

The important 195-state constructions are the original bundled-diagonal
PA195, the separate-head 195S, cached 195C, a distinct intermediate separate
195 layout, the bundled-diagonal cached PA195DC, and M195S8 with its longer
return scanner. Their source lengths are respectively 3,700, 3,673, 3,649,
3,673, 3,676 and 3,700 sites. The two successful 194 allocations both leave
60 effective decoder controls: either $67-7$ or $68-8$. Their savings
cannot be added just because their fold counts differ.

The 196-state work includes decoder reassociation, altered helper returns,
physical register permutations, cached-head commands, zero-register jumps,
and the diagonal and separate-head logical reductions. The NEG18 source
saves many primitive instructions yet retains 197 machine states in its
supplied implementation. A smaller source DAG or fewer source instructions
therefore need not reduce the binary machine: reader sharing, scanner
limitations and compatible helper reuse determine the final count.

Several early 208-state reports give conditional-context, entry-context,
history and three-cell explanations of the same physical identification.
They are alternative presentations of a shared saving, rather than four
independent savings. Later helper layouts add distinct constructions, but
the much larger raw-table census also contains unsuccessful experiments.
The imported James Chang PA209 lineage and the U209 parents are distinct;
shared ancestry does not identify every machine with the same state count.
The detailed ledger preserves each packet's proposed improvement and
reported failure or evidence boundary.

\subsection{Formalization status}

The current PA README explicitly states that the machine-specific iPRA
consistency reductions remain open for the primary PA194, original PA195
and PA196 targets~\cite{pa-readme}. It distinguishes ordinary finite-run
Lean theorems, Python certificates, conditional arithmetic endpoints and a
completed weak-arithmetic consistency equivalence. The imported PA196
formal development supplies the PA implication from nonhalting to
consistency. The converse cannot be inferred merely from external
nonhalting. The supplied later manuscripts give an ordinary syntactic
PA calibration addressing that converse; this report uses it as an
identified mathematical dependency, and does not relabel it as a new
kernel-checked theorem.

\section{The new emitter and its 194-state realization}

\subsection{A changed axiom at unchanged stack position}

With $I(a,b)=4p(a,b)$ and $U(D,A)=4p(A,D)+2$, define
\[
 P=U(D,A),\quad Q=U(D,I(A,H)),\quad R=U(D,H).
\]
The existing sixth head is $P\to(Q\to R)$; PA194Q emits
$Q\to(P\to R)$. It remains the second generated stack entry below the
separate diagonal head. Every other emitted head, guard, command and
scheduler component is retained.

This is a different numerical computation. At $A=1$, $D=H=0$,
$(P,Q,R)=(6,42,2)$. The old head has code 31,609,172; the new head has
code 76,268. The emitted theorem families are interderivable, but the
machine does not enumerate identical codes in identical stores.
Exactly 79 of the 3,649 resolved source instructions change. All others,
including their targets, agree with PA194C. The complete 190-token AX word
differs only in its 19-token sixth-head block. The new source's longest
address has length 36, compared with 37 for PA194C.

The premise exchange itself is not claimed as a new logical discovery.
It appears among the supplied earlier source-variant proposals, including
the PA195 diagonal-combined research~\cite{source-variants}. What was not
in the pinned corpus is this 194-state literal realization and its full
source and physical certificate.

\section{Physical verification and its logical scope}

The logical registers are $(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)$, represented by nine
unary blocks in a fixed physical order. A source frame contains a binary
source address $p$, a zero gap of length $48+n-|p|$ with $n\ge0$, a
delimiter, these nine register blocks and an arbitrary finite nonempty
spare list. Thus the address-plus-gap length is $48+n$; no shorter-header
contract is claimed. The checks quantify
over arbitrary natural register values, both decrement outcomes and
arbitrary spare values and length. They do not sample a bounded range of
register values. Successor frames stay in this domain: the header
parameter never decreases, and an increment appends a one-valued spare
while other primitive steps retain the spare list.

The first checker uses the inherited arithmetic-block contracts and
verifies all 4,749 primitive outcomes. It takes 1,540,295 symbolic
transitions, with at most 497 per outcome. The second checker imports
neither the optimizing compiler nor the first checker's arithmetic-block
oracle. It rederives four generic list-sweep induction lemmas from the
literal rows, resolves the source graph using two independent methods,
and then checks all 4,749 outcomes. Its replay uses 1,124,844 symbolic
transitions, at most 403 per outcome. Every certified physical duration
has a positive constant term, at least three.

A separate bytearray simulator checks the entire blank initialization.
PA194Q reaches native state 84 with head at $-2$, source site 25, logical
registers $(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$ and 26 one-valued spare registers after
exactly 57,922 steps. Every cell is compared with the intended complete
frame and no earlier halt occurs. This finite startup check establishes
initialization; it is not evidence from a long run for nonhalting.

The ordinary proof turning these contracts into a halting equivalence
requires positive duration and absence of an intermediate premature halt.
Forward source simulation concatenates the certified physical segments.
For the reverse direction, a first literal halt at time $t$ is incompatible
with $t+1$ nonhalting source steps: their positive simulations would force
one deterministic literal execution to continue nonhalting beyond $t$.
This bounded argument is formalizable in PA, including its nonstandard
models. It does not assume the desired nonhalting conclusion.

The executable verifiers are supporting mathematical certificates and
trusted programs, not a new foundational proof kernel. The retained Lean
files identify inherited interfaces and the existing implicational
exchange theorem. This archive does not claim that a new end-to-end Lean
or Rocq build proves the consistency biconditional.

\section{The inherited numerical theory and PA calibration}

For reference, the unchanged constructors besides $I,U$ are
$M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1$, $E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3$, and $N(a)=I(a,1)$.
Define $a\wedge b=N(I(a,N(b)))$ and
$\exists_i B=N(U(i,N(B)))$. The six ordinary heads of the new theory are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 L_1&=I(\exists_D U(D,H),H),\\
 L_2&=I(U(D,U(A,H)),U(A,U(D,H))),\\
 L_3&=I(M(H,D),I(E(H,A),M(A,D))),\\
 L_4&=I(E(H,D),I(M(A,H),M(A,D))),\\
 L_5&=\exists_D E(H,D),\\
 L_6^{\mathrm Q}&=I(U(D,I(A,H)),I(U(D,A),U(D,H))).
\end{aligned}
\]
The remaining implicational schema is
\[
 \rho_8(a,b,c,u)=I(I(I(c,u),a),I(I(a,c),I(b,c))),\qquad u\le a,
\]
and the separate diagonal head is $\Gamma(B)=I(B,U(B,B))$.
The guarded separation head uses
\[
\begin{aligned}
 V(a,b,c)&=I(M(a,b),U(c,M(a,b))),\\
 P(b,c)&=I(c,M(c,b)),\\
 S(a,b,c)&=U(b,\,I(M(a,b),P(b,c))\wedge I(M(c,b),c)),\\
 Q(a,b,c)&=U(c,I(S(a,b,c),N(P(b,c)))),\\
 G(a,b,c)&=\begin{cases}0,&c=a\text{ or }c=b,\\
                       N(I(V(a,b,c),Q(a,b,c))),&\text{otherwise}.
          \end{cases}
\end{aligned}
\]
Parameters range over natural codes. The rules are numerical modus ponens
and generalization $B\mapsto U(i,B)$. These are the inherited schemas with
only $L_6$ changed. The command-emitted $U(d,h)$ is justified by
generalization of the already derived cached head; it is not an
unrestricted axiom asserting every universal formula.

The supplied calibration translates finite refutations between this
separate-head numerical theory and PA. On the arithmetic side, membership
is interpreted by binary-bit membership and primitive equality by ordinary
equality. PA proves finite bit comprehension for each formula using an
induction instance. Replacing the formula in that finite derivation is a
proof-producing syntactic operation, rather than a uniform truth predicate.
The collision $a=b$ requires its separate singleton-comprehension argument.
On the reverse logical side, formula-code freshness justifies the diagonal
clause: every label occurring in a formula of code $B$ is less than $B$,
with the zero-code case handled separately. The supplied proof removes
equality from a quantified consequent before using membership-only vacuity,
so it does not assume the equality-vacuity principle it is trying to derive.

The exact retained manuscripts are in \file{references/calibration/} and
\file{references/cached_commands.tex}. They are mathematical dependencies,
not newly verified formal theorems in this packet. The present proof adds
an explicit h6 proof translation and a new literal implementation, leaving
those dependencies visible and reviewable.

\section{Search below 194 and limits of the negative result}

The discovery work attacked both parts of the state budget. Source-level
searches changed safe axiom presentations, including premise exchange,
reversal of primitive relation coding, and equivalent separation matrices.
Decoder searches reassociated the emitter while retaining valid address
and return-scanner constraints. Further searches expanded formerly opaque
copy macros, reused fixed reader subtrees, counted only graph-reachable
controls, and varied independent parameter roles within head families.

The local-state searches used complete finite windows from source
simulations, including arithmetic-block traces, and considered common-action
state identification as well as eliminating a state by replacing each
incoming transition with a bounded path reproducing its two-step effect.
Their failure is limited to the specified local sufficient rules. It does
not rule out a different encoding, a stronger invariant, a longer local
simulation or a different proof-search program.

\input{search_summary.tex}

Several tempting logical reductions were deliberately excluded. Replacing
the equality-witness head by reflexivity loses the ability to transfer
between variable domains; the supplied unequal-domain countermodel satisfies
the retained schemas but refutes the witness. Replacing the first head
$\Diamond\Box H\to H$ by universal elimination $\Box H\to H$ plus explosion
requires a new argument: the former expresses modal symmetry, which does
not follow from reflexivity in normal modal logic. The existing calibration
uses that symmetry to extend vacuity to negations. A syntactically shorter
emitter without the missing proof would not be a consistency construction.

The ZFC work supplies useful history-based decoder folds, physical-frame
verification and register-interface reuse. Its logical calibration cannot
be substituted for PA's. Likewise a small iPRA proof checker searches for
iPRA inconsistency, not PA inconsistency. One longer-term route is to extend
the concise intuitionistic system's equation-restricted progress rule to
arbitrary formulas, thereby supplying full HA induction, and then use a
syntactic negative translation between PA and HA refutations. This would
require its own precise theory, proof compiler and binary machine; no
small-state result is claimed for that route here.

\section{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Can the new sixth-head emitter be represented by 66 raw readers with
seven compatible folds, or 67 with eight, while retaining its current
return scanner? Either target would give 193 states.
\item Can a proved source invariant eliminate a runtime control rather than
only a source instruction? The final guard has tighter reachable value
bounds than its arbitrary-register contract. An optimization must bind the
invariant to the exact source and transport it through literal frames.
\item Can macros be expanded selectively so that reader reuse saves a fixed
control as well as sharing new decoder subtrees? Full expansion can destroy
useful sharing; a mixed representation deserves a cost model counting all
reachable controls and compatible folds.
\item Can the six axiom heads and the guarded separation matrix be optimized
jointly with their proof translations? The new implementation demonstrates
that a logically equivalent presentation can change physical sharing even
at identical source length. A useful search should retain a proof witness
for every permitted formula rewrite.
\item Can the physical contracts, formula-code calibration and all-store
command compiler be connected in one Lean or Rocq development, with an
explicit PA or iPRA derivation as the endpoint? The existing conditional
iPRA results and finite-run theorems are reusable components, but their
missing machine/refutation bridges must still be supplied.
\end{enumerate}
