Attribution and scope
=====================

This research packet builds on the PA-consistency machine development in
VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt at commit
17f92105e2bc4317feae6c219cb2143437b482ff, including its incoming research
packets and the imported James Chang PA machine development.

Inherited compilers, unary-register runtimes, symbolic execution engines,
source languages, calibration arguments, source maps and formal interfaces
remain attributed to their supplied sources. Original notices and
licenses are retained in licenses/ and alongside reference components.
Those include James Chang's MIT license, ProveIt's MIT No Attribution
license and Andrew James Wade's MIT No Attribution license. These notices
are not replaced by claims of authorship over the inherited work.

New contributions in this packet are the concrete PA194Q 194-state
realization of a previously proposed sixth-head premise exchange, its
source/proof binding, the complete machine-specific audits and comparison,
and the verified PA194CSZ composition with an independent invariant and
physical audit. Premise exchange, symmetric Shift and the zero-register
jump are individually prior ideas, explicitly credited in the report.

The inherited PA calibration is an identified mathematical dependency;
the new programs and receipts are not a completed Lean/Rocq formalization.
The negative searches do not establish a state lower bound.
