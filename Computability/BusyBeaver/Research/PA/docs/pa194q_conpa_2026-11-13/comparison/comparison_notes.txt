Proof-status caveat that matters to the new result
--------------------------------------------------
The supplied archive reports claim ordinary mathematical PA proofs of the
consistency biconditional for the principal194/195 designs. The repository
README explicitly says the machine-specific iPRA reductions remain open,
and distinguishes finite-run Lean theorems, Python certificates, conditional
iPRA endpoints, and a completed weak-arithmetic equivalence. The imported
PA196 development provides a PA proof of nonhalting -> consistency; this
single direction is not the converse. None of this inventory audit newly
kernel-verifies any theorem. It checks exact table identities and cosmetic
novelty, preserves the archives' stated status, and points to their proofs.
