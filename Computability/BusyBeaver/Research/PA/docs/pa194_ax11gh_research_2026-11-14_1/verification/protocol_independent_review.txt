Reviewed calculations

1. AX followed by ten Shifts has top q8, stack [t;w], and cache U(c,h).
   The eleventh Shift restores U(c,h) and places old top t in the cache.
   The twelfth can halt exactly when old t is one. All preceding tested
   heads have residue zero or two modulo four, so none is one.
   Total Cantor projections make the calculations valid when [0;0]=0.

2. Push is unconditional: its final MP comparison has an implication of
   residue zero against U(0,h) of residue two. Pop is intentionally
   partial as a returning identity: it halts for input top one. The
   theorem and compiler retain this exception, including arbitrary
   incoming dirty cache/offset values.

3. The generalization word Push;Shift;AX(i,0,0,0);Shift^11 correctly
   consumes the cache containing a before a later Shift could test it.
   It returns U(i,a) even when a=1. The ordinary and dominated-rho
   selectors restore the arbitrary underlying stack and normalize
   offset/cache. The dominated selector uses the hypothesis u<=D.

4. The MP compiler reserves a zero below the two premise results. Its
   suffix halts if the minor premise a is one, or if the consequence b
   is one; otherwise it returns (b,w,0,0). Removing the reserved zero
   would incorrectly pop the caller's stack, but the delivered word
   includes it. Prefixing the refutation compiler with AX normalizes
   every incoming store, and final Pop detects top one.

5. The R_j=(K+R_{j-1}+3)^4 bound covers the two actual recursive MP
   premise stacks [0;y] and [I(a,b),0;y]. The shrinking-domain finite
   conjunction covers every predecessor call. It is primitive recursive
   and can be used as a quantifier-free induction equation; no induction
   on an unbounded all-stores assertion is needed. For the stated word
   length 121(2^F-1), truth/axiom leaves must have height one. This
   convention was requested explicitly for the final version.

6. The scheduler encoding consumes AX fields in y,x,z order after its
   tag supplies c. Every positive continuation decreases by at least
   one, including zero-field and zero-tail aliases. Fresh candidate j
   drains within j+1 commands. The candidate N_e,0 is at least three,
   contains an all-store halting suffix, and is reached from counter
   two unless a prior halt occurs. The bound (N_e,0+2)^2 safely dominates
   the total drain. The argument is finite and model-uniform.

7. Reverse annotation uses proofs of top, cache, and canonical stack
   entries, with virtual zero tails supplied by rho(0,0,0,0). AX, MP,
   and Shift preserve the annotation or expose a proof of one. The
   offset is correctly excluded from the theorem invariant.
