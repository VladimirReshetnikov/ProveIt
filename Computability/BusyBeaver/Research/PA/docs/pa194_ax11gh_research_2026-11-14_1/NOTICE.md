# Attribution and changes

New combined AX11GH/AX11GHS construction, protocol proof, driver, and
finite bindings: research contribution dated 14 November 2026.

All source inputs are taken from Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt
repository at commit 1e1a41f799d4658b829cdd0eabae3775a0c8c4a0.

The raw GH/GHS tables, their source nodes and grammar, contextual folds,
construction compiler, support runtime, and independent literal runtime
descend from the incoming pa194gh_research_2026-11-14.zip packet.
Their earlier provenance includes James Chang's PA196 development and
Wade/K-family work. The supplied MIT licenses and the inherited
GH notice are retained under licenses/.

The independent command, source-command, and physical closure checkers
are adapted from the current incoming
pa194_ax11h_research_2026-11-14.zip. They have been changed for the
explicit binder U(c,h), the symmetric Shift alternative, new source
sizes, literal hashes, startup counts, and artifact layout. The
original packet, its notice, and its proof sources remain included.

The new builder replaces the final AX breakTo 1 by node49 (Push),
then replays the source, contextual folds, physical contracts, and
startup. The combined source semantics and command-word proof use
the explicit-binder construction of the incoming AX11G packet and
the early-halting cache-only protocol of the incoming GH packet.
Neither component idea alone is represented as new.

The inventory and novelty scripts are new current-task audit artifacts.
Their included reference tables and manifest come from the pinned
ProveIt research corpus; retaining them makes the novelty claim
reproducible without a mutable repository checkout.

Original comments in inherited compiler/checker files describe their
historical source versions. The new packet's actual claims are stated
in README.md, STATUS.json, and proof/new_protocol.tex. In particular
older inherited comments about an unfinished absolute calibration do
not identify the later incoming AX11H completion as kernel checked:
it is explicitly a written mathematical proof.


The enclosing comparison report, two guarded physical accelerations, expanded
two-sided stuttering searches, bootstrap repair searches, and unified replay
driver are new contributions of this investigation. Search inputs and
inherited methods retain the attributions in their individual README files.
The final protocol clarifies height-one proof leaves and the augmented
physical source frame following a separate focused mathematical review.
