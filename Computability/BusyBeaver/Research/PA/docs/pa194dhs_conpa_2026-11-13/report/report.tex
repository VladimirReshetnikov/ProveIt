\begin{abstract}
The requested sub-194 target was not attained. We supply the permitted
alternative: an explicit, graph-distinct, binary one-tape machine with
\textbf{194 working states}, together with an ordinary mathematical proof that
PA proves its blank-tape nonhalting equivalent to $\Con(\PA)$.
PA194DHS combines the independently selected diagonal head $\Gamma(D)$
with visible-result, no-push modus ponens and a symmetric cached-head
Shift implementation. Its command interface differs from both parent
interfaces. Its allocation is $135+69-10=194$, and its literal 388-row table
is included.

The comparison covers all 51 deliveries at the pinned repository revision:
47 filed research packets and four current incoming archives. A fresh
literal-table census recognizes 34 distinct 194-state graphs, including
auxiliary and rejected machines. PA194HS and PA194NS are exactly the same
table; PA194N6 is also repeated in two current submissions. The new table
matches none of the 34 graphs, even after control renaming, changing the
graph-spanning start, reflection, or symbol interchange.

Two separately implemented physical checks verify the complete source
interfaces. The independent literal-tape check covers all 4,752 primitive
outcomes, arbitrary natural register values and an arbitrary nonempty
spare suffix. The full blank tape reaches the exact clean source frame
after 58,014 steps. A new 263,236-pair positivity invariant justifies the
two specialized returns. As a supplementary negative result, the naive
append-zero mutation of the primary PA194 cannot be rescued by choosing
any of its 194 controls as start: four starts halt and all 190 others
reach a provable blank ray within 77 transitions. None of these results
is a state-count lower bound. A separate logical supplement shows that
negative instances of diagonal vacuity suffice for the inherited PA
refutation calibration; its direct implementations did not improve the
state count. No new Lean/Rocq kernel theorem or completed
iPRA consistency equivalence is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, conventions and evidence}
\label{sec:result}

The definitive machine is \file{machine/machine.tm}. Its working states
are $0,\ldots,193$, state $0$ is initial, and $194$ is a separate terminal
halt label. The tape alphabet is $\{0,1\}$, the tape is two-way infinite,
and the initial tape is zero everywhere. The head begins at cell zero.
Every defined transition writes one bit and moves exactly one cell left
or right. The halt label has no transition rows and is excluded from the
state count. All 194 working states are reachable in the directed control
graph; this assertion does not mean that all are visited on the blank run.

\begin{theorem}[Ordinary mathematical PA calibration]
For the literal table supplied in this package,
\[
 \boxed{\displaystyle
 \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg\Halt(\mathsf{PA194DHS},\mathrm{blank})
                 \longleftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).}
\]
\end{theorem}
The proof combines a new exact command compiler and fresh physical
certificates with the inherited ordinary separate-head PA calibration.
The logical and implementation dependencies are stated below. This
formulation does not assume $\Con(\PA)$ in order to prove the equivalence.
If PA is consistent, the machine does not halt; if a PA refutation exists,
the compiled search halts. A bounded blank-tape run by itself establishes
neither assertion.

The SHA-256 of the exact dense table, including its delivered newlines, is
\begin{quote}\small
\nolinkurl{2c5385ddcb928af834c1b959058d5fdc9e01b1d2be3ee9cf7c50557e45bfb48e}.
\end{quote}
The native presentation starts in 74, dispatches source frames in 84 and
halts in 254. \file{machine/labels.json} gives the exact dense-to-native
bijection. Dense and native presentations specify the same machine.

\subsection{What is newly established}

The positive contribution is a new combination with a different specified
command interface, a new source specialization invariant, simultaneous
physical folds, exact startup and complete independent physical replay.
The individual diagonal-head, no-push and symmetric-Shift ideas are inherited.
Their combination is not justified by assuming that the parents' physical
invariants survive: they are recomputed for this table. In particular, the
old Gamma-D positivity invariant has 263,244 pairs; the new one has 263,236.

The finite census and exact novelty check are fresh. The supplementary
all-start obstruction strengthens the previously reported failure of the
single native start 74 for the append-zero transplant. The searches below
194 are unsuccessful finite searches, described with their limits. The
negative-only diagonal supplement is a further mathematical simplification
of the searched calculus, with an independently reviewed proof and a
separate, unsuccessful implementation-cost screen.

\section{Why PA194DHS is materially different}
\label{sec:difference}

Let $p(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b$, $I(a,b)=4p(a,b)$,
$U(i,B)=4p(B,i)+2$, and $\Gamma(B)=I(B,U(B,B))$.
The store is $(t,w,d,h)$: visible theorem, Cantor-coded stack, numerical
offset and cached theorem. The full semantics are proved in the next
section. Two contrasts locate the new design precisely.

First, the new successful MP leaves the conclusion in both the visible
theorem and offset, while clearing the cache. The cached Gamma-D parent
leaves that conclusion in the cache and clears the offset. For example,
$I(0,2)=20$, so
\[
 \mathsf{MP}_{2}(0,w,0,20)=(2,w,2,0)
\]
in PA194DHS; the Gamma-D cached parent returns $(0,w,0,2)$.
The new compiler therefore uses a final Pop, with a reserved zero cell,
where the parent's compiler could use a Shift.

Second, the new AX diagonal follows $D=d+x$, while HS/NS follows the
independent input $A=c$. On the zero store, $\mathsf{AX}_{0,1,0}$ therefore
pushes $\Gamma(1)=832$ first in PA194DHS, versus $\Gamma(0)=20$ in HS/NS.
The other heads keep their former parameter assignments. This difference
cannot be explained by globally swapping parameter or register names.
After the successful $\mathsf{MP}_{2}$ example and an AX call with all three
explicit parameters zero, the new diagonal is $\Gamma(2)=5712$; both parent
protocols emit $\Gamma(0)=20$ in that same two-command experiment.

The new diagonal selector is correspondingly
\[
 \mathsf{MP}_{0};\mathsf{AX}^{D}_{0,B,0};S^2;\mathsf{Pop}^{8}.
\]
It selects $\Gamma(B)$ independently of the guarded separation head.
The table also has a different complete control graph. The novelty checker
compares full canonical transition tuples, not only digests. It finds no
match against any of the 34 supplied graphs after arbitrary state renaming,
every graph-spanning start, global left/right reflection and global symbol
interchange. The last comparison is stronger than the blank-preserving
convention requires, because the designated blank symbol is fixed at zero.
This is novelty relative to the pinned supplied corpus, not a claim about
all unpublished machines or every future repository revision.

\section{Physical construction and independent verification}
\label{sec:physical}

The complete source tree has 3,650 primitive occurrences. A decrement has
two possible outcomes, producing 4,752 source contracts in total. The
source-to-tape representation carries the binary source address, an affine
zero gap, nine unary primary registers and a nonempty finite list of spare
registers. Logical register order is $(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)$; physical positions
are $(6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1)$, numbered from zero.

\subsection{Exact allocation and simultaneous folds}

The raw machine has 204 working controls: 135 fixed controls and 69 AX
reader controls. Ten disjoint pairs are identified, giving
\[
                         135+69-10=194.
\]
For each pair, the incoming transition to one member establishes previous
bit zero and to the other establishes previous bit one. The replacement
uses that retained tape bit to distinguish the two former controls.
For both histories and both current read bits, the checker runs the final
simultaneously patched table to the exact old target, head position and
two-cell tape. Forty local cases certify the ten folds; no exterior cell
is visited during the local detours. Full physical source contracts then
check the folded table itself. The fold list and all finite local traces
are in \file{machine/folds.json} and \file{evidence/local_cases.json}.

\subsection{Fresh positivity invariant}

The ordinary source uses two unconditional returns. The physical source
replaces them with decrements whose zero branch has the same successor:
site 26 tests scratch register $X$ (logical 6), and site 3553 tests $D$
(logical 4). The source's MP change retains a different offset, so an old
Gamma-D invariant is not an admissible substitute for a fresh check.

A mask $m<512$ records exactly which of nine registers are positive.
Increment sets its bit. A decrement on a zero bit follows its zero target;
a decrement on a positive bit follows its positive target and nondeterministically
keeps or clears that bit. This is a sound finite abstraction of all natural
register values. Starting at $(25,1)$, both the ordinary and specialized
programs have the same closed relation of \textbf{263,236 pairs} $(\mathrm{pc},m)$.
Every abstract successor agrees between the two programs throughout this
relation. At the two changed sites, the tested bit is always zero.

Induction on the source run therefore proves equality of the concrete
ordinary and specialized executions from the clean seed. This is a proof
over arbitrary run lengths, not a bounded simulation. The finite relation,
its hash, masks at changed sites and closure certificate are regenerated
by \file{build.py}. The independent source splice and two control-flow
resolvers additionally check that the delivered instruction file really
is the intended modified source.

\subsection{Universal physical checks}

\file{build.py} checks the contracts using the inherited register-box
interface. \file{verification/check.py} uses a separate literal-tape
engine: it imports neither the construction compiler nor that arithmetic
oracle. Its four generic finite-list sweep lemmas are rederived from the
actual transition rows by an arbitrary one-block calculation and list
induction. Each primitive outcome is then verified on the entire symbolic
tape with nine independent natural register values, both decrement
branches, an arbitrary natural extra gap and an arbitrary nonempty finite
spare list of arbitrary natural values. The expected appended spare is
one-valued; that resource policy is part of the contract.

\input{verification_summary.tex}

Every accepted nonhalting source segment has a positive physical duration,
expressed by an affine formula with nonnegative coefficients. List-sweep
and unary-sweep rules carry their induction proofs; a long physical loop
is never replaced merely by a finite sample. This positivity and complete
termination of each segment support both directions of the finite-trace
equivalence described in the mathematical proof.

\subsection{Complete blank initialization}

The native start 74, head zero and empty set of one-cells reach dispatcher
84 at head $-2$ after \textbf{58,014 transitions}, without an earlier halt.
The source counter is 25, its path is \texttt{100000}, and the logical
register vector is $(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$. The header distance is 48 and
the complete suffix contains 26 one-valued spares. Both a set-based
simulator and an independent bytearray simulator compare the whole
finite-support tape with this expected frame. Equality on a selected
window would not establish this initialization claim.

\subsection{The append-zero transplant cannot be fixed by changing its start}

The PA engine appends one-valued spares. The ZFC work motivates trying
zero-valued spares; this changes a resource convention and requires a new
complete startup proof. Define $Z$ to be the primary PA194 table in native
labels, with exactly one transition changed:
\[
             (198,0):\quad (1,L,40)\longmapsto(0,L,40).
\]
All other transitions remain exactly those of the primary PA194 table,
including every control retained in that table. There are still 194
working controls. Earlier work already rejected its native start 74.
We check all possible working-state starts, with the blank tape and head
zero held fixed.

\begin{proposition}[All-start obstruction for this exact transplant]
The blank run of $Z$ started at native controls $0,66,94,97$ halts after
37, 33, 34, 31 transitions respectively. At each of the other 190 possible
starting controls, the run enters a certified nonhalting blank ray within
77 transitions. Thus choosing another existing initial control cannot
turn this exact mutation into the intended PA proof search.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The supplied verifier calculates the exact finite prefix for each start.
At a reported ray endpoint, the head reads zero, the transition has the
form $(q,0)\mapsto(w,d,q)$, and the entire half-line ahead of the head in
direction $d$ is zero. Induction gives the same transition forever: each
step enters a fresh zero cell, leaves any written bit behind, and never
changes state. The finite set of one-cells certifies the half-line
premise exactly. In the four halting cases, the finite prefix reaches the
unique terminal control 254. The complete case split contains every
working control and no other control.
\end{proof}
For the original start 74, the exact endpoint after 76 steps is state 35,
head 4 and one-cells $\{-3,1\}$. Its transition $35/0=1R35$ gives the
rightward ray. The maximum 77-step bound concerns entry into a certificate,
not the execution time of a nonhalting machine.

This proposition is stronger than failure to find a good starting state
within a long numerical timeout. It classifies every start by a finite
proof of its subsequent behavior. It is specific to $Z$: it does not
exclude zero-spare designs with further table or source changes, and it
does not establish any lower bound for the requested problem.
For the 190 ray starts, PA itself proves nonhalting. Assuming PA is
consistent, a PA proof of the requested equivalence for one of these
starts would therefore give a PA proof of $\Con(\PA)$, contrary to the
second incompleteness theorem. The four other starts actually halt, so
under $\Con(\PA)$ they fail the intended standard-model equivalence.
These are distinct conclusions: mere consistency is not used as a
blanket soundness assumption for every sentence PA might prove.

\subsection{Other source ideas and their limits}

A 105-way exploratory cross screen combines three source-emitter grammars,
seven command-controller choices and five arithmetic coefficient layouts.
No screened count falls below 194. In particular, combining radix 5 or 6
with the symmetric cached controller costs an extra state in the tested
layouts because two prospective folds compete for the same endpoint.
This is why reductions from different parents cannot simply be added.

We also examined restricting pointed separation to a stronger guard
$c>\max(a,b)$, hoping to replace two equality checks by a one-sided test.
The existing gate also supplies membership vacuity. The reverse-rewrite
proof needs vacuity at an original small label for a membership atom
containing the much larger fresh separator label. The stronger guard
removes those required instances. Consequently the current PA calibration
does not transfer to that proposed shortcut. A new logical proof or a
separate vacuity interface would be required; no smaller calibrated
machine is claimed on this basis.

ConciseIPRA supports the methodological use of explicit proof translations
and compact derived interfaces, but replacing the searched calculus by
iPRA would change the consistency target unless a PA calibration is proved.
The ZFC work supplies useful command, spare and startup ideas; its
calibration cannot be substituted for a PA calibration. The successful
construction preserves the required PA target throughout.

\begin{verbatim}
python3 -B verify_all.py
