\paragraph{An exact obstruction to the append-zero transplant.}
Let $T_0$ be the primary PA194 transition table, expressed in its native
control labels, with the single replacement
\[
 (198,0)\mapsto(0,\mathrm L,40)
 \qquad\text{in place of}\qquad
 (198,0)\mapsto(1,\mathrm L,40).
\]
The native table has SHA-256
\texttt{49439961cb03177c0e2e65b9009000d7e005bca9c57193f90650f5dc79bb9c77}.
Every one of its 194 working controls has been considered as an initial
control on the blank tape.  The following classification is exhaustive:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{initial control}&\text{outcome}&\text{number of transitions}\\\hline
0&\text{halt in control }254&37\\
66&\text{halt in control }254&33\\
94&\text{halt in control }254&34\\
97&\text{halt in control }254&31\\
\text{each of the other 190 controls}&\text{certified infinite ray}&\leq77
\end{array}
\]
Here the last column for an infinite ray counts the finite prefix ending
at its induction certificate.  These certificates end in control 35 for
163 initial controls, control 1 for 12, control 75 for 8, and control 6 for
7.  At each certificate the head reads zero, its zero transition returns
to the same control, and every nonzero tape cell lies strictly behind
the head in the direction of that transition.  The four zero actions are
$35/0=1\mathrm R35$, $1/0=0\mathrm L1$, $75/0=0\mathrm R75$, and
$6/0=0\mathrm R6$.  An induction on further steps proves that the head
continues into the untouched blank half-line forever.  This is an exact
nonhalting certificate, independent of a finite simulation cutoff.

An independent sparse-dictionary simulator reproduces every finite
prefix and checks its terminal condition.  Its receipt records all 194
prefix traces, and separately verifies that $T_0$ differs from the
primary PA194 table in precisely the displayed row.  Thus changing only
the initial control cannot turn this particular append-zero transplant
into an unbounded refutation search: every available initial control
already has the elementary behavior just classified.  In particular,
the 190 ray cases have PA-provable nonhalting, so (assuming PA consistent)
they cannot have a PA-provable equivalence between nonhalting and
$\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})$.  The four remaining cases actually
halt and therefore cannot have the intended standard-model equivalence
when PA is consistent.  This obstruction concerns this exact one-row
transplant; it excludes neither other transition changes nor a newly
designed initializer.
