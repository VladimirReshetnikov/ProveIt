\begin{abstract}
\textbf{No machine with fewer than 194 working states was established.}
We supply the requested alternative: a materially different 194-state,
binary, single-tape, blank-start Turing machine, \textbf{PA194NS}, together
with an ordinary mathematical proof of
\[
 \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg\Halt(\mathrm{PA194NS},\mathrm{blank})
                         \leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
\]
The new machine combines no-push modus ponens returning its conclusion to
the current-formula register with a symmetric Shift implementation that
tests only the cached formula. These features occur separately in the
supplied designs; their literal composition and its complete command
compiler are the new result. The combination needs a new contradiction
detector: a plain Shift can discard a false current formula.

The construction has 3,650 primitive source sites and 4,750 source outcomes.
Two symbolic physical checks cover arbitrary natural register values,
both decrement outcomes, a variable header gap, and an arbitrary nonempty
spare list. Exact blank execution reaches its complete source frame after
57,768 transitions. Eight simultaneous folds have 32 explicit local
contracts. The table differs from every recognized existing 194-state
graph, even allowing arbitrary control renaming, a changed spanning start,
tape reflection, and global symbol exchange.

The comparison is pinned to ProveIt commit
\nolinkurl{1e407ae841e4666f932777fad977ba784ebbcfd5}.
It covers 46 research packets and recognizes 3,741 literal-table
occurrences, 666 byte identities, and 476 action-graph classes.
The 28 old 194-state classes include supplementary and explicitly rejected
machines, not 28 independently proved consistency results.
The logical PA calibration is inherited and identified precisely.
Neither a newly compiled end-to-end Lean/Rocq theorem nor a completed
iPRA endpoint is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, conventions, and exact identity}
\label{sec:result}

The principal result is the table \file{machine/machine.tm}.
Its working controls are $0,\ldots,193$, its initial control is $0$, and
its separate terminal label is $194$. The tape is two-way infinite, with
alphabet $\{0,1\}$, initially zero everywhere; the initial head coordinate
is zero. Every transition writes one bit and moves one cell left or right.
There are exactly 388 rows, two per working control, and no outgoing halt
row. All 194 working controls are reachable in the directed transition
graph from control zero. This graph fact does not assert that the actual
blank execution visits every control.

The machine format is
\begin{center}\ttfamily state\quad read\quad write\quad L-or-R\quad target.\end{center}
Appendix~\ref{app:table} prints the complete table.
The exact SHA-256 of the dense file is
\begin{quote}\small
\nolinkurl{19935635f2c5722f5a72977d20a7019764a28573e12d1d0bce398245576c33b1}.
\end{quote}
The native presentation uses start 74 and halt 254, with SHA-256
\begin{quote}\small
\nolinkurl{0b72fc7decfe10ffe30c967f59ddfc570bc7f92177f67b72735b82a0d2fe9fb8}.
\end{quote}
The array \file{machine/labels.json} is the dense-to-native bijection,
including the halt label. Every row is checked under this bijection.

Let $H_{\NS}$ denote the ordinary arithmetic sentence that a finite
literal tape trace starts at this blank configuration and ends in halt.
Use a fixed ordinary first-order PA proof system for
$\Con(\PA)=\neg\exists p\,\Prf_{\PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner)$.
The theorem concerns these coded finite witnesses inside PA:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main-calibration}
 \PA\vdash H_{\NS}\leftrightarrow
              \exists p\,\Prf_{\PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner).
\end{equation}
Negation gives the desired consistency biconditional. The argument uses
syntactic proof transformations, loop induction, and finite-trace
composition; it does not assume PA's consistency within PA.
Actual nonhalting in the standard model follows if PA is consistent.

\subsection{What is new and what is inherited}

Relative to the supplied PA194CS table, precisely one native row changes:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:native-edit}
          (164,1):\quad 1R130\ \longmapsto\ 1R114.
\end{equation}
This redirects the successful modus-ponens return to a different existing
register-copy routine. The three-site transfer to the cache becomes a
seven-site copy to the current-formula register while retaining the
numerical offset. Both the resulting command semantics and the proof
compiler change. The arithmetic tape routines, separate-head axiom
calculus, axiom-emission order, scheduler, and 67-reader AX grammar are
retained.

The count is reconstructed from the unfurled table, rather than inferred
from its name:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:state-count}
   \underbrace{135}_{\text{fixed controls}}
   +\underbrace{67}_{\text{AX readers}}
   -\underbrace{7}_{\text{AX folds}}
   -\underbrace{1}_{\text{controller fold}}
   =194.
\end{equation}
The eight identifications are checked simultaneously. The controller fold
$41\mapsto330$ is inherited from PA194CS; it is not an eighth new AX
opportunity. No existing state saving is counted twice.

The no-push command and symmetric comparison are credited to their
supplied PA and ZFC sources~\cite{n,r,zfc}. The contribution here is their new literal
composition, its exact source binding, independent physical audit,
all-store proof compiler, and comparison against the current corpus.
The claim is novelty in this pinned corpus, not an assertion that the
general programming ideas have never appeared elsewhere.

Two especially relevant rejected ideas illustrate separate obligations.
A shortened return scanner can misread a legal source address as the
separator even when local reader folds pass. A known-zero source change

halt after 1,361 steps. A source invariant and a complete blank-to-source

\subsection{The larger lineages}

The 195-state frontier includes the bundled-diagonal PA195, separate-head
195S, cached 195C, diagonal-cached PA195DC, intermediate reader allocations,
and the M195S8 scanner variant. Their differences occur at three levels:
which numerical heads are emitted, where command results and spare zeros
are stored, and how the source tree is physically decoded. The original
PA195 and separate 195S allocations were $134+68-7$ and $134+67-6$;
the primary 194 layout combines the smaller reader allocation with the
larger compatible fold set.

The PA196 work explores reader reassociation, helper-return changes,
register placement, cached commands, known-zero jumps, and changes to the
separation/diagonal heads. The NEG18 proposal is a useful counterexample
to optimizing the wrong quantity: its shorter encoded negation yields a
197-state implementation, despite removing source instructions.
Source length and the number of binary TM controls are different costs.

The older U208/U209 family develops entry-context and history-sensitive
quotients, alternate helpers, unary power-cover sources, and explicit
counterexamples to further local contractions. Several reports give
different explanations of the same quotient. Their actual literal bytes
and proof obligations matter more than archive names. James Chang's
imported PA209 and PA196 packages provide a distinct development of the
same broad arithmetic proof-search strategy. The 46-packet ledger records
the individual contributions and unsuccessful directions rather than
presenting all copied tables as independent improvements.

\subsection{What the proof-assistant work currently establishes}

The pinned PA README explicitly distinguishes the machine-specific
consistency reductions from ordinary finite-run theorems, Python
certificates, and conditional arithmetic endpoints~\cite{pareadme}.
It states that neither machine-specific direction of the desired iPRA
equivalence is complete for the primary PA194, original PA195, or PA196.
The imported PA196 result supplies a PA implication from literal
nonhalting to PA consistency; this is not by itself the reverse direction.

The supplied later manuscripts give an ordinary syntactic PA calibration
for the changed numerical calculus~\cite{primary,calibration,chang}. We retain and use those arguments,
including their uniform PA-proof-producing soundness construction.
The new machine is proved by composing that identified mathematical
dependency with the new command compiler and physical refinement below.
No conditional Lean endpoint is silently treated as a proved biconditional.

\section{Verification evidence and its mathematical scope}
\label{sec:verification}

\input{verification_summary.tex}

The construction checker starts from the frozen source tree and physical
compiler. It rebuilds the 202-state raw machine, checks the eight
simultaneous folds, checks every source outcome, and compares the exact
blank-start tape with its intended seed. The independent checker imports
neither that compiler nor its arithmetic-box oracle. It reconstructs the
new table from the parent table and row (2), reconstructs the seven-site
source replacement by binary addresses, checks two separately implemented
control-flow resolvers, and proves its four generic register-list lemmas
from the literal transition rows before checking all source outcomes.

\subsection{What makes the symbolic checks universal}

A represented natural value $v$ has block $B(v)=0\,1^{v+1}$.
The logical-to-physical register map is
\[
                    (6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1),
\]
for logical registers $(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)$.
The symbolic frame contains nine independent arbitrary natural register
values, the first arbitrary spare value, an arbitrary finite remaining
spare list, a header distance $48+n$ with $n\ge0$, and blank exterior.
At source address $p$ the intervening zero gap is $48+n-|p|$.
For a decrement the zero case and positive case $1+v$ are separate
contracts; neither is omitted because a finite trace fails to visit it.

The certificate rules are literal transitions, constant-symbol scan
induction, affine arithmetic on run lengths, and induction over a finite
list of represented register blocks. The independent checker rederives
the one-block scan identities with arbitrary exterior contexts. A block
of value $v$ takes $v+2$ steps for each relevant traversal, and induction
extends the result to a list. For the alternative block layout, put
$C(v)=1\,0\,1^v$ and extend $B$ and $C$ to finite lists by concatenation.
The identity $C(A)1=1B(A)$ follows from the empty-list and one-block identities.
These rules support arbitrary values rather than a bounded sample of
register assignments.

Every closed source contract has a strictly positive physical duration.
The documented duration is an affine expression in nonnegative frame
parameters with positive constant term. Thus compressed list operations
cannot create a spurious zero-step source simulation. For increments,
the expected frame includes the appended spare block; for positive
decrements it includes the changed header distance. Exact complete-frame
equality, including head movement and untouched exterior, is required.

The proof status is ordinary mathematical verification using these sound
symbolic rules and executable certificates. The Python engines and their
induction rules remain part of the trusted verification base. A successful
run of an arbitrary Python program would not establish a PA theorem;
the elementary rule interpretations and the PA-internal trace argument
explain why these specific certificates have that role.

\subsection{Blank initialization is checked separately}

Both checkers independently run the literal machine from the all-zero
tape for 57,768 transitions. The result is the exact source-25 frame at
native state 84 and head $-2$, with logical vector
$(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$ and 26 spare blocks of value one.
The first uses a finite-support set; the second uses a byte array and
checks every cell of the array while checking that the head never leaves
it. The unvisited exterior is therefore still zero. Equality is not
limited to the head and state or to a guessed local window.

This finite startup check proves a finite binding fact. The infinite
nonhalting equivalence comes from the compiler, annotation invariant,
universal source-step simulation, and PA calibration. No finite-duration
simulation is extrapolated into an infinite-run assertion.

\section{The inherited logical calibration, made explicit}
\label{sec:inherited}

The numerical formula constructors remain
\[
 I(a,b)=4p(a,b),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,\quad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
Code zero denotes truth, one is $M(0,0)$, and negation is $I(B,1)$.
Every variable label occurring in formula code $B$ is strictly less than
$B$, with zero handled separately. Hence
$\Gamma(B)=B\to U(B,B)$ is a valid freshness/vacuity instance.
The separate-head source emits this head and the guarded separation gate
separately, together with the retained six logical heads and the dominated
implicational head.

For completeness, the six universally instantiated heads are
\begin{align*}
 &(\exists x_a\,U(a,C))\to C,\\
 &U(a,U(b,C))\to U(b,U(a,C)),\\
 &M(c,a)\to(E(c,b)\to M(b,a)),\\
 &E(c,a)\to(M(b,c)\to M(b,a)),\\
 &\exists x_a E(c,a),\\
 &U(a,b)\to\bigl(U(a,b\to c)\to U(a,c)\bigr).
\end{align*}
Existential quantification and conjunction here use their classical
implicational abbreviations. The dominated head is
$((H\to d)\to D)\to((D\to H)\to(A\to H))$ with $d\le D$.
The guarded head is $G(a,b,c)=\neg(V(a,b,c)\to Q(a,b,c))$ when
$c\ne a,b$, and truth otherwise, where
\begin{align*}
 V(a,b,c)&=M(a,b)\to U(c,M(a,b)),\\
 P(a,b,c)&=c\to M(c,b),\\
 S(a,b,c)&=U\bigl(b,(M(a,b)\to P(a,b,c))
                      \wedge(M(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
 Q(a,b,c)&=U(c,S(a,b,c)\to\neg P(a,b,c)).
\end{align*}
Truth, these heads, and the separate $\Gamma(B)$ instances generate the
numerical calculus under modus ponens and generalization of theorems.
The compiler's AX parameters instantiate this gate as $G(H,D,A)$.
The free-term emitter check binds this complete ordered family to the
frozen 190-token source word~\cite{primary,n}.

For the direction from a source halt to a PA refutation, interpret
$M(a,b)$ as $\operatorname{Bit}(x_a,x_b)$, $E(a,b)$ as equality, and $U$
as universal quantification. PA proves finite binary comprehension for
each arithmetic formula. Crucially, its proof is a uniform syntactic
template: given a formula code, produce the corresponding induction
instance and the finite PA derivation. PA verifies the resulting proof
code. This does not assert a uniform truth predicate or a reflection
principle for PA.

The inherited proof~\cite{calibration,primary} separately handles coincident labels in the
separation gate, so bound occurrences are not incorrectly treated as
free. It also handles $I(0,0)=0$ by an explicit truth equivalence and
canonical Cantor-stack collapse by the literal pairing identities.
Every visible formula and live canonical stack cell carries a PA proof
annotation. A halting Shift therefore extracts a PA proof of code one's
translation and then a PA proof of $0=1$.

For the opposite direction, the PA196 refutation compiler is retained
through the diagonal-calculus replacement of its equality interfaces~\cite{chang,calibration,primary}.
The supplied proof derives reverse membership rewriting using a fresh
label equal to a formula's own code and the diagonal head, then derives
the required neighborhood-equality implication and witness. These replace
the old equality-vacuity interface in the compiler. The separate-head
version has nine generated stack entries plus the saved top, so its
selectors must use the ten-entry protocol. Our command proof checks that
protocol again for PA194NS.

The audit in \file{comparison/calibration_audit.txt} found substantive
proof-producing arguments for these steps and no immediate defect in
the inspected interfaces. This is a review of the retained ordinary
proofs, not a new formalization of the PA196 compiler or of all its
transitive dependencies. Complete relevant inherited proof texts and
their provenance are included in \file{references/logical/}.

\section{Search below 194 and the limitations of the negative result}
\label{sec:search}

\input{search_summary.tex}

The decoder search starts from the frozen 67-reader, 68-reader, and
quantifier-head-exchanged shapes, using PA194NS's actual fixed-controller
rows. It jointly changes shared and occurrence-level binary associations,
the order of the seven freely emitted heads, and parameter permutations.
The objective is the number of AX readers minus the maximum compatible
history folds, with explicit scanner, address-depth, and structured-leaf
constraints. With 134 fixed controls after the controller fold, a valid
effective AX cost of 59 would yield 193. The best observed cost remains 60.
These are proposal counts, including repetitions and rejected mutations,
not counts of distinct certified machines.

Separate physical searches try to replace two controls by one and to
eliminate a control by redirecting its incoming edges. They compare exact
future local configurations within stated window and time bounds, first
with arbitrary-tape windows and then with conservative windows gathered
from every symbolic source contract and complete startup. The supplied
receipts state each window radius, time bound, and context abstraction;
none supplies a new contraction.

The short-scanner idea was examined directly. Removing a detector state
would require a stronger global address invariant. The first legal source
address already has six consecutive zero bits, and a direct deletion
fails. Rotating the shared comparison subtree can shorten that path but
changes structured break destinations, so flattened-word equality is
insufficient. Transient increment frames and blank initialization must
also satisfy any shortened-scanner proof. No accepted reduction results
from these experiments.

An independently constructed top-only Shift alternative passes the
construction checker at 195 states; the extra transfer reader costs a
control. It is retained as a research result rather than advertised as a
smaller machine. The accepted 194-state design is the explicitly verified
PA194NS table. None of these bounded or heuristic failures establishes
that 193 states are impossible.

\section{Relation to ZFC and concise-iPRA work}

The ZFC zero-spare-transfer report already develops no-push MP and the
idea that the proof-word compiler can reserve the zero formerly inserted
by the physical machine~\cite{zfc}. Its Shift tests differ, so its theorem
cannot simply be cited for PA194NS. The useful transferable idea is the
strong framed contract, valid from every top and every underlying stack,
with earlier halting allowed. The present proof rebuilds that contract for
the ten-entry PA emitter and the cache-only Shift test, including the new
final detector.

The concise-iPRA project compares complete proof checkers and proves
translations between presentations~\cite{ipra}. Its transferable lesson
here is to count and verify the complete interface: removing one action
from a checker moves an obligation to the proof input or to a derived
rule. PA194NS moves the spare-zero obligation to its proof compiler.
We do not replace PA by iPRA or infer PA consistency from a smaller iPRA
checker. The target remains the same PA refutation predicate, and Python
byte count is not used as a proxy for binary TM state count.

\section{Reproduction and package guide}

Python 3.10 or later and its standard library suffice for the accepted
construction and verification. Keep assertions enabled; do not use
\file{python -O} or \file{PYTHONOPTIMIZE}. From the package root:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -B verify_all.py --quick
python3 -B verify_all.py
\end{verbatim}
The quick mode checks integrity, exact tables, local folds, command-word
identities, complete startup, and graph novelty. It deliberately omits
the two long symbolic physical replays. The default performs those
replays as well. Fresh receipts are written to a temporary directory,
not over the delivered evidence. Individual commands are listed in the
top-level README and permit a user-selected evidence directory.
