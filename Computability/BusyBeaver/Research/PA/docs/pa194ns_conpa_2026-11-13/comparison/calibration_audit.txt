1. Why the source-to-PA-refutation direction is not reflection
------------------------------------------------------------
Let p(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b. The numerical constructors are
I(A,B)=4p(A,B), M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1, U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,
and E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3. Code 0 denotes truth and code 1 denotes
M(0,0), whose arithmetic translation is false. I(0,0)=0 is an intentional
numeric alias, so compositionality must be a proved PA equivalence rather
than syntactic identity at that point.

The arithmetic interpretation uses finite binary membership:
M(a,b) becomes Bit(x_a,x_b), E(a,b) becomes equality, and U introduces a
universal quantifier. PA proves Bit(s,z) implies z<s; in particular,
not Bit(x_0,x_0). Thus code 1 gives a PA refutation.

The separation gate uses finite comprehension for an arbitrary formula
syntax theta. The relevant uniform operation returns a PA proof of
  for all n, there exists s<2^n such that
  for all z<n, Bit(s,z) iff theta(z,u).
It instantiates one induction template with theta. At the successor step,
a classical split on theta(n) chooses either s or s+2^n. The proof generator
does not decide theta, interpret a truth predicate, or assume reflection.
Its correctness is a syntactic induction on formula/proof codes.

The coincident-label separation case matters. For distinct source and
bound labels a,b, the defining finite-set matrix is
  (Bit(x_a,z) or z=x_b) and T(c)[z/x_b],
with bound x_a+x_b+1. When a=b, the first membership antecedent becomes
Bit(z,z) under the binder and is false. The correct matrix is then
  z=x_b and T(c)[z/x_b].
The reviewed calibration handles this case explicitly; simply reusing
the distinct-label matrix with an incorrect free-variable interpretation
would be invalid.

Every variable label in a nontrivial coded formula c is strictly less than
c. This supplies the fresh label used by diagonal generalization. The
guarded-zero and implication-zero aliases are handled explicitly.

The clean scheduler store is annotated with PA proofs of the formulas in
its current top, cache and canonical live Cantor stack. For positive w,
tl(w)<w, so the canonical live entries form a bounded recursive list.
Popping numerical zero supplies truth. Pushing zero onto zero contributes
no fictitious list cell. AX, MP and Shift transform the annotations by
fixed syntactic proof operations. A real source halt therefore carries a
proof of code 1. Nothing requires an arbitrary carried numeric value to
be true before the annotation invariant has been established.

2. The PA-refutation-to-source-halt direction retains a real dependency
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Replacing equality-vacuity by the smaller diagonal clause
Gamma(B)=B -> U(B,B) requires a proof translation, not just comparable
formula lengths. The reviewed reports recover the original compiler's
reverse-membership and neighborhood-equality interfaces.

For reverse rewriting, set
  C = E(a,b) -> (chi(b) -> chi(a)),
  phi = not C,
  c = the numerical code of phi.
Pointed separation at (a,a,c), negative specialization and Gamma(phi)
supply the needed interface. Label coincidences have separate cases.
Inspection of upstream negativeSpecialization shows that it uses the
direct rewriting, witness and membership-vacuity interfaces, rather than
circularly requiring the removed equality-vacuity axiom.

For neighborhood equality, first choose z_0 as the code of E(a,b), which
is above both labels. Transport to the desired binder is justified by
equality-witness and membership-vacuity derivations; it does not silently
assume a generic alpha-conversion interface.

The finite replacement templates, syntax-code comparisons and proof
concatenations are primitive recursive. Separate Gamma and the old gate
recover the bundled gate, and conversely. A Gamma(B) instance can always
be obtained by choosing diagonal-head parameter B and the other two
labels B+1, satisfying the relevant guard.

This review found no defect in the selected mathematical argument.
Nevertheless, it is an inherited completeness theorem. The new work does
not regenerate every full PA proof certificate or claim a new Lean/Rocq
verification of the whole arithmetic translation. Repository statements
that machine-specific iPRA directions remain open refer to a stronger,
different formal endpoint and should not be presented as its proof.

3. PA194NS compiler: independent command-level review
---------------------------------------------------
PA194NS combines no-push MP returning its successful consequent in T with
Shift halting exactly when cache H=1. This changes both parent interfaces.
A plain Shift after deriving T=1 is insufficient: from (1,0,0,0) it
returns (0,0,0,0). A compiler that merely reused that final detector
would be incomplete.

The reviewed proof and symbolic checker repair the issue with:
  Push = AX(0,0,0); S^9; MP(0),
  Pop  = AX(0,0,0); S^11,
  Discard = MP(0) when the incoming offset is zero.
The exact Pop law is a case split:
  Pop(t,[a;w],d,h) halts if t=1;
  otherwise it returns (t,w,0,a).
The first ten shifts expose the saved old top in H. The eleventh performs
the contradiction test before decoding a stack cell. This law also holds
at [0;0]=0. All generated AX heads are non-one by constructor residues,
so the first ten shifts are total even for arbitrary old t=1.

The MP proof word reserves a zero below both recursive computations.
After both premise words return, its final suffix reaches
  (0,[a,I(a,b),0;w],0,0);
two Shifts either halt on antecedent a=1 or present (a,I(a,b)) to MP.
Successful MP returns b in T. The concluding Pop halts when b=1,
otherwise consumes exactly the reserved zero and returns (b,w,0,0).
This establishes a halt-or-return contract for every natural initial
top and stack, including the I(0,0)=0 and zero-stack aliases.

Generalization is total even on a=1. The generated universal code has
residue two and is at least two, so no soundness premise is smuggled into
its safety proof. The axiom, gate and dominated-head selectors restore
the original stack, zero offset and zero cache.

For a derivation p of contradiction, the word
  AX(0,0,0); C(p); AX(0,0,0); S^11
halts from every carried store. The first AX establishes the compiler's
offset/cache interface, and the last Pop is the correct detector.
Any early halt in this arbitrary-store theorem is sufficient for
completeness; it is not independently claimed to be sound.

Soundness is established separately by propagating annotations from the
actual clean seed through every real command. In that invariant, every
early halting cache already has a PA proof. This separation removes the
otherwise serious confusion between arbitrary-store early halting and
a clean blank-input consistency endpoint.

The reviewed source replacement preserves W and uses two terminating
counter loops to copy D into T while restoring D and zero scratch.
The Shift interface assumes scratch X=0 at command boundaries. A stronger
comparison-tail equivalence with arbitrary incoming scratch instead tests
h+x=1; the report correctly does not use it as the h=1 command law.

4. Scheduler, primitive recursion and the candidate offset
--------------------------------------------------------
The inherited raw command encodings with continuation z are
  enc(S,z) = p(0,z),
  enc(MP_x,z) = p(1,p(x,z)),
  enc(AX_A,x,y,z) = p(A+2,p(y,p(x,z))).
A positive continuation uses E(c::w)=1+enc(c,E(w)), with E(empty)=0.
A fresh candidate is raw, without the extra outer 1.

For positive continuation n, the fetch decodes raw n-1 and obtains
a residual at most n-1, hence strictly below n. A fresh candidate n
requires at most n+1 decoded commands. This exact convention agrees with
the upstream word-encoding and residual-termination modules.

If z encodes the all-store halting word, the raw family
  p(k+2,p(0,p(0,z)))
prepends AX(k,0,0), so every member halts from every store. The family is
strictly increasing and unbounded, and already its k=0 member is at least
three. The scheduler's initial candidate two therefore causes no loss.

Each fixed command macro is a primitive-recursive numeric transformation
with a primitive-recursive terminating source trace, by the pairing,
unpairing, drain and fixed-emitter loop bounds. Candidate execution is a
bounded iteration of those total transformations; halting can be made
absorbing for this construction. The finite search from candidate two
through the explicit target is another bounded iteration. These operations
can grow extremely quickly but remain primitive recursive. Their PA
correctness uses induction, not an unbounded minimization principle.

5. Exact tape transfer remains essential
---------------------------------------
The physical proof must cover all natural register values, both decrement
branches, the gap parameter, the spare-register list and the complete tape
frame, including zeros outside the represented support. It must also
give positive finite segment lengths and no premature physical halts.

A source halt then yields a physical halt by finite concatenation after
the verified startup. Conversely, if a physical halt occurs at time T,
T+1 nonhalting source steps would produce more than T nonhalting physical
steps in the same deterministic run. This proves the other implication
without an infinitary simulation principle.

The corpus contains two 194-state source-valid zero-jump variations whose
literal blank machines halt after 1,361 steps before reaching their clean
seed. They are direct evidence that a source CFG check or a warm-start
contract alone is insufficient. PA194NS's full startup and full-frame
receipts must accompany the source and command arguments.

6. Permissible claim
-------------------
With the retained logical-calibration dependency and the new command and
physical arguments, the proposed theorem is:
  PA proves [not Halt(PA194NS, blank) iff Con(PA)].
The standard-model nonhalting assertion consequently depends on PA's
consistency. This is a mathematical proof claim with executable finite
and symbolic certificates, not a claim that a new complete Lean/Rocq
or iPRA theorem was constructed. The finite unsuccessful searches and
the absence of recognized 10--193-state tables in this corpus establish
no lower bound on the number of states.
