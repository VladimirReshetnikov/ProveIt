The new contribution and its inherited foundations
-------------------------------------------------

The accepted construction combines the supplied PA194N no-push MP controller
with the supplied PA194Q premise-exchanged quantifier-distribution emitter,
retaining the original T+H=1 Shift test. The package adds the exact NQ source
composition, preservation and materiality account, table and receipts,
updated comparison, and scoped reduction experiments. It does not claim the
no-push command rule, premise-exchange theorem, separated-head calculus,
underlying compiler architecture, or physical proof engines as new inventions.
The 61-line K/S/MP exchange certificate is copied from PA194Q and replayed.

The negative coefficient-three result and its readable and primitive-rule
certificates are part of this investigation. Its 193-state physical artifact
is explicitly rejected: it implements an inconsistent numerical calculus.
It is not a smaller accepted PA-consistency machine. The primitive expansion
uses the retained generic implication proof templates; the separate final
primitive verifier admits only the explicitly documented primitive rules.

The calibration TeX fragments under references/logical/ are supplied
mathematical proof dependencies, not newly formalized theorems. Their old
internal citations and paths are retained as historical context; the
current locations are given by PROVENANCE.json. The report explains the
ordinary PA proof composition and distinguishes it from an end-to-end Lean
or Rocq export and from the stronger iPRA target. The executable certificates
retain an identified mathematical trusted base.
