Semantic research and the remaining one-state gap
================================================

1. Status and exact verified object
----------------------------------

The research branch did not produce an accepted machine with 194 or fewer
working states. It did produce a 195-state cross-command sharing candidate.
This distinction is deliberate: a favorable optimizer score is not being
reported as a verified smaller consistency machine.

The delivered dense table research195/machine/machine.tm has working states
0,...,194, start 0, separate halt label 195, and 390 binary transitions, and the usual
blank two-way infinite tape. Its SHA-256 is:

bc6137a920642ce7a83d00e7eb3d233843d46ae981b3ccfa520405933f34e9e7

The physical count is 134 fixed controls + 69 AX readers - 8 simultaneous
contextual identifications = 195. The fixed-state MP success edge is redirected
to an AX reader that already exists. No additional physical control implements
the new epilogue.

Fresh reproducible checks establish:

- 32 simultaneous two-cell fold contracts on the final quotient table.
- 3,753 addressed primitive source instructions and 4,886 primitive outcomes.
- All 4,886 arbitrary-register construction-runtime contracts pass, using
  the inherited arithmetic-block lemmas: 1,575,244 symbolic operations,
  maximum 488 per outcome.
- Exact whole-tape blank initialization takes 58,168 transitions, reaching
  the standard clean source seed at site 25 with logical registers
  (2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0), head -2, and 26 one-valued spare blocks.
- A 264,822-pair inductive positivity abstraction proves that the scheduler
  and final-AX known-zero decrements have the same source successors as

The source-to-physical check uses the inherited construction runtime. A new
independent literal-tape replay or kernel-checked end-to-end PA theorem is
not claimed for this research candidate. Its logical calibration below is
an ordinary proof outline, identifying exactly how the new interface can
be compiled. It remains separate from the verified table/source results.

2. The new sharing idea
----------------------

Let p(a,b) be Cantor pairing, [a;w]=p(a,w), I(a,b)=4 p(a,b), and
U(i,a)=4 p(a,i)+2. A command store is (t,w,d,h): current formula, numerical
proof stack, numerical offset, and cached formula. At successful MP, the
inherited comparison routine has erased T,H,X,Y and retains D=b, where
I(old_t,b)=old_h. The new epilogue is the already present AX fragment

    copyD ; copyD ; copyD ; cons ; incT ; incT.

Here copyD pushes T and then copies D into T while preserving D; cons
replaces the top two formulas by their encoded implication. Starting with
(T,W,D,H)=(0,w,b,0), the exact intermediate effects are

    (b,[0;w],b,0)
    (b,[b,0;w],b,0)
    (b,[b,b,0;w],b,0)
    (I(b,b),[b,0;w],b,0)
    (U(b,b),[b,0;w],b,0).

Thus successful MP returns both the consequence b on the stack and its
universal generalization U(b,b) in the top. The failure branch is unchanged,
returning (0,w,b,0). These are different command semantics from the prior
original, no-push, and cached-head MP rules.

The emitter uses the known Gamma(D) variant. Its only formula-word changes
from the primary 194 emitter are the three copies at positions 121,122,123,
changing A to D. Its final return decrements cleared D instead of taking an
unconditional jump. None of these inherited Gamma(D) or known-zero ideas is
claimed as new here. The new idea is cross-command sharing of the existing
axiom fragment as an MP epilogue, together with the adjusted proof interface.

Soundness has a short new step: when b has a derivation, generalization gives
U(b,b), and both newly pushed entries b and 0 have derivations. Every visible
formula and live canonical stack entry therefore retains a derivation.
The Cantor alias [0;0]=0 is handled by the literal projection equations;
the argument does not treat numerical stacks as free lists.

For completeness, use cached Shift: it halts exactly when h=1, otherwise
it maps (t,w,d,h) to (h,tail(w),d,head(w)). The inherited AX interface still
pushes nine generated heads plus the saved old top. Consequently

    Push = AX(0,0,0); Shift^9; MP(0)
    Pop  = AX(0,0,0); Shift^11

retain their usual contracts. Push's final MP necessarily fails because
it compares an implication with a universal code. Pop preserves its top,
clears d, and consumes one stack entry, except that it can halt early when
the preserved top is 1. Early halting is an allowed successful outcome of
a refutation compiler.

A robust clearing word, also useful for the positive-sentinel proposal below,
is

    Discard = Push; Shift; Push; Shift.

From any (t,w,d,h), its successive stores are

    (0,[t;w],0,0), (0,w,0,t),
    (0,[0;w],0,0), (0,w,0,0).

The two explicit Shifts test cache 0 and cannot halt. This identity remains
true under Cantor zero collapse and does not rely on MP(0) being a discard.
An offset i can be installed from a clean store by an empty word for i=0,
or MP(i) for i>0; the latter necessarily fails, leaving offset i.

For an MP proof node, first reserve a zero below both recursively compiled
premises. Immediately before the two premise Shifts the state is

    (0,[a,I(a,b),0;w],0,0).

If a=1, the second Shift already halts. Otherwise MP(b) succeeds and the
new epilogue returns

    (U(b,b),[b,0,0;w],b,0).

Two further Shifts either halt if b=1, or return

    (b,[0;w],b,0).

One Pop consumes the reserved zero and returns (b,w,0,0), again allowing
early halt for b=1. This supplies the new MP clause for the recursive proof
compiler. The unchanged axiom selectors use the Gamma(D) parameter choice;
the inherited generalization selector and exhaustive scheduler complete the
intended reduction from a finite refutation to a finite source halt.

The remaining logical dependency is the inherited syntactic calibration of
the separate-head numerical calculus to PA, including its code-zero alias
and bound-variable conventions. The new table checks do not independently
re-prove that calibration. The outline is not labeled as a new completed
end-to-end theorem.

3. Positive truth-sentinel proposal
----------------------------------

A second source idea replaces the successful MP zero sentinel by the positive
closed theorem 2=U(0,0):

    success: (t,w,d,h) -> (b,[2;w],b,0), where b=d+x and I(t,b)=h.

The required epilogue is incT;incT;copyD, starting after the comparison at
T=H=0,D=b. That three-token fragment occurs consecutively in the AX word,
so a suitable shared binary association might implement it without an added
control. Its source position is 38--40 in the expanded primary 190-token
emitter word.

This change would make every successful MP push visible in the numerical
stack, even when the former stack is 0: [2;w]>0. Soundness annotates the new
entry by generalization of truth, so it adds no stronger logical axiom.
However, it invalidates the old no-push identity Discard=MP(0): when the
comparison succeeds at t=h=0, the proposed MP pushes 2. The robust
Push;Shift;Push;Shift clearing word above avoids this error.

For the modified MP compiler, reserve a zero below the premises. After
success the store is (b,[2,0;w],b,0). Two Pops return (b,w,0,0), or halt
already when b=1 in the cached-Shift convention. This gives the intended
compiler adjustment. It addresses zero-stack collapse literally rather
than assuming a nonzero stack sentinel that the actual table does not write.

No 194-state physical implementation of this positive-sentinel idea is
claimed. Its optimizer candidates have not been promoted to verified
machines, and their numerical scores are deliberately not included in the
results. The idea and compiler outline are supplied for further research.

4. Search scope
---------------

search/search_ledger.json records seven completed compiled searches. They
jointly proposed 14,947,566 grammar mutations and accepted 1,627,896 annealer
moves. These are sums of event counts; they are not deduplicated layouts,
source programs, or transition tables. Shared ancestors and revisits are
intentional. Neither these counts nor failure to find a smaller accepted
candidate supplies a lower bound.

The completed searches varied association of the existing axiom macro word,
required a sound reusable MP epilogue, checked the new incoming MP context,
and scored simultaneous history folds. Later runs varied known-zero return
registers and symmetric-Shift helper interfaces. They did not search all
binary Turing machines, all proof calculi, all encodings, or all local
simulation relations. No result about global minimality follows.

Two preliminary positive-sentinel searches were stopped because their move
set rarely exposed the required subtree; they are excluded from the aggregate.
The preliminary Python beam exploration and other agents' searches are also
excluded, preventing misleading totals across unlike scopes.
