## Main design distinctions

The original nine U208 outputs use the same final one-state fold. At native172 the two preceding bits are10; at native211 reading0 the immediate predecessor is1. The merged state inspects this history through the existing helper. The exact local blocks preserve the entire tape, not just a simulated numeric example. The different native naming conventions do not give independent state savings.

James Chang's specified historical PA209 is different from the U209 parents inside the U208 reports. In the James209-to196 comparison, 134 native working labels are shared; 265 of their268 rows agree. PA196 removes75 old controls and introduces62, for a net13-state reduction. Its changes transpose the quantifier constructor's Cantor-pair arguments, remove the old seventh equality head, add guarded equality vacuity to the separation gate, and reorganize the axiom emitter. Native172 and211 are both already absent, so the U208 saving cannot simply be subtracted again.

The ordinary published PA196 source has3812 primitive sites and159 distinct internal syntax nodes. Its196 physical controls consist of36 register/tape-engine states,13 leaf routers,147 physical readers. The201-item AX word has68 raw internal nodes but uses62 physical readers after six contextual folds. Counts of source occurrences, syntax nodes, grammar rules, and TM controls measure different objects. Several shorter sources in this corpus have larger machines because they lose shared subtrees or existing helper paths.

## Evidence boundaries that should survive into a new report

The inspected published formal theorem is PA196.Arithmetic.pa_proves_nonhalting_consistency: PA proves Nonhalt→Con(PA). The published external semantic soundness and standard-model nonhalting theorem do not alone supply the internal converse. Several incoming reports give an ordinary syntactic reverse proof: interpret M(a,b) as the x_b-th bit of x_a, supply syntax-dependent PA finite-comprehension induction proofs, transform numerical-calculus derivations into PA refutations, maintain proof witnesses through the command store, and reflect a finite tape halt through positive-length source simulation. That is a viable written proof recipe, distinct from a completed formal compiler or a new kernel theorem.

The small ConciseIPRA proof checker cannot be substituted directly: the target sentence is Con(PA), and full-formula induction is used by the finite-comprehension proof supplier. A transfer from a changed calculus needs both finite refutation translations verified in PA.

The source_zero5 representation is not by itself a scanner reduction. It changes seed ordinal25's path from100000 to10100000 and preserves only71 inherited reader groups; its recorded heuristic is71+82+49=202 controls before any new sharing. Every transient return path and the blank startup must still be checked. Similarly, the157-node original source loses an existing decoder helper, and the protected fixed-node allocation model's UNSAT does not exclude more general compilations.

No finite blank simulation is used here as nonhalting evidence. Contract failures refute their stated refinement claim, usually not the candidate's ultimate blank-run Con(PA) property; the two explicit43563-step blank-halting controls are stronger counterexamples. All negative searches have restricted templates and cannot establish a global196-state lower bound.
