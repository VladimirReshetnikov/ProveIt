\begin{abstract}
No verified machine below 194 working states was obtained. The positive
result is \GS, a new binary, one-tape, two-way, blank-start 194-state machine.
It exposes the two closed subroutines inside the inherited guarded AX
constructor, changes their prefix codes together with the following push,
and removes the old shared fixed-reader entry. The final allocation is
$133+68-7=194$: 133 fixed controls, 68 grammar controls, and seven contextual
folds. All 3,671 resolved source instructions of revised \Parent\ are
preserved, although 49 source addresses change.

An independent literal-row verifier accepts all 4,778 symbolic outcomes,
derives its four list-traversal lemmas from the actual rows, and checks the
complete blank startup at 57,922 transitions. A separate source resolver,
frame-closure audit, and duration audit bind the proof to the emitted table.
The new relative argument is formalizable by quantifier-free induction in
\iPRA. In both directions, a physical halting witness $t$ transfers to a
witness bounded by $2t$ for the other machine. A second, more general
clock proof supplies the bound $9t^2+707t+58620$. Thus the inherited
written \Parent\ calibration gives
$\iPRA\vdash\Con(\PA)\leftrightarrow\neg\Halt_{\Blank}(\GS)$.
The inherited calibration is an explicit dependency; this work does not
complete its unfinished end-to-end Lean formalization.

The comparison binds 104 named constructions to literal tables and examines
548 spanning 194-state action-graph classes, plus two disconnected diagnostic
classes. \GS\ is distinct under control renaming, reflection, global bit
exchange, arbitrary spanning roots, and ignored halt-entry decorations.
The archive includes the complete transition table, deterministic builder,
certificates, comparison ledger, inherited proof sources, failed 193-state
experiment, and bounded search records. None of the negative searches is
presented as a lower bound on the state complexity of PA consistency.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, conventions, and proof boundary}
\label{sec:result}

The requested strict improvement remains open in this investigation. The
delivered fallback is a concrete new 194-state design, called \GS\ for
\emph{gate split}. Its source semantics are those of revised \Parent,
whose supplied written calibration is retained in
\path{reference/calibration_AX11H/}. The new work concerns a different
interface between the fixed decoder and the compressed AX grammar, its
physical implementation, and an explicit weak-arithmetic transfer theorem.

The tape alphabet is $\{0,1\}$ and the tape is indexed by $\mathbb Z$.
Initially every cell is zero, the head is at coordinate zero, and the
control is dense state 0. There are 194 working states, numbered
$0,\ldots,193$, and a separate halt label 194. Every working state has
exactly two transition rows, and every row writes one bit and moves one
cell left or right. There are no stationary moves, auxiliary tracks,
external inputs, or supplied initial data. The halt label is excluded
from the state count. The machine has 388 rows.

The canonical deliverable is \path{machine/machine.tm}. Its SHA-256 is
\begin{center}\small
\path{64ac4ba569b770688a39a1545d5536ad631d15dbb756a0584a8956a83943b3a8}.
\end{center}
The calibrated parent's dense table has SHA-256
\begin{center}\small
\path{c5dfe1026d077d3e062eca11edb789597bfbbced4f6040f8828ae5d467b1167a}.
\end{center}
The native table uses start 74, instruction boundary 84, and halt 254.
\path{machine/labels.json} is the checked dense-to-native bijection,
including the halt label. Native labels in this report identify inherited
compiler roles; they are not additional working states.

\begin{theorem}[New relative theorem]
\label{thm:relative}
For the exact two literal tables identified above, let $h_j(t)$ be the
primitive recursive assertion that machine $j$ has halted from blank by
time $t$, with $j=0$ the parent and $j=1$ the new machine.
The complete-tape execution contracts and their quantifier-free-induction
interpretation establish
\[
 \iPRA\vdash h_0(t)\to h_1(2t),\qquad
 \iPRA\vdash h_1(t)\to h_0(2t).
\]
Consequently \iPRA\ proves equivalence of their blank-tape halting
sentences and of their blank-tape nonhalting sentences.
\end{theorem}

\begin{corollary}[Transfer of the supplied calibration]
\label{cor:calibration}
Using the revised \Parent\ written theorem
\[
 (\mathrm{Cal}_{\rm AX11H})\qquad
 \iPRA\vdash\Con(\PA)\leftrightarrow
                    \neg\Halt_{\Blank}(\Parent),
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:relative} gives
\[
 \boxed{\iPRA\vdash\Con(\PA)\leftrightarrow
                    \neg\Halt_{\Blank}(\GS).}
\]
\end{corollary}

Here $\Con(\PA)$ uses the parent's exact syntactic consistency predicate,
\texttt{nativePAConsistency}, the negation of
\texttt{nativePARefutation}, at its pinned Foundation dependency
\path{32e1a0956a8622fad067328ca1959729a7634428}.
No consistency assumption is used to prove the equivalence.

There are three different evidential claims. The new machine and its
relative execution theorem are supplied here. The absolute PA calibration
is inherited from the revised AX11H written proof, not independently
reimplemented as a complete PA proof compiler in this response. Finally,
no new Lean kernel endpoint is supplied. The repository's main PA README
expressly keeps machine-specific internal arithmetic endpoints open;
standard finite-run proofs and Python receipts alone do not close them
\cite{pa}. The revised packet separately supplies a written mathematical
calibration \cite{ax11h}. This report preserves that distinction.

A control graph is compared through the ordered pair of outgoing actions
for each read bit. Canonical breadth-first serializations are minimized
over every root that reaches all working controls. An enlarged comparison
also permits tape reflection, exchanging the two symbols globally, and
erasing the write/move decorations on transitions into halt. The latter
two operations are used only to make structural novelty harder to claim;
global bit exchange is not asserted to preserve an all-zero initial tape.

All 548 spanning 194-state classes remain different under this enlarged
comparison. The new table has no match. The two other diagnostic classes

new table. Comparisons use the actual canonical tuples, with SHA-256 only

The greatest immediate-action congruence has exactly 194 classes for

transition-preserving bisimulation quotienting does not reduce any of
these exact action graphs. This is a finite obstruction to one method.
It says nothing about all contextual tape invariants, changed transition
rows, different source programs, or a universal lower bound of 194.

\section{Construction: expose and reassociate the guarded tail}
\label{sec:construction}

\subsection{The inherited source language}

The inherited binary source tree expands to primitive register
instructions. A source site has its binary tree address, an opcode, and
an operand. Opcodes are increment, conditional decrement, counted
\texttt{breakTo}, counted \texttt{continueTo}, and halt. A decrement
branches out of the current left subtree on zero and otherwise decrements
and advances. A counted break selects a specified left ancestor and its
following continuation; a counted continue selects a specified right
ancestor and its loop start. Therefore arbitrary tree reassociation is
not semantics preserving.

The expanded program has 3,671 sites: 1,456 increments, 1,107 decrements,
75 counted breaks, 1,032 counted continues, and one halt. Resolving the
tree gives ordinary ordinal instruction indices and explicit targets.
Those resolved instructions, rather than the shape of the tree alone,
are the semantic object compared in this construction.

\subsection{The precise source transformation}

Write
\[
 U=\mathtt{node146},\qquad V=\mathtt{node149},\qquad
 R=\mathtt{node49},\qquad \mathtt{node150}=(U,V).
\]
$U$ is the first comparison/conditional-clear part of the guarded
constructor; $V$ contains the transfer and second comparison; $R$ is
the following push. The original grammar refers to $(U,V)$ through the
fixed macro \texttt{node150}. At its unique occurrence next to the
push, replace
\[
                (A,((U,V),R))\quad\longmapsto\quad(A,(U,(V,R))).
\label{eq:rotation}
\]
The preceding AX code $A$, all nine logical registers, all numerical
constructors, both guard comparisons, and the complete four-operand
eleven-entry AX contract stay in the source program.

The following finite closed-subroutine check explains why this particular
rotation is allowed.

\section{Literal execution certificates and their arithmetic meaning}
\label{sec:physical}

\subsection{Complete source frames}

Let $B(v)=0\,1^{v+1}$ and let $B(Q)$ concatenate these blocks for a
nonempty finite list $Q$. A live extended source state is
\[
 z=(i,R_0,\ldots,R_8,n,Q),
\]
where $i$ is an ordinal site, each $R_a$ and the slack $n$ are natural
numbers, and $Q$ is the spare list. At native control 84 the head scans
the first bit of $p_j(i)$ in the complete frame
\[
 0^\infty\,1\ \underbrace{p_j(i)\,0^{48+n-|p_j(i)|}}_{\text{address and slack}}
 \,1\ B(R_{\rm physical})\ B(Q)\,0^\infty.
\]
The physical positions of logical registers $0,\ldots,8$ are
\[
                  (6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1).
\]
Fix the head coordinate of $E_j(z)$ at $-2$. The only difference between $E_0(z)$ and $E_1(z)$ is the appropriate
source address $p_j(i)$; the remaining frame conventions agree.

An increment increases one register and appends a spare block of value
one. A positive decrement reduces one register and increases slack by
one. The zero branch and the two jump opcodes preserve the register
values. The source graph determines the next site. The source halt
instruction executes to a separate absorbing code $H$. A live site
pointing at that instruction is not yet the halted source state.

\subsection{What the independent literal verifier proves}

The search checker is useful for rapidly rejecting incorrect tables,
but it has arithmetic register-box shortcuts. The final independent
checker uses the literal rows and four freshly derived list-traversal
lemmas instead. It never assumes the correctness of an arithmetic box.
Its two source resolvers must agree with the retained resolved program,
and dense/native table conjugacy is checked before any runtime claim.

For every source site, and separately for both branches of every
decrement, it produces an exact complete-tape endpoint and a 13-slot
affine duration vector. There are
\[
 1456+2(1107)+75+1032+1=4778
\]
outcomes. The fresh new-table replay accepts them all, using 1,101,826
symbolic events in total and at most 403 in any one outcome. These are
checker statistics, not bounds on the length of a machine's search.
Every live duration has constant term at least three.

The rules used to interpret these finite certificates are the following.
A literal event substitutes one actual row into the tape-step equations.
A unary self-loop crosses a run of arbitrary natural length; induction
on processed length proves its endpoint and linear duration. A generic
one-block traversal on $B(v)$ takes $v+2$ steps and preserves arbitrary
exterior contexts. Induction on a finite-list prefix then extends the
traversal to the entire list. The normalizations use
\[
 C(X)1=1B(X),\qquad
 1\operatorname{rev}(C(X))=\operatorname{rev}(B(X))1,
 \qquad C(v)=1\,0\,1^v.
\]
The empty-list and cons cases establish these identities by the same
kind of induction. All four traversals are rederived from the actual
machine rows, including the new table, before they may be used.

With the environment code as a free parameter, each induction invariant
is an equality of primitive recursive configuration functions together
with a bounded Boolean validity test. No invariant quantifies over all
environments inside an induction formula. This is the inherited
quantifier-free-induction interpretation supplied in the AX11H physical
audit, instantiated by the new successful event traces \cite{ax11h}.
Finite testing at selected register values would not supply this argument.

For all 4,777 nonhalting outcomes, a separate inspector checks zero net
head displacement, zero address overflow, and equality of the complete
48-bit-padded successor address with the target source address. Appending
the same arbitrary extra slack preserves the equality. It also checks
nonnegative integral affine coefficients and the exact three-step halt
outcome. Thus the finite data instantiate, uniformly for valid live
source states,
\begin{equation}
 \Run_j(D_j(z),E_j(z))=E_j(F(z)),\qquad D_j(z)\ge3.
 \label{eq:local}
\end{equation}
The source validity predicate is preserved. These are equations over
arbitrary natural registers and arbitrary nonempty finite spare lists,
including complete blank exteriors.

\subsection{Blank initialization}

The common initial source state is
\[
                 z_*=(25,2,0,\ldots,0,0,[1]^{26}).
\]
Both exact tables reach $E_j(z_*)$ in 57,922 transitions from their
respective blank start, at native state 84 with head coordinate $-2$.
No earlier halt occurs. The new independent dense-table startup checker
represents the entire tape as the finite set of its one cells; every
unlisted integer coordinate is zero. Its final set is compared with the
entire expected frame. This supplies the closed finite arithmetic fact
\begin{equation}
             \Run_j(57922,\Blank)=E_j(z_*).
 \label{eq:startup}
\end{equation}
The larger independent runtime checker also verifies this fact with a
finite array while checking that the head stays inside it and that all
exterior cells remain untouched. A runtime theorem conditional on an
already prepared frame would not establish \eqref{eq:startup}.

\subsection{Certificate bindings and limits}

\section{Explicit constructive halting-witness transport}
\label{sec:clocks}

All source updates and finite-tape operations have primitive recursive
encodings. Make physical halt absorbing in the arithmetic run function;
this changes no literal halting behavior and adds no working control.
Use $D_j(H)=0$ for the stopped source clock. The first derivation gives
relative equivalence with polynomial witness bounds and fixes the

\section{Attempts to obtain fewer than 194 states}
\label{sec:search}

The following records distinguish failed constructions, exhaustive finite
screens, and stochastic or budget-limited searches. Their scopes matter:
none proves that 193 states are impossible.

\subsection{A concrete 193-state nominee that fails}

A shortened return scanner deletes native state 34 and changes
$(23,0)$ to $0L75$. It relies on a smaller bound on zero runs in the
source address. Exposing the two gate subroutines was first investigated
to make that bound achievable without another reader state.

An interval dynamic program on the exact standalone 30-leaf gate finds
minimum leading-zero depth five within the tested CFG-preserving
structured-tree family. The early conditional exits force nested
boundaries. Placing that macro under another left edge creates a run of
six zeros; this is the obstruction to simply substituting the shortened
scanner into the original grammar. This result is about that specific
source representation, not all possible guarded tests.

A new nominee did reach 193 controls, retained the full 3,673-instruction
source of its experimental parent, and passed the local fold checks.
It failed the universal physical source contract at ordinal site 50,
the zero branch on physical register 2, after 70 accepted outcomes. Its
address was \texttt{101101001000000}. A literal replay with all nine
registers zero and one spare entry of value one reaches control 84 with
head displacement 9 after 91 transitions; the required next frame has
head displacement zero. The zero register has been confused with header
padding. The table and both symbolic and literal traces are retained in
\path{research/rejected_scanner193/}. It is explicitly rejected.

A larger 250-state zero-run-bounded tree passes all 4,780 symbolic
runtime outcomes, but fails the inherited exact startup test. A further
million-step inspection did not find the required seed. This bounded
failure is not a nonreachability theorem; it is enough to withhold the
required blank-start calibration. Likewise, the supplied corpus already
contains runtime193 splices whose blank start enters an outward ray, and
other 193-state tables with elementary complete-configuration cycles.
Those are counterexamples to treating prepared-frame correctness as
blank-start correctness.

\subsection{Grammar optimization in the new 133-fixed-control family}

The new construction changes the target cost: 133 fixed controls mean an
effective reader cost at most 60 would give at most 193 states. The
initial seven H/GDH searches tested 13,446,433 proposals, including
shared-DAG rotations, folds involving fixed controls, and independent
parameter permutations in six universally quantified heads. Seven further
composed-family runs made 12,633,111 proposals on ORH, AX11HMP, QAX11H,
H5X/JH5X and their symmetric variants. These are historical exploration
counts. A final audit found a defect in the inherited expanded-copy price
model, so their nominal scores are not used as a sound physical exclusion
of 193 states.

\paragraph{The discovered optimizer error.}
The expanded copy-H remainder, \texttt{node69}, was assigned native
entry 30. The actual literal parent has
\[
       166:\quad 0R19\;/\;1R179,
\]
so the correct entry is 179 and the correct reusable reader identity is
$(19,179)\mapsto166$. The inherited recognition of $(19,30)$ was wrong.
In addition, a constant charge for the fixed part can overcount controls
made unreachable by macro expansion. Recounting reachability initially
exposed several apparent 193-state tables, but their literal source
contracts failed at the expanded copy-H routine. Those failures are
retained, not promoted to results. The delivered \GS\ does not expand
copy H and its independently verified table is unaffected.

After correcting the entry and pricing only graph-reachable controls,
all 900 saved grammars were repriced with simultaneous compatible-fold
checking. Every result was 194; none remained a smaller nominee. An
independent audit also checked all ordinary copy and increment reader
identities against the fixed rows. It found that deeper gate entries
\texttt{node145}$\mapsto122$ and \texttt{node147}$\mapsto130$ require
preceding bit one: each is a contextual fold that denotes a different
source subtree after preceding bit zero. Deeper searches that freely
rotate these leaves are therefore exploratory, not calibrated machines.

\paragraph{Corrected searches.}
Two corrected 300-second H/GDH runs and two 120-second warmer runs made
\textbf{7,663,786 proposals}, with 2,712,060 accepted moves. They used
the repaired copy entry, actual fixed-control reachability, and
simultaneous fold validation. The best actual price stayed at 194,
and no strict-reduction hit appeared. Any positive price would still
need exact source equality, universal literal contracts and complete
blank startup. These checks cannot be replaced by the optimizer's
objective function.

The expanded fold screen considers every non-start control whose whole
incoming edge set writes a fixed preceding bit while moving right.
Deleting 221 can change those histories, so restricting the screen to
new AX readers could miss opportunities. A separate deterministic
enumeration generated 3,238 final-window reassociations. Its recorded
nominal best cost was 62 effective readers. Repricing that entire finite
set with the corrected scorer confirms a best total of 195. The final package contains corrected
sources, exact seeds, the 900-grammar repricing receipt, macro audit,
rejected tables and final logs under \path{research/gate_search/}.

\subsection{Physical shortcut and contextual-fold screens}

A fresh C++ screen computes an inductively closed abstraction of local
tape windows, allowing both values for newly exposed cells. For each
state/read case it intersects future complete local configurations that
could be represented by one legal TM transition. It then tries one- and
two-control deletion and pair identification compatible with those
certified shortcuts.

All nine incoming top-level native machines were screened at radius 7
and fuel 129, with roughly 0.90--0.95 million abstract contexts each.
The new gate-split table was screened at radius 9 and fuel 257 over
10,650,383 contexts. Neither screen produced a deletion or merger.
The additional shortcuts found in unaccelerated parents were the
already-known $(26,1)\mapsto1R91$ and $(101,0)\mapsto0R17$.

Independent history-fold searches covered all thirteen newest incoming
tables with radius-9, fuel-129 noncircular conditions and radius-8,
fuel-31 circular conditions. The circular runs made 15,181,260 final
joint-action checks without a new reduction. A positive-control run on
the unfolded 201-state AX11H table recovered all seven known fold pairs,
so the search was not merely failing to find any fold at all.

The full fold closures of the 201-state ORH and MP inputs were each
exhausted: 192 rooted graphs in each closure, minimum 194. Two H5XS
204-state closure traversals were stopped at their 5,000-graph expansion
budgets, with 6,600 and 5,045 discovered graphs and minima 197 and 194,
respectively. Those latter closures are not exhaustive. Two-sided
common-future screens, including one on the new gate table, likewise
found no contraction. Sources, inputs, parameters and compact receipts
are retained under \path{research/history_folds/} and
\path{research/physical_shortcuts/}.

\subsection{A separate logical recoding that did not meet the size target}

An independent numerical alternative exchanges the constructor residues
of universal quantification and equality. Keeping implication
$I(a,b)=4p(a,b)$ and membership $M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1$, use
\[
              U'(a,B)=4p(B,a)+3,\qquad E'(a,b)=4p(a,b)+2.
\]
The simultaneous recursive translation of formula codes and variable
labels is an involution fixing 0 and 1. Translating labels is essential
because the diagonal head uses a number both as a label and as a formula
code. The diagonal and the two guard equalities then commute with the
translation. Numerical order does not commute; the restricted-rho
inequality must be handled through the existing unrestricted-rho proof
template, as the retained proof fragment explains.

Free-term source evaluation checks the intended AX11H and AX11GDH
command effects, including all eleven stack entries, the fourth operand,
guards and scratch registers. The AX word grows from 190 to 202 tokens:
15 universal constructors gain an increment and three equality
constructors lose one. A 90-second search made 830,774 proposals and
253,165 accepted moves; the best effective reader cost was 65, giving
a nominal matching price of 199 states with 134 fixed controls. This
particular search uses atomic copy macros and is unaffected by the
copy-H-entry defect above. However, exact repricing of its winning
grammar finds 208 reachable raw controls and only six simultaneously
compatible folds among the eleven available opportunities: 202 controls,
not the optimistic nine-fold matching price of 199. Its 3,683 resolved
instructions agree with the corresponding tag-swapped seed. No arbitrary-
register physical runtime or blank startup is certified for this object,
and it is not a successful design at or below 194. The source, twelve
seeds, exact repricing receipt, search log and recoding argument are in
\path{research/tag_swap/}.

Removing the guarded comparisons by padding property codes was also
considered. A complete, noncircular transport of the gate conclusions
at arbitrary labels was not obtained. Replacing a modal head using
reflexivity plus explosion likewise lacked the needed symmetry
derivation. These unproved ideas are excluded from the successful
construction; the delivered machine retains both guards.

\section{What the other requested research trees contribute}

The ZFC tree supplies useful physical design patterns: command-local
field readers, contextual selectors, macro exposure, source rotations,
register allocation and exact startup checks \cite{zfc}. Its README
also stresses that separately named state savings can use the same
source roles and therefore fail to compose. Those lessons directly
motivate the simultaneous folded-table check and the exact blank
startup requirement here. Reusing a Con(ZFC) machine without changing
its proof search would retain the wrong consistency target. The
calibration to PA is not a label substitution.

The DiophantineConsistency tree measures variables, algebraic degree,
and arithmetic operation counts for equations calibrated to a specified
refutation predicate \cite{dio}. A small arithmetic circuit is not yet
a small binary blank-start TM: enumeration, coefficient construction,
evaluation and initialization all need a priced compiler. In particular,
the current tree's ZFC target cannot be silently substituted for PA.
The study suggests alternative search substrates, but no concrete
sub-194 evaluator and consistency calibration was obtained here.

ConciseIPRA studies small standalone proof checkers for presentations
bi-interpretable with intuitionistic PRA, measuring normalized source
tokens, syntax nodes and bytes \cite{concise}. Its compact scalar and
clausal calculi may inspire better encodings. Searching their own
refutations would, however, change the target to consistency of iPRA.
For this task a PA-strength interpretation with explicit weak-arithmetic
refutation transformations and a fully priced literal compiler is still
needed. Neither proof-checker byte count nor rule-family count is a
Turing-state bound.

These comparisons do not rule out a more radical approach. They explain
why none of the imported numerical size measures can be counted as an
already achieved 193-state PA-consistency machine.
