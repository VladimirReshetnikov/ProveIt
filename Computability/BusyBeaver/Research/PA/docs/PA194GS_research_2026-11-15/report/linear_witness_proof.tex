\subsection{Sharper linear halting-witness transport}
\label{sec:linear-witness}

Alignment of the two complete contract files yields an additional finite
fact: all twelve nonconstant coefficients agree for every corresponding
outcome.  Of the 4778 constant coefficients, 4736 agree, sixteen increase
by two, twenty-four decrease by two, and two increase by 44 in the new
machine.  Direct coefficient comparison establishes, for every common
live source state,
\[
 D_1(z)\le2D_0(z),\qquad D_0(z)\le2D_1(z).             \tag{L1}
\]
With the common stopped convention $D_j(H)=0$, (L1) holds at $H$ as
well.  Quantifier-free induction therefore gives
\[
 \tau_1(k)\le2\tau_0(k),\qquad \tau_0(k)\le2\tau_1(k).
                                                               \tag{L2}
\]
Put $b=57922$ and $S_j(k)=b+\tau_j(k)$.  Since both startups have
the same duration, (L2) is equivalently the pair of useful inequalities
\[
                S_{1-j}(k)+b\le2S_j(k)\quad(j=0,1).    \tag{L3}
\]

Suppose $h_j(t)$.  The already proved constructive reflection gives
$z_{t+1}=H$.  Let $k$ be the least index at most $t+1$ with $z_k=H$.
This is a \emph{bounded} minimization of a primitive recursive decidable
predicate, implemented by scanning the finite prefix; its elementary
correctness uses quantifier-free induction.  It does not use unbounded
minimization, Markov's principle, or an induction formula with an
unbounded quantifier.  Since $z_0$ is live, $k=r+1$ for some $r$,
$z_r\ne H$, and $z_{r+1}=H$.

By exact boundary simulation, the machine-$j$ configuration at time
$S_j(r)$ is the live frame $E_j(z_r)$.  Consequently
$S_j(r)<t$: if $t\le S_j(r)$, physical halt absorption would make that
same frame halted.  The only live source instruction whose successor is
$H$ is \texttt{CH}, and its exact duration in both tables is three.
Hence
\[
                     S_j(k)=S_j(r)+3\le t+2.           \tag{L4}
\]
This argument does not assume that an arbitrary halting block first
enters halt at its certified endpoint.

Combining (L3)--(L4) and $b\ge4$ gives, by ordinary cancellation of
natural-number inequalities,
\[
 S_{1-j}(k)+b\le2t+4\le2t+b,
 \qquad S_{1-j}(k)\le2t.
\]
The other machine is halted at its exact boundary time $S_{1-j}(k)$,
so absorption gives the linear witness map $L(t)=2t$ and proves
\[
 \boxed{\mathsf{iPRA}\vdash h_0(t)\to h_1(2t),\qquad
        \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash h_1(t)\to h_0(2t).}         \tag{L5}
\]
All new numerical premises are checked in the aligned-duration receipt;
the previously established polynomial bound remains a valid, independent
baseline.  The linear improvement concerns halting-witness transport
between these exact two tables, not the state count or a claim that the
machines perform every corresponding instruction in equal time.
