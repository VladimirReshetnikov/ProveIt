\section{An exact alternative coding: exchange the equality and universal tags}
\label{sec:ue-tag-swap}

This section establishes a new numerical presentation and its exact source
word.  A state bound must be supplied by a separately checked physical
implementation; the existence of a shorter or differently shared word does
not itself give that bound.

Let $p(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b$.  The old constructors are
\[
 I(a,b)=4p(a,b),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,\quad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
The new constructors retain $I,M$ and put
\[
       U'(a,B)=4p(B,a)+3,\qquad E'(a,b)=4p(a,b)+2.
\]
Numerical truth and contradiction remain respectively $0$ and $1$.
Negation is $N(B)=I(B,1)$ in both systems.  Keep the same six head
expressions, dominated implicational schema, separate diagonal, guarded
gate, modus ponens and theorem generalization, interpreting their $U,E$
symbols by the appropriate constructors.  This changes the actual formula
codes and all emitted quantified/equality formulas.

\subsection{The simultaneous code-and-label involution}

Write $\sigma(0)=0,\sigma(1)=1,\sigma(2)=3,\sigma(3)=2$.
For $n>0$, unpair $\lfloor n/4\rfloor=p(a,b)$ and define
\[
 f(0)=0,\qquad f(n)=4p(f(a),f(b))+\sigma(n\bmod4).
\]
Both decoded arguments are strictly below $n$.  In particular the
definition is a primitive-recursive course-of-values recursion, implemented
by a finite table whose entries are constructed successively.  The zero
alias $I(0,0)=0$ is handled by the displayed base case.  Strong induction
on the input, encoded as a Boolean conjunction of the assertions below the
current input, proves in iPRA
\[
                 f(f(n))=n,\qquad f(0)=0,\quad f(1)=1.
\]
For example the first sixteen values are
\[
 0,1,3,2,4,5,7,6,8,9,11,10,24,25,27,26.
\]
This is not an order-preserving recoding.

The same $f$ acts on formula codes and on variable labels.  Its equations
are therefore exact even at the self-referential numerical interfaces:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 f(I(a,b))&=I(f(a),f(b)),&
 f(M(a,b))&=M(f(a),f(b)),\\
 f(U(a,B))&=U'(f(a),f(B)),&
 f(E(a,b))&=E'(f(a),f(b)).
\end{aligned}
\]
In particular, the old diagonal $I(c,U(c,c))$ maps to the new diagonal
$I(f(c),U'(f(c),f(c)))$.  Every occurrence of a gate parameter is recoded,
including its occurrences as a formula, a binder and an atomic label.
Thus $f(G(a,b,c))=G'(f(a),f(b),f(c))$, and injectivity gives the exact guard
equivalence
\[
 (c\ne a\land c\ne b)
 \quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
 (f(c)\ne f(a)\land f(c)\ne f(b)).
\]
Failed guarded heads are zero in both systems.  Fixing labels while
recoding formulas would not prove these identities and is not the
translation used here.

\subsection{The domination restriction is expanded, not transported}

The common propositional seed is
\[
 \rho(D,A,H,u)=((H\to u)\to D)\to((D\to H)\to(A\to H)),
 \qquad u\le D.
\]
An implication arrow in this display is $I$.  The inequality $u\le D$
need not survive $f$.  Consequently a source $\rho$ row must not simply
be retagged as a primitive target row.

Use instead the fixed generic proof of unrestricted $\rho$ from the
restricted basis, recorded in
\texttt{M6.Logic.RestrictedImplication.rho8All} and in the preceding
tagged-three packet.  The generic interface needs only modus ponens,
the restricted schema, reflexivity of $\le$, and the two bounds
$a,b\le I(a,b)$.  Here the implication constructor is literally unchanged,
so all those requirements hold.  The predecessor packet includes a
68,268-row universal expansion (32,455 restricted-$\rho$ leaves and
35,813 MP rows), with its primitive bounds justified by subtree inclusion.
Its compressed SHA-256 is
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
9fd2d5082a97dc051e4b56cb6e705f1d6f4f9bcabe82eff7fcbb954c6d285278.
\end{quote}
The present proof uses that attributed finite template; it does not claim
to have rediscovered the propositional basis.

All other seed rows translate by the constructor identities.  MP translates
because implication commutes with $f$, including the zero alias.  A Gen
row at label $a$ translates to Gen at label $f(a)$.  Scanning a proof list,
expanding each $\rho$ leaf, and relocating earlier-row pointers gives a
primitive-recursive proof transformation.  A quantifier-free prefix
induction proves that its output is valid and that every translated
conclusion is $f$ of the corresponding source conclusion.  Applying the
same construction in the opposite coding gives the reverse transformation.
Since $f(1)=1$, iPRA proves the exact refutation equivalence
\[
 (\exists e\,\operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal C}(e,1))
 \longleftrightarrow
 (\exists e\,\operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal C'}(e,1)).
\]
This proof is an instance of the simultaneous code-and-label recoding
method in the earlier tagged-three construction.  Its new component is
the concrete two-tag exchange and its exact source realization.

\subsection{The AX source realization}

In the old AX word, a constructor is the \texttt{cons} macro followed by
zero, one, two or three \texttt{incT} tokens.  The word has 32 implication
constructor occurrences, ten membership occurrences, fifteen universal
occurrences and three equality occurrences.  Exchange the runs of two and
three increments immediately following each constructor; retain all other
tokens, including increments constructing the numerical constant one.
The resulting word has 202 tokens instead of 190.

The supplied checker evaluates the whole word over free arithmetic terms,
without running numerical examples or importing the construction compiler.
It compares all nine endpoint registers, the complete eleven-entry stack,
both numerical guards, the actual unconsumed fourth operand, and every
intermediate scratch-register precondition.  It checks both the ordinary
AX11H and the independent-binder/diagonal AX11GDH interfaces.  The first
retains $U'(d,h)$ and $\Gamma'(c)$; the second retains $U'(c,h)$ and
$\Gamma'(d+x)$.  The remaining entries are exactly the corresponding six
new heads, new gate, unchanged restricted $\rho$, and saved incoming top.

The command-level proof compiler remains valid after replacing $U,E$ by
$U',E'$.  Its quantitative distinctions are still exact: implications
have residue zero, universals have residue three and are positive, and
contradiction has residue one.  Hence no universal cache can match an
implication in the Push cleanup, and none of the emitted axiom heads is
the contradiction.  The Cantor stack, including $p(0,0)=0$, is unchanged.
The fourth AX operand still yields the same two branches of $I(0,z)=0$,
both of which leave top and cache zero and offset $z$.

The source change does not assert that $f(d+x)=f(d)+f(x)$.  The new
proof-search command compiler selects its offsets and operands directly
in the new numerical coding; exhaustive scheduling then finds those words.
Nor does the theorem assert a time-step conjugacy between old and new
register executions.  Physical realization requires the new grammar,
resolved source graph, literal transition table, arbitrary-register
contracts and complete blank initialization to be checked separately.
