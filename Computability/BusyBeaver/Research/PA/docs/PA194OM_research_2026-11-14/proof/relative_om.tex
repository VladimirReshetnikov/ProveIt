\label{sec:relative-om}

$M_2$ be PA194OMF.  Each is a one-tape, two-symbol, blank-start Turing
machine with 194 working controls and a separate halt control.  Dense
control 0 is initial and dense control 194 is terminal.  Their table
identities, in this order, are

In particular native 194 is a working control.  The supplied injective
label maps conjugate every literal transition to the dense tables.
Quantifier-free induction on time therefore transfers every halting
statement below to the displayed dense machines.

The argument proves the new machines' relative equivalence directly
against the calibrated AX11H source, using complete literal contracts.
It also gives a second, direct physical proof of $M_1\leftrightarrow M_2$.
The absolute consistency result imports the exact revised AX11H written
calibration.  A written proof and finite executable certificates are being
provided; a newly completed end-to-end Lean proof is not being claimed.

\subsection{One source process and three whole-tape encodings}

A live source state has the form
\[
       z=(i,R_0,\ldots,R_8,n,Q),\qquad Q=[s]X\ne[].
\]
Here $i$ is a primitive site, the nine registers are natural numbers,
$n$ is header slack, and $Q$ is the finite nonempty spare list.
Adjoin an absorbing source halt code $H$.  Let $F$ perform one instruction
of the common array.  Besides the ordinary register effect, an increment
appends 1 to $Q$, a positive decrement increments $n$, and all other live
instructions leave $n,Q$ unchanged.  A halt instruction yields $H$ and
$F(H)=H$.  Source validity and all these operations have PR Boolean codes.

For $j=0,1,2$, write $p_j(i)$ for the finite address lookup.  Let
\[
 \pi=(6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1),\qquad V_{\pi(a)}=R_a,
 \qquad B(v)=0\,1^{v+1},\qquad W(X)=\sum_{v\in X}(v+2).
\]
The boundary encoding $E_j(z)$ has native control 84, head coordinate
$-2$, outward left stream $1\,0^\infty$, and right stream
\[
 p_j(i)\,0^{48+n-|p_j(i)|}\,1\,
 B(V_0)\cdots B(V_8)B(s)B(X)\,0^\infty.                 \tag{OM6}
\]
All addresses fit within the header.  Empty tails $X$, zero registers,
and zero spare entries are allowed.  Define $E_j(H)=H$.

Encode a relative-to-head tape by two natural numbers $L,R$, with the
scanned symbol the low bit of $R$.  Writing $w$ and moving right sends
\[
 (L,R)\longmapsto(2L+w,\lfloor R/2\rfloor);
\]
moving left sends it to
\[
 (\lfloor L/2\rfloor,(L\bmod2)+2w+4\lfloor R/2\rfloor).
\]
At a terminal control, replace the configuration by the single absorbing
code $H$.  This convention preserves the literal halt predicate.  Let
$\operatorname{Run}_j(t,c)$ be the resulting PR iterate.  Its semigroup
identity and absorption law are proved by quantifier-free induction.

\subsection{Uniform literal contracts}

For each site and decrement branch the independent replay records a
nonnegative 13-entry affine duration vector, evaluated at
\[
                    (1,n,x_0,\ldots,x_8,s,W(X)).           \tag{OM7}
\]
For a positive decrement of $R_a$, the corresponding variable is
$x_a=R_a-1$; other slots are the stated input registers or tested zero.
Every variable is therefore nonnegative, and its register slot is at most
the associated $R_a$.  The final slot is the width of the same list $X$
that occurs in (OM6), not an independent numerical variable.

The independent literal runs cover all 4,778 outcomes for each new table.
For $M_1$ the replay uses 1,101,819 symbolic events; for $M_2$, 1,101,219.
Both have at most 403 events per outcome.  The checker uses literal
transitions, unary self-loops, finite-word identities, and four block
traversals rederived from the exact table.  It uses no arithmetic-box
oracle.  Every nonhalting endpoint has zero head displacement and zero
address overflow, with the computed padded address equal to the padded
address of its resolved successor.  The sole halt outcome takes three
literal steps.

Here is the uniform arithmetic content of those checks.  A unary loop
crossing $a$ identical symbols is proved by quantifier-free induction on
the processed length.  Each certified block traversal takes exactly
$v+2$ steps on its block, so an induction on a finite list's prefix gives
duration $W(X)$ and preserves both exterior contexts.  The normalizations
\[
 C(X)1=1B(X),\qquad
 1\operatorname{rev}(C(X))=\operatorname{rev}(B(X))1,
 \qquad C(v)=10\,1^v
\]
have the same finite-prefix proof.  Literal events are substitutions in
the tape-step equations.  Composition of a successful finite event trace
therefore proves its exact whole-tape endpoint at its affine duration.
These induction invariants are equalities of PR functions together with
bounded Boolean checks.  They contain no unbounded universal environment
predicate.

Select $D_j(z)$ by the source site's decidable branch and (OM7), and put
$D_j(H)=0$.  The event-calculus argument and complete source binding give
the following uniform iPRA theorem:
\[
 \begin{split}
 \operatorname{Valid}(z)&\Longrightarrow
       \operatorname{Run}_j(D_j(z),E_j(z))=E_j(F(z)),\\
 \operatorname{Valid}(z)\land z\ne H&\Longrightarrow D_j(z)\ge3.
\end{split}                                                \tag{OM8}
\]
Source validity is preserved.  At $H$ the equality uses duration zero;
no positive stopped duration is asserted.

\subsection{Explicit primitive recursive witnesses}

The common initial source state is
\[
                   z_*=(25,2,0,\ldots,0,0,[1]^{26}).
\]
Entire literal blank-tape runs give
\[
 \operatorname{Run}_j(T_j,\mathrm{blank})=E_j(z_*),
 \qquad (T_0,T_1,T_2)=(57922,58014,58010).                  \tag{OM9}
\]
These equalities compare the complete tape, state and head, including the
untouched zero exterior.  A separate small simulator in
\path{check_relative_om.py} reproduces all three exact initializations.
They are not inferred from source-level tests or a finite sample of
selected cells.

Define by primitive recursion
\[
 z_k=F^k(z_*),\qquad \tau_j(0)=0,\qquad
 \tau_j(k+1)=\tau_j(k)+D_j(z_k).
\]
Quantifier-free induction using (OM8) and source validity yields
\[
 \operatorname{Run}_j(T_j+\tau_j(k),\mathrm{blank})=E_j(z_k).
                                                               \tag{OM10}
\]
This remains exact after halting, because both processes are absorbing
and all subsequent durations are zero.

For all three machines the componentwise maximum of the recorded duration
vectors is exactly
\[
                    (534,8,4,4,4,4,4,4,2,4,4,2,2).       \tag{OM11}
\]
Consequently every live source duration is bounded by
\[
       D_j(z)\le534+8n+4\sum_{a=0}^{8}R_a+2s+2W(X).
\]
Along the run from $z_*$, quantifier-free induction proves the following
bounds whenever $z_k\ne H$:
\[
 n_k\le k,\quad \sum_a R_{a,k}\le2+k,\quad
 Q_k=[1]^{26+r_k},\quad r_k\le k.
                                                               \tag{OM12}
\]
The only operation changing the spare list appends a 1.  Hence while live,
$s_k=1$ and $W(X_k)\le75+3k$.  Substituting gives the sharper bound
\[
 D_j(z_k)\le694+18k,
 \qquad\tau_j(k)\le694k+18\sum_{r<k}r=9k^2+685k.        \tag{OM13}
\]
At $H$ the duration is zero, so the bound extends across halt.
The explicit polynomial clocks are
\[
\begin{split}
 P_0(k)&=57922+9k^2+685k,\\
 P_1(k)&=58014+9k^2+685k,\\
 P_2(k)&=58010+9k^2+685k.
\end{split}                                                  \tag{OM14}
\]
They are upper bounds for the exact clocks $T_j+\tau_j(k)$, not claims
that every instruction attains the componentwise worst case.

To reflect physical halting, another quantifier-free induction gives
\[
              z_k\ne H\ \Longrightarrow\ \tau_j(k)\ge3k. \tag{OM15}
\]
In its successor step, $z_{k+1}\ne H$ implies $z_k\ne H$ by source
absorption, so (OM8) adds at least three.  The statement deliberately
asserts no cofinality after the source has halted.

Suppose $M_j$ is halted by physical time $t$, and set $k=t+1$.
If $z_k\ne H$, (OM15) places the left side of (OM10) strictly after
$t$.  Physical absorption makes it halted, but the right side is a live
frame at control 84.  This is impossible.  Equality to the source halt
code is decidable, so intuitionistic reasoning yields $z_{t+1}=H$.
Conversely $z_k=H$ implies physical halting at its exact clock by (OM10),
and therefore by $P_j(k)$ by absorption and (OM13).

Let $h_j(t)$ be the atomic PR assertion that $M_j$ is halted by time $t$
from blank tape.  For every $i,j\in\{0,1,2\}$ we have proved
\[
              \mathrm{iPRA}\vdash
              h_i(t)\ \longrightarrow\ h_j(P_j(t+1)).    \tag{OM16}
\]
These are explicit witnesses.  The proof uses neither unbounded least-time
search nor Markov's principle.  Existential introduction and elimination
give pairwise equivalence of the three halting assertions.  Applying the
two implications under negation gives, intuitionistically,
\[
 \mathrm{iPRA}\vdash
 \neg\operatorname{Halt}_{\mathrm{blank}}(M_0)
   \longleftrightarrow\neg\operatorname{Halt}_{\mathrm{blank}}(M_j),
 \qquad j=1,2.                                             \tag{OM17}
\]
Here $\neg\exists t\,h_j(t)$ is equivalently
$\forall t\,\neg h_j(t)$ in intuitionistic logic.

\subsection{A second physical proof for the six-row variant}

PA194OMF applies the existing history selector and two local acceleration
ideas to the new combined reader table.  Their proofs are included here
to verify that their entry conditions survive the composition.  Relative
to $M_1$, the exact six changed native rows are
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
q/b&M_1&M_2\\\hline
104/1&1R124&1R127\\
127/0&0R14&0L124\\
124/0&0R62&0R8\\
124/1&1R138&1R59\\
26/1&1R42&1R91\\
101/0&0L26&0R17.
\end{array}                                                  \tag{OM18}
\]
All six occur in the new blank initialization.

First apply only the selector, obtaining an intermediate machine $S$.
Let $f(124)=127$ and let $f$ fix all other labels.  Complete predecessor
inspection gives
\[
 \operatorname{pred}_{M_1}(127)=\{110/0\mapsto0R127\},\qquad
 \operatorname{pred}_{M_1}(124)=\{104/1\mapsto1R124\}.
\]
Hence a run from control 74 has the entry invariant
\[
 q=127\Rightarrow T(h-1)=0,
 \qquad q=124\Rightarrow T(h-1)=1.
\]
The common read-one row $1R138$ needs no selection.  On a scanned zero,
the merged control moves left to selector 124, which reads and preserves
the history bit, moves right to helper 8 or 59, and that helper effects
the appropriate old transition.  The unchanged identities are
$\delta(8,0)=0R14$ and $\delta(59,0)=0R62$.
All four local cases have identical whole-tape endpoints, with one child
step for a scanned 1 and three for a scanned 0.

Map an ordinary parent configuration $(q,h,T)$ to $(f(q),h,T)$.
The PR simulation clock adds three for a zero scanned at either paired
control and one otherwise.  It lies between $t$ and $3t$.
Quantifier-free induction, followed by absorbing halting, proves
\[
       h_{M_1}(t)\Rightarrow h_S(3t),\qquad
       h_S(t)\Rightarrow h_{M_1}(t).                        \tag{OM19}
\]

For the two accelerations, the intermediate table still has exactly
\[
 \operatorname{pred}(101)=\{96/0\mapsto0R101\},\qquad
 \operatorname{pred}(26)=\{11/0\mapsto0L26,\ 101/0\mapsto0L26\},
\]
and every row entering 11 writes zero.  Induction on one literal step
therefore proves
\[
\begin{split}
 q=101&\Rightarrow T(h-1)=0,\\
 q=11&\Rightarrow T(h-1)=0\ \lor\ T(h+1)=0,\\
 q=26\ \land\ T(h)=1&\Rightarrow T(h+1)=T(h+2)=0.
\end{split}
\]
For the last clause the incoming move writes the first zero.  Entry from
101 would force the newly scanned symbol to zero; entry from 11 with a
newly scanned 1 forces its other neighboring zero, giving the second one.
The guarded whole-tape identities are
\[
 \begin{array}{rcl}
 26/1:\quad1R42,\ 0R13,\ 0L91&\rightsquigarrow&1R91,\\
 101/0:\quad0L26,\ 0R194,\ 0R17&\rightsquigarrow&0R17.
 \end{array}
\]
Their intermediate controls are nonterminal, and they inspect only the
stated finite windows.  The inspector checks all twelve admissible
five-cell windows.  Every exterior tape is therefore preserved as well.

Simulate one $M_2$ step by three $S$ steps at a shortcut and one otherwise.
The induction hypothesis identifies each child boundary with an actually
reachable $S$ configuration, so the just-proved $S$ guards apply there.
The clock again lies between $t$ and $3t$, giving
\[
              h_S(t)\Rightarrow h_{M_2}(t),\qquad
              h_{M_2}(t)\Rightarrow h_S(3t).
\]
Combining these with (OM19) gives the direct, linear witness transports
\[
       \mathrm{iPRA}\vdash
       h_1(t)\Rightarrow h_2(3t),\qquad
       h_2(t)\Rightarrow h_1(3t).                           \tag{OM20}
\]
The factor is three in each direction because the selector and shortcut
affect opposite directions of the simulation.  This proof is independent
of the common-source calibration and of any consistency assumption.

\subsection{The absolute consistency corollary and its scope}

The revised AX11H research calibration, identified by its exact table hash
above, supplies the written result
\[
 \mathrm{iPRA}\vdash
 \neg\operatorname{Halt}_{\mathrm{blank}}(M_0)
      \longleftrightarrow\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA}). \tag{OM21}
\]
Its consistency sentence is the pinned native Foundation PA consistency
sentence used by that calibration.  Its proof chain includes the literal
source simulation, exact command interface, numerical proof-list compiler,
current Foundation-to-ordinary-PAeq adapter, and model-uniform weak-arithmetic
transfer.  The generic negative transfer does not use Markov's principle.
The calibration archive's SHA-256 is\nopagebreak

\path{93ee082ef035b2d32e71d6af5239b25b4bea1773f03bea4d2c39810a7fe15075}.
\end{center}

Composing (OM17) with (OM21) proves the promised relative-to-that-calibration
absolute corollaries:
\[
 \boxed{\quad\mathrm{iPRA}\vdash
 \neg\operatorname{Halt}_{\mathrm{blank}}(\mathrm{PA194OM})
       \longleftrightarrow\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})\quad}
\]
and
\[
 \boxed{\quad\mathrm{iPRA}\vdash
 \neg\operatorname{Halt}_{\mathrm{blank}}(\mathrm{PA194OMF})
       \longleftrightarrow\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA}).\quad}
\]
This composition is purely intuitionistic.  It imports a written
mathematical theorem for the exact parent rather than silently asserting
that the unfinished Lean development is complete.  The new finite
certificates and the arithmetic arguments above establish the new relative
layer; they do not independently rebuild every inherited PA derivation.

\paragraph{Finite checks and their meaning.}
The small independent inspector checks the three literal table identities
and conjugacies, source graph equality, all reader code words and the
secondary repair, every contract index and affine maximum, complete blank
initialization, all SF predecessor sets and local windows, and constants
in (OM14).  Its receipt is \path{relative_om_inspection.json}.
The full literal replayer separately supplies uniform source contracts;
the event-calculus induction explains their meaning for arbitrary register
and spare-list values.  These two components are complementary.  Passing
bounded random tests alone would not establish (OM8) or the iPRA theorem.

\paragraph{What the other suggested projects contribute.}
The DiophantineConsistency and ConciseIPRA developments clarify the useful
proof interface: finite computations, primitive recursive witness maps and
negative weak-arithmetic transfers can be packaged independently of a
particular machine encoding.  That is the role of (OM8)--(OM17).
A shorter arithmetic sentence, a more compact proof checker, or a reduction
to a Diophantine equation does not by itself lower the number of states of
a two-symbol blank-tape machine; decoding, arithmetic and initialization
must still be realized by a complete transition table.  The present work
does not claim an exhaustive search of alternative axiomatizations.
