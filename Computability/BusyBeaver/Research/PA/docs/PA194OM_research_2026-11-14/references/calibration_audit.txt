Trusted dependencies and limits of this audit
--------------------------------------------
The cited mathematical calibration relies on the pinned upstream proof
constructors, source numerical calculus, current Foundation PA predicate,
and the machine-independent conservation/negative-translation chain. Current
Foundation gitlink is 32e1a0956a8622fad067328ca1959729a7634428 as documented by
the packet. This audit read the new TeX arguments and the main transfer Lean
interfaces; it did not independently rebuild every fixed derivation recipe,
the transitive Lean dependency closure, or the entire forward compiler. No
concrete logical gap was found in the examined chain. That is a review of an
inherited proof, not an independent kernel certification of that proof.

What a new candidate must supply
--------------------------------
An explicit iPRA finite-trace/halting equivalence with one of those exact
parents suffices to transport its written consistency theorem. If source
commands change, also give their all-store soundness and a cofinal compiler of
every numerical refutation. If only the physical implementation changes,
prove complete frame preservation, exact local endpoints, primitive-recursive
durations, and blank startup. Finite sample simulations alone do not supply
any of these uniform proofs. One-step state-graph isomorphism is not enough
to identify a materially different implementation.

Useful cross-project ideas
--------------------------
ZFC: contextual state sharing, retained finite history, and separated proof
roles are applicable only with whole-frame contracts and an independent blank
startup. Equivalent presentations and shared roles cannot have their savings
counted twice. K215's external Lean/Rocq success does not itself supply a PA
or iPRA internal endpoint for an unrelated table.
DiophantineConsistency: the decisive reusable contribution is the existing
model-uniform I-Sigma-1 to iPRA transfer machinery and primitive recursive
witness interfaces. A lower polynomial operation count does not imply a lower
blank-start binary TM state count: constants, arithmetic evaluation, tuple
enumeration, and initialization must all be compiled.
ConciseIPRA: replace expensive primitive logical interfaces by interderivable
smaller ones and expose proof witnesses rather than semantic truth tests.
Searching for an iPRA contradiction cannot replace searching for a PA
contradiction; the consistency target must remain PA.
