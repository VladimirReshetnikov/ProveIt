\section{A precise obstruction for the inherited 193-control runtime}

The supplied \texttt{pa\_sub194\_research\_2026-11-13} packet already contains
an interesting 193-control runtime.  It identifies native control 301 with
88, deletes 301, and replaces two rows of the primary 194-control table by
\[
 (88,0)\mapsto(0,L,51),\qquad (51,0)\mapsto(0,R,122).
\]
Its native table has 386 rows and SHA-256
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
50f7be44afb63293365270a83111299247d12d140614e4b4c9a52786163acb53.
\end{quote}
This is an inherited runtime construction, not a new blank-start consistency
machine.  Its 4,780 source outcomes have an inherited symbolic verification;
its initialized source frame must still be supplied.  The changed register
marker is justified by the following precise identity: after the unmarked
register delimiter, the next bit is 1, and the rows $(16,1)$ and $(122,1)$
both equal $0L1$.  This identity does not apply to a missing register during
blank initialization.

A prior supplied obstruction fixes native start 74 and permits all
permutations of the physical register selectors.  The following result
extends that obstruction to \emph{every existing initial control}.

\paragraph{Theorem (all starts, all physical allocations).}
Let $R$ be exactly the 193-control runtime just specified.  Put
\[
 K=(51,36,25,48,32,33,22,55,60).
\]
For a permutation $\pi$ of $\{0,\ldots,8\}$, replace every external selector
edge with target $K_i$ by an edge with target $K_{\pi(i)}$.  The eleven changed
edge positions are
\[
 (14,1),(17,1),(21,1),(39,1),(41,1),(45,1),
 (56,1),(61,1),(82,1),(144,1),(161,1).
\]
Leave all other rows fixed, in particular $(88,0)$ and the internal skip
chain.  For each of the $193\cdot9!=70{,}035{,}840$ choices of initial
control and allocation, its blank computation has an elementary finite
certificate of one of the following behaviors:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lr}
\hline
Behavior & Number of choices\\\hline
Finite halt & 725,760\\
An all-zero control cycle, never writing 1 & 3,628,800\\
An outward monotone zero-reading ray & 65,681,280\\\hline
Total & 70,035,840\\\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The certificate is reached by at most 94 transitions.  No choice reaches a
complete source frame with nine primary registers and a nonempty spare file.

\paragraph{Proof.}
All permuted edges are read-1 edges.  Until the first write of 1, the tape
therefore remains blank and the control trajectory is independent of the
allocation.  Enumerating the fixed read-0 graph partitions the 193 starts
into the following classes; the listed multiplicity is the number of
initial controls whose first write of 1 occurs at that control:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|rrrrrrrr|r}
First writer &0&4&16&35&66&74&97&198&No first write\\\hline
Multiplicity&1&5&2&169&2&2&1&1&10
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Before the first write, the computation merely translates the blank head.
Consequently each finite-prefix class reduces to its displayed writer as
initial control, with the same tape and a shifted origin.  The ten remaining
starts enter closed read-0 control cycles that write only 0.

Seven of the eight first-writer computations inspect no selector edge before
halting or entering a certified outward ray.  Start 0 halts after 37 steps;
start 97 halts after 31.  Starts 4, 16 and 35 already satisfy an outward-ray
certificate.  Starts 66 and 198 reach the control-35 ray after 44 and 38
steps respectively.  Only writer 74 depends on the allocation.  Before its
ray certificate, the only selector used is $(45,1)$, originally directed to
$K_7$.  If $\pi(7)=j$, it reaches native control 35 at head coordinate $7+j$
after $85+j$ steps, with precisely the one-cells $\{-3,1\}$.  Its row
$(35,0)=1R35$ then writes 1 indefinitely on fresh cells to the right.
There are thus seven allocation-independent leaves and nine leaves indexed
by $j$, only sixteen finite cases altogether.

Each one-cell support before the certified tail has size at most five.
A full source frame requires at least twelve one-cells: the left marker,
the register-file header, nine primary unary blocks, and one spare block.
No such frame occurs in a finite prefix.  Every certified rightward ray
has all cells to the right of its head zero, whereas a source frame requires
a header and register blocks there.  Every certified leftward ray has
the cell immediately left of its head still zero, whereas a source frame
requires that cell to be the one-valued left marker.
The all-zero cycles never write 1.  Weighting each partial allocation by its factorial
number of completions and each first-writer class by its multiplicity gives
the displayed counts.  The longest fixed prefix to writer 74 has length one,
so the maximum certificate clock is $1+93=94$.

The finite prefix and cycle/ray assertions are replayed by
\texttt{allocation\_all\_starts.py}.  For a monotone ray the finite
certificate lists a read-0 path, its closed control cycle, and its common
move direction.  Induction repeats this cycle on fresh zero cells, even
when 1s are written behind the head.  For a zero cycle, the tape stays
identically zero.  These inductions and the finite prefix computations are
primitive recursive and admit the usual quantifier-free internalization in
\(\mathrm{iPRA}\).  No newly compiled Lean theorem is asserted.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The theorem fixes the table except for the eleven selector targets.  It
makes no statement about changing other bootstrap rows, changing the tape
representation, altering the source program, or designing a different
193-control machine.  In particular it is not a state-count lower bound.

\section{A broader finite search for a one-row bootstrap repair}

The investigation also tested every replacement of a single transition of
$R$ by an action whose destination is one of its 193 working controls.
This gives 297,607 nontrivial edits.  The baseline was included, for 297,608
tables.  An action that instead enters the halt label cannot repair this
bootstrap: if the changed row is not used, the original rejected prefix is
unchanged; if it is used, the run halts immediately before a new source
frame can be reached.  The remaining 1,543 terminal-target edits are
therefore excluded by this simple observation, not by an unreported search.

Every possible initial control was covered by the first-write reduction.
For each mutant it suffices to start at every control whose read-0 row
writes 1; an earlier all-zero prefix is immaterial up to translation.  This
is complete for finding a source frame at any searched clock from any
initial control.  It reduces the number of literal simulations to
2,449,186.

The detector accepts \emph{any} of the 3,673 source sites, with arbitrary
values of all nine primary registers, any nonempty finite spare list,
any translation, and padding $48+n$ for $n\geq0$.  It checks the entire tape
support.  This extends the prior startup screens that accepted only sites
25--44.  It does not treat a physical frame as a valid logical seed.
For example, the detector finds the original 194-control machine's first
complete frame at source site 3625 after 56,366 transitions, before its
usual advertised seed at site 25.

The clock checkpoints satisfy $0\leq t<200{,}000$; a nonmatching run also
stops when the head displacement or the number of one-cells exceeds 20,000.
These limits are search limits, not divergence certificates.  Exact hit
replay checks all 198 entire tapes, totaling 10,049,719 transitions.

None of the 163 hit tables was validated as a repair of the inherited
source implementation.  Of these, 97 fail the stated finite register-marker
test; the other 66 fail the existing universal symbolic source-contract
checker.  For the latter 66, literal complete-frame tests at padding values
$0,1,10$ produce 93 unintended halts, 33 wrong clean source successors, and
72 runs that do not close within the separate 20,000-step countercheck
bound.  The bounded cases are recorded as unresolved, not as proofs of
permanent nonclosure.  The first two counts give explicit discrepancies from the prescribed
clean source-step interface; no correctness transfer is obtained.  They are
not arguments that
every differently interpreted program with the same table is impossible.

Thus this broader search supplies new reproducible exclusions and diagnostic
counterexamples, but no accepted 193-state blank-start consistency machine.
The 34,093 bounded bootstrap outcomes, changes to two or more unrestricted
rows, different arithmetic encodings, and different source programs remain
outside any claimed negative theorem.
