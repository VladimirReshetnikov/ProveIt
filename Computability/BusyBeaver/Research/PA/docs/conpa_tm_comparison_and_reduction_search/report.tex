\begin{abstract}
The requested target is a binary, single-tape, blank-start Turing machine
with fewer than 196 working states for which PA proves that nonhalting is
equivalent to $\Con(\PA)$. \textbf{This investigation does not establish
such a machine.} Its smallest retained state count is 196. The deliverable
is a comparison and research package, not a claim to a new Busy Beaver
state-count record.

We compare every relevant incoming archive at a pinned ProveIt snapshot,
the published 209-state machine, and its 196-state successor. The nine
designated tables in five principal 208-state submissions form two rooted
transition-graph classes; a further packet supplies eighteen locally
proved variants. We reconstruct the 196-state AX decoder exactly,
identify why the old 208-state fold is not an additional saving on that
decoder, and give two one-row changes to the 196-state interpreter that
reduce its source instruction count. The stronger change returns the
modus-ponens consequent in a different register and has an explicit
complete command compiler. Neither change lowers the physical state
count. A paper-level syntactic argument supplies the missing direction
of the PA consistency equivalence, with its unimplemented formalization
obligations stated explicitly. Failed physical layouts and the finite
scopes of unsuccessful searches are retained as reproducible evidence.
\end{abstract}

\section{Target, conventions, and provenance}

For a literal machine $M$, let $H_M$ be the arithmetical sentence asserting
the existence of a finite halting trace from its specified blank initial
configuration. The intended result is
\[
 |Q|\le195,\qquad
 \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg H_M\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
\]
The tape is infinite in both directions, its alphabet is $\{0,1\}$,
the blank symbol is $0$, and each transition writes a symbol and moves
one cell left or right. A separate terminal state has no outgoing rows
and is not counted among the working states. In particular, the published
196-state machine has states $0,\ldots,195$ and halt label $196$.
Its initial state counts as a working state. Renaming the terminal state,
omitting the initializer from the count, or changing the alphabet would
not meet the requested target under these conventions.

\subsection{Evidence levels}

Four different assertions must remain separate. A literal table check
binds a file to a state count and a hash. A local equivalence proof shows
that a transformation preserves computation under proved entry
invariants. A source or command proof explains the computation at a
higher level. Finally, an internal PA proof connects the exact trace
sentence to a syntactic consistency predicate. Passing examples at one
level does not discharge the obligations at the others.

The positive finite local checks for the eighteen 208-state helper
variants quantify over all relevant two-cell patterns and leave the
exterior tape opaque. They are finite proof checks. By contrast, the
new 196-state primitive-frame checks use concrete register values and
both decrement outcomes; they are bounded falsification tests. The
manuscript supplies the accompanying command arguments but does not
replace a missing uniform tape formalization by those examples.

\subsection{The exact conditional-history fold}

Use the native labels of the Conditional Context packet, in which halt
is 254. Its class-A reduction deletes state 172, redirects
\[
 116/0:\ 0R172\ \longmapsto\ 0R211,
 \qquad
 211/0:\ 0R157\ \longmapsto\ 0L217.
\]
The deleted rows are $172/0:0L67$ and $172/1:1R184$.
The new state 211 represents both old roles. Their zero branches remain
distinguishable because of an invariant of the tape to the left of the
head. Writing $t_j$ for the bit at offset $j$, the relevant conditions are
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
116&t_{-1}=1\\
172&t_{-2}=1\ \land\ t_{-1}=0\\
217&t_{-1}=1\\
163&t_{-1}=1\ \lor\ t_{-2}=1\\
211&t_0=1\ \lor\ t_{-1}=1.
\end{array}
\]
The full incoming-edge census proves these conditions by induction from
state 0 on every initial tape. In particular, the condition on 211 is
conditional on its scanned symbol; asserting an unconditional left-hand
one would be false.

The replacement blocks simulate one original transition in the following
numbers of steps:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}
\toprule
Original role/read &172/0&172/1&211/0&211/1\\\midrule
Replacement length&7&1&3&1\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
They restore exactly the original tape and head endpoint, with the state
identification applied. If $N_{q,b}(n)$ counts occurrences of row $(q,b)$
among the first $n$ parent transitions, the simulation clock is
\[
 \tau(n)=n+6N_{172,0}(n)+2N_{211,0}(n),\qquad n\le\tau(n)\le7n.
\]
Thus the local proof establishes halting equivalence, including blank-start
equivalence, and is arithmetizable. Multiple presentations of that same
fold do not supply multiple state savings.

\subsection{Why the old saving does not extend PA196 directly}

The old roles 172 and 211 are absent from the new AX body. PA196 uses six
different predecessor-bit folds in a new 68-node macro grammar. The
independent compiler reconstructs its unfolded 202-state table and then
reapplies those six folds; the result equals all 392 supplied rows,
after the published dense/native renaming. Consequently, simply applying
the 208-state patch to PA196 is not a defined transformation. The useful
transfer is the method---recovering control information from proved tape
context---rather than the old numerical labels.

\section{The exact numerical calculus behind the comparison}

The following definitions make the logical comparison explicit. They are
the constructors in \path{PA196/Logic/Basic.lean} of the pinned source.
For $a,b\in\mathbb N$, put
\[
 p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b,
 \quad I(a,b)=4p(a,b),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,
\]
\[
 U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,\qquad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
Here $M(a,b)$ denotes the formula whose interpretation says that $x_b$
is a member of $x_a$, and $U(a,B)$ binds variable $x_a$ in the formula
with code $B$. Define coded negation, conjunction and existence by
\[
 N(B)=I(B,1),\quad B\wedge C=N(I(B,N(C))),\quad
 \exists x_a B=N(U(a,N(B))).
\]
Code 0 decodes as truth; code 1 decodes as $x_0\in x_0$ and is the
refutation target. It is not a primitive falsity symbol. The syntactic
translation later in the report explicitly proves its falsity in PA.

In the following displays, an arrow abbreviates the numerical constructor
$I$, so a displayed formula specifies an actual natural-number code.
PA196's six unrestricted heads are
\begin{align*}
 & (\exists x_D\,U(D,H))\to H,\\
 & U(D,U(A,H))\to U(A,U(D,H)),\\
 & M(H,D)\to(E(H,A)\to M(A,D)),\\
 & E(H,D)\to(M(A,H)\to M(A,D)),\\
 & \exists x_D\,E(H,D),\\
 & U(D,A)\to\bigl(U(D,A\to H)\to U(D,H)\bigr).
\end{align*}
The restricted propositional head is
\[
 \rho_8(a,b,c,u)=((c\to u)\to a)\to((a\to c)\to(b\to c)),
 \qquad u\le a.
\]
The numerical restriction matters to the available derivations even
though every unrestricted instance is classically valid under the
intended interpretation.

For $c\ne a,b$, define the gate from
\begin{align*}
 \Delta&=E(a,b)\to U(c,E(a,b)), & V&=M(a,b)\to U(c,M(a,b)),\\
 P&=c\to M(c,b), &
 S&=U\bigl(b,(M(a,b)\to P)\wedge(M(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
 Q&=U(c,S\to N(P)), & F(a,b,c)&=N\bigl(\Delta\to(V\to Q)\bigr).
\end{align*}
If the guard fails, the operational gate returns 0. The number $c$
plays two roles in this definition: it is the matrix formula code and
the fresh variable label used for the separation witness. The decoder
bound that all variable labels occurring in code $c$ are below $c$
is therefore an essential part of the soundness proof.

PA209 uses the untransposed quantifier code $4p(a,B)+2$, a gate
$N(V\to Q)$, and an additional equality head
\[
 \rho_7(a,b,c)=E(c,a)\to
       \bigl(E(c,b)\to U(M(a,b),E(a,b))\bigr).
\]
PA196 removes this seventh head and adds $\Delta$ to the guarded gate.
Thus the reduction is a change of a precisely specified calculus and
its interpretation, followed by a better compiler layout. It cannot be
justified by deleting the old equality-head emitter without proving the
replacement equality facts.

\subsection{A source simplification which increases sharing cost}

A corrected three-category abstract interpretation, initialized at source
site 25 with logical registers $(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$, proves that register
6 is zero at site 7. Deleting the associated two primitive sites preserves
the remaining source control-flow graph. Yet the original comparison
subtrees are shared with other, unrestricted comparisons. Specializing
only this occurrence increases the number of distinct internal source
nodes from 159 to 161. Patching its shared physical decoder globally
would change other command comparisons. This is a concrete example of
why a smaller source instruction list need not mean fewer tape states.

\subsection{A rejected physical realization of the simpler Shift}

The command-level $H$-only Shift described below is valid. One attempted
source reassociation to realize it with existing decoders is not. The
attempt changes the unique outer continuation path from \code{101011}
to \code{1010011}. The existing continuation router erases two terminal
ones and crosses one zero before toggling a higher bit; the new path
requires crossing two zeros. Its apparent reuse of the old router returns
to path \code{1011} rather than the required \code{100000}.

The resulting \path{REJECTED_shift_layout.tm}, and the version combined
with the new MP branch, both halt from the blank tape in exactly
43,563 transitions. They are counterexamples to that compilation attempt,
not candidate consistency machines. The archive includes the bad tables,
the failing frame, and a reproducible blank-run check. An exhaustive
784-action attempt to repair one existing routing row found no repair
within that narrowly specified family. No impossibility claim follows.

\section{Reduction searches and the meaning of their failures}

The searches targeted changes that could be justified by small proofs:
local state folds, head-offset identifications, alternative AX grammars,
source specializations and register layouts. A positive table would still
require the source, startup and consistency bridges. A negative bounded
search establishes only that no candidate passed that search's predicate.

\subsection{Finite-window local folds}

The local checker computes an overapproximation of reachable configurations
in a window $[-k,k]$ around the head. A move introduces either value at
the newly exposed boundary cell. Starting from the blank configuration
makes the abstraction sound for all actual blank-run windows. The
replacement checker then refuses any unmodeled exterior read and requires
exact equality of tape, head and projected state at the endpoint.

Searches included pairs with an identical branch, pairs whose branch
actions have short equivalent continuations, changes to both rows,
single-state bypasses, and head offsets of two cells. The finite-window
constraints were tried at radii four and five in the recorded runs, with
explicit local step limits. No verified one-state saving was found for
the baseline or the result-in-$H$ variant. The more elaborate same-count
helper strategy from the 208 packets was also tested on 8,061,300
two-disjoint-pair/add-one-helper proposals and yielded no survivor in its
35-step local scope.

These results do not exhaust all possible tape invariants. The successful
208 reduction itself illustrates why a previously unexamined conditional
context can matter. In particular, failure of this finite-window procedure
does not prove that two roles cannot be identified by a stronger invariant
or a different simulation relation.

\subsection{Grammar, coding, and physical-price searches}

The main AX macro word has length 201 and a 68-node binary DAG.
Rotations and reparsing can alter the number of distinct subtrees and
the availability of predecessor-bit folds. A raw grammar count is not
the full objective: a layout must also preserve the resolved source
control-flow graph, the maximum zero-run constraint of the return scanner,
every arithmetic entry into a shared decoder, and the blank initializer.

One recorded priced search performed 59,686 rotations and priced 46,294
distinct trees in 240 seconds; its best physical count remained 196.
Separate catalog searches retained hundreds of equal-count layouts.
The no-long-zero-run catalog retained 129 layouts priced at 196 among
18,922 priced layouts. Additional local-fold searches on the recorded
catalogs, including 68 compatible layouts with the new MP branch, found
no accepted 195-state table.

Other finite searches permuted axiom emission order, changed compatible
antecedent order, changed constructor orientation and selected tag
assignments, and varied source register placement. The coding experiments
included sixteen combinations of transposing constructors and exchanging
the two quantifier/equality residues; no improvement over the baseline
68-node AX grammar was obtained in that family. Register transpositions
require their own startup argument, because a permutation of logical
registers changes the physical role played by the initializer.

A restricted dictionary-segmentation mixed-integer model was also run
for 180 seconds. It recovered a 68-node grammar and left a relaxation
gap. The numerical solver bound applies only to its finite dictionary
model; it is neither a bound on all grammars nor a bound on Turing-machine
state count. It is not used as a theorem in this report.

\subsection{Scanner and axiom-removal proposals}

Deleting one stage from the seven-zero left scanner fails concrete source
frames, including site 0 with path \code{000000} and site 25 with path
\code{100000}. In one site-0 example the original reaches its correct
successor in 486 steps; the shortened scanner instead produces a spurious
root at head offset $+8$ after 236 steps. Bypassing dense state 2 by state
3 also fails the primitive frame contracts.

Dropping the final source continuation is harmless for an abstract cyclic
driver, but it moves the final increment into a right-child leaf position.
The current hardware routes its occurrences as left-child leaves. That
source-level observation therefore does not yet provide a physical state
deletion.

No redundancy proof was found for the quantifier-commutation head.
The obvious derivation through full specialization is circular in this
particular calculus: compound vacuity for a quantified formula uses the
commutation head, and the required existential elimination then depends
on that vacuity. This is an unresolved optimization, not a proof that the
head is indispensable. Likewise, ConciseIPRA's quantifier-free induction
does not by itself preserve the full-PA consistency calibration.
