\section{Fresh physical contraction searches}
\label{sec:fresh-physical}
The new search did not produce a machine with fewer than 194 working
states. Its scope is nevertheless more specific than another unstructured
mutation search: it includes changes to the representation of a control
state by a neighboring tape bit or by a head displacement.

\paragraph{Head displacement.}
Suppose every transition into a control $q$ moves in direction
$d\in\{-1,1\}$. Replace those incoming moves by $-d$ and their target by
an existing control $r$. A configuration with old control $q$ and head $h$
is represented by new control $r$ and head $h-2d$ on the same tape.
The unchanged-control obligations then hold directly from the changed
incoming rows. The remaining obligation is to simulate each transition
from $q$ by a positive finite run of the child from this displaced
configuration, ending at the representation of the old successor.
The program \texttt{shift\_merge.cpp} checks these obligations on a finite
conservative set of local windows.

\paragraph{Encoding a control in the preceding tape cell.}
A second transformation flips the bit written by every incoming
transition into $q$, and redirects the transition to $r$. The
representation complements the cell at $h-d$; it can also displace the
head by $-2d$ if the incoming direction is reversed. Local simulation must
restore the complete affected tape, including this complemented cell.
\texttt{retag\_merge.cpp} retains the rows of $r$.
\texttt{retag\_actions.cpp} also synthesizes its two rows, checking the
obligations of both old controls. These changes are different from a
same-position identification of two control states.

\paragraph{Why the sufficient condition is sound.}
Let $F$ be the indicated configuration representation. At every parent
configuration covered by the invariant, require a child run of length at
least one from $F(c)$ to $F(c')$, where $c'$ is the parent successor.
The local check preserves the entire finite window, and never reads or
writes outside it, so it applies to every exterior tape. Concatenating
these witnesses simulates every finite parent run. Positivity ensures
that an infinite parent run produces an infinite child run. Determinism
then gives equivalence of halting from the represented initial
configuration. This argument is elementary enough to formalize in PA;
it is a sufficient certificate rule, not a complete minimization method.
No such contraction certificate was found for the research inputs.

These are 328 runs of sufficient-condition searches. They are not 328
distinct machines, nor an exhaustive search of all 193-state machines.
The frontier includes explicitly rejected and auxiliary tables as controls;
including them in a search does not endorse their consistency claims.
The common-action operator is adapted from the supplied PA194 work.
The fifth operator is a restricted search allowing repeated use of the
retained control when one branch already has a common action.

\paragraph{Stronger local invariants.}
The default masks are an abstract closure of the blank input, with each
newly exposed exterior bit left arbitrary. For four primary designs and
five validated rotations, stronger masks are extracted from every
universal primitive source contract and the complete blank startup.
The register-file prefix language is used instead of treating its cells as
independent bits. The construction also includes every possible interior
window of a skipped symbolic self-loop, by independently overapproximating
its consumed and remaining run lengths. The 23 arithmetic-engine controls
retain their conservative default masks. All nine tables preserve the
46 imported arithmetic-engine rows exactly.

The radius-eight masks contain 72,935 windows for PA194, 74,314 for H6,
77,302 for QH6, and 76,027 for QNS. Their respective symbolic source checks
cover 4,780, 4,876, 4,876, and 4,751 outcomes. These are universal symbolic
checks relative to the inherited arithmetic-engine lemmas, not long
concrete traces and not newly compiled proof-assistant theorems.

\paragraph{Rotations and a useful failure distinction.}
Exactly five of the 23 source-graph-preserving rotations also pass all
4,780 symbolic source outcomes and the complete startup check:
\texttt{100\_174\_right}, \texttt{2061\_2062\_left},
\texttt{2011\_2012\_left}, \texttt{2009\_2010\_right}, and
\texttt{2008\_2009\_right}.
The other eighteen are not certified by that checker. This alone is not
a literal blank-run counterexample. Detailed diagnostics of eight
outside-emitter rotations encounter unsupported symbolic steps; several
already have a register effect different from the required one. Thus a
preserved source control-flow graph and a decoder correspondence do not
by themselves justify arbitrary changes to shared physical controls.

The portable package contains frozen tables, source masks, provenance,
search programs, positive control fixtures, and exact replay receipts.
Its negative results delimit these concrete methods and bounds only.
They supply no lower bound on the state count of a consistency machine.
