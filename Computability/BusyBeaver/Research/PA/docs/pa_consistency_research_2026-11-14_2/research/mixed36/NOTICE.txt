Upstream attribution
====================

This experiment is built on research files distributed in VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt,
snapshot 567266818aaeceb0f3a8f82debf303c8d84bddaf. The exact original archive member
paths and SHA-256 hashes of copied dependencies are in PROVENANCE.json.

The numerical calculus and PA196 source/table lineage come from James Chang's
upstream BusyBeaver/M6 research as imported and adapted in ProveIt. The compiler,
symbolic register executor and fold helpers were copied without modification from
the incoming PA194NQ packet. The C++ grammar search/scoring code was copied without
modification into upstream/nested2 from the incoming PA194QNS nested-binary
experiment. The per-profile generated screen.cpp/search.cpp files make the small
multiplier and macro-name substitutions visible in prepare.py. They retain that
upstream search algorithm; this study does not claim authorship of it.

The new work consists of the mixed-radix 3/6 proposal, its two explicit source
compilation profiles, the corresponding exact emitter audit, and the recorded
bounded experiments. The raw tables and favorable nominal scores are research
artifacts. Neither profile is asserted to be a verified consistency machine.
