\paragraph{Outcome.}
This note specifies a collision-free coding and two explicit compiler profiles.
Neither profile provides a machine with fewer than 194 states. Their saved
best grammars have optimistic physical totals of 208 and 205; simultaneous
quotient and runtime proofs have not been supplied.

Let
\[
 p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b.
\]
Define the numerical constructors
\[
 I(a,b)=3p(a,b),\quad U(d,A)=3p(A,d)+2,\qquad
 M(a,b)=6p(a,b)+1,\quad E(a,b)=6p(a,b)+4.
\]
As usual, zero is a distinguished formula and $1=M(0,0)$ is the designated
contradiction code. Encoded negation is $N(A)=I(A,1)$.

\begin{proposition}
The four constructor classes are disjoint. Apart from the designated zero,
each natural number has a unique outer constructor and unique arguments.
Every argument of a positive constructor is strictly smaller than its code.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Residues modulo three separate implication, universal quantification, and
the two atomic classes. In residue one write $n=3q+1$; an even $q$ selects
membership and an odd $q$ selects equality. The quotients then uniquely
determine the pairing arguments. This gives a primitive-recursive inverse.
For $I$ with positive code, $p(a,b)>0$ and $3p(a,b)>\max(a,b)$.
The remaining constructors have positive offsets and multipliers at least
three, so the same strict domination holds, including at zero arguments.
\end{proof}

For the original radix-four constructors $I_4,M_4,U_4,E_4$, recursively
recode every formula argument \emph{and every numerical variable label}.
Thus, for example,
\[
 F(E_4(a,b))=E(F(a),F(b)),\qquad
 F(U_4(d,A))=U(F(d),F(A)),\qquad F(0)=0.
\]
The strict domination just proved makes this course-of-values recursion
well-founded. Applying the inverse constructor decomposition gives an inverse
recoding. These functions are primitive recursive: their recursion trees have
arguments below the current natural-number input, and bounded tables of earlier
values implement the recursion by primitive recursion. Constructor compatibility
also gives $F(1)=1$. It is essential to recode variable labels; leaving them
unchanged would not preserve the equality and diagonal schemas.

This bijection is not numerically increasing: $F(3)=4$ and $F(4)=3$.
Consequently the restricted numerical rule with a side condition $u\leq a$
cannot be transported by assuming that inequality is invariant. The same
repair used in the imported radix-five and radix-six packets applies at the
logical level: first derive the unrestricted implicational rule using the
generic restricted-rule derivations of $K$ and $S$. These derivations use
domination of the implication arguments by their code, which $I=3p$ retains.
The all-natural-variable freshness argument likewise uses the constructor
subterm bound, not global monotonicity of $F$.

All original outer axiom heads are implications or universals. Their positive
codes have residues zero or two modulo three and hence cannot equal the
contradiction code $1$. The distinguished zero and one remain fixed. These
facts preserve the numerical logical interface required for the original
sum-based Shift guard and the no-push successful-MP proof compiler. They give
a route for importing the written logical calibration, not a newly compiled
proof-assistant theorem or a physical machine proof.

\paragraph{Compiler profile A: selectable sextupling.}
The old coefficient-four constructor is replaced by coefficient three. A
second constructor shares its pairing prefix and multiplies the paired result
by six. Of the 59 explicit constructor occurrences in the 190-token emitter,
31 are implications, 15 universals, ten memberships, and three equalities.
Only the last thirteen use the alternative constructor. The equality suffix
changes from three to four increments. This yields 193 macro tokens. Five
new fixed readers select and implement the second path, making 139 fixed
controls before the AX readers. The two implication constructions inside
the existing parameter-adjustment macro keep coefficient three.

\paragraph{Compiler profile B: doubling the triple.}
Instead of selecting a full second constructor, construct $3p$ and then double
it for each membership or equality. The doubling macro needs three new readers
and reuses the existing double-increment reader. There are 137 fixed controls,
but the emitter now has 206 macro tokens. This prices the selection overhead
explicitly; the shared six-increment expression alone is not a complete
constant-cost constructor switch.

The nominal matching counts include the new fixed helper readers, so an
additional helper fold is not silently omitted from the reported estimates.
The emitter audits compute in a free algebra of constructors and arbitrary
initial parameters and confirm the complete output formulas. The heuristic
searches used 180 seconds each, with 3,245,431 and 2,905,346 proposed grammar
rotations. This is a bounded negative experiment. The matching is not a
certificate for simultaneous folds, and no full source simulation or blank
initialization was attempted. The two raw tables therefore remain research
artifacts of 220 and 217 controls.
