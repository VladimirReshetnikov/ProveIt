\section{Result, scope, and standard of evidence}

The comparison identifies 33 distinct 194-control action graphs presented as
consistency constructions, eleven auxiliary graphs at that size, and five
rejected graphs. The only complete 193-control graph found in the supplied
corpus is explicitly rejected: its numerical calculus derives the designated
contradiction. The catalog and the mixed-radix experiments accompanying it do
not supply a verified machine below 194 controls.

All statements refer to ProveIt commit
\texttt{567266818aaeceb0f3a8f82debf303c8d84bddaf}. The requested PA directory
contains 55 filed research packets; the requested incoming directory adds six
archives. The ledger \packet{packet_ledger_61.json} records every packet's
contribution, status, README hash and pinned source URL. This narrative groups
the proposals by their mathematical and computational changes. It should be
read together with that exhaustive ledger and the machine-level catalog, not
as a claim that every historical search transcript has been rerun.

The state convention is a deterministic, single-tape, two-symbol machine,
starting on a blank two-way tape. The reported count includes the start control
and excludes the separate halt label. A complete transition table is only one
component of the requested result. Its source program must enumerate the
correct calculus; the physical machine must implement that source, including
initialization; and the calculus must have the required consistency calibration.
Failure at any one level invalidates a smaller-machine claim.

The distinction between written proofs and formal endpoints is especially
important here. The pinned
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/567266818aaeceb0f3a8f82debf303c8d84bddaf/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/README.md}{PA README}
states that neither machine-specific direction of the desired equivalence in
iPRA has yet been established for the primary PA194, original PA195 or PA196.
The packets report ordinary mathematical PA-provability arguments and provide
substantial executable evidence. This does not make their claims false; it
does mean that ``reported construction'' in this catalog is not shorthand for
an end-to-end Lean theorem or a new independent verification of every proof.

\section{Deduplication and the actual frontier}

tables and explicit complete transition dictionaries in JSON. The recognizers
are inherited from the incoming Q-radix comparison, retained with attribution,
and rerun on the pinned tree. Exact bytes and state-renaming classes are recorded
separately. To compare native-label and densely renumbered tables, graph
canonicalization considers all roots spanning the full action graph. This
forgets the start \emph{only for graph comparison}. The selected representative's

\section{What the larger designs contribute}

The progression is more informative than the raw state totals. The U208
packets start from the earlier 209-control lineage and save one state by a
finite-history or entry-context decoder fold. Several packets describe the
same two original graphs using different local proof presentations. The later
\packet{pa208_research_2026-10-29} adds eighteen nonisomorphic helper variants,
bringing that supported U208 group to twenty classes. These are alternate
realizations of one reduction, not twenty independent additive savings.

The 196-control lineage supplies the compact register source, literal runtime
contracts and a range of decoder geometries. Head permutations, reassociation,
register A/D exchanges, known-zero returns and controller changes create
distinct physical tables without changing the final count. The imported
upstream result includes one formal PA implication from literal nonhalting to
PA consistency; the pinned README explicitly warns that this is not the other
direction or either iPRA direction. Later packets supply written reverse
calibrations and extensive physical evidence, with varying formal scope.

The diagonal-vacuity idea is a genuine logical simplification: an instance
\(B\to U(B,B)\) is justified by the code-based freshness bound. It shortens
the old 201-item emitter to a bundled 191-item emitter (3,700 source sites),
and splitting the diagonal head out yields 190 items (3,673 sites). Early
physical allocations cost 199 or 198; later allocations reach 196, 195 and
194. The original PA195 uses 68 AX readers and seven folds. PA195DC combines
the bundled diagonal emitter with the Combined controller. Separate-head
195S and 195C use 67 readers and six folds. M195S8 is another supported
eight-fold allocation. The 22 graph classes at 195 also include rejected and
experimental candidates; the suffix ``195'' is not itself evidence of validity.

Source-size reductions do not monotonically reduce table size. A 157-node
representation of the original source can require a larger physical allocation;
an equivalent reordered witness gate reduces 201 macro items to 199 but its
best supplied table costs 198; and the direct-negation NEG18 experiment cuts
the separate source from 3,673 to 3,441 sites but costs
\(134+68-5=197\) controls. Other packets contribute logical alternatives such
as splitting the first quantifier head into modal and explosion components,
unary power-cover theories, and negative countermodels. They enrich the search
space without supplying an additional accepted state saving.

\section{The 194-control families and nonadditive savings}

Most constructions share the same broad implementation: a fixed register
runtime, an AX decoder represented by a shared binary grammar, and contextual
folds whose correctness depends on the history bit carried by the tape.
The primary accounting identity is
\[
134\text{ fixed controls}+67\text{ AX readers}-7\text{ folds}=194.
\]
The alternate separate-head grammar uses 68 readers and eight folds. Changing
the source can improve one term while worsening another, and folds that need
the same surviving control cannot automatically be added.

Two concrete interaction costs explain why apparently independent improvements
have stalled at 194. In H6, the coefficient-six multiplication uses helper
330; the attractive symmetric comparison also needs a fold using that role.

logical simplification to negative-only diagonal vacuity is sufficient in the
calculus, but the tested direct compiler profiles cost at least 200, and
positive-offset variants at least 198. Logical sufficiency alone does not
price the emitter grammar, decoder paths or physical helpers.

\section{Rejected shortcuts and verification boundaries}

The most instructive failed 193-control table is the coefficient-three
shortcut in incoming PA194NQ. It replaces the quadruple-increment body by a
triple-increment body and obtains an extra history fold. The supplied physical
checks pass: its accounting is \(134+67-8=193\), with 3,612 source sites,
4,719 primitive outcomes, 32 local-fold cases and a 58,320-step startup.
Nevertheless its old tag assignment is invalid. With
\(I_3(a,b)=3p(a,b)\) and \(E_3(a,b)=3p(a,b)+3\),
\[
E_3(1,0)=I_3(0,1)=6.
\]
The full retained calculus derives code 1. The packet supplies a compact
derived-rule contradiction and a 73,393-step primitive expansion, so the
candidate is rejected at the logical layer despite excellent physical evidence.
The separate new all-offset obstruction strengthens that diagnosis for the
fixed coefficient-three implication/membership/universal template; it is
not a lower bound on arbitrary consistency machines.

Other failures occur at different layers. The seven-zero-scanner quotient
passes its local folds but violates a source-address runtime contract. Several
known-zero changes around native control 103 are valid on selected source
entries but halt during blank initialization after 1,361 steps. Two earlier
Shift layouts halt after 43,563 steps. In the DHS append-zero transplant,
all 194 possible blank starts are classified: four halt and the other 190
enter proved blank rays within the stated bounded initialization analysis.
That rejects the exact transplant, not every conceivable replacement of
the startup algorithm.

These examples impose a practical proof order. An altered logical interface
needs syntactic consistency calibration. A source change needs an all-store
command argument, not only runs from small numerical examples. A source-tree
reassociation needs scanner coverage at every resulting address. Proposed
folds need verification on the final simultaneous quotient. A literal machine
needs its exact blank startup. Standard finite-run proofs and symbolic
contracts should then be distinguished from internalization in PA or iPRA.

\section{New mixed-radix experiment and useful remaining targets}

The accompanying new source proposal uses
\[
 I=3p,\qquad U(d,A)=3p(A,d)+2,\qquad
 M=6p+1,\qquad E=6p+4.
\]
Modulo three separates implications, universals and atoms; parity of the
remaining quotient separates membership from equality. This gives a total
disjoint hereditary coding with a primitive-recursive inverse, retaining
codes zero and one. It is different from the already supplied nested-binary
proposal in the QNS packet. The latter uses an outer binary constructor with
nested atom tags, expands the emitter to 213 tokens, and remains an experiment;
it must not be presented as a new discovery or an accepted smaller machine.

Two concrete mixed-radix compiler profiles price the selection overhead.
A separately selected sextupling constructor needs five extra fixed readers,
yielding 139 fixed controls and 193 emitter tokens. A three-reader doubling
macro after the triple constructor yields 137 fixed controls and 206 tokens.
After respectively 3,245,431 and 2,905,346 heuristic rotation proposals, the
saved best grammars have 81 and 80 AX readers and nominal matchings of twelve
folds each. Their optimistic totals are therefore 208 and 205. Exact
free-constructor audits confirm the full emitted formulas, but complete
physical simulation and startup are unproved. The raw 220- and 217-control
tables are research inputs, not asserted 208- or 205-control realizations.

The negative experiments suggest concrete targets without proving a state
floor: change the physical register or address representation, find a source
geometry that really permits a shorter scanner, or derive a logically smaller
interface whose new emitter pays for its helpers. Every claimed combination
must be costed as a complete table. The present catalog preserves both useful
designs and explicit failure witnesses so that future work can avoid counting
duplicates or repeating a logically unsound one-state saving.
