\section{A command calculus that halts at the last inference}
\label{sec:halt-at-mp}

This section gives a new command-level reduction.  Its arithmetic correctness
does not by itself assert a state count for a binary tape implementation.
The formula constructors, guarded gate, and eight generated heads are those
of PA196.  Write $a::w$ for $p(a,w)$, where $p$ is Cantor pairing and
$p(0,0)=0$.  A store is $(t,w,d,h)$.
The underlying derivation compiler and auxiliary words are taken from
\texttt{PA196/Commands/Compiler.lean}, \texttt{Words.lean}, and the selector
modules at upstream commit
\texttt{407cdc356f6b586635e71ac92be394255f4bfea4}.
The command below and its revised MP compiler clause are manuscript
constructions; they are not declarations from that Lean development.

Keep AX unchanged.  Make Shift total, with update
\[
 (t,a::w,d,h)\longmapsto(h,w,d,a).
\]
For MP$(x)$ put $b=d+x$ and use the following operation:
\[
\begin{cases}
 \text{halt},& I(t,b)=h\text{ and }b=1,\\
 (b,w,0,0),& I(t,b)=h\text{ and }b\ne1,\\
 (0,w,b,0),& I(t,b)\ne h.
\end{cases}
\tag{MP$^\dagger$}
\]
Compared with the original successful branch, the zero stack item and the
copy of the consequent in the numerical cache are both removed.  The halt
test is applied only to a successfully derived consequent.

\paragraph{Soundness.}
Retain the PA196 invariant that $t$ and $h$ are derivable formula codes and
that every stack item is derivable, including the infinite zero tail.
The total Shift preserves this invariant.  AX preserves it by its unchanged
formula contracts.  On a failed MP the two live formulas become truth; on a
successful nonhalting MP its consequent is derivable by modus ponens and the
other live formula becomes truth.  A halting MP therefore yields a finite
derivation of code $1$.  This argument constructs a derivation from the
finite command trace and needs no semantic soundness theorem.

\paragraph{Auxiliary words.}
Use the same words
\[
\begin{split}
 P&=\operatorname{AX}(0,0,0)\,S^8\,\operatorname{MP}(0),\\
 D&=\operatorname{MP}(1)\,\operatorname{AX}(1,0,0)\,S^9\,
       \operatorname{MP}(0),\\
 R&=\operatorname{AX}(0,0,0)\,S^{10}.
\end{split}
\]
Their contracts are
\[
\begin{split}
 P(t,w,d,h)&=(0,t::w,0,0),\\
 D(t,w,0,0)&=(0,w,0,0),\\
 R(t,a::w,d,h)&=(t,w,0,a).
\end{split}
\]
Every MP in $P$ and $D$ fails, so its new successful branch is irrelevant.
In $P$, the compared major has universal tag $2$, whereas implication
codes are divisible by four.  In $D$, the first comparison is
$I(t,1)=0$, which is impossible; the final comparison is
$I(U(1,0),0)=0$, also impossible.  The word $R$ has no MP at all and
is now total even when the preserved top equals $1$.
For $i>0$, MP$(i)$ from $(0,w,0,0)$ fails and sets $d=i$; for $i=0$
use the empty offset word.  Hence the original generalization word
\[
 P\,\operatorname{offset}(i)\,S\,\operatorname{AX}(i,0,0)\,S^9
\]
still maps $(a,w,0,0)$ to $(U(i,a),w,0,0)$.  The original six head
selectors, restricted-propositional selector, and gate selector use only
these words and therefore retain their exact contracts.

\paragraph{Completeness with a shorter MP clause.}
Define a compiler $C$ on finite PA196 derivations.  Retain the old axiom
selectors and generalization clause.  For $p:A$ and $q:A\to B$, set
\[
 C(\operatorname{mp}(p,q))
   =C(q)\,P\,C(p)\,P\,S^2\,\operatorname{MP}(b),
\]
where $a,b$ are the codes of $A,B$.  Starting from $(t,w,0,0)$, the
induction hypotheses either already give a halt or yield the sequence
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
 C(q);P &(0,I(a,b)::w,0,0)\\
 C(p);P &(0,a::I(a,b)::w,0,0)\\
 S^2 &(a,w,0,I(a,b))\\
 \operatorname{MP}(b)&
       \text{halt if }b=1;\quad(b,w,0,0)\text{ otherwise.}
\end{array}
\]
Thus $C$ returns the proved formula while restoring the arbitrary incoming
stack and clearing both caches, or halts earlier.  Every derivation of code
$1$ ends in MP: none of the axiom heads, guarded gates, or universal
conclusions has code $1$.  Consequently its compiled word halts.
Prepending AX$(0,0,0)$ clears arbitrary incoming caches; the compiler works
with whatever top and stack that command supplies.  This gives a universally
halting word from a refutation, exactly the property required by the cyclic
enumerator.

The compiler and the trace-to-derivation construction are primitive
recursive.  Their correctness is proved by induction on finite derivations
and finite command traces, so PA can prove their correctness after the
usual coding.  This is a manuscript proof, not an additional Lean artifact.
The exceptional numeric identity $I(0,0)=0$ causes no problem in this
argument: a successful MP with consequent $0$ returns truth and continues.
In particular, successful MP is not assumed to have a positive consequent.

\paragraph{An implementation using existing arithmetic pieces.}
At the entry to the old successful MP branch the live arithmetic registers
satisfy $T=H=X=0$, $D=b$.  The existing tree \texttt{node51} drains $D$
into $T$ and $X$, giving $T=X=b,D=0$.  The existing \texttt{node19}
drains $X$ into $H$, giving $T=H=b,X=0$.  Increment $X$ and use the
old NIP comparison on $(H,X)$: it halts exactly when $b=1$, and otherwise
clears both registers.  The remaining values are $T=b,D=H=X=0$ and the
unchanged stack.  Shift needs only to clear $T$, drain $H$ into $T$, and
decode the next stack item into $H$.  Relative tree-jump targets, root
return placement, blank startup, and universal tape-frame contracts must
all be checked for a compiled table.

The concrete reconstructed source has 3,788 instruction occurrences and
162 distinct internal tree nodes.  The pinned PA196 source has 3,812
occurrences and 159 distinct internal nodes.  Thus the change removes
24 instruction occurrences but adds three internal DAG nodes; it has not
produced a smaller binary transition table.

The reconstruction checks all outgoing control edges of the 3,762 retained
sites: old sites $11$ through $3772$ map to new sites $0$ through $3761$.
The external exits are mapped by
\[
 3773\mapsto3762,\qquad3799\mapsto3781,\qquad3800\mapsto3782.
\]
This checks the unchanged AX command, MP prelude, cyclic fetch machinery,
and root stack decoder as literal control-flow fragments.  Equality of
operations and successors gives their usual step-for-step correspondence
for every register assignment until one of the mapped boundary sites.

The new successful branch starts at site $3762$.  Its first loop has
invariant $T=X=b-D$ and decreases $D$ at each iteration; its second loop
has invariant $H+X=b$ and decreases $X$.  At the comparison entry the
values are $T=H=b$, $D=0$, $X=1$.  The retained comparator halts at site
$3780$ exactly when $b=1$.  Otherwise it clears $H,X$ and reaches fetch
site $14$.  Direct induction on those loops gives $9b+9$ source transitions
to fetch for $b\ne1$, and 15 transitions to the halt instruction for $b=1$.
The new Shift at site $3782$ first clears $T$, then transfers all of $H$
into $T$, and returns through the retained stack decoder.  Its two new
loops terminate by descent of $T$ and then $H$, respectively.  No unrelated
register is changed.  These are arithmetic proofs about the source
interpreter; the physical tape refinement remains a separate obligation.

The original literal startup is proved in
\texttt{PA196/Nonhalting.lean} to reach the old fetch boundary $25$ with
$N=2$ and all other registers zero.  The corresponding new fetch boundary
is $14$, with the same source-tree path.  A physical realization must bind
its actual startup to this boundary; the source change alone does not
establish that binding.

\paragraph{Reproduction evidence.}
The accompanying \texttt{halt\_at\_mp\_checks.py} checks the exact integer
command interpreter on 81 push cases, 25 drop cases, 243 pop cases, 27
generalization cases, 125 MP compiler clauses, and 21 positive offsets.
All 522 checks pass.  These finite checks supplement the universal proof
above; they do not certify a binary tape table or any particular state count.
The independent literal-source regression script
\texttt{halt\_at\_mp\_source\_checks.py} additionally passes 240 successful
branch tests, 2,286 complete MP tests, and 1,920 total Shift tests, comparing
all nine registers, including the unrelated frame registers.  The source
snapshot and \texttt{reconstruct\_halt\_at\_mp.py} are supplied so that
the construction and the 3,762 retained control-flow sites can be checked
without a Lean installation.  The latter requires the pinned
\texttt{PA196/Program.lean} as its input.
