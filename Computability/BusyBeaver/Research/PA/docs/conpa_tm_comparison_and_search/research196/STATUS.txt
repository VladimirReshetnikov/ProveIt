Formal theorem boundary
-----------------------
The published development supplies an internal implication from nonhalting to
Con(PA), external nonhalting under consistency, and the resulting independence
statements. A PA-internal biconditional must not be silently attributed to the
published theorem statement. The reverse direction requires a separate
primitive-recursive proof translation, as the main report discusses.
