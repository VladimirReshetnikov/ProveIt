Critical review of the inherited ordinary PA calibration
========================================================

Conclusion
----------
The supplied prose provides a credible ordinary mathematical argument for the
PA-provable consistency biconditional, with explicit uniform syntactic proof
transformations. I did not find a circular use of equality vacuity, a reflection
principle, a false fresh-variable premise, or a scheduler-store reset assumption
in the inspected argument. I checked the key dependency claims against the
actual imported Lean source, rather than treating the prose's theorem heading
as verification.

This is a review of an ordinary mathematical proof and its specified interfaces.
It is not a freshly elaborated Lean or Rocq proof of the entire biconditional,
and the complete primitive-recursive proof transformers have not been newly
implemented and kernel checked in this work. The repository's incomplete iPRA
endpoint is a distinct stronger formalization goal; it does not itself reveal
a gap in the PA-level argument. For a new physical variant preserving the exact
3673-site source, the only new mathematical obligation is the physical transport;
one need not invent or re-prove a different logical calculus.

1. Diagonal freshness is a genuine syntactic inequality
-----------------------------------------------------
For Cantor pairing p(a,b), every component is at most p(a,b). For every
nonzero constructor code the factor four and its tag put each variable label
and recursive formula child strictly below the full code. The exceptional
I(0,0)=0 decodes as truth and has no variable. Hence x_B is absent from the
formula of code B, including its bound labels. Gamma(B)=B -> U(B,B) is therefore
a valid vacuity instance. Choosing c=code(not C) or z0=code(E(a,b)) yields a
specific fresh label by elementary arithmetic; no unbounded fresh-name search
or circular statement about semantic truth is used.

The ordinary PA interpretation must rename the auxiliary bound variables in
Bit, or use de Bruijn indices. The supplied proof says this explicitly, so
freshness of source labels survives expansion of the arithmetic bit formula.

2. Classical reasoning does not secretly assume explosion
---------------------------------------------------------
An initial concern is that code 1 is the membership atom M(0,0), rather than a
primitive logical falsity symbol. Extracting conjuncts from encoded negations
would not be legitimate from a pure implication basis alone. This concern is
resolved by the actual retained heads: Logic/Deduction.lean explicitly derives
Context.explosion and explosion from h1 and the dominated implication basis.
Its doubleNegationElimination then uses this explosion derivation and Peirce.
Consequently the classical gate-extraction and double-negation steps used by
the new proof are supported by retained proof templates, independently of the
removed equality-vacuity gate.

3. Reverse rewriting does not assume the missing equality vacuity
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The crucial negative-property trick changes the old choice of separator.
For the desired reverse implication C, choose phi=not C and c=code(phi).
Pointed separation at (a,a,c) gives not Q. The second part of S, followed by
propositional weakening using phi -> not chi(a), gives
  S -> U(a, M(c,a) -> not chi(a)).
Now specialize this membership-only negative consequent at b.

The actual negativeSpecialization definition in Logic/Reverse.lean imports:
  the mirrored witness exists x_a E(b,a);
  direct membership rewriting under E(b,a);
  membership vacuity of M(c,b) and chi(b), extended through implication/negation.
It does not import equalityVacuity. The equality atom inside phi has already
been removed from the quantified consequent before this specialization.
The required freshness premises a != b,c and, for nondiagonal chi, a != d,
are exactly the cases split in the prose.

Under phi, the point condition supplies M(c,a); direct rewriting under E(a,b)
supplies M(c,b). The specialized negative consequent contradicts chi(b).
This gives phi -> (S -> not P). Generalizing this closed theorem at c and
using the available Gamma(phi) gives phi -> Q, contradicting not Q. Double
negation eliminates phi and gives C. The generalization is paid for by the
specific available diagonal vacuity, not an unrestricted vacuity assumption
on the desired equality-containing conclusion.

For a=b, the reverse implication is propositional. When the fixed parameter
label d=a, direct rewriting first reaches M(b,b), after which the already
proved diagonal chi(t)=M(t,t) case reaches M(a,a). These steps cover the
coincidence cases rather than silently assuming all labels distinct.

4. Neighborhood-binder transport also uses membership-only formulas
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Gamma(E(a,b)) supplies vacuity for E(a,b) at z0=code(E(a,b)), enough to
obtain the neighborhood implication at that one fresh binder. For an arbitrary
z distinct from a,b and z0, both neighborhood matrices P(t) contain membership
atoms only. The recovered bidirectional rewrites give
  E(z,z0) -> (P(z0) -> P(z)).
The mirrored equality witness, universal distribution and membership vacuity
of P(z) at z0 specialize U(z0,P(z0)) to P(z). The antecedent U(z0,P(z0)) is
vacuous at z by membership vacuity and quantifier commutation. Generalization
then gives U(z0,P(z0)) -> U(z,P(z)). No primitive equality-vacuity instance at
arbitrary z is assumed in this transport.

I searched the imported logical and arithmetic certificate closures for
uses of equalityVacuity. The substantive completeness uses are the old
reverseChi supplier and primitiveToNeighborhood supplier, in their respective
logical and arithmetic copies. These are exactly the interfaces replaced by
the supplied diagonal proof. The equality witness itself is retained. This
supports the claimed modular reuse of the original PA-refutation compiler.

5. Finite comprehension is a proof supplier, not reflection
-----------------------------------------------------------
For any formula syntax theta(z,parameters), PA has a proof of finite bit
comprehension by induction on n: represent the first n truth values by s<2^n,
and either retain s or add 2^n according to excluded middle on theta(n).
The induction formula contains theta as a syntactic substitution parameter.
A primitive-recursive algorithm can output this one induction instance and
the fixed logical/arithmetic proof pattern; PA verifies the output is a proof
by the syntactic definition of its induction schema and proof checker.
Nothing requires evaluating theta or asserting that all PA theorems are true.
Full PA induction is used, which is precisely why one cannot simply replace
PA by iPRA in this part of the argument.

The pointed-separation supplier handles both label cases correctly. If a!=b,
the bit-set predicate is
  (Bit(x_a,z) or z=x_b) and T(c)[z/x_b]
with bound x_a+x_b+1. This covers every bit of x_a and the distinguished
point x_b. If a=b, the antecedent under the inner binder is Bit(z,z), not
Bit(x_a,z) with x_a fixed. PA refutes Bit(z,z), and the singleton predicate
  (z=x_b) and T(c)[z/x_b]
with bound x_b+1 supplies the remaining conditions. The proof explicitly
renames inner binders when inserting the witness depends on the original
free x_b, avoiding capture.

The proof also handles both numeric aliases: T(I(0,0)) is related by a fixed
proof equivalence to truth implies truth, rather than claimed syntactically
identical; T(1)=Bit(x_0,x_0) has a PA refutation even inside a binder for label
zero. Thus neither alias produces an unproved special case in the syntactic
proof-store invariant.

6. The carried store and enumeration are accounted for
-------------------------------------------------------
The source scheduler does not reset the store between candidate words. The
soundness proof annotates both visible formula codes t,h and every live
canonical stack entry with a PA proof. It tracks arbitrary annotated stores,
so AX generalization of old h is justified, successful MP constructs a proof
of its consequent, failed MP returns truth, and Shift preserves annotations.
For original Shift, t+h=1 implies t=1 or h=1, explaining why both visible
proofs are required. The Cantor stack is not treated as a free list: p(0,0)=0,
and the canonical stack ends at the first zero tail; pushing zero onto zero
leaves the empty canonical annotation, while popping zero supplies truth.

Completeness has a different universal operational property. The actual
Commands/Compiler.lean theorem haltWord_universal begins a refutation word
with AX(0,0,0), which clears the two caches even from an arbitrary dirty store.
Its protected stack tail may remain arbitrary. The compiler either halts early
or returns the derived formula while preserving that tail. This is stronger
than compilation only from the clean initial store and exactly addresses the
carried-store problem.

For the separate-head calculus the old nine-entry selectors are not retained
unchanged: the expanded PA194 calibration supplies ten-entry identities.
I checked the shifts, pop/push/discard, selectors, generalization and MP suffix
against the stated command updates. The residue facts used are adequate:
implication codes are 0 mod 4, universal codes are 2 mod 4 and at least 2.
Thus the safe shifts cannot accidentally satisfy the original t+h=1 test;
explicit early halt in an MP suffix is allowed and useful. The ten-entry
compiled refutation word still begins with AX and halts from every store.

The supplied word code has explicit continuation pointers. Every positive
pointer strictly decreases after a command, since its projections are bounded
by l-1. Hence each candidate finishes in boundedly many commands or halts.
Fresh candidates enumerate all integers from 2 upward; a refutation word
beginning with AX has its candidate number at least 3. Induction through the
preceding candidates reaches this word or an earlier halt. This is a finite
arithmetic argument, not an assumed fairness property of an infinite run.

7. The finite physical halt argument has the necessary positive lengths
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Universal source-instruction contracts supply exact finite reachability to a
next nonhalt frame, or to halt for the source halt instruction, with strictly
positive physical duration. The arithmetic macros likewise terminate at exact
command boundaries when the next command does not halt. Given Con(PA), the
annotated store makes a halting command impossible. For any alleged physical
halting time T, iterating T+1 positive command segments and their positive
physical implementations produces a run longer than T. Determinism contradicts
the alleged halt; fixed startup is checked separately. Exact reachability to
a nonhalt endpoint already excludes a hidden premature halt inside a segment.
No inverse decoding of arbitrary intermediate tapes, unbounded search for a
future frame, or external HF-model truth assertion is required.

The other direction compiles a PA refutation through the modified calculus,
uses the universally halting command word and finite enumeration argument,
and then concatenates the source/physical traces. All the arguments use
primitive-recursive constructions and PA induction on coded finite objects.

Reporting recommendation
------------------------
For the new unchanged-source physical variants, state the strongest clean new
claim as an ordinary PA-formalizable halting-equivalence theorem with the
pinned primary PA194 machine. State that the Con(PA) biconditional follows
by composing with the supplied, explicitly retained ordinary mathematical
calibration. Explain that no newly compiled end-to-end Lean/Rocq endpoint is
being delivered. Do not describe a missing iPRA theorem as a missing PA-level
mathematical argument, and do not describe Python certificate execution alone
as the arithmetic calibration.

The proof-transformer implementation and its kernel-checked uniform correctness
remain worthwhile further work. They are a formalization boundary, not a
counterexample or circularity found by this audit.
