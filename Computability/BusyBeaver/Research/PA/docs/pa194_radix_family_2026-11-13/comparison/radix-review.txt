Verdict
-------
No mathematical blocker was found in the ordinary PA-provability argument,
subject to the stated inherited PA calibration and the delivered literal
source/physical certificates. The new endpoints are not newly kernel-checked
Lean/Rocq theorems. This distinction is correctly stated in the report.

The important nontrivial points are handled correctly:

1. Hereditary recoding acts simultaneously on formula codes and numerical
   variable labels. This preserves Gamma(B)=I(B,U(B,B)) exactly. A recoding
   that leaves the labels unchanged would not establish this claim.

2. The recoding is injective but is not monotone. For example,
   F_5(40)=75>55=F_5(44). The report explicitly rejects order preservation.
   The generic finite rho8All derivation repairs the translated dominated
   axiom leaves using only reflexivity and propagation of bounds into either
   argument of implication. The bounds hold for I_k(a,b)=k*p(a,b).

3. The target derivation is freshly compiled to a target command word.
   The proof does not map old offsets or assert that F preserves ordinary
   addition, subtraction, stacks, or scheduler trajectories. Every actual
   restricted leaf of the expanded target proof has its own valid numerical
   bound, so its target offset D-u is legitimate.

4. Unused residues at radices five and six receive the total interpretation
   truth. This is an interpretation of arbitrary numerical formula inputs,
   not an extra source axiom making every such number derivable. The direct
   PA soundness argument covers them.

5. Component bounds still prove that a formula's own code is a fresh source
   variable label. The zero implication alias remains explicit. The guarded
   separation supplier uses the same capture-aware finite-comprehension
   argument and the distinct/same-label split as the inherited proof.

6. Ten-entry AX selectors and safe Shift identities use only tag separation,
   universal-code positivity, and implication-code positivity. They work
   for every k>=4. The scheduler and ordinary stack pairing remain unchanged.
   Universal halting of a compiled refutation word handles carried stores.

7. The Cons multiplier change needs a separate arithmetic-macro argument,
   in addition to primitive physical contracts. Its loop invariant is
   T+kY=T0+kY0, with exact source duration (k+2)*Y0+1. The source check binds
   precisely 61 multiplier occurrences and their surrounding Cons prefixes;
   3429 outside source sites and successor edges are preserved under ordinal
   relocation. MP uses the same Cons constructor.

8. Both formula registers and canonical stack entries are annotated in the
   sound-store invariant. Positive physical durations permit iteration past
   an alleged halting time. The checked startup is needed separately.
