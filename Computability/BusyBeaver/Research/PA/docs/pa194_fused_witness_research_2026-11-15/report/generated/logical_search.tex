\subsection{Logic and grammar searches}
\label{sec:fw-search}

The symmetric-witness incoming packet reassociated fixed source words;
its dormant per-head parameter-renaming mechanism had not been used.
We enabled simultaneous grammar moves and independent permutations of
the three ordinary parameters in each of the six original head blocks.
The guarded gate and restricted-$\rho$ block were held fixed.  Every
such permutation is a bijection on its own schema parameters, so the
same axiom family is generated and the inverse permutation recovers any
desired instance.  This is a semantic justification for the search
space, not a substitute for exact source binding of a selected result.

The search corrected the macro-length accounting for the two- and
four-increment macros before locating head boundaries.  Fixed controller
cost after its verified fold is 134 states.  The objective below is
the number of distinct reader states minus the maximum compatible
history-marker identifications; a value of 60 gives 194 total states.
Simultaneous fold compatibility, rather than a bare matching count, is
checked for competitive candidates.

The three exhaustive grids therefore evaluated 4,339,008
shape--parameter combinations.  Each grid retained 31 best-cost hits,
all with the identity parameter tuple.  The stochastic searches ran
11,172,418 iterations in total.  Iterations are not a count of distinct
machines: revisits, unsuccessful moves and proposals rejected by
structural penalties occur.  The timing-dependent iteration totals are
recorded evidence; rerunning the same seed for the same wall-clock time
need not reproduce those exact totals on different hardware.  The
selected PA194FW grammar and fold list are frozen independently of the
search and reconstruct deterministically.

For the fused grid, the two original constituent blocks may be permuted
independently.  A resulting head has the form
$N(I(h_4(\sigma(D,A,H)),V(\tau(D,A,H))))$.  Its join follows from the
two original axioms, while each projection is surjective onto its own
original family by choosing the inverse of its own permutation.  The
other constituent does not constrain that projection.  The delivered
machine uses both identity permutations, so its exact formula is the
simpler (FW-F).  The proof and free-term certificate are for that exact
selected machine.

The fused-H grammar search produced the new 194-state result.  None of
these searches produced a valid table below 194 states.  The grids are
exhaustive only over their finite retained shape sets, and the annealing
runs have no completeness guarantee.  They do not prove optimality of
the macro vocabulary, the grammar, the axioms, the controller or the
global 194-state bound.  An exploratory fusion of the J first-head
family was rejected as an accepted result: the projection argument here
uses explosion derived from the retained ordinary $h_1$, whereas the
J witness design had obtained that resource differently.  No logical
equivalence is claimed for that unverified branch.

frozen table, not arbitrary screened candidates, has the complete
source, literal-runtime and mathematical preservation certificates.
