\subsection{A relative iPRA proof for the three composites}
\label{sec:symmetric-mf}

Let $P$ be one of the literal parent tables
\texttt{PA194H5XS}, \texttt{PA194JH5XS}, and \texttt{PA194FW}, with native initial state $74$
and terminal state $254$.  Let $A$ be the accelerated intermediate table
and $C$ the corresponding delivered \texttt{\_MF} child.  All three use
one two-way binary tape, initially zero, and initial head position zero.
The terminal state is given an absorbing arithmetic transition only in
our total run function; it is not counted as a working control and is
not an extra physical transition in the delivered table.

\paragraph{The statement.}
Write $H_M(t)$ for the primitive-recursive statement that the total run
of $M$ is in its terminal state after $t$ transitions.  The exact tables
satisfy
\[
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash H_P(t)\longrightarrow H_C(5t),
 \qquad
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash H_C(t)\longrightarrow H_P(3t).
 \tag{SMF}
\]
In particular, iPRA proves positive blank-halting equivalence and its
intuitionistically valid negated consequence
$\neg\exists t\,H_P(t)\leftrightarrow\neg\exists t\,H_C(t)$.
This relative theorem does not assume the consistency of PA.
Its absolute PA-consistency consequence uses the exact supplied written
calibration of $P$, as described separately below.

\paragraph{Finite representation.}
A finite-support tape, a head position and a control are encoded by
natural numbers using two binary stacks, or any fixed primitive-recursive
equivalent encoding.  Tape-bit lookup, one-cell update, one-cell motion,
finite control maps and iteration of a fixed transition table are primitive
recursive.  Every local identity below preserves the entire tape: cells
outside the displayed finite interval are untouched.  In a numerical
encoding this is an equality with arbitrary unchanged outer stack parts,
not an equality tested only on finitely many complete configurations.

\paragraph{An inductive guard.}
At a configuration $(q,h,T)$ write $b_i=T(h+i)$, and let $B$ be the
following quantifier-free Boolean guard:
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
 q=101&\Longrightarrow& b_{-1}=0,\\
 q=11 &\Longrightarrow& (b_{-1}=0\ \lor\ b_1=0),\\
 q=26\ \land\ b_0=1&\Longrightarrow& b_1=b_2=0,\\
 q=301&\Longrightarrow& b_{-1}=0,\\
 q=88&\Longrightarrow& b_{-1}=1.
\end{array}
\]
The displayed disjunction can equivalently be written as the Boolean
identity $b_{-1}b_1=0$.  Thus the induction needs only a quantifier-free
arithmetic formula.  The guard holds at the blank start, and it is
preserved by every row of both $P$ and $A$.

The supplied independent checker verifies preservation for each of the
194 controls and all $2^7$ assignments on offsets $-3,\ldots,3$:
24,832 windows per table.  Radius three is sufficient because $B$ inspects
radius at most two and a transition moves the head by one cell.  Hence
this finite truth table exhausts all configurations with arbitrary exterior
bits; its complete case distinction is a finite proof of the universal
one-step implication.  Quantifier-free induction establishes $B$ on all
blank runs of $P$ and $A$.  This argument uses no inference from a long
finite execution.

\paragraph{Acceleration.}
Only the following two rows change from $P$ to $A$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
 (q,b)&P(q,b)&A(q,b)\\\hline
 (26,1)&(1,R,42)&(1,R,91)\\
 (101,0)&(0,L,26)&(0,R,17).
\end{array}
\]
For the first row, $B$ gives two zero cells immediately to the right.
The old path is
\[
 (26,1)\to(42,0)\to(13,0)\to91;
\]
it writes the same complete tape and ends at the same head position as
one new transition.  For the second row, $B$ gives a zero immediately to
the left.  The old path is
\[
 (101,0)\to(26,0)\to(194,0)\to17;
\]
again three old transitions equal one new transition on the complete tape.
Neither path enters terminal control $254$ internally.  The checker
exhausts all 12 relevant windows on offsets $-2,\ldots,2$.
All other rows of $P$ and $A$ agree.

Let $\alpha(0)=0$ and define $\alpha(n+1)-\alpha(n)$ to be three
when the $n$th $A$ configuration uses either special row, and one otherwise.
At terminal configurations take the increment to be one.
The clock and the machine runs are primitive recursive.  Quantifier-free
induction on the complete-configuration equality gives
\[
 P^{\alpha(n)}=A^n,
 \qquad n\le\alpha(n)\le3n.
\]
Using absorption at halt, without inverting an unbounded clock, this yields
\[
 H_P(t)\to H_A(t),\qquad H_A(t)\to H_P(3t).
 \tag{A}
\]
Indeed $P^t$ terminal implies $P^{\alpha(t)}$ terminal because
$\alpha(t)\ge t$, and the converse bound uses $\alpha(t)\le3t$.

\paragraph{Marker and reader separation.}
Define the finite control map
\[
 f(51)=400,\qquad f(301)=88,\qquad f(q)=q\quad\hbox{otherwise}.
\]
Rename the old marker $51$ to the fresh control $400$, remove reader
$301$, and reuse label $51$ as a history selector.  Ordinary rows are
conjugated by $f$.  At the exceptional controls the child rows are
\begin{align*}
 C(88,0)&=(0,L,51), & C(51,0)&=(0,R,122),\\
 C(51,1)&=(1,R,51), & C(88,1)&=(1,R,103).
\end{align*}
The exact predecessor rows of $A$ establish the two reader histories:
every edge into $301$ writes zero and moves right, and every edge into
$88$ writes one and moves right.  Neither is the initial control.  These
facts are also included in $B$.

For $q\notin\{301,88\}$, one child step from $f(q)$ is the $f$-image
of one $A$ step.  There are 384 such ordinary rows.  For the two reader
controls there are precisely four history/read cases:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
 A\hbox{ control}&\hbox{left bit}&\hbox{read bit}&
 \hbox{child steps per old step}\\\hline
301&0&0&5\\
301&0&1&1\\
88&1&0&3\\
88&1&1&1.
\end{array}
\]
The child reads only the old head cell and its left neighbor.  In the
five-step case its controls are $88,51,122,137,30$, ending at $45$;
in the three-step case they are $88,51,51$, ending at $122$.
The other two cases both end at $103$ in one step.  Every case restores
the history bit, writes exactly the old output bit, has exactly the old
net head motion and enters the mapped successor.  There is no internal
terminal control.  These four complete-tape identities are checked
literally, independently of the constructor.

Let $\beta(n)$ be the sum of these positive durations over the first
$n$ transitions of $A$, with duration one for ordinary or terminal
transitions.  The simulation equality and bounds are
\[
 C^{\beta(n)}=f(A^n),\qquad n\le\beta(n)\le5n.
\]
Quantifier-free induction proves this statement using the inductive guard
on $A$.  The map $f$ sends precisely terminal state $254$ to $254$.
Consequently
\[
 H_A(t)\to H_C(5t),\qquad H_C(t)\to H_A(t).
 \tag{M}
\]
For the reverse implication, $C^t$ terminal entails
$C^{\beta(t)}$ terminal by absorption and $\beta(t)\ge t$;
the simulation equality then forces $A^t$ terminal.  In particular there
is no need to assume an informal inverse clock or to enumerate a least
halting time.  Composing (A) and (M) proves (SMF).

\paragraph{Counting and blank initialization.}
All three parents have 194 working controls.  Acceleration changes no controls.
The separation removes $301$ and introduces $400$; $51$ is reused, so the
child still has 194 controls.  The supplied dense/native bijection maps
native $74$ to dense $0$, native $254$ to dense $194$, and the 194 working
controls bijectively to $0,\ldots,193$.  There are exactly 388 physical
rows and no auxiliary input symbols, registers, tapes or uncounted states.

All three literal blank runs reach the complete inherited source-entry frame at
native state $84$, head $-2$, after 57,812 child transitions.  The parent
frame is reached after 57,768 transitions.  The finite check reconstructs
all one-cells of the frame independently, including the primary physical
register values $(0,0,0,0,0,0,2,0,0)$ and 26 spare registers of value one.
An additional 10,000 accelerated-parent steps are synchronized on the
complete tape.  That interval contains ten uses of the $26/1$ shortcut
and one use of the removed-reader $301/0$ case.  These facts establish
that both changes affect recurring execution, while the unbounded
correctness statement comes from the preceding induction.

\paragraph{Absolute proof dependency.}
The new theorem proved directly here is (SMF) for the three exact
parents (two pinned upstream, one new in this work).  For the first two parents, the supplied symmetric-witness packet gives a written iPRA
halting equivalence of each parent to its H5X/JH5X parent.  Its retained
witness translations and revised AX11H manuscript provide the stated
absolute calibration to the mapped current Foundation PA-refutation
predicate, named
\texttt{DiophantineConsistency.IPRAPA196Consistency.nativePAConsistency}.
Composing those exact written dependencies with (SMF) yields the same
written absolute conclusion for the new children.  No new end-to-end
Lean or Rocq proof of that calibration is asserted.  The entire supplied
parent archive is retained byte-for-byte, so the dependency is reviewable
rather than replaced by a finite execution claim.  For \texttt{PA194FW},
its new source-level fusion argument and complete physical simulation in
the main construction supply the parent calibration; the present relative
proof is unchanged and contributes no further source-calculus assumption.
