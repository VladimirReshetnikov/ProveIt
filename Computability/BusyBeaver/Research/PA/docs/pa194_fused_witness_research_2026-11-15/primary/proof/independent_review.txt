Result and scope
----------------
No mathematical defect was found in the new relative preservation
argument, its AX10 command interface, its proof-to-word compiler, or its
use of the inherited calibration.  This is an independent mathematical
review and a separate check of the serialized finite formula proofs; it
is not an end-to-end Lean verification.  It does not independently
reprove the inherited absolute calibration of Foundation PA in iPRA.
That earlier written theorem and the general physical sweep/clock
lemmas remain explicitly named dependencies.

Final proofreading clarified that the STEP INCREMENT of the cumulative
physical clock is zero at the absorbing halt and positive at each live

This correction accurately states the existing proof; no mathematical

1. Literal fusion and noncircular proof basis
---------------------------------------------
The actual new axiom is F = N(I(h4,V)), where
V = U(D,N(E(H,D))) and the ordinary fifth axiom is N(V).  It is not
the superficially similar abbreviation C(h4,h5), which would insert
another double negation.  The supplied proof uses the literal term.

The three fixed certificates prove
 A -> (N(B) -> N(I(A,B))),
 N(I(A,B)) -> A,
 N(I(A,B)) -> N(B).
An independently written checker, which does not import the certificate
generator, checked all 419 serialized proof lines: 161 for fusion,
205 for the left projection, and 53 for the right projection.  It checks
the exact K/S/Peirce/h1 schemas and every earlier-premise MP index and
formula equation.  The attached fusion_audit.json records the result.

The use of explosion is justified by the retained ordinary h1:
put Y = U(0,N(U(0,A))); h1(0,A) is (Y -> 1) -> A, while K gives
1 -> (Y -> 1).  Deduction therefore yields 1 -> A.  The left projection
then uses Peirce.  The pinned RestrictedImplication.lean derivations
of K, S and Peirce use only the restricted rho row, MP, and constructor
bound facts.  They do not use h4, h5, the fused axiom, or a quantifier
axiom.  The numerical constructor dominates both arguments even at its
zero alias.  Thus the new proof does not assume what it is proving.
Object-calculus Peirce does not require excluded middle in iPRA.

Every old h4 instance is recovered by its corresponding left projection;
every old h5(D,H) is recovered using the right projection with A=0.
Conversely h4 and h5 derive the fused axiom.  Fixed proof substitution
and proof-list concatenation give primitive recursive transformations;
a bounded Boolean prefix/index-map invariant suffices for QF induction.

2. Exact ten-entry interface and word compiler
----------------------------------------------
The new source emits U(A,h), with explicit command binder A and incoming
cache h.  This differs from the calibrated AX11H entry U(d,h).  The
written proof now states this change explicitly and proves a new Gen
word; it does not infer command-stream identity from the fusion alone.

The source and command checkers were inspected for all selector entries,
residue-zero q0 through q7, residue-two q8, the guard, the restricted
rho bound u <= D, and the fourth-field MP fallthrough.  The Push, Pop,
Discard, Offset, eight selector words, restricted-row selector, and Gen
word have the claimed interfaces.  The MP compiler correctly reserves a
zero stack cell before evaluating its two premises.  Its early halts
when the minor or conclusion is 1 are valid refutation outcomes.
Generalization of 1 does not halt prematurely: AX consumes that cache
before another Shift tests it.  The final refutation suffix uses Pop,
which tests the saved top; plain Shift would not suffice.

All arguments retain total Cantor projections and p(0,0)=0.  They do
not assume a freely injective list syntax or that all implication codes
are nonzero.  The residue facts used to exclude 1 are valid for every
natural substitution.  The initial AX normalizes arbitrary stores, so
the refutation suffix works without resetting stores between candidates.

3. Weak arithmetic, scheduling, and transport
---------------------------------------------
The proof explicitly avoids induction on an unbounded universal
quantifier over base stacks.  Its primitive recursive majorant Phi_K,
word-length bound L, and decreasing bounds
 B(j) = Phi_K^[L(N-1-j)](s0)
allow every call to an earlier compiled word to use a previously proved
bounded domain.  The conjunction over earlier proof lines and bounded
store codes is a primitive recursive Boolean predicate.  The parameter
s0 is generalized only after QF induction.  This supplies the required
uniform compiler invariant.

The command encoding, residual decrease, initial fresh seed 2, and
explicit padded candidate N_e >= 3 are consistent with the interface.
The square bound (N_e+2)^2 safely dominates the candidate-draining sum.
The command soundness invariant supplies proofs of the top, cache, and
canonical stack entries, including virtual zeros at stack code zero.
It gives a positive numerical-refutation equivalence, not merely a
metatheoretic consistency argument.

The source-clock formulas agree with the retained template lemmas.
Their exact composition is primitive recursive.  The physical transport
uses the inherited arbitrary-gap, arbitrary-finite-spare-list sweep
lemmas and positive live durations, with new finite bindings explicitly
identified.  This audit did not duplicate the completed full physical
replay.  The general positive-clock converse uses finite decidable halt
flags, not an unbounded minimization or a classical existence principle.

4. Final calibration and limitations
-------------------------------------
The final chain is
 Halt(PA194FW) <-> existence of a CF numerical refutation
               <-> existence of a C4 numerical refutation
               <-> Halt(PA194AX11H),
followed by the inherited written equivalence between Con(PA) and the
negation of AX11H halting.  Intuitionistic logic transports negation
across the positive halting equivalence.  No Markov principle,
PA-consistency assumption, or double-negation elimination of an
existential is used.  In particular the proof does not claim an
unsupported positive iPRA equivalence between numerical refutations and
Foundation PA refutations.

The result is a materially changed, explicitly specified 194-state

bindings.  This review establishes no smaller-state machine, no state
lower bound, and no completed kernel formalization.
