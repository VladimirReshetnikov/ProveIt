\begin{abstract}
No valid machine with fewer than 194 working states was established in this investigation.
The requested same-size alternative is supplied by \textbf{PA194QN5}, with
\textbf{PA194QN6} as a companion. Each is a deterministic, binary, single-tape
machine, started on an entirely blank two-way tape, with 194 working states and
388 transitions. The new constructions combine the exchanged quantifier-distribution
axiom, no-push modus ponens, the original sum-based Shift guard, and formula
coefficients five and six. Their component methods are inherited; their exact
simultaneous constructions and verification are the contribution here.

The comparison covers 61 filed research packets and the five current incoming
archives at the pinned repository revision. It distinguishes duplicate tables,
different semantic designs, unsuccessful experiments, and verification status.
The frozen comparison corpus includes 76 spanning 194-state action graphs.
Neither delivered table is equivalent to any of them under state renaming,
choice of a spanning start, reflection, or global symbol exchange, even after
discarding the write/move effects of terminal transitions.

The construction checker and an independent literal-tape checker verify the
complete source interfaces. The latter checks 4,816 outcomes for QN5 and 4,877
for QN6, with arbitrary natural register values, and reconstructs its four list
lemmas from actual table rows. Both exact blank startups take 58,320 transitions.
The ordinary mathematical PA calibration composes the attributed numerical-calculus
calibration with explicit proof-word compilation and positive-duration tape
simulations. An end-to-end Lean or Rocq proof, and an internal iPRA endpoint,
are not claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, conventions, and the exact claim}

The principal file is \file{machines/PA194QN5/machine.tm}; the companion is
\file{machines/PA194QN6/machine.tm}. Their working controls are
$0,\ldots,193$, their initial control is $0$, and their sole terminal halt
label is $194$. Every transition writes one bit and moves one cell left or
right. The head starts at coordinate zero and every tape cell initially contains
zero. The halt label has no outgoing transitions and is excluded from the
state count. No nonblank seed, additional symbol, additional tape, stationary
move, or altered counting convention is used.

For $k=5,6$, write $\QN{k}$ for the corresponding literal table. The claimed
ordinary mathematical theorem, with its inherited proof dependencies made explicit
in Sections~\ref{sec:calculus}--\ref{sec:verification}, is
\[
 \boxed{\quad \PA\vdash
   \bigl(\neg\Halt(\QN{k},\mathrm{blank})\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
   \quad}
\]
Here $\Con(\PA)$ is syntactic consistency, expressed as the absence of a finite
PA proof of $0=1$. This is not an unconditional proof of actual nonhalting.
Actual nonhalting follows if PA is consistent. The equivalence itself does not
take consistency as an extra assumption inside PA.

\section{The numerical calculus and its PA calibration}
\label{sec:calculus}

\subsection{Syntax and all axiom families}

For a fixed $k\ge4$, let
\[
 p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b,
\]
\[
 I_k(A,B)=kp(A,B),\quad M_k(a,b)=kp(a,b)+1,
 \quad U_k(a,B)=kp(B,a)+2,\quad E_k(a,b)=kp(a,b)+3.
\]
Zero denotes truth and $1=M_k(0,0)$ denotes contradiction. Implication is $I_k$,
negation is $N_k(B)=I_k(B,1)$, conjunction is
$N_k(I_k(A,N_k(B)))$, and $\exists_k x_a B=N_k(U_k(a,N_k(B)))$.
The reversed argument order in $U_k$ is deliberate. Numerical codes with
residue $4,\ldots,k-1$ have the total arithmetic interpretation truth.

In the following displays, arrows and classical abbreviations mean these
numerical constructors. The six retained heads, in emission order, are
\begin{align*}
 h_1(D,A,H)&=(\exists_k x_D\,U_k(D,H))\to H,\\
 h_2(D,A,H)&=U_k(D,U_k(A,H))\to U_k(A,U_k(D,H)),\\
 h_3(D,A,H)&=M_k(H,D)\to(E_k(H,A)\to M_k(A,D)),\\
 h_4(D,A,H)&=E_k(H,D)\to(M_k(A,H)\to M_k(A,D)),\\
 h_5(D,A,H)&=\exists_k x_D E_k(H,D),\\
 h_6^Q(D,A,H)&=U_k(D,I_k(A,H))\to(U_k(D,A)\to U_k(D,H)).
\end{align*}
There is also the dominated propositional head
\[
 \rho_k(D,A,H,u)=((H\to u)\to D)\to((D\to H)\to(A\to H)),
 \qquad u\le D,
\]
and the diagonal head $\Gamma_k(B)=B\to U_k(B,B)$.
For the guarded separation head define
\begin{align*}
 V(a,b,c)&=M_k(a,b)\to U_k(c,M_k(a,b)),\\
 P(a,b,c)&=c\to M_k(c,b),\\
 S(a,b,c)&=U_k\bigl(b,(M_k(a,b)\to P(a,b,c))
                         \wedge_k(M_k(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
 Q(a,b,c)&=U_k(c,S(a,b,c)\to N_k(P(a,b,c))),\\
 G(a,b,c)&=N_k(V(a,b,c)\to Q(a,b,c)).
\end{align*}
$G(a,b,c)$ is an axiom when $c\ne a,b$; a failed guard emits truth.
The rules are modus ponens and generalization $B\mapsto U_k(i,B)$ of a theorem
with no undischarged hypotheses. These declarations, together with truth,
specify the numerical calculus $\mathcal C_k^Q$.

\subsection{Why changing the coefficient requires more than changing a numeral}

For a positive constructor code, every decoded component is smaller than the
code. Induction therefore proves that every variable label occurring in a
formula code $B$ is strictly smaller than $B$. The diagonal label $B$ is fresh
for that formula, which validates $\Gamma_k(B)$ under arithmetic translation.
The alias $I_k(0,0)=0$ is retained and handled separately; truth and this
implication instance are not assumed to be syntactically distinct.

The hereditary map from coefficient four to coefficient $k$ is
\[
 F_k(0)=0,\qquad
 F_k(4p(a,b)+r)=kp(F_k(a),F_k(b))+r\quad(n>0, 0\le r<4).
\]
Course-of-values primitive recursion implements the map. It acts on variable
labels as well as formula codes, so it preserves binding and the exact diagonal
identity
\[
 F_k(\Gamma_4(B))=\Gamma_k(F_k(B)).
\]
An analogous partial inverse $J_k$ decodes residues below four and returns zero
at the unused residues; induction gives $J_k(F_k(n))=n$. Thus $F_k$ is injective
and preserves the gate's disequality guards. It is not asserted that $J_k$
translates arbitrary coefficient-$k$ proofs back to coefficient four.

Numerical order need not be preserved. For instance $40<44$ while
$F_5(40)=75>55=F_5(44)$. A restricted $\rho$ leaf cannot simply be relabeled.
The supplied \texttt{rho8All} template derives the unrestricted propositional
schema from its dominated version using modus ponens and the bounds
$A,B\le I_k(A,B)$. Replace every such recoded leaf by this fixed finite template.
Induction on the coded proof then gives a PA-verifiable transformation of
coefficient-four refutations to coefficient-$k$ refutations \cite{radix}.

For the reverse consistency reduction, use a total \emph{syntactic} translation
to PA: $M_k(a,b)$ becomes finite binary membership
$\operatorname{Bit}(x_a,x_b)$, $E_k(a,b)$ becomes equality, $U_k$ becomes universal
quantification, unused residues become truth, and $I_k$ becomes implication
with the zero alias explicitly respected. PA has proof-producing templates
for the heads and for finite binary comprehension. They produce PA proofs,
rather than a uniform truth predicate for arbitrary arithmetic formulas.
The original separation calibration deals separately with coincident labels
and uses the code-fresh diagonal label. These are substantial inherited
ingredients, reproduced in \file{references/calibration/} \cite{primary,radix}.

\subsection{The exchanged head}

The unrestricted propositional basis derives $K$ and $S$. The standard bracket
abstraction of $\lambda f\,b\,a.\,f\,a\,b$ gives the closed schema
\[
 (A\to(B\to C))\to(B\to(A\to C)).
\]
The supplied checked derivation has 61 proof-DAG nodes. Instantiating it converts
the old distribution head into $h_6^Q$, and exchanging the two premises again
gives the reverse conversion. The other axiom families and inference rules
are unchanged. These finite proof transformations preserve code one and are
primitive recursive \cite{q}.

Consequently the inherited PA calibration applies to $\mathcal C_k^Q$ for
$k=5,6$. This use of prior mathematics is explicit: the present package does
not replace the PA-refutation compiler or separation proof by a finite machine
test. The newly checked part connects the exact composed source and binary
tables to this calculus.

\section{A proof-word compiler for the composed source}
\label{sec:compiler}

\subsection{Exact commands, including collapsed zero stacks}

Write $[a;w]=p(a,w)$, with total Cantor projections. In particular,
$[0;0]=0$ and $\operatorname{hd}(0)=\operatorname{tl}(0)=0$. A command store is
$(t,w,d,h)$: visible formula, theorem stack, operand offset, and cached formula.
For $b=d+x$, the new command is
\[
 \mathsf{MP}_x(t,w,d,h)=
 \begin{cases}(b,w,b,0),&I_k(t,b)=h,\\(0,w,b,0),&I_k(t,b)\ne h.\end{cases}
\]
The Shift command $\mathsf S$ halts if $t+h=1$ and otherwise returns
$(h,\operatorname{tl}(w),d,\operatorname{hd}(w))$.

For $D=d+x$ and $H=h+y$, the AX command returns
\[
 \mathsf{AX}_{A,x,y}(t,w,d,h)
   =(g(H,D,A),[q_1,\ldots,q_9,t;w],0,0),
\]
where $q_1=\Gamma_k(A)$, $q_2,\ldots,q_7$ are the six heads in reverse
emission order (including $h_6^Q$), $q_8=\rho_k(D,A,H,d)$,
and $q_9=U_k(d,h)$. The guarded $g$ is the displayed separation gate or zero.
The inequality for $\rho_k$ holds because $d\le d+x$. Every generated
implicational head and the gate have residue zero; $q_9$ has residue two and
is at least two. Thus none is code one, for all incoming natural stores,
without assuming consistency.

\subsection{Uniform stack operations}

Put $A_0=\mathsf{AX}_{0,0,0}$ and define
\[
 \mathsf{Push}=A_0;\mathsf S^9;\mathsf{MP}_0,
 \quad \mathsf{Pop}=A_0;\mathsf S^{11},
 \quad \mathsf{Discard}=\mathsf{MP}_0,
 \quad \mathsf{Offset}_i=\mathsf{MP}_i.
\]
Direct substitution into the command definitions gives
\begin{align*}
 \mathsf{Push}(t,w,d,h)&=(0,[t;w],0,0),\\
 \mathsf{Pop}(t,[a;w],d,h)&=(t,w,0,a),\\
 \mathsf{Discard}(t,w,0,h)&=(0,w,0,0),\\
 \mathsf{Offset}_i(0,w,0,0)&=(0,w,i,0).
\end{align*}
For Push, the final MP comparison has different residues, zero and two, so
it fails with offset zero. The last Shift in Pop tests $U_k(d,h)+t\ge2$,
so Pop preserves even a visible contradiction. Discard has identical success
and failure outputs when $d=x=0$. These identities are literal numerical
identities, including collapsed zero stacks.

From $(t,w,0,0)$, a selector for $q_j$ ($1\le j\le7$) is
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{AX}_{A,D,H};\mathsf S^{j+1};\mathsf{Pop}^{9-j}.
\]
A dominated head with $u\le a$ uses
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Offset}_u;
 \mathsf{AX}_{b,a-u,c};\mathsf S^9;\mathsf{Pop}.
\]
A permitted gate uses
$\mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{AX}_{A,D,H};\mathsf{Pop}^{10}$.
Generalization from visible theorem $a$ is compiled by
\[
 \mathsf{Gen}_i=\mathsf{Push};\mathsf{Offset}_i;\mathsf S;
                   \mathsf{AX}_{i,0,0};\mathsf S^{10},
\]
which returns $(U_k(i,a),w,0,0)$. These are the no-push compiler words;
reusing the old dummy-push cleanup word would be incorrect \cite{nopush}.

\subsection{Modus ponens and completeness from every store}

Suppose recursively compiled premise proofs return $a$ and $I_k(a,b)$, with
the surrounding stack preserved and the offset/cache cleared, or halt earlier.
Compile their MP step as
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Push};C(I_k(a,b));\mathsf{Push};
 C(a);\mathsf{Push};\\
 &\mathsf S;\mathsf S;\mathsf{MP}_b;\mathsf{Pop}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The first two commands reserve a zero below both premises. If neither recursive
call halted, the store before the two Shifts is
$(0,[a,I_k(a,b),0;w],0,0)$. The second Shift halts if $a=1$; otherwise MP
returns $(b,[0;w],b,0)$, and Pop returns $(b,w,0,0)$. The compiler supplies
and consumes the spare zero; successful MP itself no longer pushes it.

Induction on a finite derivation $\pi$ proves that $C(\pi)$, from any
$(t,w,0,0)$, either halts or returns its conclusion with the surrounding stack
unchanged. Therefore $A_0;C(\pi);\mathsf S$ halts from \emph{every} incoming
store when $\pi$ concludes one. This all-store property is necessary because
the scheduler carries its store between trials.

The scheduler uses Cantor-coded finite command words. Positive continuations
strictly decrease until a fresh candidate is selected; the fresh counter then
increases. Prefixing the uniformly halting word by $\mathsf{AX}_{j,0,0}$ gives
an unbounded family of fresh candidate codes
$p(j+2,p(0,p(0,z)))$, with $z$ the positive continuation encoding of the
remaining word. Consequently the initial fresh counter value two does not omit
all witnesses to any refutation. Primitive recursion constructs the finite
execution witness and PA verifies its correctness by induction.

\subsection{Soundness as a proof invariant}

Annotate $t,h$, and every live canonical stack entry with a derivation in
$\mathcal C_k^Q$, or with its PA proof under the syntactic interpretation.
AX adds valid heads, a generalization of the old cache, and the saved old top.
A successful MP uses the two annotated premises; failure returns truth. Shift
transports annotations. A zero stack supplies the fixed truth derivation when
popped. If Shift halts, $t+h=1$ forces one of the two annotated codes to equal
one, yielding a PA refutation.

Combining this invariant with the all-store compiler and the inherited
calculus calibration gives both syntactic reductions
\[
 \PA\vdash \Halt(\mathrm{Source}_{\mathrm{QN}k})
       \leftrightarrow \exists e\,\Prf_{\PA}(e,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner).
\]
No reflection principle or assumption of PA soundness is used to derive this
equivalence inside PA.

\section{Physical verification and the proof boundary}
\label{sec:verification}

\subsection{Checks actually executed}

\subsection{Why universal contracts and positive progress suffice}

Each source instruction is simulated by a finite positive-duration block of
literal tape transitions and returns the exact complete frame for its source
successor, with the required register update. The symbolic list lemmas are
ordinary induction arguments. Induction composes the blocks into a simulation
of every finite source run. A source halt therefore gives a tape halt.

For reflection, suppose the literal machine first halts at time $T$. If the
source has not halted in its first $T+1$ primitive steps, the positive-duration
simulation supplies a nonhalting literal run longer than $T$, contradicting
determinism. This argument uses bounded source iteration up to $T+1$, so it
does not require a nonhalting source as an infinite object. The finite tapes,
proof codes, and simulations can be encoded arithmetically and their induction
arguments formalized in PA. Prepending the checked blank startup yields the
claimed literal-machine equivalence.

\subsection{What is and is not newly certified}

The exact tables, source changes, emitter meanings, simultaneous folds,
universal source contracts and blank startups have fresh executable checks.
The logical source proof is an ordinary mathematical proof using attributed
calibration theorems. The supplied Python programs form a trusted executable
verification layer; they are not a newly constructed foundational proof kernel.

The repository's PA README explicitly states that neither machine-specific
iPRA direction is complete for its primary PA194 target \cite{status}.
Standard finite-run Lean results, a Python certificate, and a conditional
arithmetic endpoint do not fill that gap. The result here is the PA-provability
statement requested in the question, at the ordinary mathematical level, with
the proof dependencies exposed. It does not claim a new end-to-end Lean/Rocq
theorem or an iPRA theorem.

\section{Reduction attempts and their exact limits}
\label{sec:negative}

\subsection{Repairing the existing 193-state runtime core}

The incoming runtime193 experiment removes native control $301$, folds it into
$88$, and changes the relevant rows to
$87/0=0R88$, $88/0=0L51$, and $51/0=0R122$.
Its arbitrary-register source contracts pass, but the required blank
initialization does not. A runtime that works only after a clean frame is
reached is not a blank-start consistency machine \cite{runtime193}.

The new search uses the corrected pool of 327 individually neutral outside-box
row rewrites. It tests all 52,128 pairs on different rows, from all 193 existing
blank starting controls, with exact deduplication of translated configurations
before the first modified action. In total it executes 154,949,514 literal
transitions. No complete physical frame at source sites 25 through 44 is found,
even allowing all nine register values to be arbitrary. There are 775
representative runs stopped at the 200,000-step bound; the remaining runs halt
or reach certified outward blank rays.

This is a bounded screen. Pairing two individually neutral edits need not preserve
neutrality, and any positive result would still need the full simultaneous
source verification. The absence of a startup in these bounded runs does not
prove that every such pair fails forever, or that a different 193-state machine
cannot exist. The exact pool, program, input tables, receipts, and failed rows
are retained under \file{research/physical/}.

\subsection{Transferring the ZFC marker relay}

The ZFC project has a closely related delete-301 problem and an eleven-row
marker-relay repair that fails at a later ZFC source occurrence. Its transfer
to the PA core is tested here. It fails at PA source site 2, path
\texttt{00000101}, a \texttt{continueTo 2} returning to site zero, after only
four preceding primitive outcomes have passed. A concrete binary-tape
counterexample accompanies the symbolic failure: the parent returns correctly
after 32 transitions, whereas the relay returns after 54 with incorrect cells
at coordinates $3,6,8,9$. Blank start $74$ also reaches
a certified outward ray after 116 transitions. Thus the exact relay does not
become valid merely because the PA source has fewer sites.

\subsection{The QNS multiplier/comparator conflict}

The symmetric sum-Shift transformation adds a helper control and recovers its
cost through a fold involving control $41$. The coefficient-five/six multiplier
also recovers its added helper through a fold involving $41$. Applying both
independently gives 203 raw controls but only eight disjoint compatible folds,
hence 195 controls. The shared endpoint cannot be counted twice.

A new QNS5 grammar run tests 1,145,980 proposal slots, accepts 283,342 rotations,
and retains a best effective AX allocation of 60 controls, still a 195-state
machine after all costs. These counters include repeated/no-op proposals and
are not a count of distinct grammars. A separate three-way sharing screen
examines the actual source-entry contexts for controls $41,99,330$. With all
other rows fixed, no pair of replacement rows for the shared control realizes
every recorded local obligation in its 11-cell replay window. The original
bounded loop executes at most 50 transitions and accepts endpoints after
transitions 1 through 49; this exact boundary is preserved in the supplied
reproducer.
This limited failure does not exclude a redesigned helper or broader history
encoding. The raw source, context samples and search script are retained.

\subsection{Why coefficient three is not a shortcut}

The corpus already contains a physically checked 193-state coefficient-three
experiment and an explicit numerical-calculus refutation. An illustrative
collision is
\[
 E_3(1,0)=6=I_3(0,1).
\]
The existing proof calibration relies on distinct constructor meanings;
this collision is not repaired by passing source/tape tests. The incoming
research also excludes its specified attempts to rescue the equality tag by
an offset. These are obstructions to particular encodings, not a theorem
that every three-tag presentation of PA consistency is impossible.

\section{What transfers from ZFC and ConciseIPRA}

The useful ZFC transfers are concrete implementation techniques: postponing a
dummy zero push to the proof compiler, storing control information in the
preceding tape cell, source-loop rotations, and separate checks of startup and
runtime. The no-push method is used here. The failed marker-relay transfer
illustrates why the complete source-address obligations must be checked again.
The ZFC theorem itself cannot be substituted for a Con(PA) calibration;
consistency strength is part of the specification \cite{zfc}.

ConciseIPRA contributes a useful design principle: derivable logical or
computational rules can be removed from the primitive checker when the resulting
syntax and proof transformations remain explicit. Its scalar iterator and
compact native logic optimize the size of a standalone proof checker.
That metric differs from the working-state count of a binary machine,
including its decoder and initialization. Its iPRA consistency problem is
also not automatically the PA consistency problem. A promising alternative
frontend would need both a PA consistency calibration and an actual binary
implementation budget before it could replace the present calculus
\cite{ipra}. No state saving is claimed merely by citing a smaller Python checker.

\section{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Can the 193-state runtime initialize itself with a different source
layout?} The obstruction concerns a complete blank startup, not execution from
an already encoded frame. A useful next search changes the startup-generating
source structure together with the runtime rows, rather than only the initial
control or one/two neutral rows.
\item \textbf{Can a context selector share three roles?} The multiplier and symmetric
sum comparator compete for control $41$. A selector using more of the source
address could make their costs additive. Its proof must cover all actual
occurrences and the separate startup history.
\item \textbf{Can the fixed reader-fold template be escaped?} The supplied rational
dual bounds a particular pair-fold allocation problem. Source changes,
overlapping block simulations, and altered encodings lie outside that bound.
A new optimization should state exactly which restriction it removes.
\item \textbf{Can one eliminate a logical constructor rather than alias two?}
Coefficient three failed with the old equality interface. A membership-only or
definitionally encoded equality calculus would require new bidirectional proof
translations; only then is its physical cost meaningful.
\item \textbf{Can the ordinary proof be mechanized uniformly, and extended to the
targeted iPRA endpoint?} The current
nonstandard-model PA/iPRA development exposes the remaining source/calculus
calibration and physical-controller obligations. A small generic theorem for
the no-push, arbitrary-coefficient interface could cover several variants at
once while preserving exact table identities.
\end{enumerate}
