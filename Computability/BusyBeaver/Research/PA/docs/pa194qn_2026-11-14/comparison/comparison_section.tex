\subsection{What was enumerated}

786 byte identities and 535 retained table fingerprints. The fresh inventory

different stages of the corpus and must not be added or interpreted as counts
of proved machines. All 535 retained catalogue fingerprints were freshly
recomputed and agreed with the catalogue.

The full fresh inventory has 576 maximal-reachable-component fingerprints.
Of these, 563 have a spanning root and one terminal halt, so the fingerprints
classify their entire transition graphs. There are 76 such graphs with

describe the packets' claims and evidence; the structural audit does not
independently establish all of their arithmetic calibrations. One further

frontier. The fresh census and all 576 literal representatives are included.

Recognition covers complete numeric binary tables, the explicitly documented

\subsection{The precise material difference of QN5 and QN6}

Let the command store be $(t,w,d,h)$, and let $[a;w]=p(a,w)$ be the numerical
stack. The new successful MP operation returns $(b,w,b,0)$, where $b=d+x$,
instead of $(b,[0;w],b,0)$. The sum-testing Shift is retained: it halts exactly
when $t+h=1$. This is a change to the stack protocol, not a state renaming.
Its all-store compiler reserves and later removes a zero at the proof level.

\subsection{Smaller tables and failed shortcuts}

Two193-control tables require separate explanations. The factor-three table
passes its physical source contracts but has
$E_3(1,0)=I_3(0,1)=6$. With its retained heads and rules, a finite derivation
of contradiction follows; a correct implementation of that calculus is
therefore not a consistency machine. A later incoming theorem extends the
failure to every nonnegative constant equality offset in the specified
radix-three scheme. It is not an impossibility theorem for all radix-three
encodings or all193-state machines.

The other193-control table merges native301 into88 and modifies the marker
restoration path. It passes all4,780 arbitrary-register source outcomes, but
its inherited blank start moves into an outward ray. It is a useful runtime
component without a certified initializer. The supplied classification of
1,158 elementary startup variants is a finite negative result. Likewise the
restricted rational-dual bound concerns a fixed raw201 parent and a particular
fresh-selector/disjoint-fold template; it is not a lower bound for the user's
full construction problem.

Other negative examples explain why both layers must be checked. A194
short-scanner quotient passes its local folds and source CFG but fails a legal
seven-zero address. Certain known-zero comparator patches pass runtime
contracts yet halt from blank after1,361 steps. A literal append-zero transplant
has four blank-halting starts and190 outward-ray starts. Source equivalence,
runtime simulation and blank initialization are distinct obligations.

\subsection{How novelty was tested}

For each table, breadth-first enumeration follows the read0 edge before read1
and records writes, directions and destinations. The root-free fingerprint
takes all spanning roots into account. Candidate comparison additionally
allows global tape reflection, global symbol complementation, and changes to
the final write/move on entering halt. Neither QN5 nor QN6 matches any of the76
spanning194 corpus graphs under these transformations. The literal corpus,
scanner, comparison program and complete receipts are bundled for offline
reproduction. The conclusion concerns this pinned corpus, not global priority.

For historical native tables, contiguous numbering does not establish which
state was intended as the blank start. The root-free comparison deliberately
avoids this ambiguity. Symbol complementation is used only as a stronger
structural screen; it is not asserted to preserve the zero-blank computation.

\subsection{What the ZFC and concise-iPRA material contributes}

The useful transferable ideas are proof-preserving changes of command
interfaces, context-dependent decoder identifications, and explicit syntactic
proof translations. The ZFC-inspired residual-MP proposal and the concise-iPRA
proof language do not by themselves reduce the full PA binary interpreter:
their complete arithmetic coding, search scheduler and blank initializer would
also have to be costed and proved. Two further logical ideas already occur in
this corpus without completed literal implementations: transposing the Cantor
arguments of implication, and interchanging the U/E constructor tags. They
remain construction directions rather than novelty claims of this packet.

The current result supplies ordinary mathematical PA arguments with explicit
inherited calibration and universal executable certificates. It does not
claim a new end-to-end Lean/Rocq proof, an iPRA equivalence, or a global minimum
of194 states.
