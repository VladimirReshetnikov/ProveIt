\section{Result, scope, and standards of evidence}

The requested objective is a literal binary Turing machine $M$ with
strictly fewer than 194 working controls, a specified blank start, and an
ordinary mathematical proof of
\[
       \PA\vdash \bigl(\neg\Halt(M)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
\]
The comparison and experiments reported here do not meet that objective.
They produce a reusable corpus audit and a different complete command
protocol, whose current literal implementation has \prototypecount\ states.
Neither extensive failed search nor a shorter register-language program
is evidence that the requested machine cannot exist.

All source comparisons use the pinned commit printed on the title page.
The scope is the PA research tree, its 51 filed delivery directories,
the five ZIP archives currently in \path{docs/incoming}, and the historical
nested archives retained by those deliveries. The related ZFC and
ConciseIPRA directories were inspected for transferable methods; there
is no claim of a complete table census of those two much larger projects.
Appendix~\ref{app:ledger} lists every PA delivery, including source-only
and negative results that do not produce a new table.

\subsection{Three claims that must remain distinct}

An ordinary mathematical proof that PA proves an equivalence need not
come with a proof-assistant file. Conversely, checking finitely many
execution examples does not prove a universal simulation, and a theorem
that assumes missing reductions does not establish those reductions.
This report distinguishes:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Inherited mathematical results.} The numerical-calculus
calibration to PA and the arithmetic register-engine lemmas are inherited
from the supplied developments. Their exact sources are identified in
\path{references/} and the provenance files.
\item \textbf{New mathematical arguments and executable certificates.}
The command compiler, its invariant, source-controller equations, exact
blank initialization, and symbolic primitive-outcome checks are supplied
here. Symbolic runtime checks use the inherited arithmetic-box contracts.
\item \textbf{Proof-assistant endpoints.} No new end-to-end Lean or Rocq
proof of the machine-specific PA or iPRA equivalence is claimed.
\end{enumerate}

The PA root README itself says that neither machine-specific direction
has yet been established \emph{in iPRA} for primary PA194, original PA195,
or PA196. It also records substantial completed standard finite-run
Lean results, including the primary machine's calculus/clock connection.
Its remaining PA calibration and iPRA internalization must not be erased
when summarizing the project. This formalization status does not by itself
refute the packets' ordinary mathematical PA-provability claims
\cite{pa-root,primary}.

\subsection{Census method and limits}

The recognizer accepts complete numeric five-column tables with binary
read/write symbols, moves L or R, two rows per working control, and one
undefined halt label. It reads \path{.tm}, \path{.txt}, \path{.csv}, and
\path{.tsv} files, plus explicit string-valued \texttt{table} fields in
JSON; it traverses retained ZIPs recursively. Identical ZIP payloads are
visited once while their outer references are retained. It does not
decode every possible table-generating program. Qualitative proposals
are covered by the separate packet ledger.

All 9,040 expected table/text/JSON paths in the scoped PA tree were present
in the checkout at audit time. Six historical archive paths contained
four distinct outer payloads and 14 distinct recursive payloads; the five
active incoming archives bring the total to 19 unique ZIP payloads.

\section{Comparison of the designs}
\label{sec:comparison}

\subsection{The common architecture}

The main PA families combine a numerical axiom emitter, a command
enumerator, a register implementation, a binary reader for a structured
source tree, and tape-context state identifications. Each layer can
change without a proportional change in the others. In particular, fewer
source sites need not mean fewer distinct decoder subtrees.

For the primary PA194 realization the physical accounting is
\[
             134+67-7=194:
\]
134 fixed controls, 67 AX reader controls, and seven compatible contextual
identifications. The alternative association has $134+68-8=194$.
Several later designs replace this by an effective 59 AX controls above
a 135-control fixed implementation. The counts expose the principal
bottleneck: logical simplifications repeatedly reduce source descriptions
while leaving effective physical allocation unchanged \cite{primary,radix}.

\subsection{Duplicate names and materially different interfaces}

Five PA194C headline deliveries repeat one dense table. Historical
PA194HS and the filed \texttt{pa194ns\_conpa\_2026-11-13} PA194NS are
byte-identical. However, the active \texttt{PA194NS\_research.zip}'s
PA194NS uses the original sum guard $t+h=1$; it is a different graph
from the historical cache-only NS/HS. Its companion SUMS retains the
original successful-MP dummy push. PA194CSZ and PA194X have identical
dense bytes. PA194N6 appears in two deliveries. The preliminary H in
the HAZ work was already the MPH auxiliary in the N packet.

For a successful MP with conclusion $b$, the following interfaces
illustrate why names alone do not specify a construction:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{primary PA194}&(b,[0;w],b,0)\\
\text{no-push PA194N}&(b,w,b,0)\\
\text{cached MPH/HA/HAZ}&(0,w,0,b)
\end{array}
\]
The visible formula, stack, offset and cache are listed in that order.
Changing the Shift test along with this interface changes how a compiled
refutation reaches the halting test. The corresponding proof compilers
are essential parts of those designs \cite{npacket,active}.

\subsection{Why plausible combinations fail}

The corpus contains concrete counterexamples to several tempting
shortcuts. A known-zero comparator-return rewrite can satisfy clean source
contracts yet make the blank start halt after 1,361 transitions. A shorter
scanner can confuse a legal source address with a separator even though
local decoder folds pass. Radix six already uses control 330 for a
three-increment block, while a symmetric-controller fold also wants that
resource; these savings cannot simply be added.

An exact append-zero transplant is rejected for all its 194 working-state
blank starts: four halt, while the other 190 enter certified blank rays.
That excludes the particular transplant, not every zero-spare register
implementation. The DHS work already supplies a negative-only
diagonal-vacuity proof interface, but its direct negative-only compilation
profiles are at least 200 states and its positive-offset profiles at
least 198. Repeating the logical observation alone is not a reduction
\cite{radix,active}.

\section{The unchanged numerical calculus}
\label{sec:calculus}

For completeness, this section specifies the axiom interface used by
the new protocol. It is the separate-head coefficient-four interface of
the primary machine. Let
\[
 p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}{2}+b,\quad
 I(a,b)=4p(a,b),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,
\]
\[
 U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,\qquad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
Zero is the fixed truth code; one is the contradiction code $M(0,0)$.
The special zero code avoids interpreting $I(0,0)=0$ as an infinitely
recursive formula. Write $N(B)=I(B,1)$,
$B\wedge C=N(I(B,N(C)))$, and
$\exists x_a B=N(U(a,N(B)))$.
The separate diagonal head is $\Gamma(B)=I(B,U(B,B))$.

The six ordinary heads are
\begin{align*}
L_1(D,H)&=I(\exists x_D U(D,H),H),\\
L_2(D,A,H)&=I(U(D,U(A,H)),U(A,U(D,H))),\\
L_3(D,A,H)&=I(M(H,D),I(E(H,A),M(A,D))),\\
L_4(D,A,H)&=I(E(H,D),I(M(A,H),M(A,D))),\\
L_5(D,H)&=\exists x_D E(H,D),\\
L_6(D,A,H)&=I(U(D,A),I(U(D,I(A,H)),U(D,H))).
\end{align*}
The dominated propositional head is
\[
 \rho(a,b,c,u)=I(I(I(c,u),a),I(I(a,c),I(b,c))),\qquad u\le a.
\]
For the gate, define
\begin{align*}
 V(a,b,c)&=I(M(a,b),U(c,M(a,b))),\\
 P(a,b,c)&=I(c,M(c,b)),\\
 B(a,b,c)&=U\bigl(b,I(M(a,b),P(a,b,c))\wedge I(M(c,b),c)\bigr),\\
 Q(a,b,c)&=U(c,I(B(a,b,c),N(P(a,b,c)))).
\end{align*}
Then $g(a,b,c)=0$ if $c=a$ or $c=b$, and otherwise
$g(a,b,c)=N(I(V(a,b,c),Q(a,b,c)))$.
The rules are modus ponens and closed generalization, with the fixed
zero theorem. The original calibration and the separate-head proof
translation are retained in \path{references/calibration/}
\cite{calibration,separate}.

The inherited PA calibration is a syntactic proof-translation result,
not an assumption that PA is consistent. Numerical code freshness makes
the diagonal binder absent from its own decoded formula. Fixed finite
templates recover the required equality and separation interfaces. The
PA-to-calculus translation and the reverse translation are the substantive
logical dependencies; unchanged axiom generation allows the new protocol
to reuse them. This report does not replace those translations by an
unproved assertion that every numerical axiom is true.

\section{Literal implementation and machine-specific evidence}
\label{sec:literal}

The protocol proof above does not by itself allocate a Turing-machine
state. The supplied literal construction begins with the primary
194-state native table and adds two controls. The resulting 196-state
machine implements the unshifted MP target $d+x$; successful nonhalting
MP clears the offset. It reuses the original destructive comparator to
recognize target one. AX, the numerical constructors and the original
Shift are unchanged. The no-one invariant makes Shift's original halting
branch unreachable on initialized runs. Earlier positive-target
implementations required more controls; they are described as supporting
experiments, not as the main construction.

\input{literal_results.tex}

\subsection{What the verification does and does not prove}

The symbolic runtime represents nine arbitrary natural registers, an
arbitrary affine header gap, and an opaque arbitrary finite spare tail.
Primitive contracts therefore cover variable register values rather than
just a bounded list of concrete examples. Its arithmetic-box rules are
inherited lemmas; the script is not an independent proof of those lemmas.
The full fixed initialization is checked by complete tape equality,
including every nonzero cell, the head position, and the control.

The command checker separately executes 478 finite source cases. These
are useful cross-checks of arithmetic and structured exits, not substitutes
for the universal source arguments. The abstract protocol checker verifies
35 symbolic identities, 130 exact integer identities, and 419 decoder
examples. Its symbolic comparisons use equality, explicit zero aliases,
positivity, and constructor residues; unresolved comparisons fail.

One rejected preliminary construction illustrates the need to check
the entire command boundary: a locally valid break in an unshifted-target
MP prototype returned to the Shift branch rather than the command fetch.
Primitive runtime contracts and a startup check alone did not establish
the desired high-level protocol. The delivered unshifted-target construction uses a different,
fully checked source tail with the correct return to site 25. The rejected
earlier attempt is not the delivered table.

The mathematical implication is obtained by composing the stated
source-controller proof, the invariant, exact startup, primitive simulation
contracts, and the inherited PA calibration. A finite source halt gives a
finite physical halt by concatenation. In the reverse direction, positive
simulation durations and determinism compare any proposed physical halt
of length $T$ with $T+1$ nonhalting source segments. This avoids assuming
an inverse decoder for arbitrary intermediate tapes. The argument is
arithmetizable in PA. The package does not export a new kernel-checked
endpoint for the resulting literal table.

\section{Completed reduction searches and their exact scope}
\label{sec:search}

\subsection{Three configuration encodings on eight frontier machines}

Twenty-four completed searches used eight input tables and three
configuration representations. They tested 1,195,148,640 patch trials
and found no surviving patch. The trial count includes repeated or
equivalent proposed machines and is not a count of distinct designs.

For a dropped control $a$, a retained control $b$, and a common incoming
direction $d\in\{-1,+1\}$, the representations encode $a$ as $b$ using:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a head displacement from $h$ to $h-2d$;
\item complementation of the preceding cell at $h-d$;
\item both changes together.
\end{enumerate}
The corresponding incoming transition reverses its direction, complements
its written bit, or does both. One row of the retained control is
synthesized and the other is inherited. The dropped control is not the
start. The checker requires an exact encoded successor, including the
complete window tape, in 1 through 128 child transitions without reading
outside a radius-six, 13-cell window.

\input{encoding_search_table.tex}

Source windows are derived from the addressed program and universal
primitive contracts, then intersected with a coarse blank-window invariant.
Variable-length unary runs are conservatively expanded; arithmetic-box
and self-loop controls receive the entire coarse closure rather than a
sample of register values. The inherited symbolic-box lemmas remain
dependencies. Positive and deliberately corrupted toy controls were run
for all three encoding templates.

The first two templates adapt the supplied PA196 physical-search methods;
the third composes their encodings. The algebraic incoming-edge identity
for the combined case is particularly simple: a write $w$ at $x$ followed
by a move $d$ into $a$ becomes a write $1-w$ followed by a move $-d$ into
$b$, because $(x+d)-2d=x-d$ and $(x+d)-d=x$.
The full source, offline inputs, and completed receipts are in
\path{search/encodings/}.

These are exact exclusions for the finite windows, row template, step
limit and input machines used. They do not exclude a different invariant,
larger local block, simultaneous multistate rewrite, or different program.
An earlier radius-four DHS screen, recorded separately, tested another
98,247,808 trials under the first two templates; it is not included in
the 1,195,148,640 total.

\subsection{Restoring a repainted history bit}

A separate search considered all 43 recognized 194-state action graphs.
Both merged controls must have right-moving incoming transitions, each
writing a fixed old bit; the two old bits may be equal. The incoming writes
are changed to distinct control tags, and existing helpers must restore
the original history bit before reaching the exact old successor.

The search checks the four combinations of old control and current symbol.
Only the cells at offsets $-1$ and $0$ may be read; the exact successor
must be reached within nine transitions. Candidate branches are original
actions or a move-left action to an existing helper. Recursion through
an unresolved merged branch is excluded, and the start is protected.
There were 177,936,788 tested branch-action proposals and no surviving
pair of replacement rows. A three-step same-old-bit fixture succeeds,
while corrupting its restoration bit rejects the designated witness.

Unlike the preceding search, this template does not use a reachable-tape
invariant: the four local cases are universal under the stated incoming
history condition. The 43 inputs include rejected machines, so a success
on one of those would not establish consistency equivalence. All inputs,
the search script and receipts are in \path{search/history/} and
\path{references/frontier_tables/}. The finite result is not a lower
bound for unrestricted contextual quotients.

The same two-cell template was also run on the final MP196 table with
a 17-transition bound. Its 107 eligible controls yielded 4,373,528
branch-action proposals and no surviving fold. This last screen is
recorded separately and is not part of the 43-input total.

\subsection{Other bounded source investigations}

A conservative zero/positivity analysis of the N6 source visited 271,404
program-counter/mask pairs at 3,776 source sites. At three known-zero
return rows, exhaustive one-row replacements gave 6, 12 and 13 admissible
options from 1,552 actions per row. All $6\cdot12\cdot13=936$ combinations
failed to yield an ordinary bisimulation or the tested one-bit-history
identification. Those files are included as supporting research rather
than a theorem that no joint source rewrite can help.

Simple aliasing of two of the nine registers is also not justified by
generic arithmetic contracts in which the relevant scratch values can
be simultaneously positive. More global lifetime allocation, a changed
macro interface, or a different tape representation would need its own
proof. We did not exhaust all such implementations.

\section{What remains to obtain a genuine sub-194 result}

The new protocol moves contradiction detection to the step that actually
derives code one. This yields a clean, consistency-independent invariant
excluding code one from all stored formulas before a halt. The literal
implementation, however, still needs two additional reader controls to
reuse the target-one comparison. Its present state count is above the prior frontier.

A successful next reduction must reduce the complete allocated machine.
The most concrete open routes exposed by this work are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Find a source/controller allocation that exploits the no-one
invariant enough to offset the MP tail, while keeping the AX decoder and
blank initialization compatible.
\item Rebuild the register-frame implementation, possibly with a proven
zero-spare or compressed layout, and prove every arithmetic block and the
new blank seed against that layout.
\item Search simultaneous decoder/controller changes with complete
allocation accounting. Optimistic grammar scores must be followed by a
literal table, compatible fold witnesses, and startup verification.
\item Change the proof interface only with a uniform refutation compiler
in both required directions. Reducing the searched theory to a weaker
proof system changes the target consistency statement unless a suitable
calibration is supplied.
\end{enumerate}
These are directions for further work, not claims that the required further
state saving is available. The present report records the failed bound clearly
so that the comparison, proofs and search receipts can be reused without
mistaking them for a completed 193-state construction.
