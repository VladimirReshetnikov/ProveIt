## Scope and reproducibility

The comparison covers all 51 filed research packets in `Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/docs`, all five currently incoming archives, the direct PA project tables, and six historical nested-archive paths. The six historical paths contain four distinct outer archives and fourteen distinct recursive archive payloads. With the five current incoming payloads, nineteen unique ZIP payloads were visited. Identical ZIP payloads are visited once, while all their outer references are recorded.

All 9,040 expected `.tm`, `.txt`, `.csv`, `.tsv`, and `.json` paths in the scoped PA tree were present locally when audited; the checked-out commit matches the pin above. The recognizer accepts complete numeric five-column binary L/R tables with exactly one undefined halt label, including explicit string-valued `table` fields in JSON. It does not claim to decode every arbitrary program that could generate a machine. The packet ledger separately covers source-only proposals and qualitative contributions.

Observed totals: **1,542 recognized table occurrences, 731 byte forms, 503 action-graph classes admitting a spanning root, and two disconnected diagnostic classes.** The 194-state frontier contains **43 graph classes: 28 reported constructions, 10 auxiliary/search classes, and 5 explicitly rejected classes**. The forty-third class is PA194N_AD, stored only in a JSON table field. The text-file-only subset has 1,488 occurrences, 730 byte forms, 502 spanning classes, and 42 classes at 194 states.

These counts differ from older packet counts because their pinned snapshots, omission policies, scopes, and class encodings differ. They are neither counts of independently established consistency theorems nor state lower bounds. Historical `MACHINES.md` counts 4,984 original `.tm` delivery occurrences before duplicate-machine removal, whereas the present scan counts the current retained files plus active and nested archives. Its six- and nine-control synthetic fixtures have only two and four controls reachable from their supplied starts; these are not candidate consistency machines.

`audit_current.py` is the executable census; `current_census/` records every occurrence, byte identity, graph membership, nested archive identity, and exclusion. Graph hashes are calculated by enumerating roots and taking the least hash of a breadth-first canonical transition list, as in the repository's `machine_classes.py`. The hash forgets state names and, for table comparison, the designated start. The actual machine claims always retain their specified blank-start control. No reported construction with fewer than 194 states appears in this audited corpus.

## Duplicates and name collisions

1. Five PA194C headline submissions contain the same rooted action graph and dense table. They are five deliveries, not five machine designs.
2. Historical PA194HS and `pa194ns_conpa_2026-11-13`'s PA194NS are byte-identical. Both use no-push MP and symmetric cache-only Shift.
3. Current `PA194NS_research.zip`'s PA194NS is different: it retains the original sum guard `t+h=1` and reverses the destructive comparison. Its companion SUMS retains the MP dummy-zero push. Naming it simply PA194NS without its packet or guard would conflate distinct machines.
4. PA194CSZ and PA194X are the same dense bytes.
5. PA194N6 is repeated in the N5/N6 packet and the later N6 packet.
6. HAZ's preliminary H was already the auxiliary MPH table in the N packet; HA and HAZ are new physical compositions, not a new underlying MP interface.

## Proof boundary

The current root PA README explicitly states that neither machine-specific direction of the desired iPRA equivalence has been established for primary PA194, original PA195, or PA196. The primary table's standard finite-run connections are far stronger than bounded testing: the project proves its source/clock composition and the connection to a displayed separate-diagonal numerical calculus. Its PA calibration and internalization in iPRA remain open in the proof-assistant development.

The delivered research packets claim ordinary mathematical PA-provable halting/refutation equivalences. Their common logical calibration is inherited; their physical claims use exact blank initialization, universal source contracts, positive-duration trace composition, and contextual quotient lemmas. An ordinary PA proof is distinct from a new Lean/Rocq endpoint, and both are distinct from a conditional theorem that assumes the missing reductions. A newly reduced physical machine must explicitly state which inherited theorem it transports.

Important concrete failure modes already represented in the corpus:

- A legal source transformation can destroy blank initialization. Several known-zero register6 comparator-return changes pass clean-source contracts yet halt after1,361 blank-start steps.
- Removing the seventh scanner stage changes head alignment on a legal source context; an apparent194-state table can therefore be rejected despite passing local fold checks.
- H6 cannot blindly reuse the symmetric controller's state330: radix6 already uses330 for a three-increment block, and both folds consume41.
- The exact primary append-zero transplant is rejected for every working-state blank start: four halt and190 reach inductively proved blank rays. That does not exclude a redesigned zero-spare runtime.
- Optimistic grammar scores and the absence of bounded search hits are not correctness certificates or lower bounds.

## Existing negative-search scope worth avoiding

The radix packet already tried all36 pair transpositions of nine physical scanner positions on two seeds and then fixed-seed random permutations:1,275 screened tables in90seconds, with a conservative local quotient rule at radius5/300steps. It did not exhaust all9! physical register layouts or perform a full global liveness/register-allocation proof.

Several packets exhaust bounded first-action quotient closures, test row-choice state merges, perform neutral two-row rewrites, and run millions of grammar proposals. These searches are substantial but highly template-dependent. HAZ additionally tests literal-head detours, ±2-cell displacement and marker-bit representation changes at radius8/384, finding no reduction on HA/HAZ. The exact older bit-phase/head-displacement algorithms can still be worth comparing on other194 families, provided their domain differs from the already screened rules.

DHS already proves a negative-only diagonal-vacuity proof-template suffices for its inherited PA-refutation calibration. Direct negative-only compilation profiles are at least200 states; positive-offset profiles at least198. Repeating the logical observation without a new physical allocation would not establish progress below194.

The next meaningful result must therefore change at least one of: effective decoder sharing, fixed-register implementation, the joint interaction of controller/axiom folds, or a proof-search representation whose complete physical compiler is smaller. A new same-size composition is not the user’s current requested result.
