BusyBeaver development. LICENSE.JamesChang preserves its MIT notice.

Their inclusion does not imply endorsement of the new experiments.

The new work in this package consists of the pinned census assembly,
comparison and targeted related-ideas reading; the immediate-MP-halting
protocol and its command/source arguments; the exact 196 construction;
the stated local search experiments; independent finite audits; and report
assembly. It does not include a new end-to-end proof-assistant theorem.

The symbolic runtime and compiler are inherited code. The runtime uses
existing arithmetic-box lemmas as contracts, not an independently verified
new arithmetic engine. The new physical changes lie in source-reader
controls; all inherited oracle-control rows are checked unchanged.

The head-offset and neighboring-bit search programs adapt the supplied
PA consistency comparison packet's physical_search/fold_shift.cpp and
fold_bitphase.cpp. fold_combined.cpp composes their configuration encodings.
History repainting, its finite controls and the new source/controller
research are identified in the report and their respective README files.

Historical calibration fragments describe their original tables and proof
dependencies. They are included to expose the inherited ordinary PA
calibration, not to imply that a statement about a prior literal table is
automatically a statement about MP196.
