\begin{abstract}
\textbf{No justified machine with fewer than 194 working states was obtained.}
The requested fallback is realized by two new 194-state binary, one-tape,
blank-start constructions, \Mone{} and \Mtwo. They combine the supplied
H5X and JH5X numerical proof programs with two guarded execution shortcuts
and a separated marker/history-selector implementation. Every transition
is supplied, together with deterministic builders and an independent
literal-row verifier.

The new relative theorem is proved in intuitionistic primitive recursive
arithmetic with explicit bounds:
$H_P(t)\to H_M(5t)$ and $H_M(t)\to H_P(3t)$.
It composes with the supplied revised AX11H written consistency calibration
and the witness-calculus transports to give the ordinary written conclusion
$\ipra\vdash\NH(M)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)$ for both children.
The absolute calibration is an explicitly identified inherited mathematical
dependency; no new end-to-end Lean or Rocq theorem is claimed.

The census is pinned to ProveIt commit
\file{d8d4fb10149d6a76a56e615a35648c8c32847cf4}.
It distinguishes named positive proposals, experimental tables, runtime-only
cores, and malformed semantic proposals. Exact graph comparison establishes
that the two children differ from the recognized current 194-state tables,
including changes of root, reflection, symbol exchange, and halt-action
conventions. Their differences are active after initialization.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result and exact machine specification}
\label{sec:result}
Each delivered machine has one two-way-infinite tape over $\{0,1\}$,
blank symbol zero, and only left and right moves. It starts in state zero
with head coordinate zero on the entirely blank tape. Working states are
$0,\ldots,193$; label 194 is a separate terminal halt label. A transition
writes and moves before entering its target, including when the target is
halt. There are 388 transition rows. The complete dense tables appear in
Appendix~\ref{app:tables} and in the machine directories.

\subsection{What is new, inherited, and still unfinished}
The new contribution is the compatible composition on these two exact
witness-calculus parents, its complete tables, the independent verification,
the uniform relative proof, and the current comparison. The ingredients
are attributed: marker separation already occurs in PA194B and its later
LCG/LCGS compositions; the two shortcuts occur in the supplied accelerated
GH family. H5X and JH5X and their logical proof translations are supplied
in the incoming witness packet. This report does not claim to have invented
those ingredients. The exact source packets are cited in
References~\cite{witness,marker,fast}.

Three levels of evidence must remain distinct. First, this report proves
the relative iPRA result directly from the rows. Second, the absolute
PA-consistency conclusion uses the identified \emph{written} calibration
and its published proof transformations. Third, the ongoing Lean project
has not yet assembled the full physical-machine consistency theorem.
The repository's central status page describes the third level; later
incoming manuscripts supply claims and arguments at the second level.
A conditional formal endpoint is not being rebranded as a completed kernel
proof. Conversely, unfinished formalization alone does not refute a written
mathematical proof. See the pinned formal status page and the retained
calibration sources~\cite{pa,ax11h}.

We reviewed the written calibration's architecture, explicit clocks,
source-command translations, and treatment of the forward proof compiler.
We did not independently rebuild every transitive proof-generating recipe.
The exact parent packet, its proof sources, its checkers, and the revised
calibration are retained in \file{references/}. Readers can therefore
inspect the absolute dependency without reconstructing it from citations
or accepting an undocumented consistency assumption.

\subsection{The main architectural alternatives}
The named 194-state designs in the examined lineage combine an arithmetic
proof-search program with a shared unary-register interpreter and a binary
source-path decoder. Their state costs respond to interactions between
these components. Reducing the number of source tokens can remove a useful
shared subtree and increase the literal machine count.

The primary PA194 allocation has 134 fixed controls, 67 raw AX readers and
seven folds, giving $134+67-7=194$. Other constructions attain 194 by
changing both raw readers and compatible folds, including 68 minus eight
and 69 minus nine. The two witness parents use the last of these allocations.
Our physical replacement removes one reader and introduces one dedicated
marker control, so it changes the organization without changing the total.

The logical differences are substantial. Cached modus ponens changes the
meaning of the visible top and cache. No-push modus ponens removes a dummy
stack insertion. A cache-only halt test changes the compiler's final
contradiction-detection word. AX11 pushes the guarded result and consumes
a real fourth operand, so ten-entry selectors cannot simply be reused.
Explicit-binder generalization changes which parsed operand binds the
cached theorem. Diagonal relocation, the Q premise exchange, changed
constructor coefficients, and the new witness/positive-join heads require
proof translations, not merely execution tests. Physical differences
include register placement, source association, history folds, shortened
loop returns, and marker separation.

Appendix~\ref{app:families} compares every mechanism family in the current
named roster. The archive also supplies all named-design records, all
recognized table occurrences, exact provenance, and literal 194-state
representatives. Naming and file duplication are not used as proxies for
independent constructions. For example, some QN/NQ, HS/NS, and known-zero
return labels refer to repeated or closely related designs; their actual
transition identities and attributed protocols are recorded separately.

\subsection{Why the 193-state entries do not solve the task}
A runtime-only core obtained by identifying native 301 with 88 is especially
instructive. It installs $(88,0):0L51$ and $(51,0):0R122$ and removes 301.
On initialized register frames, its source contracts can hold. On blank
tape, however, the same control 51 is still responsible for allocating
previously absent register material. The shared decoder route sends that
case into an outward writing ray instead of the required marker routine.
A valid runtime contract does not initialize its own hypotheses.

The supplied exact obstruction covers all nine-register permutations of
that fixed core. Its startup reaches state 35 beyond all written ones;
$(35,0):1R35$ then gives an inductive nonhalting certificate. Other 193-state
records include source/guard failures or an inconsistent coefficient-three
numerical calculus. A refutation in the latter is a refutation of the
changed numerical calculus, not a PA refutation. Search timeouts are kept
separate from such exact counterarguments.

Our marker-separated implementation keeps the reader sharing but gives the
original marker its own control 400. This explains both why it works from
blank tape and why its count is 194 rather than 193. The extra control has
not been omitted from the accounting.

\subsection{The numerical language}
For Cantor pairing $p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b$, the coefficient-four
language uses
\[
 I(A,B)=4p(A,B),\quad U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,\quad
 M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
Code zero is the exceptional truth code; code one is the designated
contradiction. The equality $I(0,0)=0$ must be handled explicitly in syntax
and stack arguments. It is not legitimate to reason as though all
constructors were disjoint after numerical evaluation.

The inherited programs use eleven AX stack entries, an actual fourth
operand, explicit-binder generalization, no-dummy-push MP, cache-only Shift,
and the ordinary distribution-head order. Their common new witness head is
\[
 U(D,E(H,D)\to1)\to A,
\]
where $A$ is independent of $D,H$. This recovers the old witness head at
$A=1$ and supplies an explosion derivation. Put
$X=U(D,U(D,H)\to1)$. H5X retains the first head
$(X\to1)\to H$, whereas JH5X uses
\[
 (X\to H)\to H.
\]
The latter change is justified together with the new witness head, whose
independent consequent supplies the needed explosion. It is not a free
substitution of a classical truth-table equivalent before the calculus has
proved the required implication principles. The parent package includes
explicit finite proof lists for these transports.

\subsection{The two directions of the calibration}
The written soundness construction annotates every live numerical formula,
stack entry and cache with a PA proof of its translation. AX, MP and Shift
preserve those annotations by primitive-recursive proof transformations.
In the finite-set interpretation, membership is binary membership and the
separation gate is supplied by a uniformly generated PA induction-proof
pattern. A numerical halt gives a proof of the translated contradiction,
then a PA refutation. This is a syntactic construction of PA proofs, not
an appeal to PA's truth or a uniform truth predicate.

The converse compiles an arbitrary PA refutation into the numerical calculus,
then into an executable command word. The all-store compiler accounts for
carried stack contents, the eleven-entry interface, and the fourth operand.
The fair scheduler eventually executes an appropriate word; the final Pop
is chosen for the cache-only halt test. The revised manuscript supplies
primitive-recursive witness clocks and a finite-demand treatment of the
forward compiler, rather than silently appealing to arbitrary PA induction
in the ambient weak theory. The parent physical/source certificates bind
those commands to the literal tables.

\begin{theorem}[Absolute conclusion with identified inherited proof]
Using the revised AX11H written calibration and the supplied H5X/JH5X
proof transports, the two delivered machines satisfy
\[
 \ipra\vdash\NH(\Mone)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA),\qquad
 \ipra\vdash\NH(\Mtwo)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each parent $P$, the cited written calibration and its source transports
give $\ipra\vdash\NH(P)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:relative} supplies
$\ipra\vdash\NH(P)\leftrightarrow\NH(M)$ for the exact new bytes.
Composition is intuitionistically valid. No assumption $\Con(\PA)$ is
made in deriving either equivalence. The first premise is inherited and
documented; the second is proved in full in this report.
\end{proof}

\section{Initialization, validation, and material differences}
\label{sec:validation}
The parents reach the exact canonical source frame in 57,922 transitions.
The two accelerations save 52 transitions during startup, giving 57,870.
Marker separation then adds 100 transitions, yielding 57,970 for each
final machine. These numbers follow from complete literal runs, not a
window around the head.

The endpoint is native state 84 at head $-2$, source site 25, header offset
48, with physical primary register values
\[
 (0,0,0,0,0,0,2,0,0)
\]
and 26 one-valued spare registers. The logical-to-physical map is
$(6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1)$, so the logical seed is
$(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$. The independent verifier reconstructs the entire
finite set of one-cells from this layout and the actual source path; all
other cells are zero.

Startup uses the $(26,1)$ shortcut 20 times and $(101,0)$ six times.
The two expanded zero cases of the marker construction occur 14 and 22
times, respectively. Thus the exact change is
$-2(20+6)+4(14)+2(22)=48$ parent steps.
A further 10,000 synchronized accelerated steps exercise $(26,1)$ ten
more times and the $(301,0)$ replacement once more. This is direct evidence
that the changed routines affect execution after initialization. The
unbounded correctness theorem comes from the preceding induction, not
from extrapolating those 10,000 steps.

The independent checker imports no builder. It checks the parent byte
identities, exact transition changes, incoming-history premises, all 12
acceleration windows, all four marker cases, all 384 ordinary row
identities, the dense/native bijection, totality, unique halt, all-state
graph reachability, complete blank startup, and continuing synchronization.
The current-corpus novelty checker performs exact action-labeled graph
isomorphism tests; it does not infer nonisomorphism from different hashes
alone. It also verifies a positive control and the two children against
each other. Reflection, global symbol exchange, arbitrary choice of root,
and ignoring halt-entry write/move decorations are admitted in the
structural comparison. Symbol exchange is used only to strengthen the
novelty test; it is not asserted to preserve a zero-blank computation.

The source semantics are inherited, with 4,786 and 4,794 parent source
outcomes respectively. We do not need a fresh proof of every arithmetic
box for the children: the new all-tape relative theorem composes below
that entire source argument. The parent evidence and its validation
programs are bundled for audit. No Python execution is called a Lean or
Rocq kernel proof.

\section{Further reduction work in this investigation}
\label{sec:search}
\input{search_summary.tex}

The tests were chosen to resolve particular possible savings. A pair of
states with a common row need not have compatible other rows; allowing
arbitrary replacement actions broadens that test. Incoming-edge rethreading
allows different callers to use different replacements rather than forcing
one alias. A whole-instruction endpoint can accept a local path that fails
to simulate a single original transition. These screens still have specific
limitations: fixed frame conventions, specified excluded interfaces, finite
control-edit templates, and finite symbolic-execution fuel. Their failure
is not a state-count lower bound.

A separate source experiment relocates the diagonal schema in the newest
witness calculus and optimizes its tree and parameter associations. A
validated 195-state construction is retained as research, with a complete
source-graph match and construction-side symbolic contracts. It is above
the target and is not counted as a successful deliverable. The report does
not round an optimistic matching score down to a state count; simultaneous
fold compatibility, source execution and blank initialization are separate
obligations.

Five completed grammar searches made 10,597,382 proposals in total, without
finding a 194-or-lower candidate in their templates. Their best nominal
objectives were 196 for $\Gamma(H)$ relocation, 195 for H5X with
$\Gamma(D)$, 196 for JH5X with $\Gamma(D)$, and 195 for each of two further
H5X variants using parameter permutations or the implicit generalization
binder. These objective values are reported as search outcomes; the
particular 195-state table is separately reconstructed and validated.

We also rechecked an older 193-state guard repair that changes the shared
reader's zero branch through control 174. Instead of stopping at its first
source failure, the checker examined all 4,780 primary-source outcomes.
Eighty-eight fail, covering sites 3554--3620 in the generalization routine;
the blank run also reaches the outward state-35 ray at time 92. The table,
full failure list and trace are explicitly retained as rejected research.

\section{Connections with the suggested research areas}
\label{sec:related}
The ZFC machines demonstrate a useful engineering principle: optimize source
association, control sharing, history routing, and initialization together.
A history bit can replace a control distinction only when every relevant
entry, including startup, supplies that bit. The present marker separation
uses precisely this discipline. Transplanting a ZFC row by its numerical
label would not establish the needed invariant in a PA program~\cite{zfc}.

ConciseIPRA encourages explicit derivations of logical infrastructure and
small proof-checker interfaces. Its checker-size metric is different from
the number of states in a blank-start binary machine. More fundamentally,
a search for inconsistency of iPRA does not have the requested PA-consistency
calibration. Any smaller logic must retain a suitable PA proof translation.
Here weak arithmetic is the theory proving the simulation, while PA is the
theory whose inconsistency is searched for~\cite{concise}.

A Diophantine consistency representation could replace proof checking with
integer enumeration and polynomial evaluation. That is an alternative
architecture, not an immediate state reduction. The evaluator, candidate
enumerator, constants and blank initialization must all be paid for in the
same literal machine model; degree, variable count, coefficient size and
source-expression length are not themselves state counts. No such complete
sub-194 evaluator was constructed in this investigation~\cite{dioph}.

\subsection{Open construction questions}
The remaining construction questions are specific:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Can the marker and selector share a control under a different
bootstrap or register representation without losing the unallocated-field
case? The known initialized 193-state core isolates this issue sharply.
\item Can a new source association remove the six-zero path obstruction
and also preserve the incoming-history conditions of shared readers and
all transient startup addresses? Shortening AX alone cannot do this.
\item Can the 195-state diagonal-relocated witness program acquire one
additional compatible fold, or can a different schema transport alter its
sharing pattern enough to save two controls and reach 193?
\item Can a shared selector implement several history-sensitive pairs at
once, with its cost charged once and with a finite uniform simulation
certificate for the complete simultaneous table?
\item Can the local clock proof here be inserted into the ongoing Lean
arithmetic development so that each newly audited physical composition
inherits a literal machine endpoint with minimal extra formal work?
\end{enumerate}
These are unresolved construction questions. None of the finite searches
reported here settles the minimum possible state count.
