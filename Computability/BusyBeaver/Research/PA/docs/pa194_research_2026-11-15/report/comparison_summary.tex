Here ``spanning'' means that some control reaches every working control in
the directed graph. It says nothing about whether the blank computation
actually visits every control. These census classes allow arbitrary working-state
renaming and root choice, while preserving read bits, move directions and
halt-entry actions. The novelty test below additionally permits reflection,
bit exchange and erased halt-entry actions. These numbers count explicit
graphs, including experiments; they do not count established consistency
theorems.

The previous 747-key repository catalog is completely recovered. Its
all-roots key only remembers a maximal reachable component when no root
spans the graph. Auditing all 23 nonspanning groups found one group that
collapses two different 194-state diagnostics, each with a largest
reachable component of three controls. Exact whole-graph matching separates
them. The corrected total is therefore 1,154, rather than 1,153 inherited
keys; the corrected 194-state total is 530, rather than 529.

A supplementary scan inspected 12,520 JSON payload occurrences, deduplicating
ZIP payloads, and recognized 365 explicit complete 193/194-state table
occurrences. Their 198 normalized tables contributed no new graph key
beyond the \file{.tm} census. The recognizer's scope and records are supplied
in \file{comparison/json_supplement/}. Code generators are not treated as
an enumerable set of every machine they could ever produce; the comparison
concerns their supplied constructions, explicit outputs and named designs.

The 91-row named ledger extends the attributed marker-separation roster
with the four AX11GDH realizations, two separated-marker realizations, and
the H5X/JH5X pair. Aliases are bound to actual tables. A fresh independent
literal simulator also checked all 17 principal or companion machines in
the seven incoming ZIPs: each has 194 graph-reachable controls, 388 rows,
and the complete reported blank-start seed tape. That finite check
validates initialization, not the absolute consistency theorem.

Finally, a fresh independent scalar screen tested all 55 recognized
193-state graphs from all 193 possible blank starts: 10,615 runs.
It found 112 finite halts, 10,499 outward blank self-loop rays, and four
runs reaching a 100,000-transition cap. A ray is certified by an actual
configuration with zero under the head, a zero-read self-loop, and no
one-cell anywhere ahead in the direction of motion. The infinite
continuation then has a direct induction proof. The four capped runs are
left unresolved by this screen; their two source mechanisms have separate
recorded semantic or compilation failures. Neither the caps nor the rays
establish a lower bound for arbitrary PA-consistency machines.
