\subsection{Composition of the stronger witness and relocated diagonal}
Let $I(a,b)=4p(a,b)$, $U(d,a)=I(a,d)+2$, and
$\Gamma(a)=I(a,U(a,a))$. Retain the PA194H5X command interface, including
its four AX fields, eleven-entry stack, explicit-binder generalization,
no-push visible-result modus ponens, and cache-only Shift.
Replace only the diagonal entry $q_1=\Gamma(A)$ by
$q_1=\Gamma(D)$, where $D=d+x$ and $A=c$ are the accumulated AX parameters.
Retain the stronger witness entry
\[
q_3=I(U(D,I(E(H,D),1)),A).
\]
The old and new commands generate exactly the same numerical axiom families.
In particular the retained stronger witness schema has three independently
selectable parameters; diagonal relocation does not identify its consequent
parameter with its binder. The exact change to the emitted word consists of
three occurrences of CopyA replaced by CopyD within the diagonal subword.

Every generated $q_j$, $0\leq j\leq8$, is still an implication code divisible
by four; the generalization entry $q_9=U(c,h)$ has residue two. The exact
Push, Pop, offset, generalization, and MP word contracts of the H5X interface
therefore remain unchanged. They use the positions and these residue facts,
not the internal formula tree of $q_1$.

For soundness, annotate the incoming store with numerical derivations as in
the inherited all-store invariant. The changed entry is the primitive
diagonal axiom at the legitimate arbitrary numeral $D$, so its annotation
is a one-row proof. No assumption that the old offset is zero is needed.
All remaining annotations and the cache-only halt argument are unchanged.

For completeness, the normalized proof compiler enters each axiom selector
with offset and cache zero. To select a diagonal instance $\Gamma(B)$, use
AX fields $(c,x,y,z)=(0,B,0,0)$ and select rank one. Then $D=B$ and the
selected formula is literally $\Gamma(B)$. To select any other primitive
axiom, retain its previous parameter choice: changing an unselected diagonal
entry does not change that choice or the selector's proof. The selectors
are total even on dirty incoming top and stack, because all discarded
newly generated entries differ from one. Thus the same primitive recursive
proof-to-command compiler, with only this diagonal parameter substitution,
produces an all-store halting word from every numerical refutation.

The two command systems consequently halt exactly when the same numerical
calculus derives code one. Their forward and reverse witness transformations
use primitive recursion and the inherited bounded-store induction; the
change introduces no new induction principle. Once an exact literal table
and source simulation are supplied, this establishes its relative iPRA
halting equivalence to the calibrated H5X system. This statement concerns
the logical construction; a favorable grammar fitness alone is not a
literal-machine theorem or a state-count claim.
