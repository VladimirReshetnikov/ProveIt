ATTRIBUTION AND STATUS

This research is based on Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository at commit
d8d4fb10149d6a76a56e615a35648c8c32847cf4 and its attributed PA research packets.
The shared register runtime and literal Turing-machine lineage credit James
Chang and Andrew James Wade. The supplied ProveIt packets provide the source
compiler, arithmetic program, local-fold machinery and calibration ancestry.
Original component notices and licenses are preserved in licenses/, in the
unchanged parent ZIP, and alongside inherited research search code.

The H5X/JH5X numerical calculi and their proof transports are inherited from
the incoming witness packet. Marker separation and the two guarded shortcuts
are inherited ideas from the preceding marker and acceleration packets.
This release's claimed contributions are their new compatible compositions
on the exact witness parents, a complete relative iPRA proof, independent
literal certificates, current comparison, and additional reduction searches.

The large absolute PA calibration is explicitly inherited. An audit of it
is not described as a complete reimplementation. No completed end-to-end
Lean or Rocq theorem and no minimum-state lower bound are claimed.

Exact source binding and archive identities are in PROVENANCE.json and
comparison/source_blob_binding.json. These identities establish provenance;
they are not substitutes for the mathematical proof dependencies.
