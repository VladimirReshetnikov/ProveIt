WHAT THE DESIGN FAMILIES CHANGE

The oldest 194-state construction has the allocation 134 fixed controls + 67 axiom readers - 7 simultaneous contextual folds = 194. Its 68-reader/eight-fold alternative has the same final count. These are two different allocation solutions, not independent one-state savings that can simply be added.

The arithmetic/source variants change numerical constructor coefficients, the distribution-head premise order, the diagonal operand, successful MP's stack/cache behavior, the Shift contradiction guard, or the AX command's operand interface. The newer explicit-binder variants separate the generalization parameter c from the stored offset d. GD/GDH/GDHS additionally use the adjusted offset d+x for the diagonal head while retaining the original distribution order. AX11GDH adds the eleven-entry, four-operand AX interface to that independent-parameter design. Such variants require uniform syntactic proof translations and an all-store command compiler; a shorter macro word or a few tested examples do not establish calibration.

The H5X/JH5X packet makes a further mathematical change. H5X replaces U(D,E(H,D) -> 1) -> 1 by U(D,E(H,D) -> 1) -> A, with A independent of D and H. Setting A=1 recovers the original axiom, and the reverse direction uses a uniform explosion derivation. JH5X also changes the first head from (X -> 1) -> H to (X -> H) -> H, where X=U(D,U(D,H) -> 1). Its two-way syntactic certificates depend on the witness-head explosion bootstrap. These alterations reduce the AX word to 189 or 188 macro tokens, but their complete machines remain at 194 controls. The changed source has more primitive sites, illustrating why macro-token count alone is not the optimization objective.

The physical variants instead preserve the logical source while changing decoder association, known-zero returns, register placement, history selectors, constructor-loop continuations, or guarded local shortcuts. Their useful proof obligation is an exact finite-time transfer between literal machines, including initialization and intermediate times. A three-step parent trace may be replaced by one child step only when a predecessor invariant supplies the necessary neighboring tape bits. Conversely a dedicated helper may take three or five child steps to separate two control roles safely. Compositions of these mechanisms require a simultaneous check of their incoming contexts; independent local proofs cannot simply be counted as compatible state reductions.

INDEPENDENT CHECKS AND NOVELTY

A fresh, independent set-of-one-cells simulator checked all 17 principal/companion tables in the seven current incoming ZIPs. Each has exactly 194 graph-reachable working states, 388 transitions, dense start 0 and separate halt 194. At its reported initialization time each reaches native control 84 with head -2 and the same complete seed tape, including zero exterior. This is a finite whole-tape check, not a nonhalting proof. Exact hashes and startup durations are in incoming_principal_audit.csv and the replay is check_incoming.py.

For proposed new tables, novelty.py compares exact entire transition graphs against all 530 current representatives. It permits arbitrary working-state renaming and arbitrary root, global left/right reflection, global bit exchange, and erased terminal-entry write/move decoration. Bit exchange is used only to strengthen the structural novelty comparison; it does not preserve the fixed all-zero blank specification. Partition refinement merely prunes the search. Any positive match is checked on every edge, and a negative result exhausts the remaining finite mapping possibilities. This avoids treating a differing digest or differing numbering as a mathematical novelty proof.

REJECTED 193-STATE ENTRIES

A fresh independent C++ scalar simulator screened all 55 recognized spanning 193-control graphs from all 193 possible blank starts: 10,615 cases, with 112 finite halts, 10,499 certified outward blank self-loop rays and four trajectories capped at 100,000 transitions. A ray certificate consists of a finite reachable configuration, a zero-read self-loop, and an entirely blank half-line ahead of the head; the infinite continuation follows immediately, whether the loop writes zero or one behind it. The four capped trajectories remain unresolved by this simulation and belong to two already rejected mechanisms. One uses coefficient-three arithmetic with the collision E3(1,0)=I3(0,1)=6; its numerical calculus has an explicit contradiction certificate. The other miscompiles a constructor loop into a source cycle that increments two registers without decrementing its loop test. The current witness packet adds a 55th spanning 193-state table: its shorter-header JH5X runtime splice is explicitly rejected because it lacks a working blank initialization. None of these diagnoses is a lower bound of 194 for arbitrary machines.

PROOF STATUS

The current top-level PA README explicitly says the machine-specific Lean/iPRA endpoint for the primary PA194 development is unfinished. Later delivered manuscripts make an additional, different claim: a written iPRA calibration for revised AX11H, imported by the later GH/GHS, loop, GD, marker and witness constructions. These statements should not be conflated. A written theorem can exist before its proof-assistant implementation is complete; conversely a finite Python replay or a standard-natural-number Lean simulation does not by itself establish an object-theory iPRA derivation.

The inherited revised calibration archive is identified by SHA-256 93ee082ef035b2d32e71d6af5239b25b4bea1773f03bea4d2c39810a7fe15075. The report must identify its exact predicate and imported proof obligations, and distinguish the newly established relative machine theorem from the inherited arithmetic calibration. Neither Con(PA) itself nor a reflection principle is an assumption of the intended reduction. Rechecking an inherited archive's hash is an identity check, not a fresh reconstruction of all its syntactic proof compilers.

TRANSFERABLE IDEAS FROM THE THREE SUGGESTED PROJECTS

ZFC: the current README identifies K215's command-local field reader, command permutation, partial selector and compatible predecessor-bit folds as decisive physical savings. Those methods transfer as design techniques when the PA machine's actual rows, tape frames and source roles support them. K215's set-theoretic calculus cannot simply replace PA's calculus: the target here is Con(PA), so the proof-theoretic calibration must remain exact. The ZFC record also distinguishes an external exact-table Lean/Rocq theorem from an internal PA theorem; the PA comparison should retain the same distinction. Source: pinned Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/README.md, especially the K215 and K216 discussions.

DiophantineConsistency: its all-model reductions, native consistency-predicate binding, witness transformations and separately checked arithmetic circuits provide a useful proof architecture. A compact polynomial is not automatically a compact blank-tape machine: constants, witness enumeration, arbitrary-precision evaluation and initialization all have to be compiled into the state budget. No direct sub-194 route follows merely from the polynomial's variable or operation count. Source: pinned Computability/DiophantineConsistency/README.md, including its explicit native-iPRA endpoint and provenance qualifications.

ConciseIPRA: the native scalar core derives logic and equality from iteration, transport and closure rules; it offers candidates for smaller arithmetic proof-checking interfaces and for simpler metatheoretic verification. Its reported size metric is an entire standalone Python proof checker, whereas this task counts controls in a binary blank-start TM. Those metrics require a concrete compilation bridge. Further, substituting an iPRA proof search for a PA proof search would change the consistency statement; PA induction and its calibration still have to be represented. Source: pinned Logic/PrimitiveRecursiveArithmetic/ConciseIPRA/README.md, sections “The question” and “The native scalar core”.
