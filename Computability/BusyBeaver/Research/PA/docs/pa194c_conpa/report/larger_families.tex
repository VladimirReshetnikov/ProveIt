implementations, and diagnostics. The following taxonomy summarizes the
supplied mathematical arguments and implementation evidence; it does not
infer calibration from a state count. Source keys refer to the exact
archive locators below and their entries in Appendix~\ref{app:ledger}.

PA209 and U209\newline 209 states &
James Chang's PA209 has 3,948 primitive source sites; the U209 ancestor
used by the U208 packets is a different 3,909-site construction.
Their equal counts do not identify their source or their physical
controls. The published PA196 lineage already changes the quantifier
encoding, equality interface, head order, and contextual sharing
\cite{upstream,pa194}.\\[4pt]
U208\newline 208 states &
A finite-history quotient identifies old roles 172 and 211. Alternative
helpers give 20 designated, supported graph classes among 27 presentations;
this is a smaller set than all experimental 208-state graphs in the census.
The packets give local halting-equivalence arguments and inherited
calibration. Repeated entry/context/history proofs describe the same
one-state saving [L1].\\[4pt]
Two different 207 stories &
The U208 attempts include rejected 207-state frame witnesses; they are
not successful successors to U208. Separately, a repaired 157-node
representation of the PA196 source has a verified 207-state realization:
3,812 sites, 4,959 contracts, and exact startup. Lost contextual helpers
and additional routing make that smaller source DAG physically larger
[L2,L3].\\[4pt]
Integrated witness gate\newline $206\to203$ states &
A logical gate substitution removes 67 source sites, giving 3,745.
Both the initial 206-state and compressed 203-state realizations pass
4,872 source contracts and full startup checks. The packet supplies the
logical substitution argument, but its reader/routing allocation still
costs more than PA196 [L4].\\[4pt]
Published PA196 and\newline same-size physical forks &
The baseline has 3,812 sites and $36+13+147=196$ controls for the
engine, leaf routing, and readers. Reassociation, head ordering,
A/D register exchange, closing-decrement choices, and zero-jump routing
produce further 196-state implementations with distinct hypotheses.
Several closing variants edit the same row, so their savings cannot be
added. The explicit zero-jump invariant is another separate obligation
[L5].\\[4pt]
Gate/quantifier forks\newline 200, 199, 198, 196 &
The first diagonal-vacuity implementation has 3,700 sites and 199 states;
later association gives 198 and then 196. Distributed-gate compilation
has 3,773 sites and 198 states. Combining shorter negation and changed MP
with different gates yields additional 196--200 tables. The packets
separate finite implementation checks from the logical and command
translations needed for each composition [L6].\\[4pt]
Short negation\newline 197 states &
A direct-negation realization has 3,556 source sites. The later NEG18
macro reduces a separate-emitter source to 3,441 sites and 4,468 outcomes,
yet its final allocation is $134+68-5=197$. Its arithmetic macro and
scratch-register invariant are checked; losing useful shared readers
prevents the source saving from becoming a state saving [L3,L7].\\[4pt]
Changed Shift/MP\newline mostly 196 states &
H-only Shift alone has 3,811 sites. Combined Shift/MP has 3,788 sites,
4,928 uniform contracts, and exact startup. Crossing this controller
with diagonal/separate emitters gives 3,676/3,649 sites, initially at
196 states. Those fully checked constructions differ from earlier
MP-only experiments supported merely by bounded frame evidence;
two rejected Shift layouts actually halt at step 43,563 [L3,L4,L8].\\[4pt]
Diagnostics and\newline incomplete components &
A runtime-checked 198-state pruned source lacks its calibrated blank
startup; a parity-control component also has 198 states without a
complete endpoint. Short-scanner and optimistic reader quotients fail
specified frame contracts. Such tables remain in the census for
comparison, without being counted as established consistency machines
[L3,L5 and the rejected-frontier discussion].\\
