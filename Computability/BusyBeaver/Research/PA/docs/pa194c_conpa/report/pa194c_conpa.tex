\begin{abstract}
We compare the PA-consistency machine families present in the requested
ProveIt directories at commit
\texttt{768c2be1a8e4d2fcf8aa3443c7b0f084d145734c}.
The completed construction is a materially different binary, single-tape,
blank-start machine with 194 working states. It combines the 67-reader,
seven-fold AX decoder of PA194 with the cached-head Shift and modus-ponens
controller of 195C. The combination changes six native transition rows,
and implements a 3,649-site source program with 4,749 primitive outcomes.
Both a construction checker and an independent literal-tape symbolic
checker pass. The latter reconstructs its arithmetic-list lemmas from the
actual rows, checks arbitrary register values, and checks the exact
57,922-step blank startup. A syntactic consistency calibration and positive
finite-trace simulation establish the ordinary mathematical statement
\[
\PA\vdash\bigl(\neg\Halt(\M,\text{blank})\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
\]
The contribution is this checked composition and its corpus comparison;
the separate-head calculus, cached-head protocol, and decoder folds are
credited to their supplied sources. No new end-to-end Lean or Rocq endpoint
is claimed. The bounded reduction searches reported here do not establish
a smaller machine or a lower bound.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Three levels of evidence}

The report distinguishes the mathematical theorem, executable
certificates, and proof-assistant formalization. The mathematical proof
uses syntactic proof translations and finite-trace induction inside PA.
The supplied Python programs check literal table bindings, the source
graph, symbolic physical contracts, local folds, and startup. Those
programs are independently inspectable executable evidence. They are not
an exported Lean/Rocq theorem of the complete PA biconditional.

This distinction matters in the source repository: its PA194 and PA195
iPRA endpoints retain machine-specific reductions as hypotheses, whereas its
quotient developments prove ordinary finite-run statements. These are
valuable proof layers for the same table, but do not by themselves close
the PA-internal consistency equivalence~\cite{pareadme,upstream}.
The supplied PA194 and 195C manuscripts also contain ordinary mathematical
calibration arguments; their use here is explicit~\cite{pa194,pa195c}.

A failed certificate does not prove that the same table could have no
other interpretation with the desired blank-tape property. It does show
why the supplied failed construction cannot be counted as a success.
Likewise, negative search results apply to their stated rewrite families,
not to all Turing machines.

\begin{lemma}[Positive local quotient]
The folded table simulates each raw transition from an admissible entry
by a finite positive number of physical transitions, preserving the
complete tape and the projected endpoint.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An unchanged row commutes with the projection of targets. For a changed
row, the incoming context selects one of the four exhaustively checked
two-cell cases. A two-cell calculation is valid on every extension of
that window to a complete tape because its trace never reads outside
the window. The checked endpoint and positive duration prove the claim.
Induction concatenates these simulations along finite raw runs.
\end{proof}

The quotient lemma explains the sharing mechanism. The independent
whole-source check below also validates the literal final folded table,
so the physical conclusion does not depend solely on the allocator's
implementation of this argument.

\section{The cached-head command protocol}\label{sec:commands}

This section makes the semantic difference concrete and records the
proof compiler that justifies it~\cite{pa195c}. Define Cantor pairing
\[
p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b,\qquad [a;w]=p(a,w).
\]
The total projections satisfy $\operatorname{hd}[a;w]=a$ and
$\operatorname{tl}[a;w]=w$, also when $a=w=0$, where $[0;0]=0$.
Use $I(A,B)=4p(A,B)$ and $U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2$.
A command store is $\langle t,w,d,h\rangle$: current formula, coded stack,
residual counter, and cached head.

The new commands are
\[
\Shift\langle t,w,d,h\rangle=
\begin{cases}
\mathsf{halt},&h=1,\\
\langle h,\operatorname{tl}(w),d,\operatorname{hd}(w)\rangle,&h\ne1,
\end{cases}
\]
and, writing $y=d+x$,
\[
\MP_x\langle t,w,d,h\rangle=
\begin{cases}
\langle0,w,0,y\rangle,&I(t,y)=h,\\
\langle0,w,y,0\rangle,&I(t,y)\ne h.
\end{cases}
\]
Only Shift halts. A successful MP leaves the stack untouched and writes
the conclusion into $h$. This is why the unchanged old command compiler
cannot simply be assumed to work.

\subsection{AX, stack operations, and zero aliases}

The separate-head AX command pushes ten entries including the saved top:
\[
\AX_{c,x,y}\langle t,w,d,h\rangle
=\langle K(h+y,d+x,c),[q_1,\ldots,q_9,t;w],0,0\rangle.
\]
Here $q_1=\Gamma(c)$ is the separate diagonal head, the next six entries
are the six ordinary logical heads, $q_8$ is the dominated implicational
head, and $q_9=U(d,h)$. The top $K$ is the guarded gate, or zero if its
numeric guard fails. None of $K,q_1,\ldots,q_9$ equals 1: the gate and
the first eight heads have residue 0 modulo 4, and $q_9$ has residue 2.

The projection identities give two uniform, nonhalting computations:
\begin{align*}
\AX_{c,x,y};\Shift^9:
\langle t,w,d,h\rangle&\longmapsto
\langle q_8,[t;w],0,U(d,h)\rangle,\\
\AX_{c,x,y};\Shift^{10}:
\langle t,w,d,h\rangle&\longmapsto
\langle U(d,h),w,0,t\rangle.
\end{align*}
These words do not test the saved $t$, even when $t=1$.
Define
\begin{align*}
\mathsf{Push}&=\AX_{0,0,0};\Shift^9;\MP_0,\\
\mathsf{Pop}&=\AX_{0,0,0};\Shift^{11},\\
\mathsf{Discard}&=\MP_1;\AX_{1,0,0};\Shift^{10};\MP_0.
\end{align*}
Their contracts are
\begin{align*}
\mathsf{Push}\langle t,w,d,h\rangle&=\langle0,[t;w],0,0\rangle,\\
\mathsf{Pop}\langle t,[a;w],d,h\rangle&=
\begin{cases}\mathsf{halt},&t=1,\\\langle t,w,0,a\rangle,&t\ne1,
\end{cases}\\
\mathsf{Discard}\langle t,w,0,0\rangle&=\langle0,w,0,0\rangle.
\end{align*}
Push's final MP fails because an implication has residue 0 and the cached
$U$ has residue 2. Pop's last Shift tests precisely the preserved top.
For Discard, $I(t,1)>0$ forces the first MP to fail; after AX and the ten
Shifts, $I(U(1,0),0)>0$ forces the last MP to fail. These proofs include
the zero-stack alias and require no fictitious stored stack length.

\subsection{A proof compiler for the changed protocol}

Here are all selector families for the ten-entry layout, as proved in
\file{reference195C/independent_verification_composite_command_audit.tex}.
For $1\le j\le7$, the word
\[
\mathsf{Discard};\AX_{A,D,H};\Shift^{j+1};\mathsf{Pop}^{9-j}
\]
returns $q_j$ with the original underlying stack and zero caches. The
Shifts retrieve $q_j$; the Pops preserve it while discarding the remaining
generated entries and the saved zero. These Pops cannot halt because
$q_j\ne1$. Define $\mathsf{Offset}_0$ to be empty and
$\mathsf{Offset}_u=\MP_u$ for $u>0$. For $u\le a$, the word
\[
\mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Offset}_u;\AX_{b,a-u,c};
\Shift^9;\mathsf{Pop}
\]
returns the dominated head $\rho_8(a,b,c,u)$. The gate selector is
\[
\mathsf{Discard};\AX_{c,b,a};\mathsf{Pop}^{10},
\]
which returns $K(a,b,c)$, including zero on a failed guard. All three
contracts start with arbitrary top and stack and zero caches, and restore
the stack and zero caches. In particular, the diagonal head is selected by
\[
\mathsf{Discard};\AX_{B,0,0};\Shift^2;\mathsf{Pop}^8.
\]
It yields $\Gamma(B)$ with the underlying stack restored and both caches
zero, even when the gate guard fails. The generalization selector is
\[
\mathsf{Gen}_i=
\mathsf{Push};\mathsf{Offset}_i;\Shift;
\AX_{i,0,0};\Shift^{10},
\]
It sends $\langle a,w,0,0\rangle$ to
$\langle U(i,a),w,0,0\rangle$, including $a=1$.

The critical MP suffix is
\[
\mathsf{MPSuffix}_b=\Shift;\Shift;\MP_b;\Shift.
\]
It acts on $\langle0,[a,I(a,b),0;w],0,0\rangle$ by halting when
$a=1$ or $b=1$, and otherwise returning $\langle b,w,0,0\rangle$.
The first Shift retrieves $a$, the second tests it and retrieves $I(a,b)$,
MP writes $b$ into $h$, and the last Shift tests $b$ and retrieves the
dummy zero. Omitting that dummy zero would consume an entry of $w$.
The same numerical calculation works when the displayed zeros alias
the empty coded stack.

Compile an MP proof node with minor premise $a$, major premise $I(a,b)$,
and premise proof trees $p_0,p_1$ by
\[
W_p=\mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Push};W_{p_1};\mathsf{Push};
W_{p_0};\mathsf{Push};\mathsf{MPSuffix}_b.
\]
Axioms use the selectors and generalization uses $W_{p_0};\mathsf{Gen}_i$.
Induction on proof trees gives the following uniform invariant.

\begin{lemma}[Proof-word completeness]
For every proof tree $p$ of a numerical formula $A$ and every $t,w$,
$W_p$ from $\langle t,w,0,0\rangle$ either halts or returns
$\langle A,w,0,0\rangle$. If $p$ proves 1, the word
\[
\AX_{0,0,0};W_p;\mathsf{Pop}
\]
halts from every incoming command store.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The head selectors and generalization contract give the corresponding
induction cases. In the MP case, Discard and the first Push create the
dummy zero. The two premise computations and their Pushes establish the
suffix precondition; the suffix either halts or returns the conclusion.
An earlier halt is preserved by extending the word. The initial AX in
the displayed refutation word clears $d,h$, regardless of the incoming
top and stack. If its compiled proof has not already halted, Pop tests
the final top 1 before relying on the arbitrary underlying stack.
All operations and proof-tree constructions are primitive recursive.
\end{proof}

The inherited exhaustive command-word scheduler need not reset its store
between trials: the refutation word succeeds from every incoming store.
Its finite enumeration and decoding lemmas give an explicit finite
scheduler segment containing that word. This is the completeness
property used in the consistency argument, not a claim that arbitrary
trials leave the store empty.

\subsection{Binding the changed primitive loops}

The changed command tails have direct all-natural loop invariants. In
the 3,649-site source, the successful MP tail starts at ordinal 3,634.
It transfers $(D,H)=(y,0)$ to $(0,y)$ by a decrement/increment/return
loop, taking $3y+1$ primitive instructions to exit and one more to return
to fetch. The failure branch leaves $(D,H)=(y,0)$ and returns directly.
The new Shift starts at ordinal 3,638. Under its inherited scratch-entry
condition $X=0$, it clears $T$, copies $H$ into $T,X$, and restores $H$
from $X$. It reaches $(T,H,X)=(h,h,0)$ in $2t+7h+3$ instructions, then
sets $X=1$ and enters the unchanged guard and Cantor decoder. These
elementary loop calculations bind the stated command protocol to the
two altered source subtrees. The full source-graph comparison and
physical checks bind them further to the literal machine.

\section{PA-provable consistency calibration}\label{sec:calibration}

The calibration uses the same separate-head numerical calculus as 195S/C
and PA194. We recall its proof-producing content, because an external
statement that the machine never halts would not suffice for the user's
requested PA theorem~\cite{pa194,pa195c}.

\subsection{Numerical syntax and the diagonal clause}

In addition to $I,U$, set $M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1$,
$E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3$, and $N(B)=I(B,1)$. Zero is the designated truth code,
one is $M(0,0)$, and the $U$ arguments are transposed as defined above.
Conjunction and existential quantification have their classical
implicational definitions. The separate diagonal clause is
\[
\Gamma(B)=I(B,U(B,B)).
\]
All variable labels occurring in the decoded formula $B$ are strictly
below $B$. Thus $x_B$ is fresh in its own formula. Structural descent on
codes proves this, treating truth code 0 separately; the constructor
alias $I(0,0)=0$ is not an ordinary disjoint syntax case.

Write
\begin{align*}
V(a,b,c)&=M(a,b)\to U(c,M(a,b)),\\
P(a,b,c)&=c\to M(c,b),\\
S(a,b,c)&=U\bigl(b,(M(a,b)\to P(a,b,c))\wedge(M(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
Q(a,b,c)&=U(c,S(a,b,c)\to N(P(a,b,c))),\\
G(a,b,c)&=N(V(a,b,c)\to Q(a,b,c)).
\end{align*}
The AX top is $G(a,b,c)$ when $c\ne a,b$, and zero otherwise; the
separate $\Gamma(c)$ is emitted without that guard.
The six other logical heads are unchanged from the supplied PA calculus:
\begin{align*}
 &(\exists x_a\,U(a,C))\to C,\\
 &U(a,U(b,C))\to U(b,U(a,C)),\\
 &M(c,a)\to(E(c,b)\to M(b,a)),\\
 &E(c,a)\to(M(b,c)\to M(b,a)),\\
 &\exists x_a E(c,a),\\
 &U(a,b)\to\bigl(U(a,b\to c)\to U(a,c)\bigr).
\end{align*}
The dominated implicational head is
\[
 \rho_8(D,A,H,d)=((H\to d)\to D)\to((D\to H)\to(A\to H)),\qquad d\le D.
\]
The inference operations are modus ponens and generalization of
theorems. The free-algebra emitter audit retained with the package fixes
the exact parameter order and stack order of these heads.

The bundled gate
$N(\Gamma(c)\to(V(a,b,c)\to Q(a,b,c)))$ and the pair
$\Gamma(c),G(a,b,c)$ are interderivable by fixed classical implicational
proof templates. Every diagonal instance $\Gamma(B)$ is available from
a bundled gate by taking $c=B$, $a=b=B+1$. Consequently the bundled and
separate-head presentations have primitive-recursive proof translations.

\subsection{PA refutations yield numerical refutations}

The inherited PA-refutation compiler uses two-way membership rewriting,
neighborhood equality, and an equality witness. The supplied diagonal
calculus reconstructs the interfaces that formerly used
equality-vacuity. For a reverse rewrite, put
\[
C=E(a,b)\to(\chi(b)\to\chi(a)),\qquad
\varphi=N(C),\qquad c=\ulcorner\varphi\urcorner.
\]
Here $\chi$ is a membership atom with the indicated argument in either
slot, or the diagonal membership case. Code descent provides freshness.
Pointed separation, the retained equality witness, direct membership
rewriting, and membership-only vacuity derive
$\varphi\to(S\to N(P))$. Generalization at $c$, discharged using
$\Gamma(\varphi)$, gives $\varphi\to Q$, while the separation instance
provides $N(Q)$. Classical double-negation elimination gives $C$.
Coincident labels are handled by the corresponding propositional cases.

For neighborhood equality, first use the fresh label
$z_0=\ulcorner E(a,b)\urcorner$, the instance $\Gamma(E(a,b))$, and the
two-way rewrites. The equality witness and membership-only vacuity then
transport the binder to any required $z\ne a,b$. These are finite
proof templates with primitive-recursive code construction and label
comparisons. They supply the original compiler interfaces, and their
correctness can be proved in PA by induction on the source proof.
Together with Section~\ref{sec:commands} and the scheduler, this gives a
PA-verifiable translation from a PA refutation to a source halt.

\subsection{Source halts yield PA refutations}

Translate $M(a,b)$ to finite binary membership
$\operatorname{Bit}(x_a,x_b)$, $E(a,b)$ to equality, and $U(a,B)$ to
universal quantification of the translated formula. One arithmetic
definition is
\[
\operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\equiv
\exists u,v\,[s=(2u+1)2^z+v\ \wedge\ v<2^z].
\]
Exponentiation can be given its standard definitional arithmetic
representation. PA proves $\operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\to z<s$, hence the
negation of the translation of code 1. The alias $I(0,0)=0$ is handled by
a fixed proof of truth's equivalence to truth implying truth.

For each arithmetic formula $\theta(z,\vec u)$, PA proves finite binary
comprehension:
\[
\forall n\,\exists s<2^n\,\forall z<n\,
 (\operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\leftrightarrow\theta(z,\vec u)).
\]
Induct on $n$, retaining the previous code when $\neg\theta(n,\vec u)$
and adding $2^n$ when $\theta(n,\vec u)$. Substitution of the syntax of
$\theta$ into this fixed induction-proof pattern is a primitive-recursive
proof supplier, whose correctness PA verifies syntactically. This uses
neither a truth predicate for arbitrary arithmetic formulas nor a
reflection principle for PA.

For the separation gate, the finite matrix when $a\ne b$ is
\[
(\operatorname{Bit}(x_a,z)\lor z=x_b)\wedge T(c)[z/x_b],
\]
with a bound such as $x_a+x_b+1$. When $a=b$, the correct matrix is
$(z=x_b)\wedge T(c)[z/x_b]$. The binder then changes both occurrences
of the coincident label, and its other antecedent is
$\operatorname{Bit}(z,z)$, which PA refutes. Keeping this collision case
separate avoids an invalid free-variable argument. The remaining heads
have fixed first-order proof suppliers, and the diagonal head uses the
proved code-freshness fact.

Annotate the current formula $t$, cached head $h$, and every live canonical
stack entry with a PA proof of its translation. The canonical stack
representation explicitly handles $p(0,0)=0$: popping coded zero supplies
the truth proof, and pushing zero onto zero creates no spurious live cell.
AX supplies the head proofs. Successful MP combines the proofs of $t$
and $I(t,y)$ and places the resulting proof with the new cache $y$.
Failed MP supplies truth proofs for the cleared formulas. Shift moves
the annotations and can halt only at $h=1$, which therefore carries a
proof of the translated contradiction. Induction over any finite command
trace constructs a PA refutation from a halt.

\begin{proposition}[Syntactic source calibration]
Let $R_C$ be the cached-head source program, with its exact blank-derived
initial store. The proof constructions above are arithmetizable in PA and
give
\[
\PA\vdash\bigl(\Halt(R_C)\leftrightarrow
\exists p\,\Prf_{\PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner)\bigr).
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The forward implication is the proof-annotation transformation. The reverse
implication is the PA-refutation translation, the corrected cached-head
proof-word compiler, and the exhaustive scheduler. These are syntactic
transformations of finite objects. PA proves their correctness by the
induction and finite arithmetic arguments described above. In particular,
neither direction assumes that PA is sound or consistent.
\end{proof}

\section{Physical correctness and the final theorem}\label{sec:physical}

\subsection{Universal source frames}

Each source site has a binary path $p$. Its clean tape frame contains that
path, a following zero gap of length $48+n-|p|$, a marker, nine unary
register blocks, and a finite nonempty list of spare blocks. Here $n$ and
the nine register values are independent arbitrary natural numbers, and
the spare-list values are arbitrary naturals. A register value $r$ is
represented by a block of $r+1$ ones following its separator.

The logical-to-physical register permutation is
\[
(6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1).
\]
It is unchanged from the principal PA194. All increment outcomes and
both decrement outcomes are verified, together with jumps and halts.
The independent source resolver computes the graph by two methods and
checks equality with the published 195C instruction list. Merely keeping
the leaf word would not have justified relative break/continue targets.

The independent checker reconstructs four generic list-sweep lemmas
directly from the supplied literal rows. It then symbolically executes
every source outcome. Durations are nonnegative affine expressions in
the symbolic sizes with strictly positive constants. Thus these checks
cover arbitrary register values and spare-list lengths, rather than a
finite sample of concrete runs.

\subsection{Exact blank initialization}

The full blank run reaches native state 84, head position $-2$, at source
site 25 after 57,922 transitions. The logical register vector is
$(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$, with 26 spare values equal to 1 and header distance
48. Both checkers compare the entire tape, including its zero exterior,
with the prescribed source frame. One uses a finite-support set and the
other a bounded byte array with explicit bounds checks. The complete
nonzero support and source path are recorded in
\file{verification/independent/recorded_C/PA194C/blank_seed.json}.

The startup is 232 transitions shorter than that of principal PA194.
This is a startup comparison only; it does not claim uniform speedup on
all subsequent computations.

\begin{lemma}[Finite-trace transport inside PA]
PA proves that $\M$ halts from blank tape exactly when $R_C$ halts from
the specified source seed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The exact finite startup establishes the first source frame. Every source
instruction has a checked, positive-duration physical simulation to the
next frame or to halt. The finite symbolic certificates are equations
and elementary list/arithmetic inductions; their durations are primitive
recursive in the frame data. PA can verify the finite table equalities
and prove these frame lemmas by the indicated inductions. Induction on
source length concatenates them, proving that a source halt produces a
physical halt.

Conversely, suppose a physical halt occurs at time $T$. If the first
$T+1$ source steps were all nonhalting, their simulated endpoint times
would be strictly increasing natural numbers. They would therefore carry
the unique deterministic physical run beyond time $T$ without halting,
a contradiction. The necessary source prefixes and durations are finite,
primitive-recursively coded objects. This bounded argument is provable
in PA and avoids an appeal to an external infinite run. The fixed
native/dense conjugacy transfers the statement to the delivered labels.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The delivered PA consistency machine]
For the literal dense table in Appendix~\ref{app:table},
\[
\boxed{\PA\vdash\bigl(\neg\Halt(\M,\mathrm{blank})
           \leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose finite-trace transport with the syntactic source calibration,
then negate the existential PA-refutation predicate. This is an ordinary
mathematical proof of PA-provability. The supplied executable checks bind
its finite data to the literal table; an end-to-end proof-assistant
export is further work.
\end{proof}

\section{Search for fewer states}\label{sec:search}

\input{search_summary.tex}

The grammar searches begin from the actual accepted PA194 grammar.
For promising scores, their cost includes the maximum simultaneously
compatible set of history folds within the implemented two-cell family. The original
controller and the cached-head controller are searched separately,
because their fixed rows provide different possible helper detours.
Global DAG reassociations preserve sharing; a second mutation mode
rotates individual occurrences and can introduce a different sharing
pattern. Scanner penalties account for maximum zero runs and transient
increment paths. Any promising numerical score still requires exact
source-graph and universal physical verification.

The local-control searches allow existing-control detours and include
arithmetic controls in their broad finite-window screening. A separate
incoming-edge transformation attempts to remove a control by replacing
its incoming rows with simulated two-step blocks, while avoiding every
edited row. The whole-source quotient search is complementary: it checks
proposed quotients at source boundaries rather than requiring a short
local window. Each scope and step bound is recorded in the search
receipts; none is a general minimality result.

\section{Ideas transferred from ZFC and concise iPRA}

The useful transfer from the ZFC work is methodological and physical:
source reassociation, use of already written marker bits as finite-control
memory, simultaneous validation of contextual folds, and whole-tape
symbolic contracts. The independent source-language and physical-checking
components retain their upstream K225/K226 and U214 provenance. The ZFC
repository itself emphasizes that short source expressions do not alone
price a complete initialized machine~\cite{zfcreadme}.

One cannot substitute a ZFC-consistency machine for a PA-consistency
machine just because its compiler is compact: the calibration target is
different. Likewise, the concise-iPRA project measures complete checker
implementations and provides unusually economical calculi, but a small
iPRA checker is not automatically a small PA-refutation search machine.
Its parser, term evaluator, rule dispatch, arithmetic representation,
enumeration, and initialization must all be priced in the binary tape
model~\cite{iprareadme}. The present construction retains the established
PA calibration and applies the reusable compiler ideas where their
physical cost can be checked.

\section{Reproduction, limitations, and further questions}

\subsection{Reproduction}

Python 3.10 or later with its standard library is sufficient for the
delivered construction and both executable checks. Run from the package
root, with assertions enabled:
\begin{lstlisting}
python3 -B verify_release.py
python3 -B construction/build194C.py
python3 -B verification/independent/check194C.py
\end{lstlisting}
The first command checks the shipped hashes and stable receipt fields.
The second reconstructs the machine and its 195C parent; the third
checks the literal output independently. Regenerating evidence may
rewrite receipts, so run the integrity check before editing the package.
No network connection or repository checkout is needed for these checks.
The optional search tools require C++17 and are separate from verification.

Rebuild the report with
\begin{lstlisting}
python3 report/build_report.py
\end{lstlisting}
from the package root. This runs pdfLaTeX three times; a standard TeX Live
installation is required. Source references and imported licenses are retained.

\subsection{Precise limitations}

The completed machine uses 194 working states. The work establishes no
strictly smaller calibrated machine and no lower bound at 194. Its
novelty is relative to the pinned supplied corpus, not a claim about all
unpublished designs. The mathematical PA theorem depends on the
explicit inherited numerical-calculus and scheduler arguments, together
with the physical transport proved here. The source repository's open
iPRA endpoints remain open; no claim about them is implied by this
ordinary PA proof.

\subsection{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Can a scanner-valid allocation achieve at most 59 effective AX
readers with 134 fixed controls? For example, $66-7$ or $67-8$ would yield
193 states. Compatibility must be checked after all folds are applied.
\item Can a controller-specific source invariant justify a reduction that
the arbitrary-tape window abstractions necessarily miss, while retaining
an elementary inductive certificate?
\item Can the cached-head protocol share a return path or a leaf router
with an AX reader, saving a fixed control without losing a useful fold?
\item Can the guarded separation emitter use a cheaply constructed fresh
label exceeding all parameters and eliminate its freshness guard? A
logical reformulation is useful only if the repeated construction and
storage of the new label costs fewer physical controls.
\item Can the successful frame certificates and syntactic proof suppliers
be turned into a single compiled PA-internal Lean endpoint for this exact
table? The intermediate quotient theorem and a conditional arithmetic
endpoint should remain distinct until those obligations are closed.
\end{enumerate}
