\section{The inherited PA calibration and its proof boundary}
\label{sec:inherited-calibration-audit}

The consistency calibration is not inferred from a long nonhalting run
or from an external standard-model theorem. Its relevant content is two
uniform syntactic proof transformations, one from PA refutations to
refutations in the searched numerical calculus and one in the reverse
direction. Their correctness is provable in PA. The imported PA196
formal development exports the internal direction from literal
nonhalting to PA consistency. The other direction for the later
diagonal/separate-head machines is supplied by the ordinary syntactic
argument in the PA194 packet, summarized here with its essential side
conditions.

\subsection{The diagonal replacement and the completeness interfaces}

Numerical formulas use
\[
 I(A,B)=4p(A,B),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,\quad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
Code zero is a special truth code, and code one is the contradiction
$M(0,0)$. Every variable label in the formula decoded from $B$ is
strictly below $B$, with code zero treated separately. Hence
\[
                       \Gamma(B)=B\to U(B,B)
\]
is a valid vacuity clause. The retained guarded gate supplies membership
vacuity and pointed separation. It does not by itself supply unrestricted
primitive equality vacuity.

The replacement proof recovers precisely the equality interfaces used by
the PA-refutation compiler. For reverse rewriting, first take $a\ne b$
and let $\chi(t)$ be a membership atom with $t$ in either slot, or
$M(t,t)$. When a second fixed label is present, first treat it as distinct
from $a$. Define
\[
 C=E(a,b)\to(\chi(b)\to\chi(a)),\qquad
 \varphi=\neg C,\qquad c=\ulcorner\varphi\urcorner.
\]
The strict code bounds ensure every required freshness guard. Pointed
separation at $(a,a,c)$ gives $\neg Q$, with point condition
$P=\varphi\to M(c,a)$ and separation matrix $S$. The second half of
that matrix, weakened propositionally by
$\varphi\to\neg\chi(a)$, gives
\[
 S\to U(a,M(c,a)\to\neg\chi(a)).
\]
The inherited negative-specialization lemma uses only the retained
equality witness, direct membership rewriting, and membership-only
vacuity. It therefore yields
\[
                   S\to(M(c,b)\to\neg\chi(b)).
\]
Under $\varphi$, the point condition and direct rewriting yield
$M(c,b)$, whereas $\varphi$ also yields $\chi(b)$. Thus
$\varphi\to(S\to\neg P)$ is derivable. Generalizing this theorem at
$c$ and using $\Gamma(\varphi)$ pays for generalization under the
assumption $\varphi$, producing $\varphi\to Q$. Together with
$\neg Q$, classical reasoning gives $C$. The cases $a=b$ and a repeated
fixed label use propositional reasoning and the already obtained
diagonal membership case.

For neighborhood equality, choose
$z_0=\ulcorner E(a,b)\urcorner$. Then $z_0>a,b$, and
$\Gamma(E(a,b))$ permits lifting the two-way membership rewrites to
the neighborhood formula with binder $z_0$. To replace $z_0$ by any
$z\ne a,b$, use the equality witness and membership-only vacuity to
specialize the two membership matrices, then generalize at $z$.
The antecedents of this binder transport contain only membership and
are vacuous at the new label. This supplies neighborhood equality and
its existential witness without assuming the removed primitive schema.

The directly inspected PA196 source modules use primitive equality
vacuity at the reverse-rewriting and neighborhood steps, with corresponding
arithmetic certificate copies. Later modules use the derived neighborhood
interface. The displayed replacements therefore address the required
dependency. All chosen codes, numerical case splits, and concatenated
proof templates are primitive recursive; their syntactic correctness
is established by ordinary induction in PA.

Separating $\Gamma(c)$ from the gate changes the stack push count from
nine to ten. The cached protocol's explicitly adjusted proof-word
compiler accounts for that change; a nine-entry selector is not silently
reused. Thus a PA refutation yields a numerical refutation, then a
command word that halts from every incoming store, and finally a halt
of the exhaustive source scheduler.

\subsection{A uniform reverse translation into PA}

Interpret numerical membership by finite binary membership
\[
 \operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\equiv
 \exists u,v\,[s=(2u+1)2^z+v\ \wedge\ v<2^z],
\]
primitive equality by arithmetic equality, and $U(a,B)$ by
quantification of the arithmetic translation $T(B)$ at its corresponding
source label. PA proves
$\operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\to z<s$, so it proves
$\neg\operatorname{Bit}(x_0,x_0)$, the negation of the translated false
code. Auxiliary bound variables are chosen capture-avoidably; numerical
source labels may coincide and may shadow one another.

The one numerical constructor alias is $I(0,0)=0$. Its translation is
handled by the fixed equivalence of truth with truth implying truth.
Thus the uniform constructor statement is a PA proof of
$T(I(A,B))\leftrightarrow(T(A)\to T(B))$, not an assertion that the
syntax trees are identical in every case.

For arbitrary arithmetic formula syntax $\theta(z,\vec u)$, a uniform
proof-producing procedure supplies a PA proof of
\[
 \forall n\,\exists s<2^n\,\forall z<n\,
      (\operatorname{Bit}(s,z)\leftrightarrow\theta(z,\vec u)).
\]
The proof is induction on $n$. At the next bit, use $s+2^n$ if
$\theta(n,\vec u)$ and use $s$ otherwise. The formula is substituted
into one fixed induction-proof pattern. PA verifies that each resulting
line is an axiom instance or follows from its predecessors. The procedure
never evaluates $\theta$; this is not an appeal to an arithmetic truth
predicate or to a reflection principle.

For a guarded gate at $(a,b,c)$, with $c\ne a,b$, put $\phi=T(c)$.
When $a\ne b$, finite comprehension uses the matrix
\[
 (\operatorname{Bit}(x_a,z)\lor z=x_b)\wedge\phi[z/x_b]
\]
and a bound such as $x_a+x_b+1$. When the \emph{labels} satisfy $a=b$,
the first antecedent under the gate's binder is
$\operatorname{Bit}(z,z)$ and is false. The correct matrix in that case is
\[
                         (z=x_b)\wedge\phi[z/x_b].
\]
This separate case prevents an incorrect treatment of the bound
occurrence of $x_a$ as a fixed parameter. Code freshness ensures that
the witness label $c$ does not occur free in $\phi$. These witnesses give
pointed separation. Membership vacuity, the diagonal clause, and all
remaining logical heads have uniform first-order proof suppliers.

Translate a numerical proof recursively by these suppliers, ordinary
modus ponens, and generalization. At a proof of one, append the fixed
PA proof that its translation is false. Equivalently, annotate the
source command store with PA proofs of its top, cached head, and each
canonical live stack entry. Empty-stack pops use the fixed truth proof;
pushing zero onto zero retains the empty annotation. AX, MP, and Shift
preserve this invariant, and a halting Shift yields a PA refutation.

Every operation is a primitive-recursive transformation of syntax or
finite lists, and PA proves its correctness uniformly in input codes.
This uniformity is the step that makes the argument apply in all PA
models, including their nonstandard coded proofs; merely displaying
one proof for each standard input would not suffice. Combining the two
translations and the positive source-to-tape simulation gives the
ordinary PA-provable consistency equivalence asserted in the report.
The new literal endpoint is not claimed to have a newly compiled
proof-assistant kernel theorem.
