\begin{abstract}
This investigation does not establish a machine with fewer than 194 working
states. It supplies two new, mutually nonisomorphic 194-state binary,
single-tape machines starting on the all-zero tape, with complete transition
tables, deterministic builders, independent symbolic certificates, and an
explicit relative proof in intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic.
Their absolute PA-consistency corollary invokes the precisely identified
written AX11H/GH calibration supplied in the repository. No completed
end-to-end Lean theorem is claimed.

The comparison covers all 718 tracked PA transition-table files, the six
incoming archives and their distinct nested archives, and 11,847 JSON
occurrences. It identifies 139 spanning 194-state table classes, including
research diagnostics, and separates these from 54 spanning 193-state
research classes. The new machines match none of the 139 classes, even
under the strengthened graph comparison described below.

A separate mathematical result repairs radix-three formula coding by
placing the membership/equality tag in the parity of one Cantor operand.
Primitive-recursive inverse permutations, acting on formula codes and
variable labels together, yield proof translations in both directions
over iPRA. This resolves the diagonal and gate interfaces without assuming
the recoding is monotone. Its present compiled implementation does not
improve the 194-state bound.
\end{abstract}

\section{What is proved and what is supplied}

Both delivered machines use the same comparison convention as the PA
research: alphabet $\{0,1\}$, a two-way infinite tape initially all zero,
head initially at coordinate zero, and one separate halting control.
Every working-state/read-symbol pair has exactly one transition, which
writes one bit and moves one cell left or right. There are no stationary
moves, external inputs, or externally prepared tape segments. Dense
working labels are $0,\ldots,193$; the initial label is zero and terminal
label 194 is excluded from the count.

The main concrete contribution is a pair of new compositions of existing
loop-continuation and physical-acceleration ideas. The constituent
reductions are attributed to their supplied packets \cite{gh,lc}. Their simultaneous
application, complete physical verification, explicit common clock bound,
and comparison with the full current frontier are carried out here.
The changes affect recurring execution: 305 source addresses and 61
constructor-loop occurrences change layout, while two decoder paths
execute in one step instead of three. The resulting tables are different
from all previously supplied comparison graphs, rather than merely
different numberings of them.

The proof has three layers, whose scopes should remain separate:
\begin{enumerate}
\item A fresh exact-table construction and independent literal-row replay
      verify both new physical implementations.
\item A written quantifier-free induction argument establishes relative
      iPRA halting equivalence with their GH/GHS parents, with the explicit
      witness bound $\Theta(t)=13t^2+715t+58\,624$.
\item The absolute statement
      $\ipra\vdash\NH(M)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)$ follows by invoking
      the supplied written GH/GHS calibration, whose underlying AX11H
      archive is pinned by its complete hash. That large logical
      calibration is an imported mathematical dependency, not a new
      proof-assistant build performed here.
\end{enumerate}

The archive includes the complete editable \LaTeX{} manuscript and compiled
PDF; both complete dense/native tables and their bijections; parent inputs;
construction and independent verification programs; all branch certificates;
a representative of every one of the 139 historical 194-state classes;
exact novelty and provenance checkers; the new recoding proof and checks;
and the completed reduction-search records. Its top-level README gives
the entry points. All fresh conclusions are tied to exact table bytes.

\section{The absolute consistency corollary and its dependency}
\label{sec:absolute}

The new relative theorem is independent of any consistency assumption.
To obtain the requested absolute conclusion, invoke the supplied GH/GHS
written calibration \cite{gh,calibration}, whose chain is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \text{literal GH/GHS table}
 &\longleftrightarrow \text{register source}
 \longleftrightarrow \text{scheduler}\\
 &\longleftrightarrow \mathcal C_4\text{ refutation}
 \longleftrightarrow \text{Foundation PA refutation}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The exact underlying AX11H calibration archive retained here has SHA-256
\begin{center}\small\ttfamily
93ee082ef035b2d32e71d6af5239b25b4bea1773f03bea4d2c39810a7fe15075.
\end{center}
Its own ProveIt source pin is
\texttt{1c9524d8bf2e0a27b9a16ff622359927f536e663}; the Foundation
submodule pin is
\texttt{32e1a0956a8622fad067328ca1959729a7634428}.
The endpoint is its explicitly mapped \texttt{nativePAConsistency}, with
the current Foundation proof encoding and PA/PAeq adapter identified.
It is not obtained by silently equating Foundation's square-pair falsity
code 13 with the numerical calculus's contradiction code 1.

\begin{corollary}[Invoking the pinned written calibration]
For either of the two delivered machines, composition of the relative theorem
with the cited written GH/GHS theorem gives
\[
 \ipra\vdash\NH(M)\longleftrightarrow\Con(\PA).
\]
The conclusion has the same identified mathematical dependency as the
parent calibration. It is not a newly completed Lean or Rocq endpoint.
\end{corollary}

The focused review in \file{research/calibration_audit.md} checked the
interfaces most likely to hide an invalid strengthening of the base
theory: decreasing-rank finite-demand recursion, supplier stratification,
current-Foundation induction witnesses, all-store scheduler compilation,
positive physical clocks, and the reverse witness argument. The written
proof uses bounded Boolean prefix invariants rather than an unbounded
``for all environments'' induction. Growing output proof codes are handled
by explicit primitive recursion; they are not assumed bounded by the
input code. The physical reverse witness uses absorbing halting and a
positive-duration prefix at index $t+1$, avoiding Markov's principle.

No blocking defect was found in these reviewed arguments. This review
does not amount to fresh implementation of the full PA-proof compiler,
independent verification of every retained recipe, or a proof-assistant
kernel check of the entire chain. Those limits apply to the inherited
dependency, while the new literal checks and relative theorem have the
specific independently documented scope above. The maintained formal PA
README and later model-level progress notes still leave the complete
physical/calculus/compiler assembly open.

\section{Sub-194 reduction attempts and their precise outcomes}
\label{sec:attempts}

\subsection{Guarded physical contraction after the F acceleration}

A fresh finite-window abstraction was computed for the accelerated GH
table. At radius seven it reaches 912,456 abstract windows. Exposed
boundary bits are nondeterministic, so every true blank-run window is
included. For each row the search looked for a single transition that
reproduces a positive prefix of at most 161 old steps, with the same
entire window, control, and net unit head displacement. It found no
additional shortcut row and therefore no deletion in that template.

A second run used radius six, a 200-step prefix bound, and the existing
complete source/startup envelope of 30,818 windows for 171 non-arithmetic
controls. Arithmetic-box controls retained the broader abstract windows.
It likewise found no additional shortcut. The stronger source envelope
is used only as a search screen, not substituted for a new complete
proof of an accepted table. The actual programs, input tables, bounds,
and output logs are in \file{research/physical/}.

\subsection{Two pairs sharing one history selector}

The new joint-fold template tries to merge two homogeneous-history pairs
while sharing one selector. The current cell temporarily stores a bit
identifying the pair; the immediately preceding cell identifies which
member of that pair was entered. A provider then restores the original
cell and reproduces the correct old action. Four original controls would
be replaced by two merged controls and one selector, saving one state.

The finite search examines all sixteen eligible pairs in each selected
table, both temporary tags, all existing provider controls, and bounded
whole-window recovery paths. The primary table was checked at radius
seven with a 200-step provider bound. Accelerated GH was checked at
radius six with 120 steps, then with the narrower complete source/startup
envelope and 200 steps. No joint-provider nomination survived. The
generic fold criterion remains useful: ordinary read-only decoder rows
cannot restore a changed tag, so a successful construction needs suitable
destructive helpers or a new shared recovery path. This is a restricted
negative result, not a lower bound on 193-state machines.

\subsection{Repairing the inherited 193-state runtime}

The inherited Runtime193 splice merges native controls 301 and 88 and
changes the marker return. It passes its source contracts on complete
prepared frames but lacks a valid blank initializer. The corrected
individual-edit pool contains 327 proposals; earlier work screened
eligible pairs and triples. This investigation extended the search to
adaptive sets of four through sixteen encountered-row edits.

The detector requires an entire finite-support frame at native control
84 for a source site 25--44, with the marker, address, gap, nine unary
registers, a nonempty spare tail, and blank exterior all checked. It
accepts arbitrary register values at this nomination stage; a hit would
still need sound-store and simultaneous source verification. A positive
control recovered the primary's exact site-25 frame at 58,154 steps.

\subsection{A failed rho simplification and a viable recoding}

Setting the restricted propositional parameter $u=D$ looks attractive
because it removes an independently supplied operand. It does not by
itself provide a complete implicational basis. A three-valued matrix with
designated value 2 and implication table
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 \Rightarrow&0&1&2\\\hline
 0&1&1&2\\
 1&1&2&2\\
 2&1&1&2
\end{array}
\]
validates every specialized rho instance and modus ponens, while
$p\to p$ is not designated when $p=0$. The supplied finite checker
reproduces this obstruction. It concerns that proposed standalone basis;
it is not a proof that the complete numerical calculus could not recover
the missing logic from additional schemas.

The tagged radix-three encoding of the preceding section is a viable
alternative. Its inverse is explicit and its proof translation deals
with the actual diagonal, gate, and numerical domination guards. Its
physical price must nevertheless be measured after rebuilding the
source reader and folds. Adding a shared doubling macro initially gave
an optimistic price of 210. A 300-second grammar search then proposed
3,150,735 changes and accepted 203,239, reaching an optimistic price of
203. The optimistic disjoint matching bound was fourteen folds;
simultaneous compatibility checking permits only ten. The actual rebuilt
candidate has
\[
 137+80-10=207\quad\text{working controls}.
\]
It passes a free-algebra check of all eleven AX outputs, every intermediate
scratch precondition, all 4,831 construction-side source contracts, and
the complete 57,922-step blank startup. This research candidate has not
received the separate literal verifier used for the two 194-state
deliverables; neither 203 nor 210 is an accepted physical result.
The final cost and validation status are recorded separately under
\file{research/tagged3_pricing/}. The mathematical recoding theorem does
not assert that a smaller abstract constructor coefficient automatically
means fewer physical states.

The tagged-two source prototype has an optimistic price of 206, but
fails an early source contract: a changed constructor control also
served an inherited decoder-fold path. It is retained under
\file{research/tagged2_pricing/} as an explicitly unvalidated experiment.

\section{Connections with the other requested research trees}

The ZFC work \cite{zfc} supplies the most directly applicable implementation ideas:
contextual marker folds, partial selectors, command-local field readers,
and explicit blank-start validation. Its own reports emphasize that
folds can consume the same source roles and cannot simply be added.
The PA Runtime193 failure is an example of precisely this interface
problem. The ZFC tree also distinguishes an external checked machine
equivalence from an internal theorem in a particular arithmetic base;
that distinction is retained here. The tagged recoding theorem uses the
same useful lesson as the dominated-rho work: an order-restricted schema
can be transported by expanding a proof of its unrestricted form.

DiophantineConsistency \cite{dio} contributes the discipline of explicit
primitive-recursive witness maps and a fixed refutation predicate. A
small polynomial circuit or a low-variable equation does not directly
give a smaller two-symbol blank-start TM: the enumeration, arithmetic,
coefficient data, and initialization must all be compiled and counted.
The present relative theorem follows that witness-map discipline, with
an explicit quadratic bound on physical halting witnesses.

ConciseIPRA \cite{ipra} contributes compact proof-checker and rule-interface ideas,
including derived logical structure and the separation between a native
calculus and its ordinary interpretation. Its native scalar core is
calibrated to iPRA; replacing the PA refutation target by that weaker
consistency problem would change the task. A useful transfer would need
a real PA-calibrated presentation and a measured binary implementation.
The self-indexed inverse recoding above is a concrete way of changing
numerical presentation while retaining the exact consistency target.

\section{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Can operand tagging be fused into operand loading?}
      The first tagged-three implementation inserts a doubling routine.
      Doubling a parameter during its existing copy into the theorem
      register may retain more shared source subtrees and avoid separate
      helper controls. The optimization target is the final fixed plus
      reader minus fold count, under simultaneous physical validation.
\item \textbf{Can a two-residue syntax share its operand preparation?}
      The explicitly stated tagged-two variant moves a constructor bit
      into the first Cantor operand for every constructor. A common
      constructor-specific pairing routine may amortize that work.
      Scheduler and proof-stack pairing still need their own exact
      interfaces. No smaller table for this variant is claimed.
\item \textbf{Can blank initialization and the runtime splice be
      synthesized together?} The corrected neutral-edit pool limits
      the possible changes before search begins. A useful successor
      should permit coupled non-neutral edits while enforcing both
      complete-frame arrival and all source contracts. More bounded
      startup simulation alone does not close those two obligations.
\item \textbf{Can a tag-restoring helper serve several folds?}
      The shared-selector template isolates the missing operation:
      restore a temporary current-cell tag while dispatching according
      to the history bit. A suitable existing arithmetic helper, or a
      helper introduced once for several groups, could give a net state
      saving despite the present bounded screen.
\item \textbf{Can the large written calibration become executable?}
      Implement the complete derivation-list compiler, then formalize
      the finite-demand recursion theorem once and instantiate its
      strata. This would strengthen the absolute dependency of every
      machine in the AX11H family without conflating finite Python
      checks with the end-to-end iPRA theorem.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Reproduction, provenance, and references}

Run \file{python3 verify_all.py} with ordinary Python 3.10 or
later and assertions enabled. The full run reconstructs both tables in a
temporary copy, checks frozen output identities, repeats independent
literal verification, audits the common clock envelope, and compares
both exact graphs against the frozen frontier. It also checks the
universal 68,268-row rho template, the concrete guard certificate, and
the finite recoding and propositional-matrix checks. C++17 is needed only for
the optional negative-search replays. A normal pdf\LaTeX{} installation
rebuilds this manuscript with two passes from \file{report/}.

The four generic list lemmas and every branch outcome are retained,
including exact affine durations. The independent checks quantify nine
arbitrary natural registers, the affine nonnegative gap, and an arbitrary
nonempty finite spare list; they are not a test on a fixed finite range
of small register values. The recorded computational operation totals
are verification-work counts, while startup totals are literal TM steps.
The distinction is preserved in every receipt.

Original license and attribution files accompany the imported builders,
runtime engines, and proof dependencies. No remote repository was
modified in this investigation. Snapshot hashes and original ZIP hashes
permit the provenance to be checked even after the repository moves
