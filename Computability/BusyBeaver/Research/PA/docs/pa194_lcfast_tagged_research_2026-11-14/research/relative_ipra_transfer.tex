\section{An explicit relative-transfer theorem in intuitionistic PRA}
\label{sec:mathaudit-relative}

A new transition table should first be connected to an identified old
computation by a relative theorem.  The absolute PA-consistency calibration
is a separate dependency.  This separation also permits a physical-machine
optimization to be checked once and reused with any later improvement of
the logical proof.

\subsection{Total primitive-recursive semantics}

For a binary table $M$, represent the finite-support tape at its head by
natural numbers $(l,r)$, with the low bit of $r$ scanned and the low bit of
$l$ immediately to the left.  If the transition writes $w$, its tape update
is
\[
 (l,r)\longmapsto
 \begin{cases}
 (2l+w,\lfloor r/2\rfloor),&\text{right move},\\
 (\lfloor l/2\rfloor,4\lfloor r/2\rfloor+2w+(l\bmod2)),&\text{left move}.
 \end{cases}
\]
The finite table lookup and these arithmetic operations define a primitive
recursive step.  Use an absorbing arithmetic halt code $H_M$; this convention
adds no working state to the physical table.  Define
$\operatorname{Run}_M(0,x)=x$ and
$\operatorname{Run}_M(n+1,x)=\operatorname{Step}_M(
\operatorname{Run}_M(n,x))$.
Quantifier-free induction proves the iteration identity
\[
 \operatorname{Run}_M(u+v,x)
 =\operatorname{Run}_M(v,\operatorname{Run}_M(u,x))
\]
and absorption after halt.  Every predicate below is a primitive-recursive
Boolean test, so its displayed Boolean combinations can be expressed by
quantifier-free formulas in the usual language of iPRA.

Let $P$ be a total primitive-recursive source step, with an absorbing
source halt code $H$, a fixed initial state $z_0$, and a decidable validity
predicate.  Invalid codes receive arbitrary default behavior.  A valid
source state can include a program counter, logical registers, and any
auxiliary fields needed by its tape representation.  In particular a
growing zero gap and an arbitrary finite spare list must be fields of
this state; they cannot silently be held fixed during iteration.

For each $M\in\{A,B\}$, suppose the following data and facts are given:

\begin{enumerate}
\item A total primitive-recursive complete-configuration encoding $E_M$
with $E_M(H)=H_M$, and $E_M(z)\ne H_M$ for valid live $z$.
\item A primitive-recursive duration $d_M$, with $d_M(H)=0$ and
$d_M(z)\ge1$ for every valid live state.
\item Validity of $z_0$, preservation of validity by $P$, and the exact
local equation
\[
 \operatorname{Run}_M(d_M(z),E_M(z))=E_M(P(z))
 \tag{L}\label{eq:mathaudit-local}
\]
for valid $z$.  The equation is proved in iPRA, with all components of $z$
free.  A nonhalting endpoint in absorbing semantics also excludes a
hidden earlier halt inside that segment.
\item A finite, complete blank-initialization equality
\[
 \operatorname{Run}_M(b_M,\operatorname{blank}_M)=E_M(z_0).
 \tag{I}\label{eq:mathaudit-init}
\]
The equality includes the whole finite tape support and hence both zero
exteriors, as well as the head and control.
\end{enumerate}

Two implementations need not use identical auxiliary fields.  If they
implement the same logical source step, take a common source state
$(s,a_A,a_B)$ that carries both auxiliary states and updates each by its
own specified primitive-recursive rule.  The two encodings then read
their own components.  The logical projection must commute with the
source step; equal-looking source words alone are insufficient when
structured jump destinations can change under reassociation.

\subsection{Synchronization and explicit witness maps}

Define, by primitive recursion,
\[
 z_k=P^k(z_0),\qquad \tau_M(0)=0,\qquad
 \tau_M(k+1)=\tau_M(k)+d_M(z_k).
\]
The state and the clock are computed simultaneously; the clock is not
obtained by waiting for an unspecified stopping event.  From
\eqref{eq:mathaudit-local} and \eqref{eq:mathaudit-init}, quantifier-free
induction gives
\[
 \operatorname{Valid}(z_k),\qquad
 \operatorname{Run}_M(b_M+\tau_M(k),\operatorname{blank}_M)=E_M(z_k).
 \tag{S}\label{eq:mathaudit-sync}
\]
The induction formula is precisely the displayed Boolean validity test
and configuration equality.  All initial parameters are fixed outside
the induction.  If $z_k=H$, the successor equality remains valid because
both halt codes are absorbing and $d_M(H)=0$.

A second quantifier-free induction proves
\[
 z_k\ne H\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\tau_M(k)\ge k.
 \tag{P}\label{eq:mathaudit-positive}
\]
Indeed, $z_{k+1}\ne H$ implies $z_k\ne H$ by absorption, and the new
segment has duration at least one.  It follows immediately that a source
halt at step $k$ gives a physical halt at the explicit time
$b_M+\tau_M(k)$.

For the converse, suppose $M$ is halted by physical time $t$.  If
$z_{t+1}$ were live, \eqref{eq:mathaudit-positive} would give
$b_M+\tau_M(t+1)>t$.  Absorption would make the left side of
\eqref{eq:mathaudit-sync} a halted configuration, while its right side
would be live.  This is a contradiction.  Equality with the fixed source
halt code is decidable, so iPRA concludes $z_{t+1}=H$.  Thus the reverse
witness is the particularly simple function $t\mapsto t+1$.
No Markov principle, unbounded least-witness operation, or excluded middle
for an arbitrary formula is involved.

Writing $\mathcal H_M(t)$ for ``$M$ is halted by time $t$'', the two
explicit relative witness maps are
\[
 F_{A\to B}(t)=b_B+\tau_B(t+1),\qquad
 F_{B\to A}(t)=b_A+\tau_A(t+1).
\]
We have proved the following wholly relative theorem:
\[
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash
 \forall t\,[\mathcal H_A(t)\to\mathcal H_B(F_{A\to B}(t))],\\
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash
 \forall t\,[\mathcal H_B(t)\to\mathcal H_A(F_{B\to A}(t))].
\end{gathered}}
\]
Existential introduction and elimination give equivalence of the positive
halting sentences.  Intuitionistic reasoning then gives equivalence of
their negations.  Nothing in this argument identifies the source program
with a proof enumerator or assumes the consistency of PA.

\subsection{Optional polynomial clocks and concrete checking obligations}

If the exact finite contracts additionally prove a common affine bound
\[
 d_M(z_j)\le a_M+c_Mj
\]
on every live source step, then
\[
 \tau_M(k)\le a_Mk+c_M\frac{k(k-1)}2.
\]
The same inequality holds after source halt because subsequent durations
are zero.  A simpler sufficient relative map is therefore
\[
 t\longmapsto b_M+a_M(t+1)+c_M\frac{t(t+1)}2.
\]
For example, the already supplied loop-continuation packet derives
$a_M=694$, $c_M=26$, and $b_M=57922$ for its AX11H pair, yielding
$13t^2+707t+58616$.  This example is not a bound for an arbitrary new
mutation: the local coefficient vectors and frame updates must be
recomputed for the actual new table.  Primitive-recursive exact clocks
already suffice for the relative theorem; a polynomial estimate is an
additional convenience.

A successful finite executable replay supplies the machine-specific
closed facts only after the checker rules have their uniform arithmetic
interpretation.  The relevant rules in the reviewed AX11H physical proof
are literal transitions, unary-run traversal, four finite-list
traversals, and finite-word normalization.  Each is justified by explicit
quantifier-free induction.  The current tape bit, previous-bit histories,
complete frame, exact exit control, duration positivity, and unaffected
outside tape must all be included in a new local contract.  The final
simultaneously transformed table is the one to check: verifying each
proposed merge in isolation does not handle dependencies among its
helper controls.

For a direct quotient rather than a register-source compilation, $P$ may
be the old literal transition function.  Its validity predicate includes
the entry-history invariant.  The encoding maps only the old control to
the quotient control and retains the complete tape.  Finite local
simulation cases then give \eqref{eq:mathaudit-local}; the same theorem
applies without any separate theorem about the numerical calculus.

The highest-risk induction issue receives substantive treatment in these
papers.  Same-stratum recursive requests decrease a source rank; their
child parameters are computed before their proof values.  The finite
request tree is generated first and evaluated backwards, with bounded
Boolean correctness tests.  This remains primitive recursive when
parameter and proof sizes grow.  Term uniqueness is completed before
higher vector-realization calls on possibly larger replacement terms.
The Foundation adapter also supplies an explicit opening/closing
constructor and handles the changed finite order axiom.  These arguments
are materially stronger than merely asserting that standard finite-run
Lean theorems internalize.
