Attribution and scope
=====================

This research uses and extends materials in VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt,
pinned to 32d0d8b7d93a63ad4106c18cdb29b37927acdad2. It is not an upstream
release and no upstream repository files were changed.

Inherited construction, proof, runtime, inventory and search materials are
identified in construction/PROVENANCE.json, comparison/PROVENANCE.json,
research/logical/PROVENANCE.json and related per-directory records. Original
licenses are retained in construction/licenses/ and research/logical/vendor/. Author and license notices inside inherited files
remain in force. No new license for third-party material is asserted here.

New work consists of the two exact AX11QH4L/AX11QH4R physical compositions,
their reconstruction and relative trace-transfer arguments, fresh independent
audit runs, the pinned comparison and novelty check, the documented scoped
search experiments, the quantified outer-gate proof templates, and the report.

The package distinguishes ordinary mathematical arguments, finite replay
certificates, inherited assumptions and proof-assistant endpoints. A successful
Python check is not presented as a completed Lean proof. In particular the
absolute iPRA equivalence between the literal machines' nonhalting and Con(PA)
is not newly established by this package.
