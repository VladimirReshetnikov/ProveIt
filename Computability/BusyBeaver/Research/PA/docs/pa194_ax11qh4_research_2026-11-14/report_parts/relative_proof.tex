\section{The two new physical implementations}

Let $M$ denote the pinned PA194AX11QH machine whose dense table has
SHA-256
\begin{center}\small\ttfamily
19e215df20429fa6a35f0a99ba0dcfcb40893a6e4b9a4ae6e450b0225f110e50
\end{center}
Write $N_L$ and $N_R$ for PA194AX11QH4L and PA194AX11QH4R.
All three machines have a two-way infinite binary tape, blank symbol zero,
initial head coordinate zero, and a separate terminal halt control.
The dense initial control is zero; the dense halt label is 194.

The change concerns a repeated four-increment subtree.  If
$i$ denotes incrementing logical register~1, the old association is
$((i,i),(i,i))$.  The new associations are
\[
 T_L=((i,(i,i)),i),\qquad T_R=(i,(i,(i,i))).
\]
The left-to-right sequence of primitive instructions is exactly the same.
The deterministic checker proves equality of all 3,671 resolved source
instructions, including every jump and decrement target.  It also identifies
all 61 occurrences of this four-increment subtree.  Their roots occupy the
same source addresses; only addresses inside the subtrees change.

In native labels, control 61 implements the single-increment subtree,
and control 99 implements the two-increment subtree.  Introduce a helper
control 330 for $(i,(i,i))$.  Its rows are
\[
 (330,0)\mapsto(0,R,61),\qquad
 (330,1)\mapsto(1,R,99).
\]
For $N_L$, replace the two rows of control 190 by
\[
 (190,0)\mapsto(0,R,330),\qquad
 (190,1)\mapsto(1,R,61);
\]
for $N_R$, use
\[
 (190,0)\mapsto(0,R,61),\qquad
 (190,1)\mapsto(1,R,330).
\]
These are changes to the unfolded machine, before its contextual quotient.
The arithmetic list engine is unchanged.

The new association makes the incoming history at control 99 uniform.
Consequently control 41 can be folded into 99, using the replacement rows
\[
 (99,0)\mapsto(0,L,30),\qquad
 (99,1)\mapsto(1,L,2040).
\]
The seven other folds are given explicitly in the delivered
\texttt{folds.json}.  All eight are checked simultaneously, so a provider
used by one fold is allowed to be changed by another only when the complete
local identity still closes in the final table.  Each new machine therefore
has
\[
 135\text{ fixed controls}+67\text{ AX readers}-8\text{ folds}=194
\]
working controls.  This is a new physical implementation and is not a
reduction below 194 states.

\section{Finite certificates and their universal meanings}

For each folded pair $(a,b)$, every incoming transition to $a$ writes zero
and moves right, and every incoming transition to $b$ writes one and moves
right.  Neither state is the initial control.  Thus the cell immediately
left of the head records which old control was entered.  The checker
enumerates the four possibilities for that history bit and the current
bit.  It reads only those two cells and restores their exact required
contents.  All exterior tape cells remain arbitrary.  There are 32 cases
for the eight simultaneous folds, each taking one, three, or five literal
transitions in the quotient machine.

This proves a local simulation lemma, not merely a sampled execution.
Every ordinary unfolded transition projects to one quotient transition.
Every exceptional transition projects to the certified positive-length
segment.  Induction on a finite unfolded run gives a quotient run with the
same complete tape and head at its segment boundaries.  If the unfolded run
has length $t$, the corresponding quotient run has length between $t$ and
$5t$.  Positivity of the segment lengths and determinism give the reverse
halting implication by bounded segmentation of a quotient run.  In
particular, no separate reachability assumption concerning the unbounded
external tape is being inferred from a finite blank run.

The two supplied complete-machine checks have separate purposes.  The
construction replay checks source resolution, the local quotient identities,
the unchanged arithmetic-box rows, all 4,778 symbolic source outcomes, dense
renumbering, and the entire blank-start tape.  The independent literal-tape
checker imports neither the construction compiler nor its arithmetic-box
oracles.  It rederives the four unary-list sweep lemmas from the candidate
rows and uses two independent source resolvers.  Its cases quantify over
arbitrary values of the nine logical registers and an arbitrary nonempty finite
spare-register list, with both branches of every decrement represented.  Both new
independent audits passed all 4,778 outcomes.  Their duration constant
terms have minimum three and maximum 534; all variable coefficients are
between zero and eight.  The independent results must not be replaced by
the construction check's bounded abstract-operation count.

All three startup runs reach the canonical source seed at site 25.  The
logical seed has register~0 equal to two and all eight other logical registers
zero.  There are 26 spare physical registers of value one.  The entire
finite-support tape is checked, including absence of stray nonzero cells.
The parent startup takes 57,922 transitions; each new startup takes 58,088.
The native control and head coordinate after initialization
are 84 and $-2$, respectively.

\section{Relative weak-arithmetic theorem}

The machine-specific claim supplied by this transformation is
\[
 \mathsf{iPRA}\ \vdash\
 \operatorname{Halt}(N_L,\mathrm{blank})
 \leftrightarrow\operatorname{Halt}(M,\mathrm{blank})
 \leftrightarrow\operatorname{Halt}(N_R,\mathrm{blank}).
\]
Here ``supplied'' means an ordinary computer-assisted mathematical argument
with explicit finite certificates and primitive-recursive induction
invariants, not a newly compiled Lean theorem.  The following argument
explains why the relative result uses only primitive-recursive arithmetic.

Encode a finite-support tape and integer head position by natural numbers.
Literal machine iteration, register-frame construction, source-instruction
iteration, and the finite case selector are primitive recursive.  A symbolic
constant-symbol sweep asserts an equality between a primitive-recursive
iterate and its explicitly described final configuration; its proof is
quantifier-free induction on the sweep length.  A list sweep uses the same
induction on an encoded finite list, equivalently bounded recursion on its
code or length.  The finite composition of these identities gives, for each
of the 4,778 source outcomes, an equality
\[
 \operatorname{Run}_{X}\bigl(d_{X,j}(\mathbf x),E_{X,j}(\mathbf x)\bigr)
   = E_{X,j'}\bigl(F_j(\mathbf x)\bigr),
 \qquad X\in\{M,N_L,N_R\},
\]
with the appropriate absorbing halt endpoint in the halt case.  All side
conditions are explicit tests on primitive-recursive codes.  The checker
also supplies the physical duration expressions.  Their coefficients are
nonnegative and their constant terms are positive.  The literal case
certificates certify that a segment does not halt before its designated
endpoint, except when the current source instruction is the halt
instruction.

Let $s_k$ be the total, primitively recursive source iteration, including
its register-frame parameters, starting from the displayed seed.  In
particular, its state contains the header-gap parameter and the entire
finite spare-register list, in addition to the nine logical registers.
These are updated by the explicit frame equations: a positive decrement
increases the gap by one, as specified by its \texttt{delta=1} contract, and an
increment's tail operation updates the spare list.  They must not be
silently discarded from the simulation invariant.  Define
the boundary clock by primitive recursion:
\[
 C_X(0)=\operatorname{startup}_X,\qquad
 C_X(k+1)=C_X(k)+d_{X,s_k}(\mathbf x_k)
\]
until the source halt case. For a total definition, extend a halted source
state by a self-loop of duration one and keep the physical halt state
absorbing. Clock comparisons concern prefixes through the first halt; this
extension makes every boundary-clock increment positive without changing
any first-halting assertion.  The previous equations and quantifier-free induction give the
literal configuration at each $C_X(k)$.  Thus a finite source halting
run gives a finite literal halting run for every $X$.

Conversely, suppose a literal run halts at time $T$.  The explicitly checked
startup does not halt.  Before source halting, each segment length is at
least one.  Bounded search among at most $T+1$ source boundaries therefore
locates the segment containing the alleged halting time.  The local
no-premature-halt identity forces that segment to be the halt case.  This
bounded search, its accumulated clocks, and the corresponding configuration
equalities are primitive recursive; their verification uses
quantifier-free induction.  The bounded prefix condition saying that no
earlier literal transition is a halt is represented by its
primitive-recursive characteristic function, so the induction invariant
is an equality of primitive-recursive terms, not an induction instance
with an unbounded quantifier.  No unbounded minimization, induction on an
existential formula, standard-model soundness principle, or appeal to the
truth of $\operatorname{Con}(\mathsf{PA})$ is required.

The source transition function is literally identical for the three
machines, so these two directions establish the displayed relative
equivalences.  Intuitionistic propositional reasoning then yields the
relative nonhalting equivalences as well.  In particular, the desired
consistency conclusion for either new table follows from the separate
calibration hypothesis
\[
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash
 \neg\operatorname{Halt}(M,\mathrm{blank})
 \leftrightarrow\operatorname{Con}(\mathsf{PA}).
\]
This hypothesis is not established by the new physical construction.
The current repository explicitly identifies the end-to-end
machine/refutation calibration as unfinished.  A completed relative
physical proof must not be reported as an unconditional new consistency
machine theorem.

\section{A factor-two witness transfer}

Matching the independent duration certificates by source-site number and
decrement branch gives a substantially sharper bound.  For all 4,778
matched outcomes, the twelve nonconstant duration coefficients are identical
in $M,N_L,N_R$.  The differences in their constant terms satisfy
\[
 d_L-d_M\in\{0,2,4,6,8\},\qquad
 d_R-d_M\in\{-2,0,2,4,6\}.
\]
The actual coefficientwise comparisons, which are stronger than these
sets of possible differences alone, verify
\[
 d_M\le d_L\le2d_M,\qquad
 d_M\le2d_R,\qquad d_R\le2d_M.
\]
These inequalities hold for every nonnegative assignment of the symbolic
register, gap, and spare-list parameters.

The frame parameters really do agree at corresponding source boundaries.
The complete resolved source graphs and opcode/register fields are
identical.  Each increment appends the same spare block, each positive
decrement changes the same logical register and header gap, and the
remaining source instructions have the same frame effects.  In addition,
all 4,777 nonhalt outcomes have identical zero head displacement in the
three independent checks, so there is no differing source-address overflow
to hide in the gap parameter.  The unique halt instruction, site 10 at
address \texttt{001}, has duration three and displacement three in all
three tables.

The initial clocks satisfy $57922\le58088\le2\cdot57922$.  Summing the
verified segment inequalities, with the final halt instruction included,
therefore gives the following explicit bounded-witness implications:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{HaltBy}_M(t)&\Longrightarrow
     \operatorname{HaltBy}_{N_L}(2t),\\
 \operatorname{HaltBy}_{N_L}(t)&\Longrightarrow
     \operatorname{HaltBy}_M(t),\\
 \operatorname{HaltBy}_M(t)&\Longrightarrow
     \operatorname{HaltBy}_{N_R}(2t),\\
 \operatorname{HaltBy}_{N_R}(t)&\Longrightarrow
     \operatorname{HaltBy}_M(2t).
\end{aligned}
\]
Here $\operatorname{HaltBy}_X(t)$ is the primitive-recursive bounded
halting predicate for the blank-start table $X$.  The positive-duration
and prefix-nonhalting argument in the previous section identifies the
executed source instruction count of an arbitrary literal halting witness;
the displayed coefficient inequalities then bound the translated witness.
This makes the relative iPRA result a concrete bounded-witness transfer,
while leaving the separate PA-consistency calibration untouched.

\section{An explicit polynomial witness bound}

The 13 slots of every checked duration polynomial are, in order: the
constant, the header gap, the nine active register values, the first spare
register value, and the total width of the remaining spare-register list.
The maximum coefficient in each slot, for both new machines, is
\[
 (534,8,4,4,4,4,4,4,2,4,4,2,2).
\]
The parent's independent contract data have the same maxima.  Let $L_k$
be the sum of the twelve nonconstant frame parameters before source
instruction $k$.  At the seed, the active-register sum is two, the first
spare value is one, and the remaining 25 spare registers have encoding
width three each.  Therefore $L_0=78$.

An increment increases one active value by one and appends one spare
register of value one, whose encoding has width three.  It may also
increase the header gap by one when the source address overflows.  Hence
an increment increases $L$ by at most five.  A positive decrement reduces
an active value by one and increases the gap by one plus the possible
overflow bit; its net increase is at most one.  A zero decrement or break
only adds the possible overflow bit, and a continue has no positive
resource effect.  Thus quantifier-free induction proves
\[
 L_k\le78+5k.
\]
In a positive-decrement certificate, the independent variable for the
tested register is its predecessor value; using the actual register value
in $L_k$ only enlarges the upper bound.

Each nonterminal source segment and the final halt-instruction segment
therefore has duration at most
\[
 534+8L_k\le1158+40k.
\]
If $m$ source instructions are executed, \emph{counting the final halt
instruction}, the corresponding literal computation has duration at most
\[
 58088+\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}(1158+40k)
   =58088+1138m+20m^2.
\]
Every source segment has positive duration, so a literal halt at time
$t$ uses at most $t$ executed source instructions.  The convenient rounded
bound
\[
 \boxed{B(t)=60000+1200t+20t^2}
\]
therefore bounds a halting witness in any of $M,N_L,N_R$ obtained from a
halting witness at time $t$ in any other one.  All coefficients are natural
numbers.  Bounded searches through these explicitly bounded literal and
source traces can be represented by primitive-recursive characteristic
functions.  This gives a concrete bounded-witness form of the relative
halting equivalence and removes any reliance on an unspecified simulation
time function.
