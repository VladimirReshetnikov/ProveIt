U222 — provenance and inherited terms
====================================

This package was constructed for Vladimir Reshetnikov's research request
on 28 October 2026. It does not assert discovery priority, absolute state
minimality, unconditional consistency, or a new proof-assistant endpoint.
Independent reviews in this package were conducted by separate assistants
within this research collaboration; they are not external human endorsements.
