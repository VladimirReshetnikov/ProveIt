U209: three compatible decoder reductions, 29 October 2026.
Research prepared with ChatGPT for Vladimir Reshetnikov.

New in this package: a common source layout admitting the three different
state reductions in the supplied U211 packets, the exact 209-state tables,
the direct combined local proof, independent source/prefix/startup audits,
fresh universal final-table verification, comparison, report, and packaging.

The original logical theories, numerical proof calculus, compiler program,
Turing-machine architecture, and prior reductions are inherited work, not
new claims of authorship here. The three U211 proposal archives and the unary
power-cover archive are retained unchanged under reference/. Their internal
notices identify their own sources and earlier contributors, including the
Wade/TMBuilder, Mira-Cult, ProveIt, and TheoryK verification lineages where
applicable. All inherited licenses/notices are copied under licenses/.

The two modules code/runtime/native_symbolic.py and source_ops.py are copied
unchanged from the Rotated archive's checks/native56/ directory. The module
code/source_language.py is copied unchanged from the Contextual archive.
inputs/vendor_provenance.json records exact source members and SHA256 hashes.
The unmodified common U212 table/source are included under inputs/.

The proof-assistant certificates for other fixed machines are background
references. This package does not incorporate or claim a new end-to-end
Lean, Rocq, MM0, or internal-PA proof object for U209.
