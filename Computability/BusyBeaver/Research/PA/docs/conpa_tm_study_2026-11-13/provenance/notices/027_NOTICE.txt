U221: provenance and attribution
================================

This research package was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov on 28 October 2026.
It does not assert discovery priority, absolute minimality, or unconditional
consistency. Independent checks within this package were written by separate
assistant agents in one research collaboration, not external human reviewers.

New work
--------
The two-clause predecessor invariant, exact reduction of native state 200 to 209,
run-correspondence proof, source-independent finite certificates, mutation
controls, bounded known-symbol search, dense renaming, reproduction wrapper,
comparative review, and this report were developed for the present request.
