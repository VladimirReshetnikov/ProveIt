Attribution and proof scope
===========================
This packet is a new research iteration for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt
project, using the supplied U218, U222, K215 and unary-power-cover artifacts.
Their exact notices and licenses are retained in licenses/ and in the
included predecessor archive. These credit, within their stated scopes,
Mira, ProveIt contributors, Elliot Glazer, Jeff, Andrew James Wade's compiler
ancestry, BusyCoq and the Coq Library of Undecidability.

The role assignment, selected source parenthesization, adapted contextual
allocation, 214-state fold, direct 217-state local convergence, portable
reconstruction, and new independent audits were developed in this iteration.
The symbolic runtime engine and inherited arithmetic/logical proof artifacts
are reused with their precise provenance. New source audit normalization is
adapted from the supplied U222 finite binding checker. Construction searches
are not acceptance proofs: the selected literal tables pass the separately
recorded checks.

New material uses the project's MIT No Attribution terms. This notice does
not relicense inherited components. Separate reviews occurred within the
same assistant research collaboration and are not external endorsements.
No new proof-assistant endpoint compilation or global state lower bound is
claimed. The report states the exact inherited consistency dependencies.
