\section{Theory and source composition experiments}
\label{sec:theory-composition}

This section records one successful source composition and one rejected
allocation.  The successful composition has 197 working states.  Neither
experiment supplies a machine below 196 states.  All inherited source is
pinned to ProveIt commit
\texttt{906367250a7f96c923147b10df392294bced7be5}.
The immediate parents are
\texttt{pa\_consistency\_comparison\_and\_new196\_2026-11-13.zip}
and \texttt{conpa\_tm\_study\_2026-11-13.zip} in

\subsection{Direct negation composed with the diagonal gate}

Write
\[
  \operatorname{tri}(n)=\frac{n(n+1)}2,\qquad
  P(a,b)=\operatorname{tri}(a+b)+b,\qquad
  I(a,b)=4P(a,b).
\]
The numeral code for falsity used in the emitter is 1, so its negation
constructor is $N(t)=I(t,1)$.  The usual macro sequence
\texttt{push; incT; cons} constructs this code by pushing $t$ and building
an implication with consequent 1.  It can instead be constructed without
the stack operation.

The inherited register names are $T=r_1$, $W=r_2$, $A=r_3$, $D=r_4$,
$H=r_5$, $S=r_6$, $R=r_7$, and $L=r_8$.
On entry with $S=R=0$ and $T=t$, execute two increments of $R$ and then
the existing \texttt{node64}.  Its first component, \texttt{node46},
adds $\operatorname{tri}(t)+t$ to $R$ while clearing $T,S$.
Its second component, \texttt{node63}, transfers four times $R$ into $T$
and clears $R$.  Thus the resulting $T$ is
\[
  4\bigl(2+\operatorname{tri}(t)+t\bigr)
  =4\bigl(\operatorname{tri}(t+1)+1\bigr)=I(t,1).
\]
The invariant for the first component is the ordinary triangular-number
loop invariant, and the second is a fourfold transfer loop.  All other
registers, including $W$, are preserved.  This is an all-natural-number
arithmetic identity, not an extrapolation from finite inputs.

The new grammar replaces all eight old negation sequences with this
18-primitive macro and retains the diagonal gate
$\Gamma(B)=I(B,U(B,B))$.  Each replacement saves 29 primitive sites,
for a total saving of 232.  The 169 exterior source sites, their opcodes,
and their relative successors remain exactly the same as in the parent.
The emitted heads and gate are checked as terms in the free constructor
algebra; this check does not identify merely numerically equal sampled
formulas.

The exact blank run reaches native state 84 with head at $-2$ and 66

against the inherited seed.  The delivered dense table uses start state
0, working labels $0,\ldots,196$, and halt label 197; the native start
and halt labels are 74 and 254.

The frozen composition and all checks can be replayed using
\texttt{python3 -B verify.py}.  A bounded grammar search considered 100
distinct initial compositions and ran 24 cycles of 1,600 proposal
iterations, with seed 6236814.  An iteration can select a node with no
available rotation; the count is therefore not a claim that 38,400
distinct mutations were tested.  No allocation below 197 states was
found.  This is a search result for the stated representation and budget,
not a lower bound on machines or grammars.

The new checks use the inherited symbolic arithmetic-engine interfaces.
They establish the recorded source binding, local primitive contracts,
constructor identity, and startup execution.  They do not constitute a
new Lean or Rocq development of the full machine, or a new internal PA
proof of the consistency biconditional.  The proof-theoretic bridge must
be assessed separately from the state count and executable receipts.

In particular, the free-constructor checker does not track $S,R$, and
the primitive runtime checker does not prove that these registers are
zero on entry to all eight new macro calls.  The arithmetic identity
above retains that precondition.  Its intended propagation uses the
inherited clean-to-clean macro contracts: \texttt{AXMacro.registers}
materializes $r_6=r_7=0$, \texttt{AXMacro.Realizes} relates such stores,
and \texttt{AXMacro.Plan.sound} composes the contracts.  The original
\texttt{PA196.Source.AXBindings.full\_plan} certifies the original
emitter plan.  Applying this reasoning to the changed word, with the
new negation identity as one further clean-to-clean contract, is an
ordinary composition argument; the packet does not supply a newly
certified plan or a new call-site scratch-zero reachability theorem.

\subsection{A fresh-code padding lemma and its cost}

The guard in the original source prevents a property code $A$ from
being used as a binder when $A=D$ or $A=H$.  One mathematical way to
make these equalities impossible is to construct
\[
  K(D,H)=I(D+1,I(H,D+1)),\qquad
  A^\star=I(K(D,H),A).
\]
As a formula, $K(D,H)$ is an instance of
$p\mathbin{\to}(q\mathbin{\to}p)$ and is a theorem of the implicational
propositional calculus.  Consequently $A^\star$ and $A$ are logically
equivalent.

For every natural $n\geq1$, $\operatorname{tri}(n)\geq n$.  Hence whenever
$x+y\geq1$,
$I(x,y)\geq4(x+y)$.  Set $B=I(H,D+1)$.  Then
\[
 B\geq4(H+D+1)>\max(D,H),\quad
 K(D,H)\geq4B>B,\quad A^\star\geq4K(D,H)>K(D,H).
\]
Thus $A^\star>D,H$ for every $A,D,H\in\mathbb N$.
The increment of $D$ is essential: omitting it gives
$I(I(0,I(0,0)),0)=0$ when $D=H=A=0$, so the intended freshness fails
because of the zero-code alias.

The arithmetic lemma does not by itself prove completeness of a
modified calculus.  Replacing the gate property by $A^\star$ restricts
the available instances.  In the bundled diagonal design it also
produces only $\Gamma(A^\star)$, rather than arbitrary $\Gamma(B)$.
Moving an unrestricted diagonal head before the padding would address
that particular loss but would not automatically recover all
membership-vacuity instances of the original emitter.  A uniform
syntactic translation of refutations, including the relevant variable
renaming and instance-recovery arguments, is still required.
Logical equivalence of a padded property and the original property is
insufficient to supply that translation.

The physical experiment replaces the first property copy in the gate
with construction of $K$, a destructive transfer of $A$, construction
of $A^\star$, and a transfer back into $A$.  It replaces the old guard
copy and comparisons by three clearing loops.  Its best retained
allocation has 3,812 source sites, 87 reader nodes, seven local folds,
and 210 working states before validation.  Six search cycles of 700
proposal iterations with seed 971615 found no improvement.

That allocation is rejected.  The reused physical return action for
\texttt{continueTo 2} in the new transfer loop fails the symbolic
primitive contract at source ordinal 2,413, after 3,138 contracts have
passed.  No complete startup or accepted 210-state construction is
claimed.  The corrected reachability audit starts at native state 74
and gives the same count and failure.  The packet includes the frozen
grammar, fold selection, hash of the reconstructed rejected allocation,
and a replay script; it deliberately omits an invalid literal machine
from the candidate collection.  The replay command is
\texttt{python3 -B replay\_padded\_failure.py}.

\subsection{What transfers from ZFC and concise iPRA}

The
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/tree/906367250a7f96c923147b10df392294bced7be5/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC}{ZFC research}
contains reusable compiler ideas: command-local fields, source
reassociation, shared macro entry points, and folds justified by the
incoming tape context.  These operate at the representation or compiler
level and can be tested for PA without changing the target theory.
Their numerical savings are not additive across different machines:
replacing one routine can remove the sharing or the tape context that
made another saving possible.  The direct-negation experiment gives a
concrete example: 232 fewer source sites coexist with one more working
state than in the 196-state diagonal parent.

The
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/tree/906367250a7f96c923147b10df392294bced7be5/Logic/PrimitiveRecursiveArithmetic/ConciseIPRA}{ConciseIPRA research}
offers concise presentation and checking techniques for a different
theory.  Its native checker size is also not a literal two-symbol
Turing-machine state count.  A checker for iPRA refutations cannot be
substituted for the PA checker merely by relabeling its consistency
statement.  Such a substitution requires a proved uniform reduction
between the relevant refutation predicates, of the strength needed for
the claimed internal arithmetic equivalence.  No such new reduction is
provided here.  Likewise, a machine searching for ZFC refutations does
not acquire the desired $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})$ equivalence
from the similarity of its architecture.

The resulting contribution is therefore a reproducible 197-state
composition, a strict fresh-code lemma, and precise negative records for
the attempted padding allocation.  These records preserve useful
mathematical and implementation distinctions without asserting a
sub-196 result or an optimality theorem.
