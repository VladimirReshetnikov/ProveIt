ATTRIBUTION, PROVENANCE, AND SCOPE

This release compares the six U209 archives supplied through Vladimir
Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository and constructs a new 208-state candidate
using a conditional entry invariant. The new reduction deletes native172
by sharing native211 and its surrounding decoder. It is new relative to
the six supplied packets; no broader historical priority is asserted.

The machine architecture, numerical calculus, source program, previous
reductions, and universal induction engine are inherited. Preserved notices
credit Mira-Cult/Mira, Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project, Elliot Glazer,
Jeff's busy-beaver project, Andrew James Wade's Turing Machine Explorer,
and the other contributors named in the original documents. Their existing
license texts and source pins remain in licenses/, comparison/, and the
complete baseline/U209_ConPA_Joint_2026-10-29.zip ancestry. No change to
inherited licensing or repository-wide relicensing is asserted.

code/runtime/native_symbolic.py and source_ops.py are copied unchanged from
the supplied Compatibility package. code/runtime/PROVENANCE.json is its
original provenance statement. code/source_language.py and the renamed
code/source_interface.py are unchanged source-language/prefix helpers from
Joint (the latter originally independent_source.py). Their original notices
and definitions are retained. New wrappers bind the actual final table and
source bytes, which differ from the upstream runtime's original target.

The independent local verifier, standalone comparison, exact builder,
startup wrapper, universal-runtime wrapper, clean discovery program, and
new report are research artifacts prepared for this request. The local
proof is independent of the universal engine and discovery algorithm.
The exact source theory is retained as baseline/pa_unary_power_cover.zip.

Proof status is stated throughout report.pdf and README.txt. Executable
mathematical induction in Python is not a proof-assistant kernel check.
The K215/ZFC formal development concerns a different table and theory.
The inherited logical calibration is retained as a mathematical dependency;
some historical .v files have unbundled imports and are not a new complete
U208 Rocq project.
