# A concrete HA-strength use of the ConciseIPRA idea

## Scope and status

This note supplies a mathematical construction of a native scalar calculus
with the strength of Heyting arithmetic (HA), using the witness-sensitive
introduction rule from ConciseIPRA. Consequently its refutation search has
the right consistency target for PA. It does **not** supply a compiler to a
binary one-tape Turing machine, a state bound, or a new machine below 196
states. The accompanying Python program is a readable reference checker;
its checks are not a kernel verification of the metatheorem below.

The starting point is the report calculus with **full formula-template
transport**. This qualification matters: the later Lean
`ConciseIPRA/NativeOneIntro.lean` minimizes transport to `trOne`. Its ordinary
iPRA equivalence is proved there, but one must not silently reuse that
equivalence after changing the introduction rule. The proof below uses full
transport explicitly and therefore does not assert a theorem about the
modified `trOne` kernel.

## 1. The calculus

Use a single numerical sort and terms

\[
t ::= x\mid0\mid S(t)\mid I(n,a;u.h).
\]

Only `u` is bound in the body `h` of the iterator. The computation axioms
are

\[
 I(0,a;u.h)=a,\qquad
 I(Sn,a;u.h)=h[I(n,a;u.h)/u].
\]

Formulas are equations, `Q_y B(y)`, and `D_{x,y} B(x,y)`. In the last
constructor `x` and `y` are distinct bound variables. Their ordinary
readings are

\[
 Q_yB:=B(0)\to\forall y\,B(y),\qquad
 D_{x,y}B:=\exists x\,(B(x,0)\to\forall y\,B(x,y)).
\]

Designate `0=S(0)` as falsity. Sequents have finite contexts. Besides
hypothesis and the two computation axioms, use these rules:

* **Transport:** from `s=t` and `F(s)`, infer `F(t)`, for every native
  formula template `F`; substitution avoids capture.
* **QE:** from `Q_y B` and `B(0)`, infer `B(t)`.
* **DE:** from `D_{x,y}B` and a derivation of `A` under the additional
  hypothesis `Q_y B(b,y)`, infer `A`; the eigenvariable `b` is fresh in
  the outside context, the major premise, and the conclusion.
* **DI:** from `Γ,B(t,η) ⊢ B(t,Sz)`, infer `Γ ⊢ D_{x,y}B`, where `z` is
  fresh in the outside context, the witness, and the uninstantiated matrix
  apart from the displayed bound coordinates, and

\[
 \eta=\begin{cases}z&\text{if }t\text{ is literally }0,\\
                   0&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
\]

Call this calculus `W_H`. The corresponding report-level iPRA calculus
`W_i` chooses `η=z` only when the matrix is a **literal equation** and the
witness is literally zero. Thus the change is removal of the matrix test
from this one rule. It is not replacement of the theory by classical iPRA.

## 2. Recovering all the old cases

Changing the selector does not merely add rules: a case introduction at
zero with a compound matrix is no longer primitive. It must be recovered.

Put

\[
 e:=I(0,0;u.0).
\]

The term `e` is not literally zero, but the first computation axiom proves
`e=0`. Reflexivity and symmetry are already derivable using computation
and transport, with no DI instance. For reflexivity of `t`, transport the
equation `r=t`, where `r=I(0,t;u.0)`, along itself in the template `v=t`;
the result is `t=t`. Transport `s=s` along `s=t` in the template `v=s`
to obtain symmetry `t=s`.

Suppose a zero-witness case premise has been given:

\[
 \Gamma,B(0,0)\vdash B(0,Sz).
\]

First introduce the matrix `C(w,y):=B(0,y)`, in which `w` is unused,
using the nonliteral witness `e`. It selects case mode, and its premise
is exactly the displayed premise. We obtain

\[
 \Gamma\vdash D_{w,y}B(0,y).
\]

Open this with DE. The new hypothesis is `Q_y B(0,y)` and does not
actually mention the fresh witness. Under it introduce the original
matrix `B(x,y)` at witness `e`, again in case mode. Its additional
hypothesis is `B(e,0)`. Transport along `e=0` gives `B(0,0)`. QE at
`Sz` gives `B(0,Sz)`. Transport along `0=e` gives `B(e,Sz)`, as
required. DI then yields `D_{x,y}B`, and DE removes the fresh opening.

This argument works for **every matrix**, including matrices containing
`Q` and `D`. No formula normalization or arithmetic induction is involved.
Every `W_i` rule is therefore either a `W_H` rule or has this fixed
replacement. The inherited native logical macros and their derivations
remain available. In particular, the ordinary intuitionistic
connectives and quantifiers, numerical cases, and primitive-recursive
arithmetic operations are available with their usual rules.

The use of a nonliteral zero is exactly the useful idea imported from
ConciseIPRA. Its purpose here is different: it preserves the old logical
rules while allowing full induction, the strength required for PA
consistency.

## 3. Full induction is now derivable

Let `A(y)` be any native formula, not necessarily an equation, and suppose

\[
 \Gamma,A(z)\vdash A(Sz)
\]

with `z` fresh in `Γ`. Give DI an unused witness coordinate `x`, matrix
`B(x,y):=A(y)`, and literal-zero witness. Its premise is exactly the
displayed progress premise, so

\[
 \Gamma\vdash D_{x,y}A(y).
\]

DE followed by hypothesis yields

\[
 \Gamma\vdash Q_yA(y).
\]

If also `Γ ⊢ A(0)`, QE yields `Γ ⊢ A(t)` for arbitrary `t`.
Applying the ordinary universal-introduction macro (already recovered
from the old calculus) to a fresh variable gives

\[
 \frac{\Gamma\vdash A(0)\qquad
       \Gamma,A(z)\vdash A(Sz)}
      {\Gamma\vdash\forall y\,A(y)}.
\]

Equivalently, the ordinary full induction schema is derivable. This
establishes the lower inclusion of HA in `W_H`, with the native encodings
of the ordinary connectives and the definitional representation of the
primitive-recursive functions. It explains exactly why the equation test
cannot be dropped when the target is still iPRA, but can be dropped for
the present PA-consistency task.

## 4. Interpretation in HA

For the reverse inclusion, interpret `Q` and `D` by their displayed
ordinary readings. Interpret an iterator by primitive recursion. Each
finite iterator term determines a primitive-recursive function of its
free numerical variables; nested iterator bodies are treated before the
enclosing iterator. HA proves the relevant defining equations and
totality. This is a definitional extension, not an axiom asserting a
uniform evaluator for all primitive-recursive programs.

Hypothesis, transport, computation, QE, and DE are valid immediately.
For DI there are two cases:

* If the witness is literally zero, its premise yields
  `∀z (B(0,z)→B(0,Sz))`. HA induction for the possibly compound formula
  `B(0,y)` gives `B(0,0)→∀y B(0,y)`. Existential introduction with
  witness zero proves the interpretation of `D B`.
* For every other witness `t`, its premise gives
  `B(t,0)→∀z B(t,Sz)`. Under the hypothesis `B(t,0)`, numerical case
  distinction, `y=0 ∨ ∃z(y=Sz)`, yields `∀y B(t,y)`. That case
  distinction is a theorem of HA. Existential introduction with witness
  `t` again proves the interpretation of `D B`.

Thus every `W_H` theorem has an HA proof. Together with Section 3 and
the inherited logical translations, this gives proof translations in
both directions between `W_H` and HA in its conservative
primitive-recursive language extension. The numerical domain is the same
and equality is actual equality. The argument is a mathematical proof;
it has not been added to the existing Lean development or checked by its
kernel.

## 5. Why its consistency target is PA

HA proofs are also PA proofs. In the other direction, apply the
Gödel--Gentzen negative translation to PA proofs. Classical logical
rules become intuitionistically derivable rules, and a PA induction
instance translates to an HA induction instance for the translated
formula. Falsity is preserved. This yields refutation translations

\[
 \operatorname{Ref}_{\rm PA}\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Ref}_{\rm HA}\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Ref}_{W_H},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Ref}_{W_H}\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Ref}_{\rm HA}\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Ref}_{\rm PA}.
\]

These are syntactic proof translations, not assertions that PA is sound.
They can be implemented by primitive recursion on finite proof syntax:
copy a fixed proof template for a rule, substitute the supplied formulas
and terms, and choose fresh labels larger than every label already used.
The same applies to the zero-witness replacement above. The proof that
the resulting object is a valid proof is induction on the source proof.
With explicit primitive-recursive proof encodings, these construction
and checking statements are formalizable in PA. Consequently, after
fixing those encodings, PA proves

\[
 \operatorname{Con}(W_H)\leftrightarrow\operatorname{Con}({\rm PA}).
\]

This statement concerns the newly specified calculus and standard
syntactic proof predicates. It is not a theorem about PA196's literal
trace formula. A compiler from this checker to a concrete TM would still
need its own PA-internal halting/refutation equivalence.

## 6. The reference checker and its exact change

`native_ha_checker.py` implements the calculus above using finite named
syntax, capture-avoiding simultaneous substitution, and comparison
modulo alpha conversion. Its only difference between the `ipra` control
and the proposed `ha` mode is:

```python
def progress_mode(matrix, witness, logic):
    return witness == Z and (logic == 'ha' or matrix[0] == 'e')
```

This function is used **only by DI**. The implementation does not
change the computation axioms or fuse them with the introduction
dispatch. This caution matters for the upstream compact checker, whose
one Boolean expression serves several different operations. A textual
replacement in that expression has not been justified here.

Proof JSON contains `goal`, optionally `context`, optionally `logic`
(`ha` by default), and `proof`. Terms and formulas are described in the
program's header. Proof constructors are:

| Constructor | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `['hyp',i]` | Context formula at index `i` |
| `['zero',a,u,h]` | Zero computation for `I(0,a;u.h)` |
| `['succ',n,a,u,h]` | Successor computation for `I(Sn,a;u.h)` |
| `['tr',p,q,x,F]` | Transport using equality proved by `p`, minor proof `q`, template `F(x)` |
| `['qe',p,q,t]` | QE from closure `p`, base `q`, at `t` |
| `['de',p,b,q]` | DE, checking `q` under the opened closure at fresh `b` |
| `['di',x,y,B,t,z,p]` | DI with matrix `B`, witness `t`, fresh successor variable `z`, and branch proof `p` |

The verification procedure terminates on finite syntax in the abstract
unbounded computational model. Proof recursion is on strict subtrees;
substitution and alpha comparison are structural tree traversals. It
never evaluates an arbitrary iterator. An invalid constructor,
substitution mismatch, or eigenvariable violation rejects the input.
Under standard finite-string encodings, every operation is primitive
recursive, and primitive recursion on certificate size gives a
primitive-recursive checker. As usual, a concrete CPython invocation has
finite stack and memory limits; these engineering limits are not a
different logical rule.

Reproduce the small implementation checks with:

```sh
python3 verify_reference.py
python3 native_ha_checker.py < compound_progress_certificate.json
```

All nine checks in `reference_receipt.json` pass. The compound-progress
certificate is accepted in HA mode and the **same certificate** is
rejected in the iPRA control because the locally supplied hypothesis is
different. This does not claim that its conclusion separates HA from
iPRA; that conclusion is deliberately a small testing example. The
checks also cover falsity rejection, an eigenvariable failure, and
capture-avoiding substitution. They do not prove checker correctness,
the arithmetic equivalence, or any binary-TM state count. No comparison
between the readable checker's byte count and PA196's 196 states is
meaningful without a concrete compiler.

## 7. What would make this a smaller Con(PA) machine

A usable completion needs (1) a closed program that enumerates finite
certificates and halts exactly when this checker accepts falsity,
(2) an explicit compiler to blank-start, single-tape, two-symbol TMs,
(3) a literal transition table with fewer than 196 working states, and
(4) PA-internal verification connecting the literal trace predicate to
the syntactic refutation predicate. The logical construction gives the
appropriate target calculus. It does not make any of these machine-level
deliverables automatic. In particular, replacing the AX fragment of
PA196 by this checker is not a local rewrite: this kernel uses recursive
substitution and tree-shaped certificates, whereas PA196 uses its
specialized command and stack language.
