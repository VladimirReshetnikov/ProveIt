\section{Coefficient three cannot be repaired by finitely many exceptions}
\label{sec:cofinite-collision}

The earlier coefficient-three experiment has a concrete small refutation
using $E(1,0)=I(0,1)$. The following strengthening rules out an entire
repair strategy: excluding small variable labels or finitely many offending
equality-witness instances merely moves the same obstruction to larger
numbers. This concerns the unchanged logical interface; it does not rule
out a genuinely different ternary encoding or a typed proof calculus.

Let
\[
 p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b,\qquad
 I(a,b)=3p(a,b),\qquad E(a,b)=3p(a,b)+3,
\]
\[
 U(d,A)=3p(A,d)+2,\qquad N(A)=I(A,1),\qquad
 H_5(d,h)=N\bigl(U(d,N(E(h,d)))\bigr).
\]
The designated contradiction is the number one. Retain modus ponens,
the restricted implicational family
\[
 R(a,b,c,u)=I\bigl(I(I(c,u),a),I(I(a,c),I(b,c))\bigr),
 \qquad u\leq a,
\]
and theorem generalization at the labels allowed below.

\paragraph{Inherited propositional dependency.}
The generic restricted-implication construction derives
\[
 K(a,b)=I(a,I(b,a)),\qquad
 S(a,b,c)=I\bigl(I(a,I(b,c)),I(I(a,b),I(a,c))\bigr).
\]
Its exact source is
\texttt{PA/jamesyc-upstream/pa196/M6/Logic/RestrictedImplication.lean}
in the pinned repository; the earlier coefficient-three packet separately
expands its uses of these schemas into the primitive restricted head and
modus ponens. The generic argument requires only reflexivity of the
comparison and inflationary implication bounds. Here those bounds follow
from $a,b\leq p(a,b)\leq3p(a,b)$. No disjointness of constructor tags is
assumed. This paragraph identifies the inherited derived-rule dependency;
the new argument is the unbounded family below.

\paragraph{Cofinite-label obstruction.}
Fix any natural cutoff $L$. Suppose $H_5(d,h)$ is still available for every
$d,h\geq L$ and generalization is still available for every binder
$d\geq L$. Then the numerical calculus derives one. In particular, no
finite exclusion of fifth-head instances, finite forbidden set of labels,
or uniform upward shift of the unrestricted label range repairs the
coefficient-three calculus while the displayed propositional interface
is retained.

\paragraph{Proof.}
Put
\[
 T=L+1,\qquad A=I(T,T),\qquad B=I(A,1),\qquad
 h=A+1,\qquad d=B-1.
\]
The ordinary $K,S$ identity derivation proves $A=T\to T$ without any
assumption on $T$. All these are natural numbers, $A\geq T\geq1$,
$B\geq3A$, and hence $h,d\geq L$.

The two pairs $(A+1,B-1)$ and $(A,B)$ have the same sum. Their second
coordinates differ by one, so
\[
 p(A+1,B-1)+1=p(A,B).
\]
Consequently their literal numerical codes satisfy
\[
 E(h,d)=3p(A+1,B-1)+3
       =3p(A,B)=I(A,B).                                     \tag{Collision}
\]
Write $D=I(A,B)=A\to(A\to1)$. Since $A$ is a theorem, assuming $D$
and applying modus ponens twice gives one. The implicational deduction
theorem, or the explicit $K,S$ derivation immediately below, therefore
gives $N(D)=D\to1$ as a theorem. Generalization at the allowed binder
$d$ gives $U(d,N(D))$. But the retained instance $H_5(d,h)$ is exactly
its negation by (Collision). One final modus ponens gives one.
\hfill$\square$

For completeness, the propositional step uses no unbounded proof search.
Let $P=I(D,A)$. From $K(A,D)$ and the theorem $A$, derive $P$.
The identity theorem $I(D,D)$ and $S(D,A,B)$ give
$I(P,I(D,B))$, hence $I(D,B)$ by modus ponens with $P$.
Now $S(D,A,1)$ and $I(D,B)$ give $I(P,I(D,1))$; a second use of $P$
gives $I(D,1)$. Both identity theorems use the fixed five-line $K,S$
pattern
\[
 K(X,I(X,X)),\quad K(X,X),\quad S(X,I(X,X),X),\quad
 I(K(X,X),I(X,X)),\quad I(X,X).
\]
After expanding the inherited $K,S$ templates, the proof has a fixed
finite pattern independent of $L$; only its numerical instances vary.

\paragraph{Scope and significance.}
The refutation does not use code zero as a theorem, membership axioms,
equality reflexivity, equality substitution, the separation gate, or any
semantic soundness assumption. Its cause is the interaction between
the colliding implication/equality codes, unrestricted propositional
reasoning, generalization, and the fifth head.

Given any finite forbidden collection, choose $L$ larger than every
coordinate occurring in it. The constructed fifth-head instance and
binder then avoid that collection. Thus a special case rejecting
$E(1,0)$, or every equality-witness instance with a code below a chosen
bound, cannot suffice. A viable
repair must alter infinitely many relevant instances, introduce a genuine
type/grammar restriction with a new calibration, or change the constructor
or witness interface itself. None of these alternatives is claimed to
have produced a smaller machine here.

The displayed numerical functions and fixed proof-template operations
are primitive recursive. The new collision and bound calculations are
quantifier-free arithmetic identities and inequalities, so they introduce
no stronger induction requirement than iPRA. The supplied checker verifies
the finite schematic $K,S$/generalization/fifth-head derivation and exact
arithmetic instances; it is not advertised as a new proof-assistant
compilation of the inherited restricted-implication templates.
