\paragraph{The exact remaining generic premise.}
The definition in
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1c9524d8bf2e0a27b9a16ff622359927f536e663/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/isigma1-machine/ConPAMachineEndpoint.lean}{\texttt{ConPAMachineEndpoint.lean}}
is, in mathematical notation,
\[
 \operatorname{Calibrated}(T,h):\quad
 \text{for every }V\models\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1,\quad
 \operatorname{Prov}_{\mathrm{PA}}^V(\bot)
 \ \longleftrightarrow\
 \exists t\in V\;\operatorname{state}
                    (\operatorname{run}_T^V(t))=h.             \tag{Cal}
\]
Both the proof code and the time in (Cal) may be nonstandard elements of
$V$. The file
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1c9524d8bf2e0a27b9a16ff622359927f536e663/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/isigma1-machine/ConPAUnconditional.lean}{\texttt{ConPAUnconditional.lean}}
proves \texttt{machine\_endpoint} from (Cal), using its separately
discharged PA witness certificate. The word ``Unconditional'' in that
filename refers to removal of the witness-certificate hypothesis; the
table-calibration hypothesis remains explicit.

The two directions of (Cal) have different obligations. A modeled
physical halt must yield an internal numerical-calculus derivation and
then an internal PA refutation. Conversely, an arbitrary internal PA
refutation must be translated into a numerical-calculus proof, compiled
into a scheduler word, and realized by the physical table. The repository
now supplies scheduler-level components of these arguments, but its
README explicitly leaves the physical connection and full internal
forward compiler incomplete.

\paragraph{Why the written PA calibration does not automatically descend.}
The existing reports say that their proof translations are primitive
recursive and that PA proves their correctness. The correctness proof
must still be justified in the weaker base; primitive recursiveness of
the code alone does not supply that theorem. The pinned
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1c9524d8bf2e0a27b9a16ff622359927f536e663/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/pa194-forward-audit/README.md}{forward-compiler audit}
identifies unrestricted-induction uses in
\texttt{ordinary\_derivation\_requested} and
\texttt{fuel\_invariant}. Their invariants have not been shown to have
the needed restricted complexity. A previous substitution-translation
dependency has received an $\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1$ replacement, but that
does not complete the rest of the compiler.

The
\href{https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/blob/1c9524d8bf2e0a27b9a16ff622359927f536e663/Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/isigma1-transfer/README.md}{generic transfer development}
shows the useful sufficient target: an $\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1$ proof of
the positive PA-refutation/halting equivalence, followed by conservation
and G\"odel--Gentzen translation, yields the required negative iPRA
equivalence. It is therefore unnecessary to formalize the whole machine
directly in an intuitionistic calculus; the exact internal
$\mathrm{I}\Sigma_1$ calibration suffices.

and the older primitive-rule expansion/checking programs are included in

That negative result and the relative source theorem must not be
presented as a proof of (Goal).
