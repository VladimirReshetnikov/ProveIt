# Incoming PA consistency research, 14 November 2026

Commit `2337ce611a` adds five research archives under `docs/incoming`. Their
reports have been inspected for the claimed machine and proof status; their
proofs and verification scripts have not been independently audited here.

* `pa194qh_conpa_2026-11-13.zip` presents PA194QH, a different 194-working-state
  blank-start candidate. Its machine table has 388 rows, start state 0 and
  halt label 194. The table's SHA-256 is
  `0f101f654f69230744e8c9aac14a7616745e247855fd964530603ea1ac6412ba`,
  independently checked against `RESULTS.json`. The report claims a written
  PA proof of `¬Halt ↔ Con(PA)` by composing inherited calibration with new
  transformations; it explicitly does not claim a new end-to-end Lean or
  Rocq proof. This candidate is within the eventual 194-state scope, but it
  is not the currently pinned primary PA194 table.
* `pa_consistency_reduction_study_2026-11-13.zip` reports no established
  machine below 194 states. Its search-family obstructions do not claim a
  general lower bound.
* `pa_consistency_research_2026-11-14.zip` likewise reports no verified
  sub-194 machine; it describes a restricted all-offset obstruction and
  distinguishes written PA proofs from unfinished Lean/iPRA results.
* `pa_sub194_research.zip` reports a new 196-working-state MP196 design,
  not a sub-194 result.
* `pa_sub194_research_2026-11-13.zip` describes a 193-state runtime core,
  but explicitly does not claim it is a blank-start consistency machine;
  its tested unchanged startups fail.

These are report claims, not an independent validation of any consistency
reduction. The primary formal target remains the original PA194 table pinned
by SHA-256 `4bfea62b17aad7d8c8c5e682fe8942e0a86b93e4351f8e675e32705bdaae007f`.
