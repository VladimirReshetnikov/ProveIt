\subsection{Additional search for a 193-state blank-start machine}

The supplied incoming packet \texttt{pa\_sub194\_research\_2026-11-13.zip}
already contains a 193-control runtime core. In the primary native numbering,
it deletes control 301, redirects its incoming edges to 88, and changes
\[
 (88,0)\longmapsto(0,L,51),\qquad
 (51,0)\longmapsto(0,R,122).
\]
This saves one control on the prescribed runtime frames. At a unary-register
delimiter, the next cell is 1; old row $(16,1)$ and replacement row
$(122,1)$ both have action $(0,L,1)$. The marker operation therefore agrees
after two steps. The next-cell premise fails during the original blank
initialization. An independent concrete replay reaches state 35 at head
14 with support $\{-3,1\}$ after 92 steps. Its transition $(35,0)=(1,R,35)$
then gives an outward blank ray by induction.

\paragraph{Coupled startup repairs.}
The earlier packet screens individual candidate edits. The present extension
combines simultaneous edits to distinct rows, using its corrected radius-six
pool of 346 proposals. Of these, 327 are outside the arithmetic boxes and
19 are speculative box-row mutations. The latter are included only to make
the startup screen broader; their arithmetic correctness is not assumed.
Even two individually neutral edits need not remain neutral when combined.
Any successful startup would therefore require a new universal runtime
proof and the appropriate logical calibration.

The second row includes the first. Its runs consist of 117,274 halts,
11,159,792 certified outward rays, and 10,539 runs stopped at a finite
bound. The single-start screen has 58,072 rays and 413 bounded runs.

Each run is limited to 200,000 literal steps, absolute head displacement
20,000, and finite support size 20,000. The detector checks the \emph{whole}
finite tape support at native control 84, a source site from 25 through 44,
padding at least 48, nine arbitrary natural register values, and a
nonempty arbitrary spare tail. It deliberately accepts a larger class than
the logically admissible startup theorem: no proof-store zero condition is
imposed. Thus even a positive detection would be a proposal, not a
consistency-machine theorem. As a positive control, the same implementation
recovers the primary PA194 full startup frame at site 25 after exactly
58,154 steps, including all 66 one-cells and all 35 register/spare blocks.

The three-edit screen fixes native start 74 and enumerates 6,440,096
admissible distinct-row triples. Of these, 4,225,362 edit no row occurring
on the unchanged blank trajectory and are eliminated by the exact ray
argument. The remaining 2,214,734 runs execute 16,183,877,637 literal
transitions. They produce 2,134,087 certified rays and 80,647 bounded runs,
with no halt and no accepted full frame. The row-set premise of the pruning
step was independently checked against the 92-step ray certificate; the
portable program asserts it. These are results for the specified finite
candidate pool, not an exclusion of arbitrary three-row repairs.

\paragraph{Alternative local realizations of the merge.}
A separate finite synthesis modifies the merged control and one existing
helper row. The two-cell template checks 592,896 patches and produces 11
local identities. The known helper 51 has the supplied register-marker
justification; the other ten overwrite arithmetic roles. A radius-four,
100-step version checks 3,719,496 patches against every assignment of its
nine-cell window and produces 13 local identities. Its two additional
non-box alternatives change row 31/0 or 70/0. The complete symbolic source
checker rejects them at outcomes $(16,\mathrm{None})$ and
$(56,(2,\mathrm{True}))$, respectively.

An additional version uses 8,339 symbolic source windows, including
interiors of accelerated constant-symbol scans. It excludes arithmetic-box
helper mutations and finds no survivor among 3,100,352 proposals. These
counts concern explicitly bounded templates and the named source envelope.
They do not imply a lower bound of 194 states or exclude a different
arithmetic representation, initializer, source compiler, or coordinated
transformation outside the candidate pool.

The portable scripts, exact inputs, unchanged prior candidate list,
positive control, complete logs, and source attribution are in
\texttt{research/startup/}. The comparison above extends the particular
single-edit screen in the named incoming packet; it makes no general
priority claim about all historical searches in the repository.
