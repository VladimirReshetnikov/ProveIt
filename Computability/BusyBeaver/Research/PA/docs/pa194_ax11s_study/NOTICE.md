# Attribution and scope

This research builds on Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository and
the machine/compiler lineage retained in its research packets. The
repository, AX11 packet, logical manuscripts, and imported source files
are credited in `PROVENANCE.json`, the report bibliography, and the
component provenance records. Their original notices and licenses are
retained, including the supplied ProveIt, James Chang, and Wade notices.

Inherited ideas include the PA numerical proof calculus, its original
sum-based Shift, the symmetric comparator implementation, the AX11
fallthrough interface, history-conditioned folds, physical register
representation, compiler engines, and independent symbolic engines.
Their inclusion does not claim their invention here.

New work consists of the specific PA194AX11S physical/source composition,
its exact source binding and complete check receipts, the relative iPRA
source-equivalence argument, the cofinite coefficient-three collision
argument under its stated inherited propositional interface, the current
corpus comparison and reconciliation, and the scoped new search runs.

Unmodified historical reference files may describe claims made by their
authors. The current packet's claim status is stated in `README.md`,
`STATUS.json`, `report/main.pdf`, and `proof/proof_status.tex`. In
particular, inclusion of a reference file is not a new certification of
an absolute iPRA consistency theorem.

The inherited `reference/ax11_provenance.json` records its parent's own
earlier dependency revision. The enclosing archive used here is pinned
separately by this packet's top-level `PROVENANCE.json`.
