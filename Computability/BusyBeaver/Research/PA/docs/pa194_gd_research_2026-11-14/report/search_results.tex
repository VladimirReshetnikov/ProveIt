\label{sec:bounded-searches}

The searches described here did not produce a validated machine with fewer
than 194 states.  They are recorded to identify the attempted reductions and
the remaining limitations.  They are not lower bounds on the size of a
machine equivalent to PA consistency.  In particular, an unsuccessful search
within a fixed interpreter, grammar representation, or local quotient model
does not rule out a different implementation.

\subsection{Grammar and parameter-routing searches}

Eight timed runs explored reassociations, compatible sharing, and parameter
or head rearrangements.  Their logs record a total of
\textbf{37,916,566 mutation slots}, with 3,558,249 accepted mutations.
A slot is an iteration of the mutation loop; it is not necessarily a distinct
grammar or even a completed proposal, since some selected nodes admit no
rotation.  The seven ordinary runs used 600-second limits, and the head-order
run used 300 seconds.  Timing limits make a fresh run's final iteration count
hardware dependent even with the same random seed.

The score counts the variable reader contribution after the searcher's
compatible-sharing screen and its grammar penalties; it is not a complete
semantic certificate or simply the raw number of AX readers.  The recorded
effective fixed contribution is 134 except in the top-only run, where it is

hit was found.  The top-only semantic idea already occurs in the supplied
research corpus; this run is not a claim of a novel 195-state design.

The exact input templates, attribute tables, random seeds, resulting search
logs, and aggregate counters are preserved under \texttt{research/grammar/}.
The head-order implementation splits and rejoins the seven emitted head
blocks while preserving each selected block's internal tree.  Candidates
from these heuristic screens would still require source-graph, command,
physical-runtime, and calibration checks before becoming a result.

\subsection{Finite composition and continuation sweeps}

The composition screen examined
\[
 3\cdot2\cdot2\cdot3\cdot2\cdot2\cdot3=432
\]
combinations: three inherited grammar shapes; the original or explicit
binder; ordinary or exchanged distribution; diagonal parameter $A$, $D$, or
$H$; two final-AX exits; two decoder exits; and three Shift implementations.
Its minimum optimistic raw-state-minus-matching count was 194, with no count
below 194.  A portable replay reproduced all 60 qualifying rows of the
original log exactly.  This screen intentionally included counterfactual
zero-test substitutions without claiming their semantic side conditions.
The three delivered machines instead use ordinary structural exits and
receive the separate complete checks documented elsewhere in this report.

The corrected continuation sweep considered ten compatible binary shapes
of the four-increment loop in each of four parent implementations: primary,
AX11H, AX11GH, and AX11GHS.  It retained all \textbf{40 candidates} only after
checking equality of the entire resolved source instruction graph, including
all branch and jump targets.  Fresh helper labels were allocated without
overwriting any existing state; the bundled report is the corrected result.
The optimistic counts were 194 for 23 candidates and 195 for 17 candidates.
None was below 194.  These are local sharing scores, not assertions that every
optimistic matching has been certified simultaneously or that all forty
candidates are new machines.

\subsection{Local physical two-row quotients}

A separate search considered every unordered pair of the 194 nonhalting
controls in each delivered table.  It synthesized both actions of the
surviving control, permitting repeated visits to that control.  It required
the proposed replacement to reproduce the old transition's complete local
tape, head displacement, and successor within a radius-four window and at
most 100 transitions.  The screened action targets were remaining
nonhalting controls of the given table.

No quotient passed this screen.  Contexts were obtained from symbolic source
instruction executions, including interiors of accelerated scans.  This
context collection intentionally excludes startup and does not narrow the
arithmetic-engine controls.  These choices and the fixed radius, return
bound, action space, and one-control quotient shape restrict the conclusion
to the stated search model.  They do not exclude larger windows, different
control structures, simultaneous changes to other states, or a new machine
architecture.

\subsection{Replaying the research}

\texttt{research/README.md} gives offline replay commands.  The composition
and corrected loop sweeps use the bundled construction modules and inherited
input snapshots.  The grammar and physical searches use Python entrypoints
to compile the supplied C++17 sources and write fresh outputs to a chosen
directory.  The research receipts remain distinct from the positive
construction and proof certificates for PA194GD, PA194GDH, and PA194GDHS.
