\section{Comparison with the complete pinned machine corpus}
\label{sec:corpus-comparison}

\subsection{Scope, snapshot, and what was checked}

The comparison is pinned to ProveIt revision
\texttt{8d31445d858761a8f6150c2e5012adae8895e932}.
The relevant delivery collection has 87 filed packets under
\texttt{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/docs} and six pending ZIPs
under \texttt{docs/incoming}: 93 deliveries in total. A delivery is not
a distinct machine. Several packets reproduce the same transition table,
and some carry materially newer proofs for an unchanged table.

Every one of the 718 tracked PA files ending in \texttt{.tm} was read and
parsed. All six pending ZIPs were downloaded at the pinned revision; their
Git blob identities were recomputed and matched the GitHub directory
metadata. Their 3,058 top-level members include 1,439 literal machine tables
with 488 distinct byte payloads. The top-level table census therefore
contains 2,157 occurrences before any graph identification. The metadata
ledger covers all 87 filed deliveries; its summaries are explicitly
attributed to the supplied packet descriptions, rather than presented as
93 independently reconstructed arithmetic proofs.

The executable recognizer reads literal five-column transition tables. It
does not execute arbitrary generators, parse prose as a table, or claim
exhaustive recognition of tables encoded only in arbitrary JSON. Recursive
inspection of the pending nested archives expands ten distinct ZIP payloads
in all, deduplicating repeated ZIP bytes. This extension gives 2,826 literal
table occurrences and 876 distinct byte payloads. Twenty-one small,
partial, or experimental payloads fail the complete-one-terminal convention
used by the structural classifier; none removes a 194-control table from
the comparison below. The exact exclusions and all source locators are
retained in the machine-readable census.

The newly performed checks are byte-identity verification, literal parsing,
exact structural classification, comparison of the named command interfaces,
and reconciliation of proof versions. Execution and theorem-verification
claims quoted from an older packet remain attributed to that packet unless
the main report explicitly records a fresh replay. In particular, an
inventory count is not a count of established consistency theorems.

\subsection{Exact structural equivalence and the 139-class frontier}

For a complete binary table and a prospective root, enumerate controls by
the deterministic breadth-first traversal that examines read-0 before
read-1. Record the complete transition tuple in that numbering. Discard
roots that do not span all working controls, and take the least tuple over
the remaining roots. The comparison additionally permits global tape
reflection, global symbol exchange, and erasure of the final write/move
decoration on entry to halt. Thus it is a deliberately stronger structural
novelty screen than equality with the declared start. Global symbol exchange
is \emph{not} asserted to preserve an all-zero blank input. Full tuples are
compared; SHA-256 values are compact identifiers, not the definition of
equivalence. Equal identifier hashes are checked for exact tuple equality.

\subsection{Proof versions matter independently of machine identity}

The pinned top-level PA README says that the end-to-end machine-specific
iPRA endpoint is not established by the repository's completed Lean
developments. This statement must be distinguished from the newer
\emph{written mathematical} calibration retained inside several pending
archives. The revised AX11H ZIP has SHA-256
\texttt{93ee082ef035b2d32e71d6af5239b25b4bea1773f03bea4d2c39810a7fe15075}
and is preserved byte for byte in four of the current pending packets.
It supplies ten proof TeX fragments addressing the physical internalization,
source/command compiler, formula and term adapters, distribution exchange,
and both consistency directions via the stated weak-arithmetic transfer.

Later GH/GHS, fast, HR and LC\_AX11H manuscripts explicitly import this
revised written theorem. The SEL packet has an older, more limited boundary:
it proves a direct relative iPRA equivalence to the original primary, and
does not itself claim the absolute calibration. Its copy of AX11GH also
states a remaining physical-internalization obligation. These are different
proof packages for the same graph, not contradictory numerical table
identifications. A correct synthesis identifies the exact proof dependency
it uses and separately reports that no new end-to-end Lean kernel theorem
has been compiled.

The cache-only interface has a concrete compiler consequence. With visible
top $t=1$ and cache $h=0$, one Shift does not detect contradiction. The GH
compiler must finish with its certified Pop word, or with the equivalent
Push--Shift--Shift detection pattern, and must account for all four AX
operands. This is a semantic issue, not a matter of cosmetic source notation.

\subsection{Why existing 193-control files are not smaller solutions}

The historical collection includes 53 classes at 193 controls. The files
and their labels include rejected runtime splices and diagnostic source
proposals; they are not 53 accepted consistency machines. A common
193-control runtime fold merges controls that agree at initialized source
frames but disagree on the exceptional blank-start read-zero history.
Consequently, a source-frame simulation alone does not make that folded
table a blank-input consistency machine.

The recent AX11G packet reports an exhaustive all-start examination of
52 such physical tables: 10,036 starts, with 106 finite halts and 9,930
certified outward rays, all within 117 steps. The count excludes the
separate coefficient-three logical proposal, whose prior rejection has a
different reason. These are attributed prior results, not a newly performed
replay in this comparison. Later packets also report bounded multirow
startup repairs, grammar searches and contextual quotients. Their capped
runs remain unresolved by those runs; the negative searches give no lower
bound for arbitrary 193-state machines.

The practical acceptance condition is therefore joint: exact recurring
source behavior, a valid complete startup from the all-zero tape, and the
identified arithmetic calibration must apply to the \emph{same} literal
table. A low runtime state count, an apparently good prefix, or graph
novelty alone is insufficient.

\subsection{Reproducibility material}

The compact evidence consists of the 139 representative tables; the
87-packet, 110-historical-design and 11-pending-principal ledgers;
the six archive byte/Git identities; and the complete literal census.
The standalone \texttt{census\_tables.py} uses Python's standard library.
It accepts directories, literal tables and ZIPs, recursively inspects ZIPs,
and writes its complete tuples' class identifiers and representatives to a
chosen output directory. To reproduce the reported scope exactly, a fresh
run supplies the 718 tracked PA \texttt{.tm} files and the six pending
archives as inputs; passing the whole PA directory would additionally
expand its older filed ZIP archives. The program explicitly records nonspanning
tables and recognizer exclusions rather than silently treating them as
accepted candidates.
