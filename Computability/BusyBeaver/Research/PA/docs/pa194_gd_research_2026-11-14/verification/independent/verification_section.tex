\section{Fresh independent verification of the three GD tables}

All three constructions passed a second literal-tape checker that imports
neither the construction compiler nor an arithmetic-box oracle. The checker
rederives four generic unary/list-sweep lemmas from each table and checks
every primitive source branch. Its source-address graph is resolved by
two independent procedures, whose complete instruction arrays agree.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
\toprule
Table & Sites & Outcomes & Symbolic operations & Maximum & Startup\\
\midrule
PA194GD & 3,654 & 4,755 & 1,068,318 & 379 & 58,154\\
PA194GDH & 3,653 & 4,754 & 1,091,812 & 385 & 57,922\\
PA194GDHS & 3,650 & 4,750 & 1,095,734 & 385 & 57,768\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Here ``Maximum'' means the largest symbolic proof-operation count for one
source outcome. It is not a uniform bound on literal execution time for
arbitrary register values. ``Startup'' is the number of literal binary
transitions from the blank tape to the complete initial source frame.

The symbolic domain contains nine arbitrary natural primary registers,
both decrement branches, an arbitrary nonempty finite spare-register list,
an arbitrary nonnegative extra header gap, and complete blank exteriors.
These are universal symbolic contracts, not a finite range of sampled
register values. A separate closure audit verifies that every required
source branch occurs exactly once. Every coefficient of every thirteen-slot
affine duration vector is nonnegative, and every duration has constant term
at least three. All nonhalting outcomes have zero affine head motion. Each
table has exactly one halting source outcome, of duration exactly three.
Every one of its 388 literal rows occurs among the symbolic cases.

A further startup implementation uses an unbounded integer-coordinate set
of one-cells and independently compares the entire final tape support with
the encoded source frame. It verifies the complete source address, physical
register permutation, seed $(2,0,\ldots,0)$ and all 26 spare one-registers.
The check is not a local-window comparison. The dense/native label maps,
194 working controls, 388 rows and graph reachability are checked directly.

Exact complete structural tuples show that the three tables are mutually
distinct and differ from all 139 current spanning 194-control classes,
even after arbitrary state renaming, any spanning root, tape reflection,
symbol exchange and terminal-action erasure. Ordinary control bisimulation
retains 194 classes for each table. This last fact excludes a direct
context-free control quotient, but is not a lower bound against contextual
reductions or redesigned machines.

The verifiers operated on frozen copies, and a final SHA-256 audit confirms
that all input payloads remain unchanged. The retained JSON receipts,
complete symbolic contracts, four list-lemma traces, full startup supports,
class comparisons and transcript are included. These are independently
replayed implementation certificates. The source-command argument and
PA/iPRA calibration remain the separate mathematical arguments identified
in the report; no new end-to-end Lean or Rocq kernel theorem is inferred
from the Python checks.
