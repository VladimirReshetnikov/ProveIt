A completed two-sided local-convergence search on PA194C used sound
radius-six blank-start abstract reachability, giving 265,503 state/window
contexts. It examined 133,942 ordered deletion/shift/marker relations and
180,573 tested windows with bounds of 100 local transitions on each side.

scan also found no pair, including arithmetic controls excluded from an older
search. A sharper source-frame analysis produced 12,393 windows for PA194
and 12,282 for PA194C; both radius-four, 100-step two-sided scans found no

not mathematical lower bounds.

A proposed deletion of native scanner control 34 fails 113 universal source
