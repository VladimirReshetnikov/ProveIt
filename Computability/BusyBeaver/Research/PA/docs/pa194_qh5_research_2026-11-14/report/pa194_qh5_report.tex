\begin{abstract}
The strict target of fewer than 194 states was not reached. The fallback
construction is an explicit deterministic one-tape, two-symbol machine
PA194QH5 with 194 working states, 388 transitions, and an all-zero initial
tape. Its source combines coefficient-five formula constructors, exchanged
premises in the universal-distribution axiom, modus ponens without a dummy
stack push, and a Shift command that tests its cached formula. These component
ideas already occur separately in the repository; the present combination
and its checked literal table are new within the audited corpus.

The allocation is $135+67-8=194$. All 4,815 source outcomes of the
3,714-site source pass the construction verifier. Complete blank startup
takes 58,088 literal transitions. The manuscript gives an ordinary
mathematical preservation proof, with its inherited arithmetic calibration
identified, of
\[
 \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg\Halt(\mathrm{PA194QH5},\mathrm{blank})
                     \leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
\]
A newly completed Lean or Rocq proof is not claimed.

The comparison pins ProveIt at \snapshot, covers 61 filed packets and five
incoming archives, and rechecks all 535 classes of the repository's official
catalogue. A broader scan recognizes 77 distinct 194-control table classes,
including diagnostics and rejected attempts. The new table is distinct from
all of them under state renaming, choice of spanning start, reflection,
symbol exchange, and ignored terminal-edge effects. Additional searches
test compound repairs of the prior 193-state runtime and cyclic state
mergers; their negative results are bounded and are not state lower bounds.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, conventions, and evidence}

The requested fallback is delivered. PA194QH5 has working states
$0,\ldots,193$, starts in state zero with the head at cell zero, and has a
separate halt label 194. Its tape is two-way infinite, its alphabet is
$\{0,1\}$, and its initial tape is identically zero. Every action writes
one bit and moves one cell left or right. No input, extra tape symbol, or
stationary move is hidden in the count.

The authoritative dense table is \file{machine/machine.tm}; its SHA-256 is
\begin{quote}\small
\nolinkurl{94c85547b3edd0df0a441d45bf22abb52711f2b27cc9e680999232285757dba5}.
\end{quote}
The native table starts at 74 and halts at 254. The exact state bijection
is supplied in \file{machine/labels.json}. Both tables are included, and
Appendix~\ref{app:table} prints every dense transition.

Three different claims must be distinguished. The transition table and its
finite initialization are concrete computational objects. The universal
source contracts use symbolic unary registers and arbitrary spare lists,
with mathematical induction justifying their symbolic operations. The
consistency theorem then composes those implementation facts with syntactic
PA proof transformations. A long nonhalting simulation would establish none
of the latter two claims. Nor is a Python receipt an end-to-end theorem
accepted by Lean or Rocq.

The repository's current PA README explicitly distinguishes its ordinary
PA calibration claims from its unfinished internal iPRA endpoint. This
report preserves that distinction. It does not claim that the existing
iPRA formalization, or the exact new table's iPRA equivalence, is complete.
The intended base for the displayed theorem is PA.\cite{status}

\section{The strict reduction attempts and what they establish}

\subsection{The 193-state runtime and the bootstrap obstruction}

The strongest small runtime in the incoming material identifies native
control 301 with 88 and changes
\[
 \delta(88,0)=(0,L,51),\qquad
 \delta(51,0)=(0,R,122).
\]
It retains 193 working controls and passes the primary PA194 source's
4,780 symbolic outcomes from an initialized frame. The changed arithmetic
interface is justified at a unary register delimiter whose next bit is one:
the old successor 16 and the new successor 122 both act on that one as
$(0,L,1)$, giving the same two-step endpoint on the complete tape.

Blank initialization needs a different case. In the unmodified primary
startup, native state 51 is reached at a delimiter followed by zero. The
two-step marker lemma does not apply there. With the reduced table, start
74 reaches an outward-writing ray. The incoming packet gives exact finite
certificates for all unchanged-core blank starts and one-bit handovers.
Thus the smaller runtime is not a completed blank-tape consistency machine.

The present work extends the startup search to combinations of row
substitutions. Starting from the incoming corrected radius-six menu of
346 individual proposals, it adaptively explores edits that are reached
under the current edited run. It tests up to two distinct row edits from
each of the 193 starts, and up to three from start 74. A target is any
complete canonical frame at source sites 25--44, even allowing arbitrary
values in all nine registers and the spare list. This target is deliberately
broader than the logically admissible seeds.

The menu includes speculative arithmetic-box substitutions, so even a hit
would have required complete semantic verification. Source, exact inputs,
logs, and a receipt are in \file{research/startup/}.

\subsection{Cyclic mergers and eager halting}
\input{report/search_metrics.tex}

The eager-MP direction has a separate logical attraction. If contradiction
is detected at every successful MP that produces one, no completed
nonhalting command store needs to contain one. A later Shift contradiction
test can then be redundant. However, the existing test also clears
temporary registers, and its structured break addresses depend on the
enclosing binary tree. A source expression cannot be shortened merely by
deleting its halt leaf.

One focused attempted replacement changed
\file{node21=(node7,node20)} to \file{node21=(node6,node20)}, with
\file{node7=(node6,halt)}. This makes a mismatch break skip
\file{node20} instead of reaching it. The source counterexample
$t=h=0,w=3=p(2,0)$ leaves the stack equal to three and the cached formula zero;
the intended total Shift must instead pop it to stack zero and cache two.
This counterexample already satisfies the no-one store invariant.
This is a rejected rewrite, not an additional consistency construction.
The diagnostic explains why the tempting two-control saving does not
follow from the no-one invariant alone. A corrected auxiliary construction
preserves the wrapper and replaces the old Shift-only halt leaf by a
decrement that is unreachable under the invariant. It costs 197 states,
passes 4,769 source contracts, and has an exact 58,154-step startup. Its
properly scoped proof and reproducer are included in
\file{research/eager_shift/}; it is not the requested smaller machine.

\section{What transfers from ZFC and ConciseIPRA}

The relevant ZFC work contributes concrete implementation techniques:
history-sensitive folds, sharing decoder controls with arithmetic scanners,
command-local field reading, and whole-tape startup certificates. The
301-to-88 runtime experiment is an instance of this transfer. Its success
after initialization and failure before initialization show why runtime and
bootstrap obligations must be kept separate. The ZFC scan-clone diagnostics
also exhibit uses of the same helper whose distinguishing information is
far away in the control prefix; a short local window may not separate them.

ConciseIPRA contributes a different lesson. Its current scalar and clause
presentations derive logical infrastructure from small primitive rule
families and evaluation/transport schemes. This can guide a redesign of
the proof enumerator. But its declared optimization measure is the size of
a complete software proof checker, and its target theory is bi-interpretable
with iPRA. Neither quantity is a bound on the number of states of a binary
blank-tape PA consistency machine. Replacing the searched theory by iPRA
does not preserve the required consistency sentence. A transfer must retain
a PA-refutation compiler and a converse PA-proof extraction, including the
full-induction instance used for finite comprehension in the current
calibration. No smaller exact TM compiler for such a replacement is
constructed here.\cite{ipra}

\section{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Can the 193-state runtime initialize a semantically different
source frame?} The available seed theorem permits dirty work registers and
some provable formula stores. Search directly for such frames, with a
certificate that distinguishes every pre-frame exception from later runtime.
The remaining task is a whole-tape bootstrap, not another long run.
\item \textbf{Can eager MP eliminate its own extra decoding cost?} A correct
total Shift must preserve cleanup and ancestor-sensitive jumps. Reassociate
the eager-MP and Shift source together, then check resolved successors and
the no-one invariant before synthesizing a physical quotient.
\item \textbf{Can a fold recover information from the distant control prefix?}
The current local searches are bounded by tape windows and path lengths.
A carefully proved excursion to the prefix may distinguish contexts that
all nearby-marker tests identify. Its extra scanner states must be counted.
\item \textbf{Can the fixed controller and AX decoder be optimized jointly?}
The familiar $134+67-7$ and $135+67-8$ decompositions are accounting
identities, not lower bounds. Cross-component sharing is more promising
than assuming their parts are independently minimal.
\item \textbf{Can a smaller calculus retain full PA calibration?} The
evaluation/discharge ideas in ConciseIPRA suggest an alternative representation
of proof rules. The useful next deliverable would be a concrete PA-calibrated
source, an explicit command compiler, and a measured binary TM translation.
\item \textbf{Can the ordinary proof be reduced to a short formal endpoint?}
The corpus now contains numerous tables but no completed machine-specific
iPRA endpoint for the main PA family. A parametric coefficient/controller
theorem would let future variants share most of that formal proof while
requiring fresh exact-table and startup bindings.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Reproduction and deliverable map}

The new construction is self-contained. Python 3.10 or later suffices for
its builders and symbolic checkers; no external repository is needed to
rebuild or verify PA194QH5. From the archive's root run:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -B verify.py
\end{verbatim}
Run without Python's \file{-O} flag: assertions are part of the certificate
checks. The second physical engine can take several minutes. It rebuilds
the source and table, checks complete frames for arbitrary natural registers,
rederives its generic register-list lemmas from the literal arithmetic rows,
and checks the exact dense/native conjugacy and blank startup.
