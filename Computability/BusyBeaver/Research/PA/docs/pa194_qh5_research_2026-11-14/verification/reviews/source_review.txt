Radix calibration
-----------------
The hereditary map correctly recodes numerical variable labels as well as formula trees. The left inverse J supplies injectivity and thus guard preservation; it is not improperly used to translate arbitrary radix-five proofs back to radix four. The exceptional I(0,0)=0 case is addressed. The interpretation of unused residue four as truth is necessary because arbitrary command parameters may use it, and is included. The non-monotonicity of F is correctly handled by translating a restricted rho leaf through the unrestricted rho8All template, whose own target leaves satisfy their actual target order conditions. There is no unsupported claim that F preserves subtraction, addition, Cantor stacks, or literal machine trajectories.

Command compiler
----------------
Independently checked the displayed identities algebraically:
* After AX;S^9 the top is q8, stack [old_top;old_stack], and cache U(old_d,old_h). Push's final MP0 therefore fails its implication/universal residue comparison and returns the required zero top with the saved stack.
* Pop's eleventh Shift tests exactly the saved old top, then removes one underlying stack entry. It remains correct for the Cantor zero alias.
* Discard=MP0 returns the same zero store fields on both matching branches when the offset is zero, for arbitrary cache.
* Offset_i also works at i=0, where the matching branch gives the same required result.
* Gen_i carries the old formula into the cache before AX and selects U(i,a) without an unintended halt.
* The q_j selector uses j+1 Shifts and 9-j Pops. The propositional and gate selectors have the correct remaining stack counts.
* The MP compiler explicitly reserves the otherwise missing zero beneath its two premises. Its two Shifts expose a and I(a,b), then MP_b and Pop return b with a restored old stack, or halt on an intermediate/final contradiction. The all-store leading AX and final Pop handle arbitrary carried caches and stacks. A plain final Shift would be wrong, and the manuscript correctly rejects it.
