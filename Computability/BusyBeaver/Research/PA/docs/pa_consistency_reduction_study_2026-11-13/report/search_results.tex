The research artifacts separate discovery from certification. A favorable reader score is not accepted as a transition table, and a successful local tape detour is not accepted without a global source and initialization argument.

\subsection{Control sharing beyond one-step endpoints}
The finite-window searches permit the old and replacement executions to rejoin after several old steps, rather than requiring the replacement to reproduce exactly the old first transition. Complete local tape and head equality are required at the meeting point, and the replacement detour avoids the modified controls or rows in its interior. The reference segment is evaluated in the unchanged original table and may pass through those controls. The PA194N6 screens found no accepted common-action merge or edge bypass within their documented radius-five context family. The common-action merge screen used an 80-step old bound and a 300-step replacement bound; the edge-bypass screen used 300 for each.

A second screen permits whole-instruction reconvergence with arbitrary unary register values using the retained symbolic executor. The primary PA194 search produced one candidate after preliminary source-context screening; it failed the complete source-contract check. No 193-state quotient was certified by this search.

\subsection{Reachable source branches}
An inductively closed zero/positive abstraction from the actual clean seed contains 263,888 site-mask pairs. It reaches 3,671 of the 3,673 source sites and permits 4,777 of the 4,780 primitive outcome contracts. Nevertheless, the conservative symbolic executions of those reachable contracts, the protected arithmetic-block rows, and the exact startup collectively use every one of the 388 transition rows. Thus this abstraction exposes no unused transition row to exploit as a free merge condition. It does not prove that a stronger relational invariant would fail.

\subsection{Grammar, negation, and scanner searches}
For the optimized primary and Q grammars we checked all 256 choices of replacing the eight eligible negation fragments by the existing 18-instruction arithmetic implementation. None improved the optimistic effective reader score below 60 in the inherited association. Further stochastic runs jointly vary these choices and associations. Another run prices scanner extensions as physical controls rather than rejecting every longer address outright. The separate scanner-shortening search enforces the more restrictive zero-run bounds needed after removing a scanner control.

An integer-programming experiment searches repeated-substring grammars for the same macro word. Its objective is raw binary grammar cost, which does not include compatible folds, scanner feasibility, or startup. A good integer-programming result therefore still requires physical reconstruction. An initial run was terminated by the execution environment and is not recorded as completed negative evidence. A subsequent single-threaded run completed in 359.957 seconds: HiGHS reported optimum and dual bound $66.99999999999977$, with zero gap. The retained 25-production witness has exact binary cost 67 and exactly the pinned 190-token macro word, independently checked with integer and symbolic operations. The lower bound remains a solver report; no exact optimality certificate is supplied for this dictionary problem. In particular, this is different from the exact rational selector obstruction below.
