\section{What the suggested ZFC and ConciseIPRA developments contribute}
\label{sec:transferred-ideas}

The two suggested directories provide useful implementation and proof
techniques.  The consistency calibration remains a separate obligation.
The discussion here uses the same pinned repository commit as the comparison,
\texttt{4be5c6e3a10292a7466f5d3687e3b39615ff049b}.
It is based on the exact sources named below; no fresh Lean or Rocq build of
those developments was performed in this study.

\subsection{ZFC: contextual folds, return paths, and protected storage}

\paragraph{The historical $301\mathbin{\to}88$ experiment already contains
the startup warning.}
The ZFC continuation study deletes native state 301 and changes exactly
\[
  87/0=0R88,\qquad 88/0=0L51,\qquad 51/0=0R122.
\]
This is the antecedent of the PA \texttt{Runtime193} experiment.  The
\TransferSource{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/k218-continuation-search/all-starts/README.md}{ZFC all-starts record}
proves that all 217 existing choices of initial state for its exact shortened
table, on a blank tape, eventually enter an infinite zero ray.  Its certificate
condition is simple and unbounded: the scanned cell is zero, the zero transition
returns to the same state, and it moves into a half-line containing no ones.
The written cell stays behind the head, so induction preserves the condition.
Consequently a valid reduction on initialized unary-register frames does not
by itself supply a complete blank-start machine.  In particular, the known
PA runtime fold cannot be counted as a 193-state consistency construction
until its startup is repaired and the revised table is calibrated.

The follow-up
\TransferSource{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/k218-continuation-search/marker-relay/README.md}{marker-relay record}
is also instructive.  A temporary marker and six redirected return paths repair
some local behavior, but a literal counterexample at a later \texttt{CC2}
occurrence refutes the unchanged source-interface contract.  This is why a
new startup edit must be checked against every source occurrence, including
short backward returns, rather than only against its initial tape trace.

\paragraph{History can replace control state, with an exact whole-tape proof.}
The
\TransferSource{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/candidate-k215/alternatives/conditional-marker/README.md}{conditional-marker construction}
shares old roles 52 and 176 when their zero branch can recover the origin
from a neighboring bit; the exceptional incoming path is proved to read one.
Its matching blocks take one or three steps and preserve the entire tape and
head.  The
\TransferSource{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/candidate-k215/alternatives/context-returns/README.md}{history-return construction}
similarly uses one- and two-transition histories to justify five row changes.
The completed engine census frees two individual protected rows without
deleting their states.  These are concrete design principles for a future PA
fold: prove the contextual marker fact, reuse the resulting row or role, and
replay the complete changed decoder.  Their state savings cannot simply be
added to a PA table whose existing folds may already use the same roles.

\paragraph{A logical zero and a physical spare have different jobs.}
The
\TransferSource{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/candidate-k215/alternatives/zero-spare-transfer/README.md}{zero-spare transfer construction}
combines an append-zero physical allocation policy with a source-level
protected-tail argument.  Its initial safe theorem heads permanently hide an
unsafe suffix under the scheduler's proved stack-debt bound.  The same packet
contains the complete no-successful-MP-zero-push compiler; it explicitly
reserves a logical zero below the premises when cleanup needs one.  The present
AX11Q6 proof uses this latter principle in its all-store MP block.  A stored
logical zero is not a physical spare-register block, and neither can be
discarded merely because its numeral is zero.  In particular, Cantor's
$p(0,0)=0$ alias requires proofs with explicit wrapper lists over an arbitrary
fixed suffix, rather than an assumed injective stack length.

These contributions concern simulation and proof enumeration.  The inherited
ZFC numerical calculus is calibrated to $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$;
substituting its proof search would change the target predicate.  The new
194-state construction therefore retains the PA-calibrated numerical calculus
while changing its operational interface and coefficient.

\subsection{ConciseIPRA: prove a smaller interface before pricing its machine}

The
\TransferSource{Logic/PrimitiveRecursiveArithmetic/ConciseIPRA/Lean/ConciseIPRA/NativeBiInterpretation.lean}{native bi-interpretation source}
contains the explicit theorems \texttt{native\_iff\_pra},
\texttt{pra\_iff\_native}, both round-trip results, and
\texttt{native\_inconsistent\_iff}.  Their target is full first-order
intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic.  They do not identify that
calculus's refutations with PA refutations.  A smaller checker for that
calculus would thus require an additional PA calibration before it could
answer this problem.

The useful transferable method is explicit elimination of operational
structure.  In
\TransferSource{Logic/PrimitiveRecursiveArithmetic/ConciseIPRA/Lean/ConciseIPRA/NativeOneBody.lean}{\texttt{NativeOneBody.lean}},
a context entry stores one formula body instead of an antecedent/body pair.
An ordinary assumption is represented by a gate body.  Soundness and
completeness are proved through \texttt{OBPrf.toNPrf} and
\texttt{CPrf.toOB}, with a binder-depth induction supplying access to the
stored assumption.  The result is not justified by counting constructors
alone: the gate, substitutions, fresh variables, and access proof are all
part of the translation.

The
\TransferSource{Logic/PrimitiveRecursiveArithmetic/ConciseIPRA/Artifacts/source_dispatch/README.txt}{source-dispatch proposal}
offers a related example.  It proves literal-zero-source equality transport
admissible, permitting syntax to choose between two elimination majors.  The
restriction is syntactic: a nonliteral term denoting zero remains permitted.
Its README carefully distinguishes a written universal proof from executable
instances and from a mechanized theorem.  This suggests seeking a cheaper
PA command interface through derivable rules and source-controlled dispatch,
but Python tokens, AST nodes, and bytes are not binary TM states.  The complete
cost must include input enumeration, freshness or substitution machinery,
normalization if used, decoding, and blank initialization.

\subsection{Available interfaces for a future weaker-base formalization}

The PA tree already provides a precise integration target.  In
\TransferSource{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/isigma1-machine/ConPAMachineEndpoint.lean}{\texttt{ConPAMachineEndpoint.lean}},
\texttt{Calibrated T h} requires, in every model $V$ of $I\Sigma_1$,
\[
 V\models\operatorname{Prov}_{\mathrm{PA}}(\bot)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists t\in V\; \operatorname{state}(\operatorname{run}_T(t))=h.
\]
Both proof codes and clock times may be nonstandard.
\TransferSource{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/isigma1-machine/ConPAUnconditional.lean}{\texttt{ConPAUnconditional.lean}}
discharges the inherited PA witness-certificate hypothesis and derives the
iPRA consistency/nonhalting equivalence from this machine-specific calibration.
The hypothesis \texttt{Calibrated T h} remains; the theorem does not verify a
particular table.  The
\TransferSource{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/isigma1-transfer/README.md}{transfer documentation}
and
\TransferSource{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/isigma1-machine/README.md}{machine documentation}
make this boundary explicit.  These interfaces are useful future targets;
they are not invoked here as a new kernel-checked endpoint for AX11Q6.

\subsection{Three concrete questions left by the 193-state barrier}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Can the $301\to88$ initialized-frame reduction be paired with a
      different blank bootstrap whose physical rows retain all-natural
      runtime contracts?  The search target should be a complete tape seed
      admitted by the all-store compiler, including a proved protected-tail
      alternative if needed.  A clean-looking finite frame alone is
      insufficient.
\item Can an equality-free or intrinsically typed numerical calculus give
      a three-tag emitter while preserving PA refutations?  It must avoid
      the concrete alias $E_3(1,0)=I_3(0,1)=6$ and provide a new complete
      translation.  External type annotations cannot repair the unchanged
      numerical machine, which does not check them.
\item Can a derivable-rule or gate-body translation remove one complete
      decoder role, rather than only shorten source text?  Such a proposal
      should be compiled and priced together with its guards, fresh-variable
      handling, and initialization, then attached to the explicit
      \texttt{Calibrated} interface if a weaker-base formal endpoint is sought.
\end{enumerate}

These are scoped research questions, not assertions that any listed change
saves a state or that 194 states is minimal.
