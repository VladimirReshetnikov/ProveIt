\begin{abstract}
This report supplies the requested 194-state fallback: a complete binary,
single-tape, blank-start Turing machine, PA194AX11Q6, together with a written
PA-provable consistency equivalence, its exact transition table, deterministic
reconstruction, and two physical verification implementations. No machine
with fewer than 194 working states was established.

The new table combines the inherited eleven-entry AX fallthrough protocol
with coefficient-six formula arithmetic, the exchanged quantifier-distribution
head, and no-push modus ponens. It is a new verified composition of those
ingredients. Its recurring source interface differs from its nearest parents,
and its complete action graph differs from all 82 existing 194-state classes
recognized in the pinned corpus, even under the additional symmetries described
below. It is not claimed as a new general proof-search architecture.

The comparison covers 72 delivered research packets and 638 recognized
spanning action-graph classes. A fresh audit of all 52 physical 193-state
classes tests every existing state as a blank initial state: all 10,036
computations reach a halt or an inductively certified outward ray within
117 steps. A separate 193-state class has the inherited coefficient-three
logical collision. These are obstructions for the supplied tables, not a
lower bound on all possible consistency machines.

The consistency theorem uses the explicitly retained mathematical
calibration of the numerical calculus to PA. It is an ordinary mathematical
proof with reproducible executable certificates, not a newly compiled
end-to-end Lean/Rocq theorem or a completed iPRA formalization.

\section{The result and its precise scope}

The machine has working states $0,\ldots,193$, start state
$0$, and a distinct terminal halt label $194$. It starts with its head at
coordinate zero on a two-way infinite all-zero tape. The alphabet is
$\{0,1\}$. Each transition writes one bit and moves exactly one cell left or
right; the halt label has no outgoing transitions. Thus the state count is
194, excluding the halt label, with exactly 388 transition rows.

\begin{theorem}[Ordinary mathematical PA equivalence]
Let $H_M$ assert that the exact delivered table, started as above,
reaches its halt label after a finite number of steps.
Using the numerical-calculus calibration identified in
Section~\ref{sec:ax11q6-audit}, the construction proves
\[
 \PA\vdash H_M\ \longleftrightarrow\
 \exists p\,\Prf_{\PA}(p,\ulcorner0=1\urcorner).
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \boxed{\ \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg H_M\longleftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).\ }
\]
\end{theorem}

This is a proof of a biconditional in PA. It does not assert that
PA proves either its own consistency or this machine's nonhalting. If PA
is consistent, the ordinary external conclusion is that the machine does
not halt, while its nonhalting is not provable in PA.

\subsection{What is new}

Relative to PA194QN6, AX11Q6 replaces the final AX return by a push and
falls through into the existing full MP operand reader. Nine axiom entries
and the generalized cached formula are stored, together with the old visible top. The command
therefore consumes a fourth operand and exposes an eleven-entry prefix.
Its exact stack selectors and proof-word compiler differ from the ten-entry
QN6 interface.

Relative to PA194AX11, the formula constructors use coefficient six rather
than four, and the two curried premises of the sixth axiom head are exchanged.
The extra constructor reader and the additional compatible contextual fold
cancel in the state count. These changes affect recurring axiom
emission and the arithmetic performed by MP; they are not confined to
startup or state names.

Graph novelty alone would not prove semantic novelty, correct initialization,
or a consistency theorem. The contribution is not described as
the invention of AX11, Q, coefficient-six coding, the compiler, or the proof
engines: those are credited to their supplied source packets.

\subsection{Proof status}

The pinned
\Repo{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/README.md}{PA overview}
explicitly distinguishes written PA calibrations from the unfinished
machine-specific iPRA formalization. The distinction remains in this release.
The new contribution is a written composition proof, a completely bound
literal implementation, and fresh executable checks. No new end-to-end
proof-assistant endpoint was compiled.

The logical calibration is a named mathematical dependency.
Its retained arguments concern primitive-recursive translations of finite
derivations with PA-provable correctness. Sections below spell out the
sensitive parts: variable-label recoding, unused numerical residues, the
dominated implication head, zero aliases, the fourth AX operand, arbitrary
carried stacks, and positive physical durations.

\subsection{Duplicates and names}

The same principal C table appears in five packets. The cache-only
HS and one lowercase NS packet coincide, whereas the later sum-guard NS
is different. QN and NQ coincide, the two QNS deliveries coincide,
and CSZ coincides with the later X. QN6 occurs in both newest QN and QN6
archives; QH5 occurs in both AX11 and QH5 archives. Those last duplicate
table bytes were independently compared.

The ledger identifies tables by full actions rather than informal
names. A designated blank start is part of a machine theorem. A graph
census permitting different spanning roots must not silently change the
start used by a calibration proof.

\subsection{Exact novelty of the delivered table}

For the new table the comparison is stronger than basic naming
equivalence. It compares complete canonical action tuples against every
existing 194-state class while allowing arbitrary state renaming, every
graph-spanning root, global L/R reflection, global symbol complementation,
and suppression of final write/move effects on a transition into halt.
Symbol complementation is deliberately allowed as a stronger structural
comparison; it is not an equivalence preserving an all-zero blank tape.

There is no matching tuple among the 82 classes. The comparator performs
exact tuple equality, not merely a hash inequality. The receipt,
comparison code, and frontier tables are included. This proves the
stated finite graph distinction in the recognized pinned corpus; it
does not survey all machines outside that corpus.

\section{What the 193-state files actually establish}
\label{sec:193}

There are 53 distinct 193-state classes in the current recognized corpus,
not merely the two prominent older candidates. Most additional classes
are physical mutations in the newer incoming search packets.

\subsection{Fresh complete blank-start rejection replay}

For all 52 physical classes, the audit freshly ran the literal table from
\emph{every} working state on the blank tape. Thus it checked
\[
                 52\cdot193=10{,}036
\]
initial-state choices. Exactly 106 reach halt and 9,930 reach an outward
ray certificate. There are zero unresolved fuel-limited runs. The longest
prefix to a certificate has 117 transitions.

The ray certificate is an induction, not an extrapolation. At its
endpoint the head scans zero outside the finite support of the tape,
and that state's zero transition returns to itself and moves away from
the support. A written one, if any, remains behind the head.
The same condition holds at every next step. This proves infinite
outward continuation over the complete tape.

The literal tables are retained in
\file{comparison/current_census/frontier_tables/}; each initial control,
finite endpoint, and certification result is in
\file{comparison/rejections193/}. The standalone audit
rechecks the bindings and results. These computations establish the
failure of the supplied initialization schemes for every existing start.
They do not exclude a coordinated table repair or a different encoding.

A PA-provable simple ray is not a substitute for the requested
calibration. If PA is consistent, PA cannot prove its nonhalting
equivalent to $\Con(\PA)$: it already proves the ray's nonhalting and
would then prove its own consistency.

\subsection{The remaining class fails in its logic}

The inherited coefficient-three construction has physical source
contracts, but its unchanged numerical calculus has
\[
 E_3(1,0)=3p(1,0)+3=6=3p(0,1)=I_3(0,1).
\]
The supplied packet contains a primitive-rule refutation with 73,393
lines. This is a proof length, not a literal TM running time.
The logical rejection is attributed to that checked packet; this release
does not relabel its proof replay as a fresh run.

An external typing convention does not constrain the literal machine's
untyped exhaustive emission. A genuine three-tag construction must
alter the actual equality-witness/rewrite interface and prove a new
calibration. The later all-offset obstruction concerns a specified family
of unchanged heads, not all three-tag theories.

\subsection{Why the initialized-runtime saving is still useful}

The runtime edit identifies native 301 with 88 and changes
\[
 (88,0)\mapsto(0,L,51),\qquad (51,0)\mapsto(0,R,122).
\]
At a valid register delimiter the next cell is one, and the old
successor's row $(16,1)$ equals $(122,1)=(0,L,1)$. This transports the
marker lemma over arbitrary registers. The blank bootstrap does not
satisfy the same next-cell premise. Preserving all initialized source
contracts does not settle initialization.

The corpus includes broader seed theorems and extensive startup-repair
experiments. The fresh all-start replay above strengthens the comparison
of the supplied tables while leaving those repair questions open.

\subsection{The exact changes and count}

Frozen inputs and source hashes appear in
\file{candidate/PROVENANCE.json}. The builder starts from the attributed

\texttt{breakTo 1} leaf to the \texttt{node49} push, changes
successful MP from \texttt{node55} to \texttt{node54}, and realizes

In native notation the extra multiplier control is 330. Before folding,

\text{state}&\text{read }0&\text{read }1\\\hline
190&0R330&1R330\\

The no-push MP splice changes $(164,1)$ to $(1,R,114)$.
The AX return replacement is incorporated into the 67-reader grammar.
Seven compatible AX folds and the additional $41\to99$ contextual fold

\[
                         135+67-8=194.

The raw table, eight fold records, incoming contexts, and 32 local tape

\subsection{Source identity and the four-operand interface}

The QN6 parent has 3,776 resolved primitive sites. Its final AX exit at
site 3673 is replaced by the complete 19-site Push, ending in the full MP
field-reader entry at new site 3692. The other 3,775 instructions and
successors agree under relocation, and no outside edge enters the
splice interior. The resulting source has $3776-1+19=3794$ sites.

The coefficient change is confined to 61 constructor bodies. The grammar
has 190 macro tokens and no direct \texttt{node61} leaf. Its free-constructor
emitter audit identifies every generated formula and both guard outcomes,
independently of evaluating enormous numerical axiom codes.

Writing the store as $(t,w,d,h)$, let $D=d+x$ and $H=h+y$.
The command has four operands:
\[
 \mathsf A_{c,x,y,z}(t,w,d,h)
 =\bigl(0,[q_0,q_1,\ldots,q_9,t;w],z,0\bigr).
\]
There are nine axiom entries, the generalized cached formula $U(d,h)$,
and the saved old top. All are theorems on a sound input. The fourth
operand $z$ is consumed by the full MP reader after the push.
The proof compiler selects zero, but soundness covers every $z$.

\subsection{The complete numerical axiom interface}

Let $I,M,U,E$ be the coefficient-six constructors defined in the next
section; let $\neg B=I(B,1)$,
$B\wedge C=\neg I(B,\neg C)$, and $\exists_a B=\neg U(a,\neg B)$.
The six ordinary heads are
\begin{align*}
 h_1(D,H)&=(\exists_D U(D,H))\to H,\\
 h_2(D,A,H)&=U(D,U(A,H))\to U(A,U(D,H)),\\
 h_3(D,A,H)&=M(H,D)\to(E(H,A)\to M(A,D)),\\
 h_4(D,A,H)&=E(H,D)\to(M(A,H)\to M(A,D)),\\
 h_5(D,H)&=\exists_D E(H,D),\\
 h_6^Q(D,A,H)&=U(D,A\to H)\to(U(D,A)\to U(D,H)).
\end{align*}
There is also
\[
 \rho_8(D,A,H,u)=((H\to u)\to D)\to((D\to H)\to(A\to H)),
 \qquad u\le D,
\]
and $\Gamma(B)=B\to U(B,B)$. Define the guarded gate by
\begin{align*}
 V(a,b,c)&=M(a,b)\to U(c,M(a,b)),\\
 P(a,b,c)&=c\to M(c,b),\\
 S(a,b,c)&=U\bigl(b,(M(a,b)\to P(a,b,c))\wedge(M(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
 Q(a,b,c)&=U(c,S(a,b,c)\to\neg P(a,b,c)),\\
 G(a,b,c)&=\neg(V(a,b,c)\to Q(a,b,c)),\qquad c\ne a,b.
\end{align*}
The rules are modus ponens and theorem generalization
$B\mapsto U(i,B)$. There are no open assumptions.
Zero is a theorem: $\rho_8(0,0,0,0)=0$.

The stored order is
\[
 (q_0,\ldots,q_9)=
 (G(H,D,c),\Gamma(c),h_6^Q,h_5,h_4,h_3,h_2,h_1,\rho_8,U(d,h)),
\]
with $(D,c,H)$ parameters, $u=d$ for $\rho_8$, and $q_0=0$ if the
guard fails. The old offset satisfies $d\le d+x=D$.
This specifies the interfaces used by the literal source.

\section{Physical verification and PA transport}
\label{sec:verification}

The construction checker verifies every primitive over arbitrary nine
natural register values, both decrement outcomes, an arbitrary nonempty
finite spare-register tail, and an affine blank header gap.
The independent checker reconstructs four generic list lemmas directly
from the literal table. It uses no arithmetic-box oracle. It resolves
the source graph by two methods, checks dense/native conjugacy,
and replays all 4,901 outcomes.

The construction pass uses 1,571,333 symbolic operations, at most 490 per
outcome. The independent pass uses 1,149,402, at most 397 per outcome.
These count symbolic proof operations: arithmetic loops can represent
arbitrarily long concrete runs. Each independent duration has
nonnegative affine coefficients and a strictly positive constant, with
minimum constant three. An infinite source computation cannot be
collapsed into a finite physical trace.

A source frame is at native control 84 with head position $h$, with a
left marker one, source path $p$, then a zero gap, a header one, and a
unary register file. A field of value $v$ is represented by $0\,1^{v+1}$.
The physical layout and every register/spare block are checked,
not only the nine decoded values.

From the literal blank tape, after exactly 58,320 transitions the final
table reaches native state 84, head $-2$, source site 25, a header at
offset 48 from the head, logical registers
\[
          (N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)=(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0),
\]
and 26 unit spare registers. The complete finite-support tape agrees with
the specified frame, including the zero exterior. The receipt lists all
one-cell coordinates. This finite simulation establishes an initialization
lemma; it is not used to infer infinite nonhalting.

The source contracts, loop invariants, and bootstrap compose by induction
on finite traces. Determinism, no premature halt inside a contract, and
positive durations give both directions between literal and source
halting. The proof compiler and soundness invariant then give both
reductions to and from a PA refutation. The constructions use finite
syntax, primitive-recursive coding, and ordinary PA induction, without
appealing to PA soundness or a uniform arithmetic truth predicate.

Receipts distinguish finite startup, universal symbolic physical
contracts, finite source/logic certificates, and the written calibration
arguments. Python success is not represented as a new Lean kernel theorem.

\section{Fresh searches for a lower state count}

The unsuccessful searches have explicit scopes. They do not establish
that 194 is minimal.

\subsection{Shared increment descriptions}

The arithmetic search enumerated 1,486 short shared increment-DAG
descriptions with at most three additional helpers and formula
coefficients at least four. The best optimistic net count was 194;
the descriptions attaining it used coefficients four, five, or six.

The tempting coefficient-eight realization has 202 raw controls but
causes the inc2 reader to receive both predecessor history bits.
The extra $41\to99$ fold is then unavailable; seven folds give
195 states. This is a concrete loss of sharing rather than a
state saving inferred from a short addition chain.
Code and receipts are in \file{candidate/research/}.

The passes use the primary PA194, supplied PA195EvenHX, and new AX11Q6.
Their context sets come from a blank-start or all-tape finite-window
invariant, or a source-derived envelope augmented with every window of

\section{Reproduction, evidence, and further work}

\subsection{Offline reproduction}

From the extracted release root, Python 3.10 or later with its standard
library suffices for the primary construction:
\begin{Verbatim}[fontsize=\small]
python3 -B candidate/reproduce.py
python3 -B comparison/replay_audit.py
\end{Verbatim}
The first command rebuilds in a fresh temporary directory, compares the
exact table bytes, checks the source splice, all-store command identities,
premise-exchange certificate, complete startup, and both physical passes.
The full components were freshly executed for this release; the wrapper's
quick path was also smoke-tested. The \texttt{--quick} option omits the
long physical passes and is explicitly weaker.
Do not disable assertions with Python \texttt{-O}.

The second command rechecks the fixed-table rejection evidence and
the new table's novelty using bundled artifacts. Repeating the entire

\subsection{What remains open}

The requested strict state-count improvement remains open in this report.
The next targets should alter a proved interface and recompute its whole
physical cost, including startup. The preceding transfer note develops
three directions: a bootstrap-safe runtime fold, an implemented three-tag
logical interface, and a derivable-rule simplification that reduces the
final decoder.

A fourth direction is immediate MP halting. Once its one-free invariant
is proved, the entire delayed Shift sum preparation and test may be
unnecessary. The source must still preserve the top/cache movement and
clear its scratch registers. Removing only the halt leaf has already
failed: ancestor-sensitive exits skip the stack pop. A redesign must
remove the redundant computation, account for shared comparator controls,
and retain an exact blank seed. No saving for that redesign is claimed.

For formal verification, the machine's two finite-code reductions remain
to be internalized in the repository's iPRA interface. The useful
all-model endpoint and proof translations are identified in the transfer
note; they do not themselves discharge the new machine-specific obligations.
