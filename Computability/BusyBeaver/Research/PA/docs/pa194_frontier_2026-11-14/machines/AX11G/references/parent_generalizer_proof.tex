\section{PA194G: generalization controlled by the AX parameter}
\label{sec:pa194g}

The new 194-state construction PA194G changes a command interface. Its AX
command uses its explicit parameter $c$, rather than the incoming numerical
offset $d$, as the binder of the saved generalization. Thus the generated
formula is $U(c,h)$, where $h$ is the incoming cached theorem. This change
is sound for every store; it does not require the offset to represent a
provable formula. It also removes one command from the proof compiler's
generalization operation. The construction uses the existing Q orientation
of universal distribution, successful MP without a dummy push, and the
original sum-based Shift test. Those three ingredients are inherited.

The new mathematical conclusion, by composition with the retained ordinary
PA calibration, is
\[
 \mathrm{PA}\vdash
 \bigl(\neg\operatorname{Halt}_{\mathrm{blank}}(\mathrm{PA194G})
       \leftrightarrow\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})\bigr).
\]
The literal table has dense working states $0,\ldots,193$, start $0$, and
halt label $194$. Its alphabet is $\{0,1\}$, its initial tape is all zero,
and every transition moves one square left or right. The dense SHA-256 is
\begin{quote}\small\nolinkurl{28bd991a84200351f36c98afac369ab5a555eb8793821ad35555f0aa4a0fdeeb}.\end{quote}
The mathematical calibration and executable certificates below are not a
new end-to-end Lean or Rocq theorem, and establish no new iPRA endpoint.

\subsection{Numerical calculus and exact command effects}

Put
\[
 p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b,\qquad [a;w]=p(a,w),
\]
with nested brackets listed top first. The total Cantor projections satisfy
$\operatorname{hd}[a;w]=a$, $\operatorname{tl}[a;w]=w$, and
$[0;0]=\operatorname{hd}(0)=\operatorname{tl}(0)=0$.
The formula constructors are
\[
 I(A,B)=4p(A,B),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,\quad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
Code zero is truth; code one is $M(0,0)$ and is the refutation target.
Write $A\to B=I(A,B)$, $N(B)=I(B,1)$,
$A\wedge B=N(I(A,N(B)))$, and $\exists_a B=N(U(a,N(B)))$.
The axiom families retained by this construction are
\begin{align*}
 h_1(D,H)&=(\exists_D U(D,H))\to H,\\
 h_2(D,A,H)&=U(D,U(A,H))\to U(A,U(D,H)),\\
 h_3(D,A,H)&=M(H,D)\to(E(H,A)\to M(A,D)),\\
 h_4(D,A,H)&=E(H,D)\to(M(A,H)\to M(A,D)),\\
 h_5(D,H)&=\exists_D E(H,D),\\
 h_6^{Q}(D,A,H)&=U(D,A\to H)\to(U(D,A)\to U(D,H)),\\
 \rho(D,A,H,u)&=((H\to u)\to D)\to((D\to H)\to(A\to H)),
       \quad u\le D,\\
 \Gamma(B)&=B\to U(B,B).
\end{align*}
The guarded separation gate uses
\begin{align*}
 P(a,b,c)&=c\to M(c,b),\\
 V(a,b,c)&=M(a,b)\to U(c,M(a,b)),\\
 S(a,b,c)&=U\bigl(b,(M(a,b)\to P(a,b,c))\wedge(M(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
 Q(a,b,c)&=U\bigl(c,S(a,b,c)\to N(P(a,b,c))\bigr),\\
 g(a,b,c)&=\begin{cases}N(V(a,b,c)\to Q(a,b,c)),&c\ne a,b,\\0,&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
\end{align*}
The rules are modus ponens and generalization of theorems.

The Q variant interderives with the primary universal-distribution head by
the uniform propositional exchange template
$(X\to(Y\to Z))\to(Y\to(X\to Z))$, used in both directions.
The supplied finite exchange certificate and the primary PA calibration
therefore apply to the same numerical calculus. That calibration consists
of primitive-recursive translations between its refutations and PA
refutations, whose correctness PA proves. It is an inherited logical
dependency, not an inference from a long nonhalting simulation.

The command store is $(t,w,d,h)$: current theorem, stack, numeric offset,
and cached theorem. For $D=d+x$ and $H=h+y$, the new AX command is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:g-ax}
 \mathsf{AX}_{c,x,y}(t,w,d,h)
    =(g(H,D,c),[q_1,q_2,\ldots,q_9,t;w],0,0),
\end{equation}
where
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
1&\Gamma(c)\\2&h_6^Q(D,c,H)\\3&h_5(D,H)\\4&h_4(D,c,H)\\
5&h_3(D,c,H)\\6&h_2(D,c,H)\\7&h_1(D,H)\\
8&\rho(D,c,H,d)\\9&U(c,h).
\end{array}
\]
All of $g,q_1,\ldots,q_8$ have residue zero modulo four. The ninth entry
has residue two and is at least two. These are arithmetic constructor
facts, with no assumption of consistency. The exact formulas and the
complete saved stack are checked over a free term algebra in
\texttt{check\_commands.py}.

With $v=d+x$, the unchanged no-push MP and sum Shift are
\begin{align*}
 \mathsf{MP}_x(t,w,d,h)&=
 \begin{cases}(v,w,v,0),&I(t,v)=h,\\(0,w,v,0),&I(t,v)\ne h;\end{cases}\\
 \mathsf S(t,w,d,h)&=
 \begin{cases}\mathsf{halt},&t+h=1,\\
 (h,\operatorname{tl}(w),d,\operatorname{hd}(w)),&t+h\ne1.
 \end{cases}
\end{align*}
The offset may be arbitrary and has no theorem annotation.

\subsection{Total stack operations, selectors and the shorter generalizer}

Following AX, nine and ten successive Shifts give respectively
\begin{align*}
 \mathsf{AX}_{c,x,y};\mathsf S^9(t,w,d,h)
    &=(q_8,[t;w],0,U(c,h)),\\
 \mathsf{AX}_{c,x,y};\mathsf S^{10}(t,w,d,h)
    &=(U(c,h),w,0,t).
\end{align*}
Every displayed Shift is nonhalting: the tests involve two zero-residue
entries, or an entry of value at least two. The saved arbitrary old top
is not exposed to a halt test until it is paired with that universal code.
These identities use literal numerical projections and include all
zero-stack aliases.

Write $A_0=\mathsf{AX}_{0,0,0}$ and define
\[
 \mathsf{Push}=A_0;\mathsf S^9;\mathsf{MP}_0,\quad
 \mathsf{Pop}=A_0;\mathsf S^{11},\quad
 \mathsf{Discard}=\mathsf{MP}_0,\quad
 \mathsf{Offset}_i=\mathsf{MP}_i.
\]
Their contracts are
\begin{align*}
 \mathsf{Push}(t,w,d,h)&=(0,[t;w],0,0),\\
 \mathsf{Pop}(t,[a;w],d,h)&=(t,w,0,a),\\
 \mathsf{Discard}(t,w,0,h)&=(0,w,0,0),\\
 \mathsf{Offset}_i(0,w,0,0)&=(0,w,i,0).
\end{align*}
Push's last comparison is between an implication code and a universal
code, so fails. Pop's eleventh test is $U(0,h)+t\ge2$; therefore Pop is
\emph{total}, including when $t=1$. Discard's two branches coincide for
$d=x=0$, even if the old cache is nonzero. Offset succeeds harmlessly at
$i=0$ and fails harmlessly at $i>0$. The contracts also hold at $w=0$.

From an arbitrary $(t,w,0,0)$, the selector for $q_j$, $1\le j\le7$, is
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{AX}_{c,D,H};\mathsf S^{j+1};
                  \mathsf{Pop}^{9-j}.
\]
It returns $(q_j,w,0,0)$. After the initial Shifts, the last $9-j$ entries
are the unselected generated tail and saved zero; the total Pops consume
them and leave zero in the cache. For a dominated instance with $u\le D$,
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Offset}_u;
 \mathsf{AX}_{c,D-u,H};\mathsf S^9;\mathsf{Pop}
\]
returns $(\rho(D,c,H,u),w,0,0)$. The gate selector is
$\mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{AX}_{c,D,H};\mathsf{Pop}^{10}$.
Truth uses Discard.

The new generalization word is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:g-gen}
 \mathsf{Gen}_i=\mathsf{Push};\mathsf S;
                       \mathsf{AX}_{i,0,0};\mathsf S^{10}.
\end{equation}
From $(a,w,0,0)$, Push gives $(0,[a;w],0,0)$ and the next Shift gives
$(0,w,0,a)$. AX then stores $U(i,a)$ as its ninth generated entry; ten
Shifts return $(U(i,a),w,0,0)$. Every step is nonhalting, including $a=1$.
The old $\mathsf{Offset}_i$ command is absent. The new binder is the AX
operand itself; this is the exact reason the shorter word works.
For example, with $d=h=0$ and $c=1$, the prior saved head is
$U(0,0)=2$, while this command saves $U(1,0)=10$. Thus the interface
change is observable even on a store whose cached theorem is truth.

\subsection{A compiler that works from every store}

For a derivation ending in MP with premises $a$ and $I(a,b)$, use
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Push};C(I(a,b));\mathsf{Push};
 C(a);\mathsf{Push};\mathsf S;\mathsf S;\mathsf{MP}_b;\mathsf{Pop}.
\]
The initial pair reserves a zero. If both recursive calls return, the
store before the two Shifts is
$(0,[a,I(a,b),0;w],0,0)$. The first Shift gives
$(0,[I(a,b),0;w],0,a)$. The second either halts because $a=1$, or gives
$(a,[0;w],0,I(a,b))$. MP succeeds, returning $(b,[0;w],b,0)$, and the
total Pop returns $(b,w,0,0)$. In particular it can return the false code
one, which the final appended Shift recognizes.

Induction on a coded derivation now proves that, from every $(t,w,0,0)$,
$C(e)$ either halts or returns $(\operatorname{concl}(e),w,0,0)$.
The axiom cases use the selectors; the generalization case uses
\eqref{eq:g-gen}; the MP case is the preceding calculation. Every
identity covers the zero-pair aliases. Starting from arbitrary $d,h$,
$A_0$ first clears both. Hence
\[
                         A_0;C(e);\mathsf S
\]
halts from every incoming store when $e$ derives code one.

The unchanged scheduler's continuation encoding is
\[
 E(\epsilon)=0,\qquad
 E(c::w)=1+\operatorname{encodeCommand}(c,E(w)),
\]
where the latter clause applies to nonempty words and
\[
 \operatorname{encodeCommand}(\mathsf{AX}_{a,x,y},z)
       =p(a+2,p(y,p(x,z))).
\]
For $z=E(A_0;C(e);\mathsf S)$, prefixing $\mathsf{AX}_{k,0,0}$ produces
the cofinal fresh-candidate family $p(k+2,p(0,p(0,z)))$. A fresh
candidate code omits the leading one used by positive continuations. Every prefix
terminates and leaves some store to which the all-store compiler applies.
No reset assumption is needed between failed trials. The source macros
terminate, continuations decrease, and the finite witness construction is
primitive recursive with correctness provable in PA.

For soundness, annotate $t$, $h$, and each live canonical stack entry by
a proof of its translated formula. Zero supplies the truth proof. AX adds
axioms, the old top, and the valid generalization $U(c,h)$ of the incoming
cached theorem. Successful MP composes two proofs; failure writes truth.
Shift only transfers annotations. If $t+h=1$, one of these two annotated
codes is one. The inherited translation supplies a PA refutation.
Combining this annotation with the compiler gives both PA-provable finite
syntactic reductions, and hence the stated consistency equivalence once
the literal implementation is bound to the source.

\subsection{Literal binding and verification boundary}

Relative to the primary 190-token word, token 1 changes
\texttt{copyD} to \texttt{copyA}; six further token changes at positions
103, 105, 106, 107, 108, and 109 implement the existing Q premise exchange.
The exact new word is checked over the free constructor algebra. Its
chosen association is compared with the accepted primary association
populated with the same word: all resolved source instructions and
successors agree. Successful MP deletes the 19-site dummy-zero push,
old interval $[3634,3653)$, with every retained instruction and successor
checked under its relocation and no outside entry into the removed
interior. There are 3,654 primitive source sites.

The compiler constructs 134 fixed controls and 67 AX readers. Seven
contextual folds are checked simultaneously on all 28 two-cell cases;
the supplied fold set avoids a conflict present in the first maximum
matching returned by the allocation heuristic. The final count is
\[
                       134+67-7=194.
\]
All 4,755 arbitrary-register primitive outcomes pass the construction
checker, with 1,477,258 symbolic steps and maximum 470 per outcome.
The entire finite-support blank-start tape reaches source site 25,
native dispatcher 84, head $-2$, logical registers $(2,0,\ldots,0)$,
and 26 spare blocks of value one after exactly 58,154 transitions.

A separate literal-tape checker recomputes its four register-list
induction lemmas from the actual transition rows, uses two independently
implemented source resolvers, and checks the same unbounded register
contracts. Its actual replay receipt is delivered alongside the
construction receipt: 1,078,742 symbolic steps, maximum 379 per outcome,
and minimum positive duration constant three. The domain includes arbitrary natural register
values, both decrement branches, arbitrary nonempty finite lists of
natural spare values, and arbitrary extra nonnegative header gap.
These certificates are not finite-value sampling tests.

Each source step has a positive finite physical simulation. PA verifies
the register-list lemmas by induction and the fixed site contracts by
finite computation, giving a primitive-recursive source-to-tape trace
translation. Conversely, for a physical first halt at time $T$, perform
$T+1$ totalized source steps. If none halted, their positive simulations,
preceded by startup, would produce a deterministic physical trace beyond
its first halt, a contradiction. This bounded reflection argument gives
the other physical reduction. Composition with the two syntactic
reductions above proves the ordinary PA statement for the exact table.
