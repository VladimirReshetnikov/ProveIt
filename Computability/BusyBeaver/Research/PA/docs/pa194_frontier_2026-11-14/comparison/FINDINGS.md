## Mechanism families and substantive differences

Nearly all frontier constructions share a numerical proof calculus, an
enumerating command scheduler, a binary source-program tree, and a unary
register engine. A shorter source program need not produce fewer physical
states. The recurring allocation is

    fixed physical controls + AX readers - compatible contextual folds.

The primary table has 134 + 67 - 7 = 194 controls. The alternate first PA194
has 134 + 68 - 8 = 194. Source rewrites alter reader count and available
history folds together, so their apparent state savings cannot simply be
added.

* **C/CS** change successful MP to cache its consequent and clear the visible
  top/offset; their Shift guard tests the cache. CS reverses a destructive
  comparator and removes restoration work.
* **N** deletes the dummy-zero push after successful MP while retaining the
  original sum guard. Completeness needs a reserved-zero store compiler.
* **H/HS** combine no-push MP with cached-only Shift, with HS using symmetric
  comparison. A final visible contradiction must be moved into the cache;
  a plain final Shift is insufficient in the changed protocol.
* **SUMS and sum-guard NS** use symmetric comparison while retaining the
  original sum guard. They are different from the cache-only NS/HS naming.
* **Q variants** exchange the curried premises in universal distribution.
  The change recurs in axiom emission and needs the appropriate restricted-
  implication proof translation; it is not a control renumbering.
* **D/DHS** change the diagonal-vacuity head from Gamma(A) to Gamma(D), affecting
  offsets, selection and reader allocation.
* **Coefficient-five/six variants** change formula constructors and MP
  matching. Logical recoding must account for unused residues and the
  Cantor-pair zero alias.
* **J/F/A/CSZ/X/ZR/HA/HAZ** replace selected source jumps with decrements of
  registers invariantly zero, sometimes combined with register permutations.
  Their whole-startup proof is separate from source-frame correctness.
* **R/M4R and source rotations** reassociate the binary source tree while
  preserving resolved instructions. Many stored rotations are research
  auxiliaries rather than separately calibrated proposals.
* **AX11** pushes the final guarded head and falls through the complete MP
  parameter reader. Its command produces eleven entries and consumes four
  operands. A proof compiler may choose the fourth operand zero; soundness
  must cover every operand.
* **B** introduces a dedicated history selector in place of an existing
  reader-sharing arrangement. The packet supplies an exact all-tape transfer
  in which each old step becomes one through five new steps.
* **G** changes the saved generalization from U(d,h) to U(c,h), taking the
  binder from an explicit AX operand, and changes the generalization word.
* **AD, TDY, P5 and HX** alter physical register placement while keeping the
  logical source. Their nonzero tape layouts and exact blank startups differ.
* **4L** changes a four-increment physical decoder association and compensates
  one added helper with one added contextual fold; its full abstract source
  remains primary PA194's.

## Why the stored 193-state tables do not solve the task

There are 53 complete spanning 193-state graph classes; no additional one
was found in the newest five incoming archives. One is a coefficient-three
logical shortcut. Its equality and implication codes collide; the supplied
calculus admits a finite refutation. The long recorded logical certificate
length counts proof lines, not Turing-machine transitions.

identifying native 301 with 88 and changing (88,0) and (51,0). The reduced

unchanged blank bootstrap reaches an outward ray. Existing all-start

Those are genuine rejection certificates for the stated tables, not a
general lower bound. Register permutation alone also cannot fix the fixed
runtime core: the incoming nine-trace argument covers all 9! allocations
because only one permutation-sensitive selector is executed before the ray.

## Arithmetic proof boundary

The current PA README explicitly says neither machine-specific iPRA
direction has been established for the primary table. The generic endpoint
from an all-model `Calibrated T h` premise is present, but the repository
states no table satisfies that premise. The recent incoming packets now
acknowledge that their ordinary relative weak-arithmetic arguments do not
supply the missing absolute calibration.

Therefore the initial task's description of the historical designs as
already iPRA-calibrated is stronger than the checked source status. A new
machine simulation can transfer an eventual calibration; it cannot create
the absent premise. The census does not infer arithmetic strength from
standard finite-run Lean theorems, symbolic Python contracts, or a completed
modeled-scheduler theorem. All absolute iPRA statuses in the frontier ledger
remain explicitly open.
