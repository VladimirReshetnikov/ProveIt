\section{Constructive transport in intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic}
\label{sec:ipra-transport}

The machine reduction and the calibration to PA are separate assertions.
The first can be established in intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic
without a truth predicate, a soundness principle, or Markov's principle.
The following formulation makes the precise burden of a reduction certificate
explicit.

\subsection{Coded traces and the logical transport lemma}

Fix primitive-recursive encodings of finite words, finite-support binary
tapes, configurations, and finite traces. A convenient tape encoding uses
two natural numbers, the low-order bit of the right number being under the
head and the low-order bit of the left number being immediately to its left.
For each fixed finite transition table $M$, let $\mathrm{Tr}_M(u)$ be the
primitive-recursive predicate saying that $u$ is a valid blank-start trace
ending in the designated halt state. We use a Boolean characteristic
function for this predicate, so it is a quantifier-free formula in the
language of iPRA. Write
\[
 H_M\equiv\exists u\,\mathrm{Tr}_M(u),\qquad
 \mathrm{NH}_M\equiv\neg H_M.
\]
All trace coding conventions are fixed before the comparison; a change of
coding requires the corresponding elementary translation lemma.

\begin{theorem}[Constructive trace transport]
Suppose $F,G$ are specified primitive-recursive functions and that iPRA proves
\begin{align*}
 \forall u\,(\mathrm{Tr}_M(u)&\longrightarrow\mathrm{Tr}_N(F(u))),\\
 \forall v\,(\mathrm{Tr}_N(v)&\longrightarrow\mathrm{Tr}_M(G(v))).
\end{align*}
Then iPRA proves both $H_M\leftrightarrow H_N$ and
$\mathrm{NH}_M\leftrightarrow\mathrm{NH}_N$. Consequently, for any sentence
$C$, an iPRA proof of $C\leftrightarrow\mathrm{NH}_M$ yields an iPRA proof
of $C\leftrightarrow\mathrm{NH}_N$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Existential elimination on a trace witness followed by existential
introduction using $F$ proves $H_M\to H_N$; $G$ gives the reverse implication.
If $\neg H_M$ and $H_N$, the second implication gives a contradiction,
and hence $\neg H_M\to\neg H_N$. Interchanging the machines proves the
other implication. These are intuitionistic natural-deduction arguments.
No double-negation elimination for an existential sentence occurs.
\end{proof}

This theorem is deliberately relative to its two verification statements.
The fact that an external algorithm is primitive recursive does not, by
itself, prove either statement in iPRA. One must verify its computation by
quantifier-free induction or another already justified iPRA argument.

\subsection{A sufficient, checkable positive-simulation interface}

Here is a useful sufficient condition for producing $F$ and $G$. Let $R$ be
a deterministic source machine with primitive-recursive total step function
$s$, decidable halt predicate $h_R$, and initial state $r_0$. Totalization
holds a halting state fixed. Let $M$ be a literal target machine with the
same absorbing convention for its halt state. Specify the following data:

\begin{enumerate}
\item A primitive-recursive frame encoding $E(r)$ and a decidable invariant
      $I(r)$, with $I(r_0)$.
\item A fixed finite blank-start trace to $E(r_0)$, containing no halt unless
      the initial source state itself is halting.
\item A primitive-recursive segment constructor $S(r)$, including its
      exact positive length $\ell(r)\ge1$, such that, if $I(r)$ and
      $\neg h_R(r)$, it is a literal target trace from $E(r)$ to $E(s(r))$.
      All its proper-prefix configurations are nonhalting; its endpoint is
      halting exactly when $s(r)$ is halting. The invariant $I(s(r))$ holds.
\item At a source halt, $E(r)$ is a target halt. More generally, a separately
      specified terminating halt segment may be used, provided its trace
      and its finite bound are part of the interface.
\end{enumerate}

Every assertion in this list must be proved in iPRA for the chosen numeric
codes. A bounded check of an explicitly constructed finite segment is again
a primitive-recursive predicate. Thus the statement does not require a
quantifier over arbitrary infinite tapes: untouched exterior tape is handled
by a finite locality theorem, or by an arbitrary coded finite-support tape
parameter. Register lists, header gaps and loop lengths remain variables;
testing finitely many values for those variables would not suffice.

\begin{proposition}[Positive simulation gives constructive equivalence]
The preceding verified interface yields primitive-recursive trace
translations in both directions, with their correctness provable in iPRA.
Therefore iPRA proves $H_R\leftrightarrow H_M$ and
$\mathrm{NH}_R\leftrightarrow\mathrm{NH}_M$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For the forward translation, read a source trace, prepend the fixed startup,
and concatenate the segments $S(r_i)$ until its first source halt. An invalid
input receives a fixed dummy output. This is a primitive recursion on the
input trace. The verification invariant records the current source state,
the target endpoint, the accumulated trace and its length. With the
Boolean trace checker, this invariant is quantifier-free. The local segment
theorems and quantifier-free induction prove correctness.

For the reverse translation, let a valid target trace end in a halt after
$T$ steps. Compute the first $T+1$ source steps, stopping at a halt if one
is encountered. This bounded computation is primitive recursive; inspect
its finite list by a primitive-recursive search. If there is a source halt,
output the corresponding prefix trace. If there is no such halt, concatenate
the $T+1$ certified nonhalting source segments and startup. Their total length
is greater than $T$, since every segment contributes at least one literal
target step. By determinism, its prefix through time $T$ agrees with the
given target trace. But the constructed trace has no halt at time $T$ and
subsequently reaches a nonhalt macro boundary, contradicting the absorbing
halt property. Hence the bounded search succeeds.

The case distinction is on a decidable bounded search, and its failed case
has just been refuted. Intuitionistic logic therefore yields the particular
witness returned by the search, not merely its double negation. The
comparison of trace prefixes, preservation of the invariant and positivity
of the accumulated length use quantifier-free induction on the coded finite
computations. Thus the correctness of the reverse translator is an iPRA
theorem as well.
\end{proof}

When a source halt is implemented by a separate halt segment, choose the
bounded search to inspect the first $T+1$ source \emph{boundaries} and append
that segment in the forward translator. Positivity for ordinary boundaries
still proves the same reverse argument. The exact endpoint convention must
be fixed consistently; it cannot be replaced by a contract that permits an
unexplained early target halt.

\subsection{How finite local fold certificates fit the interface}

For a state fold, $E$ normally projects the source control and preserves
the tape and head. An exceptional control also has a finite history
condition, such as the value of the cell just crossed. Every incoming
source transition must establish the required condition. The finite
certificate enumerates every allowed local pattern, simulates it in the
\emph{final combined table}, and checks the exact projected endpoint, whole
local tape, head displacement, positive duration, and absence of premature
halt. A certificate for each fold separately need not validate their
simultaneous use: a helper can itself be changed by another fold.

If the local window and maximum simulation length are fixed constants, the
certificate expands to a finite collection of transition-row equalities.
The resulting local simulation theorem is provable in iPRA for arbitrary
untouched exterior tape. Quantifier-free induction then propagates the
history invariant and constructs complete trace translations as above.
This is a considerably smaller obligation than recalibrating the numerical
proof calculus to PA.

\subsection{What may and may not be concluded}

A certified replacement with the preceding interface preserves the desired
consistency equivalence at the same base-theory strength. Its exact conclusion
is
\[
 \mathrm{iPRA}\vdash\mathrm{NH}_{M_{\mathrm{new}}}
                         \leftrightarrow\mathrm{NH}_{M_{\mathrm{old}}}.
\]
If the baseline calibration is supplied, ordinary intuitionistic composition
gives
\[
 \mathrm{iPRA}\vdash\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{PA})
                         \leftrightarrow\mathrm{NH}_{M_{\mathrm{new}}}.
\]
The first theorem does not supply the premise of the second. In particular,
an external standard-run equivalence, a PA proof of the consistency
biconditional, and an iPRA proof of the biconditional are distinct claims.
Every final statement must identify which of these has actually been proved.
