\begin{abstract}
No justified blank-start construction below 194 working states was obtained.
The positive deliverables are two new 194-state binary, single-tape machines,
\texttt{PA194AX11H\_SF} and \texttt{PA194LC\_AX11H\_SF}. Each is specified by
388 literal transition rows, starts in state zero on the all-zero two-way
tape, and has a separate terminal label 194. They are distinct from each
other and from every spanning 194-state graph in the pinned comparison
corpus, even under the additional structural identifications described below.

For each new machine $N$ and its exact parent $M$, the new argument proves
in intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic the explicit implications
$H_M(t)\to H_N(3t)$ and $H_N(t)\to H_M(3t)$, where $H_M(t)$ means halted
by time $t$. The proof is an elementary finite-row simulation with
primitive-recursive clocks and quantifier-free induction. Its validity is
independent of any PA calibration. The absolute consistency corollaries
import the precisely identified revised AX11H \emph{written} calibration;
no new end-to-end Lean or Rocq theorem is claimed.

The comparison covers 87 filed packets and six current incoming deliveries.
Their union contains 139 spanning 194-state table classes, plus one
disconnected 194-control diagnostic. All 140 literal representatives,
per-table annotations, the complete delivery ledger, reproducible finite
checks, and the exact inherited calibration archive are supplied.
\end{abstract}

The start is dense control 0, the working controls are $0,\ldots,193$, and
the terminal control is 194. The head starts at coordinate zero. The alphabet
is $\{0,1\}$, zero is blank, and every transition moves left or right once.
No initial word, extra tape, stationary move, or uncounted working control
is used. Native tables retain start 74 and halt 254. In native numbering,
194 is a working control; it must not be mistaken for the halt label.

For arithmetic reasoning, extend execution at the terminal control by an
absorbing identity step. This is a definition of the run function, not an
additional transition row or state. Write
\[
 H_M(t)\;\equiv\;\text{the control of the absorbing run of $M$ at time $t$ is terminal},
 \qquad H_M\;\equiv\;\exists t\,H_M(t).
\]
Thus $\neg H_M$ is the usual blank-tape nonhalting sentence. All relative
claims below use this exact convention, including the bounded-time claims.

\subsection{What is established here and what is inherited}

There are three distinct levels of evidence.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The two literal tables, all structural premises of the local simulation,
      startup synchronization, and corpus-relative graph novelty have fresh
      executable checks. These checks are in the delivered archive.
\item The accompanying mathematical argument proves the new relative iPRA
      equivalences, with explicit $3t$ witnesses in both directions. It does
      not rely on testing a long blank prefix to establish nonhalting.
\item The absolute statement $\ipra\vdash\neg H_N\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)$
      is obtained by composing that relative theorem with the revised
      AX11H mathematical calibration. Its exact original archive is preserved,
      and the dependency is reviewed and identified in Section~\ref{sec:calibration}.
\end{enumerate}
The top-level repository README still records an incomplete formal
machine-specific endpoint. The newer incoming written calibration is a
separate mathematical artifact. Neither fact is silently substituted for
the other. In particular, the new finite checker is not described as a
Lean proof of the full PA consistency theorem.

The six new rows change the machines' working decoder behavior. All six
are actually used during blank initialization. The construction is neither
a relabeling nor a change confined to dead rows. Its ingredients have
precedents in the supplied reports; the contribution is their compatible
composition on these exact parents, the resulting new graphs, and the
short direct transfer proof.

\subsection{The apparent 193-state frontier}

The combined catalog contains 54 classes in the 193-state bin. This does
not give 54 blank-start consistency machines. The packets distinguish
clean-frame runtime cores, rejected changes, and search outputs from their
positive blank-input constructions. A runtime with 193 controls still
needs a valid blank startup. Starting at every existing control does not
add a missing initializer for free.

The newest LC packet supplies a particularly useful rejection: a candidate
passes local fold checks, has 193 controls, and has a startup trace, but its
changed source enters a six-instruction cycle that repeatedly increments
register 1 four times and register 6 once without returning to the
decrement test. Its source no longer implements the intended constructor.\cite{lc}
This is why state counting and successful finite bootstrap checks are
not enough to promote a candidate.

\begin{quote}\small
\path{93ee082ef035b2d32e71d6af5239b25b4bea1773f03bea4d2c39810a7fe15075}.
\end{quote}
The dependency is the revised packet, not an earlier delivery with the
same basename. Its final paper states the iPRA equivalence for the literal
AX11H machine and the specified Foundation consistency predicate.\cite{ax11h}

Here $\Con(\PA)$ denotes the revised packet's predicate
\texttt{nativePAConsistency}. It negates the mapped refutation predicate
\texttt{nativePARefutation} for the Foundation pin
\path{32e1a0956a8622fad067328ca1959729a7634428}. The Foundation falsity code
13 and the numerical-calculus contradiction code 1 are connected by proof
translations; they are not identified as the same natural number.

The focused review in \file{research/theory/ax11h_dependency_audit.txt}
examines the physical weak-base proof, the reverse direction, the exact
Foundation adapter, and the finite-demand forward and term constructions.
The new manuscript explicitly addresses the obstacles noted by the older
formal-development status: quantifier-free symbolic-rule correctness,
model-length computation, bounded proof suppliers, and the change in a
Foundation finite axiom. The finite-demand constructions avoid induction
over an unbounded collection of environments. The final assembly uses
the specified all-model $I\Sigma_1$ directions and the existing
conservation/negative-translation endpoints.

No concrete gap was found in the reviewed dependency chain. This is a
focused mathematical review, not an independent reconstruction of every
large proof recipe or a new kernel check. The absolute conclusion below
uses that identified theorem as an imported mathematical result.

\begin{theorem}[Principal absolute corollary]
Using the revised AX11H written calibration,
\[
 \boxed{\ipra\vdash\neg H_{\Mone}\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA).}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The direct relative theorem proves $H_{\Mone}\leftrightarrow H_{\MH}$
in iPRA, and intuitionistic logic therefore proves equivalence of their
negations. Compose this with the cited AX11H calibration. There is no
assumption that PA is consistent, no reflection principle, and no
elimination of double negation for a positive existential statement.
\end{proof}

The second machine uses the incoming LC\_AX11H parent $\MLH$. Its preserved
standalone relative proof supplies, in both directions, the primitive-recursive
bound
\[
 \Theta(t)=13t^2+707t+58\,616.
\]
Consequently the direct witnesses relative to $\MH$ may be written as
\[
 H_{\MH}(t)\to H_{\Mtwo}(3\Theta(t)),\qquad
 H_{\Mtwo}(t)\to H_{\MH}(\Theta(3t)).
\]
For its immediate LC parent, the sharper $3t$ bound in each direction
still holds. The LC-to-AX11H proof and its standalone replay inputs are
included under \file{references/lc_ax11h/}.

\begin{theorem}[Companion absolute corollary]
Using the same revised calibration and the supplied LC relative theorem,
\[
 \boxed{\ipra\vdash\neg H_{\Mtwo}\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA).}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose the two relative nonhalting equivalences and the AX11H theorem.
All witness transformations displayed above are primitive recursive.
\end{proof}

The principal SF machine has the shorter proof dependency chain. The
companion contributes a second, mutually distinct literal graph with a
different constructor-loop implementation. Both are supplied, with their
dependencies separated so that the relative results remain useful even
if the inherited absolute calibration is later revised.

\section{Effort to reduce the state count}\label{sec:search}

The unsuccessful searches are retained as reproducible research evidence,
with explicit scope. None establishes a lower bound of 194. The absence
of a hit in a finite abstraction can mean that the abstraction admits
too many contexts, that the path budget is too small, or that the needed
transformation lies outside the search language.

\subsection{Physical control reductions}

For radius $K$, a search state is the control and the $2K+1$ tape bits
at offsets $-K$ through $K$ around the head. The least closed set starts
from the blank window at control 74. Each literal transition shifts the
window, and both possible values are allowed for the newly exposed
boundary bit. Induction shows that this finite set contains every window
on the actual blank computation. It can contain spurious windows, so it
is a conservative optimization screen.

The search corpus contains 530 distinct complete literal 194-control
tables: 118 native tables from the current incoming material and
syntactically eligible LC/SEL/F combinations. The combinations are
\emph{search inputs}; no blanket correctness or novelty claim is made
for them. The positive machines in this report have their separate
complete proof checks.

The radius-nine primary invariant contains 10,590,743 abstract
configurations. The recursive search is stronger than the usual
nonrecursive history fold: either replacement row can return to the
merged control. A sound first-reentry filter eliminates impossible first
actions; every surviving pair of actions is then tested against every
relevant window. There are 776 possible actions per row: four write/move
choices times 193 retained working targets plus halt. The removed target
is identified with the retained control, so it contributes no extra action.
This represents 18,721 control pairs and 11,273,336,896 two-row definitions
before filtering. The fully tested survivors numbered 1,227,299 for the
primary, 1,167,897 for the principal SF machine, and 1,150,094 for its LC
companion. None passed the bounded local specification.

All C++ searchers, literal inputs, original receipts, and a portable
launcher are in \file{research/physical/}. The default portable replay
rechecked the primary large-window search and the three recursive searches.
The longer \texttt{--all} mode repeats the corpus and neutral-rewrite
searches. These computations neither presume nor decide nonhalting of
the consistency machines.

\subsection{Reassociation outside the axiom emitter}

Most previous grammar searches hold the fixed controller constant and
reassociate the axiom reader. This investigation also rearranged closed
source routines outside that reader. A fragment is closed when its
relative break and continue obligations do not escape it. Reassociating
three consecutive closed fragments preserves their sequential meaning;
the complete resolved instruction list was also compared for each
single rewrite used here.

Keeping the named native macro entries fixed avoids rebuilding every
caller when a shared routine is rearranged. New anonymous readers are
allocated only for newly needed pairs; unreachable controls are removed;
the resulting raw table is priced by a maximum matching of the specified
one-bit-history fold opportunities. The 25 single rewrites had optimistic
prices 194 (12 cases), 195 (11), or 196 (2). The twelve equal-price cases
passed their finite fold checks and underwent further physical merge
searches, but were not promoted without full source-frame verification.

Joint searches over two, three, and four selected rewrites produced
13,125 distinct raw tables: 292 at depth two, 2,119 additional at depth
three, and 10,714 additional at depth four. Their optimistic prices ranged
from 194 to 199; none reached 193. The cumulative price histogram was
847, 2,535, 3,759, 3,525, 1,958, and 501 at those six respective counts.
This is a lowerer-specific grammar and fold screen, not a proof that all
source-equivalent presentations have at least 194 states. The joint
tables were not all promoted to physically certified source simulations.

\subsection{An alternative arithmetic encoding}

A separate attempt reversed the shared Cantor arithmetic core. Writing
$p(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b$, the modified core computes $p(b,a)$.
Shortening Push from two preparatory transfers to one can preserve the
old stack pairing while changing formula constructors to, for example,
$I'(a,b)=4p(b,a)$. This reduced the primitive source from 3,673 to 3,457
sites, with 432 finite source arithmetic cases checked.

The literal cost was unfavorable: 206 raw controls and eight proposed
folds, for an optimistic price of 198. Old arithmetic controls are also
opcode helpers, so they cannot simply be discarded after shortening the
source. The proposed folds were not certified as a complete simultaneous
physical simulation, and the design was stopped before full verification.
It is explicitly an \emph{uncertified experiment}, not a new 198-state
consistency theorem. Its complete portable code and inputs are supplied
under \file{research/theory/}.

\section{Use of the related research programs}

The three suggested neighboring programs were inspected at the same
repository pin.\cite{zfc,related} Frozen current READMEs are included under
\file{research/related_projects/}, alongside this assessment.

The ZFC machine work supplies the most direct operational ideas:
contextual control sharing, register-routing changes, source-loop
reassociation, complete blank initialization, and positive-duration
simulation. These methods transfer to PA only after checking their
literal row and frame interfaces. The rejected 12/38 selector transplant
is a concrete example of why matching names or disjoint edit sets is
insufficient. The retained 127/124 selector is checked on the exact
intermediate table.

DiophantineConsistency is particularly relevant to proof discipline:
an exact arithmetized witness predicate, explicit finite-code interfaces,
and a native iPRA endpoint. Those ideas support the use here of explicit
primitive-recursive configuration codes and clocks. A smaller Diophantine
variable count or circuit-operation count is not a binary TM state
count; a new interpreter would have to pay for decoding, arithmetic,
control, and initialization. No unbuilt Diophantine interpreter is charged
as a state saving in this report.

ConciseIPRA minimizes complete proof-checker implementations for
presentations of iPRA. It provides a useful standard for explicit rules,
derived transformations, and whole-checker accounting. Replacing the
machine's proof search by inconsistency search for iPRA itself would
change the target from $\Con(\PA)$; it is not justified by a compact
checker. A correct alternative would need a calibrated PA proof
predicate and a fully counted binary implementation. Here iPRA is the
metatheory of the relative simulation, with all nonlogical computation
made primitive recursive.

of the 139 prior spanning classes, with global tape reflection, symbol
exchange, and ignored terminal-edge write/move decorations also allowed.

There were 215,728 exact tuple comparisons, no historical match, and no

simulation. Their 57,918-step seed checkpoints were checked by exact
whole-tape equality, including every synchronization boundary. The
all-time theorem comes from the local identities and induction, not
from extrapolating that finite prefix. The construction saves four
startup transitions relative to its immediate parent, but no claim of
uniform runtime improvement is made: selectors add steps in some
recurring cases and accelerations remove them in others.

\section{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Can a nonlocal tape representation remove one controller?}
      The current bounds are local to a finite window. A useful next
      transformation may move a persistent marker or change the relation
      between program address and register file, allowing an entire
      protocol phase to disappear. It would require a global frame
      correspondence and a new complete startup.
\item \textbf{Can the reversed Cantor core be lowered without retaining
      obsolete opcode helpers?} The source reduction is real, but shared
      physical controls currently offset it. Jointly redesigning those
      helper contexts could make the new arithmetic orientation competitive.
\item \textbf{Can closed-routine reassociation be searched together with
      context-dependent macro entry choices?} The present lowering fixes
      the named entry controls. Allowing multiple entry representations
      and a grammar cost that accounts for physical history may expose
      a saving missed by a fixed-entry matching score.
\item \textbf{Can the relative SF theorem become a small kernel-checked
      reusable component?} Its complete finite interface is only six
      changed rows, the predecessor invariants, and two bounded clocks.
      Formalizing it separately would avoid waiting for the much larger
      PA proof compiler and would provide a reusable transfer theorem.
\item \textbf{Can the revised absolute AX11H calibration be compiled
      end to end?} The exact retained dependency identifies the proof
      recipes and Foundation version. A finished kernel theorem for
      that parent would immediately transfer to the principal machine,
      and through the LC theorem to the companion.
\end{enumerate}
