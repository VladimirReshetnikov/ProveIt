## New work in this packet

The new literal constructions are **PA194AX11H** and **PA194QAX11H**. Each combines credited existing ideas. Their exact transition tables, clean builder, source maps, fresh certificates, source-contract replays, startup checks, and corpus-relative novelty receipts are new outputs for this composition. Their abstract command proof includes the H-compatible Pop law and a bounded quantifier-free proof of the all-store compiler invariant. The explicit positive-segment transport theorem separates local arithmetic obligations from global halting reflection.

The new written physical/source audit additionally proves the literal-machine to exact primitive-register-program equivalence in iPRA. It supplies every symbolic-rule schema, a primitive recursive tape/list interpretation, frame closure, and both global witness transformations. The separate new source/command proof then binds every primitive instruction to an explicit arithmetic template and composes exact command clocks. The new finite-demand term/formula/induction/proof compiler and exact current Foundation adapter supply the other model-uniform witness direction. Composing both with the pinned logical soundness and one-way transfer theorems establishes `Con(PA) ↔ ¬Halt(M)` in iPRA for both literal tables as a written mathematical theorem.

The additional grammar and physical reduction experiments were freshly run with captured inputs and limits. No sub-194 winner was obtained. The small finite logical matrices refute weakening for two restricted propositional offset interfaces only; their scope does not include the full numerical calculus. The new census and comparison ledgers extend the inspected incoming corpus to the pinned snapshot.

The report and receipts distinguish executable finite checks, the completed written mathematical iPRA equivalence, and inherited formal results. There is no new end-to-end Lean/Rocq kernel build in this release. Review receipts record mathematical review, not proof-assistant certificates; the full logical compiler is specified mathematically rather than delivered as executable proof-generating code.
