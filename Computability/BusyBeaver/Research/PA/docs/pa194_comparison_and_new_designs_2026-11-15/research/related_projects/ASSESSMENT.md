register routing, source loop association, exact blank initialization and
positive-duration simulation. Such changes must be rechecked against
their actual PA tape contexts. This delivery rejects the incompatible

DiophantineConsistency supplies a useful weak-arithmetic proof discipline:
exact witness predicates and explicitly internalized finite computations.
Its variable, degree and arithmetic-operation metrics do not automatically
yield a small binary TM. No unbuilt Diophantine interpreter is counted as
a state saving here.

ConciseIPRA emphasizes complete-checker accounting and precise derived
rules. Searching its own proof system for contradiction would target
Con(iPRA), not the requested Con(PA). A replacement proof-search language
would need an exact PA calibration and a complete binary implementation.
Here iPRA supplies the metatheory for the direct relative simulation.
