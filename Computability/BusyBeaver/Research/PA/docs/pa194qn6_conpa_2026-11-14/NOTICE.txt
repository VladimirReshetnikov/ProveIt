the existing Q axiom emitter, coefficient-six coding, N no-push controller,

symbolic verification interfaces, and contextual decoder folding methods.

and other retained packet dependencies are preserved in licenses/ and in
the corresponding research/reference subdirectories. PROVENANCE.json maps
the accepted construction inputs to exact pinned upstream paths and hashes.
The comparison records provenance for each recognized machine occurrence.

The ordinary mathematical PA proof is provided in the report and retained
reference texts. No newly compiled end-to-end Lean/Rocq theorem or completed
machine-specific iPRA formalization is asserted. Bounded negative searches
are not lower bounds. All exact novelty statements concern the recognized
literal-table formats in the pinned corpus, under the explicitly stated
graph symmetries.
