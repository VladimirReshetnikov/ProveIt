Finding
-------
The reviewed composition has no identified incompatibility between the
premise-exchanged quantifier head, coefficient-six numerical coding, no-push
MP, and the sum-one Shift guard. The ordinary mathematical proof chain gives
the claimed PA halting/refutation equivalence. Its logical calibration uses
explicit inherited syntactic proof transformations, not a newly completed
end-to-end Lean/Rocq theorem. The reviewed candidate has 194 working states;
this review does not claim a sub-194 result or a lower bound.

The finite check audit_qn6.py imports no builder, compiler, command checker, or
physical simulator from the candidate. Its receipt is independent_qn6_audit.json.
The separate physical checks were run by the construction/review team and their
literal hashes and receipts were inspected here; this reviewer did not repeat
an unnecessary third full symbolic physical validation.

1. Exact parent binding

Reconstructed the supplied Q6 native table from its pinned dense table and
label map. QN6 has exactly the same native state set, with exactly one altered
row: (164,1): (1,R,98) -> (1,R,114). The entire source node map differs only
at node163, from (node6,node55) to (node6,node54). The 3,795-site Q6 resolved
source becomes the 3,776-site QN6 source by deleting [3756,3775); the only
exterior entry into the deleted interval is the zero branch 3750 -> 3756.
Every remaining instruction and successor is equal under the explicit index
relocation. The retained seven-instruction copy loop is checked literally.

For comparison with N6, every row of all 135 raw fixed controls is identical.
The resolved source has exactly 83 changed instructions, in the inclusive
index range 2015..2133, accounting for the Q head. This is independently
checked against pinned repository blobs, rather than inferred from filenames
or trusted assertions in the new builder.

The one-row change is operationally substantive. On the sound proof store
(t,w,d,h)=(0,3,0,0), the stack is [U(0,0);0]=[2;0]=3. Successful MP_0 in Q6
pushes a dummy zero and yields w=[0;3]=9; QN6 leaves w=3. This is a concrete
change to command semantics. Structural comparison separately rules out
renaming, reflection, bit complement, start changes and terminal-action changes
against all 76 recognized existing full 194-state graphs.

2. Logical exchange

The exchanged head is precisely the two-premise exchange of universal
distribution. Exchange is derivable from the unchanged restricted
implication basis, independently of either distribution head. Inspected the
generic RestrictedImplication/ImplicationBasis proof templates and independently
replayed the serialized 61-line certificate: 17 K leaves, 14 S leaves, 30 MP
steps, no undischarged hypotheses. K and S are supplied by the unchanged
restricted basis. Numerical constructor evaluation, including I(0,0)=0,
preserves each finite MP identity. This permits proof translations in both
directions without circular use of the replaced quantifier axiom.

3. Coefficient-six calibration

The hereditary map must recode labels as well as formula constructors, since
Gamma(B) uses B itself as a variable label. The supplied inverse-on-image
recursion correctly establishes injectivity, thereby preserving binding,
shadowing and guarded disequalities. The recoding is not monotone. The proof
explicitly avoids that false shortcut: rho8All supplies the unrestricted
propositional schema through a fixed template whose own restricted leaves
use only reflexivity and domination by an implication code.

Residues four and five receive closed-truth translations. Positive numerical
formula components are smaller than their parent codes. Every decoded source
variable label in B is below B, so x_B is not free in its arithmetic
translation. Auxiliary bound variables in Bit are freshly renamed. An initial
overbroad sentence about all translated variable labels was corrected during
review to make this distinction explicit; no machine change was required.

Checked the exceptional zero implication and truth code explicitly. Finite
binary comprehension is supplied uniformly by inserting the translated
formula into a PA induction template, not by deciding truth. The gate proof
distinguishes the coincident-label case a=b using its singleton-point matrix.
Code one translates to Bit(x_0,x_0), which PA refutes. The reverse direction
uses the inherited PA-to-coefficient-four refutation compiler, hereditary
recoding and premise exchange. These are syntactic proof maps; no soundness
reflection for PA or truth predicate is assumed.

4. All-store compiler and edge cases

Manually derived the Push and total Pop identities from the exact ten-entry
AX stack, including the saved old top. After nine Shifts the generated rho
head is visible and U(d,h) is cached; after ten Shifts U(d,h) is visible and
the old top is cached. U(d,h) is at least two, so the eleventh Shift of Pop
cannot trigger the sum-one test even when the old top is one.

Checked the seven ordinary-head selectors, guarded-head selector, dominated
rho selector, and generalization word with the actual offsets and stack
positions. Discard is MP_0 when d=0 because its two branches have the same
result, even with an arbitrary old cache. MP_i on (0,w,0,0) yields offset i
uniformly, including i=0. Generalization reads the saved premise into the
cache before AX and selects U(i,a) at the correct final stack position.

The no-push MP compiler reserves exactly one zero before compiling its
premises. Immediately before the final two Shifts its store is
(0,[a,I(a,b),0;w],0,0). The second Shift either detects an already false
antecedent, or produces (a,[0;w],0,I(a,b)). MP_b then gives
(b,[0;w],b,0); total Pop consumes the reserved zero and returns (b,w,0,0).
The b=1 case works because Pop itself is total at a false top. The final
Shift detects the false conclusion. Zero-pair collapse, [0;0]=0, respects
all these equations and does not require treating numerical stacks as free
lists. Prefixing AX resets arbitrary incoming offsets/caches while the
proof compiler preserves any framed old stack.

5. Scheduling and soundness

The scheduler carries stores between candidate words; the proof therefore
uses an all-store halting word, rather than pretending that each trial starts
clean. The family of fresh candidate codes with an arbitrary leading AX
parameter is strictly increasing and unbounded, so a universally halting
refutation word is eventually tested unless halting occurs earlier. Positive
residual codes strictly decrease and constituent arithmetic loops terminate.

For the other direction, both visible and cached formulas and the canonical
Cantor stack entries are annotated by numerical/PA derivations. Offsets are
unrestricted arithmetic data. Shift transports proofs, MP composes proofs,
and AX supplies proofs. Since t+h=1 forces one annotated formula to be one,
a halt gives a PA refutation. The physical first-halt argument uses positive
finite source simulations plus determinism, not only sampled executions.

These checks support the ordinary theorem asserted in qn6_preservation.tex
while keeping its inherited proof dependencies and lack of a new trusted-kernel
endpoint visible. All findings concern the exact hashes above.
