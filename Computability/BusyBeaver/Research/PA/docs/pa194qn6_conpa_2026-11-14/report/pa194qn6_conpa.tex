\begin{abstract}
The requested strict reduction below 194 working states was not obtained.
We fulfill the alternative target with \textbf{PA194QN6}, an explicit
binary, single-tape, blank-start Turing machine with 194 working states,
whose nonhalting is provably equivalent in PA to $\Con(\PA)$ by the
ordinary mathematical proof assembled here. The construction combines
premise-exchanged quantifier distribution, coefficient-six formula coding,
and no-push modus ponens, retaining the original sum-one Shift test.
The combination is new in the inspected corpus; its ingredients are credited
to the supplied research. It is a change in formula arithmetic and proof-store
behavior, not a renamed copy of an existing transition table.

The literal allocation is $135+67-8=194$: fixed controls, axiom readers,
and simultaneous contextual folds. All 4,877 primitive source outcomes
pass both symbolic physical checkers. An independent implementation derives
four register-list lemmas from the actual rows and verifies the entire
blank initialization after 58,320 physical transitions. The action graph
is distinct from all 76 existing full 194-state graphs even under arbitrary
control renaming, a different graph-spanning root, tape reflection, symbol
complementation, and ignored terminal-edge actions.

The comparison is pinned to ProveIt revision
\texttt{2337ce611a26e308697ccbddb2a1636f989f01c3}. It covers the 61 filed
research packets, five current incoming packets, upstream snapshots, nested
archives, and recognized text/JSON machine tables. The census contains
1,832 table occurrences and 563 full single-halt graph classes, including
prototypes and rejected designs. Both reported 193-state graphs have a
specific obstruction. New bounded reduction searches, their exact domains,
and the failed startup repairs are retained. No lower bound or newly
compiled end-to-end Lean/Rocq theorem is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Outcome and literal specification}
\label{sec:outcome}

The principal deliverable is \file{machine/machine.tm}. It defines
$M=\mathrm{PA194QN6}$, with working states $0,\ldots,193$, start state 0,
and a separate terminal halt label 194. There are 388 rows, one for each
working-state/read-bit pair. The tape is two-way infinite, its alphabet is
$\{0,1\}$, and initially every cell is zero. The head begins at coordinate
zero. A row has the form
\[
    \texttt{state\quad read\quad write\quad direction\quad next},
\]
where the direction is L or R and moves the head exactly one cell. No
stationary transition, extra symbol, nonblank seed, or changed counting
convention is used. All working controls are reachable in the directed
transition graph; graph reachability does not assert that a particular
blank execution visits every state. Appendix~\ref{app:machine} lists the
entire dense table.

Let $H_M$ be the usual arithmetic sentence asserting a finite halting
computation of this literal table from that specified start, and fix a
standard primitive-recursive PA proof predicate. The result is
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\quad \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg H_M\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).\quad}
 \label{eq:main}
\end{equation}
Its proof is a composition of explicit syntactic proof transformations,
source-command correctness, and positive finite source-to-tape simulations.
It is an ordinary mathematical proof of a PA-provability statement.
The completed checks reported here do not constitute a new end-to-end
Lean or Rocq theorem, and they do not settle the repository's stronger
machine-specific iPRA formalization goal.

The strict target, fewer than 194 states, remains open in this work. The
alternative delivered here is a new verified composition at 194. Neither
the modest amount of native table editing nor the finite novelty census
is presented as a new general theorem of logic or a global priority claim.

\subsection{The two 193-state obstructions}

\paragraph{The coefficient-three machine.}
A physical implementation with $I_3(a,b)=3p(a,b)$ has a 193-state allocation,
passes its recorded physical checks, and reaches a complete blank seed.
But it retains the old four constructor tags, producing the collision
\[
 E_3(1,0)=3p(1,0)+3=6=3p(0,1)=I_3(0,1).
\]
The packet supplies an explicit numerical refutation of code one from this
collision, also expanded into 73,393 primitive numerical proof steps. Thus its
logical calibration fails. Reducing the coefficient without redesigning
the syntax is not a valid improvement. The current comparison retains it
as rejected, rather than as a smaller Con(PA) machine.

\paragraph{The 193-state runtime core.}
A different proposal merges native control 301 into 88, replaces
$88/0$ by $0L51$, and replaces $51/0$ by $0R122$. Its 4,780 source-frame
outcomes pass under the stated arithmetic-interface lemma, but the original
blank start enters a certified outward ray. Merely choosing another
existing initial control does not fix its initialization. The packet's
broader startup theorem permits arbitrary work registers at sites 25--44,
with the three proof-store registers zero; a complete physical frame is
still required. Section~\ref{sec:search} records the additional unsuccessful
repairs tried here. Runtime correctness alone is insufficient.

\section{How the new machine differs}
\label{sec:difference}

The construction is deliberately described as a composition of supplied
ideas. Its closest comparators make its exact contribution easy to assess.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lclll@{}}
\toprule Design &Coefficient& Distribution head & Successful MP & Shift test\\\midrule
PA194N6 &6& Original & No push, visible result &$t+h=1$\\
PA194Q6 &6& Premises exchanged & Dummy push &$t+h=1$\\
PA194QN/NQ &4& Premises exchanged & No push, visible result &$t+h=1$\\
PA194QH6 &6& Premises exchanged & No push, visible result &$h=1$\\
\textbf{PA194QN6} &\textbf{6}&\textbf{Premises exchanged}&\textbf{No push, visible result}&\boldmath$t+h=1$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The sum guard is a genuinely different command from the cached-only guard:
on an abstract store with $(t,h)=(1,0)$ it halts, while a cache-only test
does not; with $(t,h)=(2,1)$ the cache-only test halts and the sum test does
not. These examples distinguish command semantics, not reachable stores
under a consistency assumption. The new no-push MP keeps the stack unchanged
and returns its successful consequent in the visible theorem register.
The coefficient changes actual formula values, for example
$I_6(0,1)=12$ while $I_4(0,1)=8$.

Against the frozen N6 source, the new source has the same 3,776 primitive
sites but 83 different resolved instruction rows, in the quantifier-head
block. The difference at coefficient four is not used as a substitute for
this measured coefficient-six comparison. Against Q6, the native table
differs in exactly one row:
\[
 (164,1):\qquad (1,R,98)\longmapsto(1,R,114).
\]
The corresponding source deletes exactly the 19 instructions in the
half-open interval $[3756,3775)$, whose sole exterior entry is
$3750\to3756$. Every other instruction and successor agrees after relocation.
This small edit makes a concrete operational difference. On the sound store
$(t,w,d,h)=(0,3,0,0)$, where $3=[U(0,0);0]=[2;0]$, successful
$\mathsf{MP}_0$ leaves $w=3$ in QN6; Q6 instead pushes its dummy zero and
produces $w=[0;3]=9$. Thus ``materially different'' refers to observable
command behavior and its required proof compiler, while acknowledging the
one-row proximity of the two implementations.

The physical table is also
new under a deliberately broad symmetry test: every graph-spanning root,
arbitrary state renaming, global left/right reflection, global bit
complementation, and ignored write/direction at terminal edges. Exact
canonical transition tuples, not just unequal file hashes, are compared.
The test covers all 76 current full 194-state graphs and has a positive
control that identifies the old primary PA194 as an existing graph.
It says nothing about unmaterialized members of parameterized generators
or work outside the pinned corpus.

\label{sec:construction}

The builder starts with the frozen N6 raw fixed program and the Q axiom
reader grammar. The raw table has 135 fixed controls and 67 AX readers,
for 202 controls. The additional arithmetic helper realizes a three-increment
subtree inside the six-increment constructor loop. Eight disjoint contextual
folds remove eight controls. Their native labels and final replacement
actions are:

\end{center}

For each removed/retained pair, the two entries have distinct preceding
bits. All incoming rows establish the required context. The replacement
may inspect that preceding bit and return through an existing helper.
The checker replays all four history/current-bit combinations for each
pair against the \emph{final simultaneously folded table}, not eight
separate optimistic proposals. It preserves the complete two-cell tape,
projected control and head displacement, with arbitrary exterior tape.
All other rows commute with the control projection. Every raw step on a
justified entry context has a positive finite execution in the folded table
with the matching projected endpoint. Determinism then transfers halting
behavior; the source contracts below also validate the final folded table
directly. A final breadth-first numbering gives the dense
0--193 presentation; \file{machine/labels.json} records the exact conjugacy.

The compiler also expands the actual primitive source paths and resolves
structured jumps. Equal flattened macro words alone would be inadequate:
a \texttt{breakTo} or \texttt{continueTo} counts ancestors of one orientation,
so reassociation can change a jump destination. The independent verifier
therefore uses two source resolvers, based on different representations,
and compares the entire resulting opcode/register/successor array.
An exact source-emitter audit binds that array to the numerical formulas
and to the N6 fixed program.

The source frame contains a left marker, a binary source address, a zero
gap, a header, and unary register blocks $0\,1^{v+1}$. The logical registers
are $(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)$ and their physical positions are
$(6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1)$. The specification permits any nine natural primary
values, any nonempty finite spare list of natural values, and an arbitrary
nonnegative affine gap parameter. Both zero and positive decrement branches
are covered. The startup check compares every nonzero tape cell and the
zero exterior, not just a few registers or an observed visit to a dispatcher.

The two symbolic step counts use different symbolic rules and are not
literal Turing-machine time comparisons. In contrast, 58,320 is the exact
number of physical transitions from blank to the complete clean frame.
At that endpoint the native control is 84, the head is at $-2$, the logical
register vector is $(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$, and there are 26 spare blocks of

actual transition rows and uses no arithmetic-box oracle. It also checks
both source resolvers and the whole blank tape in a separate simulator.

The interpretation of the symbolic rules matters. A finite universal
contract is an induction argument over arbitrary unary lengths and finite
lists, supported by a finite trace certificate. It is not a bounded test
on small natural registers. These elementary inductions and the finite
control cases are formalizable in PA; their positive durations yield the
converse halting direction by determinism. The arithmetic consistency
calibration is a separate syntactic proof argument. A successful Python
run does not itself certify an internal arithmetic theorem, and the
reference Lean files included here are not advertised as a new standalone
Lean project.

\section{Research below 194: domains and failures}
\label{sec:search}

\subsection{Startup repair of the 193-state runtime core}

The existing runtime193 core was examined directly, with its exact native
rows frozen. First, all 58,485 pairs of candidate row edits at distinct row
locations were tested from each of the 193 possible initial controls:
11,287,605 simulations in total. The pool contains 346 changes suggested
by radius-six/four context analysis, including 19 speculative arithmetic-box
edits. These are search candidates, not a jointly proved neutrality class.
The acceptance detector allowed an entire source frame at any site 25--44,
any tape translation, padding at least 48, and \emph{all nine} register
values arbitrary. That is more permissive than the known sound-start
condition. No complete frame was found. The outcomes were
117,274 halts, 11,159,792 certified outward rays, and 10,539 bounded cases.
The per-run bounds were 250,000 transitions, head displacement 20,000,
and support size 20,000. The bounded cases are not divergence proofs.

Next, all $9!=362,880$ physical register permutations were tested from the
original start. Selection-router targets were permuted while the internal
scanner chain was kept fixed. Every such run entered an outward ray before
reaching the permissive complete-frame target. Transplanting the same core
patch into 49 available native 194-state variants and trying their existing
controls as starts also produced no accepted frame. Applying the same
three-row runtime patch directly to the new QN6 table likewise gives a
193-control table that reaches a certified blank ray after 92 transitions:
native control 35, head 14, and the only one-cells at $-3$ and $1$.
The row $35/0=1R35$ then marches forever into the untouched zero half-line.
These are tests of
specific transformations; they do not rule out another runtime or initializer.

A search for a second local control identification in the 193-state core
used the corrected radius-six source-context envelope and a 250-step local
bound. It examined 18,528 control pairs, 25,115,136 one-row trials and
195,563 joint candidates, finding no accepted local quotient. This is a
finite synthesis result, not a lower bound on runtime states.

\subsection{The ZFC marker relay}

The ZFC research contains an instructive repair that uses a temporary marker
to restore one bootstrap route after deleting control 301
\cite{zfcrelay}. Transplanted to the primary PA runtime, with its six
associated decoder-return redirects, the preliminary inherited-interface
screen fails nine of the 4,780 source cases. The failed sites are
2, 8, 3525, 3531, 3543, 3549, 3626, 3632 and 3660; all are structured
jumps. No complete recognized source frame was observed in the separate
2,000,001-transition blank run (the original receipt's two-million counter
has a documented off-by-one error). A fresh 116-transition prefix gives
the stronger short certificate: native control 35, head 18, one-cells
$\{-3,1,11,12\}$, and the same $35/0=1R35$ outward ray. The altered marker interface would itself
need a proof, so this preliminary screen is not called a successful runtime
verification. This transplant is rejected and is kept outside the accepted
machine directory.

\subsection{What carries over from the suggested projects}

The ZFC work contributes contextual control sharing, careful counting of
compatible folds, positive source simulations, and exact startup discipline.
The marker-relay failure above illustrates why a local success must be
rechecked on the whole new source. Its Con(ZFC) calibration does not itself
supply a Con(PA) result. ConciseIPRA contributes the method of reducing
logical interfaces by explicit proof translations and measuring the complete
checker \cite{ipra}. A shorter iPRA checker cannot simply replace this PA
calculus: the upper calibration here uses finite comprehension for arbitrary
arithmetic formulas, supplied by full PA induction. A new small calculus
would need both consistency reductions and an actually smaller compiled
binary machine. Neither checker source size nor fewer source tokens alone
implies fewer physical states.

\section{Reproduction and evidence boundaries}
\label{sec:reproduce}

Python 3.10 or later and its standard library suffice for the accepted
construction and verification. Assertions must remain enabled; do not use
\texttt{-O} or \texttt{PYTHONOPTIMIZE}. From the package root:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -B verify.py
python3 -B comparison/check_novelty.py machine/machine.tm
\end{verbatim}
The full verification reconstructs the frozen table, checks logical/source
binding and command certificates, and replays the independent physical
contracts and blank initialization. It requires no network, repository
checkout, solver, or proof assistant. The frozen comparison is likewise
self-contained. Rebuilding the complete census requires the pinned checkout;
its scope and instructions are in \file{comparison/AUDIT.md}.

From \file{report/}, build the article with:

pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error pa194qn6_conpa.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error pa194qn6_conpa.tex
\end{verbatim}
The optional startup and local synthesis experiments need a C++17 compiler;

stochastic discovery searches are unnecessary to reconstruct the accepted

The archive contains the complete dense/native/raw tables and label map;
