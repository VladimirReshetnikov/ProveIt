\begin{abstract}
This supplement supplies the complete proof translations for exchanging
curried premises in three axiom heads and for swapping the quantifier and
equality constructor tags. It also gives the matching no-push command
compiler with the original sum-based Shift guard. Numerical zero aliases,
arbitrary incoming stores, recoding of variable labels and the nonmonotone
side condition are treated explicitly. The inherited PA calibration and
restricted-implication template are identified as dependencies. These are
ordinary mathematical proofs whose syntactic reductions are verifiable in
PA, not a new end-to-end Lean or Rocq formalization. The complete physical
machines and their executable verification evidence are in the accompanying
research archive and main report.
\end{abstract}
