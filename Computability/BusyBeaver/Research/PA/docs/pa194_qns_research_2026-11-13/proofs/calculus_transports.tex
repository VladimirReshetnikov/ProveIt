\section{Two exact transports of the numerical proof calculus}
\label{sec:calculus-transports}

This section proves the new logical transformations independently of the
literal transition-table construction.  A transition table realizes a
transformation only after its full emitter and command interfaces have been
checked.  The proof below is an ordinary mathematical proof that the
corresponding syntactic reductions can be verified in PA; it is not a claim
that a new end-to-end Lean or Rocq theorem has been compiled.

\subsection{The inherited calculus and the precise dependency}

Write $p(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b$.  The standard constructors are
\[
 I(A,B)=4p(A,B),\quad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad
 U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2,\quad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
Code $0$ is truth, code $1=M(0,0)$ is contradiction, and
$N(A)=I(A,1)$.  Conjunction and existential quantification are the usual
classical abbreviations in $I,N,U$.  The six universally instantiated heads,
in their order of emission, are
\begin{align*}
 h_1(D,A,H)&=I(\exists x_D\,U(D,H),H),\\
 h_2(D,A,H)&=I(U(D,U(A,H)),U(A,U(D,H))),\\
 h_3(D,A,H)&=I(M(H,D),I(E(H,A),M(A,D))),\\
 h_4(D,A,H)&=I(E(H,D),I(M(A,H),M(A,D))),\\
 h_5(D,A,H)&=\exists x_D\,E(H,D),\\
 h_6(D,A,H)&=I(U(D,A),I(U(D,I(A,H)),U(D,H))).
\end{align*}
The unused parameter in a head is harmless.  In addition the calculus has
truth, the dominated implicational schema
\[
 \rho(D,A,H,d)=I(I(I(H,d),D),I(I(D,H),I(A,H)))\quad(d\leq D),
\]
the diagonal schema $\Gamma(B)=I(B,U(B,B))$, and the guarded separation gate
\begin{align*}
 V(a,b,c)&=I(M(a,b),U(c,M(a,b))),&P(a,b,c)&=I(c,M(c,b)),\\
 S(a,b,c)&=U\bigl(b,(M(a,b)\to P(a,b,c))\wedge(M(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
 Q(a,b,c)&=U(c,I(S(a,b,c),N(P(a,b,c)))),&G(a,b,c)&=N(I(V(a,b,c),Q(a,b,c))).
\end{align*}
The gate is an axiom when $c\ne a,b$; a failed guard returns $0$.
The inference rules are modus ponens and generalization of a theorem
$B\mapsto U(i,B)$.  These are finite proof trees with explicit axiom
parameters and inference annotations.

Call this calculus $\mathcal C$.  The inherited numerical-calculus
calibration provides primitive-recursive proof transformations in both
refutation directions between $\mathcal C$ and ordinary PA, with correctness
provable in PA.  Its nontrivial ingredients are the diagonal recovery of
reverse membership rewriting and neighborhood equality, the original
PA-refutation compiler, and the syntactic PA proof supplier for finite binary
comprehension.  The source is the primary PA194 report's
\texttt{calibration.tex} and its attributed reference texts; those arguments
are dependencies, not new results of the present emitter change.

The inherited theorem \texttt{rho8All} is used at its exact interface:
modus ponens, the dominated $\rho$ schema, reflexivity of $\leq$, and the
bounds $A,B\leq I(A,B)$ give a fixed derivation of unrestricted $\rho$.
The same finite template derives the usual $K$ and $S$ schemata, and hence
all implicational proof templates used below.  It works for any implication
operation with the displayed domination bounds, including the code-zero
alias $I(0,0)=0$; no injectivity assumption about implication is required.

\subsection{Exchange in the two equality-rewriting heads}

Define the exchanged versions
\begin{align*}
 h_3^{\mathrm e}(D,A,H)
   &=I(E(H,A),I(M(H,D),M(A,D))),\\
 h_4^{\mathrm e}(D,A,H)
   &=I(M(A,H),I(E(H,D),M(A,D))),\\
 h_6^{\mathrm e}(D,A,H)
   &=I(U(D,I(A,H)),I(U(D,A),U(D,H))).
\end{align*}
Any chosen subset of these three replacements defines a calculus
$\mathcal C_{\mathrm e}$.  The third replacement was already used in the
supplied Q family; the first two concern the membership-rewriting heads.
The emitted diagonal head may likewise be $\Gamma(A)$, $\Gamma(D)$, or
$\Gamma(H)$: the parameter is freely selectable in an AX call with zero
incoming offset and cache, so every diagonal instance remains available.
The same replacement can be combined with an independently checked
permutation of a head's three universally quantified parameters: its family
of instances is unchanged, and the proof selector uses the inverse
parameter permutation.

\paragraph{Explicit proof transformation.}
The closed combinatory term $\lambda f\,b\,a.\,f\,a\,b$ gives a fixed
$K,S$ derivation of
\[
 I\bigl(I(A,I(B,C)), I(B,I(A,C))\bigr).                 \tag{X}
\]
For a completely mechanical proof supplier, bracket abstraction is defined
by $[x]x=SKK$, $[x]t=Kt$ if $x$ does not occur in $t$, and
$[x](uv)=S([x]u)([x]v)$.  Its usual typing induction is a finite Hilbert
proof generator using only $K,S$ and modus ponens.  Apply it three times to
$f\,a\,b$ with variable types $f:A\to B\to C$, $b:B$, and $a:A$.
The delivered implementation produces 61 proof-DAG nodes: 17 $K$ leaves,
14 $S$ leaves, and 30 modus-ponens nodes. Its separate checker verifies
every leaf and edge and the closed conclusion. This is a fixed finite
derivation of (X), uniform in the numerical formula codes $A,B,C$.  Each of its $K,S$ leaves is replaced by the inherited
fixed $\rho$ proof template.

Given an ordinary $h_3$ axiom, instantiate (X) with
$(A,B,C)=(M(H,D),E(H,A),M(A,D))$ and apply modus ponens.  This gives
$h_3^{\mathrm e}$.  The analogous instances give $h_4^{\mathrm e}$ and
$h_6^{\mathrm e}$.  Interchanging $A,B$ in the same template proves the
reverse transformations.  Every other axiom and every inference is
unchanged.  Recursion on a finite derivation therefore gives
primitive-recursive maps $L,R$ satisfying
\[
 \mathrm{PA}\vdash
 \operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal C}(d,B)
   \Longrightarrow\operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal C_{\mathrm e}}(L(d),B),
\]
\[
 \mathrm{PA}\vdash
 \operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal C_{\mathrm e}}(d,B)
   \Longrightarrow\operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal C}(R(d),B).
                                                               \tag{E}
\]
Proof concatenation, fixed-template instantiation, and traversal of a coded
finite tree are primitive recursive.  PA verifies (E) by induction on that
tree.  The transformations preserve conclusions exactly, including code
one.  Thus they transfer the inherited PA refutation calibration without a
new soundness assumption or a change of logical strength.

\subsection{An independent residue-permutation construction}
\label{sec:residue-permutation}

The following alternative is a mathematical encoding transformation.  It is
included even if its literal implementation does not meet the size target;
it must not be confused with the accepted table's implemented semantics.
Keep $I,M$ unchanged and set
\[
 U^{\sigma}(a,B)=4p(B,a)+3,\qquad E^{\sigma}(a,b)=4p(a,b)+2.
\]
Replace $U,E$ consistently in every schema and in the emitted
generalization.  Let $\sigma$ fix residues $0,1$ and interchange $2,3$.
Define $F(0)=0$; for $n>0$, write $n=4p(a,b)+r$, $0\leq r<4$, and put
\[
 F(n)=4p(F(a),F(b))+\sigma(r).                         \tag{F}
\]
For every positive code its two decoded components are strictly smaller.
A primitive recursion that stores all earlier values therefore implements
(F), so $F$ is primitive recursive.  PA proves that $F(n)>0$ for $n>0$:
if $r\ne0$ its new residue is nonzero, while if $r=0$ then $(a,b)\ne(0,0)$
and induction gives a positive recoded component.

Strong induction, uniqueness of Cantor decoding, and
$\sigma^2=\mathrm{id}$ now prove
\[
 F(F(n))=n.                                             \tag{F1}
\]
Consequently $F$ is a bijection and preserves equality and disequality of
numerical labels.  It fixes $0,1$, and it exchanges $2,3$.  The constructor
identities, including the exceptional zero implication, are
\begin{align*}
 F(I(A,B))&=I(F(A),F(B)),&F(M(a,b))&=M(F(a),F(b)),\\
 F(U(a,B))&=U^{\sigma}(F(a),F(B)),&
 F(E(a,b))&=E^{\sigma}(F(a),F(b)).                       \tag{F2}
\end{align*}
One must recode numerical variable labels as well as numerical formula
codes.  Since $F$ is injective, this preserves binding, repeated variable
labels, and shadowing.  In particular,
\[
 F(\Gamma(B))=I(F(B),U^{\sigma}(F(B),F(B)))
             =\Gamma^{\sigma}(F(B)),                  \tag{F3}
\]
so diagonal self-indexing is preserved exactly.  Disequality preservation
carries the gate guard to the new guard.  Equations (F2)--(F3) carry every
other head, modus ponens, and generalization to its corresponding instance.

There is one real obstruction: $F$ is not increasing, since $2<3$ but
$F(2)=3>2=F(3)$.  Thus an old restricted $\rho$ leaf cannot simply be
recoded as a new restricted leaf.  Instead instantiate the unrestricted
$\rho$ template at the four recoded parameters.  Its own restricted leaves
have correct numerical side conditions because
$A,B\leq p(A,B)\leq4p(A,B)=I(A,B)$.  The reverse translation uses the same
method.  Recursion on proof trees gives PA-verifiable maps in both directions,
preserving a conclusion $B$ up to $F(B)$.  Since $F(1)=1$, refutations
translate both ways.  This proves the new numerical calculus is calibrated
to PA by composition with the stated inherited calibration.

The same construction can be combined with the head exchanges above.  For
formula coefficient $k>4$, use the inherited hereditary radix recoding first
and the tag transposition second.  On noncanonical residues $4,\ldots,k-1$,
the tag transposition fixes the residue.  It is still an involution of all
natural codes.  The coefficient-change soundness direction must retain the
inherited treatment of unused residues; it is not supplied by an inverse
map onto the coefficient-four syntax.

\subsection{The proof obligation for a literal emitter}

The free-constructor checker evaluates the complete emitter word as a stack
program, rather than inferring its meaning from the text labels of macros.
For the unchanged coefficient-four interface it must prove the pushed list
\[
 [t,\ U(d,h),\ \rho(D,A,H,d),\ h_1,h_2,h_3,h_4,h_5,h_6,\Gamma(A)]
\]
in order of emission, with $D=d+x$, $H=h+y$, and final guarded top
$G(H,D,A)$.  Exchanged heads or residue-swapped constructors are substituted
only where the chosen construction says so.  The last three guard macros
must retain their exact effect, including zero output on a failed guard
and the cleared temporary registers.  This algebraic check is one part of
correctness; complete source resolution, both physical decrement outcomes,
positive simulation durations, and the entire blank initialization remain
separate obligations.
