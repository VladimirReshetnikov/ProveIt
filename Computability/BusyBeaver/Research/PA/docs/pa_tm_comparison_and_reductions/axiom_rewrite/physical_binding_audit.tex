\subsection{Independent binding of the selected word to the complete source}
\label{sec:independent-source-binding}

The separate checker \file{audit_physical_binding.py} imports neither
the candidate's compiler nor its symbolic runtime. It parses the
complete delivered \file{Program.lean}, including its \texttt{trie}
root, and checks that this tree is exactly the pinned PA196 source
with only its AX subtree replaced by the delivered grammar. It
normalizes the two reused increment-pair macros and obtains exactly
the mathematically audited 199-token word.

\begin{lemma}[The selected emitter's nine stack entries]
Let the incoming accumulator be $t$, the incoming variable registers
be $d,h$, and the three post-prefix parameters be $D,H,A$.
After the selected AX word, the nine newly pushed entries, from top
to bottom, are
\[
 h5,\ h4,\ h3,\ h2,\ h6',\ h1,\
 \rho8(D,A,H,d),\ U(d,h),\ t,
\]
where $h6'$ is the antecedent-exchanged distribution head.
The enabled accumulator is the distributed gate $F_*(H,D,A)$;
the disabled accumulator is zero under the unchanged guard.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a constructor expression $X$, its mathematical postfix emitter
takes an incoming accumulator $v$ and stack $w$ to accumulator $X$
and stack $v::w$. For a variable or constant-one expression this is
the copy/push definition. In the binary-constructor case, the two
recursive emissions push the incoming accumulator and then the
first child; the constructor consumes the latter, leaving exactly
the former. Tag increments do not change the stack. This proves
the claim by structural induction on $X$.

The unchanged 18-token prefix ends with accumulator
$\rho8(D,A,H,d)$ and stack prefix $[U(d,h),t]$. The six successive
head emissions have order $[1,6',2,3,4,5]$; the final gate emission
pushes the last head. Applying the preceding stack identity gives
exactly the displayed list. The unchanged guard suffix preserves
that stack and either keeps the gate or clears the accumulator.
\end{proof}

The whole-source audit reconstructs the 3,773 primitive sites and
their paths from the delivered root. It resolves every relative
control transfer using the contiguous leaf interval of its target
subtree. The resulting instruction table agrees with every emitted
site record. Each ordinary macro's complete ordinal control graph
matches its pinned baseline pattern at every old and new occurrence;
the final closing transfer is checked separately after relocation.
All 169 exterior sites retain their original paths and primitives,
and all their control edges agree after the 39-instruction shift.
Thus no implicit assumption that an arbitrary reassociation
preserves relative jump depths enters this audit.

For the delivered 198-state table, the checker also independently
executes the 58,154-step blank-tape startup. It obtains native state
84, head position $-2$, source site 25 with path
\texttt{100000}, and the complete expected finite tape, including
the 48-cell routing gap and all 35 physical register blocks. This
checks equality of the whole finite support, not merely a tape
prefix. Arbitrary-register physical simulation remains the separate
runtime certificate's obligation.

The audit also passes with Python optimization enabled. A deliberately
changed complete source root is rejected, demonstrating that its
root check cannot be satisfied merely by preserving the AX subtree.
The exact candidate and source hashes appear in
\file{physical_binding_audit.json}.
