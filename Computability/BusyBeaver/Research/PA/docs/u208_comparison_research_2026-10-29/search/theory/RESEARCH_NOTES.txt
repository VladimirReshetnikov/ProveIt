WEAK FILTERED ADJUNCTION: AN INVESTIGATED ALTERNATIVE, NOT AN ACCEPTED MACHINE

The candidate schema, in classical first-order logic with equality, is:
  forall a,y exists b [forall x (E(x,b) -> phi(x)) and
    forall x ((E(x,a) or x=y) -> (phi(x) -> E(x,b)))].
The matrix may have parameters a,y and other variables, but not the fresh b.
Add global irreflexivity IR for the proposed machine-calculus interpretation.

At the ordinary first-order level this is equivalent to exact filtered
adjunction: exactify with the matrix
  (E(x,a) or x=y) and phi(x).
Conversely an exact filtered adjunction witness is a weak cover witness.
This syntactic transformation is effective. It does not, by itself, prove
PA-equiconsistency.

Friedman's 2002 Godel Lecture, section C2, page10, reports mutual
interpretability with PA for a four-atomic-formula filtered-adjunction
schema. The displayed variables in the PDF appear inconsistent (z versus w),
and the lecture gives no lower-interpretation proof. Do not substitute this
reference for a fully audited finite-refutation translation. Source:
https://cpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/u.osu.edu/dist/1/1952/files/2014/01/GodelLect060202-n2gdjc.pdf
The PDF was retrieved and the page visually inspected. It is retained here
as research evidence, not a new proof.

WHY FIXED LABEL5, RATHER THAN2
The first naive choice y=2 is unsound at c=2. Code2 is Q(0,0), a tautology;
then output=y and the lower clause forces E(y,y), contrary to IR.
Label5 avoids that collision because code5 is R(1,0), exactly the predicate
of being a member of the source. The self-output case can choose b=a.

The exceptional cases, under ordinary equality and the intended decoder:
- c=0: D(0)=true and output equals source. The lower clause reduces to the
  existence of a container for v5; take its singleton in the Ackermann model.
  The old-source part of the guard is tautological at the chosen output.
- c=1: D(1)=E(v0,v0) is false under IR. The output/test binders collide,
  but both clauses are true, so the existential is harmless.
- c=5: D(5)=E(v1,v0). Choose output v5 equal to source v0. The upper clause
  is tautological. The lower clause is
  (E(x,a) or x=a) -> (E(x,a) -> E(x,a)), also tautological.
For c>=2 except5, every label in D(c) is less than c, and c differs from
all three fixed roles0,1,5. Thus the output binder is fresh in the matrix.

A successful compilation would still need all of these separate proofs:
1. A fully audited PA lower interpretation of the exact chosen set theory.
2. A bridge for the equality guard. The inherited numerical logic obtains
   ordinary FOL by interpreting equality as membership indiscernibility.
   The old power-cover axioms were equality-free; this new guard is not.
   Ordinary-equality soundness and raw-code case checks do not discharge
   this completeness obligation.
3. Literal physical allocation, all-time interpreter contracts and blank
   startup calibration for a resulting machine.
