\begin{abstract}
No justified machine with fewer than 194 working states was obtained.
The positive result is two new complete blank-start machines,
\textbf{PA194H5XS} and \textbf{PA194JH5XS}, each with 194 working states,
a binary alphabet, and one two-way infinite tape.  They combine the
recent independent-consequent equality-witness presentations with a
symmetric destructive cache comparison, removing a register-restoration
loop.  The new tables are distinct from each other and from all 528
recognized prior spanning 194-state action graphs in the pinned corpus,
even under enlarged structural symmetries.  A complete new relative
mathematical proof over intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic
preserves blank-start halting.  Composition with the explicitly retained
written witness/AX11H calibration gives
\(\ipra\vdash\Con(\PA)\leftrightarrow\NH(M)\).
The absolute calibration is inherited written mathematics; an
end-to-end Lean or Rocq theorem is not claimed.  Both new literal tables
have fresh universal symbolic physical checks, full source/command
bindings, exact blank startup checks, and reproducible generators.
The comparison covers 100 delivered packets and records every recognized
table occurrence, every named positive-claim graph, and the failed or
diagnostic smaller tables separately.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, specification and evidence boundary}

The requested strict state reduction remains open in this work.  The
two delivered alternatives implement a recurring change to execution:
the old comparison restores a copied cache register and then destroys it
again, whereas the new comparison uses the copy already available and
interchanges the two destructive comparison arguments.  This preserves
the abstract command, saves a nonconstant number of source steps, and
changes the physical control graph.  The extra physical control is paid
for by an additional checked context fold.  These are new combinations
of existing, attributed ingredients, rather than new equality-witness
axioms or a new discovery of comparison symmetry.

The tape is indexed by the integers, all cells initially contain zero,
and the initial head position is zero.  There are no stationary moves.
Each row \file{q b w D r} reads \(b\), writes \(w\), moves one square
in direction \(D\in\{L,R\}\), and enters \(r\).  The terminal label
has no rows and is excluded from the state count.  The dense labels
0 through 193 are all reachable in the finite transition graph, which
does not imply that they all occur on the actual blank run.  Native
labels are a separate presentation: initial 74, dispatcher 84, terminal
254.  Native label 194 is a working label; it is not the dense halt.

Four kinds of evidence must remain separate:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Fresh finite checks bind source files, rows, label bijections,
      simultaneous folds, startup tape and source instructions.
\item Fresh symbolic physical checks cover arbitrary registers and
      spare lists using literal transitions and induction rules.
\item The new relative proof explains how those certificates and the
      source transformation yield iPRA-provable positive halting
      equivalences with primitive-recursive witness maps.
\item The absolute connection to PA refutations uses the identified
      revised written calibration, including its finite-demand proof
      compiler.  It is not newly kernel-checked by these scripts.
\end{enumerate}
This respects the repository's unfinished machine-specific Lean
development without discarding the stronger written arguments present
in later packets.

\section{Comparison of the supplied designs}
\label{sec:comparison}

\subsection{Coverage and exact identities}

The pinned tree contains 93 filed research packets under the PA
\file{docs/} directory and seven further archives under
\file{docs/incoming}.
The census checks 12,031 selected tracked Git blobs, with no missing
selected blob, and recursively inspects 23 distinct ZIP payloads.
Recognizers accept complete five-column binary tables and four explicit
JSON table encodings.  They find 5,686 table occurrences, 1,338
normalized labeled tables and 1,125 spanning action-graph classes
over all sizes.  Twenty-two parser exclusions are audited separately;
they are small or partial fixtures with at most 139 working labels and
cannot hide a 194-state isomorphic table.  These are scope-bounded
mechanical counts, not counts of consistency theorems.

For a spanning graph, a traversal from a chosen root gives an exact
ordered action tuple.  Minimizing that tuple over all spanning roots
removes dependence on the start label and state names.  The novelty
test additionally allows global tape reflection, global bit exchange,
and erasure of the write/move decoration on a final transition to halt.
It compares full tuples; hashes are only convenient identities.
Bit exchange is used as a stronger structural exclusion, not asserted
as a zero-blank equivalence.  Nonspanning tables cannot be isomorphic
to the new spanning tables and are recorded separately.

There are 528 prior spanning 194-state graph classes and 55 spanning
193-state research classes.  The 528 divide, by attributed report role,
into 84 principal constructions, seven supplements, 419 research
auxiliaries, 14 rejected tables and four seeds.  Thus there are
\emph{91 named positive-claim graphs}, not 528 independently proved
consistency machines.  The growth from the filed catalog's 139 classes
is explained exactly by 17 newly proposed graphs and 372 incoming
research auxiliaries.  In particular, 370 of those auxiliaries occur
in one physical-search corpus.  The comparison does not mistake that
large research corpus for hundreds of new positive theorems.

The complete ledgers are \file{comparison/frontier194_current.csv},
\file{comparison/named_designs_current.csv},
\file{comparison/families_current.csv}, and
\file{comparison/packet_ledger_current.csv}.  The occurrence ledger
retains precise repository or nested-archive origins.  Every named
positive-claim graph has been reconciled against literal table identity.
All 528 prior 194-state graphs, and the two new graphs, have 194 classes
under immediate-action bisimulation: no ordinary identical-action state
quotient gives a free saving.  This says nothing about context-sensitive
or variable-length simulations.

\subsection{The main architectural choices}

All small accepted candidates in this lineage retain a common basic
architecture: enumerate encoded command words; maintain a stack and
cached formula; construct numerical axioms; apply modus ponens and
generalization; and halt when a contradiction code is exposed.  Their
differences occur at three mathematically distinct levels.

At the \emph{calculus} level, constructor coefficients, quantifier
argument order, premise exchange in distribution, diagonal parameter
choice, and the equality-witness head change which numerical formulas
are generated.  These changes require proof translations that respect
the guarded separation gate, the self-indexed diagonal and the
restricted propositional axiom.  A shorter emitter is useful only if
its resulting calculus still has both directions of the PA-refutation
translation.

At the \emph{command} level, cached-head MP, visible no-push MP,
sum-versus-cache halt tests, the eleven-entry AX interface and explicit
generalization binders change the representation and stack protocol.
Their proofs must cover an arbitrary carried store, canonical zero
stack aliases, all consumed fields, and the exact selector words.
Testing a command only on an empty stack or forgetting a fourth AX
field would not establish its invariant.

At the \emph{physical} level, reader-tree sharing, contextual folds,
register allocation, loop reassociation, marker separation and return
shortcuts change the literal table.  They require whole-tape simulation
and a genuine blank startup.  A fold score, a successful source-level
run, or a runtime core with an uninitialized tape is insufficient.

The familiar letters therefore do not encode a linear sequence of
improvements.  For example, Q is a distribution-premise change; N
removes an MP dummy push; H tests the cache; S rearranges a destructive
comparison; G exposes a generalization binder; LC changes a constructor
loop layout; and F or SF denotes particular guarded physical shortcuts.
Some names are reused in the delivered material: a sum-guard NS and a
cache-guard NS are distinct.  Exact graph keys and source semantics,
rather than names alone, identify the comparison records.

Appendix~\ref{app:families} compares all 35 annotated families by
semantics, physical implementation and required preservation argument.
The CSV ledgers are the exhaustive, searchable per-design and per-packet
comparison; the family table does not replace those identities.

\subsection{Why the new pair is material}

The incoming witness packet supplies two new numerical presentations,
H5X and JH5X, but only their asymmetric cached comparison implementations.
The present pair preserves those exact emitters and changes their
recurring Shift implementation.  It deletes three primitive source
instructions, removes a restoration loop, changes raw controller
transitions and adds a non-reader context fold.  The exact step saving
depends on the unbounded cache value, as proved below.  Both tables
are absent from all 528 prior action classes and are mutually distinct.
Their novelty is a compatible, fully checked composition in this
pinned recognized corpus, not a claim that their component ideas or
all possible equivalent algorithms are new.

\section{The absolute calibration and its review}

The proof dependency is deliberately concrete.  The immediate source is
the exact incoming witness archive with SHA-256
\begin{center}\small\path{cc51ef4f2858e37cc72c5079bb6f8b0d86ce0ed9eae1aed428c1bd69baffb4cc}.\end{center}
It contains the H5X and JH5X proof translations and the unchanged revised
AX11H calibration archive, whose SHA-256 is
\begin{center}\small\path{93ee082ef035b2d32e71d6af5239b25b4bea1773f03bea4d2c39810a7fe15075}.\end{center}
The latter corresponds to the filed directory
\file{pa194_ax11h_research_2026-11-14_2}, not the older same-name packet.
The consistency endpoint is
\file{DiophantineConsistency.IPRAPA196Consistency.nativePAConsistency},
the negation of the native reading of the mapped Foundation PA-refutation
predicate.  The retained Foundation gitlink is
\path{32e1a0956a8622fad067328ca1959729a7634428}.

An independent review for this work read the three completion fragments
on the Foundation adapter, term leaves and forward calibration, totaling
2,384 source lines, together with their concluding equivalence and
soundness fragments.  It also checked sensitive claims against the
actual imported Lean logical and arithmetic-certificate sources.
The full review and its limits are in
\file{references/written_calibration_review.md}.

The central weak-arithmetic issue is whether proof-producing recursion
has actually been bounded before its recursive outputs are used.
The completion fragments organize a request by a numerical rank and
finite environment.  Child queries are computed first; same-family
ranks strictly decrease and there are at most two such children.
A complete binary request tree to the bounded depth is generated with
inactive placeholders and evaluated backwards.  Forward and backward
Boolean invariants express query validity and proof correctness.
These are primitive-recursive computations with quantifier-free
induction, rather than an invocation of induction over all possible
environments.  Calls into an already completed stratum can increase
syntax size without forming a recursive cycle.

A second sensitive point is removal of primitive equality vacuity.
The source check locates its two consumers: reverse membership rewriting
and the neighborhood-equality interface.  The new written construction
uses the diagonal clause at the code of the negation of the entire
desired reverse implication, and at the code of the desired equality,
respectively.  The old reverse-proof constructor cannot simply be
reused verbatim: one of its converse property premises would fail.
The written completion reconstructs the needed argument instead.  The
review found no concrete defect in these checked replacements.

Fixed arithmetic leaves are actual finite derivation recipes, not a
postulated calibration axiom.  Supplying those leaves alone would not
give arbitrary PA induction.  The completion separately supplies
arbitrary-property separation, the ordinal induction template,
arithmetic formula realization, and canonical matrix/closure loops.
This dependency order is part of the inherited written theorem.

This focused review is not an independent reconstruction of every
transitive lemma, an execution of the enormous PA proof compiler, or a
new end-to-end Lean build.  It is sufficient to identify what theorem
is being imported and why the newer written claim should not be
confused with the older PA-only reports.  The new relative theorem is
valid independently of accepting that absolute imported theorem; the
displayed absolute Con(PA) corollary explicitly composes with it.
No consistency assumption or PA reflection principle enters that
composition.

\subsection{What the failed cores establish}

A previously supplied 193-control core folds native 301 into 88 and
changes a marker route through native 51 and 122.  Its canonical
instruction contracts can succeed while its blank startup fails: the
initialization reaches a context where the first marker must create a
delimiter that the runtime-only replacement does not create.  This
demonstrates a real distinction between an initialized interpreter and
a complete blank-start machine.

The direct application of that core deletion to each new witness
machine was also tested here.  Each of its 193 possible initial controls
was classified: two halt and 191 reach certified outward rays, with no
bounded-limit case.  The intended native initial control 74 reaches
its ray certificate after 92 steps.  These two exact fixed-table
transplants are therefore not positive 193-state deliverables.

The outward-ray certificate is stronger than a finite timeout.  At a
certified configuration the zero transition returns to the same state,
moves outward, and every nonzero cell lies strictly behind the head.
Induction shows that the machine continues into untouched blank cells
forever.  A capped run in a larger mutation screen has no such conclusion
and remains explicitly unresolved.

A tempting engine deletion was also examined conceptually.  Native
198 distinguishes spare-tail extension from zero decrement; its
one-input branch is actively used during zero decrement and startup.
Replacing native 66's zero action by a direct return can remove the
spare-appending state but loses this other branch.  A proof of the
increment excursion alone would therefore not validate the reduction.
This is one reason the retained searches check both decrement cases
and the entire source contract set before testing startup.

\subsection{No lower-bound claim}

Neither absence of a hit in the grammar search, immediate-action
bisimulation rigidity, nor failure of these startup transplants proves
that 194 states are necessary.  The searches keep a particular numerical
calculus family, interpreter architecture, or finite local replacement
language fixed.  A smaller machine may change a global representation,
use a stronger reachable-state invariant, or reorganize source and
startup together.  The next state saving must be checked against all
three interfaces: calculus calibration, command semantics, and complete
blank physical execution.

\section{Use of the three related research areas}

The ZFC work supplies useful engineering principles: optimize the
source tree and available physical helper states together, and prove
a quantitative protection invariant when startup leaves an unsafe
stack suffix.  Its zero-spare Z215 example changes a single write bit
and initial state, then proves a debt-two protection theorem for the
exact scheduler.  For the PA family, the direct zero-spare transplant
already has an all-start halt/ray obstruction.  Physical spare blocks
and logical stack entries are distinct resources; the ZFC debt bound
cannot be transferred by matching their counts.  The present work
retains a fully clean startup and uses the physical-fold principle.

DiophantineConsistency is useful for its exact native iPRA consistency
endpoints, bounded coded computations and model-uniform proof methods.
However, the small published equations target the repository's ZFC
refutation predicate, and their circuit-operation counts are not binary
TM state counts.  Compiling an equation requires enumeration,
integer arithmetic, coefficient construction and comparison, as well
as a new PA calibration if the refutation target changes.  No verified
state price below 194 follows from the displayed small equation alone.

ConciseIPRA demonstrates that logical rules can sometimes be derived
from a much smaller native calculus and that a complete checker should
be optimized rather than only its axiom list.  Its target theory is
iPRA itself, whereas the machine must search for a PA refutation.
Replacing the searched PA-strength calculus by an iPRA-equiconsistent
calculus would change the consistency problem.  Its methodology helps
organize bounded primitive-recursive proof transformations, but its
Python byte count does not supply a smaller PA-consistency TM.

There is a concrete warning already in the PA corpus: the equality-free
radix-three deletion and deletion of the equality-witness head admit a
primitive-recursive Boolean valuation that validates the retained
calculus while falsifying contradiction.  Those deletions can make the
theory weaker enough that iPRA proves its consistency.  They are not
valid routes to the requested PA-refutation problem.  The generalized
witness presentations used here avoid that error through explicit
two-way proof translations.

\section{Reproduction and deliverables}

The archive is self-contained for positive machine reconstruction,
source binding, symbolic physical verification and novelty replay.
Python 3.10 or later with its standard library suffices.  Assertions
must remain enabled.  From the archive root, use a new output directory:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -B verify_release.py --work /absolute/path/new-replay
\end{verbatim}
The driver checks integrity, invokes the independent machine replay,
rebuilds the two tables from frozen grammar/fold data, compares exact
bytes, rechecks source commands and affine frame closure, and compares
the new graphs with all frozen prior representatives.  It also checks
the exact imported witness archive identity.  Full physical replay is
deliberately included; stochastic discovery is not needed to recreate
the machines.

The deterministic generator is \file{construction/build_symmetric.py}.
Each directory under \file{machines/} contains the dense and native
tables, label map, grammar, source sites and resolved instructions,
folds and their local traces, source/command checks, full symbolic
contracts and receipts.  The independent checker reads literal rows;
it does not call the construction compiler's arithmetic-box oracle.
Its four generic finite-list traversals are rederived from the new
table before the site checks run.

\file{comparison/} contains the exhaustive ledgers, source/census
identities, prior representatives, exact novelty checker and a prose
comparison.  Rebuilding the complete corpus scan additionally requires
the pinned ProveIt source; the offline novelty check does not.
\file{research/} retains the new bounded searches, reproducible inputs,
programs and receipts.  These records distinguish completed results
from time-capped runs.

\file{references/} preserves the original witness ZIP unchanged,
including its proof-calibration ancestry, together with the focused
review.  The report's absolute theorem invokes that written ancestry;
the replay driver does not pretend to kernel-check it.  Original
licenses and attribution are retained.  No upstream GitHub file was
modified by this work.

The report is built by running pdfLaTeX twice in \file{report/}:
\begin{verbatim}
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error report.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error report.tex
\end{verbatim}
The machine appendices are generated from the delivered row files, so
their labels and actions are bound to the same exact artifacts as the
verification receipts.
