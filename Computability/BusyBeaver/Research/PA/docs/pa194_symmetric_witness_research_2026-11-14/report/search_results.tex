\subsection{New source-grammar searches}

After constructing the symmetric witnesses, two further stochastic searches
reassociated their macro trees while preserving the flattened macro word.
Both shared-subtree and single-occurrence rotations were used; axiom order
and parameter positions were held fixed.  The controller fold $41\mapsto330$
was preapplied, leaving 134 fixed controls.  A local-context screen sought
an effective reader count of at most 59, which would price a candidate at
193 states before full source and startup verification.

Each run had a 300-second wall-time budget, with seeds 15110201 and

Heuristic penalties constrain tree height and decoder paths, so this is
not exhaustive even over reassociations.  The candidate screen is also

frozen input templates, retained grammars and unabridged logs are in
\file{research/grammar/}; no lower state price was obtained.

\subsection{Startup repairs of the 193-state core}

A separate search varied physical arithmetic and startup controllers of
the known 193-state runtime core.  A finite register-file screen produced
3,093 row alternatives.  Filtering their affected abstract source
contracts left 28.  This screening is candidate generation: the physical
tests use finite registers, and the abstract source checker has an
arithmetic-box interface.  A passing edit would still require universal
literal-tape contracts and blank initialization.

No complete startup frame was found.  The combination screen allows at
most one replacement per affected row; it does not assert that all
combinations preserve the runtime contracts simultaneously.

A broader paired-controller screen tested 12,167 distinct edit pairs from
native start 74.  Each pair changes at least one of 16:0, 24:0, 35:0 or
45:0, on the failing startup route.  It found 8,891 rays, 2,818 certified
outward drift patterns, 105 exact cycles and 353 bounded limits, again
with no complete frame.  The frame search admits arbitrary values in the
first nine registers and source sites 25 through 44; it checks the entire
finite tape support and at least ten complete register blocks.

The paired screen deliberately admits header length at least 38, a
superset of the intended sound seeds.  For this primary grammar, the
abstract source checker fails at header 38 (site 3247) and 39 (site 3248);
headers 40 and 48 pass all 4,780 abstract outcomes.  Thus the negative
header-38 screen is not a claim of universal source correctness at 38.

Each broad run has a ceiling of 200,000 transitions and additional
head/support caps.  The 12,233 capped runs across the two broad screens
are unresolved.  Rays, outward drifts and exact cycles have explicit
finite certificates; a timeout alone is not a nonhalting certificate.
The complete sources, fixed inputs, exact stopping criteria, receipts and
replay commands are in \file{research/startup/RESEARCH_NOTE.md}.
