\section{Two symmetric witness-family machines}
\label{sec:symmetric-witness}

The two new tables are denoted $M_{\mathrm{H5XS}}$ and
$M_{\mathrm{JH5XS}}$.  Their direct predecessors are the independent-
consequent witness machines PA194H5X and PA194JH5X in the incoming packet
\texttt{pa194\_witness\_research\_2026-11-15.zip} at the pinned revision.
The new construction changes the cache-test implementation, preserves
each predecessor's numerical calculus and command semantics, and uses an
additional context fold to pay for the added controller state.  This is
a composition of attributed mechanisms, not a claim that either the
witness calculus or the symmetric test is independently new.

Both machines have 194 working states, a separate terminal label 194,
start state 0, alphabet $\{0,1\}$, and one two-way infinite tape which is
initially all zero.  The head initially lies at coordinate zero.  Every
transition writes a bit and moves one square left or right.  Every
working state is reachable in the transition graph; this statement does
not assert reachability on the actual blank execution.

The dense-file SHA-256 digests are

\subsection{The retained calculus and command interface}

Let $I(A,B)=4\pi(A,B)$ for Cantor pairing $\pi$, and use
$U(d,B)=I(B,d)+2$, $E(a,b)=I(a,b)+3$, and contradiction code 1.
Both witness machines replace the original equality-witness head by
\[
  U(D,I(E(H,D),1))\longrightarrow A,
\]
where $A$ is independent of $D,H$.  Setting $A=1$ recovers the original
head; conversely the original head and explosion derive every such new
head.  The J machine additionally uses the positive-join first head
\[
  (X\longrightarrow H)\longrightarrow H,
  \qquad X=U(D,I(U(D,H),1)).
\]
The witness packet supplies the coupled proof translations, including the
explosion bootstrap needed before replacing the first head.  The new
symmetric machines retain these exact axiom emitters, their ordinary
sixth-head premise order, their separate diagonal and guarded gate, and
their dominated propositional head.

The AX command has eleven entries, an explicit generalization binder,
and a real fourth field.  MP succeeds visibly without a dummy push.
Shift halts exactly when its incoming cache $h$ is one.  Neither the
fourth AX field nor the incoming carried store is discarded in the proof.
The new free-algebra checks compare the entire AX result, including all
nine registers, both numerical guards, the scratch preconditions and
the residual fourth-field encoding.  The emitters have 189 macro tokens
for H5XS and 188 for JH5XS, exactly as in their respective predecessors.

\subsection{Removing a register-restoration loop}

Write the nine registers as $(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)$.  A clean command frame has $A=X=Y=0$;
$N,T,W,D,H,L$ are arbitrary natural numbers.  At a clean Shift
entry, let $T=t$ and $H=h$.  The asymmetric predecessor performs
the following primitive-register operations before entering its common
comparison routine:
\[
 \operatorname{Clear}(T);\quad
 \operatorname{Duplicate}(H,T,X);\quad
 \operatorname{Transfer}(X,H);\quad X{+}{+}.
\]
Immediately before comparison, $(T,H,X)=(h,h,1)$.
The new version performs
\[
 \operatorname{Clear}(T);\quad
 \operatorname{Duplicate}(H,T,X);\quad H{+}{+},
\]
giving $(T,H,X)=(h,1,h)$.  The comparison routine destructively clears
both compared registers, taking its equality exit precisely when they
were equal.  Thus both versions halt exactly at $h=1$.  On their
inequality exits they have the same full register file and execute the
same stack-pop suffix.  Their halted files also agree because $h=1$
forces the same precomparison triple.

This is a total identity for every clean incoming store, including zero
stack aliases.  It is not a simplification justified only on a sampled
run.  The source graph loses precisely the three instructions of
$\operatorname{Transfer}(X,H)$.  H5XS has 3,674 primitive sites and
JH5XS 3,680; their two-branch outcome counts are 4,782 and 4,790.

The exact cost of the comparison routine on $(H,X)=(u,v)$ is
\[
 C(u,v)=\begin{cases}
 3u+2,&u=v,\\
 3v+2,&u<v,\\
 2u+v+4,&u>v.
 \end{cases}
\]
Transfer costs $3h+1$ primitive steps, including its terminating test.
Consequently the saving in primitive source steps for one Shift is
\[
 (3h+1)+C(h,1)-C(1,h)
 =\begin{cases}0,&h=0,\\4,&h=1,\\2h+4,&h\ge2.\end{cases}
\]
This is a source-operation saving.  No assertion of the same uniform
saving in physical tape transitions is made: the two physical decoders
and their context folds have different local clocks.

\subsection{Relative iPRA theorem}

Let $P$ be either asymmetric witness predecessor and $P^S$ its new
symmetric partner.  A clean command frame contains a full coded
register file, residual candidate, fresh counter, source address,
header gap, finite nonempty spare list and tape offset.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform relative preservation]
For each of the two literal blank-start pairs,
\[
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash
    \operatorname{Halt}_{\mathrm{blank}}(P^S)
       \longleftrightarrow
    \operatorname{Halt}_{\mathrm{blank}}(P).
\]
Both directions have primitive-recursive transformations of halting
witnesses.  This is an ordinary mathematical proof; no new proof-kernel
endpoint is asserted.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The clear, duplicate, transfer and compare identities follow by
quantifier-free counter induction on their explicit primitive-register
programs.  The calculation above therefore proves equality of the
complete abstract Shift effects over iPRA.  AX and MP have identical
templates and emitters in each pair.  Candidate selection, field parsing,
fresh-counter update and the scheduler's carried store are identical.
Thus both primitive source programs implement the same totalized
abstract command scheduler at every clean command boundary.

The source-to-command clocks are explicit primitive-recursive sums of
the positive durations of the arithmetic templates.  Induction on the
number of commands in the equation between the coded source state at
the cumulative clock and its abstract command state gives the forward
simulation.  Conversely, if the source halted at time $t$ without the
first $t+1$ abstract states containing a halt, positive segment lengths
would place a nonhalting clean source frame after time $t$.  This
contradicts absorbing halting.  Bounded search through the finite prefix
extracts the corresponding command-halt witness.  All these invariant
and finite-prefix tests are primitive recursive; no unbounded universal
induction formula or Markov principle is needed.

At the physical layer, interpret each literal symbolic certificate rule
in finite binary-word or zipper coding.  A literal row is a defining
equation.  A uniform run sweep is induction on its run length; the
four arbitrary-list traversals are induction over a coded finite list
using their one-block identities.  Concatenation and complete-frame
equalities are primitive-recursive equations.  The finite certificate
composition therefore gives iPRA-correct positive local source-step
contracts.  Their finite enumeration covers each source site and both
decrement branches.  The frame-closure audit checks the exact successor
address and nonnegative duration coefficients, with a strictly positive
constant, for every outcome.  The full finite startup supplies the base
frame.  The same positive-clock argument relates the literal machine
to its primitive source in both directions, with a bounded backward
witness extraction.  Compose these two physical/source equivalences
with equality of the abstract command scheduler.

For the child physical clock one can equivalently define, before source
halting, $T(0)=57768$ and
$T(n+1)=T(n)+k(S^n(s_0))$, including the evolving gap and spare file
in $s$.  Give it arbitrary total primitive-recursive values after a
halt.  The parent clock uses its independently checked startup constant
$57922$ instead; the clocks need not agree numerically.  Then $T(n)\ge57768+3n$ on every nonhalting prefix.  The displayed
upper bound and the primitive-recursive source-state iteration also
give a primitive-recursive upper clock by summing their right-hand
sides.  Neither proof direction assumes a constant gap, a constant
spare file, or that a physical halting time occurs at a source boundary.
\end{proof}

The witness packet supplies its written relative translations to the
revised AX11H/QAX11H calculus and retains the complete revised written

\[
 \mathsf{iPRA}\vdash
 \operatorname{Con}(\mathsf{PA})
   \longleftrightarrow\neg\operatorname{Halt}_{\mathrm{blank}}(P^S).
\]
