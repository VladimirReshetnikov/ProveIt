PA MACHINE COMPARISON AT 768c2be1a8e4d2fcf8aa3443c7b0f084d145734c
================================================================

Scope and reproducibility
-------------------------
The census inspects all36 top-level ZIP archives in docs/incoming, descends
recursively into nested ZIPs, and reads all direct .tm files in the PA
research subtree. Each distinct archive body is traversed once; repeated
archive references are still recorded. Complete numeric binary five-column
.tm and .txt tables are recognized when each working state has exactly two
rows and there is exactly one target with no rows. Direct PA .txt files were
also checked separately and contain no additional recognizable tables.
Tables embedded only in arbitrary source languages or JSON are outside this
recognizer; the report and README review supplements the literal census.

Fresh results:36 top-level archives,49 distinct archive bodies,77 archive
references,maximum nesting depth8,26258 member occurrences,2519 complete
table occurrences,606 byte-distinct tables. Exact action-graph comparison
ignoring the chosen start gives437 classes; two disconnected synthetic
controls are excluded. This is deliberately NOT437 established consistency
machines. Many entries are ancestor copies, failed experimental allocations,
and negative controls. All rooted variants can differ even within one
unrooted class. Our graph comparison ranges over every root, so a mismatch
is sufficient to rule out mere rooted relabeling, while a match needs the
actual start convention before claiming the machines are identical.

census_current.py is adapted (with attribution) from the previous PA194
packet's comparison/census.py, with current pin and full class export.
Run:
  python3 -B census_current.py --repo /path/to/ProveIt --output census
The source repository must be at the pinned revision. No archive contents
are executed during the census. Census and graph canonicalization make no
claim about infinite blank-tape behavior or arithmetized consistency.

Major earlier lineages
----------------------
U208 packages share the native172/211 history fold from a U209 ancestor.
The initially advertised nine tables are two rooted graphs; the later helper
packet expands the accepted family to20 graphs. James Chang's PA209 is a
different ancestor despite its equal state count. The U208 saving cannot be
subtracted again from PA196:PA196 already replaces the relevant AX decoder.

PA196's original source has3812 primitives,201 AX macro tokens,4959 branch
contracts,58154 startup. Later196 variants change axiom association/order,
physical register placement,closing jumps,or command effects. Those are
orthogonal dimensions only when their physical row constraints are
compatible. Two rejected Shift196 tables halt from blank after43563 steps;
their finite traces have separate Lean checks in the repository.

Diagonal vacuity replaces a more costly gate by Gamma(A)=A->forall_A A,
using the constructor field-descent fact to obtain freshness of the formula
code as variable. This reduces201 to191 emitter items and3812 to3700 sites.
Saving Gamma as a separate stack head removes one cons item, giving190
items and3673 sites, but changes the AX interface from nine to ten entries.
Consequently theorem-selection command words must be recompiled. Ten-entry
selectors for original Shift/MP cannot be copied unchanged to the Combined
controller; the appropriate existing195C compiler is the correct reference.

Source instruction count is not physical state count. NEG18 saves232
source instructions (3673->3441) but its best audited physical allocation is
197, because useful shared tree shapes and contextual folds are lost.
Similarly157-node and158-node representations do not establish195-state
machines if a scanner or a decoder helper must be added back.

Direct PA subtree
-----------------
There are14 direct literal .tm files, individually catalogued in
 direct_pa_catalogue.json and direct_pa_catalogue.csv.
They comprise imported PA196/PA209, duplicate native encodings, the alternate
shorter-path196,and197/198 allocator,source-pruning,negation/MP and shortcut
experiments. The198 root-frame experiment lacks correct blank initialization;
the initial197 allocator candidate fails a symbolic runtime contract.
Startup-preserving197 candidates and physical shortcuts have stronger
finite evidence but do not improve state count.

The numerous pa195-* proof directories are not additional machine designs.
The quotient/all-tape/translation/ordinary/simulation/control/global/dense
chain develops an ordinary Lean proof of the original202-to195 halting
preservation. Register runtime,source ordinals,symbolic batches,direct
coverage and source iteration develop different portions of source-to-table
binding. The source-iteration theorem still carries a Coverage premise;
the arithmetic endpoints carry separate machine-to-refutation reduction
obligations. The complete iPRA equivalence is explicitly not claimed by
the repository. This does not refute a written proof of PA-provability:
PA is stronger, and ordinary mathematical proofs need not be formalized in
Lean to be valid. One must distinguish missing formalization from an actual
mathematical counterexample.

Evidence and limitations
------------------------
The inventory is exhaustive for the stated numeric table recognizer and the
36 top-level archives at the pin. Report-based descriptions are grounded in
the primary manuscripts and READMEs, with member locators in the catalogue.
Older reported symbolic counts are descriptive unless separately rerun in
the main construction task. All437 action graphs were not independently
proved consistent or simulated for arbitrary inputs. Complete infinite
blank execution is not tested. Failed bounded searches are not state lower
bounds. No claim of universal minimality or of a new full Lean/Rocq endpoint
is implied by the census or the novelty certificate.
