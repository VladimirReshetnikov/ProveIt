\section{Question, conventions, and acceptance criterion}

The original question asked for a machine with fewer than 209 states, after
comparing several constructions based on the proposed unary theory $\UU$.
The subsequently supplied commits already contain 208-state constructions.
The research target of this dossier is therefore \textbf{at most 207 working
states}. Reproducing a supplied U208 construction does not meet that revised
target, and a state renaming does not constitute a further saving.

Throughout, a machine has one two-way infinite tape, alphabet $\{0,1\}$,
blank symbol 0, head coordinate 0 at initialization, and an all-zero initial
tape. Every transition writes one symbol and moves exactly one position
left or right. The halt label is separate and is not a working state.
Initialization counts. Sparse native labels such as 211 or 302 are names;
they do not indicate the number of states. A row
\[
 q\quad b\quad w\quad D\quad r
\]
means: in state $q$ scanning $b$, write $w$, move in direction $D$, and enter
$r$. Entry into the halt label halts after the write and move.

An acceptable additional saving needs more than a syntactically shorter
source program or a finite blank-tape run. The intended endpoint is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:endpoint}
 \PA\vdash\neg\Halt(M,\mathrm{blank})\ \longleftrightarrow\ \Con(\PA).
\end{equation}
A local reduction can establish this by proving, within PA, equivalence
with an already calibrated parent. A changed source program instead needs
a source-level soundness and completeness argument, the corresponding
literal machine implementation, and a valid blank initialization. The
distinction matters because source decoding and arithmetic operations share
physical states in these compact machines.

\subsection{Four evidence levels}

The archive distinguishes four levels of evidence:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Exact finite data}: table totality, target closure, state count,
renaming, hashes, and rooted graph isomorphism.
\item \emph{Finite proof certificates}: bounded predecessor invariants and
whole-tape local simulation identities, or inductive symbolic runtime
contracts with arbitrary natural-number parameters.
\item \emph{Inherited mathematical calibration}: enumeration of the native
calculus and its consistency equivalence with $\UU$ and PA.
\item \emph{Proof-assistant certification}: a final theorem checked in a
kernel against the exact table and exact theory specifications.
\end{enumerate}
The supplied U208 packets and this work provide evidence at the first three
levels, with different portions newly checked here. They do not export a
new end-to-end PA, Lean, Rocq, or MM0 proof object for a smaller U machine.

The cited K215 developments are a useful standard of comparison. The
ProveIt verification directory describes external Lean and Rocq endpoint
theorems for its exact K215 table; the separate \file{k215-pa} repository
describes an internal PA theorem in Metamath Zero. The m254 directory
provides a related Coq development for a different table and theory.
Those are valuable precedents, but their certificates do not automatically
apply when the searched theory or literal source program is changed
\cite{k215verify,k215pa,m254}.

\subsection{Why the conditional history matters}

Put the head at position 0. Two facts hold at every relevant old-machine
configuration reached from state 0, even on an arbitrary initial tape:
\begin{align}
 q=172&\ \Longrightarrow\ (t(-2),t(-1))=(1,0),\label{eq:172}\\
 q=211\ \wedge\ t(0)=0&\ \Longrightarrow\ t(-1)=1.\label{eq:211}
\end{align}
For \eqref{eq:172}, the only entry into 172 is $116/0$, and the only entry
into 116 is $215/1$. These two right moves write the predecessor word 10.
Neither state is initial.

For \eqref{eq:211}, the complete entry list is $163/0:0L211$ and
$163/1:1R211$. The second directly writes the required preceding 1.
In the first case, all entries to 163 are rightward:
\[
132/1:1R163,\qquad199/1:1R163,\qquad217/0:0R163.
\]
The first two would make the zero currently scanned by 211 equal to the
one just written, a contradiction. Thus entry was via $217/0$. The only
entry into 217 is $216/1:1R217$, which supplies the required left-hand 1.
The guard is conditional on the current bit; replacing it by an
unconditional single-cell fact loses the information that makes the fold
possible.

\subsection{Four finite paths}

Write $q_h$ for a control state and head coordinate. In the old $172/0$
case the known cells are $(-2,-1,0)=(1,0,0)$, and the child takes
\begin{align*}
211_0&\to217_{-1}\to163_0\to211_{-1}\to217_{-2}\\
 &\to215_{-1}\to157_0\to67_{-1}.
\end{align*}
In the old $211/0$ case, $(-1,0)=(1,0)$ and the child takes
\[
211_0\to217_{-1}\to215_0\to157_1.
\]
Both read-1 cases take the shared action to $184_1$. Every step in these
paths writes back the bit read, and no unknown exterior cell is inspected.
The entire tape, not just a window, is preserved. The endpoints are exactly
the projected old endpoints. Intermediate returns to 211 use its new
transition; they are not assumed to satisfy the old-state meaning.

All 414 other old rows commute with $\rho$. By induction on old steps,
each old run is simulated by positive blocks of length 1, 3, or 7.
For finite halting runs,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:time}
T_M=T_P+6N_P(172,0)+2N_P(211,0),\qquad T_P\leq T_M\leq7T_P.
\end{equation}
Halting reflection can be expressed without infinite objects: if the child
halts by time $S$ while the parent has not halted within $S$ steps, the
block induction constructs a nonhalting child prefix of length at least
$S$, a contradiction. This is a direct PA-formalizable finite argument.

The Class B proof is the corresponding relabeling of the local helpers.
It does not use an additional independent invariant or save another state.

\section{Which theory and enumerator the machines implement}\label{sec:logic}

\subsection{Unary weak covering}

The proposed theory $\UU$ uses an equality-free membership language. For
each formula $\varphi(x)$ with no other free variable, it contains
\begin{equation}\label{eq:U}
\forall a\,\exists b\left[
 \forall x\bigl(x\in b\to\varphi(x)\bigr)\ \land\
 \forall x\bigl(\Sub(x,a)\to(\varphi(x)\to x\in b)\bigr)\right],
\end{equation}
where $\Sub(x,a)$ abbreviates
$\forall z(z\in x\to z\in a)$. This is weak covering: the selected
subsets of $a$ must be included, and all members of $b$ satisfy the matrix.
It is not an unrestricted exact separation axiom with arbitrary parameters.
The distinction is central to the claimed minimality of the theory
\cite{unary}.

The unary archive gives an ordinary mathematical argument for proof-code
translations in both directions between the appropriate consistency
statements. The lower interpretation needs more than a sentence saying
``finite ordinals exist.'' In particular:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Equality is represented by equality of member profiles. Substitution
in the member position requires saturated containing objects; it is not
automatic from the definition of coextensionality.
\item Numerical representatives have internal order and predecessor
properties. Induction is established before successor totality, so that
successors are not silently assumed while proving them.
\item Parameter induction is obtained by using the genuinely unary matrix
$Q(v)=\forall\vec p(A_\theta(\vec p)\to\theta(v,\vec p))$, where
$A_\theta$ expresses the base and step premises.
\item Least failed numerical tuples are bound inside unary formulas to
obtain bounded numerical comprehension with its support condition. This
does not introduce a uniform truth predicate.
\item Successors, powers of numerical sources, pair codes, and canonical
graphs for addition and multiplication are built only after the required
induction and comprehension statements.
\end{enumerate}
The inspected proof supplies these dependencies. This audit found no
concrete parameter violation or circularity in those steps. It does not
replace them by a newly implemented general proof translator.

For the upper interpretation, use $x\in a$ as the bit predicate
$\operatorname{Bit}(a,x)$. Translated subsethood bounds the possible $x$;
formula-specific PA induction forms the required finite collection. This
is compatible with unbounded formula complexity because a separate PA
proof is produced for each fixed formula.

\subsection{The native calculus is not the positive dyadic alternative}

The actual U209/U208 source retains a four-tag numerical calculus. With
the constructor used by the code,
\[
P(x,y)=\frac{(x+y)(x+y+1)}2+y,
\]
its tags are implication $4P$, membership $4P+1$, quantification $4P+2$,
and auxiliary equality $4P+3$. Code 0 is a normalized closed truth alias;
target code 1 is diagonal membership, interpreted as false under global
irreflexivity. The pairing convention must be taken from the executable
constructor, rather than guessed from the phrase ``dual Cantor pairing.''

The separate positive dyadic stream in the unary archive uses different
tags and target code 0. It is an alternative design, not the backend of
these literal tables. A short expression in that encoding is consequently
not a measured physical state saving for U208.

The machine-facing cover emitter allows matrix parameters. Its calibration
runs through unrestricted weak covering $\WW$ with global irreflexivity
$\IR$, and then through the unary theory and arithmetic. The machine does
not execute a free-variable test enforcing the unary restriction in
\eqref{eq:U}. In the inherited role-reassigned emitter, matrix code/output
label is $a$, source label is $b$, and the tested label is $c$. The gates
$c\ne a$ and $c\ne b$ have a logical role; they cannot be discarded on the
assumption that every collision is a harmless alpha-renaming.

The admitted case $a=b$ is sound, but it supplies an empty-source cover,
not a general cover. The next section turns this observation into an
obstruction to one tempting global simplification.

\subsection{The interior startup and protected stack}

The compact initializer enters an interior axiom-emitter continuation.
Its stored arithmetic value 1 is not an assumed theorem of contradiction.
The retained source bridge takes 141 source steps from the specified
interior store to the canonical frame, and the suffix argument constructs
nine genuine theorem heads above an opaque numerical tail 4.

That tail would be unsafe if exposed: $4=P(1,1)$ and $1=P(1,0)$. Soundness
therefore depends on the scheduler's protected-tail argument. The inherited
conservative stack weights are $S=-1$, $MP=0$, and $AX=10$; the maximum
initial prefix debt is two, leaving margin seven above the unsafe tail.
A ghost list length handles aliases such as $P(0,0)=0$. The completeness
compiler works above arbitrary leftover stacks after the counter/cache
reset, and successful candidate codes are cofinal.

Any rearrangement of axiom builders must preserve the necessary head count,
scratch-register contracts, and the interior suffix proof, or replace them
explicitly. Ordinary register-machine extensional equivalence without
these boundary conditions is insufficient.

\section{A finite-model obstruction to identifying source and output}\label{sec:collision}

This section gives an additional mathematical result, distinct from the
already supplied gate-deletion and propositional-row counterexamples.
It rules out fixing the independent source role equal to the output role
throughout the unchanged cover mechanism.

Define $\Emp(x)$ by $\forall z\neg(z\in x)$. Let $\CC$ consist of global
irreflexivity and all instances, with arbitrary additional matrix
parameters, of
\begin{equation}\label{eq:C}
\exists b\,\forall x\left[
 (x\in b\to\varphi(x,\vec p))\ \land\
 (\Emp(x)\to(\varphi(x,\vec p)\to x\in b))\right],
\end{equation}
with the usual freshness of the bound output variable. These are the
empty-source covers obtained from the captured subset guard in the
collision case. Under $\IR$, the captured guard
$\forall b(b\in x\to b\in b)$ is equivalent to $\Emp(x)$.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:collision}
The fixed two-element structure with universe $\{e,u\}$ and sole
membership edge $e\in u$ satisfies every instance of \eqref{eq:C} and
global irreflexivity. PA proves $\Con(\CC)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both objects are irreflexive and $e$ is the only empty object. Fix any
formula and any assignment to its parameters. If $\varphi(e,\vec p)$ is
false, choose output $e$. The upper clause is vacuous, and no empty object
both satisfies the matrix and requires membership. If
$\varphi(e,\vec p)$ is true, choose output $u$. Its sole member is $e$,
so the upper clause holds, and it contains the unique empty object
required by the lower clause. The value of $\varphi(u,\vec p)$ does not
affect either argument.

Satisfaction of coded first-order formulas in this fixed finite structure
is primitive recursive. PA verifies the preceding witness choice for every
coded axiom instance and verifies soundness by induction on finite proofs.
It follows that PA proves that there is no $\CC$-proof of a contradiction.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}
If PA is consistent, this globally collapsed cover theory cannot have a
PA-provable consistency biconditional with PA.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Such a biconditional, together with Theorem~\ref{thm:collision}, would give
$\PA\vdash\Con(\PA)$, contrary to the second incompleteness theorem for
the usual arithmetized proof predicate.
\end{proof}

The structure fails genuine full covering already for the true matrix at
source $u$. Both $e$ and $u$ are subsets of $u$, so a full cover would have
to contain both; neither object does. Thus the loss of strength is visible
inside the two-element structure.

The executable certificate checks all four possible extensions of the
matrix on $\{e,u\}$ and the displayed failure of full covering. It is a
finite corroboration of the model. The theorem for arbitrarily complex
formulas comes from the semantic argument and proof induction, not from
mistaking four matrix extensions for an enumeration of formulas. This
obstruction leaves open different encodings, additional schemas, or a
different source representation.

\section{Exact exhaustion of an axiom-parameter permutation family}\label{sec:permutations}

There is a sound way to vary the arithmetic emitter without changing its
logical axiom set. In each of the seven complete unrestricted schema rows
$(G,\rho_6,\rho_5,\rho_4,\rho_3,\rho_2,\rho_1)$, independently permute
the three enumerated parameters $a,b,c$. A permutation is a bijection of
$\mathbb N^3$, so it preserves the set of instances emitted by that row.
It can nevertheless change the sharing available in a source decoder DAG.

The seven ordinal source regions, with half-open endpoints, are
\[
[478,887),[887,1170),[1170,1466),[1466,1765),
\]
\[
[1765,2061),[2061,2411),[2411,2642).
\]
For this experiment all source association and every primitive outside the
selector substitutions are fixed. The cost is the number of distinct
structural subtrees, including primitive leaves, in the complete expanded
source. It is \emph{not} the literal TM state count.

\begin{proposition}[Finite-family result]\label{prop:permutations}
Among all $6^7=279{,}936$ independent parameter permutations in the
specified fixed-association family, the minimum source DAG size is 197.
Exactly four choices attain that minimum.
\end{proposition}

The four minimizers are the original assignment; swapping $b,c$ in
$\rho_6$; simultaneously swapping $b,c$ in $\rho_4$ and $\rho_2$; and
performing both changes. This is an exact finite exhaustion, not a random
search or a coordinate-descent conclusion.

\subsection{Why the enumeration is small and auditable}

The checker interns primitive trees structurally. It separates 146 constant
nodes, 39 dynamic nodes whose sizes prevent collisions with all other
nodes, and 49 possibly colliding dynamic templates. In this instance each
contested template depends on at most one row's permutation. Its possible
values can be precomputed. Each full choice is then evaluated by a union
of finite bitsets, with 208 IDs in the lookup universe.

The key exactness condition is that every selector macro's interior already
occurs outside the editable rows. Adding their variants to the constant
part therefore does not overcount a node that might be absent for a
particular assignment. The checker verifies this condition and the
size/dependency partition. The retained run also cross-checked 343 full
tree expansions against the decomposition. The portable artifact exposes
the full histogram and all four minimizers.

\section{Searches for an additional physical deletion}\label{sec:search}

\subsection{Both outgoing rows may change}

The supplied discovery programs mainly exploit pairs with a compatible
row and modify the other row. We broadened the search to unordered pairs
of U208 states with \emph{both} keeper transitions freely synthesized.
After the main run, the initially excluded pairs involving start state 0
were also tested, always keeping label 0 as the projected start.

For each class the resulting census is
\[
\binom{208}{2}=21{,}528\text{ pairs},\qquad
35{,}650{,}368\text{ partial single-row action trials}.
\]
A candidate action chooses its written bit, direction, and any retained
working-state target, including the keeper. The removed state is projected
to the keeper in all other targets. The new halt label is not used as a
synthesized target in this search.

Old reachable contexts are overapproximated by disjunctive cylinders
obtained from depth-12 transition histories, with initial state 0 injected
at each stage. Keeping the cylinders separate preserves correlations
between the current bit and neighboring cells; taking only the intersection
of their fixed bits would lose precisely the information used by the
existing U208 fold. Each cylinder is given three deterministic pseudorandom
exterior completions. The candidate is required to reproduce one old step
within at most 71 new steps, with the same head and whole concrete tape at
the synchronization boundary and the projected control state.

The implementation prunes a partially specified row only after a failure
that occurs before the other keeper row is needed. It then screens every
surviving pair of rows against all retained cases. The same procedure on
the U209 parent recovers the supplied saving exactly:
\[
\text{delete }211,\quad\text{keep }172,\quad
172/0=0L217,\quad172/1=1R184.
\]
This positive control checks that the search can rediscover the important
conditional-history fold and is not vacuously rejecting every candidate.

No pair survived on either U208 class, including the 207 pairs involving
state 0. This is a reproducible negative result for the stated rejection
screen. The history abstraction includes configurations not established to
be reachable by the actual proof program; finite exterior completions are
only rejection tests; and the 71-step bound and one-old-step boundary are
substantial restrictions. The result is not a lower bound on machines for
$\Con(\PA)$, or even a proof that every semantically valid quotient of U208
has been excluded.

\subsection{Source-conditioned contexts and stronger register facts}

The universal runtime was instrumented to harvest local tape windows at
all primitive contracts. A radius-4 harvest produced 21,358 local windows;
compressed scan/list entries were initially widened conservatively.
The source-conditioned screening tested 42,642 directed aliases and
370,944 one-row mutations across 448 compatible oriented pairs, first with
step bound 31, then with depth-10 history intersection and bound 45.
No survivor was obtained. These contexts add source-specific restrictions;
they are not uniformly stronger than arbitrary initial-tape histories,
because some compressed loops were widened. Intersecting the two families
does strengthen the approximation. The same acceptance boundary still applies.

Further abstract interpretation used all 512 zero/positive patterns of
the nine primary registers, retaining correlations between registers.
The resulting source invariant contains 278,384 reachable abstract
configurations and strengthens 1,649 guarded branch contracts. A separate
interval analysis adds exact-small-value and lower-bound information.
These are inductive facts about source execution, not observations from
a finite native trace.

The spare registers supply another useful invariant. The exact startup has
43 spares of value 1, an increment appends a spare of value 1, and the
remaining primitive contracts preserve the spare list. Thus every spare
can be fixed to 1 throughout a source-reachable run. A generic runtime
contract allowing arbitrary spare values is stronger than necessary.
Restricting to this closed invariant can sharpen discovery without
restricting the unbounded primary-register search.

With this invariant, precise unary-scan interiors, and the interval facts,
the restricted runtime rederives 5,079 admissible contracts with 1,243,924
symbolic inferences and still covers all 416 rows. Its radius-4 context set
has 12,490 windows. Intersecting it with depth-12 history gives 51,279
disjunctive cylinders. The full two-row screen was repeated on all 21,528
Class A pairs using these stronger contexts and the same 71-step bound;
it again produced no survivor. Its 169 checkpointed parts sum to
35,650,368 partial action trials. This second run changes the reachable
context approximation, not the boundary relation or the finite-screen
acceptance status.

\subsection{A known attractive failure: grammar sharing without runtime preservation}

The supplied newer packets already diagnose the following apparent saving.
It was also encountered independently during this work and is included as
a regression example, not as a novelty claim. In Joint native notation,
\[
155=(85,150),\quad85=(98,12),\qquad
52=(154,150),\quad154=(166,12).
\]
Reassociate each $((A,B),C)$ into $(A,(B,C))$. This makes the two inner
states have children $(12,150)$, apparently allowing one to be deleted.
The primitive word and ordinal source control graph can be preserved.
The physical runtime nonetheless changes because it uses the same graph
through a backward entry into the decoder.

An exact seventeen-step prefix witness begins at dispatcher state 84,
head 0, left marker 1, and known instruction prefix
\file{0011001000001}. Exterior cells remain opaque. The original table
first crosses the end of that prefix in state 42 with head 13 and no tape
change. The reassociated table reaches state 25 with head 13 and changes
cell 12 from 1 to 0. The first twelve transitions agree. The hidden route
includes $76\to152\to159\to118\to155\to85$.

The portable witness checker does not import the symbolic engine and fails
on any unknown exterior read. It confirms a concrete failure of the claimed
prefix contract. It does not determine the rejected table's eventual blank
behavior. In particular, it must not be advertised as a 207-state candidate
merely because combining it with the existing fold produces a table with
that number of labels.

Closed-rotation searches and context-aware allocation attempts were also
performed. Their useful role is to generate candidates; a source control
graph check alone is never the final acceptance condition. The audit trail
retains negative examples so that this distinction is testable.

\section{What the research does and does not establish}

The exact comparison identifies two rooted graph classes at each state
count: four literal graphs in total, belonging to two source-layout
lineages. It tracks their labels and verifies the same physical saving in
the later packets. The final U208 runtime contracts
have been freshly rederived for both classes. The new finite-model theorem
rules out one natural logical simplification, and the exhaustive permutation
census gives a precise minimum for one substantial source family.

None of those statements proves that 208 is minimal. State counts are the
end of a chain of choices: axioms, formula coding, enumeration program,
source association, contextual allocation, tape representation, and local
machine reductions. A negative result at one level does not bound the
other levels. Nor does a broad heuristic search establish that an
unexamined configuration relation cannot save a state.

The search code therefore records assumptions, bounds, and positive controls
alongside outcomes. A generated table that lacks a proof of its intended
behavior is kept in rejected research data rather than silently promoted
to a consistency machine. The requested construction with at most 207
working states remains unresolved in this investigation.

\section{Further research questions}\label{sec:further}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Can source layout and helper behavior be synthesized together?}
The repeated grammar failure shows that a state should carry a family of
entry contracts, including backward arithmetic entries. An allocator could
optimize these contracts jointly with source association instead of first
optimizing a pure decoder and later discovering the conflict.
\item \textbf{Can the configuration relation change economically?}
Two machines need not synchronize after every old transition with identical
head coordinates and tape. Small fixed head offsets, temporary marker
changes, or synchronization at source boundaries may permit reductions
excluded by the present local search. The associated proof should establish
positive progress and halting reflection explicitly.
\item \textbf{Can the all-spares-one invariant replace a generic list engine?}
A proof specialized to fixed spare blocks might permit a shorter scanner
or delimiter mechanism while leaving the primary registers unbounded.
The whole initialization and append invariant must remain part of the
theorem.
\item \textbf{Can axiom-builder order and association outperform parameter
permutations?} The 279,936-case result fixes association. Reordering complete
builders preserves the emitted axiom set, but interacts with scratch-state
reuse and the protected-tail argument. A physical saving needs a measured
allocation and final runtime proof.
\item \textbf{Can the positive dyadic calculus be compiled completely?}
Its alternative syntax may simplify formula construction, but the full cost
includes proof enumeration, vacuity/freshness handling, substitution if used,
and blank initialization. Comparing only formula sizes is inconclusive.
\item \textbf{Can the U/PA calibration be turned into an explicit proof
translator?} A proof-code implementation would separate the mathematical
strength question from changes to the machine backend and would provide a
clear target for the MM0 or other small-kernel route.
\end{enumerate}
