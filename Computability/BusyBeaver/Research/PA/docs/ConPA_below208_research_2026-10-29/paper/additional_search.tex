One further family deletes a state with uniformly directed incoming moves
and flips those moves when redirecting them. The configuration relation
places the projected head two cells away at the deleted-state boundary.
Allowing an arbitrary single keeper-row mutation screened 36,639 ordered
pairs and 60,674,184 actions on each U208 class, with depth-12 contexts and
a 71-step bound; neither class produced a survivor.

A completed Class A block experiment permits both old and new machines
to take positive blocks, each of length at most 31. Deletions were restricted
to $\{163,211,217,215,157,184,67,148,186\}$; every distinct noninitial keeper
and one arbitrary keeper-row replacement were considered. Depth-9 guards
gave 1,854 ordered pairs and 3,070,224 assignments. Twenty finite-screen
survivors reached the symbolic stage; none was accepted within its 4,096
branch-processing budget per guard. This does not assert that every such
transformation is semantically invalid. Incomplete source-conditioned
extensions are marked as partial and excluded from completed censuses.

At the source level, all 5,040 orders of the seven logical builders were
also tested with a particular bounded association heuristic: five closed
slots, factor counts $(1,6,6,1,16)$, and beam width 1 within each slot.
The best produced DAG had 199 nodes, compared with the original 197.
This exhausts the orders under that heuristic, not the association space.
A tied 197-node parameter variant obtained a prefix-valid 208-state
allocation after an extra protection filter was relaxed, but its fresh
universal runtime check failed at source site 23, zero branch of decrement
register 3, after 31 successful cases. It is not an accepted new machine.
