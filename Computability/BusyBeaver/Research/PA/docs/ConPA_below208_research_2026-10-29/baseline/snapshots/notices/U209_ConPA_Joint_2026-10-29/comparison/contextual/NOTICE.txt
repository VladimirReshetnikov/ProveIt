Attribution and scope
=====================

This research continues Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt work on compact Turing
machines and proof search. The supplied U212 proposals, their numerical proof
calculus, source program, startup/calibration arguments, and Theory U manuscript
are explicitly retained and attributed. The new contribution in this packet is
the six-rotation layout, contextual entry repair, and predecessor-bit fold
yielding the specified U211 table, together with the new verification/report.

The inherited compiler lineage includes Andrew James Wade's Turing Machine
Explorer, Mira's compact proof-search machines, Jeff's busy-beaver development,
and the ProveIt source and verification adaptations. Elliot Glazer's K215/K230
work provides relevant independent context; its formal ZFC endpoint is not used
as a certificate for this changed PA machine.

code/runtime/symbolic_runtime.py, code/runtime/independent_runtime.py, and
code/source_language.py are retained byte-for-byte from the selected U212
packet's code/vendor directory (and its selected U213 parent). The new builder
and wrappers use those documented interfaces. Their inherited notices and
license texts are preserved in licenses/. The exact selected U212 archive and
the original unary-theory archive remain unchanged under baseline/.

No blanket relicensing of third-party or inherited sources is asserted.
Consult the retained notices for their applicable license scopes. The archive
does not claim that earlier external formal proofs verify U211, or that the
ordinary Python induction engine is a proof-assistant kernel.
