\section{The inherited PA calibration and its audit}
\label{sec:calibration-audit}

The new source instruction does not change the numerical calculus or its
command protocol. The ordinary calibration used here is the supplied
calibration of the separate-diagonal-head calculus and cached controller.
The exact PA conclusion is obtained by the following syntactic chain:
\[
 \operatorname{Ref}_{\mathrm{PA}}\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
 \operatorname{Ref}_{\mathcal C}\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
 H_{\mathrm{cached\ source}}\quad\longleftrightarrow\quad H_P.
                                                        \tag{F3}
\]
Here every equivalence is provable in PA. The accompanying reference
proofs and their provenance are retained; the following details specify
what their use entails and why a mere appeal to external equiconsistency
would not suffice.
The full ordinary mathematical argument is retained in
\texttt{references/complete\_cached\_calibration.tex}; its logical
dependencies are retained with the original diagonal and PA195
calibration, and the final source equivalence is detailed in
\texttt{references/proof\_symmetric.tex}. This section audits and
summarizes those supplied proofs; it does not claim an independently
implemented PA proof-code compiler or a new kernel-checked calibration.

The numerical formula constructors are
\[
\begin{split}
 p(a,b)&=\tfrac12(a+b)(a+b+1)+b,\\
 I(A,B)&=4p(A,B),\qquad M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\\
 U(a,B)&=4p(B,a)+2,\qquad E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\end{split}
\]
Code zero is truth, code one is the designated contradiction $M(0,0)$,
and $N(A)=I(A,1)$. The transposed arguments of $U$ and the alias
$I(0,0)=0$ are part of the specification. The separate head is
$\Gamma(B)=I(B,U(B,B))$; the guarded separation gate and the six other
heads are those in the retained calibration. The only inference rules
are modus ponens and generalization of theorems; the retained dominated
implicational schema supplies additional axioms. There is no implicit
arbitrary-tautology or truth oracle.


For completeness, write $A\to B$ for $I(A,B)$, and expand conjunction
and existential quantification using $N$ and $U$. The six universally
parameterized heads are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &(\exists x_a\,U(a,C))\to C,\\
 &U(a,U(b,C))\to U(b,U(a,C)),\\
 &M(c,a)\to(E(c,b)\to M(b,a)),\\
 &E(c,a)\to(M(b,c)\to M(b,a)),\\
 &\exists x_a E(c,a),\\
 &U(a,b)\to\bigl(U(a,b\to c)\to U(a,c)\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
The dominated schema is
\[
 ((c\to u)\to a)\to((a\to c)\to(b\to c)),\qquad u\le a.
\]
The old guarded gate $G(a,b,c)=N(V\to Q)$ is available when $c\ne a,b$,
with the following literal numerical terms:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 V&=M(a,b)\to U(c,M(a,b)),\\
 P&=c\to M(c,b),\\
 S&=U\bigl(b,(M(a,b)\to P)\wedge(M(c,b)\to c)\bigr),\\
 Q&=U(c,S\to N(P)).
\end{aligned}
\]
A failed guard emits truth. The separate diagonal head $\Gamma(c)$ is
emitted without the guard. The bundled predecessor emits
$N(\Gamma(c)\to(V\to Q))$ instead. Classical implicational proof templates
translate the bundled gate to both separate components and conversely;
an arbitrary $\Gamma(B)$ is obtained from the bundled gate at
$(a,b,c)=(B+1,B+1,B)$, whose guard always holds. These translations are
uniform on proof codes. Explosion and double-negation elimination in
these templates are derived rules of the actual numerical calculus:
explosion follows from the first head and the implicational $K$ theorem,
and double-negation elimination then follows from Peirce's law. Treating
code one as falsity is therefore supported by derivations, not silently
added as a new rule.

\paragraph{PA refutations to numerical refutations.}
The supplied PA196 proof-producing interpretation uses bidirectional
membership rewriting, a neighborhood-equality implication, and an equality
witness. The diagonal calculus recovers those same interfaces. For the
reverse rewrite
\[
 C=E(a,b)\to(\chi(b)\to\chi(a)),
 \qquad \varphi=N(C),\qquad c=\ulcorner\varphi\urcorner,
\]
the code $c$ is larger than every variable label occurring in $C$. The
old separation gate at $(a,a,c)$, direct membership rewriting and the
retained witness turn the hypothesis $\varphi$ into the gate's forbidden
quantified consequence. The available diagonal clause
$\Gamma(\varphi)$ supplies precisely the vacuity needed to generalize
that consequence at $c$. Double-negation elimination yields $C$.
Coincident labels are handled by the separate diagonal membership case.
The neighborhood interface is first obtained at the fresh label
$\ulcorner E(a,b)\urcorner$ and then transported using the witness and
membership-only vacuity. These are finite syntactic proof templates;
substitution in the existing refutation compiler is primitive recursive
and its correctness is proved by induction on the input proof. This is
the first direction in the left equivalence of (F3).

\paragraph{Numerical refutations to PA refutations.}
The converse is a syntactic interpretation into PA, not a reflection
argument. In a fixed conservative definitional extension for
exponentiation, interpret membership by the bit predicate
\[
 B(s,z)\equiv\exists u,v\,
       \bigl(s=(2u+1)2^z+v\ \wedge\ v<2^z\bigr),
\]
equality by equality, and $U(a,A)$ by universal quantification at the
corresponding source label. PA proves $B(s,z)\to z<s$, and therefore
refutes the translation of code one. For the zero alias use a fixed PA
proof of $\top\leftrightarrow(\top\to\top)$; constructor translation is
provable equivalence, not unconditional syntactic identity.

Given the syntax of any formula $\theta(z,\vec u)$, a fixed induction
proof pattern produces a PA proof of
\[
 \forall n\,\exists s<2^n\,\forall z<n\,
              \bigl(B(s,z)\leftrightarrow\theta(z,\vec u)\bigr).
\]
At the successor step keep the previous code or add $2^n$, according to
excluded middle for $\theta(n,\vec u)$. Substituting a formula into this
induction instance, choosing fresh auxiliary bound variables, and checking
the resulting finite proof are primitive-recursive syntactic operations.
PA verifies the proof-producing operation uniformly; no formula is
semantically evaluated by it.

For distinct source labels $a,b$, the separation witness uses the bounded
matrix
\[
          (B(x_a,z)\lor z=x_b)\wedge T(c)[z/x_b].
\]
When the labels $a=b$ coincide, the first membership antecedent under the
binder becomes $B(z,z)$ and is false. The correct witness instead uses
$(z=x_b)\wedge T(c)[z/x_b]$. This label-collision case cannot be treated as
two independent free values. Code freshness makes the output label absent
from $T(c)$, and auxiliary arithmetic bound variables are renamed to avoid
capture. The remaining heads have fixed first-order proof suppliers.
Recursion on a finite numerical proof, followed by the fixed PA refutation
of $B(x_0,x_0)$ and elimination of the definitional extension, produces
a PA refutation. PA verifies this transformation by induction on the
coded proof. This proves the other direction of the left equivalence
in (F3).

\paragraph{Refutations and command search.}
The cached controller maintains a top theorem $t$, a Cantor-encoded stack
$w$, an untyped offset $d$, and a cached theorem $h$. Shift halts exactly
when $h=1$; otherwise it transfers $h$ to the top and pops the next cache.
Successful modus ponens puts its consequent in $h$, clears $t,d$, and
leaves $w$ unchanged. The separate-head AX command pushes ten entries,
so its stack selectors use the corresponding ten-entry exponents.
The inherited proof compiler produces, from a numerical refutation, a
finite command word that halts from \emph{every} incoming store. This
last uniformity is essential because earlier search trials do not reset
the store. The exhaustive scheduler eventually executes that word unless
it has already halted.

In the reverse direction annotate $t$, $h$ and each live canonical stack
entry by a finite numerical derivation. AX appends axiom/generalization
proofs, successful MP appends an MP proof, and Shift moves annotated
entries or exposes a proof of one. For $w>0$ the Cantor tail is strictly
smaller than $w$, so the live stack is finite and its annotation is
primitive recursively bounded. The alias $p(0,0)=0$ is handled by a fixed
proof of truth on underflow; it does not require an infinite proof list.
The invariant is verified by induction on finite command executions.
Together with exhaustive scheduling, this is the middle equivalence
of (F3), uniformly on proof and computation codes.

\paragraph{The symmetric Shift.}
The old cached implementation restores a scratch copy of $h$ and compares
$(H,X)=(h,1)$. The supplied symmetric implementation compares $(1,h)$.
The destructive comparator halts exactly at equality and, on inequality,
clears both operands while preserving every other register. Consequently
both implementations have the same complete surviving store and the same
halt condition. The local argument also covers arbitrary incoming scratch:
the compared pair is $(h+x,1)$ or $(1,h+x)$. Its loop induction and finite
source binding prove the last equivalence of (F3) in PA.
