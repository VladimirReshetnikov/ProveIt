TRANSFER FROM ZFC AND CONCISE iPRA
================================

All links and claims refer to ProveIt revision
1e407ae841e4666f932777fad977ba784ebbcfd5.

1. The successful-MP zero push can be removed if the proof-word compiler
reserves the needed zero explicitly. The ZFC source is
Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/candidate-k215/alternatives/
zero-spare-transfer/report.tex, section "Removing the successful MP zero
push". That no-push alternative had 216 states; the report's main
zero-spare transfer machine had 215. Its Shift guard differs from PA's t+h=1. The PA194N proof therefore
establishes the nine-selector, Push, Pop and all-store compilation identities
for the PA guard. Our composition repeats that reasoning uniformly at
constructor coefficients 5 and 6. A same-state command change is useful
even when it does not by itself eliminate a working control.

The idea is inherited. The older PA packet
conpa_tm_comparison_and_reduction_search already proposed no-push MP at
196 states, and PA194N brings it to the 194-state physical architecture.
The new contribution here is the certified composition with the radix
controller, not invention of the no-push rule.

2. ZFC's K215 also benefits from a command-local field reader, command
ordering, a partial selector and compatible marker folds. The source
README explains that a complete source/decoder/initialization compilation
determines the state count. Those savings are interface-dependent. They
cannot be subtracted from a PA194 budget without a new reader and whole
source simulation.

3. ConciseIPRA searches complete proof checkers for theories bi-interpretable
with full first-order intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic.
Its native scalar core has iterator terms and formulas built from equality,
Q and D. One-term transport, no-literal-zero transport sources, direct
progress closure, and combined introduction rules are useful ideas for
reducing certificate branching and dispatch complexity. In particular,
Artifacts/one_sided_transport and Artifacts/source_dispatch recover an
excluded literal-zero source using a different term provably equal to zero.
Artifacts/evaluation_discharge combines evaluation and elimination through
a total functional Rho operation. Those are interface-equivalence techniques,
not direct state savings for the present PA source.

These are reductions of a different calculus. Replacing the PA calculus
by a checker for that core would produce an iPRA refutation search, not an
established PA refutation search. To transfer such a design one must first
supply a PA-strength calibration (and prove both directions), then compile
its actual source and account for constructors, initialization and readers.
A small number of proof rules or Python tokens is not a binary TM state
count. This package does not claim that missing transfer.

4. The concrete lesson for the next search is to optimize representation,
command interface and compatible reader reuse together. For these new
machines, adding one fixed controller makes eight folds compatible:
135 + 67 - 8 = 194. A superficially shorter emitter can lose one of those
folds. The tested diagonal/radix-six combination reached 195; creating the
desired extra helper displaced an old fold.
