pa_consistency_comparison_and_new196_2026-11-13.zip, member
pa_consistency_research/axioms/separate196/grammar.json, at the pinned ProveIt
snapshot. changes.tex and diagonal_prior.tex are preserved mathematical

../../comparison/reference_excerpts_hashes.json; the archive itself is pinned
