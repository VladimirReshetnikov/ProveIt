\section{Diagonal vacuity in the commutation or distribution head}
Write $\Gamma(B)=B\to U(B,B)$ and let $1$ denote the numerical false
code. Each of the following modifications is made separately. The bundled
gate is replaced by the old gate $\neg(V\to Q)$, and all other axiom heads
are retained.

\paragraph{A seriality lemma with no diagonal premise.}
The ordinary first head yields explosion by the unchanged construction in
\texttt{PA196/Logic/Deduction.lean}. The ordinary sixth head gives normal
quantifier distribution and lifting of theorem implications. For every $x$,
choose the retained equality witness $\exists x_x E(0,x)$, namely
$\neg U(x,\neg E(0,x))$. Lift the explosion instance
$1\to\neg E(0,x)$ through $U(x,-)$. Composition with the witness proves
\[
 S_x:\quad\neg U(x,1).
\]
This uses neither diagonal vacuity, general equality vacuity, reverse
membership rewriting, nor a satisfaction interpretation. Lifting $S_d$
through $U(a,-)$ and using $S_a$ also proves
\[
 \neg U(a,U(d,1)).
\]
These are uniform numerical proof templates.

\paragraph{Modification of the second head.}
Put $X=U(D,U(A,H))$ and $Y=U(A,U(D,H))$. Replace $X\to Y$ by
\[
 (\Gamma(A)\to X)\to Y.
\]
The positive implicational tautology
$((G\to X)\to Y)\to(X\to Y)$ is derived using only $K$ and composition
from the retained restricted propositional axiom. Hence the original second
head is recovered before any use of the seriality lemma. The first, fifth,
and sixth heads were never changed, so all premises of that lemma remain
available. Specialize the strengthened head to $H=1$ and, for example,
$D=0$. Its consequent $Y$ is refutable by the displayed double-quantifier
instance. The strengthened head therefore gives
$\neg(\Gamma(A)\to X)$, and classical propositional reasoning gives
$\Gamma(A)$. The parameter $A$ is arbitrary, so every diagonal instance
is recovered. Conversely, the original second head and $\Gamma(A)$ prove
the strengthened head by a fixed positive implicational argument.

\paragraph{Modification of the sixth head.}
Replace
$U(D,A)\to(U(D,A\to H)\to U(D,H))$ by
\[
 U(D,A)\to
 \bigl((\Gamma(D)\to U(D,A\to H))\to U(D,H)\bigr).
\]
Again recover the ordinary sixth head first, using only positive
implicational reasoning: under the two old antecedents, $K$ gives
$\Gamma(D)\to U(D,A\to H)$, to which the strengthened head applies.
This does not presuppose quantifier distribution. After that recovery,
normal quantifier distribution and the seriality lemma are available.

Take $A=0$ and $H=1$. Code $0$ is a theorem, since the implicational
identity at $0$ has code $I(0,0)=0$. Generalization gives $U(D,0)$.
The strengthened head now yields
\[
 (\Gamma(D)\to U(D,I(0,1)))\to U(D,1).
\]
Combine this with $S_D$ to obtain
$\neg(\Gamma(D)\to U(D,I(0,1)))$, hence $\Gamma(D)$. No simplification
of the numerical formula $I(0,1)$ is needed. The parameter $D$ is arbitrary.
Conversely, the old sixth head and $\Gamma(D)$ derive the strengthened
head by a fixed propositional proof.

\paragraph{Consequences and scope.}
For each separate variant, all six original heads and every diagonal
instance have now been recovered, and conversely the old diagonal calculus
proves the new head. Thus both proof translations are primitive recursive,
and PA verifies their correctness by induction on finite derivations.
The argument recovers each changed infrastructure rule before invoking it;
there is no circular appeal to explosion or quantifier distribution.

Each change inserts the seven-token diagonal expression and one implication
constructor into a head while deleting the same eight tokens from the gate.
The AX word therefore retains 191 tokens and the nine-entry pushed stack
interface. The selected strengthened head is an implication code and cannot
equal false code $1$. A new physical compilation still needs exact source,
runtime, startup, and selector/derivation binding checks. The logical result
alone is not a machine-state reduction.
