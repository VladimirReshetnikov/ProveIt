\section{Why a physical permutation cannot repair the published 193-state startup}

This is an analytic explanation of a failed repair, not a new empirical
claim beyond the earlier exhaustive permutation screen. It concerns precisely the published runtime core with SHA-256
\texttt{50f7be44afb63293365270a83111299247d12d140614e4b4c9a52786163acb53}.
Its native start is 74. It does not rule out a different start or different
bootstrap transitions.

Let $K=\{51,36,25,48,32,33,22,55,60\}$ be the nine register-scanning
controls. Permit an arbitrary permutation of the targets of all instruction
router edges into $K$, while retaining the scan-state rows and the other
machine rows. Then every resulting blank computation from control 74 is
provably nonhalting in PA, by an explicit finite prefix followed by an
outward ray. If PA is consistent, there can consequently be no PA-provable equivalence
between the nonhalting of such a machine and $\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA})$: that equivalence would make the latter sentence a PA theorem.

The first 78 transitions are independent of the permutation: they use no
instruction-router edge into $K$. At that point the configuration has
control 45, head coordinate 4, and one-cells $\{-3,1,4\}$. Transition
$(45,1)$ erases the rightmost one, moves right to coordinate 5, and enters
some control of $K$. All cells at and to the right of coordinate 5 are zero.
The zero transitions of the scanner chain lead to control 51, then the
modified $(51,0)$ transition enters 122. On zeros the remaining fixed
control sequence is
\[
122\longrightarrow137\longrightarrow30\longrightarrow45
\longrightarrow24\longrightarrow35.
\]
No further instruction-router edge is used. Control 35 is reached strictly
to the right of the remaining support $\{-3,1\}$.

The nine possible scanner-entry controls are exhaustively represented by
the complete traces in \texttt{start74\_permutation\_obstruction.json}.
At every endpoint, $(35,0)=(1,R,35)$. Induction on the number of further
steps shows that the head remains ahead of all previously written cells,
always reads zero, and never leaves control 35. The certificate is therefore
a proof of these particular infinite runs, not an inference from a timeout.

The script \texttt{prove\_start74\_obstruction.py} checks the source table
hash, every transition of the nine prefixes, the unique instruction-router
edge used, the complete finite support, and the final outward-ray condition.
