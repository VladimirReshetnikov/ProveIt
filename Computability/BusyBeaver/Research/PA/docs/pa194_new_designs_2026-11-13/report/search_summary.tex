No verified machine below 194 controls was found. The searches were frozen

\subsection{Decoder allocation and contextual quotients}

The main fitness jointly counts shared axiom readers and compatible
history-sensitive folds, subject to the source scanner constraints. We
seeded it from both existing 194-state associations, with either the
original or cached controller. Variations included shared-subtree
rotations, single-occurrence rotations, multiple rotations, bijections
of the three parameter roles within universally instantiated axiom heads,
and axiom-head ordering. No new logical transformation is needed for
exact-word rotations. A successful parameter/order variant would also
need a correctly rebound source and axiom-selection proof; a favorable
fitness value would not suffice.

Seven stochastic runs executed at least \textbf{24,295,755 mutation-proposal
slots}. This is not a count of distinct trees or evaluated machines:
proposals can repeat or be rejected before full scoring. Two runs completed
their stopping conditions; five were stopped at the recorded checkpoint,
and their lower counts are taken from the last complete logged cycle.
All retained best scores were 60 effective readers, hence 194 total
controls with the 134-control fixed part.

A deterministic neighborhood traversal expanded 5,000 distinct roots
and evaluated 1,052,340 neighboring rotations. It admitted grammars
with scanner penalty zero and effective-reader score at most 60.
There were 587 roots still queued at the cap, so this traversal was
\emph{not} exhaustive. A separate fixed-shape screen examined all 720
orders of six heads on each of the two baseline shapes, 1,440 templates
in total, without improvement.

pair-contraction rule. It uses abstract reachable 11-cell windows

rejoining within 150 transitions. The finite closure queues exhausted:

To avoid testing only the two archived endpoints, a further pipeline
generated 1,000 grammar files, representing 606 distinct raw tables.

\subsection{Whole-controller screening}

We separately collected symbolic local tape contexts from every source
outcome and the exact startup of primary PA194 and PA194C. The first
screens retained the arithmetic-box premises and considered the 171
other controls, with radius five and local budgets of 150--200 steps.
Neither supplied a contraction.

The final PA194C screen removed that arithmetic-state exclusion. It
replayed the independent literal-tape proof, collected contexts at all
194 controls, included the interiors skipped by unary sweeps, included
the generic arbitrary-context one-block proofs used for list sweeps,
and included the full blank startup. It recorded 18,976 distinct local
views. Both a direct redirection filter and a general common-first-action
pair-merger filter, with radius five and 300-step local budgets, returned
no candidate. The context collector deliberately overapproximates unknown
lengths for screening. Any surviving edit would still have required a
new complete physical certificate.

The portable programs, seeds and frozen discovery logs are under
\file{search/decoder/}; the controller collectors, local filters and
receipts are under \file{search/controller/}. Their JSON summaries
state exact scopes and interruption boundaries.

\subsection{Further source-level work}

One sound acceptance extension is to halt when the cached formula code
has residue one modulo four. Such a code is a membership atom $M(a,b)$.
A PA proof of its arithmetic translation yields a refutation by
substituting zero for its free variables, since PA refutes
$\operatorname{Bit}(0,0)$. Conversely, contradiction code one is included,
so the old completeness route remains available. A short source guard
can perform the residue test after the cache-copy loop. Its straightforward
physical implementation introduced new reader controls while the old
equality guard was heavily shared; no state reduction was obtained.

A second idea preserves the cached Shift interface exactly: after
clearing $T$ and transferring $H$ into both $T$ and $X$, the values are
$(T,H,X)=(h,0,h)$. Set $H=1$ and use the symmetric equality test against
$X$, avoiding restoration of $H$ and the old $X=1$ setup. This reduces
the source from 3,649 to 3,646 sites. An attempted direct reassociation
failed a universal runtime contract, but adding a reader and then applying
the new $41\mapsto330$ contextual fold succeeded. This is the accepted
PA194CS construction proved in Section~\ref{sec:symmetric194}.

A final targeted search explored the symmetric implementation. From its
195-state intermediate, the declared radius-five/300-step local rule
reached three tables, including two 194-state endpoints, and exhausted
the queue. Allowing arithmetic states as merge endpoints gave the same
count. Starting instead from its fully unfolded 202-state table and
allowing every working control except the start, the search explored
576 tables, including four 194-state endpoints, and exhausted the queue.
The original seven AX folds were rebound to that raw parent. No 193-state
table was found. These separate records are under
\file{search/symmetric_shift/}; the earlier frozen search counts are
unchanged.

The successful fallbacks are PA194C and PA194CS. The failed searches do
not imply that their count is optimal, and none is used as a premise of
their proofs.
