This research starts from Vladimir Reshetnikov and the ProveIt contributors'
PA and ZFC machine collections at commit
317b07268681b44ff352d173a162e2a8c241b2fe.

James Chang supplied the imported PA196/PA209 numerical-calculus and
machine development underlying the calibration and compiler. Andrew James
Wade's unary-power and source-compiler work is also part of the retained
lineage. Supplied research packets provide the Q premise-exchange emitter,
N no-push command compiler, symmetric comparison, source decoder,
physical symbolic engines, and historical census.

The new contribution is the concrete QNS/QS composition, its exact
source-preservation argument and bindings, current-corpus comparison,
additional bounded searches, and the total nested binary-code proposal.
No global priority claim for the component techniques is made.

Original license notices are retained in licenses/, verification/reference/
and research/additional_reductions/. PROVENANCE.json records copied sources.
