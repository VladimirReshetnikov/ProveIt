\section{Results and exact conventions}

All three outputs are deterministic, single-tape machines over
$\{0,1\}$, with a two-way infinite tape, one-cell left or right moves,
and an initially all-zero tape. Their dense initial state is 0 and
initial head coordinate is 0. Working states are $0,\ldots,193$;
state 194 is the separate halt label and has no outgoing transition.
Thus each table has precisely 388 transition rows. Every working state
is reachable from the start in the directed control graph. This graph
property does not assert that every state is visited on the blank run.

The labels \R, \B, and \X\ below abbreviate the three delivered names.
The principal logical parent is the exact revised PA194AX11H table,
whose dense SHA-256 is
\begin{center}\small
\file{c5dfe1026d077d3e062eca11edb789597bfbbced4f6040f8828ae5d467b1167a}.
\end{center}
This is distinct from the repository's primary PA194 table, whose hash
begins \file{4bfea62b17aad7d8}. Keeping those two parents separate is
essential when reading the repository's formalization status.

The main new mathematical device is a \emph{joint decoder forest}:
several closed source sequences are associated together, their equal
subtrees are shared, and contextual folds are calculated on the complete
result. This differs from searching the axiom-emitter tree with every
other control frozen. Machine \X\ makes that distinction concrete: its
eighth fold joins a fixed runtime control with a shared reader.

The three final tables are mutually distinct even under a deliberately
broad graph comparison allowing arbitrary spanning roots, control
renaming, tape reflection, global symbol exchange, and ignored actions
on transitions entering halt. Global symbol exchange is used solely
to strengthen the cosmetic-novelty test; it is not asserted to preserve
the specified zero-blank start convention.

\subsection{The independent design choices}

Most of the apparent proliferation comes from combinations of a much
smaller collection of mechanisms. Constructor coefficients 4, 5, and 6
change formula coding. Q variants exchange the two curried distribution
premises. D and GD variants move the diagonal instance or expose a
separate generalization binder. The H5X families change an axiom's
witness consequent, with reverse specialization recovering the old
head. Each such change needs explicit numerical proof translations;
a shorter formula is not a state-count argument.

The command layer distinguishes ordinary dummy-zero MP, visible
no-push MP, and cached MP. Shift may test $t+h=1$ or only $h=1$;
symmetric comparators exchange the destructive comparison operands.
These choices affect the all-store proof compiler. In particular,
cache-only detection cannot be justified by silently retaining the old
final Shift: the revised AX11H compiler uses a final Pop. Cantor stack
zero is an alias, $p(0,0)=0$, so an argument assuming every syntactic
push creates a distinct live cell is invalid.

At the physical layer, register permutations, known-zero source
returns, decoder reassociations, history selectors, marker separation,
and three-to-one shortcuts are largely independent choices. Their
savings do not automatically add: removing a reader can require a new
selector for its second entry, while changing an increment loop can
remove or create a cross-routine sharing opportunity.

\section{Constructing the joint decoder forest}
\label{sec:forest}

\subsection{Three closed source sequences}

Use $(a,b)$ for a binary source node. Its ordinary reader preserves
the scanned bit and moves right, entering the left or right child
according to that bit. The source leaves carry primitive register
instructions and counted returns; their finite addresses determine
the concrete program-counter encoding.

For the operand routine define
\[
 A=\code{node32},\quad B=\code{node53},\quad
 C=\code{node55},\quad D=\code{node65}.
\]
The inherited operand tree is $((A,(B,C)),D)$. Machines \R\ and \X\
use $(A,(B,(C,D)))$; machine \B\ uses $((A,B),(C,D))$.
Their exact prefix codes are:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcccc}
\toprule
Operand & Parent & \R & \B & \X\\\midrule
$A$ & 00 & 0 & 00 & 0\\
$B$ & 010 & 10 & 01 & 10\\
$C$ & 011 & 110 & 10 & 110\\
$D$ & 1 & 111 & 11 & 111\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

For the MP sequence put $P=\code{node75}$, $Q=\code{node37}$,
$V=\code{node6}$, and $W=\code{node54}$. The inherited tree is
$((P,Q),(V,W))$. Machine \R\ uses $((P,(Q,V)),W)$; \B\ and \X\
use $(P,(Q,(V,W)))$. Their left-to-right closed block sequence is
unchanged, but the nesting changes the addresses of all primitive
instructions in those blocks.

Finally let $\J$ increment logical register 1, and let $\cc_k$ denote
the source leaf \code{continueTo} with argument $k$. The inherited
loop is
\[
 (((\J,\J),(\J,\J)),\cc_2).
\]
The three new loop trees are
\begin{align*}
 \R,\B &: ((\J,\J),((\J,\J),\cc_3)),\\
 \X &: (\J,((\J,(\J,\J)),\cc_3)).
\end{align*}
Changing the return count is necessary: its new path has one extra
right ancestor. There are 61 affected counted-return operands in the
expanded source. Their resolved target indices, and every other
resolved primitive instruction, agree exactly with the parent.
The construction proves this finite array equality instead of assuming
that arbitrary tree rotation preserves counted jumps.

\subsection{Sharing and contextual folds}

The builder starts from the literal AX11H table, replaces the three
closed routines, and interns equal binary decoder subtrees. It also
reuses any retained control whose two literal rows match a requested
reader. In particular, the new $(C,D)$ reader is shared with the AX
emitter. The AX grammar is then rebuilt against this enlarged library.
Controls made structurally unreachable are removed before counting.

A contextual fold uses the last bit written to the cell immediately
left of the head. If all incoming transitions to control $a$ preserve
0 and move right, and all incoming transitions to control $b$ preserve
1 and move right, the two controls can sometimes share an implementation
which reads that history bit. It is not enough to match one row or one
sample tape. For every selected pair and both current symbols, the
builder checks a complete local tape identity on the final simultaneous
folded table. All incoming edges are checked, including edges outside
the AX subtree; a hidden left-moving entry invalidates the premise.

Machines \R\ and \B\ use seven such folds. Machine \X\ uses eight;
the additional pair is native $41\to99$. In its raw table all incoming
edges to 41 are preserving 0/right edges, and all incoming edges to 99
are preserving 1/right edges. Its new loop exposes that distinction.
The folded action at 99 is checked for both origins and both scanned
symbols, together with the seven AX folds. Thus the extra fold is a
real cross-routine opportunity, although it compensates for the extra
fixed decoder control rather than yet lowering the final total.

\subsection{Preserving the hidden operand entry}

The original operand reader also serves a history selector. Its
incoming row $(69,0)$ moves left to native 213. The new forest retains
a separate history control 92:
\[
 \delta(69,0)=0L92,\qquad
 \delta(92,0)=0R111,\qquad
 \delta(92,1)=1R90,
\]
with the unchanged $\delta(111,0)=0R104$. If control 69 scans 0 at
coordinate $h$, and the history bit at $h-1$ is 0, the old and new
paths each take three steps, preserve both zero cells, and finish at
control 104 with head $h+1$. If the history bit is 1, they each take
two steps, preserve both cells, and finish at 90 with head $h$.
Neither path reads an exterior cell. This is a uniform two-cell
identity, not a blank-run observation.

Deleting this control would produce an attractive 193-state count but
would discard a real source behavior. The retained history entry is
why the stated budgets charge 135 or 136 fixed controls rather than
quietly retaining the old 134-control constant.

The search enumerated five operand associations, five MP associations,
and ten loop associations whose last return has a supported depth:
250 complete compiler families. Seventy have a calculated final cost
of 194. Sixty-three pass all construction-level source outcomes and
complete startup checks. Seven cost-194 proposals fail those checks
and are not promoted to machines with the required property.

The exact graph comparison finds 62 new, mutually distinct passing

\section{Independent verification of the literal machines}
\label{sec:physical}

The construction executor uses inherited register-block interfaces
to search efficiently. A separate verifier imports neither that compiler
nor its arithmetic-block oracle. It reads every literal transition,
rederives four generic list-traversal rules from those transitions,
and verifies every primitive source outcome using literal steps,
unary self-loops, finite-word normalization, and those derived rules.

For each machine it checks all 3,671 source sites and all 4,778
outcomes, including both branches of every decrement. The symbolic
state quantifies over nine arbitrary natural register values, an
arbitrary nonnegative header gap, and an arbitrary nonempty finite
spare list. Thus these certificates cover arbitrary values; they are
not tests up to a chosen numerical register bound. The proof of the
symbolic rules' arithmetic meaning remains a mathematical argument,
not a Python-to-Lean trust claim.

The verifier also independently resolves the source by arithmetic and
by interval methods, checks their agreement with the emitted instruction
array, and checks the dense/native conjugacy row by row. All nonhalting
source endpoints have zero head displacement and the correct padded
next address. No source address exceeds length 37, below the 48-cell
header capacity. The sole halt outcome has duration exactly three.

Initialization is checked separately by literal execution from a wholly
blank tape. The complete final finite array, not merely its live-register
projection, must equal the intended frame; all exterior cells remain
zero. The required startup lengths are 57,922, 58,082, and 58,180 for
\R, \B, and \X, respectively. The source begins at site 25 with
logical register vector $(2,0,\ldots,0)$, zero slack, and 26 spare
blocks of value 1.

\section{A uniform relative theorem in iPRA}
\label{sec:relative}

This section supplies the new weak-arithmetic argument. Let $M_0$
be the exact AX11H parent, and let $M_j$ be any delivered machine.
The finite data used here are bound to their exact table and contract
hashes by \file{proof/check_clocks.py}. The interpretation of the event
calculus is the supplied AX11H physical audit \cite{calibration}; the
steps needed in this instance are spelled out below.

\subsection{Common source dynamics and complete frame encodings}

A live source state is
\[
 z=(i,R_0,\ldots,R_8,n,Q),\qquad Q=[s]X\ne[].
\]
Here $i$ is a source-site index, $n$ is the header slack, and $Q$ is
the spare list. There is also a distinguished absorbing state $H$.
The common primitive instruction array defines a primitive recursive
transition $F$. An increment appends 1 to $Q$; a positive decrement
adds one to $n$; other live instructions preserve this bookkeeping.
The halt instruction returns $H$, and $F(H)=H$. Source validity is
a primitive recursive Boolean predicate and is preserved by $F$.

Let $p_j(i)$ be the finite address lookup for machine $j$, and put
\[
 \pi=(6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1),\qquad V_{\pi(a)}=R_a,
 \qquad B(v)=0\,1^{v+1}.
\]
The complete boundary encoding $E_j(z)$ has native state 84,
head coordinate $-2$, outward left stream $1\,0^\infty$, and right
stream
\[
 p_j(i)\,0^{48+n-|p_j(i)|}\,1\,
 B(V_0)\cdots B(V_8) B(s)B(X)\,0^\infty.
 \tag{1}\label{eq:frame}
\]
Set $E_j(H)=H$. Every operation in this encoding is primitive recursive.
The source tree itself need not be interpreted inside the run: $p_j$
and the instruction array are fixed finite lookups.

Encode finite-support tapes by two natural-number binary halves $L,R$,
where the low bit of $R$ is scanned. A write $w$ followed by a right
move sends these halves to $(2L+w,\lfloor R/2\rfloor)$. A left move
sends them to
\[
 (\lfloor L/2\rfloor,
    (L\bmod2)+2w+4\lfloor R/2\rfloor).
\]
On entering the halt label, canonicalize to $H$ and keep it fixed.
This preserves the literal halting predicate. The primitive recursive
iterate obeys
\[
 \Run_j(u+v,c)=\Run_j(v,\Run_j(u,c))
 \tag{2}\label{eq:semigroup}
\]
by quantifier-free induction. The same induction proves halt absorption.

\subsection{Uniform meaning of the outcome certificates}

Every outcome has an exact nonnegative 13-coefficient duration vector,
evaluated on
\[
 (1,n,x_0,\ldots,x_8,s,W(X)),\qquad
 W(X)=\sum_{v\in X}(v+2).
 \tag{3}\label{eq:valuation}
\]
For a positive decrement of register $a$, use $R_a=x_a+1$; all
other register slots have their stated ordinary values or zero tests.
The last slot is the width of the actual list $X$ in the frame,
not an unrelated free numerical parameter. Select the appropriate
duration $D_j(z)$ by the finite source site and its decidable branch;
set $D_j(H)=0$.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform local simulation]
For each parent or delivered table, iPRA proves, for every valid $z$,
\[
 \Run_j(D_j(z),E_j(z))=E_j(F(z)),
 \qquad z\ne H\ \Longrightarrow\ D_j(z)\ge3.
 \tag{4}\label{eq:local}
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Each literal event is substitution into the arithmetic tape-step
equations. A unary self-loop over a block of length $a$ is proved
correct, including its duration and unchanged exterior, by
quantifier-free induction on the processed length. Each of the four
list-traversal rules is first checked on the displayed literal rows
for one block $B(v)$ or its intermediate form, then extended to a
coded list by induction on the processed prefix. The duration sums
to $W(X)$. The normalization identities, such as
$C(X)1=1B(X)$ for $C(v)=10\,1^v$, have the same finite-prefix proof.

Composing these equalities along an accepted symbolic trace using
\eqref{eq:semigroup} proves its whole-tape endpoint and exact affine
duration. All variables remain free parameters of the quantifier-free
equalities or bounded Boolean checks; no unbounded assertion over all
environments is inserted into an induction formula. The source-endpoint
inspector verifies that the computed next address and target lookup
give equal 48-cell zero-padded words. Appending the common slack
therefore closes frame~\eqref{eq:frame}. It also verifies zero head
motion, the complete outcome index, nonnegative coefficients, and
constant term at least 3. The canonical halt case is separately the
identity at duration zero. These are finitely many checks for the
exact literal tables, with the uniform rule arguments just given.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exact clocks and a shared polynomial bound}

Let
\[
 z_*=(25,2,0,\ldots,0,0,[1]^{26}),\qquad z_k=F^k(z_*).
\]
The complete startup equalities are
\[
 \Run_j(T_j,\mathrm{blank})=E_j(z_*),
 \tag{5}\label{eq:startup}
\]
where $T_0=T_{\R}=57922$, $T_{\B}=58082$, and $T_{\X}=58180$.
These are closed finite arithmetic equalities established by the
literal startup transcripts. Define the primitive recursive clocks
\[
 \tau_j(0)=0,\qquad
 \tau_j(k+1)=\tau_j(k)+D_j(z_k).
 \tag{6}\label{eq:clock}
\]
Quantifier-free induction, including the Boolean validity invariant,
proves
\[
 \Run_j(T_j+\tau_j(k),\mathrm{blank})=E_j(z_k).
 \tag{7}\label{eq:composition}
\]
This equation stays exact after source halt, since both sides are
absorbing and subsequent durations are zero.

For all four tables the componentwise maximum of the outcome duration
vectors is exactly
\[
 (534,8,4,4,4,4,4,4,2,4,4,2,2).
 \tag{8}\label{eq:coefficients}
\]
Since $s+W(X)\le W(Q)$ and a positive decrement parameter is at most
its register value, every live state satisfies
\[
 D_j(z)\le534+8n+4\sum_a R_a+2W(Q).
\]
Along the run from $z_*$, quantifier-free induction yields
\[
 n_k\le k,\qquad \sum_a R_{a,k}\le2+k,
 \qquad W(Q_k)\le78+3k.
\]
Each primitive instruction changes at most one register by one;
only a positive decrement changes the slack; an increment appends
one width-three spare block. At $H$, define these auxiliary selectors
to be zero. Thus
\[
 D_j(z_k)\le698+18k,\qquad
 \tau_j(k)\le9k^2+689k.
 \tag{9}\label{eq:bound}
\]
Put $P_j(k)=T_j+9k^2+689k$. These polynomial times are sufficient
upper bounds; the exact clocks remain $T_j+\tau_j(k)$.

\subsection{Reflecting halts constructively}

The needed lower clock bound is conditional on the source still being
live:
\[
 z_k\ne H\ \Longrightarrow\ \tau_j(k)\ge3k.
 \tag{10}\label{eq:liveclock}
\]
Its successor step uses absorption of $F$: if $z_{k+1}\ne H$, then
$z_k\ne H$ and the next duration is at least three. It would be
incorrect to assert positive durations or cofinality after halt.

Suppose $M_j$ has halted by physical time $t$, and put $k=t+1$.
If $z_k\ne H$, then \eqref{eq:liveclock} makes
$T_j+\tau_j(k)>t$. Absorption says the left side of
\eqref{eq:composition} is halted, while its right side is a live
frame at state 84. This contradiction, together with decidability
of equality with the source halt code, gives $z_{t+1}=H$ in
intuitionistic logic. Conversely, $z_k=H$ gives a physical halt by
$P_j(k)$ from \eqref{eq:composition} and \eqref{eq:bound}.

\begin{theorem}[Explicit iPRA witness transport]
\label{thm:witness}
Let $h_j(t)$ assert that literal machine $M_j$ has halted by time $t$
from blank tape. For any parent or delivered pair $i,j$,
\[
 \ipra\vdash h_i(t)\ \longrightarrow\ h_j(P_j(t+1)).
 \tag{11}\label{eq:witness}
\]
Consequently iPRA proves their blank-tape halting equivalence and
their blank-tape nonhalting equivalence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The preceding argument transforms a physical halting witness $t$ into
the common source witness $t+1$, then into the physical witness
$P_j(t+1)$. These are explicit primitive recursive functions, not
an unbounded search for a next boundary. Existential introduction
and elimination give the positive equivalence. To transport
nonhalting, assume a halting witness on the other side, apply
\eqref{eq:witness}, and contradict the assumed nonhalting.
Neither Markov's principle nor double-negation elimination for an
existential statement is used.
\end{proof}

\section{The absolute PA consistency theorem and its dependencies}
\label{sec:calibration}

The supplied revised AX11H manuscript states and proves the ordinary
written theorem
\[
 \ipra\vdash\Con(\PA)\longleftrightarrow
           \neg\Halt_{\mathrm{blank}}(M_0).
 \tag{12}\label{eq:parent}
\]
It is the v2 packet filed at
\file{PA/docs/pa194_ax11h_research_2026-11-14_2/}, not the earlier
partial calibration. Its ten proof components, totaling 218,296
bytes, were read and matched against both the pinned repository and
the identical nested copy in the ORH incoming archive.
The complete unchanged package is included as
\file{reference/calibration_AX11H.zip}; its SHA-256 is
\begin{center}\small
\file{93ee082ef035b2d32e71d6af5239b25b4bea1773f03bea4d2c39810a7fe15075}.
\end{center}

This dependency supplies substantive mathematical content. The
command proof gives the actual four-operand, eleven-entry AX effect,
no-push MP and cache-only Shift; it handles arbitrary carried stacks
and the zero alias. Its all-store compiler invariant is bounded by
an explicit primitive recursive envelope rather than treated as an
unbounded universal induction formula. The physical and
source/command layers give exact primitive recursive clocks.

The logical compilation layers give finite-demand request trees,
strictly decreasing ranks within each stratum, and an acyclic order
of strata. Enlarged formulas arising from substitution are handled
by already constructed lower-stratum routines. They provide term
totality, coherence and uniqueness, formula realization, arbitrary
induction-axiom suppliers, and an outer proof compiler. The current
Foundation PA predicate is adapted explicitly to the ordinary PAeq
intermediate language; the source falsity code and numerical
contradiction code are not conflated.

For the weak-base step the manuscript applies the existing precise
one-way iPRA transfer endpoints to implications in candidate-PRA
models whose arithmetic reduct satisfies $I\Sigma_1$. It does not
assert that every $I\Sigma_1$ theorem is an iPRA theorem. The halt
matrix used here is an atomic primitive recursive equality, so it
meets the open/bounded hypothesis. The exact current
\file{ConPAISigma1Directions.lean} source was checked as part of the
audit. The final theorem is in the inherited
\file{proof/consistency_equivalence.tex}.

\begin{theorem}[Consistency calibration of the delivered machines]
For each of the three delivered machines $M$,
\[
 \ipra\vdash
 \neg\Halt_{\mathrm{blank}}(M)\longleftrightarrow\Con(\PA).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose the nonhalting equivalence of Theorem~\ref{thm:witness}
with the inherited written theorem~\eqref{eq:parent}. More explicitly,
a new-machine halting witness is sent to the parent by
$t\mapsto57922+9(t+1)^2+689(t+1)$, and a parent witness is sent
to the chosen new machine by the corresponding $P_j(t+1)$.
The two negated implications then compose intuitionistically with
\eqref{eq:parent}. No assumption of PA consistency is added.
\end{proof}

The numerical consistency sentence here is exactly the mapped
Foundation PA-refutation predicate used in the inherited calibration.
The included audit found no concrete fatal gap or circular use of
the desired calibration. Its verification scope is nevertheless
explicit: it inspected the dependency structure and representative
fixed proof recipes but did not independently normalize every large
native proof list or run the transitive Lean development.

The current repository correctly reports that the machine-specific
Lean composition is unfinished. That does not erase the separate
written mathematical proof, and the written proof does not complete
the Lean project. The Python certificates supply exact finite
bindings; the induction arguments supply their arithmetic meaning;
the inherited calibration supplies the PA proof translation. These
are three distinct parts of the result.

\section{Attempts to cross below 194 states}
\label{sec:search}

The search prioritized genuine state reductions before selecting the
fallback representatives. Negative results below are bounded search
results, not lower bounds. Their scripts, frozen inputs and receipts
are retained under \file{research/}.

The original physical table was tested with inherited-action control
merges, arbitrary remaining-row actions when a pair shares one row,
and sole-predecessor bypasses. Separate experiments imposed all
source endpoints instead of requiring the old intermediate control
to be revisited, allowing a larger family of possible reductions.
The ORH descendant received an independent repeat: 680 inherited
history-control fusions, 110,396 one-free-row pair trials, and 67,164
single-ingress bypass trials produced no passing 193-state table.

For the primary PA194 table, the whole-instruction search tested
57,460 inherited-row merges, 111,168 one-free-row merges and 67,936
sole-ingress bypasses. Repeating these with an independently checked
source interval invariant gave no improvement. For \X, the corresponding
counts were 57,460, 104,992 and 67,936. These symbolic searches use a
1,500-iteration limit; failure to close within that limit is not a
general impossibility result.

The known 193-state initialized runtime was also revisited. Its
initializer and runtime are coupled: changing the row needed by
startup can break the merged runtime history path. A broader set of
source-preserving transition rewrites was enumerated, then combined
in bounded blank-start searches. The resulting millions of edit
combinations produced no certified initialized 193-state machine.
Outward rays, exact cycles and fuel cutoffs are recorded separately;
a cutoff is not a proof of nonhalting or failure.

The enlarged pools contain 418 individually source-preserving row
rewrites for the primary core and 407 for the \X\ core. Completed
combined searches are summarized below. These count computations,
not distinct machines; the domains overlap. None reached a complete
source frame. The frame test allowed sites 25--44, arbitrary working
registers, arbitrary translation, padding at least 48 and a nonempty
spare list. Any hit would still require simultaneous source correctness
and a justified initial source configuration.

\subsection{What the other requested projects contribute}

The ZFC work contributes the successful practice of combining
source-loop transformations with partial history selectors, and the
warning that reductions sharing the same source roles cannot be
counted twice \cite{zfc}. The present joint forest is a concrete
application: the full literal machine, its initializer, and every
source outcome decide whether sharing is valid.

The DiophantineConsistency work emphasizes preserving a shared
arithmetic DAG rather than charging a formula only as an expanded
tree, and distinguishes closed consistency endpoints from compact
but uncalibrated source circuits \cite{diophantine}. Here that insight
motivates sharing across several decoder routines. A low-degree
equation with many witnesses does not itself yield a small blank-tape
enumerator: its fixed coefficients, enumeration and arithmetic
interpreter would all need to be charged and calibrated.

ConciseIPRA supplies small complete logical presentations,
with explicit treatments of substitution, contexts and derived
logical rules \cite{concise}. Its proof-checker byte metric is not
the TM working-state metric, and a checker for iPRA consistency
would not automatically be a checker for PA consistency. No
sub-194 compiler from that calculus is asserted here. Its useful
lesson for this task is to state the full checker and proof
translation, including logical rules, instead of treating a short
axiom list as the whole implementation.

\section{Reproduction and artifact guide}

The package is self-contained for rebuilding and checking the three
new machines. It requires Python 3.10 or later and its standard
library; run without \code{-O}. No repository checkout, network
access, search heuristic, external tape input, or proof assistant
is required for the construction and certificate replays.

\begin{verbatim}
python3 -B verify_all.py
\end{verbatim}

The release verifier reconstructs all tables in a fresh temporary
directory, compares exact tables and source artifacts, performs the
independent literal checks, verifies the common source graph and
polynomial-clock coefficients, and checks novelty. Its full replay
takes substantially longer than the few-second construction because
it rederives the universal literal contracts. The verification
receipt states exactly which checks ran.

For selected checks:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -B construction/build.py --output rebuilt
python3 -B verification/check.py rebuilt/PA194ForestB
python3 -B proof/check_clocks.py
python3 -B comparison/check_novelty.py --recompute
\end{verbatim}

The comparison directory contains exact graph representatives,
the 104-design named roster, 43 mechanism groups, all source
locators and exclusions, and the six incoming archive identities.
Recreating the complete source census requires the pinned upstream
revision; validating the new graphs against the frozen frontier
does not.

The report can be rebuilt from its directory with
\begin{verbatim}
python3 generate.py
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
\end{verbatim}
All 1,164 class identifiers and every delivered table are bound in
the package manifest. Search receipts do not certify minimality,
and no file marked rejected or diagnostic is a proposed smaller
consistency machine.
