\begin{abstract}
No justified blank-start PA-consistency machine with fewer than 194 working
states was obtained. The positive result is two new 194-state designs,
$\Mone$ and $\Mtwo$. The first combines two changes to the binary source
address tree, with the necessary repair of a shared history selector. The
second also changes a loop continuation, a second selector, and three local
runtime paths. Their complete resolved register programs are exactly the
program of the revised $\Mbase$ calibration, although 154 and 449 source
addresses, respectively, differ. Each table passes a fresh literal symbolic
audit of all 4,778 source outcomes for arbitrary natural register values,
and a complete blank initialization check. We give primitive recursive
halting-witness transformations and a written relative proof in $\ipra$.
The nonhalting/consistency corollary imports the identified revised AX11H
written mathematical calibration; it is not a newly completed Lean theorem.

The comparison covers 100 filed packets and six current incoming archives
at one pinned repository revision. A fresh explicit-table census finds 548
spanning 194-state graph classes, including research auxiliaries. Both new
tables are distinct from that corpus and from each other under the enlarged
structural comparison specified below. Reproducible unsuccessful reduction
searches and their exact limitations are retained.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, conventions, and evidence}
\label{sec:result}

The requested strict reduction below 194 states remains unresolved. This
report supplies the requested fallback: two concrete, structurally new
194-state implementations with complete initialization, source bindings,
literal contracts, and a written relative arithmetic proof. The positive
claim concerns the exact tables in this archive, not a state-count score of
an unverified grammar or a runtime requiring a prewritten tape.

Both machines have one head, one two-way infinite tape, alphabet
$\{0,1\}$, blank symbol zero, and only left or right moves. All cells
are initially zero and the head starts at coordinate zero. Dense states
are $0,\ldots,193$, initial state is 0, and halt is 194, excluded from
the working-state count. Every working state has two rows, so there are
388 transitions. All working controls are reachable in the directed
transition graph. This last statement is a finite graph fact; it does not
assert that every state occurs on the actual blank execution.

The archive contains complete dense and native transition tables. A row is
\begin{center}
\texttt{current\_state read\_bit write\_bit L\_or\_R next\_state}.
\end{center}
The dense tables, rather than a verbal construction, are definitive. Native
labels 74, 84, and 254 denote initial control, source boundary, and halt.
Native label 194 is an ordinary working control. Label maps introduce no
additional physical states.

There are three separate assertions in the result:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The finite construction and symbolic certificates bind each new
literal table to the same complete register program as the exact revised
AX11H table.
\item The sound interpretation of those certificates, with explicit clocks,
proves their blank halting equivalence in $\ipra$.
\item The absolute consistency corollary composes this new relative theorem
with the retained revised AX11H written calibration. Its enormous PA proof
compiler and unfinished end-to-end Lean formalization are not presented as
newly completed here.
\end{enumerate}
This distinction is essential. A finite experiment cannot establish a
consistency equivalence, and an incomplete Lean endpoint does not by itself
invalidate a separately supplied ordinary mathematical proof.

 ((A,(B,C)),D)\quad\longmapsto\quad(A,(B,(C,D))).

Here $A,B,C,D$ are the supplied helpers with native entry controls
104, 107, 98, and 90. The MP routine changes
\[
 ((E,F),(G,H))\quad\longmapsto\quad(E,(F,(G,H))),

with entry controls 213, 28, 77, and 114. The exact binary addresses are:

The corresponding source node is changed from
\path{node164=(node162,node163)} to
\path{node164=(node75,(node37,(node6,node54)))}.

 (100,0)\mapsto213,\quad(100,1)\mapsto174,\quad

This is not justified by an informal appeal to associativity. Source
\texttt{breakTo} and \texttt{continueTo} count branches on the address
path, so an arbitrary reassociation can change the program. Two independent
source resolvers are used, and the complete resulting arrays of operations,
registers, and successor ordinals are checked for exact equality. Both
zero and positive decrement successors are included.

\subsection{The hidden history use and its repair}
Native control 213 originally serves both as the forward operand reader
and as part of a backward history selector entered from control 69. It
cannot simply receive the new operand tree while retaining all callers.
The repaired construction redirects the latter call from 213 to 92,
repurposes 92 as a history selector, and uses the supplied reader 333 in
the forward operand tree.

There are two relevant history cases. At entry to the changed branch of
69, the current cell is zero; the cell immediately to the left is $h$.
All other tape cells are arbitrary. For $h=0$ the old and new paths are
\[
69\to213\to92\to104,
\qquad
69\to92\to111\to104.
\]
They both end one cell to the right of the original head, with both cells
unchanged. For $h=1$ the paths are
\[
69\to213\to90,
\qquad
69\to92\to90,
\]
with identical head, control, and tape endpoints. These two finite
two-cell identities, including the actual writes and moves, are retained
in \path{construction/relative_receipt.json}. They explain why the repair
is necessary and why it is valid on arbitrary surrounding tape.

\subsection{Count, complete source equality, and non-cosmetic difference}
The raw construction has 201 working controls, split as 135 fixed controls
and 66 AX reader controls. Seven disjoint, simultaneous contextual folds
give
\[
                  135+66-7=194.
\]
The local certificates are checked in the final simultaneous table;
individually valid folds are not merely assumed to commute.

Direct comparison with the revised AX11H source changes exactly 154
addresses: ordinals 224--290 and 3572--3658 inclusive. All 3,671 resolved
instructions, including every successor, remain identical. The maximum
address length is 37. Both sources have 1,456 increments, 1,107 decrements,
1,032 counted continues, 75 counted breaks, and one halt site. Thus their
4,778 outcomes consist of one outcome for each non-decrement site and two
for each decrement site.

The difference is material at the compiled-control level: two shared
routine address trees and the interpretation of a history call change.
It is not a new numerical calculus, and it is not claimed to be a
mathematical state-count breakthrough. The exact graph comparison excludes
mere renaming, reflection, different starts, and the other cosmetic
identifications tested in Section~\ref{sec:comparison}.

\section{The literal/source certificate and its arithmetic meaning}
\label{sec:physical}

\subsection{The complete frame invariant}
The logical register order is
\[
(R_0,\ldots,R_8)=(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L).
\]
Logical register $r$ is stored at physical position
\[
\pi=(6,2,0,4,3,7,8,5,1).
\]
Write $B(v)=0\,1^{v+1}$ for one unary block. At a source boundary the
head is at $h$, native control is 84, and the complete nonblank word has
the form
\[
1\;p_i\;0^{48+g-|p_i|}\;1\;
B(v_0)\cdots B(v_8)\,B(Q),                         \tag{1}
\]
starting at cell $h-1$. Here $p_i$ is the source address, $g\ge0$, the
$v_{\pi(r)}$ are the logical values $R_r$, and $Q$ is an arbitrary
nonempty finite list of spare values. Both exterior half-tapes are zero.
The frame specifies all cells, not just a window around the head.

The initial source state is ordinal 25 with logical store $(2,0,\ldots,0)$,
gap zero, and 26 spare values all equal to one. Its head coordinate is $-2$.
For each new table, literal simulation from the all-zero infinite tape
checks this entire frame after the stated startup time; the finite array
used for this check also has its head bounds checked at every step.

\subsection{Why the symbolic replay is universal}
The construction-side checker is supplemented by a different literal-tape
checker. The latter imports neither the construction compiler nor an
arithmetic-box oracle. It uses three kinds of step:
\begin{enumerate}
\item one transition from the actual table;
\item a homogeneous run traversed by a self-loop, with its exact length
added to the duration;
\item one of four list traversals, each rederived from actual rows on a
single symbolic block with arbitrary exterior contexts.
\end{enumerate}
The list traversals act on the block families
\[
B(v)=0\,1^{v+1},\qquad C(v)=1\,0\,1^v,
\]
and use the word identity $C(Q)1=1B(Q)$ and its reversal. The one-block
traversal takes exactly $v+2$ transitions. Induction on the length of a
fixed coded list gives its arbitrary-list version. This is ordinary
quantifier-free induction on a numerical prefix length with the whole
list code as a parameter.

The thirteen affine slots in a duration vector are
\[
 (1,g,x_0,\ldots,x_8,s,W(Q')),
\]
where $Q=[s;Q']$ and $W$ is the width of the very same spare tail represented
in the tape. It is not an unrelated independent variable. In a positive
decrement case, the tested register is represented as $R_a=1+x_a$, with
$x_a$ arbitrary. The zero branch specializes its tested slot to zero and
is checked separately. The other register slots retain their values, so
$x_r\le R_r$ in every case. Thus the replay covers every natural value,
not a bounded sample.

The retained interpretation proof establishes the soundness of literal,
run, list, and normalization rules in $\ipra$ \cite{physicalproof}. The
new finite audit checks that its premises hold for the new tables. In
particular, all 4,777 nonhalting endpoints are matched to their actual
resolved successor sites after padding; all duration coefficients are
nonnegative; every duration has positive constant; all address-overflow
and head-displacement fields are zero; and no nonhalting contract or
expanded list traversal contains a halt edge. The single halt contract
has the designated halt endpoint.

\subsection{An explicit uniform duration bound}
The componentwise envelope of all duration vectors, for the parent and
both new tables, is
\[
(534,8,4,4,4,4,4,4,2,4,4,2,2).                    \tag{2}
\]
At the $k$th source step of the actual initialized execution,
\[
 g\le k,\qquad \sum_{r<9}R_r\le2+k,\qquad W(Q)\le78+3k.
\]
An increment increases the register sum by one and appends a spare block
of value one; a positive decrement increases the gap by one; all other
source effects satisfy the same bounds. The first-spare contribution and
the remaining-tail contribution in (2) are bounded by $2W(Q)$ together.
Consequently the literal duration $D_k$ of the next source step obeys
\[
D_k\le534+8g+4\sum_r R_r+2W(Q)\le698+18k.         \tag{3}
\]
The lower bound $D_k\ge1$ is equally important. Summing (3), $k$ source
steps are simulated by literal time at most
\[
\boxed{\quad b_M+9k^2+689k\quad}                  \tag{4}
\]
The startup constant $b_M$ is 57,922 for the calibrated parent.
It is 58,014 for $\Mone$ and 58,010 for $\Mtwo$.
The bound concerns physical transitions along an
arbitrarily long initialized computation. The approximately 1.1 million
symbolic interpreter events in the receipts measure proof execution and
are not substituted for this physical clock.

\section{Constructive halting transfer in intuitionistic PRA}
\label{sec:ipra}

We now give the relative theorem independently of the absolute PA
calibration. Let $P$ be the common complete resolved source program and
$s_0$ its specified initial state. Extend its state by the gap, head
coordinate, and spare-list data in (1). These auxiliary data are updated
by primitive recursive functions and do not affect the source branch
choices. Make source halt and physical halt absorbing arithmetic codes.
This is a convention for the proof, not an additional machine control.

\begin{lemma}[Primitive recursive source-to-tape witness]
For each of the three machines $M$ there is a primitive recursive clock
$T_M(k)$ such that
\[
T_M(k)\le b_M+9k^2+689k
\]
and $\ipra$ proves that a source halt by $k$ produces a blank physical
halt by $T_M(k)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $s_k$ be the primitive recursive $k$-fold source iterate, with its
auxiliary frame data. Define $T_M(0)=b_M$ and
\[
T_M(k+1)=T_M(k)+D_M(s_k),
\]
using the exact affine duration of the appropriate checked contract.
After source halt use duration one. The supplementary relative proof uses a stopped clock instead; the absorbing post-halt unit steps used here give the same halting-witness bounds. Both the source iteration and this
clock are primitive recursive. Literal stepping on a finite-support tape
is primitive recursive as well, using the two binary integers encoding
the left and right halves. Its semigroup identity is proved by
quantifier-free induction on time.

Induct on $k$ in the equation
\[
 \Run_M(T_M(k),\mathrm{blank})=\Enc_M(s_k).          \tag{5}
\]
The base case is the complete finite startup. The induction step is the
checked contract, the sound interpretation of its run/list rules, and
the semigroup identity. Every term in (5) is a primitive recursive
function of the numerical parameters, so its induction formula is
quantifier free. No induction over an unbounded assertion about all
environments is used. Equation (4) follows by the same kind of induction
from (3). Halt is mapped to physical halt, proving the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Primitive recursive reflection witness]
$\ipra$ proves that a blank physical halt by $t$ implies a source halt
by $t+1$ for each of the three tables.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Positive segment durations give $T_M(t+1)\ge t+1$. If physical halt
occurs by $t$, absorption implies that the left side of (5), at
$k=t+1$, is halt. A nonhalting source frame has control 84, and so its
encoding is different from halt. The right side of (5) must therefore
be the encoding of source halt. The argument is a finite numerical
case distinction and uses the explicit witness $t+1$; it invokes neither
Markov's principle nor an unbounded search for the preceding boundary.
It also covers a purported halt inside a segment, rather than assuming
in advance that physical halts occur at source boundaries.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[New relative theorem]
For $M=\Mone$ and for $M=\Mtwo$,
\[
\ipra\vdash
 \bigl(\exists t\,\Halt_M(t)\bigr)
 \longleftrightarrow
 \bigl(\exists t\,\Halt_{\Mbase}(t)\bigr).          \tag{6}
\]
Both implications have a primitive recursive witness bound
\[
\Theta(t)=60000+9(t+1)^2+689(t+1).
\]
The two new machines consequently have equivalent blank halting predicates
over $\ipra$ as well.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Reflect the supplied physical witness to the common source witness
$t+1$, then simulate that source witness in the target table. The
constant 60,000 exceeds all three startup times. The common source
array, including both decrement targets, is checked directly against
the exact revised AX11H archive. These are explicit witness maps, so
existential introduction and elimination give (6) intuitionistically.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Consistency equivalence, with the identified inherited calibration]
Import the revised AX11H written theorem for its pinned Foundation
$\Con(\PA)$ predicate. For each $M\in\{\Mone,\Mtwo\}$,
\[
             \ipra\vdash\Con(\PA)\longleftrightarrow
                  \neg\exists t\,\Halt_M(t).       \tag{7}
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The inherited theorem gives (7) for $\Mbase$ \cite{ax11h}. Negating
both sides of the existential equivalence (6) is valid in intuitionistic
logic. Compose these two equivalences. No assumption that PA is consistent
is used.
\end{proof}

strictly decreasing recursion ranks, the current PA/ordinary-PAeq adapter
including \path{addEqOfLt}, the exact four-operand AX protocol, and the
physical/source theorem. No blocking inconsistency was found in those
inspected joins. The independent review and its limits are included under
\path{review/}.

This is a dependency audit, not a new implementation of the entire PA proof
compiler. The absolute corollary is explicitly conditional on accepting
that identified written mathematical calibration, just as a theorem
using a previously established lemma imports that lemma. The new relative
theorem (6) does not need the PA calibration. Its source equality, complete
frame closure, duration premises, and literal contracts are freshly
verified. Neither the relative theorem nor the corollary is represented
as a completed end-to-end Lean kernel theorem in this release.

\section{Attempts to get below 194 states}
\label{sec:search}

\subsection{Universal local quotients}
The fresh physical searches include every pair of non-start controls of
the primary table: $\binom{193}{2}=18{,}528$ pairs. A two-cell local search
allows either predecessor direction and temporary writes, and seeks a
replacement whose exact tape, head, and control endpoint reproduces the
old step. The first search examines 14,309,567 candidate first actions
with local length at most 32. A coupled version allows an intermediate
return to the identified state, examines 10,476,040 partial choices and
40 full coupled assignments, and permits length at most 64. Neither
produces an additional universally certified fold. The corresponding
coupled screen on the new joint tables also yields no fold.

The start state is excluded; the coupled search synthesizes only working-state targets and excludes conflicting overlapping incoming cylinders. These experiments address a well-defined local transformation family.
They do not prove that no larger-window, source-restricted, multi-state,
or representation-changing reduction exists. The search sources and exact
logs are supplied under \path{research/local_quotients/}.

\subsection{Why the 193-state runtime is not a positive result}
The existing runtime splice identifies native 301 with 88 and changes
\[
(88,0):0L51,\qquad(51,0):0R122.
\]
Its native table has SHA-256
\begin{center}\small
\hash{50f7be44afb63293365270a83111299247d12d140614e4b4c9a52786163acb53}.
\end{center}
It has valid runtime-frame contracts, but its original blank start enters
an outward ray. The original use of control 51 during construction of the
initial tape is not covered by the runtime marker condition. The surviving
193 controls are therefore an experimental core, not an initialized PA
consistency machine \cite{runtime193}.

This is the most concrete remaining one-state opportunity in the current
architecture: reuse those controls to construct a suitable frame, or alter
the splice so that the initializer survives. A table that merely runs
forever from blank fails the required internal equivalence to consistency;
one cannot promote it because external PA consistency is believed true.

\subsection{Broader initializer searches performed here}
The current HC4/C32 packet supplies a finite pool of 346 speculative row
edits, affecting 92 distinct rows, and tests every pair of edits on
different rows from every one of 193 possible initial controls
\cite{hc4}. Nineteen edits concern arithmetic-box rows and are speculative;
their presence in the pool is not an assertion of runtime correctness.

We retain the pool but broaden frame recognition in two ways. First, all
3,673 source-site addresses of the primary 193-state core are admitted,
rather than only twenty fetch sites. Second, the first pass permits header
distance 38, and a further pass permits any padding compatible with the
recognized source path. Both passes accept a minimum of twelve one-cells,
covering the all-zero-register boundary case omitted by a thirteen-cell
prefilter. The recognizer checks the entire finite support, translation,
unary blocks, and a nonempty spare tail. Arbitrary register values are
allowed at this discovery stage; a hit would still need a sound semantic
seed and a universal runtime proof.

For each of append-zero and append-one runtime variants, each pass tests
58,485 edit pairs and 11,287,605 starts, with a 200,000-transition and
20,000-cell head-displacement bound. Neither pass finds a complete frame.
The two append modes execute 1,993,659,748 and 2,173,631,284 literal
transitions, respectively. Their original classifications are retained:
233,864/117,274 halts, 11,044,078/11,159,792 outward rays, and
9,663/10,539 bounded unresolved runs. A resource-bound stop is not called
nonhalting. Frame recognition also requires the head plus 38 to remain below the positive displacement bound, a documented lookahead restriction near that boundary.

A further new search branches on a static edit only when its row is first
encountered. The original choice is then frozen, so an edit is never
introduced after an earlier inconsistent use of the same row. This avoids
enumerating many combinations of edits that a run never reaches. It
allows up to three edits from the same pool and recognizes the same broad
class of short frames. Sound pruning uses monotone outward blank rays and
exact repeated head/control configurations during an interval with no
tape changes. The finite branch cap and unresolved runs are stated in the
following actual receipts.

\section{What transfers from the other research areas}
\label{sec:alternatives}

\paragraph{ZFC machines.}
The useful transferable ideas are shared arithmetic engines, contextual
identification using nearby written bits, compact source trees, and
explicit finite simulation certificates. These are already central to the
PA frontier. A ZFC proof enumerator itself searches a different theory;
its consistency calibration cannot simply be relabelled PA. In this
review, the strongest concrete cross-interface opportunity is the
history-selector discipline: a control allocated as a decoder may also
encode call history. Any attempt to remove it must account for both uses.

\paragraph{Diophantine consistency.}
A smaller mathematical equation or system does not automatically yield a
smaller binary blank-start machine. Its tuple enumeration, integer coding,
multiplication, equality checks, and loading of constants must all be
compiled and initialized. Equivalence of the Diophantine system to
consistency addresses one logical interface; a state saving requires a
costed concrete implementation of the entire search. No such sub-194
implementation was obtained here. This observation does not rule out a
different arithmetic engine as a productive research direction.

\paragraph{ConciseIPRA.}
The simplicity of an iPRA proof checker is relevant to the metatheory and
to the presentation of the simulation proof. It is not by itself a
replacement for the PA refutation predicate being enumerated. A machine
searching for an iPRA contradiction would concern a different consistency
statement unless a separate PA-strength encoding were proved. The weak-base
lesson that does transfer is to express the runtime and proof translations
as explicit primitive recursive functions with quantifier-free invariants,
as done in Section~\ref{sec:ipra}.

\paragraph{Where a further reduction must earn its saving.}
Within the supplied architecture, three routes remain credible: construct
a real initializer for the certified 193-state core; reduce a shared fixed
routine and the AX reader graph jointly; or reduce the emitter while
replacing its exact command compiler and preserving the PA calibration.
The searches here make no claim to exhaust these routes. Merely changing
axiom notation, reducing a high-level token count, or reporting a grammar
score does not establish any of them.
