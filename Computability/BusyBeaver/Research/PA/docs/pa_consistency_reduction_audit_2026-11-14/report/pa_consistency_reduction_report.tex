\begin{abstract}
We inspect all 27 top-level archives in the requested incoming directory,
including their nested archives, and compare them with the imported PA196 and
PA209 projects and the extensive PA196--U208 comparison tree. The census finds
2,447 occurrences of complete numeric binary transition tables, representing
531 byte strings and 522 distinct labeled transition functions. These figures
include historical copies, rejected experiments and controls; they are not
counts of established consistency machines. The 14 occurrences of 195-state
tables belong to six rejected diagnostic constructions. We give a precise
logical audit, a proof-preserving family of independently reparameterized axiom
heads, new finite searches that score physical decoder sharing, and a correction
to the interpretation of intermediate dispatcher returns. \textbf{No machine
with fewer than 196 working states and the requested PA-provable consistency
biconditional is established by this investigation.} The archive is a reproducible
research result and comparison, not a claimed solution to that remaining task.
\end{abstract}

\section{Target, conventions, and result status}

The requested result is a completely specified deterministic, single-tape,
two-symbol Turing machine $T$, started on an all-zero two-way infinite tape,
such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:target}
 |Q_T|\leq195,
 \qquad
 \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg\Halt(T,\mathrm{blank})\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA)\bigr).
\end{equation}
Here $Q_T$ counts working controls; a separate halting control is excluded.
Every working control has one transition for each symbol in $\{0,1\}$, and
every transition moves left or right. We retain the binary Busy Beaver
convention of the supplied constructions.

The source snapshot is the Git commit
\begin{center}\small\ttfamily\pin\end{center}
of \href{\repo}{VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt}. All repository statements in
this report refer to this immutable revision. In particular, the state of a
proof in this snapshot takes precedence over informal descriptions in earlier
conversations or archive titles.

There are two independent completion conditions in \eqref{eq:target}. A smaller
source expression is not a smaller physical machine. A physical state reduction
does not itself prove the consistency calibration. This report keeps four
levels of evidence distinct:
\begin{enumerate}
\item a syntactically complete table and an exact working-state count;
\item preservation of a particular source program or command calculus;
\item exact blank startup and arbitrary-value primitive simulation;
\item both consistency implications formalizable inside $\PA$.
\end{enumerate}
A symbolic executable checker is stronger than testing finitely many numeric
register values when its loop rules are justified, but is still a different
artifact from a kernel-checked proof or an exported theorem of $\PA$.

\subsection{What is delivered}

The accompanying archive contains this article in \LaTeX{} and PDF, the complete
archive inventory with hashes and member paths, a per-archive comparison,
new search programs and their actual receipts, selected pinned source inputs,
and proof-audit and scanner-witness artifacts. Large historical archives are
identified by hash rather than duplicated. Reproducing the complete census
requires the pinned ProveIt checkout; reproducing the selected local searches
uses the included compact inputs.

The negative outcome is specific: the required machine was not constructed.
None of the searches proves that 196 states is a lower bound. Rejected
simulation certificates also do not, by themselves, prove that every associated
blank-run consistency statement is false.

\section{The logical endpoint: what is proved and what remains}

Let $H_{196}$ be the arithmetized halting sentence for the exact imported
table and let $N_{196}=\neg H_{196}$. The shipped declaration
\texttt{PA196.Arithmetic.pa\_proves\_nonhalting\_consistency} has the form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:oneway}
 \PA\vdash N_{196}\to\Con(\PA).
\end{equation}
The same project proves literal nonhalting in the Lean metatheory and proves
two-sided PA independence. These closed corollaries use Foundation's
standard-model consistency and soundness facts in Lean's metatheory.
Neither assertion supplies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reverse}
 \PA\vdash\Con(\PA)\to N_{196}.
\end{equation}
This limitation is already explicitly recognized in the imported-snapshot
README, \texttt{COMPARISON.md}, and
\src{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/pa196-vs-u208/PA-BRIDGE.md}{PA-BRIDGE.md}.
It is not a new defect discovered by this investigation, and it is not a claim
that \eqref{eq:reverse} is false or unprovable.

\subsection{A precise sufficient route for the missing direction}

Write $\mathrm{Tr}_T(t)$ for the decidable predicate that $t$ codes a finite
blank-to-halt trace of $T$. Write $\mathrm{NCert}(d,1)$ for acceptance of a native
derivation of the distinguished false formula, and $\mathrm{Prf}_{\PA}(p,\bot)$
for the chosen PA refutation predicate. A sufficient route is to give
primitive-recursive proof transformations $e,f$, with PA proofs of
\begin{align}
 \mathrm{Tr}_T(t)&\longrightarrow\mathrm{NCert}(e(t),1),\label{eq:extract}\\
 \mathrm{NCert}(d,1)&\longrightarrow\mathrm{Prf}_{\PA}(f(d),\bot).\label{eq:translate}
\end{align}
Existential quantification yields $H_T\to\neg\Con(\PA)$, and classical
contraposition yields $\Con(\PA)\to\neg H_T$. Together with the appropriate
forward implication, this proves the requested biconditional.

Equation~\eqref{eq:extract} must handle arbitrary internally coded finite
traces, including nonstandard lengths in nonstandard models of PA. It needs
startup, determinism, positive primitive durations, totality of nonhalting
segments, and extraction through a possibly unfinished last segment. A
source-to-tape simulation in the other direction does not automatically give
this reverse extraction.

Equation~\eqref{eq:translate} is a uniform proof transformation, not a truth
predicate for all arithmetic formulas. Under the intended bit-membership
interpretation, native axiom instances require uniform PA proof templates.
The separation template invokes PA induction with the translated input formula.
All coding bounds, variable conventions, freshness conditions and stack
certificate interfaces must be attached to the exact native calculus.

There is a particular interface issue in translating quantifier encodings:
PA196 uses the property code as a bound-variable label. Reversing the arguments
of a pairing function changes a property's formula code but does not
automatically change that label. A correct transport therefore needs a generic
fresh-binder theorem or a verified renaming, not just a statement that the
pairing arguments are swapped. The existing repository notes identify this
obligation explicitly.

\section{A sound enlarged family of axiom emitters}

The physical search need not be restricted to reassociating one fixed source
word. It may also vary a presentation while preserving the exact family of
available axiom instances. We record the justification because this is an
appropriate way to broaden the search without weakening its logical target.

Use the imported numerical constructors $\I,\M,\U,\E$, with the transposed
quantifier coding $\U(d,h)=4\operatorname{pair}(h,d)+2$. Let
$\N(B)=\I(B,1)$ and $\Ex(d,B)=\N(\U(d,\N(B)))$. In emission order the six heads are
\begin{align*}
h_1&=\I(\Ex(D,\U(D,H)),H),\\
h_2&=\I(\U(D,\U(A,H)),\U(A,\U(D,H))),\\
h_3&=\I(\M(H,D),\I(\E(H,A),\M(A,D))),\\
h_4&=\I(\E(H,D),\I(\M(A,H),\M(A,D))),\\
h_5&=\Ex(D,\E(H,D)),\\
h_6&=\I(\U(D,A),\I(\U(D,\I(A,H)),\U(D,H))).
\end{align*}
The command prefix that saves the old top, produces a generalization and
produces the restricted propositional head $\rho_8$ is kept unchanged.

\begin{theorem}[Independent head reparameterization]\label{thm:heads}
For each $i\in\{1,\ldots,6\}$ choose an arbitrary permutation $\pi_i$ of the
three coordinates $(D,H,A)$. Replace $h_i(D,H,A)$ by
$h_i(\pi_i(D,H,A))$, and emit the resulting six heads in any order. With the
same gate family, generalization rule, propositional head and modus ponens,
the native derivability relation is unchanged. The induced proof translations
in both directions are primitive recursive.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each $\pi_i$ is a bijection of $\mathbb N^3$. Therefore
\[
 \{h_i(\pi_i(v)):v\in\mathbb N^3\}
 =\{h_i(v):v\in\mathbb N^3\}
\]
as a set of numerical formula codes. This also applies when a head ignores
one coordinate. At a proof leaf using $h_i(v)$, supply the parameters
$\pi_i^{-1}(v)$ in the new presentation. Leave other axiom leaves unchanged and
recurse through modus ponens and generalization. The inverse transformation
uses $\pi_i$. Finite-tree recursion and finite coordinate permutations are
primitive recursive. No consistency or semantic soundness assumption is used.
\end{proof}

At the command interface, the existing DISCARD operation sets $d=h=0$.
Consequently AX$(c,x,y)$ realizes $D=x$, $H=y$, $A=c$, so every parameter triple
is available. The compiler chooses the inverse-permuted parameters and adjusts
the fixed Shift selector to the head's new stack position. The six heads still
have code 0 or a code at least 2: implication codes are multiples of 4, and
the existential head is itself an implication code. This preserves the
selector's required avoidance of false code 1. In this original 201-token
emitter family there are still nine pushed values including the saved top.
The separate-diagonal-head 190-macro emitter used elsewhere in the experiments
has one additional axiom head, ten pushed values, and correspondingly adjusted
selectors. None of this claims that an arbitrary new physical allocation is
correct.

\subsection{Additional justified variants and a rejected shortcut}

The executable emitter reproduces the exact 201-token baseline from
\texttt{PA196/Source/AXProgram.lean}. It also exposes six independent bit choices:
exchange curried antecedents in $h_3,h_4,h_6$; exchange equality and membership
vacuity in the outer gate; exchange the two conjunction terms in separation;
and exchange the first two antecedents in the relevant separation implication.
The head changes use implicational exchange. The gate changes use propositional
equivalence and the retained quantifier-distribution/generalization machinery.
The original $h_1$ instance family remains present.

Swapping $D,H$ throughout the gate alone is also an exact reparameterization.
The property code $A$ stays fixed, and the guard $A\ne D,H$ is symmetric. We do
not allow an unproved swap of $A$ with an object-label register: $A$ is also the
property's formula code and the designated binder.

An initially considered replacement of $h_1$ by a negation-based contrapositive
is deliberately disabled. The native calculus derives explosion using $h_1$
itself. Appealing to explosion to recover $h_1$ from its proposed replacement
would be circular. This rejected option is absent from the completed searches.

\section{Fresh reduction experiments}

This section reports experiments performed during the present investigation,
not just results copied from the incoming packets. Exact counts and input
identities are retained in the accompanying JSON receipts.

\subsection{Global recompression}

We tried 20,000 randomized repeated-pair recompressions of the closed AX
sequence, also supplying externally used subexpressions to encourage sharing.
The unchanged full program has 159 distinct internal source nodes. The best
newly sampled source has 162, with no improvement. This experiment is a
heuristic over associative grammars; its failure is not an optimality proof.
It also illustrates why a grammar search must be scored against the physical
allocation rather than an informal count of shortened arithmetic expressions.

\subsection{Exhaustive direct head substitutions}

For each of the two supplied 196-state diagonal variants, we independently
permuted $(D,H,A)$ in each of the six heads, retaining the binary association.
This gives $6^6=46,656$ cases per grammar and 93,312 cases in total.
The imported physical template contributes 134 fixed controls, so a successful
sub-196 allocation would need an AX contribution of at most 61.

For these two fixed associations, the identity parameter assignment is the
unique case with matching bound 62. Every nonidentity assignment has bound at
least 66. The eligible cases also receive the exact simultaneous-helper check.
Thus this particular exhaustive family gives no improvement. The statement is
restricted to the retained associations and imported pair-fold template;
reassociation, different helpers or another engine are not excluded.

All $6!=720$ direct reorderings of the six heads were additionally examined for
each of the two grammars, 1,440 cases. They likewise do not reduce the physical
contribution below 62. Further mixed semantic/association searches broaden this
family, but remain heuristic. Their completed proposal counts are recorded in
the generated experiment summary, rather than inferred from nominal budgets.

\subsection{Longer exact decoder helpers}

The incoming compiler's standard context fold distinguishes complementary
reader nodes by looking at the immediately preceding path bit. Its familiar
helper trace consists of a left step, a right step, and a right step. We
extended the search to arbitrary deterministic computations in a fixed finite
window, allowing the first write to flip the current bit provided every
touched bit is eventually restored. The replacement conflicting row may write
either bit, moves left, and enters an existing state; subsequent helper steps
use retained rows.

For a width $w$, a local configuration comprises a working control, a head
position in $\{-w,\ldots,0\}$, and a binary word of length $w+1$. There are
at most
\begin{equation}\label{eq:finite}
 |Q|(w+1)2^{w+1}
\end{equation}
such configurations. Starting from each allowed predecessor word, the search
runs until it exits the window or repeats an exact local configuration.
Thus it completely decides this finite-window helper question; no time cutoff
is used for an individual helper.

Source-node occurrences determine which predecessor words are admissible.
A pair can share a row only if the helper realizes the required successor
for every admitted context and restores all window bits. Proposed simultaneous
folds are replayed in the \emph{patched} table, because one fold can alter
another fold's helper. Merely finding a matching in a compatibility graph is
not enough.

The selected portfolio has 101 input grammars: the two checked diagonal
grammars, the scanner grammar, and 98 saved ties. Searches with one, two,
three and five predecessor bits found no additional compatible state-saving
fold beyond the existing short helper options. The five-bit context case
covers six tape cells including the current cell. Its search is exhaustive
only for the unchanged helper rows and the specified single-row entry template;
it is not exhaustive over new writable controllers or arbitrary tape
simulations. Every examined grammar retains effective AX cost 62, hence total
cost 196 in that compiler.

\subsection{Combining changes from different packets}

The successful-MP rewrite returning its consequent in $H$ and the shorter
negation routine are logically different transformations. We combined them
with the recent diagonal and separate-diagonal AX layouts rather than
assuming their reported savings add.

The shorter negation routine changes the AX sharing opportunities: the two
main 196-state variants become 197 states, with 68 readers but only five
compatible folds instead of six. Their symbolic contracts pass in the supplied
experiments. Across the saved tie portfolio, adding the MP rewrite keeps the
MP-only constructions at 196 and the combined NEG/MP constructions at 197;
the retained scanner-oriented combination costs 198. These are useful checks
of composability, but none is a solution to \eqref{eq:target}.

\subsection{Writable scanner synthesis}

The return engine's recognition of a long zero run is another potential
optimization point. A one-pass read-only counter is only one implementation;
in principle, a writable two-way controller could trade time for states.
The exploratory synthesis artifacts formulate exact local contracts restoring
all touched cells and test a seven-control positive control before attempting
six controls. Solver results are reported with their actual scope: a timeout
or unknown result is not UNSAT, and UNSAT for a bounded template is not a
general lower bound. No newly certified smaller scanner is used in any claimed
machine in this report. The final solver receipt states the completed outcome.
The seven-control positive instance is satisfiable and independently replayed.
The six-control writable template with a 14-step block bound is unsatisfiable;
longer or less restricted queries reported unknown. In particular, the fixed
one-symbol action and first-fresh-target restrictions are part of that finite
template, not a justified normal form for all possible scanners.

\section{Scanner audit: a dispatcher return is not an instruction endpoint}

The 195-state scanner diagnostic is not a certified consistency machine. Its
archived unrestricted symbolic check reports 96 failed contracts out of 4,780.
However, one of the accompanying informal diagnostics must be interpreted
carefully: a tape mismatch at the \emph{first} return to the dispatcher does
not by itself refute a multi-pass primitive implementation.

We checked this directly on the parent. From the standard source seed,
site 25 and registers $(2,0,\ldots,0)$, source execution reaches site 0 after
43 steps, with registers
\[
 (3,0,0,0,0,0,1,0,1).
\]
For the corresponding admissible gap-57 frame, the 196-state parent returns
to native dispatcher 84 after 134 and 290 transitions with intermediate
tapes. It reaches the specified site-4 successor frame only after 574
transitions, at its third dispatcher return. A checker that rejected the
parent at its first return would therefore reject a correct implementation.

The shortened candidate has a different finite prefix and its proposed
symbolic certificate still fails. The point of this correction is not to
accept that candidate. It is to keep the logical conclusion accurate:
failure of a sufficient simulation certificate is not automatically a
counterexample to the blank-run consistency property. Nor does a finite prefix
prove that a later rejoining is impossible.

An independent blank-run probe of the shortened candidate completed ten million
transitions without a halt or a sampled exact cycle. At that cap it was in
native state 0, at head position 18,796, with 4,688 one cells and 64,774
dispatcher visits. This is bounded observational evidence only. It establishes
neither nonhalting nor \eqref{eq:target}.

\section{What transfers from ZFC and ConciseIPRA}

The ZFC branch supplies valuable implementation techniques: closed source
reassociation checked by independent control-flow resolvers, contextual state
allocation, arbitrary-value primitive contracts, and positive-duration
simulation. Its K215 consistency calibration is about ZFC. Importing that
machine or relabeling its states does not change the target to PA.

The current ZFC README carefully distinguishes formal endpoints. It reports
compiled external Lean and Rocq correctness for K215, while leaving the
internal PA endpoint for K215 open; it separately identifies a checked internal
PA equivalence for the literal 240-state machine. We preserve those distinct
claims instead of treating all forms of formal verification as interchangeable.

ConciseIPRA provides useful primitive-recursive representations and proof
transport methods. The PA comparison's own iPRA bridge files distinguish
standard-natural-number bounded simulation from conditional internal endpoints.
For example, the endpoint file assumes the machine-specific reductions; the
negative-translation files assume appropriate source derivations and witness
certificates. A small checker for iPRA does not by itself supply a PA consistency
checker, and Python source size does not determine the state count of a complete
binary TM with startup.

\section{Conclusion and the remaining concrete obligations}

The comparison has separated repeated presentations, same-size variants,
smaller source programs, rejected 195-state diagnostics and actual physical
implementations. It has also checked new combinations and enlarged finite
search families. The strongest surviving size in these computations remains
196 working states. The requested strict improvement and full internal
biconditional remain unproved here.

A successful next construction must supply the exact table and start convention,
a justified source or calculus change, blank initialization, a sound simulation
on the admitted frames, and the two arithmetized consistency directions.
The proofs and receipts in this archive make those obligations inspectable;
they do not replace them with a count of source tokens or a long blank run.
