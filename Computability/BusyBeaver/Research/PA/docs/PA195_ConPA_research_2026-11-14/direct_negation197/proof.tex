\section{An exact direct-negation replacement}
This section records a source simplification implemented by an experimental
197-state table. It does not meet the requirement of fewer than 196 states.
The parent is the separated diagonal emitter with the Combined196 command
control changes. Its source has 3,649 sites; the replacement has 3,433.

Write $T=r_1$, $W=r_2$, $S=r_6$, and $R=r_7$, and put
\[
 p(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b.
\]
The old negation block is \texttt{push; increment T; cons}. The replacement
is \texttt{drain T into S; increment T; pair-and-times-four}.
It reuses the existing source subtrees \texttt{node37} and \texttt{node64},
whose native entry states are 28 and 106.

\paragraph{Arbitrary-value block identity.}
For every $t,w,s\in\mathbb N$, both blocks, entered with
$(T,W,S,R)=(t,w,s,0)$, terminate with
\[
 (T,W,S,R)=(4p(t+s,1),w,0,0),
\]
and preserve all other registers. In the old block, the push stores
$p(t+s,w)$ in $W$ and clears $T,S,R$. After the increment, $T=1$.
The cons block unpairs $W$ back into $t+s$ and $w$ and computes $4p(t+s,1)$.
In the new block, the first drain gives $T=0,S=t+s$; the increment gives
$T=1$; the unchanged pairing suffix gives exactly the same result.

Here is an explicit termination argument for that pairing suffix, avoiding
an inference from finite input tests. It first drains $T$ into both $S$ and
$R$. At the following checkpoint $T=0$, and the invariant is
\[
 R+\frac{S(S+1)}2=p(s_0,t_0)
\]
for the initial inputs $(S,T,R)=(s_0,t_0,0)$. A nonzero $S$ is decremented
once, $R$ is incremented once, and the remaining $S$ is drained into $T$.
Returning through the first drain changes the checkpoint values by
$(R,S)\mapsto(R+S,S-1)$, where the right-hand $S$ is the old value.
The displayed invariant is preserved, and $S$ decreases strictly.
Consequently this phase terminates with $S=T=0$ and $R=p(s_0,t_0)$.
The final loop drains $R$ while making four increments of $T$ per iteration,
giving $T=4p(s_0,t_0)$ and $R=0$. Every drain terminates by decreasing the
register drained. The argument is an elementary induction over natural
numbers and is formalizable in PA.

The entry condition $R=0$ matters. With all registers initially zero except
$R=1$, the old 47-site block returns $T=16$, whereas the new 20-site block
returns $T=12$. Thus this is not an unconditional replacement on arbitrary
source configurations.

\paragraph{The actual source invariant.}
The clean seed is source site 25 with register vector
$(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$. A bit mask records, for each register, whether its
value is zero or positive. Increment makes the corresponding bit positive;
a decrement at zero takes only its zero branch; a decrement at a positive
value takes its positive branch and nondeterministically leaves zero or a
positive value. Other instructions preserve that bit.
The computed overapproximations contain 263,244 old and 247,308 new
site/mask pairs. The replay independently checks initial inclusion and
closure under every abstract successor. Induction on a concrete execution
therefore places every reachable configuration in the abstraction. At each
of the eight replaced entries, every admitted mask has bit 7 clear.
Hence the required $R=0$ hypothesis holds without sampling a run.

The old entry ordinals are
\[
584,686,1759,1861,3007,3081,3316,3472;
\]
the new entries are
\[
584,659,1705,1780,2899,2946,3154,3283.
\]
The regions are disjoint. Their only external entry is their first site and
their only external exit their following site. The 3,273 other source rows
agree under the order-preserving relocation, including all branch targets.
The local identity and entry invariant therefore compose into a
halting-preserving source simulation from the clean seed. This argument
compares source computations; it does not substitute a finite blank-tape
prefix for a proof of nonhalting.

\paragraph{Literal implementation and limits.}
The table has 197 working states, with 68 axiom-reader states and five
compatible reader folds over the fixed 134-state part. The complete symbolic
primitive-runtime audit has 4,461 cases and a maximum of 497 symbolic steps.
The literal startup reaches the exact source frame after 57,922 steps.
The checker uses the unchanged upstream arbitrary-register block contracts;
these executable certificates are not newly exported Lean theorems. The
mathematical and encoded-proof calibration of the parent calculus remains a
separate dependency. No state saving or new end-to-end PA biconditional is
claimed for this 197-state experiment.
