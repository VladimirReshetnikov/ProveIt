\paragraph{Independent universal physical verification.}
For each of the exact literal tables $195\mathrm{S}$ and $195\mathrm{C}$,
the independent affine whole-tape checker verifies every primitive source
outcome: respectively $4{,}780$ outcomes of $3{,}673$ sites and $4{,}749$
outcomes of $3{,}649$ sites. Each contract ranges over arbitrary natural
values of all nine primary registers, arbitrary nonempty finite spare
lists, and every initial header gap at least $48$. Both decrement outcomes
are checked without a source-reachability restriction. Successful checking
requires equality of the complete symbolic tape, the prescribed control
and head displacement, and strictly positive elapsed time.

This checker uses literal transition rows, elementary induction for unary
self-loop sweeps, and four generic finite-list lemmas rederived from each
new table with arbitrary opaque surrounding tape. It does not replace
arithmetic routines by register-box oracles. The per-outcome affine step
counts and motions, the one-block lemma traces, and the exact finite blank
seeds are retained in the certificate. The complete blank runs reach the
required source-site-$25$ seed after exactly $58{,}154$ and $57{,}922$
steps. Dense/native conjugacy and both independent source CFG resolutions
are checked as well. This verifies the physical source-to-tape interface;
the logical calibration and the end-to-end internal arithmetic statement
remain separate mathematical proof obligations.
