\section{Transporting the U214 emitter to the direct-transfer source}
\label{sec:st-source}

The new source composes two independently identified parts: the direct-transfer
control structure of ST215 and the exact unary-power-cover emitter already
calibrated for U214.  In particular, the result does not inherit the fixed
K215 consistency theorem by renaming its conclusion.

\paragraph{Exact construction.}
The original ST215 source has 4,732 primitive occurrences and 201 distinct
subtrees.  Its set-theoretic emitter occupies the half-open ordinal interval
$[2884,4481)$, containing 1,597 primitive occurrences.  This interval is a
forest of five closed factors of respective sizes $132,654,652,103,56$.
Replace it by the single closed 774-primitive subtree at $[2885,3659)$ in
the primary U214 source.  This is the U214 role assignment with matrix and
output label $a$, source label $b$, and tested label $c$; the retained gate
requires $c\ne a,b$.

The result has 3,909 primitive occurrences, 197 distinct subtrees, maximum
address length 42, and maximum consecutive-zero address run six.  Its source
SHA-256 is
\begin{center}\small
\texttt{\seqsplit{7b6539dc67b66ff73f22554018d7af5985d85574460a73fea5f025103c523d00}}.
\end{center}
The deterministic script \texttt{code/build\_source.py} requires the two exact input
hashes and reconstructs these bytes; it performs no search or optimization.

\paragraph{Full control-flow audit.}
Let $f(i)=i$ for $i<4481$ and $f(i)=i-823$ otherwise.  For each of the 3,135
retained exterior primitives, the audit checks the exact operation, register
or branch operand, and every resolved ordinal successor after translation
by $f$.  It verifies that no retained control edge enters the strict interior
of the removed interval.  It separately compares every copied emitter
primitive and successor with U214, with the constant ordinal translation
$j\mapsto j-1$.  This supplies an explicit graph comparison, in addition
to the source checksums.

The critical ST215 command-return instruction remains
\texttt{CC3} at address \texttt{00111}; its new ordinal is 3873 and its
resolved target is scheduler ordinal zero.  Direct transfer starts at
ordinal 3874, address \texttt{010}, with \texttt{CD1}.  The unique source
halt is at ordinal 3894, address \texttt{11101}.  Thus the change does not
silently replace the ordinary scheduler by the rejected scratch-biased
return to a selector interior.

\paragraph{Logical interface.}
The U214 emitter has the same uniform arithmetic interface as the removed
closed computation: it pushes one freshly constructed covering component above the old
top; the unchanged surrounding code builds the complete bundle, preserves the enumeration fields, and restores the two scratch registers
to zero.  Its logical calibration, including coincident output/source labels,
small codes zero and one, and recovery of the atomic-vacuity component, is
the supplied U214 argument.  The source comparison therefore transports that
calibration to the ST215 context without changing a remaining arithmetic
macro.  This is a change of the enumerated theory to the U calculus, not a
claim that the original ST215 set-theoretic theorem has changed its meaning.

The direct-transfer command still has the all-natural contract
\[
 S(t,w,d,h)=
 \begin{cases}
  \mathsf{halt},& h=1,\\
  (h,\operatorname{tail}(w),d,\operatorname{head}(w)),&h\ne1,
 \end{cases}
\]
where $\operatorname{head}$ and $\operatorname{tail}$ are the left and right
Cantor projections.  The original ST215 strong compiler and its protected
arbitrary-tail proof apply to this same contract.  Its AX command still adds
the eight axiom outputs, a generalization of its theorem-valued incoming
cache, and the saved theorem-valued top.  Only the covering component of the gated output
has changed.  In particular, every enabled covering bundle remains an
implication code, and hence cannot equal the falsity code one merely by its
numerical constructor tag.

Every source-level step used here is a finite syntactic operation or an
ordinary induction on natural counters, finite syntax, or finite command
histories.  These arguments are formalizable in PA.  The separate literal
machine proof must additionally establish the exact physical primitive
simulation and the correct blank-start bridge.  The source audit is not a
substitute for either obligation, and no newly compiled end-to-end
proof-assistant certificate is claimed by this source transport.
