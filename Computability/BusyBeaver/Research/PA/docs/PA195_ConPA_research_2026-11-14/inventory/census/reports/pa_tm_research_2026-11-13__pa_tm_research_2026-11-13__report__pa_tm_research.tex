\begin{abstract}
We compare the nine designated 208-state tables in the supplied ProveIt
archives with James Chang's specified 209-state predecessor and current
196-state machine. The nine tables form two transition-graph isomorphism
classes. Their final state deletion is already subsumed by the replacement
of the axiom command in the 196-state construction, so their savings cannot
be added mechanically. We reproduce the current machine's finite startup
and all 4,959 arbitrary-natural instruction contracts in Lean 4.35.0,
including its tape/source halting equivalence. We also give a syntactic
proof-translation argument for the reverse consistency implication that
is not an explicit endpoint of the published Lean development.

The searches recorded here did not produce a verified machine with fewer
than 196 working states. This report is a reproducible research result and
an audit of the supplied constructions, not a claim to meet that numerical
target. The package includes the published 196-state table and a new
196-state variant with shorter source paths, checked by an independent
symbolic executor but not newly verified in Lean. Negative search results
are restricted to their stated search
families and are not lower bounds on possible constructions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Outcome, conventions, and source identities}
\label{sec:outcome}

The requested target is a one-tape, two-symbol machine with at most 195
working states, started on an all-zero tape, whose nonhalting is equivalent
to consistency of ordinary first-order PA. The intended strong calibration is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:target}
 \PA\vdash \NH(M)\leftrightarrow\Con(\PA).
\end{equation}
The work below does not exhibit an accepted table meeting the state-count
target. Keeping that limitation explicit is essential: a shorter register
program, a graph with fewer nodes, or a candidate that survives selected
runs is not yet a smaller Turing machine with the required property.

A working state has a transition on each tape symbol. The terminal label
has no transition and is not included in the working-state count. A row
$(q,b,w,D,q')$ reads $b$, writes $w$, moves one cell in direction $D$, and
enters $q'$. The tape is unbounded in both directions. All comparisons
preserve the write symbol, direction, start, and terminal control.

We freeze the following revisions, avoiding an ambiguous comparison of
moving branches:
\begin{itemize}
\item ProveIt: \file{f7d2482e35cf2f8f3bd03b5a82387ad999243548}; the
  \file{docs/incoming} archives and \file{ConciseIPRA} material~\cite{proveit,ipra}.
\item Current BusyBeaver: \file{407cdc356f6b586635e71ac92be394255f4bfea4},
  containing the 196-state table~\cite{pa196}.
\item Requested predecessor: \file{69d98241a721ead20b4f0b17df9062950b40fbfe},
  containing the 209-state table~\cite{pa209}.
\end{itemize}
The accompanying manifest records hashes of the actual inputs. Local
filenames containing ``209'' or ``207'' are not treated as evidence of
machine identity or correctness.

\section{The 208-state archives}
\label{sec:208}

All eleven ZIP archives in the specified incoming directory were inspected.
Five construction packages contain nine designated final 208-state tables;
the other packages contain comparisons, attempted further reductions, or
logical calibration. In particular, files described as rejected 207-state
candidates are not accepted constructions.

\subsection{Two machines, presented nine times}

We canonicalize a rooted transition graph by breadth-first discovery,
visiting the read-zero edge before the read-one edge and distinguishing
the halt label. The canonical form retains the two output actions at each
state. Because the input labels are fixed and transitions deterministic,
equal rooted canonical forms give an explicit action-preserving state
bijection. The checker supplies those bijections, not just hashes.

The two classes implement the same 3,909-occurrence register program, with
different tree associations and control layouts. Its primitive instruction
inventory is 1,558 increments, 1,175 conditional decrements, 80 breaks,
1,095 continues, and one halt. Thus there are 5,084 branch contracts when
both decrement outcomes are counted. The maximum path depths are 42 and
41. The portable comparison recomputes the agreement of their resolved
source control-flow graphs.

\subsection{The common final saving}

All nine tables use the same conditional-history identification of native
roles 172 and 211. The relevant entry to role 172 has preceding bits
$10$; when role 211 scans zero, its immediately preceding bit is one.
A shared read-zero transition moves left to an existing discriminator.
The two layouts number that helper differently. Their four complete local
paths have lengths $7,1,3,1$.

This is a valid kind of optimization: the previous tape cell retains
information that would otherwise be stored in the control state. However,
the nine packets do not supply nine independent deletions. Counting
renamings or alternative implementations of the same deletion more than
once would give an incorrect combined state count.

\section{The 209-to-196 reduction}
\label{sec:196}

The requested James Chang 209-state predecessor is a different transition
table from the U209 parents inside the U208 packets. Its literal working
labels are
\[
 \{0,\ldots,213\}\setminus\{50,122,150,157,205\},
\]
with halt label 215. The current table has working labels $0,\ldots,195$
and halt label 196. Both have explicit native-label maps in their Lean
sources, which make an exact structural comparison possible.

The 196-state machine decomposes as
\[
 196=36\text{ engine states}+13\text{ leaf routers}
       +147\text{ states reading 159 internal nodes}.
\]
Within the axiom command, 201 macro items form a tree with 68 distinct
internal nodes, implemented by 62 reader states. Six shared pairs account
for the difference. This shows why minimizing the number of macro items
alone is not the right objective.

\subsection{Logical and coding changes}

Let $p(a,b)=\binom{a+b+1}{2}+b$. The current constructors are
\[
 \I(a,b)=4p(a,b),\quad \M(a,b)=4p(a,b)+1,\quad
 \U(a,F)=4p(F,a)+2,\quad \E(a,b)=4p(a,b)+3.
\]
The quantifier constructor reverses the pairing arguments compared with
the predecessor. The revised calculus removes a primitive equality head,
adds equality vacuity under the existing separation guard, and rearranges
the remaining heads. These are substantive source changes, not simply
control-state aliases~\cite{pa196,pa209}.

For reference the six surviving heads are
\begin{align*}
 &(\exists x_D\,\U(D,H))\to H,\\
 &\U(D,\U(A,H))\to\U(A,\U(D,H)),\\
 &\M(H,D)\to(\E(H,A)\to\M(A,D)),\\
 &\E(H,D)\to(\M(A,H)\to\M(A,D)),\\
 &\exists x_D\,\E(H,D),\\
 &\U(D,A)\to(\U(D,A\to H)\to\U(D,H)).
\end{align*}
They accompany the restricted classical implicational head and the guarded
gate. The current paper derives the equality transport needed by its PA
interpretation without retaining the deleted primitive head.

The native-label tables have 134 working-state names in common. Of their
268 common rows, 265 agree exactly. The three differences are:

The first two changes bypass obsolete distinguishing controls; the last
enters the replacement axiom body. Overall, 75 old states disappear and
62 new states are introduced, giving $209-75+62=196$. In particular,
native states 172 and 211 are already absent. The U208 final deletion
therefore has no pair of remaining states to act on. The frequently
tempting calculation ``196 minus the U208 saving'' has no transition-level
justification.

\section{What was independently verified}
\label{sec:verification}

The table bytes are fixed by the SHA-256 digest
\begin{quote}\small
\nolinkurl{e8bbccd4cfa325251ca95066db116b5aa766e404b0971c33c02772be1c3617a8}.
\end{quote}
The published source/table checks were rerun successfully. A fresh Python
port of the symbolic executor reproduced all 4,959 instruction contracts.
A second checker inherited from the U208 research independently replayed
the current source and table. The decisive physical proof was then rebuilt
with Lean 4.35.0 in a small package depending only on its standard library.

\subsection{Universal contracts rather than sampled register values}

A frame contains a marker, a finite source path, an affine zero gap, a
header, and a complete register file. A value $v$ occupies the block
$0\,1^{v+1}$. The symbolic gap represents $n+c$ for arbitrary
$n\in\mathbb N$; the register-file segment represents every valid complete
file of length at least ten. Register marking, increment, and the two
decrement outcomes are justified by the existing unary/list lemmas.

For each of 3,812 source occurrences the checker proves a positive-length
transition to the complete successor frame, or the designated halt.
There are 1,147 decrement occurrences, hence
\[
 3,812+1,147=4,959
\]
cases. The Python port required at most 515 abstract executor steps per
case and 1,619,679 in total for the baseline. These are checker operations,
not bounds on the running time of the actual proof-search machine.

The frame gap is at least 48 and source paths have length at most 38.
The use of a symbolic complete file is essential: an optimization that
works for a few small register values can fail on longer unary blocks or
on a different spare tail. Symbolic preservation includes all of those
cells.

\subsection{The rebuilt Lean endpoints}

The reproduced physical dependency closure contains 308 upstream source
modules, with an additional guarded audit. The endpoint build reports
309 build jobs, including the package configuration. Its
main endpoints are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \file{PA196.Startup.startup_exact}: the blank run reaches the clean
  frame after exactly 58,154 steps.
\item \file{PA196.Coverage.complete}: every source occurrence has the
  required arbitrary-natural contract, including both decrement branches.
\item \file{PA196.PhysicalEndpoint.blank_halting_iff_source}: literal
  blank-tape halting is equivalent to halting of the resolved register
  source from the actual startup store.
\end{itemize}
The printed axiom dependencies are confined to Lean's usual
\file{propext}, \file{Classical.choice}, and \file{Quot.sound}; the startup
endpoint lists only \file{propext}. A separate audit in the archive checks
the final endpoint statements for unapproved axioms.

This rebuild covers the physical simulation. It does not rebuild the
entire Foundation/Mathlib arithmetic independence development, and it does
not produce a new Lean proof of the reverse implication in
equation~\eqref{eq:target}. The published arithmetic endpoint and the
written proof construction in the next section have different evidential
roles.

\section{The exact consistency claim}
\label{sec:status}

The U208 manuscripts assert the PA-provable biconditional as an ordinary
mathematical result; their accompanying status notes do not claim a new
end-to-end Lean, Rocq, MM0, or exported PA proof certificate. The logical
statement and the level of formal verification must be distinguished.

The current repository explicitly lists
\[
 \PA\vdash\NH(M_{196})\to\Con(\PA)
\]
as \file{PA196.Arithmetic.pa_proves_nonhalting_consistency}~\cite{status}.
Its external nonhalting theorem and two-sided independence theorem do not
themselves assert the converse inside PA. In particular, a Lean theorem
proved using a standard model in the metatheory is not automatically a PA
proof of an arithmetical sentence.

There is nevertheless a direct syntactic route to the missing implication.
Each accepted command-calculus axiom has a uniformly constructible PA
proof under the binary-digit interpretation, including the separation
gate. Annotating a finite command execution with derivation codes turns a
halt into a PA refutation. The following section supplies the details and
states the compilation obligations precisely. It avoids an appeal to a
uniform truth predicate for arbitrary arithmetical formulas.

\section{Reduction searches and why rejected candidates matter}
\label{sec:search}

The experiments preserved the binary alphabet, blank input, working-state
count convention, and full tape endpoint. The record distinguishes three
stages: a proposed rewrite, a finite regression survivor, and an
arbitrary-natural certified construction. None of the new proposals in
this package reaches the last stage with fewer than 196 states.

\subsection{Fixed-table control transformations}

The reproduced search families include direct context-sensitive state
identification, longer local excursions, head-offset representations,
and two simultaneous folds sharing a newly introduced discriminator.
The last option spends one new state while trying to remove at least two;
counting only the removed states would be erroneous.

A fresh bounded primitive-endpoint screen considered 366,912 one-row
fold mutations. Ten survived targeted examples but failed the full
sampled endpoint suite. A subsequent source-conditioned screen used
69,915 full-frame cases and found no survivor. These are finite rejection
results, not proofs that every possible 195-state table fails.

An additional search considered compressing all incoming excursions of a
candidate deleted state. An odd-length path can sometimes be replaced by
one transition if it leaves every other cell unchanged and ends one cell
from the entry position. For the baseline, the search used local windows
of 16 cells on either side and paths up to 45 steps. It found no state for
which all incoming rows could be eliminated this way. Unknown symbolic
cells were not assigned convenient values to make a candidate pass.

\subsection{Tree association and source specialization}

Changing the association of the 201-item axiom word can change both the
number of distinct subtrees and the availability of history-based folds.
The useful cost is therefore the number of physical reader states after
compatible folds, not just the number of distinct internal nodes. The
current cost is $68-6=62$ in that body.

The return scanner imposes an additional constraint. A long zero run
inside a newly arranged path can be mistaken for the delimiter. A grammar
with an attractive node count is rejected if the actual tape execution
returns to the wrong control location. The search tools retain this
constraint and subsequently test the complete instruction contracts.

Source reachability also identifies zero tests whose nonzero arms are
unreachable from the actual scheduler seed. The two concrete inherited
opportunities are the register-6 test at source ordinal 7 and the
register-1 cleanup at ordinal 3773. Removing source occurrences does not
monotonically improve control sharing. A register-6 specialization gives
198 working states and passes all 4,956 arbitrary-natural branch contracts
from supplied source frames. Its blank startup remains uncalibrated:
neither the published clean seed nor a decoded candidate-2 frame was
reached within 1,000,000 literal steps. It is therefore a runtime
experiment, not an accepted blank-tape consistency machine, and its state
count also exceeds the target. A straightforward 202-state cleanup layout
failed its endpoint checks and remains rejected.

Reordering the six independent axiom heads preserves the calculus: a

Lean kernel, and the physical Lean rebuild described above applies to
the published table. A final bounded fold search on this new layout
found 53 preliminary pairs and no valid additional fold.

\subsection{Safe logical variants and unsafe shortcuts}

Independent role permutations in the six surviving heads were exhausted:
all $6^6=46,656$ choices were tested with the inherited association. The
minimum remained the current 159-node full program, uniquely at the
current assignment. A further 23,040 safe variants combined 720 orders
of the six heads, three independent exchanges of implication antecedents,
the two orders of the gate's vacuity conjuncts, and a global equality
argument reversal. On the inherited 201-leaf association, the original
68-node axiom body was again the unique minimum. These facts concern that
association, not arbitrary source programs or all grammar choices.

One instructive failed simplification illustrates why the source contracts
must be read exactly. The macro denoted $P$ pushes the current accumulator
\emph{and clears it}. It is not a duplicator. Replacing a repeated formula
construction by $P$ would therefore corrupt the accumulator, even though
the suggested saving looks natural in a postfix formula tree. The
existing $P,i$ sequence constructs the constant one precisely because
$P$ clears the accumulator.

Replacing the classical implicational head by a shorter-looking principle
also requires a new completeness proof. Bounded proof searches did not
supply such a proof. No such change is used in an accepted table.

There is a concrete obstruction to dropping the first head and recovering
it from its classical dual or from a negative gate. Assign truth to code
zero, interpret implication by Boolean implication, interpret every
universal formula as true, and let both atomic constructors hold exactly
when their numerical labels agree. Then code one is true, but
$\M(0,1)=9$ is false. All remaining heads, the negative gate, modus
ponens, and generalization preserve truth, whereas the first head with
$D=0,H=9$ is false. The exceptional identity $\I(0,0)=0$ is respected.
Thus the proposed recovery fails in this calculus; invoking explosion
or double-negation elimination to repair it would assume the missing
bootstrap. The archive gives the all-instance argument and an executable
supplementary check.

\section{What ConciseIPRA contributes}

The ConciseIPRA work~\cite{ipra} supplies useful representation principles:
infer redundant certificate fields, share a syntax traversal, derive
equality laws from a restricted transport operation, and obtain freshness
from syntactic bounds. Several of these principles already have close
analogues in the PA196 proof-command design.

Its optimization objective is the size of a proof checker in a chosen
software presentation. That metric does not directly measure two-symbol
Turing-machine states. More importantly, replacing the target calculus by
one for iPRA would change the consistency statement. A proposed route
through a stronger intuitionistic theory would require explicit proof
translations relating it to full PA and a concrete compiled state count.
No sub-196 result follows merely from the compactness of an iPRA checker.

\section{Reproduction and evidence boundaries}

The archive separates the accepted published inputs from exploratory
search results. Its top-level README gives commands for:
\begin{enumerate}
\item checking hashes, rooted canonical classes, source control-flow
  agreement, and native-label maps;
\item replaying the independent Python symbolic contracts;
\item rebuilding the standard-library-only Lean physical proof and its
  axiom audit with Lean 4.35.0;
\item compiling this manuscript twice with \file{pdflatex}.
\end{enumerate}

The generated receipts identify which commands were actually run here.
The full original arithmetic development is retained as a pinned source
reference, rather than represented as a new rebuild. Search heuristics and
solver output are research artifacts. In particular, a solver's bound
for a restricted grammar model must not be advertised as a lower bound
for Turing machines satisfying equation~\eqref{eq:target}.

\section{Further research questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Can an axiom-word grammar and its state-sharing decisions be optimized
  jointly, including the return-scanner constraints and context-dependent
  leaf routers? Optimizing them separately can miss a saving.
\item Can a changed association preserve all primitive instruction
  endpoints while exposing a seventh compatible fold in the axiom body?
  This would suffice if no additional states were introduced elsewhere.
\item Can a source-conditioned invariant safely relax one universal
  branch contract and free a useful physical row? Such an invariant must
  cover startup and be inductive through the complete scheduler.
\item Is a preserving duplicate macro worthwhile after accounting for
  the register pairing routine, its scratch registers, and actual reader
  sharing? The clearing push macro cannot substitute for it.
\item Can the syntactic reverse proof compiler of
  Section~\ref{sec:reverse-consistency} be formalized against the exact
  published arithmetic trace predicate, yielding an explicit Lean theorem
  of the PA-provable biconditional?
\item Does a different PA-equivalent calculus give a cheaper axiom supplier
  after compilation, with both directions of proof translation explicit?
  A shorter formula list alone is insufficient evidence.
\end{enumerate}
