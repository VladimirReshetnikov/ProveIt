\begin{abstract}
The updated task is to find a justified construction with fewer than the
208 working states already supplied in two later commits. This dossier
does \textbf{not establish a 207-state machine}. It compares the proposals,
freshly replays both final 208-state machine classes, and records additional
research with reproducible scope and explicit proof boundaries.

The five U208 packets contain nine presentations of only two machines.
Every packet uses the same conditional-history identification of native
states 172 and 211; their savings cannot be added. Beyond that existing
fold, we prove a finite-model obstruction to globally identifying the cover
emitter's source and output roles, exhaust all 279,936 independent parameter
permutations in seven axiom rows (minimum source DAG size 197), and search
broader local reductions, including pairs whose two outgoing transitions
both change. No accepted additional deletion is claimed. The accompanying
code, exact inputs, receipts, and rejected examples make the successful
checks and negative search results independently reviewable.
\end{abstract}
