\section{The actual U213 bootstrap and its protected arbitrary tail}
\label{sec:u213-bootstrap}

The shortened initializer reaches an interior point of AX.  The appropriate
source lemma starts after the ordinary generalized-cache construction; it
does not identify this run with a complete AX invocation.  This distinction
is essential because the seed below has cache value $1$, which is used only
as an arithmetic parameter during this suffix, and has top $0$ in the place
where a complete AX would have put a quantified code.

\subsection{Literal physical frame and finite source prefix}

For the exact 213-state native table with SHA-256
\nolinkurl{bb4f5ac12b29d07d1080109af9bb564e9e11a4783efc48cd73b8f826789d01f7},
the blank run after $48{,}140$ binary steps
has native state $84$, head coordinate $-2$, and register header coordinate
$54$.  The header is thus $56$ cells to the right of the dispatcher head.
Its source address is $001100110101010$, source ordinal $136$, and its
logical register store, in order $(N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)$, is
\[
 (1,0,13,0,0,1,0,0,1).
\]
There are $43$ represented spare registers, each equal to $1$.  The exact
whole-tape check includes the address, all separators, the register header,
all register and spare blocks, the head and state, and zero exterior; there
are $120$ nonzero tape cells.  These are literal finite facts checked by
the startup replay, separately from the universal primitive simulation.

Starting instead at source ordinal $106$ with
\begin{equation}
 (N,T,W,A,D,H,X,Y,L)=(1,0,4,0,0,1,0,0,1),
 \label{eq:u213-synthetic-seed}
\end{equation}
exactly $141$ source primitive steps reach ordinal $136$ with that same
logical store.  Determinism therefore identifies the continuation of the
observed physical frame with the continuation of this interior source
seed, once the whole-tape simulation theorem is applied.  Neither the
number $4$ nor the incoming cache $1$ is assumed to be theorem-valued.

\subsection{Uniform suffix lemma}

Use $P(x,y)=(x+y)(x+y+1)/2+y$, $I(x,y)=4P(x,y)$,
$R(x,y)=4P(x,y)+1$, $Q(x,y)=4P(x,y)+2$, and
$E(x,y)=4P(x,y)+3$.  Put $\neg x=I(x,1)$ and
$\exists_x y=\neg Q(x,\neg y)$.  A displayed stack
$[x_1,\ldots,x_k;J]$ means iterated pairing above the arbitrary
natural tail $J$; its explicit length is a ghost component of the
invariant, not a claim that integer codes determine list length.

For all natural $n,z,J,c,u,h,x,y,\ell$, consider the exact source at
ordinal $106$ with store
\[
 (n,z,J,c,u,h,0,0,P(y,P(x,\ell))).
\]
Put $a=u+x$ and $b=h+y$.  The source suffix terminates normally and,
after its two return primitives, reaches scheduler ordinal $0$ with
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 N&=n,\qquad L=\ell,\qquad A=D=H=X=Y=0,\\
 T&=\begin{cases}0,&c=a\text{ or }c=b,\\B^*(a,b,c),&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}\\
 W&=[r_1(a,b,c),\ldots,r_7(a,b,c),
        \Lambda(a,b,c,u),z;J].
 \end{split}
 \label{eq:u213-suffix-contract}
\end{equation}
Here the seven rows are the retained numerical logical axioms, and
\[
 \Lambda(a,b,c,u)=
 I\bigl(I(I(c,u),a),I(I(a,c),I(b,c))\bigr).
\]
Its domination premise $u\leq a$ follows from $a=u+x$.
The role-reassigned arithmetic bundle $B^*$ is exactly the primary
U214 bundle.  In the actual bootstrap $c=a=0$, so its computed value
is discarded by the gate.

\[
 \begin{array}{c|l|l}

         \text{push old top; load }D,H,A\text{ respectively}\\

         \text{read a Cantor field into }H,D\text{ respectively}.

The statements are in \texttt{ProgLibPerm.v}.  Each gives termination
and the exact store effect for all natural inputs with scratch $X=Y=0$;

tree depths differ, and commuting consecutive increments only when
there is no branch into the second increment.  It checks this condition
against the complete source graph as well as the local graph.

The first field read is precisely $[158,172)$ and the second is

The only suffix instructions writing $L$ are ordinals
$158,167,199,208$.  No suffix instruction writes $N$.
These checks prevent an unnoticed residual proof word or changed

\paragraph{Gate and return.}
The five gate calls are exact bindings to
\texttt{w192} on $[3732,3736)$,
\texttt{ifeq131} on $[3736,3748)$,
\texttt{w211} on $[3748,3751)$,
\texttt{w197} on $[3751,3754)$, and
\texttt{ifeq131} on $[3754,3766)$.
The first consumes selector $c$ into two scratch copies.  The first
comparison clears $T$ exactly when $c=b$, clearing its comparison
registers in either case.  The two transfer loops move saved $c$ to
$X$ and $a$ to $H$, zeroing their sources; the second comparison clears
$T$ exactly when $c=a$.  Thus $A,D,H,X,Y$ all finish zero, and $W,N,L$
are unchanged throughout the gate.  The final source instructions are
\texttt{CB1} at $3766$, which targets $3873$, followed by
\texttt{CC3} at $3873$, which targets scheduler $0$.

Every macro used has a terminating all-natural contract proved by
ordinary induction.  Their finite sequential composition proves
\eqref{eq:u213-suffix-contract}; the finite binding audit establishes
that it is the contract of this exact source interval.  This is stronger
than testing the suffix on a few small numerical stores.

\paragraph{Application to the observed seed.}
For \eqref{eq:u213-synthetic-seed}, take
\[
 n=1,\quad z=0,\quad J=4,\quad c=u=0,\quad h=1,
 \quad y=1,\quad x=\ell=0.
\]
Indeed $P(1,P(0,0))=1$.  The two field reads therefore produce
$a=0$, $b=2$, and $L=0$.  At the resulting scheduler root,
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
 N=1,\qquad L=0,\qquad T=H=D=A=X=Y=0,\\
 W=[r_1(0,2,0),\ldots,r_7(0,2,0),\Lambda(0,2,0,0),0;4].
 \label{eq:u213-safe-root}
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
All nine displayed heads are numerical theorems: seven logical rows,
a dominated propositional row with $0\leq0$, and truth $0$.
The old cache value $1$ has only helped specify parameter $b=2$; it is
not among these heads.  No generalized theorem of falsity is asserted.

\subsection{All-natural protected-credit proof}

The stack tail $4$ is unsafe if exposed: $4=P(1,1)$ and $1=P(1,0)$.
Its safety therefore requires a prefix bound for the entire scheduler,
not merely for a finite sample of candidate programs.  The following
argument is the inherited three-command credit theorem, applied to the
actual nine-head root.

Let $f$ be the right Cantor projection, and
$T_s=s(s+1)/2$.  The exact source parser has command fields
\[
 S:[0],\qquad \operatorname{MP}(x):[1,x],\qquad
 \operatorname{AX}(c;x,y):[c+2,y,x].
\]
Let $W(n)$ be the finite syntactic command word parsed from a fresh
candidate $n$, ignoring a possible earlier semantic halt.  Define
$R(0)=[]$ and $R(r)=W(r-1)$ for $r>0$; the subtraction is the scheduler's
decrement before a residual command.  If $\operatorname{unpair}(n)=(a,y)$,
the source contracts give the exact recurrences
\[
 W(n)=\begin{cases}
 S;R(y),&a=0,\\
 \operatorname{MP};R(f(y)),&a=1,\\
 \operatorname{AX};R(f(f(y))),&a\geq2.
 \end{cases}
\]
These words are defined by descent, since a nonempty continuation has
candidate index strictly below $n$.  Induction gives $|W(n)|\leq n+1$,
hence $|R(r)|\leq r$.

Assign conservative weights $S=-1$, MP$=0$, AX$=10$.  Successful MP's
additional truth-head push is ignored.  Let $b(n)$ be the weight of
$W(n)$, $B(k)=\sum_{n<k}b(n)$, and $g(0)=0$, $g(r)=b(r-1)$ for $r>0$.
Every prefix of $R(r)$ has weight at least $-r$.

The projection sequence $f(0),f(1),\ldots$ is the concatenation
$[0],[0,1],[0,1,2],\ldots$.  Thus a sequence $q$ with nonnegative
initial partial sums retains that property after pullback along $f$,
and after two such pullbacks: each initial segment consists of complete
blocks and one initial partial block.  We apply this fact to
$q(r)=g(r)+1$, whose partial sum of positive length $k$ is
\[
 \sum_{r<k}q(r)=k+B(k-1).
\]

Induct jointly on Cantor diagonals to establish
$B(k)+k\geq0$ at every candidate boundary, $B(T_s)\geq-3$, and
nonnegative relative weight at every command prefix of every diagonal
$s\geq2$.  The exceptional initial words are
\[
 W(0)=[S],\qquad W(1)=[\operatorname{MP}],\qquad W(2)=[S,S],
\]
so $B(0),B(1),B(2),B(3)$ are $0,-1,-1,-3$ and the base holds.

On diagonal $s\geq2$, candidates $T_s+y$ for $0\leq y\leq s-2$
start with AX, the next starts with MP, and the last with $S$.
By the pullback lemma, the first $k$ AX candidates have total weight
\[
 10k+\sum_{y<k}g(f(f(y)))\geq9k.
\]
Only earlier candidate inequalities are used: the relevant projection
arguments are at most $s-2<T_s$.  Before AX candidate $y$ the relative
balance is at least $9y$.  After its initial AX, every prefix of its
continuation has balance at least $9y+10-y=8y+10>0$.
After the first $s-1$ candidates the balance is at least $9(s-1)$;
the MP continuation loses at most $s-1$.  The final $S$ and its
continuation lose at most $1+s$.  Their lower bound is
\[
 9(s-1)-(s-1)-1-s=7s-9>0.
\]
This proves prefix safety and nonnegative total weight on the whole
diagonal.  Since its starting balance is at least $-3$, the new
candidate boundaries satisfy $B(k)\geq-3$ and, for $k\geq3$,
$B(k)+k\geq0$.  The induction closes without an assumption about a
future diagonal.

Starting at $N=1$ with empty residual pointer, the only initial debt is
the word $W(1);W(2)=[\operatorname{MP},S,S]$, of minimum prefix
weight $-2$.  Every following complete diagonal is prefix-safe.
Thus the exact conservative required credit is $d(1)=2$.
The nine heads in \eqref{eq:u213-safe-root} leave a permanent
conservative margin of seven.

Maintain a finite list of explicit theorem heads above fixed arbitrary
tail $J$.  Ordinary AX prepends ten theorem heads, failed MP changes
none, successful MP adds theorem $0$, and a nonhalting $S$ removes one.
The conservative bound ensures an explicit head is available before
every $S$; no pop reaches $J$.  The top and cache stay theorem-valued,
and the only halt tests cache $1$, so a halt gives a numerical
refutation.  Pairing aliases cause no gap: the identity
$\operatorname{unpair}(P(x,v))=(x,v)$ holds also at $P(0,0)=0$,
and the ghost wrapper length need not be recoverable from the integer
stack code.  If a halt happens earlier, this argument applies to its
finite syntactic prefix.

For the converse, the strong compiler works from every numerical
top and stack after an AX reset of counter and cache.  Its successful
candidate codes are cofinal, so retaining an opaque tail and beginning
at $N=1$ does not change completeness.  Together with the exact
bootstrap and universal primitive simulation, this proves the
candidate's halting equivalence with numerical refutability.  The
primary U214 calibration then supplies refutability equivalence with
$\neg\operatorname{Con}(U)$ and $\neg\operatorname{Con}(\mathsf{PA})$.
All arguments here are finite syntactic constructions and induction on
natural numbers.  They give an ordinary PA-formalizable proof; this
packet does not assert a newly checked proof-assistant endpoint for U213.
