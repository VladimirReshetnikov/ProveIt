third-row actions. The third stage extends 5,097
representative-context near misses. No survivor is reported. The larger
counts do not strengthen the simulation template beyond its stated scope.
In particular, all fourteen radius-six closures admit an abstract halt;
they are safe overapproximations, not nonhalting proofs.

A synthetic four-state positive control uses exactly the same C++ search
logic and finds seventeen reported reductions: one one-row, five two-row,
and eleven three-row repairs. A separately written Python simulator checks
all 2,176 control/window obligations for these outputs, including every
original state and all thirty-two five-cell windows. This provides a
nonvacuity check of the implementation, not a completeness proof of the search.
