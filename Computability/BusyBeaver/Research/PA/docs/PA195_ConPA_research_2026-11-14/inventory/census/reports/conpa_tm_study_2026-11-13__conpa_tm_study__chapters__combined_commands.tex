\section{A complete command compiler for the combined Shift and MP changes}
\label{sec:combined-command-compiler}

This section supplies the source-level correctness argument for combining two
changes: Shift tests only the cached head, and successful modus ponens leaves
its result in that cache.  The underlying axiom calculus and all AX formulas
are exactly those of the pinned PA196 source.  The argument proves equivalence
of the proof-search problems; a physical state count and tape simulation are
separate obligations.  In particular, none of the command-word counts below
is a Turing-machine state count.

\subsection{The changed arithmetic commands}

Write a store as $\langle t,w,d,h\rangle$.  Let
$\langle a,w\rangle_C$ denote Cantor stack cons, and let
$\operatorname{hd},\operatorname{tl}$ be its total inverse projections;
both projections of zero are zero.  Retain
\[
 I(a,b)=4p(a,b),\qquad U(a,B)=4p(B,a)+2
\]
and the other PA196 formula constructors.  A command word is read from left
to right.  The modified Shift, written $S$, is
\[
 S\langle t,w,d,h\rangle=
 \begin{cases}
  \mathsf{halt},&h=1,\\
  \langle h,\operatorname{tl}(w),d,\operatorname{hd}(w)\rangle,&h\ne1.
 \end{cases}
\]
For $y=d+x$, the modified modus ponens command is
\[
 \operatorname{MP}_x\langle t,w,d,h\rangle=
 \begin{cases}
  \langle0,w,0,y\rangle,&I(t,y)=h,\\
  \langle0,w,y,0\rangle,&I(t,y)\ne h.
 \end{cases}
\]
Both branches have a defined, terminating arithmetic effect.  Only Shift
can halt.  AX is unchanged.  Put $D=d+x$, $H=h+y$, $A=c$, and let its eight
generated stack entries, listed from the top downward, be
\begin{align*}
 q_1&=\operatorname{head}_6(D,A,H),&
 q_2&=\operatorname{head}_5(D,H),\\
 q_3&=\operatorname{head}_4(D,A,H),&
 q_4&=\operatorname{head}_3(D,A,H),\\
 q_5&=\operatorname{head}_2(D,A,H),&
 q_6&=\operatorname{head}_1(D,H),\\
 q_7&=\rho_8(D,A,H,d),&q_8&=U(d,h).
\end{align*}
Then
\[
 \operatorname{AX}_{c,x,y}\langle t,w,d,h\rangle
 =\langle\operatorname{gate}(H,D,c),
       [q_1,\ldots,q_8,t]\mathbin{::}_C w,0,0\rangle.
\]
Here a list before $\mathbin{::}_C w$ means repeated Cantor cons onto $w$.
The first seven generated entries are divisible by four.  The eighth is
congruent to two modulo four.  The guarded gate is divisible by four as well,
including when it is zero.  Thus none of these generated values is one.

\subsection{Stack words and the changed pop contract}

The following two identities hold for every incoming store, with no
restriction on its saved top $t$:
\begin{align}
 \operatorname{AX}_{c,x,y};S^8:
 \langle t,w,d,h\rangle
 &\longmapsto
 \langle\rho_8(d+x,c,h+y,d),\langle t,w\rangle_C,0,U(d,h)\rangle,
 \label{eq:combined-ax8}\\
 \operatorname{AX}_{c,x,y};S^9:
 \langle t,w,d,h\rangle
 &\longmapsto\langle U(d,h),w,0,t\rangle.
 \label{eq:combined-ax9}
\end{align}
Indeed the first Shift tests the newly cleared zero cache; each later test
up to and including the ninth tests one of $q_1,\ldots,q_8$, never the saved
top.  This is why the second identity remains total even for $t=1$.

Define
\begin{align*}
 \mathsf{Push}&=\operatorname{AX}_{0,0,0};S^8;\operatorname{MP}_0,\\
 \mathsf{Pop}&=\operatorname{AX}_{0,0,0};S^{10},\\
 \mathsf{Discard}&=\operatorname{MP}_1;\operatorname{AX}_{1,0,0};S^9;
                    \operatorname{MP}_0.
\end{align*}
Their precise contracts are
\begin{align}
 \mathsf{Push}\langle t,w,d,h\rangle
   &=\langle0,\langle t,w\rangle_C,0,0\rangle,
   \label{eq:combined-push}\\
 \mathsf{Pop}\langle t,\langle a,w\rangle_C,d,h\rangle
   &=\begin{cases}\mathsf{halt},&t=1,\\
                  \langle t,w,0,a\rangle,&t\ne1,
      \end{cases}
   \label{eq:combined-pop}\\
 \mathsf{Discard}\langle t,w,0,0\rangle
   &=\langle0,w,0,0\rangle.
   \label{eq:combined-discard}
\end{align}
For Push, after~\eqref{eq:combined-ax8} the equality tested by
$\operatorname{MP}_0$ compares an implication code, congruent to zero, with
$U(d,h)$, congruent to two.  It therefore takes the failure branch and has
exactly the stated effect.  For Pop, the tenth Shift is precisely the test
of the saved top.  For Discard, the first MP fails since $I(t,1)>0$, giving
$\langle0,w,1,0\rangle$.  Identity~\eqref{eq:combined-ax9} then gives
$\langle U(1,0),w,0,0\rangle$, and the last MP fails since $I(U(1,0),0)>0$.

The old claim that Pop is total for arbitrary $t$ must therefore be replaced
by~\eqref{eq:combined-pop}.  An early halt when $t=1$ is harmless in the
refutation compiler, but this change cannot be silently omitted.

For $u\in\mathbb N$, let $\mathsf{Offset}_0$ be the empty word and let
$\mathsf{Offset}_u=\operatorname{MP}_u$ when $u>0$.  Then
\[
 \mathsf{Offset}_u\langle0,w,0,0\rangle=\langle0,w,u,0\rangle.
\]
This follows from $I(0,u)>0$ for $u>0$.  The old generalization word remains
valid without alteration:
\[
 \mathsf{Gen}_i=\mathsf{Push};\mathsf{Offset}_i;S;
          \operatorname{AX}_{i,0,0};S^9,
 \qquad
 \mathsf{Gen}_i\langle a,w,0,0\rangle
       =\langle U(i,a),w,0,0\rangle.
\]
The one Shift before AX reads a zero cache.  It retrieves $a$ into the cache;
AX then constructs its generalization.  The proof also covers $a=1$.

\subsection{All axiom selectors remain total}

For $1\le j\le6$, define
\[
 \mathsf{Select}_{j,D,H,A}=
 \mathsf{Discard};\operatorname{AX}_{A,D,H};S^{j+1};\mathsf{Pop}^{8-j}.
\]
From $\langle t,w,0,0\rangle$ this produces
$\langle q_j,w,0,0\rangle$, with the $q_j$ evaluated at the intended
parameters.  After $S^{j+1}$ the selected $q_j$ is the top, and the remaining
stack is $[q_{j+2},\ldots,q_8,0]\mathbin{::}_C w$ (with the evident empty
range at the end).  The following Pops preserve $q_j$, remove that list,
and leave the final zero in the cache.  Every such Pop is nonhalting because
$q_j\ne1$.  The intermediate cache, which may contain another formula,
is cleared by the next AX before any Shift reads it.

For $u\le a$, the unchanged restricted-propositional selector is
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Offset}_u;
 \operatorname{AX}_{b,a-u,c};S^8;\mathsf{Pop}.
\]
It returns $\langle\rho_8(a,b,c,u),w,0,0\rangle$.  The final Pop is safe
because its preserved top is an implication code.  The unchanged gate
selector
\[
 \mathsf{Discard};\operatorname{AX}_{c,b,a};\mathsf{Pop}^9
\]
returns $\langle\operatorname{gate}(a,b,c),w,0,0\rangle$.  Its preserved
top is again different from one.  Under the original guard inequalities this
is the intended bundle axiom; under a failed guard it is the harmless code
zero.  Thus every primitive axiom of the exact PA196 calculus still has a
total selector that preserves an arbitrary underlying stack.

\subsection{The new modus ponens suffix}

Successful MP now puts the conclusion in $h$, so the old cleanup suffix is
inapplicable.  A dummy zero stack entry gives a shorter correct suffix:
\[
 \mathsf{MPSuffix}_b=S;S;\operatorname{MP}_b;S.
\]
Its complete contract is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:combined-mp-suffix}
 \mathsf{MPSuffix}_b
 \langle0,[a,I(a,b),0]\mathbin{::}_C w,0,0\rangle
 =\begin{cases}
    \mathsf{halt},&a=1\text{ or }b=1,\\
    \langle b,w,0,0\rangle,&a\ne1\text{ and }b\ne1.
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}
After the first Shift, the cache is $a$.  If $a\ne1$, the second Shift
gives $\langle a,\langle0,w\rangle_C,0,I(a,b)\rangle$.  MP succeeds,
giving $\langle0,\langle0,w\rangle_C,0,b\rangle$.  The final Shift
either halts at $b=1$ or returns $b$ as the top while consuming exactly the
dummy zero.  No part of the arbitrary underlying stack is consumed.
The dummy entry is essential: omitting it consumes the first entry of $w$
and fails the compiler's stack-preservation invariant.

\subsection{A primitive recursive compiler and universal halting word}

Let $\mathcal C$ be the unchanged numeric calculus.  A proof is a finite tree
whose nodes are primitive axioms, modus ponens, or theorem generalization.
Define $W_p$ recursively.  At an axiom, use the appropriate selector above.
At generalization $p=\forall_i p_0$, put
\[
 W_p=W_{p_0};\mathsf{Gen}_i.
\]
At modus ponens, let $p_0$ prove $a$ and let $p_1$ prove $I(a,b)$, and put
\[
 W_p=\mathsf{Discard};\mathsf{Push};
     W_{p_1};\mathsf{Push};W_{p_0};\mathsf{Push};\mathsf{MPSuffix}_b.
\]
The first two words introduce the dummy zero.  Before the suffix, the three
protected entries are exactly $a$, $I(a,b)$, and zero.

\begin{lemma}[Compilation invariant]
If $p$ is a $\mathcal C$ derivation of $a$, then for every $t,w$,
the word $W_p$ from $\langle t,w,0,0\rangle$ either halts or returns
$\langle a,w,0,0\rangle$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the proof tree.  The axiom and generalization cases use the total
contracts already proved.  In the MP case, Discard and Push give
$\langle0,\langle0,w\rangle_C,0,0\rangle$.  Apply the major-premise
induction hypothesis at that stack and Push its result.  Apply the
minor-premise induction hypothesis at the enlarged stack and Push again.
An early halt persists under appending commands.  Otherwise the suffix
precondition is exact, and~\eqref{eq:combined-mp-suffix} proves the claim.
\end{proof}

If $p$ proves code one, define
\[
 \mathsf{HaltWord}_p=\operatorname{AX}_{0,0,0};W_p;\mathsf{Pop}.
\]
The first AX clears both caches of any incoming store, although it need not
erase the top or stack.  The compiler is independent of those values.  If it
returns normally, its top is one, and Pop halts by its tenth Shift before
inspecting the old underlying stack.  Thus $\mathsf{HaltWord}_p$ halts from
\emph{every} incoming store.  This is the form required by the existing word
enumerator: no assumption that its previous trials restore the zero store
is needed.

\subsection{Soundness and PA-provable calibration}

Use the following syntactic store invariant: $t$, $h$, and every finitely
decoded stack entry have $\mathcal C$ derivations.  It places no condition
on the offset register $d$.  Code zero has a fixed derivation: the instance
$\rho_8(0,0,0,0)$ is numerically zero because $I(0,0)=0$.  Thus a zero stack
tail can be padded by zero entries indefinitely.

AX preserves the invariant.  Its saved top is already derivable, its six
heads and guarded gate are primitive axioms or zero, its restricted
propositional head satisfies $d\le d+x$, and $U(d,h)$ follows by theorem
generalization from the old cache $h$.  A failed MP returns zero.  A successful
MP obtains $y$ from the derivable formulas $t$ and $I(t,y)=h$ and stores that
derived formula in $h$.  A nonhalting Shift merely moves proven formulas
between the top, cache, and stack.  A halting Shift exposes a derivation of
one in $h$.  Therefore a finite run from the clean store can halt only if
$\mathcal C$ has a refutation.

The converse follows from the universal halting word and the unchanged
exhaustive scheduler.  The axiom-selector words, proof-tree compiler,
command encodings, and extraction of a refutation from a finite run are
primitive recursive.  Their correctness proofs use arithmetic induction on
finite lists, proof trees, and finite traces.  Hence PA proves, at the
command-program level,
\[
 \operatorname{Halt}(R_{\mathrm{combined}})
 \quad\longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists p\;\operatorname{Prf}_{\mathcal C}(p,1).
\]
Composing with the existing PA-to-$\mathcal C$ refutation translation and the
written syntactic $\mathcal C$-to-PA interpretation gives
\[
 \mathrm{PA}\vdash
 \neg\operatorname{Halt}(R_{\mathrm{combined}})
       \longleftrightarrow\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{PA}).
\]
For a particular binary table, the startup, source binding, and positive
instruction simulations must additionally identify its blank-tape halting
with the displayed source-program halting inside PA.  A collection of finite
run samples alone would not establish that identification.  This section is
a written proof and executable command-level check, not a claim of a new
Lean kernel theorem for the modified table.

\paragraph{Executable supplement.}
The accompanying \texttt{check\_commands.py} checks 64 symbolic contract
cases.  It expands all eight axiom-head expressions, treats the unchanged
guarded gate by its exact parameter list and residue property, and uses
opaque arbitrary stack tails.  It distinguishes saved top one from an
arbitrary non-one value, tests all nine zero/one/other MP cases, and rejects
an equality if its truth is not forced by constructor equality, zero facts,
or residues.  It therefore does not choose an unknown branch by fiat.
The general proof above supplies the universal statement; the script is
an independent finite check of its command-word algebra.
