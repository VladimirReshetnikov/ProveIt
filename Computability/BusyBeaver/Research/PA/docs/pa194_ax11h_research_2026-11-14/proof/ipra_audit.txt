1. CENTRAL FINDING

The premise that the supplied literal 194-state tables already have an
established iPRA proof of their PA-consistency equivalence is not supported
by the pinned repository. PA/README.md explicitly says neither machine-
specific direction has been established in iPRA for primary PA194, original
PA195, or PA196. The new incoming packets advertise ordinary written PA
calibrations, not proofs in iPRA. This is not evidence that the desired
statement is false, and absence of a Lean formalization alone would not
refute an adequate ordinary mathematical proof. The actual issue is that
the inherited mathematical argument explicitly concludes PA-provability,
and its formal counterpart uses unrestricted induction at essential
uniformity steps. An unqualified iPRA claim requires another argument.

3. WHY THE WRITTEN PA CALIBRATION CANNOT JUST BE RELABELLED

The incoming primary report's calibration.tex explicitly concludes
PA proves Nonhalt(PA194) iff Con(PA). It uses primitive-recursive syntactic
operations but proves their correctness by PA induction. Primitive-
recursiveness of a function by itself does NOT imply that an arbitrary
correctness statement about it has a PRA/iPRA proof. Likewise, a standard
finite-run equivalence does not quantify over nonstandard times in models
of IΣ1. One needs a weak-arithmetic correctness argument.

The two headline examples in pa194-forward-audit are genuine:

- jamesyc-upstream/pa196/PA196/Arithmetic/Transport/
  NumericPAeqSequentDerivation.lean:
  ordinary_derivation_requested performs C := Set.univ induction on
  forall d<n, Derivation(d) -> forall f, SourceProofBudget(d,f) ->
  RequestedCertificate(f, root(d)). RequestedCertificate itself universally
  quantifies native environments and allocation parameters before asserting
  existence of a certificate. This is not presented as Σ1.

- jamesyc-upstream/pa196/PA196/Arithmetic/CompilerInvariant.lean:
  fuel_invariant performs C := Set.univ induction on
  forall bound, TableRealizes(checkerTable(fuel,bound), compilerTable(...)).
  The latter includes unbounded store/environment requirements.

A direct textual audit found 88 distinct files with explicit
C := Set.univ inductions in the imported PA196/Arithmetic and
M6PA/Calibration trees: 32 Transport, 12 Scheduler, 10 Machine,
3 Certificates, 1 CompilerInvariant, and 30 M6PA/Calibration files.
The exact paths and local contexts are in proof/full_pa_inductions.json.
This is a syntactic count, NOT a claim that 88 independent deep obstacles
remain. Many syntax equations should admit Π1 induction or bounded
witness-table replacements, and several have newer IΣ1 replacements.
But auditing only the two headline wrappers would miss real dependencies.

4. A SUFFICIENT CONSTRUCTIVE CRITERION FOR THE REQUESTED RESULT

Use a primitive-recursive PA proof predicate R(p) and a primitive-recursive
bounded machine halting predicate H_M(t), with terminal states totalized
absorbingly. Suppose explicit primitive-recursive functions F,G satisfy,
with proofs in iPRA,

  R(p) -> H_M(F(p)),
  H_M(t) -> R(G(t)).

Then iPRA proves (forall t, not H_M(t)) iff (forall p, not R(p)). The proof
is just two intuitionistically valid contraposition arguments, using the
explicit witnesses F and G. It needs neither Markov's principle nor a
truth/reflection principle for PA. This is the cleanest sufficient target
for a future fully executable proof packet.

Alternatively, proving the existential equivalence in IΣ1 and applying
the repository's already checked transfer suffices. IΣ1 syntax is often
more convenient than trying to internalize the whole simulator directly
in the specific candidate-PRA language.

5. CONCRETE WAY TO LOWER THE PARAMETERIZED COMPILERS

The source does NOT use cut elimination: NumericPAeqSequentDerivationStep
handles all ten LK rules including a cut case with two child certificates.
Preserving cut avoids a major known source of proof-size explosion.
The inspected quantifier steps request child derivations at explicitly
computed environments (eigenEnvironment, padding, or tail); these requests
are determined by input syntax and allocation, rather than unbounded proof
search. Thus enormous formula codes alone do not force non-primitive-
recursive growth: an exponential tower of input-dependent finite height
is still primitive recursive.

A useful exact replacement principle is a FINITE REQUEST TABLE.

For one fixed input proof and one fixed requested allocation, build the
finite directed request graph of actual recursive child requests. Store
with each node: its source subcode, its environment, the already computed
child indices, and an output certificate. A local certificate check is
primitive recursive. Correctness of an entire finite table is therefore a
bounded/Δ1 condition (or an open predicate after adding the relevant PR
symbols). Existence of a correct table is Σ1, so IΣ1 can induct on its
construction. A separate finite frame/renaming lemma supplies any desired
uniform conclusion after the table is built. One never inducts on a
formula of the form 'for every environment there exists a proof'.

A concrete example is the imported NNF certificate construction in
ActualCertificateEquivalenceInduction.lean. Its only parameter change
under a quantifier is param -> advance(param), while source subcodes
strictly decrease. For input source code p and parameter a, store entries
(q,k) with q+k <= p. Entry (q,k) is a pair of certificates comparing the
translation of source negation at q, using advance^k(a), with the target
negation of the translation. Process q in increasing order. A quantifier
child q'<q needs (q',k+1), and

  q' + (k+1) <= q+k <= p.

A Boolean child needs (q',k), also in the table. Thus the entire dependency
set is finite with at most (p+1)(p+2)/2 entries, including nonstandard p
internally. The required translation equalities and fixed local proof
constructors yield each entry from earlier entries. A coded table witness
makes the induction Σ1. This is a genuine method to replace that stronger
unbounded-parameter induction; it is not a claim that all actual target
certificate interfaces have already been ported or checked in this packet.

For the full compiler, one needs explicit bounds for all parameter
changes and local constructors. A sufficient majorization scheme is:
all child-request parameters are bounded by a monotone PR function G of
the parent parameters, recursion depth is bounded by source code d,
branching at each node is bounded by a PR function of those parameters,
and a monotone PR H bounds the output of each local constructor from its
inputs and child certificates. Then iterate G d times to bound request
parameters, bound the number of nodes by B^d for a resulting B, and
iterate H through that many nodes to bound certificates. Everything is PR.
But the premise that child-request formation is independent of generated
certificate values must be proved, not assumed: unrestricted nested
recursive calls can have non-PR growth even when each displayed operation
is PR. This is a concrete audit condition still to discharge across the
full supplier library.

6. PHYSICAL SIMULATION CAN USE POSITIVE SEGMENTS

A useful weak-arithmetic lemma avoids inverse decoding of arbitrary tapes.
Suppose a source computation has a primitive-recursive frame embedding E;
from every valid nonhalting frame, one source step has an exact physical
segment of length tau(c) >= 1 to the next embedded frame; tau and the full
segment witness are PR; source halts map to the physical halt; and the
finite blank startup reaches E(c0) without an earlier halt. Exact endpoint
contracts rule out a premature halt inside a successful segment, by
determinism and the absorbing/partial terminal convention.

Given a physical halting time T, generate T+1 source segments, stopping
if a source halt occurs. If none occurs, their total physical length is
at least T+1 beyond startup and the endpoint is nonhalting, contradicting
the alleged physical halt. This finite bounded process produces a source
halt witness, and if annotated source halts carry PA proof certificates,
it produces a PA refutation. The other direction concatenates the segments
for a supplied source halt. This reasoning is intuitionistic once the
witness generators and their PR invariants are available.

For the finite local folds, tau may have a fixed small bound and all
pattern equalities can be checked exhaustively; the whole-tape lift is then
particularly transparent. For register-machine macro simulation tau is
larger but remains PR if each arithmetic macro and the complete register-
file excursions have explicit total witness generators. A finite list of
sample executions is not the required universal contract.

7. SAFE CLAIM FOR ANY NEW STATE REDUCTION

If a new table N is connected to primary PA194 by an explicit positive-
segment simulation with PR witness transformers and iPRA proofs of their
correctness, one obtains in iPRA the relative halting equivalence
H_N <-> H_PA194 (or equivalence of their negations). If PA194's iPRA calibration is an assumption, then
N's iPRA calibration follows conditionally. This does not discharge the
common PA calibration. Likewise a new 194-state representation with a
changed source command protocol must update its selectors and stack
invariant before invoking the same argument.

A research report should distinguish:
  (i) exact table count/hash/startup and finite universal local certificates;
  (ii) relative source/table halting equivalence and its base arithmetic;
  (iii) ordinary written PA calibration inherited from the source;
  (iv) a proved iPRA calibration, or an explicitly unproved common premise.
Conflating these would turn a meaningful state reduction into an unsupported
claim about the main requested property.

FURTHER RESEARCH DIRECTION

A serious sub-194 construction is valuable even if it inherits the common
calibration obligation, but it must be named a candidate unless a complete
weak-arithmetic argument is supplied. The most promising proof repair is
bounded request tables plus the existing modeled-scheduler results, not
starting a new semantics or replacing the searched theory by iPRA. A
shorter iPRA proof checker recognizes refutations of a weaker theory and
does not automatically yield the required Con(PA) search.
