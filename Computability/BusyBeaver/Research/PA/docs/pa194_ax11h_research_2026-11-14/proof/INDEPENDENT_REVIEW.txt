Independent AX11H command and novelty review
==========================================

Conclusion: the proposed change has a correct all-store refutation compiler
relative to the unchanged numerical calculus C4. The previous AX11 terminal
word must change. This review does not establish the missing iPRA calibration
of C4 refutations to PA refutations, or a new kernel-checked literal-machine
bridge. Do not label the result an unconditional iPRA Con(PA) equivalence.

194 working states, 388 rows, start 0 by the supplied construction convention,

(312,0). native_delta_from_AX11.json records their exact old/new actions.

Key proof correction
Pop=A0;Shift^12 now halts exactly when its incoming visible top is 1;
otherwise it returns (top,tail(stack),0,head(stack)). The first eleven Shifts
are safe for arbitrary incoming stores because the generated heads are
residue 0 or 2 modulo 4. Push, the nine selectors, restricted-rho selection
and generalization retain their prior exact effects.

The reserved-zero MP suffix S;S;MP_b;Pop halts when a=1 or b=1 and otherwise
returns (b,w,0,0). These early halts make the inductive disjunction work:
the compiled proof word either halts or returns its conclusion while
preserving an arbitrary old stack. A calculus refutation e yields the
all-store halting word A0;W_e;Pop. A final plain Shift is wrong for this
protocol, because its cache is zero even when its visible top is one.

The proof fragment report/ax11h_relative_proof.tex gives the selectors, generalization,
MP compiler, sound-store annotation invariant, four-field scheduler encoding,
safe cofinal AX prefix, and primitive-recursive finite-witness transformations.
Zero-stack and zero-implication aliases are included explicitly.

Fresh executable audit
check_words.py imports no compiler, arithmetic oracle or tape simulator.
It checks 31 universal symbolic case identities with explicit non-one facts,
constructor residues and exhaustive equality splits. word_receipt.json is PASS.
The guarded-gate term is treated through its residue-zero/axiom interface;
this file does not reprove that gate's PA calibration.

Exact novelty
check_novelty.py compares exact canonical action tuples, not only digests.
It allows every spanning root, arbitrary control renaming, reflection, global
bit exchange and arbitrary terminal write/move effects. Of the 83 current
frontier objects, 82 have spanning roots; the other is a disconnected
diagnostic and is automatically distinct from this spanning machine.
All 82 exact comparisons return different tuples. exact_novelty.json is PASS
with no matches. Global bit exchange is only a stronger structural screen;
it is not asserted to preserve a zero-blank machine theorem.

Materiality
This changes the recurring halt protocol and its proof compiler. For example,
Shift on (1,w,0,0) halted under AX11 and continues under AX11H; Shift on
(1,w,0,1) does the reverse. That distinction is not renaming or reflection.
Nevertheless AX11 fallthrough and H cache-only testing are both inherited
ideas. The claim is a new verified combination and relative proof, not a new
arithmetical principle or a state-count improvement.

Metadata validation
The final builder and receipts use 3671 primitive sites and 4778 source
outcome contracts. The supplied AX11 predecessor has 3672 sites and 4779
contracts; those predecessor counts must not be copied into AX11H receipts.
The corrected metadata changes no table row.
