\section{Results and the exact claim boundary}
\label{sec:outcome}

The requested target combines two separate requirements: a small literal
Turing table and a consistency equivalence provable in intuitionistic
primitive recursive arithmetic. A state-count certificate or an ordinary
metatheoretic halting equivalence cannot silently discharge the second
requirement. This distinction matters here: the pinned PA README explicitly
states that neither machine-specific direction of the iPRA equivalence has
been established for its primary PA194, original PA195 or PA196 tables.
The generic transfer theorem is available, but its machine-specific
calibration hypothesis remains open.\cite{pa-readme,ipra-generic}

The work reported here has four positive outcomes.
First, a census accounts for all 72 supplied deliveries and their explicit
machine tables, separates duplicates from mechanisms, and distinguishes
principal claims from rejected experiments. Second, PA194AX11H is a
materially different 194-state table: a simultaneous composition of the
AX11 four-operand emitter and H cache test, with a changed proof compiler.
Third, executable symbolic certificates verify its complete source
interface, exact blank startup and all 4,778 literal source outcomes.
Fourth, a universal counterinterpretation refutes a specific raw-axiom
recovery argument for automatic-freshness padding.

advertised as a certified iPRA consistency machine. The term ``PA194AX11H''
identifies its ancestry and intended calibration target.

\section{The new literal machine}
\label{sec:machine}

\subsection{Conventions, size and exact identity}

The machine has one two-way infinite tape with alphabet $\{0,1\}$.
Every cell initially contains zero. Its head starts at position zero in
control zero. There are 194 working controls $0,\ldots,193$ and one terminal
control 194. A row has five fields
\[
 q\quad b\quad w\quad D\quad q',
 \qquad D\in\{L,R\}.
\]
It means: in control $q$ reading $b$, write $w$, move one cell in direction
$D$, and enter $q'$. Entry into 194 halts. The terminal is excluded from
the 194-state count. All 388 working-state/symbol pairs are present exactly
once. All 194 controls are reachable in the action graph from the declared
start; this graph fact does not assert that all occur on the blank run.

Two equivalent labelings are included. The dense table is the authoritative
user-facing object. The native table retains the inherited compiler's
labels, with start 74 and terminal 254. A checked bijection in
\path{machine/labels.json} maps dense labels to native labels and conjugates
every transition.

\subsection{The composition and its five changed rows}

AX11 supplies an eleven-entry axiom bundle that saves the incoming visible
formula and deliberately falls through the full MP-field reader. Successful
MP omits the inherited dummy push. Its fourth AX operand is real data:
the fallthrough consumes it and leaves it in the offset register. H changes
Shift to test the cached formula alone, rather than the sum of visible
formula and cache. Both ingredients already exist separately in the
supplied research; the result here is their new composition and the proof
adaptation it requires.\cite{ax11packet}

Relative to the supplied AX11 table, exactly five native transition rows
change. Here a token $wDq'$ denotes the write, move and destination fields.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{rrrr}
\toprule

\midrule
38 & 0 & $0R28$ & $0R11$\\
38 & 1 & $1R20$ & $1R122$\\
214 & 0 & $0R188$ & $0R312$\\
214 & 1 & $1R312$ & $1R20$\\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

At the source level the change is particularly transparent. In AX11's
expanded source, site 3661 is the instruction
\texttt{inc 6 3662}, incrementing the temporary register $X$ while erasing
the old visible top $T$. Deleting that increment changes the $T$-to-$X$
transfer loop into a $T$-clear loop. The following cache-to-$T/X$ copy is
unchanged, so the final one-test examines $h$ instead of $t+h$.

The new node is exactly
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
node170 = ((node36, (node167, node145)), node57).
\end{quote}
For every retained source index, the relocation is
\[
 \phi(i)=i-\mathbf 1_{i>3661}.
\]
The deleted site's incoming edge targets the new loop jump at 3661.
The independent source binder checks all 3,671 retained instructions and
every resolved edge, using two separately expressed control-flow resolvers.
It also rechecks the inherited primary-to-AX11 splices, so the new change
is bound to the pinned predecessor rather than only compared by name.

\subsection{Why this is a substantive design change}

Write a command store as $(t,w,d,h)$, for visible top, encoded stack,
offset and cached head. On $(1,w,0,0)$ the old sum-based Shift halts,
whereas AX11H's Shift continues and replaces the visible top by zero.
On $(1,w,0,1)$ the sum-based Shift continues, whereas AX11H halts.
These are valid command stores; this observation does not assume that
either is reached on a consistent blank run.

The interaction changes the Pop lemma and the terminal refutation word.
Keeping AX11's compiler verbatim would be wrong. Section~\ref{sec:ax11h-relative}
gives the necessary repair. This is more than relabeling or a differently
chosen initial control, while remaining a combination of established
mechanisms rather than a new arithmetic foundation.

The exact graph audit independently compares complete canonical tuples
against the 83 frontier entries. It allows all spanning roots, arbitrary
state renaming, global reflection, global bit exchange, and arbitrary
write/move effects on terminal transitions. None matches. One prior
diagnostic has no spanning root and therefore cannot be isomorphic to
the spanning AX11H graph. Bit exchange is a deliberately stronger
\emph{structural} test; it does not identify two zero-blank computations.
Hash equality is not used as a substitute for tuple equality in this audit.

\section{Source-to-tape evidence}
\label{sec:physical}

\subsection{Separate construction and independent replay}

The construction-side validator compiles the source tree, applies all
seven folds simultaneously and checks all 4,778 source outcomes.
Its replay takes 1,515,221 symbolic steps, with a largest case of 506
symbolic steps. All 28 two-cell contexts for the seven local folds pass.
The state count is checked on the resulting literal table, not estimated
from source length.

The independent validator imports neither the construction compiler nor
an arithmetic-box oracle. Starting from the supplied literal transitions,
it rederives four generic list identities and then checks each increment,
each jump, each halt, and both outcomes of every decrement. Its own
source graph is resolved in two ways and compared to the recorded source
instructions. It checks nine independent arbitrary natural register
values, an arbitrary affine zero gap $48+n$, and an arbitrary nonempty
finite list of arbitrary natural spare values. These are symbolic
parameters, not a finite sample of small register assignments.

The two step totals count operations of different symbolic proof engines,
not the runtime of a hypothetical PA refutation on the TM. The independent
case receipts include symbolic duration formulas and net motion. The
minimum positive constant of three, together with nonnegative parameters,
is useful for the bounded physical-reflection argument; it is not a
claimed global runtime bound.

\subsection{Complete blank initialization}

The independent startup replay uses a literal tape array, separately from
the builder's startup implementation. After exactly 57,922 transitions
from native start 74 on the all-zero tape, it reaches native control 84,
head position $-2$, at source entry 25. The logical registers are
$(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$. The physical register order is
\[
 (0,0,0,0,0,0,2,0,0),
\]
followed by 26 spare unary blocks of value one, with header gap 48.

The checker constructs the entire expected tape from the source-address
header and register/spare blocks and compares it to the entire simulated
tape. It does not merely find a recognizable local prefix. Every tape
cell visited during the simulation remains inside the checked array,
and the untouched infinite complement is zero. No earlier terminal
entry occurs. The builder receipt also lists every nonzero cell, allowing
an independent literal startup check without trusting a screenshot or
a partial tape dump.

This finite startup fact is necessary because the best supplied 193-state
runtime quotient fails its blank initialization. A valid arbitrary-frame
simulator alone does not construct a blank-start machine.

\subsection{Trusted boundaries of the certificates}

These are executable mathematical certificates implemented in Python.
Their universal content comes from symbolic tape expressions and generic
list identities, with all conditions checked by the supplied code. A
Python run is not a kernel-checked theorem. The archive exposes the
checkers, their finite inputs and the resulting receipts so their trust
assumptions can be examined.

In particular, finite startup simulation is not evidence that the machine
will never halt. It establishes the initial frame. The universal source
contracts and the command proof establish the intended relative
simulation argument. To obtain the requested theorem in iPRA, the
relevant contracts, bounds and proof translations must be internalized
with the required induction strength. The complete PA calibration cannot
be inferred from the phrase ``primitive recursive'' alone.

\section{Reproduction and artifact map}
\label{sec:reproduce}

\subsection{One offline certificate replay}

From the unpacked archive root, run
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
python3 verify\_all.py --output /tmp/ax11h-verification
\end{quote}
The verification uses the Python standard library and must be run with
assertions enabled, without \texttt{-O} or \texttt{PYTHONOPTIMIZE}.
It checks the final integrity manifest when present, rebuilds the machine
in a fresh temporary directory, compares all construction artifacts byte
for byte, rechecks the source splice and free-term emitter binding, checks
the corrected command words, reruns the independent literal-tape proof,
and compares exact action-graph tuples against all 83 frozen frontier
objects. It also checks the small numeral calculations used in the
padding countermodel. It does not need the original repository or network
access for these checks.

The final replay completed successfully. The saved summary and logs are
under \path{verification/replay/}. They explicitly record that an
unconditional iPRA consistency equivalence is not claimed. The standalone
builder's \texttt{--quick} option is only a convenience for regenerating
tables; it omits the construction-side symbolic replay and is not the
full verification procedure.

\subsection{Priority and remaining work}

AX11, H, the separate-diagonal arithmetic calculus, the compiler,
history folds and the inherited proof infrastructure are credited to
the supplied ProveIt research and its antecedents. The new claims are
the AX11H composition, its exact source splice and independent checks,
the corrected relative compiler, the current merged comparison, the
specific padding countermodel, and the explicitly delimited additional
searches. No claim of global literature priority is made.

The most useful next steps are concrete. A 193-state candidate must pass
both complete startup and all universal runtime contracts. A guardless
calculus must supply an actual PA proof translation, perhaps into a
restricted public image, rather than assume that freshness padding
recovers every old raw axiom. For AX11H itself, the remaining theorem is
the weak-arithmetic calibration: the finite witnesses and local suppliers
must be assembled into the required iPRA or all-model I$\Sigma_1$ proof.
The report's relative construction makes that obligation precise; it
does not discharge it by terminology.
