\section{Comparison of the supplied designs}
\label{sec:comparison}

\subsection{Pinned corpus and census method}

The source corpus is the repository and incoming research at the pinned
revision~\cite{repo,incoming}. The repository snapshot is
\begin{quote}\small
\nolinkurl{5ed5a41fa5932433111eea096311364c6087e8e3}.
\end{quote}
The inspected paths are the requested PA and ZFC research directories,
\file{docs/incoming}, and the relevant concise-iPRA material.
The incoming census recursively traverses ZIP members, deduplicating archive
payloads by SHA-256, and includes direct numeric \file{.tm} tables in the PA
research directory. Complete numeric binary tables in archive \file{.tm}
and \file{.txt} members are recognized when every working control has its
two rows and exactly one terminal target is undefined.

The resulting counts are 32 top-level archives, 45 distinct archive payloads,
73 archive references, maximum nesting depth eight, 25,069 file occurrences,
2,360 recognized table occurrences, 582 byte-distinct tables, and 421
classified action-preserving graph classes. The counts include ancestors,
experiments and rejected machines. They do not mean that there are 421
established consistency constructions. Two disconnected synthetic controls
are excluded from the full-graph canonicalization; a separate no-halt
synthetic control does not satisfy the table recognizer.

At 195 working states there are 37 occurrences, 14 byte forms and nine
graph classes. Exactly three are positive manuscript constructions, while
six are rejected diagnostics. No 194-state table was recognized. Tables
embedded only in arbitrary source code or JSON are outside this numeric
file census; the report and proof-layer review supplement the census.
The full member-level inventory and all 32 archive contributions are in
\file{comparison/}.

\subsection{What the larger designs contribute}

James Chang's PA209 and the U209 parent used by the U208 reports are different
machines, despite their equal counts. The original U208 improvement is a
history-sensitive control fold; later variants give 27 designated
presentations in 20 rooted graph classes. They establish that several
helper routings are possible, but their saving cannot be subtracted again
from a descendant in which the same kind of sharing is already present.

PA196 changes the quantifier coding, axiom heads, axiom order and decoder
allocation. Its original source has 3,812 primitive sites. The six-fold
allocation is already reflected in 196. Later 196-state alternatives change
axiom parenthesization, register placement, the Shift test, the MP result
location, closing jumps and source paths. Several alter the same physical
row and therefore cannot be combined by simply summing their advertised
savings.

The diagonal-vacuity presentation reduces the emitter to 191 macro tokens.
Separating the diagonal head reduces it to 190 tokens and changes the AX
stack interface from nine pushed entries to ten. This logical simplification
is the source of the principal machine. The 18-site direct-negation macro
in the latest negative research packet is a useful counterexample to a
naive metric: it reduces the expanded source to 3,441 sites but still needs
197 physical controls in its verified allocation.

The current comparison therefore has four distinct optimization levels:
logical formulas and proof compilation; command-store operations; binary
tree representation and compatible folds; and physical runtime and blank
initialization. A claim at one level does not automatically establish an
improvement at another.
