\subsection{The four-site zero-register certificate}
Let $P$ be the resolved source program for the A/D-swapped cached-MP
machine, and let $P_Z$ replace only site $3553$ of $P$:
\[
  P(3553)=\operatorname{jump}(3637),\qquad
  P_Z(3553)=\operatorname{dec}(6;3637,3554).
\]
Here register $6$ is $X$, and the first successor of a decrement is its
zero branch.  A zero branch leaves the tested register unchanged.
Both programs have $3650$ resolved source sites.

Put $Z=\{3550,3551,3552,3553\}$.  The following data are checked directly
from the complete resolved instruction arrays, independently of the
compiler and the symbolic Turing-machine simulator.  The only edges into
$Z$ from outside $Z$ are
\[
  3545\xrightarrow{X=0}3551,\qquad
  3548\xrightarrow{X=0}3550.
\]
Their source instructions are respectively
$\operatorname{dec}(6;3551,3546)$ and
$\operatorname{dec}(6;3550,3549)$.
The unchanged instructions within $Z$ are
\[
  P(3550)=\operatorname{jump}(3553),\quad
  P(3551)=\operatorname{dec}(1;3553,3552),\quad
  P(3552)=\operatorname{jump}(3551).
\]
In particular, no instruction within $Z$ increments $X$.

The predicate
\[
  \mathcal I(pc,\mathbf r)\;\equiv\;(pc\in Z\ \Longrightarrow\ r_6=0)
\]
is therefore an inductive invariant of either source program.  It is
initially true at the source entry $pc=25$, for arbitrary natural values
of all nine registers.  Every entry from outside $Z$ establishes it by a
zero test of $X$; every step from within $Z$ preserves it or exits $Z$.
At the sole changed site, $\mathcal I$ forces the new decrement to take
its zero branch.  That branch has the old jump's destination and makes
no change to any register.  Every other instruction is identical.
Induction on source time now proves equality of the complete source
configurations of $P$ and $P_Z$ at every step, until their common halt if
one occurs.  The extra positive branch in the resolved graph of $P_Z$
is unreachable from the source entry.

This is an invariant argument for all register values and all execution
lengths, rather than a bounded simulation result.  It supplies the source
equivalence needed to transfer the command-level soundness, completeness,
and scheduler argument to the modified source.  The independent blank-tape
startup certificate and the universal instruction-refinement certificate
remain necessary to connect that source theorem to the displayed binary
Turing-machine table.
