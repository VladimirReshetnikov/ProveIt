                        New 194-state PA-consistency machines
    Constructor coefficients 5 and 6, a shared decoder, and a complete corpus comparison

                              Research prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov

                                      13 November 2026, Pacific time

                                                     Abstract
        No verified machine with fewer than 194 working states was obtained. The requested alternative is supplied
    by two new, complete, blank-start binary Turing machines, M5 and M6 , with 194 working states each. They
    use the numerical formula constructors kp(a, b) + r, with k = 5, 6 and tags r = 0, 1, 2, 3, in place of the
    previous coefficient four. A restructuring of the multiplication decoder makes its two-increment subroutine
    reachable only after a written one. That context permits a three-transition implementation shared with a
    different control reached after a written zero. The count is 135 + 67 − 8 = 194.
        The new arithmetic encoding is calibrated to PA by an injective hereditary recoding of both formula codes
    and variable labels. Its failure to preserve numerical order is handled using the already proved unrestricted
    dominated-implication theorem. Independent literal-tape checks cover every primitive source outcome, derive
    the four register-list lemmas from the actual rows, and verify the complete blank startup. A third 194-state
    machine, M4R , applies the new decoder mechanism while preserving the original resolved source graph exactly.
    The results are ordinary mathematical PA-provability arguments with executable certificates; they are not
    newly compiled end-to-end Lean or Rocq theorems.

1    Result, conventions, and exact scope
Every delivered main table has alphabet {0, 1}, one two-way infinite tape, and an all-zero initial tape. Every
transition writes one bit and moves exactly one cell left or right. Working states exclude the terminal halt label.
In the dense tables the working labels are 0, . . . , 193, the start is 0, and the halt label is 194. There are exactly
388 transition rows and no outgoing row at halt.
Theorem 1 (New constructor-coefficient machines). For each k ∈ {5, 6}, the literal table Mk supplied with this
report satisfies
                               PA ⊢ ¬ Halt(Mk , blank) ←→ Con(PA) .
                                     (                                  )

The new 194-state companion M4R satisfies the same statement and has a PA-formalizable blank-halting equivalence
with the pinned primary PA194 table.

Here Con(PA) is the absence of a finite proof of 0 = 1 in a fixed ordinary recursively checked first-order PA proof
system. The theorem concerns an arithmetized finite run of the exact transition table, not just its execution over
the standard natural numbers. The proof combines the source-calculus translation in Section 5, the exact source
binding, and the universal physical simulation. Its inherited PA-refutation compiler and diagonal-calibration
interfaces are identified explicitly below.
The construction fulfills the alternative 194-state target. It does not lower the current count and does not claim
faster proof search. In fact the coefficient-5 and coefficient-6 programs contain more primitive increment sites,
and the larger numerical codes can increase running time. Their value is a different arithmetic encoding with a
fully specified state allocation and a new family for subsequent optimization.

The larger families establish the ancestry and explain why apparently independent savings often do not add.
The U208 lineage compresses an older PA checker using contextual folds, frame-aware source simulations,
register placement, and source rotations. James Chang’s PA209/PA196 lineage changes the logical interface and

subsequent diagonal 195-state designs replace a larger equality-vacuity component by code-driven diagonal vacuity.
Separating that diagonal clause into its own pushed stack head shortens the AX emitter to 190 macro tokens.
The first primary 194-state design then combines 67 raw readers with seven compatible folds. Cached-controller
and symmetric-Shift variants modify the fixed program instead. The new work changes the constructor multiplier
and its fixed decoder, while retaining the separated-head logical interface.
The novelty comparison canonicalizes the new complete transition graphs against all prior 194-state tables,
allowing arbitrary control renaming, arbitrary choice of a root reaching the whole graph, global left/right reflection,
and global read/write-bit complementation. M5 , M6 , and M4R are pairwise distinct and absent from the old
classes even under this deliberately stronger comparison. Bit complementation is used only as an exclusion test;
the delivered machines still start on zero tape.


3     Changing the constructor coefficient
Write Cantor pairing as
                                                  (a + b)(a + b + 1)
                                          p(a, b) =                  + b.
                                                          2
For a fixed k ≥ 4, define the numerical constructors

                      Ik (A, B) = kp(A, B),                             Mk (a, b) = kp(a, b) + 1,
                      Uk (a, B) = kp(B, a) + 2,                         Ek (a, b) = kp(a, b) + 3.

The transposition in Uk is intentional. In this section Mk (a, b) denotes a formula constructor, while the standalone
symbol Mk denotes the delivered machine; the arguments distinguish them. Code zero is truth, code one is
Mk (0, 0) and is the designated contradiction, and Nk (B) = Ik (B, 1) is negation. Conjunction and existential
quantification are the usual classical implicational abbreviations.
For k > 4, some residues are unused. In the PA interpretation they decode as truth. This supplies a total
interpretation of every natural number, including every possible arithmetic parameter encountered by the checker.
It does not add an axiom saying that each unused number is a theorem of the searched calculus.

3.1    The exact source change
The source register order is (N, T, W, A, D, H, X, Y, L). Thus T is register 1 and Y is register 7. The original
constructor macro node65 computes a stack operation with the effect

                                             (T, W ) = (a, p(b, w)) 7−→ (kp(b, a), w),

when entered with X = Y = 0; it restores X = Y = 0 and preserves every other register. Its unchanged prefix
decodes the stack head and computes the Cantor pair. The final loop consumes Y and increments T a fixed
number of times for each consumed unit.
In the old source node61 is four consecutive increments of register 1. It is used only by node62, the per-unit
body of this constructor loop. The new source replaces it with five or six consecutive increments. The explicit
source binding checks 61 expanded occurrences, all 61 surrounding constructor prefixes, every loop test and back
edge, all 3,429 primitive sites outside these bodies, and every outside successor under the index relocation. No
source edge enters the interior of a replacement body from outside it.
Consequently the complete source sizes are

                        3429 + 61 · 4 = 3673,          3429 + 61 · 5 = 3734,   3429 + 61 · 6 = 3795.

The literal tag increments after the constructor remain unchanged. The AX word still contains exactly 190 macro
tokens. The same constructor is used when MP constructs the implication whose code is compared with the
cached theorem; changing the emitter without changing this MP constructor would be incorrect.
Lemma 2 (Generic constructor loop). If the final loop begins with T = t and Y = y, its coefficient-k body
terminates with T = t + ky and Y = 0, preserving all other registers.

Proof. Use the invariant T + kY = t + ky. A positive loop iteration decrements Y once and increments T exactly
k times, preserving the invariant; the zero branch exits. Induction on the initial y gives termination and the
stated result. Combining this with the unchanged stack-decoding and pairing prefix proves the macro contract
above. This is a primitive-recursive finite computation with a PA proof, uniformly for each fixed k.

This macro argument is additional to the primitive instruction certificates: implementing the changed primitive
graph does not alone prove its intended constructor semantics. The archive includes the detailed source lemma
and an exact free-algebra AX-emitter audit. That audit checks all ten pushed entries, the six retained axiom
heads, the separated diagonal head, the guarded final top, and their ordering.

5     The PA-calibration argument
5.1    What is inherited and what changes
The pinned primary PA194 packet supplies an ordinary mathematical proof of its PA-provable consistency
biconditional, supported by the imported PA196 refutation compiler and a diagonal replacement argument [2, 3].
The repository’s README correctly distinguishes that argument from its unfinished end-to-end Lean/iPRA
goals [1]. The present proof does not claim an iPRA equivalence.
The new M4R has exactly the same resolved 3,673-instruction source as primary PA194. Its physical equivalence
can therefore be transported directly. For M5 and M6 , the source and its numerical computation change. They
require a calculus translation and a fresh target command compilation, which we now provide.
The six retained axiom heads, the dominated propositional head, modus ponens, and generalization of theorems
are retained. In coefficient-k notation the separated diagonal head is
                                                Γk (B) = Ik (B, Uk (B, B)).
The guarded separation gate is the same constructor expression as before, with every constructor replaced by its
coefficient-k version. Its guard is still c ̸= a, b. The proof uses the exact separate-head ten-entry stack protocol,
rather than the old nine-entry selectors.

5.2    Hereditary recoding also renames variables
For each k ≥ 4, define Fk (0) = 0. For n > 0, uniquely write

                                          n = 4p(a, b) + r,     0 ≤ r < 4,

and set
                                           Fk (n) = kp(Fk (a), Fk (b)) + r.                                       (1)
Both a, b < n in this nonzero case, so this is primitive-recursive course-of-values recursion. The same function is
applied to formula codes and to numerical variable labels.
Lemma 3 (The recoding properties). PA proves that Fk is injective, fixes 0 and 1, and preserves all four
constructors, with every argument and variable label mapped by Fk . In particular,

                                           Fk Γ4 (c) = Γk Fk (c) .
                                              (      )    (     )


Proof. Strong induction shows Fk (n) > 0 for n > 0. In an equality of two nonzero outputs, reduction modulo k
recovers the same tag r < 4 ≤ k. Injectivity of Cantor pairing then gives equality of the two recursively mapped
components. Induction on the sum of the input codes yields equality of the original components and hence of the
inputs. The zero case follows from positivity.
The constructor equations follow directly from (1), including the exceptional implication I4 (0, 0) = 0, since
Ik (0, 0) = 0. The universal constructor merely reverses the two paired arguments on both sides. The diagonal
identity follows because the binder label and the property code are both recoded by the same function. Injectivity
also preserves the separation guard.

Mapping only formula codes while keeping their old variable labels would fail at the diagonal head. The
simultaneous hereditary recoding is what makes this a translation of the actual searched calculus.


5.3    The order obstruction and its solution
It would be incorrect to say that Fk preserves numerical order. For example,

                                     40 < 44,      F5 (40) = 75 > 55 = F5 (44).

The restricted propositional row has the form

                             ((H → u) → D) → ((D → H) → (A → H)),                 u ≤ D.                          (2)

Its recoded side condition need not hold. However the generic theorem M6/Logic/RestrictedImplication.lean:
rho8All derives the unrestricted version of (2). Its assumptions are modus ponens, the restricted row, reflexivity of
the comparison relation, and propagation of a bound into either component of implication [4]. For Ik (a, b) = kp(a, b)
these component bounds hold for every k ≥ 1.
Accordingly, when translating an old derivation, replace a recoded restricted-row leaf by the generic derived
proof of its unrestricted target instance. All other axiom leaves translate directly by the constructor identities
and guard preservation; MP and generalization translate by their proof constructors. These operations give a
primitive-recursive proof translation whose correctness is provable in PA. No false monotonicity assumption about
Fk is used.
The old PA-refutation compiler therefore supplies the first syntactic implication:

                                              PA ⊢ Ref PA −→ Ref Ck .                                             (3)

Here Ck is the coefficient-k separated-head numerical calculus.

5.4    Syntactic soundness in PA
Interpret membership by finite binary membership

                                  Bit(s, z) ≡ ∃u, v [s = (2u + 1)2z + v ∧ v < 2z ],

and interpret equality and universal quantification in the ordinary way. Decode unused residues as truth. All
recursive children and all occurring variable labels of a nonzero constructor code are smaller than that code. The
truth-code exception and unused residues contain no source variables. Thus xB is absent from the interpretation
of the formula coded by B, so every Γk (B) has a uniform first-order proof.
PA proves Bit(s, z) → z < s, and hence refutes the interpretation of code one, Bit(x0 , x0 ). For any arithmetic
formula syntax θ(z, ⃗u), PA has a proof of finite bit comprehension

                                      ∀n ∃s < 2n ∀z < n (Bit(s, z) ↔ θ(z, ⃗u)).                                    (4)

The proof is induction on n, adding the bit 2n exactly in the θ(n, ⃗u) case. Substituting a formula’s syntax into
this fixed induction-proof template is a primitive-recursive operation on proofs. This is not a uniform truth
predicate or a reflection principle for PA.
The separation supplier uses (4) and the same collision split as the primary calibration. When the two source
labels a, b differ, use the bounded predicate

                                        (Bit(xa , z) ∨ z = xb ) ∧ Tk (c)[z/xb ].

When a = b, the antecedent inside the binder is Bit(z, z), which is false, and the required witness uses the singleton
predicate (z = xb ) ∧ Tk (c)[z/xb ]. Capture-avoiding renaming is part of the proof supplier. Code freshness makes
the separator label absent from the property. All remaining head suppliers are fixed first-order or propositional
proof templates.
PA therefore verifies a primitive-recursive translation from each Ck refutation to a PA refutation:

                                              PA ⊢ Ref Ck −→ Ref PA .                                              (5)

The exceptional equality Ik (0, 0) = 0 is handled by the fixed proof that truth is equivalent to truth implying
truth. It is not treated as injectivity between distinct syntactic constructors.


5.5    Compile a new target word; do not recode an old run
The map Fk is a translation of derivations. It does not preserve ordinary addition or Cantor-stack arithmetic,
and it is not a conjugacy of scheduler stores. After translating a derivation, compile a new command word using
the coefficient-k command compiler. In each actual restricted propositional step of that new proof, the target
proof supplies the required numerical bound and the relevant subtraction is performed in the target arithmetic.
The separate-head command identities retain ten pushed entries. The safe-head arguments use residues 0 and
2 modulo k, the fact that a universal code is at least 2, and Ik (0, i) > 0 for i > 0; these hold for k ≥ 4. The
generic stack identities, protection of an arbitrary stack tail, and initial AX clearing of dirty caches are otherwise
unchanged. The compiled refutation word halts from every incoming store, rather than only from a reset store.
The scheduler continues to use ordinary Cantor pairing, not Fk or coefficient-k formula pairing. Its candidate
pointer strictly decreases while a word is being read. Candidate integers are enumerated from 2 upward, and an
AX-prefixed compiled refutation word has a candidate number at least 3. Finite induction through the preceding
candidates reaches it or an earlier halt.
Conversely annotate both visible theorem codes and every live canonical stack entry with PA proofs of their
interpretations. AX, MP, and Shift preserve this annotation; either visible occurrence of code one produces a PA
refutation. The Cantor-stack alias p(0, 0) = 0 is handled explicitly: pushing zero onto an empty canonical stack
leaves it empty, and popping zero supplies the truth proof. This invariant applies to every annotated store, so no
scheduler reset is assumed.
Together with the generic constructor lemma and the source binding, these facts prove in PA that the coefficient-k
source halts exactly when a PA refutation exists.

6     Physical transport and independently replayed evidence
Every source primitive is implemented by an exact finite segment between canonical frames, with a strictly
positive duration. A frame has a source-address word, a zero gap with header constant 48, nine arbitrary natural
register values encoded in unary blocks, and an arbitrary finite nonempty spare list. The physical checks cover
increment, jump, both decrement outcomes, and halt. They do not extrapolate from finitely many register values.
The independent checker imports no builder, allocator, or arithmetic-box oracle. It derives four generic list-sweep
lemmas from the delivered literal transition rows, resolves source control flow in two independent ways, and checks
every symbolic outcome. Its startup simulation separately verifies the entire finite-support tape and zero exterior,
the head at −2, native control 84, source site 25, logical registers (2, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0), and 26 one-valued spare
blocks.

Symbolic step counts measure checker operations, not physical Turing-machine running times. The startup counts
are literal physical transitions.
The arithmetic proof of transport uses induction on the finite unary blocks, source segments, and coded runs. A
source halt yields a physical halt by concatenation. For the reverse implication, under consistency the proof-store
invariant allows arbitrarily many completed positive command segments. Given an alleged physical halting time
T , constructing T + 1 such segments gives a run longer than T . Determinism contradicts an earlier terminal halt.
Exact nonhalt endpoints exclude a hidden premature halt within a segment. The fixed blank initialization is
checked separately.
The independently replayed source graph for M4R is exactly the primary 194 source. For M5 , M6 , the two source
resolvers instead verify the changed graph, and the 61-loop binding and constructor theorem supply its new
command meaning. Those two different assertions must not be conflated.

8    What the reduction searches did and did not establish
The primary-source grammar search examined 4,773,613 proposals in its 300-second run, retaining a minimum
of 60 effective AX controls. A second search allowed eligible fixed controls as fold endpoints during grammar
mutation; its 1,618,013 proposals also retained a 194-state minimum. Exposing selected internal macro fragments
produced a further 550,868 proposals without a 193-state candidate. These searches preserve precisely described
syntactic or local simulation conditions; they do not enumerate all equivalent machines.
The constant-increment decoder search considered 1,248 DAG candidates with at most three auxiliary controls.
Its only non-four coefficients at the 194-state threshold were five and six. The further coefficient-five/six grammar
logs are included with their measured proposal counts and outcomes. Their fitness includes the extra fixed control
and the new fold; a raw fitness value 59 means 135 + 59 = 194, not 193.
Other finite searches included all 576 tables in a specified quotient closure of the 202-state CSZ parent, 459
neutral two-row rewrites of one helper followed by a fold search, and 1,275 register-scanner permutation screens.
None produced a verified 193-state table. The package records these domains, rather than turning their failure
into a lower bound.
One instructive rejected candidate replaced a source jump by a conditional decrement of a register known to be
zero at that source location. Its source contracts and invariant passed, but its modified blank bootstrap halted
after 1,361 steps. It was rejected. This example is why complete startup checking is an essential part of every
accepted table here.


9    Further research questions
1. Can the coefficient, AX association, and fixed arithmetic decoder be optimized jointly to reach 193 states?
   The new results show that preserving the old coefficient four is an unnecessary search restriction, even when
   a larger coefficient does not immediately save a state.
2. Can a helper that is initially unusable become shareable after a constant-subtree reassociation? The 41/99
   fold gives a concrete example where changing the set of incoming history bits matters more than direct row
   similarity.
3. Can a three-control region be replaced by two controls with a certified finite interface, even when no sequence
   of pairwise folds exists? Negative pair-fold searches leave this possibility open.
4. Can the 61 generic constructor-loop substitutions and the hereditary proof translation be formalized once,
   parameterized by k ≥ 4? The generic rho8All result already isolates the only order obstruction in the
   derivation translation.
5. Can proof-friendly certificate formats connect the complete physical source simulation to the arithmetic
   endpoint without obscuring positive-duration and carried-store obligations? This is the useful connection to
   the ConciseIPRA work. Replacing the searched theory by iPRA would require a separate consistency-strength
   argument and is not a valid automatic optimization.
The transferable ZFC idea is contextual use of existing tape bits to share controls, combined with explicit
source-frame invariants and exact blank initialization. The new coefficient family adds a mathematical degree
of freedom to that physical search. The remaining opportunity is to combine these choices under a correctness
certificate strong enough to reject bootstrap and scanner failures early.
