\section{Two new equivalent orientations of the first logical head}

The searches also tested a change absent from the earlier joint
premise-exchange search.  Write $\Box A=U(D,A)$ for one fixed binder,
$\Diamond A=N(\Box N(A))$, and $N(A)=I(A,1)$.  The old head is
\[
 F_D(A)=(\Diamond\Box A)\to A.
\]
Consider the following two replacements:
\[
 B_D(A)=A\to\Box\Diamond A,
 \qquad C_D(A)=N(A)\to\Box N(\Box A).
\]
Each replacement has exactly 17 emitter tokens, the same count as
$F_D$.  They change the recurring logical emitter; they are not a
permutation of Turing-machine state labels.

The replacement by $C_D$ is already a pointwise propositional
equivalence.  Indeed $F_D(A)$ is
$N(X)\to A$ with $X=\Box N(\Box A)$, while $C_D(A)$ is
$N(A)\to X$.  Classical contraposition and double-negation
elimination derive either from the other.  The retained restricted
implicational schema supplies these fixed templates.

For the replacement by $B_D$, apply the preceding observation to
$F_D(N(A))$.  Its $C$ form is
$N(N(A))\to\Box N(\Box N(A))$, from which double-negation
introduction derives $B_D(A)$.  Conversely $B_D(N(A))$ is
$N(A)\to\Box N(\Box N(N(A)))$.
The retained distribution head and theorem generalization lift the
two directions of $N(N(A))\leftrightarrow A$ through $\Box$;
contraposition lifts the resulting equivalence through $N$, and
distribution lifts it through the outer $\Box$.
This gives $C_D(A)$ and hence $F_D(A)$.

Every transformation uses a fixed finite proof template, with only
constructor application and proof-list concatenation depending on
the parameters.  Replacing axiom leaves and retaining MP/Gen gives
primitive-recursive proof translations in both directions.  Their
correctness is verified by bounded Boolean induction on the proof
list.  Thus these are valid alternative numerical calculi in the
same weak metatheory, independently of whether either emitter can
be compressed into a smaller literal table.

The two timed searches use 96 semantic seeds apiece, crossing the
new first head with equality orientation, h3/h4/h6 premise exchanges,
three diagonal parameters and two existing source-tree shapes.
Each unguarded head can permute its parameter registers during
the search.  The retained search source also performs local and
shared associativity changes.  These finite stochastic runs are
documented separately in their actual logs; they establish no
global lower bound.  No table from this experiment is promoted
as a verified consistency machine merely from its reader score.
