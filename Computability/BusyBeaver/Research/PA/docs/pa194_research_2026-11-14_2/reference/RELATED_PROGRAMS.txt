ZFC machines
------------
Inspected sources: Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/README.md;
candidate-k215/README.md; PA/docs/pa_sub194_research_2026-11-13/README.md.

K215's successful savings combine command-local field readers, command
permutation, source reassociation, contextual marker folds, and reuse of
otherwise available decoder rows. Its source-DAG size and physical state
count are explicitly separate. That methodology does transfer: a shared
history bit or a neutral detour can identify controls, but the complete
source contracts and blank initialization must both survive. Our neutral
preconditioning and two-row contraction searches use that idea directly.

The known PA193 runtime core illustrates the remaining obstruction. It
merges native 301 into 88 and sets (88,0)=0L51 and (51,0)=0R122. Its 4,780
arbitrary-register source outcomes are certified, but the unchanged blank
start reaches an outward ray; the packet also classifies other elementary
starts. The runtime saving is real, but a prepared-tape runtime is not the
requested blank-start consistency machine. K215's successful ZFC marker
folds do not automatically repair this different PA bootstrap.

K215 itself recognizes ZFC refutations. Its external Lean/Rocq correctness
theorems and its ZFC-internal ordinary calibration do not turn it into a
PA-consistency machine, and its README separately leaves an internal PA
endpoint open. Transferring its engineering techniques is legitimate;
transferring a different theory's consistency target by state-count analogy
is not. No sub-194 blank-start result follows merely from that source.

DiophantineConsistency
---------------------
Inspected sources: Computability/DiophantineConsistency/README.md;
Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA/isigma1-transfer/README.md;
the incoming AX11H/QAX11H consistency composition and source bridge.

The most directly useful contribution is the weak-base proof machinery:
all-model arithmetic reasoning in IΣ1, coded witnesses, conservation to
candidate PRA, and Gödel-Gentzen transfer of the negative consistency and
nonhalting sentences into iPRA. The PA isigma1-transfer README identifies
its endpoint theorem, model version, PA witness certificate, and its
machine-independent status. Those are precisely the dependencies used by
the incoming AX11H written calibration and the new construction's transport.

The displayed small Diophantine equations optimize variables, arithmetic
operation counts, and degree. Those metrics do not count binary TM states.
A blank-start TM still needs a tuple enumerator, evaluation arithmetic,
constants/initialization, and a literal transition implementation. Several
of the equation families also encode Con(ZFC), a different target. No
inspected source supplies a compiled sub-194 binary evaluator calibrated to
Con(PA). Polynomial compactness alone therefore cannot be booked as a
machine-state saving. The transfer and witness methods are useful here
without first building a polynomial evaluator.

ConciseIPRA
-----------
Inspected source: Logic/PrimitiveRecursiveArithmetic/ConciseIPRA/README.md,
particularly its complete-checker size metric and native scalar core.

Its objective is a small complete standalone Python proof checker for a
presentation bi-interpretable with full first-order iPRA. Its scalar terms
use an iterator, and its Q/D formulas and compact rule systems move work
between computation, transport, witness selection and syntax. Those ideas
suggest eliminating redundant operands and reusing a value already present
at an interface, as the new direct-binder Gen word does.

The advertised token/AST/byte metric is not binary-machine state count:
JSON parsing, binding/substitution, iterators, normalization and fresh-name
checks would all need actual machine compilation. Nor does calibration to
iPRA by itself supply calibration to PA consistency. Consequently the small
checker is a promising alternative architecture, but no direct replacement
of the current PA numerical calculus is justified by checker length alone.
A state-saving proposal must include the PA-strength translation, the
literal evaluator, and its blank initializer before it can improve 194.
