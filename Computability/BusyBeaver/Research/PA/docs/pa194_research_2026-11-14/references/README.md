# Historical dependencies and scope

The files in `logical/` are supplied mathematical manuscripts from the QH5,
AX11, and earlier PA consistency packets. They retain their original claims
and terminology. They are historical dependencies, not newly proved results
of this release.

The new report and `STATUS.json` control this release's claims. In particular,
an inherited PA-provable calibration and ordinary finite-run simulation do
not establish the requested iPRA theorem. The new command-level proof states
the remaining weak-arithmetic physical and PA-calibration premises explicitly.

The upstream authors, code, and licenses remain credited in `../licenses/`,
the two construction subdirectories, and the report's references.
