ATTRIBUTION AND SCOPE

This research compares the Contextual, Research/Decoder Reassociation, and
Rotated U211 packets supplied through Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository
and develops a new joint source association that retains their three distinct
state deletions simultaneously. The deletion mechanisms themselves are credited
to those packets. The novel contribution relative to the supplied inputs is
their compatible common decoder allocation and the resulting explicit U209.
No wider historical priority claim is made.

The machine architecture, numerical calculus, earlier reductions, source and
induction engine are inherited. The retained notices credit Mira-Cult/Mira,
Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project, Elliot Glazer, Jeff's busy-beaver project,
Andrew James Wade's Turing Machine Explorer, and other contributors named in
the original documents. Existing license texts and source pins are preserved
under licenses/ and within baseline/selected_U212.zip.

code/source_language.py is retained from the Contextual packet. The two files
code/symbolic_runtime.py and code/independent_runtime.py are byte-identical to
its inherited induction-engine modules. code/independent_source.py is retained
from the Research packet. code/check_finite_runtime.cpp is retained from the
Contextual packet. The new wrappers, builder, comparison and local certificate
checker are supplied as auditable research artifacts. No repository-wide
relicensing of inherited or unspecified-license materials is asserted.

comparison/ retains exact table/source snapshots and unchanged READMEs/notices
for the three incoming candidates. The original ZIP identities and pinned
download URLs are in comparison/input_archives.json. These three machines
have the same inherited logical calibration; they are not three independent
proof-assistant verifications of that calibration.

All new mathematical claims are scoped in report.pdf. In particular, a Python
symbolic-induction derivation is not a Lean, Rocq or MM0 kernel certificate.
The K215/ZFC formal results provide context, not a certificate for this U209
table. The complete research ancestry is preserved for inspection; some
retained .v reference files require imports not bundled as a new U project.
