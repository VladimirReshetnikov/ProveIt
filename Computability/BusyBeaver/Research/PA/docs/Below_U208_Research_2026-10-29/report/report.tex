\begin{abstract}
\StatusText\space
Five incoming archives contain nine literal 208-state tables but exactly two
rooted transition-graph classes. All use the same conditional history fold of
two native states, 172 and 211; their reductions cannot be stacked to obtain
207 states. Fresh runtime replays cover both classes. This note explains the
common fold, separates its finite simulation proof from the inherited
consistency calibration, and records further source and local-machine searches.
A conditional pruning removes five source primitives but increases the present
syntax DAG from 197 to 200 nodes. New seven-cell runtime projections strengthen
the search beyond recent-transition history, without yielding a validated
smaller machine. Negative results concern explicitly bounded search families,
not a lower bound on state complexity or an exhaustive search of machines.
\end{abstract}

\section{The target and what has been established}
The requested object is a deterministic binary Turing machine $M$ with at most
207 working states, including its complete blank initializer, such that
\[
 \PA\vdash\bigl(\neg\Halt(M,\mathrm{blank})\leftrightarrow\Con(\UU)\bigr).
\]
The convention is one head, one two-way infinite tape, alphabet $\{0,1\}$,
blank symbol 0, and only left and right moves. The halt label is separate and
is not counted as a working state. The initial head is at position 0 and all
tape cells are zero. Native labels in a sparse table are names, not the count;
in the Joint native labeling used below, 211 is a working label and 254 is halt.

\textbf{No validated machine meeting the target is supplied here.}
The established input level remains 208 states and 416 total binary rows.
The positive results of this investigation are an exact comparison of all
nine supplied tables, fresh runtime replays for their two graph classes,
and precisely scoped reusable proof and search evidence. These do not turn a
208-state table into a 207-state construction.

The distinction between a PA-provable equivalence and actual nonhalting is
important. A correct consistency calibration does not require assuming
$\Con(\UU)$ in order to prove the biconditional. Asserting that the machine
actually runs forever requires the corresponding consistency assumption.
Nor is an ordinary PA-formalizable argument automatically a newly exported
proof-assistant certificate. The latter is not claimed in this report.

\section{The common history fold and its invariant}
Let $A$ be the Joint 209-state parent and $B$ its Primary 208-state reduction.
The reduction deletes state 172, redirects its only entrance, and changes one
retained transition. In the notation $wDr$ for write, direction, and target,
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lll}\toprule
Row & Parent $A$ & Reduced $B$\\\midrule
$116/0$ & $0R172$ & $0R211$\\
$211/0$ & $0R157$ & $0L217$\\
$172/0$ & $0L67$ & deleted\\
$172/1$ & $1R184$ & deleted\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
Every other retained row is unchanged. Define $f(172)=211$ and $f(q)=q$ for
all other labels, including halt. At simulation boundaries, $f$ changes only
the state; it leaves the tape and head unchanged.

For a configuration $(q,h,T)$ write $t_j=T(h+j)$. The relevant parent invariant
consists of the following implications from the indicated state:
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
116&t_{-1}=1\\
172&t_{-2}=1\ \land\ t_{-1}=0\\
217&t_{-1}=1\\
163&t_{-1}=1\ \lor\ t_{-2}=1\\
211&t_0=1\ \lor\ t_{-1}=1.
\end{array}
\]
In particular, 211 reading zero implies a one immediately to the left. It does
not imply that every visit to 211 has a left-hand one. The complete incoming
census in the parent is
\begin{center}\small\begin{tabular}{cl}\toprule
Target & All incoming physical rows\\\midrule
172 & $116/0:0R172$\\
116 & $215/1:1R116$\\
211 & $163/0:0L211$, $163/1:1R211$\\
163 & $132/1:1R163$, $199/1:1R163$, $217/0:0R163$\\
217 & $216/1:1R217$\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
\begin{lemma}
Every parent configuration reached from state 0 satisfies these five
implications, on any initial binary tape.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Initially they are vacuous. An entrance to 116 or 217 writes one and moves
right. An entrance to 172 writes zero and moves right from 116, carrying its
left-hand one to offset $-2$. An entrance to 163 either writes a left-hand one
or, from 217/0, carries such a one to offset $-2$. Finally, 163/1 establishes
a left-hand one at 211; 163/0 moves left, so the former two left cells become
the new current and left cells. Their disjunction gives the condition at 211.
These are all entrances, so induction proves the claim.
\end{proof}
The census includes physical engine entrances, not merely edges of the source
syntax. This is why the proof applies to arbitrary initial tapes and not only
to an observed blank prefix or a selection of register values.

\section{Whole tape simulation and why the folds cannot stack}
Only old states 172 and 211 need multi-step treatment. The four replacement
contracts in $B$ are
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{cclrc}\toprule

211 & 1 & none beyond read & 1 & $(184,+1)$\\\bottomrule

Every block preserves the entire tape. Both one-cases use $211/1:1R184$.
The three-step zero-case follows
$211\to217\to215\to157$ at relative head positions $0,-1,0,1$,
reading and rewriting $0,1,0$. The seven-step zero-case has this complete trace:
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{rrrrr}\toprule
Step & State before & Head before & Read and write & State after\\\midrule
1&211&0&0&217\\
2&217&$-1$&0&163\\
3&163&0&0&211\\
4&211&$-1$&0&217\\
5&217&$-2$&1&215\\
6&215&$-1$&0&157\\
7&157&0&0&67\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
The final head is at $-1$. No unspecified cell is inspected, every write
preserves its cell, and no internal state is halt. The other 414 parent rows
commute with $f$ in one step. In particular, the internal return to state 211
in this seven-step block does not invoke the parent invariant afresh; the
explicit trace supplies the proof there.

\begin{theorem}
The parent and reduced machines, started in state 0 on the same tape and head,
halt together. If $N_{q,b}(n)$ counts parent transitions of type $(q,b)$ among
the first $n$ steps, they agree in tape and head, and in state after applying
$f$, at reduced time
\[
 \tau(n)=n+6N_{172,0}(n)+2N_{211,0}(n),\qquad n\le\tau(n)\le7n.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induct over parent steps using the invariant and the four contracts. Their
positive lengths make $\tau$ unbounded on an infinite parent run, and the
successive blocks cover every reduced time. None halts internally. Thus a
parent first halt gives exactly the corresponding reduced first halt, while
an infinite parent computation gives an infinite reduced computation.
\end{proof}
In Compatibility native labels, the selector 217 becomes 214, the feeder 163
becomes 216, and the right branch 215 becomes 163; the same proof applies.
History keeps the opposite merged representative. These are alternative
presentations of the same one-state saving, not two deletions that survive
simultaneously. The nine-table graph audit makes this precise. The finite
invariant, traces, and bounded clock give a PA-formalizable halting equivalence;
they do not independently establish the inherited consistency endpoint.

\section{The consistency endpoint and the verification boundary}
The theory $\UU$ is equality-free classical first-order logic over a nonempty
domain with one binary relation $E$. Write
$\operatorname{Sub}(x,a):=\forall t(E(t,x)\to E(t,a))$.
For every formula $\varphi$ with free variables contained in $\{x\}$, its
weak-cover schema is
\[
 \forall a\,\exists b\left[
  \forall x(E(x,b)\to\varphi(x))\ \land\
  \forall x(\operatorname{Sub}(x,a)\to(\varphi(x)\to E(x,b)))
 \right].
\]
Bound variables are freshened. The source $a$ is not allowed as a free matrix
parameter. A weak cover may contain additional selected objects, so one cannot
silently replace this by an exact subset filter.

The supplied calibration argues that $\UU$ and PA admit uniform finite-proof
translations verifiable in PA. Its lower interpretation uses coextensional
numerical classes, parameter internalization, numerical induction, bounded
comprehension with explicit support, and deterministic histories for arithmetic.
The upper interpretation is finite binary membership: $E(x,a)$ means that bit
$x$ of $a$ is one. For each fixed formula, PA induction supplies the required
finite characteristic code, without a uniform truth predicate.

The native source does \emph{not} literally enumerate only unary $\UU$
instances. Its machine-facing theory is unrestricted weak cover $\mathsf W$
plus irreflexivity $\mathsf{IR}$. The retained proof transport gives the cycle
\[
 \Con(\PA)\Longrightarrow\Con(\mathsf W+\mathsf{IR})
 \Longrightarrow\Con(\UU)\Longrightarrow\Con(\PA).
\]
The numerical axiom calculus, proof compiler, unbounded scheduler completeness,
and protected startup are separate obligations in that chain. In particular,
the initializer reaches an interior axiom-command suffix with an opaque stack
tail. The inherited proof builds nine theorem heads and maintains a permanent
margin of seven above that tail; a finite trace by itself would not establish
that the tail is never exposed.

The current audit freshly replays the ordinary symbolic runtime engine over
arbitrary natural register values, arbitrary gap, and finite spare-register
lists, not just small samples. Its two representative receipts are
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
Class & Primitive contracts & Covered rows & Symbolic inferences\\\midrule
Primary & 5,084 & 416 & 1,251,865\\
Compatibility & 5,084 & 416 & 1,250,564\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
The exact blank startup takes 48,140 steps, ending at native state 84, head
$-2$, source ordinal 136, with logical registers $(1,0,13,0,0,1,0,0,1)$.
The Primary full replay also checks 104 Boolean invariant cases and rejects
12 negative controls. The History release's quick replay and late-copy
conjugacies are not misreported as additional fresh full runtime proofs.

Composing the literal simulation with the \emph{inherited} numerical and theory
calibration yields the supplied 208-state endpoint
$\PA\vdash\neg\Halt(B,\mathrm{blank})\leftrightarrow\Con(\UU)$.
This audit is not a fresh kernel proof of those logical dependencies. In
particular, a separate K215 formalization concerning ZFC cannot be relabeled
as a theorem for these tables. The local proof is complete on its stated
premises; the full consistency calibration remains an explicit dependency.

\section{Conditional source pruning and the sharing obstruction}
The source lane investigates deleting two closed loops whose bodies are
unreachable under a previously checked source invariant. In old zero-based
ordinals, the guards are $R_1=0$ at 3847 and $R_6=0$ at 3891. Conditional on
those guards, bodies 3848--3849 and 3892 are never entered. The pruning packet
checks the following exact tree edits:
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lll}\toprule
Case & Replace subtree by its right child & Deleted interval\\\midrule
First & $001101110\mapsto0011011101$ & $[3847,3850)$\\
Second & $1110011\mapsto11100111$ & $[3891,3893)$\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
The first removes a closed transfer loop; the second removes a closed clearing
loop. The numerical names of ordinals are not physical machine-state labels.

\begin{proposition}
Assuming the two zero guards at every reachable loop entry, the two-loop
pruning is a source-level stuttering simulation in both directions, with
unchanged retained registers and halt behavior.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The external-entry census has only $3841/\mathrm{zero}\to3847$ and
$3889/\mathrm{zero}\to3891$. There is no outside entrance to a deleted body.
At either guarded loop entry, the conditional-decrement step sees zero,
changes no register, and immediately exits to retained ordinal 3850 or 3893.
Thus each deleted reachable segment is exactly one stuttering step, not an
unbounded hidden computation. The initial ordinal 136 is retained.

Delete the indicated intervals and map each retained old ordinal to its new
rank. Bypass any old edge into a deleted interval to that interval's retained
successor. Two independent source-control resolvers check that every new
retained edge is exactly the transported old edge with this bypass, including
both decrement branches. Induction matches every retained boundary in either
direction; the possible inserted segments are finite and nonhalting. The
halt occurrence remains retained. This establishes the claimed correspondence.
\end{proof}
The portable checker verifies syntax, both control-flow resolutions, every
retained edge, the external-entry census, and the following metrics. It does
not reprove the zero-guard invariant or certify a new binary interpreter.
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
Source & Primitive occurrences & Distinct syntax DAG nodes & Depth\\\midrule
Original & 3,909 & 197 & 42\\
First deletion only & 3,906 & 198 & 42\\
Second deletion only & 3,907 & 199 & 42\\
Both deletions & 3,904 & 200 & 42\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
Maximum zero-run length stays six. Removing executed syntax does not necessarily
improve subtree sharing: local edits can destroy repeated factors. In the
current binary table, relevant decoder entries 19/28 and 103/108 also serve
many retained source occurrences. Their global deletion is therefore not
justified by the two local guards. These DAG counts describe one syntax and
are not Turing-state lower bounds. A different representation or compiler
could behave differently; no 208-to-207 conclusion follows from this pruning.

\section{Runtime derived windows and their proof scope}
A different context source projects the full symbolic tape before every
literal inference in the 5,084 primitive contracts, together with every
configuration of the exact blank startup. For radius three, a window records
the cells at offsets $-3,\ldots,3$, with the least significant bit representing
$-3$. This brings source-boundary information into the local search rather
than relying solely on a short backward transition history.

Projection deliberately forgets correlations. A nonconstant nonnegative
affine run length $c+\sum_i a_i x_i$ permits all lengths at least $c$; a
constant run has its exact length. Only the requested prefix width is relevant.
Opaque atoms and finite-list atoms permit every bit prefix of that width.
The infinite blank exterior contributes only zeros; left and right choices
are combined independently. Every such relaxation enlarges the set of possible
windows and is therefore safe for proving a local identity on all of them.

A compressed unary or list traversal can cross configurations absent from the
literal-inference endpoints. The conservative projection assigns every window
to states used in such opaque traversals, including literal states occurring
in a proved list block. It does not infer internal invariants from endpoint
summaries. The specialized lane retains this conservative treatment and tightens only
the source-register categories justified by a separate invariant.

\begin{lemma}[Coverage of the projected windows]
Assume the inherited source semantics and full-frame symbolic runtime
contracts. Every working-state configuration of the intended blank
computation has its seven-cell window in the set recorded for that state.
The specialized sets have the same property when the checked source-category
invariant is also used.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The entire finite startup and its endpoint are explicitly included. Thereafter
source semantics decomposes execution into primitive contracts. At a literal
inference, each concrete affine-run length and atom realization is among the
projection's permitted choices, and discarding correlations can only enlarge
them. Orientation is explicit: the left prefix has length three or four,
according to which stream holds the current cell, and is reversed before
concatenation with the right prefix. Zero-length runs retain their empty case.
Every internal state of an opaque compressed traversal receives all windows.
A contract endpoint is the next complete source frame or halt. Induction over
contracts therefore covers all intermediate times. Specialization restricts
only stores and branches already excluded by the source-category invariant,
so the same proof applies to every reachable store.
\end{proof}

\section{Reproduction and the remaining constructive obligation}
No completed bounded test provides a validated 207-state machine. Failure
can reflect the representation, overapproximation, edit family, or cutoff.
It is not an exhaustive search or a lower bound.

\subsection{The offset simulation principle}
For a deleted state $q$ represented by $r$ at offset $k$, use the boundary map
$F(q,h,T)=(r,h+k,T)$ and $F(s,h,T)=(s,h,T)$ otherwise. An ordinary retained
row entering $q$ changes its displacement from $d$ to $d+k$; legality requires
$d+k\in\{-1,1\}$. At $k=\pm2$, all such entrances must point the opposite
way. If every exceptional row has a positive, nonhalting, complete-tape macro
to the image of its old successor, induction proves halting equivalence just
as in Theorem~4.1. For $q=0$, translating the homogeneous blank tape permits
initial head 0, but every later runtime occurrence of $q$ still needs its
macro proof. No successful offset candidate is claimed.

\subsection{Open constructive routes}
Three precise extensions remain open; none is promised to save a state.
First, the dead-loop deletion needs new sharing or allocation, since the fixed
AST edit costs three DAG nodes. Second, simulation boundaries could permit
controlled tape differences or phase-dependent offsets, beyond the present
complete-tape and constant-offset templates. Third, a richer source-boundary
guard domain could preserve correlations lost in seven- and nine-bit windows,
especially inside the 25 conservatively opaque engine states.

Any advance must supply an actual total table of at most 207 working states,
including initialization, with a sound boundary invariant, progress and halt
reflection, and the retained proof-search calibration. A new table requires
its own validation; relabeling a 208-state table or citing an unrelated
certificate cannot close this obligation. The target remains unmet.
