\begin{abstract}
The requested strict state-count target has not been achieved: this archive contains no accepted blank-start PA-consistency machine with fewer than 196 working states. It provides a pinned comparison of the supplied 208-, 209-, and 196-state families; two newly verified 196-state implementations of the diagonal-vacuity approach; verified scanner-oriented 196-state layouts; and reproducible certificates for the attempted further reductions. The diagonal implementation improves the incoming 199-state realization to 196 states. Separating its diagonal clause as a head shortens the AX word further, from 191 to 190 macro items, while retaining 196 physical states.

The comparison distinguishes thirty-seven supplied accepted presentations in thirty transition-graph classes. The hybrid of U208's outer program and PA196's AX passes every primitive source-frame contract at 196 states but fails blank startup. The report explains that obstruction, rejected optimistic 195-state counts, restricted allocation obstructions, and the exact scope of the bounded physical searches. It also separates the published PA-internal nonhalting-implies-consistency theorem from the additional written proof translation required for the full PA-provable biconditional. No new Lean-kernel theorem or general 196-state lower bound is claimed.
\end{abstract}
