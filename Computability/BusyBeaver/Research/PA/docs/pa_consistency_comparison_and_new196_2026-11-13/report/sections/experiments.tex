\section{Why the attempted additional state savings were rejected}
\label{sec:experiments}
Negative searches in this section have explicitly bounded templates. They do not establish a lower bound of 196 on PA-consistency machines. A valid smaller machine may require a different encoding, program, invariant, or simultaneous edit outside these templates.

A failed contract on arbitrary source values, or on values admitted by an abstract invariant, refutes that proposed simulation proof. The admitted values can overapproximate actual reachability, so such a failure does not alone disprove the edited machine's property from blank tape. The explicit outward-loop startup traces below are a different kind of evidence: they describe the actual blank computation. The literal short-scanner edit also has a concrete failure from the intended clean source seed, while its own blank-start behavior remains unproved.

\subsection{An optimistic 195-state count was not a construction}
A diagonal AX association had seventy raw reader nodes and nine individually available pair identifications. Subtracting nine from seventy suggested sixty-one reader states and hence $134+61=195$ total states. That count failed two distinct checks. Some macro occurrences entered through a side on which their existing physical implementation was not valid. After correcting those entries, several selected folds interfered with helpers used by other folds.

The replacement compatibility checker applies the proposed redirects simultaneously and replays the local obligations against that final table. The surviving jointly compatible set was smaller, and the optimistic 195-state table was rejected. The accepted 196-state tables in Section~\ref{sec:constructions} pass the stronger check and then the complete runtime check.

The search objective was also corrected to reuse native controls 99 and 190 for the existing two- and four-increment reader patterns. Treating the corresponding literal binary tuples as newly allocated grammar nodes overcharges them. Mixed-integer grammar searches additionally considered repeated substrings and free existing routines. Their raw grammar costs are exploratory objectives until entry sides, scanner bounds, helper compatibility, and startup have all been checked.

The corrected all-helper search completed 160 cycles of 1600 attempted rotations, for 256,000 proposals. Its best effective AX cost remained 62, corresponding to 196 total states above the 134-control base. This is a heuristic search, not an exhaustive enumeration of binary trees. The scanner portfolio completed a further 30,000 attempted proposals and reached the six-obstruction layout in Section~\ref{sec:constructions}. Exact saved inputs and logs accompany the code. Finite follow-up screens on 98 saved cost-62 grammars and the scanner grammar found no additional action-bisimulation merge or compatible second folding round.

\subsection{Restricted allocation has explicit Hall obstructions}
Several incoming source layouts have only 157 or 158 internal nodes. A fixed-reader allocator attempts to retain recognized subtrees, protect the rows needed by the register engine and contextual helpers, and injectively assign the remaining new source nodes to available physical labels. Removing an exploratory ordering constraint from that allocator does not make these particular domains feasible.

\subsection{The U208/PA196 hybrid and its startup obstruction}
The source hybrid grafts PA196's AX into U208 A's outside program. It has 3810 primitive sites and 159 internal DAG nodes. A prefix-only allocator can bind this source to 196 controls, but its first allocation failed a complete primitive contract at site 23. Passing a decoder-prefix test is therefore not sufficient.

A second construction retains the actual U208 outside implementation and transplants the PA196 AX implementation through exact interface bindings. Initially the complete required table has 197 controls. An outside helper uses only its read-one row in all outside contracts, and that row matches a copied AX helper. Reusing its other row yields a 196-control table. The resulting table passes all 4957 arbitrary-value instruction contracts; all 392 transition rows occur in those contracts.

It nevertheless fails as a blank-start consistency machine. From native initial state 0, after 132 steps it reaches an outward rightward loop in native state 35 on untouched blank tape. Starting in native state 74 reaches the same obstruction after 54 steps. A finite screening of every possible existing initial control finds an explicit outward blank self-loop in each case. This is an actual startup failure, not merely an unfinished long simulation. The table is a correct interpreter of supplied source frames but is not the requested blank-tape machine.

The first divergence from the original U208 blank execution is at step 124, native state 11 reading one at head position 4. The old AX prefix consumes address bits \code{011} to enter the existing paired-increment control 99. The transplanted AX has a different association at that prefix. This identifies the lost bootstrap behavior precisely. Enforcing that prefix by reassociation restores an exact source frame after 47,068 steps at the original header distance 56, but the straightforward repair costs an extra control and introduces two seven-zero addresses. A complete runtime test of site 842, decrementing register 1 in its zero case, fails. The repaired 197-state table therefore lacks the claimed runtime certificate, despite its successful finite startup; that failed arbitrary-frame obligation does not decide its actual blank behavior.

The diagonal and separated-diagonal AX versions likewise give 196-state candidate interpreters when grafted into U208's outer layout; their sources have 3698 and 3671 sites. Construction and prefix binding pass, but their full runtime contracts were not rerun. Their initial-control screens already fail before a clean frame. Thus neither shorter logical word by itself repairs the bootstrap.

\subsection{Bounded physical state-fold searches}
The baseline source has 4959 complete instruction contracts. A stronger local enclosure projects these contracts to nine tape cells, four on either side of the head, and combines them with the independent local blank-window enclosure. It admits 12,168 control/window pairs. The synthesis template identifies one ordered pair of states and permits two arbitrary changed rows in retained states, with positive simulated blocks of at most 32 steps and exact endpoint control, head, and all nine tape cells.

All 38,220 ordered baseline folds were screened. The run made 174,570,928 first-row action trials and 1,866,384,912 second-row action trials. Of 803 representative-filter passes, none survived all obligations. Counts concern trials of the template, not distinct machine isomorphism classes.

A separate limited three-row search extended the 633 representative passes from the earlier five-cell, sixteen-step two-row screen. The third changed row had to lie on every remaining failed trace. It tested 1400 eligible third-row keys and 1,097,600 actions, without a survivor. It does not cover three-row candidates whose first two repairs fail that representative filter.

Fresh two-row screens were also run on two previously accepted rotated PA196 layouts, with 2223 and 2217 five-cell contexts. Their complete contracts and startup were rechecked before searching. These bounded screens produced no additional fold. The exact receipts specify the additional new layouts and completed scopes; interrupted runs are not counted as exhaustive negatives.

\subsection{Head-offset state identifications}
Another template permits a removed state $q$ to be represented by a retained state $p$ with a two-cell head offset. If every incoming transition has direction $d\in\{-1,1\}$, redirect it to $p$ and reverse its direction. The child head is then $-2d$ relative to the parent head when the parent is in $q$, with identical tape. Outside $q$, the heads and controls agree. Modified helper states must have positive exact simulation blocks restoring the relation.

All 33,345 uniform-incoming ordered pairs failed without an additional row repair. With one arbitrary retained-row repair, 44,853 eligible keys and 35,164,752 actions were tested, again without a survivor. The remaining 4875 mixed-direction pairs were screened under a relation remembering the incoming direction; 246,176 single-helper actions produced no survivor. These screens use nine cells and at most 64 simulated steps.

The stronger two-helper uniform-offset screen also completed on all 33,345 pairs: 179,423,888 first-row and 14,854,027,776 second-row action trials produced no survivor. These are the counts of this bounded synthesis procedure, not a count of all possible 195-state machines.

A separate six-state positive control recovers the expected deletion of state 3 into state 4 at offset $-2$, on all 512 nine-cell windows at every state. That test detects mistakes in the nontrivial offset handling. It does not establish completeness of the PA196 search beyond the stated template.

\subsection{Finite register invariants}
A trit abstraction records each logical register as zero, one, or at least two. Increment and decrement use their exact sound abstract transfers; a decrement from ``at least two'' may produce either one or at least two. From the canonical source seed, the new diagonal program reaches 4,319,427 abstract site/register pairs. A separate closure audit checks 4,848,294 abstract edges.

The invariant proves a few branches impossible and validates several alternative closing blocks. The explicit halt site remains abstractly reachable. In particular, the unreachable site 8 is a continuation, not the halt instruction. This finite analysis is not a proof of the machine's nonhalting and does not contradict the required incompleteness phenomenon.

The site number matters: the zero condition at source site 3661 does not justify deleting the different decrement at site 3648. A proposed deletion of native reader 174 based on that mistaken correspondence was withdrawn. The corresponding 195-state edit is not an accepted construction.

Projecting the finite invariant to the nine register coordinates yields sound marginal ranges at each site. The full affine checker verifies 5149 restricted cases. Marginals alone leave the strongest previous five-cell context set unchanged at 2210 windows. A separate induction gives a useful tape invariant: the startup has twenty-six spare blocks of value one, and primitives preserve those values while increments append a further one-valued spare. Instantiating the first spare as one and checking the periodic interiors of the remaining list reduces the admitted set to 2173 windows. The complete 58,154-step startup is included separately. The accompanying source notes explain why testing twenty-five periodic blocks covers every longer list for these observation radii; this is an unbounded periodicity argument rather than an assumed finite spare limit.

At radius four, the corresponding nine-cell enclosure shrinks from 11,962 to 10,749 control/window pairs. Its completed two-row, 32-step screen again covers all 38,220 ordered folds: 174,648,544 first-row and 1,904,561,008 second-row trials leave no survivor after 797 representative-filter passes. The corresponding 64-step uniform-offset screen covers all 33,345 ordered pairs and 35,668,864 helper actions, again without a survivor. Completed five-cell screens and four alternative closing-block screens likewise produce no accepted deletion; their exact inputs and receipts are retained.

The separate-head source used by the final scanner experiment has 4,270,098 reachable abstract pairs and 4,792,890 audited abstract edges. Its halt remains abstractly reachable. Even after intersecting the short-scanner obligations with those trit marginals and the one-valued-spare invariant, the site-0 zero-decrement obligation fails. This sharper invariant therefore does not repair the proposed 195-state simulation proof.
