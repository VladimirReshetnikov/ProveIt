motif is absent even after state renaming. This excludes only that literal

Theorem boundary
The published PA196 inventory explicitly exports PA |- N196 -> Con(PA),
external nonhalting, and independence. The reverse internal implication is
not an exported endpoint there. This is a proof-interface gap, not evidence
that the converse is false. Several incoming reports supply written reverse
proof-translation arguments; none is a new completed internal Lean certificate.
Use the task's desired PA biconditional only after separately justifying both
directions, not by combining external truth with the exported one-way theorem.
