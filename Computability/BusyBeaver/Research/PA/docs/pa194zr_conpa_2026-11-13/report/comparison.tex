\subsection{A reproducible, explicitly scoped census}
The census recursively opens each distinct ZIP payload, retains every
reference to it, and recognizes complete numeric, five-column binary
transition tables in the supported text formats. Each recognized table
has two rows for every working control and exactly one undefined terminal
control. Direct PA tables and the six nested archives retained in the
filed historical packets are included. The recognizer does not discover
machines embedded only in arbitrary code or JSON; the manuscript comparison
supplements that deliberately narrow parser.

These counts include ancestors, repeated reference tables, rejected
experiments and small test controls. They are not counts of correct PA
consistency machines. Two disconnected synthetic tables are excluded from
the graph-class count. The recorded graph signature minimizes deterministic
BFS serialization over all initial controls that reach the whole working
graph. It retains the read and write symbols, directions and terminal
control, while forgetting the selected initial control. Thus unequal
signatures imply inequivalence under a stronger relation than merely
renaming states while preserving the designated start. Separate tests
handle reflection and global symbol interchange.

\subsection{The 194-state frontier before this work}
The supplied eight graph classes contain seven constructions with
implementation evidence and one documented rejection. The three original
194-state machines use the same 190-token separate-head emitter and the
original Shift/MP protocol. The later cached protocol changes the source
semantics, even when the arithmetic axiom family is unchanged.

``Outcomes'' counts both branches of each decrement. Startup means an
exact finite run to the entire clean source-frame tape; it is not evidence
about the eventual halting behavior. Full table hashes and locators appear
in \file{comparison/frontier194.csv}.

Five new packets principally deliver the identical PA194C table, whose
SHA-256 begins \texttt{d759313c}. They should not be counted as five new
machines. PA194CS is also already supplied: it omits a restore loop and
exchanges the operands of a destructive equality comparison, whose normal
exit clears both operands. Its extra reader is offset by a contextual fold.
PA194CZ replaces source instruction 26 by a decrement of a register proved
zero at that point. The alternate eight-fold cached machine appears as a
\emph{validated search input} in \file{pa194c_conpa.zip}; promoting it from
a search directory to a headline would not make it a new construction.

The rejected eighth class illustrates the verification boundary. Its local
folds and resolved source graph pass their checks, but its source addresses
violate the physical return scanner's delimiter condition. Enlarging the
scanner repairs the implementation at 195 states. A successful local
quotient check cannot replace a complete physical execution argument.

\subsection{Larger designs and contributions that survive comparison}
The original PA195 uses a bundled diagonal-vacuity formula, a 191-token
emitter, 3,700 source sites and 4,815 outcomes. The separate-head variant
has 190 tokens and 3,673 sites. The cached 195C and bundled 195DC variants
change where successful modus ponens stores its conclusion and where
Shift checks for contradiction. Other 195-state results include the
repaired eight-zero-scanner implementation and alternative allocations.
The broader 195-state census has 26 byte forms and 16 graph classes,
including known failures; those numbers do not certify every table.

The original PA196 has 3,812 source sites and 4,959 outcomes. Its important
successors combine diagonal vacuity, separated emitted heads, source-tree
association, physical register allocation, cached commands, and invariant
based jumps. The broad 196-state census has 163 byte forms and 99 graph
classes. Some experiments actually halt on blank input after 43,563 steps;
others fail universal source contracts. They cannot be treated as
consistency-machine improvements merely because their tables are small.

The U208 family contributes the method of recovering a merged reader's
identity from neighboring tape history. The earlier positive-design
catalogue identifies twenty graph classes among twenty-seven selected
presentations; the much broader current census includes generated
experiments and therefore has larger counts. The U209-to-U208 history
fold and the PA209-to-PA196 source redesign overlap in their inherited
machinery. Their nominal state savings cannot simply be added.

The ZFC work~\cite{zfc} motivates finite-history reader quotients and source-level
invariants. ConciseIPRA~\cite{ipra} motivates a different but complementary lesson:
proof presentations may be changed by explicit proof translations.
Neither importing an iPRA checker nor proving its consistency would by
itself give the requested PA-consistency machine. Every transfer used here
retains the PA refutation predicate as its logical endpoint.

\subsection{Mathematical proofs and the formal endpoint}
At the pinned revision the PA README explicitly says that neither
machine-specific direction of the desired \emph{iPRA} equivalence has
been established for the primary PA194. Its machine-specific conditional
endpoints must therefore remain conditional in this comparison. The
201-parent/194-dense quotient, zipper bridges, source coverage infrastructure,
and arithmetic clock equations are valuable pieces of formal verification;
they do not automatically assemble the missing internal endpoint.

This is compatible with the ordinary mathematical PA proofs in the
research manuscripts. Those proofs give syntactic transformations from PA
refutations to numerical-calculus refutations and back, command compilers,
and positive finite physical simulations. The inherited reverse
translation handles the numerical aliases at truth code zero and the
empty Cantor stack, fresh diagonal binders, and coincident variable labels.
It uses a uniform \emph{proof constructor} for finite binary-set
comprehension, not a truth predicate or an assumption of PA reflection.
The present report states exactly which written calibration is inherited
and supplies the additional finite machine and source arguments needed
for its new construction. No new end-to-end Lean or Rocq theorem is claimed.
