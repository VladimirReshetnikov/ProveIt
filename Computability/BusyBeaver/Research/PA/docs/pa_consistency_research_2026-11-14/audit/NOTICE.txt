The machine tables are exact copies of research artifacts contributed by James
Chang and the ProveIt contributors, from the archive/member identities and
SHA-256 values in selected196.json. They are not newly constructed machines.
The upstream PA196 publication snapshot is jamesyc/BusyBeaver at
407cdc356f6b586635e71ac92be394255f4bfea4. The upstream PA209 predecessor is
69d98241a721ead20b4f0b17df9062950b40fbfe. The repository census is pinned to
VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt at 906367250a7f96c923147b10df392294bced7be5.

The all-depth inventory programs audit_all_archives.py and audit_archives.py
are adapted from the corresponding programs in
pa_tm_research_2026-11-13 (2).zip!pa_tm_research_2026-11-13/comparison/.
The adaptation changes the pin to the current snapshot. The table parser and
canonicalizer are retained with their root-free scope. Their input data are
original repository ZIP files; the full ZIP corpus is not bundled again.
The independent verify_selected.py is new for this comparison and checks
rooted, action-preserving graph identities for the selected exact tables.

The classifications 'candidate', 'experimental' and 'rejected' preserve the
source manuscripts' proof-status distinctions. A candidate status is not a
new completed arithmetic biconditional or proof-assistant certificate.
The fresh replay logs repeat the supplied checkers' stated scope. Symbolic
instruction contracts invoke inherited register-engine interface contracts;
a finite startup trace is not evidence of nonhalting by itself.
