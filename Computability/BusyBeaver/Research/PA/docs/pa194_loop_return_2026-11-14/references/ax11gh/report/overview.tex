\section{Why the new designs are materially different}

The new architecture combines an explicit generalization binder,
an eleven-entry axiom operation with four encoded operands,
modus ponens without a stack push, and a cache-only Shift halt test.
Each ingredient is credited prior work; the new combined recurring
interface and its corrected proof compiler were absent from the corpus.
The immediate predecessors are the AX11G, GH/GHS, and AX11H packets~\cite{ax11g,gh,ax11h}.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
Design & Generalization binder & AX entries / fields & Shift halt test\\
\midrule
AX11G & explicit operand $c$ & 11 / 4 & $t+h=1$\\
GH, GHS & explicit operand $c$ & 10 / 3 & $h=1$\\
AX11QH & incoming offset $d$ & 11 / 4 & $h=1$\\
\textbf{AX11GH, AX11GHS} & \textbf{explicit $c$} & \textbf{11 / 4} & $\boldsymbol{h=1}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

There are simple operational witnesses, beyond graph inequality. With
incoming store $(t,w,d,h)=(0,0,0,0)$ and first AX operand $c=1$, the
new command saves $U(1,0)=10$; the offset-binder command saves
$U(0,0)=2$. With top $t=1$ and cache $h=0$, the older sum-guard Shift
halts, while the new cache-only Shift continues. Consequently a
compiler that merely leaves contradiction in the visible top and issues
one Shift would be incorrect. The new compiler uses the precise Pop
identity, including its early-halting case.

The generalization word is
\[
 \Gen_i=\Push;\Shift;\Ax_{i,0,0,0};\Shift^{11}.
\]
It maps $(a,w,0,0)$ to $(U(i,a),w,0,0)$ even when $a=1$.
The explicit binder removes the offset-setting MP needed by the
offset-binder generalizer. This shortens a recurring proof operation;
it does not reduce the physical state count. The GHS implementation
then removes three primitive sites from the recurring Shift routine by
reversing the destructive comparison operands.

All four literal tables were compared against all 110 prior complete
194-state classes. For each candidate, the multiset of working-state
indegree, number of self-loops, and equality of its two successor controls
differs from every prior class. This exact invariant is preserved under
arbitrary state renaming, a different spanning root, tape reflection,
global bit exchange, and erasure of write/move decorations on terminal
edges. Hence the novelty claim is stronger than unequal file hashes.
Within one family, its base and accelerated table share that invariant;
776 exact rooted/symmetry comparisons distinguish that pair. The full
tuple comparisons and all prior representatives are bundled.

\section{The attempt to reduce the state count}
\label{sec:search}

The main obstacle remains the difference between a correct runtime core
and a complete blank-start machine. The earlier 193-state core retains
the old source contracts after a contextual fold, but its blank startup
does not establish the required computation. This investigation used that
core as a search input and never counted it as a successful construction~\cite{reduction}.

\subsection{Broader local quotient searches}

The new physical screen allows two-sided stuttering: a replacement path
may rejoin any positive bounded future reference configuration, instead
of being required to reach only the reference's one-step successor.
Both full local tapes, head positions, and controls modulo a proposed
quotient must agree. Blank-closure windows or recorded source envelopes
bound the input contexts. These are explicit restrictions of a search

\section{Ideas from the other requested research trees}

The other three trees were reviewed for mechanisms, with the target
theory and literal machine cost kept fixed~\cite{other}.

\paragraph{ZFC.}
The ZFC work supplies useful methods for command-local fields,
history-dependent folds, exact source closure, and the distinction
between compiled finite verification and arithmetic internalization.
Its different theory-calibration endpoints cannot simply replace the
PA endpoint. The useful 193-state runtime-fold idea was already
available in the PA research, where blank initialization is the
remaining obstacle. Reusing a compiled runtime without its required
initialization would not answer the present question.

\paragraph{DiophantineConsistency.}
The decisive lesson is to state exact primitive-recursive witness
transformations and verify the correct domains in all models of the
base arithmetic. A small number of polynomial variables is not a
state bound for a binary blank-start evaluator. The delivered proof
therefore makes the proof-to-word compiler, scheduling bound, source
clock, and physical clock explicit, and uses Boolean induction
invariants rather than an empirical standard-model search claim.

\paragraph{ConciseIPRA.}
The compact scalar presentation and iterator transport are useful
for organizing a weak-arithmetic proof of the machine. They do not
by themselves lower the cost of a complete PA proof checker, formula
decoder, register engine, and blank bootstrap. Any new checker based
on that syntax must be costed as a literal binary table and supplied
with the same two consistency directions. No unsupported state saving
is inferred from a short axiomatization.

\subsection{Specific next experiments}

The most direct path to 193 remains a jointly designed runtime fold and
bootstrap. Candidate repairs should be synthesized with their full
source invariants, since individually neutral edits miss transformations
that are correct only together. The new AX11GH source also leaves decoder
association and contextual-fold combinations that are not exhausted by
pairwise quotient screens. A second route is a more global reachable
program invariant: the arbitrary-source-frame contracts intentionally
overapproximate reachable proof-search configurations, and may prevent
a fold that is sound on the actual scheduler. Such an improvement needs
a weak-arithmetic invariant, not only a finite simulation.

A change to the numerical axiom system may be more substantial, but
requires redoing both proof translations. In particular, simply deleting
an apparently redundant head or using the already rejected coefficient
three collision does not supply a calibrated smaller machine. On the
formalization side, the present finite contracts and explicit clocks
give a concrete next target: a shared kernel proof of symbolic-rule
soundness, followed by replay of the new finite certificates and the
identified inherited PA compiler.
