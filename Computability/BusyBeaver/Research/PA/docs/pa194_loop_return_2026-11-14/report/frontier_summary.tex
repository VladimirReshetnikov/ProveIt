A short exact invariant proves novelty against every one of the 232
normalized tables. For each control $q$ form the triple consisting of its
working-state indegree, its number of self-loop rows, and the Boolean saying
whether its two successors agree. Compare the histogram of these triples.
Each new histogram differs from every prior histogram, with an explicit
unequal multiplicity retained in the certificate. The two new machines
are separated from one another by the triple $(1,0,0)$: its multiplicities
are 86 and 85. This argument does not depend on a hash-based isomorphism
assumption.

The invariant is unaffected by control renaming, choice of start root,
global tape reflection, global symbol exchange, or write/move changes on
terminal edges. Thus the new graphs remain distinct under these broader
cosmetic identifications. Symbol exchange is only a stronger structural
comparison here; it is not asserted to preserve a fixed zero-blank
convention by itself. The frozen prior tables, source locators, multiplicity
witnesses and a portable verifier are supplied in the comparison directory.
