\subsection{A stronger envelope for the arithmetic controls}
\label{sec:literal-contexts}

A new collector for the exact PA194LCG table refines the contexts inside
the arithmetic engine. Earlier source-specific screens deliberately left
these controls under the generic tape-window abstraction because their
source checker treated arithmetic boxes as opaque operations. The new
collector instead instruments the independent literal checker: the four
finite-list sweep identities are first rederived from the transition rows,
and the interiors of those sweeps and of affine unary scans are included.
No arithmetic-box oracle is used.

For radius six, the completed counts are as follows. The new envelope is
a subset of the generic closure; all 194 working controls remain present.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
Population & Generic closure & Literal envelope\\
\midrule
All working controls & 271,895 & 49,297\\
The 23 arithmetic controls & 119,150 & 18,570\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{Coverage argument.}
The literal checker represents tapes by affine-length constant-symbol
runs, blank exteriors, and arbitrary finite lists of blocks
\[
 B(v)=0\,1^{v+1},\qquad C(v)=1\,0\,1^v.
\]
For each requested prefix length, the collector enumerates every possible
prefix of those streams. A nonconstant affine run with constant term $c$
is conservatively allowed every length at least $c$. A finite list is
expanded as empty or one block followed by another list; each block has
length at least two, so a fixed-length prefix query terminates. Reversed
streams are handled in the same way. Correlations between parameters on
the two sides of the head are discarded, enlarging the envelope.

If a self-loop scans a run of length $L$, an intermediate position has
$u$ written symbols behind the head and $L-u$ unread symbols ahead,
where $0\le u<L$. Every such radius-six window is included. For this
observation, lengths above $13=2\cdot6+1$ may be represented by length 13:
a window sees one endpoint or an interior stretch, and cannot distinguish
a greater separation of the endpoints. Shorter admissible lengths are
enumerated separately.

For a position inside a list traversal, the collector replays the literal
one-block identity with an arbitrary already-processed output-family
prefix and an arbitrary remaining input-family suffix. Empty choices cover
the first and last blocks. The one-block passage has duration $v+2$ and
is checked with arbitrary surrounding streams. Consequently its literal
and unary-interior windows cover every position of every finite list
sweep, without bounding list length or entries. The inherited identity
$C(A)1=1B(A)$ and its reversal justify the checker's list normalization.

The completed derivation replays all \textbf{4,778 outcomes at 3,671 source
sites}, using 1,105,118 symbolic steps, at most 385 per outcome. The
whole 57,870-step blank initialization is included separately. The checked
source-frame contracts compose these envelopes over standard finite runs;
the complete initialization supplies their starting frame. This is an
executable symbolic coverage argument with the stated stream invariants,
not an additional kernel proof of the absolute arithmetic calibration.

\paragraph{Implementation check.}
Separately, 1,200 complete literal command executions used varied register
values, header gaps and spare lists. Their 551,331 transitions encountered
zero missing windows; the longest sample took 1,014 transitions. Each run
continued to its expected complete successor frame, not merely to an
occurrence of the dispatcher control. These finite examples check the
collector's implementation; they do not replace the coverage argument.

\subsection{Reduction searches using the stronger envelope}

Three completed searches used the 49,297-window envelope:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{p{0.34\linewidth}rccr}
\toprule
Template & Targets & Old bound & New bound & Hits\\
\midrule
Two-row quotient & 18,721 pairs & 1 & 100 & 0\\
Two-sided stuttering quotient & 18,721 pairs & 12 & 128 & 0\\
Incoming-edge deletion & 193 controls & 24 & 256 & 0\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
All bounds count literal transitions within the observed window. The
quotient searches synthesize both rows of the retained control and allow
repeated visits to it. They respectively made 26,213,260 and 26,387,880
first-action trials, then checked 174,903 and 177,086 joint combinations.

Incoming-edge deletion instead rewrites each incoming row independently,
allowing either write bit, either direction, and any remaining working
control or the actual halt label as its first successor. Each replacement
must meet a positive old prefix with identical control, head position and
entire window. Its detour may not execute a removed control or a rewritten
row, so the separately chosen replacements cannot invalidate one another.
All 193 non-start deletion targets were considered.

A further targeted check of controls 83 and 190, using the generic
radius-six closure, allowed 48 old steps and 512 replacement steps. It
checked 1,552 first actions and 81 joint combinations, again without a
candidate. These results exclude only the displayed finite templates.
They are neither a general lower bound nor evidence for promoting the
failed prepared-tape Runtime193 core to a blank-start machine.

\paragraph{Artifacts and replay.}
The envelope, detailed proof notes, three synthesizers, source hashes,
sample checks and full search logs are in
\file{research/literal_contexts/}. From the archive root, run
\begin{verbatim}
python3 research/literal_contexts/reproduce_context_research.py \
  --output /tmp/lcg-context-replay
\end{verbatim}
The default regenerates the symbolic envelope and reruns the samples and
three primary searches. The optional \file{--reuse-contexts} uses the
hash-checked frozen envelope and records that omission. A separate
integration replay with that option passed against the delivered machine
and reference files, including fresh compilation of all three programs;
its receipt is retained. Full envelope generation was completed separately.
