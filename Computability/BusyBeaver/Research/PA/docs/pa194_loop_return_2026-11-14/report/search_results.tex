The fresh finite-window search computes the least abstract closure of
state/window pairs from the blank configuration, allowing either newly
exposed boundary bit. Its soundness is a direct induction on time: every
concrete successor is one of the admitted abstract successors. It then
looks for a single transition whose endpoint equals an old path of at most
31 transitions, without accessing beyond the radius-seven window and
preserving every cell except the initial write cell.

For AX11GHF, the closure has 912,456 abstract configurations and the search
finds no additional shortcut. For the loop-reassociated AX11GHF it has
915,400 configurations and finds the new $190/1$ three-to-one shortcut.
After applying that shortcut, the closure has 913,352 configurations and
no further shortcut in this family. All 194 controls remain nonempty in
each abstract closure. These are exact finite closure results and a bounded
shortcut search, not finite-run estimates.
