COMPARISON OF THE CURRENT PA CONSISTENCY MACHINE CORPUS
Pinned source: VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt@1c9524d8bf2e0a27b9a16ff622359927f536e663

1. Scope and evidence

The current corpus contains 61 filed PA research packets and 11 incoming ZIP
archives, for 72 deliveries. The complete ledger is packet_ledger72.json/csv.
All 62 top-level filed README payloads were independently fetched/read from
this commit and checked against their Git blob identities; their SHA-256
values match the earlier 66-delivery ledger. The five older incoming archive
hashes also match. The six later arrivals were individually inspected.
Historical descriptions are attributed evidence; old PA correctness proofs
were not all independently rebuilt or formalized in this comparison.

All currently tracked .tm/.txt/.tsv/.csv/.json and .zip files under PA, plus
all 11 incoming archives and nested archives, were scanned. No file-size
cutoff remains for these formats. Arbitrary executable generators, source
programs requiring execution, TeX-only tables, .jsonl, and binary encodings
are outside literal recognition. Their named proposals remain represented
at manuscript/README level. This is a precise recognizer scope, not a claim
to recover arbitrary machines from arbitrary programs.

Final scan: 9, 400 JSON payloads; 1, 867 TXT; 388 TSV; 209 CSV; 3, 356 TM;
37 ZIP references representing 25 unique ZIP payloads. Recognition found
3, 422 complete table occurrences and 752 normalized labeled tables.
Twenty-two .tm inputs were partial/nonconforming under the complete
one-halt recognizer. Thirty-five incidental one-state JSON fragments were
excluded. Both lists/counters remain in results/summary.json.

2. Frontier classification

There are 82 complete spanning-root 194-state action graphs. All 82 remain
distinct even allowing arbitrary state names, any spanning initial root,
left/right reflection, global symbol interchange, and arbitrary terminal
write/move effects. Two additional 194-row-state diagnostic graphs have no
spanning root and are kept separate; neither is an accepted consistency
construction. Symbol interchange is a structural test and need not
preserve the all-zero initial tape.

Historical evidence categories for the 82 spanning 194 graphs:
 40 reported principal constructions;
  7 supplementary constructions;
  5 reassociations with recorded full source contracts and blank startup;
 25 other research auxiliaries or seeds;
  5 explicitly rejected constructions.

There are 53 distinct complete 193-state graphs in the current literal
corpus, all research candidates. Earlier reports' statements that there
were only two 193 objects refer to their old snapshots. The latest QN6
packet alone contributes numerous variants to a bounded startup search.
None of the 53 is supplied with an accepted blank-start calibration to
Con(PA). The metadata distinguish rejection from mere lack of proof.

The smallest ordinary action-preserving bisimulation quotient of each of
the 82 spanning 194 graphs still has 194 states. This exact computation rules
out unconditional control merging in this narrow all-symbol-stream sense;
it does not rule out context-dependent folds, source transformations,
startup repairs, or an entirely different machine architecture. The two
disconnected diagnostics reduce to five classes and are irrelevant to the
consistency frontier.

3. What the 40 principal designs actually change

principal_designs40.json/csv gives a separate row for each distinct
principal graph, with constructor coefficient, quantifier head, diagonal
seed, AX interface, successful MP, Shift halt condition, comparator order,
and specific source/layout change. All 40 have 194 working states.

Their underlying machine architecture is substantially shared: numerical
proof search, command-word enumeration with persistent stores, a unary
register-file implementation, and a compressed source decoder. Variations
occur in the formula encoding, emitter, command-store protocol, decoder
association, and proved-zero return conventions.

The main mechanisms are:
* The baseline 67-reader/seven-fold and alternate 68-reader/eight-fold
  machines retain the ten-entry AX interface and sum halt test t+h=1.
* C sends a successful MP conclusion into the cached head, clears visible
  top/offset, and tests h=1. CS swaps the destructive comparison order.
* N removes the dummy-zero stack push and returns the conclusion visibly,
  preserving t+h=1. Its proof-word compiler needs the reserved-zero rule.
* H combines N's visible no-push MP with cached-only h=1 Shift; HS adds
  symmetric comparison. H is not the same as cached-result C.
* Q exchanges the curried premises of the universal-distribution head.
  Its K/S/MP exchange proof is distinct from controller optimizations.
* D emits Gamma(D) instead of Gamma(A), with changed selectors. DHS also
  uses the H/symmetric controller. Only these two principal designs use
  the Gamma(D) seed.
* Coefficients 5 and 6 replace 4 throughout formula constructors. The change
  can occupy one native transition row, yet changes all 61 multiplier
  sites, the arithmetic encoding, and proof translations. This is a new
  verified composition when not already present, not a new search method.
* J/F/A/X/ZR/HAZ replace selected jumps by decrements of registers proved
  zero; some combine A/D physical register placement. Such invariants do
  not by themselves prove correct blank startup.
* R and M4R reassociate the source decoder; resolved source identity and
  physical source simulation must be checked separately.
* AX11 pushes the final guard too, then falls through MP's full field
  reader. AX consumes a genuine fourth parameter z and leaves eleven
  stack-prefix entries (ten generated heads plus the saved old top).
  The original sum guard remains; the completeness compiler chooses z=0.

Among the 40 principal graphs there are 28 coefficient 4, six coefficient 5,
and six coefficient 6 designs; 12 use the Q premise exchange. Successful MP
is original dummy-push in 10, cached-result in 12, and visible no-push in 18.
Twenty-one use the sum guard; 19 use cached-only Shift. Fourteen use the
symmetric comparison. These are structural/protocol classifications,
not forty independently rechecked consistency theorems.

5. Why the obvious 193 objects are not solutions

The coefficient-three shortcut compresses physical arithmetic but retains
an inconsistent numerical calculus. For the old equality offset 3, the
collision E(1, 0)=6=I(0, 1), together with the retained heads/rules, yields
contradiction code 1. The supplied primitive refutation has 73, 393 steps;
a later ordinary mathematical obstruction covers every nonnegative
constant equality offset for the specified unchanged head/rule family.
That is a restricted encoding obstruction, not a state lower bound.

Runtime193 folds native 301 into 88 with rows 88/0 ->0 L 51 and 51/0 ->0 R 122.
Its source contracts pass under a canonical initialized frame, but its
original blank start enters an outward ray. The corpus's elementary and
compound startup searches have not repaired this. The new AX11/QN6/QHS
variants of this attempt are unaccepted or explicitly failed experiments.

Other near misses demonstrate that source-equivalent edits can break
initialization: a recorded comparator-return patch halts after 1, 361 blank
steps despite its source contracts. Deleting a loop or choosing a lower
optimistic grammar score is therefore insufficient.

7. The iPRA requirement

Current PA/README.md explicitly says neither machine-specific direction
of the physical PA194 consistency equivalence has been established in
iPRA. isigma1-machine supplies a generic conditional endpoint, and the
modeled scheduler has stronger proved calibration results, but the
physical-machine bridge and PA-proof compiler remain open. Existing
ordinary written PA arguments must not be relabeled as iPRA proofs.
A new same-size graph with full source/runtime certificates still needs
these logical bridges to satisfy the user's strengthened requirement.

8. Reproducibility

census.py scans the full local pinned source tree and ZIP archives.
check_novelty.py recomputes exact canonical tuples from frozen tables.
results/representatives contains one representative of every frontier
class, including failed and diagnostic ones with explicit annotation.
results/frontier_annotated.json/csv gives mechanisms and evidence labels;
principal_designs40.json/csv is the requested design-level comparison;
packet_ledger72.json/csv accounts for every supplied delivery.

No bounded search failure, exact bisimulation result, or supplied evidence
category is presented as a lower bound or an end-to-end iPRA theorem.
