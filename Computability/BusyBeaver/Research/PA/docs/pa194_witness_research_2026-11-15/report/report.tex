\begin{abstract}
This investigation constructs two new deterministic, single-tape, two-symbol
Turing machines with 194 working controls and a separate halt control.  Each
machine's blank-input nonhalting is equivalent to $\Con(\PA)$ in intuitionistic
primitive recursive arithmetic.  The written proof combines a new
uniform axiom translation, newly checked source and physical bindings,
and the explicitly retained revised AX11H mathematical calibration.
No completed end-to-end Lean proof is claimed.

The first machine replaces the fifth numerical axiom family
$U(D,E(H,D)\to1)\to1$ by $U(D,E(H,D)\to1)\to A$, where $A$ is
independent of $D,H$.  It uses the ordinary orientation of the sixth
head, the explicit-binder generalizer, and the eleven-entry AX command.
Its 189-token emitter has 3,677 primitive source sites; its literal budget
is $134+69-9=194$.  Independent literal execution covers all 4,786 source
outcomes, arbitrary primary registers and spare lists, and the complete
57,922-transition blank initialization.  The table is different from all
139 spanning 194-control graphs recognized in the pinned source corpus,
even under an enlarged structural equivalence.  The second machine also
replaces the first head $(X\to1)\to H$ by $(X\to H)\to H$, where
$X=U(D,U(D,H)\to1)$.  Its shorter, 188-token emitter independently passes
all 4,794 literal outcomes and the same complete blank initialization.
Its table is new under the same comparison and distinct from the first.

The comparison accounts for 87 filed packets and six incoming deliveries.
No machine below 194 states is established here.  Further conservative
axiom simplifications, unsuccessful reduction searches, precise finite
search scopes, and reproducible evidence are included rather than
interpreted as a lower bound.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, exact object, and proof status}
\label{sec:result}

The principal deliverables are the literal tables
\path{machines/PA194H5X/machine.tm} and
\path{machines/PA194JH5X/machine.tm}.  Each has controls $0,\ldots,193$,
start control $0$, alphabet $\{0,1\}$, and halt control $194$.
The tape is two-way infinite and initially all zero; the head initially
scans coordinate zero.  Each of its 388 rows has the format
\[
 \texttt{state\quad read\quad write\quad L/R\quad next}.
\]
Every nonhalting control has exactly one row for each scanned symbol.
Entering 194 halts after the specified write and movement.  State 194 is
not counted among the working controls.  All 194 working controls are
reachable in the transition graph; this is a graph statement, not a
claim that each is reached on the actual blank run.

\begin{theorem}[Main written theorem]
For either of the two exact blank-start machines, denoted by $M$,
\[
 \boxed{\quad\ipra\ \vdash\
  \neg\Halt_{\rm blank}(M)
       \ \longleftrightarrow\ \Con(\PA).\quad}
\]
Here $\Con(\PA)$ is the native consistency sentence fixed by the retained
Foundation/PA calibration, identified in Section~\ref{sec:calibration}.
\end{theorem}

The proof has four separable parts.  First, a new finite proof
transformation identifies the numerical theorem set after the witness
change.  Second, explicit command words identify refutations with halting
of an all-store scheduler.  Third, exact arithmetic source templates and
literal tape certificates identify that scheduler with the new binary
table.  Fourth, the revised AX11H calibration identifies the inherited
numerical calculus with the specified PA-refutation sentence.  The first
three parts are bound to the new machine here; the fourth is retained
with its exact archive and mathematical dependency record.

This is a written mathematical proof with executable finite certificates.
It is not a newly completed Lean or Rocq formalization, and the complete
PA-to-numerical proof compiler has not been implemented anew in this
investigation.  The current repository likewise leaves machine-specific
Lean endpoints open.  Neither a finite simulation nor a receipt labelled
PASS would by itself imply the displayed theorem.  The uniform induction
arguments and the identified logical calibration are essential.

No valid construction with 193 or fewer working states was obtained.
The two machines fulfill the requested same-size fallback through changed
logical emitters and new literal graphs.  The new witness head is not a
permutation of equality labels or a different association of the old
formula.  Its independent consequent permits the coupled first-head
change realized in PA194JH5X, and a further proved generalization supplies
a concrete additional reduction target.
