\subsection{Pinned scope and the meaning of the census}

The comparison is pinned to ProveIt revision
\path{8d31445d858761a8f6150c2e5012adae8895e932} \cite{pinned}.
It includes the complete supplied directories
\path{Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/PA} and \path{docs/incoming},
including recursively encountered ZIP payloads. The repository's filed-packet
ledger lists 87 deliveries; the six current incoming ZIP files give 93 delivery
records. A delivery is not necessarily a distinct design: several packages
repeat exactly the same table, and a single package may contain a principal
machine, alternative implementations, raw parents, and rejected searches.

All 27,459 files in the pinned PA/incoming manifest were independently checked
against their Git blob identities, totaling 1,253,117,203 bytes, with no
mismatch. The table recognizers inspected supported complete numeric
\path{.tm}, \path{.txt}, \path{.tsv}, \path{.csv}, and explicit JSON
representations. Recursive archive traversal found 24 distinct ZIP payloads
through 39 references. Archives are scanned once per byte identity while all
references are retained. The comparison does not execute arbitrary generators
or reconstruct a prose-only matrix from a PDF.

The resulting inventory contains 4,468 recognized matrix occurrences, 879
normalized labeled matrices, and 721 spanning action-graph classes. Seven
additional disconnected matrices are retained by literal identity. The latter
are not classified by a hash of their largest reachable component. Small
one-control and other diagnostic matrices are not proposed consistency
machines. Likewise, raw parents and failed search layouts are not promoted to
Con(PA) constructions merely because their rows are syntactically complete.

\subsection{Independent design axes at 194 states}

The primary PA194 budget is $134+67-7=194$: fixed inherited controls, AX readers,
and compatible folds. Other frontier machines use $134+68-8$, or adjust the
fixed/reader split. Counting syntax nodes, reader candidates or isolated
pairwise folds without checking their simultaneous physical realization is
not a valid machine-state calculation.

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{.20\textwidth}p{.25\textwidth}p{.48\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Axis & Representative names & What actually changes\\
\midrule\endhead
Numerical coding & radix 4, 5 and 6; N5/N6, Q5/Q6, H5/H6 &
Constructor coefficients, residues and formula codes. The numerical calculus
and exceptional small-code arguments need transport; the change is not just
a faster implementation of identical numbers.\\[4pt]
Logical emission & Q; separate or bundled diagonal; A/D diagonal &
Premise order of the distribution head, placement of diagonal vacuity, or the
chosen diagonal parameter. These alter emitted formulas or their stack
locations and require fixed proof translations.\\[4pt]
Command effects & N, C, H; Combined and reversed comparison &
Removal of a dummy MP push, caching of a theorem head, or moving the halt test
to the cached value. The contracts must hold above an arbitrary prior stack,
not merely when the stack is empty.\\[4pt]
AX interface & AX11 families &
Four command fields and eleven resulting stack entries replace the earlier
protocol. Generalization, axiom selection and stack ranks must be rebound to
the new interface.\\[4pt]
Generalization & G, GH/GHS, AX11G, AX11GH/GHS &
The incoming theorem is generalized using the explicit first AX operand
rather than the old offset. Completeness can avoid a preceding offset-setting
command, but the command compiler changes.\\[4pt]
Decoder/returns & 4L, AX11QH4L/R, LC; J/A/F/ZR returns &
Reassociation or loop continuations change physical addresses and reusable
histories; zero-register return variants change entry/exit behavior. Resolved
source control flow and startup must still be checked.\\[4pt]
History selectors & B and SEL &
Finite incoming histories allow recurring decoder pairs to share helpers.
The necessary contexts are stronger than transition-graph reachability.\\[4pt]
Physical layout & AD, P5, TDY, HX, AX11HR &
Register positions and routing targets change. A logical register permutation
must be accompanied by a whole-tape representation and startup proof.\\[4pt]
Local acceleration & AX11GHF and AX11GHSF &
Two transitions shortcut three-step detours under proved incoming-bit
invariants, preserving the entire tape and giving explicit relative clocks.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

These axes explain why composing two good ideas does not automatically save
another state. Both may depend on the same helper or incoming context; a new
source association can remove a formerly available fold. Conversely,
materially different command or logical designs can have the same total
state budget. The present new independent-consequent witness belongs to the
logical-emission axis: it changes a recurring axiom value rather than merely
choosing a new register placement or state labeling.

A direct unconditional control quotient was also checked on every current
spanning 194-state matrix. Stable action-preserving partition refinement gives
194 singleton blocks in all 139 cases. This excludes only that elementary
same-tape quotient method. It does not exclude context-dependent folds,
stuttering simulations, new source programs or different calculi.

\subsection{Proof strength and formalization status}

Three statements must be kept separate: a literal finite-run refinement, a
written proof formalizable in iPRA, and a completed proof-assistant theorem
for the exact arithmetic sentence. The primary PA README at the pinned
revision still records the machine-specific iPRA implications as open for
primary PA194, original PA195 and PA196. Standard-natural-number source and
clock simulations, native scanner equalities, modeled scheduler invariants,
and generic transfer theorems are substantial components, but do not alone
close the literal-machine endpoint.

The current endpoint is not an unspecified abstract consistency assertion.
In the supplementary source
\path{Computability/DiophantineConsistency/Lean/DiophantineConsistency/IPRAPA196Consistency.lean},
\path{paRefutation} is Foundation's actual PA provability predicate applied
to falsity. Its language-mapped version is \path{mappedPARefutation}; the
native intuitionistic reading is \path{nativePARefutation};
\path{nativePAConsistency} is its negation. The all-model bridge
\path{pa194/ipra/PA194RefutationBridge.lean} identifies the mapped sentence
with the existence of an internal Foundation PA proof of falsity in models
whose arithmetic reduct satisfies $I\Sigma_1$. It does not identify that proof
with a machine execution. The assembly in
\path{pa194/ipra/PA194PRAAssembly.lean} explicitly assumes both
machine-specific classical PRA reductions.

There is nevertheless a stronger \emph{written} calibration in the revised
packet \path{docs/pa194_ax11h_research_2026-11-14_2/proof}.
Its components address literal physical execution by positive primitive-
recursive clocks, source-command completion, finite-demand proof compilation,
term leaves, and the current Foundation-to-PAeq adapter. The adapter explicitly
handles the changed finite arithmetic axiom and the current canonical
induction closure convention; the Foundation falsity code is 13. Its intended
endpoint is the same native PA consistency sentence, not a silently changed
proof predicate.

The revised report claims the full written iPRA equivalence for AX11H and
QAX11H. The new GH/GHS, accelerated variants, LC\_AX11H and HR reports import
that theorem. By contrast, the primary-source LC variants and SEL retain a
relative theorem whose absolute primary calibration is separate. Thus it
would be inaccurate either to call every report a completed Lean proof or to
say that no written iPRA calibration is supplied anywhere in the corpus.
The present construction must name its retained calibration and verify its
new logical and physical interfaces; it cannot fill a missing theorem merely
by running a long blank prefix.

\subsection{Why the existing 193-state tables do not settle the problem}

All 54 spanning 193-state classes are retained in the inventory rather than
hidden because they are unsuccessful. They fall into substantially different
failure mechanisms.

The coefficient-three experiments change the formula coding enough that a
concrete numerical refutation is obtained. Their small count therefore does
not witness the desired consistency equivalence. The related all-offset
obstruction is a theorem about its specified recoding family, not about every
possible representation of PA proofs.

The Runtime193 line has a useful clean-frame runtime but fails the specified
blank initialization. Some tested entries halt prematurely or move outward
without constructing the required register frame. The finite register-
allocation and startup obstructions exclude those tested families; they do
not prove that a new 193-state bootstrap is impossible.

The newest rejected continuation has 3,734 source primitives, compared with
3,673 in the primary. At a positive constructor-loop entry it repeats four
increments of T, one increment of register 6, and the jump back to native
124, without performing the required decrement. The local fold cases and a
finite startup check therefore do not rescue its broken source contract.
Its class prefix is \path{07e29492a0e0}. The seven related 194-state shortened-
scanner/continuation compositions each fail 47 of 4,780 symbolic source
outcomes, despite agreement of the resolved source graph.

These examples guide the search without supplying a global lower bound.
The reusable obligations are exact emitted formulas, all-store command
contracts, simultaneous physical-fold validity, complete blank startup,
primitive-recursive clocks in both directions, and an explicit link to the
chosen PA proof predicate. The archive's negative experiments make clear
which of these obligations an attractive state count can fail.
