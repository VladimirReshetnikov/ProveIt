All-store command compiler
The important interface is exactly
  S(t,w,d,h)=halt if h=1, else(h,tail(w),d,head(w));
  MP_x success=(b,w,b,0), failure=(0,w,b,0), b=d+x.
Successful MP retains d=b and does not insert a dummy zero. Accordingly the
proof compiler first reserves a zero below the two recursively compiled
premises and ends successful MP by Pop, which clears the offset and consumes
that reserved zero. A bare final Shift would be wrong under this cache-only
halting test. The delivered compiler uses the correct Pop.

After the two Shifts, an MP suffix has the two premises in top and cache.
If a=1 the second Shift already halts; otherwise successful MP places b at
top, keeps the reserved-zero stack and sets d=b,h=0. Pop halts when b=1;
for b!=1 it removes the reserved zero, clears d, and returns(b,w,0,0).
This is valid even when b=0 or the zero stack representation collapses.
Discard=MP_0 is uniformly valid when d=0, for arbitrary t and old h, because
the success and failure branches both return(0,w,0,0).

The initial A0 before a compiled refutation clears arbitrary carried d,h;
the following proof induction works from every top/stack with cleared d,h.
The final Pop tests the produced contradiction before inspecting the old
arbitrary underlying tail. This all-store property is exactly what the
scheduler needs, since failed trials do not reset the store. AX's generated
heads are non-one by residues 0 and 2 modulo 6, so their intermediate selector
Shifts do not require assuming consistency. Total Cantor projection identities,
including [0;0]=0, are explicitly included and suffice for the stack equations.

Soundness and internalization
The finite proof-store invariant needs proof annotations for top, cache and
canonical stack entries, but not for the numerical offset d. Successful MP
updates the top annotation by MP; retaining d=b is harmless. AX's rho8 side
condition is d<=d+x. Its U(d,h) head follows by generalization from the cached
annotation. Failed gate emission and virtual stack zeros use the fixed proof
of zero. Cache-only halting therefore yields an actual numerical refutation.

The inherited radix calibration cannot be replaced by naive order-preserving
recoding. The proof correctly recodes formulas and variable labels together,
uses rho8All for arbitrary recoded parameters, and gives total interpretations
to unused residues 4 and 5. Its source/stack/scheduler pairing remains Cantor p;
formula recoding is not asserted to conjugate whole machine configurations.
Proof translations and command compilation are syntactic primitive-recursive
constructions with PA-provable induction arguments, not semantic appeals to
PA's own soundness. The positive-duration physical segments and exact startup
support both halting directions by finite determinism.

Limitations
The review does not reprove every inherited PA calibration argument, does not
provide a new proof-assistant kernel certificate, and does not establish a
state-count lower bound. Structural novelty is relative to the complete
recognized literal-table corpus at the pinned commit; source-only generators
and unmaterialized parameter families are outside that finite census. The
semantic materiality witness and new full source binding are separate from
the graph-novelty computation.
