The current census finds 1,445 recognized table occurrences, 725 byte
forms, and 499 complete one-halt action-graph classes with a spanning root.
The 499 equals the repository's 490, less three small diagnostic fixture
classes, plus twelve new classes in the current incoming archives.
Of the current classes, 40 have 194 working states: 27 have supplied
ordinary PA-equivalence manuscripts, nine are auxiliary/search tables,
and four are explicitly rejected. No eligible full consistency-candidate
table in the scanned literal formats has fewer than 194 states.

The location counts are 506 direct PA files, 127 table occurrences in
nested PA ZIPs, and 812 in the current incoming archives. Their byte forms
and graph classes overlap heavily. The scanner recognizes numeric
five-column tables in \file{.tm}, \file{.txt}, \file{.tsv}, and \file{.csv};
it does not count unmaterialized parameter families or JSON-only machine
encodings as literal tables. Named contributions outside that encoding are
still represented in the delivery ledger. The small diagnostic fixtures
are recorded separately, not treated as consistency-machine candidates.

also used as a positive control: the checker correctly finds its existing
