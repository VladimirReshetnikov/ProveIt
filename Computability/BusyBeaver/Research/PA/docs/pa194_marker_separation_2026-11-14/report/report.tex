\begin{abstract}
No justified machine below 194 working states was obtained. This report
delivers the requested fallback: two new binary, single-tape, blank-start
194-state machines, \texttt{PA194LCGMS} and \texttt{PA194LCGSMS}.
They combine the existing marker/selector separation of PA194B with the
newest LCG/LCGS loop-return designs. A direct proof in intuitionistic
primitive recursive arithmetic gives, for each exact parent--child pair,
$H_P(t)\to H_N(5t)$ and $H_N(t)\to H_P(t)$, where halting is totalized.
The absolute nonhalting/\(\Con(\pa)\) conclusion composes this new proof
with the explicitly identified revised written AX11H calibration and its
LCG/LCGS transfers. This is a mathematical dependency, not a new completed
Lean or Rocq endpoint.

The pinned comparison covers 97 deliveries, 83 named constructions or
supplements, 29 mechanism families, and all 520 recognized spanning
194-state action-graph classes, including experimental tables. Both new
tables are distinct from this complete recognized comparison set and from
each other, even under a stronger cosmetic comparison than blank-input
equivalence permits. Exact local certificates, complete initialization
checks, independent verification, search evidence, full transition tables,
and offline reproduction code accompany the report.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result, conventions, and proof boundary}
\label{sec:result}

\subsection{The two delivered machines}
Both machines have one head, one two-way infinite tape, alphabet
$\{0,1\}$, blank symbol zero, and only left or right moves. Every initial
tape cell is zero and the head starts at coordinate zero. The working
controls are $0,\ldots,193$, the initial control is 0, and 194 is the
separate terminal label. The halt label is not counted as a working state.
Every working control has both symbol transitions, so each table has
exactly 388 rows. No externally prepared tape or alternate starting
configuration is part of either construction.

Their names record their parentage: LC is the loop-continuation layout,

The S machine is the slightly quicker of this pair during initialization.
Neither new table is a state-count improvement over its parent. In fact,
the new decoder deliberately spends extra steps to distinguish history
contexts while retaining the same number of controls.

\subsection{The theorem actually established here}
Let $H_M(t)$ be the primitive recursive predicate that the totalized blank
run of $M$ has reached the halt label by time $t$, and put
\[
 \NH(M):=\forall t\,\neg H_M(t).
\]
Here and below, \(\Con(\pa)\) is the negation of the pinned Foundation
PA-refutation predicate used by the inherited AX11H paper. It is not
silently replaced by consistency of a different arithmetic presentation.

\begin{theorem}[New relative theorem]
For each of the exact pairs
\[
 (P,N)=(\texttt{PA194LCG},\texttt{PA194LCGMS}),\qquad
 (\texttt{PA194LCGS},\texttt{PA194LCGSMS}),
\]
intuitionistic primitive recursive arithmetic proves
\[
 H_P(t)\longrightarrow H_N(5t),\qquad
 H_N(t)\longrightarrow H_P(t),\qquad
 \NH(P)\longleftrightarrow\NH(N).
\]
The proof uses only the displayed transition rows, a predecessor-bit
invariant, primitive-recursive iteration, and quantifier-free induction.
\end{theorem}

Section~\ref{sec:marker-separation} proves this theorem completely for
the literal tables. By composing it with the cited written calibrations
of the two parents, the resulting written mathematical conclusion is
\[
 \boxed{\quad \ipra\ \vdash\ \NH(N)\longleftrightarrow\Con(\pa).
 \quad}
\]
The supplied parent calibration is an identified theorem dependency.
It is not an assumption that PA is consistent. The new local theorem is
independent of that calibration, while the boxed absolute conclusion uses
it. No end-to-end proof-assistant verification of the new literal bytes
is claimed. The user's stated unfinished Lean work remains unfinished
by this deliverable.

\subsection{Why these are materially different designs}
This is a composition of credited mechanisms, rather than a claim to
have invented all its ingredients. It changes recurring decoder
execution: two distinguishable reader states become one reader, and the
previous marker state becomes a history selector after the marker
scanner is moved to a fresh control. The two local zero branches take
five and three steps in place of one. The separated marker remains
available during bootstrap, precisely where the unsupported 193-state
deletion fails.

The independent verifier observes a changed zero branch again after the
clean source seed has been reached, in the next 10,000 parent steps.
Thus the modification is active during the ongoing computation as well
as initialization. Exact graph comparisons reject state renaming,
reflection, arbitrary alternative graph roots, global symbol exchange,
and changes only to the final halt-entry decoration as explanations of
the differences. Symbol exchange is included only as a stronger
structural comparison; it is not asserted to preserve a zero-blank run.
The two final tables also differ from each other under this comparison.

\subsection{The main axes of genuine design variation}
The principal 194-state designs mostly share a numerical proof-search
architecture and much of the physical runtime. The first accepted layout
has the budget
\[
 134\ \text{fixed controls}+67\ \text{AX readers}
       -7\ \text{compatible folds}=194.
\]
Some equally sized designs instead use 68 readers with eight folds,
69 readers with nine folds, or a changed fixed-control component. Thus
reader count, source length, and count after compatible folds are
different quantities. A reduction in one of them is not automatically
a reduction in the delivered blank-start machine.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Numerical calculus and coding.} Coefficients four, five,
and six change formula codes. The Q variants exchange the two premises
of the universal-distribution head. Diagonal variants change which
operand supplies a code-driven vacuity instance. These changes need
proof translations, including the exceptional truth code zero and
freshness conditions.
\item \textbf{Command protocol and storage.} Cached or visible MP,
omitted dummy pushes, the original sum guard versus cache-only halting,
and symmetric comparison change how the abstract proof stack is used.
AX11 pushes eleven entries and reads four fields. Its fourth operand
and final Pop sequence cannot be omitted from a completeness argument.
\item \textbf{Parameter routing.} Explicit generalization binders and
independent generalization/diagonal operands change which axioms and
generalizations a command can select. Their compiler words and
cofinality proofs need corresponding adjustment.
\item \textbf{Source control flow.} Known-zero returns and loop
reassociation change primitive instruction addresses and physical
decoding. The resolved successors, including break/continue targets,
must be preserved or proved equivalent. Merely preserving the leaf
sequence is insufficient.
\item \textbf{Physical allocation.} Register permutations,
history selectors, marker folds, and exact three-to-one shortcuts alter
the transition table while retaining an identified higher-level source.
These require invariants about the actual tape and a complete
initialization check.
\end{enumerate}

The detailed 29-family comparison is Appendix~\ref{app:families}; the
83 named records are in Appendix~\ref{app:named}. The complete 97-packet
ledger is Appendix~\ref{app:packets}. Machine-readable ledgers retain
long source paths, table hashes, aliases, and fuller proof-status
attributions that would obscure the printed comparison.

\subsection{What should be reused for further research}
For extending the ongoing Lean project, the primary PA194 remains the
natural integration target because its model-level and arithmetic
development is farthest along. For a new written iPRA calibration, the
revised AX11H packet supplies the most explicit retained chain, including
the finite-demand PA compiler. For local physical transformations,
LCG/LCGS are useful parents because they already combine a verified
loop layout with exact return shortcuts, and their tables and relative
proofs are packaged together.

These are different practical preferences. The new MS compositions add
alternative decoder organizations and new search seeds. They do not
supersede the primary formalization target, improve the state record,
or establish a universal speed advantage.

\subsection{The inherited proof interfaces}
The numerical coding uses Cantor pairing
\[
 \pi(a,b)=\frac{(a+b)(a+b+1)}2+b,
\]
with implication $I(a,b)=4\pi(a,b)$, membership
$M(a,b)=4\pi(a,b)+1$, universal quantification
$U(a,b)=4\pi(b,a)+2$, and equality $E(a,b)=4\pi(a,b)+3$.
Zero is retained as truth, including the alias $I(0,0)=0$, while one
is the numerical contradiction code. The finite logical heads,
dominated propositional basis, code-driven diagonal, and guarded
separation gate generate a numerical calculus with MP and theorem
generalization.

The revised calibration has several substantive stages:
\begin{enumerate}
\item It proves literal blank-machine/source equivalence by interpreting
the symbolic tape/list rules in iPRA and providing positive
primitive-recursive clocks. This covers arbitrary natural registers,
spare lists, and gaps, not only sampled arithmetic values.
\item It binds every primitive source instruction and successor to
explicit arithmetic templates. Their exact clocks use quantifier-free
counter induction, covering the four AX operands and all eleven entries.
\item It proves scheduler/numerical-refutation equivalence with an
all-store command compiler. Cache-only halting requires a final Pop;
having a visible contradiction alone does not necessarily trigger Shift.
\item It maps a numerical refutation to the current Foundation PA
predicate using model-uniform arguments over $\mathsf I\Sigma_1$.
The guarded gate is included, rather than left as an unspecified
soundness premise.
\item In the converse direction, its finite-demand construction
replaces full-PA inductions over all environments by bounded collections
of the requests actually used. It gives proof-producing term, formula,
induction, and proof-rule translations. A separate adapter connects
ordinary PAeq to the exact Foundation proof predicate.
\item The repository's weak-base transfer supplies the intuitionistic
negative consistency statement. The numerical contradiction code one
and the Foundation falsity code 13 are connected by translations;
they are not identified as integers.
\end{enumerate}

This report checked the architecture and the displayed recursion/clock
arguments and found no concrete error in the inspected interfaces. It
does not claim to have independently reconstructed every transitive
syntactic proof recipe or completed the missing Lean compiler. Those
interfaces are inherited mathematical results. The independent local
transformation theorem proved here adds no new premise about them.

\subsection{Composing the statements intuitionistically}
Suppose the supplied parent theorem is
\(\ipra\vdash\Con(\pa)\leftrightarrow\NH(P)\).
The new relative theorem gives
\(\ipra\vdash\NH(P)\leftrightarrow\NH(N)\).
Transitivity of implication in intuitionistic logic gives the boxed
conclusion in Section~\ref{sec:result}. No use of Markov's principle or
double-negation elimination for an arbitrary existential statement
appears in this composition. No assertion of either consistency or
inconsistency of PA is required.

The current PA directory README describes the incomplete Lean
machine-specific endpoint and its extensive completed intermediate
results. An unfinished formalization does not refute the revised
written argument. Conversely, passing a Python certificate does not
complete that formalization. Both distinctions are maintained throughout
this report.

\section{Investigations below 194 states}
\label{sec:search}

\subsection{Rechecking every recognized 193-state class}
The census contains 54 spanning 193-control action graphs. All 193
possible blank starts were freshly examined for every graph: 10,422
cases. The exact finite outcomes were 110 halts, 10,308 provable outward
blank self-loop rays, and four trajectories capped at 100,000 steps.
Fifty-two graphs have no capped start; their intended native-74 starts
are rays. These are exact obstructions to those proposed runtimes,
not evidence for a global state lower bound.

The four capped cases belong to two independently diagnosed designs.
The coefficient-three attempt identifies
\[
 E_3(1,0)=I_3(0,1)=6,
\]
and its unchanged numerical calculus derives contradiction. A supplied
primitive-rule certificate was freshly replayed: 73,393 proof rows,
116,171 exact arithmetic DAG terms, and final formula one. Its steps
are 34,888 restricted-rho instances, 38,503 MP applications, one Gen,
and one witness-head instance. This logical rejection is separate from
the capped physical traces. The other design has a bad loop
reassociation: a six-instruction positive branch increases the top by
four and register 6 by one without decrementing its loop test. A correct
startup cannot repair that altered source graph.

\subsection{The marker-saving obstruction}
The attractive initialized 193-state runtime merges 301 into 88 and
uses the existing marker scanner as a selector. Its local register-frame
contracts can succeed while its blank run reaches an outward ray.
The new MS construction preserves the marker in a fresh state, so the
count is $194-1+1=194$. Reconstructing this operation on the primary
PA194 independently reproduced the existing PA194B table; it was rejected
as a purported new result. Only the compatible LCG/LCGS compositions,
which pass the full current novelty screen, are delivered as new.

\subsection{Lessons from the three suggested related projects}
The ZFC research provides a useful method: distinguish runtime changes
from axiom-generator changes, preserve an exact source interface, and
prove compatible folds and full initialization together. In that lineage,
some engine savings transfer between calculi while savings attached to
different axiom subtrees do not. The new construction follows that
discipline. Replacing the PA calculus by the ZFC-calibrated K215 calculus
would change the target consistency strength and would not solve this
problem.

The DiophantineConsistency work emphasizes explicit witness maps and
all-model arithmetic interfaces. Those ideas guide the clock proof and
the separation between a finite evaluator certificate and an internal
consistency theorem. A sparse equation or a small arithmetic circuit
does not by itself give a small blank-start binary TM: the evaluator,
enumerator, constants, coding, and initialization must all be compiled
and counted. No complete smaller implementation resulted from that
direction in this investigation.

ConciseIPRA is useful as a base-theory and proof-presentation reference:
our new simulation predicates are primitive-recursive equalities, and
their invariants require only equation induction. Its small proof-checker
metric is not the machine-state metric, and replacing PA consistency by
iPRA consistency would change the objective. This report therefore uses
its weak-induction perspective without claiming an unbuilt state saving.

python3 -B verify.py --full-parent
