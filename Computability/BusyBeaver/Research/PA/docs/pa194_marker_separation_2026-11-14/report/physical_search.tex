\subsection{Additional bounded physical reduction searches}
\label{sec:ms-search}

No machine with fewer than 194 working controls was established in this
investigation.  The two marker-separated compositions were retained as the
constructive fallback.  The following additional searches record what was
attempted from the new seed; they are not an exhaustive search of 193-state
Turing machines and do not prove a lower bound.

\subsubsection{A stronger source invariant}
For $r\in\{3,4\}$, abstract each register by
$\alpha_r(n)=\min(n,r-1)$.  Thus the two domains are respectively
$\{0,1,{\geq}2\}$ and $\{0,1,2,{\geq}3\}$.
The analysis tracks the product of all nine register abstractions jointly
at every source site, from site 25 with logical registers
$(2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)$.  Increment at the top value retains the top value.
Decrement at the top value has both $r-2$ and $r-1$ as positive successors;
omitting either would be unsound.  Other arithmetic transfers are exact.
The source graph is independently reconstructed by the arithmetic and
interval resolvers, and agrees with the delivered 3\,671-site instruction
list in all 4\,778 successor checks.

The finite reachable sets were generated and then independently scanned
to check closure of every abstract outgoing edge:
\[
\begin{array}{lrr}
\text{LCG register domain}&\text{reachable site/mask pairs}&
  \text{audited abstract edges}\\\hline
\{0,1,{\geq}2\}&4\,237\,326&4\,756\,326\\
\{0,1,2,{\geq}3\}&31\,371\,382&34\,250\,434.
\end{array}
\]
For both analyses, source site 8 is unreachable and the decrement at site 7
has only its zero outcome.  There are no forced-positive exact-one
decrement sites.  Projecting the joint invariant onto individual registers
provides sound marginal bounds at each source entry.  Single small values
remain exact; a union of values is conservatively weakened to its least
member plus an arbitrary natural parameter when necessary.

These bounds were propagated through the literal symbolic machine
interpreter, not merely tested at instruction boundaries.  Each guarded
run checks 4\,776 source outcomes and 1\,097\,971 symbolic steps, including
168\,140 accelerated unary sweeps and 2\,912 arbitrary finite-list sweeps.
The exact 57\,870-step blank initialization is separately included.  The
unbounded list-block identities are unchanged.  A six-cell window on each
side of the head gives the following envelope sizes:
\[
\begin{array}{lrr}
\text{source envelope}&\text{LCG windows}&\text{LCGMS windows}\\\hline
\text{inherited literal source envelope}&49\,297&49\,375\\
\{0,1,{\geq}2\}\text{ guards}&49\,117&49\,195\\
\{0,1,2,{\geq}3\}\text{ guards}&49\,113&49\,191.
\end{array}
\]
The MS envelope is obtained by transferring every parent window through
the exact local simulation from Section~\ref{sec:marker-separation}.
When an intermediate head shift exposes an unknown boundary square,
both possible values are included.  All intermediate selector steps
are retained.  No parent window was discarded as inconsistent with the
incoming-history premise in any of these three transfers.

\subsubsection{Exact finite screening domains and results}
For every selected table and envelope, the pair screen considers all
$\binom{194}{2}=18\,721$ pairs of controls and candidate common binary
transition rows, retargeting old entries to the retained control.  It
requires positive-progress equality with some old continuation of at most
24 steps after at most 256 new steps, with both continuations remaining
inside the available radius-six window.  It checks both row choices
together after the preliminary single-row tests.  The separate deletion
screen considers all 193 noninitial controls, replacing their incoming
rows by bypass actions; its old and new continuation limits are 48 and
512 steps.  The bypass checks stop before a different modified incoming
row is used, so this is a deliberately restricted simultaneous-edit
criterion.

\text{table}&\text{envelope}&\text{row trials}&\text{joint choices}&\text{hits}\\\hline
LCGMS&inherited&26\,379\,344&174\,059&0\\
LCG&three-value guards&26\,387\,880&177\,086&0\\

\end{tabular}

Every row of this table also has zero successful deletion targets out of
193.  These are 93\,605 pair examinations and 965 deletion-target
examinations across the five distinct table/envelope combinations; they
are not counts of distinct 193-state machines.  The persistence of the
same action counts under tighter envelopes simply means that the removed
windows did not change the surviving preliminary candidates in these
particular screens.

The delivered \path{research/physical/search/} directory contains the exact source inputs,
the finite closure program, symbolic collectors, optimizer sources,
window files, logs, hashes, and a portable reproduction script.
\texttt{python3 research/physical/search/reproduce.py} reruns the finite closures and all
five screens.  Adding \texttt{--regenerate} also regenerates the symbolic
window envelopes.  These executable checks support the stated bounded
search results; they are neither a new Lean theorem nor evidence that
an unrestricted 193-state construction is impossible.
