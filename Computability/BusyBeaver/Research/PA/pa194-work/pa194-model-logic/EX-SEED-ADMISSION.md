# Seeded existential membership cases

The positive and negative membership results apply directly to arbitrary
internally coded term vectors; syntactic validity follows from the source
formula-set premises. `simRuleExs_positiveMembershipSeed` and
`simRuleExs_negativeMembershipSeed` prove the corresponding
`CutValidity.Sim.ruleExs` implications under `SeedAdmission` at the actual
`exsIntro` node. The statements cover arbitrary candidate-PRA models satisfying
IΣ₁, including nonstandard syntax and arbitrary legal source terms.

These are instances of the existential field. General equality-atom, compound
and quantified bodies and a complete compiler remain open.
`check-ex-seed-admission.sh` replays the source closure and checks four axiom
reports. Retained objects and workspace foundations require their own provenance.
