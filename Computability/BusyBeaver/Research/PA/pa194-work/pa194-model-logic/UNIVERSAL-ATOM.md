`simPushFree` selects an environment by pushing the current base label onto the free stack and incrementing the base. `simRuleAll_of_freeBridge` proves the exact numerical target conclusion of `Sim.ruleAll` assuming the displayed fixed-translation equality between Foundation `free L p` under that pushed-free environment and p under the corresponding pushed-bound environment. It leaves this source translation equality as an explicit premise.

`freeTerm_label` proves the needed variable-label equality for arbitrary model-valued source terms valid with one bound variable. `free_positiveAtom_translation` and `free_negativeAtom_translation` establish the actual fixed-translation equality for all positive and negative binary equality/membership atoms with such terms, and the `simRuleAll_positiveAtom`/`_negativeAtom` conclusions follow. Foundation `free` commutes with source and/or on arbitrary internally valid children, and `freeBridge_and`/`_or` prove the fixed-translation bridge for well-scoped children whenever the bridge holds for each child; truth and falsum are unconditional base cases. These are local conditional Boolean steps, not an internal induction over an arbitrary formula. In particular, nested binders require a lifted substitution-vector/qVec invariant; environment admissibility, Sim's all-shift/all-fresh and other quantifier fields, the complete Sim, and consistency implications remain open. `simRuleAllShift_positiveAtom` and `_negativeAtom` prove the numerical `Sim.ruleAllShift` target implication for arbitrary valid model-valued binary relation atoms under the selected pushed-free environment. Their exact source-shift/fixed-translation equality is checked at the term and atom levels, and local `shiftBridge_and`/`_or` extend this equality conditionally through source Boolean constructors. At a quantified source constructor, exact code equality is generally too strong because the pushed environment changes the binder-label base; target-provable alpha-renaming and capture/freshness reasoning remain open.

The bound on the whole free stack and reserved-label inequality remain external assumptions to be supplied by a common environment/admissibility invariant.

These are atomic field fragments plus local Boolean steps; they do not prove either universal field for arbitrary source formulas, nested quantifier renaming, full admissibility, a complete Sim, or a consistency implication.
