# Arbitrary-index trace membership directions

Given a direct trace and witness membership, the forward direction produces source membership conjoined with the property. Given the trace, source membership and property, the backward direction produces witness membership. Both code functions have fixed Σ₁ defining graphs and exact proof-value theorems, including for nonstandard inputs.

These require the listed derivations as premises; they do not themselves prove separation, induction, a theory interpretation or a machine equivalence.
