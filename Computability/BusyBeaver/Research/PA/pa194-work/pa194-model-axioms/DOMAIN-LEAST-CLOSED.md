# Universal least-existence theorem

A fixed source-language natural-deduction derivation proves that every nonempty subset of a domain set has a least element. Its exact universally closed form is identified and supplied in every internal theory over every PRA model whose arithmetic reduct satisfies IΣ₁. The fixed universal proof is selected from model-level provability; it is a standard formula and its proof does not depend on the internal property.

This theorem still requires derivations that the bad trace is a subset and is nonempty; it does not yet eliminate a least witness, establish ordinal induction, or prove the iPRA machine equivalence.
