# Internal ordinal induction for standard typed properties

For every **standard typed** arity-one source property p, and every nonempty candidate-PRA model whose induced ordered-ring reduct satisfies IΣ₁, `standardOrdinalProof` constructs an actual Foundation internal proof of `⌜inductionStatement p⌝` in the classifier-defined `adjunctiveFullSepTheory`. The property may have arbitrary standard free parameters. `standard_closed_ordinal_provable` also establishes internal provability of the previously defined universally closed numeric ordinal-induction code for every such p.

The last fixed source premise, exact adjunction, is discharged as follows. `swappedAdjunctionDerivation` uses the existing standard `ND.exactAdjunctionFromAxioms` proof with its two free parameters swapped. The union supplies its weak-adjunction, parameterized separation, reflexivity, symmetry, and membership-congruence context formulas. `swappedAdjunctionOpenProof` quotes the standard derivation and cuts those supplied formulas. `swapped_adjunction_closure` checks that its universal closure is exactly `adjunctionFormula`'s closure. The previously source-replayed model-valued closure theorem yields `adjunctionProof`, which discharges the sole premise of the existing one-premise standard induction reduction.

The model M may be nonstandard, but p is standard typed syntax: this construction does **not** prove induction for arbitrary nonstandard internal property codes or provide a uniform model-definable proof-code transformer. It is a Lean model-level result and does not establish a native iPRA proof, a PA-to-source proof-code translation, machine correctness, or Con(PA) equivalence.
