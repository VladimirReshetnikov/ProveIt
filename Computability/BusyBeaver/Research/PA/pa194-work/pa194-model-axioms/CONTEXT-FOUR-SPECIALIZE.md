# Four-fold contextual specialization

`contextFourProof` specializes four leading universal binders at arbitrary internally coded source terms under an arbitrary internal theory and sequent context. Its fixed Σ₁ proof-code graph and exact proof-code value are checked in PRA models whose arithmetic reduct models IΣ₁. This operation does not itself prove a cancellation theorem, a PA interpretation, or native iPRA equivalence.
