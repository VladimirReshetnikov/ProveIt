# A successor contains its predecessor

A fixed source-language derivation proves that if b is a successor of a, then a belongs to b. Its exact two-variable universal closure is identified, and every PRA model with IΣ₁ arithmetic reduct supplies a proof of that fixed theorem in the full adjunctive-plus-separation source. Fixed equality proofs are supplied from the source; model-level choice is used when selecting the universally closed proof.

This theorem does not itself specialize the universal proof at model indices, open a successor witness, or complete the ordinal-induction supplier or iPRA machine equivalence.
