It proves scopedness, fixed Σ₁ function graphs, the simultaneous shift of the property and both indices, and identifies the already established opened separation trace with the direct form at shifted property and source and witness index 0. These statements cover nonstandard formula codes and indices under the stated syntax hypothesis.

No derivation or proof-code supplier is constructed here; in particular this does not establish induction, a theory interpretation, an iPRA theorem, or a machine equivalence. The checker recompiles the displayed source into a fresh output with selected byte-checked dependencies; the rest of the transitive imports are prebuilt, so it is not a clean transitive build.
