PROVENANCE AND ATTRIBUTION

This research builds on the six supplied ProveIt incoming archives pinned in
sources_manifest.json, and on their retained work by Vladimir Reshetnikov,
Elliot Glazer, jeff87654, Wade, and the other authors named in the upstream
notices. The underlying Theory U arguments, numerical calculus, source
architecture, original machines, runtime induction engines, and earlier
compiler proofs remain their work.

The original archives are preserved byte-for-byte, including their license
and attribution files. Selected extracted dependencies under analysis/emitter
and analysis/theory_lineage retain their original content and provenance.
Their respective upstream licenses continue to apply. The exploratory
allocator is adapted from the ProveIt composition-allocation research code.

New work in this delivery consists of the comparative report, the documented
source and arithmetic transformations, the helper-layout constructions,
their independent incoming-census proof checker, additional search and
certificate-checking code, and this reproduction/package integration.

These attributions do not imply that upstream authors reviewed or endorsed
the new results. No priority claim is made beyond what was found during
this investigation, and no machine below208states is claimed.
