files retain their original terms; this notice neither replaces nor expands them.

MPL2.0 notices remain with their materials. No repository-wide relicensing is
asserted for upstream files whose license is unspecified.

The exact K221 endpoint is an ordinary mathematical proof formalizable in ZFC,
not a newly compiled Rocq/Lean theorem or internal PA proof-code endpoint.

claim is made.
