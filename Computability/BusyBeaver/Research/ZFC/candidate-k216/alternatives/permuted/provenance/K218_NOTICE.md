# Attribution and reuse

Prepared on 20 October 2026 for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository,
with ChatGPT-assisted research, implementation, and independent parallel
review. The new contribution is the dominated numerical-axiom recovery,
its finite proof certificate, the revised common-input enumeration
schedule, the 218-state literal construction, verification scripts,
research alternatives, and accompanying report.

The numerical calculus, seed bundle, ZFC consistency comparison,
all-natural macro library, and symbolic runtime induction engine build on
the pinned ProveIt K219 construction and its predecessors. The retained
notices credit Elliot Glazer, Mira, Jeff's busy-beaver development, Andrew
James Wade, BusyCoq, the Coq Library of Undecidability, and ProveIt's
accumulated reductions. Those contributions are not claimed as new here.

The inherited license and notices are retained in licenses/ and
provenance/. New material is supplied under the same MIT No Attribution
terms as the predecessor contribution. Existing third-party files retain
their own terms; this notice does not relicense them.

The full logical certificate is checked without invoking the proof-search
software that discovered it. Saved Prover9 input/output is included for
provenance; Prover9's implementation is not distributed in this archive.

The ordinary mathematical proof is formalizable in ZFC. A newly compiled
exact-K218 proof-assistant endpoint and an internal PA proof-code theorem
are not claimed.
