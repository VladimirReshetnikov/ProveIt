# Syntactic consistency obstruction for rho8 with U=R(A,B)

Author: drop_contract, 20 October 2026. Ordinary mathematical proof draft;
no compiler, supplied checker, or proof search has been invoked. This concerns
exact native K219 rows, not an arbitrary system that happens to use their names.
Independent review is pending.

Write I(a,b), R(a,b), Q_i a and E(a,b) for the exact numerical constructors
in Code254. Their residues modulo 4 are respectively 0,1,2,3. Each constructor
is injective in its two fields. I(a,b) >= a,b; R(a,b) > a,b; Q_i a and E(a,b)
are positive. T=0=I(0,0), F=1=R(0,0). In particular R(a,b)=F iff a=b=0.
These statements include the exceptional zero implication alias.

The modified rho8 axiom is

    H(a,b,c) := I(I(I(c,R(a,b)),a), I(I(a,c),I(b,c))).

All the remaining native rows are retained exactly:

    r1(i,j)   = I(Q_i I(E(i,j),F),F)
    r2(i,p,q) = I(Q_i I(p,q), I(Q_i p,Q_i q))
    r3(a,b,c) = I(E(a,b), I(R(c,a),R(c,b)))
    r4(i,j,p) = I(Q_i Q_j p,Q_j Q_i p)
    r5(a,b,c) = I(E(c,a), I(R(c,b),R(a,b)))
    r6(i,p)   = I(I(Q_i I(Q_i p,F),F),p)
    G(a,b,u)  = I(E(u,a),I(E(u,b),Q_{R(a,b)} E(a,b)))

For the unchanged seed-K bundle put

    V(a,b,c) = I(R(a,b),Q_c R(a,b)),
    D(a,b,c) = nks(a,b,c),                 (Q-headed)
    Bundle(a,b,c) = I(I(V(a,b,c),D(a,b,c)),F).

The internal contents of D, including support, are not simplified or changed.
Its Q-headed outer constructor is the only property used. Enabled triples have
c!=a,b. The proof below even permits all triples: adding disabled bundles
cannot invalidate the invariant. Truth, MP and closed-theorem GEN are retained.

## 1. An auxiliary class, without MP in its definition

Define S as the least class of natural codes generated by:

* each literal axiom r1--r6, G, H, and Bundle above;
* every identity I(x,x), for arbitrary natural x;
* from S(p), infer S(Q_i p), for arbitrary label i;
* from S(I(p,q)), infer S(I(Q_i p,Q_i q)), for arbitrary label i.

The last clause is quantifier monotonicity, not an assumption that this rule is
already derivable in the modified calculus. S is an auxiliary *larger* class.
We may add identities and monotonicity when building a consistency invariant.
In particular S(0), by the identity at x=0.

This is an ordinary inductive definition on finite witnesses. It makes no
semantic assignment, substitution, renaming, Lindenbaum quotient or classical
completeness assumption.

## 2. Four inversion facts

(a) No R-headed or E-headed code belongs to S. Every generator has outer tag
I or Q. The zero identity has the I residue too. Therefore F does not belong
to S.

(b) If S(Q_i p), then S(p). The last constructor of an S witness must be GEN;
all other constructors produce I-headed codes. Q injectivity identifies its
label and body. Repeat this for any finite Q-prefix.

(c) No code

    X(i,p) = I(Q_i I(Q_i p,F),F) = Ex_i Q_i p

belongs to S. Inspect the last generator. GEN has the wrong outer tag. An
identity would equate its Q-headed left field to F. Monotonicity has a Q-headed
right field, not F. For the axiom cases:

* r1: matching left Q fields and then their negation fields would require
  E(i,j)=Q_i p, impossible by tags;
* r2: its right field is I-headed, not F;
* r4: its right field is Q-headed, not F;
* r3,r5,G: their left field is E-headed, not Q-headed;
* r6,H,Bundle: their left field is I-headed, not Q-headed.

These cases exhaust S; no MP argument is used here.

(d) No literal bundle antecedent I(V(a,b,c),D(a,b,c)) belongs to S.
Its left field V is I-headed and its right field D is Q-headed. GEN has the
wrong outer tag; identities would equate I and Q; monotonicity has Q as its
left field. Among axioms, r1,r2,r4 have Q left fields; r3,r5,G have E left
fields. For the remaining three:

* r6's left field is I(Q_i(...),F); matching V=I(R(a,b),Q_c R(a,b))
  requires a Q-headed field to equal an R-headed field;
* H's left field is I(I(z,R(x,y)),x); matching V requires an I-headed
  field to equal R(a,b);
* Bundle's left field is I(V',D'); matching V requires V'=R(a,b),
  again I versus R.

## 3. The only possible H trigger

If

    S(I(I(c,R(a,b)),a)),

then a=b=0. Again inspect its last S constructor.
GEN has the wrong outer tag; monotonicity has Q instead of the I-headed left
field. For an identity, injectivity forces

    a = I(c,R(a,b)) >= R(a,b) > a,

which is impossible. For initial axioms r1,r2,r4 the left field has tag Q;
for r3,r5,G it has tag E, so none matches. Bundle matching would force its
Q-headed D to equal R(a,b), impossible. For a minor H(x,y,z), matching its
two fields yields

    R(a,b)=x,
    a=I(I(x,z),I(y,z)) >= I(x,z) >= x.

This contradicts R(a,b)>a. Finally a minor r6(i,p) forces

    a=p,  R(a,b)=F,

hence a=b=p=0. This is the single surviving case. It is real: r6(i,0)
detaches H(0,0,Q_i I(Q_i 0,F)), yielding the identity of
I(0,Q_i I(Q_i 0,F)). No stronger conclusion has been assumed from that step.

## 4. S is closed under MP

Prove, by induction on an S witness for the *major* I(p,q), that
S(p) implies S(q). For an identity this returns the supplied minor. A GEN
witness cannot have an I-headed conclusion. For monotonicity, injectivity
writes p=Q_i u and q=Q_i v. By (b), S(u). Apply the induction hypothesis to
the premise witness S(I(u,v)), obtaining S(v), and then GEN gives S(Q_i v).

It remains to consider a literal-axiom major:

* r1 needs its Q_i I(E(i,j),F) minor. Strip Q by (b), obtaining
  S(I(E(i,j),F)). This last shape is impossible: see the added inversion
  immediately below. Therefore r1 cannot fire.
* r2: its minor gives S(Q_i I(u,v)). Strip Q and apply the S monotonicity
  constructor to obtain exactly I(Q_i u,Q_i v).
* r3,r5,G need an E-headed minor, excluded by (a).
* r4: strip the two Qs from its minor and rebuild them in the other order.
* r6 needs X(i,p), excluded by (c).
* Bundle needs its literal antecedent, excluded by (d).
* H(a,b,c): by section 3 its minor forces a=b=0. Its conclusion is then
  I(I(0,c),I(0,c)), one of the admitted identities.

Added inversion used by r1: S(I(E(i,j),F)) is impossible. GEN has wrong outer
tag; identities equate E and F; monotonicity has Q as its left field.
r1,r2,r4 have Q left fields; r6,H,Bundle have I left fields. r3 and r5 do
have E left fields, but their right fields are I-headed, not F; G also has an
I-headed right field. Thus no generator fits. This proof is independent of MP.

## 5. Consequence

Induction on an actual finite modified-calculus derivation maps every line
into S: truth is identity0, axioms are generators, GEN is a generator, and
MP is section 4. Section 2(a) therefore excludes a derivation of F=1.

The argument is elementary syntax and finite-inductive reasoning, so ZFC
proves consistency of this exact modified calculus. If ZFC is consistent,
it cannot also prove Con(modified) -> Con(ZFC): that would make ZFC prove its
own consistency. The original unrestricted-rho8 calculus and accepted K219
machine are unaffected. This is not a nonhalting proof about any allocator
output lacking a completed machine/calculus correspondence.

## 6. What happens to fresh-label diagonalization

The semantic A=C=0 instance is exactly

    I(I(I(0,R(0,B)),0), I(0,I(B,0))).

Two MPs would yield I(B,0) if the first antecedent were derivable. The trigger
invariant instead forces B=0 whenever that antecedent is in S. Thus the
nonzero-B semantic shortcut has no derivation in this calculus at all.
The model assumptions that made the semantic argument work are substantially
stronger than consistency of the modified calculus.

The witness class also makes the missing negative-fragment issue explicit:
no equality theorem can be extracted from rho1/G, no r6 existential-universal
antecedent is produced, and the bundled K negative core cannot be projected
by MP. Existing unrestricted classical transport/projection lemmas therefore
cannot be applied to repair this gap without adding rules not present here.
