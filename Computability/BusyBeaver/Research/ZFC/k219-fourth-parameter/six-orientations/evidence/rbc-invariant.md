# The remaining R(B,C) and R(C,B) orientations also have a syntactic obstruction

Author: opening_encoder, 20 October 2026. Ordinary mathematical proof,
not a proof-assistant result. This extends drop_contract's auxiliary-class
argument for R(A,B). The finite constructor-schema search below suggested
the extra inert schema; the proof does not depend on the search bound.

Fix either `W(b,c)=R(b,c)` or `W(b,c)=R(c,b)` throughout. We use only:

* W has membership tag 1;
* W(b,c)>c;
* W(b,c)=F iff b=c=0.

As before, I,Q,E,R are the literal native constructors, with pair
injectivity, distinct tags, `I(x,y)>=x,y`, and the sole zero alias
`I(0,0)=0`. In particular Q is never zero. Let

    p(b,c)     = I(c,W(b,c)),
    H(a,b,c)   = I(I(p(b,c),a), I(I(a,c),I(b,c))),
    J(b,c)     = I(I(p(b,c),c), I(b,c)),
    a(b,c,d)   = W(d,p(b,c)),
    h(b,c,d)   = I(I(a(b,c,d),c), I(b,c)),
    D(b,c,d)   = I(I(h(b,c,d),p(b,c)), I(d,p(b,c))).

Here p(b,c)>c because p>=W(b,c)>c. All J and D formulas are I-headed;
their immediate left and right fields are also I-headed.

## The genuine H-to-H detachment

Fix b,c,d and abbreviate p=p(b,c), a=W(d,p), h=h(b,c,d).
The minor H(a,b,c) is exactly I(I(p,a),h).
The major H(h,d,p) is

    I(I(I(p,W(d,p)),h), I(I(h,p),I(d,p))).

Its antecedent is that minor. One MP therefore gives D(b,c,d).
All these are universal numeric-parameter instantiations of the native rule;
there is no theorem-substitution or renaming principle. This is the family
identified by root, and it is retained rather than incorrectly forbidden.

## Auxiliary class T

Use the same literal r1--r6, merged G, and unchanged bundle formulas as in
the reviewed RAB proof. Let T be generated by those axioms, H, all identities,
GEN, quantified monotonicity, and additionally every J and D formula above.
There is no MP constructor. J need not actually be derivable: T is an
auxiliary overapproximation. It even permits the disabled bundle triples.

No R- or E-headed code belongs to T, and every T(Q_i x) witness ends in
GEN, so Q stripping holds. The previous three forbidden shapes remain
forbidden:

    I(E(i,j),F),
    X(i,x)=I(Q_i I(Q_i x,F),F),
    I(V(a,b,c),nks(a,b,c)).

The old inversions apply to every old generator; the new J and D generators
have I-headed left and right fields. Thus they cannot match the first two
shapes (left E or Q), or the third shape's Q-headed right field. No new
case enters the previous r1/r6/bundle major analyses.

## H triggers produce only J or D

Suppose T(I(p(b,c),a)). Inspect its final constructor.

* GEN has the wrong outer tag. Monotonicity and r1/r2/r4 have Q left
  fields; r3/r5/G have E left fields.
* An identity forces a=p(b,c). H's consequent is then J(b,c).
* An r6(i,x) minor forces x=a, c=Q_i I(Q_i a,F), and W(b,c)=F.
  The last equality gives b=c=0, contradicting the displayed positive Q.
* A bundle minor forces its Q-headed nks field to equal W(b,c), impossible.
* An H(x,y,z) minor forces c=p(y,z), x=W(b,c), and
  a=I(I(x,z),I(y,z)). The resulting H consequent is precisely D(y,z,b).
* A J(y,z) minor forces c=p(y,z) and z=W(b,c). But
  c=p(y,z)>=z=W(b,c)>c, a contradiction.
* A D(y,z,e) minor forces its I-headed p(y,z) to equal W(b,c), impossible.

These exhaust T. The zero identity cannot be a trigger because p(b,c)>0;
identity injectivity already handles every other case.

## J never fires

Fix b,c; write w=W(b,c), p=p(b,c). A J major would require T(I(p,c)).
No generator supplies this formula:

* GEN has the wrong outer tag; monotonicity and r1/r2/r4 have Q left
  fields; r3/r5/G have E left fields.
* Identity would require p=c, contradicting p>c.
* An r6 minor forces W(b,c)=F and c=Q_i I(Q_i c,F), contradicting
  c=0 from W=F and positivity of Q.
* A bundle minor would identify its Q-headed nks with w.
* An H(x,y,z) minor forces x=w from matching the two fields of p,
  and c=I(I(x,z),I(y,z))>=x=w>c.
* A J(y,z) minor forces z=w and c=I(y,z)>=z=w>c.
* A D(y,z,e) minor forces p(y,z)=w, contradicting I versus R tags.

Thus T(I(p,c)) is impossible, independent of MP.

## D never fires

Fix b,c,d; abbreviate w=W(b,c), p=p(b,c), a=W(d,p), h=h(b,c,d).
A D major would require T(I(h,p)). Again inspect every generator:

* GEN has the wrong outer tag; monotonicity and r1/r2/r4 have Q left
  fields; r3/r5/G have E left fields.
* Identity would give h=p. Comparing right fields gives I(b,c)=w,
  impossible by tags.
* A bundle or r1 has right field F, while the desired right field p is I-headed.
* An r6 minor would have right field p and left field
  I(Q_i I(Q_i p,F),F)=h. Comparing h's right field gives F=I(b,c), impossible.
* An H(x,y,z) minor has right field I(I(x,z),I(y,z))=p;
  comparing p's right field forces I(y,z)=w, impossible.
* A J(y,z) minor has right field I(y,z)=p, so y=c and z=w.
  Its left field I(p(y,z),z)=h then gives z=I(b,c), again w=I(b,c).
* A D(y,z,e) minor has right field I(e,p(y,z))=p, forcing
  p(y,z)=w, impossible.

Some exclusions overlap deliberately (r1 is already excluded by its left
field). There is no unexamined generator.

## MP closure and consequence

Induct on the T witness for a major I(u,v), with a T(u) witness as parameter.
Identity, GEN, monotonicity and r1--r6/G/bundle cases are exactly the previous
proof. An H major yields J or D by the trigger lemma. A J or D major cannot
have its required minor by the two new forbidden-shape lemmas. Therefore T
is MP-closed and contains every derivation of the exact modified calculus.
Since T contains no R-headed code, it does not contain F.

The proof is finite syntactic reasoning and formalizable in ZFC. It proves
consistency for each of the two fixed orientations separately. It does not
assert consistency of a calculus admitting a union of different orientations
as extra axiom families. Combined with the RAB/RBA/RAC/RCA analysis, every
one of the six fixed, distinct-input membership replacements considered in
the allocation experiment is therefore provably consistent in ZFC.

Consequently, assuming ZFC consistent, none of those exact fixed variants
can have its consistency provably equivalent to Con(ZFC) in ZFC. This is a
calculus obstruction, not a machine-runtime claim. The original unrestricted
K219 and its accepted machine remain outside this argument.

## Bounded executable evidence, separate from the proof

`condensed_v2.py` uses exact native constructors, pair injectivity and the
I(0,0)=0 alias. It records explicit child instantiations for each MP or GEN
schema and separately validates them by simultaneous substitution and
normalization. Every literal bundle carries c!=a and c!=b conditions through
instantiation; only constructor-tag-disjoint inequalities are discharged.
Goals count only if no residual condition remains.

The first reviewer implementation incorrectly applied stored simultaneous
substitutions transitively and stopped at its own GEN assertion; that source,
failure record and narrow correction remain in bc-run1 and v2.diff. No
logical conclusion was taken from the failed attempt.

The corrected three-round RBC run had 58 schemas and 1,444 ordered MP
attempts. The actual full gated-bundle run had 456 schemas and 128,174
attempts, finished rounds 1--10, and stopped in round 11 at the explicit
25-second bound. It found none of the universal F, identity, K, S, Peirce,
EFQ or implication-to-truth targets. Its only new unquantified nonmodal
family was the displayed D. These negative search facts are bounded;
the exhaustive T proof above is the reason for the general conclusion.
