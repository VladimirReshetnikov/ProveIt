\begin{abstract}
We inspect the current 215-state ZFC-consistency machines, the existing Lean
and Rocq correctness developments, and all seven proposal archives in
\code{docs/incoming}. Three further formal contributions are supplied.
First, a balanced, binary-indexed certificate checker proves the exact K215
source equivalence for every register file, with the old checker as its
verified specification. Second, one finite-table checker proves arbitrary-tape
halting equivalence for the M124/FM215 marker family, including the explicitly
instantiated provider-125 member. Third, the actual Cantor command encoding
and exhaustive schedule satisfy the zero-credit prefix theorem, with no
abstract parsing or scheduler hypotheses. All new formal contributions were
compiled using Coq 8.18.0, with closed theorem assumption reports; the precise
recursive-check scopes are recorded. A wider local-fold search found no
successful reduction to 214 states. We do not claim a 214-state machine, a
state-count lower bound, or a fresh rebuild of the complete inherited
consistency endpoint. The report distinguishes existing proposals, new
verified refinements, finite corroboration, and remaining integration work.
\end{abstract}
