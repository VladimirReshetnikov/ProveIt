The machine and existing formal developments belong to the ProveIt contributors
and the upstream contributors identified in the repository's candidate-k215
NOTICE.md and proof-source notices. This packet proposes changes to those proofs;
it supplies no new Turing-machine table.

The AX refinements extend the current checked-macro-word method. The Rocq
command/codec factorization is adapted from the maintained Lean EnumPerm design.
Existing normalized certificate, shared implication, generic scheduler, direct
seed-model, and quotient-unification proposals are credited as prior repository
work in the report and are not claimed as new contributions here.

The direct seed-to-successor/Infinity route, formal adapters, source audits and
report were developed for this request. No claim of literature-wide mathematical
priority is made. New code and documentation in this packet are offered under
the accompanying MIT No Attribution license, matching the baseline repository.
