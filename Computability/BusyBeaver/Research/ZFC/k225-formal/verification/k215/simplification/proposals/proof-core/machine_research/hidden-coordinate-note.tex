\subsection{A checked obstruction to recovering atomic vacuity from the logical rows alone}

The late-matrix inflation experiment replaces the accepted bundle guard
$c\ne a,b$ by $c>a+b$.  Its open lower bridge includes recovering enough
membership vacuity.  The following countermodel shows that the retained
\emph{logical rows alone} cannot recover every missing instance.  This is
not a countermodel of the complete restricted set-theoretic bundle calculus.

Take the domain $D=\{0,1\}$ and valuations $e:\mathbb N\to D$.  Interpret
implication, falsity, equality and quantifiers by their usual Boolean and
coordinate-update operations, but interpret membership atoms by
\[
             e\models R(a,b)
       \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad e(0)=0\ \land\ e(a)\ne e(b).
\]
Every atom thus has one additional dependence on coordinate $0$.  In
particular $R(a,a)$ is always false, so the native falsity alias $R(0,0)$
is respected.

All retained propositional and quantifier schemas remain valid:
$\rho_1=\exists_i(i=j)$,
$\rho_2=\forall_i(A\to B)\to(\forall_i A\to\forall_i B)$,
$\rho_4=\forall_i\forall_j A\to\forall_j\forall_i A$, and
$\rho_6=(\exists_i\forall_i A)\to A$.
The equality-congruence rows $\rho_3,\rho_5$ preserve the same hidden
factor $e(0)=0$ on both sides.  Ordinary equality transitivity and
\emph{every} equality-vacuity instance are valid as well, hence so is the
merged equality row whenever its helper binder differs from its two
endpoint labels.  The unrestricted propositional $\rho_8$ is valid;
restricting it by numerical domination only weakens the fragment.
Theorem-only generalization and modus ponens preserve validity.

For $c>a+b$, the binder $c$ differs from $0,a,b$.  Updating it changes
none of the three coordinates used by $R(a,b)$, so every permitted
membership-vacuity instance is valid.  However, at
$e(0)=e(1)=0$, $e(2)=1$,
\[
                R(1,2)\longrightarrow\forall_0 R(1,2)
\]
is false: its antecedent is true and updating coordinate $0$ to $1$
falsifies the quantified atom.  Consequently this formula has no proof
from the stated logical fragment, even after adding all equality-vacuity
instances.  The standalone Lean file \texttt{HiddenCoordinate.lean}
formalizes this stronger fragment, checks every axiom, proves induction
on derivations, and proves this nonderivability theorem.  Lean 4.32.0
accepts it; its reported axioms are only \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.

The practical implication is narrow: an attempted proof using only the
old logical rows and cofinally fresh atomic vacuity cannot finish the
large-matrix bridge.  A successful lower reduction must use additional
consequences of the restricted seed bundles or build a different
refutation interpretation.  No conclusion about the consistency strength
of the full restricted calculus, or about existence of a 214-state
machine, follows from this countermodel.
