# Further simplification: two local commutations instead of a general source certificate

Status: independent structural/Python validation, not a kernel proof. Inputs are the exact K215 `Prog215.v` and `DagPerm.v` snapshots already hashed in `source_stats.json`. The latest targeted source check is recorded separately in `latest_status.json`.

## Main finding

The two original programs do **not** have identical ordered primitive effects. Zero register offsets did not imply that, and the checker explicitly found the differences.

However, the full control-flow topology is identical after resolving pure `EBreak`/`EContinue` transfers. Every primitive occurrence kind and every successor ordinal match. Only 512 increment labels differ, forming exactly 256 adjacent swaps:

- 175 occurrences of `Inc7; Inc6` in p215 versus `Inc6; Inc7` in dagPerm.
- 81 occurrences of `Inc1; Inc6` in p215 versus `Inc6; Inc1` in dagPerm.

These are generated by just **two shared structured-DAG nodes**, not 256 independent semantic changes:

- dagPerm node 188: `(EInc 6, EInc 7)`.
- dagPerm node 190: `(EInc 6, EInc 1)`.

Reversing the two children of **only those two nodes** makes the complete ordered primitive CFG of dagPerm exactly equal to p215, including initial ordinal 0 and every branch target. The experiment checks all 3,326 primitive occurrences, not merely observed traces, test sites, or net increment counts.

Every swapped pair's second instruction has the first as its **only predecessor**, and is not the initial state. Thus the comparison has not silently commuted instructions across a test or allowed a jump into the middle of a swapped pair. All 3,326 primitive occurrences are reachable in the symbolic CFG when both decrement outcomes are considered. Symbolic reachability does not assert that every branch is feasible for a concrete register valuation.

## Distinguish the counts

Both programs have 4,751 expanded raw source leaves:

| Kind | Count |
|---|---:|
| Increment | 1,900 |
| Decrement/test | 1,425 |
| Halt | 1 |
| Break | 98 |
| Continue | 1,327 |

Removing the 1,425 pure control leaves (98 breaks plus 1,327 continues) leaves **3,326 non-control primitive occurrences**. Contracting straight-line increments into their register-count vector gives **1,426 test/halt rows** and **2,851 outgoing branch obligations including the initial entry** (2 × 1,425 + 1).

The earlier 2,240-entry bisimulation certificate has 2,239 nonhalt entries and one halt entry, hence 4,479 branch obligations per direction (2 × 2,239 + 1). The larger count comes from multiple raw path representations of the same semantic leaf site. Canonicalizing each side to the exact path consumed by its walk gives **1,426 distinct paired sites**, eliminating **814 duplicate checkpoint rows**.

This does not contradict the earlier finding that all 2,240 literal rows were reachable and all literal path pairs distinct. Distinct raw paths can have the same consumed prefix after padding with zero bits or dropping unused zero suffixes. The analysis found:

- p215: 1,954 certificate paths shorter than their full leaf path, 192 equal-length, 94 longer; all discarded suffixes zero.
- dagPerm: 1,970 shorter, 184 equal-length, 86 longer; all discarded suffixes zero.

The pinned Rocq `RMEquiv` already defines `ipeq` (equality after zero padding) and proves `step_ipeq` and `hn_ipeq`. These are useful existing lemmas for this simplification.

## A substantially smaller proof route

Two alternatives are worth distinguishing.

### A. Two local contracts plus exact structural CFG equality

1. Define `dagPermComm` by reversing only nodes 188 and 190.
2. Prove once that two independent register increments commute pointwise, with unchanged exit/control behavior. Instantiate for registers (6,7) and (6,1).
3. Apply a generic closed-node/context transfer theorem to transport `dagPermComm` to `dagPerm`. The proof cost should follow the two shared source templates, not 256 expanded occurrences.
4. Use a generic source-to-flat-CFG theorem and kernel evaluation to prove that p215 and dagPermComm have the same exact ordinal CFG.
5. Compose with the already verified dagPerm correctness theorem. Keep the final source theorem universal in the initial natural-valued register file.

The Lean development has an existing `RMTransfer.Closed` abstraction identified by the Lean investigation. I did **not** find an equivalent whole-node replacement theorem in the inspected Rocq endpoint closure: `RMEquiv` relates small-step and structured big-step semantics, while `ProgramBisim230` instantiates the general certificate checker. Rocq needs a new shared transfer theorem or a port of that abstraction; it is not honest to describe the two contracts alone as a complete existing proof route.

### B. A canonical increment-block CFG

Alternatively, normalize every increment-only segment to its finite register-count vector, and retain its destination test/halt ordinal. The two original programs' normalized CFGs are already exactly equal in the Python experiment. This route incorporates commutation once in the generic normalizer's correctness theorem, with no candidate-specific two-node patch.

Existing `Bisim230.seg_run` and `seg_hn` already establish the semantics of increment/break/continue segments using per-register increment counts, so this is a refactoring of familiar mathematics, not an unmotivated stronger principle. The new work is the canonical ordinal representation and its sound connection to source paths, plus a justified handling of pure-control or increment-only cycles. The experiment encountered no such cycles, but the generic algorithm must reject them or model them explicitly, rather than assign them a halting destination.

The common normalized CFG JSON is 48,941 bytes. That is a diagnostic artifact, not a measured Rocq proof-source or object size. Ideally the kernel computes the normal form directly from the existing compact program data, eliminating the separately maintained 2,240-entry path-pair certificate entirely. Merely emitting the common CFG externally and trusting it would not suffice.

## Where computation is currently duplicated

Exact literal-operation counts from the independent implementation:

| Work | Old one-direction source checker | Two-program cached flat extraction |
|---|---:|---:|
| Source-site walk calls, both programs | 24,696 | 12,540 |
| Source graph edge visits, both programs | 1,570,586 | 802,170 |
| Certificate list lookups | 4,479 | 0 |
| Successful `nth_error` list-cell inspections | 5,018,706 | 0 |

The current forward and mirrored checks repeat the old side twice: 49,392 source-site walks and 10,037,412 certificate list-cell inspections by this counting model. The generic mirror theorem from the earlier report removes one repetition; canonical ordinal comparison also removes the large certificate-index lookup cost.

For list inspections, an index j contributes j+1 successful list-cell inspections. These totals describe the literal algorithm and the Python semantic instrumentation, **not** Rocq timing or exact VM allocation. VM sharing, compiler optimization, table/array representation, and kernel rechecking must be measured in a real build. The cached flat extraction already halves the measured walk work relative to one old direction; a compositional compiler traversing the structured source with an explicit control stack could avoid repeated root-to-leaf walks further, but that improvement is not implemented here.

The old certificate contains 276,072 literal path bits and 4,480 path occurrences. Most full paths are unique, so whole-path string deduplication alone is weak. Canonicalizing semantic leaf sites and removing separate correspondence data is more important than merely choosing a shorter textual path encoding.

## Generic fallback for other candidates: proved binary-trie certificate lookup

For this exact K215 pair, replacing the general certificate with the normalized CFG route is the stronger simplification. For variants that still genuinely need a correspondence certificate, the index lookup can be improved independently:

- 2,240 entries require at most 12 binary index bits.
- The actual 4,479 forward lookups perform **5,018,706** list-cell inspections, summed as index+1.
- A fixed-depth pure-inductive binary trie uses exactly **53,748 branch choices** for the same indices (4,479 × 12), or **58,227 node visits including each final leaf**.
- These are about 93.37 times fewer counted branch choices, or 86.19 times fewer node visits, than list-cell inspections. These are comparisons of different abstract operation counts, **not wall-clock speedups**.

A prefix/heap trie keyed by positive index+1 is another pure-inductive choice: storing values at internal nodes permits at most 11 branch edges/12 node visits for this data. `trie_cost_result.json` records its exact summed depth and the fixed-width alternative.

The formal change must prove that lookup in a trie built from a list equals the old `nth_error` lookup, then transport checker soundness through that refinement. No trusted mutable array primitive, native decision oracle, or new axiom is needed. Trie construction, bit extraction and proof checking still cost work and must be benchmarked. This only accelerates certificate indexing; binary-packing a 176-bit path does not remove its semantic traversal. Canonical CFG/node indices address that separate cost.

## Required semantic obligations for the generic compiler

The two routes above must not be conflated:

- Route A preserves every ordered primitive and uses exact CFG equality only **after** two shared-template commutations. Its dependencies are a local whole-node transfer theorem (already available in Lean), the two increment contracts, and a new generic flattening theorem.
- Route B does **not** change dagPerm. It uses equality of the original programs' 1,426-row increment-count CFGs and can reuse `seg_run`/`seg_hn` for block semantics. It needs a canonical-site/ordinal checker or an explicit extension of the existing checker to path equivalence.

In particular, deleting the 814 alias rows (36.34% of 2,240) does **not** immediately produce a certificate accepted by the existing Rocq `branch_ok`. That checker uses literal `lbeq` of residual paths. Before a canonicalized 1,426-row certificate can be accepted, the proof must replace/extend that test with canonical consumed paths or proved `ipeq`, then use `step_ipeq`/`hn_ipeq` to justify the semantic quotient. The Python quotient is evidence for this extension, not an implementation of its formal proof.

The generic flattening/normalization theorem must discharge all of these obligations:

1. Source graph walk terminates and reaches a genuine leaf under the existing well-formedness condition. A malformed/stuck source must not be relabelled as `EHalt`.
2. Every eliminated pure-control chain terminates at a primitive leaf. Well-formed tree walking alone does not guarantee this: `EBreak`/`EContinue` transfers can form control-only cycles. The extractor must reject such a chain, produce a checked decreasing rank/finite trace, or represent its divergence explicitly. Our executable extractor detects repeated canonical leaf sites and rejects control-only cycles.
3. Route B additionally rejects or explicitly represents increment-only cycles. An infinite increment loop must never be replaced by a finite increment vector. Our block normalizer rejects repeated increment ordinals.
4. The initial empty source path must normalize to the claimed CFG initial state and initial increment vector. Checking only internal edges is insufficient. Here both initial ordinals are 0, and the checked common initial block is empty increments followed by test ordinal 0.
5. On a nonzero decrement branch, the tested register is decremented **before** the following increment vector is applied. On the zero branch it is unchanged. Preserve the branch ordering and exact tested register.
6. Every CFG edge must identify the exact destination ordinal, with the source path related to that destination's canonical path. `ipeq` handles zero padding; it is not permission to discard arbitrary suffix bits.
7. The explicit halt leaf and its stopping behavior must be preserved/reflected. Finite internal elimination must be shown to progress or decrease its rank, so the proof cannot hide divergence behind infinite zero-step stuttering.
8. State the resulting halting equivalence for every initial natural-valued register file, then instantiate at the accepted boot state. No concrete-trace sampling or unproved reachability restriction replaces this quantification.

These obligations are why the structural/Python equality is a strong guide to a smaller proof, but not itself a semantic or kernel proof.
