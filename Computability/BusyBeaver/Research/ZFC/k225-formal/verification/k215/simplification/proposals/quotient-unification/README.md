# One bisimulation quotient: CP/GIC through a `QHyp` adapter

Lean proposal, 2026-10-21, against current `main` (phase 14). Production is left at the checked
phase-14 bytes; [`quotient-unification.patch`](quotient-unification.patch) applies to it
(`git apply --check` passes), and [`lean/`](lean/BusyBeaver/ZFC) holds the 13 resulting files.

## The duplication

`KQ1Model`, `KQ1Separation`, `KQ1Basic`, `KQ1Power`, `KQ1Replacement` and `KQ1Collect` are
generated by `generate_kq1.py` from `QuotientModel` … `QuotientCPGIC`: the same quotient
construction and the same Separation, Pairing, Union, Power, Replacement and collection proofs,
with `h : ModelsCPGIC mem` replaced by the weaker `h : KQHyp.QHyp mem`. Every proof therefore
exists twice.

## The change

The `QHyp` development becomes the only one, and the CP/GIC lane is its instance.

- `QuotientModel.ofCPGIC : ModelsCPGIC mem → QHyp mem` (one theorem) supplies all nine fields:
  the GIC half, the four `eqv_*_of_model` laws, profile pairs, and interpreted union, power and
  singleton-closed containers from the raw CP container (the three proofs formerly in
  `QuotientBasic`/`QuotientPower`, unchanged in substance).
- `QuotientModel.Domain`, `mk` and `quotientMember` are `abbrev`s of the `KQ1Model` ones at
  `ofCPGIC`. The two setoids have the same relation `Eqv mem` and differ only in proof fields, so
  definitional proof irrelevance identifies the types; no transport lemma is needed.
- Every other kept old-lane declaration keeps its name and statement and is proved by the `KQ1`
  theorem at `ofCPGIC` in one line. Kept: every name used in Lean or cited anywhere in the
  repository's records. Dropped, cited nowhere: `member_left_congr`, `member_congr`,
  `modelSetoid`, the separation matrix lemmas, the `interpreted_*` resource lemmas,
  `SingletonClosed` and its form, `trace_instance`, `replacementRename`.
- The `KQ1` files become maintained source: fork headers and CP/GIC wording removed, `KQ1Model`
  owns the formula translation, and the three trivial field lemmas (`interpreted_union := h.union a`
  and the like) are inlined. `generate_kq1.py` is retired. In `k225-formal/README.md` the generator
  sentence and the `KQ1*` description change accordingly (not part of the patch, since that file
  moves independently).

Import order: `KQHyp → KQ1Model → KQ1Separation → KQ1Power → QuotientModel → QuotientSeparation
→ …`, acyclic because the adapter needs only upstream resources and `KQ1Power.trace_instance`.
