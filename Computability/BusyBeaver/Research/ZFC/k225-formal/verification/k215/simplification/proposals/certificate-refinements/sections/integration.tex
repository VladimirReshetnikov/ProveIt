\section{Proof architecture and integration plan}\label{sec:integration}

\subsection{Which proof should be shortened?}

There are three different optimization targets. Minimizing the additional
proof for a variant favors a short transport from an accepted machine.
Minimizing an independent semantic proof favors a simple enumerator and
stable command contracts. Minimizing the maintained build favors compact
data, efficient checked lookup, and a small affected dependency closure.
One candidate need not optimize all three.

\begin{longtable}{@{}P{.25\textwidth}P{.31\textwidth}P{.38\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Choice & Main advantage & Remaining cost\\\midrule\endhead
Primary K215 & Both complete endpoints already exist; new source theorem
has the exact required type & Adopt checked refinements and rebuild the
affected clients\\[4pt]
M124 / FM215 / M125 & Two incoming-edge invariants; one shared finite
checker; arbitrary-tape transfer & Native/dense/zipper bridge and endpoint
composition; same inherited calibration\\[4pt]
CM215 & Very short transfer already delivered; existing concrete dense/Core
bridge & One extra origin case; final full-endpoint replay\\[4pt]
ZC215 & Concrete exhaustive scheduler; one occurrence injection handles an
arbitrary opaque stack tail & Exact source, binary runtime, startup, and
calibration connections\\[4pt]
Direct / forced variants & Potential source or allocation freedoms &
Derived command contracts, protected-tail accounting, and completeness
arguments can be more involved\\[4pt]
Rebalanced / reencoded variants & Smaller addresses or changed enumeration
parameters & Fewer addresses or lower cutoffs do not alone reduce the
number of semantic obligations\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

The first recommendation is therefore to keep the primary machine while
simplifying its proof. For an additional machine, use the two-marker
family if the smallest new invariant is the priority, or CM215 if the
already supplied representation bridge saves the most integration work.
For a substantially independent scheduler proof, develop ZC215 around
the concrete theorem in Section~\ref{sec:credit}. These are judgments
from the inspected obligations, not measured rankings of complete future
proof developments \cite{alternatives,incoming}.

\subsection{Recommended sequence for the current endpoint}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Freeze the public interfaces and exact table.}
Retain the native and dense table hashes and the public source theorem
\code{p215\_dagPerm}. Keep the final statements phrased in terms of the
same numerical ZFC-refutation predicate. This lets downstream proofs
remain clients of stable statements.

\item \textbf{Use the already checked one-sided source argument.}
The incoming zero-offset result permits ordinary bisimulation; symmetry
supplies the reverse implication. Reuse its compact path codec and exact
certificate rather than maintaining offset arithmetic and a second
expensive mirrored computation in this instance. The generic offset
theory may remain available for other machines.

\item \textbf{Adopt the balanced checker behind that interface.}
Move the new modules into the maintained namespace, reuse the existing
imported dependencies, and replace the body of \code{p215\_dagPerm} by
the proved balanced body. Update the certificate producer together with
its output so that regeneration preserves the change. Build
\code{Correct215} and \code{CorrectDense215} under the maintained Rocq
toolchain and retain fresh assumption reports.

\item \textbf{Combine normalization and balanced storage only after an
exact binding proof.}
The incoming Lean normalization reduces 2,240 checkpoints to 1,426.
Balanced storage and semantic normalization address different costs and
can be combined. However, the structural packet's canonical certificate
does not pass the unchanged raw-pointer checker. Port the proved decoding
or canonical-address correspondence, then prove equality with the
appropriate checker specification. This packet has not compiled the
combined 1,426-entry balanced instance.

\item \textbf{Measure the complete affected build.}
Separate generator time, data/exactness compilation, certificate
evaluation, and downstream theorem compilation. Report a cold build and
an incremental build with exact reused-object bindings. The current
single-run source timing does not predict the total.
\end{enumerate}

The same architecture is suitable for Lean, but the balanced implementation
in this delivery is Coq source. It should not be described as a new checked
Lean optimization. The incoming Lean ordinary and normalized lanes are the
starting point for that port.

\subsection{Keep four small semantic interfaces}

The clearest maintained mathematical proof separates four statements:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Numerical logic.} A syntactic ZFC refutation exists exactly
when the numerical proof system can produce its designated contradiction.
Preserve the actual contracts for arbitrary natural codes, aliases, and
nondecodable values. Adding an unproved well-formed-formula restriction
would change the problem.
\item \textbf{Enumeration and commands.} Establish command soundness and
strong completeness against the exact candidate order. For ZC215, the
closed prefix-credit theorem is the one reusable safety interface.
Successful bounded traces cannot replace exhaustive-order or completeness
arguments.
\item \textbf{Source and binary execution.} Connect semantic steps by
positive finite blocks on a quantified tape representation. A generic
positive-stuttering theorem discharges halting transport once block
contracts and progress are known. Keep exact source/table binding as a
finite check.
\item \textbf{Initialization.} Prove one exact complete postconfiguration:
control, head, every represented field, spare layout, and zero exterior.
Structured scan summaries can shorten the finite proof. The incoming
semantic seed round-trip and startup-summary proposals are useful
candidates for this layer, but their new formal versions were not checked
here.
\end{enumerate}

For variants, state these interfaces once and prove only the changed
contract. A small instance that reuses a complete parent theorem reduces
additional maintenance, while the parent's
logical dependency closure remains part of the assurance argument.

From the extracted root, run the following commands separately:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 verify_packet.py
python3 build_pdf.py
python3 balanced_lookup/reproduce.py --output /tmp/k215-balanced-fresh
python3 balanced_lookup/lookup_metrics.py
python3 marker_certificate/build.py --out /tmp/k215-marker-fresh
python3 marker_certificate/verify.py --output /tmp/k215-marker-finite.json
python3 zero_credit/build.py --out /tmp/k215-credit-fresh
\end{verbatim}

The proof commands require Coq 8.18.0 and its standard library; the balanced
folder includes its small inherited dependency closure. The generic
recursive audit can be added with \code{--check-generic}; the ordinary
baseline can be rebuilt with \code{--benchmark}. The other two proof
drivers include recursive checks. Use fresh output paths to preserve
the supplied receipts.

PDF rebuilding requires a standard LaTeX installation with the packages
listed in \code{report.tex}. Python integrity and finite-table checks need
no third-party packages. The state search additionally needs a C++17
compiler and the pinned ProveIt checkout:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 state_search/run_search.py \
  --repo /absolute/path/to/ProveIt --out /tmp/k215-search-fresh
\end{verbatim}

\code{verify\_packet.py} checks file hashes and recorded evidence bindings;
it is not a replacement for the proof-assistant runs. Logs with zero bytes
are expected for successful \code{coqchk -silent} calls, and their exit
statuses are separately recorded. Preserved notices identify inherited
construction, semantics, and proposal material.

\subsection{Acceptance criteria for a future 214-state claim}

A successful next construction should provide the literal complete binary
table with at most 214 working states, exact initialization included,
and a checked route to the same refutation predicate. A transport route
should prove a complete initial-configuration relation, whole-tape
preservation at boundaries, positive progress, designated-halt
preservation and reflection, and an exact representation bridge.
An independent route should discharge the four semantic interfaces above.
State labels, graph isomorphism, a long nonhalting trace, or an informal
claim that a state is \emph{unused} do not supply those obligations.

The deliverable advances the proof infrastructure and supplies additional
checked 215-state instances. The missing one-state reduction remains an
open construction problem in this investigation.
