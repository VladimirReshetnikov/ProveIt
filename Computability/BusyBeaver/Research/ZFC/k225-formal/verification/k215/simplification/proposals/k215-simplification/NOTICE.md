# Attribution and provenance

This research packet is prepared for incorporation into
VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt. Its repository baseline is commit
`b6c7087477e51c017003bd729e712c8969733223`.

The original K215 and conditional-marker machine constructions, source
calculus and enumerator, compiler, runtime, and existing ZFC correctness
proofs remain contributions of their original authors. The conditional-marker
machine is an existing repository candidate; this packet supplies a new
formal relative proof of it. No new machine with 214 or fewer states is
claimed.

Copied inputs and proof dependencies retain their existing licenses and
notices. Exact paths and byte identities appear in the provenance and input
manifests of the respective subdirectories. `cm215/upstream/licenses/` and
`search214/licenses/` preserve the ProveIt and inherited Wade license texts;
the source-normalization packet includes its own attribution records.

New proof modules, generators, search scripts, reproduction drivers, and
exposition in this packet may be incorporated into ProveIt under its
existing MIT No Attribution License. No additional restriction is imposed
on those new contributions.

The supplied verification receipts describe the actual commands and scopes
checked for this packet. The new relative machine proof and replacement
source proof reuse existing formal interfaces. They are not represented as
a fresh complete rebuild of the entire inherited K215-to-ZFC development.
