# Attribution and scope

This research and integration packet was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's
ProveIt project. New material follows the repository's MIT No Attribution terms
in `LICENSE`; inherited material keeps its own existing terms and notices.

The guarded left-frame decomposition builds on ProveIt's earlier
`round13-left-frame` proposal. The present packet supplies checked generic frame
proofs and refines that decomposition by allowing state-1 and state-2 return
contracts, yielding twenty local templates and checked Lean source attachments.
The exact source-action and decoder-route boundary also builds on the
repository's semantic-interface and Source193 residual-contract work.

The unchanged maintained source snapshots preserve their original headers.
`licenses/NOTICE.K215.md` records the accepted machine's ancestry, and
`licenses/K230-THIRD_PARTY.md` records the symbolic runtime's upstream origins,
including Jeff Ketchersid's m254 reference, BusyCoq, and other dependencies.
The original notices and license texts under `licenses/` remain controlling for
their respective scopes. In particular, the project license is not an assertion
that all custom third-party reference components have been relicensed.

Automated independent checks in this packet are separate implementations and
proof-assistant checks within this work; they are not external human endorsement.
No new machine-state record, discovery-priority claim, internal PA/ZFC derivation,
minimum-template result, or unconditional nonhalting theorem is asserted.
