calibration, original compiler/runtime/startup results, and the established
Lean/Rocq endpoints are inherited ProveIt contributions.

library come from K215_Checked_Simplifications_2026-10-21. The balanced lookup
library comes from K215_Certificate_Refinements_2026-10-21. The new source
component composes those existing proofs. Each inherited file keeps its notice.

binding to KSource215; it retains incoming framed-block and structural data.

the CM215 machine and underlying marker idea are prior work. Its reference
BusyCoq and K215 sources retain their own notices and licenses.

application, the exact five-zero source-class optimum, the constructive source
transformation, and the recorded restricted physical allocation attempts.

unconditional consistency result, or newly rebuilt complete ZFC endpoint.
