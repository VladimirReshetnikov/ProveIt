# Attribution and inherited terms

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project on 21 October 2026.
This packet contains a research report, proposed Lean refactorings, a guarded
primitive-contract pilot, and scripts and evidence for targeted checks.

The baseline is ProveIt commit
`2ca3a5c16f0876d2cab842056353c03a1ed2f5f0`. Modified repository files retain
their original headers. The source, machine, compact theory, compiler, proof
infrastructure, and prior simplifications retain their existing attribution.
The preserved K215 notice credits ProveIt and Mira, Elliot Glazer, Jeff
Ketchersid, Andrew James Wade, BusyCoq, and the Coq Library of Undecidability
within their respective scopes. Its references to paths describe the upstream
repository, not necessarily files redistributed in this small packet.

New report text, scripts, and refactoring contributions are provided for
incorporation under the project's MIT No Attribution terms in `LICENSE`.
This does not replace or relicense inherited third-party terms. The original
K215 notice and its immediate license/provenance files are preserved in
`notices/`.

The packet claims no priority for the existing normalized certificate, shared
bundle semantics, exact decoder-prefix proof, shared Home return, 58-step
condensed-detachment derivation, or the earlier proposal to parameterize the
named-calculus reasoning. The checked CB1 pilot adapts an explicitly uncompiled
phase-nine proposal; the exact original file and its provenance are retained
under `provenance/`.

The verification evidence concerns the specific separate Lean overlays
identified in `evidence/verification_summary.json`. It is not a new complete
K215/Rocq certificate or an internal PA/ZFC derivation of the endpoint.
