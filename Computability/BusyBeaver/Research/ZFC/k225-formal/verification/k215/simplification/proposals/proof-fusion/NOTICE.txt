ATTRIBUTION AND TERMS
====================

This contribution was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project.
Its new work is the direct contract-predicate compilation in Lean and Rocq,
the reviewable integration patches and compatibility adapters, the checked
production-interpreter locality and saved-support interfaces, and the finite
replay and source measurements described in report.tex.

The underlying calculi, implication derivation, enumerator, block contracts,
symbolic interpreter, Home shortcut, machine candidates, logical calibration
and earlier dependency-reuse design are inherited ProveIt work. Existing
normalized-certificate, semantic-coverage and quotient-unification proposals
are explicitly attributed in the report, not claimed as newly discovered.

New material follows the repository's MIT No Attribution terms, reproduced
in licenses/LICENSE.ProveIt. This does not relicense inherited third-party
components. Relevant inherited K215 notices and license copies are retained
in licenses/NOTICE.K215.md and licenses/k215-inherited/. Relative links inside
the preserved notice refer to its original location in the pinned repository.

The source baseline and all citations are pinned to:
  https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt/tree/6d9800d1ca67c52db0eb6e1c65df7b5cab9cdb95

The component reviews were performed by separate agents within the same
assistant collaboration. They are not external human reviews or endorsements.
The included compiler and kernel logs describe the actual focused checks;
they do not certify a new complete K215 endpoint build.
