# Provenance and attribution

Prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project, 21 October 2026.

The exact upstream snapshot is commit
`b6c7087477e51c017003bd729e712c8969733223` of
<https://github.com/VladimirReshetnikov/ProveIt>.
`source_index.json` records the original paths, Git blob IDs and SHA-256
digests of all fourteen inherited numerical table inputs, and paths and
digests of the retained notices. `novelty_current_tree.json` independently
records all 622 current ZFC-tree numerical table inputs used in the finite
comparison.

The original compact calculus, compiler architecture, primary K215
construction, calibrated startup, and upstream Rocq/Lean consistency
theorems remain attributed to their original authors. The retained
upstream notices credit, within their stated scopes, Mira-Cult and
ProveIt contributors, Jeff Ketchersid and collaborators, Elliot Glazer,
Andrew James Wade, BusyCoq, and the Coq Library of Undecidability.
Those notices must be read with their original scopes and dates; a
historical disclaimer about an older packet does not describe the
companion FM215 kernel receipt.

This contribution adds the FM215 four-row transformation, exact all-tape
simulation argument, generators and finite checks, full current-tree
graph comparison, bounded row/helper compression searches, and the
concrete runtime-contract counterexample to the explicitly rejected
214-state trial. The companion Lean FM215 delta theorem and generic
positive simulation theorem were prepared and checked by the separate
proof-audit role in the same research collaboration. No authorship of
upstream mathematics or earlier optimizations is asserted.

New material is prepared for incorporation in ProveIt under its MIT No
Attribution terms. The exact repository license and inherited notices
are retained under `inherited_notices/`; this does not relicense
third-party material.

There is no minimum-state claim, unconditional nonhalting claim,
certified 214-state construction, or priority claim beyond the explicitly
documented comparison against the pinned finite repository corpus.
