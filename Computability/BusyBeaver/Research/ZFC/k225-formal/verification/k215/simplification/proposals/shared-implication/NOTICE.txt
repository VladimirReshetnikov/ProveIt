PROVENANCE AND ATTRIBUTION

This packet proposes changes against Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository
at 2ca3a5c16f0876d2cab842056353c03a1ed2f5f0.

The existing machine, its mathematical calibration, proof infrastructure and
checked endpoint are upstream work. Inherited headers and attributions are
retained in the replacement files. In particular, the K-family runtime follows
Elliot Glazer's k230-rocq development, with earlier machine and symbolic-runtime
work by Jeff Ketchersid and collaborators, as credited by the upstream sources.

The old Lukasiewicz certificates were an attributed port of Jeff's CD254.v;
the new patch records that historical origin. The dominated-implication
derivation, original renderer and 58-step core are already in ProveIt. The new
contribution here is a five-step S extension, its shared unrestricted adapter,
synchronized producer changes, and the resulting removal of duplicate proof work.

The semantic SiteSpec design was previously proposed in ProveIt's incoming
further-simplifications/runtime_contexts report. This packet credits that design
and provides its concrete Lean implementation, old-interface wrappers, mixed
coverage lemma, verification drivers and focused compiler evidence.

The finite diagonal countermodel and mathematical exposition were prepared
for this proposal. No novelty or minimality claim is made for classical
implicational axiom systems.

This packet does not assign a new blanket license to inherited third-party
sources or remove their original provenance. It includes no compiled proof
objects and makes no new machine-state-count claim.
