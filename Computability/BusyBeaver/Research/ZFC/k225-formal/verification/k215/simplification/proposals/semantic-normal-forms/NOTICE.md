# Attribution and result boundaries

This research package was prepared for Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt.
It builds on the exact repository snapshots and original research identified
in `provenance.json`, the report references, and component notices.

Inherited material includes the accepted K215 machine and theorem interfaces,
the source language and native calculus, prior seed encodings, the 1,426-vertex
canonical graph and 1,059-drain census, the existing marker alternatives,
zero-credit scheduler work, and the allocator/rotation ancestry. Those discoveries
are not attributed to this package.

New contributions include the checked seed semantic-normal-form refactoring,
the compiled universal drain/rank theorem with literal checks, the AX9 source
factorizations and written compiler repair, checked protected-tail and finite
ghost-zero semantic lemmas, deterministic source edits, expanded bounded fold
search, and the resulting allocation/search evidence.

New proof, report and verifier material follows ProveIt's MIT No Attribution
terms. Inherited licenses and notices remain alongside the corresponding inputs;
no inherited material is relicensed. No global priority claim is made.
