## New work

GC215 changes AX's generalization head from `Q_U(oldH)` to `Q_C(oldH)`,
where C is the private parameter encoded by the command tag. Its strong
generalization compiler consequently omits the former counter-loading
command. The exact two-site source change, corrected allocation, and
conditional physical merge of native roles 129 and 310 produce the new
215-state table. The complete source/runtime/startup checks bind the
ordinary calibration to that literal table.

The other new principal result is the quantifier-padding theorem: restricting
the sixth native axiom to matrices larger than the binder, or than a specified
total primitive-recursive cutoff, preserves every raw theorem code. Its
107-node certificate and independent readers are supplied. The remaining
research notes distinguish proved interfaces from unsuccessful implementation
searches and incomplete logical fragments.

## Exact scope

The machine theorem is an ordinary mathematical proof formalizable in ZFC,
using the pinned native-calculus calibration. The executable checker proves
symbolic primitive contracts using literal transitions and induction rules;
the default verifier audits saved metadata, and `--runtime` freshly derives
all 6,151 cases. Neither mode is a new Lean/Rocq kernel endpoint.

The finite dominated-rho8 certificate is only one part of the inherited
set-theoretic calibration. Its successful replay is not presented as a new
proof of the entire comparison with ZFC. No unconditional nonhalting theorem,
new internal PA endpoint, 214-state construction, or state-count lower bound
is claimed. Graph novelty is relative to the pinned corpus and includes
state relabeling and global tape reflection; it is not semantic inequivalence
or a worldwide priority claim.
