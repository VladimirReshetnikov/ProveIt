# Targeted scientific accuracy review of the report

The reviewer compared the full report draft through references against the
state-search receipts, source composition and kernel-check receipts, Lean
decoder context theorem, and newly compiled production-selector adapter.
This is an internal technical review, not an external endorsement or an
independent rebuild of every proof.

## Assessment

No mathematical or state-arithmetic blocker was found.

- The balanced/normalized result preserves the exact all-register source
  theorem and public facade. It does not claim to rebuild the binary/ZFC
  endpoint. Endpoint lookup counts are explicitly distinguished from total
  kernel work and from wall-clock speedups.
- The decoder result quantifies over arbitrary complete tapes and derives
  every internal source path from the initial marker and pointer bits. The
  primitive-edge, return/frame, maintained-identity, and initialization
  boundaries are stated explicitly.
- The production selector has exactly r+3 commands; for the nine AX rows
  this is 12. Both NIP counts, unchanged counter, final zero cache, and the
  opaque-tail claim are correct. Its exact production command binding is
  compiled, but existing compiler-client replacement remains work to do.
- The marker family stays at 215 working states because its selector uses
  the state freed by merging roles. The comparison with CM215's integration
  status and ZC215's cleaner scheduler is appropriately qualified.
- All five fresh search counts and bounds match the receipts. The report
  correctly avoids treating overlapping specifications as distinct machines,
  distinguishes filter failure from semantic inequivalence, and states no
  state-count lower bound or verified 214-state result.

## Requested precision edits

1. Narrow the abstract's "previously untested final depth-41 R41 table" to
   the table omitted by its original one-row fold census. Other incoming
   searches already cover R41 in different families. Section 7 already
   contains the precise statement.
2. Describe the zero-credit injection as one into **DROP occurrences
   belonging to earlier raw codes**, rather than an injection into raw
   codes alone. Different marked positions can map to distinct DROP
   occurrences of the same code; occurrence multiplicity is essential.

No further edits were requested.
