1. Mathematical contracts

PASS: Both scheduler kernels use only exact candidate-index succession at
pending=0 and strict pending decrease with fixed index at pending<>0, for
successful steps. There is no refill-rank bound and no premise that the initial
state is at a candidate boundary. The lexicographic measure
(target-index(state), pending(state)) proves reach-target-or-halt; a step that
halts is handled before any successor law is needed.

PASS: complete_at fixes one target first and quantifies over boundary states
actually reachable from the starting state. This avoids the invalid exchange
of "for every payload there is a later candidate" with "there is a later
candidate that works for every reachable payload". The parity counterexample
has pending identically zero, advances at every successful step, satisfies
payloadwise cofinal immediate halting, and nevertheless has a nonhalting aligned
orbit. The source proofs and recorded axiom-free Lean check support this claim.

PASS: Concrete EnumPerm completeness can start in an active candidate. Its
fixed compiled candidate is selected using the original starting index and
works uniformly for the boundary payload reached later. Soundness still needs
the original invariant. Do not describe halts_iff_any/enum_from_iff as an
equivalence for every arbitrary malformed register state without that premise.

2. Consistency endpoint and proof-theoretic scope

The pinned Lean endpoint literally states
  BlankHalts machine215 <-> BProv ZFCax_s [] Form.fBot
and, by negation,
  (not BlankHalts machine215) <-> not BProv ZFCax_s [] Form.fBot.
These are external proof-assistant theorems about the repository's exact
finite-refutation predicate. They do not themselves exhibit a coded derivation
inside ZFC or PA of the equivalence. The report should separate this checked
external statement from the user's desired internal provability qualification.
No unconditional Con(ZFC), nonhalting, machine minimization or lower bound is
proved by these refactors. The literal 215-state machine is unchanged.

Lean's standard axiom reports must not be called axiom-free. The generic
scheduler reach/complete proofs report propext and Quot.sound; its invariant
transport is axiom-free. Concrete Lean completeness clients additionally use
Classical.choice. The shared abstract implication exports are axiom-free.
Rocq component reports are closed under the global context. None of this alone
supplies the separate internal arithmetization/provability result.
