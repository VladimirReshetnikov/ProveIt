\section{Further research after these two changes}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{How much kernel work is actually saved by the fork?} The finite replay counts predict less duplicated computation, but evaluator sharing, proof-term representation and normalization cost may dominate. Measure whether the shared prefix survives elaboration and kernel checking rather than being expanded twice by generated terms.
\item \textbf{Can prefixes be shared across distinct sites?} This report shares only the two outcomes of each decrement. A trie or DAG of common decoder prefixes might share work between sites, but must preserve exact source PCs, left frames, physical-register identities, fuel behavior and positive progress. Quantify the extra checker and soundness-lemma cost before proposing a new certificate format.
\item \textbf{When should compatibility adapters be retired?} The dependency cut is deliberately additive in total source. A later repository-wide client inventory could determine whether wrappers remain useful. Removing them would require a separate migration and compatibility test, not merely the smaller endpoint census.
\item \textbf{Is a shared C222 compiler algebra worthwhile now?} Direct C222 compilation is already integrated. Its remaining Lean proofs are only about 30 and 37 lines. A generic many-field interface and two adapters may cost more than the duplication it removes. Defer this abstraction until total helper, wrapper and dependency costs show a clear benefit.
\item \textbf{What exactly is needed for an internal ZFC equivalence?} The existing external proof-assistant theorem and the intended theorem encoded inside ZFC are distinct deliverables. Specify the internal machine semantics, proof coding and derivation target, then audit which current lemmas admit the required internal formalization. This is not a claim that ZFC proves its own consistency or the machine's nonhalting.
\end{enumerate}

The first acceptance milestone is modest and concrete: an unchanged K215 endpoint statement, a passing retained compatibility scope, and fresh source-bound kernel checks for the dependency cut and decrement fork. More ambitious abstractions should be justified by measurements after that milestone, not by repeating proposals the repository has already adopted.
