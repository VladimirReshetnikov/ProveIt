\section{A checked direct proof of unrestricted \(\rho_8\)}
\label{sec:rho8-direct}

Relative to ProveIt commit
\texttt{53269bcab1efc0f82d3022645bd7cf0c22d84959},
the accompanying Lean patch reduces \texttt{DomRho8.lean} from
\(30{,}114\) to \(26{,}709\) bytes, a reduction of \(3{,}405\) bytes
(\(11.31\%\)). It proves the identical \texttt{rho8\_all} statement,
and the unchanged immediate adapter \texttt{KCodeDom} also compiles.
This comparison is with the current, already simplified source.

Write \(a\to b\) for the arithmetic code \(\operatorname{wim}(a,b)\).
The predicate \(T\) contains truth code \(0\), is closed under modus
ponens, and contains the dominated rows
\[
 \rho_8(a,b,c,u)
 =
 ((c\to u)\to a)\to((a\to c)\to(b\to c))
 \qquad (u\le a).
\]
The desired theorem removes the condition \(u\le a\).
Every variable here ranges over all natural-number codes; no
well-formedness or injectivity assumption is introduced.

The retained hierarchy provides
\[
\begin{aligned}
 K(a,b)&:\quad a\to(b\to a),\\
 J(a,b,c)&:\quad (a\to b)\to(((a\to c)\to a)\to b),\\
 B(a,b,c)&:\quad (a\to b)\to((b\to c)\to(a\to c)).
\end{aligned}
\]
Two other retained schemas are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathrm{cd99}(a,b,x)&:\quad
       (a\to b)\to(a\to(x\to b)),\\
 \mathrm{cd88}(a,x,b)&:\quad
       (a\to(x\to b))\to((x\to a)\to(x\to b)).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus two applications of modus ponens establish the derived rule
\[
 T(a\to b)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 T((x\to a)\to(x\to b)).
 \tag{Lift}
\]
The orientation of \(B\) matters: a known right-hand implication
cannot simply be supplied as its first curried premise.

Set
\[
 q=(c\to u)\to c,\qquad
 f=(c\to u)\to a,\qquad
 g=a\to c,\qquad
 d=b\to c.
\]
Applying \(J(c,d,u)\) to \(K(c,b)\) gives \(T(q\to d)\).
Composition gives \(T(f\to(g\to q))\).
Two instances of \textup{(Lift)} give
\[
 T\bigl((f\to(g\to q))\to(f\to(g\to d))\bigr).
\]
One final modus ponens therefore proves \(T(\rho_8(a,b,c,u))\).

This construction retains all \(58\) needed bootstrap schemas and
\(52\) CD schemas, omitting \(\mathrm{cd100},\ldots,\mathrm{cd106}\).
It removes the auxiliary \texttt{Proof} relation, its soundness theorem,
and the two deduction lemmas. The classical content remains in \(J\);
the simplification removes a general metatheorem from this particular
derivation.

Lean \(4.32.0\) freshly compiled the \(13\)-module closure through
\texttt{KCodeDom}. Both the new root and the unchanged adapter have
assumption reports \texttt{[propext, Quot.sound]}.
A direct Coq mirror also freshly compiled under Coq \(8.18.0\), with
the two pinned arithmetic/code dependencies unchanged, and passed
independent \texttt{coqchk}. Its root is closed under the global context.
The direct mirror is \(24{,}352\) bytes and \(440\) lines, compared with
\(22{,}673\) bytes and \(1{,}169\) lines for the current reflected proof.
It offers a common direct proof structure; it is not a Coq byte-count
improvement.

The Lean graph slice removes the incidental exported helper
\texttt{distribution}; the unchanged endpoint adapter does not use it.
Retaining that helper and its seven schemas is an alternative when
complete helper-API compatibility is desired. The patch also normalizes
CRLF to LF before checking the pinned CD230 template hash, allowing
byte-exact regeneration from the current Git checkout.

These checks concern the dominated-row logical interface. They are not
a fresh rebuild of the complete binary-machine-to-ZFC endpoint, an
internal ZFC or PA arithmetization, or a reduction in the number of
machine states. The ordinary repository endpoint build remains the
integration gate. Exact sources, patches, hashes, generator receipts,
compiler logs and the independent kernel-check log accompany this report.
