SOURCE-SEARCH RESULT: A REBALANCED 215-STATE MACHINE
=================================================

Frozen candidate
---------------
The selected table is ../../machine/k215r.native.tm, with its complete source
../../machine/sites215r.json. It has 215 working states and 430 transitions.
All state counts exclude the separate halt state. It starts on the blank binary
tape, in native state 0 with head at 0, and uses only left and right moves.

The original search history contained fourteen rotations. Two pairs of inverse
row operations commute with every intervening operation, so four steps can be
deleted. Root and the source-search agent independently found the same deletion,
at original indices 0,3,8,11. Both ten-step reconstructions produce exactly the
same native table, dense table, and complete source as the fourteen-step history.

Compared with accepted candidate-k215, the native table changes nineteen rows
on eleven states. Its rooted deterministic transition graph is not isomorphic
to that of the primary candidate: BFS canonical forms differ. No physical state
has been deleted. This is a new 215-state source layout, not a 214-state result.

The source retains all 4,751 ordered primitives, all 6,176 guarded primitive
cases, and exactly the same ordinal control-flow graph. It still has 202
distinct source subtrees and maximum zero run 6. Its longest source address is
41 bits rather than 47. Sum of the 4,751 address lengths falls from 160,305 to
149,265, a reduction of 11,040 bits (6.8869 percent). The respective unweighted
mean lengths are 33.7413176173 and 31.4175962955. There are 3,734 shorter

source-address measurements, not a claim about the frequency of instructions

Ordinary proof structure
------------------------
1. At source level, a closed factor has no break or continuation whose target
   escapes that factor, and contains no halt instruction. The inherited boundary
   calculation verifies (0,0,false) for each factor. Reassociation preserves its
   ordered execution, normal exit, and divergence. Thus associativity of closed
   sequential factors is a source-program equivalence, including nontermination.

2. The reconstruction also checks the whole result without relying solely on
   that local theorem: ordered opcode/register lists are equal by ordinal; the
   complete guarded control graph is equal under each of two independently
   written graph resolvers. The identity map on the ordinal instruction number
   and all nine natural registers is therefore a bisimulation of the two
   source programs. This preserves the accepted command compiler and its
   calibration to the dominated numerical calculus without changing any axiom,
   proof rule, raw numerical-code convention, or enumeration hypothesis.

3. Every physical rotation uses two literal pure decoder rows. Both read
   symbols are preserved and the head moves one cell right. The new table is
   the stated four-row redirection. This does not assert all-tape equivalence
   between the old and new complete Turing machines: their program-counter
   encodings differ. The reconstruction instead checks all 4,750 actual
   internal decoder prefixes against the new source and then relies on the
   full arbitrary-natural primitive theorem for the actual table.

4. The unchanged inherited symbolic runtime checker completed all 6,176 cases
   on this exact table/source, with no failed cases. Its report status is
   UNIVERSAL_MATHEMATICAL_PASS. The cases quantify over arbitrary natural
   register values, an arbitrary natural gap, and a nonempty arbitrary finite
   spare list; their unary/list induction certificates are stronger than a
   finite sample of numeric inputs. The fresh run took 134.448450074 seconds.
   This is an ordinary mathematical checker, not a new Lean/Rocq kernel proof.

5. The exact blank execution reaches the same calibrated frame as the primary
   K215 after 95,714 transitions: native dispatcher 84, head -8, gap 1, logical
   registers [4,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0], forty unit spares, and the whole zero exterior.
   The saved startup.json includes the complete transition trace and final tape.
   The independent encoding-agent audit replays every transition in this prefix
   and verifies the exact final tape and zero exterior.

6. Composing source bisimulation, the new primitive theorem, positive runtime
   progress, the exact blank prefix, and the accepted calculus calibration gives
   the requested ordinary equivalence formalizable in ZFC. It does not prove
   Con(ZFC), absolute nonhalting, a new PA-internal endpoint, or an exact-table
   proof-assistant endpoint. No new formalism is needed for this candidate.

Useful search boundaries and side results
----------------------------------------
A closed-only AST beam of width 256 explored 319,346 distinct sources before a
180-second search cap. It found 202-subtree sources of depth 40 but no smaller
DAG. The best depth-40 source could not be allocated by the inherited restricted
injective role-pool allocator: 35 new roles had at most about 28 compatible
available physical states. That is a restriction of that allocator, not a
state-count lower bound.

A generalized source search allowed explicit changes of CB/CC depth operands,
provided both complete ordinal CFGs stayed equal. It considered 40,606 proposed
rotations, accepted 29,629 syntactically admissible retaggings, and encountered
20,578 distinct resulting sources before its 180-second cap. No improvement
on the 202-subtree boundary was found in that run. This is a bounded negative.

A targeted zero-run search reduced the number of source addresses containing
six consecutive zeros from 37 to two at 204 source subtrees. One additional
closed rotation of a repeated 160-leaf factor, with factors of sizes 52,28,80,
removes the last two occurrences. The exact zero5/sites.json has 205 distinct
subtrees, 4,751 leaves, depth 45 and maximum zero run 5; its complete source
word and guarded CFG equal the accepted primary source. A subsequent width-256
closed beam, constrained to zero run at most 5, reached a fixed frontier after
76,207 distinct sources and retained 205 as its best DAG count. Physical role-
pool allocation failed (108 new roles, at most about 89 candidates per role).
No binary state-count saving follows from this source-only result. It gives a
concrete scanner/source tradeoff to revisit with a different allocator.

Safe register renaming was also tried. Five direct exchanges of the candidate
counter N (register 0) with nonformula registers 3,4,6,7,8 were synthesized.
Exchanges with 3,4,7 failed early symbolic cases; exchange with 6 failed literal
decoder-prefix checks. These failures are checker failures, not by themselves
semantic impossibility proofs. Exchanging N with L (0 and 8) passed all 6,176
symbolic runtime cases, but the literal blank run halts after 15,607 steps.
Thus its otherwise-correct interpreter does not have a calibrated blank seed.
This is a useful concrete example of why a runtime theorem alone is insufficient.
The corresponding exchange in commands120 halts after 5,151 steps; the composed
allocation variant halts after 15,613 steps. None is an accepted candidate.

A C++ scanner checked every one of the 215 possible start states of the N/L
exchange, up to one million transitions per start. It found 210 exact outward
rays, three halts, and two still-unresolved bounded runs (start states 66,94),
and no safe root frame. Do not state that this proves every start impossible.
The same scanner on the accepted primary table recovers its exact 95,714-step
seed from start 0, with the complete correct register/spare list. The source,
positive control, and per-start records are retained.
