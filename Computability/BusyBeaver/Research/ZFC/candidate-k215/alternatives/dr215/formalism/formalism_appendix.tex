\section{A calibrated formalism family: antecedent exchange}
The numerical calculus can be changed without changing a single derivable
code. Retain the accepted dominated $\rho_8$ basis, all other rules and the
literal gated bundle. This retained basis derives $K$ and $S$, hence the
exchange theorem
\[
 (X\to(Y\to Z))\to(Y\to(X\to Z)).
\]
Indeed, under assumptions $f:X\to(Y\to Z)$, $y:Y$ and $x:X$, two modus
ponens steps yield $Z$. The finite $K/S$ deduction construction discharges
$x$, $y$ and $f$ in that order. No generalization is used in this derivation.
Swapping $X,Y$ supplies the converse. Thus replacing either orientation by
the other in any axiom schema preserves every numerical theorem code.
This argument requires neither syntax injectivity nor formula substitution;
it also applies at the native alias $I(0,0)=0$.

We applied this transformation to the two curried antecedents of
$\rho_2,\rho_3,\rho_5$ and the merged equality row $G$.
For example,
\[
 G(A,B,C)=E(C,A)\to
       \bigl(E(C,B)\to Q_{R(A,B)}E(A,B)\bigr)
\]
may be replaced by the row with $E(C,A)$ and $E(C,B)$ exchanged.
Both proof systems retain the same dominated $\rho_8$ basis, and therefore
each derives every omitted axiom of the other by exchange and modus ponens.
Replacing axiom leaves proves equality of all finite derivability predicates.
This supplies primitive-recursive refutation translations and preserves the
accepted consistency calibration.

In the exact K215 source, changing registers $4\leftrightarrow5$ in the
$\mathrm{CI}/\mathrm{CD}$ operands of the closed interval $[479,643)$
performs this exchange in $G$. All source paths, opcodes and guarded
successor ordinals remain unchanged. Its source DAG has $205$ nodes,
against $202$ in the baseline. Exhausting all $6^7=279{,}936$ independent
parameter permutations of the seven logical rows gives a minimum of $204$,
with four minimizers. A width-$64$ closed-rotation beam subsequently visits
$76{,}675$ sources over $11$ rounds and still finds $204$ as its best saved
count. These are scoped source results, not binary-machine state bounds or
global minimality assertions.

Individual closed-row re-emission gives best saved counts $205$ for exchanged
$\rho_2$, $205$ for exchanged $\rho_3$, $207$ for exchanged $\rho_5$, and $206$
for exchanged $G$. The original association improves the last count to $205$.
The dual-$\rho_6$ experiment reaches $203$ source nodes with maximum zero run
$8$, but remains uncalibrated: its usual classical equivalence argument uses
ex falso, whereas the deleted original $\rho_6$ supplies ex falso. A separate
refutation translation is still required. The complete theorem-family note,
literal sources, finite census, bounded-search receipts and portable scripts
are included in the formalism appendix directory.


On the new direct-transfer source DR215, the same exhaustive $6^7$ row
permutations have minimum $201$ source nodes (four minimizers). Applying
$G$ exchange first and then exhausting all $6^7$ permutations gives minimum
$203$ (four minimizers). An unrestricted closed-rotation beam on DR215 visits
$75{,}077$ sources over $11$ rounds and retains $201$ as its best saved
count. These final finite-family bounds and heuristic results do not rule
out $200$-node sources outside the families searched.
