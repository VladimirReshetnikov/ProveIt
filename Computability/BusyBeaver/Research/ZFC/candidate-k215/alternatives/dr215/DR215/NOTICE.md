# Provenance and notices

DR215 is a research derivative of Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt repository,
snapshot `b6fb232916d008383e7cf2990d68ddb30fded7db`, principally
`Computability/BusyBeaver/Research/ZFC/candidate-k215/alternatives/direct-transfer/`.

The exact ST215 parent machine and source are included under `inputs/`.
The direct-transfer compiler, protected-tail theorem, native-calculus
calibration, dominated-rho8 certificate and symbolic runtime engine are
inherited predecessor material. Files under `references/` preserve their
original text, including historical machine names and examples. The DR215
proof specifies the current changed row and actual bootstrap separately.

The rho6 parameter symmetry and the same two native row changes already
occur in the repository's `alternatives/rho6/` research. Their prior provenance
is acknowledged. The contribution here is the exact composition with the
direct-transfer ST215 source, a repaired strong compiler for that composition,
fresh quantified runtime and blank startup, and frozen-corpus comparison.

The three proof-engine modules and the fixed-certificate checker were copied
without semantic edits. Verification drivers are adapted or newly written
for DR215. The corpus was independently collected by the parallel engine
investigation from the same pinned repository; its manifest records every
original path and hash. No ownership of predecessor work is claimed.

Original notices, licenses and source pins are retained under `licenses/`
and `references/`. Please preserve them when incorporating this packet.
