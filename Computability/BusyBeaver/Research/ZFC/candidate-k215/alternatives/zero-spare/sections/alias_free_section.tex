\section{An implication fragment independent of the truth alias}
\label{sec:alias-free-IKP}

The inherited full dominated-$\rho_8$ certificate uses the literal
arithmetic identity $I(0,0)=0$. This creates a separate issue when
considering an implication constructor with a nonzero residue tag.
The following new finite derivation removes that dependence for
identity, weakening, and Peirce's law. It does not claim the whole
classical-basis or encoding-calibration theorem.

For this section let $X$ be any set, let $I:X\times X\to X$ be any
binary operation, written $a\to b$, and let $\operatorname{Th}$ be a
predicate on $X$. Define
\[
 L(a,b,c,u)=((c\to u)\to a)\to((a\to c)\to(b\to c)).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Two subterm schemas derive $I,K,P$]
\label{thm:alias-free-IKP}
Suppose $\operatorname{Th}$ is closed under modus ponens and contains
every instance of
\[
 \mathrm A_0:\ L(u,b,c,u),\qquad
 \mathrm A_1:\ L(z\to u,b,c,u).
\]
Then it contains every instance of
\[
 p\to p,\qquad p\to(q\to p),\qquad ((p\to q)\to p)\to p.
\]
No truth constant, equation on $I$, injectivity, order, or interpretation
of members of $X$ as formulas is assumed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The following is a complete 22-node schematic derivation.
Its notation is Polish implication: $Cuv$ means $u\to v$, and letters
are metavariables universally quantified independently in each row.
$\mathrm{CD}(i,j)$ means instantiate major row $i$ and minor row $j$
so that the minor is exactly the antecedent of the major, then apply
modus ponens. Every required substitution is explicitly stored in
\texttt{research/alias\_free/alias\_free\_ikp.json}; the table gives the resulting schemas.

Rows 11, 15 and 21 are the three asserted schemas.
The independent forward checker evaluates all stored substitutions and
checks exact antecedent and consequent equality at each of the twenty
modus-ponens nodes. It checks the two axiom shapes structurally.
There is no implication normalization, truth leaf, unification, or
search in that checker. The resulting finite derivation is valid under
every valuation in $X$: equal formal expressions have equal evaluations,
whether or not distinct expressions can have the same value.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Application to every nonnegative implication tag]
\label{cor:alias-free-tags}
Let $P$ be Cantor pairing and let $I_j(a,b)=4P(a,b)+j$ with $j\geq0$.
Any numerical calculus closed under modus ponens and containing
$L(A,B,C,U)$ whenever $U\leq A$, using $I_j$ as implication, derives
every numerical instance of identity, weakening, and Peirce.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
For $\mathrm A_0$, the restriction is $u\leq u$.
For $\mathrm A_1$, it is $u\leq I_j(z,u)$.
Cantor pairing dominates each coordinate, and hence
$I_j(z,u)\geq u$. Theorem~\ref{thm:alias-free-IKP} now applies directly
to $X=\mathbb N$. Its argument includes every natural assignment,
including zero, aliases, and codes not decoded by a conventional
formula grammar.
\end{proof}

In particular, this corollary applies to the proposed
$I_3/R_1/Q_0/E_2$ constructor assignment, in which the old equation
$I(0,0)=0$ is absent. It establishes three exact logical interfaces for
that encoding without translating the numerical order or the shared
formula-code/variable-label namespace.

The remaining gap is explicit. This certificate does not derive
distribution $S$, composition $B$, or the unrestricted four-argument
$L$ schema. The earlier full certificate therefore cannot yet be
replaced by this fragment to obtain the alternate encoding's complete
consistency calibration. The unresolved derivations are further
research questions; incomplete searches are not evidence that they
are impossible. The released zero-spare machine retains the inherited
implication constructor and its existing full calibration, so its
correctness does not depend on resolving this separate question.

The proof search adapted the repository's existing
condensed-detachment helpers. The new contribution is the two-schema
derivation, its explicit-substitution certificate, and its independent
forward checker; the search infrastructure is inherited.
