\begin{abstract}
We construct a materially different 215-working-state, binary, single-tape machine started on an everywhere blank tape. Its command language is the newly published MP-first transfer language; its physical allocator uses zero-valued spare blocks instead of the parent's unit-valued blocks. Neither feature is claimed as newly invented in isolation. Their combination has a different transition graph and a different initialization proof. The exact 73,172-step blank prefix reaches a matched interior axiom-emission frame with an initially unsafe cache. Completion of that axiom leaves eight theorem heads above a suffix whose theoremhood is not assumed. A Cantor-diagonal credit theorem for the MP-first enumeration bounds all future conservative debt by one, permanently protecting the suffix below at least seven heads.

The machine is accompanied by its full transition table, source, construction recipe, exact native/dense startup replay, and arbitrary-natural primitive-runtime certificate. The numerical calculus and its ZFC calibration are inherited with explicit attribution. This is a new composition and ordinary mathematical proof, not a claim of 214 states, minimum size, unconditional nonhalting, or a newly compiled proof-assistant endpoint for these exact bytes.
\end{abstract}

\section{Complete canonical transition table}
The working labels are $0,\ldots,214$. The initial state is 0; halt $H$ denotes label 215 and has no outgoing transitions. The initial head is at coordinate 0 on an all-zero tape. An entry $wDq$ writes $w$, moves one cell in direction $D$, and enters $q$. This is the complete machine, not a preinitialized simulator.
