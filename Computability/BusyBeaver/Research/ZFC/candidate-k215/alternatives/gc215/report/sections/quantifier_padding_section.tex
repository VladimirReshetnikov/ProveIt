\subsection{A quantified axiom sampler with a strict numerical bound}
\label{sec:quantifier-padding}
Let $P(a,b)=(a+b)(a+b+1)/2+b$, $I(a,b)=4P(a,b)$,
$F=1$, $\neg A=I(A,F)$, and
$Q_iA=4P(i,A)+2$.  Existential quantification means the literal numerical
operation $E_i A=\neg Q_i\neg A$.  The sixth native axiom is
\[
 R_6(i,A)=(E_iQ_i A)\to A.
\]
All parameters below range over natural numbers, whether or not they
are images of an ordinary formula encoder.

\paragraph{Strict matrix restriction.}
Retain the native implication basis, normality $R_2$, modus ponens,
and generalization of closed theorems. Replace the sixth axiom family
by only its instances with $i<A$. The resulting numerical calculus
derives every unrestricted sixth-row instance, at its original code.
The weaker restriction $i\leq A$ therefore has the same property.

For a proof, fix $i,A$, and put
\[
 e=i+1,\qquad W=I(e,e),\qquad M=I(W,A).
\]
The identity formula $W$ is a theorem of K and S. Consequently the
same two implication schemas prove both $A\to M$ and $M\to A$.
All these are closed theorems. Generalization and $R_2$ transport the
first implication through $Q_i$. Implicational contraposition,
normality, and contraposition once more give monotonicity of the
literal operation $E_i$. Thus
\[
 E_iQ_i A\longrightarrow E_iQ_i M.
\]
The restricted axiom $R_6(i,M)$ and $M\to A$ now give $R_6(i,A)$.
This argument uses neither the unrestricted sixth row nor explosion.
In particular it does not silently replace relative falsity by an
ordinary primitive false constant.

The restriction is met for every numerical input, including zero:
\[
 W=8(i+1)(i+2),\qquad
 M\geq4W=32(i+1)(i+2)>i.
\]
The last inequality has the positive polynomial witness
$32i^2+95i+64$. A positive padding parameter avoids the exceptional
identity $I(0,0)=0$ entirely.

The supplied independent checker verifies a forward Hilbert certificate
with 107 nodes and 124 expression nodes. Its only inference and axiom
kinds are K (28), S (21), MP (53), $R_2$ (2), closed generalization (2),
and a single strictly permitted $R_6$ instance. It checks every exact
inference and the full dependency closure. The existing dominated-$R_8$
certificate supplies K and S independently of $R_6$; the construction is
therefore noncircular.

\paragraph{Arbitrary finite cutoffs.}
More generally, for any specified total primitive-recursive function $g$,
retaining only matrices $A>g(i)$ still recovers the full sixth row.
Replace $e$ above by $g(i)+1$. Only the padding parameter changes:
all $Q_i$ operators retain binder $i$, and
$M\ge32(g(i)+1)(g(i)+2)>g(i)$. For each $g$ whose totality is formalized
in ZFC, the finite proof replacement and the resulting equality of
all native theorem codes are formalizable there. This claim concerns a
full numerical tail for each binder, not an arbitrary sparse cofinal
subset of matrices.

\paragraph{Source interface and remaining allocation problem.}
The accepted AX command sets $U=d$ and $A=d+x$. Replacing just its
sixth output by $R_6(U,A)$ would enumerate all pairs with $U\leq A$.
The preceding theorem supplies its exact logical completeness.
A desired restricted row $R_6(i,b)$ is selected from a clean source
state by
\[
 \mathsf{DISCARD};\mathsf J(i);
 \mathsf{AX}(0;b-i,0);
 S^{7};H^{3}.
\]
All other source compiler blocks retain their contracts if the other AX
outputs and reset behavior remain unchanged. An unrestricted-row proof
is first translated using the 107-node certificate.

This is an actionable logical interface, not yet a smaller machine.
In the current postfix emitter, $U$ has already been consumed into the
partial eighth-row formula before the sixth row is constructed. A new
emission schedule or temporary-storage arrangement is still needed to
turn this restriction into a physical saving. In particular, incrementing
the common $A$ input would change other axioms and is not justified by
this sixth-row theorem.
