# Provenance and incorporation

Prepared for incorporation into Vladimir Reshetnikov's ProveIt project.
Date: 20 October 2026 in America/Los_Angeles (21 October 2026 UTC).

This packet derives its numerical calculus, binary architecture, source-language
semantics, transfer language, strong derivation compiler, and runtime proof
framework from the pinned ProveIt snapshot and its cited incoming research.
Those inherited contributions are explicitly attributed in the report.

The new primary candidate is the MP=0/S=1 enumeration and its exact eight-row
allocation; new work includes its one-head scheduler proof, exact-case replay,
independent audits and comparisons, one-orientation commutation reduction,
and the separately labeled short-transfer and scanner research.

Attribution license. Existing upstream notices and licenses are preserved in
`reference/transfer-parent/licenses` and `reference/transfer-parent/provenance`.
New code and text in this packet are provided for the same project use under
MIT No Attribution. No inherited authorship is reassigned.

The parent report and some inherited documentation discuss the ideal transfer
with only a cache guard. For the literal composite body, the authoritative
specification is the two-guard equation in the new report and the preserved
`reference/transfer-parent/proofs/composite_transfer_review.md`.
