# Represented relativization and conditional internal proof transport

`InternalRelativization.lean` defines one total represented relativizer on every code of an arbitrary IΣ1 model. Atoms and propositional connectives retain their syntax; universal and existential quantifiers acquire the one-slot domain guard. The file proves formation, negation, shift, substitution and free-variable laws, and maps internal sequents and bounded domain-side sets using actual HFS Sigma1 replacement.

`derivable_relSet` transports every internal source derivation, including nonstandard codes. Its induction predicate is Sigma1 and existentially carries an internal side-set bound. It explicitly assumes a well-formed shift-fixed domain, variable-only closed terms, and target derivability of every relativized source axiom with finitely many domain side formulas. The conclusion retains those side formulas. Their discharge, actual L-domain/axiom proofs, the C254 reverse adapter and the final PA equivalence are separate obligations.

The source follows upstream commits `81003cbe3b` and `1dd92a0e7e` by Vladimir Reshetnikov with Claude Opus5.5 attribution. The local change before checking corrects only the header's description of the remaining obligations. The original11-line/five-probe audit is preserved. A separate `InternalRelativizationExtendedAudit.lean` checks11 ordered theorem axiom reports and12 generic callers, including internal formation and full conditional transport.
